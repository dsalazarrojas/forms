# Health Informatics Developer Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and professional information from aspiring health informatics developers for training and development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal and professional details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, marked with a `*` symbol in the label.
3. Review your submission before submitting it, making sure that all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name***: Enter your full first name. This is the first field on the form.
* **Last Name***: Enter your full last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if available.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Education Background**: Describe your educational background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Work Experience**: Briefly describe your work experience, including positions and companies.
* **Skills**: Select all the programming languages you are proficient in, such as C++, Java, Python, and JavaScript.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title, or leave it blank if you don't have one.
* **Current Work Place**: Enter your current workplace.
* **Expected Graduation Date**: If applicable, enter the date you expect to graduate.
* **Resume**: Upload or paste a link to your resume, for reference purposes.
* **Photo**: Upload or paste a link to a profile picture, for identity verification.
* **Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to have your data used for training and development purposes.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the program.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your registration form.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes, if needed.
* **Job Title 1-7**: Enter any additional job titles, if applicable.
