# Academic Mentorship Session Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants and mentors for recording academic mentorship sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your participant or mentor name in the "Participant name" or "Mentor name" field, respectively.
2. Enter the "Session date" of the scheduled session.
3. Check or uncheck "Consent to record session" as per your preference.
4. Select the "Use of recording" option that best describes how you want the recording to be used.
5. If necessary, specify "Access restrictions" to restrict who will have access to the recording.
6. Sign your full name in the "Consent signature" field to confirm your consent.
7. Date the "Consent date" field to record the date the consent was given.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (Participant name, text, required): Enter your full name as the participant.
* **Mentor name** (Mentor name, text, required): Enter your full name as the mentor.
* **Session date** (Session date, date, required): Enter the scheduled date of the mentorship session.
* **Consent to record** (Consent to record session, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to record the session.
* **Use of recording** (Use of recording, select_one, required): Choose how you want the recording to be used.
	+ For participant review only: For your personal review only.
	+ For training materials with consent: For use in training materials, with your consent.
	+ For research with anonymization: For research purposes, with your identity removed.
	+ Other: Specify your own use case.
* **Retention period** (Retention period for recording, select_one, optional): Choose how long you want the recording to be stored.
	+ 6 months: Store the recording for 6 months.
	+ 1 year: Store the recording for 1 year.
	+ Indefinitely: Store the recording indefinitely.
	+ Other: Specify your own retention period.
* **Access restrictions** (Access restrictions, text, optional): Specify who will have access to the recording.
* **Consent signature** (Consent signature, text, required): Sign your full name to confirm your consent.
* **Consent date** (Consent date, date, required): Date the consent was given.
