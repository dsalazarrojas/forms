# Grant Proposal Coaching Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grant Proposal Coaching Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from you to provide personalized grant proposal coaching services. Our goal is to help you create effective grant proposals that increase your chances of securing funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name in the field marked "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your valid email address in the field marked "Email".
4. Enter your contact number in the field labeled "Contact Number".
5. In the "Grant Goals" field, describe the specific goals you are seeking to achieve with grant proposals.
6. In the "Availability" field, specify your preferred schedule for coaching sessions.
7. Select all applicable coaching services you are interested in from the dropdown menu in the field labeled "Coaching Services". You can select multiple services if needed.
8. Choose a date for the coaching session in the field marked "Schedule Date".
9. Select a time for the coaching session in the field labeled "Schedule Time".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to allow us to communicate with you.
* **Contact Number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number in case we need to contact you.
* **Grant Goals** (`grant_goals`, text, required/optional): Describe the specific goals you are seeking to achieve with grant proposals.
* **Availability** (`scheduling_preferences`, text, required/optional): Specify your preferred schedule for coaching sessions.
* **Coaching Services** (`coaching_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable coaching services you are interested in.
	+ Grant Proposals: Help with creating grant proposals.
	+ Grant Writing Services: Comprehensive grant writing services.
	+ Grant Strategy Sessions: Coaching to develop a grant proposal strategy.
	+ Grant Writing: Writing grant proposals for you.
	+ Review and Feedback: Review of your grant proposal and feedback on improvement.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for the coaching session.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, required/optional): Select a time for the coaching session.
