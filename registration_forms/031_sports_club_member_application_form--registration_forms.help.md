# sports-club-member-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users who wish to register as a member of the sports club. It collects personal and emergency contact information to help us serve you better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details (Name, email, phone number).
2. Provide your address and age.
3. Enter your birthdate.
4. Add emergency contact information (name, phone number, relationship, and note).
5. Specify your sports interest (e.g., Basketball, Soccer, Tennis).
6. Choose your availability (e.g., all day, morning, afternoon).
7. Provide an optional note for your availability.
8. Select a payment plan (e.g., month-to-month, bi-monthly, quarterly).
9. If necessary, provide additional emergency contact information (relationship, note, phone number, email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Please fill in your full name as you want it to be displayed in the club.
* **email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **address**: Fill in your address for us to know where to reach you.
* **age**: Enter your age for age-relevant services.
* **birthdate**: Enter your birthdate for age-related services.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Choose how you're related to the emergency contact person (e.g., first parent, first spouse/partner).
* **Emergency Contact Note**: Provide any additional information about the emergency contact person.
* **Sports Interest**: Choose the sports you're interested in (e.g., Basketball, Soccer, Tennis).
* **Availability**: Select the times you're available (e.g., all day, morning, afternoon).
* **Availability Note**: Provide any additional information about your availability.
* **Payment Plan**: Choose the payment plan that suits your needs (e.g., month-to-month, bi-monthly, quarterly).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other**: If the emergency contact person is not listed, provide their relationship here (e.g., first parent).
* **Emergency Contact Note Alt**: Provide any additional information about the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Alt**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person's alternate contact.
* **Emergency Contact Alt**: Enter any other contact information for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Alt**: If the emergency contact person's relationship is not listed, choose from the options (e.g., first parent).
* **Emergency Contact Interest**: Choose the interest of the emergency contact person (e.g., Sports, Non sports, Both).
* **Emergency Contact Interest Alt**: Choose the interest of the emergency contact person's alternate contact (e.g., Sports, Non sports, Both).
* **Emergency Contact Alt Alt**: Enter any other contact information for the emergency contact person's alternate contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Alt Alt**: If the emergency contact person's relationship is not listed, choose from the options (e.g., first parent).
