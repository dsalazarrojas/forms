# Nursery Field Trip Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians for their child's field trip registration. It covers various aspects of the child's medical details, vehicle information, and other relevant fields. Please complete the form accurately to ensure a smooth and safe field trip experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your contact phone number for easy communication.
3. If your child has any medical details, such as allergies or medical conditions, please specify them in the "Medical Details" field.
4. Indicate whether you grant permission for your child to participate in the field trip.
5. Confirm whether your child has a medical certificate.
6. Confirm whether you own a vehicle for your child to travel in.
7. If your child has a vehicle, provide the vehicle details, including make, model, year, color, license number, and plate number.
8. If necessary, provide additional medical notes or details.
9. If you have any medical conditions for your child, please specify them in the "Medical Condition" field.
10. Finally, confirm any allergies your child may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, text, required/optional): Specify any medical details or conditions your child may have.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you grant permission for your child to participate in the field trip.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm whether your child has a medical certificate.
* **Vehicle** (`vehicle`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether your child will be traveling in a vehicle.
* **Date of Birthday** (`date_of_birthday`, date, required/optional): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required/optional): Enter your child's time of birth.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional medical notes or details.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): Specify any medical conditions your child may have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional): Confirm any allergies your child may have.
* **Medical Condition Note** (`medical_condition_note`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional medical condition notes.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required/optional): Enter the make of your child's vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required/optional): Enter the model of your child's vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the year of your child's vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, required/optional): Enter the color of your child's vehicle.
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the license number of your child's vehicle.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle registration number.
* **Vehicle Insurance Number** (`vehicle_insurance_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle insurance number.
* **Vehicle Plate Number** (`vehicle_plate_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the plate number of your child's vehicle.
* **Vehicle Registration Number Plate Number** (`vehicle_registration_number_plate_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the plate number on the vehicle registration number.
* **Vehicle Registration Number License Number** (`vehicle_registration_number_license_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the license number on the vehicle registration number.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition_note_2`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional medical condition notes.

Note: Please review the form carefully before submitting, and feel free to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
