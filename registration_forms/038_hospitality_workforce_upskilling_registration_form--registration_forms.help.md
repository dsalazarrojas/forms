# Hospitality Workforce Upskilling Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Hospitality Workforce Upskilling program. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly to ensure that your application is processed correctly. This form is for individuals who are genuinely interested in upskilling and are not just registering out of obligation.
## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review each field to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
- Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
- Review and submit your form once you have completed it.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address where your registration confirmation and program updates will be sent. Please ensure it is up-to-date and correct.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number for us to contact you with important updates and notifications.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Please enter your current job title as this will help us tailor the program to your needs.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, required): Please enter the name of the training provider you would like to upskill with. This will help us ensure that you are registered with the correct provider.
* **Training Course** (`training_course`, text, required): Please enter the name of the training course you would like to take. This will help us ensure that you are registered for the correct course.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you would like to start the training course. This will help us schedule the course and provide you with the necessary resources.
