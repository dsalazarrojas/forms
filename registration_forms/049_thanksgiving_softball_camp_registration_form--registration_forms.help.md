# Thanksgiving Softball Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for the Thanksgiving Softball Camp. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

- Fill in the participant information, including the participant name, date of birth, email, phone number, street, city, state, and zip code.
- Provide contact information for the emergency contact, including their name, relationship, phone number, street, city, state, and zip code.
- Answer the medical questions, including medical conditions, allergies, and medications.
- Confirm your payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the participant's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone_number`, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Street** (`participant_street`, text, required): Enter the participant's street address.
* **City** (`participant_city`, text, required): Enter the participant's city.
* **State** (`participant_state`, text, required): Enter the participant's state.
* **Zip** (`participant_zip`, text, required): Enter the participant's zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`participant_emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`participant_emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`participant_emergency_contact_phone_number`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Street** (`participant_emergency_contact_street`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's street address.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`participant_emergency_contact_city`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's city.
* **Emergency Contact State** (`participant_emergency_contact_state`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's state.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (`participant_emergency_contact_zip`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's zip code.
* **Medical Conditions** (`participant_medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the participant has any medical conditions.
* **Allergies** (`participant_allergies`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the participant has any allergies.
* **Medications** (`participant_medications`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the participant takes any medications.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required): Enter any additional participant information (if necessary).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter any additional contact information (if necessary).
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, required): Enter any event information (if necessary).
* **Registration Information** (`registration_info`, text, required): Enter any registration information (if necessary).
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter payment information (if necessary).
* **Emergency Contact Street** (`participant_emergency_contact_street`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's street address.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`participant_emergency_contact_city`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's city.
* **Emergency Contact State** (`participant_emergency_contact_state`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's state.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (`participant_emergency_contact_zip`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's zip code.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
