# Kayaking Safety Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in a kayaking safety course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name, email, and phone number.
2. Indicate your relationship to the participant (e.g., parent, friend, family).
3. Provide emergency contact information, including name, relationship, phone number, and email.
4. List any relevant medical conditions or certifications for both the participant and emergency contact.
5. Fill in any additional information about the participant that we should know.
6. Fill in the instructor's name, phone number, email, and address.
7. Indicate the instructor's type (e.g., waterfront, riverine, lakefront, oceanfront).
8. List any relevant certification or experience.
9. Add any additional information about the instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This is the participant's personal information.
* **Participants Name**: This is the participant's name.
* **Participants Email**: This is the participant's email address.
* **Participants Phone**: This is the participant's phone number.
* **Participants Waivers**: This is an acknowledgement that the participant and their legal guardian(s) has read, sign, and submit this waiver. (This field is optional)
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Participant**: This is the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, friend, family).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This is the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: This is the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Certification**: This is the certification or qualification of the emergency contact person (e.g., First Responder, EMT, Firefighter, Doctor).
* **Medical Conditions**: This is any relevant medical information about the participant.
* **Participant Medical Conditions**: This is any relevant medical information about the participant.
* **Participant Medical Certification**: This is the medical certification or qualification of the participant (e.g., Asthma, Diabetes, Epilepsy, Other).
* **Participant Medical Information**: This is any additional information about the participant's medical condition.
* **Participant Dietary Restriction**: This is any dietary restriction or preference of the participant.
* **Participant Allergies**: This is any allergy information about the participant.
* **Participant Important Information**: This is any additional information about the participant that we should know.
* **Instructor Name**: This is the name of the instructor.
* **Instructor Phone**: This is the phone number of the instructor.
* **Instructor Email**: This is the email address of the instructor.
* **Instructor Address**: This is the address of the instructor.
* **Instructor Type**: This is the type of instructor (e.g., waterfront, riverine, lakefront, oceanfront).
* **Instructor Certification**: This is the certification or qualification of the instructor.
* **Instructor Experience**: This is the experience of the instructor.
* **Instructor Additional Information**: This is any additional information about the instructor.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information about the participant and instructor.
* Some fields are required, so make sure to fill them in before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* We value your safety and well-being, so please take care when filling out this form.
