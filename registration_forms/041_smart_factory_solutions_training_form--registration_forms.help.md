# Smart Factory Solutions Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in the Smart Factory Solutions Training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Select the date of the training event in the "Date" field.
5.  Choose the time of the training event in the "Time" field.
6.  Enter any comments about yourself or the training in the "Comments" field (optional).
7.  Select your department from the list in the "Department" field.
8.  If applicable, select your manager's name from the list in the "Manager" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it is spelled.
*   **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it is spelled.
*   **Email (email)**: Enter your valid email address.
*   **Date (date)**: Select the date of the training event using the date format (month/day/year).
*   **Time (time)**: Choose the time of the training event using the 12-hour format (e.g., 01:00 PM).
*   **Location (note)**: Enter any notes or comments about the training location.
*   **Comments (text)**: Enter any comments about yourself or the training.
*   **Department (select multiple)**: Choose one or more departments that apply to you.
*   **Manager (select one)**: Select your manager's name from the list.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
*   Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, contact the form owner at form@gic.mx.
