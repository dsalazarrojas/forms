# Retail Automation Training Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Automation Training Program Registration Form is used to register users for a training session in the Retail Automation Training Program. This form collects user information and preferences to facilitate a smooth and efficient training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) if they are required.
2. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred training session time from the "Training Session" dropdown list.
6. Choose a date for the training session in the "Training Date" field.
7. Enter the location for the training session in the "Training Location" field.
8. Provide any additional details about your registration in the "Additional Details" field.
9. Review your registration information in the "Training Notes" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your registration or any other comments.
* **How do you know us?** (`retail_automation_training_program`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about how you know us.
* **What is your name?** (`retail_automation_training_program_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date for the training session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time for the training session.
* **Training Session** (`retail_automation_training_session`, select_one, optional): Select the time for the training session.
* **Training Date** (`training_session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the training session.
* **Training Location** (`training_session_location`, text, optional): Enter the location for the training session.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your registration.
* **Training Notes** (`retail_automation_training_program_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes about the training session.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your registration.
* **Back** (`retail_automation_training_program`, text, optional): Click this button to return to the previous page.

Note: Please ensure that you fill in all required fields and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth and efficient training experience.
