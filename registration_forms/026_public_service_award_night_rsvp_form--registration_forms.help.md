# public_service_award_night_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register guest attendance at a public service award night. Please fill out the form with your details and confirm your attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest count, including yourself.
2. List the names of each guest attending.
3. Enter the email addresses of each guest (if they have one).
4. Check if you will be attending (yes/no).
5. Select your preferred RSVP type (In Person/Virtual).
6. Choose your dietary preference (Vegan, Vegetarian, Omnivore, or None).
7. Confirm your RSVP time and date (if applicable).
8. Add any special instructions or requests.
9. Confirm your attendance status (True/False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`public_service_award_night_rsvp_form`, text, not applicable): This is the form title.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, **required**): Enter the number of guests you will be attending, including yourself.
* **Guest Attendee Names** (`guest_attendee_names`, text, not applicable): List each guest's name.
* **Guest Attendee Email** (`guest_attendee_email`, email, not applicable): Enter the email address of each guest (if they have one).
* **Will Attend** (`will_attend`, select_one, **required**): Check if you will be attending (yes/no).
* **RSVP Type** (`rsvp_type`, select_one, **required**): Select whether you will be attending In Person or Virtual.
* **Guest Diet** (`guest_diet`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose your preferred diet type (Vegan, Vegetarian, Omnivore, or None).
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, not applicable): If you need to specify a time, enter it here (if not, leave blank).
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, not applicable): If you need to specify a date, enter it here (if not, leave blank).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, not applicable): If you have any special requests or instructions, add them here.
* **Confirm Attendance** (`confirm_attendance`, select_one, **required**): Confirm your attendance status (True/False).
