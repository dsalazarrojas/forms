# Emergency Release Authorization Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about emergency contact persons and their relationship with the individual in need of care or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the accurate information.
2. Review each field carefully and provide as much detail as necessary.
3. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name and contact information of the organization.
* **Emergency contact person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, `false`): Check the box that describes the relationship between the emergency contact person and the individual in need of care or support.
* **Address** (`address`, `note`, `false`): Enter the address of the emergency contact person, if applicable.
* **Emergency release date** (`emergency_release_date`, `date`, `false`): Enter the date on which the emergency release was given, if applicable.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, `false`): Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `text`, `false`): Enter the contact email of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact note** (`contact_note`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional contact information or notes about the emergency contact person.
* **Alternate contact phone** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone`, `text`, `false`): Enter the alternate contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Alternate contact email** (`emergency_contact_alternate_email`, `text`, `false`): Enter the alternate contact email of the emergency contact person.
* **Alternate contact note** (`emergency_contact_alternate_note`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional alternate contact information or notes about the emergency contact person.
* **Organization approval** (`organization_approval`, `select_one`, `false`): Check the box indicating whether the organization has approved the emergency contact person.
