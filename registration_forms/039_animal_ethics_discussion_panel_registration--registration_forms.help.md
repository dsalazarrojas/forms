# Animal Ethics Discussion Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of registering for the Animal Ethics Discussion Panel. The form is split into several sections that will help us understand your background, interests, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to include all required fields. Required fields are marked as such in the form.
2. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form. Your information will help us tailor the discussion to meet your needs.
3. Review and confirm your submission before clicking "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (`1`, `Full Name`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`2`, `Email Address`, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for updates and confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`3`, `Phone Number`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Organization or Institution** (`4`, `Organization or Institution`, optional): If you are representing an organization, please enter its name.
* **Job Title or Role** (`5`, `Job Title or Role`, optional): Enter your job title or role for reference.
* **Registration Introduction** (`6`, `Animal Ethics Discussion Panel Registration`, required): This is a placeholder text. Please ignore it.
* **How Will You Attend** (`7`, `How Will You Attend`, required): Select one of the options provided:
	+ In-person: You will attend the event in person.
	+ Virtual - online: You will attend the event remotely.
	+ Hybrid - either: You will attend both in person and remotely.
* **Which Panel Date Will You Attend** (`8`, `Which Panel Date Will You Attend`, required): Select the date of the panel you would like to attend. Please note that dates may change.
* **Your Professional Category** (`9`, `Your Professional Category`, required): Select your relevant category:
	+ Veterinarian: You work in the field of veterinary medicine.
	+ Researcher: You are a researcher in animal-related studies.
	+ Ethics scholar: You have a focus on animal ethics.
	+ Animal welfare advocate: You advocate for animal welfare.
	+ Student: You are a student studying animal-related topics.
	+ General public: You are not in a related field but have an interest in animal ethics.
	+ Policy maker: You are a policy maker in animal-related fields.
	+ Other: Select this option if your category is not listed.
* **Experience Level with Animal Ethics** (`10`, `Your Experience with Animal Ethics`, required): Select your level of experience:
	+ Expert - work in the field: You have extensive experience working in animal-related fields.
	+ Knowledgeable - studied the topic: You have studied animal ethics in depth.
	+ Interested - learning more: You are interested in learning more about animal ethics.
	+ New to the topic: You are new to the subject of animal ethics.
* **Topics You Are Most Interested In** (`11`, `Topics You Are Most Interested In`, required): Select all topics that apply:
	+ Animal research ethics
	+ Farm animal welfare
	+ Companion animal ethics
	+ Wildlife conservation ethics
	+ Animal rights philosophy
	+ Veterinary ethics
	+ Policy and legislation
	+ Cultural perspectives
* **How Would You Like to Participate** (`12`, `How Would You Like to Participate`, required): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Attendee only: You will attend the event without contributing.
	+ Attendee with questions: You will attend and ask questions.
	+ Panelist: You will participate in the panel discussion.
	+ Moderator: You will moderate a panel discussion.
	+ Presenter: You will present a topic.
* **If Presenting, What Is Your Topic** (`13`, `If Presenting, What Is Your Topic`, optional): Enter a brief description of your presentation.
* **Brief Abstract or Description** (`14`, `Brief Abstract or Description`, optional): Enter a 200-word abstract or description of your presentation.
* **What Questions Would You Like Discussed** (`15`, `What Questions Would You Like Discussed`, optional): Enter your questions or topics for discussion.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`16`, `Dietary Requirements`, optional): Select one of the options provided:
	+ None
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-free
	+ Kosher
	+ Halal
	+ Other
* **Accessibility Needs** (`17`, `Accessibility Needs`, optional): Enter any accessibility needs or requirements.
* **Are You Interested in Networking Opportunities** (`18`, `Are You Interested in Networking Opportunities`, optional): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Yes - very interested
	+ Yes - somewhat interested
	+ False
* **Do You Need Continuing Education Credits** (`19`, `Do You Need Continuing Education Credits`, optional): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Yes - please provide
	+ False
* **What Type of CE Credits** (`20`, `What Type of CE Credits`, optional): Enter the type of CE credits you need.
* **Registration Fee Category** (`21`, `Registration Fee Category`, required): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Professional - full price
	+ Student - discounted
	+ Early bird
	+ Group rate
	+ Complimentary
* **Payment Method** (`22`, `Payment Method`, optional): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Credit card
	+ Invoice
	+ Purchase order
	+ Check
	+ Waived
* **How Did You Hear About This Panel** (`23`, `How Did You Hear About This Panel`, optional): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Email
	+ Social media
	+ Colleague
	+ Website
	+ Organization newsletter
	+ Other
* **What Are Your Expectations** (`24`, `What Are Your Expectations`, optional): Enter your expectations for the event.
* **Additional Comments** (`25`, `Additional Comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **I Accept the Event Terms and Conditions** (`26`, `I Accept the Event Terms and Conditions`, required): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Yes - I accept
	+ False
* **Attendee Signature** (`27`, `Attendee Signature`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Date** (`28`, `Date`, required): Enter today's date.

Please review the form carefully and submit your registration by clicking the "Submit" button.
