# Team Leader Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Leader Training Registration Form is designed to collect information from team leaders who wish to register for training sessions. This form helps us understand their needs, preferences, and expectations, ensuring we provide the best possible experience for our team leaders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the fields with your relevant details.
3. Select the correct options for each field, if applicable.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Leader Training Registration Form** (`title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which is also the name of the form.
* **Registration Form Page 1** (`registration_form_page_1`, text, not required): This is the first page of the form where you can enter any relevant information you'd like to share with us.
* **Registration Form Page 2** (`registration_form_page_2`, text, not required): This is the second page of the form where you can enter any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, text, not required): This field allows you to select multiple options from a list. Please choose all that apply.
* **Time** (`time`, text, not required): Enter your availability for the training session.
* **Date** (`date`, text, not required): Enter the date you'd like to attend the training session.
* **Note** (`note`, text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, text, not required): Enter a decimal value (e.g., a score or rating).
* **Integer** (`integer`, text, not required): Enter an integer value (e.g., a whole number).
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, text, not required): This is another select multiple field where you can choose multiple options.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, text, not required): This field allows you to select one option from a list.
* **Decimal 2** (`decimal_2`, text, not required): Enter another decimal value.
* **Integer 2** (`integer_2`, text, not required): Enter another integer value.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, text, not required): Enter the date for another training session.
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, text, not required): Enter the time for another training session.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
* **Select One 3** (`select_one_3`, text, not required): This field allows you to select one option from a list.
* **Select Multiple 3** (`select_multiple_3`, text, not required): This is another select multiple field where you can choose multiple options.
* **Decimal 3** (`decimal_3`, text, not required): Enter another decimal value.
* **Integer 3** (`integer_3`, text, not required): Enter another integer value.
* **Date 3** (`date_3`, text, not required): Enter the date for another training session.
* **Time 3** (`time_3`, text, not required): Enter the time for another training session.
* **Note 3** (`note_3`, text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
* **Decimal 4** (`decimal_4`, text, not required): Enter another decimal value.
* **Integer 4** (`integer_4`, text, not required): Enter another integer value.
* **Date 4** (`date_4`, text, not required): Enter the date for another training session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can process your registration.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask our team for assistance.
* We'll be in touch to confirm your registration and provide further instructions.
