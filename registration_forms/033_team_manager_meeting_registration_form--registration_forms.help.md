# Team Manager Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from team members for team manager meetings. It helps team members to express their interest in attending and provide their preferences for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields, if any.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
3. Submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Manager Meeting Registration**: This field is the title of the meeting. Type a brief description of the meeting, and it will appear on the calendar for reference.
* **Name**: Enter your name, as you would like it to appear on the meeting calendar.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and meeting reminders.
* **Team**: Select your team from the list, if applicable.
* **Date**: Choose the date of the meeting.
* **Time**: Choose the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Topic**: Briefly describe the meeting topic.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Agenda**: Write down any additional notes or topics you would like to discuss during the meeting.
* **RSVP**: Select whether you will be attending the meeting ('True') or not ('False'). If you select 'True', you will be included in the meeting planning.
* **Scheduling Preference 1** and **Scheduling Preference 2**: You have the option to select your scheduling preferences for future meetings. These preferences will be taken into account for future meeting planning. Select 'Yes' if you prefer to be scheduled at a certain time or location, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
- If you have already registered for this meeting, do not submit another registration.
- Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
- Review the meeting calendar for any updates or changes.
- Contact the team manager or meeting organizer if you have any questions or concerns.
