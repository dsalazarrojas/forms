# Online Personal Branding Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants for an online personal branding workshop. Please fill out the form to book your spot and provide your contact details to receive further instructions and updates about the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have a valid email address to provide your contact details.
2. Fill out the form with your name, contact information, and workshop details.
3. Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required/optional: false): Enter the date you would like to attend the online personal branding workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required/optional: false): Provide the location of the workshop, which can be your office address or a specific online platform you'll be using.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to during the workshop.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional: false): Provide any additional contact information that the workshop organizers may need to get in touch with you.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional: false): Enter a valid email address where you can receive updates and instructions about the workshop.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any last-minute updates.
* **Participant Email (workshop)** (`participant_email_workshop`, email, required/optional: false): Enter a separate email address that the workshop organizers will use to send you workshop-related updates.
* **Workshop Start Time** (`workshop_start_time`, time, required/optional: false): Enter the start time of the workshop you are booking.
* **Workshop End Time** (`workshop_end_time`, time, required/optional: false): Enter the end time of the workshop you are booking.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your email addresses and phone number for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any information, feel free to contact the workshop organizers.
- If you're having trouble filling out the form, don't hesitate to ask for help.
 
Please note that the form is designed to be optional, except for the email address for communication purposes. The required fields are for workshop details and participant information. The form is a general guide and can be adjusted based on specific requirements.
