# Massage Therapy Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Therapy Client Intake Form is a necessary step in the massage therapy process. It helps us understand your personal details, medical history, and preferences to tailor the massage sessions to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select your options as needed. Make sure to be accurate and truthful with your responses, as they will be reviewed by medical professionals before your first appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Your given name, e.g., John Doe.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Your surname, e.g., Doe.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): The email address you can be reached at.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your phone number for contacting you.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Your address for sending notifications or emergency services.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): The date of your birth.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, optional): A brief description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, surgeries, or injuries.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): Any medications you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `text`, optional): A person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, optional): The phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): Your relationship to the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Medical Providers** (`medical_providers`, `text`, optional): Your current medical providers (e.g., doctor, specialist).
* **Massage Frequency** (`massage_frequency`, `text`, optional): The frequency of your regular massages.
* **Massage History** (`massage_history`, `note`, optional): A brief description of your massage history, including any previous massage therapists or experiences.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): Any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical History Comments** (`medical_history_comments`, `text`, optional): Additional comments about your medical history.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you require emergency services, select "Yes" for each service you need.
* **Emergency Contacts Services** (`emergency_contacts_services`, `select_one`, required): If you need emergency contact services, select "Yes" or "Yes with Medical Information".
* **Therapist** (`therapist`, `text`, optional): The name of your preferred massage therapist.
* **Massage Duration** (`massage_duration`, `text`, optional): The length of your desired massage sessions.
* **Preferred Massage Area** (`preferred_massage_area`, `select_one`, optional): Your preferred massage area (e.g., Head and neck, Shoulders).
* **Massage Intensity** (`massage_intensity`, `select_one`, optional): Your preferred massage intensity (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Medical History Additional Information** (`medical_history_additional`, `text`, optional): Any additional information about your medical history.
* **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_signature`, `text`, optional): The signature of your assigned massage therapist.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Your signature confirming your consent and agreement with the form.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): The date of your intake form completion.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact our team.
- This form is for your safety and comfort, so please be honest and complete it thoroughly.
- Your information will be reviewed by medical professionals before your first appointment.
