# Africa Week Business Networking Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business information and preferences to ensure the best possible experience for attendees at the Africa Week Business Networking event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your full name, professional title, and email address.
2. Enter your company or organization's name and address.
3. Choose your country of origin and business sector.
4. Describe your business and its size.
5. Identify your networking goals and specific interests.
6. Choose how you plan to attend the event (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
7. Indicate any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
8. Specify your experience with doing business in Africa.
9. Select the countries you are most interested in for business.
10. Describe how you plan to contribute to Africa's development through your business.
11. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
12. Verify that all information provided is accurate.
13. Provide an electronic signature to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Professional Title** (Required): Enter your current job title or professional designation.
* **Company or Organization** (Required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter a professional email address for event communications.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your professional phone number for contact.

### Business Information

* **Country of Origin** (Required): Choose the African country you represent or have connections with.
* **Business Sector** (Required): Choose the sector your business operates in.
* **Business Description** (Required): Briefly describe your business or organization.
* **Years in Business** (Required): Choose how many years you have been in business.
* **Business Size** (Required): Choose the number of employees in your business.
* **Africa Business Experience** (Required): Choose your experience with doing business in Africa.
* **Countries of Interest** (Required): Choose the countries you are most interested in for business.

### Networking Goals

* **Networking Objectives** (Required): Choose your primary objectives for attending the event.
* **Specific Interests** (Required): Describe your specific business interests or opportunities.
* **Expected Outcomes** (Required): Describe the outcomes you hope to achieve from this networking opportunity.

### Event Participation

* **Attendance Mode** (Required): Choose how you plan to attend the event (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Optional): Indicate any dietary restrictions we should be aware of.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Required): Indicate if you have any accessibility requirements.
* **Accessibility Details** (Optional): If yes, specify your accessibility requirements.
* **Special Requests** (Optional): Describe any special requests or considerations for the event.

### Registration

* **Registration Confirmation** (Required): Confirm your registration details.
* **Terms Agreement** (Required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Information Accuracy** (Required): Confirm that all information provided is accurate.
* **Electronic Signature** (Required): Provide an electronic signature to confirm your registration.

Note: The asterisk symbol (*) denotes a required field. Please fill out all required fields to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
