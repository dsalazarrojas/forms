# Volunteer Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteer Communication Form" is designed to collect information from volunteers about their contact details, event participation, and communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information section with your details.
2. Choose your role and team for the event (if applicable).
3. Select your shift start and end times.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
5. Confirm your relationship with the person you'll be contacting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This section asks for your contact details such as your name, email address, phone number, and address.
* **Name**: Enter your name to help us identify you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you quickly.
* **Address**: Enter your address to help us send you important information about the event.
* **Event Name**: If you're attending an event, enter the name of the event so we know which one you'll be participating in.
* **Volunteer Role**: Choose your role in the event (Volunteer Leader, Team Member, Participant, or Other).
* **Team Name**: If you're part of a team, enter your team name.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the time you'll start your shift.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the time you'll end your shift.
* **Shift Duration**: Enter the duration of your shift.
* **Volunteer Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **How do you know the person?**: Choose how you know the person you'll be contacting (Team, Supervisor, or Other).
* **Supervisor**: Confirm if you have a supervisor (Yes/No).
* **Supervisor Name**: If you have a supervisor, enter their name.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can contact you.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the "Volunteer Communication Form" with ease and confidence.
