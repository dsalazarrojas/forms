# Global Pandemic Preparedness Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the purposes of the Global Pandemic Preparedness Forum. This information will be used to create a profile for each user and to facilitate interaction within the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the First Name field with your full first name.
2. Fill in the Last Name field with your full last name.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field. Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. Fill in the Phone field with your contact phone number.
5. Select one option from the Select One field.
6. Select multiple options from the Select Multiple field. You can select more than one option.
7. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
8. Enter your Time of Birth in the Time of Birth field in HH:MM format. If you are unsure about your Time of Birth, leave this field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Select One**: Choose one option from the list. If none of the options are correct, please contact the relevant authorities or your supervisor for clarification.
* **Select Multiple**: Select multiple options from the list. You can select more than one option.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your Date of Birth in YYYY-MM-DD format. If you are unsure, leave this field blank.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your Time of Birth in HH:MM format. If you are unsure, leave this field blank.
