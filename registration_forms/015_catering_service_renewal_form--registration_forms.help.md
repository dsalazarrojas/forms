# Catering Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Service Renewal Form is designed to collect information about your current services, preferences, and contact details. This form helps our team understand your needs and provide the best possible catering experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form in a step-by-step manner, starting from the top of the form.
3. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Number** (`account_number`, `text`, required): Enter your unique account number associated with your current catering service.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your account.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your active email address.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your active phone number.
* **Current Service Plan** (`current_plan`, `text`, optional): Describe your current service plan in detail, if applicable.
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the start date of your current service.
* **Current Service Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the current service expiration date.
* **Satisfaction with Current Services** (`satisfaction_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of satisfaction with our current services.
* **Do you wish to renew your service?** (`renewal_decision`, `select_one`, required): Choose to renew or not renew your service.
* **Preferred Renewal Term** (`renewal_term`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred renewal term (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years).
* **Would you like to modify your service plan?** (`plan_modification`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you want to modify your service plan.
* **Describe Desired Modifications** (`desired_modifications`, `text`, optional): If modifying your service plan, describe the desired changes.
* **Is billing contact the same as above?** (`billing_contact_same`, `select_one`, required): If the billing contact is the same as the account holder, select 'Yes'.
* **Billing Contact Name** (`billing_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the billing contact if different from the account holder.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the full address for the billing contact.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, ACH, PayPal, Same as before).
* **Enable Auto-Renewal for Future Years?** (`auto_renewal_option`, `select_one`, optional): Choose to enable auto-renewal for future years.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests or notes for our team.
* **If not renewing, please explain** (`reason_for_non_renewal`, `text`, optional): If not renewing, provide feedback for our improvement.
* **I confirm the information provided is accurate** (`renewal_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
