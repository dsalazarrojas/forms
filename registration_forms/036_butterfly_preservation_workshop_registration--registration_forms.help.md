# Butterfly Preservation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Butterfly Preservation Workshop Registration form is designed to collect essential information from participants to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees. This guide will walk you through each field of the form, explaining its purpose and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: Start by filling in your Participant Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Choose your Age Group: Select your age category from the provided options.
3. Indicate your Prior Butterfly Knowledge: Choose how much you know about butterflies.
4. Select your Workshop Interests: Choose all the aspects of butterfly preservation that interest you.
5. Choose your Preferred Workshop Date: Select a date for the workshop that suits you best.
6. Choose your Preferred Workshop Time: Select a time slot for the workshop that suits you best.
7. Select your Preferred Workshop Duration: Choose how long you would like to attend the workshop.
8. Specify your Location Preference: If you have a preference for indoor or outdoor sessions, or both.
9. Mention any Accessibility Needs: If you have any special requirements, please mention them.
10. Indicate if you will attend as part of a Group: Choose if you will be attending with others.
11. If Group, specify the Number of People: If you are attending with others, specify the total number of people.
12. Motivate your Interest in the Workshop: Briefly mention why you are interested in the workshop.
13. Indicate Prior Workshops Attendance: If you have attended nature or science workshops before, choose accordingly.
14. Specify your Workshop Goals: Describe what you hope to learn from the workshop.
15. Express your Willingness to Contribute: Choose how much you are willing to contribute to butterfly conservation.
16. Confirm your Registration: Finally, confirm your understanding of the workshop details and your registration.
