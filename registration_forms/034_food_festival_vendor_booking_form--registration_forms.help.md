# Food Festival Vendor Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you register as a vendor for the food festival. Please fill out the form with your details to ensure that you can participate in the festival.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your user information.
2. Enter your event information.
3. Provide product information.
4. Enter your contact details.
5. Add your vendor information.
6. Read and agree to our terms and conditions.
7. Provide your payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information

* **User Information** (page 1): This is where you will enter your personal details.
  * Name: Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
  * Email: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
  * Phone: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.

### Event Information

* **Event Information** (page 2): This is where you will enter details about the event you are participating in.
  * Event Name: Enter the name of the event you will be participating in.
  * Event Date: Enter the date of the event.
  * Event Time: Enter the time of the event.

### Product Information

* **Product Information** (page 3): This is where you will enter details about your product.
  * Product Name: Enter the name of your product.
  * Product Description: Enter a brief description of your product.

### Contact Details

* **Contact Details** (page 4): This is where you will enter your contact details.
  * Contact Person: Enter the name of the person who will be handling your contact.
  * Contact Email: Enter the email address that we can use to contact you.
  * Contact Phone: Enter the phone number that we can use to contact you.

### Vendor Information

* **Vendor Information** (page 5): This is where you will enter your vendor information.
  * Vendor Name: Enter your business name.
  * Vendor Address: Enter your business address.
  * Vendor Phone: Enter your business phone number.

### Terms and Conditions

* **Terms and Conditions** (page 6): This is where you will read and agree to our terms and conditions.
  * Please read the terms carefully and agree to them by selecting the correct option.

### Agreement

* **Agreement** (page 7): This is where you will select one of the agreement options.
  * Please select one of the options to agree to the terms and conditions.

### Payment Information

* **Payment Information** (page 8): This is where you will enter your payment information.
  * Please enter your payment information.

## Tips

* Please fill out all required fields.
* Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
