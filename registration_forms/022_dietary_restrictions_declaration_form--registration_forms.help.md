# Dietary Restrictions Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dietary Restrictions Declaration Form is a tool to inform event staff about your dietary needs, ensuring that you receive suitable food and drink options during the event. This form helps us to provide a comfortable and inclusive experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Participant Information section with your name and any other relevant details.
2.  Choose your dietary restrictions from the options provided in the Dietary Restrictions section.
3.  If you have any additional dietary restrictions not listed, please specify them in the Other Dietary Restrictions section.
4.  Provide your Email and Phone numbers in the Email and Phone sections, respectively, so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Information**
    (participant_information): 
    This section is used to collect your participant information, including name and other relevant details. 
*   **Dietary Restrictions**
    (dietary_restrictions, select_multiple):
    This section provides a list of common dietary restrictions. Please select all that apply to you. You can choose multiple options. 
    Please note that some options might not be listed; if that is the case, please use the **Other** option to specify your restrictions.
*   **Any other dietary restrictions?**
    (note, text): 
    If you have any additional restrictions not listed, please specify them here. This field is optional.
*   **Email**
    (email, email):
    Please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Phone**
    (phone, text):
    Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

*   Please double-check the options selected to ensure accuracy.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer back to the form description above.
*   This form is designed to be used for event and program registrations. If you need to declare your dietary restrictions for a different purpose, please use the appropriate form for that purpose.
