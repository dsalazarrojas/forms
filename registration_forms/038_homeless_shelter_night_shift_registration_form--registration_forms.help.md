# Homeless Shelter Night Shift Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeless Shelter Night Shift Registration Form is designed to collect information about your availability and duties during your assigned shifts. This form is used for internal purposes by homeless shelter staff to manage shift schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the form link or open the form in your browser.
* Scroll through each page of the form, which is divided into several sections.
* Fill out each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form (<name>, <type>, optional)**: This is the main registration section. Enter any necessary information you'd like to include.
* **shift_date (<name>, <type>, optional)**: Select the date of your shift. This is the date you will be working.
* **shift_time (<name>, <type>, optional)**: Select the time of your shift. This is the time you will be working.
* **shift_duty (<name>, <type, optional, select_one>)**: Choose the type of duty you will be performing during your shift. You can choose from Volunteer, Staff, or Both.
* **volunteer_name (<name>, <type, optional)**: If you are a volunteer, enter your name here.
* **contact_number (<name>, <type, optional)**: Enter your contact number, if you'd like to be reached about your shift.
* **shift_start (<name>, <type, optional)**: Select the date and start time of your shift.
* **shift_end (<name>, <type, optional)**: Select the date and end time of your shift.
* **note (<name>, <type, optional>)**: Add any necessary notes about your shift.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with a supervisor or manager.
* Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
