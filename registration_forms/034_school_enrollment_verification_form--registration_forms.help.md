# School Enrollment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Enrollment Verification Form is used to verify student enrollment status in schools. This form helps gather basic information about students and parents to facilitate administrative and educational processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Provide the student's ID number in the "Student ID Number" field.
3.  Enter the student's date of birth in the "Student Date of Birth" field. 
4.  Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
5.  Provide the parent's ID number in the "Parent ID Number" field.
6.  Enter the student's attendance date in the "Student Attendance Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student.
*   **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, text, optional): Enter the student's ID number.
*   **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YY).
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name.
*   **Parent ID Number** (`parent_id_number`, text, optional): Enter the parent's ID number.
*   **Student Attendance Date** (`student_attendance_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the student's attendance.

## Tips
*   Please enter accurate and clear information in each field to ensure proper verification.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the relevant school authorities for assistance.
*   If you are unable to complete this form, please refer to the provided help guide for further assistance. 

Note: This help guide is a general explanation and may not cover all possible scenarios or specific requirements of your institution.
