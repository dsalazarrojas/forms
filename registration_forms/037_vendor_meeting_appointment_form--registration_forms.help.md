# Vendor Meeting Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Meeting Appointment Form is designed to schedule appointments with vendors for internal meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the meeting date in the date field. This is the date when the meeting will take place.
2. Enter the meeting time in the time field. This is the time of the meeting.
3. Enter the vendor's name in the vendor name field. This should be the name of the company or individual you are meeting with.
4. Provide a brief description of the meeting's purpose in the meeting purpose field. This helps to understand the context and scope of the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Date
* **Meeting Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the meeting in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/15/20).

### Meeting Time
* **Meeting Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the meeting in the format HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 02:00 PM).

### Vendor Name
* **Vendor Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the vendor or individual you are meeting with (e.g., John Doe Inc.).

### Meeting Purpose
* **Meeting Purpose** (text, required): Briefly describe the purpose of the meeting (e.g., Discuss Project Proposal).

### Meeting Location
* **Meeting Location** (text, optional): If the meeting location is not specified, you can leave this field blank or provide an approximate location.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your input before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you need to schedule multiple meetings with the same vendor, you can fill out this form for each meeting separately.
