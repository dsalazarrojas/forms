# Gaming Community Member Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for new members of the gaming community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. You can skip any fields that are not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name. This will be used to address you in community communications.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for important community announcements and updates.
* **Username** (`Username`, `text`, required/optional): Choose a unique username that will identify you in the community.
* **Role** (`Role`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your role within the community. Options are Member, Moderator, or Admin.
* **Discord Tag** (`Discord Tag`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your Discord tag, if you have one.
* **Discord ID** (`Discord ID`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your Discord ID, if you have one.
* **Gaming Group** (`Gaming Group`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the gaming group you are interested in joining.
* **Accept Terms** (`Accept Terms`, `note`, required/optional): Check this box to accept the community's terms of service.
* **Accept Privacy** (`Accept Privacy`, `note`, required/optional): Check this box to accept the community's privacy policy.
* **Terms of Service** (`Terms of Service`, `note`, required/optional): Read and understand the community's terms of service.
* **Privacy Policy** (`Privacy Policy`, `note`, required/optional): Read and understand the community's privacy policy.
* **Member Of** (`Member Of`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the boxes of the member(s) you are a member of.
* **Other Member Of** (`Other Member Of`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any other member(s) you are a member of.
* **Member Count** (`Member Count`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of members you are a member of.
* **Joined Date** (`Joined Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you joined the community.
* **Joined Time** (`Joined Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you joined the community.
* **Joined At** (`Joined At`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time you joined the community.
* **Last Seen** (`Last Seen`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you last joined the community.
* **Last Seen At** (`Last Seen At`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time you last joined the community.
* **Timezone** (`Timezone`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your timezone.
* **Country** (`Country`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your country of residence.
* **Language** (`Language`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your native language.
* **Age** (`Age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Bio** (`Bio`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief bio about yourself.
* **Created At** (`Created At`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date your account was created.

Note: Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
