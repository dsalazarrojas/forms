# Accounting Data Management Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to register new clients with the Accounting Data Management (ADM) service. It collects essential information to provide personalized services and manage your financial data effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring you provide accurate information for each field.
2. Enter your primary contact's name and contact email address.
3. Specify your organization's name and type (e.g., Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship, or Non-Profit).
4. Enter your tax ID number (EIN or SSN).
5. Select the services you require from our range of services (e.g., Bookkeeping, Tax Preparation, Payroll, Financial Consulting, Auditing).
6. Estimate your annual revenue (optional).
7. Specify the current accounting software you are using (e.g., QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, FreshBooks, Excel).
8. List the individuals authorized to access your financial data.
9. Enter the name and title of the authorized representatives.
10. Choose a registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Contact Name** (Primary Contact Name, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Organization Name** (Organization Name, text, required): Enter the full legal name of your organization.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter the email address of your primary contact.
* **Entity Type** (Entity Type, select_one, required): Select your organization's legal structure (e.g., Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship, Non-Profit).
* **Tax ID Number** (Tax ID Number, text, required): Enter your EIN or SSN.
* **Services Requested** (Services Requested, select_multiple, required): Select all the services you require from our range of services (e.g., Bookkeeping, Tax Preparation, Payroll, Financial Consulting, Auditing).
* **Estimated Annual Revenue** (Estimated Annual Revenue, number, optional): Enter an estimate of your annual revenue for service tiering purposes.
* **Current Accounting Software** (Current Accounting Software, select_one, required): Specify the current accounting software you are using (e.g., QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, FreshBooks, Excel).
* **Authorized Representative 1** (Authorized Representative 1, text, required): Enter the name and title of the authorized representative.
* **Authorized Representative 2** (Authorized Representative 2, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the optional second contact.
* **Registration Date** (Registration Date, date, required): Choose the date of your registration.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately.
* If you need assistance or have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll review and process your registration promptly upon form submission.
