# Trade Certification Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for trade certification enrollment. Please use it to provide your details accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Applicant Name** in the text field at the top of the page.
2. Choose the **Program** you are interested in from the dropdown list.
3. If applicable, select the **Application Status** you are currently in from the dropdown list.
4. If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the **Comments** field.
5. Fill in your **Contact Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Address** if applicable.
6. If you're a representative of a **Trade Provider**, please provide their name in the **Trade Provider** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (Text, Optional): This is where you'll enter your name.
* **Select Program** (Select One, Optional): Choose the program you're interested in from the list provided.
* **Application Date** (Date, Optional): If you're currently applying, this is where you'll enter the date of your application.
* **Application Status** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the status of your application from the list provided. If you're not sure, just leave it blank.
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): Any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Contact Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person to contact, if applicable.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address, if applicable.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address, if applicable.
* **Trade Provider** (Text, Optional): If you're a representative of a trade provider, enter their name here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields, if applicable.
* If you're unsure about any field, just leave it blank and we'll get back to you.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
