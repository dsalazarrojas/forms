# 5K Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The 5K Sign Up Form is designed for registering participants in a 5K race. Please complete this form to provide your personal information, select your preferred shirt size, choose your participation type, and agree to the waiver and safety instructions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, following the instructions for each field.
2. Make sure to enter your correct contact information to receive updates and messages from the organizers.
3. Select your preferred shirt size from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose your participation type: Competitive 5K, Recreational 5K, or Virtual run.
5. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them in the "Allergies or medications" field.
6. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
7. Accept or decline the waiver and safety instructions by selecting the corresponding option.
8. If you'd like to add a donation to the event charity partner, choose one of the available options.
9. Finally, enter any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text): Enter your full name as you'd like it to appear on results. (Required)
* **Date of birth** (date): Required for age categories.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for registration confirmation and updates. (Required)
* **Phone** (phone): Optional but recommended for race-day messages. (Optional)
* **Shirt size** (select_one): Choose your preferred shirt size: S, M, L, or XL. (Required)
* **Category** (select_one): Select your participation type: Competitive 5K, Recreational 5K, or Virtual run. (Required)
* **Allergies or medications** (text): List any medical conditions or allergies that the organizers should be aware of. (Optional)
* **Emergency contact (Name — Phone)** (text): Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact. (Required)
* **I accept the race waiver and safety instructions** (select_one): Accept or decline the waiver and safety instructions. (Required)
* **Would you like to add a donation to your registration?** (select_one): Choose to add $5 or $10 to the event charity partner. (Optional)
* **Additional comments or requests** (text): Enter any additional comments or requests you may have. (Optional)

Note: The form is designed to be filled out by potential participants of a 5K race. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
