# investment_compliance_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for an investment compliance workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Select the workshop dates you wish to attend from the "Workshop Dates" field.
4. Choose the time slot you prefer from the "Workshop Time" field.
5. Indicate your participant status by selecting either "Participant" or "Non-Participant" from the "Participant Status" field.
6. Select the workshop type you are interested in from the "Workshop Type" field.
7. Choose the focus area of the workshop you are interested in from the "Workshop Focus Area" field.
8. Select a specific topic of interest from the "Workshop Topic" field.
9. Provide your company name in the "Participant Company" field.
10. Enter your title in the "Participant Title" field.
11. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
12. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
13. Enter your extension in the "Extension" field.
14. Enter your fax number in the "Fax" field.
15. Enter your street address in the "Street" field.
16. Enter your city in the "City" field.
17. Enter your state or province in the "State/Province" field.
18. Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
19. Enter your country in the "Country" field.
20. Enter the workshop location in the "Workshop Location" field.
21. Select the date you will be attending the workshop from the "Workshop Date" field.
22. Select the type of workshop attendance (in-person or virtual) from the "Workshop Attendance Type" field.
23. If applicable, provide additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.
24. If applicable, provide any additional information in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name to register for the workshop.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to enable communication with you.
* **Workshop Dates** (`participant_workshop_attendance_date`, select_one, required): Select the dates you wish to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`participant_workshop_attendance_time`, select_one, required): Choose the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_participation_status`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your participant status by selecting "Participant" or "Non-Participant".
* **Workshop Type** (`participant_workshop_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Focus Area** (`participant_workshop_focus_area`, select_multiple, required): Choose the focus area of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Topic** (`participant_workshop_topic`, select_one, required): Select a specific topic of interest.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, text, required): Enter your title.
* **Department** (`participant_department`, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Contact Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Extension** (`participant_extension`, text, required): Enter your extension.
* **Fax** (`participant_fax`, text, required): Enter your fax number.
* **Street** (`participant_street`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`participant_city`, text, required): Enter your city name.
* **State/Province** (`participant_state_province`, text, required): Enter your state or province name.
* **Postal Code** (`participant_postal_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`participant_country`, text, required): Enter your country name.
* **Workshop Location** (`participant_workshop_location`, text, required): Enter the workshop location.
* **Workshop Date** (`participant_workshop_date`, date, required): Select the date you will be attending the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`participant_workshop_time`, time, required): Select the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
* **Workshop Attendance Type** (`participant_workshop_attendance_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of workshop attendance (in-person or virtual).
* **Additional Comments** (`participant_additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information if applicable.
* **Additional Information** (`participant_additional_info`, note, optional): Provide any additional information if applicable.
