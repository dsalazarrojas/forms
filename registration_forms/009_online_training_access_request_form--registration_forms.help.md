# online_training_access_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The online training access request form is designed for users to request access to online training resources within our organization. This form will help us to ensure that you receive the correct training resources to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This must be in the format of [name@domain.com](mailto:name@domain.com).
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your chosen training program from the "Training Program" dropdown menu.
6. If applicable, select your desired training date in the "Training Date" field.
7. If applicable, select your preferred training time in the "Training Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address in this field. Please format it as [name@domain.com](mailto:name@domain.com).
* **Phone** (text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Training Program** (select_one, optional): Select one of the training programs offered.
* **Training Date** (date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the training, select it here. Note: This field only appears if you select a training program.
* **Training Time** (time, optional): If you have a preferred training time, select it here. Note: This field only appears if you select a training program and a training date.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure the correct training resources are provided. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
