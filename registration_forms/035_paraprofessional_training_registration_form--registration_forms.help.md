# Paraprofessional Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Paraprofessional Training Registration Form is designed to collect information from professionals to register for training sessions. This form helps to streamline the training process by gathering essential details in one place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field, which will be used to contact you about your registration.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field, which will be used to contact you in case we need to reach you.
5. Select the training type that you are interested in from the Training Type dropdown list.
6. Choose the date you would like to start the training from the Start Date field.
7. Choose the date you would like to end the training from the End Date field.
8. Select the time you would like to start the training from the Start Time field.
9. Select the time you would like to end the training from the End Time field.
10. Choose your preferred language from the Preferred Language dropdown list.
11. Select the payment method you would like to use from the Payment Method dropdown list.
12. Enter the date you would like to make the payment in the Payment Date field.
13. Add any additional comments or notes about your registration in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your registration.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Training Type** (Training Type, text, required/optional): Select the type of training you are interested in (e.g., course, workshop, conference).
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to start the training.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to end the training.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, required/optional): Select the time you would like to start the training.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, required/optional): Select the time you would like to end the training.
* **Preferred Language** (Preferred Language, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred language for the training (e.g., English, French, Spanish).
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the payment method you would like to use (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check).
* **Payment Date** (Payment Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to make the payment.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
