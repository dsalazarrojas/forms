# Speech Contest Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speech Contest Enrollment Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants to enroll in a speech contest. It helps the organizers to gather details about the participant and their entry in the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to enable communication with the organizers.
3. Provide your Phone number for easy contact.
4. Choose your Speaker Type from the provided options.
5. Select your Category from the given options.
6. If you are a school participant, fill in your School Name and School ID.
7. Optionally, fill in the Contest Date if you know it already.
8. Select your Entry Type from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please fill in your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please fill in your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please provide a valid phone number for easy contact.
- **Speaker Type** (`speaker_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the correct speaker type from the options.
  - Option 1: Participant
  - Option 2: Judge
  - Option 3: Other
- **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the relevant category for your entry.
  - Category 1: Option 1
  - Category 2: Option 2
  - Category 3: Option 3
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Please enter your school name if you are a school participant.
- **School ID** (`school_id`, number, optional): Please enter your school ID if you are a school participant.
- **Contest Date** (`contest_date`, date, optional): If you know the contest date, please fill it in for easy reference.
- **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of entry for your presentation.
  - Entry 1: Option 1
  - Entry 2: Option 2
  - Entry 3: Option 3
