# Sustainable Ingredient Development Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to register their interest in sustainable ingredients. Users can submit information about themselves, including their first name, last name, email, ingredient name, ingredient type, category, evaluation date, evaluation time, rating, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Enter your Information
- Please fill out the form with your first name, last name, and email address.
- Select the type of ingredient you are interested in (e.g., Vegetable, Fruit, etc.).
- Choose the category for the ingredient (e.g., Sustainable, Non-Sustainable, etc.).
- Optionally, enter the evaluation date and time.
- Enter your rating for the ingredient.
- Add any comments about the ingredient.
- Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

### Page 1: User Info
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.

* **Ingredient Name**: Enter the name of the ingredient you are interested in.

* **Ingredient Category**: Choose a category for the ingredient (e.g., Sustainable, Non-Sustainable, etc.).
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of your evaluation (not required).
* **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of your evaluation (not required).
