# Music School Enrollment Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians for their children who wish to enroll in music lessons at the music school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and contact information on the first page.
3. Select the programs and instruments that interest your child on the second page.
4. Choose the lesson length and frequency for your child's lessons.
5. Enter the start and end dates for your child's lessons.
6. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, required): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`form_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Parent Name** (`form_parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone** (`form_parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Programs** (`form_programs`, select multiple, optional): Select the music programs your child is interested in (e.g. Music Lessons, Instrument Lessons, Theory Lessons, Practice Room).
* **Instruments** (`form_instruments`, select multiple, optional): Select the instruments your child is interested in learning (e.g. Piano, Guitar, Violin, Drums).
* **Lesson Length** (`form_lesson_length`, select one, optional): Choose the length of each lesson (e.g. 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 60 minutes).
* **Lesson Frequency** (`form_lesson_frequency`, select one, optional): Choose how often your child will have lessons (e.g. once a week, twice a week, three times a week).
* **Start Date** (`form_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date for your child's lessons.
* **End Date** (`form_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date for your child's lessons.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure your child's lessons are scheduled correctly.
* Select programs and instruments that your child is truly interested in to make the most of their lessons.
* Choose lesson length and frequency that suits your child's schedule and availability.
* Enter start and end dates that fit your child's lesson plan.
* If any fields are not applicable or not relevant to your child, do not complete them.
