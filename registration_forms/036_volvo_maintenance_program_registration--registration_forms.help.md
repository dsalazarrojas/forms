# Volvo Maintenance Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volvo Maintenance Program Registration form is designed to help customers enroll their vehicles in the program. This form is used to collect information about the vehicle, the service center, and the contact person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Vehicle Details" section with the required information about your vehicle.
2.  Select the service center where you want to perform the maintenance.
3.  Provide your contact information, including your name and email address, in the "Contact Info" section.
4.  In the "Submission Details" section, provide any additional details about your submission.
5.  In the "Route Request" section, select the type of route you want to take (e.g., None, Other).
6.  In the "Route Details" section, provide any additional details about your route.
7.  Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Details (Text)**: This is the first section of the form where you need to provide details about the vehicle you want to register for the maintenance program. This includes the vehicle make, model, year, and any other relevant information.
*   **Service Center (Select One)**: Select the service center where you want to perform the maintenance. You can choose from the options provided or select "Other" if your service center is not listed.
*   **Contact Info (Text)**: This is where you provide your contact information, including your name and email address, so we can reach out to you about your maintenance program.
*   **Submission Details (Text)**: In this section, you can provide any additional details about your submission, such as the reason for registering your vehicle or any specific requirements you have.
*   **Route Request (Select Multiple)**: Select the type of route you want to take for your maintenance. You can choose from the options provided or select "Other" if you have a specific route in mind.
*   **Route Details (Text)**: In this section, you can provide any additional details about your route, such as the specific route you want to take or any specific requirements you have.
*   **Notes (Note)**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your registration.
*   **Route (Select Multiple)**: This field is not applicable, as it is not present in the form.
