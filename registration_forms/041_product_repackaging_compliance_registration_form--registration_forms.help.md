# Product Repackaging Compliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Repackaging Compliance Registration Form is used to submit information about the repackaging of products. This form helps us to ensure quality assurance and compliance with repackaging regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Select the options for the corresponding fields from the dropdown menus.
3. Ensure that you submit all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product ID** (`product_repackaging_form_product_id`): Enter the unique ID of the product being repackaged.
* **product_id**: Enter the product ID, if applicable.
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`): Enter your company name or the name of the customer who is submitting this form.
* **repackaging_site** (`repackaging_site`): Select the site where the product was repackaged from the dropdown menu.
* **product_type** (`product_type`): Select the type of product from the dropdown menu.
* **packaging_type** (`packaging_type`): Select the type of packaging used for the product.
* **batch_id** (`batch_id`): Enter the batch ID of the product being repackaged. (Note: This field is not typically required, but may be needed for specific products or circumstances.)
* **production_date** (`production_date`): Enter the date the product was produced.
* **production_time** (`production_time`): Enter the time the product was produced.
* **repackaging_date** (`repackaging_date`): Enter the date the product was repackaged.
* **repackaging_time** (`repackaging_time`): Enter the time the product was repackaged.
* **customer_email** (`customer_email`): Enter your company email address or the email address of the customer who is submitting this form.
* **customer_phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your company phone number or the phone number of the customer who is submitting this form.
* **production_location** (`production_location`): Enter the location where the product was produced.
* **repackaging_location** (`repackaging_location`): Enter the location where the product was repackaged.
* **repackaging_comments** (`repackaging_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the repackaging of the product.
* **customer_comments** (`customer_comments`): Enter any comments from the customer about the repackaging of the product.
* **quality_assurance** (`quality_assurance`): Enter any quality assurance information related to the repackaged product.
* **repackaging_insurance** (`repackaging_insurance`): Enter any repackaging insurance information related to the product.
* **repackaging_warranty** (`repackaging_warranty`): Enter any repackaging warranty information related to the product.
* **packaging_instructions** (`packaging_instructions`): Enter any packaging instructions related to the product.
* **certification_number** (`certification_number`): Enter any certification number related to the product.
* **packaging_inspection** (`packaging_inspection`): Select the type of packaging inspection from the dropdown menu.
* **repackaging_certificate** (`repackaging_certificate`): Enter any repackaging certificate information related to the product.
* **repackaging_status** (`repackaging_status`): Select the status of the repackaged product from the dropdown menu.
* **product_repackaging_status** (`product_repackaging_status`): Select the status of the product repackaging from the dropdown menu.
