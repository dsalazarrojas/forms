# workforce_wellbeing_webinar_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering attendees and their representatives for a webinar on workforce wellbeing. It gathers relevant details for both the attendee and the company representative, including contact information and agreement specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration details section with your information, including email address, event date, start time, end time, and any additional notes.
2. Move to the contact details section and fill out the company representative's information, including email address, phone number, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration form** (`1`, text, required): Enter your name to complete the registration process.
* **Attendee details** (`2`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about yourself, such as your job title or position within the company.
* **Contact details** (`3`, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including your name, role, and company name.
* **Email address** (`6`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Company** (`7`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Job title** (`8`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Phone number** (`9`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company address** (`10`, text, optional): Enter your company address.
* **Event date** (`11`, date, required): Enter the date of the webinar.
* **Event start time** (`12`, time, required): Enter the start time of the webinar.
* **Event end time** (`13`, time, required): Enter the end time of the webinar.
* **Webinar topic** (`14`, text, optional): Enter the topic of the webinar.
* **Speaker** (`15`, text, optional): Enter the name of the speaker.
* **Company representative** (`16`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company representative.
* **Company representative email** (`17`, email, optional): Enter the email of the company representative.
* **Company representative phone number** (`18`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the company representative.
* **Company representative address** (`19`, text, optional): Enter the address of the company representative.
* **Agreement** (`4`, text, optional): This field may be used to enter any agreement or terms and conditions related to the webinar.
* **Agreement text** (`20`, text, optional): Enter any agreement or terms and conditions text.
* **Agreement date** (`21`, date, required): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement time** (`22`, time, required): Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Agreement status** (`23`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the agreement from the options: Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
* **Agreement comments** (`24`, text, optional): Enter any comments related to the agreement.
* **Notes** (`25`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about the webinar registration.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you are a company representative, ensure that you have the authority to fill out this form on behalf of your company.
- Review your form entries carefully before submitting to avoid errors and ensure accurate representation of your company's information.
