# Heritage Site Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for membership at a heritage site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Membership Details section with your information.
2. Enter your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. Select your membership type from the options provided.
4. Enter your email address and confirm it in the Email and Confirm Email fields.
5. Select your payment method and enter the required payment information.
6. Enter the expiration month and year of your payment card.
7. Enter the CVV of your payment card.
8. Confirm your payment card information.
9. Add any additional notes or status comments if desired.
10. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Type** (`Membership Type`, select_one, required): Select your membership type from the options provided.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, select_one, required): Confirm your email address by selecting 'True' from the dropdown list.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your membership (if applicable).
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your membership.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`City`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`State`, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): Select your payment method from the options provided.
* **Payment Card Number** (`Payment Card Number`, text, optional): Enter your payment card number.
* **Expiration Month** (`Expiration Month`, select_one, optional): Select the expiration month of your payment card.
* **Expiration Year** (`Expiration Year`, select_one, optional): Select the expiration year of your payment card.
* **CVV** (`CVV`, text, required): Enter the CVV of your payment card.
* **Payment Card CVV** (`Payment Card CVV`, text, optional): Enter the CVV of your payment card.
* **Confirm Payment Card** (`Confirm Payment Card`, select_one, required): Confirm your payment card information by selecting 'True' from the dropdown list.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Membership Status** (`Membership Status`, select_one, optional): Select your membership status from the options provided.
* **Status Comments** (`Status Comments`, text, optional): Enter any status comments.
* **Submitted by** (`Submitted by`, select_one, required): Select whether you submitted this form or not.
* **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Confirm your email address and payment card information to ensure accuracy.
* Select your membership type and payment method carefully, as these will determine your membership status.
* Enter any additional notes or status comments if you have any questions or concerns about your membership.
