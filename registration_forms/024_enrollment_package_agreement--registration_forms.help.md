# Enrollment Package Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Enrollment Package Agreement form is a mandatory form designed to collect essential details from students for enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student ID number in the **Student ID** field.
2. Provide your full name in the **Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Choose your program of choice from the dropdown options in the **Program** field.
6. Select your date of birth from the calendar in the **Date of Birth** field.
7. Select your date of admission from the calendar in the **Date of Admission** field.
8. Enter your address in the **Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (Student ID, Number, Required): Enter your student ID number assigned to you by your school or institution.
* **Name** (Name, Text, Required): Provide your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during business hours.
* **Program** (Program, Select One, Required): Choose your program of interest from the options provided.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date, Required): Select your date of birth from the calendar.
* **Date of Admission** (Date of Admission, Date, Required): Select your date of admission from the calendar.
* **Address** (Address, Text, Required): Enter your address where you can be reached during non-business hours.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your input information for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your school or institution's support team for assistance.
