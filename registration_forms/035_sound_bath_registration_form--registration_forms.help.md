# Sound Bath Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound Bath Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending a sound bath session. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Select your preferred session time and type from the respective dropdown menus.
3. Choose the attendee type that best describes you.
4. Confirm your email address to ensure we can contact you.
5. Provide any relevant health information, if necessary.
6. Add any additional notes or comments.
7. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your full last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Confirm Email** (select_one, required): Confirm that the email address you provided is correct.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Session Time** (time, optional): Select your preferred session time (10/20/30/40/60 minutes).
* **Health Information** (note, optional): Provide any health information that may affect your participation in the session.
* **Preferred Session Time** (select_one, optional): Select your preferred session time from the available options.
* **Attendee Type** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the categories that best describe you (Sound Bath Practitioner, Wellness Studio, Retreat Organizer, Other).
* **Session Type** (select_one, optional): Select the session type (10/20/30/40/60 minutes).
* **Session Date** (date, optional): Select the date you prefer for the session.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Other** (select_one, optional): Select if your email is not listed above.
* **Other** (select_one, optional): Select your preferred attendee type if not listed above.
