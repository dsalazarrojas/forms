# Interdisciplinary Research Cluster Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interdisciplinary Research Cluster Registration Form is designed to gather information about research clusters, which are groups of researchers from different disciplines working together on a specific research project. This form is used to register and track research clusters, and provide information about their goals, participants, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*). These include Cluster Lead Name, Cluster Lead Email, and Cluster Lead Phone.
2. Provide a brief description of the research cluster, including its goals and expected outcomes.
3. List the research cluster members, including their names, emails, and phone numbers.
4. Indicate whether each member is actively participating in the research cluster by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the research cluster.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cluster Lead Name** (Cluster Lead Name, text, *): Enter the name of the person leading the research cluster.
* **Cluster Lead Email** (Cluster Lead Email, email, *): Enter the email address of the person leading the research cluster.
* **Cluster Lead Phone** (Cluster Lead Phone, text, *): Enter the phone number of the person leading the research cluster.
* **Registration Form** (Registration Form, text, ): Provide a brief description of the research cluster, including its goals and expected outcomes.
* **Cluster Info** (cluster_info, text, ): Enter additional information about the research cluster.
* **Research Participants** (Research Participants, text, ): List the research cluster members, including their names, emails, and phone numbers.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, ): Enter the contact details for the research cluster, including any additional information that might be useful.
* **Research Cluster Goals** (Research Cluster Goals, text, ): Enter the goals of the research cluster.
* **Expected Outcomes** (Expected Outcomes, text, ): Enter the expected outcomes of the research cluster.
* **Research Cluster Description** (Research Cluster Description, text, ): Enter a brief description of the research cluster.
* **Research Cluster Duration** (Research Cluster Duration, number, ): Enter the duration of the research cluster (e.g., months, years, etc.).
* **Research Cluster Members** (Research Cluster Members, select_multiple, ): Indicate whether each research cluster member is actively participating in the research cluster by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Research Cluster Member 1-10** (Research Cluster Member 1-10, select_multiple, ): Indicate whether each research cluster member is actively participating in the research cluster by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Research Cluster Notes** (Research Cluster Notes, note, ): Enter any additional notes or comments about the research cluster.

Note: The research cluster members fields (Research Cluster Member 1-10) are identical and repeat the same options. Please select "Yes" or "No" for each member individually.
