# Woodworking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a woodworking event. It is used to collect information from participants about themselves and the event they are registering for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's first name in the "Participant First Name" field.
2. Enter the participant's last name in the "Participant Last Name" field.
3. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant E-mail" field. Make sure to use a valid email address.
4. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field. This is optional.
5. Select a date for the event from the calendar in the "Event Date" field.
6. Choose the time for the event from the time picker in the "Event Time" field.
7. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Location" field.
8. Enter any additional notes about the participant in the "Participant Notes" field. This is optional.
9. Select the participant's payment method from the dropdown list in the "Participant Payment Method" field.
10. If the participant's payment method is not listed, enter an alternative method in the "Participant Payment Method Other" field.
11. Enter the registration fee for the participant in the "Participant Registration Fee" field.
12. Select the registration status of the participant from the dropdown list in the "Participant Registration Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Participant First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Participant Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Participant E-mail**: Enter your email address. A valid email address is required.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is optional.
* **Event Date**: Select a date for the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time**: Choose a time for the event from the time picker.
* **Event Location**: Enter the name of the event location.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the participant. This field is optional.
* **Participant Payment Method**: Select how you plan to pay for the event.
* **Participant Payment Method Other**: If your payment method is not listed, enter an alternative method.
* **Participant Registration Fee**: Enter the registration fee for the participant.
* **Participant Registration Status**: Select the registration status of the participant from the dropdown list.
