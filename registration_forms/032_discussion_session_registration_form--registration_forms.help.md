# Discussion Session Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Discussion Session Registration Form is a tool for participants to register for an upcoming discussion session. It is used to collect necessary information from participants, including their name and email for contact and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Discussion Session Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Registration Form** field with your name, which is a required field to identify yourself as a participant.
2. Fill in your **email** address in the **Event Date** field, which is used to set a specific date for the discussion session. Please enter a valid date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required): Your name as a participant.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address, which will be used for contact and communication purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter a specific date for the discussion session in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are entering a valid email address in the **email** field.
* Make sure to fill in the **Event Date** field with a specific date to ensure you don't miss the session.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
