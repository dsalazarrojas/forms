# intouch_after_school_program_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intouch After School Program registration form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in participating in the Intouch After School Program. This form is used to gather demographic data, program preferences, and contact information from students. By filling out this form, students will be able to register for the program and provide the necessary details for program administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field, which must be a valid email address.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Select your preferred Program Type from the drop-down menu in the "Program Type" field.
6. Enter the start date of your participation in the "Start Date" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter the end date of your participation in the "End Date" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Enter your Contact Number in the "Contact Number" field.
9. Enter your Student ID in the "Student ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of program you are interested in (Afternoon Program, Full Day Program, or Both Programs).
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you would like to start participating in the program in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you would like to end participating in the program in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Please enter a contact number that we can use to reach out to you.
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Please enter your student ID as it appears on your official documentation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please ensure the dates entered for "Start Date" and "End Date" are in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
