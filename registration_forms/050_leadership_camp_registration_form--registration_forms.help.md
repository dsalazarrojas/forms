# Leadership Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Camp Registration Form is a form used to collect contact information and family member details from participants of the leadership camp. This form helps us to get to know you and your family better, allowing us to provide the best experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your personal information: Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the corresponding fields.
2. Add your phone number and address: Enter your phone number and address in the respective fields.
3. Provide your educational background: Tell us about your educational background.
4. Add family member information: If applicable, please add information about your family members by filling out the Family Member fields.
5. Review and submit: Review your filled form, and submit it when you're ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **State**: Enter your state or region.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Educational Background**: Tell us about your educational background, e.g., school, degree, or certification.
* **Family Member 1 First Name**: If applicable, enter the first name of the first family member.
* **Family Member 1 Last Name**: If applicable, enter the last name of the first family member.
* **Family Member 2 First Name**: If applicable, enter the first name of the second family member.
* **Family Member 2 Last Name**: If applicable, enter the last name of the second family member.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Make sure to enter your family member's information correctly.
* If you're having trouble with the form, feel free to ask for help.
