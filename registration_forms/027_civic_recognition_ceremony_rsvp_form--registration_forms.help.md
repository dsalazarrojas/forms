# Civic Recognition Ceremony RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests who have received an invitation to the civic recognition ceremony. Please fill it out to let us know if you will be attending and to provide your personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the ceremony program.
2. **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you would like to receive updates about the ceremony.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, if you would like to receive calls or text messages from us.
4. **You are attending as**: Select how you will be attending the ceremony (e.g., as an award recipient, guest of an award recipient, etc.).
5. **Will you be attending the ceremony?**: Let us know if you will be attending the ceremony or if you are unable to attend.
6. **Number of additional guests (if permitted)**: If you are bringing more guests than permitted, please indicate the number.
7. **Meal Preference**: Choose your preferred meal type (e.g., Standard, Vegetarian, Vegan).
8. **Food Allergies**: If you have severe food allergies, please list them so we can make necessary arrangements.
9. **Accessibility Needs**: If you require any accessibility accommodations, please provide details.
10. **Awards Recognition Mention**: If you are an award recipient, you can specify how you would like your name to appear in the ceremony program.
11. **Photo Release Consent**: Check if you consent to being photographed at the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **You are attending as**: This field helps us understand your relation to the ceremony. Please select one of the options:
	+ An Award Recipient: If you are receiving an award.
	+ A Guest of an Award Recipient: If you are attending on behalf of an award recipient.
	+ Community Partner/Sponsor: If you represent a community partner or sponsor.
	+ Government Representative: If you represent the government.
	+ Volunteer/Staff: If you are a volunteer or staff member.

* **Will you be attending the ceremony?**: Please select:
	+ Yes, I will attend: If you are able to attend.
	+ No, I am unable to attend: If you are not able to attend.

* **Number of additional guests (if permitted)**: If you are bringing more guests than permitted, please enter the number.

* **Meal Preference**: Choose your preferred meal type from the options provided.

* **Food Allergies**: If you have severe food allergies, please provide a list so we can make necessary arrangements.

* **Accessibility Needs**: If you require any accessibility accommodations, please specify them.

* **Awards Recognition Mention**: If you are an award recipient, you can specify how you would like your name to appear in the ceremony program.

* **Photo Release Consent**: Check the box if you consent to being photographed at the ceremony.

* **RSVP Date**: Select the RSVP date you received with your invitation.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
