# officer_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The officer nomination form is used to collect information about a nominee for an officer position in an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* **voter_name**: Enter the name of the person making the nomination.
	* **nominee_name**: Enter the name of the person being nominated.
	* **officer_position**: Select the officer position the nominee is being nominated for.
2. Provide optional information about the nominee:
	* **basis_of_nomination**: Choose the reason for the nomination (Nomination by Board, Employee, or Customer).
	* **qualifications**: Enter any relevant qualifications or achievements of the nominee.
	* **nominee_email**: Enter the nominee's email address.
	* **nominee_phone**: Enter the nominee's phone number.
3. Add any additional comments or information:
	* **voter_email**: Enter your email address as the nominator.
	* **voter_phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **voter_message**: Enter any additional comments or information about the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **voter_name**: Enter the name of the person making the nomination.
* **nominee_name**: Enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **basis_of_nomination**: Choose the reason for the nomination (Nomination by Board, Employee, or Customer).
	+ Nomination by Board: Selected if the board of directors is nominating the nominee.
	+ Nomination by Employee: Selected if an employee is nominating the nominee.
	+ Nomination by Customer: Selected if a customer is nominating the nominee.
* **qualifications**: Enter any relevant qualifications or achievements of the nominee.
* **nominee_email**: Enter the nominee's email address.
* **nominee_phone**: Enter the nominee's phone number.
* **officer_position**: Select the officer position the nominee is being nominated for.
	+ President: Selected if the nominee is being nominated for president.
	+ Vice President: Selected if the nominee is being nominated for vice president.
	+ Secretary: Selected if the nominee is being nominated for secretary.
	+ Treasurer: Selected if the nominee is being nominated for treasurer.
* **officer_post**: Select whether the nominee is available to hold the position.
	+ Yes: Selected if the nominee is available.
	+ No: Selected if the nominee is not available.
* **officer_name**: Enter the name of the person being nominated (only required if officer_position is not selected).
* **voter_email**: Enter your email address as the nominator.
* **voter_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **voter_message**: Enter any additional comments or information about the nomination.
