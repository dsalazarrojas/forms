# Smoothie and Bowl Maker Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to register as a smoothie and bowl maker for a workshop. It will help us understand your personal information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select the number of years of experience you have in the "experience years" field.
6. Enter the location of your workshop in the "workshop location" field.
7. In the "menus" field, provide a brief description of the type of menus you offer.
8. In the "photos" field, provide a brief description of your photos or visuals showcasing your work.
9. Choose your maker type in the "maker type" dropdown menu:
	* Maker
	* Food Truck
	* Brand
	* Restaurant
	* Other (please specify)
10. In the "maker info" field, provide any additional information about yourself as a maker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name, as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name, as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, `number`, required: false): Select the number of years you have experience in making smoothies and bowls.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of your workshop or where you can be found.
* **Menus** (`menus`, `note`, required: false): Describe the types of menus you offer, e.g. vegan, gluten-free, etc.
* **Photos** (`photos`, `note`, required: false): Upload or describe photos of your work or workshop.
* **Maker Type** (`maker_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how you identify as a maker:
	+ Maker
	+ Food Truck
	+ Brand
	+ Restaurant
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Maker Info** (`maker_info`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional information about yourself as a maker. 

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter your information accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
