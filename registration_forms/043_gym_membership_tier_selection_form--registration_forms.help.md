# Gym Membership Tier Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Membership Tier Selection Form is designed to help users select their gym membership tier and provide necessary information for the gym administration to process their membership application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your desired membership tier from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. Choose your plan options (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually) that suits your needs.
4. Select your payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
5. Enter your membership start and end dates.
6. Choose your membership duration (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
7. Provide your email address (if applicable).
8. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
9. Read and sign the acknowledgment section to confirm that you have understood and agree to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Tier Selection (required)
* **Tier Selection**: Select your desired membership tier from the options provided (Bronze, Silver, or Gold).
### Member Name (required)
* **Member Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
### Plan Choices (required)
* **Plan Choices**: Choose your plan options (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually) that suits your needs.
### Membership Start Date (required)
* **Membership Start Date**: Enter the date you want your membership to start.
### Membership End Date (required)
* **Membership End Date**: Enter the date you want your membership to end.
### Membership Duration (required)
* **Membership Duration**: Choose your membership duration (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
### Payment Method (required)
* **Payment Method**: Select your payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
### Email (optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you have one.
### Phone Number (optional)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
### Signed Acknowledgment (required)
* **Signed Acknowledgment**: Read and sign this section to confirm that you have understood and agree to the terms.
