# Business Partners Appreciation Day RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for business partners to RSVP and share their preferences for the appreciation day event. It collects information about attendees, including their names, contact details, partnership duration, attendance status, meal preferences, and other necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the attendee's name and email address.
2. Provide the company name and job title.
3. Select the partnership duration.
4. Choose the attendance status.
5. Select the event date.
6. Enter the number of guests.
7. List the names of additional guests (if any).
8. Select the meal preference.
9. Enter any dietary restrictions (if applicable).
10. Choose the T-shirt size (if applicable).
11. Select the activity preferences.
12. Indicate transportation needs.
13. Indicate parking needs.
14. Specify any accessibility needs.
15. Nominate a partner for recognition (if applicable).
16. Share a testimonial interest.
17. Describe the partnership highlights (if applicable).
18. Share future expectations.
19. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Attendee Name** (Full name as it should appear)
*   The field for the attendee's name is for entering the name of the person who is attending the appreciation day event.
*   Please ensure that the name is accurate and complete.

### **Email Address** (Email for confirmation)
*   Enter your email address so that we can confirm your RSVP.
*   Please ensure that the email is accurate and up-to-date.

### **Phone Number** (Contact phone)
*   Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   This field is not required but provides a convenient contact method.

### **Company Name** (Your organization)
*   Enter the name of the company or organization you are affiliated with.
*   This field is required for registration purposes.

### **Job Title** (Your position)
*   Enter your job title or position.
*   This field is required for registration purposes.

### **Partnership Duration** (How long have you been a partner)
*   Select the duration of your partnership from the provided options.
*   This field helps us understand your partnership history.

### **Attendance Status** (Will you attend)
*   Choose whether you will attend the appreciation day event.
*   Please select the option that best describes your attendance status.

### **Event Date** (Date of appreciation day)
*   Enter the date of the appreciation day event.
*   This field is required for event organization purposes.

### **Number of Guests** (Total attendees including you)
*   Enter the total number of guests attending with you.
*   This field helps us plan for catering and other needs.

### **Guest Names** (Names of additional attendees)
*   Enter the names of any additional guests attending with you.
*   If applicable, please list all guests.

### **Meal Preference** (Select your meal choice)
*   Choose your preferred meal option from the provided choices.
*   Please select the meal that best suits your dietary needs.

### **Dietary Restrictions** (Any allergies or restrictions)
*   If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please enter them here.
*   This field is not required but helps us provide suitable meals.

### **T-Shirt Size** (For appreciation gift)
*   If you would like to receive a T-shirt, select your size.
*   This field is not required but provides an option for an appreciation gift.

### **Activity Preferences** (Select activities you are interested in)
*   Choose the activities you are interested in attending from the provided options.
*   Please select all applicable activities.

### **Transportation Needed** (Do you need transportation assistance)
*   Select whether you need transportation assistance or have other arrangements.
*   This field helps us provide suitable transportation.

### **Parking Needed** (Will you need parking)
*   Indicate whether you need parking for the event.
*   Please select the option that best suits your needs.

### **Accessibility Needs** (Any accommodations needed)
*   If you require any accessibility accommodations, please enter them here.
*   This field is not required but provides an option for special needs.

### **Award Nomination** (Would you like to nominate a partner)
*   If you would like to nominate a partner for recognition, please select the option.
*   This field is not required but provides an opportunity for recognition.

### **Nomination Details** (Describe your nomination)
*   If you have nominated a partner, please enter the details of your nomination.
*   This field is not required but provides additional information.

### **Testimonial Interest** (Would you be willing to share a testimonial)
*   If you are willing to share a testimonial, please select the option.
*   This field is not required but provides a chance to share your experiences.

### **Partnership Highlights** (What has been the highlight of our partnership)
*   If you would like to share the highlight of your partnership, please enter your thoughts.
*   This field is not required but provides an opportunity for reflection.

### **Future Expectations** (What are you looking forward to)
*   If you would like to share your future expectations, please enter your thoughts.
*   This field is not required but provides a chance to share your aspirations.

### **Additional Comments** (Any other information)
*   If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them here.
*   This field is not required but provides an option for further details.
