# Team Sports Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals participating in team sports to ensure their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Select your preferred contact method from the "Contact Information" dropdown menu.
5. If applicable, provide information about your parent/guardian in the "Parent/Guardian Information" field.
6. If necessary, provide any relevant medical information in the "Medical Information" field.
7. List any medical conditions you have that may impact your participation in team sports.
8. Provide any allergies or medical conditions that may be relevant to your participation in team sports.
9. Enter the emergency contact's name and contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field.
10. Enter your current address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 01/01/2000).
* **Contact Information** (Select One): Select how you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency or important update. Options include:
	+ Phone: We will contact you via phone.
	+ Email: We will contact you via email.
	+ Both: We will contact you via both phone and email.
* **Parent/Guardian Information** (Select Multiple): If you are under 18, please select the type of parent or guardian that best applies to you. Options include:
	+ Father or Mother
	+ Grandparent
	+ Other
* **Medical Information** (Note): If you have any medical conditions or concerns that may impact your participation in team sports, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Medical Condition** (Text): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation in team sports, list them here.
* **Allergies** (Text): If you have any allergies, list them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your current address.
