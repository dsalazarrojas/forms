# Strategic Execution Framework Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us collect necessary information to make the workshop a success for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Select your role in the workshop from the dropdown menu in the "Role" field.
7. Choose the date of the workshop from the calendar in the "Workshop Date" field.
8. Select the time of the workshop in the "Workshop Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name to help us address you personally in communications regarding the workshop.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you regarding the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name to help us better understand your affiliation and context.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title to help us understand your position and responsibilities.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your role at the workshop: Workshop Organizer or Participant.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose a date for the workshop that suits you best.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time slot for the workshop that you prefer.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear when filling out the form.
* Ensure that your email address and phone number are correct and up-to-date.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please let us know in the notes section after this form.
