# Career Fair Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Fair Attendance Form is a registration tool for attendees to provide their information and express their interest in attending the career fair. It is used to collect necessary details to ensure a smooth and organized event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
2. Fill out the "Attendee name", "Email", and "Consent to share contact with employers" fields with the requested information.
3. If applicable, select your interested sessions from the "Session interests" field.
4. If you have any accessibility needs, please specify them in the "Accessibility needs" field.
5. If you would like to share your contact information with employers, please select "Yes" in the "Consent to share contact with employers" field.
6. Estimate your expected arrival time at the event in the "Arrival time estimate" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Please fill out your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, not required): If you are representing an organization, please specify it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please provide a valid email address for event communications.
* **Role or major** (`role_or_major`, text, not required): If you are a student, please provide your major or course of study.
* **Session interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, not required): Select all sessions you are interested in attending. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, not required): If you have any accessibility needs, please specify them here.
* **Consent to share contact with employers** (`consent_to_share_contact`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" to allow employers to contact you.
* **Arrival time estimate** (`arrival_time_estimate`, time, not required): Estimate your expected arrival time at the event.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them here.
