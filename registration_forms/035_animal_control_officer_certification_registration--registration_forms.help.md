# Animal Control Officer Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Control Officer Certification Registration form is used to collect information from ACOs who are interested in obtaining certification through our training and examination process. This form helps us assess your qualifications, work experience, and other relevant factors to ensure that you are a good fit for our program.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in your personal contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Provide your current employer and job title, as well as the type of organization you work for.
3.  Choose the correct type of certification you are seeking, along with your preferred training format and location.
4.  Share your relevant work experience, skills, and education background.
5.  Disclose any criminal convictions and background check requirements.
6.  Confirm your employer's sponsorship status and preferred payment method.
7.  Provide emergency contact information and any special accommodations you may need.
8.  Finally, sign and date the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (Applicant Name, text, required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email Address** (Contact email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your registration.
*   **Phone Number** (Contact phone, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for updates or questions.
*   **Home Address** (Street, city, state, zip, text, required): Enter your current address for our records.
*   **Animal Control Officer Certification Registration** (Registration, note, required): This is a required field for you to acknowledge that you are registering for ACO certification training and examination.
*   **Date of Birth** (DOB, date, required): Enter your date of birth to verify your age and eligibility for the certification.
*   **Current Employer** (Agency or organization, text, required): Enter the name and type of your current employer for our records.
*   **Type of Employer** (Organization type, select_one, required): Choose the correct type of organization you work for (e.g., municipal, county, private company, animal shelter, humane society, law enforcement, or other).
*   **Current Job Title** (Position, text, required): Enter your current job title to help us understand your experience level.
*   **Years Experience** (Years, select_one, required): Select the number of years you have been working in animal control or a related field.
*   **Highest Level of Education** (Education, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education completed (e.g., high school or equivalent, some college, associate degree, bachelor's degree, or master's degree or higher).
*   **Field of Study** (Major or concentration, text, optional): If you have completed a degree or have relevant coursework, provide the field of study for our records.
*   **Type of Certification Sought** (Certification level, select_one, required): Choose the type of certification you are seeking (e.g., basic, advanced, supervisor, instructor, or recertification).
*   **Preferred Training Format** (How to attend, select_one, required): Select your preferred training format (e.g., in-person, online, or hybrid).
*   **Preferred Training Location** (If in-person, text, optional): If you prefer in-person training, enter the location you would like it to be held.
*   **Preferred Training Dates** (When available, text, optional): If you have specific dates in mind, enter them for our records.
*   **Have You Completed ACO Training Before** (Previous training, select_one, required): Disclose your previous ACO training experience.
*   **Previous Certification Details** (If applicable, text, optional): If you have completed previous certification training, provide details about it.
*   **Relevant Experience** (Work history, text, required): Describe your relevant work experience in animal control or a related field.
*   **Relevant Skills** (Select all that apply, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the relevant skills you possess (e.g., animal handling, law enforcement, public education, report writing, conflict resolution, first aid, wildlife management, or legal procedures).
*   **Can You Perform Physical Duties Required** (Physical capability, select_one, required): Indicate your ability to perform physical duties required for the certification.
*   **Are You Willing to Undergo Background Check** (Screening, select_one, required): Confirm your willingness to undergo a background check.
*   **Do You Have Any Criminal Convictions** (Must disclose, select_one, required): Disclose any criminal convictions you may have.
*   **If Yes, Please Explain** (Details, text, optional): If you have criminal convictions, provide details about them.
*   **Is Your Employer Sponsoring This Certification** (Sponsorship, select_one, required): Confirm your employer's sponsorship status.
*   **Payment Method** (How to pay, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
*   **Do You Require Special Accommodations** (ADA or other, text, optional): If you require special accommodations, describe them for our records.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (Contact person, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (Contact number, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Additional Comments** (Any other info, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information you think would be helpful for us to know.
*   **Applicant Signature** (Type full name, text, required): Sign your full name to complete the registration process.
*   **Date** (Today's date, date, required): Enter the current date to confirm your registration.
