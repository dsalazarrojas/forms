# employee_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee registration form is used to collect essential information about new employees, including their name, contact details, and job title.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, click on the "New Employee" link and select the "Employee Registration Form" from the list.
2. Fill out the form with the necessary information, following the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the first name of the employee.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the last name of the employee.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmed`, `text`, **required**): Re-enter the email address to confirm it.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter the phone number of the employee.
* **Confirm Phone** (`phone_confirmed`, `text`, **required**): Re-enter the phone number to confirm it.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the employee's date of birth.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the employee's job title.

## Tips
* Be thorough when filling out the form to ensure accuracy.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
