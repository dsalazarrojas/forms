# Men's Metro Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Men's Metro Registration Form is designed to collect information for the metro event registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and official email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Select your gender from the options provided.
4. Choose your event type(s) from the list of available events.
5. Enter your metro location.
6. Select the event date.
7. Choose the event time.
8. Confirm your registration by filling in the email and phone number confirmations.
9. Agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
10. Finally, submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name for registration purposes.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name for registration purposes.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your official email address to enable easy communication about the event.

### Phone Number
* **Phone** (`phone`, required: false): Enter your phone number to ensure that we can contact you about the event.

### Gender
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your gender from the available options.

### Event Type
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the event type(s) that interest you the most.

### Metro Location
* **Metro Location** (`metro_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter your metro location for registration purposes.

### Event Date
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the event.

### Event Time
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the event time that suits you best.

### Registration Date
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is not relevant for users.

### Note
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.

### Email Confirmation
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_conf`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email confirmation to confirm your registration.

### Phone Number Confirmation
* **Phone Number Confirmation** (`phone_conf`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number confirmation to confirm your registration.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click to complete your registration.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to complete your registration.
- If you encounter any errors or issues while filling out the form, refer to the "Error" fields for more information.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
