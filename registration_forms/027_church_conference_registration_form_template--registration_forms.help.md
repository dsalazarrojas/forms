# Church Conference Registration Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for the church conference. It requires personal information (name, email, phone, city and state, church affiliation, and emergency contact), registration options, workshop interests, and any special needs such as childcare. The form is designed to collect the necessary details for attendee registration and to ensure a smooth experience for everyone attending the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your attendee's full name, email, phone number, city and state.
2.  Choose your church affiliation and denomination (if applicable).
3.  Select your registration option and workshop interest.
4.  If you require on-site childcare, select 'True' and list the names and ages of the children.
5.  Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
6.  Provide any additional information about your preferences or needs.
7.  Finally, confirm that you consent to your photo being taken for promotional materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Attendee Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required)
Enter your name in the required format of First Name Last Name.

### **Email** (`email`, `email`, required)
Enter your email address.

### **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required)
Enter your phone number.

### **City and State** (`city_state`, `text`, required)
Enter your city and state.

### **Church Affiliation** (`header_church_info`, `note`, required)
This section is optional and is used to identify your church affiliation. If you are not affiliated with a church, you can leave this blank.

### **Church Name** (`church_name`, `text`, required)
Enter your church name.

### **Denomination** (`church_denomination`, `text`, required)
Enter your church denomination (if applicable).

### **Conference Options** (`header_options`, `note`, required)
Select one of the registration options: Early Bird Registration, Standard Registration, or On-Site Registration.

### **Registration Option** (`registration_option`, `select_one`, required)
This section is a sub-section of the registration options. Please select one of the options from the dropdown list.

### **Workshop Track Interest** (`workshop_interest`, `select_one`, required)
Choose your primary focus area from the dropdown list.

### **Childcare Needed** (`child_care_needed`, `select_one`, required)
If you have children ages 2-10, select 'True' and list the names and ages of the children in the next field.

### **Childcare Details** (`child_care_details`, `text`, optional)
List the names and ages of the children requiring on-site childcare.

### **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required)
Enter the name and phone number of the person not attending the conference who can be contacted in case of an emergency.

### **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional)
Enter any additional information that we should know about you.

### **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, `select_one`, required)
Select 'True' to confirm that you consent to your photo being taken for promotional materials.
