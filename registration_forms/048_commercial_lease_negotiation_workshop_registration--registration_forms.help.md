# Commercial Lease Negotiation Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees to the Commercial Lease Negotiation Workshop. Fill out this form to secure your spot at the workshop and choose your preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information, including your attendee name, job title, company, email, phone number, and other relevant details.
2. Select your preferred workshop date and format (in-person or online).
3. Choose the session tracks that interest you the most.
4. Indicate your primary learning goal and any additional comments.
5. If applicable, provide your meal preference and dietary restrictions.
6. Select your registration type and payment method.
7. Enter any promo code if you have one.
8. Finally, provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Full Name (1)**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title (2)**: Your position at your company or organization.
* **Company Name (3)**: Name of your company or organization.
* **Email Address (4)**: Enter your email address for workshop updates and communication.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Your contact number for any additional questions or concerns.
* **Profession (7)**: Select the best description of your profession (e.g. Attorney, Real Estate Agent, etc.).
* **Years of Experience (8)**: Choose the number of years of experience you have in real estate or leasing.
* **Workshop Date (10)**: Select the preferred date for the workshop (if applicable).
* **Workshop Format (11)**: Choose between in-person, live online, or recorded session.
* **Session Tracks (12)**: Select all the tracks that interest you.
* **Primary Learning Goal (14)**: Choose what you want to learn from the workshop (e.g. Basic Lease Terms, Negotiation Skills, etc.).
* **Current Challenges (15)**: Describe any challenges you are facing in your current work.
* **Meal Preference (17)**: Select your meal preference for in-person events.
* **Dietary Restrictions (18)**: Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Registration Type (20)**: Select your registration type (e.g. Early Bird, Standard, etc.).
* **Payment Method (21)**: Choose how you will pay for the workshop (e.g. credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **Promo Code (22)**: Enter any discount code you may have.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (24)**: Select where you learned about the workshop.
* **Accessibility Needs (25)**: Describe any accessibility needs you may have.
* **CE Credits Needed (26)**: Indicate if you need continuing education credits.
* **Additional Comments (27)**: Provide any additional comments or information.
