# Job Screening Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect information from job applicants to facilitate the internal screening process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by filling in your Job Screening Application details.
* Provide your Candidate Details, including any relevant information about yourself.
* If applicable, upload your Resume to support your application.
* Finally, provide the Job Posting details you are applying for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Screening Application** (`job_screening_application`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief introduction to your application. Please enter a short description of your job screening application.
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, optional): In this field, please provide any relevant information about yourself that is not included in your resume, such as your work experience, skills, or qualifications.
* **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, note, optional): If you have a resume, please upload it to support your application. This will help us review your qualifications more efficiently.
* **Job Posting** (`job_posting`, text, optional): In this field, please enter the job posting ID or a brief description of the job you are applying for.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for guidance.
* We appreciate your time and effort in filling out this form accurately and thoroughly.
