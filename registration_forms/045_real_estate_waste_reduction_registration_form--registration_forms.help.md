# Real Estate Waste Reduction Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Waste Reduction Registration Form is used to collect information from property owners and stakeholders about their waste reduction efforts. This form helps to track and monitor progress in waste reduction programs, enabling the development of effective strategies to reduce waste.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill out the required information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your property and circumstances.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to ensure accurate registration.
4. Attach any required documents and upload them as specified.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Waste Reduction Program** (`waste_reduction_program`): Choose the relevant waste reduction program you are registering for. This will help the form owner understand your interest in the program.
  - Options: Select One: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter your property address accurately for easy identification and tracking.
  - Type: Text
- **Property Type** (`property_type`): Select all relevant property types that apply to your property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
  - Type: Select Multiple: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- **Reduction Strategies** (`reduction_strategies`): If applicable, describe any waste reduction strategies you are currently implementing on your property.
  - Type: Note
- **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Choose the status of your submission.
  - Options: Select One: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- **Upload Document** (`upload_document`): Attach any supporting documents as specified.
  - Type: Date
- **Notes** (`notes`): Provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.
  - Type: Text
- **Additional Instructions** (`additional_instructions`): Add any additional information to assist the form owner.
  - Type: Text
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of your submission.
  - Type: Time
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
  - Type: Text
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
  - Type: Email
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
  - Type: Text
- **Submission Status 2** (`submission_status_2`): Choose the status of your submission.
  - Options: Select One: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- **Upload Document 2** (`upload_document_2`): Attach any supporting documents as specified.
  - Type: Text
  - Hint: This field may not be required in all cases.
- **Submission Date 2** (`submission_date_2`): Enter the date of your submission.
  - Type: Date
  - Hint: This field may not be required in all cases.
- **Notes 2** (`notes_2`): Provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.
  - Type: Text
  - Hint: This field may not be required in all cases.
- **Additional Instructions 2** (`additional_instructions_2`): Add any additional information to assist the form owner.
  - Type: Text
  - Hint: This field may not be required in all cases.
- **Form Owner** (`form_owner`): Enter the name of the form owner.
  - Type: Text
