# Household And Territory Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering information about households and territories. It's used to collect necessary details for our records and to track changes in property status and territory type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the household information in the "Household Info" field.
2. Identify the contact person for the household in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Provide details about the residents in the "Resident Details" field.
4. Enter information about the property in the "Property Info" field.
5. Provide information about the territory in the "Territory Info" field.
6. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Choose the correct property type in the "Property Type" field.
8. Select the current status of the property in the "Property Status" field.
9. Check if the property is located on the main territory in the "Property Location" field.
10. Indicate the area of the property in the "Property Area" field.
11. Choose the type of territory in the "Territory Type" field.
12. Select the status of the territory in the "Territory Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Info** (`household_info`, text, optional): Enter the name and address of the household, including the street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the household.
* **Resident Details** (`resident_details`, text, optional): List the names and ages of all residents in the household.
* **Property Info** (`property_info`, text, optional): Enter the property's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Territory Info** (`territory_info`, text, optional): Enter the territory's name and any other relevant details.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the household or territory that might be helpful.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the property is a house, apartment, or other.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of the property (Active or Inactive).
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, select_one, optional): Check if the property is located on the main territory (Yes or No).
* **Property Area** (`property_area`, select_one, optional): Choose the area of the property (Yes or No).
* **Territory Type** (`territory_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of territory (Yes or No).
* **Territory Status** (`territory_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the territory (Active or Inactive).

Tips:
* Make sure to double-check the information you enter in each field to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, ask a supervisor or a colleague for help.
* This form is meant to be filled out by authorized personnel only.
* Please do not enter any confidential or sensitive information in this form.
