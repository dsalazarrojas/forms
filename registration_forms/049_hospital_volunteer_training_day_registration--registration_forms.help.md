# Hospital Volunteer Training Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for hospital volunteers to register for a training day, providing information about their availability and contact preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Volunteer Sign-up number, which is an identifier for your volunteer account.
2. Select your preferred training sessions from the options provided.
3. Enter your first and last name.
4. Provide your email address and phone number.
5. Choose your preferred contact method: Call or Text.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Sign-up (ID)**: A unique identifier for your volunteer account.
* **Training Sessions**: Select the sessions you are interested in attending. You can choose one or multiple sessions.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Call or Text).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out this form for the correct purpose (volunteer training day registration).
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your hospital representative for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed in a single page, so please ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting.
