# Retail Innovation Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Innovation Lab Registration Form is a straightforward tool for potential members to provide their information and register for the lab's programs and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the User Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide the necessary information for the Innovation Lab in the Innovation Lab Information section.
3. In the Submission section, describe your project or innovation you are interested in submitting.
4. Enter your contact information in the Contact section.
5. Read and agree to the terms in the Agreement section.
6. Add any additional comments or thoughts in the Comments section.
7. Finally, submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: This section requires your basic contact information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Innovation Lab Information**: Provide a brief description of your background and experience in retail innovation, including any relevant skills or qualifications.
* **Submission**: Describe your project or innovation you are interested in submitting to the lab. Be as detailed as possible, as this will help us understand your proposal.
* **Contact**: Enter your contact information, including your name and email address. This will help us reach you and discuss your project further.
* **Agreement**: Please read and agree to our terms, including our code of conduct and data privacy policy. This is a mandatory requirement for registration.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your project or innovation. This is an optional section but will help us understand your perspective better.
