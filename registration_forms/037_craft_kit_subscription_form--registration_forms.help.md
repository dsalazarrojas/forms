# craft_kit_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new subscribers to a craft kit subscription service. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
4. Enter your city in the "City" field.
5. Enter your state in the "State" field.
6. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Select your preferred craft kit from the "Craft Kit" dropdown list.
10. Choose one or more subscription types from the "Subscription Type" dropdown list.
11. Enter your start date in the "Start Date" field.
12. Enter your end date in the "End Date" field.
13. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Notes" field (optional).
14. If necessary, enter the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field (optional).
15. If necessary, enter the user assigned to you in the "Assigned User" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name in this field.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your street address in this field.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city in this field.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state in this field.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in this field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Craft Kit** (`craft_kit`, select_one, required): Select the craft kit you would like to subscribe to.
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more subscription types that apply to you.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to start your subscription.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date your subscription will end.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your subscription.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If a tool has been assigned to you, enter it in this field.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): If a user has been assigned to you, enter it in this field. 

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you need to add or remove a subscription type, use the "Subscription Type" field's dropdown list.
* If you need to add or remove a craft kit, use the "Craft Kit" field's dropdown list.
* Be sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
