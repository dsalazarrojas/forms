## Visitor Badge Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Badge Check In Form is used to record the details of visitors who enter your premises for a badge check-in process. This helps to ensure that visitors are accurately accounted for and can be quickly checked out when they leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your Email in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. If you are visiting on behalf of a company, fill in the name of your Company.
6. Select your Department or area you are visiting (if applicable).
7. Review and submit the form to complete the check-in process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
- **Company**: If you are visiting on behalf of a company, enter the name of the company.
- **Department**: If your visit is related to a specific department, enter the name of the department you are visiting.
