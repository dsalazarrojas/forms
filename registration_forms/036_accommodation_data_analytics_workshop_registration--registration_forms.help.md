# Accommodation Data Analytics Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accommodation Data Analytics Workshop Registration form is designed to gather information from attendees to tailor the event to their needs and interests, and to provide relevant information and resources to attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, professional title, and organization name.
2. Provide your professional email address and phone number.
3. Indicate your level of experience with data analysis and your years of experience in the hospitality industry.
4. Choose your preferred workshop session (morning, afternoon, or both).
5. Select the analytics tools you are familiar with.
6. Identify which accommodation metrics you are most interested in learning about.
7. Choose which data challenges you face in your daily work.
8. Provide your emergency contact name and phone number.
9. Indicate your interest in networking opportunities.
10. Choose whether you would like to receive follow-up information about accommodation analytics.
11. Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as per your ID document.
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of organization you represent (e.g., Hotel Chain, Independent Hotel, etc.).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Years in Industry** (`years_in_industry`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience in the hospitality industry (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, etc.).
* **Data Analysis Experience** (`data_analysis_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience with data analysis (Beginner, Intermediate, etc.).
* **Workshop Session Choice** (`workshop_session_choice`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred workshop session (Morning Session, Afternoon Session, etc.).
* **Analytics Tools Experience** (`analytics_tools_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose which analytics tools you are familiar with.
* **Accommodation Metrics Focus** (`accommodation_metrics_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose which accommodation metrics you are most interested in learning about.
* **Data Challenges** (`data_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose which data challenges you face in your daily work.
* **Workshop Objectives** (`workshop_objectives`, `text`, required): Enter what you hope to achieve from this workshop.
* **Special Dietary Requirements** (`special_dietary_requirements`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose which dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose which accessibility needs you have for the workshop.
* **Networking Interest** (`networking_interest`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your interest in networking opportunities.
* **Follow Up Interest** (`follow_up_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would like to receive follow-up information about accommodation analytics.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your full name as per your ID document.
* Be honest about your level of experience with data analysis and analytics tools.
* Choose the options that best fit your needs and interests.
* If you have any special dietary requirements or accessibility needs, please let us know so we can provide necessary accommodations.
* If you are interested in networking opportunities, let us know so we can facilitate introductions and connections.
