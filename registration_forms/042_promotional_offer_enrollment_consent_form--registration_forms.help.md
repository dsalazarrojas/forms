# promotional_offer_enrollment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll in promotional offers and discounts provided by our company. It will only take a few minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To enroll, please provide the following information:

1. Business Name: Enter your company name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
2. Address: Enter your business address to receive mail and email communications.
3. Zip Code: Enter your company's zip code to ensure accurate mail delivery.
4. Contact Number: Enter the phone number where you can be reached.
5. Email: Enter your company email address to receive notifications.
6. Consent: Check "Yes" to receive promotional offers and discounts. If you check "No", you will not be included in our marketing efforts.
7. Additional Comments: If you'd like to provide any additional information, please type it in this field.
8. Terms and Conditions: Check "Yes" to indicate that you have read and accepted our terms and conditions.
9. Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes (if required).
10. Time of Birth: Enter your time of birth for age verification purposes (if required).
11. Enrollment Confirmation: Check "Yes" to confirm your enrollment in promotional offers and discounts.
12. Enrollment Date: Enter the date you enrolled in our promotional offers and discounts.
13. Enrollment Time: Enter the time you enrolled in our promotional offers and discounts.
14. Enrollment Status: Select "Active" if you are currently enrolled, or "Inactive" if you are not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your business address to receive mail and email communications.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Enter your company's zip code to ensure accurate mail delivery.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address to receive notifications.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" to receive promotional offers and discounts. If you check "No", you will not be included in our marketing efforts.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you'd like to provide any additional information, please type it in this field.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" to indicate that you have read and accepted our terms and conditions.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes (if required).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth for age verification purposes (if required).
* **Enrollment Confirmation** (`enrollment_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" to confirm your enrollment in promotional offers and discounts.
* **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you enrolled in our promotional offers and discounts.
* **Enrollment Time** (`enrollment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you enrolled in our promotional offers and discounts.
* **Enrollment Status** (`enrollment_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Active" if you are currently enrolled, or "Inactive" if you are not.

## Tips
* Make sure to check the "Consent" field to receive promotional offers and discounts.
* Check the "Terms and Conditions" field to confirm you have read and accepted our terms and conditions.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form should only be completed once.
