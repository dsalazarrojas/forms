# expo_decorator_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expo Decorator Registration Form is a standardized form used to gather essential information about individuals or organizations registering to become Expo Decorators. This form is intended to facilitate their participation in Expo events and ensure they are aware of the terms and conditions of their involvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field. 
2.  Review each field carefully and enter the requested details. 
3.  You can submit your form once you have completed all required fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: Enter your name and other contact information for identification purposes. 
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact details, including your email and phone number. 
* **Address**: Enter your physical address for delivery and communication purposes. 
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and notification purposes. 
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for direct communication. 
* **Company**: Enter your company name or affiliation for identification purposes. 
* **Role**: Select the role that best describes your involvement with Expo, such as "Service Provider" or "Event Host". 
* **Experience**: Choose your relevant experience in the expo industry. 
* **Bio**: Provide a brief description of yourself or your company for Expo representatives. 
* **Services**: Enter any relevant services you offer. 
* **Availability**: Enter your availability for future events, including dates and times. 
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any special requirements or requests you have for your participation. 
* **Rate**: Enter your rate or pricing for your services. 
* **Agreement**: Select the agreement that best represents your commitment to Expo, such as "Agreement 1" or "Agreement 2". 

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields. 
* Review your form carefully before submission to avoid errors. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the Expo team for assistance.
