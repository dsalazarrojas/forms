# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering students for a course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the course details.
2. Fill out the trainer details.
3. Fill out the student details.
4. Fill out the registration details.
5. Confirm the details.
6. Select the registration type.
7. Choose the course start date.
8. Choose the course end date.
9. Enter the student's name.
10. Enter the student's email address.
11. Enter the student's phone number.
12. Add any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Details**: The course title is required to be filled out. Please enter the name of the course.
* **Trainer Details**: This field is not required, but it's useful for the administration team to know who is teaching the course.
* **Student Details**: This field is required. Please enter the student's information.
* **Registration Details**: This field is required. Please enter the relevant information.
* **Confirm Details**: This field is required. Please confirm the details.
* **Registration Type**: This field is required. Please select the type of registration.
* **Course Start Date**: This field is required. Please choose the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date**: This field is required. Please choose the end date of the course.
* **Student Name**: This field is required. Please enter the name of the student.
* **Student Email**: This field is required. Please enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: This field is required. Please enter the student's phone number.
* **Notes**: This field is not required, but it's useful for additional comments or information.

Note: The fields marked as `required` are obligatory for the form to be considered valid.
