# Music School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music School Registration Form is a necessary step for students to register for music lessons or classes. This form collects essential details to help the school prepare for your classes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, fill out the required information for the student's name, email address, class days, class frequency, music instrument, music experience, and emergency contact details.
2. Choose the appropriate class days for your music lessons.
3. Indicate how often you would like to attend classes by selecting from the provided options (daily, weekly, or biweekly).
4. Choose one music instrument from the available options (piano, guitar, violin, or drums).
5. Select your current music experience level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
6. Enter the name, relation, phone number, and email address of your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **student_email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **student_phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number for easy contact.
* **class_days** (`class_days`, select_one, required): Choose one or multiple days of the week for your music lessons (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday).
* **start_time** (`start_time`, text, optional): Enter the preferred start time for your music lessons.
* **class_frequency** (`class_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how often you would like to attend classes (daily, weekly, or biweekly).
* **additional_info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your music preferences.
* **music_instrument** (`music_instrument`, select_one, required): Choose one music instrument from the available options (piano, guitar, violin, or drums).
* **music_experience** (`music_experience`, select_multiple, required): Select your current music experience level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
* **emergency_contact_name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, text, optional): Describe your relation to the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.
* Selecting class frequency and class days will help the school prepare for your lessons.
