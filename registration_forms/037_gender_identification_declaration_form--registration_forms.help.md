# Gender Identification Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your gender identity and declaration. It's a crucial step for individuals who identify as non-binary, transgender, or have other gender identities that are different from their assigned sex at birth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you're filling it out for the correct purpose and with the correct intention.
2. Select one of the options on the first page to indicate whether you have a gender identity that is different from your assigned sex at birth.
3. On the second page, select any additional preferred names that you identify with.
4. On the third page, please provide any information about your birth certificate, if applicable.
5. On the fourth page, select one of the options to indicate your gender identity.
6. On the fifth page, select any additional genders that are not listed in the previous page.
7. On the sixth page, provide a declaration of your gender identity.
8. On the seventh page, provide any additional information that is relevant to your gender identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Yes/No** (<id: 1>, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you have a gender identity that is different from your assigned sex at birth.
* **Preferred Names** (<id: 2>, optional): If you have any additional names that you prefer to be known by, please select them here.
* **Birth Certificate** (<id: 3>, optional): If you have a birth certificate, please provide its details here.
* **Gender Identity** (<id: 4>, required): Please select your gender identity from the options provided.
* **Additional Genders** (<id: 5>, optional): If your gender identity is not listed, please select "Yes" here to provide additional information.
* **Declaration** (<id: 6>, optional): Please provide a brief explanation of your gender identity.
* **Additional Information** (<id: 7>, optional): Is there any additional information about your gender identity that we should know?
