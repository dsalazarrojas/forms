# Wildlife Preservation Symposium Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Wildlife Preservation Symposium. Please fill it out carefully to ensure that you are correctly registered for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please enter a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This can be your work or personal phone number.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
7. Select your country of residence from the "Country" dropdown menu.
8. (Optional) If you have a state where you are from or based in, select it in the "State" dropdown menu. This is not required.
9. Enter the city where you are from in the "City" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required):** Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text, required):** Enter your last name.
* **Email (email, required):** Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone (text, required):** Enter your phone number. This can be your work or personal phone number.
* **Address (text, required):** Enter your address. This can be your work or personal address.
* **Zip (number, required):** Enter your zip code.
* **Country (select, required):** Select your country of residence.
* **State (select, optional):** If you have a state where you are from or based, select it. This is not required.
* **City (text, optional):** Enter the city where you are from. This is not required.
