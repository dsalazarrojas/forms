# Livestock Disease Prevention Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Disease Prevention Forum Registration Form is designed to collect basic contact information and preferences from users who want to join the forum. Please fill out the form to register for the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to send the form.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. Make sure to check the "Submit" button to review your information before sending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Data** (`form_data`, text, not required): This field is for any additional information you want to provide about your registration.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear in the forum.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, not required): Select one option from the list provided.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple options from the list provided.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments you have.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out accurate and complete information to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues while filling out the form, contact the forum administrators.
* You can submit the form multiple times if you need to, but please be aware that the form is not designed to be reused or duplicated.
* You can also use the "Submit" button to review your information before sending it.
