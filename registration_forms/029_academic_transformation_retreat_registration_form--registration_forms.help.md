# Academic Transformation Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Academic Transformation Retreat, a valuable opportunity for participants to engage in discussions, workshops, and networking. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have read and understand the instructions and explanations provided.
2. Fill out each field carefully and accurately according to your preferences and requirements.
3. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First name
* **Participant's First Name** (`participant_first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last name
* **Participant's Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email address
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for retreat communications.

### Phone number
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number here.

### Institution or organization
* **Institution or Organization** (`institution`, `text`, required): Please enter your institution or organization name.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department name within your institution.

### Role
* **Your Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Please select your role among the options provided:
	+ Faculty
	+ Department Chair
	+ Dean or Associate Dean
	+ Staff
	+ Graduate Student
	+ Other

### Sessions of interest
* **Which retreat sessions are you interested in attending (select all that apply)** (`retreat_sessions`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the sessions that align with your interests and goals:
	+ Opening keynote
	+ Curriculum innovation workshop
	+ Technology in the classroom
	+ Assessment and evaluation strategies
	+ Student engagement roundtable
	+ Closing reflection and action planning

### Attendance days
* **Which days will you attend** (`attendance_dates`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days you plan to attend the retreat:
	+ Day 1 only
	+ Day 2 only
	+ Both days

### Accommodation needed
* **Do you need overnight accommodation?** (`accommodation_needed`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you require overnight accommodation or not.

### Dietary restrictions
* **Any dietary restrictions or food allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or food allergies, please list them here.

### Accessibility needs
* **Any accessibility accommodations required** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please specify them here.

### Topics of interest
* **What topics are you most interested in exploring at the retreat** (`topics_of_interest`, `text`, optional): Describe the topics you are eager to explore during the retreat.

### Transportation
* **How will you be traveling to the retreat?** (`transportation`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred mode of transportation:
	+ Driving
	+ Public transit
	+ Carpooling
	+ Flying
	+ Other

### Emergency contact name
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of your emergency contact.

### Emergency contact phone number
* **Emergency contact phone number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

### Agreement
* **I confirm my registration and agree to the retreat participation guidelines** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm your registration and agree to the retreat participation guidelines:
	+ Yes - I confirm
	+ Other

Note: This guide is intended to be helpful and practical. If you have any questions or doubts, please contact the retreat organizers for clarification. 

Remember to review and double-check your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
