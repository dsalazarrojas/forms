This form is for registration to the Carpentry Contest Tournament. It's a contest for carpentry enthusiasts to showcase their skills and projects. To complete this form, please ensure you have the required information and follow the instructions below.

## Purpose
The Carpentry Contest Tournament Entry Form is designed for participants to register for the contest. It collects personal and project information necessary for the contest organizers to manage and judge entries.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by selecting the "Section Participant Information"** to provide your personal details.
2. **Fill in your "Full Name," "Email Address," and "Phone Number"** accurately and truthfully.
3. **Enter your "Age"** as it must be 18 or older to participate.
4. **Under "Experience and Skill Level,"** select your relevant carpentry experience level (Apprentice, Journeyman, Master, or Hobbyist).
5. **Select your "Primary Carpentry Specialty,"** which is the area you excel in.
6. **Choose your "Selected Tournament Division"** (Individual - Speed, Individual - Craftsmanship, Team - Project Build).
7. **Select your "Competition Category"** (Furniture, Structural, Artistic, or Sustainable Design).
8. **Check if you will bring your own power tools**.
9. **Provide a brief description of your project** under "Proposed Project Brief".
10. **Select the types of wood you'd like to work with**.
11. **Choose the minimum workspace size you need** (Standard 10x10, Large 15x15, or Extra Large 20x20).
12. **Confirm you have a valid workshop safety certification**.
13. **Agree to the tournament's liability and safety terms**.
14. **Choose how you'd like to pay the entry fee** (Credit Card, PayPal, Check, or Sponsor-paid).
15. **Enter emergency contact information**.
16. **Select a date for registration**.
17. **Finally, sign your name digitally** as a signature of your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your real full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: A valid email address associated with your name.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Age**: The participant's age, which must be 18 or above to register.
* **Professional Level**: A select one option for your level of carpentry experience (Apprentice, Journeyman, Master, or Hobbyist).
* **Primary Carpentry Specialty**: The specific area of carpentry you focus on (Framing, Cabinetry, Joinery, Wood Turning, Restoration, or Other).
* **Selected Tournament Division**: The contest division you'd like to enter (Individual - Speed, Individual - Craftsmanship, Team - Project Build).
* **Competition Category**: The area of focus of your project (Furniture, Structural, Artistic, or Sustainable Design).
* **Bring Your Own Tools**: Confirm if you'll bring your own power tools to the contest.
* **Proposed Project Brief**: A brief description of the project you'd like to enter, including its details and requirements.
* **Preferred Wood Type**: Select the type of wood you'd like to use for your project (Pine, Oak, Walnut, Cherry, Maple, or Plywood).
* **Minimum Workspace Size Required**: The minimum space size you need for your project (10x10, 15x15, or 20x20).
* **Workshop Safety Certification**: Confirm if you have a valid workshop safety certification.
* **Liability and Safety Terms**: Agree to the liability and safety terms of the tournament.
* **Entry Fee Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your entry fee (Credit Card, PayPal, Check, or Sponsor-paid).
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and phone number of a contact person for emergency purposes.
* **Date of Registration**: Enter the date you are registering for the contest.
* **Digital Signature**: Type your name digitally as a signature of your participation.

## Tips

* Ensure you have a valid workshop safety certification before proceeding.
* Agree to the tournament's liability and safety terms.
* Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure your project description is detailed enough for judges to categorize.
