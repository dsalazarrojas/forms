# Auto Loan Payment Protection Insurance Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enroll a borrower in the Auto Loan Payment Protection Insurance (ALPP) program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Primary Borrower Details**: Please fill in the information for the primary borrower. This includes:
	* `Borrower Full Name`: The legal name of the borrower as it appears on the loan agreement.
	* `Loan Account Number`: The unique identifier for the loan.
2. **Co-Applicant Details**: If there is a co-applicant, please fill in their information. This includes:
	* `Co-Applicant Full Name`: The legal name of the co-applicant.
	* `Co-Applicant Email`: The email address of the co-applicant (optional).
3. **Coverage Selection**: Please select the level of payment protection you want to enroll in:
	* `Coverage Type`: Choose from `Life and Disability`, `Life Only`, `Involuntary Unemployment`, or `Full Protection Bundle`.
4. **Additional Information**: Please fill in:
	* `Monthly Benefit Amount`: The requested protection amount.
	* `Monthly Coverage Premium`: The premium amount to be added to your loan payment.
	* `Enrollment Effective Date`: The date when the coverage is set to begin.
5. **Authorization and Signature**: Please review and confirm:
	* `I confirm I am currently employed and meet all eligibility criteria`: Confirm that you are employed and meet the eligibility criteria.
	* `Authorize Premium Billing`: Confirm that you want to add the premium to your monthly loan payment.
	* `Electronic Signature Consent`: Confirm that you agree to use an electronic signature for this enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Borrower Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on the loan agreement.
* **Loan Account Number**: Enter your unique loan identifier.
* **Borrower Email**: Enter your email address for enrollment confirmation.
* **Borrower Phone**: Enter your best contact number.
* **Co-Applicant Full Name**: Enter the full name of the co-applicant (leave blank if no co-applicant).
* **Co-Applicant Email**: Enter the email address of the co-applicant (optional).
* **Coverage Type**: Select the level of payment protection you want to enroll in.
* **Monthly Benefit Amount**: Enter the requested protection amount.
* **Monthly Coverage Premium**: Enter the premium amount to be added to your loan payment.
* **Enrollment Effective Date**: Enter the date when the coverage is set to begin.
* **I confirm I am currently employed and meet all eligibility criteria**: Confirm that you are employed and meet the eligibility criteria.
* **Authorize Premium Billing**: Confirm that you want to add the premium to your monthly loan payment.
* **Electronic Signature Consent**: Confirm that you agree to use an electronic signature for this enrollment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please review your internal documentation or seek assistance from a qualified staff member.
* Duplicate enrollments can be rejected or delayed in processing, so it's essential to review the information carefully to avoid errors.
