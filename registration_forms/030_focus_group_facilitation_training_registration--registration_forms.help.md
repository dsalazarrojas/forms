# Focus Group Facilitation Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect contact and demographic information from individuals interested in the Focus Group Facilitation Training. This includes details such as contact information, training level, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "First Page" option to begin the form.
2. Fill in your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your name in the "name" field.
4. Select the contacts that will be involved in the training from the "Contact" field.
5. Choose your level of training in the "Training Level" field.
6. Select a date in the "Date" field, if applicable.
7. Choose a time in the "Time" field, if applicable.
8. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
9. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
10. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
11. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
13. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
14. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page (id: 1)**: This page is the introduction to the form, and no further information is required.
* **email (id: 2)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **name (id: 3)**: Provide your full name for reference.
* **Contact (id: 4)**: Select the contacts involved in the training from the drop-down menu.
* **Training Level (id: 5)**: Choose your level of training experience, which will help us tailor the training to your needs.
* **Date (id: 6)**: Select a date for the training, if applicable.
* **Time (id: 7)**: Choose a time for the training, if applicable.
* **First Name (id: 8)**: Enter your first name for reference.
* **Last Name (id: 9)**: Enter your last name for reference.
* **Organization (id: 10)**: Enter your organization or company name.
* **Job Title (id: 11)**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Phone (id: 12)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes (id: 13)**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Confirm Email (id: 14)**: Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Phone (id: 15)**: Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Use the "Confirm" fields to ensure your information is correct.
