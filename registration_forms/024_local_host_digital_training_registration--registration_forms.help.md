# local_host_digital_training_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register local digital training sessions. The purpose of this form is to collect information from attendees who wish to attend these sessions. The form is used to gather details about the attendee, such as their experience level and session preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the session(s) you wish to attend by choosing from the available options.
2. Indicate your experience level in the session(s) you've chosen.
3. Provide any additional details you'd like to share about yourself as an attendee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): This field is a text input where you can enter any additional information about your registration. This could include your name, email address, or any other relevant details.
* **Session Choices** (`session_choices`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the available session options to register for that session.
* **Experience Levels** (`experience_levels`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your experience level for each session you've selected. Options are beginner, intermediate, and advanced.
* **Session Choices 2** (`session_choices_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional session options to register for.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details you'd like to share about yourself as an attendee.
* **Attendee Details 2** (`attendee_details_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details you'd like to share about yourself as an attendee.

## Tips
* Please select only the sessions you are interested in attending.
* Be sure to provide accurate information when filling out the form.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please let us know in the additional details fields.
