# Service Design Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Design Registration Form is a critical tool for registering and managing workshops. It gathers essential details about the workshop, including its type, duration, date, and contact information. Accurate and comprehensive information is crucial to ensure that your workshop is properly registered and managed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Workshop Details**: This section requires information about the workshop, including its type, duration, and date. Please select the appropriate workshop type from the options provided and choose the correct duration in hours.
2. **Attendee Information**: Provide the details of the attendees, including their email addresses, if applicable.
3. **Contact Information**: Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you about your workshop.
5. **Workshop Type**: Select the type of workshop from the options provided.
6. **Workshop Length**: Choose the duration of the workshop in hours.
7. **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop.
8. **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop.
9. **Service Type**: Select the type of service provided from the options provided.
10. **Participant Count**: Enter the number of attendees.
11. **Business Name**: Enter your business name.
12. **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
13. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
14. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
15. **Service Designer**: Indicate if you have a service designer for this workshop.
16. **Service Designer Information**: If you have a service designer, provide their details.
17. **Service Designer Phone**: Enter the service designer's phone number.
18. **Service Designer Email**: Enter the service designer's email address.
19. **Service Designer Notes**: Provide any additional information about the service designer.
20. **Workshop Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Details**: (`workshop_details`, text, required) This field requires a brief description of your workshop.
* **Attendee Information**: (`attendee_info`, text, required) Please enter the details of the attendees, including their names and email addresses.
* **Contact Information**: (`contact_info`, text, required) Enter the name and phone number of the contact person.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, required) Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you about your workshop.
* **Workshop Type**: (`workshop_type`, select_one, required) Select the type of workshop from the options provided.
* **Workshop Length**: (`workshop_length`, select_multiple, required) Choose the duration of the workshop in hours.
* **Workshop Date**: (`workshop_date`, date, required) Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time**: (`workshop_time`, time, required) Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Service Type**: (`service_type`, select_multiple, required) Select the type of service provided from the options provided.
* **Participant Count**: (`participant_count`, number, required) Enter the number of attendees.
* **Business Name**: (`business_name`, text, required) Enter your business name.
* **Business Address**: (`business_address`, text, required) Enter your business address.
* **Contact Person**: (`contact_person`, text, required) Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, required) Enter your phone number.
* **Service Designer**: (`service_designer`, select_one, required) Indicate if you have a service designer for this workshop.
* **Service Designer Information**: (`service_designer_info`, text, required) If you have a service designer, provide their details.
* **Service Designer Phone**: (`service_designer_phone`, text, required) Enter the service designer's phone number.
* **Service Designer Email**: (`service_designer_email`, email, required) Enter the service designer's email address.
* **Service Designer Notes**: (`service_designer_notes`, text, required) Provide any additional information about the service designer.
* **Workshop Notes**: (`workshop_notes`, text, required) Enter any additional notes about the workshop.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields accurately and comprehensively.
* Review your form before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us.
