# Startup Incubation Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Startup Incubation Program Registration Form. The form is used to register your startup for the program, which provides resources, funding, and support for startups to grow and develop their businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to select the correct legal entity type for your startup.
3. Choose the correct category and industry for your startup.
4. Add any additional information you think is relevant to your application.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Time in Business** (`time_in_business`, `number`, optional): Enter the time in business (e.g., months or years).
* **Legal Entity** (`legal_entity`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of legal entity for your startup:
	+ Sole Proprietorship
	+ Partnership
	+ Corporation
* **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id_number`, `number`, optional): Enter your tax ID number (if applicable).
* **Startup Name** (`startup_name`, `text`, optional): Enter a nickname or abbreviation for your startup.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your startup.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the category and industry for your startup:
	+ Technology
	+ Finance
	+ Retail
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, optional): Enter your industry.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of your office or location.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city of your office or location.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state of your office or location.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `number`, optional): Enter the zip code of your office or location.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people in your team.
* **Founding Date** (`founding_date`, `time`, optional): Enter the date when your startup was founded.
* **Founding Time** (`founding_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when your startup was founded.
* **Founding Location** (`founding_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where your startup was founded.
* **Founding Country** (`founding_country`, `text`, optional): Enter the country where your startup was founded.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of your website.
* **LinkedIn Profile** (`linkedin_profile`, `text`, optional): Enter your LinkedIn profile URL.
* **Instagram Profile** (`instagram_profile`, `text`, optional): Enter your Instagram profile URL.
* **Twitter Profile** (`twitter_profile`, `text`, optional): Enter your Twitter profile URL.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about your startup.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the program administrators for support.
