# emotional_intelligence_workshop_admission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for an Emotional Intelligence Workshop. It collects information about the participant, their contact details, and preferences for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the text field marked "Participant Name".
2. Enter your email address in the text field marked "participant_email".
3. Enter your phone number in the text field marked "Participant Phone".
4. If you have a birthdate, enter it in the date field marked "Date of Birth". This is optional.
5. Select the dates you can attend the workshop from the multiple-choice options in the field marked "Workshops".
6. Select your workshop preferences from the options in the field marked "Workshop Preferences". You can choose only one preference.
7. Enter any statement or comments about yourself and the workshop in the text field marked "Participant Statement". This is optional.
8. Select your agreement to participate in the workshop from the options in the field marked "Agreement". You must select one of the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (participant_name, text, required)**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the workshop.
* **Participant Email (participant_email, email, required)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Participant Phone (phone, text, required)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth, date, optional)**: If you have a birthdate, enter it to help us tailor the workshop content.
* **Workshops (workshop_dates, select_multiple, required)**: Select all the workshop dates you can attend.
* **Workshop Preferences (workshop_preferences, select_one, optional)**: Select your preferred workshop style.
* **Participant Statement (participant_statement, note, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about yourself and the workshop.
* **Agreement (agreement, select_one, required)**: Select "I agree" to participate in the workshop.
