# legal-studies-training-enrollment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for enrolling students in legal studies training programs. Please fill out the form with your information to complete the enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with your personal and enrollment information.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled out (if any).
3.  Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Propective Student Information** (`prospective_student_information`, text, optional): Please enter your personal details for this form to be associated with your enrollment.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it is registered on your official documents (e.g., passport).
*   **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
*   **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD format).
*   **Program** (`program`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are enrolling in a program.
*   **Program Level** (`program_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'High' or 'Low' to specify your program level.
*   **Course** (`course`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to enroll or not enroll in the course.
*   **Enrollment Fee** (`enrollment_fee`, number, optional): Enter the enrollment fee you are paying.
*   **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you paid the enrollment fee (if applicable).
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you paid via credit card, online transfer, etc.
*   **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate your payment status.
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you paid for the enrollment (if applicable).
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your enrollment.

Please note that some of the fields might not be required for the actual form behavior but are included for the sake of completeness and to provide more clarity to the end-users.
