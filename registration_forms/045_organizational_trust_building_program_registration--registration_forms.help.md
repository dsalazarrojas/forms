# Organizational Trust Building Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Organizational Trust Building Program Registration form is designed to gather information from participants about their details, scheduling preferences, and expectations from the training program. This form is a crucial part of the registration process, ensuring that all participants have a clear understanding of what to expect and that their scheduling preferences are taken into account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with your participant details, including your name and email address.
2. Select your role in the organization (e.g., employee, manager, etc.).
3. Describe your expectations from the training program.
4. Choose your preferred schedule date and time.
5. If necessary, provide any participant requirements.
6. Indicate your preferences for the trainer.
7. Enter the name of the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address.
* **Role**: Select your position within the organization (e.g., employee, manager, etc.).
* **Expectations**: Describe your expectations from the training program, including what you hope to achieve or learn.
* **Scheduling Preferences**: Choose your preferred schedule date and time for the training program.
* **Schedule Date**: Choose a date for the training program.
* **Schedule Time**: Choose a time for the training program.
* **Participant Requirements**: If you have any specific requirements or needs, describe them here.
* **Trainer Preferences**: Describe your preferences for the trainer, including any specific skills or experience you would like them to have.
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the trainer you would like to work with, if known.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible in your answers to ensure that your needs and preferences are accurately met.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the form administrators.
* Please fill in all required fields (marked as required: true) to ensure your submission is complete.
