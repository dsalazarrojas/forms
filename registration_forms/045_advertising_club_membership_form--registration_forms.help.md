# Advertising Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in joining the Advertising Club, a professional organization for networking and professional development in the advertising industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your full name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your professional information, such as your current company name, job title, and years of experience in the industry.
3. Choose your membership type (Individual, Corporate, Student, Senior, Emeritus, or Other) and membership duration (Annual, Multi-year, or Lifetime).
4. Select the local chapter you would like to join.
5. Specify your professional interests and networking goals.
6. Choose how you would like to pay for your membership (Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, or Company Reimbursement).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id: 2, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 3, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Address** (id: 4, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 5, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (id: 6, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Company Name** (id: 8, required): Enter the name of your current company.
* **Job Title** (id: 9, required): Enter your current job title in the advertising industry.
* **Industry Sector** (id: 10, required): Select the industry sector you work in (Advertising/Marketing, Technology, Entertainment, Retail, Healthcare, Finance, Education, or Other).
* **Years of Experience** (id: 11, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in the advertising industry.
* **Membership Type** (id: 13, required): Choose the type of membership you are applying for (Individual, Corporate, Student, Senior, Emeritus, or Other).
* **Membership Duration** (id: 14, required): Choose how long you would like your membership to last (Annual, Multi-year, or Lifetime).
* **Chapter Affiliation** (id: 15, required): Enter the local chapter you would like to join.
* **Professional Interests** (id: 17, required): Select the aspects of advertising that interest you most (Digital Marketing, Brand Strategy, Creative Development, Media Planning, Analytics, Account Management, or Other).
* **Networking Goals** (id: 18, required): Specify your networking goals through the club.
* **Payment Method** (id: 20, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your membership (Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, or Company Reimbursement).
