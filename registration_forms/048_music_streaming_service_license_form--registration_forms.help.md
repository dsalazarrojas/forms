# music_streaming_service_license_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a music streaming service license application. It is intended for artists and their authorized representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct license type from the available options.
3. Provide the necessary information about the territories and platforms where the music will be streamed.
4. Enter the start and end dates and times of the streaming service.
5. Upload or enter images for the artist and manager.
6. Provide information about the music files and their URLs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title** (music_streaming_service_license_form, required): Enter a brief title for the music streaming service license.
* **license_type** (license_type, required): Select the type of license being applied for.
* **license_term** (license_term, required): Enter the length of time the license will be in effect.
* **territory** (territory, not required): Select the territories where the music will be streamed.
* **territory_list** (territory_list, not required): Confirm or deny the selected territories.
* **territory_description** (territory_description, required): Provide a description for the selected territories.
* **license_start_date** (license_start_date, not required): Enter the start date of the streaming service.
* **license_end_date** (license_end_date, not required): Enter the end date of the streaming service.
* **license_start_time** (license_start_time, not required): Enter the start time of the streaming service.
* **license_end_time** (license_end_time, not required): Enter the end time of the streaming service.
* **artist_name** (artist_name, required): Enter the name of the artist.
* **artist_image** (artist_image, not required): Upload an image for the artist.
* **music_files** (music_files, not required): Enter information about the music files.
* **music_file_urls** (music_file_urls, not required): Enter URLs for the music files.
* **streaming_platforms** (streaming_platforms, not required): Select the platforms where the music will be streamed.
* **streaming_platform_list** (streaming_platform_list, not required): Confirm or deny the selected platforms.
* **streaming_platform_description** (streaming_platform_description, not required): Provide a description for the selected platforms.
* **streaming_start_date** (streaming_start_date, not required): Enter the start date of the streaming service.
* **streaming_end_date** (streaming_end_date, not required): Enter the end date of the streaming service.
* **streaming_start_time** (streaming_start_time, not required): Enter the start time of the streaming service.
* **streaming_end_time** (streaming_end_time, not required): Enter the end time of the streaming service.
* **artist_manager** (artist_manager, not required): Enter the name of the artist's manager.
* **manager_image** (manager_image, not required): Upload an image for the manager.
* **manager_name** (manager_name, not required): Enter the name of the manager.
* **manager_description** (manager_description, not required): Provide a description for the manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* If any fields are unsure or unclear, contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
