# Store Layout Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Layout Optimization Registration Form is a tool designed to gather information from store owners or participants in store layout optimization processes. This form aims to gather relevant details about stores, which will help in optimizing their layout for better customer satisfaction and overall retail experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Registration Form** section with your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2.  In the **participant_info** section, provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the store layout optimization process.
3.  In the **store_info** section, provide details about the store where you are involved in the layout optimization process. This could be your own store or the store where you work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (id: 1, type: text, required: false): This is the registration form section where you can fill out your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **participant_info** (id: 2, type: text, required: false): This section is for providing any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the store layout optimization process.
* **store_info** (id: 3, type: text, required: false): This section is for providing details about the store where you are involved in the layout optimization process. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure we can contact you back.
* If you are providing your own store information, ensure that you are the authorized person to do so.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Double check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
