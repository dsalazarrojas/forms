# Mentorship Program Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants and mentors in the Mentorship Program. The collected data will be used by program administrators and coordinators to evaluate the program's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Program Details" section with the relevant information about the program.
2. Enter the participant's details in the "Participant Details" section.
3. Provide the mentor's details in the "Mentor Details" section.
4. Describe the program's outcomes in the "Program Outcomes" section.
5. Evaluate the program by providing feedback in the "Evaluation" section.
6. Review and agree to the participant's terms of use in the "Participant Agreement" section.
7. Review and agree to the mentor's terms of use in the "Mentor Agreement" section.
8. Click the "Submit Form" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Program Details
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, not required): This section is for entering information about the program, such as its title, description, and any other relevant details.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, not required): This section is for entering information about the participant, such as their name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Mentor Details** (`mentor_details`, text, not required): This section is for entering information about the mentor, such as their name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Program Outcomes** (`program_outcomes`, text, not required): This section is for describing the program's outcomes, such as what was achieved, what was learned, and any other relevant information.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, text, not required): This section is for evaluating the program, providing feedback, and suggesting areas for improvement.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, text, not required): This section is for reviewing and agreeing to the participant's terms of use.
* **Mentor Agreement** (`mentor_agreement`, text, not required): This section is for reviewing and agreeing to the mentor's terms of use.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, not required): This is the final step to submit the form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all relevant sections to provide accurate and comprehensive feedback.
* Review the participant's and mentor's terms of use carefully before agreeing to them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program administrators or coordinators.
