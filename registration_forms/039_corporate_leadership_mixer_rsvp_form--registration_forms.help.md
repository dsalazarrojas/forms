# Corporate Leadership Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for guests to RSVP to the Corporate Leadership Mixer event. Please fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For guests you are bringing, fill out their information as well.
3. Select the correct RSVP options for yourself and your guests.
4. Enter any additional message or comments about your attendance or any guests you are bringing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the event materials.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Will you be attending?** (`rsvp`, select_one, required): Select either "True" or "False" to indicate whether you will be attending the event.
* **Guest Name (2)** (`guest_name`, text, optional): If you are bringing a guest, enter their name.
* **Guest E-mail** (`guest_email`, email, optional): If you are bringing a guest, enter their email address.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): If you are bringing a guest, enter their phone number.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Include any additional comments or messages about your attendance or the guests you are bringing.
* **Guest Preferences** (`guest_preferences`, text, optional): If you are bringing a guest, provide any dietary or accessibility preferences for them.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): Enter your position or title within your company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team you belong to within your company.
* **What's your relationship with the guest?** (`guest_relationship`, text, optional): If you are bringing a guest, describe your relationship with them (e.g., colleague, client, friend).
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your RSVP (this will be automatically populated based on the event date).
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your RSVP (this will be automatically populated based on the event time).
* **RSVP Message** (`rsvp_message`, text, optional): Include any additional comments about your RSVP or any guests you are bringing.
