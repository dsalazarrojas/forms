# Film Camp Detailed Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a film camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your participant name.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your contact number (if applicable).
4. Select the name of the camp you are registering for.
5. Enter your registration date.
6. Choose your participant type (you may select multiple options).
7. If your participant type is not listed, you can select "other" and provide a custom type.
8. Add any additional notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached (if applicable).
* **Camp Name** (camp_name, select_one, required): Select the name of the camp you are registering for from the list.
* **Registration Date** (registration_date, date, required): Enter the date you are registering for the camp.
* **Participant Type** (participant_type, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of participant you are (e.g., student, instructor, etc.). You can select multiple options.
* **Custom Participant Type** (participant_type_other, text, optional): If your participant type is not listed, select "other" and enter your custom type.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your registration.
