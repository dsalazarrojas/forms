# Community Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about community groups for registration purposes. Please provide accurate and relevant details about your group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Answer each field accurately and truthfully.
3. For select_one fields, choose the most suitable option for your group.
4. For select_multiple fields, select all applicable options.
5. Enter text fields in the format specified (e.g., email addresses should be formatted as `example@example.com`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Name (1)**: Enter the official name of your group.
* **Contact First Name (2)**: Enter the legal first name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Last Name (3)**: Enter the legal last name of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address (4)**: Enter the primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Enter the primary contact phone number.
* **Group Type (6)**: Select the type of group you represent (e.g., Sports Team, Hobby Club, etc.).
* **Number of Members (7)**: Enter an approximate number of members in your group.
* **Meeting Frequency (8)**: Select how often your group meets (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, etc.).
* **Group Description (9)**: Enter a brief description of your group.
* **Group Goals (10)**: Enter your main objectives for the group. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Space Requirements (11)**: Select the type of space you need for your group.
* **Preferred Meeting Days (12)**: Select all days that work best for your group's meetings.
* **Preferred Meeting Time (13)**: Select the time of day that works best for your group's meetings.
* **Is Membership Open to New Members (14)**: Indicate if your group is open to new members.
* **Membership Requirements (15)**: If applicable, enter any requirements for joining your group.
* **Membership Dues or Fees (16)**: If applicable, enter any membership costs.
* **Age Restrictions (17)**: Select any age restrictions for joining your group.
* **Accessibility Needs (18)**: If your group requires accommodations, enter a description.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (19)**: If you'd like, select how you learned about this registration form. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Additional Comments (20)**: Enter any other information about your group. (Note: This field is optional.)

Note: For any field with an option list, please select the most suitable option for your group. If none of the options suit your needs, please select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
