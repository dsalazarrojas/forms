# osha_certification_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for registering certification training programs. The purpose of this form is to gather data on employees who have completed or are enrolled in specific training programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone).
2. Select the relevant information about your training program (Training Program, Training Date, Certification Type, Training Status).
3. Add any additional comments or notes about your training (Training Notes).
4. If applicable, provide information about the assigned tool or dates (Assigned Tool, Assigned Date).
5. Fill in the submission and update information (Submitted On, Submitted By, Created By, Updated By, Deleted By, Last Updated By).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please fill in your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please fill in your phone number.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, required): Select the specific training program you are enrolled in or have completed.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Fill in the date of your training completion or enrollment.
* **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of certification you have or are seeking.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your training (Active or Inactive).
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, required): Fill in the course ID number.
* **Training Notes** (`training_notes`, note, false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your training.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, false): If applicable, provide information about the tool assigned to you for the training.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, false): If applicable, fill in the date the tool was assigned.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, false): Fill in the date you submitted this form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, false): Fill in the name of the person who submitted this form.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, false): Fill in the name of the person who created this form.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, date, false): Fill in the date this form was created.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, false): Fill in the name of the person who last updated this form.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, date, false): Fill in the date this form was last updated.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, text, false): Fill in the name of the person who deleted this form.
* **Deleted Date** (`deleted_date`, date, false): Fill in the date this form was deleted.
* **Last Updated By** (`last_updated_by`, text, false): Fill in the name of the person who last updated this form.
* **Last Modified** (`last_modified`, date, false): Fill in the date this form was last modified.
* **Last Modified By** (`last_modified_by`, text, false): Fill in the name of the person who last modified this form.
* **Last Modified Date** (`last_modified_date`, date, false): Fill in the date this form was last modified.
