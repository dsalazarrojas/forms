# MBA Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from candidates for the MBA program enrollment. Please use this guide to ensure that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your candidate details, including any relevant background information.
2. Provide your contact information.
3. Indicate your interests in the program.
4. Upload any required documents, such as your resume and transcript.
5. Select your preferred campus and program length.
6. Confirm your email address and phone number.
7. Verify your password (if required).
8. Check any relevant checkboxes.
9. Select your preferred language and upload any additional images or videos.
10. Finally, submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`): This section is to provide your personal details, such as name, date of birth, and address.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Please enter your email address and phone number to enable us to contact you about your application.
* **Program Interests** (`program_interests`): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in the MBA program, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`): Please enter your highest level of education completed, including the name of the institution and date of completion.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`): Briefly describe your work experience, including job title, company, and duration.
* **Resume Upload** (`upload_resume`): Upload your up-to-date resume to support your application.
* **Transcript Upload** (`upload_transcript`): Upload your transcript from your previous educational institution.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`): Re-enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`): Re-enter your phone number for verification purposes.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`): Re-enter your password for verification purposes (if required).
* **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`): Enter the date you are available to start the program.
* **Time** (`time`): Select your preferred time for the program.
* **Note** (`note`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Note (optional)** (`note_text`): Enter any additional comments or notes (optional).
* **Program** (`program`): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in the program.
* **Campus** (`campus`): Select 'Yes' if you prefer to attend the program on-campus.
* **Program Length** (`program_length`): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in a program of a certain length.
* **Language** (`language`): Select 'Yes' if you prefer to communicate in a certain language.
* **Upload Image** (`upload_image`): Upload any additional images to support your application.
* **Upload Video** (`upload_video`): Upload any additional videos to support your application.
* **Checkboxes** (`confirm_checkbox1`, `confirm_checkbox2`, `confirm_checkbox3`, `confirm_checkbox4`): Select 'Yes' for each checkbox that applies to your application.
* **Upload Resume**, **Upload Transcript** (`upload_resume`, `upload_transcript`): Upload any required documents, such as your resume and transcript.
