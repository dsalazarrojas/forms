# talent_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Registration Form is designed for users to register their talents and provide essential information about themselves. This form is used for internal purposes, such as tracking user registration and talent pool management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Talent Registration Form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your title in the "Talent Registration Form" field.
2. Upload or attach your resume in the "Resume" field.
3. Add a profile photo in the "Profile Photo" field.
4. Provide additional details about yourself in the "Additional Details" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (note that this should be a valid email address).
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional but recommended for better communication).
7. If you need to provide any further information, use the "Note" field (optional).
