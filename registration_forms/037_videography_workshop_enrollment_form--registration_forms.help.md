# Videography Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Videography Workshop Enrollment Form is designed to collect registration information from participants for the Videography Workshop. This form helps us understand your interests, skills, and needs to tailor the workshop experience to your expectations. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with your correct and accurate information.
3. Double-check your input for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information (Page 1)** (`registration_info`, text, **optional**): Enter your basic contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Workshop Details (Page 2)** (`workshop_details`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional details about the workshop that interests you, such as your experience level or equipment preferences.
* **Registration Details (Page 3)** (`registration_details`, text, **optional**): Enter any registration information specific to the workshop, like workshop dates and times.
* **Additional Information (Page 4)** (`additional_info`, text, **optional**): Share any other information about yourself that might help us tailor the workshop experience, such as your availability or special requirements.
* **Review (Page 5)** (`review`, text, **optional**): Please review your completed form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
