# religious_reunion_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting information from attendees of a religious reunion event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your main information, which will help us understand your background and connection with the religious group.
2. Provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you for any updates or information about the event.
3. Choose your RSVP status for the event by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
4. If your RSVP status is 'No', you can provide a brief message explaining your reason for not attending.
5. If you are able to provide an RSVP date and time, select 'Yes' and fill in the date and time.
6. If you need to provide any additional comments, you can do so in the 'Additional Comments' section.
7. In case of 'Other' RSVP status, provide the details in 'RSVP Status Other' and 'RSVP Status Other Note' fields.
8. For any special requests, you can provide them in the 'Special Requests' section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Information**: Please provide your name, any relevant religious background, and a brief family background.
* **Contact Information**: 
  * **Contact Name**: Please provide your name or the name of the person you are registering on behalf of.
  * **Contact Email**: This is the email address we will use to contact you about the event.
  * **Contact Phone**: This is your contact phone number.
  * **Contact Address**: Provide your address for any mail or package delivery.
  * **Contact City**: The city where you reside.
  * **Contact State**: The state where you reside.
  * **Contact Zip**: The zip code of your residence.
* **RSVP Choice**:
  * **Yes**: You are attending the event.
  * **No**: You are not attending the event. Please provide a brief message explaining your reason.
* **RSVP Date**: Select 'Yes' if you can RSVP with a date. 
* **RSVP Time**: Select 'Yes' if you can RSVP with a time.
* **RSVP Date** (If 'Yes' is selected): Select a date for the event you will be attending.
* **RSVP Time** (If 'Yes' is selected): Select a time for the event you will be attending.
* **RSVP Status Other**: If your RSVP status is not listed, please choose 'Other' and provide the details in the 'RSVP Status Other' field.
* **RSVP Status Other**: Provide the details of your RSVP status.
* **RSVP Status Other Note**: If you need to provide additional information for your RSVP status, you can do so here.
* **RSVP Date Other**: If your RSVP status is not listed but you can provide a date, select 'Yes' and fill in the date.
* **RSVP Time Other**: If your RSVP status is not listed but you can provide a time, select 'Yes' and fill in the time.
* **RSVP Status Note**: If you need to provide a note for your RSVP status, you can do so here.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any further comments about the event or the registration process, please provide them here.
* **Religious Background**: Provide a brief description of your religious background.
* **Family Background**: Provide a brief description of your family background.
* **Other Background**: Provide any other relevant background information.
