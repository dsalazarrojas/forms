# Activities Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to register their child for activities such as sports camps, art classes, music lessons, and more. It collects essential information to ensure a smooth registration process and effective communication with the organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure successful submission.
2. Enter the child's legal name, date of birth, and other personal details in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide the parent or guardian's contact information, including their phone number and email address.
4. Specify an emergency contact person and their contact details in case of emergencies.
5. Choose the activity the child wishes to participate in from the options provided.
6. Select the specific dates or time period for the activity, if applicable.
7. Indicate if the child has any special needs or accommodations required.
8. List any medical conditions or allergies the child has, if applicable.
9. Choose the preferred method of transportation for the child to and from the activity.
10. Select the payment method for the registration fee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child's Full Name (1)**: Enter the child's legal name as it appears on their passport or birth certificate.
* **Child's Date of Birth (2)**: Enter the child's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (3)**: Enter the full name of the parent or guardian registering the child.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone (4)**: Enter the primary phone number for the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email (5)**: Enter the email address for communication regarding the activity.
* **Emergency Contact Name (6)**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (7)**: Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Activity Choice (8)**: Select the activity the child wishes to participate in from the options provided.
* **Activity Dates (9)**: Specify the specific dates or time period for the chosen activity, if applicable.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations (10)**: Check 'Yes' if the child requires any special needs or accommodations.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies (11)**: List any medical conditions or allergies the child has, if applicable.
* **Transportation Arrangements (12)**: Choose the preferred method of transportation for the child to and from the activity.
* **Registration Fee Payment (13)**: Select the preferred method of payment for the registration fee.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accuracy before submission to avoid errors.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If the child has any medical conditions or allergies, please list them clearly for us to provide necessary accommodations.
