# Nursing Program Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users to attend the Nursing Program Workshop. It will help the organization manage workshop attendees and provide necessary information for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Select which day you will attend the workshop.
5. Optionally, enter the number of applicants (e.g., how many people will be attending with you).
6. If you have any personal information you'd like to share with the organizers, enter it in the "Personal information" field.
7. Add any comments or notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, such as "John".
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, such as "Doe".
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, such as "john.doe@example.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, including the country code (e.g., "+1 123 456 7890").
* **Which day will you attend?** (`attending_day`, select_one, optional): Select which day you will attend the workshop. Choose from the available options:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
* **Number of applicants** (`number_of_applicants`, number, optional): If you are bringing anyone with you, enter the number of attendees, including yourself.
* **Personal information** (`personal_info`, note, optional): If you have any personal information you'd like to share with the organizers, enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about your registration, such as special requests or accommodations.

## Tips
* Please double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask the organizers for clarification.
* Please be aware that your registration is not complete until you have submitted the form.
