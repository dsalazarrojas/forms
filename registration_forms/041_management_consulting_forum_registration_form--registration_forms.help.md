## Management Consulting Forum Registration Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

The purpose of this form is to allow individuals to register their information and provide their consent for participating in a management consulting forum. This form helps us understand who is attending and ensures we have their up-to-date contact information and consent.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your consent status from the options provided in the "Consent" field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents. Please ensure it is spelled correctly.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format of +X XXX XXX XXXX, where X represents digits 0-9. This will help us reach out to you with any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the standard format of `username@domain.com`. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **Consent**: Select "I agree" if you are consenting to participate in the management consulting forum. Select "I do not agree" if you are not consenting.
