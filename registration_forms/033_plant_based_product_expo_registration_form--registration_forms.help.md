# Plant Based Product Expo Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for exhibitors to register for the Plant Based Product Expo. Please ensure you have all required documents and information ready for a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Event Name (field 1) with the name of your event or booth name.
2. Enter your Exhibitor Name (field 3), Company Name (field 4), and Email (field 5) details.
3. Select your Exhibitor Product Categories (field 7) for the products you will be showcasing.
4. Enter a brief description of your products in Exhibitor Product Description (field 8).
5. Confirm your Booth Number (field 9) where you will be located.
6. If applicable, provide any Special Request (field 10) for accommodations or services.
7. Enter the Event Start Date (field 11), Start Time (field 12), End Date (field 13), and End Time (field 14) of your event.
8. Enter the Event Location (field 15) and Event Description (field 16).
9. Indicate if this is a public Event (field 17).
10. Provide a link to your Attendance List (field 18), if applicable.
11. Confirm your Exhibitor Attendance Confirmation (field 20) and Agreement (field 21) with the terms of service.
12. Indicate your agreement with the Exhibitor Terms of Service (field 22) and Additional Terms (field 23).
13. Finally, provide your Exhibitor Signature (field 24) to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Name (field 1)
* **Event Name** (`text`, `required`, `true`): Enter the name of your event or booth name.

### Exhibitor Name (field 3)
* **Exhibitor Name** (`text`, `required`, `true`): Enter your company name or name as it appears on your business card.

### Exhibitor Company (field 4)
* **Exhibitor Company** (`text`, `required`, `true`): Enter your company name as it appears on your business card.

### Exhibitor Email (field 5)
* **Exhibitor Email** (`email`, `required`, `true`): Enter your company email address.

### Exhibitor Phone (field 4)
* **Exhibitor Phone** (`text`, `required`, `false`): Enter your company phone number.

### Exhibitor Website (field 6)
* **Exhibitor Website** (`text`, `optional`, `false`): Enter your company website URL.

### Exhibitor Product Categories (field 7)
* **Exhibitor Product Categories** (`select_multiple`, `required`, `true`): Select the categories your products fall under.

### Exhibitor Product Description (field 8)
* **Exhibitor Product Description** (`text`, `required`, `true`): Enter a brief description of your products.

### Booth Number (field 9)
* **Booth Number** (`text`, `required`, `true`): Confirm your assigned booth number.

### Special Request (field 10)
* **Special Request** (`text`, `optional`, `false`): If you require any special arrangements or services, enter here.

### Event Start Date (field 11)
* **Event Start Date** (`date`, `required`, `true`): Enter the start date of your event.

### Event Start Time (field 12)
* **Event Start Time** (`time`, `required`, `true`): Enter the start time of your event.

### Event End Date (field 13)
* **Event End Date** (`date`, `required`, `true`): Enter the end date of your event.

### Event End Time (field 14)
* **Event End Time** (`time`, `required`, `true`): Enter the end time of your event.

### Event Location (field 15)
* **Event Location** (`text`, `required`, `true`): Enter the location of your event.

### Event Description (field 16)
* **Event Description** (`text`, `required`, `true`): Enter a brief description of your event.

### Event Type (field 17)
* **Event Type** (`select_multiple`, `required`, `true`): Confirm if your event is public or not.

### Attendance List (field 18)
* **Attendance List** (`text`, `optional`, `false`): If you have an attendance list, provide a link here.

### Exhibitor Attendance Confirmation (field 20)
* **Exhibitor Attendance Confirmation** (`select_one`, `required`, `true`): Confirm your attendance at the event.

### Exhibitor Agreement (field 21)
* **Exhibitor Agreement** (`select_one`, `required`, `true`): Confirm you agree to the terms of service.

### Exhibitor Terms of Service (field 22)
* **Exhibitor Terms of Service** (`select_one`, `required`, `true`): Confirm you agree to the terms of service.

### Exhibitor Additional Terms (field 23)
* **Exhibitor Additional Terms** (`select_one`, `required`, `true`): Confirm you agree to the additional terms.

### Exhibitor Signature (field 24)
* **Exhibitor Signature** (`text`, `required`, `true`): Enter your signature to confirm your registration.

### Assigned Tool (field 25)
* **Assigned Tool** (`text`, `optional`, `false`): This field is likely a placeholder for an assigned tool or equipment.

Please note that the fields marked as `required` must be completed, while optional fields can be left blank or filled as needed.
