# School Registration Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general school registration information form designed to collect basic data about students and their parents for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out your name as a student or parent.
2. Enter your school year or the year you are currently in.
3. Provide your valid email address for contact purposes.
4. Enter the name of the person we can contact in case we need to reach out to a parent or guardian.
5. Enter the email address of the person who will be contacted (usually a parent or guardian).
6. Select your current grade level (from 1st to 5th grade).
7. Enter your date of birth.
8. Add the phone number of the person who can be contacted for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your name.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, number, **Optional**): Enter the year you are currently in.
* **Student E-mail** (`student_email`, email, **Required**): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent Contact Name** (`parent_contact_name`, text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the person we can contact.
* **Parent E-mail** (`parent_email`, email, **Optional**): Enter the email address of the person who can be contacted.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, **Optional**): Select your current grade level (from 1st to 5th grade).
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, **Optional**): Enter your date of birth.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter the phone number of the person who can be contacted.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the school or the person in charge for guidance.
- Be accurate when entering your date of birth to ensure correct record-keeping.
