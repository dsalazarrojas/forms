# team_building_activity_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for team building activities reservations. Please fill out the form with the required information to reserve a team building activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the activity you'd like to reserve.
2. Choose a date for the activity.
3. Select a time for the activity.
4. Indicate if participants are expected or not.
5. Enter the names of the participants.
6. Provide the contact person's information.
7. Enter the contact person's email address.
8. Enter the contact person's phone number.
9. Enter the name of the organization or group associated with the activity.
10. Enter the address of the location where the activity will take place.
11. Provide a brief description of the activity.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you're filling out the form for an activity you're authorized to reserve.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or administrator for clarification.
