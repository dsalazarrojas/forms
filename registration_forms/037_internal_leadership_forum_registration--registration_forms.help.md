# Internal Leadership Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for internal leadership registration, allowing employees or leadership team members to sign up for various sessions, meetings, or events within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user details in the first section.
2. Specify your session preferences in the second section.
3. Provide logistics information (e.g., transportation, accommodation) in the third section.
4. Add any additional notes in the fourth section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Details
* **User Details** (user_details, text, required/optional): This section collects basic information about you, such as your name, contact details, or any other relevant user information. 

### 2. Session Preferences
* **Session Preferences** (session_preferences, text, required/optional): Here, you can specify which sessions you are interested in attending, any specific preferences (e.g., language, location) for those sessions, or any other session-related details.

### 3. Logistics
* **Logistics** (logistics, text, required/optional): This section is for providing information on how you plan to get to the sessions, such as transportation methods, accommodation arrangements, or other logistics-related details.

### 4. Notes
* **Notes** (notes, note, required/optional): If you have any additional comments, questions, or concerns that you'd like to share with the organizers, please add them here. 

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If a field is not marked as required but you wish to provide more information, you can add it here.
* Double-check your information before submission to ensure it is accurate.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
