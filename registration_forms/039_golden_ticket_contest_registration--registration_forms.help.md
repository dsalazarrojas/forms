# Golden Ticket Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register themselves for the Golden Ticket Contest. The contest is open to individuals who are 18 or older and reside in the United States, Canada, or Mexico. Please confirm that you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the First name field with your given name.
2. Fill out the Last name field with your family name.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. If provided, enter your phone number in the Phone field. This is optional.
5. Select your Country of residence from the dropdown list.
6. If you select a country, you must also select your State of residence from the list.
7. Enter your Zip code in the Zip field.
8. Confirm that you are 18 or older in the Eligibility field.
9. Click the Submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`name`, `text`, required): Your given name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Your family name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number (if provided).
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Select your country of residence from the list.
* **State** (`state`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your state of residence from the list.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required): Your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Are you 18 or older?** (`eligibility`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are 18 or older.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to complete your registration.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms`, `note`, optional): Please read and agree to our Terms and Conditions.
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy`, `note`, optional): Please read and agree to our Privacy Policy.
