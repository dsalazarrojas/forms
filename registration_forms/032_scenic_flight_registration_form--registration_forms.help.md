# Scenic-Flight-Registration-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scenic-flight-registration-form is a form designed to gather information from users related to scenic flight registration. It's used to collect personal details, tour selections, date and time, flight details, payment methods, contact information, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in the following order:
	* Personal Info: Enter your personal details such as your name, address, and contact number.
	* Select Tour: Choose the tour you are interested in by selecting the relevant options.
	* Date and Time: Enter the date and time of your scenic flight.
	* Flight Details: Provide any additional information about your flight.
	* Payment Method: Select the payment method you plan to use (Yes or No).
	* Confirm Reservation: Confirm your reservation by selecting a time.
	* Contact Info: Enter your contact information for communication purposes.
	* Additional Services: Select any additional services you require (Yes or No).
	* Emergency Contact: Specify an emergency contact person (Yes or No).
	* Special Requests: Enter any special requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other personal details you feel are relevant.
* **Select Tour** (`tour_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the tour you are interested in by selecting the relevant options (Yes or No).
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of your scenic flight. Please note that this field is date-based and not time-based.
* **Flight Details** (`flight_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your flight, such as flight type, duration, or any special requirements.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method you plan to use (Yes or No). This will help us determine how to process your payment.
* **Confirm Reservation** (`confirm_reservation`, time, optional): Confirm your reservation by selecting a time. This will help us ensure that your reservation is confirmed and ready for processing.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information for communication purposes.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services you require (Yes or No).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Specify an emergency contact person (Yes or No).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests you may have.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accurate information is provided.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify them in the 'Special Requests' field.
* Make sure to choose the correct payment method to avoid any confusion.
* If you need assistance with any of the fields or have questions, feel free to contact us.
