# Summer School Enrollment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help parents or guardians register their child for a summer school program. It collects necessary information for processing and verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Summer School Enrollment Form**: Enter the title of the form.
2. **Parent Information**: Provide the parent/guardian's name and contact details.
3. **Student Information**: Enter the student's name, email, and phone number.
4. **Course Selection**: Choose the relevant course option from the drop-down menu.
5. **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
6. **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
7. **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
8. **Course Duration**: Select the duration of the course in days.
9. **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the course.
10. **End Date**: Enter the end date of the course.
11. **Submit**: Once all fields are filled, click the "Submit" button to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer School Enrollment Form** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the form, e.g., "John Doe - Summer School Enrollment."
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the parent/guardian's name and contact details.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name, email, and phone number.
* **Course Selection** (`course_selection`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the relevant course option from the drop-down menu.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, number, required/optional): Select the duration of the course in days.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required/optional): Once all fields are filled, click the "Submit" button to complete the registration process.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
* Ensure that you've filled all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant authorities or contact information provided on this form.
