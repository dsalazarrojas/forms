# trade-season-pass-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trade-season-pass-registration form is used to collect user information for trade season pass registration. This form is designed to gather essential details from users to process their pass registration efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your general information, which may include your name, contact details, or other relevant personal data.
* **Pass Type** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of pass you are applying for from the options provided. This could be a Single Pass, Couple Pass, or Family Pass.
* **Pass Preferences** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the pass preferences that best suit your needs. You can select one or multiple options, such as a Weekend Pass, Full Season Pass, or Single Weekend.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are only submitting this form once with accurate and complete information.
* Double-check your selected pass type and preferences to guarantee the correct pass is applied to your account.
* If you have any doubts or issues, contact the relevant event or festival organizers for assistance.
