# Library Membership Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a library membership application. It will be used to determine if the applicant is eligible for library membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" if you are eligible for library membership.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter your birthplace in the "Birthplace" field.
8. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
9. Select "Yes" for "Library Access" if you are eligible for library membership.
10. Select "Yes" for "Resource Access" if you have access to the library resources.
11. Select "Yes" for "Terms Accepted" if you agree to the library terms and conditions.
12. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
13. Enter any additional fields as required in the "Additional Fields" field.
