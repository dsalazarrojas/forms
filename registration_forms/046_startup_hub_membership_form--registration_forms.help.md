# Startup Hub Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Hub Membership Form is designed to collect essential information about your startup to facilitate its registration and integration with our platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, fill out the form by providing your first name, last name, and email address. This information will be used to create your member profile and for communication purposes.
2. If you have a business name, provide it in the "Business Name" field. If not, you can leave this field empty.
3. Enter your phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you in case of any updates or notifications.
4. Select your date of birth to help us understand your age and demographic details.
5. If you're a founder or representative of a business, provide your business address to help us get in touch with you.
6. Enter your name as the founder of the business, if applicable.
7. Briefly describe your business in the "Description" field to help us understand your startup's concept and goals.
8. Finally, describe your membership plan in the "Membership Plan Description" field to help us tailor the resources and support we provide to your business needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your personal last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Select your date of birth to help us understand your age and demographic details.
* **Business Name** (text, optional): Enter your business name if applicable.
* **Business Address** (text, required): Enter your business address for contact and meetup purposes.
* **Founder Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the founder or representative of the business.
* **Description** (text, required): Briefly describe your business to help us understand your startup's concept and goals.
* **Membership Plan Description** (text, required): Describe your membership plan to help us tailor the resources and support we provide to your business needs.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* You can leave the "Business Name" field empty if you don't have a business name.
* Be as descriptive as possible in the "Description" and "Membership Plan Description" fields to help us provide tailored support and resources.
