# Virtual Job Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from attendees and exhibitors for a virtual job fair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (user_info, text, required):
  * Enter your name and contact information.
* **Job Details** (job_details, text, optional):
  * Provide any relevant information about your job or employment opportunity.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, optional):
  * Enter any relevant contact information for your job or employment opportunity.
* **Event Info** (event_info, text, required):
  * Enter any additional event information, such as the job fair title or description.
* **Job Fair** (job_fair, text, required):
  * Enter the name of the job fair you are attending or participating in.
* **Job Type** (job_type, select_one, required):
  * Select one of the options provided to indicate the type of job you are seeking or offering.
* **Job Category** (job_category, select_one, required):
  * Select one of the options provided to indicate the category of job you are seeking or offering.
* **Job Level** (job_level, select_one, required):
  * Select one of the options provided to indicate the level of the job you are seeking or offering.
* **Job Fair Purpose** (job_fair_purpose, select_multiple, optional):
  * Select all that apply to indicate the purpose of the job fair.
* **Job Venue** (job_venue, select_multiple, optional):
  * Select all that apply to indicate the venue of the job fair.
* **Job Target Audience** (job_target_audience, select_multiple, optional):
  * Select all that apply to indicate the target audience for the job fair.
* **Job Format** (job_format, select_multiple, optional):
  * Select all that apply to indicate the format of the job fair.
* **Job Language** (job_language, select_multiple, optional):
  * Select all that apply to indicate the language of the job fair.
* **Job Industry** (job_industry, select_multiple, optional):
  * Select all that apply to indicate the industry of the job fair.
* **Job Fair Location** (job_fair_location, text, optional):
  * Enter the location of the job fair.
* **Job Fair URL** (job_fair_url, text, optional):
  * Enter the URL of the job fair.
* **Job Fair Agenda** (job_fair_agenda, text, optional):
  * Enter a brief description of the job fair agenda.
* **Job Fair Sponsors** (job_fair_sponsors, text, optional):
  * Enter a list of job fair sponsors.
* **Job Fair Partners** (job_fair_partners, text, optional):
  * Enter a list of job fair partners.
* **Job Fair Host** (job_fair_host, text, optional):
  * Enter the name of the job fair host.
* **Job Fair Coordinator** (job_fair_coordinator, text, optional):
  * Enter the name of the job fair coordinator.
* **Job Fair Contact** (job_fair_contact, text, optional):
  * Enter the contact information for the job fair.
* **Job Fair Email** (job_fair_email, text, optional):
  * Enter the email address for the job fair.
* **Job Fair Phone** (job_fair_phone, text, optional):
  * Enter the phone number for the job fair.
* **Job Fair Notes** (job_fair_notes, text, optional):
  * Enter any additional notes about the job fair.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the job fair coordinator.
* Review the help guide for any specific requirements or regulations.
