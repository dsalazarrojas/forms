# Environmental Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering individuals for environmental membership. It's meant for people who are interested in supporting environmental initiatives or want to be part of our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field. Please make sure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your Organization Name (if applicable).
5. Enter your Phone Number (optional).
6. Enter your Address (optional).
7. Enter your City (optional).
8. Select your State from the dropdown list.
9. Enter your Zip Code (optional).
10. Select your Membership Status from the dropdown list.
11. Enter any additional Notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is for your email address. Please make sure it's a valid email address.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If you're filling out this form on behalf of an organization, enter their name here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your address for our records, enter it here.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your city for our records, enter it here.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select your state from the dropdown list.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your zip code for our records, enter it here.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of your membership status options.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information you'd like to provide, enter it here.
