# dog_registration_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is a dog registration form used to collect necessary information about a dog's health, vaccinations, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Camper Info (Camper Info)**: Please enter the dog's name, breed, age, and other relevant information in this field. This section is optional.
2. **Camper Vaccinations (Camper Vaccinations)**: Select all vaccinations that the dog has received, such as Rabies, Distemper, and Leptospirosis. This section is required.
3. **Medical History (Camper Medical History)**: Please enter any relevant medical history about the dog, such as allergies or previous medical conditions. This section is optional.
4. **Camper Medical Notes (Camper Medical Notes)**: Please enter any additional medical notes about the dog. This section is optional.
5. **Additional Information (Camper Additional Information)**: Please enter any additional information about the dog, such as its behavior, dietary needs, or any other relevant details. This section is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper Info (Camper Info)** (`text`, optional): Enter the dog's name, breed, age, and other relevant information.
* **Camper Vaccinations (Camper Vaccinations)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all vaccinations that the dog has received.
	+ Select from: Rabies, Distemper, Leptospirosis
* **Medical History (Camper Medical History)** (`text`, optional): Enter any relevant medical history about the dog.
* **Camper Medical Notes (Camper Medical Notes)** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional medical notes about the dog.
* **Additional Information (Camper Additional Information)** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the dog.
	+ This field can be used for any relevant details about the dog's behavior, dietary needs, or other relevant information.
