# Leadership And Resilience Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Leadership And Resilience Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, not required): This field is for general participant information and can be filled in if necessary.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Company** (`company`, text, not required): If you represent a company, please enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (`role`, text, not required): Please enter your role in the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, not required): Enter the department you belong to in your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your job title.
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Enter your location.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Work** (`time_of_work`, time, not required): Enter your working hours.
* **Note** (`note`, text, not required): Add any additional notes about yourself.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Select Multiple 3** (`select_multiple_3`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Select Multiple 4** (`select_multiple_4`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Select Multiple 5** (`select_multiple_5`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Select Multiple 6** (`select_multiple_6`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Select Multiple 7** (`select_multiple_7`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, not required): Select one option that applies to you.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, select_one, not required): Select one option that applies to you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Select a date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Select a time.
* **Text 1** (`text_1`, text, not required): Enter any additional text.

Note: The fields marked as "not required" can be filled in if you feel it's necessary, but it's not mandatory.
