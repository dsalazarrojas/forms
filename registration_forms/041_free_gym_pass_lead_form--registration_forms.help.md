# Free Gym Pass Lead Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Free Gym Pass Lead Form is a tool used to capture leads from potential gym-goers and gather information about their interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the `Name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Choose your fitness interests from the list in the `Fitness Interests` field.
5. Select how often you plan on visiting the gym in the `Gym Frequency` field.
6. Choose a time of day you plan on visiting the gym in the `Gym Time` field.
7. Select a date for your planned gym visit in the `Gym Date` field.
8. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the `Gym Note` fields.
9. Review and submit the form to send in your lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Information**: This is the main section where you will enter your details, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Name**: Enter your full name to help us identify and contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which we will use to communicate with you about your gym pass.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which we may use to contact you for follow-up questions or to confirm your visit.
* **Fitness Interests**: Select all the types of fitness activities you are interested in, such as Weightlifting, Cardio, Yoga, etc.
* **Gym Frequency**: Choose how often you plan on visiting the gym, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Gym Time**: Select a time of day you plan on visiting the gym, such as 8am, 12pm, etc.
* **Gym Date**: Select a date for your planned gym visit.
* **Gym Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments you have about your visit.
* **Gym Note 2-16**: These are additional note fields where you can enter more specific details or comments about your visit.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your form to send in your lead.
