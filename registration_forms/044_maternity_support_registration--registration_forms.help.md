# Maternity Support Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pregnant or postpartum individuals seeking support. The form is used to identify their support needs and preferences for follow-up contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your contact details, including your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or In-person).
2. Select your current maternity status and the type of support you are seeking.
3. Provide the contact information of the person responsible for your care (Contact Person).
4. Choose the frequency and date for follow-up contact.
5. Provide the location and note for follow-up meetings.
6. If necessary, provide additional details for support needs or follow-up notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Provide your full name and email address. This information will be used to contact you for follow-up support.
* **Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact for support (Email, Phone, or In-person). This will help us tailor our support to your needs.
* **Maternity Status**: Select your current maternity status (First trimester, Second trimester, Third trimester, or Postpartum). This will help us understand your current support needs.
* **Support Needs**: Select the types of support you are seeking (Emotional, Physical, Informational, Financial). This will help us identify your specific needs.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name and email address of the person responsible for your care. This will help us contact the right person for follow-up support.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to be contacted for support (Every 2 weeks or Every 4 weeks). This will help us ensure consistent support.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for your next follow-up meeting. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Support Group**: Select whether you will be part of a support group (True or False). This will help us plan group meetings.
* **Support Group Time**: Enter the time for support group meetings. This will help us schedule meetings that work for you.
* **Support Group Date**: Enter the date for support group meetings. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Submission**: Choose how you would like to submit the form (Submit or Save and Continue Later).
