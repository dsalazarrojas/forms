# Youth Leadership Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for anyone interested in volunteering their time and skills to the Youth Leadership Program. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure you're registered as a volunteer and to help us coordinate your role within the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form). Start with the first page and work your way through the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work or personal email address where you can be reached. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role within the program from the options provided (Volunteer, Mentor, Organizer).
* **Program Date** (`program_date`, date, optional): If you know the specific date of the program, you can enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): If you know the start time of the program, you can enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): If you know the end time of the program, you can enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the program if you're aware of it. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter a contact phone number where we can reach you for program-related matters.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you're registering multiple people, please fill out the form separately for each individual.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, don't hesitate to contact the program administrators.
