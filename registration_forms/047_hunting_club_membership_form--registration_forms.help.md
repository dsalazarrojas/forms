# Hunting Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hunting Club Membership Form" is designed for new members to register their interest in joining the hunting club. This form collects essential information to help you become an active and valued member of our community.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Ensure your email address is valid and active.
- For fields with options, select the most relevant choice to express your preferences.
- You can add any additional comments in the "Note" section to provide more context about your experience or interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid and active email address to communicate with the club and receive updates.
- **Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your physical address for delivery or meeting purposes.
- **Hunting Experience** (`hunting_experience`, `number`, optional): If you have any hunting experience, enter the number of years you have been hunting.
- **Hunt Frequency** (`hunt_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more options to indicate your preferred hunt frequency:
  - Option 1: Daily
  - Option 2: Weekly
  - Option 3: Monthly
- **Favorite Game** (`favorite_game`, `select_one`, optional): Select your favorite game type:
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
- **Join Date** (`join_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to start hunting with us.
- **Join Time** (`join_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you plan to start hunting with us.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience, interests, or any other information to help us better understand your preferences.
- **Membership Terms** (`membership_terms`, `select_one`, required): Accept one of the membership terms:
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out all required fields to avoid delays in processing your membership application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
