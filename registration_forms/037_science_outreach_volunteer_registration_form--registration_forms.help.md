# Science Outreach Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from volunteers who wish to participate in science outreach activities. It helps the organizers collect necessary details about the volunteers' background, availability, and skills.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **User Name** in the first field. This is the name that will be used to identify you as a volunteer.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the second field. This is the contact information that will be used to communicate with you.
3. Provide your **Phone** number in the third field. This is the number that will be used to contact you in case of any updates or emergencies.
4. Specify the **Program Name** you are interested in, if any.
5. Enter your **Volunteer Start Date**, the date you are available to start volunteering.
6. Enter your **Volunteer End Date**, the date you plan to stop volunteering.
7. Indicate your **Volunteer Hours Per Week**, the number of hours you can commit to volunteering each week.
8. List your **Availability Days**, the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
9. Enter your **Availability Time**, the time slots you are available to volunteer.
10. Specify your **Primary Location**, the location where you would like to volunteer.
11. Provide any **Secondary Location**, if you are available to volunteer at multiple locations.
12. Choose your **Volunteer Type** from the options: Volunteer, Staff, or Both.
13. You may add any **Availability Notes**, if you have any specific preferences or requirements.
14. In **Volunteer Education**, you may describe any relevant educational background or experience.
15. In **Volunteer Experience**, you may list any relevant experience or skills you have.
16. In **Volunteer Skills**, you may list any specific skills you possess that would be beneficial for the volunteer role.
17. In **Volunteer Interests**, you may specify any interests that align with the volunteer role.
18. In **Volunteer References**, you may provide any relevant references or contacts.
19. Finally, please sign the **Volunteer Agreement**, indicating your commitment to volunteer.
20. You may also specify the **Tool Used** for your volunteer work, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be identified as a volunteer.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number that can be used for emergencies or updates.
* **Program Name**: If you have a specific program in mind, enter it here.
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer End Date**: Enter the date you plan to stop volunteering.
* **Volunteer Hours Per Week**: Indicate the number of hours you can commit to volunteering each week.
* **Availability Days**: List the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
* **Availability Time**: Indicate the time slots you are available to volunteer.
* **Primary Location**: Enter the location where you would like to volunteer.
* **Secondary Location**: If you are available to volunteer at multiple locations, list them here.
* **Volunteer Type**: Choose from Volunteer, Staff, or Both.
* **Availability Notes**: Add any specific preferences or requirements for volunteering.
* **Volunteer Education**: Describe any relevant educational background or experience.
* **Volunteer Experience**: List any relevant experience or skills you have.
* **Volunteer Skills**: List any specific skills you possess that would be beneficial for the volunteer role.
* **Volunteer Interests**: Specify any interests that align with the volunteer role.
* **Volunteer References**: Provide any relevant references or contacts.
* **Volunteer Agreement**: Sign this to indicate your commitment to volunteer.
* **Tool Used**: If you have specific tools or software used for your volunteer work, enter it here.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to provide all necessary information to ensure your application is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organizers for assistance.
