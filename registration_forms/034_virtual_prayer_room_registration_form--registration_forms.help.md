# Virtual Prayer Room Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interests, preferences, and experiences related to prayer, which will help us tailor the Virtual Prayer Room experience to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each page tab to navigate through the form sections.
2. Fill in the information as prompted for each section.
3. Review each field's label and explanation for clarity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (<name>): This field is for your name and any other relevant details about you (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Prayer Preferences** (<type>): Describe your prayer preferences, such as what you like or dislike about prayer or how you usually approach it.
* **Prayer Habits** (<type>): Share your habits related to prayer, like how often you pray or what time of the day you usually pray.
* **Prayer Experiences** (<type>): Share any significant prayer experiences or events that have had an impact on you.
* **Agreement** (<type>): Sign your name to confirm that you've read and understood the form instructions and guidelines.
* **Signature** (<type>): This is a placeholder for a digital signature, which we will use for verification purposes only.
