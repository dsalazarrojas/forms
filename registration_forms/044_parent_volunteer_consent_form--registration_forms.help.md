# Parent Volunteer Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain parental consent for a child's participation in a volunteer activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and contact information (page 1).
2. Select the volunteer group(s) your child will be participating in (page 2).
3. Provide the date(s) of the volunteer activity (page 2).
4. If applicable, enter the time(s) of the volunteer activity (page 2).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your child's participation (page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Parent Information** (Required: False)
* **parent_information** (parent_info): This is the section where you will enter your name and contact information. Please provide your full name and a valid email address or phone number so we can get in touch with you.

### **Contact Information** (Required: False)
* **contact_information** (contact_info): This section is optional but recommended. Please enter any contact information for you or your child that may help us reach you or your child during the volunteer activity.

### **Volunteer Group** (Required: False)
* **volunteer_group** (Volunteer Group 1, Volunteer Group 2, Volunteer Group 3): Select the volunteer group(s) your child will be participating in. Please choose all that apply.

### **Date** (Required: False)
* **date** (date): This is where you will enter the date of the volunteer activity. Please enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### **Time** (Required: False)
* **time** (time): This is where you will enter the time of the volunteer activity. Please enter the time in the format 12:00 PM for example. If not applicable, leave this field blank.

### **Note** (Required: False)
* **note** (note): This is an optional section where you can add any additional comments or notes about your child's participation. Please keep it brief and clear.
