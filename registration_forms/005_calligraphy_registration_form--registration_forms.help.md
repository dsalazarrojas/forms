# Calligraphy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Calligraphy Registration Form is a crucial part of the class registration process. It collects the necessary information from participants, ensuring that they can successfully attend the class.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Participant Full Name in the **Participant Full Name** field.
2.  Provide your valid **Email Address** for class updates and materials.
3.  If required, enter your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
4.  Choose your **Class Level** from the options provided (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
5.  Select your preferred **Preferred Session** date or time.
6.  Indicate whether you require **Materials Needed** for the class.
7.  Provide your **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** for emergencies.
8.  Briefly describe your **Prior Calligraphy Experience**, if applicable.
9.  Note any **Accessibility Needs**, if required.
10. Enter any **Additional Notes** for the instructors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Use the email address you check most frequently for class updates and materials.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Class Level** (`class_level`, select\_one, required): Choose the level of calligraphy experience you have:
    +   Beginner: No prior calligraphy experience.
    +   Intermediate: Some calligraphy experience.
    +   Advanced: Significant calligraphy experience.
*   **Preferred Session** (`preferred_session`, text, required): Select the date or time you prefer for the class:
    +   Monday at 9:00 AM
    +   Tuesday at 2:00 PM
    +   Wednesday at 10:00 AM
    +   Thursday at 12:00 PM
*   **Materials Needed** (`materials\_needed`, select\_one, required): Indicate if you require materials provided for the class:
    +   True: Provide materials.
    +   False: Do not provide materials.
*   **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
*   **Prior Calligraphy Experience** (`prior_experience`, text, optional): Briefly describe any previous training or classes you have attended.
*   **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Note any accommodations required due to a disability or other reasons.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any other information for the instructors.
