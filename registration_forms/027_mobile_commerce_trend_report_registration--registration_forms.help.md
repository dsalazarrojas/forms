# mobile_commerce_trend_report_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on mobile commerce trends from business users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely, providing all the required and optional fields.
- Enter your Business Name, which is your company's name.
- Enter your Email address, which will be used to contact you.
- Enter your First and Last Name.
- Enter your Phone number.
- Enter your Company name.
- Enter your Job Title.
- Enter your Department.
- Select your Job Function.
- Read and agree to our terms and conditions.
- Select your Job Type (B2B or B2C).
- Select your Sales Channel (Online, Offline, or Both).
- Select your Report Type (Monthly, Bi-Annually, or Annually).
- Select your Report Frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
- Enter your Marketing Budget.
- Enter your Sales Budget.
- Enter your Conversion Rate.
- Enter your Cost per Acquisition.
- Enter your Customer Acquisition Cost.
- Enter your Customer Lifetime Value.
- Enter your Retention Rate.
- Enter your Churn Rate.
- Enter your Revenue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department.
- **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, required): Enter your job function.
- **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you understand and agree to our terms and conditions, disagree, or need to review them.
- **Accept** (`accept`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you accept our terms and conditions or not.
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether your job type is B2B or B2C.
- **Sales Channel** (`sales_channel`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your sales channel (Online, Offline, or Both).
- **Report Type** (`report_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your report type (Monthly, Bi-Annual, or Annually).
- **Report Frequency** (`report_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your report frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
- **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, `number`, required): Enter your marketing budget.
- **Sales Budget** (`sales_budget`, `number`, required): Enter your sales budget.
- **Conversion Rate** (`conversion_rate`, `number`, required): Enter your conversion rate.
- **Cost per Acquisition** (`cost_per_acquisition`, `number`, required): Enter your cost per acquisition.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost** (`customer_acquisition_cost`, `number`, required): Enter your customer acquisition cost.
- **Customer Lifetime Value** (`customer_lifetime_value`, `number`, required): Enter your customer lifetime value.
- **Retention Rate** (`retention_rate`, `number`, required): Enter your retention rate.
- **Churn Rate** (`churn_rate`, `number`, required): Enter your churn rate.
- **Revenue** (`revenue`, `number`, required): Enter your revenue.
