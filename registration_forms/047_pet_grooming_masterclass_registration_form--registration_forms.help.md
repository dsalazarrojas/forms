# Pet Grooming Masterclass Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pet owners who are interested in attending a pet grooming masterclass. The form will help us understand their preferences and needs, allowing us to tailor the session to their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Ensure that you have filled in all the necessary information for a complete and accurate submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Pet Salon Information (salon_info)** (`salon_info`, text, optional)
This field is where you can provide information about the pet salon you are registered with, if applicable. If you are not registered with a salon, you can leave this field blank.

### **Pet Information (pet_info)** (`pet_info`, text, optional)
In this field, please provide details about the pet you are registering. This can include the pet's name, species, age, or any other relevant information.

### **Session Preferences (session_preferences)** (`session_preferences`, text, optional)
Here, you can specify any special requests or preferences you have for the masterclass session. You can include anything from equipment or resource requirements to learning objectives.

### **Attendee Details (attendee_details)** (`attendee_details`, text, optional)
In this field, please provide any relevant details about yourself, the person attending the session. This can include your name, contact information, or any other relevant details.

### **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
This field is for providing your contact information, such as your email or phone number. This will help us get in touch with you with any updates or reminders about the session.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields for a complete submission.
- If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
