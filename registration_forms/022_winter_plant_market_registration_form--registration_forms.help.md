# Winter Plant Market Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Plant Market Registration Form is a form for vendors to register themselves and their products on the Winter Plant Market. It is used to collect personal and business information from the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your Vendor Name and Address in the `Vendor Name` and `Vendor Address` fields respectively.
4. Select the type of product you are offering from the list in the `Product Name` field.
5. Provide a description of your product in the `Product Description` field.
6. Enter the quantity of product you have available in the `Quantity` field.
7. Enter the price of your product in the `Price` field.
8. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Your first name, which is used to identify you as a vendor on the market.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Your email address, which is used for communication with the market administrators.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of your business or organization.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required: false): The address of your business or organization.
* **Product Name** (`product_names`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of product you are offering from the list.
*   *   Apple Tree
*   *   Cherry Tree
*   *   Maple Tree
*   *   Other (Specify in notes)
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of your product.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false): The amount of product you have available for sale.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, required: false): The price of your product.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Your phone number, which is used for communication with the market administrators.
