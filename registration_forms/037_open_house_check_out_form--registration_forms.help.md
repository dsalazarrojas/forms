# Open House Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Open House Check Out Form is a data collection tool for real estate agents and event organizers to gather information on visitors and leads from open house events. It helps track the performance of events and agents, providing valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Visitor Feedback**. This is an optional field where you can provide any comments or suggestions you received from visitors about the event.
2. Fill in your **Name**, making sure to enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
3. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used for contact and follow-up purposes.
4. Provide your **Phone Number**, which will be used for quick contact and communication.
5. Select the **Lead Source** that best describes how you heard about the open house event.
6. Choose the **Lead Agents** who interacted with you during the event. Select all applicable agents.
7. Enter the **House Address** of the open house event, including street, city, and state.
8. Enter the **Event Date** and **Event Time**, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` and `HH:MM AM/PM` respectively.
9. Enter the **Event Duration** in minutes.
10. If applicable, enter the **Number of Visitors** and **Number of Leads** generated during the event.
11. Enter any additional **Notes** about your experience at the open house event.
12. Set the **Lead Status** as follows:
	* Qualified: indicates a potential buyer is ready to buy or sell.
	* Not Qualified: indicates the visitor is not ready to buy or sell at this time.
	* Follow up Needed: indicates the visitor needs further communication or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Feedback**: This field is for any comments or suggestions you received from the visitors, such as feedback on the event's organization, presentation, or amenities.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for quick contact and communication.
* **Lead Source**: Choose how you heard about the open house event:
	+ Referrals: from a referral from a friend or family member.
	+ Open House: from attending an open house event.
	+ Other: specify other sources in the notes field.
* **Lead Agents**: Choose all applicable real estate agents who interacted with you during the event:
	+ Real Estate Agent: the agent who represented the property or helped with the event.
	+ Other: specify other agents or types in the notes field.
* **House Address**: Enter the address of the open house event, including street, city, and state.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the open house event in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event in minutes.
* **Number of Visitors**: Enter the number of visitors that attended the event.
* **Number of Leads**: Enter the number of qualified leads generated from the event.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about your experience at the event.
* **Lead Status**: Set the lead status as follows:
	+ Qualified: ready to buy or sell.
	+ Not Qualified: not ready to buy or sell at this time.
	+ Follow up Needed: requires further communication or follow-up.
