# Smart Hospitality Cluster Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering a smart hospitality cluster, which is a grouping of companies and organizations that are working together to achieve common goals and objectives in the hospitality industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Cluster Name field with the name of your cluster.
2. Provide a brief description of your cluster in the Description field.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the Interest Areas question, indicating whether your cluster has specific interest areas.
4. Choose the type of cluster you are registering (Yes/No).
5. Enter the number of employees in your cluster.
6. Fill in the Company Name field with the name of your company.
7. Provide a brief description of your company in the Company Description field.
8. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the Type question, indicating whether your company is the cluster's main hub.
9. Enter your company's email address in the Email field.
10. Enter your company's website in the Website field.
11. Enter the industry your company operates in the Industry field.
12. Enter the address of your company in the Address field.
13. Enter the phone number of your company in the Phone field.
14. Enter the phone extension of your company in the Phone Extension field.
15. Enter the phone number of the cluster's representative in the Phone 3 field.
16. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the Company Rep question, indicating whether your company is a representative of the cluster.
17. Choose the type of network your cluster belongs to (Yes/No).
18. Choose the status of your cluster's network (Active/Inactive).
19. Finally, fill in the First Name, Last Name, and Position fields with the information of the cluster's representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cluster Name**: The name of your cluster. This is a required field and should be a unique name that represents your cluster.
* **Description**: A brief description of your cluster.
* **Interest Areas**: Does your cluster have specific interest areas? Answer "Yes" or "No".
* **Cluster Type**: What type of cluster are you registering? Answer "Yes" or "No".
* **Number of Employees**: How many employees are in your cluster?
* **Company Name**: The name of your company.
* **Company Description**: A brief description of your company.
* **Type**: Is your company the cluster's main hub? Answer "Yes" or "No".
* **Email**: The email address of your company.
* **Website**: The website of your company.
* **Industry**: The industry your company operates in.
* **Address**: The address of your company.
* **Phone**: The phone number of your company.
* **Phone Extension**: The phone extension of your company.
* **Phone 2**: The phone number of the cluster's representative.
* **First Name**: The first name of the cluster's representative.
* **Last Name**: The last name of the cluster's representative.
* **Position**: The position of the cluster's representative.
* **Email (2)**: The email address of the cluster's representative.
* **Phone (3)**: The phone number of the cluster's representative.
* **Ext Phone**: The phone extension of the cluster's representative.
* **Company Rep**: Is your company a representative of the cluster? Answer "Yes" or "No".
* **Network Type**: What type of network does your cluster belong to? Answer "Yes" or "No".
* **Network Status**: What is the status of your cluster's network? Choose from Active or Inactive.
