# Team Recognition Event Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request a team recognition event, such as an awards ceremony, team dinner, or other celebration. This event is meant to recognize team members' contributions and acknowledge their hard work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Recognition Event Request** (`team_recognition_event_request_form`): Enter a brief description of the event you are requesting.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead_name`): Enter the name of the team lead or person responsible for the event.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`): Enter the email address of the team lead or person responsible for the event.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`): Enter the phone number of the team lead or person responsible for the event.
* **Team Recognition Event** (`team_recognition_event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Select the type of event (e.g. Awards Ceremony, Team Dinner, Other).
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Team Members Attending** (`team_members_attending`): List the names of team members attending the event.
* **Event Date Range** (`team_recognition_event_date_range`): Enter the date range of the event (e.g. January 1-31, February 1-28).
* **Event Time Range** (`team_recognition_event_time_range`): Enter the time range of the event (e.g. 10am-12pm).
* **Event Location** (`team_recognition_event_location`): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Notes** (`team_recognition_event_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the event.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter your name and email address so we know who submitted the request.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing the event, including event date, time, location, and description.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your team lead or HR representative for guidance.
