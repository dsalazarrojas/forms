# Islamic Academy Interview Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Islamic Academy Interview Registration Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your background and requirements for the interview. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth and effective interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to provide all the required information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new_applicant** (`text`, optional): This field is for you to introduce yourself and provide any relevant background information about your application.
* **contact_info** (`text`, optional): Please provide your contact information, including phone number and email address, so we can get in touch with you for any further questions or updates.
* **guardian_info** (`text`, optional): If you are under 18 years old, please provide information about your guardian or parent who can be contacted for any questions or concerns.
* **applicant_info** (`text`, optional): Please provide any additional information about yourself that may be relevant to your application.
* **parent_info** (`text`, optional): If you are under 18 years old, please provide information about your parent or guardian who can be contacted for any questions or updates.
* **payment_info** (`text`, optional): If applicable, please provide payment information, including method of payment and any relevant financial details.
* **medical_info** (`text`, optional): If you have any medical concerns or needs, please share this information with us.
* **upload_documents** (`text`, optional): If you have any supporting documents, please upload them here.
* **additional_info** (`text`, optional): If you have any additional information about yourself that may be relevant, please share it with us.
* **signature** (`text`, optional): Please sign this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **date_of_birth** (`date`, optional): Please provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **time_of_interview** (`time`, optional): If you have a preferred interview time, please specify it in 24-hour format (e.g. 08:00).
* **phone** (`text`, optional): Please provide your phone number so we can contact you for any further questions or updates.
* **email** (`email`, optional): Please provide your email address so we can contact you for any further questions or updates.
* **address** (`text`, optional): Please provide your address so we can send any relevant information or updates to you.

Note: This form is for information gathering purposes only and all fields are optional unless specifically marked as required.
