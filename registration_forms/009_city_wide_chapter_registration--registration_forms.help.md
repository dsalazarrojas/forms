# City Wide Chapter Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Wide Chapter Registration form is designed to collect information about local chapters, including chapter details, leadership team members, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the chapter name and ID fields. The chapter name is the official name of your local chapter, while the chapter ID is the unique identifier assigned to your chapter.
2. Select the meeting frequency of your chapter. This indicates how often your chapter meets.
3. Enter the contact information for the chapter president, including their email address and phone number.
4. Enter the contact information for the vice president, if applicable.
5. Enter the contact information for the treasurer, if applicable.
6. Fill in the contact information for the chapter office, including the phone number and email address.
7. Enter the approximate number of active members in your chapter.
8. List any key partner organizations associated with your chapter.
9. Provide any additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Chapter Name**: The official name of your local chapter.
	+ Required.
* **Chapter ID or Number**: The unique identifier assigned to your chapter.
	+ Not required, but recommended for tracking purposes.
* **City**: The city where your chapter is located.
	+ Required.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where your chapter is located.
	+ Required.
* **Chapter Founded Date**: The date when your chapter was established.
	+ Not required.
* **Chapter President or Director Name**: The name of the president or director of your chapter.
	+ Required.
* **President Email Address**: The email address of the president or director of your chapter.
	+ Required.
* **President Phone Number**: The phone number of the president or director of your chapter.
	+ Required.
* **Vice President or Co-Director Name**: The name of the vice president or co-director of your chapter.
	+ Not required.
* **Vice President Email Address**: The email address of the vice president or co-director of your chapter.
	+ Not required.
* **Treasurer Name**: The name of the treasurer of your chapter.
	+ Not required.
* **Treasurer Email Address**: The email address of the treasurer of your chapter.
	+ Not required.
* **Chapter Office Phone**: The phone number of the chapter office.
	+ Not required.
* **Chapter Email Address**: The email address of the chapter office.
	+ Required.
* **Chapter Website**: The website of your chapter.
	+ Not required.
* **Current Active Members**: The approximate number of active members in your chapter.
	+ Not required.
* **Meeting Frequency**: The frequency of your chapter meetings.
	+ Select from the options: Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, As needed.
	+ Not required.
* **Primary Activities and Focus Areas**: A brief description of the primary activities and focus areas of your chapter.
	+ Not required.
* **Key Partner Organizations**: A list of key partner organizations associated with your chapter.
	+ Not required.
* **Additional Contact Information**: Any additional contact information for your chapter.
	+ Not required.
