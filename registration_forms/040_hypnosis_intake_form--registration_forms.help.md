# Hypnosis Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hypnosis Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their medical history, emergency contact details, and other relevant health information. This form helps hypnosis practitioners ensure safe and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each page of the form, starting with the client information.
2.  Fill in your name, age, date of birth, phone number, and email.
3.  Provide details about your medical history, including any allergies or medications you are currently taking.
4.  Specify your emergency contact's name, relationship, and phone number.
5.  Enter details about your medical provider's name and phone number.
6.  Record any medical tests you have undergone, including their description, result, and date.
7.  Submit the completed form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information**:
    *   **Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
    *   **Age** (number): Provide your current age in years.
    *   **Date of Birth** (date): Enter the date of your birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
    *   **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
    *   **Email** (email): Enter your email address for secure communication.
*   **Medical History**:
    *   **Medical History** (text): Describe any chronic medical conditions or allergies you may have.
    *   **Medication** (select multiple): Check any medications you are currently taking. 
    *   **Medical Conditions** (text): List any current medical conditions.
    *   **Additional Medical History** (text): Describe any other medical concerns or conditions.
*   **Emergency Contact**:
    *   **Emergency Contact** (text): Specify the name and title of your emergency contact.
    *   **Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
    *   **Relationship** (select one): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, guardian).
*   **Medical Provider**:
    *   **Medical Provider** (text): Enter your medical provider's name.
    *   **Phone** (text): Enter your medical provider's phone number.
    *   **Relationship** (select one): Specify your relationship with your medical provider (e.g., doctor, nurse).
*   **Medical Tests**:
    *   **Medical Tests** (text): Describe any medical tests you have undergone.
    *   **Description** (text): Describe the tests or procedures performed.
    *   **Result** (text): Enter the results of your medical tests.
    *   **Date** (date): Enter the date of your medical tests in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
*   **Medical Provider Details**:
    *   **Medical Provider Name** (text): Enter your medical provider's name.
    *   **Medical Provider Phone** (text): Enter your medical provider's phone number.
    *   **Relationship** (select one): Specify your relationship with your medical provider (e.g., doctor, nurse).
