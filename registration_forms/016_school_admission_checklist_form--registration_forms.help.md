# School Admission Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Admission Checklist Form is a tool for collecting information from prospective students and their families to facilitate a smooth and efficient school admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your prospective student's name, contact information, and family details.
2. Provide any additional information you'd like to share with the school.
3. Attach any required documents to support your application.
4. Sign the form to confirm your agreement with the provided information.
5. Submit the form to initiate the admission process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prospective_student (Page 1)**: 
 Enter your prospective student's name and any other details you'd like to provide.
* **parent_contact_info (Page 2)**: 
 Enter your contact information so the school can get in touch with you.
* **student_contact_info (Page 3)**: 
 Enter your prospective student's contact information, if different from your own.
* **family_info (Page 4)**: 
 Describe your family structure and any relevant information about your family.
* **school_info (Page 5)**: 
 Enter any relevant school-specific information, such as previous school affiliation or student interests.
* **student_details (Page 6)**: 
 Provide any additional details about your prospective student, such as special needs or requirements.
* **student_documents (Page 7)**: 
 Attach any required documents, such as ID, birth certificate, or medical records.
* **parent_signature (Page 8)**: 
 Sign this form to confirm the provided information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **submit (Page 9)**: 
 This is the final step, where you submit your application and initiate the admission process.
* **notes (Page 10)**: 
 Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
