# director-interview-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The director-interview-registration form is a tool for registering candidates for interviews, gathering their details, and facilitating the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Candidate Details**
	* Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details as required.
	* Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. **CV Upload**
	* If you have a CV, upload it to provide more information to the hiring panel.
	* This is optional, and you can choose not to upload it if you don't have one.
3. **Interview Scheduling**
	* Select a date and time for your interview from the provided options.
	* Ensure you choose a time that works for you and the hiring panel.
4. **Portfolio Upload**
	* If applicable, upload any relevant portfolio work or examples that demonstrate your skills.
	* This is optional and only necessary if you're applying for a position that requires a portfolio.
5. **HR Notes**
	* This is a private field for the HR department to take notes about your application.
	* You can enter any relevant comments or information that you think would be helpful for the HR department.
6. **Recruiter Comments**
	* This is a private field for recruiters to leave comments or feedback about your application.
	* You won't be able to see these comments, but the hiring panel and HR department will see them.
7. **Hiring Panel**
	* Select one or multiple members from the list provided.
	* Choose members who you feel are relevant to your application or industry experience.
8. **HR Approval**
	* The HR department will review your application and select one or multiple approval options.
	* This is the final step before the hiring process moves forward.
