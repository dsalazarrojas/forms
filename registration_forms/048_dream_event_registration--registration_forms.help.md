# Dream Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dream Event Registration form is designed to collect essential information from attendees about a dream event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Information** page with relevant details about the event.
2. Provide your contact information as a participant in the **Participant Info** page.
3. Ensure you have filled out all required fields to submit the form successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Information (Page 1)
* **Event Information (EventInfo)** (EventInfo, text, required: no): Provide details about the event, such as event name, date, time, and location.
* **Event Information (EventInfo)** (EventInfo, text, required: no): Add any additional information about the event.

### Participant Info (Page 2)
* **Participant Info (Participant)** (Participant, text, required: no): Enter your name and contact information to register as a participant.
