# Creative Visual Artist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Visual Artist Application Form is a submission form for creative visual artists to apply for a specific opportunity. It aims to collect information about the artist's background, professional experience, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required sections (Resume, Portfolio Samples, and Bio Statement) with accurate and complete information about yourself.
2. Provide a brief description of your artistic background in the Artist Background field (optional but recommended).
3. Ensure you have at least 3 attachments of your work samples in the Portfolio Samples field.
4. Enter your contact information in the Contact Info field.
5. Click the submit button to complete the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Artist Background** (Text, Optional): Please describe your artistic background and experience in this field.
- **Resume** (Text, Required): Attach a current version of your resume that highlights your creative visual artist experience, education, and skills.
- **Portfolio Samples** (Text, Required): Attach up to 3 samples of your work that demonstrate your artistic skills and style. Ensure they are clear, legible, and in a suitable format.
- **Bio Statement** (Text, Required): Write a brief statement about yourself and your artistic style, highlighting your unique strengths and qualifications.
- **Contact Info** (Text, Optional): Provide your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.

## Tips
* Ensure the information you provide is accurate and complete.
* Be concise and clear in your descriptions.
* Use suitable formats for your attachments (e.g., JPEG, PDF, etc.).
* Only attach the required number of samples.
* Proofread your work for errors and typos.
