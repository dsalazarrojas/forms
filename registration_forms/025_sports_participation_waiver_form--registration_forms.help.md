# sports_participation_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports participation waiver form is required for all participants to ensure their safety and well-being during sports activities. This form collects crucial information about the participant, their emergency contact, and medical history to provide a secure and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the participant information section with your personal details.
2. Provide emergency contact information, including the contact person's name, relationship, and contact details.
3. Answer the medical history questions truthfully, including any conditions, medications, and medical supervision requirements.
4. Select the correct medical supervision options for your safety.
5. Confirm your emergency contact's medical supervision details.
6. Sign the participant consent to confirm you have read and understood the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_information (Text)**: Provide your name, age, and date of birth for identification purposes.
* **emergency_contact_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_relationship (Text)**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, guardian, or spouse).
* **emergency_contact_address (Text)**: Enter the emergency contact's address for easy contact.
* **emergency_contact_phone (Text)**: Provide the emergency contact's phone number for quick contact.
* **emergency_contact_dob (Date)**: Enter the date of birth of the emergency contact person.
* **participant_medical_conditions (Select Multiple)**: List any medical conditions you may have, such as asthma, diabetes, or heart conditions.
* **participant_medication (Text)**: List any medications you take, including dosages and frequencies.
* **participant_consent (Select One)**: Confirm your consent to participate, by selecting "I agree."
* **emergency_contact_medical_conditions (Select Multiple)**: List any medical conditions of the emergency contact, such as heart conditions or allergies.
* **emergency_contact_medication (Text)**: List any medications the emergency contact takes.
* **emergency_contact_consent (Select One)**: Confirm the emergency contact's consent to participate, by selecting "I agree."
* **participant_employment_status (Select One)**: Indicate whether you are employed or unemployed.
* **participant_health_insurance (Select One)**: Confirm whether you have health insurance.
* **participant_medical_supervision (Select One)**: Confirm whether you agree to medical supervision during the activity.
* **participant_medical_supervision_type (Select Multiple)**: Select the types of medical supervision you require, such as on-site or off-site supervision.
* **participant_medical_supervision_provider (Text)**: Provide the name and contact information of your medical supervision provider.
* **participant_medical_insurance_plan (Text)**: Enter your health insurance plan details.
* **participant_employer (Text)**: Provide your employer's name and contact information.
* **participant_employment_supervisor (Text)**: Provide your employment supervisor's name and contact information.
* **participant_medical_supervisor (Text)**: Provide the name and contact information of your medical supervisor.
