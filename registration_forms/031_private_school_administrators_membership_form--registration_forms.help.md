# Private School Administrators Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Private School Administrators Membership Form" is used to collect information from private school administrators to facilitate communication and provide relevant services to their institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **Role** (`Role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role within the school (School Administrator, Director, Teacher, Other).
* **School Name** (`School Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's name.
* **School Address** (`School Address`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's address.
* **School City** (`School City`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's city.
* **School Country** (`School Country`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's country.
* **School Zip** (`School Zip`, `number`, optional): Enter your school's zip code.
* **School State** (`School State`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's state.
* **Membership Interests** (`Membership Interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your membership interests (Academic Development, Professional Development, Networking, Leadership).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about your school or membership interests.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your information is accurate.
* **School Administrator Name** (`School Administrator Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your school administrator's name.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
* The information collected will be used to personalize the experience of private school administrators and provide relevant services to their institutions.
