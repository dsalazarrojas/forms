# Participation Request For Cyber Event - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to submit their interest in participating in a cyber event. It is used to gather information about the participant and the event they are interested in. The form is also used by event organizers to keep track of participants and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant's name and email address in the required fields.
2. If you are a participant, select your participant type (Participant, Speaker, or Sponsor) from the dropdown menu. If you are not one of the above, type in your own participant type in the "Participant Type Other" field.
3. Provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to your participation in the event.
4. Enter the name of the event you are interested in participating in.
5. Choose the date and start time of the event from the calendars.
6. Add any additional notes about the event.
7. Submit the form to complete your participation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it would be listed in the event participant list.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for event organizers and organizers to contact you with event updates and information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by event organizers or other participants.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself that is relevant to your participation in the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are interested in participating in.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of the event from the calendar.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of participant you are (Participant, Speaker, or Sponsor).
* **Participant Type Other** (`participant_type_other`, text, optional): Enter your own participant type if you are not a Participant, Speaker, or Sponsor.
* **Additional Participant Information** (`participant_info_other`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself that is not listed in the "Participant Information" field.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit your participation request.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the event.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure that event organizers can contact you.
* If you are a Sponsor, be prepared to provide additional information about your sponsorship.
* If you are a Speaker, be prepared to provide information about your presentation.
