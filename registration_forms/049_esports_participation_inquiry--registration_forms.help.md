# Esports participation inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esports participation inquiry form is designed to gather information from players interested in participating in esports events. This form helps the organizers understand the player's interest, availability, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a star \*).
3. For the "Event availability" field, please enter the date of the event for which you are interested in participating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player interest**: \* (Required) - Please enter a brief description of why you are interested in participating in esports events.
* **Team details**: \* (Required) - If you are interested in joining a team, please provide a brief description of the team you are interested in.
* **Player details**: \* (Required) - Please provide your name, age, and contact information.
* **Event availability**: \* (Required) - Please enter the date of the event for which you are interested in participating.
* **Contact info**: (Optional) - If you are willing, please provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **School info**: (Optional) - Please provide your school name and any other relevant information about your school.
* **Gaming community**: (Optional) - If you are part of a gaming community, please describe it briefly.
* **Follow up**: \* (Required) - Indicate if you are willing to follow up with our team after filling out this form. This is an important step in the recruitment process.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the event organizers for clarification.
* The more information you provide, the better we can understand your interests and availability.
