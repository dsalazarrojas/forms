# Mathematics Qualification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mathematics Qualification Registration form is used to register for a mathematics exam. It collects information about the candidate's education level, exam preferences, and contact details. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Candidate Details**: Provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. **Education Level**: Select your current education level from the given options (High School, Bachelor Degree, Doctoral).
3. **Exam Preferences**: Choose the mathematics subject you want to register for (Mathematics, Statistics, Calculus).
4. **Exam Date**: If you have a preferred exam date, select it from the given options.
5. **Exam Time**: If applicable, select your preferred exam time.
6. **Consent**: A text box for any additional comments or notes.
7. **Preferred Exam Time**: Select your preferred exam time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
8. **Preferred Exam Date**: Select your preferred exam date (Today, Next Week, Next Month).
9. **Email**: Enter your email address.
10. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
11. **Address**: Provide your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current education level (High School, Bachelor Degree, Doctoral).
* **Exam Preferences** (`exam_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the mathematics subject you want to register for (Mathematics, Statistics, Calculus).
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required/optional): If you have a preferred exam date, select it.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, required/optional): If applicable, select your preferred exam time.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required/optional): A text box for any additional comments or notes.
* **Preferred Exam Time** (`preferred_exam_time`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred exam time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Preferred Exam Date** (`preferred_exam_date`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred exam date (Today, Next Week, Next Month).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, number, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Provide your address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately to avoid any errors or omissions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your preferred email address or phone number.
