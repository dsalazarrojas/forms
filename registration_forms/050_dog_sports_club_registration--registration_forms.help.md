# Dog Sports Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register your dog in the Dog Sports Club. This form helps us gather information about your dog and your preferences for training classes and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details: Enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Dog Owner Info" field.
2. Enter your owner's name: Fill in your owner's name in the "Owner Name" field.
3. Provide your contact information: Enter your email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields respectively.
4. Provide dog information: Fill in your dog's breed, weight, and license number in the "Dog Breed", "Dog Weight", and "Dog License" fields.
5. Choose your classes: Select one or multiple classes for your dog from the "Classes" dropdown menu, and choose how frequently you'd like to attend the classes from the "Class Frequency" dropdown menu.
6. Select activities: Select one or multiple activities for your dog from the "Activities" dropdown menu.
7. Instructor signature: Sign the "Owner Signature" field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
8. Finalize your registration: Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Owner Info** (`dog_owner_info`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number to provide your personal details.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter your owner's name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, required): Enter your dog's breed for our records.
* **Dog Weight** (`dog_weight`, text, required): Enter your dog's weight for our records.
* **Dog License** (`dog_license`, text, required): Enter your dog's license number for our records.
* **Classes** (`classes`, select_one, required): Select one or multiple classes for your dog from the provided options.
* **Class Frequency** (`class_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how frequently you'd like to attend the classes.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, required): Sign to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`, text, required): Sign to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Activities** (`activities`, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple activities for your dog from the provided options.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required): Enter the class name.
* **Class Date** (`class_date`, date, required): Enter the class date.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, time, required): Enter the class time.
* **Class Duration** (`class_duration`, text, required): Enter the class duration.
* **Class Location** (`class_location`, text, required): Enter the class location.
* **Owner Notes** (`owner_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about your dog.
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, text, required): Sign to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Back to** (`back_to`, text, required): Enter the "Back to" field for further instructions.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required): Click to cancel your registration.
* **Microchip** (`dog_microchip`, text, required): Enter your dog's microchip number.
* **Medical** (`dog_medical`, text, required): Enter any medical information about your dog.
