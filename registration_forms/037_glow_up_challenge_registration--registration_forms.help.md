# Glow Up Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Glow Up Challenge Registration form is a collection of questions designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in the Glow Up Challenge. This form is intended to be filled out by participants to provide accurate and relevant information for the challenge organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Select your gender from the "Gender" field.
7. Select the type of challenge you are interested in from the "Challenge Type" field.
8. Select your level of participation from the "Participant Level" field.
9. Select whether you prefer to be contacted or not from the "Contact Preference" field.
10. Read and agree to the terms of consent in the "Consent" field.
11. Enter any additional comments you have regarding your participation in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your gender identity.
* **Challenge Type** (`challenge_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of challenge that interests you.
* **Participant Level** (`participant_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your level of participation in the challenge.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you want to be contacted or not.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms of consent.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments regarding your participation.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
