This form, known as the "Sensory Event Registration," is designed to capture user contact information and accessibility needs for events where multiple sessions are being held. The purpose of the form is to ensure that attendees can provide their information accurately and that event organizers can prepare to accommodate their needs. It is not intended for general public use or for events without multiple sessions.

The form is comprised of multiple pages, each with its own set of fields. The first page asks for general contact information, including email, first name, and last name. The subsequent pages ask about accessibility needs, such as dietary restrictions, hearing assistance, vision assistance, and physical movement needs. The final page allows users to select the sessions they plan to attend and provide additional information.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your contact information on the first page. This includes your email address, first name, and last name.
2.  On the accessibility page, select any applicable options for dietary restrictions, hearing assistance, vision assistance, and physical movement needs. You can choose multiple options for each section.
3.  For each session you plan to attend, select the corresponding date from the available options.
4.  Finally, provide any additional information you'd like to share with the event organizers.

Here is a field-by-field explanation of the form:

*   **Registration Contact** (contact_page): This field asks for your email address to contact you regarding the event.
*   **First Name** (contact_page_first_name): This field requests your first name for identification purposes.

*   **Phone** (contact_page_phone): This field is for your phone number in case event organizers need to contact you.

*   **Hearing Assistance** (accessibility_page_hearing): This field provides options for hearing assistance you may need.
*   **Vision Assistance** (accessibility_page_vision): This field offers options for vision assistance you may need.
*   **Physical Movement** (accessibility_page_moving): This field allows you to choose physical movement needs you may have.
*   **Other** (accessibility_page_other): This field is for any other accessibility needs you may have.
*   **Sessions Attending** (registration_page_session): This field lets you select which sessions you plan to attend.

*   **Session 2** (registration_page_session_2): This field asks for the date you plan to attend Session 2.

*   **Language Assistance** (accessibility_page_language): This field provides options for language assistance you may need.
