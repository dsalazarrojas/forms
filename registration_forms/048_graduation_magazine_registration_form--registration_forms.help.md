# graduation_magnitude_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for graduation registration purposes, including personal details and graduation-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "full name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select your date of birth from the calendar in the "date of birth" field.
5. Enter your student ID, if applicable, in the "student id" field.
6. Provide the name of your university in the "university" field.
7. Enter any graduation details in the "graduation details" field (optional).
8. Select your graduation date from the calendar in the "graduation date" field (optional).
9. Choose your graduation month from the dropdown list in the "graduation month" field (optional).
10. Enter your graduation year in the "graduation year" field (optional).
11. If applicable, upload or select your photo in the "photo" field (optional).
12. Provide any additional graduation-related information in the "other details" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **full name** (text, required): Enter your full name, including your first name, last name, and any middle name.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **date of birth** (date, required): Select your date of birth from the calendar.
* **student id** (number, optional): Enter your student ID, if applicable.
* **university** (text, required): Enter the name of your university.
* **graduation details** (note, optional): Enter any additional graduation-related information.
* **graduation date** (date, optional): Select your graduation date from the calendar.
* **graduation month** (select_one, optional): Choose your graduation month from the list.
* **graduation year** (number, optional): Enter your graduation year.
* **photo** (select_multiple, optional): If applicable, upload or select your photo.
* **other details** (text, optional): Enter any additional graduation-related information.
* **photo** (select_multiple, optional, note: This field is likely intended for uploading or selecting a photo, but its label might be unclear. Please double-check the field's purpose and intent): If applicable, select or upload your photo in this field.
