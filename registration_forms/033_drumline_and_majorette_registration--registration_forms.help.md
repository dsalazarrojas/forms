# drumline_and_majorette_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The drumline and majorette registration form is designed to collect information from students participating in the drumline and majorette program at your school. This form aims to gather necessary details to facilitate communication and organization for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Name.
2. Select your Grade Level (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, or other).
3. Choose the instrument you will be playing in the drumline and majorette program.
4. Select the contact person for the program (e.g., Parent, Teacher, or Other).
5. Enter your Email address.
6. Enter your Phone number.
7. Enter your School's Name.
8. Enter your School's Phone number.
9. Enter your School's Email address.
10. Provide any Additional Information about yourself or your school.
11. Confirm your registration by selecting 'True' (indicating that you have read and understood the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your school's records.
* **Grade Level**: Select your grade level in the school (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, or other).
* **Instrument**: Choose the instrument you will be playing in the drumline and majorette program (e.g., Snare, B-flat, Tenor, or other).
* **Contact Person**: Select who will be the primary contact person for the program (e.g., Parent, Teacher, or Other).
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your personal phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **School Name**: Enter your school's name as it appears on official records.
* **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **School Email**: Enter your school's email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional details about yourself or your school that will help the program organizers.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that you have read and understood the form by selecting 'True'.
