# Yoga Class Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yoga Class Reservation Form is designed to collect information from users for yoga class reservations. Please complete this form to let us know about your interest in joining a yoga class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select your preferred class type from the "Class Type" dropdown menu (Asana, Vinyasa, or Restorative).
5. Specify your availability for the class by selecting one or multiple options from the "Availability" field.
6. Add any additional notes about your reservation in the "Notes" field.
7. Click "Submit" to complete and submit your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name to help us get in touch with you about your class reservation.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about class details and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about your class reservation.
* **Class Type** (`Class Type`, select_one, required): Select your preferred class type:
	+ Asana: Focuses on physical postures and movement.
	+ Vinyasa: Combines postures with breathing and flow.
	+ Restorative: Relaxes and rejuvenates with supported postures.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available for the class.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available for the class.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select your availability for the class:
	+ Available: You are available for the class.
	+ Unavailable: You are not available for the class.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your reservation.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click to complete and submit your reservation.
