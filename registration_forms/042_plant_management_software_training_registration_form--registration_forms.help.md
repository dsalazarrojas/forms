# Plant Management Software Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration for the Plant Management Software Training, which is a course designed to educate users on the effective use of the Plant Management Software.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your training enrollment information.
2. Provide your contact information.
3. Select your training topics of interest.
4. Add any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Enrollment** (text): This field is where you can provide your training preferences such as your name, and any other relevant training information.
* **Email** (email): Your email is required for communication and will be used by the training administrators.
* **Name** (text): Please enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Company** (text): Enter the name of your company.
* **Department** (text): If you are registering as part of a department, please enter it here. This is an optional field.
* **Job Title** (text): Enter your job title or position.
* **Training Date** (date): The date you are interested in attending the training session.
* **Training Time** (time): The time you would like to attend the training session.
* **Training Time Duration** (text): Enter the duration of the training session you wish to attend.
* **Location** (text): The location of the training session.
* **Comments** (text): If you have any comments or requests, please add them here. This is an optional field.
* **Training Topics** (select multiple): Select all the topics you are interested in learning about. Please select 'Yes' for each topic that is relevant to you.
* **Other Training Topics** (text): If there are any additional topics you are interested in learning about, please enter them here. This is an optional field.
* **Contact Info** (text): Please provide your contact information for any additional communication.
* **Additional Comments** (note): If you have any additional comments or information you'd like to provide, please add them here. This is an optional field.
