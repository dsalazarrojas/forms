# Exam Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Exam Sign Up Form is a tool to help you store and manage your exam sign-ups for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To sign up for an exam, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your exam title. This is an optional field.
2. On the second page, enter your name as you would like it to appear on your exam certificate.
3. Select your desired exam option from the drop-down menu. This will allow you to choose the specific exam you wish to take.
4. Choose a date and time for the exam you selected. This will be the date and time you will attend the exam.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exam Title** (`title`, `text`, optional): Enter a short title for the exam you wish to take.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your exam certificate.
* **Exam** (`exam_sign_up_form_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select your desired exam option from the provided choices.
* **Date** (`exam_sign_up_form_4`, `date`, optional): Choose a date for the exam you selected.
* **Time** (`exam_sign_up_form_5`, `time`, optional): Choose a time for the exam you selected.

## Tips

* Please ensure you are eligible to access and use this form before submitting your information.
* Duplicate submissions will be rejected.
