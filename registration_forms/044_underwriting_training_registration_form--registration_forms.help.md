# Underwriting Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals registering for underwriting training programs. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your registration is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
*   Make sure to select the correct training type and location from the options provided.
*   Select the correct registration method (Online or Offline) and enter the corresponding date.
*   Double-check your contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone) for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company or organization name.
*   **Role** (`role`, text, required): Enter your role in the underwriting training program.
*   **Training Type** (`training_type`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options:
    *   Underwriting 1
    *   Underwriting 2
    *   Underwriting 3
*   **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the training in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your training session.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the training will take place.
*   **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of training hours you plan to attend.
*   **Training Hours Ending** (`training_hours_ending`, number, optional): Enter the number of training hours you plan to attend until the end.
*   **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, number, optional): Enter the registration fee you have paid or plan to pay.
*   **Registration Method** (`registration_method`, select one, required): Select how you will be registering for the training:
    *   Online
    *   Offline
*   **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you registered for the training in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
