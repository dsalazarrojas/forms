# Art Class Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Art Class Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from students who wish to enroll in an art class. This form helps us to understand your background, artistic experience, and any special needs or concerns you may have. By filling out this form, you will be able to register for the class that best suits your interests and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a class that you are interested in from the available options.
2. Fill out the student information section with your details (full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number).
3. Provide the necessary emergency contact information (emergency contact name and phone number).
4. Indicate your enrollment term or session preference.
5. Choose your art experience level (e.g., no experience, beginner, intermediate, advanced, or professional).
6. Answer whether you have taken art classes before and provide details if applicable.
7. Describe your artistic interests or specialties.
8. State why you are taking the class (goals and objectives).
9. Optionally, list any specific skills you want to learn.
10. If you have any accessibility or mobility needs, allergies, or health concerns, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Art Class Selected** (`class_selected`, text, required): Select the art class you are interested in enrolling in.
* **Enrollment Term or Session** (`enrollment_term`, text, required): Choose the term or session you wish to enroll in.
* **Art Experience Level** (`art_experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your level of art experience (no experience, beginner, intermediate, advanced, or professional).
* **Have you taken art classes before?** (`previous_classes`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have taken art classes before.
* **If yes, describe the classes you have taken** (`previous_classes_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the art classes you have taken.
* **Artistic Interests or Specialties** (`artistic_interests`, text, optional): Describe your artistic interests or specialties.
* **Why are you taking this class?** (`class_goals`, text, required): State your reasons for taking the class.
* **Specific Skills You Want to Learn** (`specific_skills`, text, optional): List any specific skills you wish to acquire in the class.
* **Accessibility or Mobility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Share any accessibility or mobility needs you may have.
* **Allergies or Health Concerns** (`allergies_concerns`, text, optional): List any allergies or health concerns (art supply sensitivities, etc.).
* **I consent to participate in this art class** (`informed_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to participate in the art class.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please contact us at [insert contact info].
