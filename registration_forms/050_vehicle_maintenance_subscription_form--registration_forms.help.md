# Vehicle Maintenance Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you subscribe to our vehicle maintenance service, which provides regular checks and services to keep your vehicle in top condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your email address, vehicle year, vehicle make, service preferences, payment method, customer name, and customer phone number (if applicable).
2. Make sure to select your preferred services (e.g., oil change, tire rotation, brake service, etc.) and payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
3. Click submit to send your subscription request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, `false`): Please enter your email address.
- **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, `false`): Enter the year of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the make of your vehicle (e.g., Honda, Toyota, Ford).
- **Service Preferences** (`service_preferences`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Choose the services you'd like us to perform on your vehicle (e.g., oil change, tire rotation, brake service, etc.).
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, `false`): Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, `false`): If you are not an authorized vehicle owner, enter the name of the customer.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, `false`): If you are not an authorized vehicle owner, enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any issues with your subscription.
- If you need help with the form, contact us at your convenience.
- Our team will review your subscription request and contact you soon.
