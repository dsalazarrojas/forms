# Integrative Health And Yoga Research Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for an Integrative Health And Yoga Research project. It collects demographic information and consent from individuals who wish to participate in the research study.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided the correct information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`participant_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This section collects basic contact information from the participant.
* **Health Information** (`health_info`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is for the participant to share any health-related information.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required: false):
	+ Please provide your consent to participate in the research study.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Share your contact information so the research team can reach you.
* **University Participant** (`university_participant`, text, required: false):
	+ Check if you are a university participant.
* **Researcher Participant** (`researcher_participant`, text, required: false):
	+ Check if you are a researcher participant.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm your participant consent.
* **Participant Health Info** (`participant_health_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Share any relevant health information.
* **Participant Health Info** (`participant_health_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Please note that this field is a duplicate, and you may want to review and update the information accordingly.

## Tips
1. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
