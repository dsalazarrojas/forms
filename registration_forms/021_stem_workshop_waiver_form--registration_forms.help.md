# STEM Workshop Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide is intended for users who need to fill out the STEM Workshop Waiver Form, which is required for participating in the STEM workshop. The purpose of this guide is to walk the user through the form's sections and fields to help them complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, ensure you have all the necessary information ready. The form is organized into sections with clear labels. Fill out the fields as instructed, and make sure to review your answers carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, not required): Fill out this field with your name, as you will be registered for the workshop.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Provide your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Waiver Acknowledgement** (`waiver_acknowledgement`, select_one, not required): Select "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions." to confirm your understanding of the workshop's rules.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, not required): Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, not required): List any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the workshop.
* **Meds** (`medications`, text, not required): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, not required): List any allergies you have.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number 2** (`emergency_contact_phone_number_2`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship_2`, text, not required): Specify the relationship between you and the secondary emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name 2** (`emergency_contact_name_2`, text, not required): Enter the name of the secondary emergency contact.
* **Waiver Acknowledgement 2** (`waiver_acknowledgement_2`, select_one, not required): Select "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions." to confirm your understanding of the workshop's rules.
* **Emergency Contact Address 2** (`emergency_contact_address_2`, text, not required): Enter the address of the secondary emergency contact.
* **Medical Treatment Procedure** (`medical_treatment_procedure`, text, not required): If you have medical conditions, list the procedures or treatments you require or are undergoing.
* **Medical Condition Details** (`medical_condition_details`, text, not required): Provide more information about your medical conditions if necessary.
* **Medical Treatment Procedure 2** (`medical_treatment_procedure_2`, text, not required): If you have medical conditions, list the procedures or treatments you require or are undergoing.
* **Emergency Contact Name 3** (`emergency_contact_name_3`, text, not required): Enter the name of the tertiary emergency contact.
* **Waiver Acknowledgement 3** (`waiver_acknowledgement_3`, select_one, not required): Select "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions." to confirm your understanding of the workshop's rules.
* **Medical Treatment Procedure 3** (`medical_treatment_procedure_3`, text, not required): If you have medical conditions, list the procedures or treatments you require or are undergoing.

## Tips

- Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness before submission.
- If you have any questions or doubts, do not hesitate to contact the workshop organizers for clarification.
