# Event Seating Arrangement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Seating Arrangement Form is used to collect information about an event's seating arrangement to help event organizers and hosts plan and organize their events more efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the necessary information about your event.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields (those marked as "required").
3. If a field is not marked as required, you can choose whether or not to fill it out, depending on your specific needs.
4. Submit the form to complete the event's seating arrangement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### event_name (Text) 
The event name is the name of the event. Please fill out the name of your event.
### event_type (Select One)
Indicate whether your event is a "Yes" or "No" type event. This will help us determine the type of seating arrangement needed.
### event_location (Select Multiple)
Select all the locations where your event will take place. You can choose multiple locations if needed.
### seating_capacity (Select One)
Indicate whether your event has a seating capacity. Please choose "Yes" or "No" based on your event's needs.
### seating_arrangement (Select One)
Indicate whether you need a special seating arrangement for your event. Please choose "Yes" or "No" based on your event's needs.
### special_requests (Text)
If you have any special requests for the seating arrangement, please specify them here.
### contact_name (Select Multiple)
Select all the contact names for your event. Please only select individuals who will be in charge of contacting about the seating arrangement.
### contact_email (Email)
Enter the email of the person responsible for the event's seating arrangement. This will be used for contact purposes.
### contact_phone (Text)
Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the event's seating arrangement. This will be used for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as soon as possible to ensure that all necessary arrangements are made for the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with the seating arrangement.
