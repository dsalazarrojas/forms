# corporate_inclusion_strategy_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the corporate inclusion strategy workshop. It gathers essential information from participants to facilitate their registration and ensure they are properly prepared for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your workshop details, including the date and time you plan to attend.
2. Provide your personal details, such as your name, role, email, phone number, and company information.
3. Confirm your consent to participate in the workshop.
4. Provide emergency contact information and contact details for the workshop organizers.
5. Answer additional questions about your role, team, and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, `text`, required): This field allows you to enter any relevant information about the workshop you want to register for.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required): Please enter a brief description of your role within your company.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your official company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, `text`, required): The location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required): The date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required): The time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, `number`, required): The duration you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Topic** (`workshop_topic`, `text`, required): The topic of the workshop you plan to attend.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information or comments about your registration, enter them here.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent to participate in the workshop.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide contact information for an emergency contact person in case of an incident.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Provide your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Provide your emergency contact's email address.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department within your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title within your company.
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your team within your company.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter your company's location.
* **Role 2** (`role_2`, `text`, required): Enter your secondary role within your company.
* **Role 3** (`role_3`, `text`, required): Enter your tertiary role within your company.
* **Role 4** (`role_4`, `text`, required): Enter your fourth role within your company.
* **Role 5** (`role_5`, `text`, required): Enter your fifth role within your company.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have the necessary approvals from your company to participate in the workshop.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, ask your HR department or the workshop organizers for clarification.
