### curbside_service_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to register for curbside service for their vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Enter any additional details as needed.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info** (`user_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your name, company, and other relevant information about yourself.
- **Address** (`address`, Text, Optional): Enter your address so we can find you for curbside service.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you to confirm your service details.
- **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can send you updates about your service.
- **Service Info** (`service_info`, Text, Optional): Provide details about the service you are requesting, such as vehicle type or service description.
- **Service Time** (`service_time`, Time, Optional): Select the time of day you would like to receive service. Please note that availability may vary based on our service schedule.
- **Service Date** (`service_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date you would like to receive service. Please note that availability may vary based on our schedule.
- **Service Notes** (`service_notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional information or notes about your service request.

**Tips:** Please ensure you have provided accurate information so we can provide efficient and effective service. Also, please review your submission before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
