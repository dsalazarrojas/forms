# Library Reading Challenge Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Library Reading Challenge Signup form, which is used to sign up schools and teachers for the Library Reading Challenge program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions required for your school and teacher.
2. Select the correct options for your school and teacher participation in the challenge.
3. Enter the required information for your school and teacher.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Email (`user_email`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter your email address here. This will be used as a contact point for further communication and updates about the challenge.

### 2. Name (`user_name`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter your name as you would like to be known on the challenge.

### 3. School (`school_name`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Select 'Yes' if your school is participating in the challenge.

### 4. Teacher (`teacher_name`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Select 'Yes' if the teacher is participating in the challenge.

### 5. Start Date (`start_date`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter the start date of the challenge if it is not the current date.

### 6. End Date (`end_date`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter the end date of the challenge if it is not the current date.

### 7. School Year (`school_year`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Select 'Yes' if the current school year is participating in the challenge.

### 8. Grade Level (`grade_level`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Select 'High' or 'Low' to indicate the grade level of the participating students.

### 9. Teacher Grade Level (`teacher_grade_level`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter the grade level of the teacher participating in the challenge.

### 10. Teacher (`teacher_name`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Select 'Yes' if the teacher is participating in the challenge.

### 11. School Type (`school_type`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Select 'Yes' if the school type is participating in the challenge.

### 12. School Address (`school_address`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter the address of the school.

### 13. Teacher School (`teacher_school`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Select 'Yes' if the teacher school is participating in the challenge.

### 14. Teacher Subjects (`teacher_subjects`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Select the subjects taught by the teacher participating in the challenge.

### 15. School Zip (`school_zip`)
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter the zip code of the school.

Note: Please ensure that all fields with a 'Yes/No' option are selected correctly as they are used to determine participation status.
