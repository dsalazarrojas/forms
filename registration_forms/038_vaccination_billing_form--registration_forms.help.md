# Vaccination Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccination Billing Form is a one-page form used to collect information from patients for accurate and timely billing.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the patient's name in the `patient_name` field.
2.  Enter the patient's age in the `patient_age` field.
3.  Enter the patient's email in the `patient_email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **patient_name** (text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
*   **patient_age** (number, optional): Enter the patient's age to determine eligibility for vaccination services.
*   **patient_email** (email, required): Enter the patient's email address for communication and billing purposes.
