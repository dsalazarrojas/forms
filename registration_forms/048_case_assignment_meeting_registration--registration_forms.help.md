# Case Assignment Meeting Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Assignment Meeting Registration form is used to gather participant information for the case assignment meeting. This form collects details such as participant's preferences, availability, and specific requirements for a smooth meeting experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately.
2. Select only the options that apply to you.
3. If a field is not required, but you have information to share, please enter it in the field.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your business cards or identification.
* **Employee ID** (required): Enter your employee ID number to ensure accurate tracking.
* **Department or Unit** (required): Enter your department or unit name.
* **Job Title** (required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Work Email** (required): Enter your work email address for meeting invites and links.
* **Preferred Meeting Date** (required): Select one date from the meeting schedule that suits you best.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (required): Select one time slot from 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, or 4:00 PM.
* **Mode of Participation** (required): Select the mode of participation that suits you best (In-person, Virtual (Zoom or Teams), or Hybrid).
* **Current Active Case Load** (required): Enter the number of active cases you are currently assigned to.
* **Areas of Expertise** (required): Select all areas of expertise that apply to your work (Legal, Medical, Finance, Social Work, Technical, Admin).
* **Are you available to take on new cases?** (required): Select one of the options: True, Limited, or False.
* **Preparatory Questions** (optional): Please use this section to ask any questions you may have about the meeting or the cases.
* **Specific Types of Cases you are interested in** (optional): List any specific case types you are interested in.
* **Do you need access to specific case files before the meeting?** (required): Select Yes or No.
* **List the files or case numbers requested** (optional): If you selected Yes, list the required files or case numbers here.
* **Dietary Requirements** (optional): Select all dietary requirements that apply to you (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Halal, Kosher, None).
* **Any accessibility requirements?** (optional): If you have any accessibility requirements, please enter them here.
* **Registration Status** (required): Select one of the options: Confirmed, Pending Approval, or Canceled.
* **I have informed my supervisor of this meeting** (required): Select True, False, or N/A.
* **Signature of Registrant** (required): Type your name as a digital signature.

Please review the form carefully before submission, and ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
