# parental_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Authorization Form is a document that provides parental consent for a child's participation in various activities, programs, or events. It ensures that the child's parents or guardians are aware of and agree to their child's involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's first name, last name, and birth date.
2. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Indicate your relationship to the child (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
4. Provide your own contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
5. Select whether or not you are the child's guardian (yes/no).
6. If you are the child's guardian, fill in your name and phone number.
7. Enter the date and time of the authorization.
8. Sign and date the authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name (1)**: Enter your child's first name as it is written on their official documents.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
- **Last Name (2)**: Enter your child's last name as it is written on their official documents.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
- **Birth Date (3)**: Enter your child's birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
  * Type: Date field
  * Required: No
- **Email (4)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
  * Type: Email field
  * Required: No
- **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
- **Address (6)**: Enter your address where you can be reached.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
- **Relationship (7)**: Indicate whether you are the child's guardian.
  * Type: Select multiple option
  * Required: No
- **Guardian First Name (8)**: Enter your first name.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No (if you select "Yes" for Guardian Relationship)
- **Guardian Last Name (9)**: Enter your last name.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No (if you select "Yes" for Guardian Relationship)
- **Guardian Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No (if you select "Yes" for Guardian Relationship)
- **Guardian Email (11)**: Enter your email address.
  * Type: Email field
  * Required: No (if you select "Yes" for Guardian Relationship)
- **Guardian Relationship (12)**: Indicate whether you are the child's guardian (yes/no).
  * Type: Select multiple option
  * Required: No
- **Authorization Date (13)**: Enter the date of the authorization in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
  * Type: Date field
  * Required: No
- **Authorization Time (14)**: Enter the time of the authorization in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
  * Type: Time field
  * Required: No
- **Authorization Signature (15)**: A space for the guardian's signature.
  * Type: Note field
  * Required: No
- **Reason for Participation (16)**: A description of the purpose for which you are authorizing the child's participation.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate contact information in case we need to contact you regarding the child's participation.
- If you are a guardian, make sure to fill in the necessary fields for your contact information.
- Sign and date the authorization for your signature to be valid.
