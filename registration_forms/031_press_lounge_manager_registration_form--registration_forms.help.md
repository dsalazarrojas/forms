# press_lounge_manager_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The press_lounge_manager_registration_form is a registration form designed for press lounge managers to provide their details. This form helps the system to store and update the necessary information to manage the press lounge effectively. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **General Info (Page 1)**: 
	* Enter your general information, including any details you want to be associated with your profile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`text`, Required: false): This field is for entering your general information, providing a brief description of yourself as a press lounge manager. 
* **Experience** (`select_multiple`, Required: false): Choose your relevant experience from the provided options to showcase your expertise and qualification. Select as many options as you like, or choose none if not applicable. 
* **Skills** (`text`, Required: false): List your relevant skills, separated by comma, to help us get a better understanding of what you can offer as a press lounge manager. You can select as many skills as you like, but it is not mandatory. 
## Tips

* Make sure to fill out your information accurately and truthfully, as it affects the management and communication within the press lounge.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact a manager or IT representative.
