# Vehicle Registration Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather vehicle registration information from the owner or authorized person, to verify and document vehicle ownership and authorization status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields marked with <code>required</code>
2. Enter the correct vehicle information (Vehicle Number and Vehicle Type)
3. Choose the correct authorization status (Authorization Type and Authorized Person Name)
4. Sign and date the form (Owner Signature and Authorized Person Signature)
5. Ensure all fields have valid data and are accurate

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Information (Vehicle Number)**: Enter the vehicle's unique identification number for registration purposes.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle being registered (Yes or No). This field helps categorize vehicles.
* **Owner Signature**: The owner of the vehicle must sign the form to confirm their ownership and agreement with the terms.
* **Authorized Person Name**: Enter the authorized person's name who is signing on behalf of the owner.
* **Authorized Person Signature**: The authorized person must sign the form to confirm their authorization to act on behalf of the owner.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date when the form was signed, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time of Signature**: Enter the time when the form was signed, in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle for registration purposes.
* **Authorization Type**: Select the type of authorization granted (Yes or No).
* **Authorized Person Name**: Enter the name of the person authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
* **Vehicle Owner Name**: Enter the name of the vehicle owner for registration purposes.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle being registered (Yes or No).
* **Vehicle Number**: Enter the unique identification number of the vehicle for registration purposes.
* **Authorization Information**: This field is not used in this form as there are separate fields for authorization status (Yes or No) and authorized person details.
* **Authorization Status**: This field is not used in this form as it is a sub-field of **Authorization Type** and **Authorized Person Name**.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields have valid data and are accurate.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting.
* Verify the vehicle's registration status before proceeding.
