# Ultimate Frisbee League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering players and teams into the Ultimate Frisbee League.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your player information, including your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Provide your team information, including your team name (if applicable).
3. Enter your payment information, including the payment method and amount.
4. Enter the date and time you would like to play.
5. Add any additional notes or comments (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information** (`player_info`, text, required): This field is where you can enter any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to your registration.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): This is your name.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): This is the name of your team (if you're registering as a team).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your phone number (if you want to be contactable by phone).
* **League Information** (`league_info`, text, required): This is where you can enter any information about the league that you think is important.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required): This is the number of players on your team (if you're registering as a team).
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required): This is where you can enter any information about your payment method and amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): This is how you are paying for the league. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): This is the amount you are paying.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): This is the date you would like to play.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This is the time you would like to play (if you want to specify).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This is where you can enter any additional notes or comments.
## Tips
Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
