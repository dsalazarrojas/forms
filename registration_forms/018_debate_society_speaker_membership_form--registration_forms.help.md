# Debate Society Speaker Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Debate Society Speaker Membership Form is a registration form designed for individuals interested in joining the Debate Society. The form collects information about your personal and professional details to help us understand your interests, expertise, and availability for potential debates and events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal and professional details.
2. Select your interests and expertise from the provided options.
3. Upload any necessary documents or resumes if required.
4. Confirm your name, email, and preferred contact method.
5. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Information (1)**: This field is a text field where you can enter your name.
* **Speaker Contact (2)**: This field is a text field where you can enter your contact information.
* **Contact Email (3)**: Enter your email address here.
* **Contact Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number here.
* **Speaker Interests (5)**: Choose your interests from the provided options (Debate, Politics, Current Events, Social Justice, Arts and Culture).
* **Speaker Expertise (6)**: Choose your level of expertise from the provided options (Advanced, Intermediate, Beginner).
* **Bio (7)**: Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Upload Documents (8)**: Upload any supporting documents if required.
* **Upload Resume (9)**: Upload your resume if required.
* **Upload Additional Docs (10)**: Upload any additional documents if required.
* **Submit (11)**: Click this button to submit your form.
* **Confirm Submit (12)**: Confirm that you have submitted your form correctly.
* **Name (13)**: Enter your name again for verification purposes.
* **Affiliation (14)**: Enter your affiliation (e.g., University, Organization).
* **Speaker Experience (15)**: Enter your speaking experience in years.
* **Debate Topics (16)**: Choose your debate topics from the provided options (Public Speaking, Debating, Argumentation, Philosophy, Politics).
* **Debate Format (17)**: Choose your preferred debate format from the provided options (In-Person, Online, Virtual).
* **Preferred Contact (18)**: Choose your preferred contact method from the provided options (Email, Phone, Message).
* **Additional Notes (19)**: Enter any additional notes or information.
* **Terms and Conditions (20)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Confirm Name (21)**: Enter your name again for verification purposes.
* **Confirm Email (22)**: Enter your email address again for verification purposes.
* **Confirm Phone (23)**: Enter your phone number again for verification purposes.
* **Confirm Terms (24)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Thank You Message (25)**: A message to thank you for your interest in joining the Debate Society.

Please note that some fields may not be required, but it's essential to fill them out correctly to ensure accurate processing of your application.
