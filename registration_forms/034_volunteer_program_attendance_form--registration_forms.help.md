The purpose of this form is to gather information from volunteers who have attended a program. The form is designed to collect data on the volunteer's name, the name of the program they attended, the date and time of the event, the location, the type of volunteer, contact number, email, and any additional notes or comments. This form is used to track and verify attendance for volunteer programs, which is a crucial task for program administrators and organizers. If you have already completed this form for a particular volunteer and program, do not attempt to submit it again.

## Volunteer Program Attendance Form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields (as indicated by a star \* in the form) are completed.
2. Provide accurate information for each field, as it will be used for official records and communication with volunteers.
3. Make sure to enter the correct volunteer name, program name, event date, event time, location, volunteer type, and contact number.
4. Enter a valid email address for the volunteer.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name**: Enter the name of the volunteer who attended the program.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program the volunteer attended.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the program in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the program in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the program, e.g., room number, building name, or venue name.
* **Volunteer Type**: Choose the type of volunteer (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Contact Number**: Enter the volunteer's contact number.
* **Email**: Enter the volunteer's email address.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the volunteer's attendance or participation.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out and accurate.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask for assistance from program administrators or organizers.
- Do not hesitate to contact us if you need further clarification on any field.
