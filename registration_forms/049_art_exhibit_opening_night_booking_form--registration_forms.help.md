# Art Exhibit Opening Night Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book attendance and gather information for the Art Exhibit Opening Night event. The purpose of this form is to collect details from potential attendees, including their name, contact information, and preferences for the event. This information will help us prepare for the event and provide personalized experiences for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your relationship to the gallery (e.g., artist, collector, friend).
3. Enter the name of the exhibit you would like to attend.
4. Choose the date and time for the opening night event.
5. Select the number of guests you will be bringing.
6. Confirm your attendance and provide any additional guest names.
7. If necessary, indicate any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
8. Specify whether you will need parking assistance.
9. If you have any special requests or notes, please include them in the "Any Special Requests or Notes" field.
10. Finally, let us know if you would like to receive gallery updates and how you heard about this event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (id: 2): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email Address** (id: 3): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone Number** (id: 4): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for important updates.
- **Relationship to Gallery** (id: 5): Select how you are connected to the gallery (e.g., artist, collector, friend).
- **Exhibit Name** (id: 7): Enter the name of the exhibit you would like to attend.
- **Opening Night Date** (id: 8): Select the date of the opening night event.
- **Opening Night Time** (id: 9): Choose the time of the opening night event.
- **Gallery Location or Address** (id: 10): Enter the location or address of the gallery.
- **Will You Attend Opening Night** (id: 12): Confirm that you will attend the opening night event.
- **Number of Guests Attending** (id: 13): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing with you.
- **Names of Additional Guests** (id: 14): Enter the names of your additional guests (if any).
- **Dietary Restrictions** (id: 16): If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them here.
- **Do you need accessibility accommodations** (id: 17): Indicate whether you need accessibility accommodations.
- **Describe Your Accessibility Needs** (id: 18): Provide details about your accessibility needs.
- **Will You Need Parking Assistance** (id: 19): Specify if you will need parking assistance.
- **Any Special Requests or Notes** (id: 21): Enter any special requests or notes you may have.
- **Would you like to receive gallery updates** (id: 22): Choose to receive gallery updates.
- **How did you hear about this event** (id: 23): Specify how you heard about this event.
