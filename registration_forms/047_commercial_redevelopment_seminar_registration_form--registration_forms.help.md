# Commercial Redevelopment Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for a Commercial Redevelopment Seminar. Please fill out the required fields to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the seminar, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name, email address, organization, and job title.
2.  Select your preferred seminar track.
3.  Choose your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`attendee_full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
*   **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, where a confirmation will be sent after registration.
*   **Organization/Company** (`attendee_organization`, `text`, required): Specify the name of your organization or company.
*   **Job Title** (`professional_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position within your organization.
*   **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
    +   **Real Estate Development**
    +   **Urban Planning**
    +   **Architecture and Design**
    +   **Construction**
    +   **Government/Municipal**
    +   **Finance/Investment**
*   **Preferred Seminar Track** (`selected_track`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following tracks:
    +   **Track A - Adaptive Reuse Strategies**
    +   **Track B - Financing Large-Scale Redevelopment**
    +   **Track C - Sustainable Urban Design**
    +   **Track D - Public-Private Partnerships**
*   **Interested in the Networking Breakout Session?** (`breakout_session_interest`, `select_one`, required): Yes or No
*   **Dietary Restrictions for Lunch** (`dietary_restrictions_seminar`, `text`, optional): Specify any dietary restrictions you may have, so we can ensure suitable arrangements.
*   **Special Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs_seminar`, `text`, optional): If you have any special accessibility requirements, please specify them here.
*   **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_seminar`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following:
    +   **Credit Card**
    +   **Corporate Invoice**
    +   **Wire Transfer**
