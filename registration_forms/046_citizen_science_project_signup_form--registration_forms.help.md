# Citizen Science Project Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in participating in citizen science projects. This form helps our team to assess your background, interests, and availability to match you with suitable projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and date of birth.
2. Select your highest education level and occupation (if applicable).
3. Choose the projects you are interested in participating in.
4. Select your availability for participation.
5. Indicate how many hours you can commit per week.
6. Choose your level of citizen science experience.
7. Select any relevant skills you have.
8. List any equipment you have available for use.
9. Indicate if you have reliable transportation.
10. Choose your preferred location for participation.
11. Select whether you prefer working alone or in a team.
12. List any additional training you need.
13. Share your motivation for participating in the project.
14. List any accessibility needs you have.
15. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
16. Agree to the liability waiver and photo release permission.
17. Select a start date for your participation.
18. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be listed in project documentation.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address we will use to contact you about project updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Home Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Please select your highest level of education completed.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): If you have a profession or occupation, please enter it.
* **Which Project Are You Interested In** (`project_selection`, select_multiple, required): Select all projects that you are interested in participating in.
* **When Are You Available** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Select all days of the week and times of day you are available.
* **How Many Hours Per Week Can You Commit** (`time_commitment`, select_one, required): Choose how many hours per week you can commit to participating in the project.
* **Previous Citizen Science Experience** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your level of experience with citizen science projects.
* **Relevant Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all skills you have that are relevant to participating in the project.
* **Equipment You Have Available** (`equipment`, select_multiple, required): Select all equipment you have available for use in the project.
* **Do You Have Reliable Transportation** (`transportation`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have reliable transportation to project locations.
* **Preferred Location for Participation** (`location_preference`, text, optional): If you have a preferred location for participating, please enter it.
* **Do You Prefer Working Alone or in a Team** (`team_preference`, select_one, required): Choose whether you prefer working alone or in a team.
* **What Training Would You Need** (`training_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all training you need to participate in the project.
* **Why Do You Want to Participate** (`motivation`, text, required): Share your motivation for participating in the project.
* **Any Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please list them.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to the liability waiver and photo release permission.
* **Photo Release Permission** (`photo_release`, select_one, required): Grant or deny permission for photos to be taken for project documentation.
* **When Can You Start** (`start_date`, date, required): Choose a start date for your participation.
* **Any Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
