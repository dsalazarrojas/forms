# Data Driven Operations Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect data from participants for training events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information section with your name, email, and contact phone number.
2. Select the participant start and end dates and times.
3. Choose your registration type and status from the dropdown menus.
4. Enter the event ID if required.
5. Select your location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Participant Information, text, required/optional)**: Enter your name, email, and contact phone number in this section.
* **Name (Name, text, required/optional)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email (Email, email, required/optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, required/optional)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Participant Start Date (Participant Start Date, date, required/optional)**: Enter the date you plan to start participating.
* **Participant Start Time (Participant Start Time, time, required/optional)**: Enter the time you plan to start participating.
* **Participant End Date (Participant End Date, date, required/optional)**: Enter the date you plan to end participating.
* **Participant End Time (Participant End Time, time, required/optional)**: Enter the time you plan to end participating.
* **Registration Type (Registration Type, select_one, required/optional)**: Choose the type of registration you are submitting (Yes/No).
* **Registration Status (Registration Status, select_one, required/optional)**: Choose the status of your registration (Open/Closed/Cancelled).
* **Event ID (Event ID, number, required/optional)**: Enter the event ID if required.
* **Location (Location, text, required/optional)**: Select your location.
