# Prop Trading Intensive Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prop Trading Intensive Registration form is designed to gather necessary information from participants to register them for our Prop Trading Intensive program. This form is used to ensure that all participants have a unique email address and to gather their experience level, trading goals, and program selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's details in the Participant Details section, including experience level, trading goals, and email address.
2. Select the Prop Intensive Programs that apply to you in the Program Selection section.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This section collects personal and program-specific details from participants.
	+ **Experience Level**: This field asks for your level of trading experience. Please select from the options provided.
	+ **Participant Goals**: This field is for your trading goals. Enter your goals in the text box provided.
	+ **Participant Experience**: This field asks for your years of trading experience.
	+ **Participant Email**: This is the participant's email address. Please enter a unique email address that is not already registered in the system.
	+ **Participant Phone**: This field is for your contact phone number.
* **Program Selection**: 
	+ **Prop Intensive Programs**: Select the Prop Intensive Programs that apply to you. This field allows you to select multiple programs.

## Tips
Please ensure that you select all relevant Prop Intensive Programs that apply to you. This will help us to ensure that you are registered in the correct program. Also, please enter a unique email address to avoid duplicate registrations.
