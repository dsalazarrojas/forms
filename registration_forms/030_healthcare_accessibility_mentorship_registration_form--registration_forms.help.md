# Healthcare Accessibility Mentorship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register individuals or entities for a healthcare accessibility mentorship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether or not you are registering for the mentorship program.
2. Provide contact information, including your email address and phone number (if applicable).
3. Choose your availability for the mentorship program.
4. Select the mentorship programs you are interested in or currently participating in.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
6. Choose your desired mentor for the program.
7. Select the program you are applying for.
8. Choose your application status.
9. Select who submitted the application.
10. Choose the submission status.
11. Provide submission details, including date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mentorship Application Form** (Yes/No, required): Select whether or not you are registering for the mentorship program.
* **Contact Information** (Select One, required): Choose your contact information type: Option 1, Option 2.
* **Availability** (Select Multiple, required): Choose your availability for the mentorship program. Options include time slots or days of the week.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (Note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Mentorship Programs** (Select Multiple, required): Select the mentorship programs you are interested in or currently participating in.
* **Additional Comments** (Note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Mentor** (Select One, required): Choose your desired mentor for the program.
* **Program** (Select One, required): Select the program you are applying for.
* **Application Status** (Select One, required): Choose your application status.
* **Submitted By** (Select One, required): Choose who submitted the application.
* **Submission** (Select One, required): Choose the submission status.
* **Submission 1** (Select One, not required): Not applicable for this form. 
* **Submission 2** (Select One, not required): Not applicable for this form.
* **Submission 3** (Select One, not required): Not applicable for this form.
* **Submission 4** (Select One, not required): Not applicable for this form.
* **Submission 5** (Select One, not required): Not applicable for this form.
* **Submission 6** (Select One, not required): Not applicable for this form.
