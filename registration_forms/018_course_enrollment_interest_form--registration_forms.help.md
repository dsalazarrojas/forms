# Course Enrollment Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Enrollment Interest Form is designed to collect information from users interested in enrolling in various courses. This information will help us understand your preferences and demographics, and we will use it to contact you to discuss further details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Follow the instructions on each page to ensure you submit all required information.
2.  Enter your responses in the text areas provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Page (\*)

* **First Page (\*):** 
This is the first page of the form. It is a text field where you can enter any information you'd like about yourself or the course you are interested in. Please make sure to enter all required information in this field.

### Second Page (\*)

* **Second Page (\*):** 
This is the second page of the form. It is a text field where you can enter any additional information related to your interest in the course. Please make sure to enter all required information in this field.

### Third Page (\*)

* **Third Page (\*):** 
This is the third page of the form. It is a text field where you can enter any final information related to your interest in the course. Please make sure to enter all required information in this field.

## Tips
