# Club Membership Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the Club Membership Order Form for individuals interested in joining a club for networking, learning, events, and community service. The form aims to collect your personal details, address information, membership preferences, and payment data to process your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2.  Enter your phone number and date of birth.
3.  Provide your address information, such as street address, city, state/province, and country.
4.  Select your preferred membership type (e.g., Standard, Premium, or VIP).
5.  Choose the membership duration that suits you best (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or annually).
6.  Select the start date for your membership.
7.  Choose your primary interests or focus areas (e.g., networking, learning, events, or community service).
8.  Decide whether you'd like to subscribe to the club's newsletter.
9.  Specify your preferred communication method (e.g., email, phone, or text).
10. Enter the membership fee and initial payment amount in USD.
11.  Choose your payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, or bank transfer).
12.  Finally, accept the membership terms and conditions by selecting "Yes, I accept."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your street address or postal address.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside or work.
*   **State - Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you reside or work.
*   **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code or zip code of your address.
*   **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country where you reside or work.
*   **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred membership type, such as Standard, Premium, or VIP.
*   **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your membership (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or annually).
*   **Membership Start Date** (`membership_term_start`, date, required): Enter the date when you'd like your membership to begin.
*   **Primary Interests or Focus Areas** (`primary_interests`, select_multiple, required): Choose your primary interests or focus areas, such as networking, learning, or events.
*   **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`newsletter_subscription`, select_one, required): Select whether you'd like to subscribe to the club's newsletter.
*   **Preferred Communication Method** (`communication_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or text).
*   **Membership Fee - USD** (`membership_fee`, number, required): Enter the membership fee in USD based on your selected tier.
*   **Initial Payment Amount - USD** (`initial_payment`, number, required): Enter the initial payment amount in USD.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
