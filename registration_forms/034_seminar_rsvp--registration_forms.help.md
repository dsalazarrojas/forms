# seminar_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to RSVP (Respond to a Seminar) by indicating their presence, providing their contact information, and selecting their preferred attendance options. It collects essential details for seminar organization and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your attendance status from the dropdown menu for 'attendees'.
2. Fill in your name as you would like it to be known during the seminar.
3. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter your email address for communication updates and reminders.
5. Choose a date for your RSVP, when you can attend the seminar.
6. Select your estimated arrival time to help with seminar organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendees (Select)**:
	+ This field asks whether you will be attending the seminar or not.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you are planning to attend.
	+ Choose 'No' if you are unable to attend.
* **Name**:
	+ Enter the name you would like to be known by during the seminar.
	+ This can be your full name or a nickname.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number for seminar organizers to contact you.
* **Email**:
	+ Provide your email address for seminar updates, reminders, and communication.
* **RSVP Date**:
	+ Select the date you will attend the seminar.
	+ This helps the organizers with event planning.
* **Arrival Time**:
	+ Choose your estimated arrival time to help with seminar organization.
	+ This ensures that you are accounted for and can be greeted properly.

## Tips
- Double-check your details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Make sure to select a date and arrival time that suits your schedule.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, consider mentioning them in the comments section (not provided in this form).
- Contact the seminar organizers if you have any questions or concerns.
- Keep an eye on your email for updates and reminders about the seminar.
