# Employee Training Program Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Training Program Attendance Form is used to record your attendance status, session details, and other relevant information for quality control, monitoring, and evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including:
	* Employee Training: A text field for general training information.
	* Training Session: Select one option (yes or no) to indicate if you attended the training session.
	* Session Date: Enter the date of the training session.
	* Session Time: Enter the time of the training session.
	* Attendance Status: Select multiple options to describe your attendance (e.g., attended, partially attended, did not attend).
	* Training Location: Select one option (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2) or enter a custom location.
	* Session Duration: Select one option (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2) or enter a custom duration.
	* Session Time: Enter the time of the training session.
	* Employee Email: Enter your work email address.
	* Employee Phone: Enter your phone number.
	* Training Coordinator: Enter the name of the person who coordinated the training program.
	* Comments: Enter any additional comments or notes.
	* Training Program: Enter the name of the training program.
	* Session Type: Enter the type of session (e.g., workshop, seminar, or presentation).
	* Session Type Description: Enter a brief description of the session type.
	* Attendance Date: Enter the date you attended the training session.
	* Session Start Date: Enter the start date of the training session.
	* Session End Date: Enter the end date of the training session.
	* Training Location: Select one option (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2) or enter a custom location.
	* Session Duration: Select one option (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2) or enter a custom duration.
	* Session Time: Enter the time of the training session.
2. Review and submit the form when finished.
