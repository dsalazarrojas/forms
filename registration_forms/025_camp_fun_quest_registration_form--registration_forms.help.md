# Camp Fun Quest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for the Camp Fun Quest program. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an * (asterisk).
2. For select fields (e.g., "Gender", "Quest Theme", "Dietary Requirements"), choose one option from the list.
3. Enter dates and phone numbers in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY for dates, with no dashes).
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`quest_participant_name`, `text`, *): Enter the full name of the participant.
* **Date of Birth** (`quest_participant_dob`, `date`, *): Enter the participant's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2000).
* **Gender** (`quest_participant_gender`, `select_one`, *): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-binary
	+ Other
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`guardian_full_name_quest`, `text`, *): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`guardian_phone_quest`, `text`, *): Enter the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email Address** (`guardian_email_quest`, `email`, *): Enter the contact email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Select Your Quest Theme** (`quest_theme_choice`, `select_one`, *): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Treasure Island Mystery
	+ Galactic Explorers
	+ Medieval Kingdom
	+ Jungle Adventure
	+ Superhero Training Academy
* **Participant Experience Level** (`quest_experience_level`, `select_one`, *): Choose one of the following options:
	+ First-time Quester
	+ Experienced Explorer
	+ Master Adventurer
* **Known Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions_quest`, `text`, ): Enter any known medical conditions of the participant (e.g., Asthma, epilepsy, heart concerns).
* **Known Allergies** (`allergies_list_quest`, `text`, *): Enter any known allergies of the participant (e.g., food, medicine, environmental).
* **Does the Participant Carry an Epi-Pen?** (`epi_pen_carrier`, `select_one`, *): Choose one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements_quest`, `select_multiple`, ): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Nut-Free
	+ Gluten-Free
	+ Dairy-Free
* **Media Release Consent** (`media_release_consent_quest`, `select_one`, *): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - I Consent
	+ No - I do not consent
* **Liability Waiver and Release** (`liability_waiver_quest`, `select_one`, *): Choose one of the following options:
	+ I Accept and Agree
	+ Other
* **Payment Method Preference** (`payment_preference_quest`, `select_one`, *): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Online Credit Card
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Pay at Registration
* **Registration Submission Date** (`quest_reg_timestamp`, `date`, *): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024).
