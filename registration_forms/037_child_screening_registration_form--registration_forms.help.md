# Child Screening Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for child screening registration, where parents or guardians can provide their contact information and their child's details for a screening appointment. The form helps us to gather essential information about the child and their needs for the screening process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration information section with your contact details.
2. Provide the child's details in the child details section.
3. Select the type of screening requested.
4. Choose a preferred location for the screening.
5. If applicable, indicate if the child has had a previous screening and list any current concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Name**: (`guardian_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Email Address**: (`guardian_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and results.
* **Primary Phone Number**: (`guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter the primary phone number of the child's contact person.
* **Child Full Name**: (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the child.
* **Date of Birth**: (`child_dob`, date, required): Select the date of birth of the child.
* **Child Gender**: (`child_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the child's gender.
* **Type of Screening Requested**: (`screening_type`, select_one, required): Choose the primary area of screening requested.
* **Preferred Screening Location**: (`preferred_location`, select_one, required): Choose a convenient location for the screening.
* **Has Child Had a Previous Screening?**: (`previous_screening`, select_one, required): Indicate if the child has had a previous screening.
* **List any Current Concerns**: (`current_concerns`, text, optional): List any current concerns or issues related to the screening area.
* **Preferred Screening Date**: (`preferred_screening_date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred date for the screening, subject to availability.
* **Child Screening Information**: (`section_registration_info`, note): This section provides background information for registration purposes.

Note: All fields marked with `*` are required.
