# Medical Humanities Conference Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees and speakers for the Medical Humanities Conference. The form is used to gather preferences, roles, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through the form to ensure you understand the purpose and fields involved.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, taking into account the specific requirements and preferences.
3. Fill in the fields with accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Choices** (`session_choices`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the sessions you would like to attend by choosing the options 'Yes' for each relevant session.
* **Roles** (`roles`, select_one, optional): Choose your role at the conference, selecting 'Yes' or 'No' as your response.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of yourself, including any relevant information you would like to share with other attendees.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_one, optional): Select your preference for the session time and date, choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Session** (`session`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the session you are interested in, but not among the listed options.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start the session, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to end the session, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to have the session, in the 24-hour format HH:MM.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but it's essential to fill in all relevant information to ensure your preferences are accurately recorded.
