# Speechwriting Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speechwriting Workshop Booking Form is used to register participants for a Speechwriting Workshop. This form helps the organizers collect necessary information from attendees, including participant details, contact information, workshop preferences, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details in the "Participant Details" field.
2. Fill in your contact details, including email and phone number, in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the workshop you are interested in from the "Workshop Selection" field.
4. Choose your preferred time for the workshop from the "Preferred Time" field.
5. Indicate your availability to attend the workshop in the "Availability" field.
6. Add any comments or additional information in the "Comments" field.
7. Confirm your agreement to the workshop terms in the "Agreement" field.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details (1)** (`participant_details`, text, false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details (2)** (`contact_details`, text, false): Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.

### Workshop Details
* **Workshop Details (3)** (`workshop_details`, text, false): Enter any additional information about the workshop you are interested in.

### Preferred Time
* **Preferred Time (4)** (`preferred_time`, time, false): Select your preferred time for the workshop. You can choose a specific time or leave it blank for default.

### Availability
* **Availability (5)** (`availability`, select_multiple, false): Indicate your availability to attend the workshop. Select multiple options if applicable.

### Workshop Selection
* **Workshop Selection (6)** (`workshop_selection`, select_one, false): Choose the workshop you are interested in attending.

### Comments
* **Comments (7)** (`comments`, note, false): Add any additional comments or information about your workshop preferences.

### Agreement
* **Agreement (8)** (`agreement`, select_one, false): Confirm your agreement to the workshop terms.

### Email
* **Email (9)** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone (10)** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number.

### Date
* **Date (11)** (`date`, date, false): Select the date you would like to attend the workshop.

Remember to review your submission carefully before submitting it.
