# Catering Service Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Service Portal Access Form is a registration form for users to access the catering service portal. This form collects personal and account information, as well as preferences for portal access and notification.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll through the form and fill in the required fields (marked as "required").
2. Fill in all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Fill in your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill in your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Fill in your email address which will be used as your username for the portal. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Fill in your phone number for contact purposes.
* **User Role** (`user_role`, select_one, required): Select the type of user you are (Client, Catering Manager, Event Planner, Administrator, Other).
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of account you wish to create (Individual, Business, Non-Profit, Government, Educational).
* **Portal Features of Interest** (`portal_features`, select_multiple, required): Select the portal features you are interested in using (Menu Selection, Order Management, Payment Processing, Reporting and Analytics, Customer Feedback, Event Scheduling, Inventory Management).
* **Notification Preference** (`notification_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select the method you prefer to receive notifications (Email, Phone, SMS, In-App, None).
* **Expected Frequency of Portal Use** (`frequency_of_use`, select_one, required): Select how often you expect to use the portal (Daily, Multiple Times Per Week, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Fill in your organization's name if you are registering on behalf of an organization.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, required): Fill in your organization's address if you are registering on behalf of an organization.
* **City, State, Zip Code** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Fill in your organization's city, state, and zip code if you are registering on behalf of an organization.
* **Years of Catering Experience** (`catering_experience`, select_one, required): Select the level of your catering experience (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years, No experience).
* **Technical Proficiency Level** (`tech_proficiency`, select_one, required): Select your technical proficiency level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert).
* **I agree to the terms of service and privacy policy** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service and privacy policy.
* **I consent to receive updates and information about catering services** (`communication_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to receive updates and information about catering services.

Please fill in all required fields and submit the form to complete your registration.
