# Reusable Product Booth Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from vendors for the internal assessment of their products. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that our internal teams can evaluate their products efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
   - **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the company or vendor applying for the product booth.
   - **Vendor Product**: Provide a brief description of the product you are applying for.
   - **Vendor Business**: Enter the name of the business related to the product or vendor.
   - **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this application.
   - **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
   - **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
   - **Product Details**: Enter a detailed description of the product.

2. Make sure to fill in all fields marked as "required" accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name**: This is the name of the company or vendor applying for the product booth.
* **Vendor Product**: This is a brief description of the product you are applying for. Please be specific about the product and its features.
* **Vendor Business**: Enter the name of the business related to the product or vendor. This could be the name of the company that owns the product or the company's department/section responsible for the product.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this application.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person to ensure that we can get in touch with them if needed.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person so that we can reach them if needed.
* **Vendor Address**: Although not required, if you want to provide the address of the vendor's office or the company's location, please enter it here.
* **Product Details**: This is the most important part of the application. Please enter a detailed description of the product, including its features, benefits, and any other relevant information.
* **Business**: Although not required, providing a brief description of the business can help our internal teams to understand the context of the product and its fit within our business strategy.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
