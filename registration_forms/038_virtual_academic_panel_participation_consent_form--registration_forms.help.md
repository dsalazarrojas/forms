# virtual_academic_panel_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Academic Panel Participation Consent Form is used to register participants in a virtual academic panel and collect their details, including consent status and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section by providing your name, email, and institution.
2. Select your role in the virtual academic panel.
3. Enter the purpose of your participation.
4. Provide your consent by selecting one of the available options.
5. Review the terms and conditions and acknowledge your agreement.
6. Add any additional information as required.
7. Confirm your participant status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: Enter your name and any additional details required in this section.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive important updates and information related to the event.
* **Institution**: Enter your institution's name and any other relevant details.
* **Role**: Select your role in the virtual academic panel, such as student, staff, or faculty.
* **Consent**: Select one of the available options to indicate your consent status.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions of participation.
* **Participant Agreement**: Select one of the available options to indicate your agreement status.
* **Event Details**: Select the event details that apply to you.
* **Participant Info**: Select any additional information that applies to you.
* **Contact Details**: Select any contact details that apply to you.
* **Participant Address**: Select any additional address details that apply to you.
* **Event Schedule**: Select any schedule details that apply to you.
* **Participant Schedule**: Indicate your schedule status, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Event Participants**: Indicate if you are willing to participate in the event, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Additional Info**: Select any additional information that applies to you.
* **Agreement Date**: Select the date of your agreement.
* **Agreement Time**: Select the time of your agreement.
* **Participant Status**: Select your participant status, one of the available options.

Tips:
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before proceeding.
* Verify that the participant details you provide are accurate.
* Confirm your participant status before submitting the form.
