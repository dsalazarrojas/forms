# Marine Biology Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marine Biology Workshop Admission Form is designed to gather information from participants about their interest in attending the Marine Biology Workshop. This form helps us understand who is interested in attending, their preferences for the workshop type, dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
2. Select the type of workshop you are interested in attending (Marine Biology or Ecology).
3. Choose the duration of the workshop (1 day, 2 days, or 3 days).
4. Indicate if you will be attending the workshop (True or False).
5. If you will be attending, provide your workshop capacity (number of attendees you will be bringing).
6. Enter the workshop location, if applicable.
7. Provide any additional comments or information about the workshop.
