# Online School Setup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about an online school, including its name, type, location, contact details, and administrative information. The data collected through this form will be used to create a comprehensive profile of your school in our system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `School Name` field with the name of your school.
2. Select the type of school from the options provided in the `School Type` field.
3. Enter the location of your school in the `School Location` field.
4. Provide the contact number of your school in the `Contact Number` field.
5. Enter the website of your school in the `Website` field.
6. Select the timezone of your school from the options provided in the `Timezone` field.
7. Fill in the `Launch Date` and `Launch Time` fields with the dates and times when your school will be launched.
8. Select the timezone of your school director in the `School Director Timezone` field.
9. Fill in the `School Director Name`, `School Director Title`, and `School Director Email` fields with the relevant details.
10. Enter the location of your school director in the `School Director Location` field.
11. Select the timezone of your school director from the options provided in the `School Director Timezone` field.
12. Fill in the `School Director Launch Date` and `School Director Launch Time` fields with the dates and times when your school director will be launched.
13. Provide any additional information about your school director in the `School Director Additional Info` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of your school.
* **School Type** (Optional, Select One): Select the type of school from the available options.
* **School Location** (Optional, Text): Enter the location of your school.
* **Contact Number** (Optional, Text): Enter the contact number of your school.
* **Website** (Optional, Text): Enter the website of your school.
* **Timezone** (Optional, Select One): Select the timezone of your school.
* **Launch Date** (Optional, Date): Fill in the launch date of your school.
* **Launch Time** (Optional, Time): Fill in the launch time of your school.
* **Time Zone** (Optional, Select One): Select the timezone of your school.
* **Timezone Note** (Optional, Note): Provide any additional information about your school's timezone.
* **School Email** (Required, Email): Enter the email of your school.
* **School Admin Email** (Required, Email): Enter the email of your school admin.
* **School Admin Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of your school admin.
* **School Admin Title** (Required, Text): Enter the title of your school admin.
* **School Director Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of your school director.
* **School Director Title** (Required, Text): Enter the title of your school director.
* **School Director Email** (Optional, Email): Enter the email of your school director.
* **School Director Phone** (Optional, Text): Enter the phone number of your school director.
* **School Director Location** (Required, Text): Enter the location of your school director.
* **School Director Timezone** (Optional, Select One): Select the timezone of your school director.
* **School Director Launch Date** (Optional, Date): Fill in the launch date of your school director.
* **School Director Launch Time** (Optional, Time): Fill in the launch time of your school director.
* **School Director Timezone Note** (Optional, Note): Provide any additional information about your school director's timezone.
* **School Director Website** (Optional, Text): Enter the website of your school director.
* **Additional Info** (Optional, Text): Provide any additional information about your school director.

Note: The fields marked as "Optional" can be skipped if not applicable to your school.
