# Charter School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for new students applying to a charter school. It collects essential information about the student and their parent or guardian to streamline the admission process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Read through each section carefully before filling out any information.
*   Enter the student's first and last name exactly as it appears on official documents, such as government ID or passport.
*   Format the date of birth as MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/12/1999).
*   Select the correct grade the student is applying for from the list.
*   Provide accurate and up-to-date information for the parent or guardian's contact details.
*   For the parent or guardian's address, include the correct city and state or province code.
*   Select the correct school district if the student is currently attending school.
*   If the student receives special education services, select 'Yes' for that option.
*   If applicable, mention any additional support or accommodations the student may need.
*   Ensure accurate and up-to-date academic performance information, if applicable.
*   Select 'Yes' to consent to the admission process if you have read and understood it.
*   Review the information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter the student's first name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Last Name**: Enter the student's last name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Date of Birth**: Format the date as MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Gender**: Select the student's gender.
*   **Grade Applying For**: Choose the correct grade the student is applying for.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian providing this information.
*   **Parent Phone Number**: Enter the parent or guardian's contact phone number.
*   **Parent Email Address**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
*   **Home Address**: Enter the student's home address.
*   **City**: Enter the city of the parent or guardian's residence.
*   **State or Province**: Enter the state or province code.
*   **Zip Code**: Enter the postal code.
*   **Current School District**: Enter the current school district if the student is attending school.
*   **Academic Performance**: Select the approximate academic performance level of the student, if applicable.
*   **Special Education Services**: Select 'Yes' if the student receives special education services.
*   **Student Interests and Strengths**: Mention any notable interests or strengths of the student.
*   **Additional Support or Accommodations**: Mention any additional support or accommodations the student may need.
*   **Parent Consent**: Select 'Yes' to consent to the admission process.
*   **Application Submission Date**: Select today's date, which will be automatically populated with the current date.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
*   Double-check the information before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the charter school's administration for assistance.
