# AI In Medicine Symposium Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The AI In Medicine Symposium Registration form is designed to collect information about attendees to provide a personalized experience and ensure that we can cater to their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your email address for registration confirmation and updates.
3. If needed, add your phone number for urgent communication.
4. Select your professional title (e.g., Dr., Prof., MD, RN, PhD).
5. Enter your institution or organization name (e.g., hospital, clinic, university, company).
6. Choose your experience level with AI in medicine (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional).
7. Indicate if you will be attending in-person, virtually, or both.
8. Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies.
9. Check any accessibility requirements or special requests.
10. Select your networking interests and preferred session topics.
11. Provide emergency contact information if needed.
12. Indicate how you heard about the symposium.
13. Make any special requests or accommodations.
14. Choose if you want to receive information about future medical AI events.
15. Add any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): We'll use this for registration confirmation and updates.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): Optional, for urgent communication if needed.
* **Professional title** (`professional_title`, text, optional): e.g., Dr., Prof., MD, RN, PhD.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required): e.g., hospital, clinic, university, company name.
* **Medical specialty or area of expertise** (`specialty_area`, text, optional): e.g., Radiology, Surgery, Cardiology, General Practice.
* **Experience level with AI in medicine** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional.
* **Registration type** (`attendance_type`, select_one, required): Choose In-person, Virtual, Hybrid (attend both).
* **Dietary restrictions or allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Please specify any food allergies or dietary requirements.
* **Accessibility requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Choose Wheelchair access, Sign language interpreter, Large print materials, Audio assistance, Seating near front, None required.
* **Preferred session topics** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply: Diagnostic imaging AI, Surgical robotics, Drug discovery AI, Patient monitoring systems, Electronic health records, Ethics in medical AI, Regulatory considerations, AI in mental health.
* **Networking interests** (`networking_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Choose Researchers, Clinicians, Tech developers, Hospital administrators, Investors, Policy makers.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): In case of emergency during the symposium.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_phone`, phone, optional): Optional.
* **How you heard about the symposium** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Choose Social media, Colleague recommendation, Medical journal, Conference website, Previous attendee, Professional association, Other.
* **Special requests or accommodations** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Any additional needs we should be aware of.
* **Marketing consent** (`marketing_consent`, select_one, optional): Choose True, False.
* **Additional comments or questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other information you'd like to share.

Note: All fields with `required` set to `true` are essential to complete. Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately to allow us to provide a personalized experience for you.</thinking>
