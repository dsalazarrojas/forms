# photographer_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photographer_onboarding_form is a registration form designed for photographers to onboard and provide essential information about themselves, their contact details, and their studio. It's intended to streamline the process of registering photographers for various purposes, such as exhibitions, collaborations, or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on each page, marked as "required".
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration** (1): Enter your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share about yourself as a photographer.
* **Contact** (2): Provide your contact information, such as email, phone number, or social media handle, so that we can reach out to you.
* **Photographer** (3): Give us an overview of your photography style, experience, or any notable projects you've worked on.
* **Studio** (4): Share information about your studio, such as its name, address, and contact details.
* **Upload Photos** (5): Attach photos that showcase your work. This will help us get a better understanding of your photography style and experience.
* **Review and Submit** (6): Double-check your information and upload your photos. Once you're satisfied with your answers, click the submit button to complete your registration.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible, especially when sharing your contact information and studio details.
* Use clear and legible fonts when uploading photos.
* Ensure your studio address is accurate and up-to-date.
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
