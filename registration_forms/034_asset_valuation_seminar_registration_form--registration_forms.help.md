# Asset Valuation Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Valuation Seminar Registration Form is designed to gather attendee information for planning and logistical purposes. It helps us ensure that attendees' specific needs, preferences, and requirements are captured accurately to provide an enjoyable and productive experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details in the following fields:
	* **Attendee Name**: Enter your full name as it should be recorded on the seminar records.
	* **Job Title**: Provide your job title or position in your organization.
	* **Company/Organization**: Specify the name of your company or organization.
	* **Industry**: Choose the industry related to your work.
	* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for confirmation and communication.
	* **Phone Number**: Provide your contact phone number.
2. Choose your seminar details:
	* **Seminar Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the seminar.
	* **Seminar Format**: Choose the format of the seminar that suits your needs (In Person, Virtual Live Stream, or Hybrid).
	* **Venue Location**: Select the location of the seminar if you are attending in person.
	* **Valuation Experience Level**: Choose your level of experience in asset valuation (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
3. Select your preferences:
	* **Topics of Interest**: Choose the topics you would like to attend or learn about during the seminar.
	* **Session Preference**: Select the session type that suits your schedule (Full Day Seminar, Morning Session Only, Afternoon Session Only, or Multi-Day Workshop).
	* **Meal Preference**: Choose your dietary preference (Standard, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Kosher, or Halal).
4. Provide additional information:
	* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special accessibility needs, describe them here.
	* **Registration Type**: Choose the type of registration that applies to you (Early Bird, Standard, Group Rate, Student Rate, or VIP Package).
	* **Registration Fee**: Enter the registration fee you paid or will pay.
	* **Payment Method**: Choose how you made or will make the payment (Credit Card, Company Invoice, Purchase Order, Bank Transfer, or Check).
	* **Purchase Order Number**: If applicable, provide the purchase order number.
	* **Request Certificate of Attendance**: Choose whether you want a certificate of attendance.
	* **Need Continuing Education Credits**: Choose whether you need continuing education credits.
	* **Interested in Networking Events**: Choose whether you are interested in networking events.
	* **Need Hotel Accommodation**: Choose whether you need hotel accommodation.
	* **Number of Hotel Nights**: If you need accommodation, enter the number of nights you require.
5. Provide your agreement:
	* **Consent to Receive Marketing**: Choose whether you consent to receive marketing emails from us.
	* **How Did You Hear About Seminar**: Choose how you heard about the seminar (Email Newsletter, Social Media, Colleague, Professional Association, Website, or Other).
6. Sign off:
	* **Attendee Signature**: Type your name to complete the registration.
