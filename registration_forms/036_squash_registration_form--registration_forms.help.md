# Squash Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Squash Registration Form is designed to collect participant information for squash tournaments or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above and review each section carefully to ensure accurate and complete data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information:**
	+ **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's name as it should be displayed on their registration.
	+ **Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
	+ **Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Squash Information:**
	+ **Squash Experience**: Indicate if the participant has experience with squash (Yes/No).
	+ **Tennis Level**: Specify the participant's tennis level (High/Low).
	+ **Squash Level**: Specify the participant's squash level (High/Low).
* **Tournament Information:**
	+ **Participating in Tournament**: Indicate if the participant will be participating in a tournament (Yes/No).
	+ **Tournament Date**: Enter the date of the tournament in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
	+ **Venue**: Enter the venue where the tournament will take place.
	+ **Entrants**: Indicate if the participant will be entrants (Yes/No).
* **Player Details:**
	+ **Player Status**: Specify the participant's status (Active/Inactive).
	+ **Squash Club**: Enter the participant's squash club name.
	+ **Player Squash Club**: Enter the participant's squash club name.
	+ **Player Squash Club Status**: Specify the participant's squash club status (Active/Inactive).
* **Payment Method:**
	+ **Payment Method**: Indicate if the participant will be paying for the tournament (Yes/No).

Note: Ensure you fill in all required fields to complete your registration accurately.
