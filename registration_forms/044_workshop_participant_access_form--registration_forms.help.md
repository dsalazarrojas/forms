# Workshop Participant Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Participant Access Form is used to collect information from workshop participants, ensuring they have the necessary access and resources during the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used for communication related to the workshop.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. In the "Participant Details" field, describe any relevant information about your participation in the workshop.
6. For each question, select the corresponding answer from the dropdown list:
    * "Partcipant Status" - Select "Active" to confirm your participation in the workshop, or "Inactive" otherwise.
    * "Workshop Participant Access Form Part10" - Select "Yes" if you have attended the workshop before, or "No" otherwise.
    * "Workshop Participant Access Form Part18" - Enter a number to indicate your level of experience with the workshop topic.
7. In "Participant Notes", provide any additional information about your workshop participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for workshop communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you have one.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, optional): Describe your participation in the workshop.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, `select_one`, required): Select "Active" to confirm your participation, or "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Workshop Participant Access Form Part10** (`workshop_participant_access_form_part10`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have attended the workshop before, select "Yes".
* **Workshop Participant Access Form Part18** (`workshop_participant_access_form_part18`, `number`, optional): Enter a number to indicate your level of experience with the workshop topic.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about your workshop participation.
* **Workshop Participant Access Form Part17** (`workshop_participant_access_form_part17`, `text`, optional): Describe your workshop participation.
