# Tourism Regeneration Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in the Tourism Regeneration Initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details on page 1 (Participant Details).
2. Select your project interests on page 2 (Project Interests).
3. Choose your contribution type on page 3 (Contribution Type).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in your name and any other basic details you'd like to share.
* **Project Interests** (`project_interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the types of projects you are interested in, such as Tourism Recovery, Tourism Development, or Tourism Innovation.
	+ Tourism Recovery: Projects focused on rebuilding and rejuvenating tourism infrastructure and attractions.
	+ Tourism Development: Projects aimed at expanding tourism offerings and services.
	+ Tourism Innovation: Projects exploring new and creative ways to experience and engage with tourism.
* **Contribution Type** (`contribution_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would like to contribute to the initiative, such as making a financial contribution or offering your skills and expertise.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select all that apply for Project Interests, as you can be interested in multiple types of projects.
* If you have any other type of contribution in mind, please use the "Other (please describe)" option under Contribution Type.
