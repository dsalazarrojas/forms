# Auction Bid Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auction Bid Form is designed for bidders to submit their bids on items in an auction. To complete the form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the bidder information section, providing your bidder number (if known), full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter the item lot number and a description of the item you are bidding on.
3. Set your maximum bid amount.
4. Select your preferred bid type (Absentee Bid, Phone Bid, or In-Person Bid).
5. Accept the auction terms and conditions by checking the I Agree box.
6. Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Wire Transfer, Cash, or Check).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Bidder Number** (`bidder_number`, text, optional): Enter your assigned bidder number if you have one.
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Your full name is required to complete the form.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive important bidding information.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your bid.
- **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address for invoicing purposes.
- **Item Lot Number** (`item_lot_number`, text, required): Find the item lot number in the auction catalog.
- **Item Name or Description** (`item_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the item you are bidding on.
- **Maximum Bid Amount** (`bid_amount`, number, required): Set your maximum bid amount for the item.
- **Bid Type** (`bid_type`, select_one, required): Select Absentee Bid, Phone Bid, or In-Person Bid for the item.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Check the box to accept the auction terms and conditions.
- **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Wire Transfer, Cash, or Check).
