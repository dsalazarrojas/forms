# Vendor Registration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Registration Request Form is a form used by businesses and individuals to register with the organization. It collects necessary information such as business name, contact details, and services offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the business name field with your company's or business's name as it is registered.
2. Provide a valid email address in the contact email field.
3. Fill out the contact phone field with your phone number.
4. Fill out the address fields with your business's or company's address.
5. Select the services offered by your business or company from the options provided.
6. Fill out the company name field with your company's name.
7. Select the company type from the options provided.
8. Select the company status from the options provided.
9. Fill out the owner name field with your name.
10. Fill out the owner phone field with your phone number.
11. Fill out the owner email field with your email address.
12. Fill out the owner address fields with your address.
13. Fill out the owner address line 2 field with your address line 2.
14. Fill out the owner city field with your city.
15. Fill out the owner state field with your state.
16. Select your country from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name**: Enter the name of your company or business as it is registered. This will be used to identify your business and appear on your listing.
- **Contact Email**: Provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
- **Address**: Enter your business or company's address.
- **Address Line 2**: Enter any additional address line 2 information, if applicable.
- **City**: Enter your city.
- **State**: Enter your state.
- **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
- **Country**: Select your country from the options provided.
- **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person you want to be contact with.
- **Role**: Enter the role of the contact person.
- **Website**: Enter your company or business's website.
- **About**: Enter a brief description of your business or company.
- **Industry**: Enter the industry your business or company belongs to.
- **Services Offered**: Select the services offered by your business or company from the options provided.
- **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
- **Company Type**: Select the type of your company from the options provided.
- **Company Status**: Select the status of your company from the options provided.
- **Owner Name**: Enter your name.
- **Owner Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Owner Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Owner Address**: Enter your address.
- **Owner Address Line 2**: Enter any additional address line 2 information, if applicable.
- **Owner City**: Enter your city.
- **Owner State**: Enter your state.
- **Owner Country**: Select your country from the options provided.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out as much information as possible to ensure a smooth registration process.
* Use valid contact details that we can use to contact you.
* Ensure that the services offered by your business or company are accurate and up-to-date.
