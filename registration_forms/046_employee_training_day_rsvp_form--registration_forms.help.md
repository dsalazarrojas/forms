# Employee Training Day RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to register their interest and provide information for the employee training day. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that the training session is tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Select a date for the training session.
5. Choose a time for the training session.
6. Add any additional notes about your training needs.
7. Select one option from the list.
8. Select multiple options from the list.
9. Enter any other email address associated with your account.
10. Enter a decimal number if applicable.
11. Enter an integer number if applicable.
12. Enter any text to complete the training day survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select a date for the training session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select a time for the training session.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your training needs.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose one option from the list below to indicate your interest in specific topics.
  • Option 1
  • Option 2
  • Option 3
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options from the list below to indicate your interests.
  • Multiple Option 1
  • Multiple Option 2
* **Another Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter any other email address associated with your account.
* **Decimal Number** (`decimal`, number, required): Enter a decimal number if applicable.
* **Integer Number** (`integer`, number, required): Enter an integer number if applicable.
* **Text Input** (`text`, text, required): Enter any text to complete the survey.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review the form before submission to ensure that all required fields are filled.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.
