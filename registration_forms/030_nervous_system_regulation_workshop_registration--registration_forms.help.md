# Nervous System Regulation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nervous System Regulation Workshop Registration form is used to collect information from individuals interested in attending the Nervous System Regulation Workshop. This form helps the organizers to manage workshop registrations and ensure that attendees have the necessary details recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Workshop Title** (Field 1) to describe the workshop you are interested in.
2. Provide your **Attendee Name** (Field 2) to identify yourself as an attendee.
3. Enter your **Email** (Field 3) to ensure we can communicate with you regarding the workshop.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** (Field 4) for any necessary follow-up communication.
5. Choose a **Date** (Field 5) for the workshop you would like to attend.
6. Select a **Time** (Field 6) for the workshop you would like to attend.
7. Confirm your **Consent** (Field 7) by acknowledging that you have read and agree to our workshop terms and conditions.
8. Specify your **Preferences** (Field 8) by selecting one or more options that apply to you.
9. In **Select One** (Field 9), choose one option from the list provided.
10. In **Select Multiple** (Field 10), choose one or more options from the list provided. **Please note that both Fields 9 and 10 are mandatory**.
11. Finally, enter a **Decimal Value** (Field 11) if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Title** (Field 1, text): Enter the title of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Attendee Name** (Field 2, text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the workshop.
* **Email** (Field 3, email): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (Field 4, text): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Date** (Field 5, date): Select a date for the workshop you would like to attend. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (Field 6, time): Select a time for the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Consent** (Field 7, note): Confirm that you have read and agree to our workshop terms and conditions.
* **Preferences** (Field 8, select multiple): Specify any preferences that apply to you, such as dietary needs or accommodations.
* **Select One** (Field 9, select one): Choose one option from the provided list. **This field is mandatory**.
* **Select Multiple** (Field 10, select multiple): Choose one or more options from the list provided. **This field is mandatory**.
* **Decimal Value** (Field 11, number): Enter a decimal value if applicable.
