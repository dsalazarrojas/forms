# Green Materials Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Materials Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect contact information and workshop preferences from individuals interested in attending the Green Materials Workshop. This form will help the organizers to track participants' details and facilitate communication during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your **First name** and **Last name** fields with your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- Enter your **Email** address to allow us to contact you with updates and information regarding the workshop.
- Provide your **Phone** number to enable us to reach you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
- Choose the **Workshop** option that best suits your interest. Please note that this field is not strictly required.
- If you have a specific **Workshop Name**, please enter it in the corresponding field.
- Select a **Date** for the workshop that you are interested in attending.
- Choose your preferred **Time** slot for the workshop.
- If you have any **Additional comments** or details, please provide them in the **Note** field.
- Enter your **Category** of interest (e.g., material, energy, etc.).
- If you have a specific **Category Name**, please enter it.
- Choose whether you are interested in being assigned a **Tool** during the workshop by selecting either "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and information regarding the workshop.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number to enable us to reach you with last-minute updates or changes.
* **Workshop**: Select the workshop that interests you the most.
* **Workshop Name**: If you have a specific workshop in mind, enter it here.
* **Date**: Select a date for the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Time**: Choose your preferred time slot for the workshop.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or details you would like to share with us.
* **Category**: Enter the category of interest that best describes your area of interest (e.g., material, energy, etc.).
* **Category Name**: If you have a specific category in mind, enter it here.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose whether you would like to be assigned a tool during the workshop by selecting "Yes" or "No".
