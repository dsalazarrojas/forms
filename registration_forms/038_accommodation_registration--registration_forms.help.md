# Accommodation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accommodation Registration form is designed to gather essential information from guests for their stay at the accommodation. This includes personal details, accommodation preferences, and travel information to ensure accurate and efficient services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Registrant Full Name** in the field labeled "Registrant Full Name". This is your full legal name.
2. Enter your **Email** in the field labeled "Registrant Email". This will be used for email confirmations and communication.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the field labeled "Registrant Phone". This is for contact purposes.
4. Enter the **Event Name** in the field labeled "Event Name". This is the name of the event requiring accommodation.
5. Choose your **Check-In Date** in the field labeled "Check In Date". This is the date of your arrival.
6. Choose your **Check-Out Date** in the field labeled "Check Out Date". This is the date of your departure.
7. Select your preferred **Accommodation Type** from the options provided, such as Single Room, Double Room, Twin Room, Suite, Shared Room, or Other.
8. Choose your **Smoking Preference** from the options Non-smoking, Smoking, or No preference.
9. Select your **Accessibility Requirements** from the provided options, such as Wheelchair accessible, Visual aids, Hearing accessible, Mobility aids, None, or Other.
10. Choose your preferred **Meal Plan Selection** from the options No meals, Breakfast only, Half board (breakfast and dinner), Full board (all meals), or Flexible dining.
11. Select your **Special Dietary Requirements** from the options Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Nut allergy, Diabetic, Halal, Kosher, or Other.
12. Choose how you will **Arrive** at the accommodation from the options Flight, Train, Bus, Car, or Other.
13. Enter the **Arrival Flight Train Number** if applicable. If not, leave this field blank.
14. Choose your estimated **Arrival Time** in the format HH:MM or HH:MM AM/PM.
15. Choose how you will **Depart** from the accommodation from the options Flight, Train, Bus, Car, or Other.
16. Enter the **Departure Flight Train Number** if applicable. If not, leave this field blank.
17. Choose your estimated **Departure Time** in the format HH:MM or HH:MM AM/PM.
18. Enter your **Emergency Contact Name** in the field labeled "Emergency Contact Name". This is the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
19. Enter your **Emergency Contact Phone** in the field labeled "Emergency Contact Phone". This is the phone number of your emergency contact.
20. If needed, describe your **Additional Requests** or special arrangements in this field.
