# Community Participation Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for participants to provide their information and consent for the use of their personal data, images, and audio/video recordings during community events. It is a crucial document that ensures participants are aware of how their data will be used and shared.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Date of Birth**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, **Street Address**, **City**, **State**, and **ZIP Code** in the required fields.
2. Select your **Event Name** from the provided options.
3. Choose the **Event Date** of the event you are participating in.
4. Provide the **Event Location** where the event takes place.
5. Select your **Media Release Authorization**, indicating whether you give permission for your photos to be used for promotional purposes.
6. Choose whether you **Consent to Photography**, **Consent to Video Recording**, and **Consent to Audio Recording**.
7. Select all applicable **Usage Scope** for how your data will be used (e.g., website, social media, print materials, etc.).
8. Decide whether your **Name** can be used in conjunction with media.
9. Read and agree to the **Liability Release**, acknowledging the importance of this legal information.
10. Select whether you **Agree to Release Liability**.
11. Optionally, provide any **Relevant Medical Conditions** that we should know about.
12. Sign the **Date of Agreement**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address**: Your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Street Address**: Your residential or business address.
* **City**: The city where you reside or work.
* **State**: The state where you reside or work.
* **ZIP Code**: The zip code of your postal address.
* **Event Name**: The event you are participating in.
* **Event Date**: The date of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Location**: The venue where the event takes place.
* **Media Release Authorization**: Permission to use your photos and recordings for promotional purposes.
* **Consent to Photography**: Permission to take your photos during the event.
* **Consent to Video Recording**: Permission to record your video during the event.
* **Consent to Audio Recording**: Permission to record your audio during the event.
* **Usage Scope**: Select all applicable ways your data will be used (e.g., website, social media, print materials, etc.).
* **Name Usage**: Decide whether your name can be used in conjunction with media.
* **Liability Release**: Read and agree to the legal information.
* **Agree to Release Liability**: Confirm your agreement to the terms.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions**: Provide any medical conditions we should know about.
* **Date of Agreement**: Sign the date of your agreement.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact us for clarification.
* Ensure you understand the terms and conditions before signing the agreement.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
