# Dog Training Class Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from pet owners to register their dogs for a training class. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Pet Owner" from the provided options (Pet Owner 1, Pet Owner 2, Pet Owner 3).
2.  Choose the "Training Class" that suits your needs (Class 1, Class 2, Class 3).
3.  Enter the "Date" you wish to attend the class.
4.  Select the "Time" for your preferred class time.
5.  Optionally add any "Note" about your pet or the class.
6.  Enter your "Email" address.
7.  Enter your "Phone" number.
8.  Provide "Pet Information" about your dog.
9.  Select your dog's "Pet Type" (Dog, Cat, Other).
10.  Enter the "Class Length" of the training (if applicable).
11.  Choose the "Training Level" for your dog (if applicable).
12.  Enter your dog's "Pet Name".
13.  Enter your "Owner Information" (if applicable).
14.  Enter your "Name" and "Address" and other details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner** (`select_one`, required): Select the pet owner from the provided options.
* **Training Class** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose the training class that suits your needs.
* **Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date you wish to attend the class.
* **Time** (`time`, required): Enter the time for your preferred class time.
* **Note** (`note`, optional): Optionally add any note about your pet or the class.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Pet Information** (`text`, optional): Provide pet information about your dog.
* **Pet Type** (`select_one`, required): Select your dog's type (Dog, Cat, Other).
* **Class Length** (`text`, optional): Enter the class length (if applicable).
* **Training Level** (`text`, optional): Choose the training level for your dog (if applicable).
* **Pet Name** (`text`, required): Enter your dog's name.
* **Owner Information** (`text`, optional): Enter your owner information (if applicable).
* **Owner** (`text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Address** (`text`, required): Enter your address.
* **Payment Status** (`select_one`, required): This might be related to the payment process (Paid, Unpaid, Refunded).
* **Submission Status** (`select_one`, required): This might be related to the submission and review process (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Class Status** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose the class status (Open, Closed, Full).
* **Class Capacity** (`text`, optional): Enter the class capacity (if applicable).
* **Class Description** (`text`, optional): Enter any class description (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`select_multiple`, required): Select your payment method (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal).
