# Loyalty Rewards Program Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in joining a loyalty rewards program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal information, such as your first name, business name, and contact number.
2. Choose your preferred method of contact (email, phone, or text).
3. Select the rewards you are interested in.
4. Fill out your subscription details, including the subscription start date.
5. Select your current subscription status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business or organization.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes. If you don't have a business email, enter your personal email.
- **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for business or personal purposes.
- **What kind of rewards do you offer?**: Choose the types of rewards that you are offering to your customers. You can choose multiple options.
- **How do you prefer to contact you?**: Select your preferred method of contact (email, phone, or text).
- **Loyalty Program Details**: Enter any additional details about your loyalty program.
- **What interests you most about this loyalty program?**: Enter the reasons why you are interested in this loyalty program.
- **Subscription Date**: Enter the date when you would like to start your subscription.
- **Subscription Status**: Select your current subscription status (active, pending, or cancelled).
- **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about your subscription.
- **Business Name (2)**: Enter any additional business information (if applicable).
- **Business Address**: Enter your business address (if applicable).
- **Contact Number 2**: Enter any additional contact number (if applicable).
- **Email 2**: Enter any additional email (if applicable).
- **Business Email**: Enter your business email (if applicable).
- **Business Website**: Enter your business website (if applicable).
- **Facebook**: Enter your Facebook handle or URL (if applicable).
- **Twitter**: Enter your Twitter handle or URL (if applicable).
- **Instagram**: Enter your Instagram handle or URL (if applicable).
- **YouTube**: Enter your YouTube channel or URL (if applicable).
- **Other**: Enter any other social media channels or platforms you have (if applicable).
- **Business Facebook 2**: Enter any additional Facebook information (if applicable).
- **Business Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your business (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your subscription details are up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation in keeping your data accurate and up-to-date.
