# junior_tennis_camp_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The junior tennis camp registration form is designed to collect participant information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for each attendee. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately, providing all required details for your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
* Start by entering your participant information, including name and age.
* Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* Indicate your level of tennis experience (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
* If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please describe them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
* If you have any medical notes or special requirements, add them to the "Notes" field.
* For emergency contact details, provide the name and number of the contact person, as well as your relationship with them.
* Review your information carefully and submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Age** (number, optional): Please provide your age to help us ensure a safe and suitable environment.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Add your phone number for additional contact purposes.
* **Tennis Experience** (select multiple, optional): Choose your level of tennis experience (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
* **Emergency Contact** (text, optional): Provide the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Number** (text, optional): Add the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please describe them here.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional medical notes or requirements.
* **Relationship** (select multiple, optional): Choose how you are related to the emergency contact person (Parent, Guardian, or Coach).
