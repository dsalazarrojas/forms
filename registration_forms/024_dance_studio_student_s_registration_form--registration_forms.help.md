# Dance Studio Student's Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Studio Student's Registration Form is a crucial document that provides necessary information about a student to the dance studio's staff and administrators. This form helps us understand the student's background, health, and contact details, allowing us to tailor our services and ensure a safe learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in sections, following the instructions for each field.
- Enter accurate and complete information for each section.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and relevance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Information** (`student_information`, `text`, required)
    Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a student.
*   **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `text`, required)
    Enter your birth date in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1999).
*   **School Information** (`school_information`, `text`, required)
    Enter your school's name and any other relevant details about your school.
*   **Grade** (`grade`, `number`, required)
    Select your grade level from a list of available options.
*   **Emergency Medical Treatment** (`emergency_medical_treatment`, `text`, required)
    In case of an emergency, provide your medical treatment or any allergies you may have.
*   **Is Student Free from Infectious Disease or Conditions?** (`infectious_disease`, `select_one`, required)
    Select 'True' if you are free from infectious diseases or conditions, or 'False' if you have any.
*   **Any Activity Student Should be Restricted from?** (`restricted_activity`, `text`, optional)
    If you have any conditions that may affect your ability to participate in certain activities, please specify here.
*   **Any Physical, Mental or Other Condition that Would Require Special Attention or Medication While in Class?** (`special_attention`, `text`, optional)
    If you have any condition that requires special attention or medication while in class, specify here.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional)
    Provide your contact information, including your parent's or guardian's name and phone number, to ensure we can reach you in case of an emergency.
