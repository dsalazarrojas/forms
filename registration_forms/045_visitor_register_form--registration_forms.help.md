# Visitor Register Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Register Form is a tool designed to collect vital information from visitors, allowing businesses or organizations to better understand who is entering their premises, their purpose of visit, and other relevant details. This form will help maintain a record of visitors, which can aid in providing excellent customer service, improving security, or facilitating communication.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Gather Your Information
- Please fill in your First name.
- Enter your Last name.
- Select the Date of your visit.
- If you're willing to, provide the Time of your visit.
- Enter your Contact number.
- If you'd like, provide your Email address.
- State your Purpose of visit.
- Optionally, include any Notes relevant to your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: A field where you enter your personal name.
  - **<label>First name</label>** (`<name>first_name</name>`, `text`, `required/true`): Enter your personal name, as you prefer it to be spelled.

- **Last name**: A field where you enter your surname.
  - **<label>Last name</label>** (`<name>last_name</name>`, `text`, `required/true`): Enter your surname for accurate identification purposes.

- **Date**: A field where you enter the date of your visit.
  - **<label>Date</label>** (`<name>date</name>`, `date`, `required/true`): The date of your visit in the standard format of month/day/year or day/month/year or month-day-year.

- **Time**: A field where you may optionally enter the time of your visit.
  - **<label>Time</label>** (`<name>time</name>`, `time`, `required/false`): If you're willing, provide the time of your visit for scheduling or other reasons.

- **Contact number**: A field where you enter your contact number.
  - **<label>Contact number</label>** (`<name>contact_number</name>`, `text`, `required/true`): A means to contact you for any reason related to your visit.

- **Email address**: A field where you may optionally enter your email address.
  - **<label>Email address</label>** (`<name>email</name>`, `text`, `required/false`): A way to communicate with you about your visit or other matters.

- **Purpose of visit**: A field where you state the reason for your visit.
  - **<label>Purpose of visit</label>** (`<name>purpose</name>`, `text`, `required/true`): Briefly describe the reason for visiting, which aids in planning and organization.

- **Notes**: A field where you may add any additional comments or details about your visit.
  - **<label>Notes</label>** (`<name>notes</name>`, `note`, `required/false`): If needed, provide any additional information or thoughts about your visit.

## Tips
- Please be honest and precise when filling out this form.
- Use your best judgment when providing your contact information.
- Remember, your input will aid in better management and service.
- If unsure, don't hesitate to ask if you need any clarification.
