# Interclub Uniform Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interclub Uniform Registration form is designed for users to register their interest in joining an interclub event or participating in an activity. This form collects personal and event-specific information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email format.
3. Select the date of the event that interests you from the "Event Date" field. Choose a date when you plan to attend the event.
4. Choose the time of the event from the "Event Time" field. This will help us schedule the event accordingly.
5. Write any additional message or comments in the "Message" field, if you have something to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **User Email** (email, required/optional): Input your valid email address.
* **Event Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date of the event that you wish to attend.
* **Event Time** (time, required/optional): Choose the time of the event that you plan to participate in.
* **Message** (text, required/optional): Share any additional information or comments with us.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
* Use a valid date and time for the event to avoid confusion.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements, include them in the message field for our attention.
