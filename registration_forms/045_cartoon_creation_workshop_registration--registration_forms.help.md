# Cartoon Creation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cartoon Creation Workshop Registration" form is designed to gather information from participants to register for a cartoon creation workshop. It collects personal details, workshop preferences, and medical/consent information for participant safety and workshop organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your participant details, including your name, age, and email address.
2. Select your preferred workshop session and time slot.
3. Choose your current drawing experience level and areas of interest.
4. Indicate if you will bring your own digital drawing tablet or if you need to rent one.
5. Provide your favorite cartoon characters or artists for us to tailor the workshop content.
6. Share your experience with drawing software, if any.
7. Fill out emergency contact information, including a contact's name and phone number.
8. Specify any medical conditions or allergies.
9. Grant permission to use your photos for promotional purposes and agree to the workshop terms and conditions.
10. Finally, confirm your registration and payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (2)**: Please enter your complete name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Age of Participant (3)**: Enter your age for proper group placement and workshop organization.
* **Email Address (4)**: Provide a valid email address for workshop updates, instructions, and communication.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (5)**: If you're under 18, please enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Selected Session (7)**: Select your preferred workshop session: Morning (9am-12pm), Afternoon (1pm-4pm), or Full Day (9am-4pm).
* **Current Drawing Experience (8)**: Choose your current drawing experience level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Areas of Interest (9)**: Select all the areas you're interested in learning about: Character Design, Storyboarding, Digital Inking, Coloring, Animation Basics, and Caricatures.
* **Bring Own Tablet (11)**: Indicate if you'll bring your own digital drawing tablet or if you need to rent one.
* **Favorite Cartoon Characters or Artists (12)**: Share your favorite characters or artists for us to tailor the workshop content.
* **Software Experience (13)**: If you have experience with drawing software like Photoshop, Procreate, or Clip Studio, let us know.
* **Emergency Contact Name (15)**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (16)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Allergies or Medical Conditions (17)**: Specify any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs.
* **Photo Release (19)**: Grant permission to use your photos for promotional purposes on our website and social media.
* **Terms Acceptance (20)**: Confirm that you agree to the workshop terms and conditions, including codes of conduct and safety rules.
* **Registration Fee Paid (22)**: Select the status of your registration fee payment.
* **Payment Method (23)**: Choose your preferred payment method: Credit Card, PayPal, or Cash at Door.
* **Digital Signature (25)**: Type your name as a digital signature for participant confirmation.
