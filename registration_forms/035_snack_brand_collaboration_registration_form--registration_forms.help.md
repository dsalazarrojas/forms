# Snack Brand Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snack Brand Collaboration Registration Form is designed to collect information from snack brands interested in collaborating with us. The form is used to gather essential data to evaluate and respond to collaboration requests effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Collaboration Request" field with a clear and concise description of your collaboration proposal.
2. Ensure you provide a valid "Form ID" to help us track your submission.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your completed form to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Collaboration Request** (`collaboration_request`, text, required): Please provide a clear and concise description of your collaboration proposal, including the specific details about your brand and the collaboration you are seeking.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required): Enter a valid "Form ID" to help us track your submission. Please ensure it is accurate to avoid duplicate submissions.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have a preferred tool or platform for collaboration, please mention it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information about your collaboration proposal, you can include them in this field.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Please select a relevant category for your brand to help us better understand your proposal.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your brand and the products/services you offer.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to send your completed form to us.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your collaboration request is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues or need assistance with the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
