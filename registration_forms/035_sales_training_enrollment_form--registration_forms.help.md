# Sales Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to enroll participants in sales training programs. Fill out this form to provide essential information about the participant, including their name, organization, and emergency contact details. Additionally, the form asks about the training program they wish to enroll in, the start date and time, and the duration of the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by providing the participant's details in the first few fields. Enter the participant's name, organization, and emergency contact information. Next, select the training program they wish to enroll in by choosing from the options provided. Then, set the start date and time of the training by using the date and time input fields. Finally, provide the expected duration of the training by entering the number of days or hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (Participant Name)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant who is enrolling in the training program.
* **Organization (organization)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization the participant is associated with.
* **Training Program (training_program)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the sales training program the participant wishes to enroll in. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Start Date (start_date)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date on which the participant plans to start the training program.
* **Start Time (start_time)** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of day when the participant plans to start the training program.
* **Training Duration (training_duration)** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the expected duration of the training program in days or hours.
* **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the participant's emergency contact.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful submission.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided before submitting.
* If you need to enroll in multiple training programs, repeat this form for each program.
