# Speaker Registration And Material Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for speakers to register and upload materials related to their sessions. It's essential to use this form only once per speaker.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your speaker information in the "Speaker Information" section.
2. Provide details about your session in the "Session Info" section.
3. Upload any relevant files for your session in the "Material Upload" section.
4. Select the type of files you have uploaded in the "Speaker Files" section.
5. Confirm that you have uploaded all required files in the "Confirm" section.
6. If you have any session files to upload, do so in the "Session Files" section.
7. Provide more details about your session in the "Session Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Information**: This field is for you to input your name, title, and any other information about yourself as a speaker.
* **Session Info**: This field is for you to provide a brief description of your session, including the title, date, and time.
* **Material Upload**: This field is for you to upload any files related to your session, such as slides, handouts, or videos.
* **Speaker Files**: This field is for you to select the type of files you have uploaded in the "Material Upload" section.
* **Confirm**: This field is to confirm that you have uploaded all required files.
* **Session Files**: This field is for you to upload any files related to your session, such as session notes or additional resources.
* **Session Details**: This field is for you to provide more details about your session, such as the session description and tags.
