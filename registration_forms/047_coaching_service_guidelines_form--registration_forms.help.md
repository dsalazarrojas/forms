# Coaching Service Guidelines Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Coaching Service Guidelines Form is an essential document that outlines the terms, conditions, and expectations of our coaching service. This form ensures we both understand each other's roles, responsibilities, and boundaries, ensuring a productive and respectful coaching relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Read each section carefully** and ensure you understand all the guidelines and policies outlined.
2. **Enter your details** in the required fields, such as your name, email address, phone number, and coaching package selection.
3. **Check the boxes** for each section, confirming you have read and understand the guidelines and policies.
4. **Submit** the form to complete the coaching service agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name, as this will be used for correspondence and record-keeping.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address, as this is how we'll communicate with you.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number, allowing us to reach you for scheduling and updates.
- **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your coach, as this will be used for communication and session scheduling.
- **Service Package Selected** (`service_package`, select_one, required): Choose the coaching package that suits your needs, and we'll outline the terms and conditions for that specific package.
- **Service Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you'd like your coaching services to start.
- **Service End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you'd like your coaching services to end.
- **Number of Sessions Included** (`session_included`, select_one, required): Choose the number of sessions included in your package, which will determine the total coaching hours and cost.
- **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of each coaching session, which will determine the coaching time and schedule.
- **Session Format** (`session_format`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to conduct your coaching sessions, such as in-person, video call, or phone call.
- **Scheduling Policy Understood** (`scheduling_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand how to book sessions and manage your schedule.
- **Required Booking Notice** (`booking_notice`, select_one, required): Choose the notice period for booking sessions, which will help us ensure timely scheduling.
- **Cancellation Policy Understood** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the cancellation policies and procedures.
- **Cancellation Fee Policy** (`cancellation_fee`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the cancellation fees and consequences.
- **Rescheduling Policy Understood** (`rescheduling_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand how to reschedule sessions.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to pay for your coaching services, such as upfront full payment, per session payment, or payment plan.
- **Accepted Payment Methods** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the accepted payment methods, such as credit card, debit card, bank transfer, or PayPal.
- **Late Payment Policy Understood** (`late_payment_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the late payment policies and consequences.
- **Refund Policy Understood** (`refund_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the refund policies and procedures.
- **Confidentiality Policy Accepted** (`confidentiality_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and accept our confidentiality policy.
- **Communication Guidelines Understood** (`communication_guidelines`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand our communication guidelines and expectations.
- **Between-Session Support** (`between_session_support`, select_one, required): Choose the level of support you'd like to have between sessions, such as email support, phone support, or scheduled calls.
- **Homework or Assignment Expectations** (`homework_expectations`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the homework or assignment expectations between sessions.
- **Progress Reporting Expectations** (`progress_reporting`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you'd like to track progress, either through regular assessments, session reviews, goal tracking, or informal check-ins.
- **Code of Conduct Accepted** (`code_of_conduct`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and accept our code of conduct and professional standards.
- **Professional Boundaries Understood** (`boundaries_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and respect our professional boundaries.
- **Service Termination Policy** (`termination_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand how our coaching services can be terminated.
- **Client Responsibilities Understood** (`client_responsibilities`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your responsibilities as a client.
- **Coach Responsibilities Understood** (`coach_responsibilities`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your coach's responsibilities.
- **I Acknowledge Reading All Guidelines** (`guidelines_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you've read and understood all guidelines and policies.
- **Date of Acknowledgment** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time you confirm reading and understanding all guidelines and policies.

## Tips
- Please take your time to carefully review each section and confirm your understanding before completing the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
- Once you've completed the form, review your confirmation email for accuracy and completeness.
- Please ensure you've read and understood all guidelines and policies before proceeding with our coaching services.
