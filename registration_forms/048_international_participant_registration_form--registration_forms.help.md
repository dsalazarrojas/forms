# International Participant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Participant Registration Form is used to collect personal details of international participants, including their name, contact information, country of origin, and registration details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select your country of origin from the "Country Of Origin" dropdown menu.
4. Fill in your nationality in the "Nationality" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Enter your date of registration in the "Date Of Registration" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
9. Enter the time of registration in the "Time Of Registration" field in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
10. Fill in your address in the "Address" field.
11. Fill in your organization in the "Organization" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Country Of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, select_one, required): Select your country of origin from the dropdown menu.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, text, optional): Enter your nationality here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date Of Registration** (`date_of_registration`, date, optional): Enter your date of registration in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Registration** (`time_of_registration`, time, optional): Enter the time of registration in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address here.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization name here.
