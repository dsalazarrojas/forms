# Aqua Registration And Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through each field of the Aqua Registration And Waiver Form to help you understand what is required and what to expect.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions and fields carefully before proceeding.
2. Ensure that you have all the required information and documents ready.
3. Select the correct options for each field accurately.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official ID or government records.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for important communications and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Program Information** (`program_info`, note, optional): This field is for additional comments or information related to your registration.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Swimming Lessons, Water Aerobics, Aquatic Fitness, or Competitive Swimming.
* **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Competitive.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Child (Under 12), Teen (12-17), Adult, or Senior (65+).
* **Health and Safety Acknowledgment** (`health_conditions`, text, optional): Please note any medical conditions or health issues that may impact your participation.
* **Allergies or Medications** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or medications you have.
* **I agree to the waiver and liability release** (`waiver_agree`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you agree to the waiver and liability release.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

**Tips**: Please ensure that you have the consent of your emergency contact person before submitting this form. Also, review your answers carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors or omissions.
