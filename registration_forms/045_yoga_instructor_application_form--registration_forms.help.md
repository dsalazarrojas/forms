# Yoga Instructor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yoga Instructor Application Form is a tool for potential applicants to provide their information and details for inclusion in the yoga directory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Provide your Phone number.
3. Enter your Experience as a yoga instructor.
4. List any certifications you hold.
5. Write a brief Bio about yourself.
6. Choose the type of studio where you work (you can select multiple options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have experience as a yoga instructor.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, required/optional): List any yoga certifications you hold, separated by commas.
* **Bio** (`bio`, `note`, required/optional): Write a brief description of yourself and your teaching style.
* **Studio Type** (`studio_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the types of studios where you work, selecting from Yoga Studio, Private Yoga Studio, or Yoga School.
