# Volunteer Work Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the Volunteer Work Authorization Form, a crucial document that ensures you have the necessary information and permissions to start your volunteer work. Please review this guide carefully to ensure a smooth and accurate completion of the form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all required information and documents ready.
2. Fill out the form with the necessary details, paying attention to each field and its specific requirements.
3. Make sure to review and agree to the terms and conditions at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the organization you will be working with.
* **Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Work Start Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date you started working with the organization.
* **Job Title** (7, text, required): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Work Type** (8, text, required): Briefly describe the type of work you will be doing.
* **Work Shift** (9, text, required): Specify your work shift or schedule.
* **Work Hours** (10, time, required): Enter your expected work hours.
* **Work Frequency** (11, text, required): Indicate how often you will be working.
* **Additional Comments** (12, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your work.
* **Terms Agreed** (13, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Reference Contact Person** (14, text, optional): Provide the name and email of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Reference Email** (15, text, optional): Enter the email address of the reference contact person.
* **Reference Phone** (16, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the reference contact person.
* **Organizational Unit** (17, text, optional): Specify the department or team you will be working with.
* **Supervisory Committee** (18, text, optional): Provide the name of the supervisory committee or team.
* **Supervisor Name** (19, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (20, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (21, text, optional): Enter the email address of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Job Title** (22, text, optional): Enter the job title of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Work Type** (23, text, optional): Briefly describe the type of work your supervisor is responsible for.
* **Supervisor Supervisory Level** (24, text, optional): Specify your supervisor's level of authority.
* **Additional Info** (25, text, optional): Add any additional information about your work or role.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or seek guidance from your supervisor.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact the relevant contact person.
