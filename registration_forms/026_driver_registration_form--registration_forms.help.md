# Driver Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from drivers for registration, application, or other driving-related services.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the form sections as needed, ensuring you provide accurate and complete information for each section.
2.  Review each section carefully to ensure you've provided all necessary details.
3.  Once completed, submit the form according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Registration Form** (page 1, id: 1): Enter your driver registration information here.
*   **Driver Info** (page 1, id: 2): Provide your driver information, such as driver's license number, name, etc.
*   **Vehicle Info** (page 1, id: 3): Enter your vehicle details, including make, model, and vehicle identification number.
*   **Address Info** (page 1, id: 4): Share your address and contact information.
*   **License Info** (page 1, id: 5): Add your driver's license number or ID.
*   **Contact Info** (page 1, id: 6): Complete your contact information, including phone number and email address.

## Tips
*   Ensure you've filled out all necessary fields for the intended purpose of the form.
*   Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
*   If any fields are unclear, refer to the provided field explanations for more information.
*   If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team for assistance.
