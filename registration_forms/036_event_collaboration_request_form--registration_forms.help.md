# Event Collaboration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Collaboration Request Form is a tool used to request collaboration or partnership for events such as conferences, festivals, or other gatherings. This form helps us to gather essential information about your event and your collaboration request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for your event in the `event_collaboration_request_form_title` field.
2. Provide the name of the partner or sponsor you are requesting collaboration with in the `partner_sponsor_name` field.
3. Briefly describe your collaboration request in the `event_collaboration_request` field.
4. Indicate whether or not you have proposal ideas for the collaboration in the `proposal_ideas` field.
5. Select all successful events you have organized in the past in the `successful_events_ideas` field.
6. Choose the type of collaboration you are requesting (Yes/No) in the `event_collaboration_request_form_submit` field.
7. Provide additional details about the collaboration you are requesting in the `collaboration_details` field (note this is a free-form text field).
8. Lastly, confirm if you have received a success response regarding the collaboration request in the `event_collaboration_request_form_submit_success` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_collaboration_request_form_title**: A brief title of your event.
* **partner_sponsor_name**: The name of the partner or sponsor you are requesting collaboration with.
* **event_collaboration_request**: A brief description of your collaboration request.
* **proposal_ideas**: Indicate if you have any proposal ideas for the collaboration.
* **successful_events_ideas**: A list of successful events you have organized in the past.
* **collaboration_details**: Additional information about the collaboration request.
* **event_collaboration_request_form_submit**: Indicate if you are requesting collaboration.
* **event_collaboration_request_form_submit_success**: Confirm if you have received a success response regarding the collaboration request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are the authorized representative of your organization or event before submitting this form.
* Be as concise and detailed as possible when providing information about your collaboration request.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
