# Summer Camp Child Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to register their child for a summer camp program. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in the First Name field with the child's first name.
2. Fill in the Last Name field with the child's last name.
3. Enter the Email address of the parent or guardian.
4. Fill in the Phone number field with the child's contact number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the child's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the child's contact phone number.
