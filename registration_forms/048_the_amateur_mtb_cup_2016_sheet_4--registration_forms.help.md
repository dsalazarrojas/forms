# The Amateur Mtb Cup 2016 Sheet 4 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration and event management purposes to collect participant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your race name.
2. Provide your full name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select your gender (Female or Male).
6. Enter your age.
7. Enter your birth country.
8. Enter your zip code.
9. Enter the name of your emergency contact.
10. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
11. Select your emergency contact relationship.
12. Enter the phone number of another emergency contact (if applicable).
13. Select your birthday (day).
14. Enter the name of your second emergency contact (if applicable).
15. Enter the phone number of your second emergency contact (if applicable).
16. Enter the relationship of your second emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Race Name** (`race_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the race you are participating in.
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender (Female or Male).
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Birth Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country where you were born.
* **Zip Code** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Select your emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, text, optional): If you have a second emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name 2** (`emergency_contact_name_2`, text, optional): If you have a second emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship_2`, text, optional): If you have a second emergency contact, select their relationship.
* **Birthdate Day** (`birthdate_day`, select_one, required): Select the day of your birth (1-31).
* **Birth Date** (`birthdate`, date, optional): Enter your birthdate.
* **Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your second emergency contact.
* **Name 2** (`emergency_contact_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of your second emergency contact.

## Tips

- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
- Ensure that you have the permission of your emergency contacts to disclose their contact information.
- Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
