# Beauty Pageant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Pageant Registration Form is designed to collect information from beauty pageant contestants to facilitate a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the personal information section, including First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and Age Category.
2. Provide your Email Address, Phone Number, and Street Address, City, State/Province, and ZIP Code.
3. If you have a parent or guardian, please enter their Name, Email, and Phone Number.
4. Select your Pageant Category or Title and enter the Pageant Date.
5. Describe your Talent Category and provide a brief description of your Talent Description.
6. If you have previous pageant experience, indicate whether you have participated in a pageant before (Yes/No) and list any previous titles or awards you've received.
7. If you have a social cause or platform, describe it and explain how you will support it.
8. Finally, review and agree to the pageant rules and standards, consent to the use of your photos, and confirm your understanding of the entry fee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required, Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Required, Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Required, Date): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Age Category** (Required, Select One): Select your age category (Teen, Young Adult, Adult, Senior).
* **Email Address** (Required, Email): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required, Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (Required, Text): Enter your street address.
* **City** (Required, Text): Enter your city.
* **State/Province** (Required, Text): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (Required, Text): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (Optional, Text): If applicable, enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (Optional, Email): If applicable, enter your parent or guardian's email.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (Optional, Text): If applicable, enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Pageant Category or Title** (Required, Text): Select your pageant category or title.
* **Pageant Date** (Required, Date): Enter the date of the pageant in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Talent Category** (Required, Text): Describe your talent category (e.g., singing, dancing, speaking).
* **Talent Description** (Required, Text): Briefly describe your talent or ability.
* **Special Skills or Accomplishments** (Optional, Text): List any special skills or accomplishments you'd like to mention.
* **Previous Pageant Experience** (Required, Select One): Indicate if you have participated in a pageant before (Yes/No).
* **Previous Titles or Awards** (Optional, Text): List any previous titles or awards you've received.
* **Social Cause or Platform** (Required, Text): Describe the social cause or platform you support.
* **Platform Description** (Required, Text): Explain how you will support your social cause or platform.
* **Rules and Standards Agreement** (Required, Select One): Agree to the pageant rules and standards (Yes/No).
* **Photo Release** (Required, Select One): Consent to the use of your photos for promotional purposes (Yes/No).
* **Entry Fee Confirmation** (Required, Select One): Confirm your understanding of the entry fee (Yes/No).

Tips:

* Ensure the date of birth is in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* If you have a parent or guardian, ensure they have given you permission to participate.
* Be clear and concise in your talent description and platform description.
* Ensure you have reviewed and agreed to the pageant rules and standards.
* Confirm your understanding of the entry fee.
