# Arts And Crafts Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register volunteers for arts and crafts events. It helps us to gather information about your availability, skills, and interests to ensure a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Use the "Select One" or "Select Multiple" options according to your selection.
3. Make sure to double-check your entries before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`volunteer_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`volunteer_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is not required, but it helps us to know you better.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Please enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Please enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Please enter your state or province.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Please enter your zip or postal code.
* **Areas of Interest for Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest_areas`, select_multiple, required): Please select all areas that interest you for volunteering, such as Event Setup, Vendor Coordination, Participant Registration, etc.
* **Experience with Arts and Crafts** (`experience_with_arts`, select_one, optional): This field is not required, but please choose an option that best describes your experience level with arts and crafts.
* **Relevant Skills or Expertise** (`volunteer_skills`, text, optional): If you have any relevant skills or expertise, please describe them here.
* **Availability for Volunteering** (`availability`, select_one, required): Please select your availability for volunteering, such as Weekdays Only, Weekends Only, Flexible, etc.
* **Hours Available per Week** (`hours_per_week`, number, optional): If you have a specific number of hours per week available for volunteering, please enter it here.
* **Duration of Volunteer Commitment** (`volunteer_duration`, text, optional): If you can commit to a specific duration, please describe it here.
* **Willing to Undergo Background Check (if required)** (`background_check`, select_one, optional): Please select whether you are willing to undergo a background check if required.
* **Have Your Own Transportation** (`transportation`, select_one, optional): Please select whether you have your own transportation.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you want us to notify someone in case of an emergency, please enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): If you want us to notify someone in case of an emergency, please enter their phone number.
* **What Motivates You to Volunteer** (`motivations`, text, optional): If you want to share what motivates you to volunteer, please describe it here.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): If you have any other information that would be helpful for us to know, please add it here.
