# Home Goods Shopping Spree Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Goods Shopping Spree Entry form is a registration form for the Home Goods Shopping Spree program. It is designed to collect user's personal information to register for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Address.
5. Enter your Phone number.
6. Enter your Date of Birth.
7. Check the box to confirm you consent to receive the terms and conditions of the giveaway.
8. Enter the date and time you want to enter for the giveaway.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for reference purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your street address or PO box, city, state, and zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **I consent to receive** (`consent`, note, optional): Check this box to confirm you consent to receive the terms and conditions of the giveaway.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, time, optional): Enter the date and time you want to enter for the giveaway.
