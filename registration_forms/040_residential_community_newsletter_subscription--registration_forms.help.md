# residential_community_newsletter_subscription - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and newsletter preferences for a residential community newsletter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the User Details field with your personal details.
2. Select your preferred newsletter preferences from the Newsletter Preferences field.
3. Leave any additional comments or notes in the Comments field if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Details** (`User Details`, `text`, required):
    *   This field is intended for the user to provide their personal details.
*   **Newsletter Preferences** (`Newsletter,Announcements,Both`, `select_one`, optional):
    *   This field is intended to allow users to select their preferred newsletter preferences. Users can choose to receive Newsletters, Announcements, or both.
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, optional):
    *   This field is intended for users to leave any additional comments or notes about their subscription.
