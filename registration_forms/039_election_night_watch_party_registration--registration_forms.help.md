# Election Night Watch Party Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in participating in a watch party for an election event. It will help us get in touch with you and ensure you receive any important updates or reminders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with your information:

1. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
4. **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the standard international format (e.g., "+1-123-456-7890").
5. **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if you are planning to attend the watch party, and "False" otherwise.
6. **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your attendance.

*   **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

*   **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or information about your attendance.

*   Please make sure your email address is valid and can be reached.
