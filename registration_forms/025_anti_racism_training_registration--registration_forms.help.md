# Anti Racism Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anti Racism Training Registration form is designed to register participants for upcoming anti-racism workshops. Please fill out this form to provide your details and preferences for a smooth and inclusive experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Participant Details** section with your full name.
2. Enter your **Contact Email** address, which will receive confirmation of your registration.
3. If you are representing an organization or school, enter your **Organization or School** name.
4. Choose your preferred **Session Choice** from the available workshop slots.
5. If applicable, provide your **Dietary Requirements** and **Special Needs or Accommodations** for accessibility support.
6. Optionally, provide your **Emergency Contact Phone** number for our records.
7. Review and submit your form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed during the workshop.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you will receive confirmation of your registration.
* **Organization or School** (Optional): If you are representing an organization or school, enter the name of your institution.
* **Session Choice**: Select the workshop time slot that suits you best. Please note that workshop slots are limited.
* **Dietary Requirements** (Optional): If you have specific dietary needs, enter them here.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (Optional): If you require accessibility support or have specific material needs, enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Optional): Provide an emergency contact phone number for our records.

**Tips**
- Double-check your email address for correct formatting and spelling.
- Make sure to select a session time slot that suits your schedule.
- If you have dietary requirements, please ensure you select a correct option or provide a detailed explanation.
- If you have accessibility needs, please provide as much detail as possible for our support team.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to avoid any errors.
