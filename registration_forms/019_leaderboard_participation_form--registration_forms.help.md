# Leaderboard Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leaderboard Participation Form is used to register participants for a leaderboard, a ranking system used to track and compare the performance of individuals or teams in a competition or event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your participant details in the required fields:
	* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on the leaderboard.
	* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
	* **Leaderboard Name**: Enter the name of the leaderboard you want to participate in.
	* **Participant Consent**: Choose "Consent" to indicate that you agree to participate and have your data shared.
	* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you participated in.
	* **Event ID**: Enter the ID of the event you participated in.
	* **Participant ID**: Enter your participant ID to identify you on the leaderboard.
2. Fill in any optional fields as necessary:
	* **Participant Message**: Enter a message or comment to share with the leaderboard administrators.
	* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number to receive updates and notifications.
	* **Participant Location**: Enter your location to help the leaderboard administrators understand your participation context.
	* **Participant Rating**: Enter a rating score out of 100 to assess your performance.
	* **Participant Rating Notes**: Enter notes about your rating score.
