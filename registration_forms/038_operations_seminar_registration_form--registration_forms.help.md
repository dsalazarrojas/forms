# operations_seminar_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operations seminar registration form is designed to collect personal details from users who want to attend the seminar. It is used to help the seminar organizers manage the registrations and provide the necessary information to the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Select the time you want to attend in the "Time" field.
5. Select the date you want to attend in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your registration confirmation.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for seminar updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for seminar updates and information.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you want to attend the seminar. Please note that this is a date and time selection field.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you want to attend the seminar.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive seminar updates and information.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please contact the seminar organizers in advance.
* If you need to cancel or modify your registration, please contact the seminar organizers directly.
