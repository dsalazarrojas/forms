# customer_retention_pilot_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer retention pilot registration form is used to gather information about potential participants in the customer retention pilot program. The form is designed to collect personal and professional details about the individual, which will be used to create a customized retention strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email Address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your Company Name in the "Company Name" field.
5. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Describe your Job Function in the "Job Function" field.
7. Enter your Department in the "Job Department" field.
8. Select your Team from the options provided.
9. Enter your Role in the "Role" field.
10. Enter the Location in the "Location" field.
11. Select the Timezone you are in.
12. Choose your language and native language if necessary.
13. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
14. Select your Job Status from the options provided.
15. Add any additional comments or details about your Job Status in the "Job Status Note" field.
16. Add any notes about your Job Title in the "Job Title Note" field.
17. Add any notes about your Company in the "Company Note" field.
18. Add any notes about your Team in the "Team Note" field.
19. Add any notes about your Location in the "Location Note" field.
20. Add any notes about your Role in the "Role Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you work for.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required): Describe your job function or role.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you work for.
* **Job Department** (`job_department`, text, required): Enter your department in the company.
* **Team** (`team`, text, required): Select your team from the options provided.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Enter your role.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your location.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, time, required): Enter the timezone you are in.
* **Language** (`language`, text, required): Enter your language.
* **Native Language** (`native_language`, text, required): Enter your native language.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required): Select your job status.
* **Job Status Note** (`job_status_note`, note, required): Add any notes about your job status.
* **Job Title Note** (`job_title_note`, note, required): Add any notes about your job title.
* **Company Note** (`company_note`, note, required): Add any notes about your company.
* **Team Note** (`team_note`, note, required): Add any notes about your team.
* **Location Note** (`location_note`, note, required): Add any notes about your location.
* **Role Note** (`role_note`, note, required): Add any notes about your role.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your employment.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your employment.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, not required): Select your current job status.
* **Job Status Note** (`job_status_note`, note, not required): Add any notes about your current job status.
* **Job Title Note** (`job_title_note`, note, not required): Add any notes about your job title.
* **Company Note** (`company_note`, note, not required): Add any notes about your company.
* **Team Note** (`team_note`, note, not required): Add any notes about your team.
* **Location Note** (`location_note`, note, not required): Add any notes about your location.
* **Role Note** (`role_note`, note, not required): Add any notes about your role.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before filling out this form.
* Check the options for "Team" and "Job Status" fields to ensure you select the correct options.
* Fill out the "Job Status Note", "Job Title Note", "Company Note", "Team Note", "Location Note", and "Role Note" fields only if necessary.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
