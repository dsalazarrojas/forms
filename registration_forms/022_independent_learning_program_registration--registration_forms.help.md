# Independent Learning Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering independent learning programs, which are programs or courses not offered by the institution but instead are taken on an individual's own initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your first name and last name in the `Participant First Name` and `Participant Last Name` fields respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the `Participant Email` field. This is the contact information we'll use to reach you.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Participant Phone` field.
4. Enter your student ID or any other relevant student number in the `Student Number` field.
5. If you're registering for a program, provide the course title in the `Course Title` field.
6. If you know when the program starts and ends, provide these dates in the `Program Start Date` and `Program End Date` fields respectively.
7. Enter the duration of the program in the `Duration` field.
8. If you're associated with an institution, enter it in the `Institution` field.
9. Select the status of your program (Pending, Approved, or Denied) in the `Program Status` field.
10. If you have a participant institutional affiliation, enter it in the `Participant Affiliation` field.
11. Select the level of your program (Undergraduate, Graduate, or Certificate) in the `Level` field.
12. Enter the department you're associated with in the `Department` field.
13. Enter your area of interest in the `Area of Interest` field.
14. Enter the faculty you're associated with in the `Faculty` field.
15. Enter your instructor's name in the `Instructor` field.
16. If you have any additional notes, enter them in the `Additional Notes` field.
17. If you've received institutional approval for your program, enter it in the `Institutional Approval` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Participant Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address here. This is the contact information we'll use to reach you.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, text, required): Enter your student ID or any other relevant student number here.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the course or program you're registering for.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the program if you know it.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the program if you know it.
* **Duration** (`program_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of the program.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the institution you're associated with here.
* **Program Status** (`program_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your program (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Participant Affiliation** (`participant_institutional_affiliation`, text, required): Enter your participant institutional affiliation here.
* **Level** (`program_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of your program (Undergraduate, Graduate, or Certificate).
* **Department** (`participant_department`, text, required): Enter the department you're associated with here.
* **Area of Interest** (`participant_program_area_of_interest`, text, required): Enter your area of interest here.
* **Faculty** (`program_faculty`, text, required): Enter the faculty you're associated with here.
* **Instructor** (`participant_instructor`, text, required): Enter your instructor's name here.
* **Additional Notes** (`participant_additional_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes here.
* **Institutional Approval** (`participant_institutional_approval`, text, required): Enter your institutional approval here if you've received it.

**Tips:** 
* Ensure that you're filling out the form accurately and completely. 
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, ask your program administrator or instructor for help. 
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they're correct.
