# Tournament Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a tournament. Please ensure that you have not already registered by checking the internal registration system before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field next to "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field next to "Last Name".
3. Enter your **Email** address in the text field next to "Email". This must be a valid email address.
4. Select your **Player Type** from the dropdown menu next to "Player Type". You can choose between "Individual" and "Team".
5. If you selected "Team", enter your **Team Name** in the text field next to "Team Name".
6. Select your **Team Role** from the dropdown menu next to "Team Role". You can choose between "Captain" and "Member".
7. Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field next to "Phone".
8. If you'd like to add any additional comments, enter them in the text field next to "Comments".
9. Enter the **Date and Time** of the event you plan to attend in the date field next to "Date Time 1".
10. Enter the **Time of Day** for the event you plan to attend in the text field next to "Time 1".
11. Enter the **Date and Time** of the event you plan to attend in the date field next to "Date Time 2".
12. Enter the **Time of Day** for the event you plan to attend in the text field next to "Time 2".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Player Type**: Choose whether you are an individual or a team.
	+ **Individual**: You are participating as an individual.
	+ **Team**: You are participating as a team.
* **Team Name**: Enter your team's name if you are a team.
* **Team Role**: Choose whether you are the captain or a member of the team.
	+ **Captain**: You are the captain of the team.
	+ **Member**: You are a member of the team.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your registration.
* **Date Time 1**: Enter the date and time of the event you plan to attend.
* **Time 1**: Enter the time of day for the event you plan to attend.
* **Date Time 2**: Enter the date and time of the second event you plan to attend.
* **Time 2**: Enter the time of day for the second event you plan to attend.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter valid email and phone numbers.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact the tournament administrators.
