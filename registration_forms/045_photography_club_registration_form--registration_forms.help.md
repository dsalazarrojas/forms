# photography_club_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography club registration form is used to collect information from new members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First name" field with your first name.
2. Fill out the "Last name" field with your last name.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Select your desired membership level from the "Membership level" field.
7. Select all applicable photo categories for your specialization in the "Photo category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Membership level** (`select_one`, optional): Select your desired membership level: Basic, Premium, or Lifetime.
* **Photo category** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable photo categories for your specialization.
