# Tenant Inclusion Forum Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Tenant Inclusion Forum Registration form is designed for tenants to provide information about their registration for events, meetings, or activities within the tenant inclusion forum. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences for accommodations and accessibility requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title to describe the purpose of your registration.
2. Provide your first name and last name.
3. Enter your contact email address.
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
5. Select the event type (optional).
6. Specify any accessibility needs you may have.
7. Add any additional information about your registration (if necessary).
8. Confirm your form title.
9. Add any additional information or notes (if necessary).
10. If applicable, provide your contact phone number and accessibility needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the purpose of your registration.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of your event or meeting.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of your event or meeting.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event or meeting (if applicable).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Specify any accessibility needs you may have (e.g., Wheelchair, Sign Language).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your registration (if necessary).
* **Form Title Confirmation** (`form_title_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm your form title.
* **Additional Info 2** (`additional_info_2`, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes (if necessary).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, select_one, optional): Provide your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Accessibility Needs 2** (`accessibility_needs_2`, select_multiple, optional): Specify any additional accessibility needs you may have (if applicable).
* **Additional Info 3** (`additional_info_3`, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes (if necessary).
* **Contact Email 2** (`contact_email_2`, email, optional): Provide your contact email address (if applicable).
* **Additional Info 4** (`additional_info_4`, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes (if necessary).
