The form purpose is to collect information from attendees who wish to register for the Sports Broadcasting Workshop. This form is a crucial part of the registration process, and the data collected will be used to tailor the workshop experience to each attendee's needs. The form will not be duplicated, and each attendee can only register once.

## Sports Broadcasting Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field. This is the name you wish to be referred to during the workshop.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field. This is your family name.
3. Enter your Email in the **Email** field. This will be used for communication and follow-up.
4. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field. This is your contact number, used for any workshop-related calls or updates.
5. Select the Workshop you wish to attend in the **Workshop** field. Choose from options **Workshop A**, **Workshop B**, or **Workshop C**.
6. Enter the Date you wish to attend the workshop in the **Date** field. This is the day you plan to attend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop** (required: false): Select the workshop you wish to attend. Choose from options **Workshop A**, **Workshop B**, or **Workshop C**.
* **Date** (required: false): Enter the date you wish to attend the workshop.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter your email address correctly to ensure that you receive any communications and updates from us.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the workshop, do not hesitate to contact us.
* If you have any special requests or needs, let us know in the notes field (not shown here) so we can tailor the workshop experience to your needs.
