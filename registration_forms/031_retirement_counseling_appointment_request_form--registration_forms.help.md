# Retirement Counseling Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a retirement counseling appointment with our organization. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we can provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date for your appointment in the "Date" field.
6. Select a time for your appointment in the "Time" field.
7. Choose the type of appointment you are requesting (in-person or virtual) in the "Appointment Type" field.
8. Enter the reason for your visit in the "Reason for Visit" field.
9. Enter your retirement date in the "Retirement Date" field.
10. Enter the name of the counselor you would like to meet with in the "Counselor" field.
11. If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`name`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`name`, `date`, required): Please select a date for your appointment. This can be a date in the past, present, or future.
* **Time** (`name`, `time`, required): Please select a time for your appointment. Please ensure that the time you select is available with the counselor you have chosen.
* **Appointment Type** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you would like an in-person or virtual appointment. This will help us determine the best format for your meeting.
* **Reason for Visit** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter a brief description of why you are requesting a retirement counseling appointment.
* **Retirement Date** (`name`, `date`, required): Please enter your retirement date.
* **Counselor** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the counselor you would like to meet with.
* **Additional Comments** (`name`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date so that we can contact you with appointment details.
* If you are requesting an in-person appointment, please ensure that you are available at the selected time and date.
* If you are requesting a virtual appointment, please ensure that you have a stable internet connection and a suitable device for the meeting.
