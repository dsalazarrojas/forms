# Athlete Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to ensure that you, as the athlete, understand and agree to the terms and conditions of participating in the activity. It includes sections for personal information, participation details, risk acknowledgment, and medical and insurance information, as well as a media release and signature section.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2.  Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3.  Review each section carefully before moving on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (Page 1)
*   **-- Participant Information --**: This section is a heading for the next set of fields.
*   **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your full name.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Residential Address**: Enter your home address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
*   **-- Participation Details --**: This section is a heading for the next set of fields.
*   **Organization or Event Name**: Enter the name of the organization or event you're participating in.
*   **Sport or Activity Type**: Enter the sport or activity type you'll be participating in.
*   **Season or Event Date Range**: Enter the date range of the season or event.
*   **-- Risk Acknowledgment and Waiver --**: This section is a heading for the next set of fields.
*   **I acknowledge the inherent physical risks associated with this activity**: Select "I agree" to confirm you understand the risks.
*   **I voluntarily assume all risks of injury or loss related to participation**: Select "I acknowledge and assume all risks" to confirm you're taking on the risks.
*   **I release the Organization from any and all liability for injuries**: Select "I agree to release liability" to confirm you're releasing the organization from liability.
*   **I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Organization from any claims**: Select "I agree to indemnify" to confirm you're indemnifying the organization.
*   **-- Medical and Insurance Information --**: This section is a heading for the next set of fields.
*   **I certify that I am physically fit to participate in the activity**: Select "I certify fitness" to confirm you're fit to participate.
*   **Health Insurance Provider**: Enter your health insurance provider.
*   **Policy Number**: Enter your policy number.
*   **I consent to emergency medical treatment if deemed necessary**: Select "Yes, I consent" to confirm you're consenting to emergency medical treatment.
*   **-- Media and Image Release --**: This section is a heading for the next field.
*   **I consent to the use of my image or likeness in promotional materials**: Select "Yes, I consent" to confirm you're consenting to the use of your image or likeness.
*   **-- Final Acknowledgment and Signature --**: This section is a heading for the next set of fields.
*   **I have read and fully understand the terms of this waiver**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm you've read and understood the waiver.
*   **Athlete Electronic Signature**: Type your full name.
*   **Parent or Guardian Electronic Signature**: If you're under 18, have a parent or guardian sign this section.
*   **Relationship to Minor**: If signed by guardian, enter the relationship to the minor.
*   **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of your signature in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Parent or Guardian Electronic Signature (if under 18)**: If you're under 18, have a parent or guardian sign this section.
*   **Relationship to Minor**: If signed by guardian, enter the relationship to the minor.
