# Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exam Registration Form is used to register students for exams, collect their details, and gather information about their exam preferences, particularly their preferred exam schedule options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Student Name** in the first field. This is your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your **Student Email**, which should match the email address you regularly use. This is for communication purposes.
3. Provide the **Exam Name** you are registering for. This should be the exact name of the exam, as it appears on official materials.
4. Select your preferred **Exam Date** for the exam. Be sure to choose a date that aligns with your available schedule.
5. Choose your preferred **Exam Time** for the exam. Select the time that best suits your needs.
6. If applicable, enter your **Exam Location**. This is the specific location where the exam will take place.
7. Enter your **Student Phone** for emergency contact purposes, if you wish to provide this information.
8. Enter any **Note** you'd like to include with your registration. This is optional.
9. Select your preferred **Exam Schedule** options. You can choose one or multiple options from the list provided.
