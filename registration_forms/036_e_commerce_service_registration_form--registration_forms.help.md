## e_commerce_service_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "e_commerce_service_registration_form" is a registration form used to capture information from users interested in enrolling in various ecommerce services. This form is a crucial step in the onboarding process, ensuring that all necessary details are collected for proper service provisioning and future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the registration form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field. This is the first name that you use to identify yourself.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field. This is the name by which you are known to others.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is the address where you will be contacted for service updates and notifications.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will be used for communication and service-related calls.
5.  Select the date when your email was verified in the "Verified at" field. This is to track the email verification process.
6.  Choose the service type you are interested in from the "Service Type" dropdown menu. This will determine the specific service package and pricing.
7.  Select all relevant service plans you require from the "Service Plan" multi-select menu. This will allow us to tailor the service according to your needs.
8.  Optionally, review and agree to the terms and conditions of service provided in the "Agreement" field.
9.  Confirm your understanding of and agreement with the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" drop-down menu.
10. Select the date when you agree to the terms and conditions in the "Verified at" field.
11. Add any additional notes regarding your service requirements or needs in the "Notes" field. This is an optional field for additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name (Text, Required):** Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name (Text, Required):** Enter your last name here.
- **Email (Email, Required):** Enter a valid email address for service updates and notifications.
- **Phone (Text, Required):** Enter your phone number for communication and service calls.
- **Verified at (Date, Required):** Select the date your email was verified.
- **Service Type (Select One, Required):** Choose the service type you are interested in.
- **Service Plan (Select Multiple, Required):** Select all relevant service plans that you require.
- **Agreement (Note, Optional):** Review and agree to the terms and conditions of service, if you so choose.
- **Terms and Conditions (Select One, Required):** Confirm your understanding of and agreement with the terms and conditions.
- **Verified at (Date, Required):** Select the date you agree to the terms and conditions.
- **Notes (Note, Optional):** Add any additional notes or requirements you may have for the service.
