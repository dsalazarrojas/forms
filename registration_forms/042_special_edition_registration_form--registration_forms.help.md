# Special Edition Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Special Edition Registration Form is a multi-page form used to collect user information for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your First Name on Page 1.
2. Enter your Email address and Confirm Email on Page 1.
3. Enter your Password and Confirm Password on Page 1.
4. On Page 2, select a Special Offer from the dropdown list.
5. On Page 2, select multiple offers that apply to you from the Confirm Offer field.
6. On Page 2, select when you Registered At from the dropdown list.
7. On Page 3, enter your Phone number on Page 3.
8. On Page 3, enter your Date on Page 3.
9. On Page 3, enter your Time on Page 3.
10. On Page 3, enter any Notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required/optional): Confirm your email address for verification purposes.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required/optional): Enter a password of 8 or more characters.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, text, required/optional): Confirm your password for verification purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select your date of registration.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select your registration time.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your registration.
* **Special Offer** (`special_offer`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the available special offers.
* **Confirm Offer** (`confirm_offer`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple special offers that apply to you.
* **Registered At** (`registered_at`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select when you registered.
