# Smart Lodging Technology Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Lodging Technology Workshop Registration form is designed to collect information from participants interested in attending the workshop. This form helps us understand your interests, preferences, and any special requirements you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email address.
3. If you have a Phone number, please enter it.
4. Select your interests in each of the Session Interests fields by checking the boxes that apply to you.
5. If you have any Special Requirements, please describe them in the Special Requirements field.
6. Choose your accommodation preferences by selecting "Yes" or "No" for the Accommodation Type field.
7. If you need to specify a room number, enter it in the Accommodation Number field.
8. Select your preferred accommodation date and time.
9. If you have any other special requirements, describe them in the Special Requirements Other field.
10. Check the boxes for each Session Interests field as they apply to you.
11. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your Email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a Phone number, please enter it.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes that apply to you to indicate your interest in each session.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Describe any special requirements you may have.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for the accommodation type that applies to you.
* **Accommodation Number** (`accommodation_number`, number, optional): If you need to specify a room number, enter it here.
* **Accmodation Date** (`accommodation_date`, date, required): Select your preferred accommodation date.
* **Accommodation Time** (`accommodation_time`, time, required): Select your preferred accommodation time.
* **Special Requirements Other** (`special_requirements_other`, text, optional): Describe any other special requirements you may have.
* **Session Interests 01** (`session_interests_01`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes that apply to you to indicate your interest in each session 01.
* This applies to all Session Interests fields (`session_interests_02` to `session_interests_13`): Check the boxes that apply to you to indicate your interest in each session.

Please make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form. If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
