# Property Cloud Platform Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information for properties on the Property Cloud Platform. It's a crucial step in creating or updating property records, allowing property owners or administrators to provide necessary details about their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "Enter First Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Enter Email" field.
3. Fill in your Password in the "Password" field. (Please note that this field is not required, but it's recommended for security purposes.)
4. Provide your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select one of the options for "Select One" field.
6. Select multiple options for "Select Multiple" field.
7. Provide a valid Date in the "Date" field.
8. Select a Time in the "Time" field.
9. Enter any additional Note in the "Note" field. (This field is not required.)
10. Enter Property Name in the "Property Name" field.
11. Enter Property Address in the "Property Address" field.
12. Select Property Type in the "Property Type" field.
13. Select Property Status as either "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Property Status" field.
14. Select multiple Property Category options in the "Property Category" field.
15. Enter Property Description in the "Property Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Enter Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required: false): Enter your password (recommended for security purposes).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the available options.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Provide a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time in 12-hour or 24-hour format.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional note about your property.
* **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, required: false): Enter your property name.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required: false): Enter your property address.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, required: false): Enter your property type.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your property as either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Property Category** (`property_category`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple categories for your property.
* **Property Description** (`property_description`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your property.
