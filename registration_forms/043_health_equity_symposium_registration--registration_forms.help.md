# health_equity_symposium_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential attendees of the Health Equity Symposium. It helps the organizers understand who is interested in attending specific sessions and what their accessibility needs may be.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your session interests by choosing from the list provided.
2.  If you have a session ID, please enter it in the corresponding field. Note: Without further information, please contact the symposium organizers for guidance on this field.
3.  Enter your name in the provided text field.
4.  Enter your organization's name in the provided text field.
5.  Provide your contact information in the text field.
6.  Enter your email address in the provided email field.
7.  Indicate if you have any accessibility needs.
8.  Select your interests from the second list of session interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Interests** (`Session Interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your interests from the provided list to help the organizers understand what sessions you are interested in.
* **Session ID** (`Session ID`, number, required/optional): Enter the session ID if you have one. Please note that the purpose of this field is not clear and it would be best to contact the symposium organizers for guidance.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name in this text field.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, required/optional): Enter your organization's name in this text field.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information in this text field.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in this email field.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`Accessibility Needs`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if you have any accessibility needs.
* **Session Interests 2** (`Session Interests 2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your interests from the second list of session interests.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your information is properly recorded.
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact the symposium organizers for assistance.
