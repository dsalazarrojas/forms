# Trade Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for businesses to register their trade information for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your business.
* **Trade Type** (`trade_type`, select_one, required: false): Select whether your trade is 'Yes' or 'No' (this may be for businesses that offer trading services).
* **Trade Description** (`trade_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your trade.
* **Business Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your business's physical address.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Website** (`website`, text, required: false): Enter your business website.
* **Date of Establishment** (`date_of_establishment`, date, required: false): Enter the date your business was established (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Place of Establishment** (`place_of_establishment`, text, required: false): Enter the place where your business is established.
* **Registration Number** (`registration_number`, text, required: false): Enter your business registration number.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the owner of your business.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the owner.
* **Owner E-mail** (`owner_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the owner.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the owner.
* **Trade License** (`trade_license`, text, required: false): Enter any trade licenses you have.
* **Business Name (En)** (`business_name_en`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your business in the English language.
* **Business Address (En)** (`business_address_en`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your business in the English language.
* **Owner Name (En)** (`owner_name_en`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the owner in the English language.
* **Owner Address (En)** (`owner_address_en`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the owner in the English language.
* **Trade License (En)** (`trade_license_en`, text, required: false): Enter any trade licenses you have in the English language.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Continue' to proceed, 'Back' to return to the previous page, or 'Finish' to submit the form.
