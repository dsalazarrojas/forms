This is a form for conference registration, which is a crucial part of the event planning process. The form collects essential information from attendees, including their personal details, conference preferences, and contact information. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## Purpose

The Conference Registration form is designed to gather information from attendees for conference organization and communication purposes. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience during the conference.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in a new browser tab or window.
2.  Enter your first name, last name, and phone number accurately.
3.  Provide your email address and choose a password for conference communication.
4.  Enter your organization's name, job title, and address line 1 correctly.
5.  Select your country of residence and preferred meal option.
6.  Choose the registration type that suits your needs (Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, etc.).
7.  Select the days you plan to attend the conference.
8.  Specify your meal preference and dietary restrictions if applicable.
9.  Indicate whether you will attend networking events.
10.  Mention any accessibility needs.
11.  Provide emergency contact information if required.
12.  Opt-in or opt-out of marketing communications.
13.  Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your given name as you would like it to be displayed on conference materials.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your family name as you would like it to be displayed on conference materials.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address for conference updates.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number for emergency contacts.
*   **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter your company or institution's name.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your professional title or position.
*   **Address Line 1** (`address_line1`, `text`, required): Enter your street address for conference materials and catering purposes.
*   **Address Line 2** (`address_line2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional address information (apartment, suite etc.).
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city name.
*   **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province name.
*   **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
*   **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Select your country of residence.
*   **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your registration category (Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, etc.).
*   **Which days will you attend** (`conference_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the days you plan to attend the conference.
*   **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your meal preference (No Preference, Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.).
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): Specify any dietary allergies or restrictions.
*   **Will you attend networking events** (`networking_events`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you will attend networking events.
*   **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): Mention any special accessibility needs.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **May we contact you about future events?** (`marketing_consent`, `select_one`, required): Opt-in or opt-out of marketing communications.
*   **I accept the terms and conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Agree or disagree to the conference's terms and conditions.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Conference Registration form accurately and efficiently, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience during the conference.
