# Student Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Check Out Form is used by authorized personnel to verify student identity and details at the time of check-out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the student number, name, contact number, and email.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, or you will not be able to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_number** (`student_number`, number, required): Enter the unique student number assigned to the student.
* **name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the student.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email of the student.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
