# Public Policy Panel Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Policy Panel Sponsorship Form is used to gather information from potential sponsors for public policy panel events. It helps us understand each sponsor's goals, expectations, and requirements for the sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Name field with the title of the event you wish to sponsor.
2. Enter the Contact Name and Contact Email of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
3. Enter the Contact Phone number of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
4. Enter the name of the Organization associated with the sponsorship.
5. Fill in the Event Date and Event Time of the event.
6. Enter the Event Location of the event.
7. Choose the Sponsorship Level (Bronze, Gold, or Silver) that best reflects your involvement.
8. Fill in the Sponsorship Commitment details.
9. Enter the Sponsorship Duration, if applicable.
10. Add any additional Event Details.
11. Enter the Event Budget.
12. Fill in the Payment Terms for the sponsorship.
13. Choose the payment schedule and method (Option A, Option B, or Option C).
14. Select the Sponsorship Agreement that best suits your needs.
15. Fill in any Contact Notes for the sponsor.
16. Enter any Sponsor Notes.
17. Enter the Sponsor Email and Sponsor Phone number.
18. Enter the Sponsor Name and Sponsor Title.
19. Enter the Sponsor Organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): This is the title of the event you wish to sponsor.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): The name of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): The name of the organization associated with the sponsorship.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): The email of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): The date of the event.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): The time of the event.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): The location of the event.
- **Sponsorship Commitment** (`sponsorship_commitment`, text, optional): A description of your sponsorship commitment.
- **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, select multiple, optional): Choose from Bronze, Gold, or Silver to reflect your level of involvement.
- **Sponsorship Duration** (`sponsorship_duration`, text, optional): The duration of the sponsorship (if applicable).
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Any additional details about the event.
- **Event Budget** (`event_budget`, number, optional): The budget for the event (if applicable).
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): The payment terms for the sponsorship.
- **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, select one, optional): Choose from Option A, Option B, or Option C.
- **Payment Deadline** (`payment_deadline`, date, optional): The payment deadline (if applicable).
- **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, text, optional): Any contact notes for the sponsor.
- **Sponsor Notes** (`sponsor_notes`, text, optional): Any sponsor notes.
- **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, optional): The email of the sponsor.
- **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the sponsor.
- **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, optional): The name of the sponsor.
- **Sponsor Title** (`sponsor_title`, text, optional): The title of the sponsor.
- **Sponsor Organization** (`sponsor_organization`, text, optional): The organization of the sponsor.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately, and make sure to read the sponsorship agreement carefully before submitting the form. If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
