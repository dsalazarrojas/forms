# public_health_initiative_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Health Initiative Enrollment Form is a registration form for individuals to enroll in various health programs. This form is used to collect basic contact information and preferences for the selected program(s).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Date of Birth, Phone, and Address on the first page.
2. On the second page, select the Health Program(s) you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
3. Confirm your enrollment by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Confirm" field.
4. Optionally, select a follow-up date for any additional information or reminders.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Address**: Enter your address for any mail or in-person follow-ups.
* **Health Program**: Select the program(s) you are interested in from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or multiple programs.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your enrollment by selecting "True" or "False". This will be used to finalize your enrollment.
* **Follow Up**: Select a date for any additional information or reminders you may need.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your enrollment application. 

## Tips
- Make sure to enter valid and accurate information to ensure smooth communication.
- You can select multiple programs by holding the 'Ctrl' key and clicking on multiple options in the "Health Program" field.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us at [email address] or [phone number].
