# free_course_preview_lead_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The free_course_preview_lead_form is a registration form designed to gather contact information and follow-up preferences from users interested in previewing a free course. Fill out this form to express your interest in a course without committing to a purchase.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred contact method from the "How do you want to be contacted?" dropdown menu.
5. Choose a date and time for follow-up in the "When would you like to start?" field.
6. Enter any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
7. (Optional) Enter any additional email addresses or names if desired.
8. (Optional) Enter any additional dates or times if desired.
9. (Optional) Enter any additional notes if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **How do you want to be contacted?** (`select_one`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options: Phone, Email, or Both.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the following options: Phone, Email, or Both.
* **When would you like to start?** (`date`, date, required: false): Choose a date and time for follow-up communication.
