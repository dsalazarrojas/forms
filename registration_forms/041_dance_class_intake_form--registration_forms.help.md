# Dance Class Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Class Intake Form is designed to collect information from prospective dance students to help us tailor our classes to their needs and preferences. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to ensure you have the best possible experience in our dance classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply go through each page, filling in the relevant information as prompted. You can move from one page to another by clicking on the 'Next' button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Student Information**
 Student Information (`student_information`, `text`, optional): 
This field is for you to provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a student. Please type in your name and any other information you think would be helpful for us to know.

### 2. **Contact Preferences**
 Contact Preferences (`contact_preferences`, `text`, optional): 
Here, you can let us know how you would like to be contacted - for example, phone, email, or in person. This will help us get in touch with you effectively.

### 3. **Payment Method**
 Payment Method (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): 
You can select from the options provided to choose how you would like to pay for your classes. This can be either online or in person.

### 4. **Experience**
 Experience (`experience`, `text`, optional): 
This field is where you can describe your past dance experience, if any. This will help us place you in the right level of class for your skill.

### 5. **Emergency Contact**
 Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
Please select who we should contact in case of an emergency - this could be a parent or guardian, or another contact of your choice. You can select multiple options if applicable.

### 6. **Class Preferences**
 Class Preferences (`class_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
Here, you can choose which dance class styles you are interested in - Ballet, Contemporary, Hip Hop, or Tap. You can select multiple options if you're interested in multiple styles.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields, even if they're optional, to ensure we can tailor our classes to your needs.
- You can always reach out to us if you have any questions or need more information.
- If you have any issues with submitting the form, don't hesitate to contact us.
