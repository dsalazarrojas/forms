# Flooring Promotion Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flooring Promotion Registration form is used to collect information for a flooring promotion. This form will help us track and follow up on promotions in a structured manner.

## How To Complete This Form
- To register a flooring promotion, please fill out this form carefully and accurately.
- Make sure to review each field for its required status and provide relevant information.
- If you're unsure about a field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
### Last Name
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
### Address
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### Date & Time
* **Date & Time** (`date_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the promotion.
### Category
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category for this promotion:
  - **Residential**: For promotions related to residential properties.
  - **Commercial**: For promotions related to commercial properties.
  - **Other**: For promotions that don't fit into the above categories.
### Promotion
* **Promotion** (`promotion`, select_multiple, optional): Check to indicate if this promotion is active:
  - **Yes**: The promotion is active.
  - **No**: The promotion is not active.
### Follow-up
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Check to indicate if follow-up is desired:
  - **Yes**: Follow-up is desired.
  - **No**: Follow-up is not desired.
### Lead Source
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, optional): Check to indicate the lead source:
  - **Yes**: The lead source is relevant.
  - **No**: The lead source is not relevant.
### Assigned to
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Check to indicate who is assigned to the promotion:
  - **Yes**: Someone is assigned to this promotion.
  - **No**: No one is assigned to this promotion.
### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool or resource assigned to this promotion.
  This field should contain the name or ID of the assigned tool or resource. 

Note: Please review each field carefully and make sure to provide accurate information. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
