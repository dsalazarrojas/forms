# Virtual Team Building Activity Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects registration information for participants in Virtual Team Building Activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form to register yourself or a team member for the Virtual Team Building Activity.
2. Enter your First Name.
3. Enter your Email (this will be used to contact you about activity updates and information).
4. Enter your Phone number (optional, but recommended for ease of contact).
5. Choose the date of your activity from the provided calendar.
6. Once you've completed all the required fields, click "Submit" to register.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you prefer to be known.
* **Email**: A working email address to receive updates and information about the activity.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number for easier communication (optional).
* **Activity Date**: Select the date of the Virtual Team Building Activity for which you are registering.
 
## Tips
- Ensure that you are registering for the correct team and activity.
- Review your information carefully before submitting.
- If you need to make any changes, please contact the activity organizers for assistance.
