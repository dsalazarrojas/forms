# Community Class Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Class Payment Form is used to register participants for a community class, collecting necessary information for payment and participation details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked as required.
2. Select the class and payment plan you wish to enroll in.
3. Ensure accurate information is entered, especially for participant contact details and class session dates.
4. Review the terms and conditions, and acknowledge your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Participant** (`participant_full_name_pay`, text, required): This is the participant's full name, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email_pay`, email, required): This is the participant's email address, used for payment confirmation and class communication.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`participant_phone_pay`, text, required): This is the participant's primary contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob_pay`, date, required): This is the participant's date of birth, used to verify age eligibility for age-restricted classes.
* **Class Selection** (`class_name_requested_pay`, text, required): This is the class you wish to register for, such as Yoga for Beginners or Intermediate Spanish.
* **Class Session Date** (`class_session_date_requested_pay`, date, required): This is the date of the class session you wish to attend.
* **Class Fee Category** (`class_fee_category_pay`, select_one, required): This is the fee category that applies to your class registration.
* **Total Amount to be Paid** (`total_class_fee_amount_pay`, number, required): This is the total fee amount due for the selected class.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_selection_pay`, select_one, required): This is your preferred payment method, such as Credit Card or PayPal.
* **Acknowledgment of Participation Terms** (`class_participation_acknowledgment_pay`, select_one, required): This is an acknowledgment of your understanding and agreement with the class participation terms.
* **Acknowledgment of Refund Policy** (`refund_policy_acknowledgment_pay`, select_one, required): This is an acknowledgment of your understanding and agreement with the class refund policy.
* **Photo Release Permission** (`photo_release_option_pay`, select_one, required): This is an acknowledgment of your permission to use your photo or video for promotional purposes.
* **Date of Registration** (`submission_date_final_pay`, date, required): This is the date you are registering for the class.
* **Digital Signature** (`participant_signature_digital_pay`, text, required): This is your digital signature, which confirms your registration.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_final_pay`, text, optional): This is an optional field for any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source_pay`, select_one, optional): This is an optional field to indicate how you heard about this class.
