# Personal Trainer Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a person's personal preferences and requirements for a personal trainer. The goal is to provide the best possible match between the individual and a suitable trainer, based on their specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts and provide the required information for each field. Please make sure to fill out all the required fields, as they are necessary for us to understand your requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Fitness Goal** (Select One, Required): Select your primary fitness goal (e.g., Weight Loss, Muscle Gain, etc.).
* **Experience Level** (Select One, Required): Indicate your level of fitness experience (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Availability** (Date, Required): Enter the date and time you are available to work with a trainer.
* **Availability Time** (Time, Required): Enter the specific time you are available to work with a trainer.
* **Location** (Text, Required): Enter the location where you would like to train.
* **Trainer Preference** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of training you prefer (e.g., Personal Training, Group Training, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (Select One, Optional): Indicate the relationship between you and the emergency contact person (e.g., Spouse, Parent, Friend).
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Text, Optional): Enter the email address of an emergency contact person.
* **Personal Trainer Recommendation** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate if you have any personal preference or recommendation for a trainer.
* **Additional Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional information or comments about your preferences.
* **Trainer Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of a trainer you have worked with before.
* **Trainer Contact Email** (Text, Optional): Enter the email address of a trainer you have worked with before.
* **Trainer Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of a trainer you have worked with before.
* **Trainer Experience Level** (Select One, Optional): Indicate the experience level of a trainer you prefer (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Trainer Availability** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the times of day you prefer for a trainer to be available.
* **Training Days** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the days of the week you prefer for training sessions.
* **Training Times** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the times of day you prefer for training sessions.
* **Comments** (Note, Required): Enter any additional information or comments about your preferences.

## Tips
Please make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in each field to help us find the best possible match for you. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
