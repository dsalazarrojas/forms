# Study Abroad Orientation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for students to provide basic information and contact details for the Study Abroad Orientation Registration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section of the form with your information carefully.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. Review your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information**: This section is for you to provide your student ID number.
* **Personal Information**: This section is for you to provide your personal details, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Information**: This section is for you to provide your contact information, such as your email and phone number.
* **Academic Program**: This section is not required, but if you have any information about your academic program, please provide it here.
* **Emergency Contact**: This section is for you to provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, relationship, contact information, and permission to contact them.
* **Student Declaration**: This section is for you to declare your understanding of the Study Abroad Orientation Registration.
* **Additional Information**: This section is for you to provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your Study Abroad experience.
* **Nationality**: Select your nationality from the options provided.
* **Passport**: Indicate whether you have a passport or not.
* **Visa Status**: Indicate whether you have a visa or not.
* **Language**: Select your primary language from the options provided.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact**: Select your relationship with your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Provide the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Provide the email of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Provide the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Student Declaration Checkbox**: Declare your understanding of the Study Abroad Orientation Registration by selecting the correct option.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
