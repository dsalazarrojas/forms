# School Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Course Registration Form is a tool for students to register for courses in a school setting. By filling out this form, students can provide their necessary information and course details to be enrolled in a new course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your student information in the "Student Information" section.
2.  Select the course you want to register for in the "Select a Course" section.
3.  Fill out the course details in the "Course Details" section.
4.  Provide the course name, start date, end date, time, and total weeks of the course in the respective fields.
5.  Select the class days for the course.
6.  Enter the classroom name and class size.
7.  Choose the course level (if applicable).
8.  Enter the course level other (if applicable).
9.  Enter your student email and phone number (if applicable).
10.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (1)**: Enter your name, student ID, and other relevant student information.
* **Select a Course (2)**: Choose the course you want to register for from the available options.
* **Course Details (3)**: Enter any additional details about the course you want to register for.
* **Course Name (4)**: Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Teacher Name (5)**: Enter the name of the teacher for the course (if applicable).
* **Start Date (6)**: Enter the start date of the course.
* **End Date (7)**: Enter the end date of the course.
* **Course Time (8)**: Enter the time of the course in 24 hour format.
* **Total Weeks (9)**: Enter the total number of weeks for the course.
* **Class Days (10)**: Select the days of the week when the class will meet.
* **Classroom (11)**: Enter the name of the classroom where the course will be held.
* **Class Size (12)**: Enter the size of the class.
* **Location (13)**: Enter the location of the course (if applicable).
* **Course Level (14)**: Choose the level of the course (if applicable).
* **Other Level (15)**: Enter other course level details (if applicable).
* **Student Email (16)**: Enter your student email address.
* **Student Phone (17)**: Enter your student phone number.
* **Submit (19)**: Click this button to submit your registration.
