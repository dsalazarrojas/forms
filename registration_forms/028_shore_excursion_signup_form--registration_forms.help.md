# Shore Excursion Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users for a shore excursion sign-up purpose. It is intended for users to provide required details about themselves and their tour payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as you will not be able to submit the form without providing this information.
2. Enter your name and any other relevant details in the "UserInfo" section.
3. Provide the required tour details in the "TourInfo" section.
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section.
5. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **UserInfo (Text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **TourInfo (Text)**: Enter the required information about your tour, including any specific details or requirements. Please note that this field is required to ensure we have the correct tour details.
* **Payment Info (Text)**: Enter your payment information, including any relevant credit card details. Please note that this field is required to complete the sign-up process.
* **Excursus Info (Text)**: This field is optional and intended for any additional information about your shore excursion. You can provide any relevant details or comments that you think will be helpful for the tour operator.
