# Regional Accommodation Data Project Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by organizations to register their regional accommodation data projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your organization's name in the `organization_name` field.
2. Provide your contact person's name and email address in the `contact_person` and `contact_email` fields respectively.
3. Indicate your organization's participation status in the `participation_status` field. Options include:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
	* Option 4
4. Enter any additional participation notes in the `participation_notes` field.
5. Select the current status of your project in the `project_status` field. Options include:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
	* Option 4
6. Enter the start date of your project in the `project_start_date` field.
7. Specify the location of your project in the `location` field.
8. Choose the type of accommodation from the `accommodation_type` field. Options include:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
	* Option 4
9. Enter the accommodation capacity in the `accommodation_capacity` field.
10. Provide any additional accommodation notes in the `accommodation_notes` field.
11. Enter your contact person's name in the `contact_person_name` field.
12. Enter your contact person's phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
13. Enter any additional contact notes in the `contact_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter your contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact person's email address.
* **Participation Consent** (`participation_consent`, note, required: false): This field is currently not used in the form. Please ignore.
* **Participation Status** (`participation_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose your organization's participation status.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Participation Notes** (`participation_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional participation notes.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the current status of your project.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Specify the location of your project.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of accommodation.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Accommodation Capacity** (`accommodation_capacity`, number, required: false): Enter the accommodation capacity.
* **Accommodation Notes** (`accommodation_notes`, text, required: false): Provide any additional accommodation notes.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, required: false): Enter your contact person's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional contact notes.

## Tips
