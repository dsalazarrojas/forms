# Gift Subscription Fulfillment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from gift subscription customers for order fulfillment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the order information, including order date, order notes, order amount, and payment method.
2.  Enter the customer's name, email, phone number, and shipping address.
3.  Choose the subscription type and gift details.
4.  Enter the order status and order ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Order Information (1)**: Enter the order date and notes for the order.
*   **Subscription Type (2)**: Choose the type of order, which can be Gift Subscription, Gift Certificate, or Other.
*   **Customer Name (3)**: Enter the customer's name for the order.
*   **Customer Email (4)**: Enter the customer's email for communication purposes.
*   **Customer Phone (5)**: Enter the customer's phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Order Date (6)**: Enter the order date for the fulfillment schedule.
*   **Order Notes (7)**: Add any additional notes for the order.
*   **Order Amount (8)**: Enter the total amount for the order.
*   **Payment Method (9)**: Choose the payment method for the order, which can be Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other.
*   **Payment Details (10)**: Enter any additional payment details.
*   **Gift Details (11)**: Enter any gift details for the order.
*   **Order Status (12)**: Choose the status of the order, which can be Pending, Shipped, or Cancelled.
*   **Customer Address (13)**: Enter the customer's address for shipping purposes.
*   **Gift Card Details (14)**: Enter any gift card details for the order.
*   **Shipping Address (15)**: Enter the shipping address for the order.
*   **Gift Subscription ID (16)**: Enter the gift subscription ID for tracking purposes.
*   **Order ID (17)**: Enter the order ID for tracking purposes.
*   **Order Number (18)**: Enter the order number for reference purposes.
*   **Subscription Plan (19)**: Enter the subscription plan for the order.
*   **Order Date 2 (20)**: Enter the order date for the fulfillment schedule.
*   **Order Details (21)**: Add any additional order details.
*   **Notes (22)**: Enter any additional notes for the order.
*   **Order Date 3 (23)**: Enter the order date for the fulfillment schedule.
*   **Customer Phone 2 (24)**: Enter the customer's phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Gift Subscription ID 2 (25)**: Enter the gift subscription ID for tracking purposes.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
*   Review the form carefully before submission.
*   Ensure that the form is filled in according to the customer's needs and order details.
