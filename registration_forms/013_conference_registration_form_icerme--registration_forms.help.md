# Conference Registration Form (ICERME) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Registration Form is a mandatory form for all participants attending the ICERME conference. It is used to collect essential information for registration and conference organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure to answer every required field.
3. Submit the form after completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your contact number for communication.
* **Institution or University** (4): Enter your affiliation.
* **Department** (5): Enter your department name (optional).
* **Academic or Professional Position** (6): Enter your title or position.
* **Country of Residence** (7): Select your country of residence.
* **Participant Category** (8): Select your registration type (e.g., Faculty/Professor, Researcher, etc.).
* **Conference Registration Type** (9): Select the type of conference registration you are applying for (e.g., Full Conference with Proceedings, Conference Only, etc.).
* **Paper Submission** (10): If submitting a paper, select your presentation type (Oral, Poster, Both).
* **Paper Title** (11): Enter your paper title if submitting a paper.
* **Co-Authors** (12): List co-authors if applicable.
* **Research Area** (13): Select your primary research area.
* **IEEE Membership** (14): Select your IEEE membership status.
* **IEEE Member ID** (15): Enter your IEEE member ID if applicable.
* **Conference Banquet** (16): Select whether you will attend the banquet.
* **Number of Guests** (17): Enter the number of additional guests attending.
* **Meal Preference** (18): Select your meal preference for the banquet.
* **Certificate of Participation** (19): Select whether you need a certificate of participation.
* **Conference Proceedings** (20): Select your preferred format for conference proceedings.
* **Special Needs or Dietary Requirements** (21): Enter any special needs or requirements.
* **Payment Method** (22): Select your preferred payment method.
* **I Accept Conference Terms** (23): Select whether you agree to the conference terms.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
