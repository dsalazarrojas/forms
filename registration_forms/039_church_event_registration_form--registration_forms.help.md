# Church Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Event Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees and participants for a specific event organized by the church. This form helps to ensure accurate record-keeping, facilitate communication, and provide a smooth event experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event you are registering for from the provided options.
2. Provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
3. If registering for a youth or child, please enter their date of birth.
4. Indicate your affiliation with the church (member, regular attendee, visitor, or guest speaker).
5. If applicable, indicate if a fee is required for this event and choose your registration fee level (Adult Registration, Student/Youth, Family Rate, or Donation Based).
6. Select your preferred payment method (Online Payment, Cash at Door, Check by Mail, or Prepaid).
7. Enter your emergency contact name and phone number.
8. Choose your dietary requirements (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten Free, Nut Free, or Dairy Free).
9. Confirm that you understand and agree to participate and follow all event rules by selecting the correct option.
10. Complete the form with the required information, and once you've reached the end of the form, click submit to register for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Select Event
* **Select Event** (`Select Event`, `select_one`, required): Choose the event you are registering for. This is a crucial step to ensure you are registering for the correct event.

### Full Name
* **Full Name** (`registrant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to by the church.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address_registration`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address to facilitate communication and updates about the event.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`mobile_phone_registration`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number to allow for easy contact in case of any event-related issues.

### Date of Birth (If youth/child)
* **Date of Birth (If youth/child)** (`date_of_birth_youth_only`, `date`, optional): If registering for a youth or child, enter their date of birth to help the church staff with their specific needs and requirements.

### Church Affiliation Status
* **Are you a member or visitor?** (`church_affiliation_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your relationship with the church to help the staff plan the event accordingly.

### Is there a fee for this event?
* **Is there a fee for this event?** (`fee_payment_required`, `select_one`, required): If a fee is required for this event, proceed to the next step to choose your registration fee level.

### Select Registration Fee Level
* **Select Registration Fee Level** (`payment_amount_selected`, `select_one`, optional): If a fee is applicable, choose your desired registration fee level. This helps to ensure accurate payment and billing.

### Preferred Payment Method
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_selection`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred payment method for this event. Options include Online Payment, Cash at Door, Check by Mail, and Prepaid.

### Emergency Contact Name and Phone
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact_info_registration`, `text`, required): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact to keep them informed about the event.

### Dietary Requirements
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements_registration`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your dietary preferences to ensure the church catering team can provide the necessary arrangements.

### I consent to participate and follow all event rules
* **I consent to participate and follow all event rules** (`participation_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to follow all event rules to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all.

### Registration Date
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date_timestamp`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your registration to track attendance and other event-related activities.

### Tips
* Please review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the church staff.
* Thank you for your cooperation and participation in the event!
