# Digital Inclusion In Higher Education Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information for the Digital Inclusion In Higher Education initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Information page.
2. Select your Access Needs from the provided options.
3. Choose your Support Needs from the provided options.
4. Select your Accommodations from the provided options.
5. Add any Additional Comments in the text field.
6. Click Submit to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, Optional): Please fill in your basic information such as name and contact details.
* **Access Needs** (`access_needs`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the types of access you require to participate in the initiative. Please choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Support Needs** (`support_needs`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the types of support you require to participate in the initiative. Please choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, select_multiple, Optional): Select any accommodations you require to participate in the initiative. Please choose from the options: Option 1 and Option 2.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, Optional): Add any additional comments or information that will help us better understand your needs and requirements.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, Optional): Please review your application and click Submit to submit it for processing.
