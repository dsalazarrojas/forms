# Program Kit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information and kit selections for program registration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" section.
2. Select your kit preferences in the "Kit Selections" section.
3. Enter your shipping information, including name, email, phone, street, city, state, and zip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Details
**Participant Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### Participant Name 
**Participant Age** (number): Enter your age.

### Kit Selections 
Select all applicable kits you are interested in. Options: Yes, No.

### Shipping Information 
Enter your shipping information, including name, email, phone, street, city, state, and zip.

#### Shipping Information
**Name** (text): Enter your name for shipping purposes. 
**Email** (email): Enter your email for shipping purposes. 
**Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for shipping purposes. 
**Street** (text): Enter your shipping street address. 
**City** (text): Enter the city for your shipping address. 
**State** (text): Enter the state for your shipping address. 
**Zip** (text): Enter the zip code for your shipping address.

#### Tips

* Make sure to double-check your participant details and kit selections for accuracy.
* Ensure your shipping information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues with kit delivery or participant registration, contact your program administrator.
