# Dodgeball Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dodgeball Registration Form is for registering users for the Dodgeball tournament. It is used to collect information about the user, including their name, email, date of participation, team members, time of participation, note, phone number, team member count, age, and address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name, which is your name as it appears on your ID.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
3. If you are participating on a specific date, enter it in the Date field. If you are not participating on a specific date, leave this field blank.
4. Select the Team Members you will be participating with from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter your Time of participation, which is the time you plan to start your game.
6. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the Note field.
7. Enter your Phone number, which will be used for contact purposes.
8. Enter the number of Team Members you will be participating with.
9. If you want to disclose your age, enter it in the Age field. If you are not comfortable disclosing your age, leave this field blank.
10. Enter your Address, which will be used for communication and updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you are participating on a specific date, enter it here. If you are not participating on a specific date, leave this field blank.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_one, required): Select the team members you will be participating with from the dropdown menu.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter your Time of participation, which is the time you plan to start your game.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your Phone number, which will be used for contact purposes.
* **Team Member Count** (`team_member_count`, number, required): Enter the number of Team Members you will be participating with.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): If you want to disclose your age, enter it here. If you are not comfortable disclosing your age, leave this field blank.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your Address, which will be used for communication and updates.
