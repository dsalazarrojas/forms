# Data Literacy Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Literacy Training Request Form is designed to collect information from participants about their training preferences, needs, and requirements. This form will help trainers to tailor the training sessions to meet the needs of the participants.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in your personal details such as name, email, phone number, and location.
- Select your preferred training type (Data Literacy, Data Visualization, or Business Intelligence).
- Specify your session preferences, including date, time, and location.
- Provide any additional comments or requests you may have.
- Review and agree to the participant and trainer agreements.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Location**: Enter your location.

### Session Preferences
* **Session Preferences**: Select your preferred date for the training session.
* **Time**: Select your preferred time for the training session.
* **Training Type**: Select the type of training you are interested in (Data Literacy, Data Visualization, or Business Intelligence).

### Additional Information
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of your preferred trainer.
* **Session Notes**: Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.

### Agreements
* **Participant Agreement**: Review and agree to the participant agreement.
* **Trainer Agreement**: Review and agree to the trainer agreement.

### Other Options
* **Other Requests**: Enter any other requests or comments you may have.
* **Session Note 2**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Participant Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the participant.
* **Other Agreement**: Review and agree to the other agreement.
* **Participant Agreement 2**: Review and agree to the participant agreement 2.
