# Facilitation Schedule Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for facilitation schedules, including workshop details, participant information, facilitator information, and participant schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the workshop details section, which includes information about the workshop.
2. Next, provide participant information, including any relevant details about the participants.
3. Then, add facilitator information, which includes details about the facilitator.
4. Finally, specify the participant schedule, which includes any relevant details about the participant's schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, text, required/optional): This section is used to collect information about the workshop. Please fill out this section with a brief description of the workshop, including its title, date, and time.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required/optional): This section is used to collect information about the participant. Please fill out this section with any relevant details about the participant, such as their name, title, and contact information.
* **Facilitator Information** (`facilitator_info`, text, required/optional): This section is used to collect information about the facilitator. Please fill out this section with any relevant details about the facilitator, such as their name, title, and contact information.
* **Participant Schedule** (`participant_schedule`, text, required/optional): This section is used to collect information about the participant's schedule. Please fill out this section with any relevant details about the participant's schedule, such as their availability and any conflicts they may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your information is accurately recorded.
