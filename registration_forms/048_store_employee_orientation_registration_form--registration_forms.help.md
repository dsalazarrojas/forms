# Store Employee Orientation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Store Employee Orientation Registration Form is used to register new employees for store orientation. This form will help ensure that all necessary information is collected and stored accurately for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information for the new employee.
2. Double-check the entered information for accuracy.
3. Review and select the correct schedule type for the employee.
4. Enter any additional notes if necessary.
5. Verify the employee's email address.
6. Review and confirm the entered store information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required, Text): Enter the employee's first name in the given format.
* **Last Name** (Required, Text): Enter the employee's last name in the given format.
* **Email** (Required, Email): Enter the employee's email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Phone** (Required, Text): Enter the employee's phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Store Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the store where the employee will be working.
* **Store Location** (Required, Text): Enter the address or location of the store where the employee will be working.
* **Store Manager** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the store manager or supervisor.
* **Start Date** (Required, Date): Enter the date the employee is expected to start working.
* **End Date** (Optional, Date): Enter the date the employee's orientation is expected to end.
* **Schedule Type** (Required, Select One): Select the schedule type the employee will be working.
* **Note** (Optional, Note): Enter any additional notes or information about the employee's orientation.
* **Email Verify** (Required, Select One): Verify the employee's email address to prevent spam and ensure email deliverability.
* **Store ID** (Optional, Number): Enter the store's ID number if necessary for internal use.
