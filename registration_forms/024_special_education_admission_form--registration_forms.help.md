# Special Education Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking special education support. The form is used to gather contact information and demographic data necessary for processing student admissions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter your first and last names correctly.
3. Enter your correct date of birth in the `Date of Birth` field.
4. Enter your valid email address in the `Email` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text, Optional)**: Enter your given name, which is the name given to you at birth.
* **Last Name (text, Optional)**: Enter your surname or family name.
* **Date of Birth (date, Optional)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email (email, Optional)**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone (text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your admission application.
* If you need help or have questions, contact our admissions team at [insert contact info].
