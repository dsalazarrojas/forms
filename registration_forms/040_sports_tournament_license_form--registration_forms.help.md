# sports_tournament_license_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports tournament license form is used to register for a sports tournament. This form collects information from participants and teams to verify their identity and eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Participants Name**: Enter the name of the participant who is registering for the tournament.
	* **Participants Email**: Enter the email address associated with the participant.
	* **Participants Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
2. Provide team information (if applicable):
	* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team that will be participating in the tournament.
	* **Team Email**: Enter the email address associated with the team.
	* **Team Phone**: Enter the phone number of the team.
3. Select relevant options:
	* **Select Participants**: Choose whether this is a participant registration.
	* **Select Team**: Choose whether this is a team registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: This is the name of the participant who is registering for the tournament.
* **Participant Email**: This is the email address associated with the participant.
* **Participant Phone**: This is the phone number of the participant.
* **Team Name**: This is the name of the team that will be participating in the tournament.
* **Team Email**: This is the email address associated with the team.
* **Team Phone**: This is the phone number of the team.
* **Event Details**: This field is for additional event details, if any.
* **Tournament Date**: Enter the date of the tournament.
* **Tournament Time**: Enter the time of the tournament.
* **License Number**: This is an optional field for the participant or team license number.
* **Select Participants**: Choose whether this is a participant registration.
* **Select Team**: Choose whether this is a team registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the tournament date and time to ensure you don't miss the tournament.
