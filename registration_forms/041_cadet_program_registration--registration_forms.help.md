# Cadet Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cadet Program Registration form is used to collect essential information from cadet applicants to register them for the program. This form helps gather details such as contact information, medical needs, and personal preferences to provide an efficient and safe experience for the cadets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and fill out each required field (marked as "required").
2.  Enter accurate and up-to-date information for the cadet and their guardian (if applicable).
3.  Select the correct options for the cadet's medical needs and interests.
4.  Confirm the cadet's agreement to abide by the program's Code of Conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Cadet Full Name** (`cadet_full_name`, Text, Required): Enter the cadet's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`cadet_dob`, Date, Required): Enter the cadet's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Gender** (`gender`, Select One, Required): Select the cadet's gender (Male, Female, Non-Binary, or Prefer not to say).
*   **Current School Grade** (`school_grade`, Number, Required): Select the cadet's current school grade level.
*   **Guardian Full Name** (`guardian_name`, Text, Required): Enter the guardian's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Guardian Contact Phone** (`guardian_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the guardian's primary contact phone number for emergency situations.
*   **Guardian Email Address** (`guardian_email`, Email, Required): Enter the guardian's email address for program communications.
*   **Known Allergies or Medical Conditions** (`allergies_medical_conditions`, Text, Required): List any known allergies or medical conditions that the cadet may have.
*   **Any medications required during program hours** (`medications_required`, Text, Required): List any medications that the cadet may require during program hours.
*   **Self-Assessed Physical Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, Select One, Required): Assess the cadet's physical fitness level (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Need Accommodations).
*   **Why are you interested in the Cadet Program?** (`interest_reason`, Text, Required): Enter a brief statement explaining why the cadet is interested in the program.
*   **Preferred Program Cycle** (`program_selection`, Select One, Required): Select the preferred program cycle (Spring Semester, Summer Intensive, Fall Semester, or Annual Program).
*   **Initial Uniform Size Estimate** (`uniform_size_pref`, Select One, Required): Select the initial uniform size estimate (XS, S, M, L, XL, or XXL).
*   **I agree to abide by the Cadet Code of Conduct** (`code_of_conduct_ack`, Select One, Required): Confirm agreement to abide by the Cadet Code of Conduct.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Use the correct format for dates (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   If unsure about any field, contact the program administrators for clarification.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
