# Makeup Artist Licensing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for a makeup artist licensing application. It's a standard form used for official government licensing purposes, ensuring that applicants provide accurate and complete details about themselves, their licenses, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and other personal details on page 1 (Applicant Details).
2.  Choose the type of license you are applying for on page 6 (Certification).
3.  Fill in your address details on page 10 (Address).
4.  Ensure you fill in all mandatory fields (marked as 'required').

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Name** (page 1): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
- **Street** (page 13): Enter your street address.
- **City** (page 12): Enter your city name.
- **State** (page 13): Enter your state or province name.
- **Zip** (page 14): Enter your postal/zip code.
- **Certification Type** (page 6): Choose the type of certification you are applying for (State License, Federal License, or Certification).
- **State License** (page 7): Enter your state-issued license number or any other state-related details.
- **Federal License** (page 8): Enter any federal-issued license number or details.
- **Certification** (page 9): Enter any additional certification details, if required.
- **Address** (page 10): Enter your complete address.
- **Contact Information** (page 4): Enter your contact details (likely phone number and email).
- **Payment Details** (page 3): Fill in any payment information (method or amount).
