**Form Purpose**
-------------

The Startup Pitch Judge Registration form is designed to collect and store information about startup judges for a pitch competition. This form helps in creating a database of judges who can evaluate and guide startups during the competition. It captures details such as their name, email, phone number, area of expertise, and availability. This form is crucial for efficient matching of startup pitches with suitable judges.

**How To Complete This Form**
-------------------------

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Fill in your name and email**: Provide your name and email address in the respective fields, which will be used to communicate with you.
2.  **Choose your area of expertise**: Select the area of expertise that you are most familiar with, such as Business Development, Data Science, Design, Finance, Marketing, Product Development, or Software Development.
3.  **Choose your availability**: Select the days of the week you are available to judge, which could be Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday.
4.  **Provide a brief description**: Write a brief description of yourself, which will help startups understand your qualifications.
5.  **Enter your form ID**: This is a unique ID assigned to this form.
6.  **Form Type and Form Type ID**: These fields are for internal use and can be left blank.
7.  **Form Version and Form Version ID**: These fields are also for internal use and can be left blank.
8.  **Category and Form Status**: These fields are for internal categorization and status of the form.
9.  **Save Your Changes**: After filling in all the required fields, click the submit button to save your changes.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**
---------------------------

### Judge Details

*   **Judge Name**: Your name that will be displayed to the startup pitchers.
    (`Judge Name`, `text`, `required`: true)
*   **Judge Email**: Your email address that will be used for communication with you.
    (`Judge Email`, `email`, `required`: true)
*   **Judge Phone**: Your phone number that will be used for emergency contacts.
    (`Judge Phone`, `text`, `required`: true)
*   **Judge Expertise**: Your area of expertise that will be matched with the startup pitches.
    (`Judge Expertise`, `select_one`, `required`: false)
*   **Judge Availability**: The days of the week you are available to judge.
    (`Judge Availability`, `select_multiple`, `required`: false)
*   **Judge Description**: A brief description of your qualifications and experience.
    (`Judge Description`, `note`, `required`: false)
