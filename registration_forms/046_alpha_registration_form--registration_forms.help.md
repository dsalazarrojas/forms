# Alpha Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about you and your preferences for joining the Alpha program. It will help us understand your motivations, demographics, and interests, allowing us to tailor the experience to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked as 'true' in the form schema.
2. For optional fields, you can choose to skip or fill out as much or as little information as you'd like.
3. Ensure you review and understand our terms of participation before proceeding.
4. If you encounter any issues or have concerns, you can use the 'questions or concerns' field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name (required). This is the name you'd like to be known by within the Alpha community.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name (required). This is the surname you'd like to use.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address (required). We'll use this for updates and communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number (required). This will allow us to reach you for any last-minute updates.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your full mailing address (optional). This is helpful for sending out materials and notifications.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (optional). This helps us understand your age group.
* **Gender**: Select your gender (optional). This helps us tailor the experience to your needs.
* **Marital Status**: Select your current marital status (optional). This is for our records and planning purposes.
* **Church Membership**: Indicate whether you're a member of a church (required). This helps us understand your spiritual background.
* **Church Attendance**: Select how often you attend church (optional). This helps us gauge your level of spiritual engagement.
* **Why Do You Want to Join Alpha?**: Explain your motivation for joining Alpha (required). This helps us understand your reasons for participating.
* **Faith Background**: Describe your faith background (required). This helps us tailor the experience to your spiritual journey.
* **Previous Alpha Experience**: Indicate whether you've taken Alpha before (required). This helps us understand your prior experience.
* **Session Preference**: Select your preferred session type (required). This helps us schedule the course to your needs.
* **Childcare Needed**: Indicate whether you require childcare (required). This helps us plan for your family's needs.
* **Number of Children**: If you need childcare, enter the number of children you have (optional). This helps us plan for their care.
* **Children Ages**: List the ages of your children (optional). This helps us understand their needs.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Enter any dietary restrictions you have (optional). This helps us provide suitable meals.
* **Interested in Volunteering?**: Indicate whether you're interested in volunteering (optional). This helps us understand your willingness to help.
* **Volunteer Areas of Interest**: Select all volunteer areas you're interested in (optional). This helps us allocate your skills effectively.
* **Small Group Preference**: Select your group preference (optional). This helps us tailor the group dynamics.
* **Prayer Request**: Enter any specific prayer requests you have (optional). This helps us understand your spiritual needs.
* **Questions or Concerns**: Enter any questions or concerns you may have (optional). This helps us address them before the course starts.
* **How Did You Hear About Alpha?**: Select how you heard about Alpha (optional). This helps us understand our outreach efforts.
* **Who Referred You?**: Enter the name of the person who referred you (optional). This helps us track referrals.
* **Preferred Start Date**: Enter your preferred start date for the course (required). This helps us schedule the course to your needs.
* **Commitment Understanding**: Confirm that you understand the course length and commitment (required). This ensures you're aware of the program's duration.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share (optional). This helps us understand you better.
* **Terms Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to participate in the Alpha program (required). This confirms your understanding of the terms.
