# Creative Producer Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for a creative producer fellowship. The purpose of this form is to gather details about your background, artistic vision, and goals, as well as your contact and experience information. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure your application is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields. If applicable, enter your **Contact Number** to ensure easy communication.
2. Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you about your application.
3. In the **Background** field, briefly describe your background in the arts.
4. In the **Artistic Vision** field, describe what you see yourself contributing to the arts community through this fellowship.
5. Upload a URL to your **Portfolio** if you have a website or online presence showcasing your work.
6. Select the **Skills** you possess, such as Photography, Writing, Design, or Other.
7. In the **Goals** field, specify what you hope to achieve through this fellowship.
8. In the **Recommendations** field, you can provide any relevant information or references.
9. Finally, select **Submit** to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number (optional).
* **Background**: Briefly describe your background in the arts.
* **Artistic Vision**: Describe what you see yourself contributing to the arts community through this fellowship.
* **Portfolio**: Upload a URL to your portfolio or website.
* **Experience**: Briefly describe your experience in the arts.
* **Skills**: Select the skills you possess, such as Photography, Writing, Design, or Other.
* **Goals**: Specify what you hope to achieve through this fellowship.
* **Recommendations**: Provide any relevant information or references.
* **Submit**: Select this option to complete and submit your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete application.
* Use a professional email address and ensure it's accurate.
* Upload a URL to your portfolio or website to showcase your work.
* Be specific and concise in your answers to provide a clear picture of your background, artistic vision, and goals.
* Ensure your recommendations are relevant and support your application.
* Review your application carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
