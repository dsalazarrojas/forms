# Weekend Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for a weekend trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the 4 main fields:

1. Fill out the participant information (page 1).
2. Provide details about yourself (page 2).
3. Let us know your trip preferences (page 3).
4. Enter your emergency contact information (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> Participant Info </label> (Page 1, participant_info)
* Participant info: Your basic contact information (e.g., name, username).
### <label> Participant Details </label> (Page 2, participant_details)
* This section is for you to provide any additional details about yourself that you feel are relevant to the trip.
### <label> Trip Preferences </label> (Page 3, trip_preferences)
* Let us know about any specific preferences you have for the trip (e.g., accommodations, activities).
### <label> Emergency Contact </label> (Page 4, emergency_contact)
* Enter a contact person's information in case of an emergency.
### <label> Submit </label> (Page 5, submit)
* Click to submit your registration.
