# Brewery Tasting Tour RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brewery Tasting Tour RSVP Form is designed to register visitors for a guided tour of the brewery. It ensures that the visitor can choose their preferred tour date and time and provides essential information to the brewery about the guests' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in a single sitting, as it will be saved as you progress.
- Ensure that you enter all required fields (indicated by `**Required** in parentheses).
- Review your submission before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Primary Guest Name** (`primary_guest_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the main guest who will be attending the tour.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **Required**): Enter the email address associated with the main guest for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the contact phone number for the main guest, so the brewery can reach out if needed.
* **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, `date`, **Required**): Select the tour date you would like to attend from the available options.
* **Tour Time** (`tour_time`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose your preferred tour time from the available options (e.g., 10 AM, 12 PM, 2 PM, 4 PM, 6 PM).
* **Number of Guests in Your Party** (`group_size`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the total number of people attending the tour.
* **Names of Other Guests** (`guest_names`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide names of any additional guests attending the tour. Separate names with commas or enter one name per line if needed.
* **All Guests are 21 or Older** (`age_requirement`, `select_one`, **Required**): Confirm that all guests in your party are 21 or older.
* **Tour Type Preference** (`tour_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose your preferred type of tour (e.g., Standard brewery tour, Premium tasting tour, VIP exclusive tour, Brewery and taproom combo, Beer and food pairing).
* **First Time Visitor** (`first_time_visitor`, `select_one`, **Required**): Confirm if this is the main guest's first visit to the brewery.
* **Your Beer Experience Level** (`beer_experience`, `select_one`, **Required**): Self-identify your knowledge level about beer (beginner, casual, enthusiast, connoisseur).
* **Favorite Beer Styles** (`favorite_beer_styles`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select any beer styles you enjoy (e.g., IPAs, Lagers, Stouts, Wheat beers, Sours, Amber ales, Porters, No preference).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Check any dietary restrictions you or your guests may have (e.g., Gluten-free, Vegan, Vegetarian, Nut allergy, Dairy-free, None).
* **Special Occasion** (`special_occasions`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Indicate if this visit is for a special occasion (e.g., birthday, anniversary, corporate outing, bachelor/bachelorette party).
* **Special Occasion Details** (`celebration_details`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide any additional information about the special occasion if applicable.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, **Optional**): Mention any specific accessibility needs or accommodations required for the tour.
* **Transportation** (`transportation`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Indicate how you plan to arrive (e.g., need parking info, have own transportation, need public transit info, other).
* **How to Contact You** (`contact_preferences`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone call, text message, no contact needed).
* **Photography Consent** (`photography_consent`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Confirm whether you are comfortable with photography for social media.
* **Special Requests or Comments** (`additional_requests`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide any additional information or requests you have.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment** (`cancellation_notice`, `select_one`, **Required**): Confirm that you understand and agree to the brewery's cancellation policy.
