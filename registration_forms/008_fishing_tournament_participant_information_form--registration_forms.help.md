# Fishing Tournament Participant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect contact and participant information from individuals who are interested in or have already registered for the fishing tournament. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure you receive updates and potential spot assignments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name in the **participant_name** field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the **participant_email** field.
3. Provide your phone number in the **participant_phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (Text):
	+ This field is used to collect your participant name.
	+ Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the tournament participant list.
* **participant_email** (Email):
	+ This field is required to collect a valid email address where you can be reached for tournament updates and communications.
	+ Please ensure the format is in the standard email format (e.g., [name]@[domain].com).
* **participant_phone** (Text):
	+ This field is used to collect your phone number for any potential follow-up questions or updates from the tournament organizers.
	+ Please enter your phone number in the format you prefer to be contacted.
