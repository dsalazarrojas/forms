# IT Support Course Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register a participant's interest in an IT support course. It is designed to gather information about the participant's preferences and needs for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the course you are interested in from the "Select a Course" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you have any additional notes or information, you can enter them in the "Notes" field.
6. If you have any specific course name, start date, or end date, you can enter them in their respective fields.
7. Confirm your enrollment in the "Confirm Enrollment" dropdown menu.
8. Finally, if you have any additional information, you can enter it in the "Additional Info" field.
9. Select the course location from the "Course Location" multiple-select menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a Course** (`course_selection`, select_one, required: false): Select the course you are interested in from the dropdown menu. Please make sure to select the correct course to ensure you are registered for the correct course.
* **Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification. Please make sure to enter your name correctly to avoid any delays in processing your registration.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached. Please make sure to enter a valid email address to receive any course materials or updates.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached. Please make sure to enter a valid phone number to receive any course updates or reminders.
* **Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required: false): If you have any additional notes or information about your registration, you can enter them here. Please keep your notes concise and relevant to the course.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): If you have a specific course name, you can enter it here. Please make sure to enter the correct course name to ensure you are registered for the correct course.
* **Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, required: false): If you have a specific start date for the course, you can enter it here. Please make sure to enter the correct start date to ensure you are registered for the correct course.
* **End Date** (`course_end_date`, date, required: false): If you have a specific end date for the course, you can enter it here. Please make sure to enter the correct end date to ensure you are registered for the correct course.
* **Confirm Enrollment** (`confirm_enrollment`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your enrollment in the course by selecting the "Enrollment Confirmed" option. Please make sure to confirm your enrollment to proceed with the registration process.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, required: false): If you have any additional information about your registration, you can enter it here. Please keep your notes concise and relevant to the course.
* **Course Location** (`course_location`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the course location from the multiple-select menu. Please make sure to select the correct location to ensure you are registered for the correct course location.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number to receive any course materials or updates.
