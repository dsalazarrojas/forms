# Beef Rate Of Gain Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Beef Rate Of Gain Registration" form is used to record and report the weight gain of beef animals. This form is essential for tracking and maintaining accurate records of animal weight, feed, and management practices. By providing accurate information, this form helps ensure the quality and integrity of beef production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* "Event or Show Name" (Event Name)
	* "Producer/Exhibitor Name" (Producer Full Name)
	* "Email Address" (Contact Email)
	* "Animal Tag/ID Number" (Animal Tag ID)
	* "Primary Breed or Breed Type" (Primary Breed)
	* "Animal Date of Birth" (Birth Date of Animal)
	* "Initial Weigh-in Date" (Initial Weigh-in Date)
	* "Initial Weight (lbs)" (Initial Weight)
	* "Final Weigh-in Date" (Final Weigh-in Date)
	* "Final Weight (lbs)" (Final Weight)
	* "Official Witness or Weigh-master Name" (Official Witness Name)
	* "I Certify" (Certify)
	* "Date of Registration" (Date of Registration)
2. Select from the available options:
	* "Primary Feed Type" (Feed Type)
	* "Were any feed additives or supplements used?" (Feed Additives)
	* "Notes on Health or Management" (Management Notes)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event or Show Name** (Event Name): Enter the name of the event or show where the animal is participating.
* **Producer/Exhibitor Name** (Producer Full Name): Enter the name of the producer or exhibitor who owns the animal.
* **Email Address** (Contact Email): Enter the email address of the producer or exhibitor for contact purposes.
* **Animal Tag/ID Number** (Animal Tag ID): Enter the unique identification number of the animal.
* **Primary Breed or Breed Type** (Primary Breed): Choose the primary breed or type of animal being registered.
* **Animal Date of Birth** (Birth Date of Animal): Enter the date of birth of the animal.
* **Initial Weigh-in Date** (Initial Weigh-in Date): Enter the date of the initial weigh-in.
* **Initial Weight (lbs)** (Initial Weight): Enter the initial weight of the animal in pounds.
* **Final Weigh-in Date** (Final Weigh-in Date): Enter the date of the final weigh-in.
* **Final Weight (lbs)** (Final Weight): Enter the final weight of the animal in pounds.
* **Official Witness or Weigh-master Name** (Official Witness Name): Enter the name of the official witness or weigh-master who conducted the weigh-in.
* **Were any feed additives or supplements used?** (Feed Additives): Select whether any feed additives or supplements were used on the animal.
* **Notes on Health or Management** (Management Notes): Enter any notes on the animal's health or management history.
* **I Certify** (Certify): Select to certify the accuracy of the weight measurements.
* **Date of Registration** (Date of Registration): Enter the date of registration for the animal.
