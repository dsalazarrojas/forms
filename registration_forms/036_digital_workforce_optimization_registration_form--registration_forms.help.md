# Digital Workforce Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Workforce Optimization Registration Form is designed to collect employee information for the Digital Workforce Optimization process. This form helps us understand your job title, employee role, and other relevant details to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options from the dropdown menus for job titles and other fields.
3. Use your actual email address and phone number.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
5. Double-check your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Page (First Page)**: This is the starting page of the form.
- **Employee Information (Employee Information)**: Enter a brief description of the employee.
- **Job Title (Job Title)**: A text field for entering your job title.
- **Email (Email)**: Enter your actual email address.
- **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your actual phone number.
- **Job Title 2 (Job Title 2)**: Select one of the available job titles (Manager, Director, Team Member).
- **Job Title 3 (Job Title 3)**: Select multiple job titles (Manager, Director, Team Member).
- **Date (Date)**: Enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Time (Time)**: Enter a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional comments or details about your job title.
- **Manager (Manager)**: Select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have a manager.
- **Job Title 4 (Job Title 4)**: A text field for entering your job title.
- **Job Title 5 (Job Title 5)**: A numeric field for entering a job title number.
- **Job Title 6 (Job Title 6)**: A numeric field for entering a job title number.
- **Job Title 7 (Job Title 7)**: Select one of the available job titles (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract).
- **Job Title 8 (Job Title 8)**: Select multiple job titles (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract).
- **Job Title 9 (Job Title 9)**: Enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Job Title 10 (Job Title 10)**: Enter a time in the format HH:MM.
- **Job Title 11 (Job Title 11)**: A note field for entering additional comments or details.
- **Job Title 12 (Job Title 12)**: Enter your email address.
