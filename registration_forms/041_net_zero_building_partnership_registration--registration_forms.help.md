The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from organizations that are interested in participating in the Net Zero Building Partnership. This form will help the Net Zero Building Partnership team understand the type of organization and its expertise, and will enable them to determine the best way to onboard the partner and provide support throughout the partnership.

The registration form has 11 fields, and the fields with a "note" type will have a larger text area for notes. This form is not intended for public use, and it is recommended to fill it out only by authorized personnel from the Net Zero Building Partnership team or its partners.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration information page with your organization's details.
2. Select your organization's type from the drop-down menu.
3. Provide your contact's first name and last name.
4. Enter your contact's email address and phone number.
5. Describe your areas of expertise and interest.
6. Select the type of partnership you are interested in.
7. Describe the onboarding process you would prefer.
8. Add any additional notes about your partnership interest.
