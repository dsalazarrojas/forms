# Accountants Society Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential members of the Accountants Society, including personal details, professional information, and membership preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information section, which includes your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your membership type (Student, Associate, Full, Fellow, Honorary, etc.) and provide your current professional status (Practicing accountant, etc.).
3. Enter your current employer or firm name and job title.
4. Specify how you heard about the society (colleague referral, etc.).
5. If applicable, select areas of interest for society activities and indicate your volunteer interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
  - This is a required field that helps us know your identity.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your professional email address.
  - This is a required field to ensure we can contact you.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number.
  - This is a required field for communication purposes.
- **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your mailing address.
  - This is a required field for postal correspondence.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter your date of birth.
  - This field is optional but helps us understand your profile.
- **Membership Type Requested** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of membership you prefer.
  - This is a required field to assess eligibility for membership.
- **Current Professional Status** (`professional_status`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose your current status in the accounting profession.
  - This is a required field to understand your experience.
- **Current Employer or Firm** (`employer`, `text`, `required`): Enter your current employer or firm name.
  - This is a required field for professional reference.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, `required`): Enter your job title.
  - This is a required field to understand your role.
- **Years in the Accounting Profession** (`years_in_profession`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of years you have been in the accounting profession.
  - This is a required field to assess your experience.
- **Professional Certifications Held** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the professional certifications you hold.
  - This is a required field to understand your qualifications.
- **Area of Specialization** (`specialization`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose your area of specialization.
  - This is a required field to understand your focus.
- **How Did You Hear About the Society** (`referral`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how you heard about the society.
  - This is a required field for evaluation purposes.
- **Areas of Interest for Society Activities** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select all the areas of interest for society activities.
  - This is an optional field to understand your interests.
- **Would You Be Interested in Volunteering for Society Committees** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, `optional`): Indicate your interest in volunteering.
  - This is an optional field to understand your willingness to contribute.
- **I Agree to Abide by the Society Code of Ethics and Bylaws** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, `required`): Confirm your agreement to abide by the society's code of ethics and bylaws.
  - This is a required field to ensure understanding of society rules.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any additional comments or information.
  - This is an optional field for further explanation.
