# Skincare Product Reseller Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Product Reseller Application Form is designed to collect information from resellers who wish to sell skincare products. This form helps us understand their reseller status and preferences, which we will use to tailor our services and products accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for your reseller status.
3. Enter your email address and password.
4. Fill out the other fields as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reseller Info** (`reseller_info`, `text`, required/optional): This is a text field where you can provide any relevant information about yourself as a reseller.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your address where we can send any communication related to your reseller account.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive any updates and notifications from us.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your password to keep your account secure.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): This is a dropdown menu where you must select one of the available options.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): This is a multiple-select dropdown menu where you must select multiple options.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Please select the date you are available to start selling with us.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time you are available to start selling with us.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to add to your application.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, required/optional): Re-enter your password to confirm your account information. 

Note: Please review each field carefully to ensure it is filled out correctly. The form will not submit if any required fields are left blank. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you're not sure about any of the fields, ask for clarification.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure you've filled out all required fields.
