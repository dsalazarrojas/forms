# Creative Assessment Practice Exchange Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the "Creative Assessment Practice Exchange Registration" form. The form is intended for individuals interested in exchanging creative practices, such as sharing their work, seeking feedback, or collaborating with others in the creative field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Info" section with your details, such as your name and any other relevant information you'd like to share.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section, including your email address and any other contact methods you prefer.
3. List your interests in the "Interests" section, so that others with similar interests can connect with you.
4. Describe your relevant experience in the "Experience" section, highlighting your skills and areas of expertise.
5. Specify your preferences for practice exchange in the "Practice Exchange" section, including any specific types of exchanges you're looking for (e.g., feedback, mentorship, etc.).
6. In the "Confirm" section, simply type your name to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`title`, `text`, optional): This section is where you can provide basic information about yourself, such as your name and any other details you'd like to share.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): In this section, enter your email address and any other contact methods you prefer, so that others can reach out to you.
* **Interests** (`interests`, `text`, optional): List your areas of interest, such as specific creative fields or skills, so that others with similar interests can connect with you.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, optional): Share your relevant experience, including any notable projects, skills, or achievements that showcase your expertise.
* **Practice Exchange** (`practice_exchange`, `text`, optional): Specify your preferences for practice exchange, such as seeking feedback, mentorship, or collaborative projects.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `text`, optional): Type your name to confirm your registration and ensure that your information is accurate.
