# Digital Marketing Certification Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Marketing Certification Enrollment Form is designed to collect personal and professional details from individuals interested in obtaining a digital marketing certification. The information collected will be used for program enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
2. Choose your answer to the program question.
3. Enter the date of interest.
4. Provide the number of years of experience in the field.
5. Enter your current company name and location.
6. List your relevant skills and education.
7. (Optional) Attach your resume and provide any additional certification information.
8. Choose whether to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (id: 1)**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email (id: 2)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone (id: 3)**: Enter your active phone number.
* **Program (id: 4)**: Select 'Yes' if you wish to enroll in the program.
* **Date of Interest (id: 5)**: Enter the date you are interested in starting the program.
* **Years of Experience (id: 6)**: Enter the number of years you have worked in the field.
* **Company (id: 7)**: Enter the name of your current company.
* **Location (id: 8)**: Enter your current location.
* **Education (id: 9)**: List your relevant education background.
* **Skills (id: 10)**: List your relevant skills.
* **Resume (id: 11)**: (Optional) Attach your resume for further evaluation.
* **Certification (id: 12)**: (Optional) Provide any additional certification information.
* **Other Information (id: 13)**: (Optional) Enter any other relevant information.
* **Submit (id: 14)**: Select 'Yes' to submit your application.
