# Music Business Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from music businesses for proposal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your company's information in the required fields.
* Provide a brief description of your music business.
* Choose the business model and target market that best fits your business.
* Provide a financial projection of your business.
* List the members of your management team.
* Outline your marketing strategy.
* Choose the sales channels for your music business.
* Provide the product launch date.
* Set the price of your product.
* Answer yes or no to the business model option question.
* Select the best option for each of the three selection questions.
* Provide your phone number.
* Enter your email address.
* Enter your website address.
* List your social media presence.
* Upload a file if necessary.
* Provide a description of your product.
* Upload a product image.
* Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_label** (text, required): This is the label of the form.
* **company_name** (text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **business_description** (text, optional): Briefly describe your music business.
* **business_model** (text, optional): Enter your business model.
* **target_market** (text, optional): Enter your target market.
* **product_services** (text, optional): Enter the services your product offers.
* **competition** (text, optional): Briefly describe the competition.
* **financial_projection** (text, optional): Provide a financial projection of your business.
* **management_team** (text, optional): List the members of your management team.
* **marketing_strategy** (text, optional): Outline your marketing strategy.
* **sales_channels** (text, optional): Choose the sales channels for your music business.
* **product_launch_date** (date, optional): Enter the product launch date.
* **product_price** (number, optional): Set the price of your product.
* **business_model_option** (select multiple, optional): Answer yes or no to the business model option question.
* **option_1** (select one, optional): Select the best option for your music business.
* **option_2** (select multiple, optional): Select the best options for your music business.
* **option_3** (select one, optional): Select the best option for your music business.
* **phone_number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **website** (text, optional): Enter your website address.
* **social_media** (text, optional): List your social media presence.
* **file_upload** (text, optional): Upload a file if necessary.
* **product_description** (text, optional): Provide a description of your product.
* **product_image** (text, optional): Upload a product image.
* **submit_button** (note, optional): Click the submit button to send the form.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is intended to be filled out by registered music businesses only. Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely before submitting it.
