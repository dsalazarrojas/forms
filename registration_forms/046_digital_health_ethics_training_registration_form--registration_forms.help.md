# Digital Health Ethics Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register individuals for digital health ethics training. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the individual's training date, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and surname in the "First name" and "Last name" fields.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to contact you about your training.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Select a date for the training from the calendar picker or enter it manually in the "Training date" field.
5. Enter the duration of the training in the "Training duration" field.
6. Choose the location of the training in the "Training location" field.
7. Enter the name of the training instructor in the "Training instructor" field.
8. Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact name" and "Emergency contact phone" fields.
9. Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact relationship" field.
10. Sign and date the form with your signature in the "Submitter signature" field.
11. Enter your organization and title in the "Submitter organization" and "Submitter title" fields.
12. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Submitter note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Training date** (`training_date`, date, required): Select a date for the training or enter it manually.
* **Training time** (`training_time`, time, optional): This field is not required. You can leave it blank.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Training location** (`training_location`, text, required): Choose the location of the training.
* **Training duration** (`training_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the training.
* **Training instructor** (`training_instructor`, text, required): Enter the name of the training instructor.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Submitter signature** (`submitter_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form with your signature.
* **Submitter name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter note** (`submitter_note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Submitter organization** (`submitter_organization`, text, required): Enter your organization.
* **Submitter title** (`submitter_title`, text, required): Enter your title.
* **Submitter company** (`submitter_company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Submitter designation** (`submitter_designation`, text, required): Enter your designation.
* **Submitter abbreviation** (`submitter_abbreviation`, text, optional): Enter any abbreviation for your organization or title.
* **Submitter jobtitle** (`submitter_jobtitle`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Submitter position** (`submitter_position`, text, optional): Enter your position.
