# Launch Event Coordination Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Launch Event Coordination Absence Form is used to report your availability and reason for absence for upcoming events. This form helps event coordinators understand who will be absent and why, ensuring that necessary arrangements can be made for a smooth event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the event title, date, and time to ensure you are filling out the correct form.
2. Enter your name and select a reason for your absence (if applicable).
3. Add any additional notes for the event coordinator to consider.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the event you are coordinating for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Prophet's Name** (`prophets_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you are reporting for (if applicable).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Absence Reason** (`absence_reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for your absence (if applicable).
* **Absence Note** (`absence_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the event coordinator.
* **Event Time Note** (`event_time_note`, time, optional): Enter any specific time-related details for the event.
* **Event Date Note** (`event_date_note`, date, optional): Enter any specific date-related details for the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.

Tips:
- Be sure to fill out the form for each event you need to report for.
- Check your email and phone number for accuracy before submitting the form.
