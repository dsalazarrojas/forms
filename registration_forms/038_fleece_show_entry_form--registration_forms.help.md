# Fleece Show Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fleece Show Entry Form is designed to collect exhibitor details, fleece information, and entry information for a trade show or exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field with the requested information.
2. For each field, please make sure to enter the correct information according to the field's label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **exhibitor_details** (exhibitor_details): Enter your exhibitor's name and any other relevant details.
* **exhibitor_fleece_info** (exhibitor_fleece_info): Provide any information related to the fleece, such as its characteristics, features, or unique selling points.
* **entry_info** (entry_info): Enter any information about the entry, such as its title, description, or category.
* **entry_classing** (entry_classing): Classify the entry according to the given options or enter any relevant text information.

## Tips
* Please double-check your input for accuracy to ensure the collected information is correct and helpful.
* If you need to add any additional information or clarify any points, please feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
