# retail_store_showcase_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to reserve a time slot for a retail store showcase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the `name` field with your name.
2. Enter your `email` address in the `email` field.
3. Enter your `phone` number in the `phone` field.
4. Choose the `date` and `time` for the event.
5. Enter the `event_name` and `event_description` of the event.
6. Select the `event_start_date` and `event_end_date` of the event.
7. Enter the `showcase_location` of the event.
8. Choose the `showcase_start_time` and `showcase_end_time` of the event.
9. Enter the `capacity` of the event.
10. Enter the number of `available_slots` for the event.
11. Enter the number of `available_time_slots` for the event.
12. Enter the `time_per_slot` for the event.
13. Select the `reservation_type` of the event from the available options.
14. Enter a `customer_note` if any additional information needs to be shared.
15. Fill in the `form_id`, `customer_id`, `event_id`, and `showcase_id` fields with the corresponding IDs, if required.
14. Finally, select one or more `slot` options for the event from the available slots.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name**: A field to fill in your name.
* **email**: A field to enter your email address.
* **phone**: A field to enter your phone number.
* **date**: A field to select the date of the event.
* **time**: A field to select the time of the event.
* **event_name**: A field to enter the name of the event.
* **event_description**: A field to enter the description of the event.
* **event_start_date**: A field to select the start date of the event.
* **event_end_date**: A field to select the end date of the event.
* **showcase_location**: A field to enter the location of the event.
* **showcase_start_time**: A field to select the start time of the event.
* **showcase_end_time**: A field to select the end time of the event.
* **capacity**: A field to enter the capacity of the event.
* **available_slots**: A field to enter the number of available slots for the event.
* **available_time_slots**: A field to enter the number of available time slots for the event.
* **time_per_slot**: A field to enter the time per slot for the event.
* **reservation_type**: A field to select the type of reservation from the available options.
* **customer_note**: A field to enter any additional notes from the customer.
* **form_id**: A field to fill in the form ID, if required.
* **customer_id**: A field to fill in the customer ID, if required.
* **event_id**: A field to fill in the event ID, if required.
* **showcase_id**: A field to fill in the showcase ID, if required.
* **slot**: A field to select one or more time slots for the event from the available options.
