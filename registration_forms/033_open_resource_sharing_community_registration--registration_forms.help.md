# Open Resource Sharing Community Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Resource Sharing Community Registration Form is a simple, user-friendly form designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the Open Resource Sharing Community. The form is meant to be completed by those wishing to become members of the community, and it should only take a few minutes to fill out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Registration Form section, which includes fields such as your name, email, and contact information.
2. In the Member Info section, provide details about yourself, including your first name, last name, and any other relevant information.
3. If you have something to offer, such as expertise or resources, please specify what you can offer to the community in the "What Can Offer" field.
4. In the "What Do You Need" field, describe what you're looking for within the community, such as resources or connections.
5. If you're interested in exchanging resources with others, select the appropriate option in the "Community Resource Exchange" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): This section is the starting point for your registration. It's a placeholder field that helps us understand that you're interested in joining the community.
### Member Info
* **Member Info** (`member_info`, text, required: false): This section is for providing personal details about yourself, including your name, email, and any other relevant information.
### What Can Offer
* **What Can Offer** (`what_can_offer`, text, required: false): If you have expertise, resources, or something else to offer to the community, please describe it briefly.
### What Do You Need
* **What Do You Need** (`what_need`, text, required: false): In this field, describe what you're looking for within the community, such as resources, connections, or expertise.
### Community Resource Exchange
* **Community Resource Exchange** (`resource_exchange`, select_multiple, required: false): If you're interested in exchanging resources with other members, select the options that apply to you.
*Note: Please select all that apply.
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your email address.
### First Name
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
### Email
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
