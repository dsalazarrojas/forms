# productivity_coaching_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Productivity Coaching Intake Form is designed to gather information from clients to personalize and schedule coaching sessions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill in the required fields first.
2.  Move on to the optional fields.
3.  Fill in the fields that best match your preferences and schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, required): This is the section where you will provide your name, email, phone number, and other contact information. Please fill in your real name and contact details accurately.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required): This field is used to determine your work hours. Please enter your total work hours per day.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required): This field is used to determine your goals for our coaching sessions. Please describe your goals clearly and concisely.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, time, required): This field is used to determine your available days for coaching sessions. Please select your available days.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, select_one, required): This field is used to determine your work schedule. Please select your preferred schedule.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is your email address. Please fill in a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This is your phone number. Please fill in a valid phone number.
* **Available Times** (`available_times`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to determine your available times for coaching sessions. Please select your available times.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to determine your preferred contact method. Please select your preferred contact method.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is used to note any additional information about your coaching sessions. Please use this field for any notes or comments.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, email, required): This is the email address of your coach. Please do not fill in this field.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): This is your client email address. Please do not fill in this field.
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, text, required): This is the phone number of your coach. Please do not fill in this field.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): This is your client phone number. Please do not fill in this field.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, optional): This field is used to determine your preferred language. Please select your preferred language.
* **Client Time Zone** (`client_timezone`, select_one, optional): This field is used to determine your client time zone. Please select your client time zone.
* **Client Time Zone Offset** (`client_timezone_offset`, number, optional): This field is used to determine your client time zone offset. Please fill in this field if necessary.
* **Preferred Time Zone** (`preferred_timezone`, select_one, optional): This field is used to determine your preferred time zone. Please select your preferred time zone.
* **Preferred Time Zone Offset** (`preferred_timezone_offset`, number, optional): This field is used to determine your preferred time zone offset. Please fill in this field if necessary.
* **Note 2** (`note`, note, optional): This field is used to note any additional information about your coaching sessions. Please use this field for any notes or comments.
* **Note 3** (`note`, note, optional): This field is used to note any additional information about your coaching sessions. Please use this field for any notes or comments.
