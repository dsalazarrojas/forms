# University Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who wish to apply for university admission. It will help university officials track and manage applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To start, fill out the form as prompted. Each page will ask for different information to help you get accepted into the university you choose. You may need to go back to previous pages at any time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **University Admission Form**: This is the header of the form, it does not require a field value. It's just the title of the form.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name exactly as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address. This is where you will receive updates about your application status.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you if there's any urgent matter related to your admission.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **University Name**: You have two options for this field: 'Yes' or 'No'. Please select the name of the university you applied to. If you selected 'Yes' you don't need to enter more details below.
* **Program Name**: You have two options for this field: 'Yes' or 'No'. Please select the name of the university program you applied to. If you selected 'Yes' you don't need to enter more details below.
* **Application Status**: Select one from the given options: 'Pending', 'Approved', or 'Rejected'.
* **Note**: Please add any relevant information you feel is important for the admission officials to know about your application.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the application. 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check that the information you enter is accurate to avoid any confusion.
* You can go back and forth between pages at any time.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your application, please contact the relevant department or university official.
* This form is for internal use, please do not use it for other purposes.
