# nise_stim_course_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect data for course registration, including course details and student information. It ensures that all necessary information is recorded accurately to facilitate efficient management of courses and communication with students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Registration form data** section with any additional comments or remarks.
2. Enter the **course name** in the **Course name** field.
3. Select the **course type** from the options provided (Course or Workshop).
4. Add a brief description of the course in the **Course description** field.
5. Enter the date the student enrolled in the **Enrollment date** field.
6. Set the start and end times for the course in the **Start time** and **End time** fields, respectively.
7. Enter the location of the course in the **Course location** field.
8. Select the status of the course from the options provided (Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled).
9. Add any notes about the course in the **Notes** field.
10. Enter the student's email address in the **Email** field.
11. Enter the student's phone number in the **Phone** field.
12. Enter the name of the training provider in the **Provider name** field.
13. Enter the name of the training provider in the **Training provider** field.
14. Enter the name of the program coordinator in the **Program coordinator** field.
15. Enter the phone number of the program coordinator in the **Program coordinator phone** field.
16. Enter the email address of the program coordinator in the **Program coordinator email** field.
17. Set the date to follow up with the student in the **Follow-up date** field.
18. Set the time to follow up with the student in the **Follow-up time** field.
19. Select the status of the follow-up (Followed up or Not followed up).
20. Add any follow-up notes about the student in the **Follow-up notes** field.
21. Select the payment status of the student (Paid or Unpaid).
22. Enter the method of payment in the **Payment method** field.
23. Enter the student ID in the **Student ID** field.
24. Enter the student's name in the **Student name** field.
