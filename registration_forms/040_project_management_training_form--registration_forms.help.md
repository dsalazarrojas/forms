# Project Management Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information for project management training sessions. Fill out this form to get enrolled in a training session tailored to your needs, with follow-up actions and resources delivered to your email.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form:

1. Enter your participant information in the corresponding fields, such as your name, email, phone number, company, job title, and location.
2. If you have already participated in a training session, you may not need to fill out this form again.
3. Select one of the two available training session options if you wish to enroll in a session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your participant info, if applicable.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two available training session options to enroll in a session.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, which will be used for participant management and communication.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address to receive follow-up actions and resources.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for participant communication and follow-up.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name, which will be used for participant management and communication.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title, which will be used for participant management and communication.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, text, optional): Enter your location, which will be used for participant management and communication.
