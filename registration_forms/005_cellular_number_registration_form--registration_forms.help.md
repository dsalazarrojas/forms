# Cellular Number Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering a cellular number with a subscription service. It collects personal and identification details to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the subscriber's full name.
2. Enter the subscriber's email address.
3. Enter the primary contact number for follow-up.
4. Enter the cellular number you wish to register.
5. Choose the service type (prepaid or postpaid).
6. Select the type of SIM card (physical or eSIM).
7. Select the network provider (national, regional, or local).
8. Provide identification information, including the type of ID (national ID, passport, or driver's license) and the ID number.
9. Enter the date of registration.
10. Agree to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscriber Full Name**: Enter the subscriber's full name.
* **Subscriber Email**: Enter the subscriber's email address for confirmation and billing purposes.
* **Primary Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for follow-up regarding your registration.
* **Mobile Number to Register**: Enter the cellular number you wish to register.
* **Service Type**: Choose between prepaid and postpaid subscription models.
* **SIM Card Type**: Select between physical and eSIM card types.
* **Network Provider**: Select the network provider (national, regional, or local).
* **Identification Type**: Select the type of identification document (national ID, passport, or driver's license).
* **ID Number**: Enter the number on your identification document.
* **Date of Registration**: Enter the date of registration.
* **Do you agree to the Terms of Service**: Select "I Agree" to proceed with registration.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check the identification information for correct formatting and clarity.
* Read and agree to the terms of service before proceeding.
