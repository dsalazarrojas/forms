# Corporate Retreat Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants attending a corporate retreat. The form is intended to ensure that participants understand and acknowledge the risks associated with the retreat, and provide necessary medical and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your emergency contact.
3. Sign the Participant Declaration Agreement and Participant Risk Acknowledge Agreement forms to acknowledge that you have read and understood the risks and terms of the retreat.
