# Umpire Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Umpire Information Form is a template for collecting personal and contact information from users, which can be used as a starting point and modified according to specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the form to open it.
- Fill in your personal information on **page 1** (Personal Information).
- Fill in your contact information on **page 2** (Contact Information).
- In **page 3** and **page 4**, you can provide your availability and qualifications.
- In **page 5**, specify your umpire availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information (page 1)
* **Personal Information** (text, optional): This field is for providing any additional personal information that you think is relevant to your umpire status.
### Contact Information (page 2)
* **Contact Information** (text, optional): This field is for providing your contact information, such as phone number, email, or address.
### Availability (page 3)
* **Availability** (text, optional): This field is for specifying any availability information related to your umpire services.
### Qualifications (page 4)
* **Qualifications** (text, optional): This field is for listing any relevant qualifications or skills you have for umpire services.
### Umpire Availability (page 5)
* **Umpire Availability** (text, optional): This field is for specifying your availability to work as an umpire.
