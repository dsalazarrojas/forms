# Client Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Registration form is used to capture essential information about clients, including their personal and business details. This data helps us understand our clients' needs and tailor our services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Information section with your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. In the Business Information section, provide your company name and business type (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-profit, or Other).
3. In the Billing Address section, fill out the street address, city, state, ZIP or postal code, and country.
4. Answer the question in the Shipping Address section to determine whether the shipping address is the same as the billing address.
	* If you select 'True', the shipping address section will be the same as the billing address.
	* If you select 'False', fill in the shipping street address, city, and state.
5. Finally, add any special requirements or notes in the Special Requirements or Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (text, required)
	* Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 2. **Last Name** (text, required)
	* Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 3. **Email Address** (email, required)
	* Enter your valid email address that we can use for communication.
### 4. **Phone Number** (text, required)
	* Provide your phone number as it appears on your identification documents.
### 5. **Company Name** (text, required)
	* Enter your company's name as it appears on your business documents.
### 6. **Business Type** (select-one, required)
	* Select the type of business you operate (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-profit, or Other).
### 7. **Street Address** (text, required)
	* Enter the street address of your billing address.
### 8. **City** (text, required)
	* Enter the city of your billing address.
### 9. **State or Province** (text, required)
	* Enter the state or province of your billing address.
### 10. **ZIP or Postal Code** (text, required)
	* Enter the ZIP or postal code of your billing address.
### 11. **Country** (text, required)
	* Enter the country of your billing address.
### 12. **Shipping Address Same as Billing** (select-one, required)
	* Select 'True' if the shipping address is the same as the billing address; otherwise, select 'False'.
### 13. **Shipping Street Address** (text, optional)
	* Enter the shipping street address if 'False' is selected in the previous field.
### 14. **Shipping City** (text, optional)
	* Enter the shipping city if 'False' is selected in the previous field.
### 15. **Shipping State or Province** (text, optional)
	* Enter the shipping state or province if 'False' is selected in the previous field.
### 16. **Special Requirements or Notes** (text, optional)
	* Add any special requirements or notes you'd like to be considered.

This help guide should provide a clear and concise understanding of the Client Registration form's purpose and requirements. By following these steps, users can complete the form accurately and efficiently.
