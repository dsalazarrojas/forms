# Science Fair Participation Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help students and their guardians register for science fairs. It collects information about the student and their project, as well as contact details for parents and teachers. Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information.
2. Provide details about your project, including title, category, and description.
3. Enter details about the participant, including name, school, grade, and age.
4. Enter contact information for the participant's parent or guardian.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **Zip code**: Enter your zip code (numeric only).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Project title**: Enter a brief title for your project.
* **Project category**: Enter a category for your project (e.g., biology, physics, etc.).
* **Project description**: Describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Participant name**: Enter the name of the participant (you or someone else).
* **Participant school**: Enter the name of the participant's school.
* **Participant teacher**: Enter the name of the participant's teacher.
* **Participant grade**: Enter the grade level of the participant.
* **Participant age**: Enter the age of the participant.
* **Project advisor**: Enter the name of the project advisor (if applicable).
* **Student investigator**: Enter the name of the student investigator (if applicable).
* **Parent name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **School name**: Enter the name of the school.
* **Teacher name**: Enter the name of the teacher.
* **School department**: Enter the department of the school (if applicable).
* **Teacher phone**: Enter the phone number of the teacher.
* **Submit**: Review your submission and submit the form. You have two options to choose from:
	+ I am happy with this form submission.
	+ I need help with this form submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you need help with any of the fields, choose "I need help with this form submission" at the submit step.
