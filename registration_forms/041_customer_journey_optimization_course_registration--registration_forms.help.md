# Customer Journey Optimization Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register you for the Customer Journey Optimization Course, a learning experience tailored to help you improve your customer journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to receive important updates and information about the course.
3. Provide your Phone Number for communication and follow-up purposes.
4. Specify your Learning Goal(s) by selecting the desired options or typing in your own goal.
5. Select your Session Preferences for the course.
6. Provide your Participant Details for us to better understand your background and experience.
7. Specify your Participant Goals for the course.
8. List your Participant Preferences for the course.
9. Select a Session Date and Time that suits your schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and information about the course. 
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for communication and follow-up purposes.
- **Learning Goal** (`learning_goal`, text, required/optional): Specify your goal for taking the course (e.g., improve customer experience, increase sales, etc.).
- **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the session preferences that best fit your learning style (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, etc.).
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your background and experience related to the customer journey.
- **Participant Goals** (`participant_goals`, text, required/optional): Specify your goals for participating in the course.
- **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, text, required/optional): List your preferences for the course (e.g., pace, topics, format, etc.).
- **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for your session.
- **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for your session.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to get the most out of the course.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [your contact information].
