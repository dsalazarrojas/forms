# Childcare Health Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects essential health information about a child, including their medical history, allergies, medications, and any special needs or requirements. It helps childcare providers understand the child's health status, enabling them to provide safe and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form for each child attending childcare.
2. Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please consult with your child's doctor or healthcare provider.
4. Ensure that you sign and date the form to confirm that you have provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Date** (1, Intake Date, required): Date of health intake.
* **Child First Name** (2, Child First Name, required): Legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name** (3, Child Last Name, required): Legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth** (4, Child Date of Birth, required): Child's birth date.
* **Child Age** (5, Child Age, required): Current age of the child in years.
* **Child Gender** (6, Child Gender, required): Select the child's gender.
* **Parent/Guardian First Name** (7, Parent or Guardian First Name, required): Legal first name of parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Last Name** (8, Parent or Guardian Last Name, required): Legal last name of parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (9, Parent or Guardian Email, required): Primary email address for contact.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (10, Parent or Guardian Phone, required): Primary phone number for contact.
* **Primary Care Physician Name** (11, Primary Care Physician Name, required): Name of child's doctor.
* **Primary Care Physician Phone** (12, Primary Care Physician Phone, required): Phone number for doctor's office.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (13, Medical Insurance Provider, required): Name of insurance company.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (14, Insurance Policy Number, required): Policy or member number.
* **Immunization Status** (15, Immunization Status, required): Select the child's immunization status.
* **Immunization Record Provided** (16, Immunization Record Provided, required): Whether a copy of immunization records has been provided.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions** (17, Chronic Medical Conditions, required): List any chronic medical conditions.
* **Allergies** (18, Allergies, required): List all known allergies (food, medication, environmental, insect).
* **Allergy Severity** (19, Allergy Severity, required): Select the severity of allergic reactions.
* **EpiPen Required** (20, EpiPen Required, required): Whether an EpiPen is required.
* **Current Medications** (21, Current Medications, optional): List all current medications with dosages and schedules.
* **Medication Authorization** (22, Medication Authorization, required): Authorization to administer medications.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (23, Dietary Restrictions, required): List any dietary restrictions or special diets.
* **Feeding Instructions** (24, Feeding Instructions, optional): Any special feeding instructions.
* **Sleep Requirements** (25, Sleep Requirements, optional): Any special sleep requirements or nap schedules.
* **Developmental Concerns** (26, Developmental Concerns, optional): Any developmental concerns or delays.
* **Behavioral Concerns** (27, Behavioral Concerns, optional): Any behavioral concerns staff should know about.
* **Recent Hospitalizations** (28, Recent Hospitalizations, required): Whether the child has been hospitalized in the past year.
* **Hospitalization Details** (29, Hospitalization Details, optional): If yes, please provide details.
* **Additional Health Information** (30, Additional Health Information, optional): Any other health information staff should know.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (31, Parent or Guardian Signature, required): Electronic signature of the parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date** (32, Signature Date, required): Date of electronic signature.

## Tips
* Always answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any information, please consult with your child's doctor or healthcare provider.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
* This form should be completed for each child attending childcare.
