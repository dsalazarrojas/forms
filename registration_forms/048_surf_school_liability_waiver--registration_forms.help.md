# Surf School Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from participants who sign up for a surf school program. It includes fields for consent, emergency contact details, participant relationship, participant name, and date of birth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Read through each section carefully to understand what information is required.
2.  Fill out the fields as needed, taking into account the optional fields may not be necessary for all participants.
3.  Ensure you fill out all required fields to avoid any potential issues with your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant Consent**
 (`participant_consent`, `note`, not required): Please provide your consent to participate in the surf school program by checking the box indicating that you agree to the terms and conditions of the program. This will help us to understand your willingness to take part and to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

### **Emergency Contact**
 (`emergency_contact`, `text`, not required): In case of an emergency, please provide the contact information of a person or people who we can reach out to in case of an issue. This could be a family member, friend, or any other trusted individual.

### **Relationship to Participant**
 (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, not required): If you have filled out emergency contact information, please describe your relationship to the individual(s) you provided the contact information for.

### **Participant Name**
 (`participant_name`, `text`, not required): This field is a good opportunity to provide your name and confirm your registration.

### **Date of Birth**
 (`date_of_birth`, `date`, not required): If you want to provide your birth date for any reason, use the date format MM / DD / YYYY to ensure accuracy and clarity.

### **Tips**
If you have any trouble filling out this form or if you need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to the surf school staff. They are always happy to help.
