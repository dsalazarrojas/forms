# registration_update_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for updating existing user registration information. It is designed to gather the most recent updates from the user regarding their personal and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To update your registration information, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the required information in the designated fields.
3. Make sure you have filled all fields correctly before submitting the form.
4. Submit the form to update your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of who this update is for (e.g., employee number, name, etc.).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter contact information for the person or department this update is related to (e.g., name, email, phone).
* **Address Info** (`address_info`, text, required: false): Enter the updated address information for the user (e.g., work location, etc.).
* **Update Info** (`update_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional update information for this registration (e.g., new role, job function, etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments regarding this update.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the user you are updating (if you know it).
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the user.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the user.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the updated email address of the user.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the updated phone number of the user.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the updated address of the user.

## Tips
* Double-check all information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to include all necessary information in the correct fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or administrator for assistance.
