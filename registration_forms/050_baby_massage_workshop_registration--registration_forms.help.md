# Baby Massage Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Baby Massage Workshop Registration form is designed to collect information from parents/guardians and their babies for a baby massage workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Section 1: Participant Information**
	* Complete the parent/guardian's name: Enter the full name of the parent/guardian.
	* Complete the email address: Enter the email address of the parent/guardian.
	* Complete the phone number: Enter the phone number of the parent/guardian.
2. **Section 2: Baby Information**
	* Complete the baby's name: Enter the baby's full name.
	* Complete the baby's date of birth: Enter the baby's date of birth.
	* Complete the baby's age (in months): Enter the baby's age in months.
	* Complete the baby's gender: Select the baby's gender (Male, Female, or Prefer not to specify).
3. **Section 3: Workshop Details**
	* Select the workshop date: Choose the date of the workshop you are attending.
	* (Optional) Select the workshop time: Choose the time of the workshop if you are concerned about arrival and departure times.
	* (Optional) Select the workshop duration: If you have any specific requests for workshop duration, please specify.
4. **Section 4: Experience and Preferences**
	* Select prior baby massage experience: Choose whether you have experience with baby massage, some experience, or are a beginner.
	* Specify learning goals: What do you hope to learn from the workshop?
	* Specify baby health concerns: If you have any concerns, please share them with us.
	* Specify skin sensitivities or allergies: If your baby has any skin sensitivities or allergies, please let us know.
5. **Section 5: Workshop Logistics**
	* Select the number of attendees: If you are attending with others, please specify how many will be attending.
	* Specify if other children will be attending: Let us know if other children will be attending the workshop.
	* Specify accessibility needs: If you or your baby have any mobility, hearing, or other needs, please let us know.
6. **Section 6: Payment Information**
	* Pay the registration fee: Enter the registration fee for the workshop.
	* Select a payment method: Choose how you would like to make payment (Credit Card, PayPal, Cash, or Check).
7. **Section 7: Consent and Agreements**
	* Confirm baby's health: Confirm that your baby is healthy and able to participate.
	* Confirm waiver and terms: Accept the liability waiver and terms of the workshop.
	* Specify photo consent: Choose whether you are willing to be photographed during the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Your full name as the parent/guardian.
* **Email Address**: Your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Baby's Name**: The full name of your baby.
* **Baby's Date of Birth**: The date of birth of your baby.
* **Baby's Age (in months)**: The age of your baby in months.
* **Baby's Gender**: Select the correct gender for your baby (Male, Female, or Prefer not to specify).
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date of the workshop you are attending.
* **Workshop Time**: Optional, but please select the time you will arrive and depart from the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration**: Optional, but please specify the duration you prefer for the workshop.
* **Prior Baby Massage Experience**: Select whether you have experience with baby massage, some experience, or are a beginner.
* **Learning Goals**: Specify what you hope to learn from the workshop.
* **Baby Health Concerns**: If you have any concerns, please share them with us.
* **Skin Sensitivities or Allergies**: If your baby has any skin sensitivities or allergies, please let us know.
* **Number of Attendees**: Enter the number of people attending with you, including yourself and your baby.
* **Will Other Children Be Attending?**: Yes or No.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you or your baby have any mobility, hearing, or other needs, please specify.
* **Registration Fee**: Enter the payment for the workshop.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to make payment (Credit Card, PayPal, Cash, or Check).
* **I Agree to Liability Waiver and Terms**: Confirm that you accept the liability waiver and terms of the workshop.
* **Permission to Take Photos**: Choose whether you are willing to be photographed during the workshop.
* **Additional Notes or Questions**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please share them with us.
