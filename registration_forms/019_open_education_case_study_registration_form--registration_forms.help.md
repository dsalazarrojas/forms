# Open Education Case Study Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Education Case Study Registration Form is designed to gather information about participants in an Open Education Case Study. This form helps administrators or educators collect relevant details about students or learners who are participating in the case study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Participant Name**, which is the name of the participant.
2. Enter the **Email** of the participant.
3. Choose the **Organization** where the participant works or studies.
4. Enter the participant's **Job Title**.
5. Select the **Role** of the participant in the case study (if not required).
6. (Optional) Enter the **Contact Number** and **Address** of the participant.
7. Choose the **Participant Type** of the participant (e.g., student, teacher, etc.).
8. Provide any additional **Comments** about the participant.
9. Enter the **Case Study ID**, which is a unique identifier for the case study.
10. Choose the **Assigned Tool** used in the case study.
11. Enter the **Submitted By**, which should be the name of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the participant.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: Yes
* **Organization**: Select the organization where the participant works or studies.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the participant.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Role**: Select the role of the participant in the case study (if not required).
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the participant (if required).
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Address**: Enter the address of the participant (if required).
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Participant Type**: Choose the type of participant (e.g., student, teacher, etc.).
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the participant.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: No
* **Case Study ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the case study.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool assigned to the participant.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes

Note: This form is for individual participant registration, and all fields marked as required must be filled out for the form to be complete.
