# yoni_therapys_vaginal_steam_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The yoni_therapys_vaginal_steam_intake_form is designed to collect medical history and treatment details for patients undergoing vaginal steam treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Patient Name if required.
2. Enter your Email address if required.
3. Provide your Date Of Birth.
4. Share your Medical History.
5. Describe the Steam Treatment Results.
6. Enter the Medical Procedure Date.
7. Enter the Medical Procedure Time.
8. Add Medical Procedure Notes.
9. Schedule your Next Visit.
10. Specify the Steam Treatment Frequency.
11. Select the Medical Procedure Type (Natural Steam or Steam).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Share any relevant medical history, including medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.
- **Steam Treatment Results** (`steam_treatment_results`, text, required/optional): Describe any results or progress you've experienced after the treatment.
- **Medical Procedure Date** (`medical_procedure_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the medical procedure.
- **Medical Procedure Time** (`medical_procedure_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the medical procedure.
- **Medical Procedure Notes** (`medical_procedure_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the medical procedure.
- **Next Visit** (`next_visit`, date, required/optional): Schedule your next visit for further treatment.
- **Steam Treatment Frequency** (`steam_treatment_frequency`, text, required/optional): Specify how often you'd like to have steam treatments.
- **Medical Procedure Type** (`medical_procedure_type`, select_one, required): Select either Natural Steam or Steam as the medical procedure type.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of your input.
* Ask your healthcare professional if you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields.
