# Stand In And Double Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a production or service for temporary takeover by another person or entity. It captures essential details for a seamless handover and handback process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Production Name field with the name of the production or service being taken over.
2. Enter the Contact Person field with the name of the person responsible for the takeover.
3. Enter the Email and Phone fields with the contact information for the Contact Person.
4. Provide an Address (if applicable) and Double Details for the production or service being taken over.
5. Enter the Double Name and Double Phone fields with the name and phone number of the person taking over the production or service.
6. Enter the Double Email field with the email address of the person taking over the production or service.
7. Provide Production Details for the production or service being taken over.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Production Name** (`production_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the production or service being taken over.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the takeover.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the takeover.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the takeover.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the production or service being taken over (if applicable).
* **Double Details** (`double_details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about the production or service being taken over.
* **Double Name** (`double_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person taking over the production or service.
* **Double Phone** (`double_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person taking over the production or service.
* **Double Email** (`double_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person taking over the production or service.
* **Production Details** (`production_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional details about the production or service being taken over.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure smooth handover and handback processes.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your production manager or team lead for clarification.
- If you've already registered this production and double name combination, the system will prompt you to confirm if you want to update the existing registration or create a new one.
