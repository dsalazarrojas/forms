# Virtual Recruitment Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Recruitment Fair Registration Form is designed to collect information from job seekers about their job title, job type, work arrangement, and other relevant details. This form will be used to manage registrations for the fair and provide information to participating employers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address.
3. Enter your **Contact Number**.
4. Select your **Job Title** (if it is not listed, please enter your **Other Job Title**).
5. Select your **Job Type** (if it is not listed, please enter your **Other Job Type**).
6. Select your **Job Status** (if it is not listed, please enter your **Other Job Type**).
7. Select your desired **Work Arrangement** (office, remote, hybrid, or flex).
8. If applicable, upload your **Resume** and **Cover Letter**.
9. If necessary, enter your **Job Start Date** and **Job End Date**.
10. Review the **Terms and Conditions** and **Privacy Policy**.
11. Click the **Submit** button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
  * Type: text.
  * Required: false.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
  * Type: text.
  * Required: false.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
  * Type: email.
  * Required: false.
- **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
  * Type: text.
  * Required: false.
- **Job Title**: Select your job title from the list (if it is not listed, enter your **Other Job Title**).
  * Type: select_one.
  * Required: true.
- **Other Job Title**: Enter your job title if it is not listed.
  * Type: text.
  * Required: false.
- **Job Type**: Select your job type from the list (if it is not listed, enter your **Other Job Type**).
  * Type: select_multiple.
  * Required: true.
- **Job Status**: Select your job status from the list (if it is not listed, enter your **Other Job Type**).
  * Type: select_multiple.

  * Type: select_multiple.
  * Required: true.
- **Job Start Date**: Enter your job start date (if applicable).
  * Type: date.
  * Required: false.
- **Job End Date**: Enter your job end date (if applicable).
  * Type: date.
  * Required: false.
- **Other Job Type**: Enter your job type if it is not listed.
  * Type: text.
  * Required: false.
- **Resume**: Upload your resume.
  * Type: text.
  * Required: false.
- **Resume URL**: Enter the URL of your resume.
  * Type: text.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
- This form is for registration purposes only, and information will be used to provide you with relevant job listings and opportunities.
