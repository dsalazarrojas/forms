# expo-media-partner-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for Expo Media Partners to register for the event, providing necessary information for coverage and partnership opportunities. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Outlet Name**: Enter the name of your media outlet (e.g., newspaper, magazine, blog).
* **Media Contact Info**: Enter the contact information for your media outlet (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Coverage Plan**: Select how you plan to cover the event (e.g., Press Pass, Interview, Press Conference, Booth).
* **Coverage Areas**: Select the areas of the event you plan to cover (e.g., Main Stage, Exhibition Halls).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your coverage (e.g., the event's start date).
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your coverage (e.g., the event's end date).
* **Coverage Hours**: Enter the hours you plan to cover (e.g., 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM).
* **Credential Needed**: Check if you need any credentials for coverage (e.g., press pass).
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your coverage plans.
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of your contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your contact person.
* **Social Media Links**: Enter any relevant social media links for your media outlet.

## Tips
* Please double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact info].
