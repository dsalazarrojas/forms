# grace_transformation_challenge_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The grace_transformation_challenge_application form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in the Grace Transformation Challenge. This form helps us understand your personal goals, motivations, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide accurate and honest information about yourself and your emergency contact.
3. Make sure to review your submission before submitting it for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **emergency_contact_person**: Enter the name and phone number of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
* **member_name**: Type your name.
* **member_email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **member_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **member_goal**: Describe your fitness transformation goal.
* **member_mission**: Explain what motivates you to want to be fit and healthy.
* **member_height**: Enter your height in centimeters (e.g., 175 cm).
* **member_weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms (e.g., 60 kg).
* **member_bday**: Enter your birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **member_goal_date**: Enter your goal date in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/2024).
* **member_goal_weight**: Enter your goal weight in kilograms (e.g., 50 kg).
* **member_notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your goals and motivations.
* **member_address**: Enter your home address.
* **member_city**: Enter your city.
* **member_state**: Enter your state or province.
* **member_postal_code**: Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **member_phone_type**: Check 'Yes' if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **emergency_contact_name**: Enter the name of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_bday**: Enter the emergency contact's birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **emergency_contact_phone_type**: Check 'Yes' if the emergency contact has a phone.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your submission for accuracy before submitting it.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help.
- This form is designed to be completed in one session, so take your time and be thorough.
