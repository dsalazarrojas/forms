# Power Yoga Masterclass Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Power Yoga Masterclass Registration Form" is a form designed to collect participant and masterclass information for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1.  Enter the participant's form data on the first page.
2.  Fill in the masterclass details on the second page.
3.  Add any additional notes or comments on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Form Data** (`participant_form_data`, text, not required)
    *   This is the first page of the form where you can enter the participant's name, email address, and other relevant details.
*   **Masterclass Data** (`masterclass_data`, text, not required)
    *   This page is used to fill out details about the masterclass, such as the class type, location, and dates.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, not required)
    *   This is a text area where you can add any additional comments or notes about the participant or the masterclass.
