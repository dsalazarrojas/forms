# Product Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a crucial step in the product registration process, allowing us to collect and store valuable information about your product and yourself. Providing accurate and complete information will help us to serve you better and ensure a smooth experience with our products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required details about your product and yourself.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Title** (`product_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief and descriptive title of your product.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required): Describe your product in a few words, so we can better understand it.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, required/optional): Upload a relevant image of your product, if possible.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required): Enter the price of your product.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name to help us identify you as our customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your product.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to facilitate communication.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter your address to enable efficient delivery or support.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, required): Enter your state or region to help us better serve you.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, number, required): Enter your zip or postal code for efficient delivery or support.

## Tips

* Always enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth process.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your cooperation in helping us serve you better.
