# Trail Life Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trail Life Registration form is designed to collect information from users interested in registering with us. This form is used to gather details about the user's identity, employment, and work history to ensure accurate and efficient registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Check the "Are you interested in Trail Life Leadership?" field and select "yes" if you are interested.
4. Enter your contact information, employment details, and work history as prompted.
5. Review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`trail_life_registration_page_1`, text): Enter a brief description of your registration.
* **Name** (`trail_life_registration_name`, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`trail_life_registration_email`, email): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`trail_life_registration_phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Number of Youth** (`trail_life_registration_youth_count`, number): Enter the number of youths you are registering.
* **Age of Youths** (`trail_life_registration_youth_ages`, text): Enter the ages of the youths you are registering.
* **Are you interested in Trail Life Leadership?** (`trail_life_registration_leadership`, select_one): Select "yes" if you are interested in Trail Life Leadership.
* **Additional Information** (`trail_life_registration_additional_info`, text): Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Notes** (`trail_life_registration_notes`, note): Enter any notes or comments about your registration.
* **Street Address** (`trail_life_registration_address`, text): Enter your street address for contact purposes.
* **City** (`trail_life_registration_city`, text): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`trail_life_registration_state`, text): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`trail_life_registration_zip`, text): Enter your postal code.
* **Agency/Group** (`trail_life_registration_agency`, text): Enter the name of your agency or group.
* **Employer** (`trail_life_registration_employer`, text): Enter your current employer's name.
* **Start Date** (`trail_life_registration_start_date`, date): Enter the start date of your employment.
* **End Date** (`trail_life_registration_end_date`, date): Enter the end date of your employment.
* **Hours Worked** (`trail_life_registration_hours`, time): Enter the hours you have worked for your employer.
* **Wages** (`trail_life_registration_wages`, number): Enter your current wages.
* **Employment Length** (`trail_life_registration_employment_length`, text): Enter the length of your employment.
* **Worksite** (`trail_life_registration_worksite`, text): Enter the name of your worksite.
* **Position** (`trail_life_registration_position`, text): Enter your current position.
* **Occupation** (`trail_life_registration_occupation`, text): Enter your current occupation.
* **Worked For** (`trail_life_registration_worked_for`, select_one): Select the organization you currently work for.
* **Comments** (`trail_life_registration_comments`, text): Enter any comments or additional information you would like to provide.

Please submit your completed form to complete your registration process.
