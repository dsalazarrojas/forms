# Student ID Card Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student ID Card Registration Form is used to collect information from students to register for a student ID card. This form is intended to gather necessary details such as name, contact information, photo, and student ID card details for the student ID card issuance process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Upload clear and high-quality photos of the student ID card front and back sides.
3. Upload a copy of a valid government-issued ID.
4. Select the type of student ID card from the options provided.
5. Provide the student ID number and expiration date if applicable.
6. Upload the student's signature, if required.
7. Review and submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address used for communication.
* **Student phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Student address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Student photo** (`student_photo`, text, required): Upload a clear and high-quality photo of yourself.
* **Photo Front** (`photo_front`, text, required): Upload a clear and high-quality photo of your student ID card front side.
* **Photo Back** (`photo_back`, text, required): Upload a clear and high-quality photo of your student ID card back side.
* **Student document** (`student_document`, text, required): Upload a copy of a valid government-issued ID.
* **Student signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Upload a clear and high-quality photo of your signature.
* **Student ID number** (`student_id_number`, text, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student ID type** (`student_id_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of student ID card, such as Student ID, National ID, Driver's license, or Passport.
* **Student ID expiration** (`student_id_expiration`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of your student ID.
* **Student ID issued date** (`student_id_issued_date`, date, required): Enter the date your student ID was issued.
* **Student ID card** (none, text, required): This field is for internal use only, and its value will be filled out automatically.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Use high-quality and clear photos to avoid errors in processing.
* Review the form before submission for any errors or issues. 
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
