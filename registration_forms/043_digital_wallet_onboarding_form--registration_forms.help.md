# Digital Wallet Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and contact information for the purpose of opening a digital wallet account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Use the correct format for date and phone numbers (MM/DD/YYYY for date and (XXX) XXX-XXXX for phone number).
3. Choose the correct account type and nationality from the select options.
4. Select the correct country from the list.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions by checking the "accept" field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Account Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of account you want to open (Savings, Current, Credit, None).
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Nationality** (select_one, optional): Choose your nationality (Yes, No).
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (select_one, optional): Choose your state (Yes, No).
* **Zip** (number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (select_one, optional): Choose your country (Yes, No).
* **Acceptance** (time, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
