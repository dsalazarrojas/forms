# Beauty Blogger Collaboration Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Beauty Blogger Collaboration Registration form is designed to gather information from beauty bloggers interested in collaborating with your brand. By filling out this form, you'll be able to showcase your blog or channel's presence, content focus, and collaboration preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the fields.
3. If you have any previous collaborations, feel free to list them in the "Previous Brand Collaborations" field.
4. Make sure to specify your rate card availability and share a link to it if you have one.
5. Finally, write a brief explanation of why you want to collaborate with our brand in the "Why Do You Want to Collaborate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Blogger Name**: The name of your blog or channel.
* **Real Name**: Your legal name, as per your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Your business email address, where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Blog or Channel Name**: The name of your blog or channel.
* **Website URL**: The URL of your website.
* **Instagram Handle**: Your Instagram handle.
* **Instagram Followers**: Approximate number of followers on Instagram.
* **YouTube Channel**: Your YouTube handle, if applicable.
* **YouTube Subscribers**: Approximate number of subscribers on YouTube.
* **TikTok Handle**: Your TikTok handle, if applicable.
* **TikTok Followers**: Approximate number of followers on TikTok.
* **Content Focus**: Select the types of content you focus on (e.g., Makeup Tutorials, Skincare Reviews, etc.).
* **Primary Audience**: Select the age group of your followers.
* **Collaboration Type Interested In**: Select the types of collaborations you're interested in (e.g., Product Reviews, Sponsored Posts, etc.).
* **Previous Brand Collaborations**: List any previous brand collaborations you've done.
* **Media Kit Available**: Specify if you have a media kit available and if it's online or not.
* **Media Kit Link**: Share a link to your media kit, if applicable.
* **Rate Card Available**: Specify if you have a rate card available.
* **Why Do You Want to Collaborate**: Write a brief explanation of why you want to collaborate with our brand.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional information you'd like to add.
