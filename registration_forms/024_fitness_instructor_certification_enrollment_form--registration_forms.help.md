# Fitness Instructor Certification Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help fitness instructors enroll for certification. The form collects information about the instructor, their training organization, and their certification status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), with your accurate information.
2. Make sure to choose the correct options for fields like certification level, certification type, and certification status.
3. Enter your certification date and expiration date if applicable.
4. Check the "User Agreement" field to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Training Organization Name** (`training_organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the training organization where you received your certification.
* **Training Organization Address** (`training_organization_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the training organization.
* **Training Program Name** (`training_program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the training program you completed.
* **Certification Level** (`certification_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of your certification (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
* **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of your certification (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3).
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your certification.
* **Certification Number** (`certification_number`, text, required): Enter the number of your certification.
* **Instructor ID** (`instructor_id`, text, required): Enter your instructor ID.
* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your certification (Active, Pending, Inactive).
* **Certification Expiration Date** (`certification_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of your certification.
* **Training Program ID** (`training_program_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the training program you completed.
* **Trainer ID** (`trainer_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the trainer who certified you.
* **Certification Level Details** (`certification_level_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about your certification level.
* **Training Organization Website** (`training_organization_website`, text, required): Enter the website of the training organization.
* **Training Organization Email** (`training_organization_email`, text, required): Enter the email of the training organization.
* **Training Organization Phone** (`training_organization_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the training organization.
* **User Agreement** (`user_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Check the box to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Review your information and submit the form.
* **Back** (`back_button`, text, optional): Click to go back to the previous page.
* **Cancel** (`cancel_button`, text, optional): Click to cancel the form submission.
