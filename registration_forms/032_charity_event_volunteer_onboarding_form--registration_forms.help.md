# Charity Event Volunteer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for the charity event. It will help us understand your availability, skills, and experience, which will be used to place you in the most suitable position during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address in the respective fields.
3. Enter your contact number in the Phone Number field.
4. Fill in your Date of Birth for age verification.
5. Provide your Mailing Address for communication purposes.
6. Enter the name and contact number of your Emergency Contact.
7. Select your availability for the event from the list of options.
8. Select your preferred roles from the list of options.
9. If you have any prior volunteer experience, describe it in the "Prior Volunteer Experience" field.
10. Select any relevant skills you bring to the event.
11. Choose your T-Shirt Size.
12. If you have any dietary restrictions, select them from the list of options.
13. Consent to a background check, if required.
14. Accept the liability waiver.
15. Let us know how you heard about us.
16. Write a brief statement explaining why you want to volunteer.
17. Add any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full address for communication purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Event of Interest** (`event_interest`, `text`, required): Select the event you are interested in.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select when you can help during the event from the list of options.
* **Role Preferences** (`role_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your preferred roles from the list of options.
* **Prior Volunteer Experience** (`prior_experience`, `text`, optional): If you have any prior volunteer experience, describe it in this field.
* **Relevant Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any relevant skills you bring to the event from the list of options.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose your T-Shirt size.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, select them from the list of options.
* **Consent to Background Check** (`background_check`, `select_one`, required): If required, consent to a background check.
* **Accept Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Accept the liability waiver.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know how you heard about us.
* **Why Do You Want to Volunteer** (`motivation`, `text`, required): Write a brief statement explaining why you want to volunteer.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments you may have.
