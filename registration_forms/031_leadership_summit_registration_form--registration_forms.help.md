# Leadership Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for a leadership summit. It gathers information about the participants, including their contact details, company, job title, and notes. The form is intended to be completed by individuals who wish to attend the leadership summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review and edit your input before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the participant_email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of yourself or your company.
* **Participant Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, not required): Enter your company name.
* **Participant Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your job title.
* **Participant Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who will be your contact point.
* **Participant Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you registered for the summit.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes about yourself or your company.
* **Participant Company Name** (`company_name`, text, not required): Enter your company name.
* **Participant Department** (`department`, text, not required): Enter your department name.
* **Participant Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, not required): Enter your job title 2.

# Tips
