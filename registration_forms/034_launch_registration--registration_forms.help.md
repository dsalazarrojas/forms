# Launch Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The launch registration form is used to collect information about the launch team and details about the launch event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the launch details, such as date and time, notes, and estimates.
3. Select the options for the launch team members and other relevant information.
