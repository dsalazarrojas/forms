# Odissi Dance Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register for a dance event or workshop. Please fill in your personal and workshop preferences to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification document.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification document.
	* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Age**: Enter your age.
	* **Level**: Select your current dance level (Bás or æk).
2. Choose your workshop:
	* **Workshop**: Select one or multiple workshops you'd like to attend.
	* **Payment**: Select your preferred payment method.
	* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method (method 1, 2, or 3).
3. Confirm your workshop schedule:
	* **Class Date**: Enter the date of your preferred class.
	* **Class Time**: Enter the time of your preferred class.
	* **Class Location**: Enter the location of your preferred class.
4. Review and add notes (optional):
	* **Instructor Note**: Add any notes for the instructor.
	* **Class Note**: Add any notes for the class.
	* **Workshop Note**: Add any notes for the workshop.
	* **Payment Note**: Add any notes for payment.
	* **Payment Method Note**: Add any notes for payment method.
	* **Submission Note**: Add any notes for submission.
5. Submit your registration:
	* Ensure all required fields are filled.
	* Review your selection and notes before submission.
