# Resort Visitor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect visitor information for visitor management and communication purposes at the resort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **user name** in the "user name" field.
2. Enter your **email address** in the "email" field.
3. Provide your **phone number** in the "phone" field.
4. Enter the **date of arrival** and **date of departure** in their respective fields.
5. Select the **purpose of visit** from the available options (Leisure or Business).
6. If applicable, enter your **room number** in the "room number" field.
7. If necessary, provide any **special requirements** in the "special requirements" field.
8. Enter your **arrival time** and **departure time** in their respective fields.
9. Indicate if there is a **guest type** by selecting "Yes" or "No".
10. You can also include any additional **notes** if necessary.
11. Select the **assigned user** who will be handling your visit (chatjimmy or Other).
12. If required, confirm that you have the necessary **assigned tool** for your visit (Yes or No).
13. Finally, provide any additional **special requirements 1-10** as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of arrival**: Enter the date you plan to arrive at the resort.
* **Date of departure**: Enter the date you plan to leave the resort.
* **Purpose of visit**: Select the reason for your visit (Leisure or Business).
* **Room number**: If you have a room reservation, enter the room number here.
* **Special requirements**: If you have any special requirements or requests, enter them here.
* **Arrival time**: Enter the time you plan to arrive at the resort.
* **Departure time**: Enter the time you plan to leave the resort.
* **Guest type**: Indicate if you will be bringing guests with you.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with the resort staff.
* **Assigned user**: Select the resort staff member who will be handling your visit (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Assigned tool**: Confirm if you have the necessary tool for your visit (Yes or No).
* **Special requirements 1-10**: Enter any additional special requirements you may have.
