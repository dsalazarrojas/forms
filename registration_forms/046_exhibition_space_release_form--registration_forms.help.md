# exhibition_space_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from exhibitors for exhibition space releases. It is intended to be used by exhibitors to share their details, booth requests, and other contact information. Please use this form for its intended purpose only.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your exhibitor info, including any additional notes if necessary.
2. Select the booth request that best suits your needs from the dropdown list.
3. Add your email address and phone number (if applicable) for contact purposes.
4. Double-check your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant exhibitor details.
* **Booth Info**: Add your booth information, such as your booth number and setup details.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact details, including phone and email (if provided).
* **Booth Requests**: Choose one request that best suits your needs from the dropdown list:
	+ Request 1
	+ Request 2
	+ Request 3
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments that might be necessary for your exhibition space release.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your completed form.
