# ATC Client Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The ATC Client Registration form is designed to collect information about you as a client to facilitate the registration process for our services. This includes your personal and professional details, as well as your preferences for events and accommodations. Your input will help us tailor our services to meet your needs and provide you with a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These include:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
	* Industry
	* Company Size (if applicable)
	* Which After the Close events are you interested in
	* Do you have any dietary restrictions or allergies
	* I agree to the terms and conditions of participation
2. For fields not marked with an asterisk, you may choose to provide information, but it is optional.
3. Select your industry sector from the drop-down menu.
4. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please specify them in the details field.
5. For accessibility needs, please choose "True" if you require special accommodations.
6. If you agree to receive marketing communications, select "True".
7. Please confirm your agreement with our policies by selecting "True".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (*): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (*): Enter your last name.
* **Company Name** (optional): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Job Title** (optional): Enter your professional title.
* **Email Address** (*): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (*): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Industry** (*): Select your industry sector from the drop-down menu.
* **Company Size** (optional): Select the approximate number of employees in your company.
* **Which After the Close events are you interested in** (*): Select all that apply.
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions or allergies** (optional): Choose "True" if you have dietary restrictions.
* **If yes, please specify your dietary restrictions** (optional): Enter details about your dietary needs.
* **Do you have any accessibility needs we should accommodate** (optional): Choose "True" if you require special accommodations.
* **If yes, please specify your accessibility needs** (optional): Enter details about your required accommodations.
* **May we send you information about future events and services** (optional): Choose "True" if you agree to receive marketing communications.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions of participation** (*): Confirm your agreement with our policies by selecting "True".
