# Climbing Competition Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for registering for a climbing competition. To ensure that you are eligible to register, please check if you have all required fields completed and if the information you provide is accurate. If you have any doubts or questions, please feel free to reach out to us.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the correct options for each field as prompted.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Gender** (Gender, select one, optional): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Gym/Organization** (Gym/Organization, text, optional): If you are affiliated with a gym or organization, please enter it here.
* **Competition Name** (Competition Name, text, required): Enter the name of the competition you are registering for.
* **Competition Date** (Competition Date, date, required): Enter the date of the competition.
* **Type of Competition** (Type of Competition, select one, required): Select the type of competition (Bouldering, Speed climbing, Sport/Lead climbing, or Mixed).
* **Age Division** (Age Division, select one, required): Select your age division (Youth (under 18), Junior (18-35), Senior (35-49), or Master (50+)).
* **Skill Division** (Skill Division, select one, required): Select your skill division (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Elite, or Professional).
* **Years of climbing experience** (Years of climbing experience, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been climbing.
* **Previous competitions attended** (Previous competitions attended, number, optional): Enter the number of previous competitions you have attended.
* **Best previous competition result** (Best previous competition result, text, optional): If you have participated in competitions before, describe your best result.
* **Any current injuries or medical conditions?** (Any current injuries or medical conditions?, text, optional): If you have any current injuries or medical conditions, please describe them.
* **I acknowledge and accept the competition rules and liability waiver** (I acknowledge and accept the competition rules and liability waiver, select one, required): Select "Yes" to acknowledge and accept the competition rules and liability waiver.
* **Parent/Guardian consent (for minors)** (Parent/Guardian consent (for minors), select one, required): Select "Yes" if you are a minor, "No" if you are 18 or older, or "Not applicable" if you are an adult.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

Please note that all fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields and must be completed in order to register for the competition.
