# Community Housing Project Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for community housing projects to register and provide essential information about their projects. The form is required for all projects seeking support, funding, or recognition from the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your housing project in the "Name of Housing Project" field.
2. Select your role in the project from the "Registration Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the primary contact person's name and email address in the "Primary Contact Person" field.
4. If applicable, enter the business or organization name in the "Business or Organization Name" field.
5. Enter the contact email address in the "Contact Email Address" field.
6. Provide the project location or proposed area in the "Project Location or Proposed Area" field.
7. Choose the estimated project timeline from the "Estimated Project Timeline" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the anticipated number of housing units in the "Anticipated Number of Housing Units" field.
9. Select the current funding status of the project from the "Current Funding Status" dropdown menu.
10. Choose all that apply from the "Sustainability and Green Features" options.
11. Enter a brief description of the project's goals in the "Brief Description of Project Goals" field.
12. Enter the date of registration in the "Date of Registration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name of Housing Project**: Enter a brief title for your project, e.g., "Green Village Community Housing Project".
* **Registration Type**: Select your role in the project from the following options:
	+ Individual Developer
	+ Non-Profit Organization
	+ Government Agency
	+ Private Construction Firm
	+ Community Land Trust
* **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your project.
* **Business or Organization Name**: If your project is a business or organization, enter the name here. Otherwise, leave blank.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Project Location or Proposed Area**: Enter the address or neighborhood of the project location.
* **Estimated Project Timeline**: Choose the estimated project timeline from the following options:
	+ Under 6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 12-24 months
	+ More than 2 years
* **Anticipated Number of Housing Units**: Enter the number of housing units planned for your project.
* **Current Funding Status**: Select the current funding status of your project from the following options:
	+ Fully Funded
	+ Seeking Public Grants
	+ Private Financing Pending
	+ Public-Private Partnership
	+ Crowd-funded or Local
* **Sustainability and Green Features**: Select all the sustainability and green features that apply to your project:
	+ Solar Power Systems
	+ Water Recycling/Graywater
	+ Energy Efficient Appliances
	+ Low-Impact Building Materials
	+ Proximity to Public Transit
* **Brief Description of Project Goals**: Enter a brief description of the project's goals.
* **Date of Registration**: Enter the date you are submitting your project registration.

**Tips**

* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the project administrator for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
