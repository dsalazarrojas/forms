# Accessibility In Care Design Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us prepare an inclusive environment for all workshop participants. Please complete it carefully to ensure your comfort and engagement in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for all required fields.
- For "Professional Role or Title", enter your current role or position in the care sector.
- In "Dietary Needs" section, mention any food allergies or dietary preferences that we need to consider for in-person attendees.
- In the "Session Selection" section, choose the workshops you are interested in attending.
- In "Attendance Mode", select how you plan to participate (in-person, virtual, or on-demand).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: A required field where you should enter your name as it should appear on your certificate.
* **Professional Role or Title**: A required field where you should enter your current role or position in the care sector.
* **Email Address**: A required field for workshop link and materials. Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Select the sessions you wish to attend**: Choose the workshops that interest you the most from the available options.
* **Attendance Mode**: Select how you plan to participate (in-person, virtual, or on-demand).
* **Accessibility Requirements**: If you have any specific needs for accessibility, such as closed captioning or sign language interpretation, please select them for our consideration.
* **Dietary Needs for In-Person Attendees**: If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please mention them so we can accommodate you.
* **Size of your organization**: Indicate the size of your organization for context in the workshops.
* **Commit to attending the selected sessions**: Confirm that you commit to attending the workshops you selected.
* **Any other information you would like to share**: If you have any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us, please include them here.
