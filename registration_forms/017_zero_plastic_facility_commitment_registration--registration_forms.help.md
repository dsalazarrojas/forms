# Zero Plastic Facility Commitment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zero Plastic Facility Commitment Registration form is designed to gather information from facilities that are committed to reducing or eliminating plastic use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are a facility representative or authorized to commit on behalf of a facility.
2. Select the correct options for the fields that are relevant to your facility.
3. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) and provide any additional information in the optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of your facility, including its name and address.
* **Zero Plastic Initiatives (2)**: Select all the zero plastic initiatives that your facility is committed to or willing to commit to. This can include practices like using biodegradable packaging, reducing single-use plastics, and eliminating microplastics.
* **Zero Plastic Commitment (3)**: Indicate whether your facility is committed to zero plastic practices. Select "True" if you are committed, and "False" if you are not.
* **Sustainability Program (4)**: Choose whether your facility has an existing sustainability program or is starting a new one.
* **Authorized Commitment (5)**: Enter the number of authorized individuals who can confirm your facility's commitment to zero plastic practices.
* **Facility Address (6)**: Enter the physical address of your facility.
* **Facility Contact (7)**: Provide the name and contact information of the facility contact person.
* **Facility Email (8)**: Enter the email address of your facility.
* **Facility Phone (9)**: Enter the phone number of your facility.
* **Facility Website (10)**: Enter the website of your facility.
* **Sustainability Programs (11)**: Select all the sustainability programs that your facility is committed to or is willing to commit to. This can include carbon footprint analysis, energy efficiency, water conservation, supply chain management, and waste reduction.
* **Additional Info (12)**: Provide any additional information about your facility's commitment to zero plastic practices.
* **Facility Type (13)**: Enter a brief description of your facility type (e.g., manufacturing, retail, restaurant, etc.).
