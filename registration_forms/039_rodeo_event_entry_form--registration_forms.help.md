# Rodeo Event Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for riders and their horses participating in a rodeo event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required for the form.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by a *).
3. Select the applicable options for optional fields.
4. Review your completed form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for correspondence and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Event Id** (`event_id`, select_one, optional): Select the rodeo event you wish to participate in.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you wish to register for the event.
* **Rider Level** (`rider_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select your riding level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Horse Id** (`horse_id`, select_multiple, optional): Select the horse you will be riding (Yes, No).
* **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, required): Enter the time you wish to register for the event.
* **Rider Weight** (`rider_weight`, number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Horse Age** (`horse_age`, number, required): Enter the age of the horse you will be riding.
* **Horse Breed** (`horse_breed`, select_one, optional): Select the breed of your horse (Quarter, Arabian, Other).
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_multiple, optional): Select the gender of the horse you will be riding (Male, Female, Other).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relation** (`emergency_relation`, select_one, optional): Select the relation of the emergency contact person (Parent, Child, Other).
* **Emergency Phone 2** (`emergency_phone2`, text, optional): Enter an additional phone number for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, text, optional): Enter an email address for the emergency contact person.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, number, required): Enter the registration fee for the event.
* **Registration Fee Status** (`registration_fee_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your registration fee (Paid, Pending, Cancelled).
* **Registration Fee Date** (`registration_fee_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your registration fee was paid.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event you will be participating in (Competition, Practice, Other).
* **Event Level** (`event_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of event you will be participating in (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any tool that has been assigned to you for the event.

This form is designed to collect all necessary and optional information for a successful registration process. Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
