# Parish Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parish Registration Form is designed to collect essential details about individuals or entities related to a parish. Completing this form accurately ensures that the parish administration has the necessary information to provide relevant services, support, or updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the First Name field with your or your organization's name as it should be recorded in the parish records.
2. Enter your Last Name in the corresponding field.
3. Provide a valid Email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your Phone Number for easy contactability.
5. Enter the Parish ID, a unique identifier for the parish you are registering with. If you don't have a Parish ID, you can leave this field blank.
6. Enter your Address for accurate contact and logistical purposes.
7. If applicable, enter the name of the Contact Person responsible for the registration or inquiry.
8. Write any additional Message you have for the parish administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your or your organization's name as it should be recorded in the parish records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your or your organization's surname for accurate record-keeping.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes. This will help the parish administration keep you informed about updates, events, and services.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for easy communication and outreach from the parish.
* **Parish ID** (`parish_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the parish you are registering with. If you don't have a Parish ID, you can leave this field blank.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your or your organization's address for accurate contact and logistical purposes.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person responsible for the registration or inquiry.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Write any additional message you have for the parish administration.
