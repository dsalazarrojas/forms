# Animal Adoption Appointment Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for users to reserve an appointment for animal adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the "Reservation Information" page by clicking on the corresponding tab.
3. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), as they are essential for the reservation process.
4. Enter your name in the "Reserver name" field.
5. Enter the date of the appointment in the "Reservation date" field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
6. Enter the time slot for the appointment in the "Reservation time" field (format: HH:MM).
7. If you have the Animal ID, enter it in the "Pet ID" field.
8. Enter your contact email in the "Contact email" field, which will be used for confirmation.
9. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone number" field.
10. Add any additional reservation notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reserver name** (`reserver_name`, text, required): This is the full name of the person making the reservation.
* **Reservation date** (`reservation_date`, date, required): This is the date for the appointment (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Reservation time** (`reservation_time`, time, required): This is the time slot for the appointment (format: HH:MM).
* **Pet ID** (`pet_id`, text, optional): This is the Animal ID if known.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This is the contact email for confirmation.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): This is the optional contact phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): This is any additional reservation notes.
