# Player Fundraising Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals and couples to sign up for a fundraising event, setting a specific goal for their fundraising efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "player_first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "player_last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "player_email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "player_phone" field.
5. Select your relationship status ("single", "married", "in a relationship", or "other") in the "player_relationship" field.
6. If you are a couple, enter your partner's first name in the "partner_first_name" field and their last name in the "partner_last_name" field. If not applicable, leave these fields blank.
7. If you are a couple, enter your partner's email address in the "partner_email" field and their phone number in the "partner_phone" field. If not applicable, leave these fields blank.
8. Set your fundraising goal in the "fundraising_goal" field. This is a required field to help us plan for the event.
9. Select your registration type ("Couple", "Individual", or "Neither") in the "is_couple" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Player Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Player Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for event updates and communications.
* **Player Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for event updates and communications.
* **Player Relationship**: Select your relationship status as it applies to you. This will help us understand your registration context.
* **Partner First Name** (if applicable): Enter your partner's first name as it is written on their identification documents.
* **Partner Last Name** (if applicable): Enter your partner's last name as it is written on their identification documents.
* **Partner Email** (if applicable): Enter your partner's valid email address where they can be reached for event updates and communications.
* **Partner Phone** (if applicable): Enter your partner's phone number where they can be reached for event updates and communications.
* **Partner Relationship** (if applicable): Select your partner's relationship status as it applies to them. This will help us understand your registration context.
* **Fundraising Goal**: Enter the target amount you aim to raise for the event.
* **Is Couple**: Select whether you are a couple or not. This will help us understand your registration context.
