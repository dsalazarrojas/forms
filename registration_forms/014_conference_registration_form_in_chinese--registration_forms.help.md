# Conference Registration Form In Chinese - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for conference registration. Please fill it out with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields.
2. Select the correct options for each field as instructed.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **姓名 (xing_ming)** (`xing_ming`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **電子邮件 (you_xiang)** (`you_xiang`, email, required): Enter your contact email address. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **電話號碼 (dian_hua)** (`dian_hua`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **单位或機構 (dan_wei)** (`dan_wei`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **職位 (zhi_wei)** (`zhi_wei`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **國家 (guo_jia)** (`guo_jia`, select_one, required): Select the country you are from.
* **城市 (cheng_shi)** (`cheng_shi`, text, required): Enter the city you are from.
* **注册类型 (zhu_ce_lei_xing)** (`zhu_ce_lei_xing`, select_one, required): Choose your registration type: full-day participation, one-day participation, online participation, student participation, or exhibitor participation.
* **參會日期 (can_hui_ri_qi)** (`can_hui_ri_qi`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you will attend. You can choose one or multiple days.
* **分論題 (fen_lun_tan)** (`fen_lun_tan`, select_one, optional): Select the topics you are interested in. You can choose one or multiple topics.
* **餐飲選擇 (can_guan_xuan_ze)** (`can_guan_xuan_ze`, select_one, required): Select your food preferences.
* **特殊要求 (te_shu_yao_qiu)** (`te_shu_yao_qiu`, text, optional): If you have any special requirements or needs, please specify them here.
* **酒店預訂 (jiu_dian_yu_ding)** (`jiu_dian_yu_ding`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need to book a hotel room.
* **房间类型 (fang_jian_lei_xing)** (`fang_jian_lei_xing`, select_one, optional): If you need a room, select the type of room you prefer.
* **接送服務 (jie_song_fu_wu)** (`jie_song_fu_wu`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need airport pickup or drop-off service.
* **社交活動 (she_jiao_huo_dong)** (`she_jiao_huo_dong`, select_one, required): Indicate if you will participate in social activities.
* **參會證書 (zheng_shu)** (`zheng_shu`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need a participation certificate.
* **無障礙需求 (wu_zhang_ai_xu_qiu)** (`wu_zhang_ai_xu_qiu`, text, optional): If you have any special needs or requirements, please specify them here.
* **接收推廣信息 (tui_guang_xin_xi)** (`tui_guang_xin_xi`, select_one, required): Indicate if you want to receive information about future events.
* **支付方式 (zhi_fu_fang_shi)** (`zhi_fu_fang_shi`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **優惠碼 (you_hui_ma)** (`you_hui_ma`, text, optional): If you have a voucher code, please enter it here.
* **接受條款和條件 (jie_shou_tiao_kuan)** (`jie_shou_tiao_kuan`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of participation.
