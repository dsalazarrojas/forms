# Community Stay Development Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Community Stay Development Program. It is intended to collect information about you, including your contact details, personal preferences, and background information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Select relevant options for fields with options.
4. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name in the format "First Name Last Name".
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Which development program are you interested in?** (`program_interest`, select_one, required): Select one option from the list provided.
* **Preferred Program Duration** (`program_duration`, select_one, required): Select one option from the list provided.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`preferred_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like to begin the program.
* **Do you have prior experience in this field?** (`prior_experience`, select_one, required): Select one option from the list provided.
* **Why do you want to join this program?** (`motivation_statement`, text, required): Briefly describe your motivation and goals for joining the program.
* **What skills can you contribute to the program?** (`skills_to_contribute`, text, optional): List any relevant skills or talents you can contribute.
* **Do you have any accommodation needs?** (`accommodation_needs`, text, optional): Enter any accommodation needs, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility requirements.
* **How will you travel to the program location?** (`transportation_method`, select_one, required): Select one option from the list provided.
* **Do you have any medical conditions we should know about?** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions that may require attention during the program.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your insurance company.
* **I consent to a background check if required** (`consent_background_check`, select_one, required): Select one option from the list provided.
* **I consent to photos/videos being taken for promotional use** (`consent_media_use`, select_one, required): Select one option from the list provided.
* **How did you hear about this program?** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Select one option from the list provided.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): This field will be automatically filled out with today's date.

Note: This form is intended to collect information for registration purposes only. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the program administrators.
