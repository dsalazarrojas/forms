# Technology Fair Booth Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for booking a booth at the Technology Fair. The form is used to gather essential details such as exhibit name, booth type, and contact person's information. It also captures the technology fair's date, time, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full company name in the "Exhibit Name" field.
2. Select the type of booth you want to book (e.g., 3m X 3m, 6m x 6m, 12m X 12m, or Other).
3. Enter the number of exhibitors attending.
4. Enter the name and email of the contact person.
5. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
6. Describe any specific booth requirements in the "Booth Requirements" field.
7. Choose the date, time, and location for the Technology Fair.
8. Indicate whether you will be attending the fair online or offline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibit Name** (`exhibit_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of your company as it will be displayed on the fair's promotional materials.
* **Booth Type** (`booth_type`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the size of the booth you need. Options include 3m X 3m, 6m x 6m, 12m X 12m, or Other.
* **Number of Exhibitors** (`number_of_exhibitors`, number, **Required**): Enter the number of people attending your booth.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your booth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Booth Requirements** (`booth_requirements`, text, **Required**): Describe any special requirements for your booth, such as electricity or internet access.
* **Technology Fair Date** (`technology_fair_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date of the Technology Fair in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2024-03-12).
* **Technology Fair Time** (`technology_fair_time`, time, **Required**): Enter the time of the Technology Fair in HH -MM -SS format (e.g., 09:00 - 17:00).
* **Technology Fair Location** (`technology_fair_location`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the location of the Technology Fair. Options include Technology Center, Science Museum, Convention Center, or Online.
* **Online Booking** (`online_booking`, select_one, **Required**): Indicate whether you will be attending the fair online or offline.
* **Booth Size** (`technology_fair_size`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the size of the booth you need. Options include 3m X 3m, 6m x 6m, 12m X 12m, or Other.
* **Note** (`note`, note, **Optional**): Add any additional comments or notes about your booth.

Note: This guide will be helpful for end users to understand the form structure and requirements.
