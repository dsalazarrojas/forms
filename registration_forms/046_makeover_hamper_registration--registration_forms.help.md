# Makeover Hamper Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeover Hamper Registration form is used to register yourself or your hamper-related information for a makeover. Fill out the form to provide us with your contact details and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill out the fields with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required: False): Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (Text, Required: False): Enter your address for delivery or pick-up purposes.
* **City** (Text, Required: False): Enter the city for delivery or pick-up purposes.
* **Country** (Select One, Required: False): Select the country for delivery or pick-up purposes.
* **Consent** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Select whether you consent to the terms and conditions for the makeover.
* **Entry Date** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date you would like to register for the makeover.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Make sure to select the correct country for delivery or pick-up purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* This form is for registration purposes only and does not imply any commitment or obligation.
