# Party Stylist Job Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Stylist Job Registration Form is designed to gather information from potential candidates interested in job openings within the industry. This form helps us understand your qualifications, experience, and job preferences, making it easier for us to match you with suitable job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), to ensure your application is complete.
* Enter your first name, last name, email address, and phone number accurately.
* Provide your experience in years, job title, and job description.
* Indicate your desired salary and available work days and hours.
* Specify your available dates for work and education level.
* List your relevant skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)** (`first_name`, text, false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`last_name`, text, false): Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, text, false): Enter your phone number.
* **Experience Years (Experience Years)** (`experience_years`, number, false): Enter your years of experience in the industry.
* **Job Title (Job Title)** (`job_title`, text, false): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Job Description (Job Description)** (`job_description`, text, false): Briefly describe your work experience and job responsibilities.
* **Work Type (Work Type)** (`work_type`, text, false): Specify the type of work you are interested in (e.g., freelance, full-time, part-time).
* **Desired Salary (Desired Salary)** (`desired_salary`, number, false): Enter your desired salary.
* **Available Days (Available Days)** (`available_days`, time, false): Specify your available days for work (e.g., Monday to Friday, weekends only).
* **Available Hours (Available Hours)** (`available_hours`, time, false): Specify your available hours for work (e.g., 8 AM to 5 PM).
* **Available Dates (Available Dates)** (`available_dates`, date, false): Enter your available dates for work (e.g., specific dates or a range of dates).
* **Education Level (Education Level)** (`education_level`, text, false): Specify your education level (e.g., degree, diploma, certification).
* **Skills (Skills)** (`skills`, text, false): List your relevant skills and experience in the industry.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [insert contact information].
