# Pet Retail Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pet-related retail partners for the purpose of creating a database of partners for the pet industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the "pet_details" field on page 1 with your pet-related information.
2. On page 2, fill out the "partner_details" field with your business details.
3. On page 3, provide any additional "review_details" that are relevant to your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Details (page 1, pet_details)
* **Pet Details**: A field to provide information about your pet-related business (e.g., pet store, pet groomer, pet supply store).

### Partner Details (page 2, partner_details)
* **Partner Details**: A field to provide information about your business (e.g., business name, address, contact person).

### Review Details (page 3, review_details)
* **Review Details**: A field to provide any additional information about your business that you think would be helpful for our records (e.g., services offered, hours of operation).
