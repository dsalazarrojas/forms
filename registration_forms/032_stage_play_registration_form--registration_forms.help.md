# Stage Play Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Play Registration Form is designed to collect information about stage plays from participants. This form is used by event organizers or administrators to gather details about stage plays for events like theater festivals, performances, or competitions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team name in the **team_name** field.
2. Enter the original name of the play in the **original_play_name** field.
3. Upload the script of the play in the **upload_script** field.
4. Choose whether the play belongs to a specific category by selecting an option in the **category** field.
5. Select the genre of the play from the options provided in the **genre** field.
6. Enter the date of the event in the **date_of_event** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter the start time of the play in the **start_time** field.
8. Indicate whether the team members are participating by selecting an option in the **team_members** field.
9. Enter the email address of the team in the **email** field in the format of [user@domain.com](mailto:user@domain.com).
10. Enter the phone number of the team in the **phone_number** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team_name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of your team.
* **original_play_name** (text, required: false): Enter the original name of your play.
* **script** (text, required: false): Upload the script of your play.
* **upload_script** (text, required: false): Upload the script of your play (same as **script**).
* **category** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if your play belongs to a specific category.
	+ Select 'No' if your play does not belong to a category.
* **genre** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if your play belongs to a specific genre.
	+ Select 'No' if your play does not belong to a genre.
* **date_of_event** (date, required: false): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **start_time** (time, required: false): Enter the start time of the play.
* **team_members** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if your team members are participating.
	+ Select 'No' if your team members are not participating.
* **email** (email, required: false): Enter the email address of your team in the format [user@domain.com](mailto:user@domain.com).
* **phone_number** (text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your team.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to complete the form successfully.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* Ensure that the email address is in the correct format.
* If any of the fields are marked as required, make sure to fill them in accordingly.
* If you need help or have any questions, contact the event organizers or administrators.
