# Chiropractic New Patient Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chiropractic New Patient Registration Form is used to gather essential information about new patients. This information will help us to better understand your medical history, provide personalized care, and communicate with you effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

Please fill out this form completely and accurately. Each section is explained below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents. (Text, Required)
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your best phone number for us to contact you for appointments. (Text, Required)
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for appointment reminders and correspondence. (Email, Required)
* **Address**: Enter your home address for our records. (Text, Optional)
* **Insurance Provider**: If you have insurance, please enter your insurance provider's name. (Text, Optional)
* **Policy Number**: If you have insurance, enter your policy number or member ID. (Text, Optional)
* **Reason for Visit**: Briefly describe the reason for your visit. (Text, Required)
* **Referral Source**: How did you hear about our clinic? (Select One)
	+ **Physician**: Your doctor or health care professional recommended us.
	+ **Friend or Family**: A friend or family member referred you to our clinic.
	+ **Online Search**: You searched for us online.
	+ **Other**: Other (please specify)
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact, such as a family member or friend. (Text, Required)
