# Lean Innovation Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your information and preferences for the Lean Innovation Forum. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that your registration is successful.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number (optional) in the "Phone" field.
4. If you are representing a company, please enter its name in the "Company" field (optional).
5. Select your position within the company in the "Position" field (optional).
6. Choose your role in the forum by selecting at least one option in the "Role" field (required).
7. Agree to share your registration information, including your name and email, with the forum organizers and attendees (required).
8. Confirm that you agree to publish your name and bio on the forum's communication channels (required).
9. Optionally, choose to agree to receive emails from the forum organizers.
10. Choose whether to post your content on the forum's communication channels (optional).
11. Select whether to share your content on other platforms (e.g., social media) (optional).
12. Select whether to share your content on other platforms (e.g., X, Y, or Z) (optional).
13. Provide any additional notes about your registration or preferences in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Company** (Optional): Enter the name of the company you represent (if applicable).
* **Position** (Optional): Select your position within the company (if applicable).
* **Role** (Required): Select at least one role in the forum (e.g., speaker, attendee, or facilitator).
* **Agree to Share** (Required): Confirm that you agree to share your registration information with the forum organizers and attendees.
* **Agree to Publish** (Required): Confirm that you agree to publish your name and bio on the forum's communication channels.
* **Agree to Email** (Optional): Choose to receive emails from the forum organizers (if applicable).
* **Agree to Post** (Optional): Choose to post your content on the forum's communication channels (if applicable).
* **Agree to Share on X, Y, or Z** (Optional): Select whether to share your content on other platforms (if applicable).
* **Notes** (Optional): Provide any additional notes about your registration or preferences.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that you have made the necessary selections to register successfully.
