# Strategic Innovation Accelerator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Innovation Accelerator Registration Form is used to collect information about programs applying or participating in the innovation ecosystem. This form is designed to help us better understand your needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Program Title** field with the actual title of the program you are registering.
2. Enter your **Applicant Name** in the designated field.
3. Provide your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you about your program.
4. Enter your **Phone** number for easier communication.
5. Describe your **Program Goals** in the space provided.
6. Enter the **Program Start Date** and **Program End Date**, which will help us understand your program timeline.
7. Specify the **Program Duration** (in months) for your program.
8. Select the type of **Program Type** you are registering (Accelerator or Innovation Program).
9. (Optional) Attach **Supporting Documents**, if needed, to provide additional context.
10. Add **Additional Info**, if you have any further details you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Title (text, Required)**: Enter the actual title of the program you are registering.
* **Applicant Name (text, Required)**: Enter your name as the applicant or representative of the program.
* **Email (email, Required)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (text, Required)**: Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Program Goals (text, Required)**: Describe your program's goals in this field.
* **Program Start Date (date, Required)**: Enter the start date of your program.
* **Program End Date (date, Required)**: Enter the end date of your program.
* **Program Duration (number, Required)**: Specify the duration of your program in months.
* **Program Type (select_one, Required)**: Choose between Accelerator or Innovation Program.
* **Supporting Documents (note, Optional)**: Attach any supporting documents you'd like to provide, if needed.
* **Additional Info (text, Optional)**: Share any additional information you'd like about your program.
