# Medical History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical History Form is a tool used to collect important medical information from patients, which can be used to inform their treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all necessary information.
2. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or assistance from a healthcare professional.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Name (<name>, <type>: text, required)
Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Date of Birth (<name>, <type>: date, required)
Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Medical History (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
Describe any medical conditions, illnesses, or injuries you have experienced.

### Allergies (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
List any allergies you have, including medications, foods, or substances.

### Medical Condition (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
Describe any current or past medical conditions you have.

### Medications (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
List any medications you are currently taking.

### Medical Procedure (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
Describe any medical procedures you have undergone, such as surgeries or treatments.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, ask for clarification from a healthcare professional.
- Review your answers before submitting the form.
- If you are unable to complete the form, seek assistance from a healthcare professional.
