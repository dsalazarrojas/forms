# milestone_celebration_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The milestone celebration RSVP form is used to track and manage attendees and their responses to a milestone celebration event. It is essential to fill this form accurately to ensure that the event planners can manage attendance and communication effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Event Details**: Enter the event details in the first field. This field is used to collect information about the event.
2. **RSVP**: Select your RSVP response from the dropdown list to let us know if you can attend the event or not.
3. **Attendees**: Select how many attendees are coming with you from the dropdown list.
4. **Attendee 1 Information**:
	* **Name**: Enter the name of the first attendee.
	* **Email**: Enter the email address of the first attendee.
	* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the first attendee.
	* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the first attendee.
	* **Comment**: Enter any comments about the first attendee.
	* **Attendee 1 Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the first attendee.
5. **Event Information**:
	* **Date/Time**: Enter the date and time of the event.
	* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.
	* **Note**: Enter any additional information about the event.
6. **Form Status**:
	* **RSVP Date**: Enter the date when you replied to the event.
	* **RSVP Time**: Enter the time when you replied to the event.
	* **Form Submitted**: Enter the date and time when you submitted the form.
	* **Form Received**: Enter the date and time when the form was received by the event planners.
	* **Form Verified**: Enter the date and time when the form was verified by the event planners.
	* **Form Confirmed**: Enter the date and time when the form was confirmed by the event planners.
	* **Form Responded**: Enter the date and time when the response was received by the event planners.
	* **Form Completed**: Enter the date and time when the form was completed.
7. **Event Status**:
	* **Event Status**: Select the status of the event from the dropdown list.
8. **Attendee 2 Information**:
	* **Name**: Enter the name of the second attendee.
	* **Email**: Enter the email address of the second attendee.
	* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the second attendee.

## Tips

* Please fill in all fields accurately to ensure that the event planners can manage the event effectively.
* If you are not attending the event, select "No" for RSVP to help the event planners plan accordingly.
* If there are any issues with the form, please contact us.
