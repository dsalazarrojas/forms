# Vendor Appreciation Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Appreciation Party RSVP Form is used to collect information from vendors who want to attend the party. This information will help us plan the party and ensure that everyone has a great time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, as this information is necessary for planning and organization.
2. Make sure to enter your name, email, and phone number correctly.
3. Choose your RSVP status (Yes, No, or Not Sure) to let us know if you can attend.
4. If you are attending, provide any special requests or dietary restrictions you may have.
5. If you are not attending, please let us know why (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name**: Enter your name, as we want to personalize our welcome and recognition for you.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with party details and updates.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **rsvp**: Choose "Yes" if you are attending the party, "No" if you are not, and "Not Sure" if you are still deciding.
* **comments**: If you have any special requests or comments, please let us know here. This will help us tailor the party to your needs.
* **guest_name**: If you are bringing a guest, enter their name here.
* **company_name**: If you want to be recognized for your company, enter its name here.
* **job_title**: Enter your job title or role in your company.
* **contact_info**: If you want to be contacted by us, enter your contact information here.
* **attending**: Choose "True" if you are attending the party, "False" if you are not, and "Not Sure" if you are still deciding.
* **special_requests**: If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know here.
* **dietary_restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions or requirements, choose the relevant option here.
