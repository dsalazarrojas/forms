# Leadership Coaching Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Coaching Program Registration Form is a mandatory form that collects information from participants to register for the program. This form helps program administrators ensure that all necessary details are collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in all the required fields marked as "required" with an asterisk (*).
- Select the correct options for each field where applicable.
- Ensure that the dates entered for "Start Date" and "End Date" are in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Select the correct options for "Program Type", "Location", "Payment Terms", "Payment Amount", and "Payment Method" fields.
- Enter your full name and email address correctly.
- Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Notes" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, *): This field is for entering your name and any other identifying details.
* **Choose Program** (`program_selection`, `select_one`, *): Please select "Yes" if you are interested in enrolling in the program.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, *): Enter the date when you want to start the program in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, *): Enter the date when you want to end the program in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, `number`, *): Enter the duration of the program you are interested in.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, *): Enter the name of your coach or the name of the person who will be coaching you.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, *): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, ): This field is optional, but it's recommended to enter a valid contact address for any communication purposes.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, ): If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, *): Enter your email address.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, `select_multiple`, *): Please select "Yes" for each program type that applies to your interest.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, *): Please select "Yes" if you are interested in enrolling in this location.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, *): Please select "Yes" for each payment term that applies to your interest.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `select_one`, *): Please select "Yes" for the payment amount you are willing to pay.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, *): Please select "Yes" for your preferred payment method.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `text`, *): Please enter "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions.
