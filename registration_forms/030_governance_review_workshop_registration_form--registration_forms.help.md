# Governance Review Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Governance Review Workshop Registration Form is a tool for participants to provide their details to register for the Governance Review Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not already registered for this workshop.
2. Fill in your details accurately.
3. Submit your form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter your full name to help us identify you as a participant.
* **Participant Email (participant_email)**: Enter your email address to help us contact you and send workshop updates.
* **Participant Phone (participant_phone)**: Provide your contact phone number for any further communication or updates.
* **Workshop Location (workshop_location)**: Not a required field, but feel free to fill it in if you'd like to provide this information.

## Tips
* Please be accurate while filling in your details to ensure smooth communication and planning for the workshop. 
* If you have any special requests or requirements, let us know in the comments section (not included in this form). 
* We will be in touch with you soon after receiving your form submission. 

Note: This guide assumes that the form fields are for a basic registration process, without any additional fields like comments or special requests.
