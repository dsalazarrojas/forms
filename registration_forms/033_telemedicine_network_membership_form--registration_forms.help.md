# Telemedicine Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and preferences from individuals who want to join the Telemedicine Network. It is used to create your membership profile and provide you with access to the services you've selected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your basic details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. Share your medical history in the Medical History section.
4. Select the telehealth services you are interested in from the Telehealth Services Selected section.
5. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth section.
6. Add your phone number in the Phone Number section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address and any other relevant contact details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **Telehealth Services Selected** (`services_selected`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the telehealth services you are interested in, such as video consultations or medication management.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communications or appointments.
