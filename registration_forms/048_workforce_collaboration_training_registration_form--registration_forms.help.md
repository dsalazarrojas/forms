# Workforce Collaboration Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Collaboration Training Registration Form is a tool used to register employees' training needs and preferences. It helps training administrators communicate with employees and ensures that training is delivered effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee name, email, and phone number to identify yourself.
2.  Select the type of training you need.
3.  Choose the date and time for the training.
4.  Enter the duration of the training.
5.  Provide any additional comments or requests.
6.  Confirm with your manager that you can attend the training.
7.  Select the training status (e.g., pending, scheduled, or completed).
8.  Enter any comments or feedback from your manager or the training coordinator.
9.  Confirm your attendance status (e.g., present, absent, or late).
10. Enter any comments or feedback about your attendance.
11. Finally, enter any comments or feedback from the training coordinator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Training Details (id: 1)**: Enter a brief description of the training you need or want.
*   **Employee Name (id: 3)**: Enter your employee name.
*   **Employee Email (id: 4)**: Enter your employee email.
*   **Employee Phone (id: 5)**: Enter your employee phone number.
*   **Employee Position (id: 6)**: Enter your employee position or role.
*   **Training Type (id: 7)**: Select the type of training you need or want (e.g., online, in-person, or virtual).
*   **Training Date (id: 8)**: Enter the date of the training.
*   **Training Time (id: 9)**: Enter the time of the training.
*   **Training Duration (id: 10)**: Enter the duration of the training.
*   **Training Location (id: 11)**: Enter the location of the training.
*   **Comments (id: 12)**: Enter any additional comments or requests.
*   **Manager Confirmation (id: 13)**: Confirm with your manager that you can attend the training.
*   **Training Status (id: 14)**: Select the status of the training (e.g., pending, scheduled, or completed).
*   **Employee Comments (id: 15)**: Enter any comments or feedback from you or your manager.
*   **Training Notes (id: 16)**: Enter any notes or feedback from the training coordinator.
*   **Manager Comments (id: 17)**: Enter any comments or feedback from your manager.
*   **Training Attendance (id: 18)**: Enter your attendance status (e.g., present, absent, or late).
*   **Attendance Status (id: 19)**: Select your attendance status.
*   **Attendance Comments (id: 20)**: Enter any comments or feedback about your attendance.
*   **Form Submitted By (id: 21)**: Enter who submitted the form (e.g., you or someone else).
*   **Training Coordinator (id: 22)**: Enter the name of the training coordinator.
*   **Training Facilitator (id: 23)**: Enter the name of the training facilitator.
*   **Training Coordinator Comments (id: 24)**: Enter any comments or feedback from the training coordinator.
*   **Form Reviewed By (id: 25)**: Enter who reviewed the form (e.g., you or someone else).
