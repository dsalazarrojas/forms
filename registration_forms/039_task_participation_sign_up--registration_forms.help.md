# Task Participation Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the Task Participation Sign Up form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Organization Name in the Organization Name field.
2. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
5. Select your Task Preferences (Volunteer, Task Leader, or Task Participant) in the Task Preferences field.
6. Enter your Availability for the task in the Availability field.
7. Select your Timezone (Pacific, Eastern, Central, Mountain, or Atlantic) in the Timezone field.
8. Confirm your Name in the Confirm Name field (this is a verification step to ensure your name accuracy).
9. Confirm your Email address in the Confirm Email field (this is a verification step to ensure your email accuracy).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name (Organization Name)**: This field is for entering the name of your organization or group you are representing.
* **First Name (First Name)**: This field is for entering your given name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: This field is for entering your surname or last name.
* **Email (Email)**: This field is for entering your contact email address.
* **Task Preferences (Task Preferences)**: This field allows you to specify your role in the task, such as Volunteer, Task Leader, or Task Participant.
* **Availability (Availability)**: This field is for entering your availability for the task, e.g., a specific date or time range.
* **Timezone (Timezone)**: This field is for selecting your time zone, which helps with scheduling purposes.
* **Confirm Name (Confirm Name)**: This field is for verifying that your name is accurate.
* **Confirm Email (Confirm Email)**: This field is for verifying that your email address is accurate.
