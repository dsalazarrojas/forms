# Financial Process Innovation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Process Innovation Registration form is a tool designed to collect and review innovative financial process ideas for potential implementation. This form helps us understand the concept, scope, and expected outcome of each project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise description of your project in the Project Details section.
2. Share your contact information, including your name and email, in the Contact Information section.
3. Describe your expected output in the Output section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): This section is where you provide a detailed description of your financial process innovation. Please be as specific as possible about your idea, including the process and any relevant context or background information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): This section is where you share your name and email address so we can get in touch with you for further information or discussions.
* **Output** (`output`, text, required): This section is where you describe the expected outcome or result of your financial process innovation. Please be as detailed as possible about what you hope to achieve with your idea.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in each section.
* Please avoid submitting incomplete or vague submissions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
