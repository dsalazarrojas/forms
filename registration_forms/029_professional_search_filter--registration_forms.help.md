# Professional Search Filter - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect professional information for search filtering purposes. It will help in filtering data based on specific criteria for professional search results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This field requires a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your **Position Title** from the available options in the "Position Title" field.
6. Enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field.
7. Select your **Experience** from the available options in the "Experience" field.
8. Select one or multiple **Industries** that you are associated with from the available options in the "Industry" field.
9. Select **Job Type** as either "Yes" or "No" from the available options in the "Job Type" field.
10. Select **Job Status** as either "Active" or "Inactive" from the available options in the "Job Status" field.
11. Enter your **Job Start Date** in the "Job Start Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
12. Enter your **Job End Date** in the "Job End Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
13. Enter your **Job Location** in the "Job Location" field.
14. Enter your **Job Description** in the "Job Description" field.
15. Select your **Skills** as either "Yes" or "No" from the available options in the "Skills" field.
16. Select your **Certifications** as either "Yes" or "No" from the available options in the "Certifications" field.
17. Enter any **Job Status Note** in the "Job Status Note" field.
18. Enter any **Notes** in the "Notes" field.
19. Enter any **Job Notes** in the "Job Notes" field.
20. Enter your **Experience Note** in the "Experience Note" field.
21. Enter your **Experience Years** in the "Experience Years" field.
22. Enter your **Experience Months** in the "Experience Months" field.
23. Enter your **Job Hours** in the "Job Hours" field.
24. Enter your **Experience Hours** in the "Experience Hours" field.
25. Enter any **Job Type Note** in the "Job Type Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address. This field requires a valid email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Position Title** (5, text, required): Select your position title from the available options.
* **Job Title** (6, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Experience** (7, select_one, required): Select your experience level from the available options.
* **Industry** (8, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple industries you are associated with.
* **Job Type** (9, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have a job type.
* **Job Status** (10, select_one, required): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your job status.
* **Job Start Date** (11, date, required): Enter your job start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Job End Date** (12, date, required): Enter your job end date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Job Location** (13, text, required): Enter your job location.
* **Job Description** (14, text, required): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Skills** (15, select_multiple, required): Select your skills as either "Yes" or "No" from the available options.
* **Certifications** (16, select_multiple, required): Select your certifications as either "Yes" or "No" from the available options.
* **Job Status Note** (17, text, required): Enter any additional information about your job status.
* **Notes** (18, text, required): Enter any additional notes about yourself.
* **Job Notes** (19, text, required): Enter any additional job-specific notes.
* **Experience Note** (20, text, required): Enter any additional information about your experience.
* **Experience Years** (21, number, required): Enter your years of experience.
* **Experience Months** (22, number, required): Enter your months of experience.
* **Job Hours** (23, number, required): Enter your job hours.
* **Experience Hours** (24, number, required): Enter your experience hours.
* **Job Type Note** (25, text, required): Enter any additional information about your job type.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email address is valid.
* Select only one or multiple industries that apply to you.
* Enter your job start and end dates in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Enter a brief description of your job.
* Select only one or multiple skills and certifications that apply to you.
* Enter any additional notes or information that you think is relevant.
