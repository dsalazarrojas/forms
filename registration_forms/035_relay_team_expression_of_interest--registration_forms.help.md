# relay_team_expression_of_interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "relay_team_expression_of_interest" form is used for teams to express their interest in participating in events and activities. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Ensure that you are a team representative and have the necessary permissions to fill out this form on behalf of your team.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying extra attention to required fields.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name (<participant_name>, Text, Required)
The participant name is the name of the person filling out the form, not the team name. Please enter your name.

*   **Team Name** (<team_name>, Text, Optional)
    Enter your team's name, if different from your name.

*   **Team Manager Name** (<team_manager_name>, Text, Required)
    Enter the name of your team manager.

*   **Team Experience** (<team_experience>, Text, Required)
    Describe your team's experience in events or activities.

*   **Team Experience Years** (<team_experience_years>, Number, Required)
    Specify the number of years your team has been active.

*   **Team Manager Experience** (<team_manager_experience>, Text, Optional)
    Describe your team manager's experience.

*   **Team Type** (<team_type>, Select One, Required)
    Choose your team type from the options provided. Options are: 
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
    *   Option 3

*   **Other Team Type** (<team_type_other>, Text, Optional)
    Enter an other team type if not listed in the options above.

*   **Event Type** (<event_type>, Select Multiple, Required)
    Choose the types of events your team is interested in. Options are: 
    *   Option 4
    *   Option 5

*   **Event Dates** (<event_dates>, Date, Required)
    Enter the dates of the event.

*   **Event Start Time** (<event_start_time>, Time, Required)
    Enter the start time of the event.

*   **Event Duration** (<event_duration>, Number, Required)
    Specify the duration of the event.

*   **Event Venue** (<event_venue>, Text, Required)
    Enter the event venue.

*   **Event Details** (<event_details>, Text, Required)
    Describe the event details.

*   **Team Size** (<team_size>, Number, Required)
    Specify the size of your team.

*   **Team Location** (<team_location>, Text, Optional)
    Enter your team's location, if applicable.

*   **Team Language** (<team_language>, Text, Optional)
    Enter your team's language, if applicable.

*   **Team Notes** (<team_notes>, Note, Optional)
    Enter any additional notes about your team.

*   **Manager Name** (<manager_name>, Text, Required)
    Enter your team manager's name.

*   **Manager Contact Info** (<manager_contact_info>, Text, Optional)
    Enter your team manager's contact information, if applicable.

*   **Manager Email** (<manager_email>, Email, Required)
    Enter your team manager's email.

*   **Manager Phone** (<manager_phone>, Text, Required)
    Enter your team manager's phone number.

*   **Manager Experience** (<manager_experience>, Text, Optional)
    Describe your team manager's experience.
