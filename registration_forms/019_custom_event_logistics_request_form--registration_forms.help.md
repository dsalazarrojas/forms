# custom_event_logistics_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users coordinate event logistics with ease. It collects details about the event, vendor, and participant information, as well as contact information, to facilitate the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the relevant fields with the required information. The form is not strictly required, so users can choose which details to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**:
	+ A text field for entering event details. This could be the name of the event, its date, time, location, or any other relevant information.
* **Vendor Details**:
	+ A text field for entering vendor information. This could be the vendor's name, contact details, or any other relevant information.
* **Participant Details**:
	+ A text field for entering participant information. This could be the participant's name, contact details, or any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info**:
	+ A text field for entering contact information. This could be the name, email, or phone number of the person responsible for the event.
## Tips
* Please provide as much or as little information as you need in each field.
* You can submit any or all of the fields, depending on your preference.
* If you need help with event logistics, this form can assist you.
* Please note that the form fields are free text, so you can format your response as you wish.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
