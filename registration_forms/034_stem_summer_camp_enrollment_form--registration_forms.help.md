# stem_summer_camp_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stem_summer_camp_enrollment_form is designed to collect participant and parent information, payment details, and medical information for the summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information on **Page 1: Participant Information**.
2. Enter your parent's information on **Page 2: Parent Information**.
3. Submit your payment details on **Page 3: Payment Information**.
4. Disclose any medical information on **Page 4: Medical Information**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1: Participant Information**
  - **Participant Information** (`text`, optional): This section is for you to enter your name, age, and contact details.
* **Page 2: Parent Information**
  - **Parent Information** (`text`, optional): Enter your parent's name, contact details, and any relevant information about your child's care.
* **Page 3: Payment Information**
  - **Payment Information** (`text`, optional): This field is for payment method and details.
* **Page 4: Medical Information**
  - **Medical Information** (`text`, optional): If your child has any medical conditions, allergies, or requires specific care, please provide detailed information here.

## Tips
- Please double-check your input for accuracy to ensure a smooth enrollment process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the camp staff.
- Review the camp's policies and terms before proceeding with the registration.
- You are free to modify this form for your specific use, just give credit to its creators.
