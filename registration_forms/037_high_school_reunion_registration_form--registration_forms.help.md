# High School Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Reunion Registration Form is designed to collect vital data from attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience during the reunion.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **name** in the first field.
2. Specify your **profession** in the second field.
3. Select your **family status** from the third field.
4. Enter your **name** in the fourth field.
5. Provide your **phone number** in the fifth field.
6. In the sixth field, select whether you will **bring guests** and choose their names (if any).
7. Select the **purchase order** (product(s) you wish to purchase) from the eighth field.
8. Enter the **price** you are willing to pay for the selected product.
9. Verify that the **total amount** displayed is accurate and adjust if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (Required, text): Please enter your name as it is known today.
* **profession** (Required, text): Specify your current profession or occupation.
* **family_status** (Required, text): Select your family status from the options provided.
* **name** (Required, text): This field is a duplicate of the first field for verification purposes.
* **phone** (Required, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **will_bring_guests** (Required, select_multiple): Select the number of guests you plan to bring and their names from the options below.
	+ Guest 1
	+ Guest 2
	+ Guest 3
* **guest_1_name**, **guest_2_name**, **guest_3_name** (Optional, text): Enter the names of your selected guests (if any).
* **purchase_order** (Required, select_one): Choose the product(s) you wish to purchase from the options provided.
* **price** (Required, number): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the selected product.
* **total** (Required, number): This field is automatically calculated and displayed for your convenience.
