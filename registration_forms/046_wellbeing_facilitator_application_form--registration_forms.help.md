# wellbeing-facilitator-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register as a wellbeing facilitator, providing a platform for individuals to offer their services in areas such as mental, physical, emotional, and spiritual wellbeing.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled in.
- Please note that some fields are optional, but providing detailed information will help to showcase your expertise and increase your chances of being approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Applicant Email** (2, email, optional): Enter your personal email address that we can use to communicate with you regarding your application.
* **Applicant Phone** (3, text, optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Wellbeing Facilitation Areas** (4, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more areas that you are experienced in, such as:
	+ Physical Wellbeing
	+ Mental Wellbeing
	+ Emotional Wellbeing
	+ Spiritual Wellbeing
* **Work Experience** (5, text, optional): Briefly describe your relevant work experience in the wellbeing field.
* **Education** (6, text, optional): Mention any relevant education you have received, such as degrees or certifications.
* **Wellbeing Services Offered** (7, text, optional): Specify the wellbeing services you offer, such as coaching, therapy, or workshops.
* **Certification** (8, text, optional): If applicable, provide any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
