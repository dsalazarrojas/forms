# Gym Membership Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Gym Membership Payment Form is used to collect payment information from gym members. This includes selecting the type of membership, payment method, payment schedule, and any additional details required for processing membership payments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your preferred **Membership Type** (Free, Premium, or Basic).
3. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank).
4. Enter the **Payment Date** for your membership payment.
5. Select the **Membership Term** (Month, Month-to-Month, Month-to-Year, or Year).
6. Enter the **Payment Amount** (if required).
7. Choose your **Payment Frequency** (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
8. Add any additional **Note** regarding your membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for membership updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for membership updates.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, optional): Choose from Free, Premium, or Basic membership options.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select from Stripe, PayPal, or Bank payment options.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your membership payment.
* **Membership Term** (`membership_term`, select_one, optional): Choose from Month, Month-to-Month, Month-to-Year, or Year membership term options.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount (if required).
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually payment frequency options.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding your membership.
