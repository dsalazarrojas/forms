# Matrimonial Meet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Matrimonial Meet Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in meeting for a matrimonial purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which include First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Preferred Meet Time, Preferred Meet Day, Availability, Preferred Event Type, Preferred Event Topic, and Preferred Event Venue.
2. You can choose your preferred meet time from the available options.
3. Select all the preferred meet days that work for you.
4. Provide your availability in the format HH:MM.
5. Fill out the preferred event type, topics, and venues you are interested in.
6. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Preferred Meet Time** (select_one, required): Choose a time when you are available to meet.
* **Preferred Meet Day** (select_multiple, required): Select all the days you are available to meet.
* **Availability** (time, required): Enter your availability in the format HH:MM.
* **Personal Info** (note, optional): Provide any additional personal information you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Preferred Event Type** (select_multiple, required): Choose the types of events you are interested in.
* **Preferred Event Topic** (select_multiple, required): Select the topics you are interested in discussing or learning.
* **Preferred Event Venue** (select_multiple, required): Choose the venues where you would like to meet.

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only and should not be used for any other use cases not related to matrimonial meets. 

# Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form to avoid any potential issues.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, reach out to the support team for assistance.
