# Sponsorship Review Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a sponsorship review panel with expertise in specific areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the panelists with expertise in the areas you need by checking the boxes next to their names.
2. Enter the name of each panelist.
3. Enter the email address of each panelist.
4. List the expertise areas you need help with.
5. Enter the availability start date for each panelist.
6. Enter the availability end date for each panelist.
7. Describe the availability of each panelist.
8. Describe the availability of each reviewer.
9. Add any additional comments about the panelists or their review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Step 1 - Select Your Panel
* **Select Your Panel** (`sponsorship_review_panel_registration_page`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the panelists with expertise in the areas you need. This field allows you to choose from a list of options and select multiple panelists.

### Panelists Name
* **Panelists Name** (`panelists_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of each panelist you have selected.

### Panelists Email
* **Panelists Email** (`panelists_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of each panelist you have selected.

### Expertise Areas
* **Expertise Areas** (`expertise_areas`, text, required: false): List the expertise areas you need help with.

### Availability Start Date
* **Availability Start Date** (`availability_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when each panelist becomes available to review sponsorships.

### Availability End Date
* **Availability End Date** (`availability_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when each panelist is no longer available to review sponsorships.

### Panelist Availability
* **Panelist Availability** (`panelist_availability`, text, required: false): Describe the availability of each panelist, including their schedule and any limitations.

### Reviewer Availability
* **Reviewer Availability** (`reviewer_availability`, text, required: false): Describe the availability of each reviewer, including their schedule and any limitations.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about the panelists or their review, such as their experience or qualifications.

Note: This form is not meant to be a comprehensive review of a panelist's qualifications, but rather a way to register their availability and expertise for review purposes. If you need to review a panelist's qualifications, please contact the relevant authority.
