# Emergency Preparedness Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to register for an emergency preparedness forum. It captures essential information about the participant, their organization, and the event they are attending.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the participant's name in the `participant name` field.
2. Enter the name of the organization the participant is associated with in the `organization name` field (if applicable).
3. Enter the participant's first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
4. Enter the participant's contact email and phone numbers in the `contact email` and `phone` fields (if applicable).
5. Confirm the participant's attendance to the event by selecting one of the options in the `participant attendance` field.
6. Enter the date and time of the event in the `date of event` and `time of event` fields (if applicable).
7. Enter a brief description of the event in the `event description` field (if applicable).
8. Enter the event location in the `event location` field (if applicable).
9. If applicable, add an emergency contact's name, email, phone, and relationship in the corresponding fields.
10. Confirm the participant's signature by selecting one of the options in the `participant signature` field.
11. Enter the date and time of signature in the `participant signature date` and `participant signature time` fields (if applicable).
12. Add any additional notes about the participant's participation in the `participant notes` field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's full name as it appears on their ID or business card.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the participant's organization, if applicable.
* **Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the participant's contact person.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the participant's contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the participant's contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant's contact person.
* **Participant Attendance**: Confirm the participant's attendance to the event by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Date of Event**: Enter the date of the event the participant is attending (if applicable).
* **Time of Event**: Enter the time of the event the participant is attending (if applicable).
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event the participant is attending (if applicable).
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event the participant is attending (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the participant's emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the participant's emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the participant's emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant's emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the participant and their emergency contact person.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the participant's participation in the event.
* **Participant Signature**: Confirm the participant's signature by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Participant Signature Date**: Enter the date the participant signed the agreement (if applicable).
* **Participant Signature Time**: Enter the time the participant signed the agreement (if applicable).
* **Participant Signature Note**: Enter any additional notes about the participant's signature.
* **Participant Agreement**: Confirm the participant's agreement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Event Date**: Confirm the event date by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Event Time**: Confirm the event time by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the event organizers for clarification.
* This form is not a substitute for other registration processes, such as in-person registration.
