# Butcher Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from competitors about their qualifications, experience, and preferences for the Butcher Competition. Completing this form will help the competition organizers assess your suitability for the competition and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information (pages 1-3)
	* Competitor Name: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on your competition materials.
	* Email Address: Enter your contact email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
2. Provide details about your butchering experience (pages 4-5)
	* Business or Shop Name: Enter the name of your butcher shop or business.
	* Business Address: Enter your street address (optional).
	* City: Enter your city (optional).
	* State or Province: Enter your state or province (optional).
	* Experience Level: Choose how many years you have been butchering.
	* Specialty: Describe your area of expertise or specialty.
3. Choose your competition category (page 6)
	* Competition Category: Select the category in which you want to compete.
4. Add any prior competition experience (page 7)
	* Previous Competition Experience: Select how many times you have competed before.
	* Prior Competition Awards or Recognitions: Describe any awards or recognitions you have received.
5. Choose your preferred competition date (page 8)
	* Preferred Competition Date: Select the date you would like to compete.
6. Confirm your registration fee (page 9)
	* Registration Fee Confirmation: Acknowledge receipt of fee details.
7. Specify any special requirements (page 10)
	* Special Equipment or Tools Needed: Describe any special equipment or tools you need.
8. Provide dietary restrictions or allergies (page 11)
	* Dietary Restrictions or Allergies: List any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
9. Agree to competition rules and code of conduct (page 12)
	* Rules and Judging Criteria: Acknowledge that you understand and agree to the competition rules.
	* Code of Professional Conduct: Acknowledge that you understand and agree to the code of professional conduct.
10. Team Members (page 13)
	* Team Members (if applicable): List the names of other team members (if applicable).
11. Add any additional comments (page 14)
	* Additional Comments or Questions: Provide any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competitor Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on your competition materials.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Business or Shop Name** (4, text, required): Enter the name of your butcher shop or business.
* **Business Address** (5, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (6, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (7, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **Experience Level** (8, select_one, required): Choose how many years you have been butchering.
* **Competition Category** (9, select_one, required): Select the category in which you want to compete.
* **Your Specialty or Expertise** (10, text, optional): Describe your area of expertise or specialty.
* **Previous Competition Experience** (11, select_one, required): Select how many times you have competed before.
* **Prior Competition Awards or Recognitions** (12, text, optional): Describe any awards or recognitions you have received.
* **Preferred Competition Date** (13, date, optional): Select the date you would like to compete.
* **Registration Fee Confirmation** (14, select_one, required): Acknowledge receipt of fee details.
* **Special Equipment or Tools Needed** (15, text, optional): Describe any special equipment or tools you need.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (16, text, optional): List any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Rules and Judging Criteria** (17, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand and agree to the competition rules.
* **Code of Professional Conduct** (18, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand and agree to the code of professional conduct.
* **Team Members (if applicable)** (19, text, optional): List the names of other team members (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (20, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
