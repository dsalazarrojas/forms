# sports_club_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration purposes of a sports club membership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by an *) with accurate and valid information.
2. Review and ensure that all the information is correct before submitting the form.
3. If you are reapplying for membership, please select the "Rejoining Member" option for the membership type field.
4. For "membership_start_date" and "membership_end_date", use the correct date format (dd/mm/yyyy).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **email**: Enter your email address that you check regularly. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **membership_type**: Choose your membership type (Member, New Member, Rejoining Member).
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **address**: Enter your current address.
* **emergency_contact_details**: Enter details about the person you want to contact in case of emergencies.
* **emergency_contact_email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Enter the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **membership_status**: Choose your current membership status (Active, Inactive, Cancelled).
* **membership_start_date**: Enter the date you started your membership.
* **membership_end_date**: Enter the date your membership ended or will end.
* **membership_status_reason**: Enter the reason for your current membership status.
* **membership_type_description**: Enter a description of your membership type.
* **emergency_contact_relationship_description**: Enter a description of your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **membership_start_date_note**: Enter any additional information about your membership start date.
* **membership_end_date_note**: Enter any additional information about your membership end date.
* **membership_status_note**: Enter any additional information about your membership status.
* **emergency_contact_details_note**: Enter any additional information about the emergency contact details.
* **membership_type**: Choose your membership type (Member, New Member, Rejoining Member).
* **membership_category**: Choose your membership category (Full, Social, Trial).
* **membership_fee_paid**: Choose whether you have paid your membership fee (Paid, Not Paid, Partially Paid).
* **membership_fee**: Enter the membership fee you have paid.

Please review the form carefully and fill it out accurately to ensure that your membership information is up-to-date and correct. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the sports club administration for assistance.
