# Church Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for the Church Conference. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Attendee Information: Enter your name, email address, phone number, and position/role in the Church.
2. Select your Registration Type: Choose the type of registration that applies to you (Full Conference, One Day Pass, Student/Senior, or Volunteer).
3. Choose your Breakout Sessions: Select up to three topics of interest for the breakout sessions.
4. Provide Dietary Requirements: Select your dietary requirements for the provided lunch.
5. Travel and Accommodation: If you need hotel recommendation/booking information, select "Yes" and we will provide you with details.
6. Accessibility Needs: If you have any specific accessibility needs, such as wheelchair access or sign language interpretation, please let us know.
7. Referral Source: Tell us how you heard about this conference (Social Media, Email Invitation, Word of Mouth, Worship Bulletin, or Denominational Newsletter).
8. Other Comments or Special Requests: If you have any other comments or special requests, please include them here.
9. Agreement to Terms: Finally, please agree to the conference terms and cancellation policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Attendee Name)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for conference updates and digital materials.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Home Church / Organization**: Enter your home church or organization name (optional).
* **Role/Position**: Enter your role or position in the Church.
* **Registration Type**: Select the type of registration that applies to you (Full Conference, One Day Pass, Student/Senior, or Volunteer).
* **Preferred Breakout Sessions**: Select up to three topics of interest for the breakout sessions.
* **Dietary Requirements for Provided Lunch**: Select your dietary requirements (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten Free, Nut Allergy, Dairy Free).
* **Do you require hotel recommendation/booking info?**: Select "Yes" if you need hotel recommendation/booking information.
* **Specific Accessibility Needs**: If you have any specific accessibility needs, enter them here.
* **How did you hear about this conference?**: Select how you heard about this conference (Social Media, Email Invitation, Word of Mouth, Worship Bulletin, or Denominational Newsletter).
* **Other Comments or Special Requests**: Enter any other comments or special requests you may have.
* **I agree to the conference terms and cancellation policy**: Select "I Agree" to agree to the conference terms and cancellation policy.
