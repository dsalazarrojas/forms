# Employee Integration Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register employees for an Employee Integration Workshop, a program designed to facilitate collaboration, innovation, and growth within our organization. This workshop aims to enhance employee engagement, productivity, and overall work experience. By registering participants, we can better plan and tailor the workshop content to meet the needs of attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To register for the Employee Integration Workshop, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" section. This includes your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field. This is a text field where you can input your name as it would be displayed in the workshop program.
3. Enter your participant email address in the "Participant Email" field. This is where we will contact you with workshop updates, reminders, and other important information.
4. In the "Participant Phone" field, enter your contact phone number. This is optional but helps us reach you in case of last-minute updates or cancellations.
5. Choose your session schedule by selecting a date for the workshop. Please note that you can only choose one date at a time.
6. Choose your session time slot for the workshop. Please select the time that works best for you.
7. Select your preferred workshop from the "Workshop Participants" dropdown menu. If you have a specific workshop in mind, please enter the title of the workshop you are interested in, or simply select one from the provided options.
8. If you have any additional workshop participants, type their names in the "Workshop Participants" field. Please separate multiple names with commas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This form is not currently being used for the "Participant Details" field. If you need to enter additional contact information, please do so at your discretion.
* **Participant Name**: Your name as it would be displayed in the workshop program.
* **Participant Email**: The email address we will use to contact you with workshop updates, reminders, and other important information.
* **Participant Phone**: Your contact phone number. Optional, but helpful for last-minute updates or cancellations.
* **Session Schedule**: The date you would like to attend the workshop. Please note that you can only choose one date.
* **Session Time**: The time slot you prefer for the workshop. Please select the time that works best for you.
* **Session Date**: The date of the workshop. This field is currently not being used for the "Session Date" field; instead, we will use the "Session Schedule" field to determine the date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Participants**: A dropdown menu with available workshop options. Choose the workshop that best suits your interests.
* **Workshop Participants (other)**: If you have any additional workshop participants, enter their names here, separated by commas.
