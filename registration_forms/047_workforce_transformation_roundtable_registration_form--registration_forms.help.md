# Workforce Transformation Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Workforce Transformation Roundtable event. Please fill out this form to provide us with your registration information and event preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct information.
2. Enter your name, organization, and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Select your role and interests related to the event.
4. Choose a payment method and terms that suit your needs.
5. Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your registration.
6. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information**: This field is where you enter your registration information. Please enter your name, organization, and email address to help us identify you.
* **Event Name**: This field is not required, but please enter the name of the event you are interested in attending.
* **Attendee Name**: This field is where you enter your name as it will be displayed during the event.
* **Role**: This field is where you select your role or position in the organization or industry related to the event topic.
* **Organization**: This field is where you enter the name of your organization.
* **Email**: This field is where you enter your email address. Please ensure it is valid and active.
* **Phone**: This field is optional, but please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Bio**: This field is optional, but please enter a brief bio or description of yourself.
* **Event Interests**: This field is where you select the topics or interests related to the event. Please choose one or more from the list.
* **Additional Information**: This field is optional, but please enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your registration.
* **Payment Information**: This field is where you enter any additional payment information.
* **Payment Method**: This field is where you select the method of payment you prefer (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Terms**: This field is where you select the payment terms you agree to (e.g., Net 30, Net 60, Payable Now).
* **Payment Amount**: This field is optional, but please enter the payment amount if applicable.
* **Confirm**: This field is where you confirm your registration by entering a confirmation code or message.

Note: Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
