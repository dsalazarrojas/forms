# urban_redevelopment_forum_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The urban redevelopment forum registration form is used to register events, activities, or initiatives related to urban renewal and development. This form allows you to share your events with the community and facilitate collaboration and discussion. Please note that this form is intended for legitimate uses only, and we reserve the right to review and approve submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have a clear event or activity in mind that you'd like to promote on the urban redevelopment forum.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, including:
	* Your name and contact email.
	* Event name and description.
	* Date and time of the event.
	* Venue and any other relevant details.
3. Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: <label> 
	+ A brief introduction about yourself or your organization.
* **Contact Email** (<type> email)
	+ The email address where you can be reached for more information or updates about the event.
* **Contact Phone** (<type> text)
	+ Your phone number for additional contact or discussion.
* **Event Name** (<type> text)
	+ A clear and concise name of the event or activity.
* **Event Date** (<type> date)
	+ The date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Event Time** (<type> time)
	+ The time of the event in 12-hour format (e.g., 2:00 PM).
* **Event Venue** (<type> text)
	+ The location where the event will take place.
* **Event Description** (<type> text)
	+ A brief description of the event, including its purpose, objectives, and any other relevant details.
Note that the form is not intended for spam or other non-legitimate activities. Please use it responsibly and with the understanding that your submissions may be reviewed and approved by the forum administrators.
