# Property Investment Accelerator Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential participants of the Property Investment Accelerator program. It helps the team to understand your investment goals, experience, and interests, allowing for a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in the "Registration Details" section with your basic contact information.
2. In the "Investment Goals" section, indicate whether you have investment goals for the program. Select "Yes" if you are interested in participating.
3. In the "Experience" section, select "Yes" if you have experience relevant to property investment, or "No" otherwise.
4. In the "Participant Information" section, indicate whether you have any participant information that is relevant to your investment goals.
5. In the "Additional Comments" section, enter any additional information that you believe will help us to better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details** (Registration Details): This is the first section of the form, which asks for your basic contact information. Enter your name, email address, and phone number to get started.
* **Investment Goals** (Investment Goals): Check "Yes" if you have investment goals for the program. This will help us understand your interests and tailor the experience for you.
* **Experience** (Experience): Check "Yes" if you have experience relevant to property investment. This will help us understand your background and provide relevant resources.
* **Participant Information** (Participant Information): Check "Yes" if you have participant information that is relevant to your investment goals. This will help us understand your situation and provide personalized support.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments): Enter any additional information that you think will help us understand your needs better.
