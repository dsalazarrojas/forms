# Summer Chess Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Chess Camp Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians to register their child(ren) for the summer chess camp. This form helps us ensure that we have all the necessary details to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about your child(ren).
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary details.
3. Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Chess Camp Form**: This is the title of the form, and it is not a field to be filled out.
* **Participant Info**: Please provide a brief description of your child(ren) participating in the camp.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your child's name.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your child's email address (if applicable).
* **Participant Phone Number**: Enter your child's phone number (if applicable).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your child's care in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions**: If your child has any medical conditions, please describe them briefly.
* **Allergies**: If your child has any allergies, please list them.
* **Special Needs**: If your child has any special needs, please describe them briefly.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you would like to use (bank transfer, paypal, or credit card).
* **Medical Release**: Indicate whether you grant permission for your child to participate in activities that may involve some risk (True/False).
* **Payment Terms**: Please read and agree to the payment terms (note field for text description).
* **Participant Info**: Enter any additional information about your child (if needed).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the camp or registration process.
* **Confirm Payment**: Indicate whether you have confirmed your payment (True/False).
