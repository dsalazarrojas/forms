# Summer School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer School Registration Form is designed to collect information from students registering for a summer school program. This form helps us gather essential details about students and their attendance to better understand their needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Summer School Student Details**: Fill out the fields as they are described below.
2. **Summer School Course Details**: Enter the details of the course you want to register for.
3. **Summer School Experience**: Share any relevant experience you have that may be helpful for us to know.
4. **Student Attendance Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the summer school program.
5. **Student Start Date**: Select the start date of your attendance.
6. **Student End Date**: Select the end date of your attendance.
7. **Student Attendance Time**: Specify the time you plan to attend the summer school program.
8. **Student Start Time**: Specify the start time of your attendance.
9. **Student End Time**: Specify the end time of your attendance.
10. **Student Attendance Notes**: Add any additional notes about your attendance.
11. **Student Start Notes**: Add any additional notes about your start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer School Student Details**: This is where you enter your personal details.
* **Summer School Course Details**: This field is for the course you want to register for.
* **Summer School Experience**: Share any relevant experience that may be helpful for us to know.
* **Student Attendance Date**: Select the date you will attend the summer school program.
* **Student Start Date**: This is the date when you start attending the summer school program.
* **Student End Date**: This is the date when you finish attending the summer school program.
* **Student Attendance Time**: Specify the time you plan to attend the summer school program.
* **Student Start Time**: This is the time when you start attending the summer school program.
* **Student End Time**: This is the time when you finish attending the summer school program.
* **Student Attendance Notes**: Add any additional notes about your attendance.
* **Student Start Notes**: Add any additional notes about your start date.
