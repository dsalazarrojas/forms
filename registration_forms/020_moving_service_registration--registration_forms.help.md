# Moving Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to register their moving service with the company. It includes essential information about the customer and the service details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your customer details, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Provide your moving information, such as the type of move and any specific details about the move.
3. Enter the date and start and end times for the move.
4. Choose the type of move (local or long distance).
5. Select the pickup details (address or contact) and any other relevant details.
6. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
7. Provide your address for the moving service.
8. Enter any additional message or notes about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**:
	+ **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Moving Information**:
	+ **Please provide your moving details**: Enter a brief description of your move, including any specific details about the service you need.
* **Move Date**:
	+ **Move Date**: Enter the date of the move in the required format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Start Time**:
	+ **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the move in the required format (HH:MM).
* **End Time**:
	+ **End Time**: Enter the end time of the move in the required format (HH:MM).
* **Move Type**:
	+ **Move Type**: Select the type of move (Local or Long distance).
* **Pickup Details**:
	+ **Pickup Address**: Select this option if you want to specify the pickup address.
	+ **Pickup Contact**: Select this option if you want to specify the pickup contact.
* **Contact**:
	+ **Contact**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**:
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**:
	+ **Address**: Enter your address for the moving service.
* **Message**:
	+ **Message**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the service.
