# Talent Hunt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Hunt Form is a registration form used to gather information from job seekers and candidates who are interested in job opportunities. It is used by the talent acquisition team to collect relevant details about the candidate, their job requirements and job status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" with your accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that the information provided is correct.
3. Use the available options for fields with multiple options (e.g., select_one, select_multiple).
4. Upload any required files according to the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Registration** (text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter a brief title of the job you are seeking.
* **Job Category** (select_one, optional): Select the category of the job you are seeking (e.g., 'Yes' for active job, 'No' for inactive job).
* **Job Location** (text, optional): Enter the location where you are looking for the job.
* **Job Description** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job you are seeking.
* **Job Requirements** (text, optional): Enter the job requirements.
* **User Phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **User Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Job Status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of the job (e.g., 'Active' for active job, 'Inactive' for inactive job).
* **Job Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of the job (e.g., 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no).
* **Job Tags** (text, optional): Enter any tags related to the job.
* **Job Tags 2** (text, optional): Enter any additional job tags.
* **Upload File** (text, optional): Upload any required file for the job.
* **Upload File 2** (text, optional): Upload another required file for the job.
* **Upload File 3** (text, optional): Upload a third required file for the job.
* **User Address** (text, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **User Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about yourself.
* **Job Date** (date, optional): Enter the job date.
* **Job Time** (time, optional): Enter the job time.
* **Job Status 2** (select_one, optional): Select the status of the job (e.g., 'Active' for active job, 'Inactive' for inactive job).
* **Job Type 2** (select_one, optional): Select the type of the job (e.g., 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no).
* **User Phone 2** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **User Email 2** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **User Email 3** (email, optional): Enter another email address.
* **User Email 4** (email, optional): Enter a fourth email address.

Note: All fields are optional, however it's recommended to fill in as much information as possible to help the talent acquisition team better understand your job requirements and qualifications.
