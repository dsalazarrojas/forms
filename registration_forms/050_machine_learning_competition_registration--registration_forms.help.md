# machine-learing-competition-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage participant data for a machine learning competition. It is intended to gather demographic and contact information from each participant, as well as details about their submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field. This is required to verify your identity.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Participant Address" field.
5. Enter any secondary email addresses in the "Participant Email 2" field, if applicable.
6. Enter your website, if you have one, in the "Participant Website" field.
7. Upload a profile picture or image in the "Participant Image" field.
8. Provide any additional notes about yourself in the "Participant Notes" field.
9. Select your category in the "Participant Category" field.
10. Enter your submission ID, if you have been assigned one, in the "Participant Submission ID" field.
11. Select your submission status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Submission Status" field.
12. If applicable, enter your start and end dates and times in the "Participant Start Date", "Participant End Date", "Participant Start Time", and "Participant End Time" fields.
13. If applicable, enter your arrival and departure dates and times in the "Participant Arrival Date", "Participant Departure Date", "Participant Arrival Time", and "Participant Departure Time" fields.
14. If applicable, enter your duration of activity in the "Participant Duration" field.
15. If applicable, enter the distance traveled in the "Participant Distance Traveled" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competition Name**: The name of the machine learning competition you are participating in.
* **Participant Name**: Your name.
* **Participant Email**: Your email address. This is required to verify your identity.
* **Participant Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Participant Address**: Your address.
* **Participant Email 2**: A secondary email address, if you have one.
* **Participant Website**: Your website, if you have one.
* **Participant Image**: A profile picture or image associated with you.
* **Participant Notes**: Any additional notes or comments about yourself.
* **Participant Category**: Your category or team in the competition.
* **Participant Submission ID**: The ID of your submission, if assigned.
* **Participant Status**: The status of your submission, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Participant Start Date**: The start date of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant End Date**: The end date of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant Start Time**: The start time of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant End Time**: The end time of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant Date**: The date of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant Time**: The time of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant Departure Date**: The departure date of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant Departure Time**: The departure time of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant Arrival Date**: The arrival date of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant Arrival Time**: The arrival time of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant Duration**: The duration of your activity, if applicable.
* **Participant Distance Traveled**: The distance traveled during your activity, if applicable.
* **Submission Status**: The status of your submission, either "Active" or "Inactive".

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled in for the form to be submitted. Optional fields are not required but may provide additional context or information for the competition administrators.
