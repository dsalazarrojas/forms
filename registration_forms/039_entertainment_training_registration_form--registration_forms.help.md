# Entertainment Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Training Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from users for events, workshops, or courses related to entertainment. Please complete this form to register for such training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: 
   * `first_name`: Please enter your first name.
   * `last_name`: Enter your last name.
   * `email`: Provide your email address.
   * `phone`: Enter your phone number.

2. Select the training type:
   * `training_type`: Choose whether you are attending a `workshop`, `class`, or `course`.

3. Schedule your training:
   * `start_date`: Enter the start date of your training event.
   * `end_date`: Enter the end date of your training event.
   * `end_time`: Enter the end time of your training event.

4. Specify your location and preferences:
   * `location`: Choose where you will be attending the training (`Online`, `In Person`, or `Both`).

5. Add any additional notes:
   * `notes`: Enter any comments or additional information about your training.

6. Assign and confirm:
   * `submitted_by`: Confirm who submitted this form (this will be filled in by default).
   * `submitted_date`: Enter the date you submitted this form (this will be filled in by default).
   * `assigned_to`: Select the user to whom this form will be assigned.
   * `assigned_date`: Enter the date the form was assigned.

* **form_id**: This is the unique identifier for the form, which should be filled in to ensure you're using the correct form or to identify the form within the system. **Required: False**
* **first_name**: Enter your first name. **Required: True**
* **last_name**: Enter your last name. **Required: True**
* **email**: Provide your email address. **Required: True**
* **phone**: Enter your phone number. **Required: True**
* **training_type**: Choose the type of training you are attending (`workshop`, `class`, or `course`). **Required: True**
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of your training event. **Required: True**
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of your training event. **Required: True**
* **end_time**: Enter the end time of your training event. **Required: True**
* **location**: Choose where you will be attending the training (`Online`, `In Person`, or `Both`). **Required: True**
* **notes**: Enter any comments or additional information about your training. **Required: False**
* **submitted_by**: This field is automatically filled in by the system and should not be edited. **Required: True**
* **submitted_date**: This field is automatically filled in by the system and should not be edited. **Required: False**
* **assigned_to**: Select the user to whom this form will be assigned. **Required: True**
* **assigned_date**: Enter the date the form was assigned. **Required: True**
