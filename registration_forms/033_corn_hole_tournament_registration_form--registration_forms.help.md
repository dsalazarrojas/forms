# corn-hole-tournament-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participant registration in a corn hole tournament. Fill it out with your details to register your team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it is to be printed on the tournament program and any marketing materials.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter your team name as it will be displayed on the tournament scoreboard and website.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (yyyy-mm-dd or mm-dd-yy).
* **Player Roles** (`player_roles`, select_one, optional): Choose a role for your player.
*   • **Role 1** (`player_roles_1`, text, optional): Enter details for the first role.
*   • **Role 2** (`player_roles_2`, text, optional): Enter details for the second role.
*   • **Role 3** (`player_roles_3`, text, optional): Enter details for the third role.
*   • **Role 4** (`player_roles_4`, text, optional): Enter details for the fourth role.
*   • **Role 5** (`player_roles_5`, text, optional): Enter details for the fifth role.
*   • **Role 6** (`player_roles_6`, text, optional): Enter details for the sixth role.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication with the tournament organizers and other teams.

## Tips
* If you have more than six players, you can leave the last two fields (Role 5 and Role 6) blank.
* Ensure your email address is accurate to receive important updates and instructions about the tournament.
