# Venture Capital Investor Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for venture capital investors who want to partner with the company. It will help us understand your investment preferences, type of investment, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your investor type from the options provided.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Investor Type**: Choose one of the options that best describes your type of investment (e.g., Private Equity, Angel Investor, Venture Capital Firms, etc.).
* **Investor Name**: Enter your full name or name of your representative.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you for any additional information.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any confusion or delay in our communication.
* If you are representing a company or organization, please enter the company name in the investor name field.
