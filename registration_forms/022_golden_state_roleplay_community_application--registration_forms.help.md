# golden_state_roleplay_community_application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Golden State roleplay community is a platform for people to engage in roleplaying activities, share ideas, and build relationships. This form is designed to help us get to know you and your character better, so we can provide a great experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information about yourself and your character.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *) as these are essential for us to understand your character's profile.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance from our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Register** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you enter your username to access the platform.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your real name, which will not be displayed publicly.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address for contact and notification purposes.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Roleplay Experience** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Share your roleplay experience, including your level of involvement and favorite activities.
* **Character Name** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your character's name in the roleplay world.
* **Character Type** (`7`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose between "Human" and "Non-Human" to describe your character's nature.
* **Character Age** (`8`, `number`, required: false): Enter your character's age in the roleplay world.
* **Character Gender** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or more from "Male", "Female", and "Other" to describe your character's gender.
* **Character Race** (`10`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose between "Human" and "Non-Human" to describe your character's race.
* **Roleplay History** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Share your roleplay history, including notable stories or achievements.
* **Roleplay Frequency** (`12`, `select_one`, required: true): Select how often you engage in roleplay activities, from "Daily" to "Rarely".
* **Character Concept** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Describe your character's concept or theme.
* **Character Concept Description** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your character's concept.
* **Why Join** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Explain why you'd like to join the Golden State roleplay community.
* **Character Picture** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Upload a photo or image of your character.
* **Submit** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Review and submit your application for review.
* **Roleplay Group** (`18`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose between "Golden State" or "Other" to select your roleplay group affiliation.
* **Roleplay Group Other** (`19`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "Other", provide the name of your roleplay group.
* **Roleplay Group Description** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Describe your roleplay group, including its purpose and goals.
* **Roleplay Group Address** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Enter your roleplay group's address or location.
* **Roleplay Group Phone** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Provide your roleplay group's phone number.
