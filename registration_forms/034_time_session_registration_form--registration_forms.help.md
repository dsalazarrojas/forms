# Time Session Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register and schedule a time session for a meeting, appointment, or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the information required.
2. Enter your event details (event name, date, time, location).
3. Provide your participant details (name, email, phone number).
4. Add any additional details you want to share about the event.
5. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the event, including its purpose and nature.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `2023-07-01`).
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` (e.g., `02:00 PM`).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event (e.g., "Summer Camp" or "Conference 2024").
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the participant, such as their role or position.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the event (e.g., "Conference Room A" or "Online").
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information you want to share about the event.
