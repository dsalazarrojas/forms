# Community Book Club RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help members of the community book club confirm their attendance and provide necessary information about their upcoming meeting. It will help the club organizer track attendance, plan for refreshments, and make sure everyone is informed about the meeting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Confirm your attendance**: Check the box next to "Will you be attending" to indicate whether you will be attending the meeting or not.
2. **Provide RSVP Information**: Fill in the required fields, such as your name, email, and phone number.
3. **Choose your membership status**: Select your current membership status from the options provided.
4. **Choose the meeting location**: Select where the meeting will be held from the options provided.
5. **Provide meeting details**: If you're attending, provide the meeting date and time, and if you're bringing guests, enter their names and numbers.
6. **Choose refreshment options**: Indicate whether you'll be bringing refreshments to share.
7. **Add any additional comments or questions**: If you have any other comments or questions, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Information**: This is the main section where you'll confirm your attendance and provide your contact details.
	+ **RSVP Date**: Enter the date of this RSVP.
	+ **RSVP ID**: Enter a unique reference number for this RSVP.
* **Member Information**: Enter your contact details, such as your full name, email, and phone number.
* **Membership Status**: Select your current membership status from the options provided.
* **Meeting Information**: If you're attending, provide the meeting date and time. If you're bringing guests, enter their names and numbers.
* **Meeting Location**: Select where the meeting will be held from the options provided.
* **Meeting Location Details**: Enter the specific address or link to the meeting location if it's not a standard location.
* **Current Book**: Enter the title of the current book being discussed.
* **Attendance**: Check the box indicating whether you'll be attending or not.
* **Bringing Guests**: Check the box indicating whether you'll be bringing guests or not.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests you'll be bringing, if any.
* **Guest Names**: Enter the names of your guests, if applicable.
* **Refreshments**: Check the box indicating whether you'll bring refreshments to share.
* **Refreshment Details**: Enter what you'll bring for refreshments, if you've chosen to bring some.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Book Suggestions**: Enter any book suggestions for future meetings.
* **Discussion Topics**: Enter any discussion topics you'd like to cover during the meeting.
* **Volunteering**: Check the box indicating whether you'd like to help organize future meetings.
* **Hosting Interest**: Check the box indicating whether you'd be willing to host a future meeting.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other comments or questions you have.

Note: The required fields are marked as such in the form, and the optional fields can be filled in as needed.
