# Community Defense Signal Group Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Defense Signal Group Sign Up form collects information from individuals interested in joining the Signal group to ensure a smooth and effective group experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields to provide accurate contact and personal information.
2. Select the relevant Signal group category that interests you the most.
3. Agree to the group's guidelines and terms of use.
4. Provide a brief explanation of your background and skills that would make you a good fit for the group.
5. Indicate how you heard about this form.
6. Provide an emergency contact person and their contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of User** (`user_full_name_sig`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be used in the Signal group.
* **Email Address** (`user_email_sig`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and results from the group.
* **Signal Phone Number** (`user_signal_phone_sig`, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`user_dob_sig`, date, required): Enter your date of birth to help the group tailor its activities to your needs.
* **Residential Address** (`user_address_sig`, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Primary Signal Group Interest** (`primary_signal_group_interest_sig`, select_one, required): Select the category that interests you the most, such as Neighborhood Safety or Mutual Aid/Support.
* **Agree to Signal Group's Guidelines** (`signal_group_guideline_agreement_sig`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the group's terms of use.
* **Key Skills and Expertise** (`key_skills_expertise_sig`, text, required): Describe your skills and expertise that would be beneficial to the group.
* **Previous Signal Group Experience** (`previous_signal_group_experience_sig`, text, required): Share any experience you have in similar communities or Signal groups.
* **Reason for Joining Signal Group** (`reason_for_joining_signal_group_sig`, text, required): Explain why you are interested in joining the Signal group.
* **Digital Signature** (`user_signature_digital_sig`, text, required): Confirm that the provided information is accurate and true.
* **Date of Sign Up** (`submission_date_final_sig`, date, required): Date and time of form submission.
* **Newsletter Opt-in** (`newsletter_opt_in_sig`, select_one, required): Choose to receive the community center's newsletter.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source_sig`, select_one, optional): Share how you heard about this form.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_full_name_sig`, text, required): Provide an emergency contact person's name and their contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_sig`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
