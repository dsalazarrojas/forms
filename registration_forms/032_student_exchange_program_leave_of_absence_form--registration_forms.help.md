# Student Exchange Program Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by students to submit leave of absence requests for the Student Exchange Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have read and understood the purpose of this form.
2. Fill out all the required fields, indicated by a red star (\*).
3. Select the relevant options for each field based on your situation.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (page 1): This is a note field that provides additional context to the form.
* **Student Information** (page 2): Enter your personal details such as name or ID number.
* **Leave Type** (page 3): Select the reason for your leave from the options provided (Medical Leave, Personal Leave, Academic Leave).
* **Start Date** (page 4): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (page 5): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason** (page 6): Provide a brief description of your reason for leave.
* **Contact Number** (page 7): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
* **University Name** (page 8): Select your affiliated university from the options provided (University A, University B, University C).
* **Student ID** (page 9): Enter your student ID number.
* **Submitted By** (page 10): Select the user who submitted the form from the options provided (User 1, User 2, User 3).
* **Submitted On** (page 11): Enter the date the form was submitted.

Note: Some fields may have additional guidance in the hints or options provided.
