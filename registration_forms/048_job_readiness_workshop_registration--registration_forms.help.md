# Job Readiness Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary details from you to register for the Job Readiness Workshop. It's crucial to provide accurate information to ensure we can keep you informed about the workshop schedule and any updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "participant_name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your email address in the "participant_email" field. Please use a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. In the "participant_phone" field, enter your phone number. This will help us reach out to you in case of any updates.
4. If you're registering on behalf of your company, please enter the company name in the "participant_company" field.
5. If you're registering for a specific job title, enter it in the "participant_jobtitle" field. This is optional.
6. If you're registering as a participant who is a manager or representative of a company, please enter your name in the "participant_managername" field and the company name in the "participant_managervia" field. These fields are optional.
7. Indicate whether you will be attending the workshop alone or with attendees in the "participant_attendees" field. Please select 'Yes' if you'll be attending with others.
8. Choose the date you plan to attend the workshop in the "participant_date" field.
9. Select the time slot you prefer to attend the workshop in the "participant_time" field.
10. In the "participant_notes" field, provide any additional comments or details you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name**: A required field to capture your name.
* **participant_email**: A required field to enter your valid email address.
* **participant_phone**: A required field to enter your phone number.
* **participant_company**: An optional field for your company name if you're registering on behalf of your company.
* **participant_jobtitle**: An optional field to enter your job title.
* **participant_role**: An optional field to enter your role.
* **participant_managername**: An optional field to enter your name if you're registering as a manager or representative.
* **participant_managervia**: An optional field to enter the company name you represent.
* **participant_attendees**: A required field to indicate if you'll be attending with others.
* **participant_date**: A required field to select the workshop date.
* **participant_time**: A required field to select the workshop time slot.
* **participant_notes**: An optional field for any additional comments or details.
