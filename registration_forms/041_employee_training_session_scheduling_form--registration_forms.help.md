# Employee Training Session Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for employees to schedule their training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the employees participating in the training session.
2. Choose the training date and time that works best for you.
3. Choose the training duration you'd like to attend.
4. Provide a date that you are available to attend the training session.
5. Select the topics you'd like to cover during the training session.
6. Choose where you'd like to attend the training session (office, online, or remote).
7. Select the training instructor you'd like to attend.
8. Add any comments you have about the training session.
9. Add any additional notes you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Selection**: Select all employees participating in the training session.
* **Training Session Details**: Choose the date and time of the training session.
	+ Note: The available dates for the training session are '2023-02-20', '2023-02-27', and '2023-03-13'.
* **Training Start Time**: Choose the start time of the training session.
	+ Available options: 9:00 am, 10:00 am, 11:00 am, 12:00 pm, 1:00 pm.
* **Training Duration**: Choose the duration of the training session.
	+ Available options: 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours.
* **Employee Availability**: Provide a date that you are available to attend the training session.
* **Training Topics**: Select the topics you'd like to cover during the training session.
	+ Note: You can select multiple topics.
* **Training Location**: Choose where you'd like to attend the training session.
	+ Available options: Office, Online, Remote.
* **Training Instructor**: Select the training instructor you'd like to attend.
* **Employee Comments**: Add any comments you'd like to share about the training session.
* **Employee Notes**: Add any additional notes you'd like to share.
* **Training Session ID**: This is a unique ID assigned to each training session.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not currently used.

**Tips**:

* Make sure to select all relevant employees participating in the training session.
* Double-check the training date and time before submitting the form.
* If you need to cover multiple topics, make sure to select all relevant topics.
* Choose the training location that best suits your needs.
* Ensure you select the correct training instructor.
* Use the comments and notes sections to provide any additional information about the training session.
* The training session ID is for internal use only.
