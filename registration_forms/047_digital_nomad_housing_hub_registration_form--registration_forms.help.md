# Digital Nomad Housing Hub Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for digital nomads registering for the Housing Hub service. It gathers essential information about you to provide tailored support and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out your address, which is the location you'd like to live in while using the Housing Hub service.
- Fill out your contact details, including phone and email address.
- Enter your name and any other information required for your employment or visa status.
- Select your job title, employment type, and visa type as applicable.
- Provide your experience in a specific area (optional).
- List any languages you speak.
- Choose your preferred communication language.
- Read and provide any notice or comments you'd like to add (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Address**: Enter your home or work address where you'd like to live.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
  * Type: email
  * Required: false
- **Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Location**: Enter the location you'd like to live in.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Job Title**: Select your job title for our records.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Employment Type**: Select your employment status (Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Writer, etc.).
  * Type: select_one
  * Options: Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Writer, Artist
  * Required: false
- **Experience**: Enter your experience in a specific area (optional).
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
- **Languages**: List any languages you speak.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Visa Type**: Select your visa status (Tourist, Work, Study, etc.).
  * Type: select_one
  * Options: Tourist, Work, Study, Other
  * Required: false
- **Nationality**: Enter your nationality.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false
- **Time Zone**: Select your time zone.
  * Type: time
  * Required: false
- **Available Time**: Enter your available time for meetings and consultations.
  * Type: time
  * Required: false
- **Preferred Communication**: Select your preferred communication language.
  * Type: select_one
  * Options: English, Spanish, French
  * Required: false
- **Notice**: Enter any additional notice or comments.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
- **Notice 2**: Enter any additional notice or comments (optional).
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
- **Notice 3**: Enter any additional notice or comments (optional).
  * Type: note
  * Required: false

Note: The fields marked as required are required to be filled out for the form to be valid. However, some fields may not be applicable or relevant to certain users, and it's up to the user to fill them out accurately and honestly.
