# Preventive Medicine Innovation Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preventive Medicine Innovation Summit Registration form is a tool for gathering essential information from attendees about their participation in the summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select your attendance type from the list provided (Healthcare Professional or Innovator).
2.  Enter your name in the designated field.
3.  Provide your email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and information.
4.  Enter your phone number for any follow-up conversations.
5.  If necessary, provide any additional key details about your participation in the summit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendance Type**: Choose the category that best describes your role or focus area at the summit.
* **Name**: Please enter your full name as you want to be addressed during the event.
* **Email**: Enter the email address we will use to send you updates, announcements, and information.
* **Phone**: Add your phone number for any follow-up conversations or in case we need to reach you urgently.
* **Key Details (if applicable)**: If you need to provide specific details about your participation, please enter them here. This field is meant for any additional information that does not fit into the other fields.
