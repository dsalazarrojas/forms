# B2B Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for B2B (Business-to-Business) registration. It is intended for companies or business entities to provide their contact information and details for various business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by filling out the required fields, marked as "true" in the table below.
2.  Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are accurately filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company name** (1, `text`, required): Enter the legal name of your company.
*   **Contact person** (2, `text`, required): Fill in the name of the primary contact person for your company.
*   **Contact email** (3, `email`, required): Enter your company's business email address.
*   **Contact phone number** (4, `text`, optional): Provide your direct line phone number, if applicable.
*   **Business type** (5, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best describes your business type from the options provided.
*   **Company website** (6, `text`, optional): Enter your company's website address, if applicable.
*   **VAT or tax identification number** (7, `text`, optional): If your company has a VAT or tax identification number, enter it here.
*   **Agreement to terms and conditions** (8, `select_one`, required): Indicate your acceptance of the terms and conditions by selecting "True".
*   **Additional notes** (9, `text`, optional): Provide any additional relevant details or notes regarding your company.
*   **Registration date** (10, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this registration.

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are filled to complete the submission process.
*   Double-check that all information entered is accurate and up-to-date.

This help guide is intended to guide you through the process of completing the B2B Registration Form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
