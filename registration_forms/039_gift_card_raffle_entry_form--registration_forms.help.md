# Gift Card Raffle Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for submitting entries for a gift card raffle. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to participate in the raffle.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your personal information in the "Submitter Info" section.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your middle name (if applicable) in the "Middle Name" field.
4. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
8. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
9. Enter the name of the raffle you are participating in in the "Raffle Name" field.
10. Enter the value of the gift card you are interested in in the "Gift Card Value" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Info (submitter_info)**: Enter your name and any other relevant information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your first name.
* **Middle Name (middle_name)**: Enter your middle name (if you have one). If you don't have a middle name, you can leave this field blank.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted if you win.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date Of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address (address)**: Enter your home or office address where you can be contacted or mailed.
* **Raffle Name (raffle_name)**: Enter the name of the raffle you are participating in.
* **Gift Card Value (gift_card_value)**: Enter the value of the gift card you are interested in winning.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Check that you haven't already submitted an entry before.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the organizers of the raffle.
