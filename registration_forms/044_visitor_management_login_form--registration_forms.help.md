# Visitor Management Login Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect visitor management data for a company or organization. It is used for logging visitor information, such as first and last name, email, phone number, company, visitor type, department, visit date and time, and any additional notes. The form is essential for keeping accurate records and providing a smooth experience for visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field, following the standard email format (e.g., [username]@domain.com).
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field, including the country code.
5. Enter the name of your company in the **Company** field (if applicable).
6. Select your visitor type from the **Visitor Type** dropdown menu:
	* **Company**: For guests or staff visiting on behalf of a company.
	* **Guest**: For individual visitors.
	* **Staff**: For employees or personnel visiting from the organization.
7. Enter the department you will be visiting in the **Department** field (if applicable).
8. Enter the date of your visit in the **Date** field using the date format MM/DD/YY.
9. Enter the time of your visit in the **Time** field using the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
10. Add any additional notes in the **Note** field if necessary.
