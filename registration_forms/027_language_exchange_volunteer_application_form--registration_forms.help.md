# language_exchange_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in language exchange programs as volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Please fill in your First Name on the first page.
- Next, fill in your Last Name.
- Enter your Email address.
- Provide your Contact Number.
- Select the language you are proficient in from the list.
- Choose the language(s) you are interested in learning/teaching.
- Indicate the number of hours you are available to volunteer per week.
- Select a date when you are available to volunteer.
- If you have had previous experience in language exchange programs, select "True". If not, select "False".
- If you have any references, please provide them in the notes field.
- Finally, add any additional information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`language_exchange_volunteer_info`, text, required): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide your contact number.
* **What is your native language?** (`language_proficiency_level`, select one, required): Select the language you are proficient in from the list: English, Spanish, French, Chinese, Arabic.
* **Which language(s) do you want to learn/teach?** (`language_interests`, select multiple, required): Choose the language(s) you are interested in learning/teaching.
* **How many hours per week are you available to volunteer?** (`availability`, number, required): Indicate the number of hours you are available to volunteer per week.
* **When are you available to volunteer?** (`availability_time`, date, required): Select a date when you are available to volunteer.
* **Have you had any previous experience in language exchange programs?** (`language_exchange_volunteer_experience`, select one, required): If you have had previous experience, select "True", otherwise select "False".
* **Please provide 2-3 references** (`language_exchange_volunteer_references`, note, required): Provide 2-3 references for the organization to contact.
* **Additional info about you** (`language_exchange_volunteer_additional_info`, text, required): Add any additional information about yourself.
