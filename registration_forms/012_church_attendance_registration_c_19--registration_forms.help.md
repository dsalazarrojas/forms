# Church Attendance Registration C-19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals attending a church event during the COVID-19 pandemic. The information gathered will be used for contact tracing and to ensure the well-being of all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section of the form accurately and truthfully.
2. Answer all required fields (marked as "true" in the form).
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask a church representative for clarification.
4. Once you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This is a required field.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. This is a required field.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional). This can help us contact you if needed.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (optional). This can help us understand any age-related factors.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event you're attending. This is a required field.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event you're attending (optional). This can help us plan the event schedule.
* **Attendance Status**: Choose how you plan to attend the event:
	+ **Present**: You will attend the event.
	+ **Absent**: You will not attend the event.
	+ **Late**: You will arrive late to the event.
	+ **Excused**: You have a valid reason for not attending (please provide details in the "Additional Information" section).
* **Vaccination Status**: Choose your vaccination status:
	+ **Fully vaccinated**: You have received all recommended COVID-19 vaccinations.
	+ **Partially vaccinated**: You have received some, but not all, recommended COVID-19 vaccinations.
	+ **Unvaccinated**: You have not received any COVID-19 vaccinations.
	+ **Prefer not to say**: You do not wish to disclose your vaccination status.
* **Do you have any COVID-19 symptoms?**: Answer:
	+ **No**: You do not have any COVID-19 symptoms.
	+ **Yes**: You have some COVID-19 symptoms.
	+ **Unsure**: You're unsure or do not know.
* **Have you had a fever in the last 24 hours?**: Answer:
	+ **No**: You have not had a fever.
	+ **Yes**: You have had a fever.
	+ **Unsure**: You're unsure or do not know.
* **Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19 in the last 2 weeks?**: Answer:
	+ **No**: You have not been exposed.
	+ **Yes**: You have been exposed.
	+ **Unsure**: You're unsure or do not know.
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 2 weeks?**: Answer:
	+ **No**: You have not tested positive.
	+ **Yes**: You have tested positive.
	+ **Unsure**: You're unsure or do not know.
* **Number of Household Members Attending**: Enter the number of household members attending the event (optional). This can help us plan for the event capacity.
* **Children Under 18 Attending**: Enter the number of children under 18 attending the event (optional). This can help us plan for child-friendly activities.
* **I consent to share my information for contact tracing if needed**: Choose:
	+ **Yes, I consent**: You agree to share your contact information for contact tracing purposes.
	+ **No, I do not consent**: You do not want to share your contact information.
* **I acknowledge my information will be kept confidential**: Choose:
	+ **Yes, I acknowledge**: You understand that your information will be kept confidential.
	+ **No**: You do not acknowledge this.
* **Any Accessibility or Accommodation Needs**: Enter any additional information about your accessibility needs or accommodations (optional). This can help us provide the best experience for you.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any question, ask a church representative for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you've answered all required fields correctly.
