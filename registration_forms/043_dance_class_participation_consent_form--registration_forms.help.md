# Dance Class Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from students and their parents or guardians for participation in dance classes. It gathers essential information for the safety and well-being of students during classes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the parent or guardian's name in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Provide the parent or guardian's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
4. Enter the student's date of birth (DOB) in the "Student DOB" field.
5. Select the student's class from the "Student Class" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the name of the emergency contact and their relationship with the student in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Relationship" fields.
7. List any medical conditions the student has in the "Medical Conditions" field.
8. List any allergies the student has in the "Allergies" field.
9. If applicable, enter the medical procedure the student requires in the "Medical Procedure" field.
10. Select the student's insurance provider from the "Insurance Provider" dropdown menu.
11. Choose multiple medical insurance options the student has from the "Medical Insurance" dropdown menu.
12. Select the person authorized to make medical decisions for the student from the "Signature" dropdown menu.
13. Write any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
14. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (text): Enter your name as a student.
* **Parent Name** (text): Enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent Phone** (text): Provide your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Student DOB** (date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Student Class** (select_one): Select the class you want to join.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (text): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (text): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Procedure** (text): If applicable, enter any medical procedures you require.
* **Insurance Provider** (select_one): Select your insurance provider.
* **Medical Insurance** (select_multiple): Choose multiple medical insurance options you have.
* **Signature** (select_multiple): Select the person authorized to make medical decisions for you.
* **Note** (note): Write any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
