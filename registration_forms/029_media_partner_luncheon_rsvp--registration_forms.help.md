# Media Partner Luncheon RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from media partners about their attendance at the Media Partner Luncheon. Please complete this form accurately to ensure that your details are captured correctly for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each page and fill out the relevant fields with your information.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Click the "Submit" button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_form_1`): This page is where you can enter your name and any other relevant guest information.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_form_2`): If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free), please specify them here.
* **Guest Count** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_form_3`): Please provide the number of guests you will be attending with.
* **Additional Information** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_form_4`): If you have any other relevant details or requests, please type them in here.
* **Success** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvpsuccess`): This is a success message that will display after you have submitted your form successfully.
