# Workshop Instructor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect workshop information from instructors for display on the workshop catalog page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Basic Info** page with your workshop title and any additional basic information you'd like to share.
2. On the **Workshop Info** page, provide any additional details about your workshop.
3. On the **Workshop Dates** page, select whether or not your workshop is available for registration.
4. Enter the **Workshop Duration** for your workshop in the required format.
5. On the **Workshop Type** page, select the type of workshop you are offering.
6. On the **Workshop Venue** page, choose whether or not you have a preferred workshop venue.
7. On the **Instructors** page, select which instructors will be leading your workshop.
8. On the **Instructor Info** page, provide any additional information about your instructors.
9. On the **Course Materials** page, provide any relevant materials that workshop attendees will need to bring or use during your workshop.
10. On the **Language** page, select the language of your workshop.
11. On the **Workshop Description** page, provide a brief description of your workshop.
12. On the **Outcomes** page, describe the learning outcomes of your workshop.
13. On the **Location** page, select the location of your workshop.
14. On the **Capacity** page, choose whether or not you have limited capacity for your workshop.
15. On the **Status** page, select the status of your workshop (active or inactive).
