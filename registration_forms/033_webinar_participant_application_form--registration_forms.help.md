# webinar_participant_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The webinar participant application form is a registration form for potential webinar participants to express their interest and provide more context for the webinar hosts or organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your **Company** information in the "Company" field.
6. Enter your **Department** in the "Department" field.
7. Enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field.
8. In the "Question" field, provide any relevant background or context regarding your interest in the webinar, such as your areas of expertise or any specific questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name (Text, Optional)**
* A field to provide your given name.
### **Last Name (Text, Optional)**
* A field to provide your surname.
### **Email (Email, Optional)**
* A field to provide your email address. This is where you will likely receive follow-up communication from the webinar hosts.
### **Phone (Text, Optional)**
* A field to provide your phone number. This can be helpful for the hosts to reach you more easily.
### **Company (Text, Optional)**
* A field to provide your company information. If you are affiliated with a company, you may want to provide this.
### **Department (Text, Optional)**
* A field to provide your department or team name if you are affiliated with a company.
### **Job Title (Text, Optional)**
* A field to provide your job title or position.
### **Question (Text, Optional)**
* A field to provide any background or context regarding your interest in the webinar. This can be a good opportunity to ask questions or share your expertise.
### **Tips**
* Please make sure to enter your information accurately and thoroughly so that the webinar hosts can best understand your interests and provide relevant content or support. 
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please do not hesitate to contact the hosts directly.
