# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration details for an event. It is designed to gather information from registrants to track their attendance, confirm their registration status, and provide them with a confirmation page after completing the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address and Mobile Phone number.
3. Provide your Organization and Position/Job title.
4. Enter the Event Title, Date, Start Time, and End Time.
5. Describe the Event in the provided space.
6. Select the Registration Type and choose the relevant options for the number of Registrants.
7. Confirm your Mobile Phone and Email address for verification purposes.
8. Confirm your Organization and Position/Job title.
9. Confirm the Event Title and Date.
10. Confirm the Start Time and End Time.
11. Confirm your Event Description.
12. Select the Registration Type again to confirm.
13. Confirm your Registrants.
14. If needed, add any Additional Information.
14. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to verify your registration.
* **Mobile Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required): Enter your mobile phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization** (`user_organization`, text, required): Enter your organization's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Position** (`user_position`, text, required): Enter your job title or position as it appears on official documents.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, required): Select the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`event_end_time`, time, required): Select the end time of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required): Describe the event in a few words.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of registration you are applying for.
* **Registrants** (`registrants`, select_multiple, required): Choose the number of registrants for this event.
