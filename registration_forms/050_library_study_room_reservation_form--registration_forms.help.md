# Library Study Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Study Room Reservation Form is used to reserve a study room in the library. Please use this form to request a study room for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the desired **Room Selection** option from the list provided. You can choose from the following options: Main Library, Annex, or Law Library.
2. Enter your **Start Date** and **End Date** in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Choose your preferred **Room Type** from the options provided. Please select Quiet Room, Group Study Room, or Study Room according to your needs.
4. Provide a brief **Comment** if you need any special arrangements.
5. Enter your **Name**, **Affiliation**, **Email**, and **Phone** as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Room Selection (Main Library, Annex, Law Library)**: Select the desired library location for your study room reservation.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you want to start using the study room in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you want to end using the study room in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Room Type (Quiet Room, Group Study Room, Study Room)**: Choose the room type that suits your needs. Quiet Room is ideal for solo study, Group Study Room is best for group work, and Study Room is a general-purpose room.
* **Comment**: Provide any additional information about your reservation or special requests.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you want it to be recorded in the library's records.
* **Affiliation (Student, Faculty)**: Select your affiliation with the university or institution.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your affiliation.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any communication related to your reservation.
* **Phone**: This field is optional and not required for reservation, but please note that the library may use it to contact you for any purposes related to your reservation.
* **User Input**: This field is not a formal field but a general text input for any additional information or comments.
