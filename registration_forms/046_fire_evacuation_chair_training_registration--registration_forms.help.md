# fire_evacuation_chair_training_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and trainers for fire evacuation chair training sessions. The form is used to ensure that all necessary details are captured to facilitate the training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information required.
3. Review the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or passport.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the training session in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the training session in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the trainer.
- **Trainer Email** (`trainer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the trainer.
- **Trainer Phone** (`trainer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the trainer.
- **Trainer License Number** (`trainer_license_number`, text, optional): Enter the trainer's license number.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the training administrator.
- Please be as accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure that we can provide the best possible support.
