# Zoo Volunteer Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the zoo's volunteer program. Please fill out the form completely to help us get to know you better and ensure you receive the right information and opportunities about our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. Fill out the "Date of Birth" fields (Day, Month, and Year) with your date of birth.
3. Enter your time of day in the "Time of Day" fields (Hour, Minute, and Second).
4. Select your volunteer type preferences from the "Volunteer Type" options.
5. If you're interested in volunteering, select your preferred frequency and duration from the "Volunteer Commitment Frequency" and "Volunteer Commitment Duration" fields.
6. Fill out any additional information in the "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3" fields as needed.
7. Enter any additional notes about your application in the "Application Notes" field.
8. Confirm your submission by filling out the "Submission Confirmation" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, not required): Enter your date of birth.
  - **Day** (`date_of_birth_day`, `number`, not required): Enter the day of your birth.
  - **Month** (`date_of_birth_month`, `number`, not required): Enter the month of your birth.
  - **Year** (`date_of_birth_year`, `number`, not required): Enter the year of your birth.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, not required): Enter your time of day.
  - **Hour** (`time_of_day_hour`, `number`, not required): Enter the hour of your day.
  - **Minute** (`time_of_day_minute`, `number`, not required): Enter the minute of your day.
  - **Second** (`time_of_day_second`, `number`, not required): Enter the second of your day.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or multiple volunteer types.
* **Option 1** (`option1`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Option 2** (`option2`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Volunteer Commitment Duration** (`volunteer_commitment_duration`, `number`, not required): Enter your volunteer commitment duration.
* **Volunteer Commitment Frequency** (`volunteer_commitment_frequency`, `select_one`, not required): Select your volunteer commitment frequency.
* **Option 3** (`option3`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Application Notes** (`application_notes`, `note`, not required): Enter any additional notes about your application.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, `text`, not required): Confirm your submission.
* **Submission Confirmation 2** (`submission_confirmation_2`, `text`, not required): Confirm your submission.
* **Submission Confirmation 3** (`submission_confirmation_3`, `text`, not required): Confirm your submission.
* **Submission Confirmation 4** (`submission_confirmation_4`, `text`, not required): Confirm your submission.
