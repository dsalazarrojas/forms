# Festival Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Check In Form is used to register attendees for a festival event. It is designed to collect basic information about each attendee, including their name, check-in date and time, and contact information. This form ensures that we have accurate and up-to-date information for each attendee, which is necessary for event organization and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Festival Check In Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `name` field.
2. Select your check-in date and time in the `checkin_date` and `checkin_time` fields.
3. (Optional) Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5. (Optional) Add any additional information or notes in the `note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the festival event you are attending.
* **name** (text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on event materials and communications.
* **checkin_time** (date, required): Select your check-in time, which is the time you plan to arrive at the festival.
* **checkin_date** (date, required): Select your check-in date, which is the date you plan to attend the festival.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive event communications or updates.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by festival staff or volunteers.
* **note** (note, optional): Add any additional information or notes about your attendance, such as any dietary restrictions or special requests.
