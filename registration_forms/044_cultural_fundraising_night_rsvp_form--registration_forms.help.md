# Cultural Fundraising Night RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cultural Fundraising Night RSVP Form" is a simple form designed to collect RSVP information from attendees of the Cultural Fundraising Night event. Please fill out this form to let us know if you can join us and provide any additional comments or details we should be aware of.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your Name in the "Name" field.
2. Add your Email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address.
3. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field, if required.
4. Indicate the number of guests you plan to bring with you in the "Guest Count" field.
5. Provide your RSVP Time and Date in the "RSVP Time" and "RSVP Date" fields, respectively.
6. Add any additional comments or messages in the "Comments" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed during the event.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates or communications.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have a phone number, please enter it for us to contact you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
- **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, `number`, required: false): Let us know how many guests you plan to bring with you to the event.
- **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, `date`, required: false): Select the time you plan to attend the event.
- **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you plan to attend the event.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or messages you'd like to share with us.
- **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you plan to arrive or leave the event.
- **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you plan to attend the event.
