# Website And App User Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user profile information for the website and app users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Password in the "Password" field.
5. Confirm your Password in the "Confirm Password" field.
6. Fill in your Username in the "Username" field.
7. Fill in your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Select your Country in the "Country" field.
9. Select your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
10. Choose your Gender in the "Gender" field.
11. Select your preferred Language in the "Language" field.
12. Select your Time Zone in the "Time Zone" field.
13. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
14. Fill in any additional note in the "Note" field.
15. Upload your Profile Picture in the "Profile Picture" field.
16. Enter your Time Zone in the "Time Zone" field (Note: This field has the same label as field 12, to avoid duplication, please choose the correct one).
17. Enter your Website address in the "Website" field.
18. Select your preferred App Platform in the "App Platform" field.
19. Enter the version of the App you are using in the "App Version" field.
20. Fill in the date and time when you created your profile in the "Created At" field.
21. Fill in the date and time when you last updated your profile in the "Updated At" field.
22. Fill in the date and time of your last login in the "Last Login At" field.
23. Fill in the date and time of your last update in the "Last Update At" field.
24. Enter the name of the App you are using in the "App Name" field.
25. Enter the version of the App you are using in the "App Version" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: <code>first_name</code> (<type>text</type>): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: <code>last_name</code> (<type>text</type>): Enter your last name.
* **Email**: <code>email</code> (<type>email</type>): Enter your email address.
* **Password**: <code>password</code> (<type>text</type>): Enter a password for your account.
* **Confirm Password**: <code>confirm_password</code> (<type>text</type>): Confirm your password.
* **Username**: <code>username</code> (<type>text</type>): Create a username for your account.
* **Phone**: <code>phone</code> (<type>text</type>): Enter your phone number.
* **Country**: <code>country</code> (<type>text</type>): Select your country.
* **Date of Birth**: <code>date_of_birth</code> (<type>date</type>): Select your date of birth.
* **Gender**: <code>gender</code> (<type>select_one</type>): Choose your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Language**: <code>language</code> (<type>select_multiple</type>): Select your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, Chinese).
* **Time Zone**: <code>time_zone</code> (<type>select_one</type>): Select your time zone (Pacific, Mountain, Central, Eastern).
* **Address**: <code>address</code> (<type>text</type>): Enter your address.
* **Note**: <code>note</code> (<type>note</type>): Add any additional note about yourself.
* **Profile Picture**: <code>profile_picture</code> (<type>text</type>): Upload your profile picture.
* **Time Zone**: <code>time_zone</code> (<type>time</type>): Select your time zone (Note: This field has the same label as field 12, to avoid duplication, please choose the correct one).
* **Website**: <code>website</code> (<type>text</type>): Enter your website address.
* **App Platform**: <code>app_platform</code> (<type>select_one</type>): Select the app platform you are using (iOS, Android, Windows).
* **App Version**: <code>app_version</code> (<type>number</type>): Enter the version of the app you are using.
* **Created At**: <code>created_at</code> (<type>date</type>): Fill in the date and time when you created your profile.
* **Updated At**: <code>updated_at</code> (<type>date</type>): Fill in the date and time when you last updated your profile.
* **Last Login At**: <code>last_login_at</code> (<type>date</type>): Fill in the date and time of your last login.
* **Last Update At**: <code>last_update_at</code> (<type>date</type>): Fill in the date and time of your last update.
* **App Name**: <code>app_name</code> (<type>text</type>): Enter the name of the app you are using.
* **App Version**: <code>app_version</code> (<type>number</type>): Enter the version of the app you are using.
