# primary_stage_letter_recognition_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a student and their teacher, which will be used to analyze and provide feedback on the student's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the required fields, such as Teacher's Name and Student's Name.
2. For Teacher's Age and Student's Age, select a number between 1 and 100.
3. In the Student Gender field, select Male, Female, or Other.
4. For Student Confidence Level, select High, Medium, or Low.
5. For Teacher's Experience, select Years 1-2, Years 3-5, Years 6-10, or 10+.
6. For Student's Grade Level, select Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3, ..., College.
7. Fill in the School Name field with the name of the student's school.
8. Enter the student's Email Address.
9. Fill in the School Phone field with the school's contact number.
10. In the Teacher Notes field, write any additional information about the teacher.
11. In the Student Notes field, write any additional information about the student.
12. Finally, select Yes or No to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher's Name**: Enter the name of the student's teacher.
* **Student's Name**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Teacher's Age**: Select a number between 1 and 100 to represent the teacher's age.
* **Student's Age**: Select a number between 1 and 100 to represent the student's age.
* **Student Gender**: Select Male, Female, or Other to represent the student's gender.
* **Student Confidence Level**: Select High, Medium, or Low to represent the student's confidence level.
* **Teacher's Experience**: Select Years 1-2, Years 3-5, Years 6-10, or 10+ to represent the teacher's experience.
* **Student's Grade Level**: Select Grade 1, Grade 2, ..., College to represent the student's grade level.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Email Address**: Enter the student's email address.
* **School Phone**: Enter the school's contact number.
* **Teacher Notes**: Write any additional information about the teacher.
* **Student Notes**: Write any additional information about the student.
* **Submit**: Select Yes or No to submit the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and complete.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact your administrator or support team.
