# protein_structure_vocabulary_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The protein structure vocabulary quiz is designed to assess a student's understanding of protein structure terminology. It is a series of questions that cover various aspects of protein structure, and the user's answers will be evaluated against the correct options provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student Name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field. 
3. Retype the email address in the "Confirm Email" field to ensure it matches.
4. If you wish to submit your quiz answers, select one of the options in the "Complete Quiz" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (Student Name, text, required)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the results of the quiz.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter a valid email address where you would like to receive your results.
* **Confirm Email (Student Email Confirmed, email, required)**: Retype your email address to ensure it is accurate and complete.
* **Complete Quiz (Quiz Completed, select_one, required)**: If you want to submit your quiz answers, select one of the options provided.
* **Score (Score, number, required)**: Enter your score as it appears on the results page.
* **Student ID (Student ID, text, required)**: (This field is not currently used, it is intended for further development, but it is required due to the original schema configuration. It should be ignored for the purpose of this form.)

* **Student ID (Student ID, text, required)**: This field is intended to store a unique student identifier, but it is currently not being used. Please do not enter any information in this field, as it is not relevant to the current functionality of the form.

**Tips**

* Make sure to retype your email address correctly in both the "Email" and "Confirm Email" fields to ensure they match.
