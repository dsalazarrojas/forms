# Adult Physical Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used for adult physical exams, providing a comprehensive structure for healthcare professionals to gather and document vital information about patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Ensure the form is being completed for a single adult patient.
* Review the form carefully before starting to understand what information is required.
* Enter the patient's name accurately in the "Patient name" field.
* Measure and record the patient's height in centimeters in the "Height in centimeters" field.
* Record the patient's weight in kilograms in the "Weight in kilograms" field.
* Record the blood pressure reading in the format "systolic over diastolic" in the "Blood pressure reading" field.
* If applicable, record the resting heart rate in beats per minute (bpm) in the "Resting heart rate bpm" field.
* If applicable, record the respiratory rate in breaths per minute in the "Respiratory rate breaths per minute" field.
* In the "General exam findings" field, note any significant observations or findings from the physical exam.
* In the "Recommended follow up actions" field, list any necessary tests, referrals, or treatments recommended for the patient.
* Finally, enter the name of the healthcare professional who performed the exam in the "Examiner name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient name**: Enter the patient's first and last name accurately.
* **Height in centimeters**: Measure and record the patient's height in centimeters.
* **Weight in kilograms**: Record the patient's weight in kilograms.
* **Blood pressure reading**: Record the blood pressure reading in the format "systolic over diastolic".
* **Resting heart rate bpm**: Record the resting heart rate in beats per minute if applicable.
* **Respiratory rate breaths per minute**: Record the respiratory rate in breaths per minute if applicable.
* **General exam findings**: Note any significant observations or findings from the physical exam.
* **Recommended follow up actions**: List any necessary tests, referrals, or treatments recommended for the patient.
* **Examiner name**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional who performed the exam.

## Tips
* Ensure the form is filled out accurately and thoroughly for each patient.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
* Use clear and concise language when documenting findings and recommendations.
* Store the completed form as part of the patient's medical record for future reference.
* If you are unsure about any field or information, consult with a colleague or medical expert for guidance.
