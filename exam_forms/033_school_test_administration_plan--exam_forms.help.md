# School Test Administration Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to plan and organize exams for schools, ensuring that all necessary details are gathered to facilitate a smooth and efficient exam administration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the school in the "School Name" field.
2. Select the date of the exam in the "Exam Date" field.
3. Enter the test start time in the "Test Start Time" field.
4. Enter the duration of the exam in the "Test Duration" field.
5. Enter the location of the exam in the "Exam Location" field.
6. In the "Student Includes" field, select whether the exam includes students with accommodation or without accommodation, or both.
7. In the "Staff Includes" field, select the types of staff members that will be involved in the exam, such as test proctors, teachers, or both.
8. In the "Test Documents" field, select the documents that will be required for the exam, such as student IDs or test answers.
9. In the "Special Requests" field, select any special requests that need to be accommodated, such as accommodations or translations.
10. Enter any additional information for the exam supervisor in the "Exam Supervisor" field.
11. Enter any additional information for the exam coordinator in the "Exam Coordinator" field.
12. Enter any additional notes for staff in the "Staff Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the exam.
* **Test Start Time** (`test_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the exam will start.
* **Test Duration** (`test_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the exam in minutes.
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the exam.
* **Test Proctoring** (`test_proctoring`, note, required: false): Enter any notes about test proctoring.
* **Student Includes** (`student_includes`, select_one, required: true): Select whether the exam includes students with accommodation or without accommodation, or both.
* **Staff Includes** (`staff_includes`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the types of staff members that will be involved in the exam, such as test proctors, teachers, or both.
* **Test Documents** (`test_documents`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the documents that will be required for the exam, such as student IDs or test answers.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, select_multiple, required: true): Select any special requests that need to be accommodated, such as accommodations or translations.
* **Exam Supervisor** (`exam_supervisor`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information for the exam supervisor.
* **Exam Coordinator** (`exam_coordinator`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information for the exam coordinator.
* **Staff Notes** (`staff_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes for staff.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the entered information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the exam administration team for clarification.
