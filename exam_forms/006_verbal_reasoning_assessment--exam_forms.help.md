# Verbal Reasoning Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Verbal Reasoning Assessment is designed to evaluate your verbal reasoning skills by asking you to respond to a series of questions that assess your ability to understand and analyze information, think critically, and articulate your thoughts clearly. This assessment is typically used to evaluate candidates for positions requiring strong verbal reasoning skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and respond to each one as accurately as possible.
2. Fill in your answers in the spaces provided for each page.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed, as indicated by the 'required' label next to each field.
4. You can add any additional comments you'd like to provide context or clarify your answers.
5. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Answer** (required: true, type: text): This field is for you to enter your first answer to the question presented.
* **Second Answer** (required: true, type: text): This field is for you to enter your second answer to the question.
* **Third Answer** (required: true, type: text): This field is for you to enter your third answer to the question.
* **Page Two** (required: false, type: text): This field is not required, but you can use it to enter any additional information or observations you'd like to make.
* **Question One** (required: true, type: select_one): This field is a multiple-choice question where you can select one of the two options provided: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Question Two** (required: true, type: select_one): This field is another multiple-choice question where you can select one of the two options provided: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Question Three** (required: true, type: select_one): This field is a third multiple-choice question where you can select one of the two options provided: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Page Three** (required: false, type: text): This field is not required, but you can use it to enter any additional information or observations you'd like to make.
* **Final Answer** (required: true, type: text): This field is for you to enter your final answer to the question presented.
* **Additional Comments** (required: false, type: note): This field is for you to add any additional comments or context to your answers.

# Tips
* Please take your time to carefully consider each question before responding.
* Ensure your responses are accurate and relevant to the question asked.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
