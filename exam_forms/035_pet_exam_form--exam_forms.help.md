# Pet Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Exam Form is a simple form used to collect information about a pet's examination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information about your pet.
2. Select the correct breed and health status for your pet from the provided options.
3. Add any relevant symptoms or notes in the text box.
4. If applicable, fill in the contact phone number of the pet's owner.
5. Sign and date the form with your veterinarian's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Exam Form (1)
* **Pet Exam Form**: A text field where you can enter a brief description of the pet exam.

### Pets Name (2)
* **Pets Name**: Enter the name of the pet being examined.

### Pet Breed (3)
* **Pet Breed**: Select the breed of the pet from the options: Cat, Dog, or Other.

### Pet Age (4)
* **Pet Age**: Enter the age of the pet.

### Pet Health (5)
* **Pet Health**: Select the health status of the pet from the options: Healthy, Sick, or Dead.

### Pet Symptoms (6)
* **Pet Symptoms**: Enter any relevant symptoms or notes about the pet's health.

### Veterinarian's Signature (7)
* **Veterinarian's Signature**: Sign and date the form with your veterinarian's signature.

### Contact Phone (8)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the pet's owner, if applicable.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to select the correct breed and health status for your pet.
* Add any relevant symptoms or notes to help the veterinarian provide the best possible care.
* If you are not the pet owner, ensure to obtain the pet owner's contact information for follow-up.
