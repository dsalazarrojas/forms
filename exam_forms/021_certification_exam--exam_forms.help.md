# Certification Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Certification Exam form is used to record and track details related to a candidate's certification exam, including exam date, time, location, and performance. This form helps certification administrators and proctors monitor and evaluate exam progress and results accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. For each field, enter the required information, following the specific instructions for each field:
	* **Exam Date**: Enter the date of the exam in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/23).
	* **Exam Time**: Select the start time of the exam in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 AM).
	* **Candidate Name**: Enter the full legal name of the candidate, as it appears on their identification.
	* **Candidate ID Number**: Enter the candidate's unique identification number.
	* **Email Address**: Enter the email address associated with the candidate.
	* **Certification Name**: Enter the name of the certification being pursued.
	* **Exam Version**: Enter the version or form number of the exam.
	* **Exam Duration**: Select the total time allowed for the exam (e.g., 60 minutes).
	* **Exam Location**: Select where the exam is being taken (e.g., Testing Center).
	* **Proctor Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the proctor.
	* **Total Questions**: Enter the number of questions on the exam.
	* **Passing Score Required**: Enter the minimum score required to pass.
	* **Section 1-3 Scores**: Enter the score achieved by the candidate in each section.
	* **Total Score**: Enter the overall score achieved by the candidate.
	* **Exam Result**: Select the final result of the exam (e.g., Pass).
	* **Accommodations Used**: Select whether any accommodations were provided (e.g., Extended Time).
	* **Exam Completion Status**: Select whether the candidate completed the exam (e.g., Yes - Completed).
	* **Irregularities Noted**: Select whether any irregularities were observed during the exam (e.g., No irregularities).
	* **Irregularity Description**: If irregularities were noted, describe them in this field.
	* **Candidate Signature**: Enter your full name as the candidate's signature.
	* **Proctor Signature**: If applicable, enter the proctor's signature.
	* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the exam.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, `date`, `true`): Enter the date of the exam in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/23). This ensures accurate record-keeping and tracking of exam schedules.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, `time`, `true`): Select the start time of the exam in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 AM). This helps proctors and administrators schedule and manage exam sessions.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, `true`): Enter the full legal name of the candidate, as it appears on their identification. This ensures accurate identification and recording of candidate information.
* **Candidate ID Number** (`candidate_id`, `text`, `true`): Enter the candidate's unique identification number. This helps track individual candidate progress and results.
* **Email Address** (`candidate_email`, `email`, `true`): Enter the email address associated with the candidate for exam results and communication.
* **Certification Name** (`certification_name`, `text`, `true`): Enter the name of the certification being pursued. This helps track which certification exams are being administered.
* **Exam Version** (`exam_version`, `text`, `true`): Enter the version or form number of the exam. This helps ensure that candidates complete the correct exam version.
* **Exam Duration** (`exam_duration`, `select_one`, `true`): Select the total time allowed for the exam in minutes. Choose from 60, 90, 120, 180, or 240 minutes.
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, `select_one`, `true`): Select where the exam is being taken: Testing Center, Online Proctored, Workplace, Educational Institution, or Other.
* **Proctor Name** (`proctor_name`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, enter the name of the proctor who administered the exam.
* **Total Questions** (`total_questions`, `number`, `true`): Enter the number of questions on the exam. This helps track exam difficulty and completion rates.
* **Passing Score Required** (`passing_score`, `number`, `true`): Enter the minimum score required to pass. This helps set a pass/fail threshold for the exam.
* **Section 1-3 Scores** (`exam_section_1`, `exam_section_2`, `exam_section_3`, `number`, `false`): Enter the score achieved by the candidate in each section (optional).
* **Total Score** (`total_score`, `number`, `false`): Enter the overall score achieved by the candidate (optional).
* **Exam Result** (`exam_result`, `select_one`, `true`): Select the final result of the exam (e.g., Pass).
* **Accommodations Used** (`accommodations_used`, `select_one`, `true`): Select whether any accommodations were provided (e.g., Extended Time).
* **Exam Completion Status** (`exam_completion`, `select_one`, `true`): Select whether the candidate completed the exam (e.g., Yes - Completed).
* **Irregularities Noted** (`irregularities_noted`, `select_one`, `true`): Select whether any irregularities were observed during the exam (e.g., No irregularities).
* **Irregularity Description** (`irregularity_description`, `text`, `false`): If irregularities were noted, describe them in this field.
* **Candidate Signature** (`candidate_signature`, `text`, `true`): Enter your full name as the candidate's signature.
* **Proctor Signature** (`proctor_signature`, `text`, `false`): If applicable, enter the proctor's signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, `true`): Enter the date of the exam in MM/DD/YY format (e.g., 01/01/23).
