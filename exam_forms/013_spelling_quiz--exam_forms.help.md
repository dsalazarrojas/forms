# Spelling Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Spelling Quiz form is to test a person's ability to spell words correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Input your score in the Score field.
4. List the words you are to be spelled in the Words List field.
5. Input the correct answer in the Correct Answer field.
6. Choose your Grade Level from the dropdown menu.
7. Select the Subject Area where you wish to apply your spelling skills.
