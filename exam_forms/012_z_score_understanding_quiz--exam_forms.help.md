# Z Score Understanding Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users understand the concept of Z score, which is a statistical measure of standard deviation. The quiz will guide the user through a series of questions to calculate and interpret Z scores.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct response for each question based on the options provided.
2. Fill in any numerical values required for calculation.
3. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Z-Score Basics** (`1 - 0.5`, `1.5 - 2`, `2 - 2.5`, `2.5 - 3`): Select the range that best describes your understanding of Z score.
* **Z-Score Formula** (`type: text`): This field is not currently being used for this form, so you can ignore it.
* **Mean** (`type: number`): Enter the mean value of your data set.
* **Standard Deviation** (`type: number`): Enter the standard deviation value of your data set.
* **Data Point** (`1 - 1.5`, `2 - 2.5`, `3 - 3.5`): Select the data point you are using for calculation.
* **Z-Score Value** (`type: number`): This field is for the calculated Z-score value, which will be calculated based on your inputs.
* **Standard Deviation Value** (`type: number`): This field is for the standard deviation value, which is also used in the calculation.
* **Standard Deviation Value 2** (`type: number`): Another field for standard deviation value 2, likely for second dataset or calculation.
* **Standard Deviation Value 3** (`type: number`): Another field for standard deviation value 3, likely for third dataset or calculation.
* **Mean Value** (`type: number`): Enter the mean value of your data set.
* **Mean Value 2** (`type: number`): Another field for mean value 2, likely for second dataset or calculation.
* **Mean Value 3** (`type: number`): Another field for mean value 3, likely for third dataset or calculation.
* **Z-Score** (`Less than 2`, `2 or more`): Select the range that best describes your calculated Z-score.
* **Result** (`Z-Score is less than 2`, `Z-Score is 2 or more`): Select the result of your calculation based on the Z score.
* **Score** (`type: number`): This field is not currently being used for this form, so you can ignore it.
* **Answer** (`0`, `1`): Enter your answer, either 0 or 1, to confirm your result.
* **Note** (`type: note`): Additional notes about your calculation or data.
* **Email** (`type: email`): Enter your email for any further contact or communication.
* **Phone** (`type: text`): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
* **Name** (`type: text`): Enter your name, useful for identification.
* **Submission** (`Submit`, `Reset`): Submit or reset your form to start over.
* **Quiz End** (`Finish`, `Continue`): Select to finish or continue with the quiz.
* **Quiz End Note** (`type: note`): Additional notes about the quiz end.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `Other`): Final submission or other action.

Please note that this form is designed to guide the user through understanding Z score and its calculations, but the actual calculation process is not shown in this help guide.
