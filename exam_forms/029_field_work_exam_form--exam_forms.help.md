# Field Work Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will help you in understanding and completing the Field Work Exam Form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form for every field work exam instance.
- Ensure that the exam date and duration information is filled out only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Fieldwork Exam** (`form_group_1`, note, optional): This is the main section of the form where you can enter a brief description or notes about the field work exam.

- **Field 1** (`field_work_exam_field_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of Field 1.

- **Field 2** (`field_work_exam_field_2`, select_one, required): Select one of the available options for Field 2.

- **Date of Fieldwork** (`date_of_fieldwork`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the field work took place.

- **Date of Exam** (`date_of_exam`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the exam took place.

- **Time of Exam** (`time_of_exam`, time, optional): Enter the time at which the exam took place.

- **Location of Exam** (`location_of_exam`, text, optional): Enter the location where the exam took place.

- **Observers at Exam** (`observers_at_exam`, select_multiple, optional): Select all observers who were present during the exam.

- **Exam Duration** (`exam_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the exam.

- **Score 1** (`score_1`, number, optional): Enter the score of Field 1.

- **Score 2** (`score_2`, number, optional): Enter the score of Field 2.

## Tips

- Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that you do not need to repeat the field work exam.
