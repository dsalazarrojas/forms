# Electrochemistry Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a learner's knowledge in electrochemistry. It is intended to be completed by learners after taking a quiz. The form collects information about the learner's name, quiz results, and other details. It is a crucial tool for educators to track and evaluate learner progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Learner Name" field.
2. Select your quiz results as "Pass" or "Fail".
3. If required, enter the time taken to complete the quiz in the "Time Taken" field.
4. Optionally, provide your participant name and participant answers.
5. Enter any comments from your educator in the "Educator Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Learner Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your name as a learner. This will help the educator identify you in their records.
* **Score (Number, Optional)**: If you scored on this quiz, enter your score here.
* **Time Taken (Time, Optional)**: If you want to track the time taken to complete this quiz, enter it here.
* **Quiz Results (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select whether you passed or failed the quiz.
* **Participant Name (Text, Optional)**: If you want to provide an alias or nickname for the quiz, you can enter it here.
* **Participant Answers (Text, Optional)**: If you want to provide more information about your answers, you can enter it here.
* **Educator Comments (Text, Optional)**: If your educator has comments about your performance, you can enter them here.
* **Question 1 (Text, Not Required)**: This is the first question of the quiz. Please provide your answer to this question in the text field.
* **Participant Name (Text, Optional)**: If you want to provide an alias or nickname for the quiz, you can enter it here.
* **Score (Number, Optional)**: If you scored on this quiz, enter your score here.
* **Participant Answers (Text, Optional)**: If you want to provide more information about your answers, you can enter it here.
* **Time Taken (Time, Optional)**: If you want to track the time taken to complete this quiz, enter it here.
