# Online Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple online quiz designed to collect information from students for assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow these steps:
	* Enter your Student Name in the "Student Name" field.
	* Select the type of question you are answering from the "Question Type" dropdown menu.
	* Answer the corresponding question in the "question" field.
	* Enter your answer to the question in the "answer" field.
	* Repeat this process for each question.
2. Click the "Submit" button to submit your quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or transcript.
* **Question Type** (`question_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of question you are answering (e.g. multiple choice, true or false, etc.).
* **Question** (question, text, optional): Enter the question you are attempting to answer.
* **Answer** (answer, text, optional): Enter your answer to the question.
* **Question 2** (question_2, text, optional): Enter the question you are attempting to answer (this is a repeat question).
* **Answer 2** (answer_2, text, optional): Enter your answer to the question.
* **Question 3** (question_3, text, optional): Enter the question you are attempting to answer (this is a repeat question).
* **Answer 3** (answer_3, text, optional): Enter your answer to the question.
* **Question 4** (question_4, text, optional): Enter the question you are attempting to answer (this is a repeat question).
* **Answer 4** (answer_4, text, optional): Enter your answer to the question.
* **Question 5** (question_5, text, optional): Enter the question you are attempting to answer (this is a repeat question).
* **Answer 5** (answer_5, text, optional): Enter your answer to the question.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct question type for each question.
* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your instructor or school administrator.
