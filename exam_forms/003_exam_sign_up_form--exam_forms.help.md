# Exam Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for an exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your **Name** in the `Name` field.
2. Enter the **Exam Name** in the `Exam Name` field.
3. Enter the date of the exam in the `Date` field in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4. Enter the time of the exam in the `Time` field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
5. Enter the location of the exam in the `Location` field.
6. Enter your **Email** in the `Email` field.
7. Enter your **Phone** in the `Phone` field.
8. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sign Up Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Name**: Enter your personal name.
* **Exam Name**: Enter the name of the exam you are signing up for.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the exam in mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 01/15/2022).
* **Time**: Enter the time of the exam in HH:MM AM/PM format (e.g., 02:30 PM).
* **Location**: Enter the location of the exam.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
