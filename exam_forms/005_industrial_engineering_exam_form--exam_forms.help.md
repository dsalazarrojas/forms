# industrial_engineering_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to industrial engineering exams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide exam information, including the exam name, date, and time.
2. Enter student details, such as name, department, and email.
3. Answer the question 1 field.
4. If necessary, add any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exam Info** (`exam_info`, text, optional): Enter the exam name, date, and time.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, optional): Enter the student's name, department, and email.
* **Question Info** (`question_info`, text, optional): Enter additional information about the question.
* **Question 1** (`answer`, text, optional): Answer the question.
* **Exam Info 2** (`exam_info_2`, text, optional): Enter any further exam details.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
* Double-check the exam and student information for accuracy.
* Enter clear and concise answers to questions.
* Use the "Additional Info" field for any additional comments or notes.
