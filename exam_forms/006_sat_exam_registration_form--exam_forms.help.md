# SAT Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SAT Exam Registration Form is a document that helps you register for a SAT exam. Fill out this form to provide us with your details so we can register you for the exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields and select options where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information** (Text): This field is for you to enter your name and any other relevant student details.
- **Exam Details** (Text): Enter the details of the exam you are interested in registering for.
- **Payment Information** (Text): This is where you can provide any payment information related to your exam registration.
- **Submit** (Text): Click this button to submit your form.
- **Test Score** (Number): If you have taken a test, enter your score here.
- **SAT Date** (Date): Select the date you would like to take the SAT exam.
- **SAT Time** (Time): Select the time you would like to take the SAT exam.
- **Exam Location** (Select One): Choose where you would like to take the SAT exam from the provided options.
- **Student Name** (Text): Enter your name as it is on your ID or driver's license.
- **Student Email** (Email): Enter your email address associated with your student account.
- **Student Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
- **Student Address** (Text): Enter your address for exam location details.
- **Exam Type** (Select Multiple): Choose between SAT and ACT exam types.
- **Test Score 1-6** (Number): If you have taken other tests, enter your scores here.
- **Exam Location 2** (Select One): Choose where you would like to take the ACT exam.
- **Exam Type 2** (Select Multiple): Choose between SAT and ACT exam types for this location.
- **SAT Date 2** (Date): Select the date you would like to take the ACT exam.
- **SAT Time 2** (Time): Select the time you would like to take the ACT exam.
- **Student Name 2** (Text): Enter your name as it is on your ID or driver's license.
- **Student Email 2** (Email): Enter your email address associated with your student account.
- **Student Phone 2** (Text): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
- **Student Address 2** (Text): Enter your address for exam location details.
