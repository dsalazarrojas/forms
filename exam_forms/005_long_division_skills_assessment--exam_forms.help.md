# Long Division Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Long Division Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's long division skills, which is a fundamental math operation in arithmetic. This assessment is intended for students or individuals who need to demonstrate their proficiency in performing long division calculations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are in a quiet and distraction-free environment.
2. Read each question carefully and choose the correct answer based on your knowledge.
3. Fill out the form with the required questions (marked as "required").
4. If a question is not required, feel free to answer it if you wish, but it will not affect your overall assessment score.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.
