### How To Complete This Form

Fill out this form to register for a microbiology exam. Enter all required fields and fill out the form completely.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Exam Date**: Enter the date of your exam.
* **Exam Time**: Enter the time of your exam.
* **Exam Type**: Select the type of exam you are registering for (Biology, Chemistry, Physics).
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of your school.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of your school.
* **Testing Coordinator**: Enter the name of the testing coordinator.
* **Exam Location**: Enter the location of your exam.
* **Exam Duration**: Select the duration of your exam (60 minutes, 90 minutes, 120 minutes).
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Name**: Enter your student name.
* **Student Grade**: Enter your student grade level.
* **Student School**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of your teacher.
* **Student Age**: Enter your age.
* **Student Gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female).
* **Student Birthdate**: Enter your birthdate.
* **Student School ID**: Enter your school ID.
* **School Admin Name**: Enter the name of your school admin.
* **School Admin Email**: Enter the email address of your school admin.
