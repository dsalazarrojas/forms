# opengl_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The opengl_knowledge_quiz form is designed to collect information from users about their knowledge and experience with OpenGL, a computer graphics rendering engine.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Name and Email Address.
2. Answer the Quiz Questions by filling out the text fields labeled "Question 1" to "Question 12".
3. Choose the Quiz Type from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter your Score (e.g., 0-100).
5. Optionally, enter the Score Out Of, which seems to be a hint rather than a mandatory field.
6. Record the Duration of the quiz in the provided field.
7. Enter the Date Completed in the provided field.
8. Optionally, enter the Time Spent on the quiz in the text field.
9. If you have any feedback, provide it in the provided field.
10. Click "Submit" to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz Questions (1-12)** (text, required): Fill out each question with a text answer.
* **User Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the user taking the quiz.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Quiz Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of quiz you took.
* **Score** (number, optional): Enter your score out of the maximum.
* **Score Out Of** (number, optional): Hint: Enter the maximum score for the quiz.
* **Duration** (time, optional): Record the duration of the quiz.
* **Date Completed** (date, optional): Record the date and time you completed the quiz.
* **Time Spent** (text, optional): Record the time spent on the quiz.
* **Feedback** (text, optional): Provide any additional feedback you have about the quiz.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click to submit your answers.
