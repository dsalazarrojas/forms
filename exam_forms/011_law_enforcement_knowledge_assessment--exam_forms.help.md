# Law Enforcement Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Enforcement Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate the knowledge and understanding of law enforcement officers regarding various aspects of their duties and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the instructions for each field below to understand what is being asked.
2. Select the correct answers for each field based on your knowledge and understanding.
3. Fill out the form completely, but do not spend more than 15-20 minutes on it.
