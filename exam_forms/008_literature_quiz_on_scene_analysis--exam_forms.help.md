# Literature Quiz On Scene Analysis - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess students' understanding of a given literature work's scene analysis. The form is intended for students to evaluate their own performance and provide feedback to teachers on their analysis. The teacher can use the form to monitor student progress and provide guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form in its entirety, answering all questions and providing comments as needed.
*   Select the correct options for each question, taking care to answer "Yes" or "No" according to your actual performance.
*   If you need to provide more detailed explanations or comments, use the text fields for "answer" and "comments".
*   Ensure that you provide a valid teacher's evaluation date and time, if applicable.
*   Finally, review your form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student name** (`student_identification`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or other official documents.
*   **Scene** (`scene_question`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you felt you fully understood the scene and "No" otherwise.
*   **Answer** (`scene_question_answer`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" for each correct answer or "No" for incorrect answers. Please provide explanations if needed in the text field "answer".
*   **Comments** (`student_answer_comment`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations related to your scene analysis.
*   **Score** (`student_score`, number, optional): Enter the number of points you think you scored.
*   **Teacher comments** (`teacher_comment`, text, optional): Enter a comment for your teacher to reference during the evaluation process.
*   **Form completion status** (`form_completion_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Active" if the form is complete and "Inactive" otherwise.
*   **Teacher evaluation date** (`teacher_evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your teacher's evaluation.
*   **Teacher evaluation time** (`teacher_evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your teacher's evaluation.
*   **Teacher evaluation status** (`teacher_evaluation_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Active" or "Inactive" according to your teacher's status.
*   **Teachers evaluation** (`teacher_evaluation`, note, optional): Enter your teacher's evaluation comments in this section.
*   **Student performance score** (`teacher_evaluation`, number, optional): Enter your teacher's score for this evaluation.
