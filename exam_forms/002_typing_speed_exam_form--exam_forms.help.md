# Typing Speed Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Typing Speed Exam Form is designed to assess an individual's typing speed and proficiency. It collects information on a user's ability to quickly and accurately type out given text.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2.  Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3.  Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4.  Type in your Age in the "Age" field.
5.  Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
6.  Input your Email address in the "Email" field.
7.  Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
8.  Enter your typing speed in words per minute (wpm) in the "Speed" field.
9.  Enter your accuracy rate as a percentage in the "Accuracy" field.
10. Enter the time it takes you to complete a given task in the "Time" field in the format of hh:mm.
11. If required, select one of the options for "Category" in the "Category" field.
12. Select all applicable options for "Category" in the "Category" field.
13. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input_1`, text, *): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`user_input_2`, text, ): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Age** (`user_input_3`, number, *): Enter your age in years.
* **Date of Birth** (`user_input_4`, date, ): Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (`user_input_5`, email, ): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`user_input_6`, text, ): Enter your phone number.
* **Speed** (`user_input_7`, number, *): Enter your typing speed in words per minute (wpm).
* **Accuracy** (`user_input_8`, number, *): Enter your accuracy rate as a percentage.
* **Time** (`user_input_9`, time, *): Enter the time it takes you to complete a given task in the format of hh:mm.
* **Note** (`user_input_10`, note, ): Enter any additional notes or comments related to your typing speed and accuracy.
* **Category** (`user_input_11`, select_multiple, ): Select all applicable options from the list (Cat, Dog).
* **Category** (`user_input_12`, select_one, *): Select one of the options from the list (Cat, Dog, Bird).
* **Typing Speed** (`user_input_13`, text, *): Enter your typing speed as a text description.
