# Graphic Design Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graphic Design Exam Form is designed to collect and assess a student's graphic design project. This form is used to evaluate a student's performance based on their project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form header, which contains general information about the form.
2. Enter the student's information, including their name, and any other relevant details.
3. Provide details about the project, including its title and any notable aspects.
4. Answer questions about the student's performance, such as whether they met specific requirements and achieved certain goals.
5. Select whether the student's design projects met the necessary criteria.
6. Enter any comments or feedback about the student's work.
7. Summarize the project outcome, including any notable achievements or challenges.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This section contains general information about the form and provides a summary of the project. Enter a brief description of the project and any other relevant details.
* **Student Info**: Enter the student's name and any other relevant information about them. This could include their grade level or any other relevant details.
* **Project Info**: Enter a brief description of the project, including its title and any notable aspects.
* **Project Title**: Enter the title of the project.
* **Project Details**: Enter any additional details about the project, such as its objectives or scope.
* **Performance Questions**: Answer the following questions about the student's performance:
	+ Yes or No: Did the student meet the specific requirements of the project?
	+ Yes or No: Did the student achieve the project's goals?
* **Rubric Score**: Enter a score between 0 and 100, indicating the student's overall performance on the project.
* **Student Designs**: Select which of the student's designs met the necessary criteria:
	+ Yes or No: Did the student's first design meet the requirements?
	+ Yes or No: Did the student's second design meet the requirements?
* **Educator Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the student's work. This could include suggestions for improvement or praise for their skills.
* **Project Outcome**: Summarize the project outcome, including any notable achievements or challenges.
