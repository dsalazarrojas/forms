# Ectoparasite Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Ectoparasite Knowledge Quiz is a self-assessment tool designed to evaluate your knowledge on various ectoparasite-related topics. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your knowledge on the topic.
2. Use the options provided for select_multiple, select_one, and select options.
3. Enter your response in the text fields (e.g., flea_biology, ticks, lice, veterinary_precautions, and veterinary_organizations).
4. Once you've answered all questions, submit your form and review your results.
