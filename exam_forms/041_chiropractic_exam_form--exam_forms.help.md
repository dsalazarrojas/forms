# Chiropractic Exam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chiropractic Exam Form is a standard document used by clinicians to gather and document information about patients undergoing a chiropractic examination. The form serves as a comprehensive guide for clinicians to collect accurate and relevant data during the examination process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are crucial for the accurate completion of the examination process.
2. Enter the patient's full legal name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the examination in the "Exam Date" field using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Describe the main reason for the visit in the "Presenting Complaint" field.
5. If applicable, rate the patient's pain level using the "Pain Scale" field (0-10).
6. Document any notable findings regarding the patient's range of motion and mobility in the "Range of Motion Findings" field.
7. Record any neurologic findings, such as reflexes, sensation, and motor strength, in the "Neurologic Findings" field.
8. Provide the clinician's working diagnosis in the "Diagnosis" field.
9. Outline the recommended treatment and follow-up plan in the "Treatment Plan" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (patient_name)**: Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Exam Date (exam_date)**: Enter the date of the examination using the (YYYY-MM-DD) format.
* **Presenting Complaint (presenting_complaint)**: Describe the main reason for the visit.
* **Pain Scale (pain_scale)**: Rate the patient's pain level from 0 to 10 (optional).
* **Range of Motion Findings (range_of_motion)**: Note any notable findings regarding the patient's mobility and limitations.
* **Neurologic Findings (neurologic_findings)**: Record any neurologic findings, such as reflexes, sensation, and motor strength.
* **Diagnosis (diagnosis)**: Provide the clinician's working diagnosis.
* **Treatment Plan (treatment_plan)**: Outine the recommended treatment and follow-up plan.
