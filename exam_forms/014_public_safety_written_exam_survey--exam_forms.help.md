# Public Safety Written Exam Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Safety Written Exam Survey is a form used to gather feedback and insights from candidates who have attended a public safety session.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps outlined in the "How to Complete This Form" section.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the session title?** (`session_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the session you attended.
* **What were the session instructions?** (`session_instructions`, text, required): Describe the instructions you received during the session, including any specific tasks or requirements.
* **How difficult was the session?** (`difficulty_level`, select_one, required): Select the difficulty level of the session using the options provided.
* **What feedback do you have?** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide any feedback you may have about the session, such as what you liked or disliked.
* **Any comments about the exam?** (`exam_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them in this field.
## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and comments.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, be sure to include them.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
