# genetics_certification_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and exam-related data for certification purposes in the field of genetics. It helps to create a record of user credentials and exam details, which can be useful for administrative or verification tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal details, such as your name, email, and contact phone number.
2. If applicable, select your degree level (Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral) from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date and time of the exam you are taking.
4. Add any additional notes about your exam experience, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info
* **User Info**: (`user_info`, text, optional) Enter your personal details, such as your first and last name.

### Name
* **Name**: (`name`, text, optional) Enter your full name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: (`last_name`, text, optional) Enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional) Enter your valid email address.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone**: (`contact_phone`, text, optional) Enter your contact phone number.

### Degree
* **Degree**: (`degree`, select_one, optional) Select your degree level (Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral).

### Exam Date
* **Exam Date**: (`exam_date`, date, optional) Enter the date of the exam you took or will take.

### Exam Time
* **Exam Time**: (`exam_time`, time, optional) Enter the time of the exam you took or will take.

### Notes
* **Notes**: (`notes`, note, optional) Enter any additional notes about your exam experience.
