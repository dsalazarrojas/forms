# International Economics Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The International Economics Quiz is a form designed to gather information from users to assess their knowledge and understanding of international economics concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form layout and ensure you are familiar with the required information.
2. Select all applicable options for "User Input" and "Multiple Choice Question" fields, as they allow multiple selections.
3. For the "Date", "Email", "Phone", "Time", "Text Field", "Decimal Input", "Integer Field" fields, enter the requested information accurately.
4. In the "Note" field, provide any additional comments or information relevant to the quiz.
5. Enter your answer or text response in the "International Economics Quiz Question" field.
6. Review your selections and responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to select any applicable options that best describe your input. Select "Yes" if the option applies to you, "No" otherwise.
  - **Multiple Choice Question** (Select One): This field presents a single question with two possible answers. Select the one that best answers the question.
  - **Date**: Enter a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy (month/day/year or day/month/year).
  - **Email**: Enter your email address.
  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  - **Time**: Enter a time in the format 12:00 (HH:MM AM/PM).
  - **Text Field**: Enter a short text response to the question.
  - **Decimal Input**: Enter a decimal number (0.00).
  - **Integer Field**: Enter an integer number.
  - **International Economics Quiz Question**: Provide your answer to the question in this text field.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have reviewed all fields before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact our support team.
- The information gathered from this form will help us understand your preferences and needs better.
