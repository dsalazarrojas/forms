# G Protein Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "G Protein Quiz," is designed to test your knowledge of G proteins, a crucial component in cellular signaling pathways. The form will assess your understanding of the primary function, main components, activation mechanisms, and regulation of G proteins. It's a self-assessment tool to gauge your understanding of these complex biological molecules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions.
2. Answer the questions in the relevant sections below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the primary function of G proteins? (Page 1)** (`g_protein_structure`, text, required: false):
  This field asks for the main purpose or function of G proteins. Please provide a brief description of their primary role in cellular processes.
* **What are the three main components of G proteins? (Page 1)** (`g_protein_components`, select_one, required: true):
  This field requires you to choose the three main components of G proteins. The options are G alpha subunit, G beta and gamma subunit, and G beta-gamma subunit.
* **How are G proteins activated? (Page 1)** (`g_protein_activation`, select_multiple, required: false):
  This field asks for the activation mechanisms of G proteins. Select all that apply:
	+ G protein coupled receptors
	+ G protein coupled receptors with seven transmembrane domains
	+ Other
* **How are G proteins inhibited? (Page 1)** (`g_protein_inhibition`, select_multiple, required: false):
  This field requires you to choose the inhibition mechanisms of G proteins. Select all that apply:
	+ G protein coupled receptor kinase
	+ Other
* **What is the role of the G alpha subunit? (Page 1)** (`g_alpha_subunit`, text, required: false):
  This field is for you to describe the function of the G alpha subunit. Please provide a brief explanation of its role in G proteins.
* **Which of these G protein coupled signaling pathways do you know? (Page 1)** (`g_protein_signaling_pathways`, select_multiple, required: true):
  This field asks you to select all the G protein coupled signaling pathways you're familiar with. Choose from the options:
	+ G protein coupled receptor kinase
	+ G protein coupled receptor kinase 2
	+ Other
* **How are G proteins regulated? (Page 1)** (`g_protein_regulation`, text, required: false):
  This field is for you to explain how G proteins are regulated. Please provide a brief description of their regulation mechanisms.
* **What are some examples of G protein interactions? (Page 1)** (`g_protein_interactions`, note, required: false):
  This field is for you to describe some examples of G protein interactions. Please provide a list or brief explanations of their interactions. 

Please note that some fields have optional answers or choices; you can answer those according to your knowledge. This form is designed to test your understanding, not create a comprehensive guide. If you're unsure, it's okay to leave fields blank or provide "Don't know" as an answer.
