# OpenEndedQuiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The OpenEndedQuiz form is a tool for collecting open-ended answers from users. It is designed to be a simple and efficient way to collect user feedback or opinions that are not limited by predefined options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Scroll down to the desired page and click on the "OpenEndedQuestion" field.
2. Type your answer to the question in the text box provided.
3. Click "Next" or "Save" to move to the next page or submit your responses.
4. If you need to go back to a previous page, click on the "Back" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **OpenEndedQuestion (Page 1)**:
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Enter your answer to the first question.
	+ Required: No, you can skip this question if you don't have an answer.
* **AnotherOpenQuestion (Page 2)**:
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Enter your answer to the second question.
	+ Required: No, you can skip this question if you don't have an answer.
* **OpenEndedQuizForm (Page 3)**:
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Enter your answer to the third question.
	+ Required: No, you can skip this question if you don't have an answer.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question to the best of your ability.
