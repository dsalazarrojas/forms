# Electrolyte Imbalances Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrolyte Imbalances Knowledge Quiz is a tool designed to assess the knowledge of medical professionals on the subject of electrolyte imbalances. This quiz is meant to test their understanding of general knowledge, electrolyte disorders, nursing interventions, lab values, time, note-taking, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page and question carefully.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Knowledge** (`General Knowledge`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture your general knowledge about electrolyte imbalances. Please type your response in the space provided.
* **Electrolyte Disorders** (`Electrolyte Disorders`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to determine whether you have knowledge of electrolyte disorders. Please select 'Yes' if you're familiar with them, or 'No' if you're not.
* **Nursing Interventions** (`Nursing Interventions`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to capture your knowledge of nursing interventions for electrolyte imbalances. Please select all applicable options.
* **Lab Values** (`Lab Values`, `number`, optional): This field is used to record any notable lab values related to electrolyte imbalances. Please enter a numeric value in this field.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, optional): This field is used to record the time of the assessment or intervention. Please use the format 'HH:MM' or 'HH:MM:SS'.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): This field is used to capture any additional notes about the case or scenario. Please type your response in the space provided.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): This field is used to capture your email address for contact purposes. Please enter your email address in the format 'username@example.com'.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture your phone number for contact purposes. Please enter your phone number without any special characters.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, optional): This field is used to record the date of the assessment or intervention. Please use the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Exam Date** (`Exam Date`, `date`, optional): This field is used to record the date of the exam or assessment. Please use the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.

Note: All fields are optional, and you're not required to fill out any fields that don't pertain to your knowledge or experience with electrolyte imbalances.
