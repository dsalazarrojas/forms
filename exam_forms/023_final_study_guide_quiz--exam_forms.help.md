# Final Study Guide Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Final Study Guide Quiz is a form designed to gather information from students at the end of a study session or program. It helps instructors or program administrators collect data on student progress, performance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Student Information: This is a required field where you should enter your student name and other relevant details.
	* Quiz Information: Describe the quiz or study session you have completed.
2. Answer the questions:
	* Question 1: Select all applicable answers from the options provided.
	* Question 2: Choose the best answer from the available options.
3. Enter dates and times:
	* Date: Enter the date on which you completed the quiz or study session.
	* Time: Enter the time it took you to complete the quiz or study session.
4. Optional fields:
	* Email: If you have a specific email address, enter it here. This is an optional field.
	* Phone: If you have a specific phone number, enter it here. This is an optional field.
	* Notes: You may add notes to provide additional information about your experience, challenges, or progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Student Name):** A field to enter your student name.
* **Quiz Information:** A field to describe the quiz or study session you have completed.
* **Question 1:** Select all applicable answers from the provided options.
* **Question 2:** Choose the best answer from the available options.
* **Date:** Enter the date on which you completed the quiz or study session.
* **Time:** Enter the time it took you to complete the quiz or study session.
* **Email:** (Optional) Enter your specific email address.
* **Phone:** (Optional) Enter your specific phone number.
* **Note 1-16:** (Optional) Add notes to provide additional information about your experience, challenges, or progress.

Note: The notes fields are optional and can be used to add any additional comments or thoughts you have.
