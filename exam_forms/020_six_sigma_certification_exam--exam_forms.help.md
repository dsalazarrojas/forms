# Six Sigma Certification Exam - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Six Sigma Certification Exam form is a crucial document used to assess the readiness of a candidate for Six Sigma certification. It is used to gather information about the candidate's identity, contact details, and the submitter's (trainer or training provider) information. This form is essential for ensuring that the certification process is conducted fairly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Candidate Details:
	* Enter your name in the `Candidate Name` field.
	* Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
	* Check the `Readiness Assessment` field to indicate your readiness for the certification exam.
2. Provide Additional Comments (if necessary):
	* If you have any additional comments or concerns, enter them in the `Additional Comments` field.
3. Enter Submitter Information:
	* Enter your submitter's name in the `Submitter Name` field.
	* Enter your submitter's email address in the `Submitter Email` field.
	* Enter your submitter's phone number in the `Submitter Phone` field.
	* Choose your submitter's role from the `Submitter Role` dropdown menu (e.g., `Training Provider`, `Workplace Learning Team`).
	* Enter your submitter's department or team in the `Submitter Department` field.
4. Confirm Submitter Information:
	* Check that you agree with the certification terms in the `I Agree` field.
	* Enter any comments or concerns in the `I Agree Comments` field (if necessary).
	* Date and time of agreement in the `I Agree Date` and `I Agree Time` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details**
	* (`Candidate ID`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your unique ID number.
	* (`Candidate Name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Readiness Assessment** (`select_one`, required: `false`): Choose whether you are ready for the certification exam.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, required: `false`): Enter any additional comments or concerns.
* **Submitter Information**
	* (`Submitter Name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name as the submitter.
	* (`Submitter Email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address as the submitter.
	* (`Submitter Phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
	* (`Submitter Role`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose your role as the submitter from the dropdown menu (e.g., `Training Provider`, `Workplace Learning Team`).
	* (`Submitter Department`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your department or team name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Details**
	* (`Submitter Certifies`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check whether you are the submitter who certifies that the candidate is ready for the exam.
	* (`Submitter Certifies Comments`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any comments or concerns as the submitter.
	* (`Submitter Signature`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your signature as the submitter.
	* (`I Agree`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check that you agree with the certification terms.
	* (`I Agree Comments`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any comments or concerns as you agree with the certification terms.
	* (`I Agree Date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date you agree with the certification terms.
	* (`I Agree Time`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time you agree with the certification terms.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information for both the candidate and submitter details.
* Review and verify the information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or training provider before proceeding.
