# Exam Restriction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to request restrictions on their exams, allowing them to provide additional information or requirements that need to be considered by the department or faculty members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "Student Information" section with your name, ID number, and student email address.
2. Provide a brief description of the exam restriction in the "Exam Restrictions" section.
3. If necessary, upload relevant documents in the "Upload Documents" section.
4. Get department approval by providing your department's representative with your exam restriction request.
5. Follow up on the submission status by checking the "Submission Status" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information**: This section is where you enter your student information, including your name, ID number, and student email address. This is a required field, and your correct information is essential for processing your request.
* **Exam Restrictions**: This section is where you describe the exam restriction you are requesting. Be as specific as possible about the reason for the request.
* **Upload Documents**: If you need to provide additional documents to support your exam restriction, you can upload them here. This could include medical certificates, academic certificates, or other relevant papers.
* **Department Approval**: This section is where you indicate that you have received approval from your department or faculty member. Please make sure to get approval before submitting this form.
* **Follow-up**: This section is for you to track the status of your exam restriction request. Check here to see if your request has been processed or if you need to follow up with the relevant department or faculty member.
* **Submission Status**: This section shows the status of your exam restriction request. Check here to see if your request has been submitted and processed.
