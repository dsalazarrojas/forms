# Ancient Egypt Architecture Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your knowledge of ancient Egyptian architecture. This quiz will test your understanding of various aspects of ancient Egyptian architecture, including temples, pyramids, and other structures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student full name, student ID number, and course name on pages 1-3.
2. Select the correct answers to the multiple-choice questions on pages 6-17.
3. Write a brief paragraph describing the evolution of pyramid construction from the Step Pyramid to the Great Pyramid for the essay question on page 18.
4. Optionally, provide a self-assessment of your confidence level in your answers on page 19.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID number as assigned to you by your institution.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Type the name of the course or program you are enrolled in.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this exam.
* **Ancient Egypt Architecture Exam Instructions** (`exam_intro`, note, not required): This section is a note field where you can read the instructions for the exam.
* **Which pharaoh commissioned the Great Pyramid of Giza** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Ramses II, Khufu (Cheops), Tutankhamun, or Akhenaten.
* **What is the name of the entrance corridor feature in Egyptian temples** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Hypostyle hall, Pylon gateway, Sacred barque chamber, or Offering table room.
* **The Valley of the Kings is located near which modern Egyptian city** (`question_3`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Cairo, Alexandria, Luxor, or Aswan.
* **What architectural element supported the massive stone roofs in Egyptian temples** (`question_4`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Flying buttresses, Massive columns with papyrus and lotus capitals, Wooden trusses, or Stone arches.
* **Which temple complex is dedicated to the god Amun-Ra** (`question_5`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Abu Simbel, Temple of Hatshepsut, Karnak Temple, or Philae Temple.
* **The Step Pyramid of Djoser was designed by which architect** (`question_6`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Imhotep, Senenmut, Hemiunu, or Bakenkhonsu.
* **The primary purpose of pyramid texts** (`question_7`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Historical records, Religious spells for the afterlife, Architectural blueprints, or Tax records.
* **What material was commonly used for Egyptian temple columns** (`question_8`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the options Limestone, Sandstone, Granite, Marble, Basalt.
* **The Sphinx at Giza has the body of a** (`question_9`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Eagle, Human (pharaoh), Jackal, or Falcon.
* **What orientation did most Egyptian temples follow** (`question_10`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options North-South alignment, East-West alignment with the Nile, Random orientation, or Circular arrangement.
* **Which structure connects the valley temple to the mortuary temple** (`question_11`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Causeway, Bridge, Tunnel, or Staircase.
* **The obelisk was a symbol of which Egyptian deity** (`question_12`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options Osiris, Ra (sun god), Isis, or Anubis.
* **Essay Question - Describe the evolution of pyramid construction from the Step Pyramid to the Great Pyramid** (`essay_question`, text, required): Write a brief paragraph explaining the architectural developments from the Step Pyramid to the Great Pyramid.
* **How confident are you in your answers** (`confidence_level`, select_one, not required): Select the correct answer from the options Very Confident, Somewhat Confident, Neutral, Somewhat Uncertain, or Very Uncertain.
