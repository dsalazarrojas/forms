# Laboratory Materials Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Materials Knowledge Quiz form is designed to collect information about a user's demographics and preferences. This information will help in providing personalized results and insights for the users who complete the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing your information in each field.
2. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Question 1: What is your name?
* **What is your name?** (`question_1`, text, required/optional): This is a text field where you should enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### Question 2: What is your age?
* **What is your age?** (`question_2`, number, required/optional): This is a numerical field where you should enter your current age.

### Question 3: What is your email?
* **What is your email?** (`question_3`, email, required/optional): This is an email field where you should enter your official email address.

### Question 4: What is your phone?
* **What is your phone?** (`question_4`, text, required/optional): This is a text field where you should enter your phone number.

### Question 5: Section 1
* **Section 1** (`section_1`, note, required/optional): This is a section header that separates the questions into different groups. You do not need to enter anything here.

### Question 6: What is your favorite color?
* **What is your favorite color?** (`question_5`, select_one, required/optional): This is a single-selection field where you can choose your favorite color from the options: Red, Blue, or Green.

### Question 7: What is your age group?
* **What is your age group?** (`question_6`, select_multiple, required/optional): This is a multiple-selection field where you can choose your age group from the options: 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, or 65+.

### Question 8: What is your date of birth?
* **What is your date of birth?** (`question_7`, date, required/optional): This is a date field where you should enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Question 9: What time is it?
* **What time is it?** (`question_8`, time, required/optional): This is a time field where you should enter the current time in 12-hour format.

### Question 10: How many hours do you spend in the lab?
* **How many hours do you spend in lab?** (`question_9`, number, required/optional): This is a numerical field where you should enter the number of hours you spend in the lab.
