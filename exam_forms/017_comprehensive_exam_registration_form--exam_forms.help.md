# Comprehensive Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Comprehensive Exam Registration Form is used to register candidates for comprehensive exams. This form collects personal and exam-related information to facilitate the exam process and ensure a smooth experience for candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information ready, including your full legal name, student or registration ID, email address, and preferred exam date.
2. Select your preferred exam location between onsite or online proctored options.
3. Indicate if you need any testing accommodations.
4. Provide any accommodation details if required.
5. Enter your payment reference if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate name** (Candidate name, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
- **Candidate id** (Candidate id, text, optional): Enter your student or registration ID.
- **Email address** (Email address, email, required): Enter your email address for exam confirmation and results.
- **Exam date** (Exam date, date, required): Select your preferred exam date.
- **Exam location** (Exam location, select_one, required): Select your preferred exam location between onsite or online proctored options.
- **Accommodation needed** (Accommodation needed, select_one, required): Indicate if you need testing accommodations.
- **Accommodation details** (Accommodation details, text, optional): Provide any accommodation requests if necessary.
- **Payment reference** (Payment reference, text, optional): Enter your payment ID or PO if applicable.
