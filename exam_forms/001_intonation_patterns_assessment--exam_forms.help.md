# Intonation Patterns Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intonation Patterns Assessment form is used to evaluate and assess the intonation patterns of a subject. This form helps to identify the type of intonation pattern exhibited by a student or subject.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select a subject from the drop-down menu for the "Subject" field.
2. Enter the name of the student or subject in the "Name" field. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information, as this field is required.
3. Choose the correct "Class" for the assessment. This field is also required.
4. Choose one of the following options for "Pattern":
	* Intonation Rising: The subject exhibits a rising intonation pattern.
	* Intonation Falling: The subject exhibits a falling intonation pattern.
	* Intonation Level: The subject exhibits a level intonation pattern.
	* No Intonation: The subject does not exhibit any intonation pattern.
	* No Intonation Level: The subject does not exhibit any intonation pattern.
5. Choose one of the following options for "Pitch":
	* Pitch Rising: The subject exhibits a rising pitch pattern.
	* Pitch Falling: The subject exhibits a falling pitch pattern.
	* Pitch Level: The subject exhibits a level pitch pattern.
	* No Pitch: The subject does not exhibit any pitch pattern.
	* No Pitch Level: The subject does not exhibit any pitch pattern.
6. Choose one or more of the following options for "Rhythm":
	* Rhythmic: The subject exhibits a rhythmic intonation pattern.
	* Non-Rhythmic: The subject does not exhibit a rhythmic intonation pattern.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field (optional).
8. Select a score out of 10 for the assessment in the "Score" field. Make sure to enter a valid number.
9. Confirm your submission by selecting the "Confirm Submit" button.
10. Review your submission by selecting "Confirm View" to review the filled-out form.
11. Save and print or export the form as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subject** (`Subject`, text, required): Enter the name of the subject being assessed.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student or subject being assessed.
* **Class** (`Class`, text, required): Choose the correct class for the assessment.
* **Pattern** (`Pattern`, select_one, required): Choose the correct intonation pattern exhibited by the subject.
* **Pitch** (`Pitch`, select_one, required): Choose the correct pitch pattern exhibited by the subject.
* **Rhythm** (`Rhythm`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the correct rhythmic patterns exhibited by the subject.
* **Score** (`Score`, number, required): Enter a score out of 10 for the assessment.
* **Confirm Submit** (`Confirm Submit`, text, required): Confirm your submission after reviewing the form.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the assessment.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Finalize the submission.
* **Save and View** (`Save and View`, text, optional): Save and view the filled-out form.
* **Print** (`Print`, text, optional): Save and print the filled-out form.
* **Export** (`Export`, text, optional): Save and export the filled-out form.
* **Back** (`Back`, text, optional): Go back to the form to make changes.
* **Save** (`Save`, text, optional): Save the filled-out form without printing or exporting.
* **View** (`View`, text, optional): View the filled-out form without saving changes.
* **Confirm Print** (`Confirm Print`, text, optional): Confirm printing the filled-out form.
* **Confirm View** (`Confirm View`, text, optional): Confirm viewing the filled-out form.
* **Confirm Export** (`Confirm Export`, text, optional): Confirm exporting the filled-out form.
