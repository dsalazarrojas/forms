# exam_schedule_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exam schedule communication form is used to capture information from students about their exam schedule, which is then used to update their exam schedules and facilitate communication with exam staff or proctors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the exam schedule details, including the exam date and time.
2. Provide the exam location where the exam will take place.
3. Enter any additional instructions or comments about the exam.
4. Enter your name, email, and phone number so that we can contact you with any updates or confirmations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **exam_schedule**: <i class="fa fa-calendar"></i> (Date, Required): Enter the date of your exam.
* **exam_location**: <i class="fa fa-map-marker"></i> (Text, Required): Enter the location of your exam.
* **exam_time**: <i class="fa fa-clock"></i> (Time, Required): Enter the time of your exam.
* **exam_instructions**: <i class="fa fa-commenting"></i> (Text, Required): Enter any additional instructions or comments about your exam.
* **exam_date**: <i class="fa fa-calendar"></i> (Date, Required): Confirm the date of your exam.
* **student_name**: <i class="fa fa-id-card"></i> (Text, Required): Enter your name as a student.
* **student_email**: <i class="fa fa-envelope"></i> (Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **student_phone**: <i class="fa fa-phone"></i> (Text, Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your exam details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will use this information to update your exam schedule and keep you informed about any updates or changes.
