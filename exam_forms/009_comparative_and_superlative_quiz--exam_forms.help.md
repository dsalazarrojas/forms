# Comparative And Superlative Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge of comparative and superlative forms in English language. It consists of two sections: "Comparatives" and "Superlatives", where you will be presented with questions that require you to choose the correct comparative or superlative form of a given word or phrase.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the "Student Identification" section with your student's details.
2. In the "Student Full Name" field, enter your student's name as it appears in the school record.
3. In the "Class / Section" field, enter your student's class and section.
4. Select the current date in the "Date" field.
5. Proceed to the "Comparatives" section and answer the questions presented there. Select the correct comparative form from the options provided.
6. In the "Superlatives" section, answer the questions presented there. Select the correct superlative form from the options provided.
7. In the "Rules and Usage" section, answer the question about the usage of comparative adverbs.
8. Finally, in the "Providing Forms" section, type your answer to the questions about the comparative and superlative forms of the adverbs "quickly" and "well".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your student's name as it appears in the school record.
* **Class / Section** (`class_section`, text, required): Enter your student's class and section.
* **Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Select the current date.
* **This mountain is (high) than the one we climbed last week.** (`q1_comparative`, select_one, required): Choose the correct comparative form from: higher, more high, highest, highly.
* **Learning English is (difficult) than I expected.** (`q2_comparative`, select_one, required): Choose the correct comparative form from: difficulter, more difficult, most difficult, as difficult.
* **My new car is (fast) than my old one.** (`q3_comparative`, select_one, required): Choose the correct comparative form from: faster, more fast, fastest, fastly.
* **Jupiter is the (large) planet in our solar system.** (`q4_superlative`, select_one, required): Choose the correct superlative form from: larger, more large, largest, mostly large.
* **She is the (intelligent) person in the class.** (`q5_superlative`, select_one, required): Choose the correct superlative form from: intelligenter, more intelligent, most intelligent, intelligentest.
* **That was the (bad) movie I have ever seen.** (`q6_superlative`, select_one, required): Choose the correct superlative form from: badder, worst, worse, most bad.
* **We use (more) for adjectives with three or more syllables.** (`rules_q7`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer: True or False.
* **Provide the comparative form of the adverb - quickly.** (`comparative_adverbs`, text, required): Type the correct comparative form of the adverb "quickly".
* **Provide the superlative form of the adverb - well.** (`superlative_adverbs`, text, required): Type the correct superlative form of the adverb "well".

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask your teacher or instructor for clarification.
