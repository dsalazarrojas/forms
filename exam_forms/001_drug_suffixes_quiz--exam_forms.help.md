# Drug Suffixes Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a quiz to test your knowledge on drug suffixes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available options for drug class and select the correct class for the drug.
2. Select the correct suffixes for the drug, if applicable.
3. Enter any additional information or answer in the "Drug Prefix" field.
4. Enter your answer to the question.
5. Review your selections and ensure everything is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drug Class** (`drug_class`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the class of the drug from the available options: Acetate, Amino, Citrate, Hydrochloride, Lysine, Maleate, Mesylate, Sulfate, Tosylate.
* **Drug Suffixes** (`drug_suffixes`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the correct suffixes for the drug. This field is optional.
* **Drug Prefix** (`drug_prefix`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information or prefix related to the drug. This field is optional.
* **Drug Suffix** (`drug_suffix`, `text`, required: false): Enter the specific suffix of the drug. This field is optional.
* **Drug Answer** (`answer`, `text`, required: false): Enter your answer to the question. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Please ensure you're only submitting this form once.
* Double-check your selections to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to the available options for guidance.
