# Vision Exam Results Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vision Exam Results Form Template is used to document the results of an eye examination for a patient. This form gathers information about the patient's vision, including their name, contact information, and test results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the email address of the patient.
3. Record the date and time the examination was conducted.
4. Report the results of the refraction test, including any relevant measurements.
5. Report the results of the visual acuity test, including any relevant measurements.
6. Indicate whether the field of vision is within the normal range or not.
7. Report the eye pressure measurements.
8. Document the cornea status, selecting all that apply.
9. Enter the name of the doctor who conducted the examination.
10. Enter any contact information for the patient.
11. Provide any additional comments or notes about the examination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`1_first_name`)
* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.

### Last Name (`1_last_name`)
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.

### Email (`1_email`)
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.

### Date Taken (`1_date_taken`)
* **Date Taken**: Record the date the examination was conducted.

### Time Taken (`1_time_taken`)
* **Time Taken**: Record the time the examination was conducted.

### Refraction (`1_refraction`)
* **Refraction**: Report the results of the refraction test, including any relevant measurements.

### Visual Acuity (`1_visual_acuity`)
* **Visual Acuity**: Report the results of the visual acuity test, including any relevant measurements.

### Field of Vision (`1_field_of_vision`)
* **Field of Vision**: Indicate whether the field of vision is within the normal range or not.

### Eye Pressure (`1_eye_pressure`)
* **Eye Pressure**: Report the eye pressure measurements.

### Cornea Status (`1_cornea_status`)
* **Cornea Status**: Document the cornea status, selecting all that apply.

### Doctor Name (`1_doctor_name`)
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who conducted the examination.

### Contact Info (`1_contact_info`)
* **Contact Info**: Enter any contact information for the patient.

### Comments (`1_comments`)
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the examination.

Note: The form has a total of 12 fields, but some fields are not required.
