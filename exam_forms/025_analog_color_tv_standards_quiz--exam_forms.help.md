# Analog Color TV Standards Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Analog Color TV Standards Quiz form is used to collect information from students related to television broadcasting standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your full name** (`participant_name`):
	* This is the field where you should enter your full name.
2. **Choose the correct television standard** (`question_1`):
	* Select the correct standard used in North America between NTSC, PAL, and SECAM.
3. **Enter the number of lines in NTSC standard** (`question_2`):
	* Type the exact number of lines used in the NTSC standard.
4. **Enter the color subcarrier frequency in MHz** (`question_3`):
	* Type the decimal value of the color subcarrier frequency in MHz.
5. **Add instructor comments (optional)** (`instructor_comments`):
	* If you have any specific comments for the instructor, you can enter them here.
6. **Agree to have your answers reviewed for grading** (`consent`):
	* Select 'True' to agree to have your answers reviewed for grading.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**:
	* This field is for your full name.
	* It is an obligatory field and should be completed accurately.
* **Which standard is primarily used in North America**:
	* This field allows you to select the correct television standard used in North America.
* **Number of lines in NTSC standard**:
	* This field requires you to type the exact number of lines used in the NTSC standard.
	* Make sure to enter the correct value as any incorrect information may affect grading.
* **Color subcarrier frequency in MHz**:
	* This field requires you to enter the decimal value of the color subcarrier frequency in MHz.
* **Instructor comments**:
	* This field is for any additional comments you may want to provide for the instructor.
* **I agree to have my answers reviewed for grading**:
	* Select 'True' to agree to have your answers reviewed for grading purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields accurately.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask your instructor.
