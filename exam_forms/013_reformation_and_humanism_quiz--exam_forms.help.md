# Reformation And Humanism Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to evaluate your knowledge about the Protestant Reformation, its philosophical background, and the era in which it took place. The quiz consists of 6 text-based questions that assess your understanding of key aspects of the Reformation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply type your answers to each question in the corresponding text field. You can type as much or as little as you need to provide a clear and concise answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Protestant Reformation
* **Protestant Reformation** (`protestant_reformation`, text, required/optional): Answer this question in a brief paragraph, providing a brief overview of the Protestant Reformation.
* **Church History** (`church_history`, text, required/optional): Describe the historical context of the Reformation, including key events and figures.
* **Philosophical Background** (`philosophical_background`, text, required/optional): Explain the philosophical influences that shaped the Reformation, including key thinkers and ideas.
* **Theology** (`theology`, text, required/optional): Define the theological principles of the Reformation, including key tenets and practices.
* **Reformation Era** (`reformation_era`, text, required/optional): Describe the time period in which the Reformation took place, including key events and dates.
* **Conclusion** (`conclusion`, text, required/optional): Summarize your understanding of the Reformation, providing a brief conclusion to your answers.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise answers to each question.
* Type as much or as little as you need to answer each question.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
