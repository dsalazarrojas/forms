# LSAT Exam Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The LSAT exam registration form is designed for prospective law students to provide their information to register and prepare for the exam.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Test Score** if you have already taken the LSAT exam. The test score is a required field for registration.
2. Choose a **Exam Date** for your test-taking session. Select the date you plan to take the exam.
3. Enter your **Email** address for contact and communication purposes. This is a required field for registration.
4. Provide your **Phone Number** for test-related communication and updates.
5. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** as they appear on your identification documents.
6. Enter your **Address** for test location and logistics purposes.
7. Enter your **Street** address as part of your location information.
8. Enter your **City** as part of your location information.
9. Enter your **State** as part of your location information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Score** (`test_score`, number, required): Enter your previously obtained LSAT exam test score. If you don't have a score, leave this field blank.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your test taking session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for test-related updates and communications.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for test location and logistics purposes.
* **Street** (`street`, text, optional): Enter your street address as part of your location information.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city as part of your location information.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state as part of your location information.

## Tips

* Double-check that all required fields are filled in accurately before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your test score and exam date are correct and up-to-date.
* Review your form submission before finalizing it to avoid any errors or missing information.
