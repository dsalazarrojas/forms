# expression_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The expression_quiz form is designed to gauge your knowledge and usage of English idioms. It's a set of questions that aim to understand your familiarity with idiomatic expressions in the English language. This form will help us better understand how you engage with and use idioms in your daily life.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, and provide as much detail as you can.
2. Select the options that best apply to you for each question.
3. Fill in the text fields with the information requested.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
