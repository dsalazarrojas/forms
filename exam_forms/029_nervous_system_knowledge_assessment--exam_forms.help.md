# Nervous System Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the user's knowledge about the nervous system, specifically about its different components such as nerve cells, fibers, roots, and trunks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the multiple-choice questions about the nervous system concepts on page 1.
2. Provide feedback about your experience with the form on page 2.
3. Review and confirm your results on page 3.
4. Submit your form to complete the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nervous System Concepts** (`nervous-system-concepts`): Select all that apply from the list of options to demonstrate your knowledge about the nervous system.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`): Enter the date you completed this assessment to track your progress.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Provide any comments or suggestions about this form or its questions.
* **Results** (`results`): Review and confirm your results after completing the assessment.
* **Submit Button** (`submit`): Click to submit your assessment and review your results.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly to ensure that your results are reliable.
