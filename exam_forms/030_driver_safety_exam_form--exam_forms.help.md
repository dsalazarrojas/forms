# Driver Safety Exam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Driver Safety Exam Form is a collection of fields designed to gather information about a driver's vehicle and license details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter your Vehicle Make in the Vehicle Make field.
5. Enter your Vehicle Year in the Vehicle Year field.
6. Enter your Driver License Number in the Driver License Number field.
7. Select the Vehicle Model of your vehicle in the Vehicle Model field.
8. Enter the Vehicle Color of your vehicle in the Vehicle Color field.
9. Enter the year your Vehicle License was issued in the Vehicle Year Issued field.
10. Select if you have a valid Vehicle License in the Vehicle License State field.
11. Select your Vehicle License Class type (e.g., Active, Inactive) in the Vehicle License Class field.
12. Enter your Vehicle License Type in the Vehicle License Type field.
13. Select the Vehicle License Status of your vehicle license in the Vehicle License Status field.
14. Enter your Vehicle License Number in the Vehicle License Number field.
15. Select the Vehicle License State of your vehicle license in the Vehicle License State field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your driver's license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your driver's license.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the make of your vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required/optional): Select the model of your vehicle (e.g., Camry, Mustang, etc.).
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the color of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year Issued** (`vehicle_year_issued`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the year your vehicle license was issued.
* **Vehicle License State** (`vehicle_license_state`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate you have a valid vehicle license, 'No' otherwise.
* **Vehicle License Class** (`vehicle_license_class`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your vehicle license class type (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Vehicle License Type** (`vehicle_license_type`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of your vehicle license.
* **Vehicle License Status** (`vehicle_license_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the status of your vehicle license (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your vehicle license number.
* **Vehicle License State** (`vehicle_license_state`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate you have a valid vehicle license, 'No' otherwise.
