# exam_results_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exam_results_release_form is a tool used to capture and record exam information for students. This form helps in documenting exam details, including results, dates, and attendance, which can be valuable for tracking and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the relevant details about the student, such as their name and contact information (student name and student email).
2. Provide the exam date and time, as well as any remarks if necessary.
3. Enter the exam results, which include the exam score and overall grade if applicable.
4. Complete any other fields as necessary or required for your specific needs, such as attendance or additional remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student being recorded.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Provide the email address of the student, which can be used for contact or communication purposes.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
- **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the student's parent or guardian.
- **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the exam.
- **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the exam if necessary.
- **Essay/Prose** (`prose`, note, optional): A field for any additional comments or remarks related to the exam.
- **Exam Results** (`exam_results`, number, optional): Enter the results of the exam, which could be a score or grade.
- **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the exam results are to be released.
- **Attendance** (`exam_attendance`, text, optional): Provide any information about the student's attendance during the exam.
- **Grade** (`grade`, number, optional): Enter the grade obtained by the student in the exam.
- **Exam Score** (`exam_score`, number, optional): Enter the score obtained by the student in the exam.
- **Overall Grade** (`overall_grade`, number, optional): Enter the overall grade or score for the exam, if applicable.

## Tips
- Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- Ensure that all necessary information is captured according to your specific needs.
- This form is not a substitute for official records, but it can help in maintaining supplementary data for tracking and reporting purposes.
