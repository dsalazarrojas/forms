# BLS Certification Exam - Help Guide

## Purpose
The BLS Certification Exam is a form designed to collect information from medical professionals taking the BLS exam. This form covers personal details, medical professional information, and BLS-specific knowledge questions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready, including contact details, medical professional information, and BLS knowledge.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide your organization or employer information, if applicable.
4. Choose your profession or role in the medical field.
5. Select the type of certification you are seeking (Initial, Renewal, or Challenge Exam).
6. If renewal, provide the date of your last certification.
7. Enter the date of the exam you are taking.
8. Provide the name of your instructor.
9. Specify the location where you received your training.
10. Answer the BLS knowledge questions, including compression rate, compression depth, rescue breaths ratio, AED usage, choking response, and practical skills assessment.
11. Enter your written exam score, if applicable.
12. Select the final exam result.
13. Provide your instructor's signature, if applicable.
14. The certification number will be generated if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: This is your legal name as it should appear on the certificate.
* **Email Address**: This is your contact email address, which will be used for certificate delivery.
* **Phone Number**: This is your contact phone number for further information.
* **Date of Birth**: This is your date of birth, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Organization or Employer**: This is the organization or employer you work for, if applicable.
* **Profession or Role**: This is your profession or role in the medical field, such as Nurse, Physician, EMT or Paramedic, etc.
* **Certification Type**: This is the type of certification you are seeking, such as Initial, Renewal, or Challenge Exam.
* **Previous Certification Date**: If you are renewing your certification, enter the date of your last certification.
* **Exam Date**: This is the date of the exam you are taking.
* **Instructor Name**: This is the name of your course instructor.
* **Training Location**: This is the location where you received your training.
* **CPR Compression Rate Knowledge**: This is the recommended rate for CPR compression, which is 100 to 120 per minute.
* **Compression Depth Knowledge**: This is the recommended compression depth for adults, which is at least 1 inch.
* **Rescue Breaths Ratio**: This is the compression-to-breath ratio for single rescuer, which is 15 compressions to 2 breaths.
* **AED Usage Knowledge**: This is when an AED should be used, which is as soon as available.
* **Choking Response Knowledge**: This is the first response to a choking adult, which is back slaps.
* **Practical Skills Assessment**: This is a yes or no answer, indicating whether you passed the practical skills evaluation.
* **Written Exam Score**: This is the percentage score on the written exam.
* **Exam Result**: This is the final certification outcome, which is either Pass - Certified or Fail - Retake Required.
* **Instructor Signature**: This is the instructor's name or signature, if applicable.
* **Certification Number**: This is the assigned certification ID, which will be generated if required.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure of any field, consult your instructor or exam materials for guidance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
