# Business Licensing Exam Study Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us determine which study materials you need to prepare for your licensing exam. Please fill out this form to let us know what kind of materials you require and how you'd like to receive them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Applicant Email**: Enter your email address where you can receive the study materials.
	* **Applicant Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
	* **Mailing Address**: Enter your address where you'd like to receive physical materials (if needed).
2. Select your exam type:
	* **Licensing Exam Type**: Choose the type of exam you're preparing for (e.g., Real estate license, Contractor license, etc.).
3. Provide exam details:
	* **Scheduled Exam Date**: If you know your exam date, please enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
4. Choose your material preferences:
	* **Preferred Material Format**: Select how you'd like to receive your materials (e.g., Digital download, Online access, etc.).
	* **Material Types Needed**: Choose all the types of materials you require (e.g., Study guide, Practice exams, etc.).
5. Share your background:
	* **Prior Experience in Field**: Indicate your level of experience in the field (e.g., No experience, Less than 1 year, etc.).
	* **Education Level**: Choose your highest completed education level (e.g., High school, Some college, etc.).
6. Provide additional information:
	* **Previous Exam Attempts**: If you have taken this exam before, let us know how many times.
	* **Study Timeline**: Choose how long you plan to study (e.g., Less than 2 weeks, 2-4 weeks, etc.).
	* **Preferred Learning Style**: Select how you learn best (e.g., Reading text, Watching videos, etc.).
7. Optional fields:
	* **Budget for Study Materials**: If you're concerned about costs, let us know how much you're willing to spend.
	* **Employer Sponsorship**: If your employer is sponsoring your exam materials, let us know.
	* **Accessibility Needs**: If you require any accessibility accommodations, please indicate.
	* **Language Preference**: If you prefer to receive materials in a different language, let us know.
	* **How Did You Hear About Us**: If you'd like to share how you found out about us, please select from the options.
	* **Additional Requests**: If you have any other requests or needs, please let us know in this field.
