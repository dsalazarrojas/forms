# Docker Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your knowledge about Docker, a platform for containerization of applications. It is designed to evaluate your understanding of Docker's benefits, use cases, best practices, and common mistakes. This quiz will help you evaluate your familiarity with Docker and identify areas where you can improve your skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each question carefully and select the most accurate response from the options provided.
2. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability, even if you are not familiar with a particular topic.
3. For text fields, provide a brief explanation in the provided text box.
4. For select_one and select_multiple fields, select one or multiple options that you believe are correct.
5. Click the submit button to submit your answers and review them before submission.
