# Mini Exam Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mini Exam Quiz form is a series of short questions designed to assess your knowledge on specific topics. It is meant to be a fun and interactive way to gauge your understanding of the subject matter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer the ones you know the most about.
2. Select the best possible answer for each question, using the options provided.
3. Enter your email address and phone number, if you wish to be contacted with your score.
4. Click the "Submit" button to complete the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **main page**: This is the introductory page with general information about the quiz.
* **question 1**: Choose the option that best answers the question presented.
* **question 2**: Choose the option that best answers the question presented.
* **score**: Enter your self-assessed score for each question.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your answers and receive your score.
* **email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted with your score.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted with your score.

## Tips
* Be honest with your answers, as the quiz is meant to be a self-assessment.
* Do not worry about getting a high or low score, it's just for fun!
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
