# Elementary School Entrance Exam Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to register their child for an elementary school entrance exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Applicant Name, which is the name of the child registering for the exam.
2. Enter the Parent Name, which is the name of the parent or guardian responsible for the child.
3. Select the grade level of the child registering for the exam.
4. Enter the date of the exam in the Test Date field.
5. Enter the time of the exam in the Test Time field.
6. Enter the address of the school where the exam will take place in the School Address field.
7. Enter the email address of the parent or guardian in the Email field.
8. Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian in the Phone field.
9. Click the Submit button to confirm the submission of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the child registering for the exam.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian responsible for the child.
* **Grade Level** (`grade`, select_one, required/optional): Select the grade level of the child registering for the exam.
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the exam.
* **Test Time** (`test_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the exam.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the school where the exam will take place.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required/optional): Click this button to confirm the submission of the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that the name and contact information entered are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please contact us for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
