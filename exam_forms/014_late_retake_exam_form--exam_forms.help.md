# late_retake_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to retake exams that you have previously failed or not completed. Please fill in the form with your reason for retaking the exam and your class schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the reason for retaking the exam in the "Reason" field.
2. Select your class schedule for the exam in the "Classes" field.
3. Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retake Exams**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter the reason for retaking the exam.
* **Reason**: Please enter the reason why you are retaking the exam. This will help the examiner understand your situation.
* **Classes**: This field allows you to select the classes you are taking that are relevant to the exam. If you are not taking any classes, please select "No".
* **Submit**: This is the final step to submit your form. Once you click this button, your form will be processed.
