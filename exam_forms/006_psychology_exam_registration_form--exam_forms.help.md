# Psychology Exam Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Psychology Exam Registration Form is designed to gather information from candidates to facilitate a smooth and efficient exam experience. It is used to collect data about the candidate's enrollment status, session scheduling preferences, accommodation needs, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.
2. For each field, provide the requested type of information (e.g., date, time, text, etc.).
3. Be sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Candidate Enrollment** (`candidate_enrollment`, `number`, optional)
	* This field is used to indicate if the candidate is enrolled in any educational institution.
### 2. **Session Scheduling** (`session_scheduling`, `date`, optional)
	* Provide your preferred session schedule (date) for the exam.
### 3. **Testing Center** (`testing_center`, `select_one`, optional)
	* Select 'Yes' if you require a testing center for the exam.
### 4. **Accommodation Requests** (`accommodation_requests`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	* List any accommodation requests you may have, such as 'Yes' for wheelchair accessibility or 'No' if you don't need any accommodations.
### 5. **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, `date`, optional)
	* Enter the date you prefer for the exam.
### 6. **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, `time`, optional)
	* Enter the time you prefer for the exam.
### 7. **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional)
	* Enter the name of the school or institution you're affiliated with.
### 8. **Exam Session ID** (`exam_session_id`, `text`, optional)
	* Enter a unique ID for the exam session, if required.
### 9. **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, optional)
	* Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
### 10. **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional)
	* Enter your email address for communication purposes.
### 11. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
	* Enter your contact phone number.
### 12. **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, optional)
	* Enter the date of the exam session.
### 13. **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, optional)
	* Enter the time of the exam session.
### 14. **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	* Select 'Yes' for any accommodation requests, such as wheelchair accessibility.
### 15. **Exam Type** (`exam_type`, `select_one`, optional)
	* Select 'Yes' if you're taking a specific type of exam.
### 16. **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, `select_one`, optional)
	* Select 'Yes' if you require the exam to be held at a specific location.

**Tips**

* Be sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask your exam administrator for help.
* This form will help us tailor the exam experience to your needs; your cooperation is greatly appreciated.
