# professional_certification_practice_exam_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals assess their readiness for a professional certification exam. The goal is to provide a self-assessment tool to gauge an individual's knowledge and experience before taking the exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select your response from the available options.
2. For the date and time fields, enter the date and time in the correct format (DD/MM/YY and HH:MM).
3. For the email and phone fields, enter your valid email address and phone number.
4. For the text field, enter a brief description of your relevant experience.
5. For the integer field, enter a whole number that represents your relevant experience (e.g., number of years of experience).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Multiple Fields** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the fields that apply to your situation.
* **Select One Field** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the available options that best describes your situation.
* **Date Field** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Time Field** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Text Field** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your relevant experience.
* **Integer Field** (`number`, required: false): Enter a whole number that represents your relevant experience.
