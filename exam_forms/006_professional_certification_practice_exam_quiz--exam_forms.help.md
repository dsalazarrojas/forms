# professional_certification_practice_exam_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals assess their readiness for a professional certification exam. The goal is to provide a self-assessment tool to gauge an individual's knowledge and experience before taking the exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select your response from the available options.
2. For the date and time fields, enter the date and time in the correct format (DD/MM/YY and HH:MM).
3. For the email and phone fields, enter your valid email address and phone number.
4. For the text field, enter a brief description of your relevant experience.
5. For the integer field, enter a whole number that represents your relevant experience (e.g., number of years of experience).
