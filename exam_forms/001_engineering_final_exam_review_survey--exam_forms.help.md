# engineering_final_exam_review_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The engineering final exam review survey is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from engineering students for the purpose of reviewing their exam session. This form will help to identify areas of improvement and suggest ways to better support students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options in the "Session feedback" field (field 1) to provide a brief description of your experience with the exam session.
2. For the "Session effectiveness" field (field 2), select one of the options to rate how effective you found the session.
3. In the "Suggestions for improvement" field (field 3), provide any suggestions you have for improvement for the exam session.
4. Select one or more options in the "Exam preparation" field (field 4) to indicate whether you found the exam preparation helpful or not.
5. In the "Instructor feedback" field (field 5), provide any feedback you have for the instructor who led the session.
6. For the "Student feedback" field (field 6), select one or more options to indicate whether you felt supported by your peers or not during the session.
7. Enter the date of the review session in the "Review session date" field (field 7).
8. Enter the time of the review session in the "Review session time" field (field 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session feedback (Field 1)** (`session_feedback`, select_one, not required): This field is a brief description of your experience with the exam session.
* **Session effectiveness (Field 2)** (`session_effectiveness`, select_one, not required): This field is used to rate how effective you found the session.
* **Suggestions for improvement (Field 3)** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, not required): This field is for providing any suggestions you have for improvement for the exam session.
* **Exam preparation (Field 4)** (`exam_preparation`, select_multiple, not required): This field is used to indicate whether you found the exam preparation helpful or not.
* **Instructor feedback (Field 5)** (`instructor_feedback`, text, not required): This field is for providing feedback for the instructor who led the session.
* **Student feedback (Field 6)** (`student_feedback`, select_multiple, not required): This field is used to indicate whether you felt supported by your peers or not during the session.
* **Review session date (Field 7)** (`review_session_date`, date, not required): This field is for entering the date of the review session.
* **Review session time (Field 8)** (`review_session_time`, time, not required): This field is for entering the time of the review session.
