**Knowledge Assessment On ERP And ITIL - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the knowledge of IT professionals about Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and IT Infrastructure Library (ITIL) principles and practices. It is meant to gauge the level of understanding and familiarity of IT service management processes and systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer each question based on your knowledge and experience.
3. Fill out each field as instructed and submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ERP Module** (id: 2, type: select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if you are familiar with ERP principles and practices, 'No' if not.
* **ITIL Principles** (id: 3, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' for each ITIL principle you are familiar with.
* **ERP And ITIL Relationship** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): Describe your understanding of the relationship between ERP and ITIL.
* **Organization Impact** (id: 5, type: number, required: false): Enter the number of organizations where you have applied ERP and/or ITIL principles.
* **Knowledge Areas** (id: 6, type: select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if you have knowledge in the areas covered by ITIL, 'No' if not.
* **Form Submission** (id: 7, type: date, required: false): Enter the date of your form submission.
