# Sympathomimetic Pharmacology Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sympathomimetic Pharmacology Quiz is designed to evaluate user knowledge of pharmacology concepts related to sympathomimetic drugs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question in the "Quiz Questions" section with your best effort.
2. For the "Pharmacology Drug Information" section, select one of the provided options, indicating whether the drug has a certain property or does not.
3. In the "Pharmacology Mechanisms" section, provide a brief explanation of the mechanism of action.
4. The "Pharmacology Drug Interactions" section is a text field where you can provide any additional information about potential drug interactions.
5. In the "Pharmacology Nursing Implications" section, provide any relevant information about the implications of this drug on nursing practice.
6. Enter a score for the quiz out of 10 in the "Pharmacology Quiz Score" section.
7. Provide any feedback or comments in the "Pharmacology Quiz Feedback" section.
8. Enter the date of completion in the "Pharmacology Quiz Completion Date" section.
9. Enter the time of completion in the "Pharmacology Quiz Time" section.
10. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Pharmacology Quiz Notes" section.
11. Enter your email address in the "Pharmacology Quiz Email" section.
12. Enter your phone number in the "Pharmacology Quiz Phone" section.
13. Provide any additional comments in the "Pharmacology Quiz Comments" sections 3-13.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Questions (1)** (`quiz_questions`, text, required): Answer each question with your best effort.
* **Pharmacology Drug Information (2)** (`pharmacology_drug_info`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for the drug's property.
* **Pharmacology Mechanisms (3)** (`pharmacology_mechanisms`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of the mechanism of action.
* **Pharmacology Drug Interactions (4)** (`pharmacology_drug_interactions`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about potential drug interactions.
* **Pharmacology Nursing Implications (5)** (`pharmacology_nursing_implications`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the implications of this drug on nursing practice.
* **Pharmacology Quiz Score (6)** (`pharmacology_quiz_score`, number, optional): Enter a score for the quiz out of 10.
* **Pharmacology Quiz Feedback (7)** (`pharmacology_quiz_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments.
* **Pharmacology Quiz Completion Date (8)** (`pharmacology_quiz_completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of completion.
* **Pharmacology Quiz Time (9)** (`pharmacology_quiz_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of completion.
* **Pharmacology Quiz Notes (10)** (`pharmacology_quiz_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Pharmacology Quiz Email (11)** (`pharmacology_quiz_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Pharmacology Quiz Phone (12)** (`pharmacology_quiz_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Pharmacology Quiz Comments** (`pharmacology_quiz_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments in the sections 3-13.

Note that all fields with "Comments" in their name allow you to provide additional comments, and you can provide any information that you think is relevant to the question or section.
