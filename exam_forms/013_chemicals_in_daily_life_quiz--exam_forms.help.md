# Chemicals In Daily Life Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This quiz is designed to test your knowledge of chemical concepts and their applications in everyday life.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, considering your current knowledge and understanding.
2. Select the correct option for each question from the given options.
3. Rate your confidence level for each question after answering.
4. Provide any additional comments about the quiz at the end.
