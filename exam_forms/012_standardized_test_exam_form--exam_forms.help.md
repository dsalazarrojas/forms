# Standardized Test Exam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details about a standardized test you have taken. Please fill it out with your test results and relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Type your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Choose the type of test you took in the "Test Type" field.
4. Enter your grade or score in the "Grade" field.
5. Select the date you took the test in the "Date" field.
6. Choose the time you took the test in the "Time" field.
7. Optionally, provide additional notes about your test experience in the "Note" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Select the result of your test in the "Test Result" field.
10. Enter your test score in the "Test Score" field.
11. Enter the total score out of, if applicable, in the "Score Out of" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your test experience. This field is not required.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification or document.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Test Type** (`test_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of test you took from the provided options.
* **Grade** (`grade`, text, optional): Enter your grade or score achieved in the test.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you took the test.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time you took the test.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Optionally, provide additional notes or comments about your test experience.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, select_multiple, optional): Select the result of your test from the provided options.
* **Test Score** (`test_score`, text, optional): Enter your test score.
* **Score Out of** (`score_out_of`, text, optional): Enter the total score out of, if applicable.
