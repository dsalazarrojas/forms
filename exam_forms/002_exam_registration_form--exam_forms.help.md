# Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exam Registration Form is designed to collect information from students for exam registration purposes. This form is essential for students to register for exams and ensure that their registration is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your student name in the "student name" field.
2. Enter your student email in the "student email" field. Make sure to enter a valid email address that can be reached by the university.
3. Enter your student phone number in the "student phone" field.
4. Enter the exam ID in the "exam id" field.
5. If you wish to schedule the exam, enter the exam date and time in the "exam date" and "exam time" fields. Note that these fields are optional.
6. Enter the exam location in the "exam location" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or details, you can enter them in the "exam details" field.
8. Select the status of your exam from the "exam status" dropdown menu.
9. Choose the type of exam you are taking from the "exam type" dropdown menu.
10. Select all the exam centers where you would like to take the exam from the "exam center" dropdown menu.
11. Enter any comments for the exam in the "exam comments" field. This field is optional.
12. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "submitted by" field.
13. If you wish to track the submission time, enter the date and time of submission in the "submitted on" field. Note that this field is optional.
14. Enter the name of the exam center where you will be taking the exam in the "exam center name" field. This field is optional.
15. Enter the name of the exam type you are taking in the "exam type name" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter your student name in this field.
* **Student Email (student_email)**: Enter your student email address in this field. Make sure to enter a valid email address that can be reached by the university.
* **Student Phone (student_phone)**: Enter your student phone number in this field.
* **Exam ID (exam_id)**: Enter the exam ID in this field.
* **Exam Date (exam_date)**: If you wish to schedule the exam, enter the date of the exam in this field. Note that this field is optional.
* **Exam Time (exam_time)**: If you wish to schedule the exam, enter the time of the exam in this field. Note that this field is optional.
* **Exam Location (exam_location)**: Enter the location of the exam in this field.
* **Exam Details (exam_details)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the exam in this field. This field is optional.
* **Exam Status (exam_status)**: Select the status of your exam from the dropdown menu. Options include "Pending", "Confirmed", and "Cancelled".
* **Exam Type (exam_type)**: Choose the type of exam you are taking from the dropdown menu. Options include "Theory", "Practical", and "Theory-Practical".
* **Exam Center (exam_center)**: Select all the exam centers where you would like to take the exam from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or multiple centers.
* **Exam Comments (exam_comments)**: Enter any comments for the exam in this field. This field is optional.
* **Submitted By (submitted_by)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form in this field.
* **Submitted On (submitted_on)**: If you wish to track the submission time, enter the date and time of submission in this field. Note that this field is optional.
* **Exam Center Name (exam_center_name)**: Enter the name of the exam center where you will be taking the exam in this field. This field is optional.
* **Exam Type Name (exam_type_name)**: Enter the name of the exam type you are taking in this field. This field is optional.
