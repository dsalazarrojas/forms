# Cosmetology Practical Exam Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cosmetology Practical Exam Evaluation Checklist is a tool for examiners to evaluate a student's performance during a cosmetology practical exam. It helps examiners assess the student's skills and provide feedback on their strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name and ID number in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the examiner's ID number.
3. Record the exam start and end dates.
4. Assess the skills evaluated during the exam by checking the "Skills Evaluated" field.
5. Select the exam location and type.
6. Enter the exam duration and time.
7. Assess the exam result and select the corresponding option.
8. Enter any comments or feedback for the student.
9. Enter any additional exam notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exam Form** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is not used in the actual form completion, but it is included in the form's metadata.
* **Student Name** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the student's name as it appears on their ID card.
* **Student ID** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their ID card.
* **Examiner ID** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your own ID number as the examiner.
* **Score** (`5`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the score given to the student, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any comments or feedback for the student.
* **Skills Evaluated** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the skills that were evaluated during the exam by checking the corresponding boxes. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Exam Start Date** (`8`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date and time the exam started.
* **Exam End Date** (`9`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date and time the exam ended.
* **Skills Assessed** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the skills that were assessed during the exam by checking the corresponding boxes. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Exam Location** (`11`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the location where the exam took place. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Examiner Name** (`12`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name as the examiner.
* **Score Outcome** (`13`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the final score outcome, if applicable.
* **Outcome Comments** (`14`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any comments on the outcome of the exam.
* **Exam Duration** (`15`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the duration of the exam in hours and minutes.
* **Exam Type** (`16`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the type of exam taken. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Student Feedback** (`17`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any feedback the student gave about their performance.
* **Examiner Feedback** (`18`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your feedback on the student's performance.
* **Exam Date** (`19`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date the exam took place.
* **Exam Session** (`20`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the exam session. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Exam Status** (`21`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the status of the exam. Options: Active, Inactive
* **Exam Time** (`22`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the duration of the exam in hours and minutes.
* **Skills Level** (`23`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the level of skills demonstrated by the student. Options: High, Low
* **Exam Result** (`24`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the result of the exam. Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Exam Notes** (`25`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional notes about the exam.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of your entries.
* Use clear and concise language when entering comments and feedback.
* Review the exam result carefully and select the correct option.
* Use this form to guide your evaluation, but also use your professional judgment when assessing the student's performance.
