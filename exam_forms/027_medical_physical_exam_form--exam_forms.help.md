# Medical Physical Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Physical Exam Form is a crucial tool for healthcare professionals to gather essential information about patients undergoing a medical examination. It helps in creating a comprehensive patient profile and facilitates accurate documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing general information about the patient, including their name, date of birth, and medical record number.
2.  Proceed to the patient's medical history, detailing any allergies, medications, and previous medical conditions.
3.  Record the patient's physical exam details, including their vital signs, medical specialty, and exam type.
4.  Specify the patient's gender and age.
5.  Include any relevant medical notes and the date of the examination.
6.  Finally, fill in the details of the examiner, including their name, license number, and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: A text field where you can enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other general information.
* **Patient Information (2)**: Enter the patient's details, such as their date of birth, address, and contact information.
* **Medical History (3)**: Record any significant medical history or previous medical conditions that may be relevant to the patient's current examination.
* **Additional Medical History (4)**: Provide more detailed medical history if necessary.
* **Physical Exam (5)**: Document the findings of the physical exam, including any notable observations or results.
* **Vital Signs (6)**: Enter the patient's vital signs, such as their heart rate, temperature, and blood pressure.
* **Allergies (7)**: List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medications (8)**: Record any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.
* **Family History (9)**: Document any significant family medical history.
* **Social History (10)**: Provide information on the patient's social history, including their occupation and smoking habits.
* **Medical Notes (11)**: Include any relevant medical notes or observations from the examination.
* **Exam Date (12)**: Enter the date of the examination.
* **Examiner Name (13)**: Enter your name as the examiner.
* **Examiner License (14)**: Enter your medical license number.
* **Examiner Title (15)**: Enter your medical title or position.
* **Hospital (16)**: Enter the hospital name or facility where the examination took place.
* **Doctor (17)**: Enter the doctor's name who performed the examination.
* **Nurse (18)**: Enter the nurse's name who assisted with the examination.
* **Other Staff (19)**: Enter any other staff members involved in the examination.
* **Medical Specialty (20)**: Select the relevant medical specialty for the examination.
* **Exam Type (21)**: Select the type of examination, such as initial, follow-up, or consultation.
* **Patient Gender (22)**: Choose the patient's gender.
* **Patient Age (23)**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Medical Record Number (24)**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Medical Record ID (25)**: Enter the patient's medical record ID.
