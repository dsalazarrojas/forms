# traffic_enforcement_agent_exam_preparation_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quiz for Traffic Enforcement Agents to test their knowledge and abilities. It is designed to prepare them for their exams and assess their understanding of various traffic enforcement-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the correct answer.
2. Fill in any numerical questions with the correct numerical value.
3. Enter your personal information, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
4. Review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (id: 1, label: introduction, type: text, optional): This field is an introduction to the quiz, explaining the purpose and rules of the assessment.
* **Exam Part A** (id: 2, label: exam_part_a, type: select_one, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have taken a certain action or have a certain qualification.
* **Question One** (id: 3, label: question_one, type: number, optional): Fill in the numerical value that corresponds to your answer to question one.
* **Question Two** (id: 4, label: question_two, type: number, optional): Fill in the numerical value that corresponds to your answer to question two.
* **Exam Part B** (id: 5, label: exam_part_b, type: select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your situation.
* **Question Three** (id: 6, label: question_three, type: date, optional): Fill in the date that corresponds to your answer to question three.
* **Question Four** (id: 7, label: question_four, type: time, optional): Fill in the time that corresponds to your answer to question four.
* **Note** (id: 8, label: note, type: note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes that you would like to include with your submission.
* **Email** (id: 9, label: email, type: email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone** (id: 10, label: phone, type: text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Submitted By** (id: 11, label: submitted_by, type: select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you submitted the form.
* **Submitted At** (id: 12, label: submitted_at, type: time, optional): Fill in the time of submission (if applicable).
