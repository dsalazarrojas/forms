# lean_six_sigma_certification_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lean_six_sigma_certification_exam_form is a form designed to collect information for Lean Six Sigma Certification Exam submissions. This form is intended for candidates seeking certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the Exam Submission page with required information about the exam.
* Move to the Candidate Information page and fill out the requested candidate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exam Submission** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the first page of the form, where you should provide the relevant details about the exam you are taking. This page includes fields such as exam date, location, and level.
* **Candidate Info** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Please select the relevant option from the Candidate Info dropdown that applies to you.
* **Email Address** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Candidate Name** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Exam Location** (`6`, `text`, optional): Provide the location of the exam.
* **Exam Date** (`7`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the exam.
* **Exam Time** (`8`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the exam.
* **Exam Duration** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the duration of the exam.
* **Exam Level** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all relevant options from the Exam Level dropdown.
* **Exam Notes** (`11`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the exam.
* **Exam Category** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the relevant option from the Exam Category dropdown.
* **Exam Comments** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the exam.
* **Form Comments** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about the form.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check your information before submission.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact support.
