# Automated Quiz Score Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for a quiz that assesses general knowledge and critical thinking skills. By completing this form, participants will receive feedback on their performance and potentially identify areas where they need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling out your Participant Information**:
	* Enter your full name and email address to identify yourself as a participant.
	* Leave the Participant ID or Student Number field optional, but if you have one, please fill it out for your records.
2. **Enter Quiz Information**:
	* Type in the title of the quiz you completed.
	* Select the date you took the quiz.
3. **Answer the Quiz Questions**:
	* Select the correct answers to each question using the provided options.
	* Make sure to select the correct answer for each question carefully.
4. **Evaluate Your Performance**:
	* Choose how you found the quiz difficulty level.
	* Estimate the time spent on the quiz.
5. **Submit Your Results**:
	* Read over your answers carefully to ensure they are complete and accurate.
	* Select "Yes, submit" to confirm you are satisfied with your answers.
6. **Leave Additional Feedback** (optional):
	* You can add any comments or suggestions for future quiz development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Type in your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you with results and feedback.
* **Participant ID or Student Number**: Leave optional, but fill in if you have one for your records.
* **Quiz Title**: Type in the title of the quiz you just completed.
* **Quiz Date**: Enter the date you took the quiz.
* **Question 1 - What is the capital of France?**: Choose Paris from the options.
* **Question 2 - What is the chemical formula for water?**: Choose H2O from the options.
* **Question 3 - Who wrote Romeo and Juliet?**: Choose William Shakespeare from the options.
* **Question 4 - What year did World War II end?**: Choose '1945' from the options.
* **Question 5 - What is the largest planet in our solar system?**: Choose Jupiter from the options.
* **Question 6 - Which element has the chemical symbol Au?**: Choose Gold from the options.
* **Question 7 - What is the square root of 144?**: Choose '12' from the options.
* **Question 8 - Which country is home to the Great Wall?**: Choose China from the options.
* **How did you find the quiz difficulty?**: Choose how you found the quiz difficulty level from the options.
* **Approximate Time Spent**: Leave this field optional, but estimate the time in minutes you spent on the quiz.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments**: You can add any comments or suggestions for future quiz development.
* **I confirm my answers are complete and accurate**: Select "Yes, submit" to confirm you are satisfied with your answers.
