# Open Ended Student Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Ended Student Response Form is designed to collect student feedback, comments, and grades for assessment and evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, please follow these steps:
    - Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
    - Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
    - Enter your Email address in the **Email** field.
    - Provide your comments in the **Comments** field (optional).
    - Choose the name of the course you are submitting for in the **Course** field.
    - Enter your Teacher's Name in the **Teacher Name** field.
    - Enter the date of submission in the **Submission Date** field.
    - Enter the time of submission in the **Submission Time** field.
    - Select your Student ID status in the **Student ID** field (select one of the options).
    - Select your Student ID status in the **Student ID 2** field (select one of the options).
    - Enter your Grade Point values in the **Grade Point**, **Grade Point 2**, **Grade Point 3**, **Grade Point 4**, **Grade Point 5**, **Grade Point 6**, **Grade Point 7**, **Grade Point 8**, **Grade Point 9**, and **Grade Point 10** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Comments** (optional): Provide any comments about your learning experience.
* **Course** (required): Choose the name of the course you are submitting for.
* **Teacher Name** (required): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Submission Date** (required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (required): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Student ID** (required): Select one of the options for your student ID status.
* **Student ID 2** (required): Select one of the options for your student ID status.
* **Grade Point**, **Grade Point 2**, **Grade Point 3**, **Grade Point 4**, **Grade Point 5**, **Grade Point 6**, **Grade Point 7**, **Grade Point 8**, **Grade Point 9**, and **Grade Point 10** (optional): Enter your grade points for each field, or leave them blank if not applicable.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and truthful information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your teacher or instructor for guidance.
* The more detailed and honest your responses, the more valuable the data will be for your instructors.
