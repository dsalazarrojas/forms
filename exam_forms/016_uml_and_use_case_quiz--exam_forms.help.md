# UML And Use Case Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about UML (Unified Modeling Language) and Use Case Quiz data. It is used to gather user responses for a specific quiz that covers UML and use case concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Name` field with your name.
2. Choose the correct option from the `Use Case Quiz` field.
3. Enter your response for the `Answer` field.
4. Select any applicable options from the `Submission` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `Email` field (optional).
6. Set the `Submission Date` and `Submission Time` fields according to your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Use Case Quiz** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct option that best describes your choice for the UML and use case quiz question.
* **Answer** (`3`, `number`, required): Provide your response for the UML and use case quiz question. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Submission Date** (`5`, `date`, required): Select the date of when you are submitting the quiz.
* **Submission Time** (`6`, `time`, required): Select the time of when you are submitting the quiz.
* **Submission** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose any applicable options that describe the submission circumstances.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes (if you wish to be contacted about your submission).
