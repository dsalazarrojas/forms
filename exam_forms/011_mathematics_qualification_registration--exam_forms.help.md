# Mathematics Qualification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register individuals for a mathematics qualification, allowing them to access specific tools and resources tailored to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your education level from the "Education Level" field.
6. Choose your exam preferences from the "Exam Preferences" field.
7. Confirm your consent by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Consent" field.
8. Add any additional note or comment in the "Note" field.
9. If applicable, select a tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Finally, upload any relevant output file in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your highest level of education completed (A-Levels, Bachelors, Masters, PhD).
*   **Exam Preferences** (`exam_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the subject areas you wish to specialize in (Math, Science, English, Other).
*   **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm your consent for processing your information (True/False).
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional note or comment that would be helpful to us.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): If assigned a tool, enter the name of the tool here.
*   **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required: false): Upload any relevant output files related to your qualification.
