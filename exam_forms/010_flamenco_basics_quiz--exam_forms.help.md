# Flamenco Basics Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flamenco Basics Quiz form collects information from students and their teachers for administrative or assessment purposes. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your data is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Flamenco Basics Quiz form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your level of expertise from the Level field.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the Notes field (this field is optional).
7. Enter your school's name in the School field (select from the options provided).
8. Enter your school's address in the School Address field.
9. Enter your teacher's first name in the Teacher First Name field.
10. Enter your teacher's last name in the Teacher Last Name field.
11. Select your teacher's name from the Teacher field (select from the options provided).
12. Enter your teacher's email address in the Teacher Email field.
13. Enter your teacher's phone number in the Teacher Phone field (this field is optional).
14. Enter your school's phone number in the School Phone field.
15. Enter your school's second address in the School Address 2 field.
16. Enter your school's third address in the School Address 3 field.
17. Enter your school's fourth address in the School Address 4 field.
18. Enter your teacher's second phone number in the Teacher Phone 2 field (this field is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as per the school's records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as per the school's records.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for correspondence.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that the school can use to contact you.
* **Level**: Select your level of expertise in Flamenco, which could be beginner, intermediate, advanced, or master.
* **Notes**: Add any comments or notes you have about your participation in the program (this field is optional).
* **School**: Select your school from the options provided.
* **School Address**: Enter your school's address.
* **Teacher First Name**: Enter your teacher's first name as per the school's records.
* **Teacher Last Name**: Enter your teacher's last name as per the school's records.
* **Teacher**: Select your teacher's name from the options provided.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter your teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter your teacher's phone number (this field is optional).
* **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Address 2**: Enter an additional address for your school.
* **School Address 3**: Enter another address for your school.
* **School Address 4**: Enter a final address for your school.
* **Teacher Phone 2**: Enter your teacher's second phone number (this field is optional).
