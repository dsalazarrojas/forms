# Sentence Type Identification Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to identify the type of sentence (declarative, imperative, interrogative, or exclamatory) based on a series of numerical answers provided by the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will be asked to provide numerical answers to 24 questions. Please answer each question according to the given options and ensure that you fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Declarative Sentences** (select_one): Choose the type of sentence that best describes the question.
* **Answer1-Answer24** (number): Provide a numerical answer corresponding to the question.
* **Notes** (note): Enter any additional comments or notes related to the question.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further assistance.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further assistance.
* **Submit** (text): Click this button to submit your answers and get your results.
