# Exam Package Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you, as a student, express your preferences for exam packages. By filling out this form, you will be able to suggest the type of exam package that best suits your learning goals and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, choose the most relevant response based on your preferences.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Exam Goals
* **Student Exam Goals** (1. *required*): This field is where you can briefly describe your primary or most important exam goals. Please be as specific as possible to help us understand what you are aiming to achieve.

### Package Interests
* **Package Interests** (2, *select one*): Select the interest that best represents your preferred exam package topic. You can choose only one option, and your selection will help us understand what type of exam package would be most appealing to you.
  * 1: Business or Entrepreneurship
  * 2: Computer Science or IT
  * 3: Other (please specify)

### Study Format Preferences
* **Study Format Preferences** (3, *select multiple*): Choose the study format that best suits your learning style. You can select multiple options, and your choices will help us understand how you prefer to study. 
  * 1: Online video lectures
  * 2: In-person classes
  * 3: Self-paced study materials
  * 4: Interactive simulations

## Tips
* Please take your time to think about your preferences and choose the options that best represent your goals and interests.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us.
* Your input will help us create more relevant and effective exam packages for you and your peers.
