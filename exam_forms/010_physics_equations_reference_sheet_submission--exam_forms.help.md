# Physics Equations Reference Sheet Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physics Equations Reference Sheet Submission form is designed to collect information from users about physics equations. Users can submit and share their knowledge about physics equations, which can be useful for educational or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title in the "physics_equations" field.
2. Write a detailed description of the equation in the "descriptions" field.
3. Provide sources for the equation in the "sources" field.
4. Enter additional physics equations in the "physics_equations_2" and "physics_equations_3" fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **physics_equations** (`physics_equations`, text, required: false)
* Enter a brief title of the physics equation you want to submit.

### **descriptions** (`descriptions`, text, required: false)
* Provide a detailed description of the equation, explaining its relevance and context.

### **sources** (`sources`, text, required: false)
* Enter sources where you found the equation, including websites, books, or research papers.

### **physics_equations_2** (`physics_equations_2`, text, required: false)
* Enter additional physics equations that are related to the equation you submitted earlier.

### **physics_equations_3** (`physics_equations_3`, text, required: false)
* Enter more physics equations that are relevant to the topic.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the equation and its sources.
* If you have multiple equations to submit, use the additional fields (physics_equations_2 and physics_equations_3) to enter them.
