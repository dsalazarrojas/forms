# renaissance_and_reformation_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Renaissance and Reformation Assessment Form" is designed to evaluate the impact of the Renaissance and Reformation on European society and culture. This form aims to collect responses to specific questions related to the era's key events, figures, and ideas. Your goal is to provide a concise and informed assessment of how the Renaissance and Reformation shaped European society, including art, politics, and culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2.  Provide responses to all relevant questions, as indicated by the required flag in each question.
3.  If a question has a text or date field, please enter your response in the format required (e.g., date in mm/dd/yyyy format).
4.  If a question has a select_one or select_multiple option, select the relevant response from the given options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Section 1 Q1: section_1_q1** (`text`, optional): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe a key event from the Renaissance or Reformation.
* **Section 1 Q1 Self: section_1_q1_self** (`time`, optional): This question is related to the timeframe of the event you selected in the previous question. Please enter the year of the event or a brief description of the time period.
* **Section 1 Q2: section_1_q2** (`select_one`, optional): This question asks about the impact of the event on art and culture. Please select the most relevant response from the given options.
* **Section 1 Q3: section_1_q3** (`select_multiple`, optional): This question assesses the effect of the event on politics and religion. Select all applicable responses.
* **Self-Assessment: self_assessment** (`select_multiple`, optional): This question allows you to evaluate the relevance of the event or figure to the broader context of the Renaissance and Reformation.
* **Section 2 Q1: section_2_q1** (`text`, optional): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe a key figure from the Renaissance or Reformation.
* **Section 2 Q2: section_2_q2** (`select_one`, optional): This question asks about the significance of the figure on the Renaissance or Reformation. Please select the most relevant response from the given options.
* **Section 2 Q3: section_2_q3** (`select_multiple`, optional): This question assesses the impact of the figure on art and culture. Select all applicable responses.
* **Section 2 Q4: section_2_q4** (`select_multiple`, optional): This question evaluates the effect of the figure on politics and religion. Select all applicable responses.
* **Section 2 Q5: section_2_q5** (`text`, optional): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe a key aspect of the figure's influence on the Renaissance or Reformation.
* **Date: date** (`date`, optional): This question asks for the date of a specific event or figure. Please enter the date in mm/dd/yyyy format.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately based on your knowledge of the Renaissance and Reformation.
* If a question is unclear, please refer to historical resources or context to ensure accurate responses.
* For select_multiple questions, select all applicable responses to demonstrate a comprehensive understanding.
