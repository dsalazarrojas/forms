# vlan_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vlan_knowledge_assessment form is designed to assess users' knowledge on VLAN (Virtual Local Area Network) configurations, interfaces, connections, and other related aspects. This form is meant to be used by network administrators or engineers to gauge their understanding of VLAN concepts and identify areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a basic understanding of VLAN concepts and terminology.
2. Review each field carefully and provide answers based on your knowledge.
3. Fill in the fields with your thoughts and answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vlan_config** (`vlan_config`, Text, Optional): This field is meant for you to describe your current VLAN configuration experience. Provide a concise but detailed answer about your experience with VLAN configurations.

* **network_interfaces** (`network_interfaces`, Select One, Optional): Select one of the following: `interface`, `switch`, or `router`, to indicate which type of network interface you are familiar with.

* **cable_connections** (`cable_connections`, Number, Optional): This field is for you to provide the number of cable connections you are familiar with. Be as specific as possible.

* **vlan_id** (`vlan_id`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the VLAN IDs you are familiar with. You can select multiple options.

* **vlan_name** (`vlan_name`, Text, Optional): Provide the name of a VLAN you are familiar with.

* **vlan_description** (`vlan_description`, Text, Optional): Describe the purpose or function of the VLAN you selected in the previous field.

* **vlan_status** (`vlan_status`, Select One, Optional): Choose the status of the VLAN you are familiar with from the options: `active`, `inactive`, or `pending`.

* **vlan_priority** (`vlan_priority`, Number, Optional): If applicable, provide the priority of the VLAN you are familiar with.

* **vlan_comments** (`vlan_comments`, Note, Optional): This field is for you to provide any comments or notes about the VLAN you are familiar with.

* **vlan_solutions** (`vlan_solutions`, Select Multiple, Optional): If you have experience with VLAN solutions, select all the applicable options, `Yes`, or `No`.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when completing each field.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided relevant information.
* If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or indicate that it's unknown.
* This form is meant to be a self-assessment, so take your time and be thorough.
