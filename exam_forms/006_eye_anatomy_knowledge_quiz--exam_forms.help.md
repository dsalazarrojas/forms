# Eye Anatomy Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Eye Anatomy Knowledge Quiz form is designed to test your knowledge about different parts of the eye. This form will guide you through a series of questions and options to help you assess your understanding of eye anatomy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the correct option for each of the five eye parts: sclera, iris, pupil, retina, and lens.
2. For each selected option, provide a text response in the text field below.
3. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Eye Parts**: Select the correct option for each of the five eye parts. The options are:
	+ Sclera
	+ Iris
	+ Pupil
	+ Retina
	+ Lens
* **What is the Sclera**: Enter a short text response to describe the sclera.
* **What is the Iris**: Enter a short text response to describe the iris.
* **What is the Pupil**: Enter a short text response to describe the pupil.
* **What is the Retina**: Enter a short text response to describe the retina.
* **What is the Lens**: Enter a short text response to describe the lens.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your answers.
* **Image**: This field is not currently being used in this form.
