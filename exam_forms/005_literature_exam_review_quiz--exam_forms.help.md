# Literature Exam Review Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Literature Exam Review Quiz form is used to collect information from students about their readiness and preferences for a literature exam. It is used to ensure that students are aware of their own strengths and areas where they need more practice and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field, but this is not required.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, but this is not required.
5. Select the date and time you plan to take the exam in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. In the "Literature Exam Readiness" section, select one or more of the following:
	* Not Ready
	* Somewhat Ready
	* Very Ready
7. In the "Exam Subject" section, select one or more subjects you want to major in:
	* English
	* History
	* Math
	* Science
	* Other
8. Select the location where you plan to take the exam in the "Exam Location" field.
9. In the "Exam Notes" section, add any additional comments or information about the exam.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, optional): Enter your last name, but it's not required.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, optional): Enter your phone number, but it's not required.
* **Which author wrote 'To Kill a Mockingbird'** (`literature_question`, required): Select the author from the drop-down menu.
* **Who wrote The Great Gatsby** (`literature_question`, required): Select the author from the drop-down menu.
* **Who wrote The Catcher in the Rye** (`literature_question`, required): Select the author from the drop-down menu.
* **Note** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the exam.
* **Date** (`date`, required): Select the date you plan to take the exam.
* **Time** (`time`, required): Select the time you plan to take the exam.
* **How ready are you for the literature exam** (`literature_exam_readiness`, required): Select your level of readiness for the exam.
* **Which subject do you want to major in** (`literature_exam_subject`, required): Select one or more subjects you want to major in.
* **Where are you taking the exam** (`literature_exam_location`, required): Select the location where you will take the exam.
* **What other notes** (`literature_exam_notes`, required): Add any additional comments or information about the exam.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct date and time for the exam.
* Check your selections in the "Exam Subject" and "Exam Location" sections for accuracy.
* Use the "Exam Notes" section to provide any additional context or information about your exam.
