# Dosage Calculation Simulation Sign Up - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for a dosage calculation simulation activity. It collects information from participants and instructors to organize and manage the simulation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **Name** and **Email** in the respective fields.
2. Enter the **Simulation Date** and **Simulation Time** for the activity.
3. Select the **Simulation Type** you're interested in.
4. Enter your **Start Date** and **End Date** for the activity.
5. Provide the number of **Simulation Hours** you need for the activity.
6. Add any additional **Simulation Minutes** (if needed).
7. Specify the **Simulation Location** where the activity will take place.
8. Enter the **Instructor Name** and **Instructor Email** for the activity.
9. Outline your **Learning Goals** and provide more details in the **Learning Goals Details** field.
10. Leave any **Instructor Feedback** in the optional field (if desired).
11. Sign the form with your **Participant Signature**.
12. Confirm that you've read and agree to the **Participant Agreement Terms** and sign the **Participant Agreement Date**.

* **Name**: A required field where you can enter your name as it will be displayed in the simulation activity.
* **Email**: A required field where you can enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Simulation Date**: A required field where you can select the date of the simulation activity.
* **Simulation Time**: A required field where you can select the time of the simulation activity.
* **Simulation Type**: A required field where you can select the type of simulation activity you're interested in.
* **Start Date**: A required field where you can select the start date of the activity.
* **End Date**: A required field where you can select the end date of the activity.
* **Simulation Hours**: A required field where you can enter the number of hours needed for the activity.
* **Simulation Minutes**: An optional field where you can enter additional minutes if needed.
* **Simulation Location**: A required field where you can specify the location of the activity.
* **Instructor Name**: A required field where you can enter the name of the instructor leading the activity.
* **Instructor Email**: A required field where you can enter the email address of the instructor.
* **Learning Goals**: A required field where you can outline your learning goals.
* **Learning Goals Details**: A required field where you can provide more details about your learning goals.
* **Instructor Feedback**: An optional field where you can leave feedback for the instructor.
* **Participant Signature**: A required field where you can sign the form to confirm your participation.
* **Instructor Signature**: A required field where the instructor can sign the form to confirm their participation.
* **Participant Agreement Terms**: A required field where you can read and agree to the terms of the simulation activity.
* **Participant Agreement Date**: A required field where you can sign the date confirming you've read and agreed to the terms.
