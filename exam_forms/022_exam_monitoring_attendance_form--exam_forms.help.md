# Exam Monitoring Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to monitor and record student attendance during exams, ensuring that all students are accounted for and present.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Enter the session ID to identify the exam session.
4. Record the start and end times of the exam in the respective "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
5. Indicate whether the student attended the exam by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Attended Students" field.
6. Enter the name of the proctor who was present during the exam in the "Proctor" field.
7. Provide any additional supervision notes in the "Supervision Notes" field (optional).
8. Enter the email address of the student in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the student in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter your name and initials in the "Submitted By" field to indicate that you filled out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (<name>, <text>, required)**: Enter the name of the student who attended the exam.
* **Student ID (<name>, <number>, required)**: Enter the ID number of the student who attended the exam.
* **Session ID (<name>, <number>, required)**: Enter the ID number of the exam session.
* **Start Time (<name>, <time>, required)**: Record the start time of the exam.
* **End Time (<name>, <time>, required)**: Record the end time of the exam.
* **Attended Students (<name>, <select_multiple>, required)**: Select "Yes" if the student attended the exam, "No" otherwise.
* **Proctor (<name>, <text, required>)**: Enter the name of the proctor who was present during the exam.
* **Supervision Notes (<name>, <note, optional>)**: Provide any additional supervision notes about the exam.
* **Email (<name>, <email, required>)**: Enter the email address of the student.
* **Phone (<name>, <text, required>)**: Enter the phone number of the student.
* **Submitted By (<name>, <text, required>)**: Enter your name and initials to indicate that you filled out the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the attendance status of each student.
* If a student did not attend the exam, explain the reason in the "Supervision Notes" field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
