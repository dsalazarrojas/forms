# venture-capital-knowledge-quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test knowledge about venture capital, a type of investment firm that provides funding to startups and small businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the context of the form.
2. Select the category that best describes your knowledge.
3. Enter your name, email, phone number, date, and time.
4. Answer each of the three multiple-choice questions with the corresponding answer field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.
