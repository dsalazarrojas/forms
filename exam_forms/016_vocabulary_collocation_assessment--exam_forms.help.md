# Vocabulary Collocation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vocabulary Collocation Assessment form is a tool used to evaluate students' understanding of vocabulary collocations. It consists of various fields that capture different aspects of student performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the collocation question on the first page.
2. Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
3. Enter your score for the collocation question.
4. Add any comments or notes as a teacher or student.
5. Select the submission status of your work.
6. Enter your completion time and status.
7. Review and complete other fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **collocation_question (text, required)**: This field asks the student to provide a response to a collocation question.
* **correct_answer (select_one, optional)**: Choose the correct answer to the collocation question.
* **collocation_score (number, optional)**: Enter a score for your response to the collocation question.
* **teacher_comments (note, optional)**: Add any comments or notes as a teacher.
* **students_answer (text, optional)**: Enter a response to the collocation question as a student.
* **students_notes (note, optional)**: Add any comments or notes as a student.
* **submission_status (select_one, optional)**: Select the status of your submission (submitted, pending, or cancelled).
* **students_score (number, optional)**: Enter a score for your response to the collocation question as a student.
* **teacher_score (number, optional)**: Enter a score for your response to the collocation question as a teacher.
* **completion_status (select_one, optional)**: Select the status of your completion (completed, incomplete, or not started).
* **completion_time (time, optional)**: Enter the time it took you to complete the assessment.
* **students_completion_time (time, optional)**: Enter the time it took you as a student to complete the assessment.
* **form_completion_status (select_one, optional)**: Select the status of the form (completed or not completed).
* **submission_date (date, optional)**: Enter the date of your submission.
* **students_submission_date (date, optional)**: Enter the date of your submission as a student.
* **teacher_comments (note, optional)**: Add any comments or notes as a teacher.
* **teacher_comments (note, optional)**: Add any comments or notes as a teacher.
* **students_comments (note, optional)**: Add any comments or notes as a student.
* **form_version (select_one, optional)**: Select the version of the form (1, 2, or 3).
