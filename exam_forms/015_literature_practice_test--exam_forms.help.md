# Literature Practice Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge and skills in literature, providing a practice test to evaluate your understanding of literary concepts, texts, and questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available questions and answer options provided.
2. Fill in the answers according to your knowledge and best judgment.
3. Be mindful of the required questions (marked as "required" in the form). Answer these questions accurately as they will be considered when calculating your practice test scores.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Test 1
* **literature_practice_test** (`test_1`, `number`, required: false): This field is an optional test identifier, but it's not critical to your understanding. You can leave it blank.
* **question** (`question_1`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief answer to this question according to your understanding of the literature.
* **question** (`question_2`, `text`, required: false): Provide another answer to this question as per your knowledge of the literature.
* **yet_another_question** (`question_3`, `date`, required: false): This field is optional and not directly related to the test. You can leave it blank.
* **question_time** (`question_time`, `time`, required: false): Another optional field that is not critical to the test.
* **last_question** (`question_4`, `number`, required: false): This is the last question on the first page.
* **test_2** (`test_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is not essential to the test, so you can leave it blank.

### Page 2
* **question** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply according to your knowledge.
* **answer** (`question_answer`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the options that best answer this question from the options provided.
* **answer** (`question_answer_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one answer from the options that best answer this question.
* **yet_another_question** (`question_3`, `text`, required: false): Another optional question not directly related to the test.
* **question_time** (`question_time`, `time`, required: false): Not critical to the test, so you can leave it blank.
* **test_2** (`test_2`, `text`, required: false): This is not essential to the test, so you can skip it.
* **another_question** (`question_2`, `text`, required: false): Select the correct answer to this question as per your understanding.

### Tips
* Ensure you answer the required questions (marked as "required") accurately for an accurate assessment.
* If unsure about any question, refer back to the relevant literary texts or resources for clarification.
* This form is designed to be a practice test, so it's okay to ask for help or clarification if needed.
* Remember that this form is not a formal assessment, and scores are for practice purposes only.
