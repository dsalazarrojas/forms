# Parasympatholytics Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parasympatholytics Knowledge Quiz form is designed to collect data about users' experiences with certain drugs. This data will help us better understand the effects of these medications and their interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For the "What is the name of the drug you are referring to?" field, please enter the name of the drug you are taking or have taken.
3. For the "How much of the drug were you given?" field, select the correct dosage amount and unit of measurement.
4. For the "What is the drug class?" field, choose the correct category for the drug from the provided options.
5. In the "What are the side effects you experienced?" field, select all applicable options from the list.
6. For the "What is your correct answer?" field, choose whether your answer was correct or incorrect.
7. For the "What is the date and time of the event?" field, enter the date and time of the event as it occurred.
8. For the "What is the time of the event?" field, enter the time of the event as it occurred.
9. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the "Do you have any additional note?" field.
10. If you would like to provide your contact information, please enter your email address in the "What is your contact email?" field and your phone number in the "What is your phone number?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the name of the drug you are referring to?** (drug_name, text, required): This field is where you enter the name of the drug you are taking or have taken.
- **How much of the drug were you given?** (drug_dosage, number, required): Please select the correct dosage amount and unit of measurement for the drug.
- **In what unit are the drug measurements?** (drug_unit, text, optional): This field is where you can specify the unit of measurement for the drug, if it's not already specified.
- **What is the drug class?** (drug_class, select_one, required): Choose the correct category for the drug from the provided options.
- **What are the side effects you experienced?** (side_effects, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options from the list of potential side effects.
- **What is your correct answer?** (correct_answer, select_one, required): Choose whether your answer was correct or incorrect.
- **What is the date and time of the event?** (event_date_time, date, required): Enter the date and time of the event as it occurred.
- **What is the time of the event?** (event_time, time, required): Enter the time of the event as it occurred.
- **Do you have any additional note?** (note, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about your experience, you can enter them here.
- **What is your contact email?** (contact_email, email, optional): If you would like to provide your contact information, enter your email address here.
- **What is your phone number?** (contact_phone, text, optional): If you would like to provide your contact number, enter it here.
