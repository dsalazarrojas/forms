# Retail Security Guard Entrance Exam Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information from potential candidates who want to take the retail security guard entrance exam. Please follow the instructions below to ensure you complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the candidate information section with your name, if you wish.
2. Select your preferred scheduling preference (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
3. Choose a date and time for the exam that works best for you.
4. Confirm that you meet the eligibility requirements.
5. Enter your email address and/or phone number (if you wish).
6. Leave notes or additional comments if you have any specific requests or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Info**: This section is for your basic contact information. If you wish, you can enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Scheduling Preferences**: This field allows you to choose when you want to take the exam. Select one of the three options: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Scheduling Date**: Enter the date you prefer for the exam. This will be used to schedule your exam appointment.
* **Scheduling Time**: Choose a time for the exam that suits you best.
* **Eligibility Confirmation**: Please confirm whether you meet the eligibility requirements. This is an important step to ensure you are qualified to take the exam.
* **Email**: If you have a email address, please enter it here. This will be used for exam notifications and updates.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it here. This will be used in case we need to contact you.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional comments or requests you may have. This is an optional field for any further information you want to provide.
