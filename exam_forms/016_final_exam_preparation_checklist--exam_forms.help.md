# final_exam_preparation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals prepare for a final exam by providing a checklist of key areas to review and prepare for the exam. It is intended to be completed by the individual taking the exam to ensure they are adequately prepared.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the relevant information for each section. You may refer to your notes, textbooks, or other resources as needed to answer each question. If a field is not relevant or required, you can skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Study Progress** (`study_progress`, number, required: false): Enter your current progress in your studies, on a scale of 1-100.
- **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the exam, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, number, required: false): Rate your level of confidence in the material, on a scale of 1-100.
- **Revision Plan** (`revision_plan`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your plan for reviewing and preparing for the exam, including any specific topics or areas of focus.
- **Study Topics** (`study_topics`, select multiple, required: false): Select all relevant study topics that you have completed or plan to complete before the exam.
  - `Yes` - You have completed this topic
  - `No` - You have not completed this topic
- **Study Resources** (`study_resources`, text, required: false): Enter any additional resources or materials you have used to study for the exam.
- **Study Schedule** (`study_schedule`, text, required: false): Describe your plan for how you will continue to study and prepare for the exam leading up to the exam date.
- **Exam Notes** (`exam_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or observations that may be relevant to your exam preparation.
- **Tutors** (`tutors`, select one, required: false): Have you worked with or plan to work with a tutor for this exam?
  - `Yes` - You have or plan to work with a tutor
  - `No` - You do not have or plan to work with a tutor
- **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, select one, required: false): Where do you plan to take the exam?
  - `Yes` - This is the location you have or plan to use
  - `No` - This is not the location you have or plan to use
- **Confirm Exam Date** (`exam_date_confirmation`, date, required: false): Confirm the date of the exam, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Confirm Exam Time** (`exam_time_confirmation`, time, required: false): Confirm the time of the exam, in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
- **Exam Comments** (`exam_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about your exam preparation.
- **Confirm Tutors** (`exam_tutors_confirmation`, select one, required: false): Have you confirmed the exam tutors?
  - `Yes` - You have confirmed the exam tutors
  - `No` - You have not confirmed the exam tutors

## Tips
This form should take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete. Please be as thorough as possible in your answers to ensure you are well-prepared for the exam.
