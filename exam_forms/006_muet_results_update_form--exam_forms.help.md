## MUET Results Update Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The MUET Results Update Form is designed to allow users to update their MUET results, specifically for students who have taken the exams. This form is intended for those who need to make changes or corrections to their exam results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the update in the "Form Title" field.
2. Enter the name of the institution where the exam was taken in the "Institution Name" field.
3. Enter the exam roll number, roll number, and session of the exam in the respective fields.
4. Select the subjects and marks obtained in each subject using the "Subject-wise Marks" field.
5. If required, upload any supporting documents related to the exam.
6. Select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the update.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution where the exam was taken.
* **Exam Roll Number** (`exam_roll_number`, text, required): Enter the exam roll number.
* **Roll Number** (`roll_number`, text, required): Enter the roll number.
* **Session** (`session`, select_one, required): Select the session of the exam (e.g., 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012).
* **Subject-wise Marks** (`subject_wise_marks`, select_multiple, required): Select the subjects and marks obtained in each subject (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Urdu, Computer).
* **SubjectWise Marks** (`subjectwise_marks`, number, optional): Enter the marks obtained in each subject (this field is optional and should not be filled unless required).
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, required): Enter any relevant notes about the supporting documents.
* **Upload Supporting Documents** (`upload_supporting_documents`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents related to the exam (this field is optional).
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select "Submit" to complete the form.
* **Exam Year** (`exam_year`, text, optional): Enter the year of the exam (this field is optional).
* **Exam Month** (`exam_month`, text, optional): Enter the month of the exam (this field is optional).
* **Supporting Docs** (`supporting_docs`, note, optional): Enter any notes about the supporting documents.
* **Supporting Doc** (`supporting_doc`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents related to the exam (this field is optional).
* **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the supporting documents.
* **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document_attachment`, text, optional): Upload any additional supporting documents related to the exam.
* **Supporting Doc** (`supporting_doc_attachment`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the supporting documents.
* **Supporting Doc 2** (`supporting_document_attachment_2`, text, optional): Upload any additional supporting documents related to the exam.
* **Upload Additional Document** (`upload_additional_document`, text, optional): Upload any additional documents related to the exam.
* **Supporting Attachment** (`supporting_attachment`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the supporting documents.
* **Supporting Attachment 2** (`supporting_attachment_2`, text, optional): Upload any additional supporting documents related to the exam.
* **Upload Additional Attachment** (`upload_additional_attachment`, text, optional): Upload any additional attachments related to the exam.
* **Supporting File** (`supporting_file`, text, optional): Upload any additional supporting files related to the exam.
* **Supporting File Attachment** (`supporting_file_attachment`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the supporting files.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled correctly before submitting the form.
* Be sure to upload any supporting documents or attachments as needed.
* If you have any doubts or issues while filling the form, please contact your admin for assistance.
