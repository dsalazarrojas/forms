# PTE Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTE Exam Registration Form is a tool for students to register for an exam. It gathers essential information required for the exam administration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student details section with your personal information.
2. Indicate your exam preferences in the exam preferences section.
3. Provide your institution details in the institution details section (if applicable).
4. Sign the form electronically (if you have a digital signature capability).
5. Fill out your name and other required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details** (text): Enter your personal details, including your name, student ID, and any other relevant information.
* **Exam Preferences** (select one): Indicate whether you will be attending the exam (Yes/No).
* **Institution Details** (text): If you are a representative of an institution, enter your institution's name and other relevant details.
* **Electronic Signature** (note): Please sign this form electronically if you have the capability to do so. This is a required field for electronic submission.
* **Student Name** (text): Enter your name as it will appear on the exam certificate.
