# Sensitivity Toward Cultural And Ethnic Differences Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess an individual's sensitivity toward cultural and ethnic differences. It aims to evaluate their ability to acknowledge and appreciate diversity within a multicultural society. The form is a crucial tool for promoting empathy, understanding, and inclusivity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Choose the correct answer for each question, based on your thoughts and feelings.
3. Make sure to fill out every question, as some questions are marked as "required."
