# Vessel Operator Orientation Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vessel Operator Orientation Registration form is designed to gather information about your vessel operations, including the type of vessel you operate, your operating experience, and your training status. This information will help us understand your specific needs and provide you with the best possible support and guidance throughout your vessel operations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Vessel Operator Orientation Registration form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your vessel operations.
2. Select the correct options for "Vessel Type" and "Vessel Size" that match your operation.
3. Enter your operating experience in the "Operating Experience" field.
4. If you have completed or are in progress with training, select the corresponding options in the "Training Status" and "Vessel Certification" fields.
5. Fill in the contact information, including the "Contact Name", "Contact Title", "Contact Phone", and "Contact Email" fields.
6. Select the "Training Date" and "Training Time" for the training you have completed or are in progress.
7. Enter the name and details of the training facility where you received your training.
8. Enter the name of your vessel and facility information, including "Facility Name", "Facility Address", "Facility City", "Facility State", and "Facility Zip".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (id: 1): Enter your name as it appears on your vessel registration certificate.
* **Vessel Details** (id: 2): Enter a brief description of your vessel, including its type, size, and any unique features.
* **Operator Details** (id: 3): Enter your name, title, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Details** (id: 4): Enter the contact information for the person responsible for your vessel operations, including their name, title, phone number, and email.
* **Vessel Operator Details** (id: 5): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as the vessel operator.
* **Vessel Type** (id: 6): Select the type of vessel you operate (Commercial, Private, or Charter).
* **Vessel Size** (id: 7): Select the size of your vessel (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Operating Experience** (id: 8): Enter the number of years you have operated a vessel of your selected type and size.
* **Training Status** (id: 9, id: 10): Select the status of your training (Completed, In Progress, or N/A).
* **Vessel Certification** (id: 11, id: 12): Select the certification you have obtained for your vessel (Commercial, Private, or Charter).
* **Contact Name** (id: 13): Enter the name of the person responsible for your vessel operations.
* **Contact Title** (id: 14): Enter the title of the person responsible for your vessel operations.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 15): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your vessel operations.
* **Contact Email** (id: 16): Enter the email of the person responsible for your vessel operations.
* **Training Date** (id: 17): Select the date you completed or started your training.
* **Training Time** (id: 18): Select the time of day you completed or started your training.
* **Training Facility** (id: 19): Enter the name of the training facility where you received your training.
* **Vessel Name** (id: 20): Enter the name of your vessel.
* **Facility Name** (id: 21): Enter the name of the facility where you operate your vessel.
* **Facility Address** (id: 22): Enter the address of the facility where you operate your vessel.
* **Facility City** (id: 23): Enter the city where you operate your vessel.
* **Facility State** (id: 24): Enter the state where you operate your vessel.
* **Facility Zip** (id: 25): Enter the zip code of the facility where you operate your vessel.

Note: Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with submitting the form.
