# New Car Purchase Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information about new car purchases, including vehicle details, sales history, and other relevant data. It will help ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and consistently, reducing errors and making it easier to track vehicle purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the vehicle make from the available options (Honda, Toyota, Ford, General Motors, Nissan).
2. Choose a "Yes" or "No" for whether an inspection was done.
3. Enter the vehicle year.
4. Enter the vehicle plate number.
5. Enter the Vehicle VIN (Vehicle Identification Number).
6. Enter the vehicle color (optional).
7. Enter the vehicle condition (optional).
8. Enter the vehicle's previous owners (optional).
9. Choose a "True" or "False" for whether an inspection was done.
10. Choose one or multiple "True" for inspections (optional).
11. Enter the vehicle inspection results (optional).
12. Enter the vehicle inspection photos (optional).
13. Enter the vehicle inspection reports (optional).
14. Select a date for the vehicle sale date.
15. Enter the vehicle sale price.
16. Choose a "Yes" or "No" for whether there were previous sales.
17. Choose a "Full", "Partial", or "None" for the vehicle warranty.
18. Choose "Active" or "Inactive" for the vehicle insurance status (optional).
19. Select "Yes" or "No" for vehicle registration status.
20. Enter the vehicle purchase order number.
21. Select a date for the vehicle delivery date.
22. Choose "Active" or "Inactive" for the vehicle delivery status (optional).
23. Enter any additional notes about the vehicle.
24. Enter any notes from the sale manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle make**: Select the make of the vehicle being purchased.
* **Vehicle model**: Choose "Yes" if an inspection was done.
* **Vehicle year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle plate number**: Enter the vehicle's plate number.
* **Vehicle VIN**: Enter the vehicle's unique identification number.
* **Vehicle color**: Enter the color of the vehicle (optional).
* **Vehicle condition**: Enter the condition of the vehicle (optional).
* **Vehicle previous owners**: Enter any previous owners of the vehicle (optional).
* **Vehicle inspection done**: Choose "True" if an inspection was done.
* **Vehicle inspections**: Choose one or multiple "True" for inspections.
* **Vehicle inspection results**: Enter the results of the vehicle inspection (optional).
* **Vehicle inspection photos**: Enter any photos taken during the inspection (optional).
* **Vehicle inspection reports**: Enter any reports from the inspection (optional).
* **Vehicle sale date**: Select a date for the vehicle sale.
* **Vehicle sale price**: Enter the price of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle previous sales**: Choose "Yes" if there were previous sales.
* **Vehicle warranty**: Choose "Full", "Partial", or "None" for the vehicle warranty.
* **Vehicle insurance**: Choose "Active" or "Inactive" for the vehicle insurance status.
* **Vehicle registration**: Choose "Yes" or "No" for vehicle registration status.
* **Vehicle purchase order number**: Enter the purchase order number.
* **Vehicle delivery date**: Select a date for the vehicle delivery.
* **Vehicle delivery status**: Choose "Active" or "Inactive" for the vehicle delivery status.
* **Vehicle notes**: Enter any additional notes about the vehicle.
* **Sale manager notes**: Enter any notes from the sale manager.
