# Vehicle Ownership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help users complete the Vehicle Ownership Application form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vehicle Ownership Application form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Dealer Info** section with your dealership's information.
2. Provide detailed information about your **Vehicle Details** in the next section.
3. Attach any necessary **Supporting Documents** that prove your vehicle ownership.
4. Finally, select your signature from the dropdown menu in the **Signature** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Dealer Info
• **Dealer Info** (`dealer_info`, text, not required): Fill out this section with your dealership's name, address, and other relevant information.

### Vehicle Details
• **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, required): Provide detailed information about the vehicle you own or are applying for, including its make, model, year, and VIN.

### Supporting Documents
• **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, not required): Attach any relevant documents that support your vehicle ownership application, such as a title, registration, or proof of insurance.

### Signature
• **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Choose your signature from the dropdown menu to confirm your identity.
