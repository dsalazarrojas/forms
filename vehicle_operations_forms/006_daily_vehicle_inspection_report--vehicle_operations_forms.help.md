# Daily Vehicle Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Vehicle Inspection Report is used to gather information from vehicle inspectors for the purpose of identifying potential issues with a vehicle that require maintenance. This form helps to ensure that vehicles are inspected regularly and any problems are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Enter the Company Name of the vehicle owner or operator.
3. Enter the Inspectors Name.
4. Enter the Vehicle Id, which is a unique identifier for the vehicle.
5. Select the type of vehicle (e.g. Vehicle, Car, Truck).
6. Enter the current odometer reading.
7. Enter the current fuel level (e.g. Low, Medium, High).
8. Check the status of the engine oil level, transmission oil level, and other systems and components that require inspection.
9. Check the status of various vehicle components, including brakes, tires, suspension, steering, frame, wheels, body, glass, electrical systems, safety belts, and parking brakes.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the vehicle owner or operator.
* **Inspectors Name**: Enter your name and contact information for future reference.
* **Vehicle Id**: Enter a unique identifier for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle (e.g. Vehicle, Car, Truck).
* **Odometer Reading**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Fuel Level**: Select the current level of fuel in the vehicle (e.g. Low, Medium, High).
* **Engine Oil Level**: Check the status of the engine oil level (e.g. Low, Medium, High).
* **Transmission Oil Level**: Check the status of the transmission oil level (e.g. Low, Medium, High). (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Coolant Level**: Check the status of the coolant level (e.g. Low, Medium, High). (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Brake Status**: Check the status of the brake system (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Tire Status**: Check the status of the tires (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Suspension Status**: Check the status of the suspension system (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Steering Status**: Check the status of the steering system (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Frame Status**: Check the status of the frame (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Wheel Status**: Check the status of the wheels (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Body Status**: Check the status of the body (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Glass Status**: Check the status of the glass (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor). (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Electrical System Status**: Check the status of the electrical system (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Safety Belt Status**: Check the status of the safety belt (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Parking Brake Status**: Check the status of the parking brake (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Parking Brake Warning**: Enter any additional comments about the parking brake system.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle. (Note: This field is not required.)
