# Vehicle Weight Rating Correction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Weight Rating Correction Form is used to correct inaccurately or outdated weight ratings of vehicles. This form provides a structured way to update the weight rating of a vehicle, ensuring that the correction is properly documented and reviewed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the vehicle information in the "vehicle_info" field.
2.  Select the corrected weight rating from the "weight_rating" dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the reason for the correction in the "correction_request" field.
4.  Fill in the "adjustment_date" and "adjustment_time" fields with the date and time of the correction.
5.  Add any additional comments in the "comments" field.
6.  Fill in the "reviewed_date" and "reviewed_time" fields with the date and time of the review.
7.  Enter the name of the reviewer in the "reviewer" field.
8.  Fill in the "approved_by" field with the name of the person approving the correction.
9.  Fill in the "approved_date" and "approved_time" fields with the date and time of approval.
10.  Finally, select the new status of the vehicle from the "status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Vehicle Weight Rating Correction Form**
    *   **base_info** (Text, Optional): Enter information about the vehicle, such as make, model, and year.
    *   **vehicle_info** (Text, Optional): Enter the vehicle information, such as vehicle number or VIN.
    *   **weight_rating** (Select One, Optional): Select the corrected weight rating from the dropdown menu.
    *   **correction_request** (Text, Optional): Enter the reason for the correction, explaining why the weight rating was incorrect or outdated.
    *   **adjustment_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the adjustment.
    *   **adjustment_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the adjustment.
    *   **comments** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the correction.
    *   **review_comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments or notes from the reviewer.
    *   **reviewer** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
    *   **reviewed_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the review.
    *   **reviewed_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the review.
    *   **approved_by** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person approving the correction.
    *   **approved_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of approval.
    *   **approved_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of approval.
    *   **status** (Select One, Optional): Select the new status of the vehicle from the dropdown menu.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submission.
*   If you're unsure about any field, check with your supervisor or the relevant department.
*   This form is for correcting existing weight ratings, not creating new ones.
*   Use this form to ensure that weight ratings are accurate and up-to-date.
