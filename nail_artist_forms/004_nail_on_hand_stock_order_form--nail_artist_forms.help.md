# nail_on_hand_stock_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The nail_on_hand_stock_order_form is used for customers to place orders for stock items. The form gathers information required for the order such as product name, quantity, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the stock items you would like to order using the `Stock Order Form` dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the `Order Date` in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3.  Enter the `Product Name` of the stock item(s) you are ordering.
4.  Enter the `Quantity Ordered` of the stock item(s).
5.  Enter the `Total Cost` of the order without decimal places (USD).
6.  Select the `Payment Terms` option that applies to your order.
7.  Enter your `Customer Name`.
8.  Enter your `Contact Number`.
9.  Enter your `Email`.
10.  Add any additional `Notes` about your order if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Stock Order Form** (`Stock Order Form`, `select_multiple`): A dropdown menu where you can select the stock item(s) you would like to order.
*   **Order Date** (`Order Date`, `date`): Enter the date of the order in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Product Name** (`Product Name`, `text`): Enter the name of the stock item(s) you are ordering.
*   **Quantity Ordered** (`Quantity Ordered`, `number`): Enter the quantity of the stock item(s) you are ordering.
*   **Total Cost** (`Total Cost`, `number`): Enter the total cost of the order without decimal places (USD).
*   **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, `select_one`): Select the payment term that applies to your order. The options are `Option 1` and `Option 2`.
*   **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, `text`): Enter your name.
*   **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`): Enter your contact number.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`): Enter your email address.
*   **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`): Add any additional notes about your order if necessary.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out properly to avoid any issues with the order.
*   If you are unsure about a field, please refer to the field explanation above.
*   Please note that this form is for ordering stock items and should only be filled out once per order.
