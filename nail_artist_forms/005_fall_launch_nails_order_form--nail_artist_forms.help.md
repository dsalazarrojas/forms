# fall-launch-nails-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information for a fall launch nails order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information to the best of your ability.
2. Review all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that you have filled in all required fields (indicated by *).
4. Double-check your email and phone number for correct formatting.
5. Review and understand the shipping and payment options before selecting them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (1, Customer Information, required): Enter the customer's name and contact details.
* **Product Info** (2, Product Info, optional): Select the type of nail polish you want to order (Nail Polish, Nail Lacquer, or Nail Varnish).
* **Order Details** (3, Order Details, optional): Enter the number of units you want to order.
* **Shipping Address** (4, Shipping Address, optional): Enter the shipping address in the format of "Street Address", "City", "State", and "Zip Code".
* **Shipping Options** (5, Shipping Options, optional): Select the shipping option (Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, or UPS Shipping).
* **Payment Method** (6, Payment Method, optional): Select the payment method (Credit Card, Cash, or PayPal).
* **Order Date** (7, Order Date, optional): Enter the date you want to order.
* **Order Time** (8, Order Time, optional): Enter the time you want to order.
* **Note** (9, Note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Email** (10, Email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (11, Phone, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Note** (12, Customer Note, optional): Add any additional customer notes.
* **Shipping Date** (13, Shipping Date, optional): Enter the date you want to ship.
* **Shipping Time** (14, Shipping Time, optional): Enter the time you want to ship.
* **Assigned To** (15, Assigned To, optional): Select who is assigned to your order (John Doe or Jane Doe).
* **Customer Name** (16, Customer Name, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (17, Customer Email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (18, Customer Phone, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (19, Customer Address, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer City** (20, Customer City, optional): Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer State** (21, Customer State, optional): Enter the customer's state.
* **Customer Zip Code** (22, Customer Zip Code, optional): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Customer Country** (23, Customer Country, optional): Enter the customer's country.
* **Customer Notes** (24, Customer Notes, optional): Add any additional customer notes.
* **Form Owner** (25, Form Owner, optional): Enter the form owner.

Note: All fields with a red asterisk (*) are required to be filled out. Please review the form carefully to ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
