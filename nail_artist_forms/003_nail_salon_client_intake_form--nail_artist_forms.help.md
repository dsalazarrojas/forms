# Nail Salon Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients before booking an appointment at a nail salon. By providing accurate and complete data, you'll help our team tailor services to meet your unique needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the required fields. You can leave optional fields blank if they don't apply to you.
2. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Contact Details** (`client_contact_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to store your contact information, such as your name and phone number.
* **Client Payments** (`client_payments`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the payment methods you'd like to use for your appointment. Please select all applicable options.
* **Client E-Signatures** (`client_esignatures`, date, optional):
	+ This field is not currently relevant to the form's intended use case. Please ignore it.
* **Appointment Booking** (`appointment_booking`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the appointment time and date that suits you best.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address to receive communication from our salon.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our team for assistance.
* This form will help us provide the best possible service for your upcoming appointment. Thank you for your cooperation!
