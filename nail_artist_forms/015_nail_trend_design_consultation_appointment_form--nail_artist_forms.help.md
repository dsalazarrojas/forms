# Nail Trend Design Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and prepare for nail art consultations. It collects information from clients, including their details, preferences, and appointment specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details on the "Client Details" page.
2. On the "Nail Artist Preferences" page, specify your preferred nail design style, color, and any special requests.
3. Choose your preferred appointment time and date on the "Appointment Time" and "Appointment Date" page.
4. Select your appointment location on the "Appointment Location" page.
5. Add any additional comments or notes on the "Additional Comments" page.
6. Finally, confirm your appointment status on the "Appointment Status" page.

* **Nail Artist Preferences (Nail Artist Preferences)** (`text`, `optional`): Specify your preferred nail design style, color, and any special requests for your consultation.
* **Appointment Status (Appointment Status)** (`text`, `optional`): Confirm your appointment status with the technician.
