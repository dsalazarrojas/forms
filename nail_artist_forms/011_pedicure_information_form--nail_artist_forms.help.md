# Pedicure Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your health status and medical history for a safe and effective pedicure experience. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read each question and answer accordingly.
2. Answer all questions truthfully.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for advice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client's Health Conditions** (`clients_health_conditions`, text, required: false): Please list any health conditions you currently have, such as diabetes, allergies, or any other condition that may affect your pedicure experience.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required: false): If you are taking any medications, please indicate "Yes" next to each medication you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, required: false): If you are allergic to any substances or materials, please choose "Yes" from the options below:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Previous Injuries** (`previous_injuries`, select_multiple, required: false): If you have had any previous injuries, please indicate "Yes" next to each injury you have had. This includes cuts, burns, or other injuries that may affect your pedicure experience.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, select_one, required: false): Please choose "Yes" if you have undergone any medical procedures, such as surgery, implants, or other invasive treatments.
* **Health History** (`health_history`, text, required: false): Please provide any relevant health history information that may affect your pedicure experience.
* **Medical Conditions 2** (`medical_conditions_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Please list any other health conditions you may have, such as high blood pressure, heart disease, or other conditions that may affect your pedicure experience.
* **Previous Injuries 2** (`previous_injuries_2`, select_multiple, required: false): If you have had any previous injuries, please indicate "Yes" next to each injury you have had.
* **Medical Procedures 2** (`medical_procedures_2`, select_one, required: false): Please choose "Yes" if you have undergone any medical procedures, such as surgery, implants, or other invasive treatments.

## Tips

* If you have any concerns or questions, please ask your healthcare provider for advice.
* If you are unsure about any question, please answer "Unknown" or "Not Applicable" to indicate that you are not certain.
* Please be accurate and honest when answering the questions to ensure a safe and effective pedicure experience.
