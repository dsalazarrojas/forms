# Nail Strips Inventory Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for nail artists to submit their nail strip inventory orders. It helps us process your orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Product ID** of the nail strip you are ordering.
2. Specify the **Quantity** of the nail strip you need.
3. Select the **Color** of the nail strip (if applicable).
4. Choose the **Size** of the nail strip (if applicable).
5. Enter the **Order Date** and **Order Time** (if applicable).
6. Add any additional **Note** about the order.
7. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** contact information.
8. Optionally, specify the **Assigned Tool** and **Product Name**.
9. Enter the **Form ID** and **Category** (if applicable).
10. Provide a brief **Description** of the order.
11. Specify the **Output File** and **Assigned Tool 2** (if applicable).
12. Enter the **Form ID 2** and **Category 2** (if applicable).
13. Provide a brief **Description 2** of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the product being ordered.
* **Quantity**: Specify the number of the product being ordered.
* **Color**: Select the color of the product being ordered (if applicable).
* **Size**: Choose the size of the product being ordered (if applicable).
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order (if applicable).
* **Order Time**: Enter the time of the order (if applicable).
* **Note**: Add any additional information about the order.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Assigned Tool**: Optionally, specify the tool assigned to this order.
* **Product Name**: Optionally, specify the name of the product being ordered.
* **Form ID**: Optionally, specify the form ID for this order.
* **Category**: Optionally, specify the category of this order.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the order.
* **Output File**: Optionally, specify the output file associated with this order.
* **Assigned Tool 2**: Optionally, specify the tool assigned to this order.
* **Form ID 2**: Optionally, specify the form ID for this order.
* **Category 2**: Optionally, specify the category of this order.
* **Description 2**: Provide a brief description of the order.
