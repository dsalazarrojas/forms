# winter_wonderland_nail_strip_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in purchasing nail strips for the Winter Wonderland season. It helps gather necessary details for efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1.  Enter your name in the text input field labeled "Name."
2.  Enter your email address in the email input field labeled "Email Address."
3.  Enter your phone number in the text input field labeled "Phone Number."
4.  Enter your address for shipping in the text input field labeled "Address."
5.  Select the type(s) of nail strip you would like to order from the "Product Type" multiple select input.
6.  Choose the color you would like from the "Color" select one input.
7.  Enter the quantity of nail strips you would like to order in the "Quantity" input.
8.  If you have any special requests or messages, enter them in the "Special Request" text input. (Optional)
9.  Enter any general comments or notes in the "Comments" text input. (Optional)
10.  Provide any additional information or notes in the "Form Notes" text input.
11.  Finally, select the customer support representative who will be handling your order from the "Assigned Tool" select one input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to be associated with your order.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address for communication and order confirmation.
*   **Phone Number** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
*   **Address** (`text`, required): Enter your address for shipping purposes.
*   **Product Type** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose the type(s) of nail strip you want to order (Strip, Gel, Acrylic).
*   **Color** (`select_one`, required): Select the color of your preferred nail strip (Red, Blue, Green).
*   **Quantity** (`number`, required): Enter the quantity of nail strips you would like to order.
*   **Special Request** (`text`, optional): Enter any special requests or messages.
*   **Comments** (`note`, optional): Enter general comments or notes.
*   **Form Notes** (`text`, required): Enter any additional information or notes that will help with order processing.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`select_one`, required): Choose which customer support representative will be handling your order (ChatJimmy, ChatSally, ChatBob).
