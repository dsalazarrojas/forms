# Nail Polish Matte Finish Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain a client's consent for a nail polish service with a matte finish. It also collects basic information for the service appointment, including health details, client preferences, and contact information. Please ensure you understand the form's purpose and the required information before starting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

* Answer all the questions in the form honestly and thoroughly.
* Select the "Consent" option from the "client_consent" field if you agree to the nail polish service with a matte finish.
* Provide any health details that may be relevant to the service in the "client_health_details" field.
* Sign your name in the "signature" field to confirm your identity.
* Choose your preferred nail artist from the "nail_artist_name" field.
* If you wish to include additional comments or information, use the "contact_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_consent** (Select Multiple): Please choose "Consent" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of the nail polish service with a matte finish. The other option "Decline" will cancel the service appointment.
* **client_health_details** (Text): This field is for any health details that may be relevant to the service. This could include any allergies, sensitivities, or other information that may affect the treatment.
* **signature** (Signature): This field is for your electronic signature, confirming that you have read and agreed to the terms of the service.
* **nail_artist_name** (Select One): Please choose your preferred nail artist from the provided options (Jimmy, John, Jane).
* **nail_artist_signature** (Signature): This field is for the nail artist's signature, confirming that they have read and agreed to the terms of the service.
* **date** (Date): Please provide the date of the service appointment.
* **time** (Time): Please provide the time of the service appointment.
* **contact_details** (Note): This field is for any additional comments or information that you may want to include.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the terms of the service before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
