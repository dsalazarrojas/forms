# nail_care_frequency_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "nail_care_frequency_survey" form, explaining each field and providing instructions on how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Nail Care Frequency Survey**
Start by selecting the name of the survey you are completing (e.g., "Nail Care Frequency Survey").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. How often do you get your nails done? (survey_frequency)**: This question asks about your frequency of getting your nails done. Select the option that best describes your behavior:
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Sometimes
	+ Often
	+ Daily
* **2. What color nail polish do you prefer? (nail_polish_color)**: Choose the color of nail polish you typically use:
	+ Clear
	+ Pink
	+ Red
	+ Purple
	+ Other
* **3. Which brand of nail polish do you usually use? (nail_polish_brand)**: Select the brand of nail polish you usually use:
	+ OPI
	+ Essence
	+ Revlon
	+ Other
* **4. Which nail product do you usually use? (nail_product)**: This question asks about the type of nail products you use. Select all that apply:
	+ Nail polish remover
	+ Nail file
	+ Cuticle pusher
	+ Nail care oils
	+ Other
* **5. Which nail service do you usually get done? (nail_service)**: This question asks about the types of nail services you usually receive. Select all that apply:
	+ Gel polish
	+ Dip powder
	+ Acrylic
	+ Other
* **6. How often do you have your nails done? (nail_frequency)**: This question is asking about the frequency of having your nails done. Select all that apply:
	+ Every 4 weeks
	+ Every 6 weeks
	+ Every 8 weeks
	+ Every 12 weeks
	+ Other
* **7-25. (Nail Frequency2, Product2, Service2, etc.)**: These questions are similar to 1-6, but with slightly different options. Please select the options that best describe your behavior.

## Tips
- Please be honest about your nail care habits.
- Select the options that best fit your behavior.
- If you're unsure, choose the most likely option.
- You can select multiple options for questions 4, 5, and 6.
- Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure the survey results are helpful for research.
