# Nail Shape Customization Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experiences with our nail shape customization services. Your input will help us improve our services and better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select your answers from the options provided.
3. If a question doesn't apply to you, please select "N/A" (Not Applicable).
4. Take a few minutes to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask your nail artist if you need help.
