# inspiration_nails_sample_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inspiration_nails_sample_request_form is a form used by customers to request samples of nail polish products. It is used to collect contact information, nail polish preferences, and sample request details to process sample requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information, including your name and contact details.
2. Select your nail polish preferences from the available options.
3. Specify the type of samples you are requesting (Nail Polish Strip Samples, Nail Polish Liquid Samples, or Nail Polish Creme Samples).
4. Indicate the quantity of samples you are requesting.
5. Choose whether you would like to be followed up on regarding your sample request.
6. Add any additional comments about your sample request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`): Enter your name, company (if applicable), and other relevant contact details.
* **Nail Polish Preferences** (`nail_polish_preferences`): Select your preferred nail polish color from the provided options (Red, Pink, Blue, or Green).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your email address or phone number to ensure we can contact you about your sample request.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address where we can contact you about your sample request.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your sample request.
* **Sample Requests** (`sample_requests`): Select the type of samples you are requesting (Nail Polish Strip Samples, Nail Polish Liquid Samples, or Nail Polish Creme Samples).
* **Sample Quantity** (`sample_quantity`): Enter the quantity of samples you are requesting.
* **Follow-Up** (`follow_up`): Choose whether you would like to be followed up on regarding your sample request.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about your sample request.
