# COVID 19 Pandemic Nails Salon Services Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information and consent from clients to ensure a safe and healthy environment in the nail salon during the COVID-19 pandemic. It covers various aspects including client details, service requests, health declaration, allergy information, and consent agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each section and ensure you understand the information requested.
- Fill in your full name, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
- Select the services you wish to receive from the provided options.
- Answer the health declaration questions honestly, indicating whether you have any COVID-19 symptoms, have been diagnosed, or have come into contact with a confirmed case.
- If applicable, list any allergies or skin conditions and describe them in detail.
- Confirm your understanding of COVID-19 transmission and the salon's safety measures.
- Check the consent boxes to agree to receiving the services and accepting the potential COVID-19 transmission risks.
- Finally, confirm the date of your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full Name** (Client Name, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Phone Number** (Client Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number where the salon can contact you.
- **Email Address** (Client Email, email, required): Enter your valid email address for future contact and communication.
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Fill in your date of birth for age verification purposes.
- **Appointment Date** (Appointment Date, date, required): Select the date you wish to receive the services.
- **Services Requested** (Services Requested, select multiple, required): Choose the services you would like to receive from the provided options.
- **Any Special Requests** (Any Special Requests, text, optional): If you have any specific requests or preferences for your appointment, please describe them here.
- **I do not have any COVID-19 symptoms** (COVID-19 symptoms, select one, required): Answer this question truthfully.
- **I have not been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days** (No COVID-19 diagnosis, select one, required): Answer this question truthfully.
- **I have not been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case** (No close contact, select one, required): Answer this question truthfully.
- **I do not have a fever or chills** (No fever or chills, select one, required): Answer this question truthfully.
- **Do you have allergies to nail salon products?** (Product Allergies, select one, required): If you have allergies, please select 'True'.
- **If yes, list allergies** (Allergy List, text, optional): If you answered 'True' to the previous question, list your allergies here.
- **Do you have any skin or nail conditions?** (Skin or Nail Conditions, select one, optional): If you have conditions, please select 'True'.
- **Describe conditions** (Condition Details, text, optional): If you selected 'True' for the previous question, describe your conditions here.
- **I understand how COVID-19 is transmitted** (Understand Transmission, select one, required): Confirm your understanding of COVID-19 transmission.
- **I understand the salon safety measures** (Understand Safety Measures, select one, required): Confirm your understanding of the salon's safety measures.
- **I consent to receive the requested nail services** (Consent to Services, select one, required): Select 'I consent' to confirm your agreement to receive the services.
- **I accept the potential COVID-19 transmission risks** (Accept Transmission Risks, select one, required): Select 'I accept' to confirm you understand the risks.
- **I release the salon from liability related to COVID-19** (Release from Liability, select one, required): Select 'I agree' to confirm your understanding and agreement.
- **Date of Consent** (Consent Date, date, required): Select the date you are giving your consent.
