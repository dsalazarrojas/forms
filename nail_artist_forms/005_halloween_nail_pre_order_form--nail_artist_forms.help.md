# Halloween Nail Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to pre-order their Halloween nails, allowing them to choose from a variety of options such as nail theme, design, size, and color.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Halloween theme you would like to have for your nails.
2. Choose the nail service you would like to have. 
3. Select multiple nail designs from the available options.
4. Enter any additional nail art you would like to have.
5. Choose the size of the nails you would like.
6. Select the color of the nails you would like.
7. Choose the nail pattern you would like.
8. Enter your desired nail style.
9. Enter the quantity of nails you would like to order.
10. Enter any additional information about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Halloween Theme** (`halloween_theme`, text, optional): 
  * Please enter a brief description of your preferred Halloween theme.
- **Nail Service** (`nail_service`, select one, optional): 
  * Please select the type of nail service you would like to have, such as Halloween Nails or Halloween Nails and Nails.
- **Nail Design** (`nail_design`, select multiple, optional): 
  * Please select multiple nail designs from the available options, such as Black Nails, White Nails, Red Nails, or Blue Nails.
- **Nail Art** (`nail_art`, text, optional): 
  * Please enter any additional nail art you would like to have, such as nail stickers or nail gems.
- **Nail Size** (`nail_size`, select one, optional): 
  * Please select the size of the nails you would like, such as Small, Medium, or Large.
- **Nail Color** (`nail_color`, select one, optional): 
  * Please select the color of the nails you would like, such as Red, Pink, or Purple.
- **Nail Pattern** (`nail_pattern`, select multiple, optional): 
  * Please select multiple nail patterns from the available options, such as Polka dots, Stripes, or Swirls.
- **Nail Style** (`nail_style`, text, optional): 
  * Please enter your desired nail style, such as a specific style or design.
- **Nail Quantity** (`nail_quantity`, number, optional): 
  * Please enter the quantity of nails you would like to order.
- **Additional Information** (`client_info`, note, optional): 
  * Please enter any additional information about your order.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select multiple options for nail design and nail pattern if you want to have a combination of designs.
* If you have any specific requests or special instructions, please enter them in the "Additional Information" field.
