# Nail Polish Durability Expectation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather user feedback on the durability and quality of nail polishes to help the manufacturer understand user expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please answer each question to the best of your ability. The form is divided into sections for better understanding. Please read each question carefully and follow the instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you notice streaks with this polish?**
  (select multiple): This question asks about the frequency of streaks you observe with the polish. Choose all that apply from the options provided.
  * Often: You frequently notice streaks with the polish.
  * Rarely: You rarely notice streaks with the polish.
  * Never: You do not notice streaks with the polish.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the polish?**
