# Nail Art Glitter Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information and consent from clients to apply for nail art glitter services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Client Details in the first section, including any relevant information about your name, contact number, and email address.
2. In the Allergy Information section, provide any allergies or sensitivities you may have that may affect the services you require.
3. Sign the Signature section to confirm you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the service.
4. Fill in your Contact Information in the last section, including any relevant details about your contact number and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (Client Details, text, required): This is the first section of the form where you need to fill in your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Allergy Information** (Allergy Information, text, required): This section is used to provide any allergies or sensitivities you may have that may affect the services you require. Please ensure you provide accurate information so that the service provider can take necessary precautions.
* **Signature** (Signature, note, required): This is the section where you need to sign to confirm you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the service. Please make sure to sign with a clear and legible writing.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): This is the last section where you need to fill in any relevant details about your contact number and address. Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all sections completely and accurately.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure it is correct.
* Sign the signature section carefully, as it confirms you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the service.
