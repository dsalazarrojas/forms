# Nail Client Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for internal use by nail salons or technicians to manage client bookings, collecting client information and booking details for operational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's First Name.
2. Enter the client's Email address.
3. Choose the Preferred Date for the booking.
4. Select the Start Time for the appointment.
5. Choose the Service Type from the provided options.
6. Enter any Special Requests you have for the client.
7. Confirm the booking by selecting True from the Confirm Booking field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the client's name.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Preferred Date**: Choose the date you would like to book the appointment.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time of the appointment.
* **Service Type**: Choose the type of service you would like to provide to the client.
* **Service Details**: Enter any additional information about the service details.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you have for the client.
* **Confirm Booking**: Confirm the booking by selecting "True".
