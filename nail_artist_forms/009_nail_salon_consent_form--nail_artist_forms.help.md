# Nail Salon Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information and consent from clients for nail services provided by the salon. It is a crucial document that ensures clients understand the terms and conditions of services offered.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter a brief title for the form in the **Form Title** field.
2. Provide a short description of the form in the **Form Description** field.
3. If the client has been referred to a specific nail artist, indicate this in the **Form Category** field. If not, choose 'No'.
4. In the **Nail Artists** field, select all the nail artists the client has been referred to by. You can select multiple artists.
5. Fill in the client's **Client Name**.
6. Enter the client's **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** for easy communication.
7. Provide the client's **Email Address**.
8. Enter the client's **Contact Number**.
9. Enter the client's **Date Of Birth** for age verification.
10. Fill in the client's **Social Security** number (or equivalent ID) for identification purposes.
11. In the **Consent** field, type in any comments or special requests the client may have.
12. Read and agree to the **Terms Of Service** by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
13. Sign and date the form by typing in your **Signature** and entering the date in the **Signed At** field.
14. Enter your IP address and user agent information in the **IP Address** and **User Agent** fields for tracking and security purposes.
15. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for **Acceptance** and **Refusal** to indicate your agreement or refusal to the terms of service.
16. In the **Comments** field, add any additional comments or special requests the client may have.
17. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for **Termination** if you need to end the client relationship.
18. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for **Agreement** to indicate your agreement to the terms of service.
19. If there are any other agreements or details not specified in this form, select 'Yes' for **Other Agreement**.
20. In the **Other Details** field, provide any additional information or details not specified in this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: A short title for the form.
* **Form Description**: A brief description of the form.
* **Form Category**: If the client has been referred to a specific nail artist, choose 'Yes', otherwise choose 'No'.
* **Nail Artists**: If the client has been referred to multiple nail artists, select all the artists in this field.
* **Client Name**: The client's full name.
* **Contact Email**: The client's email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone**: The client's phone number for communication.
* **Email Address**: The client's email address.
* **Contact Number**: The client's phone number.
* **Date Of Birth**: The client's date of birth for age verification.
* **Social Security**: The client's social security number (or equivalent ID) for identification purposes.
* **Consent**: Any comments or special requests the client may have.
* **Terms Of Service**: Read and agree to the terms of service.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form for verification.
* **Signed At**: Enter the date and time of signing for verification.
* **IP Address**: Enter the client's IP address for tracking and security purposes.
* **User Agent**: Enter the client's user agent information for tracking and security purposes.
* **Acceptance**: Select 'Yes' to indicate your agreement to the terms of service.
* **Refusal**: Select 'Yes' to indicate your refusal to the terms of service.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or special requests the client may have.
* **Termination**: Select 'Yes' to indicate the need to end the client relationship.
* **Agreement**: Select 'Yes' to indicate your agreement to the terms of service.
* **Other Agreement**: If there are any other agreements or details not specified in this form, select 'Yes'.
* **Other Details**: Provide any additional information or details not specified in this form.
