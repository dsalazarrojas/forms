# Nail Technician Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients before a nail technician consultation to provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's name in the "Client's Name" field.
2. Select the type of nail you are interested in from the "Nail Type" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the desired nail shape from the "Nail Shape" dropdown menu. You can select multiple options.
4. Select the color you would like to have for your nails from the "Nail Color" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the desired length for your nails in the "Nail Length" field.
6. Enter the width of your nails in the "Nail Width" field.
7. Check the box if you would like to have your nails thickened.
8. Check the box if you would like to have your nails strengthened.
9. Check the box if you would like to have your nail shape strengthened.
10. Check the box if you would like to have your nail color strengthened.
11. Enter your contact information in the "Client's Contact Information" field.
12. If you have any additional comments or requests, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client's Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
  This field is for your reference and will not be used for any other purpose.
- **Nail Type** (2, select_one, required): Select the type of nail you are interested in.
  This will help the nail technician understand your preferences and provide the best service for you.
- **Nail Shape** (3, select_multiple, optional): Choose the shape of your nail.
  You can select multiple options if you have multiple nails that need to be shaped.
- **Nail Color** (4, select_one, optional): Select the color you would like to have for your nails.
  This will help the nail technician choose the best color for you.
- **Nail Length** (5, number, optional): Enter the desired length for your nails.
  Please use whole numbers only.
- **Nail Width** (6, number, optional): Enter the width of your nails.
  Please use whole numbers only.
- **Nail Thickening** (7, select_one, optional): Check the box if you would like to have your nails thickened.
  This service can add strength and durability to your nails.
- **Nail Strengthening** (8, select_one, optional): Check the box if you would like to have your nails strengthened.
  This service can add resistance to nail breaks and damage.
- **Shape Strengthening** (9, select_one, optional): Check the box if you would like to have your nail shape strengthened.
  This service can help maintain the shape of your nails.
- **Color Strengthening** (10, select_one, optional): Check the box if you would like to have your nail color strengthened.
  This service can help your nails look brighter and more vibrant.
- **Client's Contact Information** (11, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
  This will allow the nail technician to get in touch with you about your appointment or any concerns.
- **Additional Comments** (12, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests.
  This is your chance to share any other concerns or preferences with the nail technician.
