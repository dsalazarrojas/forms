# Reference Check For Team Members - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about team members' performance and behavior to help team managers make informed decisions. Please complete this form accurately and truthfully to provide a fair evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your answer for each of the questions below. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your team manager for guidance.
2. Answer all fields that are required (marked as "Required: true" in the form).
3. You may leave fields that are not required blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reference Check 1** (`reference_check_form_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Please choose whether this team member has a good reference relationship (`Yes` or `No`). If you are unsure, please select `No`.
* **Reference Relationship** (`reference_check_form_2`, text): Please provide the name and job title of the reference person you have chosen (e.g., "John Smith, Team Lead").
* **Reference Experience** (`reference_check_form_3`, text): Please describe your working relationship with the reference person, including how long you've worked together and any notable events or projects you've collaborated on.
* **Reference Performance** (`reference_check_form_4`, select_one, required: false): Please choose how you would rate this team member's performance (`Yes` or `No`).
* **Manager Reference** (`reference_check_form_5`, select_one, required: false): Please choose whether you have a working relationship with this team member's manager (`Yes` or `No`).
* **Direct Report** (`reference_check_form_6`, text): Please describe any direct reports or feedback from this team member's manager or other team members.
* **Notes** (`reference_check_form_7`, note, required: false): If you have any additional information or comments about this team member, please enter it below.
