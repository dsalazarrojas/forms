# food_service_worker_recommendation_letter_request - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from a food service worker who is recommending an applicant for a job. The purpose of this form is to gather the recommender's contact information, job details, and signature, which will be used to verify the applicant's work history and experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the information requested.
2. Ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: Enter the name of the applicant you are recommending.
* **Recommender Name**: Enter your name as the recommender.
* **Recommender E-mail**: Enter your email address as the recommender.
* **Recommender Title**: Enter your job title or position as the recommender.
* **Recommender Department**: Enter the department you are part of as the recommender.
* **Recommender Organization**: Enter the organization you are a part of as the recommender.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job the applicant was working in when you were their supervisor.
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the date the applicant started their job.
* **Job End Date**: Enter the date the applicant left their job (if applicable).
* **Job Start Month**: Enter the month the applicant started their job (if not provided in the "Job Start Date" field).
* **Job Start Year**: Enter the year the applicant started their job (if not provided in the "Job Start Date" field).
* **Recommender Signature**: Sign your name, confirming that the information provided is accurate.

Note: If a field is marked as required, please fill it in as it is essential for the form to be processed correctly.
