# Teaching Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teaching Reference Request Form is used to collect information from teachers for teaching references.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user information:
	* user name: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	* school name: Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with.
2. Provide contact information:
	* teacher email: Enter your email address.
	* school email: Enter your school's email address.
3. Specify student details:
	* grade level: Select the current grade level of the student.
	* department: Enter the department the student is affiliated with.
	* subject area: Enter the subject area the student is studying.
4. Select a reference type:
	* reference request type: Choose the type of reference you need:
		+ Option 1
		+ Option 2
		+ Option 3
5. Provide a reason for the reference request:
	* reference request reason: Describe why you are requesting a reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Your name as it appears on your identification.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with.

### Contact Information
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school's email address.

### Student Details
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, text, optional): Select the current grade level of the student.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department the student is affiliated with.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, text, optional): Enter the subject area the student is studying.

### Reference Request
* **Reference Request Type** (`reference_request_type`, select_one, true): Choose the type of reference you need. (Note: Required field)
* **Reference Request Reason** (`reference_request_reason`, text, optional): Describe why you are requesting a reference.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not already registered or submitted a duplicate reference request.
* Double-check your contact information to avoid any errors.
* Describe your reference request reason clearly to help the teacher provide an accurate reference.
* If you have any questions, contact your school administration for assistance.
