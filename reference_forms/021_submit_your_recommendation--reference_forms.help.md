# submit your recommendation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user recommendations for internal and external stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your contact details, including your name and email address.
3. Select the type of recommendation you are submitting.
4. Choose the files you want to attach with your recommendation (if applicable).
5. Provide permission for us to use your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false): Please provide your name and email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Type Of Recommendation (id: 2)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the type of recommendation you are submitting. Options are:
	+ Internal
	+ External
	+ Both
* **Type Of Recommendation 2 (id: 3)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is a duplicate of the "Type Of Recommendation" field, please make sure to fill out both fields consistently.
* **Do You Give Us Permission (id: 4)** (`text`, required: true): Please type "yes" in this field to confirm you give us permission to use your information.
* **File Uploads (id: 5)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): If you selected "Internal" or "External" in the "Type Of Recommendation" field, you will be prompted to select which files to attach. Please choose your files from the options provided.
* **File Uploads 2 (id: 6)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is similar to "File Uploads", please fill it out if you selected "Internal" or "External" in the "Type Of Recommendation" field.
* **File Uploads 3 (id: 7)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is identical to "File Uploads", please fill it out if you selected "Internal" or "External" in the "Type Of Recommendation" field.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have permission to submit on behalf of others.
- Please fill out the form consistently for both "Type Of Recommendation" fields.
- Please attach files relevant to your recommendation.
