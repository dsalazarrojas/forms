# Rental Reference Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect reference information from tenants when they are renting a property, to verify their rental history and gather relevant details about their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" with the requested information.
2. Enter your tenant name and move-in date in the respective fields.
3. Provide the property address where you rented.
4. Enter the name and contact information of your rental reference.
5. Specify your relationship with the reference and a brief note about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started renting the property.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the property you rented.
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your rental reference.
* **Reference Relationship** (`reference_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with your reference (e.g. previous landlord, property manager, etc.).
* **Reference Contact Info** (`reference_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information of your reference (e.g. phone number, email, etc.).
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, required): Enter the date you finished renting the property.
* **Reference Note** (`reference_note`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience with the property.
