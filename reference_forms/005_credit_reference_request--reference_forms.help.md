# Credit Reference Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a person's employment and contact details to aid in a credit application or verification process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your personal details in the required fields.
* Select your employment status (Active, Inactive, or None) in the Employer Information field.
* Enter your date of birth, employment start date, and social security number in the respective fields.
* Enter your contact information (phone number and email) in the Contact Information fields.
* Enter your employer's address in the Employer Address fields.
* Enter any additional details or comments in the fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address 1** (`address1`, text, required: false): Enter your current address.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's address (if different from your current address).
* **Employer Information** (`employer`, select_one, required: false): Select your employment status (Active, Inactive, or None).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required: false): Enter your social security number (if applicable).
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter your employment start date.
* **Employer Address 1** (`employer_address_1`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's address 1.
* **Employer Address 2** (`employer_address_2`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's address 2.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's phone number.
* **Employer Email** (`employer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your employer's email address.

Note: All fields are optional unless specifically marked as required. Please fill in all relevant fields to provide complete and accurate information.
