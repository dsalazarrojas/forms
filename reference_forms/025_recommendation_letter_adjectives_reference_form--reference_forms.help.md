# Recommendation Letter Adjectives Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and comments from references about a candidate's performance, skills, or character.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each section of the form as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Info** (Page 2): Please enter the candidate's name, job title, and department or organization they worked in or are associated with.
* **Reference Info** (Page 3): Please enter your name and title/position as the reference, as well as your contact information (email and phone number).
* **Feedback** (Page 4): Please provide your honest feedback about the candidate, using the select_multiple options:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neither Agree Nor Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ No Opinion
* **Additional Comments** (Page 5): Please provide any additional comments or information about the candidate that you would like to add.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective in your feedback
* Keep your feedback concise and clear
* Proofread your responses for accuracy
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
