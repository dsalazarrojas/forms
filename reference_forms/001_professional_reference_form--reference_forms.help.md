# professional-reference-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect professional references from job applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information about the candidate's job history.
2. Enter the contact information for the reference (email, phone number).
3. Provide a professional referral message, explaining why you are referring the candidate.
4. Rate the candidate's overall quality and provide a reason for the referral score.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's job title while they worked with you.
* **Job Employer Name** (`job_employer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company or organization the candidate worked for.
* **Job Employer Website** (`job_employer_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the company or organization the candidate worked for.
* **Job Employer Contact Info** (`job_employer_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the job employer.
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a reference.
* **Reference Email** (`reference_email`, text, required): Enter your email address as a reference.
* **Reference Phone** (`reference_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as a reference.
* **Reference LinkedIn Profile** (`reference_linkedin_profile`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your LinkedIn profile.
* **Reference Twitter Profile** (`reference_twitter_profile`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your Twitter profile.
* **Reference Facebook Profile** (`reference_facebook_profile`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your Facebook profile.
* **Professional Referral Message** (`professional_referral_message`, note, required): Enter a brief message explaining why you are referring the candidate.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, number, optional): Rate the candidate's overall performance (1-10).
* **Professional Referral Score Reason** (`professional_referral_score_reason`, note, required): Enter a brief reason for the rating score.
* **Recommended for Position** (`recommended_for_position`, select one, required): Check if you recommend the candidate for a specific position.
* **Recommended for Hire** (`recommended_for_hire`, select one, required): Check if you recommend the candidate for hiring.
* **Overall Quality of Candidate** (`overall_quality_of_candidate`, select one, required): Rate the candidate's overall quality (excellent, good, satisfactory, unsatisfactory).
* **Professional Referral Score** (`professional_referral_score`, number, required): Rate the candidate's overall performance (1-10).
* **Professional Referral Score Reason** (`professional_referral_score_reason`, note, required): Enter a brief reason for the referral score.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled out before submitting the form.
