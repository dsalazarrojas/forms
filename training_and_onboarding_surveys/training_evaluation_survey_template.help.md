# training_evaluation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training evaluation survey template" is a tool used to collect feedback on your recent training experience. This form helps to identify areas for improvement in future training programs and content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and think about your recent training experience.
2. Choose your answer from the provided options for each question.
3. Select all applicable options for the "Which training sessions or modules did you find most valuable?" question.
4. Enter your thoughts and suggestions for "What aspect of the training would you most like to see improved?" and "Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for future training?"

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied were you with the overall training experience?** (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the training. Choose the answer that best reflects your feelings.
* **Which training sessions or modules did you find most valuable?** (Select all that apply): This question allows you to identify the most valuable training sessions or modules. Select all the sessions or modules that you think were the most beneficial.
* **Was the training event well-organized and clearly structured?** (True, False, Somewhat): This question assesses the organization and structure of the training event. Choose True if the event was well-organized, False if it wasn't, and Somewhat if it was somewhat well-organized but not entirely.
* **How relevant was the training content to your job responsibilities?** (Extremely relevant, Very relevant, Moderately relevant, Slightly relevant, Not relevant at all): This question measures the relevance of the training content to your work. Choose the answer that best describes your experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend this training to a colleague?** (Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely): This question helps us understand how likely you are to suggest this training to others. Choose the answer that best reflects your intention.
* **What aspect of the training would you most like to see improved?** (Text input): Here, you can provide specific feedback on what you think could be improved about the training. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for future training?** (Text input): This is an open-ended question where you can share any additional thoughts or ideas you have for future training programs and content.
