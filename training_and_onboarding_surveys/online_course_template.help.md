# online_course_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "online_course_template" form is designed to collect feedback from online course participants. This form helps us understand their overall satisfaction with the course, identify valuable topics or modules, assess the platform's usability, and determine the effectiveness of the course content. Your input is essential in shaping future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback on the online course, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the level of satisfaction you felt with the overall Online Course experience.
2. Select all the topics or modules you found most valuable.
3. Answer whether the online course platform was easy to navigate and use.
4. Evaluate how well the course content met your learning objectives.
5. Indicate how likely you are to recommend this online course to a colleague or friend.
6. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please specify them.
7. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied were you with the overall Online Course experience?** (`options: Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): Choose the level of satisfaction that best describes your experience with the online course.
- **Which topics or modules did you find most valuable?** (`options: Module 1: Introduction`, `Module 2: Core Concepts`, `Module 3: Advanced Practice`, `Practical Exercises`, `Q&A Sessions`, `Supplementary Materials`): Select all the topics or modules you found most valuable or useful.
	+ Note: You can select multiple options.
- **Was the online course platform easy to navigate and use?** (`options: true`, `Mostly`, `false`): Choose whether the platform was easy to use or not.
- **Did the course content meet your learning objectives?** (`options: Completely`, `Mostly`, `Partially`, `Not at all`): Evaluate how well the course content met your learning objectives.
- **How likely are you to recommend this online course to a colleague or friend?** (`options: Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): Indicate how likely you are to recommend this online course to others.
- **What aspects of the online course could be improved?** (`text`): Provide specific suggestions or feedback on areas that need improvement.
- **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`text`): Share any other thoughts or ideas you have about the online course.
