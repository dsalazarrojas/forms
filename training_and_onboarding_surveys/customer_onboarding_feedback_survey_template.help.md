# Customer Onboarding Feedback Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their overall onboarding experience with our product. The goal is to identify areas of improvement and make adjustments to make the process more efficient and effective for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the five options for the first question, "How satisfied are you with the overall customer onboarding process?"
2. Select all the options that apply for the second question, "Which aspects of the onboarding process did you find most valuable?". You can select multiple options.
3. Choose one of the five options for the third question, "Was the onboarding process clear and easy to follow?".
4. Select one of the five options for the fourth question, "How confident do you feel using our product after completing the onboarding?"
5. Type in your answer for the fifth question, "Do you have any suggestions for improving the onboarding experience?"
6. Type in your answer for the sixth question, "What information or resources do you feel were missing from the onboarding process?"

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied are you with the overall customer onboarding process?** (`Very satisfied`, `single`, required): Please select one of the five options that best describes your overall satisfaction with the onboarding process. This will help us understand your general experience with our product.
- **Which aspects of the onboarding process did you find most valuable?** (`Product demo`, `Setup guide`, `Welcome emails`, `Support contact`, `Live training sessions`, `multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to you, as this will help us understand what parts of the onboarding process you found most beneficial.
- **Was the onboarding process clear and easy to follow?** (`Very confident`, `Moderately confident`, `Neutral`, `Slightly uncertain`, `Not confident at all`, `single`, required): Choose one of the five options that best describes your experience with the clarity and ease of the onboarding process.
- **Do you have any suggestions for improving the onboarding experience?** (`text`, optional): If you have any ideas or feedback for improving our onboarding process, please type them in here. We appreciate your input!
- **What information or resources did you feel were missing from the onboarding process?** (`text`, optional): If you feel that there were any missing resources or information during the onboarding process, please type them in here so we can improve future experiences.
