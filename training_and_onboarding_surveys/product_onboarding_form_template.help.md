# Product Onboarding Form Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on your experience with the product onboarding process. Your responses will help us understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to improve the product and provide a better experience for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question according to your experience with the product onboarding process. For multiple-choice questions, select all the options that apply. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you feel is necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with your overall product onboarding experience?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the onboarding process. Please choose the option that best reflects your feelings.
* **How easy was it to understand and apply the training materials provided during onboarding?** (`Very easy`, `Easy`, `Neutral`, `Difficult`, `Very difficult`): This question assesses your experience with the training materials. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Which aspects of the onboarding were most helpful to you?** (`Guided product tour`, `Documentation/guides`, `Video tutorials`, `Live training sessions`, `Support team assistance`): This question allows you to choose the onboarding materials that were most helpful to you. Select all the options that apply.
* **Do you feel confident using the product independently after completing the onboarding?** (`true`, `Mostly`, `Not really`, `false`): This question checks your confidence level in using the product after completing the onboarding process.
* **What specific improvements would you suggest for the product onboarding process?** (`text`): This question is open-ended, allowing you to provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the onboarding process.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding your onboarding?** (`text`): This question is also open-ended, providing a space for you to share any additional thoughts or concerns about your onboarding experience.
