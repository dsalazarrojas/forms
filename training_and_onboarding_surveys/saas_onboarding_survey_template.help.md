**saas_onboarding_survey_template - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on your recent SaaS onboarding experience and training process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your level of satisfaction with the overall SaaS onboarding experience.
2. Check all the options that apply to the aspects of the onboarding process that you found most valuable.
3. Choose your confidence level using the software independently after onboarding.
4. Provide any suggestions you have for improving the SaaS onboarding program.
5. Describe the biggest challenge you faced during onboarding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied were you with the overall SaaS onboarding experience?**
	+ **Very satisfied**: You were extremely happy with the onboarding process and felt it met all your expectations.
	+ **Somewhat satisfied**: You were somewhat happy with the onboarding process but felt it could be improved.
	+ **Neutral**: You were neutral or neutralized about the onboarding process.
	+ **Somewhat dissatisfied**: You were somewhat unhappy with the onboarding process.
	+ **Very dissatisfied**: You were extremely unhappy with the onboarding process.
* **Which aspects of the onboarding process did you find most valuable?**
	+ **Initial account setup**: The process of setting up your account was smooth and efficient.
	+ **Core feature walkthroughs**: The step-by-step walkthroughs of core features were clear and effective.
	+ **Integrations setup**: The integration process was seamless and easy to follow.
	+ **Accessing support resources**: The support resources were readily available and helpful.
	+ **Best practices and tips**: The best practices and tips provided were useful and informative.
	+ **Data import/migration**: The data import and migration process was smooth and efficient.
* **Was the onboarding process well-structured and easy to follow?**
	+ **Yes**: The onboarding process was clear and easy to follow.
	+ **No**: The onboarding process was confusing or difficult to follow.
* **How confident do you feel using the software independently after onboarding?**
	+ **Very confident**: You feel extremely confident using the software independently.
	+ **Moderately confident**: You feel somewhat confident using the software independently.
	+ **Slightly confident**: You feel a bit confident using the software independently.
	+ **Not confident at all**: You do not feel confident using the software independently.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving our SaaS onboarding program?**
	+ Provide your honest and candid feedback on how to improve the onboarding process.
* **What was the biggest challenge you faced during onboarding?**
	+ Describe the biggest challenge you faced during onboarding.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help improve the onboarding process.
* Select all the options that apply to the aspects of the onboarding process that you found most valuable.
* Provide clear and concise answers to the questions, especially for the open-ended questions.
* If you have any suggestions, be specific and provide concrete ideas for improvement.

By following these instructions, you will be able to provide valuable feedback that will help improve the SaaS onboarding process.
