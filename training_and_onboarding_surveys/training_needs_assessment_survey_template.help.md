# Training Needs Assessment Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your current training needs, preferences, and expectations. It will help us identify areas of improvement and development to better tailor our training programs to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. For the question "Which training topics or skills development areas would be most valuable to you?", select all the options that apply to you.
3. Be specific with your comments and suggestions in the "Do you have any other comments or suggestions regarding training and development?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with the current training opportunities available to you?**
	+ Very satisfied: You're very happy with the current training opportunities.
	+ Somewhat satisfied: You're somewhat happy, but there's room for improvement.
	+ Neutral: You're neutral about the current training opportunities.
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied: You're somewhat unhappy with the current training opportunities.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You're very unhappy with the current training opportunities.
* **Which training topics or skills development areas would be most valuable to you?** (Select all that apply)
	+ Technical skills: Training on technical skills or knowledge.
	+ Communication skills: Training on communication skills or soft skills.
	+ Leadership development: Training on leadership skills or abilities.
	+ Product knowledge: Training on product-specific knowledge or information.
	+ Project management: Training on project management skills or abilities.
* **What training format do you prefer?**
	+ In-person workshops: A face-to-face training session.
	+ Online live sessions: A live online training session.
	+ Self-paced modules: Training materials and resources available at your own pace.
	+ Blended learning: Combination of face-to-face and online training.
* **How likely are you to participate in future training programs?**
	+ Very likely: You're very likely to participate.
	+ Likely: You're somewhat likely to participate.
	+ Neutral: You're neutral about participating.
	+ Unlikely: You're somewhat unlikely to participate.
	+ Very unlikely: You're very unlikely to participate.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions regarding training and development?**
	+ Share your thoughts and suggestions about training and development opportunities.

## Tips
- Be honest and specific with your answers.
- If you're unsure about any question, try to answer based on your best judgment.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
