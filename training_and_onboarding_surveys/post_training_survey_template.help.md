# post_training_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from training program attendees, helping us improve future programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions based on your experience with the recent training program.
2. Select all the sessions or topics that you found most valuable.
3. Choose your response for each question, providing your honest feedback.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How satisfied were you with the program overall?
* **Your Overall Satisfaction** (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the training program. This will help us understand your overall experience.
### Which sessions or topics did you find most valuable?
* **Valuable Sessions** (Module A: Core Concepts, Module B: Advanced Techniques, Workshop: Practical Application, Q&A Session, Networking Event, Other): Select all the sessions or topics that you found most valuable or useful. This will help us identify areas of strength in the program.
### Was the event well organized and structured?
* **Event Organization** (True, False, Partially): Let us know if you felt the event was well-planned and structured. Your feedback will help us improve future event organization.
### How likely are you to attend a similar training in the future?
* **Likelihood to Attend Future Training** (Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely): Rate your likelihood of attending similar training programs in the future.
### Additional Comments or Suggestions
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: If you have any thoughts or ideas on how we can improve the training program, please share them here. Your feedback is valuable to us.
