# employee_orientation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the employee orientation and onboarding experience, with the aim of improving the program's effectiveness and employee satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, choosing from the provided options that best fit your experience.
2. Select all applicable topics that were covered effectively during orientation.
3. Choose your level of satisfaction with the provided information and support you received.
4. Write down one thing that could improve the orientation experience.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions you have for the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`question1`, `single`): How satisfied were you with the overall employee orientation program? Choose from Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Usefulness of Information** (`question2`, `single`): How would you rate the usefulness of the information provided during orientation? Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Topics Covered** (`question3`, `multiple`): Which of the following topics were covered effectively during orientation? (Select all that apply) Choose from:
	+ Company culture
	+ Job responsibilities
	+ Benefits & HR
	+ IT setup
	+ Safety procedures
	+ Team introductions
* **Support Feeling** (`question4`, `single`): Do you feel adequately supported as a new employee after orientation? Choose from True, Mostly, Somewhat, or Not yet.
* **Improvement Suggestion** (`question5`, `text`): What one thing could significantly improve the employee orientation experience? Describe in your own words.
* **Additional Comments** (`question6`, `text`): Do you have any additional comments or suggestions regarding your onboarding? Share your thoughts.
