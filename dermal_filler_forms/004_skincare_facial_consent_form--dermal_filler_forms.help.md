# Skincare Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients before their upcoming visit to the skincare clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer all questions on each page to provide accurate and complete information.
* Review the fields carefully to ensure you are providing the correct type of information.
* If you are unsure about a specific field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Upcoming Visit
* **Upcoming Visit** (`upcoming_visit`, text, not required): Write a brief description of your upcoming visit, including the date and time of the appointment, and any specific treatments or procedures you are scheduled to undergo.

### Skincare Expectations
* **Skincare Expectations** (`skincare_expectations`, text, not required): Describe your expectations for your skincare treatment, including any specific goals or concerns you have.

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, not required): List any medical conditions you have that may be relevant to your treatment, such as allergies or sensitivities.

### Medications
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, not required): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.

### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, not required): List any allergies you have that may be relevant to your treatment, such as food or environmental allergies.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, not required): Provide the contact information for an emergency contact, in case of an unexpected situation.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Provide your contact number for follow-up or scheduling purposes.

### Contact Relationship
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, text, not required): Describe your relationship with the person providing your contact information.

### Consent Type
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of consent you are giving, either "General" or "Specific".

### Consent Date
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, not required): Date of the consent.

### Consent Time
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, not required): Time of the consent.

### Consent Signature
* **Consent Signature** (`consent_signature`, note, not required): Space for the patient's signature.

### Signature Date
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, not required): Date of the signature.

### Consent Status
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, select_multiple, not required): Select the status of your consent, either "Active" or "Inactive".

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, not required): Provide any relevant medical history, such as previous treatments or medical conditions.

### Physician Signature
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, note, not required): Space for the physician's signature.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability and with honesty.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* This form is for your protection and the protection of the healthcare professionals who will be providing your care.
