# dermal_diagnosis_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dermal_diagnosis_form is designed to collect information for a dermal diagnosis, which refers to the examination and treatment of skin conditions or issues. This form is intended to help medical professionals or healthcare providers make informed decisions about a client's skin health.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2.  Provide a brief summary of the client's condition in the **Condition Summary** field.
3.  Describe the location of the condition in the **Location** field.
4.  Share the client's medical history, if applicable, in the **Medical History** field.
5.  Enter the dermal diagnosis in the **Dermal Diagnosis** field.
6.  Suggest any recommended treatment for the client's condition in the **Recommended Treatment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or patient for whom the dermal diagnosis is being performed.
*   **Condition Summary**: Provide a brief summary of the client's condition, including any relevant symptoms or concerns.
*   **Location**: Describe the location of the condition on the client's body, such as the affected area or region.
*   **Medical History**: Share any relevant medical history that may impact the dermal diagnosis, including previous treatments or allergies.
*   **Dermal Diagnosis**: Enter the diagnosis of the client's skin condition, based on the information provided.
*   **Recommended Treatment**: Suggest any recommended treatment or course of action for the client's condition.

## Tips

Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to inform the dermal diagnosis. If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please refer to a medical professional for guidance.
