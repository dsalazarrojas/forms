# dermal_filler_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that you're aware of your medical history, potential allergies, and other essential information for safe and successful dermal filler treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form completely, including all required and optional sections.
- Ensure all dates and times are entered in the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (1, text): The title of this form, which is self-explanatory.
* **Please fill out this form completely** (2, text): A reminder to fill out the form thoroughly.
* **Patient Name** (3, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Medical History** (5, text): Describe any medical conditions you have, including surgeries, allergies, or other relevant information.
* **Allergies** (6, text): List any allergies you have, including any medications or substances you're allergic to.
* **Allergies List** (7, select multiple): Select "Yes" if you have any of the listed allergies, or "No" if you don't.
* **Medical Conditions** (8, text): Describe any medical conditions you have that may affect your treatment.
* **Surgical History** (9, text): Describe any surgeries you've had, including dates and types of procedures.
* **Medication List** (10, select multiple): Select "Yes" if you take any of the listed medications, or "No" if you don't.
* **Medical Tests** (11, text): Describe any recent medical tests or exams you've had.
* **Patient Signature** (12, text): Sign and date your name to confirm that you've reviewed and completed this form.
* **Date** (13, date): Enter the date you're having the dermal filler treatment.
* **Time** (14, time): Enter the time you're having the dermal filler treatment.
* **Patient Phone** (15, text): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Medical Provider** (16, text): Enter the name of your doctor or medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (17, text): Enter the phone number of your medical provider.
* **Patient Email** (18, email): Enter your email address in case we need to contact you.
* **Medical History Important** (19, text): Describe any information about your medical history that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Medication List 2** (20, select multiple): Select "Yes" if you take any of the listed medications, or "No" if you don't.
* **Medical Test Results** (21, text): Describe any recent medical test results, including dates and types of tests.
* **Medical Test Results List** (22, select multiple): Select "Yes" if you have any of the listed medical test results, or "No" if you don't.
* **Medical Procedure** (23, text): Describe the medical procedure or treatment you're having.
* **Medical Provider Name** (24, text): Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Title** (25, text): Enter the title or specialty of your medical provider.
