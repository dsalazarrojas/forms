# dermal_filler_aftercare_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the dermal filler aftercare form is to collect information from patients after they have received dermal filler treatments. This form helps to ensure that the patient is following proper aftercare instructions to achieve the best possible results from their treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Provide any aftercare advice or instructions you have received from your healthcare provider in the "Aftercare Advice" field.
6. Select the follow-up appointment schedule that best suits your needs from the "Follow Up Appointments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`name`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for follow-up appointments and information.
* **Phone** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up appointments and information.
* **Address** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Aftercare Advice** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Provide any instructions or advice you have received from your healthcare provider regarding aftercare for your treatment.
* **Follow Up Appointments** (`name`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the schedule for your follow-up appointments, such as 1 hour, 1 week, or 2 weeks.
