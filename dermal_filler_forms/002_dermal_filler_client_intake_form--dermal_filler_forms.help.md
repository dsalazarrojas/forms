## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Info)** (`1`, text, required/required): This is the client's basic information including their name and any other details that might be useful for our records.
* **Contact Information (Contact Info)** (`2`, text, required/optional): Please enter the client's contact information for our staff to contact them.
* **Medical Information (Medical Info)** (`3`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant medical history that might affect the treatment.
* **Appointment Information (Appointment Info)** (`4`, text, required/optional): Enter the details about the appointment such as date and time.
* **Dermal Filler History (Dermal Filler History)** (`5`, text, required/optional): Describe your previous dermal filler experiences, successes, and failures.
* **Medical History (Medical History)** (`6`, text, required/optional): List any relevant medical history that might impact the treatment.
* **Allergies (Allergies)** (`7`, text, required/optional): If the client has any allergies, please list them here.
* **Emergency Contacts (Emergency Contacts)** (`8`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information of emergency contacts in case the client cannot be reached by us.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)** (`9`, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions that might affect the treatment.
* **Treatment Options (Treatment Options)** (`10`, select_one, required/optional): Select the desired treatment option from the available choices.
* **Medical Procedure (Medical Procedure)** (`11`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the medical procedure you are interested in.
* **Appointment Preferences (Appointment Preferences)** (`12`, text, required/optional): State any specific preferences for the appointment such as time or location.
* **Dermal Filler Type (Dermal Filler Type)** (`13`, select_one, required/optional): Select the dermal filler type you would like to have.
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date)** (`14`, date, required/optional): Enter the desired date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time (Appointment Time)** (`15`, time, required/optional): Choose the desired time for the appointment.
* **Preferred Contact Method (Preferred Contact Method)** (`16`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Note (Note)** (`17`, note, required/optional): Add any comments or notes about the client.
* **Email (Email)** (`18`, email, required/optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`19`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address (Address)** (`20`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's address.
* **Dermal Filler Procedure (Dermal Filler Procedure)** (`21`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the dermal filler procedure you want to have.
* **Follow Up (Follow Up)** (`22`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the follow-up schedule you would like to have.
* **Insurance Provider (Insurance Provider)** (`23`, select_one, required/optional): Select your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Plan (Insurance Plan)** (`24`, select_one, required/optional): Select your insurance plan.
* **Consent (Consent)** (`25`, select_one, required/true): This is a required field indicating that the client has given consent for the treatment. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If unsure about any information, please contact the staff for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
