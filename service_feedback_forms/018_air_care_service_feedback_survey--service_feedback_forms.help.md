# Air Care Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback about your recent service experience with us. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Please save your answers as you complete each section, and review them before submitting.
2. If you need help or clarification on any field, ask us before proceeding.
3. Answer each question truthfully, and provide as much detail as possible.
4. You can save and review your answers before submitting, but please make sure to submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, required)
	+ Full name as you would like us to address you.
* **2. Date of Service** (`Date of Service`, required)
	+ The date when our service was performed.
* **3. Type of Service** (`Type of Service`, required)
	+ Select the type of service we provided (AC installation, AC repair, etc.).
* **4. Technician Name** (`Technician Name`, optional)
	+ If you want to mention a specific technician's name, please type it here. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **5. Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, required)
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with our service (Very satisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
* **6. Technician Professionalism** (`Technician Professionalism`, required)
	+ Rate the technician's professionalism (Excellent, Good, etc.).
* **7. Technician Knowledge** (`Technician Knowledge`, required)
	+ Rate the technician's knowledge (Excellent, Good, etc.).
* **8. Punctuality** (`Punctuality`, required)
	+ Rate how on time the technician arrived (Early, On time, etc.).
* **9. Quality of Work** (`Quality of Work`, required)
	+ Rate the quality of work performed (Excellent, Good, etc.).
* **10. Cleanliness** (`Cleanliness`, required)
	+ Rate how well the technician cleaned up after the work (Excellent, Good, etc.).
* **11. Value for Money** (`Value for Money`, required)
	+ Rate the value for the price paid (Excellent, Good, etc.).
* **12. Was Your Problem Resolved** (`Was Your Problem Resolved`, required)
	+ Choose if we completely, partially, or not resolved your issue.
* **13. Explanation Clarity** (`Explanation Clarity`, required)
	+ Rate how clearly the technician explained the work (Very clear, Clear, etc.).
* **14. Pricing Satisfaction** (`Pricing Satisfaction`, required)
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with the pricing (Very satisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
* **15. Scheduling Ease** (`Scheduling Ease`, required)
	+ Rate how easy it was to schedule the service (Very easy, Easy, etc.).
* **16. Recommendation Likelihood** (`Recommendation Likelihood`, required)
	+ Choose how likely you are to recommend us (Very likely, Likely, etc.).
* **17. Areas for Improvement** (`Areas for Improvement`, required)
	+ Select any areas where we could improve our service (Response time, Pricing, etc.).
* **18. Additional Services Needed** (`Additional Services Needed`, required)
	+ Select any additional services you might need from us (Heating repair, Duct sealing, etc.).
* **19. Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, optional)
	+ Any other feedback or comments about your experience.
* **20. May We Follow Up** (`May We Follow Up`, required)
	+ Choose if you're okay with us contacting you about your feedback (True, False).
* **21. Survey Date** (`Survey Date`, required)
	+ The date you're completing this survey.

Please note that some fields may have specific guidance provided in the field label or hint, so be sure to read those carefully. If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to ask.
