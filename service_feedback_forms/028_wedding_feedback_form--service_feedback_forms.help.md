# Wedding Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with wedding planners, coordinators, and services. The purpose of this form is to help us improve our services and provide better support to our clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are providing feedback on the correct wedding event.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as required) with accurate information.
3. Select the relevant options for each field as needed.
4. Provide detailed comments and feedback in the Wedding Comments field.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Organization** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering the name of the wedding organization or company that you are providing feedback on.
* **Wedding Catering** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the type of wedding catering service you experienced (e.g. Wedding Planner, Wedding Organizer, etc.).
* **Wedding Photography** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for selecting multiple types of wedding photography service you experienced (e.g. Wedding Planner, Wedding Organizer, etc.).
* **Date of Wedding** (`4`, `date`, required: false): This field is for entering the date of the wedding event.
* **Time of Wedding** (`5`, `time`, required: false): This field is for entering the time of the wedding event.
* **Venue of Wedding** (`6`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering the venue where the wedding event took place.
* **Number of Guests** (`7`, `number`, required: false): This field is for entering the number of guests attending the wedding event.
* **Number of Services** (`8`, `number`, required: false): This field is for entering the number of services provided during the wedding event.
* **Satisfaction Level of Wedding Planner** (`9`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the satisfaction level of the wedding planner.
* **Satisfaction Level of Wedding Coordinator** (`10`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the satisfaction level of the wedding coordinator.
* **Satisfaction Level of Wedding Organizer** (`11`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the satisfaction level of the wedding organizer.
* **Satisfaction Level of Wedding Services** (`12`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the satisfaction level of the wedding services provided.
* **Wedding Comments** (`13`, `note`, required: false): This field is for providing detailed comments and feedback on the wedding experience.
* **Communication Level of Wedding Planner** (`14`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the communication level of the wedding planner.
* **Communication Level of Wedding Coordinator** (`15`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the communication level of the wedding coordinator.
* **Communication Level of Wedding Organizer** (`16`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the communication level of the wedding organizer.
* **Communication Level of Wedding Services** (`17`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the communication level of the wedding services provided.
* **Availability of Wedding Planner** (`18`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the availability of the wedding planner.
* **Availability of Wedding Coordinator** (`19`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the availability of the wedding coordinator.
* **Availability of Wedding Organizer** (`20`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the availability of the wedding organizer.
* **Availability of Wedding Services** (`21`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the availability of the wedding services provided.
* **Communication Level with Wedding Planner** (`22`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the communication level with the wedding planner.
* **Communication Level with Wedding Coordinator** (`23`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the communication level with the wedding coordinator.
