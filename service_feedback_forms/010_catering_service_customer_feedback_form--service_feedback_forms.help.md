# Catering Service Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on various aspects of the catering service experience. Your input will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the type of event you attended.
3. Rate the quality of the food, service, and overall experience.
4. Provide feedback on the food freshness, temperature, and presentation.
5. Rate the staff's professionalism, efficiency, and attentiveness.
6. Rate the setup and cleanliness of the facilities.
7. Select whether you found value for money and your overall satisfaction with the service.
8. Provide comments and suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Your Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Your Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Type of Event** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event you attended (e.g., Wedding, Corporate Event, Birthday Party, etc.).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): If applicable, please enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Overall Food Quality** (`food_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall quality of the food, choosing from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Taste** (`food_taste`, select_one, required): Rate the taste of the food, choosing from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Temperature** (`food_temperature`, select_one, required): Was the food served at the correct temperature? Select Yes, No, or Some items were incorrect.
* **Food Freshness and Presentation** (`food_freshness`, select_one, required): Rate the freshness and presentation of the food, choosing from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Staff Professionalism and Courtesy** (`staff_professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate the staff's professionalism and courtesy, choosing from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Service Efficiency and Responsiveness** (`service_efficiency`, select_one, required): Rate the service efficiency and responsiveness, choosing from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Staff Attentiveness to Guests** (`staff_attentiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the staff's attentiveness to guests, choosing from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Table Setup and Presentation** (`setup_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the table setup and presentation, choosing from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Cleanliness of Facilities and Equipment** (`cleanliness`, select_one, required): Rate the cleanliness of the facilities and equipment, choosing from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate the value for money, choosing from Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, or Not Worth the Cost.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction, choosing from Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Would you Recommend this Catering Service?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Would you recommend this catering service? Choose from Yes, definitely, Probably yes, Unsure, Probably not, or No, definitely not.
* **Comments and Suggestions for Improvement** (`comments_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have for improving our service.
* **Would you use this Catering Service again?** (`future_business`, select_one, required): Would you use this catering service again? Choose from Yes, No, or Maybe.
