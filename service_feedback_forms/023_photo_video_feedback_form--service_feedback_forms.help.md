# photo_video_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on both photos and videos. The form will ask for user input, the type of feedback they are providing, and any additional comments or suggestions they may have. This form is meant to be completed by users to give feedback on photos and videos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the type of feedback you are providing: photo or video.
2. If you are providing photo feedback, enter your comments about the photo in the "Photo Feedback" field.
3. If you are providing video feedback, enter your comments about the video in the "Video Feedback" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. If you would like to contact the team, please enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Type** (`Feedback Type`, required): Select the type of feedback you are providing (photo or video).
* **Photo Feedback** (`Photo Feedback`, optional): Enter your comments about the photo.
* **Video Feedback** (`Video Feedback`, optional): Enter your comments about the video.
* **Suggestion** (`Suggestion`, optional): Enter any suggestions or ideas you may have.
* **Email** (`Email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by the team.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the team.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you may have.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your feedback to help us improve.
* You can provide feedback on multiple photos or videos by submitting multiple forms.
* If you have any technical issues while submitting the form, please contact us.
