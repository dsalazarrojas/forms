# Restaurant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Feedback Form is a tool designed to gather opinions from customers on their experience at the restaurant. The form helps the restaurant owners understand what they are doing right and wrong, providing valuable insights to improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the rating question with a number between 1 and 5 to rate your overall experience.
2. For the other questions, select the option that best describes your experience.
3. In the Comment field, add any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to share.
4. If you'd like to provide your contact information (email or phone number), please do so in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall** (rating, number, required): Rate your overall experience with the restaurant. Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Food Quality** (select_one, required): How would you rate the quality of the food you had at the restaurant?
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Service Quality** (select_multiple, optional): How would you rate the quality of the service you received?
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Cleanliness** (select_one, optional): Was the restaurant clean and well-maintained?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Food Service** (select_multiple, optional): Was the food service attentive and responsive?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Food Value** (select_one, optional): Was the food reasonably priced for what you got?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Service Value** (select_one, optional): Was the service value reasonable considering the quality of service you received?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Time Waited** (time, optional): Approximately how long did you wait for your food or service?
* **Comment** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Recommend** (select_one, required): How likely are you to recommend this restaurant to others?
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Not likely
	+ Not at all likely
* **Email** (email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up questions, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up questions, please provide your phone number.
