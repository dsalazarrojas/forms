# Cosmetic Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cosmetic Feedback Form is a tool that helps us understand your experience with our cosmetic services. By filling out this form, you can share your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions on how we can improve our services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your feedback in the "form_title" field.
2. Rate your experience with our service by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "rating" field.
3. Share your thoughts and suggestions in the "comment" field.
4. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email address in the "contact_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: A brief description of your feedback (e.g., "Satisfied with the service" or "Disappointed with the quality").
* **Rating**: A number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Comment**: A text field where you can elaborate on your feedback and suggestions.
* **Contact Email**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, enter your email address here.
