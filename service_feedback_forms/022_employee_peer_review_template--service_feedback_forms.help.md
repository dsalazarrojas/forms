# Employee Peer Review Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate a peer review process, allowing employees to provide feedback and evaluations on each other's performance. It is designed to cover various aspects of an employee's work, such as performance areas, goals, and overall rating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the sections below and provide feedback on each section.
2. Be honest and objective in your assessment.
3. Ensure that you have thoroughly completed each section before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Peer Review** (`peer_review`, note, required: false): This section is for any general comments or observations about the employee's performance.
* **Performance Areas** (`performance_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all areas where the employee has performed well. Select multiple options as necessary.
	+ Yes: The employee has performed well in this area.
	+ No: The employee has not performed well in this area.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Describe areas where the employee has excelled and provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Goals** (`goals`, select_one, required: false): Select one goal that the employee has achieved.
	+ Yes: The employee has achieved this goal.
	+ No: The employee has not achieved this goal.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): Provide suggestions for improvement or areas of concern.
* **Performance Issues** (`performance_issues`, text, required: false): Describe any performance issues or areas for improvement.
* **Action Plans** (`action_plans`, text, required: false): Outline action plans to address performance issues or achieve goals.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required: false): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to reflect your overall assessment of the employee's performance.
	+ 1/5: Poor performance
	+ 2/5: Below average performance
	+ 3/5: Average performance
	+ 4/5: Above average performance
	+ 5/5: Excellent performance
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required: false): Sign to indicate that you have submitted this form.
* **Review Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, required: false): Sign to indicate that you have reviewed and submitted this form.

## Tips

* Be thorough and honest in your assessment.
* Ensure that you have covered all relevant sections.
* Sign and date the form to indicate that you have reviewed it.
