# prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our service providers. Your input will help us improve and provide better services to you and others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
3. Provide any comments you'd like to share about your experience.
4. Choose the reasons for your visit (relaxation, wellness, or other).
5. Select the type of session you had (massage, aromatherapy, or other).
6. Indicate how often you visit us (first time, returning customer, or unsure).
7. Choose your preferred contact method.
8. Provide your rating for the overall satisfaction of your visit.
9. Select the type of session you had (massage, aromatherapy, or other).
10. Choose the reasons for your visit (relaxation, wellness, or other).
11. Provide the duration of your visit.
12. Indicate how often you visit us (first time, returning customer, or unsure).
13. Select the type of visit (massage, aromatherapy, or other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, but this is not required.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, but this is not required.
* **Rating** (`rating`, text, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service. You may type in a number or a short phrase to describe your experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments about your experience.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, select_one, optional): Select the name of the therapist you visited.
* **Massage Type** (`massage_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of massage you had (S Swedish, Deep Tissue, or Shiatsu).
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, optional): Provide the length of your session.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
* **Would you recommend?** (`recommend`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you would recommend the service.
* **Visit Reason** (`visit_reason`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the reasons for your visit (relaxation, wellness, or other).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_one, optional): Rate your feedback (good, fair, or needs improvement).
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you visit us (first time, returning customer, or unsure).
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Provide your preferred contact method.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, optional): Provide the duration of your visit.
* **Visit Frequency 2** (`visit_frequency_2`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you visit us (first time, returning customer, or unsure).
* **Visit Type** (`visit_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of visit (massage, aromatherapy, or other).
* **Visit Frequency 3** (`visit_frequency_3`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you visit us (first time, returning customer, or unsure).
* **Overall Satisfaction 2** (`overall_satisfaction_2`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
* **Session Type 2** (`session_type_2`, select_one, optional): Select the type of session you had (aromatherapy or massage).
* **Visit Reason 2** (`visit_reason_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the reasons for your visit (relaxation, wellness, or other).
* **Visit Length** (`visit_length`, number, optional): Provide the length of your visit.
* **Visit Reason 3** (`visit_reason_3`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the reasons for your visit (relaxation, wellness, or other).
