# Marine Refit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about their experiences with the Marine Refit service. Your input helps the company improve their service and make it more satisfactory for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields (marked as `*` below) and provide any additional information you think is relevant to your experience with Marine Refit. You don't need to fill out all fields if you don't have something to say about a particular area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, *): Please enter the name of the team that interacted with you about the Marine Refit service.
* **Project Id** (`project_id`, `number`, *): Enter the ID of the project you were working on during your interaction with the Marine Refit service.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, *): Describe your overall experience with the Marine Refit service. What did you like or dislike about it?
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, ): If you have any background information about your interaction with the Marine Refit service, please provide it here. This can be helpful in understanding your feedback in context.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, `select_multiple`, ): If you have an opinion about the quality of the Marine Refit service, select High, Medium, or Low to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, `note`, ): If you have suggestions on how the Marine Refit service can be improved, provide them here.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `note`, ): If you have any additional comments or concerns that you'd like to share with the company about their service, this is the place to do it.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form, so your feedback is helpful to the company.
* You can skip any field that doesn't apply to your experience with Marine Refit.
* If you're unsure about any field, just leave it blank and move on to the next one. The company wants to hear from you, not confuse you with complicated language!
