# Laundry Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with laundry services.

## How To Complete This Form

*   **Step 1: Fill out your personal details**, including your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
*   **Step 2: Provide your **Email** for any follow-up communication.
*   **Step 3: Leave your **Feedback** about your experience with the service.
*   **Step 4: Rate your experience** by selecting from **Good**, **Average**, or **Poor**.
*   **Step 5: Select the specific **Laundry Service** aspects that met your expectations**.
*   **Step 6: Select a **Time** of day when you used the service.
*   **Step 7: Choose a **Date** when you used the service.
*   **Step 8: Rate the quality of the service at that specific **Time Of Day**.
*   **Step 9: Choose the specific **Laundry Day** you used the service.
*   **Step 10: Enter your goals for using the service in the **Goals** field.
*   **Step 11: Optionally, add any additional **Comments** about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
*   **Email**: This is your contact email address.
*   **Feedback**: Share your experience with the service. Please be specific about what went well or what didn't.
*   **Rating**: Select how you rate your experience. Good, Average, or Poor.
*   **Laundry Service**: Please select the aspects that met your expectations. You can choose one or multiple options.
*   **Time**: Select a time when you used the service.
*   **Date**: Select the date when you used the service.
*   **Time Of Day**: Select the specific time of day when you used the service.
*   **Laundry Day**: Choose the specific day you used the service.
*   **Goals**: Please enter your goals for using the service.
*   **Comments**: This field is optional, where you can add any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips

*   Please be as specific as possible in your feedback and ratings.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete.
*   Use the **Laundry Service** field to provide detailed feedback on specific aspects of the service.
*   If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at **form@gic.mx**.
