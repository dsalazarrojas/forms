# Aftersales Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback about your experience with our aftersales service. Your feedback will help us understand your experience and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill in your contact information in sections 1-4 (customer name, email, phone number, and purchase date) and ensure that you enter your full name and a valid email address.
2. Select the type of service you received in section 8.
3. Rate your experience in sections 10-12 (service quality, staff professionalism, and response time).
4. Answer questions about your experience in sections 13-14 (service resolution satisfaction, communication effectiveness).
5. Indicate how likely you are to recommend our aftersales service in section 15.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback in sections 16-17 (positive aspects, areas for improvement, and additional comments).
7. Let us know if you are interested in future services or updates in section 19.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (`customer_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used for follow-up if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact if needed.
* **Date of Purchase** (`purchase_date`, date, required): When did you make your purchase?
* **Product or Service Purchased** (`product_service_purchased`, text, required): What product or service did you purchase?
* **-- Service Interaction Details --** (`service_interaction_header`, note, required): This is a header for the following fields, please skip it.
* **Date Service Was Received** (`service_received_date`, date, required): When did you receive the aftersales service?
* **Type of Aftersales Service** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service you received. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Installation
	+ Repair
	+ Maintenance
	+ Technical Support
	+ Training
	+ Warranty Service
	+ Returns/Exchanges
	+ Other
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text, required): Who provided the service?
* **Overall Service Quality Rating** (`service_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall quality of the service. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Staff Professionalism Rating** (`staff_professionalism_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the professionalism of the staff. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Response Time Rating** (`response_time_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the response time. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Service Resolution Satisfaction** (`service_resolution_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Were you satisfied with how your issue was resolved? Please choose from the following options:
	+ Completely Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Completely Dissatisfied
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the communication during the service. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Likelihood to Recommend Service** (`service_recommendation_likelihood`, select_one, required): How likely are you to recommend our aftersales service to others? Please choose from the following options:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very Unlikely
* **Positive Aspects** (`positive_aspects`, text, optional): What did you like most about the service?
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): What could we improve in our service?
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other feedback or comments about the service
* **Interest in Future Services** (`future_service_interest`, select_one, optional): Would you be interested in our future services or updates? Please choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Maybe
