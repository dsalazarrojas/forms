# Pet Sitter Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience with a pet sitter. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and provide your honest feedback. Your input will help us improve our pet sitting services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each field with the requested information.
2. For rating fields, select one of the provided options.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Feedback" field.
4. Provide your contact phone number for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Details (Visit Details)** (`visit_details`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the visit, including the date and any relevant details.
* **Rating (rating_score)** (`rating_score`, number, required: false): Rate the overall service experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback (feedback)** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts and feedback on your experience with the pet sitter.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Rating 2 Service Quality (Rating 2 Service Quality)** (`rating_service_quality`, select_one, required: false): Rate the service quality of the pet sitter.
* **Rating 2 Service Attentiveness (Rating 2 Service Attentiveness)** (`rating_service_attentiveness`, select_one, required: false): Rate the attentiveness of the pet sitter.
* **Rating 3 Service Quality (Rating 3 Service Quality)** (`service_rating_3`, select_one, required: false): Rate the overall service quality of the pet sitter.
* **Rating 3.1 Service Quality (Rating 3.1 Service Quality)** (`service_rating_3_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate specific aspects of the service experience.
* **Rating 3.1 Service Quality (Rating 3.1 Service Quality)** (`service_rating_3_1_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate specific aspects of the service experience.
* **Rating 1 Service Quality (Rating 1 Service Quality)** (`rating_service_quality_1`, select_one, required: false): Rate the overall service quality of the pet sitter.

Note: If you are unsure about the answer to any question, please don't hesitate to provide a brief explanation in the "Feedback" field.
