# workplace_housekeeping_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the state of the workplace, including cleanliness standards, frequency of checks, and other relevant factors. This information is crucial for maintaining a clean and safe work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on the actual state of the workplace.
3. Make sure to enter the correct date and time for the checkup and next checkup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workplace Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the workplace being checked.
* **Frequency 1** (Select multiple, Required): Select 'Yes' if the specific frequency is applicable to the workplace.
* **Frequency 2** (Select multiple, Required): Select 'Yes' if the specific frequency is applicable to the workplace.
* **Last Checkup** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the last checkup.
* **Next Checkup** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the next checkup.
* **Cleanliness Standards** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments on the cleanliness standards of the workplace.
* **Frequency 3** (Select multiple, Required): Select 'Yes' if the specific frequency is applicable to the workplace.
* **Next Checkup Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the next checkup, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool** (Select One, Optional): Select 'Yes' if a specific tool is assigned to the user.
* **User Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **User Position** (Text, Optional): Enter the position of the person completing the form, if applicable.
* **Date Checked** (Date, Required): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Checked By** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who checked the workplace.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the workplace.
* **Completed** (Select One, Required): Select 'Yes' if the form is completed accurately.

* **Checked At** (Time, Optional): Enter the time the form was completed, if applicable.

* **User Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person completing the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly.
