# Fitness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our fitness services. If you have had a positive or negative experience, a suggestion, or a concern, we would like to hear about it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant "fitness service" field to specify the type of service you are providing feedback on.
2. Enter the date you received the service.
3. Write a few words about your experience in the "comments" field.
4. Enter your email address, so we can contact you if needed.
5. Provide your contact number, so we can reach out to you for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **fitness service** (`fitness_service`, text, Optional): Select the type of fitness service you are providing feedback on.
* **service date** (`service_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date you received the service.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): Write a few words about your experience with the service.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address, so we can contact you if needed.
* **contact number** (`contact_number`, text, Optional): Provide your contact number, so we can reach out to you for follow-up.
