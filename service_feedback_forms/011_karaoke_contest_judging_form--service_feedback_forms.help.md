# Karaoke Contest Judging Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Karaoke Contest Judging Form is designed to collect the scores and feedback from multiple rounds of a karaoke contest. This form helps the contest judges to evaluate and compare the performances of all participants across different rounds and declare the winner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Contestant Details` page with the necessary information about the contestant.
2. Proceed to the `Round 1 Scores` page and enter the scores for round 1.
3. Continue to the `Round 2 Scores` page and enter the scores for round 2.
4. Next, enter the scores for `Round 3 Scores`.
5. Then, enter the scores for `Round 4 Scores`.
6. On the final page, provide the scores for `Overall Score 4` and `Overall Score 5`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contestant Details (contestant_details)**:
  * Type: Text input field
  * Purpose: Enter the details about the contestant, such as name, performance name, or other relevant information.
  * Required: Not required, but highly recommended.
* **Round 1 Scores (round_1_scores)**:
  * Type: Text input field
  * Purpose: Enter the scores for round 1, typically on a scale of 1-10 or 1-100.
  * Required: Not required, but recommended for accurate assessment.
* **Round 2 Scores (round_2_scores)**:
  * Type: Text input field
  * Purpose: Enter the scores for round 2, typically on a scale of 1-10 or 1-100.
  * Required: Not required, but recommended for accurate assessment.
* **Round 3 Scores (round_3_scores)**:
  * Type: Text input field
  * Purpose: Enter the scores for round 3, typically on a scale of 1-10 or 1-100.
  * Required: Not required, but recommended for accurate assessment.
* **Round 4 Scores (round_4_scores)**:
  * Type: Text input field
  * Purpose: Enter the scores for round 4, typically on a scale of 1-10 or 1-100.
  * Required: Not required, but recommended for accurate assessment.
* **Winner (winner)**:
  * Type: Text input field
  * Purpose: Identify the winner of the contest.
  * Required: Not required, but recommended for accuracy.
* **Winner Contact Info (winner_contact_info)**:
  * Type: Text input field
  * Purpose: Enter the contact information for the winner, such as email or phone number.
  * Required: Not required, but recommended for effective communication.
* **Overall Score 4 (overall_score_4)**:
  * Type: Number input field
  * Purpose: Enter the overall score for round 4.
  * Required: Not required, but recommended for accurate assessment.
* **Overall Score 4 (2) (overall_score_4_2)**:
  * Type: Number input field
  * Purpose: Enter the alternative overall score for round 4 (if needed).
  * Required: Not required, but recommended for accurate assessment.
* **Overall Score 5 (overall_score_5)**:
  * Type: Number input field
  * Purpose: Enter the overall score for the final round.
  * Required: Not required, but recommended for accurate assessment.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and consistent scores across all rounds for each contestant.
* Use a standard score scale (e.g., 1-10 or 1-100) for each round.
* If a round is not applicable, leave the field blank or enter "N/A" (Not Applicable).
* For overall scores, ensure that the scores are consistent with the scores entered for each round.
