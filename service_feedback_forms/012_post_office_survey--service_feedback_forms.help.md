# Post Office Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the post office services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
- Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
- For optional fields, you can choose to leave blank if you don't have any information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Post Office Survey**: This is the title of the form. Please fill in this text box with any information you'd like to provide about your experience with the post office.
* **Contact Information**: This field is for any additional contact information you'd like to provide. It's optional, so you can leave it blank if you don't have any contact information to share.
* **Email**: This is an optional field for your email address. Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by the post office.
* **Phone Number**: This is an optional field for your phone number. Please enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by the post office.
* **Service Rating**: This field is for you to rate your overall experience with the post office service. Please select a number from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Staff Helpfulness**: This field is for you to rate how helpful the post office staff were during your visit. Please select a number from 1-5, where 1 is not helpful and 5 is very helpful.
* **Post Office Hours**: This field is for you to report the hours you visited the post office. Please enter the date and time you visited the post office.
* **Visit Frequency**: This is a required field for you to select how often you visit the post office. Please choose from the options: Post Office, Post Office 2.
* **Staff Helpfulness Agreement**: This is a required field for you to agree or disagree with the statement "The post office staff were helpful during my visit". Please select one of the options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree.
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for any additional comments you'd like to make about your experience with the post office. Please feel free to share any thoughts or suggestions you have.
