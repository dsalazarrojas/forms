# Community Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your community service experience and opinions on various aspects of volunteering.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your contact information, including your first name, last name, and email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up).
2. Select your age group from the provided options.
3. Indicate whether you are currently volunteering, your past volunteering experience, or if you have never volunteered.
4. Choose all types of community service you have participated in.
5. Select how often you volunteer.
6. Choose how many hours you dedicate to volunteering per month.
7. Rate your motivation for volunteering.
8. Provide your overall satisfaction with volunteering and the impact you feel you make.
9. Evaluate how well organizations support their volunteers.
10. Identify any barriers to volunteering.
11. Assess whether you feel your skills are utilized during volunteering.
12. Rate the adequacy of the training you received.
13. Indicate your intention to continue volunteering.
14. Recommend volunteering to others.
15. Suggest what would encourage you to volunteer more.
16. Rate the community impact of volunteering.
17. Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up).
* **ZIP Code** (ZIP Code, text, required): Enter your zip code where you live.
* **Age Group** (Age Group, select_one, optional): Select your age group from the provided options.
* **Are You Currently Volunteering** (Volunteer Status, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are currently volunteering, have recently volunteered, or have never volunteered.
* **Types of Service You Have Done** (Service Types, select_multiple, required): Choose all types of community service you have participated in.
* **How Often Do You Volunteer** (Frequency, select_one, required): Select how often you volunteer.
* **Average Hours Per Month** (Hours Per Month, select_one, required): Choose how many hours you dedicate to volunteering per month.
* **What Motivates You to Volunteer** (Motivation, select_multiple, optional): Select all that motivate you to volunteer.
* **Satisfaction Ratings** (Satisfaction Ratings, note, optional): Rate your satisfaction with your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, select_one, optional): Evaluate your overall satisfaction with volunteering.
* **How Much Impact Do You Feel You Make** (Impact, select_one, optional): Rate the impact you feel you make through your volunteering.
* **How Well Do Organizations Support Volunteers** (Organization Support, select_one, optional): Evaluate how well organizations support their volunteers.
* **Barriers to Volunteering** (Barriers, select_multiple, optional): Select any barriers to volunteering you face.
* **Do You Feel Your Skills Are Well Utilized** (Skills Contribution, select_one, optional): Assess whether you feel your skills are utilized during volunteering.
* **Did You Receive Adequate Training** (Training, select_one, optional): Rate the adequacy of the training you received.
* **Do You Plan to Continue Volunteering** (Continue Volunteering, select_one, required): Indicate your intention to continue volunteering.
* **Would You Recommend Volunteering** (Recommend, select_one, required): Recommend volunteering to others.
* **What Would Help You Volunteer More** (Increase Volunteering, select_multiple, optional): Suggest what would encourage you to volunteer more.
* **How Would You Rate Community Impact of Volunteering** (Community Impact, select_one, required): Rate the community impact of volunteering.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by individuals who have volunteered in their community at some point in their lives.
