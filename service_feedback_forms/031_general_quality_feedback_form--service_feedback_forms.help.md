# General Quality Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general quality feedback on your service experience. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions and provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer it to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select the corresponding option for "Contactable" if you were able to contact someone from our staff during your visit.
3. For the remaining fields, provide a brief description or explanation in the text box.
4. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall** (<overall_rating>): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service. Please choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Service** (<service_quality>): Describe your experience with the service quality. What did we do well? What could we improve on?
* **Taste** (<taste>): Share your feedback on the taste of our food and drinks.
* **Appearance** (<appearance>): Comment on the appearance of our premises and equipment.
* **Customer Service** (<customer_service>): Tell us about your interaction with our staff, including their friendliness, attentiveness, and helpfulness.
* **Staff Courtesy** (<staff_courtesy>): Describe how courteous our staff was during your visit.
* **Contactable** (<contactable>): Check "Yes" if you were able to contact someone from our staff during your visit.
* **Contact Info** (<contact_info>): Provide any additional contact information, such as your phone number or email, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Comments** (<comments>): Share any general comments or suggestions you may have about your visit.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions to ensure we get a complete picture of your experience.
* If you have trouble with a particular question, feel free to ask our staff for help.
* If you've already submitted feedback, you can edit and resubmit to update your existing feedback.
