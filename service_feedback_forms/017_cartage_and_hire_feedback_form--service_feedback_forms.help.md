# Cartage And Hire Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our cartage and hire services. Your input will help us identify areas where we excel and where we may need to improve, allowing us to make informed changes to enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review each question carefully and select the response that best describes your experience.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as you can about your experience.
3. If you have any issues or problems that you would like to report, please describe them in the "Issues Encountered" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cartage and Hire Service Feedback** (`feedback_header`): This field is a note that provides a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Your Name** (`customer_name`): This is an optional field where you can provide your name for our records.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`): This field is optional and is used for communication purposes only, in case we need to contact you for more information.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`): This field is optional and is used for our team to contact you if needed.
* **Date of Service** (`service_date`): Please select the date when you received our service.
* **Type of Service Used** (`service_type`): Select the category of service you received. If none of the options match your experience, you can select "Other" and provide more details.
* **Specific Service Category** (`service_category`): Select the specific service category related to your experience. If none of the options match, you can select "Other" and provide more details.
* **Booking Experience Rating** (`booking_experience`): Rate your experience with booking our services.
* **How Did You Book the Service** (`booking_method`): Select how you booked our service.
* **How Easy Was the Booking Process** (`booking_ease`): Rate the ease of use of our booking process.
* **Staff Interaction Rating** (`staff_interaction`): Rate the quality of interaction you had with our staff.
* **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`): Rate the professionalism of our staff.
* **Punctuality Rating** (`punctuality`): Rate our punctuality and on-time performance.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`): Rate the condition of the equipment or vehicle provided.
* **Was the Equipment Suitable for Your Needs** (`equipment_suitability`): Rate the suitability of the equipment or vehicle for your needs.
* **Overall Service Quality** (`service_quality`): Rate the overall quality of our service delivery.
* **Care in Handling Goods or Equipment** (`handling_care`): Rate the quality of handling provided.
* **Communication** (`communication`): Rate our communication and updates provided during the service.
* **Pricing Fairness** (`pricing`): Rate the fairness of our pricing.
* **Pricing Transparency** (`pricing_transparency`): Rate our clarity of charges.
* **Safety Measures Observed** (`safety_measures`): Rate our safety protocols.
* **Did You Experience Any Issues** (`issues_encountered`): Select if you experienced any issues during the service.
* **Describe Any Issues** (`issue_description`): If you experienced any issues, please describe them in detail.
* **How Well Were Issues Resolved** (`resolution_satisfaction`): Rate how well your issues were resolved.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service.
* **How Likely Are You to Use Our Services Again** (`likelihood_return`): Rate how likely you are to use our services again.
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Us** (`recommend_to_others`): Rate how likely you are to recommend us to others.
* **What Was the Best Aspect of the Service** (`best_aspect`): If you were satisfied with any part of our service, please highlight it.
* **What Areas Need Improvement** (`improvement_areas`): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please describe them.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): If you have any other feedback, please provide it.
