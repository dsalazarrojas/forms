# Bike Auto Services Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a bike's maintenance and repair needs. The information gathered will help us assess the situation and provide you with the necessary services to get your bike back on the road safely and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below, and we'll be in touch shortly with our recommendations and availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the bike's owner or responsible party.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Bike Make and Model** (`bike_make_model`, text, required): Specify the type and model of your bike, e.g., "Trek Domane" or "Giant Escape".
* **Type of Bike** (`bike_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of bike you have, such as "Road Bike" or "Electric Bike".
* **What is the Primary Issue with Your Bike?** (`primary_issue`, text, required): Describe the main problem you're experiencing with your bike.
* **Services Requested** (`services_requested`, select_multiple, required): Select all services that apply to the issue, e.g., "Brake Adjustment" and "Tire/Tube Replacement".
* **Is the Bike Making Any Unusual Noises?** (`noise_description`, text, required): If yes, describe the sound and where it comes from.
* **When Was the Bike Last Serviced?** (`last_service_date`, date, required): Enter the approximate date of the last service.
* **How Urgent is This Repair?** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Choose how quickly you need the repair, e.g., "Not Urgent - within a week" or "Urgent - ASAP".
* **Estimated Budget for Repairs** (`budget_estimate`, number, optional): Enter your estimated budget for the repairs in your local currency (if applicable).
* **Preferred Drop-off Date** (`drop_off_date`, date, required): Select the date you'd like to drop off your bike.
* **Additional Comments or Instructions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional information or instructions for our team.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Our team will review your submission and contact you soon to discuss the next steps.
