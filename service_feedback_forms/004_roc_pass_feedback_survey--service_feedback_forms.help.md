# Prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Prophets" form is designed to collect feedback and service satisfaction ratings from customers. It aims to gather information about the quality of service provided and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully.
2. For each field, fill in the information as instructed:
	* Satisfaction scores should be numerical ratings from 1 to 5.
	* Feedback text fields should be filled with detailed comments or suggestions.
	* Service usage and improvement areas selection fields should be filled with specific information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Satisfaction** (Field 1, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **ROCK Pass Feedback** (Field 2, text, required: false): Describe your experience with the service. What did you like or dislike about it?
* **Service Usage** (Field 3, text, required: false): Share how you used the service, e.g., which features or services you used.
* **Improvement Areas** (Field 4, select multiple, required: false): Select all areas where you'd like to see improvement:
	+ Improvement in Service Quality
	+ Improvement in Response Time
	+ Improvement in Communication
	+ Improvement in Overall Satisfaction
* **ROCK Pass Service Score** (Field 5, number, required: false): Rate your service experience with a numerical score.
* **Improvement Opportunities** (Field 6, text, required: false): Suggest specific areas where the service can improve.
* **Suggestions** (Field 7, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **ROCK Pass Service Score 2** (Field 8, number, required: false): Another numerical score for your service experience.
* **Comments** (Field 9, text, required: false): Share any further thoughts about your experience.
* **ROCK Pass Service Score 3** (Field 10, number, required: false): A third numerical score for your service experience.
* **Satisfaction Score 4** (Field 11, number, required: false): Another satisfaction score.
* **Improvement Opportunities 2** (Field 12, text, required: false): Additional opportunities for improvement.
* **Satisfaction Score 5** (Field 13, number, required: false): A final satisfaction score.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your feedback.
* Select all applicable improvement areas that apply to your experience.
* Use numerical scores to rate your satisfaction.
* Provide specific examples or suggestions for improvement.
