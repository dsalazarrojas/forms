# Service Quality Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our service. It helps us understand areas where we excel and where we need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of feedback you are providing (Service Quality, Service Delivery, or Customer Experience).
2. Rate the service quality on a scale of 1-10.
3. Describe any pain points that we have missed.
4. Provide any suggestions you may have for us.
5. Indicate how you were contacted (phone, email, in-person, etc.).
6. Specify through which channel you contacted us (phone, email, social media, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, **Required**):
	* This field is used to indicate the type of feedback you are providing. Please select one of the options: Service Quality, Service Delivery, or Customer Experience.
- **Service Quality Level** (`service_quality_level`, number, **Required**):
	* This field is used to rate the service quality on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
- **Pain Points** (`pain_points`, note, **Required**):
	* This field is used to describe any pain points that we have missed. Please provide detailed descriptions of any issues you experienced.
- **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, note, **Optional**):
	* This field is used to provide any suggestions you may have for us to improve our service.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_multiple, **Required**):
	* This field is used to indicate how you were contacted by us. Please select all that apply from the options: Phone, Email, In-person.
- **Contact Channel** (`contact_channel`, select_multiple, **Required**):
	* This field is used to specify through which channel you contacted us. Please select all that apply from the options: Phone, Email, Social Media, In-person.
