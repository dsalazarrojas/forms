# Travel Agency Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the travel agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide feedback about your stay, rating our services, and rate your experience.
3. Choose the options that best describe your stay and preferences.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to include them in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter your contact information (phone number and address) and how you heard about us.
6. Finally, answer any optional questions, such as "Will visit again?" and "Visit duration".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name, as we want to know who is providing feedback.
* **E-Mail** (`contact_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address, so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **How was your stay?** (`feedback_message`, Note, Optional): Share your experience with us, what did we do well or poorly?.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Select One, Optional): Choose a rating between 1-5 stars to rate our services.
* **Rate our service** (`rating_score`, Number, Optional): Enter a numerical rating between 1-10 to rate our services.
* **Rating comment** (`rating_comment`, Note, Optional): Provide more detailed feedback about your rating.
* **How was your stay?** (`how_was_it`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options that best describe your stay.
* **What was it?** (`what_was_it`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options that best describe your experience.
* **What did it?** (`what_did_it`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options that best describe your experience.
* **What would improve?** (`what_would_improve`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options that could improve our services.
* **What didn't improve?** (`what_didnt_improve`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options that didn't meet your expectations.
* **Will visit again?** (`will_visit_again`, Select One, Required): Answer this question with either "True" or "False", indicating if you would visit us again.
* **How well hosted?** (`how_well_hosted`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options that best describe our hosting services.
* **Any comments?** (`comments`, Note, Optional): Share any additional thoughts or feedback about your stay.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address, so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number, so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Optional): Enter your address, so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **City** (`city`, Text, Optional): Enter your city, so we can better understand your location.
* **Country** (`country`, Text, Optional): Enter your country, so we can better understand your location.
* **Zip** (`zip`, Text, Optional): Enter your zip code, so we can better understand your location.
* **How did you hear?** (`how_did_you_hear`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options that best describe how you heard about us.
* **How did you find us?** (`how_did_you_find_us`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options that best describe how you found our agency.
* **Why didn't you visit?** (`why_didnt_visit`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options that best describe why you didn't visit our agency.
* **How many visited?** (`how_many_visited`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of people who visited our agency.
* **Visit duration** (`visit_duration`, Time, Optional): Enter the duration of your visit, in the format "HH:MM:SS".
