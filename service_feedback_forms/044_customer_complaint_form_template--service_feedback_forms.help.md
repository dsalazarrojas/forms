# Customer Complaint Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Complaint Form Template is designed to collect and track customer complaints and feedback. This form will help us understand the issue, gather information, and address the customer's concerns in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your complaint.
2. Provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates on your issue.
3. Describe the incident or problem you are experiencing in the "Describe your issue" field.
4. If possible, attach any relevant files or documents to support your complaint.
5. Add any additional comments or information you think will help us understand your issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Complaint Form
This field is where you can enter a brief description of your complaint.

* **Customer Complaint Form** (`customer_complaint_form_template`, text, required): Enter a concise summary of your complaint. Please keep it brief, but make sure to include all relevant details.

* **How can we assist you?** (`customer_complaint`, text, required): Describe the issue you are experiencing and how we can help.

* **Describe your issue** (`customer_complaint_description`, text, required): Provide detailed information about the incident or problem you are experiencing. This will help us understand the root cause of your complaint.

* **Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can address you properly.

* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or updates.

* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for further assistance.

* **Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter your address so we can keep in touch with you.

* **Date of incident** (`customer_complaint_date`, date, optional): If you know the date of the incident, please enter it here. This will help us track the timeline of your complaint.

* **Time of incident** (`customer_complaint_time`, time, optional): If you know the time of the incident, please enter it here. This will help us track the timeline of your complaint.

* **Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, required): Please provide any feedback or comments you think will help us improve our service.

* **Attachments** (`customer_attachment`, text, optional): If you have any files or documents that support your complaint, please attach them here.

* **Additional comments** (`customer_additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any further information or comments about your complaint, please enter them here.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and concise as possible when filling out this form.
- Attach any relevant files or documents to support your complaint.
- If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
