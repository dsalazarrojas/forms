## NDT Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NDT Service Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about the Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) service provided. This form helps to gather valuable information to improve the service and ensure it meets customer needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the description of the service provided on page 1.
2. Enter the job number related to the service on page 2.
3. Select the date of inspection on page 3.
4. Enter the name of the inspector on page 4.
5. Evaluate the condition of the equipment on page 5.
6. Select the areas inspected during the service on page 6.
7. Provide any remarks or comments on page 7.
8. Rate the quality of the service on page 8.
9. Evaluate your overall satisfaction on page 9.
10. Mention any areas for improvement on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non-Destructive Testing Service** (`non_destructive_testing_service`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can enter a brief description of the service provided.
* **Job Number** (`job_number`, `number`, required: false): This field is where you enter the job number related to the service provided.
* **Date Of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, `date`, required: false): This field is where you select the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you enter the name of the inspector who performed the service.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is where you evaluate the condition of the equipment used during the inspection. You can select from the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent.
* **Inspection Area** (`inspection_area`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is where you select the areas inspected during the service. You can choose from the following options: Area 1, Area 2, Area 3, Area 4, Area 5, Area 6.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is where you enter any remarks or comments about the service. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Service Quality Rating** (`service_quality_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is where you rate the quality of the service provided. Please enter a number between 1 and 5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is where you evaluate your overall satisfaction with the service. You can choose from the following options: Very Unsatisfactory, Unsatisfactory, Satisfactory, Good, Excellent.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you mention any areas that require improvement.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is where you select the service quality aspects that you found during the inspection. You can choose from the following options: Cleanliness, Communication, Efficiency, Friendliness, Other.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all fields to provide detailed feedback.
* Please choose the correct options for fields with multiple options.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the person who distributed the form for guidance.
