# Mobile Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their service experience, helping us improve the quality and meet their needs better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the category that best describes the service you're providing feedback on: Mobile App, Website, or Service.
2.  Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
3.  Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.
4.  Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
5.  Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
6.  Click submit to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Category**: The category you're providing feedback on. Choose from Mobile App, Website, or Service.
    *   This field helps us understand which aspect of our service you're giving feedback on.
*   **Customer Rating**: Rate your overall experience with our service on a scale of 1-5.
    *   This will help us understand how satisfied you are with our service.
*   **Comment**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.
    *   This is an opportunity for you to share your thoughts on how we can improve our service.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
    *   This is the best way for us to contact you if we need more information or have questions.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
    *   This is an optional field, but it helps us get in touch with you more easily.
*   **Submit**: Click this button to send your feedback to us.
    *   This is the final step to send us your feedback and help us improve our service.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
*   We appreciate your thoughts and suggestions.
*   We'll use your feedback to improve our service and make it better for everyone.
