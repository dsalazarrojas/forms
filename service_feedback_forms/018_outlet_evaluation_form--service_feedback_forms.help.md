# Outlet Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outlet Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback, opinions, or information about an outlet's performance, products, or services. It helps us understand your experience and provides valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your honest and specific feedback about the outlet.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by asterisk \*).
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. If you have no feedback or information to share, please do not submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Outlet Name** (`outlet_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the outlet you are evaluating.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the outlet, such as street address or city.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Share any contact information about the person evaluating the outlet.
* **Product Lines** (`product_lines`, `text`, required: false): Describe the types of products or services offered by the outlet.
* **Product Types** (`product_types`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the types of products or services you are evaluating, such as "Men", "Women", "Children", or "Other".
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you visited or evaluated the outlet.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you visited or evaluated the outlet.
* **Overall Evaluation** (`overall_evaluation`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the outlet on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the outlet.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person evaluating the outlet.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the company or organization the contact person represents.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title or position of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter any tools or systems assigned to you for this evaluation.

Note: The form can be completed by anyone with relevant feedback or information to share. Please do not submit the form if you have no feedback or information to provide.
