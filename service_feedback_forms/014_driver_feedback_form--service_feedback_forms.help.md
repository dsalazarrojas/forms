# Driver Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Feedback Form is a tool for customers to provide feedback on their recent ride experience. This form helps our team understand their satisfaction level and any areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Customer Id, which is your unique identifier.
2. Choose a rating for your overall experience.
3. Provide a brief feedback on your experience.
4. Enter the name of the driver who provided your ride.
5. Rate the driver's performance (if you feel comfortable doing so).
6. Enter the vehicle number (if available).
7. Finally, leave a message with your feedback on the experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Id (1)**: Please enter your unique Customer Id, which can be found on the receipt or your account information.
* **Rating (2)**: Choose a rating for your experience, where:
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Feedback (3)**: Provide a brief message with your thoughts about the experience. This will help us understand your satisfaction level.
* **Driver Name (4)**: Enter the name of the driver who provided your ride.
* **Driver Rating (5)**: If you're comfortable doing so, rate the driver's performance using this field.
* **Vehicle Number (6)**: If you know the vehicle number, please enter it. This will help us to identify the vehicle involved.
* **Feedback Message (7)**: This is the final opportunity to share any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
