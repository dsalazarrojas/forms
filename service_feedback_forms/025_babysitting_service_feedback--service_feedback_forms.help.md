# Babysitting Service Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Babysitting Service Feedback form is designed to allow parents or guardians to provide their feedback on the babysitting service they have utilized. This feedback is essential for us to understand their experience, identify areas of improvement, and provide quality services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Parent or Guardian Name
	* Email Address
	* Babysitter Name
	* Date of Service
	* Duration of Care
	* Number of Children
	* Overall Satisfaction
	* Punctuality
	* Professionalism
	* Interaction with Children
	* Safety and Supervision
	* Communication
	* Followed Parent Instructions
	* Would You Hire Again
	* Would You Recommend
3. Fill out the optional sections if you have additional comments, best qualities, and areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Babysitter Name** (4, text, required): Enter the name of the babysitter who provided care.
* **Date of Service** (5, date, required): Enter the date when the care was provided.
* **Duration of Care** (6, select_one, required):
	+ Less than 2 hours
	+ 2 to 4 hours
	+ 4 to 6 hours
	+ 6 to 8 hours
	+ Over 8 hours
	+ Overnight
* **Number of Children** (7, number, required): Enter the number of children that were cared for.
* **Ages of Children** (8, text, required): List the ages of all the children.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (9, select_one, required):
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Punctuality** (10, select_one, required):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Professionalism** (11, select_one, required):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Interaction with Children** (12, select_one, required):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Safety and Supervision** (13, select_one, required):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Communication** (14, select_one, required):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Followed Parent Instructions** (15, select_one, required):
	+ Yes, completely
	+ Mostly
	+ Somewhat
	+ No, did not follow
* **Would You Hire Again** (16, select_one, required):
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Would You Recommend** (17, select_one, required):
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Best Qualities** (18, text, optional): Describe what you appreciated most about the service.
* **Areas for Improvement** (19, text, optional): Suggest areas for improvement.
* **Incident Report** (20, text, optional): Report any incidents or issues that occurred during care.
* **Additional Comments** (21, text, optional): Provide any other feedback or comments.
* **Date of Feedback** (22, date, required): Enter today's date.

Please fill out this form with accuracy and honesty to provide valuable feedback that will help us improve our services.
