# Background Check Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from individuals who have used the background check service. Your input is crucial in helping us improve and refine the service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your contact information (phone number and company name, if applicable).
3. Select the date you used the service.
4. Choose the type of background check you received.
5. Rate your experience with the service, including satisfaction, ease of ordering, turnaround time, report accuracy, and customer service.
6. Provide feedback on the price value, report clarity, and technology platform.
7. Let us know if you would use the service again and if you would recommend it to others.
8. If you wish, add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
9. Finally, select the date you completed the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if you wish to provide this information).
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **Date of Service** (`service_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you used the background check service.
* **Type of Background Check** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of background check you received (e.g., employment verification, education verification, etc.).
* **Number of Checks Ordered** (`number_of_checks`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate number of checks ordered.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Ease of Ordering** (`ease_of_ordering`, `select_one`, required): Rate the ease of ordering the service (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Turnaround Time** (`turnaround_time`, `select_one`, required): Rate the speed of results (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Accuracy of Reports** (`accuracy_of_reports`, `select_one`, required): Rate the accuracy of the reports (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `select_one`, required): Rate the support received from customer service (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required): Rate the price value of the service (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Report Clarity** (`report_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate how clear the reports were (Very Clear, Clear, Neutral, Unclear, Very Unclear).
* **FCRA Compliance Support** (`compliance_support`, `select_one`, required): Rate the compliance guidance provided (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Technology Platform** (`technology_platform`, `select_one`, required): Rate the online platform used (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Would Use Service Again** (`would_use_again`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you would use the service again (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Would Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you would recommend the service to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Best Features of Service** (`best_features`, `text`, optional): Enter what you liked most about the service.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, optional): Enter what you think could be improved.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any other feedback or comments.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you completed the survey.
