# Customer Service Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience with our service. Your feedback will help us identify areas where we can improve and make changes to better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Rate your satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-5.
3. Be honest and provide any additional feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you rate our service (1-5)**: Rate your experience with our service, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What did we do well**: Share with us what we did well in our service. This is your opportunity to praise us!
* **What could we do better**: Tell us about any areas where we fell short in our service. Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve.
* **How likely are you to recommend us (1-5)**: Rate your likelihood of recommending our service to others, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Do you have any additional feedback**: Share any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions you may have about your experience with our service. This is your chance to speak up! 

## Tips
* Be as honest as possible when rating and commenting.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the form.
* Don't hesitate to share any feedback, no matter how small it may seem.

Please note that your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our service for future customers. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
