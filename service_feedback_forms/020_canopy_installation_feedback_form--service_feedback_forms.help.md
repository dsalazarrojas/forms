# Canopy Installation Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the installation of a canopy. The feedback will help us improve our service and provide better experiences for our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

* Answer the questions carefully and honestly to provide us with valuable feedback.
* Fill out all required fields (fields marked with *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (4, text, *): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (5, email, *): Enter your email address.
* **Installation Date** (8, date, *): Enter the date of the installation.
* **Type of Canopy Installed** (9, select_one, *): Select the type of canopy installed (Fixed Roof Canopy, Retractable Canopy, Portable/Tent Canopy, Shade Sail, Custom Design, Other).
* **Installation Location** (10, text, ): Enter the location where the canopy was installed (e.g., backyard, deck, commercial).
* **Professionalism of Installation Crew** (12, select_one, *): Rate the professionalism of the installation crew (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Punctuality and Schedule Adherence** (13, select_one, *): Rate the punctuality and schedule adherence of the installation crew (Excellent - On time, as scheduled, Very Good - Mostly on time, Good - Generally reliable, Fair - Some delays, Poor - Significant delays).
* **Crew Cleanliness and Site Management** (14, select_one, *): Rate the cleanliness and site management of the crew (Excellent - Very clean, professional, Very Good - Clean and organized, Good - Acceptable, Fair - Some mess left behind, Poor - Very messy).
* **Overall Quality of Installation** (16, select_one, *): Rate the overall quality of the installation (Excellent - Exceeds expectations, Very Good - High quality work, Good - Satisfactory, Fair - Some issues, Poor - Significant problems).
* **Structural Integrity and Safety** (17, select_one, *): Rate the structural integrity and safety of the installation (Excellent - Feels very secure, Very Good - Sturdy and safe, Good - Adequate, Fair - Some concerns, Poor - Significant safety concerns).
* **Finish and Appearance Quality** (18, select_one, *): Rate the finish and appearance quality of the installation (Excellent - Perfect finish, Very Good - High quality appearance, Good - Acceptable appearance, Fair - Minor issues, Poor - Significant appearance problems).
* **Pre-Installation Communication and Planning** (20, select_one, *): Rate the pre-installation communication and planning (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication During Installation** (21, select_one, *): Rate the communication during installation (Excellent - Frequent updates, Very Good - Regular communication, Good - Adequate communication, Fair - Minimal communication, Poor - No communication).
* **Clarity of Instructions and Warranties** (22, select_one, *): Rate the clarity of instructions and warranties (Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear, Very Unclear).
* **Were costs what was quoted?** (24, select_one, *): Select how accurate the costs were (Yes, exact match, Yes, very close, Somewhat accurate, No, significantly higher).
* **Value for Money** (25, select_one, *): Rate the value for money (Excellent - Great value, Very Good - Good value, Good - Acceptable value, Fair - Somewhat overpriced, Poor - Poor value).
* **Were there any issues during installation?** (27, select_one, ): Select if there were any issues (No issues, Minor issues - quickly resolved, Some issues - mostly resolved, Significant issues - not fully resolved).
* **If issues occurred, how well were they handled?** (28, select_one, ): Rate the handling of issues (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Not Applicable).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (30, select_one, *): Rate your overall satisfaction (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Would you recommend us to others?** (31, select_one, *): Select if you would recommend us to others (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Unsure, Probably not, Definitely not).
* **What could we improve?** (32, text, ): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
