# Laptop Rental Service Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users who have rented a laptop from our service. It helps us understand their overall experience, including the condition of the laptop, support quality, and any other comments or feedback they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are aware of all the fields and what information is being collected.
2. If you have rented a laptop from our service, please fill out the form completely and honestly.
3. Select a rating for the overall quality of the service you experienced.
4. Indicate the condition of the laptop you received.
5. Describe your experience with the support you received from our service.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with our service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one): This field allows you to rate your overall experience with our laptop rental service. Please select "Good" if you were satisfied with the service, and "Fair" if there were some issues.
* **Laptop Condition** (`laptop_condition`, text): Describe the condition of the laptop you received. This could include comments on how it was clean, functional, or if there were any issues or defects.
* **Support Quality** (`support_quality`, text): Describe the quality of support you received from our service. This could include comments on how responsive, helpful, or courteous the support staff were.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_multiple): Indicate if you experienced effective communication from our service. Select "Yes" if you received timely and clear information, and "No" if you did not.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text): Use this space to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our service. This is your opportunity to share any other thoughts or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please describe them in the "Additional Feedback" field.
* If you have suggestions for how we can improve our service, please include them in the "Additional Feedback" field as well.
