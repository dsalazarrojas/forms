# Customer Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide feedback to service providers on areas where they can improve. It helps them understand what went wrong and where they can make changes to enhance customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, you will need to provide some information about your experience. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and do not hesitate to elaborate if you need to provide more detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your complaint about?** (`customer_complaint_form_1`): This question allows you to describe your problem or issue with a service provider. Please provide a clear and concise statement of what happened.
* **How long have you had to wait?** (`customer_complaint_form_2`): This question is about the length of time you have had to wait for a resolution or resolution attempt. If you're unsure, you can answer "Don't know" or provide an approximate time.
* **What is the estimated time of the incident?** (`customer_complaint_form_3`): Here, you need to provide the date or approximate date of the incident you're reporting.
* **What was the resolution outcome?** (`customer_complaint_form_4`): This question asks about whether the issue was resolved, and if so, whether it was satisfactory. Select "Yes" if the issue was resolved to your satisfaction, and "No" otherwise.
* **Was the solution satisfactory?** (`customer_complaint_form_5`): Similar to the previous question, this one is about whether the solution provided was satisfactory or not.
* **How satisfied were you with the solution?** (`customer_complaint_form_6`): This question uses a scale to gauge your level of satisfaction with the solution provided.
* **How satisfied were you with the service?** (`customer_complaint_form_7`): Another satisfaction question, this one about the overall service provided.
* **What was the problem with the solution?** (`customer_complaint_form_8`): This is an open question where you can provide more details about what was wrong with the solution or service.
* **Would you like to provide any additional comments?** (`customer_complaint_form_9`): If you have more information you'd like to share, this is the field for it.
* **Do you have any further complaints?** (`customer_complaint_form_10`): This question is for additional issues or complaints you may have that weren't addressed in your initial complaint.
* **What is the name of your service provider?** (`customer_complaint_form_11`): This is to identify the service provider you're reporting on.
* **What is the contact information for the service provider?** (`customer_complaint_form_12`): Finally, providing contact details can help with follow-up or further action.

# Tips
- Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
- If you have any supporting documents or evidence, consider attaching them with your submission.
- If you need help or have questions, please reach out to your service provider or contact us for support.
