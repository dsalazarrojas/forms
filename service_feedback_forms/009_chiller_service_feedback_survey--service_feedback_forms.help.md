# Chiller Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with our services. Your input will help us to identify areas of improvement and provide better service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
2. If you are unsure about a particular question, use the "Not sure" or "Neutral" options.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information** (`id: 1`, `type: note`, required: false`): This section is for general information about yourself. While optional, it helps us to get a better understanding of who you are and why your feedback is important to us.
* **Your Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false`): If you want to be identified in your feedback, please provide your name. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Company Name** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false`): If you want to provide information about your company, please do so here. This helps us to understand the context of your feedback.
* **Email Address** (`id: 4`, `type: email`, required: false`): For follow-up purposes, please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false`): If you want to be contacted by phone for follow-up purposes, please provide your phone number.
* **Service Details** (`id: 6`, `type: note`, required: false`): Describe the service you received, including date, time, and any other relevant information.
* **Date of Service** (`id: 7`, `type: date`, required: true`): Please provide the approximate date of the service.
* **Type of Service Received** (`id: 8`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Choose the type of service you received, such as "Routine maintenance", "Emergency repair", "Installation", etc.
* **Technician Name** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false`): If you remember the name of the technician, please provide it.
* **Location of Serviced Equipment** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: true`): Describe the location where the service was performed, such as a building or facility.
* **Scheduling Experience** (`id: 11`, `type: note`, required: false`): Describe your experience with scheduling the service, including any challenges you faced.
* **How easy was it to schedule the service** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate how easy it was to schedule the service.
* **Was the appointment time convenient** (`id: 13`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate how convenient the appointment time was.
* **Did the technician arrive on time** (`id: 14`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate the technician's punctuality.
* **Technician Performance** (`id: 15`, `type: note`, required: false`): Describe your experience with the technician's performance.
* **Technician Professionalism** (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate the technician's professionalism.
* **Technician Knowledge and Expertise** (`id: 17`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate the technician's knowledge and expertise.
* **Technician Courtesy and Respect** (`id: 18`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate the technician's courtesy and respect.
* **Work Area Cleanliness** (`id: 19`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate the cleanliness of the work area.
* **Service Quality** (`id: 20`, `type: note`, required: false`): Describe the overall service quality.
* **Was your problem fully resolved** (`id: 21`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate whether your problem was fully resolved.
* **Overall Service Quality Rating** (`id: 22`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate the overall service quality.
* **Value for Money** (`id: 23`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate the value for money.
* **Communication and Explanation** (`id: 24`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate how well the technician communicated with you.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`id: 25`, `type: note`, required: false`): Describe your overall satisfaction with the service.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`id: 26`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate your overall satisfaction.
* **Would you recommend our services to others** (`id: 27`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate your willingness to recommend our services.
* **Would you use our services again** (`id: 28`, `type: select_one`, required: true`): Rate your likelihood to use our services again.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`id: 29`, `type: text`, required: false`): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please provide them.
* **May we contact you for follow-up** (`id: 30`, `type: select_one`, required: false`): Choose whether you're okay with follow-up contact from us.
