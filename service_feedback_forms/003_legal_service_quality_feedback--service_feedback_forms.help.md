# Legal Service Quality Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback on the quality of legal services provided to clients. Your input will help us understand what we do well and identify areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Submit" or "Clear" button to begin. 
2. Fill in the form as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Service Quality Feedback** (`legal_service_quality_feedback`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your experience with the legal service. If you have any specific issues or concerns, provide them here.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Choose a rating for the legal service: Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent.
* **Feedback** (`legal_service_quality_feedback`, text, optional): This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback about the legal service. Please be specific about what you liked or disliked about the service.
* **Service Providers** (`service_providers`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant service providers you interacted with during the legal service: Lawyer, Paralegal, Clerk, Not applicable, or Other.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so we can get back to you regarding your feedback.

## Tips
* Please provide feedback on the most recent legal service interaction.
* If you have already submitted feedback, please do not resubmit.
* Be specific and clear when describing your experience.
* Select all relevant service providers that you interacted with.
