# Service Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Service Feedback Form is designed to collect comments, ratings, and feedback from customers about our services. This form is used to gather information that can help us improve our service quality and make informed business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into two pages. Click on the "Next" or "Previous" buttons to navigate between pages.
2. On the first page, you will be asked to rate your experience and provide a comment about our service.
3. On the second page, you can provide additional feedback and comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating** (`service_feedback_form_first_page_rating`, Number, **Required**): Please rate your experience with our service, using a number between 1 and 5 to indicate the level of satisfaction.
* **Comment** (`service_feedback_form_first_page_comment`, Text, **Optional**): Share your thoughts and comments about your experience with our service.
* **Feedback** (`service_feedback_form_first_page_feedback`, Text, **Optional**): If you have any additional feedback or comments, you can enter them here.
* **More Feedback** (`service_feedback_form_second_page_feedback`, Text, **Optional**): If you have any more information you would like to add, please enter it here.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide constructive feedback that can help us improve our service.
- If you have any concerns or issues, please describe them in detail so we can address them.
- Your feedback is greatly appreciated and helps us provide better services to our customers.
