# Makeup Pre Procedure Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-procedure questionnaire designed to gather information from clients about their makeup preferences and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's name, contact number, and email address in the first section.
2. Choose your preferred makeup type from the "Preferred Makeup Type" dropdown menu.
3. Indicate your desired makeup look by selecting one or multiple options from the "Desired Makeup Look" dropdown menu.
4. Specify the time you would like to spend on the procedure.
5. Select the services you would like to receive during the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Preferred Makeup Type** (`preferred_makeup_type`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ Label 1
	+ Label 2
	+ Label 3
* **Desired Makeup Look** (`desired_makeup_look`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple options from the following:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
* **Procedure Time** (`procedure_time`, time, required: false): Specify the time you would like to spend on the procedure.
* **Desired Services** (`desired_services`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following services:
	+ Service 1
	+ Service 2
	+ Service 3
