# Cosmetic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their cosmetic preferences, interests, and purchasing habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Choose the options that best fit your preferences.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all the options that apply.
4. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers.
5. If you need help with any question, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Please provide a brief description of yourself.
* **What do you prefer**: Select all the options that best fit your preferences for cosmetics.
* **What services do you want**: Select the services you are interested in getting from our company.
* **What interest are you interested in**: Select the option that best describes your interest in cosmetics.
* **How often do you make purchases?**: Select all the options that best fit your purchasing frequency for cosmetics.
* **How often do you make purchases 1**: This question is similar to the previous one, but with slightly different options.
* **What interest are you interested in 1**: Similar to the previous question, but with different options.
* **Customer Information 1**: Please provide a brief description of yourself.
* **Customer Information 2**: Another brief description of yourself.

Note: The form is designed to be simple and easy to use, and the fields are self-explanatory. If you need help with any question, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
