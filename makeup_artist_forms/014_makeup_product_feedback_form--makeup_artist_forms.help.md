# Makeup Product Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Makeup Product Feedback Form is a tool for customers to provide feedback on makeup products. It helps the company gather information about the customer's experience with the product, such as whether they liked or disliked it, what they thought about the product's color and type, and any other relevant comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the product by selecting either "Yes" or "No" for the "Submit" field.
2. Provide a description of your experience with the product in the "Description" field.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, you can add them in the "Feedback" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
5. Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on in the "Product Name" field (optional).
6. Enter the color of the product in the "Product Color" field (optional).
7. Enter the type of the product in the "Product Type" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit** (`form_submit`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to submit your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the product from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your experience with the product.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to contact you if we need more information.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Color** (`product_color`, text, optional): Enter the color of the product.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of product (e.g., eyeshadow, lipstick, etc.).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Add any comments or suggestions about the product.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to finalize your feedback submission.

## Tips

* Please answer all the required fields (Rating and Submit).
* You can leave any optional fields blank if you don't have any information to provide.
* Be as descriptive as possible in the "Description" and "Feedback" fields.
* Make sure to enter the correct email address in the "Email" field if you want to receive a response.
* Proofread your submission before selecting "Yes" to submit your feedback.
