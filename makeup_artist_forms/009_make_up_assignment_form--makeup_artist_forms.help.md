# make_up_assignment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "make_up_assignment_form" is a form designed to collect information about student absences. It's intended for educators to gather details from students about their absences, which can help maintain smooth communication and attendance records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Enter a brief explanation for your absence in the `Reason for Absence` field.
3. Select your preferred contact method from the `Contact Method` dropdown.
4. Add any additional comments or notes in the `Message` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a student.
* **Reason for Absence** (`Reason for Absence`, text, optional): Describe the reason you're absent from class.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted by your teacher (Contact Teacher or Contact Parent).
* **Message** (`Message`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or messages, you can enter them here.

## Tips
- Please ensure you complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your teacher.
- Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you've filled it out correctly.
