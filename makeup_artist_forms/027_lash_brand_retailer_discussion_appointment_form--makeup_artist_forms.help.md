# Lash Brand Retailer Discussion Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help sales representatives efficiently schedule and prepare for appointments with retailers in the lash brand industry. It gathers essential information to facilitate productive discussions, track progress, and follow up on sales opportunities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information for the appointment, such as the retailer's name, contact information, and meeting details.
2. Fill in the required fields, including Retailer Name, Appointment Date, Appointment Time, Location, Contact Number, Sales Rep, Retailer Email, Store Hours, and Date of Last Meeting.
3. Select the appropriate Appointment Type and Meeting Type from the provided options.
4. Indicate if Products Discussed were a part of the meeting.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retailer Name** (required): Enter the name of the retailer you are meeting with.
* **Appointment Date** (required): Enter the date of the appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Appointment Time** (required): Enter the time of the appointment in the format 12:00 AM/12:00 PM.
* **Appointment Location** (required): Enter the location where the appointment will take place.
* **Contact Number** (required): Enter the retailer's contact number for communication purposes.
* **Appointment Type** (select_one, options: Consultation, Training prophets, Event, required): Select the type of appointment from the given options.
* **Additional Comments** (optional, note type): Enter any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Sales Rep** (required): Enter your sales representative name or ID.
* **Retailer Email** (required): Enter the retailer's email address.
* **Retailer Phone** (optional, text type): Enter the retailer's phone number.
* **Store Hours** (required): Enter the retailer's store hours of operation.
* **Meeting Type** (select_one, options: Meet-and-greet, Meeting, Event, Other, required): Select the type of meeting from the provided options.
* **Products Discussed** (select_multiple, options: Yes, No, required): Check if products were discussed during the meeting.
* **Next Steps** (optional, text type): Enter any next steps or follow-up actions to be taken.
* **Retailer Manager** (required, text type): Enter the name of the retailer manager.
* **Retailer Notes** (optional, note type): Enter any additional retailer notes or information.
* **Assistant Notes** (optional, note type): Enter any additional assistant notes or information.
* **Date of Last Meeting** (required): Enter the date of the last meeting with the retailer.
* **Last Meeting Outcome** (required): Enter the outcome of the last meeting with the retailer.
