# Makeup Artist Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Makeup Artist Onboarding Form is designed to be a one-time registration process for new artists. By filling out this form, you will be able to:

*   Showcase your artistic skills and profile in our platform
*   Get discovered by potential collaboration or partnership opportunities
*   Receive invitations to exclusive events and projects

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your information in the required fields.
2.  Make sure to fill out all the required fields, marked with a star \*.
3.  Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
4.  Select your social media presence from the options provided.
5.  Choose your work location from the options provided.
6.  Indicate your available workdays by selecting multiple options.
7.  Fill out any optional fields that you feel are relevant to your profile.
8.  Review your form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Artist Name** (`new_artist_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear in our platform.
*   **Artist Email** (`artist_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address that we can contact you with.
*   **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your work phone number that we can contact you with.
*   **Artist Website** (`artist_website`, Text, Optional): Enter your website URL if you have one.
*   **Years Of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, Number, Optional): Enter your number of years of experience as a makeup artist.
*   **Social Media** (`social_media`, Select One, Required): Select one or more of the following social media platforms that you are active on: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook.
*   **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your portfolio, if you have one.
*   **Bio** (`bio`, Note, Optional): Enter a short description about yourself and your artistic style.
*   **Work Location** (`work_location`, Select One, Required): Choose the city where you are available to work: Los Angeles, New York, Miami.
*   **Available Days** (`available_days`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the days you are available to work.
