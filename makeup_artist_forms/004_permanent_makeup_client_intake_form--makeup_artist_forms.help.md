# Permanent Makeup Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for permanent makeup artists to collect essential information from clients before providing services. It's crucial to complete this form accurately to ensure a safe and successful treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the **Client Information** section.
2. Share your contact details, such as phone number and email address, in the **Contact Details** section.
3. Answer the question about your medical history in the **Medical History** section.
4. Add any additional notes about your medical history in the **Medical History Notes** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Details (Text)**: Provide your phone number and email address so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
* **Medical History (Text)**: Briefly describe any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns that may affect the permanent makeup process.
* **Medical History Notes (Text)**: Add any additional information or comments about your medical history that will help us better understand your situation.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your cooperation in helping us provide the best possible service for you.
