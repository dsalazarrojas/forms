# Wedding Hair And Makeup Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to help us understand your wedding hair and makeup needs, preferences, and requirements. Please answer the questions honestly to help us provide you with the best services for your special day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, choosing all that apply for multiple selections.
2. If you're unsure or have no preference, select "None" or leave the field blank.
3. Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you regarding your services.
4. Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Day Details** (`wedding_day_details`): Select "True" or "False" to indicate if you have a wedding day (e.g., ceremony, reception, photoshoot) where you would like our services.
- **Hair Services** (`hair_services`): Choose all the hair services you're interested in:
  - Haircut
  - Color
  - Style
  - Extensions
  - Hair Accessories
- **Makeup Services** (`make_up_services`, `make_up_services_2`): Choose all the makeup services you're interested in:

- **Bridal Party Details** (`bridal_party_details`): Please provide details about the members of the bridal party (e.g., bridesmaids, groomsmen).
- **Hair Color** (`hair_color`): Enter your hair color or color preference.
- **Hair Style** (`hair_style`): Choose your desired hair style:
  - Curly
  - Straight
  - Wavy
  - Updo

  - Half-up with veil
- **Bridal Participants** (`bridal_participants`): Choose all the members of the bridal party:

- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Select your preferred contact method:

  - Inquire about payment

- **Photoshoot Details** (`photoshoot_details`): Please provide details about the photoshoot (e.g., location, number of guests).
