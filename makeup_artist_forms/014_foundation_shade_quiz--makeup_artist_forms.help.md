# Foundation Shade Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foundation Shade Quiz is a tool designed to help you find the perfect foundation shade and tone for your skin. By answering a few questions, you'll be able to narrow down your options and find a foundation that matches your skin's unique characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your skin tone from the options provided.
2. Choose your skin type (normal, oily, dry, combination, or sensitive).
3. Select your preferred foundation characteristics, including shade, undertone, and finish.
4. Click submit to receive your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Skin Tone** (`pale`, `fair`, `light`, `medium`, `tan`, `olive`, `dark`, `very dark`): Please select your natural skin tone from the options provided. This will help us determine your foundation shade.
- **Skin Type** (`normal`, `oily`, `dry`, `combination`, `sensitive`): Choose your skin type, as this will help us narrow down your foundation options.
- **Undertone** (`pink`, `yellow`, `neutral`, `gold`, `blue`): Select your undertone to help us match your skin's undertone.
- **Foundation Shade** (`light`, `medium`, `tan`, `dark`): Choose the foundation shade that best matches your skin tone.
- **Skin Condition** (`normal`, `sensitive`, `eczema`, `acne`, `rosacea`): Select your skin condition to help us understand any skin concerns you may have.
- **Preferred Foundation Shade** (`fair`, `light`, `medium`, `tan`, `dark`): Select the foundation shade that you prefer.
- **Preferred Foundation Tone** (`cool`, `warm`, `neutral`): Choose the foundation tone that you like best.
- **Preferred Foundation Finish** (`matte`, `satin`, `creamy`, `gel`): Select the foundation finish that you prefer.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when selecting your skin tone and type, as this will help us provide the best results.
- If you have any skin concerns or conditions that are not listed, please contact us with more information.
- If you have any further questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
