# Makeup Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Makeup Appointment Form is used to book a makeup service for a client. Please fill out the form with the required information to ensure a successful booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Client Name` field with the client's name.
2. Enter the client's `email` address in the `email` field.
3. Select the desired `Date` for the appointment.
4. Choose the `Start Time` and `End Time` for the appointment.
5. Select the `Appointment Type` from the available options (Makeup, Lashes, or Nails).
6. Enter any additional notes in the `Note` field.
7. Confirm that you have reviewed the form by selecting `Submit` or `Cancel`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name as you would like it to appear on their appointment card.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address to keep them informed about their appointment.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Choose the start time of the appointment.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Choose the end time of the appointment.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of appointment (Makeup, Lashes, or Nails).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a phone number to contact the client.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or information about the appointment.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that you have reviewed the form by selecting either `Submit` or `Cancel`.
