# Personal Image Makeover Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Image Makeover Appointment Form is used to book a personal image makeover session with a suitable consultant. The form collects information from clients to understand their preferences, goals, and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not been previously assigned a consultant.
2. Fill in the required fields with your details.
3. Choose your preferred session type.
4. Select your preferred consultant(s) from the options provided.
5. Add any relevant goals and preferences.
6. Provide your appointment details.
7. Enter your contact information.
8. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our appointment records.
* **Session Type** (`Session Type`, select, optional): Choose the type of session you are interested in booking.
* **Session Date** (`Session Date`, date, optional): Select a date for your session. We recommend providing the earliest available date to increase the chances of getting a suitable consultant.
* **Session Time** (`Session Time`, time, optional): Choose a time for your session.
* **Consultant** (`Consultant`, multiple select, optional): Select up to three consultants whose expertise you would like to work with.
* **Goals and Preferences** (`Goals and Preferences`, text, optional): Describe your goals and preferences, so we can best match you with a suitable consultant.
* **Appointment Details** (`Appointment Details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about your appointment, such as preferred location or duration.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, so we can get in touch with you before and after your appointment.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
* **Submitted by** (`Submitted by`, text, optional): This field is only used for internal purposes. Please do not fill it in.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth booking process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will do our best to match you with a suitable consultant based on your preferences.
