## <figure>Microblading Consent & Appointment Form - Help Guide

### <figure>Purpose

The Microblading Consent & Appointment Form is a document that gathers crucial information from customers for their microblading appointment. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured, enabling our team to provide personalized care and attention to the customer's needs.

### <figure>How To Complete This Form

1.  **Appointment Form** (<name>Appointment Form, <type>text, required): Please enter your details for your appointment, including your desired time and date.
2.  **Consent Form** (<name>Consent Form, <type>select_one, required): Indicate your consent for our services by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
3.  **Contact Details** (<name>Contact Details, <type>text, required): Provide your contact details for communication purposes.
4.  **Medical History** (<name>Medical History, <type>text, required): Share any medical history or allergies that may affect the treatment.
5.  **Appointment Preferences** (<name>Appointment Preferences, <type>select_multiple, optional): Specify any special appointment preferences you may have.
6.  **Contact Person** (<name>Contact Person, <type>select_one, required): Identify a contact person for emergencies or important updates.
7.  **Signature** (<name>Signature, <type>note, required): Please sign this form to confirm your consent for our services.
8.  **Date** (<name>Date, <type>date, required): Enter the date of your appointment.
9.  **Time** (<name>Time, <type>time, required): Enter the time of your appointment.
10. **Email** (<name>Email, <type>email, required): Provide your email address for communication.
11. **Phone** (<name>Phone, <type>text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
12. **Address** (<name>Address, <type>text, required): Provide your address for our records.
13. **Appointment Time** (<name>Appointment Time, <type>time, required): Enter the time of your appointment.
14. **Appointment Date** (<name>Appointment Date, <type>date, required): Enter the date of your appointment.
15. **Contact Method** (<name>Contact Method, <type>select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, text).
16. **Emergency Contact** (<name>Emergency Contact, <type>text, required): Identify an emergency contact person and their relationship to you.
17. **Emergency Contact Relationship** (<name>Emergency Contact Relationship, <type>text, required): Specify your relationship to the emergency contact.
18. **Allergy or Medical Condition** (<name>Allergy or Medical Condition, <type>text, optional): If you have any allergies or medical conditions, please note them here.
19. **Medication** (<name>Medication, <type>text, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
20. **Medical Condition** (<name>Medical Condition, <type>text, required): Describe any medical conditions you may have.
21. **Medical Provider** (<name>Medical Provider, <type>text, required): Provide your medical provider's name and contact information.
22. **Medication List** (<name>Medication List, <type>text, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
23. **Other Medical History** (<name>Other Medical History, <type>text, required): Share any other medical history or concerns.
24. **Consent** (<name>Consent, <type>select_multiple, required): Confirm your consent for our services.

### <figure>Tips

* Please fill the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you need assistance or clarification, feel free to contact us.
