# Personalized Makeup Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Makeup Color Match Form is designed to help users discover makeup colors that complement their natural skin tone and personal characteristics. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form questions carefully and answer them based on your personal characteristics.
2. Select your skin tone from the options provided.
3. Choose your hair color from the options provided.
4. Select your eye color(s) from the options provided.
5. Choose a personal style to help us get a better understanding of your makeup preferences.
6. If you have a favorite color, please share it with us.
7. Indicate if you'd like to see recommended products for your skin tone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text): This is a space to introduce yourself and share any relevant information about your skin, hair, or style that could impact the color match.
* **Skin Tone** (Select One): Choose your natural skin tone: Fair, Medium, or Tan. This will help us understand your undertone and recommend colors that complement it.
* **Hair Color** (Select Multiple): Choose all the hair colors that apply to you: Blonde, Brown, or Red. 
* **Eye Color** (Select Multiple): Select all the eye colors that apply to you: Blue, Green, or Brown. 
* **Style** (Text): Describe your personal style, do you prefer bright, bold, and fun or soft, natural, and subtle? 
* **Favorite Color** (Text): If you have a favorite color, please share it with us! This can help us get a better understanding of your color preferences.
* **Match Results** (Note): This is where we will display your personalized makeup color match results.
* **Recommended Products** (Select Multiple): Choose 'Yes' if you would like to see recommended products for your skin tone.

Note: Please answer each question truthfully to get the best results.
