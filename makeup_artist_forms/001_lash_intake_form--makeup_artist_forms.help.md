# lash_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide their information and preferences for lash extensions services. It guides clients through the process of choosing their desired lash style, services, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the "Lash Extension Intake" page with your general information.
2.  Review and acknowledge the Lash Extension Consent Form.
3.  Select your desired "Client Information" from the options provided.
4.  Provide your "Contact Information" for communication purposes.
5.  Choose the services you wish to receive from the list of options.
6.  Specify your "Lash Style" and "Lash Size" preferences.
7.  Identify your "Client Witnessed By" information.
8.  Provide the name and title of the witness, if applicable.
9.  Sign the "Staff Signature", "Client Signature", and "Witness Signature" fields as required.
10.  Add any "Additional Notes" in the free-form text fields.
11.  Grant permission for your "Photo Release" by filling out the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Lash Extension Intake** (`1`): This is the main page for providing your general information.
*   **I have read and understand the Lash Extension Consent Form** (`2`): This section acknowledges that you have read and understood the consent form.
*   **Client Information** (`3`): Enter your name, address, phone number, and email for client details.
*   **Contact Information** (`4`): Enter your email, phone number, and address for communication purposes.
*   **Services** (`5`): Select the services you wish to receive from the list of options provided.
*   **Lash Style** (`6`): Enter the style of lashes you desire.
*   **Lash Size** (`7`): Enter the desired size of lashes.
*   **Client Witnessed By** (`8`): Identify the witness or person present during your appointment.
*   **Client Witnessed By Options** (`9`): This is a free-form text field for the witness's name.
*   **Witness Title** (`10`): Enter the title of the witness, if applicable.
*   **Witness Name** (`11`): This is a free-form text field for the witness's name.
*   **Witness Signature** (`15`): Sign your name as a witness.
*   **Staff Signature** (`13`): Sign your name as a staff member performing the service.
*   **Client Signature** (`14`): Sign your name as the client.
*   **Additional Notes** (`16`): Add any additional comments or remarks about your service.
*   **Client Notes** (`17`): Add any additional comments or remarks.
*   **Service Notes** (`18`): Add any additional comments or remarks about your service.
*   **Photo Release** (`19`): Grant permission for your photo to be taken.
*   **Client Photo** (`20`): Enter a photo of yourself (if applicable).
*   **Lash Artist** (`21`): Enter the name of the lash artist performing the service.
*   **Lash Technician** (`22`): Enter the name of the lash technician performing the service.
*   **Lash Manager** (`23`): Enter the name of the lash manager, if applicable.
*   **Date** (`24`): Enter the date of your appointment.
*   **Witness Name** (`9`): This is a free-form text field for the witness's name.
*   **Witness Title** (`10`): Enter the title of the witness, if applicable.
*   **Witness Phone** (`11`): Enter the witness's phone number.
*   **Date and Time** (`12`): Enter the date and time of your appointment.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
*   Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication and service delivery.
*   If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to use the free-form text fields provided.
*   Grant permission for your photo release if you wish to have photos taken during the service.
*   Sign all required signature fields as requested.
