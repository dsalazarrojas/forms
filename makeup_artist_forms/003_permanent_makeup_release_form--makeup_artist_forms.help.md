## Permanent Makeup Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Release Form is a necessary step before receiving permanent makeup services. This form is to ensure that you understand and agree to our photo release and signature process. Please take a moment to read through the form carefully and fill it out completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address (optional, but recommended).
3. Provide your **Phone** number (optional, but recommended).
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format **YYYY-MM-DD** (required).
5. Select the **Photo Release** option that best fits your preference.
6. Sign the **Date** field with the current date (required).
7. Sign the **Signature** field with your signature (required).
8. You are not required to sign the **Photo Release Signature** field, but if you choose to grant photo release, sign this field as well and enter the date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email**: Your email address where you can be reached (optional).
- **Phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached (optional).
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format **YYYY-MM-DD**.
- **Photo Release**: Choose **I give my consent to take photos and/or videos** if you are comfortable with being photographed. If not, choose **I do not give my consent to take photos and/or videos**.
- **Date**: Sign with the current date to acknowledge your understanding of the photo release and signature process.
- **Signature**: Sign your name to acknowledge your understanding of the photo release and signature process.
- **Photo Release Signature**: If you chose to grant photo release, sign this field as well.
- **Photo Release Signature Date**: If you chose to grant photo release, enter the date you signed the photo release signature field.
- **Photo Release Signature**: This field is not required to be signed. If you signed the photo release, do not sign this field.
