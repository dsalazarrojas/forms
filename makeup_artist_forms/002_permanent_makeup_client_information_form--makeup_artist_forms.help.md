# Permanent Makeup Client Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for clients to provide their personal and service-related information, allowing our artists to better understand their needs and preferences. Please ensure you have all necessary information ready before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step guide below:

1. Start by providing your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field to be contacted for further instructions or confirmations.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field for our team to reach you.
5. Select the desired permanent makeup service from the "Desired Service" dropdown list.
6. Choose your preferred artist from the "Preferred Artist" dropdown list.
7. Select a date that works for your appointment in the "Preferred Date" field.
8. Choose a time that suits you best in the "Preferred Time" field.
9. If needed, add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
10. Choose your preferred contact method (phone, email, or text) in the "Contact Method" field.
11. Select your preferred contact method in the "Contact Preferred Method" field.
12. Enter any contact notes in the "Contact Note" field.
13. Enter a contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
14. Enter a contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
15. If other contact method is selected, enter details in the "Contact Other" field.
16. Select a preferred time window of 15 minutes or less in the "Preferred Time Window" field.
17. Add any additional appointment notes in the "Appointment Note" field.
18. Choose the appointment method (in person or over video call) in the "Appointment Method" field.
19. Select a date for your appointment in the "Appointment Date" field.
20. Choose an appointment time in the "Appointment Time" field.
21. Enter the appointment address in the "Appointment Address" field.
22. Enter the appointment location in the "Appointment Location" field.
23. Add any additional comments about your appointment in the "Additional Comments" field.
24. Sign your name in the "Client Signature" field.
25. Our artist will sign their name in the "Artist Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (3, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
- **Contact Number** (4, text, required: false): Enter your contact number for our team to reach you.
- **Desired Service** (5, select_one, required: false): Select the permanent makeup service you want (e.g., Ombre Brows, Microblading, etc.).
- **Preferred Artist** (6, select_one, required: false): Choose the artist you prefer to perform your service.
- **Preferred Date** (7, date, required: false): Select a date that works for your appointment.
- **Preferred Time** (8, time, required: false): Choose a time that suits you best for the appointment.
- **Note** (9, note, required: false): Add any additional notes, if needed.
- **Contact Method** (10, select_multiple, required: false): Select how you want to be contacted (phone, email, or text).
- **Contact Preferred Method** (11, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or text).
- **Contact Note** (12, text, required: false): Enter any contact notes, if needed.
- **Contact Email** (13, email, required: false): Enter a contact email address.
- **Contact Phone** (14, text, required: false): Enter a contact phone number.
- **Contact Other** (15, text, required: false): If other contact method is selected, enter details.
- **Preferred Time Window** (16, number, required: false): Select a time window of 15 minutes or less.
- **Appointment Note** (17, note, required: false): Add any additional appointment notes.
- **Appointment Method** (18, select_one, required: false): Choose the appointment method (in person or over video call).
- **Appointment Date** (19, date, required: false): Select a date for your appointment.
- **Appointment Time** (20, time, required: false): Choose an appointment time.
- **Appointment Address** (21, text, required: false): Enter the appointment address.
- **Appointment Location** (22, text, required: false): Enter the appointment location.
- **Additional Comments** (23, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about your appointment.
- **Client Signature** (24, text, required: false): Sign your name, please.
- **Artist Signature** (25, text, required: false): Our artist will sign their name after your appointment.

Note: Please ensure you have all necessary information ready before completing this form. If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us.
