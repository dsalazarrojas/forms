# permanent_make_up_consent_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client consent and medical history before permanent makeup procedures. It ensures that clients are aware of the risks and benefits of the treatment and provides essential information for the artist to understand their medical background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the questions regarding client consent, medical history, and permanent makeup design.
2. Enter the client's information in the "client_information" field.
3. Sign the "client_signature" field with your signature.
4. Fill out the "date_of_consent" and "time_of_consent" fields with the date and time of when you signed the form.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "permanent_make_up_design" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_consent**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you are consenting to the permanent makeup procedure. Select "No" if you are not consenting.
* **client_information**: Enter your name, address, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **medical_history**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a medical history that may impact the procedure. Select "No" if you do not have a medical history.
* **client_signature**: Sign this field with your handwritten signature to confirm that you are the client and agree to the terms.
* **date_of_consent**: Fill out this field with the date you signed the form.
* **time_of_consent**: Fill out this field with the time of when you signed the form.
* **artist_signature**: This field should be signed by the artist performing the procedure.
* **permanent_make_up_design**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you are willing to proceed with the permanent makeup design. Select "No" if you are not willing to proceed.
* **date_of_consent**: This field is used to record the date of when you signed the form.
* **time_of_consent**: This field is used to record the time of when you signed the form.

## Tips
* Please complete the form accurately and honestly to ensure the best possible outcome.
* Have a duplicate of the form filled out and kept on file for future reference.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the risks and benefits of the treatment.
