# Lash Adhesive Brand Training Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule a training session for a Lash Adhesive Brand product line. It collects information about the appointment details, brand, start and end dates, training type, location, attendees, training duration, language, appointment status, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your appointment details, including the date and time of the session.
* Select the brand you want to train on from the available options.
* Choose the start and end dates of the training session.
* Select the type of training you need, such as in-person, virtual, or online.
* Select the location where the training will take place.
* List the attendees who will be participating in the training.
* Enter the duration of the training session.
* Choose the language of the training.
* Select the status of the appointment, such as pending, confirmed, or completed.
* Add any additional notes about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the appointment, including the date and time of the session.
* **Brand** (`brand`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the brand you want to train on from the available options.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the training session.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the training session.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of training you need, such as in-person, virtual, or online.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the location where the training will take place.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, text, required/optional): List the attendees who will be participating in the training.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the training session in minutes.
* **Training Language** (`training_language`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the language of the training.
* **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the appointment, such as pending, confirmed, or completed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the session.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Form Submission Time** (`form_submission_time`, time, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Form Submission IP Address** (`form_submission_ip_address`, text, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Form Submission User Agent** (`form_submission_user_agent`, text, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Form Submission User Platform** (`form_submission_user_platform`, text, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
