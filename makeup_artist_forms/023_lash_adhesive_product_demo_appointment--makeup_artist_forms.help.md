# Lash Adhesive Product Demo Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for a product demo appointment for a lash adhesive product. The fields in this form will help us understand the details about the product, the artist, and the salon.

## How To Complete This Form

This guide will walk you through the form fields, step-by-step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Demo Info** (`product_demo_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the product demo. Please enter a short paragraph summarizing the purpose and scope of the demo.
* **Artist Info** (`artist_info`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the artist conducting the demo. Please enter the name of the artist and any other details relevant to the demo.
* **Salon Info** (`salon_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide information about the salon where the demo will take place. Please enter the name of the salon and any other details relevant to the demo.
* **Demo Info** (`demo_info`, text, optional): This field is used to gather details about the demo itself. Please enter any other information relevant to the demo, such as date, time, and location.

## Tips

* Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, as this information will help us better understand the product demo and its context.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us.
