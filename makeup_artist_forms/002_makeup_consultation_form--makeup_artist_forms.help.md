# Makeup Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a makeup consultation. Fill out this form to provide details about the customer's visit, including their name, phone number, email, and any additional information or description about the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title to give a brief description of the consultation.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Provide the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer E-mail" field.
5. Choose the category of the consultation (e.g., Cosmetic Department, Other, Sale) in the "Form Category" field.
6. Add any relevant tags or keywords related to the consultation in the "Form Tags" field.
7. Select the date of the consultation in the "Form Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the consultation. This will be used to identify the form and make it easier to search.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name. This is an essential piece of information to identify the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number. This will help us contact the customer with updates or schedule further consultations.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address. This will allow us to contact the customer with updates or send them appointment reminders.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category of the consultation (e.g., Cosmetic Department, Other, Sale). This will help us organize and categorize the consultations.
* **Form Tags** (`form_tags`, text, optional): Add any relevant tags or keywords related to the consultation. This will help us filter and search for consultations.
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the consultation. This will help us schedule and plan the consultation.

Note: All fields are optional, but the more information you provide, the better we can serve the customer.
