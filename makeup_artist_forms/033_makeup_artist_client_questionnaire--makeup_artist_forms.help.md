# Makeup Artist Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients to provide a personalized and safe makeup service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure each field is filled in as required by the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Occupation**: Enter your occupation.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, or text).
* **Preferred Contact Method Other**: If your preferred contact method is not listed, enter it here.
* **Social Media**: Select your social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship of the emergency contact person (spouse, parent, child, or other).
* **Medical Conditions**: Select any medical conditions you have (diabetes, allergies, or asthma).
* **Medication**: Select any medication you are taking (none, yes).
* **Medical Conditions Other**: If you have any other medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Hair Type**: Select your hair type (wavy, straight, or curly).
* **Eye Type**: Select your eye type (hazel, blue, or green).
* **Skin Type**: Select your skin type (fair, medium, or dark).
* **Makeup Experience**: Select your makeup experience level (none, some, or advanced).
* **Makeup Experience Other**: If you have other makeup experience, enter it here.
* **Schedule**: Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about your appointment.
* **Photo Count**: Enter the number of photos to be taken during the appointment.
