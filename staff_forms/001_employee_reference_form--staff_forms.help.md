# Employee Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from an employee's professional contact, the referee, in order to provide additional context for future employment or promotional decisions. The information collected will be used by the employee's team or HR team to get in touch with the referee if needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the employee's name as the reference is for this specific employee.
2. Enter the employee's email address in the format `example@example.com`.
3. Provide the referee's name, title or position.
4. Enter the referee's email address in the format `example@example.com`.
5. Enter the referee's phone number, including the country code and area code (e.g., +1 123 456 7890).
6. Enter the referee's occupation or job title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (1, text, required): The name of the employee being referenced.
* **Employee Email** (2, email, required): The email address of the employee being referenced.
* **Referee Name** (4, text): The name of the person who can vouch for the employee's work quality or performance.
* **Referee Email** (5, email): The email address of the person who can vouch for the employee's work quality or performance.
* **Referee Phone** (6, text): The phone number of the person who can vouch for the employee's work quality or performance.
* **Referee Occupation** (7, text): The occupation or job title of the person who can vouch for the employee's work quality or performance.
