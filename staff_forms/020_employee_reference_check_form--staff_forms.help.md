# employee_reference_check_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employee reference checks, typically conducted by HR or management teams to gather information about an employee's performance, job history, and professional references.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant fields that pertain to the specific employee's situation.
2. Fill out the employee's personal details such as job title, years worked, and date of birth.
3. Provide references from employees A, B, C, D, E, F, and G.
4. Include manager and supervisor references.
5. Fill out the employee's salary history, including last salary and reason for leaving.
6. Add any additional employee notes or comments.
