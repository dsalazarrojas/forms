# sabbatical_leave_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a sabbatical leave request to your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Request Details field with a clear and concise description of your leave request.
2. Enter your name in the Staff Member field.
3. Select the Start Date field to choose the date you would like your sabbatical to begin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional): This field is where you provide a brief description of your sabbatical leave request. Please be as clear and concise as possible. This information will help your HR representative process your request.
* **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your name. Please ensure it matches your official personnel record.
* **Start Date** (`sabbatical_start_date`, date, optional): This field is where you select the date you would like your sabbatical to begin. Please choose a specific date, and ensure it is in the format YYYY-MM-DD. 

## Tips
* Please ensure you have the necessary approvals and permissions before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please reach out to your HR representative or management before submitting the form.
* Be clear and concise in your request details to ensure efficient processing.
