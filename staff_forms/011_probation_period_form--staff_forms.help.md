# Probation Period Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Probation Period Form is used to track and document the start and end dates of a staff member's probation period, as well as any relevant remarks or additional comments. This form is typically filled out by HR or management personnel to keep records up to date and ensure accurate tracking of staff performance and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant start date for the staff member's probation period. If the exact date is not known, please leave this field blank.
2. Select the relevant end date for the staff member's probation period. If the exact date is not known, please leave this field blank.
3. Enter any additional remarks about the staff member's performance or progress during the probation period. This field is optional and can be left blank if no additional comments are necessary.
4. Enter any further additional remarks or comments about the staff member's performance or progress during the probation period. This field is optional and can be left blank if no further comments are necessary.
5. Enter the staff member's ID number, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date (start_date)** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the staff member's probation period.
* **End Date (end_date)** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the staff member's probation period.
* **Remarks (remarks)** (`remarks`, text, optional): Enter any remarks or comments about the staff member's performance or progress during the probation period.
* **Additional Remarks (remarks_2)** (`remarks_2`, text, optional): Enter any further remarks or comments about the staff member's performance or progress during the probation period.
* **Staff ID (staff_id)** (`staff_id`, number, optional): Enter the staff member's ID number, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure accurate records.
* If the exact dates are not known, please leave the relevant fields blank.
* If no additional comments are necessary, please leave the remarks fields blank.
