# Employee Change Of Address Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Change Of Address Form is used to update an employee's address in the company's records. This form is typically used for employees who are changing their permanent address. The information collected through this form will be used to update the employee's profile and ensure that they receive important company communications and notifications at their new address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your new address in the "New Address" field.
2. Enter your old address in the "Old Address" field (if applicable).
3. Select the "Effective Date" when you would like your address change to take effect.
4. Choose the contact person to be notified of this address change.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Address**: Enter the new address where you can be contacted. This can include your street address, apartment number, city, state, and zip code.
* **Old Address**: If you are updating your address because you are moving from a previous location, please enter the old address here. This is optional, but it helps us keep a record of your previous address for historical purposes.
* **Effective Date**: Choose the date when you would like the address change to take effect. This is usually the current date, but you can select a future date if you have a specific reason for delaying the address change.
* **Contact Person**: Select the person you would like to be notified of this address change. This could be your manager, supervisor, HR representative, or accounting/IT department.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can update your contact information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to update your email address if it has changed as well.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the HR department for assistance.
