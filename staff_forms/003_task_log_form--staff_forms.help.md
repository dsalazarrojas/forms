# Task Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Task Log Form is used to log and track tasks and their corresponding details. This form helps to gather information about tasks, including the category and staff member responsible for the task.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the appropriate category for the task.
2. Choose the staff member responsible for the task.
3. Select all applicable staff members (if multiple are assigned).
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category of the task you are logging.
* **Staff Form** (`staff_form`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all staff members responsible for the task.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the staff member assigned to the task.
