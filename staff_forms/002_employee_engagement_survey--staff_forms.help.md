# employee_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your job satisfaction, team environment, and engagement levels. This data will help management and HR teams understand employee sentiments and make informed decisions to improve the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your employee ID (this is a unique identifier assigned to you by the company).
2. Select your department from the dropdown list.
3. Indicate whether you have a manager or not.
4. Rate your job satisfaction, team environment, and engagement levels.
5. Provide any suggestions or comments you have about your job or team.
6. Enter your email address and phone number (this information is required for data verification purposes).
7. If you have any manager or team-related comments or suggestions, please provide them below.
8. Rate your manager's performance and your overall engagement level.
9. Choose a date and time for the survey.
10. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID assigned by the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Select your department from the dropdown list to help us better understand your team dynamics.
* **Manager** (`manager_id`, select_one, required): Check the box if you have a manager.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Provide your job title for more detailed information.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your job satisfaction level from Satisfied to Dissatisfied.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have good or no communication with your manager.
* **Team Environment** (`team_environment`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your team environment from Good to Fair.
* **Engagement** (`engagement`, number, optional): Enter your engagement level, with higher numbers indicating higher engagement.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions or comments about your job or team.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, optional): Enter any manager-related comments or suggestions.
* **Manager Suggestion** (`manager_suggestion`, text, optional): Enter any manager-related suggestions or comments.
* **Employee Comment** (`employee_comment`, text, optional): Enter any employee-related comments or suggestions.
* **Employee Suggestion** (`employee_suggestion`, text, optional): Enter any employee-related suggestions or comments.
* **Manager Rating** (`manager_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your manager's performance from Good to Fair.
* **Employee Rating** (`employee_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall engagement level from Good to Fair.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the survey.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed the survey.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address for data verification purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for data verification purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Job Satisfaction Rating** (`job_satisfaction_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your job satisfaction level from Satisfied to Dissatisfied.
* **Team Environment Rating** (`team_environment_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your team environment from Good to Fair.
* **Job Satisfaction Scale** (`job_satisfaction_scale`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your job satisfaction scale from Satisfied to Dissatisfied.
* **Engagement Rating** (`engagement_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall engagement level from Good to Fair.

**Tips**:

* Please fill out all required fields marked with \*.
* You can leave optional fields blank if you don't feel comfortable providing that information.
* Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
