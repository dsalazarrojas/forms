# Employee Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process employee transfers within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the employee list to select the employee to be transferred.
2. Enter the transfer date, which should be the date of the transfer.
3. Provide transfer details, if necessary.
4. Select the new department for the employee.
5. Enter the employee's ID number.
6. Enter the employee's name.
7. Enter the employee's new position.
8. Enter the effective date of the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Employee** (`Yes/No`, required, select_one): Choose "Yes" to select the employee to be transferred.
* **Transfer Date** (``, required, date): Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Transfer Details** (``, optional, text): Provide any additional details regarding the transfer.
* **New Department** (`Yes/No`, optional, select_multiple): Choose "Yes" to select the new department for the employee.
* **Employee ID** (`number`, required, number): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Employee Name** (``, required, text): Enter the employee's name.
* **New Position** (``, optional, text): Enter the new position of the employee.
* **Old Position** (``, optional, text): Not required, use if the employee had a different position in the past.
* **Effective Date** (``, required, date): Enter the date when the transfer becomes effective.
