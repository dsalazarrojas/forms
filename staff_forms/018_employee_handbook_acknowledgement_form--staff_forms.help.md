## Employee Handbook Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Handbook Acknowledgement Form is a document that confirms an employee's understanding of the company's employee handbook. It is a mandatory form that must be completed by all employees after reading the handbook.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the company's employee handbook.
2. Confirm that you have read and understood the handbook by completing the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Handbook Acknowledgement** (1): This field is a numeric field where you should enter a "1" to indicate that you have acknowledged reading the employee handbook.
  - **Employee Handbook Version** (2): Enter the version number of the employee handbook you have read.
  - **Date Of Viewing** (3): Enter the date when you last viewed the employee handbook.
  - **Date Of Acknowledgement** (4): Enter the date when you acknowledged reading and understanding the employee handbook.
  - **Employee Signature** (5): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the employee handbook.
  - **Supervisor Signature** (6): If your supervisor has reviewed your understanding, ask them to sign below.
  - **Date Of Signature** (7): Enter the date when you and your supervisor signed the acknowledgement.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the entire employee handbook before completing this form.
* Ensure that you have reviewed all sections and understand all policies and procedures outlined in the handbook.
* Keep a record of the employee handbook version you have read and signed.
