# Employee Handbook Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Employee Handbook Acknowledgement (1)**: 
  - Enter "1" to confirm that you have read and understood the handbook.
• **Employee Handbook Version (2)**: 
  - Enter the version number of the handbook you have read.
• **Date Of Viewing (3)**: 
  - Enter the date you last viewed the handbook.
• **Date Of Acknowledgement (4)**: 
  - Enter the date you acknowledged reading and understanding the handbook.
• **Employee Signature (5)**: 
  - Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the handbook.
• **Supervisor Signature (6)**: 
  - If your supervisor has reviewed your understanding, ask them to sign below.
• **Date Of Signature (7)**: 
  - Enter the date you and your supervisor signed the acknowledgement.
