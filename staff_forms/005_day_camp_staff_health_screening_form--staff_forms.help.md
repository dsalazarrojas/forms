# Day Camp Staff Health Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Day Camp Staff Health Screening Form is a crucial document that helps the camp director ensure the health and safety of all staff members. The form collects personal and medical information to identify any potential risks or concerns that may impact a staff member's work at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out your personal details, including your height and weight.
2.  Report any medical history, allergies, or conditions that may impact your work.
3.  Answer questions about your health screenings, vaccinations, and medical check-ups.
4.  Provide any additional health-related information or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details**: Enter your name and any other personal details as required.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous injuries you've experienced.
* **Allergies**: Check the boxes for any allergies you have, or select "Other" if you have an allergy not listed.
* **Medical Notes**: Provide any additional medical notes or concerns you'd like to share with the camp director.
* **Health Screening Question 1**: Answer "True" or "False" to indicate if you have any health concerns or issues at this time.
* **Health Screening Question 2**: Answer "True" or "False" to indicate if you've been vaccinated against COVID-19.
* **Health Screening Question 3**: Describe any medical conditions or allergies that may impact your work at the camp.
* **Health Screening Question 4**: Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Health Screening Question 5**: Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Health Screening Question 6**: Enter your body mass index (BMI).
* **Health Screening Question 7**: Describe any other health questions or concerns you'd like to share with the camp director.
* **Last Medical Check-up**: Enter the date of your last medical check-up.
* **Medical Provider**: Enter the name of your regular medical doctor.
* **Health Screening Question 8**: Answer "True" or "False" to indicate if you have health insurance.
* **Health Screening Question 9**: Select how often you get regular check-ups.
* **Health Screening Question 10**: Select your blood type.
* **Health Screening Question 11**: Describe any health screening results you'd like to share.
* **Additional Information**: Describe any other information you'd like to share with the camp director.
* **Last Medical Check-up 1**: (Optional) Enter the date of your last medical check-up.
* **Medical Provider 1**: (Optional) Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Last Medical Check-up 2**: (Optional) Enter the date of your last medical check-up.
* **Medical Provider 2**: (Optional) Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Last Medical Check-up 3**: (Optional) Enter the date of your last medical check-up.
* **Medical Provider 3**: (Optional) Enter the name of your medical provider.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, ask for clarification from the camp director.
* Keep the information up to date, as medical status can change over time.
* If you have any medical concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the camp director.
