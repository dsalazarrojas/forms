# Employee Confidentiality Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Confidentiality Form is used to collect employee confidentiality agreements, ensuring employees understand and agree to the company's confidentiality policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Company Information**: Enter your company name and any other relevant details.
2. **Employee Name**: Type in your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
3. **Employee Title**: Provide your job title or position within the company.
4. **Confidentiality Agreement**: Select all the options that apply to you:
	* I have read and understood the terms and conditions of this agreement
	* I am aware that I am not allowed to share confidential information with anyone outside of the company
	* I will not disclose confidential information to any third party
5. **Signature Date**: Select the date you sign this form.
6. **Signature**: Print your name as it appears on your ID or business card.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information** (`company_information`, text, optional): Enter the name and any other relevant details of your company.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Type in your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, optional): Provide your job title or position within the company.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to you, indicating you have read and understood the terms, are aware of the limitations, and will not disclose confidential information.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Select the date you sign this form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Print your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
