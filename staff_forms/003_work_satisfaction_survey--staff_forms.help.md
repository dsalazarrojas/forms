# Work Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Satisfaction Survey is a form used to gather feedback from employees on their job satisfaction and areas for improvement. It is designed to be completed by individual employees who have had significant experience in their current role (at least 6 months) and have not completed the survey in the past 3 months.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a rating for each question based on your current level of satisfaction.
2. Provide a brief description for each question, if applicable.
3. Make sure to answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is valuable for improving your work experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with your job**:
	+ This question asks about your overall level of satisfaction with your job. Select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Pay and benefits**:
	+ This question asks you to describe your current pay and benefits, including any bonuses or other perks you receive. This will help us understand how well we are meeting your compensation needs.
* **Work environment**:
	+ This question asks you to describe your work space, equipment, and any other aspects of your physical work environment. This will help us understand how well we are providing a comfortable and productive workspace.
* **Work life balance**:
	+ This question is a multiple-choice question asking about your balance between work and personal life. Select all that apply.
* **Work with people**:
	+ This question asks you to describe your coworkers and managers, including any positive or negative interactions you have had. This will help us understand how well we are fostering a positive and supportive team dynamic.
* **What suggestions do you have for management**:
	+ This question is an open-ended question where you can provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving your work experience or the organization as a whole.
* **Job title**:
	+ This question asks about your current job title and the level of responsibility you feel you have. Select the option that best describes your situation.
* **Overall work satisfaction**:
	+ This question is the final question and asks about your overall level of satisfaction with your job. Select the option that best describes your feelings.
