# Emergency Contact Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Contact Form Template is a form used to collect emergency contact information from staff members. This form is used to assign forms to staff members, and to track the last updated date of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency contact form template** (`emergency contact form template`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the form.
* **Contact Person** (`contact person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person who is the primary contact for this form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Other Contact Info** (`other contact info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information for the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency contact name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency contact relationship`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the relationship between the staff member and the emergency contact, such as Family Member, Friend, or Other.
* **Emergency Contact Name 2** (`emergency contact name 2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (`emergency contact relationship 2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the relationships between the staff member and the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency contact phone 2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2** (`emergency contact email 2`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address 2** (`emergency contact address 2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Other Info 2** (`emergency contact other info 2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information for the second emergency contact.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the form.
* **Assigned Form Type** (`assigned form type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of form this is, such as Staff, Staff Member, or Staff Position.
* **Assigned Form Group** (`assigned form group`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the group this form is assigned to, such as Residential, Business, or Other.
* **Assigned Form Owner** (`assigned form owner`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the owner of this form, such as chatjimmy, admin, or manager.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the tool used for this form, such as chatjimmy, admin, or manager.
* **Assigned Form Category** (`assigned form category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category of this form, such as Staff Forms or Other.
* **Assigned Form Version** (`assigned form version`, `text`, required: false): Enter the version of the form.
* **Assigned Form ID** (`assigned form id`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the ID of the form.
* **Assigned Form Name** (`assigned form name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the form.
* **Assigned Form Date** (`assigned form date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date this form was assigned to the staff member.
* **Assigned Form Last Updated** (`assigned form last updated`, `date`, required: false): Enter the last updated date of the form.
