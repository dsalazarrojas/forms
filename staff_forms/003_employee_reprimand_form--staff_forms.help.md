# employee_reprimand_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee reprimand form is a formal document used to record and document disciplinary actions taken against employees. It's essential to use this form for official, documented reprimands only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with the relevant details about the incident.
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for the reprimand, specifying the specific behavior or actions that led to the disciplinary action.
3. Describe the specific behavior or actions that necessitated the reprimand.
4. Include your employee signature to acknowledge that you received the reprimand and understand its implications.
5. Include your manager's signature to confirm the reprimand and take responsibility for the action.
6. Fill in the date and time of the reprimand.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **general_info** (`general_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you'll provide a brief description of the incident.
* **reason_for_reprimand** (`reason_for_reprimand`, text, required: false): Clearly state the reason for the reprimand, specifying the specific behavior or actions that led to the disciplinary action.
* **specific_behavior** (`specific_behavior`, text, required: false): Describe the specific behavior or actions that necessitated the reprimand.
* **employee_signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you received the reprimand and understand its implications.
* **manager_signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date the form to confirm the reprimand and take responsibility for the action.
* **date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the reprimand was given.
* **time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the reprimand was given.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any part of the process, consult with your HR department or supervisor.
* Keep a copy of the reprimand form for your records, and submit the original to the relevant authorities.
