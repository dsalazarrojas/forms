# End Of Shift Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Shift Report Form is a crucial document that provides essential information about a shift, including employee name, shift duration, hours worked, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and data at hand.
2. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the shift start time in the "Shift Start Time" field.
4. Enter the shift end time in the "Shift End Time" field.
5. Enter the time you finished the shift in the "Date Shift End" field.
6. Report the hours you worked in the "Hours Worked" field.
7. If applicable, report your break time, break duration, and break frequency.
8. Select your work status and reason for unavailability (if any).
9. Enter any comments or notes in the "Comments" and "Manager Comments" fields.
10. Sign and date the form to confirm its completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or in your personnel file.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, required): Enter the time you started your shift.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, required): Enter the time you finished your shift.
* **Date Shift Started** (`date_shift_started`, date, required): Enter the date you started your shift.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required): Report the total number of hours worked during the shift.
* **Break Time** (`break_time`, number, optional): If you took a break, enter the duration of the break.
* **Break Frequency** (`break_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the break frequency from the options above.
* **Break Start Time** (`break_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you started your break.
* **Break End Time** (`break_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you finished your break.
* **Break Description** (`break_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your break.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current work status.
* **Reason Unavailable** (`reason_unavailable`, text, optional): If you were unavailable, provide a reason.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the shift.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or instructions from your manager.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm the accuracy of the information.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): Sign your manager's name to confirm the accuracy of the information.
* **Date Shift Ended** (`date_shift_end`, date, required): Enter the date the shift ended.
* **Time Shift Worked** (`time_shift_worked`, time, optional): Report the time you finished the shift.
* **Overtime Hours** (`overtime_hours`, number, optional): Report any overtime hours worked.
* **Notes Manager** (`notes_manager`, text, optional): Enter any notes from your manager.
