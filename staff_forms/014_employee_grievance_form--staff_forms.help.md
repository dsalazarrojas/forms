# Employee Grievance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees about grievances they have experienced while working at the organization. This will help the management team identify and address potential issues that may be affecting employee well-being or performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of your grievance in the "Issue Description" field.
5. If applicable, select a date for when the grievance occurred using the "Date Grievance Occurred" field.
6. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official records.
* **Job Title**: Enter your official job title within the organization.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing. This will help the management team understand the root cause of the problem.
* **Date Grievance Occurred**: If you are reporting a grievance that occurred on a specific date, please select it using the calendar picker. If it's ongoing or continuous, you can leave this field blank.
