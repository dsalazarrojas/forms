# Build Your Own Training Manual Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees create their own training manuals that are customized to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form in the following order:

1. Provide the title and description of the manual.
2. Select the department or team that the manual is for.
3. Choose the target audience for the manual.
4. Select the topics to include in the manual.
5. Decide on the manual's structure and format.
6. Determine the distribution method for the manual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Training manual title
* Training manual title: Enter a brief and descriptive title for your manual. This will be the title that appears on the cover of the manual.
### 2. Manual description
* Manual description: Provide a brief overview of the manual's purpose and content. This should be a summary of what the manual covers.
### 3. Department
* Department or team: Select the department or team that the manual is for. This will help you determine the content and tone of the manual.
### 4. Target audience
* -- Target Audience --: Choose the target audience for this manual. This will help you tailor the content and structure to their needs.
### 5. Select topics
* Select topics to include: Choose the topics that are relevant to the manual's purpose. You can select multiple topics from the list provided.
### 6. Manual format
* Manual format: Decide on the format of the manual, such as PDF, HTML, or printed booklet.
### 7. Color scheme
* Preferred color scheme: Select the color scheme for the manual. This can be black and white, the company's brand colors, or a custom scheme.
### 8. Include images and diagrams
* Include images and diagrams: Decide whether to include images and diagrams in the manual.
### 9. Target audience for manual
* Target audience for this manual: Choose the target audience for the manual. This should be the same as the target audience from question 4.
### 10. Is manual required reading?
* Is this manual required reading?: Determine whether this manual is required reading for the target audience.
### 11. Select additional topics
* Select additional topics: Choose any additional topics that are not on the list provided.
### 12. Manual structure
* -- Manual Structure --: Describe the structure of the manual, including any additional sections or chapters.
### 13. Table of contents
* Include table of contents?: Decide whether to include a table of contents in the manual.
### 14. Index included
* Include index?: Decide whether to include an index in the manual.
### 15. Glossary included
* Include glossary of terms?: Decide whether to include a glossary of terms in the manual.
### 16. Manual format
* Manual format: Choose the format for the manual, such as PDF or HTML.
### 17. Distribution method
* How should this manual be distributed?: Choose the distribution method for the manual, such as email or printed copies.

Note that some of the fields are not required, so feel free to fill out only what is necessary for your manual.
