# Worker Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Satisfaction Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from employees on their overall job satisfaction. It is designed to help management identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to create a better work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the link to access the form.
2. Select the page with the question that you feel most relevant to answer first.
3. For questions with scale-based answers (1-10), please respond with a number between 1 and 10.
4. For questions with text-based answers, please provide a brief description.
5. If a question is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel?** (`how_do_you_feel`, number, required: false):
	+ Please answer with a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Your work performance** (`work_performance`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your current work performance in a few words.
* **Your work load** (`work_load`, number, required: false):
	+ Answer with a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Your communication** (`communication`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your current level of communication with your colleagues and management.
* **Your collaboration** (`collaboration`, number, required: false):
	+ Answer with a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Your support** (`support`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe any support or resources you feel you are receiving from your colleagues or management.
* **Your work-life balance** (`work_life_balance`, number, required: false):
	+ Answer with a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Your job security** (`job_security`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe any concerns you have about your job security.
* **Your career development** (`career_development`, number, required: false):
	+ Answer with a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Your job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your overall satisfaction with your job and work environment.
