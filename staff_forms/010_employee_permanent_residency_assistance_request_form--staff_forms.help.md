# employee_permanent_residency_assistance_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_permanent_residency_assistance_request_form" is a form used by employees to request assistance with obtaining permanent residency in a specific location. It helps the team determine the assistance required and process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have answered all relevant questions.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have experienced issues with your permanent residency status.
3. Enter your employee name and number accurately.
4. Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your current status.
5. If applicable, enter the date you need assistance with and the duration of the assistance required.
6. Enter any additional information or notes about your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Permanent Residency** (`select_multiple`, id: 1): Please select "Yes" if you are experiencing issues with your permanent residency status, "No" otherwise.
* **Employee Name** (`text`, id: 2): Enter your full employee name as per your employee ID badge or personnel records.
* **Employee Number** (`text`, id: 3): Enter your employee number as per your employee ID badge or personnel records.
* **Employee Status** (`select_one`, id: 4): Select "Active" if you are currently employed with the company, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Date Prophets** (`date`, id: 5): Enter the date when you need assistance with your permanent residency status. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Prophets Time** (`time`, id: 6): Enter the duration of the assistance required. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Note** (`note`, id: 7): Enter any additional information or notes about your situation to help our team better understand your request.
