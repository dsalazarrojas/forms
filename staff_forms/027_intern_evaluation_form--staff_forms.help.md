# Intern Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback on an intern's performance during their tenure. It is a comprehensive tool to assess their work, behavior, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have not already completed an evaluation for the same intern during this period.
2. Answer all required fields (indicated by *) and provide clear, honest feedback.
3. For select_one fields, choose one of the provided options.
4. For date fields, enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter the first name of the intern being evaluated.

### Page 1: Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter the last name of the intern being evaluated.

### Page 1: Department
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Please select the department the intern worked under.

### Page 1: Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Please enter the job title of the intern's position.

### Page 1: Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date the intern started their work.

### Page 1: End Date
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date the intern ended their work.

### Page 1: Department
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Please select the department the intern worked under.

### Tips
* Be specific and provide concrete examples to help the intern understand areas of improvement.
* Use positive and constructive feedback to encourage growth and development.
* Submit the form promptly once completed to ensure timely feedback.
