# time_clock_exception_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The time_clock_exception_form is a tool for capturing and reporting exceptions related to time clocks or time-keeping systems within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start time of the exception in the "Start time" field.
2. Enter the end time of the exception in the "End time" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the exception in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the exception. This is the time when the exception occurred.
* **End time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the exception. This is the time when the exception ended.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the exception, explaining what happened and why it occurred.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your description, providing enough detail for reviewers to understand the exception.
* If you're unsure about an exception, ask a supervisor for guidance before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you're not reporting a duplicate exception, and review existing records for similar entries.
