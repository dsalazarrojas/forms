# New Hire Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect new hire information for HR and HR-related purposes. Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure proper onboarding and integration into the company's system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and understand its purpose.
2. Fill in all required fields (indicated by **required:** true).
3. Double-check your information for spelling and formatting errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your job title as it is recognized in the company's system.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Format as MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your personal email address. 
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal phone number.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work email address.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, `text`, required: false): Identify your direct supervisor.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department as it is recognized in the company's system.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Format as MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/02/2024).

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields or require further assistance, please contact your HR representative for support.
