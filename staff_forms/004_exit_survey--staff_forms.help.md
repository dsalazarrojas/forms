# Exit Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Exit Survey form is designed to collect information from employees regarding their departure from their current or last job. This form will help gather details about their reasons for leaving, last job specifics, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Please ensure you have all the required information before starting to complete the form**.
2.  Select all the relevant options that apply to you for each section.
3.  Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
4.  If a field is not relevant, you can leave it blank or provide a brief note explaining why it's not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Reasons for Leaving** (`reasons_for_leaving`): Please select all the reasons that apply to why you are leaving your current or last job.
*   **Last Day of Work** (`last_day_of_work`): Enter the date you last worked at your current or last job.
*   **Last Month of Work** (`last_month_of_work`): Select the month you last worked at your current or last job.
*   **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of your current or last manager.
*   **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`): Provide a brief explanation for why you are leaving your current or last job.
*   **Last Job Title** (`last_job_title`): Enter the job title you held at your current or last job.
*   **Last Job Classification** (`last_job_classification`): Enter the job classification you held at your current or last job.
*   **Last Job Department** (`last_job_department`): Enter the department you worked in at your current or last job.
*   **Job Classification** (`job_classification`): Select all the job classifications that apply to your current or last job.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your current or last job title.
*   **Job Level** (`job_level`): Select the level of your current or last job.
*   **Last Job's Manager's Name** (`manager_name_last_job`): Enter the name of your last manager.
*   **Last Position** (`last_position`): Enter your last position held.
*   **Reason for Leaving Last Job** (`reason_for_leaving_last_job`): Provide a brief explanation for why you left your last job.
*   **Last Organisation** (`last_organisation`): Enter the name of your last organisation or company.
*   **Notice Period** (`notice_period`): Select the notice period that applied to your last job.
*   **Notice Period Start Date** (`notice_period_start`): Enter the start date of your notice period.
*   **Notice Period End Date** (`notice_period_end`): Enter the end date of your notice period.
*   **Last Salary** (`last_salary`): Enter your last salary or annual remuneration.
*   **Last Job's Classification** (`job_classification_last_job`): Enter the classification of your last job.
*   **Last Job Type** (`last_job_type`): Enter the type of your last job.
*   **Reason For Leaving Last Job 2** (`reason_for_leaving_last_job_2`): Provide an additional reason for why you left your last job.
*   **Reason For Leaving 2** (`reason_for_leaving_2`): Provide an additional reason for why you are leaving your current job.
*   **Manager Name Last Job 2** (`manager_name_last_job_2`): Enter the name of your last manager.
*   **Last Organisation's Name** (`last_organisation_name`): Enter the name of your last organisation or company.
*   **Last Job's Level** (`job_level_last_job`): Select the level of your last job.

**Note:** If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please contact your HR department or management team for guidance.
