# Intern Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Survey form is designed to gather information from interns about their work experience, including the project title, start and end dates, feedback, employer's name, location, department, supervisor's name, contact email, and contact phone number.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Review the form fields

Each field is clearly labeled and explained in the following sections.

### Step 2: Fill Out the Form

1. **Survey Name (`intern_survey_name`): **Text**
   Enter the name of the project where you worked as an intern.

2. **Start Date (`start_date`): **Date**
   Enter the date when your internship started.

3. **End Date (`end_date`): **Date**
   Enter the date when your internship ended.

4. **Feedback (`feedback`): **Text**
   Share your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about your internship experience.

5. **Employer Name (`employer_name`): **Text**
   Enter the name of your company or organization's name where you worked as an intern.

6. **Employer Location (`employer_location`): **Text**
   Enter the location of your company or organization's name where you worked as an intern.

7. **Department (`department`): **Text**
   Enter the department where you worked as an intern.

8. **Supervisor Name (`supervisor_name`): **Text**
   Enter the name of your supervisor while working as an intern.

9. **Contact Email (`contact_email`): **Email**
   Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.

10. **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`): **Text**
   Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.

### Tips

- Fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Ensure you provide the correct dates for your internship start and end.
- Be honest and sincere in your feedback, as it will help improve our internship programs.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
