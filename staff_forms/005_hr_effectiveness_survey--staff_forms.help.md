# HR Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from staff members on the effectiveness of the HR department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to your experience with the HR department.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, type them in the "Survey Comments" field.
4. If you have any feedback for HR, type it in the "Employee Feedback" field.
5. Finally, select the date you would like to have this form submitted by.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Forms** (`staff_forms`, select_multiple, required: no): This field asks you to select whether you found the HR forms to be helpful or not. Select all the forms that apply to your experience.
* **Performance Assessment** (`performance_assessment`, select_one, required: no): This field asks you to assess whether the HR department has performed well on their performance.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, note, required: no): This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback on the HR department's performance.
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, date, required: no): This field is for providing feedback on the HR department's performance to specific employees.
* **HR Functional Assessment** (`hr_functional_assessment`, time, required: no): This field is for assessing the time spent on HR tasks.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask your HR representative for clarification.
* The HR department will review your feedback to understand their performance and make improvements.
