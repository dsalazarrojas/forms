# School Custodian Duties Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help school custodians report their daily tasks and duties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the answers to each question on the following pages.
* Each question is a separate field where you can provide detailed information about your daily tasks and duties.
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
