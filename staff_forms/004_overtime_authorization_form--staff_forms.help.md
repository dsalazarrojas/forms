# Overtime Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to capture and manage overtime requests from staff members.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the staff member's name in the **Staff Name** field.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the overtime period in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields, respectively.
3. Enter the total hours worked in the **Total Hours** field.
4. Select the overtime type from the **Overtime Type** dropdown menu (Regular, Double, or Triple).
5. Choose the manager's approval level from the **Manager Approval** dropdown menu (Manager, Team Lead, or Department Head).
6. Enter the department in the **Department** field, if applicable.
7. Enter comments or additional information in the **Comments** field, if necessary.
8. Once the form is completed, select the approved-by option for the manager's level from the **Approved By** dropdown menu (Manager, Team Lead, or Department Head).
9. Enter the date and time of approval in the **Approved Date** and **Approved Time** fields, respectively.
10. Enter the total hours approved in the **Total Hours Approved** field.
11. Select the overtime status from the **Overtime Status** dropdown menu (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
12. Enter overtime notes in the **Overtime Notes** field, if necessary.
13. Finally, enter manager comments in the **Manager Comments** field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Name**: Enter the staff member's name, as it appears on their identification card or personnel documents.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the overtime period, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the overtime period, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Total Hours**: Enter the total hours worked during the overtime period.
* **Job Title**: Enter the staff member's job title.
* **Project Number**: Enter the project number associated with the overtime request.
* **Overtime Type**: Select the type of overtime from the dropdown menu (Regular, Double, or Triple).
* **Manager Approval**: Choose the manager's approval level from the dropdown menu (Manager, Team Lead, or Department Head).
* **Department**: Enter the department the staff member works in, if applicable.
* **Comments**: Enter comments or additional information about the overtime request, if necessary.
* **Approved By**: Select the manager's approval level from the dropdown menu (Manager, Team Lead, or Department Head).
* **Approved Date**: Enter the date of approval.
* **Approved Time**: Enter the time of approval.
* **Total Hours Approved**: Enter the total hours approved.
* **Overtime Status**: Select the status of the overtime request from the dropdown menu (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
* **Overtime Notes**: Enter notes about the overtime request, if necessary.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter comments from the manager, if necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth approval process.
* If a staff member is not listed in the **Approved By** field, please contact HR or the system administrator for assistance.
