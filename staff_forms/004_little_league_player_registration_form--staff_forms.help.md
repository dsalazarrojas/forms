# Little League Player Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Little League Player Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians about their child's participation in a Little League program. It is crucial to complete this form accurately and thoroughly, as it will help the league administrators to provide proper services and support to the player.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the correct options for the player's position, division, and league type.
3. Add any necessary medical conditions or notes.
4. Obtain signatures from the player, parent, and relevant personnel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter the player's name as it appears on their official documents (e.g., birth certificate).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's name who is responsible for the player's care and supervision.
* **Date of Birth** (`player_dob`, date, required): Enter the player's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter the parent's or guardian's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the parent's or guardian's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any medical conditions that the player is currently experiencing or has experienced in the past.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, required): Add any relevant medical notes or comments.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, select_one, required): Select the player's position on the team (e.g., Pitcher, Catcher, etc.).
* **Division** (`division`, select_one, required): Select the division the player will be playing in (e.g., American, National, etc.).
* **League Type** (`league_type`, select_one, required): Select the league type the player will be playing in (e.g., Spring Baseball, Winter Softball, etc.).
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team the player will be playing for.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, text, required): Obtain permission from the parent or guardian for the player's participation.
* **Emergency Permission** (`emergency_permission`, text, required): Obtain permission from the emergency contact person for the player's participation.
* **Coach Permission** (`coach_permission`, text, required): Obtain permission from the coach for the player's participation.
* **Medical Admin Permission** (`medical_admin_permission`, text, required): Obtain permission from the medical administrator for the player's participation.
* **Player Signature** (`player_signature`, text, required): The player must sign the form to confirm their agreement to participate.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): The parent or guardian must sign the form to confirm their agreement to participate.
* **Coach Signature** (`coach_signature`, text, required): The coach must sign the form to confirm their agreement to supervise the player.
* **Medical Admin Signature** (`medical_admin_signature`, text, required): The medical administrator must sign the form to confirm their agreement to provide medical clearance.
* **Emergency Contact Permission** (`emergency_contact_permission`, text, required): Obtain permission from the emergency contact person for the player's participation.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, text, required): The emergency contact person must sign the form to confirm their agreement to provide emergency contact information.
* **Team Manager** (`team_manager`, text, required): Enter the name of the team manager or contact person.
