# exit_interview_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees who are leaving the company. The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and insights from departing employees to help the organization improve its processes and policies. Please only complete this form if you are an employee who is officially leaving the company. If you are not, please do not fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by reading the entire form and understanding the questions we are asking.
2. Provide honest and accurate information about your experience and reasons for leaving the company.
3. Fill out each field carefully and thoroughly.
4. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome** (`welcome`, note, required: false): This is a welcome note to get you started with the form.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the company you are leaving.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required: false): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): The department you are leaving.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, required: false): How many years you have been with the company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Your job title while working with the company.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required: false): Please explain your reason(s) for leaving the company in a few sentences.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please include them in the "Reason for Leaving" field.
* If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to ask for help.
