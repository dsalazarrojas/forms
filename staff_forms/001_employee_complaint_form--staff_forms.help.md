# Employee Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Complaint Form is a document used to report employee complaints, which can include harassment, bullying, or any other grievances that affect work performance or employee well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please review the form carefully before submitting your complaint. Make sure you have provided all the necessary information to help the HR representative understand the situation.
2.  Complete the form honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible about your complaint.
3.  Select the correct options for the "reported_by" fields, indicating whether you or the person you are reporting is responsible for the complaint.
4.  Choose the correct complaint type and status to describe the issue and its current status.
5.  Provide any additional notes or comments to support your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Complaint Form**: This is the form title, which should not be altered.
* **reported_by**: Indicate whether this complaint is about you or reported by someone else.
*   *   **Yes** - This complaint is about you or your work.
*   *   **No** - This complaint is about someone else or their work.
* **complaint_description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue or concern you are reporting.
* **complaint_type**: Select the type of complaint that best describes the issue.
*   *   **Yes** - This is a complaint about harassment or bullying.
*   *   **No** - This is not a complaint about harassment or bullying.
*   *   Other (Specify): If the issue doesn't fit into the above categories, please specify it here.
* **complaint_date**: Enter the date the issue occurred or started.
* **complaint_time**: Enter the time the issue occurred or started.
* **reported_employees**: Indicate whether the complaint is about one or more employees.
*   *   **Yes** - This complaint is about one or more employees.
*   *   **No** - This complaint is not about employees.
* **reported_by_job_title**: Select the job title of the person you are reporting.
*   *   **Yes** - This complaint is about this job title.
*   *   **No** - This complaint is not about this job title.
* **reported_by_department**: Select the department of the person you are reporting.
*   *   **Yes** - This complaint is about this department.
*   *   **No** - This complaint is not about this department.
* **reported_to_job_title**: Select the job title of the person you are reporting to.
*   *   **Yes** - This complaint is about this job title.
*   *   **No** - This complaint is not about this job title.
* **reported_to_department**: Select the department of the person you are reporting to.
*   *   **Yes** - This complaint is about this department.
*   *   **No** - This complaint is not about this department.
* **reported_by_supervisor**: Indicate whether the complaint is about a supervisor.
*   *   **Yes** - This complaint is about a supervisor.
*   *   **No** - This complaint is not about a supervisor.
* **reported_to_supervisor**: Indicate whether the complaint is about a supervisor.
*   *   **Yes** - This complaint is about a supervisor.
*   *   **No** - This complaint is not about a supervisor.
* **complaint_status**: Select the status of the complaint.
*   *   **Active** - This complaint is active and being addressed.
*   *   **Inactive** - This complaint is inactive and not being addressed.
* **notes**: Provide any additional information or comments to support your complaint.

## Tips
*   If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact the HR representative or your supervisor for guidance.
*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or mistakes.
*   Provide as much detail as possible in the complaint description to help address the issue effectively.
*   Keep the complaint description concise and focused on the issue at hand.
