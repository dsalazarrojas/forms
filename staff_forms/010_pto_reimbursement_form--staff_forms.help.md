# PTO Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and track PTO (Paid Time Off) reimbursements from employees. It is used by HR to manage and process employee PTO requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your PTO Request: In the "PTO Request" field, briefly describe the reason for your PTO request.
2. Select Start and End Dates: Enter your start and end dates for your PTO period.
3. Enter Reason for Leave: In the "Reason" field, provide a detailed explanation for your PTO request.
4. Enter Reimbursement Amount: Enter the amount of reimbursement you are requesting.
5. Select Status: Choose the current status of your PTO request from the options provided.
6. Add Comments (Optional): If you have any additional comments or information, you can add them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **PTO Request** (`pto_request`, text, optional): A brief description of your PTO request.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): The start date of your PTO period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): The end date of your PTO period.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): A detailed explanation for your PTO request.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): The amount of reimbursement you are requesting.
* **Status** (`status`, select one, optional): The current status of your PTO request.
  * Options: Pending, Approved, Reimbursed
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or information about your PTO request.
