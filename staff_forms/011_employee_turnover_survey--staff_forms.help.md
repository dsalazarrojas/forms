# Employee Turnover Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Turnover Survey is designed to collect feedback from employees who are leaving or have left the company. The goal is to gather data on reasons for turnover, job satisfaction, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. Provide detailed answers for text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee supervisor rating** (1): Rate your supervisor's leadership and guidance.
* **Manager Rating** (2): Rate your manager's performance.
* **Turnover reason** (3): Describe the reason you are leaving or have left the company.
* **Job satisfaction** (4): Rate your satisfaction with your current job.
* **Job performance** (5): Rate your performance in your current job.
