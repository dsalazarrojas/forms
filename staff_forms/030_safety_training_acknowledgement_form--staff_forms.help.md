# Safety Training Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the acknowledgement of safety training received by employees. Completing this form ensures that employees understand their safety training responsibilities and acknowledges receipt of proper safety procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct option for how you received safety training from the dropdown menu on page 1.
2. Enter the date you received safety training on page 2.
3. Enter your Employee name on page 3.
4. Select your Department from the dropdown menu on page 4 (optional).
5. Enter your Manager's name on page 3.
6. Enter your Job title on page 3.
7. Indicate on page 7 whether you have completed the safety training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I have received safety training** (`select_one`, required):
	+ This field is used to select how you received your safety training. Choose from the options: 
		+ Safety Training Video
		+ Safety Training Manual
		+ Safety Training Session
* **Date received safety training** (`date`, required):
	+ Enter the date you received safety training. This should be the actual date, not the expected date.
* **Employee name** (`text`, required):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Department** (`select_one`, optional):
	+ If applicable, select your department from the list to help us understand your role within the organization.
	+ You can leave this field blank if you're not affiliated with any department.
* **Manager name** (`text`, required):
	+ Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Job title** (`text`, required):
	+ Enter your official job title as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Completed safety training** (`select_one`, required):
	+ Indicate whether you have completed the safety training by selecting either `True` or `False`.
