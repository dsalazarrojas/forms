## Employee Payroll Change Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document changes to an employee's payroll information. It is intended for use by HR personnel to track and verify changes to employee pay rates, dates of change, reasons for change, assigned personnel, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the change before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as such in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.
3. Use the "assigned_to" field to select the personnel responsible for the change.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.
5. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Employee Payroll Change Form** (employee_payroll_change_form)
This is the title of the form, which should be filled in for reference and tracking purposes only.

### 2. **Pay Rate** (pay_rate)
*Type: Number*
*Required: No*
Enter the new pay rate for the employee. This should be a numerical value.

### 3. **Date of Change** (date_of_change)
*Type: Date*
*Required: No*
Enter the date when the pay rate change took effect.

### 4. **Reason for Change** (reason_for_change)
*Type: Text*
*Required: No*
Explain why the pay rate change is being made.

### 5. **Assigned To** (assigned_to)
*Type: Select One*
*Required: Yes*
Select the personnel responsible for the pay rate change from the available options.

### 6. **Notes** (notes)
*Type: Note*
*Required: No*
Provide any additional comments or notes about the pay rate change.

### 7. **Email Address** (email_address)
*Type: Email*
*Required: No*
Enter the employee's email address, if applicable.

### 8. **Phone Number** (phone_number)
*Type: Text*
*Required: No*
Enter the employee's phone number, if applicable.

### 9. **Employee Status** (employee_status)
*Type: Select Multiple*
*Required: Yes*
Select all applicable employee statuses that are affected by the pay rate change.
