# Multi Page Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multi Page Employee Evaluation Form is designed to help managers and supervisors assess employee performance, track progress towards goals, and provide feedback. This form is divided into multiple pages, each covering different aspects of employee evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing employee details on page 1, including employee contact information.
2. On page 2, enter the evaluator's comments and the employee's expected goals.
3. On page 3, rate the employee's performance level, set objectives, and select the overall performance and employee status.
4. On page 4, enter the job position, evaluation method, and evaluation comments.
5. On the final page, rate the employee's performance, goals status, job title, supervisor name, department, team members, reporting line, and work location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_details** (text): Enter the employee's details, including their name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **contact_details** (text): Enter the employee's contact information, including phone number and address.
* **evaluator_comments** (text): Enter comments from the evaluator about the employee's performance.
* **expected_goals** (text): Enter the employee's expected goals and objectives.
* **employee_comments** (text): Enter any comments from the employee about their performance.
* **performance_level** (select_one): Select the employee's performance level from Excellent, Needs Improvement, Good, or Poor.
* **performance_objectives** (text): Enter the employee's performance objectives.
* **evaluation_date** (date): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **next_evaluation_date** (date): Enter the next evaluation date.
* **overall_performance** (select_one): Select the employee's overall performance from Excellent, Needs Improvement, Good, or Poor.
* **employee_status** (select_one): Select the employee's status from Active, Inactive, or Terminated.
* **job_position** (text): Enter the employee's job position.
* **evaluation_status** (select_one): Select the evaluation status from Completed, Not Started, or In Progress.
* **evaluation_by** (text): Enter who conducted the evaluation.
* **performance_rating** (number): Enter the employee's performance rating.
* **goals_status** (select_one): Select the status of the employee's goals from Met, Not Met, or In Progress.
* **job_title** (text): Enter the employee's job title.
* **supervisor_name** (text): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **department** (text): Enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **team_members** (text): Enter the team members the employee is part of.
* **reporting_line** (text): Enter the employee's reporting line.
* **work_location** (text): Enter the employee's work location.
* **evaluation_method** (text): Enter the method used for evaluation.
* **evaluation_comments** (text): Enter comments about the evaluation.
* **evaluation_status_comments** (text): Enter comments about the evaluation status.
