# Disciplinary Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a disciplinary action taken against an employee, including the incident description, policy violated, action taken, and outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each disciplinary action incident separately.
2. Provide the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the incident in the "Date" field (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
4. Describe the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
5. Select the relevant policy that was violated in the "Policy Violation" field.
6. Enter the action taken in the "Action Taken" field.
7. Enter the date the action was taken in the "Date of Action" field.
8. Check if the employee has been terminated in the "Employee Terminated" field.
9. Provide the termination reason in the "Termination Reason" field (if applicable).
10. Enter the supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
11. Enter the supervisor's title in the "Supervisor Title" field.
12. Select who took the action in the "Action Taken By" field.
13. Add any additional comments about the disciplinary action in the "Disciplinary Action Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name affected by the disciplinary action.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Describe the incident in detail.
* **Policy Violation** (`policy_violation`, select_one, required): Select the relevant policy that was violated.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, required): Describe the action taken against the employee.
* **Date of Action** (`date_of_action`, date, required): Enter the date the action was taken in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Employee Terminated** (`employee_terminated`, select_one, required): Check if the employee has been terminated.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, text, optional): Provide the reason for termination (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the supervisor's name involved in the disciplinary action.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, required): Enter the supervisor's title.
* **Action Taken By** (`action_taken_by`, select_one, required): Select who took the action (Supervisor, HR, Management, or Other).
* **Disciplinary Action Comments** (`disciplinary_action_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments about the disciplinary action.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the supervisor's name involved in the disciplinary action.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, required): Enter the supervisor's title.
* **Employee Terminated** (`employee_terminated`, select_one, required): Check if the employee has been terminated.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, text, optional): Provide the reason for termination (if applicable).
