# Identity Badge Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Identity Badge Request Form is used to request a new identity badge for employees who need one. This form helps ensure that all badge requests are processed efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name as it appears on your ID.
2. Enter your employee number (if known).
3. Select the type of badge you need.
4. Choose the reason for your badge request.
5. Select your department.
6. Enter your supervisor's name.
7. Enter your badge number (if known).
8. Select the shifts you will be working.
9. Enter your employee ID number (if known).
10. Select the status of your badge.
11. Enter your department name (if known).
12. Select the badge expiration date (if known).
13. Enter the badge issuance date (if known).
14. Select the tool or equipment assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, number, optional): If you know your employee number, enter it here to help us process your request efficiently.
* **Badge Type** (`badge_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of badge you need (e.g., Badge Type 1, Badge Type 2, Badge Type 3).
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for your badge request.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select your department (e.g., Department 1, Department 2, Department 3).
* **Supervisor's Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Badge Number** (`badge_number`, number, optional): If you have a badge number, enter it here.
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the shifts you will be working (e.g., Shift 1, Shift 2, Shift 3).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Badge Status** (`badge_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your badge (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Badge Expiration** (`badge_expiration`, date, optional): Enter the badge expiration date.
* **Badge Issuance Date** (`badge_issuance_date`, date, optional): Enter the badge issuance date.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool or equipment assigned to you.
