# employee_safety_performance_review_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The employee_safety_performance_review_form is a safety performance review form used to assess an employee's behavior and response in safety-related incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please enter the necessary information in each field. All fields are optional unless marked as required.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant personnel for assistance.

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the employee's last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Please enter the employee's email address.
* **Department** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's department.
* **Manager** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee's manager.

## Tips
