# Tuition Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tuition Reimbursement Form is used to request reimbursement for tuition expenses. This form should be completed by employees who need to request reimbursement for educational expenses related to their job duties or career development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Employee Information, including your name and ID number.
2. Describe your Reimbursement Request, including the purpose of the reimbursement.
3. Indicate whether you are requesting reimbursement for 'Yes' or 'No'.
4. Enter the Date of Service, which is the date you completed the course or training.
5. Enter the total Reimbursement Amount.
6. Provide a brief Reimbursement Reason, explaining why you are requesting reimbursement.
7. Sign and date the form as the Employee.
8. Your Department Manager should sign and date the form to confirm approval.
9. If your Department Manager is not present, the Department Manager should sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information (1)**: Enter your name and ID number.
  - **Reimbursement Request (2)**: Describe the purpose of the reimbursement request.
  - **Reimbursement Details (3)**: Select 'Yes' if you are requesting reimbursement, otherwise select 'No'.
  - **Date of Service (4)**: Enter the date you completed the course or training.
  - **Reimbursement Amount (5)**: Enter the total reimbursement amount.
  - **Reimbursement Reason (6)**: Provide a brief reason for the reimbursement.
  - **Employee Signature (7)**: Sign and date to confirm you filled out this form truthfully.
  - **Department Manager Signature (8)**: Your Department Manager's signature and date to confirm approval.
  - **Department Manager Name (10)**: Your Department Manager's name.
  - **Department Manager Title (11)**: Your Department Manager's title.
- **Manager Signature (8)** and **Manager Name (10)** and **Manager Title (11)**: If your Department Manager is not available, please sign and date as the Department Head of the team.
