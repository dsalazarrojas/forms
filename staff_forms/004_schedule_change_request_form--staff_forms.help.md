# Schedule Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Schedule Change Request Form is used to request changes to a student's schedule. This form is completed by staff members to provide relevant information for processing and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Student ID.
2. Provide the Reason for Change in the dedicated text field.
3. Select the Date and Time of the scheduled change.
4. Choose the new Start and End Time for the scheduled change.
5. Enter any additional comments from the teacher.
6. Select the status for Teacher Approval and the assigned tool.
7. Optionally, fill in the status of Staff Approval and the date and time of staff approval.
8. Finally, select the status of Staff Approval and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Please fill in your first name as it appears on your staff ID or badge.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Please fill in your last name as it appears on your staff ID or badge.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, Text, Required): Please enter the ID number of the student whose schedule you are requesting a change for.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, Text, Required): Please describe the reason for the schedule change request.
* **Date of Change** (`date_of_change`, Date, Required): Choose the date of the scheduled change.
* **Time of Change** (`time_of_change`, Time, Required): Choose the time of the scheduled change.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Required): Choose the start time of the scheduled change.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, Required): Choose the end time of the scheduled change.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, Text, Required): Enter any comments from the teacher related to the schedule change request.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, Select One, Required): Select the status for Teacher Approval and the assigned tool.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Select One, Required): Choose the tool assigned to the student for the scheduled change.
* **Teacher Approval Status** (`teacher_approval_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): Enter the status of Teacher Approval. If approved, please select the corresponding option.
* **Staff Approval Date** (`staff_approval_date`, Date, Optional): If approved, please enter the date of staff approval.
* **Staff Approval Time** (`staff_approval_time`, Time, Optional): If approved, please enter the time of staff approval.
* **Staff Approval Status** (`staff_approval_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): Enter the status of Staff Approval. If approved, please select the corresponding option.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, Date, Optional): The date and time you submitted the form.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, Time, Optional): The time you submitted the form.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
