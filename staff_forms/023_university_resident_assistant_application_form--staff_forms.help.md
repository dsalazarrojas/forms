# University Resident Assistant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from staff members applying for the University Resident Assistant position. The application process helps us to evaluate each candidate's background and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a successful application, please follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in your personal details.
2. Answer the required questions to the best of your ability.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
4. Make sure to include all emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, text, optional): Write a brief introduction about yourself, mentioning any relevant personal details that would make you a suitable candidate for this position.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your permanent address.
* **Contact Telephone** (`contact_telephone`, text, optional): Enter your contact telephone number.
* **Contact Telephone Extension** (`contact_telephone_extension`, number, optional): If your contact telephone number has an extension, please enter it here.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to 3 emergency contacts.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current employment status.
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Select your relevant educational background.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, optional): Enter your start date of employment if applicable.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, optional): Enter your end date of employment if applicable.
* **Employment Duration** (`employment_duration`, number, optional): If you have been employed elsewhere, please enter the length of your employment in weeks.
* **Work Hours Per Week** (`work_hours_per_week`, number, optional): Enter your average weekly work hours.
* **Position** (`position`, select_one, optional): Choose your current or desired position.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select any departments you have worked with or would be interested in.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Select any roles you have held or would be interested in.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any skills you possess or would like to acquire for this position.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant work or academic experiences.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, optional): Enter your availability in the format of 24-hour clock.
* **Availability End** (`availability_end`, time, optional): Enter the time when you are no longer available.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
