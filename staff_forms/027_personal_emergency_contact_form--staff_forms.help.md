# personal_emergency_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather emergency contact information from users in case of an unexpected situation or emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Complete each page of the form, starting from the first emergency contact.
3. Enter the name, phone number, and email address for each emergency contact.
4. Select the relationship type for each emergency contact (Friend, Family Member, or Coworker).
5. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
6. Repeat steps 2-5 until all emergency contacts are listed.
7. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact 1, 2, 3, 4, 5** (<type> text)
    * Enter the name, phone number, and email address of each emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (<type> select one)
    * Select the relationship type (Friend, Family Member, or Coworker) for each emergency contact.
* **Additional Notes** (<type> text)
    * Enter any additional information that you want to provide about your emergency contacts.
* **Assigned to** (<type> select one)
    * Select the contact person who will be assigned to handle any emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact 6** (<type> text)
    * Enter the name, phone number, and email address of the sixth emergency contact, if needed.
