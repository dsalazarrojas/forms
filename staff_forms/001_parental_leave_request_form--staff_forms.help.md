# Parental Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Parental Leave Request Form, which is used to capture information for an employee's parental leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID to identify yourself as the employee requesting leave.
2. Provide the start date of your leave.
3. Provide the end date of your leave.
4. Specify the reason for your leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (employee_id, Number, Required: false):
  Your Employee ID is a unique identifier used to track your employment status and records. Enter your ID to ensure your leave request is associated with your personnel files.
* **Leave Start Date** (leave_start_date, Date, Required: false):
  The date from which you plan to begin your parental leave. Enter the date in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Leave End Date** (leave_end_date, Date, Required: false):
  The date on which you plan to end your parental leave. Enter the date in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Reason for Leave** (reason_for_leave, Text, Required: false):
  Please provide a brief explanation for your leave request, including the reason you are taking parental leave. This will help the HR team assess and process your request efficiently.
