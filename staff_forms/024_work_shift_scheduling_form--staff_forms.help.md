# Work Shift Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's work shift schedule, including start and end times, day of the week, and availability.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the start and end times for your work shift.
2.  Choose the days of the week you are available to work.
3.  Indicate if you are available to work on weekends.
4.  Confirm your availability to work with other team members.
5.  Enter the number of hours you are available to work.
6.  Select your availability during the day (morning, afternoon, evening, or overnight).
7.  Add any additional notes about your work shift schedule.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Week Starts** (`week_start`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when your work shift starts.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of your work shift.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of your work shift.
*   **Day** (`day`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the days of the week you are available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are an employee.
*   **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are available to work with team members.
*   **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours you are available to work.
*   **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your availability during the day (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Overnight).
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your work shift schedule.
