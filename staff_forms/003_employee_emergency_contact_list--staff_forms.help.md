# Employee Emergency Contact List - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Emergency Contact List form is a tool to collect and store information about the emergency contacts of employees. This information is used internally for contact and notification purposes in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your first emergency contact.
2. Choose their relationship to you (e.g., Spouse, Parent, Brother, etc.).
3. Enter their phone number.
4. Enter their email address.
5. Repeat steps 1-4 for each additional emergency contact you need to add.
6. Enter any additional information you want to add (e.g., comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency_contact_name_1_label** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your first emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_name_2_label** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your second emergency contact. This field is not required.
* **emergency_contact_relationship_1_label** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the relationship between you and this emergency contact. Options include: Emergency Contact 1, Emergency Contact 2, Emergency Contact 3.
* **emergency_contact_phone_1_label** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_email_1_label** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **employee_emergency_contact_list_form_2_label** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information you want to add about your emergency contact. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each emergency contact.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only and should not be shared with external parties.
