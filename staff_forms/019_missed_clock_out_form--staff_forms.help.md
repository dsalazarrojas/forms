# Missed Clock Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Missed Clock Out Form is a tool for staff members to report instances where they have missed clocking out on time. This form helps staff members to accurately record their missed clock out occurrences and provides managers with the necessary information to address any issues related to time management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the staff member who missed clocking out on time.
2. Provide a brief reason for the missed clock out.
3. Enter the duration of time missed.
4. Enter the date of the missed clock out.
5. (Optional) If applicable, provide details about any incentives or deductions that may be associated with the missed clock out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Member**: Enter the name of the staff member who missed clocking out on time.
* **Reason for Missed Clock Out**: Describe the reason why the staff member missed clocking out on time. This could be due to personal reasons, meetings, or other work-related tasks.
* **Missed Clock Out Duration**: Enter the duration of time that the staff member was late clocking out.
* **Missed Clock Out Date**: Enter the date of the missed clock out.
* **Missed Clock Out Incentives/Deductions**: If applicable, provide details about any incentives or deductions that may be associated with the missed clock out.
* **Manager's Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the manager to address the missed clock out.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields.
- Double-check the entered information for accuracy.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your manager for clarification.
