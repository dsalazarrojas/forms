# Unpaid Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify the organization of an unpaid leave of absence. Please fill out the form below to provide necessary information for processing your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Start Date" field and select the date you would like your leave of absence to begin.
2.  Click on the "End Date" field and select the date you would like your leave of absence to end.
3.  Select the reason for your request from the provided options (Leave of Absence, Medical, Family, or Other).
4.  Enter the number of days you would like to take for your unpaid leave of absence.
5.  Answer the "Staff ID" question by selecting "Yes" if you are a staff member and "No" if you are not.
6.  Answer the "Staff Name" question by selecting "Yes" if you are a staff member and "No" if you are not.
7.  Select your department from the provided options.
8.  Select your job title from the provided options.
9.  Enter your start time and end time for your leave of absence.
10.  Select the type of leave you would like to take (Yes or No).
11.  Select the status of your leave (Active or Inactive).
12.  Please review your completed form and make sure all required information is accurate before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like your leave of absence to begin.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like your leave of absence to end.
*   **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, required: false): Select the reason for your request from the provided options (Leave of Absence, Medical, Family, or Other).
*   **Number of Days** (`number_of_days`, required: false): Enter the number of days you would like to take for your unpaid leave of absence.
*   **Staff ID** (`staff_id`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you are a staff member and "No" if you are not.
*   **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you are a staff member and "No" if you are not.
*   **Department** (`department`, required: false): Select your department from the provided options.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, required: false): Select your job title from the provided options.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, required: false): Enter your start time for your leave of absence.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, required: false): Enter your end time for your leave of absence.
*   **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, required: false): Select the type of leave you would like to take (Yes or No).
*   **Leave Status** (`leave_status`, required: false): Select the status of your leave (Active or Inactive).
 
## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for assistance.
*   This form is for staff members only, so please answer "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
*   Please select the correct department and job title to ensure accurate processing of your request.
