# Request For Salary Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a request to change your salary. This form is for employees who wish to request a salary change and need to provide necessary information to support their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is required.
2.  Fill in your name, job title, and staff ID.
3.  Enter your current salary and the proposed salary you are requesting.
4.  Provide a brief reason for your salary change request.
5.  Sign and date the form once you have completed it.
6.  Submit the form to the HR department for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (id: 2)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Job Title (id: 3)**: Please enter your current job title.
* **Salary (id: 4)**: Enter your current salary.
* **Reason For Change (id: 5)**: Please explain why you are requesting a salary change. This should include any relevant details about your role, performance, or industry standards.
* **Proposed Salary (id: 6)**: Enter the salary you are requesting.
* **Signature Date (id: 7)**: Please sign and date the form once you have completed it.
* **Staff ID (id: 8)**: Please enter your staff ID number. This is a unique identifier for you within the company.
