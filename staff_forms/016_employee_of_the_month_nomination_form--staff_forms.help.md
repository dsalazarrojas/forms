# Employee Of The Month Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate an employee for employee of the month recognition. Please use it to provide a brief description of the employee's work performance or contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid reason for the nomination.
2. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the employee's months of service.
4. Write a brief description of the reason for the nomination in the "Nomination Reason" field.
5. Choose the date of the nomination in the "Nomination Date" field.
6. Select "Yes" in the "Employee of the Month Nomination" field.
7. Optionally, enter your name as the person nominating in the "Nominated By" field.
8. Finally, select the "Selected Date" field with the date you want the nomination to be effective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
* **Months of Service** (number): Enter the number of months the employee has been with the company.
* **Nomination Reason** (text): Explain why you are nominating the employee. Keep it brief and concise.
* **Nomination Date** (date): Enter the date you are nominating the employee.
* **Nominated By** (text): Enter your name as the person nominating the employee (optional).
* **Employee of the Month Nomination** (select one): Check "Yes" to confirm the nomination.
* **Selected Date** (date): Select the date you want the nomination to be effective.

## Tips
- Please make sure to only nominate employees once per month.
- You can nominate any colleague who meets the criteria.
- Keep the nomination reason concise and clear.
- You can select any date within the current or future month.
