# Exit Interview Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from departing employees to understand their reasons for leaving and their new job details. It helps the HR team to identify trends and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each field carefully and provide accurate information as prompted.
* Enter your manager's name and job title as they appear on your last working day.
* Provide the date you left the company.
* Clearly explain your reasons for leaving.
* Enter your new job title, new company, new manager's name, and new position.
* Confirm the currency of your new salary.
* Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager as they appear on your last working day.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your last working day.
* **Date Left**: Enter the date you left the company.
* **Reasons Leaving**: Explain your reasons for leaving the company.
* **New Job Title**: Enter your new job title.
* **New Company**: Enter the name of your new company.
* **New Manager Name**: Enter the name of your new manager.
* **New Position**: Enter your new position.
* **New Salary**: Enter your new salary.
* **New Salary Currency**: Select the currency of your new salary.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate and truthful information to help us understand your reasons for leaving.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact your HR representative for assistance.
