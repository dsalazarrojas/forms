# Holiday Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to document and track holiday releases for students, capturing essential information about the student's name, parent's contact information, date of release, reason for release, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

- Begin by filling in your student's name.
- Enter your parent or guardian's name.
- Provide your parent or guardian's contact phone number.
- Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
- Select the date you are releasing your child from school.
- Provide a reason for the release (e.g., family vacation, personal reasons, etc.).
- Sign the form with your parent or guardian's name.
- Sign the form with your student's name, indicating your agreement with the release terms.
- Optionally, select the staff member assigned to your child for the release.
- Confirm whether the form was submitted or not.
- Select the date and time the form was submitted.
- Enter the staff member assigned to your child for the release.
- Confirm who submitted the form.
- Select the date and time the form was approved or denied.
- Select the status of the approval (Approved or Denied).
- Provide any comments or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Release Form** (`holiday_release_form_title`, required): This is the title of the form, which should be filled in with your child's name.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, required): Enter your child's name as it appears on their school record.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, required): Enter your full name, including your title (Mr./Ms./Mrs./Dr., etc.).
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, required): Enter your contact phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, required): Enter your email address in the format [yourname@email.com](mailto:yourname@email.com).
* **Date of Release** (`date_of_release`, required): Select the date you are releasing your child from school.
* **Time of Release** (`time_of_release`, optional): Select the time of release if applicable.
* **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, required): Provide a brief reason for the release (e.g., family vacation, personal reasons, etc.).
* **Signature of Parent** (`signature_of_parent`, required): Sign the form with your full name.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, required): Sign the form with your full name, indicating your agreement with the release terms.
* **Date** (`student_signature_date`, required): Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Note** (`student_signature_note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information related to the release.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, required): Select the staff member assigned to your child for the release (e.g., ChatJimmy, JohnDoe, JaneDoe, etc.).
* **Other** (`assigned_user_other`, optional): If the assigned user is not on the list, enter the name of the assigned user.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, optional): Confirm whether the form was submitted or not.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, optional): Confirm who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_at`, optional): Select the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_user_at`, optional): Enter the staff member assigned to your child for the release.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, optional): Select the date and time the form was approved or denied.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, optional): Select who approved or denied the form.
* **Approved Status** (`approved_status`, optional): Select the approval status of the form (Approved or Denied).
* **Approved By Status** (`approved_by_status`, optional): Enter any comments or additional information related to the approval status.
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information related to the form.
