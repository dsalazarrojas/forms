# Payroll Deduction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Deduction Form is used to capture information about the types of payroll deductions an employee wants to enroll in or change. This includes the name of the employee, the type of deduction, the amount, and other relevant details. The form helps employees manage their payroll deductions efficiently and ensures that their benefits and plans are up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Payroll Deduction Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Employee Name** in the corresponding field.
2. Select the **Deduction Item** you want to enroll in or change.
3. Enter the **Amount** you want to deduct.
4. Choose the **Frequency** of the deduction (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
5. Select whether the deduction is **Active** or **Inactive**.
6. Enter the **Start Date** when you want the deduction to begin.
7. Enter the **End Date** when you want the deduction to end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel file.
* **Deduction Item** (`deduction_item`, text, required): Select the type of deduction you want to enroll in or change, such as health insurance or retirement plan.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you want to deduct from your paycheck.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you want the deduction to occur (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Active** (`active`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the deduction is currently active or inactive.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you want the deduction to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you want the deduction to end.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your benefits and payroll policies before filling out this form.
* Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
* This form is only for payroll deductions related to employee benefits and plans. Do not use this form for other types of deductions or payments.
