# Verbal Warning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Verbal Warning Form is used to document and track verbal warnings issued to employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's details in the "Employee" field.
2. Select the date of the verbal warning in the "Employee Date" field.
3. Select the manager's number from the options provided.
4. Enter the reason for the reprimand in the "Reason for Reprimand" field.
5. Enter any additional details about the verbal warning in the "Details" field.
6. Select the outcome of the verbal warning from the options provided in the "Outcome" field.
7. Add any comments regarding the verbal warning in the "Comments" field.
8. Select whether the form was submitted by the manager or not.
9. If submitted by the manager, enter their name, title, email, and phone number in the relevant fields.
10. Select the current status of the form from the options provided in the "Status" field.
11. Enter a description of the status in the "Status Description" field.
12. Add any comments about the status in the "Status Comments" field.
13. Select whether the form is closed or not.
14. If closed, enter a description of the reason for closure in the "Closed Description" field.
15. Add any comments about the closure in the "Closed Comments" field.
