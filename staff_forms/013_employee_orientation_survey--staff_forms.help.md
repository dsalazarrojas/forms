# Employee Orientation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and information about new hires after they have completed the employee orientation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields: Job Title, Date Hired, Supervisor Name, and Questions.
* The Questions field is a multiple-select field where you can choose all that apply. Please select all the topics that were most helpful during your orientation.
* You can provide any additional comments or feedback in the Comments field.
* If you have any contact information that you would like to be reached out to (Email and Phone), please include it.
* To submit the form, please select Save and Finish from the Submit field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire** (`new_hire`, text, optional): Enter your name as a new hire to help us track your progress.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title to help us understand your role within the company.
* **Date Hired** (`date_hired`, date, required): Enter the date you were hired to help us track your start date.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name to ensure they are aware of your submission.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your orientation process.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you would like to share with the HR representative.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Questions** (`questions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the topics that were most helpful during your orientation.
	+ Select all that apply.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select Save and Finish to submit your form and Save and Exit Without Saving to exit the form without saving your changes.
