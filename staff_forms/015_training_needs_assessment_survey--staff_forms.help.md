# Training Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Needs Assessment Survey is a form designed to collect information from staff members on their training needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand what information is being asked.
2. Answer each question truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose one or multiple options that best fit your response.
4. Enter your email address and phone number if available.
5. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Training Needs Assessment Survey Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Needs** (`training_needs_assessment_survey_staff`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can describe your training needs and areas where you would like to improve.
* **Training Preferences** (`training_needs_assessment_survey_preferences`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This field allows you to indicate your willingness to participate in training programs.
* **Skill Levels Assessments** (`skill_levels_assessments`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose the skills you are proficient or need improvement in.
* **Development Needs** (`development_needs`, text, required: false):
	+ Write any additional information on your development needs.
* **Training Needs Assessment Survey Comments** (`training_needs_assessment_survey_comments`, note, required: false):
	+ Enter any comments or feedback on your training needs.
* **Training Needs Assessment Survey Email** (`training_needs_assessment_survey_email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address so we can reach out to you for more information.
* **Training Needs Assessment Survey Phone** (`training_needs_assessment_survey_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you with more information.
* **Training Needs Assessment Survey Date** (`training_needs_assessment_survey_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter a date for the training session you would like.
* **Training Needs Assessment Survey Time** (`training_needs_assessment_survey_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter a time for the training session you would like.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the tool you would like to use for training sessions.

## Tips

* Answer truthfully and to the best of your ability to provide accurate information on your training needs.
* Make sure to enter accurate contact information so we can contact you for more information.
* If you have already completed this form, there's no need to do so again.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the training team.
