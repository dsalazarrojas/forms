# wage_increase_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `wage_increase_form` is used for employees to request a wage increase and for managers to review and approve, deny, or keep such requests pending. This form helps employees communicate their reasons for the increase and allows managers to track and respond to these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and other required or optional information as needed.
2. Enter the reason for your wage increase request.
3. Select the effective date when the wage increase will take effect.
4. Choose the approval status of your manager (approved, denied, or pending).
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (`user_input`, text, optional):** Enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Reason for Request (`reason_for_request`, text, optional):** Provide a clear explanation of why you're requesting a wage increase.
* **Effective Date (`effective_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date when the wage increase will take effect.
* **Manager Approval (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional):** Choose whether your manager has approved, denied, or kept your request pending.
* **Comments (`comments`, note, optional):** Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
