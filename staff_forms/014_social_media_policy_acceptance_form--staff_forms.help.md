# Social Media Policy Acceptance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media policy acceptance form is used to obtain acceptance from staff members regarding the company's social media policy document.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the company's social media policy document carefully and understand its contents.
2. Answer the questions in this form based on your understanding of the policy.
3. Enter your responses in the text fields provided.
4. Select the appropriate answer from the drop-down menu.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1** (Page 1, text, optional)
	+ This is the first page of the form, where you can enter any additional comments or questions you have about the policy.
* **Page 2** (Page 2, select_one, optional)
	+ Select "Yes" if you have read and understood the social media policy document, or "No" if you have not yet read the document.
* **Page 3** (Page 3, text, optional)
	+ This is the final page of the form, where you can enter any additional comments or questions you have about the policy.
	+ Please note that this field is optional, but it is recommended that you provide any additional information or context that may be relevant to your understanding of the policy.
