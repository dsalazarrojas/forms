# Paternity Leave Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paternity Leave Application Form is a document designed for employees to submit their application for paternity leave. This form helps employees provide necessary information about their leave, including the start and end dates of their leave, and their name. The completed form will be reviewed and approved or denied by the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details, including your name.
2.  Choose the start and end dates of your leave.
3.  Ensure you have filled in all required information and that your dates are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **employee (name)** (`employee`, text, not required): 
    Enter your name as it appears on your ID or as you prefer to be referred to in the company.
*   **leave_start_date** (`leave_start_date`, date, not required): 
    Enter the date you plan to start your paternity leave from. This date should be in the format of mm/dd/yyyy. Ensure it's accurate and follows the company's leave policy.
*   **leave_end_date** (`leave_end_date`, date, not required): 
    Enter the date you plan to end your paternity leave to. This date should be in the format of mm/dd/yyyy. Ensure it's accurate and follows the company's leave policy.
*   **employee_name (name)** (`employee_name`, text, not required): 
    This field is used for consistency and reference; enter your name to associate your request with your ID or personnel record in the company directory.

## Tips
Please ensure all dates are entered in the format of mm/dd/yyyy. If you're unsure, please contact the HR department for guidance.
