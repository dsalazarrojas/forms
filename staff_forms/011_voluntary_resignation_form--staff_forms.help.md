# Voluntary Resignation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by employees who are voluntarily leaving the organization. It will help us understand the reason for their departure and facilitate a smooth transition of their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Verify that you are eligible to fill out this form**: Please ensure that you are an employee who is voluntarily leaving the organization. If you are unsure, please refer to your HR department or supervisor.
2. **Gather necessary information**: Have all required information ready, such as your employee ID, job title, last day of work, reason for leaving, department, and manager's signature.
3. **Fill out the form**: Start by filling out your employee ID, job title, last day of work, reason for leaving, and department.
4. **Sign the form**: Have your manager sign the form, indicating their acknowledgment of your resignation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID (1)**: Enter your unique employee ID number as listed in our employee database. 
- **Job Title (2)**: Select your job title as it is currently listed in our employee database.
- **Date of Last Day of Work (3)**: Enter the date when you will be leaving the company.
- **Reason for Leaving (4)**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are leaving the organization. Please be concise but clear.
- **Department (5)**: Select the department you are currently working in or the one you will be moving to, if you are transferring.
- **Manager Signature (6)**: Date and sign the form, indicating their approval of your resignation.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the information required in this form, please consult your HR department or supervisor for guidance.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
- Please note that this form is a mandatory document for all voluntary resignations.
