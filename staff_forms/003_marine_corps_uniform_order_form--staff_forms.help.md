# Marine Corps Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marine Corps Uniform Order Form is used to collect information from customers for Marine Corps uniform orders. This form gathers essential details to ensure accurate and efficient processing of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the customer name field.
2. Add your email address in the email field. If you don't have an email address, you can leave this field blank.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone number field. If you don't have a phone number, you can leave this field blank.
4. Select the correct uniform type from the options provided. This will help us process your order accurately.
5. Enter your unit number, which is a required field for Marine Corps orders.
6. Choose the correct size for your uniform from the size options.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the notes field, if needed.
8. Select the category of the order from the options provided.
9. Enter a brief description of the order in the description field, if needed.
10. Enter the assigned tool number, which is a required field for Marine Corps orders.
11. Enter the assigned form ID, which is a required field for Marine Corps orders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to associate with your order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address, enter it here. This will help us contact you about your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This will help us contact you about your order.
* **Uniform Type** (`uniform_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of uniform you need (Marine Corps, Army, Navy, Air Force, or Other).
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, `number`, required): Enter your unit number for Marine Corps orders.
* **Size** (`size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the correct size for your uniform.
* **Any Notes** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the assigned tool number for your order.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the category of the order (Staff Forms, Customer Forms, Sales minimalist).
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the order, if needed.
* **Assigned Form ID** (`assigned_form_id`, `text`, required): Enter the assigned form ID for your order.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, `text`, optional): This field is not relevant to the form's purpose and can be ignored.
