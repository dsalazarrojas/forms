# Virtual Office Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Office Onboarding Form is a necessary step in the onboarding process for new clients. This form collects essential information about the client's company, which is required for setting up a virtual office environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by filling out the Company Information page, which includes fields such as Company Name, Address, and Company Website.
* Move on to the Contact Info page, which contains fields for Contact First Name, Company Phone, and Company Email.
* On the next page, enter the Billing Information and User Info pages.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, denoted by a (*) symbol.
* Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company as it should appear in our system.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the company's physical address.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required/optional): Enter the company's website URL if applicable.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, number, required/optional): Enter the company's phone number.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the company's email address.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Company Info** (`company_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the company.
* **Purchase Details** (`purchase_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any purchase information for the company.
* **Billing Information** (`billing_info`, text, required/optional): Enter billing information for the company.
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any user information for the company.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or support team for assistance.
