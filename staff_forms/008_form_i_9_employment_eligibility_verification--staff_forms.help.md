# Form I 9 Employment Eligibility Verification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Form I 9 Employment Eligibility Verification is used to verify the employment eligibility of employees and ensure compliance with the Fair Credit Reporting Act of 1986.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category from the options provided in the "Category" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the form in the "Description" field.
3. Choose the tool that was assigned to the employee from the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field.
4. Enter the ID numbers of the relevant forms in the "Form IDs" field.
5. Enter the file name and location of the output file in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, required): Choose the category that best describes the form's purpose, such as new hire or rehire.
* **Description** (`Description`, required): Briefly describe the form's purpose and content.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, required): Select the tool assigned to the employee from the options provided.
* **Form IDs** (`Form IDs`, required): Enter the ID numbers of the relevant forms.
* **Output File** (`Output File`, required): Enter the file name and location where the output file will be stored.
* **Description** (`Description`, required): This is a duplicate field and should not be filled out. Please ignore this field.
