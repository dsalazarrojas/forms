# COVID 19 Vaccine HR Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Vaccine HR Survey is designed to collect information from employees about their willingness to get the COVID-19 vaccine, their reasons for getting vaccinated or not, and any concerns or concerns they may have. This information will help the HR department to better understand employee needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each page carefully and fill out the required information
* Provide your basic details, including your name, employee ID, and department
* Answer questions about your willingness to get vaccinated and your reasons for getting or not getting vaccinated
* Select all that apply if you have concerns or reasons for getting vaccinated or not
* Provide any additional comments or questions you may have

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee ID**: Your employee identification number.
* **Department**: Your department or team.
* **Direct Manager Name**: Name of your supervisor.
* **Employment Type**: Select one of the following options: Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, or Temporary.
* **On-Site Work Frequency**: Select one of the following options: Full-Time On-Site, Hybrid Schedule, Remote, or Occasional On-Site.
* **Interested in Getting the COVID-19 Vaccine**: Select one of the following options: Yes, definitely interested, Probably yes, Unsure, Probably not, or Definitely not.
* **When Would You Get Vaccinated**: Select one of the following options: Immediately, Within 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, After more information available, or Not planning to vaccinate.
* **Reasons You Would Get Vaccinated**: Select all that apply:
	+ Protect myself from COVID-19
	+ Protect my family
	+ Return to normal work conditions
	+ Protect vulnerable populations
	+ Required by employer
	+ Required for travel
	+ Recommended by healthcare provider
	+ Peer influence
* **Concerns About the Vaccine**: Select all that apply:
	+ Speed of vaccine development
	+ Safety concerns
	+ Side effects
	+ Allergic reactions
	+ Effectiveness concerns
	+ Religious reasons
	+ Political reasons
	+ Previous adverse vaccine reactions
	+ Pregnancy or breastfeeding
	+ No concerns
* **Pre-Existing Health Conditions**: Do you have any pre-existing health conditions? If yes, please describe them.
* **Immunocompromised Status**: Do you have a compromised immune system? Select one of the following options: Yes, No, Unsure, or Prefer Not to Say.
* **Pregnant or Nursing**: Are you pregnant or nursing? Select one of the following options: Yes, No, Neither, or Prefer Not to Say.
* **Had COVID-19**: Have you had COVID-19? Select one of the following options: Yes, confirmed positive, Yes, probably had it, No, or Unsure.
* **Date of COVID-19 Infection**: If you had COVID-19, please provide the approximate date of infection.
* **Number of Vaccine Doses**: How many doses of the COVID-19 vaccine you have received? 
* **Information Needed to Make Decision**: Select all that apply:
	+ Safety and efficacy data
	+ Long-term side effects data
	+ How vaccines work
	+ Comparison of vaccine types
	+ Information for my health condition
	+ Religious or ethical guidance
	+ Employer policy clarification
	+ Testimonials from vaccinated people
	+ One-on-one consultation
	+ None needed
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Any additional comments or questions you may have.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and is not a binding agreement or contract. The information collected will be used to support employee needs and concerns related to the COVID-19 vaccine.
