### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (Employee Information, text, required: false): 
  Fill in your personal details.
* **Employment Details** (Employment Details, text, required: false): 
  Provide details about your current and previous employment, including the company name, job title, and employment dates.
* **Salary Information** (Salary Information, text, required: false): 
  Enter your current and previous salary details, including the salary amount and payment frequency.
* **Certification** (Certification, select_multiple, required: false): 
  If you are certified, select 'Yes'.
* **Employer Information** (Employer Information, text, required: false): 
  Enter the details of your current and previous employer, including the company name, address, and contact information.
