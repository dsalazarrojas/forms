# Temporary Duty Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request temporary duties, which means being assigned to a different location or unit for a short duration.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill in all required fields.
* For non-mandatory fields, fill in as much relevant information as possible.
* Review and proofread the form before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Temporary Duty Request**:
	+ A brief description of the temporary duty you are requesting.
	+ This is the main purpose of the form, where you can describe why you need to be assigned to a different location or unit.
* **Date of Request**:
	+ Enter the date you are submitting the request.
* **Time of Request**:
	+ Enter the time you are submitting the request.
* **Duty Location**:
	+ Select the location or unit you would like to be assigned to.
	+ Please select one of the available options from the dropdown list.
* **Unit Assigning**:
	+ Indicate whether your unit is aware of and assigning you to this task.
* **Reason for Request**:
	+ Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting to be assigned to this location or unit.
* **Remarks**:
	+ This is a free text field for any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Signature**:
	+ A field for the submitter's signature. 

Note: This guide is a general template, the actual form fields and their labels might vary based on the specific form configuration. 

## Tips

* Only submit a request when you are certain you need to be assigned to a different location or unit for a short duration.
* Be clear and concise when providing information in each field to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
