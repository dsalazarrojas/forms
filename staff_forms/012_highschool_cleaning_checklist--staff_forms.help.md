# Highschool Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the cleaning schedule and other relevant details for high school cleaning checks. It is intended for school administration and maintenance staff to ensure that all areas of the school are kept clean and tidy. This form helps to track the cleaning schedule, identify areas of concern, and plan for future cleaning tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the high school.
2. **Enter the Building Name** (`building_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the building or location within the school.
3. **Specify the Room Number** (`room_number`, number, required: false): Enter the number of the room you are responsible for cleaning.
4. **Indicate the Floor Number** (`floor_number`, number, required: false): Enter the floor number of the room you are responsible for cleaning.
5. **Select a Cleaning Schedule** (`clean_schedule`, select_one, required: false): Choose from the following options:
	* **Minimalist**: A quick and simple cleaning schedule.
	* **Weekly**: A regular cleaning schedule to keep the area clean throughout the week.
	* **Monthly**: A cleaning schedule to keep the area clean and tidy on a monthly basis.
	* **Quarterly**: A more thorough cleaning schedule to deep clean the area every 3 months.
6. **Enter the Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you plan to start the cleaning task.
7. **Add Goals and Notes** (`id_goals`, note, required: false): Enter any specific goals or notes related to the cleaning task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the high school you are responsible for cleaning.
* **Building Name**: Enter the name of the building or location within the school.
* **Room Number**: Enter the number of the room you are cleaning.
* **Floor Number**: Enter the floor number of the room you are cleaning.
* **Cleaning Schedule**: Choose a schedule that fits your needs:
	+ **Minimalist**: A quick and simple cleaning schedule.
	+ **Weekly**: A regular cleaning schedule to keep the area clean throughout the week.
	+ **Monthly**: A cleaning schedule to keep the area clean and tidy on a monthly basis.
	+ **Quarterly**: A more thorough cleaning schedule to deep clean the area every 3 months.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you plan to start the cleaning task.
* **Goals and Notes**: Enter any specific goals or notes related to the cleaning task. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, even if it is not marked as "required".
* Double-check the cleaning schedule to ensure it fits your needs and the needs of the school.
* Use the "Goals and Notes" field to add any specific details or reminders about the cleaning task.
