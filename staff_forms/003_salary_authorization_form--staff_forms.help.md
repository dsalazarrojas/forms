# Salary Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request authorization for salary adjustments or bonuses for specific employees.

## How To Complete This Form

* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
* Select the correct employee ID from the provided list.
* Enter the date you are requesting authorization for.
* Enter the bonus amount or authorized salary adjustment.
* Enter the reason for the authorization (e.g., promotion, bonus, raise).
* Enter any additional comments if necessary.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id** (Employee ID, number, **required**): Enter the ID of the employee requesting authorization.
* **authorization_date** (Authorization Date, date, **required**): Enter the date you are requesting authorization for.
* **bonus_amount** (Bonus Amount, number, **optional**): Enter the bonus amount if applicable.
* **authorized_amount** (Authorized Amount, number, **optional**): Enter the authorized amount if applicable.
* **authorized_by** (Authorized By, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the person authorizing the request.
* **reason_for_authorization** (Reason for Authorization, text, **optional**): Enter a brief description of the reason for the authorization.
* **comments** (Comments, text, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or notes about the request.
