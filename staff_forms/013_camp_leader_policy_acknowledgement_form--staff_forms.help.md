# Camp Leader Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Leader Policy Acknowledgement Form is designed to ensure that all camp staff members understand and acknowledge the various policies that govern their work and responsibilities. This form is a critical part of the onboarding process for new staff members and is reviewed and updated annually.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete each field with the requested information.
2.  Ensure that you have read and understood each policy before selecting "Yes - I acknowledge" for the relevant questions.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns about a policy, list them in the "Questions or Concerns About Policies" field.
4.  Sign and date the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leader First Name** (`leader_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Leader Last Name** (`leader_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your employee ID number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, select_one, required): Select your role at the camp, such as Camp Counselor, Senior Counselor, or Activity Leader.
* **Camp Location** (`camp_location`, select_one, required): Select the camp location where you will be working.
* **Employment Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started working at the camp.
* **Policy Handbook Received** (`policy_handbook_received`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I have received it" if you have received the camp policy handbook.
* **Child Protection Policy Acknowledgement** (`child_protection_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the child protection policy.
* **Safety Protocols Policy Acknowledgement** (`safety_protocols_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the safety protocols policy.
* **Emergency Procedures Policy Acknowledgement** (`emergency_procedures_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the emergency procedures policy.
* **Code of Conduct Policy Acknowledgement** (`code_of_conduct_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the code of conduct policy.
* **Confidentiality Policy Acknowledgement** (`confidentiality_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the confidentiality policy.
* **Social Media Policy Acknowledgement** (`social_media_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the social media policy.
* **Medication Administration Policy Acknowledgement** (`medication_administration_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the medication administration policy. If not applicable to your role, select "Not applicable to my role".
* **Transportation Policy Acknowledgement** (`transportation_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the transportation policy. If not applicable to your role, select "Not applicable to my role".
* **Incident Reporting Policy Acknowledgement** (`incident_reporting_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the incident reporting policy.
* **Harassment Prevention Policy Acknowledgement** (`harassment_prevention_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the harassment prevention policy.
* **Technology Use Policy Acknowledgement** (`technology_use_policy`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I acknowledge" if you have read and understood the technology use policy.
* **Background Check Completed** (`background_check_completed`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - completed" if you have completed the background check.
* **CPR Certification Status** (`cpr_certification`, select_one, required): Select your current CPR status.
* **First Aid Certification Status** (`first_aid_certification`, select_one, required): Select your current first aid status.
* **Additional Training Needed** (`additional_training_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the provided list.
* **Questions or Concerns About Policies** (`questions_or_concerns`, text, optional): List any questions or concerns you have about the policies.
* **Leader Signature** (`leader_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as a signature.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
