# Daycare Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about the daycare experience of children in a childcare center or school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the service provider from the list of options to indicate whether a service was provided.
2. Enter the name of the child receiving the service.
3. Enter the date of service.
4. Enter the time of service.
5. Enter the number of children receiving the service.
6. Enter the name of the daycare (optional).
7. Enter any comments or notes about the service provided.
8. Enter the name of the staff member providing the service.
9. Enter the phone number of the staff member providing the service.
10. Enter the email address of the staff member providing the service.
11. Select whether an assigned tool was used during the service.
12. Enter any additional notes or comments about the service provided.
13. Select whether the daycare has a license to operate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Provider** (`daycareserviceprovider`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether a service was provided.
- **Child's Name** (`child_name`): Enter the name of the child receiving the service.
- **Date Of Service** (`date_of_service`): Enter the date of service provided to the child.
- **Time Of Service** (`time_of_service`): Enter the time of service provided to the child.
- **Number Of Children** (`number_of_children`): Enter the number of children receiving the service.
- **Daycare Name** (`daycare_name`): Enter the name of the daycare (optional).
- **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any comments or notes about the service provided.
- **Staff Name** (`staff_name`): Enter the name of the staff member providing the service.
- **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`): Enter the phone number of the staff member providing the service.
- **Staff Email** (`staff_email`): Enter the email address of the staff member providing the service.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select whether an assigned tool was used during the service.
- **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the service provided.
- **Daycare License** (`daycare_license`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the daycare has a license to operate.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information about the service provided.
* Ensure that the daycare name is entered correctly, if applicable.
* Enter any comments or notes about the service provided to help staff members understand the context of the service.
* If a tool was assigned, select "Yes" to indicate this.
* Select "Yes" if the daycare has a license to operate.
* Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.
