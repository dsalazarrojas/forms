# it_new_staff_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_new_staff_request_form is used by new staff members to request the creation of a new staff profile. This form collects necessary information for the new profile creation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, select the corresponding page with each question and fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure that you fill all the required fields marked as "required".
3. For "date_of_birth" field, it is not always required, but it's recommended for accurate staff records.
4. For "job_title" field, ensure to choose the correct job title from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`new_staff_request_form_1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`new_staff_request_form_2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Select the job title you will be working as, from the provided options or enter it manually.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to which we can send communications to.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth if you want to provide it for accurate staff records.
* **Job Title Confirmation** (`new_staff_request_form_7`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you want to be assigned to a job title. 
* **Department Selection** (`new_staff_request_form_8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all departments you want to be a part of.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current job status.
* **Job Status Note** (`job_status_note`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about your job status.
