# Employee Raise Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Raise Form is designed to gather information on employee progress, goals, and development needs. This form will be used by HR or management to assess an employee's performance and plan for future growth and raises.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information about your performance, goals, and development needs.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, but concisely, in the space provided.
3. If you're unsure about a particular field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
4. Submit the form only once per review period to avoid duplication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. employee_raise_form_1** (`employee_raise_form_1_label`)
Provide a brief description of your job title and responsibilities.

### **2. employee_raise_form_2** (`employee_raise_form_2_label`)
Describe your current performance in the past review period, highlighting your achievements and accomplishments.

### **3. employee_raise_form_3** (`employee_raise_form_3_label`)
List your short-term and long-term goals for the next review period, ensuring they align with company objectives and your job description.

### **4. employee_raise_form_4** (`employee_raise_form_4_label`)
Identify areas where you'd like to improve or develop new skills, and describe how you plan to address them.

### **5. employee_raise_form_5** (`employee_raise_form_5_label`)
Suggest any additional training or resources you need to support your growth and development.

### **6. employee_raise_form_6** (`employee_raise_form_6_label`)
Provide feedback or suggestions for improvements to our company or management practices that you've encountered during the review period.

### **7. employee_raise_form_7** (`employee_raise_form_7_label`)
List any significant events or milestones achieved during the review period, such as promotions, new responsibilities, or team collaborations.

### **8. employee_raise_form_8** (`employee_raise_form_8_label`)
Describe your expectations for the next review period, including any changes in your role or responsibilities you're anticipating.

### **9. employee_raise_form_9** (`employee_raise_form_9_label`)
Provide a summary of your overall performance and growth during the review period, highlighting your strengths and areas for improvement.

### **10. employee_raise_form_10** (`employee_raise_form_10_label`)
Attach any supporting documents, such as performance reports, certificates, or references, to supplement your form responses.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Use specific examples and metrics to illustrate your achievements.
* Proofread your form before submission to ensure completeness and clarity.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR or management team.
