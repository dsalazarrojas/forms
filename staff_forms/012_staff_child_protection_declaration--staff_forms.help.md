# Staff Child Protection Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to ensure that staff members are aware of and have agreed to child protection policies within the organization or school setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and the purpose of the form.
2.  Answer the questions accurately and honestly.
3.  Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Staff Child Protection Declaration Form** (`staff_child_protection_declaration_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main section of the form where you can enter your declaration.
*   **Have you agreed to our child protection policies?** (`staff_agreement`, select_one, required/optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have agreed to the child protection policies, or 'No' if you haven't.
*   **Date of Declaration** (`declaration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are making this declaration.
*   **Declared By** (`declared_by`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the person making this declaration.
*   **School Agreement** (`school_agreement`, text, required/optional): If you're filling out this form for a school setting, enter any relevant details about your agreement with the school.
*   **Organization Agreement** (`organization_agreement`, text, required/optional): If you're filling out this form for an organization setting, enter any relevant details about your agreement with the organization.
*   **Staff Training** (`staff_training`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant details about your staff training in child protection, if applicable.

## Tips
*   Be thorough when filling out the form, ensuring you answer accurately and honestly.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
*   Keep a copy of your declaration for your records.
