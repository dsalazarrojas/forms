# Mandatory Overtime Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for mandatory overtime complaints. It will help us understand when employees are being asked to work beyond their regular working hours and provide a way for managers to track and manage these requests. Please ensure you only submit one complaint per form. If you have multiple complaints, you can submit multiple forms. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form accurately and effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select your answer for "User" (Page 1).
2. Choose the "Complaint Type" you are experiencing (Page 2).
3. Provide a detailed description of your reason for the complaint in the "Reason" field (Page 3).
4. Add any additional details about the situation in the "Additional Details" field (Page 4).
5. Select a resolution status for your complaint in "Resolution Status" (Page 5).
6. Enter your manager's name in "Manager's Name" (Page 6).
7. Check if your manager will receive an email about this complaint in "Manager's Email" (Page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User (Page 1)
* **User**: This field is used to select whether you are a user experiencing the complaint. Please choose the correct answer from the dropdown list.

### Complaint Type (Page 2)
* **Complaint Type**: This field is used to select the type of complaint you are experiencing. Please choose the correct type from the dropdown list.

### Reason (Page 3)
* **Reason**: Please provide a detailed description of the reason you are experiencing mandatory overtime.

### Additional Details (Page 4)
* **Additional Details**: Add any additional information that may help your manager understand the situation.

### Resolution (Page 5)
* **Resolution**: Please select a resolution status for your complaint.

### Action (Page 6)
* **Action**: This field is intended for your manager's action plan, but please leave it empty as it is not required to be filled in.

### Manager's Name (Page 7)
* **Manager's Name**: Please enter your manager's name so they can be notified about your complaint.

### Manager's Email (Page 8)
* **Manager's Email**: Check if your manager will receive an email about this complaint. Please note that this feature is not enabled for this form.

### Resolution Status (Page 9)
* **Resolution Status**: Select whether your complaint is active or inactive.
Please ensure to fill out the form accurately and honestly, and do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
