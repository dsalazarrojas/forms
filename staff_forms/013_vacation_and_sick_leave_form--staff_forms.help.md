# Vacation And Sick Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation and Sick Leave Form is used to record employee's vacation and sick leave. This form is used to submit leave requests, track leave balance, and manage leave status.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, click on the "Employee Information" page and fill in the required fields with your employee details.
- Select the "Start Date" and "End Date" for your leave.
- Choose the reason for your leave and select the correct "Leave Status" from the dropdown menu.
- Sign and date the form with your employee signature.
- If applicable, provide any additional comments or notes for your manager to review.
- Once your manager has reviewed and approved your leave request, they will sign and date the form with their manager signature and add any necessary comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`Employee Name`, `text`, required): This field captures the employee's name.
* **Manager ID** (`Manager ID`, `number`, optional): Enter your manager's ID.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason** (`Reason`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your leave.
* **Days Off** (`Days Off`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you will be taking off.
* **Leave Status** (`Leave Status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your leave from the dropdown menu.
	+ Pending: Your leave request is waiting for approval.
	+ Approved: Your leave request has been approved.
	+ Rejected: Your leave request has been rejected.
	+ Expired: Your leave request has expired.
* **Employee Signature** (`Employee Signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date with your employee signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`Manager Signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date with your manager signature.
* **Manager Comment** (`Manager Comment`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes for your manager.
* **Approval Note** (`Approval Note`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes for approval.
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, `text`, required): Enter your employee name.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`Department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Team** (`Team`, `text`, optional): Enter your team name.
* **Date Submitted** (`Date Submitted`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted** (`Time Submitted`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Manager's Name** (`Manager's Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager's Email** (`Manager's Email`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's email.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, `text`, optional): Enter your employee ID.
* **Department ID** (`Department ID`, `text`, optional): Enter your department ID.
* **Team ID** (`Team ID`, `text`, optional): Enter your team ID.
* **Leave Balance** (`Leave Balance`, `number`, required): Enter your current leave balance.
* **Remaining Leave** (`Remaining Leave`, `number`, optional): Enter the remaining leave days.
* **Approval Status** (`Approval Status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your leave from the dropdown menu.
	+ Pending: Your leave request is waiting for approval.
	+ Approved: Your leave request has been approved.
	+ Rejected: Your leave request has been rejected.
	+ Expired: Your leave request has expired.

## Tips
- Be sure to accurately enter your employee information and leave details to ensure correct tracking and approval.
- If you have any questions or concerns about your leave request, please reach out to your manager or HR representative.
- Make sure to review and submit the form completely and accurately to avoid delays or rejections.
