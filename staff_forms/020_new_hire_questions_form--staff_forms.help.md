# new_hire_questions_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new hire questions form is designed to collect information about new hires, including their personal details, job title, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first_name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **last_name** in the next field.
3. Enter your **email_address** in the following field.
4. Select your **hire_date** from the date picker.
5. Enter your **job_title** in the next field.
6. Enter your **department** in the optional field, if applicable.
7. Enter your **job_description** in the optional field, if applicable.
8. Enter your **job_location** in the optional field, if applicable.
9. Enter your **manager_name** in the optional field, if applicable.
10. Enter your **hire_manager_name** in the optional field, if applicable.
11. Enter your **hire_manager_email** in the optional field, if applicable.
12. Enter your **hire_manager_phone** in the optional field, if applicable.
13. Enter your **emergency_contact** in the next field.
14. Select your **emergency_contact_relation** in the dropdown.
15. Enter your **emergency_contact_phone** in the next field.
16. Enter your **emergency_contact_email** in the optional field, if applicable.
17. Enter your **emergency_contact_address** in the optional field, if applicable.
18. Enter your **emergency_contact_city** in the optional field, if applicable.
19. Enter your **emergency_contact_state** in the optional field, if applicable.
20. Enter your **emergency_contact_zip** in the optional field, if applicable.
21. Enter your **emergency_contact_country** in the optional field, if applicable.
22. Enter any additional information about your **emergency_contact_other** in the optional field, if applicable.
23. Enter the **emergency_contact_other_phone** in the optional field, if applicable.
24. Enter the **emergency_contact_other_email** in the optional field, if applicable.
25. Enter the **emergency_contact_other_address** in the optional field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Hire Date** (date, required): Select the date you were hired.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (text, optional): If you have a department, enter it here. 
* **Job Description** (text, optional): If applicable, enter a brief description of your job role. 
* **Job Location** (text, optional): If you have a specific job location, enter it here. 
* **Manager Name** (text, optional): If you have a manager, enter their name. 
* **Hire Manager Name** (text, optional): If you have a hire manager, enter their name. 
* **Hire Manager Email** (email, optional): If you have a hire manager, enter their email. 
* **Hire Manager Phone** (text, optional): If you have a hire manager, enter their phone number. 
* **Emergency Contact** (text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (text, required): Select the relation of the emergency contact (e.g. parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact City** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the city of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact State** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the state of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the zip code of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Country** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the country of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Other** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Other Phone** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Other Email** (email, optional): If applicable, enter the email of the emergency contact other.
* **Emergency Contact Other Address** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the address of the emergency contact other.
