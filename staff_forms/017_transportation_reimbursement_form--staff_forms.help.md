# Transportation Reimbursement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request reimbursement for transportation-related expenses. It will help you to provide necessary information about your travel, including destination, dates, travel method, and reimbursement details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the travel destination.
2. Enter the dates of your travel.
3. Choose the method of travel.
4. Enter the reimbursement amount.
5. Enter the mileage rate.
6. Enter the rates for business and personal meals.
7. Enter the lodging rate.
8. Enter any other rate applicable to your travel expenses.
9. Add any additional notes about your travel.
10. Select the category and category list for your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Travel Destination
* `travel_destination`: This field is used to enter the destination of your travel.
### Date of Travel
* `date_of_travel`: This field is used to enter the dates of your travel.
### Time of Travel
* `time_of_travel`: This field is currently not supported by the form's field types, but it is likely intended to be a field for entering the time of day or travel duration. However, since it is a `time` field type, but not used in the YAML, please use with caution.
### Method of Travel
* `method_of_travel`: This field is used to select the method of travel.
### Reimbursement Amount
* `reimbursement_amount`: This field is used to enter the reimbursement amount.
### Mileage Rate
* `mileage_rate`: This field is used to enter the mileage rate.
### Business Meal Rate
* `business_meal_rate`: This field is used to enter the rate for business meals.
### Personal Meal Rate
* `personal_meal_rate`: This field is used to enter the rate for personal meals.
### Lodging Rate
* `lodging_rate`: This field is used to enter the lodging rate.
### Other Rate
* `other`: This field is used to enter any other rate applicable to your travel expenses.
### Notes
* `notes`: This field is used to add any additional comments or notes about your travel.
### Assigned Tool
* `assigned_tool`: This field is used to select the tool assigned to process your reimbursement request.
### Category
* `category`: This field is used to select the category for your reimbursement request.
### Category List
* `category_list`: This field is used to select a list of categories for your reimbursement request.
### Form ID
* `form_id`: This field is not currently used in the form, so please use with caution.
### Assigned User
* `assigned_user`: This field is not currently used in the form, so please use with caution.
 
**Tips**: Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field's explanation above.
