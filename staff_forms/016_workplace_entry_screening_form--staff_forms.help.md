# Workplace Entry Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employees entering the workplace, particularly those who have recently traveled or may have symptoms that could pose a risk to others. This information will help in taking necessary precautions to ensure a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant sections based on your current situation.
	* If you have traveled recently, please select the "Travel History" section.
	* If you are experiencing symptoms, please select the "Symptoms" sections.
2. Provide accurate contact information and medical history as required.
3. Ensure all relevant fields are filled in, and submit the form.
