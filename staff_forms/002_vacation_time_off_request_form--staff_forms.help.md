# vacation_time_off_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to request time off for personal or work-related absences. Use this form to inform your manager or HR of your vacation plans, medical leave, or family-related needs. Ensure that you're not duplicating a request already made.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the reason for your request. Choose the most accurate option from the provided selection: "work", "medical", or "family".
2.  Enter the number of days you would like to take off.
3.  Choose a start date for your time off.
4.  Choose an end date for your time off.
5.  If applicable, specify the number of hours you would like to request off, if your role allows for part-time leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **prophets** (`prophets`, select_one, optional): Choose the reason for your time off from the options provided. This could be "work", "medical", or "family".
    *   Please select the most accurate option that corresponds to your actual reason for the time off.
*   **time_off_days** (`time_off`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you would like to take off.
    *   Enter the total number of days you need for your time off.
*   **start_date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Choose the start date for your time off.
    *   Enter the date you plan to begin your time off.
*   **end_date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Choose the end date for your time off.
    *   Enter the date you plan to end your time off.
*   **hours_requested** (`hours_requested`, number, optional): If your role allows for part-time leave, specify the number of hours you would like to request off.
    *   Enter the total number of hours you need for your time off.
*   **reason** (`reason`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or explanations for your request, please provide them here.
    *   Enter any other relevant details about your time off.
