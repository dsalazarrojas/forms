# New Employee Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Employee Details Form is used to onboard new employees into the company's system by collecting and capturing their essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form carefully and ensuring you have the necessary permissions to access and process this employee's data.
2. Select the new employee's department from the options provided.
3. Enter the new employee's name, salary, date of birth, date of hire, department phone, email, and phone number.
4. If the new employee has a job title, enter it in the corresponding field.
5. Review and submit the form to complete the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`new_employee_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the new employee's name as per the company's official records.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the department where the new employee will be working.
* **Department Phone** (`department_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the new employee's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the new employee's phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the new employee's job title, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter the new employee's date of birth.
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, `date`, required: false): Enter the new employee's date of hire.
* **Date of Resignation** (`date_of_resignation`, `date`, required: false): Not applicable for new employees, this field is meant for employees who are leaving the company.
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, required: false): Enter the new employee's salary.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and process this employee's data.
* Review and submit the form to complete the onboarding process.
