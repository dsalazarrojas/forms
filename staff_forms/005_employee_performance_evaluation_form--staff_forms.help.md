This help guide is intended to provide clear instructions and explanations to end-users on how to complete the employee performance evaluation form. This form is used to evaluate employee performance during a specific period and gather feedback on areas of improvement. It should only be used for this purpose and not for any other form of evaluation or assessment. The following guide covers the necessary steps to complete the form correctly.

The form consists of the following sections:

## Purpose

The employee performance evaluation form is used to assess an employee's performance over a given period and to identify areas where they may need improvement. This form helps to gather feedback and recommendations from the employee's supervisor or manager.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate performance period from the available options. This is where you'll select the date range for the performance evaluation.
2. Enter the employee's name and job title.
3. Input the employee's performance score, which should reflect their performance over the selected period.
4. List the employee's strengths, which are their positive qualities or skills that contributed to their performance.
5. List areas for improvement, which are specific areas where the employee can develop or improve.
6. Provide recommendations for future development or improvement.
7. Select the overall performance, which reflects the employee's performance over the selected period.
8. Select the review status, which indicates the current status of the review.
9. Select the review date status, which indicates when the review was completed or started.
10. Select the review period status, which indicates whether the review is active, completed, or in progress.
11. Select the review status, which indicates the current status of the review.
12. Enter any review comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Period** (number): Enter the performance period for which you're evaluating the employee's performance.
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Job Title** (text): Enter the job title of the employee being evaluated.
* **Performance Score** (number): Input the employee's performance score, which reflects their performance over the selected period.
* **Strengths** (note): List the employee's strengths or positive qualities that contributed to their performance.
* **Areas For Improvement** (note): List specific areas where the employee can develop or improve.
* **Recommendations** (note): Provide recommendations for future development or improvement.
* **Overall Performance** (time): Select the overall performance, which reflects the employee's performance over the selected period.
* **Email** (email): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Date Start Period** (date): Enter the start date of the performance period.
* **Date End Period** (date): Enter the end date of the performance period.
* **Date Last Review** (date): Enter the date of the last review.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter the employee's phone number (optional).
* **Manager Review Status** (select multiple): Select the current status of the manager's review.

*   - In Progress
*   - Not Reviewed
* **Review Date Status** (select multiple): Select the current status of the review date.

*   - Completed

* **Review Comments** (text): Enter any comments or feedback about the review.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the evaluation process.
* Be as objective and unbiased as possible when evaluating the employee's performance.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback and recommendations.
* Keep the review private and confidential.
* Use the correct performance period when filling out the form.
* Ensure the manager review status, review date status, review period status, and review status are correctly selected.
* Enter any relevant review comments or feedback.
