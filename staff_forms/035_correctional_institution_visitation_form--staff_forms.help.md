# Correctional Institution Visitation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Correctional Institution Visitation Form is a tool for tracking and recording visitation information from visitors at the correctional institution. This form is used to collect vital information from visitors, including contact details, visit dates and times, and visit reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the visitor's details, including their name and contact information.
2. Select the visit date and time.
3. Specify the reason for the visit.
4. Indicate whether the visit is approved or not.
5. If necessary, provide additional information about the visit, such as its duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the visitor.

### **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for the visit.

### **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional)
Enter the phone number of the person or organization responsible for the visit.

### **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional)
Enter the email address of the person or organization responsible for the visit.

### **Visit Date** (`visitor_date`, date, optional)
Select the date of the visit using the calendar.

### **Visit Time** (`visitor_time`, time, optional)
Select the time of the visit.

### **Visit Reason** (`visit_reason`, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of the purpose or reason for the visit.

### **Visit Type** (`visit_type`, select_one, optional)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the visit is approved or not.

### **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, text, optional)
Enter the expected duration of the visit, if applicable.

### **Vehicle** (`vehicle`, text, optional)
Enter information about the vehicle used for the visit, if applicable.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but it's always best to provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate records and to prevent any potential security risks. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification from the institution's staff.
