# Employee Attitude Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and opinions from employees on various aspects of their work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly, as your feedback is valuable to management.
2. Choose the correct answer for each question based on your own experiences and opinions.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) after the field label.
4. If you have any comments or additional feedback, please use the "Feedback" field to provide more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Management Support** (`management_support`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes, always
	+ No, rarely
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Choose one:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your work experience.
* **Job Security** (`job_security`, number, optional): Rate your job security on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very low and 10 is very high.
* **Team Work** (`team_work`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
