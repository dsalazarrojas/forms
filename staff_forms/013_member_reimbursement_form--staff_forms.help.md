# Member Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Member Reimbursement Form is used by staff members to request reimbursement for expenses incurred while performing their duties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your member details, including your name and any other necessary information.
2. Describe your expense, including any submitted documents.
3. Enter the amount of the expense.
4. Select the staff member in charge of your submission.
5. Choose the staff members who will receive the submission.
6. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
7. Add any additional notes about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **member_form** (Member Details, text, required): This is where you will enter your member details, including your name and any other necessary information.
* **expense_details** (Expense Details, text, optional): This field is where you can describe your expense, including any submitted documents. Please make sure to clearly explain your expense so that it can be properly reviewed.
* **expense_amount** (Expense Amount, number, optional): This field is where you can enter the amount of the expense. Please make sure to enter the correct amount to ensure accurate reimbursement processing.
* **submitted_documents** (Submitted Documents, text, optional): This field is where you can attach any submitted documents related to your expense.
* **submission_date** (Submission Date, date, optional): This field is where you can enter the date you submitted your expense.
* **staff_incharge** (Staff Incharge, select one, optional): This field is where you can select the staff member in charge of your submission.
* **email** (Email, email, optional): This field is where you can enter your email address so that staff can contact you about your submission.
* **phone** (Phone, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your phone number so that staff can contact you about your submission.
* **notes** (Notes, note, optional): This field is where you can add any additional notes about your submission.
* **submitted_by** (Submitted By, select multiple, optional): This field is where you can select the staff members who will receive your submission.
* **submitted_to** (Submitted To, select multiple, optional): This field is where you can select the staff members who will receive your submission.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, as it will be reviewed and processed by staff. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
