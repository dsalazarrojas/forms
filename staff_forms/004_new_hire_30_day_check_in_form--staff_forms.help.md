# New Hire 30 Day Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire 30 Day Check In Form is used to document new hire information, track onboarding progress, and ensure that all necessary steps have been completed within the 30-day onboarding period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the new hire's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the new hire's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the new hire's email address in the "Email" field (in the correct format, e.g., [newhire@example.com](mailto:newhire@example.com)).
4. Enter the new hire's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the new hire's department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date the new hire was hired in the "Date Hired" field.
7. Enter the new hire's job title and position in the "Job Title" and "Position" fields.
8. Enter the new hire's supervisor's name in the "Supervisor" field.
9. Enter the new hire's location in the "Location" field.
10. Select the new hire's job status (Full Time, Part Time, or Contract) from the "Job Status" dropdown menu.
11. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the new hire's first name as they prefer to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the new hire's last name as they prefer to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the new hire's email address in the correct format (e.g., [newhire@example.com](mailto:newhire@example.com)).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the new hire's phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department that the new hire will be working in (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Operations).
* **Date Hired** (`date_hired`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the new hire was hired (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the new hire's job title.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, optional): Enter the new hire's position.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, `text`, optional): Enter the new hire's supervisor's name.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter the new hire's location.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the new hire's job status (Full Time, Part Time, or Contract).
* **Additional Comments** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the new hire's onboarding progress.
