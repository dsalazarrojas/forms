## Purpose

The Competency Evaluation Form is a tool used by managers to evaluate a staff member's competency and performance over a specific period. This form helps identify areas of strength and weakness, providing a basis for development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the staff member's name and the evaluation period in the respective fields.
2.  Evaluate the staff member's behavioral and professional abilities by selecting the appropriate ratings for each competency (initiative, reliability, professionalism, conflict resolution, etc.).
3.  Provide a list of the staff member's major achievements during the evaluation period.
4.  Set specific, measurable goals for the next evaluation period.
5.  Add any general comments from the supervisor and sign off on the evaluation.
6.  Confirm that you have discussed this evaluation with your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you enter the full name of the employee being evaluated.
*   **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period_range`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the period over which the evaluation is being conducted (e.g., Annual Review 2025, Probationary Review).
*   **Initiative and Self-Direction** (`initiative_competency_rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field assesses the staff member's ability to take responsibility for tasks without constant supervision. Select the level of initiative demonstrated by the staff member:
    *   Always demonstrates
    *   Often demonstrates
    *   Sometimes demonstrates
    *   Rarely demonstrates
    *   Not observed
*   **Reliability and Consistency** (`reliability_competency_rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field evaluates the staff member's ability to deliver work on time and meet commitments. Select the level of reliability demonstrated:
    *   Highly Reliable
    *   Consistent
    *   Inconsistent
    *   Unreliable
*   **Professionalism and Ethics** (`professionalism_rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field assesses the staff member's adherence to company values and professional standards. Select the level of professionalism demonstrated:
    *   Role Model
    *   Professional
    *   Needs Guidance
    *   Unprofessional
*   **Conflict Resolution and Negotiation** (`conflict_resolution_rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field evaluates the staff member's ability to handle disagreements constructively. Select the level of conflict resolution demonstrated:
    *   Exceptional
    *   Competent
    *   Developing
    *   Struggles with conflict
*   **Major Achievements in this Period** (`primary_achievements_summary`, `text`, required/optional): List significant contributions or projects completed during the evaluation period.
*   **Goals for the Next Evaluation Period** (`development_goals_next_period`, `text`, required/optional): Set specific, measurable goals for the next evaluation period.
*   **Manager General Comments** (`manager_comments_final`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any general comments from the supervisor.
*   **I have discussed this evaluation with my manager** (`staff_signature_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that you have discussed this evaluation with your manager.
*   **Date of Sign-off** (`evaluation_signoff_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of sign-off.

Note: The date of sign-off is automatically filled in by the system based on the current date.

This form is designed to help staff members grow and improve their skills and performance over time. By completing this evaluation, you will be able to identify areas of strength and weakness, and set goals for improvement.
