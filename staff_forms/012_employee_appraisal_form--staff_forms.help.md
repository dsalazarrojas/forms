# employee_appraisal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Appraisal Form is used to evaluate an employee's performance and provide feedback. This form is essential for regular check-ins and evaluations to help employees grow and improve their skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the employee's name on the first page of the form.
2. Provide a rating for the employee's performance using the scale provided.
3. Describe the employee's performance in the "employee_performance" field, providing specific examples and feedback.
4. Add any relevant comments for the appraiser's feedback.
5. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
6. Check if the evaluation has been assigned to someone ("Yes" or "No").
7. Select if the evaluation will be assigned to multiple people ("Yes" or "No").
8. Select the category of the employee being appraised.
9. Enter the employee's email address for communication.
10. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being appraised.
* **Rating**: A numerical score out of 10 to represent the employee's performance, where higher scores indicate better performance.
* **Employee Performance**: Describe the employee's strengths and weaknesses, with specific examples and feedback.
* **Comments**: Additional feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* **Date**: The date of the evaluation.
* **Time**: The time of the evaluation.
* **Assigned to**: Check if the evaluation has been assigned to a specific person.
* **Assigned by**: Select if the evaluation will be assigned to multiple people.
* **Employee Category**: The category of the employee being appraised.
* **Email**: Enter the employee's email address for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide specific examples and feedback for the employee's performance.
* Be honest and objective in your evaluation.
* Use the "assigned to" and "assigned by" fields to track who is responsible for the evaluation.
* Use the "employee category" field to categorize the employee being appraised.
