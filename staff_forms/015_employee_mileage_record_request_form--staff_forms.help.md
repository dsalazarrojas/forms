# Employee Mileage Record Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees regarding their mileage records for reimbursement purposes. It is used to track the start and end dates of employee work periods, including the start and end mileage readings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter the date when you started your work period in the "Date Started" field.
3. Enter the date when you completed your work period in the "Date Completed" field.
4. Enter your start and end mileage readings in the "Start Mileage" and "End Mileage" fields respectively.
5. Select your employer from the drop-down list in the "Employer" field.
6. Enter any additional notes regarding your work period in the "Notes" field.
7. Optionally, enter your email address in the "Email" field for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer**: Select the name of your employer from the drop-down list.
* **Date Started**: Enter the date when you started your work period.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date when you completed your work period.
* **Start Mileage**: Enter the mileage reading when you started your work period.
* **End Mileage**: Enter the mileage reading when you completed your work period.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your work period.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Employer**: Select your employer from the drop-down list. (Note: This field is identical to the field on page 1, this field is not a duplicate but allows for selection from different options, if any available.)

This guide is meant to be used with the Employee Mileage Record Request Form, and it is recommended to be reviewed by the employee before submitting the form.
