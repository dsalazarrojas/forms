# Employee Absence Return To Work Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Absence Return To Work Form is used to communicate with employees regarding their return to work after an absence. This form allows employees to provide their expected date of return to work and any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Please fill in all the required fields (if any) and provide any relevant comments or information.
2. If you are returning to work, select the "On Duty" return to work status.
3. If you are not returning to work, select the "Not On Duty" return to work status.
4. If there is no information about your return to work status, select the "Unknown" return to work status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Physician's Name** (`physician_name`): Enter the name of the physician who has authorized you to return to work.
* **Signature** (`signature`): This field is for the employee's signature, which is optional but recommended for official purposes.
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`): Provide any relevant information about your employment status, such as your job title or department.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`): Enter the name of the doctor who has authorized you to return to work.
* **Signature** (`doctor_signature`): This is the doctor's signature, which is optional but recommended for official purposes.
* **Date Of Return To Work** (`date_return_to_work`): Select a date when you plan to return to work. 
* **Return To Work Status** (`date_return_to_work_status`): Choose whether you are required or not required to return to work on the selected date. 
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or information about your return to work status.
* **Date Returned** (`date_returned`): Enter the date you actually returned to work.
* **Return To Work Status** (`return_to_work_status`): Choose whether you were required or not required to return to work on the date you actually returned. 
* **Doctor Note** (`doctor_note`): Enter any additional information or notes from your doctor regarding your return to work status. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that the provided information is accurate and true to your situation.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact your supervisor or HR representative.
