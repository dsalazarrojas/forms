# weekly_timesheet_adjustment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly_timesheet_adjustment_request_form is a form used to request adjustments to an employee's timesheet for a specific week. This form is used to submit adjustments such as over-clocking, under-clocking, overtime, or other adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have not already submitted an adjustment for the same week.
* Select the relevant information from the options below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Enter your employee ID or name</span>. Please enter your unique identifier or name as it appears on your timesheet.
* **Week**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Select the week you are making the adjustment for</span>. Choose the week you are making adjustments for from the provided options.
* **Adjustment Type**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Select the type of adjustment you are making</span>. Choose from the options:
	+ Regular: A change to your regular hours worked.
	+ Overclock: Working more hours than scheduled.
	+ Underclock: Working less hours than scheduled.
	+ Overtime: Working additional hours beyond your regular hours.
	+ Other: Selecting this option will allow you to enter a custom adjustment type.
* **Start Date**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Enter the start date of the adjustment period</span>. Please enter the start date of the adjustment period in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Enter the end date of the adjustment period</span>. Please enter the end date of the adjustment period in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Hours**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Enter the total hours of adjustment</span>. Please enter the total number of hours of adjustment.
* **Notes**: <span style="font-weight: bold;">Provide a brief description of the adjustment</span>. Add any relevant details about the adjustment, such as any relevant documentation or context.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
* Please note that this form is only for adjustments to your own timesheet, and not for other employees. If you need to make adjustments for another employee, you will need to use a different form.
