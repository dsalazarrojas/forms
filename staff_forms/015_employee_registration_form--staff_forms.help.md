# Employee Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Registration Form is a simple registration form created for HR departments to accurately capture essential details of new employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Job Title.
5. Enter your Start Date of employment.
6. Enter your End Date of employment (optional).
7. Select your Department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your given name or the name your colleagues know you by.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the last name you were born with or the name your friends and family know you by.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your official company email address for communication.
* **Job Title** (`position`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your official job title and responsibilities.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your start date of employment in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your expected or actual end date of employment in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`. This field is not required.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Choose the department that you report to.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
- Enter your job title that reflects your actual job responsibilities. 
- If unsure, contact your HR department for guidance.
