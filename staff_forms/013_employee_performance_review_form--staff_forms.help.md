# Employee Performance Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate employee performance reviews, gathering feedback, strengths, and areas for improvement to promote growth and development within the organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the review date and enter the date of the performance review.
2. Enter your name as the reviewer.
3. Enter the employee's name.
4. Assess the employee's performance level, selecting from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
5. Provide feedback on the employee's performance.
6. Identify areas for improvement.
7. Note the employee's strengths.
8. Add any additional areas for improvement.
9. Set goals for the employee.
10. Develop a plan for improvement.
11. Sign and date the form with your reviewer signature.
12. Sign and date the form with the employee's signature.
13. Enter the date the review was completed.
14. Assess the overall performance of the employee.
15. Identify additional areas for improvement.
16. Provide comments, if needed.
17. Offer any recommendations.
18. Update the performance review status.
19. Enter the date of the next review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Review Date**: The date of the performance review.
* **Reviewer Name**: The name of the reviewer.
* **Employee Name**: The name of the employee being reviewed.
* **Performance Level**: A subjective evaluation of the employee's performance, with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback**: Comments or observations about the employee's performance.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Specific areas where the employee can improve.
* **Strengths**: The employee's positive qualities or skills.
* **Additional Areas for Improvement**: Any extra areas where the employee can improve.
* **Goals**: Goals for the employee's growth and development.
* **Development Plan**: A plan to help the employee improve.
* **Reviewer Signature**: The signature of the reviewer, confirming they have reviewed the form.
* **Employee Signature**: The signature of the employee, indicating they have seen and agreed with the review.
* **Date Reviewed**: The date the review was completed.
* **Overall Performance**: A subjective evaluation of the employee's overall performance, with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Areas for Improvement 3**: Additional areas for improvement.
* **Comments**: Comments or observations about the review.
* **Recommendations**: Any suggestions or recommendations for the employee.
* **Next Review Date**: The date of the next performance review.
* **Performance Review Status**: The status of the performance review, with options: Pending, In Progress, Completed, or Closed.
* **Date Reviewed 2**: The date of the next performance review.
*   **Employee Performance**: This field is redundant and is not used in this form.
* **Recommendation 2**: The recommendation field is removed as it was a duplicate field. 

**Tips**
* Be thorough when providing feedback and areas for improvement to help the employee grow.
* Use concrete and specific examples to support your feedback and recommendations.
* Keep the development plan realistic and achievable.
* Ensure the employee signs and dates the form, indicating they have seen and agreed with the review.
* Update the performance review status regularly.
