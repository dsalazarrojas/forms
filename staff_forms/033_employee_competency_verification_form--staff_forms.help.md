# Employee Competency Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a user's job title, job type, work experience, education, and other related details for competency verification. This form helps to gather relevant data for evaluating an employee's job competency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the options for 'Job Title', 'Job Type', and 'Job Category' fields as applicable to your job.
2. Select multiple options for 'Work Experience' fields as they apply to your job.
3. Enter 'Education' details if you have any relevant educational qualifications.
4. Provide a brief description of your 'Job Location' and 'Job Description'.
5. Fill in 'Reporting Manager' details, if applicable.
6. Select 'Job Level', 'Job Grade', and other category type as required.
7. Enter 'Job Function' details, if relevant.
8. Fill in any other required fields, such as 'Job Type Id', 'Job Category Id', etc.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (1, select_one, required/optional): Select the job title that best describes your job role.
* **Job Type** (2, select_one, required/optional): Choose the job type that corresponds to your job role.
* **Job Category** (3, select_one, required/optional): Select the job category that applies to your job.
* **Work Experience** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple options that apply to your work experience.
* **Education** (5, date, required/optional): Enter your education details if applicable.
* **Job Location** (6, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your job location.
* **Job Status** (7, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of your job.
* **Job Description** (8, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Reporting Manager** (9, text, required/optional): Fill in the reporting manager's details, if applicable.
* **Job Level** (10, select_one, required/optional): Select the job level that corresponds to your job.
* **Job Grade** (11, select_one, required/optional): Choose the job grade that applies to your job.
* **Job Category Type** (12, text, required/optional): Enter any other category type that applies to your job.
* **Job Function** (13, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your job function.
* **Job Type Id** (14, text, required/optional): Enter the job type ID.
* **Job Category Id** (15, text, required/optional): Enter the job category ID.
* **Job Subcategory Id** (16, text, required/optional): Enter the job subcategory ID.
* **Job Level Id** (17, text, required/optional): Enter the job level ID.
* **Job Grade Id** (18, text, required/optional): Enter the job grade ID.
* **Job Family Id** (19, text, required/optional): Enter the job family ID.
* **Job Status Id** (20, text, required/optional): Enter the job status ID.
* **Job Category Id** (21, text, required/optional): Enter the job category ID.
* **Job Function Id** (22, text, required/optional): Enter the job function ID.
* **Job Title Id** (23, text, required/optional): Enter the job title ID.
* **Job Location Id** (24, text, required/optional): Enter the job location ID.
* **Reporting Manager Id** (25, text, required/optional): Enter the reporting manager's ID.
