# Day Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Day Off Request Form is used by staff members to request a day off from work. This form is designed to ensure that staff members can request time off in a structured and organized way, so that management can review and approve or deny the request accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your requested day off details, including the date of absence, start and end time, and reason for taking the day off.
2. Ensure that you have discussed your request with your supervisor or HR representative before submitting the form.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Day Off Request** (`day_off_request`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Date of Absence** (`date_of_absence`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to take off.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, not required): Enter the start time of your absence.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, not required): Enter the end time of your absence.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, not required): Enter a valid reason for taking the day off.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, not required): Provide your signature to confirm the request.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, note, not required): This field is for internal use only.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Approved Status** (`approved_status`, select_multiple, not required): Select the approved status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.

Note: The fields marked as "not required" are optional, but it's essential to fill them out accurately and completely for a smooth approval process.
