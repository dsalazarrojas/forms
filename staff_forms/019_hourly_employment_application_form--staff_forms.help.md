# Hourly Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect detailed information from job applicants about their employment history, education, and skills acquired.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your employer name as it is listed on your identification or business card.
2. Enter the job title you are applying for.
3. Describe your educational background, including institutions and degrees earned.
4. Provide a brief summary of your work experience, including job titles and duration.
5. List your relevant skills, including programming languages, tools, and software proficiency.
6. Repeat steps 3, 4, and 5 for each additional section, as there are multiple sections of education, experience, and skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, required): Please enter the name of your current or previous employer as it is listed on your business card or identification.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, required): Enter the job title you are applying for or currently hold.
- **Education 1** (`education`, required): Provide a brief description of your educational background, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
- **Experience 1** (`experience`, required): Describe your work history, including job titles, duration, and job descriptions.
- **Skills 1** (`skills`, optional): List your relevant skills, including programming languages, tools, and software proficiency.
- **Education 2** (`education_2`, optional): Similar to Education 1, but for additional education section.
- **Experience 2** (`experience_2`, optional): Similar to Experience 1, but for additional experience section.
- **Skills 2** (`skills_2`, optional): Similar to Skills 1, but for additional skills section.
- **Education 3-8** (`education_3`-`education_8`, optional): Additional sections for education background.
- **Experience 3-8** (`experience_3`-`experience_8`, optional): Additional sections for work experience.
- **Skills 3-8** (`skills_3`-`skills_8`, optional): Additional sections for skills acquired.

## Tips
- Make sure all required fields are filled out completely.
- Use separate lines or bullet points for listing multiple institutions, jobs, or skills.
- If you have a lot of experience, please be concise but still provide detailed information.
- If you have multiple education backgrounds, list each one separately and provide details.
