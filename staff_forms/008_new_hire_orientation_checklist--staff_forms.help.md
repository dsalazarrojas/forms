# New Hire Orientation Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Hire Orientation Checklist is used to gather information about new hires, ensuring a smooth transition into the company. This form captures essential details about the employee's identification, employment, and supervisor's information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the employee's Date of Birth, Hire Date, and Employee ID.
3. Select the Job Title and Department from the list provided.
4. Enter the Job Status (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
5. Enter the name of the employee's Supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter the employee's first name as it is typically spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's last name as it is typically spelled.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the employee's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee started working for the company.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Select the employee's job title from the list provided.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select the employee's department from the list provided.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, text, required): Select the employee's current job status (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee's direct supervisor. 

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact the HR department for guidance.
