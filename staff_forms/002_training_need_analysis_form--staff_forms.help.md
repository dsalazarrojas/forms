# Training Need Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Need Analysis Form is designed to assist in identifying the training needs of staff members. This form helps managers and administrators assess whether staff members require training on specific topics, allowing them to focus on areas where development is needed to improve performance or meet job demands.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "page1" as the starting page.
2. Review the pages and ensure each section is relevant to the training needs of the staff member. Each page contains fields for analysis and feedback.
3. Provide accurate and thoughtful feedback for each question.
4. Once you've completed all the pages, submit the form to submit your analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **page1 (Page 1, Note, Optional)**: This page is a general information page, providing an opportunity to record any relevant notes or comments about the staff member's performance and training needs.
* **main (Page 2, Select Multiple, Optional)**: On this page, please indicate whether the staff member requires training on the following areas:
	+ Yes: The staff member requires training on this area.
	+ No: The staff member does not require training on this area.
* **main (Page 3, Number, Optional)**: Enter the number of years the staff member has been performing in their current role.
* **main (Page 4, Select One, Optional)**: Please select one of the following:
	+ Yes: The staff member requires additional support to perform their role effectively.
	+ No: The staff member is performing well and does not require additional support.

Note: All fields are optional and can be completed by the staff member themselves or their manager. Please review and provide accurate feedback for each section to ensure a thorough analysis of training needs.
