# Training Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from staff members on their experience with the training program. This will help the training team to understand their strengths and weaknesses, and make improvements for future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Select one of the options for "how_satisfied" in the first section.
3. Choose one of the options for "overall_satisfaction" in the second section.
4. If you have any areas for improvement, type them in the "areas_for_improvement" section.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the "comments" section.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How Satisfied (`how_satisfied`, `select_one`, **Yes/No**)
* This question asks you to rate how satisfied you were with the training.

### Overall Satisfaction (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, **Satisfied/Dissatisfied**)
* This question asks for your overall satisfaction with the training.

### Areas for Improvement (`areas_for_improvement`, `note`, **Text Input**)
* This section is for any areas that you think could be improved in future training sessions.

### Comments (`comments`, `note`, **Text Input**)
* This section is for any additional comments or feedback you have about the training session.
