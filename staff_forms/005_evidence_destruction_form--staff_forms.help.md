# evidence_destruction_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The evidence_destruction_form is designed to capture information about the destruction of evidence in a structured and organized manner. It serves as a template for staff members to report the destruction of evidence, which may include documents, data, or other materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the evidence_destruction_form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the form category (staff_forms or other) to indicate the type of form you are completing.
*   Provide a brief description of the form in the "what is evidence_destruction_form" field.
*   Enter the description of the evidence being destroyed in the "description" field.
*   Enter the title of the form in the "form_title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Staff Forms** (`form_category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category that best describes the type of form you are completing.
    *   This field helps to classify the form as either a staff form or other.
*   **What is evidence_destruction_form?** (`evidence_destruction_form_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the form to provide context.
    *   This field is for providing a brief description of the form, explaining what the form is about.
*   **Description** (`form_description`, text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the evidence being destroyed.
    *   This field is for providing a detailed explanation of the evidence being destroyed.
*   **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the form.
    *   This field is for providing a title to the form, which can be a name that captures the essence of the form's content.
