# Inventory Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assist staff in managing inventory data accurately and efficiently. This form is used to input detailed information about items, including store name, date, time, description, quantity, price, and category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the store name from the options listed below (if applicable).
2. Enter the manager's name.
3. Fill in the date of the inventory management action.
4. Enter the time of the inventory management action.
5. Describe the item being managed.
6. Enter the quantity of the item.
7. Input the price of the item.
8. Choose the category of the item from the options listed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the store where the item is located.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the manager responsible for the item.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inventory management action. Note that this field is for the date, not the timestamp.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the inventory management action. Note that this field is for the time, not the date.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the item being managed.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of items available in stock.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required: false): Input the price of the item.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the item from the options listed below:
	+ electronics
	+ clothes
	+ home goods
	+ books
	+ sports
	+ toys

## Tips

* Be thorough when filling out this form to ensure accurate inventory management.
* Double-check the entered information to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult your supervisor or colleague for clarification.
* Make sure to update this form regularly to reflect changes in stock levels and prices.
