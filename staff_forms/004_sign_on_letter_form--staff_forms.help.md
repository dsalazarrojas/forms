# sign_on_letter_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a sign-on letter, typically used in business or professional settings. The form allows users to input their name, organization's name, and the group they belong to, as well as any additional participation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name**: Type your name in the text field provided.
2. **Enter your organization's name**: Type the name of your organization in the text field provided.
3. **Select your group**: Choose one or more groups that you belong to from the list of options.
4. **Add any additional participation details**: Type any relevant information about your participation in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign On Letter Form** (`text`, optional): This field is where you enter your name for the sign-on letter.
* **Organization Name** (`text`, optional): This field is where you enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Group** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more groups that you belong to from the list of options.
* **Participation** (`text`, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your participation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in the fields.
* If you belong to multiple groups, select all relevant groups in the **Organization Group** field.
* If you have any additional participation details, provide a clear and concise description in the **Participation** field.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you may leave them blank if not applicable.
