# Civil Service Employee Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help civil service employees evaluate their own performance and set goals for future periods. It's an internal tool for self-reflection and growth, and is used to assess individual progress, identify areas for improvement, and provide input for performance evaluations. Please use this form to be honest and thorough in your self-assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your full name, position title, and review period (e.g., Jan to Dec 2025).
2. Describe your major accomplishments during the review period, highlighting key achievements.
3. Set specific, measurable performance goals for the next period.
4. Identify any training or development needs to improve your skills or performance.
5. If necessary, specify any challenges faced or resource constraints that impacted your work.
6. State any manager support needed.
7. Rate your overall performance on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
8. Finally, submit the form with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Position Title** (2, text, required): Provide your job title as per the official records.
* **Review Period** (3, text, required): Enter the period for which you are being evaluated, using the format "Jan to Dec 2025".
* **Key Achievements** (4, text, optional): List your major accomplishments during the review period. Be specific and detailed in your descriptions.
* **Performance Goals for Next Period** (5, text, optional): Set specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) goals for the next review period.
* **Training and Development Needs** (6, text, optional): Identify areas where you need training or development to improve your performance.
* **Challenges Faced** (7, text, optional): Describe any significant challenges or resource constraints that impacted your work during the review period.
* **Manager Support Needed** (8, text, optional): If you require specific assistance from your manager, please specify.
* **Overall Self-Rating** (9, number, required): Rate your performance on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Submission Date** (10, date, required): Enter the current date to complete the form.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your self-assessment.
* Use specific examples and metrics to support your accomplishments and goals.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and clarity.
* Submit the form with the current date to demonstrate your commitment to timely evaluation and growth.
