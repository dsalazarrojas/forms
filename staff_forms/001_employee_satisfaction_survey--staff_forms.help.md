# Employee Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Satisfaction Survey is a tool to gather feedback and insights from employees on their work experience. It is a voluntary survey that allows employees to share their thoughts and feelings on their contact details, departments, job titles, work hours, and manager names.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select your contact details (contact_details).
3. Choose your department and departments you work with (department and department_2).
4. Input your job titles (job_title and job_title_2).
5. Input your work hours (work_hours, work_hours_2, and work_hours_3).
6. Select your manager names (manager_name and manager_name_2).
7. Review your responses and make any necessary changes.
8. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_satisfaction_survey** (`employee_satisfaction_survey`, note, optional):
	+ This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose one of the provided options for your contact details:
		- John
		- Jane
		- Joe
* **department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more departments you work with:
		- Marketing
		- Sales
		- Support
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, optional):
	+ Input your job title as it is currently used on your employee profile.
* **job_title_2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional):
	+ Input your job title as it is currently used on your employee profile (if different from job_title).
* **work_hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional):
	+ Input the number of hours you work per week.
* **work_hours_2** (`work_hours_2`, number, optional):
	+ Input the number of hours you work per week (if different from work_hours).
* **work_hours_3** (`work_hours_3`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the days you work:
		- Monday
		- Tuesday
		- Wednesday
* **department_2** (`department_2`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more departments you work with:
		- Marketing
		- Sales
		- Support
		- Other
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose one of the provided options for your manager's name:
		- John
		- Jane
		- Joe
* **manager_name_2** (`manager_name_2`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose one of the provided options for your manager's name:
		- John
		- Jane
		- Joe

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can leave the field blank.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use the free-text field (employee_satisfaction_survey).
* The form is only to be filled out by employees, so please do not share your login credentials with anyone.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
