# COVID 19 Staff And Visitor Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track staff and visitor information for COVID-19 purposes, including entry and exit dates and times, person type, contact information, and health screening questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct person type from the options provided.
2. Enter the full name of the person, including first and last name.
3. Enter the company or department name.
4. Enter the phone number and email address of the person.
5. Select the area of the facility visited.
6. Select the names of any staff members or people met during the visit.
7. Enter any vehicle information, if applicable.
8. Answer the health screening questions, including temperature reading and symptoms.
9. Answer the exposure and travel history questions.
10. Check the box to consent to contact tracing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`): The date of entry into the facility.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`): The time of entry into the facility.
* **Exit Time** (`exit_time`): The time of exit from the facility.
* **Person Type** (`person_type`): Choose the correct category for the person visiting (Employee or staff, Contractor or vendor, Visitor, Delivery person, Patient or client, Other).
* **Full Name** (`full_name`): Enter the first and last name of the person.
* **Company or Department** (`company_or_department`): Enter the organization or department name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter the contact email.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the person's job title, if applicable.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`): Enter the person's street address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city of residence.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state or province of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`): Enter the postal code.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`): Choose the reason for the visit (Business meeting, Delivery or pickup, Maintenance or repair, Healthcare appointment, Interview, Training or education, Social visit, Other).
* **Purpose Details** (`purpose_details`): Enter more information about the visit.
* **Areas of Facility Visited** (`areas_visited`): Select the locations within the building visited.
* **Names of People Met** (`people_met`): Enter the names of staff or people met during the visit.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`): Enter the vehicle details, if applicable.
* **Temperature Reading** (`temperature`): Enter the temperature reading, if applicable.
* **Symptoms Screening** (`symptom_screening`): Answer the questions about COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Recent Exposure** (`recent_exposure`): Answer the question about contact with a COVID-19 positive person.
* **Recent Travel** (`recent_travel`): Answer the question about travel history.
* **Mask Worn** (`mask_worn`): Check if you are wearing a mask.
* **Hand Sanitized** (`hand_sanitized`): Check if you sanitized your hands.
* **Host or Staff Member Name** (`host_name`): Enter the name of the person receiving you.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Enter any other information.
* **Contact Tracing Consent** (`contact_tracing_consent`): Check if you consent to contact tracing.
