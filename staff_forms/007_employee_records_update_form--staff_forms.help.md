# Employee Records Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Records Update Form is designed to update existing employee records, keeping their information up-to-date and accurate.
## How To Complete This Form
To update an employee's record, follow these steps:
1. Enter the employee's First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select the employee's Department Id.
4. Enter the employee's Department Name.
5. Select the employee's Position Id.
6. Enter the employee's Position Name.
7. Enter the employee's Job Title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's last name.
* **Department Id** (`Department Id`, `number`, required): Select the employee's department ID. If you're unsure, you can check the list of available departments.
* **Department Name** (`Department Name`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's department name.
* **Position Id** (`Position Id`, `number`, required): Select the employee's position ID. If you're unsure, you can check the list of available positions.
* **Position Name** (`Position Name`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's position name.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's job title.

## Tips
- Double-check the employee's existing record before making changes.
- Use the correct format for department and position IDs.
- If you need to update multiple employees' records, fill out the form multiple times, or use our bulk update feature.
