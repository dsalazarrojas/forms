# employee_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_information_form is a data collection tool used to gather information about employees. This form is used to update or create employee records in the organization's database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your Email address in the **Email** field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your Job Title in the **Job Title** field.
5. Enter the date you were hired in the **Date Hired** field. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
7. Enter your Department in the **Department** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name, e.g., John.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name, e.g., Doe.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address, e.g., johndoe@example.com.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your current job title, e.g., Software Engineer.
* **Date Hired** (`date_hired`, date, required: false): Enter the date you were hired in the format MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 01/01/2020.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, e.g., 123-456-7890.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter your department, e.g., IT Department.
