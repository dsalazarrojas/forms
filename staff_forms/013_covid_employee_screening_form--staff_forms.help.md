# Covid Employee Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather relevant information regarding an employee's health status and travel history in regards to COVID-19. This form is used by HR or management to ensure that employees are accurately reporting their health conditions and travel experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your General Info in the first section.
2. Fill in your Employee Details in the second section.
3. Select your current Health Status from the options provided in the third section.
4. Enter your current body temperature, if applicable.
5. Choose any applicable Symptoms from the list provided.
6. Describe any recent Exposure History.
7. Provide a summary of your recent Travel History.
8. Select the most suitable option regarding Contact Trace, if applicable.
9. Choose the best option for Health Clinics, if applicable.
10. Add any additional Notes, if necessary.
11. Select who Submitted You to fill out this form.
12. Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info**: This section is where you provide basic information such as your name, employee ID, and contact information.
* **Employee Details**: Enter your job title, department, and any other relevant employee information.
* **Health Status**: Select the option that best describes your current health status. This will help HR or management track and monitor employees' health in relation to COVID-19.
* **Temperature**: If you have a fever or other health concerns, enter your current body temperature.
* **Symptoms**: Select any symptoms you are experiencing related to COVID-19, such as cough, sore throat, etc.
* **Exposure History**: Describe any recent exposures to COVID-19, such as contact with an infected person or visiting a high-risk area.
* **Travel History**: Provide a brief summary of your recent travel history, including destinations visited and dates traveled.
* **Contact Trace**: Select who you were in contact with regarding COVID-19, if applicable.
* **Health Clinics**: If you visited a health clinic or received a COVID-19 test, select the most relevant option.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information that you feel is relevant to your health status or travel history.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted you to complete this form.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date you submitted this form.

This form is intended to be completed by employees to provide accurate and up-to-date information regarding their health status and travel history. It is essential to be honest and accurate when filling out this form to help prevent the spread of COVID-19.
