# Visitor Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Log Form is used to record visitor information and interactions with staff members. It should be used for visitors who come to the office to meet with staff members. This form is not intended for internal staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name here.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name here.

### Title
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, optional): Enter your title or profession here.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address here. Please use the format: `your_email@example.com`.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number here.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your visit in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your visit.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your visit here.

### Category
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, optional): This field is currently not used. You can leave it blank.

### Staff Name
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the staff member you are meeting with.

### Staff Title
* **Staff Title** (`staff_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the staff member you are meeting with.

### Purpose
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for your visit here.

### Visitors
* **Visitors** (`visitors`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all visitors in your group, separated by commas. For example: "Guest, Tour, Workshop".

### Staff Signature
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, `text`, optional): This field is not currently used. You can leave it blank.

### Visitor Signature
* **Visitor Signature** (`visitor_signature`, `text`, optional): This field is not currently used. You can leave it blank.
