# Per Diem Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Per Diem Request Form is designed to help staff members request reimbursement for expenses related to work-related travel. This form will guide you through the steps to fill out the form and help you understand the purpose of each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the requested travel dates, specifying the start and end dates of your trip.
* Select the destination of your trip from the dropdown menu.
* Enter the dates you will be traveling from your home city to the destination, if applicable.
* Enter the total allowance amount you are requesting.
* Provide any additional information about your trip in the "Additional Info" field.
* If applicable, attach any supporting documents or receipts to your request.
* Select the approver and/or employees involved in your trip.
* Enter any comments or notes about your request.
* Finally, select the current status of your request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Per Diem Request Date**: This field is used to specify the start date of your request.
* **Per Diem Request Date To**: This field is used to specify the end date of your request.
* **Per Diem Request Destination**: This field allows you to select the destination of your trip from the provided options.
* **Per Diem Request Travel Date From**: This field is used to specify the date you will be traveling from your home city.
* **Per Diem Request Travel Date To**: This field is used to specify the date you will be traveling to the destination.
* **Per Diem Request Allowance**: This field is used to enter the total allowance amount you are requesting.
* **Per Diem Request Additional Info**: This field is used to provide any additional information about your trip.
* **Per Diem Request Attachment**: This field is used to upload any supporting documents or receipts related to your request.
* **Per Diem Request Approver**: This field is used to select the person who will review and approve your request.
* **Per Diem Request Employees**: This field is used to select the employees who will be involved in your trip.
* **Per Diem Request Comments**: This field is used to enter any comments or notes about your request.
* **Per Diem Request Approval Status**: This field is used to select the current status of your request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected). 

Note: Fields with "required: false" labels may be left blank or skipped if not applicable, but it's recommended to fill out all relevant information to ensure accurate processing of your request.
