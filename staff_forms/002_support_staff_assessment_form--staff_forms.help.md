# Support Staff Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Support Staff Assessment Form is used to collect feedback or assessment on staff performance to evaluate and improve work quality and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the relevant information from the available options in each field.
* Ensure you are filling in all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
* For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (Select)**: This field is used to indicate whether the staff member has provided user info or not. Select 'Yes' if the staff member has provided user info and 'No' otherwise.
* **Performance (Number)**: This field is used to assess the staff's performance on a scale of 0 to 100. Please input a number within this range to provide an accurate assessment.
* **Feedback (Text)**: This field is used to provide any feedback or comments on the staff's performance. Please provide any relevant information or suggestions you think would be helpful.
* **Comments (Text)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or information that are not covered in the feedback section.
* **Assigned Tool (Select)**: This field is used to indicate whether the staff has been assigned a tool or not. Select 'Yes' if the staff member has been assigned a tool and 'No' otherwise.
* **Form Id (Text)**: This field is used to identify the form being assessed. Please provide a unique identifier for the form.
* **Category (Text)**: This field is used to provide the category of the form. Please provide the relevant category name or description.
* **Description (Text)**: This field is used to describe the form being assessed. Please provide a brief description of the form.
* **Form Title (Text)**: This field is used to provide the title of the form. Please provide the title of the form.
* **Assigned To (Select)**: This field is used to indicate whether the form is assigned to a staff member or not. Select 'Yes' if the form is assigned to a staff member and 'No' otherwise.
