# Disciplinary Action Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and track employee disciplinary actions within the organization. It is designed to gather information related to incidents or actions that require disciplinary measures, and to record the steps taken and outcome of the disciplinary process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Employee ID (1)**: Enter the employee's ID number to identify the individual involved in the incident.
2.  **Date of Incident (2)**: Enter the date of the incident or action that requires disciplinary measures.
3.  **Description (3)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including the actions taken or decisions made.
4.  **Action Taken (4)**: Describe the disciplinary actions taken, such as verbal or written warnings, suspension, or termination.
5.  **Outcome (5)**: Choose the outcome of the disciplinary process, which is one or both of:
    *   Yes: The disciplinary process is complete and the outcome is favorable.
    *   No: The disciplinary process is still ongoing or not complete.
6.  **Employee ID (6)**: This field is a duplicate of the employee ID already provided in field 1. It is recommended to leave this field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee ID (1)**: A unique identifier assigned to each employee, used to track individual records.
*   **Date of Incident (2)**: The date when the incident or action occurred.
*   **Description (3)**: A detailed account of the incident or action, including all relevant information and context.
*   **Action Taken (4)**: A description of the disciplinary actions taken, including any verbal or written warnings, suspension, or termination.
*   **Outcome (5)**: An indicator of the status of the disciplinary process, which can be either complete or still ongoing.
*   **Employee ID (6)**: Another duplicate field of the employee ID provided in field 1.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you have permission to access and use this form.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure you are entering the correct information.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact the relevant authorities or supervisors for clarification.
*   Save your progress regularly to avoid losing information.
*   This form is only to be used for internal organizational purposes and should not be shared with external parties.

Please note that field 6 is not necessary to be filled, as it is a duplicate of field 1.
