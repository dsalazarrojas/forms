# Employee Self Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your performance, skills, and overall rating as an employee. It is used to provide a comprehensive overview of your strengths and weaknesses, which can be used for future development and growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
2. For the "Assigned Tools" fields, provide specific details about the tools you have been assigned to work with.
3. Ensure all fields are filled out completely, unless specified otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This field is used to capture any general information about you, such as name, address, etc.
* **Work Performance**: This field is used to evaluate your performance in your current role. You can select up to 5 options to describe your performance.
* **Skills**: This field is used to evaluate your skills and competencies in your current role. You can select one option to describe your skills.
* **Career Development**: This field is used to provide any additional information about your career development goals and aspirations.
* **Communication Skills**: This field is used to rate your communication skills, using a numerical value.
* **Work Life Balance**: This field is used to rate your work-life balance, using a numerical value.
* **Feedback**: This field is used to provide any feedback or comments about your performance or any issues you would like to address.
* **Overall Rating**: This field is used to rate your overall performance, using one of the three options provided.
* **Date**: This field is used to record the date of completion of this form.
* **Time**: This field is used to record the time of completion of this form.
* **Note**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes about your performance.
* **Email**: This field is used to record your email address, which will be used for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: This field is used to record your phone number, which will be used for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool 1-12**: These fields are used to capture any additional information about the tools you have been assigned to work with. Please provide specific details for each tool.

Note: The form fields are not required to be filled out completely. However, please ensure that all required fields are accurately and honestly answered.
