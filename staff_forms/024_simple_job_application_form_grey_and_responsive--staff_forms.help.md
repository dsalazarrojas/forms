# simple_job_application_form_grey_and_responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic job application information to facilitate the job application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your job application details in the fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application** (`job_application`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Job Skills** (`job_skills`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you possess the necessary skills for the job by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the hiring team or the job poster.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it outside of our organization.
