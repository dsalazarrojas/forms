# Employee Direct Deposit Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees to enroll them in direct deposit for their paychecks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Company Name, Address, Zip Code, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, and Bank Account.
2. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name and phone number.
3. Enter your email address and phone number.
4. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your physical address where you live.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required): Enter your 9-digit social security number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Bank Account** (`bank_account`, text, required): Enter your bank account number for direct deposit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
