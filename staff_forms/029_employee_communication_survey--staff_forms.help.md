# Employee Communication Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Employee Communication Survey" form is designed to gather information about how employees communicate with each other and with their managers through different channels such as email, phone, in-person, etc. The purpose of this survey is to collect data on employee communication preferences, frequency of communication, and channel preferences for different types of communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Use the provided examples to guide you through the survey.
3. Take your time to answer each question carefully.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro-page** (`intro-page`, `note`, required: false): This is an introduction page to the survey, providing a brief overview of the survey's purpose and goals.
* **How often do you get emails?** (`how_often_email`, `number`, required: false): How often do you receive emails from your team, managers, or other departments?
* **How often do you get SMS?** (`how_often_sms`, `number`, required: false): How often do you receive SMS messages from your team, managers, or other departments?
* **How often do you get calls?** (`how_often_calls`, `number`, required: false): How often do you receive phone calls from your team, managers, or other departments?
* **How often do you have meetings?** (`how_often_meetings`, `number`, required: false): How often do you have meetings with your team or managers?
* **How often do you use Instant Message?** (`how_often_instant_msg`, `number`, required: false): How often do you use Instant Message to communicate with your team or managers?
* **How often do you use Video Call?** (`how_often_video_call`, `number`, required: false): How often do you use Video Call to communicate with your team or managers?
* **How often do you use Text Message?** (`how_often_text_msg`, `number`, required: false): How often do you use Text Message to communicate with your team or managers?
* **How often do you get phone calls?** (`how_often_phone_call`, `number`, required: false): How often do you receive phone calls from your team, managers, or other departments?
* **How often do you have in-person communication?** (`how_often_in_person`, `number`, required: false): How often do you have in-person communication with your team or managers?
* **What is your channel preference?** (`what_channel_pref`, `select_one`, required: false): What is your preferred channel of communication (Email, SMS, Instant Message, Phone, In Person, Video Call)?
* **How often do you get emails from your manager?** (`how_often_email_to_manager`, `number`, required: false): How often do you receive emails from your manager?
* **How often do you get emails from your direct manager?** (`how_often_email_to_direct_manager`, `number`, required: false): How often do you receive emails from your direct manager?
* **How often do you get emails from your indirect manager?** (`how_often_email_to_indirect_manager`, `number`, required: false): How often do you receive emails from your indirect manager?
* **How often do you get emails from your team?** (`how_often_email_to_team`, `number`, required: false): How often do you receive emails from your team?
* **Email preference** (`email_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): What is your email preference frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Never)?
* **Meeting preference** (`meeting_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): What is your meeting preference frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Never)?
* **Instant Message preference** (`instant_msg_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How often do you use Instant Message (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)?
* **Video Call preference** (`video_call_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How often do you use Video Call (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)?
* **Text Message preference** (`text_msg_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How often do you use Text Message (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)?
* **Phone Call preference** (`phone_call_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How often do you use Phone Call (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)?
* **In-person preference** (`in_person_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How often do you have in-person communication (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)?
* **Manager Communication preference** (`manager_communication_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How often do you prefer to communicate with your manager (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)?
* **Direct Manager Communication preference** (`direct_manager_communication_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How often do you prefer to communicate with your direct manager (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)?
* **Indirect Manager Communication preference** (`indirect_manager_communication_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How often do you prefer to communicate with your indirect manager (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)?
