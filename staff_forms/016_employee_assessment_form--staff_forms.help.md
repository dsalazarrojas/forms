# Employee Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Assessment Form is designed to collect employee's details for general assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the employee's last name.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title or role.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the employee's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Contact Number**: Enter the employee's contact number.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information about the employee.
* If any of the fields are not applicable, leave them blank or enter "Not Applicable" as a placeholder.
* Please use the correct format for the Date of Birth field (YYYY-MM-DD).
