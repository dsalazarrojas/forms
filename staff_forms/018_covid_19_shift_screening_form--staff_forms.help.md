# COVID 19 Shift Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Shift Screening Form is a tool used to collect information about employees' health status, potential exposure risks, and other relevant details before they start their shift. This form helps to identify potential COVID-19 cases and prevent the spread of the virus within the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you are being screened for work using the date picker.
2. Select the time of your screening using the time picker.
3. Choose your shift type from the available options (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, or Rotating).
4. Enter your employee full name, employee ID, department, and position.
5. Report your current temperature in Fahrenheit.
6. Answer the questions about symptoms, household exposure, travel, and previous positive test results.
7. Select your vaccination status (Fully vaccinated with booster, Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, or Not vaccinated).
8. Indicate if you will be required to wear PPE and if you have access to the required PPE.
9. Select your screening result (Cleared for work, Not cleared - needs evaluation, Not cleared - positive test, or Not cleared - exposure risk).
10. Provide the name and title of the person conducting the screening.
11. Sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Screening Date**: Enter the date you are being screened for work using the date picker.
* **Screening Time**: Select the time of your screening using the time picker.
* **Shift Type**: Choose your shift type from the available options (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, or Rotating).
* **Employee Full Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID.
* **Department**: Enter your department.
* **Position**: Enter your job title.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your direct manager's name.
* **Current Temperature**: Report your current temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Do you have a fever of 100.4 F or higher?**: Answer this question truthfully.
* **Are you currently experiencing any symptoms?**: Answer this question truthfully.
* **Which symptoms are you experiencing?**: Select all applicable symptoms from the list.
* **Does anyone in your household have COVID-19 symptoms?**: Answer this question truthfully.
* **Has anyone in your household tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days?**: Answer this question truthfully.
* **Have you had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days?**: Answer this question truthfully.
* **Have you traveled outside the area in the past 14 days?**: Answer this question truthfully.
* **List travel locations**: Enter cities and states visited (optional).
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 90 days?**: Answer this question truthfully.
* **What is your vaccination status?**: Select your vaccination status from the available options.
* **Will you be required to wear PPE during your shift?**: Indicate if you will be required to wear PPE.
* **Do you have access to the required PPE?**: Indicate if you have access to the required PPE.
* **Screening Result**: Select your clearance status (Cleared for work, Not cleared - needs evaluation, Not cleared - positive test, or Not cleared - exposure risk).
* **Screener Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the screening.
* **Screener Title**: Enter the title of the person conducting the screening.
* **Screener Signature Date**: Date and time of screening completion.
* **I certify that the information provided is accurate and I understand I must report any symptoms that develop during my shift**: Agree or disagree to this statement.
