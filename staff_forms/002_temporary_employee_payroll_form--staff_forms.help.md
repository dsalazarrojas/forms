# Temporary Employee Payroll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for temporary employees' payroll. It is essential to fill it out accurately to ensure that the employee's pay is processed correctly and on time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2.  Review the form for accuracy before submitting.
3.  Ensure you have all necessary documents or information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Hire Date** (`<name>`, `date`, required/optional: required): This field collects the date of when the employee started working for the company.
*   **Job Title** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional: required): Enter the employee's job title as assigned by the department they will be working in.
*   **Social Security Number** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional: required): Enter the employee's social security number for payroll processing.
*   **Tax Filing Status** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required/optional: optional): This field is used to determine how you file your taxes. Select one of the following:
    *   Single
    *   Married
    *   Separately
*   **Tax Exemption** (`<name>`, `number`, required/optional: optional): Enter your tax exemption number, if applicable.
*   **First Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional: required): Enter the employee's first name.
*   **Last Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional: required): Enter the employee's last name.
*   **Employee ID** (`<name>`, `number`, required/optional: required): Enter the employee's ID number, which is required for payroll processing.

Note: This form has no fields for email, contact information or address so they are not included in the field-by-field explanation. The form is for basic employee information and tax related fields.
