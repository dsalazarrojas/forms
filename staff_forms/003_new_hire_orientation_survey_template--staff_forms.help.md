# New Hire Orientation Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Orientation Survey Template is a tool to gather feedback from new hires on their experience with the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each section thoroughly and honestly.
2. If you have already submitted feedback, please do not submit another form unless you have new information or concerns.
3. If you are unsure about any section, please contact the responsible person listed on the form.
4. Please be specific with your feedback, providing examples or details whenever possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Orientation Survey Template** (`text`, required): A brief description of the new hire experience.
* **Onboarding Process** (`note`, optional): If you have any feedback or comments about the onboarding process, please provide them here.
* **Feedback on the New Hire Process** (`text`, optional): If you have any feedback or comments about the new hire process, please provide them here.
* **Overall Impression on the New Hire Process** (`select_one`, optional): Please select one of the options that best describes your overall impression:
	+ Option 1: The onboarding process was clear and efficient.
	+ Option 2: The onboarding process was somewhat clear, but had a few issues.
	+ Option 3: The onboarding process was confusing and needed improvement.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the onboarding process, please provide them here.
* **New Hire Survey Completion Time** (`time`, optional): The time it took you to complete this form:
	+ Please format as HH:MM (Hours:Minutes) or just enter the time it took you.
