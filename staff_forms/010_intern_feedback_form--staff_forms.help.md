# Intern Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from interns on their work experience, supervisor, and work environment. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest and constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your supervisor's feedback about your work experience.
2. Fill in your supervisor's name and email address so they can contact you.
3. Fill in your supervisor's phone number so they can contact you directly.
4. Review and submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intern Feedback Form 1** (`intern_feedback_form_1`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is where you can fill in your supervisor's feedback about your work experience. Please provide any comments, suggestions, or observations that you think your supervisor can use to improve the work experience.
* **Intern Supervisor Feedback** (`intern_supervisor_feedback_label`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is where you can provide additional feedback about your supervisor's feedback. If you have any questions or concerns about their feedback, you can express them here.
* **Intern Supervisor Name** (`intern_supervisor_name_label`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is where you can fill in your supervisor's name. Please make sure to spell their name correctly.
* **Intern Supervisor Email** (`intern_supervisor_email_label`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is where you can fill in your supervisor's email address. Please make sure to type it correctly.
* **Intern Supervisor Phone** (`intern_supervisor_phone_label`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is where you can fill in your supervisor's phone number. Please make sure to type it correctly.

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive with your feedback.
* Keep your feedback clear and concise.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to your supervisor or HR team.
