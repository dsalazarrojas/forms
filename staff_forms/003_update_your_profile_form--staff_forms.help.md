# Update Your Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to update your personal profile information, allowing you to review and modify your details as needed. Please fill out this form to ensure your profile accurately reflects your current information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each field with your personal details as follows:
	+ First name
	+ Last name
	+ Email (a valid email address)
	+ Phone number (optional)
	+ Address (optional)
	+ Zip code
	+ Birthday (in YYYY-MM-DD format)
	+ Time of birth (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address is required for communication and verification purposes. Please enter a current and active email address that you have access to.
* **Phone number**: If you'd like to add or update your phone number, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Address**: If you'd like to add or update your address, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Zip code**: Enter your current zip or postal code as it is displayed on your identification documents.
* **Birthday**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).
* **Time of birth**: If you'd like to add or update your time of birth, enter it here. This is optional. Please format the time as HH:MM in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:30).

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out to update your profile correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for assistance from our support team.
* Once you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
