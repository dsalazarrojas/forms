# Daily Shift Report Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to provide an efficient way for employees to report their daily shift information. It captures essential details such as shift date, start and end times, work hours, break time, employee status, assigned tool, and shift notes. Completing this form ensures accurate and timely record-keeping, helping management to monitor and manage shifts effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the shift date on the first page.
2. Enter your employee name as it appears on your personnel record.
3. Report your shift start and end times accurately.
4. Enter the number of hours you worked and any breaks you took.
5. Select your employee status, which indicates whether you are active or inactive during the shift.
6. Indicate whether a tool was assigned to you during the shift.
7. Provide any notes about your shift, such as notable events or issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your shift in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, required/optional): Report the start time of your shift in 24-hour format (`HH:mm` or `HH:MM:SS`).
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, required/optional): Report the end time of your shift in 24-hour format (`HH:mm` or `HH:MM:SS`).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of hours you worked during the shift.
* **Break Time** (`break_time`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of break minutes you took during the shift.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Active" if you were working during the shift, "Inactive" if you were not.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Check "Yes" if a tool was assigned to you during the shift, "No" otherwise.
* **Shift Notes** (`shift_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any notes about your shift, such as notable events or issues.

## Tips

* Be sure to report your shift dates accurately to ensure proper record-keeping.
* Use your personnel record name as your employee name.
* Enter work hours and break time in decimal format (e.g., 5.5 for 5 hours and 30 minutes).
* Select "Yes" or "No" for the assigned tool option only if you had a tool assigned to you during the shift.
* Use this form to report any notable events or issues that occurred during your shift.
