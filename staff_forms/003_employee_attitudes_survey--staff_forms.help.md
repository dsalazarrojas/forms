This help guide explains the Employee Attitudes Survey form, a tool to gather feedback from employees about their experiences and perceptions in the workplace.

To ensure you receive the most accurate and comprehensive feedback, please fill out the form thoroughly and truthfully. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, explaining what each question is asking and how to provide the best possible response.

**Internal Reasoning**

The purpose of this form is to gauge employee attitudes and opinions on various aspects of their work environment. This information will help management identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to create a more positive work atmosphere. This form is not mandatory, and respondents' answers will not affect their job performance or salary. All answers are kept confidential and only used for internal analysis.

## Purpose
The Employee Attitudes Survey is a tool to gather information about your work experience and perceptions. Your feedback will help shape the company culture and improve work quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and provide thoughtful responses.
2. Answer all questions, even if you are unsure or uncomfortable with a topic.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
4. You can skip questions if you prefer, but this may impact the survey's overall analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey**:
	+ **Employee Survey (1)**: Share your thoughts and opinions about your work experience. Please type in your response, and it should be as detailed as possible.
* **Job Title Rating**:
	+ **Job Title Rating**: Evaluate your job title on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied". Think about how your job title aligns with your skills and responsibilities.
* **Average Hours Worked Per Week**:
	+ **Average Hours Worked Per Week**: Estimate the number of hours you work on a typical week, including work hours and any work-related activities outside of work hours.
* **Level of Motivation**:
	+ **Level of Motivation**: Choose one of the options: "High", "Medium", or "Low", based on how motivated you feel about your work and job responsibilities.
* **Overall Job Satisfaction**:
	+ **Overall Job Satisfaction**: Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied". Think about how your job makes you feel and whether it aligns with your expectations.
* **Department Satisfaction**:
	+ **Department Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your department on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied". Consider how well your department works together and communicates.
* **Comfort Level with Workload**:
	+ **Comfort Level with Workload**: Evaluate how comfortable you feel with your workload on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Comfortable" and 5 is "Very Comfortable". Think about whether you have enough resources and support to perform your job duties.
* **Manager Satisfaction**:
	+ **Manager Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your manager on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied". Consider how well your manager communicates and supports you.
* **Break Frequency**:
	+ **Break Frequency**: Estimate the number of breaks you take during the workday. This will help us understand your work pace and productivity.
* **Feeling of Being Overworked or Underworked**:
	+ **Feeling of Being Overworked or Underworked**: Choose one of the options: "Often", "Sometimes", "Rarely", or "Never", based on how frequently you feel overworked or underworked.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**:
	+ **Suggestions for Improvement**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have to improve the work environment, processes, or your job role.
* **Job Title Satisfaction**:
	+ **Job Title Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your job title on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied". Think about how well your job title aligns with your strengths and interests.
* **Employee Survey (2)**:
	+ **Employee Survey (2)**: This is a duplicate question, allowing you to reiterate any points you mentioned in the first question.
* **Manager Survey**:
	+ **Manager Survey**: Share your thoughts about your manager and their leadership style. Be specific about what you like and dislike about them.
