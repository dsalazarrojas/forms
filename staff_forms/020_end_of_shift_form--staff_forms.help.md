# End Of Shift Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Shift Form is used to record employee information at the end of their shift, including their name, shift start and end times, and any notes they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the **employee_name** field.
2. Record the start time of your shift in the **shift_start_time** field.
3. Record the end time of your shift in the **shift_end_time** field.
4. If applicable, enter your employee ID in the **employee_id** field.
5. Add any notes or comments you'd like to make in the **employee_notes** field.
6. Review your entries to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel document.
* **shift_start_time** (Time, Optional): Record the time your shift started. Format should be in a 24-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **shift_end_time** (Time, Optional): Record the time your shift ended. Format should be in a 24-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **employee_id** (Text, Optional): If you have an employee ID, enter it here.
* **employee_notes** (Text, Optional): Make any additional comments or notes about your shift. This field is not required, but can be useful for recording important information.

## Tips
* Please ensure your entries are accurate and complete to maintain consistency in our records.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
