# Intern Performance Scorecard - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of an intern based on their skills, abilities, and work quality. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure that the intern receives a fair and accurate assessment of their work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for each field.
2. Fill in the numerical ratings for each of the 5 performance categories.
3. Provide a brief comment for each category to explain your evaluation.
4. Choose whether or not the intern has the required skills or not.
5. Select the date when you completed the form and the date when you reviewed the intern's work.
6. Indicate whether or not you were the reviewer of the intern's work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`, `select_one`, required): Select the name of the intern you are evaluating.
* **rating1** (`rating_1`, `number`, required): Rate the intern's performance in one category (e.g., teamwork, communication).
* **Comment for rating1** (`comment_1`, `text`, required): Explain your reasoning behind the rating 1.
* **rating2** (`rating_2`, `number`, required): Rate the intern's performance in another category (e.g., time management, problem-solving).
* **Comment for rating2** (`comment_2`, `text`, required): Explain your reasoning behind the rating 2.
* **rating3** (`rating_3`, `number`, required): Rate the intern's performance in yet another category (e.g., adaptability, leadership).
* **Comment for rating3** (`comment_3`, `text`, required): Explain your reasoning behind the rating 3.
* **rating4** (`rating_4`, `number`, required): Rate the intern's performance in the fourth category (e.g., initiative, creativity).
* **Comment for rating4** (`comment_4`, `text`, required): Explain your reasoning behind the rating 4.
* **rating5** (`rating_5`, `number`, required): Rate the intern's performance in the fifth category (e.g., professionalism, reliability).
* **Comment for rating5** (`comment_5`, `text`, required): Explain your reasoning behind the rating 5.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you completed this form.
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you reviewed the intern's work.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether or not you were the reviewer of the intern's work.
