# Staff Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Information Form is used to collect and store staff member information for various HR-related purposes, such as updating staff records, onboarding new employees, or tracking employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields, marked as "true" in the form.
2. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
5. Select your employment status from the Employment Status dropdown menu.
6. For optional fields, provide any additional information relevant to your staff record, such as contact phone numbers, supervisory relationships, and job details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`1`, `text`, required: false)
Your First Name is a text field where you can enter your first name.

### Last Name (`2`, `text`, required: true)
This is a text field to enter your last name.

### Email (`4`, `email`, required: true)
Enter your email address in this field.

### Job Title (`3`, `text`, required: false)
Enter a brief description of your job title.

### Contact Phone (`5`, `text`, required: false)
If applicable, enter your primary contact phone number.

### Contact Phone Extension (`6`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the extension number for your contact phone.

### Job Status (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select one or more of the following:

* Employed
* Unemployed
* On Leave

### Employment Status (`8`, `select_one`, required: true)
Choose one of the following:

* Full-time
* Part-time
* On Contract

### Date of Birth (`9`, `date`, required: false)
Enter your date of birth, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Date of Hire (`10`, `date`, required: false)
Enter your date of hire, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Employment Type (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select one or more of the following:

* Full-time
* Part-time

### Supervisor Name (`12`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of your supervisor.

### Supervisor Phone (`13`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the phone number of your supervisor.

### Supervisor Email (`14`, `email`, required: false)
Enter the email address of your supervisor.

### Department (`15`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the department you belong to.

### Reporting Manager Name (`16`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of your reporting manager.

### Reporting Manager Phone (`17`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the phone number of your reporting manager.

### Reporting Manager Email (`18`, `email`, required: false)
Enter the email address of your reporting manager.

### Job Code (`19`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the job code.

### Job Family (`20`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the job family.

### Job Class (`21`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the job class.

### Job Grade (`22`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the job grade.

### Job Band (`23`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the job band.

### Job Level (`24`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the job level.

### Job Category (`25`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the job category.

Note that this form is comprehensive and may contain fields not directly relevant to the typical staff onboarding process. Review and remove any unnecessary fields as required.
