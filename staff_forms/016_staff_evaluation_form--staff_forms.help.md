# Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Evaluation Form is used to evaluate staff members' performance, focusing on supervisor feedback, evaluation status, and key competencies. This form helps ensure that evaluations are completed accurately and consistently, promoting a fair and reliable performance assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure the staff member you are evaluating is active and not in evaluation status "inactive".
2. Select the supervisor who evaluated the staff member.
3. Enter the date of the evaluation.
4. Choose the time period for the evaluation.
5. Enter the name of the staff member being evaluated.
6. Indicate whether the staff member has demonstrated specific competencies (competency 1).
7. Add any comments about the evaluation.
8. Record the staff evaluation form's submitted status (evaluation form submitted) and completion status (evaluation form completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **staff_evaluation_form** (Page 1): Enter a brief description of the evaluation. This field is not required.
* **supervisor** (Page 2): Select the supervisor who conducted the evaluation. This field is not required.
* **date** (Page 3): Enter the date of the evaluation. This field is not required.
* **time** (Page 4): Choose the time period of the evaluation. This field is not required.
* **staff_member_name** (Page 5): Enter the name of the staff member being evaluated. This field is not required.
* **competency_1** (Page 6): Check if the staff member has demonstrated the specific competency (1). This field is not required.
* **comments** (Page 7): Add any comments about the evaluation. This field is not required.
* **evaluation_form_submitted** (Page 8): Record the status of the evaluation form submission. This field is not required.
* **evaluation_form_completed** (Page 9): Mark the completion status of the evaluation form. This field is not required.
* **evaluation_form_status** (Page 10): Select the status of the evaluation form (Active or Inactive). This field is not required.
