# Fingerprint Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect fingerprints for various purposes. To ensure accurate data collection, please fill out the form completely and carefully. Before proceeding, review each field to understand its purpose and ensure you are entering the correct information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form title with a brief description of your fingerprint scan or usage.
2. Under "Fingerprint Consent", select the option that best describes the purpose of your fingerprint scan.
3. If necessary, provide consent for the fingerprint scanner by selecting the relevant options from "Fingerprint Scanner Consent".
4. Enter the date and time you intend to perform the fingerprint scan.
5. Include any additional notes or comments about your fingerprint scan.
6. Enter your email address to be contacted for any further information or updates.
7. Finally, enter your phone number for future contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: (text, required, optional) Enter a brief description of your fingerprint scan or usage.
* **Fingerprint Consent**: (select_one, required, optional) Select the option that best describes the purpose of your fingerprint scan.
* **Fingerprint Scanner Consent**: (select_multiple, required, optional) Select the relevant options for the fingerprint scanner if necessary.
* **Date**: (date, required, optional) Enter the date you intend to perform the fingerprint scan.
* **Time**: (time, required, optional) Enter the time you intend to perform the fingerprint scan.
* **Note**: (note, required, optional) Include any additional notes or comments about your fingerprint scan.
* **Email**: (email, required, optional) Enter your email address for future contact.
* **Phone**: (text, required, optional) Enter your phone number for future contact.
