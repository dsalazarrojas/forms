# Daycare Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Incident Report Form is designed to document any incidents that occur at the daycare center. It helps staff members report accidents, injuries, or near misses that require immediate attention or follow-up action. This form ensures a thorough and accurate recording of the incident, which can aid in providing quality care to the children and staff members, as well as support the center's risk management and prevention efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the date of the incident from the calendar.
2.  Enter the name of the staff member who was involved in the incident.
3.  Indicate if the staff member was injured during the incident.
4.  Enter the date of birth of the staff member involved.
5.  Enter the time of the incident.
6.  Enter the location where the incident occurred.
7.  Describe the incident in detail, providing as much information as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident, using the calendar to select the correct date.
*   **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member who was involved in the incident.
*   **Was Staff Member Injured?** (`was_staff_member_injured`, select_one, required): Check 'True' if the staff member was injured during the incident.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the staff member involved.
*   **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Describe the incident in detail, including the circumstances and any relevant details.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure that the incident is properly documented.
- If the staff member was injured, please ensure that you report the incident to the supervisor or manager immediately.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask for assistance from your supervisor or manager.
