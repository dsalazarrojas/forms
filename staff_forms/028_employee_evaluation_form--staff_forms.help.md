# employee_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_evaluation_form" is used to record and evaluate employee performance. It helps managers and HR personnel assess an employee's skills, progress, and performance level, which aids in making informed decisions about employee growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the relevant information about the employee being evaluated.
2. Select the department that the employee belongs to from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose the project the employee has worked on from the list.
4. If applicable, list the skills that were tested during the evaluation.
5. Rate the employee's performance level using the provided scale.
6. Enter the score based on the performance evaluation.
7. Enter the date when the evaluation was completed.
8. Record the time taken to complete the evaluation.
9. Enter the name and other details of the person who completed the evaluation (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **Department**: Select the department that the employee belongs to. Options include Human Resources, Finance, IT, Marketing, Sales, Customer Service, Operations, Quality Assurance, and Engineering.
* **Project Name**: Choose the project the employee has worked on from the list.
* **Job Title**: Select the job title of the employee, such as Manager, Team Lead, Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Marketing Manager, Sales Representative, Customer Service Representative, Quality Assurance Engineer, Operations Manager.
* **Skills Tested**: If applicable, list the skills that were tested during the evaluation. This is an open text field.
* **Performance Level**: Rate the employee's performance level using the provided scale.
* **Score**: Enter the score based on the performance evaluation.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date when the evaluation was completed.
* **Time Completed**: Record the time taken to complete the evaluation.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name and other details of the person who completed the evaluation.
