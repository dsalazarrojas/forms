# Probationary Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Probationary Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback on new employees' performance and adjustment to their role, work environment, and relationships with colleagues and supervisors. This information will be used to inform future decisions regarding promotions, training, or support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide honest and constructive feedback about the employee's performance during their probationary period.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience or feedback from colleagues or supervisors.
3. If unsure about any question, use the 'Unknown' or 'Other' option.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement in the 'Comments' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Employees** (`new_employees`, note, not required): A space to note any new employees you have worked with during their probationary period.
- **Adjusting to Work** (`adjusting_to_work`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply regarding the employee's adjustment to their new role, such as "Yes, easily" or "Yes, with some difficulty".
- **New Role** (`new_role`, select_one, not required): Indicate if the employee is working in a new role during their probationary period.
- **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, note, not required): A space to note the employee's work environment, including any factors that may have affected their performance.
- **New Technologies** (`new_technologies`, text, not required): A field to note any new technologies or tools the employee has learned or is using during their probationary period.
- **Training Received** (`training_received`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply regarding the training or feedback the employee received during their probationary period, such as "On-the-job training" or "Mentorship".
- **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate the employee's job satisfaction, if applicable.
- **Communication with Colleagues** (`communication_with_colleagues`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply regarding the employee's communication with colleagues, such as "Good" or "Some improvement needed".
- **Communication with Supervisors** (`communication_with_supervisors`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply regarding the employee's communication with supervisors, such as "Good" or "Some improvement needed".
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate the employee's overall satisfaction with their role, using one of the provided options.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
