# Employee Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Onboarding Survey is designed to gather information about new employees' onboarding experience, feedback, and satisfaction. This survey is meant to be filled out during the onboarding process to help HR or employee engagement teams track and analyze employee sentiment and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal information (first name, last name, email, and date of birth).
2. Answer the feedback questions (feedback question 1, feedback question 2, and feedback question 3).
3. Enter your manager's details (manager name, email, and phone).
4. Select your answers to the survey questions (Employee Survey question 1 and Employee Survey question 2).
5. Rate your manager's rating and your feedback rating (Excellence, Good, Fair, or Poor).
6. Rate your employee and manager satisfaction (High, Medium, or Low).
7. Indicate your retention status (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Date joined** (`date_joined`, required): Enter the date you started working with the company.
* **Note** (`note`, optional): Any additional comments or notes about your onboarding experience.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Manager name** (`manager_name`, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager email** (`manager_email`, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager phone** (`manager_phone`, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager rating** (`manager_rating`, required): Rate your manager's performance (Excellence, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Feedback rating** (`feedback_rating`, required): Rate your feedback on the onboarding process (Excellence, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Employee Survey question 1** (`employee_survey_1`, required): Select one or more options that apply to your feedback on the onboarding process.
* **Employee Survey question 2** (`employee_survey_2`, required): Select one or more options that apply to your feedback on the onboarding process.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Manager Satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, required): Rate your manager's satisfaction with their performance (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Retention** (`retention`, required): Indicate whether you plan to stay with the company (True or False).
