# Daycare Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help daycare staff members conduct thorough cleaning checks on various areas of the facility, ensuring a safe and clean environment for children and staff. The form will guide staff members to inspect different zones of the daycare, including staff areas, bathrooms, kitchens, office areas, storage areas, play areas, playgrounds, outdoor areas, and trash areas. The staff member will report any issues or notes on each area, and provide a date and time for the inspection. This form will help maintain a clean and safe facility that protects the health and well-being of both children and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
- Open the Daycare Cleaning Checklist form in the desired location, typically on a tablet or laptop.
- Select an area from the "Staff Areas" field to start the inspection.
- For each selected area, choose the relevant options that apply, using "Select One" for areas with specific characteristics (e.g. staff areas with "Option 1" or "Option 2" characteristics).
- For areas with multiple options, use "Select Multiple" to choose all applicable options.
- Type any relevant notes or observations in the "Notes" field.
- Enter the date of the inspection in the "Date" field.
- Enter the time of the inspection in the "Time" field.
- If applicable, provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Staff Areas
* **Staff Areas** (`Staff Areas`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the staff areas to be inspected. This field helps you focus on the staff areas of the daycare.

### Bathrooms
* **Bathrooms** (`Bathrooms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all applicable options for the bathrooms area, such as cleanliness, safety, or other relevant characteristics.

### Kitchen Areas
* **Kitchen Areas** (`Kitchen Areas`, `text`, required: false): Provide any notes or observations about the kitchen areas, such as cleanliness, condition of appliances, or other relevant information.

### Office Areas
* **Office Areas** (`Office Areas`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notes or observations about the office areas, such as cleanliness, condition of equipment, or other relevant information.

### Storage Areas
* **Storage Areas** (`Storage Areas`, `text`, required: false): Describe the condition of the storage areas, including cleanliness and storage capacity.

### Play Areas
* **Play Areas** (`Play Areas`, `text`, required: false): Record any observations or notes about the play areas, such as cleanliness, safety, or other relevant information.

### Playgrounds
* **Playgrounds** (`Playgrounds`, `text`, required: false): Describe the condition of the playgrounds, including cleanliness and play equipment.

### Outdoor Areas
* **Outdoor Areas** (`Outdoor Areas`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notes or observations about the outdoor areas, such as cleanliness, safety, or other relevant information.

### Trash Areas
* **Trash Areas** (`Trash Areas`, `text`, required: false): Describe the condition of the trash areas, including cleanliness and waste management.

### Other Areas
* **Other Areas** (`Other Areas`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notes or observations about other areas not listed above.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): Write any additional comments or observations about the areas inspected.

### Date
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Time
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes, if desired.
