# Resignation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to formally notify the organization of an employee's intention to resign from their position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name in the "prophets name" field.
2. Select your job title from the list in the "job title" field.
3. Optionally, provide a reason for your resignation, if it is not already stated.
4. Choose a notice period for your resignation.
5. Select the last day you worked from the calendar.
6. Enter your employee ID number.
7. Leave any comments for your supervisor, if needed.
8. Select your department from the list.
9. Enter the date you plan to resign.
10. Choose the reason for your resignation from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name
* **Prophet's Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employment contract or ID.

### Job Title
* **Job Title**: Select your current job title from the list.

### Reason for Resignation (Optional)
* **Reason for Resignation**: If you don't wish to provide a reason, you can leave this field blank. This field is optional.

### Notice Period
* **Notice Period**: Select the number of weeks or months you will provide before your last day of work. This field is required.

### Last Day Worked
* **Last Day Worked**: Select the date you plan to leave the company. This field is required.

### Employee ID
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number as it appears on your contract or ID card. This field is required.

### Supervisor Comments (Optional)
* **Supervisor Comments**: If you wish to provide any additional comments or context for your supervisor, you can do so here. This field is optional.

### Department
* **Department**: Select your current department from the list. This field is required.

### Resignation Date
* **Resignation Date**: Enter the date you plan to formally resign from your position. This field is required.

### Reason for Resignation
* **Reason for Resignation**: Choose the reason for your resignation from the list provided. This field is required.
