# Workplace Safety And Concerns Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety And Concerns Form is designed to collect information from employees about workplace safety and concerns. This form will help the management team to monitor and address potential safety risks and issues in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields.
2. If a field is not required, feel free to provide any relevant information that you think may be helpful.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. contact
* **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering your name and contact information. Please provide your name and any other relevant contact information that you think will help the management team contact you.

### 2. date
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): This field is for selecting the date when the concern occurred or will occur. You can choose a date from the calendar or type it manually.

### 3. time
* **Time** (`time`, required: false): This field is for selecting the time when the concern occurred or will occur. You can choose a time from the dropdown list.

### 4. location
* **Location** (`text`, required: false): This field is for specifying the location where the concern occurred or will occur.

### 5. concern_type
* **Concern Type** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for selecting multiple concern types that apply to your situation. Please choose all the types that apply to your concern. Available options are:
    + 'Yes'
    + 'No'

### 6. concern
* **Concern** (`text`, required: false): This field is for detailing the specific concern or issue that you are reporting.

### 7. contact_info
* **Contact Info** (`text`, required: false): This field is for providing any additional contact information that you think will help the management team contact you.

### 8. email
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): This field is for providing your email address. Please use a valid email address that the management team can use to contact you.

### 9. message
* **Message** (`text`, required: false): This field is for providing any additional information that you think will help the management team address your concern.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling in each field to help the management team understand your concern.
* If you have any attachments or supporting evidence that may be relevant to your concern, please attach them to the email or provide them in person.
* If your concern requires urgent attention, please mark it as urgent when submitting the form.
* Remember to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
