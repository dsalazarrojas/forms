# thanksgiving_break_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Thanksgiving Break Check Out Form is designed to provide students with a convenient way to check out with the staff during holidays like Thanksgiving Break. This form helps to ensure a smooth and secure check out process for students who are leaving campus during the break.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student information section with your name and ID number.
2. Provide your parent's information, if applicable.
3. Enter your guardian's contact information, if applicable.
4. Fill in the check out information section with your current location and plans.
5. Select your check out status (On Campus, Off Campus, or Home).
6. Enter any staff notes or special instructions.
7. Choose your signature type (Signature or Other).
8. Select your staff signature type (Signature or Other).
9. Enter the date and time of your check out.
10. Select your student dorm and staff dorm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (Student Information): Enter your name and ID number.
* **Parent Information** (Parent Information): Enter your parent's name and contact information, if applicable.
* **Guardian Contact** (Guardian Contact): Enter your guardian's name and contact information, if applicable.
* **Check Out Information** (Check Out Information): Enter your current location and plans, such as "Off Campus" or "Home".
* **Check Out Status** (Check Out Status): Select one of the following options:
	+ On Campus: You are staying on campus.
	+ Off Campus: You are leaving campus and going off-campus.
	+ Home: You are going home.
* **Staff Notes** (Staff Notes): Enter any special instructions or notes for staff.
* **Student Signature** (Student Signature): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Signature: You have signed the form digitally.
	+ Other: You have a different way of signing (please specify).
* **Staff Signature** (Staff Signature): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Signature: You have signed the form digitally.
	+ Other: You have a different way of signing (please specify).
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date of your check out.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time of your check out.
* **Student Dorm** (Student Dorm): Select one of the following options:
	+ Main Dorm: You are staying in the Main Dorm.
	+ South Dorm: You are staying in the South Dorm.
	+ North Dorm: You are staying in the North Dorm.
* **Staff Dorm** (Staff Dorm): Select one of the following options:
	+ Main Dorm: The staff member is staying in the Main Dorm.
	+ South Dorm: The staff member is staying in the South Dorm.
	+ North Dorm: The staff member is staying in the North Dorm.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any special instructions or requests, please enter them in the Staff Notes section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the staff member assigned to your dormitory.
