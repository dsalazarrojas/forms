# Shift Handover Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Handover Checklist is a form used to record important information and details about the shift just completed. It helps to facilitate a smooth handover process between shifts, ensuring that essential information is passed on and critical tasks are acknowledged.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Shift Handover Checklist, follow these steps:

* Review the previous shift's details (pages 2 and 10) to understand the context of the current shift.
* Record the start and end times of your current shift (page 1).
* Document any handover details (page 3) and actions taken during the shift (page 4).
* Report any issues or concerns (page 6).
* Provide shift comments (page 7).
* Indicate whether this is your first shift (page 11).
* Enter any relevant report IDs (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, page 1): Record the start time of your current shift.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, page 2): Record the end time of your current shift.
* **Handover Details** (`handover_details`, page 3): Describe any important information or events that occurred during the shift, such as meetings, incidents, or critical tasks.
* **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`, page 4): Select all the actions that were completed during the shift. These actions may include tasks, projects, or activities.
* **Report** (`report`, page 5): Enter any additional information or comments related to your report.
* **Handover Issues** (`handover_issues`, page 6): Document any problems or issues that arose during the shift, such as equipment malfunctions or personnel concerns.
* **Shift Comments** (`shift_comments`, page 7): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the shift.
* **Next Shift** (`next_shift`, page 8): Enter the number of the next shift you will be working on.
* **End Time of Previous Shift** (`end_time_previous_shift`, page 9): Record the end time of the previous shift.
* **Start Time of Previous Shift** (`start_time_previous_shift`, page 10): Record the start time of the previous shift.
* **First Shift** (`first_shift`, page 11): Check this box if you are starting your first shift.
* **Report ID** (`report_id`, page 12): Enter any relevant report ID numbers related to your shift.
