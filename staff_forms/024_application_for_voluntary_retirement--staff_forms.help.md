# Application For Voluntary Retirement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by staff members to notify their supervisor and HR department of their intention to retire voluntarily.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name in the **Employee name** field.
2. Enter your internal staff number in the **Employee ID** field.
3. Select the date you intend to retire in the **Proposed retirement date** field.
4. Provide an optional explanation for your retirement in the **Reason for retirement** field.
5. Choose your preferred pension arrangement from the **Pension option selected** field.
6. Enter your contact email for future correspondence in the **Contact email** field.
7. Indicate the status of your supervisor's approval in the **Supervisor approval** field.
8. Add any relevant notes for the HR office in the **HR comments** field.
9. Specify any documents uploaded to support your retirement application in the **Documents uploaded** field.
10. Finally, type your full name to confirm submission in the **Final signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your internal staff number.
* **Proposed retirement date** (`retirement_date`, date, required): Select the date you intend to retire.
* **Reason for retirement** (`reason_for_retirement`, text, optional): Provide an optional explanation for your retirement.
* **Pension option selected** (`pension_option`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred pension arrangement.
	+ Immediate pension: Receive pension benefits immediately.
	+ Deferred pension: Receive pension benefits at a later date.
	+ Lump sum: Receive a one-time payment instead of regular pension benefits.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for future correspondence.
* **Supervisor approval** (`supervisor_approval`, select_one, required): Select the status of your supervisor's approval.
	+ Pending: Approval is pending.
	+ Approved: Supervisor has approved your retirement.
	+ Denied: Supervisor has denied your retirement request.
* **HR comments** (`hr_comments`, text, optional): Add any relevant notes for the HR office.
* **Documents uploaded** (`documents_uploaded`, text, optional): Specify any documents uploaded to support your retirement application.
* **Final signature** (`final_signature`, text, required): Type your full name to confirm submission.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the HR department for assistance.
* Once you have submitted this form, you will receive further instructions on the next steps for your retirement.
