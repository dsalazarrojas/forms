# Telework Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telework Request Form is a tool used by employees to request approval to work from a remote location. This form is used to communicate the employee's work arrangement preferences with their manager, ensuring that all necessary information is collected for the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with your employee information, including your name, job title, and request details.
2.  Enter the date you would like to start working from a remote location.
3.  Select your preferred work hours.
4.  Enter your manager's name for approval.
5.  Enter your department and job location.
6.  Select the approver's name and department.
7.  Enter your email address and phone number.
8.  Provide any additional comments or notes if necessary.
9.  Submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Your employee name is required to be entered for form submission.
*   **Telework Start Date** (`telework_start_date`, date, optional): The date you would like to start working from a remote location.
*   **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you would like to work from a remote location.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title for reference.
*   **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): The location where you plan to work from, outside of the office.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name for approval.
*   **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current approval status of your request.
*   **Reason for Denial** (`reason_for_denial`, text, optional): If your request is denied, provide the reason for denial.
*   **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the requestor of the form.
*   **Approvers** (`approver`, text, optional): Enter the names of those approving your request.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department for reference.
*   **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you will be working from.
*   **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, optional): Enter your manager's title for reference.
*   **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): The date your request was approved.
*   **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
*   **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Approval Notes** (`approval_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your request.
*   **Approval Comment** (`approval_comment`, text, optional): Enter any approval comments from your manager.
*   **Requestor Note** (`requestor_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes from you as the requestor.
*   **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes from your manager.
*   **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the approver.
*   **Approver Department** (`approver_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the approver.

Please note that this form is designed to be filled out in a straightforward manner, and all fields are optional unless specified otherwise.
