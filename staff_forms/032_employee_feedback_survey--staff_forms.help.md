# Employee Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees about their managers and work environment. It's intended to help managers understand their team's needs and concerns, and to improve the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read through the form carefully and ensure you're filling out the right information.
2. Answer each question honestly and concisely.
3. If you're unsure about any question, you can ask your manager for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This field is a brief overview of the form. Please read it carefully and make sure you understand the purpose of this form.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager. This will help them understand who is providing feedback about them.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as an employee. This will help the manager know who is providing feedback about them.
* **What feedback do you have for your manager?**: Please provide specific feedback about your manager. Try to be as detailed as possible.
* **Is there anything else you wanted to share with your manager?**: If there's anything else you'd like to tell your manager, please add it here.
* **How supportive is your manager?**: Answer the question with one of the following options:
	+ Very Supportive
	+ Moderately Supportive
	+ Not Supportive
* **What is the communication style of your manager?**: Answer the question by selecting one or more of the following options:
	+ Regular
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **What is the work environment like at your workplace?**: Choose one of the following options to describe the work environment:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
* **Submit form**: Please submit this form once you've completed all the questions. This will send your feedback to your manager.

**Tips**

* Please be honest and objective when filling out this form.
* Try to provide specific examples or incidents that describe your experiences.
* If you're unsure about anything, don't hesitate to ask your manager for clarification.

Note: The fields with a star (*) are required fields. Please fill out all required fields to ensure that your feedback is submitted correctly.
