# Remote Or Hybrid Working Arrangement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the current or proposed remote or hybrid working arrangement of a staff member. This includes details about the job, work arrangement, work location, and other relevant job information. The information collected will be used to assess and adjust working arrangements as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your job title and a brief description of your job, including any relevant details about your role and responsibilities.
2. Specify your current or proposed work arrangement (e.g., full-time, part-time, or remote).
3. Provide the location where you work or plan to work (e.g., office, home, or a specific location).
4. Describe your work pattern, including your work schedule and any specific work hours.
5. Enter the date you started your job and the date you plan to end your job (if applicable).
6. Specify your current job status (e.g., active or inactive).
7. Select the type of job you have (e.g., full-time, part-time, or remote).
8. Provide the name of your reporting line or supervisor.
9. List the names of your team members.
10. Enter a detailed description of your job, including your role and responsibilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Details** (`Job Details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your job, including your job title and a clear understanding of your role and responsibilities.
* **Work Arrangement** (`Work Arrangement`, text, required): Specify your current or proposed work arrangement (e.g., full-time, part-time, or remote).
* **Work Location** (`Work Location`, text, required): Provide the location where you work or plan to work (e.g., office, home, or a specific location).
* **Work Pattern** (`Work Pattern`, text, required): Describe your work pattern, including your work schedule and any specific work hours.
* **Job Start Date** (`Job Start Date`, date, required): Enter the date you started your job.
* **Job End Date** (`Job End Date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to end your job (if applicable).
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, select_one, required): Specify your current job status (e.g., active or inactive).
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of job you have (e.g., full-time, part-time, or remote).
* **Reporting Line** (`Reporting Line`, text, required): Provide the name of your reporting line or supervisor.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team lead or supervisor.
* **Team Members** (`Team Members`, text, required): List the names of your team members.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, note, required): Enter a detailed description of your job, including your role and responsibilities.
