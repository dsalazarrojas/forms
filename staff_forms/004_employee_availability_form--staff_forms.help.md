# Employee Availability Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Availability Form is a crucial tool for employees to report their availability status, ensuring that they are aware of their work schedule and can coordinate tasks with other team members and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand its purpose and the information you need to provide.
2. Answer the questions on each page, providing accurate and up-to-date information about your availability.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to your manager for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Availability Status** (`employee_availability`, `text`, optional): This field is used to report your overall availability status. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are available to work during your scheduled shifts.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the days and shifts you are available to work. Select multiple options if you are available for more than one shift.
* **Availability Date** (`availability_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for which you are reporting your availability.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of your availability period.
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you are available to work.
* **Employee Note** (`employee_note`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your availability.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, `text`, optional): Add any comments or notes for your manager to review.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether your manager has approved your availability.
* **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your manager's approval.
* **Manager Approval Time** (`manager_approval_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your manager's approval.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, `text`, optional): Add any comments or notes from your manager.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your availability information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to your manager for approval.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your availability, contact your manager or HR representative.
