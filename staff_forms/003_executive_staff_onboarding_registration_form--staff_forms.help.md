# Executive Staff Onboarding Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Staff Onboarding Registration Form is designed to gather essential information about new executive staff members, including their contact details, job role, and supervisor's contact information. This information will be used for official records and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the New Employee Name field with the name of the new employee.
2. Enter the Job Title of the new employee in the Job Title field.
3. Provide a brief description of the new employee's job role in the Job Description field.
4. Enter the name of the reporting manager in the Reporting Manager field.
5. Select the department that the new employee will be working in the Department field.
6. Enter the new employee's email address in the Email field.
7. Enter the new employee's phone number in the Phone field.
8. Enter the new employee's Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
9. Enter the date the new employee was hired in the Hire Date field.
10. Enter the date the new employee started their job in the Job Start Date field.
11. Enter the name of the new employee's manager in the Manager Name field.
12. Enter the email address of the new employee's manager in the Manager Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Employee Name**: A text field to enter the name of the new employee.
* **Job Title**: A text field to enter the job title of the new employee.
* **Job Description**: A text field to enter a brief description of the new employee's job role.
* **Reporting Manager**: A text field to enter the name of the new employee's reporting manager.
* **Department**: A text field to select the department that the new employee will be working in.
* **Email**: An email field to enter the new employee's email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter the new employee's phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: A date field to enter the new employee's Date of Birth.
* **Hire Date**: A date field to enter the date the new employee was hired.
* **Job Start Date**: A date field to enter the date the new employee started their job.
* **Manager Name**: A text field to enter the name of the new employee's manager.
* **Manager Email**: An email field to enter the email address of the new employee's manager.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you only submit new employee information once, to avoid creating duplicate records.
* Double-check the information entered in each field for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant department or manager for assistance.
