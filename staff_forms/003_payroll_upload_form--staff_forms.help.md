# Payroll Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Payroll Upload Form is used to upload payroll data for a given pay period. This form is required for staff to upload their payroll information in a secure and standardized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the pay date for the pay period you are uploading.
2. Enter the name and address of the employer.
3. Enter the pay rate and pay amount for the given pay period.
4. Select the type of pay being uploaded (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
5. Upload a file containing the relevant payroll information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pay Date** (`pay_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date for the pay period you are uploading.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company or organization paying the staff member.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the company or organization paying the staff member.
* **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, number, required: false): Enter the pay rate for the given pay period.
* **Pay Amount** (`pay_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total pay amount for the given pay period.
* **Type of Pay** (`upload_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of pay being uploaded (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, required: false): Upload a file containing the relevant payroll information.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct pay date for the given pay period.
* Verify the accuracy of the employer's name and address.
* Enter the correct pay rate and pay amount for the given pay period.
* Select the correct type of pay being uploaded.
* Ensure the uploaded file is in the correct format (e.g. CSV, Excel).
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
