# Correction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting corrections on products from staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name where the issue occurred.
2. Describe the product issue in as much detail as possible.
3. Provide the date when the issue happened.
4. Identify the staff member who reported the issue.
5. Enter a detailed description of the issue.
6. If required, attach any relevant supporting documents or files.
7. Enter the staff member's contact email address.
8. (Optional) Enter the staff member's phone number.
9. (Optional) Enter the company address where the issue occurred.
10. Choose the expected resolution for the issue.
11. If required, enter any actual resolutions that were applied to the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter the name of the company where the issue occurred.
* **Product Issue** (`product_issue`, required): Describe the product issue in as much detail as possible. Try to be specific about the symptoms, steps to reproduce, and any error messages or logs.
* **Date Issue Happened** (`date_issue_happened`, required): Enter the date when the issue happened in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, required): Enter the name of the staff member who reported the issue.
* **Description** (`description`, required): Enter a detailed description of the issue, including any steps taken to attempt to resolve it.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, optional): If required, attach any supporting documents or files related to the issue. This could include screenshots, error logs, or other relevant evidence.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter the staff member's email address so that corrections can be sent to them.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter the staff member's phone number. This is an optional field and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, optional): Enter the address of the company where the issue occurred. This is an optional field and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Expected Resolution** (`expected_resolution`, optional): Choose the expected resolution from the options provided.
* **Actual Resolution** (`actual_resolution`, optional): If required, enter any actual resolutions that were applied to the issue, separated by commas.
