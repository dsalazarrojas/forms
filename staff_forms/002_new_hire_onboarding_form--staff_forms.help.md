# New Hire Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to provide a structured approach to collecting information from new hires. It helps the HR or personnel department gather all the necessary details for a smooth onboarding process, facilitating a faster integration into the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Employee Info** page, providing your name, job title, and department.
2. Enter your **Contact Info**, including your phone number and email address.
3. Complete the **Employment Details** section, providing information about your job role, start date, and any relevant work experience.
4. Specify your **Benefits Info**, including any benefits you are eligible for (e.g., health insurance, 401k).
5. Provide **Emergency Contact Info**, including phone numbers and email addresses of your emergency contacts.
6. Add any **Additional Info** you think would be helpful for your new employer.
7. Select the **Certification Info** that applies to you from the dropdown menu (e.g., First Aid, CPR, Safety Specialist, Security Specialist).
8. Choose the **Training and Orientation** option that best fits your onboarding needs (e.g., New Employee Orientation, New Employee Training).
9. Select the **Background Check** option that applies to you (e.g., FBI/FCRA Background Check, FCRA Background Check, No Background Check).
10. Choose the **E-Sign** option that applies to you (e.g., E-Sign, E-signing, No E-Sign).
11. Select the **Signature** option that applies to you (e.g., Signature, Signed, Signed and Dated).
12. Review your information and **Submit** the form.
