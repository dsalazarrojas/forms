# Former Employee Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Former Employee Information Form is a tool used to collect information from former employees to maintain updated contact details and employment history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your former employment status from the options provided.
2. If applicable, provide a brief reason for leaving in the text field.
3. Enter your employment date in the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4. Select your former manager's name from the options.
5. Choose the department you were a part of during your employment.
6. Select your job title from the options.
7. If you have any additional comments or details, please provide them in the text field.
8. Finally, choose your preferred contact method from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Former Employment Status** (`Former employment status`, select_multiple, required): Please select the reason that best describes your current employment status. This will help us understand your current employment situation.
* **Reason For Leaving** (`Reason For Leaving`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide a more detailed reason for leaving, please write it here. This will help us better understand your experience.
* **Employment Date** (`Employment Date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you started working at our organization in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, select_one, optional): Select the name of your former manager from the options provided. This will help us ensure we update your contact details correctly.
* **Department** (`Department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department you belonged to during your employment. This will help us tailor our communication accordingly.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, select_one, optional): Select the job title you held during your employment. This will help us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or details you'd like to share, please provide them here.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, text, mail, or in person).

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can ask your HR representative for guidance.
* The form can be completed on desktop, tablet, or mobile devices. Ensure you save or submit the form on completion.
