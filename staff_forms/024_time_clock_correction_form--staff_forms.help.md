# Time Clock Correction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to correct errors in employee time clock entries. To ensure accurate time tracking, users must submit this form to adjust the start and end times of their work hours.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" if the error occurred in your work hours.
2. Enter your Employee ID to identify yourself in the system.
3. Enter the date of the error in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the start time of your work hours as they should have been recorded.
5. Enter the end time of your work hours as they should have been recorded.
6. Enter the total duration of your work hours as they should have been recorded.
7. Select the correct day of the week your error occurred on.
8. Enter the corrected date of your work hours as they should have been recorded.
9. Enter any additional comments or explanations for your correction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start of work hours correction form** (`time_clock_correction_form_start`, select_one): Choose "Yes" to indicate that you are correcting an error in your work hours.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text): Enter your unique ID assigned to you by the system.
* **Date of error** (`date_of_error`, date): Enter the date of the error in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Start time** (`start_time`, time): Enter the start time of your work hours as they should have been recorded.
* **End time** (`end_time`, time): Enter the end time of your work hours as they should have been recorded.
* **Corrected duration** (`corrected_duration`, number): Enter the total duration of your work hours as they should have been recorded.
* **Correct to** (`correct_to`, select_one): Select the correct day of the week your error occurred on.
* **Corrected date** (`correct_date`, date): Enter the corrected date of your work hours as they should have been recorded.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): Enter any additional comments or explanations for your correction.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any additional issues.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor for clarification.
* Before submitting the form, double-check that you haven't already submitted a correction for the same error.
