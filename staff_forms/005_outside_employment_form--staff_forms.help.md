> # Outside Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for reporting outside employment or work that may affect an employee's work performance, commitment, or other work-related matters. It should only be completed by employees if you have outside work arrangements that may impact your job. 

If you are unsure about whether you need to complete this form, please consult with your manager or HR department before proceeding. 

This form is meant to be a one-time submission for each new outside work engagement. If you have multiple outside work engagements, complete this form once for each one.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the sections below with the required information. The form has eight sections. Each section is described below.
2. Please fill out each section completely and accurately.
3. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send it to your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant information to help your manager identify you in their response.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, required: false): Provide your name and any other personal details that may be relevant to your outside employment.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, required: false): Describe your outside employment in as much detail as possible.
* **Outside Employment** (`outside_employment`, text, required: false): Describe your outside work arrangement, including dates of employment and hours worked.
* **Employment Terms** (`employment_terms`, text, required: false): List any specific rules or guidelines from your outside employment that may impact your work performance or commitments.
* **Employee Agreement** (`employee_agreement`, text, required: false): If required, confirm you have read and understood the employment terms and conditions.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, select_one, required: false): Confirm with your manager whether you have permission to engage in this outside employment. Choose 'Yes' if your manager has confirmed, 'No' otherwise.
* **Outside Work Details** (`outside_work_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information your manager may need to know about your outside employment.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, required: false): Your manager can leave any additional comments or notes about your outside employment here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have discussed and obtained permission from your manager before engaging in any outside employment.
* If you are unsure about the impact of your outside employment on your work, please consult with your manager or HR department.
* This form should be completed once for each outside employment engagement. If you have multiple outside work engagements, submit each one separately.
