# Employee Benefits Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request benefits from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
* Select the type of benefit you are requesting from the "Employee Benefit" field.
* Provide your employee details, including name, employee ID, and phone number.
* Choose the locations where you would like to receive the benefit.
* If you have already submitted a request, select the "Re-Submit Request" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or in company records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, Optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Benefit** (`employee_benefit`, `select_one`, Optional): Select one of the following benefits:
	+ 401(k)
	+ Health Insurance
	+ Life Insurance
	+ Flexible PTO
* **Benefit Details** (`benefit_details`, `text`, Optional): Describe your benefit request in detail.
* **Work Location** (`location`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the locations where you would like to receive the benefit:
	+ New York
	+ Los Angeles
	+ San Francisco
	+ Chicago
* **Date of Submission** (`submit_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date you submitted your request.
* **Time of Submission** (`submit_time`, `time`, Optional): Enter the time you submitted your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the tool you would like to be assigned:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ Other
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, `text`, Optional): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `email`, Optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, `text`, Optional): Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Note** (`confirm_note`, `note`, Optional): Confirm additional information about your request.
* **Confirmation Status** (`confirmation_status`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the status of your confirmation:
	+ pending
	+ confirmed
	+ cancelled
* **Date of Confirmation** (`confirmation_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date of your confirmation.
* **Time of Confirmation** (`confirmation_time`, `time`, Optional): Enter the time of your confirmation.
* **Note of Confirmation** (`confirmation_note`, `note`, Optional): Enter the note about your confirmation.
* **Re-Submit Request** (`confirm_submit_request`, `text`, Optional): If you have already submitted a request, select this option to re-submit your request.
* **Re-Submit Request Confirmation** (`confirmation_submit_request`, `text`, Optional): Enter the reason for re-submitting your request.
* **Re-Submit Request Status** (`confirm_submit_request_status`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the status of your re-submit request:
	+ pending
	+ confirmed
	+ cancelled
* **Confirm Confirmation Status** (`confirm_confirmation_status`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the status of your confirmation:
	+ pending
	+ confirmed
	+ cancelled
* **Confirm Date of Confirmation** (`confirm_date_of_confirmation`, `date`, Optional): Confirm the date of your confirmation.
* **Confirm Note of Confirmation** (`confirm_note_of_confirmation`, `note`, Optional): Confirm any additional information about your confirmation.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting your request.
* If you have any issues with your request, contact the HR department for assistance.
* Be patient and wait for the HR department to process your request.
* If you have already submitted a request and need to re-submit it, select the "Re-Submit Request" option.
