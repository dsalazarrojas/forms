# employee_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and experience data from employees to help management understand their perspective and improve working conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name where indicated.
2. Type your current position in the space provided.
3. Select the time period you've been with the company from the options.
4. Choose your current satisfaction level with your role.
5. Type your department or team name in the space provided.
6. Enter your manager's name, if applicable.
7. Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (`company_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your current company.
* **Position (`position`, text, optional): Type the name of your current position or role within the company.
* **Experience (`experience`, time, optional): This field indicates the period of time you've been with the company. Choose the duration that best matches your tenure.
* **Satisfaction Level (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, optional): Select the level that best describes your current level of satisfaction with your role or position.
* **Department (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of your department or team.
* **Manager Name (`manager_name`, text, optional): If you have a manager, please enter their name.
* **Feedback (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any comments or suggestions you have about your experience working with the company.
