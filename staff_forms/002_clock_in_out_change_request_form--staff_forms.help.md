# Clock In Out Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clock In Out Change Request Form is used to correct discrepancies between the actual clock in and clock out times recorded in the system and the actual times worked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name, employee ID number, and department.
2. Enter the date of the clock in/out correction.
3. Enter the correct clock in and clock out times.
4. Select the reason for the clock in/out correction.
5. Provide a detailed explanation for the request.
6. If you have supporting evidence, select "Yes" to indicate that you have it.
7. Confirm that you understand company policy and authorize the request.
8. Enter your contact information for status updates on this request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Your full name as shown on payroll records.
* **Employee ID Number**: Your employee ID number as recorded in the system.
* **Department**: The department or area of work you belong to.
* **Direct Manager Name**: The name of your direct manager.
* **Date of the Clock In/Out to be Corrected**: The date when the clock in/out correction is being requested.
* **Current Clock In Time**: The time shown in the system for clock in.
* **Requested Correct Clock In Time**: The correct time you actually clocked in.
* **Current Clock Out Time**: The time shown in the system for clock out.
* **Requested Correct Clock Out Time**: The correct time you actually clocked out.
* **Total Time Discrepancy - Minutes**: The difference between the clock in and clock out times.
* **Reason for Clock In/Out Correction**: Select the reason for the clock in/out correction.
	+ System error
	+ Forgot to clock in/out
	+ Technical issue
	+ Manual entry error
	+ Other
* **Detailed Explanation**: Provide context for the time correction request.
* **Do you have supporting evidence - receipt, text, etc.?**: Select "Yes" if you have evidence to support your request.
* **I authorize this time correction**: Confirm that you understand company policy and authorize the request.
* **Date of Request**: The date you are submitting this form.
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address for status updates on this request.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for urgent communication (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Please keep in mind that supporting evidence may be requested by management if needed.
