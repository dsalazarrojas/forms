# Online Staff Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Staff Registration Form is designed to collect information from staff members for various internal purposes such as training, events, or meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and title in the **Registration Form** page.
2. Enter your contact information, including your contact number and email, on the **Contact Information** page.
3. Add your address on the **Contact Information** page.
4. Attach your resume, if applicable, on the **Resume** page.
5. Select assigned tools on the **Assigned Tool** page.
6. Choose your available date and time on the **Date and Time** page.
7. Add any additional notes on the **Note** page.
8. Enter your phone number on the **Phone** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`title`, text, required: false): Fill in your name and title to begin the registration process.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name and contact number, to ensure we can contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required: false): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your work or home address.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: false): Attach your resume, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool 1** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select one assigned tool.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple assigned tools.
* **Assigned Tool 3** (`assigned_tool_3`, select_one, required: false): Select one assigned tool.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Choose your available date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose your available time.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes you'd like to add.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
