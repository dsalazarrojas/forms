# emergency-contact-sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Contact Sheet is a form used to collect information from students, parents, or staff for emergency contact purposes. It is designed to gather the necessary details to quickly contact emergency contacts in case of an illness, injury, or other emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the required fields for your situation and make sure not to duplicate any existing information.
2. Fill out the required fields with the correct information, including:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
3. If applicable, select the relationship of the emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Teacher, Administrator, Faculty, Staff).
4. Enter the emergency contact's first name and last name.
5. Enter the emergency contact's phone number, without any punctuation and exactly 10 digits.
6. If applicable, select the relationship of the emergency contact.
7. Enter the emergency contact's email address.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (`first_name`, Text, Required)
The First Name field is used to collect the first name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.

* Please enter the first name of the emergency contact person.

### Last Name (`last_name`, Text, Required)
The Last Name field is used to collect the last name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.

* Please enter the last name of the emergency contact person.

### Relationship (`relationship`, Select Multiple, Optional)
This field is used to specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.

* Select the relationship that best describes your relationship with the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact First Name (`emergency_contact_first_name`, Text, Optional)
The Emergency Contact First Name field is used to collect the first name of the emergency contact person.

* Please enter the first name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Last Name (`emergency_contact_last_name`, Text, Optional)
The Emergency Contact Last Name field is used to collect the last name of the emergency contact person.

* Please enter the last name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, Optional)
The Emergency Contact Phone field is used to collect the phone number of the emergency contact person.

* Please enter a 10-digit phone number without any punctuation for the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Relationship (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Select One, Optional)
This field is used to specify the relationship between the emergency contact person and the person being contacted.

* Select the relationship that best describes the emergency contact person's relationship with the person being contacted.

### Emergency Contact Email (`emergency_contact_email`, Email, Optional)
The Emergency Contact Email field is used to collect the email address of the emergency contact person.

* Please enter a valid email address for the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Please review and submit the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for the emergency contact person.
* If you need to make changes to the form, please review and update the form accordingly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the appropriate authority for assistance.
