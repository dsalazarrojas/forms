# Employee Absenteeism Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track employee absenteeism, allowing you to record and report reasons for absences, duration, and other relevant details. Use this form to report your absence from work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as required, and select the relevant options from the dropdown menu when applicable. Make sure to fill in all required fields. You can navigate between the pages of the form using the 'Next' and 'Previous' buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reason for Absence (Page 4)**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select the reason for your absence. Choose from the following options:
  * Medical Reasons
  * Personal Reasons
  * Other
* **Number of Absences**: Enter the number of times you have been absent from work. This field is for numerical values only (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Duration of Absence**: Enter the duration of your absence in time format (e.g., 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, etc.).
* **Start Date (Page 7)**: Select the date when your absence started.
* **End Date (Page 8)**: Select the date when your absence ended.
* **Employee Absenteeism Form Page 1-3**: These fields are free-text areas where you can enter any additional information about your absence. Please use them to provide any relevant details about your absence.
