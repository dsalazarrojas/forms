# Employee Refusal Of Medical Treatment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to capture information from employees who refuse medical treatment in a hospital setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

### Step 1: Employee Information

*   Enter the employee's name in the **employee_name** field.
*   Enter the date of refusal in the **date_of_refusal** field.
*   Select the type of treatment refused (Medical or Non-Medical) from the **treatment_refused** field.
*   Enter the reason for refusal in the **reason_for_refusal** field.
*   Enter any relevant medical conditions in the **medical_condition** field.
*   Sign and date the form with the doctor's signature in the **doctor_signature** field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee who is refusing medical treatment.
*   **date_of_refusal**: Enter the date when the employee refused medical treatment.
*   **treatment_refused**: Select the type of treatment refused (Medical or Non-Medical).
*   **reason_for_refusal**: Enter the reason why the employee refused medical treatment.
*   **medical_condition**: Enter any relevant medical conditions of the employee.
*   **doctor_signature**: Sign and date the form with the doctor's signature.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all necessary fields are completed accurately to ensure effective communication with other hospital staff.
*   Double-check that the form is filled out correctly to prevent errors or confusion.
*   Use this form as a template for future refusals of medical treatment to ensure consistency across the hospital.
*   Consult with hospital policy to ensure that the form is in line with hospital procedures.
