# Employee Supervision Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Supervision Form is a tool used to collect important details from employees for the purpose of supervision and performance evaluation. This form helps supervisors and HR personnel to get a clear understanding of an employee's job title, performance, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
2. Select your Job Title from the options provided.
3. Enter your Email and Phone numbers accurately.
4. Fill in the Supervision Date and Supervision Time if relevant.
5. Provide a note in the Performance Evaluation Form section with any necessary comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`employee_supervision_form_1`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`employee_supervision_form_2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`employee_supervision_form_3`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Job Title** (`employee_supervision_form_4`, select one, required): Select your job title from the available options (Manager, Assistant Manager, Team Lead, or Employee).
* **Email** (`employee_supervision_form_5`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`employee_supervision_form_6`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Supervision Date** (`employee_supervision_form_7`, date, required): Enter the date of your supervision meeting or evaluation.
* **Supervision Time** (`employee_supervision_form_8`, time, required): Enter the time of your supervision meeting or evaluation.
* **Performance Evaluation Form** (`employee_supervision_form_9`, note, required): Provide any comments or feedback about your performance or any areas of improvement.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling in your details.
* Ensure all fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
