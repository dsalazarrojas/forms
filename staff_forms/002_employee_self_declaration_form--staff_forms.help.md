# Employee Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Self Declaration Form is a document designed to gather essential personal and professional information from employees. It is intended for internal use within your organization to collect data that will help in the management and development of your team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. Choose your department from the available options in the "Department" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your unique employee identification number or code assigned to you by your HR or management.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Select your department from the available options, this will help in identifying your workplace and role within the organization.
