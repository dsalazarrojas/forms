# Employee Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a personal evaluation tool for employees to assess their own performance, set goals, and identify areas for improvement. It is meant to be a self-reflection exercise, providing an opportunity for personal growth and development within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into five pages, each addressing a different area of your performance and development. Please review each page carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2.  Start by reviewing the "Performance" page (Page 1). This page provides an opportunity for you to reflect on your accomplishments in your role and assess your progress.
3.  Moving on to the "Goals" page (Page 2), select one or more options that align with your career goals and development areas.
4.  Next is the "Performance Improvement" page (Page 3), where you can choose an option to identify areas where you'd like to improve.
5.  On the "Development Areas" page (Page 4), select one or more options for areas where you'd like to develop new skills or knowledge.
6.  Finally, on the "Recommendations" page (Page 5), include any recommendations for your own growth and development.
