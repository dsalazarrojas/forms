# Overtime Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Overtime Waiver Form is used to request overtime compensation for an employee. This form should be completed by the employee to provide details about their overtime work period, reasons for the waiver, and required approvals. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure the overtime waiver process is properly handled.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Overtime Waiver Form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have completed your regular work hours accurately in your timesheet or time tracking system.
2. Fill out the required information for your overtime work period, including the "Employee Id", "Date Of Waiver", "Period Of Waiver", "Start Date", and "End Date".
3. Select a valid "Waiver Type" from the provided options. This is typically determined by company policy or your supervisor.
4. Optionally, provide a "Reason" for the waiver, which will help your manager or approver understand your overtime work.
5. Sign the "Employee Signature" field to confirm you are requesting this overtime.
6. Have your approver sign and provide their name and title in the "Approver Signature", "Approver Name", and "Approver Title" fields to approve your overtime waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id (Employee Id, number, required):** Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Date Of Waiver (Date Of Waiver, date, required):** Enter the date you are requesting the overtime waiver for.
* **Period Of Waiver (Period Of Waiver, number, required):** Enter the number of hours or period of overtime you are requesting to be waived.
* **Start Date (Start Date, date, required):** Enter the start date of your overtime work period.
* **End Date (End Date, date, required):** Enter the end date of your overtime work period.
* **Waiver Type (Waiver Type, select_one, required):** Choose the type of overtime waiver you are requesting based on company policy or your supervisor's guidance.
* **Reason (Reason, text, optional):** Optionally, enter a brief explanation for your overtime waiver (e.g., "Family emergency").
* **Employee Signature (Employee Signature, text, required):** Sign your name to confirm you are requesting this overtime waiver.
* **Approver Signature (Approver Signature, text, required):** Your approver will sign their name to approve your overtime waiver.
* **Approver Name (Approver Name, text, optional):** Enter your approver's name to confirm their approval.
* **Approver Title (Approver Title, text, optional):** Enter your approver's title to confirm their authority to approve your overtime waiver.
