# Military Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Military Leave Form is a form designed to help active-duty military personnel request and document leaves of absence for military duties. This form will help the user provide necessary details for their leave, including dates and reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the first text field.
2. Provide your start date in the second field.
3. Enter your end date in the third field.
4. Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Military Leave, Emergency Leave, or Other Leave) from the fourth field.
5. Optionally, select additional types of leave you are requesting in the fifth field.
6. Enter your return date in the seventh field.
7. Provide a reason for your leave in the ninth field.
8. Confirm the type of leave you are requesting in the twelfth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user-info** (Text): Enter your name and any other identifying information as required.
* **start-date** (Date): Enter the date you wish to begin your leave.
* **end-date** (Date): Enter the date you wish to end your leave.
* **leave-type** (Select One): Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Military Leave, Emergency Leave, or Other Leave).
* **leave-type-2** (Select Multiple): If necessary, select additional types of leave you are requesting.
* **start_date** (Date): Confirm the start date of your leave (if necessary, please ensure this matches the start date entered in field **start-date**).
* **end-date-2** (Date): Confirm the end date of your leave (if necessary, please ensure this matches the end date entered in field **end-date**).
* **reason** (Text): Provide a reason for your leave.
* **return-date** (Date): Enter the date you plan to return from your leave.
* **reason-2** (Text): Confirm or provide additional information for your reason (if necessary).
* **military-leave-confirmation** (Select One): Confirm whether your leave is related to military duties.
* **military-leave-confirmation-2** (Select Multiple): Confirm whether your leave is related to military duties (if necessary).
* **military-leave-confirmation-3** (Select Multiple): Confirm whether your leave is related to military duties (if necessary).
* **military-leave-confirmation** (Select Multiple): Confirm whether your leave is related to military duties (if necessary).
