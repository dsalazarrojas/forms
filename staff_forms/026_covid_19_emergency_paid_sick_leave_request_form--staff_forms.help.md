# COVID 19 Emergency Paid Sick Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Emergency Paid Sick Leave Request Form is designed to collect information from employees who are requesting emergency paid sick leave due to COVID-19-related reasons. This form ensures that employees can request time off in a timely and efficient manner, while maintaining transparency and documentation in the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields marked with *.
2. Provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department or team name.
* **Position or Job Title** (`position`): Enter your job title or position.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Phone Number** (`employee_phone`): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email Address** (`employee_email`): Enter your work email address.
* **Date of Request** (`request_date`): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`): Enter the date you would like to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`): Enter the date you would like to end your leave.
* **Total Hours Requested** (`total_hours`): Enter the total hours you would like to take for your leave.
* **Type of Leave** (`leave_type`): Select the reason for your leave from the options provided:
	+ COVID-19 related illness
	+ COVID-19 exposure quarantine
	+ Care for family member with COVID-19
	+ Childcare due to school closure
	+ Other COVID-19 related reason
* **Reason for Leave Request** (`reason_description`): Describe the reason for your leave in detail.
* **Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19?** (`covid_diagnosis`): Select 'Yes' if you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, 'No' otherwise.
* **Date of Diagnosis** (`diagnosis_date`): Enter the date of diagnosis if you select 'Yes' above.
* **Are you caring for a family member with COVID-19?** (`family_illness`): Select 'Yes' if you are caring for a family member with COVID-19, 'No' otherwise.
* **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_documentation`): Select all relevant documentation that you are providing to support your leave request, such as doctor notes, test results, or quarantine orders.
* **Supervisor Approval Status** (`supervisor_approval`): Select the status of your supervisor's approval.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): Enter the date of your supervisor's approval.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide to your supervisor.

Note: This guide is intended to be a helpful resource for employees completing the form. Please review it carefully and ask questions or seek assistance if you are unsure about any part of the form.
