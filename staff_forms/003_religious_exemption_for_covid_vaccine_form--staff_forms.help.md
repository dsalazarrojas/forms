# Religious Exemption For Covid Vaccine Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to submit their request for a religious exemption from COVID-19 vaccination. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that your concerns are understood and processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the provided fields, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (`employee`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Religious Beliefs** (`religious_beliefs`, `text`, required: false): Describe your religious beliefs and how they relate to the COVID-19 vaccination.
* **Reason For Exemption** (`reason_for_exemption`, `text`, required: false): Specify the reason why you're requesting a religious exemption from the COVID-19 vaccination.
* **Doctor's Note** (`doctor_s_note`, `text`, required: false): If you have a letter from a medical professional supporting your exemption, please attach it here. Otherwise, provide a brief explanation of your concerns.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Completing this form accurately and truthfully will help ensure that your concerns are processed correctly.
