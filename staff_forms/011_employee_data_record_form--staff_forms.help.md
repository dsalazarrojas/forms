# Employee Data Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Data Record Form is used to create or update records of new or existing employees within the organization. It helps to maintain accurate and up-to-date information about employees, including their name, job title, job role, contact details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Job Title
	* Job Role
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Reporting Manager
	* Hours Worked
2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Department
	* Date Hired
	* Notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (text, optional)
First Name is the given name of the employee. Enter the employee's first name as it is known to them.

### 2. **Last Name** (text, optional)
Last Name is the surname of the employee. Enter the employee's last name as it is known to them.

### 3. **Job Title** (text, required)
Job Title is the title of the employee's position within the organization. Enter the employee's job title as it is currently assigned to them.

### 4. **Job Role** (select_one, required)
Job Role is a selection from the available job roles within the organization. Choose the job role that best describes the employee's responsibilities.

### 5. **Job Role Options** (text, optional)
This field is not used for most users.

### 6. **Email** (email, required)
Email is the employee's primary contact email address. Enter the employee's email address in the format `example@example.com`.

### 7. **Phone** (text, required)
Phone is the employee's primary contact phone number. Enter the employee's phone number in the format `123-123-1234`.

### 8. **Department** (text, optional)
Department is the employee's department within the organization. Enter the employee's department name as it is known to them.

### 9. **Reporting Manager** (text, required)
Reporting Manager is the name of the employee's direct manager. Enter the name of the employee's reporting manager.

### 10. **Date Hired** (date, optional)
Date Hired is the date the employee was hired. Enter the date the employee was hired in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 11. **Hours Worked** (number, required)
Hours Worked is the number of hours the employee works per week. Enter the number of hours the employee works in whole numbers (e.g., 20, 40,  etc.).

### 12. **Notes** (note, optional)
Notes is a field for additional comments about the employee. Enter any relevant notes or comments about the employee.

Please note that this form is designed to be used for new or updated employee records. If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
