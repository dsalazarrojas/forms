# Expo Health & Safety Staff Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register Expo Health & Safety staff members, ensuring that they have provided necessary contact information and emergency contact details in case of an incident or accident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Start by filling out the "User" field with your name and contact information.
2. If necessary, provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact Form" field.
3. Review and submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form
The "Form" field is not applicable in this case, as it is a placeholder and does not require input.

### User
* **User** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to input your name and contact information. Please fill out your name and any relevant contact details (e.g., phone number, email, etc.) to ensure that we can reach you in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Form
* **Emergency Contact Form** (`text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact person that we should be aware of, please provide their name and contact information in this field. This information will only be used in case of an emergency, and it is optional.
