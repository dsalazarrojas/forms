# Return To Work Letter - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Return To Work Letter" form is used to document an employee's return to work after an extended absence, ensuring a clear record of their absence and return.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee ID of the employee who is returning to work.
2. Select the Start Date and End Date of the employee's absence.
3. Provide a clear Reason for Returning, explaining why the employee is returning to work.
4. Sign the Signature of Employee field with your actual signature.
5. The Manager Name field is optional and can be left blank.
6. Sign the Signature of Manager field with your actual signature.
7. Enter the current Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter the unique ID of the employee who is returning to work. This is typically an employee number or ID badge.
* **Start Date (2)**: Select the date the employee started their absence.
* **End Date (3)**: Select the date the employee plans to end their absence.
* **Reason for Returning (4)**: Explain why the employee is returning to work. This should include details about what the employee worked on during their absence, any progress made, etc.
* **Signature of Employee (5)**: Sign your name to confirm the information provided.
* **Signature of Manager (6)**: Sign the manager's name to confirm that the employee's return is approved.
* **Manager Name (7)**: If applicable, enter the name of the manager who is approving the employee's return.
* **Date (8)**: Enter the current date.
## Tips

* Ensure that the Reason for Returning is clear and concise.
* Sign the Signature of Employee and Signature of Manager fields with your actual signature.
* Keep the form up-to-date and accurate to ensure that all relevant information is documented.
