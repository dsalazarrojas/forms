# Paid Time Off (PTO) Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Paid Time Off (PTO) Request Form is used by employees to request paid time off from work. This form helps management track and manage these requests, ensuring accurate records are kept and decisions can be made accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the Employee field.
2. Enter the start date of the paid time off period in the Start Date field. Ensure it is in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
3. Enter the end date of the paid time off period in the End Date field. Ensure it is in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
4. Provide a brief reason for your paid time off in the Reason field.
5. Select the status of your request in the Status field (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
6. Fill in any additional information in the Paid Time Off Form fields (forms 6-24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`Employee`, `text`, required): Enter your name here. This will help management identify who submitted the request.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you want your paid time off to start. Ensure it is in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you want your paid time off to end. Ensure it is in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Reason** (`Reason`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation for your paid time off request.
* **Status** (`Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your request (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Paid Time Off Form 6** (`Paid Time Off Form 6`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information you may want to provide.
* (The rest of the fields, Paid Time Off Form 7-24, are optional and can be used to gather more detailed information or provide space to enter other related details.)


## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check the dates for the correct format.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing additional information in the Paid Time Off Form fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to management or HR.
