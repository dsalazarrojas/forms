# Team Member Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Member Evaluation Form is a tool for team members to self-evaluate their performance and provide feedback on their strengths and weaknesses. This form is designed to help team members reflect on their progress, identify areas for improvement, and set goals for future growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the field label.
2. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
3. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
4. Select your department from the list of options in the **Department** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
6. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
7. Select the date you would like to be evaluated on from the calendar in the **Evaluation Date** field.
8. Enter the time you would like to be evaluated at in the **Evaluation Time** field.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes about your evaluation in the **Evaluation Note** field.
10. Write a brief explanation of your rating in the **Rating (1-5) Comment** field.
11. Select your rating (1-5) in the **Rating (1-5)** field.
12. Optionally, select "Submit" to indicate that you're finished and ready for your evaluation.
13. Review and submit your form to save your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list of options to indicate your team affiliation.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your evaluation.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to make it easier for us to reach you.
* **Evaluation Date**: Select the date you would like to be evaluated on.
* **Evaluation Time**: Select the time you would like to be evaluated at.
* **Evaluation Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Enter a brief explanation of your rating in this field.
* **Rating (1-5)**: Select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your self-evaluation score.
* **Rating (1-5) Comment**: Enter a brief explanation of your rating in this field.
* **Submit**: Optionally select "Submit" to indicate you're finished with your evaluation and ready for feedback.

Note: The required fields are **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Rating (1-5)**.
