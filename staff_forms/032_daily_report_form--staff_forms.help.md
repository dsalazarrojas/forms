## Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Report Form is a tool used by staff members to report their daily activities, tasks, and issues. It helps management stay updated on progress and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields and make sure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill out the daily_activities field with a brief description of your daily activities.
3. Fill out the tasks_completed field with a list or brief description of tasks you completed.
4. Fill out the tasks_in_progress field with a list or brief description of tasks you are currently working on.
5. Fill out the issues_or_challenges field with any issues or challenges you have faced.
6. In the comments field, add any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **daily_activities** (text, optional): Briefly describe your daily activities.
* **tasks_completed** (text, optional): List or brief description of tasks you completed.
* **tasks_in_progress** (text, optional): List or brief description of tasks you are currently working on.
* **issues_or_challenges** (text, optional): Report any issues or challenges you have faced.
* **comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you have.
