# simple_job_application_form_white_and_responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect job application information from applicants. It gathers contact details, job preferences, and work experience for the hiring team to review and consider for potential positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" section, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your job title, which will help the hiring team understand your background and qualifications.
3. Choose your preferred job location from the list provided.
4. Select the status of your job application.
5. Enter your work experience, including the date you started and ended your previous jobs.
6. Choose the method by which you would like to be contacted for further communication.
7. Select your current work status.
8. Finally, provide the date you submitted your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name and email address to contact you for further communication.
* **Job Title**: Enter a brief description of your job title, such as "Software Engineer" or "Data Analyst".
* **Job Location**: Choose the location where you would like to work, such as "New York" or "Remote".
* **Job Status**: Select the current status of your job, such as "Available" or "Employed".
* **Work Experience**: Enter the date you started and ended your previous job, including the job title and company name.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Contact Method**: Choose the method by which you would like to be contacted, such as "Phone" or "Email".
* **Work Status**: Select your current work status, such as "Full-time" or "Part-time".
* **Job Type**: Choose the type of job you are applying for, such as "Full-time" or "Contract".
* **Job Location**: Select multiple locations where you would like to work, such as "New York" and "Chicago".
* **Resume**: Attach a copy of your resume or CV for further review.
* **Application Form Submitter**: Confirm that you have submitted your application form.
