# employee_opinion_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from employees and non-employees on various aspects of the organization.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are answering the questions that apply to you.
2. Answer each question in as much detail as possible.
3. Select the options that best fit your opinion or experience.
4. If a question does not apply to you, simply skip it.
5. Finally, submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**:
	+ A brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **What do you think is the biggest change we can make to improve our organization?**:
	+ Answer this question to provide your thoughts on potential changes that could improve the organization.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how would you rate the organization's communication?**:
	+ Rate the organization's communication on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very poor and 10 being excellent.
* **What do you like most about the organization?**:
	+ Share what you value most about the organization.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how would you rate the work-life balance of the organization?**:
	+ Rate the organization's work-life balance on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very poor and 10 being excellent.
* **What do you like least about the organization?**:
	+ Share what you value least about the organization.
* **Do you feel that your work is meaningful and contributes to the organization's goals?**:
	+ Answer yes, no, or not sure.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to recommend the organization to a friend or family member?**:
	+ Rate how likely you are to recommend the organization on a scale of 1-10.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with your job?**:
	+ Rate your satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-10.
* **Do you feel that the organization is transparent about its goals and objectives?**:
	+ Answer yes, no, or not sure.
* **What changes would you suggest to improve the organization?**:
	+ Provide specific suggestions for improvements.
* **Are there any other comments or suggestions you would like to make?**:
	+ Add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Is the organization doing enough to support diversity and inclusion?**:
	+ Answer yes, no, or not sure.
* **Is there anything else you would like to say about your experience with the organization?**:
	+ Add any final thoughts or comments.
* **What are your thoughts on how the organization can improve communication?**:
	+ Share your thoughts on how the organization can improve communication.
* **Do you have any other thoughts or suggestions about the organization?**:
	+ Add any additional thoughts or suggestions.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share?**:
	+ Add any final thoughts or comments.
* **Are you an employee or a non-employee?**:
	+ Answer employee, non-employee, or other.
