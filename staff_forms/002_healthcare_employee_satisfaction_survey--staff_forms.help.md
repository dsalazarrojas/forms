# healthcare_employee_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help healthcare employees express their level of satisfaction with their job, team, and compensation. This information will be used to identify areas of improvement and provide opportunities for growth and development within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your response to each question, using the provided options.
2. Make sure to provide your actual name and department in the respective fields.
3. Complete all the questions as honestly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Please enter your actual name as it appears in our records.
- **Department**: Enter the department where you work.
- **Survey Section**: Please add any additional comments or feedback about your job or team.
- **Question 1**: How often do you feel valued by your team?
	+ Select one of the options: **Very Valued**, **Somewhat Valued**, **Not Valued**.
- **Very Valued**: Please select this option if you feel you are valued by your team.
- **Question 2**: How often do you feel supported in your role?
	+ Select one of the options: **Very Supported**, **Somewhat Supported**, **Not Supported**.
- **Very Supported**: Please select this option if you feel you are supported in your role.
- **Question 3**: Are you satisfied with your benefits?
	+ Select one of the options: **Very Satisfied**, **Somewhat Satisfied**, **Not Satisfied**.
- **Very Satisfied**: Please select this option if you are satisfied with your benefits.
- **Question 4**: Are you satisfied with your compensation?
	+ Select one of the options: **Very Satisfied**, **Somewhat Satisfied**, **Not Satisfied**.
- **Very Satisfied**: Please select this option if you are satisfied with your compensation.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
- If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the **Survey Section** field.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback and insights.
