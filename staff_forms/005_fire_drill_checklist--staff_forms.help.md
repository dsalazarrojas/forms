# Fire Drills Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Drills Checklist is a tool to collect information on fire drills and safety procedures in a facility. It's used to gather data on the fire alarm system, fire drill schedule, and various safety equipment and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
4. If you're unsure about any information, don't hesitate to seek guidance from a supervisor or the safety officer.
