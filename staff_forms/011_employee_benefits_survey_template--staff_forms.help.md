# Employee Benefits Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Benefits Survey Template is designed to collect information on employee benefits, including job title, department, and benefits provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to understand what information is being collected.
2. Enter your job title as it is currently listed in your organization's records.
3. Identify your department and enter it as it is listed.
4. Specify the benefits provider (e.g. insurance company, HR team, etc.).
5. Enter your name as it appears on your employee records.
6. Enter your job classification (if applicable).
7. Enter your current salary range.
8. Provide a brief description of your benefits, including any additional details or comments.
9. Enter any relevant benefit details, such as policy numbers or provider contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (required): Enter your current job title as it is listed in your organization's records.
* **Department** (required): Identify your department and enter it as it is listed.
* **Benefits Provider** (required): Specify the benefits provider (e.g. insurance company, HR team, etc.).
* **Employee Name** (required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee records.
* **Job Classification** (optional): Enter your job classification (if applicable).
* **Salary Range** (optional): Enter your current salary range.
* **Benefits Description** (optional): Provide a brief description of your benefits, including any additional details or comments.
* **Benefit Details** (optional): Enter any relevant benefit details, such as policy numbers or provider contact information.
