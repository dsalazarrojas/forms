# New Manager Welcome Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide an onboarding process for new managers, helping them navigate the organization's policies, procedures, and expectations as they transition into their new role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and accurately to ensure a smooth transition into your new role.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
3. Save your answers to the form for future reference and reference purposes.
