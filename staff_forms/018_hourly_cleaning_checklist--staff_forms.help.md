# Hourly Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hourly Cleaning Checklist is a form used by staff to report their cleaning activities and time spent on each area or section of the facility.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the first page, which includes fields for the start and end times, as well as the area or section you are cleaning.
2. On subsequent pages, repeat the same fields (start and end times, area or section, and comments) for each area or section you are cleaning.
3. Be sure to fill out each field accurately and completely, as this information will be used to monitor and evaluate cleaning schedules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_time** (time, required): Enter the start time of your cleaning activity.
* **end_time** (time, required): Enter the end time of your cleaning activity.
* **area_cleaned** (select_one, optional): Select the area or section of the facility you are cleaning. Options include: Bathroom, Kitchen, Bedroom, Hallway, Living Room, Backyard, Garage, Office.
* **area_description** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the area or section you cleaned.
* **start_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your cleaning activity. (Note: This field may not be relevant if your activity is part of a recurring schedule.)
* **start_time_1**, **start_time_1** (time, optional): Enter the start time of your cleaning activity on the second and subsequent pages. (Note: These fields are repeated for each subsequent page.)
* **end_time_1**, **end_time_1** (time, optional): Enter the end time of your cleaning activity on the second and subsequent pages. (Note: These fields are repeated for each subsequent page.)
* **area_cleaned_1**, **area_cleaned_1** (select_one, optional): Select the area or section of the facility you are cleaning on the second and subsequent pages. (Note: These fields are repeated for each subsequent page.)
* **area_description_1**, **area_description_1** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the area or section you cleaned on the second and subsequent pages. (Note: These fields are repeated for each subsequent page.)
* **time_spent** (number, optional): Enter the amount of time spent cleaning the area or section.
* **time_spent_1**, **time_spent_1** (number, optional): Enter the amount of time spent cleaning the area or section on the second and subsequent pages. (Note: These fields are repeated for each subsequent page.)
* **second_page**, **second_page_comments** (text, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the second page of cleaning activity. (Note: This field is repeated for each subsequent page.)
* **area_cleaned_3**, **area_cleaned_3** (select_one, optional): Select the area or section of the facility you are cleaning on the third page. (Note: This field is repeated for each subsequent page.)
* **area_description_3** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the area or section you cleaned on the third page. (Note: This field is repeated for each subsequent page.)
* **time_spent_3**, **time_spent_3** (number, optional): Enter the amount of time spent cleaning the area or section on the third page. (Note: This field is repeated for each subsequent page.)
* **comments** (text, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the cleaning activity.

Note: The fields with an underscore suffix (e.g., `start_time_1`) are repeated for each page of the form.
