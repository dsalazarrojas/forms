# Contact Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Information Update Form is used to collect and update the contact details of students and their parents/guardians. This form is crucial for maintaining accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand its sections and fields.
2. Update each field accordingly, making sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in red).
3. Ensure that you have provided valid and accurate information.
4. Once you have finished updating all fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level the student is currently in from the provided options.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
* **Primary Parent / Guardian Name**: Enter the primary parent/guardian's name.
* **Primary Parent Email**: Enter the primary parent/guardian's email address.
* **Primary Parent Phone**: Enter the primary parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Secondary Parent / Guardian Name**: Enter the secondary parent/guardian's name, if applicable.
* **Street Address**: Enter the student's street address.
* **City**: Enter the city where the student resides.
* **State**: Enter the state where the student resides.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the student's zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Student**: Select the relationship of the emergency contact person to the student (e.g., mother, father, etc.).
* **Emergency Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Date These Changes Take Effect**: Select the date when the changes should take effect.
* **Reason for Update**: Select the reason for updating the contact information from the provided options or enter "Other" if it doesn't fit into the given categories.
* **Secondary Parent / Guardian Name**: If the student has a secondary parent/guardian, enter their name.
* **Secondary Parent / Guardian Email**: If the student has a secondary parent/guardian, enter their email address.
* **Secondary Parent / Guardian Phone**: If the student has a secondary parent/guardian, enter their phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [support contact].
* Double-check the form for any required fields before submitting it.
* If you need to update multiple students' contact information, please submit a new form for each student.
