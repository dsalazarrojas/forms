# Employee Referral - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Referral" form is a tool for employees to refer a candidate they know who may be a good fit for a job opening at our organization. If you are familiar with a candidate who you believe would excel in a role, you can use this form to suggest them for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the candidate's first and last names in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. If you think the candidate would be a good fit for a particular department (Marketing, Sales, or HR), select it from the "Department" dropdown list.
3. Enter the candidate's email address to allow your manager and other relevant teams to contact them.
4. Briefly describe the candidate's job title and their relationship with you.
5. If applicable, provide your manager's name, email address, and phone number for the candidate to contact.
6. Click "Submit" to send the referral information to the relevant teams for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the candidate's first name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the candidate’s last name (e.g., Doe).
* **Department** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the department you think the candidate would be a good fit for (e.g., Marketing, Sales, HR).
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the candidate’s professional email address.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Briefly describe the candidate’s job title or position.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the candidate's phone number, if applicable.
* **Manager Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the candidate’s manager.
* **Manager Email** (email, optional): Enter the candidate’s manager’s email address.
* **Manager Phone** (text, optional): Enter the candidate's manager’s phone number.
