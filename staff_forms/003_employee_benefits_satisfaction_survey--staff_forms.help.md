# Employee Benefits Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees rate their satisfaction with the benefits package provided by the organization. The survey aims to gather feedback on various aspects of the benefits package, including health insurance, retirement package, employee assistance program, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose and instructions of the survey.
2. For each question, select the corresponding option that best reflects your level of satisfaction or response.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the survey's purpose and instructions.
* **Benefits Package**: Select all the benefits packages that you currently receive or are interested in. (Health Insurance, Retirement Package, Employee Assistance Program)
* **Satisfaction with Health Insurance**: Rate your satisfaction with the health insurance package. (Strongly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied)
* **Satisfaction with Retirement Package**: Rate your satisfaction with the retirement package. (Strongly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied)
* **Satisfaction with Employee Assistance Program**: Rate your satisfaction with the employee assistance program. (Strongly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied)
* **Benefits Package Value**: Rate the value you derive from the overall benefits package. (Enter a numerical value)
* **Satisfaction with Benefits Package**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the benefits package. (Enter a numerical value)
* **Suggest an Improvement**: Provide any suggestions or improvements you would like to see in the benefits package.
* **Benefits Satisfied**: Rate your satisfaction with the benefits package. (Strongly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied)
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the benefits package. (Enter a numerical value)
* **Suggest a Change**: Provide any suggestions or changes you would like to see in the benefits package.
* **Employee Nickname**: Enter your employee nickname or ID number.
* **Employee Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions or improvements you would like to see in the organization.
* **Employee Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you would like to share.
* **Supervisor Nickname**: Enter your supervisor's nickname or ID number.
* **Supervisor Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions or improvements you would like to see in the organization.
* **Supervisor Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you would like to share.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Please keep your answers concise and clear.
* Your feedback will help us understand your needs and improve the benefits package.
