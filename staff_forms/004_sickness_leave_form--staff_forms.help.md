# Sickness Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sickness Leave Form is used to request a leave of absence from work due to illness or medical reasons. This form is typically completed by employees who are experiencing health issues that prevent them from performing their work-related duties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start Date**: Enter the date from which you are requesting to start your leave. This is the date you will begin your absence from work.
2. **End Date**: Enter the date you expect to return to work.
3. **Reason**: Briefly describe the reason for your leave. This will help your manager understand the nature of your condition and make necessary arrangements.
4. **Approved By**: Select the person in your approval hierarchy who can approve your leave. Options are typically your supervisor or manager.
5. **Leave Credit**: If you have accrued leave credits, you can enter the amount you wish to take. Note: Only enter this if your company has a leave credit system in place.
6. **Remaining Leave**: If you have leave credits left, enter the remaining amount. Note: Only enter this if your company has a leave credit system in place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date**: A date field where you can select the start date of your leave. Enter the date you will begin your absence from work.
* **End Date**: A date field where you can select the end date of your leave. Enter the date you expect to return to work.
* **Reason**: A text field where you can provide a brief explanation of your reason for requesting leave. This will help your manager understand the nature of your condition.
* **Approved By**: A dropdown menu where you can select the person in your approval hierarchy who can approve your leave. Options are typically your supervisor or manager.
* **Leave Credit**: A numerical field where you can enter the amount of leave credits you wish to take, if your company has a leave credit system in place.
* **Remaining Leave**: A numerical field where you can enter the remaining amount of leave credits you have, if your company has a leave credit system in place.
* **Approved By**: This field is automatically populated with the name of the person who approved your leave. It will not be editable by the user.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate and clear information in the "Reason" field to help your manager make necessary arrangements.
* If you have accrued leave credits, make sure to enter the correct amount in the "Leave Credit" field.
* If you are unsure about which person in your approval hierarchy can approve your leave, refer to your company's policies or ask your HR representative.
