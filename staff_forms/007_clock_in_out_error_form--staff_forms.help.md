This form is intended to be filled out by employees when they have experienced errors while clocking in or out. It is designed to capture relevant information about the errors that occur, including the date and time they occurred, the type of error, and the steps taken to correct it. The form aims to standardize the process of documenting such errors, ensuring consistent and accurate reporting, and facilitate a quick resolution by providing relevant context and supporting documentation.

Before proceeding to fill this form, please ensure that you are in a state to provide an accurate report of the error that occurred. The information requested will be used by your supervisors or management to review and address the issue.

**Clock In Out Error Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Clock In Out Error Form is designed to document errors that occur while clocking in or out. This includes instances where you clocked in or out incorrectly, had an issue with your clocking system, or need to report a discrepancy in your work hours.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the available sections and fields to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in your **Employee Name**, **Employee ID**, and **Email Address** in the respective fields.
3. Choose the **Type of Clock Error** that occurred from the provided options.
4. Enter the **Date of Error** when the error occurred.
5. If applicable, provide **Actual Arrival Time** and **Actual Departure Time** to provide context.
6. Select the **Type of Shift** you were working on.
7. Choose the **Supervisor or Manager Name** who will review your report.
8. Select the **Cause of the Error** from the provided options or describe the cause in detail.
9. Indicate if this is your **First Timing Error**.
10. Select the **Resolution** or actions to be taken to correct the error.
11. Enter the **Time Adjustment** needed in minutes (positive or negative).
12. Select whether this error is your **First Error** or if it has happened before.
13. Enter your **Form Submission Date** (automatically populated).
14. Provide any **Additional Notes or Comments** regarding the error.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Provide your full legal name as per your employee records.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your staff identification number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work or contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number (optional but recommended for follow-up communication).
* **Error Details**: Provide information about the clock error that occurred.
* **Date of Error**: The date when the error occurred.
* **Type of Clock Error**: Choose the error type that best describes what happened.
* **Actual Arrival Time**: Enter the time you actually arrived at work (if clocked in incorrectly).
* **Actual Departure Time**: Enter the time you actually left work (if clocked out incorrectly).
* **Recorded Clock In Time**: The time shown in the system if you were clocked in (if applicable).
* **Recorded Clock Out Time**: The time shown in the system if you were clocked out (if applicable).
* **Type of Shift**: Indicate the type of shift you were working on.
* **Supervisor or Manager Name**: Provide the name of the person overseeing your shift.
* **What caused the Error**: Choose the cause of the error or describe it in detail.
* **Cause of Error**: Select the cause of the error or describe it in detail.
* **First Timing Error**: Indicate whether this is your first error or if you have made errors before.
* **Time Adjustment**: Enter the time adjustment needed in minutes.
* **Resolution**: Choose the actions needed to correct the error.
* **Submission Date**: The date this form is being submitted.
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Provide any additional information or context about the error.

**Tips**

* Be as specific as possible when describing the error and its cause.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Review the form for accuracy before submitting it to avoid errors or inconsistencies.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or manager for guidance.
