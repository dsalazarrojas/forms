# HR Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for an HR investigation. It may be used for various types of investigations, such as harassment, bullying, or workplace accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident or issue being investigated.
2. Provide the names of the employees involved in the incident or issue.
3. Write a detailed description of what happened.
4. Identify the investigator and their role in the investigation.
5. Enter the date of the investigation.
6. Record any notes from the investigator.
7. If applicable, enter the names of any witnesses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investigation Date** (form_1, date, required): Enter the date the incident or issue occurred. This date should be formatted as a regular date, e.g., MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee Involved 1** (form_2, text, required): Enter the name of the first employee involved in the incident or issue.
* **Employee Involved 2** (form_3, text, required): Enter the name of the second employee involved in the incident or issue.
* **Description** (form_4, note, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Investigator** (form_5, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the investigation.
* **Date of Investigation** (form_6, date, required): Enter the date the investigation was completed.
* **Investigator Notes** (form_7, note, required): Record any notes from the investigator.
* **Witness 1** (form_8, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the first witness.
* **Witness 2** (form_9, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the second witness.
* **Witness 3** (form_10, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the third witness.
* **Witness 4** (form_11, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the fourth witness.
* **Witness 5** (form_12, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the fifth witness.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and objective information to help HR resolve the issue.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with HR or the investigator.
* This form is intended for internal use only.
