# employee_appreciation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate recognition and appreciation among team members, promoting a positive and supportive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Thanks Title**: Provide a title for the appreciation message. You can use this field to highlight the specific reason why you're showing appreciation.
2. **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the colleague you're showing appreciation to.
3. **Reason for Thanks**: Describe the reason why you're showing appreciation to the colleague.
4. **Appreciation Message**: Write a personal message expressing your appreciation to the colleague.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **thanks_title** (`thanks_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for the appreciation message, e.g., "Congratulations on Completing Project X!" or "Thank you for your hard work!"
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the colleague you're showing appreciation to.
* **reason_for_thanks** (`reason_for_thanks`, text, optional): Describe the reason why you're showing appreciation to the colleague, e.g., "for consistently delivering high-quality work" or "for going above and beyond".
* **appreciation_message** (`appreciation_message`, text, optional): Write a personal message expressing your appreciation to the colleague, e.g., "Your dedication and hard work have not gone unnoticed!" or "Thank you for being an amazing team player!"
