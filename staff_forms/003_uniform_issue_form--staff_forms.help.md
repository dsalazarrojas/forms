# Uniform Issue Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Issue Form is a tool used to report uniform issues reported by staff members. This form collects information about the issue, the staff member's role, equipment type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and role to identify yourself and your position.
2. Select the type of uniform equipment that is not functioning correctly.
3. Enter the date the issue occurred.
4. If applicable, enter the quantity of uniforms affected.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about the issue.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Select who submitted the form (if multiple staff members can submit).
8. Enter the date the form was submitted.
9. If applicable, select who created the form (if multiple staff members can create).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification card.
  - **Role (2)**: Select your job title or position from the options provided.
  - **Department (3)**: If you are experiencing an issue with a specific department, enter the department name.
  - **Equipment Type (4)**: Select the type of uniform equipment that is not functioning correctly (e.g., Jacket, Pants, Hat).
  - **Issue Date (5)**: Enter the date the issue occurred, in the format MM/DD/YY.
  - **Quantity (6)**: If the issue affects multiple uniforms, enter the quantity.
  - **Note (7)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the issue.
  - **Email (8)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
  - **Phone (9)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  - **Submitted By (10)**: Select who submitted the form (if multiple staff members can submit).
  - **Submitted Date (11)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
  - **Created By (12)**: Select who created the form (if multiple staff members can create).
