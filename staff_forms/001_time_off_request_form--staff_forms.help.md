# Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Off Request Form is used to request time off for an employee to take a break. This form is intended for employees who need to take time off for various reasons such as personal or family emergencies, doctor's appointments, or vacation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your employee information.
2. Enter your start date of absence.
3. Enter your end date of absence.
4. Choose the correct time interval for your absence.
5. Provide any additional comments about your request.
6. Confirm that your information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your company email address.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to begin your time off.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like to return to work.
* **Time Interval**: Choose the time interval that best fits your needs.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional information about your request.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that your request is accurate and complete.
