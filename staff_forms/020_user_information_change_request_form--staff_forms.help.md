# User Information Change Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to submit a request to change user information. Please use this form to update your title, department, email, or phone number. This form is not intended for non-personal changes or bulk changes. Ensure that the information you are requesting to change is accurate and relevant to your role within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form:

1.  Enter your User ID (not required but will be used to track changes)
2.  Enter your current title
3.  Enter your new title
4.  Enter your new department
5.  Enter your new email address
6.  Enter your new phone number

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (optional): Enter your User ID (if known). This will help us track the changes to your information.
* **Current Title** (required): Enter your current job title as it currently appears in the system.
* **New Title** (required): Enter your new job title as it should appear in the system.
* **Department** (required): Enter your new department as it should appear in the system.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your new email address. Please format as `yourname@yourdomain.com` (e.g., `johndoe@example.com`).
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your new phone number. Please format as `1234567890` (e.g., `123-456-7890` or `123-456-7890 ext. 123`).

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information you are requesting to change is accurate and relevant to your role within the organization.
*   If you are unsure about what information can be changed or why, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
*   This form should only be used for personal changes, not for changes to non-personal information or for changes on behalf of others.
