# Employee Discipline Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document incidents involving employees, such as misconduct, poor performance, or policy violations, and to track the actions taken as a result of these incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have reviewed and understood the relevant policies and procedures related to employee conduct and behavior.
*   Verify that you are not duplicating effort by checking that a similar incident has not already been documented.
*   Fill in the employee ID number of the employee who was involved in the incident.
*   Enter the date of the incident using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details or circumstances.
*   Select an action that was taken as a result of the incident from the list of options provided.
*   Enter your signature below to confirm that you have completed this form accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Required): Enter the ID number of the employee who was involved in the incident. This will help ensure that the incident is accurately linked to the correct employee.
*   **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the incident in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Description of Incident** (`description_of_incident`, Note, Required): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details or circumstances.
*   **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, Select One, Required): Select an action from the list of options provided as a result of the incident.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required): Enter your signature below to confirm that you have completed this form accurately and honestly.

## Tips
*   Always review the relevant policies and procedures related to employee conduct and behavior before completing this form.
*   Ensure that you have accurately completed the form by double-checking your responses.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or guidance.
*   Keep a record of all incidents and actions taken for future reference and potential use in performance evaluations or other reviews.
