# Personnel Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personnel Request Form is a tool for users to request personnel-related services, such as employment, changes to job status, or other personnel concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "New Employment Request" page with your request details.
2. Select the job title that best describes your request.
3. Choose the department that your request is related to.
4. Select the job category that applies to your request.
5. Choose the employment type that suits your needs.
6. Enter the start and end dates for your request (if applicable).
7. Select the job status that best describes your request.
8. Enter any reason for leave or other personnel concerns.
9. Provide contact information for the person handling your request.
10. Enter the number of department employees involved.
11. Enter the department budget (if applicable).
12. Describe the job and responsibilities involved.
13. List any required documents.
14. Enter any comments or additional information.
15. Select the approval status and date (if applicable).
16. Enter any reject reason and date (if applicable).
17. Select the request status and date (if applicable).
18. Choose whether the request is assigned to someone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Employment Request** (`new_employment_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Select the job title that best describes your request.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the department that your request is related to.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job category that applies to your request.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the employment type that suits your needs.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date for your request (if applicable).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date for your request (if applicable).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select the job status that best describes your request.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Enter any reason for leave or other personnel concerns.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Provide contact information for the person handling your request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email for the person handling your request.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone for the person handling your request.
* **Department Employees** (`department_employees`, number, optional): Enter the number of department employees involved.
* **Department Budget** (`department_budget`, number, optional): Enter the department budget (if applicable).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Describe the job and responsibilities involved.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, text, optional): List any job responsibilities.
* **Required Documents** (`required_documents`, note, optional): List any required documents.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or additional information.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the approval status.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the approval date (if applicable).
* **Reject Reason** (`reject_reason`, text, optional): Enter any reject reason.
* **Reject Date** (`reject_date`, date, optional): Enter the reject date (if applicable).
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, select_one, optional): Select the request status.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the request date.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the request is assigned to someone.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the request is assigned to someone.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your request is accurate and complete before submitting.
* Do not submit duplicate requests.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the person handling your request for clarification.
