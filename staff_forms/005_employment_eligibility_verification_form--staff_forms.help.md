# Employment Eligibility Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an employee's employment status and history. It helps employers or HR representatives to verify employee information, track employment dates, and document reasons for termination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the employee's information, such as their name and ID, on page 1.
* Provide the employee's address and employer information on page 1.
* Enter the employee's employment details, including dates of employment, reason for termination, and certification status on page 2.
* Enter the employee's financial data, including gross salary, net pay, and hours worked on page 3.
* Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information**: Enter the employee's full name and ID number.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number or Employee ID number.
* **Employee Address**: Enter the employee's current or last known address.
* **Employer Information**: Enter the name and address of the employer.
* **Company**: Enter the company name and address.
* **Department**: Enter the department the employee worked in.
* **Supervisor**: Enter the name of the employee's supervisor.
* **Date Of Employment**: Enter the date the employee started working.
* **Last Day Of Employment**: Enter the date the employee last worked.
* **Reason For Employment Termination**: Choose a reason for the employee's termination.
* **Certification**: Choose the current employment status.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date the employee's employment was terminated.
* **Termination Reason**: Enter a brief explanation for the reason of termination.
* **Last Date Worked**: Enter the last date the employee worked.
* **Last Date Paid**: Enter the last date the employee was paid.
* **Hours Worked**: Enter the number of hours the employee worked before termination.
* **Rate Of Pay**: Enter the employee's hourly rate of pay.
* **Gross Salary**: Enter the employee's gross salary.
* **Net Pay**: Enter the employee's net pay.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign the form to confirm employee information is accurate.
* **Employer Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm the accuracy of the employment information.
