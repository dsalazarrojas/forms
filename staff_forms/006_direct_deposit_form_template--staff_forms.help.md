# Direct Deposit Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for direct deposit payroll services. It gathers employee details and bank account information to facilitate direct deposit payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the bank account number in the "Bank Account Number" field.
3. Enter the bank's name in the "Bank Name" field.
4. Enter the routing number in the "Routing Number" field.
5. Enter the account number in the "Account Number" field.
6. Enter the employee's address in the "Employee Address" field.
7. Select the authorization date for direct deposit payments in the "Authorization Date" field.
8. Select the date to stop direct deposit payments in the "Authorization End Date" field.
9. Enter the name of the authorized person in the "Authorized Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Bank Account Number**: Enter the employee's bank account number where they want to receive direct deposit payments.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of the employee's bank.
* **Routing Number**: Enter the routing number for the employee's bank account.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number for the employee's bank account.
* **Employee Address**: Enter the employee's address, which may be required for direct deposit setup.
* **Authorization Date**: Select the date from which direct deposit payments will start being made to the employee's account.
* **Authorization End Date**: Select the date when direct deposit payments will stop being made to the employee's account.
* **Authorized Person**: Enter the name of the person authorized to make direct deposit payments on behalf of the employee.

## Tips
- Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that the employee's bank account information is correct to avoid any issues with direct deposit payments.
- If you're unsure about any information, consult with the payroll department or the employee directly.
