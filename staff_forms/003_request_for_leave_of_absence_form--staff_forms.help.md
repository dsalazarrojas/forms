## Request For Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from employees who need to take a leave of absence. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide the necessary details for your leave of absence, ensuring that your supervisor and HR department have all the information they need for a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee details, including your name, employee ID, and any other relevant information.
2. Choose the start and end dates of your leave, using the calendar or date selector provided.
3. Provide a clear reason for your leave, so that your supervisor and HR department can understand your situation.
4. Enter your contact information, so that we can reach out to you for any further questions or updates.
5. If your leave involves a specific start and end time, please select the start and end times from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): This field is for your employee information, including your name and any other relevant details that you would like to provide. Please make sure to include your employee ID or other contact information, if applicable.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, optional): This field is for selecting the start date of your leave. Please use the calendar or date selector provided to select a specific date or range of dates.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, optional): This field is for selecting the end date of your leave. Please use the calendar or date selector provided to select a specific date or range of dates.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): This field is for providing a clear reason for your leave, so that your supervisor and HR department can understand your situation. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This field is for providing your contact information, so that we can reach out to you for any further questions or updates.
* **Leave Start Time** (`leave_start_time`, time, optional): This field is for selecting the start time of your leave, if applicable. Please use the time selector provided to select a specific time.
* **Leave End Time** (`leave_end_time`, time, optional): This field is for selecting the end time of your leave, if applicable. Please use the time selector provided to select a specific time.
* **Expected Return Date** (`expected_return_date`, date, optional): This field is for selecting the date you plan to return to work. Please use the calendar or date selector provided to select a specific date.
