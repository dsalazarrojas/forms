# Daily Observation Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Observation Report form is a standardized tool used to record staff observations, providing valuable insights for reporting and evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, select the correct date in the **observation_date** field.
- Enter the **staff_name** and **staff_number** fields accurately.
- Choose the correct department in the **department** field by selecting from the options provided.
- Record your **observed_by** name in the **observed_by** field.
- Enter your **observations** and any additional **notes** relevant to your work.
- Finally, add your **signature** for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### observation_date
*   **observation_date** (date): Enter the date of the observation in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### staff_name
*   **staff_name** (text): Enter your name as a staff member.

### staff_number
*   **staff_number** (number): Enter your staff number, which may be your ID number or personnel number.

### department
*   **department** (select_one): Select the department you belong to from the options provided.

### observed_by
*   **observed_by** (text): Enter the name of the person who observed you.

### observations
*   **observations** (text): Record your observations in detail, including any relevant information.

### notes
*   **notes** (text): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding your observations.

### signature
*   **signature** (text): Add your signature for verification purposes.

### date_observed
*   **date_observed** (date): This field is redundant and not to be filled out.
