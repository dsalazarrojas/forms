# New Employee Email Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create new employee email addresses and associated metadata. Fill out this form to add new employees to your database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter the employee's last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter the employee's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the employee's department from the "Department" field.
5. Enter the employee's job title in the "Job title" field.
6. Select the employee's role from the "Role" field.
7. Select the employee's status as either "active" or "inactive" from the "Status" field.
8. Enter any additional notes about the employee in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the date the employee was hired in the "Hire date" field.
10. Select the employee's manager from the "Manager" field.
11. Select the date the form was created by from the "Created date" field.
12. Select the date the form was last updated from the "Updated date" field.
13. Select the date the form was deleted by from the "Deleted date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (text)**: Enter the employee's first name. (Required: no)
* **Last name (text)**: Enter the employee's last name. (Required: no)
* **Email (email)**: Enter the employee's email address. (Required: false)
* **Department (select multiple)**: Select the department this employee belongs to. Options: option 1, option 2, option 3.
* **Job title (text)**: Enter the employee's job title. (Required: false)
* **Role (select one)**: Select the employee's role. Options: option 1, option 2, option 3.
* **Manager (text)**: Enter the employee's manager's name. (Required: false)
* **Hire date (date)**: Enter the date the employee was hired. (Required: false)
* **Status (select one)**: Select the employee's status as either "active" or "inactive". Options: active, inactive. (Required: true)
* **Notes (note)**: Enter any additional notes about the employee. (Required: false)
* **Created by (text)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Created date (date)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Updated by (text)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Updated date (date)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Deleted by (text)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Deleted date (date)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Departmental (text)**: Enter a description of the department. (Required: false)
* **Job title ID (number)**: Enter the job title ID. (Required: false)
* **Role ID (number)**: Enter the role ID. (Required: false)
* **Created at (date)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Updated at (date)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Deleted at (date)**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Deleted by ID (number)**: Enter the deleted by ID. (Required: false)
* **Deleted at ID (number)**: Enter the deleted at ID. (Required: false)
* **Status ID (number)**: Enter the status ID. (Required: false)

Please note that fields with "(Required: false)" are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
