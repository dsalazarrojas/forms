# Employee Complaint Investigation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and investigate employee complaints in a structured and organized manner. It is intended to help investigate and resolve complaints efficiently and fairly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter the title of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* Complete the complaint information (complaint info, complaint date, complaint time) on the main page.
* Select the type of complaint (harassment, sexual harassment, retaliation, bullying) in the select multiple options field.
* Enter any relevant information about the complainant (complainant info).
* Enter a brief description of the complaint (complaint description).
* Enter any relevant information about the investigator (investigator info).
* Sign and date the form (signature and date sent).
* Select the status of the complaint (open, closed, escalated).
* Schedule and note follow-up dates and times (follow-up date, follow-up time, next follow-up date, next follow-up time).
* Enter the action to be taken for follow-up (follow-up action, next follow-up action).
* Enter the resolution of the complaint (complaint resolution).
* Add any notes on the investigation (notes).
* Select the method of follow-up (email, phone, meeting, other).
* Enter any additional information on follow-up (follow-up info, next follow-up info).
* Sign the form as the investigator (investigator's signature).
* Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **complaint-info**: Enter a brief description of the complaint, including any relevant details.
* **complaint-date**: Enter the date the complaint occurred.
* **complaint-time**: Enter the time the complaint occurred.
* **complaint-type**: Select the type of complaint (harassment, sexual harassment, retaliation, bullying).
* **complainant-info**: Enter any relevant information about the person making the complaint.
* **complaint-description**: Enter a brief description of the complaint.
* **investigator-info**: Enter any relevant information about the investigator.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form.
* **Date sent**: Enter the date the form is sent to the investigator.
* **complaint-status**: Select the status of the complaint (open, closed, escalated).
* **follow-up-date**: Enter the date for the first follow-up.
* **follow-up-time**: Enter the time for the first follow-up.
* **follow-up-action**: Enter the action to be taken for follow-up.
* **next-follow-up-date**: Enter the date for the next follow-up.
* **next-follow-up-time**: Enter the time for the next follow-up.
* **next-follow-up-action**: Enter the action to be taken for the next follow-up.
* **complaint-resolution**: Enter the resolution of the complaint.
* **notes**: Enter any notes on the investigation.
* **follow-ups**: Select the method of follow-up (email, phone, meeting, other).
* **follow-up-info**: Enter any additional information on follow-up.
* **signature-info**: Enter a note about the signature.
* **next-follow-up-info**: Enter a note about the next follow-up.
* **submit**: Submit the form.

Tips:
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Make sure to enter all relevant information.
* Review and verify all the information entered.
* Sign and date the form as the investigator.
* Keep a record of all follow-ups and investigations.

Note: This form is designed to be used as a template for investigation and resolution of employee complaints. It is not a formal complaint form for external complaints, but rather for internal use within the organization.
