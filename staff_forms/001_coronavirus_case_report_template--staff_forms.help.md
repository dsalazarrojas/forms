# Coronavirus Case Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report cases of COVID-19 in the workplace. It helps staff members report symptoms, locations, and other relevant information to ensure accurate tracking and reporting of cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the case before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Make sure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`case_report_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person affected by COVID-19.
* **Date** (`case_report_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the case occurred or was detected.
* **Location** (`case_report_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the case occurred or was detected.
* **Description** (`case_report_description`, `text`, required): Describe the situation or circumstances surrounding the case.
* **Symptoms** (`case_report_symptoms`, `text`, optional): List any symptoms experienced by the person affected by COVID-19.
* **Contact** (`case_report_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter any contact information for the person affected by COVID-19.
* **Other** (`case_report_other`, `note`, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the case.

## Tips
* Be as specific and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, leave it blank or contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* Make sure to report only new, previously unreported cases to avoid duplication.
