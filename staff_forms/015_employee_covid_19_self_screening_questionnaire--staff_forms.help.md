# employee_covid_19_self_screening_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The employee_covid_19_self_screening_questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from employees about their health status and travel history to ensure a safe and healthy work environment. This form will help the management track and prevent the spread of COVID-19 in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "General Information" section with your basic details, such as your name and employee ID.
2. Answer the "Symptoms" question truthfully, detailing any symptoms you have experienced.
3. Enter your current temperature if you have taken one.
4. Indicate your recent travel history, including any destinations you have visited.
5. Describe any close contacts you have had with people who may be infected.
6. Enter any travel history, including dates and destinations.
7. Indicate the duration of any symptoms you have experienced.
8. Provide information about your health care provider.
9. Select your testing status (Positive, Negative, or Not Tested).
10. Indicate your vaccination status (Fully Vaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, or Not Vaccinated).
11. Enter your vaccination date, if vaccinated.
12. Enter any information about your vaccinated location.
13. Enter any information about your vaccination type (if applicable).
14. Enter the vaccine dose(s) you have had.
15. Enter the vaccine lot number (if applicable).
16. Enter any additional information about your vaccination dose(s).
17. Provide information about your vaccination certificate (if applicable).
18. Provide information about your vaccination card (if applicable).
19. Enter any medical records you would like to share.
20. Enter information about your doctor or health care provider.
21. Enter your doctor's phone number.
22. Enter your doctor's email address.
23. Enter your doctor's office address.
