# Employee Attestation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Attestation Form is used to capture and document an employee's attestation information, which is crucial for our organization's operational processes and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about yourself and your supervisory contacts.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permission or approval from your supervisor or manager before filling out this form.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.
