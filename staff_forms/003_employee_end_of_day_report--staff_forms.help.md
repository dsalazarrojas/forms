# Employee End Of Day Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow employees to report their activities and progress at the end of each day. This form is used to track employee hours worked, notes, and other relevant information, and is typically used to support time tracking, payroll processing, and other HR-related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Employee Name" field to enter your name as an employee.
2. Choose the "Date" of the day you are reporting on.
3. Enter the number of "Hours Worked" for the day.
4. Enter any "Notes" relevant to your work or activities.
5. Provide your "Email" address.
6. Enter your "Phone" number (optional).
7. Select "Category" to indicate if you worked on a specific project or task.
8. Enter the "Project" name and description (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Select the date for which you are reporting on your activities.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, Number, Optional): Enter the total number of hours you worked today.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text area, Optional): Enter any notes or comments about your work or activities.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Category** (`category`, Select_one, Optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you worked on a specific project or task, or "No" if you did not.
* **Project** (`project`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the project or task you worked on, if applicable.
