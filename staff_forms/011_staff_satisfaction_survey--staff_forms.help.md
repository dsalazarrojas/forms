# staff_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The staff satisfaction survey is a tool to gather feedback and suggestions from staff members to improve the work environment and employee satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Select your position from the provided options in the "position" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Provide your contact phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement in the "suggestion" field.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with a number (e.g., 1-5) in the "rating" field.
7. Type any additional comments or feedback in the "comments" field.
8. Choose the category that best describes the feedback or suggestion you are providing in the "category" field. You can select from:
	* Staff
	* Management
	* Facilities
	* Training
	* Other
9. If you have selected "Other" in the "category" field, please provide a brief description of the category in the "category_other" field.
10. Click "submit" to send your feedback and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name to help us identify you and your feedback.
* **position** (text, optional): Select your position in the organization to help us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **phone** (text, optional): Provide your contact phone number for future reference.
* **suggestion** (text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement.
* **rating** (number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with a number (e.g., 1-5).
* **comments** (note, optional): Type any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **category** (select one, optional): Choose the category that best describes the feedback or suggestion you are providing.
* **category_other** (text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the "category" field, provide a brief description of the category.
* **submit** (text, required): Click "submit" to send your feedback and suggestions to our team.
