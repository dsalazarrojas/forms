# Shift Swap Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Swap Request Form is a tool to help employees request shifts with their colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. If applicable, select the start date and start time of your shift in the "shift_start_date" and "shift_start_time" fields, respectively.
3. Similarly, enter the end date and end time of your shift in the "shift_end_date" and "shift_end_time" fields, respectively.
4. Choose the date you would like to swap your shift with another employee in the "swap_date" field.
5. Select the time you would like to start your shift on the swap date in the "swap_time" field.
6. Choose who you would like to swap shifts with from the list provided in the "swapped_with" field.
7. Enter a reason for your shift swap request in the "reason" field.
8. (Optional) Provide any additional comments related to your shift swap request in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name, as it appears on your work records.
* **shift_start_date**: Enter the date you are scheduled to start your shift. This field is optional.
* **shift_start_time**: Enter the time you are scheduled to start your shift. This field is optional.
* **shift_end_date**: Enter the date you are scheduled to end your shift. This field is optional.
* **shift_end_time**: Enter the time you are scheduled to end your shift. This field is optional.
* **swap_date**: Enter the date you would like to swap shifts. This field is optional.
* **swap_time**: Enter the time you would like to start your shift on the swap date. This field is optional.
* **swapped_with**: Select who you would like to swap shifts with from the list provided. This field is mandatory.
* **reason**: Enter a reason for your shift swap request. This field is mandatory.
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments related to your shift swap request. This field is optional.

## Tips
- Be honest when filling out the form for accurate processing.
- Ensure you have permission from the person you're requesting to swap shifts with.
- Review the form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you need assistance with shift swaps, reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
