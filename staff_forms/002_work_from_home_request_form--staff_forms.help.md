# Work From Home Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request permission to work from home due to reasons such as illness, family emergencies, or other unforeseen circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Info section with your full name, employee ID, and department.
2. Select the Start Date and End Date of your work-from-home period.
3. Type in the Location where you will be working from (e.g., your home address).
4. Sign the E-Signature field with your electronic signature.
5. Select the Supervisor Approval field to choose whether you need approval for the work-from-home period.
6. If necessary, add any additional Notes about your work-from-home request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your full name, employee ID, and department. Please make sure to include your correct information so that your supervisor can easily identify you.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): This field is where you select the start date of your work-from-home period. Please use the calendar to select the correct date.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): This field is where you select the end date of your work-from-home period. Please use the calendar to select the correct date.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): This field is where you type in the location where you will be working from (e.g., your home address).
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, text, optional): This field is where you electronically sign the form with your name.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you select whether you need approval for the work-from-home period. Choose from the options: Approved, Denied, or Pending.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is where you can add any additional notes about your work-from-home request.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Submit the form only once you have received approval from your supervisor.
* Be honest and clear in your request and notes to ensure that your supervisor can process your request efficiently.
