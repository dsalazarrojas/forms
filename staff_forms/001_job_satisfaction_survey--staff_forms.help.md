# Job Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect information about your job satisfaction. It's meant to gather feedback from employees to help management understand their perspectives and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading this guide to understand the purpose and requirements of the survey.
2. Please answer all the questions truthfully, keeping in mind that this is an anonymous form.
3. Select the correct options for each question based on your honest opinion.
4. Use the provided hints to guide you in answering each question.
5. Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask for assistance if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Satisfaction Survey** (Job Satisfaction Survey, text, optional):
	+ This is the title of the form. Please leave this field as is, as it's not a question that requires an answer.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Rating** (Rating, number, optional):
	+ Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not satisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Satisfaction** (Satisfaction, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with your job. You can select either "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have about your job or the company.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Department** (Department, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Check the boxes for any departments that you have experience with.
* **Submit** (Submit, note, optional):
	+ Click this button to submit your survey once you've finished filling out the form.
* **Date Completed** (Date Completed, date, optional):
	+ This field will automatically be populated with the date you submit the form.
* **Time Completed** (Time Completed, time, optional):
	+ This field will automatically be populated with the time you submit the form.
