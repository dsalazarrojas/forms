# Employee Safety Violation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and track employee safety incidents. It helps to provide detailed information about incidents, their causes, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of the incident, its cause, and any actions taken or planned.
3. Select the correct status for the incident from the options provided.
4. Fill in the relevant fields for the reporting employee and reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for any general information related to the incident.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the ID number of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide a detailed description of the cause of the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe any actions taken or planned as a result of the incident.
* **Action Planned** (`action_planned`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe any actions planned as a result of the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the current status of the incident from the options: Active, Inactive, or Closed.
* **Incident Status Date** (`incident_status_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date the incident status was last updated.
* **Incident Status Time** (`incident_status_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time the incident status was last updated.
* **Incident Status Description** (`incident_status_description`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide a detailed description of the current status of the incident.
* **Reporting Employee Name** (`reporting_employee_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Reporting Employee ID** (`reporting_employee_id`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the ID number of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Reporting Employee Name 2** (`reporting_employee_name_2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the employee who reported the incident (if different from the reporting employee).
* **Reporting Employee Email** (`reporting_employee_email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter the email of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Reporting Employee Phone** (`reporting_employee_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Reviewing Employee Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the employee who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewing Employee ID** (`reviewer_id`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the ID number of the employee who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewer Employee Name** (`reviewer_employee_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the employee who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewing Employee ID** (`reviewer_employee_id`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the ID number of the employee who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewing Employee Email** (`reviewer_employee_email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter the email of the employee who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewing Employee Phone** (`reviewer_employee_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of the employee who reviewed the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Provide clear and concise information to help facilitate the review and resolution of the incident.
* Use the select_one field for incident status to indicate the current status of the incident.
* Double-check all the fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
