# Direct Deposit Update Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for updating information related to direct deposit for an individual or company. It's used to update details such as bank account information, deposit frequencies, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information before starting.
2. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
3. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Provide your Bank Name and Routing Number to update your bank account information.
5. Enter your Account Number and Account Type for accurate account details.
6. Enter your Address details, including City, State, and Zip code.
7. Update the Bank Address and Phone/Fax details if applicable.
8. Select the Direct Deposit Frequency and Ending dates according to your needs.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we use to communicate with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for any updates or queries.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of your bank or financial institution.
* **Bank Name (continued)**: Enter any additional details to specify the bank you use.
* **Routing Number**: Enter your bank's routing number for direct deposit purposes.
* **Account Number**: Enter your account number for direct deposit purposes.
* **Account Type**: Select the type of account you have (Checking, Savings, Money Market).
* **Bank Account Type**: Select the type of account you have (Checking, Savings, Money Market).
* **Bank Branch**: Enter your bank branch information, if applicable.
* **Bank ABA**: Enter your bank's ABA (Routing) number, if applicable.
* **Bank Routing**: Enter your bank's routing number, if applicable.
* **Bank Account**: Enter your bank account number, if applicable.
* **Direct Deposit Ending**: Enter the ending date for your direct deposit.
* **Direct Deposit Starting**: Enter the starting date for your direct deposit.
* **Direct Deposit Frequency**: Select the frequency of your direct deposit (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Semi-Monthly, Annually, Bi-Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your address line 1 information.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your address line 2 information.
* **City**: Enter your city name.
* **State**: Enter your state or province name.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code or postal code.
