# Occupational Health And Safety Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Occupational Health and Safety Questionnaire is a form used to gather information from employees about their workplace, job title, and supervisor, as well as their personal contact information and emergency services. This information helps the organization ensure a safe and healthy work environment for all employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your workplace address in the "Workplace address" field.
2. Enter your supervisor's name in the "Supervisor name" field.
3. Enter your own name in the "Employee name" field.
4. Enter your employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job title" field.
6. Enter your supervisor's work email address in the "Supervisor work email" field.
7. Enter your own work email address in the "Employee work email" field.
8. Select your supervisor's name from the "Supervisors" dropdown menu.
9. Select your preferred safety training option from the "Safety training" dropdown menu.
10. Enter any known hazards in the "Hazards" field.
11. Select any applicable safety controls from the "Controls" dropdown menu.
12. Enter any equipment information in the "Equipment" field.
13. Enter any observations in the "Observations" field.
14. Enter any prevention methods in the "Prevention" field.
15. Enter any mechanical control information in the "Mechanical control" field.
16. Enter any chemical control information in the "Chemical control" field.
17. Enter any physical control information in the "Physical control" field.
18. Enter any fire prevention information in the "Fire prevention" field.
19. Enter any safety equipment information in the "Safety equipment" field.
20. Enter any medical treatment information in the "Medical treatment" field.
21. Enter any medical equipment information in the "Medical equipment" field.
22. Enter any emergency services information in the "Emergency services" field.
23. Enter any emergency contact information in the "Emergency contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace address** (`Workplace address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the workplace where you work.
* **Supervisor name** (`Supervisor name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Employee name** (`Employee name`, `text`, required): Enter your own name.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, `text`, required): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Job title** (`Job title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or role in the organization.
* **Supervisor work email** (`Supervisor work email`, `email`, required): Enter your supervisor's work email address.
* **Employee work email** (`Employee work email`, `email`, required): Enter your own work email address.
* **Supervisors** (`Supervisors`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your supervisor's name from the dropdown menu.
* **Safety training** (`Safety training`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred safety training option from the dropdown menu.
* **Hazards** (`Hazards`, `text`, required): Enter any known hazards in your workplace.
* **Controls** (`Controls`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any applicable safety controls from the dropdown menu.
* **Equipment** (`Equipment`, `text`, required): Enter any relevant equipment information.
* **Observations** (`Observations`, `text`, required): Enter any observations or comments.
* **Prevention** (`Prevention`, `text`, required): Enter any prevention methods or protocols.
* **Mechanical control** (`Mechanical control`, `text`, required): Enter any mechanical control information.
* **Chemical control** (`Chemical control`, `text`, required): Enter any chemical control information.
* **Physical control** (`Physical control`, `text`, required): Enter any physical control information.
* **Fire prevention** (`Fire prevention`, `text`, required): Enter any fire prevention information.
* **Safety equipment** (`Safety equipment`, `text`, required): Enter any safety equipment information.
* **Medical treatment** (`Medical treatment`, `text`, required): Enter any medical treatment information.
* **Medical equipment** (`Medical equipment`, `text`, required): Enter any medical equipment information.
* **Emergency services** (`Emergency services`, `text`, required): Enter any emergency services information.
* **Emergency contact** (`Emergency contact`, `text`, required): Enter any emergency contact information.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is intended to be completed by all employees, including those with managerial roles.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please do not hesitate to contact your HR representative.
