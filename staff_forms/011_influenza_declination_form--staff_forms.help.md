# Influenza Declination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influenza Declination Form is a document used to declare one's declination or lack of symptoms for the influenzavirus. It is a crucial form for medical professionals to assess and monitor the risk of infection transmission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields marked as required (true) in the form.
2. Enter your name (person_name) to identify yourself.
3. Enter your staff ID (staff_id) to authenticate your identity.
4. Provide a reason for declination (reason_for_declination) if applicable.
5. Record the date (date_declination) and time (time_declination) of declination if necessary.
6. (Optional) Enter the name of the hospital you are affiliated with (hospital_name).
7. (Optional) Provide information about your healthcare provider (healthcare_provider).
8. (Optional) Share any relevant medical history (medical_history).
9. Submit the completed form to the relevant medical authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **person_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
* **staff_id**: Enter your unique staff ID number.
* **reason_for_declination**: Provide the reason for your declination, e.g., lack of symptoms, vaccination, etc.
* **date_declination**: Enter the date you first declinated or last felt well.
* **time_declination**: Enter the time of day when you first declinated or last felt well.
* **hospital_name**: Enter the name of your affiliated hospital, if applicable.
* **healthcare_provider**: Enter the name and ID of your healthcare provider, if applicable.
* **medical_history**: Provide any relevant medical history, e.g., previous illnesses, allergies, etc.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If unsure about any field, please consult with a healthcare professional.
* This form is meant for internal medical staff use only.
