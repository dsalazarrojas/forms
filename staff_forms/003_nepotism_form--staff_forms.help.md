# Nepotism Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nepotism Form is used to collect sensitive information about potential cases of nepotism in the workplace. This form helps to ensure that all cases are thoroughly investigated and addressed in a confidential and unbiased manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the First Name of the individual involved in the potential case of nepotism.
2. Enter the Last Name of the individual involved.
3. Enter the Email Address of the individual involved.
4. Enter the Phone Number of the individual involved, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the individual involved in the potential case of nepotism.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the individual involved.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the individual involved. This will help in case further communication is needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the individual involved, if available. This will help in case further communication is needed.
