# Employee Laptop Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Laptop Agreement Form is used to document the agreement between the employee and the company regarding the use and maintenance of the company-provided laptop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Enter the date the laptop was issued in the `date_of_issue` field.
3. Enter the date you're agreeing to the terms in the `agreement_date` field.
4. Enter your employee ID in the `employee_id` field (if required).
5. Enter the model of the laptop you're using in the `laptop_model` field (if required).
6. Enter the make of the laptop you're using in the `laptop_make` field (if required).
7. Enter the year of the laptop (if required).
8. Enter the serial number of the laptop in the `laptop_serial_number` field (required).
9. Enter your responsibility statement in the `responsibility_statement` field (required).
10. Sign the form with your signature in the `employee_signature` field (required).
11. Have your manager sign the form in the `manager_signature` field (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_laptop_agreement_form**: Enter your name to confirm that this is your laptop agreement form.
* **employee_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **date_of_issue**: Enter the date the laptop was issued to you.
* **issue_date**: This date is not required for you to fill in, as it's likely a system-generated date.
* **employee_id**: Enter your employee ID if required by your HR department.
* **laptop_model**: Enter the model of your laptop, e.g. "Lenovo ThinkPad".
* **laptop_make**: Enter the make of your laptop, e.g. "Lenovo".
* **laptop_year**: Enter the year of your laptop, e.g. 2022.
* **laptop_serial_number**: Enter the serial number of your laptop for tracking purposes.
* **agreement_terms**: This field is not used in this form, but it's likely for additional terms and conditions.
* **responsibility_statement**: Enter a statement confirming you understand and will follow the company's laptop usage policies.
* **employee_signature**: Sign the form to confirm you've read and agree to the terms.
* **manager_signature**: Sign the form to confirm you're an approver or supervisor.
* **agreement_date**: Enter the date you're agreeing to the terms.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" are not required to be filled in, but it's good practice to fill them in for tracking purposes.
