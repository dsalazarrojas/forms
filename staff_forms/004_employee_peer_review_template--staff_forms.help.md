# Employee Peer Review Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Peer Review Template is a form used to evaluate an employee's performance and provide feedback for growth and development. It's essential to use this template to help employees understand their strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the employee's performance and provide an overall rating.
2. Offer feedback on the employee's strengths.
3. Identify areas for improvement and specific behavioral issues.
4. Choose development areas that the employee needs to focus on.
5. Enter the date of the last review and the date of the next review.
6. Sign and date the form as the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, `number`, required): Rate the employee's overall performance, on a scale of 1-5.
- **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required): Provide specific, constructive feedback on the employee's performance.
- **Strengths** (`Strengths`, `text`, required): List the employee's strengths and areas of excellence.
- **Areas For Improvement** (`Areas For Improvement`, `text`, required): Identify specific areas where the employee needs to improve.
- **Development Areas** (`Development Areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the areas where the employee needs to focus their development efforts (e.g., Communication, Teamwork, Leadership).
- **Specific Behavioral Issues** (`Specific Behavioral Issues`, `text`, required): Specify any specific behavioral issues that need to be addressed.
- **Date Of Last Review** (`Date Of Last Review`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the last review, if applicable.
- **Date Of Next Review** (`Date Of Next Review`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the next review.
- **Peer Reviewer Name** (`Peer Reviewer Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the reviewer.
- **Reviewer Signature** (`Reviewer Signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date the form as the reviewer.

## Tips
- Be specific and objective when providing feedback and rating.
- Focus on behavioral issues and performance rather than personality traits.
- Keep the form concise and easy to read.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and consistency.
