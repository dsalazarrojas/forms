# Shift Changeover Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shift Changeover Form is a document used to record and report changes to shifts in the workplace. It is used to update staff schedules, manage shift swaps, and ensure accurate attendance and hours reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the shift changeover in the "Shift Changeover Form" field.
2. If the shift changeover involves a change in category, select the relevant category from the list of options.
3. Provide a description of the shift changeover, if necessary.
4. Enter the date of the shift changeover.
5. Enter the time of the shift changeover.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shift Changeover Form (form_title)** (`text`, **required**): Enter a brief title for the shift changeover.
* **Category (form_category)** (`select_multiple`, **required**): Choose a category related to the shift swap.
* **Description (form_description)** (`text`, **optional**): Provide a detailed description of the shift changeover if needed.
* **Date (form_date)** (`date`, **optional**): Enter the date of the shift changeover.
* **Time (form_time)** (`time`, **optional**): Enter the time of the shift changeover.

## Tips

* This form is used for internal reporting and tracking purposes only.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you need to report a shift swap that spans multiple dates or times, use multiple instances of this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
