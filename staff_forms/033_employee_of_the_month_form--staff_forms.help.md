# Employee Of The Month Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to formally recognize and celebrate employees within the organization for their exceptional work and contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee" field.
2. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager" field.
3. Select the start and end dates of the recognition period using the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
4. Provide a reason for the recognition in the "Reason for Recognition" field.
5. Choose any applicable awards or rewards from the "Recognition Award" field.
6. Enter the date of the recognition event in the "Recognition Date" field.
7. Specify the time of the recognition event in the "Recognition Time" field.
8. Enter the amount of the recognition reward in the "Recognition Amount" field.
9. Add any additional comments about the recognition event in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee_of_the_month_form_employee`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being recognized.
* **Manager** (`employee_of_the_month_form_manager`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Start Date** (`employee_of_the_month_form_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the recognition period.
* **End Date** (`employee_of_the_month_form_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the recognition period.
* **Reason for Recognition** (`employee_of_the_month_form_reason_for_recognition`, text, required): Provide a reason for recognizing the employee's work and contributions.
* **Recognition Award** (`employee_of_the_month_form_recognition_award`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more awards or rewards given to the employee.
* **Recognition Date** (`employee_of_the_month_form_recognition_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the recognition event.
* **Recognition Time** (`employee_of_the_month_form_recognition_time`, time, required): Specify the time of the recognition event.
* **Recognition Amount** (`employee_of_the_month_form_recognition_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the recognition reward.
* **Comments** (`employee_of_the_month_form_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments about the recognition event.
