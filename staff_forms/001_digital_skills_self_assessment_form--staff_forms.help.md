# digital_skills_self_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This self-assessment form is designed to help users evaluate their digital and soft skills in a professional setting. It will help you identify your areas of strength and areas for improvement in digital skills, communication, time management, problem-solving, adaptability, leadership, collaboration, and technical skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply select the relevant options for each question, and provide any additional comments you have about your skills. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' for questions that ask about specific skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance (id: 2)** (`performance`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the performance-related skills that you are proficient in. This includes any skills or experiences that demonstrate your ability to meet or exceed expectations.
* **Communication Skills (id: 3)** (`communication_skills`, `select_one`, required: false): This question assesses your ability to effectively communicate with others. Select 'Yes' if you feel you have strong communication skills.
* **Time Management (id: 4)** (`time_management`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your ability to manage your time effectively. Select all the time management skills that you are proficient in.
* **Problem Solving (id: 5)** (`problem_solving`, `select_one`, required: false): This question evaluates your ability to analyze problems and find effective solutions. Select 'Yes' if you feel you have strong problem-solving skills.
* **Adaptability (id: 6)** (`adaptability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question assesses your ability to adapt to changing situations or environments. Select all the adaptability skills that you are proficient in.
* **Leadership (id: 7)** (`leadership`, `select_one`, required: false): This question evaluates your ability to lead or motivate others. Select 'Yes' if you feel you have strong leadership skills.
* **Collaboration (id: 8)** (`collaboration`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your ability to work with others towards a common goal. Select all the collaboration skills that you are proficient in.
* **Digital Skills (id: 9)** (`digital_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question assesses your proficiency in various digital skills, such as software proficiency, data analysis, or programming languages. Select all the digital skills that you are proficient in.
* **Digital Skills Self-Assessment Form Comments (id: 10)** (`digital_skills_self_assessment_form_comments`, `note`, required: false): Use this field to provide additional comments or feedback about your digital skills or any other aspects of your skills that are not covered in the previous questions.
* **digital_skills_self_assessment_form (id: 1)** (`digital_skills_self_assessment_form`, `note`, required: false): This is a placeholder page. You can use this field to provide general comments or feedback about the form.

## Tips
- Be honest with yourself when completing this form. Select only the skills that you are truly proficient in.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it, as there is no save function.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can always provide additional comments or feedback in the "Digital Skills Self-Assessment Form Comments" field.
