# Employee Improvement Suggestion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Improvement Suggestion Form is designed to allow employees to submit suggestions for improvements to the workplace, work processes, or policies. This form is used to gather feedback and ideas from employees to help the organization improve its operations and provide a better work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Employee Improvement Suggestion Form" link to access the form.
2.  Select the category that best describes the type of improvement you are suggesting.
3.  Choose the subcategory related to your suggestion.
4.  Enter your employee ID (if you are an employee).
5.  Provide a clear and concise text explaining your suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Improvement Suggestion**: This field is where you should enter your improvement suggestion.
    It is a text field where you can enter any ideas or suggestions you have for improving the workplace.
*   **Suggestion Category**: This field allows you to select the category of your suggestion.
    Please choose the one that best describes the type of improvement you are suggesting.
*   **Suggestion Subcategory**: This field allows you to select the subcategory of your suggestion.
    This helps to further specify the type of improvement you are suggesting.
*   **Employee ID**: This field is for employees to enter their employee ID.
    If you are an employee, please enter your employee ID if required.
*   **Suggestion Text**: This field is where you should provide the details of your suggestion.
    Please enter a clear and concise text explaining your improvement suggestion.
