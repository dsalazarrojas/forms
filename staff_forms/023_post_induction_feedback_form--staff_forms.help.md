## post_induction_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from new employees about their induction experience, helping the organization to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name
2.  Enter your last name
3.  Select your role (Trainee, Supervisor, or Trainer)
4.  Enter your email address
5.  Enter your phone number
6.  Select the date and time of your induction
7.  Choose how you rate your induction experience (Excellent, Satisfactory, or Unsatisfactory)
8.  Add any additional comments or feedback
9.  Select who submitted the form (Trainee, Supervisor, or Trainer)
10.  Select the date and time you submitted the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your role within the organization.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Date of Induction** (`date_of_induction`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of your induction.
- **Time of Induction** (`time_of_induction`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time of your induction.
- **How was your induction experience?** (`feedback`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate your induction experience as Excellent, Satisfactory, or Unsatisfactory.
- **Comments** (`comment`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select who submitted the form (Trainee, Supervisor, or Trainer).
- **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date and time you submitted the form.
- **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of who submitted the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and reflects your genuine experience.
* If you are a supervisor or trainer, please only complete this form if you are providing feedback on an individual's induction experience.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to the HR department for assistance.
