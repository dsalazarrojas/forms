# Employee Separation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Separation Form is used to record an employee's separation from the company, providing essential information about their reason for separation, date of separation, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely, paying special attention to required fields.
2.  Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
3.  Enter the employee's ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
4.  Provide the reason for the employee's separation in the "Reason for Separation" field.
5.  Enter the date of the employee's separation in the "Separation Date" field.
6.  Indicate whether the employee will be leaving the company with a "Yes" or "No" answer in the "Department" field.
7.  Enter the name of the employee's supervisor in the "Supervisor Name" field.
8.  If applicable, provide the reason for the employee's separation in the "Termination Reason" field.
9.  Enter the date the employee will last work on in the "Last Working Day" field.
10. Enter the employee's final salary in the "Final Salary" field.
11. If applicable, indicate if the employee will be on a notice period with a "Yes" or "No" answer in the "Notice Period" field.
12. If the employee separated due to previous job offers, provide a reason for separation in the "Reason for Separation Had Previous Job Offers" field.
13. If the employee did not join another company, provide a reason for separation in the "Reason for Separation Did Not Join Another Company" field.
14. If other reasons for separation apply, enter them in the "Reason for Separation Other" field.
15. If applicable, provide any comments in the "Comments" field.
16. Enter a unique ID for the form in the "Employee Separation Form ID" field.
17. Indicate whether the form is assigned to a specific individual with a "Yes" or "No" answer in the "Assigned To" field.
18. Indicate whether the form was assigned by a specific individual with a "Yes" or "No" answer in the "Assigned By" field.
19. Enter the date the form was assigned in the "Date Assigned" field.
20. If applicable, provide the time the form was reported in the "Time Reported" field.
21. Indicate whether the form was reported to a specific individual with a "Yes" or "No" answer in the "Reported To" field.
22. If applicable, provide the time the form was reported by in the "Time Reported By" field.
23. Indicate the status of the form with a selection in the "Form Status" field.

* **Employee Name (Employee Name)**: Enter the name of the employee separating from the company.
* **Employee ID (Employee ID)**: Enter the employee's ID number for accurate tracking.
* **Reason for Separation (Reason for Separation)**: Provide the reason for the employee's separation from the company.

* **Department (Department)**: Indicate whether the employee will be leaving the company.
* **Supervisor Name (Supervisor Name)**: Enter the name of the employee's supervisor.
* **Termination Reason (Termination Reason)**: If applicable, provide the reason for the employee's termination.
* **Last Working Day (Last Working Day)**: Enter the date the employee will last work on.
* **Final Salary (Final Salary)**: Enter the employee's final salary amount.
* **Notice Period (Notice Period)**: If applicable, indicate whether the employee will be on a notice period.
* **Reason for Separation Had Previous Job Offers (Reason for Separation Had Previous Job Offers)**: If the employee separated due to previous job offers, provide the reason for separation.
* **Reason for Separation Did Not Join Another Company (Reason for Separation Did Not Join Another Company)**: If the employee did not join another company, provide the reason for separation.
* **Reason for Separation Other (Reason for Separation Other)**: If other reasons for separation apply, enter them here.
* **Comments (Comments)**: If applicable, provide any additional comments about the employee's separation.
* **Employee Separation Form ID (Employee Separation Form ID)**: Enter a unique ID for the form to track and reference.
* **Assigned To (Assigned To)**: Indicate whether the form is assigned to a specific individual.
* **Assigned By (Assigned By)**: Indicate whether the form was assigned by a specific individual.
* **Date Assigned (Date Assigned)**: Enter the date the form was assigned.
* **Time Reported (Time Reported)**: If applicable, provide the time the form was reported.
* **Reported To (Reported To)**: Indicate whether the form was reported to a specific individual.
* **Time Reported By (Time Reported By)**: If applicable, provide the time the form was reported by.
