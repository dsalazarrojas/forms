# new_user_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The new user request form is used to capture information from new users about their account request. This form helps our team understand the purpose and requirements of the new account, making it easier to process the request and assign the correct tools and permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to help us verify your identity.
3. Provide your Phone number for us to contact you if needed.
4. Select your Department to help us understand your role within the organization.
5. Choose your Job Title to help us understand your position within the department.
6. Enter the Reason for Requesting Account to help us understand your requirements.
7. Select the Assigned Tool to help us understand what you need access to.
8. Enter the Form ID to help us track your request.
9. If you are repeating a previous request, please fill in the Repeat First Name, Last Name, Email, Department, Job Title, Request Reason, Assigned Tool, and Form ID fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address to help us verify your identity.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select your department from the list to help us understand your role.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Choose your job title from the list to help us understand your position.
* **Reason for Requesting Account** (`request_reason`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the purpose of your account request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Select the tool you need access to.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of this form to help us track your request.
* **Repeat First Name** (`repeat_first_name`, text, optional): If you are repeating a previous request, enter the first name of the original user.
* **Repeat Last Name** (`repeat_last_name`, text, optional): If you are repeating a previous request, enter the last name of the original user.
* **Repeat Email** (`repeat_email`, email, optional): If you are repeating a previous request, enter the email address of the original user.
* **Repeat Phone** (`repeat_phone`, text, optional): If you are repeating a previous request, enter the phone number of the original user.
* **Repeat Department** (`repeat_department`, text, optional): If you are repeating a previous request, select the department of the original user.
* **Repeat Job Title** (`repeat_job_title`, text, optional): If you are repeating a previous request, choose the job title of the original user.
* **Repeat Request Reason** (`repeat_request_reason`, text, optional): If you are repeating a previous request, enter a brief description of the original user's request reason.
* **Repeat Assigned Tool** (`repeat_assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you are repeating a previous request, select the tool originally assigned to the user.
* **Repeat Form ID** (`repeat_form_id`, text, optional): If you are repeating a previous request, enter the original Form ID.
* **Repeat Department 2** (`repeat_department_2`, text, optional): Not applicable.
* **Repeat Job Title 2** (`repeat_job_title_2`, text, optional): Not applicable.
* **Repeat Request Reason 2** (`repeat_request_reason_2`, text, optional): Not applicable.
* **Repeat Assigned Tool 2** (`repeat_assigned_tool_2`, text, optional): Not applicable.
* **Repeat Form Id 2** (`repeat_form_id_2`, text, optional): Not applicable.

## Tips
Please ensure that all mandatory fields are filled in before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
