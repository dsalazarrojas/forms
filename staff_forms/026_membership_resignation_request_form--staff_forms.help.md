# membership_resignation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The membership resignation request form is a tool for staff members to submit their resignation from the club. This form helps to ensure a smooth transition of information and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Member Number** as they appear on your membership records.
2. Next, provide a clear explanation for **Reason For Resigning**. If your reason is not listed, please select **Other (if other, please specify)** and provide a brief description.
3. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** so we can contact you with any further instructions or information.
4. Choose a suitable **Date Of Resignation** and **Time Of Resignation** for your resignation to take effect.
5. Confirm your **Confirmation Of Resignation** by selecting **I am providing my consent to resign from the club** or **I am not providing my consent to resign from the club**.
6. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the **Notes** field.
7. Finally, confirm your resignation with **Confirmation Of Resignation Other** by selecting **Yes** or **No**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your membership records.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your membership records.
* **Reason For Resigning**: Provide a clear explanation for your resignation. If not listed, please select **Other (if other, please specify)** and provide a brief description.
* **Member Number**: Your member ID number as it appears on your membership records.
* **Email**: Your contact email address for further instructions or information.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number for further instructions or information.
* **Date Of Resignation**: Choose the date you wish to resign from the club.
* **Time Of Resignation**: Choose the time you wish to resign from the club.
* **Confirmation Of Resignation**: Confirm your intention to resign from the club.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information about your resignation.
* **Confirmation Of Resignation Other**: Confirm your intention to resign from the club (if you have already confirmed it).
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the staff member or department that will be handling your resignation details.
* **Date Assigned**: Enter the date your resignation was assigned to the staff member or department.
* **Time Assigned**: Enter the time your resignation was assigned to the staff member or department.

Tips: Please review your entries carefully before submitting the form to ensure all information is accurate and complete. If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to contact your assigned staff member or department.
