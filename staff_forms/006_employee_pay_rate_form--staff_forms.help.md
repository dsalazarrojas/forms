# Employee Pay Rate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Pay Rate Form is used to collect and track employee pay rate information, including salary, pay date, currency, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's salary in the "employee_salary" field. This field is optional.
2. Select the pay date from the calendar.
3. Choose the currency for the pay rate from the dropdown list.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about the pay rate in the "pay_rate_notes" field (optional).
5. Select the person who submitted the form from the dropdown list:
	* Manager
	* Director
	* HR
6. Enter the submission date (not required).
7. Select the person who reviewed and approved the submission:
	* Manager
	* Director
	* HR
8. Choose the review status from the dropdown list:
	* In Progress
	* Approved
	* Rejected

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_salary**: Enter the employee's salary. This field is optional.
* **pay_date**: Select the date the pay rate applies.
* **pay_rate**: Enter the employee's pay rate.
* **currency**: Choose the currency for the pay rate from the options:
	+ USD
	+ EUR
	+ JPY
* **pay_rate_notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the pay rate (optional).
* **submitted_by**: Select the person who submitted the form from the dropdown list:
	* Manager
	* Director
	* HR
* **submission_date**: Enter the date the form was submitted (not required).
* **submitter**: Select the person who submitted the form from the dropdown list:
	* Manager
	* Director
	* HR
* **review_status**: Choose the review status from the dropdown list:
	+ In Progress
	+ Approved
	+ Rejected

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
