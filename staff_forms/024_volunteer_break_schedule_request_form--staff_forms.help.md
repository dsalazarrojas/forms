# Volunteer Break Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Break Schedule Request Form is used to request a break schedule for a specific shift. Please fill out this form with the requested information to help ensure that you are aware of your scheduled breaks and can plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Volunteer Shift Details" page with your requested shift start and end time.
2. Enter your name in the "Volunteer Name" field.
3. Select your requested break duration and frequency in the "Break Frequency" and "Break Frequency Other" fields, respectively. If you select "Break Frequency Other", please also provide the requested break duration in the "Break Frequency Other" field.
4. If you need to, select which volunteers will cover your shift while you're on a break in the "Shift Coverage" field.
5. Provide any additional coverage details in the "Coverage Details" field.
6. Review your submission and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of your volunteer shift. Please use the format HH:MM or MM:DD:SS, for example, 08:00 or 14:45.
* **Volunteer Shift End Time**: Enter the end time of your volunteer shift. Please use the format HH:MM or MM:DD:SS, for example, 10:00 or 16:00.
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your volunteer ID card or in our system.
* **Break Duration**: Select the length of your requested break. Options are 1, 5, 10, or 15 minutes.
* **Break Frequency**: Select how often you'd like to take breaks. Options are 1, 5, 10, or 15 minutes.
* **Break Frequency Other**: If you select "1", "5", "10", or "15" is not enough, enter a different break frequency in this field. For example, if you need a 20-minute break, enter "20".
* **Shift Coverage**: Select which volunteers will cover your shift while you're on a break. Select all applicable volunteers, for example, Volunteer 1, Volunteer 2, and Volunteer 3.
* **Coverage Details**: Provide any additional information about coverage for your shift, such as specific tasks or responsibilities you'd like covered while you're on a break.
* **Submit**: Review your submission and click the "Submit" button to submit your break schedule request.
