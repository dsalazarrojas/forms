# Staff Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Skills Survey is a tool used to assess the skills and interests of staff members and provide feedback and comments from managers. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form and what each field means.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as honestly as possible and provide your feedback and comments in the text fields. Make sure to submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Survey**: This is an introductory note that explains the purpose and instructions of the survey.
* **What's Your Interest?**: This question asks you to select which tasks you are interested in or have experience in. Please select the options that apply to you.
* **What's Your Experience?**: This question asks you to select which tasks you have experience in. Please select the options that apply to you.
* **How Confident Are You?**: This question asks you to select how confident you feel about your skills and experience. Please select the option that best describes your confidence level.
* **Skills**: This question asks you to select the skills you have or are interested in developing. Please select all that apply.
* **Taking On Additional Tasks**: This question asks you to select if you are willing to take on additional tasks. Please select the option that applies to you.
* **Do You Want Skills Training?**: This question asks you to select if you would like to receive skills training. Please select the option that applies to you.
* **Next Steps**: This question asks you to select the next steps you would like to take to develop your skills. Please select all that apply.
* **Feedback**: This is a text field for you to provide feedback and comments about your experience and skills.
* **Additional Comments**: This is a text field for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Manager Feedback**: This is a text field for your manager to provide feedback and comments about your performance and skills.
* **Manager Signature**: This is a text field for your manager to sign and date their comments.
* **Manager Name**: This is a text field for your manager to enter their name.
* **Manager Title**: This is a text field for your manager to enter their title.
* **Manager Department**: This is a text field for your manager to enter their department.
* **Manager Email**: This is a text field for your manager to enter their email.
* **Manager Phone**: This is a text field for your manager to enter their phone number.
* **Manager Comments**: This is a text field for your manager to enter any comments or suggestions.

Note: Please make sure to review and submit the form when you are finished.
