# Employee Equipment Issue Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report issues with company assets, such as broken or stolen equipment. It helps the management and maintenance teams keep track of problems and ensure they are resolved efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible about the issue.
2. Make sure to select the correct department and asset type to help the relevant teams address the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required: false): Describe the issue with the asset, including any relevant details about what happened or when it occurred.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person reporting the issue.
* **Reported Date** (`reported_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the issue was reported.
* **Reportee Name** (`reportee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person affected by the issue.
* **Asset Name** (`asset_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name or model of the asset that's experiencing the issue.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false): Select the department that manages the asset, which can be Engineering, IT, Finance, Marketing, or HR.
* **Asset Type** (`asset_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of asset that's experiencing the issue, which can be a Computer, Monitor, Printer, Keyboard, or Mouse.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of issue that the asset is experiencing, which can be Broken, Damaged, or Stolen.
* **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the issue, such as what happened or how to reproduce it.
* **Reportee Phone** (`reportee_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person affected by the issue.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person affected by the issue.
* **Reported By (Position)** (`reported_by_title`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose who reported the issue from Manager or Employee.
 
## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the issue is addressed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant department or asset owner for assistance.
