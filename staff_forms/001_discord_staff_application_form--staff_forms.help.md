# discord_staff_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The discord_staff_application_form is designed to collect information from staff members who are applying for Discord server staff positions. This form will be reviewed by staff administrators to assess the applicant's qualifications and suitability for a role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **first_name** field with your first name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
2. Enter your **email** in the format of your email address (e.g., `username@example.com`).
3. In the **discord_tag** field, provide any relevant Discord tags or roles you have on the server (e.g., `@username`).
4. Explain why you are interested in becoming a staff member in the **why_staff** field. This is an opportunity to showcase your motivation and enthusiasm for the role.
5. Describe your relevant experience in the **experience** field. This can include any past experiences or roles you've held that demonstrate your qualifications for the position.
6. If applicable, indicate your availability in the **availability** field by selecting all the days and times you are available to work.
7. In the **timezone** field, enter your local time zone (e.g., EST, PST, GMT).
8. The **timezone_offset**, **timezone_offset_hrs**, and **timezone_offset_mins** fields are related to your time zone's offset from UTC. If you're not familiar with this concept, you can leave these fields blank, and staff administrators will calculate it for you. However, if you have a specific offset (e.g., -5 or +2), enter it here to ensure accuracy.
9. Once you have filled out all the required fields, review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
* **discord_tag** (Text, Optional): Provide any relevant Discord tags or roles you have on the server.
* **why_staff** (Text, Optional): Explain why you are interested in becoming a staff member.
* **experience** (Text, Optional): Describe your relevant experience that demonstrates your qualifications for the role.
* **availability** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate your availability by selecting all the relevant days and times.
* **timezone** (Text, Optional): Enter your local time zone (e.g., EST, PST, GMT).
* **timezone_offset** (Number, Optional): Enter your time zone's offset from UTC if you know it.
* **timezone_offset_hrs** (Number, Optional): Enter the hour offset from UTC if you know it.
* **timezone_offset_mins** (Number, Optional): Enter the minute offset from UTC if you know it.

## Tips
* Double-check your email address for accuracy to ensure it can be reached by staff administrators.
* Be concise and clear in your responses to the **why_staff** and **experience** fields.
* If you're unsure about your time zone or offset, you can consult online resources or your operating system's settings to find the correct information.
