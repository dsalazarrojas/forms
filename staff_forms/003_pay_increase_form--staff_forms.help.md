# Pay Increase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pay Increase Form is used by staff members to request a pay increase for themselves or their colleagues. It gathers essential information to review and process the request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2.  Start on the first page, "Pay_Increase_Request". Enter your pay increase request in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pay_Increase_Request** (`form_1`): Enter a clear and concise description of why you are requesting a pay increase. This should be a detailed explanation of your reasons for requesting a pay increase.
*   **Type_Of_Increase** (`type_of_increase`): Select the type of increase you are requesting. Options include:
    *   Increase: This is a straightforward pay increase request.
    *   Revert: This is a request to revert to a previous salary amount.
    *   Both: This is a combined request for both an increase and a revert.
*   **Amount_Of_Increase** (`amount_of_increase`): Enter the amount you are requesting for your pay increase. Please note that this should be a numerical value.
*   **Effective_Date** (`effective_date`): Select the date when the pay increase should take effect. Please choose a valid date.
*   **Justification_Letter** (`justification_letter`): Attach a brief justification letter explaining your reasons for requesting a pay increase. Please keep this concise.
*   **Approval_Status** (`approval_status`): Select the status of your request. Options include:
    *   Approved: Your request has been approved.
    *   Denied: Your request has been denied.
    *   Pending: Your request is still pending.
*   **Signature_Of_Decision_Maker** (`signature_of_decision_maker`): Attach the signature of the decision maker who reviewed and approved or denied your request.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when explaining your reasons for requesting a pay increase in the "Pay_Increase_Request" field.
*   Ensure that you select the correct type of increase in the "Type_Of_Increase" field.
*   Double-check that you enter the correct amount in the "Amount_Of_Increase" field.
*   Enter a valid date in the "Effective_Date" field.
*   Keep the justification letter concise in the "Justification_Letter" field.
*   Select the correct status of your request in the "Approval_Status" field.
*   Ensure that the decision maker signs and dates the "Signature_Of_Decision_Maker" field.
*   Submit only one request per pay increase.
