# Audio Coordinator Staff Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the purpose and requirements of the Audio Coordinator Staff Contract form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the required information.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address for work-related communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for work-related calls and messages.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your full address for correspondence and mail delivery.
* **Position Title**: Enter your official job title as approved by your supervisor or HR department.
* **Department**: Select the department or division where you'll be working. The options are:
	+ Audio Engineering
	+ Live Sound
	+ Studio Production
	+ Post-Production
	+ Broadcast
	+ Events
* **Employment Type**: Choose the type of employment you have with the company. The options are:
	+ Full-Time
	+ Part-Time
	+ Contract
	+ Freelance
	+ Temporary
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you started your employment with the company.
* **Contract End Date**: If you have a contract end date, enter it. If not, leave it blank.
* **Hourly Rate**: Enter your agreed-upon hourly compensation in USD.
* **Overtime Eligible**: Select whether this position is eligible for overtime. The options are:
	+ Yes, eligible for overtime
	+ No, salaried position
* **Work Schedule**: Select your expected work schedule. The options are:
	+ Standard business hours
	+ Evening shifts
	+ Weekend events
	+ Variable schedule
	+ On-call as needed
* **Primary Responsibilities**: Select the primary duties you'll be performing as an Audio Coordinator. The options are:
	+ Audio system setup
	+ Live sound mixing
	+ Equipment maintenance
	+ Staff supervision
	+ Client consultation
	+ Inventory management
	+ Technical troubleshooting
* **Equipment Provided**: Select whether the company will provide equipment for your work. The options are:
	+ Yes, all equipment provided
	+ Partial equipment provided
	+ No, use personal equipment
* **Travel Required**: Select whether travel is required for your role. The options are:
	+ Yes, frequent travel
	+ Occasional travel
	+ Local travel only
	+ No travel required
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: Agree to maintain confidentiality of client information. The options are:
	+ Yes, I agree
	+ No, I do not agree
* **Non-Compete Agreement**: Agree to non-compete terms during employment. The options are:
	+ Yes, I agree
	+ No, I do not agree
* **Benefits Enrollment**: Select your benefits enrollment status. The options are:
	+ Yes, enrolling in all benefits
	+ Partial benefits enrollment
	+ Declining benefits
	+ Not eligible
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Employee Signature**: Type your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you signed this contract.

## Tips

* Double-check all required fields to avoid delays in processing the contract.
* Ensure you've filled out all fields accurately to avoid errors.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure you've entered correct information.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
* This form is a critical part of the onboarding process. Completing it accurately and correctly will ensure a smooth onboarding experience.
