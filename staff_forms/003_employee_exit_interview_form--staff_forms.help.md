# Employee Exit Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees who are leaving the company. The purpose of this form is to collect details about the employee's reason for leaving, job title, department, and other relevant information. This data will be used to improve the company's understanding of its current and future workforce, helping to identify areas of improvement and trends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information. Make sure to provide your current contact information and the reason for your leaving. If you are leaving the company, you will be asked to provide the date you left and your job start date. You will also be asked to provide the name and email of your new manager. Please fill in all the fields as accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, false): This field is for any additional information you wish to share about your leaving the company.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, false): Please provide your current address, phone number, or any other contact details so that we can reach you after you leave the company.

### Company Information
* **Company Information** (`company_info`, text, false): If you have any specific issues or concerns about your departure from the company, please use this field to communicate.

### Employment Information
* **Employment Information** (`employment_info`, text, false): This field is for any information you wish to share about your employment history with the company.

### Reason for Leaving
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_leaving`, text, false): Please provide a clear and specific reason for your leaving the company. This will help us understand what we can do better in the future.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, false): Please provide your current job title.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, false): Provide the name of the department you were working in.

### Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, false): Please provide the name of your current manager.

### Date Left
* **Date Left** (`date_left`, date, false): Please enter the date you left the company.

### Job Start Date
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, false): Enter the date you started working at the company.

### New Manager
* **New Manager** (`manager_name_new`, text, false): If you are leaving the company, please provide the name of your new manager.

### New Manager E-mail
* **New Manager E-mail** (`new_manager_email`, text, false): If you are leaving the company, please provide the email of your new manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to help us understand your reasons for leaving.
* Please answer all fields truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* You can reach us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
