# Shop Assistant Opening Routine Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shop assistant opening routine checklist is designed to ensure that all necessary preparations are completed before assisting customers, maintaining a secure and smooth shopping environment, and providing excellent customer service.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and ensure you have a good understanding of what is being asked.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. For fields with options, select the most appropriate option that reflects your situation.
4. Fill in all required fields.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all necessary sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Opening Preparation**
	+ Label: `shop_assistant_opening_routine_checklist_1`
	+ Description: Describe the steps taken to prepare for the opening shift. This is where you document your preparation routine, such as reviewing procedures, reviewing schedules, and ensuring you have all necessary supplies and equipment.
* **Shop Equipment**
	+ Label: `shop_assistant_opening_routine_checklist_2`
	+ Description: Select `Yes` if all necessary equipment is in good working order. This includes items like cash registers, credit card machines, and other essential tools.
* **Shop Opening Procedures**
	+ Label: `shop_assistant_opening_routine_checklist_3`
	+ Description: Describe the procedures you follow for opening the shop, such as turning on lights, adjusting temperature, and preparing the display area.
* **Shop Opening Preparation Checklist**
	+ Label: `shop_assistant_opening_routine_checklist_4`
	+ Description: List the specific tasks you need to complete before opening the shop, like counting inventory and checking the stockroom.
* **Shop Opening**
	+ Label: `shop_assistant_opening_routine_checklist_5`
	+ Description: Enter the exact time you start your opening shift, ensuring you arrive at the scheduled time.
* **Shop Security**
	+ Label: `shop_assistant_opening_routine_checklist_6`
	+ Description: Describe the measures you take to ensure the shop is secure, such as locking doors, turning on alarms, and checking for potential hazards.
* **Shop Cash and Credit**
	+ Label: `shop_assistant_opening_routine_checklist_7`
	+ Description: Select `Yes` for cash and credit options available, and `No` if not. This ensures accurate reporting and accounting.
* **Shop Opening Routine Checklist**
	+ Label: `shop_assistant_opening_routine_checklist_8`
	+ Description: Document any specific tasks or procedures you need to complete at the beginning of your shift, like checking inventory, reviewing schedules, or preparing displays.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible in your answers, ensuring accurate documentation of your opening routine.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or manager.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, it's always best to double-check your shop's policies and procedures.
* This form is a helpful tool for maintaining a smooth and secure shopping environment; use it wisely!
