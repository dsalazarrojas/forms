# Employee Exit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Exit Form is a document used to collect information about an employee's departure from the company. This form is intended to be filled out by the employee when they are leaving the organization and helps to document their last day of work and the reason for their departure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the employee information page (`employee_exit_form_1`).
2. Select the date of your last day of work (`date_of_last_day`).
3. Choose the reason for your exit (`employee_exit_reason`).
4. Provide a brief explanation for your reason for leaving (`reason_for_exit`).
5. Select your employee type (`employee_type`), which is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information**: This page is where you can enter your basic information such as name and ID. Please ensure that all the required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* **Date of Last Day**: This field is used to select the date of your last working day at the company. Please use the calendar picker to choose the correct date.
* **Employee Exit Reason**: This field is a select-one field where you can choose the reason for your exit. The options available are Voluntary, Involuntary, and Other. Select the reason that best describes your situation.
* **Reason for Exit**: Here, you can provide a brief explanation for your reason for leaving the company. This field is optional, but it is recommended that you provide a clear and concise explanation.
* **Employee Type**: This field is a required select multiple field where you can select your type of employment with the company. The available options are Full-time, Part-time, and Contract. Please ensure that you select the correct type for yourself.
* **Manager's Comment**: This is an optional field where the manager can provide additional comments or information about the employee's exit.
