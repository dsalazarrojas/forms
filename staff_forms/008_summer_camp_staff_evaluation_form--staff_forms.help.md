# summer_camp_staff_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The summer camp staff evaluation form is used to gather feedback from campers about their experience with a staff member, with the goal of evaluating the staff member's performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the camp counselor's ID number from the list of options for "camp_counselor_id"
2.  If desired, select a rating (High/Low) for the counselor's "performance_rating" and provide a brief note in the "staff_feedback" field
3.  If desired, provide a note in the "camper_feedback" field
4.  Choose a rating for the overall experience from 1-5 in the "overall_experience" field
5.  Choose a date from the calendar to complete the "date" field
6.  Enter the time of day you completed the form in the "time" field
7.  Click submit to submit your feedback

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camp Counselor ID (camp_counselor_id, number, required)**: Enter the ID number of the camp counselor you are evaluating.
* **Camper ID (camper_id, number, required)**: Enter your unique camp ID number.
* **Performance Rating (performance_rating, select_one, optional)**: Choose "High" or "Low" to indicate how you felt about the counselor's performance.
* **Staff Feedback (staff_feedback, note, optional)**: Provide a brief note about your feedback for the staff member.
* **Camper Feedback (camper_feedback, note, optional)**: Provide a note about your experience with the staff member.
* **Overall Experience (overall_experience, number, optional)**: Choose a number from 1-5 to rate your overall experience with the staff member.
* **Date (date, date, optional)**: Choose a date from the calendar to complete the form.
* **Time (time, time, optional)**: Enter the time of day you completed the form.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest feedback to help staff members improve their performance.
* You can skip fields that are not relevant to your experience with the staff member.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the camp administration.
