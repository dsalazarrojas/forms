# Daily Gear Checkout List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Gear Checkout List Form is a tool for staff members to report equipment checked out for their daily use.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **equipment_list**: Choose from the list of equipment you have checked out today.
	* Select all the equipment you have checked out for your daily activities.
2. **staff_name**: Enter your full name as a staff member.
	* This field is required to ensure that you are reporting for your own equipment usage.
3. **checkout_date**: Choose the date you checked out the equipment.
	* Select the date you took the equipment out for use.
4. **return_date**: Choose the date you returned the equipment.
	* Select the date you returned the equipment after use.
5. **assigned_to**: Choose 'Yes' if the equipment is assigned to you.
	* This field is used to indicate whether the equipment is assigned to you or not.
6. **equipment_description**: Enter a brief description of the equipment.
	* This field is optional but helps to provide more information about the equipment.
7. **department**: Choose the department the equipment belongs to.
	* Select the department that the equipment is assigned to.
8. **staff_department**: Enter your department as a staff member.
	* This field is optional but helps to provide more information about your department.
9. **location**: Choose the location of the equipment.
	* Select the location where the equipment is used.
10. **staff_location**: Enter your location as a staff member.
	* This field is optional but helps to provide more information about your location.
11. **equipment_status**: Choose the status of the equipment.
	* Select whether the equipment is Active or Inactive.
12. **notes**: Enter any additional notes about the equipment.
	* This field is optional but helps to provide more information about the equipment.
13. **assigned_tool**: Enter any additional information about the equipment.
	* This field is optional but helps to provide more information about the equipment.
