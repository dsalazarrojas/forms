# Shift Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Log Form is used to record information about shifts, including start and end times, status, team assignments, and any relevant comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting to fill out the form.
2. Enter the start date of the shift in the "Start Date" field.
3. Enter the start time of the shift in the "Shift Start Time" field.
4. Enter the end time of the shift in the "Shift End Time" field.
5. Select the status of the shift from the "Shift Status" options.
6. Choose the team(s) that the shift is assigned to from the "Team" options.
7. If applicable, enter the supervisor's name or other relevant contact information in the "Supervisor" field.
8. Enter any comments or notes about the shift in the "Comments" field.
9. Indicate whether the shift was assigned to a specific employee by selecting "True" or "False" from the "Assigned To" field.
10. Enter any additional notes or information about the shift in the "Notes" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the employee or contact person in the "Phone" field.
12. Enter the email address of the employee or contact person in the "Email" field.
13. Select the shift type by choosing "True" or "False" from the "Shift" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date**: Enter the date on which the shift starts.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the time when the shift starts.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the time when the shift ends.
* **Shift Status**: Select the current status of the shift, which can be "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Team**: Choose the team(s) that the shift is assigned to.
* **Supervisor**: Enter the name or contact information of the supervisor for the shift.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the shift.
* **Assigned To**: Indicate whether the shift was assigned to a specific employee by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about the shift.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee or contact person.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the employee or contact person.
* **Shift**: Select the shift type by choosing "True" or "False".
