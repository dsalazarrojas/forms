# employee_probation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee probation form is used to assess an employee's performance during their probationary period by their supervisor. The form helps gather feedback and evaluation data to make decisions about continued employment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out this form in your official capacity as a supervisor.
2. Provide accurate and honest feedback about the employee's performance.
3. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the employee's identification number.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, required): Enter the employee's job position.
* **Probation Period** (`probation_period`, number, optional): Enter the length of the employee's probationary period (if applicable).
* **New Hire** (`new_hire`, text, optional): Enter the date of the employee's new hire (if applicable).
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Unsatisfactory
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments about the employee's performance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the employee's performance.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the supervisor.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the supervisor. 
*   **Phone Number** should be replaced with **Supervisor's Phone Number** to avoid confusion with the employee's phone number.
