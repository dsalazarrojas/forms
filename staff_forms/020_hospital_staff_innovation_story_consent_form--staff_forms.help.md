# Hospital Staff Innovation Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Staff Innovation Story Consent Form is used to collect consent from hospital staff members to share their innovation stories. This form ensures that staff members understand the purpose and implications of sharing their stories and provides them with the opportunity to give their consent before sharing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form by providing the following information:

* First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on official hospital documents.
* Email: Enter your email address as it appears in hospital records.
* Innovation Story: Describe your innovation and its purpose.
* Date of Innovation: Enter the date when your innovation was developed or implemented.
* Time of Innovation: Enter the time of day when your innovation was developed or implemented.
* Department: Indicate if your innovation applies to your department.
* Role: Check all roles that apply to your innovation (e.g., staff member, manager, leader).
* Permission to Share: Indicate whether you give permission to share your innovation story with others.
* Comments: Provide any additional comments or context about your innovation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Your first name as it appears on official hospital documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears in hospital records. This will be used to contact you regarding your innovation story.
* **Innovation Story** (`innovation_story`, text, required): Describe your innovation and its purpose. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Date of Innovation** (`date_of_innovation`, date, required): Enter the date when your innovation was developed or implemented.
* **Time of Innovation** (`time_of_innovation`, time, required): Enter the time of day when your innovation was developed or implemented.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Indicate if your innovation applies to your department.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Check all roles that apply to your innovation (e.g., staff member, manager, leader).
* **Permission to Share** (`permission_to_share`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you give permission to share your innovation story with others.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or context about your innovation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and clear information about your innovation story.
* Be honest about your roles and departments involved.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permission to share your innovation story before checking the 'Yes' option.
* Use proper format for dates (mm/dd/yy) and time (HH:MM AM/PM).
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your department or HR representative for assistance.
