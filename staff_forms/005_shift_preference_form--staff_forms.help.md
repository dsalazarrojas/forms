# Shift Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Preference Form is designed to collect information about your work preferences for shifts, allowing our team to better understand your availability and schedule needs. By completing this form, you'll help us create schedules that fit your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Shift Preference Form, follow these steps:
* Review each field carefully and enter your preferences accordingly.
* For fields with options, select the relevant choices from the list.
* If a field is marked as optional, you can skip it or provide information as necessary.
* Make sure to save your changes after completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Preference** (`shift_preference_form`, `text`, optional):
  Enter a brief description or explanation of your preferences for shifts.
* **start_time** (`start_time`, `time`, optional):
  Enter your preferred start time for shifts. Format should be 24-hour time (e.g., 08:00 or 14:30).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, optional):
  Enter your preferred end time for shifts. Format should be 24-hour time (e.g., 16:00 or 20:30).
* **Shift Length** (`shift_length`, `number`, optional):
  Enter the number of hours you'd prefer to work in a shift. This will help us determine your available shifts.
* **Shift Length** (same as Shift Length, `shift_length_hours`, `number`, optional):
  Same as above.
* **Break Time** (`break_time`, `time`, optional):
  Enter your preferred break time within a shift. Format should be 24-hour time (e.g., 12:00 or 15:00).
* **Break Length** (`break_length`, `number`, optional):
  Enter the length of your break in minutes. This will help us determine your break schedule.
* **Break Frequency** (`break_frequency`, `number`, optional):
  Enter how many breaks you'd like to take within a shift.
* **Break Length** (same as Break Length, `break_length_minutes`, `number`, optional):
  Same as above.
* **Preferred Shifts** (`preferred_shifts`, `select_multiple`, required):
  Select the days you'd prefer to work shifts. Options include:
  + Monday
  + Tuesday
  + Wednesday
  + Thursday
  + Friday
  + Saturday
  + Sunday
* **Earliest Start Time** (`start_time_earliest`, `time`, optional):
  Enter the earliest time you're available to start a shift.
* **Latest Start Time** (`start_time_latest`, `time`, optional):
  Enter the latest time you're available to start a shift.
* **Preferred Start Time** (`start_time_preferred`, `time`, optional):
  Enter your preferred start time for shifts.
* **Earliest End Time** (`end_time_earliest`, `time`, optional):
  Enter the earliest time you're available to end a shift.
* **Latest End Time** (`end_time_latest`, `time`, optional):
  Enter the latest time you're available to end a shift.
* **Preferred End Time** (`end_time_preferred`, `time`, optional):
  Enter your preferred end time for shifts.

## Tips
* Be honest about your availability and preferences to help us create schedules that work for you.
* If you have specific needs or constraints, use the appropriate fields to describe them.
* Review your answers carefully before saving your changes to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
