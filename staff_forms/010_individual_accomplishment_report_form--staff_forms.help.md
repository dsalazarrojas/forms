# Individual Accomplishment Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help staff members report on their accomplishments and progress within their role. This form is used to gather information on the successes and challenges faced by staff members, allowing managers to review and provide feedback on their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. On the **First Page**, enter your name in the **Header** field and describe your accomplishment in the **Body** field.
2. On the **Second Page**, indicate whether or not you have completed an accomplishment in the **Accomplishment Section** field. If you have completed an accomplishment, enter a brief description of it in the **Accomplishment Section Header** and **Accomplishment Section Body** fields.
3. On the **Review Section**, write a brief review of your accomplishments and provide any comments or feedback you have for the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Header** (`first_page_header`, text, required): Enter your name in this field.
* **Body** (`first_page_body`, text, required): Describe your accomplishment in this field.
* **Accomplishment Section** (`accomplishment_section`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether or not you have completed an accomplishment. If yes, proceed to enter details in the next fields.
* **Accomplishment Section Header** (`accomplishment_section_header`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your accomplishment if you selected "Yes" in the **Accomplishment Section** field.
* **Accomplishment Section Body** (`accomplishment_section_body`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of your accomplishment if you selected "Yes" in the **Accomplishment Section** field.
* **Second Page** (`second_page`, note, required): This is the second page of the form where you can enter a brief review and comments.
* **Review Section Header** (`review_section_header`, text, optional): Enter a brief header for your review section.
* **Review Section** (`review_section`, text, optional): Enter a brief review of your accomplishments and provide any comments or feedback you have for the reviewer.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have for the reviewer.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit the form.
