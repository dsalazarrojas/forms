# Daily Shift Pass On Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Shift Pass On Form is a tool used to transfer shift responsibilities to another staff member.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the next shift from the "Next Shift" field.
2. Choose the staff member who will be taking over the shift from the "Staff" field.
3. Provide the name of the replacement staff member in the "Replacement Shift Staff Name" field.
4. Select the start time of the replacement shift from the "Replacement Shift Start Time" field.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field, if necessary.
6. If applicable, select "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Night" from the "Replacement Shift" field.
7. If applicable, select "Shift Leader", "Senior Staff", or "Junior Staff" from the "Replacement Shift Staff" field.
8. Enter the date in the "Date" field, if necessary.
9. If applicable, select "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Night" from the "Shift" field.
10. Enter the start time of the replacement shift in the "Shift Start Time 2" field.
11. Enter the name of the replacement staff member in the "Replacement Staff Name" field.
12. If applicable, select "Shift Leader", "Senior Staff", or "Junior Staff" from the "Replacement Staff" field.
13. Select the start time of the replacement shift from the "Replacement Shift Start Time" field.
14. Enter the name of the replacement staff member in the "Replacement Staff Name 2" field.
15. Select "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Night" from the "Next Shift 2" field.
16. Enter the date in the "Date 2" field, if necessary.
17. Enter the start time in the "Start Time" field.
18. Select "Shift Leader", "Senior Staff", or "Junior Staff" from the "Staff Member" field.
19. Enter the name of the replacement shift in the "Replacement Shift Name" field.
20. Enter the name of the replacement staff member in the "Replacement Staff Name" field.
21. Enter additional comments or notes in the "Comments 2" field, if necessary.
