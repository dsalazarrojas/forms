## Notice Of Disciplinary Action Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **Notice Of Disciplinary Action Form** is used to document instances of disciplinary action taken by staff members, including the reason for the action and the date it was taken.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure that this form is being filled out for a valid reason.
2. Review the form fields to ensure they match the specific incident or action being reported.
3. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
4. Sign the form in the appropriate fields to indicate approval or acknowledgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **staff_notice_of_disciplinary_action** (`staff_notice_of_disciplinary_action`, text, optional): A brief statement of the reason for the disciplinary action.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): The name of the employee involved in the disciplinary action.
* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, number, optional): The employee's identification number.
* **reason_for_disciplinary_action** (`reason_for_disciplinary_action`, text, optional): A detailed explanation of the reason for the disciplinary action.
* **action_taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional): A brief statement of any action taken as a result of the disciplinary action.
* **date_of_action** (`date_of_action`, date, optional): The date the disciplinary action was taken.
* **time_of_action** (`time_of_action`, time, optional): The time the disciplinary action was taken.
* **staff_signature** (`staff_signature`, note, optional): A note field for the staff member's signature, indicating approval or acknowledgment of the form.
* **employee_signature** (`employee_signature`, note, optional): A note field for the employee's signature, indicating acceptance or acknowledgment of the disciplinary action.
* **action_effective_date** (`action_effective_date`, date, optional): The date the disciplinary action is effective.
