# Employee Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Emergency Contact Form is a vital document that stores essential contact information for emergency contacts, including their relationship to the employee and medical details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that the form is being completed for the purpose of storing contact information for emergency contacts.
2.  Fill in the contact name(s) and their relevant details such as email, phone number and address.
3.  Specify the relationship between the employee and the contact(s) (Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, or Other).
4.  Confirm if the contact has medical information (Yes/No).
5.  If the contact has medical information, provide the name, phone number, email, and address.
6.  Confirm if the contact is an emergency contact (Yes/No).
7.  If the contact is an emergency contact, specify the level of urgency (e.g., High, Medium, Low).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Emergency Contact Form 1 Label**: This is the title of the form, which is a standard field to help with identification and organization.
*   **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact, such as a spouse, child, or parent.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact. This will allow for efficient communication in case of an emergency.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Contact Relation**: Select the relationship between the employee and the contact (Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, or Other).
*   **Contact Relation Other**: If the relation is 'Other', provide a brief explanation or description of the relationship.
*   **Relation Name**: Enter the name of the relation (if applicable).
*   **Relation Phone**: Enter the phone number of the relation (if applicable).
*   **Relation Email**: Enter the email of the relation (if applicable).
*   **Relation Address**: Enter the address of the relation (if applicable).
*   **Medical Contact**: If the contact has medical information, mark 'Yes'. Otherwise, choose 'No' if there is no medical information available.
*   **Medical Name**: Enter the medical contact's name (if applicable).
*   **Medical Phone**: Enter the medical contact's phone number (if applicable).
*   **Medical Email**: Enter the medical contact's email (if applicable).
*   **Medical Relation**: If the medical contact has a relation to the employee, specify 'Yes' or 'No' (if 'Yes', provide 'Medical Relation Other' for further description).
*   **Medical Relation Other**: If the medical contact has a relation to the employee, describe it (if 'Yes' is selected for 'Medical Relation').
*   **Emergency Contact**: Mark 'Yes' if the contact is an emergency contact for the employee.
*   **Emergency Contact Urgent**: Specify the level of urgency (High, Medium, Low) if the contact is an emergency contact.
*   **Confirm Urgent**: Confirm the level of urgency for the emergency contact.

Note: This form is designed to gather vital contact information for employees in case of emergencies. If you have any further questions or concerns, please refer to your supervisor or HR department.
