# cricket_club_member_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "cricket_club_member_registration_form" is a registration form designed for individuals to sign up as members of a cricket club. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure smooth processing of your membership application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your personal first name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your personal last name.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter your **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address associated with your identity.
5. Enter your **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your phone number or any other contact information.
6. Select your **Membership Type** (`membership_type`): Choose between Full Member, Associate Member, or Honorary Member.
7. Enter your **Address** (`address`): Provide your current address for communication purposes.
8. Add any **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): You can provide any additional information you'd like the club to know about you.
9. Click on **Submit** (`submit`): Once you've completed all the required fields, click this button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your personal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your identity.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number or any other contact information.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Choose between Full Member, Associate Member, or Honorary Member.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Provide your current address for communication purposes.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, required): You can provide any additional information you'd like the club to know about you.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your registration once you've completed all the required fields.
