# Employee Happiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to measure employee happiness and satisfaction within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the option that best describes your feelings or answer for each question.
3. Take as much time as you need to complete the form.
4. You can skip any question if you feel it's not relevant or you don't have an answer.
