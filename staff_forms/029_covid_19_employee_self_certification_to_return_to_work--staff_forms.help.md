# COVID 19 Employee Self Certification To Return To Work - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to self-certify their fitness to return to work after a period of absence due to COVID-19. It helps to assess whether the employee has completed their recovery and is ready to resume their duties safely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the employee information section (questions 2-5).
2. Answer the COVID-19 status section (questions 6-10) based on your current health status.
3. Review and confirm your certification in the health certification section (questions 11-14).
4. Sign and date the certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee ID** (3, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (4, text, required): Select the department you belong to.
* **Job Title** (5, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears in the company directory.
* **Supervisor Name** (6, text, required): Enter the name of your immediate supervisor.
* **Were you diagnosed with COVID-19?** (8, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes' if you've been diagnosed with COVID-19, 'No' if not.
* **Date of Diagnosis** (9, date, optional): Enter the date you were diagnosed with COVID-19. Only required if you answered 'Yes' to the previous question.
* **Current Recovery Status** (10, select_one, optional): Choose how long you've been recovering.
* **How long were you away from work?** (11, number, optional): Enter the number of days you've been away from work.
* **Unit of time** (12, select_one, optional): Select the unit of time for your answer to the previous question.
* **Do you currently have COVID-19 symptoms?** (14, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes' if you have symptoms, 'No' if not.
* **If yes, describe current symptoms** (15, text, optional): Describe your current symptoms if you answered 'Yes' to the previous question.
* **Fever-free for at least 24 hours without medication** (16, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes' if you've been fever-free for 24 hours, 'No' if not, 'Not applicable' if you were never febrile.
* **Symptoms are improving** (17, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes' if you've experienced improvement in your symptoms, 'No' if not, 'Not applicable' if you were never symptomatic.
* **Have completed isolation period as recommended** (18, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes' if you've completed isolation, 'No' if not, 'Not applicable' if you never needed to isolate.
* **Physician has cleared you to return to work** (19, select_one, optional): Answer 'Yes' if your doctor has cleared you, 'No' if not, 'Not applicable' if you never needed medical clearance.
* **All information in this certification is truthful and accurate** (21, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes' if all information provided is true, 'No' if not.
* **I understand my obligation to protect coworkers and workplace** (22, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes' to acknowledge your responsibility.
* **I commit to following all workplace safety protocols** (23, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes' to confirm your commitment.
* **Date of Certification** (24, date, required): Enter the date you're certifying yourself fit to return to work.
* **Employee Signature** (25, text, required): Sign and date the certification to confirm your commitment.
