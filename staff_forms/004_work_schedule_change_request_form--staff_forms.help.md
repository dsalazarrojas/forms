# Work Schedule Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Schedule Change Request Form is a tool used to request changes to your work schedule. This form is intended to gather necessary information for your manager to review and approve your requested schedule change.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please fill out the form with your requested schedule change details.
* Make sure to provide accurate information in the required fields.
* Double-check that your shift start and end dates and times are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Schedule Change Request Form 1 (text)**: A text field for describing your reason for requesting a schedule change.
* **Shift Type** (`select_one`): 
	+ Select the type of shift you are currently working (e.g., 1, 2, or 3).
* **Shift Start Date** (`date`): Enter the date you were scheduled to start your shift.
* **Shift Start Time** (`time`): Enter the time you were scheduled to start your shift.
* **Shift End Date** (`date`): Enter the date your shift was supposed to end.
* **Shift End Time** (`time`): Enter the time your shift was scheduled to end.
* **Shift Reason** (`text`): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of your schedule change request.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`select_one`):
	+ Choose a reason for your manager's confirmation or approval of your schedule change.

Please note that the form is optional, and it is a good idea to review your manager's approval process for schedule changes before filling it out.
