# pre-employment-physical-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather pre-employment physical information from new hires to ensure they have the necessary medical clearance and insurance information before starting work.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form in its entirety before submitting.
- If you need assistance, contact your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your social security number as it appears on your official identification.
* **Height in inches**: Enter your height in inches as measured recently.
* **Weight in pounds**: Enter your weight in pounds as measured recently.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions or concerns you have.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have, if any.
* **Medical Restrictions**: Select all medical restrictions that apply (Limited, Full).
* **Medical Clearance**: Select the medical clearance status (Clear, Conditional, Not Clear).
* **Medical Supervision**: Select all medical supervision options that apply (Yes, No).
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship to the emergency contact (Parent, Spouse, Child, Other).
* **Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Medical Insurance**: Enter your medical insurance information.
* **Type**: Select your medical insurance type (Private, Group, Medicaid, Other).
* **Provider**: Enter your medical insurance provider information.
* **Group Provider**: Enter your group medical insurance provider information.
* **Group Name**: Enter your group medical insurance name.
* **Primary Care Provider**: Enter your primary care provider information.
* **Secondary Care Provider**: Enter your secondary care provider information.
* **Treatment Plan**: Enter any treatment plans or medications you are currently taking.
* **Dosage**: Enter any dosage information for medications you are currently taking.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional medical notes or concerns.
