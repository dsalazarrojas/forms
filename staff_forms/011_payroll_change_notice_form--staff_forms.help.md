# payroll_change_notice_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "payroll_change_notice_form" is used to document changes to an employee's pay rate, start and end times, and other payroll-related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and approvals before completing this form.
2. Enter the employee's name in the **employee_name** field.
3. Select the notice type from the options provided in the **notice_type** field.
4. Enter the pay start date in the **pay_start_date** field.
5. Enter the reason for the pay change in the **reason** field.
6. Enter the employee's ID number in the **employee_id** field.
7. Enter the new pay rate in the **pay_rate** field.
8. Enter the pay start time in the **pay_start_time** field.
9. Enter the pay end date in the **pay_end_date** field.
10. Enter the pay end time in the **pay_end_time** field.
11. Enter the date of the change in the **change_date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee whose pay information is being changed.
* **notice_type**: Select the type of notice being made (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **pay_start_date**: Enter the date on which the employee's pay will begin changing.
* **reason**: Enter a brief explanation for the pay change (e.g., promotion, job change, etc.).
* **employee_id**: Enter the employee's ID number.
* **pay_rate**: Enter the new pay rate for the employee.
* **pay_start_time**: Enter the time when the employee's pay will begin changing.
* **pay_end_date**: Enter the date on which the employee's pay will end changing.
* **pay_end_time**: Enter the time when the employee's pay will end changing.
* **change_date**: Enter the date on which the pay change took effect.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check that the employee's ID number and pay rate are correct.
* Enter the correct pay start and end dates and times for the employee.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
