# Employee Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from employees about their training experiences, helping us improve and refine our training programs to better support you and your colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Provide any additional information or comments in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Rating** (`employee_rating`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field asks you to rate your overall experience with the training program. You can select one or multiple options: Good, Fair.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, select_one, required: true)
	+ This field asks if you have attended a training program. You can select either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the date of the training program you attended.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the duration of the training program you attended.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the location of the training program you attended.
* **Training Facilitator** (`training_facilitator`, text, required: false)
	+ This field asks for the name of the facilitator of the training program.
* **Training Notes** (`training_notes`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is for any additional notes or comments about the training program.
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field asks if you have received any feedback from staff after the training program. You can select one or multiple options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Manager Feedback** (`manager_feedback`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field asks if you have received any feedback from your manager after the training program. You can select one or multiple options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Completed Training** (`completed_training`, number, required: false)
	+ This field asks how many hours of training you have completed.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, required: false)
	+ This field asks how many hours were spent in the training program.
* **Attended Training** (`attended_training`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field asks if you have attended a training program. You can select either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Completed Hours** (`completed_hours`, number, required: false)
	+ This field asks how many hours of training you have completed.
* **Hours Spent** (`hours_spent`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field asks if you spent any hours on the training program. You can select one or multiple options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Training Satisfaction** (`training_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field asks about your overall satisfaction with the training program. You can select either 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied'.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field asks about your overall satisfaction with the training experience. You can select either 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied'.
* **Training Value** (`training_value`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field asks if the training program was valuable to you. You can select either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Training Relevance** (`training_relevance`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field asks if the training program was relevant to your job. You can select either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Completed Survey** (`completed_survey`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field asks if you have completed the survey. You can select either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Survey Frequency** (`survey_frequency`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field asks if you want to participate in future surveys. You can select either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is for any additional information about what you would like to see in future training programs.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is for any additional comments or information about your training experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is for any additional comments or feedback about the training program.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ This field is for your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for your phone number.
