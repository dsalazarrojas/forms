# Contact Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from individuals, including full name, email address, phone number, organization or company affiliation, job title, address, and preferred contact method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
4. If you have an organization or company affiliation, enter it in the "Organization or Company" field.
5. If you have a job title, enter it in the "Job Title" field.
6. Enter your street address in the "Address Line 1" field.
7. Enter your apartment or suite number in the "Address Line 2" field.
8. Enter your city in the "City" field.
9. Enter your state or province in the "State or Province" field.
10. Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
11. Select your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
12. Select the purpose of contact from the "Purpose of Contact" dropdown menu.
13. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
14. Finally, select the "Consent to Contact" checkbox to indicate your agreement to be contacted regarding your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address, which we will use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Organization or Company** (Organization or Company, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Address Line 1** (Address Line 1, text, optional): Enter your street address or P.O. Box number.
* **Address Line 2** (Address Line 2, text, optional): Enter your apartment or suite number.
* **City** (City, text, optional): Enter your city name.
* **State or Province** (State or Province, text, optional): Enter your state or province name.
* **Postal Code** (Postal Code, text, optional): Enter your ZIP code or postal code.
* **Country** (Country, select_one, optional): Select your country from the dropdown menu.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Preferred Contact Method, select_one, required): Select how you would like us to contact you, whether by email, phone, or mail.
* **Purpose of Contact** (Purpose of Contact, select_one, required): Select the purpose of your contact, which can be website registration, event sign-up, newsletter subscription, or general inquiry.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, enter them here.
* **Consent to Contact** (Consent to Contact, select_one, required): Select "True" to indicate that you agree to be contacted regarding your inquiry.
