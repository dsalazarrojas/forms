# Maternity Leave Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by employees to apply for maternity leave, which is an essential part of their employment benefits. Maternity leave allows employees to take time off from work to care for their newborn child. This form template will guide you through the process of submitting a complete and accurate application for maternity leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out your employee name and ID number in the respective fields.
3. Select your department from the available options.
4. Provide the start and end dates of your maternity leave.
5. Specify the reason for your leave.
6. Enter the date you plan to return to work.
7. Specify the duration of your leave (in days).
8. Choose the status of your leave application (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
9. Enter your name and the date you submitted the form.
10. Finally, assign your leave request to a specific person and the date it was assigned to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): This field is used to enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select your department (Sales, Marketing, or IT).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date you plan to start your maternity leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date you plan to end your maternity leave.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): This field is used to specify the reason for your leave.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, number, optional): This field is used to specify the duration of your leave (in days).
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): This field is used to choose the status of your leave application (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your name.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the person assigned to handle your leave request.
* **Assigned On** (`assigned_on`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date your leave request was assigned.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the person who approved or rejected your leave request.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to avoid errors.
* Enter accurate dates for start, end, and return dates.
* Specify a valid reason for your leave.
* Enter accurate duration of leave.
* Choose the correct status for your leave application.
* Enter correct names and dates for submitted by, assigned to, and approved by fields.
