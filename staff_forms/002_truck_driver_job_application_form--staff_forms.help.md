# Truck Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Truck Driver Job Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from potential truck drivers interested in joining the company. We use this form to gather essential details about the applicants, including their contact information, background, and availability for hauling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Provide a valid Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
5. Select your Driver Experience from the dropdown menu in the Driver Experience field.
6. Indicate your availability for loading, commuting, and hauling in the Available to Load, Available to Commute, and Available to Haul fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email**: A valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Driver Experience**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-5 years
	+ More than 5 years
* **Available to Load**: Indicate your availability to load:
	+ True (you're available to load)
	+ False (you're not available to load)
	+ Maybe (you're unsure)
* **Available to Commute**: Indicate your availability to commute:
	+ True (you're available to commute)
	+ False (you're not available to commute)
	+ Maybe (you're unsure)
* **Available to Haul**: Indicate your availability to haul:
	+ True (you're available to haul)
	+ False (you're not available to haul)
	+ Maybe (you're unsure)

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
