### Onboarding Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

### Purpose

The Onboarding Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from new employees on their experiences with the onboarding process. This form helps us understand what worked well and what didn't, so we can continuously improve our onboarding procedures.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability. The more specific and detailed your feedback, the more valuable it will be to us.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best describes your experience.
3. Use the space provided for open-ended questions to type your thoughts and suggestions.
4. Make sure to complete all questions, but do not worry if you're unsure about some of the questions or need more time to think.
