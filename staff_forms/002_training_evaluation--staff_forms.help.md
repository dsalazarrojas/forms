# Training Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Evaluation form is used to collect feedback from trainees about their experience in a course or training session. This feedback is used to help the training team refine their programs and improve the quality of their instruction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions about the course you attended, including your rating of the instructor and what you found most helpful.
* Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the course.
* Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* Enter your phone number so we can reach you if necessary.
* Add any additional comments or notes about your experience in the course.
* Finally, record the date and time of your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Evaluation Form (1)**: This is the main form where you will provide feedback about the course.
* **Course (2)**: Select the course you attended from the list of options.
* **Trainee Name (3)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or badge.
* **How would you rate the instructor? (4)**: Rate the instructor as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What did you find most helpful in this course? (5)**: Enter any notes or comments about what you found most helpful in the course.
* **Rating (6)**: Enter a numerical rating for the course.
* **Date (7)**: Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Time (8)**: Enter the time you completed the course.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if necessary.
* **Note (11)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Comments (12)**: Enter any further comments or suggestions you have about the course.
* **Evaluation Date (13)**: Record the date and time you completed this evaluation.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Use proper formatting for dates and times.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
