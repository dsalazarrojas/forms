# Professional Development Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Professional Development Feedback Survey form, which is designed to gather your feedback on the seminars and workshops you have attended. The information collected will help us identify areas of improvement and develop more effective programs for you and your colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2. Answer each question truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you have attended multiple seminars or workshops, you can complete this form for each event separately.
4. Take as much time as needed to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief text field where you can introduce yourself and provide any context for your feedback.
* **Rate this seminar**: Select your rating for the seminar from the options provided (Good, Fair).
* **Rate this workshop**: Select your rating for the workshop from the options provided (Good, Fair).
* **How do you rate the seminar?**: Select your rating for the seminar from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **How do you rate the workshop?**: Select your rating for the workshop from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **What content of the seminar was most valuable to you?**: Select the content that you found most valuable from the options provided (Presentation, Facilitator, Materials, Other (please specify)).
* **What content of the workshop was most valuable to you?**: Select the content that you found most valuable from the options provided (Presentation, Facilitator, Materials, Other (please specify)).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the seminar or workshop you attended.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the seminar or workshop you attended.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the seminar or workshop you attended.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of the seminar or workshop you attended.
* **Facilitator**: Provide the name of the facilitator of the seminar or workshop.
* **Seminar Location**: Enter the location of the seminar.
* **Seminar Name**: Enter the name of the seminar.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments about your experience.
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Recommendations**: Provide any recommendations for future programs.
* **Feedback**: Provide any general feedback about the seminar or workshop.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (for follow-up communication).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (for follow-up communication).
* **Do you have any other comments?**: Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **How would you rate the content of the seminar?**: Select your rating for the seminar content from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **How would you rate the content of the workshop?**: Select your rating for the workshop content from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
