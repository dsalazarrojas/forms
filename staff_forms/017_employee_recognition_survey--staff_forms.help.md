# Employee Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about employee recognition programs, including feedback on the frequency and effectiveness of these programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form on a daily or weekly basis, depending on the schedule you are given.
2. Answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
4. If you are unsure about a question, leave it blank or ask your manager for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, required): Your employee ID number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): The name of your manager.
* **Feedback On Recognition Programs** (`feedback_on_recognition_programs`, text, optional): Your feedback on the recognition programs, including what is working well and what could be improved.
* **Frequency Of Recognition Events** (`frequency_of_recognition_events`, select_one, optional): How often you receive recognition events, such as:
	+ Less than once a week
	+ Once a week
	+ Once a month
	+ Every few weeks
* **Perceived Value Of Recognition Programs** (`perceived_value_of_recognition_programs`, number, optional): Your perception of the value of recognition programs on a scale of 1-5.
* **Manager Feedback** (`manager_feedback`, text, optional): Feedback from your manager on recognition programs.
* **Feedback On Recognition Process** (`feedback_on_recognition_process`, text, optional): Feedback on the recognition process.
* **Frequency Of Recognition Events By Department** (`frequency_of_recognition_events_by_department`, select_one, optional): How often recognition events occur in your department, such as:
	+ Less than once a week
	+ Once a week
	+ Once a month
	+ Every few weeks
* **Perceived Value Of Recognition Programs By Department** (`perceived_value_of_recognition_programs_by_department`, number, optional): Your perception of the value of recognition programs in your department on a scale of 1-5.
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, select_multiple, optional): Whether your manager provides support for recognition events, such as:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Recognition Events By Department** (`recognition_events_by_department`, select_multiple, optional): Whether recognition events occur in your department, such as:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Total Recognition Events** (`total_recognition_events`, number, optional): The total number of recognition events you have received.
* **Average Recognition Events** (`average_recognition_events`, number, optional): The average number of recognition events you receive per period.
* **Average Recognition Events By Department** (`average_recognition_events_by_department`, number, optional): The average number of recognition events you receive per department.
* **Manager Recognition Events** (`manager_recognition_events`, select_multiple, optional): Whether your manager receives recognition events, such as:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Manager Recognition Events By Department** (`manager_recognition_events_by_department`, select_multiple, optional): Whether managers in your department receive recognition events, such as:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Employee Recognition Events** (`employee_recognition_events`, select_multiple, optional): Whether you receive recognition events, such as:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date you are filling out the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time you are filling out the form.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Any additional comments or notes you would like to provide.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Employee Recognition Events By Department By Date** (`employee_recognition_events_by_department_by_date`, select_one, optional): How often you receive recognition events in your department by date, such as:
	+ Less than once a week
	+ Once a week
	+ Once a month
	+ Every few weeks
* **Manager Recognition Events By Department By Date** (`manager_recognition_events_by_department_by_date`, select_one, optional): How often managers in your department receive recognition events by date, such as:
	+ Less than once a week
	+ Once a week
	+ Once a month
	+ Every few weeks

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you are unsure about a question, leave it blank or ask your manager for clarification.
* Fill out the form on a regular basis, ideally on a daily or weekly basis.
* Use the feedback to provide insights and suggestions for improvements to the recognition process.
