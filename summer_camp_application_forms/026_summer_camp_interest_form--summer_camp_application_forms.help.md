# Summer Camp Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us understand your interest in attending a summer camp. By providing your information, you'll be helping us plan and prepare for the upcoming summer camp season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the correct options and fill in the required information for each field. You can submit the form once you've finished filling out all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Summer Camp Title (Text Field)
• **Summer Camp Title**: Enter the title of the summer camp you are interested in.
### Summer Camp Description (Text Field)
• **Summer Camp Description**: Please provide a brief description of the summer camp you are interested in.
### Summer Camp Date (Date Field)
• **Summer Camp Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the summer camp.
### Summer Camp Time (Time Field)
• **Summer Camp Time**: Enter the start and end time of the summer camp.
### Summer Camp Location (Text Field)
• **Summer Camp Location**: Enter the location of the summer camp you are interested in.
### Anticipated Summer Camp (Select Multiple Field)
• **Anticipated Summer Camp**: Select all the summer camps you are interested in, separated by commas. For example, "Yes, Yes, No" if you are interested in two summer camps and not interested in one.
### Register for Summer Camp (Number Field)
• **Register for Summer Camp**: If you are interested in registering for one or more summer camps, enter the number of camps you plan to attend.
### Summer Camp Comments (Text Field)
• **Summer Camp Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your interest in summer camp.
