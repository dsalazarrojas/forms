# Summer Camper Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camper Registration Form is designed to collect information about each camper's personal details and medical conditions to ensure a safe and enjoyable stay at the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your camper's first name and last name.
2. Enter your camper's email address to help us contact them if needed.
3. Enter your camper's phone number for emergency contact purposes.
4. Provide their date of birth to help us determine their age and eligibility for certain activities.
5. If your camper is returning to the camp, select "Yes" for the "Is Returning" field.
6. If your camper is eligible for a camp scholarship, select "Yes" for the "Is Camp Scholarship Worthy" field.
7. Indicate whether your camper is a council member or not.
8. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
9. Specify your relationship with the emergency contact person.
10. If your camper has any medical conditions, please enter them in the "Medical Condition" field.
11. If your camper has any medical notes or allergies, please enter them in the "Medical Notes" field.
12. If your camper has any allergies, please enter them in the "Allergy Info" field.
13. Indicate whether your camper has any dogs or other pets.
14. If your camper has any other pets, specify their type.
15. If your camper is a solo, twin, or multiple camper, select the corresponding option.
16. Enter any additional comments about your camper.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your camper's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your camper's last name.
* **Email**: Enter your camper's email address to help us contact them if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your camper's phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your camper's date of birth to help us determine their age and eligibility for certain activities.
* **Age**: Enter your camper's age.
* **Is Returning**: If your camper is returning to the camp, select "Yes".
* **Returning Date**: If your camper is returning, enter their returning date.
* **Is Camp Scholarship Worthy**: If your camper is eligible for a camp scholarship, select "Yes".
* **Is Council Member**: Indicate whether your camper is a council member or not.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Specify your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition**: If your camper has any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Medical Notes**: If your camper has any medical notes or allergies, enter them here.
* **Allergy Info**: If your camper has any allergies, enter them here.
* **Has Dog**: Indicate whether your camper has a dog.
* **Has Cat**: Indicate whether your camper has a cat.
* **Has Other Pet**: If your camper has other pets, specify their type.
* **Is Solo**: If your camper is a solo camper, select "Yes".
* **Is Twin**: If your camper is a twin camper, select "Yes".
* **Is Triple**: If your camper is a triple camper, select "Yes".
* **Is Quad**: If your camper is a quad camper, select "Yes".
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your camper.
