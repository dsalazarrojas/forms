# Tennis Summer Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Summer Camp Application Form is designed for students to apply to a summer tennis camp program. This form helps the camp administrators to gather demographic information, medical details, and tennis-related information from the students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Indicate your student's gender.
3. Enter your student's birthdate.
4. Provide your parents' or guardian's name, phone number, and email.
5. Identify any medical conditions or medical notes that may be relevant to the camp.
6. Indicate your student's tennis level and experience.
7. Select the days and hours you are available for the camp.
8. Sign the agreement form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter your student's name to help us identify them.
- **Student Email**: Enter your student's email address.
- **Student Phone**: Enter your student's phone number.
- **Student Gender**: Choose whether your student is male or female.
- **Student Birthdate**: Enter your student's birthdate in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Parents Name**: Enter your parents' or guardian's name.
- **Parents Phone**: Enter your parents' or guardian's phone number.
- **Parents Email**: Enter your parents' or guardian's email address.
- **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
- **Emergency Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
- **Emergency Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
- **Medical Condition**: Check if your student has any medical conditions.
- **Medical Note**: Enter any relevant medical notes or information.
- **Tennis Level**: Choose your student's tennis level (High or Low).
- **Tennis Experience**: Indicate whether your student has tennis experience.
- **Available Days**: Select the days you are available for the camp.
- **Available Hours**: Select the hours you are available for the camp.
- **Application Form**: This field is for any additional comments or information you may want to provide.
- **Agreement**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the camp.

Please note that the camp administrators will review each form carefully to ensure your student's suitability for the program.
