# United States Marine Corps Officer Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about candidates interested in joining the United States Marine Corps as an officer. The form helps the screening team evaluate their qualifications, background, and suitability for officer positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields.
3. Use the options and select options that apply to you.
4. Provide any additional information in the 'Additional Information' section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (1): Enter your name, title, and any other relevant information about your application.
* **Name** (2): Enter your full name.
* **Street** (3): Enter your street address.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (5): Enter your email address.
* **Screening Officer** (6): Enter the name of your screening officer.
* **Screening Officer's Phone** (7): Enter the phone number of your screening officer.
* **Security Clearance** (8): Select the level of security clearance you have: 
  - Clearance Level
  - Secret
  - Top Secret
* **Date of Birth** (9): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Social Security Number** (10): Enter your social security number.
* **Gender** (11): Select your gender:
  - male
  - female
* **Education** (12): Select whether you have a degree:
  - Yes
  - No
* **Military Experience** (13): Select your military experience:
  - 0
  - 1-5 years
  - 6-10 years
  - 11-15 years
  - 16-20 years
  - 21-25 years
  - More than 25 years
* **Highest Degree** (14): Select whether you have a degree:
  - Yes
  - No
* **Field of Study** (15): Select whether you have a degree in a specific field:
  - Yes
  - No
* **Language** (16): Select the languages you're proficient in:
  - English
  - French
  - Spanish
  - Other (Please specify in the 'Additional Information' section)
* **Additional Information** (17): Provide any additional information about your application.
* **Branch** (18): Select the branch you're applying to:
  - Marines
  - Army
  - Navy
* **Discharge Status** (19): Select your discharge status:
  - Active
  - Inactive
* **Medical Condition** (20): Enter any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition Details** (21): Enter more details about your medical condition.
* **Medical Provider** (22): Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider's Phone** (23): Enter the phone number of your medical provider.
* **Medical Clearance** (24): Select whether you have medical clearance:
  - No medical clearance
  - Medical clearance
* **Medical Condition** (25): Enter any other medical conditions you have, if applicable.

Tips:
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
- If you have any medical conditions, provide the details in the 'Medical Condition Details' field.
- Use the 'Additional Information' section to provide any other details about your application that aren't covered in the form fields.
