# Summer School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and parents to register for summer school programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information. Make sure to select the correct options for each field and review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school the student is currently attending or will be attending.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required/optional): Select the student's grade level from 1 to 12.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the parent's email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **School Start Date** (`school_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the school year.
* **School End Date** (`school_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the school year.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the teacher's name who recommended the student for the program.
* **School Notes** (`school_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the student's school.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to the student for the summer school program. Options: ChatJimi, Other.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments from the teacher about the student.
* **School Category** (`school_category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category of the school program. Options: Summer Camps, Summer Camp Application Forms.
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required/optional): This field is automatically populated with the date and time of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required/optional): This field is automatically populated with the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administrator for assistance.
