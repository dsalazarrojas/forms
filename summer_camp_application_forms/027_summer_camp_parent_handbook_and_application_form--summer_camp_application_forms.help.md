# Summer Camp Parent Handbook And Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather important information about your child's health, emergency contact details, and other relevant information to ensure their safety and well-being during the summer camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and what information is required.
3. If you are unsure about any field, ask a camp staff member for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Information**: Please provide a brief description of your child's summer camp experience, including their name, age, and any previous camp experience.
* **Parent Information**: Please provide your name and contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name, relationship, phone number, and address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Information**: Please answer the following questions:
	+ **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions that affect your child's daily life.
	+ **Medical Allergies**: List any allergies your child has, including the substance and severity of the allergy.
	+ **Medical Conditions**: Describe any other medical conditions that may impact your child's summer camp experience.
	+ **Medical Procedures**: List any medical procedures your child has undergone.
	+ **Medical Notes**: Provide any additional medical information that may be relevant to your child's care.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Provide the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Camper Information**: Provide any additional information about your child, such as their hobbies or interests.
* **Parent Signature**: Please sign this form to confirm that you have reviewed and provided accurate information.
* **Medical Authorization**: Provide any medical authorization or release forms required by the camp.
* **Consent**: Please indicate your consent for your child to participate in the summer camp experience.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any field.
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need assistance, do not hesitate to ask a camp staff member for help.
* This form is a requirement for your child's participation in the summer camp experience.
* Failure to complete this form may result in your child not being able to participate in the camp.
