# 2020 Young Innovators Quest Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2020 Young Innovators Quest Application is a form designed for young innovators to apply for a summer camp that provides opportunities to learn and grow in various skill areas. The application will guide you through the necessary information to ensure we can cater to your needs and provide the best experience possible.
## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring you answer all required fields. You can navigate through the pages using the next and previous buttons. If you need assistance, contact us at [support email or contact info].

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Use a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): Include your country code in the phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
* **Age** (`age`, integer, optional): If unsure, approximate your age.
* **Experience level** (`experience_level`, select_one, optional): Select your current skill level from Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from Social media, Friend/Family, Website, Flyer/Poster, Other.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Name of person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_phone`, phone, optional): Include country code in the phone number.
* **Parental consent** (`parental_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have parental consent.
* **Transportation needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need transportation.
* **Preferred activities** (`preferred_activities`, select_multiple, optional): Select all activities that you would like to participate in, from Robotics, Coding, Art, Sports, Music, Outdoor Exploration.
* **Skill area focus** (`skill_area`, select_one, optional): Choose one area to focus on from STEM, Arts, Sports, Leadership, None.
* **Availability start date** (`availability_start_date`, date, required): Enter the camp start date you are available.
* **Meal preferences** (`meal_preferences`, text, optional): Mention any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Medical conditions or allergies** (`emergency_medical_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical information.
* **Special needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Mention any special needs you have.
* **Prior camp experience** (`prior_camp_experience`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have attended a similar camp before.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other information you would like to share.

Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely, as this will help us understand your needs and preferences for the summer camp. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, contact us at [support email or contact info].
