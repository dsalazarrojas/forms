# Summer Camp Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Intake Form is used to gather essential information from parents or guardians of children attending our summer camp. This information includes medical history, emergency contact details, and other relevant information that helps ensure the camper's safety and well-being during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the camper's information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
3. Use the available options for select fields, such as Camper's Grade and Session Preferences.
4. Enter a valid payment method and amount.
5. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter the camper's first name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Address (text)**: Enter the address of the parent/guardian's residence.
* **Emergency Contact Name (text)**: If applicable, enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (text)**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (text)**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Camper's Name (text)**: Enter the camper's name.
* **Medical Conditions (text)**: List any medical conditions or allergies of the camper.
* **Allergies (text)**: List any allergies of the camper.
* **Medical Procedure (text)**: If applicable, enter any medical procedures related to the camper.
* **Camper's Date of Birth (date)**: Enter the camper's date of birth.
* **Camper's Gender (select_one)**: Select the camper's gender.
* **Camper's Grade (select_one)**: Select the camper's grade level.
* **Session Preferences (select_multiple)**: Select the session times and days the camper will attend.
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Select the payment method for the camper's admission.
* **Payment Date (date)**: If applicable, enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Amount (number)**: Enter the payment amount for the camper's admission.
* **Notes (note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Emergency Contact Notes (note)**: Enter any emergency contact information.
* **Camper's Parent Name (text)**: Enter the name of the camper's parent/guardian.
* **Camper's Parent Relationship (text)**: Describe your relationship to the camper.
* **Camper's Parent Phone (text)**: Enter the camper's parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Camper's Parent Email (email)**: Enter the camper's parent/guardian's email address.
* **Camper's Parent Address (text)**: Enter the address of the camper's parent/guardian's residence.
