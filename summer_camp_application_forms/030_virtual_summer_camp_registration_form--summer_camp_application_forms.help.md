# Virtual Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to register their children for virtual summer camps. It will help us understand the details of the enrollment, such as the child's information, parent's contact details, and camp preferences. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in all the required fields as accurately as possible.
*   Review each section carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary details.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact us and we will assist you accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Summer Camp Enrollment Details** (id: 1, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional information about your child's summer camp experience, such as their interests or any previous participation in similar programs.
*   **Parent Info** (id: 2, text, optional): Please enter your name and contact information, including your relationship with the child.
*   **Children Info** (id: 3, text, optional): Enter your child's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
*   **Summer Camp** (id: 4, select_one, required): Choose the camp that your child would like to attend from the options provided.
*   **Session Details** (id: 5, select_multiple, required): Select the sessions your child would like to attend.
*   **Notes** (id: 6, note, optional): If you have any special requests or comments, please enter them here.
*   **Email** (id: 7, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any important updates or information.
*   **Phone** (id: 8, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for any reason.
*   **Date of Birth** (id: 9, date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth for age verification purposes.
*   **Time of Day** (id: 10, time, optional): Indicate the time your child prefers to attend the camp.
*   **Parent Name** (id: 11, text, optional): Enter your name for our records.
*   **Parent Email** (id: 12, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
*   **Parent Phone** (id: 13, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 14, text, optional): Enter an emergency contact person's name.
*   **Emergency Contact Info** (id: 15, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's information.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 16, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
*   **Camp Start Date** (id: 17, date, optional): Enter the start date of the camp.
*   **Camp End Date** (id: 18, date, optional): Enter the end date of the camp.
*   **Session Duration** (id: 19, text, optional): Indicate the duration of each session.
*   **Camp Location** (id: 20, text, optional): Enter the location of the camp.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a successful registration process.
