# summer_youth_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for the summer youth program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, click on the "Start Form" button to access the form.
2. Fill out each section of the form, providing accurate and complete information.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, text, optional): This section is for providing personal details about yourself. If you have any medical conditions or special needs, please provide information in this section.
* **Essay** (`Essay`, text, optional): This section is an opportunity to provide additional context or information about your background and experiences.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, optional): This section is for providing your contact information to facilitate communication with the program administrators.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): This section is for providing your address for delivery purposes, but it is optional.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): This section is for providing your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): This section is for providing your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Birth Date** (`Birth Date`, date, optional): This section is for providing your date of birth.
* **Camp Experience** (`Camp Experience`, text, optional): This section is to provide information about your experience with the summer camp program or similar programs.
* **Camp References** (`Camp References`, select_one, optional): Please select all references that apply to your background and experience.
	+ 'Yes' (Select this if you have camp experience or references)
	+ 'No' (Select this if you do not have camp experience or references)
* **List of References** (`List of References`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all references that apply to your background and experience.
	+ 'Yes' (Select this if you have references)
	+ 'No' (Select this if you do not have references)
* **Type of Reference** (`Type of Reference`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of reference that best describes your background and experience.
	+ 'Yes' (Select this if you have references)
	+ 'No' (Select this if you do not have references)
* **Application Status** (`Application Status`, select_one, optional): Please select the status of your application.
	+ 'Active' (Select this if your application is active and you are being considered for the program)
	+ 'Inactive' (Select this if your application is inactive and you are not being considered for the program)

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
