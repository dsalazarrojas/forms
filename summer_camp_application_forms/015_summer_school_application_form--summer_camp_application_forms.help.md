# Summer School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer School Application Form is a template used to collect information from students or guardians applying for summer school programs. This form helps the application team gather essential details about the applicant's interest in the summer school program.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Summer School Application Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form's pages and sections to understand the layout and requirements.
2. Fill out the required fields marked with asterisks (*).
3. Choose from the available options for select fields, such as dropdown menus and radio buttons.
4. Enter your name and contact information accurately and completely.
5. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer School Application Form** (`summer_school_application_form`, text, required): 
    Enter a brief description of the summer school program you are interested in.
* **Tuition Fees** (`tuition_fees`, select_one, optional): 
    Choose 'Yes' if you are willing to pay the tuition fees for the summer school program.
* **Select Tuition Fees** (`select_tuition_fees`, select_one, optional): 
    Choose an option corresponding to the selected tuition fees (this field seems redundant with "Tuition Fees" and is likely an oversight).
* **Select One Option 1** (`select_one_option1`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Select all options that apply regarding your willingness to pay tuition fees.
* **Select Multiple Tuition Fees** (`select_multiple_tuition_fees`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Select all options that apply regarding your willingness to pay tuition fees.
* **Select Multiple Option 1** (`select_multiple_option1`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Select all options that apply regarding your willingness to pay tuition fees.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): 
    Enter the date of your first day of attendance or expected start date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): 
    Enter your expected start time.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): 
    Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): 
    Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): 
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Note: Some fields seem unnecessary or overlapping, and the form may benefit from being simplified or reorganized for better usability. 

Tips:

* Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting.
* Make sure to choose the correct options for all select fields.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, include them in the "Note" field.
