# Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Enrollment Form is a crucial document that helps the summer camp staff understand your child's needs, medical history, and emergency contact information. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure a wonderful experience for your child during the camp stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Summer Camp Child Info" section with your child's details, including their name, and any other relevant information.
2.  In the "Summer Camp Parent Info" section, provide your contact information and any necessary details about your relationship with the child.
3.  If applicable, select "Yes" or "No" for emergency contacts, and provide any relevant contact details.
4.  In the "Medical History" section, describe any health conditions that may affect your child's stay at the camp.
5.  Choose how you would like to manage payment for the summer program.
6.  Provide any other relevant information in the "Other Medical Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Child Info** (`child_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter your child's name, and any other details that you think the camp staff should know.
* **Summer Camp Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as phone number and address.
* **Summer Camp Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select_multiple, required: false): If there is someone else that the camp should contact in case of an emergency, please select "Yes" and provide their name and contact information.
* **Summer Camp Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe any health conditions that may affect your child's stay at the camp.
* **Summer Camp Payment Info** (`payment_info`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you want to manage payment for your child's stay at the camp. Options are "No Payment" and "Make Payment Through Office".
* **Other Medical Info** (`other_medical_info`, note, required: false): This field is for any other relevant medical information that you think the camp staff should know.
* **File** (`file`, text, required: false): This field is not currently utilized in this form, so it's best to skip it.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best experience for your child.
- If you have any questions, don't hesitate to contact the camp staff for assistance.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to avoid missing any important details.
