# royal_family_kids_camp_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for kids to apply to Royal Family Kids Camp. The form is used to gather information about the camper, their experience with the camp, and their relationship with the camp. The form helps the camp staff to get to know each camper better and provide an optimal experience for them during the camp period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the text field.
2. Enter your age as a whole number.
3. Check the box for your experience level with the camp, selecting either "Yes" or "No".
4. For "Application Type", select either "Yes" or "No" depending on whether you are applying for the first time or not.
5. Choose whether you are a "New Camper", "Returning Camper", or "Returning Camper with Experience" to indicate your relationship with the camp.
6. Write a text about your experience with Royal Family Kids Camp in the "Experience About RFK" field.
7. Share your experience with the charity that Royal Family Kids Camp supports in the "Experience About Charity" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name in this field. Please use your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age** (`2`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age in whole numbers. If you are under 18, please enter 0.
* **Experience** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is about your experience with Royal Family Kids Camp. Select "Yes" if you have attended the camp before, and "No" if this is your first time applying.
* **Application Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): This field helps us understand whether you are applying for the first time or not. Select "Yes" if this is your first time applying, and "No" if you have applied before.
* **New or Returning Camper** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your relationship with the camp from the options provided. If you have never been to the camp before, choose "New Camper". If you have been to the camp before, select "Returning Camper" and if you have experience from previous years, select "Returning Camper with Experience".
* **Experience About RFK** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Share with us about your experiences at Royal Family Kids Camp. This can include stories about friends you made, activities you enjoyed, or any notable moments from your time at the camp.
* **Experience About Charity** (`7`, `text`, required: false): If you have any experience with the charity that Royal Family Kids Camp supports, please share it with us in this field. This can include any fundraising efforts, volunteer work, or other activities you have done with the charity.
