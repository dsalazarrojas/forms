# Camper Personal Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camper Personal Information Request Form is a form designed to gather essential details about your child's personal information, medical history, and contact information. This form is required to be completed by parents or guardians to ensure their child's safety and well-being during their stay at the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the camper's first name.
2.  Enter the camper's last name.
3.  Enter the camper's nickname, if applicable.
4.  Enter the camper's date of birth.
5.  Enter the camper's age.
6.  Select the camper's gender.
7.  Select the grade the camper has completed.
8.  Select the grade the camper will be entering next.
9.  Enter the name of the school the camper currently attends.
10.  Enter the name of the parent or guardian 1.
11.  Enter the relationship of the parent or guardian 1 to the camper.
12.  Enter the contact phone number for the parent or guardian 1.
13.  Enter the email address of the parent or guardian 1.
14.  Enter the occupation of the parent or guardian 1.
15.  Enter the name of the parent or guardian 2, if applicable.
16.  Enter the relationship of the parent or guardian 2 to the camper, if applicable.
17.  Enter the contact phone number for the parent or guardian 2, if applicable.
18.  Enter the email address of the parent or guardian 2, if applicable.
19.  Enter the home address of the camper.
20.  Enter the city or town where the camper resides.
21.  Enter the state or province where the camper resides.
22.  Enter the ZIP code of the camper's residence.
23.  Select if the camper has siblings attending the camp.
24.  Enter the names of the camper's siblings.
25.  Select if the camper has attended the camp before.
26.  Enter any previous camp experiences, if applicable.
27.  Select all the ways you heard about this camp.
28.  Select all the camper's interests and hobbies.
29.  Enter the camper's favorite school subject.
30.  Select the camper's personality.
31.  Enter any special talents the camper has.
32.  Enter the camper's goals for the summer camp.
33.  Enter any additional information the camper's parent or guardian would like to provide.
34.  Sign and date the form by the parent or guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Camper First Name** (`camper_first_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's first name.
*   **Camper Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's last name.
*   **Camper Nickname** (`camper_nickname`, text, optional): Enter the camper's nickname, if applicable.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the camper's date of birth.
*   **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter the camper's current age.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select the camper's gender.
*   **Grade Completed** (`grade_completed`, select_one, required): Select the grade the camper has completed.
*   **Grade for Next School Year** (`school_grade_next_year`, text, optional): Select the grade the camper will be entering next.
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school the camper currently attends.
*   **Parent or Guardian 1 Full Name** (`parent_guardian_1_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the first parent or guardian.
*   **Parent or Guardian 1 Relationship** (`parent_guardian_1_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship of the first parent or guardian to the camper.
*   **Parent or Guardian 1 Phone** (`parent_guardian_1_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number for the first parent or guardian.
*   **Parent or Guardian 1 Email** (`parent_guardian_1_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the first parent or guardian.
*   **Parent or Guardian 1 Occupation** (`parent_guardian_1_occupation`, text, optional): Enter the occupation of the first parent or guardian.
*   **Parent or Guardian 2 Full Name** (`parent_guardian_2_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second parent or guardian, if applicable.
*   **Parent or Guardian 2 Relationship** (`parent_guardian_2_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship of the second parent or guardian to the camper, if applicable.
*   **Parent or Guardian 2 Phone** (`parent_guardian_2_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for the second parent or guardian, if applicable.
*   **Parent or Guardian 2 Email** (`parent_guardian_2_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the second parent or guardian, if applicable.
*   **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter the home address of the camper.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city or town where the camper resides.
*   **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where the camper resides.
*   **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the ZIP code of the camper's residence.
*   **Home Phone** (`home_phone`, text, optional): Enter the landline of the camper's home.
*   **Do You Have Siblings Attending Camp** (`siblings_at_camp`, select_one, required): Select if the camper has siblings attending the camp.
*   **Sibling Names** (`sibling_names`, text, optional): Enter the names of the camper's siblings.
*   **Has Camper Attended Camp Before** (`previous_camp_experience`, select_one, required): Select if the camper has attended the camp before.
*   **Previous Camp Details** (`previous_camp_details`, text, optional): Enter any previous camp experiences.
*   **How Did You Hear About This Camp** (`how_heard_about_camp`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ways you heard about this camp.
*   **Camper Interests and Hobbies** (`camper_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the camper's interests and hobbies.
*   **Camper Favorite School Subject** (`camper_favorite_subject`, text, optional): Enter the camper's favorite school subject.
*   **How Would You Describe Camper Personality** (`camper_personality`, select_multiple, required): Select the camper's personality.
*   **Does Camper Have Special Talents** (`special_talents`, text, optional): Enter any special talents the camper has.
*   **What Are Camper Goals for Camp** (`goals_for_camp`, text, optional): Enter the camper's goals for the summer camp.
*   **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information the camper's parent or guardian would like to provide.
*   **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form by the parent or guardian.
*   **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was signed.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to our camp staff for assistance.
