# Sports Summer Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Summer Camp Application Form is designed to collect information from potential participants for various sports-related summer camps. This form will be used to gather data on the participant's preferences and schedule, as well as the camp's specifics such as sport and start/end dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
   * camp_name: Enter the name of the camp you are applying for.
   * email: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
   * phone: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
   * sport: Select the preferred sport for the camp (Tennis, Taekwondo, Basketball, Volleyball, Chess, or Swimming).
   * preferred_schedule: Choose the preferred schedule for the camp (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).

2. Provide your schedule information:
   * start_date: Enter the start date of the camp.
   * end_date: Enter the end date of the camp.
   * start_time: Enter the start time of the camp.
   * end_time: Enter the end time of the camp.

3. Provide any additional information:
   * address: Enter your address for any necessary arrangements.
   * note: Enter any additional comments or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **camp_name**: The name of the camp you are applying for.
* **sport**: Choose the sport that interests you the most.
* **preferred_schedule**: Select the schedule that suits you best (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **address**: Enter your address for any necessary arrangements.
* **note**: Enter any additional comments or details.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the camp.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of the camp.
* **start_time**: Enter the start time of the camp.
* **end_time**: Enter the end time of the camp.
