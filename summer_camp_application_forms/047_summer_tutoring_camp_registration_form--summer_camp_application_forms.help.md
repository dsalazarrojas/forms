# Summer Tutoring Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Tutoring Camp Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from students who wish to register for the summer tutoring camp. This form will help you gather necessary details to manage student registrations and provide them with relevant services during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you wish to participate in the summer tutoring camp.
2. Provide your student's name.
3. Enter your parent's name.
4. Enter your student's email address.
5. Enter your student's phone number.
6. Enter your student's date of birth.
7. Choose the grade level your student is currently in.
8. List the subjects your student is interested in.
9. Enter any additional interests or information your student would like to share.
10. Choose the start and end dates for the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you wish to participate in the summer tutoring camp.
* **Student Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your student's name.
* **Parent Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your parent's name.
* **Student Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your student's phone number.
* **Student DOB** (`date`, required: false): Enter your student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student Grade Level** (`number`, required: false): Choose your student's current grade level.
* **Student Subjects** (`text`, required: false): List the subjects your student is interested in or currently studying.
* **Student Interests** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional interests or information your student would like to share.
* **Program Start Date** (`date`, required: false): Choose the start date for the program.
* **Program End Date** (`date`, required: false): Choose the end date for the program.

## Tips
- Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information for your student's registration.
- If you have any issues or questions while completing the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- Once you have completed the form, please review it carefully before submitting to ensure all required information is provided.
