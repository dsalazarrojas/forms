# summer-dance-camp-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "summer-dance-camp-application-form" is used for students to apply for a summer dance camp. You will need to provide personal and contact information, make preferences for the camp, and sign the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have provided accurate and complete information.
2. Fill in your personal information in the "Applicant Info" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
4. Specify your payment method for the camp fee in the "Fee Payment" field.
5. Select your preferences for the camp in the "Camp Preferences" field.
6. Provide information about your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
7. If you have any medical information that we need to know, please enter it in the "Medical Info" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your application in the "Additional Notes" field.
9. Sign the application to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info (Text)** (`applicant_info`, required: false): Please enter your name, age, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Info (Text)** (`contact_info`, required: false): Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address for your parent/guardian.
* **Fee Payment (Text)** (`fee_payment`, required: false): Indicate how you plan to pay the camp fee. You can choose to pay by check, credit card, or other means.
* **Camp Preferences (Select Multiple)** (`camp_preferences`, required: false): Select the options that best describe your preferences for the camp. You can choose multiple options.
* **Emergency Contact (Text)** (`emergency_contact`, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of a responsible adult who can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Info (Text)** (`medical_info`, required: false): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please enter them here.
* **Additional Notes (Text)** (`additional_notes`, required: false): Add any additional comments or questions you have about the camp or application process.
* **Signature (Text)** (`signature`, required: false): Sign the application to confirm that it is complete and accurate.
