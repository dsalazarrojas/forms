# online_summer_camp_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their favorite summer camps and contact details. This information will be used to create a customized summer camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your favorite summer camps on **page 1**.
2. Enter your contact information on **pages 2-3**.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Once you have completed all pages, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Summer Camps (Page 1)
* **summer_camps** (`text`, required: false): Describe your favorite summer camps in a few sentences.
* **Loving Summer Camps** (`text`, required: false): Tell us about the summer camps that you love the most.
* **Which Camp to Book** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose the summer camp that you would like to book.
* **Next Summer's Experience** (`text`, required: false): Share your expectations for next summer's experience.
* **Form Submission** (`text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about your experience.

### Contact Information (Page 2)
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Next Summer's Experience 2** (`text`, required: false): Share your expectations for next summer's experience again.
* **Form Submission 2** (`text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about your experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for support.
