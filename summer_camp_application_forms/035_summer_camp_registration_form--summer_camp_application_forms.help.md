# Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This is a form to register for the Summer Camp. Fill it out to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
3. Use a valid email address and phone number.
4. Be honest about any medical conditions or allergies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for updates and information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Reported Medical Condition** (`emergency_reported_condition`, text, optional): If you have any reported medical conditions, describe them here.
* **Known Medical Condition** (`emergency_medical_reported_medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any known medical conditions, describe them here.
* **Allergies** (`emergency_medical_allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have, e.g., peanuts, nuts, shellfish, etc.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all the required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- Do not create duplicate records.
