# Virtual Summer Camp Project Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Summer Camp Project Registration Form is designed to collect information from project participants, including user and project details, to enable effective registration and management of summer camp projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your basic information, including your name, phone number, and email.
2.  Describe your summer camp project, including its name, description, start date, and end date.
3.  Select your project type, tags, and status.
4.  Indicate your education level.
5.  Choose your location.
6.  Upload supporting documents for your project.
7.  Complete the submission section with a brief description of your project (500 words or less).
8.  Review and submit your registration form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (page 1):
    *   **User Name** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter your name.
    *   **Phone Number** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
    *   **Email** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter your email.
*   **Project Information** (page 2):
    *   **Project Name** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter your project name.
    *   **Project Description** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Write a 200-word description of your project.
    *   **Start Date** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter the start date of your project.
    *   **End Date** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter the end date of your project.
*   **Contact Information** (page 3):
    *   **Phone** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
    *   **User Phone** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the project contact.
*   **Project Details** (page 4):
    *   **Project Type** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Select your project type (Project A, Project B, Project C).
    *   **Project Tags** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter any relevant tags for your project.
    *   **Project Status** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Select your project status (Active, Inactive, Pending).
    *   **User Education** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Indicate your education level.
*   **Project Location** (page 6):
    *   **Project Location** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Enter your project location.
*   **Submissions** (page 7):
    *   **Submission** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Describe your project in 500 words or less.
*   **File Uploads** (page 8):
    *   **File Upload 1** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Upload any supporting documents.
    *   **File Upload 2** (`<label>`, `<type>`, required/optional): Upload more documents or supporting information.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid errors.
*   Be as descriptive as possible in the submission section.
*   Upload relevant supporting documents to enhance your project's visibility.
*   Review your form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
