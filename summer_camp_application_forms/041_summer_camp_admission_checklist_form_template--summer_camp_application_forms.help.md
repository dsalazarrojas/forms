# Summer Camp Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information for campers to provide their medical clearance and contact details for summer camp admission. Please fill out this form to ensure a smooth and safe camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please carefully follow the instructions and guidelines below:

1. Enter your name and information in the corresponding fields.
2. For medical history questions, select the relevant options that apply to you.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
4. If any medical issues require further explanation, please provide details in the respective notes fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Camper Information (id: 1)
* **Camper Information** (`Camper Information`, text, required): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information that will help us get in touch with you.

### Report Errors (id: 2)
* **Report Errors** (`Report Errors`, text, optional): If you have any errors or issues with your medical clearance, please describe them in this field.

### Medical History (id: 3)
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, select multiple, required): Select any medical conditions, allergies, or illnesses that apply to you.

### Medical History Details (id: 4)
* **Medical History Details** (`Medical History Details`, note, optional): If you have any additional details about your medical history, please provide them here.

### Contact Information (id: 5)
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.

### Medical Clearance (id: 6)
* **Medical Clearance** (`Medical Clearance`, select one, required): Select the type of medical clearance you have.

### Medical Certifications (id: 7)
* **Medical Certifications** (`Medical Certifications`, select multiple, optional): Check any medical certifications you have.

### Contact Relationship (id: 8)
* **Contact Relationship** (`Contact Relationship`, text, optional): If you have a relationship with the emergency contact, please describe it.

### Medical Clearance Notes (id: 9)
* **Medical Clearance Notes** (`Medical Clearance Notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your medical clearance, please provide it here.

### Camper Information Form ID (id: 10)
* **Camper Information Form ID** (`Camper Information Form ID`, number, required): Enter a unique ID for your form.

### Camp Name (id: 11)
* **Camp Name** (`Camp Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the camp you are applying to.

### Medical History Allergies (id: 12)
* **Medical History Allergies** (`Medical History Allergies`, select multiple, optional): Select any allergies that apply to you.

### Medical History Conditions (id: 13)
* **Medical History Conditions** (`Medical History Conditions`, select multiple, optional): Select any medical conditions that apply to you.

### Medical History Illnesses (id: 14)
* **Medical History Illnesses** (`Medical History Illnesses`, select multiple, optional): Select any medical illnesses that apply to you.

### Medical History Surgeries (id: 15)
* **Medical History Surgeries** (`Medical History Surgeries`, select multiple, optional): Select any medical surgeries that apply to you.

### Medical Clearance Notes Other (id: 16)
* **Medical Clearance Notes Other** (`Medical Clearance Notes Other`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your medical clearance, please provide it here.

### Camp Staff Info (id: 17)
* **Camp Staff Info** (`Camp Staff Info`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the camp staff.

### Medical Clearance Expiration (id: 18)
* **Medical Clearance Expiration** (`Medical Clearance Expiration`, date, required): Enter the date of your medical clearance expiration.

### Emergency Contact (id: 19)
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information for your emergency contact.

### Medical Clearance Notes Camp (id: 20)

### Emergency Contact Relationship (id: 23)

### Contact Relationship Name (id: 25)
