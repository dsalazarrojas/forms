# Summer Camp Detailed Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Detailed Registration Form is designed to collect personal and medical information from participants to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience at the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the participant's state of residence.
3. Provide the participant's date of birth.
4. Choose the participant's T-shirt size.
5. List any medical allergies or conditions the participant has.
6. List any medications the participant is currently taking.
7. List any allergies the participant has to medications.
8. Provide any special needs or requirements the participant may have.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the participant.
10. Select the counselors, therapists, and therapy sessions the participant will be attending.
11. Select any family members who will be accompanying the participant.
12. Choose the participant's employment status and provide their employer's contact information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's address.
* **City** (`participant_city`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's city.
* **State** (`participant_state`, `select_one`, required): Select the participant's state of residence.
* **Zip** (`participant_zip`, `number`, required): Enter the participant's zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, `date`, required): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`participant_tshirtsizes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the participant's T-shirt size.
* **Medical Allergies** (`participant_allergies`, `text`, required): List any medical allergies or conditions the participant has.
* **Meditations** (`participant_medication`, `text`, required): List any medications the participant is currently taking.
* **Meditation Allergies** (`participant_medication_allergies`, `text`, required): List any allergies the participant has to medications.
* **Special Needs** (`participant_special_needs`, `text`, required): Provide any special needs or requirements the participant may have.
* **Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the participant.
* **Counselors** (`participant_counselors`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the counselors the participant will be attending.
* **Therapist** (`participant_therapist`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the therapist the participant will be attending.
* **Therapy Sessions** (`participant_therapy_sessions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the therapy sessions the participant will be attending.
* **Family Members** (`participant_family_members`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any family members who will be accompanying the participant.
* **Employment Status** (`participant_employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the participant's employment status.
* **Employer Name** (`participant_employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's employer's name.
* **Employer Address** (`participant_employer_address`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's employer's address.
* **Employer Phone** (`participant_employer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's employer's phone number.
* **Employer Email** (`participant_employer_email`, `email`, required): Enter the participant's employer's email address.
* **Job Title** (`participant_employer_job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's job title.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If the participant has any special needs or requirements, please provide detailed information to ensure proper arrangements can be made.
* If the participant has any allergies or medical conditions, please list them carefully to avoid any potential risks.
* Please ensure that the participant has selected their correct T-shirt size to avoid any size issues during the camp.
