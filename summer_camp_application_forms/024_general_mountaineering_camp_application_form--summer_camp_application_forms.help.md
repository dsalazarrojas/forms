# General Mountaineering Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Mountaineering Camp Application Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in attending the camp. The form is used to gather details about the user, their application information, and other relevant details such as medical information, payment status, and camper type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Info page with your personal details.
2. On the Application Info page, provide the necessary information about your application.
3. In the Camp Preferences page, specify your preferred camp location and camper type.
4. On the Emergency Contacts page, enter contact information for someone who can be reached in case of an emergency.
5. In the Payment Info page, select your preferred payment method and enter any additional payment details.
6. On the Camper Info page, provide information about the person applying.
7. In the Medical Info page, report any medical conditions or allergies.
8. On the Medical Conditions page, list any medical conditions you have.
9. In the Allergy Info page, report any allergies you have.
10. On the Payment Method page, select your preferred payment method.
11. In the Payment Details page, enter any additional payment details.
12. On the Camper Preferences page, specify any special needs or preferences.
13. In the Special Needs page, report any special needs you have.
14. On the Medical Notes page, enter any relevant medical notes.
15. In the Insurance Info page, report any insurance information.
16. On the Medical History page, enter your medical history.
17. In the Medical Certificate page, upload or enter any relevant medical certificates.
18. On the Camper Info page, provide information about the person applying.
19. In the Camp Location page, select the location where you would like to camp.
20. On the Dates page, enter any dates you would like to camp.
21. In the Camper Type page, select the type of camper you are.
22. On the Payment Status page, select whether you have paid or not.
23. In the Status page, select your status.
24. On the Comments page, enter any comments or feedback.
25. In the Notes page, enter any additional notes.
