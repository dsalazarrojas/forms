# Camp Volunteer Staff Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Volunteer Staff Reference Form is a document that helps us assess the character, reliability, and other qualities of potential staff members. It is used by our camp staff to make informed decisions about who to select for volunteer positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Ensure that the reference is up-to-date and relevant to the applicant.
3. Submit only one reference per applicant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): The full name of the applicant.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, email, required): The contact email of the applicant.
* **Applicant Phone** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): The contact phone number of the applicant.
* **Position Applying For** (`position_applying_for`, select_one, required): Select the volunteer role the applicant is applying for. Options are:
	+ Camp counselor
	+ Activity leader
	+ Support staff
	+ Kitchen helper
	+ Administrative support
	+ Maintenance
	+ Medical staff
	+ Other
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, text, required): The full name of the reference.
* **Reference Title or Position** (`reference_title`, text, required): The professional title of the reference.
* **Reference Organization** (`reference_organization`, text, required): The company or organization the reference works for.
* **Reference Email** (`reference_email`, email, required): The contact email of the reference.
* **Reference Phone** (`reference_phone`, text, required): The contact phone number of the reference.
* **Relationship to Applicant** (`relationship_to_applicant`, select_one, required): Select how you know the applicant. Options are:
	+ Employer or supervisor
	+ Teacher or professor
	+ Pastor or ministry leader
	+ Colleague or coworker
	+ Friend
	+ Neighbor
	+ Family friend
	+ Other
* **How Long Have You Known the Applicant** (`relationship_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration you have known the applicant. Options are:
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6 months to 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ Over 5 years
* **How Would You Rate the Applicant Character** (`applicant_character_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's character from 1 to 5. Options are:
	+ 5 - Excellent
	+ 4 - Very Good
	+ 3 - Good
	+ 2 - Fair
	+ 1 - Poor
* **How Reliable Is the Applicant** (`applicant_reliability`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's reliability. Options are:
	+ Very reliable
	+ Mostly reliable
	+ Somewhat reliable
	+ Not very reliable
	+ Unreliable
* **How Responsible Is the Applicant** (`applicant_responsibility`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's responsibility. Options are:
	+ Very responsible
	+ Responsible
	+ Somewhat responsible
	+ Not very responsible
	+ Irresponsible
* **How Would You Rate Their Honesty and Integrity** (`applicant_honesty`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's honesty and integrity from 1 to 5. Options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How Would You Rate Their Work Ethic** (`applicant_work_ethic`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's work ethic from 1 to 5. Options are:
	+ 5 - Excellent
	+ 4 - Very Good
	+ 3 - Good
	+ 2 - Fair
	+ 1 - Poor
* **How Well Does the Applicant Work with Others** (`applicant_teamwork`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's teamwork skills. Options are:
	+ Excellent team player
	+ Good team player
	+ Works well alone
	+ Some teamwork challenges
	+ Poor team player
* **How Well Does the Applicant Interact with Youth** (`applicant_youth_interaction`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's interaction with youth. Options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Have not observed
* **What Are the Applicant's Greatest Strengths** (`applicant_strengths`, text, required): Describe the applicant's greatest strengths.
* **What Areas Could the Applicant Improve** (`applicant_weaknesses`, text, optional): Describe areas for the applicant's growth.
* **Do You Have Any Concerns About This Applicant** (`concerns_about_applicant`, text, optional): Describe any concerns you have.
* **Would You Trust this Applicant with Your Children** (`would_trust_with_children`, select_one, required): Select if you would trust the applicant with your children. Options are:
	+ Yes - completely
	+ Yes - with some reservations
	+ Not sure
	+ False
* **Would You Recommend this Applicant for Camp Volunteer Staff** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Select if you would recommend the applicant. Options are:
	+ Yes - highly recommend
	+ Yes - recommend
	+ Somewhat recommend
	+ Would not recommend
	+ Cannot recommend
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other information about the applicant.
* **Reference Signature** (`reference_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the reference.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date of signature.
