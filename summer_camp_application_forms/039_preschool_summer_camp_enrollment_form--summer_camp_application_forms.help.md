# Preschool Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for enrolling your child into our preschool summer camp program. We need some basic information about you and your child to better serve you and ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and contact information, including your email address and phone number.
2. Provide your child's name and age.
3. Select the start and end dates for your child's summer camp enrollment.
4. Choose the days and hours of the summer camp that best fit your schedule.
5. Select the type of payment you would like to use.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about your child's interests and needs.
7. Select the type of camp experience (full day, half day, or half week) that your child will be attending.
8. Choose your child's interests (e.g. art, music, sports, reading).
9. Select your child's availability (e.g. morning, afternoon, lunchtime).
10. Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
11. Confirm that you have read and agree to our terms and conditions.
11. Review and confirm the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name and your child's name for accurate records.
* **Contact Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter your child's name.
* **Age** (number, required): Enter your child's age.
* **Start Date** (date, required): Enter the start date of your child's summer camp enrollment.
* **End Date** (date, required): Enter the end date of your child's summer camp enrollment.
* **Days of Week** (select_multiple, required): Select the days of the week your child will attend summer camp.
* **Hours of Summer Camp** (select_multiple, required): Select the hours of the summer camp that best fit your schedule.
* **Payment Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of payment method you would like to use.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your child's interests and needs.
* **Camp Type** (select_multiple, required): Select the type of camp experience (full day, half day, or half week) your child will be attending.
* **Student Interests** (select_multiple, required): Select the activities your child is interested in (e.g. art, music, sports, reading).
* **Student Availability** (select_multiple, required): Select the availability of your child (e.g. morning, afternoon, lunchtime).
* **Emergency Contact** (text, required): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Confirmation Terms Agreement** (select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Confirmation Terms** (text, required): Review and confirm the terms and conditions.
* **Terms** (text, required): Review and confirm the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (text, required): Review and confirm the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (select_multiple, optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our customer support team.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the form instructions above for clarification.
