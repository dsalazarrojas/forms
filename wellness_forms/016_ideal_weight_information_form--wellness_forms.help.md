# ideal weight information form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ideal weight information form" is a digital form designed to collect information about an individual's ideal weight. This form is meant to be filled out by individuals who want to track their weight goals and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully, one page at a time.
- Ensure that each field is accurate and relevant to your situation.
- If you have any special instructions or additional comments, please use the "special instructions" and "additional comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the dropdown menu. Please choose either "female" or "male".
- **Height** (`height`, `number`, optional): Enter your height in centimeters or meters. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
- **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required): Enter your current weight in kilograms or pounds.
- **Ideal Weight** (`ideal_weight`, `number`, required): Enter your ideal weight in kilograms or pounds.
- **Ideal Weight Range Start** (`ideal_weight_range_start`, `number`, required): Enter the lower bound of your ideal weight range in kilograms or pounds.
- **Ideal Weight Range End** (`ideal_weight_range_end`, `number`, required): Enter the upper bound of your ideal weight range in kilograms or pounds.
- **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, optional): If you have any special instructions or considerations that may help us understand your ideal weight, please enter them here.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your ideal weight, please enter them here.

## Tips
- Be honest when filling out this form to ensure accurate data.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider or a registered dietitian for guidance.
- This form is meant to be filled out by individuals who want to track their weight goals and progress.
