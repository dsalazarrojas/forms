# Health And Wellbeing Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool for users to rate their health and wellbeing, set goals, and receive follow-up reminders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on each page, rating your health and wellbeing on a scale of 1-5.
2. For each page, select all the options that apply to you.
3. When asked about your goals, select all the options that you would like to achieve.
4. Answer the follow-up questions to receive reminders and guidance on achieving your goals.
5. Enter your contact information to receive follow-up reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Is Wellness?**: A brief introduction to the form.
* **How Do You Rate Your Physical Health?** (`id: 2`): Rate your physical health on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very bad and 5 being very good.
* **How Do You Rate Your Mental Health?** (`id: 3`): Rate your mental health on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very bad and 5 being very good.
* **How Do You Rate Your Emotional Health?** (`id: 4`): Rate your emotional health on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very bad and 5 being very good.
* **Average Sleep Hours Last Night** (`id: 5`): Enter the number of hours you slept last night.
* **Hours Per Week Exercising** (`id: 6`): Enter the number of hours you exercise per week.
* **How Much Do You Spend On Fast Food Last Week?** (`id: 7`): Enter the amount you spent on fast food last week.
* **How Many Social Connections Do You Have Per Week?** (`id: 8`): Select all the social connections you have per week (e.g., Friends, Family, Colleagues, Online communities, Other).
* **Work-Life Balance** (`id: 9`): Select how you perceive your work-life balance (e.g., Very good, Somewhat good, Neutral, Somewhat bad, Very bad).
* **How Stressful Is Your Life?** (`id: 10`): Select how stressful you feel your life is (e.g., Very low, Low, Moderate, High, Very high).
* **What Is Your Top Goal?** (`id: 11`): Select all the goals you want to achieve (e.g., Improve physical health, Improve mental health, Improve relationships, Improve productivity, Other).
* **What Is Your Sleep Goal?** (`id: 12`): Select your ideal sleep duration (e.g., Less than 5 hours, 5-7 hours, 8-10 hours, More than 10 hours).
* **What Is Your Exercise Goal?** (`id: 13`): Select your exercise goal (e.g., None, Less than 1 hour per week, 1-2 hours per week, More than 2 hours per week).
* **What Is Your Nutrition Goal?** (`id: 14`): Select all the nutrition goals you want to achieve (e.g., Eat less junk food, Eat more fruits, Eat more vegetables, Eat more lean proteins, Eat more whole grains).
* **What Is Your Relationships Goal?** (`id: 15`): Select all the relationships goals you want to achieve (e.g., Improve family connections, Improve friendships, Improve professional network, Improve online communities, Other).
* **What Is Your Productivity Goal?** (`id: 16`): Select all the productivity goals you want to achieve (e.g., Improve focus, Improve time management, Improve work-life balance, Other).
* **What Is Your Stress Goal?** (`id: 17`): Select how you want to manage stress (e.g., None, Reduce stress, Manage stress, Eliminate stress).
* **Would You Like To Receive Follow-Up Reminders?** (`id: 18`): Select yes or no to receive follow-up reminders.
* **Email** (`id: 19`): Enter your email address to receive follow-up reminders.
* **Phone** (`id: 20`): Enter your phone number to receive follow-up reminders.
* **Comments** (`id: 21`): Enter any additional comments you have.
* **How Often Do You Want To Receive Follow-Up Reminders?** (`id: 22`): Select the frequency of follow-up reminders (e.g., Once a week, Twice a month, Once a month, Every 2 months, Every 3 months).
* **How Do You Want To Receive Follow-Up Reminders?** (`id: 23`): Select the method for follow-up reminders (e.g., Email, Phone, Text message, Other).
* **Follow-Up Message** (`id: 24`): Enter any message you would like to include with follow-up reminders.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 25`): Enter any additional comments you have.
