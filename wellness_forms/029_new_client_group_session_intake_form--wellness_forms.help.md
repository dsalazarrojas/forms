# new-client-group-session-intake-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for group session intake purposes. It is intended to gather relevant details about the client, including their client group session intake form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Group Session Intake** (`id: 1`): This field is the header for the client group session intake form.
* **Client Details** (`id: 2`): Please provide your client's details, such as any relevant information about their needs or concerns.
* **Health History** (`id: 3`): Share any health history related information about your client.

## Tips
- Take your time to fill out the form accurately.
- Double-check your entries before submitting.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
