# Workplace Wellness Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Wellness Program Intake Form is used to collect information about your health status, medical history, and other relevant details to support your well-being and ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations. Please submit this form only when necessary and not as a repetitive or duplicate effort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, job title, department, and email address in the provided fields.
2. If applicable, provide your phone number and work location.
3. If you have a supervisor, provide their name and phone number.
4. If you have an emergency contact person, provide their name and phone number.
5. Answer the questions about your medical history, doctor's information, and reported diseases, if applicable.
6. Ensure you provide your doctor's contact information, including phone number and email address.
7. Confirm your submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as per your official company records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): This will help us better understand your job role and responsibilities within the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Specify your department or team within the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a work phone, please provide it.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Specify your work location, including address or a brief description.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): If you have a supervisor, please provide their name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): If you have a supervisor, please provide their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or disabilities, please describe them briefly.
* **Medical Note** (`medical_note`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments about your medical history or condition, please provide them here.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your past medical history, if applicable.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): If you have a doctor, please provide their name.
* **Reported Diseases** (`doctor_reported_diseases`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any reported diseases, please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Doctor's Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): If you have a doctor, please provide their phone number.
* **Doctor's Email** (`doctor_email`, email, optional): If you have a doctor, please provide their email address.
* **Doctor's License Number** (`doctor_license_number`, text, optional): If you have a doctor, please provide their license number.
* **Doctor's State** (`doctor_license_state`, text, optional): If you have a doctor, please provide their state license number.
* **Doctor's Note** (`doctor_note`, text, optional): If you have any comments about your doctor or their services, please provide them here.
* **Doctor's Facility** (`doctor_facility`, text, optional): If you have a doctor, please provide their facility or clinic name.
* **Doctor's Address** (`doctor_address`, text, optional): If you have a doctor, please provide their address.
* **Reported Diseases 2** (`doctor_reported_diseases_2`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any additional reported diseases, please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Please confirm your submission date.
