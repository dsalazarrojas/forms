# Positive Vibes Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather participant details and emergency contact information for the "Positive Vibes" program. The goal is to obtain explicit consent from participants and inform them of the terms and conditions of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your participant details in the "Participant Details" section.
2. List your emergency contacts in the "Emergency Contacts" section.
3. If applicable, choose whether you give consent for the program in the "Consent" section.
4. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (Text, Required): 
Enter your name, email address, and any other identifying information as prompted.

### Emergency Contacts
* **Emergency Contacts** (Text, Required): 
List the names and contact information of your emergency contacts, in case we need to reach out to you during the program.

### Consent
* **Consent** (Select Multiple, Optional): 
Check "Yes" to indicate that you agree to participate in the "Positive Vibes" program, and "No" to decline. Note that consent is not required to participate.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (Note, Optional): 
Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the "Positive Vibes" program.
