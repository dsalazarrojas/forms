# Wellness Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Product Order Form is used to collect information for placing orders related to wellness products. This form is used to gather details about the order, shipping, payment, and customer contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Order Details section with the required information about the order.
2. Provide your shipping address in the Shipping Address section.
3. Choose the type of product you wish to order in the Product Type section.
4. Select the shipping method for your order in the Shipping Method section.
5. Choose your payment method in the Payment Method section.
6. Add any customer notes or comments in the Customer Notes section.
7. Provide your contact number in the Contact Number section.
8. Enter your email address in the Email Address section.
9. Enter the total order value in the Order Total section.
10. Select the status of your order in the Order Status section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, not required): This is the main section where you will enter the details about your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, not required): Enter your shipping address so we can deliver your order to the correct location.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, not required): If you know the date you would like to receive your order, please enter it here.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the type of wellness product you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, not required): If you want to order more than one item, enter the quantity here.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, not required): Select the shipping method you would like to use for your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the payment method you would like to use for your order.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `note`, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your order here.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, not required): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, not required): Enter the total value of your order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of your order, such as pending, processing, or completed.
