# Acupuncture New Client Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acupuncture New Client Form is a comprehensive form designed to capture essential personal and medical information from new clients. This form helps the acupuncturist understand your background, medical history, and preferences before providing treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that you've provided your correct contact information, including email and phone number.
3. If you have any allergies or medical conditions, list them in the "Allergies and current medications" field.
4. Describe any significant medical history in the "Significant medical history" field.
5. Review and agree to the clinic's policies and consent to treatment in the "Consent to treatment and privacy policy" field.
6. If you need to, provide additional information in the "Practitioner intake notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): This field requires your complete name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy, depending on your preference.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Your email address is used for appointment confirmations and important communications from the clinic.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your phone number is used for contact and appointment reminders.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Please provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Allergies and current medications** (`allergies_and_medications`, `text`, required): List all your current medications and allergies, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Significant medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Describe any significant medical history, including surgeries, chronic conditions, and previous injuries.
* **Consent to treatment and privacy policy** (`consent_to_treatment`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the clinic's policies and consent to treatment by selecting "I agree".
* **Practitioner intake notes** (`practitioner_notes`, `text`, optional): This field is for the acupuncturist's notes, and is not required but can be helpful for them to understand your needs better.
