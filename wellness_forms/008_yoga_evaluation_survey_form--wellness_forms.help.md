# Yoga Evaluation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from yoga students about their experiences and preferences. By completing this form, you will be helping us understand what we can do to improve our yoga classes and better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and honestly, as it will help us understand your thoughts and feelings.
- You can skip questions that are not relevant to you, but please make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Survey** (`survey_intro`, `note`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey. You can read it to understand the purpose and context of the questions that follow.
- **Rating** (`rating_scale`, `number`, optional): Please rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
- **Instructor Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the helpfulness of the instructor by selecting "excellent", "average", or "poor".
- **Class Satisfaction** (`class_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how satisfied you are with the class by selecting "very satisfied", "somewhat satisfied", "not satisfied", or "very dissatisfied".
- **Class Length** (`class_length`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how long you would like the class to be, selecting from "too short", "just right", or "too long".
- **Difficulty Level** (`class_difficulty`, `number`, optional): Rate the difficulty level of the class, but please note that this field is currently empty and may not be applicable.
- **Instructor Helpfulness** (`instructor_helpfulness`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the helpfulness of the instructor by selecting "excellent", "average", or "poor".
- **Class Location** (`class_location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose where you would like to take the class, selecting from "online", "in person", or "other".
- **Class Time** (`class_time`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select what time of day you would like to take the class, choosing from "morning", "afternoon", or "evening".
- **Class Frequency** (`class_frequency`, `number`, optional): Indicate how many classes you would like to take per month, but please note that this field may not be applicable.
- **Instructor Familiarity** (`instructor_familiarity`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your familiarity with the instructor by selecting "very familiar", "somewhat familiar", or "not familiar".
