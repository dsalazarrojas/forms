# heath_challenge_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The heath_challenge_feedback_form is designed to gather feedback from participants who have completed a health challenge or wellness program. This form helps us understand your experience and gather insights to improve future programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **participant_id**: This is your unique participant ID, which helps us identify you in our records.
	* **submitted_at**: This field is for the date and time you submitted your feedback.
2. Choose one of the following options for **challenge**: Select the type of health challenge you participated in:
	* Fitness Competition
	* Weight Loss
	* Healthy Eating
3. Provide your rating for the program:
	* This field is optional, but we appreciate your honest feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_id**: A unique identifier for you as a participant.
* **challenge**: Select the type of health challenge you participated in.
* **rating**: Rate your overall experience with the program (Very Good, Good, Satisfactory, or Unsatisfactory).
* **comments**: Choose one or more comments that apply to your experience:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Unsatisfactory
* **phone**: An optional field to provide your phone number.
* **email**: An optional field to provide your email address.
* **comments**: An optional field for any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us through email or phone. If you are experiencing technical issues or have issues with this form, please report them to our support team.
