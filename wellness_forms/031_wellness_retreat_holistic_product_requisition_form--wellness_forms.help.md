# Wellness Retreat Holistic Product Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request wellness-related products that require special handling instructions or procedures. If you are an employee requesting one of these products, please fill out this form carefully to ensure your product is processed efficiently and safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's full name in the "guest_full_name" field.
2. Enter the guest's email address in the "guest_email" field.
3. Enter the name of the product you are requesting in the "product_name" field.
4. Enter the product category in the "product_category" field.
5. Enter the quantity of the product you are requesting in the "requested_quantity" field.
6. Provide any special instructions for the product in the "special_instructions" field.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **guest_full_name** (`guest_full_name`, text, required): Enter the guest's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **guest_email** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter the guest's email address for communication.
* **product_name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the exact name of the product you are requesting.
* **product_category** (`product_category`, text, optional): Enter the category of the product if known (e.g., essential oil, supplement, etc.).
* **requested_quantity** (`requested_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the product you are requesting.
* **special_instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): If there are any special instructions for handling or processing this product, please provide them here.
* **form_end** (`form_end`, note, required): This is the final page of the form. Please review your entries before submitting.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the Wellness Team for assistance.
- This form is only to be used for wellness-related products that require special handling instructions or procedures.
- Please do not duplicate a previous request for the same product.
