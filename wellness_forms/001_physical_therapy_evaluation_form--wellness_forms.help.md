# physical_therapy_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The physical therapy evaluation form is designed to collect information about a patient's physical condition, focusing on various bodily systems and overall sensory status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the requested information for each section, and answer each question as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that you answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) at the end of the label.
3. If a field does not apply to you, simply type "N/A" or "Not Applicable" to indicate this.
4. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, not required): This section is for providing your basic details, such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, not required): Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments that may be relevant to your current condition.
* **Musculoskeletal System** (`musculoskeletal_system`, text, not required): Report any issues or problems you are experiencing within your musculoskeletal system, including joints, muscles, and bones.
* **Neurological System** (`neurological_system`, text, not required): Describe any neurological conditions, such as dizziness, numbness, or loss of coordination, you are experiencing.
* **Cardiovascular System** (`cardiovascular_system`, text, not required): Report any issues or concerns related to your heart and circulatory system.
* **Respiratory System** (`respiratory_system`, text, not required): Describe any breathing difficulties or respiratory conditions you are experiencing.
* **Musculoskeletal Sensory System** (`musculoskeletal_sensory_system`, text, not required): Report any issues related to your musculoskeletal system's sensory abilities, such as pain or numbness.
* **Neurological Musculoskeletal System** (`neurological_musculoskeletal_system`, text, not required): Describe any conditions affecting both your neurological and musculoskeletal systems.
* **Overall Sensory Status** (`overall_sensory_status`, text, not required): Provide an overall assessment of your sensory status, including any issues with touch, sight, smell, or hearing.
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, number, not required): Rate your current pain level from 1-10, with 1 being minimal pain and 10 being extreme pain.
* **Medications List** (`medications_list`, text, not required): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, not required): Provide additional information about your medical history, including any recent medical events or diagnoses.

Note: Please keep in mind that this form is not intended to be a comprehensive medical evaluation, but rather a guide for you to provide your self-reported information. If you have any concerns or questions, please consult a medical professional.
