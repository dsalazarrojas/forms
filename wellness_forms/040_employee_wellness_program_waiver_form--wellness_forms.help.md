# employee_wellness_program_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee wellness program waiver form is used to gather information and consent from employees for the employee wellness program. This form ensures that employees have the necessary medical clearance and understanding of the program's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and select the relevant options. Make sure to review each field carefully before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Date Of Birth
* **Date Of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.

### Medical Notes
* **Medical Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant medical notes or concerns that you would like to share with the program administrators.

### Email
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.

### Select Programs
* **Select Programs** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the programs that you would like to participate in.

### Terms And Conditions
* **Terms And Conditions** (Text, Optional): Enter your understanding of the program's terms and conditions.

### Acknowledgement
* **Acknowledgement** (Select One, Optional): Select the option that best represents your understanding of the program.

### Date Signed
* **Date Signed** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you signed this form.

### Signature
* **Signature** (Text, Optional): Sign this form to indicate your consent and understanding of the program's terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative.
* This form is for your own records, please keep a copy for your personal reference.
