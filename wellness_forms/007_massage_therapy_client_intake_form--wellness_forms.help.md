# Massage Therapy Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from massage therapy clients for initial contact, appointment, or session purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general contact information in the "General Information" page.
2. Enter contact information for the client's emergency contact person.
3. List any medical history or concerns in the "Medical History" page.
4. Indicate the client's massage therapy preferences.
5. Provide any additional medical notes if required.
6. Enter billing information for the session or appointment.
