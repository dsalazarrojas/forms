# Yoga Studio Participant Health Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential health information from yoga studio participants. This information helps the studio staff assess each participant's suitability for physical activities and ensures their safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To fill out this form, find a quiet and comfortable place to sit where you can focus on your answers.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. If you need help or clarification on any question, don't hesitate to ask the studio staff.
4. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Please provide your name, email address, and phone number so the studio can contact you in case of emergencies or to send you reminders.
* **Emergency Information** (`emergency_information`, text, required: false): If you have any emergency contacts or next of kin, please list their names and contact information. This helps the studio staff reach you or your emergency contacts if needed.
* **Health Declaration** (`health_declaration`, text, required: false): Describe any health conditions, injuries, or medications that might affect your yoga practice. Be honest about any physical limitations or health concerns.
* **Signed Waiver** (`signed_waiver`, text, required: false): Please sign this waiver to acknowledge that you've read and understood the studio's health and safety policies and procedures. This is a required field for studio participation.

## Tips

* If you're unsure about any question or feel uncomfortable answering it, talk to the studio staff for assistance.
* If you need to make changes to your answers, don't hesitate to contact the studio staff.
* Remember to submit the form once you've completed it, and review your answers before doing so.
