# Holistic Coaching Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from clients before proceeding with holistic coaching services, ensuring a clear understanding and agreement to the terms of the coaching process.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form in the following steps:

1.  Provide the client's name and any additional contact information they may provide.
2.  Enter the client's email address to ensure they receive any necessary updates and communication.
3.  Enter the client's phone number if they'd like to be contacted.
4.  Enter your own name and any relevant contact information.
5.  Briefly describe your credentials or experience as a coach.
6.  Check the box to indicate that the client has given consent.
7.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate that the consent has been given.
8.  Select the date on which the consent was given.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text, required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Contact Info** (Text, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to have for the client, such as their address or emergency contact details.
* **Client Email** (Email, optional): Enter the client's email address so they can receive any necessary updates and communication.
* **Client Phone** (Text, optional): Enter the client's phone number if they'd like to be contacted.
* **Coach Name** (Text, optional): Enter your own name as the coach.
* **Coach Info** (Text, optional): Briefly describe your credentials or experience as a coach.
* **Consent Given** (Select One, optional): Check the box to indicate that the client has given their consent.
* **Consent Date** (Date, optional): Select the date on which the consent was given.
* **Consent Date** (Date): Select the date on which the consent was given.

## Tips
- Please fill in all the necessary information for accurate record-keeping and compliance.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- This form is designed to be used for all clients, even if you've worked with them before.
