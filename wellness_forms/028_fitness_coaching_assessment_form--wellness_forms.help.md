# Fitness Coaching Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about you so our fitness coach can better understand your goals, habits, and challenges. Please answer these questions to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure you have permission to share your medical history with the coach.
3. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your coach for clarification.
4. Review and submit your form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FirstName**: Please enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **LastName**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Age**: Enter your age in years.
* **PhoneNumber**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted by your coach.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted by your coach.
* **Goal**: Select 'Yes' if you are committed to achieving a fitness goal, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Goal1**, **Goal2**, ..., **Goal8**: If you selected 'Yes' for 'Goal', choose one of the options provided. These fields are meant to help the coach understand your specific goals.
* **MedicalHistory**: Please describe any medical conditions or concerns that may impact your fitness journey.
* **ExerciseFrequency**: Select 'Yes' if you regularly exercise, 'No' if you do not.
* **Frequency1**, **Frequency2**, ..., **Frequency8**: If you selected 'Yes', choose the frequency options that best fit your current exercise habits.
* **Nutrition**: Describe your current nutrition habits, including any dietary restrictions or preferences.
