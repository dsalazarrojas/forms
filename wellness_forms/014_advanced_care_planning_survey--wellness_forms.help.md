### Advanced Care Planning Survey - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is designed to help patients, their families, and healthcare providers have open and honest discussions about advanced care planning. It collects essential information about the patient's preferences and wishes regarding their medical treatment and care, including their preferred treatment settings, life-sustaining treatments, pain management, spiritual care, family involvement, end-of-life care, and organ donation.

### How To Complete This Form

#### To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details in the patient information section.
2. Select your preferred treatment setting from the options provided.
3. Indicate your preferences for life-sustaining treatments, pain management, spiritual care, and family involvement.
4. Make decisions about end-of-life care, including hospice care acceptance and organ donation.
5. Share your thoughts and preferences for funeral and memorial arrangements.
6. Finally, provide any additional comments or information you wish to be considered in your future care.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

#### 1.  **Patient Full Name** (Required)
* A legal full name of the patient.

#### 2.  **Date of Birth** (Required)
* The patient's date of birth.

#### 3.  **Contact Person Name** (Required)
* Name of the primary contact person.

#### 4.  **Contact Person Relationship** (Required)
* Relationship to the patient (e.g., family member, friend, caregiver).

#### 5.  **Contact Person Phone** (Required)
* Phone number of the primary contact person.

#### 6.  **Preferred Treatment Setting** (Required)
* Choose where you would prefer to receive medical care if you are seriously ill:
	* Hospital
	* Home with visiting nurses
	* Hospice facility
	* Nursing home
	* Undecided

#### 7.  **Life-Sustaining Treatments Preference** (Required)
* Choose your preference for life-sustaining treatments if recovery is unlikely:
	* Yes, all possible measures
	* Only measures that offer hope of recovery
	* Comfort care only
	* Depends on circumstances
	* Unsure

#### 8.  **Pain Management Priority** (Required)
* Choose how important pain management is to you:
	* Pain management is top priority
	* Balance between comfort and life extension
	* Extending life is more important than pain
	* Unsure

#### 9.  **Spiritual Care Preferences** (Required)
* Choose your preference for spiritual or religious care as part of your treatment:
	* Yes, regularly
	* Yes, if needed
	* No
	* Unsure

#### 10.  **Family Involvement Preference** (Required)
* Choose how involved you want your family to be in medical decisions:
	* Family makes decisions together
	* Patient decides with family input
	* Patient decides alone
	* Designated person decides
	* Other arrangement

#### 11.  **Hospice Care Acceptance** (Required)
* Choose your acceptance of hospice care if curative treatment is no longer effective:
	* Yes, definitely
	* Yes, possibly
	* Unsure
	* No, not interested

#### 12.  **Organ Donation Preference** (Required)
* Choose your preference for organ donation:
	* Yes, I consent to organ donation
	* No, I do not wish to donate
	* Family decision
	* Unsure

#### 13.  **Funeral Arrangements Preference** (Required)
* Choose your preference for funeral or memorial arrangements:
	* Yes, detailed plans
	* Yes, general preferences
	* No preferences
	* Family decides
	* Unsure

#### 14.  **Advance Directive Exists** (Required)
* Choose if you have an advance directive or living will:
	* Yes
	* No
	* Planning to create one
	* Unsure

#### 15.  **Healthcare Proxy Designated** (Required)
* Choose if you have designated a healthcare proxy or power of attorney:
	* Yes
	* No
	* Planning to designate
	* Unsure

#### 16.  **Additional Comments** (Optional)
* Any other thoughts or preferences regarding future care.

**Tips:** Take as much time as you need to complete this form and don't hesitate to ask for help if you need it. This information will help your healthcare providers understand your wishes and preferences, ensuring that you receive the care you want and need.
