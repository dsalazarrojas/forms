# Student Daily Wellness Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Daily Wellness Screening Form is designed to help students assess their physical, emotional, and social well-being. The data collected will help staff members and counselors provide targeted support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form daily, at the same time each day.
- Answer each question honestly, as they pertain to your current state.
- Select all that apply for checkbox questions.
- Choose the option that best describes your current state for select one questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction** (id: 1)
- This is the introduction section of the form.
- It's a text field where you can provide any additional context or comments.
- This field is optional.

### **Physical Health** (id: 2)
- This section is about your current physical health.
- Answer questions about your physical symptoms or discomforts over the past 24 hours.
- This field is optional.

### **Physical Health Question 1** (id: 3)
- Answer if you have experienced any physical symptoms or discomforts over the past 24 hours.
- Select all that apply: 
  - Feeling fatigued
  - Experiencing pain or aches
  - Nausea or vomiting

### **Energy Levels** (id: 4)
- Rate your energy levels today.
- Select the option that best describes your current energy levels:
  - Very high
  - High
  - Average
  - Low
  - Very Low
- This field is required.

### **Difficulty Swallowing** (id: 6)

### **Physical Health Question 3** (id: 7)
