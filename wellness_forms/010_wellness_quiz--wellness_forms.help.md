# Wellness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Quiz is a self-assessment tool designed to help individuals evaluate their overall well-being. It collects information on demographics, health habits, and wellness practices to provide insights into areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Select all that apply or choose the most relevant option for each field.
3. If you're unsure about any field, use the provided options as guidance.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, Optional): A free-text field where you can provide any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **User Demographics** (`demographic_info`, text, Optional): Provide general information about your age, occupation, or other relevant details.
* **Wellness Behaviors** (`well_be`, text, Optional): Describe your daily habits related to exercise, diet, or other wellness activities.
* **Sleep Habits** (`sleep_habits`, number, Optional): Select the number of hours you sleep per night or enter a specific number if you have a different sleeping pattern.
* **Medication Use** (`medication_use`, text, Optional): List any medications you're currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, select_one, Optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Depression
	+ Anxiety
	+ None
	+ Other
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, Optional): Provide any relevant medical conditions you have or have had.
* **Health Risk Factors** (`health_risk_factors`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Sedentary
	+ Smoking
	+ High Blood Pressure
	+ Diabetes
	+ Heart Disease
	+ None
	+ Other
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Lifestyle Choices** (`lifestyle_choices`, text, Optional): Describe your daily habits, such as regular exercise or balanced diet.
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patterns`, text, Optional): Describe your sleep patterns, such as difficulty falling asleep or excessive daytime sleepiness.
* **Physical Health** (`physical_health`, text, Optional): Provide any information about your physical health, such as chronic conditions or recent injuries.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text, Optional): Describe your mental health, including any stressors, anxiety, or other concerns.
* **Wellness Practices** (`wellness_practices`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Regular Exercise
	+ Balanced Diet
	+ Mindfulness
	+ Meditation
	+ Stress Management
	+ None
	+ Other
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Health Outcome** (`health_outcome`, text, Optional): Describe your expected health outcome, such as improving physical or mental health.
* **Physical Activity** (`physical_activity`, select_one, Optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
	+ Very High
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Health Risk Factors** (`health_risk_factors`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Sedentary
	+ Smoking
	+ High Blood Pressure
	+ Diabetes
	+ Heart Disease
	+ None
	+ Other
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Health Outcome** (`health_outcome`, text, Optional): Describe your expected health outcome, such as improving physical or mental health.
* **Mental Health Score** (`mental_health_score`, number, Optional): Rate your mental health on a scale (1-5).
* **Physical Health Score** (`physical_health_score`, number, Optional): Rate your physical health on a scale (1-5).
* **Health Risk Factors Score** (`health_risk_factors_score`, number, Optional): Calculate based on selected health risk factors.
* **Welllness Score** (`welllness_score`, number, Optional): A calculated score based on the user's answers.
* **Welllness Score 2** (`welllness_score_2`, number, Optional): A secondary calculated score based on the user's answers.
* **Welllness Score 3** (`welllness_score_3`, number, Optional): A tertiary calculated score based on the user's answers.
* **Welllness Score 4** (`welllness_score_4`, number, Optional): A fourth calculated score based on the user's answers.
* **Welllness Score 5** (`welllness_score_5`, number, Optional): A fifth calculated score based on the user's answers.

Note that each field's label and explanation may be subject to change based on the user's input and calculations. Please review each field carefully and answer truthfully for an accurate assessment. If you have any concerns or questions, contact the support team for assistance.
