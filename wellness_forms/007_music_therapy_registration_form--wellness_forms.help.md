# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose

This help guide is for the "<string>" form. The form is used for registering music therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the music therapy session.
2. Choose the category of the session (e.g. Music Therapy, Wellness Programs, or Other).
3. Provide a brief description of the session.
4. Enter the registrant's name.
5. Enter the contact person's first name.
6. Enter the contact person's last name.
7. Enter the contact person's email address.
8. Enter the contact person's phone number.
9. Add any session details.
10. Enter any additional form IDs if needed.
11. Enter the assigned tool (if applicable).
12. Add any notes for the session.
13-25. Enter any additional form IDs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Therapy Registration Form** (`title`): Enter the title of the music therapy session. This will be the title of the session.
* **Category** (`category`): Choose the category of the session from the list (e.g. Music Therapy, Wellness Programs, or Other).
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of the session.
* **Registrant Name** (`registrant_name`): Enter the name of the person registered for the session.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`): Add any additional details about the session.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`): Enter any additional form IDs if needed.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the assigned tool (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes for the session.
* **Form IDs 2-13** (`form_ids2-form_ids13`): Enter any additional form IDs if needed.
