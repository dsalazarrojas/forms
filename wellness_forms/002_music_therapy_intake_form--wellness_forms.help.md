This form is intended for patients and their guardians to provide personal and medical information for music therapy treatment. It collects contact and medical history, medication and allergy information, emergency contacts, and progress tracking. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a specific aspect of the patient's health and treatment.

# music_therapy_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information about the patient and their medical history, contact details, and music therapy treatment goals and progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the patient, including their contact details and medical history.
2. List any medications the patient is currently taking.
3. Report any allergies or medical conditions the patient may have.
4. Record the patient's vital signs.
5. Provide details about any medication allergies.
6. Report the patient's medical history.
7. Outline the patient's music therapy goals and plans.
8. Document music therapy progress.
9. List emergency contact information and their relationship to the patient.
10. Provide the patient's family history.
11. Record the patient's personal goals.
12. Report the patient's current status.
13. Evaluate the outcome of music therapy treatment.
14. Record any outcome notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide general information about the patient.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact details, such as address and phone number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Share the patient's medical history and any relevant conditions.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, required/optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required/optional): Report any allergies the patient has.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, `text`, required/optional): Record the patient's vital signs.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, `text`, required/optional): Specify any medication allergies.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Provide additional medical history details.
* **Music Therapy Goals** (`music_therapy_goals`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the patient's music therapy goals and plans.
* **Music Therapy Plan** (`music_therapy_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Record the patient's music therapy treatment plan.
* **Music Therapy Progress** (`music_therapy_progress`, `text`, required/optional): Document music therapy progress.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): List emergency contact information.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the patient's family history.
* **Patient Goals** (`patient_goals`, `text`, required/optional): Record the patient's personal goals.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, `text`, required/optional): Report the patient's current status.
* **Music Therapy Outcome** (`music_therapy_outcome`, `text`, required/optional): Evaluate the outcome of music therapy treatment.
* **Music Therapy Outcome Notes** (`music_therapy_outcome_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Record any outcome notes.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Record the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required/optional): Report the patient's medical condition.
* **Music Therapy Frequency** (`music_therapy_frequency`, `number`, required/optional): Determine the frequency of music therapy sessions.
* **Music Therapy Duration** (`music_therapy_duration`, `number`, required/optional): Specify the duration of music therapy treatment.
