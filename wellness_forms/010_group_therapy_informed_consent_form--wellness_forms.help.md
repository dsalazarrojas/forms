# Group Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to be completed by clients participating in group therapy sessions. The form helps to inform clients about the benefits and risks associated with group therapy, as well as their rights and responsibilities as clients. It ensures that clients are aware of what they can expect from the therapy sessions and what is expected of them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1.  Read each question carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2.  Choose the correct options for each question from the available choices.
3.  Make sure to sign and date the form to indicate that you have read and understood the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is Group Therapy? (1)**: This question is to understand what clients know and think about group therapy in general. Please provide your understanding or answer based on your knowledge.
* **Client Consent (2)**: This question asks if you have given your consent to participate in group therapy. Please select "Yes" if you have given your consent, and "No" if you have not.
* **Informed Consent (3)**: This question asks if you understand the risks and benefits of group therapy. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Services (4)**: This question asks if you are aware of the services that will be provided during the group therapy sessions. Please select "Yes" if you are aware, and "No" if you are not.
* **Confidentiality (5)**: This question asks about your understanding of confidentiality in group therapy. Please select "Yes" if you understand that what is discussed during sessions is confidential, and "No" if you do not.
* **Therapist Role (6)**: This field is for the therapist to note their role and responsibility in the group therapy sessions. Please do not answer this question.
* **Client Privileges (7)**: This question asks if you are aware of your privileges as a client during group therapy. Please select all the options that apply to you.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to read the information provided carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about anything.
*   If you are unsure about any of the options, please discuss with your therapist.
*   This form is a guide to help you understand the basics of group therapy. It is not a substitute for professional advice from a therapist.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to ask your therapist.
