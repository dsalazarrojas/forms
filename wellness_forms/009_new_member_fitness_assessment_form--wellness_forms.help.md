# New Member Fitness Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the fitness and medical readiness of new members to join a fitness program or organization. It's essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required information in each section, and don't leave any fields blank unless explicitly marked as optional.
2. Be truthful and detailed in your responses, especially when answering medical history questions.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask for help.
