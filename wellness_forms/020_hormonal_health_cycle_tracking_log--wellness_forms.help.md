# Hormonal Health Cycle Tracking Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Hormonal Health Cycle Tracking Log" form is designed to help you track your hormonal health cycle, including symptoms, mood, lifestyle factors, and personal details. This form is intended to be a personal and private record of your menstrual cycle and its effects on your health. By completing this form, you can gain insights into your hormonal fluctuations and patterns, allowing you to better understand and manage your health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

* Select one of the following options for the "Cycle Phase":
	+ Menstrual: Your period or menstrual phase.
	+ Follicular: The follicular phase, typically the time of ovulation.
	+ Ovulatory: The ovulatory phase, typically the time leading up to ovulation.
	+ Postmenstrual: The postmenstrual phase, typically after your period.
* Select all relevant symptoms from "Symptoms" and "Symptoms 2":
	+ Fatigue: Feeling tired or sluggish.
	+ Moodiness: Changes in your mood or emotional state.
	+ Breast Pain: Pain or tenderness in your breasts.
	+ Headaches: Frequent or severe headaches.
	+ Nausea: Feeling queasy or sick to your stomach.
	+ Vomiting: Vomiting or experiencing nausea.
	+ Weight Fluctuations: Unusual changes in your weight.
	+ Other Symptoms: Select "Other Symptoms" if you experience any other symptoms not listed above.
* Select one of the following options for "Mood":
	+ Very Sad: You're feeling very sad or depressed.
	+ Somewhat Sad: You're feeling somewhat sad, but okay.
	+ Neutral: You're feeling neutral, no strong emotions.
	+ Happy: You're feeling happy or content.
	+ Very Happy: You're feeling extremely happy.
* Select all relevant lifestyle factors from "Exercise":
	+ No Exercise: You're not exercising at all.
	+ Light Exercise: You're exercising lightly, e.g., walking or light physical activity.
	+ Medium Exercise: You're exercising moderately, e.g., regular gym sessions.
	+ Vigorous Exercise: You're exercising vigorously, e.g., intense workouts or sports.
* Select all relevant lifestyle factors from "Sleep":
	+ Poor Sleep: You're not sleeping well or getting enough sleep.
	+ Average Sleep: You're sleeping an average amount, but not too much or too little.
	+ Good Sleep: You're sleeping well and getting enough rest.
* Enter any additional notes about your cycle or health in the "Notes" and "Notes 1" fields.
* Select the date and time of your cycle phase in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
* Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, if you want to be contacted by a healthcare professional or receive reminders.
* Select the tool or resource your healthcare provider has assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cycle Phase** (Select One, required/optional): Choose the current phase of your menstrual cycle.
* **Symptoms** (Select Multiple, required/optional): Select all symptoms you're experiencing, such as fatigue, moodiness, breast pain, etc.
* **Symptoms 2** (Select Multiple, required/optional): Select all additional symptoms you're experiencing, similar to "Symptoms" above.
* **Mood** (Select One, required/optional): Choose how you're feeling, e.g., very sad, happy, etc.
* **Exercise** (Select Multiple, required/optional): Select all your exercise habits, e.g., no exercise, light, medium, or vigorous exercise.
* **Sleep** (Select Multiple, required/optional): Select all your sleep habits, e.g., poor, average, or good sleep.
* **Notes** (Text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your cycle or health.
* **Notes 1** (Text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your cycle or health.
* **Date** (Date, required/optional): Enter the date of your cycle phase.
* **Time** (Time, required/optional): Enter the time of your cycle phase.
* **Email** (Email, required/optional): Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted by a healthcare professional or receive reminders.
* **Phone** (Text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted by a healthcare professional or receive reminders.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, required/optional): Select the tool or resource your healthcare provider has assigned to you, if applicable.
