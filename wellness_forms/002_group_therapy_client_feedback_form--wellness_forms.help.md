# group_therapy_client_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from clients who have attended therapy sessions. It helps therapists understand how well their sessions have been received and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your overall satisfaction with the session.
2. Rate the helpfulness of the session.
3. Choose the areas of focus for the session.
4. Enter the date and time of the session.
5. Provide feedback comments about the session.
6. Enter your email address and phone number (optional).
7. Enter any assigned tool used during the session (optional).
8. Record any session notes (optional).
9. Enter any client notes (optional).
10. Describe the session outcomes.
11. Provide recommendations for future sessions.
12. Identify areas for improvement.
13. Record additional session outcomes (optional).
14. Provide recommendations for future sessions (optional).
15. Record additional session outcomes (optional).
16. Provide recommendations for future sessions (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, not required): Enter your thoughts and feelings about the session.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, not required): The name of the therapist who provided the therapy session.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, not required): Additional details about the session.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session, using the scale provided.
* **Helpfulness of the Session** (`helpfulness`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all that apply from High, Medium, and Low to rate how helpful the session was.
* **Areas of Focus** (`specific_areas`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply from Personal, Interpersonal, Family, and Other to describe the areas focused on during the session.
* **Date of Session** (`date_of_session`, date, not required): Enter the date of the session.
* **Time of Session** (`time_of_session`, time, not required): Enter the time of the session.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments about the session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, not required): Enter any assigned tool used during the session.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text, not required): Record any session notes.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, text, not required): Enter any client notes.
* **Session Outcomes** (`session_outcomes`, text, not required): Describe the outcomes of the session.
* **Recommendations for Future Sessions** (`recommendations_for_future_sessions`, text, not required): Provide any recommendations for future sessions.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, not required): Identify any areas for improvement.
* **Session Outcomes 2** (`session_outcomes_2`, text, not required): Additional session outcomes.
* **Recommendations for Future Sessions 2** (`recommendations_for_future_sessions_2`, text, not required): Additional recommendations for future sessions.
* **Session Outcomes 3** (`session_outcomes_3`, text, not required): More session outcomes.
* **Recommendations for Future Sessions 3** (`recommendations_for_future_sessions_3`, text, not required): More recommendations for future sessions.
* **Therapist Recommendations** (`therapist_recommendations`, text, not required): Provide any recommendations from the therapist.
