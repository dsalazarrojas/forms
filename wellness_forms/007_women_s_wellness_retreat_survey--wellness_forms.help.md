# Women's Wellness Retreat Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women's Wellness Retreat Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience at the event. Please provide your honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of 8 fields. Please complete the form by following the steps below:

1. **Introduction**: A brief message about the survey.
2. **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. **Email**: Enter the email address you used to register for the event. We'll use this to follow up with you and send you relevant information.
4. **Event Type**: Select the type of event you attended (e.g. yoga, meditation, workshop, etc.).
5. **Date**: Enter the date you attended the event.
6. **Time**: Enter the time you attended the event.
7. **Satisfaction**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the event. Please choose one of the following:
	* Very Dissatisfied
	* Somewhat Dissatisfied
	* Neutral
	* Somewhat Satisfied
	* Very Satisfied
8. **Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This field is a message to explain the purpose of the survey.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter the email address you used to register for the event.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you attended to help us improve the program.
* **Date**: Enter the date you attended the event to help us track the events' schedule.
* **Time**: Enter the time you attended the event to help us track the events' schedule.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the event. This will help us know what we can improve.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the event.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask us and we'll be happy to help.
* If you have any technical issues while filling out this form, please contact us at your earliest convenience.
