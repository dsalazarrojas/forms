# Wellness Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Wellness Coaching Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your current habits and preferences related to exercise, nutrition, sleep, and motivation. The information gathered will help you better understand your wellness status and set goals for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the options that best apply to you.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions (indicated by a asterisk *).
4. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (id: 2, type: email, required: false): This field is where you can provide your email address to help us get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Availability** (id: 3, type: time, required: false): This field asks about your available time for exercise and other activities.
* **Medical Conditions** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): Please note any medical conditions that may impact your wellness habits.
* **Exercise Frequency** (id: 5, type: select_one, required: false): Select the time of day when you usually exercise: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Never.
* **Sleep Duration** (id: 6, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose the duration of sleep you get: 7-8 hours, 8-10 hours, 10-12 hours, Less than 7 hours, or More than 12 hours.
* **Nutrition** (id: 7, type: select_one, required: true): Please select your current diet type: Vegetarian, Vegan, Omnivore, or None of the above.
* **Goals** (id: 8, type: text, required: false): Share your short-term and long-term goals for improvement.
* **Exercise Facilities** (id: 9, type: select_multiple, required: false): Check the facilities where you usually exercise: Home, Studio, Gym, or Outdoor.
* **Stress Management** (id: 10, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the strategies you use for stress management: Mindfulness, Journaling, Meditation, Exercise, or Other.
* **Exercise Preference** (id: 11, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of exercise you enjoy: Cardio, Strength, Endurance, Flexibility, or Other.
* **Motivation** (id: 12, type: select_one, required: false): Please assess your motivation level: Motivated, Not Motivated.
* **Exercise Familiarity** (id: 13, type: select_multiple, required: false): Evaluate your exercise experience: High, Medium, Low.
* **Social Support** (id: 14, type: select_multiple, required: false): Check the social support you have for your exercise routine: Family, Friends, Colleagues, or None.
* **Exercise Experience** (id: 15, type: select_one, required: false): Please assess your exercise experience: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced.
* **Goals Duration** (id: 16, type: select_multiple, required: true): Please select the duration of your goals: Short-term, Long-term, Both.
* **Sleep Quality** (id: 17, type: select_multiple, required: true): Evaluate the quality of your sleep: Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Nutrition Planning** (id: 18, type: select_one, required: false): Please select whether you have a nutrition plan: True, False.
* **Exercise Facility Preference** (id: 19, type: select_multiple, required: true): Choose your preferred exercise facility: Home, Studio, Gym, Outdoor.
* **Exercise Facility Availability** (id: 20, type: select_multiple, required: false): Check the availability of your preferred exercise facility: True, False.
* **Motivation** (id: 21, type: text, required: false): Share any thoughts or feelings about your motivation.
* **Exercise Schedule** (id: 22, type: select_multiple, required: false): Please select whether you have an exercise schedule: True, False.
* **Nutrition Facility Preference** (id: 23, type: select_multiple, required: true): Choose your preferred nutrition facility: Home, Online, Store, None.
* **Nutrition Facility Availability** (id: 24, type: select_multiple, required: false): Check the availability of your preferred nutrition facility: True, False.
