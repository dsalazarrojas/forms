# share_your_recovery_journey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The share_your_recovery_journey form is designed to collect information about your recovery journey, including your story, recovery insights, and experiences with organization support and healing supports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Be honest and genuine in your responses.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **share_your_recovery_story** (`story`, `text`, required: false): Share your personal story of recovery, including any challenges you faced and how you overcame them.
* **share_your_recovery_insights** (`recovery_insights`, `text`, required: false): Share any insights or takeaways you gained from your recovery journey.
* **share_your_recovery_permissions** (`permissions`, `text`, required: false): Share any permissions or licenses you have received as part of your recovery process.
