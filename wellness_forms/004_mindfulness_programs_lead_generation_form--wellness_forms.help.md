# Mindfulness Programs Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mindfulness Programs Lead Generation Form is a tool used to capture information from potential leads interested in mindfulness programs. This form helps our team gather essential details to follow up on potential leads and ensure that we're targeting the right audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Name** field with your full name.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the format `example@example.com`.
3. Enter your **Phone** number (optional).
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (if required).
5. Select your **Interested Programs** from the list provided.
6. Enter your **Preferred Location** (optional).
7. Write any additional **Notes** you'd like to mention (optional).
8. Choose your **Lead Source** from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This field requires your full name. Please enter your name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone**: This field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Date of Birth**: If required, please enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., 1990-01-01).
* **Interested Programs**: Select one or more programs that interest you. Options include:
	+ Mindfulness Programs
	+ Yoga Programs
	+ Meditation Programs
	+ Wellness Programs
	+ Other (Please specify in the **Notes** field)
* **Preferred Location**: If you're interested in programs available in a specific location, please mention it here. (Optional)
* **Notes**: Add any additional information you'd like to share about your interest in mindfulness programs. (Optional)
* **Lead Source**: Where did you hear about our mindfulness programs? Choose an option from the provided list. 

Note: All fields marked as optional are not required for you to fill out, but please do so if applicable.
