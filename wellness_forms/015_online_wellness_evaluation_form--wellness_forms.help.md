# Online Wellness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Online Wellness Evaluation Form is to collect information about your health history and wellness goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all the questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information.
3. Make sure to review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <label>Health History</label> (type: text, <required/optional>: false)
* This field is used to collect information about your past medical history, including any illnesses, injuries, or conditions you may have experienced.

### 2. <label>Wellness Goals</label> (type: text, <required/optional>: false)
* This field is used to understand what you would like to achieve in terms of your overall wellness and fitness.

### 3. <label>Specific Concerns</label> (type: text, <required/optional>: false)
* This field is used to gather information about any specific concerns you may have about your health or wellness.

### 4. <label>Contact Info</label> (type: text, <required/optional>: false)
* This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name, email address, or phone number, so we can get in touch with you regarding your progress or any questions you may have.

### 5. <label>Medical History</label> (type: text, <required/optional>: false)
* This field is used to gather any additional information about your medical history, including any surgeries, medications, or other relevant health information.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for internal use only and your information will be kept confidential.
* Please review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
* You can use any format to answer the questions, but please be concise and clear in your responses. 

Note: This form is designed to be simple and easy to use, but if you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask and we will be happy to guide you further.
