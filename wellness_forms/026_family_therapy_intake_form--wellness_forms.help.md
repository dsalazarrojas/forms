# Family Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Therapy Intake Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information from family members seeking therapy services. This form helps us understand your family dynamics, goals, and preferences, allowing us to tailor our services to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all required fields carefully, as they are marked with an *asterisk*.
2. Select the correct options for each field, and make sure to save your progress frequently.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Information** (1) (*Required*): This field is used to collect general information about your family, such as names, ages, and relationships. Please provide accurate and up-to-date details.
* **Contact Information** (2) (*Required*): Enter your contact details, including phone numbers and email addresses, so we can reach out to you for scheduling and updates.
* **Relationship Status** (3) (*Required*): Choose your relationship status from the options provided, as this helps us understand your family dynamics.
	+ Single: You are single and not in a committed relationship.
	+ Married: You are in a monogamous relationship.
	+ Divorced: You are divorced or separated.
	+ In a Domestic Partnership: You are in a domestic partnership, such as a civil union or common-law marriage.
* **Children** (4) (*Required*): Select the correct number of children you have, as this helps us tailor our services to your family's needs.
	+ None: You do not have any children.
	+ 1-2: You have one or two children.
	+ 3-4: You have three or four children.
	+ 5 or More: You have five or more children.
* **Therapist Preferences** (5) (*Optional*): Choose the preferences for your therapist, such as male or female.
* **Therapy Goals** (6) (*Optional*): Describe your therapy goals, such as coping with stress or improving relationships.
* **Therapy Frequency** (7) (*Optional*): Select the frequency of your therapy sessions, such as daily or bi-weekly.
* **Therapy Duration** (8) (*Optional*): Choose the duration of your therapy sessions, such as 1-2 weeks or 1-3 months.
* **Payment Information** (9) (*Optional*): Select your preferred payment method, such as insurance or self-pay.
* **Additional Information** (10) (*Optional*): Provide any additional details that might be helpful for us to know, such as medical conditions or scheduling constraints.
* **Therapist Availability** (11) (*Optional*): Confirm whether your therapist is available for sessions or not.

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the Family Therapy Intake Form with confidence, ensuring you provide the necessary information for our team to tailor our services to your family's specific needs. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
