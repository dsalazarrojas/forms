# yogaConsultationForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather personal and medical information to provide a safe and effective yoga practice experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in each field.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information** (`personalInformation`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect your name and contact information. Please provide your full name and a contact method (e.g., email or phone number) so that we can reach you after the form is submitted.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medicalConditions`, `text`, required: false): Please list any medical conditions you are aware of that may affect your practice (e.g., injury, medical condition, or allergy).
* **Previous Yoga Experience** (`previousYogaExperience`, `text`, required: false): Share your past experience with yoga, including any notable practices or teachers you have worked with.
* **Goals and Objectives** (`goalsAndObjectives`, `text`, required: false): Describe your goals and objectives for starting or continuing your yoga practice (e.g., stress relief, flexibility, or improving physical health).
* **Medical Clearance** (`medicalClearance`, `text`, required: false): If you have any medical clearance or documentation from a healthcare professional, please attach it here or explain the context.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergencyContact`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of a person we can contact in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough when completing this form to ensure a safe and effective yoga experience.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your form submission and contact you to discuss your practice details further.
