# Student Daily Wellness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Daily Wellness Assessment Form is a tool used by students to report their daily wellness status to the wellness team. This form helps the team assess the students' overall well-being and provide support when needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your Student Name in the Student Name field.
* Enter your Student ID in the Student ID field.
* Select the Date of the assessment.
* Enter the Time of the assessment.
* Provide a detailed response to the Wellness Questions.
* Add any Comments or Notes if needed.
* Enter your Email address in the Email field.
* Optional: Add any additional comments or information in the Comments field.
* Optional: Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Student Name
* A field to enter your name as a student.
### 2. Student ID
* A field to enter your student ID number.
### 3. Date
* The date of the assessment.
### 4. Time
* The time of the assessment.
### 5. Wellness Questions
* A space to write down any thoughts, feelings, or concerns you may have about your well-being.
### 6. Comments
* An optional field for you to add any additional comments or information about your well-being.
### 7. Email
* A field to enter your Email address.
### 8. Phone
* An optional field to enter your phone number.
### 9. Note
* An optional field for any additional notes or comments.
 
### Tips:
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in the required fields (Student Name, Student ID, Date, Time, and Email).
* Be as detailed as possible in the Wellness Questions section to help the wellness team understand your well-being.
* Use the Comments and Note fields to provide any additional context or information that may be helpful for the wellness team to understand your situation.
