# Holistic Health Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Health Awareness Survey is a form designed to gather information from users on their personal and demographic details, as well as their perceptions of their well-being, stress levels, and self-care habits.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with your honest and accurate answers.
- Make sure to select the correct options for each question.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to contact the administrator for further clarification.
- Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (Page 1)
    * **Personal Details** (`demographic_details`, Text, Optional): This section is where you can enter your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Assess Your Wellbeing** (Page 2)
    * **Stress Levels** (`stress_score`, Number, Optional): This question asks you to rate your current stress levels, with High, Low, and Moderate being the possible options.
* **Stress Management** (Page 3)
    * **Stress Management** (`stress_management`, Select One, Optional): This question asks you to select your level of stress management, with High, Low, and Moderate being the possible options.
* **Mindfulness Practices** (Page 4)
    * **Mindfulness Practices** (`mindfulness_practices`, Select Multiple, Required): This section asks you to select your mindfulness practices, which can include Yoga, Meditation, Exercise, and Sleep.
* **Self-Care Routine** (Page 5)
    * **Self-Care Routine** (`self_care_routine`, Select Multiple, Required): This section asks you to select your self-care routine, which can include Morning Meditation, Evening Journaling, Healthy Eating, and Social Connection.
* **Health Status** (Page 6)
    * **Emotional Health** (`emotional_health`, Number, Optional): This question asks you to rate your emotional health, with the options being 1-10.
* **Physical Health** (Page 7)
    * **Physical Health** (`physical_health`, Number, Optional): This question asks you to rate your physical health, with the options being 1-10.
* **Mental Health** (Page 8)
    * **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, Number, Optional): This question asks you to rate your mental health, with the options being 1-10.
* **Spiritual Health** (Page 9)
    * **Spiritual Health** (`spiritual_health`, Number, Optional): This question asks you to rate your spiritual health, with the options being 1-10.
* **Overall Health** (Page 10)
    * **Overall Health** (`overall_health`, Number, Optional): This question asks you to rate your overall health, with the options being 1-10.
* **Demographic Details** (Page 11)
    * **Gender** (`demographic_gender`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter your gender.
    * **Age** (`demographic_age`, Number, Optional): This question asks you to enter your age.
    * **Country of Residence** (`demographic_country`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter your country of residence.
    * **Occupation** (`demographic_occupation`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter your occupation.
    * **Marital Status** (`demographic_marital_status`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter your marital status.
    * **Income** (`demographic_income`, Number, Optional): This question asks you to enter your income.
    * **Hobbies** (`demographic_hobbies`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter your hobbies.
    * **Education Level** (`demographic_education`, Select Multiple, Required): This question asks you to select your education level, with Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, and Post-Graduate being the possible options.
    * **Living Arrangement** (`demographic_living_arrangement`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter your living arrangement.
    * **Family Status** (`demographic_family_status`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter your family status.
    * **Language** (`demographic_language`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter your language.
    * **Religion** (`demographic_religion`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter your religion.
    * **Other Hobbies** (`demographic_hobbies_other`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter any other hobbies.
    * **Comments** (`demographic_comments`, Text, Optional): This question asks you to enter any comments you might have.
