# Daily Health Symptom Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's health symptoms on a daily basis. It is designed to be completed by users at any given time, and it is expected that users will complete this form on a daily basis for a continuous period.

## How To Complete This Form

*   This form is divided into several sections. Each section is designed to gather specific information about your health symptoms.
*   Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
*   For each question, you are asked to select one or multiple options that best describe your symptoms.
*   You are also asked to provide additional information in the user input fields. This is where you can provide any other relevant information that you feel is important for your health.
