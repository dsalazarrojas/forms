# Evening Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Evening Reflection Survey is a self-assessment tool designed to help you reflect on your daily experiences, mood, and stress levels. It is a personal exercise to gain insights into your emotional well-being and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question based on your experiences and feelings from the current day.
2. Select the option that best describes your morning mood and current stress level.
3. If you experienced stress, describe the cause(s) in the text field provided.
4. Rate your day using the provided scale (or describe your rating in the text field).
5. If you have any comments or suggestions, please share them in the text field provided.
