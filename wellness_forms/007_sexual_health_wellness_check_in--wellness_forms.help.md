# Sexual Health Wellness Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your current sexual health and well-being. Please answer each question honestly to help you better understand your current situation and identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to you.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to our team for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sexual Health Topic** (`select_one`, not required): This question asks about the topic you are most concerned about. Please select one of the options that best fits your concerns.
	+ Hormonal imbalances: Hormonal imbalances can affect many aspects of your health, including your mood, energy levels, and sexual health.
	+ Sexual desire: This refers to your interest and enthusiasm for sex.
	+ Sexual orientation: This refers to your sexual identity or the gender you are attracted to.
	+ Body image: This refers to your perception of your body and its appearance.
	+ Other: If none of the above options apply to you, select "Other" and describe your concerns in the comments section below.
* **Who do you usually have sex with?** (`select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your sexual partners. Select all that apply.
	+ Men: You usually have sex with men.
	+ Women: You usually have sex with women.
	+ Both: You usually have sex with both men and women.
	+ Not sure: If you are unsure or identify as non-binary, please select "Not sure".
	+ Other: If none of the above options apply to you, select "Other" and describe your concerns in the comments section below.
* **With whom do you usually have sex?** (`text`, not required): This question asks about your current sexual partners. Please enter the names or initials of your partners.
* **What is your current sexual health?** (`select_one`, not required): This question asks about your current sexual health. Please select one of the options that best describes your situation.
	+ Good: You are currently experiencing good sexual health.
	+ Fair: You are experiencing some concerns or issues with your sexual health.
	+ Poor: You are currently experiencing poor sexual health.
* **Follow-up** (`select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your follow-up preferences. Select all that apply.
	+ Yes: You would like to schedule a follow-up.
	+ No: You do not want to schedule a follow-up.
	+ Not sure: If you are unsure, please select "Not sure".
* **Comments** (`text`, not required): This section is for any additional comments or concerns you would like to share with us.
* **Phone number** (`text`, not required): Please enter your phone number for follow-up contact.

## Tips
* Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to our team for support.
* This form should only be filled out once a month to help track your progress and identify areas for improvement.
