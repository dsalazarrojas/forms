# yoga_instructor_estimate_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help yoga instructors estimate their business needs and preferences for services. It's intended for internal use by your organization to gather information about your services and pricing. Please complete this form accurately to ensure your information is up-to-date and helpful for our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by carefully reading the instructions and purpose of this form.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the provided fields to input your information accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pricing** (`pricing`, number, required: false): This field is for estimating your pricing for your services. Enter a specific number to provide a clear and concise estimate.
* **Yoga Services** (`yoga_services`, text, required: false): Describe the services you offer, such as private sessions, group classes, workshops, or special events.
* **Services Rendered** (`services_rendered`, text, required: false): List the specific services you render, such as yoga instruction, meditation, or other activities.
* **Services Description** (`services_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your services, including any unique features or qualifications.
* **Pricing Frequency** (`pricing_frequency`, select_one, options: [Yes, No], required: false): Check if you want to provide pricing estimates on a frequent basis (yes) or not (no).
* **Pricing Duration** (`pricing_duration`, select_one, options: [Yes, No], required: false): Check if you want to provide pricing estimates for short or long durations (yes/no).
* **Pricing Frequency (Other)** (`pricing_frequency_other`, text, required: false): If your pricing frequency is not included in the previous options, describe your pricing frequency in this field.
