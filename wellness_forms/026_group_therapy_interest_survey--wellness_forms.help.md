# Group Therapy Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Therapy Interest Survey is designed to gather information about your preferences and interests for group therapy sessions. This will help us tailor the experience to your needs and provide a suitable environment for you to participate in.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form completely, providing all the required information.
2.  For each question, choose the answer that best represents your preference or interest.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (Page 1)
* **Participant Information**: Please enter your name and any other contact information as requested.
   (`participant_info`, `text`, optional): Provide your name and any additional contact information.

### What are you interested in? (Page 1)
* **What are you interested in?**: Select all the areas of therapy that interest you.
(`therapy_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from the options provided to indicate your interested areas of therapy.

### Preferred Time (Page 1)
* **Preferred time**: Please select your preferred time for the therapy session.
(`preferred_time`, `time`, required): Choose your preferred time for the session.

### Preferred Date (Page 2)
* **Preferred date**: Please select your preferred date for the therapy session.
(`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Choose your preferred date for the session.

### Which areas of therapy interest you? (Page 2)
* **Which areas of therapy interest you?**: Select all the areas of therapy that interest you.
(`therapist_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from the options provided to indicate your interested areas of therapy.

### Group size (Page 3)
* **Group size**: Please select the preferred size of the group.
(`group_size`, `number`, required): Choose the group size you prefer.

### Session Frequency (Page 3)
* **Session frequency**: Please select the frequency of your preferred therapy sessions.
(`session_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred session frequency.

### Therapist Gender (Page 4)
* **Therapist gender**: Please select your preference for therapist gender.
(`therapist_gender`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred therapist gender.

### Therapist Experience (Page 4)
* **Therapist experience**: Please select your preference for therapist experience.
(`therapist_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred therapist experience.

### Payment Method (Page 5)
* **Payment method**: Please select your preferred payment method.
(`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method.

### Availability (Page 5)
* **Availability**: Please select your availability for the therapy sessions.
(`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your availability for the sessions.

### Location (Page 6)
* **Location**: Please select the preferred location for the therapy sessions.
(`location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your preferred location for the sessions.

### Other Information (Page 7)
* **Other**: Please provide any additional information you would like to share.
(`other_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any other information you would like to share.

### Tips
1.  Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2.  Select the options that best represent your preferences.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
