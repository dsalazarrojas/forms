# Personal Trainer Injury Waiver And Media Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a personal trainer's consent and media release from clients before they start training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "Waiver And Media Consent" section with your name and a brief statement agreeing to allow the photographer and videographer to take photographs and videos of you during your workout sessions.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and date of birth.
3. Provide your emergency contact person's information, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waiver And Media Consent**: This is the first section of the form where you need to provide a brief statement agreeing to allow the photographer and videographer to take photographs and videos of you during your workout sessions.
* **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History**: This section is optional and should be filled out if you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to exercise or participate in the training sessions.
* **Injury Waiver**: Please acknowledge that you understand that exercise and physical activity can be hazardous to your health and that you are aware of the risks involved.
* **Consent To Use Media**: Select one of the two options: "I agree to allow the photographer and videographer to take photographs and videos of me during my workout sessions" or "I do not agree to allow the photographer and videographer to take photographs and videos of me during my workout sessions".
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification.
* **Emergency Contact Person**: Please enter the name and phone number of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact person (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Child, Spouse).
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Note**: This section is optional and can be used to provide additional information about your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Note 2**: This section is optional and can be used to provide additional information about your emergency contact person.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Date Of Signature**: Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Time Of Signature**: Enter the time you sign the form.
* **Trainer Signature**: The trainer will sign and date this section to confirm their agreement to provide training services.
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the trainer.
* **Trainer Title**: Enter the title of the trainer (e.g., Personal Trainer, Coach).
* **Trainer Employer**: Enter the name of the trainer's employer.
* **Trainer License**: This section is optional and can be used to provide additional information about the trainer's license.

Note: Please review each section carefully and make sure to complete it accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your personal trainer for assistance.
