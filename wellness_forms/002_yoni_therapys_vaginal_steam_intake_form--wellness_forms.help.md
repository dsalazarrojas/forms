# yoni_therapys_vaginal_steam_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The yoni_therapys_vaginal_steam_intake_form is used to collect patient information and treatment preferences for Yoni Therapy, a vaginal steam treatment. This form helps the treatment provider understand the patient's medical history, treatment history, and personal preferences to provide the most effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each field to ensure accurate completion.
2. Provide all required information to ensure accurate treatment.
3. Double-check your email and phone number for correct spelling and formatting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): 
This field is for you to provide a brief introduction about your medical condition, symptoms, or any previous medical history you may have.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): 
Provide a detailed description of any medical conditions or procedures you have undergone, including surgeries, medications, or previous diagnoses.
* **Treatment History** (`treatment_history`, text, required: false): 
Describe any previous treatments, therapies, or interventions you have received for your medical condition.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false):
Check all applicable boxes if you have any allergies or sensitivities, such as latex, medications, or materials.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required: false):
Indicate if you have any of the following medical conditions:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Treatment Frequency** (`treatment_frequency`, text, required: false): 
Please describe how frequently you would like to receive treatments.
* **Steam Settings** (`steam_settings`, select_one, required: true): 
Select the desired steam setting for your treatment:
	+ Steam
	+ Low Pressure
	+ High Pressure
	+ Vaginal Steam
	+ Other (please describe)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): 
Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share with your treatment provider.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): 
Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): 
This field is not used for patient data but rather as a placeholder for submission. Do not provide any input.
