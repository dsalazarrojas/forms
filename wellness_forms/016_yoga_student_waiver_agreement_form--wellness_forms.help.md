# Yoga Student Waiver Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students participating in yoga classes, including their emergency contact details, medical information, and waiver details. This form is designed to ensure that students are aware of their responsibilities and rights while participating in yoga activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide your emergency contact information, including their name and contact details.
3. Share your medical information, including any health conditions or allergies.
4. Read and agree to the waiver terms, which outline your responsibilities and the risks associated with participating in yoga classes.
5. Provide any additional emergency details, such as the contact information for an emergency contact.
6. Fill in your student information, including your student ID number or other relevant details.
7. Provide information about your instructor or class details, including the name of your instructor and class type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (page 1)**: Enter your name and contact information to let us know who you are and how we can contact you.
* **Emergency Contact Information (page 2)**: Provide the name and contact details of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Information (page 3)**: Share any health conditions or allergies that might affect your participation in yoga activities.
* **Waiver Information (page 4)**: Read and agree to the waiver terms, which outline your responsibilities and the risks associated with participating in yoga classes.
* **Emergency Details (page 5)**: If you have any special emergency instructions, provide them here.
* **Student Information (page 6)**: Enter your student ID number or other relevant details to help us keep track of your student information.
* **Instructor Information (page 7)**: Provide information about your instructor, including their name and class type, to help us keep track of your class schedule.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in your participant information accurately to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
* Provide clear and up-to-date emergency contact information to help us reach you in case of an emergency.
* Share any medical information that might affect your participation in yoga activities, so we can provide proper care and attention.
* Read and understand the waiver terms before agreeing to them.
* Keep your student information up-to-date to ensure you receive important class schedules and updates.
