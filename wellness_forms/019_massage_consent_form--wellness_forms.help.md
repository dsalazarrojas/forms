# Massage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Consent Form is designed to collect information from clients regarding their consent for massage therapy sessions. This form helps ensure that clients understand and agree to the treatment process, which is essential for providing safe and effective massage therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information on the "Client Information" page.
2. Enter the details of your massage session on the "Massage Details" page.
3. Read and agree to the consent terms on the "Consent" page. Select 'Yes' to confirm your consent.
4. Provide any additional contact information or notes on the "Contact" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information** (`Client Information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
- **Massage Details** (`Massage Details`, text, required/optional): Describe the massage session you are interested in, including date, time, and any specific areas of focus.
- **Consent** (`Consent`, select_one, required/optional): Read and agree to the provided consent terms. Select 'Yes' to confirm your consent.
  Please note that this field is not intended to be checked for completeness or correctness; rather, it is designed to ensure you have read and agreed to the terms.
- **Contact** (`Contact`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information, such as email or phone number, so we can stay in touch with you about your massage session. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have read and understood all the consent terms before selecting 'Yes' on the "Consent" page.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
- We will contact you regarding your massage session according to the contact information provided.
- If you need to make any changes to your massage session, please let us know as soon as possible.
