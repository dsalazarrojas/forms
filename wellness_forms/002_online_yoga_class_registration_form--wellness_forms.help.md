# online_yoga_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects user information for an online yoga class program. Fill out the form to get personalized class schedule recommendations and stay updated on class schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts and fill out each field accordingly. Each field will guide you through the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, not required): Please provide your name and any other information you're comfortable sharing about yourself.
* **Class Info** (`class_info`, text, not required): Let us know what type of class you are interested in (e.g. Hatha, Vinyasa, Restorative, etc.).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Share your email and/or phone number so we can send you updates about your class schedule.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, not required): Specify your preferred time slots for the online class (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, not required): Any additional comments or questions you'd like to share about your interests or needs.
* **Payment** (`payment`, text, not required): Although not required, feel free to include any questions or concerns you may have about payment options.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, not required): This is a text field for agreement; we are assuming it's here to get you to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Agreement** ( duplicate of `agreement`, text, not required): This field is a duplicate of the previous one and is here to get you to agree to our terms and conditions again.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, not required): This is a text field for you to review and agree to the terms and conditions of the online yoga class program.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click this button to submit your registration form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required information for our follow-up email with your personalized class schedule.
* You can always reach out to us for any questions or clarifications.
