# iv_therapy_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for IV therapy sessions. It's essential to fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that your treatment needs are properly assessed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Information (page 1)**: Provide your basic contact and demographic information.
2. **Contact Details (page 2)**: Enter your contact details, including phone number and email.
3. **Medical History (pages 3-20)**: Provide detailed information about your medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or allergies. There are 18 fields to collect comprehensive information.
4. **Medical Allergies (page 21)**: List any allergies you have.
5. **Medications (page 22)**: Report all medications you are currently taking.
6. **Medical Tests (page 23)**: Provide information on any recent medical tests or screenings.
7. **Medical History 2-20 (pages 24-41)**: Continue providing detailed information about your medical history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (patient_info, text, required)**: Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information.
	+ This field is required and should be filled in accurately.
* **Contact Details (contact_details, text, required)**: Enter your phone number and email.
	+ This field is required and will help us contact you.
* **Medical History (medical_history, text, required)**: Describe any previous illnesses, injuries, or medical conditions.
	+ Be as detailed as possible, including dates and outcomes.
* **Medical Allergies (medical_allergies, text, required)**: List all allergies you have, including any medications or substances that trigger reactions.
	+ Include any known allergies.
* **Medications (medications, text, required)**: List all medications you're currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
	+ Include any prescription and non-prescription medications.
* **Medical Tests (medical_tests, text, required)**: Provide information on recent medical tests, including dates and results.
	+ Include any ongoing tests or screenings.
* **Medical History 2-20 (medical_history2-20, text, required)**: Continue providing detailed information about your medical history, with each field asking about a specific aspect of your medical history.

Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns.
