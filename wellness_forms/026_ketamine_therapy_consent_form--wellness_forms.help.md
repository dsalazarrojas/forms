# Ketamine Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ketamine Therapy Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent for ketamine therapy from patients. This form will collect necessary information to ensure the patient's safety and well-being during the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's information section with your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide details about your treatment, including the type and frequency of therapy.
3. Indicate your consent for the treatment by selecting the "Consent" option.
4. List any emergency contacts and their contact information.
5. Disclose any medical history, allergies, and medical conditions.
6. Include any relevant medical procedure information.
7. Add any additional patient notes that may be helpful for your clinician.
8. Provide information about your clinician, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect basic patient information such as name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
  - **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide details about the treatment, including the type and frequency of therapy.
  - **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to indicate whether you consent to the treatment.
  - **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required/optional): This field is used to list any emergency contacts and their contact information.
  - **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): This field is used to disclose any medical history that may be relevant to your treatment.
  - **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, required/optional): This field is used to list any medication allergies that you may have.
  - **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required/optional): This field is used to disclose any medical conditions that you may have.
  - **Medical Procedure Info** (`medical_procedure_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide information about any relevant medical procedures.
  - **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, note, required/optional): This field is used to add any additional patient notes that may be helpful for your clinician.
  - **Clinician Info** (`clinician_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide information about your clinician, if applicable.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- Ensure that you understand the consent process and the implications of the treatment.
- Review the form carefully before signing.
- Ask your clinician if you have any questions or concerns.
