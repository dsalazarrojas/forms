# Massage Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information and consent for massage therapy sessions. It's meant to be used by massage therapists, spas, or wellness centers to gather necessary details from clients before providing massage services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the Client Information section with your name and contact information.
* Provide details about the massage you are requesting in the Massage Information section.
* Read and sign the Consent section carefully to indicate your understanding of the massage therapy process and the terms of service.
* Sign and date the Client Signature section to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (client_info)**: Enter your name and contact information, including your phone number and email address. This is the first step in establishing communication with the massage therapist.
* **Massage Information (massage_info)**: Describe the type of massage you are requesting, including any specific areas of focus or techniques you are interested in. This helps the massage therapist understand your needs and preferences.
* **Consent (consent)**: Please read this section carefully and sign it if you understand and agree to the massage therapy process and terms of service. This is a crucial step to ensure we can provide you with safe and effective services.
* **Client Signature (client_signature)**: Sign and date this section to confirm your consent and agreement to the terms outlined in the Consent section.
