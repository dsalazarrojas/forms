# Personal Clarity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's personal clarity, which is a measure of one's sense of direction, motivation, and alignment with their values and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question or select an option that best describes your thoughts, feelings, and experiences. You can complete the form at your own pace and in your own time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
