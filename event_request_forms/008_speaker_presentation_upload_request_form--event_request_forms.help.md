# speaker presentation upload request form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Presentation Upload Request Form is a tool used by speakers to submit their presentation files and associated event information for an upcoming event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Speaker Information section with your details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the Upload Presentation section, select the presentation file you want to upload and fill in the File Name field with the name of the file.
3. In the Session Details section, fill in the Event Date, Event Time, and Event Name fields with the relevant information for the event where your presentation will be held.
4. In the File Upload section, select the file type of your presentation (e.g. PDF, DOCX, PPT, MP4, MP3) and fill in the File Size field with the size of the file.
5. Review and confirm the details in the Upload Status, Event Status, and Session Status fields to ensure they match your expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Information**: This section is used to collect your contact information and presentation title.
	+ **Speaker Name**: Your name as the speaker.
	+ **Speaker Email**: Your email address, where we can contact you for any updates or queries.
	+ **Speaker Phone**: Your phone number, where we can contact you if needed.
* **Upload Presentation**: This section is used to select and upload your presentation file.
	+ **File**: Upload your presentation file in one of the supported formats (PDF, DOCX, PPT, MP4, MP3).
* **Session Details**: This section is used to provide more information about the event and session where your presentation will be held.
	+ **Event Date**: The date of the event where your presentation will be held.
	+ **Event Time**: The time of the event where your presentation will be held.
	+ **Event Name**: The name of the event where your presentation will be held.
	+ **Session Name**: The name of the session where your presentation will be held.
* **File Upload**: This section is used to select and upload your presentation file.
	+ **File Type**: The type of your presentation file (PDF, DOCX, PPT, MP4, MP3).
	+ **File Name**: The name of your presentation file.
	+ **File Size**: The size of your presentation file.
* **Upload Status, Event Status, and Session Status**: Review and confirm the status of your presentation upload, the status of the event, and the status of the session to ensure they match your expectations.
	+ **Upload Status**: Success or Failure.
	+ **Event Status**: Open or Closed.
	+ **Session Status**: Active or Inactive.
