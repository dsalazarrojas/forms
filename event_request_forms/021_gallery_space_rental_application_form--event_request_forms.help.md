# gallery_space_rental_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gallery space rental application form is used to submit a request for space rental in the gallery. Fill out this form to share your event details, schedule, and contact information. This form is used by gallery staff to review and consider your event request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information in each field.
2. Make sure to submit your form before the event date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): The date of the event you wish to hold in the gallery.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): The time of day you wish to start your event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): The time of day you wish to end your event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): The name of your event. This could be a meeting, performance, exhibit, or other activities.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your event, including any specific requirements or requests.
* **Scheduling Details** (`scheduling_details`, text, required/optional): Share any scheduling information that is specific to your event, such as setup and teardown times.
* **Planning Information** (`planning_information`, text, required/optional): Share any relevant planning information that is necessary to complete your event.
* **Applicant Approvals** (`applicant_approvals`, text, required/optional): If you need any special approvals, please list them here.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for your event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): This is the final step to submit your form.
