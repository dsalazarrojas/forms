# cultural_exchange_performance_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "cultural_exchange_performance_consent_form" is a form designed to collect participant details, emergency contacts, and signatures for a cultural exchange performance event. It helps ensure that all participants have provided their consent and necessary contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Complete each field as prompted:
	* Enter your participant details in the "Participant Details" section.
	* Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contacts" section.
	* Select your role(s) in the "Roles" section.
	* Choose your signature(s) in the "Signatures" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): This section is for you to enter your personal details, such as name, email, and contact number.

### Emergency Contacts
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false): In case of an emergency, please provide contact information for at least one emergency contact person.

### Roles
* **Roles** (`roles`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the roles that apply to your participation in the event. This includes Dancer, Musician, and Teacher.

### Signatures
* **Signature 1** (`participant_signature`, select_one, required: false): Please select your signature from the options provided.
* **Signature 2** (`participant_signature_2`, select_one, required: false): Select a second signature if applicable.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided signatures for your consent.

### Emergency Contact 1
* **Emergency Contact 1** (`emergency_contact_details`, note, required: false): Provide contact information for one emergency contact person, including name, email, and contact number.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_details_2`, note, required: false): Provide contact information for a second emergency contact person, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your form carefully before submission.
* Ensure you have provided all necessary information and signatures.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us at contact email.
