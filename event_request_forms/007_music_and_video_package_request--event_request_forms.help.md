# Music And Video Package Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music And Video Package Request form is designed to collect information for creating a customized music and video package for upcoming events. This form is meant for clients to submit their requirements and preferences for their event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the event details, such as date, time, and location.
* Select the package selections that best fit your event.
* Choose your preferred music and video types (e.g., pop, rock, jazz).
* Enter any additional contact information to ensure we can reach out to you.
* Add any notes or comments about your event or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your event, including any relevant details.

### Package Selections
* **Package Selections** (`package_selections`, select multiple, optional): Choose from the available package options:
	+ Basic Package
	+ Premium Package
	+ Deluxe Package

### Song Preferences
* **Song Preferences** (`song_preferences`, select multiple, optional): Choose your preferred music genres:
	+ Pop
	+ Rock
	+ Jazz

### Video Preferences
* **Video Preferences** (`video_preferences`, select multiple, optional): Choose your preferred video types:
	+ Live
	+ Recorded
	+ None

### Event Date
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your event (if known).

### Event Time
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your event (if known).

### Client Preferences
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or preferences you have for your event.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, number, optional): Enter your contact number.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your event or preferences.
