# Artist Dressing Room Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the artist's dressing room at an event. The form asks for your contact information, event details, access type, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth and secure access experience for the artist.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your full name.
2. Choose your role at the event.
3. Provide your email address and contact phone number (if applicable).
4. Enter the event name, date, and performance time.
5. Select the type of access you need.
6. Choose how long you need access for.
7. Explain why you need access to the artist's dressing room.
8. If you're part of a group, list the names and roles of others in your group.
9. Confirm that you've been security cleared, have valid ID, and agree to maintain confidentiality.
10. Confirm that you've reviewed and agreed to follow the venue's policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`requester_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Role or Position** (`requester_role`): Choose your role or position at the event (e.g. Artist, Band Member, Manager or Agent, etc.).
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`): Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event or show.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`): Enter the scheduled performance time.
* **Type of Access Requested** (`access_type`): Choose the type of access you need to the artist's dressing room (e.g. Artist dressing room access, Green room access, etc.).
* **Duration of Access Needed** (`access_duration`): Choose how long you need access for (e.g. 1-2 hours before performance, Full day of event, etc.).
* **Reason for Access Request** (`reason_for_access`): Explain why you need access to the artist's dressing room.
* **Are You Part of a Group?** (`is_group`): Choose whether you're part of a group or not.
* **Group Size** (`group_size`): Enter the total number of people in your group (if applicable).
* **Group Member Names and Roles** (`group_members`): List the names and roles of others in your group (if applicable).
* **Have You Been Security Cleared?** (`security_clearance`): Confirm that you've been security cleared.
* **Do You Have Valid Identification?** (`credentials_provided`): Confirm whether you have valid ID or if you'll provide it at the event.
* **I Acknowledge Dressing Room Access Policies** (`policy_acknowledgment`): Confirm that you agree to follow the venue's policies.
* **I Agree to Maintain Confidentiality** (`confidentiality_agreement`): Confirm that you agree to maintain confidentiality.
* **Date of This Request** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you're submitting this request.

Note: This form is designed to be completed online, but if printed, please ensure that all relevant sections are filled out clearly and thoroughly.
