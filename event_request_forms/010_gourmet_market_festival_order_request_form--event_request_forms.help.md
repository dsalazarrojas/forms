# gourmet_market_festival_order_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about orders for a Gourmet Market Festival. It is designed to gather details about the order, including pickup or delivery information, payment, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Information** in the first field.
2. If you have pickup information, enter it in the second field.
3. If you have any additional order information, enter it in the third field.
4. If you need to provide delivery information, enter it in the fourth field.
5. Select your **Order Type** (Pickup or Delivery) in the fifth field.
6. Enter your **Order Time**, **Order Date**, and **Order Start Time** and **Order End Time** in the sixth, seventh, eighth, and ninth fields respectively.
7. Enter your **Delivery Address** in the tenth field.
8. Enter any **Vendor Information** in the eleventh field.
9. Add any additional **Notes** in the twelfth field.
10. Enter your **Order ID** and **Event Information** in the thirteenth and fourteenth fields respectively.
11. Add any **Contact Information** in the fifteenth field.
12. Enter any additional **Order Details** in the sixteenth field.
13. Enter any **Vendor Notes** in the seventeenth field.
14. Add any **Event Notes** in the eighteenth field.
15. Enter any additional **Payment Information** in the nineteenth field.
16. Enter any final **Notes 3** in the twentieth field.
17. Finally, enter any additional **Vendor Information 2** in the twenty-first field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (text, Required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Pickup Information** (text, Optional): If you are picking up your order, enter your pickup details here.
* **Order Information** (text, Optional): If you have any additional information about your order, enter it here.
* **Delivery Information** (text, Optional): If you are having your order delivered, enter your delivery information here.
* **Fulfillment** (text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your order fulfillment.
* **Payment Information** (text, Optional): Enter any payment information related to your order.
* **Order Details** (text, Optional): Enter any additional details about your order.
* **Notes** (text, Optional): Add any notes about your order.
* **Order Type** (Select One, Optional): Select whether you are picking up or having your order delivered.
* **Order Time** (Date, Optional): Enter the time you would like to order.
* **Order Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you would like to order.
* **Order Start Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the start time for your order.
* **Order End Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the end time for your order.
* **Delivery Address** (text, Optional): If you are having your order delivered, enter your address here.
* **Vendor Information** (text, Optional): Enter any information about the vendor or supplier.
* **Notes 2** (text, Optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Order ID** (text, Optional): Enter your order ID number.
* **Event Information** (text, Optional): Enter any information about the event related to your order.
* **Contact Information** (text, Optional): Enter any contact information for the person placing the order.
* **Order Details 2** (text, Optional): Enter any additional details about your order.
* **Vendor Notes** (text, Optional): Enter any notes from the vendor or supplier.
* **Event Notes** (text, Optional): Enter any notes about the event related to your order.
* **Payment Info 2** (text, Optional): Enter any additional payment information.
* **Notes 3** (text, Optional): Add any final notes about your order.
* **Vendor Info 2** (text, Optional): Enter any additional vendor information.
