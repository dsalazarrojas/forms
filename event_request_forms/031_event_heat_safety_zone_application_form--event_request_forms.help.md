# event_heat_safety_zone_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for event organizers to submit an application to create a heat safety zone for a specific event, ensuring the safety of attendees in case of extreme heat conditions.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the form's text field "event_request_form" on Page 1.
2.  On the same page, fill out the text fields "heat_relief_plans", "document_safety_measures", "site_maps", and "emergency_services" with the required information for your heat safety zone application.
3.  On the final page, fill out the text field "contact_info" to provide your contact details.
4.  Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "submit" button to finalize your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_request_form** (Page 1, Required): Enter a brief description of your event, including its name, date, location, and expected number of attendees.
* **heat_relief_plans** (Page 1, Optional): Provide detailed plans for how you will keep your event attendees safe in case of extreme heat conditions, such as having shade, water, and cooling measures in place.
* **document_safety_measures** (Page 1, Optional): Describe any safety measures that you will have in place, such as first aid kits, safety equipment, and emergency response plans.
* **site_maps** (Page 1, Optional): Upload or provide a link to a site map of the event location, including any accessible areas and emergency exits.
* **emergency_services** (Page 1, Optional): List any emergency services you will have available during the event, such as medical staff, first aid stations, and communication protocols.
* **contact_info** (Page 2, Optional): Fill out your organization's contact information, including name, address, and phone number, so we can follow up with any questions or concerns.
* **submit** (Page 2, Optional): Once all fields are completed, click the "submit" button to finalize your application.

## Tips
