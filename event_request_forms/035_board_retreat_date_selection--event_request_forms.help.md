# Board Retreat Date Selection - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Board Retreat Date Selection form helps board members choose their preferred dates for the board retreat, ensuring that all members can attend at the same time. This form also collects information about accommodation needs, dietary requirements, and travel assistance needs to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available dates and select all the dates you are available for the retreat.
2. Enter your full name as per the format required for scheduling purposes.
3. Provide any accommodation needs or requirements for the retreat.
4. Specify any dietary requirements or allergies.
5. Indicate whether you need travel assistance.
6. Add any additional comments or planning notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member name (Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on the scheduling records.
  * This field is required to ensure accurate planning and scheduling.
* **Preferred dates (Required)**: Select all the dates you are available for the retreat.
  * Select the dates you are available using the provided options, making sure to choose all dates that work for you.
* **Accommodation needs**: Specify any accommodation needs or requirements, such as wheelchair accessibility.
  * This field is used to plan for accommodations during the retreat, so provide any details that will make your stay comfortable.
* **Dietary requirements**: Indicate any dietary needs or allergies, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
  * This field helps the planning committee ensure that all meals are inclusive and cater to your needs.
* **Travel assistance required**: Indicate whether you need travel assistance.
  * This field helps the planning committee plan for any additional support needed for transportation or travel.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or planning notes.
  * This field is an opportunity to provide any other information that might be helpful for the planning process.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all available dates carefully, as this will help with scheduling and planning.
* Be accurate when providing your full name, as this is used for scheduling purposes.
* Provide specific information about your accommodation needs to ensure a comfortable retreat experience.
* Clearly indicate your dietary requirements to ensure inclusive meals.
* Indicate your travel assistance needs to get help with transportation or travel arrangements.
* Use the comments field to provide any additional information that will help with the planning process.
