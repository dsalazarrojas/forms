# Girls Sports Event Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect proposals for girls sports events that promote girls' participation in sports. The goal is to support and foster a healthy and active lifestyle for girls, and this form is a crucial step in making these events a reality.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief and clear title for the event.
2. Enter the location where the event will take place.
3. Describe the event in a concise and detailed manner.
4. Specify the name of the athlete (if applicable).
5. Enter the name of the school (if applicable).
6. Select the age range of the participants (High School, Elementary, or Middle School).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event, such as a tournament, game, or competition.
* **Location**: Enter the physical address or venue where the event will take place.
* **Description**: Provide a brief and concise description of the event, including its purpose, format, and any relevant details.
* **Athlete Name**: If the event is focused on a specific athlete, enter their name here.
* **School Name**: If the event is associated with a specific school, enter the name here.
* **Age Range**: Select the age range of the participants from the options provided: High School, Elementary, or Middle School.
