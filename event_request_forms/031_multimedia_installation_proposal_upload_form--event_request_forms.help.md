# Multimedia Installation Proposal Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Multimedia Installation Proposal Upload Form" is used to submit multimedia installation proposals for review and consideration for a specific event. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and relevant to the event's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the proposal details in the "proposal" field.
2. Select the relevant "galleries" and "venues" for your proposal.
3. Choose the event teams involved in your proposal.
4. Provide a clear and concise submission with your proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **proposal**: (`proposal`, text, required): This field is for the main proposal text. Please provide a clear and concise description of your multimedia installation, including the concept, objectives, and expected outcomes.
* **files**: (`files`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files or documents to support your proposal, such as images, videos, or technical specifications.
* **schedule**: (`schedule`, time, optional): If your multimedia installation has specific time requirements, please provide the schedule details.
* **galleries**: (`galleries`, select_one, required): Select the galleries where your multimedia installation will be displayed. Please choose all that apply.
* **venues**: (`venues`, select_multiple, required): Select the venues where your multimedia installation will be displayed. Please choose all that apply.
* **event teams**: (`event teams`, select_multiple, required): Select the event teams involved in your multimedia installation, such as technical, creative, or operational teams.
* **submit**: (`submit`, text, required): This is where you will submit your proposal. Please ensure that it is clear and concise, and includes all the necessary information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
* Double-check that you have selected all the relevant galleries, venues, and event teams.
* Use clear and concise language in your proposal to ensure it is easily understandable.
* Attach relevant files to support your proposal for better evaluation.

This help guide aims to provide a clear and concise explanation of the multimedia installation proposal upload form to ensure end-users can complete it accurately and effectively.
