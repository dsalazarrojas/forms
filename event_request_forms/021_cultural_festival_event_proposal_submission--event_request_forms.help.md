# cultural-festival-event-proposal-submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural-festival-event-proposal-submission form is used to collect information for a cultural festival event proposal. This form is essential for the internal processing of event proposals, ensuring that all relevant details are collected and processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about your event. You can find the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Festival Name
* **Festival Name** (`festival_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of your cultural festival event.

### Event Dates
* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, `date`, optional): Enter the dates of your event in the standard date format (DD/MM/YYYY).

### Event Time
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your event in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### Performance Duration
* **Performance Duration** (`performance_duration`, `text`, optional): Enter the duration of your performance in hours or minutes.

### Event Category
* **Event Category** (`event_category`, `select_one`, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your event is a part of a specific category. This field is not explicitly defined in the provided YAML, but it seems to be used for categorization; we will assume it as intended for filtering or classification purposes.

### Description
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your event in a few sentences or paragraphs.

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the contact person for your event.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.

### Performance Type
* **Performance Type** (`performance_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your event has a specific type of performance. This field is not explicitly defined in the provided YAML, but it seems to be used for filtering or classification purposes.
