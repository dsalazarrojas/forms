# Stage Video Screen Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request updates to stage video screens. It collects information about the report date, stage, screen ID, request type, description, email, phone, and any additional note or attachment required for the update.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Report Date** field with the current date.
2. Select the **Stage** you wish to update from the options provided.
3. Enter the **Screen ID** of the screen you wish to update.
4. Choose the **Request Type** of update you are requesting (e.g., bug, feature, or feedback).
5. Provide a brief description of the update in the **Description** field.
6. Enter your **Email** so we can contact you for additional information.
7. Provide your **Phone** number so we can reach you.
8. Add any additional **Note** or **Attachment** to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Report Date** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_report_date`, date, **required: false**): Enter the date you are reporting the update.
  This date will help us track the timeline of updates and ensure that we have accurate information.
- **Stage** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_stage`, select_one, **required: false**): Select the stage of the screen you wish to update.
  This will help us understand which screen needs the update and prioritize our work accordingly.
- **Screen ID** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_screen_id`, number, **required: false**): Enter the screen ID you wish to update.
  This is a unique identifier for the screen you want to update.
- **Request Type** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_request_type`, select_multiple, **required: false**): Choose the type of update you are requesting.
  This will help us understand the nature of the update and process it efficiently.
- **Change Request** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_change_request`, text, **required: false**): Provide a brief description of the update.
  This will help us understand the scope of the update and plan our work.
- **Description** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_description`, text, **required: false**): Enter a detailed description of the update.
  This will help us understand the update and ensure we complete it accurately.
- **Email** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_email`, email, **required: false**): Enter your email address so we can contact you for additional information.
  This will enable us to reach out to you with questions or progress updates.
- **Phone** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_phone`, text, **required: false**): Provide your phone number so we can reach you.
  This will enable us to contact you directly and clarify any questions.
- **Note** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_note`, note, **required: false**): Add any additional notes or comments.
  This will help us remember important details about your request.
- **Attachment** (`stage_video_screen_update_request_form_page_1_attachment`, text, **required: false**): Attach any relevant documents or files to support your request.
  This will help us understand the update better and process it efficiently.
