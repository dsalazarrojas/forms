# fashion_week_inquiry_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who have inquiries or requests related to the fashion week event. It is intended to help our team understand your needs and provide accurate information to assist with your query.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review your submission before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Submit the form to send your inquiry to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are inquiring about.
* **event_date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **event_time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **contact_name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the contact person.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **event_description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event if necessary.
* **event_details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the event.
* **fashion_week_inquiries_attachment** (`fashion_week_inquiries_attachment`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents or images to your inquiry.
* **fashion_week_inquiries_note** (`fashion_week_inquiries_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments to your inquiry.
* **fashion_week_inquiries_select_one** (`fashion_week_inquiries_select_one`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have any specific requests or requirements for the event.
* **fashion_week_inquiries_select_multiple** (`fashion_week_inquiries_select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you have any additional information or requests for the event.
* **fashion_week_inquiries_date** (`fashion_week_inquiries_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to attend the event.
* **fashion_week_inquiries_time** (`fashion_week_inquiries_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to attend the event.
* **fashion_week_inquiries_email** (`fashion_week_inquiries_email`, email, optional): Enter an email address if you have any email-specific requests.
* **fashion_week_inquiries_phone** (`fashion_week_inquiries_phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number if you have any phone-specific requests.
