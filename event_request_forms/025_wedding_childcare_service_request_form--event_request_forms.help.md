# Wedding Childcare Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for wedding childcare service requests. It helps event planners provide essential details for our team to prepare for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form with the following information:

*   Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately.
*   Provide a brief description of your event in the "Event Details" field.
*   Choose your preferred start and end times for our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Wedding Childcare Request** (`yes/no`): Indicate if you need our childcare services for your wedding event.
*   **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest you are making the request for.
*   **Contact Phone Number**: Provide your contact phone number so we can reach you in case of any questions or updates.
*   **Event Details**: Describe your event in detail, including the number of guests attending, any unique requirements, and any special requests.
*   **Event Date**: Enter the date of your wedding event.
*   **Start Time**: Select the time you would like our childcare services to begin.
*   **End Time**: Select the time our childcare services should end.
*   **Venue**: Specify the location of your wedding event.
*   **Event Type**: Indicate whether your event is a wedding (yes/no), helping us tailor our services accordingly.
*   **Guest Count**: Please provide an estimate of the number of guests attending your event.
*   **Childcare Providers**: Indicate if you need our standard or custom childcare providers for your event.
*   **Special Requests**: If you have any special requirements or requests, please describe them here.
*   **Contact Email**: Provide your email address so we can contact you with updates or questions.
*   **Event Planner**: Enter your name or title for reference and contact purposes.

## Tips

*   Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, don't hesitate to contact us.
*   We will respond promptly to your request, and our team will work with you to tailor our services according to your needs.
