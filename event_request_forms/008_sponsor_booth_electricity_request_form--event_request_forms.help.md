# Sponsor Booth Electricity Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Booth Electricity Request Form is used to request electricity for a sponsor booth during an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **event_name** in the first field.
2. Select the correct **event_date** using the date picker.
3. Choose the **booth_location** from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter your **power_request** details.
5. Select the desired **installation_window** using the time picker.
6. Choose the **approved_by** from the drop-down menu (if this field is required for your specific situation).
7. You can add any additional **notes** if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_name**: Enter the name of the event you are participating in. This helps us associate your request with the correct event.
* **event_date**: Select the date of the event using the calendar picker. This ensures that we can schedule electricity installation and management accordingly.
* **booth_location**: Choose the main location of your booth, such as Main Stage, Food Court, or Outdoor. This helps us determine the specific electricity infrastructure needed for your booth.
* **power_request**: Describe your electricity requirements in detail, including the type, quantity, and duration of power needed. This helps us to estimate the total power demand and ensure a smooth electricity supply.
* **installation_window**: Select a specific time frame for the electricity installation, taking into account the event schedule and other logistical constraints.
* **approved_by**: Choose the person or role responsible for approving your electricity request. This helps us track the progress and ensure that your request is approved on time.
* **notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your electricity request, such as specific technical requirements or concerns. This helps us to provide better support and service.
