# Hospitality Suite Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you request access to a hospitality suite. Fill out this form to submit your request and ensure that you have read and agreed to the rules and regulations of the establishment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click the "Submit" button at the end of the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Your full name is required. Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or as you would like it to be known by the establishment.
* **Visit Start Date**: The date you plan to start your visit. Please select the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Visit End Date**: The date you plan to end your visit. Please select the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Purpose**: A brief description of the purpose of your visit. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Rule Acknowledgment**: A series of 21 optional fields to confirm you have read and agreed to the rules and regulations of the establishment. Please tick the box to confirm.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your dates to ensure they are correct.
- Be as detailed as possible in the "Purpose" field.
- Review all 21 "Rule Acknowledgment" fields before submitting the form.
