# Museum Auditorium Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Auditorium Rental Application Form is a request form for users to submit their event details to rent the museum's auditorium. This form will help us understand your event needs and facilitate the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Event Request Details" section with a brief description of your event.
2. In the "Event Details" field, provide specific details about your event, such as the type of event and any special requirements.
3. In the "Contact Information" field, enter your contact name, email, and phone number so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
4. Select the event requirements that apply to your event in the "Event Requirements" field.
5. Choose the event date and time in the "Event Dates" field.
6. Finally, select the payment method for your event in the "Payment Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Request Details** (`event_request_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe the event you are planning to hold at the museum auditorium.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Provide specific details about your event, such as the type of event and any special requirements.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
* **Event Requirements** (`event_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Select the event requirements that apply to your event, such as sound system, lighting, or catering.
* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, select_one, optional): Choose the date and time of your event from the provided options.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_information`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method for your event, such as online payment or bank transfer.
