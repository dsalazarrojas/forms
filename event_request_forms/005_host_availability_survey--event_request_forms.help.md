# Host Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Host Availability Survey form is designed to collect essential information from hosts about their availability and event preferences. This data will be used for event planning, coordination, and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Host Availability Survey form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide a brief description of your availability, including your preferred dates and times for events.
2.  Select the type of event you are interested in hosting or participating in.
3.  Indicate your preferred start and end times for events.
4.  Add any additional notes or comments about your availability or event preferences.
5.  Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
6.  Confirm your email address and phone number to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Host Availability** (`host_availability`, text, required): This field is where you can describe your availability for events, including specific dates and times.
*   **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event you are interested in hosting or participating in.
*   **Available Dates** (`available_dates`, date, optional): If you have specific dates in mind, list them here.
*   **Event Preferences** (`event_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Check the types of events you are interested in hosting or participating in.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter your preferred start time for events.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter your preferred end time for events.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Add any extra comments or information about your availability or event preferences.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Confirm your email address for accuracy.
*   **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Confirm your phone number for accuracy.
*   **Other Event Preferences** (`other_event_preferences`, text, optional): Add any other event preferences or information not covered in the previous fields.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when providing your availability and event preferences.
*   Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
*   If you have any additional notes or comments, use the "Additional Notes" field to provide more information.
