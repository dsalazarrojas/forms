# Festival Publicity Collaboration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Publicity Collaboration Request Form is a tool for festival organizers to submit a collaboration request to our publicity team for promotional activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the festival name** (`festival_name`): Enter the name of your festival.
2. **Select your publicity type** (`publicity_type`): Choose from Publicity Agency, Media Outlet, or Event Promoter.
3. **Provide your contact details** (`contact_name`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone`): Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
4. **Submit your proposal** (`proposal_description`): Briefly describe your proposal or idea for collaboration.
5. **Attach relevant files** (`proposal_files`): If applicable, attach any supporting files or documents related to your proposal.
6. **Specify your collaboration interest** (`publicity_collaboration_interest`): Indicate why you are interested in collaborating with us.
7. **Your collaboration details** (`publicity_collaboration_requester`): Enter your details as a collaborator, if applicable.
8. **Collaborator contact info** (`publicity_collaboration_requester_phone`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_email`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_address`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_country`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode1`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode2`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode3`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode4`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode5`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode6`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode7`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode8`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode9`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode10`): Enter the contact information of the collaborator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Publicity Request** (`publicity_request`): This is a text field where you can type in your festival publicity request.
- **Festival Name** (`festival_name`): Enter the name of your festival. This is an essential piece of information for our team to know what festival you're representing.
- **Publicity Type** (`publicity_type`): Select from Publicity Agency, Media Outlet, or Event Promoter to indicate the nature of your collaboration request.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name as the contact person for your festival.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
- **Proposal Description** (`proposal_description`): Briefly describe your proposal or idea for collaboration.
- **Proposal Files** (`proposal_files`): Attach any supporting documents or files related to your proposal.
- **Publicity Collaboration Interest** (`publicity_collaboration_interest`): Indicate why you're interested in collaborating with us.
- **Collaboration Details** (`publicity_collaboration_requester`): Enter any additional details about your collaboration.
- **Collaborator Contact Info** (`publicity_collaboration_requester_phone`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_email`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_address`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_country`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode1`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode2`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode3`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode4`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode5`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode6`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode7`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode8`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode9`, `publicity_collaboration_requester_postalcode10`): Enter the contact information of the collaborator.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill in all required fields. Make sure to attach relevant files if applicable. Double-check your proposal description and ensure it's clear and concise. If you're unsure about anything, contact us for guidance.
