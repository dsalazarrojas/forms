# Book Club Author Visit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Club Author Visit Request Form is used to request an author visit for your book club. It's essential to provide accurate and relevant information to facilitate a successful visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields (Club name, Contact person, Contact email, Preferred dates, Audience size, and Format preference).
2.  Provide your club's name and contact information in the "Club name" and "Contact person" fields, respectively.
3.  Enter a valid email address in the "Contact email" field for scheduling purposes.
4.  Indicate your preferred dates for the author visit in the "Preferred dates" field.
5.  Specify the estimated number of attendees in the "Audience size" field.
6.  Choose your preferred format for the visit (In person, Virtual, or Hybrid) in the "Format preference" field.
7.  If you'd like to propose a budget for the author's honorarium, enter a number in the "Honorarium budget" field. This is optional.
8.  Add any additional notes or special requests in the "Additional notes" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Club name (Club name)** (`text`, required): This is the name of your book club.
    *   Please provide the accurate name of your book club so we can associate this request with the right club.
*   **Contact person (Contact person)** (`text`, required): This is your name and contact information.
    *   Enter your name as the contact person for the club, so we can reach out to you regarding the author visit.
*   **Contact email (Contact email)** (`email`, required): This is your email address.
    *   Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for scheduling and updates.
*   **Preferred dates (Preferred dates)** (`text`, required): This is the range of dates you'd like the author to visit.
    *   Provide a specific or general range of dates you'd like the author to visit, so we can plan accordingly.
*   **Audience size (Audience size)** (`number`, required): This is the estimated number of attendees.
    *   Enter the estimated number of attendees to help us plan the visit and ensure there are enough resources.
*   **Format preference (Format preference)** (`select_one`, required): Choose whether you'd like the author visit to be In person, Virtual, or Hybrid.
    *   Choose the format that best suits your book club's needs.
*   **Honorarium budget (Honorarium budget)** (`number`, optional): This is the proposed budget for the author.
    *   If you'd like to propose a budget for the author's visit, enter a number here. This is optional.
*   **Additional notes (Additional notes)** (`text`, optional): This is for any special requests or topics.
    *   Add any additional comments or requests you may have for the author visit.
