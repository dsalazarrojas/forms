# Temporary Public Safety Plan Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Temporary Public Safety Plan Application Form is designed for event organizers who need to request a temporary public safety plan due to a specific event or situation. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide the required information for your safety plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for Event Organizers (Section 1).
2. Provide your contact information as the Contractor (Section 2).
3. Enter the relevant details about the Public Agency you are working with (Section 3).
4. Clearly describe your Safety Plan Request (Section 4).
5. Attach any necessary Supporting Documents (Section 5).
6. Provide Key Event Details (Section 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizers** (Text Field, Optional):
	+ Enter your event name and contact details.
* **Contractor** (Text Field, Optional):
	+ Provide your name and contact information.
* **Public Agency** (Text Field, Optional):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the public agency you are working with.
* **Safety Plan Request** (Text Field, Optional):
	+ Describe the specific reason for your safety plan request, including any relevant event details.
* **Supporting Documents** (Text Field, Optional):
	+ Attach any relevant documents that support your safety plan request, such as photos or videos.
* **Key Event Details** (Text Field, Optional):
	+ Enter any crucial event information that will aid in creating your temporary public safety plan.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have attached all necessary and relevant information to support your safety plan request.
- Double-check that you have provided accurate contact information for all relevant parties.
- Review your completed form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
