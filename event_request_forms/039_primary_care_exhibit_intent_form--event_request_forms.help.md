# Primary Care Exhibit Intent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Primary Care Exhibit Intent Form is used to capture information from exhibitors about their exhibit's purpose, focus areas, and availability. This form helps event planners understand the exhibitor's intentions and ensure that their needs are met during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your exhibitor contact information**: Enter your name and contact details in the first field.
2. **Select your focus areas**: Choose up to three focus areas that best represent your exhibit.
3. **Indicate your availability**: Select the dates you are available for the event.
4. **Describe the purpose of your exhibit**: Briefly explain the purpose of your exhibit in the text field.
5. **Indicate primary care focus areas**: Choose up to three primary care focus areas that apply to your exhibit.
6. **Provide your email address and phone number**: Enter your email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information.
7. **Select your availability**: Choose a time slot that works best for you.
8. **Add any additional notes**: Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your exhibit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Contact Information (1)** (`exhibitor_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your name, contact information for our records.
* **What are your focus areas? (2)** (`focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to three focus areas (e.g., Primary Care, Medical, Wellness).
* **What dates are you available? (3)** (`availability`, `date`, required): Enter the dates you are available for the event.
* **What is the purpose of your exhibit? (4)** (`exhibit_intent`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your exhibit.
* **Additional Notes (5)** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your exhibit.
* **Primary Care Focus Areas (6)** (`primary_care_focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to three primary care focus areas that apply to your exhibit.
* **Email (7)** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Phone (8)** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Availability (9)** (`availability_2`, `time`, required): Select a time slot that works best for you.
* **Additional Notes 2 (10)** (`additional_notes_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your exhibit.

Note: If you need further assistance or clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
