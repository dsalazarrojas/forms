# Music Festival Backstage Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request backstage access for music festival events. Fill out this form to submit your request for access to the festival grounds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the festival details and ensure that you have the correct information about the event.
2. Enter your name and affiliation (if applicable).
3. Choose your role (Media, Crew, VIP Guest, or Partner).
4. Enter your email address and phone number.
5. Select a date and time for your access request.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments about your request.
7. Submit your completed form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Details**:
	+ This section is where you enter information about the festival for which you are requesting backstage access. Make sure to double-check the festival details to ensure you have the correct information.
* **Name**: 
	+ This is your name. Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or badge.
* **Affiliation**:
	+ This field is for you to indicate your affiliation with the festival or event (if any).
* **Role**:
	+ Select your role at the festival. This could be Media (e.g. photographer, journalist), Crew (e.g. stage manager, sound engineer), VIP Guest (e.g. invited artist or guest), or Partner (e.g. festival partner or sponsor).
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address where we can contact you about your request.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your request.
* **Date**:
	+ Select the date you are requesting backstage access for.
* **Time**:
	+ Select the time you are requesting to enter the festival grounds.
* **Notes**:
	+ Use this field to provide any additional information about your request.
* **Backstage Access Request**:
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or requests about your backstage access.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you have reviewed our backstage access policy and that you are aware of any specific rules or restrictions that may apply to your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our festival staff.
