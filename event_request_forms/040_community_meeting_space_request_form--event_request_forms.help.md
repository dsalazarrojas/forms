# Community Meeting Space Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Community Meeting Space Request Form is designed to collect essential information for requesting a meeting space within the community. Fill out this form to provide details about your event and ensure its successful execution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully, as indicated in the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Review your submission before submitting, making sure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, as this will be used for contact and verification purposes.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization or group, provide its name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and confirmation purposes.
* **Requested date** (`requested_date`, date, required): Choose the date for which you are requesting the meeting space.
* **Start time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the event start time, if applicable.
* **End time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the event end time, if applicable.
* **Expected attendance** (`expected_attendance`, number, required): Estimate the number of people attending the event.
* **Special requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you need specific equipment or services (e.g., AV, seating, accessibility), describe them here.
* **Agreement to terms** (`agreement_to_terms`, select_one, required): Select "Agree" to confirm acceptance of the venue rules and regulations.
