# spa-products-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering spa products from various suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing.
2. Review each field explanation to understand the purpose of each field.
3. Make sure to choose the correct supplier for your product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Quantity** (`product_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Product description** (`product_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the product being ordered.
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Product supplier** (`product_supplier`, select_one, optional): Choose the company that supplies the product (Company A, Company B, or Company C).
* **Delivery date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected delivery date.
* **Delivery time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the expected delivery time.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Additional notes about the order.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Identify any tool associated with the order.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **Form category** (`form_category`, text, optional): Enter the category of the form.
* **Form version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the form.
* **Output file** (`output_file`, text, optional): Enter a filename for the output.
