# Beard Steam Facial Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beard Steam Facial Consent Form is a document that ensures clients understand and provide consent for the beard steam facial treatment process, which may involve applying steam to the face to clean pores and improve skin health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your client's name and contact information, including phone number and email.
2.  Answer questions related to their health, skin, and breathing conditions.
3.  Indicate your client's experience with steam treatments and their level of sensitivity to heat.
4.  Confirm your understanding of the treatment process and consent to the treatment.
5.  Acknowledge potential risks associated with the treatment.
6.  Confirm that you will follow aftercare instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your client's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your client's last name.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your client's phone number for contact.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your client's email address for contact.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your client's appointment.
* **Facial Hair Type** (`facial_hair_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the type of facial hair your client has.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your client's skin type.
* **Current Skin Conditions** (`skin_conditions`, `text`, required): List any current skin conditions your client has, such as acne or rosacea.
* **Sensitivity to Heat** (`heat_sensitivity`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your client's level of sensitivity to heat.
* **Known Allergies** (`known_allergies`, `text`, optional): If your client has any known allergies, list them here.
* **Medications Affecting Skin** (`medications_affecting_skin`, `text`, optional): If your client is taking any medications that affect their skin, list them here.
* **Recent Treatments** (`recent_treatments`, `text`, optional): If your client has had any recent facial treatments, list them here.
* **Breathing Conditions** (`breathing_conditions`, `text`, optional): If your client has any breathing conditions, such as asthma, list them here.
* **Previous Experience with Steam Treatments** (`steam_tolerance`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your client's experience with steam treatments.
* **Understand Treatment** (`understand_treatment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the treatment process.
* **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your client consents to the treatment.
* **Acknowledge Risks** (`acknowledge_risks`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that your client is aware of potential risks associated with the treatment.
* **Follow Aftercare** (`follow_aftercare`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your client will follow aftercare instructions.
* **Client Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign here to confirm that your client has understood and consented to the treatment.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of consent.
