# lash_length_expectation_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **lash_length_expectation_survey** form is designed to gather information about your preferences and expectations related to eyelashes or lashes. This form will help us understand what you're looking for in terms of lash length and style.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer the questions about your lash preferences and expectations.
3. Provide any additional information you have in the "Any other info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form.
* **How long is your lashes?** (`lash_length`, `number`, required: false): Please enter the current length of your lashes.
* **What style do you like on your lashes?** (`lash_style`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the options below:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What color do you like on your lashes?** (`lash_color`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the colors you like:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What texture do you like on your lashes?** (`lash_texture`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the options below:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What is your expected lash length?** (`lash_length_expectation`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length you expect or desire for your lashes.
* **What style do you prefer on your lashes?** (`lash_style_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the styles you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What color do you prefer on your lashes?** (`lash_color_preference`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the colors you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Any other info?** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional information you think might be helpful to us.

Note: The form is not required, so feel free to fill it out as much or as little as you like. If you have any questions or concerns, please let us know.
