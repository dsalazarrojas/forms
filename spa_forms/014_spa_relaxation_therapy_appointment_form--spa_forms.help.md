# Spa Relaxation Therapy Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spa Relaxation Therapy Appointment Form is designed to allow clients to schedule appointments with our spa's relaxation therapists. This form is used to collect essential information to ensure a smooth and effective therapy experience. Please fill out this form to request an appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Name** in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select a date for your **appointment_date** using the date picker.
3. Choose a time for your appointment using the **appointment_time** field.
4. Select one or more therapists from the "Therapist" field.
5. Specify the duration of your appointment in the "duration" field.
6. Enter your **Contact Number** in the "Contact Number" field.
7. Leave a **message** in the **message** field if you have any specific requests or concerns.
8. From the dropdown menu, select an **available_therapist** to ensure availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name (client_name)
* **Client Name** (text): Enter your name to identify yourself as the client.

### appointment_date (appointment_date)
* **appointment_date** (date): Choose a date for your appointment.

### appointment_time (appointment_time)
* **appointment_time** (time): Select a time for your appointment.

### Therapist (select_therapist)
* **Therapist** (select multiple): Choose one or more therapists from the options.

### Duration (duration)
* **Duration** (number): Specify the length of your appointment.

### Contact Number (contact_number)
* **Contact Number** (email): Enter your email address to contact you about your appointment.

### Message (message)
* **Message** (note): Leave a message if you have any specific requests or concerns.

### Available Therapist (available_therapists)
* **Available Therapist** (select one): Ensure that your selected therapist is available for your appointment.
