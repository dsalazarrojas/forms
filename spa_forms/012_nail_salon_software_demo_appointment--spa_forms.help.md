# Nail Salon Software Demo Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from leads for a demo appointment at a nail salon. The gathered data will help the salon assess the lead's preferences and tailor their demo according to the lead's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your details.
2. Provide accurate and clear information in each field.
3. Review and submit the form once you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **salon_info** (Text Field, Optional)
* This field is for you to input your salon's name or any other relevant information about your business.

### **Lead Details** (Text Field, Optional)
* Enter your name and contact information so the salon can reach out to you.

### **Demo Details** (Text Field, Optional)
* Provide any additional details about the demo appointment you're interested in, such as specific services or time slots you prefer.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure the salon receives your message.
* Be clear and concise in your answers to help the salon better understand your needs.
* Double-check your input before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
