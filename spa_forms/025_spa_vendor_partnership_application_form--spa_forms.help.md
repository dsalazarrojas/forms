# Spa Vendor Partnership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spa Vendor Partnership Application Form is a tool used by external spas to express their interest in partnering with us. By filling out this form, you'll provide us with the necessary information we need to evaluate your partnership potential and discuss the possibilities with you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first and last name.
2. Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
3. Provide a phone number where we can reach you.
4. Enter your spa's name.
5. Add any additional vendor details in the "Vendor Details" field.
6. Enter your address for our records.
7. Provide the name of the contact person we can reach out to.
8. Enter the contact number of the contact person.
9. Finally, write any additional comments or questions you may have in "Message".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number where we can reach you.
* **Spa Name** (`spa_name`, text, optional): Enter your spa's name.
* **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, note, optional): Add any additional details about your spa that you think might be helpful for us to know.
* **Address** (`vendor_address`, text, optional): Enter your spa's address for our records.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person we can reach out to.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Write any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you've filled out all required fields, especially your email address.
* If you have any trouble understanding a field, feel free to ask us, and we'll be happy to help.
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
