# Spa Treatment Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Treatment Discharge Form is a standard procedure that ensures clients acknowledge and confirm their understanding of the treatments they have received, aftercare instructions, and follow-up appointments. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that both the client and spa staff are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the client's file or treatment plan to ensure that you are aware of the agreed-upon treatments and any important details.
2.  Have the client read and sign the form, ensuring they have understood the information provided.
3.  Ensure that the client has acknowledged and understood the aftercare instructions and any follow-up appointments.
4.  Complete the form with the client's signature and date of discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Acknowledgement
*   **Client Acknowledgement (number)**: This field is used to confirm that the client has acknowledged the information provided by the spa staff. Please enter the correct number to indicate their acknowledgement.

### Client Signature
*   **Client Signature**: This field is for the client to sign, confirming that they have read and understood the information provided. Please ensure that they sign this field to validate their understanding.

### Therapist Signature
*   **Therapist Signature**: This field is for the spa staff member who provided the treatment to sign, confirming that they have reviewed the client's file and provided all necessary information.

### Discharge Date
*   **Discharge Date**: This field is used to record the date and time the client was discharged from the spa. Please ensure that this date is accurate and up-to-date.

### Spa Treatment Details
*   **Spa Treatment Details (text)**: This field is used to record any additional information about the client's treatment, such as aftercare instructions or follow-up appointments. Please provide a brief description of the treatment, including any important details that may be relevant to the client's aftercare.

## Tips
*   Always review the client's file or treatment plan before completing this form to ensure that you are aware of the agreed-upon treatments and any important details.
*   Ensure that the client has read and understood the information provided before they sign the form.
*   Complete the form accurately and thoroughly, as this will help maintain accurate records and ensure that both the client and spa staff are on the same page.
*   Keep in mind that this form is a standard procedure, and it's essential to complete it for every client who receives a treatment at the spa.
