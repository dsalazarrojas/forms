# Nail Salon Social Media Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to help the nail salon better understand your social media preferences and contact habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the survey.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. You can skip any question if you are unsure or do not want to provide information.
4. Once you have completed the survey, click on the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page**:
	+ This is the first page of the survey.
	+ You can skip this page if you are not interested in providing any information.
* **Social Media Preferences**:
	+ Select one or more social media platforms you are interested in using to engage with the salon.
	+ Options include Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, YouTube, and Snapchat.
* **Email Frequency**:
	+ Indicate how often you would like to receive emails from the salon.
	+ Choose a number to represent your preferred frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Phone Freq**:
	+ Indicate how often you would like to receive phone calls or messages from the salon.
	+ Choose a number to represent your preferred frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
