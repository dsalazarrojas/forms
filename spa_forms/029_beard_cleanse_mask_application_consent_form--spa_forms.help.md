This form is used for clients to provide information about themselves and their skin and treatment preferences. The form is used by estheticians to understand the client's skin type, medical history, and treatment preferences before performing a beard cleanse mask treatment. The form ensures that the client understands the treatment process, risks, and their role in the treatment, and provides a clear statement of consent.

# Beard Cleanse Mask Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through each field of the form and help you fill out your application for the beard cleanse mask treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question truthfully and completely.
2. Fill out each field with your information.
3. Check the corresponding boxes for each question that applies to your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (<code>client_first_name</code>):** Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name (<code>client_last_name</code>):** Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Date of Birth (<code>date_of_birth</code>):** Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address (<code>email_address</code>):** Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (<code>phone_number</code>):** Enter your contact phone number.
* **Treatment Date (<code>treatment_date</code>):** Enter the date you plan to receive the treatment.
* **Esthetician Name (<code>esthetician_name</code>):** Enter the name of the esthetician performing the treatment.
* **Skin Type (<code>skin_type</code>):** Choose the type of skin you have:
	+ Normal
	+ Oily
	+ Dry
	+ Combination
	+ Sensitive
* **Current Skin Conditions (<code>skin_conditions</code>):** Check any conditions you currently have:
	+ Acne
	+ Rosacea
	+ Eczema
	+ Psoriasis
	+ Dermatitis
	+ None
* **Known Allergies (<code>allergies</code>):** List any allergies you have.
* **Current Medications (<code>medications</code>):** List any medications you are currently taking, especially skin medications.
* **Previous Treatments (<code>previous_treatments</code>):** If you have had any previous treatments, list them.
* **I Understand the Treatment (<code>treatment_understood</code>):** Check if you understand the treatment process.
* **Risks Have Been Explained (<code>risks_explained</code>):** Check if the risks of the treatment have been explained to you.
* **All Questions Answered (<code>questions_answered</code>):** Check if all your questions have been answered.
* **I Consent to the Treatment (<code>consent_given</code>):** Check if you consent to the treatment.
* **Medical Clearance Needed (<code>medical_clearance</code>):** Check if you need medical clearance for the treatment.
* **Consent for Before and After Photos (<code>photo_consent</code>):** Check if you consent to taking before and after photos.
* **Client Signature (<code>client_signature</code>):** Type your full name as a signature.
* **Signature Date (<code>signature_date</code>):** Enter the date you sign the form.

By following this guide, you will complete the form and provide the necessary information for the treatment. Please fill out each field truthfully and completely, and do not hesitate to ask questions if you need clarification on any of the fields.
