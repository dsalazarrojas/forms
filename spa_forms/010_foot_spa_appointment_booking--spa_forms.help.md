# Foot Spa Appointment Booking - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for booking an appointment at the spa. It includes fields for appointment date, customer name, email, phone number, and appointment time. Please ensure you are booking for a date and time that suits your schedule, and provide accurate contact information for smooth communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired appointment date for your visit.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
5. Choose the appointment time that suits your schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appointment Date** (1): Select the date for your visit to the spa. The format should be in YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-07-25).
* **Name** (2): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (3): Provide a valid email address for us to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Appointment Time** (5): Select the time for your appointment from the available options. 

## Tips
Please ensure that you book a date and time that suits your schedule to avoid last-minute cancellations. If you have any special requests or requirements, feel free to mention them in the comments section (not included in this form).
