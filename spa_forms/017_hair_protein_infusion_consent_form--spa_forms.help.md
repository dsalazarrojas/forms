# Hair Protein Infusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Protein Infusion Consent Form is designed to collect information from clients before proceeding with a hair protein infusion treatment. This form helps ensure that clients understand the procedure, risks, and benefits involved in the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the Client Details section.
2. Provide any relevant health information in the Health Information section.
3. Describe the treatment you are receiving in the Treatment Details section.
4. In the Consent section, provide a brief summary of your understanding of the treatment and its risks.
5. In the Signed Consent section, select "I have read and understand the above information." if you have reviewed and agreed to the treatment. Alternatively, select "I have not read and do not understand the above information." if you need further clarification.
6. Enter your phone number in the Phone section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (required): Enter your name and contact information, such as your phone number.
* **Health Information** (not required): Provide any relevant health information that may impact the treatment.
* **Treatment Details** (not required): Describe the specific treatment you are receiving.
* **Consent** (required): Provide a brief summary of your understanding of the treatment and its risks.
* **Signed Consent** (required): Select the option that best describes your understanding of the treatment.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number.
