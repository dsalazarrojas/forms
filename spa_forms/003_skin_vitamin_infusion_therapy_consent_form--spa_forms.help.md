# Skin Vitamin Infusion Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for patients to provide their consent for skin vitamin infusion therapy. The form collects personal and medical details, as well as signatures from the patient and medical practitioner, to document the consent process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the patient's personal details on page 1.
2. Provide your medical history on page 2.
3. Read and sign the consent form on page 3.
4. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
5. Sign the medical practitioner's copy of the form to confirm their acceptance of your consent.
6. Date and time of consent should be filled by the medical practitioner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details (page 1)**
	+ This page requires your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact information.
* **Medical History (page 2)**
	+ This page is for you to provide any relevant medical history that may be important for your treatment.
* **Consent Form (page 3)**
	+ This section outlines the terms and conditions of your consent for skin vitamin infusion therapy.
	+ Please read and sign this section to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Signature Form (page 4)**
	+ Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Medical Practitioner (page 5)**
	+ This field is for the medical practitioner to sign and confirm their acceptance of your consent.
* **Patient Signature (page 6)**
	+ Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Medical Practitioner Signature (page 7)**
	+ Sign your name to confirm your acceptance of the consent form.
* **Consent Date (page 8)**
	+ Date of consent should be filled by the medical practitioner.
* **Consent Time (page 9)**
	+ Time of consent should be filled by the medical practitioner.

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand the consent form before signing it.
* Fill in all required fields accurately and honestly.
* Ask your medical practitioner if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
