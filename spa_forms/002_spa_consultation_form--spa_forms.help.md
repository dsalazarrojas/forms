## Spa Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Consultation Form is a tool designed to help users book and schedule spa appointments. This form collects essential information from users, including their contact details, preferred appointment times and dates, and the services they wish to book.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred appointment time in the "Preferred Time" field.
5. Select your preferred appointment date in the "Preferred Date" field.
6. Choose the services you would like to book from the "Services" field.
7. Select the type of massage you would like to receive from the "Massage Type" field.
8. Choose the duration of your massage from the "Massage Duration" field.
9. Select the time of your appointment from the "Appointment Time" field.
10. Select the date of your appointment from the "Appointment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive confirmation and communication about your appointment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of day you would prefer for your appointment.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Select a date that suits you for your appointment.
* **Services** (`services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the services you would like to book, such as Massage, Facial, Manicure, Pedicure, or Other. You can select multiple services.
* **Massage Type** (`massage_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of massage you would like to receive, such as Deep Tissue, Swedish, Hot Stone, Aromatherapy, or Other.
* **Massage Duration** (`massage_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the duration of your massage, such as 15, 30, 60, or 90 minutes.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of your appointment.
