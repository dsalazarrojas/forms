# Client Intake For Personal Care Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Intake For Personal Care Services form is a crucial document that helps us understand your needs and preferences for your appointment. Please take a few minutes to fill it out accurately, so our therapists or technicians can provide you with the best service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct appointment type and date and time that suits you.
3. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please list them so we can take necessary precautions.
4. Choose your preferred therapist or technician if applicable.
5. Review the consent option and select accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Use the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-02-15).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number for appointment confirmations and updates.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email for appointment confirmations and receipts.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the service you are booking (Facial, Massage, Manicure, Pedicure, Hair Service, or Other).
* **Allergies or Sensitivities** (`skin_allergies_or_sensitivities`, text, required): List any known allergies or sensitivities, including products or ingredients.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, required): Include all prescribed and over-the-counter medications or treatments.
* **Preferred Therapist or Technician** (`preferred_therapist`, text, required): Optional, choose your preferred therapist or technician if you have a preference.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Select the time for your appointment.
* **Additional Requests or Notes** (`additional_requests`, text, required): Enter any additional requests or notes you may have, such as preferred pressure or music during the appointment.
* **Consent** (`consent_for_services`, select_one, required): Select your consent option for treatment communication and follow-up.
* **Consent Options:**
	+ "Yes I consent to the treatment and communication"
	+ "No I do not consent at this time"
	+ "Contact me to discuss consent options"
