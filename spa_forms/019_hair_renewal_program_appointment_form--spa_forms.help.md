# hair_renewal_program_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created to gather information from potential clients interested in joining a hair renewal program. It is designed to be as straightforward and simple as possible to minimize confusion and errors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Client Details** in the first page.
2. Select your **Hair Concerns** from the options provided.
3. Choose your preferred date for the appointment.
4. Select your **Hair Renewal Program** interests.
5. Choose your preferred time for the appointment.
6. Enter your **Contact Details**.
7. Select your preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, or In-person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, optional): This field is for entering your name and any other relevant information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Hair Concerns** (`hair_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the hair concerns you have, such as Baldness, Hair loss, Thinning, or Other. If you have any other concern not listed, please select "Other" and add it in the space provided.
* **Preferred Dates** (`preferred_dates`, date, optional): Choose a date that works best for your appointment.
* **Hair Renewal Program** (`hair_renewal_program_interests`, select_one, optional): Select the interest that best describes your motivations for joining the hair renewal program. Options are Interest in hair restoration, Interest in hair growth, and Interest in scalp health.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Choose a time that suits you best for the appointment.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are available to come in for an appointment.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, or In-person).

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling in your contact details and preferred dates and times.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your form submission and get back to you as soon as possible.
* Please note that this form is not a binding contract, and your submission does not guarantee an appointment.
