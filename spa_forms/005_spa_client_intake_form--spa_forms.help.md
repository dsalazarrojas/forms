# Spa Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Client Intake Form is designed to collect relevant information from clients before their spa sessions. This form helps our staff prepare for the client's session and ensure their safety and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review and submit your responses before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Information**
	* **Client Information** (`client_info`, `text`, not required): Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
### 2. **Contact Information**
	* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): You can enter your contact details, such as phone number, email, or any other relevant contact info.
### 3. **Medical History**
	* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, not required): Please share any relevant medical information, such as allergies or medical conditions.
### 4. **Payment Information**
	* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, not required): Indicate how you intend to pay for your session (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
### 5. **Appointment**
	* **Appointment** (`appointment`, `text`, not required): Describe your appointment details, such as the type of service you want.
### 6. **Session Notes**
	* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, `text`, not required): Add any additional notes about your session, such as specific requests or concerns.
### 7. **Medical Information**
	* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, `text`, not required): If you have any medical conditions, please provide details about them.
### 8. **Emergency Contact Info**
	* **Emergency Contact Info** (`contact_info_2`, `text`, not required): If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their details.
### 9. **Payment Method**
	* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you intend to pay for your session (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
### 10. **Payment Amount**
	* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you intend to pay.
### 11. **Start Time**
	* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Indicate the start time for your session.
### 12. **End Time**
	* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Indicate the end time for your session.
### 13. **Appointment Date**
	* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for your appointment.
### 14. **Medical Conditions**
	* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, not required): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them.
### 15. **Medications**
	* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, not required): If you take any medications, please list them.

Note:
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields.
* Be accurate and truthful when filling out this form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact our staff for assistance.
