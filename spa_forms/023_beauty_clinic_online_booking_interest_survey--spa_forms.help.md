# Beauty Clinic Online Booking Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from clients about their interest in online booking and feedback on what features they would like to see in an online platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Information**: This section is where you will provide your basic contact information.
	* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in our system.
	* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
2. **Client Status**: This section is where you will indicate your status as a client.
	* **Current Client**: Select whether you are a regular, occasional, or new client.
3. **Online Booking Interests**: This section is where you will indicate your interest in online booking and preferred method of booking.
	* **Interest in Online Booking**: Choose how interested you are in booking online.
	* **Importance of Easy Online Booking**: Rate how important it is for online booking to be easy and seamless.
	* **Preferred Booking Method**: Select your preferred method of booking, such as phone call, in-person, online portal, mobile app, email, or text message.
4. **Services**: This section is where you will select which services you are interested in booking online.
	* **Primary Service**: Select the service that interests you the most.
	* **Additional Services**: Choose any other services you would like to book online.
5. **Barriers and Concerns**: This section is where you will provide any barriers or concerns you have about online booking.
	* **Barriers to Online Booking**: If you have any concerns or barriers to online booking, please describe them.
	* **Privacy Concerns**: Indicate any concerns you have regarding privacy.
	* **Comfort Level with Technology**: Rate your comfort level with technology.
6. **Additional Feedback**: If you have any additional feedback or suggestions for our online platform, please provide them below.
