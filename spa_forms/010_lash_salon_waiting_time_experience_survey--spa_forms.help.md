# lash_salon_waiting_time_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from customers about their waiting time experience at the salon. Your input will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each page carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Use the provided options for questions that have options.
3. Enter your comments in the "Comments" field if you want to provide additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome To Our Salon** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This page introduces the purpose of the survey and welcomes the user.
* **How Do You Rate Your Experience** (`experience_rating`, `number`, required: true): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 100.
* **How Long Was You Were Waited** (`time_spend_wait_area`, `time`, required: true): Estimate the time you spent waiting in the waiting area.
* **How Long Was Your Service** (`time_spend_service_area`, `time`, required: true): Estimate the time you spent in the service area.
* **Were The Staff Helpful** (`staff_friendly`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Very Helpful
	+ Somewhat Helpful
	+ Not Helpful
	Choose one of the options above that best describes your experience.
* **Was The Salon Clean** (`cleaness`, `select_multiple`, required: true):
	+ Clean
	+ Somewhat Clean
	+ Not Clean
	Choose all the options that best describe the cleanliness of the salon.
* **Was Communication With Staff** (`communication`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	Choose one of the options above that best describes your experience with staff communication.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: true): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 100.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **How Can We Contact You** (`contact_us`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information if you'd like us to follow up with you.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate with your feedback.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
