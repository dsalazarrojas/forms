# Tanning Preparation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information and confirm tanning preparation details before a tanning appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Client Details** on page 1. This includes any relevant information about the client.
2. Select one or more **Tanning Instructions** from page 2. This helps the tanning staff understand how to prepare the client's skin for tanning.
3. Confirm your **Consent** on page 3. This is a required step to ensure you are aware of and agree to the preparation process.
4. Enter your **Appointment Date** on page 4. Select the date of your scheduled tanning appointment.
5. Enter your **Appointment Time** on page 5. Select the time of your scheduled tanning appointment.
6. Enter your **Client Phone** on page 6. This is the client's contact phone number.
7. Enter your **Email** on page 7. This is the client's contact email address.
8. Add any **Client Notes** on page 8. This is an optional field for any additional information you want to provide.
9. Select the **Tanning Staff** on page 9. This is an important field, as it ensures the correct staff member is assigned to the client.
10. Enter your **Confirm Email** on page 10. This is a confirmation of your email address.
11. Enter your **Confirm Phone** on page 11. This is a confirmation of your phone number.
12. Finally, select the **Confirm Staff** on page 12. This is a confirmation of the tanning staff assigned to the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (Text, Required): Enter any relevant information about the client.
* **Tanning Instructions** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more tanning options to ensure the staff understands how to prepare the client's skin.
* **Consent** (Select One, Optional): Confirm that you are aware of and agree to the preparation process.
* **Appointment Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the scheduled tanning appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the scheduled tanning appointment.
* **Client Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the client's contact email address.
* **Client Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional information about the client.
* **Tanning Staff** (Select One, Required): Select the correct staff member assigned to the client.
* **Confirm Email** (Email, Optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (Text, Optional): Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Staff** (Select One, Optional): Confirm the assigned tanning staff.

### Tips

* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our staff for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so please take your time to ensure accuracy.
