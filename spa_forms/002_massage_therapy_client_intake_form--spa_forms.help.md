# massage_therapy_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and medical information from massage therapy clients to provide an accurate massage therapy plan and ensure the best possible outcomes for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. If you have any concerns or questions, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information**: This section collects general information about you, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal information.
* **Medical History**: Please provide a detailed account of your medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or conditions that may affect your massage therapy treatment.
* **Medical History 2**: This section is a continuation of your medical history, providing additional information about any previous injuries, illnesses, or conditions that may affect your massage therapy treatment.
* **Massage Preferences**: Here, you can indicate your massage preferences, including the type of massage you're looking for and any areas of tension or concern.
* **Appointment Info**: Please provide details about your appointment, including the date, time, and location.
* **Signature**: If you wish to sign this form electronically, please sign below. This is optional and not required for form submission.
* **Medical Clearance**: Please provide information about any medical clearance or consent you have obtained from a healthcare professional.
* **Medical Clearance 2**: This section is a continuation of medical clearance, providing additional information about any medical clearance or consent you have obtained from a healthcare professional.

## Tips
* If you have any questions or concerns while completing this form, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* Please review each field carefully and answer truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please inform your massage therapist before your appointment.
* This form is for internal use only and any data collected will be kept confidential.
