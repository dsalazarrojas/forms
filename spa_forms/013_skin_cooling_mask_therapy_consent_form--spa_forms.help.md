# Skin Cooling Mask Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Cooling Mask Therapy Consent Form is designed to gather necessary information and consent from clients before initiating skin cooling mask therapy. This form ensures that clients understand the treatment process, potential risks, and benefits associated with the therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your health details in the Client Health Details section.
2. Sign your consent to proceed with the therapy in the Consent section.
3. Sign your name as proof of consent in the Client Signature section.
4. Indicate whether you will receive any treatments during the therapy session in the Treatments Provided section.
5. If applicable, provide additional notes on the treatments in the Treatment Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Health Details** (`client_health_details`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your health status, medical conditions, or allergies that may be relevant to the skin cooling mask therapy.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): Please provide your consent to proceed with the skin cooling mask therapy. This includes understanding the potential risks and benefits associated with the treatment.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, required): Sign your name as proof of consent. This is a required field and must be completed by the client.
* **Treatments Provided** (`treatment_procedure`, select_multiple, required): Please select whether you will receive any treatments during the skin cooling mask therapy session. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Treatment Notes** (`treatment_notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional notes or comments regarding the treatments, please provide them in this section.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly, as it will help the therapist provide the best possible care for your skin cooling mask therapy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your therapist or healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is intended to be filled out before each therapy session.
