# Hair Volume Root Boost Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect consent and information from clients for a hair volume root boost treatment. It is used to ensure that clients are aware of the process and agree to the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic information in the "Client Details" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. If necessary, provide any relevant health history information in the "Health History" section.
4. Read and agree to the terms of the consent form in the "Consent Form" section.
5. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "Client Signature" section to indicate your consent.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Staff Signature" section to indicate the staff member's consent.
7. If the staff member is signing, provide your name in the "Staff Name" section.
8. If the staff member is signing, provide your email address in the "Staff Email" section.
9. If the staff member is signing, provide your phone number in the "Staff Phone" section.
10. If applicable, provide your name in the "Staff Name 2" section (e.g., for second staff member signing).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details (1)**: Enter your name, address, and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Provide your phone number, email address, or other contact information so we can reach you.
* **Health History (3)**: If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please let us know in this section.
* **Consent Form (4)**: Carefully read the terms and agree to the treatment.
* **Client Signature (6)**: Choose "Yes" to indicate your consent.
* **Staff Signature (7)**: If a staff member is signing for you, choose "Yes" to confirm their consent.
* **Staff Name (8)**: If a staff member is signing for you, please enter their name.
* **Staff Email (9)**: If a staff member is signing for you, please enter their email address.
* **Staff Phone (10)**: If a staff member is signing for you, please enter their phone number.
* **Staff Name 2 (11)**: If a second staff member is signing for you, please enter their name.

## Tips
* Make sure to sign and date both the client signature and staff signature sections.
* If you are signing for a client, make sure to provide accurate contact information for the staff member signing.
