# Spa Guest Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests for collaboration purposes at a spa or wellness center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your guest name.
2. Provide the name of the collaboration you are interested in.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select any special programs you are interested in (if applicable).
6. Describe any previous interactions you have had with the spa.
7. Share any preferences you have for your collaboration experience.
8. Add any additional comments or information you would like to share.
9. If applicable, choose the tool or service you would like to be assigned to you.
10. If applicable, provide your spa ID and user ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, Text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to by the spa staff.
* **Collaboration Name** (`collaboration_name`, Text, required/optional): Provide the name of the collaboration you are interested in.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that the spa staff can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, Text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that the spa staff can contact you.
* **Special Programs** (`special_programs`, Select Multiple, required/optional): Select any special programs you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Interactions** (`interactions`, Text, required/optional): Describe any previous interactions you have had with the spa.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, Text, required/optional): Share any preferences you have for your collaboration experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information you would like to share with the spa staff.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Select One, required/optional): Choose the tool or service you would like to be assigned to you (if applicable).
* **Spa ID** (`spa_id`, Number, required/optional): If applicable, provide your spa ID.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, Number, required/optional): If applicable, provide your user ID.

## Tips
- Please fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
- Ensure that you select the correct special programs and assigned tool if applicable.
- Add any additional comments or information you would like to share with the spa staff.
- Review your form before submitting to ensure that all information is correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the spa staff for assistance.
