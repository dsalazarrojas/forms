# Hair Spa Protein Mask Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from clients before providing hair spa services such as masks, hair treatments, and chemical treatments. It helps the spa to understand the client's needs, medical conditions, and allergies, ensuring a safe and effective service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Client Name** (Field 1) with your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Choose your preferred **Service Type** (Field 2) from the options provided (Mask, Hair Treatment, Chemical Treatment, or Scalp Treatment).
3. Select your preferred **Contact Information** (Field 3) from the options provided (Email, Phone, or Address).
4. Enter the **Consent Date** (Field 4) by selecting the date you are giving consent for.
5. In the **Pre-Service Screening** (Field 5), list any known medical conditions or allergies you may have.
6. Sign your **Signature** (Field 6) to confirm you have read and understand the service agreement.
7. Provide detailed **Service Details** (Field 7) about the services you want to receive.
8. Select your **Service Agreement** (Field 8) option, either "Agree" or "Decline".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you want to receive (Mask, Hair Treatment, Chemical Treatment, or Scalp Treatment).
* **Contact Information**: Select how you want to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Address).
* **Consent Date**: The date you are giving consent for the services.
* **Pre-Service Screening**: List any known medical conditions or allergies you may have that may affect the service.
* **Signature**: Your signature confirms you have read and understand the service agreement.
* **Service Details**: Provide detailed information about the services you want to receive.
* **Service Agreement**: Select "Agree" if you have read and understand the service agreement, or "Decline" if you do not.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with *) for a complete and valid submission.
* Review the **Service Agreement** carefully before selecting "Agree".
