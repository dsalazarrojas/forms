# Beard Shaping Service Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather client's consent for a beard shaping service, ensuring they are aware of the procedure and its associated risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Read each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
*   Enter your full name, phone number, and email address in the "Client Information" section.
*   Provide your current beard length, density, and face shape in the "Beard Information" section.
*   In the "Health Information" section, mention any current skin conditions or allergies that may affect the treatment.
*   Confirm your understanding of the procedure, consent to the service, and acknowledge potential risks in the "Service Consent" section.
*   Finally, sign the form by typing your name in the "Client Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your records.
*   **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further instructions.
*   **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your appointment.
*   **Appointment Date** (Required): Choose a date and time for your appointment.
*   **Appointment Time** (Required): Choose a time for your appointment.
*   **Current Beard Length** (Required): Select the current length of your beard from the options provided.
*   **Beard Density** (Required): Choose the density of your beard from the options provided.
*   **Face Shape** (Required): Select your face shape from the options provided.
*   **Previously Shaped Beard** (Required): Confirm if you have had your beard shaped before.
*   **Current Skin Conditions** (Optional): If you have any skin conditions, such as psoriasis or eczema, please mention them here.
*   **History of Ingrown Hairs** (Required): Confirm if you have ever experienced ingrown hairs.
*   **Allergies to Products** (Optional): If you have any allergies to products, please mention them here.
*   **Recent Cuts or Abrasions** (Required): Confirm if you have had any recent cuts or abrasions.
*   **I Understand the Procedure** (Required): Confirm you understand the procedure and its associated risks.
*   **I Consent to this Service** (Required): Confirm you consent to the service.
*   **I Acknowledge Potential Risks** (Required): Confirm you understand and acknowledge potential risks such as nicks, irritation, or temporary redness.
*   **I will Follow Aftercare Instructions** (Required): Confirm you will follow aftercare instructions.
*   **Client Signature** (Required): Sign your name by typing it below to confirm your consent.
