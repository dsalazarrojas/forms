# Spa Customer Injury Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Customer Injury Incident Report is used to document and report incidents involving customers that occur in a spa setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the incident using the date picker.
2. Choose the location where the incident occurred from the dropdown menu.
3. Select the type of incident that occurred from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter a detailed description of what happened.
5. Enter the customer's name and contact information.
6. Enter the staff member's name and contact information.
7. Select the staff member who reported the incident.
8. Enter the date the incident was reported.
9. Choose whether follow-up actions have been completed or not.
10. Enter any internal notes about the incident.
11. Enter any follow-up notes about the incident.
12. Enter any injuries or issues reported by the customer or staff.
13. Enter any first aid provided to the customer or staff.
14. Enter any first aid notes about the incident.
15. Enter the date the incident was reported.
16. Sign the form to confirm that the incident has been reported.
17. Review and sign the form by a senior staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: The date of the incident. This is the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Location**: The location where the incident occurred. Choose the spa where the incident took place.
* **Incident Type**: The type of incident that occurred. Choose from the options: Injury, Illness, or Other.
* **Description**: A detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Customer Name**: The name of the customer involved in the incident.
* **Customer Email**: The email address of the customer involved in the incident.
* **Customer Phone**: The phone number of the customer involved in the incident.
* **Customer Signature**: The customer's signature, indicating they have reported the incident.
* **Staff Name**: The name of the staff member involved in the incident.
* **Staff Email**: The email address of the staff member involved in the incident.
* **Staff Phone**: The phone number of the staff member involved in the incident.
* **Incident Reporter**: The staff member who reported the incident.
* **Incident Date Reported**: The date the incident was reported.
* **Follow-up Status**: Choose whether follow-up actions have been completed or not.
* **Internal Notes**: Any internal notes about the incident.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Any follow-up notes about the incident.
* **Customer Injuries**: Any injuries or issues reported by the customer.
* **Staff Injuries**: Any injuries or issues reported by the staff member.
* **Staff First Aid**: Any first aid provided to the staff member involved in the incident.
* **Staff First Aid Notes**: Any notes about the first aid provided to the staff member.
* **Customer First Aid**: Any first aid provided to the customer involved in the incident.
* **Customer First Aid Notes**: Any notes about the first aid provided to the customer.
* **Incident Report Date**: The date the incident was reported.
* **Incident Reporter Signature**: The signature of the staff member who reported the incident.
* **Spa Reviewed By**: The signature of the senior staff member who reviewed the form.

Note: This guide assumes that the form fields and options will be populated by the spa's management system.
