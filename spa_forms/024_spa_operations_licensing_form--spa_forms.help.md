# Spa Operations Licensing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for spa businesses to provide necessary information for operations licensing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section, including your name, address, and phone number.
2. Choose your business ownership type (Owner, Partner, or Sole) in the "Business Ownership" section.
3. Select the services offered by your spa in the "Services Offered" section.
4. Choose the contact person handling the licensing process in the "Contact Person" section.
5. Enter your business name and address in the "Business Address" and "Business Name" sections, respectively.
6. Provide your phone number and email (optional) in the "Phone Number" and "Email" sections.
7. Enter the date your business was established in the "Date Established" section.
8. Enter your business hours of operation in the "Business Hours" section.
9. Describe your business in the "Business Description" section.
10. Select the licenses required for your business in the "Licenses Required" section.
11. Enter your license numbers for each selected license in the "License Numbers" section.
12. Enter the application fee for your spa operations licensing.
13. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`text`, required): Enter your name, address, and other contact details.
* **Business Ownership (Business Ownership)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the type of business ownership (Owner, Partner, or Sole).
* **Business Address (Business Address)** (`text`, required): Enter your business address.
* **Services Offered (Services Offered)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the services offered by your spa (Massage, Facials, Pedicures, or Other).
* **Contact Person (Contact Person)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the contact person handling the licensing process (Jane, John, or Doe).
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`text`, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Name (Business Name)** (`text`, required): Enter your business name.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date Established (Date Established)** (`date`, required): Enter the date your business was established.
* **Business Hours (Business Hours)** (`time`, required): Enter your business hours of operation.
* **Business Description (Business Description)** (`text`, required): Describe your business.
* **Licenses Required (Licenses Required)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the licenses required for your business (License 1, License 2, or License 3).
* **License Numbers (License Numbers)** (`text`, required): Enter your license numbers for each selected license.
* **Application Fee (Application Fee)** (`number`, required): Enter the application fee for your spa operations licensing.
* **Submit (Submit)** (`note`, required): Click "Submit" to complete the form.
