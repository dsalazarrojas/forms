# Cryotherapy Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a client's consent for cryotherapy facial treatments at the spa. The client will be asked to provide their details, medical history, and confirm their consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your details in the Client Details section.
2. In the Medical History section, please describe any past medical conditions or treatments you have undergone that may impact your ability to receive cryotherapy facial treatment.
3. In the Treatment Consent section, select 'Yes' if you give your consent for the treatment. If you are not comfortable with the treatment, select 'No'.
4. In the Consent Given section, confirm that you have given your consent for the treatment.
5. Add any relevant medical conditions or comments in the Medical Conditions section.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes for the spa staff, please provide them in the Client Comments section.
7. Finally, any additional comments or notes from the spa staff will be provided in the Spa Staff Comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**:
	+ This is where you will provide your basic contact information, such as your name, address, and contact number.
* **Medical History**:
	+ Please describe any past medical conditions or treatments that may impact your ability to receive cryotherapy facial treatment. This may include surgeries, allergies, or other relevant information.
* **Treatment Consent**:
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are willing to receive cryotherapy facial treatment. If you are not, select 'No'.
* **Consent Given**:
	+ Confirm that you have given your consent for the treatment by selecting 'Yes'.
* **Medical Conditions**:
	+ If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to receive cryotherapy facial treatment, please provide a brief description.
* **Client Comments**:
	+ If you have any additional comments or notes for the spa staff, please provide them here.
* **Spa Staff Comments**:
	+ Any additional comments or notes from the spa staff will be provided here.
