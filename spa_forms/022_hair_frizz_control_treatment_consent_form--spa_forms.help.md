# hair_frizz_control_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "hair_frizz_control_treatment_consent_form" is a form designed to collect client information, contact details, and treatment consent for hair frizz control treatments. This form helps ensure that clients are aware of and agree to the treatment process and its implications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's details in the "Client Details" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Enter any relevant health information in the "Health Information" field.
4. Select your consent for the treatment in the "Treatment Consent" field (select either "Yes" or "No").
5. Sign and date the "Signature" field to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details
* **Client Details**: Enter the client's name, contact name, email, and date of birth.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address.

### Health Information
* **Health Information**: Provide any medical or health conditions that may affect treatment, such as allergies, medications, or medical implants.

### Treatment Consent
* **Treatment Consent**: Select "Yes" if you are willing to undergo the hair frizz control treatment, or "No" if you decline. This field is required.

### Signature
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your consent for the treatment. This field is required.
