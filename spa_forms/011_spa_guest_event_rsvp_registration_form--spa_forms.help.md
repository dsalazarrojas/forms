# spa_guest_event_rsvp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVPs and registration information from guests for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Enter the name of the event you are attending in the "Event Name" field.
4. If you're bringing guests with you, specify their count in the "RSVP Count" field.
5. If you have any special requests or requirements, type them in the "Special Request" field (optional).
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you'd like to be contacted.
7. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field if you'd like to be contacted.
8. (Optional) Enter the event date and time in the "Event Date" and "Event Start Time" fields.
9. Click submit to finalize your RSVP and registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name (text, required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Date of Birth (date, required)**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Event Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **RSVP Count (number, required)**: Specify the number of guests attending with you.
* **Special Request (text, optional)**: If you have any special requests or requirements, type them here.
* **Email (email, optional)**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Event Date (date, optional)**: Enter the date of the event (if different from your birthday).
* **Event Start Time (time, optional)**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time (time, optional)**: Enter the end time of the event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you're using the correct date format (mm/dd/yyyy) when entering your date of birth.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the event organizer for clarification.
