# Hydrodermabrasion Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain clients' consent and gather information about their medical history, preferred contact method, and agreement to undergo hydrodermabrasion procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly in the spaces provided.
2. Select the appropriate answers for each question based on your understanding.
3. Sign and date the **Signature and Consent** field to indicate your agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client History**: Please describe your medical history, previous treatments, or any allergies you may have. This is to ensure we can perform the procedure safely.
* **Treatment Awareness**: Please select the treatments you are aware of from the list: Hydrodermabrasion, Chemical Exfoliation, or Microdermabrasion. This is to confirm your understanding of the procedures we will perform.
* **Signature and Consent**: Please sign and date this form to confirm you understand and agree to the terms of the treatment.
* **Contact Details**: Please select how you would like us to contact you after the procedure: Phone, Email, or In Person. This ensures we can reach you with any post-procedure instructions or concerns.
* **Signature**: Please sign with your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide any additional contact information you feel is necessary for us to reach you, such as your address.
