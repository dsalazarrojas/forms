# Skin Care Product Sensitivity Test Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Care Product Sensitivity Test Consent Form is a crucial document that collects sensitive information from clients to aid in the development and improvement of skin care products. This form is designed to gather accurate and reliable data to inform product testing and development processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Client Name", "Contact Email", and "Contact Phone" fields.
2. Select your skin type from the "Skin Type" dropdown menu.
3. Provide a brief description of your skin history in the "Skin History" field.
4. Enter the date and time you are providing consent for the skin care product sensitivity test in the "Consent Date" and "Consent Time" fields.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have used skin care products in the past ("Skin Care Products").
6. Describe any skin symptoms you are experiencing in the "Skin Symptoms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (form_title, text): This field is the heading of the form and serves as a title.
* **Client Name** (client_name, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information or to receive updates on your skin care product sensitivity test.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required): Please provide your phone number where you can be reached during the testing process.
* **Skin Type** (skin_type, select_multiple, required): Select your skin type from the dropdown menu to indicate your skin characteristics.
* **Skin History** (skin_history, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your skin history, including any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your skin.
* **Consent Date** (consent_date, date, required): Enter the date on which you are providing consent for the skin care product sensitivity test.
* **Consent Time** (consent_time, time, required): Enter the time at which you are providing consent for the skin care product sensitivity test.
* **Skin Care Products** (skin_care_products, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" if you have used skin care products in the past, otherwise answer "No".
* **Skin Symptoms** (skin_symptoms, text, required): Describe any skin symptoms or issues you are experiencing that may be relevant to the skin care product sensitivity test.

Note: All information collected will be kept confidential and used to inform the development and improvement of skin care products. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
