# Massage Pressure Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Pressure Preference Survey is designed to gather information about your preferences for massage pressure, duration, and frequency, as well as your contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred massage pressure by clicking on one of the options (Gentle, Moderate, or Firm).
2. Choose your preferred massage frequency by selecting one of the options (Every Day, Every Other Day, or Every Week).
3. Enter your email address and/or phone number to ensure we can contact you with updates and reminders about your massage sessions.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about your massage preferences in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Massage Pressure Preference** (`massage_pressure_preference`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the pressure level that you prefer for your massage sessions. Options are: Gentle, Moderate, and Firm.
* **Preferred Massage Pressure** (`preferred_massage_pressure`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose one or more pressure levels that you prefer for your massage sessions. Options are: Light, Medium, and Heavy.
* **Massage Duration** (`massage_duration`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the length of your desired massage session in minutes. This will help our therapists tailor the session to your needs.
* **Time Between Massages** (`time_between_massages`, time, required: false):
	+ If you'd like to schedule multiple massages, enter the time interval between each session (e.g., 1 week, 2 weeks, etc.).
* **Massage Frequency** (`massage_freq`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select how often you would like to receive massages. Options are: Every Day, Every Other Day, or Every Week.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and reminders about your massage sessions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number for us to contact you with urgent matters or schedule confirmations.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your massage preferences or any concerns you'd like to share with our team.
