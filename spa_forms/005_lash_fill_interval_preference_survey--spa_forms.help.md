# Lash Fill Interval Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Fill Interval Preference Survey is designed to gather information about your preferences for lash fill frequency, satisfaction, and communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select your preferred frequency of lash fills from the options provided.
2.  Rate your satisfaction with the current lash fill intervals using the scale provided.
3.  Choose the factors that influenced your current lash fill intervals.
4.  Indicate whether you would like a more personalized approach to lash fills.
5.  If you prefer to be contacted via email or phone, select the frequency of communication you prefer.
6.  Enter your email address and/or phone number if you would like to be contacted.
7.  Provide your name and any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What frequency is most appealing to you? (1)**: This question asks about your ideal frequency for lash fills. Please select one of the options provided.
*   **How satisfied are you with our current lash fill intervals? (2)**: This question rates your satisfaction with the current frequency of lash fills. Please select one of the options provided.
*   **Which of the following influenced your current fill interval? (3)**: Choose all that apply. These options represent different factors that may have influenced your current lash fill frequency.
*   **Would you prefer a more personalized service approach? (4)**: This question asks if you would like the service to be more tailored to your needs.
*   **What would you like your fill interval to be? (5)**: If you have a specific frequency in mind, please enter it here. Note that this is optional.
*   **On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with your current fill intervals? (6)**: This question rates your satisfaction with the current lash fill intervals. Please enter a number between 1 and 10.
*   **Is there anything else you'd like to add or comment about your current fill intervals? (7)**: This is an open-ended question for any additional feedback or comments you might have.
*   **How often would you like to receive emails from us? (8)**: Select the frequency of email communication you prefer.
*   **How often would you like to receive phone calls from us? (9)**: Select the frequency of phone communication you prefer.
*   **Do you have any other comments or suggestions? (10)**: This is another open-ended question for any additional feedback or comments.
*   **Email address (11)**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted via email.
*   **Phone number (12)**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
*   **Name (13)**: Please enter your name.
*   **Comments (14)**: This is a text field for any additional comments or feedback.

**Tips**

*   Please be honest and provide accurate information about your preferences.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
*   Thank you for taking the time to complete this form! Your feedback is valuable to us.
