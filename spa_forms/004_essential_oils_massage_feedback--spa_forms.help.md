# Essential Oils Massage Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client feedback on their experiences with essential oils massages.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are accurate.
- Select the correct options for the "Massage Type" field according to the type of massage you received.
- Rate your satisfaction level for each of the "Client Satisfaction Rating," "Massage Quality," "Therapist Performance," "Relaxation Level," and "Overall Satisfaction" fields using the provided numbers.
- Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields for contact information.
- Optionally, provide any comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.
- If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Satisfaction Rating** (`client_rating`, `number`, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your massage experience, using the numbers 1-5.
  - 1: Very dissatisfied
  - 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
  - 3: Neutral
  - 4: Somewhat satisfied
  - 5: Very satisfied
- **Massage Type** (`massage_type`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more of the massage types you received, including:
  - Essential Oil Massage
  - Aromatherapy
  - Swedish Massage
  - Deep Tissue Massage
  - Other
- **Massage Quality** (`massage_quality`, `number`, not required): Rate the quality of the massage you received, using the numbers 1-5.
  - 1: Very poor
  - 2: Poor
  - 3: Average
  - 4: Good
  - 5: Excellent
- **Therapist Performance** (`therapist_performance`, `number`, not required): Rate the performance of your massage therapist, using the numbers 1-5.
  - 1: Very poor
  - 2: Poor
  - 3: Average
  - 4: Good
  - 5: Excellent
- **Relaxation Level** (`relaxation_level`, `number`, not required): Rate how relaxed you felt after the massage, using the numbers 1-5.
  - 1: Not relaxed at all
  - 2: Somewhat relaxed
  - 3: Neutral
  - 4: Somewhat relaxed
  - 5: Very relaxed
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience, using the numbers 1-5.
  - 1: Very dissatisfied
  - 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
  - 3: Neutral
  - 4: Somewhat satisfied
  - 5: Very satisfied
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for contact information.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for contact information.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
