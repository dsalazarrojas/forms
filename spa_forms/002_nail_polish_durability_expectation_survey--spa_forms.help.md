# Nail Polish Durability Expectation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your nail polish expectations, specifically regarding durability and removal. Please fill out this form to provide accurate information to our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Client Information" page and enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. For each polish color, please select the corresponding questions and provide the requested information.
3. Make sure to select the correct polish color from the options provided.
4. Estimate the removal time for each polish and enter it in the corresponding field.
5. Provide accurate information about the removal method and removal cost for each polish.
6. Review your form and make sure to enter all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (form_1)** (`Client Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and email address to help us contact you regarding this survey.
* **Polish Color (polish_1_color)** (`Polish Color`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the color of your polish from the provided options.
* **Polish Wear Time (polish_1_wear_time)** (`Polish Wear Time`, `select_one`, required/optional): Estimate the wear time for this polish.
* **Polish Quality (polish_1_quality)** (`Polish Quality`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the quality level of your polish from the provided options.
* **Polish Removal Method (polish_1_removal_method)** (`Polish Removal Method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the removal method used for this polish.
* **Wear Time (polish_2_wear_time)** (`Wear Time`, `select_one`, required/optional): Estimate the wear time for this polish.
* **Quality (polish_2_quality)** (`Quality`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the quality level of your polish from the provided options.
* **Removal Method (polish_2_removal_method)** (`Removal Method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the removal method used for this polish.
* **Polish Color (polish_2_color)** (`Polish Color`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the color of your polish from the provided options.
* **Removal Time (polish_2_removal_time)** (`Removal Time`, `number`, required/optional): Estimate the removal time for this polish.
* **Removal Cost (polish_2_removal_cost)** (`Removal Cost`, `number`, required/optional): Estimate the removal cost for this polish.
* **Form 3 (form_3)** (`Form 3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any other relevant information about this polish.
