# Spa Business Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Business Loan Application Form is designed to provide an easy-to-use interface for spa businesses to submit loan applications for financial assistance. This form helps us understand your business needs and requirements to make an informed decision on your loan request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked as required.
2. Make sure you have all necessary documents and information to complete each field.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Spa Business Loan Application Form** (`spa_business_loan_form_data`, text, not required)
This is the title of the form that is not required to be filled out.

### 2. **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, not required)
Please enter the name of the business owner.

### 3. **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, not required)
Enter the name of your spa business.

### 4. **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, not required)
Provide the address of your business location.

### 5. **City** (`city`, text, not required)
Enter the city where your business is located.

### 6. **State** (`state`, text, not required)
Enter the state where your business is located.

### 7. **Zip** (`zip`, text, not required)
Enter the zip code of your business location.

### 8. **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required)
Enter a contact person's name and phone number for follow up.

### 9. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required)
Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.

### 10. **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required)
Enter the phone number of your business.

### 11. **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, not required)
Select the type of business (Spa, Wellness, Medical, or Other).

### 12. **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, not required)
Enter the amount of loan you are requesting.

### 13. **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, number, not required)
Enter the term of the loan in months or years.

### 14. **Collateral** (`collateral`, text, not required)
Describe any collateral you are offering for the loan.

### 15. **Business License** (`business_license`, text, not required)
Enter your business license number or name.

### 16. **Business License State** (`business_license_state`, select_one, not required)
Select yes or no if you have a business license.

### 17. **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, text, not required)
Enter your tax ID number.

### 18. **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, not required)
Enter the name of your bank.

### 19. **Bank Account** (`bank_account`, text, not required)
Enter your bank account number.

### 20. **Business Documents** (`business_documents`, text, not required)
Describe the documents you are attaching for your loan request.

### 21. **Documents Uploaded** (`documents_uploaded`, text, not required)
Describe the documents you have uploaded.

### 22. **Loan Decision Date** (`loan_decision_date`, date, not required)
Enter the date of the loan decision.

### 23. **Loan Decision Time** (`loan_decision_time`, time, not required)
Enter the time of the loan decision.

### 24. **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required)
Additional notes about your loan request.

### 25. **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, not required)
Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
