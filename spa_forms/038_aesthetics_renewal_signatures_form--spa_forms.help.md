# Aesthetics Renewal Signatures Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aesthetics Renewal Signatures Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients who have previously received aesthetic treatments to ensure they agree to ongoing treatments and acknowledge current clinic policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, fill in your client ID and name.
2. Select your date of birth to verify your identity.
3. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. Answer the health status update question, indicating if you have experienced any changes since your last visit.
5. If you have experienced changes, provide a description of these changes in the text field.
6. Confirm if you have made any changes to your medications; if so, list them in the text field.
7. Select your current pregnancy or breastfeeding status.
8. Reaffirm your consent for ongoing treatments by selecting the appropriate option.
9. Acknowledge the clinic's policies by selecting the correct option.
10. Sign and date the form to finalize your renewal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Client Identification --**:
	+ **Client ID** (`client_id_header`): This is a unique identifier assigned to your client record. Please leave this field as is.
	+ **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`): Select your date of birth to confirm your identity.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your active email address for communication purposes.
* **-- Health Status Update --**:
	+ **Any changes to your health?** (`health_changes`): Answer yes or no to indicate if you've experienced any changes since your last visit.
	+ **Description of Changes** (`health_changes_desc`): If you answered yes, provide a detailed description of the changes you've experienced.
* **Any changes to medications?** (`med_changes`): Answer yes or no to indicate if you've made any changes to your medications.
* **Updated Medication List** (`med_list`): If you answered yes, list your updated medication list in this field.
* **Pregnancy/Breastfeeding Status** (`pregnancy_check`): Select your current status to confirm whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
* **-- Consent Renewal --**:
	+ **Reaffirm my original consent for ongoing treatments** (`consent_renewal`): Select I Reaffirm to confirm your consent.
* **Clinic Policy Acknowledgment** (`policy_acknowledgment`): Select I Agree to acknowledge the current clinic policies.
* **Today's Date** (`signature_date`): This will be automatically filled in based on your system date and time.
* **Client Signature** (`signature`): Sign this form to confirm your consent and renew your agreement.
