# Wellness Retreat Holistic Product Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers or users to request access to massage, yoga, meditation, or other holistic services offered by the wellness retreat. The form helps us understand their product preferences, contact information, and payment methods to provide a smooth and personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product or service you are interested in from the options provided.
2. Choose your preferred date and time for the service.
3. Provide your contact email address.
4. Provide your contact phone number.
5. List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
6. Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Cash, PayPal, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select a Product** (`product_request`, select_one, required): Choose the specific massage, yoga, or meditation service you are interested in. Selecting the correct option will help us provide the best possible experience for you.
* **Preferred Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, required): Choose a date and time that suits you best for your service. Please ensure you have enough time for a full session.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them here so we can take necessary precautions.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with further details about your service.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with further details.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional questions or requests, please use this field to provide more information.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, text, optional): If you have any other questions or need further clarification, please use this field to provide details.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your service (Credit Card, Cash, PayPal, or Other).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): If you have any special instructions or requests for your service, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Please select your preferred product/service carefully as this will determine the specifics of your session.
- Ensure you have enough time for the full session on your preferred date and time.
- If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them accurately to ensure your safety.
- If you have any additional questions or requests, please use the "Additional Comments" or "Additional Questions" fields to provide more information.
- Please choose a payment method that suits you best to avoid any inconvenience during the checkout process.
