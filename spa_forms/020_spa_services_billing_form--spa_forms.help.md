# Spa Services Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Spa Services Billing Form is used to collect billing information for services provided to clients, such as Massages, Facials, and Haircuts.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the available services offered by the spa to select the correct service.
2.  Enter the date of service using the date field.
3.  Select the duration of the service using the time field.
4.  Enter the amount to be billed.
5.  Click "Submit" when you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Billing Service** (`billing_service`, select_one, required): Select the service provided to the client from the available options: Massage, Facial, or Haircut.
    Enter the date of service in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Billing Date** (`billing_date`, date, required): Enter the time of service in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **Billing Time** (`billing_time`, time, required): Enter the amount to be billed using the format $X.XX or X.XX, where X is the amount.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure accurate billing and processing. 
*   Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are complete. 
*   If you need help or clarification on any field, please contact the spa staff.
