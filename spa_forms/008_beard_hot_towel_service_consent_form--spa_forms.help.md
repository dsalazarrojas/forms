# Beard Hot Towel Service Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent and information from clients for the "Hot Towel Service" treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your first and last name in the first two fields.
* Enter your phone number and email address in the next two fields.
* Enter your date of birth.
* Answer the following questions about your health and preferences:
	+ What are your current skin conditions?
	+ What is your facial hair type?
	+ Are you sensitive to heat?
	+ Have you recently shaved or cut your beard?
	+ Do you have any open cuts or wounds on your face?
	+ Do you have any allergies to products?
* Schedule your service:
	+ Select the date and time for your treatment.
* Confirm your understanding and consent:
	+ Do you understand the hot towel service process?
	+ Are you aware of the potential risks (burns, irritation, or redness)?
	+ Do you consent to the treatment?
	+ Will you follow aftercare instructions?
* Sign and date your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Current Skin Conditions**: Describe any skin conditions you have (e.g. sensitive skin, eczema, psoriasis).
* **Facial Hair Type**: Select the type of your facial hair (Coarse, Medium, Fine, Curly, or Straight).
* **Sensitivity to Heat**: Indicate if you are sensitive to heat (None, Mild, Moderate, High).
* **Recently Shaved or Cut**: Indicate if you have recently shaved or cut your beard (Yes, No).
* **Open Cuts or Wounds**: Indicate if you have any open cuts or wounds on your face (Yes, No).
* **Known Allergies to Products**: If you have any allergies to products, describe them.
* **Scheduled Service Date**: Select the date for your service.
* **Service Time**: Select the time for your service.
* **I Understand the Hot Towel Service Process**: Confirm that you understand the process.
* **I Acknowledge Potential Risks**: Confirm that you are aware of the potential risks.
* **I Consent to the Hot Towel Service**: Confirm that you consent to the treatment.
* **I Will Follow Aftercare Instructions**: Confirm that you will follow aftercare instructions.
* **Client Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Consent Date**: Date your consent.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, and if you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
