# Nail Salon Customer Retention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experiences with the salon, with the goal of evaluating service quality and customer retention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the type of service you received at the salon from the service type field.
2. Identify the technician who provided your service using the nail technician field.
3. Indicate the date of your visit using the date field.
4. Answer the questions about your satisfaction with the service quality and your visit frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **salon_id** (Number): This field allows you to enter your salon's ID number, but it is not required.
* **visit_date** (Date): Please enter the date of your visit to the salon.
* **service_type** (Select One): Choose the type of service you received, such as Basic Manicure or Spa Day.
* **nail_technician** (Select Multiple): Select the technicians who provided your service.
* **satisfaction** (Select One): Choose how satisfied you were with your visit, with options for satisfied, dissatisfied, or not applicable.
* **service_quality** (Select One): Rate the quality of the service, with options for excellent, good, or fair.
* **visit_habits** (Select Multiple): Indicate how often you visit the salon, with options for regularly, occasionally, or rarely.
* **email** (Email): If you would like to provide your email address, please enter it here.
* **phone** (Text): If you would like to provide your phone number, please enter it here.
* **comments** (Text): If you have any comments or suggestions for the salon, please enter them here.
* **recommended** (Select One): Indicate whether you would recommend the salon to others, with options for true, false, or not applicable.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions truthfully to help the salon understand your experience.
* If you have any suggestions or comments for the salon, please use the comments field.
* If you would like to provide your contact information, please do so to be kept in touch with the salon.
