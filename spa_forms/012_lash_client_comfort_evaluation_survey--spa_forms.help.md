# lash_client_comfort_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the comfort level and satisfaction of clients after receiving a treatment, likely in a medical or aesthetic context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the **Introduction** section to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Enter your **client_name** and **email** in the corresponding fields.
3. Answer the questions about your **comfort_level**, **pain_level**, and **satisfaction** in the select-one fields.
4. If applicable, provide the name of the technician who performed the treatment in the **technician_name** field.
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience in the **any_comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a note field that serves as an introduction to the form.
* **client_name**: Enter your name as the client who received the treatment.
* **email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for follow-up.
* **comfort_level**: Rate your overall comfort level during the treatment on a scale from **Very comfortable** to **Not comfortable at all**.
* **pain_level**: Rate the level of pain you experienced during the treatment on a scale from **None** to **Severe**.
* **satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the treatment on a scale from **Very satisfied** to **Not satisfied at all**.
* **technician_name**: If you wish to provide the name of the technician who performed the treatment, enter it here.
* **any_comments**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide your true feelings and experiences.
* If you have any suggestions or complaints, please provide them in the **any_comments** field.
* This form is for the benefit of the treating professionals to improve future treatments.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
