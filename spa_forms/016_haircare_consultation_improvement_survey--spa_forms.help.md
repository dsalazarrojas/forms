# Haircare Consultation Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "Haircare Consultation Improvement Survey," is used to gather feedback from clients on their recent haircare consultation experience. The purpose of this form is to identify areas of improvement and to ensure that our haircare services meet our clients' needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "What did we get right?" section, which allows you to provide feedback on the aspects of your recent haircare consultation that met your expectations.
2. Next, move on to the "What did we get wrong?" section, where you can mention any areas that did not meet your expectations.
3. Please indicate if you are available for a follow-up consultation in the "Are you available for a follow-up consultation?" section.
4. Enter your contact email address in the "Email" field.
5. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Answer the "What is your age?" question by entering a numerical value.
7. Select your gender from the options provided in the "What is your gender?" question.
8. Choose your hair type from the options provided in the "What is your hair type?" question.
9. Select the hair services you are interested in from the options provided in the "What is your preferred service?" question.
10. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What did we get right?** (`consultation_improvement_survey_page`, text, required): This section is where you can provide feedback on the aspects of your recent haircare consultation that met your expectations.
* **What did we get wrong?** (`consultation_improvement_survey_what_we_didnt_get_right_page`, text, required): This section is where you can mention any areas that did not meet your expectations.
* **Are you available for a follow-up consultation?** (`consultation_improvement_survey_follow_up_page`, select_one, required): Please indicate if you are available for a follow-up consultation.
* **Email** (`consultation_improvement_survey_email_page`, email, required): Please enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`consultation_improvement_survey_phone_page`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **What is your age?** (`consultation_improvement_survey_age_page`, number, required): Answer this question with a numerical value representing your age.
* **What is your gender?** (`consultation_improvement_survey_gender_page`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **What is your hair type?** (`consultation_improvement_survey_hair_type_page`, select_one, required): Choose your hair type from the provided options.
* **What is your preferred service?** (`consultation_improvement_survey_service_page`, select_multiple, required): Select the hair services you are interested in from the options provided.
* **Note** (`consultation_improvement_survey_note_page`, note, required): Add any additional comments or feedback in this field.
