# spa_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "spa_consent_form" is a form for obtaining customer or employee consent for a spa or wellness-related service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field according to the following instructions.
2. Make sure to provide accurate information in each field.
3. Review your completed form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### form_header (page 1)
* **form_header** (`page 1`, `note`, not required): The form header is a note field that serves as a heading for the form.

### customer_signature (page 2)
* **client_signature** (`customer_signature`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your response to the question of whether you give your consent for the spa or wellness-related service.

### service_details (page 3)
* **service_details** (`service_details`, `text`, not required): Provide a description of the spa or wellness-related service you are giving your consent for.

### signature (page 4)
* **signature** (`signature`, `number`, required): Enter a unique identifier or number to represent your consent.

### date (page 5)
* **date** (`date`, `not required): Enter the date you are giving your consent for the spa or wellness-related service.

### time (page 6)
* **time** (`time`, `not required): Enter the time you are giving your consent for the spa or wellness-related service.

### assigned_user (page 7)
* **assigned_user** (`assigned_user`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the service is assigned to you or not.

### completed_by (page 8)
* **completed_by** (`completed_by`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the service is completed by you or not.

## Tips
- Be accurate and clear when providing your responses.
- Make sure to review your form before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your administrator or customer support.
