# spa_health_code_compliance_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spa health code compliance inspection form is used to inspect and evaluate the compliance of a spa facility with local health and safety regulations.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out all relevant sections that apply to your facility.
2.  Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
3.  Make sure to answer all questions completely and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Spa Inspection Form**: This is the main section of the form where you will be listing all relevant details about your facility.
* **Facility Info**: Provide any other relevant information about your facility.
* **General Info**: This section is for any other general information about your facility.
* **Equipment Details**: List all the equipment you have in your facility, including electrical and water equipment.
* **Electrical Equipment Details**: Provide a detailed description of your electrical equipment.
* **Water Equipment Details**: Describe your water equipment.
* **Chemical Equipment Details**: List your chemical equipment.
* **Waste Disposal**: Describe your waste disposal procedures.
* **Pest Control**: Explain your pest control methods.
* **Fire Safety**: Describe your fire safety procedures.
* **Emergency Procedure**: Describe your emergency procedures.
* **Spa License**: Provide your spa license number.
* **Inspector Notes**: Add any notes or comments from the inspector.
* **Facility Manager Signature**: Sign this section to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign this section to confirm that the inspection was completed.
* **Inspection Date**: Date of the inspection.
* **Spa ID**: Your spa ID number.
* **Facility ID**: Your facility ID number.
* **Facility Address**: Provide your facility's address.
* **Facility City**: Provide your facility's city.
* **Facility State**: Provide your facility's state.
* **Facility Zip**: Provide your facility's zip code.
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Provide your contact email.
* **Assigned User**: Provide the name of the assigned user.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to ensure the form is completed correctly.
