# Hair Texture Relaxation Service Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from clients before providing hair texture relaxation services. It ensures clients are informed about potential risks and consequences of the treatment and provide their consent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, "Client Details", enter your name and any other personal details as required.
2. On the second page, "Allergies and Medical Conditions", list any known allergies or medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
3. On the third page, "Risks", carefully read and acknowledge the potential risks associated with the hair texture relaxation service.
4. On the fourth page, "Signature", provide your signature to confirm your understanding and consent for the treatment.

* **Client Details** (`hair_texture_relaxation_service_consent_form_1`, text, optional): This section captures your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Allergies and Medical Conditions** (`hair_texture_relaxation_service_form_2`, text, optional): List any known allergies or medical conditions that may impact your treatment. This will help our staff provide you with the best possible care.
* **Risks** (`chemical_relaxing_service_risks_1`, text, optional): Carefully read and acknowledge the potential risks associated with the hair texture relaxation service. This is an essential step to ensure you understand the potential consequences of the treatment.
* **Risks** (`hair_texture_relaxation_service_risks_2`, text, optional): Another section to acknowledge the potential risks associated with the hair texture relaxation service. Please review this carefully to ensure you understand what you're consenting to.
* **Signature** (`hair_texture_relaxation_service_consent_form_3`, text, optional): Provide your signature to confirm your understanding and consent for the treatment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have an email address, please enter it so we can contact you with any updates or questions.
