# skin_detox_program_appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients schedule appointments and provide information related to skin detox programs. It collects details about the program, client, and appointment scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Program Details" section to provide information about the skin detox program.
2. In the "Skin Details" section, enter any relevant information about the skin detox program.
3. Move on to the "Health Details" section to share any health-related information about the client.
4. Schedule the appointment in the "Schedule Appointment" section, providing the date, time, and other relevant details.
5. Add payment information in the "Payment Details" section, if applicable.
6. Complete the "Client Info" section with the client's contact information.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.
8. Enter the client's email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" sections, respectively.
9. Select the program type, duration, frequency, and status in the "Program Type", "Program Duration", "Program Frequency", and "Program Status" sections.
10. Confirm the scheduled time and date in the "Schedule Time", "Date", "Schedule Time2", and "Date2" sections.
11. Select the contact person and payment method in the "Contact Person" and "Payment Method" sections.
12. Enter any additional payment details in the "Payment Details" section.
13. Finally, review your information and submit the form to complete the skin detox program appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Details** (Program Details): Enter a brief description of the skin detox program.
* **Skin Details** (Skin Details): Enter any relevant skin detox program details.
* **Health Details** (Health Details): Provide any health-related information about the client.
* **Schedule Appointment** (Schedule Appointment): Schedule the appointment, including date, time, and other details.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details): Enter any payment information, if applicable.
* **Client Info** (Client Info): Complete the client's contact information.
* **Notes** (Notes): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Schedule Time** (Schedule Time): Select the schedule time.
* **Date** (Date): Select the date of the appointment.
* **Program Type** (Program Type): Select the type of skin detox program.
* **Program Duration** (Program Duration): Select the duration of the program.
* **Program Frequency** (Program Frequency): Select the frequency of the program.
* **Schedule Time2** (Schedule Time2): Select an additional schedule time.
* **Date2** (Date2): Select an additional date.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person): Select the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number): Enter the contact number.
* **Program Status** (Program Status): Select the status of the program.
* **Program Price** (Program Price): Enter the program price.
* **Notes2** (Notes2): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method): Select the payment method.
* **Email2** (Email2): Enter the email address.
* **Phone2** (Phone2): Enter the phone number.
* **Schedule Time3** (Schedule Time3): Select an additional schedule time.
