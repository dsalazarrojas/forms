# Spa Manager Leadership Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register spa managers for the Spa Manager Leadership Training program. It collects essential information to tailor the training to each manager's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Phone Number**, as this is the primary contact method for communication and training updates.
3. Provide the name of the **Spa Location** where you work.
4. Enter the **Manager ID** assigned to you.
5. Select the **Training Date** you are available to attend.
6. Choose the **Training Time** that suits you best.
7. Enter the location of the **Training Session**.
8. Select the **Number of Training Hours** you require.
9. Add any additional **Manager Notes** and **Spa Notes** about your training needs or concerns.
10. Indicate your **Training Agenda** interest (Yes/No).
11. Select your preferred **Payment Method** for the training.
12. Enter your **Manager Email**, **Manager Phone Number**, **Manager Address**, **Spa Manager Email**, **Spa Manager Phone Number**, and **Spa Manager Address**.
13. Specify your **Manager Availability** for the training session.
14. Add any additional **Note** about your training registration.
15. Enter the **Spa Name** where you work.
16. Provide the **Spa Address**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number as the primary contact method.
* **Spa Location**: Enter the name of the location where you work.
* **Manager ID**: Enter the ID assigned to you as a manager.
* **Training Date**: Select the date you are available to attend the training session.
* **Training Time**: Choose the time slot that best suits you for the training session.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the training session will take place.
* **Training Hours**: Specify the number of hours you require for the training session.
* **Manager Notes**: Add any additional comments or concerns about your training needs.
* **Spa Notes**: Add any additional comments or concerns about your training needs or spa operations.
* **Training Agenda**: Indicate your interest in the training agenda (Yes/No).
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method for the training session.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your work email address for communication and updates.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your work phone number as the primary contact method.
* **Manager Address**: Enter your work address for communication and updates.
* **Spa Manager Email**: Enter the email address of your spa's management team.
* **Spa Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of your spa's management team.
* **Spa Manager Address**: Enter the address of your spa's management team.
* **Manager Availability**: Specify your availability for the training session.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or concerns about your training registration.
* **Spa Name**: Enter the name of your spa.
* **Spa Address**: Enter the address of your spa.
