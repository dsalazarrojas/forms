# Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from you, our valued client, to better understand your needs and preferences for a massage session. By providing the required details, you'll be assisting our massage therapists in tailoring a personalized experience just for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Age in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the benefits of massage therapy you have experienced in the text field.
3. Indicate whether you have any known medical conditions.
4. Enter your Contact Number to help us reach out to you for scheduling purposes.
5. Provide your Email address so we can stay in touch.
6. Choose your Preferred Time from the dropdown list.
7. Select your Preferred Day from the dropdown list.
8. Choose whether you're interested in receiving a message from a massage therapist.
9. Select the Type of Massage you'd like to receive.
10. Select the Area of Focus for your massage session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age**: Please enter your age for our records.
* **What benefits of massage therapy have you experienced?**: Describe the benefits of massage therapy that you have experienced, such as relaxation, pain relief, etc.
* **Medical Condition**: Indicate whether you have any known medical conditions that may impact your massage session.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a valid phone number where we can reach you for scheduling and other purposes.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address so we can stay in touch.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose your preferred time for a massage session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Preferred Day**: Choose your preferred day for a massage session (Monday to Sunday).
* **Interested in receiving a message from a massage therapist?**: Indicate whether you'd like to receive a message from a massage therapist to discuss your session details.
* **Type of Massage**: Select the type of massage you'd like to receive (S Swedish, Deep Tissue, Sports).
* **Area of Focus**: Select the area(s) of your body you'd like to focus on during your massage session (Back, Neck, Shoulders, Arms, Legs).
