# Medical Aesthetic Procedures Delegation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about medical aesthetic procedures, allowing healthcare providers to track and document procedures performed on clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for provider information, including your name, title, and signature.
2. Provide the client's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact number.
3. Select the type of medical aesthetic procedure performed using the drop-down menu.
4. Enter the date and time of the procedure.
5. Add any relevant notes about the procedure and the client's medical history.
6. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Information** (`provider_information`, `text`, required): Enter your name, title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): If necessary, provide a brief summary of the client's medical history, including any relevant allergies or concerns.
* **Procedure Description** (`procedure_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the medical aesthetic procedure performed, including any relevant details or complications.
* **Provider Notes** (`provider_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the procedure.
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's name and any other relevant demographic information.
* **Name** (`client_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`client_dob`, `date`, optional): Record the client's date of birth.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's contact number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's medical record number, if applicable.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether the procedure was provided by a medical professional.
* **Medical Aesthetic Procedure** (`medical_aesthetic_procedure`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of medical aesthetic procedure performed from the drop-down menu.
* **Date of Procedure** (`date_of_procedure`, `date`, optional): Record the date of the procedure.
* **Time of Procedure** (`time_of_procedure`, `time`, optional): Record the time of the procedure.
* **Medical Aesthetic Procedure Description** (`medical_aesthetic_procedure_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the medical aesthetic procedure performed, including any relevant details.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, optional): Include the client's signature, if applicable.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, `note`, optional): Include your signature, if applicable.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional; however, it's recommended that you fill in as much information as possible to ensure accurate and complete record-keeping.
