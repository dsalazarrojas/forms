# Purpose
This form is required to ensure that the client provides informed consent to the massage therapy session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Client Name, Contact Number, and Contact Email) in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the type of massage you would like to receive (Swedish, Deep Tissue, Sports, or Myofascial Release).
3. Choose the pressure level you would like to receive (Light, Moderate, Firm, or Strong).
4. Indicate the areas you would like to be treated (yes or no) by selecting the corresponding options.
5. Confirm your consent by signing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Number**: This is the number we will use to contact you about your massage appointment.
* **Contact Email**: Please enter your email address so we can reach you with updates and reminders.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please list them here.
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please list them here.
* **Massage Type**: Choose the type of massage you would like to receive. Our options are:
	+ **Swedish**: A relaxing and soothing massage.
	+ **Deep Tissue**: A more intense massage targeting deeper tissues.
	+ **Sports**: A massage designed for athletes and individuals who are active.
	+ **Myofascial Release**: A massage targeting specific areas of tension in the muscles.
* **Pressure Level**: Choose the pressure level you would like to receive:
	+ **Light**: A gentle and relaxing massage.
	+ **Moderate**: A balanced and soothing massage.
	+ **Firm**: A more intense massage.
	+ **Strong**: A very intense massage.
* **Areas Treated**: Please indicate which areas you would like to be treated:
	+ **Yes**: Select areas you would like to be treated.
	+ **No**: Select areas you do not want to be treated.
* **Time of Massage**: If you have a preference for the time of your massage, please enter it here.
* **Date of Massage**: If you have a preference for the date of your massage, please enter it here.
* **Massage Length**: If you have a preference for the length of your massage, please enter it here.
* **Massage Description**: If you have any specific requests or concerns, please describe them here.
* **Client Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Therapist Signature**: Our licensed massage therapist will sign to confirm they have read and understood your consent.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please return the completed form to us before your massage therapy session.
