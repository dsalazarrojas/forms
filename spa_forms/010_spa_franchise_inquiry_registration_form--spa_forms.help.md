# spa_franchise_inquiry_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals or businesses interested in exploring franchise opportunities with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If you need help or have questions, you can contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for us to address you properly.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for us to address you properly.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter its name.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your company or business.
* **Franchise interest** (`franchise_interest`, select_one, required): Choose how much you know about franchising:
	+ Franchising 101: You're just starting to learn about franchising.
	+ Limited liability: You have some experience with franchising but need guidance.
	+ Business partnership: You have experience with business partnerships, but not franchising specifically.
	+ Corporate: You have experience with franchises or business partnerships.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the person's name we should contact.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Franchise location** (`franchise_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your proposed business or franchise.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the website of your company or business.
* **Interests** (`franchise_interests`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of franchises you're interested in:
	+ Spa business: A spa-based business model.
	+ Salon: A beauty-focused business model.
	+ Wellness center: A wellness-focused business model.
	+ Medical spa: A medical-focused business model.
	+ Other: Other types of franchises.
* **Spa type** (`spa_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of spa you want to open:
	+ Day spa: A spa that offers services by the hour or day.
	+ Medical spa: A spa that offers medical services.
	+ Salon: A beauty-focused business model.
	+ Wellness center: A wellness-focused business model.
	+ Other: Other types of spas.
* **Franchise experience** (`franchise_experience`, select_multiple, required): Choose your level of experience with franchising:
	+ None: No experience with franchising.
	+ Some: Limited experience with franchising.
	+ Extensive: Significant experience with franchising.
* **Business plan** (`business_plan`, select_one, required): Have you prepared a business plan:
	+ True: You have a business plan in place.
	+ False: You do not have a business plan.
* **Marketing budget** (`marketing_budget`, number, optional): Enter the amount you're prepared to invest in marketing.
* **Location details** (`location_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your proposed location.
* **Franchise lead source** (`franchise_lead_source`, text, optional): Enter where you heard about our company.
* **Franchise lead status** (`franchise_lead_status`, select_one, required): Choose your status as a franchise lead:
	+ New: You're new to our franchise opportunities.
	+ Active: You're actively exploring our franchise opportunities.
	+ Converted: You've already converted or applied.
	+ Lost: You've lost interest or decided not to pursue.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be followed up:
	+ Phone: Phone call or email.
	+ Email: Email only.
	+ Email and phone: Both phone call and email.
	+ Never: Never contact me.
* **Follow up time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day you'd like to be followed up.
* **Follow up frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, text, optional): Enter how often you'd like to be followed up.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Submitted date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
