# Head Spa Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Head Spa Intake Form is used to collect client information and preferences for spa appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Client Information.
2. Provide your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Select your preferred Appointment Date.
5. Choose your preferred Appointment Time.
6. Provide any Special Requests or Services you would like.
7. Select your preferred Appointment Preferences (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
8. Select your preferred Provider(s) from the list.
9. Select your preferred Time slot from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Please enter your name, email, and other relevant contact information.
	+ **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	+ **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose the time slot you prefer for your appointment.
* **Service Details**: Enter any special requests or services you would like.
* **Special Requests**: Provide any specific needs or requirements.
* **Appointment Preferences**: Select whether you prefer Morning, Afternoon, or Evening appointment times.
* **Preferred Provider**: Select the providers you would like to be matched with.
* **Preferred Time**: Select your preferred time slot.

**Tips**
* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Please be accurate when selecting your preferred provider and time to avoid any scheduling conflicts.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, be sure to include them in the "Service Details" field.
