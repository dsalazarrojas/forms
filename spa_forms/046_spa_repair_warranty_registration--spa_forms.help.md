# Spa Repair Warranty Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your spa repair service with our organization. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth warranty registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: **spa_repair_warranty_form**, **email**, and **spa_name**. These are essential for us to process your registration properly.
2. Provide any additional information in the optional fields: **contact_info**, **service_details**, **warranty_info**, **repair_info**, and **spa_owner_signature**.
3. If you have a **customer_signature**, please include it if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **spa_repair_warranty_form**: Enter a brief description of the repair service performed on your spa.
- **contact_info**: Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and address for our records.
- **service_details**: Describe the services performed on your spa, including any relevant details about the repair process.
- **warranty_info**: Provide information about the warranty provided for your spa repair service.
- **repair_info**: Enter any relevant details about the repair process.
- **customer_signature**: If possible, include a signature from the customer who authorized the repair.
- **spa_owner_signature**: If possible, include a signature from the spa owner or representative who authorized the repair.
- **phone**: Enter your phone number for our records.
- **email**: Enter your email address for our records.
- **spa_name**: Enter the name of your spa.
- **spa_repair_warranty_form_submitted**: This field is automatically filled out by the system after submission.
Please note that you can only submit this form through our electronic system. If you're having trouble submitting, please contact our support team.
