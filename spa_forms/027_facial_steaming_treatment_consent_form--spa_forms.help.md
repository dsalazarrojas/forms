# Facial Steaming Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information and obtain consent from clients prior to undergoing a facial steaming treatment. It collects demographic information, medical condition, and consent regarding potential temporary redness or stinging sensations caused by the treatment. The form also includes space for client and doctor signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and instructions.
2. If applicable, provide information in all required fields (identified by a red star).
3. Fill in the client information (Client Name, Contact Phone, Email, Date of Birth, and Age) if it's necessary for your treatment.
4. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your treatment, please provide details in the field "Medical Condition".
5. Read and choose the consent statement that best describes your understanding of the facial steaming treatment.
6. Sign the form with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facial Steaming Treatment** (`form_1`, `note`, not required): This is a brief description of the treatment you are about to undergo. Please review this section carefully before signing the form.
* **Client Information** (`form_2`, `text`, not required): This section collects demographic information about the client. Please fill in the following fields:
	+ **Client Name**: Your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
	+ **Contact Phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
	+ **Email**: Your email address.
	+ **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
	+ **Age**: Your age.
* **Medical Condition** (`form_6`, `text`, not required): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your treatment, please provide details here.
* **Consent** (`form_7`, `select_one`, not required): Please choose one of the following statements:
	+ I understand that the facial steaming treatment may cause some temporary redness or stinging sensation.
	+ I do not understand that the facial steaming treatment may cause some temporary redness or stinging sensation.
	+ I am unsure that the facial steaming treatment may cause some temporary redness or stinging sensation.
* **Client Signature** (`form_11`, `text`, not required): Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understand the information provided.
* **Doctor Signature** (`form_12`, `text`, not required): The doctor will sign to confirm that they have reviewed and explained the treatment and its potential risks to the client.
