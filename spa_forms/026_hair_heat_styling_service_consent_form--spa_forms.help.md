# Hair Heat Styling Service Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from clients about their hair styling preferences and health considerations before providing a hair heat styling service. It will be used by service providers to ensure they have the necessary consents and information to deliver safe and effective services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Provide your client's details in the "Client Details" section.
2. Answer the questions in the "Health Information" section about any health considerations that may affect your client's hair styling service.
3. Read and sign the "Consent" section to confirm your understanding and agreement to the service terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about your client.
* **Health Information** (`health_info`, text, required/optional): Answer any health-related questions in this section, such as allergies, medical conditions, or previous injuries that may impact your client's hair styling service.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required/optional): Read and sign this section to confirm your understanding and agreement to the service terms, including any necessary precautions and procedures that will be followed during the service.
