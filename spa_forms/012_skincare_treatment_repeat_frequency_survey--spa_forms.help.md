# Skincare Treatment Repeat Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather client feedback on their repeat visits, satisfaction with previous treatment, and any subsequent actions or visits they would like to schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select all the frequencies at which you would like to receive treatment from the "Client Treatment Frequency" page.
2. On the "Satisfaction Level" page, choose how satisfied you are with your current treatment.
3. On the "Preferred Visit Frequency" page, choose your preferred visit frequency.
4. On the "Treatment Habits" page, select any treatments you currently practice or are interested in trying.
5. On the "Satisfaction Comments" page, please provide any additional comments about your current treatment.
6. On the "Next Steps" page, choose whether you would like to schedule a follow-up visit.
7. On the "Client Visit History" page, select any previous visits you have had.
8. On the "Treatment Frequency" page, enter your treatment frequency in the provided field.
9. On the "Frequency" page, enter the frequency of your current treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Treatment Frequency** (Select multiple options):
	+ Select all the frequencies at which you would like to receive treatment.
* **Satisfaction Level** (Select one option):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with your current treatment.
* **Preferred Visit Frequency** (Select one option):
	+ Choose your preferred visit frequency.
* **Treatment Habits** (Select multiple options):
	+ Select any treatments you currently practice or are interested in trying.
* **Satisfaction Comments** (Note):
	+ Please provide any additional comments about your current treatment.
* **Next Steps** (Select one option):
	+ Choose whether you would like to schedule a follow-up visit.
* **Client Visit History** (Select multiple options):
	+ Select any previous visits you have had.
* **Treatment Frequency** (Number):
	+ Enter your treatment frequency.
* **Frequency** (Number):
	+ Enter the frequency of your current treatment.
