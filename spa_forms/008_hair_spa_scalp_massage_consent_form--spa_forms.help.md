# Hair Spa Scalp Massage Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Spa Scalp Massage Consent Form is a document intended for clients who are interested in availing themselves of our scalp massage services. This form is a required step before receiving treatment, and it's used to gather essential details that help our team ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for you. Please take a moment to read through this guide to understand the purpose of each field and complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details.
- If you have any issues or are unsure about a field, do not hesitate to contact our support team for assistance.
- Make sure to submit the form before proceeding with your treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Health Details (scald_massage_details)** (`scalp_massage_details`, text, required): This is the main section for you to provide any health concerns, allergies, or medical conditions that might affect your scalp massage experience. Be as detailed as possible to ensure our staff can tailor the treatment to your needs.
- **Phone (phone)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you if any issues arise or to confirm treatment details.
- **Email (email)** (`email`, email, required): Your email address will be used for communication purposes, such as treatment confirmation and updates.
- **Address (address)** (`address`, text, required): Provide your current address for billing and treatment logistics purposes.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any treatment delays or cancellations.
- If you've experienced any changes to your health status or conditions since the last treatment, update this form accordingly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
