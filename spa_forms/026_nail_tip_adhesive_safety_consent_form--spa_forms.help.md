# Nail Tip Adhesive Safety Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain the client's consent for nail tip adhesive safety procedures. It helps establish a legal agreement between the client and the nail technician, ensuring that both parties are aware of the potential risks and benefits associated with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Select the type of nail treatment you are seeking from the "Nail Type" field.
7. Select the nail technician(s) that will be performing the treatment from the "Nail Technician" field.
8. Check the box to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the treatment risks and benefits.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field is used to record your first name, which will be used to identify you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This field is used to record your last name, which will be used to identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is used to record your email address, which will be used to contact you if necessary.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is used to record your phone number, which will be used to contact you if necessary.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is used to record your date of birth, which will be used to track your age and medical history.
* **Nail Type** (`nail_type`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the type of nail treatment you are seeking.
*   * Select one of the following options: Acrylic, Gel, Nail Tips, Nail Wraps.
* **Nail Technician** (`nail_technician`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select the nail technician(s) that will be performing the treatment.
*   * Select one or more of the following options: John, Jane, Bob, Alice.
* **Consent** (`consent`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date and time you provided your consent for the treatment.
* **Note** (`consent_note`, note, optional): This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about your treatment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This field is used to submit the form and complete the consent process.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have read and understood all the fields before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field or requirement, please consult with your supervisor or relevant authority.
*   The completed form will be used as proof of consent, so please ensure that all fields are accurately filled out.
*   If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please inform your nail technician before treatment.
*   Please review the risks and benefits of each nail treatment option carefully before selecting.
