# hair_root_lift_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a consent form for hair root lift treatment. It helps clients provide their health details, schedule an appointment, and sign their consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your client health details in the **Client Health Details** field.
2. Enter your client's details in the **Client Details** field.
3. Provide the schedule for your appointment in the **Schedule Appointment** field.
4. Read and sign the **Signed Consent** field to confirm your consent for the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Health Details (Text)**: This field is where you enter your health details, such as any medical conditions or allergies, that the practitioner should be aware of before providing the treatment.
* **Client Details (Text)**: Enter your client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details in this field.
* **Schedule Appointment (Text)**: Provide the schedule for your appointment, including the date, time, and duration, to ensure the practitioner is aware of your availability.
* **Signed Consent (Text)**: This field is for you to sign and confirm your consent for the treatment.
