# High Frequency Skin Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High Frequency Skin Treatment Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from clients for high-frequency skin treatments. This form ensures that clients understand the risks and benefits associated with the treatment, as well as their rights and responsibilities as clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Client Information" field.
2. Provide a brief medical history in the "Medical History" field.
3. In the "Treatment Information" field, select the type of treatment you are interested in, how often you would like to receive it, and what condition you are looking to address.
4. Sign and date the form using the "Client Signature" field. This confirms that you have read and understood the consent form.
5. The "Provider Signature" field is for the healthcare provider to sign and date, confirming they have informed you of the risks and benefits of the treatment.
6. Enter the date you submitted the form in the "Date Submitted" field.
7. Enter the date the treatment was completed in the "Date Completed" field.
8. Enter the time the treatment was completed in the "Time Completed" field.
9. If you have any comments or concerns, you can enter them in the "Client Comments" field.
10. The provider can add any medical comments in the "Medical Comments" field.
11. Finally, both you and the provider must sign and date the form, confirming that all information is accurate and you are aware of the treatment risks and benefits.
