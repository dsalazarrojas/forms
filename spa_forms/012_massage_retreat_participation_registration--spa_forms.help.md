# Massage Retreat Participation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Retreat Participation Registration form is designed for individuals to register their interest in participating in a massage retreat. This form collects necessary information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the First Name field.
2. Enter your valid **Email** in the Email field.
3. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Select the start and **End Date** for your massage retreat participation.
5. Choose whether you **Yes** or **No** to participating in each of the massage types offered.
6. Select your **Preferred Massage Time** from the available options.
7. Provide any **Notes** about your experience or requirements.
8. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.
9. Confirm your **Consent** to participate in the massage retreat.
10. Indicate if you have any **Medical Concerns**.
11. List any **Allergies** you may have.
12. Describe your **Medical History**.
13. Provide information for your **Emergency Contact**, including their **Relationship**, **Number**, and **Message**.
14. Choose your **Payment Method** (Yes/No).
15. Select your **Payment Card Type** (if applicable).
16. Enter your **Card Number** (if applicable).
17. Enter your **CVV** (if applicable).
18. Enter your **Exp. Date** (if applicable).
19. Enter your **Zip Code**.
20. Confirm your **Agreement** to the massage retreat terms.
21. Sign your name in the **Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone**: Provide a working phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Start Date**: Select the date you would like to start participating in the massage retreat.
* **End Date**: Select the date you would like to end participating in the massage retreat.
* **Massage Type**: Choose which massage types you are interested in participating in.
* **Preferred Massage Time**: Select the time of day you would like to receive massages.
* **Notes**: Any additional information about your experience or requirements.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the massage retreat terms and conditions.
* **Consent**: Confirm your consent to participate in the massage retreat.
* **Medical Concerns**: Indicate if you have any medical concerns that we should be aware of.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you may have.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical history that may impact your participation in the massage retreat.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide information for your emergency contact, including their relationship, number, and message.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Indicate your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Message**: Enter a message for your emergency contact in case of an emergency.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your massage retreat participation.
* **Payment Card Type**: Select the type of payment card you will be using (if applicable).
* **Card Number**: Enter your payment card number (if applicable).
* **CVV**: Enter your credit card verification value (if applicable).
* **Exp. Date**: Enter your payment card expiration date (if applicable).
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Agreement**: Confirm your agreement to the massage retreat terms.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your participation.
