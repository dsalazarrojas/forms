# Wax Product Skin Reaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about their experience with a wax product and their skin reaction to it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User ID in the designated field.
2. Enter the name of the product you used in the Product Name field.
3. Select one or more reactions you experienced in the Reaction field.
4. Enter any relevant information about the area of skin affected by the reaction in the Skin Area field.
5. Enter the date you experienced the reaction in the Reaction Time field.
6. Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience in the Comments field.
7. Enter a rating for the product in the Product Rating field.
8. Select whether or not you would use the product again in the Will Use Again field.
9. Enter any relevant information about your skin condition in the Skin Condition field.
10. Enter any recommended products in the Recommended Products field.
11. Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience in the Additional Comments field.
12. Enter any relevant contact information in the Contact Information field.
13. Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
14. Enter any phone number associated with your contact information in the Phone Number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (1, number, required: false): Enter your unique User ID to identify your account.
* **Product Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product you used.
* **Reaction** (3, select multiple, required: false): Select one or more reactions you experienced (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Skin Area** (4, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the area of skin affected by the reaction.
* **Reaction Time** (5, date, required: false): Enter the date you experienced the reaction.
* **Comments** (6, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Product Rating** (7, number, required: false): Enter a rating for the product.
* **Will Use Again** (8, select one, required: false): Select whether or not you would use the product again (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Skin Condition** (9, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about your skin condition.
* **Recommended Products** (10, text, required: false): Enter any recommended products.
* **Additional Comments** (11, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Contact Information** (12, text, required: false): Enter any relevant contact information.
* **Email Address** (13, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (14, text, required: false): Enter any phone number associated with your contact information.

Note: All fields are optional, but filling out as many fields as possible will help us gather more accurate and comprehensive feedback.
