# lash_technician_team_meeting_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule a team meeting for lash technicians to discuss important topics or agenda items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the meeting date using the calendar picker.
2.  Choose the meeting location from the available options.
3.  Enter the start time of the meeting.
4.  Specify the duration of the meeting.
5.  Enter the name of the lash technician team attending the meeting.
6.  Provide a brief summary of the meeting subject.
7.  Add any relevant notes or comments.
8.  Choose which team members will be attending the meeting.
9.  Briefly describe the meeting agenda.
10.  If necessary, specify the action required after the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Meeting Date** (`team_meeting_date`, Date, required): Select a date for the meeting to be held. This date should be in the future.
*   **Meeting Location** (`salon_location`, Select Multiple, required): Choose the location where the meeting will take place. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm the meeting location.
*   **Start Time** (`team_meeting_start_time`, Time, required): Enter the start time of the meeting in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
*   **Meeting Duration** (`team_meeting_duration`, Number, required): Specify the duration of the meeting in minutes.
*   **Lash Technician Team Name** (`lash_technician_team_name`, Text, required): Enter the name of the team of lash technicians attending the meeting.
*   **Meeting Subject** (`team_meeting_subject`, Text, required): Provide a brief summary of the meeting subject or topic.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, Note, required): Add any relevant notes or comments about the meeting.
*   **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, Select Multiple, required): Choose which team members will attend the meeting. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm attendance.
*   **Meeting Agenda** (`meeting_agenda`, Text, required): Describe the meeting agenda or topics to be discussed.
*   **Action Required** (`action_required`, Text, required): Specify any action items that require attention after the meeting.

## Tips
- Be sure to choose a date and time that is realistic and feasible for all team members.
- Clearly communicate the meeting location and agenda to all team members.
- Ensure that all necessary team members are selected to attend the meeting.
- Take notes during the meeting to reference later if needed.
- Clearly document any actions or tasks assigned to team members after the meeting.
