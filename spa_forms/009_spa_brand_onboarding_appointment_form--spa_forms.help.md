# Spa Brand Onboarding Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential new clients or partners for a spa brand's onboarding process. It will help the spa brand to better understand the client's needs and provide a personalized onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each field explanation to understand its purpose.
- Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly to ensure accurate information is collected.
- Review your answers before submitting to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Onboarding Form** (`onboarding_form`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): This is the first section of the form where you can provide any additional information or comments about your onboarding request.
- **Spa Details** (`spa_details`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): If you have already interacted with the spa brand before, please provide your previous interaction details (e.g., previous appointment schedule, communication, or interactions).
- **Onboarding Appointment** (`onboarding_appointment`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Describe the reason or purpose of your onboarding request (e.g., new client, partner, or team member).
- **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Indicate your current status in the onboarding process (e.g., new inquiry, scheduled, or completed).
- **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, Date, Required/Optional: Required): Select a date and time for your onboarding appointment.
- **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, Date, Required/Optional: Required): Select a specific date for your onboarding appointment (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the spa brand's support team.
- Review your answers before submitting to ensure they are accurate and complete.
