# spa_party_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "spa_party_inquiry_form" is a form designed to gather information from customers for spa party reservations and inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields to provide the necessary information for your spa party reservation.
2. Enter your reservation date and time in the "Reservation Date" and "Reservation Time" fields.
3. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Customer Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
4. Provide your contact information to allow the spa team to follow up with you regarding your reservation.
5. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your inquiry to the spa team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Spa Party Inquiry Form** (`spa_party_inquiry_form_name`, text, optional): This is the title of your spa party inquiry. Please enter the name of your desired spa party treatment or package.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to schedule your spa party.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to schedule your spa party.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to ensure the spa team can identify you and follow up with your reservation.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so the spa team can contact you with further instructions and confirmations.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so the spa team can contact you with further instructions and confirmations.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct reservation date and time to ensure that the spa team can accommodate your request.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy to ensure that the spa team can contact you successfully.
* If you have any specific requests or special instructions, please include them in the "Spa Party Inquiry Form" field.
* The spa team will respond to your inquiry shortly after submission, please allow some time for them to process your request.
