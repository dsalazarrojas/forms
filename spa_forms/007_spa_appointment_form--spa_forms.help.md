## Purpose
The Spa Appointment Form is designed to allow users to schedule appointments for spa services. This form is used to collect information from clients to ensure that each appointment is tailored to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Client Info** field, which is located on the first page of the form.
2. Select a date and time for your appointment in the **Date** and **Time** fields, which are also located on the first page of the form.
3. Indicate if you would like to receive a service during your appointment by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Service** field.
4. Enter the duration of your service in the **Duration** field, which is required for all appointments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client Info (text, required)**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed. This field is required to ensure that our spa team can personalize your experience.
- **Date (date, required)**: Select a date for your appointment from the calendar. Please note that this form will only allow you to select a date within a certain range of dates, depending on our availability.
- **Time (time, required)**: Select a time for your appointment from the available options. Please choose a time that works best for you, but be aware that our spa staff may need to adjust the time if needed.
- **Service (select_one, optional)**: If you would like to receive a service during your appointment, please select **Yes** from the dropdown menu. If you do not desire any services, select **No**.
- **Duration (number, required)**: Enter the length of your appointment in the specified units (e.g., minutes). This will help our spa staff to tailor your experience accordingly.

## Tips

- Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in advance so we can accommodate your needs.
- Our spa staff will contact you to confirm your appointment details after you have submitted this form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
