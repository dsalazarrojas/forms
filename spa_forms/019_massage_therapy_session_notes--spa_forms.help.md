This form is designed to document massage therapy session notes. It is intended to provide a brief and structured way to collect information from clients, including session details, outcomes, and contact information. This form aims to help practitioners gather and record essential data that can be used for future reference, client follow-up, and possibly, for billing and administrative purposes.

The form is organized into three sections: Session Details, Session Outcomes, and Client Contacts. Each section has a specific purpose and set of fields to fill in.

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect notes from massage therapy sessions. It is a tool for practitioners to document the details of each session, which can help in providing better care to clients and improving the overall quality of service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Session Details**: This section is used to record the basic information about the client's session. Please fill in the following fields:
	* **Session Title** (text): A brief title for the session, e.g., "Back Pain Relief Session"
	* **Session Notes** (text): A detailed description of the session, including any notable issues or discussions that came up during the session.

2. **Session Outcomes**: Choose the outcomes of the session from the provided options:
	* **Positive** (select): The client experienced a positive outcome or improvement after the session.
	* **Negative** (select): The client experienced a negative outcome or no improvement after the session.
	* **No Change** (select): The client's condition showed no change after the session.
	* **Unable to assess** (select): The practitioner was unable to assess the client's outcome due to incomplete or missing information.

3. **Client Contacts**: Choose the preferred contact method for follow-up:
	* **Phone** (select): The client prefers to be contacted by phone.
	* **Email** (select): The client prefers to be contacted by email.
	* **Text** (select): The client prefers to be contacted by text message.

## Tips
- Be as concise and accurate as possible when describing the session details and outcomes.
- Make sure to choose the most accurate outcome and contact method for each session.
- If you are unsure about any information, please use the "Unable to assess" option for outcomes and choose "Unable to contact" for follow-up.

Note: This form is designed to be used in a massage therapy context, so the fields and options are tailored to meet those specific needs. However, this form can be adapted and used in other contexts with some modifications.
