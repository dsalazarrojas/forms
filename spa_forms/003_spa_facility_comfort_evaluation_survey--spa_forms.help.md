# Spa Facility Comfort Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Facility Comfort Evaluation Survey is a tool used to assess the comfort and satisfaction levels of spa facility users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Rate the overall comfort of the facility on a scale of 1-5.
3. Rate the ambiance, cleanliness, comfort, service, and favorite facilities.
4. Choose the services you used during your visit.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Provide any suggestions or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: A brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose.
* **Overall Rating (2)**: Rate the overall comfort of the facility on a scale of 1-5.
* **Ambiance Rating (3)**: Rate the ambiance of the facility on a scale of 1-5.
* **Cleanliness Rating (4)**: Rate the cleanliness of the facilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comfort Rating (5)**: Rate the comfort of the facilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Rating (6)**: Rate the service provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Favorite Facility (7)**: Choose your favorite facility from the options provided.
* **Services Used (8)**: Choose the services you used during your visit from the options provided.
* **Suggestions (9)**: Provide any suggestions or comments you may have.
* **Email (10)**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Please rate each field based on your actual experience.
* If you have not used a particular service, select "Other".
* You can skip any questions you do not want to answer.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please be specific and provide details.
* Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
