# wellness_center_treatment_reaction_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect incident reports from staff members in a wellness center, focusing on reactions and responses to incidents that have occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Staff Member" field with the name of the staff member who reported or witnessed the incident.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
3. Enter the time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field.
4. Describe the details of the reaction in the "Reaction Details" field.
5. Provide any follow-up information or actions taken in the "Follow-ups" field.
6. Enter the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
7. Describe any staff reactions or responses in the "Staff Reactions" field.
8. Enter any notes or additional information in the "Incident Notes" field.
9. Outline any next steps or actions planned in the "Next Steps" field.
10. Assign the incident to a specific staff member in the "Assigned To" field.
11. Enter the current status of any follow-up actions in the "Follow-up Status" field.
