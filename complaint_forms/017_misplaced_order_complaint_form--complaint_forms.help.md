# Misplaced Order Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report misplaced orders and provide details about the issue.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Describe the misplaced order in the "Misplaced Order" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of why the order was misplaced in the "Reason for Misplacement" field.
4. Enter the Order ID, if known.
5. Enter the expected and actual delivery dates, if known.
6. Share any additional details about the order in the "Order Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: This is where you will enter your name and contact details so we can get in touch with you to follow up on the issue.
* **Misplaced Order**: Provide a brief description of the misplaced order, including the order number or any other identifying information.
* **Reason for Misplacement**: Give a detailed explanation of why you think the order was misplaced, such as a mistake in shipping or packaging.
* **Order ID**: If you know the order ID, enter it here. This will help us to locate the order quickly.
* **Expected Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you expected the order to be delivered.
* **Actual Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you actually received the order, or when you realized it was missing.
* **Order Details**: Share any additional details about the order, such as any specific items missing or damaged, to help us to locate and correct the issue quickly.

## Tips
* Please provide as much information as possible about the misplaced order to help us to locate and correct the issue quickly.
* If you are unsure about any of the details, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
