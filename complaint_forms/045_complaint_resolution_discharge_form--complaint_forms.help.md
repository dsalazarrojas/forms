# Complaint Resolution Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Complaint Resolution Discharge Form is a crucial document used to finalize the resolution of a complaint. It ensures that all necessary information is captured, including the method of resolution, financial cost, and lessons learned from the experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that the case has been thoroughly investigated and resolved.
2. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*), as thoroughly as possible.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Complaint Case Number (*)
* **Complaint Case Number (case_closure_id)**: Enter the unique ID assigned to this complaint.

### 2. Complainant Name (*)
* **Complainant Name (complainant_name_discharge)**: Enter the name of the person or group receiving the resolution.

### 3. Final Resolution Outcome
* **Final Resolution Outcome (resolution_outcome_selection)**: Choose the primary method of resolution from the provided options.
	+ Financial Refund / Compensation: A monetary award made to the complainant.
	+ Product Replacement / Repair: Replacing or repairing the defective product.
	+ Service Credit / Discount Voucher: Awarding the complainant a voucher or credit for service.
	+ Formal Apology (No financial cost): A written apology, no financial compensation involved.
	+ Process / Policy Change: Implementing changes to policies or procedures to prevent similar issues.

### 4. Total Financial Cost of Resolution (*)
* **Total Financial Cost of Resolution (resolution_cost_decimal)**: Enter the total financial amount in USD, or 0 if none.

### 5. Has this resolution been approved by the Department Head?
* **Has this resolution been approved by the Department Head? (department_head_approval_status)**: Select the status of approval from the options provided.
	+ Yes, approved: The resolution has been formally approved.
	+ No, approval not required for this amount: The resolution does not require department head approval.
	+ Pending Approval: The resolution is still awaiting approval.

### 6. Lessons Learned from this Case
* **Lessons Learned from this Case (lessons_learned_reflection)**: Reflect on how to prevent this issue from happening again.

### 7. I confirm that this case is now resolved and no further action is required
* **I confirm that this case is now resolved and no further action is required (case_resolution_agreement_ack)**: Select whether you agree that the case is resolved and no further action is required.
	+ I so confirm: Indicating that the case is fully resolved.
	+ Other: Select if further action is required.

### 8. Date of Discharge
* **Date of Discharge (discharge_date_final)**: Enter the official date of case closure.

### 9. Staff Member Name (Discharging Officer)
* **Staff Member Name (Discharging Officer) (discharge_signature_staff)**: Enter the name of the staff member finalizing the closure.

### 10. Case Status - CLOSED
* **Case Status - CLOSED (closure_note_final)**: This is a read-only note confirming the case status.

Note: This form serves as an official record of complaint resolution, ensuring transparency and accountability within the organization. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly is crucial for maintaining a record of complaint resolution and preventing similar issues in the future.
