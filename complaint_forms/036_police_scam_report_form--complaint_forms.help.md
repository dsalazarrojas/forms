# Police Scam Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Scam Report Form is a tool used to report incidents of suspected scams or fraudulent activities to the authorities. This form helps to gather essential information about the incident, allowing the police to investigate and take action against scammers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide as much detail as possible about the incident, including the date and time it occurred.
2. Enter the suspect's name, if known.
3. Provide the location where the incident took place.
4. Share any relevant contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
5. Attach any supporting evidence, like photos or videos, that can help the police investigate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident details**: This field is a free-text area where you can provide as much information as possible about the incident, including what happened, who was involved, and any other relevant details.
* **Date of incident**: Enter the date when the scam occurred, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Suspect name**: If you know the name of the person or entity involved in the scam, enter it here.
* **Location**: Describe the location where the scam took place, including the address or a nearby landmark.
* **Contact info**: If you have any contact information for the scammer, such as a phone number or email address, provide it here.
* **Supporting evidence**: Attach any photos, videos, or other documents that can help the police investigate the scam, such as receipts or messages.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible about the incident to help the police investigate effectively.
* If you have any additional information or attachments that are not mentioned on this form, feel free to include them in the supporting evidence section.
* If you're reporting a scam that occurred online, make sure to include any relevant screenshots or URLs.
* Remember to report any other incidents you're aware of that may be related to this scam.
