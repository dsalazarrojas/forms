# Supermarket Customer Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience at a supermarket. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help us understand and address your issue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address in the respective fields.
2. Describe the issue in detail in the "Issue Description" field.
3. Select the supermarket where you made the purchase and the date and time of the purchase.
4. Enter the amount paid for the purchase.
5. Select the complaint type and multiple complaint types, if applicable.
6. Enter the date and time the issue occurred (if different from the date and time of purchase).
7. Select the status of the issue resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you wish to be addressed.
* **Address**: Enter your address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you for further clarification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can send updates about your issue.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue you experienced at the supermarket.
* **Supermarket Name**: Enter the name of the supermarket where you made the purchase.
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date of the purchase in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Purchase Time**: Enter the time of the purchase in the format 12:00 AM/12:00 PM.
* **Amount Paid**: Enter the amount you paid for the purchase.
* **Complaint Type**: Select the category that best describes your issue (e.g., "Quality of Product", "Service", "Other").
* **Select Multiple Complaint Types**: Select all complaint types that apply to your issue.
* **Select Complaint Details**: Select all complaint details that apply to your issue.
* **Issue Date**: Enter the date the issue occurred, if different from the date of purchase. (Optional)
* **Issue Time**: Enter the time the issue occurred, if different from the time of purchase. (Optional)
* **Resolution Status**: Select the status of the issue resolution (Resolved, Not Resolved, or Pending).
