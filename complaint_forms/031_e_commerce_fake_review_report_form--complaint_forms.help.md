# E Commerce Fake Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Fake Review Report Form is used to report suspicious activity on fake reviews on e-commerce platforms, such as Amazon and eBay. This form helps identify and track reviews that may be fake or misleading, allowing for quick action to be taken to remove them from the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the marketplace from which the fake review was reported (e.g., Amazon, eBay, or Other).
2. Enter the product name and link to the product page.
3. Provide a detailed description of the fake review details, including the title and body of the review.
4. Select the indicators of the fake review (e.g., Item 1, Item 2, Item 3).
5. Enter the reason for reporting the review.
6. Enter your email address and phone number (optional).
7. Enter any additional notes about the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Marketplace**: Select the marketplace from which the fake review was reported (e.g., Amazon, eBay, or Other).
  - This field is required and helps us identify the platform where the fake review was found.
- **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being reviewed.
  - This field helps us verify the product information and investigate further.
- **Product Link**: Enter the link to the product page on the marketplace.
  - This field helps us access the product information and investigate further.
- **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product at the time of the review.
  - This field helps us verify the product information and investigate further.
- **Product Rating**: Enter the rating of the product at the time of the review.
  - This field helps us verify the product information and investigate further.
- **Review Title**: Enter the title of the fake review.
  - This field helps us identify the content of the fake review.
- **Review Body**: Enter a detailed description of the fake review.
  - This field provides essential information about the fake review.
- **Review Date**: Enter the date when the fake review was posted.
  - This field helps us track the timeline of the fake review.
- **Report Date**: Enter the date when the report was submitted.
  - This field helps us track the report submission timeline.
- **Submitter Information**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number (optional).
  - This field provides contact information for follow-up questions or verification purposes.
- **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the report.
  - This field allows you to provide additional context or information about the report.
- **Fake Review Indicators**: Select one or more indicators of the fake review (e.g., Item 1, Item 2, Item 3).
  - These fields help us identify the specific characteristics of the fake review.
- **Report Reason**: Enter the reason for submitting the report.
  - This field helps us understand the motivation behind the report and take appropriate action.
- **Review Submission Status**: Enter the current status of the review submission (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
  - This field helps us track the progress of the report.
- **Review Submission History**: Enter any previous submission history of the report.
  - This field helps us track any previous attempts to submit the report.
- **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
  - This field provides contact information for follow-up questions or verification purposes.
- **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.

- **Submitter Name**: Enter your name.
  - This field provides contact information for follow-up questions or verification purposes.
- **Review Indicators List**: Select one or more indicators of the fake review (e.g., Item 1, Item 2, Item 3).
  - This field helps us identify the specific characteristics of the fake review.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete report.
- Be as detailed as possible when describing the fake review.
- If you have any further information about the review, please include it in the Submitter Notes field.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
