# Opposition To Venue Changes Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users who wish to express opposition to changes made to a venue. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to help us understand your concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: *Venue Address*, *Name of Submitter*, *Description of Complaint*, *Email of Submitter*, *Contact Number*, *Date of Complaint*, *Time of Complaint*, *Reason for Complaint*, and *Opposition to Changes*.
2. Provide a brief *Description of Complaint* explaining why you are opposing the changes.
3. If applicable, upload *Supporting Documents* that back up your opposition.
4. Fill out any optional fields if you have additional information to provide: *Opposing Party*, *Opposing Party Name*, *Opposing Party Address*, *Opposing Party Email*, *Opposing Party Phone*, *Opposing Party Contact Info*, *Opposing Party Notes*, *Opposing Party Agreement*, *Opposing Party Rems*, *Opposing Party Declaration*, *Supporting Evidence*, *Supporting Statements*, *Opposing Party Statements*.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Venue Address**: The address of the venue you are objecting to changes.
* **Name of Submitter**: Your name, as the person submitting this form.
* **Description of Complaint**: A brief description of why you are opposing the changes made to the venue.
* **Email of Submitter**: Your email address, so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Contact Number**: Your phone number, so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Date of Complaint**: The date you first noticed the changes.
* **Time of Complaint**: The time you first noticed the changes.
* **Reason for Complaint**: A detailed description of why you are opposing the changes.
* **Opposition to Changes**: A detailed description of why you are opposing the changes.
* **Supporting Documents**: Any additional documents or evidence you have that supports your opposition.
* **Opposing Party**: If you are objecting on behalf of another party, please select "yes" or enter their name.
* **Opposing Party Name**: The name of the opposing party.
* **Opposing Party Address**: The address of the opposing party.
* **Opposing Party Email**: The email of the opposing party.
* **Opposing Party Phone**: The phone number of the opposing party.
* **Opposing Party Contact Info**: Any additional contact information for the opposing party.
* **Opposing Party Notes**: Any additional notes about the opposing party.
* **Opposing Party Agreement**: If the opposing party has agreed to your opposition, select "yes".
* **Opposing Party Rems**: This field is currently unused. Please ignore.
* **Opposing Party Declaration**: If the opposing party has made a declaration, please provide it.
* **Supporting Evidence**: Any additional evidence you have to support your opposition.
* **Supporting Statements**: Any additional statements or testimony you have that supports your opposition.
* **Opposing Party Statements**: Any statements made by the opposing party.
* **Submitted By**: Your name, for your reference.

Please note that all fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if you do not have the information or feel it is unnecessary. However, please be as thorough and accurate as possible, as this will help us understand your concerns more effectively.
