# Hosting Service Downtime Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a service downtime incident from the user. It helps us understand the nature of the incident, its impact, and any relevant context, which allows us to investigate and resolve the issue promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the type of incident that occurred (Hardware, Software, or Network).
2. Enter the date the incident occurred.
3. Describe the incident in detail.
4. Choose the impact level of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Specify which services were affected by the incident.
6. Provide a detailed description of the impact of the incident.
7. Enter the date the incident was reported.
8. Choose whether the incident has been resolved.
9. Enter any additional resolution notes.
10. Enter the date the resolution was made.
11. Select whether follow-up is needed.
12. Enter any follow-up notes.
13. Enter the follow-up date.
14. Add any user comments.
15. Add any reviewer comments.
16. Add any reviewee comments.
17. Select whether the reviewer needs to follow up.
18. Select whether the reviewee needs to follow up.
19. Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
20. Specify which users were affected by the incident.
21. Enter the incident ID (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Type**: Choose the category of the incident (Hardware, Software, or Network). This helps us understand the root cause of the issue.
* **Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Description**: Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant events or circumstances surrounding it.
* **Impact**: Choose the level of impact the incident had on your services (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Affected Service**: Select all services that were affected by the incident. This will help us understand which services were impacted.
* **Affected Location**: Describe the location affected by the incident.
* **Impact Description**: Provide a detailed description of the impact of the incident, including any specific effects or consequences.
* **Reported Date**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Resolution**: Choose whether the incident has been resolved.
* **Resolution Notes**: Enter any additional information about the resolution of the incident.
* **Resolution Date**: Enter the date the resolution was made.
* **Follow-up**: Select whether follow-up is needed for the incident.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for the follow-up.
* **User Comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the user.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Reviewee Comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the reviewee.
* **Reviewer Follow-up**: Select whether the reviewer needs to follow up.
* **Reviewee Follow-up**: Select whether the reviewee needs to follow up.
* **Reporter**: Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Affected Users**: Specify which users were affected by the incident.
* **Incident ID**: Enter the incident ID (if available).
