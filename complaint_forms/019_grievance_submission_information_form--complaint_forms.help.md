# grievance_submission_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The grievance_submission_information_form is a standardized form designed to gather information about a grievance that has been submitted to the institution. This form is used to collect relevant details regarding the incident, ensuring that all necessary information is collected in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as best as possible. The fields marked as "required" must be filled out for the form to be considered complete.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure that your grievance is thoroughly understood.
3. Review your submission before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Submitter Data
**submitter_data** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to gather any additional information about the submitter. If applicable, please provide relevant details such as name, role, or position.

### 2. Complaint Type
**complaint_type** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required: false): The complaint_type field allows you to classify the type of grievance submitted. Please select the category that best matches your complaint. Options include:

*   **Physical Complaint**: for issues related to physical harm or damage
*   **Non-Physical Complaint**: for issues not related to physical harm or damage, such as administrative or service-related complaints
*   **Service**: for issues related to service or process complaints
*   **Other**: for any other type of complaint that does not fit into the above categories

### 3. Grievance Date
**grievance_date** (`<name>`, `date`, required: false): This field is used to record the date the grievance was reported or occurred. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 4. Grievance Time
**grievance_time** (`<name>`, `time`, required: false): This field is used to record the time the grievance was reported or occurred. Please enter the time in the correct format (HH:MM:SS).

### 5. Grievance Details
**grievance_details** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide a detailed description of the grievance. Please provide as much information as possible.

### 6. Grievance Image
**grievance_image** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): If an image or evidence related to the grievance is available, please upload it here.

### 7. Submitter Email
**submitter_email** (`<name>`, `email`, required: false): This field is used to collect the contact email of the submitter. Please enter a valid email address.

### 8. Submitter Phone
**submitter_phone** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the contact phone number of the submitter. Please enter a valid phone number.

### 9. Submitter Address
**submitter_address** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the contact address of the submitter. Please enter a valid address.

### 10. Submitter Name
**submitter_name** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the name of the submitter. Please enter the name of the person submitting the grievance.
