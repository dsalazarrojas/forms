# Festival Vendor Dispute Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Vendor Dispute Incident Report Form is designed to document and manage incidents or disputes between festival vendors and the festival organization. This form helps festival organizers collect and track information about incidents, their resolution, and any follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, but note that some fields are not required.
2. Select the incident type from the dropdown menu to categorize the incident.
3. Upload any relevant evidence, such as photos or documents, to the Evidence Attachment field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Date (1)
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): The date the incident occurred. This field is not required but helps track the timing of incidents.
### Vendor Name (2)
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): The name of the vendor involved in the incident.
### Incident Location (3)
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): The location where the incident occurred.
### Incident Type (4)
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options provided to categorize the incident type. This field is required to ensure accurate tracking.
### Incident Description (5)
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): A detailed description of what happened during the incident.
### Evidence Attachment (6)
* **Evidence Attachment** (`evidence_attachment`, text, optional): If available, upload any evidence related to the incident, such as photos or documents.
### Follow-up Date (7)
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): The date of any follow-up actions taken.
### Incident Outcome (8)
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options provided to indicate the outcome of the incident.
### Follow-up Notes (9)
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text, optional): Any notes or details from follow-up actions.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact festival organizers for assistance.
* Ensure that you have permission to attach evidence, if any.
* This form is intended for internal use by festival organizers; please do not share it publicly.
