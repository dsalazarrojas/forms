# Online Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online complaint form is designed to collect information about your complaints or issues with our company or organization. Please use this form to provide us with clear and concise details about your issue, and we will do our best to resolve it as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide clear and concise details about your complaint or issue.
3. Choose how satisfied you are with our service.
4. Share your feedback on how we can improve our service.
5. Fill in any additional contact information if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Please fill in your name, email address, and phone number so that we can get in touch with you to discuss your complaint.
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, required: false): Provide clear and concise details about your complaint or issue.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): If you would like to be contacted, please fill in your contact information.
* **How Satisfied Are You** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with our service, from 1-10.
* **How Can We Improve** (`customer_feedback`, note, required: false): Share your feedback on how we can improve our service.
* **Company** (`company_name`, text, required: false): If you are complaining about a specific company, please fill in their name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Fill in your email address if you would like to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Fill in your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): If you are complaining about a specific date, please fill it in.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): If you are complaining about a specific time, please fill it in.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click the submit button to send your complaint or feedback to us.

## Tips
* Please be as clear and concise as possible when providing details about your complaint or issue.
* Please do not resubmit your complaint if you have already submitted it.
* Please do not use this form to spam or abuse our system.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact our customer support team.
