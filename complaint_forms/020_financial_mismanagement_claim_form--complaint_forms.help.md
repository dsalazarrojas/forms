# Financial Mismanagement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Mismanagement Claim Form is designed to report instances of financial mismanagement within your organization. Fill out this form to initiate an investigation and potential action to rectify the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. **Report Financial Mismanagement** - Briefly describe the situation or issue you're experiencing with your finances.
2. **Report Financial Mismanagement Issues** - Describe the specific problems you've encountered, such as unauthorized expenses, discrepancies, or other financial irregularities.
3. **Provide Evidence** - Attach any relevant documents or supporting materials that demonstrate the financial mismanagement.
4. **Financial Impact** - Estimate the financial implications of the mismanagement, including any direct or indirect costs you've incurred.
5. **Submit Claim** - Enter a detailed description of your claim, following the format specified in the fields above.
6. **Review and Acknowledge** - Confirm the accuracy of your submission and acknowledge that you understand the purpose and potential consequences of this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Financial Mismanagement Issues** (Text, Optional)

* **Provide Evidence** (Text, Optional)
