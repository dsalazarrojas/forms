# Food Quality Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and address food quality complaints from customers. It's essential to follow this guide to ensure that customers have a clear understanding of what's expected from them, and for the team to efficiently process and address customer complaints.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, ensuring you provide all required information.
2. Enter your contact email and phone number so that the team can contact you for further information or follow up.
3. Describe the food item you are complaining about in the "Food Item Description" field.
4. In the "Issue Description" field, provide a clear and concise description of the issue you experienced.
5. In the "Complaint Details" field, provide any additional information that might be helpful for the team to address your complaint.
6. Select the category of your complaint from the "Complaint Category" dropdown menu.
7. If relevant, select any food allergens that you might have been exposed to in the "Food Allergens" field.
8. Choose the resolution action that you'd like the team to take in the "Resolution Action" field.
9. If necessary, attach any supporting documentation or photos in the "Attachment File" field and provide a brief description of the attachment in the "Attachment Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, required): Enter the email address that we can use to contact you about your complaint.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further information or follow up.
* **Food Item Description** (`Food Item Description`, required): Describe the food item you are complaining about in as much detail as possible.
* **Issue Description** (`Issue Description`, required): Provide a clear and concise description of the issue you experienced.
* **Complaint Details** (`Complaint Details`, required): Provide any additional information that might be helpful for the team to address your complaint.
* **Complaint Category** (`Complaint Category`, required): Select the category of your complaint from the dropdown menu.
* **Food Allergens** (`Food Allergens`, optional): If you have a food allergy, select the relevant allergen(s) that you might have been exposed to.
* **Resolution Action** (`Resolution Action`, required): Choose the resolution action that you'd like the team to take.
* **Attachment File** (`Attachment File`, optional): Attach any supporting documentation or photos to help the team address your complaint.
* **Attachment Comment** (`Attachment Comment`, optional): Provide a brief description of the attachment.

## Tips
* Please be as clear and concise as possible when describing the issue and your complaint.
* Ensure that you have attached any supporting documentation or photos before submitting the form.
* The team will contact you via phone or email to follow up on your complaint and gather more information if needed.
