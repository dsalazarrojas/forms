# damaged-goods-complaint-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about damaged goods that you have purchased from our company. Please fill out this form if you have any issues with a product you've bought from us, so we can assist you in resolving the problem.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your details in the "Customer" field.
2. Describe the damage in the "Description of Damage" field.
3. If you have any visual evidence of the damage, upload it in the "Image or Video of Damage" field.
4. Add your signature in the "Customer Signature" field to confirm your identity.
5. Provide your name in the "Customer Name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field so we can contact you about your complaint.
7. Enter your address in the "Customer Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer (id: 1)**: Enter your name as the customer who purchased the damaged goods.
* **Damaged Goods (id: 2)**: Describe the type of goods that were damaged.
* **Description of Damage (id: 3)**: Provide a detailed description of the damage, including what happened and how it affected the product.
* **Image or Video of Damage (id: 4)**: If possible, upload an image or video of the damage to help us understand the issue. This will help us to quickly resolve your complaint.
* **Customer Signature (id: 5)**: Sign your name to confirm that the information you've provided is accurate and true.
* **Customer Name (id: 6)**: Enter your name as the customer who purchased the damaged goods.
* **Customer Email (id: 7)**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your complaint and any updates.
* **Customer Address (id: 8)**: Enter your address where we can send any replacements or refunds if necessary.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form only if you have a genuine concern about a product purchased from our company.
* Make sure to attach an image or video of the damage if you have one, as it will help us to quickly resolve your complaint.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) and we'll be happy to assist you.
