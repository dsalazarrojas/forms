# Neighbor Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neighbor Complaint Form is a tool used to document and manage complaints made by residents against their neighbors. It helps to gather essential information and track the progress of the complaints.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name as the complainant in the "Complainant's Name" field.
2. Provide your contact information (phone number or email address) in the "Complainant's Contact Info" field.
3. Enter your neighbor's name in the "Neighbor's Name" field.
4. Describe the problem in the "Description of the Problem" field.
5. Indicate whether any action has been taken by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Action Taken" field.
6. Enter any witness information in the "Witness Info" field.
7. If applicable, enter the police report number in the "Police Report Number" field.
8. Enter the date you made the complaint in the "Date Complained" field.
9. If the case has been resolved, enter the date in the "Date Resolved" field.
10. Provide the officer's contact information (name or badge number) in the "Officer's Contact" field.
11. Enter the officer's name in the "Officer's Name" field.
12. Indicate whether the officer has a phone number by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Officer's Phone" field.
13. Enter the police department name in the "Police Department" field.
14. Enter the case number, if applicable, in the "Case Number" field.
15. Select the status of the case as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Case Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* Complainant's Name (`complainant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the complainant.
* Complainant's Contact Info (`complainant_contact_info`, select_one, optional): Choose your phone number or email address.
* Neighbor's Name (`neighbor_name`, text, optional): Enter your neighbor's name.
* Description of the Problem (`description`, text, optional): Describe the issue with your neighbor.
* Action Taken (`action_taken`, select_one, required): Indicate whether any action has been taken.
* Witness Info (`witness_info`, text, optional): Provide any witness information.
* Police Report Number (`police_report_num`, number, optional): Enter the police report number, if applicable.
* Date Complained (`date_complained`, date, optional): Enter the date you made the complaint.
* Date Resolved (`date_resolved`, date, optional): Enter the date the case was resolved.
* Officer's Contact (`officer_contact`, select_one, optional): Choose the officer's name or badge number.
* Officer's Name (`officer_name`, text, optional): Enter the officer's name.
* Officer's Phone (`officer_phone`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the officer has a phone number.
* Police Department (`police_dept`, text, optional): Enter the police department name.
* Case Number (`case_number`, number, optional): Enter the case number, if applicable.
* Case Status (`case_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the case as "Active" or "Inactive".

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the complaint can be efficiently handled.
* If the case has been resolved, update the "Date Resolved" field.
* If the officer's phone number is applicable, select "Yes" in the "Officer's Phone" field.
* Ensure that the "Case Status" field is filled in as "Active" or "Inactive".
