# divorce_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The divorce complaint form is designed to gather information about a divorce complaint. This form is intended to be used by individuals who are going through a divorce process and need to provide details about their situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the first page, which asks for the reason for divorce. Please select the most accurate description of why you are seeking a divorce.
2.  On the second page, please provide the name of your spouse.
3.  On the third page, describe the reason for separation.
4.  On the fourth page, enter the date of separation.
5.  On the fifth page, describe the court order.
6.  On the sixth page, list any divorce documents you are submitting.
7.  On the seventh page, describe the custody arrangement.
8.  On the eighth page, list any assets involved in the divorce.
9.  On the ninth page, report any debt related to the divorce.
10. On the tenth page, describe the financial support arrangements.
11. On the eleventh page, list the grounds for divorce.
12. On the twelfth page, describe the divorce filing fees.
13. On the thirteenth page, describe the divorce process.
14. On the fourteenth page, list any other expenses related to the divorce.
15. On the fifteenth page, enter the reason for divorce (if not already provided).
16. On the last page, confirm that you have filled out all pages accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reason for Divorce** (`reason_for_divorce`, text, required): This is the main reason for your divorce complaint. Please select the most accurate description from the provided options.
* **Spouse Name** (`spouse_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your spouse, as it appears on official documents.
* **Reason for Separation** (`reason_for_separation`, text, required): Describe the reason for your separation from your spouse.
* **Date of Separation** (`date_of_separation`, date, required): Enter the date of your separation from your spouse.
* **Court Order** (`court_order`, text, required): Describe any court orders or rulings related to your divorce.
* **Divorce Documents** (`divorce_documents`, text, required): List any divorce documents you are submitting as evidence.
* **Custody Arrangement** (`custody_arrangement`, text, required): Describe the custody arrangement for any children involved.
* **Assets** (`assets`, text, required): List any assets involved in the divorce, such as property or investments.
* **Debt** (`debt`, text, required): Report any debt related to the divorce.
* **Financial Support** (`financial_support`, text, required): Describe the financial support arrangements, such as alimony or child support.
* **Grounds for Divorce** (`grounds_for_divorce`, text, required): This field is intended to gather more details about the reasons for your divorce.
* **Divorce Filing Fees** (`divorce_filing_fees`, text, required): Describe the fees associated with filing for divorce.
* **Divorce Process** (`divorce_process`, text, required): Describe the process you have undergone or are undergoing for your divorce.
* **Other Expenses** (`other_expenses`, text, required): List any other expenses related to the divorce.
* **Reason for Divorce 2** (`reason_for_divorce_2`, text, optional): This field is intended to gather more details about the reasons for your divorce. (This field is not a substitute for the first reason for divorce field.)
* **Reason for Divorce 2** (`reason_for_divorce_2`, text, optional): This field is intended to gather more details about the reasons for your divorce. (This field is not a substitute for the first reason for divorce field.)
