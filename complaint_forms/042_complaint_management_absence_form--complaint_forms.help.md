# Complaint Management Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Complaint Management Absence Form is designed to collect detailed information regarding employee absence complaints, including disputes over leave balances, policies, or other issues. This form will help the complainant provide a clear and structured account of their issue, allowing for efficient investigation and resolution by HR or management.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, please select the "Employee Full Name" field and enter your full legal name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2.  Next, select your current "Department" from the available options. This field is required for tracking and resolving issues efficiently.
3.  Enter the name of your "Reporting Manager" to facilitate proper communication and resolution.
4.  Provide a specific "Date of Disputed Absence" where the issue occurred.
5.  Select the primary reason for your absence complaint from the list of options under "Nature of Absence Complaint". This will help in categorizing and addressing the complaint correctly.
6.  Enter a detailed description of the dispute under "Detailed Narrative of Dispute". This is your opportunity to fully explain the nature of your complaint.
7.  If you have already provided supporting documentation, select "Yes, already submitted" under "Have you provided supporting documentation?".
8.  If you have not submitted documentation but plan to, select "No, but will submit" to indicate that you will provide it later.
9.  If documentation is not required for your case, select "No, not required" from the same field.
10.  Provide any "Expected Impact on Leave Balance" that you believe will occur due to this dispute.
11.  Under "Desired Resolution from HR", explain the expected resolution or outcome you are seeking from HR regarding the issue.
12.  Enter the current date as the "Date of Submission" to record the form submission date.
13.  Finally, acknowledge the accuracy of the information provided by selecting "I so certify" under "I certify that the information provided is accurate".

## Tips

- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to facilitate efficient resolution of your complaint.
- If you have supporting documentation, attach or link it with your submission for ease of reference.
- If you have any further documentation that is not attached, indicate that you will submit it at a later time.
- Keep your narrative clear and concise, focusing on the essential details of your dispute.
- If you're unsure about any part of the form, please don't hesitate to seek guidance from your HR department.
