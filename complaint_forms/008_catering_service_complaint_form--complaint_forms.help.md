# Catering Service Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your complaint and provide a platform for you to communicate with our team. Please use this form to report any issues you faced with our catering service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address to contact you.
2. Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you.
3. Choose the date of the event that had the issue.
4. Provide the name of the catering company that served the event.
5. Specify the location of the event.
6. Select the type of event from the provided options.
7. Choose the category of the complaint.
8. Select the severity level of the issue.
9. Describe the specific details of the complaint.
10. If applicable, mention specific issues that you faced.
11. Describe how the issue affected your event.
12. Check if any guests mentioned the issue.
13. Specify how you would like to resolve the issue (e.g., refund, replacement service, etc.).
14. If you would like compensation, provide an estimate of the amount.
15. If you have evidence or witnesses, provide their details.
16. If you've had previous complaints, indicate if this is a repeat issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required/true): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required/true): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required/true): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required/true): Enter the date of the event where you experienced the issue.
* **Catering Company Name** (`catering_company`, required/true): Enter the name of the catering company that provided the service.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, required/true): Enter the location where the event took place.
* **Type of Event** (`event_type`, required/true): Select the type of event from the provided options (e.g., Wedding, Corporate Event, etc.).
* **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, required/true): Choose the category of the complaint from the provided options (e.g., Food Quality, Food Safety, etc.).
* **Severity Level** (`complaint_severity`, required/true): Select the severity level of the issue from the provided options (e.g., Minor Inconvenience, Serious Problem, etc.).
* **Detailed Description of Complaint** (`complaint_description`, required/true): Describe the specific details of the issue you faced.
* **Specific Issues Encountered** (`specific_issues`, optional/false): Mention any specific issues that you faced, if applicable.
* **How Did This Affect Your Event?** (`event_impact`, optional/false): Describe how the issue affected your event.
* **Did Guests Mention the Issue?** (`guest_feedback`, optional/false): Check if any guests mentioned the issue.
* **Desired Resolution** (`desired_resolution`, required/true): Specify how you would like to resolve the issue (e.g., refund, replacement service, etc.).
* **Compensation Amount Requested** (`compensation_amount`, optional/false): If applicable, estimate the amount of compensation you are requesting.
* **Do You Have Photographic Evidence or Receipts?** (`evidence_available`, optional/false): Check if you have any evidence or receipts that can support your complaint.
* **Witness Names** (`witness_names`, optional/false): If applicable, provide the names of witnesses who can confirm the issue.
* **Have You Complained to This Company Before?** (`previous_complaints`, optional/false): Check if you've had previous complaints with this company.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preference`, required/true): Select your preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
