# Civic Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Civic Issue Report Form is designed to gather information about issues or concerns that need attention from local authorities. This form allows citizens to report problems they have encountered, such as safety concerns, noise disturbances, or environmental issues, and provides a way for authorities to quickly understand the situation and take appropriate action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information if you wish to be contacted for updates.
2. Provide a description of the issue, including the location and category it falls under.
3. Choose a category for the issue from the provided options (e.g., Safety, Noise, Environmental, Infrastructure).
4. Indicate the urgency level of the issue (1-5).
5. Attach any relevant photos or documents that support your report.
6. Specify how you would like the issue to be handled.
7. Choose whether you want to receive follow-up updates about the issue.
8. Finally, submit the form to send your report to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporter name**: Enter your name (if you wish to be identified) or leave this field blank for an anonymous report.
* **Contact information**: Provide an email or phone number where you can be reached for updates or further discussion.
* **Issue location**: Enter the address or description of where the issue is occurring.
* **Issue category**: Select the category that best describes the issue (e.g., Safety, Noise, Environmental, Infrastructure).
* **Description of the issue**: Clearly describe the problem, including any details or timeframes relevant to the issue.
* **Urgency level**: Indicate how quickly you believe the issue requires attention, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Attachments instructions**: If you have photos or documents related to the issue, follow the instructions provided to attach them.
* **Desired resolution**: Specify how you would like the issue to be handled (e.g., repair, replacement, removal).
* **Follow-up preference**: Choose whether you want to receive updates or follow-ups about the issue.
* **Submission date**: This field will automatically populate with the current date. Do not alter it.
