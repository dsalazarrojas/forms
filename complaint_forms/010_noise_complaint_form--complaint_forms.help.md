# Noise Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Noise Complaint Form is a tool for reporting noise-related issues. It allows you to report a noise complaint and provide necessary details about the type of noise, its location, and the impact on you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and address to help us identify you as the complainant.
2. Provide your contact email and phone number so we can reach out to you if necessary.
3. Describe the noise issue in the `Description` field.
4. Choose the type of noise you are experiencing from the `Type of Noise` dropdown menu.
5. Indicate where the noise is coming from in the `Location of Noise` field, selecting all relevant locations.
6. Specify the frequency of the noise in the `Frequency of Noise` field, selecting all relevant days or times.
7. Provide the level of noise by entering a numerical value in the `Level of Noise` field.
8. If known, specify the date and time the noise started.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Noise Complaint Form**: Enter the title of the form for reference.
* **Complainant Name**: Enter your name, so we can identify you as the person submitting this complaint.
* **Address**: Enter your address to help us locate the issue.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the noise issue you're experiencing.
* **Type of Noise**: Select all relevant types of noise from the dropdown menu to indicate the nature of the disturbance.
* **Location of Noise**: Select all locations where the noise is being experienced.
* **Frequency of Noise**: Indicate the days and times the noise is happening.
* **Level of Noise**: Enter a numerical value to represent the level of noise you're experiencing.
* **Date**: If known, specify the date the noise started.
* **Time**: If known, specify the time the noise started.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your complaint and we will review and address your issue.
