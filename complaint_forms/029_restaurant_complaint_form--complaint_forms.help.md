# Restaurant Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Complaint Form is designed to collect and track customer complaints within the restaurant environment. This form aims to provide an efficient means for customers to report issues and for restaurant staff to address and manage these complaints effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the complaint details**:
   - Ensure you have the correct complaint date and time.
   - Provide a brief description of the issue you're reporting.

2. **Select the relevant department**: Select the department that you believe is responsible for the issue. This will help staff to direct the complaint to the right area for resolution.

3. **Enter your contact information**: Provide your name, email, and phone number so that the restaurant can contact you regarding your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Date**: Enter the date you experienced the issue at the restaurant.
* **Complaint Time**: Enter the time you experienced the issue at the restaurant.
* **Complaint Description**: Briefly describe the issue you faced, including any relevant details.
* **Department**: Select the department you believe is responsible for the issue. This could be:
	+ **Kitchen**: Issues related to food quality, preparation, or presentation.
	+ **Front Desk**: Issues related to reservations, billing, or front-of-house service.
	+ **Management**: General issues or concerns that don't fit into other categories.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be known.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your email address so that the restaurant can contact you regarding your complaint.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the restaurant can contact you regarding your complaint.
* **Complaint ID (Optional)**: If you have a complaint number or reference point, enter it here.
* **Staff Comment**: This field is reserved for staff to note any further actions or comments regarding your complaint.
* **Customer Signature**: This field is not currently used but may be used in the future for tracking customer verification.
