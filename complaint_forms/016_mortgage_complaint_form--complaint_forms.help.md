# Mortgage Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to provide a platform for customers to report and track any issues they encounter related to their mortgage services. 

## How to Complete This Form
1. Enter your First Name in the text field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the text field labeled "Last Name".
3. Provide your phone number in the text field labeled "Phone Number".
4. Enter your email address in the text field labeled "Email".
5. Enter your address in the text field labeled "Address".
6. Describe the issue you're facing in the text field labeled "Issue Description".
7. Enter your Mortgage Number in the text field labeled "Mortgage Number".
8. Select the date when the issue occurred from the date field labeled "Issue Date".
9. Enter the time when the issue occurred in the time field labeled "Issue Time".
10. Choose the type of issue from the dropdown menu labeled "Issue Type".
11. Enter more details about the issue in the text field labeled "Issue Details".
12. Select the contact preferences for follow-up from the checkbox menu labeled "Contact Preferences".
13. Enter the amount of your mortgage in the number field labeled "Mortgage Amount".
14. Select the type of property from the dropdown menu labeled "Property Type".
15. Enter the state of your property in the text field labeled "Property State".
16. Enter the city of your property in the text field labeled "Property City".
17. Enter the zip code of your property in the text field labeled "Zip Code".
18. If your property is of a different type, please enter it in the text field labeled "Other Property Type".
19. Select the status of the issue from the dropdown menu labeled "Issue Status".
20. Describe the steps taken to resolve the issue, if any, in the text field labeled "Issue Resolution".
21. Enter any follow-up information in the text field labeled "Follow-up".
22. Enter your name in the text field labeled "Contact Name".
23. Enter your phone number in the text field labeled "Contact Phone".
24. Enter your email address in the text field labeled "Contact Email".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (Required)
* The field is for your first name.

### Last Name (Required)
* The field is for your last name.

### Phone Number (Required)
* The field is for your phone number.

### Email (Required)
* The field is for your email address.

### Address (Required)
* The field is for your address.

### Issue Description (Required)
* The field is to describe your issue.

### Mortgage Number (Required)
* The field is to enter your mortgage number.

### Issue Date (Required)
* The field is to select the date when the issue occurred.

### Issue Time (Required)
* The field is to select the time when the issue occurred.

### Zip Code (Required)
