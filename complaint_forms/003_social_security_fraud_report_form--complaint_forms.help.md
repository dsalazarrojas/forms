# Social Security Fraud Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Security Fraud Report Form is a tool used to report suspected social security fraud cases. This form collects vital information to aid in the investigation and resolution of these cases. Please provide as much detail as possible to help speed up the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2.  Ensure you've provided all necessary details to help us investigate the case.
3.  Double-check your entries before submission to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person reporting the fraud.
    This field is for identification purposes only.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: true): Enter a valid contact number where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
    This is a required field to ensure we can contact you for additional information.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the fraud case.
    This field helps us understand the context of the reported case.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for further communication.
    This field is optional but recommended for faster response times.
*   **Report Type** (`report_type`, select_one, required: true): Select the type of report you are submitting.
    Choose from the options provided to help us categorize the report.
*   **Reported Date** (`reported_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date the fraud occurred.
    This is a required field to help us understand the timeline.
*   **Reported Time** (`reported_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the fraud occurred.
    This field is optional but helpful for precise timing.
*   **Reported Time Ending** (`reported_time_ending`, time, required: false): Enter the ending time of the reported event.
    This field is optional but may be necessary for understanding the duration.
*   **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date you're submitting this report.
    This is a required field to track the submission timeline.
*   **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you're submitting this report.
    This field is optional but helpful for precise submission timing.
*   **Submit Time Ending** (`submit_time_ending`, time, required: false): Enter the ending time of submission.
    This field is optional but may be necessary for understanding the submission process.
*   **Reported Location** (`reported_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the fraud occurred.
    This field helps us understand the context of the case.
*   **Reported Address** (`reported_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address related to the reported case.
    This field is optional but helpful for geographical context.
*   **Submit Location** (`submit_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you're submitting the report.
    This field is optional but recommended for our reference.
*   **Submit Address** (`submit_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where you're submitting from.
    This field is optional but helps us respond to you efficiently.
*   **Reported Person Name** (`reported_person_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person involved in the fraud case.
    This field is optional but helps in identifying the individual.
*   **Submit Person Name** (`submit_person_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the report.
    This field is optional but recommended for our reference.
*   **Reported Person Title** (`reported_person_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the person involved in the reported case.
    This field is optional but helps us understand their role.
*   **Submit Person Title** (`submit_person_title`, text, required: false): Enter your title or the title of the person submitting the report.
    This field is optional but recommended for our reference.
*   **Report Type Other** (`report_type_other`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information not listed in the options.
    This field is for providing extra context not covered by the standard options.
*   **Reported Person Other Title** (`reported_person_other_title`, text, required: false): Enter any additional title information for the reported person.
    This field is optional but helps in understanding their role.
*   **Submit Other Person Title** (`submit_other_person_title`, text, required: false): Enter any additional title information for the person submitting or reporting.
    This field is optional but recommended for our reference.
*   **Reported Person ID** (`reported_person_id`, text, required: false): Enter the ID of the person involved in the reported case.
    This field is optional but helps in identifying the individual.
*   **Submit Person ID** (`submit_person_id`, text, required: false): Enter your ID or the ID of the person submitting the report.
    This field is optional but recommended for our reference.
*   **Submit Comments** (`submit_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the case.
    This field is optional but helpful for providing further insight.

Note: Some fields are required for the form submission. Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your report is processed accurately and efficiently.
