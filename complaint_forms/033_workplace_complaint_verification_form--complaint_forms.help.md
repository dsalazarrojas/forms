# Workplace Complaint Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Complaint Verification Form is used to gather and document information about complaints or issues raised in the workplace. It helps to ensure that all relevant details are collected and recorded, making it easier to resolve the issue and follow up on the status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Report Issue**: Provide a brief description of the issue being reported.
2. **Description**: Describe the incident or situation that led to the complaint.
3. **Date of Event**: Enter the date the event occurred.
4. **Time of Event**: Enter the time the event occurred.
5. **Reportee**: Identify the person or team involved in the incident.
6. **Reporter**: Identify the person reporting the complaint.
7. **Contact Info**: Provide contact information for the reportee or other relevant parties.
8. **Witness**: Indicate if anyone witnessed the incident.
9. **Complaint Details**: Provide additional information about the issue, if necessary.
10. **Resolution**: Choose the desired resolution for the complaint.
11. **Follow up**: Schedule a follow-up to check on the status of the complaint.
12. **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up.
13. **Follow Up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up.
14. **Follow Up Status**: Indicate the status of the follow-up (Active or Inactive).
15. **Follow Up Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the follow-up.
16. **Complaint Category**: Choose the category of the complaint (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
17. **Complaint Type**: Choose the type of complaint (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
18. **Reported By**: Indicate who reported the complaint.
19. **Reportee Contact**: Provide contact information for the reportee.
20. **Additional Complaint Details**: Provide any additional information about the complaint (optional).

**Tips**

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue and providing additional information.
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the hints provided for guidance.
* If additional details about the complaint are needed, use the "Additional Complaint Details" fields to provide more information.
