# Municipality Citizen Input Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from citizens with complaints or concerns regarding the municipality. 
The data is used for internal records and to help the municipality understand the needs and concerns of its residents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the department you would like to address your concern to (e.g., Department of Public Works, Department of Health, etc.).
2. Provide a brief description of your concern.
3. Choose the category that best describes your concern (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, etc.).
4. Select the method you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, or In person).
5. Provide your name (if you wish to be contacted by name).
6. Enter your email address (if you have one).
7. Enter your phone number (if you have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (Department, Select One, Required): Choose the department you would like to address your concern to. Please select from the provided options (Department of Public Works, Department of Health, Department of Finance).
* **Concern Description** (Concern Description, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your concern. This will help the municipality understand the scope of the issue.
* **Concern Category** (Concern Category, Select One, Required): Choose the category that best describes your concern. Please select from the provided options (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, Select One, Required): Select the method you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or In person).
* **Resident Name** (Resident Name, Text, Optional): If you would like to be contacted by name, please provide your name. This will help the municipality address your concern directly.
* **Resident Email** (Resident Email, Email, Optional): If you have an email address, please enter it. This will help the municipality contact you directly.
* **Resident Phone** (Resident Phone, Text, Optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it. This will help the municipality contact you directly.
