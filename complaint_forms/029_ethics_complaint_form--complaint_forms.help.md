# Ethics Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethics Complaint Form is designed to collect information about ethics-related complaints or incidents that occur within an organization. This form helps to gather essential details for investigation and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following fields with accurate and concise information:
	* **Complainant Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity submitting the complaint.
	* **Incident Date**: Provide the date the incident occurred.
	* **Incident Location**: Describe the location where the incident took place.
	* **Complainant Details**: Add any additional information about the complainant.
	* **Respondent Details**: If applicable, provide details about the person or entity being complained against.
	* **Witness Details**: If applicable, provide information from witnesses.
	* **Nature of Incident**: Describe the nature of the incident in a few words.
	* **Report Type**: Choose the type of report: Ethical Behavior, Misconduct, or Abuse.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complainant Name** (complainant_name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person submitting the complaint.
* **Incident Date** (incident_date, Date, Required): Provide the date the incident occurred in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Incident Location** (incident_location, Text, Required): Describe the location where the incident took place.
* **Complainant Details** (complainant_details, Text, Optional): Add any additional information about the complainant.
* **Respondent Details** (respondent_details, Text, Optional): If applicable, provide details about the person or entity being complained against.
* **Witness Details** (witness_details, Text, Optional): If applicable, provide information from witnesses.
* **Nature of Incident** (nature_of_incident, Text, Optional): Describe the nature of the incident in a few words.
* **Report Type** (report_type, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of report:
	+ Ethical Behavior: Concerns about unethical behavior or actions.
	+ Misconduct: Incidents involving unacceptable behavior or actions.
	+ Abuse: Incidents involving harassment, discrimination, or other forms of abuse.

## Tips
- Ensure the information provided is accurate and concise.
- If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the designated contact for assistance.
- If you're filling out the form on behalf of someone else, ensure you have their permission to do so.
