# Harassment Reporting Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents of harassment, bullying, or other forms of abuse that have occurred within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of incident you are reporting (Harassment, Bullying, or Other).
2. Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
3. Provide the location of the incident.
4. Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
5. Enter the ID, name, email, and phone number of the person reporting the incident (Reporter ID, Reporter Name, Reporter Email, and Reporter Phone).
6. Enter the ID, name, email, and phone number of any witnesses (Witness ID, Witness Name, Witness Email, and Witness Phone).
7. Select the person or department to whom the incident was reported (Incident Reported To).
8. Choose the reporting officer(s) who will handle the incident (Reporting Officer).
9. Enter the date and time the incident was reported.
10. Select the reporting officer who reported the incident (Reported By).
11. Add any additional comments about the incident.
12. Sign the form with your signature.
13. Attach any relevant documents or evidence related to the incident.
14. Confirm that the form has been completed (Reporting Form Completed).
15. Select the status of the submission (In Progress, Completed, Cancelled, or Reopened) and enter any comments about the status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident you are reporting (Harassment, Bullying, or Other).
	+ Harassment: This refers to incidents involving unwelcome or uninvited behavior, including comments, gestures, or actions that are intended to embarrass or demean.
	+ Bullying: This refers to repeated or persistent actions that are intended to make someone feel hurt, intimidated, or isolated.
	+ Other: This is for incidents that do not fit into either the Harassment or Bullying categories.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Location of Incident**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description of Incident**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including any relevant details about what happened, who was involved, and what was said or done.
* **Reporter ID**: Enter the ID of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Name**: Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Email**: Enter the email address of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person reporting the incident.
* **Witness ID**: Enter the ID of any witnesses.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of any witnesses.
* **Witness Email**: Enter the email address of any witnesses.
* **Witness Phone**: Enter the phone number of any witnesses.
* **Incident Reported To**: Select the person or department to whom the incident was reported.
* **Reporting Officer**: Choose the officer who will handle the incident.
* **Date Reported**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Time Reported**: Enter the time the incident was reported.
* **Reported By**: Select the officer who reported the incident.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the incident.
* **Signature**: Sign the form with your signature.
* **Attachment**: Attach any relevant documents or evidence related to the incident.
* **Reporting Form Completed**: Confirm that the form has been completed.
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of the submission (In Progress, Completed, Cancelled, or Reopened).
* **Submission Status Comments**: Enter any comments about the submission status.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to the best of your ability. Duplicate or incomplete reports may be discarded. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the reporting officer or department for guidance.
