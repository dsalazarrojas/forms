# National Park Entry Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about complaints or issues that occurred during a visit to a national park. It is meant to be completed by visitors to report incidents, provide feedback, or express concerns about their experience. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your visit and the national park you visited.
2. Provide your contact information (if you wish to receive a response).
3. Specify whether you had interaction with park staff during your visit.
4. If you experienced any issues or problems during your visit, describe them in the text field.
5. If you wish to receive an email or phone call from the park staff, provide your email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **national park location**: Enter the name of the national park where you visited.
* **national park type**: Indicate if you visited the park as a yes/no response (e.g., "Yes, I visited a park", "No, I did not visit a park").
* **date of visit**: Enter the date you visited the national park.
* **description of problem**: Briefly describe the issue or problem you experienced during your visit.
* **contact info**: Enter your contact information so that park staff can reach out to you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **interaction with staff**: Indicate if you interacted with national park staff during your visit.
* **national park staff response**: Enter any feedback you received from national park staff regarding your visit.
* **email**: Enter your email address so that park staff can reach out to you.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so that park staff can call you.
* **address**: Enter your home or work address (if you want to receive a response by mail).
* **zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **city**: Enter your city of residence.
* **state**: Enter your state of residence.
* **national park visit**: This field is not used in the form but is part of the form structure.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help the park staff to better understand and address your concerns.
