# Doctor Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help patients express their concerns or complaints about their experience with a doctor. It provides a platform for patients to provide feedback on the doctor's performance, medical services, and overall care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before submitting.
2. Fill in the required fields (none are actually required for this form, but they will be filled in for the purpose of this example).
3. Provide feedback on the doctor's performance, medical services, and overall care using the provided fields.
4. Select the "Submit" option to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Medical Services** (`medical_services`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is intended for patients to provide feedback on the medical services they received from the doctor. Please describe any issues or concerns you had with these services.

### 2. **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to rate the quality of services they received from the doctor. Please rate your experience with a brief description.

### 3. **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to provide the name of the doctor they received care from.

### 4. **Complaint** (`complaint`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to express any complaints or concerns they have about their experience with the doctor.

### 5. **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to sign their name, confirming they have submitted this form.

### 6. **Date** (`date`, `date`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to select the date they received care from the doctor.

### 7. **Time** (`time`, `time`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to select the time they received care from the doctor.

### 8. **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, `select_one`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to select an option for submitting their feedback. Currently, options are "Submit" and "Other". Please choose "Submit" to send your feedback.

### 9. **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to provide additional feedback or comments about their experience with the doctor.

### 10. **Concerns** (`concerns`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to express any concerns they have about their experience with the doctor.

### 11. **Patient Care** (`patient_care`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to rate their experience with the doctor's patient care.

### 12. **Service Care** (`service_care`, `text`, `optional`)

### 13. **Doctor Compliance** (`doctor_compliance`, `text`, `optional`)

### 15. **Patient Satisfaction** (`patient_satisfaction`, `text`, `optional`)

### 16. **Doctor Professionalism** (`doctor_professionalism`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to rate their experience with the doctor's professionalism.

### 17. **Doctor Communication** (`doctor_communication`, `text`, `optional`)
* This field is for patients to rate their experience with the doctor's communication.

### 18. **Doctor Helpfulness** (`doctor_helpfulness`, `text`, `optional`)

### 19. **Doctor Friendliness** (`doctor_friendliness`, `text`, `optional`)
