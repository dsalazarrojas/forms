# Student Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Complaint Form is a tool used by students to report issues or complaints they are facing on campus. This form is designed to be a clear and efficient way for students to communicate their concerns to the university administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter your student ID number**: This field is used to identify you as a student. Please enter your student ID number in the corresponding field.
2. **Enter your name**: This field is used to record your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
3. **Enter your email address**: This field is used to contact you about your complaint.
4. **Enter your phone number**: This field is optional, but it can be used to contact you about your complaint.
5. **Describe your complaint**: In this field, please provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are facing.
6. **Select the type of complaint**: Choose the category that best describes your complaint from the options provided.
7. **Submit or save draft**: You can choose to submit your complaint, save it as a draft, or cancel it.
8. **Attach any relevant documents**: If you have any supporting documents or evidence, you can attach them to this form.
9. **Provide additional comments**: If you have any additional information you'd like to share about your complaint, you can do so in this field.
10. **Select the category for your complaint**: Choose the category that best fits your complaint from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_id**: Enter your student ID number to identify yourself as a student.
* **name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your complaint.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone about your complaint.
* **complaint_description**: Provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are facing.
* **complaint_type**: Select the category that best describes your complaint from the options provided.
* **submit**: You can choose to submit your complaint, save it as a draft, or cancel it.
* **attachments**: Attach any supporting documents or evidence related to your complaint.
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share about your complaint.
* **category**: Select the category that best fits your complaint from the options provided.
* **assigned_tool**: Provide any additional information about the issue you are facing.
* **category_comment**: This field is not currently in use.
* **attachments_comment**: This field is not currently in use.
* **assigned_tool**: This field is not currently in use.
* **submit**: This field is not currently in use.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions and explanations.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact the university administration for assistance.
* If you have any supporting documents or evidence, please attach them to the form.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the "comments" field.
