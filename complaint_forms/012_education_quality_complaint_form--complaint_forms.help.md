# Education Quality Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Education Quality Complaint Form is designed to collect complaints and feedback from students, staff, and faculty regarding issues related to the quality of services provided by the education institution. It helps the administration to identify areas of improvement and take corrective actions for better student services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out each field carefully, providing as much detail as possible to help us understand the issue better.
* You can submit the form multiple times if you are reporting multiple issues.
* Make sure to review the fields below for their specific requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Info (Page 1)**:
  * This is where you provide your basic information as a student, staff, or faculty member, including your name or contact details.
* **Description (Page 2)**:
  * Describe the issue you are complaining about in as much detail as possible. This will help us understand the problem and address it more effectively.
* **Issues (Page 3)**:
  * Select all the issues that apply to the problem you described. You can choose more than one issue if needed.
  * If none of the given options fit your issue, you can select "Other issues" and provide a description in the "Description" field.
* **Submit Date/Time (Page 4)**:
  * Enter the date when you first encountered the issue or when you submitted the complaint.
* **Submit Time (Page 5)**:
  * Enter the exact time you submitted the complaint or when you first noticed the issue.
* **Submit Date/Time (Page 6)**:
  * This field is a combination of the submit date and time.
* **Submitter (Page 7)**:
  * Provide your name or contact details so we know who submitted the complaint.
* **Contact Number (Page 8)**:
  * If you would like to be contacted regarding the issue, please provide your contact number.
* **Contact Email (Page 9)**:
  * Provide your email address if you would like to be contacted regarding the issue.
* **Comments (Page 10)**:
  * Additional comments or details that you would like to share about your complaint.

## Tips
Please note that all information you provide in this form is kept confidential and used solely for improvement purposes. If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the administration for assistance.
