# Software Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Complaint Form is designed to collect detailed information about software-related complaints, allowing the development team to understand the issues and improve the software accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Software Complaint Form" to access the form.
2. Enter a detailed description of the issue in the "Software Complaint" field.
3. If applicable, select the "Software Version" you are using and enter the version number.
4. Enter the "Report Date" when you encountered the issue.
5. Enter the "Report Time" when you encountered the issue.
6. Choose the "Complaint Type" from the dropdown menu: Bug, Feature Request, or Other.
7. Select all applicable "Problem Areas" where the issue occurred, using the dropdown menu.
8. If you have more information about the issue, provide it in the "Additional Feedback" field.
9. Enter your "Contact Name" to ensure the development team knows who to contact.
10. Enter your "Contact Email" to ensure the development team can contact you for follow-up.
11. Enter your "Contact Phone" number for immediate contact.
12. Finally, click the "Submit Form" button to submit your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Software Complaint** (`Software Complaint`, Text, Required): Describe the issue you are experiencing with the software in detail.
* **Software Version** (`Software Version`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the version of the software you are using.
* **Report Date** (`Report Date`, Date, Required): Enter the date when you encountered the issue.
* **Report Time** (`Report Time`, Time, Required): Enter the time of day when you encountered the issue.
* **Complaint Type** (`Complaint Type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of complaint: Bug, Feature Request, or Other.
* **Problem Area** (`Problem Area`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable areas where the issue occurred (e.g., UI, Functionality, Performance, Data, Security).
* **Additional Feedback** (`Additional Feedback`, Text, Optional): Provide more details about the issue.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, Text, Required): Enter your name so the development team knows who to contact.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for follow-up.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for immediate contact.
* **Submit Form** (`Submit Form`, Note, Required): Click to submit your complaint.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue in the "Software Complaint" field.
* If you are unsure of the software version, it's okay to leave it blank.
* Ensure you enter the correct date and time of the issue in the "Report Date" and "Report Time" fields.
* Select all applicable "Problem Areas" to help the development team understand the scope of the issue.
* Proofread your complaint before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
