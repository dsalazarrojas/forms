# grievance_resolution_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the resolution of grievances. It allows users to record details about a complaint, provide evidence attachments, and track the resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all the required fields**: The form has multiple pages. Please ensure that you complete each page according to the required fields. For example, on page 1 (complaint details), fill out "Complaint title", "Complaint description", "Complaint date", and "Complaint status" fields. On page 2 (resolution), fill out "Resolution process", "Resolution status", "Resolution outcome", and "Resolution comments" fields. Please note that "Evidence attachments" field should be used for attaching any relevant documents or files to the grievance.

2. **Attach evidence**: In the "Evidence attachments" field, you can attach any relevant documents or files that support your complaint. This could be emails, photos, or videos.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint details**: This is where you provide a detailed description of your complaint. Please be as specific as possible to help the grievance team understand the issue.
* **Employee name**: Please enter the name of the employee involved in the complaint.
* **Complaint date**: Enter the date when the complaint occurred.
* **Evidence attachments**: As mentioned earlier, this is where you attach any relevant documents or files to support your complaint.
* **Resolution process**: This is where you describe the steps taken to resolve the grievance.
* **Resolution status**: Select the current status of the resolution.
* **Resolution outcome**: Select the outcome of the resolution process.
* **Resolution comments**: Enter any comments related to the resolution.
* **Grievance type**: Select the type of grievance (if applicable).
* **Resolution outcome**: Select the outcome of the resolution process.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the complaint.
* **Employee signature**: Sign this form as the employee who submitted the grievance.
* **Witness signature**: If a witness is involved, sign this form as the witness.
* **Manager signature**: Sign this form as the manager involved in the resolution process.
* **Grievance ID**: Enter a unique ID number assigned to the grievance.
* **Resolution date**: Enter the date of resolution.
* **Grievance status**: Select the status of the grievance.
* **Resolution comments**: Enter any additional comments about the resolution.
* **Complaint status**: Select the status of the complaint.
* **Form submitted by**: Select who submitted the form.
* **Resolution authorization**: Select if the resolution has been authorized.
* **Grievance resolution**: Select the resolution status of the grievance.
* **Manager approval**: Select if the manager has approved the resolution.
* **Employee approval**: Select if the employee has approved the resolution.
* **Resolution note**: Enter any additional notes about the resolution.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach any relevant evidence that can support your complaint.
* Be specific and concise in your responses.
* Ensure that you understand the resolution process and outcome before filling out this form.
