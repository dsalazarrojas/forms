# Employee Formal Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides instructions and explanations for completing the Employee Formal Complaint Form, which is used to report and document formal complaints from employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Information section with your details, including your name, title, department, and employee ID.
2. Select the Complaint Type that best describes the nature of your complaint.
3. Provide a clear and detailed description of the complaint.
4. If applicable, enter the date, time, and location of the incident.
5. Enter the name of the complaintant, if different from your own.
6. Enter the department and manager's name who is relevant to the complaint.
7. Select the complaint status and resolution status as applicable.
8. Add any relevant notes from the employee and manager.
9. Finally, submit the form for review and resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information** (`employee_formal_complaint_form_page_1`): This section requires your personal details as the complaining employee.
  - **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
  - **Employee Title** (`employee_title`): Enter your job title or position.
  - **Department** (`department`): Enter the department you belong to.
  - **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number.
- **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`): Select the type of complaint you are filing (Harassment, Bullying, Retaliation, or Other).
- **Description** (`description`): Provide a detailed description of the complaint, including dates, times, and locations if applicable.
- **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the incident, if known.
- **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the incident, if known.
- **Location** (`location`): Enter the location of the incident, if known.
- **Complaintant Name** (`complaintant_name`): If the complaint is against another employee, enter their name.
- **Complaintant Name** (`complaintant_name`): Enter the name of the individual you are complaining about.
- **Department** (`department`): Enter the department of the complaintant.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of the manager responsible for the complaintant.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter the ID of the employee filing the complaint.
- **Complaint Number** (`complaint_number`): Enter a unique number for the complaint.
- **Complaint Status** (`complaint_status`): Select the current status of the complaint (Open, Closed, Resolved, or Pending).
- **Resolution** (`resolution`): Enter the resolution or outcome of the complaint.
- **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`): Enter the date of the resolution, if applicable.
- **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time`): Enter the time of the resolution, if applicable.
- **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`): Select the current status of the resolution (Resolved, Open).
- **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`): Add any additional comments or details from the employee.
- **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`): Add any additional comments or details from the manager.
- **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`): Select the current status of the resolution.
