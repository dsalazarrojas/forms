# Book Availability Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about book availability complaints, allowing us to understand the nature of the issue and take appropriate action to resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field to enable us to contact you regarding your complaint.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field if you have one.
4. Specify the title of the book you are complaining about.
5. Identify the author of the book.
6. Provide the ISBN number of the book if known.
7. Indicate where you were looking for the book (e.g., Physical Store / Library Branch, Online Catalog, Mobile App).
8. Enter the branch or store location where you checked for the book.
9. Provide the date you checked for the book's availability.
10. Select the primary issue you encountered (e.g., Out of Stock, Missing from Shelf, Incorrect Catalog Info, Reserved but not available).
11. Enter a detailed description of the issue you faced.
12. Choose your preferred resolution (e.g., Notify me when in stock, Hold the book for me, Order a new copy, Speak with a manager).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your complaint.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Book Title**: Enter the title of the book you are complaining about.
* **Author Name**: Enter the author of the book you are complaining about.
* **ISBN Number**: If you know the ISBN number of the book, enter it here.
* **Where were you looking for this book?**: Select where you were looking for the book (e.g., Physical Store / Library Branch, Online Catalog, Mobile App).
* **Branch or Store Location Name**: Enter the branch or store location where you checked for the book.
* **Date you checked for availability**: Enter the date you checked for the book's availability.
* **What was the primary issue?**: Select the primary issue you faced (e.g., Out of Stock, Missing from Shelf, Incorrect Catalog Info, Reserved but not available).
* **Detailed Description of the Issue**: Enter a detailed description of the issue you faced.
* **How can we resolve this for you?**: Choose your preferred resolution (e.g., Notify me when in stock, Hold the book for me, Order a new copy, Speak with a manager).
