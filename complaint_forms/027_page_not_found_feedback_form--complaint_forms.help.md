# Page Not Found Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help our team understand the issue you experienced when accessing a page on our website. Your feedback will be used to identify and fix the problem as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
* Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) with as much detail as possible.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask us through our contact form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of what you were trying to access, including any relevant details such as the page title.
* **Broken Link** (`broken_link`, text, required: false): Describe the broken link you encountered, including any error messages or other relevant information.
* **Impact** (`impact`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply to indicate how the issue impacted you:
	+ What the visitor was trying to reach
	+ How the issue affected you
	+ What to fix next
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted for further assistance or updates, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by phone for further assistance or updates, please provide your phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you'd like to submit your feedback:
	+ Submit
	+ Do not submit

## Tips
* Be honest and clear about your experience.
* If you need help with anything, feel free to ask us through our contact form.
* We'll do our best to address the issue as soon as possible based on your feedback.
