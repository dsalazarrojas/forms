# Credit Dispute Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and resolve credit disputes. It is intended to help consumers report inaccuracies in their credit reports and allow them to select a resolution option. If the form is submitted, a credit dispute report will be created.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and accurate report of the inaccuracies found in your credit report.
2. Enter your consumer information for reference purposes.
3. Describe the details of the dispute.
4. Select the resolution option that best addresses your situation.
5. Choose any relevant attachments to support your dispute.
6. Select the option to submit or cancel the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Inaccuracies** (`report_inaccuracies`, text, optional): Report any inaccuracies found in your credit report.
* **Consumer Information** (`consumer_info`, text, optional): Enter your consumer information for reference purposes.
* **Dispute Details** (`dispute_details`, text, optional): Describe the details of the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution** (`dispute_resolution`, select_one, required): Select the resolution option that best addresses your situation, such as "Resolution 1" or "Resolution 2".
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, select_multiple, required): Choose any relevant attachments to support your dispute.
	+ Select all attachments that relate to the dispute.
* **Submit/Cancel** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select "Submit" to create a credit dispute report, or "Cancel" to end the process.
