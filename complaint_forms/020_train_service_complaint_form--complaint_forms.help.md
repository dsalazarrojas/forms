# Train Service Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for passengers to report complaints and issues related to train services. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your experience and help us improve the quality of our train services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct train number from the options provided.
3. Describe the incident or issue you experienced in the space provided.
4. Select the date and time of the incident from the calendar.
5. Choose the train station where the incident occurred.
6. Select the type of train from the options provided.
7. Select the mode of transportation (Train, Bus, or Ferry) that was involved in the incident.
8. Choose the category of the incident from the options provided.
9. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger ID**: Please enter your passenger ID or a unique identifier that you have been issued.
* **Train Number**: Enter the number of the train that was involved in the incident.
* **Description**: Provide a brief but detailed description of the incident or issue you experienced.
* **Date**: Select the date of the incident from the calendar. Make sure to select the correct date.
* **Time**: Select the time of the incident from the time options.
* **Train Station**: Enter the name of the train station where the incident occurred.
* **Train Type**: Select the type of train that was involved in the incident (Train, Bus, or Ferry).
* **Mode of Transportation**: Select all the modes of transportation that were involved in the incident (Train, Bus, or Ferry).
* **Category**: Choose the category of the incident from the options provided (e.g., Other, Train Delay, Train Service).
* **Submit**: This is not a field for input, but rather an instruction to submit your completed form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have more information to add, please include it in the description field.
* If you have any supporting documentation or evidence of the incident, please attach it separately.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our train services.
