# Harassment Complaint Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Harassment Complaint Absence Form is used to document and manage incidents of harassment that affect employees' work experience. Fill out this form to provide detailed information about the harassment you have experienced or witnessed, and any absence impact on work operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your details in the **User Input** field.
2. Describe the harassment incident or complaint in the **Complaint Description** field.
3. Provide the date the harassment occurred in the **Date Occurred** field.
4. Indicate whether you are requesting time off due to the harassment in the **Time Off Request** field. Please select "Yes" if you need time off due to the incident.
5. Enter the start date and end date of your requested time off in the **Time Off Start** and **Time Off End** fields, if applicable.
6. Write a response from your manager in the **Manager Response** field, if applicable.
7. Select the current status of your absence request in the **Status** field.
8. Add any additional notes about the incident in the **Notes** field.
9. Enter your work email address in the **Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Complaint Description** (text, required/optional): Describe the harassment incident or complaint, including as much detail as possible.
* **Date Occurred** (date, required/optional): Enter the date the harassment occurred.
* **Time Off Request** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are requesting time off due to the harassment.
* **Time Off Start** (date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your requested time off, if applicable.
* **Time Off End** (date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your requested time off, if applicable.
* **Manager Response** (text, required/optional): Write a response from your manager, if applicable.
* **Status** (select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of your absence request (Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **Notes** (note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the incident.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your work email address.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and truthfully as possible. If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your HR representative or supervisor for guidance.
