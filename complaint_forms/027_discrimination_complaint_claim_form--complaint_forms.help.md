# discrimination_complaint_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit a discrimination complaint claim. It's used to document and track discrimination complaints within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details: First and last names, email address, and phone number. These will be used to contact you about your complaint.
2. Provide incident details: Describe the incident, including the date it occurred and the type of discrimination you experienced (e.g., harassment, retaliation, hostile work environment, or unfair treatment).
3. Identify witnesses: If there were any witnesses, provide their names and contact information.
4. Select the reporting officer: Choose the person you would like to report your complaint to (HRC Manager, HR Generalist, or Manager).
5. Attach supporting documentation: If you have any supporting evidence, such as emails or witness statements, you can attach them here.
6. Report the status of your complaint: Once your complaint is resolved, you can update the complaint status to reflect the outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (name): Your first name.
- **Last Name** (last_name): Your last name.
- **Email** (email): Your email address.
- **Phone** (phone): Your contact phone number.
- **Date of Incident** (date_of_incident): The date the incident occurred.
- **Description of Incident** (description_of_incident): A brief description of what happened.
- **Discrimination Type** (discrimination_type): Choose the type of discrimination you experienced:
  • Harassment
  • Retaliation
  • Hostile work environment
  • Unfair treatment
- **Reporting Officer** (reporting_officer): Choose the person you would like to report your complaint to:
  • HRC Manager
  • HR Generalist
  • Manager
- **Supporting Documentation** (supporting_documentation): If you have any supporting evidence, such as emails or witness statements.
- **Witnesses** (witnesses): If there were any witnesses, provide their names and contact information.
- **Reporting Date** (reporting_date): The date you filed your complaint.
- **Resolution Outcome** (resolution_outcome): Once your complaint is resolved, select the outcome:
  • Resolved
  • Open
  • Escalated
  • Unresolved
- **Resolution Date** (resolution_date): The date your complaint was resolved.
- **Action Taken** (action_taken): A description of any actions taken to address the complaint.
- **Next Steps** (next_steps): If there are any next steps or follow-ups, describe them here.
- **Complaint Status** (complaint_status): Select the current status of your complaint:
  • Open
  • Escalated
  • Unresolved
  • Resolved
- **Complaint Notes** (complaint_notes): Any additional comments or notes about your complaint.
