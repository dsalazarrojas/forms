# Business License Dispute Resolution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about business license disputes. Please use this form to report any issue that has occurred with your business license. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the business name that is filing the dispute.
2. Enter the license number related to the dispute.
3. Describe the nature of the dispute in the "Dispute summary" field.
4. Provide the date when the dispute arose.
5. Specify the resolution or remedy you are seeking.
6. Check if you will provide any supporting documents.
7. Enter your contact email for correspondence.
8. Enter your contact phone number for urgent follow-up (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business name** (business_name, text, required): Enter the name of your business that is filing the dispute.
- **License number** (license_number, text, required): Enter the number of the license associated with the dispute.
- **Dispute summary** (dispute_summary, text, required): Describe the nature of the dispute in brief. Be as detailed as possible to help us resolve the issue efficiently.
- **Date of issue** (date_of_issue, date, optional): Specify the date when the dispute arose.
- **Desired outcome** (desired_outcome, text, required): Specify the resolution or remedy you are seeking.
- **Supporting documents** (supporting_documents, select_one, optional): Check 'True' if you will provide supporting documents. If so, please indicate where we can find them.
- **Contact email** (contact_email, email, required): Enter your contact email for correspondence about the dispute.
- **Contact phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for urgent follow-up (if needed).
- **Additional notes** (additional_notes, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information that can help us resolve the dispute.
