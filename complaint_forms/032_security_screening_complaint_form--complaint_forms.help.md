# Security Screening Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for security-related complaints. It helps our team to investigate and address security issues promptly.
## How To Complete This Form
To file a complaint, simply navigate through the form's single page and fill in the required fields. Start by entering your name and email (though these are not required but are useful for follow-up correspondence).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **screening_form** (`screening_form`, `text`, optional): This is the main field where you can describe the nature of your security complaint in detail. Please provide a clear and concise statement of the issue you are facing.
- **name** (`name`, `text`, optional): If you prefer, you can optionally enter your name to be associated with your complaint. This information will help us to contact you if necessary.
- **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Similar to the name field, you can optionally enter your email address if you wish to be contacted about your complaint.
- **complaint_description** (`complaint_description`, `note`, optional): Here, describe the specific issue you are experiencing regarding security. Please be as detailed as possible to help our team understand and address your concern.
