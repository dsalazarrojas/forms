# Consumer Complaint Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the resolution of a consumer complaint, including details about the complaint process, customer feedback, and resolution outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the complaint history and relevant details.
2. Provide an update on the resolution process and outcome.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
4. Enter any additional feedback or comments from the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint ID** (`complaint_id`, text, required): Enter the reference number for the complaint.
* **Original Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, date, required): Enter the date the complaint was filed.
* **Customer First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the customer.
* **Customer Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's mailing address.
* **Product or Service** (`product_service`, text, required): Enter the product or service the complaint was about.
* **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the complaint from the list.
* **Original Complaint Summary** (`original_complaint`, text, required): Enter a brief summary of the customer's original complaint.
* **Investigation Summary** (`investigation_summary`, text, required): Enter a summary of what was found during the investigation.
* **Resolution Offered** (`resolution_offered`, text, required): Enter the solution or offer made to the customer.
* **Resolution Type** (`resolution_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of resolution that was offered (e.g., refund, replacement, etc.).
* **Resolution Amount** (`resolution_amount`, number, optional): Enter the dollar amount of the resolution if applicable.
* **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`, date, required): Enter the date the resolution was offered or provided to the customer.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate customer satisfaction with the resolution.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback from the customer.
* **Is Issue Fully Resolved** (`issue_resolved`, select_one, required): Select whether the issue is fully resolved or not.
* **Follow-up Required** (`follow_up_required`, select_one, required): Select if additional follow-up is needed.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the scheduled follow-up date if additional follow-up is needed.
* **Complaint Handled By** (`handled_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the representative who handled the complaint.
* **Handler Title** (`handler_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the representative.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Enter the date the complaint is being closed.
* **Customer Acknowledgment** (`customer_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select whether the customer acknowledges the resolution.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional relevant information about the complaint resolution process.

Note: The customer complaint discharge form is an important part of our customer service process, and your attention to detail and accuracy in completing this form will help ensure that we can provide the best possible service to our customers.
