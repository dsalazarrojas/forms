# resort_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The resort complaint form is a tool for guests to provide feedback or complaints about their stay. It helps the resort staff to address any issues promptly and improve the overall guest experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Guest Name** in the first field.
2. Select the type of complaint you would like to report from the **Complaint Type** dropdown menu (Hotel, Restaurant, or Other).
3. Provide a detailed description of your complaint in the **Description** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Enter the name of the resort you were staying at in the **Resort Name** field.
7. If you have stayed at the resort on a specific date, enter it in the **Date of Stay** field.
8. If you have stayed at the resort during a specific time, enter it in the **Time of Stay** field (optional).
9. Enter the room number you were staying in the **Room Number** field.
10. If you would like to report an issue with a specific staff member, enter their name in the **Staff Member** field (optional).
11. If you have any further feedback, comments, or suggestions, please provide it in the **Feedback** field (optional).
12. If you have an attachment (e.g., a photo or document) that supports your complaint, you can upload it in the **Attachment** field (optional).
13. If you have an attachment, please specify its type (Document, Image, or Audio) in the **Attachment Type** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Complaint Type**: Select the category that best describes your complaint (Hotel, Restaurant, or Other).
* **Description**: Briefly describe the issue or concern you have, and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Resort Name**: Enter the name of the resort where you were staying.
* **Date of Stay**: If you are reporting a complaint about a specific stay, enter the dates you stayed.
* **Time of Stay**: If you can remember the time you stayed at the resort, enter it here (optional).
* **Room Number**: Enter the room number you were staying in.
* **Staff Member**: If you would like to report an issue with a specific staff member, enter their name (optional).
* **Feedback**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about your stay.
* **Attachment**: If you have an attachment that supports your complaint, upload it here (optional).
* **Attachment Type**: If you have uploaded an attachment, specify its type (Document, Image, or Audio).
