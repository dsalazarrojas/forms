# Warranty Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any issues or defects you have encountered with a product you have purchased from us.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the product you are experiencing issues with from the product dropdown list.
2. Enter a brief description of the issue.
3. Provide the date on which you first noticed the issue.
4. Enter your message to our support team.
5. Select the type of complaint you are submitting (e.g. defect, damage, etc.).
6. Select the status of your warranty (if applicable).
7. Enter your name and contact information so we can respond to your complaint.
8. Enter any additional product images or notes that may help us understand the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`): Enter the name of the product you are experiencing issues with.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`): Provide a brief description of the issue you are experiencing with the product.
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`): Enter the date on which you first noticed the issue.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`): Enter your message to our support team describing the issue in more detail.
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`): Select the type of complaint you are submitting (e.g. defect, damage, etc.).
* **Warranty Status** (`warranty_status`): If you have a warranty on the product, select the status of your warranty.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name so we can contact you.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address so we can respond to your complaint.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): Enter your address so we can send you any replacement parts or instructions.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`): Enter the price of the product (if applicable).
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the issue.
* **Message 2** (`customer_message2`): Enter your message to our support team (if you have more to add).
* **Product Image 1** (`product_image`): Upload any relevant images of the product to help us understand the issue.
* **Product Image 2** (`product_image2`): Upload any additional images of the product to help us understand the issue.
* **Product Image 3** (`product_image3`): Upload any further images of the product to help us understand the issue.
* **Customer Name 2** (`customer_name2`): Enter your name (if you are submitting a complaint on behalf of someone else).
* **Customer Email 2** (`customer_email2`): Enter the email address of the person submitting the complaint (if they are not you).
* **Customer Phone 2** (`customer_phone2`): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the complaint (if they are not you).
* **Message 2** (`customer_message3`): Enter your message to our support team (if you have more to add).
* **Customer Note 2** (`customer_note2`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the issue.
* **Warranty Number** (`warranty_number`): Enter the warranty number (if you have one).
* **Warranty Date** (`warranty_date`): Enter the date your warranty started (if applicable).
* **Warranty Status** (`warranty_status`): Select the status of your warranty (if you have one).
* **Message 3** (`customer_message3`): Enter any further messages to our support team.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`): Enter the price of the product (if applicable).
