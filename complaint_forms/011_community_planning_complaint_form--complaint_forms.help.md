# Community Planning Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect community planning complaints from residents to help address issues related to zoning, infrastructure, parks, public safety, and environmental concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your full name.
2.  Enter your address for easy location identification.
3.  Provide your email address for contact purposes.
4.  Enter your phone number for further communication.
5.  Select the specific category of your complaint.
6.  Describe your concern in as much detail as possible, focusing on the issue and its impact.
7.  Specify if you've already contacted anyone else about the issue.
8.  Check if you'd like to be contacted for further resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Submit Your Community Planning Concerns --**: This is an introductory note that explains the purpose of the form and encourages residents to submit their concerns.
*   **Resident Full Name**: Enter your full name for identification and contact purposes.
*   **Resident Address**: Type your address for easy location identification of the issue.
*   **Resident Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
*   **Resident Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further communication and follow-up.
*   **Complaint Category**: Choose a category that best describes your concern. Select from the provided options: Zoning and Land Use, Infrastructure and Roads, Parks and Recreation, Public Safety, Environmental Concerns, or Other.
*   **If Other - Please Specify**: If you select 'Other', please provide a brief description of the category.
*   **Date the Issue Occurred or Was Observed**: Select the date when you first noticed the issue or problem.
*   **Precise Location of the Planning Issue**: Enter the exact location where you're experiencing the issue.
*   **Detailed Description of Complaint**: Describe your concern in as much detail as possible, focusing on the issue and its impact.
*   **What is your desired resolution?**: Specify what you'd like to see done about the issue.
*   **Have you contacted anyone else about this issue?**: Check if you've already contacted anyone else about this issue.
*   **If yes - who did you contact?**: If you've contacted someone else, specify their name or department.
*   **Would you like to be contacted regarding this complaint?**: Choose if you want to be contacted for further resolution or updates.
*   **-- Privacy Statement --**: This is a note stating that all submissions are kept confidential but may be shared with relevant city departments.
*   **Today's Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date for record-keeping purposes.
