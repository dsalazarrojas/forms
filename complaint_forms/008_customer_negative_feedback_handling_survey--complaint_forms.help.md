# Customer Negative Feedback Handling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about customer complaints and provide a channel for feedback handling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your contact information: Please fill in your customer name and contact number in the fields provided.
2. Describe your issue: In the "Feedback Description" field, provide a detailed description of your concern.
3. Choose feedback type: Select the type of feedback you are providing (e.g., complaint, suggestion, etc.).
4. Choose feedback frequency: Indicate how often the issue occurs (e.g., frequently, occasionally, rarely).
5. Choose follow-up action: Decide how you would like us to follow up on your issue (e.g., follow up with you, send to customer support team, etc.).
6. Evaluate customer service: Rate your experience with our customer service team.
7. Check for issue recurrence: Specify if the issue is recurring or not.
8. Rate customer response time: Provide a numerical rating for the time it took for our customer service team to respond to your issue.
9. Choose issue category: Select the category of the issue you're facing (e.g., technical, non-technical).
10. Add any additional comments: Provide any additional details or context about your issue.
