# Process Server Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Process Server Complaint Form" is designed to gather information about a complaint or concern related to a process serving experience. This form allows individuals to provide details about a process server's performance, which can help identify areas for improvement and ensure that process servers meet high standards of service.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your **first name** (`first_name`) - This field is not mandatory, but please enter your name if you wish to submit a complaint in your own name.
- Enter your **last name** (`last_name`) - Again, this field is optional, so feel free to leave it blank if you prefer anonymity.
- Select a **date of the event** (`date_of_event`) - Choose the date on which the process server's actions led to your complaint.
- Choose a **time of the event** (`time_of_event`) - Select the time of day when the issue occurred.
- Provide a detailed **complaint description** (`complaint_description`) - This is where you describe what happened in a clear and concise manner. Please be as specific as possible to help us better understand the situation.
- If your complaint does not fit in the provided description fields, use the **other details** (`other_details`) field for additional information.
- To contact you, please select a **contact method** (`contact_method`) - You can choose to be contacted via phone, email, or another method if you prefer.
- If you have a **phone number** (`phone_number`) that you'd like us to use to contact you, please enter it here.
- Enter your **email address** (`email_address`) - This will be used to contact you, unless you prefer a phone call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name if you wish to submit a complaint in your name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional, but you can enter your last name for reference.
* **Date of event** (`date_of_event`, `date`, required: false): Select the date on which the process server's actions led to your complaint.
* **Time of event** (`time_of_event`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of day when the issue occurred.
* **Complaint description** (`complaint_description`, `note`, required: false): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Other details** (`other_details`, `note`, required: false): Use this field if your complaint doesn't fit in the description field.
* **Contact method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (phone, email, or other).
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): This field is used for contact, unless you've chosen a phone call.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when describing the complaint.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
- Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services. Thank you for taking the time to submit your complaint!
