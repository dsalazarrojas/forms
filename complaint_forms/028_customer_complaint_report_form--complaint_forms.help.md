# customer_complaint_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document customer complaints, allowing for a thorough understanding of customer issues and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Customer Info** field with the customer's name, address, and contact information.
2.  Select the **Complaint Type** that best describes the issue.
3.  Describe the **Description of Complaint** in the **Description of Complaint** field.
4.  Choose the **Contact Method** that the customer used to contact you.
5.  Select the **Customer Complaint Status** to indicate the current status of the complaint.
6.  Choose the **Response Status** to indicate the current response status.
7.  Enter the **Response Date** for the response.
8.  If needed, attach any **Attachments** related to the complaint.
9.  Enter any **Additional Comments** regarding the complaint.
10.  If necessary, repeat the above steps for **Customer Complaint Page 2**.
11.  Sign and date your report with your **Signature** and **Date**.
12.  Finally, enter your contact information with **Email** and **Phone Number**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (1)**: Enter the customer's name, address, and contact information.
* **Complaint Type (2)**: Select the type of complaint (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Description of Complaint (3)**: Provide a detailed description of the complaint.
* **Contact Method (4)**: Choose how the customer contacted you (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Customer Complaint Status (5)**: Indicate the current status of the complaint.
* **Response Status (6)**: Indicate the response status.
* **Response Date (7)**: Enter the date of the response.
* **Attachment (8)**: If necessary, attach any relevant documents or files.
* **Additional Comments (9)**: Enter any additional comments or information regarding the complaint.
* **Customer Complaint Page 2 (10)**: Repeat the above steps for this page.
* **Customer Info 2 (11)**: Enter the customer's name, address, and contact information for this page.
* **Description of Complaint 2 (12)**: Provide a detailed description of the complaint for this page.
* **Contact Method 2 (13)**: Choose how the customer contacted you for this page.
* **Customer Complaint Status 2 (14)**: Indicate the current status of the complaint for this page.
* **Response Status 2 (15)**: Indicate the response status.
* **Response Date 2 (16)**: Enter the date of the response.
* **Attachment 2 (17)**: If necessary, attach any relevant documents or files.
* **Additional Comments 2 (18)**: Enter any additional comments or information regarding the complaint.
* **Customer Signature (19)**: Sign your report with your signature.
* **Customer Signature (20)**: Sign the report with the customer's signature.
* **Date (21)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time (22)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Email (23)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (24)**: Enter your phone number.

This is just a basic guide, you can modify as necessary to suit your specific needs.
