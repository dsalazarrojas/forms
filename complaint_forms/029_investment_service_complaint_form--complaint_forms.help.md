# investment_service_complaint_form_title - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit complaints about the investment services provided by the company. Your feedback is crucial in helping us identify and resolve any issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Be as detailed as possible when describing your complaint.
3. Review the form for any mistakes before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Details** (Complaint Details, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you can describe your complaint in as much detail as possible. Please provide a clear and concise description of the issue you're experiencing.
* **Client Information** (Client Information, text, optional): This page is for you to provide any relevant information about yourself, such as your name, contact number, or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional): This page allows you to provide your contact information, which will help us reach out to you regarding your complaint.
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, optional): Describe the investment service you're complaining about, including any relevant dates, times, or transactions.
* **Complaint Type** (Complaint Type, select one, optional): Choose the type of complaint you're experiencing, such as "Investment Product Issue," "Service Quality Issue," or "Communication Issue."
* **Complaint Description** (Complaint Description, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your complaint, including any relevant dates, times, or events.
* **Expected Resolution** (Expected Resolution, text, optional): Specify what you expect to happen as a resolution to your complaint.
* **Attachments** (Attachments, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents related to your complaint.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): If you have any further information or questions, please include them here.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you regarding your complaint.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible in your complaint description to ensure we can provide the best possible resolution.
- Make sure to attach any relevant files or documents to support your complaint.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact the support team for assistance.
