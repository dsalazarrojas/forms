# Bylaw Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to report bylaw issues, such as noise disturbances, property maintenance concerns, and zoning irregularities, to allow the local authorities to take action and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your personal details in the "Reporter Information" section, including your full name or indicate if you wish to remain anonymous.
2.  Provide your contact information, such as a phone number or email, for follow-up purposes in the "Reporter Contact" section.
3.  Enter the address or location where the issue occurred in the "Address or Location of Issue" section.
4.  Select the category that best describes the issue in the "Bylaw Category" section.
5.  Provide a detailed description of the issue, including dates and times, in the "Description of Issue" section.
6.  Indicate whether you have attached any photos or evidence related to the issue in the "Photos Attached" section.
7.  Specify your desired outcome in the "Desired Outcome" section.
8.  Grant permission to contact you for follow-up purposes in the "Allow Follow up Contact" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Reporter Information

*   **Reporter Name** (text, required): Enter your full name or indicate if you wish to remain anonymous.
*   **Reporter Contact** (text, optional): Provide your contact information, such as a phone number or email, for follow-up purposes.

### Address or Location of Issue

*   **Address or Location of Issue** (text, required): Enter the address or location where the issue occurred, including street address or nearest intersection.

### Bylaw Category

*   **Bylaw Category** (select_one, required): Select the category that best describes the issue from the provided options.

### Description of Issue

*   **Description of Issue** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue, including dates and times.

### Photos Attached

*   **Photos Attached** (select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have attached any photos or evidence related to the issue.

### Desired Outcome

*   **Desired Outcome** (text, optional): Specify your desired outcome or resolution for the issue.

### Allow Follow up Contact

*   **Allow Follow up Contact** (select_one, required): Grant permission to contact you for follow-up purposes.
