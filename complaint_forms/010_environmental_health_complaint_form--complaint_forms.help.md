# Environmental Health Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Health Complaint Form is used to report environmental health concerns or issues.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form completely and accurately.
- Please report only once for each unique issue.
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible about the problem.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): Enter your valid email address.
  - **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
  - **Problem Description** (`problem_description`, Text, Required): Please describe the concern in as much detail as possible.
  - **Location Type** (`location_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of location where the issue is occurring:
    - **Address**
    - **Building**
    - **Neighborhood**
    - **Street**
    - **Facility**
  - **Latitude** (`latitude`, Number, Required): Enter the latitude coordinate of the location.
  - **Longitude** (`longitude`, Number, Required): Enter the longitude coordinate of the location.
  - **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional context or information that may be helpful for our team to address the issue.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you are unsure about the location type, please choose the most accurate option.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- Please note that this form is for reporting environmental health concerns only. If you are reporting a non-environmental issue, please contact us directly.
