# Fraudulent Transaction Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to report suspicious or fraudulent transactions to the relevant authorities. If you suspect a transaction to be fraudulent, please fill out this form to provide a detailed report. This will help prevent further fraudulent activities and ensure the protection of our organization's assets.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill out each field with the required information, making sure to enter all relevant details.
3. Double-check your input to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter suspicious activity name** (`suspicious_activity_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the suspicious activity that you wish to report.
* **Transaction date** (`transaction_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the transaction that you suspect to be fraudulent.
* **Transaction amount** (`transaction_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the transaction.
* **Account number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Enter the account number associated with the transaction.
* **Card number** (`card_number`, text, optional): Enter the card number used in the transaction.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the fraudulent transaction.
* **Reporting officer** (`reporting_officer`, text, optional): Enter your name and position for identification purposes.
* **Report date** (`reporting_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting the report.
* **Report time** (`reporting_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting the report.
* **Total fraud amount** (`fraud_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the fraud.
