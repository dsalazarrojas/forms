# Insurance Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect complaints or feedback from insurance customers. Please use this form to report any issues or concerns you have experienced with your insurance provider.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your complaint details in the "Complaint Details" field.
2. Select the category of your complaint from the "Complaint Category" dropdown list.
3. Enter the description of your complaint in the "Description of Complaint" field.
4. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
5. Enter the name of your insurance provider.
6. Enter the claim number (if applicable).
7. Select the status of your claim from the "Claim Status" dropdown list.
8. Choose the outcome of your complaint from the "Complaint Outcome" dropdown list.
9. Enter the date of the complaint and contact date if applicable.
10. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of your complaint.
* **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, select_one, Required):
	+ Choose the category of your complaint (Service, Coverage, Claim Handling).
* **Description of Complaint** (`complaint_description`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter a detailed description of your complaint.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter your full name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Claim Number** (`claim_number`, number, Optional):
	+ Enter the claim number (if applicable).
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, Required):
	+ Choose the status of your claim (Open, Closed, Pending).
* **Date of Complaint** (`complaint_date`, date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date of your complaint.
* **Contact Date** (`contact_date`, date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date you were contacted.
* **Complaint Outcome** (`complaint_outcome`, select_one, Required):
	+ Choose the outcome of your complaint (Resolved, Pending, Unresolved).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information about your complaint.
