# Caller ID Spoofing Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Caller ID Spoofing Complaint Form is designed to collect information from individuals who have been victims of caller ID spoofing. This form will help investigators gather details about the incident, track down the scammers, and prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about the incident.
2.  Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as required).
3.  You can attach any supporting evidence or files to the form if relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Complainant Name**: Your name as the person filing the complaint.
*   **Contact Phone or Email**: The best way for our team to contact you for follow-up questions or updates.
*   **Date of Incident**: The date when the spoofed call occurred.
*   **Spoofed Caller ID Number**: The number that appeared on your caller ID during the call.
*   **Number Receiving the Call**: The number on which you received the spoofed call.
*   **Description of the Call**: Provide detailed information about what was said or requested during the call.
*   **Evidence or Supporting Files**: Attach any relevant files or descriptions of evidence, such as screenshots or logs.
*   **Severity of Impact**: Rate the severity of the impact on you or your organization (High, Medium, Low).
*   **Police Report Filed**: If a police report has already been filed regarding this incident.
*   **Preferred Follow Up Method**: How you would like to be contacted for updates (Email, Phone, No Follow Up).

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete all required fields accurately.
*   Attach any relevant evidence or files to support your complaint.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
