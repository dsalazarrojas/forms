# request_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The request_complaint_form is a tool used to collect user complaints in a standardized format. It is a multi-page form that guides users through the process of providing detailed information about their complaint.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by selecting the correct complaint type from the options provided in the "Complaint Type" field.
2. Describe the nature of your complaint in the "Description" field.
3. Provide your email address (if you have one) and phone number (if you have one) in the "Email" and "Phone" fields respectively. These are optional fields.
4. If you would like to be assigned to a specific individual or team for handling your complaint, select the correct option in the "Assigned to" field.
5. Ensure that you complete the "Date" and "Time" fields with the relevant details if applicable.
6. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to complete the form and submit your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Type** (Required): This field allows you to choose the type of complaint you are reporting. Please select the option that best describes your complaint from the options provided.
* **Description** (Required): Here, you need to provide a detailed description of your complaint. Explain the issue you are experiencing and what you have tried so far to resolve it.
* **Email** (Optional): If you have an email address, please provide it so that we can contact you for further clarification or updates about your complaint.
* **Phone** (Optional): If you have a phone number, please provide it so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Date**: This field is used to select the date when the issue occurred. Please ensure that you enter the correct date in the format specified.
* **Time**: Select the time when the issue occurred. Please ensure that you enter the correct time in the format specified.
* **Assigned to**: If you have a specific individual or team assigned to handle your complaint, please select them from the options provided.
* **Submit** (Required): Once you have completed all the fields, click on this button to submit your complaint. This will finalize your submission and allow us to process it further.
