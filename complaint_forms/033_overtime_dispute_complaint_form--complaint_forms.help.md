# overtime_dispute_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable employees to report and submit their overtime dispute complaints to the HR department. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your complaint is handled efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Attach any supporting documentation or evidence as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information**: Enter the name and address of the company where you worked to report the overtime dispute.
  * This field is not mandatory but is useful for reference purposes.
* **Complaint Description**: Briefly describe the situation where you were not paid for work performed beyond regular working hours.
  * This field is not mandatory but helps us understand the context of your complaint.
* **Overtime Period**: Enter the number of days or weeks where you worked overtime.
  * This field helps us understand how long you worked beyond your regular hours.
* **Hours Owed**: Enter the number of hours you believe you are owed.
  * This field is crucial in determining the amount of unpaid overtime.
* **Pay Date**: Enter the date when you should have been paid for your overtime.
  * This field is essential in tracking the timeline of your pay dispute.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth processing of your complaint.
* Attach any supporting documents or evidence related to your complaint.
* If you have already submitted this form, please do not resubmit. Contact HR directly to follow up on your case.
