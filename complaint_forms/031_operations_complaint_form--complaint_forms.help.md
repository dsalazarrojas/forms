# operations_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operations complaint form is used to collect information from users about any issues they have experienced with the system. This form helps to track and address complaints in a structured and organized manner, ensuring that the operations team can quickly understand the issue and work on resolving it. Please note that this form is meant to be used in a controlled and monitored environment where users submit their complaints through the provided interface.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the first field, "User Name".
2. Provide your email address in the "User Email" field.
3. Choose the date of the issue in the "Complaint Date" field.
4. Enter a detailed description of the issue in the "Complaint Description" field.
5. Select the assigned operator from the drop-down menu in the "Assigned Operator" field.
6. Choose the type of operation that was being performed in the "Operation Type" field.
7. Select the date and time of the operation in the "Operation Date" and "Operation Time" fields respectively.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Choose the channel through which the complaint was submitted in the "Submitted Via" field.
10. Select the tool that was assigned to address the issue in the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. Choose any applicable categories in the "Category" field.
12. Select any additional tags in the "Tags" field.
13. Add any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field (optional).
14. Indicate whether the complaint was submitted by the user in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it will be displayed on the complaint record.
* **User Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Complaint Date** (date, required): Enter the date on which you experienced the issue.
* **Complaint Description** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue you are reporting.
* **Assigned Operator** (select_one, required): Choose the name of the operator who will address your issue.
* **Operation Type** (select_multiple, required): Select one or more types of operations that were performed.
* **Operation Date** (date, required): Select the date on which the operation was performed.
* **Operation Time** (time, required): Select the time on which the operation was performed.
* **Notes** (note, required): Add any additional information about the issue.
* **Submitted Via** (select_one, required): Choose the channel through which you submitted the complaint.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required): Select the tool that will be used to address the issue.
* **Category** (select_multiple, optional): Select any applicable categories for your issue.
* **Tags** (select_multiple, optional): Add any additional tags to help categorize your issue.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the issue.
* **Submitted By** (select_one, required): Indicate whether the complaint was submitted by you.
