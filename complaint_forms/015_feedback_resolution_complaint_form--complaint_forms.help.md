# Feedback Resolution Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Feedback Resolution Complaint Form is a tool for reporting issues with products or services you have used. It helps us identify and resolve problems to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of issue you are reporting (Product, Service, or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of the problem you encountered.
3. Share your feedback on the issue to help us understand it better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Type** (`Product`, `Service`, or `Other`):
    This field asks you to choose the type of issue you are experiencing. Select the one that best describes your problem.

* **Description** (`text`):
    In this field, please provide a clear and concise summary of the issue you are experiencing. Try to include as much detail as possible to help us understand what happened.

* **Feedback** (`text`):
    Share your thoughts on what we can do to improve. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us resolve the issue and prevent similar problems in the future.
