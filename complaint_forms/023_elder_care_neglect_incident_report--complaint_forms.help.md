# Elder Care Neglect Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elder Care Neglect Incident Report form is designed to document incidents of elder care neglect in a structured and detailed manner. This form is meant to be filled out by a person witnessing or reporting an incident related to neglect or suboptimal care of an elderly individual.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure you only fill out this form for a legitimate incident.
- Gather as much information as you can about what happened, including dates, times, and details regarding the incident.
- Complete all required fields (as marked on the form).
- Review the form thoroughly before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Details**
  (id: 1, text, required/optional): Describe the incident, including what happened, where it occurred, and any relevant details.
- **Resident Information**
  (id: 2, text, required/optional): Provide the resident's name, date of birth, address, or any other identifying information that may be relevant to the report.
- **Reporter Information**
  (id: 3, text, required/optional): Include your name, contact information (email, phone, etc.), and any other relevant details about who you are and why you are reporting the incident.
- **Report Date**
  (id: 4, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the incident occurred (or is suspected to have occurred), using the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time of Day**
  (id: 5, number, required/optional): Enter the time of day when the incident occurred (or is suspected to have occurred), using a 24-hour format (00-23).
