# court_services_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The court services complaint form is used to file a complaint with the court services department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of complaint you are filing (General, Dispute, or Other).
2. Enter your full name.
3. Enter your contact email address.
4. Enter your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Court Services Complaint** (`complaint_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of complaint you are filing. This will help the court services department understand the nature of your complaint.
* **full_name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number, including any necessary country code or area code.
