## Purpose

The Real Estate Mismanagement Claim Form is designed to collect information about incidents or issues that have occurred in a property. This form helps to report and resolve these incidents efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

3. Make sure to attach any relevant supporting documents when necessary.
