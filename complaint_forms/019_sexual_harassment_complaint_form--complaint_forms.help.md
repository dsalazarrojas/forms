# Sexual Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sexual Harassment Complaint Form is designed to help individuals report incidents of sexual harassment in a confidential and secure environment. The form will be used to collect information about the incident, including details about the harasser, the incident date and time, and the resolution process. This information will help the investigator to conduct a thorough investigation and take appropriate action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide general information about yourself, including your name and contact details.
2. Identify the harasser and provide any relevant information about them.
3. Describe the incident, including the date and time it occurred, and the location.
4. Provide any witness information, if applicable.
5. Provide any support information, such as support person or contact details.
6. Describe the resolution, including any actions taken or outcomes.
7. Provide any witness statements or statements from the harasser.
8. Update the resolution status of the incident.
9. Add any notes or comments about the incident.
