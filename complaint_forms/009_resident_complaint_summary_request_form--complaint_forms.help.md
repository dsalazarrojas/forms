# Resident Complaint Summary Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Complaint Summary Request Form is designed to collect resident complaints in a facility. This form is used to gather information about the concerns or problems experienced by residents during their stay, and to help facility staff address these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and date of the complaint.
2. Describe the issue you experienced in the complaint description field.
3. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address (optional).
4. If you are able to, sign the resident signature field.
5. If a facility staff member is submitting this form on your behalf, they will sign the staff signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Complaint Form 1**: This is the main description field for your complaint. Please provide as much detail as possible about the issue you experienced.
* **Resident Name**: This is your name, please enter it as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Complaint**: Enter the date you first experienced the issue.
* **Time of Complaint**: If relevant, enter the time you first experienced the issue. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Complaint Description**: This is the main description field for your complaint. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Contact Number**: This is your contact number, where we can reach you if we need more information or clarification.
* **Email Address** (optional): If you have an email address you'd like to be contacted on, please enter it here.
* **Resident Signature**: This is where you sign your name, confirming that the information you provided is accurate. Please use your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Staff Signature** (optional): If a facility staff member is submitting this form on your behalf, they will sign this field.
