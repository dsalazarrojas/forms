# Mobile Payment Not Received Dispute Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers report and dispute mobile payment not received issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide as much detail as possible about your experience. Include any relevant dates and amounts, and make sure to provide accurate contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact info**: Please enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you to discuss your issue.
* **Order details**: Describe your order, including the date and amount you paid, to help us understand your situation.
* **Payment info**: Share any relevant payment information, such as the payment method and transaction ID.
* **Reason for dispute**: Explain why you have not received the goods or services you expected. This could be due to issues with delivery, product quality, or other reasons.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you have already submitted a dispute or complaint about this issue, do not resubmit.
