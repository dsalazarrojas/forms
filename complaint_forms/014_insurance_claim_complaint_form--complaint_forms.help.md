# insurance_claim_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting insurance claims. Fill out this form to report any issues or complaints related to your insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Complaint Details field with a brief description of your complaint.
2. Enter your Name and Email address to help us contact you about your claim.
3. Provide your Phone number so we can reach you if needed.
4. Select the type of complaint you are experiencing (e.g. General Insurance Claim, Policyholder Issue, Provider Issue, etc.).
5. Enter a detailed Description of your complaint.
6. If applicable, provide the Claim Number and Insurance Provider information.
7. Upload any relevant Attachments that support your claim.
8. Choose who Submitted Your Claim (e.g. Policyholder, Insurance Provider, etc.).
9. Select the date when you Submitted Your Claim.
10. Choose who you Submitted To (e.g. Insurance Provider, Underwriter, etc.).
11. If you submitted your claim by phone, enter the date and time.
12. If you submitted your claim by phone, select who you submitted To (e.g. Insurance Provider, Other).
13. Enter any additional Notes about your claim.
14. Choose the current Claim Status (e.g. Open, Closed, Resolved).
15. If applicable, enter the Resolution Date and select who Resolved Your Claim.
16. Choose who Resolved Your Claim (e.g. Policyholder, Insurance Provider, etc.).
17. Enter the Attachment Count.
18. Select who Updated Your Claim (e.g. Policyholder, Insurance Provider, etc.).
19. Enter the date when Your Claim was Last Updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Details**: Enter a brief description of your complaint.
* **Name**: Enter your name, so we can contact you about your claim.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, so we can reach out to you about your claim.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, so we can contact you if needed.
* **Complaint Type**: Select the type of complaint you are experiencing (e.g. General Insurance Claim, Policyholder Issue, Provider Issue, etc.).
* **Description**: Enter a detailed description of your complaint.
* **Claim Number**: If applicable, enter the claim number related to your issue.
* **Insurance Provider**: If applicable, enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Attachment**: If applicable, upload any supporting documents or evidence for your claim.
* **Uploaded By**: If applicable, select who uploaded the attachment (e.g. Policyholder, Insurance Provider, etc.).
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted your claim (e.g. Policyholder, Insurance Provider, etc.).
* **Submitted On**: Select the date when you submitted your claim.
* **Submitted To**: Select who you submitted your claim to (e.g. Insurance Provider, Underwriter, etc.).
* **Submitted By Phone**: If applicable, select who submitted your claim by phone (e.g. Policyholder, Insurance Provider, etc.).
* **Submitted On Phone**: If applicable, select the date and time when your claim was submitted by phone.
* **Submitted To Phone**: If applicable, select who you submitted your claim to by phone (e.g. Insurance Provider, Other, etc.).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your claim.
* **Claim Status**: Select the current status of your claim (e.g. Open, Closed, Resolved).
* **Resolution Date**: If applicable, enter the date when your claim was resolved.
* **Resolution By**: Select who resolved your claim (e.g. Policyholder, Insurance Provider, etc.).
* **Resoved By**: Select who resolved your claim (e.g. Policyholder, Insurance Provider, etc.).
* **Attachment Count**: If applicable, enter the number of attachments uploaded with your claim.
* **Updated By**: Select who updated your claim (e.g. Policyholder, Insurance Provider, etc.).
* **Updated On**: Select the date when your claim was last updated.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your insurance claim is processed efficiently. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
