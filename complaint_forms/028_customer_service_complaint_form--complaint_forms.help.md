This form is designed to help us understand and address customer complaints. It is intended for customers to describe their complaints in detail, providing us with valuable feedback that we can use to improve our service.

## Customer Service Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document customer complaints, allowing us to understand their concerns and take appropriate actions to resolve them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. For optional fields, provide as much information as you feel is relevant to your complaint.
3. Ensure that you sign and confirm your signature to validate the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Complaint Form**:
	+ This is the title of the form.
* **Customer Complaint**:
	+ Please enter a brief description of your complaint.
* **Complaint Description**:
	+ Provide a detailed description of your complaint.
* **Contact Number**:
	+ Enter your contact number so we can reach you to discuss your complaint.
* **Email Address**:
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you about your complaint.
* **Complaint Type**:
	+ Select "Yes" if your complaint is related to a specific issue or "No" if it is a general complaint.
* **Resolution Date**:
	+ Enter the date when you would like your complaint to be resolved.
* **Resolution Time**:
	+ Enter the time when you would like your complaint to be resolved.
* **Resolution Status**:
	+ Select "Active" if your complaint is ongoing or "Inactive" if it has been resolved.
* **Customer Signature**:
	+ Sign your name to confirm that you have made this complaint.
* **Customer Signature Confirmation**:
	+ Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Business Signature**:
	+ A representative of our company will sign to confirm that we received your complaint.
* **Business Signature Confirmation**:
	+ Enter the date our representative signed the form.
* **Assigned Agent**:
	+ Select "Yes" if you would like to be assigned to a specific agent or "No" if you do not have a preference.
* **Assigned Agent Confirmation**:
	+ Enter the name of the agent assigned to your complaint.
* **Complaint Category**:
	+ Select "Yes" if your complaint falls under a specific category or "No" if it is a general complaint.
* **Assigned Department**:
	+ Enter the department that is responsible for your complaint.
* **Assigned Date**:
	+ Enter the date when your complaint was assigned to a department.
* **Assigned Time**:
	+ Enter the time when your complaint was assigned to a department.
* **Assigned User**:
	+ Enter the name of the user assigned to your complaint.
* **Customer Notes**:
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about your complaint.
* **Business Notes**:
	+ Our representative will add notes or comments about your complaint.
* **Follow-up Date**:
	+ Enter the date when you would like us to follow up on your complaint.
* **Follow-up Time**:
	+ Enter the time when you would like us to follow up on your complaint.
* **Follow-up Status**:
	+ Select "Active" if you would like us to follow up on your complaint or "Inactive" if it has been resolved.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by customers. If you are a representative of the business, please refer to the corresponding section for more information on how to complete the form.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible when describing your complaint.
* Ensure that you sign and confirm your signature to validate the complaint.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact our customer service department.
