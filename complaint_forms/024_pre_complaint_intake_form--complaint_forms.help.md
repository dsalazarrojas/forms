# pre-complaint-intake-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect incident details from customers or staff members to facilitate a swift and effective resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all required fields and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields. The form is divided into the following sections:

1. Incident Details
2. Incident Date and Time
3. Incident Reporter Information
4. Description and Resolution Outcome
5. Contact Information

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Incident Details
* **Incident Details (incident_details)**: Provide a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details such as the location and type of incident.
### 2. Incident Date and Time
* **Date Incident Happened (date_incident_happened)**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time Incident Happened (time_incident_happened)**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
### 3. Incident Reporter Information
* **Report Who Reported (report_who_reported)**: Identify who reported the incident (e.g., customer, staff member).
### 4. Description and Resolution Outcome
* **Description (description)**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant background information.
* **Resolution Outcome (resolution_outcome)**: Select the current status of the incident: resolved, ongoing, or pending.
### 5. Contact Information
* **Customer Contact Info (customer_contact_info)**: Provide contact information for the customer, if applicable.
* **Staff Contact Info (staff_contact_info)**: Provide contact information for the staff member reporting the incident, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate incident report.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the incident management team for guidance.
* If you've already reported this incident, please do not report it again.
