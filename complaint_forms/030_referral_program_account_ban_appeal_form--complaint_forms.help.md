# Referral Program Account Ban Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Program Account Ban Appeal Form is designed to provide a structured way for users to appeal against a previously imposed referral program account ban. Please use this form to provide a clear and accurate representation of your case, including a clear reason for the ban and any supporting details that may be relevant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Provide a clear reason for the ban in the "Reason for Ban" field.
3. Enter the date of the ban in the "Date of Ban" field.
4. If relevant, include any supporting details in the "Supporting Details" field.
5. Enter your name and email address in the "Submitter Name" and "Submitter Email" fields.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field (optional).
7. Enter a message to the support team in the "Submitter Message" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account Information** (`Account Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your account information, including your Account ID and Account Name.
* **Account ID** (`Account ID`, `text`, required): A unique identifier for your account.
* **Account Name** (`Account Name`, `text`, required): The name of your account.
* **Reason for Ban** (`Reason for Ban`, `text`, required): Please provide a clear and concise reason for the ban.
* **Date of Ban** (`Date of Ban`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the ban.
* **Supporting Details** (`Supporting Details`, `text`, optional): If relevant, please provide any additional details that may support your appeal.
* **Submitter Name** (`Submitter Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`Submitter Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`Submitter Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Submitter Message** (`Submitter Message`, `text`, optional): Enter a message to the support team (optional).

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your submission.
* Duplicate appeals will not be entertained.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [support email].
