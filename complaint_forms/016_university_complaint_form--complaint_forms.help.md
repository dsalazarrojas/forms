# University Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Complaint Form is designed to help the university administration collect information and feedback from students about their experiences and issues within the university. This form allows students to share their concerns and problems with the administration, enabling them to make informed decisions to improve university services and facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure that you understand what is required of you.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address, so that the university can contact you to discuss your complaint.
3. Describe the issue you are experiencing in detail in the text field labeled "Describe your issue".
4. You can attach photos or videos to support your complaint.
5. Choose the category of your complaint from the dropdown menu.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your complaint to the university administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (Page 1): This field allows you to provide some basic information about yourself.
* **Describe your issue** (Page 2): In this field, please describe the issue you are experiencing in as much detail as possible.
* **Upload Evidence** (Page 3): If you have any photos or videos that can help support your complaint, you can upload them here.
* **Contact Information** (Page 4): Please enter your contact information, including your email address, so that we can get in touch with you to discuss your complaint.
* **Attachments** (Page 5): If you have any supporting documents or photos, you can attach them here.
* **Submit** (Page 6): This is the final step. Click "Submit" to send your complaint to our administration team.
* **Review and Submit** (Page 7): Double-check your complaint before submitting it to ensure everything is complete and accurate.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Ensure that your complaint is relevant to the university and its policies.
* Attach any supporting evidence to help us understand the issue better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your university email address.
* We value your feedback and will review your complaint as soon as possible.
