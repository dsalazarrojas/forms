# Workplace Harassment Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a workplace harassment claim. Please provide all necessary information to help us investigate and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form to ensure that your claim is properly documented.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Report Incident** (`report_incident`, text, optional): Describe the incident or situation that you experienced or witnessed.
  • This is the initial description of the incident that happened in the workplace.
  • Be as detailed as possible to help us understand the situation.
- **Date of Incident** (`date_incident`, date, optional): The date the incident occurred.
  • If you are unsure of the date, try to estimate as closely as possible.
- **Describe Incident** (`describe_incident`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
  • This is an opportunity to provide any additional details that may be helpful in investigating the incident.
- **Witness Names** (`witness_names`, text, optional): List the names of any witnesses who saw or heard something about the incident.
  • If there were multiple witnesses, list each name and their contact information if available.
- **Witness Contact Info** (`witness_contact_info`, text, optional): Include any contact information for witnesses, such as email or phone number.
  • This will help us reach out to them for additional information.
- **Witness Statements** (`witness_statements`, text, optional): Provide a statement from each witness, if available.
  • This can be a brief statement or a detailed account of what they saw or heard.
- **Harasser Details** (`harasser_details`, text, optional): Provide any details about the harasser, such as their name or title.
  • This will help us identify the person involved and investigate the incident.
- **Date Harassment Stopped** (`date_harassment_stopped`, time, optional): The date the harassment stopped.
  • If the harassment is ongoing, estimate the date it stopped or is expected to stop.
- **Resolution Outcome** (`resolution_outcome`, select_one, optional): Choose how the resolution of the incident was or will be.
  • Select from the options: Successful resolution, Unsuccessful resolution, or Ongoing resolution.
  • This will help us track the status of the incident.
- **Resolution Details** (`resolution_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the resolution.
  • This can be a description of what was done to resolve the incident or what steps were taken.
- **Actions Taken** (`action_taken`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the actions taken to resolve the incident.
  • Choose from the options: Investigation, Discipline, Counseling, Training, or Other.
  • If multiple actions were taken, select all applicable options.
- **Resolution Outcome 2** (`resolution_outcome_2`, select_one, optional): Another option to select the resolution outcome.
  • This is another choice for resolution outcome with similar options as above.
  • Select from: Successful resolution, Unsuccessful resolution, or Ongoing resolution.
  • Duplicate of resolution outcome field.
- **Resolution Outcome 3** (`resolution_outcome_3`, select_one, optional): Another choice for resolution outcome.
  • Similar to above, select from: Successful resolution, Unsuccessful resolution, or Ongoing resolution.
  • Duplicate of resolution outcome field.

Note: Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form, and review it carefully before submitting. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
