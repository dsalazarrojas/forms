# Illegal Fireworks Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from witnesses about incidents involving illegal fireworks.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the report date and time in the "Report Date" and "Report Time" fields.
2. Enter the name of the person you suspect of using fireworks in the "Suspect Name" field.
3. Enter the address of the suspect in the "Suspect Address" field.
4. Enter the license plate number of the suspect's vehicle in the "Suspect Vehicle License Plate" field (if applicable).
5. Enter a description of the suspect's vehicle in the "Suspect Vehicle Description" field (if applicable).
6. Enter a description of the incident in the "Suspect Description" field.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question of whether the suspect is using fireworks in the "Witness Statement" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Witness Name" field.
9. Enter your address in the "Witness Address" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Witness Contact Number" field.
11. Enter the license plate number of your vehicle in the "Witness Vehicle License Plate" field (if you're reporting as a witness).
12. Enter a description of your vehicle in the "Witness Vehicle Description" field (if you're reporting as a witness).
13. Enter a description of yourself in the "Witness Description" field.
14. Sign your name in the "Witness Signature" field.
15. If you're an officer, enter your name in the "Officer Name" field.
16. If you're an officer, enter your title in the "Officer Title" field.
17. If you're an officer, enter your agency in the "Officer Agency" field.
18. If you're an officer, enter your phone number in the "Officer Contact Number" field.
19. If you're an officer, enter any notes or comments about the incident in the "Officer Notes" field.
20. If you're an officer, enter the date and time you completed this form in the "Officer Date" and "Officer Time" fields.
