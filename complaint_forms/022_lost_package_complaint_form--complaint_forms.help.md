# Lost Package Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lost Package Complaint Form is used to report packages that have not been received by the customer. It is intended to help resolve issues related to missing shipments and provide a clear record of the customer's complaint.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Lost Package Complaint Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and phone number in the **Enter Customer Name** and **Customer Phone** fields, respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the **Customer Email** field.
3. Describe the missing package details in the **Package Details** field.
4. Enter the tracking number of the lost package in the **Package Tracking Number** field.
5. Provide the reason for the lost package in the **Reason for Lost Package** field.
6. Add any additional comments related to the lost package in the **Additional Comments** field.
7. Select the person to whom you would like to report the lost package in the **Assigned To** field. (Please note that this field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.)
8. If you are the customer, please provide your signature and photo in the **Customer Signature** and **Customer Photo** fields, respectively. If you are the store manager, please provide your signature and photo in the **Store Manager Signature** and **Store Manager Photo** fields, respectively.
9. Once you have completed all the fields, submit the form to report the lost package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Customer Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Customer Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Package Details** (text, optional): Describe the package in detail, including its contents and any other relevant information.
* **Package Tracking Number** (text, optional): Enter the tracking number of the lost package.
* **Reason for Lost Package** (text, optional): Provide a clear explanation for why you think the package was lost.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional information that may be helpful in resolving the issue.
* **Assigned To** (select one, optional): Select the person you would like to report the lost package to. (Please note that this field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.)
* **Customer Signature** (note, required if applicable): If you are the customer, please provide your signature to confirm that you are the person reporting the lost package.
* **Customer Photo** (note, required if applicable): If you are the customer, please provide a photo of yourself to confirm your identity.
* **Store Manager Signature** (note, required if applicable): If you are the store manager, please provide your signature to confirm that you are aware of the lost package.
* **Store Manager Photo** (note, required if applicable): If you are the store manager, please provide a photo of yourself to confirm your identity.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are not the customer, please ensure that the customer's details and signature are provided.
* If you are unsure of any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
