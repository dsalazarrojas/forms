# health_outreach_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document complaints from patients and track the progress of resolving them. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure the provider receives the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Describe the complaint in the **Complaint Details** field.
4. Select how you would like to be contacted in the **Contact Method** field.
5. Select the healthcare provider(s) involved in the **Provider** field.
6. Enter the date of the visit in the **Date of Visit** field.
7. Enter the time of the visit in the **Time of Visit** field.
8. Briefly describe the issue again in the **Description** field.
9. Confirm the follow-up status in the **Follow-up Status** field.
10. If necessary, select the provider(s) assigned to follow up on the issue in the **Assigned To** field.
11. (Optional) Add any comments in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient who is raising the complaint.
* **Email**: Provide your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Complaint Details**: Describe the issue or concern you are experiencing.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted: via **Phone**, **Email**, or **In-person**.
* **Provider**: Select the healthcare provider(s) who is/are involved in your issue: **Doctor**, **Staff**, or **Nurse**.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit when the issue occurred.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of the visit.
* **Description**: Briefly restate the complaint for the provider's reference.
* **Follow-up Status**: Confirm the follow-up status as **Scheduled**, **Complete**, or **Not Scheduled**.
* **Assigned To**: Select the provider(s) who will follow up on the issue (if different from the one involved).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the issue (optional).
* **Provider**: (Optional) Select additional healthcare provider(s) involved in the issue.
