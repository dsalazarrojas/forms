# Product Defectiveness Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report any defects or issues experienced with a product.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Ensure that each field is filled out only once per defect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

1. **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the product that is experiencing the defect.
2. **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of product that is experiencing the defect. Options: Hardware, Software, Service.
3. **Defect Description** (`defect_description`, text, required): Describe the defect or issue experienced with the product.
4. **Defect Location** (`defect_location`, text, required): Provide the location where the defect occurred.
5. **Defect Date** (`defect_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the defect occurred.
6. **Defect Time** (`defect_time`, time, required): Enter the time when the defect occurred.
7. **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, required): This field is not required. If you have an image of the product that shows the defect, you can upload it here.
8. **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer or product owner reporting the defect.
9. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the customer or product owner.
10. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address of the customer or product owner.
11. **Defect Severity** (`defect_severity`, select_one, required): Select the severity of the defect. Options: Critical, High, Medium, Low.
12. **Defect Status** (`defect_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the defect. Options: Open, Closed, In Progress.
13. **Follow Up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, required): This field is not required. If you have any follow-up notes or comments, you can enter them here.
14. **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): This field is not required. If you have any follow-up plans or actions, enter the date here.
15. **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required): This field is not required. If you have any follow-up plans or actions, enter the time here.
16. **Product Category** (`product_category`, text, required): Enter the category of the product.
17. **Product Subcategory** (`product_subcategory`, text, required): Enter the subcategory of the product.
18. **Defect Recurring** (`defect_recurring`, select_one, required): Select whether the defect is recurring. Options: True, False.
19. **Defect Recurring Frequency** (`defect_recurring_frequency`, text, required): If the defect is recurring, enter the frequency.
20. **Defect Last Occurrence** (`defect_last_occurrence`, date, required): Enter the date of the last occurrence of the defect.
21. **Defect First Occurrence** (`defect_first_occurrence`, date, required): Enter the date of the first occurrence of the defect.
22. **Defect Total Occurrences** (`defect_total_occurrences`, number, required): Enter the total number of occurrences of the defect.
23. **Defect Last Resolution Method** (`defect_last_resolution_method`, text, required): Enter the method used to resolve the last occurrence of the defect.
24. **Defect Resolution Status** (`defect_resolution_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the resolution. Options: Resolved, Not Resolved, In Progress.
25. **Defect Reopening Status** (`defect_reopening_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the defect reopening. Options: Reopened, Not Reopened, Not Applicable.
