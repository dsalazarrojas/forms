# HR Complaint Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Complaint Information Form is designed to gather relevant information regarding employee complaints. This form helps HR departments collect and track complaints in an organized and structured manner. **Please ensure that you are filling a complaint form for the correct reason and not duplicating efforts.** If you are unsure, contact HR or your supervisor for guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Provide a valid contact number to ensure that we can reach out to you for any follow-up questions.
3. Select a date for the complaint from your calendar and enter it in the **Complaint Date** field.
4. Describe the issue or complaint in the **Complaint Details** field.
5. Choose a category for your complaint from the options provided in the **Complaint Category** field.
6. Provide a description of the complaint in the **Complaint Description** field.
7. If applicable, select the **Resolution Status** of your complaint.
8. Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor in the **Manager Name** field.
9. Choose your reporting relationship with your manager in the **Employee Manager Relation** field.
10. Enter your department and position in the **Employee Department** and **Employee Position** fields (optional).
11. If you have any supporting documents, attach them in the **Supporting Documents** field.
12. If necessary, select the **Complaint Outcome** and describe the **Action Taken** and **Action Taken By** fields.
13. Finally, select the **Follow-up** status for any follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Information**: This is the main section of the form where you need to provide an overview of your complaint.
* **Employee Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel file.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a valid contact number that we can use to reach out to you for further information or follow-up.
* **Complaint Date**: Select a date from your calendar when the issue or complaint occurred.
* **Complaint Details**: Describe the issue or complaint in as much detail as possible.
* **Complaint Category**: Select the category of your complaint from the provided options (Harassment, Retaliation, Other).
* **Complaint Description**: Provide a detailed description of your complaint.
* **Resolution Status**: Select the status of your complaint from the provided options (Not Resolved, In Progress, Resolved).
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Employee Manager Relation**: Choose your relationship with your manager from the provided options (Direct Report, Second Report, Not Applicable, Other).
* **Employee Department**: Enter your department name (optional).
* **Employee Position**: Enter your position name (optional).
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any supporting documents related to your complaint.
* **Complaint Outcome**: Select the outcome of your complaint from the provided options (Not Resolved, In Progress, Resolved).
* **Action Taken**: Describe any actions taken to address your complaint.
* **Action Taken By**: Describe who took the action to address your complaint.
* **Action Taken Date**: Select the date when the action was taken (optional).
* **Follow-up**: Select the status of any follow-up actions (Not Done, In Progress, Done).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information about your complaint.
