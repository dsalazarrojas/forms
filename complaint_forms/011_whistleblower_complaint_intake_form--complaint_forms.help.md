# Whistleblower Complaint Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Whistleblower Complaint Intake Form is a tool for reporting misconduct or complaints. It helps ensure that reported incidents are documented and addressed in a fair and timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a clear and concise description of the misconduct or complaint in the **complaint_form** field.
2. If applicable, choose the category of misconduct being reported in the **report_misconduct** field.
3. In the **describe_misconduct** field, provide further details about the incident.
4. In the **report_identity** field, select the appropriate option that best describes your identity as a whistleblower.
5. Provide any additional information related to the misconduct in the **report_details** field.
6. In the **submit_form** field, choose which department(s) you would like to receive your report.
7. Confirm your submission by choosing the corresponding option in the **confirm_submission** field.
8. Finally, provide any relevant contact information, such as your name and contact number, in the **contact_information** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Form (complaint_form)**: A free-text field where you can provide a clear and concise description of the misconduct or complaint.
* **Report Misconduct (report_misconduct)**: A field to choose the category of misconduct being reported (if applicable).
* **Describe Misconduct (describe_misconduct)**: A field to provide further details about the incident.
* **Report Identity (report_identity)**: A field to select the option that best describes your identity as a whistleblower.
* **Report Details (report_details)**: A field to provide any additional information related to the misconduct.
* **Submit Form (submit_form)**: A field to choose which department(s) you would like to receive your report.
* **Confirm Submission (confirm_submission)**: A field to confirm your submission by choosing the corresponding option.
* **Contact Information (contact_information)**: A field to provide any relevant contact information, such as your name and contact number.

## Tips

- Please ensure that your complaint is clear and concise, as this will help us process it more efficiently.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
- This form is designed to gather information about misconduct within the organization; please only use it for this purpose.
