# Hurt Feelings Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hurt Feelings Complaint Form is a tool for reporting instances of hurt feelings caused by workplace conflicts, misunderstandings, or other personal issues. It helps our team understand the complaint and address it fairly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the description of the issue that has caused you harm.
2. Select the date when you first noticed or experienced this issue.
3. Choose who reported the issue: yourself (if you are reporting on behalf of yourself), John, Jane, or Admin (if you are reporting on behalf of someone else).
4. Select the category that best describes the issue (Category A, Category B, or Category C).
5. Update the status of the issue (Open or Closed, if it has already been resolved).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Description (complaint_description)**: Please provide a clear and concise description of what happened, including any relevant details such as dates, times, and locations.
* **Date (report_date)**: Select the date when you first noticed or experienced the issue. This helps us understand the timeline of the incident.
* **Reported By (reported_by)**: If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, please select their name (John, Jane, or Admin). If you are reporting on yourself, select 'Yourself'.
* **Category (category)**: Choose the category that best describes the issue. This helps us understand the type of problem and allocate resources accordingly.
* **Status (status)**: If the issue has already been resolved, select 'Closed'. If it is still open and being addressed, select 'Open'.
