Before submitting the form, please make sure you have not already filed a complaint about the same issue, as it may cause duplicate processing and delays in resolution.

# Late Delivery Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Late Delivery Complaint Form is for customers to report instances of late delivery of products or services. This form helps us to understand the issue, investigate, and take necessary actions to resolve the complaint and prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information to help us contact you to discuss the issue.
2. Provide the date of the expected delivery, so we can verify the late delivery.
3. Write a brief description of the issue, explaining what happened and how it affected you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: This is your name, as it appears on your order or account with us.
* **Contact Phone**: This is your phone number where you can be reached to discuss the issue.
* **Contact Email**: This is your email address where we can contact you to discuss the issue.
* **Delivery Date**: This is the date when you expected to receive the delivery. Please ensure you enter the correct date to help us investigate the issue.
* **Description**: This is a brief explanation of what happened and how the late delivery affected you. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand the situation and resolve the issue efficiently.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when describing the issue.
- If you have any supporting documents or evidence (e.g., order confirmation, delivery receipt), attach them to your complaint email or mail them to us.
- We will review and investigate your complaint within 3-5 business days. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at the provided contact information.
