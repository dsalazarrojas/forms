# Funny Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funny Complaint Form is designed to gather feedback and complaints from customers, allowing them to describe a problem they've encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the problem type**: Choose the category that best describes the issue you're experiencing. Select from: error, mistake, issue, problem, question, or concern.
2. **Describe the issue**: Provide a brief but clear explanation of what happened. This will help us understand the context of your complaint.
3. **Enter your name**: If you'd like to share your name, please do so. This will help us contact you or follow up on your issue, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the problem**: This field lets you choose a category for your complaint. Select one of the six options to help us understand what type of issue you're experiencing.
* **Describe the issue**: This field is where you can provide more details about what happened. Keep it brief but clear, and we'll appreciate the context.
* **Your Name**: This field is optional, but if you'd like to share your name, we'll use it to follow up on your issue or contact you directly.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing the issue to help us better understand your complaint.
* If you have any attachments or evidence related to your complaint, feel free to attach them to this form.
* We'll review your complaint and get back to you as soon as possible.
