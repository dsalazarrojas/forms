# Missing Loyalty Points Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to help users claim their missing loyalty points for a specific purchase. Fill out this form to provide the necessary information to verify your missing points.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information about your purchase, including the date, time, and details of the item purchased.
2. Provide any relevant documentation or proof of purchase, such as a photo or receipt, in the "photo_proof" field.
3. Select the relevant information about the loyalty program you are claiming points for.
4. Provide a brief description of the issue and how it should be resolved in the "claim_details" field.
5. If applicable, select the "assign_to" and "reviewer_completed" fields to assign the claim to a specific reviewer and mark it as completed.
6. Fill out the "email", "phone", "reviewer_email", and "reviewer_phone" fields with the contact information of the reviewer assigned to your claim.
7. Select the "reviewer_assigned" and "reviewer_completed" fields to indicate whether the claim has been assigned to and completed by the reviewer.
8. Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **purchase_details** (Purchase Details, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the purchase, including the item name and price.
* **purchase_date** (Purchase Date, date, required): Enter the date of the purchase.
* **purchase_time** (Purchase Time, time, required): Enter the time of the purchase.
* **item_details** (Item Details, text, required): Provide the specific details about the item purchased, including the quantity and any relevant serial numbers or model numbers.
* **store_details** (Store Details, text, optional): If the purchase was made in a physical store, please provide the store name and location.
* **photo_proof** (Photo Proof, text, optional): Attach a photo or scan of the receipt or proof of purchase.
* **loyalty_program** (Loyalty Program, text, required): Select the loyalty program you are claiming points for.
* **claim_details** (Claim Details, text, required): Briefly describe the issue and how it should be resolved.
* **resolution** (Resolution, text, optional): If you have already spoken with a representative and received a resolution, please provide the details of the resolution.
* **notes** (Notes, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the claim.
* **email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **assign_to** (Assign To, select multiple, optional): Select the reviewer to whom you would like to assign this claim.
* **reviewer_name** (Reviewer Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer assigned to your claim.
* **reviewer_comments** (Reviewer Comments, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the reviewer.
* **reviewer_notes** (Reviewer Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the reviewer.
* **reviewer_email** (Reviewer Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **reviewer_phone** (Reviewer Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **reviewer_assigned** (Reviewer Assigned, select multiple, optional): Select whether the claim has been assigned to the reviewer.
* **reviewer_completed** (Reviewer Completed, select multiple, optional): Select whether the claim has been completed by the reviewer.
* **assign_to** (Assign To, select multiple, optional): Select the reviewer to whom you would like to assign this claim.
* **reviewer_assigned** (Reviewer Assigned, select multiple, optional): Select whether the claim has been assigned to the reviewer.
* **reviewer_completed** (Reviewer Completed, select multiple, optional): Select whether the claim has been completed by the reviewer.
