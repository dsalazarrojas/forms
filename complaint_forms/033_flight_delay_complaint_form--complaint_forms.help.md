# Flight Delay Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flight Delay Complaint Form is designed to collect information about flight delays, allowing us to better understand and address these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page that best describes your experience with the flight delay.
2. Fill out the required fields for each page.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flight Delay** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_1_page`): Describe the flight delay you experienced. This field is optional but recommended for detailed information.
* **Flight Delay Type** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_2_page`): Choose the type of flight delay you experienced from the options provided. This field is optional.
* **Delay Reason** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_3_page`): If not selected, please describe the reason for the flight delay. This field is optional.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_4_page`): Select all relevant flight numbers that are relevant to the delay.
* **Passenger Name** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_5_page`): Provide the name of the passenger who experienced the flight delay.
* **Passenger Phone** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_6_page`): Enter a valid phone number for the passenger.
* **Passenger Email** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_7_page`): Enter a valid email address for the passenger. Note: This field is not displayed in the form, but it's used internally for communication purposes.
* **Flight Date** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_8_page`): Select the date of the flight delay.
* **Flight Time** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_9_page`): Select the time of the flight delay.
* **Other Details** (`flight_delay_complaint_form_10_page`): Provide any additional information about your experience with the flight delay. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field explanation above.
* If you need help or assistance, contact us at [support email/phone number].
