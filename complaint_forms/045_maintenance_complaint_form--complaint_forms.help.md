# Maintenance Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report maintenance complaints to the maintenance team. It allows you to provide details about the issue and its location, and helps the team to track and prioritize complaints.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Describe the maintenance complaint in the "Complaint Description" field.
2. Specify the location of the complaint in the "Complaint Location" field.
3. Provide additional details about the complaint in the "Complaint Details" field.
4. Select the maintenance team responsible for addressing the complaint from the "Maintenance Team" dropdown.
5. Choose the priority level of the complaint from the "Priority" dropdown.
6. Select the category of the complaint from the "Category" dropdown.
7. Choose the status of the complaint from the "Status" dropdown.
8. Select the person or team to assign the complaint to from the "Assigned To" dropdown.
9. Add any comments about the complaint in the "Comments" field.
10. Optionally, enter the date and time the complaint was submitted using the "Date Submitted" and "Time Submitted" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Complaint** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the maintenance complaint.
* **Complaint Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Complaint Location** (Text, Optional): Specify the location where the complaint was found.
* **Complaint Details** (Text, Optional): Add any additional information about the complaint.
* **Maintenance Team** (Select One, Optional): Select the team responsible for addressing the complaint.

	+ Option 2: Team B
	+ Option 3: Team C
* **Priority** (Select One, Optional): Choose the level of priority for the complaint.
	+ Option 1: Low
	+ Option 2: Medium
	+ Option 3: High
* **Category** (Select One, Optional): Select the category of the complaint.
	+ Option 1: Category 1
	+ Option 2: Category 2
	+ Option 3: Category 3
* **Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the current status of the complaint.
	+ Option 1: New
	+ Option 2: In Progress
	+ Option 3: Resolved
* **Assigned To** (Select One, Optional): Select the person or team to assign the complaint to.
	+ Option 1: John Doe
	+ Option 2: Jane Smith
	+ Option 3: Team Lead
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments about the complaint.
* **Date Submitted** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the complaint was submitted (in date format).
