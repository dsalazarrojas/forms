# Member Complaint And Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document member complaints and appeals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are experiencing a complaint or appeal.
2. Provide a detailed description of your complaint or appeal in the 'Member Complaint Details' field.
3. If you are appealing, provide additional information about your appeal in the 'Appeal Details' field.
4. Choose the type of complaint or appeal you are experiencing in the 'Complaint Type' field.
5. Provide more information about the facility issue in the 'Facility Issue Details' field.
6. Enter your name and contact information in the 'Member's Name' and 'Member's Contact Info' fields.
7. If applicable, enter the date and time of the event in the 'Date of Event' and 'Time of Event' fields.
8. If you are reporting an issue with a facility, enter the address and location of the facility in the 'Facility Address' and 'Facility Location' fields.
9. Choose the staff member who is employed at the facility in the 'Staff Employed At' field.
10. If you are reporting an issue with a staff member, enter their name in the 'Reporting Staff Member' field.
11. Provide more information about the facility issue and misconduct in the 'Facility Issue Description' and 'Misconduct Description' fields.
12. Propose a resolution in the 'Resolution' field.
13. Provide your next steps in the 'Next Steps' field.
14. Enter the date of the resolution in the 'Date of Resolution' field.
15. Enter the action taken by the staff in the 'Action Taken' field.
16. Enter your comments in the 'Member Comments' field.
17. Enter the staff's comments in the 'Staff Comments' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Complaint Form**: A select multiple question that asks if the member has experienced a complaint or appeal.
	+ Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are experiencing a complaint or appeal.
	+ Select 'No' to indicate that you are not experiencing a complaint or appeal.
* **Member Complaint Details**: A text field where the member can provide a detailed description of their complaint or appeal.
* **Appeal Details**: A text field where the member can provide additional information about their appeal.
* **Complaint Type**: A select one question that asks the member to choose the type of complaint or appeal they are experiencing.
	+ Choose the type of complaint or appeal you are experiencing.
* **Facility Issue Details**: A text field where the member can provide more information about the facility issue.
* **Member's Name**: A text field where the member can provide their name.
* **Member's Contact Info**: A select multiple question that asks if the member's contact information is available.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are available to provide your contact information.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not available to provide your contact information.
* **Date of Event**: A date field where the member can provide the date of the event.
* **Time of Event**: A time field where the member can provide the time of the event.
* **Facility Address**: A text field where the member can provide the address of the facility.
* **Facility Location**: A text field where the member can provide the location of the facility.
* **Staff Employed At**: A select one question that asks the member to choose which staff member is employed at the facility.
	+ Choose the staff member who is employed at the facility.
* **Reporting Staff Member**: A text field where the member can provide the name of the staff member who is reporting the issue.
* **Facility Issue Description**: A text field where the member can provide more information about the facility issue.
* **Misconduct Description**: A text field where the member can provide more information about the misconduct.
* **Resolution**: A text field where the member can provide their proposed resolution.
* **Next Steps**: A text field where the member can provide their next steps.
* **Action Taken**: A text field where the staff can provide their action taken.
* **Date of Resolution**: A date field where the staff can provide the date of the resolution.
* **Staff Comments**: A text field where the staff can provide their comments.
* **Member Comments**: A text field where the member can provide their comments.
