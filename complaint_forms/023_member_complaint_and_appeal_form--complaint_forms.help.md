# Member Complaint And Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document member complaints and appeals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are experiencing a complaint or appeal.
2. Provide a detailed description of your complaint or appeal in the 'Member Complaint Details' field.
3. If you are appealing, provide additional information about your appeal in the 'Appeal Details' field.
4. Choose the type of complaint or appeal you are experiencing in the 'Complaint Type' field.
5. Provide more information about the facility issue in the 'Facility Issue Details' field.
6. Enter your name and contact information in the 'Member's Name' and 'Member's Contact Info' fields.
7. If applicable, enter the date and time of the event in the 'Date of Event' and 'Time of Event' fields.
8. If you are reporting an issue with a facility, enter the address and location of the facility in the 'Facility Address' and 'Facility Location' fields.
9. Choose the staff member who is employed at the facility in the 'Staff Employed At' field.
10. If you are reporting an issue with a staff member, enter their name in the 'Reporting Staff Member' field.
11. Provide more information about the facility issue and misconduct in the 'Facility Issue Description' and 'Misconduct Description' fields.
12. Propose a resolution in the 'Resolution' field.
13. Provide your next steps in the 'Next Steps' field.
14. Enter the date of the resolution in the 'Date of Resolution' field.
15. Enter the action taken by the staff in the 'Action Taken' field.
16. Enter your comments in the 'Member Comments' field.
17. Enter the staff's comments in the 'Staff Comments' field.
