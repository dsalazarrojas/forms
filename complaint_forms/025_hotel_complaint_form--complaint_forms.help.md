# Hotel Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help guests report any issues they experienced during their stay at the hotel. Please fill out this form to inform the hotel management about any problems you encountered, and we will review and respond to your complaint as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your guest name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Enter the room number where you were staying.
3. Describe the issue that you want to report in the "Complain description" field.
4. If you want to be contacted by our team, please provide your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name (text)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or reservation confirmation.
* **Room Number (number)**: Enter the room number where you were staying.
* **Complain description (text)**: Describe the issue you experienced during your stay. Please be as detailed as possible so we can understand your complaint.
* **Email (email)**: If you want to be contacted by our team, please provide your email address. This will help us to respond to your complaint directly and keep you updated on our progress.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the issue you experienced.
* Please note that all complaints will be reviewed and responded to in a timely manner.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
