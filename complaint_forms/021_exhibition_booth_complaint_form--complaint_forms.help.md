# Exhibition Booth Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibition Booth Complaint Form is designed to allow attendees, exhibitors, or staff to report any issues or concerns related to the exhibition or event. This form serves as a channel for reporting incidents, defects, or problems that need attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the type of complaint you are reporting (e.g., Incident, Defect, Problem).
3. Describe the issue or concern in detail, providing as much information as possible.
4. Provide the date and time the issue occurred.
5. If applicable, attach a photo or additional proof related to the complaint.
6. Sign and submit the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, optional): This field is where you provide your name, contact details, and any other relevant information that will help us contact you to discuss your complaint.
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose from the options to specify the type of complaint you are reporting (e.g., Incident, Defect, Problem).
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Describe the issue or concern in as much detail as possible, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, `date`, optional): Provide the date and time the issue occurred.
* **Photo Proof** (`photo_proof`, `text`, optional): If applicable, attach a photo or screenshot that demonstrates the issue or concern.
* **Additional Proof** (`additional_proof`, `text`, optional): If additional information is required to support your complaint, you can provide it in this field.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, optional): Sign and submit the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click to submit the form.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, optional): Click to cancel the form without submitting it.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not already reported the same issue or concern before filling out this form.
* If you are unsure, feel free to contact the exhibition staff or management for clarity.
* If you need to submit multiple complaints, you can fill out this form multiple times, but please be aware that duplicate reports may be ignored.
* If you are reporting a critical or urgent issue, please contact the exhibition staff or management directly for prompt attention.
