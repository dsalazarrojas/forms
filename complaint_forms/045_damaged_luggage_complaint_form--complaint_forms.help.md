# Damaged Luggage Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to be used to report any damages or issues encountered during your travel. By submitting this form, you will provide valuable feedback to help us improve our services and prevent similar issues from happening in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the date of your travel.
3. Describe your damaged luggage in the 'Describe Luggage' field.
4. Check if the airline was involved in the issue, and select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
5. Identify the contact person for the incident, and select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
6. Provide a detailed description of the issue you experienced.
7. Once you have completed all the fields, click on "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date**: Please select the date you traveled. This will help us track the complaint over time.
* **Describe Luggage**: Briefly describe the condition of your luggage before and after the issue occurred. This will help us understand the extent of the damage.
* **Report Airline**: Check 'Yes' if the airline staff were involved in the incident. This could include flight attendants, ground crew, or check-in staff.
* **Contact Person**: Check 'Yes' if you were able to contact a person related to the incident, such as a supervisor or manager.
* **Describe Issue**: Explain the issue you experienced with your luggage in as much detail as possible. Include any relevant photos or videos if available.
