# Global Shipping Program Negative Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any negative issues that have occurred during the global shipping program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Shipping Issue Form** (`shipping_issue_form`, `text`, optional)
This is the first page of the form, where you can report any shipping issues that have occurred.

### **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional)
Please provide any relevant customer information, such as their name, address, and contact details.

### **Shipping Issue** (`shipping_issue`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose 'Yes' if the shipping issue has occurred, 'No' otherwise.

### **Product Details** (`product_details`, `text`, optional)
Provide any product details related to the shipping issue, such as the product name and model number.

### **Date** (`shipping_date`, `date`, optional)
Please select the date when the shipping issue occurred.

### **Time** (`shipping_time`, `time`, optional)
Select the time when the shipping issue occurred.

### **Shipping Issue Type** (`shipping_issue_type`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose the type of shipping issue that occurred, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### **Shipping Issue Description** (`shipping_issue_description`, `text`, optional)
Describe the shipping issue in detail.

### **Product Image** (`product_image`, `text`, optional)
Attach any relevant product images related to the shipping issue.

### **Shipping Issue Attachment** (`shipping_issue_attachment`, `text`, optional)
Attach any relevant shipping issue attachments.

### **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, `text`, optional)
Provide any additional comments from the customer.

### **Customer Expected Resolution** (`customer_expected_resolution`, `text`, optional)
Describe the customer's expected resolution for the shipping issue.

### **Resolution Action** (`resolution_action`, `text`, optional)
Describe the action taken to resolve the shipping issue.

### **Resolution Outcome** (`resolution_outcome`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose 'Yes' if the resolution was successful, 'No' otherwise.

### **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`, `date`, optional)
Select the date when the resolution was completed.

### **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time`, `time`, optional)
Select the time when the resolution was completed.

### **Issue Resolution Manager Comments** (`issue_resolution_manager_comments`, `text`, optional)
Provide any additional comments from the issue resolution manager.

### **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Choose any follow-up actions that were taken, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional)
Select the date for follow-up actions.

Note: This form is subject to changes based on business requirements and feedback. Please review the form before submission for any updates.
