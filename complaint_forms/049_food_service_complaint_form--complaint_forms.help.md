# Food Service Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Service Complaint Form is designed for customers to report their complaints or issues with the food service provided by a restaurant or food establishment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and fill out any required information.
2. Enter your customer information, including your contact number and email address (if provided).
3. Describe your food service complaint or issue in the text area provided.
4. If you have any additional information or suggestions for improvement, enter them in the note field.
5. Review the submission confirmation options and select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, or any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Food Service Experience** (`food_service_experience`, text, optional): Describe your recent visit to the food service establishment, including the date, time, and any details about your experience.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, text, optional): Describe your complaint or issue with the food service provided.
* **Food Service Complaint** (`food_service_complaint`, text, optional): If you have any specific complaints or issues with the food service, enter them here.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them here.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you to follow up on your complaint.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you to follow up on your complaint.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Confirm Submission 1** (`confirm_submit`, select one, optional): Confirm that you want to submit this complaint.
* **Confirm Submission 2** (`confirm_submit_2`, select one, optional): Confirm that you agree with the submission.
* **Confirm Submission 3** (`confirm_submit_3`, select one, optional): Confirm that you are certain about your submission.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your complaint or issue.
* If you have any supporting documents or photos, please attach them to your submission.
* We will review and respond to your complaint as soon as possible.
