# Sound Quality Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound Quality Complaint Form is used to document and track complaints related to sound quality issues within the organization. This form helps gather information about the complaint, including category, description, and details of the issue. It also captures information about the reporter, location, and status of the complaint.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form fields as needed.
2. Select the category of the complaint.
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue.
4. Identify the sound problem type (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
5. Provide any additional details about the sound problem.
6. Specify the location where the issue occurred.
7. Enter the date and time when the issue occurred.
8. Specify who reported the issue.
9. Enter the name of the person who reported the issue.
10. Select the status of the issue (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
11. Specify whether the issue has been assigned to someone ("Yes" or "No").
12. Enter any resolution or resolution details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sound Quality Complaint** (`sound_quality_complaint_form`): Enter a brief description of the complaint, if needed.
* **Category** (`category`): Select the category of the complaint (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a detailed description of the issue.
* **Sound Problem Type** (`sound_problem_type`): Select the type of sound problem (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Sound Problem Details** (`sound_problem_details`): Enter any additional details about the sound problem.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the issue occurred.
* **Date Occurred** (`date_occurred`): Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Time Occurred** (`time_occurred`): Enter the time when the issue occurred.
* **Reporter** (`reporter`): Enter your name as the reporter.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`): Enter the name of the person who reported the issue.
* **Status** (`status`): Select the status of the issue (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Select whether the issue has been assigned to someone ("Yes" or "No").
* **Resolution** (`resolution`): Enter any resolution or details about the resolution.
