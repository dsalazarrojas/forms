# Overbooking Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Overbooking Complaint Form is used to report issues with overbooked services or products. It is an essential tool for customer service to resolve these issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name: This is the name of the person submitting the complaint.
2. Select the overbooking date: This is the date on which the overbooking occurred.
3. Enter customer service number: This is a unique identifier for the customer's service or product.
4. Describe the issue: Please provide a detailed description of the overbooking issue.
5. Attach relevant documents: You can attach any documents that support your complaint.
6. Select the status: Choose whether the issue is Active, Resolved, or Pending.
7. Add comments: If you have any additional comments or information to share with customer service.
8. Enter submission date and time: This is the date and time at which you submitted the form.
9. Enter submission location: This is the location where you submitted the form.
10. Enter company information: This is the name of your company and department responsible for the issue.
11. Enter floor information: If the issue occurred on a specific floor, enter the floor number and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter your name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name so that customer service can contact you.
* **Overbooking date** (`overbooking_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date on which the overbooking occurred.
* **Customer service number** (`customer_service_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier for the customer's service or product.
* **Issue description** (`issue_description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, `note`, required/optional): Attach any documents that support your complaint.
* **Status** (`issue_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether the issue is Active, Resolved, or Pending.
* **Submitter name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Submitter email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter address** (`submitter_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your work address.
* **Submitter note** (`submitter_note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the issue.
* **Submitter attachment** (`submitter_attachment`, `note`, required/optional): Attach any additional documents or files.
* **Submitter comments** (`submitter_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about the issue.
* **Submitter date** (`submitter_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date at which you submitted the form.
* **Submitter time** (`submitter_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time at which you submitted the form.
* **Submitter location** (`submitter_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where you submitted the form.
* **Company** (`submitter_company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Department** (`submitter_department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department responsible for the issue.
* **Floor** (`submitter_floor`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the floor number.
* **Floor name** (`submitter_floor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the floor.

Note: Fields marked as `required/optional` are not strictly required but are necessary for accurate processing and resolution of the issue.
