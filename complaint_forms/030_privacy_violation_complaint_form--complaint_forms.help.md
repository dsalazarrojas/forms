# Privacy Violation Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Privacy Violation Complaint Form is used to report incidents of privacy violations that have occurred on our platform. Please use this form to submit a detailed report of the incident and any supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the complainant's name, email, and phone number to provide contact information for follow-up communication.
2. Enter the incident date and time in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields, respectively.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Violation Detail" field.
4. Enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
5. In the "Complaint Description" field, explain what happened and how it affected you.
6. If relevant, attach any supporting documents or evidence that support your complaint.
7. Click the "Submit" button to submit your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complainant Name**: Enter the name of the person who experienced the privacy violation.
* **Complainant Email**: Enter the email address of the person who experienced the privacy violation. This will be used for follow-up communication.
* **Complainant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who experienced the privacy violation.
* **Violation Detail**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible. What happened, where did it happen, and what was the impact on you?
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Complaint Description**: Explain how the privacy violation affected you. How did it make you feel, and what did you experience during or after the incident?
* **Supporting Documents**: If you have any supporting evidence or documentation that can help us investigate, please attach it here.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your complaint.
