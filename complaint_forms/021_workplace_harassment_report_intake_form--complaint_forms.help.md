# Workplace Harassment Report Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents of workplace harassment that you have experienced or witnessed. Please fill out this form to report any incidents that you feel comfortable sharing with HR or management. Your report will help us to investigate and address the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to select the type of incident you experienced, provide details about the incident, and submit your report. Please fill out the form in as much detail as possible, and remember to include your name, contact information, and a brief description of what happened.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type** (select_one): Select the type of incident you experienced (e.g. Harassment, Bullying, Other).
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the incident, including month and year (e.g. January 2022).
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the incident (optional).
* **Incident Location** (text): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (note): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Witness Name** (text): If you have a witness, enter their name.
* **Relationship to Witness** (text): Describe your relationship to the witness (e.g. colleague, supervisor, etc.).
* **Witness Name 2** (text): If there is a second witness, enter their name.
* **Relationship to Witness 2** (text): Describe your relationship to the second witness (e.g. colleague, supervisor, etc.).
* **Witness Phone** (text): If you have a phone number for the witness, enter it.
* **Witness Email** (email): If you have an email address for the witness, enter it.
* **Reporter Name** (text): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (text): Enter your job title.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Department** (text): Enter your department.
* **Incident Reported To** (text): Enter the person or department to whom the incident was reported.
* **Incident Description** (note): Provide a brief description of what happened.
* **Resolution** (note): Enter any resolution or outcome of the incident.
* **Reporter Notes** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Reporter Signature** (note): Add a signature to confirm that you are the reporter.
* **Witness Signature** (note): If you are not the witness, add a signature from the witness to confirm their identity.
* **Submit** (text): Click to submit your report.
* **Cancel** (text): Click to cancel and exit the form.

Please note that you are not required to fill out every field, but the more information you provide, the more helpful it will be for us to investigate and address the incident. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to HR or management for support.
