# Pet Product Recall Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about pet product recalls from pet owners or caregivers. It helps us to investigate and address any issues related to pet products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Provide your contact information (name, email, address, phone number).
* Share details about your pet (type, product recall status, recall category, and description).
* Add any relevant images or details about the product recall.
* Specify the veterinary clinic where you took your pet for treatment (if applicable).
* Confirm if you have submitted a recall previously.
* Select the correct date and time of the recall.
* Provide any additional notes about the recall.
* Enter the name and contact information of the veterinarian who treated your pet (if applicable).
* Review and submit the form.
