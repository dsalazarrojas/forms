# Food Pest Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Pest Complaint Form is designed to gather information from customers who have experienced issues with food establishments. This form will help us identify potential issues and take corrective action to improve customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your details.
2. Describe the incident in the complaint details section.
3. Select the food establishment where the incident occurred.
4. Specify the location of the incident.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, **required**): Please provide your name, address, and contact information to help us contact you for further assistance.
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, `text`, **required**): Describe what happened, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, **required**): Please enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Food Establishment** (`food_establishment`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the food establishment where the incident occurred from the list.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all locations where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `note`, **required**): Describe what happened, including any details that may help us investigate the incident.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `date`, **required**): Please sign and date this section to confirm that you have completed the form.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, `date`, **required**): A staff member will sign and date this section once they have investigated the incident.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
* Ensure that you have completed all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Please note that this form is intended for customers who have experienced issues with food establishments, not for general customer feedback or comments.
