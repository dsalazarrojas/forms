# HR Complaint Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Complaint Intake Form is used to collect information about a complaint made by an employee or a third-party individual. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured and reviewed by the HR department before further action is taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the complainant's name in the "Complainant Name" field.
2. Enter the contact number of the complainant in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Select the department related to the complaint from the "Department" field.
4. Enter the date of the complaint in the "Complaint Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Complainant Name (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the complainant.

*   This field is used to record the name of the person filing the complaint.
*   The name should be written in the same format as a standard name (e.g., "John Doe" or "Jane Smith").
*   This field is not required, but it is recommended that you complete it for identification purposes.

### Contact Number (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the complainant.

*   This field is used to record the contact number of the person filing the complaint.
*   The number should be in the format of a standard phone number (e.g., "123-456-7890" or "123.456.7890").
*   This field is not required, but it is recommended that you complete it for contact purposes.

### Department (, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the department related to the complaint.

*   This field is used to categorize the complaint by department.
*   Please select all departments that are related to the complaint (e.g., "Yes" for all departments).
*   This field is not required, but it helps the HR department to prioritize and direct the complaint to the correct department.

### Complaint Date (, date, required: false)
Enter the date of the complaint.

*   This field is used to record the date when the complaint was made.
*   Please enter the date in the standard format (e.g., "YYYY-MM-DD" or "MM-DD-YYYY").
*   This field is not required, but it helps the HR department to track the timeline of the complaint.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete all required fields as much as possible to ensure that the HR department receives accurate information.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask the HR department for assistance.
*   Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully to help the HR department to handle the complaint efficiently.
