# Trash Pickup Delay Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for residents of the community who experience a delay in their trash pickup. It is used to collect information and provide a way for residents to report issues with trash collection schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the form with your complaint details. If you're unsure of any information, please leave it blank and we'll do our best to assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Your Complaint**: In this section, provide a brief summary of your complaint.
* **Describe Your Situation**: Please provide a detailed description of the circumstances surrounding your trash pickup delay.
* **Property Manager**: Select the name of your property manager from the list.
* **Delay Duration**: Choose how long the trash has not been collected from the options provided.
* **Contact Information**: If you want to be contacted for follow-up or clarification, provide your contact information.

## Tips
* Please note that all fields are optional, but providing as much detail as possible will help us assist you more efficiently.
* If you are unsure of any information, please leave it blank and we'll do our best to help you.
* For questions or concerns not addressed by this form, please contact us directly.

This form is designed to gather the necessary information to help us understand and resolve your issue.
