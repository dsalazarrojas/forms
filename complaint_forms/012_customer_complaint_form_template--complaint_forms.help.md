# Customer Complaint Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who want to report a complaint or issue with our product or service. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to help us address their concerns efficiently and resolve the complaint or issue as quickly as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and contact information on pages 1 and 2.
2. Describe your complaint or issue in the complaint details field on page 3.
3. Select the type of issue you are experiencing on page 4.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted on page 5.
5. Add any attachments or comments on pages 6-7.
6. Select the status of your issue on page 8.
7. Set the priority of your issue on page 9.
8. Add any tags or labels on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1: What is your name**
	+ This is the field where you can enter your name.
* **Page 2: Customer name**
	+ This is the field where you can enter the name of the customer who is reporting the issue.
* **Page 3: contact information**
	+ This is the field where you can enter any additional contact information, such as email or phone number, if you'd like.
* **Page 3: complaint details**
	+ This is the field where you can describe your complaint or issue in as much detail as possible.
* **Page 4: issue type**
	+ Select the type of issue you are experiencing (e.g., Customer Support, Billing, Technical Support).
* **Page 5: contact preferences**
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, Message).
* **Page 6: description**
	+ This is the field where you can add any additional comments or descriptions about your complaint or issue.
* **Page 6: complaint category**
	+ Select the category of your complaint or issue (e.g., Product, Service, Other).
* **Page 6: complaint date**
	+ Enter the date you are experiencing the issue.
* **Page 6: complaint time**
	+ Enter the time you are experiencing the issue.
* **Page 7: complaint description**
	+ This is the field where you can describe your complaint or issue in more detail.
* **Page 7: attachments**
	+ If you have any attachments, such as screenshots or images, you can upload them here.
* **Page 7: comments**
	+ This is the field where you can add any additional comments or descriptions about your complaint or issue.
* **Page 8: resolution**
	+ Select the status of your issue (e.g., Pending, Resolved, In Progress).
* **Page 8: escalation**
	+ Indicate if your issue requires escalation (e.g., True, False).
* **Page 8: priority**
	+ Select the priority of your issue (e.g., Critical, Medium, Low).
* **Page 9: tags**
	+ Add any tags or labels to your issue (e.g., Critical, Important, Non-Critical, Non-Important).
* **Page 10: complaint type**
	+ Select the type of issue you are experiencing (e.g., Complaint, Issue, Inquiry).
* **Page 10: complaint status**
	+ Select the status of your issue (e.g., Active, Resolved, Pending).
* **Page 10: complaint priority**
	+ Select the priority of your issue (e.g., Critical, Low, Medium, None).
* **Page 11: submitted by**
	+ Select who submitted the issue (e.g., Customer, Staff).
* **Page 12: submitted date**
	+ Enter the date the issue was submitted.
* **Page 13: status date**
	+ Enter the date the issue was updated or resolved.
* **Page 14: resolution date**
	+ Enter the date the issue was resolved.
* **Page 14: resolution time**
	+ Enter the time the issue was resolved.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any attachments, please upload them to help us better understand your issue.
* If you have any additional comments or descriptions, please add them in the comments field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team directly.
