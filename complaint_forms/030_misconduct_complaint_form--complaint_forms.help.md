# Misconduct Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Misconduct Complaint Form is a tool for reporting and tracking incidents of bullying, coercion, or other types of misconduct. It is designed to be used in workplaces or educational institutions to provide a formal and confidential channel for reporting and addressing these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide as much detail as possible about the complaint in the "Complaint Details" field.
2. Enter information about the reportee, including their name and any other relevant details.
3. Select the type of misconduct that occurred (e.g. Bullying, Coercion, or Other).
4. Describe the incident in detail, including the date and location.
5. If applicable, provide information about any witnesses.
6. Select the status of your complaint from the options provided (New, In Progress, Closed, or On Hold).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_form_input`): This field allows you to provide a brief description of the complaint. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Reportee Details** (`reportee_details`): Please provide as much information as possible about the reportee, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **Misconduct Type** (`misconduct_type`): Select the type of misconduct that occurred (e.g. Bullying, Coercion, or Other).
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including any relevant context or circumstances.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Location of Incident** (`location_of_incident`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`): If applicable, provide information about any witnesses, including their names and contact information.
* **Complaint Status** (`complaint_status`): Select the status of your complaint from the options provided (New, In Progress, Closed, or On Hold).

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure that your complaint is thoroughly understood.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
* This form is intended to be used for reporting incidents of misconduct, not for reporting non-work-related issues. If you are unsure about whether to use this form, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
