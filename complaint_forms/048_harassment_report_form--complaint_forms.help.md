# Harassment Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Harassment Report Form is used to report incidents of harassment that have occurred within the company or organization. The form is designed to gather information about the incident and provide a clear and concise way to report such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the reported_by field and fill in your name and title to identify yourself as the person reporting the incident.
2. Enter the incident_date field with the date the incident occurred.
3. Enter the incident_time field with the time the incident occurred.
4. Enter the incident_location field with the location where the incident occurred.
5. In the details field, provide a detailed description of what happened.
6. If there were any witnesses, enter their names in the witnesses_names field. 
7. If you have any contact information for the witnesses, enter it in the witnesses_contacts field.
8. For the reported_to field, enter the department or unit that should be notified about the incident.
9. Select the department field to indicate if the incident is related to a specific department.
10. If you want to follow up on the incident, select the follow_up field.
11. If you have any additional contact information for the department, enter it in the department_contacts field.
12. Finally, if you want to contact the prophets (presumably a team or group of people), enter their contact information in the prophets_contacts field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **reported_by** (`reported_by`, `text`, required): Enter your name and title to identify yourself as the person reporting the incident.
* **incident_date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **incident_time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **incident_location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **details** (`details`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **prophets_names** (`prophets_names`, `select_multiple`, required): List the names of any witnesses who saw or heard the incident.
* **witnesses_contacts** (`witnesses_contacts`, `text`, required): Enter any contact information for the witnesses.
* **reported_to** (`reported_to`, `text`, required): Enter the department or unit that should be notified about the incident.
* **department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the incident is related to a specific department.
* **department_contacts** (`department_contacts`, `text`, required): Enter any contact information for the department.
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Select if you want to follow up on the incident.
* **prophets_contacts** (`prophets_contacts`, `text`, required): Enter any contact information for the prophets.
