# Library Services Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Services Complaint Form is designed to gather feedback from patrons about their experiences with the library services. This form helps the library identify areas for improvement and ensures that issues are addressed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the complaint form.
2. Fill out the Patron Feedback field with a brief description of your experience.
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the Complaint Description field.
4. Select the category of the issue from the Category dropdown menu.
5. Choose the department that is best equipped to handle your issue from the Department dropdown menu.
6. If you have a patron email address, please enter it in the Patron Email field.
7. If you have a patron phone number, please enter it in the Patron Phone field.
8. If you have a library staff email address, please enter it in the Library Staff Email field.
9. Enter the date you experienced the issue in the Date field.
10. Select the current status of the issue from the Status dropdown menu.
11. If you would like to add any additional comments or resolution to the issue, please enter it in the Resolution field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patron Feedback** (`patron_feedback`, text, optional): A brief description of your experience with the library services.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, text, optional): A detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Category** (`issue_category`, select_one, optional): The category of the issue, such as Complaint Forms, Administrative Forms, or Other.
* **Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): A description of the issue.
* **Department** (`complaint_department`, select_one, optional): The department best equipped to handle your issue, such as Circulation, Reference, Collection Development, Administration, Technical, or Other.
* **Date** (`complaint_date`, date, optional): The date you experienced the issue.
* **Patron Email** (`patron_contact_email`, email, optional): Your patron email address.
* **Patron Phone** (`patron_phone`, text, optional): Your patron phone number.
* **Library Staff Email** (`library_staff_email`, email, optional): A library staff email address who can assist with your issue.
* **Library Phone** (`library_phone`, text, optional): The library phone number.
* **Status** (`issue_status`, select_one, optional): The current status of the issue, such as Open, Pending, or Closed.
* **Resolution** (`issue_resolution`, text, optional): Any comments or resolution to the issue.
