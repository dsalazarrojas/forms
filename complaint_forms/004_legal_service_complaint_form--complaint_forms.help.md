# legal_service_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The legal service complaint form is designed to collect information from users who wish to submit a complaint to the legal service team. This form helps to gather details about the complaint, which will be reviewed and addressed by the relevant department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field (optional).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Describe the complaint in the "Description of Complaint" field.
5. Select the relevant complaint category from the "Select Complaint Category" dropdown menu.
6. Confirm whether you have reviewed the form in the "Review" field (dropdown menu).
7. Confirm whether you agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agree to Terms and Conditions" field (dropdown menu).
8. Click the "Submit" button to send the form to the relevant department for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name in this field.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact number if you wish to be contacted regarding your complaint.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **Description of Complaint** (text, required): Describe the issue or problem you are experiencing.
* **Date of Complaint** (date, optional): Enter the date on which the complaint occurred.
* **Time of Complaint** (time, optional): Enter the time at which the complaint occurred.
* **Additional Details** (note, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful in reviewing your complaint.
* **Select Complaint Category** (select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your complaint from the dropdown menu.
* **Submit** (select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **Review** (select_one, optional): Confirm whether you have reviewed the form.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (select_one, required): Confirm whether you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to submit your complaint form.

Please note that all required fields must be completed before submitting the form.
