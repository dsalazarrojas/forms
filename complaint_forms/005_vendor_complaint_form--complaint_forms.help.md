# Vendor Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Complaint Form is a tool for vendors to submit complaints or concerns they have while interacting with our company or services. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vendor Complaint Form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor who is filing the complaint.
2.  Enter the **Vendor Service**: Describe the service or product you interacted with that led to the complaint.
3.  Provide a detailed **Complaint Description**: Explain the reason for the complaint in a clear and concise manner.
4.  Enter the **Complaint Date**: Specify the date on which the issue occurred.
5.  Enter the **Complaint Time**: Enter the time when the issue occurred.
6.  Assign a **Complainant**: Choose the person within our company to whom you would like to direct your complaint (e.g., "chatjimmy", "adminuser", or "defaultuser").
7.  Select the **Complaint Status**: Choose the status of your complaint from the available options (e.g., "New", "In Progress", or "Resolved").
8.  Enter the **Vendor Complaint Number**: This is a unique number assigned to each complaint, which helps us track and manage the issue.
9.  Enter the **Vendor Phone**: Provide your contact phone number to help us contact you for further information or updates.
10. Enter your **Vendor Email**: Provide your email address, which we will use for communication and updates related to your complaint.
11. Enter the **Vendor Note**: If you have any additional information or comments regarding the complaint, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vendor Name (required)**: Enter the name of the vendor who is filing the complaint.
*   **Vendor Service (required)**: Describe the service or product that led to the complaint.
*   **Complaint Description (required)**: Provide a detailed explanation of the complaint.
*   **Complaint Date (required)**: Enter the date on which the issue occurred.
*   **Complaint Time (required)**: Enter the time when the issue occurred.
*   **Assigned To (optional)**: Choose the person within our company to whom you would like to direct your complaint.
*   **Complaint Status (optional)**: Select the status of your complaint from the available options.
*   **Vendor Complaint Number (required)**: This is a unique number assigned to each complaint.
*   **Vendor Phone (optional)**: Provide your contact phone number to help us contact you for further information or updates.
*   **Vendor Email (optional)**: Provide your email address, which we will use for communication and updates related to your complaint.
*   **Vendor Note (optional)**: If you have any additional information or comments regarding the complaint, enter them here.
