# Phone Service Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to submit complaints about their phone service in a structured and organized manner, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and addressed properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Phone Service" option from the drop-down menu.
2. Choose the provider of the phone service that you are complaining about.
3. Describe your complaint in the "Complaint" field.
4. Provide the date and time of the complaint.
5. If applicable, provide a contact number and contact email for follow-up.
6. You can also upload an attachment to support your complaint, if necessary.
7. Finally, submit the form to send the complaint to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Phone Service (<name>)** (`text`, required): This is the main field where you describe the phone service you are complaining about.
* **Provider (<name>)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the provider of the phone service you are complaining about.
* **Complaint (<name>)** (`text`, optional): Describe your complaint about the phone service.
* **Date of Complaint (<name>)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the complaint.
* **Time of Complaint (<name>)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the complaint.
* **Contact Number (<name>)** (`select_one`, optional): If you have a contact number for the provider, please enter it here.
* **Contact Email (<name>)** (`select_multiple`, optional): If you have a contact email for the provider, select "Yes" to indicate that it is available.
* **Description (<name>)** (`text`, optional): Add any additional information or context about your complaint.
* **Attachment (<name>)** (`note`, optional): If you have any supporting files or documents to attach to your complaint, upload them here.
* **Submit (<name>)** (`text`, optional): Click the "Submit" button to send your complaint to our team.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" are not required but are included for your convenience and to provide as much information as possible about your complaint.
