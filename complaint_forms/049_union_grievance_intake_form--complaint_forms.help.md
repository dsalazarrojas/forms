# Union Grievance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from union members regarding their grievances. It is intended to gather relevant details to help in resolving the issues in a fair and timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of grievance you are reporting (e.g. Union Matter, Personal Matter, or Other).
2. Provide your contact information (name, email, phone number).
3. Describe the grievance in as much detail as possible.
4. Select the location and supervisor involved.
5. Record the date and time the grievance occurred.
6. Describe the location of the grievance.
7. Provide a description of the grievance.
8. Suggest any resolution or next steps.
9. Attach any evidence related to the grievance.
10. Sign the form to confirm that you have completed it accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grievance Type** (grievance_type): Select the type of grievance you are reporting (e.g. Union Matter, Personal Matter, or Other).
* **Grievant Name** (grievant_name): Enter your name.
* **Grievant Email** (grievant_email): Enter your email address.
* **Grievant Phone** (grievant_phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Grievance Details** (grievance_details): Provide more details about the grievance.
* **Grievant Worksites** (grievant_worksite): Select the location where the grievance occurred.
* **Grievant Supervisor** (grievant_supervisor): Select the supervisor involved.
* **Grievance Date** (grievance_date): Record the date the grievance occurred.
* **Grievant Time** (grievant_time): Record the time the grievance occurred.
* **Grievant Location** (grievant_location): Describe the location of the grievance.
* **Grievant Description** (grievant_description): Provide a description of the grievance.
* **Grievant Resolution** (grievant_resolution): Enter the resolution of the grievance.
* **Grievant Attachment** (grievant_attachment): Attach any evidence related to the grievance.
* **Grievant Suggestion** (grievant_suggestion): Suggest any resolution or next steps.
* **Grievant Comments** (grievant_comments): Provide any additional comments.
* **Grievant Signature** (grievant_signature): Sign the form to confirm that you have completed it accurately.
* **Grievant Date Signed** (grievant_date_signed): Record the date you signed the form.
* **Grievant Time Signed** (grievant_time_signed): Record the time you signed the form.
* **Grievant Agreed** (grievant_agreed): Confirm that you agree with the terms of the form.
* **Grievant Remarks** (grievant_remarks): Enter any additional remarks.
* **Grievant Other Comments 2** (grievant_other_comments_2): Enter any additional comments.
* **Grievant Other Comments 3** (grievant_other_comments_3): Enter any additional comments.
* **Grievant Other Comments 4** (grievant_other_comments_4): Enter any additional comments.
* **Grievant Other Comments 5** (grievant_other_comments_5): Enter any additional comments.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide all the required information to help in resolving the grievance efficiently. Also, make sure to attach any evidence related to the grievance to support your claim.
