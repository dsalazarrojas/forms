# Paternity Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about paternity-related complaints, which will be reviewed and addressed by relevant authorities or management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Use the "Complaint Details" field to describe the issue or concern you are experiencing.
3. If you are uploading a supporting document, please make sure it is relevant to your complaint.
4. Enter your information and that of the submitter in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant identification information.
* **Complainant Name** (`complainant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the complainant.
* **Complainant Contact Details** (`complainant_contact_details`, email, optional): Enter your email address or other contact information.
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, optional): Describe the issue or concern you are experiencing.
* **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document`, text, optional): If you have any relevant documents to support your complaint, upload them here.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself as the submitter.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Contact Details** (`submitter_contact_details`, email, optional): Enter your email address or other contact information as the submitter.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, text, optional): Enter your position or role as the submitter.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team you belong to as the submitter.
* **Upload File** (`file_upload`, text, optional): Upload any relevant supporting documents or files.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Make sure to keep a record of your submission for future reference.
