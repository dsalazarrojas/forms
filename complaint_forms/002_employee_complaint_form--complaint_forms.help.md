# employee_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee complaint form is a tool for reporting incidents or grievances involving colleagues or supervisors. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to help the organization address and resolve issues promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the type of complaint you are reporting (e.g., Employee vs Employee, Employee vs Supervisor, or Employee vs Senior Manager).
2. Provide the name of the employee involved in the complaint (if known).
3. Enter the title of the reported employee (if known).
4. Identify the supervisor or team lead of the reported employee (if known).
5. Describe the details of the incident or complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Type** (<code>select_one, options: Employee vs Employee, Employee vs Supervisor, Employee vs Senior Manager): Select the type of complaint you are reporting. This will help the organization understand the nature of the issue.
* **Reported Employee** (<code>text): Provide the name of the employee who is involved in the complaint (if known).
* **Reported Employee Title** (<code>text): If possible, enter the reported employee's title or position (if known).
* **Reported Employee Supervisor** (<code>select_one, options: Supervisor, Team Lead): Identify the supervisor or team lead of the reported employee (if known).
* **Complaint Details** (<code>text): Describe the incident or grievance in detail, providing as much information as possible.
