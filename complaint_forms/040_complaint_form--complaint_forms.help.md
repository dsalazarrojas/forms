# Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Complaint Form is a tool for submitting formal complaints to our organization. It provides a structured way to gather detailed information about the incident, ensuring that we can effectively address your concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information. Please take your time to provide as much detail as possible.
2. Select the category of complaint that best describes the issue you're experiencing.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident, if applicable.
4. Provide a detailed description of what happened.
5. Share any previous attempts to resolve the issue and what was the outcome.
6. Specify your desired outcome or remedy.
7. Confirm that the information provided is true and accurate.
8. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complainant Full Name (Complainant Full Name)** (`complainant_full_name`, required): Please enter the full name of the person filing the complaint.
* **Contact Email (complainant_email_address)** (`complainant_email_address`, required): Enter the email address where you want to receive updates regarding the complaint.
* **Phone Number (complainant_phone_number)** (`complainant_phone_number`, optional): Provide the best number for contact, if you want to be reached via phone.
* **Category of Complaint (complaint_category_formal)** (`complaint_category_formal`, required): Select the primary area of concern from the provided options.
* **Date of Incident (complaint_incident_date_formal)** (`complaint_incident_date_formal`, required): Enter the date when the event occurred.
* **Approximate Time of Incident (complaint_incident_time)** (`complaint_incident_time`, optional): If applicable, provide the approximate time of the incident.
* **Location of Incident (complaint_location_formal)** (`complaint_location_formal`, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred, e.g., Store Branch, Online Platform, Specific Office.
* **Detailed Description of the Complaint (full_complaint_narrative)** (`full_complaint_narrative`, required): Provide a full narrative of the events, including any relevant details.
* **Previous Attempts to Resolve (previous_resolution_attempts_formal)** (`previous_resolution_attempts_formal`, optional): Describe any previous attempts to resolve the issue and what was the outcome.
* **Desired Outcome or Remedy (desired_outcome_formal)** (`desired_outcome_formal`, required): Specify your desired outcome or remedy, e.g., Refund, Apology, Change in policy.
* **Verification Acknowledgment (verification_acknowledgment_formal)** (`verification_acknowledgment_formal`, required): Confirm that the information provided is true and accurate.
* **Date of Submission (complaint_submission_date_formal)** (`complaint_submission_date_formal`, required): Enter the current date to confirm submission.

Please note that some fields may not be applicable in all cases, but they are all required to be filled out when they are applicable. If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our customer service department for assistance.
