# Discrimination Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discrimination Complaint Form is a tool to report and document incidents of discrimination that have occurred within the organization or institution. This form helps to gather information about the incident, identify the parties involved, and document the category of discrimination that occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
2. Enter the Complainant's name in the "Complainant's Name" field.
3. Enter the Respondent's name in the "Respondent's Name" field.
4. If applicable, enter the date of the incident in the "Date of Incident" field.
5. If you are the Complainant, please sign and date the form in the "Complainant Signature" field. If you are not the Complainant, please ensure they sign and date the form in the "Proctor's Signature" field.
6. If applicable, enter the names of any witnesses in the "Witness Names" field.
7. Provide a description of the incident in the "Description of Incident" field.
8. If applicable, enter the Proctor's signature and date in the "Proctor's Signature" and "Date of Proctors Signature" fields.
9. Select all applicable categories of discrimination that occurred in the "Category of Discrimination" field.
10. If other categories of discrimination were involved, please specify in the "Other Category of Discrimination" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details (Text)**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including what happened, where it happened, and any relevant details.
* **Complainant's Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the person experiencing the discrimination.
* **Respondent's Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the person or entity involved in the incident.
* **Date of Incident (Date)**: Enter the date the incident occurred, if known.
* **Complainant Signature (Text)**: If you are the Complainant, please sign and date the form to confirm your identity.
* **Witness Names (Text)**: Enter the names of any witnesses who may have observed the incident.
* **Description of Incident (Note)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant details.
* **Proctor's Signature (Text)**: If you are not the Complainant, please ensure the Proctor signs and dates the form to confirm their identity.
* **Date of Proctors Signature (Date)**: Enter the date the Proctor signed the form.
* **Category of Discrimination (Select Multiple)**: Select all applicable categories of discrimination that occurred, such as "Race", "Religion", "Sex", "Disability", "Age", "National Origin", or "Other" (specify below).
* **Other Category of Discrimination (Text)**: If other categories of discrimination were involved, please specify them in this field.

## Tips
- Please ensure you are honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can skip it or seek guidance from a supervisor or HR representative.
- Remember to sign and date the form if you are the Complainant or Proctor.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
