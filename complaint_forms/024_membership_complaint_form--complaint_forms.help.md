# Membership Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Complaint Form is designed to gather feedback from members on their experiences with the membership program. This form will help the organization understand the areas of improvement needed to better serve its members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Membership Complaint Form on the designated page.
2. Please ensure you have completed the required fields (none).
3. Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or None) in the contact information section.
4. Briefly describe your complaint in the complaint details section.
5. Click the "submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Membership Complaint Form
* **Membership Complaint Form** (`text`, optional): This section is where you can provide any feedback or comments regarding your membership experience. Please be concise and clear in your explanation.

### Page 2 - Complaint Details
* **Complaint Details** (`text`, optional): In this section, you can describe your complaint in as much detail as possible. This will help us better understand the issue and make necessary improvements.

### Page 3 - Form Page 3
* **Form Page 3** (`text`, optional): This section is currently empty, please do not complete it.

### Page 4 - Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`select_one`, optional): Select your preferred method of contact:
	+ Phone: We will contact you via phone to discuss your complaint.
	+ Email: We will contact you via email to discuss your complaint.
	+ None: You do not want to be contacted.

### Page 5 - Complaint Details
* **Complaint Details** (`text`, optional): In this section, you can describe your complaint in as much detail as possible. This will help us better understand the issue and make necessary improvements.

### Page 6 - Form Page 6
* **Form Page 6** (`text`, optional): This section is currently empty, please do not complete it.

### Page 7 - Submit Button
* **Submit Button** (`text`, optional): This is the final step. Please click the "submit" button to send your complaint to us.
