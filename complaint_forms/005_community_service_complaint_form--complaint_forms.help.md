# Community Service Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Community Service Complaint Form" is a tool for residents to report complaints about community services. This form helps us identify and address issues, ensuring that our services are delivered effectively and residents' needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your details, including your full name and contact email and phone number.
2.  Select the preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Mail) to get in touch with you.
3.  Choose the type of service you are complaining about (Waste Management, Parks and Recreation, Road Maintenance, Public Library, Social Services, or Other).
4.  If you are complaining about "Other," provide the name of the service.
5.  Report the date and time of the incident.
6.  Specify the location where the incident occurred.
7.  Describe the complaint in detail, including as much information as possible.
8.  Indicate the priority of the complaint (Low, Medium, or High).
9.  If you have previously reported this issue, provide the reference number.
10.  If you have reported this issue before, select "Yes" and provide the reference number.
11.  Decide if you want to submit your complaint anonymously (Yes or No - keep me informed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your full name to help us identify you for follow-up communication.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, **Required**): Provide your contact email address so that we can get in touch with you.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information.
*   **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, **Required**): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail).
*   **Which Service Are You Complaining About?** (`service_type`, select_one, **Required**): Select the type of service you are complaining about (Waste Management, Parks and Recreation, Road Maintenance, Public Library, Social Services, or Other).
*   **Specify Other Service** (`service_other`, text, **Optional**): If you selected "Other," provide the name of the service.
*   **Date of the Incident** (`incident_date`, date, **Required**): Report the date of the incident.
*   **Time of the Incident** (`incident_time`, time, **Optional**): If relevant, provide the time of the incident.
*   **Location of the Incident** (`incident_location`, text, **Required**): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Detailed Description of the Complaint** (`complaint_description`, text, **Required**): Provide a detailed description of the complaint.
*   **Priority of the Complaint** (`complaint_priority`, select_one, **Required**): Indicate the priority of the complaint (Low, Medium, or High).
*   **Have You Reported This Issue Before?** (`previously_reported`, select_one, **Required**): If you have reported this issue before, select "Yes" and provide the reference number.
*   **Previous Report Reference Number** (`previous_report_reference`, text, **Optional**): If you have reported this issue before, provide the reference number.
*   **What Outcome Are You Seeking?** (`desired_outcome`, text, **Required**): Describe the outcome you're looking for to resolve the issue.
*   **Submit Anonymously** (`anonymous_submission`, select_one, **Required**): Choose whether to submit your complaint anonymously (Yes or No - keep me informed).
