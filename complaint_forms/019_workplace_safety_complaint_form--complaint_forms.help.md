# Workplace Safety Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to report safety complaints and issues in the workplace. It helps to identify and track potential safety risks and concerns, allowing the management to take prompt action and provide necessary resolutions.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Complete the "Employee Name", "Employee Email", "Description of Complaint", "Category of Complaint", "Type of Complaint", and "Reported By Department" fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2.  Select the correct "Reported By", "Workplace Location", and "Assigned To" fields from the provided options.
3.  Fill out the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields automatically using the browser's date and time picker.
4.  Choose the correct "Follow-up" status and select the appropriate "Resolved Status" and "Resolved By" if applicable.
5.  Optionally, add additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
6.  Click the "Submit" button to send the form to the designated authorities for review and resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Safety Complaint**: This is the main field where you can describe the safety complaint or issue you have encountered.
* **Employee Name**: This is your name as an employee.
* **Employee Email**: This is your work email address.
* **Description of Complaint**: Please provide a detailed description of the safety complaint or issue.
* **Category of Complaint**: This field categorizes the complaint under General Concerns, Near Miss, or Incident Report.
* **Type of Complaint**: This field classifies the complaint as a Safety Issue, Security Issue, or Other.
* **Reported By**: Choose the employee who reported this complaint from the provided options.
* **Workplace Location**: Select the location where the complaint occurred (e.g., Head Office, Branch Office, Home Office).
* **Reported By Department**: Select the department where the employee who reported this complaint works.
* **Submission Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date.
* **Submission Time**: This field is automatically populated with the current time.
* **Resolved By**: Select the employee or department that resolved the complaint.
* **Resolved Status**: Choose the status of the complaint (e.g., Not Resolved, Resolved).
* **Follow-up**: Choose whether follow-up is required for this complaint.
* **Assigned To**: Select one or more employees or departments that will follow up on this complaint.
* **Assigned Tool**: Optional field for assigning a specific tool to resolve the complaint.
* **Assigned User**: Optional field for assigning a specific user to resolve the complaint.
* **Follow-up Comments**: Optional field for adding additional comments for follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date.
* **Follow-up Time**: This field is automatically populated with the current time.
