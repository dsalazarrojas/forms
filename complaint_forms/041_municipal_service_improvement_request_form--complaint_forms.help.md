# Municipal Service Improvement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form allows citizens to submit feedback and suggestions on how municipal services can be improved. It helps us to identify areas that need improvement and provide better services to the community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields as best you can, and make sure to submit the form.
2. If you have any additional comments or details, you can include them in the "Service Description" field.
3. If you'd like us to follow up with you, select one or more follow-up methods in the "Follow-up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request** (`service_request`, text, optional): Briefly describe the service improvement request you have. This could be a suggestion, a complaint, or a comment on how we can better serve you.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Please provide your name so we can contact you directly.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, select_one, optional): Choose the area where you would like to see the service improvement, e.g. Downtown, Uptown, or both.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, optional): Provide more details about your service improvement request, if desired.
* **Service Priority** (`service_priority`, select_one, optional): If you have a specific priority for this request, please choose High, Medium, or Low.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you'd like us to follow up with you, e.g. Follow-up via Phone, Follow-up via Email, or Follow-up via Chat.
