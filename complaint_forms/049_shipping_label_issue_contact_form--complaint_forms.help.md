# Shipping Label Issue Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report issues with shipping labels. Please provide as much detail as possible to help our team troubleshoot and resolve the problem quickly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" if you're experiencing a shipping label issue.
2. Enter your contact name.
3. Provide your contact email address.
4. Describe the shipping label issue in as much detail as possible.
5. Add any additional customer messages or notes related to the issue.
6. If you'd like to assign a specific user to handle your case, select "Yes" from the "Assigned User" dropdown.
7. If you've selected "Yes" in the previous step, enter the user's name.
8. You can add any further customer notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Label Issue** (`shipping_label_issue_contact_form`): Select "Yes" if you're experiencing a shipping label issue.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your account or the business card associated with your shipment.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address to ensure our team can reach you for further updates.
* **Shipping Label Issue Description** (`shipping_label_issue_description`): Please describe the issue with your shipping label as clearly as possible. This will help our team understand the problem and resolve it quickly.
* **Customer Message 1** (`customer_message`): Add any additional details or notes about the issue.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user_id`): Select "Yes" to assign a specific user to handle your case. Please note that this is not a mandatory field.
* **Assigned User Name** (`assigned_user_name`): If you've selected "Yes" in the previous step, enter the name of the user you'd like to handle your case.
* **Additional Customer Message 2** (`customer_message_2`): You can add any further details or notes about the issue here.
* **Additional Customer Message 3** (`customer_message_3`): This is another opportunity to add any additional details or notes about the issue, if needed.
