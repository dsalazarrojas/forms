# Overcharge Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Overcharge Complaint Form is used to collect complaints about overcharging issues from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a detailed description of the overcharge issue in the "Overcharge Details" field.
2. Provide the total amount charged to the customer in the "Total Charged" field.
3. Enter the expected amount for the service or product in the "Expected Amount" field.
4. Enter the actual amount paid by the customer in the "Actual Amount" field.
5. Calculate and enter the difference between the expected and actual amounts in the "Difference" field.
6. If available, provide the name of the customer service representative handling the case in the "Customer Service Rep" field.
7. Enter any resolution or outcome of the complaint in the "Resolution" field.
8. Finally, provide any additional feedback or comments from the customer in the "Customer Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overcharge Details** (Overcharge Complaint Form, text, required): Enter a clear and concise description of the overcharge issue, including relevant dates and amounts.
* **Total Charged** (Total Charged, number, required): Provide the total amount charged to the customer.
* **Expected Amount** (Expected Amount, number, required): Enter the expected amount for the service or product.
* **Actual Amount** (Actual Amount, number, required): Enter the actual amount paid by the customer.
* **Difference** (Difference, number, required): Calculate and enter the difference between the expected and actual amounts.
* **Customer Service Rep** (Customer Service Rep, text, optional): If available, enter the name of the customer service representative handling the case.
* **Resolution** (Resolution, text, optional): Enter any resolution or outcome of the complaint.
* **Customer Feedback** (Customer Feedback, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments from the customer.
