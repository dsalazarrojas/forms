# Teacher Behavior Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Behavior Complaint Form is designed to allow students, parents, or staff to report incidents or complaints about a teacher's behavior. These reports can help identify patterns or issues that need attention, and provide a formal channel for addressing them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Teacher Behavior Complaint Form**
	* Write a detailed description of the incident, including the teacher's name and any relevant context.
2. **Teacher Name**
	* Enter the name of the teacher involved in the incident.
3. **Reason for Complaint**
	* Provide a brief explanation of why you are making the complaint.
4. **Description of Incident**
	* Give a detailed account of what happened, including dates and times if relevant.
5. **Email Address**
	* Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates or next steps.
6. **Phone Number**
	* Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you urgently.
7. **Date of Incident**
	* Enter the date of the incident (mm/dd/yyyy format).
8. **Time of Incident**
	* Enter the time of the incident (12:00 hour format).
9. **Assigned Tool**
	* Select the tool that was assigned to the teacher during the incident. If unsure, select "other".
10. **Submit**
	* Once you have completed the form, select "Submit" to send the complaint to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Behavior Complaint Form**: This field is the main description of the incident.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher involved in the incident.
* **Reason for Complaint**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are making the complaint.
* **Description of Incident**: Give a detailed account of what happened, including dates and times if relevant.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you urgently.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time of the incident (12:00 hour format).
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that was assigned to the teacher during the incident. If unsure, select "other".
* **Submit**: Once you have completed the form, select "Submit" to send the complaint to our team.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you have reported the incident accurately and fairly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a school administrator or support staff for assistance.
* Remember that all submissions will be treated with confidentiality and respect.
