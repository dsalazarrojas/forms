# Pharmacy Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pharmacy Complaint Form is designed to allow customers to report incidents or issues that occurred at our pharmacy. This form is intended for customers who want to file a complaint, ask a question, or provide feedback about their experience at our pharmacy. Please use this form to provide as much detail as possible to help us understand and address your concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "form_id" field and enter your unique form identifier if required.
2.  Enter the date of the incident in the "incident_date" field. This will help us track the issue more effectively.
3.  In the "incident_description" field, describe the incident that occurred at our pharmacy. Provide as much detail as possible about what happened.
4.  Enter the name of the pharmacy where the incident occurred in the "pharmacy_name" field.
5.  If you have a contact number, please enter it in the "contact_number" field so we can get back to you.
6.  If you have an email address, please enter it in the "email_address" field so we can contact you.
7.  In the "issue_description" field, describe the issue you are experiencing or the problem you want us to know about.
8.  Sign your name on the "signature" field. This is required to confirm that the complaint was submitted by you.
9.  Finally, enter your name in the "submitted_by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_id** (Number): Enter your unique form identifier if required.
* **Incident Date** (Date): Enter the date of the incident that occurred at our pharmacy.
* **Incident Description** (Text): Describe the incident that occurred at our pharmacy. This will help us understand what happened.
* **Pharmacy Name** (Text): Enter the name of the pharmacy where the incident occurred.
* **Contact Number** (Text): If you have a contact number, enter it so we can get back to you.
* **Email Address** (Email): If you have an email address, enter it so we can contact you.
* **Issue Description** (Text): Describe the issue you are experiencing or the problem you want to report.
* **Signature** (Note): Sign your name on this field to confirm that the complaint was submitted by you.
* **Submitted By** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your form submission.
* **Submitted Date** (Date): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Assigned To** (Text): This field will be filled in by our team after reviewing your submission.
