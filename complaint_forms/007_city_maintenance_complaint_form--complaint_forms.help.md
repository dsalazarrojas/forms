# City Maintenance Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Maintenance Complaint Form is a tool that allows citizens to report maintenance issues in the city. This form helps the city's maintenance team to identify and address problems such as potholes, broken streetlights, and graffiti, among others.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in your information, including your name and email address.
3. Select the type of maintenance issue you are reporting.
4. Provide the location of the issue, including the street address or nearest intersection.
5. Describe the issue in detail, including any relevant information about the problem.
6. If you have any photo evidence, please take note that it can be emailed separately to maintenance-city.gov.
7. Indicate the perceived urgency level of the issue.
8. Provide the date when you first noticed the issue.
9. Confirm that you are willing to be contacted for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Report a Maintenance Issue --** (Page 1)
	+ A brief introduction to the form and a call to action to report a maintenance issue.
* **Your Full Name** (`reporter_name`, required): Your name will be used to address the issue and provide updates.
* **Your Email Address** (`reporter_email`, required): Your email address will be used to follow up on the issue and provide updates.
* **Type of Maintenance Issue** (`issue_type`, required): Select the type of maintenance issue you are reporting, such as pothole or road damage, broken streetlight, etc.
* **Location of Issue** (`issue_location`, required): Provide the street address or nearest intersection where the issue is located.
* **Detailed Description** (`issue_description`, required): Describe the issue in detail, including any relevant information about the problem.
* **Please note - Photo evidence can be emailed separately to maintenance-city.gov** (`photo_evidence_note`, optional): If you have any photo evidence, you can email it separately to maintenance-city.gov.
* **Perceived Urgency** (`urgency_level`, required): Indicate the perceived urgency level of the issue, such as emergency, high, medium, or low.
* **When did you first notice this issue?** (`first_reported_date`, required): Provide the date when you first noticed the issue.
* **May we contact you for more information?** (`contact_consent`, required): Confirm that you are willing to be contacted for more information about the issue.
* **A tracking number will be sent to your email after submission** (`tracking_number_note`, optional): A tracking number will be sent to your email after you submit the form.
* **Report Date** (`report_timestamp`, required): The date and time when you submitted the form.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the issue is properly reported.
* If you have any photo evidence, please email it separately to maintenance-city.gov.
* The city's maintenance team will review and address the issue as soon as possible.
* You will receive a tracking number via email after submitting the form, which you can use to track the status of the issue.
