# ticket_pricing_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ticket_pricing_complaint_form is used to collect feedback from customers regarding ticket pricing issues.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the event where the pricing issue occurred.
2.  Enter the ticket price range that you experienced.
3.  Enter the date of the event where the pricing issue occurred.
4.  Enter your name as it will be displayed on the complaint.
5.  Enter your email address.
6.  Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **complaint_form** (required): This is the main text field where you will describe the pricing issue you experienced.
*   **event_ticket** (optional): Enter the ticket number or name if you remember it.
*   **price_range** (optional): Enter the price range you were expecting or actually paid for the ticket.
*   **event_date** (optional): Enter the date of the event where the pricing issue occurred.
*   **attendee_name** (optional): Enter your name to be displayed on the complaint.
*   **email** (optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding this issue.
*   **event_name** (optional): Enter the name of the event where the pricing issue occurred. 

## Tips
*   Please provide as much information as you can in each field to ensure we understand the pricing issue you encountered. 
*   If you have already submitted a complaint for the same issue, please do not resubmit.
