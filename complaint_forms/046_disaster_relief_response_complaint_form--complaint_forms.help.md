# Disaster Relief Response Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents or issues that occurred during or after a disaster relief response. It will be used to help identify patterns, address concerns, and improve future response efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable complaints from the "Yes" options in the **User Input** section.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident or issue in the **description** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **email** field to receive updates on the status of your complaint.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field, if applicable.
5. Select the date and time of the incident or issue from the calendar in the **date** and **time** fields.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident or issue in the **note** field.
7. Choose the category that best describes your complaint from the **what is your complaint about** dropdown menu.
8. Select the person assigned to investigate or handle your complaint from the **assigned to** dropdown menu.
9. Leave the **submitted**, **received**, and **resolved** dates blank, as these fields are automatically populated by our system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`user_input`): Select all applicable complaints from the options provided. This is the initial step in reporting an incident or issue.
*   **description** (`description`): A detailed description of the incident or issue.
*   **email** (`email`): Enter your email address to receive updates on the status of your complaint.
*   **phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number, if applicable, to facilitate communication.
*   **date** (`date`): Select the date of the incident or issue.
*   **time** (`time`): Select the time of the incident or issue.
*   **note** (`note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident or issue.
*   **what is your complaint about** (`complaint_type`): Choose the category that best describes your complaint.
*   **assigned to** (`assigned_to`): Select the person assigned to investigate or handle your complaint.
*   **submitted** (`submitted`): Leave this field blank, as it is automatically populated by our system.
*   **received** (`received`): Leave this field blank, as it is automatically populated by our system.
*   **resolved** (`resolved`): Leave this field blank, as it is automatically populated by our system.
*   **comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident or issue.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out this form.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
*   We appreciate your help in making our disaster relief response efforts more effective and efficient.
