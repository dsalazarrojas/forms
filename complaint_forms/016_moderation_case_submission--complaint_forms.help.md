# moderation_case_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "moderation_case_submission" form is designed for submitting incident reports for moderation review. It helps track and manage incidents, including their priority, status, and follow-up details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide clear and detailed description** of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
2. Select the **priority** of the incident from the dropdown menu (Low, Medium, High).
3. Choose the **status** of the incident from the dropdown menu (In Progress, Completed, Resolved).
4. Enter the **date** and **time** of the incident in the respective fields.
5. Specify the **case manager** and **incident reporter** (optional).
6. Enter the **incident reporter's email** and **phone number** (optional).
7. **Enter any additional follow-up details** in the "Follow-up Details" field (optional).
8. Schedule the **follow-up date** and **next update date** (optional).
9. **Track follow-up status** from the dropdown menu (In Progress, Completed, Resolved).
10. **Mark the last updated date** and **next update date** (optional).
11. Select the **assigned tool** for the case.
12. Enter the **created by** and **updated by** fields (optional).
13. **Enter the form ID**.
14. Finally, ensure that all fields are filled out completely, as marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Details
* **Incident Description**: Please provide a clear and detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Status**: Select the status of the incident (In Progress, Completed, Resolved).
* **Case Priority**: Choose the priority of the incident (Low, Medium, High).
* **Case Manager**: Enter the name of the case manager.
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter the name of the incident reporter (optional).
* **Incident Reporter Email**: Enter the email of the incident reporter (optional).
* **Incident Reporter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the incident reporter (optional).
* **Follow-up Details**: Provide any additional follow-up details (optional).
* **Follow-up Schedule**: Schedule the next follow-up date and time (optional).
* **Follow-up Manager**: Enter the name of the follow-up manager (optional).
* **Follow-up Note**: Enter any follow-up notes (optional).
* **Follow-up Status**: Track the follow-up status (optional).
* **Form ID**: Enter the form ID.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the assigned tool for the case.
* **Created By**: Enter the name of who created the form.
* **Created At**: Enter the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated By**: Enter the name of who updated the form (optional).
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the form was updated (optional).
* **Incident Tags**: Enter keywords separated by commas to categorize the incident.
* **Tags**: Select multiple tags to categorize the incident.
* **Last Updated**: Track the last updated date for the form.
