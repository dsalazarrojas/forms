# Shipping Container Issue Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Container Issue Complaint Form is used to report any issues or problems related to shipping containers. This form helps the company to gather information about the issues and take necessary actions to resolve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information about the shipping container issue you are experiencing. The fields are:
* **Container Issue**: A brief description of the issue with the shipping container.
* **Container Location**: The location where the shipping container is currently at or was located.
* **Description**: A detailed description of the issue.
* **Contact Name**: The name of the contact person or individual responsible for the issue.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person or individual.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person or individual. Please note that this field is optional and only required if the contact email is confirmed.
* **Is Contact Email Confirmed?**: A confirmation checkbox to indicate if the contact email is verified.
* **Issue Category**: Select the category of the issue (Mechanical, Electrical, Environmental, or Other).
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method (Air, Ground, or Both).
* **Shipping Mode**: Select the shipping mode (Regular, Priority, Express, or Both).
* **Shipping Date**: The date when the shipping container was scheduled to be shipped.
* **Shipping Time**: The time when the shipping container was scheduled to be shipped.
* **Issue Date**: The date when the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time**: The time when the issue occurred.
