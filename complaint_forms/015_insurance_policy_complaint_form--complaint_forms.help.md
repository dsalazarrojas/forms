# insurance_policy_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The insurance policy complaint form is used to report policy-related complaints or issues to us. Please fill out this form to submit your concerns and help us understand and address them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please make sure you are reporting a complaint that you have not already reported, and that you have tried to resolve the issue with your policy provider or through other internal channels first.
2. Answer the questions below to provide as much detail as possible about your complaint.
3. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your complaint to us for review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (`insurance_policy_form_complaint_form_1`, Text, Optional)**: This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the purpose of the complaint. Please enter a brief description of the reason you are filing this complaint.
* **Policy Number (`policy_number`, Text, Optional)**: If you know your policy number, please enter it here. This will help us locate your policy more easily.
* **Policy Provider (`policy_provider`, Text, Optional)**: If you know the name of your policy provider, please enter it here.
* **Complaint Description (`complaint_description`, Text, Optional)**: Describe your complaint in as much detail as possible. Please provide any relevant dates, times, or details about what happened.
* **Complaint Type (`complaint_type`, Multi-select, Optional)**: Choose all that apply:
  • Yes
  • No
* **Complaint Status (`complaint_status`, Single-select, Optional)**: Choose one:
  • Active
  • In-active
  • Resolved
  • Closed

## Tips
* Please fill out this form as accurately and completely as possible.
* If you are unsure about what to fill out, please contact us for guidance.
* We will review and respond to your complaint as soon as possible after receiving your submission.
