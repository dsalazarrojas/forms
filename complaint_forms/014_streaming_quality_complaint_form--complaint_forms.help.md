# Streaming Quality Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streaming Quality Complaint Form is designed to collect information about streaming quality issues encountered by users. This form will help identify common problems and aid in improving streaming services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Describe the issue you experienced with the streaming service in the "Streaming Issue Description" field.
2. Select the "Streaming Service Provider" from the drop-down menu.
3. Specify the date the issue occurred using the "Streaming Issue Date" field.
4. Choose the time the issue occurred using the "Streaming Issue Time" field.
5. Select the "Streaming Issue Type" from the drop-down menu.
6. Type "Streamed Video" to describe the video you were streaming at the time of the issue.
7. Finally, enter your details in the "User" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Streaming Issue Description** (`streaming_quality_complaint_form_1`, text, required): Enter a clear and detailed description of the issue you encountered with the streaming service.
* **Streaming Service Provider** (`streaming_quality_complaint_form_2`, select_one, required): Select the company offering the streaming service where you experienced the issue.
* **Streaming Issue Date** (`streaming_quality_complaint_form_3`, date, required): Enter the date you experienced the issue with the streaming service.
* **Streaming Issue Time** (`streaming_quality_complaint_form_4`, time, required): Enter the time you experienced the issue with the streaming service.
* **Streamed Video** (`streaming_quality_complaint_form_5`, text, required): Describe the video you were watching at the time of the issue.
* **Streaming Issue Type** (`streaming_quality_complaint_form_6`, select_one, required): Choose the type of issue you experienced with the streaming service.
* **User** (`streaming_quality_complaint_form_7`, text, required): Enter your details to help us contact you regarding your issue.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing the issue you experienced.
* Make sure to select the correct streaming service provider to help us identify the correct issue.
* Check that the date and time are correct to ensure accuracy.
* Provide a clear and concise description of the streamed video to help us identify the issue.
* Select the correct issue type to help us understand the nature of the problem.
* Enter your details accurately so we can contact you regarding your issue.
