# Quality Control Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Quality Control Complaint Form" is a tool used to report issues or defects with products. If you've experienced a problem with a product you've purchased, please fill out this form to help us identify and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Select the correct "Complaint Type" to indicate the nature of the issue.
- Enter the "Product Name" as it appears on the product packaging or documentation.
- Provide a detailed description of the problem you've experienced.
- Enter the "Date of Purchase" if you purchased the product in question.
- If you have a "Serial Number" for the product, please enter it.
- Attach any relevant "Product Image" or documentation that supports your complaint.
- Enter your "Customer Name" as it appears on the product's packaging or documentation.
- If you have a "Contact Number" where we can reach you, please enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Type**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you've experienced a problem with the product. Select 'No' if you're simply checking in.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you're reporting issues with. This should match the name on the product packaging or documentation.
* **Problem Description**: Provide a clear and detailed description of the issue you've experienced.
* **Date of Purchase**: Enter the date you purchased the product, if applicable.
* **Serial Number**: If you have a serial number for the product, enter it here. This helps us identify the specific item.
* **Product Image**: Attach any relevant images or documentation that support your complaint.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on the product's packaging or documentation.
* **Contact Number**: If you have a phone number where we can contact you, please enter it. This helps us reach you if we need more information or to discuss your complaint.
* **Purchase Date**: This field is not currently being used. Please do not fill it out.
* **Time of Purchase**: This field is not currently being used. Please do not fill it out.
* **Purchase Date**: This field is a duplicate of "Date of Purchase". Please do not fill it out.
* **Product Image**: This field is not required but can be used to attach relevant images or documentation to support your complaint.
* **Complaint Details**: This field can be used to provide additional information about your complaint that doesn't fit in the "Problem Description" field.
