# Electronic Toll Tag Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electronic Toll Tag Complaint Form is designed for customers to report incidents or complaints related to their electronic toll tags. This form collects essential information about the incident, allowing us to assist you in resolving the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide a clear and concise description of the incident in the <Incident Description> field.
2.  Choose the type of incident you experienced from the <Tag Issue> dropdown menu.
3.  Select the date and time of the incident in the <Incident Date> and <Incident Time> fields, respectively.
4.  Specify the location where the incident occurred in the <Incident Location> field.
5.  If relevant, attach supporting files to help us better understand the incident.
6.  Choose the status of the incident from the <Incident Status> dropdown menu.
7.  If necessary, provide a reason for the incident status in the <Incident Status Reason> field.
8.  Finally, click the <Submit> button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Complaint Form**: This section is where you can provide a detailed description of the issue you experienced with your electronic toll tag.
*   **Tag Details**: Please provide any relevant information about your electronic toll tag, such as its ID number or serial number.
*   **Incident Details**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including what happened and when.
*   **Contact Information**: If you'd like to be contacted for further assistance, please provide your contact information.
*   **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 02/12/2023).
*   **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).
*   **Incident Location**: Specify the exact location where the incident occurred.
*   **Tag Issue**: Choose the type of issue you experienced with your electronic toll tag:
    + Issue with the toll gate
    + Issue with the toll reader
    + Issue with the toll tag
    + Other
*   **Incident Reported**: Enter the date you reported the incident.
*   **Incident Status**: Choose the current status of the incident:
    + Open
    + Closed
    + Resolved
*   **Incident Status Reason**: If you chose "Closed" or "Resolved" as the incident status, please provide a brief reason for the status change.
*   **Supporting File 1-10**: If you have any supporting files or evidence related to the incident, please attach them here. You can attach up to 10 files.
*   **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to send the form and receive further assistance.
