# sports_program_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting complaints or concerns about the sports programs offered by the organization. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your complaint, and we will investigate and address the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the program that you are complaining about in the "program" field.
2. Fill in the name and contact information of the parent/guardian.
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue or concern.
4. Select the specific program, staff member, and date related to your complaint.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program** (`program`): Enter the name of the program that you are complaining about.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a detailed description of the issue or concern.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the date of the issue or concern.
* **Time** (`time`): Select the time of the issue or concern.
* **Program** (`program`): Select all programs that are related to your complaint.
* **Staff** (`staff`): Select the staff member who is related to your complaint.
* **Program Date** (`program_date`): Select the specific date of the program related to your complaint.
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional notes or comments about your complaint.
* **Multiple Programs** (`multiple_programs`): Select all programs that are related to your complaint.
* **Multiple Staff** (`multiple_staff`): Select all staff members related to your complaint.
* **Single Program** (`single_program`): Select a single program that is related to your complaint.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible about your complaint to help us better understand and address the issue.
* Make sure to select the correct program, staff member, and date related to your complaint.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please include them in the "note" field.
