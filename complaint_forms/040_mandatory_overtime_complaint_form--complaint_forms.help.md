# Mandatory Overtime Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report instances of mandatory overtime complaints from employees. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your experience and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form with accurate and honest information about your experience.
2. Make sure to provide your employee name and complaint date.
3. Clearly describe the incident in the complaint description field.
4. Specify the manager's name who requested the overtime.
5. Select the type of complaint you are filing (e.g., Mandatory Over-Time or Mandatory Over-Time - Other).
6. Choose the reason for your complaint (e.g., Workload or Other).
7. Select the expected outcome for your complaint (e.g., Resolution, Review, or No Change).
8. Optionally, select multiple manager actions that you would like them to take (e.g., Resolution, Review, or No Change).
9. Provide any additional comments or details about your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your employee name to file the complaint under your account.
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the incident occurred or when you became aware of it.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, text, required): Describe the incident in detail, including the circumstances surrounding the mandatory overtime demand.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Specify the name of the manager who requested the overtime.
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of complaint you are filing (e.g., Mandatory Over-Time or Mandatory Over-Time - Other).
* **Reason for Complaint** (`reason_for_complaint`, select_multiple, required): Choose the reason why you feel the mandatory overtime was unjustified or excessive (e.g., Workload or Other).
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, select_one, required): Specify the outcome you are looking for in response to your complaint (e.g., Resolution, Review, or No Change).
* **Manager Response** (`manager_response`, text, required): Enter the response or comment from the manager regarding your complaint.
* **Manager Action** (`manager_action`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more actions you would like the manager to take in response to your complaint (e.g., Resolution, Review, or No Change).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or details about your complaint.
