# Fashion Retail Customer Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Retail Customer Service Report form is used to report customer service incidents, allowing the customer service team to document and resolve issues, identify trends, and improve customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Customer Service Incident, Other).
2. Provide a brief description of the incident, in 25 words or less.
3. Share additional details about the incident.
4. Select the store location where the incident occurred.
5. Date and time of the incident.
6. If applicable, provide the employee name and contact information.
7. Describe the resolution status and outcome.
8. Provide a follow-up action or description of the customer experience.
9. Check if any coaching was needed or provided.
10. If necessary, provide additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Incident**: Select the type of incident that occurred.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the incident, in 25 words or less.
* **Incident Details**: Share additional details about the incident.
* **Store Location**: Select the store location where the incident occurred.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Employee Name**: If applicable, enter the employee's name involved in the incident.
* **Employee Contact**: If applicable, enter the employee's contact information.
* **Resolution Status**: Select the status of the resolution.
* **Resolution Outcome**: Describe the resolution outcome.
* **Follow-up Action**: Describe the follow-up action or customer experience.
* **Coaching Needed**: Check if any coaching was needed or provided.
* **Coaching Outcome**: Describe the coaching outcome.
* **Customer Follow-up**: Describe the customer follow-up.
* **Notes**: Additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If a field is not applicable, select "Other" or "N/A" as necessary.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
