# Charity Event Mismanagement Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who have experienced mismanagement issues with charity events. The purpose of the form is to collect specific details about the event and the complainant's experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your personal information (Section 1)
*   Provide details about the charity event (Section 2)
*   Describe the mismanagement issue you encountered (Section 3)
*   Share any specific incidents or evidence you have (Section 4)
*   Estimate the financial impact of the issue (Section 5)
*   Indicate if there were witnesses (Section 6)
*   Provide witness contact information (Section 7)
*   Describe the desired outcome or resolution (Section 8)
*   Let us know if you have attached any supporting documents (Section 9)
*   Enter the date you are filing this complaint (Section 10)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Your Information
* **Your Full Name**: Please enter your complete name.
*   **Email Address**: Enter the email address you would like to be contacted at for further updates or clarification.
*   **Phone Number**: If you have a preferred contact number, please enter it.
*   **Your Role at the Event**: Choose the category that best describes your role at the event (Attendee, Volunteer, Vendor, Staff Member, Other).
### Event Details
*   **Name of Event**: Enter the name of the event in question.
*   **Date of Event**: Enter the date the event took place.
*   **Event Location**: Enter the city, venue name, or address where the event occurred.
*   **Event Organizer or Organization Name**: Enter the name of the event organizer or organization.
### Complaint Details
*   **Type of Mismanagement**: Choose the category that best describes the type of mismanagement issue you experienced (Financial Mismanagement, Poor Organization, Misleading Information, Safety Concerns, Volunteer Mistreatment, Communication Failure, Unauthorized Fund Usage, Other).
*   **Detailed Description of the Complaint**: Provide a clear description of what happened and why you believe it was mismanagement.
*   **Specific Incidents or Examples**: If applicable, provide concrete examples or evidence to support your complaint.
*   **Financial Impact to You**: If this issue resulted in financial losses for you, estimate that amount.
*   **Were There Witnesses**: Choose whether or not there were witnesses.
*   **Witnesses Names and Contact Information**: If there were witnesses, provide their names and contact information.
*   **Resolution Sought**: Describe the outcome you would be satisfied with to resolve this complaint.
*   **Do You Have Supporting Documents**: Let us know if you have attached any documents to support your complaint.
*   **Date of This Complaint**: Enter the date you are filing this complaint.

The form is designed to be as clear and straightforward as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the contact provided in the form's introduction or at [support email/phone number].
