The purpose of this form is to gather information about a customer complaint. This form is designed to collect details about the customer's issue, the order they are concerned about, and their contact information. The form helps the customer service team to efficiently address the complaint by providing them with essential details about the problem and how to contact the customer. It's essential to fill out the form only when you have a valid complaint, to avoid wasting time and resources.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the form fields according to the instructions provided for each field.
3.  Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (Customer Info, text, required: false)
    *   Fill in your full name and any other contact information you'd like to provide.
*   **Complaint Details** (Complaint Details, text, required: false)
    *   Describe the issue you're facing. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your problem better.
*   **Order Details** (Order Details, text, required: false)
    *   Provide as much information as you can about your order, including the order date and time.
*   **Product Info** (Product Info, text, required: false)
    *   Give us more details about the product you're complaining about.
*   **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select one, required: false)
    *   Choose how you would like to be contacted: Phone, Email, or In Person.
*   **Contact Information** (Contact Info, text, required: false)
    *   Provide any additional contact information you're willing to share.
*   **Order ID** (Order ID, number, required: false)
    *   If possible, provide the ID of the order that's relevant to your complaint.
*   **Complaint Type** (Complaint Type, select multiple, required: false)
    *   Choose the type of complaint you're reporting. This will help us categorize your issue and address it more efficiently.
*   **Attachments** (Attachments, note, required: false)
    *   If you need to attach any relevant files or documents, click here and upload them.
*   **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required: false)
    *   Provide the date of the order that's causing the issue.
*   **Order Time** (Order Time, time, required: false)
    *   If you have the time of the order, provide it to help us understand the timing.
*   **Customer Message** (Customer Message, text, required: false)
    *   If there's any more information you'd like to share with us, add it here.
*   **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, required: false)
    *   Provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
*   **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, required: false)
    *   Share your email address so we can keep in touch.
*   **Attachment Files** (Attachment Files, select multiple, required: false)
    *   If you need to attach any documents or files, choose "Yes" here.
*   **Product ID** (Product ID, number, required: false)
    *   If you're complaining about a specific product, provide its ID.
*   **Product Price** (Product Price, number, required: false)
    *   If you have the price of the product, add it here.
*   **Customer Note** (Customer Note, text, required: false)
    *   Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to include.
*   **Order Status** (Order Status, select multiple, required: false)
    *   Choose how the order currently stands: Active or Inactive.
*   **Attachment Description** (Attachment Description, text, required: false)
    *   Explain the file you've attached, if any.
*   **Customer Service Name** (Customer Service Name, text, required: false)
    *   If you'd like to address a specific customer service representative, provide their name.
*   **Customer Service Position** (Customer Service Position, text, required: false)
    *   If you know the position of the customer service representative, let us know.
*   **Attached By** (Attached By, text, required: false)
    *   If you've attached a file, let us know who attached it.
*   **Attached At** (Attached At, date, required: false)
    *   If you've attached a file, provide the date and time it was attached.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting the form.
*   Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue.
*   If you have any attachments, attach them to the relevant fields.
*   If you're unsure about any field, leave a note and we'll get back to you.
