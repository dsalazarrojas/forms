# Incident Complaint Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Complaint Form is used to document and report workplace incidents. This form is meant to be used for new, unreported incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as required.
2. Use the date field to enter the date the incident occurred.
3. Enter a brief description of the incident in the incident description field.
4. Choose the resolution from the options provided.
5. If you are reporting on behalf of an employee, enter their department and position in the corresponding fields.
6. If you have any additional details about the incident, please enter them in the incident details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee reporting the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the incident.
* **Resolution** (`incident_resolution`, select_one, required): Choose the resolution from the options provided.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, optional): If reporting on behalf of an employee, enter their department.
* **Employee Position** (`employee_position`, text, optional): If reporting on behalf of an employee, enter their position.
* **Employee Supervisor** (`employee_supervisor`, text, optional): If reporting on behalf of an employee, enter their supervisor's name.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already reported this incident before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Use this form only for new, unreported incidents.
