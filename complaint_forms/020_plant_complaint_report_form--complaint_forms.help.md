# Plant Complaint Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about complaints related to plants. It's intended for reporting issues such as damage, disease, or pest infestations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of plant affected by the issue (e.g., trees, shrubs, flowers, or grasses)
2. Describe the issue in as much detail as possible
3. If applicable, upload a photo of the affected plant
4. Provide the name of the staff member who is reporting the issue (if applicable)
5. Enter the date and time the issue was submitted
6. Enter the location where the issue was reported
7. Enter the location where the issue occurred

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Issue**: This is the main description of the issue with the plant.
* **Plant Type**: Select the type of plant affected by the issue (e.g., trees, shrubs, flowers, or grasses).
* **Issue Description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any symptoms or observations.
* **Photo**: If applicable, upload a photo of the affected plant to help with identification and investigation.
* **Staff Member**: If reporting on behalf of a staff member, enter their name.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the issue was submitted.
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time the issue was submitted.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name or the name of the staff member submitting the issue.
* **Plant Location**: Enter the location where the issue occurred.
* **Issue Location**: Enter the location where the issue was reported.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when describing the issue.
* Make sure to attach a photo if it can help with identification or investigation.
* Double-check your information before submitting to ensure accuracy.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and will help gather essential information to address plant-related issues. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
