# Damaged Goods Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report damaged goods received from our company. Please fill out this form with as much detail as possible so we can process your claim as quickly and efficiently as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form fields with your information and details about the damaged goods you received.
2. Make sure to include your email address so we can contact you with updates on the status of your claim.
3. If you need to attach any supporting documents or images, please contact our customer service department to arrange for their upload.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Damaged Goods Claim Form** (`form_1`, text, optional): This is the main title of the form. It's not required to fill out, but it's a good idea to give your claim a name so we can easily identify it later.
* **Claim Details** (`form_2`, text, optional): Please describe the damaged goods you received, including any relevant details such as the product's condition and any defects you noticed upon inspection.
* **Claimant Info** (`form_3`, text, optional): This is the section to provide your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details about you as the claimant.
* **Product Info** (`form_4`, text, optional): Describe the product that was damaged, including its product code or model number if applicable.
* **Damage Description** (`form_5`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the damage you observed, including any photos or videos that can help us assess the issue.
* **Claimant Phone** (`form_6`, text, optional): This is your contact phone number, so we can easily reach out to you with updates on your claim.
* **Claimant Email** (`form_7`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can send you updates and notifications about the status of your claim.
* **Claim Date** (`form_8`, date, optional): The date you received the damaged goods. If you're unsure, please use the best estimate you can provide.
