# Teacher Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Complaint Form is a tool for students to report any concerns or grievances they have about their teachers. It helps to document and address these issues in a clear and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name and ensure it matches their identity.
2. Enter the teacher's name who is being complained about.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for the complaint type, depending on whether there was a complaint.
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident or issue.
5. Enter the student's email address.
6. Enter the student's phone number.
7. Enter the teacher's email address.
8. Enter the teacher's phone number.
9. Enter your name, as the person submitting the complaint.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Enter your email address again for confirmation.
13. Enter your phone number again for confirmation.
14. Enter any additional comments or remarks about the submission.
15. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have read and agreed to the submitter agreement.
16. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have acknowledged the submission.
17. Leave any additional notes or comments for the submitter.
18. Select a time zone (UTC) for the submission.
19. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the submission is within the correct time zone.
20. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the submission is correct.
21. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the submission is accurate.
22. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the submission is valid.
23. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the submission is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Teacher Name (2)**: Enter the teacher's name who is being complained about.
* **Complaint Type (3)**: Select "Yes" if there was a complaint, "No" otherwise.
* **Detail Description (4)**: Provide a clear and detailed description of the incident or issue.
* **Student Email (5)**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone (6)**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Teacher Email (7)**: Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone (8)**: Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Submitter Name (9)**: Enter your name as the person submitting the complaint.
* **Submitter Email (10)**: Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone (11)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Email 2 (12)**: Enter your email address again for confirmation.
* **Submitter Phone 2 (13)**: Enter your phone number again for confirmation.
* **Submitter Comment (14)**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the submission.
* **Submitter Agreement (15)**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agreed to the submitter agreement.
* **Submitter Acknowledgement (16)**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you have acknowledged the submission.
* **Submitter Note (17)**: Leave any additional notes or comments for the submitter.
* **Submitter Date (18)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter Time (19)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitter Timestamp (20)**: Enter the timestamp of submission.
* **Submitter Time Zone (21)**: Select "Yes" if the submission is within the correct time zone.
* **Submitter Timezone (22)**: Select "Yes" if the submission is correct.
* **Submitter Timezone 2 (23)**: Select "Yes" if the submission is valid.
* **Submitter Timezone 3 (24)**: Select "Yes" if the submission is correct.
* **Submitter Timezone 4 (25)**: Select "Yes" if the submission is valid.
