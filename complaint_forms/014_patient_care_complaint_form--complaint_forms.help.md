# patient_care_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients about their complaints or concerns regarding their care. It will help our team understand their needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and ensure you have all the necessary information to fill out the form completely.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in each field.
3. Click submit to send the form to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name**: This is your name, where you should enter your full name as it appears on your records.
* **complaint_type**: Choose whether your complaint is Medical or Non-Medical.
* **description**: Please provide a detailed description of your complaint or concern.
* **date**: Enter the date of your complaint.
* **phone_number**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **complaint**: This is an optional field where you can provide more details about your complaint.

Note: If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
