# Maladministration Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to report incidents or complaints in a structured and standardized way, helping to streamline the reporting process and facilitate effective communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
   - Read through the form carefully.
   - Fill out each field as instructed.
   - Make sure to complete all required fields.
   - Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **incident_type** (select_one): Choose the type of incident or complaint you are reporting (e.g., Incident Report, Complaint Form).
* **location** (text): Enter a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **incident_description** (text): Provide details about the incident or complaint, including what happened, when, and why.
* **report_date** (date): Enter the date you are reporting the incident or complaint.
* **incident_time** (time): Enter the time of the incident or complaint.
* **reporter** (text): Enter your name or ID number.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **reporting_status** (select_one): Choose the status of your report (e.g., New Report, In Review, Approved, Rejected).
* **incident_status** (select_one): Choose the status of the incident (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **location_2** (text): Enter a brief description of the location where the incident occurred (if different from previous field).
* **comments** (text): Provide any additional comments or details about the incident or complaint.
* **submitted_by** (text): Enter your name or ID number (if different from previous field).
* **submitted** (date): Enter the date you submitted the report.
* **submit_time** (time): Enter the time you submitted the report.
* **report_id** (text): Enter a unique identifier for your report (if provided by the system).
* **report_status** (select_one): Choose the status of your report (e.g., Open, Closed).

Note: The fields with similar labels have been merged and explained together to avoid duplication.
