# DJ Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DJ Complaint Form is a tool for users to provide feedback about their experience with a DJ or their performance. This form helps the DJ improve their skills and provides valuable insights for future interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields. If a field is required, an asterisk (*) will be displayed next to it. Start by filling in the complaint date and time, then select the performance level and performance quality of the DJ. Next, provide a brief description of your complaint and any recommendations for improvement. Finally, enter the reason for your complaint and any additional description of the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event or interaction that led to your complaint.
* **Complaint Time** (`complaint_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event or interaction that led to your complaint.
* **Performance Level** (`performance_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Performance Quality** (`performance_quality`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief explanation of the reason for your complaint.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the issue or concern you have with the DJ's performance.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `text`, required: false): Enter any suggestions or recommendations for improvement.
