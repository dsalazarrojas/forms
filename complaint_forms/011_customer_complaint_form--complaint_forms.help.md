# Customer Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Complaint Form is designed to collect information from customers about their complaints or issues with our services or products. This form is intended to help us identify and address potential problems and improve our customer experience. Please use this form to submit genuine complaints and feedback, and we'll do our best to respond to each one.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Customer email" field. Please note that we'll use this email to contact you if needed.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer phone" field.
4. Describe your complaint or issue in the "Complaint details" field.
5. If relevant, select the category of your complaint in the "Complaint category" field. This will help us direct your issue to the right team.
6. If you'd like to send us a message, you can add any additional information in the "Customer message" field.
7. You can also select multiple statuses from the "Customer status" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or documentation.
* **Customer email** (`customer_email`, required/optional): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you for further discussion or updates.
* **Customer phone** (`customer_phone`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Complaint details** (`complaint_details`, required/optional): Describe your complaint or issue in as much detail as possible.
* **Complaint date** (`complaint_date`, required/optional): If you know the date of your complaint, please enter it here.
* **Complaint time** (`complaint_time`, required/optional): If you know the time of your complaint, please enter it here.
* **Complaint category** (`complaint_category`, required/optional): Select the category that best describes your complaint from the provided options.
* **Customer message** (`customer_message`, required/optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Assigned user** (`assigned_user`, required/optional): Please don't fill in this field unless you're told otherwise.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, required/optional): Please don't fill in this field unless you're told otherwise.
* **Customer status** (`customer_status`, required/optional): Select any applicable statuses from the provided options, but please don't over-select.
