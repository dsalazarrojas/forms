# Harassment Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Harassment Incident Form is a systematic way to report and document incidents of harassment that occur within the workplace. This form helps to ensure that all incidents are handled promptly and in accordance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and details about the incident before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Reported By

* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the person reporting the incident.

### Page 2: Incident Information

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): If the incident occurred outside of work, please specify the location.

### Page 3: Incident Details and Status

* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of incident (Harassment, Bullying, or Other).
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the incident (Pending, Closed).
* **Incident Severity** (`incident_severity`, number, required): Rate the severity of the incident on a scale of 1-5.
* **Incident Resolution** (`incident_resolution`, text, optional): If resolved, provide a brief explanation of how it was handled.
* **Incident Closure Date** (`incident_closure_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident was closed.

### Page 4: Reporting Information

* **Reported To** (`reported_to`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person or department the incident was reported to.
* **Reported To Manager** (`reported_to_manager`, text, optional): If the incident was reported to a manager, please enter their name and title.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): Obtain a signature from the manager confirming they have received the report.
* **Manager Date** (`manager_date`, date, required): Date the manager signed the report.
* **Reported By Signature** (`reported_by_signature`, text, required): Sign the report to confirm it is accurate.
* **Reported By Name** (`reported_by_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported By Title** (`reported_by_title`, text, optional): Enter your title or position.
* **Reported By Manager Name** (`reported_by_manager_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the manager reporting the incident.
* **Reported By Manager Title** (`reported_by_manager_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the title of the manager reporting the incident.
