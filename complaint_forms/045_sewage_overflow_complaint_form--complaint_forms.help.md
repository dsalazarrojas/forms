# Sewage Overflow Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sewage Overflow Complaint Form is designed to report sewage overflows in the community. This form will help us to identify the location and severity of the issue, which will allow us to respond and fix the problem efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   Enter your contact information, including your name and contact number.
*   Describe the issue in the `Description` field, including as much detail as possible about the location, severity, and any other relevant information.
*   If you have a photo of the issue, upload it in the `Photo` field.
*   Select the `Severity` level of the issue, using one of the options provided.
*   Provide the `Location` where the issue is occurring.
*   Enter the date and time of the issue in the `Date` and `Time` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Name (`user_name`)
*   Enter your name, so we can contact you to discuss the issue.

### Address (`address`)
*   Enter the address where the issue is occurring.

### Contact Number (`contact_number`)
*   Enter your phone number so we can contact you to discuss the issue.

### Description (`sewage_description`)
*   Describe the issue in as much detail as possible, including any other relevant information.

### Photo (`photo_attachment`)
*   If you have a photo of the issue, upload it here. This will help us to better understand the problem.

### Severity (`severity`)
*   Select the severity level of the issue, using one of the options provided:
	+   Low: A minor issue that is not causing significant problems.
	+   Medium: An issue that is causing some problems but not severe.
	+   High: A serious issue that is causing significant problems.

### Location (`location`)
*   Enter the location where the issue is occurring.

### Date (`date`)
*   Enter the date of the issue.

### Time (`time`)
*   Enter the time of the issue.

Note: This form is designed to collect information about sewage overflows. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and relevant to the issue. If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for guidance.
