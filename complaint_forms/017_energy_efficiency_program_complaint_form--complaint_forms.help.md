# Energy Efficiency Program Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Efficiency Program Complaint Form is used to report issues or concerns related to the energy efficiency program. This form helps the program team to quickly and efficiently gather information about the issues and address them in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
4. Choose the type of issue you are reporting (Energy or Non-Energy).
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments or information about the issue.
6. Submit the form to submit your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the issue you are experiencing in as much detail as possible.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you regarding your complaint.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Date**: Select the date you experienced the issue.
* **Time**: Select the time you experienced the issue (optional).
* **Location**: Provide the location where you experienced the issue (optional).
* **Issue Type**: Choose the type of issue you are experiencing (Energy or Non-Energy).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional information you would like to share about the issue.
* **Submitter ID**: Enter your identification number or any other relevant identifier.
