The Lawyer Conduct Complaint Form is designed to provide a structured way to report and manage complaints against lawyers. This form is used to gather information from complainants (individuals or organizations) about specific conduct they believe constitutes unprofessional behavior or misconduct by lawyers. The form will help in tracking and investigating these complaints, ensuring that any issues are addressed in a fair and transparent manner. Before filling out this form, please ensure that you have not already submitted a complaint on this matter, as duplicate submissions may cause confusion and delays in processing.

# Lawyer Conduct Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lawyer Conduct Complaint Form is a critical tool for reporting and managing complaints against lawyers. By filling out this form, you will provide essential information that will help us investigate and address your concerns efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the type of complaint you are submitting (e.g., Unprofessionalism, Conflict of Interest, etc.).
2.  Provide your name and contact number, so we can reach out to you for further information or clarification, if needed.
3.  Describe the specific conduct or issue you are complaining about.
4.  If you have any evidence supporting your complaint (e.g., documents, videos, or audio recordings), select the relevant type.
5.  Finally, provide details about the lawyer involved, including their name, tool used, and any relevant dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **complaint_form** (Select Multiple): Choose the type of complaint you are submitting.
    *   **Client Name**: Enter the name of the lawyer/client involved.
    *   **Client Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the lawyer/client.
    *   **Complaint Description**: Describe the specific conduct or issue you are complaining about.
*   **complaint_type** (Select One): Select the type of complaint you are submitting.
    *   **Lawyer Name**: Enter the name of the lawyer involved.
*   **contact_number** (Text): Enter the contact number of the person submitting the complaint.
*   **email** (Email): Enter the email address of the person submitting the complaint.
*   **complaint_date** (Date): Enter the date of the complaint.
*   **complaint_time** (Time): Enter the time of the complaint.
*   **description** (Text): Provide any additional information about the complaint.
*   **evidence** (Select Multiple): If you have any evidence supporting your complaint (e.g., documents, videos, or audio recordings).
    *   **evidence_type**: Select the type of evidence you have.
*   **assigned_to** (Text): Enter the name of the person or organization assigned to handle the complaint.
*   **status** (Select One): Select the status of the complaint (e.g., New, In Progress, Resolved).
*   **submitted_date** (Date): Enter the date the complaint was submitted.
*   **updated_date** (Date): Enter the date the complaint was updated.
*   **closed_date** (Date): Enter the date the complaint was closed.
*   **submitted_by** (Text): Enter the name of the person submitting the complaint.
*   **assigned_tool** (Text): Enter the name of the tool used to assign the complaint to.
*   **submitted_by_email** (Email): Enter the email address of the person submitting the complaint.
*   **submitted_by_phone** (Text): Enter the contact number of the person submitting the complaint.
*   **closed_by** (Text): Enter the name of the person closing the complaint.
*   **closed_tool** (Text): Enter the name of the tool used to close the complaint.
*   **submitted_by_title** (Text): Enter the title of the person submitting the complaint.
*   **submitted_by_name** (Text): Enter the name of the person submitting the complaint.
*   **submitted_by_position** (Text): Enter the position of the person submitting the complaint.
*   **submitted_by_department** (Text): Enter the department of the person submitting the complaint.
*   **submitted_by_date** (Date): Enter the date the complaint was submitted.

Note: The fields are labeled according to the YAML structure provided.
