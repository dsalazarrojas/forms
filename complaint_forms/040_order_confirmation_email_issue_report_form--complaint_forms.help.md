# Order Confirmation Email Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Confirmation Email Issue Report Form is used to report issues with order confirmation emails. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Make sure you have experienced an issue with your order confirmation email.
* Enter your User ID and Order ID to identify the affected order.
* Describe the issue you are experiencing with the email in the `Issue Description` field.
* Specify the `Email Body` of the email as you received it.
* Identify the `Order Status` of the order as it currently is.
* Choose the `Expected Email Subject` as it should have been.
* Choose the `Order Date` of the order.
* Specify the `Support Agent` who you would like to handle your issue.
* Choose the `Follow Up Status` as it currently is.
* Choose the `Next Follow Up Date` if applicable.
* Add any additional `Follow Up Notes` if necessary.
* Choose the `Support Agent Assigned` who will handle your issue.
* Add `Issue Resolution Notes` if you have attempted to resolve the issue.
* Choose the `Issue Resolution Status` of your issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (1): Enter your unique User ID associated with your account.
* **Order ID** (2): Enter the ID of the order that experienced the issue.
* **Email Subject** (3): Describe the subject of the email you received.
* **Email Body** (4): Enter the text of the email you received.
* **Issue Description** (5): Describe the issue you are experiencing with the email.
* **Expected Email Attachment** (6): If you expected an attachment with the email, specify its name here.
* **Received Email Attachment** (7): If you received an attachment with the email, specify its name here.
* **Order Status** (8): Enter the current status of your order (e.g., Pending, Cancelled, etc.).
* **Expected Email Subject** (9): Choose the correct subject of the email from the options provided.
* **Order Date** (10): Enter the date when the order was made.
* **Support Agent** (11): Enter the name of the support agent you would like to handle your issue.
* **Follow Up Status** (12): Choose the status of your follow up (e.g., Open, In Progress, etc.).
* **Next Follow Up Date** (13): If applicable, enter the date of your next follow-up.
* **Follow Up Notes** (14): Add any additional notes about your follow-up progress.
* **Support Agent Assigned** (15): Choose the support agent assigned to handle your issue.
* **Issue Resolution Notes** (16): Add any additional notes about the issue resolution.
* **Issue Resolution Status** (17): Choose the status of your issue resolution (e.g., Resolved, Not Resolved, etc.).
