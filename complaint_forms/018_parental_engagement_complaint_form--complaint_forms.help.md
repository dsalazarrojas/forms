# Parental Engagement Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow parents or guardians to submit complaints or concerns related to their child's education, such as issues with teachers, classroom environment, or school policies. This form will be reviewed by the institution's administration to understand the concerns and take necessary actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name (full name) in the "full_name" field. This will help us to contact you in case we need more information.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field. This is how we will contact you to follow up on your complaint.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field. This is an optional field for us to reach out to you if needed.
4. In the "complaint" field, describe your concern in as much detail as possible so we can understand your issue.
5. If you have any attachments or evidence related to your complaint, you can select from the options provided in the "attachments" field.
6. Please select from the "submitted_by" field who submitted this complaint (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3). This information helps us in tracking and addressing the concern.
7. You don't need to fill up the "submitted_at" and "updated_at" fields as these are system-generated timestamps.
8. If you have any attachment to support your complaint, enter it in the "attachment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **full_name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your name (first name and last name) for us to contact you.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **complaint** (`complaint`, text, optional): Describe your concern in as much detail as possible.
* **attachments** (`attachments`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the options provided if you have any attachments related to your complaint.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted this complaint (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **attachment** (`attachment`, text, optional): If you have any attachment, enter it here.
 
## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing your concern in the "complaint" field.
* If you have any attachment, make sure to select it in the "attachments" field for us to review it.
* If you select "Option 1" in the "submitted_by" field, please make sure to follow up with us if you need any further assistance.
