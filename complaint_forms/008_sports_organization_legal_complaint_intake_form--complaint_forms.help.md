# sports_organization_legal_complaint_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to submit complaints or concerns regarding the sports organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User First Name.
2. Fill in your User Last Name.
3. Enter your User Email address.
4. Select the type of sport you are affiliated with (e.g. American Football, Baseball, etc.).
5. Choose the nature of your complaint (e.g. Harassment, Misrepresentation, etc.).
6. Provide a detailed description of your complaint.
7. Select who submitted the complaint (e.g. Employee, Coach, etc.).
8. Choose who the complaint is being submitted to (e.g. Head of Organization, Legal Department, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required: false): This is the first name of the person submitting the complaint.
* **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required: false): This is the last name of the person submitting the complaint.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): This is the email address of the person submitting the complaint.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): This field is not currently being used.
* **Sport Type** (`sport_type`, select_one, required: false): This is the type of sport the complaint is related to (e.g. American Football, Baseball, etc.).
* **Complaint Nature** (`complaint_nature`, select_multiple, required: false): This is the nature of the complaint (e.g. Harassment, Misrepresentation, etc.). Please select all applicable options.
* **Description** (`complaint_description`, note, required: false): This is a detailed description of the complaint.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required: false): This is the role of the person submitting the complaint (e.g. Employee, Coach, etc.).
* **Submission To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, required: false): This is the person or department that the complaint is being submitted to (e.g. Head of Organization, Legal Department, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to help with the resolution process.
* Only submit one complaint per form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact the sports organization for assistance.
