# pool_access_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pool_access_complaint_form" is a complaint form used to report issues or concerns related to a pool. It is used to collect information about the user's name, pool ID, complaint date, and complaint description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "User name" field.
2. Enter the pool ID, which can be found on the pool's sign or in the pool's documentation.
3. Choose the date of the complaint from the calendar.
4. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Complaint description" field.
5. If desired, you can attach an image to help support your complaint.
6. Select a pool manager to assign the complaint to from the list of available options.
7. Choose the current status of the complaint from the list of available options.
8. Add any additional notes about the complaint.
9. If desired, enter the person or team assigned to resolve the complaint and when it was assigned.
10. If the complaint is resolved, choose the person or team who resolved it.
11. Enter any comments about the complaint.
12. The form will automatically fill in the created by field with your username.
13. If the complaint is updated, the form will automatically fill in the updated by field with your username.
14. Enter any other information about the complaint.
