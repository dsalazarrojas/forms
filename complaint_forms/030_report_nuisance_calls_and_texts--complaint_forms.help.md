# Report Nuisance Calls And Texts - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report and track nuisance calls and texts received by the organization. This helps in keeping track of incidents and their status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each field and provide detailed answers in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Nuisance Calls And Texts** (1, Report Nuisance Calls And Texts, required: false): This field is used to indicate whether the form is for reporting a new incident or not.
* **Date And Time Of Incident** (2, Date And Time Of Incident, required: false): Enter the date and time when the nuisance call or text was received.
* **Incident Details** (3, Incident Details, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the type of call or text and its nature.
* **Supporting Evidence** (4, Supporting Evidence, required: false): Attach any relevant supporting evidence, such as screenshots or audio recordings, that can help in understanding the incident.
* **Nuisance Cause** (5, Nuisance Cause, required: false): Describe the cause of the nuisance call or text.
* **Nuisance Type** (6, Nuisance Type, required: false): Check whether the nuisance call or text was of a type that requires immediate attention or not.
* **Reported By** (7, Reported By, required: false): Check whether the incident was reported by a user or not.
* **Follow Up Status** (8, Follow Up Status, required: false): Check the status of the follow-up actions taken on the incident.
* **Follow Up Date** (9, Follow Up Date, required: false): Enter the date when the follow-up actions were taken.
* **Follow Up Time** (10, Follow Up Time, required: false): Enter the time when the follow-up actions were taken.
* **Follow Up Comments** (11, Follow Up Comments, required: false): Provide any additional comments about the follow-up actions taken.
* **Follow Up User** (12, Follow Up User, required: false): Check whether the follow-up actions were taken by a user or not.
* **Report Nuisance Calls And Texts Report** (13, Report Nuisance Calls And Texts Report, required: false): This field is likely used to track the overall status of reported incidents, possibly for further analysis.
