# Ministry Leader Monthly Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ministry Leader Monthly Report Form is designed to gather information from ministry leaders on their monthly activities, finances, and other relevant details. This form helps the organization track and analyze the progress and performance of its ministry leaders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required".
3. Provide accurate and concise answers to each question or prompt.
4. Use the space provided for Notes to add any additional comments or explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Monthly Operations** (`ministry_leader_monthly_report_form_1`): Describe the main activities and operations conducted during the month.
- **Monthly Activities** (`ministry_leader_monthly_report_form_2`): List the key activities and events held during the month.
- **Monthly Finances** (`ministry_leader_monthly_report_form_3`): Enter the total amount of funds received and spent during the month.
- **Notes** (`ministry_leader_monthly_report_form_4`): Add any additional comments or explanations about the activities, finances, or other relevant details.
- **Email** (`ministry_leader_monthly_report_form_5`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`ministry_leader_monthly_report_form_6`): Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
- Be concise and accurate when filling in the form.
- Use the provided space for Notes to add any additional comments.
- Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
