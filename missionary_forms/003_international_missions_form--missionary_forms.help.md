# International Missions Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The International Missions Form is designed to collect information about international missions, which are projects or initiatives that take place outside of the user's country of origin. This form helps mission planners and stakeholders understand the mission's context, risks, and potential challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Mission Details**: Provide a brief description of the mission's objectives, goals, and scope.
2. **Mission Dates**: Specify the start and end dates of the mission.
3. **Mission Locations**: List the countries or regions where the mission will take place.
4. **Mission Expenses**: Estimate the total expenses for the mission, including travel, accommodation, and other costs.
5. **Expenses Per Day**: Break down the estimated expenses per day of the mission.
6. **Mission Expenses Category**: Identify the category or categories of expenses, such as food, transportation, and equipment.
7. **Mission Objectives**: Describe the mission's primary objectives, outcomes, or deliverables.
8. **Mission Duration**: Specify the duration of the mission, including the number of days or weeks.
9. **Mission Team**: List the team members involved in the mission, including their roles and responsibilities.
10. **Mission Risks**: Identify potential risks or challenges associated with the mission.
11. **Mission Success Criteria**: Define the criteria for measuring the mission's success.
12. **Mission Evaluation**: Evaluate the mission's progress and outcomes.
13. **Confirm Mission Dates**: Confirm the mission dates provided.
14. **Confirm Mission Locations**: Confirm the mission locations provided.
15. **Confirm Mission Expenses**: Confirm the mission expenses provided.
16. **Confirm Mission Expenses Category**: Confirm the mission expenses category provided.
17. **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information or comments.
18. **Confirmation**: Please confirm that the form is complete and accurate.
