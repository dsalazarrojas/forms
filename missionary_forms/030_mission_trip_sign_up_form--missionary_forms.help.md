# Mission Trip Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mission Trip Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from users for a mission trip. This form helps the trip organizers to gather details about the trip, including its name, leader's name, date range, time range, duration, location, cost, organizer, sponsor, contact name, phone number, email, additional information, and confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), on each page.
2. Make sure to review and confirm your information before submitting the form.
3. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mission Trip Name** (`mission_trip_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the mission trip.
* **Trip Leader's Name** (`trip_leader_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the trip leader.
* **Trip Date Range** (`trip_date_range`, date, required): Select the date range of the trip.
* **Trip Time Range** (`trip_time_range`, time, required): Select the time range of the trip.
* **Trip Duration** (`trip_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the trip.
* **Trip Location** (`trip_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the trip.
* **Trip Cost** (`trip_cost`, number, required): Enter the estimated cost of the trip.
* **Trip Organizer** (`trip_organizer`, select_one, required): Select the organization that is organizing the trip.
* **Trip Sponsor** (`trip_sponsor`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sponsor(s) for the trip.
* **Trip Contact Name** (`trip_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the trip contact.
* **Trip Contact Phone** (`trip_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the trip organizer.
* **Trip Contact Email** (`trip_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the trip organizer.
* **Additional Information** (`trip_additional_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the trip.
* **Trip Confirmation** (`trip_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm that the trip information is accurate.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Submit the form to complete and sign up for the mission trip.
