# Stoa Tournament Script Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stoa Tournament Script Submission Form is intended for participants in the Stoa Tournament to submit their scripts for review and evaluation. The form is used to gather information about the participant's script, including its name, description, and submission details. Submissions are collected to ensure that the participant's entry is properly recorded and processed for the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the name of your **Script** in the "Script Name" field.
3. Upload your **Script File** by selecting or entering the file path.
4. Provide a brief **Script Description** in the "Script Description" field.
5. Enter the date of your **Submission Date** in the date field.
6. Select your **Submission Time** in the time field.
7. Specify your **Submission Timezone** if necessary.
8. Enter your **Participant Email** in the "Participant Email" field.
9. Enter your **Participant Phone** number in the "Participant Phone" field.
10. Enter your **Participant Institution**, **Participant Department**, **Participant Address**, **Participant City**, **Participant State**, and **Participant Country** details.
11. Click the "Submit" button to send your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (Text): Enter your name as a participant in the Stoa Tournament.
* **Script Name** (Text): Enter the name of your script that you are submitting for review.
* **Script File** (Text): Upload your script file or select the file location.
* **Script Description** (Text): Describe your script briefly, including its contents and features.
* **Submission Date** (Date): Enter the date of your submission in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Submission Time** (Time): Select the time of your submission in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Submission Timezone** (Text): Specify the time zone you are in, if necessary.
* **Participant Email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Institution** (Text): Enter your institution's name.
* **Participant Department** (Text): Enter your department's name.
* **Participant Address** (Text): Enter your address details.
* **Participant City** (Text): Enter the name of your city.
* **Participant State** (Text): Enter the name of your state or region.
* **Participant Country** (Text): Enter the name of your country.
