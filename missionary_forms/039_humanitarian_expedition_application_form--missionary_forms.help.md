# Humanitarian Expeditions Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants interested in humanitarian expeditions. It is used for expedition planning and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your name**: Provide your full name as you would like to be referred to in the expedition.
2. **Select your assigned tool**: Choose the tool you are assigned to use during the expedition.
3. **Specify your purpose**: Explain what you hope to achieve on this expedition.
4. **Provide contact information (optional)**: If you have a contact email or phone number, please enter it to facilitate communication.
5. **Submit your form**: Once you have filled out all required fields, click submit to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Your full name as you would like to be referred to in the expedition.
* **Expedition Date**: The date of the expedition you are interested in participating in.
* **Expedition Time**: The time of the expedition you are interested in participating in.
* **Purpose of Participation**: A brief explanation of what you hope to achieve on this expedition.
* **Contact Email (optional)**: Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone (optional)**: Your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Assigned Tool**: The tool you have been assigned to use during the expedition. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Notes**: Any additional information you would like to provide about your participation.
* **Submitted By**: The person who submitted this application (you).
* **Submitted At**: The date and time this form was submitted.
  Please note that some of these fields are not required, but it's recommended to fill them out for better communication and organization.

Note: This help guide is designed to be concise and clear, with each field explained in detail for better understanding.
