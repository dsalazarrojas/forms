# Church Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Donation Form is a tool for individuals to make donations to the church. It allows us to collect essential information about the donor, their donation details, and their payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name and email address.
2. Select your payment method and provide the check number if applicable.
3. Choose your donation frequency and type.
4. Indicate whether your donation is tax-deductible.
5. Decide on your recognition level and communication preferences.
6. Optionally, provide a message or prayer intention related to your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Optional. Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for any updates or queries.
* **Are You a Church Member**: Select 'Yes' if you are a current member of the church; 'No' if not. If you are not a member but would like to be considered for membership, select 'Prospective member'.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose whether this is a one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annual donation.
* **Type of Donation**: Select the type of donation (e.g., general offering, tithe, building fund, etc.).
* **Specific Program or Cause**: If applicable, describe the specific program or cause you are supporting.
* **In-Kind Donation Description**: If an in-kind donation is made, describe the item(s) donated.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., cash, check, credit or debit card, bank transfer, or pledge).
* **Check Number**: If a check is used, enter the check number.
* **This Donation is Tax-Deductible**: Indicate whether this donation is tax-deductible.
* **Mailing Address for Tax Receipt**: Enter your mailing address for tax receipt purposes.
* **Donation Receipt Requested**: Check 'Yes' if you would like a receipt for your donation.
* **Recognition Level**: Choose how you would like to be recognized for your donation (e.g., anonymous, private recognition only, published in the bulletin, etc.).
* **Updates about Your Donation**: Check 'Yes' if you would like updates about how your donation is used.
* **Newsletter Signup**: Check 'Yes' if you would like to be signed up for the church newsletter.
* **Message or Prayer Request**: Optionally, enter a message or prayer intention related to your donation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the church administration.
* Do not hesitate to ask questions or seek clarification if needed.
