# Ministry Volunteer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining or participating in the ministry's volunteer programs. It helps us understand your interests and preferences to best place you in a suitable volunteer group or role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your volunteer type from the options provided.
2. Fill in your first and last name.
3. Provide your email address so we can contact you.
4. Enter your phone number for us to get in touch with you.
5. Share a brief message about your volunteer interests or goals.
6. Choose the volunteer group(s) you're interested in or would like to join.
7. If you've selected "Other Group," please specify the name of the group.
8. If you've selected "Other" for the assigned tool, please provide the name of the tool you're interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **volunteer_type**: Select the type of volunteer you're interested in or aspire to be: Ministry Volunteer or Ministry Staff, or choose "Other" for a more specific role.
* **first_name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about volunteer opportunities.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for us to get in touch with you.
* **message**: Share a brief message about your volunteer goals or interests.
* **volunteer_group**: Choose the volunteer group(s) you're interested in or would like to join. Options include Faith Missionary Group, Youth Group, or Other Group (if you select "Other Group," please provide the name of the group).
* **volunteer_group_other**: If you selected "Other Group," enter the name of the group you're interested in.
* **assigned_tool**: If you've been assigned a tool for your volunteer role, select it here. Options include chatjimmy or other tools. If you select "other," please provide the name of the tool.
* **assigned_tool_other**: If you selected "Other" for the tool, provide the name of the tool you're interested in using.
