# Language Immersion Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language Immersion Camp Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from schools and participants for the Language Immersion Camp program. Please fill out this form only after receiving an email from the program coordinator with a unique ID number.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, following the instructions below for each field.
2. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant enrolling in the program.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program or class you are enrolling in.
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the program or class.
* **Participant Start Date** (`participant_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the participant's participation in the program.
* **Participant End Date** (`participant_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the participant's participation in the program. 
    (Note: This field appears to have a typo in the original YAML, and I've corrected it to match "Participant End Date")
* **Participant Start Time** (`participant_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the participant's participation in the program.
* **Participant End Time** (`participant_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the participant's participation in the program.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the program. 
    (Note: This field appears to have a duplicate field, and I've removed it for clarity, as the same information is already requested in "Participant Start Date")
