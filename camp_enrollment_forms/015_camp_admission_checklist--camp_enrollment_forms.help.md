# Camp Admission Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Admission Checklist is a form designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians of campers to ensure a smooth and safe experience for each camper during the camp or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the camper's full name as it appears on their registration.
2.  Enter the camper's date of birth.
3.  Enter the name and contact information of the primary contact person (parent or guardian).
4.  Provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
5.  If applicable, list any medical conditions or allergies the camper has.
6.  If applicable, specify the medications the camper is taking, including dosage schedules.
7.  Confirm that consent has been signed for the camper's participation.
8.  List the names and relationship of any authorized pickup persons.
9.  If applicable, specify any food restrictions or special diets the camper requires.
10. If applicable, note any special needs or accommodations required during the camp.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Camper Full Name** (`camper_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's full name as it appears on their registration.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the camper's date of birth.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person (parent or guardian).
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If the camper has any medical conditions or allergies, list them here.
*   **Medications to be Administered** (`medications`, text, optional): If the camper is taking any medications, specify them and their dosage schedules.
*   **Parent Guardian Consent Signed** (`consent_signed`, select_one, required): Confirm that consent has been signed for the camper's participation. Options: 'True' or 'False'.
*   **Authorized Pickup Persons** (`pickup_authorized_persons`, text, required): List the names and relationships of any authorized persons for pickup.
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If the camper has any food restrictions or special diets, specify them here.
*   **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs`, text, optional): If the camper requires any special support during the camp, note it here.

## Tips
*   Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure the camper's needs are met and that everyone involved is informed.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact the camp administration for guidance.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
*   Keep the form up to date and make sure to sign the consent form when necessary.
