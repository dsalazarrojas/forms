# Film Camp Detailed Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants in a film camp. It collects detailed information from each participant, including their personal details, skills, and confirmation of their registration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Camp Name**: Enter the name of the camp you are registering for.
2. **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. **Participant Age**: Enter your age.
6. **Participant Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options.
7. **Participant Skills**: List any relevant skills you have that align with the camp's requirements.
8. **Participant Interests**: List any interests you have that align with the camp's focus.
9. **Participant Employment Status**: Select your current employment status from the provided options.
10. **Participant References**: If required by the camp, provide any necessary references or attachments.
11. **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
12. **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted this form.
13. **Submission Location**: Enter the location where you submitted this form.
14. **Submission Attachments**: Attach any relevant documents or files required by the camp.
15. **Submission Video Link**: If required by the camp, provide a link to a video showcasing your skills or interests.
16. **Confirmation Message**: A message to confirm your registration and any other relevant details.
17. **Confirmation Date**: Enter the date you confirmed your registration.
18. **Confirmation Time**: Enter the time you confirmed your registration.
19. **Confirmation Location**: Enter the location where you confirmed your registration.
20. **Confirmation Attachments**: Attach any necessary attachments or documents to confirm your registration.
21. **Confirmation Comments**: Any other comments or information you'd like to include to confirm your registration.
22. **Confirmation Signature**: Sign this field to confirm your registration.
23. **Confirmation Other Details**: If necessary, provide any additional details not captured in the previous fields.
24. **Confirmation Other Comments**: Any other comments or information you'd like to include to confirm your registration.
