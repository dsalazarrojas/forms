# Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the enrollment of children in the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form title.
2. Fill in the required information of the parent or guardian.
3. Fill in the emergency contact information.
4. Fill in medical conditions, medical history, allergies, and medical history and medications.
5. Sign as the parent.
6. Confirm the emergency contact and sign the form again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): This field is used to enter a title for the form. Please enter a title that identifies the form and the child enrolled.
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Child First Name** (`child_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name** (`child_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_dob`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the child.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the child.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, note, required): Enter any medical conditions of the child.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required): Enter any medical history of the child.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, note, required): Enter any allergies of the child.
* **Medications** (`medication`, note, required): Enter any medications of the child.
* **Child Medical Record** (`child_medical_record`, text, required): Enter the medical record of the child.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required): Sign as the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Signature** (`emergency_signature`, note, required): Confirm as the emergency contact person.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the submit button to complete the form.

Note: This form is designed to collect all necessary information for the enrollment of children in the summer camp. Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely before submitting the form.
