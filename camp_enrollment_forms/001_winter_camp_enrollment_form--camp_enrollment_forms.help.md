# Winter Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for winter camp enrollment. It's a multi-page form that gathers data from parents and/or guardians for their kids.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form page by page. You will be asked to provide information on each page before moving on to the next.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields. A field is marked as "required" if it's necessary for processing your enrollment.
3. If a field is optional, you can leave it blank if you don't have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect the parent's/guardian's name and contact information.
* **Kid Info** (`kid_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect the child's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Session Info** (`session_info`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the session options you'd like to enroll your child in. You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **Parent Contact** (`parent_contact`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect the parent's/guardian's contact information, such as phone number and/or address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information about your child's needs or requirements.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates and notifications about the camp.
