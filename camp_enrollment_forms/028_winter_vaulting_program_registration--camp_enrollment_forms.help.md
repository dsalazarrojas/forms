# Winter Vaulting Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who want to register for the Winter Vaulting Program. It helps us to collect necessary information from participants to ensure their safety and successful participation in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information in the first field. This is a required field.
2. Provide emergency contact information in the second field. This is optional, but we encourage you to fill it out in case of any emergencies.
3. Fill in your medical details in the third field. This is also optional, but it's essential for us to know about any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the program.
4. Check the box in the fourth field to indicate that you accept the waiver. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information**: <type:text> <required: true> 
   Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a participant in the program.

### Emergency Contact Information
* **Emergency Contact Information**: <type:text> <required: false> 
   Enter the name and contact information of a person we can contact in case of an emergency.

### Medical Details
* **Medical Details**: <type:text> <required: false> 
   If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the program, please enter them here.

### Waiver Acceptance
* **Waiver Acceptance**: <type:text> <required: true> 
   Check this box to indicate that you accept the waiver and the terms and conditions of the program.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Review all the fields carefully before submitting the form.
