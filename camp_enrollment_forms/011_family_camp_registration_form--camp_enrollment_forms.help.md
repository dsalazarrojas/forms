### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (`First name`, `text`, required: false): 
    A field for the individual's first name.
*   **Last name** (`Last name`, `text`, required: false): 
    A field for the individual's last name.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): 
    Enter the individual's email address.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): 
    Provide the individual's phone number.
*   **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required: false): 
    If the individual has any health concerns or medical conditions that the camp staff should know about, please describe them here.
*   **Emergency contact name** (`Emergency contact name`, `text`, required: false): 
    The name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency contact email** (`Emergency contact email`, `email`, required: false): 
    The email of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency contact phone** (`Emergency contact phone`, `text`, required: false): 
    The phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Previous camping activity** (`Previous camping activity`, `text`, required: false): 
    If the individual has any previous camping or outdoor experience, please describe it here.
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): 
    Use this field to provide any further comments or explanations about the individual.
*   **Questions/Comments** (`Questions/comments`, `text`, required: false): 
    Use this field to ask any questions or provide further comments about the camp or the program.
