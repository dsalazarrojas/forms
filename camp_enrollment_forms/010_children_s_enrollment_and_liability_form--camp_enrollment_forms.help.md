# Children Enrollment And Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children Enrollment And Liability Form is a necessary document that requires the submission of specific information about a child's registration, medical conditions and allergies, and other essential details for their participation in the program. This form ensures that the program staff has the necessary information to provide a safe and secure environment for the child.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure that you have the necessary information about the child, such as their full name, date of birth, and parent/guardian name.
* Complete the registration details, including the child's name, date of birth, and parent/guardian name.
* Provide the emergency contact information, including the name, relationship, and phone number of the emergency contact.
* List any medical conditions or allergies the child may have and any required actions or precautions.
* If you are authorizing someone else to pick up the child, list their names.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Name** (`Child Name`, required): This is the full name of the child enrolling in the program. Please enter the child's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, required): This is the child's date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`Parent or Guardian Name`, required): This is the name of the parent or guardian signing this form.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, required): This is the name, relationship, and contact information of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, required): This is the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions and Allergies** (`Medical Conditions and Allergies`, optional): This is a field for listing any medical conditions or allergies the child may have.
* **Liability Release Agreement** (`Liability Release Agreement`, required): Please select "I agree" if you understand and agree to the program's terms and conditions.
* **Authorized Pickup Persons** (`Authorized Pickup Persons`, required): This is the list of people authorized to pick up the child from the program.
* **Photo Consent** (`Photo Consent`, optional): Select "Yes allow" if you permit the program staff to use images of your child for program materials.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`Special Needs or Accommodations`, optional): This is a field for listing any special needs or accommodations the child may require during the program.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [program contact information].
