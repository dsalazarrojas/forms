# Firearms Suspension Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Firearms Suspension Notification Form is used to report the suspension of firearms to relevant authorities. This form provides a standardized way for firearm owners to notify authorities about the suspension of their firearms, ensuring that the information is accurate and easily tracked.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to submit this form.
2.  Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are completed accurately.
3.  Enter the correct firearm license number.
4.  Select the correct firearm type from the options provided.
5.  Provide a clear reason for the suspension.
6.  Enter the suspension date and the date when the suspension expires.
7.  Enter the firearm owner's first name, last name, and address.
8.  Identify the agency and officer involved in the suspension.
9.  Provide additional details about the incident or reason for suspension.
10.  Enter the date and location of the incident.
11.  Enter the officer's name and badge number.
12.  Select the department of the officer involved.
13.  Select whether the agency or officer was involved in the suspension.
14.  Select whether the suspension was done by an officer or another agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Firearm Suspension Details** (`firearm_suspension_details`): This field should be used to provide a brief description of the firearm suspension.
* **Firearm License Number** (`firearm_license_number`): Enter the license number associated with the suspended firearm.
* **Firearm Type** (`firearm_type`): Select the type of firearm that has been suspended from the options provided.
* **Reason for Suspension** (`reason_for_suspension`): Provide a clear and accurate reason for the firearm suspension.
* **Suspension Date** (`suspension_date`): Enter the date when the firearm was suspended.
* **Suspension Expires** (`suspension_expires`): Enter the date when the suspension is expected to expire.
* **Firearm Owner First Name** (`firearm_owner_first_name`): Enter the first name of the firearm owner.
* **Firearm Owner Last Name** (`firearm_owner_last_name`): Enter the last name of the firearm owner.
* **Firearm Owner Address** (`firearm_owner_address_1`): Enter the address of the firearm owner.
* **Suspended By** (`firearm_suspended_by`): Identify the agency or officer involved in the suspension.
* **Suspension Reason** (`suspension_reason`): Provide additional details about the reason for the suspension.
* **Suspension Notes** (`firearm_suspension_notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the suspension.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the incident or reason for the suspension.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location of the incident or reason for the suspension.
* **Officer** (`incident_officer`): Enter the name of the officer involved.
* **Officer Number** (`incident_officer_number`): Enter the badge number of the officer involved.
* **Officer Department** (`incident_officer_department`): Identify the department of the officer involved.
* **Agency** (`firearm_suspended_by_agency`): Select whether the agency was involved in the suspension.
* **Suspended By Officer** (`firearm_suspended_by_officer`): Select whether an officer was involved in the suspension.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   Review the form before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled in.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
