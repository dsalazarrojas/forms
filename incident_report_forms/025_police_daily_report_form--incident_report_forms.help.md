# Police Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Daily Report Form is used to collect and document information about incidents reported to the police department. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and facilitating efficient investigation and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the report date in the "Report Date" field.
2.  Fill in the "Reported By" field with the name of the person reporting the incident.
3.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Incident Type" field, depending on the type of incident reported.
4.  Provide a description of the incident in the "Description" field.
5.  Enter the location where the incident occurred.
6.  Enter the time the incident was reported.
7.  Select the current incident status ("Active" or "Inactive").
8.  Enter any additional details about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
9.  If applicable, enter a case number.
10.  Select or enter the category of the incident (e.g., theft, assault, etc.).
11.  If the incident category is not listed, enter the category in the "Other Incident Category" field.
12.  Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
13.  Enter the name of the suspect.
14.  Enter the address of the suspect.
15.  Enter the date of birth of the suspect.
16.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the suspect's race.
17.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the suspect's gender.
18.  Enter the age of the suspect.
19.  Enter the height of the suspect.
20.  Enter the weight of the suspect.
21.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the suspect's eye color.
22.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the suspect's hair color.
23.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the suspect's skin color.
24.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the suspect's eyewear.
25.  Enter the name of the officer handling the case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the incident was reported.
*   **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, required/optional): Fill in the name of the person reporting the incident.
*   **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on the type of incident reported.
*   **Description** (`description`, select_one, required/optional): Provide a description of the incident.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Reported At** (`reported_at`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the incident was reported.
*   **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the current incident status ("Active" or "Inactive").
*   **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the incident.
*   **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter a case number.
*   **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select or enter the category of the incident (e.g., theft, assault, etc.).
*   **Other Incident Category** (`incident_category_other`, text, required/optional): If the incident category is not listed, enter the category in this field.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the incident.
*   **Suspect Name** (`suspect_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the suspect.
*   **Suspect Address** (`suspect_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the suspect.
*   **Suspect Date of Birth** (`suspect_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the suspect.
*   **Suspect Race** (`suspect_race`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on the suspect's race.
*   **Suspect Gender** (`suspect_gender`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on the suspect's gender.
*   **Suspect Age** (`suspect_age`, number, required/optional): Enter the age of the suspect.
*   **Suspect Height** (`suspect_height`, number, required/optional): Enter the height of the suspect.
*   **Suspect Weight** (`suspect_weight`, number, required/optional): Enter the weight of the suspect.
*   **Suspect Eye Color** (`suspect_eye_color`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on the suspect's eye color.
*   **Suspect Hair Color** (`suspect_hair_color`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on the suspect's hair color.
*   **Suspect Skin Color** (`suspect_skin_color`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on the suspect's skin color.
*   **Suspect Eyewear** (`suspect_eyewear`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on the suspect's eyewear.
*   **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the officer handling the case.

## Tips

*   Always ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer to the guidelines and instructions above.
*   Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
*   If you need assistance with completing the form, contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a supervisor.
