# Workplace Safety Incident Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Incident Discharge Form is a document used to document and report workplace incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the incident and gather all necessary information before filling out this form.
2. Ensure that you have reviewed and understood the company's incident reporting policies and procedures.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely, including providing detailed information about the incident.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have reviewed and filled out the form accurately.
