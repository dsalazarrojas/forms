# Healthcare Adverse Event Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to report incidents in a healthcare setting that could have adverse effects on patients. This includes reporting events that have actually occurred or near misses, which could potentially lead to harm or near misses. The form collects information about the incident, including the patient's name, date of birth, date and time of the incident, type of incident, location, description of the incident, and any actions taken or outcomes of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Provide the patient's name.
2.  Enter the patient's date of birth.
3.  Report the date and time of the incident.
4.  Select the type of incident (Adverse Event, Near Miss, or Unanticipated Event).
5.  Enter the location of the incident.
6.  Describe the incident in detail.
7.  Report the cause of the incident.
8.  Report any actions taken as a result of the incident.
9.  Report the patient's current status.
10. Report the outcomes of the incident.
11. Enter any notes regarding the incident outcomes.
12. Report the incident review status.
13. Report the follow-up date and time for the incident.
14. Report the follow-up status.
15. Enter the name and role of the person submitting the report.
16. Enter the name and note of the reviewer.
17. Enter the incident ID (if available).
18. Enter the incident version (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **incident_report_form**: The title of the form, which indicates that this is an incident report form.
*   **patient_name**: The name of the patient involved in the incident.
*   **patient_dob**: The patient's date of birth.
*   **event_date**: The date and time of the incident.
*   **incident_report_type**: The type of incident (Adverse Event, Near Miss, or Unanticipated Event).
*   **report_date**: The date the report was completed.
*   **report_time**: The time the report was completed.
*   **location**: The location where the incident occurred.
*   **event_description**: A detailed description of the incident.
*   **incident_cause**: The cause of the incident.
*   **action_taken**: Any actions taken as a result of the incident.
*   **patient_status**: The patient's current status.
*   **incident_outcomes**: The outcomes of the incident.
*   **incident_outcome_details**: Additional details about the incident outcomes.
*   **incident_outcome_notes**: Any notes regarding the incident outcomes.
*   **incident_review_status**: The current review status of the incident.
*   **follow_up_date**: The follow-up date and time for the incident.
*   **follow_up_time**: The follow-up time for the incident.
*   **follow_up_status**: The follow-up status of the incident.
*   **submitter**: The name of the person submitting the report.
*   **submitter_role**: The role of the person submitting the report.
*   **reviewer**: The name of the person reviewing the report.
*   **reviewer_note**: Any notes from the reviewer.
*   **incident_id**: The ID of the incident (if available).
*   **incident_version**: The version of the incident (if applicable).

## Tips

*   Ensure that all relevant fields are filled out to provide a thorough report.
*   Please note that not all fields are required; however, it is essential to fill out all applicable fields.
*   If a field is not applicable to the incident, please leave it blank.
*   Ensure that the date and time fields are formatted correctly.
*   If you are unsure about a field or section, please consult with your supervisor or the quality management team.
*   This form is for reporting incidents that could have adverse effects on patients; if the incident is minor or routine, do not submit it using this form.
*   This form is for internal reporting and quality management purposes; do not submit it to external parties unless explicitly instructed to do so.
