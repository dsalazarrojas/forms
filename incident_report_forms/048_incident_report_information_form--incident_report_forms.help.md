# Incident Report Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Report Information Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect and record detailed information about incidents that have occurred. The form helps in maintaining accurate and organized records, facilitates incident management, and enables swift decision-making.
## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: First Step
* **first_step**: A brief description of the incident.
*   This field is not required, but it's a good opportunity to provide a concise overview of the incident.

### Step 2: Incident Date
* **incident_date**: Date the incident occurred or is expected to occur.
*   Enter the date in the correct format, using the calendar or manually inputting the date.

### Step 3: Incident Location
* **incident_location**: The location where the incident occurred or is expected to occur.
*   Provide the exact location where the incident took place or will take place.

### Step 4: Incident Time
* **incident_time**: The time the incident occurred or is expected to occur.
*   Enter the time in the correct format, using the clock icon to select the time.

### Step 5: Incident Description
* **incident_description**: A detailed description of the incident.
*   Provide a clear and concise description of what happened, including any relevant details.

### Step 6: Incident Reporter
* **incident_reporter**: The person reporting the incident.
*   Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.

### Step 7: Incident Reporter Contact
* **incident_reporter_contact**: Contact information for the incident reporter.
*   Provide a phone number or email address for follow-up communication.

### Step 8: Incident Reporter Department
* **incident_reporter_department**: The department the incident reporter belongs to.
*   Enter the department the reporter is associated with.

### Step 9: Incident Reporter Title
* **incident_reporter_title**: Title of the incident reporter.
*   Enter the title or position of the reporter.

### Step 10: Incident Reporter Relationship
* **incident_reporter_relationship**: Relationship between the reporter and the incident.
*   Describe the nature of the reporter's relationship with the incident.

### Step 11: Incident Status
* **incident_status**: Select the current status of the incident.
*   Choose from the provided options, such as "Incident Status" or "Other" and provide a description.

### Step 12: Incident Status Notes
* **incident_status_notes**: Additional notes about the incident status.
*   Enter any additional comments about the incident status.

### Step 13: Reporting Team
* **incident_reporting_team**: Select the team responsible for the incident.
*   Choose from the provided options, such as Team 1, Team 2, or Team 3.

### Step 14: Reporting Team Note
* **incident_reporting_team_note**: Additional notes about the reporting team.
*   Enter any additional comments about the reporting team.

### Step 15: Reporting Team Leader
* **incident_reporting_team_leader**: Contact information for the team leader.
*   Enter the title or name of the team leader and their contact details.

### Step 16: Reporting Team Leader Title
* **incident_reporting_team_leader_title**: Title or position of the team leader.
*   Enter the title or position of the team leader.

### Step 17: Reporting Team Leader Contact
* **incident_reporting_team_leader_contact**: Contact information for the team leader.
*   Enter the contact details for the team leader.

### Step 18: Reporting Team Leader Relationship
* **incident_reporting_team_leader_relationship**: Relationship between the team leader and the incident.
*   Describe the nature of the team leader's relationship with the incident.

### Step 19: Reporting Team Leader Department
* **incident_reporting_team_leader_department**: Department the team leader belongs to.
*   Enter the department the team leader is associated with.

### Step 20: Reporting Team
* **incident_reporting_team**: Select the team members involved.
*   Choose from the provided options, such as Team 1, Team 2, or Team 3.

### Step 21: Reporting Note
* **incident_reporting_note**: Additional notes about the reporting process.
*   Enter any additional comments about the reporting process.

### Step 22: Reporting Date
* **incident_reporting_date**: Date of the report.
*   Enter the date the report was submitted.

# Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information for accuracy and completeness.
* Use the provided options carefully and select the most relevant ones.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the relevant section above for guidance.
