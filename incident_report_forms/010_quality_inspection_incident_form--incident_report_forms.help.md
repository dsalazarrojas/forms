# Quality Inspection Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Inspection Incident Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur during quality inspections. It helps in documenting incidents, improving transparency and accountability, and enabling swift action to be taken when necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps:

1. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields, respectively.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
3. Specify the type of incident by selecting the corresponding option from the "Incident Type" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the category of the incident from the "Incident Category" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the location where the incident took place in the "Incident Location" field.
6. Provide the name and email address of the person reporting the incident in the "Contact Name" and "Contact Email" fields.
7. If applicable, upload an image or video of the incident in the "Incident Image" or "Incident Video" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred. This will help in tracking the history of incidents over time.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident occurred. This will help in scheduling any necessary actions or follow-ups.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Describe the location where the incident took place. This could be a room, floor, or specific area within a building.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed account of what happened. Be as descriptive as possible to help in understanding the incident better.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of incident from the dropdown menu. This could be a "Quality Control" or "Material Defect" incident, depending on the context.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, select one, optional): Choose the category of the incident from the dropdown menu. This could be "Product", "Process", or "Equipment" related.
* **Report By** (`report_by`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person reporting the incident. This ensures accountability and helps in tracking who reported the incident.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person involved in the incident or the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person involved in the incident or the person reporting the incident. This helps in getting in touch with them for further clarification or action.
* **Incident Image** (`incident_image`, note, optional): Upload an image that supports your incident report. This could be a photo or screenshot related to the incident.
* **Incident Video** (`incident_video`, note, optional): Upload a video that supports your incident report. This could be a recording related to the incident.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that you're uploading relevant images or videos that are clear and relevant to the incident. Avoid uploading unnecessary files that could compromise the integrity of the incident report.
