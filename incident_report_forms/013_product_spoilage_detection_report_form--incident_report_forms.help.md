# Product Prophets Detection Report form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to report any incidents related to spoilage or detection of products. It helps gather essential information for the incident manager to investigate and take appropriate actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as per the instructions below.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
3. Click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Spoilage detection** (product_prophets_detection)
(`product_prophets_detection`, `text`, required)
Enter a brief description of the spoilage or detection incident, including any details about the products involved.

### **Date** (date)
(`date`, `date`, required)
Enter the date the incident occurred.

### **Time** (time)
(`time`, `time`, required)
Enter the time the incident occurred.

### **Incident location** (incident_location)
(`text`, `required`)
Enter the location where the incident occurred.

### **Product description** (product_description)
(`text`, `optional`)
Enter a detailed description of the product involved, if applicable.

### **Product serial number** (product_serial_number)
(`text`, `optional`)
Enter the product's serial number, if available.

### **Product weight** (product_weight)
(`number`, `optional`)
Enter the weight of the product, if applicable.

### **Spoilage reason** (spoilage_reason)
(`text`, `optional`)
Enter the reason for the spoilage or detection.

### **Prophets evidence** (evidence)
(`note`, `optional`)
Upload any supporting evidence for the incident, such as photos or documents.

### **Incident manager** (incident_manager)
(`text`, `optional`)
Enter the name of the person in charge of managing the incident.

### **Incident date** (incident_date)
(`text`, `optional`)
Enter the date of the incident (if different from the incident date field).

### **Incident time** (incident_time)
(`text`, `optional`)
Enter the time of the incident (if different from the time field).

Please note that this form is only for reporting spoilage or detection incidents. If you have any issues with other types of incidents, please report them through a different channel.
