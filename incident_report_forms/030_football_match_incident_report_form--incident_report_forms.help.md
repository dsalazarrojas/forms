# Football Match Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Football Match Incident Report Form is used to document incidents that occur during football matches, such as injuries, red cards, or other notable events. This form helps to gather accurate information for review, investigation, and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the event where the incident occurred (if known).
2. Select the time of the incident.
3. Enter the name of the team involved in the incident.
4. Identify the team's manager.
5. Select the type of incident (e.g., Red card, Yellow card, Injury).
6. Describe the incident in detail (e.g., explain what happened).
7. Enter the name of the witness (if applicable).
8. Identify the player injured (if applicable).
9. Enter the name and ID of the referee who witnessed the incident.
10. Enter the name and address of the stadium where the incident occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Event Date
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event where the incident occurred.

### 2. Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select the time of the incident.

### 3. Team Name
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the team involved in the incident.

### 4. Team Manager
* **Team Manager** (`team_manager`, text, required: false): Identify the team's manager.

### 5. Incident Type
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of incident (e.g., Red card, Yellow card, Injury).

### 6. Incident Details
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, note, required: false): Describe the incident in detail.

### 7. Witness
* **Witness** (`witness`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the witness (if applicable).

### 8. Player Injured
* **Player Injured** (`player_injured`, select_one, required: false): Identify the player involved in the incident.

### 9. Referee Name
* **Referee Name** (`referee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the referee who witnessed the incident.

### 10. Referee ID
* **Referee ID** (`referee_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the referee who witnessed the incident.

### 11. Stadium Name
* **Stadium Name** (`stadium_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the stadium where the incident occurred.

### 12. Stadium Address
* **Stadium Address** (`stadium_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the stadium.

### 13. Venue
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, required: false): Enter the venue information.

### 14. Incident Description
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the incident.
