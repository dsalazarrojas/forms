# Crash Risk Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crash Risk Analysis Form is a tool used to collect and analyze data on potential crash risks. This form is intended for use by individuals who are reporting a potentially hazardous situation on the road, such as a near-miss incident or a crash report.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
2. Complete each field as directed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Crash Risk Factors
* **Crash Risk Factors** (`crash_risk_factors`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is a text area where you can describe the crash risk factors that were present during the incident. Please provide a detailed description of the conditions that contributed to the potential crash.

### 2. Crash Risk Factors
* **Crash Risk Factors** (`crash_risk_factors`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ This field allows you to select multiple options regarding the presence or absence of crash risk factors. Choose all that apply.
		+ Yes - A crash risk factor was present.
		- No - No crash risk factors were present.

### 3. Crash Risk Analysis Form
* **Crash Risk Analysis Form** (`crash_risk_analysis_form_3`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is a text area for additional comments or notes. Please use this space to provide any further information that is not covered in the other fields.

### 4. Crash Probability
* **Crash Probability** (`crash_probability`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ This field asks you to select the likelihood of a crash occurring. Choose only one of the options provided.

## Tips
- Please be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure that the data is useful for analysis.
- If you are unsure about any information or have any questions, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all fields are complete and accurate.
- If you have already submitted a crash report, please do not fill out this form unless there is new or updated information to be included.
