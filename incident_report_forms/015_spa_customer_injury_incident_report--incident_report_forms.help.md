# spa_customer_injury_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents involving customers that result in injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" field.
2. Provide details about the customer in the "Customer Details" field.
3. Describe the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Select the type of incident that occurred from the "Incident Type" field.
5. Enter any follow-up actions taken or planned in the "Follow-up Actions" field.
6. Obtain a signature from the customer in the "Customer Signature" field.
7. Obtain a signature from a staff member in the "Staff Signature" field.
8. (Optional) Enter the date and reviewer of the reviewed document in the "Review Date" and "Reviewed By" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): Enter any relevant details about the customer.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., fall, slip, other).
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required): Have the customer sign this form to acknowledge the incident.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, text, required): A staff member must sign this form to approve or acknowledge the incident.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, text, required): List any follow-up actions taken or planned.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): (Optional) Enter the date the document was reviewed.
* **Reviewed By** (`review_by`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the reviewer's name or title.
