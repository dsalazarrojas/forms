# Vessel Impact Monitoring Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to monitor the impact of incidents on vessels, providing critical information for analysis, reporting, and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vessel Impact Monitoring Log Form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the required fields.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to provide detailed information about the incident cause and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vessel Impact Monitoring Log Form** (`vessel_impact_monitoring_log_form`, text, optional): This field is a placeholder for the form title and should be completed with a descriptive name for the form instance.
* **Vessel Status** (`vessel_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the vessel:
	+ Active: The vessel is operational and functioning normally.
	+ Inactive: The vessel is not currently in operation or is out of service.
	+ Unknown: The vessel's status is unknown or cannot be determined.
* **Vessel Type** (`vessel_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of vessel:
	+ Vessel: A traditional vessel or boat.
	+ Other: A vessel type not listed here (please describe in the `incident_cause` field).
	+ Both: The vessel is both a traditional vessel and another type.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, required): Describe the cause of the incident, providing as much detail as possible. This field is where you should provide information about any unusual or extraordinary events that affected the vessel.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred, providing the exact coordinates or a detailed description.

## Tips
- Make sure to be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
- If unsure about any field, use your best judgment or ask for help.
- Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure consistency and accuracy.
