# Cybersecurity Alert Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and release cybersecurity incidents to the relevant teams and stakeholders. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information about the incident to ensure proper incident management and response.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Network, System, Application, or Other).
2. Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
3. Provide a brief summary of the incident.
4. Add any additional details about the incident.
5. Specify the assets affected by the incident.
6. Rate the incident's impact on your organization.
7. Select the severity of the incident.
8. Specify the status of the incident (e.g., Active, Inactive).
9. Choose the team assigned to the incident (if applicable).
10. Choose the priority level of the incident (if applicable).
11. Enter your name and any additional notes about the incident.
12. Enter the time it took the team to respond to the incident.
13. Specify the status of the team's response (e.g., Active, Inactive).
14. Enter any notes from stakeholders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Network, System, Application, or Other).
* **Incident date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident summary**: Provide a brief summary of the incident.
* **Incident details**: Add any additional details about the incident.
* **Affected assets**: Specify the assets affected by the incident.
* **Incident impact**: Rate the incident's impact on your organization.
* **Incident severity**: Select the severity of the incident (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Critical).
* **Incident status**: Specify the status of the incident (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Assigned team**: Choose the team assigned to the incident (if applicable).
* **Priority level**: Choose the priority level of the incident (if applicable).
* **Incident reporter**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Reporter notes**: Add any additional notes about the incident.
* **Team response time**: Enter the time it took the team to respond to the incident.
* **Team response status**: Specify the status of the team's response (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Stakeholder notes**: Enter any notes from stakeholders.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the incident.
