# Calamity Response Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Calamity Response Assessment form is a crucial tool for collecting and tracking information about calamities, facilitating effective response and recovery efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the Reporter's details: Provide the name, job title, and organization of the person reporting the calamity.
2. Enter calamity details: Enter the date, time, location, and type of calamity that occurred.
3. Report the severity and impact: Select the severity level, number of casualties, and extent of property damage.
4. Describe response actions and ongoing threats: Provide information on emergency services deployed and any ongoing threats.
5. Summarize the situation and request additional resources: Enter a brief summary of the calamity and any additional resources needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter Name (1)**: The name of the person reporting the calamity.
* **Reporter Title (2)**: The job title or role of the person reporting the calamity. This can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Organization (3)**: The company or agency the person reporting the calamity is from.
* **Contact Email (4)**: The email address of the person reporting the calamity, used for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Phone (5)**: The phone number of the person reporting the calamity, used for emergency contact.
* **Incident Date (6)**: The date the calamity occurred.
* **Incident Time (7)**: The time the calamity occurred. This is optional, but helps with time-stamped reporting.
* **Incident Location (8)**: The specific location where the calamity occurred.
* **Calamity Type (9)**: The type of calamity that occurred. Select from the list of options.
* **Severity Level (10)**: The severity level of the calamity, from Minor to Catastrophic.
* **Casualties Reported (11)**: The number of injuries or fatalities reported.
* **People Affected (12)**: The approximate number of people impacted by the calamity.
* **Property Damage (13)**: The extent of property damage caused by the calamity.
* **Infrastructure Damage (14)**: Check any infrastructure that has been damaged, such as roads or utilities.
* **Emergency Services Deployed (15)**: List any emergency services that have been deployed to the scene.
* **Evacuation Status (16)**: Indicate whether an evacuation was ordered.
* **Shelters Established (17)**: If emergency shelters have been set up.
* **Immediate Needs (18)**: Check what immediate needs are required, such as food, water, or search and rescue.
* **Response Actions Taken (19)**: Describe any response actions taken so far.
* **Ongoing Threats (20)**: List any ongoing dangers that continue to pose a risk.
* **Resources Requested (21)**: Describe any additional resources needed.
* **Assessment Summary (22)**: Summarize the overall situation.
* **Follow-up Required (23)**: If further assessment is needed, indicate within how many hours.
* **Additional Notes (24)**: Leave any other relevant information or observations.

Tips:
- Be as detailed as possible when providing information.
- Ensure accuracy in all entries, as this data informs response efforts.
- If unsure about any field, contact your emergency management team for guidance.
