# DLP Incident Response Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track DLP incidents, ensuring that all relevant details are collected and recorded in a consistent and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident from the drop-down menu. This will help categorize the incident and aid in response efforts.
2. Enter **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details or background information.
3. **Enter Incident Date**: Type in the date of the incident.
4. **Enter Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
5. **Enter Incident Reported By**: Identify the person who reported the incident.
6. **Enter Incident Reported To**: Identify the person who received the report.
7. **Enter Incident Responded By**: Identify the person who responded to the incident.
8. **Enter Incident Responded To**: Identify the person who was responded to.
9. **Incident Closed**: Enter the date the incident was closed.
10. **Enter Incident Closed By**: Identify the person who closed the incident.
11. **Incident Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
12. **Incident Note Level**: Select the severity level of the incident from the options provided.
13. **Incident Response Team Note**: Add any relevant notes for the incident response team.
