# crowdstrike_incident_response_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document information about incidents that need to be addressed. This form will be used by incident response teams to quickly gather the essential details of an incident and categorize it for efficient response and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief summary of the incident in the **Incident Summary** field.
2.  Enter the date the incident occurred in the **Incident Date** field.
3.  Select the type of incident in the **Incident Type** field.
4.  Enter a detailed description of the incident in the **Incident Description** field.
5.  Indicate the impact of the incident in the **Impact** field.
6.  List the affected assets in the **Affected Assets** field.
7.  Select the status of the incident in the **Incident Status** field.
8.  Enter any additional notes about the incident in the **Notes** field.
9.  Provide your contact information in the **Contact Information** field.
10. Describe the incident response in the **Incident Response** field.
11. Select the status of the incident response in the **Incident Response Status** field.
12. Categorize the incident based on the impact and priority in the **Incident Classification** and **Incident Priority** fields.
13. Select the incident category and subcategory from the options provided in the **Incident Category**, **Incident Classification Category**, and **Incident Classification Subcategory** fields.
14. Classify the assets affected by the incident in the **Asset Classification** and **Asset Classification Subcategory** fields.
15. Identify the cause of the incident in the **Incident Cause** field.
16. Classify the incident cause in the **Incident Cause Classification** field.
17. Select the asset classification category and subcategory from the options provided in the **Asset Classification Category** and **Asset Classification Subcategory** fields.
18. If necessary, provide an incident response plan and select its status in the **Incident Response Plan** and **Incident Response Plan Status** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Summary**: A brief summary of the incident.
    *   **Incident Date**: Date the incident occurred.
    *   **Incident Type**: The type of incident (e.g., Phishing, Malware, Ransomware).
    *   **Incident Description**: A detailed description of the incident.
    *   **Impact**: The impact of the incident (High, Medium, Low).
    *   **Affected Assets**: The assets affected by the incident.
    *   **Incident Status**: The status of the incident (Open, Closed).
    *   **Notes**: Additional notes about the incident.
    *   **Contact Information**: Your contact information.
    *   **Incident Response**: A description of the incident response.
    *   **Incident Response Status**: The status of the incident response (In Progress, Completed).
    *   **Incident Classification**: The classification of the incident based on the impact and priority.
    *   **Incident Priority**: The priority of the incident.
    *   **Incident Category**: The category of the incident.
    *   **Incident Classification Category**: The category of the incident classification.
    *   **Incident Classification Subcategory**: The subcategory of the incident classification.
    *   **Asset Classification**: The classification of the affected assets.
    *   **Asset Classification Subcategory**: The subcategory of the affected assets.
    *   **Incident Cause**: The cause of the incident.
    *   **Incident Cause Classification**: The classification of the incident cause.
    *   **Incident Cause Subcategory**: The subcategory of the incident cause.
    *   **Asset Classification Category**: The category of the affected assets.
    *   **Asset Classification Subcategory**: The subcategory of the affected assets.
    *   **Incident Response Plan**: An incident response plan, if necessary.
    *   **Incident Response Plan Status**: The status of the incident response plan.
    *   **Incident Response Plan Status**: The status of the incident response plan.
