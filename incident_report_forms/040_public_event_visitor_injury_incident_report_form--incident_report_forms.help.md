# Public Event Visitor Injury Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report injuries or incidents that occurred during a public event. It collects information about the event, participants involved, and witness details. This form is essential in gathering necessary information for follow-up actions and incident reporting.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill out the form as soon as possible after the incident has occurred.**
2.  **Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.**
3.  **If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or event coordinator for guidance.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title**: Enter a brief description of the event, such as the event name or title.
* **Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred. If the incident occurred over multiple days, please enter the date the incident was first reported.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred, including any specific details such as room number or area.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event that was occurring at the time of the incident.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Witness Count**: Enter the number of witnesses who saw or reported the incident.
* **Witness Names**: List the names of the witnesses.
* **Witness Contacts**: Enter any contact information for the witnesses, such as phone numbers or emails.
* **Witness Relationships**: Briefly describe the relationship between the witness and the participant(s) involved.
* **Injured Participant Count**: Enter the number of participants involved in the incident.
* **Injured Participants**: List the names of the participants involved in the incident.
* **Injured Participant Contacts**: Enter any contact information for the injured participants, such as phone numbers or emails.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Treatment Administered**: Enter any medical treatment or care that was provided to the injured participants.
* **Follow-Up Plan**: Describe any planned follow-up actions or meetings to discuss the incident.
* **Follow-Up Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the follow-up plans.
* **Submitting Officer**: Select the officer responsible for submitting the report.
* **Submit Time**: Enter the time the report was submitted.
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **Event ID**: Enter the ID number assigned to the event.
* **Location ID**: Enter the ID number assigned to the location.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name as the person submitting the report.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Organization**: Enter your organization or affiliation.

Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, and if you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or event coordinator for guidance.
