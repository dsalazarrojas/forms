# Home Damage Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents related to home damage caused by accidents, weather conditions, or other events.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the date and time of the incident using the date and time fields.
2. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the **Incident Details** field.
3. Specify which part of your home was damaged in the **Damaged Property** field (e.g., kitchen, bedroom, living room, bathroom, or yard).
4. Estimate the damage cost using the **Damage Amount** field (this field is optional).
5. If the damage is related to a specific category (e.g., fire, water, storm, burglary, or other), select the corresponding category in the **Incident Category** field (this field is optional).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred. This is a required field.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time at which the incident occurred. This is a required field.
* **Incident Details**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident. Be as thorough as possible to ensure that your report is accurate.
* **Damaged Property**: Select the area of your home that was damaged (e.g., kitchen, bedroom, living room, bathroom, or yard).
* **Damage Description**: Enter a detailed description of the damage. This can include what happened, how it happened, and any relevant context.
* **Damage Amount**: Estimate the cost of the damage, if known. This is an optional field.
* **Damage Cost**: This field is currently not used in the explanation, as the form is asking for estimated damage amount, but the field is named "damage_cost".
* **Incident Category**: Select the category under which the incident falls (e.g., fire, water, storm, burglary, or other). This field is optional.
## Tips
* Please ensure that you have already reported this incident in the past before filling out this form again. If you're unsure, you can check with our support team.
* If you're unsure about any field or have further questions, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
* Fill out this form as thoroughly as possible to ensure that the incident is properly documented and addressed.
