# Distribution Center Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that have occurred at a distribution center. Incidents can include fires, equipment malfunctions, personnel accidents, and other notable events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the distribution center location where the incident occurred.
2. Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
3. Select the type of incident that took place (Fire, Equipment, or Personnel).
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details.
5. Add any attachments related to the incident, such as photos or videos.
6. Identify the employee involved, including their name and ID number.
7. Identify the reporting officer, including their name and ID number.
8. Enter the date and time of the report.
9. Describe any injuries or safety incidents that occurred.
10. Report any damage or loss resulting from the incident.
11. Identify any vehicles involved in the incident, if applicable.
12. List the employees involved, if applicable.
13. Add any additional details or descriptions of the incident, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distribution Center Location** (`distribution_center_location`): Enter the name of the distribution center where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time the incident occurred in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select the type of incident that occurred (Fire, Equipment, or Personnel).
* **Attachments** (`attachments`): Attach any relevant photos, videos, or other supporting documents to the incident report.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter the ID number of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Reporting Officer Name** (`reporting_officer_name`): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporting Officer ID** (`reporting_officer_id`): Enter the ID number of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`): Enter the date the incident was reported in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Reporting Time** (`reporting_time`): Enter the time the incident was reported in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `incident_description2`, `incident_description3`, `incident_description4`, `incident_description5`, `incident_description6`, `incident_description7`): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Safety Incidents** (`incident_safety_incidents`): Describe any safety incidents or injuries that occurred.
* **Incident Injuries** (`incident_injuries`): Describe any injuries or harm suffered as a result of the incident.
* **Incident Damage** (`incident_damage`): Report any damage or loss resulting from the incident.
* **Vehicles Involved** (`incident_vehicles_involved`): Identify any vehicles involved in the incident, if applicable.
* **Employees Involved** (`incident_employees_involved`): List the employees involved in the incident, if applicable.
