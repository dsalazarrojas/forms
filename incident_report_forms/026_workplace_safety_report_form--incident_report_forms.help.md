# Workplace Safety Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Report Form is designed to gather information related to workplace safety incidents, such as accidents, near-miss events, or any other incidents that may pose a risk to employees' health and well-being. This form helps in investigating, documenting, and preventing future occurrences, promoting a safer working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and effectively:

1.  Fill in the "Reporting Employee" field with the name of the employee reporting the incident.
2.  Enter the date of the incident in the "Date of Incident" field. Make sure to use the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field. Be as specific as possible about what happened, including any injuries or damages suffered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reporting Employee
**Reporting Employee** (`reporting_employee`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for the name of the employee who is reporting the incident.

### Date of Incident
**Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident using the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### Incident Description
**Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident, including any injuries or damages suffered.
