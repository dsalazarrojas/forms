# loading_dock_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "loading_dock_incident_form" is a form used for reporting incidents that occur at loading docks, such as accidents, injuries, or other notable events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all applicable reporting areas where the incident occurred.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM, respectively.
3. Briefly describe what happened during the incident.
4. Specify the cause of the incident, if known.
5. Provide the location where the incident took place.
6. (Optional) Attach any relevant images or photos of the incident.
7. Enter the name and phone number of the person reporting the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reporting Area** (optional): 
    Select one or multiple areas from where the incident may have occurred, such as a dock or a nearby warehouse. This helps us understand the context of the incident.
- **Incident Date** (optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Incident Time** (optional): Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Incident Description** (optional): Briefly describe what happened during the incident. Be as detailed as possible.
- **Cause of Incident** (optional): If known, specify the cause or reason behind the incident.
- **Incident Location** (optional): Provide the exact location where the incident took place.
- **Incident Image** (optional): Attach any relevant images or photos of the incident. This can be helpful in understanding the situation.
- **Reporter Name** (optional): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
- **Reporter Phone** (optional): Enter the phone number of the person reporting the incident.
