# structural_failure_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The structural_failure_incident_form is designed to collect information about structural failure incidents that have occurred. This information is used to help identify patterns and causes of structural failures, and to inform decision-making and corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form as instructed below.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
3. Enter information according to your knowledge and experience related to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Structural Reporter** (`incident_reporter`): Enter your name and title or position.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Structural Failure Description** (`structural_failure_description`): Enter a detailed description of the structural failure.
* **Cause** (`cause`): Enter the root cause of the failure.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`): Enter the root cause of the failure.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`): Enter any corrective actions taken or planned to address the failure (optional).
* **Responsible Party** (`responsible_party`): Enter the name of the person or team responsible for the failure.
* **Structural Failure Type** (`structural_failure_type`): Select the type of structural failure that occurred (structural system failure, equipment failure, or other).
* **Equipment Failed** (`equipment_failed`): Enter a description of the equipment that failed (optional).
* **Structural Failure Location** (`structural_failure_location`): Enter the location of the structural failure.
* **Reporter Name** (`incident_reporter_name`): Enter your name as the incident reporter.
* **Reporter Email** (`incident_reporter_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Reporter Phone** (`incident_reporter_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`incident_reporter_department`): Select your department (Operations, Maintenance, or Administration).
* **Type** (`incident_type`): Select the type of incident (Structural Failure or Other).
* **Subcategory** (`incident_subcategory`): Select the subcategory of the incident (Safety Inspection, Preventive Maintenance, or Repair).
* **Status** (`incident_status`): Select the status of the incident (Open, Closed, or Under Investigation).
* **Comments** (`incident_comments`): Enter any additional comments related to the incident (optional).
* **Priority** (`incident_priority`): Select the priority level of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Frequency** (`incident_frequency`): Select the frequency of the incident (One-Time, Occasional, or Regular).
* **Reoccurrence** (`incident_reoccurrence`): Select whether the incident will reoccur (True or False).
* **Cause** (`incident_cause`): Enter a description of the cause of the failure (optional).
* **Nature** (`incident_nature`): Enter a description of the nature of the failure (optional).

This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to navigate. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to this guide or ask your supervisor or colleague for assistance.
