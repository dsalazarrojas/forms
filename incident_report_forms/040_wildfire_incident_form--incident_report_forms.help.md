# wildfire_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wildfire_incident_form is used to report and manage wildfires. Fill out this form to provide crucial information about a wildfire incident to help emergency services respond effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the location of the wildfire incident in the **incident_location** field.
2. Select the time the wildfire occurred in the **incident_time** field.
3. Choose the type of wildfire in the **incident_type** field.
4. Enter the size of the wildfire, if applicable, in the **incident_size** field.
5. Select the fire department responsible for responding to the incident in the **fire_department** field.
6. Enter any additional details about the incident in the **incident_details** field.
7. Provide the contact information of the person reporting the incident, including their name, email, and phone number in the **contact_name**, **contact_email**, and **contact_phone** fields.
8. Attach any relevant photos of the incident in the **incident_photo** field.
9. Attach any relevant videos of the incident in the **incident_video** field.
10. Select the response team that will respond to the incident from the **response_team** field.
11. Choose the status of the incident in the **incident_status** field.
12. Select the priority of the incident in the **incident_priority** field.
13. Choose the category of the incident in the **incident_category** field.
14. Select the date the incident occurred in the **incident_date** field.
15. Choose the start time of the incident in the **incident_start_time** field.
16. Choose the end time of the incident, if applicable, in the **incident_end_time** field.
17. Enter the duration of the incident, if applicable, in the **incident_duration** field.
18. Enter the latitude and longitude of the incident location in the **incident_latitude** and **incident_longitude** fields.
19. Enter the elevation of the incident location in the **incident_elevation** field.
20. Enter the altitude of the incident location in the **incident_location_altitude** field.
21. Enter the affected areas of the incident in the **incident_affected_areas** field.
22. Enter any additional information about the incident in the **incident_additional_info** field.
