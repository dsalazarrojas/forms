# Service Interruption Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the documentation and management of service interruptions, enabling swift resolution and analysis of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out this form, please ensure you have all the necessary information about the incident at hand.
- Review each field carefully and enter the relevant details accurately.
- If a field is optional, please do not hesitate to provide additional information if you feel it would be helpful in resolving the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Interruption Description (id: 1, <label>: service_interruption_description, <type>: text, <required>: false)
* Briefly describe the service interruption that occurred, including details about the nature of the interruption and any relevant context.

### Service Type (id: 2, <label>: service_type, <type>: select_one, <required>: false)
* Select the type of service that was interrupted (e.g. electrical, network, server).

### Start Time (id: 3, <label>: start_time, <type>: time, <required>: false)
* Enter the time when the service interruption started.

### End Time (id: 4, <label>: end_time, <type>: time, <required>: false)
* Enter the time when the service interruption ended.

### Duration (id: 5, <label>: duration, <type>: number, <required>: false)
* Enter the length of time the service was interrupted.

### Impact (id: 6, <label>: impact, <type>: select_one, <required>: false)
* Select the impact of the service interruption on your team or organization (e.g. low, medium, high).

### Severity (id: 7, <label>: severity, <type>: select_one, <required>: false)
* Select the severity of the service interruption (e.g. minor, moderate, major).

### Status (id: 8, <label>: status, <type>: select_multiple, <required>: false)
* Choose the current status of the service interruption (e.g. pending, resolved, closed).

### Resolution Time (id: 9, <label>: resolution_time, <type>: number, <required>: false)
* Enter the estimated time required for the service to be restored.

### Incident Description (id: 10, <label>: incident_description, <type>: text, <required>: false)
* Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant events or circumstances leading up to and following the service interruption.

### Root Cause (id: 11, <label>: root_cause, <type>: text, <required>: false)
* Identify the root cause of the service interruption, if known.

### Corrective Action (id: 12, <label>: corrective_action, <type>: text, <required>: false)
* Describe any actions taken to resolve the service interruption, including any steps or measures taken to prevent future occurrences. 

## Tips
* If you're unsure about any field or its relevance, please consult with your team or supervisor.
* Ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
