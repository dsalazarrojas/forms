# Workplace Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Incident Form is used to report incidents that have occurred while an employee is on the job. This includes injuries, illnesses, or other work-related incidents. Reporting incidents helps to ensure that necessary actions are taken to prevent similar incidents in the future and to support employee health and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Incident Details page (Page 1) with the date and time of the incident, as well as a brief description of what happened.
2. On the Location page (Page 2), provide the location where the incident occurred.
3. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Injury, Illness, Other).
4. On the Action Taken page (Page 3), describe any actions you took or plan to take in response to the incident.
5. In the Incident Reporting page (Page 4), write any additional comments or notes about the incident.
6. If you are a reporting officer, indicate whether you are reporting the incident (True or False).
7. Finally, provide any contact details if you have any information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (Page 1, `incident_details`):
  + A text field for entering a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Location** (Page 2, `location`):
  + A text field for entering the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (Page 3, `incident_type`):
  + A dropdown menu to select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Injury, Illness, Other).
* **Incident Date** (Page 4, `incident_date`):
  + A date field for entering the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Page 5, `incident_time`):
  + A time field for entering the time the incident occurred.
* **Location Details** (Page 6, `location_details`):
  + A text field for entering more information about the location where the incident occurred.
* **Cause** (Page 7, `cause`):
  + A text field for entering the cause of the incident.
* **Action Taken** (Page 8, `action_taken`):
  + A text field for entering any actions you took or plan to take in response to the incident.
* **Incident Reporting** (Page 9, `incident_reporting`):
  + A note field for entering any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Reporting Officer** (Page 10, `reporting_officer`):
  + A dropdown menu to select whether you are a reporting officer (True or False).
* **Contact Details** (Page 11, `contact_details`):
  + A text field for entering any contact information, such as phone number or email.
