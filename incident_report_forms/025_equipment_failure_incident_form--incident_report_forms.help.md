# Equipment Failure Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Failure Incident Form is used to report incidents related to equipment failures. This form helps maintenance teams to quickly identify and address the issue, reducing downtime and ensuring that our equipment is back online as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Use the Report Form section to briefly describe the incident.
3. Provide a detailed summary of the incident in the Incident Summary section.
4. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the Incident Date and Incident Time sections.
5. Describe the equipment involved in the equipment description section.
6. Select the type of equipment involved using the Equipment Type dropdown.
7. Provide the location where the incident occurred in the Location section.
8. If necessary, submit additional comments or attachments to support your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form** (Required): Briefly describe the incident in this section. Please include any relevant details such as error messages or symptoms.
* **Incident Summary** (Optional): Provide a detailed summary of the incident, including any steps taken to address the issue.
* **Incident Date** (Required): Enter the date the incident occurred in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Incident Time** (Required): Enter the time the incident occurred in the HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Equipment Description** (Optional): Describe the equipment involved in the incident, including any relevant model numbers or serial numbers.
* **Equipment Type** (Optional): Select the type of equipment involved using the dropdown.
* **Location** (Optional): Provide the location where the incident occurred, including the room or floor number.
* **Submit**: Click submit to review and save your report.
