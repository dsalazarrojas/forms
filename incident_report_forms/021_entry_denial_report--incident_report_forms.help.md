# Entry Denial Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entry Denial Report form is used to document incidents that have been denied or disputed. This form captures essential details about the incident, including the date, time, reason for denial, evidence, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the incident date in the "Incident Date" field.
2. Enter the incident time in the "Incident Time" field.
3. Provide a brief explanation for the reason for denial in the "Reason for Denial" field.
4. If applicable, upload any supporting evidence in the "Evidence Uploaded" field.
5. If necessary, provide more details about the reason for denial in the "Reason for Denial Details" field.
6. Enter the name of the person reporting the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field.
7. Enter the name of the officer reporting the incident in the "Reporting Officer" field.
8. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Reason for Denial** (`reason_for_denial`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for the reason the incident was denied or disputed.
* **Evidence Uploaded** (`evidence_uploaded`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, upload any supporting evidence related to the incident.
* **Reason for Denial Details** (`reason_for_denial_details`, `text`, required: false): If necessary, provide more details about the reason for denial.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the officer reporting the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the incident.

## Tips
* Be concise and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that all required information is included.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid duplicate submissions.
