The purpose of this form is to collect information about damage or issues reported in common areas, such as kitchen, living room, bedroom, etc. This form is used for damage reporting purposes by property managers or maintenance personnel to document and track the damage, which helps in maintaining the building's condition and ensuring prompt action is taken to resolve the issue. This form is likely used for a residential property or a common area within a building. It may not be necessary to fill out this form if the reported issue is already known or has been previously reported.

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

### Damage Report For Common Areas - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form as instructed, providing as much detail as possible about the damage or issue reported.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Damage Report** (`1`, `text`, required): Describe the damage or issue reported in detail.
*  **Damage Location** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Select the common area where the damage or issue was found, such as Kitchen, Living Room, Bedroom, etc.
*  **Date** (`3`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the damage or issue was discovered.
*  **Description** (`4`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the damage or issue, including any other details that may be helpful.
*  **Photos Count** (`5`, `number`, required): Report the number of photos taken of the damage or issue.
*  **Tenant Name** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the tenant who reported the damage or issue.
*  **Tenant Contact** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information for the tenant, such as phone number or email.
*  **Email** (`8`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the property manager or maintenance person who is handling the issue.
*  **Property Manager** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the property manager or maintenance person handling the issue.
*  **Damage Cause** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the damage was caused by the tenant or if it was an accident.
*  **Damage Type** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable types of damage or issue, such as Water, Fire, Wind.
*  **Additional Information** (`12`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful in resolving the issue.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form. The information collected will help in tracking and resolving the reported damage or issue.
