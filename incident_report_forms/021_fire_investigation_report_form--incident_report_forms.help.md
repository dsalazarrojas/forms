# Fire Investigation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire investigation report form is designed to collect information about fire-related incidents, including incident location, date, time, and description. It also captures details about the investigator, witnesses, and suspects, such as their names, departments, and vehicle information. This form helps law enforcement or fire departments to collect and analyze data about fire incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the incident location field (ID 1) with the address or location where the incident occurred.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the incident date field (ID 2).
3. Enter the time of the incident in the incident time field (ID 3).
4. Provide a brief description of the incident in the incident description field (ID 4).
5. Select the fire station that responded to the incident (ID 5).
6. Enter the name of the investigator assigned to the case (ID 6).
7. Select the department of the investigator (ID 7).
8. Enter any witness statements or information (ID 8 and ID 20).
9. Enter the witness's contact information (ID 9).
10. Enter any details about injuries or damage caused by the incident (ID 11 and ID 12).
11. Enter any suspect information (ID 13).
12. Enter a description of the suspect (ID 14).
13. Enter vehicle information, including make, model, and year (ID 15, ID 16, and ID 18).
14. Enter the vehicle license number (ID 19).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident location (ID 1)**: Enter the address or location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident date (ID 2)**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident time (ID 3)**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident description (ID 4)**: Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Fire station (ID 5)**: Select the fire station that responded to the incident.
* **Investigator name (ID 6)**: Enter the name of the investigator assigned to the case.
* **Investigator Department (ID 7)**: Select the department of the investigator.
* **Witness statement (ID 8)**: Enter any witness statements or information.
* **Witness contact info (ID 9)**: Enter the witness's contact information.
* **Witness statements (ID 10)**: Enter any additional witness statements or information.
* **Incident injuries (ID 11)**: Enter any details about injuries caused by the incident.
* **Incident damage (ID 12)**: Enter any details about damage caused by the incident.
* **Suspect info (ID 13)**: Enter any suspect information.
* **Suspect description (ID 14)**: Enter a description of the suspect.
* **Vehicle info (ID 15)**: Enter any vehicle information.
* **Vehicle make (ID 16)**: Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle model (ID 17)**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle year (ID 18)**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle license (ID 19)**: Enter the vehicle license number.
* **Suspect vehicle (ID 21)**: Enter any suspect vehicle information.
* **Suspect vehicle info (ID 22)**: Enter any suspect vehicle details.
* **Suspect vehicle make (ID 23)**: Enter the make of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect vehicle model (ID 24)**: Enter the model of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect vehicle year (ID 25)**: Enter the year of the suspect vehicle.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required" in the form schema and are marked as such in the field-by-field explanation.
