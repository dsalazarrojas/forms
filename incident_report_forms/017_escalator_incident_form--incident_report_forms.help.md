# Escalator Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to report incidents involving escalators, such as accidents or near-misses, to ensure that necessary actions are taken to prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Select the date and time the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" field.
3. Indicate whether the incident involved "Yes" or "No" in the "Type" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the location where the incident occurred in the "Location" field.
5. Enter a detailed account of what happened in the "Details" field.
6. If applicable, upload an image related to the incident in the "Image" field.
7. Provide a statement of what was witnessed, if relevant, in the "Witness Statement" field.
8. Enter the name of the witness, if applicable, in the "Witness Name" field.
9. Provide the contact number of the witness, if applicable, in the "Witness Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date (1)**: The date and time the incident occurred. This field is required to track the timing of the incident.
* **Type (2)**: Indicate whether the incident involved "Yes" or "No" in this field. This field helps to categorize the incident as either an actual accident or a near-miss.
* **Location (3)**: Provide a brief description of the location where the incident occurred. This field should include details such as the floor, direction, and any other relevant information.
* **Details (4)**: Enter a detailed account of what happened during the incident. This field should include information on the circumstances surrounding the incident.
* **Image (5)**: If an image related to the incident is available, upload it to this field. This can be useful for investigators to reference during their review.
* **Witness Statement (6)**: If applicable, enter a statement from the witness of the incident. This field can help to provide additional context and support for the incident report.
* **Witness Name (7)**: Enter the name of the witness, if applicable. This field helps to identify the individual who witnessed the incident.
* **Witness Contact Number (8)**: Enter the contact number of the witness, if applicable. This field allows for further communication with the witness.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Details" and "Witness Statement" fields.
* If an image is available, upload it to provide a visual reference point.
* If a witness is available, provide their name and contact number to facilitate further communication.
