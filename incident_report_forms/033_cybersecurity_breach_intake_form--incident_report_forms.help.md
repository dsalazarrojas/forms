# Cybersecurity Breach Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about cybersecurity breaches or incidents that have occurred within our organization. It helps the incident response team to quickly identify and respond to the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide a concise summary of the incident in the "Incident Summary" section.
2.  Select the appropriate "Incident Type" from the dropdown list, which categorizes the incident as Critical, High, Medium, or Low.
3.  Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" sections.
4.  Specify the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" section.
5.  Describe the incident in detail in the "Incident Description" section.
6.  List the affected assets in the "Impacted Assets" section.
7.  Indicate the incident's impact level as High, Medium, or Low in the "Impact Level" section.
8.  Select the status of the incident as Active, Inactive, or Resolved in the "Incident Status" section.
9.  Choose the category of the incident as Data, Network, System, or Application in the "Incident Category" section.
10. Provide your name, email, and phone number in the "Incident Reporter" section.
11. Enter your department and job title in the "Incident Reporter Department" and "Incident Reporter Job Title" sections.
12. Add any comments about the incident in the "Incident Reporter Comments" section.
13. Identify the person who reviewed the incident, their name, phone number, and status.
14. Provide comments about the incident in the "Incident Reviewer Comments" section.
15. Specify the resolution status in the "Incident Resolution" section.
16. Enter the date the incident was resolved in the "Incident Resolution Date" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Summary** (Text, Required): A brief overview of the incident, including its nature and cause.
*   **Incident Type** (Select One, Required): The level of severity of the incident (Critical, High, Medium, or Low).
*   **Incident Date** (Date, Required): The date the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Time** (Time, Required): The time the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Location** (Text, Required): The location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Description** (Text, Required): A detailed description of the incident.
*   **Impacted Assets** (Text, Required): A list of the assets affected by the incident.
*   **Impact Level** (Select One, Required): The impact of the incident on the organization (High, Medium, or Low).
*   **Incident Status** (Select One, Required): The status of the incident (Active, Inactive, or Resolved).
*   **Incident Category** (Select Multiple, Required): The category of the incident (Data, Network, System, or Application).
*   **Incident Reporter Name** (Text, Required): Your name as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident Reporter Email** (Email, Required): Your email address as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident Reporter Phone** (Text, Required): Your phone number as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident Reporter Department** (Text, Required): Your department as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident Reporter Job Title** (Text, Required): Your job title as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident Reporter Comments** (Text, Optional): Additional comments about the incident from the reporter.
*   **Incident Assessor Name** (Text, Required): The name of the person who assessed the incident.
*   **Incident Assessor Phone** (Text, Required): The phone number of the person who assessed the incident.
*   **Incident Assessor Comments** (Text, Required): Comments from the incident assessor about the incident.
*   **Incident Reviewer Name** (Text, Required): The name of the person who reviewed the incident.
*   **Incident Reviewer Phone** (Text, Required): The phone number of the person who reviewed the incident.
*   **Incident Reviewer Comments** (Text, Required): Comments from the incident reviewer about the incident.
*   **Incident Reviewer Status** (Text, Required): The status of the incident reviewer.
*   **Incident Resolution** (Text, Required): The resolution status of the incident.
*   **Incident Resolution Date** (Date, Required): The date the incident was resolved.
