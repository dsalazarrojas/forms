# Accident Investigation Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and investigate incidents or accidents that occurred during work hours or on company premises, to ensure a safe working environment and to identify and implement corrective actions to prevent similar incidents. It is essential to fill this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a thorough investigation and proper handling of the case.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee ID and full name in the corresponding fields.
2.  Provide your department and position title to specify your work context.
3.  Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
4.  Record the date and time when the accident occurred.
5.  Specify the location where the accident happened.
6.  Describe the accident and its details.
7.  Choose the type of accident that occurred.
8.  Describe the injuries sustained.
9.  Select the body parts affected by the injury.
10. Choose the type of medical treatment received.
11. Enter the name of the treating physician (if applicable).
12. Set the date when your leave begins and ends.
13. Select the type of leave being taken.
14. Indicate if a fitness evaluation is required before returning to work.
15. Specify any work restrictions upon return.
16. Select the current status of the accident investigation.
17. Plan any corrective actions to prevent similar accidents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Department**: Specify the department you belong to.
* **Position Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Accident Date**: Enter the date of the accident.
* **Accident Time**: Enter the time of the accident.
* **Accident Location**: Enter the specific location where the accident occurred.
* **Accident Description**: Describe the accident in detail.
* **Accident Type**: Choose the type of accident that occurred.
* **Injuries Sustained**: Describe the injuries sustained during the accident.
* **Body Parts Affected**: Select all the body parts affected by the injury.
* **Medical Treatment Received**: Choose the type of medical treatment received.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the treating physician (if applicable).
* **Leave Start Date**: Enter the date when your leave begins.
* **Anticipated Return Date**: Enter the expected date of your return to work.
* **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave being taken.
* **Fitness Evaluation Required**: Indicate if a fitness evaluation is required before returning to work.
* **Work Restrictions**: Specify any work restrictions upon return.
* **Investigation Status**: Select the current status of the accident investigation.
* **Corrective Actions Planned**: Plan any corrective actions to prevent similar accidents.
