# Hazard Reporting System Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hazard Reporting System Access Form is designed to gather information about your access to the hazard reporting system. This form helps us understand who has access to the system, when you last used it, and any concerns or suggestions you may have. This data will help us improve the system and ensure that everyone has access to the necessary resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your department from the "what_is_your_department" field.
2. Enter your supervisor's name in the "who_is_your_supervisor" field.
3. Provide the date of your last access to the system in the "when_was_your_last_access" field.
4. Indicate whether you have accessed the system (yes or no) in the "what_did_you_access" field.
5. Choose how many times you have accessed the system in the "how_many_times" field.
6. Confirm how you access the system (yes or no) in the "how_do_you_access_the_system" field.
7. Select the devices you use to access the system in the "what_device_do_you_use" field.
8. Enter any additional comments or reasons for accessing the system in the "what_is_your_reason" field.
9. Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions of the system in the "do_you_accept" field.
10. Provide any further comments or suggestions in the "what_is_your_comment" field (optional).
11. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the system in the "what_is_your_agreement" field.
12. Enter any concerns you may have in the "what_is_your_concern" field (optional).
13. Provide any suggestions for improvement in the "what_is_your_suggestion" field (optional).
14. Enter any additional comments in the "additional_comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_is_your_department**: This field is used to select your department to ensure that we have accurate records of who has access to the system within each department.
* **who_is_your_supervisor**: Please enter the name of your supervisor to help us understand who you report to within the organization.
* **when_was_your_last_access**: Provide the date of your last access to the system to help us track usage and identify any potential issues.
* **what_did_you_access**: Indicate whether you have accessed the system, and help us understand if there are any issues with the system's availability or accessibility.
* **how_many_times**: Select how many times you have accessed the system to help us understand usage patterns.
* **how_do_you_access_the_system**: Confirm how you access the system to help us understand the types of devices or methods used.
* **what_device_do_you_use**: Select the devices or methods you use to access the system to help us identify any technical issues.
* **what_is_your_reason**: Enter any additional comments or reasons for accessing the system to help us understand the context of your access.
* **do_you_accept**: Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions of the system.
