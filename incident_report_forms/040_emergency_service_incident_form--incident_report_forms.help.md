# emergency_service_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that require emergency service assistance. Please use this form to provide detailed information about the incident, including its type, date, time, location, reporter, and any relevant notes or photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Fire, Police, Other).
2.  Enter the date of the incident.
3.  Enter the time of the incident.
4.  Provide the location where the incident occurred.
5.  Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
6.  Add any additional notes about the incident in the "Incident Details Note" field.
7.  If applicable, attach a photo related to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Type** (Select One): Choose the type of incident that occurred (Fire, Police, Other).
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date of the incident in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour format (e.g., 02:30 PM).
* **Location** (Text): Provide the exact address or location where the incident occurred.
* **Reporter** (Text): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Details Note** (Note): Add any additional comments or explanations about the incident.
* **Photo** (Text): If relevant, attach a photo related to the incident.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form as accurately as possible to ensure that the incident can be properly assessed and addressed. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the emergency service team for assistance.
