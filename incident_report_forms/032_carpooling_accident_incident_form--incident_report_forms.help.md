# Carpooling Accident Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carpooling Accident Incident Form is designed to collect detailed information about incidents that occur within a carpooling environment.
## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form in its entirety to ensure accurate reporting of the incident.
- Enter the date the incident was reported (page 2).
- Enter the name of the person completing the form (page 3).
- Select the role of the person completing the form (page 4).
- Enter the date and time of the accident (page 6 and 7).
- Enter the location where the accident occurred (page 8).
- Provide a detailed description of what happened during the accident (page 9).
- Select the weather conditions at the time of the accident (page 10).
- Select the road conditions at the time of the accident (page 11).
- Enter the make and model of the vehicle involved (page 13).
- Enter the license plate number of the vehicle involved (page 14).
- Enter the name of the driver involved (page 15).
- List any passengers or witnesses involved (pages 17 and 19).
- Describe any injuries sustained during the accident (page 18).
- Determine if the police were notified (page 21).
- Enter the police report number if provided (page 22).
- Describe the damage sustained during the accident (page 23).
- Sign and date the form to confirm its accuracy (page 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date Reported** (Page 2, `report_date`, required): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Reporter Name** (Page 3, `reporter_name`, required): Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Role in Carpool** (Page 4, `reporter_role`, required): Select the role of the person completing this form.
* **Date of Accident** (Page 6, `accident_date`, required): Enter the date of the accident.
* **Time of Accident** (Page 7, `accident_time`, required): Enter the time of the accident.
* **Location (Address or Intersection)** (Page 8, `accident_location`, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description of what happened** (Page 9, `accident_description`, required): Explain the events leading up to and following the accident in detail.
* **Weather conditions at time of accident** (Page 10, `weather_conditions`, required): Select the weather conditions at the time of the accident.
* **Road conditions** (Page 11, `road_conditions`, required): Select the road conditions at the time of the accident.
* **Vehicle Make and Model** (Page 13, `vehicle_make_model`, required): Enter the make and model of the vehicle involved in the accident.
* **License Plate Number** (Page 14, `vehicle_plate`, required): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle involved.
* **Driver Name** (Page 15, `driver_name`, required): Enter the name of the driver involved.
* **List of passengers involved** (Page 17, `passengers_involved`, optional): List any passengers involved in the accident.
* **Any injuries?** (Page 18, `injured_persons`, optional): Describe any injuries sustained during the accident or state "None".
* **Witness Names and Contact** (Page 19, `witness_names`, optional): List any witnesses involved, including their contact information.
* **Was police notified?** (Page 21, `police_notified`, required): Determine if the police were notified.
* **Police Report Number** (Page 22, `police_report_number`, optional): Enter the police report number if provided.
* **Description of vehicle damage** (Page 23, `estimated_damage`, required): Describe the damage sustained during the accident.
* **I certify this report is true and accurate** (Page 25, `reporter_confirmation`, required): Sign and date the form to confirm its accuracy.
