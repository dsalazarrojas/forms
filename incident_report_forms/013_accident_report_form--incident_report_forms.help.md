# Accident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Accident Report Form is a tool for gathering comprehensive information about incidents that occurred within the organization or involving personnel. It serves to document the details of the incident, including the date, time, location, and circumstances surrounding the incident.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Reporter Information** section with your details, including your name, phone number, and email address. 
2. Select your relationship to the incident from the provided options. 
3. In the **Accident Details** section, provide a date and time of the incident, the location where it happened, and a description of what occurred. 
4. For the **Injured Person** section, provide the name and date of birth (if applicable) of the person involved in the incident, and describe the nature and extent of their injuries. 
5. Answer the question about medical treatment received, and if applicable, provide the name of the hospital or medical facility where treatment was received. 
6. In the **Additional Information** section, you can provide any other relevant details about the incident, including witness names and contact information, and whether a police report was filed or an insurance claim was made. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Name of Person Reporting**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
  (Required)
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during the day.
  (Required)
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
  (Required)
- **Relationship to the Incident**: Select the category that best describes your role in the incident (Injured party, Witness, Supervisor, First responder, Other).
  (Required)
- **Date of Accident**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
  (Required)
- **Time of Accident**: Enter the time on which the incident occurred.
  (Required)
- **Location of Accident**: Provide the full address or description of where the incident occurred.
  (Required)
- **Type of Accident**: Select the category that best describes the type of incident that occurred (Motor vehicle collision, Slip, trip, or fall, Workplace injury, Sports or recreation injury, Property damage, Other).
  (Required)
- **Description of What Happened**: Provide a detailed account of the events surrounding the incident, including what happened before, during, and after the incident.
  (Required)
- **Name of Injured Person**: Enter the name of the person involved in the incident.
  (Required)
- **Date of Birth of Injured Person**: Enter the date of birth of the person involved in the incident (if applicable).
  (Optional)
- **Description of Injuries**: Describe the nature and extent of the person's injuries.
  (Required)
- **Was Medical Treatment Received**: Answer if medical treatment was received. If yes, select one of the provided options.
  (Required)
- **Name of Hospital or Medical Facility**: Enter the name of the hospital or medical facility where medical treatment was received (if applicable).
  (Optional)
- **Was a Police Report Filed**: Answer if a police report was filed.
  (Required)
- **Police Report Number**: Enter the number of the police report (if applicable).
  (Optional)
- **Has an Insurance Claim Been Filed**: Answer if an insurance claim has been made.
  (Required)
- **Additional Comments**: Provide any other relevant information about the incident.
  (Optional)
- **Names and Contact Information of Witnesses**: Enter the names and contact information of individuals who witnessed the incident.
  (Optional)
- **Additional Comments**: This field can be used to provide any other relevant information about the incident.
  (Optional)
