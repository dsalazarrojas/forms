# Injury Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the details of an employee's injury that occurred during the incident.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, as indicated by the "required" label next to each field.
2. Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident and injury.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Injury Declaration**: Briefly describe the injury that occurred.
* **Incident**: Describe the incident that led to the injury.
* **Injury**: Describe the nature and extent of the injury.
* **Medical Attendance**: Check if you received medical attendance and provide details.
* **Injury Cause**: Identify the cause of the injury.
* **Injury Date/Time**: Enter the date and time the injury occurred.
* **Injury Type**: Choose "Yes" if the injury was work-related.
* **Injury Severity**: Choose "Yes" if the injury was severe.
* **Injury Symptoms**: Provide a detailed description of your symptoms.
* **Medical Attention Needed**: Check if you need medical attention and provide details.
* **Medical Attention Taken**: Check if you received medical attention.
* **Medical Attendance**: Check if you are seeking medical attendance and provide details.
* **Medical Report**: Enter the medical report number (if applicable).
* **Medical Report Status**: Enter the status of the medical report (if applicable).
* **Medical Report Date**: Enter the date the medical report was issued.
* **Medical Report Time**: Enter the time the medical report was issued.
* **Injury Description**: Describe the injury in detail.
* **Witness**: Identify any witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness Details**: Provide a description of the witness.
* **Witness Position**: Check if the witness was at the incident location.
* **Witness Description**: Describe the witness's account of the incident.
* **Medical Certificate**: Enter the medical certificate number (if applicable).
* **Incident Report Form**: Enter any additional details about the incident (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the incident.
