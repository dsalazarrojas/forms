# Parachuting Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parachuting Incident Form is designed to gather information about incidents that occur during parachuting activities. This form is intended to capture details about the incident, its severity, and any relevant circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
2. Provide the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
3. Enter the name of the skydiver involved in the incident.
4. If applicable, provide the name of the instructor who was present during the jump.
5. Describe the equipment used during the incident.
6. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., "Instructor-related Incident", "Student-related Incident", etc.).
7. Specify the location where the incident took place.
8. If the skydiver has experience, enter the level (number).
9. Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
10. If available, include any witness statements or statements about what was observed during the incident.
11. If a photo was taken during the incident, describe it briefly.
12. If witness statements about the photo are available, include them.
13. Indicate the relationship between the skydiver and their instructor (e.g., "related", "not related").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Provide the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Skydiver Name** (`skydiver_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the skydiver involved in the incident.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of the instructor who was present during the jump.
* **Equipment Used** (`equipment_used`, text, optional): Describe the equipment used during the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select one, optional): Select the type of incident that occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the incident took place.
* **Skydiver Experience** (`skydiver_experience`, number, optional): If the skydiver has experience, enter the level (number).
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Witness Statements** (`witness_statements`, text, optional): If available, include any witness statements or statements about what was observed during the incident.
* **Photo Taken** (`photo_taken`, text, optional): If a photo was taken during the incident, describe it briefly.
* **Witness Statements Photo** (`witness_statements_photo`, text, optional): If witness statements about the photo are available, include them.
* **Skydiver Instructor Relation** (`skydiver_instructor_relation`, select multiple, optional): Indicate the relationship between the skydiver and their instructor (e.g., "related", "not related").
* **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, text, optional): Provide the name of the person who reported the incident.
