# Website Downtime Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the IT team quickly gather information about website downtime incidents, enabling them to identify and resolve the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form with the details of the incident, providing accurate and clear information to aid in swift resolution.
- Ensure that each field is filled out completely and accurately.
- If a field is not applicable, mark it as such (e.g., "N/A" or "No").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, date, required: false): Report the time the incident occurred. This field is optional, but providing an accurate date and time helps in identifying patterns and root causes.
* **Duration** (`incident_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the incident in minutes.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`, text, required: false): Describe the error message you encountered. This helps in understanding the symptoms of the incident.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the root cause of the incident. This helps in identifying potential solutions.
* **Affected Areas** (`affected_areas`, select multiple, required: false): Check the affected areas of the website. Options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Resolution Steps** (`resolution_steps`, select multiple, required: false): Describe the steps taken to resolve the incident. Options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Impact On Business** (`impact_on_business`, text, required: false): Report the impact of the incident on business operations.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required: false): Enter your name and role in the company.
* **Incident Reporter Email** (`incident_reporter_email`, email, required: false): Provide your work email.
* **Incident Reporter Phone** (`incident_reporter_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Incident Reporter Job Title** (`incident_reporter_job_title`, text, required: false): Report your job title.

## Tips

* Ensure that each field is filled out accurately and completely.
* If a field is not applicable, mark it as such (e.g., "N/A" or "No").
* Review the form thoroughly before submitting it to ensure that the information is provided in a concise and clear manner.
