# Incident Exposure Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Incident Exposure Report form is used to report incidents that may pose a risk to individuals or equipment. This form is essential for documenting and tracking incidents to ensure that the necessary actions are taken to prevent or resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the incident.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident.
3. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident.
5. Specify the severity of the incident.
6. Select any applicable categories for the incident.
7. Choose the incident type.
8. Enter the name and contact information of the supervisor involved.
9. Enter the name and contact information of the safety officer involved.
10. Update the follow-up status.
11. Enter the current incident status.
12. Provide any action taken to address the incident.
13. Enter the outcome of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `text`, optional): Select the category of the incident to help classify and prioritize it.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of the incident.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter the specific location where the incident occurred.
* **Date Of Exposure** (`date_of_exposure`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Time Of Exposure** (`time_of_exposure`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the incident in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Severity** (`severity`, `select_one`, optional): Specify the severity of the incident, choosing from 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Category 0** (`category_0`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any applicable categories for the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of incident from the available options.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the supervisor involved.
* **Safety Officer** (`safety_officer`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the safety officer involved.
* **Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `text`, optional): Update the follow-up status to track progress.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `text`, optional): Enter the current incident status.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, `text`, optional): Provide any action taken to address the incident.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, `text`, optional): Enter the outcome of the incident.
