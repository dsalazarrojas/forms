# Spoiled Food Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to report incidents of spoiled food. It's essential to accurately collect information about the incident to take swift and effective action and prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the food service provider from the list.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the "Date Of Incident" field.
3. Enter the date you are reporting the incident in the "Date Of Report" field.
4. Describe the product that was spoiled in the "Product Description" field.
5. Select the type of product from the "Product Type" field.
6. Enter the quantity of the spoiled product.
7. Select the reason for spoilage from the "Reason Of Spoilage" field.
8. Enter the location where the spoilage occurred.
9. Select the storage area where the product was stored.
10. Enter the storage temperature and time.
11. Enter the spoilage date.
12. Describe the product condition.
13. Select the packaging type.
14. Enter the storage location.
15. Select the packaging condition.
16. Select the storage area temperature.
17. Select the packaging temperature.
18. Select the reason for spoilage type.
19. Select the location of spoilage.
20. Select the cause of spoilage.
21. Enter any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Service Provider**: Enter the name of the food service provider.
* **Date Of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Date Of Report**: Enter the date you are reporting the incident in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Product Description**: Describe the product that was spoiled.
* **Product Type**: Select the type of product that was spoiled.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the spoiled product.
* **Reason Of Spoilage**: Select the reason for spoilage (e.g., temperature, contamination, etc.).
* **Location Of Occurrence**: Enter the location where the spoilage occurred.
* **Storage Area**: Enter the storage area where the product was stored.
* **Storage Temperature**: Enter the storage temperature.
* **Storage Time**: Enter the storage time.
* **Spoilage Date**: Enter the date the product spoiled.
* **Product Condition**: Describe the product condition at the time of spoilage.
* **Packaging**: Enter the packaging type.
* **Packaging Type**: Select the packaging type (e.g., box, bag, etc.).
* **Storage Area Temp**: Enter the storage area temperature.
* **Storage Location**: Enter the storage location.
* **Packaging Condition**: Select the condition of the packaging.
* **Packaging Temp**: Enter the packaging temperature.
* **Reason For Spoilage**: Select the reason for spoilage.
* **Spoilage Type**: Select the type of spoilage (e.g., mold, slimy texture, etc.).
* **Location Of Spoilage**: Select the location where the spoilage occurred.
* **Cause Of Spoilage**: Select the cause of spoilage.
* **Other Details**: Enter any additional information about the incident.

Please take the time to carefully and accurately complete this form to ensure a thorough understanding of the incident.
