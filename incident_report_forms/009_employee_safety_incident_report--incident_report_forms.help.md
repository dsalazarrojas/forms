# Employee Safety Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture incident details from employees after a workplace injury or incident has occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: * Employee Name, Incident Date, Incident Location, and Incident Description.
2. Fill out the optional fields as needed: * Affected People (if someone was hurt), Incident Cause, and Reported By.
3. Be as thorough as possible when filling out the fields, as this information will help management and HR teams respond to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Incident Details
**Incident Date** (3): Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is the day the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (4): Enter the time of the day the incident happened.
* **Incident Location** (5): Enter the exact location where the incident occurred, including the building or department name.

### 2. Employee Information
**Employee Name** (2): Enter your full name as it is listed in HR records.
 
### 3. Incident Information
**Incident Description** (6): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Affected People** (7): Check 'Yes' if anyone was hurt during the incident.
 
### 4. Incident Reporting
**Incident Cause** (8): Enter the reason or cause of the incident if known.
* **Reported By** (9): Enter your name and title.
