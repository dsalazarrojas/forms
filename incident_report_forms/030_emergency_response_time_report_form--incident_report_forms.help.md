# emergency_response_time_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about emergency response time. It is intended for reporting emergency response times, providing details on the time elapsed from the start of an emergency to the response time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Incident Report Form (`incident_report_form`)
 * **Incident Report Form**: Enter a brief summary of the incident.
 * Hint: This field is not required but can provide context to the emergency response time report.

### 2. incident_date (Date)
 * **incident_date**: Enter the date of the incident in YYYY-MM-DD format.
 * Hint: This field is required and should be the date the incident occurred.

### 3. response_time (Time)
 * **response_time**: Enter the time elapsed since the incident started.
 * Hint: This field is required and should be the time the response team arrived.

### 4. first_responder_name (Text)
 * **first_responder_name**: Enter the name of the first responder.
 * Hint: This field is required and should be the name of the individual who first responded to the incident.

### 5. agency_id (Text)
 * **agency_id**: Enter the agency ID number (e.g., 1234).
 * Hint: This field is required and should be the unique identifier of the agency responding.

### 6. incident_location (Text)
 * **incident_location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
 * Hint: This field is required and should be the exact location of the incident.

### 7. incident_type (Text)
 * **incident_type**: Enter the type of incident (e.g., fire, medical, etc.).
 * Hint: This field is required and should be the type of emergency response.

### 8. response_time_notes (Note)
