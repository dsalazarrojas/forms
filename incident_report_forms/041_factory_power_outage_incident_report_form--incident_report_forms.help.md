# Factory Power Outage Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Power Outage Incident Report Form is designed to gather information on power outages that have occurred in the factory. The form is meant to be completed by individuals who have witnessed or responded to the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the date and time the incident occurred.
2. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g. Electrical Outage, Equipment Failure, etc.).
3. Provide a brief summary of what happened during the incident.
4. Identify the location(s) where the incident occurred.
5. Check if personnel were involved in the incident (yes or no).
6. Check if equipment was affected by the incident (yes or no).
7. Provide an explanation for the root cause of the incident.
8. Outline any maintenance actions taken or planned for the incident.
9. Summarize any next steps for the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Report Form** (incident_report_form): This is the main section for providing details about the incident.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (incident_type): Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g. Electrical Outage, Equipment Failure, etc.).
* **Incident Summary** (incident_summary): Provide a brief summary of what happened during the incident.
* **Location** (incident_location): Choose the location(s) where the incident occurred.
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		- Transformer 1
		- Transformer 2
		- Transformer 3
		- Transformer 4
		- Transformer 5
		- Other (specify in comments)
* **Personnel Involved** (personnel_involved): Check if personnel were involved in the incident (yes or no).
* **Equipment Affected** (equipment_affected): Check if equipment was affected by the incident (yes or no).
* **Root Cause** (root_cause): Provide an explanation for the root cause of the incident.
* **Maintenance Action** (maintenance_action): Outline any maintenance actions taken or planned for the incident.
* **Next Steps** (next_steps): Summarize any next steps for the incident.
* **Comments** (comments): Add any additional comments or information about the incident.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language when providing information.
* Review the form thoroughly before submitting it.
