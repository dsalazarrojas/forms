# Patient Care Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Care Incident Report form is designed to collect information about unexpected events or incidents that have occurred within a healthcare setting. This form helps to document the incident, identify the root cause, and plan corrective actions to prevent similar incidents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Patient Care Incident Report form, follow these steps:

*   Select the type of incident that occurred from the dropdown menu.
*   Enter the date and time of the incident using the date and time format.
*   Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
*   Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
*   Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
*   List any witnesses to the incident.
*   Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   Select the category of the incident from the dropdown menu.
*   Describe the root cause of the incident.
*   Enter any corrective actions or follow-up comments (optional).
