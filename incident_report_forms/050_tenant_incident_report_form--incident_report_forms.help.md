# Tenant Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Incident Report Form is designed to collect incident reports from tenants, providing facility managers and maintenance teams with a standardized and structured way of capturing information about incidents that occur within a particular area or space. The form helps to ensure that all necessary information is captured and reduces the likelihood of duplication of effort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the incident in the **Incident Report** field (page 1).
2. Provide a more detailed description of the incident in the **Incident Description** field (page 2).
3. If necessary, enter the date and time of the incident in the **Incident Date** and **Incident Time** fields (page 3).
4. Select the category of the incident from the dropdown options (page 4).
5. Identify the person who reported the incident and the organisation responsible for reporting the incident in the **Reported By** and **Reported By Organisation** fields (page 5).
6. Select the current status of the incident from the dropdown options (page 6).
7. Enter any additional details about the incident in the **Incident Details** field (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Report** (page 1): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Description** (page 2): Provide a more detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (page 3): Enter the date of the incident (if applicable).
* **Incident Time** (page 3): Enter the time of the incident (if applicable).
* **Incident Category** (page 4): Select the category of the incident from the dropdown options:
	+ Dispute
	+ Lease Issue
	+ Repair Request
	+ Other
* **Select Category** (page 4): This field is a redundant label for the dropdown options above.
* **Reported By** (page 5): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Organisation** (page 5): Enter the name of the organisation responsible for reporting the incident.
* **Incident Status** (page 6): Select the current status of the incident from the dropdown options:
	+ Active
	+ Closed
	+ Resolved
	+ On Hold
* **Incident Details** (page 7): Enter any additional details about the incident.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the incident report is complete and accurate.
* If you're unsure about the category of the incident, select the "Other" option and provide a brief description in the **Incident Details** field.
* If you need help with filling out the form, please contact the facility management team for assistance.
