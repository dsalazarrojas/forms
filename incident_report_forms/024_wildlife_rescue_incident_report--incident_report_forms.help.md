# Wildlife Rescue Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Rescue Incident Report form is used to document and track wildlife rescue incidents. It is a critical tool for gathering information about rescued animals, their locations, and the circumstances of their rescue. By completing this form, you will be contributing to the organization's efforts to manage and respond to wildlife rescue incidents effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Select the Incident Date field and enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the Animal Sighting Location in the text field provided.
4. Select the type of animal that was rescued from the list of options.
5. Provide a brief description of the animal in the text field.
6. Enter the name of the person who rescued the animal (if known).
7. Enter the contact number of the person who reported the incident.
8. Optionally, select the status of the animal from the list of options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date (1)** (required: false)
This field collects the date of the incident. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Animal Sighting Location (2)** (required: true)
Enter the location where the animal was seen, such as a specific address or a general area.
* **Animal Type (3)** (required: true)
Select the type of animal that was rescued from the list of options. This will help in identifying the species and providing targeted support.
* **Animal Description (4)** (required: false)
Enter a brief description of the animal, including any notable features or characteristics.
* **Rescuer Name (5)** (required: false)
Enter the name of the person who rescued the animal, if known.
* **Contact Number (6)** (required: true)
Enter the contact number of the person who reported the incident. This will help in follow-up communication.
* **Animal Status (7)** (required: false)
Select the status of the animal from the list of options, such as "Injured", "Healthy", or "Missing".
* **Incident Date-Time (8)** (required: false)
This field is not currently used in the form, but it is intended for future enhancements. Please ignore it.
