# Cheerleading Demerit Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cheerleading Demerit Form Template is designed to report and document incidents that have occurred during practice, competition, or other team events. This form will help you to identify and address issues related to team behavior, sportsmanship, and adherence to team rules.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Be as objective as possible when describing the incident.
3. Select the correct type of offense from the available options.
4. Enter the number of demerit points assigned (usually 1-5).
5. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
6. List any witnesses to the incident, if applicable.
7. Choose the action taken by the coach from the available options.
8. Enter the total accrued demerits (if applicable).
9. Acknowledge receipt of the demerit and reasons for it.
10. Fill out the reporting coach's name and the date the form was filed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cheerleader Name (cheerleader_name_inc)**: Enter the name of the cheerleader who committed the offense.
* **Date of Incident (incident_occurrence_date)**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident (incident_occurrence_time)**: Enter the time the incident occurred (if applicable).
* **Location of Incident (incident_location_desc)**: Enter a detailed description of the location where the incident occurred (e.g. Gym, Competition Venue, Bus).
* **Type of Offense (demerit_offense_type)**: Select the primary category of the offense from the available options (Unexcused Absence, Tardiness, etc.).
* **Demerit Points Assigned (demerit_points_assigned)**: Enter the number of demerit points assigned (usually 1-5).
* **Detailed Description of Incident (incident_description_detailed)**: Provide an objective and detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Witnesses (if any) (witnesses_to_incident)**: List any witnesses to the incident, if applicable.
* **Action Taken by Coach (immediate_disciplinary_action)**: Select the action taken by the coach from the available options (Verbal Warning, etc.).
* **Total Accrued Demerits (total_accrued_demerits)**: Enter the total accrued demerits (if applicable).
* **Athlete Acknowledgment (athlete_ack_of_demerit)**: Select "Acknowledged" to confirm receipt of the demerit and reasons for it.
* **Reporting Coach Name (reporting_coach_full_name)**: Enter the full name of the coach who is filing this report.
* **Date Filed (report_filing_timestamp)**: Enter the date the report was filed.
