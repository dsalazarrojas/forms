# Guard Line Input Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that occur during patrols or security checks. It helps in recording specific details such as location, time, and type of incident, aiding in the management and monitoring of these events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by selecting the Patrol Location** (`patrol_location`, text, required/optional): This is the physical area where the patrol was conducted. Enter the specific street address, building, or landmark where the incident occurred.
2. **Enter the Start Time** (`start_time`, date, required): This is the time the patrol began. Use the date field to select the start date.
3. **Enter the End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): This is the time the patrol ended or when you encountered the incident. Enter the time as it occurred or when the patrol ended.
4. **Enter the Checkpoint Location** (`checkpoint_location`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the specific location of the checkpoint, such as a street corner or a specific building.
5. **Provide a detailed Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Describe what happened during the patrol, including any incidents, interactions, or notable events.
6. **Mark the Patrol Status** (`patrol_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the patrol, whether On Route, Completed, or Suspended.
7. **Mark the Checkpoint Status** (`checkpoint_status`, select_one, required/optional): If you have set or encountered a checkpoint, indicate its status as Active or Inactive.
8. **Select the Incident Type(s)** (`incident_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of incident from Fire, Security, or Medical.
9. **Enter the Patrol Frequency** (`patrol_frequency`, number, required): Report how often the patrol occurs (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
10. **Enter the Checkpoint Frequency** (`checkpoint_frequency`, number, required/optional): If applicable, report how often the checkpoint occurs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patrol Location**: Enter the specific location of the patrol.
* **Start Time**: The time the patrol started.
* **End Time**: The time the patrol ended or when you encountered an incident.
* **Checkpoint Location**: The specific location of the checkpoint if you encountered one during the patrol.
* **Incident Description**: A detailed description of what happened during the patrol.
* **Patrol Status**: The current status of the patrol, whether On Route, Completed, or Suspended.
* **Checkpoint Status**: The status of the checkpoint, Active or Inactive.
* **Incident Type**: The type of incident that occurred, which could be Fire, Security, or Medical.
* **Patrol Frequency**: How often the patrol happens (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Checkpoint Frequency**: How often the checkpoint happens if you encountered one during the patrol.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete all required fields accurately for thorough reporting.
- For fields with options, choose the option that best describes your situation.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult your patrol or checkpoint procedures for guidance.
