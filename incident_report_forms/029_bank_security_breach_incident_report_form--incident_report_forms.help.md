# Bank Security Breach Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bank Security Breach Incident Report Form is used to document and report security breaches that affect the bank's operations, systems, or customer information. This form is essential for identifying and responding to potential security threats, ensuring the bank's reputation, and maintaining the trust of its customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your details as the Reporting Official.
2. Enter your name, job title, and branch or department affected.
3. Provide your emergency contact phone number.
4. Select the date and time when the breach was discovered.
5. Choose the primary category of the security breach from the provided options.
6. Assess the initial severity level of the breach.
7. Select the affected systems or applications.
8. Provide an estimated number of customers impacted.
9. Describe the incident and the steps taken to mitigate it.
10. Provide a link to any evidence or logs related to the incident (if available).
11. Indicate whether law enforcement has been notified.
12. Complete the form with your timestamp for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter Full Name**: Enter your full name as the reporting official.
* **Job Title / Role**: Enter your job title or role within the bank.
* **Branch or Department Affected**: Specify the branch or department affected by the breach.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Provide your emergency contact phone number.
* **Date of Discovery**: Enter the date when the breach was discovered.
* **Time of Discovery**: Enter the time when the breach was discovered.
* **Type of Security Breach**: Select the primary category of the breach from the options provided.
* **Initial Severity Assessment**: Assess the initial severity level of the breach.
* **Affected Systems or Applications**: Select the systems or applications affected by the breach.
* **Estimated Number of Customers Impacted**: Provide an estimated number of customers impacted by the breach.
* **Detailed Description of the Incident**: Provide a detailed account of what happened, how it was found, and the current status.
* **Immediate Actions / Mitigation Steps Taken**: Describe the steps taken to contain and mitigate the breach.
* **Link to Evidence or Logs (if available)**: Provide a link to any evidence or logs related to the incident.
* **Has Law Enforcement been Notified?**: Indicate whether law enforcement has been notified.
* **Report Completion Timestamp**: Complete the form with your timestamp for record-keeping purposes.
