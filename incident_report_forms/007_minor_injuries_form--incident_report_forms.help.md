# Minor Injuries Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report minor injuries that occur during work hours or on company premises.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the details of the injury in the "Injury Details" section.
2.  Enter the employee details in the "Employee Details" section.
3.  Describe the injury in the "Describe Injury" section.
4.  Enter the date and time the injury occurred in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5.  Provide the location where the injury occurred.
6.  If a witness was present, provide their details in the "Witness Details" section.
7.  Enter the witness's name, relationship, phone number, and email address.
8.  If a medical provider was involved, indicate if they provided medical attention and enter their name.
9.  Enter the medical provider's relationship with the employee.
10.  Enter the medical report (if applicable).
11.  Enter the employee's name and any other required information.
12.  Finally, sign and date the form to confirm its completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Injury Details** (`injury_details`, text, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to enter the details of the injury.
*   **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to enter the employee's details.
*   **Describe Injury** (`injury_description`, text, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to describe the injury in more detail.
*   **Date** (`injury_date`, date, required/optional):
    *   Enter the date the injury occurred.
*   **Time** (`injury_time`, time, required/optional):
    *   Enter the time the injury occurred.
*   **Location** (`injury_location`, text, required/optional):
    *   Provide the location where the injury occurred.
*   **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to enter the witness's details.
*   **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter the witness's name.
*   **Relationship** (`witness_relationship`, select one, required/optional):
    *   If a witness was present, choose the relationship to the employee.
*   **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter the witness's phone number.
*   **Witness Email** (`witness_email`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter the witness's email address.
*   **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter a statement from the witness.
*   **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter the employee's phone number.
*   **Medical Attention** (`medical_attention`, select one, required/optional):
    *   If a medical provider was involved, indicate if they provided medical attention.
*   **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter the medical provider's name.
*   **Relationship with Medical Provider** (`medical_provider_relationship`, select one, required/optional):
    *   If a medical provider was involved, indicate their relationship with the employee.
*   **Medical Report** (`medical_report`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter the medical report (if applicable).
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter the employee's name.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter the supervisor's name.
*   **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required/optional):
    *   Enter the supervisor's phone number.
*   **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, required/optional):
    *   This field is for the employee to sign the form to confirm its completion.
*   **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, note, required/optional):
    *   This field is for the witness to sign the form to confirm its completion.
*   **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, note, required/optional):
    *   This field is for the supervisor to sign the form to confirm its completion.
*   **Medical Provider Signature** (`medical_signature`, note, required/optional):
    *   This field is for the medical provider to sign the form to confirm its completion.
