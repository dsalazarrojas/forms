# incident_investigation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The incident_investigation_checklist form is designed to aid in the investigation and documentation of incidents that occur within the workplace. It is a crucial tool for collecting and organizing essential details about the incident, which helps in understanding the cause, effect, and potential consequences of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Employee Incident** field, providing a brief description of the incident.
2. Next, enter the **Incident Description** field, elaborating on the incident's details.
3. Record the **Date and Time Occurred** field accurately, as this will help in timing the incident chronologically.
4. Enter the **Location Incident Occurred** field, specifying where the incident took place.
5. In the **Involved Personnel** field, indicate if any personnel were involved or affected by the incident.
6. The **Witness Summary** field is optional but provides an opportunity to collect a brief summary of witness statements.
7. You can also provide detailed **Witness Statements** in the corresponding field.
8. The **Incident Cause** field is where you should describe the root cause of the incident.
9. In the **Incident Effect** field, outline the effects or consequences of the incident.
10. Finally, provide any **Corrective Actions Planned** to mitigate or rectify the incident's impact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Employee Incident (<type: text>)**
This field is where you should briefly describe the incident involving the employee.

### **Incident Description (<type: text>)**
Here, you provide an elaborated description of the incident, including its context and any other relevant details.

### **Date and Time Occurred (<type: date>)**
Enter the date and time the incident occurred for precise timing.

### **Location Incident Occurred (<type: text>)**
Specify the exact location where the incident happened.

### **Involved Personnel (<type: select_multiple>)**
Indicate if any personnel were directly involved or affected by the incident.

### **Witness Summary (<type: text>)**
Optional field to collect a brief summary of witness statements.

### **Witness Statements (<type: text>)**
This field is for detailed witness statements, should you wish to collect them.

### **Incident Cause (<type: text>)**
Describe the root cause of the incident.

### **Incident Effect (<type: text>)**
Outline the effects or consequences of the incident.

### **Corrective Actions Planned (<type: text>)**
Detail any corrective actions or measures planned to mitigate or rectify the incident's impact.
