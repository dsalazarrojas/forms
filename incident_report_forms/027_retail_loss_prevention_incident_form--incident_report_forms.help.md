# Retail Loss Prevention Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Retail Loss Prevention Incident Form is a tool used to report incidents of loss prevention, including theft, fraud, and other types of losses that have occurred in the retail environment. This form is used to gather information about the incident, including details about the employee involved, the incident itself, and any witnesses. The form is designed to be completed by employees and managers to report and track incidents of loss prevention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) with the necessary information.
2. Select the relevant options for the "Loss Type" and "Incident Type" fields using the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional information or notes in the "Notes" and "Review Notes" fields.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (*): Enter the name of the employee who was involved in the incident.
* **Incident Date** (*): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Optional): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (Optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of what happened.
* **Loss Amount** (*): Enter the amount of loss or damage incurred.
* **Loss Type** (*): Select the type of loss that occurred using the dropdown menu:
	+ Theft
	+ Fraud
	+ Other
* **Incident Type** (*): Select the type of incident using the dropdown menu:
	+ Employee Error
	+ Shoplifting
	+ Supplier Theft
	+ Customer Theft
	+ External Theft
* **Employee ID** (Optional): Enter the ID number of the employee involved.
* **Witness Description**: Enter a brief description of any witness who witnessed the incident.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the witness.
* **Witness Phone**: Enter the phone number of the witness.
* **Witness Email**: Enter the email of the witness.
* **Witness Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the witness and the employee involved.
* **Incident Status** (*): Select the status of the incident using the dropdown menu:
	+ Closed
	+ In Progress
	+ Pending
	+ Under Investigation
* **Notes** (*): Enter any additional information or notes about the incident.
* **Submitted By** (*): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted At** (*): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Reviewed At** (Optional): Enter the date and time the form was reviewed.
* **Reviewer Name** (*): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Review Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional information or notes from the reviewer.
* **Review Status** (*): Select the review status using the dropdown menu:
	+ Closed
	+ Open
