# Charity Donation Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Donation Incident Report Form is a tool used by charity organizations to report and manage incidents that have occurred during the process of accepting and processing donations. This form helps to ensure that any issues are investigated and resolved efficiently and effectively, maintaining the integrity and reputation of the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your name and contact email address in the "reporter name" and "contact email" fields. This will allow us to get in touch with you for any follow-up questions or to provide updates on the incident.
2. Choose the category of incident that best describes the situation. Select from the options "Fraud", "Misallocation", "Technical error", "Donor complaint", or "Other".
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details or circumstances surrounding the incident.
4. If applicable, enter the monetary value involved in the incident.
5. List the names or organizations involved in the incident.
6. Describe the immediate actions taken at the time of the incident.
7. Indicate whether further investigation is required by selecting "True" or "False" in the "follow up required" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporter Name**: <reporter_name> (*reporter name*, **text**, required): Your name, as the person submitting the report.
* **Contact Email**: <contact_email> (*contact email*, **email**, optional): Your contact information for follow up.
* **Incident Date**: <incident_date> (*incident date*, **date**, required): The date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type**: <incident_type> (*incident type*, **select one**, required): The category of incident that best describes the situation.
	+ Select from: Fraud, Misallocation, Technical error, Donor complaint, Other
* **Description**: <description> (*description*, **text**, required): A detailed description of the incident.
* **Amount Involved**: <amount_involved> (*amount involved*, **number**, optional): The monetary value involved in the incident, if applicable.
* **Parties Involved**: <parties_involved> (*parties involved*, **text**, optional): The names or organizations involved in the incident.
* **Immediate Action Taken**: <immediate_action_taken> (*immediate action taken*, **text**, optional): The steps taken at the time of the incident.
* **Follow Up Required**: <follow_up_required> (*follow up required*, **select one**, required): Indicate whether further investigation is required.
	+ Select from: True, False
