# Dam Breach Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dam Breach Incident Form is used to document and report incidents of dam breaches, which can cause significant environmental and social impacts. This form is used to gather essential information about the incident, including emergency contacts, dam description, location, incident summary, type, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (emergency_contacts_name): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone** (emergency_contacts_phone): Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email** (emergency_contacts_email): Enter the email address of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Location** (location): Describe the location of the dam breach incident.
* **Dam Description** (dam_breach_incident_form_part_2): Give a brief description of the dam breach incident.
* **Incident Summary** (dam_breach_incident_form_part_3): Summarize the incident, including its severity and impact.
* **Incident Type** (dam_breach_incident_form_part_4): Select the type of incident from the options provided:
	+ Dam Breach
	+ Flood
	+ Fire
	+ Other (if the incident does not fit into any of the above categories)
* **Other (if selecting "Other")** (dam_breach_incident_form_part_4_other): If you selected "Other" above, describe the incident in more detail.
* **Dam Status** (dam_breach_incident_form_part_5): Select the current status of the dam:
	+ Damaged
	+ Intact
	+ Partially Damaged
* **Other (if selecting "Other")** (dam_breach_incident_form_part_5_other): If you selected "Other" above, describe the current status of the dam in more detail.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any information, consult with your team or supervisor before submitting the form.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing fields before submission.
