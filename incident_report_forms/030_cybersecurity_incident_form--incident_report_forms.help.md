# Cybersecurity Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Incident Form is used to collect detailed information about incidents that may affect the organization's systems, data, or employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Incident Information** section with a brief description of the incident.
2. In the **Type of Incident** section, select the most accurate type of incident from the available options (e.g., malware, phishing, data breach, etc.).
3. In the **Incident Impact** section, describe the potential or actual impact of the incident (e.g., financial loss, data loss, system downtime, etc.).
4. In the **Reporting Person** section, enter your name and title.
5. In the **Reporting Person's Contact Info** section, provide your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).
6. In the **Affected Systems/Devices** section, list the systems or devices affected by the incident.
7. In the **Breach Details** section, provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant screenshots or files.
8. In the **Response Actions Taken** section, describe the actions taken to respond to the incident.
9. Finally, in the **Review and Submit** section, review your entire form for accuracy and completeness, and submit it for further action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Information (1)**: A brief description of the incident.
* **Type of Incident (2)**: The type of incident that occurred (e.g., malware, phishing, data breach, etc.).
* **Incident Impact (3)**: The potential or actual impact of the incident (e.g., financial loss, data loss, system downtime, etc.).
* **Reporting Person (4)**: Your name and title.
* **Reporting Person's Contact Info (5)**: Your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).
* **Affected Systems/Devices (6)**: The systems or devices affected by the incident.
* **Breach Details (7)**: A detailed description of what happened, including any relevant screenshots or files.
* **Response Actions Taken (8)**: The actions taken to respond to the incident.
* **Review and Submit (9)**: Review your entire form for accuracy and completeness, and submit it for further action.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the incident response team or your manager.
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Once submitted, this form will be reviewed and acted upon promptly.
