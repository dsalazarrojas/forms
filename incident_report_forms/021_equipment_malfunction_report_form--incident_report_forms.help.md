# Equipment Malfunction Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment malfunction report form is used to document and report malfunctions or issues with equipment used in our facility. This form helps the maintenance team quickly identify and address the problem, reducing downtime and costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **equipment_type**, **equipment_name**, **location**, **issue_description**, **issue_date**, **status**, and **submitter**. These fields ensure that we have essential information about the malfunction and can quickly identify the equipment involved.
2. Enter a brief description of the issue in the **issue_description** field.
3. Select the type of equipment that is malfunctioning from the **equipment_type** field.
4. Enter the location of the equipment in the **location** field.
5. Set the **issue_date** to the date when the issue was first noticed.
6. Set the **status** to either **Open**, **In Progress**, or **Completed** depending on the current status of the issue.
7. Enter your name and contact information in the **submitter** field.
8. If you have any additional comments or information to provide, enter them in the **comments** field.
9. For completed issues, set the **repair_date** field to the date when the repair was completed.
10. If you are assigning the issue to a specific person, enter their name in the **assigned_to** field and the date they were assigned the issue in the **assign_date** field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the issue in the **notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User**: Enter your name and contact information for follow up questions.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment that is malfunctioning (e.g., Heavy Equipment, Hand Tools, Office Supplies).
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the exact model or type of equipment that is malfunctioning.
* **Location**: Enter the exact location where the equipment is located.
* **Issue Description**: Enter a brief description of the problem or issue with the equipment.
* **Issue Date**: Enter the date when the issue was first noticed.
* **Repair Date**: Enter the date when the repair was completed (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the issue.
* **Status**: Set the status of the issue (Open, In Progress, Completed).
* **Submitter**: Enter your name and contact information for follow-up questions.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person assigned to handle the issue (if applicable).
* **Assign Date**: Enter the date when the issue was assigned to the person (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the issue.
