# General Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for reporting incidents that have occurred in the workplace. To ensure the form is used correctly, please review each field carefully and provide accurate information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, simply select the "Report" option from the dropdown list, enter the witness's name and email address if available, choose the date of the incident, and provide a detailed description of what happened, including any witness statement if applicable. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not an incident occurred.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the person or people who witnessed the incident, if applicable.
* **Witness Email**: Enter the email address of the witness, if available. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Incident Date**: Select the date of the incident using the date picker. If the incident is ongoing or has not yet occurred, you can choose an anticipated date.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant details such as cause, effects, and any actions taken.
* **Witness Statement**: If you are a witness to the incident, provide a written statement detailing what you saw or experienced.
