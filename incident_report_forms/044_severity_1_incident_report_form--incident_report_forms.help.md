# Severity 1 Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about Severity 1 incidents, which are incidents that significantly impact business operations, critical systems, or pose an imminent threat to safety. The goal is to document the incident and its resolution in a structured and detailed manner, allowing for efficient incident management and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the Incident Type from the options provided (Hardware, Software, Network).
2. Enter the Incident Description in the text field.
3. Choose the Incident Status (Open or Resolved) and enter the Incident Start Date and Time, as well as the Resolved Date and Time if applicable.
4. Fill out the Incident Reporter's contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
5. Choose how the incident was reported (IT, Operations).
6. Add any Additional Info or Comments about the incident.
7. Select any relevant Tags for the incident (ITIL, Other).
8. Add any attachments related to the incident.
9. Enter any Comments or Status Updates about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`): A brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`): The person reporting the incident. Please enter your full name and contact information (phone number and email).
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): The category of the incident (Hardware, Software, Network).
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): A detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`): The current status of the incident (Open or Resolved).
* **Start Date** (`incident_start_date`): The date the incident started.
* **Start Time** (`incident_start_time`): The time the incident started.
* **End Date** (`incident_end_date`): The date the incident was resolved.
* **End Time** (`incident_end_time`): The time the incident was resolved.
* **Incident Reporter Phone** (`incident_reporter_phone`): The phone number of the incident reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Email** (`incident_reporter_email`): The email address of the incident reporter.
* **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`): How the incident was reported (IT, Operations).
* **Resolution Date** (`incident_resolution_date`): The date the incident was resolved.
* **Resolution Time** (`incident_resolution_time`): The time the incident was resolved.
* **Additional Info** (`incident_additional_info`): Any additional information about the incident.
* **Comments** (`incident_comments`): Any comments or notes about the incident.
* **Tags** (`incident_tags`): Select relevant categories (ITIL, Other) to help identify and filter incidents.
* **Other (Specify)** (`incident_tags_other`): If you selected "Other" as a tag, enter the relevant category here.
* **Attachments** (`incident_attachments`): Upload any relevant attachments related to the incident.
* **Comments** (`incident_comments`): Any further comments or updates about the incident.
* **Status Update** (`incident_status_update`): The current status of the incident (Open, Closed).
* **Status Update Comments** (`incident_status_update_comments`): Any comments or updates about the status.
* **Update Date** (`incident_status_update_date`): The date the status was updated.
* **Update Time** (`incident_status_update_time`): The time the status was updated.
