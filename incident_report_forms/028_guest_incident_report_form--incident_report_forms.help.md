# Guest Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Incident Report Form is used to document and track incidents reported by guests. This form is designed to provide the hotel management with the necessary information to understand and address the issue promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "guest_info" field, which asks for your personal details.
2. Next, select the type of incident that occurred from the "incident_type" field, which will help us understand the nature of the issue.
3. Please provide a brief description of what happened in the "incident_description" field.
4. If you have any additional details or comments, you can add them in the "incident_detail" field.
5. Specify the location where the incident occurred in the "incident_location" field.
6. If you have any information about what caused the incident, please provide it in the "incident_cause" field.
7. Finally, provide your contact information in the "contact_info" field, so we can reach out to you if needed.
8. Optionally, if you have any comments or feedback, please add them in the "guest_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **guest_info**: Please provide your name, phone number, and email address so we can contact you.
* **incident_date**: Please select the date the incident occurred.
* **incident_time**: Please select the time the incident occurred.
* **incident_type**: This field will help us understand the type of incident (e.g., Guest Compliment, Guest Complaint, Guest Incident).
* **incident_description**: Describe what happened in a few words or sentences.
* **incident_detail**: Add any additional details or comments about the incident.
* **incident_location**: Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **incident_cause**: If you know what caused the incident, please provide that information.
* **contact_info**: Please provide your contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **guest_comments**: Any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **incident_status**: Please select the status of the incident (e.g., In Progress, Closed, Pending).
* **incident_respondent**: Please provide a name or department who responded to the incident.
* **incident_reported_by**: Not applicable in this form version (defaulted to "Guest").
