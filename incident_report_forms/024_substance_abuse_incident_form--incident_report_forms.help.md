# Substance Abuse Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and gather information about incidents involving substance abuse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of substance involved in the incident from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident using the date and time input fields.
3. Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
4. Choose the status of the incident from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
6. Identify the officer or person reporting the incident.
7. Finally, enter the date the incident was reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details**: This field is where you can enter any relevant details about the incident, such as the location, date, and time.
* **Substance Type**: Select the type of substance involved in the incident. This will help us understand the scope and severity of the incident.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: This field is where you can provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Status**: Choose the status of the incident from the dropdown menu. This will help us determine the severity and urgency of the situation.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Reporting Officer**: Identify the officer or person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Reported**: Enter the date the incident was reported.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact the relevant authorities or experts for guidance.
* This form is for documentation and information purposes only, and it is not intended to be used as a substitute for professional medical or therapeutic advice.
