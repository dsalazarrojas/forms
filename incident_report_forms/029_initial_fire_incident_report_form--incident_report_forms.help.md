# Initial Fire Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Initial Fire Incident Report Form is used to report incidents of fire in a specific location, providing essential details about the incident, including its location, type, date, time, description, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the location where the incident occurred in the **Incident Location** field.
2.  Choose the type of incident (e.g., Fire) from the **Incident Type** dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the date and time of the incident using the **Incident Date** and **Incident Time** fields.
4.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the **Incident Description** field.
5.  Select the level of urgency of the incident (Low, Medium, or High) in the **Urgency** field.
6.  Choose the current status of the incident (Active or Resolved) in the **Status** field.
7.  Determine the priority of the incident (Low, Medium, or High) in the **Incident Priority** field.
8.  Identify the team(s) responsible for responding to the incident in the **Responding Team** field.
9.  Enter your name and contact information in the **Reporter** and **Reporter Contact Info** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false)
Enter the location where the incident occurred, such as the room number, building, or area.

### **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false)
Choose the type of incident that occurred, like Fire.

### **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the date when the incident occurred.

### **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false)
Enter the time when the incident occurred.

### **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false)
Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.

### **Urgency** (`incident_urgency`, select_one, required: false)
Select the level of urgency of the incident (Low, Medium, or High).

### **Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required: false)
Choose the current status of the incident (Active or Resolved).

### **Incident Priority** (`incident_priority`, select_one, required: false)
Determine the priority of the incident (Low, Medium, or High).

### **Responding Team** (`incident_responding_team`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the team(s) responsible for responding to the incident.

### **Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required: false)
Enter your name.

### **Reporter Contact Info** (`incident_reporter_contact_info`, text, required: false)
Enter your contact information.

Tips:

*   Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure that the incident is reported correctly.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant team or supervisor for assistance.
*   This form is designed to be completed in a timely manner to facilitate prompt response and resolution.
