# Select Your Safety Representative - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to facilitate the selection of a safety representative and provide context about the incident. Users are advised to use it judiciously, ensuring that the information is accurate and not duplicated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in all required fields (marked with asterisk *) as you would any other form.
*   For optional fields, provide as much detail as you need or feel is relevant.
*   Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menus as necessary.
*   Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select Safety Representative** (`safety_representative_selection`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more options from the list to identify who should be notified about the incident.
    *   Select 'Yes' if you need someone to be aware of the incident.
    *   Select 'No' if you don't need someone to be notified.
*   **Reason For Selection** (`reason_for_selection`, text, required: false): Describe the reason why you're selecting someone as your safety representative.
*   **Reason For Selection Details** (`reason_for_selection_details`, text, required: false): Provide additional context or explanation for your selection.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the incident started.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the incident ended.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false): Choose the department related to the incident.
*   **Department Details** (`department_details`, text, required: false): Explain any details about the department involved.
*   **Selected Reason** (`selected_reason`, text, required: false): Enter the reason why the safety representative was selected.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Keep your responses clear and concise.
*   Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submission.
