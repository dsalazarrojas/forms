# Pedestrian Accident Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to report incidents involving pedestrians. It is intended for use by witnesses, police officers, or other individuals who have information about a pedestrian accident. The form is used to collect details about the accident, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the incident using the calendar picker.
2. Enter the time of the incident.
3. Provide the location where the incident occurred.
4. Choose the type of incident from the options provided.
5. Indicate if a vehicle was involved in the incident.
6. Indicate if a pedestrian was involved in the incident.
7. Select the level of injuries sustained by the pedestrian.
8. Choose the level of damage to any vehicle involved.
9. Choose the level of damage to the pedestrian.
10. Indicate if you were a witness to the incident.
11. If you were a witness, select the level of severity of the officer's report.
12. Provide any additional notes about the incident.
13. Enter the name of the witness.
14. Enter the phone number of the witness.
15. Enter the email address of the witness.
16. Enter any additional information about the witness.
17. Enter any other phone number for the witness.
18. Enter any other email address for the witness.
19. Enter the address of the witness.
20. Enter the city of the witness.
21. Enter the state of the witness.
22. Enter the zip code of the witness.
23. Enter the country of the witness.
24. Specify the relationship between the witness and the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **accident_date** (Date): The date of the incident.
* **time** (Time): The time of the incident.
* **location** (Text): The location where the incident occurred.
* **incident_type** (Select One): The type of incident (e.g. Pedestrian vs Vehicle, Pedestrian vs Vehicle-Person, etc.).
* **vehicle_involved** (Select Multiple): Indicate if a vehicle was involved in the incident.
* **pedestrian_involved** (Select Multiple): Indicate if a pedestrian was involved in the incident.
* **pedestrian_injuries** (Select One): The level of injuries sustained by the pedestrian (e.g. minor, No injuries, Critical, etc.).
* **vehicle_damage** (Select One): The level of damage to any vehicle involved (e.g. None, Minor, Moderate, etc.).
* **pedestrian_damage** (Select One): The level of damage to the pedestrian (e.g. None, Minor, Moderate, etc.).
* **witness** (Select One): Indicate if you were a witness to the incident.
* **officer_prophets** (Select One): The level of severity of the officer's report (e.g. None, Minor, Moderate, etc.).
* **officer_notes** (Note): Any additional notes about the incident.
* **witness_name** (Text): The name of the witness.
* **witness_name_phone** (Text): The phone number of the witness.
* **witness_name_email** (Email): The email address of the witness.
* **witness_statement** (Text): Any additional information about the witness.
* **witness_phone** (Text): Any other phone number for the witness.
* **witness_email** (Email): Any other email address for the witness.
* **witness_address** (Text): The address of the witness.
* **witness_city** (Text): The city of the witness.
* **witness_state** (Text): The state of the witness.
* **witness_zip** (Text): The zip code of the witness.
* **witness_country** (Text): The country of the witness.
* **witness_relationship** (Text): Specify the relationship between the witness and the incident.
* **witness_other** (Text): Any other information about the witness.
