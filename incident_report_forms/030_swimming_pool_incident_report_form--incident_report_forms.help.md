# Swimming Pool Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about incidents that occur in swimming pools. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to help with investigation and potential prevention or resolution of similar incidents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through each section carefully, and answer every question that applies to your situation.
2.  Fill out the General Information section with your basic contact information and details about the incident.
3.  In the Incident Details section, provide as much information as possible about what happened.
4.  In the Investigation section, describe what you found out or discovered about the incident.
5.  In the Incident Reporter Information section, provide your contact information and any other relevant details.
6.  In the Additional Information section, include any other comments or context you think might be helpful.
