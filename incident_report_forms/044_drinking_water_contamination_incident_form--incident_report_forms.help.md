# Drinking Water Contamination Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to report incidents of drinking water contamination. It will help the authorities to gather critical information about the incident and take necessary actions to protect public health and the environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the following information:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Drinking Water Incident** (`drinking_water_incident`): Briefly describe the incident of drinking water contamination. Please provide a concise and factual account of what happened.
* **Water Source** (`water_source`): Specify the source of the contaminated water (e.g., well, river, tap, etc.).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time at which the incident occurred.
* **Location** (`location`): Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* **Affected Population** (`affected_population`): Identify the number of people or community affected by the incident.
* **Contamination Type** (`contamination_type`): Select 'Yes' if the water is contaminated, 'No' otherwise.
* **Contamination Level** (`contamination_level`): Select the level of contamination (Low, Moderate, High).
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`): Enter your name or the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact phone number or the phone number of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your contact email or the email of the person reporting the incident.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`): Date on which this form is submitted.

Note: This form is for reporting incidents of drinking water contamination. Please report all incidents, regardless of their severity.
