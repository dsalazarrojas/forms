# Warehouse Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Incident Report Form is designed to report incidents that occur within the warehouse. This form is used to gather information about the incident, including date, time, location, and description, as well as details about the incident reporter and any subsequent updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Incident Date and time of the incident in the respective fields.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the Incident Description field.
3. Specify the location where the incident occurred in the Incident Location field.
4. Enter the name and title of the incident reporter in the Incident Reporter and Title fields.
5. Select the correct department for the incident reporter in the Department field.
6. Choose the type of incident from the list provided in the Type field.
7. Indicate the status of the incident in the Status field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes regarding the incident in the Comments and Notes fields.
9. Finally, update the status of the incident as necessary in the Status Updated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date**: The date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: The time at which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: A detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Location**: The location within the warehouse where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Reporter**: The name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Title**: The title of the person reporting the incident.
* **Department**: The department associated with the incident reporter. Please select from the available options.
* **Type**: The type of incident that occurred (e.g. Fire, Other).
* **Status**: The current status of the incident (e.g. Confirmed, Unconfirmed, In Progress, Resolved).
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or notes regarding the incident.
* **Notes**: Any additional information about the incident.
* **Status Updated**: The date the status was updated.
* **Incident Report Status**: The final status of the incident report (e.g. Open, Closed).
