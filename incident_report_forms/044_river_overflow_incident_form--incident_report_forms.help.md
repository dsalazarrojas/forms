# River Overflow Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about river overflow incidents. Fill out this form to provide a comprehensive description of the incident, including its location, type, and response status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details (Page 1: Incident Details).
2. Specify the name of the river affected (Page 1: river_name).
3. Provide the location of the incident (Page 1: location).
4. Describe the type of incident (Page 1: incident_type).
5. Indicate the severity of the incident (Page 1: severity).
6. Select the date and time of the incident (Page 1: incident_date and incident_time).
7. Identify the areas affected by the incident (Page 1: affected_areas).
8. Report the current response status (Page 1: response_status).
9. Add any additional response details (Page 1: response_details).
10. Enter the date of the response (Page 1: response_date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details**: Enter a short description of the incident, including any relevant details such as causes, consequences, or actions taken.
* **River Name**: Specify the name of the river affected by the incident.
* **Location**: Provide the exact location of the incident, including any geographical coordinates if possible.
* **Description**: Enter a detailed description of the incident, including any supporting information or evidence.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred, which can be Flood, Drought, Water Shortage, or Water Quality Issue.
* **Severity**: Choose the severity level of the incident, which can be Low, Moderate, High, or Critical.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM:SS AM/PM.
* **Affected Areas**: Identify the areas affected by the incident, which can be Downstream, Upstream, or Both.
* **Response Status**: Report the current response status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Response Details**: Add any additional information about the response efforts or measures taken.
* **Response Date**: Enter the date of the response in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Ensure you submit a report for each incident separately.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* Provide as much detail as possible, but be concise and focused on the essential facts.
