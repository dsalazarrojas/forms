# streetlight_failure_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "streetlight_failure_incident_form" is a form designed for reporting streetlight failures. It is used to gather information about the incident, including the streetlight's ID, location, and condition, as well as contact details of the submitter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the problem in the "description_of_problem" field.
2. Provide the streetlight's ID in the "streetlight_id" field.
3. Enter the location of the streetlight in the "address" field.
4. Select the condition of the streetlight from the "streetlight_condition" dropdown menu.
5. If available, attach a photo of the failed streetlight in the "photo_evidence" field.
6. Select the submit option from the "submit" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
9. Provide any additional information about the incident in the "additional_info" field.
10. Select how you want to submit the report: by submitting directly, emailing, or calling.

  * This is the title of the form, which should be filled out with a brief description of the problem.
