## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document information about an incident involving a fire that has occurred. It's used for reporting, documenting, and investigating the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer all questions carefully and accurately, and make sure to include any necessary details. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for guidance from your supervisor or the incident investigator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: <i class="fa fa-calendar" aria-hidden="true"></i> (1) - Please enter the date the incident occurred in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Location**: <i class="fa fa-map-pin" aria-hidden="true"></i> (2) - Describe the location where the incident took place, including the address, building, or area.
* **Incident Description**: <i class="fa fa-commenting" aria-hidden="true"></i> (3) - Provide a brief but detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Witnesses**: <i class="fa fa-users" aria-hidden="true"></i> (4) - Select from the list of possible witnesses: Firefighter, Witness, or Other. Please note that this information will be used to identify and contact potential witnesses.
* **Damage Reported**: <i class="fa fa-building" aria-hidden="true"></i> (5) - Report the amount of damage sustained during the incident, using numbers (e.g., 0 = no damage, 1 = minor damage).
