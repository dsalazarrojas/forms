# Leak Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Leak Intake Form is used to report incidents related to leaks in the system. It helps to gather essential information about the leak, such as location, type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps mentioned above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location**: Enter the place where the leak occurred. This could be a specific room, building, or section.
* **Describe the leak**: Write a clear and detailed description of the leak, including any symptoms or signs that you noticed.
* **Type of leak**: Select one of the options provided to indicate the type of leak (e.g., Pipe, Pump, Drain, or Other).
* **Date of leak**: If possible, enter the date of the leak in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of leak**: Choose the time the leak started.
* **Photo of leak**: If you have a photo of the leak, upload it to provide visual evidence.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or observations about the leak.
* **Assign to**: Select one or more individuals from the options provided to be assigned to handle the issue. This could be a team member or a specific role.
* **Category**: Choose a category that best describes the issue (e.g., Leak, Water, Maintenance, etc.).
* **Priority**: Select the priority level of the issue. This will help determine the urgency and attention required.
* **Status**: Provide a brief update on the status of the issue (e.g., Open, In Progress, Resolved, etc.).
