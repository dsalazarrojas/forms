# Flood Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flood Incident Form is used to report flood incidents to the local authorities. This form helps emergency services respond promptly and effectively to flood events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the location of the incident.
2. Choose the type of flood incident (e.g., Flood, Storm surge, Flash flood, Landslide, or Other).
3. Enter the date of the incident in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
4. Enter the time of the incident in the 12:00 AM format.
5. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
6. If applicable, select the number of people affected by the incident.
7. If emergency services were contacted, select the services that were contacted (e.g., Fire department, Police department, Other).
8. Select who reported the incident (e.g., Emergency services, Local authority, Community member).
9. If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, enter their name or contact information.
10. If you are reporting as a member of the emergency services, describe their response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident location** (incident_location): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident type** (incident_type): Select the type of flood incident that occurred (e.g., Flood, Storm surge, Flash flood, Landslide, or Other).
* **Incident date** (incident_date): Enter the date of the incident in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Incident time** (incident_time): Enter the time of the incident in the 12:00 AM format.
* **Incident description** (incident_description): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the flood incident.
* **Affected population** (affected_population): If applicable, enter the number of people affected by the incident.
* **Emergency services contacted** (emergency_services_contacted): Select the emergency services that were contacted (e.g., Fire department, Police department, Other).
* **Incident reported by** (incident_reported_by): Select who reported the incident (e.g., Emergency services, Local authority, Community member).
* **Incident reported number** (incident_reported_number): If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, enter their name or contact information.
* **Emergency services response** (emergency_services_response): Describe the response of emergency services if you are reporting as a member of those services.
