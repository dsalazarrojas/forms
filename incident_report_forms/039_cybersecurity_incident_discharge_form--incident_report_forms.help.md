# Cybersecurity Incident Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Incident Discharge Form is used to document and discharge cybersecurity incidents within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand the purpose and scope.
2. Select the correct incident date from the calendar picker.
3. Choose the type of incident that occurred from the options provided.
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the text field.
5. Identify the threat actor(s) involved in the incident.
6. Select the affected assets that were compromised during the incident.
7. Indicate the time when the incident was first reported.
8. Select the current status of the incident.
9. Enter the name and title of the person who reported the incident.
10. Optionally, enter additional information about the incident reporter (e.g. department, email address).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Select the date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident from the provided options (e.g. Malware, Phishing, Ransomware, Other). If unsure, select "Other" and provide details.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident. Be as specific as possible about the incident's impact, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Threat Actor**: Identify who or what was responsible for the incident (e.g. Human, Nation-state, Other). If unsure, select "Other" and provide details.
* **Affected Asset**: Identify the assets that were compromised during the incident (e.g. Computer Network, Data, Software, Hardware, Personnel). Select all that apply.
* **Incident Reported**: Indicate when the incident was first reported (e.g. 2022-01-01 12:00).
* **Incident Status**: Choose the current status of the incident from the provided options (e.g. Active, Resolved, Inactive).
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter your name and title for reporting the incident.
* **Incident Reported By**: Optionally, provide additional information about yourself, such as your department and email address.
