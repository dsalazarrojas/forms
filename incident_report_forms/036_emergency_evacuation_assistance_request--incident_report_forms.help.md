# emergency-evacuation-assistance-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting emergency evacuation assistance in situations such as natural disasters, accidents, or other crises. It is used to provide critical information about incidents to help responders prioritize and allocate resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your request, which is the summary of your incident.
2. Choose the type of incident, such as Evacuation, Evacuation due to weather, or Emergency assistance.
3. Provide the location of the incident in the "location" field.
4. Describe the incident in the "description" field, giving as much detail as possible.
5. Enter the name and phone number of your contact person in case responders need to reach out to you.
6. Enter your contact email address so that responders can get in touch with you.
7. If you need assistance with items such as food, clothing, or transportation, choose the type from the drop-down list.
8. Add any additional information about the assistance you need in the "assistance-notes" field.
9. If you have designated an emergency contact, enter their name and contact information in the "emergency-contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Emergency Evacuation Assistance Request Form** (1, text): Enter a brief summary of the incident that requires assistance.
- **incident-type** (2, select_one): Choose the type of incident, such as Evacuation, Evacuation due to weather, or Emergency assistance.
- **location** (3, text): Enter the location of the incident, including city, state, or zip code.
- **description** (4, text): Describe the incident in detail, including any specific needs or circumstances.
- **contact-name** (5, text): Enter the name of the person who can be contacted about the incident.
- **contact-phone** (6, text): Enter the phone number of the person who can be contacted about the incident.
- **contact-email** (7, email): Enter the email address of the person who can be contacted about the incident.
- **assistance-type** (8, select_one): Choose the type of assistance needed, such as Transportation, Shelter, Food, Clothing, or Other.
- **assistance-notes** (9, note): Add any additional information about the assistance you need.
- **emergency-contact** (10, text): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
