# Security Incident Response Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document information about security incidents that have occurred within an organization. It helps in tracking the status of incidents, facilitating easier reference and review.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by selecting the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Malware/Security Breach, Unauthorized Access, Data Theft, etc.).
2.  Specify the location where the incident occurred.
3.  Enter the date and time when the incident occurred.
4.  Choose the status of the incident (Open, Closed, Resolved).
5.  Provide a brief description of the cause of the incident.
6.  Enter a detailed description of the incident.
7.  Select the category of the incident (e.g., IT Security, Facilities, Physical Security, Network Security).
8.  Select the person who reported the incident.
9.  Select the person who reviewed the incident.
10. Choose the status of the review (Open, Closed, Completed).
11. Select the person who reviewed the incident.
12. Enter the resolution taken for the incident.
13. Enter the date and time when the incident was resolved.
14. Enter any follow-up information related to the incident.
15. Enter the date and time of the follow-up.
16. Enter any additional comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`): Enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Malware/Security Breach, Unauthorized Access, Data Theft, etc.).
* **Affected Assets** (`affected_assets`): Enter the assets affected by the incident (e.g., servers, computers, etc.).
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date/Time** (`incident_date_time`): Enter the date and time when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`): Choose the status of the incident (Open, Closed, Resolved).
* **Incident Resolved Date/Time** (`incident_resolved_date_time`): Enter the date and time when the incident was resolved.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`): Enter a brief description of the cause of the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`): Select the category of the incident (e.g., IT Security, Facilities, Physical Security, Network Security).
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`): Select the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reviewer** (`incident_reviewer`): Select the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Incident Review Status** (`incident_review_status`): Choose the status of the review (Open, Closed, Completed).
* **Incident Reviewed By** (`incident_reviewed_by`): Enter the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Incident Resolution** (`incident_resolution`): Enter the resolution taken for the incident.
* **Incident Resolution Date/Time** (`incident_resolution_date_time`): Enter the date and time when the incident was resolved.
* **Incident Follow-up** (`incident_follow_up`): Enter any follow-up information related to the incident.
* **Incident Follow-up Date/Time** (`incident_follow_up_date_time`): Enter the date and time of the follow-up.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the incident.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information to ensure that the incident is properly documented and resolved.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer to the above explanations or consult with the incident response team.
