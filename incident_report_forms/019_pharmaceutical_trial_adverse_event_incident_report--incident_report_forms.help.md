# Pharmaceutical Trial Adverse Event Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report adverse events that occurred during a pharmaceutical trial. This form collects information on the incident, including the sponsor, date and location of the event, and details about the event, including the adverse event cause, type, and outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the sponsor of the trial (Sponsor, Research Site, Investigator, or Other)
2. Enter the date and time of the event
3. Enter the location where the event occurred
4. Provide a brief summary of the incident
5. Describe the cause of the adverse event
6. Select the type of adverse event (Adverse Event, Serious Adverse Event, Serious and Unexpected Adverse Event, or Unexpected Adverse Event)
7. Select the patient's sex (Male, Female, or Other)
8. Enter any details about the medication involved, including the dose and frequency
9. Enter the start and stop dates of the medication
10. Provide a reason for stopping the medication (if applicable)
11. Describe the outcome of the event
12. Enter the follow-up dates

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor (2)**: Select the sponsor of the trial. This can be the company conducting the trial, the research site, the investigator, or another entity.
* **Date Of Event (3)**: Enter the date of the event. This is the date when the adverse event occurred.
* **Time Of Event (4)**: Enter the time of the event. This is the time when the adverse event occurred.
* **Location Of Event (5)**: Enter the location where the event occurred.
* **Incident Summary (1)**: Provide a brief summary of the incident.
* **Adverse Event Cause (7)**: Describe the cause of the adverse event.
* **Adverse Event Type (8)**: Select the type of adverse event. This can be Adverse Event, Serious Adverse Event, Serious and Unexpected Adverse Event, or Unexpected Adverse Event.
* **Patient (9)**: Select the sex of the patient. This can be Male, Female, or Other.
* **Medication (10)**: Enter any details about the medication involved, including the dose and frequency.
* **Dose (11)**: Enter the dose of the medication involved in the adverse event.
* **Frequency (12)**: Enter the frequency of the medication involved in the adverse event.
* **Start Date (13)**: Enter the start date of the medication involved in the adverse event.
* **Stop Date (14)**: Enter the stop date of the medication involved in the adverse event.
* **Reason For Stopping (15)**: Provide a reason for stopping the medication (if applicable).
* **Outcome (16)**: Describe the outcome of the event.
* **Followup Date (17)**: Enter the follow-up date for the event.
* **Next Followup Date (18)**: Enter the next follow-up date for the event.
