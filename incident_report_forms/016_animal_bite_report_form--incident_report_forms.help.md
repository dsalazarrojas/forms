# Animal Bite Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Bite Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents involving animal bites. It will be used to document the incident, gather necessary data, and help prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the reporter's details, including their full name and contact information.
2. Provide the date of the report and the date of the incident.
3. Describe the location and circumstances of the incident.
4. Specify the type of animal involved and its characteristics.
5. Detail the victim's contact information and any injuries sustained.
6. Answer questions about the animal's vaccination status, behavior, and any other relevant details.
7. Specify the location and nature of any bite injuries.
8. Confirm whether medical treatment was sought.
9. Describe the circumstances surrounding the incident.
10. Confirm whether the animal was provoked or not.
11. Provide any witness information.
12. Confirm whether the animal is known for aggressive behavior.
13. Notify animal control if necessary.
14. Specify whether follow-up action is required.
15. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporter Full Name** (`reporter_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Contact Information** (`reporter_contact`, text, required): Provide your phone number and email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date of Report** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the current date when filling out this form.
* **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`, text, required): Describe the location of the incident in detail.
* **Type of Location** (`location_type`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Private residence, Public park, Workplace, School, Veterinary clinic, Animal shelter, Street or sidewalk, or Other public place.
* **Victim Full Name** (`victim_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person bitten.
* **Victim Date of Birth** (`victim_dob`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the person bitten.
* **Victim Contact Information** (`victim_contact`, text, required): Provide the phone number and email address of the person bitten.
* **Animal Information** (`animal_information`, note, optional): Provide any additional details about the animal involved.
* **Species of Animal** (`animal_species`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Dog, Cat, Horse, Rodent, Wildlife, Livestock, Other, or Unknown.
* **Breed if Known** (`animal_breed`, text, optional): If you know the animal's breed, please describe it.
* **Physical Description** (`animal_description`, text, optional): Describe the color, size, and any other distinguishing features of the animal.
* **Is the animal Owned** (`animal_owner`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Yes - known owner, Stray - no owner, Wildlife, Unknown.
* **Owner Name and Contact** (`owner_name`, text, optional): If the animal is owned, provide the owner's name and contact information.
* **Is the animal's Vaccination Status Known** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Yes - current on vaccinations, Yes - not current, No - unknown, Not applicable - wildlife.
* **Bite Details** (`bite_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional details about the incident, such as the circumstances surrounding the bite.
* **Body Area Bitten** (`body_area_bitten`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Hand or fingers, Arm, Leg or foot, Face or head, Neck, Torso, Multiple areas, or Other.
* **Severity of Injury** (`injury_severity`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Minor - no broken skin, Minor - superficial scratches, Moderate - puncture wounds, Severe - deep lacerations, Severe - required medical attention, Critical - hospitalization required.
* **Medical Treatment Sought** (`medical_treatment`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: No treatment needed, Yes - first aid only, Yes - doctor visit, Yes - emergency room, Yes - hospitalized.
* **Describe the Circumstances** (`incident_circumstances`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Was the Animal Provoked** (`provocation`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: No - unprovoked, Yes - accidental, Yes - intentional, Unsure.
* **Witnesses** (`witnesses`, text, optional): Provide any information about witnesses, if applicable.
* **Is this Animal Known for Aggressive Behavior?** (`previous_incidents`, select_one, optional): If the animal is known for aggressive behavior, choose Yes - known history.
* **Was Animal Control Notified?** (`animal_control_notified`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Yes - already notified, No - will notify, Not applicable.
* **Is Follow-up Required?** (`follow_up_required`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided: Yes - investigation needed, Yes - quarantine monitoring, No further action.
* **Reporter Signature** (`reporter_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as the reporter.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the current date when signing the form.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely. If you are unsure about any information, please contact your local animal control or health authority for guidance.
