# customs_breach_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report incidents related to customs breaches. It helps to gather accurate information about the incident, including its date, type, location, and status, and provides a platform for reporting and tracking progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure that all relevant information is captured.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`1`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Breach Type** (`2`, `select_one`, `Human Error, System Failure, Other`): Choose the type of breach that occurred (`Human Error, System Failure, or Other`).
* **Affected Location** (`3`, `text`, `required: `false`): Provide the location affected by the breach.
* **Description** (`4`, `text`, `required: `false`): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Details** (`5`, `text`, `required: `false`): Enter any additional details about the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (`6`, `text`, `required: `false`): Enter the name or ID of the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`7`, `select_one`, `Open, Closed, In Progress`): Choose the status of the incident (`Open, Closed, or In Progress`).
* **Incident Type** (`8`, `select_one`, `System Incident, Non-System Incident`): Choose the type of incident (`System Incident or Non-System Incident`).
* **Reported By** (`9`, `text`, `required: `false`): Enter the name or ID of the person who reported the incident.
* **Report Comments** (`10`, `text`, `required: `false`): Enter any comments or notes about the report.
* **Incident Status Comments** (`11`, `text`, `required: `false`): Enter any comments or notes about the incident status.
* **Assigned Tool** (`12`, `text`, `required: `false`): Enter the tool or equipment assigned to the incident.
* **Created** (`13`, `date`, `required: `false`): This field is not editable.
* **Due Date** (`14`, `date`, `required: `false`): This field is not editable.
* **Priority** (`15`, `select_one`, `Low, Medium, High`): Choose the priority level of the incident (`Low, Medium, or High`).
* **Assigned To** (`16`, `text`, `required: `false`): Enter the name or ID of the person assigned to the incident.
* **Incident Reported By** (`17`, `text`, `required: `false`): Enter the name or ID of the person who reported the incident.

Note: If you are unsure about any of these fields, it is recommended to consult with the relevant stakeholders or refer to the organization's incident management process guidelines.
