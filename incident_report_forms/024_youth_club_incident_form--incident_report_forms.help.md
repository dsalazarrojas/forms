# Youth Club Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Youth Club Incident Form" is a tool for documenting incidents that occur within a youth club. It helps gather information about the incident, its location, and its resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the date when the incident occurred in the "Date of Incident" field.
2.  Enter the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
3.  Answer whether an incident occurred by selecting either "Yes" or "No" in the "Type of Incident" field.
4.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Description of Incident" field.
5.  Specify who reported the incident in the "Reported By" field.
6.  Indicate if there were any witnesses by selecting either "Yes" or "No" in the "Witnesses" field.
7.  Document the resolution or outcome of the incident in the "Resolution" field.
8.  (Optional) Record the findings of the investigation in the "Investigation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the incident, such as a room or a specific area within the club.
*   **Type of Incident** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether an incident did occur by selecting "Yes" or "No".
*   **Description of Incident** (`incident_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
*   **Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, `text`, required): Specify who reported the incident. This could be the person who experienced the incident or someone else who witnessed it.
*   **Witnesses** (`incident_witnesses`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if anyone witnessed the incident by selecting "Yes" or "No". Even if there are no witnesses, you can still submit the form.
*   **Resolution** (`incident_resolution`, `text`, required): Describe the outcome or resolution of the incident.
*   **Investigation** (`incident_investigation`, `text`, optional): (Optional) Document the findings of the investigation, if conducted.
