# Incident Report Closure Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to formally acknowledge and document the closure of an incident. This process helps to ensure transparency and accuracy in tracking incident status and prevents duplication of effort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Select the status of the incident as 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the incident has been formally closed.
* Enter the full name of the person who closed the incident.
* Enter the date and time of the incident close in the corresponding format (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM:SS AM/PM).
* Provide a brief reason for closing the incident. This should clearly explain the actions taken to resolve the incident.
* If desired, enter a note about the incident close. This could include additional context or information about the closure.
* Enter the email address of the person who closed the incident.
* Optionally, enter the phone number of the person who closed the incident.
* Provide a comment about the incident close. This could include any additional context or information about the closure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Report Closer** (<name>, <type: select_one>): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the incident has been formally closed.
* **Report Closer** (<name, <type: text>): Enter the full name of the person who closed the incident.
* **Report Date** (<name, <type: date>): Enter the date of the incident close in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Report Time** (<name, <type: time>): Enter the time of the incident close in the 12 hour AM/PM format.
* **Report Closer Reason** (<name, <type: text>): Enter a brief reason for closing the incident. This should clearly explain the actions taken to resolve the incident.
* **Report Closer Note** (<name, <type: note>): If desired, enter a note about the incident close. This could include additional context or information about the closure.
* **Report Closer Email** (<name, <type: email>): Enter the email address of the person who closed the incident.
* **Report Closer Phone** (<name, <type: text>): Optionally, enter the phone number of the person who closed the incident.
* **Report Closer Comment** (<name, <type: note>): Provide a comment about the incident close. This could include any additional context or information about the closure.
