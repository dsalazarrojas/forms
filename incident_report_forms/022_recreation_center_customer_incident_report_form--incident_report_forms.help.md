# Recreation Center Customer Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recreation Center Customer Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur at the recreation center, such as injuries, property damage, or other types of incidents. This form helps customers report incidents in a structured and detailed manner, ensuring that the recreation center management can respond effectively and take necessary actions.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in the incident details, including the incident type, date, time, description, location, cause, and any witnesses.
3. If applicable, select the incident type (injury, property damage, etc.).
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
5. Fill in the reporter's information, including their name, contact, department, position, and any additional extensions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Details

* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (Injury, Property Damage, etc.).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Cause**: Explain the reason or cause of the incident.
* **Incident Witnesses**: List any witnesses who saw or heard about the incident.
* **Incident Resolution**: Explain how the incident was resolved or handled.
* **Incident Safety Procedure**: Describe the safety procedures that were followed or not followed during the incident.
* **Incident Safety Measures**: Describe any safety measures that were taken after the incident.

### Incident Reporter's Information

* **Incident Reporter's Name**: Enter your name.
* **Incident Reporter's Contact**: Enter your contact information (phone number, email, etc.).
* **Incident Reporter's Department**: Enter your department or section.
* **Incident Reporter's Position**: Enter your position or title.
* **Incident Reporter's Extensions**: Enter any additional extensions or information.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- If you are unsure about any information, ask for assistance or clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
