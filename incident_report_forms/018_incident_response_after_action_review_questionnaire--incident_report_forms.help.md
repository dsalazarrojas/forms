# Incident Response After-Action Review Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help incident responders and commanders review and reflect on their response efforts, identifying areas of success and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer the initial questions about the incident, including the initial report, response plan, and resources deployed.
2.  Describe the outcomes of the response efforts and the lessons learned from the experience.
3.  Identify any notable challenges faced during the response efforts.
4.  Evaluate the effectiveness of the response team, including their preparedness and ability to respond effectively.
5.  Provide recommendations for future responses and identify areas that require further action.
6.  Summarize your satisfaction with the response efforts and any notable successes or achievements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the initial incident report?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_1`): Provide a detailed description of the incident report, including any relevant details and information.
* **Who was the incident commander?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_2`): Enter the name and title of the incident commander, if applicable.
* **What was the initial response plan?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_3`): Describe the initial response plan and its goals, including any specific objectives and targets.
* **What were the primary response resources deployed?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_4`): Select all resources that were used during the response, such as emergency responders, communication equipment, medical resources, or logistics support.
* **What were the outcomes of the response efforts?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_5`): Describe the outcomes of the response efforts, including any successes or setbacks.
* **What were the lessons learned from the response efforts?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_6`): Provide any lessons learned from the experience, including any areas for improvement or best practices.
* **Was there a need for additional response resources?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_7`): Evaluate whether additional resources were needed during the response.
* **Were there any notable challenges faced during the response efforts?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_8`): Describe any notable challenges faced during the response efforts.
* **What were the primary causes of the incident?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_9`): Identify the primary causes of the incident, such as natural disaster, human error, or technological failure.
* **What were the primary recommendations for future responses?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_10`): Provide recommendations for future response efforts, including any areas of improvement or best practices.
* **Was the communication plan effective?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_11`): Evaluate the effectiveness of the communication plan during the response.
* **What were the results of the incident review?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_12`): Summarize the results of the incident review.
* **Were there any notable successes or achievements during the response efforts?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_13`): Describe any notable successes or achievements during the response efforts.
* **What were the primary areas that required further action after the incident?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_14`): Identify areas that required further action after the incident.
* **How did you feel about the overall response efforts?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_15`): Evaluate your satisfaction with the response efforts.
* **Was the response team adequately prepared for the incident?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_16`): Evaluate the response team's preparedness for the incident.
* **Were there any notable issues with the incident response plan?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_17`): Identify any issues with the incident response plan.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of the incident response team?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_18`): Evaluate the incident response team's effectiveness.
* **Was the response team able to respond effectively?** (`incident_response_after_action_review_19`): Evaluate the response team's ability to respond effectively.

## Tips
When filling out this form, users should:

*   Provide as much detail as possible in their responses.
*   Ensure they answer all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Review and reflect on the incident thoroughly before completing this form.
*   Consider any lessons learned and recommendations for future responses.
*   Evaluate the effectiveness of the response team and their ability to respond effectively.
*   Identify areas that require further action after the incident.
*   Be honest and thorough in their evaluations and recommendations.
