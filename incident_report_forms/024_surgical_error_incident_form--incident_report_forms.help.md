# Surgical Error Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgical Error Incident Form is designed to collect information on incidents that may have occurred during a surgical procedure. This form helps identify and report any potential errors that could impact patient care and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Incident Report Form" page.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required."
3. Ensure that you fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
4. If you encounter any issues or need help, don't hesitate to contact your hospital's administration or the person in charge of incident reports.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Incident Report Form** (`incident_report_form`, text, required)
Fill out the "Incident Report Form" section with a brief description of the incident, including the relevant details about what happened during the procedure.

### 2. **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required)
Enter the date the incident occurred.

### 3. **Surgeon's Name** (`surgeon_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the surgeon who performed the procedure.

### 4. **Surgical Error** (`surgical_error`, select multiple, required)
Select the options that best describe the type of surgical error or incident that occurred, from the options 'Yes' and 'No'.

### 5. **Patient's Safety Outcome** (`patient_safety_outcome`, note, required)
Fill out this section with any additional comments about the patient's safety outcome after the procedure. Please be as detailed as possible.
