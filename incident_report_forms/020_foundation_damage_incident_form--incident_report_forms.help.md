# Foundation Damage Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foundation Damage Incident Form is a crucial tool for reporting and recording incidents of damage to foundations. It ensures that all relevant details are captured accurately and consistently, facilitating effective investigation and resolution of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the contact name and email (if available) of the individual or team member responsible for reporting the incident.
2. Provide the phone number and address of the contact person, if applicable.
3. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident.
5. Write a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details and observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): This field is used to record the name of the person or team responsible for reporting the incident.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): If the contact person has an email address, enter it here.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person, if applicable.
- **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Record the address of the contact person, if applicable.
- **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident in the format 12:00 PM.
- **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Write a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details and observations.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the contact information to ensure it's up-to-date and correct.
- Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
- If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, consult with a supervisor or colleague for guidance.
