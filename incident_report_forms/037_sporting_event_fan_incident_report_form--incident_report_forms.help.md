# Sporting Event Fan Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sporting Event Fan Incident Report Form is a tool used to collect information about incidents that occurred during sporting events. This information will be used to improve the safety and security of future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Incident Report Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Event Details**: This field is optional but will help us understand the context of the incident.
* **Incident Location**: Please indicate the exact location of the incident, such as a specific seat or section in the venue.
* **Incident Description**: This field should describe what happened during the incident, including any injuries or damage caused.
* **Incident Type**: Please select the type of incident from the options provided:
	+ Medical: This includes any medical emergencies, injuries, or illnesses.
	+ Security: This includes any incidents involving security personnel or law enforcement.
	+ Crowd Control: This includes any incidents involving crowd management or crowd-related issues.
	+ Other: This is for any other type of incident not listed above.
* **Incident Cause**: Please describe the cause of the incident, such as an accident or a deliberate action.
* **Incident Consequence**: Describe the consequences of the incident, including any injuries, damage, or disruption.
* **Incident Resolution**: If the incident has been resolved, check "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **Follow-up Information**: If follow-up is required, select "True" or "False" accordingly.
* **Incident Reporter**: Please enter your name.
* **Incident Reporter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Incident Reporter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Incident Reporter Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Incident Reporter Position**: Enter your position.
* **Incident Reporter Supervisor**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Incident Reporter Supervisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of your supervisor.
* **Incident Reporter Supervisor Email**: Enter the email address of your supervisor.
* **Incident Reporter Supervisor Department**: Enter the department of your supervisor.
* **Incident Reporter Supervisor Position**: Enter the position of your supervisor.
* **Additional Information**: This field is for any other information you would like to provide.
* **Comments**: This field is for any comments or suggestions you would like to make.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your report.
