# Stop Work Authority Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stop Work Authority Reporting Form is used to document and report on incidents that have occurred on a project. This form is used to gather information about the incident, such as the date and time it occurred, details about what happened, and the current status of whether work has stopped or will stop due to the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the incident date from the calendar pop-up or manually enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. Enter the time when the incident occurred. Please use the time format 12-hour clock (e.g., 08:00 AM).
3. In the incident_details field, provide a brief description of what happened.
4. In the incident_description field, provide more details about the incident.
5. Answer the question "Stop Work Authority" by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
6. If work stopped, provide a reason for stopping work in the reason_for_stopping_work field.
7. In the next_steps field, describe any next steps that will be taken as a result of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. reporting_form (id: 1)
* **reporting_form**: This is a text field where you can enter any additional comments or information related to the incident.

### 2. incident_date (id: 2)
* **incident_date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred. Please use the calendar pop-up or manually enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### 3. incident_time (id: 3)
* **incident_time**: Enter the time when the incident occurred. Please use the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 08:00 AM).

### 4. incident_details (id: 4)
* **incident_details**: Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.

### 5. incident_description (id: 5)
* **incident_description**: Provide more details about the incident.

### 6. stop_work_authority (id: 6)
* **Stop Work Authority**: Answer "Yes" if work stopped due to the incident, "No" otherwise.

### 7. reason_for_stopping_work (id: 8)
* **reason_for_stopping_work**: If work stopped, provide a reason for stopping work.

### 8. next_steps (id: 7)
* **next_steps**: Describe any next steps that will be taken as a result of the incident.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your team or project manager.
* Make sure to save your progress as you fill out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
