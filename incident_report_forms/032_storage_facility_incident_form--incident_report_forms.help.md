# Storage Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Storage Facility Incident Form is designed to gather essential details about incidents that occur within storage facilities. This form helps in the collection of accurate information, aiding in the prompt investigation, analysis, and resolution of the incident. It is crucial to report all incidents, regardless of their severity or nature, to ensure the safety and well-being of all personnel and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure the form is completed accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure the incident has not been previously reported or documented. If in doubt, consult with management or relevant authorities.
2.  Enter a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field (page 1).
3.  Select the relevant incident type from the options provided in the "Incident Type" field (page 1).
4.  Choose the "Incident Date" (page 1) and "Incident Time" (page 1) fields accurately, ensuring correct dates and times are recorded.
5.  Enter the "Storage Facility ID" (page 1) number, which helps identify the specific facility where the incident occurred.
6.  In the "Incident Location" field (page 2), provide a detailed description of the location where the incident took place.
7.  In the "Incident Description" field (page 2), give a comprehensive description of the incident, including what happened, the circumstances surrounding it, and any notable details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Incident Description** (`storage_facility_incident_form_page_1`, `text`, Required): A detailed explanation of what happened during the incident, including any notable details that can help in the analysis and resolution process.
*  **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, Required): The date on which the incident occurred. Enter the date accurately to assist in organizing and tracking incidents chronologically.
*  **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, Required): The time of day when the incident took place. This information helps in scheduling follow-up actions and investigations.
*  **Storage Facility ID** (`storage_facility_id`, `number`, Required): A unique identifier for the storage facility where the incident happened. This helps in identifying the specific location and tracking incidents associated with it.
*  **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, Required): Choose from pre-defined options such as "Accident/Injury" or "Near Miss." This helps in categorizing incidents for further analysis and response.
*  **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, Optional): Provide a detailed description or address of the specific location within the storage facility where the incident occurred.
*  **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, Required): A comprehensive description of the incident, including its causes, effects, and any relevant context.

## Tips
- Be accurate and specific when filling out the form to ensure clear understanding of the incident.
- If unsure about any field or process, consult with management or relevant authorities for assistance.
- Keep the descriptions clear and concise, with an emphasis on providing relevant details that aid in investigation and resolution.
- If multiple incidents are reported, ensure each form is filled out accurately and uniquely.
