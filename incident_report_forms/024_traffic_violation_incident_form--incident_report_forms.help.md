# traffic_violation_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about traffic violation incidents, such as accidents or citations, involving vehicles on the road.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that you are reporting a valid incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident, if known.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required/optional): Enter the make of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the year of the vehicle involved in the incident.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and clear information to ensure that the incident is properly recorded.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor for guidance.
* Only use this form for traffic-related incidents.
