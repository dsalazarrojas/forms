# sporting_event_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and report incidents related to sporting events. It helps to document the details of the incident, including the type, location, and description, as well as any associated media (image, video, audio). The form also allows for review and management of the incident, including reviewer information and review status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Incident, Injury, or Other).
2. Enter the date and time of the incident.
3. Provide the location where the incident occurred.
4. Describe the incident in detail.
5. (Optional) Add any relevant images or videos to support your report.
6. Enter a caption for the image or video.
7. Provide any audio recordings related to the incident.
8. Enter a caption for the audio recording.
9. If you are reporting the incident, enter your name and contact information (incident Reporter).
10. Select the review status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
11. Enter any review comments or feedback.
12. Provide the reviewer's name and contact information (Reviewer).
13. Record the date and time the review was completed.
14. If the incident has been resolved, select "True" for incident_closed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Type** (`Incident`, `Injury`, `Other`): Select the type of incident that occurred.
- **Incident Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the incident.
- **Incident Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the incident.
- **Incident Location** (`text`): Provide the location where the incident occurred.
- **Incident Description** (`text`): Describe the incident in detail.
- **Incident Image** (`text`): Add any relevant images to support your report.
- **Incident Image Alt** (`text`): Enter a brief description of the image.
- **Incident Image Caption** (`text`): Enter a caption for the image.
- **Incident Video** (`text`): Add any relevant videos to support your report.
- **Incident Video Alt** (`text`): Enter a brief description of the video.
- **Incident Video Caption** (`text`): Enter a caption for the video.
- **Incident Audio** (`text`): Add any relevant audio recordings to support your report.
- **Incident Audio Alt** (`text`): Enter a brief description of the audio.
- **Incident Audio Caption** (`text`): Enter a caption for the audio.
- **Incident Reporter** (`text`): Enter your name and contact information if you are reporting the incident.
- **Review Status** (`Pending`, `Approved`, `Rejected`): Select the review status of the incident.
- **Review Comments** (`text`): Enter any review comments or feedback.
- **Reviewer** (`text`): Enter the reviewer's name and contact information.
- **Review Date** (`date`): Enter the date the review was completed.
- **Review Time** (`time`): Enter the time the review was completed.
- **Incident Closed** (`True`, `False`): Select "True" if the incident has been resolved.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to add any relevant media (images, videos, audio) to support your report.
* Enter the reviewer's contact information if you are not the original reporter.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
