# Substance Discrepancy Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Substance Discrepancy Report form is a tool used to document and track reported substance mismatches or discrepancies in a facility or process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including:
	* Report Details: Provide a brief description of the incident.
	* Incident Date: Enter the date the incident was reported.
	* Incident Location: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
	* Substance Name: Enter the name of the substance involved in the incident.
	* Mismatched Substances: Select 'Yes' if the incident involved a mismatched substance, 'No' otherwise.
	* Mismatched Substances Details: Provide additional information about the mismatched substances.
	* Substance Name (for mismatched substances): Enter the name of the mismatched substance.
	* Substance Type (for mismatched substances): Enter the type of the mismatched substance.
	* Quantity (for mismatched substances): Enter the quantity of the mismatched substance.
	* Incident Reporter: Enter your name and title.
	* Incident Manager: Enter the name and title of the person responsible for managing the incident.
	* Date Reported: Enter the date the incident was reported.
	* Investigation Result: Provide a brief description of the outcome of the investigation.
	* Root Cause: Enter the root cause of the incident.
	* Recommended Actions: Enter any recommended actions or steps to be taken.
	* Additional Comments: Enter any additional comments about the incident.
	* Report Closed: Enter the date the report was closed.
	* Review Comments: Enter any comments made during the review process.
	* Review Date: Enter the date the review was made.
	* Review Status: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the review.
	* Status: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Details** (`report_details`, text, required): A brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): The date the incident was reported.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): The location where the incident occurred.
* **Substance Name** (`substance_name`, text, optional): The name of the substance involved in the incident.
* **Substance Description** (`substance_description`, text, optional): A description of the substance involved in the incident.
* **Mismatched Substances** (`mismatched_substances`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if the incident involved a mismatched substance, 'No' otherwise.
* **Mismatched Substances Details** (`mismatched_substances_details`, text, optional): Additional information about the mismatched substances.
* **Substance Name (for mismatched substances)** (`mismatched_substances_details_substance_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the mismatched substance.
* **Substance Type (for mismatched substances)** (`mismatched_substances_details_substance_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of the mismatched substance.
* **Quantity (for mismatched substances)** (`mismatched_substances_details_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the mismatched substance.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, optional): Enter your name and title.
* **Incident Manager** (`incident_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for managing the incident.
* **Date Reported** (`incident_date_reported`, date, optional): The date the incident was reported.
* **Investigation Result** (`investigation_result`, text, optional): A brief description of the outcome of the investigation.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, optional): Enter the root cause of the incident.
* **Recommended Actions** (`recommended_actions`, text, optional): Enter any recommended actions or steps to be taken.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the incident.
* **Report Closed** (`report_closed`, date, optional): Enter the date the report was closed.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments made during the review process.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the review was made.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the review.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the incident.
