# Baggage Handling Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents involving baggage handling during air travel, such as damaged, lost, or delayed baggage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
3. Use the select_one option for questions that require a choice from a list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section 1: -- Incident Information --
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time the incident occurred in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Location of Incident**: Enter the location where the incident happened, such as "Terminal 2" or "Baggage Claim 3".

### Section 2: -- Baggage Details --
* **Baggage Tag Number**: Enter the unique identifier number on the baggage tag.
* **Type of Baggage**: Enter the type of baggage (e.g., suitcase, backpack, duffel).
* **Color and Description**: Enter the color and a brief description of the baggage.
* **Condition of Baggage**: Select the condition of the baggage from the list (Intact with Minor Damage, Significantly Damaged, Completely Destroyed, Partially Missing, or Completely Missing).
* **Description of Damage (if applicable)**: Enter a detailed description of any damage to the baggage.
* **Estimated Value of Baggage and Contents**: Enter the estimated value of the baggage and its contents in dollars.

### Section 3: -- Passenger Information --
* **Passenger Name**: Enter the name of the passenger who was affected by the incident.
* **Passenger Email**: Enter the email address of the passenger.
* **Passenger Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the passenger.
* **Flight Confirmation Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the flight.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number.

### Section 4: -- Witness Information (if applicable) --
* **Witness Name (if applicable)**: Enter the name of any witness (if applicable).
* **Witness Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the witness (if applicable).

### Section 5: -- Resolution Information (if applicable) --
* **Compensation Claim Status**: Select the current status of the claim from the list (Claim Filed, Pending Review, Under Investigation, Resolved, or Denied).

### Section 6: -- Confirmation --
* **I Confirm This Report is Accurate and Complete**: Select "I Confirm" to confirm that the report is accurate and complete. If you are unsure, select "I Do Not Confirm".
