# Accident Investigation Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accident Investigation Report form is used to collect and document information about accidents that have occurred within the organization. This form is a critical tool for understanding the circumstances of the accident, identifying root causes, and implementing corrective actions to prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information in each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Use the provided options for fields with specific options, such as accident type and severity.
3. If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or the relevant department.
4. Ensure that you are not duplicating an incident that has already been investigated and reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Number** (`1`, `text`, required): A unique identifier for this investigation report.
* **Investigator Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Name of the person conducting the investigation.
* **Investigator Title** (`3`, `text`, required): Title or position of the investigator.
* **Accident Date** (`4`, `date`, required): Date when the accident occurred.
* **Accident Time** (`5`, `time`, required): Time when the accident occurred.
* **Investigation Date** (`6`, `date`, required): Date when the investigation was conducted.
* **Accident Location** (`7`, `text`, required): Specific location where the accident occurred.
* **Injured Person Name** (`8`, `text`, required): Full name of the person injured in the accident.
* **Injured Person Employee ID** (`9`, `text`, optional): Employee ID of the injured person (if applicable).
* **Injured Person Department** (`10`, `text`, optional): Department of the injured person (if applicable).
* **Accident Type** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Type of accident that occurred (e.g. Slip/Trip/Fall, Struck by Object, etc.).
* **Accident Severity** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Severity level of the accident (e.g. Fatality, Lost Time Injury, etc.).
* **Immediate Cause** (`13`, `text`, required): What was the immediate cause of the accident?
* **Root Causes** (`14`, `text`, required): What were the underlying root causes of the accident?
* **Contributing Factors** (`15`, `text`, required): What factors contributed to the accident?
* **Witnesses List** (`16`, `text`, optional): Names and contact information of witnesses.
* **Evidence Collected** (`17`, `text`, required): Description of evidence collected during investigation.
* **Corrective Actions Required** (`18`, `text`, required): What corrective actions are required to prevent recurrence?
* **Preventive Measures** (`19`, `text`, required): What preventive measures should be implemented?
* **Responsible Department** (`20`, `text`, required): Which department is responsible for implementing corrective actions?
* **Implementation Deadline** (`21`, `date`, required): Deadline for implementing corrective actions.
* **Follow Up Date** (`22`, `date`, required): Date for follow-up to verify corrective action implementation.
