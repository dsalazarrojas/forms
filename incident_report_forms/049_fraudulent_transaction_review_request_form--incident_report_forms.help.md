# Fraudulent Transaction Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fraudulent Transaction Review Request Form is a tool used to collect information about suspected fraudulent transactions. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are gathered and reviewed by the relevant team to prevent and detect fraudulent activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Transaction Details** page with information about the transaction, including any relevant dates.
2. On the **Suspect Info** page, enter details about the individual or entity suspected of committing the transaction.
3. On the **Transaction Amount** and **Transaction Type** pages, specify the amount and type of transaction.
4. Choose the **Suspect Location** where the transaction took place.
5. Select the **Suspect Phone** and **Suspect Email** of the individual or entity suspected.
6. Enter the suspect's **Suspect Address** if available.
7. Choose the **Suspiciousness Level** of the transaction, selecting from **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**.
8. Select the **Transaction Date** when the transaction occurred.
9. Enter your **Report By** name and any relevant details.
10. Optionally, specify the **Reason For Suspiciousness**, if you feel it's necessary.
11. You can add **Supporting Documents** for further evidence.
12. Finally, enter your **Submitter** name and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transaction Details**: Enter a brief description of the transaction, including any relevant dates.
* **Suspect Info**: Enter details about the individual or entity suspected of committing the transaction.
* **Transaction Amount**: Enter the amount of the transaction.
* **Suspect Location**: Enter the location where the transaction occurred.
* **Suspect Phone**: Enter the suspect's phone number.
* **Suspect Email**: Enter the suspect's email address.
* **Suspect Address**: Enter the suspect's address if available.
* **Suspiciousness Level**: Select the level of suspiciousness from **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**.
* **Transaction Date**: Choose the date when the transaction occurred.
* **Report By**: Enter your name and any relevant details.
* **Reason For Suspiciousness**: Provide a reason for the suspiciousness.
* **Supporting Documents**: Add any supporting documents for evidence.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you're providing accurate and relevant information.
* Do not submit duplicate reports for the same transaction.
* Attach supporting documents when available.
