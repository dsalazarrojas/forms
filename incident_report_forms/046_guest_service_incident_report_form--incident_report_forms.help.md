# Guest Service Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Service Incident Report Form is used to document incidents that occurred during a guest's stay at the hotel, ensuring that all incidents are investigated and documented for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date when the incident occurred in the `Incident Date` field.
2. Select one or more categories that apply to the incident in the `Incident Category` field.
3. Choose the location where the incident occurred in the `Incident Location` field.
4. Briefly describe the incident in the `Incident Description` field.
5. Select the actions taken to resolve the incident in the `Action` field.
6. Enter the guest's name and email address in the `Guest Name` and `Guest Email` fields, respectively.
7. Enter the employee's name and department in the `Employee Name` and `Employee Department` fields, respectively.
8. Enter the employee's ID in the `Employee ID` field.
9. Schedule a follow-up date for the incident in the `Follow-up Date` field.
10. Select the status of the follow-up in the `Follow-up Status` field.
11. Confirm whether the guest and employee were notified in the `Guest Confirmation` and `Employee Confirmation` fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date**: The date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Category**: Select one or more categories that apply to the incident, such as Hotel, Food and Beverage, Housekeeping, or Room Service.
* **Incident Location**: Choose the location where the incident occurred, such as Front Desk, Pool Area, Restaurant, or Room.
* **Incident Description**: Briefly describe the incident.
* **Action**: Select the actions taken to resolve the incident, such as Follow-up, Review, or Resolution.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the guest's name.
* **Guest Email**: Enter the guest's email address.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name.
* **Employee Department**: Select the employee's department, such as Front Desk, Food and Beverage, Housekeeping, or Engineering.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID.
* **Follow-up Date**: Schedule a follow-up date for the incident.
* **Follow-up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up, such as Done or In progress.
* **Guest Confirmation**: Confirm whether the guest was notified of the incident.
* **Employee Confirmation**: Confirm whether the employee was notified of the incident.
* **Employee Confirmation**: Confirm whether the employee was notified of the incident.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Only complete one form per incident.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact hotel management for assistance.
