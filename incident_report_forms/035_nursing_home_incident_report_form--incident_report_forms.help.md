# Nursing Home Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to document and report incidents that occur within the nursing home setting. It provides a structured format for collecting details about the incident, including the resident involved, the incident's type, date, and time, as well as the outcome and staff members involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Resident Name** field with the name of the resident who was involved in the incident.
2. Select the **Incident Type** from the available options to indicate the nature of the incident.
3. Enter the **Incident Date** and **Incident Time** in the provided fields to detail when the incident occurred.
4. Describe the location where the incident took place in the **Incident Location** field.
5. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the **Incident Narrative** field.
6. List the names of staff members involved in the incident in the **Staff Involved** field.
7. If applicable, select the **Outcome** of the incident from the available options.
8. Fill in the **Reported By** fields with the person's name, title, department, extension, and contact information.
9. Select the **Reviewed By** fields with the person's name, title, department, extension, and contact information.
10. Select the **Review Status** and **Status** fields as applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Name**: The name of the resident involved in the incident.
* **Incident Type**: The nature of the incident, such as fall, medication error, or medical emergency.
* **Incident Date**: The date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: The time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: The location where the incident took place.
* **Resident Condition**: The condition of the resident at the time of the incident, such as alert, restless, or confused.
* **Incident Narrative**: A detailed description of what happened.
* **Staff Involved**: List the names of staff members involved in the incident.
* **Outcome**: The outcome of the incident, such as resolved, pending, or referred to a higher authority.
* **Reported By**: The person who reported the incident, including their name, title, department, extension, and contact information.
* **Reviewed By**: The person or team who reviewed the incident report, including their name, title, department, extension, and contact information.
* **Review Status**: The current status of the incident review process.
* **Status**: The current status of the incident, such as open, closed, or pending.
* **Reported By (Title)**: The title of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Department**: The department of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Extension**: The extension or office of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Phone**: The phone number of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Email**: The email address of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reviewed By (Title)**: The title of the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewed By Department**: The department of the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewed By Extension**: The extension or office of the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewed By Phone**: The phone number of the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewed By Email**: The email address of the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Review Date**: The date the incident was reviewed.
* **Review Status**: The status of the review process.
* **Status**: The status of the incident.

Please ensure all fields are filled out thoroughly and accurately to aid in proper incident reporting and follow-up actions.
