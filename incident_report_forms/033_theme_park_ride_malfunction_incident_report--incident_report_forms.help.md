# theme_park_ride_malfunction_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report malfunctions that occur during theme park ride operations. It helps theme park staff and management gather information about the incident, including its date, time, status, and cause, to take prompt actions and prevent future malfunctions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Ride Name** of the malfunctioning ride.
2. Enter the **Incident Date** and **Time of Incident** when the malfunction occurred.
3. Choose the **Ride Status** after the malfunction, from the options **Ride Open**, **Closed**, or **Under repair**.
4. Provide a detailed description of the **Incident Description**, including the circumstances leading up to the incident.
5. Enter any **Follow-up Details** regarding the actions taken or planned to be taken after the incident.
6. Provide the **Incident Cause**, if known.
7. Describe any **Follow-up Actions** planned or taken to address the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ride Name** (`ride_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the ride that malfunctioned.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the malfunction occurred.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, text, optional): Enter the time when the malfunction occurred.
* **Ride Status** (`ride_status`, select_one, optional): Choose from **Ride Open**, **Closed**, or **Under repair** to indicate the status after the malfunction.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the incident, including the circumstances leading up to the malfunction.
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up details regarding actions taken or planned to be taken after the incident.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, optional): Enter the cause of the malfunction, if known.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, text, optional): Describe any follow-up actions planned or taken to address the incident.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the incident to help the theme park management team take prompt and effective actions.
- If unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or indicate "Unknown" in the field.
- This form is designed to be used for reporting malfunctions only, not for other incidents. If you need to report a different type of incident, please use the appropriate form.
