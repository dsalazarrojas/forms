# Fire Drill Coordination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request coordination for a fire drill, which is necessary for safety reasons such as a required bi-yearly drill for compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Request Form, including the fire location, type, and other relevant details.
2. Select the date and start time of the fire drill.
3. Provide your contact information, including first and last name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (id: 1, <text>, required: false): This field is a general description of the request.
* **Fire Location** (id: 2, <select_one>, required: false): Select the location where the fire drill will take place. Options are Central Office, Main Building, and Training Room.
* **Fire Type** (id: 3, <select_multiple>, required: false): Select one or multiple types of fires that will be simulated during the drill. Options are Fire Alarm, Emergency Response, and Evacuation.
* **Fire Date** (id: 4, <date>, required: false): Enter the date of the fire drill.
* **Fire Time** (id: 5, <time>, required: false): Enter the start time of the fire drill.
* **Fire Duration** (id: 6, <number>, required: false): Enter the duration of the fire drill in minutes.
* **Fire Start Time** (id: 7, <time>, required: false): Enter the start time of the fire drill.
* **Contact First Name** (id: 8, <text>, required: false): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (id: 9, <text>, required: false): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (id: 10, <email>, required: false): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 11, <text>, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Notes** (id: 12, <note>, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the fire drill.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your request is properly processed.
* Use valid options for select fields to ensure that your request is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your local safety officer.
