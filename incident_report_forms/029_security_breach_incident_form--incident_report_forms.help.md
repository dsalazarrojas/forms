# security_breach_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **security_breach_incident_form** is a form used to collect and document information about security incidents within an organization. It helps to ensure that all relevant details are captured and stored in a centralized location, aiding in the investigation and resolution of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident that occurred, such as "Incident", "Security Incident", or "Policy Violation".
2. Enter the **Date Occurred**: Provide the date the incident occurred.
3. Enter the **Time Occurred**: Provide the time the incident occurred.
4. Enter the **Location**: Provide the location where the incident occurred.
5. Enter a detailed **Description**: Describe what happened, including any relevant details.
6. Select the **Severity**: Choose the level of severity of the incident, such as "Low", "Medium", or "High".
7. Choose the **Status**: Select the current status of the incident, such as "Open" or "Closed".
8. Enter the **Reporter**: Identify the person or team that reported the incident.
