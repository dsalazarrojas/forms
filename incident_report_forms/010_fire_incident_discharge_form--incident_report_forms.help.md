### This help guide will cover the purpose of the Fire Incident Discharge Form, how to complete it, and an explanation of each field.

### Fire Incident Discharge Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Fire Incident Discharge Form is a document used to gather and record information about incidents that have occurred, especially those related to fires. It is designed to ensure that all relevant details are captured, investigated, and reviewed for proper incident closure and resolution.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct incident date and time from the calendar input fields.
2.  Provide a brief description of the incident in the Incident Description field.
3.  Specify the cause of the incident in the Incident Cause field.
4.  Enter the investigation notes in the Incident Investigation Notes field.
5.  Identify the responsible person for the incident in the Incident Responsible Person field.
6.  Select the incident type (Fire or Other) and priority (High, Medium, Low) from the respective dropdown menus.
7.  Choose the incident status (Not Started, In Progress, Closed, Suspended) and severity (Critical, Minor) from the respective dropdown menus.
8.  Indicate the incident disposition (Accepted, Declined, In Progress) and review status (Pending, Approved, Rejected) from the respective dropdown menus.
9.  Enter any relevant review notes in the Incident Review Notes field.
10.  Specify the reviewer's name and the date of the review in the Reviewer and Reviewed On fields.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the incident.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location of the incident.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Provide a brief description of the incident.
*   **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`): Specify the cause of the incident.
*   **Incident Investigation Notes** (`incident_investigation_notes`): Enter any notes related to the investigation.
*   **Incident Responsible Person** (`incident_responsible_person`): Identify the person responsible for the incident.
*   **Incident Responsible Person Title** (`incident_responsible_person_title`): Enter the title of the person responsible for the incident.
*   **Incident Responsible Person Department** (`incident_responsible_person_department`): Enter the department of the person responsible for the incident.
*   **Incident Responsible Person Contact Info** (`incident_responsible_person_contact_info`): Enter the contact information of the person responsible for the incident.
*   **Incident Investigation Status** (`incident_investigation_status`): Choose the status of the investigation (Not Started, In Progress, Closed, Suspended).
*   **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select the type of incident (Fire or Other).
*   **Incident Priority** (`incident_priority`): Choose the priority of the incident (High, Medium, Low).
*   **Incident Severity** (`incident_severity`): Choose the severity of the incident (Critical, Minor).
*   **Incident Disposition** (`incident_disposition`): Choose the disposition of the incident (Accepted, Declined, In Progress).
*   **Incident Disposition Notes** (`incident_disposition_notes`): Enter any notes related to the incident disposition.
*   **Incident Review Status** (`incident_review_status`): Choose the review status of the incident (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
*   **Incident Review Notes** (`incident_review_notes`): Enter any review notes.
*   **Incident Reviewer** (`incident_reviewer`): Identify the reviewer.
*   **Incident Reviewed By** (`incident_reviewed_by`): Enter the person's name who reviewed the incident.
*   **Incident Reviewed On** (`incident_reviewed_on`): Enter the date of the review.
*   **Incident Dispositioned By** (`incident_dispositioned_by`): Identify the person who dispositioned the incident.
*   **Incident Dispositioned On** (`incident_dispositioned_on`): Enter the date of the incident disposition.
*   **Incident Investigation Closed By** (`incident_investigation_closed_by`): Identify the person who closed the investigation.
*   **Incident Investigation Closed On** (`incident_investigation_closed_on`): Enter the date of the investigation closure.

### Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide or consult with the relevant authorities.
