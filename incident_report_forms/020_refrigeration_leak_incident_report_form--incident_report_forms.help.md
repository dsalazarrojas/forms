# Refrigeration Leak Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help incident reporters gather and record information about refrigeration leaks in a clear and consistent manner. It ensures that all necessary details are captured to facilitate thorough investigation and corrective action.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Select the correct date** of the leak from the date field.
2.  **Select the correct time** of the leak from the time field.
3.  **Enter the location** where the leak occurred.
4.  **Describe the incident** in as much detail as possible using the Leak Description field.
5.  **Identify the category** of the leak from the drop-down menu.
6.  **Choose the leak priority** (High, Medium, or Low) from the drop-down menu.
7.  **Select the status** of the leak from the drop-down menu (Reported, Confirmed, Under investigation, Resolved).
8.  **Enter the name** of the person completing this report.
9.  **Enter the Job title or position** of the person completing this report.
10.  **Enter the contact information** for the person completing this report.
11.  **Enter the department or team** involved in the report.
12.  **Describe the cause** of the leak.
13.  **Describe the consequences** of the leak.
14.  **Enter actions taken** to correct the leak.
15.  **Enter actions taken** to prevent future leaks.
16.  **Enter any other actions** taken.
17.  **Enter the name** of the person reviewing this report.
18.  **Enter the Job title or position** of the reviewer.
19.  **Enter the department or team** of the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leak Date**: Select the date of the leak.
*   **Leak Time**: Select the time of the leak.
*   **Leak Location**: Enter the location where the leak occurred.
*   **Leak Description**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
*   **Leak Category**: Choose the category of the leak from the drop-down menu.
*   **Leak Priority**: Choose the priority of the leak from the drop-down menu (High, Medium, or Low).
*   **Leak Status**: Select the status of the leak from the drop-down menu (Reported, Confirmed, Under investigation, Resolved).
*   **Incident Reporter**: Enter the name of the person completing this report.
*   **Incident Reporter Title**: Enter the Job title or position of the person completing this report.
*   **Incident Reporter Contact**: Enter the contact information for the person completing this report.
*   **Incident Reporter Department**: Enter the department or team involved in the report.
*   **Leak Cause**: Describe the cause of the leak.
*   **Leak Consequences**: Describe the impact of the leak.
*   **Leak Corrective Actions**: Enter actions taken to correct the leak.
*   **Leak Preventative Actions**: Enter actions taken to prevent future leaks.
*   **Other Actions**: Enter any other corrective or preventative actions taken.
*   **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person reviewing this report.
*   **Reviewer Title**: Enter the Job title or position of the reviewer.
*   **Reviewer Department**: Enter the department or team of the reviewer.
