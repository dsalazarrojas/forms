# subway_safety_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to report incidents that occur in subway environments for review and follow-up by authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have not already reported this incident.
2. Fill out the "Incident Details" page.
3. Select a valid "Date" and enter the incident's details.
4. Enter the "Location" where the incident occurred.
5. Choose the type of incident that occurred from the "Incident Type" options.
6. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Description" field.
7. Select the current status of the incident from the "Status" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details (incident_details)**: This is the main section for providing a brief summary of the incident.
* **Date (incident_date)**: Enter the date of the incident, using the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Location (location)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the subway line and station, if applicable.
* **Incident Type (incident_type)**: Choose the type of incident from the options provided. This will help authorities quickly categorize and review the incident.
* **Description (incident_description)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Status (incident_status)**: Select the current status of the incident, whether it is "Active" or "Inactive".

## Tips
* Double-check the information you provide to ensure it is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the authorities for assistance.
* You can save your progress on the form and return to it later, but please ensure that you submit the completed form once you have finished filling it out.
