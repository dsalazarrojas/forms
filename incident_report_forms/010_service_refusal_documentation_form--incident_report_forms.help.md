# Service Refusal Documentation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document instances where a service was refused by a customer or a service provider. It helps in tracking and managing service refusals, providing valuable information for service resolution and customer relationship management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the basic information in the first section (e.g., **Service Refusal Form**, **Customer Refusal Reason**, **Confirmation Status**, **Staff Confirmation**, etc.).
2. Fill out the details about the service refusal, including **Service Refusal Reasons**, **Service Refusal Resolution**, **Service Refusal Status**, **Incident ID**, **Incident Type**, **Incident Category**, **Incident Subcategory**, **Service Refusal Details**, etc.
3. Specify the assigned team member, tool, and location (if applicable).
4. Set the date and assigned time for the service refusal (if applicable).
5. Enter any additional comments (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Refusal Form**: This is the header section of the form where the basic information is collected. It should not be filled out as part of the form, it's just for reference.
* **Customer Refusal Reason** (`customer_refusal_reason`, text): This field is used to record the reason provided by the customer for refusing the service. Please provide a clear and concise description.
* **Confirmation Status** (`confirmation_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the service refusal, whether it's confirmed, not confirmed, or unknown.
* **Staff Confirmation** (`staff_confirmation`, text): This field is used to record any confirmation from staff regarding the service refusal. If confirmed, please provide a brief description.
* **Staff Confirmation Details** (`staff_confirmation_details`, text): This field is optional and can be used to provide additional details from the staff confirming the service refusal.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date of the service refusal. This is a required field.
* **Time** (`time`, time): This field is optional and should be filled out if the refusal was at a specific time.
* **Location** (`location`, text): This field is used to record the location where the service refusal occurred.
* **Service Refusal Reasons** (`service_refusal_reasons`, text): Provide a clear and detailed description of the reasons for the service refusal.
* **Service Refusal Resolution** (`service_refusal_resolution`, select_one): Choose the resolution status of the service refusal, whether it's refused, accepted, or partially refused.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information about the service refusal.
* **Assigned Team Member** (`assigned_team_member`, select_one): Select whether a team member was assigned to resolve the service refusal.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Select whether a tool was assigned to resolve the service refusal.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date): Enter the date when the team member was assigned to resolve the service refusal. This field is required.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, time): Enter the time when the team member was assigned to resolve the service refusal. This field is required.
* **Assigned Location** (`assigned_location`, text): Enter the location where the team member was assigned to resolve the service refusal. This field is required.
* **Service Refusal Details** (`service_refusal_details`, text): Provide a detailed description of the service refusal.
* **Incident ID** (`incident_id`, text): Enter the ID of the incident related to the service refusal.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one): Choose the type of incident related to the service refusal, whether it's yes or no.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, select_one): Choose the category of incident related to the service refusal, whether it's yes or no.
* **Incident Subcategory** (`incident_subcategory`, select_one): Choose the subcategory of incident related to the service refusal, whether it's yes or no.
* **Service Refusal Status** (`service_refusal_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the service refusal, whether it's active or inactive.
* **Staff Confirmer** (`staff_confirmer`, select_one): Select whether staff confirmed the service refusal, whether it's yes or no.
* **Staff Confirmer Details** (`staff_confirmer_details`, text): This field is optional and can be used to provide additional details from the staff confirming the service refusal.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the review for the service refusal, whether it's active or inactive.

Note: Please fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
