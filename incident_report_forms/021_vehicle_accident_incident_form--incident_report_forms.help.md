# Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vehicle accident incident. It helps us to gather accurate and complete details about the incident, including the location, time, date, vehicle involved, and any injuries or damage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
2. Use the correct format for the date and time fields (MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM respectively).
3. If a field is not applicable or not known, please leave it blank or use "Unknown" or "Not Applicable" as needed.
4. If there are multiple drivers or witnesses, please separate their names with commas in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the driver involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter the color of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Body Type** (`vehicle_body_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of vehicle body involved in the incident.
* **Accident Cause** (`accident_cause`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the cause of the incident.
* **Witness Names** (`witness_names`, text, optional): Enter the names of any witnesses, separating them with commas.
* **Vehicle Damage** (`vehicle_damage`, text, optional): Describe any damage sustained by the vehicle involved in the incident.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact your supervisor or the incident management team.
* If you have trouble filling out the form, try using a print screen or copy and paste the information from another source to ensure accuracy.
* Remember to save your form submission for your records, in case of future reference or investigation.
