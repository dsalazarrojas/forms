# free_police_incident_report_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or witnesses who have witnessed or reported an incident to the police department. The goal is to provide as much detail as possible to aid the police in investigating and responding to the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the incident date and time from the calendars or enter the date and time manually.
2.  Enter the location of the incident.
3.  Provide a brief description of the incident in the `incident_details` field.
4.  Enter a detailed description of the incident in the `incident_description` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **incident_date** (Date): This field is used to record the date of the incident. Select the date from the calendar or enter it manually.
*   **incident_time** (Time): This field is used to record the time of the incident. Select the time from the calendar or enter it manually.
*   **incident_location** (Text): This field is where you enter the location of the incident.
*   **incident_details** (Text): This field is where you enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details.
*   **incident_description** (Text): This field is where you enter a detailed description of the incident, including as much information as possible.

## Tips
*   Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to aid the police department in their investigation.
*   If you are unsure about any information, it is best to err on the side of caution and provide as much detail as you can.
*   Do not leave any fields blank if possible, as this can hinder the police department's ability to fully investigate the incident.
