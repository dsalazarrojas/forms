# Accident CCTV Footage Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Accident CCTV Footage Request Form is designed to facilitate the process of requesting CCTV footage from our system. This form collects essential information to ensure that the footage is securely and efficiently provided to the requesting party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name, title, and organization as the requesting party.
2.  Provide a valid contact email and phone number for follow-up communications.
3.  Enter the authorization document number (e.g., case number, warrant number, or other official document number).
4.  Specify the date, time, location, and duration of the accident.
5.  Select the purpose of requesting the footage.
6.  Choose the type of legal authorization supporting this request.
7.  Specify how you plan to use the requested footage.
8.  Choose how long you want the footage to be retained after viewing.
9.  Identify who will have access to view the footage.
10.  Confirm that you acknowledge the privacy requirements for handling this footage.
11.  Provide any special handling instructions.
12.  Add any additional comments or information about the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name (1)** (`requestor_name`, `text`, required): This is your name as the requesting party.
* **Requestor Title (2)** (`requestor_title`, `text`, required): This is your job title or role as the requesting party.
* **Requestor Organization (3)** (`requestor_organization`, `text`, required): This is the organization or agency making the request.
* **Requestor Contact Email (4)** (`requestor_contact_email`, `email`, required): This is your email address for follow-up communications.
* **Requestor Contact Phone (5)** (`requestor_contact_phone`, `text`, required): This is your phone number for follow-up communications.
* **Authorization Document Number (6)** (`authorization_document_number`, `text`, required): This is the case number, warrant number, or other official document number authorizing this request.
* **Accident Date (7)** (`accident_date`, `date`, required): This is the date when the accident occurred.
* **Accident Time (8)** (`accident_time`, `time`, required): This is the approximate time when the accident occurred.
* **Accident Location (9)** (`accident_location`, `text`, required): This is the specific location where the accident occurred.
* **Requested Footage Date (10)** (`requested_footage_date`, `date`, required): This is the date for which footage is being requested.
* **Requested Footage Time Range (11)** (`requested_footage_time_range`, `text`, required): This is the time range for which footage is being requested.
* **Camera Locations Requested (12)** (`camera_locations_requested`, `text`, required): This is the specific camera locations or areas to be reviewed.
* **Purpose of Request (13)** (`purpose_of_request`, `select_one`, required): This is the purpose of requesting this footage.
* **Legal Authorization Type (14)** (`legal_authorization_type`, `select_one`, required): This is the type of legal authorization supporting this request.
* **Footage Usage Purpose (15)** (`footage_usage_purpose`, `select_multiple`, required): This is how you plan to use the requested footage.
* **Footage Retention Period (16)** (`footage_retention_period`, `select_one`, required): This is how long you want the footage to be retained after viewing.
* **Viewing Party Details (17)** (`viewing_party_details`, `text`, required): This is who will have access to view the footage.
* **Privacy Compliance Acknowledgment (18)** (`privacy_compliance_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): This is your acknowledgment of the privacy requirements for handling this footage.
* **Special Handling Instructions (19)** (`special_handling_instructions`, `text`, optional): This is any special instruction for handling the footage.
* **Additional Comments (20)** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): This is any other information about the request.

Please note that this guide is a general help guide and might not cover every possible scenario or field. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask.
