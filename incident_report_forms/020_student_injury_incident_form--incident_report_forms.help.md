# Student Injury Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student Injury Incident Form" is used to record and report incidents involving students, with the goal of gathering essential information to address the incident promptly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the location where the incident occurred.
2. Indicate if the incident was of a specific type (e.g., injury, accident, assault).
3. Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
5. Enter any injury details, if applicable.
6. Enter the student's name.
7. Enter the student's grade (if available).
8. Enter the date the incident was reported.
9. Select the status of the incident.
10. Enter the name of the individual who reported the incident.
11. Enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
12. Enter the student's phone number (if available).
13. Enter the student's email (if available).
14. Enter the name of the teacher or staff member who reported the incident.
15. Enter the name of any witnesses, if applicable.
16. Enter the email of any witnesses, if applicable.
17. Enter the phone number of any witnesses, if applicable.
18. Enter the title of any witnesses, if applicable.
19. Enter the department of any witnesses, if applicable.
20. Enter the role of any witnesses, if applicable.
20. Enter the location of any witnesses, if applicable.
21. Enter the student's ID number (required for tracking and identification).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **incident_location (Text):** Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **incident_type (Select Multiple):** Check one or more boxes to indicate the type of incident, such as injury, accident, or assault.
* **incident_date (Date):** Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **incident_time (Time):** Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **description_of_incident (Text):** Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **injury_details (Text):** Describe any injuries sustained in the incident.
* **student_name (Text):** Enter the name of the student involved.
* **student_grade (Number):** Enter the student's grade level or year.
* **incident_date_reported (Date):** Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **incident_status (Select One):** Select the status of the incident, either Active or Inactive.
* **incident_reported_by (Text):** Enter the name of the individual who reported the incident.
* **parent_name (Text):** Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **student_phone (Text):** Enter the student's phone number (if available).
* **student_email (Email):** Enter the student's email address (if available).
* **teacher_name (Text):** Enter the name of the teacher or staff member who reported the incident.
* **witness_name (Text):** Enter the name of any witnesses, if applicable.
* **witness_email (Email):** Enter the email of any witnesses, if applicable.
* **witness_phone (Text):** Enter the phone number of any witnesses, if applicable.
* **witness_title (Text):** Enter the title of any witnesses, if applicable.
* **witness_department (Text):** Enter the department of any witnesses, if applicable.
* **witness_role (Text):** Enter the role of any witnesses, if applicable.
* **witness_location (Text):** Enter the location of any witnesses, if applicable.
* **student_id (Number):** Enter the student's ID number (required for tracking and identification).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or check "N/A" if prompted.
* If the student's grade is not applicable, check "N/A" for the grade field.
* Double-check the accuracy of all information entered before submitting the form.
