# Volunteer Fire Department Run Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Fire Department Run Log is a form used to document and report on incidents responded by the department. It captures key information about the incident, including date, time, location, and details about the response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the `Incident Date` field.
2. Record the time of the incident in the `Incident Time` field.
3. Select the type of call the department responded to (e.g., "Yes" or "No") in the `Call Type` field.
4. Enter a brief description of the incident in the `Incident Description` field.
5. Record whether units arrived to respond to the incident in the `Units Arrived` field.
6. Select whether units were responding to the incident (e.g., "Yes" or "No") in the `Units Responding` field.
7. Select whether units returned after responding to the incident (e.g., "Yes" or "No") in the `Units Returned` field.
8. Record any injuries sustained in the `Injuries` field.
9. Record the type of injuries sustained in the `Injury Type` field.
10. Enter a description of the injuries sustained in the `Injuries Description` field.
11. Record whether there was property damage in the `Property Damage` field.
12. Enter a description of the property damage in the `Property Damage Description` field.
13. Record whether a vehicle was involved in the incident (e.g., "Yes" or "No") in the `Vehicle Involved` field.
14. Enter the type of vehicle involved in the incident in the `Vehicle Type` field.
15. Record the vehicle license number in the `License` field.
16. Record whether the vehicle driver sustained injuries (e.g., "Yes" or "No") in the `Driver Injuries` field.
17. Record whether passengers in the vehicle sustained injuries (e.g., "Yes" or "No") in the `Passengers Injuries` field.
18. Record the number of passengers in the vehicle in the `Passengers` field.
19. Record whether the incident is active or inactive (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive") in the `Incident Status` field.
20. Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident in the `Notes` field.
21. Attach any relevant photos of the incident in the `Photos` field.
22. Enter the name of the person who submitted the report in the `Submitted By` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Fire Department**: Enter the name of the volunteer fire department responding to the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Call Type**: Select whether the department responded to a yes or no call.
* **Location**: Enter a description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Enter a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Response**: Enter a brief description of the response to the incident.
* **Units Arrived**: Record whether units arrived to respond to the incident.
* **Units Responding**: Select whether units were responding to the incident.
* **Units Returned**: Select whether units returned after responding to the incident.
* **Injuries**: Record the number of injuries sustained during the incident.
* **Injury Type**: Enter a description of the type of injuries sustained.
* **Injuries Description**: Enter a description of the injuries sustained.
* **Property Damage**: Record whether there was property damage.
* **Property Damage Description**: Enter a description of the property damage.
* **Vehicle Involved**: Record whether a vehicle was involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Type**: Enter a description of the type of vehicle involved.
* **License**: Enter the license number of the vehicle involved.
* **Driver Injuries**: Record whether the vehicle driver sustained injuries.
* **Passengers Injuries**: Record whether passengers in the vehicle sustained injuries.
* **Passengers**: Enter the number of passengers in the vehicle.
* **Incident Status**: Record whether the incident is active or inactive.
* **Photos**: Attach relevant photos of the incident.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the report.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully read through the form before filling it out to ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
* Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, it's best to ask for clarification before proceeding.
* Remember to attach any relevant photos or documentation to the form as needed.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you've captured all necessary information.
