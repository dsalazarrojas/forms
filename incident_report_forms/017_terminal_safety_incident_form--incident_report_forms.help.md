# Terminal Safety Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Terminal Safety Incident Form is used to report incidents that occur at a terminal. It is essential to fill out this form promptly and accurately to aid in the reporting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Report Incident" page, where you can describe the incident in a few words.
2. On the "Incident Description" page, provide a detailed description of what happened, including any circumstances surrounding the incident.
3. On the "Incident Date" page, select the date the incident occurred.
4. On the "Incident Time" page, select the time the incident occurred.
5. On the "Terminal ID" page, enter the terminal's ID number.
6. Finally, on the "Incident Details" page, provide any additional information about the incident, such as the location, people involved, or any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Incident** (Text, Optional): Describe the incident briefly.
* **Incident Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any circumstances surrounding the incident.
* **Incident Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Time, Optional): Select the time the incident occurred.
* **Terminal ID** (Number, Optional): Enter the terminal's ID number.
* **Incident Details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about the incident, such as location, people involved, or other relevant details.
