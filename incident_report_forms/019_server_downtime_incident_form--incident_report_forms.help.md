# Server Downtime Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Server Downtime Incident Form is used to collect and document server downtime incidents. This form helps teams quickly identify and resolve issues, minimizing the impact on users and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Description" field.
4. Select the category and subcategory of the incident in the "Category" field.
5. Specify the affected server in the "Affected Server" field.
6. Choose the status of the incident from the "Status" field.
7. If the incident has been resolved, enter the resolution date in the "Resolved Date" field.
8. Enter any additional notes about the incident in the "Notes" field.
9. Select the affected team in the "Affected Team" field.
10. Enter the person who resolved the incident in the "Resolved By" field.
11. Enter the escalation level and time in the "Escalation Level" and "Escalation Time" fields.
12. Enter any escalation notes in the "Escalation Notes" field.
13. Enter the person who reported the incident in the "Reported By" field.
14. Enter the last updated time and date in the "Last Updated" and "Last Updated By" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Server Downtime Incident Form** (`name: server_downtime_incident_form`): This is the main field where you can enter a brief title for the incident report.
- **Incident Date** (`name: incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
- **Description** (`name: incident_description`): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.
- **Start Time** (`name: incident_start_time`): Enter the start time of the incident.
- **End Time** (`name: incident_end_time`): Enter the end time of the incident.
- **Category** (`name: incident_category`): Select the main category and subcategory of the incident.
- **Affected Server** (`name: incident_affected_server`): Specify the affected server.
- **Status** (`name: incident_status`): Choose the status of the incident from "Pending", "In Progress", or "Resolved".
- **Resoved Date** (`name: incident_resolved_date`): If the incident has been resolved, enter the date it was resolved.
- **Notes** (`name: incident_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the incident.
- **Affected Team** (`name: incident_affected_team`): Select the team affected by the incident.
- **Resolved By** (`name: incident_resolved_by`): Enter the person who resolved the incident.
- **Escalation Level** (`name: incident_escalation_level`): Enter the level of escalation (e.g., minor, major).
- **Escalation Time** (`name: incident_escalation_time`): Enter the time it took for the incident to escalate.
- **Escalation Notes** (`name: incident_escalation_notes`): Enter any notes about the escalation process.
- **Reported By** (`name: incident_reported_by`): Enter the person who reported the incident.
- **Last Updated** (`name: incident_last_updated`): Enter the last updated time of the incident.
- **Last Updated By** (`name: incident_last_updated_by`): Enter the person who last updated the incident.
