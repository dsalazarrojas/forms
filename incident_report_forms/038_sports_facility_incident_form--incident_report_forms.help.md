# Sports Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Facility Incident Form is used to report incidents that occur within sports facilities, such as accidents, injuries, or other significant events that require attention from facility management or maintenance personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the fields that need to be completed.
2. Select the correct facility ownership status for the incident (yes or no).
3. Enter the date the incident occurred.
4. Enter the time the incident occurred.
5. Provide a detailed description of what happened.
6. Specify the tool or equipment used in the incident (if applicable).
7. Indicate the investigator assigned to the incident.
8. Identify the person reporting the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Ownership**: Select "Yes" if the facility is currently being used by or managed by the sports facility itself. Select "No" if the facility is privately owned or operated by a third party.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: Provide a detailed description of where the incident occurred within the sports facility.
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including any circumstances leading up to the incident.
* **Assigned Tool**: If a tool or equipment was involved in the incident, please specify what it was.
* **Incident Investigator**: Identify the person or team responsible for investigating the incident.
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter your name and contact information so that we can contact you for further information or clarification.
