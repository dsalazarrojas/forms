# Hotel Guest Security Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about security incidents that may have occurred at a hotel, allowing hotel management to investigate and take corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details.
2. Enter the name of the guest involved in the incident.
3. Select the date and time the incident occurred.
4. Choose the location where the incident took place.
5. Choose the type of incident that occurred (e.g. hotel room security breach, theft, assault).
6. Provide a summary of what happened during the incident.
7. If the guest responded to the incident, enter their response.
8. Select the outcome of the incident (e.g. confirmed, unconfirmed, under investigation).
9. Choose the follow-up actions that were taken (e.g. follow-up 1, follow-up 2, follow-up 3).
10. Enter any additional details about the follow-up actions taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident description**: A brief description of what happened during the incident. This can include details such as the location, time, and circumstances surrounding the incident.
* **Guest name**: The name of the guest involved in the incident. If the guest is not known, please enter "Unknown".
* **Incident date**: The date the incident occurred. Please enter in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident time**: The time the incident occurred. Please enter in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Incident location**: The location where the incident took place. This can be a room number, floor, or public area.
* **Incident type**: The type of incident that occurred (e.g. hotel room security breach, theft, assault). Select from the available options.
* **Incident summary**: A brief summary of what happened during the incident. Provide as much detail as possible.
* **Guest response**: If the guest responded to the incident, enter their response. If no response was given, please enter "No response".
* **Incident outcome**: The outcome of the incident (e.g. confirmed, unconfirmed, under investigation). Select from the available options.
* **Follow-up**: The follow-up actions taken in response to the incident. Select one or more of the available options.
* **Follow-up details**: Any additional details about the follow-up actions taken. Provide as much information as possible.
