# Slip And Fall Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about slips and falls that occur within the workplace. It helps to document the incident, gather relevant details, and facilitate a thorough investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before filling it out.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
3. Select the time of day for the incident from the "Incident Time" field.
4. Fill in the name of the employee involved in the incident in the "Employee Name" field.
5. Describe the location where the incident occurred in the "Location" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the "Description" field.
7. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., walking surface, stairs, or other).
8. Answer whether a slip or fall was involved in the incident.
9. Indicate whether the employee was aware that the location was closed.
10. If applicable, provide the name of any witness to the incident in the "Witness" field.
11. Enter the date the incident was reported in the "Report Date" field.
12. Enter the name of the person who reported the incident in the "Reported By" field.
13. Enter the incident ID, if assigned, in the "Incident ID" field.
14. If assigned, enter the name of the person assigned to investigate the incident in the "Assigned To" field.
15. Select the current status of the incident (open, closed, or in progress).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the day when the incident occurred.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., walking surface, stairs, or other).
* **Was a slip or fall involved?** (`slip_or_fall`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if a slip or fall was involved in the incident.
* **Was employee aware that the location was closed?** (`employee_awareness`, select_one, required: true): Check if the employee was aware that the location was closed.
* **Witness** (`witness`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of any witness to the incident.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident ID** (`incident_id`, text, required: false): Enter the incident ID, if assigned.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required: false): If assigned, enter the name of the person assigned to investigate the incident.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of the incident (open, closed, or in progress).

## Tips

* Fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form is a general guide, and you may need to add or modify fields based on your organization's specific policies and procedures.
