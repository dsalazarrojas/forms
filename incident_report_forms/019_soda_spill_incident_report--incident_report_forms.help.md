# Soda Spill Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a spill incident that occurred in a soda facility. The purpose of this form is to help identify the cause of the spill, assess the impact on personnel, environment, and equipment, and document steps taken to mitigate and prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the spill location where the incident occurred.
2. Provide a brief description of the spill.
3. Select the date and time of the spill.
4. Enter the volume of the spilled substance.
5. Specify the type of liquid that was spilled.
6. Check if the spill was cleaned up and if so, how it was done.
7. Identify the cause of the spill.
8. List any contaminants or hazards associated with the spill.
9. Report any rags used to clean up the spill.
10. List any personnel involved in the incident.
11. List any equipment involved in the incident.
12. Identify any hazards associated with the spill.
13. List the person who reported the incident.
14. List any vehicles involved in the incident.
15. List any witnesses to the incident.
16. Report any injuries sustained during the incident.
17. Report any vehicles damaged during the incident.
18. Describe the environmental impact of the spill.
19. List any other affected parties or assets.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Spill Location** (`spill_location`): Specify the exact location where the spill occurred, such as a specific room, area, or equipment zone.
* **Spill Description** (`spill_description`): Provide a brief explanation of what happened during the spill, including any symptoms or effects observed.
* **Spill Date** (`spill_date`): Enter the date the spill occurred using the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Spill Time** (`spill_time`): Enter the time the spill occurred using 24-hour format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Spill Volume** (`spill_volume`): Measure the volume of the spilled substance in units, e.g., liters or gallons.
* **Spill Liquid** (`spill_liquid`): Specify the type of liquid that was spilled, e.g., soda syrup, water, or oil.
* **Spill Cleaned Up** (`spill_cleaned`): Check if the spill was cleaned up and specify how it was done, e.g., with a mop, a spill kit, or by using equipment.
* **Spill Cause** (`spill_cause`): Identify the root cause of the spill, e.g., human error, equipment failure, or maintenance issue.
* **Spill Contaminants** (`spill_contaminants`): List any substances or materials that were contaminated during the spill.
* **Spill Rags** (`spill_rags`): Specify any rags or absorbent materials used to clean up the spill.
* **Spill Personnel** (`spill_personnel`): List any personnel involved in the incident, including their names and roles.
* **Spill Equipment** (`spill_equipment`): List any equipment involved in the incident, including their serial numbers or IDs.
* **Spill Hazards** (`spill_hazards`): Identify any hazards associated with the spill, e.g., slippery surfaces, electrical shock, or fire risk.
* **Spill Reporter** (`spill_reporter`): Identify the person who reported the incident.
* **Spill Vehicles Involved** (`spill_vehicles_involved`): List any vehicles involved in the incident, including their license plate numbers.
* **Spill Witnesses** (`spill_witnesses`): List any witnesses to the incident, including their names and contact information.
* **Spill Injuries** (`spill_injuries`): Report any injuries sustained during the incident, including their nature and severity.
* **Spill Vehicles Damaged** (`spill_vehicles_damaged`): List any vehicles damaged during the incident, including their descriptions and serial numbers.
* **Spill Environmental Impact** (`spill_environmental_impact`): Describe the environmental impact of the spill, e.g., soil contamination, water pollution, or air quality issues.
* **Spill Other Affected** (`spill_other_affected`): List any other affected parties or assets, e.g., neighboring facilities, customers, or employees.
