# Near Miss Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture information about near miss incidents that occurred within our organization. Near miss incidents are events that do not result in harm or injury but have the potential to do so. Reporting these incidents helps us identify areas for improvement and reduce the risk of future incidents.

Before starting to fill out this form, please check that you are not duplicating a report that has already been submitted. If you are unsure, please contact the relevant department or team to confirm.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the incident name in the text field provided.
2.  Select the date and time of the incident using the date and time pickers.
3.  Enter the location where the incident occurred.
4.  Describe the incident in the text field provided.
5.  Choose the nature of the incident from the list of options provided.
6.  Identify the type of near miss from the list of options provided.
7.  Indicate the level of the incident from the list of options provided.
8.  Describe the circumstances surrounding the near miss.
9.  Identify the root cause of the incident from the list of options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Name** (`incident_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the incident.
*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the incident using the date picker.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the incident using the time picker.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
*   **Incident Nature** (`incident_nature`, text, optional): Choose the nature of the incident from the list of options provided.
*   **Near Miss Type** (`near_miss_type`, text, optional): Identify the type of near miss from the list of options provided.
*   **Incident Level** (`incident_level`, text, optional): Indicate the level of the incident from the list of options provided.
*   **Near Miss Circumstances** (`near_miss_circumstances`, text, optional): Describe the circumstances surrounding the near miss.
*   **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, optional): Identify the root cause of the incident from the list of options provided.
