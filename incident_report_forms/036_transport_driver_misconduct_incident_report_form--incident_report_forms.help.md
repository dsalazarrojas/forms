# Transport Driver Misconduct Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document incidents involving misconduct by transport drivers. It provides a structured and detailed account of the incident, including general information, witness statements, and actions taken. This form is a critical component of our investigation process and helps ensure that incidents are thoroughly documented and analyzed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2.  Fill in the **General Information** section with any relevant details about the incident.
3.  Describe the **Incident Description** in the space provided.
4.  Identify any witnesses and list their names in the **Witness Names** field.
5.  Capture witness statements in the **Witness Statements** field, including any relevant details.
6.  Provide details about the **Vehicle Details** if applicable.
7.  Document the **Action Taken** to address the incident.
8.  If applicable, enter any **Driver Details** or **Rider Details**.
9.  Report the **Incident Outcome**.
10.  Add any **Additional Comments** if necessary.
11.  Select the **Investigation Status** to indicate the current status of the investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Required, General Information): Enter the date, time, and location of the incident, as well as any other relevant details.
* **Incident Description** (Required, Text): Describe the incident, including what happened and any relevant circumstances.
* **Witness Names** (Required, Select up to 5): List the names of witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness Statements** (Required, Text): Capture witness statements, including any relevant details they may have witnessed.
* **Vehicle Details** (Optional, Text): Enter any details about the vehicle involved in the incident, such as make, model, and license plate number.
* **Action Taken** (Required, Text): Document any action taken in response to the incident.
* **Driver Details** (Optional, Text): Enter any details about the driver involved in the incident, such as name, license number, and contact information.
* **Rider Details** (Optional, Text): Enter any details about any passengers or riders involved in the incident.
* **Incident Outcome** (Required, Text): Report the outcome of the incident, including any injuries, damages, or other consequences.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional, Text): Enter any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Investigation Status** (Required, Text): Select the current status of the investigation, such as "Active", "Closed", or "Pending".
