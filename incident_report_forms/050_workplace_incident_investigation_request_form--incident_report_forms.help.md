# workplace_incident_investigation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an investigation into a workplace incident. It gathers essential information to aid in the investigation process, including the type of incident, location, and witness details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Fire, Electrical, Chemical, Mechanical, or Other).
2. Choose the location where the incident took place (e.g., Floor 1, Floor 2, Floor 3, or Other - Specify).
3. Enter the date of the incident using the date picker.
4. Enter the time of the incident using the time picker.
5. List the names of any witnesses who may have observed or witnessed the incident.
6. Provide contact information for the witnesses.
7. Describe the incident in detail, including what happened and any relevant details.
8. Specify the cause of the injury, if applicable.
9. Describe the treatment provided, if any.
10. Add any additional investigation notes.
11. Enter the name of the investigator assigned to the case.
12. Choose the department of the investigator (e.g., HR, Management, Facilities, or Other - Specify).
13. Enter the title of the investigator (e.g., Investigator, Team Lead, or Manager).
14. Add any notes from the investigator.
15. Choose the current investigation status (e.g., Pending, Completed, or Not Required).
16. (Optional) Click the Submit button to save your progress and continue with the investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Fire, Electrical, Chemical, Mechanical, or Other).
* **Location**: Choose the location where the incident took place (e.g., Floor 1, Floor 2, Floor 3, or Other - Specify).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident using the date picker.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident using the time picker.
* **Witness Names**: List the names of any witnesses who may have observed or witnessed the incident.
* **Witness Contact Info**: Provide contact information for the witnesses.
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in detail, including what happened and any relevant details.
* **Cause Of Injury**: Specify the cause of the injury, if applicable.
* **Treatment**: Describe the treatment provided, if any.
* **Investigation Notes**: Add any additional investigation notes.
* **Investigator Name**: Enter the name of the investigator assigned to the case.
* **Investigator Department**: Choose the department of the investigator (e.g., HR, Management, Facilities, or Other - Specify).
* **Investigator Title**: Enter the title of the investigator (e.g., Investigator, Team Lead, or Manager).
* **Investigator Notes**: Add any notes from the investigator.
* **Investigation Status**: Choose the current investigation status (e.g., Pending, Completed, or Not Required).
* **Submit**: (Optional) Click the Submit button to save your progress and continue with the investigation.
* **Review and Approval**: Choose the level of review and approval required for the investigation (e.g., None, HR Review, Manager Review, or Completed).

Note: Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed before submitting the form.
