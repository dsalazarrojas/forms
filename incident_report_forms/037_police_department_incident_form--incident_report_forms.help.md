To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the correct officer department**: Choose the department that the officer who reported the incident belongs to, either Police, Sheriff's Office, or select 'Custom' if it's not listed.
2. **Enter the date and time**: Enter the date and time of the incident using the date field.
3. **Enter the location**: Provide a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
4. **Enter the officer's name and department**: Enter the name of the officer who reported the incident and their department.
5. **Enter the date and time issued**: Enter the date and time when the incident was issued or reported.
6. **Enter the status**: Select the status of the incident, either 'Active' or 'Closed'.
7. **Provide a description**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
8. **Enter any additional details**: Fill in any additional details that may be relevant to the incident.
