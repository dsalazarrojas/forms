# Vehicle Accident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Accident Report Form is designed to collect information about vehicle accidents for use by fleet management, insurance companies, and law enforcement agencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each field carefully and completely.
* Start with the vehicle information section, providing details about the vehicle involved in the accident.
* Move on to the accident details section, including the date and location of the accident.
* Continue to the witness information section, providing details about any witnesses to the accident.
* Be sure to include accurate and complete contact information for witnesses, including email address.
* Finally, add any additional photos or notes about the incident as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info_1`, `Vehicle Information 1`, required/optional): This section provides details about the vehicle involved in the accident. Please provide accurate information about the vehicle's make, model, and year.
* **Date of Accident** (`date_of_accident`, `Date of Accident`, required/optional): Enter the date on which the accident occurred.
* **Location of Accident** (`location_of_accident`, `Location of Accident`, required/optional): Provide the location where the accident occurred.
* **Vehicle Information 2** (`vehicle_info_2`, `Vehicle Information 2`, required/optional): This section provides further details about the vehicle involved in the accident. Please provide accurate information about the vehicle's make, model, and year.
* **Driver Information** (`driver_info`, `Driver Information`, required/optional): If available, provide information about the driver involved in the accident, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, `Vehicle License Plate`, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Year Made** (`vehicle_year_made`, `Vehicle Year Made`, required/optional): Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Year Model** (`vehicle_year_model`, `Vehicle Year Model`, required/optional): Enter the model year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, `Vehicle Color`, required/optional): Choose the color of the vehicle involved in the accident.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `Vehicle Type`, required/optional): Choose the type of vehicle involved in the accident (e.g. car, truck, motorcycle).
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, `Vehicle Status`, required/optional): Choose the status of the vehicle after the accident (e.g. damaged, undamaged).
* **Witness Information** (`witness_info`, `Witness Information`, required/optional): If witnesses were present, check the boxes to indicate if witnesses were present, and enter their information, including name, phone number, and email.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `Witness Name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the witness.
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, `Witness Phone`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the witness.
* **Witness Email** (`witness_email`, `Witness Email`, required/true): Enter the email address of the witness.
* **Incident Photo 1** (`incident_photo1`, `Incident Photo 1`, required/optional): Attach any additional photos or notes about the incident.
* **Incident Photo 2** (`incident_photo2`, `Incident Photo 2`, required/optional): Attach any additional photos or notes about the incident.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out each field accurately and completely to ensure the form is completed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or the form's administrator for guidance.
* The form is designed to be used for vehicle accidents only, so please do not use it for any other type of incident or report.
