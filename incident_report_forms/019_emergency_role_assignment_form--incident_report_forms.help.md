# emergency_role_assignment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "emergency_role_assignment_form" form is used to report an incident that requires emergency response and assign responsibility to the relevant personnel. This form should only be used for emergencies that require immediate attention from designated responders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are reporting an incident that actually requires emergency response.
2.  Enter your contact information (contact name, phone number, and email) to enable the emergency responders to contact you.
3.  Select the emergency contact person from the list of available options.
4.  Fill out the date and time of the incident.
5.  Provide the location where the incident occurred.
6.  Assign the responsibility for responding to the incident to the relevant personnel (A or B).
7.  (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes about the incident as the reporter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report An Incident**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to be contacted for further information.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select the emergency contact person (John Doe or Jane Doe) who will be responsible for responding to the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Assigned Responsibility**: Select one or both of the responders (A or B) who will be responsible for responding to the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident (optional). 

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accurate information is provided.
- Use the form for genuine emergency incidents only.
