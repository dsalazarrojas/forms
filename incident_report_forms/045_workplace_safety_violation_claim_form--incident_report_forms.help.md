# Workplace Safety Violation Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Violation Claim Form is designed to document and report workplace safety incidents, including hazards, injuries, and near misses that pose a risk to employees' health and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the "incident_date" field.
2. **Briefly describe the hazard or unsafe condition**: In the "workplace_safety_violation_claim_description" field, describe the hazard or unsafe condition that led to the incident in a few sentences.
3. **Witness statement (if applicable)**: If there were witnesses to the incident, provide a brief statement about what they saw or experienced in the "witness_statement" field.
4. **Select workplace safety violations**: Choose all that apply from the "workplace_safety_violations" list, which includes options like Near Miss, Hazard, Injury, and Near Miss with Injury.
5. **Supervisor and Employee Signatures**: Both the supervisor and employee must sign and date the form to confirm that it has been filled out accurately.
6. **Report to**: Select the person or department that the incident should be reported to from the "Report To" dropdown menu, which includes options like Manager, Supervisor, and HR.
7. **Additional Workplace Safety Violations**: If there were other safety incidents or hazards present, describe them in the "workplace_safety_violations_2", "workplace_safety_violations_3", and "workplace_safety_violations_4" fields.
8. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident in the "workplace_safety_violation_claim_form_notes" field.
9. **Additional information (if applicable)**: If applicable, enter the assigned tool or equipment used during the incident in the "assigned_tool" field.
10. **Submitter and Form Completed By**: Fill out the "workplace_safety_violation_claim_form_submitter" and "form_completed_by" fields with your name and signature.
11. **Date Completed**: Enter the date the form was completed in the "completed_on" field.
11. **Completed By**: Enter your name and signature in the "completed_by" field.
