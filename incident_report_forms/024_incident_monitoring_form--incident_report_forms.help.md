## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Incident ID (1)
Incident ID

* A unique identifier for the incident.

### Incident Type (2)
incident_type

* Select 'Yes' if the incident is reportable, 'No' otherwise.

### Incident Date (3)
incident_date

* Date of the incident. Enter in the format: MM/DD/YYYY.

### Incident Details (4)
incident_details

* A brief description of the incident.

### Incident Location (5)
incident_location

* Select 'Yes' if the incident occurred at the company's location, 'No' otherwise.

### Incident Status (6)
incident_status

* Select the current status of the incident: Open, Closed, On hold, or Waiting for response.

### Incident Notes (7)
incident_notes

* Any additional information about the incident.

### Incident Resolution (8)
incident_resolution

* Select the resolution status of the incident: No resolution, No resolution yet, or Resolved.

### Incident Reporter (9)
incident_reporter

* Select 'Yes' if the incident was reported by an employee, 'No' otherwise.

### Incident Report Date (10)
incident_report_date

* Select the date when the incident was reported.

### Incident Reporter Phone (11)
incident_reporter_phone

* The phone number of the person who reported the incident.

### Incident Reporter Email (12)
incident_reporter_email

* The email address of the person who reported the incident.

### Incident Reporter Notes (13)
incident_reporter_notes

* Any additional notes about the incident reporter.

### Resolved By (14)
incident_resolved_by

* Select 'Yes' if the incident was resolved by an employee, 'No' otherwise.

### Resolved Date (15)
incident_resolved_date

* Date when the incident was resolved.

### Resolved By Phone (16)
incident_resolved_by_phone

* The phone number of the person who resolved the incident.

### Resolved By Email (17)
incident_resolved_by_email

* The email address of the person who resolved the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your selections for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
