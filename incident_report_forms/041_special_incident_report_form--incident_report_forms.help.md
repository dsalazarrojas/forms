# Special Incidnet Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Incidnet Report Form is used to report incidents that are not part of the regular incident report process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the General Information page with your details and any relevant information about the incident.
* On the Incident Nature page, select one or more of the options for the type of incident that occurred.
* On the Incident Details page, provide any additional notes about the incident.
* On the Hazard Control page, select whether or not the hazard was controlled or not.
* On the Witness List page, provide any witnesses or individuals who may have information about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (General Information, Text, Required)**: Fill in your contact information and any relevant details about the incident.
* **Incident Nature (Incident Nature, Select Multiple, Required)**: Select one or more options for the type of incident that occurred. This helps us to better understand the incident and provide the appropriate support.
* **Incident Details (Incident Details, Note, Optional)**: Provide any additional notes or information about the incident. This can include descriptions, dates, and any other relevant details.
* **Hazard Control (Hazard Control, Select One, Required)**: Select whether or not the hazard was controlled or not. This helps us to determine the level of risk involved in the incident.
* **Witness List (Witness List, Text, Optional)**: List any witnesses or individuals who may have information about the incident. This can include names, positions, and contact information.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling in the General Information and Incident Details fields.
* Make sure to select all relevant options for Incident Nature and Hazard Control.
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form, please reach out to your supervisor or contact the incident reporting team.
