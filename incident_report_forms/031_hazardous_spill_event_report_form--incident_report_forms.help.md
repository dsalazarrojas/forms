# Hazardous Spill Event Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hazardous Spill Event Report Form is used to report incidents involving hazardous materials that have occurred on-site. This form is a critical tool for documenting and tracking incidents to ensure the safety and well-being of employees, visitors, and the environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Select the relevant information for each field.
3.  Enter your contact information (Submitter Info, Submitter Email, and Submitter Phone).
4.  Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter** (Text):
	+ Enter your name and title/position.
* **Incident Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Location** (Text):
	+ Enter a description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Report Type** (Select):
	+ Choose the type of incident that occurred: Spill, Other, or Report.
* **Hazardous Materials Involved** (Select Multiple):
	+ Check the types of hazardous materials involved (Fuel, Chemicals, Other, or Gas).
* **Description** (Text):
	+ Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Actions Taken** (Text):
	+ Describe the actions taken to contain or mitigate the spill.
* **Incident Status** (Select):
	+ Choose the current status of the incident: Active or Closed.
* **Submitter Info** (Text):
	+ Enter any additional information about the submittor (yourself).
* **Submitter Email** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Submitted By** (Text):
	+ If someone else submitted the form on your behalf, enter their name and title/position.

Note: The location field is not required, but it's recommended that you provide a description of the location if it was a spill event.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
* Review your submission before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or safety officer for guidance.
