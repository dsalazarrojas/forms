# UV Burn Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents related to UV exposure among employees, such as sunburns or excessive sun exposure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out each field carefully, using the following guidelines:**
	* If unsure, leave a field blank or ask your supervisor/HR representative for guidance.
	* If you have any issues or questions, refer to this guide or contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time on the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident took place.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the full name of the employee affected by the incident.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee affected by the incident.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the full name of the employee's supervisor.
* **Employee Department**: Enter the employee's department where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Sun Protection Used**: Describe any sun protection measures taken before or during the incident (e.g. sunscreen, hat, sunglasses).
* **Sun Protection Provided**: Describe any sun protection measures provided to the employee after the incident (e.g. first aid, medical attention).

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
