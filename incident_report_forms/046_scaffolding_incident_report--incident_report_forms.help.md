# Scaffolding Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help report incidents related to scaffolding on construction sites. It is used to gather information about incidents that have occurred, including details about what happened, when and where they occurred, and any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Incident Details** page with any relevant information about the incident.
2. On the **Site Details** page, provide information about the site where the incident occurred.
3. In the **Incident Reporter** page, enter your name and contact information.
4. On the **Contact Details** page, provide information about who to contact for further questions or concerns.
5. Enter the **Incident Date** and **Incident Time** on the respective pages.
6. In the **Incident Location** field, provide the exact location where the incident occurred.
7. In the **Incident Description**, **Incident Injuries**, **Incident Debris**, **Incident Equipment**, and **Incident Weather** fields, provide any information about what happened during the incident.
8. Click submit when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details**: This is the main page where you will enter information about the incident. 
* **Site Details**: Provide information about the site where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Contact Details**: If you have a contact person for further questions or concerns, enter their name and contact information.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the exact location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Injuries**: If anyone was injured during the incident, enter a description of the injuries.
* **Incident Debris**: If there was any debris or material scattered around after the incident, enter a description of what it was.
* **Incident Equipment**: If any equipment was involved in the incident, enter a description of what it was and its condition.
* **Incident Weather**: If the weather was a contributing factor to the incident, enter a description of the conditions.

## Tips
* Please be as thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact your site management or safety team for clarification.
* This form is used for reporting incidents only, and it is not meant to be used for any other purpose.
