# Road Traffic Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about road traffic incidents, such as accidents, road closures, and other events that affect road usage.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, but only for one incident per form submission.
- Enter the date and location of the incident.
- Provide a description of the incident, including any relevant details.
- Use the remaining fields to provide additional information about the incident.
