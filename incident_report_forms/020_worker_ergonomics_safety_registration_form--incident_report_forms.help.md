# Worker Ergonomics Safety Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about worker ergonomics safety incidents, near-misses, and injuries. It is meant for internal use to document and register incidents that have occurred during work-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review and understand the purpose and instructions of the form.
2.  Fill out the form in its entirety, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
4.  Ensure you have permission from your supervisor or manager before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Worker Ergonomics Safety Registration Form**: This is the main title of the form and a placeholder for your name.
    *   Enter your name as it appears on your company ID or personnel card.
*   **Section 1: This field is currently empty and not displayed in the current form layout.
*   **Title**: Enter a brief title or subject of your incident report.
*   **Body**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident, including what you were doing, what you saw or felt, and any other relevant information.
*   **Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred. If the incident occurred on a span of time, please enter the start time.
*   **Section 2: This field is currently empty and not displayed in the current form layout.**
    *   **Header**: Enter a brief title or subject of your incident report in this section.
*   **Section 1 Title/Body**: This field is currently empty and not displayed in the current form layout.
*   **Section 2 Title/Header**: This field is currently empty and not displayed in the current form layout.

## Tips
*   If you have any supporting documentation or evidence related to the incident, please include it with your form submission.
*   If the incident was reported to a supervisor or manager, make sure to also provide the incident report number or reference number.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please refer to your supervisor or manager for guidance.
*   Please be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure that you provide the most helpful information to the safety team.
*   If the form has already been filled out by you or another worker, do not fill it out again unless you are updating information or have new information to add.
