# Pilot Issue Reporting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pilot Issue Reporting Survey is a form used to report issues that occur during a pilot. It helps in identifying and documenting issues in a systematic way, making it easier to track and address them.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to understand what information is required.
2. For the "Report Issue" field, select "Yes" if an issue occurred during the pilot and "No" if it did not.
3. In the "Report Description" field, provide a detailed description of the issue that occurred.
4. Enter the date of when the issue occurred.
5. For the "Time" field, enter the time when the issue occurred.
6. Choose the location from the options where the issue occurred.
7. In the "Cause" field, select the possible causes of the issue (e.g., "Yes" for "Yes" option or "No" for "No" option).
8. Choose the effect of the issue from the options.
9. Enter the name of the person or team leading the pilot.
10. Enter the name of the person or team responsible for reporting the issue.
11. Enter the contact information of the reporting officer (phone number and email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Report Issue
* **Report Issue** (Select multiple): Check the box if an issue occurred during the pilot.

### Report Description
* **Report Description** (Text): Describe the issue that occurred during the pilot.

### Date
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date when the issue occurred.

### Time
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time when the issue occurred.

### Location
* **Location** (Select One): Choose the location where the issue occurred.

### Cause
* **Cause** (Select Multiple): Select possible causes of the issue (e.g., "Yes" for "Yes" option or "No" for "No" option).

### Effect
* **Effect** (Select One): Choose the effect of the issue from the options.

### Reporting Pilot
* **Reporting Pilot** (Text): Enter the name of the person or team leading the pilot.

### Reporting Officer
* **Reporting Officer** (Text): Enter the name of the person or team responsible for reporting the issue.

### Reporting Officer Contact
* **Reporting Officer Contact** (Text): Enter the contact information of the reporting officer (phone number and email).

### Reporting Officer Contact Phone
* **Reporting Officer Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the reporting officer.

### Reporting Officer Contact Email
* **Reporting Officer Contact Email** (Text): Enter the email of the reporting officer.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible in the "Report Description" field.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.
- If you have any questions, contact the reporting officer for assistance.
