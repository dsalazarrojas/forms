# Fire Department Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting incidents handled by the fire department. It helps to record details about emergency calls, training drills, and special events.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Select the type of call that was made. This can be an "Emergency Call," "Training Drill," or "Special Event."
2. List the situations that were found during the incident.
3. Enter the name of the officer in charge of the incident.
4. Choose the units that were involved in the incident.
5. Choose the units that arrived at the scene.
6. Record the date and time of the incident.
7. Enter the location of the incident.
8. Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
9. Enter the incident number assigned to the incident.
10. Choose the units that were assigned to the incident.
11. Choose the units that were sent to the incident.
12. Enter the title of the officer in charge of the incident.
13. Enter the status of the incident.
14. Enter any notes about the incident.
15. Choose the follow-up action required for the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Call Type** (Select One): This field allows you to select the type of call that was made, such as an "Emergency Call" or "Training Drill."
* **Situation Found** (Select Multiple): This field is for listing the situations that were found during the incident. You can select from the options "Fire", "Rescue", "Medical", or "Other."
* **Officer In Charge** (Text): This field is for entering the name of the officer in charge of the incident.
* **Units Involved** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to choose the units that were involved in the incident.
* **Units Arrived** (Select Multiple): This field is for choosing the units that arrived at the scene.
* **Date Of Incident** (Date): This field is for recording the date of the incident.
* **Time Of Incident** (Time): This field is for recording the time of the incident.
* **Location** (Text): This field is for entering the location of the incident.
* **Description Of Incident** (Text): This field is for providing a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Number** (Text): This field is for entering the incident number assigned to the incident.
* **Assigned Units** (Select Multiple): This field is for choosing the units that were assigned to the incident.
* **Units Sent** (Select Multiple): This field is for choosing the units that were sent to the incident.
* **Officer In Charge Title** (Text): This field is for entering the title of the officer in charge of the incident.
* **Incident Status** (Text): This field is for entering the status of the incident.
* **Incident Notes** (Text): This field is for entering any notes about the incident.
* **Follow Up Action** (Select Multiple): This field is for choosing the follow-up action required for the incident.

## Tips

* Fill out the form accurately and completely to help the fire department optimize its response and services.
* Ensure you have the necessary information before filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the fire department's administrative office for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
