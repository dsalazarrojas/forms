# Policy Violation Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Policy Violation Incident Form is designed to collect detailed information about incidents involving policy violations within an organization. This form helps in documenting the incident, identifying causes, and facilitating further action or investigation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the type of incident (Compliance or Non-Compliance) that occurred.
2.  Enter the date and time the incident took place in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the incident, including relevant details.
4.  Specify the location where the incident occurred.
5.  Describe any incident causes or contributing factors.
6.  Identify the person reporting the incident and provide their name.
7.  Specify any policy violation involved in the incident.
8.  Provide information about any witnesses present during the incident.
9.  Enter the name of the reporting officer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **incident_type** (`incident_type`, Select One, not required): Select one of the two options, "Compliance" or "Non-Compliance", to categorize the type of incident.
*   **incident_date_time** (`incident_date_time`, Date, not required): Enter the date and time of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS.
*   **incident_description** (`incident_description`, Text, not required): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including relevant details.
*   **incident_location** (`incident_location`, Text, not required): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
*   **incident_details** (`incident_details`, Text, not required): Describe any incident causes or contributing factors.
*   **incident_causes** (`incident_causes`, Text, not required): Describe any incident causes or contributing factors.
*   **reportee_name** (`reportee_name`, Text, not required): Identify the person reporting the incident and provide their name.
*   **policy_violation** (`policy_violation`, Text, not required): Specify any policy violation involved in the incident.
*   **witness** (`witness`, Text, not required): Provide information about any witnesses present during the incident.
*   **reporting_officer** (`reporting_officer`, Text, not required): Enter the name of the reporting officer.
