The Incident Report Communication Form is designed to gather information about incidents that have occurred within the organization. It is used to communicate incident details to relevant stakeholders, including team members, management, and external parties. This form helps to ensure that all relevant information is captured and shared efficiently, reducing the risk of miscommunication and facilitating prompt resolution.

1. Fill in the **Incident Report Form** field with a brief description of the incident.
