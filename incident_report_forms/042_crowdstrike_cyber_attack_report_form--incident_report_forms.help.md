# CrowdStrike Cyber Attack Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CrowdStrike Cyber Attack Report Form is a tool used to report and document cybersecurity incidents. The form is designed to collect detailed information about each incident, enabling the incident response team to respond quickly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
* Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, using the options provided.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate and complete.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or incident response team for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Title (1)**: Enter a clear and concise title of the incident.
* **Incident Description (2)**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the date and time it occurred.
* **Date Incident Happened (3)**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type (4)**: Select the type of incident from the provided options.
* **Incident Severity (5)**: Assess the severity of the incident using the provided options.
* **Date Reported (6)**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Date Detected (7)**: Enter the date the incident was detected.
* **Incident Status (8)**: Select the status of the incident from the provided options.
* **Incident Summary (9)**: Summarize the incident, including the impact and key findings.
* **Incident Impact (10)**: Describe the impact of the incident on the organization.
* **Incident Response (11)**: Describe the response actions taken to address the incident.
* **Incident Resolution (12)**: Describe the resolution or outcome of the incident.
* **Incident Investigators (13)**: List the investigators involved in the incident.
* **Incident Assets Involved (14)**: List the assets involved in the incident.
* **Incident Assets Impacted (15)**: List the assets impacted by the incident.
* **Incident Assets Affected (16)**: List the assets affected by the incident.
* **Incident Assets Owners (17)**: List the owners of the assets involved in the incident.
* **Incident Assets Type (18)**: Describe the type of assets involved in the incident.
* **Incident Incident Type Details (19)**: Provide additional details about the incident type.
* **Incident Incident Type (20)**: Describe the type of incident.
* **Incident Incident Category (21)**: Describe the category of the incident.
* **Incident Incident Subcategory (22)**: Describe the subcategory of the incident.
* **Incident Incident Subcategory Details (23)**: Provide additional details about the subcategory.
* **Incident Incident Status (24)**: Describe the status of the incident.
* **Incident Incident Resolution (25)**: Describe the resolution of the incident.

Note: The fields marked as required are essential to complete for the form to be considered valid. Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information for these fields.
