# drug_safety_communication_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and communicate potential drug safety issues and follow-up on incidents related to drug safety. It is intended for use by authorized personnel to gather and track information about incidents that may pose a risk to patients or healthcare workers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the incident, including the date, time, and any relevant information about the incident.
2. Enter patient information, including name, address, and any relevant medical history.
3. Enter reporter information, including name, title, and contact details.
4. Enter contact information for follow-up purposes.
5. Describe the incident cause and outcome.
6. Provide a follow-up plan and status.
7. If applicable, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the incident was successfully followed up.
8. Enter the follow-up date and time.
9. Enter any follow-up comments or notes.
10. Enter follow-up contact information.
11. Update the follow-up status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`): Enter a brief description of the incident, including date, time, and any relevant information.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`): Enter patient name, address, and any relevant medical history.
* **Reporter Information** (`reporter_information`): Enter reporter name, title, and contact details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter contact information for follow-up purposes, including name, phone number, and email address.
* **Drug Safety Communication** (`drug_safety_communication`): Enter any relevant information about the incident and the drug involved.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`): Describe the cause of the incident.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`): Describe the outcome of the incident.
* **Follow-up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`): Enter a plan for follow-up, including any relevant steps or actions.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`): Enter the current follow-up status.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the date for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`): Enter the time for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`): Enter any comments or notes about the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Contact Number** (`follow_up_contact_number`): Enter the phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Follow-up Email** (`follow_up_email`): Enter the email address for follow-up contact.
* **Follow-up Address** (`follow_up_address`): Enter the address for follow-up contact.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Status Update** (`follow_up_status_update`): Enter an update on the follow-up status.
* **Drug Safety Communication ID** (`drug_safety_communication_id`): Enter a unique ID for the drug safety communication.
* **Incident ID** (`incident_id`): Enter a unique ID for the incident.
* **Reporter ID** (`reporter_id`): Enter a unique ID for the reporter.
* **Follow-up ID** (`follow_up_id`): Enter a unique ID for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Type** (`follow_up_type`): Enter the type of follow-up.
* **Follow-up Category** (`follow_up_category`): Enter the category of the follow-up.
* **Form Completion Date** (`form_completion_date`): Enter the date the form was completed.
