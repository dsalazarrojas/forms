# Office And Gym Drop Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents or issues related to office and gym facilities. Please complete this form to provide detailed information about the incident, including location, description, and any evidence collected.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Incident Form (page 1) with all the required information, including a brief description of the incident.
2. On page 2, select one of the options from the Facilities And Gym Drop Report dropdown menu to indicate the type of report being made.
3. For each of the remaining forms (pages 3-15), enter any relevant details or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Form** (`incident_form`, `text`, `required`): This is the first page of the form. Enter a brief description of the incident, including date, time, and location.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details_form`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing more details about the incident, if necessary.
* **Facilities Form** (`facilities_form`, `text`, `required`): Enter any information related to facilities or equipment involved in the incident.
* **Facilities And Gym Drop Report** (`facilities_and_gym_drop_report_form`, `select_one`, `required`): Select one of the options provided to indicate the type of report being made (e.g. maintenance request, safety issue, etc.).
* **Facilities** (`facilities_form2`, `text`, `optional`): If relevant, enter any details about the facilities or equipment involved in the incident.
* **Monitor** (`monitor_form`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any observations or comments related to the incident.
* **Evidence** (`evidence_form`, `text`, `optional`): If evidence was collected or taken during the incident, enter it here.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness_form`, `text`, `optional`): If the incident was related to cleanliness, enter any details about the conditions or circumstances.
* **Safety** (`safety_form`, `text`, `optional`): If the incident was related to safety, enter any details about the hazards or risks involved.
