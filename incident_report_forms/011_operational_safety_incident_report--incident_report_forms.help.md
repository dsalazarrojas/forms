# operational_safety_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about operational safety incidents that occur within an organization. It helps to gather details about the incident and improve safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for each field and type it into the corresponding text box.
2. Make sure all required fields are filled out.
3. Submit the completed form for review and analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Involved** (`employee_involved`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the incident occurred (if known).
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, not required): Enter the time the incident occurred (if known).
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, text, not required): Describe any injuries sustained during the incident (if applicable).
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, required): If there were witnesses, provide their statements or descriptions.
* **Investigation** (`investigation`, text, required): Describe the actions taken to investigate the incident.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, not required): Suggest possible causes of the incident (if known).
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, required): Identify the root cause of the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, required): Describe the actions taken to mitigate or resolve the incident.
* **Lesson Learned** (`lesson_learned`, text, required): Document any lessons learned from the incident.
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, text, not required): Suggest measures to prevent similar incidents in the future (if applicable).
* **Incident Report** (`incident_report`, text, not required): Attach any supporting documents or additional information related to the incident.
* **Assigned Responder** (`assigned_responder`, text, not required): Identify the responder assigned to the incident (if applicable).
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, not required): ( duplicate field, not required, can be ignored).

Please note that the "Incident Cause" and "Root Cause" fields are duplicate fields and should not be completed.
