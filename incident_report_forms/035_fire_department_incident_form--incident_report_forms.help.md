# Fire Department Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Department Incident Form is used to report incidents that have occurred, including fires, medical emergencies, and rescues. This form helps the fire department gather essential information to respond effectively and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter the **Incident Date** (Page 1, field 1) - This field is for recording the date of the incident. Use the format MM/DD/YY.
2.  Enter the **Incident Time** (Page 1, field 2) - This field is for recording the time of the incident. Use the format 24-hour format (HH:MM).
3.  Provide the **Location** (Page 1, field 3) - Describe the exact location where the incident occurred.
4.  Choose the **Incident Type** (Page 1, field 4) - Select 'Fire', 'Medical', or 'Rescue' from the drop-down menu, depending on the nature of the incident.
5.  Enter a detailed **Description** (Page 1, field 5) - Provide a short description of what happened.
6.  Choose how the incident was **Reported** (Page 1, field 6) - Select 'Officer', 'Civilian', or 'Other' from the drop-down menu, depending on who reported the incident.
7.  Enter the **Report Time** (Page 1, field 7) - This field is for recording the time the report was received. Use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
8.  If the incident occurred on a specific **Incident Date 2** (Page 1, field 8), enter it - Use the MM/DD/YY format.
9.  If there is a second **Incident Time 2** (Page 1, field 9), enter it - Use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
10.  Choose how the **Incident Status** (Page 1, field 11) - Select 'Active', 'Closed', or 'In Progress' from the drop-down menu.
11.  Choose the **Incident Priority** (Page 1, field 12) - Select 'Low', 'Medium', or 'High' from the drop-down menu.
12.  Enter the name of the **Incident Responder** (Page 1, field 13) - This field is for recording the person or team responding to the incident.
13.  Optionally, enter the name of another **Incident Responder** (Page 1, field 14) - This field is for recording additional responders.
14.  If necessary, enter the name of a third **Incident Responder** (Page 1, field 15) - This field is for recording more responders.
