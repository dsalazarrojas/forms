# Incident Response Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Response Monitoring Form is a tool used to collect and track incident data for monitoring and reporting purposes. It's likely intended for use by IT teams or incident response teams to gather information about incidents that have occurred or are occurring within their organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the location of the incident from the provided options.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident.
3. Choose the type of incident (e.g., Network, Server, System).
4. Select the current status of the incident (e.g., Open, Closed, In Progress).
5. Enter the time taken to resolve the incident.
6. Choose the responders involved in handling the incident.
7. Provide a brief description of the incident.
8. Add any attachments related to the incident, such as reports or media uploads.
9. Enter any additional comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location (2)**: Select the geographical location where the incident occurred or is occurring.
  * Options: North America, South America, Europe
* **Incident Date (3)**: Enter the date when the incident occurred or is expected to occur.
* **Incident Time (4)**: Enter the time when the incident occurred or is expected to occur.
* **Incident Type (5)**: Choose the type of incident (e.g., Network, Server, System).
  * Options: Network, Server, System
* **Incident Status (7)**: Select the current status of the incident (e.g., Open, Closed, In Progress).
  * Options: Open, Closed, In Progress
* **Resolution Time (8)**: Enter the time taken to resolve the incident.
* **Responders (9)**: Choose the personnel involved in handling the incident.
  * Options: John, Jane, Bob
* **Incident Description (6)**: Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Attachments (13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19)**: Upload any relevant media or reports related to the incident.
  * These fields are intended for uploading supporting files such as reports, email attachments, images, audio, video, other attachments, or other media.
* **Category (22)**: Choose a category for the incident (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
  * Options: Category 1, Category 2, Category 3
* **Assigned To (23, 24)**: Select or enter the person responsible for handling the incident.
  * Options: chatjimmy, chatjimmy1, chatjimmy2
* **Comments (25)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the person responsible for handling the incident or reporting the incident. If you are unsure about any field or option, please reach out to the relevant team for clarification.
