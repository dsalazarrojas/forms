# Employee Injury Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document employee injuries and incidents to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately.
*   Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please ask for assistance or clarification from your supervisor or HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date**: The date of the incident.
*   **Employee Name**: The name of the employee involved.
*   **Job Title**: A brief description of the employee's job or position.
*   **Department**: The department the employee is assigned to. Select all applicable departments.
*   **Location**: The location where the incident occurred.
*   **Type of Injury**: Check if the injury is Medical or Work-Related.
*   **Incident Description**: A detailed description of what happened during the incident.
*   **Employee Status**: Check if the employee is Active or Inactive.
*   **Supervisor Name**: The name of the supervisor involved.
*   **Supervisor Phone**: The supervisor's phone number.
*   **Report To**: Who the incident was reported to (HR, Manager, or Supervisor).
*   **Incident Reported By**: Who reported the incident (Employee, Manager, or Supervisor).
*   **Incident Status**: The status of the incident (Open or Closed).
*   **Incident Closed Date**: The date the incident was closed.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please ask for assistance or clarification.
*   Complete the form in a timely manner to ensure efficient reporting and response.
