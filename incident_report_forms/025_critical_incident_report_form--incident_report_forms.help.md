# Critical Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Critical Incident Report Form is designed to gather information about critical incidents that occur within the organization. It is used to track and analyze incidents such as accidents, security breaches, or other significant events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible.
2. Choose the correct category for the incident from the list provided.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident.
4. Provide a brief description of the incident, including its location.
5. Explain the cause of the incident.
6. Describe the effect of the incident.
7. Enter your name and title as the incident reporter.
8. Attach any relevant documents or evidence to support your report (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, select_one, not required): Select the category that best describes the incident, such as Accident or Security Breach.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, not required): Describe the incident as thoroughly as possible.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, not required): Explain the reason or cause of the incident.
* **Incident Effect** (`incident_effect`, text, not required): Describe the impact or effect of the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, not required): Enter your name and title as the incident reporter.
* **Incident Review** (`incident_review`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Incident Attachment** (`incident_attachment`, text, not required): Attach any relevant documents or evidence to support your report (optional).

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to ensure accurate tracking and analysis of the incident.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or your supervisor for guidance.
* Remember to attach any relevant documents or evidence to support your report.
