# Guest Room Damage Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report any damages or issues that occurred in a guest room. By filling out this form, you will provide valuable information to help us understand the incident and prevent future occurrences. Please only fill in the form when there is actual damage or an incident has occurred in one of our guest rooms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a detailed description of the incident that occurred in the guest room, including any relevant dates and times.
2. Choose whether the guest room was damaged (select at least one from the options).
3. Select a date for the incident, as accurately as possible.
4. Choose the current status of the incident (select one from the options).
5. Enter any additional notes about the incident status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **incident_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location or room number where the incident occurred.
* **damage_description** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened in the guest room, including any relevant dates and times.
* **damage_category** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check all applicable categories where the room was damaged, for example, 'Yes' to indicate that the room was damaged.
* **damage_date** (Date, Optional): Select the date the damage occurred in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YY).
* **incident_status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the current status of the incident, for example, 'Active' to indicate that the incident is still ongoing.
* **incident_status_note** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or updates about the incident status.

## Tips

* If you are unsure whether to fill out this form, please contact our support team for guidance.
* Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible, especially when describing what happened.
* If you are filling out the form for another person or team, please make sure they have given you their permission.
* If the incident is ongoing, please keep this form up to date as new information becomes available.
