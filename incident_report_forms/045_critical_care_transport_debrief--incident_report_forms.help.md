# Critical Care Transport Debrief - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Critical Care Transport Debrief form is designed to gather information about critical care transports, including patient details, transport team, transport type, date and time, and transport outcome. This form helps healthcare professionals document and review transports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the transport team involved from the options provided in the "Transport Team" field.
3. Choose the type of transport from the "Transport Type" field.
4. Enter the date of the transport in the "Transport Date" field.
5. Enter the time of the transport in the "Transport Time" field.
6. Select all applicable outcomes from the "Transport Outcome" field.
7. Enter any comments about the transport in the "Transport Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Transport Team**: Select the team that was involved in the transport, such as a critical care transport team or a general medical team.
* **Transport Type**: Choose the type of transport that took place, such as critical care transport or general medical transport.
* **Transport Date**: Enter the date of the transport in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Transport Time**: Enter the time of the transport in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Transport Outcome**: Select all applicable outcomes from the options provided, such as Critical Care Transport Team, Transfer to Another Hospital, or Disposition.
* **Transport Comments**: Enter any additional comments or details about the transport.
