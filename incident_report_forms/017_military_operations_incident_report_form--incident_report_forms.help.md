# Military Operations Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Military Operations Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about significant events that occur during military operations. This form will help gather accurate details about the incident to aid in reporting, investigation, and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Incident Date**: Please enter the date the incident occurred. This date should be in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. **Time In**: Enter the time the incident occurred. Use military time format, e.g., 1400/1500 hours.
3. **Unit Name**: Enter the name of the unit involved in the incident.
4. **Unit Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
5. **Unit Type**: Enter the type of unit involved in the incident, e.g., infantry, artillery, or support unit.
6. **Incident Type**: Select 'Yes' if the incident was significant or 'No' if it was minor.
7. **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident, including any contributing factors.
8. **Incident Escalation**: Describe any actions taken or steps to mitigate the incident.
9. **Incident Resolution**: Explain how the incident was resolved, including any lessons learned.
10. **Personnel Injured**: List any personnel involved or affected by the incident.
