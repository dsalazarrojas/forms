# Coworking Space Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect incident reports from coworking spaces within the organization, allowing for swift and accurate handling of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coworking Space** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the coworking space where the incident occurred.
* **Issue Description** (2, text, required): Describe the incident in detail, including what happened and any relevant details.
* **Issue Location** (3, text, required): Enter the location within the coworking space where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Staff Member Involved** (5, select_one, required): Check if a staff member was involved in the incident (Yes/No).
* **Witness** (6, text, optional): If available, enter any witness's name or contact information.
* **Coworker Involved** (7, text, optional): If a coworker was involved, please enter their name or contact information.
* **Incident Time** (8, time, required): Enter the time of the incident in 24 hour format (HH:MM).
* **Incident Description** (9, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Resolution Status** (10, select_one, required): Select the current resolution status of the incident (Active/Inactive).
* **Resolution Time** (11, time, required): Enter the time the incident was resolved in 24 hour format (HH:MM).
* **Follow Up** (12, select_one, required): Check if follow up is required (Yes/No).

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each question accurately and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the incident response team.
* This form is meant to be completed promptly, but if you have to leave it incomplete, come back to it when ready.
* Please review and submit the form after you've gathered all necessary information.
