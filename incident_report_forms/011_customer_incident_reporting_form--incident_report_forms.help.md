# Customer Incident Reporting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Incident Reporting Form is used to document and report incidents or issues experienced by customers with our products or services. This form provides a structured way to gather information about the incident and track its resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the incident you experienced.
2. Provide the date and time it occurred.
3. Select how you would like us to contact you (phone, email, or text message).
4. Choose where the incident occurred (onsite, offsite, or remote).
5. Indicate the type of incident (technical, service, or product).
6. Choose the status of the incident (new, ongoing, or resolved).
7. Provide any additional comments or details about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Incident Information** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_1`): Enter a brief description of the incident you experienced. This will help us understand what happened and how we can assist you.
* **Incident Date** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_2`): Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_3`): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_4`): Enter your name as the customer reporting the incident.
* **Contact Method** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_5`): Choose how you would like us to contact you to discuss the incident:
	+ Phone: We will contact you over the phone.
	+ Email: We will contact you via email.
	+ Text Message: We will contact you via text message.
* **Incident Location** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_6`): Choose where the incident occurred:
	+ Onsite: The incident occurred at our office or location.
	+ Offsite: The incident occurred at a location not owned or operated by us.
	+ Remote: The incident occurred remotely or online.
* **Incident Type** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_7`): Choose the type of incident:
	+ Technical: A technical issue or error occurred.
	+ Service: A service-related issue or error occurred.
	+ Product: An issue with our product occurred.
* **Incident Status** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_8`): Choose the status of the incident:
	+ New: The incident is new and has not been resolved.
	+ Ongoing: The incident is still ongoing and being addressed.
	+ Resolved: The incident has been resolved.
* **Incident Resolution** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_9`): Provide any information about how the incident was resolved.
* **Customer Response** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_10`): Enter any response or feedback you have about the incident.
* **Employee Name** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_11`): Enter the name of the employee who assisted or is assisting with the incident.
* **Employee Email** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_12`): Enter the email address of the employee who assisted or is assisting with the incident.
* **Employee Phone** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_13`): Enter the phone number of the employee who assisted or is assisting with the incident.
* **Customer Response Date** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_14`): Enter the date and time you responded to the incident.
* **Incident Resolution Date** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_15`): Enter the date and time the incident was resolved.
* **Incident Resolution Time** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_16`): Enter the time it took to resolve the incident.
* **Customer Response Time** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_17`): Enter the time it took for you to respond to the incident.
* **Additional Comments** (`customer_incident_reporting_form_18`): Provide any additional information or comments about the incident.
