# pony_club_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Pony Club Incident Report** form is used to document and report incidents that have occurred at the Pony Club, allowing staff to gather information, take necessary actions, and prevent similar incidents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the pages**: The form is divided into multiple pages, each with specific fields to fill out. Please navigate through the pages in the order they appear.
2. **Complete each field**: Each field is designed to gather specific information about the incident. Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Activity Description (Page 1)**:
	+ **Activity Description**: A brief description of what happened during the incident, including any details you witnessed or were involved in.
* **Incident Date (Page 1)**:
	+ **Incident Date**: The date on which the incident occurred.
* **Time (Page 1)**:
	+ **Time**: The time of day when the incident happened.
* **Member Name (Page 1)**:
	+ **Member Name**: The name of the member who was involved in or witnessed the incident.
* **Type of Incident (Page 1)**:
	+ **Type of Incident**: Select all applicable types of incident that occurred (e.g. 'Yes' for Yes, 'No' for No).
* **Incident Details (Page 2)**:
	+ **Incident Details**: A more detailed description of what happened, including any relevant context or information.
* **Tips**:
	+ Please fill out the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
	+ If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult with a staff member or supervisor.
	+ Make sure to report any incidents that may affect the safety or well-being of members or staff.
