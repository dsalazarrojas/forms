# CrowdStrike Outage Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crow Strike Outage Report Form is used to document and report incidents or outages that occur during a service. This form provides essential information about the incident, which helps teams analyze and resolve issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the Service Name to identify the affected service.
2. Enter the Start Time of the incident.
3. Enter the End Time of the incident.
4. Provide an estimate of the Duration of the incident.
5. Select the Impact level of the incident from High, Medium, or Low.
6. Enter a detailed Description of what happened.
7. Enter the Cause of the incident, if known.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Name (text)**: Enter the name of the service that experienced the outage or incident.
* **Start Time (time)**: Enter the exact time when the incident started.
* **End Time (time)**: Enter the exact time when the incident ended.
* **Duration (number)**: Estimate the duration of the incident in minutes or hours.
* **Impact (select_one)**: Select the level of impact on the service, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Description (text)**: Provide a clear and detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Cause (text)**: Enter the identified cause of the incident, if known.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when entering dates and times to ensure accurate logging.
* If unsure about the impact level, refer to the service's documentation or previous incident reports for guidance.
* A detailed description can help teams analyze and resolve issues more efficiently.
* If the cause is unknown, still enter a description of what you observed during the incident to help identify potential root causes.
