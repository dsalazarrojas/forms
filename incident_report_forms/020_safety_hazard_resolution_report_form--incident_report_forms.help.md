# Safety Hazard Resolution Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Hazard Resolution Report Form is designed to collect and document information about safety hazards that occur in the workplace, with the goal of identifying root causes and implementing mitigation and corrective actions to prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Provide a thorough and accurate description of the safety hazard that occurred in **Report Details**.
2. Describe the **Hazard Details**, including any relevant facts and circumstances surrounding the incident.
3. Identify the **Root Cause** of the hazard, which is the underlying reason or set of circumstances that led to the incident.
4. Outline a **Mitigation Plan** to address the hazard and prevent similar incidents in the future.
5. Document **Corrective Actions** that will be taken to correct the situation and prevent future occurrences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Report Details
* **Report Details** (`report_details`, text, optional): A brief summary of the safety hazard, including any relevant dates, times, locations, and other essential facts.

### Hazard Details
* **Hazard Details** (`hazard_details`, text, optional): A detailed description of the safety hazard, including any injuries or near-misses that occurred, and any other relevant circumstances.

### Root Cause
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, optional): The underlying reason or set of circumstances that led to the safety hazard, including any systemic or procedural factors that contributed to the incident.

### Mitigation Plan
* **Mitigation Plan** (`mitigation_plan`, text, optional): A step-by-step plan to address the safety hazard and prevent similar incidents in the future, including any necessary training, equipment, or procedural changes.

### Corrective Actions
* **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, text, optional): The specific actions that will be taken to correct the situation and prevent future occurrences, including any necessary repairs, replacements, or changes to policies or procedures.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form to ensure that the safety hazard resolution process is thorough and effective.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or a safety professional for guidance.
* Please note that this form is for reporting safety hazards, not for reporting minor incidents or near-misses.
