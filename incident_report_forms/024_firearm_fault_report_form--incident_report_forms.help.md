# Firearm Fault Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Firearm Fault Report Form is used to gather information about a firearm malfunction or fault. It is used to identify and document the issue, allowing for efficient resolution and prevention of future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Read through each field carefully and fill out the form step by step.
*   For fields with options or select menus, select the most accurate option that reflects your answer.
*   Be as detailed as possible when entering information to help in the resolution of the issue.
*   If any supporting documentation or evidence is available, attach it to the form.
*   Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
*   If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact the relevant authority or department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident details**: Enter a brief overview of the incident or fault.
*   **What malfunction**: Select the type of malfunction from the list.
*   **Condition of firearm at time of malfunction**: Select the condition of the firearm at the time of the malfunction.
*   **Type of firearm involved**: Select the type of firearm involved.
*   **Date of malfunction**: Enter the date of the malfunction in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Time of malfunction**: Enter the time of the malfunction in 24-hour format.
*   **Location of malfunction**: Enter the location of the malfunction.
*   **Witness statement**: If applicable, enter a statement from any witnesses.
*   **Supporting files**: If applicable, attach supporting files or documentation.
*   **Model of firearm**: Enter the model of the firearm.
*   **Serial number of firearm**: Enter the serial number of the firearm.
*   **Calibre of firearm**: Enter the calibre of the firearm.
*   **Weight of firearm**: Enter the weight of the firearm.
*   **Length of firearm**: Enter the length of the firearm.
*   **Barrel length of firearm**: Enter the barrel length of the firearm.
*   **Incident description**: Enter a detailed description of the incident or fault.
*   **Malfunctions description**: Enter a detailed description of the malfunction or fault.

Please note that this form is for reporting malfunctions or faults of firearms. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the relevant authority or department.
