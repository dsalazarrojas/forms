## Foreign Object Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Foreign Object Incident Report Form is a tool used to report and document foreign object incidents within an organization or facility. The form helps collect relevant information about the incident, including the date, time, location, and condition of the object.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*).
2. Provide the date and time of the incident.
3. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
4. Describe the incident in detail.
5. Select the type and condition of the object involved.
6. Specify the person who reported the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Incident (required)**: Enter the date of the incident using a standard date format (DD-MM-YYYY).
* **Time of Incident (required)**: Enter the time of the incident using 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Location (required)**: Enter the exact location where the incident occurred.
* **Description of Incident (required)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Object Type (required)**: Select the type of object involved in the incident.
* **Object Condition (required)**: Select the condition of the object after the incident.
* **Reported By (required)**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Title (optional)**: Enter the title or position of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Organization (optional)**: Enter the name of the organization or department that reported the incident.
* **Date 2 (optional)**: Enter an alternative date if the incident occurred on multiple days.
* **Time 2 (optional)**: Enter an alternative time if the incident occurred at multiple times.
* **Incident Location 2 (optional)**: Enter an alternative location if the incident occurred at multiple locations.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate reporting.
