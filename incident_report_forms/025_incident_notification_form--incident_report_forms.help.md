# Incident Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Incident Notification Form is used to report and document incidents that occur within the organization. This form helps to quickly and accurately gather information about the incident, ensuring that relevant details are recorded and communicated to relevant parties.

<thinking>...

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please select the incident category from the drop-down menu to indicate the nature of the incident.
* Fill in the description of the incident in the text field, including as much detail as possible.
* Enter the date and time of the incident.
* Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* Enter the name and contact information of the person or team that reported the incident.
* Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* If necessary, attach any relevant documentation or images to support the incident report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Category** (Required: False)
	+ Select the category of the incident from the drop-down menu.
* **Description** (Required: False)
	+ Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Date** (Required: False)
	+ Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time** (Required: False)
	+ Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Location** (Required: False)
	+ Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Contact** (Required: False)
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the person or team that reported the incident.
* **Reporter** (Required: False)
	+ Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Notes** (Required: False)
	+ Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **Attachment** (Required: False)
	+ If necessary, attach any relevant documentation or images to support the incident report.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling in the description of the incident.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant team or manager for guidance.
* Remember to keep the attachment section for any additional information that may be necessary to support the incident report.
