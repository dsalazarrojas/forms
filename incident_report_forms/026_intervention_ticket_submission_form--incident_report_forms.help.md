# Intervention Ticket Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intervention Ticket Submission Form is designed to collect information related to incidents or issues that require attention. This form helps gather details about the incident, including user information, incident specifics, and additional details such as location, category, and status.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your user details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2.  Describe the incident, including what happened, where it occurred, and any relevant dates and times.
3.  Choose the category and status of the incident from the provided options.
4.  Provide any additional information or tags related to the incident.
5.  Attach any relevant files or documents.
6.  Provide any notes or comments about the incident.
7.  Indicate if the incident has been resolved or if follow-up is required.
8.  Confirm if the incident has been reopened or closed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Details**: Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
*   **Incident Details**: Describe the incident, including what happened, where it occurred, and any relevant dates and times.
*   **Location**: Enter the location where the incident took place.
*   **Category**: Choose the category of the incident from the provided options.
*   **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Contact**: Enter your contact information for follow-up.
*   **Message**: Provide any additional information or comments about the incident.
*   **Category**: Choose the category of the incident from the provided options.
*   **Status**: Choose the status of the incident from the provided options.
*   **Priority**: Choose the priority of the incident from the provided options.
*   **Type**: Enter the type of the incident.
*   **Urgency**: Enter the urgency of the incident.
*   **Impact**: Enter the impact of the incident.
*   **Reporter**: Enter your information as the reporter of the incident.
*   **Assignee**: Enter the person assigned to handle the incident.
*   **Comment**: Provide any comments or information about the incident.
*   **Tags**: Choose the tags related to the incident from the provided options.
*   **File**: Attach any relevant files or documents.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the incident.
*   **Resolution**: Enter the resolution of the incident.
*   **Follow-up**: Enter any follow-up information about the incident.
*   **Reopened**: Indicate if the incident has been reopened.
*   **Closed**: Indicate if the incident has been closed.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have not already reported this incident or issue.
*   Complete each field accurately to ensure that your report is properly recorded and addressed.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the form administrator for assistance.
