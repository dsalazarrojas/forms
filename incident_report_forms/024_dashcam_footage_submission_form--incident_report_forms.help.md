### Dashcam Footage Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dashcam Footage Submission Form is used to capture detailed information about a dashcam footage submission. It helps us to understand the context and circumstances surrounding the incident, making it easier to review and process your footage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select "Submit"**: Start by reviewing and completing all required fields to ensure accurate and thorough submission.
2. **Fill in each field**:
	* Fill in the **Date** and **Time** fields with the correct timestamp of the incident.
	* Enter the **Vehicle Registration Number**, which helps us identify the vehicle involved.
	* Select the **Type of Incident** from the options provided.
	* Enter a description of the **Location** where the incident occurred.
3. **Submit your form**: Once all fields are filled in, review your submission for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`date`, `required: false`): Enter the date of the incident in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`time`, `required: false`): Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the vehicle's registration number for identification purposes.
* **Type of Incident** (`select_one`, `required: true`):
	+ Choose a category that best describes the type of incident you were involved in (e.g., Roadside, Intersection, Work Zone, Other).
* **Location** (`text`, `required: false`): Provide a detailed description of the location where the incident occurred.

### Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and thorough submission.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before clicking the "Submit" button.
