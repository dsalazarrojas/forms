# isolation_strapping_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Isolation Strapping Incident Report form is designed to collect information about incidents that require strapping, which is the process of temporarily halting or suspending an incident from being investigated or escalated further.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the incident date and time from the calendar picker.
2.  Enter the incident location where it occurred.
3.  Enter your name and title as the incident reporter.
4.  Choose the status of the incident from the dropdown menu.
5.  Briefly describe the incident in the description field.
6.  Provide the strapping date and time for the incident.
7.  Explain the reason for strapping the incident.
8.  Add any additional details or information about the strapping.
9.  Upload any supporting evidence, such as photos or documents, if required.
10. Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, Text, Optional): Enter your name and title as the incident reporter.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, Select One, Required): Choose the status of the incident from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Cancelled
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe the incident that requires strapping.
* **Strapping Date** (`strapping_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the incident was strapped.
* **Strapping Time** (`strapping_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time the incident was strapped.
* **Strapping Reason** (`strapping_reason`, Text, Optional): Explain the reason why the incident was strapped.
* **Strapping Details** (`strapping_details`, Text, Optional): Add any additional details or information about the strapping.
* **Photo Evidence 1** (`supporting_evidence_1`, Text, Optional): Upload a photo that supports the strapping of the incident.
* **Document Evidence 1** (`supporting_evidence_2`, Text, Optional): Upload a document that supports the strapping of the incident.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information to facilitate efficient processing of the incident.
* Attach all relevant evidence to support your strapping request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your team or supervisor for guidance.
* This form is a template and may be customized or adapted according to your organization's specific needs.
