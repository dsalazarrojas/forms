# Child Safety Violation Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document a child safety violation incident, collecting information from the reporter about the incident, the child involved, and the actions taken in response.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date and time of the incident report submission.
2. Provide the reporter's information, including their first and last name, role, email, and phone number.
3. Enter the incident details, including the date and time of the incident, location, and a detailed description of what happened.
4. Report on the child involved, including their name, age, grade level, and any injuries sustained.
5. Answer questions about the violation type, severity, and immediate actions taken.
6. Indicate whether any witnesses were present and list their names.
7. Specify any follow-up actions that are required.
8. Add any additional comments or measures that could prevent similar incidents.
9. Sign and date the form electronically.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use accurate and detailed information when describing the incident and any injuries.
* If unsure about any field, ask for guidance from a supervisor or support staff.
