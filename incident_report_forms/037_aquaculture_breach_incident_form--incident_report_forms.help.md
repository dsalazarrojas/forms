# Aquaculture Breach Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aquaculture Breach Incident Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur in an aquaculture facility. This form helps to document and report incidents, ensuring that necessary information is collected and acted upon in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the "Date of Incident" field.
2. Enter the time of the incident in the "Time of Incident" field.
3. Enter the name of the facility where the incident occurred in the "Facility Name" field.
4. Enter the location of the facility in the "Facility Location" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Description of Breach" field.
6. Indicate the severity level of the incident by selecting the appropriate level from the "Breach Severity Level" dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, enter the affected area or pond in the "Affected Area or Pond" field.
8. If applicable, enter the estimated water loss in gallons in the "Estimated Water Loss (gallons)" field.
9. If applicable, describe the impact on fish stock in the "Impact on Fish Stock" field.
10. Enter the name of the person who reported the incident in the "Reported By" field.
11. Enter the email address of the person who reported the incident in the "Reporting Person Email" field.
12. Describe any immediate actions taken to address the incident in the "Immediate Actions Taken" field.
13. If applicable, describe any follow-up actions required in the "Follow-up Actions Required" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Information
* **Date of Incident (2)**: Enter the date of the incident, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Incident (3)**: Enter the time of the incident, in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **Facility Name (4)**: Enter the name of the facility where the incident occurred.
* **Facility Location (5)**: Enter the location of the facility, e.g., country, region, or building number.
* **Description of Breach (6)**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Breach Severity Level (7)**: Select the severity level of the incident, which determines the level of response required.

  * Minor
  * Moderate
  * Major
  * Critical

### Incident Details
* **Affected Area or Pond (8)**: If applicable, enter the affected area or pond.
* **Estimated Water Loss (gallons) (9)**: If applicable, enter the estimated water loss in gallons.
* **Impact on Fish Stock (10)**: If applicable, describe the impact on fish stock.

### Contact Information
* **Reported By (11)**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reporting Person Email (12)**: Enter the email address of the person who reported the incident.

### Additional Information
* **Immediate Actions Taken (13)**: Describe any immediate actions taken to address the incident.
* **Follow-up Actions Required (14)**: If applicable, describe any follow-up actions required.

**Tips**

* Always complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the incident is properly documented and addressed.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, contact your supervisor or designated aquaculture expert for guidance.
