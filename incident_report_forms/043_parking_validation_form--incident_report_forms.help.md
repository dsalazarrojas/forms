# Parking Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and validate parking transactions, including ticket numbers, vehicle details, parking locations, and validation status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the parking ticket number.
* Fill out the vehicle details (make and model).
* Enter the license plate number.
* Select the parking location.
* Choose the validation date and time.
* Enter the parking duration.
* Enter the parking rate.
* Enter the validation amount.
* Provide any additional notes.
* Select the parking validation status (Valid, Invalid, or Pending).
* Enter the contact name (if applicable).
* Enter the contact phone number (if applicable).
* Enter the contact email (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parking Ticket Number**: Enter the parking ticket number issued by the Parking Enforcement officer. If you do not have the ticket number, this form cannot be submitted.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., Camry, Mustang, etc.).
* **License Plate Number**: Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Parking Location**: Enter the location where the vehicle was parked.
* **Validation Date**: Enter the date when the parking ticket was validated.
* **Validation Time**: Enter the time when the parking ticket was validated.
* **Parking Duration**: Enter the duration of parking time.
* **Parking Rate**: Enter the parking rate applied to the vehicle.
* **Validation Amount**: Enter the amount validated for the parking ticket.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the parking transaction.
* **Parking Validation Status**: Select the status of the parking validation (Valid, Invalid, or Pending).
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person (if applicable).

Note: All fields are optional, but some may be required depending on the parking validation status.
