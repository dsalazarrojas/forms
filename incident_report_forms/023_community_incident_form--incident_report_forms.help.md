# Community Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Incident Form is a crucial tool for reporting incidents that have occurred within the community. This form helps gather essential information about the incident, which is then used to take appropriate actions and prevent similar incidents from happening again.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields:
	* **Reporter name**: Enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
	* **Date of incident**: Report the date and time of the incident.
	* **Location of incident**: Provide the address or a detailed description of the location where the incident occurred.
	* **Incident description**: Give a detailed summary of what happened.
	* **Follow up needed**: Indicate if further action is required.
2. If applicable, provide additional information:
	* **People involved**: List the names or descriptions of anyone who was involved in the incident.
	* **Immediate actions taken**: Describe any immediate actions you took or were taken by others during the incident.
	* **Contact phone**: If you have a contact phone number, please provide it for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporter name** (reporter_name, text, required): Enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Contact phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): If you have a contact phone number, please provide it for follow-up communication.
* **Date of incident** (incident_date, date, required): Report the date of the incident, including the day, month, and year.
* **Time of incident** (incident_time, time, optional): Provide the approximate time of the incident.
* **Location of incident** (location_of_incident, text, required): Provide the address or a detailed description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident description** (incident_description, text, required): Give a detailed summary of what happened.
* **People involved** (people_involved, text, optional): List the names or descriptions of anyone who was involved in the incident.
* **Immediate actions taken** (immediate_actions_taken, text, optional): Describe any immediate actions you took or were taken by others during the incident.
* **Follow up needed** (follow_up_needed, select_one, required): Indicate if further action is required. Choose one of the options: 'True' or 'False'.
