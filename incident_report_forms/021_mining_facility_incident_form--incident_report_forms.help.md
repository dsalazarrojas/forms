# Mining Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mining Facility Incident Form is a tool used to document and report incidents that occur within a mining facility. It's designed to gather essential information about the incident, including the date and time it occurred, the location, and a detailed description of what happened.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the "Date Occured" field.
2.  Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Description" field.
3.  Choose the type of incident that occurred from the "Incident Type" select menu.
4.  Enter any additional relevant details about the incident type in the "Incident Types" field (this field is optional).
5.  Enter the name and contact information for someone responsible or involved in the "Contact Name", "Contact Phone", and "Contact Email" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Mining Facility Incident**: This field is for providing a brief description of the incident.
    Enter a clear and concise description of what happened.
*   **Date Occured**: Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
    This is a critical field for tracking and investigation purposes.
*   **Location**: Enter the location of the incident.
    This field helps understand the context of the incident.
*   **Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
    This field is essential for understanding the incident and its impact.
*   **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident that occurred from the select menu.
    This field helps to categorize and report incidents.
*   **Incident Types**: Enter any additional information about the incident type.
    This field is optional and might be removed or made optional.
*   **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible or involved.
    This field is helpful for follow-up communication.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible or involved.
    This field is helpful for follow-up communication.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the person responsible or involved.
    This field is helpful for follow-up communication.
*   **Assigned Tool**: Enter any tool or equipment assigned to the incident.
    This field is optional and might be removed or made optional.

## Tips

*   Please provide accurate and clear information about the incident.
*   If you're unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
*   Complete the form as thoroughly as possible to ensure accurate reporting and investigation.
