# Community Care Unusual Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about unusual incidents that occur in a community care setting. This information will be used to report and investigate incidents, and to take appropriate actions to prevent or address future incidents.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Date and approximate time of the incident
2.  Location where the incident occurred within the facility
3.  List the names and roles of individuals involved
4.  Provide a brief description of the incident, including events leading up to and the outcome
5.  State the immediate steps taken at the scene
6.  List the names and contact information of any witnesses
7.  Describe any harm or injury observed
8.  Enter your name as the person completing the report
9.  Suggest recommended follow-up actions and the responsible party

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident date** (`incident_date`, required): Enter the date the incident occurred in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Incident time** (`incident_time`, optional): Enter the approximate time the incident occurred.
* **Location of incident** (`location_of_incident`, required): Describe the exact location within the facility where the incident occurred.
* **Individuals involved** (`individuals_involved`, required): List the names and roles of people involved in the incident.
* **Brief description of incident** (`incident_description`, required): Provide a detailed account of the incident, including events leading up to and the outcome.
* **Immediate actions taken** (`immediate_actions_taken`, required): Describe the steps taken at the scene to address the incident.
* **Witness names** (`witness_names`, optional): If there are witnesses, list their names and contact information (phone or email).
* **Harm or injury** (`harm_or_injury`, optional): Describe any harm or injury observed.
* **Reporter name** (`reporter_name`, required): Enter your name as the person completing the report.
* **Recommended follow-up actions** (`follow_up_actions`, optional): Suggest follow-up actions and identify the responsible party.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
*   Ensure accuracy and completeness in reporting all relevant information.
*   If there are witnesses, include their names and contact information.
*   Describe any harm or injury observed, if applicable.
*   Enter your name as the person completing the report.
*   Suggest follow-up actions and identify the responsible party, if applicable.
