# crowdstrike_global_threat_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The crowdstrike_global_threat_report_form is a form designed to collect information about global threats or incidents that may impact the organization's security posture. It is intended to be completed by individuals who identify a potential threat or incident and need to report it to the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the form fields with as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### global-threat-report-form (1)
* **global-threat-report-form** (`global-threat-report-form`, text, required): This field is the title of the form. Please enter the title of the report, such as "Global Threat Report: Phishing Attack".

### report-form-section (2)
* **report-form-section** (`report-form-section`, text, required): This field is used to describe the section of the report being submitted. Please enter a brief description of the section, such as "Malware Attack".

### threat-info (3)
* **threat-info** (`threat-info`, text, required): This field is used to provide more information about the threat or incident. Please provide as much detail as possible, including any relevant dates, times, and locations.

### threat-tactics-techniques (4)
* **threat-tactics-techniques** (`threat-tactics-techniques`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select multiple threat tactics or techniques that are relevant to the threat or incident. Please select all that apply, such as "Social Engineering" or "Phishing".

### incident-info (5)
* **incident-info** (`incident-info`, text, required): This field is used to provide more information about the incident, such as any relevant dates, times, and locations.

### date-time (6)
* **date-time** (`date-time`, date, required): This field is used to select a date and time related to the threat or incident. Please select a date and time that best reflects the timeline of the event.

### time (7)
* **time** (`time`, time, required): This field is used to select a time of day related to the threat or incident. Please select a time that best reflects the timeline of the event.

### time-zone (8)
* **time-zone** (`time-zone`, select_one, required): This field is used to select a time zone related to the threat or incident. Please select the time zone where the incident occurred.

### location (9)
* **location** (`location`, text, required): This field is used to provide the location where the threat or incident occurred. Please enter the city, country, or region where the incident occurred.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your local support team for guidance.
* Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and completely before submitting it to the relevant authorities.
