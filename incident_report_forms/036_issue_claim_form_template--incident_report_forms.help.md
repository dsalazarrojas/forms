# Issue Claim Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Issue Claim Form Template is designed to collect detailed information about incidents that occur within the organization. It helps in documenting and tracking incidents, facilitating prompt resolution and minimizing the impact of such events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided below.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Issue Claim** (`issue_claim_form`)
- This is the main title for your incident report. It should clearly and concisely describe the incident you are reporting.
- **Required**

### 2. **Incident Date** (`incident_date`)
- Enter the date when the incident occurred, using the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Required**

### 3. **Incident Time** (`incident_time`)
- Enter the time when the incident occurred (e.g., `08:00` for 8 am).
- **Optional**

### 4. **Incident Location** (`incident_location`)
- Enter the location where the incident occurred (e.g., room number, building, or floor).
- **Required**

### 5. **Incident Description** (`incident_description`)
- Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident.
- **Required**

### 6. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`)
- Enter the contact information of those involved or affected by the incident.
- **Required**

### 7. **Incident Status** (`incident_status`)
- Choose whether the incident is **Active** or **Inactive**.
- **Required**

### 8. **Incident Status Options** (`incident_status_options`)
- Select multiple options that apply to your incident status (e.g., if you chose **Active**, you might select Option 1 and Option 2).
- **Optional**

### 9. **Incident Priority** (`incident_priority`)
- Choose whether the incident has a **Yes** or **No** priority level.
- **Required**

### 10. **Incident Priority Options** (`incident_priority_options`)
- Select multiple options that apply to your incident priority level (e.g., if you chose **Yes**, you might select Option 1 and Option 2).
- **Optional**

### 11. **Incident Type** (`incident_type`)
- Choose whether the incident is **Yes** or **No** in type.
- **Required**

### 12. **Incident Type Options** (`incident_type_options`)
- Select multiple options that apply to your incident type (e.g., if you chose **Yes**, you might select Option 1 and Option 2).
- **Optional**

### 13. **Incident Category** (`incident_category`)
- Choose whether the incident falls under a **Yes** or **No** category.
- **Required**

### 14. **Incident Category Options** (`incident_category_options`)
- Select multiple options that apply to your incident category (e.g., if you chose **Yes**, you might select Option 1 and Option 2).
- **Optional**

### 15. **Incident Resolution** (`incident_resolution`)
- Enter the resolution or outcome of the incident.
- **Required**

### 16. **Incident Resolution Status** (`incident_resolution_status`)
- Choose whether the resolution is **Active** or **Inactive**.
- **Required**

### 17. **Incident Resolution Status Options** (`incident_resolution_status_options`)
- Select multiple options that apply to your incident resolution status (e.g., if you chose **Active**, you might select Option 1 and Option 2).
- **Optional**

### 18. **Incident Comment** (`incident_comment`)
- Enter any additional comments or feedback regarding the incident.
- **Optional**

### 19. **Incident Attach File** (`incident_attach_file`)
- Attach relevant files related to the incident (e.g., images, videos, or audio recordings).
- **Optional**

### 20. **Incident Attach Link** (`incident_attach_link`)
- Provide a link to external resources related to the incident.
- **Optional**

### 21. **Incident Attach Image** (`incident_attach_image`)
- Attach images related to the incident.
- **Optional**

### 22. **Incident Attach Audio** (`incident_attach_audio`)
- Attach audio recordings related to the incident.
- **Optional**

### 23. **Incident Attach Video** (`incident_attach_video`)
- Attach videos related to the incident.
- **Optional**

### 24. **Incident Attach Other** (`incident_attach_other`)
- Attach other relevant resources related to the incident.
- **Optional**

### 25. **Incident Review** (`incident_review`)
- Enter a final review or assessment of the incident's impact and resolution.
- **Required**
