# public_utility_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about public utility incidents, including customer details, incident specifics, and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form with the required information to the best of your ability.
2. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Customer Details
* **Customer Name** (`customer_details`): Enter the name of the customer affected by the incident.
* **Customer Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact phone number of the customer.

### 2. Address
* **Address** (`address`): Select the relevant address from the options provided, or enter a new address if it is not listed.

### 3. Incident Date
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Choose the date of the incident from the options provided.
* Note: Date and time fields will likely be synchronized.

### 4. Incident Time
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Choose the time of the incident from the options provided.

### 5. Incident Location
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the physical location of the incident.

### 6. Description
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of the incident.

### 7. Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person to contact for further information.

### 8. Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact phone number.

### 9. Incident Type
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select the type of incident from the options provided.

### 10. Service Area
* **Service Area** (`service_area`): Enter the service area affected by the incident.

### 11. Date
* **Date** (`incident_date2`): Choose the date of the incident from the options provided.
* Note: Date and time fields will likely be synchronized.

### 12. Time
* **Time** (`incident_time2`): Choose the time of the incident from the options provided.

### 13. Utility
* **Utility** (`utility`): Enter the type of utility affected by the incident.

### 14. Status
* **Status** (`incident_status`): Select the current status of the incident from the options provided.

### 15. Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes about the incident.

### 16. Customer Notes
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Enter any additional notes from the customer.

### 17. Customer Agreed
* **Customer Agreed** (`customer_agreed`): Enter any agreement or acknowledgement from the customer.

### 18. Employee Notes
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`): Enter any additional notes from the employee handling the incident.

### 19. Incident Cause
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`): Enter the cause of the incident.

### 20. Resolved
* **Resolved** (`incident_resolved`): Select the status of the incident from the options provided.

### 21. Follow-up
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`): Enter any follow-up actions or next steps.

### 22. Follow-up Date
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Choose the date for follow-up actions.

### 23. Follow-up Time
* **Time** (`follow_up_time`): Choose the time for follow-up actions.

### 24. Follow-up Location
* **Follow-up Location** (`follow_up_location`): Enter the location for follow-up actions.

### 25. Follow-up Notes
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`): Enter any notes about the follow-up actions.

## Tips

* If the form is not complete, please fill out as much of it as you can and submit it for review.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
