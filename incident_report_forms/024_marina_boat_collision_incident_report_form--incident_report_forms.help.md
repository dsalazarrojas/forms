# Marina Boat Collision Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marina Boat Collision Incident Report Form is used to report incidents that occur in marina environments, such as boat collisions. This form is used to gather information about the incident, including details about the boats involved, the location and time of the collision, and the contact information of the reporting officer. The form is a crucial tool for marina authorities to quickly and effectively respond to and investigate incidents.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Marina Boat Collision Incident Report Form" to access the form.
2.  Fill in the form with all the required information. You will be asked to provide details about the boat(s) involved in the incident, including their type, length, width, draft, and speed. You will also be asked to report on the time and location of the collision, as well as whether any other vessels were involved.
3.  Report whether there were any witnesses to the incident and, if so, provide a description of what they witnessed.
4.  Provide a description of the damage caused by the collision, including any amounts of damages.
5.  Enter the name, email, and phone number of the reporting officer.
6.  Enter the incident report number and date and time of the report.
7.  Select the status of the incident report (Active or Inactive).
8.  Click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Boat Details** (Boat Details, Text, optional): Enter a brief description of the boats involved in the incident.
* **Collision Details** (Collision Details, Text, optional): Describe the circumstances surrounding the collision.
* **Boat Type** (Boat Type, Select, optional): Report if the boat involved was a "Yes" or "No" type.
* **Boat Length** (Boat Length, Number, optional): Enter the length of the boat in meters or feet.
* **Boat Width** (Boat Width, Number, optional): Enter the width of the boat in meters or feet.
* **Boat Draft** (Boat Draft, Number, optional): Enter the draft of the boat in meters or feet.
* **Boat Speed** (Boat Speed, Number, optional): Report the speed of the boat at the time of the collision.
* **Boat Direction** (Boat Direction, Select, optional): Report if the boat was traveling in the direction of "Yes" or "No".
* **Time At Collision** (Time At Collision, Date, optional): Enter the date and time of the collision.
* **Location At Collision** (Location At Collision, Note, optional): Enter a description of the location where the collision occurred.
* **Witnesses** (Witnesses, Select Multiple, optional): Report if there were any witnesses to the incident and provide a description of what they witnessed.
* **Other Vessels Involved** (Other Vessels Involved, Select Multiple, optional): Report if other vessels were involved in the incident.
* **Damage Description** (Damage Description, Text, optional): Provide a detailed description of any damage caused by the collision.
* **Damage Amount** (Damage Amount, Number, optional): Report the estimated cost of any damages sustained.
* **Reporting Officer Name** (Reporting Officer Name, Text, optional): Enter the name of the officer who reported the incident.
* **Reporting Officer Email** (Reporting Officer Email, Email, optional): Enter the email of the officer who reported the incident.
* **Reporting Officer Phone** (Reporting Officer Phone, Text, optional): Enter the phone number of the officer who reported the incident.
* **Incident Report Number** (Incident Report Number, Number, optional): Enter the incident report number.
* **Incident Report Date** (Incident Report Date, Date, optional): Enter the date of the report.
* **Incident Report Time** (Incident Report Time, Time, optional): Enter the time of the report.
* **Incident Report Status** (Incident Report Status, Select, optional): Report the status of the incident as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Incident Report Closed** (Incident Report Closed, Number, optional): Report if the incident report is closed, with an estimate of the number of days it was closed for.
