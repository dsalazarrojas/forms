# Festival Vendor Dispute Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about disputes or incidents involving festival vendors. It helps festival staff or management document and follow up on incidents, gathering details about the incident, evidence, and witness statements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are marked as required in the form and are necessary for the report to be considered complete.
2. Enter the incident date and time in the date and time fields, respectively.
3. Select the type of incident that occurred from the incident type field.
4. Enter the vendor's name and contact information in the vendor name and contact info fields.
5. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the incident description field.
6. If relevant, attach any evidence related to the incident in the evidence field.
7. If applicable, provide any witness information in the witness info field.
8. Select the current follow-up status from the follow-up status field.
9. Enter any response from the vendor in the vendor response field.
10. Enter the outcome of the incident in the incident outcome field.
11. Provide any additional notes or comments in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, text, required): Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., "Vendor was not performing well", "Vendor was overcharging customers", etc.).
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the vendor involved in the incident.
* **Vendor Contact Info** (`vendor_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the vendor, such as phone number, email, or address.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Evidence** (`evidence`, text, optional): If you have any evidence related to the incident, such as photos or videos, attach it here.
* **Witness Info** (`witness_info`, text, optional): If there were any witnesses to the incident, provide their contact information or a brief description of what they saw.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current follow-up status of the incident (e.g., "Follow-up Scheduled", "Follow-up Completed", "Follow-up Not Needed").
* **Vendor Response** (`vendor_response`, text, optional): Enter any response from the vendor related to the incident.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, text, optional): Enter the outcome of the incident (e.g., "Issue resolved", "Vendor apologized", etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*).
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any evidence or witness statements related to the incident, attach them to the form.
