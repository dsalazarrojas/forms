# Unauthorized Access Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unauthorized Access Incident Form is a tool used to report incidents of unauthorized access to network resources. This form helps gather information about the incident, including date, time, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident using the calendar picker.
2. Enter the time of the incident using 24-hour format (HH:MM).
3. Enter a brief description of what happened, including any relevant details about the incident.
4. Enter the user ID of the individual involved in the incident (if applicable).
5. Enter the username of the individual involved in the incident (if applicable).
6. Enter the IP address of the device or system involved in the incident (if applicable).
7. Enter the email address of the individual or system involved in the incident (if applicable).
8. Enter the phone number of the individual or system involved in the incident (if applicable).
9. Select the type of incident that occurred (Phishing, Scanning, or Unauthorized Access).
10. Select the level of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
11. Select the users affected by the incident (Admin, User, or Guest).
12. Enter the name of the person who reported the incident (if applicable).
13. Enter the name of the reporting officer (if applicable).
14. Mark the date the incident was reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident.
- **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
- **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident, including any relevant details.
- **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the user ID of the individual involved in the incident (if applicable).
- **Username** (`username`, text, required/optional): Enter the username of the individual involved in the incident (if applicable).
- **IP Address** (`ip_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the IP address of the device or system involved in the incident (if applicable).
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the individual or system involved in the incident (if applicable).
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the individual or system involved in the incident (if applicable).
- **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of incident that occurred (Phishing, Scanning, or Unauthorized Access).
- **Incident Level** (`incident_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the level of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Affected Users** (`affected_users`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the users affected by the incident (Admin, User, or Guest).
- **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident (if applicable).
- **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the reporting officer (if applicable).
- **Reported** (`reported`, date, required/optional): Mark the date the incident was reported.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a thorough report.
- If applicable, provide as much detail as possible to help in the investigation process.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or reporting officer for guidance.
