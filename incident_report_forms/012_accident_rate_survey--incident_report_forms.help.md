## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the reporting period (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annually, or Annually) that applies to your data.
2. Enter the start and end dates for the selected period.
3. Fill out the organization information, including name and department/division.
4. Provide details on the number of employees, work hours, recorded incidents, near misses, first aid cases, and lost time injuries that occurred during the reporting period.

7. Describe any safety initiatives implemented during this period.
8. Add any additional comments or recommendations.
