# Educational Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Facility Incident Form is used to document incidents that occur within an educational facility, such as schools, universities, or other educational institutions. This form helps to gather important information about the incident, including the date and time it occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Incident Form" page to start filling out the form.
2. Provide the date of the incident in the "date" field. Make sure to enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Enter the time of the incident in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Form** (educational_facility_incident_form_page_1, text, required): Enter a brief description of the incident. This is an open-ended field where you can provide as much information as you need to describe what happened.
* **date** (incident_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident. This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide accurate and precise information.
* **Time** (incident_time, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident. Like the "date" field, this is not required but is essential for a more detailed investigation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a thorough investigation.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the form administrator: form@gic.mx.
