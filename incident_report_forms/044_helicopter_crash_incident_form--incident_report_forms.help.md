# Helicopter Crash Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about helicopter crash incidents, aiming to gather details about the incident, helicopter, and incident reporters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with a asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Select the relevant options for the select fields.
3. Enter accurate information for text fields.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Prophets** (`incident_prophets`, text, optional): Describe the incident propehets involved in the crash.
* **Date of Crash** (`date_of_crash`, date, optional): Enter the date of the helicopter crash incident.
* **Helicopter Model** (`helicopter_model`, select_one, optional): Choose the model of the helicopter involved in the crash.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Describe the location of the helicopter crash incident.
* **Time of Crash** (`time_of_crash`, time, optional): Enter the time of the helicopter crash incident.
* **Aircraft Registration** (`aircraft_registration`, text, optional): Enter the registration number of the helicopter involved in the crash.
* **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who reported the incident (Pilot, Crew, or Authority).
* **Crash Cause** (`crash_cause`, select_one, required): Select the reason for the crash (Engine Failure, Human Error, or Mechanical Failure).
* **Flight Recorder Data** (`flight_recorder_data`, text, optional): Enter any relevant flight recorder data.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Describe the incident in detail.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reporter.
* **Pilot In Charge** (`pilot_in_charge`, text, optional): Enter the name of the pilot in charge.
* **Time Crash** (`time_crash`, time, optional): Enter the time of the crash.
* **Aircraft Type** (`aircraft_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of aircraft involved in the crash.
* **Aircraft Manufacturer** (`aircraft_manufacturer`, text, optional): Enter the manufacturer of the aircraft.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, optional): Enter the flight number of the aircraft.
* **Aircraft Registration 2** (`aircraft_registration_2`, text, optional): Enter the registration number of the second aircraft (if applicable).
* **Reported By** (`incident_reported_by_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who reported the incident (Pilot, Crew, or Authority).
* **Incident Location 2** (`incident_location_2`, text, optional): Enter the location of the incident 2.
* **Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Description** (`incident_description_2`, text, optional): Describe the incident in detail 2.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* Use clear and concise language to fill out the text fields.
* Select the correct options for select fields.
