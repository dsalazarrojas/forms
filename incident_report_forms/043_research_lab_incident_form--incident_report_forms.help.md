# research_lab_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Lab Incident Form is designed to document and track incidents that occur in the lab setting. This form will help investigators gather information about the incident, including its type, date, time, location, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready. Start by selecting the correct incident type and providing the date and time the incident occurred. If a witness was present, select the appropriate option to indicate their involvement. Then, provide details about the incident, including its location and any relevant comments. Move on to report the incident, including the date and time it was reported, and the individual who reported it. If follow-up actions are necessary, indicate their completion status and date. Finally, update the incident status and provide any comments or further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (Introduction, text, required): Briefly describe the purpose and context of the incident report.
- **Incident Type** (Incident Type, select_one, required): Indicate whether an incident occurred.
- **Incident Date** (Incident Date, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
- **Incident Time** (Incident Time, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
- **Location** (Location, text, optional): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
- **Incident Details** (Incident Details, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
- **Witness** (Witness, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if a witness was present during the incident.
- **Investigation Status** (Investigation Status, select_one, required): Select the current status of the investigation.
- **Follow-Up Actions** (Follow-Up Actions, text, optional): Describe any follow-up actions taken or planned.
- **Root Cause** (Root Cause, select_one, required): Indicate whether a root cause was identified.
- **Incident Report Date** (Incident Report Date, date, required): Enter the date the incident was reported.
- **Incident Report Time** (Incident Report Time, time, required): Enter the time the incident was reported.
- **Reported By** (Reported By, text, optional): Specify the individual who reported the incident.
- **Reported By Phone** (Reported By Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual who reported the incident.
- **Reported By Email** (Reported By Email, email, optional): Enter the email of the individual who reported the incident.
- **Incident Reported** (Incident Reported, select_one, required): Indicate if the incident was reported.
- **Follow-Up** (Follow-Up, text, optional): Describe any follow-up actions taken or planned.
- **Follow-Up Date** (Follow-Up Date, date, optional): Enter the date follow-up actions were completed or planned.
- **Follow-Up Time** (Follow-Up Time, time, optional): Enter the time follow-up actions were completed or planned.
- **Follow-Up Status** (Follow-Up Status, text, optional): Specify the status of follow-up actions.
- **Incident Status** (Incident Status, text, required): Update the status of the incident.
- **Incident Status Date** (Incident Status Date, date, optional): Enter the date the incident status was updated.
- **Incident Status Time** (Incident Status Time, time, optional): Enter the time the incident status was updated.
- **Incident Status Comment** (Incident Status Comment, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the incident status.
- **Incident Status Comment Date** (Incident Status Comment Date, date, optional): Enter the date the incident status comment was added.

**Tips**:
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Use specific dates and times to accurately record incident details.
- If a field is not applicable to the incident, mark it as "N/A" or leave it blank.
- Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in incident status and status comments.
