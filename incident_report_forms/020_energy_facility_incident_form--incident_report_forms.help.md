# Energy Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about incidents that have occurred at energy facilities, including the type of incident, facility details, and any relevant descriptions or actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of incident that occurred from the list of options provided.
2. Enter the facility ID number (if applicable).
3. Enter the name of the facility where the incident occurred.
4. Select the date and time of the incident.
5. Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
6. Enter the location of the facility where the incident occurred.
7. Provide a brief description of the incident.
8. Enter any additional details about the incident.
9. Identify the root cause of the incident.
10. Describe any corrective actions taken or planned.
11. Outline any follow-up actions to be taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Type** (Select One):
	+ Choose from Natural Disaster, Equipment Failure, or Human Error.
* **Facility ID** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the facility's identifier number, if applicable.
* **Facility Name** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the name of the facility where the incident occurred.
* **Event Date** (Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date of the incident in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Event Time** (Time, Optional):
	+ Enter the time of the incident in the correct format (HH:MM:SS).
* **Incident Reporter** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Facility Location** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the location of the facility where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (Text, Optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Details** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter any additional details about the incident.
* **Root Cause** (Text, Optional):
	+ Identify the root cause of the incident.
* **Corrective Action** (Text, Optional):
	+ Describe any corrective actions taken or planned.
* **Follow-Up Action** (Text, Optional):
	+ Outline any follow-up actions to be taken.
