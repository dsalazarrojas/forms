# Misdiagnosis Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Misdiagnosis Incident Form is used to report incidents where a patient has been misdiagnosed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that the incident has already been reported and investigated. If not, please report the incident before filling out this form.
2. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible. You can add comments if necessary.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Healthcare Professional** (`healthcare_professional`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare professional who was responsible for the misdiagnosis.
* **Patient** (`patient`, text, required): Enter the name of the patient who was misdiagnosed.
* **Date Of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required): Enter the date the misdiagnosis occurred.
* **Time Of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, required): Enter the time the misdiagnosis occurred.
* **Location Of Incident** (`location_of_incident`, text, required): Enter the location where the misdiagnosis occurred.
* **Misdiagnosis Type** (`misdiagnosis_type`, text, required): Describe the type of misdiagnosis.
* **Contributing Factors** (`contributing_factors`, text, required): List any factors that contributed to the misdiagnosis.
* **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`, text, required): Describe the actions taken after the misdiagnosis was identified.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Add any comments or additional information.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool assigned to investigate the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Location Of Event** (`location_of_event`, text, required): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Misdiagnosis Date** (`misdiagnosis_date`, date, required): Enter the date the misdiagnosis was identified.
* **Misdiagnosis Time** (`misdiagnosis_time`, time, required): Enter the time the misdiagnosis was identified.
* **Patient Dob** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Weight** (`patient_weight`, number, required): Enter the patient's weight.
* **Patient Height** (`patient_height`, number, required): Enter the patient's height.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter the patient's age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select the patient's gender.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_one, required): Select the patient's ethnicity.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, required): Select the medical conditions related to the incident.
* **Medical Condition List** (`medical_condition_list`, select_multiple, required): Select the medical conditions related to the incident.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person assigned to investigate the incident.

Note: Some fields have multiple options, select the relevant option that best describes the incident.
