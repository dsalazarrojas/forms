# Use Of Force Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended to collect information about an incident where force may have been used. The form is used to document and assess the circumstances surrounding the incident, including the date, details of the incident, and the response and status of the investigation. This form is used to ensure that incidents are thoroughly documented and investigated in accordance with relevant policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the `incident_date` field.
2. Provide a detailed account of the use of force in the `use_of_force_details` field.
3. Select the officer in charge from the `officer_in_charge` field.
4. List any suspects involved in the incident in the `suspect` field.
5. List any witnesses to the incident in the `witnesses` field.
6. Select the officers involved in the incident in the `officer_involved` field.
7. Provide a detailed account of the incident location in the `incident_location` field.
8. Enter the time of the incident in the `incident_time` field.
9. Select the officer's response to the incident in the `officer_response` field.
10. Select the status of the investigation in the `investigation_status` field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the incident in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Use Of Force Details**: Provide a detailed account of the use of force, including what happened and why it was necessary.
* **Officer In Charge**: Select the officer who was in charge of the incident.
	+ Options: John Doe, Jane Smith, Jim Brown
* **Suspect**: Select all suspects involved in the incident.
	+ Options: Suspect 1, Suspect 2, Suspect 3
* **Witnesses**: Select all witnesses to the incident.
	+ Options: Witness 1, Witness 2, Witness 3
* **Officer Involved**: Select all officers involved in the incident.
	+ Options: Officer 1, Officer 2, Officer 3
* **Incident Location**: Provide a detailed account of the incident location, including the address, room number, or other relevant details.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Officer Response**: Select the officer's response to the incident.
	+ Options: Response 1, Response 2, Response 3
* **Investigation Status**: Select the status of the investigation.
	+ Options: Open, Closed
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the incident, including any relevant details or context.
