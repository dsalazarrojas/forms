# cyber_security_incident_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and track cyber security incidents within the organization. It gathers information about the incident type, date, time, location, description, reporter, status, and assignee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of incident you are reporting.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident.
3. Enter a detailed description of the incident.
4. Enter your name and title (if applicable) as the incident reporter.
5. Select the current status of the incident.
6. Select the assignee for the incident.
7. Click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one): Choose the type of incident you are reporting, such as "Information Technology Incident" or "Security Incident".
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date): Enter the date of the incident in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time): Enter the time of the incident in the format 12:00 AM/PM or 24-hour format.
- **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
- **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
- **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text): Enter your name and title (if applicable).
- **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_multiple): Select the current status of the incident, such as "Pending", "In Progress", or "Resolved".
- **Assignee** (`incident_assignee`, select_one): Choose the person or team responsible for handling the incident, such as "John" or "Jane".
