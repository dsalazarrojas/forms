# Production Downtime Investigation Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to investigate and document production downtime incidents. The purpose of this form is to gather detailed information about the incident, identify its root cause, and document the corrective actions taken to prevent future occurrences. This form will be used by production team members and supervisors to report, analyze, and manage production downtime incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the incident details and ensure that all necessary information is gathered and documented.
2. Fill in the incident start and end dates and times to provide a clear timeline of the incident.
3. Enter the duration of the incident, which is the length of time the production line was down.
4. Provide a brief description of the root cause of the incident.
5. Outline the corrective actions taken to prevent future occurrences.
6. Check the follow-up status to indicate whether follow-up actions are still needed.
7. Select the incident type from the provided options (e.g., Manufacturing, Equipment failure, Human error, etc.).
8. Enter the production line affected and the asset involved in the incident.
9. Indicate the shift during which the incident occurred and the employees and supervisors involved.
10. Select the department and location where the incident occurred.
11. Enter the asset type and condition before and after the incident.
12. Indicate the asset status before and after the incident.
13. Provide a detailed description of the root cause and corrective actions taken.
14. Check the status of the corrective actions and follow-up actions.
15. Enter the date and time of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Start** (`incident_start`): Enter the date and time when the incident started.
* **Incident End** (`incident_end`): Enter the date and time when the incident ended.
* **Duration** (`duration`): Enter the length of time the production line was down, in hours.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`): Provide a brief description of the root cause of the incident.
* **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`): Outline the corrective actions taken to prevent future occurrences.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`): Check whether follow-up actions are still needed.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select one of the provided options (e.g., Manufacturing, Equipment failure, Human error, etc.).
* **Production Line** (`production_line`): Enter the production line affected by the incident.
* **Shift** (`shift`): Select the shift during which the incident occurred (e.g., 1st Shift, 2nd Shift, 3rd Shift).
* **Employee** (`employee`): Enter the employee(s) involved in the incident.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`): Enter the supervisor(s) involved in the incident.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department where the incident occurred (e.g., Manufacturing, Engineering, Operations).
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Asset** (`asset`): Enter the asset involved in the incident.
* **Asset Type** (`asset_type`): Select the asset type (e.g., Machine, Equipment, Other).
* **Asset Condition** (`asset_condition`): Select the asset condition before and after the incident (e.g., Good, Bad, Fair, New).
* **Asset Status** (`asset_status`): Select the asset status before and after the incident (e.g., In Use, Out of Use, Maintenance).
* **Root Cause Description** (`root_cause_description`): Provide a detailed description of the root cause of the incident.
* **Action Description** (`action_description`): Outline the corrective actions taken to prevent future occurrences.
* **Corrective Action Status** (`corrective_action_status`): Check the status of the corrective actions (e.g., Open, Closed, In Progress, Pending).
* **Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_status`): Check whether follow-up actions are still needed.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Shift Hours** (`shift_hours`): Enter the number of hours the production line was down.
