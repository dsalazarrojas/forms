# Historical Site Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Historical Site Incident Form is used to document and record incidents that have occurred at a site. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that necessary steps are taken to prevent similar incidents in the future. By completing this form, users can provide a detailed account of what happened, including the incident type, location, and description, as well as any hazards involved and the actions taken to address the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine the incident type: Select the correct type of incident from the provided options (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
2. Enter the site name: Type in the name of the site where the incident occurred.
3. Specify the location: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
4. Choose the incident date and time: Select the date and time of the incident using the date and time input fields.
5. Provide a detailed description of the incident: Enter a clear and concise description of what happened.
6. Identify affected areas: List any areas that were affected by the incident.
7. List hazards involved: Describe any hazards that were involved in the incident.
8. Describe the investigation: Explain how the incident was investigated.
9. Outline actions taken: Describe the actions taken in response to the incident.
10. Add any additional notes: Enter any additional information regarding the incident.
11. Identify the incident reporter: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
12. Check if the incident escalated: Select "Yes" if the incident required escalation.
13. Enter responder information: Enter the number of responders and the time they responded.
14. Enter any further details: Enter any additional information about the responder.
