# Food Processing Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Food Processing Facility Incident Form is used to record and report incidents that occur within the facility. This includes any events or issues that may have affected the production process, product quality, or overall facility operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start filling out the form, please select the date and time of the incident from the calendars provided for `incident_date` and `incident_time` fields.
2.  For `incident_location`, describe the area or location where the incident occurred.
3.  In `incident_summary`, provide a brief summary of the incident, including any relevant details or context.
4.  If an incident involved multiple facilities, select all applicable facilities for `facility_id_2` field.
5.  Choose the cause(s) of the incident from the `incident_cause` fields.
6.  Finally, select the facility involved in the incident from the `facility_id_3` field, if applicable.
