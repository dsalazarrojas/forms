# Prison Injury Compensation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prison Injury Compensation Claim Form is designed to collect information regarding incidents that have occurred within a prison setting, focusing on the compensation that can be claimed for these incidents. To ensure accuracy and efficiency, please review this guide to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2.  Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident details
**Incident details (incident_details, text, Optional)**: Provide a clear and concise description of the incident that occurred.

### Claimant details
**Claimant details (claimant_details, text, Optional)**: Enter the relevant details about the claimant, such as their name and any other identifying information.

### Compensation details
**Compensation details (compensation_details, text, Optional)**: Describe the type and amount of compensation the claimant is seeking.

### Follow-up details
**Follow-up details (follow_up, text, Optional)**: Provide any additional information that is relevant to the claim.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
*   Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident and the compensation sought.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
*   This form is for official use only and all fields are subject to review and validation.
