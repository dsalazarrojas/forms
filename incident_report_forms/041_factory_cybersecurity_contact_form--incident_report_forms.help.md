# Factory Cybersecurity Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Cybersecurity Contact Form is designed to collect information about incidents related to cybersecurity threats or issues that have occurred within the factory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the incident date in the "Incident Date" field.
2. Enter the incident time in the "Incident Time" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
4. Write a detailed summary of the incident in the "Incident Summary" field.
5. Enter your contact information in the "Incident Reporter" field.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Incident Reporter Info" field.
7. If applicable, attach any relevant files or screenshots to the "Incident Attachment" field.
8. Provide any additional comments about the incident in the "Incident Comments" field.
9. If you have taken a screenshot of the incident, attach it to the "Incident Screenshot" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to send the report to the cybersecurity team.
11. Click the "Cancel" button to discard your report and start over.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required/optional): Describe where the incident occurred, including the location within the factory.
* **Incident Summary** (`incident_summary`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed summary of the incident, including what happened and why.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Incident Reporter Info** (`incident_reporter_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about yourself, such as your department or team.
* **Incident Attachment** (`incident_attachment`, text, required/optional): Attach any relevant files or screenshots related to the incident.
* **Incident Comments** (`incident_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Incident Screenshot** (`incident_screenshot`, text, required/optional): Attach a screenshot of the incident, if applicable.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to send the report to the cybersecurity team.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required/optional): Click this button to discard your report and start over.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the report.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or a member of the cybersecurity team for assistance.
* The cybersecurity team will review and follow up on all reports submitted through this form.
* Reports will be treated as confidential and handled according to factory security policies.
