# Ingredient Contaminant Alert Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to report incidents of contaminated ingredients to the management team for prompt action and prevention of future contamination issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each section carefully and fill in the required information. Make sure to attach any relevant photos or notes to support your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ingredient name** (`ingredient`, text): Please enter the name of the ingredient that was contaminated.
* **Severity level** (`severity_level`, select_one, High/Low): Choose the severity level of the contamination (High or Low) based on the potential impact on the product or customer.
* **Contaminant type** (`contam_type`, select_multiple, Type 1/Type 2/Type 3): Select all relevant types of contamination for the ingredient. Please note that some ingredients might be prone to multiple types of contaminants.
* **Location** (`location`, text): Describe the location where the ingredient was found to be contaminated (e.g., production line, storage, or packaging area).
* **Affected location** (`severity_location`, select_multiple, Kitchen/ Packaging/Store): Specify the areas affected by the contamination (e.g., kitchen, packaging, or store shelves).
* **Date** (`severity_date`, date): Enter the date on which the contamination was detected.
* **Time** (`severity_time`, time): Enter the time when the contamination was detected.
* **Notes** (`severity_note`, note): Add any additional comments or descriptions to support your report.
* **Attach photos** (`photos`, text): Upload any photos or images that support your report.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text): Click this button to submit your report.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your contact email address for follow-up communications.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up communications.
* **Submit** (`submit2`, text): This is another submit button, please don't worry, just ignore it!

Note: Please remember to review your report carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is provided.
