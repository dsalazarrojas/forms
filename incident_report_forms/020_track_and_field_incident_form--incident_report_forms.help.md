# Track And Field Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on incidents that have occurred during Track and Field events, ensuring accurate and efficient reporting and management of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the athlete or individual involved in the incident.
2. Indicate if the athlete was injured during the event.
3. Choose the time when the incident occurred (pre-event, during-event, or post-event).
4. Provide the location where the incident happened.
5. Enter the specific event the athlete was participating in when the incident occurred.
6. Describe the incident in detail.
7. Enter the date and time of the incident.
8. Enter the end time of the incident, if applicable.
9. Enter the location of the incident again, if necessary.
10. Provide additional details about the incident.
11. Choose the type of incident (yes or no).
12. Determine the severity of the incident (minor, moderate, or severe).
13. Choose the outcome of the incident (resolved, ongoing, or unknown).
14. Select who reported the incident (athlete, coach, or official).
15. Enter any information on how the incident was resolved.
16. Enter the outcome of the incident resolution.
17. Enter any additional information or notes on the incident.
18. Upload any images or videos related to the incident (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Injured**: Check if the athlete was injured during the event.
* **Event Occurred During Event**: Select the time when the incident occurred (pre-event, during-event, or post-event).
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident happened.
* **Athlete Participating Event**: Enter the specific event the athlete was participating in when the incident occurred.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Time End**: Enter the end time of the incident, if applicable.
* **Incident Location (Again)**: Enter the location of the incident again, if necessary.
* **Incident Details**: Enter any additional details about the incident.
* **Incident Type**: Choose if the incident occurred (yes or no).
* **Incident Severity**: Determine the severity of the incident (minor, moderate, or severe).
* **Incident Outcome**: Choose the outcome of the incident (resolved, ongoing, or unknown).
* **Incident Reported By**: Select who reported the incident (athlete, coach, or official).
* **Incident Resolution**: Enter any information on how the incident was resolved.
* **Incident Resolution Outcome**: Enter the outcome of the incident resolution.
* **Incident Note**: Enter any additional information or notes on the incident.
* **Incident Image**: Upload any images or videos related to the incident (optional).

Note: This form is designed to be completed in a clear and concise manner, and it's essential to ensure that all necessary fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays or confusion in the incident reporting and management process.
