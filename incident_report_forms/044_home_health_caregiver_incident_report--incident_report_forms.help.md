# home_health_caregiver_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track incidents reported by caregivers or agencies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of incident (Caregiver or Agency)
2. Enter the incident date and location
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident
4. Select the follow-up contact and date
5. Select the contact person and their contact method (e.g. call, email, visit)
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the incident and follow-up contact

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident (Caregiver or Agency)
  • If the incident is related to a caregiver, select "Caregiver". If it is related to an agency, select "Agency".
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred
* **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident
* **Follow up**: Select the follow-up contact method (e.g. First Contact, Second Contact, Third Contact)
* **Follow up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up contact
* **Reported by**: Select who reported the incident (Caregiver or Agency)
* **Incident Status**: Select the status of the incident (Open or Closed)
* **Resolution**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the incident resolution
* **Outcome**: Select the outcome of the incident (Good, Poor, Unknown)
* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of the agency involved in the incident
* **Agency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the agency
* **Agency Fax**: Enter the fax number of the agency
* **Agency Email**: Enter the email of the agency
* **Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the caregiver involved in the incident
* **Caregiver Phone**: Enter the phone number of the caregiver
* **Caregiver Email**: Enter the email of the caregiver
* **Follow up Contact**: Select the follow-up contact method (First Contact, Second Contact, Third Contact)
* **Follow up Person**: Enter the name of the person who made the follow-up contact
* **Follow up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up contact
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the incident and follow-up contact
* **Follow up Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the follow-up contact
* **Follow up Contacted by**: Select who made the follow-up contact (Caregiver or Agency)
* **Follow up Contact Method**: Select the method of the follow-up contact (Call, Email, Visit)
* **Follow up Person**: Enter the name of the person who made the follow-up contact
* **Notes2**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the incident and follow-up contact

**Tips**
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure accurate tracking and resolution of the incident.
* If the incident status is "Open", make sure to follow up regularly to ensure resolution.
* If the incident status is "Closed", make sure to update the resolution notes to reflect the outcome.
* Use the follow-up contact method and date fields to track the progress of the incident resolution.
