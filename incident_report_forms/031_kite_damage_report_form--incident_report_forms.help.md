# Kite Damage Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kite Damage Report Form is used to document incidents involving damaged kites. This form helps gather essential information to track and manage incidents, enabling swift action to be taken to address the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of the incident.
2. Briefly describe the issue with the kite.
3. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
4. Select the type of equipment affected.
5. Enter the extent of the damage (if applicable).
6. Upload photos of the damage (if available).
7. Choose the status of the report (e.g., submitted, reviewed, approved, rejected).
8. Provide any additional comments.
9. Enter your name and contact information.
10. Select the owner of the report.
11. Identify the person or team responsible for addressing the issue.
12. Enter any comments for the person or team reviewing the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): The date when the incident occurred.
* **Description** (`description`): Briefly describe the issue with the kite.
* **Location** (`location`): Specify where the incident occurred.
* **Equipment Affected** (`equipment_affected`): Select the type of equipment that was damaged.
* **Damage Amount** (`damage_amount`): Enter the extent of the damage (if applicable).
* **Damage Photos** (`damage_photos`): Upload photos of the damage (if available).
* **Report Status** (`report_status`): Choose the status of the report (e.g., submitted, reviewed, approved, rejected).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or details.
* **Submitter's Name** (`submitter_name`): Enter your name.
* **Submitter's Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Report Owner** (`report_owner`): Select the owner of the report.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Identify the person or team responsible for addressing the issue.
* **Comments Owner** (`comments_owner`): Enter the name of the person reviewing the report.
* **Submitter's Phone** (`submitter_phone`): Enter your phone number.

Please fill out the form completely and accurately, and do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
