# Volunteer Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteer Incident Report Form" is a tool used to collect data about incidents that occurred during volunteer work. It is used by volunteers, team members, or other individuals who report incidents that require follow-up actions or reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the volunteer's name in the **Volunteer Name** field.
2.  Select the date and time the incident occurred in the **Incident Date** and **Incident Time** fields.
3.  Enter the location where the incident occurred in the **Incident Location** field.
4.  Provide a brief description of the incident in the **Incident Description** field.
5.  Select the volunteer's role during the incident in the **Volunteer Role** field.
6.  Choose the incident type from the **Incident Type** field (e.g., Incident, Near Miss, etc.).
7.  Enter the outcome of the incident in the **Incident Outcome** field.
8.  Indicate if follow-up is required in the **Follow-up Needed** field.
9.  Enter the name of the person who submitted the report in the **Submitted By** field.
10.  Enter the name of the person who submitted the report in the **Submitter Name** field.
11.  Enter the email address of the person who submitted the report in the **Submitter Email** field.
12.  Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the report in the **Submitter Phone** field.
13.  Enter the organization where the incident occurred in the **Organization** field.
14.  Enter the name of the event where the incident occurred in the **Event Name** field.
15.  Provide a brief description of the incident type in the **Incident Type Description** field.
16.  Select the person who reported the incident from the **Incident Reporter** field (e.g., Volunteer, Team Member, etc.).
17.  Enter the title of the person who reported the incident in the **Incident Reporter Title** field.
18.  Enter the unit responsible for reporting the incident in the **Incident Reporting Unit** field.
19.  Enter any additional comments about the follow-up process in the **Follow-up Comments** field.
