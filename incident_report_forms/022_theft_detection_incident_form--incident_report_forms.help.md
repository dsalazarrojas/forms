# Theft Detection Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theft Detection Incident Form is designed to gather information about incidents of theft that have occurred within our organization. This form should only be used for genuine incidents of theft, and not for other types of incidents or reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
2. Enter the time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field.
3. Provide a brief description of what happened in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Estimate the amount of loss (if any) in the "Loss Amount" field.
5. Specify the location where the loss occurred in the "Loss Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred. This is the date when the theft took place.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident occurred. This is the time when the theft took place.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident. Try to be as detailed as possible to help us understand the situation.
* **Loss Amount** (`loss_amount`, number, optional): If applicable, estimate the amount of loss (e.g., financial or other) that occurred due to the incident.
* **Loss Location** (`loss_location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the loss occurred (e.g., office, store, or other areas within our organization).

## Tips
Please note that this form is for reporting genuine incidents of theft. If you are unsure whether the incident is theft or not, please consult with your supervisor or manager first. Also, be sure to submit only one report per incident. Duplicate reports can cause confusion and delay the resolution process.
