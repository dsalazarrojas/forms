# Cruise Line Health Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise Line Health Incident Report Form is designed to gather information about incidents that have occurred on cruise lines, ensuring that the management team is informed and can take prompt action to maintain the health and safety of passengers and crew.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form page by page, starting with the "Incident Report" page.
2. Enter the required information for each field, making sure to complete all mandatory fields.
3. If you're unsure about any field or have questions, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Incident Report (`incident_report`, `number`, required)

* Enter a brief description of the incident.

### Date Of Incident (`date_of_incident`, `date`, optional)

* Enter the date the incident occurred, if known.

### Location Of Incident (`location_of_incident`, `text`, required)

* Enter the location where the incident occurred.

### Description Of Incident (`description_of_incident`, `text`, required)

* Enter a detailed description of the incident.

### Affected Persons (`affected_persons`, `select_multiple`, required)

* Select all individuals affected by the incident.

### Follow Up Action (`follow_up_action`, `select_one`, optional)

* Indicate whether follow-up action is required or not.

### Follow Up Status (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, optional)

* Indicate the status of the follow-up action (Active or Inactive).

### Incident Reported By (`incident_reported_by`, `select_multiple`, required)

* Indicate whether the incident was reported by an individual or a group.

### Contact Information (`contact_information`, `text`, required)

* Enter contact information for further follow-up.

### Incident Reported Time (`incident_reported_time`, `time`, required)

* Enter the time the incident was reported.

### Reported By Name (`reported_by_name`, `text`, required)

* Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.

### Reported By Position (`reported_by_position`, `text`, required)

* Enter the position or role of the person reporting the incident.

### Follow Up Details (`follow_up_details`, `text`, required)

* Enter any follow-up details.

### Incident Type (`incident_type`, `select_multiple`, required)

* Select the type of incident that occurred.

### Subtype (`incident_subtype`, `select_multiple`, required)

* Select the subtype of the incident.

### Follow Up Completed (`follow_up_completed`, `select_one`, required)

* Indicate whether follow-up action is completed.

### Follow Up Completed Date (`follow_up_completed_date`, `date`, optional)

* Enter the date follow-up action was completed.

### Follow Up Completed By (`follow_up_completed_by`, `text`, required)

* Enter the person completing the follow-up action.

## Tips

* Please complete all mandatory fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If unsure about any field or have questions, refer to the form instructions or seek assistance from the management team.
* The form is designed to support incident reporting and follow-up actions effectively.
