# crowdstrike_issue_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report incidents that have occurred within the crowdstrike system. It helps to collect information about the incident, including its type, location, status, and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
2. Enter a detailed description of what happened.
3. Specify the date and time when the incident occurred.
4. Fill out the fields related to the affected user, if applicable.
5. Choose the status of the incident (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
6. Indicate the priority level of the incident (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
7. Enter any comments or details about the resolution of the incident.
8. If applicable, provide any attachments or additional information related to the incident.
9. Specify the date and time when the incident was closed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Select One: Incident Type
*   **incident_type**: This field allows you to identify the type of incident that occurred. Please select one of the provided options, either "Yes" or "No".

### Text Field: Description
*   **description**: This field is where you will provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident. Be as detailed as possible to help others understand the situation.

### Date Field: Incident Date
*   **incident_date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred.

### Text Field: Incident Location
*   **incident_location**: This field is used to specify the location of the incident.

### Text Field: Affected User
*   **affected_user**: If the incident involved a specific user, enter their name here.

### Select One: Incident Status
*   **incident_status**: Choose the status of the incident from the provided options, either "Active" or "Inactive".

### Select One: Incident Priority
*   **incident_priority**: Select the priority level of the incident, either "Yes" or "No".

### Text Field: Incident Description
*   **incident_description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.

### Text Field: Incident Comments
*   **incident_comments**: Enter any comments or details about the incident resolution.

### Text Field: Incident Attachment
*   **incident_attachment**: If you have any attachments or additional information related to the incident, enter them here.

### Text Field: Incident Resolution
*   **incident_resolution**: Enter the resolution or outcome of the incident.

### Select One: Incident Resolution Status
*   **incident_resolution_status**: Choose the status of the incident resolution from the provided options, either "Active" or "Inactive".

### Date Field: Incident Close Date
*   **incident_close_date**: Enter the date when the incident was closed.

### Time Field: Incident Close Time
*   **incident_close_time**: Enter the time when the incident was closed.

### Text Field: Affected User Email
*   **affected_user_email**: If the incident involved a specific user, enter their email here.

### Text Field: Affected User Phone
*   **affected_user_phone**: If the incident involved a specific user, enter their phone number here.

### Text Field: Affected User Department
*   **affected_user_department**: If the incident involved a specific user, enter their department here.

### Text Field: Affected User Job Title
*   **affected_user_job_title**: If the incident involved a specific user, enter their job title here.

### Text Field: Affected User Manager
*   **affected_user_manager**: If the incident involved a specific user, enter their manager's name here.

### Text Field: Affected User Manager Email
*   **affected_user_manager_email**: If the incident involved a specific user, enter their manager's email here.

### Text Field: Affected User Manager Phone
*   **affected_user_manager_phone**: If the incident involved a specific user, enter their manager's phone number here.

### Text Field: Incident Reporter
*   **incident_reporter**: Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.

### Email Field: Incident Reporter Email
*   **incident_reporter_email**: Enter your email here.

### Text Field: Incident Reporter Phone
*   **incident_reporter_phone**: Enter your phone number here.

### Text Field: Incident Resolution Comments
*   **incident_resolution_comments**: Enter any comments or details about the incident resolution.
