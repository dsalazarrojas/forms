# Safety Awareness Session Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to report and record the progress and outcome of a safety awareness session, providing essential information about the trainees, trainers, and sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Trainee ID, which refers to the unique identifier of the trainee who participated in the session.
2. Provide the Date the session took place.
3. Enter the Time of the session.
4. Enter the ID of the Trainer, who led the session.
5. List the Session Topics that were covered during the session.
6. Add any Safety Concerns raised during the session.
7. Record any Safety Practice or tips that were discussed or implemented during the session.
8. Highlight the Safety Emphasis of the session, focusing on the key aspects of safety that were highlighted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Trainee Id** (`trainee_id`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the unique identifier of the trainee who participated in the session.

### 2. **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date the safety awareness session took place.

### 3. **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false)
Enter the time the session started and ended.

### 4. **Trainer Id** (`trainer_id`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the ID of the trainer who led the session.

### 5. **Session Topics** (`session_topics`, `text`, required: false)
Describe the safety awareness topics that were covered during the session. List multiple topics if necessary.

### 6. **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, `text`, required: false)
Record any safety concerns raised during the session.

### 7. **Safety Practice** (`safety_practice`, `text`, required: false)
Note any new or revised safety practices or tips that were discussed or implemented during the session.

### 8. **Safety Emphasis** (`safety_emphasis`, `text`, required: false)
Highlight the main emphasis or focus of the session on safety, such as fire safety or emergency procedures.

## Tips
* Ensure accurate records are kept to ensure that future sessions build upon previous knowledge.
* Duplicate reports can be time-consuming and confusing; avoid filling out this form multiple times for the same session.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changing safety concerns and procedures.
