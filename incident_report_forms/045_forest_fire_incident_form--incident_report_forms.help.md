# Forest Fire Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forest Fire Incident Form is a tool used to gather essential information about forest fire incidents. The form helps responders collect details quickly and efficiently, ensuring that the information is accurate and comprehensive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the incident.
2.  Provide the date and time of the incident using the date and time fields.
3.  Specify the location where the incident occurred.
4.  Indicate if any vehicles were involved in the incident.
5.  Describe any relevant weather conditions that may have contributed to the incident.
6.  Report the number of injuries and deaths resulting from the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Forest Fire Incident
*   The first field is for entering the brief title of the incident.

### 2. Incident Date
*   This field is for entering the exact date of the incident.

### 3. Incident Time
*   This field is for entering the exact time of the incident.

### 4. Incident Location
*   Enter the specific location where the incident occurred.

### 5. Incident Vehicles
*   Select 'Yes' if vehicles were involved in the incident; otherwise, select 'No'.

### 6. Incident Weather Conditions
*   Describe any relevant weather conditions that may have contributed to the incident.

### 7. Incident Injuries
*   Report the number of people injured in the incident.

### 8. Incident Deaths
*   Report the number of people killed in the incident.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the date and time are accurate and precise.
*   Use clear and concise language when describing the incident location, weather conditions, and injuries/deaths.
*   If multiple vehicles were involved, select 'Yes' for each vehicle involved.
*   This form is designed to be a quick and efficient tool for collecting information. Please complete it as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
