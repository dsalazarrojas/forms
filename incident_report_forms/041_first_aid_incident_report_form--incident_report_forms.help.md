## First Aid Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for reporting incidents that require immediate first aid attention. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information to provide prompt and effective care for the patient. It's meant for healthcare professionals, medical staff, or first aid responders to document and analyze incidents efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Incident Report Form**: This is the main section where you'll write a brief description of the incident, including the circumstances and any notable events that led to the incident.
2. **Date Of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
3. **Time Of Incident**: Enter the time of the incident in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
4. **Incident Details**: Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident, including any injuries, symptoms, or other relevant information.
5. **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient involved in the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Report Form (1)
* **Incident Report Form (1)**: A brief description of the incident, describing the circumstances and events surrounding the incident.

### Date Of Incident (2)
* **Date Of Incident (2)**: Enter the date of the incident, using the format `MM/DD/YY`.

### Time Of Incident (3)
* **Time Of Incident (3)**: Enter the time of the incident, using the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.

### Incident Details (4)
* **Incident Details (4)**: Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident, including any injuries, symptoms, or other relevant information.

### Patient Name (5)
* **Patient Name (5)**: Enter the name of the patient involved in the incident.

## Tips
* Always fill out the form as soon as possible after the incident to ensure accurate and detailed information.
* Be thorough and clear in your descriptions to help first aid responders provide the best possible care.
* If you're unsure about any information, consult with a medical professional for guidance.
