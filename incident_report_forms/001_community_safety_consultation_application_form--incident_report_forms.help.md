# Community Safety Consultation Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your community safety concerns and provide you with guidance on the next steps to resolve them. Please ensure you have not submitted a duplicate report by checking our [reporting history](link to reporting history).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your full name, email address, phone number, and residential address.
2. Describe the safety concerns you wish to discuss, including details about the location and date of the incident.
3. Choose your preferred consultation method and time for our team to contact you.
4. Indicate the level of urgency and if you have reported this concern previously.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Full Name
Full Name (`full_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your complete legal name.

### Email Address
Email Address (`email_address`, `email`, **Required**): We will use this to send you confirmation and follow-up messages.

### Phone Number
Phone Number (`phone_number`, `text`, **Required**): Include your area code.

### Residential Address
Residential Address (`residential_address`, `text`, **Required**): Your home or organization address.

### Are you applying as an individual or on behalf of an organization?
Are you applying as an individual or on behalf of an organization? (`applicant_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the most appropriate option.

### Organization Name
Organization Name (`organization_name`, `text`, **Optional**): Leave blank if applying as an individual.

### Safety Concerns
What are your primary safety concerns? (`safety_concerns`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select all that apply.

### Location of the Concern
Location of the Concern (`incident_location`, `text`, **Required**): Be as specific as possible (e.g. cross streets).

### Date of most recent incident
Date of most recent incident (`date_of_concern`, `date`, **Optional**): Enter the date of the most recent incident, if applicable.

### Please provide a detailed description of the safety concern
Please provide a detailed description of the safety concern (`description_of_concern`, `text`, **Required**): Include any specific events or patterns observed.

### How urgent is this consultation?
How urgent is this consultation? (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select an urgent level from the options provided.

### Preferred Consultation Method
Preferred Consultation Method (`consultation_method`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose your preferred communication channel.

### Preferred days and times for consultation
Preferred days and times for consultation (`preferred_time`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your preferred days and times.

### Have you reported this concern previously?
Have you reported this concern previously? (`previous_reports`, `select_one`, **Required**): Answer "Yes" if you have reported this concern before.

### Previous Report Details
Previous report details (`previous_report_details`, `text`, **Optional**): If yes, enter any reference numbers or previous dates.

### Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when describing your safety concerns.
* If you are reporting an emergency situation, please contact 911 immediately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will respond to your submission as soon as possible.
* Please allow at least 3-5 business days for a response.
