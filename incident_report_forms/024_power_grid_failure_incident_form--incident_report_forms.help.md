# Power Grid Failure Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents related to the power grid, such as power outages, disruptions, or failures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date and time of the incident.
2. Describe the incident in detail, including its location and the areas affected.
3. Identify the power lines involved in the incident.
4. Select the type of incident (Entire Grid, Partial Grid, or None).
5. Enter the person who reported the incident.
6. Enter the date and time when the incident was reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grid Failure Date** (`grid_failure_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Describe the incident in detail, including its location and the areas affected.
* **Grid Failure Location** (`grid_failure_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Grid Failure Time** (`grid_failure_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Grid Power Line 1** (`grid_power_line_1`, text, optional): Enter the first power line involved in the incident.
* **Grid Power Line 2** (`grid_power_line_2`, text, optional): Enter the second power line involved in the incident.
* **Grid Power Line 3** (`grid_power_line_3`, text, optional): Enter the third power line involved in the incident.
* **Grid Power Line 4** (`grid_power_line_4`, text, optional): Enter the fourth power line involved in the incident.
* **Affected Areas** (`grid_failure_affected_areas`, select_one, optional): Select the type of incident (Entire Grid, Partial Grid, or None).
* **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Date Reported** (`incident_date_reported`, date, optional): Enter the date when the incident was reported.
* **Incident Time Reported** (`incident_time_reported`, time, optional): Enter the time when the incident was reported.
