# Sample Change Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and manage changes to a project or task.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the change in the "Description of Change" field.
2. Select "Agreed" or "Not Agreed" to indicate whether the change has been approved.
3. Choose whether the change request was made by "Customer" or "Contractor".
4. Enter any additional comments or notes about the change in the "Notes" field.
5. Enter the date when the change request was made in the "Change Request Date" field.
6. Enter the email of the person who requested the change in the "Email" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the person who requested the change in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Change Request Date** (5): Enter the date when the change request was made.
* **Agreed Change Request** (4): Select "Agreed" to indicate that the change has been approved. 
* **Requested By** (6): Choose between "Customer" or "Contractor" to specify who made the change request.
* **Description of Change** (3): Enter a brief description of the change.
* **Client Change Request** (2): Enter the name of the client requesting the change.
* **Notes** (8): Enter any additional comments or notes about the change.
* **Email** (9): Enter the email of the person who requested the change.
* **Phone** (10): Enter the phone number of the person who requested the change.
* **Agreed Change Request** (4): Select "Agreed" or "Not Agreed" to indicate whether the change has been approved.
* **Change Order Form 2** (7): Enter any additional comments or notes about the change.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and clear information in all fields.
