# Change Order For Landcape Business - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document changes made to a landscape project after the original contract has been signed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the date and time the change order was issued.
2.  Enter the unique identifier for the change order.
3.  Enter the job or project number as per the original contract.
4.  Enter the customer's name as per the original contract.
5.  Enter the customer's contact information, including phone and email.
6.  Enter the address of the property where work is being done.
7.  If available, enter the original contract date and amount.
8.  Enter a brief description of the change, including the type of change and why it is being made.
9.  Specify the area or feature that is being affected by the change.
10. Enter the materials originally specified for the project.
11.  Enter the new materials to be used for the change.
12.  Enter the cost of the change.
13.  Enter the total cost after the change.
14.  Indicate the impact of the change on the project timeline.
15.  If the change affects the timeline, enter the new estimated completion date.
16.  Briefly explain the reason for the change.
17.  Select the current status of the change (Approval, Approved, Rejected).
18.  Select whether the contractor is authorized to make the change (Yes, Pending, No).
19.  Select whether the customer has accepted the change (Accepted, Pending, Declined).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Change Order Date**: <i class="fab fa-lg fa-clock"></i> (1, date, required) - The date when the change order was issued.
    *   This field is the date and time when the change order was made, and it is a required field.
*   **Change Order Number**: (2, text, required) - Unique identifier for the change order.
    *   This field is used to track and reference the change order.
*   **Job or Project Number**: (3, text, required) - Landscape project identifier.
    *   Enter the job or project number as per the original contract.
*   **Customer Name**: (4, text, required) - Property owner name.
    *   Enter the customer's name as per the original contract.
*   **Customer Contact Information**: (5, text, required) - Phone and email.
    *   Enter the customer's contact information, including phone and email.
*   **Property Address**: (6, text, required) - Where work is being done.
    *   Enter the address of the property where work is being done.
*   **Original Contract Date**: (7, date, required) - When contract was signed.
    *   If available, enter the original contract date.
*   **Original Contract Amount**: (8, number, required) - Initial project cost.
    *   Enter the original contract amount.
*   **Original Work Description**: (9, text, required) - What was originally contracted.
    *   Enter a brief description of the work originally contracted.
*   **Type of Change**: (10, select_one, required) - Category of modification.
    *   Select the type of change being made.
*   **Description of Change**: (11, text, required) - What is being changed.
    *   Enter a brief description of the change being made.
*   **Area or Feature Affected**: (12, text, required) - Which part of landscape.
    *   Specify the area or feature being affected by the change.
*   **Original Materials Specified**: (13, text, required) - What was originally planned.
    *   Enter the original materials specified for the project.
*   **New Materials Specified**: (14, text, required) - What will be used instead.
    *   Enter the new materials to be used for the change.
*   **Cost of Change**: (15, number, required) - Amount for modification.
    *   Enter the cost of the change.
*   **New Contract Total**: (16, number, required) - Total after change.
    *   Enter the total cost after the change.
*   **Impact on Timeline**: (17, select_one, required) - Does change affect schedule.
    *   Indicate the impact of the change on the project timeline.
*   **New Estimated Completion Date**: (18, date, required) - If timeline affected.
    *   If the timeline is affected, enter the new estimated completion date.
*   **Reason for Change**: (19, text, required) - Why change is needed.
    *   Briefly explain the reason for the change.
*   **Approval Status**: (20, select_one, required) - Current status.
    *   Select the current status of the change (Approval, Approved, Rejected).
*   **Contractor Authorization**: (21, select_one, required) - Authorized by contractor.
    *   Select whether the contractor is authorized to make the change (Yes, Pending, No).
*   **Customer Acceptance**: (22, select_one, required) - Customer agrees to change.
    *   Select whether the customer has accepted the change (Accepted, Pending, Declined).
## Tips

*   Be accurate and detailed when filling out this form.
*   Make sure to update the form as soon as a change is made to the project.
*   This form is for documentation and communication purposes only.
