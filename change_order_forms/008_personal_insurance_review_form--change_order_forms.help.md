# Personal Insurance Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Insurance Review Form is used to gather information about an individual's or entity's insurance policies. This form helps to update and verify the accuracy of existing insurance records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the insurance company that issued the policy.
2. Enter the policy name and select the policy type (e.g., automobile, health, etc.).
3. Enter the policy's effective and end dates.
4. Choose the policy's status (active, inactive, or cancelled).
5. Select the premium frequency (monthly, quarterly, semi-annually).
6. Enter the premium amount.
7. Choose the payment method (cash, check, credit).
8. Enter the policy holder's name, contact number, email, address, city, state, postal code, and country.
9. Provide any additional notes about the policy holder.
10. Select the relationship between the policy holder and the insured entity (self, spouse, business).
11. Choose how the policy holder holds the policy (full, joint, co-owners).
12. Select the policy holder's employment status (employed, retired, disabled, unemployed).
13. Enter the policy holder's annual income.
14. Enter the policy holder's net worth.
15. Select the type of insurance policy holder has (health, life, auto, home, business).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`): This field is used to select the insurance company that issued the policy.
* **Policy Name** (`policy_name`): Enter the name of the policy.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`): Select the type of policy (e.g., automobile, health, etc.).
* **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`): Enter the date the policy became effective.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`): Enter the date the policy will end.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`): Choose the status of the policy (active, inactive, cancelled).
* **Premium Frequency** (`premium_frequency`): Select how often the premium is paid (monthly, quarterly, semi-annually).
* **Premium Amount** (`premium_amount`): Enter the amount of the premium.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose the method of payment (cash, check, credit).
* **Policy Holder Name** (`policy_holder_name`): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Contact Number** (`policy_holder_contact_number`): Enter the contact number of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Email** (`policy_holder_email`): Enter the email of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Address** (`policy_holder_address`): Enter the address of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder City** (`policy_holder_city`): Enter the city of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder State** (`policy_holder_state`): Enter the state of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Postal Code** (`policy_holder_postal_code`): Enter the postal code of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Country** (`policy_holder_country`): Enter the country of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Notes** (`policy_holder_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the policy holder.
* **Relationship** (`policy_holder_relationship`): Select the relationship between the policy holder and the insured entity (self, spouse, business).
* **Policy Holder Ownership** (`policy_holder_ownership`): Choose how the policy holder holds the policy (full, joint, co-owners).
* **Policy Holder Employment** (`policy_holder_employment`): Select the policy holder's employment status (employed, retired, disabled, unemployed).
* **Annual Income** (`policy_holder_annual_income`): Enter the annual income of the policy holder.
* **Net Worth** (`policy_holder_net_worth`): Enter the net worth of the policy holder.
* **Insurances** (`policy_holder_insurances`): Select the type of insurance policy holder has (health, life, auto, home, business).

Tips:

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authority.
* This form is not for submitting new insurance policies, but for reviewing existing ones.
