# Automotive Enrollment And Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process changes and updates to an account's automotive enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of request you are making by choosing from the "Type of Request" field. This field is required and helps us process your change efficiently.
2. Enter the date of your request in the "Request Date" field. This field is required and helps us track the timeline of your change.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Customer Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields. These fields are required for us to reach out to you for confirmation and other updates.
4. Enter your customer or account ID if you have one. This is optional but helps us look up your information more easily.
5. Enter your vehicle information in the "Vehicle Year", "Vehicle Make", "Vehicle Model", and "License Plate Number" fields. These fields are required to identify your vehicle and process the change.
6. Describe the changes you are requesting in the "Describe Changes Requested" field. This field is required to help us understand what you are looking for.
7. Select the "Coverage or Program Type" field to indicate which type of coverage you are enrolling or changing. This field is required and helps us process your change efficiently.
8. Choose a "Coverage Level" option that best suits your needs.
9. Indicate whether you have attached supporting documents, such as registration and proof of ownership, by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Supporting Documents Attached" field.
10. Select "Yes, I authorize" to confirm that you are authorizing the changes to your account.
11. Choose your preferred contact method for confirmation and updates by selecting from the "Preferred Contact Method for Confirmation" field.
12. Enter the date when you would like the change to take effect in the "Change Effective Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Request Type --**: Select the type of change you are requesting.
* **Type of Request**: Choose from the following options:
	+ New Enrollment
	+ Update Information
	+ Change Coverage
	+ Add Vehicle
	+ Remove Vehicle
	+ Transfer Ownership
* **Request Date**: Enter the date of your request.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer or Account ID**: Enter your customer or account ID if you have one.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle VIN**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of your vehicle.
* **License Plate Number**: Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
* **Describe Changes Requested**: Describe the changes you are requesting in this field.
* **Coverage or Program Type**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Basic
	+ Premium
	+ Comprehensive
	+ Fleet Program
	+ Warranty
	+ Extended Coverage
* **Coverage Level**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Single Vehicle
	+ Multi-Vehicle
	+ Fleet
	+ Family
* **Supporting Documents Attached**: Indicate whether you have attached supporting documents by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **I authorize these changes to my account**: Select "Yes, I authorize" to confirm that you are authorizing the changes.
* **Preferred Contact Method for Confirmation**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Text Message
	+ Mail
* **Change Effective Date**: Enter the date when you would like the change to take effect.
