# Center And Shift Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to submit requests to change their center or shift assignment. Please use this form to request changes to your schedule or center assignment due to valid reasons such as personal or family reasons, medical reasons, transport or commute issues, or education/school related reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your employee information, including your full name, employee ID, and email address.
2. Provide your current center or department assignment and the shift you are currently working on.
3. Select the type of change you wish to request, which could be a change to the center or department, a shift change, or both.
4. If applicable, provide the name of the new center and shift you wish to be assigned to.
5. Select the effective date of the change and whether the change is permanent or temporary.
6. If the change is temporary, provide the end date of the change.
7. Choose a reason category for your request, which can be personal or family, medical, transport or commute, education or school, or relocation.
8. Provide a detailed explanation for your reason, including any relevant context or supporting information.
9. Acknowledge that your request is subject to approval and business needs.
10. Confirm your request submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your unique employee identification number.
* **Employee Email Address** (`employee_email`): Enter your email address as it appears in your personnel records.
* **Current Center or Department** (`current_center_dept`): Enter the name of your current center or department.
* **Current Shift** (`current_shift_assignment`): Select the shift you are currently assigned to, which could be Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Night Shift.
* **Effective Date of Change** (`change_effective_date`): Select the date when you would like the change to take effect.
* **Type of Change Request** (`request_type`): Select the type of change you wish to request, which could be Center / Department change, Shift change, or Both Center and Shift change.
* **Requested New Center** (`requested_new_center`): If you selected "Center / Department Change" or "Both Center and Shift Change", enter the name of the center or department you wish to be assigned to.
* **Requested New Shift** (`requested_new_shift`): If you selected "Shift change" or "Both Center and Shift Change", select the new shift you would like to be assigned to.
* **Change Permanency** (`change_permanency`): Select whether the change is permanent or temporary.
* **End Date of Temporary Change** (`temporary_end_date`): If the change is temporary, enter the date when you will return to your original schedule.
* **Reason Category** (`reason_category`): Select the primary reason for your request, which can be Personal / Family, Medical, Transport / Commute, Education / School, or Relocation.
* **Detailed Reason for Request** (`detailed_reason_explanation`): Provide a detailed explanation for your reason, including any relevant context or supporting information.
* **Acknowledgement of Policy** (`request_policy_agreement`): Select that you understand that your request is subject to approval and business needs.
* **Date of Submission** (`request_submission_date`): Confirm the date when you submit your request.
