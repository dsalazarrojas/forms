# Contract Change Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track modifications to the original contract terms, ensuring that all changes are properly recorded and agreed upon by all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the contractor's name and address in the "Contractor Name" and "Contractor Address" fields.
2. Enter the contractor's phone number and email in the "Contractor Phone" and "Contractor Email" fields.
3. Select the date of the change order in the "Change Order Date" field.
4. Enter the sequential number of the change order in the "Change Order Number" field.
5. Enter the original contract number and date in the "Original Contract Number" and "Original Contract Date" fields.
6. Enter the name of the project in the "Project Name" field.
7. Enter the location of the project in the "Project Location" field.
8. Enter a detailed description of the change in the "Change Description" field.
9. Select the reason for the change from the drop-down menu in the "Reason for Change" field.
10. Enter the work affected by the change in the "Work Affected" field.
11. Enter the original contract amount in the "Original Contract Amount" field.
12. Enter the total of previous change orders in the "Previous Change Orders Total" field.
13. Enter the cost of this change order in the "This Change Order Amount" field.
14. Enter the updated total contract amount in the "New Contract Total" field.
15. Enter the number of days added or subtracted from the original completion date in the "Time Impact" field.
16. Enter the original completion date in the "Original Completion Date" field.
17. Enter the revised completion date in the "Revised Completion Date" field.
18. (Optional) Enter the cost of materials, labor, and equipment in the "Materials Cost", "Labor Cost", and "Equipment Cost" fields.
19. (Optional) Enter the markup percentage for overhead and profit in the "Overhead and Profit" field.
20. Sign the form as the contractor and owner, respectively, by typing their name in the "Contractor Signature" and "Owner Signature" fields.
21. Date the signatures in the "Contractor Signature Date" and "Owner Signature Date" fields.
22. (Optional) Enter the architect's name in the "Architect Approval" field.
23. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contractor Name**: Enter the name of the company or individual performing the work.
* **Contractor Address**: Enter the full address of the company or individual performing the work.
* **Contractor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the company or individual performing the work.
* **Contractor Email**: Enter the email address of the company or individual performing the work.
* **Change Order Date**: Enter the date of the change order.
* **Change Order Number**: Enter the sequential number of the change order.
* **Original Contract Number**: Enter the contract reference number.
* **Original Contract Date**: Enter the date of the original contract.
* **Project Name**: Enter the title of the project.
* **Project Location**: Enter the job site address.
* **Change Description**: Enter a detailed description of the change.
* **Reason for Change**: Select the reason for the change from the drop-down menu.
* **Work Affected**: Enter the work affected by the change.
* **Original Contract Amount**: Enter the original price of the contract.
* **Previous Change Orders Total**: Enter the sum of previous changes.
* **This Change Order Amount**: Enter the cost of this change.
* **New Contract Total**: Enter the updated total contract amount.
* **Time Impact**: (Optional) Enter the number of days added or subtracted from the original completion date.
* **Original Completion Date**: Enter the original completion date.
* **Revised Completion Date**: (Optional) Enter the new completion date.
* **Materials Cost**: (Optional) Enter the cost of materials.
* **Labor Cost**: (Optional) Enter the cost of labor.
* **Equipment Cost**: (Optional) Enter the cost of equipment.
* **Overhead and Profit**: (Optional) Enter the markup percentage for overhead and profit.
* **Contractor Signature**: Sign the form as the contractor.
* **Contractor Signature Date**: Date the signature.
* **Owner Signature**: Sign the form as the owner.
* **Owner Signature Date**: Date the signature.
* **Architect Approval**: (Optional) Enter the architect's name.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other information.
