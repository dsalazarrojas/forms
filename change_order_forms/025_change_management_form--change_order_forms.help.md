# Change Management Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Change Management Form is used to document and track changes in your organization. This form helps to ensure that changes are properly assessed, approved, and implemented. It captures key information about the change, such as the reason for the change, scope, and impact, to facilitate smooth execution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested information for each field.
2. For fields with a date format, enter dates in YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. For fields with a selection option, choose one of the provided options.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Change Request Date** (`change_request_date`): Enter the date when the change was submitted.
* **Change ID Number** (`change_id`): Enter a unique identifier for the change, if applicable.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`): Enter the name of the person requesting the change.
* **Requester Department** (`requester_department`): Enter the department of the person requesting the change (optional).
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`): Enter the email address of the person requesting the change.
* **Requester Phone** (`requester_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the change (optional).
* **Change Title** (`change_title`): Enter a brief title of the change.
* **Detailed Description of Change** (`change_description`): Provide a comprehensive description of the change, including the why and how.
* **Reason for Change** (`change_reason`): Explain why the change is needed.
* **Type of Change** (`change_type`): Choose the category of change (Emergency, Urgent, Standard, Minor, or Major).
* **Affected Systems or Services** (`affected_systems`): List the systems or services impacted by the change.
* **Scope of Change** (`change_scope`): Specify how extensive the change is (Single System, Multiple Systems, Department-wide, Organization-wide, or External Impact).
* **Proposed Implementation Date** (`implementation_date`): Enter the date when the change should occur.
* **Implementation Time Window** (`implementation_window`): Specify the time period for implementation (optional).
* **Implementation Steps** (`implementation_steps`): Detail the steps to implement the change.
* **Rollback Plan** (`rollback_plan`): If the change is rolled back, describe the steps to reverse it (optional).
* **Risks and Potential Impact** (`risks_identified`): Identify any potential risks or impacts.
* **Risk Mitigation Strategy** (`mitigation_strategy`): Explain how the risks will be managed.
* **Testing Completed** (`testing_completed`): Indicate if the change has been tested.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`): Select the current approval status (Pending Review, Approved, Conditional Approval, Rejected, or Awaiting Signatures).
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`): Enter the name of the manager approving the change (optional).
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): Enter the date of approval (optional).
* **Communication Plan** (`communication_plan`): Outline how stakeholders will be notified (optional).
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`): Include any other relevant information.

By following these guidelines, you will be able to complete the Change Management Form accurately and effectively.
