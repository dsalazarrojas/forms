# Electric Service Load Change Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request changes to an electric service load. This form should be completed to request a change to the electric service load for a specific customer. Please ensure that this request is not a duplicate and that you have checked if any similar requests have been made recently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name as it appears on their account.
2. Enter the customer's address as it appears on their account.
3. Select the type of service being requested.
4. Choose the date of the request.
5. Choose the time of the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account.

### Customer Address
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter the customer's address as it appears on their account.

### Service Type
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service that the customer is requesting.

### Request Date
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the request.

### Request Time
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the customer is aware that this form is for a specific service change request and that they understand the impact of the change.
* If the customer has any questions or concerns, please direct them to contact your team.
* Before submitting the form, review it for completeness and accuracy to avoid any delays in processing the request.
