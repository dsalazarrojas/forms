# Engineering Change Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Engineering Change Order Form is used to document changes made to the engineering process. It is a critical tool for tracking and managing changes to ensure that all necessary information is captured and that the change is approved before being implemented.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of change from the options provided.
2. Enter a detailed description of the change.
3. Provide the reason for the change.
4. Select the impact of the change.
5. Enter the submitter's name, email, and department.
6. Enter the reviewer's name, email, and department.
7. Enter the reviewer's comments and select their approvals.
8. Enter the approver's name, email, and department.
9. Select the current status of the change.
10. Enter the change number.
11. Select the type of change.
12. Enter the change version.
13. (Optional) Enter the submitted date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit** (`submit_page`, `select_one`): Select the option that best describes the type of submission.
* **Change Description** (`change_description`, `text`): Enter a detailed description of the change, including any necessary context or background information.
* **Change Reason** (`change_reason`, `text`): Enter the reason why the change was made.
* **Change Impact** (`change_impact`, `select_one`): Select the impact of the change (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`): Enter the name of the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, `text`): Enter the department of the submitter.
* **Change Date** (`change_date`, `date`): Enter the date the change was made.
* **Change Time** (`change_time`, `time`): Enter the time the change was made.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, `text`): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, `email`): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Department** (`reviewer_department`, `text`): Enter the department of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`): Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Approvals** (`reviewer_approvals`, `select_multiple`): Select the approvals from the options provided.
* **Approver** (`approver`, `text`): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, `email`): Enter the email address of the approver.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, `text`): Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **Approver Department** (`approver_department`, `text`): Enter the department of the approver.
* **Change Status** (`change_status`, `select_one`): Select the current status of the change (New, In Progress, Approved, or Cancelled).
* **Change Number** (`change_number`, `text`): Enter the number of the change.
* **Change Type** (`change_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of change (Design, Specification, or Process).
* **Change Version** (`change_version`, `text`): Enter the version of the change.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`): Enter the date the change was submitted (optional).
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, `time`): Enter the time the change was submitted (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields.
* Double-check that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the engineering department.
