# replacement diploma request form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a replacement diploma in case of loss, damage, or non-receipt of the original diploma. It is intended for genuine requests and should not be used for duplicate or test purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Replacement Request section with a clear and concise description of why you are requesting a replacement diploma.
2. If you need to ship the replacement diploma, fill out the Shipping Info section with your shipping details.
3. Indicate whether you would like to pay for the replacement fee.
4. Select the fee type that applies to your situation.
5. Enter the fee amount (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Replacement Request
* **Replacement Request (text)**: Describe the reason you are requesting a replacement diploma. Please be concise and clear about why you need a replacement.

### Shipping Info
* **Shipping Info (text)**: If you would like to have your replacement diploma shipped to you, fill out this section with your shipping details.
* **Name on Shipping Label (text)**: Enter the name of the person to whom the diploma should be shipped.
* **Street (text)**: Enter the street address where the diploma should be shipped.
* **City (text)**: Enter the city where the diploma should be shipped.
* **State (select one)**: Choose 'Yes' if you would like to have your diploma shipped to a different state or country.
* **Zip/Postal Code (text)**: Enter the zip or postal code where the diploma should be shipped.

### Payment and Fee
* **Payment Option (select one)**: Indicate whether you would like to pay for the replacement fee.
* **Replacement Fee (select multiple)**: Select the fee type that applies to your situation. Please note that not all options may be applicable in all cases.
* **Fee Amount (number)**: Enter the fee amount (if applicable). Please ensure the amount is accurate and reflects the correct total fee.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your shipping information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* Please note that shipping costs and fees may apply.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
