# Tool Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tool Order Form is used to collect shipping details, contact information, and tool preferences for ordering purposes. This form helps us efficiently process orders and ensures that we have the necessary information to deliver the tools correctly and on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the shipping details (section 1).
2. Provide your contact details (section 2).
3. Indicate if you have any damaged tools (section 3).
4. Choose your preferred tool type (section 4).
5. Enter your shipping address (section 5).
6. Add any additional order notes (section 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shipping_details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter your shipping details, such as the shipping address and any special instructions.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact name, phone number, and email address.
* **damaged_tool_number** (`damaged_tool_number`, text, optional): If you have any damaged tools, enter the tool number(s) so we can take care of them separately.
* **preferred_tool_type** (`preferred_tool_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred tool type from the available options.
* **shipping_address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
* **order_notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order, such as specific delivery instructions or special requests.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to help us process your order efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Double-check your contact details and shipping address for accuracy.
* If you have damaged tools, please indicate the tool number(s) so we can handle them separately.
