# Power Of Attorney Change Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request changes to a power of attorney agreement. It captures the relevant information of the person making the request, the change details, and the approvers involved in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the **Name** and **Email** fields, respectively.
2. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.
3. Enter the date of the change request in the **Change Date** field.
4. State the reason for the change in the **Reason** field.
5. Enter the name, phone number, and email address of the person approving the change in the **Approver Name**, **Approver Phone**, and **Approver Email** fields, respectively.
6. Enter any additional review notes in the **Review Note** field.
7. Enter your signature in the **Form Signature** field.
8. If there are multiple approvers, enter their names, phone numbers, and email addresses in the **Approver Name 2**, **Approver Phone 2**, and **Approver Email 2** fields, respectively.
9. Enter the date the approver approved the change in the **Date Approved 2** field.
10. Select the status of the request in the **Status** dropdown menu.
11. Enter any additional approver 2 notes in the **Approver Note 2** field.
12. Select the reviewer who will review the request in the **Reviewer** dropdown menu.
13. Enter the date the reviewer has reviewed the request in the **Date Reviewed** field.
14. Enter any additional reviewer notes in the **Review Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: Enter a brief title for the form.
* **Name (Name)**: Enter the name of the person making the request.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the person making the request.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person making the request.
* **Change Date (Change Date)**: Enter the date of the change request.
* **Reason (Reason)**: Enter the reason for the change request.
* **Approver Name (Approver Name)**: Enter the name of the person approving the change.
* **Approver Phone (Approver Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person approving the change.
* **Approver Email (Approver Email)**: Enter the email address of the person approving the change.
* **Review Note (Review Note)**: Enter any additional review notes.
* **Form Signature (Form Signature)**: Enter your signature.
* **Approver Name 2 (Approver Name 2)**: If there are multiple approvers, enter the name of the second approver.
* **Approver Phone 2 (Approver Phone 2)**: If there are multiple approvers, enter the phone number of the second approver.
* **Approver Email 2 (Approver Email 2)**: If there are multiple approvers, enter the email address of the second approver.
* **Approver Note 2 (Approver Note 2)**: Enter any additional notes from the second approver.
* **Date Approved 2 (Date Approved 2)**: If there are multiple approvers, enter the date the second approver approved the change.
* **Status**: Select the status of the request (approved, approved with conditions, rejected, pending, in progress, completed, closed, awaiting).
* **Status Note (Status Note)**: Enter any additional notes for the status.
* **Reviewer (Reviewer)**: Select the reviewer who will review the request.
* **Date Reviewed (Date Reviewed)**: Enter the date the reviewer reviewed the request.
* **Review Note (Review Note)**: Enter any additional reviewer notes.
* **Approver Name 2 (Approver Name 2)**: If there are multiple approvers, enter the name of the second approver.
* **Approver Phone 2 (Approver Phone 2)**: If there are multiple approvers, enter the phone number of the second approver.
* **Approver Email 2 (Approver Email 2)**: If there are multiple approvers, enter the email address of the second approver.
* **Approver Note 2 (Approver Note 2)**: If there are multiple approvers, enter any additional notes from the second approver.
* **Date Approved 2 (Date Approved 2)**: If there are multiple approvers, enter the date the second approver approved the change.

Note: This form has multiple approvers fields which can be confusing. It is recommended to fill in the required fields first and then fill in the additional approver fields only if necessary. 

# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all the necessary information is filled in.
* If there are any issues with the form, contact your supervisor or IT department for assistance.
