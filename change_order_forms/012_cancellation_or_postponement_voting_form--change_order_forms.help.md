# Cancellation Or Postponement Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants regarding the cancellation or postponement of an event. The goal is to understand their preferences and concerns to make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name, email address, and event title in the corresponding fields. These details will be used to verify your identity and confirm your registration.
2. Choose your preferred action: Postpone to a later date, Cancel the event entirely, or Keep the current date if possible.
3. If you choose to postpone, select the timeframe you prefer for the new event date.
4. Provide a brief explanation for your choice, if you'd like.
5. If the event cannot proceed, select your preferred refund option: Full Refund, Credit for a Future Event, or a Donation to the Organization.
6. Choose how you'd like to receive updates on the decision and any further communication.
7. Agree or disagree with the final decision made by the committee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as registered for the event.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address for confirmation of your vote.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the full title of the event in question.
* **Ticket or Registration Number** (`ticket_number`): Enter the number found in your original confirmation email.
* **Preferred Action** (`preferred_action`): Choose what should be done with the event: Postpone to a later date, Cancel the event entirely, or Keep the current date if possible.
* **Preferred Postponement Timeframe** (`postponement_timeframe`): If you choose to postpone, select the timeframe for the new event date: 1-3 months later, 4-6 months later, Next Year, or Not interested in postponement.
* **Reason for your choice** (`vote_reason`): Briefly explain your preference. (Optional)
* **Refund Preferences** (`refund_preferences_section`): If the event cannot proceed, select how you'd like your registration fee handled: Full Refund, Credit for a Future Event, or a Donation to the Organization. (Optional)
* **If Postponed, will you still attend?** (`future_attendance_interest`): Choose if you'd like to attend if the event is postponed: Yes, No, or Undecided.
* **If Cancelled, preferred refund or credit** (`cancelled_refund_preference`): If the event is cancelled, select how you'd like your registration fee handled: Full Refund, Credit for a Future Event, or a Donation to the Organization.
* **Impact of the situation on your ability to attend** (`attendance_impact`): Describe any barriers you face for the current date. (Optional)
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_section`): Select how you'd like to be kept in touch: Email, SMS, or Phone Call. (Optional)
* **How would you like to receive updates** (`update_method`): Choose how you'd like to receive updates: Email, SMS, or Phone Call.
* **I agree to the final decision made by the committee** (`final_decision_agreement`): Agree or disagree with the final decision. 
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): This field is automatically filled in and not editable. 

Note: Please fill in all required fields. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
