# Language Camp Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and experience data from participants of language camps.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the summer camps you have attended from the options provided in the "Summer Camp Experience" field.
2. Provide your participant name, email, and contact number in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose your age group from the options provided in the "Age Group" field.
4. Choose the session type you attended from the options provided in the "Session Type" field.
5. Rate your experience with the session by providing a number in the "Participant Rating" field.
6. If you have any additional feedback, please provide it in the "Feedback" field.
7. Select the languages you are interested in from the options provided in the "Participant Language" field.
8. Check if you were staff or a participant during the session in the "Staff Attended" and "Session Attended" fields.
9. If you have any additional information, please provide it in the "Additional Info" field.

## Tips

* Please answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Info" field.
* Please note that this form will be reviewed and used to improve future language camps.
