# Camp Games Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from camp participants about their experience with the games played during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Participant Name" field and enter your name, if you choose to do so. This field is optional.
2. Select the "Game Name" field and enter the name of the game you are reviewing. This field is required.
3. Choose an answer for each question by selecting the corresponding option.
   *   **Enjoyment Level** (Required): Rate how much you enjoyed the game. You have 4 options: Loved It, Liked It, Neutral, or Did Not Like It.
   *   **Difficulty Rating** (Optional): Rate the difficulty level of the game. You have 3 options: Too Easy, Appropriate, or Too Hard.
   *   **Fairness Rating** (Optional): Rate how fair the game was for all players. You have 3 options: True, Somewhat, or False.
   *   **Suitable Age Group** (Optional): Select the age group that this game is best suited for. You have 4 options: Under 8, 8-12, 13-17, or 18+.
   *   **Overall Comment** (Optional): Enter any final thoughts about the game.
4. Choose whether you would play the game again by selecting one of the options: True, Maybe, or False.
5. Finally, enter any safety concerns you noticed during play or suggestions for improvement for the game in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name if you choose to do so.
*   **Game Name** (`game_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of the game you are reviewing.
*   **Enjoyment Level** (`enjoyment_level`, select_one, Required): Rate how much you enjoyed the game.
*   **Difficulty Rating** (`difficulty_rating`, select_one, Optional): Rate the difficulty level of the game.
*   **Fairness Rating** (`fairness_rating`, select_one, Optional): Rate how fair the game was for all players.
*   **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, text, Optional): Enter ideas to improve game rules or setup.
*   **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, text, Optional): Enter any safety issues noticed during play.
*   **Suitable Age Group** (`suitable_age_group`, select_one, Optional): Select the age group that this game is best suited for.
*   **Overall Comment** (`overall_comment`, text, Optional): Enter final thoughts about the game.
*   **Would Play Again** (`would_play_again`, select_one, Required): Indicate interest in replaying the game.
