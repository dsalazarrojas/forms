# Summer Camp Participation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and staff members about their summer camp experiences, demographics, and contact information. The goal is to understand camp participation patterns and gather feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **How many campers will participate**: This field allows you to indicate the number of people who will attend the summer camp. Please enter a whole number, and you can also leave this field blank if you're unsure.
2. **What is your age range?**: Select all that apply from the dropdown menu to indicate your age range. You can choose from 18-25 or 26-35.
3. **Gender**: Choose your gender from the dropdown menu.
4. **Number of days attended**: If you're a participant, please enter the number of days you plan to attend the summer camp. If you're a staff member, you can skip this field.
5. **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, or phone number so we can get in touch with you.
6. **What is your interest?**: Select all that apply from the options below to indicate which activities you're interested in: 
   * Music
   * Dance
   * Art
   * Sports
7. **Additional comments**: Share any additional thoughts, feedback, or suggestions you have about the summer camp.
8. **How can we contact you?**: Let us know your preferred contact method: 
   * Email
   * Phone
   * Other
9. **Contact email/phone**: If you selected "Email" or "Phone" as your preferred contact method, enter your email or phone number here.
10. **Form completion**: Enter the number of pages you've completed in the form.
11. **Additional comments**: Leave any additional comments or feedback about the form or the summer camp.
