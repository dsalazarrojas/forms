# Camp Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience at the summer camp program. It's an optional form, and you can skip it if you want to provide anonymous feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your participant name (if you want to be associated with your feedback) or leave it blank for anonymous feedback.
2. Indicate the name of the camp program you are evaluating.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
4. Rate the quality of the activities provided.
5. Rate the support you received from staff.
6. You can skip this question if you didn't participate in the program or didn't have any issues with facilities.
7. Answer whether you achieved the learning outcomes you expected.
8. If you have any suggestions for improvement, feel free to share them with us.
9. Indicate how likely you are to recommend this program to others.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: This is a text field where you can provide your name or leave it blank for anonymous feedback.
* **Program Name (program_name)**: Please enter the name of the camp program you're evaluating.
* **Overall Satisfaction (overall_satisfaction)**: Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale from 1-5, where:
  - Very Satisfied: Excellent experience.
  - Satisfied: Good experience.
  - Neutral: Average experience.
  - Dissatisfied: Poor experience.
  - Very Dissatisfied: Extremely poor experience.
* **Activities Rating (activities_rating)**: Rate the quality of the activities provided on a scale from 1-4, where:
  - Excellent: Very engaging and enjoyable.
  - Good: Satisfactory activities.
  - Fair: Average activities.
  - Poor: Unacceptable activities.
* **Staff Support Rating (staff_support_rating)**: Rate the quality of support from staff on a scale from 1-4, where:
  - Excellent: Very helpful and supportive staff.
  - Good: Satisfactory support.
  - Fair: Average support.
  - Poor: Unacceptable support.
* **Facilities Rating (facilities_rating)**: You can skip this question if you didn't have any issues with facilities. Rate the condition of facilities and equipment on a scale from 1-4, where:
  - Excellent: Very clean and well-maintained facilities.
  - Good: Satisfactory facilities.
  - Fair: Average facilities.
  - Poor: Unacceptable facilities.
* **Learning Outcomes (learning_outcomes)**: Answer whether you achieved the learning outcomes you expected on a scale from 1-3, where:
  - True: You achieved the learning outcomes you expected.
  - Somewhat: You partially achieved the learning outcomes you expected.
  - False: You did not achieve the learning outcomes you expected.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (suggestions_for_improvement)**: Provide any concrete suggestions you have for improving future camps.
* **Likelihood to Recommend (likelihood_to_recommend)**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend this program to others on a scale from 1-4, where:
  - Very Likely: Definitely recommend.
  - Likely: Probably recommend.
  - Unlikely: Not likely.
  - Very Unlikely: Not at all likely.
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments)**: Provide any additional feedback you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
- We appreciate your time and input in helping us improve our summer camp services.
