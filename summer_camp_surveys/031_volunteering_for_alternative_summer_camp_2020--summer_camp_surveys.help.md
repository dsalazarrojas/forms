# Volunteering For Alternative Summer Camp 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for the Alternative Summer Camp 2020. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Summer Camp Survey page to provide general information.
2. On the Contact Person page, enter the name of the contact person.
3. Enter the phone number of the contact person on the Phone Number page.
4. Enter the email of the contact person on the Email page.
5. On the Volunteer Experience page, describe your previous volunteer experience.
6. On the Volunteer Position page, specify the position you are interested in volunteering for.
7. On the Volunteer Role page, specify the role you are interested in taking on during the camp.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Summer Camp Survey** (`summer_camp_survey`, text, optional): This page is where you can provide any relevant information about your interest in volunteering for the Alternative Summer Camp 2020.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person who is submitting this form.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person. Please format it as you would in a standard US number (###-#####-####).
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person. This will be used for further communication.
- **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, text, optional): Describe any previous volunteer experience you have, including relevant skills or roles.
- **Volunteer Position** (`volunteer_position`, text, optional): Select the position you are interested in volunteering for, or specify a position that is not listed.
- **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, text, optional): Select the role you are interested in taking on during the camp, or specify a role that is not listed.
