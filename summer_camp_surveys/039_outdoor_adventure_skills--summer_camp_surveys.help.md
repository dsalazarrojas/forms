# Outdoor Adventure Skills - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants for a summer adventure skills survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your contact number to enable us to reach out to you in case of any updates or queries.
3. If you're not yet 18 years old, please skip the "Date of Birth" field as it's not required.
4. Select the adventure skills you have experience with from the options provided for "Rock climbing", "Water sports", and "Land sports".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name (1)**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name (2)**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email (3)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates or queries.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number (4)**: Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you for any updates or queries.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth (5)**: If you're 18 or older, please enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD". This field is not required for participants under 18.

### Rock climbing
* **Rock climbing (6)**: Please select the adventure skill "Climbing" if you have experience with rock climbing. If not, select one of the other options that apply to you.

### Water sports
* **Water sports (7)**: Please select the adventure skill "Kayaking" or "Rafting" if you have experience with water sports. Select only one option.

### Land sports
* **Land sports (8)**: Please select the adventure skill "Hiking", "Cycling", "Geocaching", or "Orienteering" that applies to you. Select only one option.

### Notes
* **Notes (9)**: Enter any additional information or comments about your adventure skills that you'd like to share with us. This is optional and not required.

### Tips
* Ensure that you're filling out the form for the correct purpose and do not duplicate your entry.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
