# Virtual Summer Camp Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a virtual summer camp, including its details and contact person. This information will help us understand the camp's specifics and facilitate communication with the designated contact person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Virtual Summer Camp" field with the name of the virtual summer camp you're interested in.
2.  Enter the expected number of campers who will be attending the camp.
3.  Choose the start date of the camp from the available options.
4.  Select whether the camp starts at an early start time or not.
5.  Determine if the camp ends at an early end time or not.
6.  Identify the contact person for the camp, selecting all relevant options.
7.  Enter the contact person's phone number.
8.  Enter the contact person's email address.
9.  Specify the venue of the virtual summer camp.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Virtual Summer Camp**: Enter the name of the virtual summer camp you're interested in.
* **How many campers are expected to attend?**: Enter the number of campers who are expected to attend the virtual summer camp. This information helps us understand the camp's size and plan accordingly.
* **Select the start date of the camp**:
	+ Choose "Yes" if the camp starts on the selected date.
* **Select the start time of the camp**:
	+ Choose "Yes" if the camp starts at an early start time.
* **Select the end time of the camp**:
	+ Choose "Yes" if the camp ends at an early end time.
* **Who will be the contact person for the camp?**:
	+ Select all relevant contact persons.
* **Who will be the contact person for the camp?**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
*   **Contact person email:** Enter the contact person's email address.
*  **What is the venue of the virtual summer camp?**: Enter the location where the virtual summer camp will take place.
