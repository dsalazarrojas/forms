## Purpose
This form is for parents and guardians of children attending a summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing the required information.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Camp Info
* **Camp Info (1)**: 
	+ Enter the name of the camp your child will be attending.
* **Contact Info (2)**: 
	+ Enter your personal contact details (name, email, phone number) so we can reach out to you with updates and important information.
* **Medical Info (3)**: 
	+ Provide any relevant medical information or allergies we should be aware of.
* **Emergency Contact (4)**: 
	+ Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Info (5)**: 
	+ Provide more detailed contact information for the emergency contact person, including their phone number and relationship to your child.
* **Camp Details (6)**: 
	+ Enter any additional details about the camp your child will be attending, such as the address or any other relevant information.
* **Camp Location (7)**: 
	+ Enter the location of the summer camp your child will be attending.
* **Dates Attending (8)**: 
	+ Enter the dates your child will be attending the summer camp.
* **Dates Info (9)**: 
	+ Enter any additional information about the dates of attendance.
* **Camper Info (10)**: 
	+ Enter your child's name, age, and any other relevant information about them.
* **Medical Info (11)**: 
	+ Enter any medical information or allergies your child has.
* **Emergency Contact Info (12)**: 
	+ Enter any additional information about the emergency contact person, such as their relationship to your child.

## Tips
- Please double-check the information you enter for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
