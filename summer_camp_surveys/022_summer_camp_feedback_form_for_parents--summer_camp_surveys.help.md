# Summer Camp Feedback Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Summer Camp Feedback Form For Parents" is designed to gather information and feedback from parents about their child's experience at the summer camp. This form helps the camp administrators understand the needs and concerns of parents and their children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question carefully, and provide as much detail as possible.
2.  If you have not attended the summer camp, please indicate so in the "has_campers" field.
3.  If you have attended, enter the names of all the campers in the "camper_names" field.
4.  Select your preferred contact method in the "contact_method" field.
5.  Enter your email address in the "email" field, if you selected the email contact method.
6.  Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field, if you selected the phone contact method.
7.  Enter the name of the summer camp in the "camp_name" field.
8.  Enter the address of the summer camp in the "camp_address" field.
9.  Enter the city of the summer camp in the "camp_city" field.
10.  Select the state where the summer camp is located in the "camp_state" field.
11.  Enter the zip code of the summer camp in the "camp_zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **has_campers** (Yes/No): Please select "Yes" if you have attended the summer camp with your child(ren) and "No" if you haven't.
*   **camper_names**: Enter the name(s) of the child(ren) who attended the summer camp. You can enter multiple names separated by commas or one name per line.
*   **email**: Enter your email address for contact.
*   **contact_method** (Phone/Email/Email): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
*   **phone_number**: Enter your phone number for contact.
*   **camp_name**: Enter the name of the summer camp where your child(ren) attended.
*   **camp_address**: Enter the address of the summer camp.
*   **camp_city**: Enter the city where the summer camp is located.
*   **camp_state** (CA/NJ/Other): Select the state where the summer camp is located.
*   **camp_zip**: Enter the zip code of the summer camp.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as required in the form).
*   If you have any other comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "camper_names" field.
*   Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
