# Camp Ministries Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Ministries Reference Form is designed to gather information from referees who have had personal or professional interactions with candidates for camp ministry positions. This form helps assess a candidate's suitability for the role by gathering feedback from individuals who can vouch for their character and abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Referee Name and contact information in the "Referee Name" and "Referee Contact Information" fields, respectively.
2. Enter the Candidate Name in the "Candidate Name" field.
3. Select the Relationship to the Candidate in the "Relationship to Candidate" field.
4. Rate the candidate's suitability in the "Suitability Rating" field.
5. Provide any relevant strengths and areas for development in the "Candidate Strengths" and "Areas for Development" fields.
6. Indicate your recommendation for the candidate in the "Recommendation" field.
7. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
8. Enter the Date of Reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referee Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the person providing the reference.
* **Referee Contact Information** (Required, Text): Enter the phone number or email of the referee.
* **Candidate Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the volunteer or applicant.
* **Relationship to Candidate** (Required, Text): Select how the referee knows the candidate (e.g., colleague, friend, supervisor).
* **Suitability Rating** (Required, Select One):
	+ Highly Suitable
	+ Suitable
	+ Unsure
	+ Not Suitable
* **Candidate Strengths** (Optional, Text): List key strengths and qualities observed.
* **Areas for Development** (Optional, Text): Suggest areas for growth or training.
* **Recommendation** (Required, Select One):
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Additional Comments** (Optional, Text): Provide any other relevant information for selection.
* **Date of Reference** (Optional, Date): Enter the date this reference was provided.

## Tips
* Only one reference per candidate is required.
* Referees should only provide their own information once per candidate, even if they have multiple interactions with the same candidate.
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and relevant to the candidate's suitability for the role.
