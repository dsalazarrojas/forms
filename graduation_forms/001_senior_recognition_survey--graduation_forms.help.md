# Senior Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Recognition Survey is a form used to gather information about a senior who is being recognized, allowing for a personalized and meaningful recognition award or gift to be created. This form is designed to be completed by someone who knows the senior well and is intended for internal use within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the senior's achievements, including any notable accomplishments or milestones they have reached.
2. Share a story or anecdote about the senior that highlights their personality or accomplishments.
3. Enter the senior's contact information, including their first and last name, email address, and phone number.
4. Provide any additional contact information or details that would be useful for the senior to receive their recognition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Senior Acheivements**: (`senior_achievments`, text, required/optional): Describe the senior's achievements, including any notable accomplishments or milestones they have reached.
* **Story Tell**: (`story_tell`, text, required/optional): Share a brief story or anecdote about the senior that highlights their personality or accomplishments.
* **Contact Details**: (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the senior's contact information, including their name, email address, and phone number.
* **First Name**: (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the senior's first name.
* **Last Name**: (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the senior's last name.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the senior's email address.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the senior’s phone number.
* **Photo**: (`photo`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the senior's photo, if applicable.
