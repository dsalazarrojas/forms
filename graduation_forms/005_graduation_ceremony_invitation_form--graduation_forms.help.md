# Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a graduation ceremony, including details about the ceremony itself, the invitations, and the graduates. It should be used by administrators or staff to plan and coordinate the graduation ceremony.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the graduation ceremony in the "Title" field.
2. Fill in the graduation details such as "Graduation Year", "Graduation Month", "Graduation Day", "Graduation Time", and "Graduation Location".
3. Select the options for "Ceremony Type" and "Ceremony Invitation Status".
4. Choose whether or not to send invitations ("Ceremony Invitations") and enter any additional message if desired.
5. Optionally, select the "Ceremony Start Time" and "Ceremony End Time" if the ceremony is not a simple ceremony.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form** (`graduation_ceremony_invitation_form`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the graduation ceremony.
* **Title** (`title`, text, not required): Enter the title of the graduation ceremony.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person or class graduating.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the graduation ceremony.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the graduation ceremony.
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, text, not required): Enter the year of the graduation.
* **Graduation Month** (`graduation_month`, text, not required): Enter the month of the graduation.
* **Graduation Day** (`graduation_day`, text, not required): Enter the day of the graduation.
* **Graduation Time** (`graduation_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation Location** (`graduation_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the graduation ceremony.
* **Ceremony Type** (`ceremony_type`, text, not required): Select the type of ceremony (e.g. commencement, convocation).
* **Graduation Class** (`graduation_class`, text, not required): Enter the graduation class or cohort.
* **Graduation School** (`graduation_school`, text, not required): Enter the graduation school or institution.
* **Graduation Program** (`graduation_program`, text, not required): Enter the graduation program or major.
* **Ceremony Color** (`ceremony_color`, text, not required): Enter the color scheme or theme for the ceremony.
* **Ceremony Invitations** (`ceremony_invitations`, select_multiple, not required): Choose whether or not to send invitations. Select "Yes" to send invitations, or "No" to not send invitations.
* **Invitation Message** (`invitation_message`, text, not required): Enter a personal message to include with the invitations.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation Time** (`graduation_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the graduation ceremony.
* **Ceremony Start Time** (`ceremony_start_time`, time, not required): Enter the start time of the ceremony.
* **Ceremony End Time** (`ceremony_end_time`, time, not required): Enter the end time of the ceremony.
* **Ceremony Invitation Status** (`ceremony_invitation_status`, text, not required): Enter the status of the invitations (e.g. sent, pending, not sent).
* **Ceremony Invitation Form Size** (`ceremony_invitation_form_size`, text, not required): Enter the size of the invitation form.
* **Ceremony Invitation Type** (`ceremony_invitation_type`, text, not required): Enter the type of invitation (e.g. printed, digital).
