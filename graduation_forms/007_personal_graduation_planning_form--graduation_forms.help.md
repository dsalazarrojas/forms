## Personal Graduation Planning Form - Help Guide

# Purpose
This form is designed to help students track their personal graduation planning, including their expected graduation date, academic plan, and committee members. It aims to streamline the graduation planning process, ensuring that students and graduation committees can stay organized and on track.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the expected graduation date in the "Graduation Date" field.
3. Choose the expected degree from the list in the "Expected Degree" field.
4. Provide a brief academic plan in the "Academic Plan" field.
5. Describe the student's graduation plan details in the "Graduation Plan Details" field.
6. Select the members of the graduation committee in the "Graduation Committee Members" field.
7. Choose the chair of the graduation committee from the list in the "Graduation Committee Chair" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (text): The student's name.
* **Graduation Date** (date): The date the student plans to graduate.
* **Expected Degree** (select_one): Choose the expected degree the student is working towards (e.g., Value 1, Value 2, Value 3).
* **Academic Plan** (note): A brief description of the student's academic plan.
* **Graduation Plan Details** (text): A detailed description of the student's graduation plan.
* **Graduation Committee Members** (select_multiple): Select multiple members of the graduation committee (e.g., Value 1, Value 2, Value 3).
* **Graduation Committee Chair** (select_one): Choose the chair of the graduation committee from the list (e.g., Value 1, Value 2, Value 3).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the graduation planning process runs smoothly.
* Double-check your selection in the "Expected Degree" field to ensure you are selecting the correct option.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your graduation committee or academic advisor for clarification.
* Remember to keep your academic plan and graduation details clear and concise for easy reference.
