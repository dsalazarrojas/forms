# graduation_ceremony_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The graduation ceremony RSVP form is a vital tool for the university to gather essential information from graduates about their attendance and guest count. By completing this form, graduates can help the university plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your General Information page with your details.
2. Proceed to the Guest Details page, where you can enter the number of guests you plan to bring.
3. On the Graduation Date page, select the correct date for your graduation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name as you prefer to be listed on the ceremony program.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total number of guests you plan to bring with you to the ceremony.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the correct date of your graduation from the provided calendar.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help the university plan accordingly.
- If you have any special requests or needs, please contact the university in advance to discuss further arrangements.
- You can submit your RSVP form online, and the university will review and confirm your details before the ceremony.
