# School Graduation Ceremony Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from students, parents, and staff about their experience at the school graduation ceremony. It will help the school administration understand what went well and what can be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Use the provided text boxes for written comments and feedback.
3. Select all the relevant options for your favorite parts of the ceremony.
4. Rate your overall experience with the ceremony using the rating system.
5. Finally, review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (`text`, required): This is the main feedback section where you can provide your thoughts and opinions about the graduation ceremony. Please be as detailed and specific as possible.
* **Event Details** (`text`, required): Describe the ceremony you attended, including the date, time, and any notable events that occurred.
* **Overall Experience** (`text`, required): Rate your overall experience at the graduation ceremony, using your own words.
* **Positive Impressions** (`text`, required): Share any positive aspects of the ceremony that stood out to you.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`text`, required): Identify any areas that could be improved for future ceremonies.
* **Suggestions** (`text`, required): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the graduation ceremony.
* **Favorite Part** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all the parts of the ceremony that you enjoyed the most.
* **Rating** (`select_one`, required): Rate your overall experience with the ceremony, using the 1-5 rating scale.
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the ceremony.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions truthfully and as thoroughly as possible.
* Use clear and concise language in your written responses.
* Select all the relevant options for your favorite parts of the ceremony.
* Be respectful and constructive in your feedback, focusing on how to improve the ceremony for future events.
