# graduation_party_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for guests to provide their contact information and RSVP status for a graduation party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and contact information on the "Guest" page.
* Enter the date you plan to attend the party on the "Attending Date" page.
* Choose the start and end times of the party on the "Start Time" and "End Time" pages.
* Select your RSVP status on the "RSVP" page.
* Provide any additional message or comments on the "Message" page.
* If applicable, enter the graduation date and time on the "Graduation Date" and "Graduation Time" pages.
* If applicable, enter the graduation year on the "Graduation Year" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest** (text): Enter your name as the guest.
* **Attending Date** (date): Enter the date you plan to attend the party. 
* **Start Time** (time): Enter the start time in 24-hour format.
* **End Time** (time): Enter the end time in 24-hour format.
* **RSVP** (select one): Choose how you plan to attend the party.
	+ Maybe: You are unsure if you will attend.
	+ True: You will definitely attend.
	+ False: You will not attend.
* **Contact Info** (text): Enter your contact information so the host can reach out to you.
* **Message** (text): Provide any additional message or comments to the host.
* **Graduation Date** (date): Enter the graduation date if applicable.
* **Graduation Time** (time): Enter the graduation time in 24-hour format if applicable.
* **Graduation Year** (number): Enter the graduation year if applicable.
