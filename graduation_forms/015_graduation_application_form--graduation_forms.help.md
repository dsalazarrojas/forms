# Graduation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students who are applying to graduate from an educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please make sure you have all the necessary information required before filling out this form.
- Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
- If you need help, do not hesitate to contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name**: Enter your legal first name here.
- **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name here.
- **Graduation Date**: Enter the date of your graduation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Student ID**: If you are a student, enter your student ID here. This can be your student ID number or any other unique identifier.
- **Email**: Enter your valid email address here.
- **Contact Number**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
- **Program Completed**: Select all the programs you have completed during your education (e.g. High School, College, University).
- **Graduation Year**: Enter the year you graduated.
- **Graduation Certification**: Select the type of certification you have received (e.g. Bachelor's Degree, Associate's Degree, High School Diploma).
- **Graduation School**: Enter the name of the school where you graduated.
