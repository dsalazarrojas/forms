# University Bookstore Regalia Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for regalia orders during graduation events. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (if any) and any additional fields that may be relevant to your order.
2. Review and submit your form.
3. If you need to make any special arrangements or have any questions, please contact the store staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Graduation Regalia Form**: This field is required to be filled out for every order.
    • **Student Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your University records.
    • **Email**: Enter your University email address to facilitate order tracking and communication.
    • **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication if needed.
    • **Regalia Type**: Check 'Yes' if you want to order regalia, 'No' otherwise.
    • **Regalia Color**: If you want to order regalia, select the color you prefer.
    • **Size**: If you want to order regalia, select the size you need.
    • **Package Type**: Check 'Yes' if you want to order a package deal, 'No' otherwise.
    • **Pickup or Delivery**: Check 'Yes' if you want to pick up your order, 'No' if you want it delivered.
    • **Special Instructions**: If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please specify them here.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues, please contact the store staff for assistance.
