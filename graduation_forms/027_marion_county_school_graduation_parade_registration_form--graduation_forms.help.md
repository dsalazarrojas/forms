# marion_county_school_graduation_parade_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the Marion County School Graduation Parade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your school.
2. Select the date and time of the graduation parade.
3. Enter the address of your school.
4. Enter the location of the graduation parade.
5. Enter the start and end times of the graduation parade.
6. Enter the route of the graduation parade.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the graduation parade organizer.
8. Enter the name and contact information of the graduation parade sponsor.
9. Enter the name of the school class and parent name for the graduation parade.
10. Enter the email and phone number of the parent associated with the graduation parade.
11. If the graduation parade will have early release, select 'True', otherwise select 'False'.
12. Add any additional comments about the graduation parade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Graduation Parade Date** (3, date, required): Select the date of the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Time** (4, time, required): Select the time of the graduation parade.
* **School Address** (5, text, required): Enter the address of your school.
* **Graduation Parade Location** (6, text, required): Enter the location of the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Start Time** (7, time, required): Select the start time of the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade End Time** (8, time, required): Select the end time of the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Route** (9, text, required): Enter the route of the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Organizer** (10, text, required): Enter the name of the graduation parade organizer.
* **Graduation Parade Organizer Email** (11, email, required): Enter the email of the graduation parade organizer.
* **Graduation Parade Organizer Phone** (12, text, required): Enter the phone number of the graduation parade organizer.
* **Graduation Parade Organizer Name** (13, text, required): Enter the name of the graduation parade organizer.
* **Graduation Parade Sponsor** (14, text, required): Enter the name of the graduation parade sponsor.
* **Graduation Parade Sponsor Phone** (15, text, required): Enter the phone number of the graduation parade sponsor.
* **Graduation Parade Sponsor Name** (16, text, required): Enter the name of the graduation parade sponsor.
* **Graduation Parade School Class** (17, text, required): Enter the name of the school class participating in the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Parent Name** (18, text, required): Enter the name of the parent associated with the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Parent Email** (19, email, required): Enter the email of the parent associated with the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Parent Phone** (20, text, required): Enter the phone number of the parent associated with the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Total Students** (21, number, required): Enter the total number of students participating in the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Bus Count** (22, number, required): Enter the number of buses participating in the graduation parade.
* **Graduation Parade Early Release** (24, select_one, required): Select 'True' if the graduation parade will have early release, otherwise select 'False'.
* **Graduation Parade Comments** (25, note, required): Enter any additional comments about the graduation parade.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the entered information.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your school administration.
