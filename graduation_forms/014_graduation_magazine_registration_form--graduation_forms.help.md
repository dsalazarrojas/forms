# Graduation Magazine Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graduation Magazine Registration Form is designed to collect information from graduates for the graduation magazine. The purpose of this form is to obtain relevant details from the graduate's profile, including name, contact information, graduation details, and academic background. This information will be used to create a personalized entry in the graduation magazine.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your graduation date in the "graduation_date" field using the calendar icon.
6. Enter your graduation year in the "graduation_year" field.
7. Enter the name of your school in the "school_name" field.
8. Select your graduation status in the response to "graduation_degree" question.
9. Enter your major in the "major" field.
10. Enter the name of your university in the "university" field.
11. Select your category of study in the "category" field.
12. Write a brief description of yourself in the "description" field, noting that this field is a text box for free-form entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name here.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address here. Please use a valid email format (e.g., @example.com).
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number here.
* **graduation_date** (`graduation_date`, date, required: false): Select your graduation date using the calendar icon. Please note that this field will default to the current date if left blank.
* **graduation_year** (`graduation_year`, number, required: false): Enter your graduation year here.
* **school_name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school here.
* **graduation_degree** (`graduation_degree`, select_one, required: false): Choose your graduation status from the dropdown list: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **major** (`major`, text, required: false): Enter your major here.
* **university** (`university`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your university here.
* **category** (`category`, text, required: false): Select your category of study here.
* **description** (`description`, note, required: false): Write a brief description of yourself here. This field is for free-form entry.
