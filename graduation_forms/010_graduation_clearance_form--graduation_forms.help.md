# Graduation Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Clearance Form is used to record a student's graduation status and clearance. It captures details about the student, their degree, and their clearance status. This form is intended for academic institutions or universities to use for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the student's student ID and ID type (Student ID or Student ID Number).
3. Select the student's type (Undergraduate, Graduate, or Non-Degree).
4. Choose the degree title (Bachelor, Master, or Doctoral).
5. Select the student's major (Business, Science, or Arts).
6. Enter the department (text field).
7. Enter the advisor's name (text field).
8. Enter the student's date of birth.
9. Enter the date of clearance.
10. Sign the form with your digital signature.
11. Enter the graduation date.
12. Enter the graduation year.
13. Select the clearance status (Cleared or Not Cleared).
14. Add any remarks (text field).
15. Select the clearance status (Cleared or Not Cleared).
16. Enter any clearance comments.
17. Select the student status (Active, Inactive, or Leave of Absence).
18. Enter any student status comments.
19. Select the departmental clearance status (Cleared or Not Cleared).
20. Enter any departmental clearance comments.
21. Enter the graduation officer's name and signature.
22. Enter the submitted by field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first_name, text, not required): Enter the student's first name.
- **Last Name** (last_name, text, not required): Enter the student's last name.
- **Student ID** (student_id, text, not required): Enter the student's ID number.
- **Student ID Type** (student_id_type, select_one, not required): Select the type of student ID (Student ID or Student ID Number).
- **Student Type** (student_type, select_multiple, not required): Select the student's type (Undergraduate, Graduate, or Non-Degree).
- **Degree Title** (degree_title, select_one, not required): Select the degree title (Bachelor, Master, or Doctoral).
- **Major** (major, select_multiple, not required): Select the student's major (Business, Science, or Arts).
- **Department** (department, text, not required): Enter the department where the student is studying.
- **Advisor** (advisor, text, not required): Enter the advisor's name.
- **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, not required): Enter the student's date of birth.
- **Date of Clearance** (date_of_clearance, date, not required): Enter the date of clearance.
- **Signature** (signature, text, not required): Sign the form with your digital signature.
- **Graduation Date** (graduation_date, date, not required): Enter the graduation date.
- **Graduation Year** (graduation_year, number, not required): Enter the graduation year.
- **Graduation Clearance** (graduation_clearance, select_one, not required): Select the clearance status (Cleared or Not Cleared).
- **Remarks** (remarks, note, not required): Enter any remarks about the student.
- **Clearance Status** (clearance_status, select_one, not required): Select the clearance status (Cleared or Not Cleared).
- **Clearance Comments** (clearance_comments, text, not required): Enter any clearance comments.
- **Student Status** (student_status, select_multiple, not required): Select the student status (Active, Inactive, or Leave of Absence).
- **Student Status Comments** (student_status_comments, text, not required): Enter any student status comments.
- **Departmental Clearance Status** (departmental_clearance_status, select_one, not required): Select the departmental clearance status (Cleared or Not Cleared).
- **Departmental Clearance Comments** (departmental_clearance_comments, text, not required): Enter any departmental clearance comments.
- **Graduation Officer** (graduation_officer, text, not required): Enter the graduation officer's name.
- **Graduation Officer Signature** (graduation_officer_sign, text, not required): Enter the graduation officer's signature.
- **Submitted By** (submitted_by, text, not required): Enter the person submitting the form.

Note: The fields marked as not required are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
