# Graduation Lei Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graduation Lei Order Form is designed to help you create an order for a graduation lei to celebrate the occasion of a graduate. Please provide the following information to process your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your form ID to associate your order with your school or organization.
2. Enter the customer name and school name for your graduation ceremony.
3. Choose whether you would like to pickup your lei or have it delivered.
4. Select your payment method for the order.
5. Enter the desired pickup or delivery date and time for your lei.
6. Enter the delivery address if you chose delivery in step 3.
7. Provide any additional payment information if required.
8. Add any order notes or comments about your order.
9. Enter the school email and phone number for contact purposes.
10. Enter the customer email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for your school or organization.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer to whom the lei will be presented.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school or organization associated with the graduation ceremony.
* **Pickup or Delivery** (`pickup_or_delivery`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you would like to pickup your lei or have it delivered.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the payment method you would like to use for your order (Cash, Credit, PayPal).
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required: false): Enter the desired date to pickup your lei.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required: false): Enter the desired time to pickup your lei.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the desired date for your lei to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Enter the desired time for your lei to be delivered.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address to which your lei will be delivered if you chose delivery in step 3.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional payment information required.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about your order.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required: false): Enter the school email address for contact purposes.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the school phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer email address for contact purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your school or organization for clarification.
- If you need to make any changes to your order after submission, contact your school or organization directly.
