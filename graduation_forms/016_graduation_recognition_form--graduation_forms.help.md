# Graduation Recognition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Recognition Form is a document that allows students to report their graduation milestones in their academic programs. It is used to collect and verify student details for graduation ceremonies, recognition events, or other celebrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Choose the student's grade level from the dropdown list in the "Student Grade" field.
4. If the student's grade level is not listed, you can provide more information in the "Student Grade Other" field.
5. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Fill in the student's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official records.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number as issued by the university or institution.
* **Student Grade**: Select the student's grade level from the list provided (High, Low, Average).
* **Student Grade Other**: If the student's grade level is not listed, provide more information about their current grade level (optional).
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address where they can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number where they can be contacted.
* **Tips**: Be sure to double-check the student's information for accuracy before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for verification.
