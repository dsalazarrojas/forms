# Graduation Apparel Order Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students or staff members who are ordering graduation apparel.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of graduation apparel you want to order from the list provided.
2. Enter your name and contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Provide your graduation year, date, and time if applicable.
4. Choose a payment method (Online or Offline).
5. Select the order status from the options provided (Draft, Completed, or Approved).
6. Enter the graduation apparel price and order quantity if applicable.
7. Add any additional comments or special requests in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduation Apparel Order Form** (`graduation_apparel_order_form`):
	+ This is the main section of the form where you will select the type of graduation apparel you want to order.
* **Graduation Apparel Order** (`graduation_apparel_order`):
	+ Choose the type of graduation apparel you want to order (Graduation Cap, Graduation Gown, or Graduation Robes).
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`):
	+ Enter the year you graduated or will graduate.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`):
	+ Enter the date of your graduation ceremony if applicable.
* **Name** (`name`):
	+ Enter your name as it should appear on your graduation apparel.
* **Email** (`email`):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`):
	+ Enter the number of graduation apparel you want to order.
* **Graduation Apparel Price** (`graduation_apparel_price`):
	+ Enter the price of the graduation apparel.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`):
	+ Choose how you want to pay for your graduation apparel (Online or Offline).
* **Graduation Apparel Order Status** (`graduation_apparel_order_status`):
	+ Select the status of your graduation order (Draft, Completed, or Approved).
* **Graduation Apparel Order Date** (`graduation_apparel_order_date`):
	+ Enter the date you want to receive your graduation apparel.
* **Graduation Apparel Order Time** (`graduation_apparel_order_time`):
	+ Enter the time you want to receive your graduation apparel.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Add any additional comments or special requests for your graduation apparel.
* **Graduation Form Additional Comments** (`graduation_form_additional_comments`):
	+ Add any additional comments or special requests for the graduation order process.
* **Graduation Apparel Order Status** (`graduation_apparel_order_status`):
	+ Select multiple status options for your graduation order (Draft, Completed, or Approved).

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct type of graduation apparel and status options that apply to your order.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date contact information and graduation details.
* Use the comments section to add any additional information or special requests that may be helpful for the graduation order process.
