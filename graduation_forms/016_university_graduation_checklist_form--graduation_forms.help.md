# University Graduation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Graduation Checklist Form is a tool used by students to provide essential information about their graduation status and submitted documents to university administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your email address associated with your university account.
3. Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
4. Provide your expected graduation date.
5. Select your current graduation status (Applied, Eligible, Ineligible, or Not Applied).
6. Select all documents you have submitted (Transcript, GPA Report, Diploma, or Certificate).
7. Choose who submitted the documents (Student, Advisor, Dean, or Other).
8. If necessary, provide any additional comments about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address associated with your university account.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Graduation Date**: Enter your expected graduation date.
* **Graduation Status**: Select your current graduation status:
	+ Applied: You have submitted your application for graduation.
	+ Eligible: You meet all the requirements for graduation.
	+ Ineligible: You do not meet all the requirements for graduation.
	+ Not Applied: You have not yet submitted your application for graduation.
* **Submitted Documents**: Select all documents you have submitted:
	+ Transcript: Official transcript from your university.
	+ GPA Report: Official report of your GPA.
	+ Diploma: Official diploma from your university.
	+ Certificate: Official certificate from your university.
* **Submitted By**: Choose who submitted the documents:
	+ Student: You submitted the documents yourself.
	+ Advisor: Your academic advisor submitted the documents.
	+ Dean: Your department head submitted the documents.
	+ Other: Another person submitted the documents.
* **Comments**: If necessary, provide any additional comments about your submission.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your university administrator for guidance.
