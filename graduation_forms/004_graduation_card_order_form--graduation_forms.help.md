# Graduation Card Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graduation Card Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for ordering graduation cards.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Enter your name and phone number to ensure we can contact you for any further information.
- Enter your email address for easy communication and to receive updates on your order status.
- Enter your date of birth to ensure we can personalize the graduation card.
- Select "Yes" or "No" for "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields based on your order requirements.
- You can enter any additional notes about your order in the "Note" field.
- Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you in case of any issues with your order.
- Finally, review all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduation Card Order Form Name** (`graduation_card_order_form_name`, text, optional):
  This field is the title of your graduation card order.
  Please enter the name of the order in a clear and concise manner.
* **Graduation Card Order Phone Number** (`graduation_card_order_form_phone_number`, number, optional):
  Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Graduation Card Order E mail** (`graduation_card_order_form_email`, email, optional):
  Enter your email address for easy communication and to receive updates on your order status.
* **Date of Birth** (`graduation_card_order_form_date_of_birth`, date, optional):
  Enter your date of birth to ensure we can personalize the graduation card.
* **Select One** (`graduation_card_order_form_select_one`, select_one, optional):
  Select "Yes" if you want to order one graduation card. Select "No" if you want to order more than one graduation card.
* **Select Multiple** (`graduation_card_order_form_select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select "Yes" if you want to order more than one graduation card. Select "No" if you want to order one graduation card.
* **Time** (`graduation_card_order_form_time`, time, optional):
  Please note that this field is not used in the form currently. You can ignore it.
* **Note** (`graduation_card_order_form_note`, note, optional):
  Enter any additional notes about your order in this field.
* **Phone** (`graduation_card_order_form_phone`, text, optional):
  Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you in case of any issues with your order.
