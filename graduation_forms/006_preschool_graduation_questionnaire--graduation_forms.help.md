# preschool_graduation_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from parents and teachers about a child's graduation from preschool. It helps in understanding the child's development and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer every question that applies to you. If a question doesn't apply, leave it blank.
2. Be as detailed and specific as possible in your answers.
3. If you need help or have questions, contact your child's teacher or school administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Child Development (1)
* **Child Development** (`child_development`, text, optional): Describe the child's development and progress during their time in preschool. This could include any notable achievements, challenges, or areas of growth you've observed.

### Parent Feedback (2)
* **Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and observations about your child's development and progress. This is a good place to mention any strengths, weaknesses, or areas you'd like to see improvement in.

### Teacher Suggestions (3)
* **Teacher Suggestions** (`teacher_suggestions`, note, optional): Share any suggestions or recommendations from the teacher about your child's development and progress. This could include areas where the teacher has seen growth or areas where additional support would be helpful.

### Teacher Feedback (4)
* **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, text, optional): Share the teacher's thoughts and observations about your child's development and progress. This could include any notable achievements, challenges, or areas of growth you've observed.

### Graduation Date (5)
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your child is graduating from preschool. This will help us keep track of your child's progress and milestones.

### Teacher Evaluation (6)
* **Teacher Evaluation** (`teacher_evaluation`, select multiple, optional): The teacher will evaluate whether they have seen:
	+ Yes: notable growth or progress
	+ No: no notable growth or progress

### Teacher Suggestions 2 (7)
* **Teacher Suggestions 2** (`teacher_suggestions_2`, note, optional): Share any additional suggestions or recommendations from the teacher about your child's development and progress. This could include areas where the teacher has seen growth or areas where additional support would be helpful.

### Parent Evaluation (8)
* **Parent Evaluation** (`parent_evaluation`, select one, optional): The parent will evaluate whether they have seen:
	+ Yes: notable growth or progress
	+ No: no notable growth or progress
