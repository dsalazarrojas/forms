# Graduation Regalia Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for graduation regalia, which is a ceremonial garment typically worn during commencement ceremonies. The form is intended to gather information about the type and details of the regalia ordered, including size, color, and delivery options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the type of regalia you wish to order from the "Regalia Order" options.
3. Choose the color of the regalia from the "Regalia Color" options.
4. Enter the size of the regalia.
5. Enter the cost of the regalia.
6. Select the delivery option for the regalia.
7. Enter any relevant information for the student, such as their student ID number.
8. Enter your payment method and type (e.g., cash, card, or other).
9. Submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduation Regalia Order Form** (`graduation_regalia_order_form`): This is the main form title and header.
* **Graduation Sizing Details** (`graduation_sizing_details`): Enter the details about the regalia size you wish to order.
* **Delivery Type** (`delivery_type`): Select how you would like to receive your regalia (e.g., delivery to the store, campus store, or pickup).
* **Store ID** (`store_id`): Enter a store ID if applicable.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Regalia Order** (`regalia_order`): Select the type of regalia you wish to order (e.g., cap and gown, hood and cap, etc.).
* **Regalia Color** (`regalia_color`): Choose the color of the regalia.
* **Regalia Size** (`regalia_size`): Enter the size of the regalia.
* **Regalia Cost** (`regalia_cost`): Enter the cost of the regalia.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the method of payment you wish to use (e.g., cash, card, or other).
* **Payment Method Type** (`payment_method_type`): Enter any additional information about your payment method.
* **Regalia Set** (`regalia_set`): Select any additional regalia sets you wish to order (e.g., hood and cap, etc.). 
* **Hood and Scabbard** (`regalia_set`): Select any additional regalia sets you wish to order (e.g., hood and scabbard, etc.).

Note: The "Regalia Set" and "Hood and Scabbard" options are likely duplicates and should be merged into one option. However, based on the provided YAML structure, they are treated as separate options in the "Regalia Order" field. 

Also, the "Other" option in the "Regalia Color" field could be explained further to avoid confusion.
