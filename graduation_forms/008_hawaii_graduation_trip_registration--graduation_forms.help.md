# Hawaii Graduation Trip Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help students complete the Hawaii Graduation Trip Registration form. By following these steps, students can register for the graduation trip and ensure that they have submitted all necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your student details.
2. Choose your room preferences for the trip.
3. Enter emergency contact information, including names and contact details.
4. Agree to the school's policies and hold harmless the school and its representatives.
5. Enter your email address for future communication.
6. Provide any additional participant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, student ID, and any other relevant details.
* **Room Preferences** (`room_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose 1, 2, or 3 options for each room type, separated by commas. For example, "Single occupancy: 1, Double occupancy: 2, Suite: 3".
* **Emergency Contact 1** (`emergency_contact1`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact2`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of another emergency contact.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): Agree to the school's policies and hold harmless the school and its representatives.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for future communication.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about participants.
* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant school information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid delays or issues with the trip.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your school representative for help.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
