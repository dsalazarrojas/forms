# Graduation Cap And Gown Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to order graduation caps and gowns for upcoming graduations. It collects necessary information for processing and customization of the graduation attire.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the size of the graduation cap and gown on the **Select Size** page. Choose from the available options to determine the correct fit for your graduation attire.
2.  On the **Select Accessory** page, choose any additional accessories you would like to include with your graduation attire, such as a hood or tassel.
3.  On the **Graduation Gown** page, please note any specific details or requests you may have for your graduation gown.
4.  On the **Student Info** page, enter your student information, including name, student ID, and any other required details.
5.  On the **Student Info Form** page, confirm if you have read and understood the terms of the order.
6.  Finally, review and submit your order on the **Submit** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select Size** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the size of your graduation cap and gown. This will help us to determine the correct fit for your attire.
*   **Select Accessory** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose any additional accessories you would like to include with your graduation attire, such as a hood or tassel.
*   **Graduation Gown** (`note`, required/optional): Note any specific details or requests you may have for your graduation gown.
*   **Student Info** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your student information, including name and student ID.
*   **Student Info Form** (`select_one`, required/optional): Confirm if you have read and understood the terms of the order.
