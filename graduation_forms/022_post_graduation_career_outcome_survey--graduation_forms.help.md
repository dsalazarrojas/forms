# Post Graduation Career Outcome Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Post Graduation Career Outcome Survey is designed to gather information about your career outcomes after graduation. This form will help us understand your current situation, job satisfaction, and future career goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the options that best describe your current situation for each field. If you're unsure about a particular field, try to answer based on your current circumstances. Don't worry if you're not sure or don't have all the information – just do your best!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job 1**: Select the job that best describes the current job you're employed in (if any).
* **Job 2**: This field is not currently being used. Feel free to skip it.
* **Yes/No**: Indicate whether you are currently employed or not.
* **Current Work Hours**: Enter the number of hours you work per week, if applicable.
* **Current Job Tenure**: Enter the number of years you've been working in your current job, if applicable.
* **Employed/Unemployed/Retired/Other**: Select the status that best describes your current employment situation.
* **Employed/Unemployed/Retired/Other**: Select the status that best describes your current student status.
* **Full-time/Part-time/Intern/Other**: Select the status that best describes your current employment status.
* **Student/Non-Student/Other**: Select the status that best describes your current student status.
* **Bachelor's/Master's/Doctorate/Other**: Select the level of education you've completed.
* **Manager/Non-Manager/Other**: Select your current career level.
* **Industry 1/Industry 2/Other**: Select the industry you're currently working in.
* **Job 1/Job 2/Other**: Select the type of job you're currently in (if any).
* **Job 1/Job 2/Other**: Select the type of job you're currently in (if any).
* **Job 1/Job 2/Other**: Select the type of job you're currently in (if any).
* **High/Low/Neutral**: Rate your satisfaction with your current job, on a scale of high, low, or neutral.
* **High/Low/Neutral**: Rate your satisfaction with your current career, on a scale of high, low, or neutral.
* **Leader/Non-Leader/Other**: Select whether you're a leader or not in your current role.
* **High/Low/Neutral**: Rate your current job satisfaction, on a scale of high, low, or neutral.
* **High/Low/Neutral**: Rate your current career satisfaction, on a scale of high, low, or neutral.
* **High/Low/Neutral**: Rate your current career satisfaction, on a scale of high, low, or neutral.
* **Manager/Non-Manager/Other**: Select your current career level.
* **Manager/Non-Manager/Other**: Select your current career level.
* **Manager/Non-Manager/Other**: Select your current career level.

Note that some fields may be skipped or not relevant to your current situation, so feel free to focus on the fields that matter most to you.
