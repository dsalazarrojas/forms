# Graduation Thesis Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the upload and registration of graduation thesis documents. This form ensures that the required information is collected and stored accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure that the file you upload is the correct type (Document, Image, or Video).
3. Double-check the uploaded file name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false)
Student Name: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.

### 2. **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required: false)
Course Title: Enter the title of the course for which you are submitting the thesis.

### 3. **Upload Thesis File** (`upload_file`, text, required: false)
Upload Thesis File: Click to upload your thesis document in Document, Image, or Video format.

### 4. **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false)
Date Submitted: Enter the date you submitted your thesis for evaluation.

### 5. **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required: false)
Review Status: Select the status of your review, which can be Approved, Not Evaluated, or Rejected.

### 6. **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false)
Comments: Add any comments or feedback from the reviewer.

### 7. **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false)
Submitted By: Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.

### 8. **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Email: Enter your institutional email address as it appears in the university records.

### 9. **Department** (`department`, text, required: false)
Department: Enter your department name.

### 10. **Semester** (`semester`, number, required: false)
Semester: Enter the semester you are graduating.

### 11. **File Type** (`file_type`, select_one, required: true)
File Type: Select the type of file you are uploading, which can be Document, Image, or Video.

### 12. **File Path** (`file_path`, text, required: false)
File Path: This field will be automatically populated once you upload a file.

### 13. **Uploaded By** (`uploaded_by`, text, required: false)
Uploaded By: Enter the name of the person who uploaded the file.

### 14. **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false)
Review Date: Enter the date of your review completion.

### 15. **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required: false)
Graduation Date: Enter your graduation date.

### 16. **File Size** (`file_size`, number, required: false)
File Size: Enter the size of the uploaded file.

### 17. **File Count** (`file_count`, number, required: false)
File Count: Enter the count of files uploaded.

### 18. **Uploaded Date** (`uploaded_date`, date, required: false)
Uploaded Date: This field will be automatically populated once you upload a file.

### 19. **File Name** (`file_name`, text, required: false)
File Name: Enter the name of your uploaded file.

### 20. **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false)
Student Email: Enter your institutional email address as it appears in the university records.

### 21. **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required: false)
Department Name: Enter the name of your department.

### 22. **Semester Name** (`semester_name`, text, required: false)
Semester Name: Enter the name of the semester you are graduating.

### 23. **File Type Name** (`file_type_name`, text, required: false)
File Type Name: This field will be automatically populated once you select the file type.

### 24. **Status Name** (`review_status_name`, text, required: false)
Status Name: This field will be automatically populated once you select the review status.

### 25. **Uploaded By Name** (`uploaded_by_name`, text, required: false)
Uploaded By Name: This field will be automatically populated once you upload a file and select the upload type.
