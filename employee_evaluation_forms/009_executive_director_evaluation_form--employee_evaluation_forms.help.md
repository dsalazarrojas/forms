# executive_director_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The executive director evaluation form is used to assess the performance of executive directors within the company. It helps to provide an objective evaluation of their work, goals, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select the evaluation date in the "Evaluation Date" field.
4. Provide any comments about the employee's performance in the "Comments" field (optional).
5. If applicable, enter the tool or resource assigned to the employee in the "Assigned Tool" field.
6. Enter the evaluation score for the employee in the "Evaluation Score" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the executive director being evaluated.
* **Job Title** (text, required/optional): Enter the executive director's job title.
* **Evaluation Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date of the evaluation.
* **Comments** (note, required/optional): Provide any comments or feedback about the executive director's performance (optional).
* **Assigned Tool** (text, required/optional): If the executive director was assigned a specific tool or resource, enter it here (optional).
* **Evaluation Score** (number, required/optional): Enter a score from 1-5 to evaluate the executive director's performance (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to keep the evaluation score between 1 and 5.
- Ensure the comments are constructive and objective.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled in.
