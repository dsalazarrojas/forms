# Parent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Parent Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback and thoughts from parents or guardians on their child's experience in the educational institution. This form allows parents to provide their perspective and help the institution improve the student's overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and permissions required to complete the form accurately.
2. Answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing detailed feedback on your child's progress and well-being.
3. Fill out the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment to ensure accuracy and attention to detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, **Required**): Please enter your child's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, Email, Optional): If you have an email address for your child, please enter it here. This will help the institution contact you with updates and information.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, Number, **Required**): Please select your child's current grade level.
* **Student Class** (`student_class`, Text, Optional): If your child is enrolled in a specific class, please enter the class name here.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, Select One, **Required**): Please select the type of feedback you are providing. You can choose from Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, Text, **Required**): Please provide detailed feedback on your child's progress and well-being. This is an essential part of helping us improve the student's experience.
* **Feedback Urgency** (`feedback_urgency`, Select Multiple, Optional): If you have immediate or short-term feedback, please select the corresponding option.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, Text, **Required**): Please sign this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true to your knowledge.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, Text, **Required**): Please ask your child to sign this form to confirm that they have been informed of the feedback and are aware of their progress.
