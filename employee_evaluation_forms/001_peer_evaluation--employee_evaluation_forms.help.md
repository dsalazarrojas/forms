# Peer Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Peer Evaluation form is used for employees to rate and provide feedback on their colleagues. This form helps to assess performance, identify areas for improvement, and facilitate growth among team members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the colleague you would like to evaluate.
2. Choose how you rated them from the provided scale.
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments about your colleague.
4. Rate your colleague on specific criteria.
5. Enter the date of the evaluation.
6. Optionally, you can provide a rating score and select the rating scale.
7. Enter any peer comments or feedback.
8. If applicable, enter the evaluated colleague's details such as name, title, department, email, and phone number.
9. Specify who evaluated them, including their name, title, department, email, and phone number.
10. Finally, submit the form.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when rating and providing feedback on your colleagues.
* Use the provided scale for rating and feedback to ensure consistency.
* Clearly explain your evaluation and feedback to help the colleague understand their performance.
* Keep in mind that the evaluation form is a confidential tool for personal growth and development.
