# Manager Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Manager Evaluation Form is used to provide a comprehensive review of an employee's performance, focusing on various aspects of their work and behavior. This form is designed to help managers provide accurate and constructive feedback to employees, supporting their growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the employee's performance and behavior during the relevant period, referring to any relevant documentation or records.
2. Carefully fill out the required fields, including "employee_name", "initiative", and "evaluation_date".
3. Provide accurate and clear ratings for numerical fields, such as "performance_rating", "leadership_rating", etc.
4. Offer specific and concrete feedback in the "comments" fields, providing context and explanation for your ratings.
5. Be respectful and constructive in your feedback, avoiding criticism or blame.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (Text, Required): Enter the employee's name.
* **rating** (Numerical, Optional): Provide a rating for the employee's performance, on a scale of 1-5.
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Offer additional context and explanation for your rating.
* **initiative** (Select Multiple, Required): Assess the employee's willingness to take on new challenges and responsibilities.
	+ Strong: The employee consistently takes the lead on new projects and initiatives.
	+ Weak: The employee rarely takes the lead on new projects and initiatives.
	+ Neutral: The employee occasionally takes the lead on new projects and initiatives.
	+ Undecided: The employee is not sure or unable to commit to taking the lead.
* **feedback** (Text, Optional): Provide specific and concrete feedback on the employee's performance.
* **suggestions** (Text, Optional): Offer suggestions for areas the employee can improve.
* **action_plan** (Text, Optional): Outline specific steps the employee can take to address areas for improvement.
* **evaluation_date** (Date, Optional): Record the date of the evaluation.
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Additional comments and context for the evaluation.
