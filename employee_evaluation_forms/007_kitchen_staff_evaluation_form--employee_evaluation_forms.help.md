# kitchen_staff_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kitchen_staff_evaluation_form is a performance evaluation form used to assess the performance of kitchen staff. It's designed to collect feedback from supervisors and help identify areas of improvement for staff development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the Introduction page to get a clear understanding of the evaluation process.
2. Evaluate the performance of the individual using the rating scale provided.
3. Enter the name and email of the supervisor who conducted the evaluation.
4. Enter the contact phone number of the supervisor.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Performance** (Rating Scale, required/optional): 
    Use the rating scale to evaluate the performance of the individual. The scale is used to provide an objective measure of their work quality and areas of improvement.
- **Supervisor** (text, optional): 
    Enter the name of the supervisor who conducted the evaluation.
- **Supervisor Email** (email, optional): 
    Enter the email address of the supervisor who conducted the evaluation.
- **Supervisor Phone** (text, optional): 
    Enter the contact phone number of the supervisor who conducted the evaluation.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are evaluating the individual fairly and objectively.
- Use the rating scale to provide specific feedback on areas of improvement.
- If the individual is a new staff member, please provide constructive feedback that will help them understand their performance expectations.
- If the individual is an experienced staff member, please highlight their strengths and areas that require improvement.
