# Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Evaluation Form is used to evaluate an employee's performance over a specific period of time, providing feedback to the employee and the organization on their progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all required information and materials before starting the evaluation process.
2.  Select the employee you are evaluating (in the "Employee" field).
3.  Choose the "Overall Performance Rating" (1-100) based on the employee's performance.
4.  Enter comments on the employee's performance and areas of improvement in the "Comments" and "Areas For Improvement" fields.
5.  Select the "Target Date" for the evaluation.
6.  Choose the "Evaluation Date" for the evaluation.
7.  Select the "Reviewer Rating" to indicate the reviewer's assessment of the employee's performance.
8.  Enter comments from the reviewer and the employee in the "Reviewer Comments" and "Employee Comments" fields.
9.  Select the "Performance Level" and "Performance Level Type" based on the evaluation.
10. Choose the status of the performance evaluation form as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Performance Evaluation Form Status" field.
11. Select the "Target Performance Level" and "Target Performance Level Type" for the employee.
12. Finally, choose the "Performance Evaluation Form Status" and enter the "Performance Level Scale Range" for the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Performance Rating (1)**: Select a number from 1 to 100 to rate the employee's overall performance.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback on the employee's performance.
* **Strengths**: List the employee's strengths.
* **Areas For Improvement**: List areas where the employee needs improvement.
* **Target Date**: Enter the date by which the employee aims to achieve their goals.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
* **Reviewer Rating**: Choose a number from 1 to 100 to indicate the reviewer's rating.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter comments from the reviewer on the employee's performance.
* **Employee Comments**: Enter comments from the employee on their performance.
* **Performance Level (1)**: Choose a number from 1 to 100 to select the employee's performance level.
* **Performance Level Type (1)**: Select the type of performance level, e.g., High, Low.
* **Performance Level Scale Range (1)**: Enter the performance level scale range, e.g., 1-100.
* **Review Date (1)**: Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Reviewer**: Enter the reviewer's email address.
* **Employee**: Enter the employee's name.
* **Performance Level Type Scale (1)**: Enter the performance level type scale.
* **Target Performance Level (1)**: Enter the target performance level.
* **Target Performance Level Type (1)**: Enter the target performance level type.
* **Performance Evaluation Form Status**: Select the status of the performance evaluation form, e.g., Active or Inactive.
* **Draft (1)**: Enter the draft status of the performance evaluation form.
* **Submitted (1)**: Enter the submitted status of the performance evaluation form.
* **Completed (1)**: Enter the completed status of the performance evaluation form.
* **Cancelled (1)**: Enter the cancelled status of the performance evaluation form.
* **Performance Level Scale (1)**: Enter the performance level scale.
* **Performance Level Type Scale (1)**: Enter the performance level type scale.
* **Review Date (1)**: Enter the review date.
