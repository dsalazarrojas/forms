# Employee Self Appraisal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Self Appraisal Form is a tool used for employees to assess and evaluate their own performance and progress. It helps employees reflect on their strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement, and to set goals and objectives for future development. This form is meant to be filled out by employees to provide a self-assessment of their performance and progress, and can be used in conjunction with manager evaluations to create a comprehensive performance review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title field with a clear and concise title for the appraisal.
2. Select the relevant category from the drop-down menu to categorize the appraisal.
3. Enter a brief description of the appraisal in the description field.
4. Enter the due date for the appraisal in the due date field.
5. Enter the name of the manager who will be reviewing the appraisal.
6. Choose a rating for the manager's evaluation (High, Medium, or Low).
7. Enter any comments from the manager in the manager comment field.
8. Enter your own name as the employee filling out the form.
9. Choose a rating for your own evaluation (High, Medium, or Low).
10. Enter any comments from your own evaluation in the employee comment field.
11. Enter the name of the person who completed the form.
12. Enter the date and time when the form was completed.
13. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form (if applicable).
14. Enter the date and time when the form was reviewed.
15. Select the current status of the form (Draft, Approved, or Rejected).
16. Enter the version number of the form.
17. Select the current status of the form (Draft, Approved, or Rejected).
18. Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
19. Enter the date and time when the form was updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Self Appraisal Form** (1): Enter a clear and concise title for the appraisal.
* **Category** (2): Select a category for the appraisal from the drop-down menu (e.g., Employee Evaluation Forms, Other).
* **Description** (3): Enter a brief description of the appraisal.
* **Due Date** (4): Enter the date by which the appraisal is due.
* **Manager Name** (5): Enter the name of the manager who will be reviewing the appraisal.
* **Manager Rating** (6): Choose a rating for the manager's evaluation (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Manager Comment** (7): Enter any comments from the manager in the manager comment field.
* **Employee Name** (8): Enter your own name as the employee filling out the form.
* **Employee Rating** (9): Choose a rating for your own evaluation (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Employee Comment** (10): Enter any comments from your own evaluation in the employee comment field.
* **Completed By** (11): Enter the name of the person who completed the form.
* **Completed Date** (12): Enter the date when the form was completed.
* **Completed Time** (13): Enter the time when the form was completed.
* **Employee Self Appraisal Form Completed By 2** (14): Enter the name of the person who completed the form (this field is a duplicate of field 11, but may be used for different purposes).
* **Reviewed By** (15): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form (if applicable).
* **Reviewed Date** (16): Enter the date when the form was reviewed.
* **Reviewed Time** (17): Enter the time when the form was reviewed.
* **Employee Self Appraisal Form Reviewed By 2** (18): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form (this field is a duplicate of field 15, but may be used for different purposes).
* **Status** (19): Select the current status of the form (Draft, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Version** (20): Enter the version number of the form.
* **Version Number** (21): Enter the version number of the form (this field may be used for different purposes).
* **Employee Self Appraisal Form Status 2** (22): Select the current status of the form (Draft, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Updated By** (23): Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
* **Updated Date** (24): Enter the date when the form was updated.
* **Updated Time** (25): Enter the time when the form was updated.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Keep in mind that this form is a self-assessment tool, and your ratings and comments should reflect your honest and objective evaluation of your performance.
