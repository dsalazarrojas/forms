# Performance Appraisal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Appraisal Form is designed to provide a structured framework for evaluating employee performance, identifying areas of improvement, and setting goals for growth and development. It helps employees and supervisors track progress and stay on track with performance expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Employee Name
	* Employee ID
	* Start Date
	* End Date
	* Evaluation Period
2. Select the relevant options:
	* Performance Rating
	* Performance Rating Scale
	* Performance Improvement Plan
3. Provide feedback and comments:
	* Comments (for the employee)
	* Employee Comments (for the employee)
	* Reviewer Comments (for the reviewer)
4. Review and sign off:
	* Reviewer Signature
	* Employee Signature

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their personnel record.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the employee's ID number as assigned by HR.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the evaluation period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the evaluation period.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, text, required): Enter the period during which the employee's performance is being evaluated.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, optional): Select the overall performance rating from 'Good' and 'Fair'.
* **Performance Rating Scale** (`performance_rating_scale`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more ratings from 'Good', 'Fair'.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, number, optional): Enter the rating for category 1 (e.g., Sales, Productivity).
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, number, optional): Enter the rating for category 2 (e.g., Quality, Customer Service).
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, number, optional): Enter the rating for category 3 (e.g., Communication, Teamwork).
* **Rating 4** (`rating_4`, number, optional): Enter the rating for category 4 (e.g., Leadership, Initiative).
* **Rating 5** (`rating_5`, number, optional): Enter the rating for category 5 (e.g., Attendance, Punctuality).
* **Performance Improvement Plan** (`performance_improvement_plan`, text, optional): Enter a plan of action for the employee's performance improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any general comments on the employee's performance.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments from the employee on their performance.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments from the reviewer on the employee's performance.
* **Review Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **Performance Improvement Plan Status** (`performance_improvement_plan_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the Performance Improvement Plan from 'Active' and 'Inactive'.
* **Evaluation Form Status** (`evaluation_form_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the evaluation form from 'Active' and 'Inactive'.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's signature.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): Enter the employee's signature.

Note: Required fields are marked as required.
