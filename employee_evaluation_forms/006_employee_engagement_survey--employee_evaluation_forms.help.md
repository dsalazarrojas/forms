# Employee Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on employee engagement and satisfaction. It's a self-reporting tool to help the management team identify areas of improvement in various departments and create a better work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your employee name in the **Employee Name** field.
2.  Select your department from the list of options provided in the **department** field.
3.  Enter your job title in the **job title** field.
4.  Rate your satisfaction level with a number in the **Satisfaction** field.
5.  Choose your response to the question about communication in the **Communication** field.
6.  Provide any additional feedback or comments in the **any additional feedback** field.
7.  Enter your manager's name in the **Manager name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ID card or official documents.
*   **department** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the department you belong to (Human Resources, Accounting, Engineering, etc.).
*   **job title** (text, required: false): Enter your official job title.
*   **Satisfaction** (number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with your work experience. For example, 5 is "Very Satisfied" and 1 is "Very Dissatisfied".
*   **Communication** (select_one, required: false): Choose how you feel about the communication style in your department. For example, Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither, Somewhat Disagree, Strongly Disagree.
*   **any additional feedback** (note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your work experience.
*   **Manager name** (text, required: false): Enter your manager's name. 

## Tips

*   Be honest and thoughtful in your responses.
*   Provide specific examples when giving feedback.
*   Keep your responses concise and clear.
*   Review the form before submitting to ensure you've completed it accurately.
