# Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for restaurant employees to evaluate their team members or supervisors based on their performance, feedback, and overall score.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose the appropriate rating category for your evaluation: "Yes" or "No".
2. Enter the name of the person you are evaluating, the restaurant staff member.
3. Select the date of the evaluation.
4. Choose who is conducting the evaluation: "Manager", "Supervisor", "HR", or "Staff".
5. Provide any comments or feedback for the evaluation.
6. Enter any email address for further communication.
7. Enter a phone number for further communication or reference.
8. Choose your rating for the employee's performance: "Good" or "Fair".
9. Select the corresponding rating scale for the evaluation.
10. Enter the time spent on the evaluation.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when evaluating your team members or supervisors.
* Ensure all fields are filled in correctly to avoid any errors.
* If unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
* Keep all evaluations professional and respectful.
