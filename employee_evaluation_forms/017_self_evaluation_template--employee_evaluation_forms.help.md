# self_evaluation_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The self-evaluation template is a form designed to help employees reflect on their job performance, goals, and areas for improvement. It's a self-assessment tool to help employees evaluate their own progress and identify areas that need attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Be honest and objective in your assessment.
