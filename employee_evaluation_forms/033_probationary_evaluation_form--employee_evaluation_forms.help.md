# Probationary Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Probationary Evaluation Form is a tool used by management to assess the progress and satisfaction of new employees during their probationary period. It helps to identify areas of improvement and provides an opportunity for open and honest feedback between the employee and management. The form is designed to be completed by the new employee and returned to management for review and discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Please select the options that best describe your situation and feelings.
3. Be specific when providing feedback and reasons for your answers.
4. If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to your management representative for guidance and support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How are you adjusting to your new role?** (`new_employment_status`): Please describe how you are finding your new role. Are you adjusting well, or do you need more time?
* **How satisfied are you with your new job?** (`job_satisfaction`): Rate your level of satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being not satisfied and 5 being very satisfied. Please explain your reason for your rating.
* **How would you describe your new workplace environment?** (`new_workplace_environment`): Describe the atmosphere and culture of your new workplace.
* **How have you received support for your success in your new role?** (`support_for_success`): Describe any support you have received, or lack thereof, from management and colleagues.
* **How would you describe communication with management in your new role?** (`communication_with_management`): Describe the quality and effectiveness of communication with your manager and other members of management.
* **How would you describe feedback from management in your new role?** (`feedback_from_management`): Describe the type and frequency of feedback you have received from management.
* **Why are you satisfied or dissatisfied with your new job?** (`job_satisfaction_reason`): If you are not satisfied, please explain the reasons why.
* **Overall, how do you feel about your new role?** (`overall_feeling`): Rate your overall feeling about your new role on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being not satisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **How will your employment status change in the next 6-12 months?** (`new_employment_status_change`): Select all that apply (full-time, part-time, contract, freelancer).
* **What is your reason for the expected change in employment status?** (`reason_for_change`): Please explain the reason for your expected change in employment status.
* **How would you describe communication with your colleagues?** (`communication_with_colleagues`): Describe the quality and effectiveness of communication with your colleagues.
* **How would you describe your team's collaboration?** (`team_work`): Describe the level of collaboration and teamwork within your team.
* **How would you describe your job description?** (`job_description`): Describe the tasks and responsibilities assigned to you in your new role.
* **What are you expecting from your new role?** (`new_role_expectation`): Describe your expectations and goals for your new role.
* **Support For Success 2** (`support_for_success_2`): Describe any additional support you have received or need for success in your role.
* **Job Satisfaction Reason 2** (`job_satisfaction_reason_2`): If you are not satisfied, please explain the reasons why.
* **Overall Feeling 2** (`overall_feeling_2`): Rate your overall feeling about your new role on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being not satisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **New Employment Status Change Two** (`new_employment_status_change_two`): Select all that apply (full-time, part-time, contract, freelancer).
* **Reason For Change Two** (`reason_for_change_two`): Please explain the reason for your expected change in employment status.
* **Communication With Colleagues Two** (`communication_with_colleagues_two`): Describe the quality and effectiveness of communication with your colleagues.
* **Team Work Two** (`team_work_two`): Describe the level of collaboration and teamwork within your team.
* **Job Description Two** (`job_description_two`): Describe the tasks and responsibilities assigned to you in your new role.
* **New Role Expectation Two** (`new_role_expectation_two`): Describe your expectations and goals for your new role.
* **Support For Success Two** (`support_for_success_two`): Describe any additional support you have received or need for success in your role.
* **How would you describe feedback from management?** (`feedback_from_management_two`): Describe the type and frequency of feedback you have received from management.
