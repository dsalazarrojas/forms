# st_pete_pier_ambassador_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in becoming a St. Pete Pier Ambassador, a group of volunteers who help share the history and stories of the pier with visitors. The form will help us understand your background, interests, and availability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all required fields and make sure to double-check your submissions for accuracy. The form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields that we'll use to assess your eligibility and suitability for the role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, required): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Confirm Phone** (`phone_confirmation`, text, required): Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Confirm Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth_confirmation`, date, required): Confirm your date of birth to ensure accuracy.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age, which will help us understand your background.
* **Confirm Age** (`age_confirmation`, number, required): Confirm your age for verification purposes.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter your zip code for location verification.
* **Confirm Zip** (`zip_confirmation`, number, required): Confirm your zip code to ensure accuracy.
* **What is your purpose for visiting the pier?** (`select_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ I want to learn about the pier and city history
	+ I am a local and I love the pier
	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Other (Please specify)** (`select_purpose_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" above, provide a brief description of your purpose.
* **What are you most interested in while visiting the pier?** (`select_interests`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options that apply:
	+ Art and Culture
	+ Food and Drink
	+ History and Architecture
	+ Nature and Outdoors
	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Select Interests Other** (`select_interests_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" above, provide a brief description of your interests.
* **Have you uploaded your documents?** (`upload_documents`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Upload Documents Other** (`upload_documents_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" above, provide a description of your documents.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Verify Email** (`email_verification`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following:
	+ Verify
	+ Not verify
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are available to visit the pier.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are available to visit the pier.
* **Day** (`select_day`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Select Day Other** (`select_day_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" above, provide a brief description of your day.
* **Week** (`select_week`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple time slots:
	+ 8:00-10:00 AM
	+ 10:00-12:00 PM
	+ 12:00-2:00 PM
	+ 2:00-4:00 PM
	+ 4:00-6:00 PM
	+ 6:00-8:00 PM
	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Select Week Other** (`select_week_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" above, provide a brief description of your week.
