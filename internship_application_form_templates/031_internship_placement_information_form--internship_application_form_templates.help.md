# Internship Placement Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about students' internship placement details. The form is designed to collect necessary details about the students' names, contact information, academic background, and internship specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Provide the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
4. Enter the name of the university the student is attending in the "University Name" field.
5. Choose the internship duration in the "Internship Duration" field (if applicable).
6. Select the type of internship the student is applying for in the "Internship Type" field.
7. Enter the expected start and end dates of the internship in the "Internship Start Date" and "Internship End Date" fields.
8. Provide the expected graduation date of the student in the "Expected Graduation Date" field.
9. Select the student's major and minor fields of study in the "Student Major" and "Student Minor" fields.
10. Write a brief description of the internship in the "Internship Details" field.
11. Enter the address of the university the student is attending in the "University Address" field.
12. Enter the student's address in the "Student Address" field.
13. Select the city where the student lives in the "Student City" field.
14. Choose the state where the student lives in the "Student State" field.
15. Enter the student's zip code in the "Student Zip" field.
16. Select the state of the university the student is attending in the "University State" field.
17. Enter the city of the university the student is attending in the "University City" field.
18. Enter the zip code of the university the student is attending in the "University Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, optional): Enter the full name of the student.
* **Student Email** (Student Email, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (Student Phone, text, optional): Provide the student's phone number.
* **University Name** (University Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the university the student is attending.
* **Internship Duration** (Internship Duration, number, optional): Choose the length of the internship (if applicable).
* **Internship Type** (Internship Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of internship the student is applying for.
* **Internship Start Date** (Internship Start Date, date, optional): Enter the expected start date of the internship.
* **Internship End Date** (Internship End Date, date, optional): Enter the expected end date of the internship.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (Expected Graduation Date, date, optional): Provide the expected graduation date of the student.
* **Student Major** (Student Major, text, optional): Select the student's major field of study.
* **Student Minor** (Student Minor, text, optional): Select the student's minor field of study.
* **Internship Details** (Internship Details, note, optional): Write a brief description of the internship.
* **University Address** (University Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the university the student is attending.
* **Student Address** (Student Address, text, optional): Enter the student's address.
* **Student City** (Student City, text, optional): Select the city where the student lives.
* **Student State** (Student State, text, optional): Choose the state where the student lives.
* **Student Zip** (Student Zip, text, optional): Enter the student's zip code.
* **University State** (University State, text, optional): Select the state of the university the student is attending.
* **University City** (University City, text, optional): Enter the city of the university the student is attending.
* **University Zip** (University Zip, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the university the student is attending.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authority for clarification.
