# summer_internship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Summer Internship Application Form is a tool to help applicants provide their information and qualifications for a summer internship opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and enter your information accurately.
2. Provide all required information (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Ensure that your answers are clear and concise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page (first_page)** 
    * Label: first_page 
    * Description: This field is the first page of the form.

* **Internship Details (internship_details)**
    * Label: internship_details 
    * Description: This field is for you to provide any additional details about your internship goals and expectations. Please be specific about the type of internship you're looking for and what you hope to gain from this experience.

* **Education Details (education_details)**
    * Label: education_details 
    * Description: This field is for you to provide more information about your educational background, including relevant courses, degrees, or other educational experiences.

* **Education Background (education_background)**
    * Label: education_background 
    * Description: This field is for you to describe your educational background, including degrees, institutions, or programs you've completed or are currently pursuing.

* **Academic Record (academic_record)**
    * Label: academic_record 
    * Description: This field is for you to provide your academic record, including GPA, relevant courses, or academic achievements.

* **Work Experience (work_experience)**
    * Label: work_experience 
    * Description: This field is for you to provide any relevant work experience, including job titles, duration, and skills gained.

* **Availability (availability)**
    * Label: availability 
    * Description: This field is for you to indicate your availability to start the internship. Please enter the number of weeks you expect to be available.

* **Expected Start Date (expected_start_date)**
    * Label: expected_start_date 
    * Description: This field is for you to enter the expected start date of the internship.

* **Expected End Date (expected_end_date)**
    * Label: expected_end_date 
    * Description: This field is for you to enter the expected end date of the internship.

* **Expected Duration (expected_duration)**
    * Label: expected_duration 
    * Description: This field is for you to indicate the expected duration of the internship in weeks.

* **Expected Hours Per Week (expected_hours_per_week)**
    * Label: expected_hours_per_week 
    * Description: This field is for you to indicate the expected number of hours per week you'll work.

* **Expected Number of Hours (expected_number_of_hours)**
    * Label: expected_number_of_hours 
    * Description: This field is for you to indicate the expected total number of hours for the internship.

* **Internship Description (internship_description)**
    * Label: internship_description 
    * Description: This field is for you to describe the internship you're applying for, including its purpose and goals.

* **Skills (skills)**
    * Label: skills 
    * Description: This field is for you to list any relevant skills you have, including programming languages, software proficiency, or other relevant abilities.

Note: Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and relevant to your qualifications for the internship.
