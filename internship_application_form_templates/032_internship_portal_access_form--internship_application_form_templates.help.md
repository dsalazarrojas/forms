# Internship Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Portal Access Form is a form for students to request access to the internship portal. This form is designed to collect necessary information to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details: Enter your student name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your internship-specific information: Enter the name of the internship, your job position, and the company.
3. Set up your access settings: Choose your start and end dates, and select your preferred access frequency and type.
4. Add any other requests or comments: If you have any additional requirements or questions, use the "Other Requests" field to include them.
5. Get signatures: A student and staff signature are required to process your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter your full name as a student.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the name you are known by in the university.
- **Student Email**: Enter your university-issued email address.
  * Type: Email
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the email address we use to contact you.
- **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is your contact number for any further questions.
- **Internship Name**: Enter the name of the internship you are applying for.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the name of the internship as listed in the portal.
- **Job Position**: Enter your job position in the internship.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is your job title in the internship.
- **Company**: Enter the name of the company you are working with.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the name of the company hosting the internship.
- **Start Date**: Enter the date you started the internship.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the date you began the internship.
- **End Date**: Enter the date you ended the internship.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the date the internship ended.
- **Duration**: Enter the total duration of the internship in weeks or days.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the total number of weeks or days you worked.
- **Access Frequency**: Choose how often you accessed the portal.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: Select how often you plan to access the portal.
- **Access Type**: Choose the type of access you need.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: Select all that apply to describe your access needs.
- **Other Requests**: Include any additional requirements or questions.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Use this field to provide any extra information or questions.
- **Student Signature**: Provide your signature to confirm your request.
  * Type: Note
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is your agreement to the terms and conditions.
- **Faculty Signature**: Provide the signature of the faculty member involved.
  * Type: Note
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the agreement of the faculty member involved.
- **Staff Signature**: Provide the signature of the staff member involved.
  * Type: Note
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the agreement of the staff member involved.
- **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the date the form was submitted.
- **Date Reviewed**: Enter the date the form was reviewed by staff.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: This is the date the form was reviewed.
