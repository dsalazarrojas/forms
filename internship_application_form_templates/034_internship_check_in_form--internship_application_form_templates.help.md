# internship_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for interns to check in with their supervisor or manager during their internship. It helps ensure they are on track and provides an opportunity for feedback and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Form** with any relevant details.
2. Provide **Internship Details**, such as your current progress and any challenges you're facing.
3. Choose your **Check-In Frequency**, indicating how often you'd like to check in with your supervisor.
4. Add any **Feedback** on your experience so far.
5. Enter **Comments** if you have anything else you'd like to share.
6. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** of your internship.
7. Choose who you want to be able to **Submit** the form on your behalf.
8. Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** when you submit the form.
9. Provide your **Email** address for easy communication.
10. Enter your **Phone** number so your supervisor can reach out if needed.
11. Add any **Notes** about your internship.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form regularly to keep your supervisor informed of your progress.
* Be honest and open in your feedback and comments to ensure you're getting the most out of your internship.
* If you're unsure about anything, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor for guidance.
