# Donor Experience Internship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Experience Internship Application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in gaining hands-on experience in donor engagement and stewardship. By providing accurate and honest answers, applicants will help us better understand their qualifications and fit for the internship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and other requested details to get started.
2.  Provide your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
3.  Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
4.  Share your educational background to help us understand your qualifications.
5.  Briefly describe your work experience, highlighting any relevant experience you have had in donor engagement or stewardship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (1): Enter your full name, which will be used to address you throughout the application process.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Email** (2): Provide a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about your application.
    *   **Type:** Email
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Phone** (3): Enter your contact phone number so we can get in touch with you if needed.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Education** (4): Share your educational background, highlighting any relevant degrees or certifications.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Work Experience** (5): Briefly describe any previous experience you have had in donor engagement or stewardship, or any other relevant work experience.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, contact us at [insert contact info].
*   The email address you provide will be used only for communication purposes related to this application.
