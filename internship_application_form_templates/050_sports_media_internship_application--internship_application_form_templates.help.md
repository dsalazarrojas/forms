# Sports Media Internship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Sports Media Internship Application form. Please ensure you read and understand the purpose of this form before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the **Applicant Information** page with your basic details.
*   Provide your **Contact Information** on a separate page.
*   Share your **Education History** in a concise format.
*   Describe your relevant **Work Experience**.
*   Upload your **Resume** in PDF format.
*   Select your relevant **Skills** from the provided options.
*   Choose your **Language Proficiency**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Applicant Information**: This section is used to capture your basic details such as your name and any other information you'd like to provide about yourself.
*   **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact details such as email, phone number, or any other way we can reach out to you.
*   **Education History**: This section is used to understand your educational background and qualifications.
*   **Work Experience**: Share your relevant work experience that aligns with the internship position you're applying for.
*   **Resume**: Please upload your resume in PDF format as requested in the hint.
*   **Skills**: Select all the skills you have from the options provided. These skills will be considered when evaluating your application.
*   **Language Proficiency**: Choose all the languages you are proficient in from the options provided.
