# online-internship-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and professional information from students or young professionals applying for an internship position with our company. It's crucial to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as it will be reviewed as part of the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form instructions and requirements carefully before starting to fill out the form.
2. Answer each question truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to attach any required documents or files as specified in the instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal details** (`Personal details`, `text`, required: false): This section is meant to gather your personal details such as your name, address, and any other relevant information that you feel is necessary to share.
- **Contact info** (`Contact info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
- **Education** (`Education`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your highest level of education achieved. You can choose from Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, or Doctoral degree.
- **Skills** (`Skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the skills you possess that are relevant to the internship position. You can select multiple skills from the options provided.
- **Availability** (`Availability`, `date`, required: false): If you're available to start the internship program, select the date you're available to begin.
- **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `text`, required: false): Share your previous work experience that's relevant to the internship position.
- **Referees** (`Referees`, `text`, required: false): List the names and contact information of your referees who can vouch for your skills and experience.
- **Referees info** (`Referees info`, `text`, required: false): Provide more information about your referees, such as their job titles or relationship with you.
- **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required: false): This is the final step to submit your application. Make sure to review your application before clicking the submit button.
- **Upload files** (`Upload files`, `note`, required: false): Attach any required documents or files as specified in the instructions.
- **Upload files 2** (`Upload files 2`, `note`, required: false): This is a second opportunity to attach any additional files or documents.
- **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): Leave any additional comments or notes about your application.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
- **Upload files 3** (`Upload files 3`, `note`, required: false): This is the last chance to attach any other required documents or files.

Note that the following fields are not required for completion: Personal details, Education, Skills, Availability, Work Experience, Referees, Referees info, Upload files, Upload files 2, Upload files 3, Email, Phone, and Notes. However, it's essential to fill out these fields truthfully and accurately as they can provide valuable information about your application. 

## Tips
- Make sure to attach all required documents or files as specified in the instructions.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Be as detailed as possible in your work experience and referees sections.
- Use the email address and phone number you're willing to use for communication.
- Review your application carefully before submitting it.
