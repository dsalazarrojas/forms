# Summer Internship Application Form CEOs Of Tomorrow - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential interns who are interested in participating in the "CEOs of Tomorrow" summer internship program. The data collected will be used to review and select candidates for the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you provide all required fields (if any).
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Info (Program Info)**: This field is not required and serves as a placeholder for additional information about the program.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)**: Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Guardian Information (Guardian Information)**: This field is likely intended to collect information about a parent or guardian's contact information.
* **Quick Quiz (Quick Quiz)**: Choose "Yes" if you are interested in participating in the program, "No" otherwise.
* **Availability (Availability)**: Enter the date when you are available to start the internship.
* **Availability 2 (Availability 2)**: Enter the time when you are available to start the internship.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submit (Submit)**: This is a required field and should be filled out before submission.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submission. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the program managers.
