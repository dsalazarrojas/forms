# Internship Acceptance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare the acceptance of an internship opportunity by a student. It captures essential information about the internship's start and end dates, student details, and the status of the internship acceptance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are using this form for its intended purpose - declaring the acceptance of an internship opportunity.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Internship Start Date (internship_start_date)**: Enter the date when the internship is expected to begin. If you are unsure, enter the expected start date.
* **Internship End Date (internship_end_date)**: Enter the date when the internship is expected to end. If you are unsure, enter the expected end date.
* **Student Acceptance Date (student_acceptance_date)**: Enter the date the student is accepting the internship.
* **Internship Title (internship_title)**: Enter the title of the internship, for example, "Software Development Intern" or "Marketing Intern".
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the name of the student accepting the internship, as it appears on their ID or official documents.
* **Student Email (student_email)**: Enter the student's email address, as it is registered with the university or institution.
* **Student Phone (student_phone)**: Enter a valid phone number for the student, where they can be reached.
* **Internship Acceptance Status (internship_acceptance_status)**: Select the status of the internship, either "Active" (indicating the internship has been accepted) or "Inactive" (indicating it has not been accepted).
* **Internship Supervisor Signature (internship_supervisor_signature)**: This field is not required, but if you need to attach a note, this is where you would enter it.
