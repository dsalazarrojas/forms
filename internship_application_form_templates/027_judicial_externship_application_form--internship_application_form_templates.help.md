# Judicial Externship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Judicial Externship Application Form is designed to collect necessary information and documentation from applicants for judicial externship placements or programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
2. You will be asked to upload various documents, such as agreements, resumes, and transcripts. Please make sure to upload the correct and relevant documents for each field.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting the form to ensure that all required information is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, `text`, **required: false**): This section is for you to provide your personal contact and demographic information.
* **Externship Details** (`externship_details`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe your experience, skills, and qualifications relevant to the externship you are applying for.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, `text`, **required: false**): Provide details about your educational background and credentials.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Externship Commitments** (`externship_commitments`, `text`, **required: false**): This section is for you to declare your commitment to the externship and your understanding of the program's expectations.
* **Upload Externship Agreement** (`upload_externship_agreement`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload a copy of the externship agreement or contract, if applicable.
* **Upload Other Document** (`upload_other_document`, `text`, **required: false**): This field is for you to upload any other relevant documents, such as letters of recommendation or evaluation forms.
* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload your resume or CV to provide a summary of your professional experience.
* **Upload Letter of Interest** (`upload_letter_of_interest`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload a letter expressing your interest in the externship program.
* **Upload Transcript** (`upload_transcript`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload your transcript or academic record, if applicable.
* **Upload External Link** (`upload_external_link`, `text`, **required: false**): This field is for you to upload any external links or references that are relevant to your application.
* **Upload Other Document 2** (`upload_other_document2`, `text`, **required: false**): This field is for you to upload any other relevant documents, such as additional letters of recommendation.
* **Upload Certification** (`upload_certification`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload any relevant certifications or credentials.
* **Upload License** (`upload_license`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload any relevant licenses or permits.
* **Upload Degree** (`upload_degree`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload proof of your degree or academic credentials.
* **Upload Externship Agreement Template** (`upload_externship_agreement_template`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload a template for the externship agreement or contract.
* **Review Form** (`review_form`, `text`, **required: false**): This field is for you to review the form before submission.
* **Upload Letter of Recommendation** (`upload_letter_of_recommendation`, `text`, **required: false`): Upload a letter of recommendation from a relevant individual or organization.
* **Upload Supervisor Evaluation** (`upload_supervisor_evaluation`, `text`, **required: false`): Upload an evaluation or assessment from a supervisor or mentor.
* **Upload Other Document 3** (`upload_other_document3`, `text`, **required: false`): This field is for you to upload any other relevant documents.
* **Upload Externship Program** (`upload_externship_program`, `text`, **required: false`): Upload information about the externship program or opportunity.
* **Upload Externship Agreement Signed** (`upload_externship_agreement_signed`, `text`, **required: false`): Upload a signed copy of the externship agreement or contract.
* **Review Form 2** (`review_form2`, `text`, **required: false`): This field is for you to review the form before submission.
* **Upload Other Document 4** (`upload_other_document4`, `text`, **required: false`): This field is for you to upload any other relevant documents.

Please note that some fields may not be explicitly labeled, so you can assume the field label as a description of the field's purpose. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, feel free to contact the form creator or the externship program administrator.
