# public_health_informatics_fellowship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applying to the Public Health Informatics Fellowship. It collects general information, experience, motivation, and supporting documents from applicants. The purpose of this form is to assess the qualifications and fit for the fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form
Before you start, make sure to have all the necessary information ready, such as your resume, CV, and a brief motivation statement. The form is split into several sections:

- General Information: Provide a brief introduction and contact information.
- Experience: Describe your relevant work experience, especially in the field of public health informatics.
- Motivation: Explain why you are a good fit for the fellowship program.
- Resume/CV: Upload your resume and CV to showcase your qualifications.
- Additional Info: Provide any additional information that might be relevant to your application.
- References: List your professional references.
- Skills: Select the skills you have, especially in data science, programming, and data visualization.
- Research Background: Describe any relevant research experience or projects.
- Education: List your educational background, especially in relevant fields.
- Work Experience: Describe your relevant work experience.
- Awards & Certifications: List any relevant awards or certifications.
- Language: Select your spoken language.
- Motivation (again): Explain your motivation for applying to the fellowship program again, this time selecting specific reasons from the given options.
