# Audio Postproduction Fellowship Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates interested in participating in the Audio Postproduction Fellowship program. The fellowship aims to provide a chance to develop skills in audio postproduction, and this form is used to assess the applicant's qualifications, motivation, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the personal details section, which includes your full name, educational background, and contact information.
2. Proceed to the technical proficiency section, where you will select your relevant software skills and mention any experience with plugin suites.
3. In the portfolio and motivation section, provide a link to your portfolio or showreel, and write a brief motivation letter explaining why you are interested in this fellowship.
4. Finally, in the availability and specialization section, select your weekly availability and preferred area of specialization within audio postproduction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **University or Academic Affiliation** (`educational_background`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current or most recent academic institution.
* **Year of Graduation** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required): Provide the year you graduated or expect to graduate.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Fill in your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Primary Software Proficiency** (`software_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the audio postproduction software you are proficient in, such as Pro Tools, Izotope RX, or Adobe Audition.
* **Familiarity with Plugin Suites** (`plugins_experience`, `text`, optional): If you have experience with plugin suites like Waves, FabFilter, or Soundtoys, mention it here.
* **Experience with Sound Design and Foley** (`sound_design_exp`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of experience in sound design and foley, from beginner to advanced levels.
* **Link to Portfolio or Showreel** (`portfolio_link`, `text`, required): Provide a URL to your portfolio or showreel, where we can see your work.
* **Why do you want this fellowship?** (`motivation_letter`, `text`, required): Write a brief letter explaining why you are interested in this fellowship and what you hope to achieve.
* **Weekly Availability** (`internship_availability`, `select_one`, required): Select how many hours per week you are able to dedicate to the fellowship.
* **Preferred Area of Specialization** (`specialization_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred area within audio postproduction, such as dialogue and ADR editing, sound effects and sound design, or mixing and mastering.
* **Academic or Professional Reference Name** (`reference_name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of a reference who can vouch for your skills and character.
* **Reference Contact Information** (`reference_contact`, `text`, required): Leave your reference's contact information for verification purposes.
