# internship_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Authorization Form is a tool used to collect information from applicants for internal internship placements within an organization. This form is designed to be completed by applicants to provide their details and confirm their understanding of the internship placement and agreement terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details as an applicant.
2. Provide your supervisor's details if required.
3. Select the internship placement you are applying for.
4. Review and agree to the terms of the internship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Details
* **Applicant Details** (`Applicant Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details as an applicant.

### Supervisor Details
* **Supervisor Details** (`Supervisor Details`, `text`, required: false): If your supervisor is involved in your internship, enter their name and title.

### Internship Placement
* **Internship Placement** (`Internship Placement`, `text`, required: false): Select the specific internship placement you are applying for.

### Agreement Terms
* **Agreement Terms** (`Agreement Terms`, `text`, required: false): Read through and agree to the terms of the internship by selecting "Yes" or "No".
