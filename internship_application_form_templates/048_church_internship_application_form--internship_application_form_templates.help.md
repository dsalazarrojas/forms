# Church Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Internship Application Form is designed to gather information from potential interns interested in joining the church's internship program. This form helps the church evaluate your qualifications, interests, and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2.  If you're unsure about any question, feel free to contact the church's internship team for clarification.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`applicant_name_intern`, `text`, required): Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email Address** (`applicant_email_intern`, `email`, required): A valid email address where the church can contact you.
- **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone_intern`, `text`, required): Your phone number where the church can reach you.
- **Current School or Institution** (`current_school_institution`, `text`, required): The name of your current school or institution.
- **Major or Area of Study** (`degree_focus_area`, `text`, required): Your major or area of study related to your intended internship track.
- **Current Year in Program** (`year_in_program`, `select_one`, required): Select your current year in your academic program.
- **Preferred Internship Track** (`internship_track_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary interest area for the internship, such as Youth Ministry, Worship and Music, and so on.
- **Intended Term** (`term_of_internship`, `select_one`, required): Select the term you plan to intern, whether it's Summer, Fall, Spring, or Full Year.
- **Weekly Hours Commitment** (`weekly_hours_availability`, `number`, required): Specify how many hours you are available to commit per week for the internship.
- **Faith Journey Brief** (`faith_journey_brief_intern`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your faith journey and call to ministry.
- **Previous Ministry Experience** (`previous_ministry_experience_intern`, `text`, required): List any previous ministry or volunteer experience you've had.
- **Top 3 Goals for Internship** (`intern_learning_goals`, `text`, required): Specify your top 3 goals for this internship.
- **References** (`personal_references_intern`, `text`, required): Provide the names and contact information for two references, one of which must be a pastor or professor.
- **Background Check Authorization** (`background_check_auth_intern`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you authorize a background check as part of the application process.
- **Date of Application** (`application_date_intern`, `date`, required): The date you submit this application form.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be completed to ensure a successful application.
