# Internship Leave Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Internship Leave Application Form is a form used by employees to apply for leave during their internship period. It helps the supervisor to gather necessary information about the leave request and determine the approval status.

## How To Complete This Form

- To start, select the "Application Date" field and enter the date when you submitted the leave application form.
- Fill in your "Employee Name" and provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
- In the "Leave Reason" field, explain the reason for your leave in brief.
- Select the "Leave From" and "Leave To" fields to specify the dates of your leave.
- Enter the "Leave Duration" field to specify how many days you will be away.
- Enter the "Return Date" field to specify the date when you will return to work.
- Select your "Supervisor Name" and enter their contact information.
- Choose the "Approval Status" to indicate the current status of your leave request.
- You can add any additional comments in the "Notes" field.
- Select your "Department" where you work, and "Job Title" to help the supervisor understand your role and responsibilities.
- Select your "Role" to indicate whether you are a supervisor, team member, or intern.
- Select your "Status" to indicate whether you are active, inactive, or pending.
- Select your "Location" to indicate whether you are working from office, remote, or hybrid.
- Select your "Shift" to indicate which shift you are working.
- Enter your "Manager Name" for your direct supervisor.
- Enter your "Approval Date" if the approval process is complete.
- Enter any additional comments about your leave request in the "Comments" field.
- Enter your "Employee ID" for identification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (1, date, required): Enter the date when you submitted this form.
* **Employee Name** (2, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID card.
* **Leave Reason** (3, text, required): Provide a brief explanation of the reason for your leave.
* **Leave From** (4, date, required): Specify the date you want to leave from work.
* **Leave To** (5, date, required): Specify the date you want to return to work.
* **Leave Duration** (6, number, required): Enter the number of days you will be away.
* **Return Date** (7, date, required): Specify the date you will return to work.
* **Supervisor Name** (8, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Approval Status** (9, select_multiple, required): Select the current status of your leave request.
* **Notes** (10, text, required): Add any additional comments about your leave request.
* **Email** (11, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (12, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (13, select_one, required): Select the department where you work.
* **Job Title** (14, select_one, required): Select your job title.
* **Role** (15, select_one, required): Select your role.
* **Status** (16, select_one, required): Select your status.
* **Location** (17, select_one, required): Select your location.
* **Shift** (18, select_one, required): Select your shift.
* **Manager Name** (19, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Approval Date** (20, date, required): Enter the date when the approval process is complete.
* **Comments** (21, text, required): Enter any additional comments about your leave request.
* **Employee ID** (22, text, required): Enter your employee ID.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your dates and duration to ensure they are correct.
- If you have any doubts or questions, contact your supervisor or HR department.
