# Internship Placement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employers to request authorization to place students in internships. The form collects necessary information about the student and employer for the internship placement process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form fields as prompted. If a field is marked as required, it is necessary to fill it out.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the student such as name and ID number. Please provide any relevant details about the student.

* **Employer Information** (`employer_information`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the employer such as company name and contact details. Please provide any relevant details about the employer.

* **Placement Details** (`placement_details`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the internship placement such as job title and duration. Please provide any relevant details about the internship placement.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary authorization to submit this form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form author at form@gic.mx.
