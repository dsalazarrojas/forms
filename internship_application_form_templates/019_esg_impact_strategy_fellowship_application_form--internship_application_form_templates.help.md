# ESG Impact Strategy Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to apply for the ESG Impact Strategy Fellowship. The purpose of this fellowship is to support individuals who are passionate about making a positive impact in their communities and careers. The form gathers information about your personal details, project goals, and other relevant information to assess your eligibility and suitability for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling it out.
2.  Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to evaluate your application.
3.  Ensure that you have attached all required documents, such as your resume and transcript.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Details** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
*   **Project Goals** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the project goals that align with your interests and goals.
*   **Project Title** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title of your project.
*   **Fellowships** (`4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the fellowship you are applying for.
*   **Contact Details** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact details, including your address and social media handles.
*   **Project Start Date** (`6`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of your project.
*   **Project End Date** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of your project.
*   **Project Duration** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the duration of your project in months.
*   **Project Location** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of your project.
*   **Project Website** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the website of your project.
*   **Personal Statement** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief personal statement about yourself.
*   **Resume Attachment** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Attach your resume to support your application.
*   **Transcript Attachment** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Attach your transcript to support your application.
*   **Project Attachment** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Attach a document related to your project.
*   **Reference 1** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your first reference.
*   **Reference 2** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your second reference.
*   **Reference 3** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your third reference.
*   **Reference 4** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your fourth reference.
*   **Reference 5** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your fifth reference.
*   **Agreement** (`20`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the terms of your agreement.
*   **Review Statement** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a statement about your review of the fellowship terms.
*   **Review Attachment** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Attach a document related to your review statement.
*   **Review Statement Agreement** (`23`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your agreement to the review statement.
*   **Review Attachment Agreement** (`24`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your agreement to the review attachment.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`25`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

1.  Ensure that you have attached all required documents to support your application.
2.  Review the form carefully before submitting it.
3.  Double-check that you have selected all relevant options accurately.
