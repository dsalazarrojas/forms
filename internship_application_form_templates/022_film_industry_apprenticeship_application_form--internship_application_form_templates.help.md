# Film Industry Apprenticeship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Industry Apprenticeship Application Form is a tool designed to collect information from individuals interested in applying for an apprenticeship in the film industry. The form is meant to gather relevant details about the candidate, their experience, and preferences to ensure a smooth and successful application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Page 1: Candidate Details**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
2. **Page 2: Portfolio**: Share your portfolio, including any relevant work or projects you'd like to showcase. This could be a link, a PDF, or a brief description.
3. **Page 3: Preferences**: List your preferred areas of interest within the film industry and any specific skills or qualifications you have. This could include acting, writing, directing, editing, and more.
4. **Page 4: Contact**: Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any social media links (if applicable).
5. **Page 5: Submission**: Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (Page 1):
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Contact Information**: Provide your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Portfolio** (Page 2):
	+ Share a link, PDF, or brief description of your portfolio, highlighting your relevant work or projects.
* **Preferences** (Page 3):
	+ List your preferred areas of interest within the film industry (e.g., acting, writing, directing, editing, etc.).
	+ Mention any specific skills or qualifications you possess that you believe would make you a strong candidate.
* **Contact** (Page 4):
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ Include any social media links where we can find you.
* **Submission** (Page 5):
	+ Review and submit your completed application.
