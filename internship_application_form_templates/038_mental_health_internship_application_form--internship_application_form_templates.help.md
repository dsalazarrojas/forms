# Mental Health Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Internship Application Form is designed to gather essential information from potential interns to ensure they are qualified and suitable for the internship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Provide clear and honest answers to each question.
2. Ensure you meet the required qualifications and criteria for the internship.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details** (Required)
	+ This field is for providing your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **Experience** (Required)
	+ Describe your relevant work experience, education, and training related to the mental health field.
* **Documents** (Optional)
	+ Select all relevant documents that support your application, such as academic certificates or professional licenses.
* **Medical Report** (Required)
	+ Upload a copy of your medical report or other relevant health information.
* **Emergency Contact** (Required)
	+ Provide contact information for a friend, family member, or colleague who can be reached in case of an emergency.
	+ Note: This is not for your personal medical contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Required)
	+ Enter the name of the emergency contact person provided above.
* **Relationship** (Required)
	+ Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Required)
	+ Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Optional)
	+ Enter the email address of the emergency contact person (if available).
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Optional)
	+ Enter the address of the emergency contact person (if available).
* **Signature** (Required)
	+ Sign and date your application to confirm its accuracy.
* **Medical License** (Optional)
	+ If applicable, provide a copy of your medical license.
* **License Type** (Required)
	+ Select whether your medical license is issued in the United States or not.
* **License Number** (Required)
	+ Enter your medical license number (if applicable).
* **License Expiration** (Required)
	+ Enter the date your medical license will expire (if applicable).
* **Medical School** (Optional)
	+ Enter the name of your medical school (if applicable).
* **Graduate Degree** (Optional)
	+ Enter the name of your graduate degree (if applicable).
* **Graduate School** (Optional)
	+ Enter the name of your graduate school (if applicable).
* **Completed Degree** (Optional)
	+ Enter the name of your completed degree (if applicable).
* **Completed Degree School** (Optional)
	+ Enter the name of your completed degree school (if applicable).
* **Medical Specialty** (Optional)
	+ Enter your medical specialty (if applicable).
* **Internship Length** (Required)
	+ Enter the length of the internship you are applying for.
* **Internship Type** (Required)
	+ Select whether the internship is clinical or non-clinical.
* **Start Date** (Required)
	+ Enter the start date of the internship.
* **End Date** (Required)
	+ Enter the end date of the internship.

Please ensure to fill out each field accurately and completely before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
