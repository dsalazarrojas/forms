# stem_hear_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for internship applications. Please fill it out carefully to provide the hiring team with accurate and detailed information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of application you are submitting.
2. Fill in your personal details, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide your education and work experience in the text areas.
4. Select your skills, language proficiency, and motivation for the internship.
5. Attach your resume, cover letter, and transcript, if applicable.
6. Fill in any additional notes or comments.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Type** (`application_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of application you are submitting.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number.
* **Application Deadline** (`application_deadline`, `date`, required: false): Enter the application deadline in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your application.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple types of applications.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Not to be filled by applicant.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Fill in any additional notes or comments.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required: false): Attach your resume.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, required: false): Attach your references.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required: false): Describe your work experience.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false): Describe your education.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, required: false): List your relevant skills.
* **Project Work** (`project_work`, `text`, required: false): Describe your project work.
* **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, `text`, required: false): Write a personal statement.
* **Language Proficiency** (`language_proficiency`, `text`, required: false): Describe your language proficiency.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, required: false): Explain your motivation for the internship.
* **Resume Attachment** (`resume_attachment`, `text`, required: false): Attach your resume.
* **Cover Letter Attachment** (`cover_letter_attachment`, `text`, required: false): Attach your cover letter.
* **Transcript Attachment** (`transcript_attachment`, `text`, required: false): Attach your transcript.
* **Application Attachment** (`application_attachment`, `text`, required: false): Attach any other relevant application materials.
