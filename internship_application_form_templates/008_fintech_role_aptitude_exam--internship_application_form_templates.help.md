# Fintech Role Aptitude Exam - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your aptitude for a fintech role by assessing your skills and preferences in areas relevant to the industry, such as programming languages, database systems, operating systems, and frameworks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the first three fields. Please ensure that your Email is accurate as it will be used to contact you.
2. Select your favorite programming language from the dropdown menu in the "What is your favorite programming language?" field.
3. Choose your favorite database system from the dropdown menu in the "What is your favorite database system?" field.
4. Enter the number of years you have experience with IT in the "How many years of experience do you have with IT?" field.
5. Select your favorite operating system from the dropdown menu in the "What is your favorite operating system?" field.
6. Enter the number of hours you spend on IT per week in the "How many hours per week do you spend on IT?" field.
7. Select your favorite programming frameworks from the dropdown menu in the "What is your favorite programming framework?" field. You can select multiple frameworks if applicable.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly.
* **What is your favorite programming language?** (`question_1`, select_one, optional): This question is about your preferred programming language. Please choose one that you enjoy using.
	+ Select: Java, Python, C++, JavaScript
* **What is your favorite database system?** (`question_2`, select_one, optional): This question is about your preferred database system. Please choose one that you like to work with.
	+ Select: MySQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, SQLite
* **How many years of experience do you have with IT?** (`question_3`, number, optional): This question is about your experience with IT. Please answer as accurately as possible.
* **What is your favorite operating system?** (`question_4`, select_one, optional): This question is about your preferred operating system. Please choose one that you like to work on.
	+ Select: Windows, Linux, macOS, ChromeOS
* **How many hours per week do you spend on IT?** (`question_5`, number, optional): This question is about the time you dedicate to IT activities per week. Please answer as accurately as possible.
* **What is your favorite programming framework?** (`question_6`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about the frameworks you enjoy using. Please select multiple if you have experience with more than one.
	+ Select: Spring, Django, React, Angular, Node.js
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to complete and submit your form.
