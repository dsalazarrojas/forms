# Digital Optimization Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect applications from individuals interested in participating in a digital optimization internship. This form is designed to be completed by individuals who want to showcase their skills and qualifications for the internship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions to provide a comprehensive application.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk).
3. If a field is not required, feel free to provide as much information as you'd like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form 1** (`application_form`, text, optional): This section is for you to briefly introduce yourself and provide a short description of your background and qualifications.
* **Contact Form 1** (`contact_form`, text, optional): In this section, please provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Resume Form 1** (`resume_form`, text, optional): Please attach or paste a link to your resume, so we can review your qualifications in more detail.
* **Application Form 2** (`application_form_2`, text, optional): This section is similar to the first application form, providing another opportunity to showcase your background and qualifications.
* **Contact Form 2** (`contact_form_2`, text, optional): Another section to provide your contact details.
* **Resume Form 2** (`resume_form_2`, text, optional): Another section to attach or paste a link to your resume.
* **Skills Form** (`skills_form`, text, optional): In this section, list your relevant skills and experience.
* **Work Experience Form** (`work_experience_form`, text, optional): Please describe your previous work experience, highlighting relevant projects and skills applied.
* **Education Form** (`education_form`, text, optional): Share your educational background, including relevant courses or certifications.
* **Skills Form 2** (`skills_form_2`, text, optional): List any additional skills or experience you'd like to highlight.
* **Work Experience Form 2** (`work_experience_form_2`, text, optional): Describe any additional work experience that is relevant to the internship.

## Tips

* Please be as concise as possible while still providing enough information for us to assess your qualifications.
* If you have any attachments or links, please ensure they are secure and do not contain any malware.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
