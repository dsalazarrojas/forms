# Business Analyst Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential candidates for a business analyst internship opportunity. Your application will be reviewed thoroughly, and providing accurate details is crucial to ensure consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2. Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Attach a URL to your resume or describe it in the text field if you're unable to upload.
4. Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If you choose to provide this information, please use the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **University or College** (`university`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Degree Program** (`degree_program`, `select_one`, required): Select the major or program you are or have been enrolled in.
* **Current Year of Study** (`current_year`, `select_one`, required): Choose the year you are currently in.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`expected_graduation`, `date`, required): If you are a student, enter the date you expect to graduate. If not, enter the date you graduated.
* **Skills and Experience** (`skills_experience`, `note`, optional): Describe any relevant background, skills, or experience you have.
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any relevant software and tools you are familiar with.
* **Programming Languages** (`programming_languages`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any programming languages you can use.
* **Relevant Coursework** (`relevant_coursework`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant coursework.
* **Previous Internships** (`previous_internships`, `text`, optional): Describe any previous internship experience.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant work experience.
* **Relevant Projects** (`projects`, `text`, optional): Describe any projects you have worked on.
* **Internship Preferences** (`internship_preferences`, `note`, optional): Describe any specific requirements or preferences for the internship.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the earliest date you can start.
* **Preferred Duration** (`preferred_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the duration of the internship you prefer.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of hours you can commit to per week.
* **Location Preference** (`location_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred work location.
* **Compensation Requirement** (`compensation_requirement`, `select_one`, required): Select the compensation option that best suits you.
* **Why Are You Interested** (`why_interested`, `text`, required): Explain why you are interested in this internship opportunity.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your career aspirations.
* **Resume** (`resume_upload`, `text`, required): Attach a URL to your resume or describe it in the text field.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, optional): Provide reference names and contacts.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant information.

# Tips
* Please ensure that you have attached or described your resume accurately.
* If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided before submitting your application.
