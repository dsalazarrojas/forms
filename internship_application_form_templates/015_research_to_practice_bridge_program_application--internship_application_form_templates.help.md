### Research To Practice Bridge Program Application - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for the Research To Practice Bridge Program application. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to increase your chances of being considered for the program.

### How To Complete This Form

- **Carefully review the form**: Before you start filling out the form, make sure you meet the program's eligibility criteria and understand the required qualifications and research interests.
- **Fill out the form in sections**: Complete each section of the form in the order displayed.
- **Use clear and concise language**: Clearly and concisely answer each question, providing enough detail to support your application.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research To Practice Bridge Program Application** (`title`, `text`, **required**): Enter the title of your application.
* **Researcher Info** (`researcher_info`, `text`, **required**): Please describe your current research background and experience. Be specific about your areas of research interest and what motivates you to apply for this program.
* **Qualifications** (`researcher_qualifications`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all relevant qualifications that apply:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Practitioner Info** (`practitioner_info`, `select_one`, **required**): Select one qualification that applies:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Practitioner Info** (`practitioner_application_form`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select one qualification that applies:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Submit Application** (`submit_application`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you plan to submit your application.
* **Research Area(s) of Interest** (`research_area_of_interests`, `text`, **optional**): If there is any other area you are interested in, you can provide more details here.
* **Other** (`research_areas_of_interests_other`, `text`, **optional**): If any other areas, you can provide more details here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, **required**): Enter your contact email for us to reach out to you with further inquiries.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): If you would like to provide any additional comments, feel free to do so.

### Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your application.
* Make sure to proofread your application before submission.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask.
