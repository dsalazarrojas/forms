# Performance Management Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Management Fellowship Application form is used to collect information from applicants who are applying for a fellowship position. This information will be used to evaluate the applicant's suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your personal details, including:
	* First Name: Enter your first name in the text box provided.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name in the text box provided.
2. Next, provide your contact information:
	* Email: Enter your email address in the required format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
3. If applicable, upload any relevant documents:
	* CV/Resume: Upload your current CV/Resume.
	* Portfolio: Upload your portfolio or any other relevant documents.
	* Transcripts: Upload your transcripts.
	* Other Document: You can upload any other relevant document.
	* Cover Letter: Upload your cover letter.
	* Additional Documents: Upload any other relevant documents.
4. Provide your work experience details:
	* Work Experience: Enter a brief description of your work experience.
5. Provide your education details:
	* Education: Enter a brief description of your education background.
6. Provide your interests and skills:
	* Interests: Enter a brief description of your interests.
	* Skills: Enter a brief description of your skills.
	* Languages: Enter a brief description of your language skills.
7. Provide your referees details:
	* Referees: Select up to 4 referees from the options provided.
	* Referees details: Enter a brief description of your referees' details.
	* How do you know them?: Enter a brief description of how you know your referees.
	* Referees name: Select one of the provided options for your referees' name.
