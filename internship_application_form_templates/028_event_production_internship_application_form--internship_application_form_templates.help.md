# Event Production Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Production Internship Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in applying for the Event Production Internship. It will help the assigned team review and assess your qualifications, skills, and experience for the internship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your address.
2. Provide your education background and relevant skills you have acquired.
3. If you have referees, provide their contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Select your application status and type.
5. Choose the submission status and submission date/time.
6. Review and submit your application form.
