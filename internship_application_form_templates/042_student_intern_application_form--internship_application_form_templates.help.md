# Student Intern Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students who are interested in applying for an internship. The form will be reviewed and considered for placement in our company's internship program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form carefully**: Before starting to fill out the form, ensure you have all the necessary documents and information ready.
2. **Fill out the form**: Complete each field with the requested information.
3. **Submit the form**: Once complete, submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Contact Information (contact_information, text, required/optional)**: Please enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your application.
### Educational Background (Page 2)
* **Educational Background (educational_background, text, required/optional)**: Describe your educational background, including any relevant coursework, degrees, or certifications.
### Work Experience (Page 3)
* **Work Experience (work_experience, select multiple, required/optional)**: Check any relevant work experience you have, such as part-time jobs or volunteer work.
### Skills (Page 4)
* **Skills (skills, select multiple, required/optional)**: Select any relevant skills you have, such as programming languages, tools, or certifications.
### References (Page 5)
* **References (references, number, required/optional)**: Please list 2-3 professional references, including their name, job title, company, and contact information.
### Available Start Date (Page 6)
* **Available Start Date (available_start_date, date, required/optional)**: Enter the date you are available to start the internship, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
