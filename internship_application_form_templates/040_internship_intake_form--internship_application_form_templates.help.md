# internship_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internship intake form is a comprehensive form designed to collect information about your academic, professional, and personal background, as well as your preferences and availability. This information will be used to assess your eligibility and suitability for an internship opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section carefully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for your Work Status, Application Type, and Internship Type.
3. If you have any relevant work experience, please select the correct duration.
4. If you are available for an internship, select the correct availability option.
5. Please ensure that your email address is valid and your phone number is correct.
6. Upload a resume or provide a link to it.
7. Finally, review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Resume**: Upload your resume or provide a link to it.
* **Start Date**: Enter the expected start date of your internship.
* **End Date**: Enter the expected end date of your internship.
* **Application Type**: Select the type of internship you are applying for (Intern, Co-op, Part-time, or Full-time).
* **Work Status**: Select your current work status (Currently employed, Unemployed, or Internship).
* **Location**: Enter your preferred location for the internship.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of yourself and your background.
* **Skills**: List any relevant skills you possess.
* **Education**: Select your highest level of education completed (High School Diploma, Bachelor, Master, or PhD).
* **Work Experience**: Select the duration of your work experience (0-1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or 5+ years).
* **Availability**: Select your availability for the internship (Full-time, Part-time, or Intern).
* **Available Date**: Enter the date you are available to start the internship.
* **Salary Range**: Enter a number representing your expected salary range.
* **Benefits**: Select whether you expect benefits (True or False).
* **Salary**: Enter a number representing your expected salary.
* **Internship Type**: Select the type of internship you are applying for (Academic, Research, or Development).
* **Other Work Status**: Enter any other work status that may apply to you.
* **Job Title**: Enter a job title or role you are applying for.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are applying to.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Benefits**: Select whether you expect benefits (True or False).
* **Salary**: Enter a number representing your expected salary.
