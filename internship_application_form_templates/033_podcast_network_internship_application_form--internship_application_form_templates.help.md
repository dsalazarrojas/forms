# Podcast Network Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in an internship program at a podcast network. By completing this form, you will be sharing your personal details, address, education background, and work experience to help us understand your qualifications and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Please provide only true and honest information.
3. Ensure that you fill out only one form per person.
4. Double-check your answers for completeness and correctness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal details** (`personal_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for you to provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your current address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Education background** (`education_background`, `text`, required: false): Share your educational history, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **Work experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required: false): Describe your previous work experience, including any relevant roles or positions you've held.
 
 ## Tips

* Make sure to be accurate and thorough when filling out this form.
* Attach any supporting documents or references as needed.
* Follow up with a brief cover letter or introduction if desired.
