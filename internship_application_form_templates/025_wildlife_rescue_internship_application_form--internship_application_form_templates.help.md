# Wildlife Rescue Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect relevant information from applicants interested in participating in a wildlife rescue internship program. It will be used to assess their background, motivation, and availability to ensure they are a good fit for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
3. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Education
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering your highest level of education completed or currently pursuing. 

### Experience
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for describing any experience you have that's relevant to wildlife rescue or conservation. 

### Motivation
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for telling us why you are interested in the wildlife rescue internship. 

### Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for specifying your available dates to start the internship.

### Tips
* Please provide clear and concise answers to the questions.
* Double-check your information before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
