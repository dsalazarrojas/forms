# intern-application-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The intern-application-form is a template used to collect information from individuals applying for internships. This form helps gather essential details about the applicant's personal and professional background, which will be used to evaluate their eligibility for the internship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field (page 1).
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field (page 2).
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (page 3).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (page 4).
5. Select your start date of availability for the internship in the "Start date" field (page 5).
6. Specify your end date of availability for the internship in the "End date" field (page 6).
7. Enter your desired job position in the "Job position" field (page 7).
8. Enter your expected salary for the internship in the "Salary" field (page 8).
9. Choose one of the available options for "Select one" (page 9).
10. Select multiple options from the list for "Select multiple" (page 10).
11. Enter any additional comments or notes about your application in the "Note" field (page 11).
12. Choose a date for your availability in the "Date" field (page 12).
13. Select a time for your availability in the "Time" field (page 13).
14. Enter your full address in the "Address" field (page 14).
15. Enter your city in the "City" field (page 15).
16. Enter your state in the "State" field (page 16).
17. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field (page 17).
18. Enter your country of residence in the "Country" field (page 18).
19. Enter your job title or position in the "Job title" field (page 19).
20. Enter the name of the university you attended in the "University" field (page 20).
21. Enter your major or field of study in the "Major" field (page 21).
22. Enter your degree earned in the "Degree" field (page 22).
23. Enter your GPA on a 4.0 scale in the "GPA" field (page 23).
24. Enter your graduation date in the "Graduation date" field (page 24).
25. Enter your skills or qualifications relevant to the internship in the "Skills" field (page 25).
