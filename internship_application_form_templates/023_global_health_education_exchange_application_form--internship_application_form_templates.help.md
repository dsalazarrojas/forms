# Global Health Education Exchange Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Health Education Exchange Application Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in the Global Health Education Exchange program. This program fosters international collaborations and exchange of knowledge, skills, and best practices in global health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the `User Information` page.
2. Provide details about your educational background and any relevant certifications in the `Education Background` page.
3. Share your professional experience in the `Professional Experience` page.
4. Describe your work experience and any relevant skills you possess in the `Work Experience` page.
5. Select the skills you possess from the list in the `Skills` page. This is a required field to ensure you are qualified for the program.
6. Enter your education details and any relevant certifications in the `Education Details` page.
7. List any work certifications you have obtained in the `Work Certifications` page.
8. Specify your language proficiency in the `Language` page.
9. Confirm your submission in the `Submit` page.
10. Provide contact information for follow-up communication in the `Contact Information` page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This section is for entering your name, email address, and other relevant contact details.
* **Education Background**: Please describe your academic background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant coursework.
* **Professional Experience**: Share a brief overview of your work experience, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your work experience, highlighting any relevant skills, achievements, and accomplishments.
* **Skills**: Select all the skills you possess from the list, as these are required for program participation.
* **Education Details**: Provide additional details about your educational background, including any relevant coursework or research experience.
* **Work Certifications**: List any work-related certifications you have obtained, such as licenses or specialized training.
* **Language**: Indicate your proficiency in different languages.
* **Submit**: Confirm that you have reviewed and completed the form accurately.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your phone number and email address for follow-up communication.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields accurately.
- Double-check your submission for any errors before confirming.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
