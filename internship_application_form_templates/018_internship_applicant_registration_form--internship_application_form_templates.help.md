# Internship Applicant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Applicant Registration Form is a document that helps the company assess your eligibility and suitability for an internship position. It collects information about your personal details, skills, availability, and career goals to match you with the best internship opportunities available at our company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your active Email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Provide your Phone number to be reached at for further discussions.
4. Upload or paste your Resume to showcase your experience and skills.
5. Specify the Date Available for the internship.
6. Select the relevant Skills you have, such as Programming, Coding, Data Analysis, etc.
7. Choose the desired Internship Length from the options.
8. Select your Preferred Workload from the available options.
9. (Optional) Provide information about your previous Work Experience, Education Background, Language Proficiency, Career Goals, and Personal Interests.
10. If applicable, provide details about your References.
11. Finally, add any Additional Info that you feel is relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your active email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further discussions.
* **Resume**: Upload or paste your resume to show us your experience and skills. 
* **Date Available**: Select the date you can start the internship.
* **Skills**: Select all the relevant skills you possess from the provided options. Examples include Programming, Coding, Data Analysis, etc.
* **Internship Length**: Choose the desired duration of the internship from the options provided.
* **Preferred Workload**: Select your preferred workload hours per week.
* **Work Experience**: (Optional) Describe your previous work experience in the field you are applying for.
* **Education Background**: (Optional) Provide information about your academic background.
* **Language Proficiency**: (Optional) Select all languages you are proficient in.
* **Career Goals**: (Optional) Describe your career goals and aspirations.
* **Personal Interests**: (Optional) Enter your personal interests outside of work.
* **References**: (Optional) Provide details about your professional references.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your application.
