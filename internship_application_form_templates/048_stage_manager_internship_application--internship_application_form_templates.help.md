# Stage Manager Internship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Manager Internship Application form is designed to collect necessary information from applicants for the Stage Manager position. This form will be used to evaluate your suitability for the internship and ensure that your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Write a brief motivation statement in the "Motivation" field, explaining why you are interested in the Stage Manager Internship.
6. Specify your availability in the "Availability" field.
7. Upload your resume in the "Resume" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that will be used to contact you about your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during business hours.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, Text, Required): Write a brief statement explaining why you are interested in the Stage Manager Internship and what you can bring to the role.
* **Availability** (`availability`, Text, Required): Specify your availability for the internship, including your start and end dates.
* **Resume** (`resume`, Text, Required): Upload your resume in PDF or docx format, showcasing your relevant experience and skills.
