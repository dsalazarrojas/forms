# Summer Intern Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Intern Application Form is designed to help us get to know more about you and your qualifications for the internship position. This form is typically completed by students or recent graduates looking to gain work experience in a specific field over the summer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Make sure you have all the necessary information and documents ready before starting.
* Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Application Information
* **Application Information** (`Application Information`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first page of the form where you can enter any additional information about your application, such as your interest in the internship position or any relevant experience you have.

### Name
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your full name as you would like it to be displayed on any company materials or communications.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): This field is for your email address, which will be used for any communication with us about your application.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your phone number, which we may use to contact you about your application.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for your date of birth, which will help us verify your eligibility for the internship program.

### Resume
* **Resume** (`Resume`, `text`, required/optional): Please attach a copy of your resume, which should include your education, work experience, and any relevant skills or certifications.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`Skills`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all the skills you have experience with or are proficient in. This will help us match you with the best possible internship positions.

### Experience
* **Experience** (`Experience`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to describe any relevant work experience you have, including internships, part-time jobs, or volunteering.

### Availability
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for you to indicate how many weeks or months you are available to work during the summer. Please select a number from the dropdown menu.

### Education
* **Education** (`Education`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide information about your education, including your degree, institution, and graduation date.

### Expected Graduation Date
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`Expected Graduation Date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your expected graduation date.

### Availability Date
* **Availability Date** (`Availability Date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for you to indicate when you are available to start work during the summer.

### Availability Time
* **Availability Time** (`Availability Time`, `time`, required/optional): This field is for you to indicate how many hours or days per week you are available to work during the summer.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, required/optional): This is a free text field for any additional comments or notes you would like to share with us.

### Availability Phone
* **Availability Phone** (`Availability Phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your phone number, which we may use to contact you about your availability.

### Availability Email
* **Availability Email** (`Availability Email`, `email`, required/optional): This field is for your email address, which we may use to contact you about your availability.

### Availability Address
* **Availability Address** (`Availability Address`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your mailing address.

### Availability Zip
* **Availability Zip** (`Availability Zip`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your zip code.

### Availability City
* **Availability City** (`Availability City`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your city of residence.

### Availability State
* **Availability State** (`Availability State`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your state of residence.

### Availability Country
* **Availability Country** (`Availability Country`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your country of residence.

### Availability Notes
* **Availability Notes** (`Availability Notes`, `text`, required/optional): This is a free text field for any additional comments or notes about your availability.

### Availability Other
* **Availability Other** (`Availability Other`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about your availability.

### Availability Reference
* **Availability Reference** (`Availability Reference`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to provide any references or contacts who can vouch for your character and work ethic.

### Availability Agreement
* **Availability Agreement** (`Availability Agreement`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to sign an agreement to confirm your availability and understanding of the internship program.

### Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
