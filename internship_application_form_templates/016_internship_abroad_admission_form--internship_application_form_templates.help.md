# internship-abroad-admission-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and academic information from students applying for internships abroad. It will help us assess their eligibility and prepare their applications for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student id and name.
2. Provide your university name and program name (if applicable).
3. Enter your email address and phone number.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Provide your passport number.
6. Choose your preferred visa type.
7. Enter the location and duration of your desired internship.
8. List the activities you'd like to participate in during your internship.
9. Indicate your language proficiency (English, Spanish, Chinese, or French).
10. Provide the dates you are available for the internship.
11. Upload your resume.
12. Upload your transcript.
13. Upload a recommendation letter.
14. Write a personal statement.
15. Indicate your language requirements (English, French, or German).
16. Enter your country, city, state, and zip code.
17. Provide the name of the host university.
18. Enter the supervisor's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: A unique identification number for you as a student.
* **Name**: Your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **University Name**: The name of your current or alumni university.
* **Program Name**: If you have a specific program in mind for your internship.
* **Email**: A valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Passport Number**: Your passport number for identity verification.
* **Visa Type**: Select your preferred visa type for your internship.
* **Internship Location**: The location where you'd like to complete your internship.
* **Internship Duration**: The length of time you'd like to spend on your internship.
* **Internship Activities**: A brief description of the activities you're interested in during your internship.
* **Language**: Indicate your proficiency level in English, Spanish, Chinese, or French.
* **Internship Availability**: The dates you are available for the internship.
* **Resume**: An uploaded version of your resume.
* **Transcript**: An uploaded version of your transcript.
* **Recommendation Letter**: An uploaded letter of recommendation from a professor or mentor.
* **Personal Statement**: A written statement about your goals and motivations for the internship.
* **Language Requirements**: Indicate your language proficiency level in English, French, or German.
* **Country**: The country where you'd like to complete your internship.
* **City**: The city where you'd like to complete your internship.
* **State**: The state where you'd like to complete your internship.
* **Zip Code**: The zip code for the internship location.
* **Host University**: The name of the host university for your internship.
* **Supervisor Name**: The name of the person who will be supervising you during your internship.

Please note that some fields may not be required, but they are included for your convenience and to help us assess your eligibility for the program.
