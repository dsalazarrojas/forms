# Internship Rotation Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form provides an overview of an internship rotation, which is a critical component of an internship program. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the start and end dates of each rotation, as well as key details about the rotations, such as the department, supervisor, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the rotation start date in the `rotation_start_date` field.
2. Enter the rotation end date in the `rotation_end_date` field.
3. Select the department for the rotation in the `rotation_department` field.
4. Enter the supervisor's name in the `supervisor_name` field.
5. Enter the supervisor's title in the `supervisor_title` field.
6. Enter the supervisor's phone number in the `supervisor_phone` field.
7. Enter the supervisor's email address in the `supervisor_email` field.
8. Describe the learning objectives of the rotation in the `learning_objectives` field.
9. Enter any comments from the supervisor in the `supervisor_comments` field.
10. Enter the rotation goals for the rotation, using the `rotation_goals`, `rotation_goals_1`, `rotation_goals_2`, etc. fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rotation Start Date** (`rotation_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the rotation will start.
* **Rotation End Date** (`rotation_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the rotation will end.
* **Rotation Department** (`rotation_department`, text, required): Select the department where the rotation will take place.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor for the rotation.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the supervisor for the rotation.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the supervisor for the rotation.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the supervisor for the rotation.
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, text, required): Describe the objectives of the rotation.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, note, required): Enter any comments from the supervisor.
* **Rotation Goals** (`rotation_goals`, text, required): Enter the goals of the rotation. (Note: This field is repeated up to 15 times, `rotation_goals_1` to `rotation_goals_15`, which allows you to enter up to 15 goals for the rotation.)
* **Rotation Goals 1-15** (`rotation_goals_1` to `rotation_goals_15`, text, optional): Enter any additional goals for the rotation.
