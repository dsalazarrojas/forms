# Summer Apprenticeship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Apprenticeship Application Form is designed to collect information about your background, skills, and experience to help us assess your suitability for the Summer Apprenticeship Program. The form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields that require your attention.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by filling out the first page of the form, which asks for your basic contact information.
2. On the next page, you will be asked to select your preferred type of internship (Summer Intern, Part-time Intern, or Full-time Intern).
3. You will also be asked to upload your resume and provide information about your skills, education, and work experience.
4. The following sections will ask about your availability, start and end dates, salary expectations, and language proficiency.
5. Finally, you will be asked to provide emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required, Text): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required, Text): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (Optional, Email): If you have a email address, please enter it here. This will be used for communication purposes only.
* **Phone** (Optional, Text): Please enter your phone number. This will be used for communication purposes only.
* **Summer Internship Program** (Required, Select One): Please select your preferred type of internship (Summer Intern, Part-time Intern, or Full-time Intern).
* **Resume** (Required, Text): Please upload your resume to provide us with a copy of your professional experience and qualifications.
* **Skills** (Optional, Text): Please list any relevant skills you have that would be beneficial for the internship.
* **Education** (Optional, Text): Please list any relevant education or courses you have completed that would be beneficial for the internship.
* **Work Experience** (Optional, Text): Please list any relevant work experience you have had that would be beneficial for the internship.
* **Relevant Experience** (Optional, Text): Please list any relevant experience you have had that would be beneficial for the internship.
* **Why Choose This Program** (Optional, Text): Please explain why you are interested in this program and what you hope to gain from it.
* **Availability** (Optional, Date): Please select the start and end dates of your availability for the internship.
* **Start Date** (Optional, Date): Please select the start date of your preferred internship start date.
* **End Date** (Optional, Date): Please select the end date of your preferred internship end date.
* **Salary Expectations** (Optional, Number): Please enter your expected salary for the internship.
* **Available** (Required, Select Multiple): Please select whether you are available to start the internship immediately.
* **Language Proficiency** (Required, Select One): Please select your proficiency in languages (English, French, Spanish).
* **Language Proficiency Other** (Optional, Text): Please provide any additional language proficiency information not listed above.
* **Emergency Contact** (Optional, Text): Please enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Optional, Text): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Optional, Text): Please enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Optional, Text): Please enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Created By** (Optional, Text): Please enter the name of the person who created the application form.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" must be completed to submit the application form.
