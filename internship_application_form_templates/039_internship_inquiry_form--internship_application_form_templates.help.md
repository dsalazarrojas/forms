# Internship Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Inquiry Form is used to collect information about an internship opportunity. This form gathers details about the internship such as institution, program title, start and end dates, internship type, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "User name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Type the name of the institution in the "Institution name" field.
5. Enter the title of the internship program in the "Program title" field.
6. Select the start date of the internship from the calendar.
7. Select the end date of the internship from the calendar.
8. Choose the type of internship from the options provided in the "Internship type" field.
9. Select the type of institution from the options provided in the "Institution type" field.
10. Enter the location of the internship in the "Location" field.
11. Choose the duration of the internship from the options provided in the "Duration" field.
12. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact person" field.
13. Enter the URL of the institution in the "Institution URL" field.
14. Enter the contact number of the institution in the "Contact number" field.
15. Enter the email of the contact person in the "Contact email" field.
16. Enter the address of the institution in the "Institution address" field.
17. Add any additional information about the institution in the "Institution notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User name (Text):**
  Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email (Email):**
  Enter your email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone number (Text):**
  Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Institution name (Text):**
  Enter the name of the institution where the internship will take place.
* **Program title (Text):**
  Enter the title of the internship program.
* **Start date (Date):**
  Select the date you plan to start the internship.
* **End date (Date):**
  Select the date you plan to end the internship.
* **Internship type (Select one):**
  Choose the type of internship you are applying for (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Institution type (Select one):**
  Choose the type of institution where the internship will take place (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Location (Text):**
  Enter the location of the internship.
* **Duration (Select one):**
  Choose the duration of the internship (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Contact person (Text):**
  Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Institution URL (Text):**
  Enter the URL of the institution.
* **Contact number (Text):**
  Enter the contact number of the institution.
* **Contact email (Email):**
  Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Institution address (Text):**
  Enter the address of the institution.
* **Institution notes (Text):**
  Add any additional information about the institution.
