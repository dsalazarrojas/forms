# Veterinary Clinical Placement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for veterinary professionals interested in seeking out clinical placements. It is a self-contained, online form that helps the user provide relevant information about themselves, their availability, and placement preferences in a clinical setting. The information collected will help the placement committee to match the user with suitable placement opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the personal details section with your name and other relevant personal information.
2. Provide information on your availability, including dates you are free to work and any restrictions or limitations.
3. Share your experience in a clinical setting, including any relevant certifications, qualifications or training.
4. Specify your placement preferences, including the type of placement you are interested in and any specific requirements.
5. Confirm your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (1)** (`name`, `text`, required): Provide your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Availability (2)** (`availability`, `text`, required): Share your availability, including dates you are free to work and any restrictions or limitations.
* **Experience (3)** (`experience`, `text`, required): Share your experience in a clinical setting, including any relevant certifications, qualifications or training.
* **Placement Preferences (4)** (`placement_preferences`, `text`, required): Specify your placement preferences, including the type of placement you are interested in and any specific requirements.
* **Confirmation (5)** (`confirmation`, `text`, required): Confirm that the information you have provided is accurate and complete.
* **Supervision (6)** (`supervision`, `text`, not required): This section is not currently required. Please do not fill this in as it is not necessary for this form.
