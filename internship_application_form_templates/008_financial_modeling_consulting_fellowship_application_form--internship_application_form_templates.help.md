# Financial Modeling Consulting Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Modeling Consulting Fellowship is a program designed to provide opportunities for individuals to develop their skills in financial modeling and consulting. This application form is a crucial step in the selection process, and your responses will be reviewed by the fellowship selection committee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise answer to each question, ensuring that you have provided all the necessary information.
2. Proofread your responses to ensure that they are accurate and free of errors.
3. If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact the fellowship administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to provide a brief description of your interest in the fellowship program.
* **Contact Information** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to provide your address, which will help us to contact you if needed.
* **Financial Background** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to describe your experience and knowledge in financial modeling and consulting.
* **Educational Background** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to describe your educational background, including any relevant courses or certifications.
* **Work Experience** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to describe your work experience, including any relevant projects or achievements.
* **Education** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to describe your educational background, including any relevant courses or certifications.
* **Skills** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to list any relevant skills you have, such as Excel, Python, or data analysis.
* **Resume** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to attach your resume or CV, which will be reviewed by the fellowship selection committee.
* **Transcript** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to attach a copy of your transcript, which will be reviewed by the fellowship selection committee.
* **References** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to provide names and contact information for your professional references.
* **Submit** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to submit your application, which will be reviewed by the fellowship selection committee.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
* Ensure that your resume and transcript are attached as required.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact the fellowship administrators.
* The selection committee will review your application and contact you for further information if needed.
