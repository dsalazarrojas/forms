# Equine Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential interns who are applying for an equine internship position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Application Details" section with your name and a brief description of your experience and qualifications.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
3. List your previous horse experience and equine certifications, if any.
4. Select your availability for work, including start and end dates.
5. Choose your preferred work schedule.
6. Specify your salary requirements.
7. Provide emergency contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
8. Answer questions about your medical information.
9. List any references you have.
10. Specify your skills and qualifications.
11. Confirm your availability for multiple work periods.
12. Confirm your emergency contact relationship and provide additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details**: A brief description of your experience and qualifications.
* **Contact Info**: Your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Address**: Your address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Horse Experience**: Your previous horse experience.
* **Equine Certifications**: Any equine-related certifications you may have.
* **Availability**: The dates you are available for work.
* **Expected Start Date**: The date you expect to start work.
* **Expected End Date**: The date you expect to finish work.
* **Work Schedule**: Your preferred work schedule.
* **Salary Requirements**: Your expected salary requirements.
* **Emergency Contact**: Your emergency contact's name, phone number, and email.
* **Medical Info**: Your medical information.
* **References**: Any relevant references.
* **Skills**: A list of your skills and qualifications.
* **Availability 2**: Confirm your availability for multiple work periods.
* **Availability 3**: Confirm your availability for multiple work periods.
* **Availability 4**: Confirm your availability for multiple work periods.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Confirm your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Your emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Your emergency contact's address.
* **Terms & Conditions**: A text field for any additional comments or requirements.

Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in this form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
