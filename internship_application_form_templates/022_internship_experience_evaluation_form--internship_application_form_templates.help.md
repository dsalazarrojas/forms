# Internship Experience Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's internship experience and to provide an opportunity to rate their satisfaction with the program. The information collected from this form will help identify areas for improvement and allow the organization to make informed decisions about future internship programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the following fields:
	* **Company Name**: the name of the company where you participated in the internship
	* **Job Title**: your job title during the internship
	* **Program Location**: the location where the internship took place
	* **Program Start Date**: the start date of the internship
	* **Program End Date**: the end date of the internship
	* **Internship Experience**: a description of your experiences during the internship
	* **Program Satisfaction**: a rating of your satisfaction with the program
	* **Rating Scale**: a scale to rate your satisfaction with the program
	* **Rating**: a numerical rating of your satisfaction with the program
	* **Comments**: any additional comments you would like to provide

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the company where you participated in the internship
* **Job Title (id: 3)**: Enter your job title during the internship
* **Program Location (id: 4)**: Enter the location where the internship took place
* **Program Start Date (id: 5)**: Enter the start date of the internship
* **Program End Date (id: 6)**: Enter the end date of the internship
* **Internship Experience (id: 7)**: Describe your experiences during the internship, including any notable achievements or challenges you faced
* **Program Satisfaction (id: 8)**: Rate your satisfaction with the program, using the rating scale provided
* **Rating Scale (id: 9)**: A scale to rate your satisfaction with the program:
	+ **Excellent**: I was extremely satisfied with the program
	+ **Satisfactory**: I was fairly satisfied with the program
	+ **Fair**: I was somewhat satisfied with the program
	+ **Poor**: I was not satisfied with the program
* **Rating (id: 10)**: Enter a numerical rating of your satisfaction with the program
* **Comments (id: 11)**: Enter any additional comments you would like to provide about your experience

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and honestly
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your experiences during the internship
* The rating scale is based on the following criteria:
	+ **Excellent**: the program met all of your expectations and went above and beyond
	+ **Satisfactory**: the program met all of your basic needs and expectations
	+ **Fair**: the program met some of your needs and expectations, but not all of them
	+ **Poor**: the program did not meet any of your needs and expectations
