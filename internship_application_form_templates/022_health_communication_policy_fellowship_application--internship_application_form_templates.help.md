## health_communication_policy_fellowship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for a fellowship program under the Health Communication Policy. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from applicants, which will be reviewed and used in the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Attach your resume as an uploaded file in the "Resume Attachment" field.
6.  Describe your educational background in the "Education" field.
7.  Share your work experience in the "Work Experience" field.
8.  Upload your resume as a supporting document in the "Resume" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
*  **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
*  **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you regularly check for updates.
*  **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number that the organization can use to reach you.
*  **Resume Attachment**: Attach your latest resume as a file, in a standard format.
*  **Education**: Briefly describe your education background, including the institution and relevant coursework or degrees.
*  **Work Experience**: Provide an overview of your work experience, highlighting relevant positions and roles.
*  **Resume**: Upload your updated resume as a supporting document.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
*   Keep the "Education" and "Work Experience" fields concise and to the point.
*   Ensure that your resume is in a standard format and is an up-to-date representation of your current work experience.
*   If you have any difficulties or questions about the form, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
