# Internship Placement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to seek approval from the management for a student's internship placement. To ensure that the form is filled out correctly, please make sure that the internship placement is not already approved or pending approval. If you are filling out this form for a previously approved or pending placement, please do not proceed. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Enter a brief description of the internship placement.
2.  Enter the student's information, including name, email, and address.
3.  Enter the start date of the internship.
4.  Click the "Submit" button to save your changes and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Internship Approval Form** (`internship_placement_approval_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the internship placement, including the company's name and the student's job title.
* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the student's information.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the student.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the student.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's address.
* **Start Date** (`internship_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the internship. Please use the standard date format MM/DD/YYYY. 

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and do not hesitate to contact the internship coordinator if you have any questions or concerns. Also, please note that the form requires approval from management, and it may take some time to process.
