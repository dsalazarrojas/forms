# Restaurant Internship Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Internship Referral Form is designed to collect information about an internship opportunity at a restaurant, helping HR departments or recruitment teams gather details about the job and the person referring the candidate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that you are referring a legitimate internship opportunity that has not been previously submitted.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, including:
	* Restaurant name
	* Restaurant address
	* Referral date
	* Job title
	* Job type (or other if not listed)
	* Job location (or other if not listed)
	* Job status (open, filled, or closed)
	* Referral type (or other if not listed)
	* Contact email
	* Contact phone
	* Contact name
	* Contact title
	* Contact company
	* Contact job title
	* Job open date
	* Job closing date
	* Referral source (or other if not listed)
	* Referral source other (if not listed)
3. Enter the correct information for the fields that are not automatically populated, such as the job location and referral type.
4. Review your completed form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the restaurant where the internship opportunity is available.
* **Restaurant Address** (`restaurant_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the restaurant where the internship opportunity is available.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date the referral was made.
* **Referral Time** (`referral_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the referral, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the internship opportunity.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required): Enter the type of job, or select "other" if it's not listed.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the internship opportunity, or select "other" if it's not listed.
* **Job Location Other** (`job_location_other`, text, optional): If "other" is selected for job location, enter the specific location.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the internship opportunity (open, filled, or closed).
* **Job Status Other** (`job_status_other`, text, optional): If "other" is selected for job status, enter the specific status.
* **Referral Type** (`referral_type`, text, required): Enter the type of referral, or select "other" if it's not listed.
* **Referral Type Other** (`referral_type_other`, text, optional): If "other" is selected for referral type, enter the specific type.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person referring the candidate.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person referring the candidate.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person referring the candidate.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person referring the candidate.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, text, optional): Enter the company of the person referring the candidate.
* **Contact Job Title** (`contact_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the person referring the candidate.
* **Job Open Date** (`job_open_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the internship opportunity.
* **Job Closing Date** (`job_closing_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the internship opportunity.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, text, required): Enter the source of the referral, or select "other" if it's not listed.
* **Referral Source Other** (`referral_source_other`, text, optional): If "other" is selected for referral source, enter the specific source.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
 

Please note that some fields are marked as optional. However, it's essential to fill out as many fields as possible to ensure the form is filled accurately.
