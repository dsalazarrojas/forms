# Dental Implant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Implant Consent Form is a document designed to gather essential information and consent from patients before undergoing a dental implant procedure. It helps to ensure that both the patient and the dentist are on the same page regarding the procedure's details, potential risks, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Patient Information** (`patient_info`):
	* Enter your name and contact information.
	* Provide your address and contact number.
2. **Dentist Information** (`dentist_info`):
	* Enter the name of your dentist or oral surgeon.
	* Provide their contact information.
3. **Implant Details** (`implant_details`):
	* Briefly describe the implant procedure you are about to undergo.
	* Include the location and number of implants required.
4. **Procedure Risks** (`procedure_risks`):
	* List any potential risks associated with the procedure.
	* Be as detailed as possible, including any allergies or sensitivities.
5. **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`):
	* Sign your name to confirm you have read and understand the form.
6. **Dentist Signature** (`dentist_signature`):
	* Sign your name as the dentist or oral surgeon performing the procedure.
7. **Date**:
	* Enter the date of the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Patient Information** (Patient Information, text, required): Enter your basic contact information and details for communication with your dentist.
• **Dentist Information** (Dentist Information, text, required): Provide the contact information of your dentist or oral surgeon for any follow-up communication.
• **Implant Details** (Implant Details, text, required): Describe the dental implant procedure, including the location and number of implants.
• **Procedure Risks** (Procedure Risks, text, required): List any potential risks or complications associated with the procedure, including any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
• **Patient Signature** (Patient Signature, text, required): Sign to confirm you have read and understood the form.
• **Dentist Signature** (Dentist Signature, text, required): Sign as the dentist or oral surgeon performing the procedure.
• **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the procedure.

## Tips
- Please review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you are unsure about any information, discuss with your dentist before completing the form.
- Ensure both you and your dentist sign the form to confirm understanding and consent.
