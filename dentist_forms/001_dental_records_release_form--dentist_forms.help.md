# Dental Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Records Release Form is designed to collect information from patients for the release of their dental records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section.
2. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text): Enter your name and other relevant patient details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text): Describe your medical history, including any allergies, medications, or medical conditions.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, text): List any medical procedures or treatments you have received.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple): Select any allergies you have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple): Select any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Records** (`medical_records`, text): Provide any additional medical records or information.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text): Enter the name and title of your healthcare provider.
* **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, text): Enter the name of the medical facility where you received treatment.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text): Enter the name and contact information of emergency contacts.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date): Enter the date of release of your dental records.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date): Enter the expiration date of your dental records.
* **Signed** (`signed`, text): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Dental Records Release Form** (`dental_records_release_form`, text): Enter a description of the dental records being released.
* **Other Providers** (`other_providers`, select_multiple): Select any other healthcare providers you would like to include.
* **Medical Procedure Details** (`medical_procedure_details`, text): Provide any additional details about the medical procedure.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature Name** (`signature_name`, text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Signature Title** (`signature_title`, text): Enter your title or profession.
* **Signature Position** (`signature_position`, text): Enter your position or job title.
* **Signature Date Time** (`signature_date_time`, date): Enter the date and time of your signature.
* **Signature** (`signature_signature`, text): Sign your name as a final step to confirm your consent.
* **Signature Doctor** (`signature_doctor`, text): Enter the name and title of the doctor signing the form.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text): Enter the unique identifier for your medical records.
* **Medical Record Name** (`medical_record_name`, text): Enter the name of the medical record being released.
