# Cochlear Implant Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients considering cochlear implants. It helps the medical team understand the patient's hearing history, medical background, and motivations for seeking an implant. This information will aid in assessing the patient's suitability and eligibility for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in the following fields:
    - **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be used in medical records.
    - **Date of Birth**: Enter your birth date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
    - **Email Address**: Provide a valid email address that can be used for communication purposes.
    - **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
2.  Select the following options for your hearing loss:
    - **Duration of Hearing Loss**: Choose the duration of your hearing loss from the provided options.
    - **Type of Hearing Loss**: Select the type of hearing loss you experience (Conductive, Sensorineural, Mixed, or Unknown).
    - **Hearing Loss Progression**: Indicate whether your hearing loss is getting worse over time.
    - **Currently using Hearing Aids**: Confirm if you are currently using hearing aids.
    - **Benefit from Hearing Aids**: Rate the benefit you experience from using hearing aids.
3.  Provide details about your medical history:
    - **Other Medical Conditions**: List any other relevant health issues.
    - **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
    - **Allergies**: Mention any known drug or other allergies.
    - **Previous Surgeries**: Confirm if you have had any previous ear surgeries.
4.  Share your motivations for seeking a cochlear implant:
    - **Hearing Loss Duration**: (This field is not actually part of the form, I apologize for the mistake. Please skip it).
    - **Knowledge about Cochlear Implants**: Rate your level of knowledge about cochlear implants.
    - **Expectations from an Implant**: Describe what you hope to achieve with an implant.
    - **Quality of Life Impact**: Describe the impact of your hearing loss on your daily activities.
5.  Confirm your support system:
    - **Family Support**: Confirm if your family is supportive of your decision.
    - **Support Person**: If applicable, provide the name of the person who will support you during and after surgery.
6.  If you have any additional information or questions, please provide it in the **Referring Physician Name** field.
