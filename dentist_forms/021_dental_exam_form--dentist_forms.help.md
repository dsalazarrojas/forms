# dental_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental_exam_form is designed to collect and document a patient's medical and dental history, exam date, and treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields.
2. Use a computer or mobile device to access the form.
3. Enter the patient's information accurately and completely.
4. Save and print the form if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant identifying information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medications, or previous medical conditions.
* **Dental History** (`dental_history`, text, required: false): Record the patient's dental history, including any previous treatments, dental work, or notable dental issues.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the examination.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select one, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the patient has a medical condition.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition_options`, text, required: false): List any additional medical conditions the patient may have.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of the treatment plan.
* **Dentist Signature** (`dentist_signature`, note, required: false): Add any notes or comments from the dentist.
* **Practice Info** (`practice_info`, text, required: false): Enter the practice's name and address.
* **Practice** (`practice_info_name`, text, required: false): Enter the practice's name.
* **Address** (`practice_info_address`, text, required: false): Enter the practice's address.
* **Phone** (`practice_info_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the practice's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the patient or consult with other healthcare professionals if necessary.
* This form is for internal use only and is not meant to be shared or used for external purposes. 
Please note that this form does not collect any sensitive or protected data. 
You can contact us at form@gic.mx for any questions or concerns.
