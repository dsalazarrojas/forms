# Dental Office Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Office Discharge Form is designed to collect essential information from patients before discharging them from the dental office. It is meant to provide a clear and concise way to document the patient's visit, ensuring that all necessary details are gathered and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Describe the procedure performed in the "Procedure Description" field.
3. Provide post-treatment instructions in the "Post Treatment Instructions" field.
4. List any medications prescribed in the "Medication" field.
5. Share the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
6. Report any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
7. Select one of the available options for "Patient Signature" and "Doctor Signature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name, as it appears on their medical records.
* **Procedure Description**: Describe the dental procedure performed, including any notable findings or results.
* **Post Treatment Instructions**: Provide clear instructions for the patient to follow after treatment, such as dietary restrictions or medication use.
* **Medication**: List any medications prescribed to the patient, including dosage and frequency.
* **Medical History**: Share any relevant medical history that may impact the patient's care.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Patient Signature**: Select one of the provided options to indicate the patient's understanding and agreement with the treatment and instructions.
* **Doctor Signature**: Select one of the provided options to indicate the doctor's verification and approval of the patient's information and treatment.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are accurately completed before signing the form.
- Review the form thoroughly to ensure that all necessary information is included.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Store the completed form in the patient's file for future reference.
