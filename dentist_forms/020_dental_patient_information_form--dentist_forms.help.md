# Dental Patient Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dental Patient Information Form is designed to gather essential details about a dental patient, enabling the dentist to provide personalized care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields accurately.
2. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY for the Date of Birth field.
3. Enter your address in the format of Street Number, Street Name, City, State, Zip.
4. If you have a medical condition or allergy, select the relevant option from the drop-down menu, or enter a description if the condition is not listed.
5. If you have an allergy, you can select multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address 1**: Enter the street number of your address.
* **Address 2**: Enter the street name or additional address information.
* **City**: Enter the city of your address.
* **State**: Enter the state of your address.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Medical History**: This is a text field for you to provide any relevant medical history or conditions that may be important for your dental care.
* **Medical Condition**: Select from the drop-down menu if you have a condition such as Diabetes, Epilepsy, Allergies, or Heart Condition. If you have another condition, select "Other" and enter a description.
* **More Information**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide more information about your medical condition.
* **Allergies**: Select from the drop-down menu if you have allergies such as Peanut, Shellfish, or Sulfites. If you have another allergy, select "Other" and enter a description.
* **Allergies (more information)**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide more information about your allergy.
