# Denture Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent from patients for denture procedures or treatment. It contains fields for patient and dentist details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the **Patient Name** field, entering your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- Enter your **Patient Email** in the email field. If you don't have an email address, you can leave this field blank.
- In the **Dentist Name** field, write in the name of the dentist who will be performing the procedure.
- Enter the phone number of the dentist in the **Dentist Phone** field.
- Fill in the **Dentist Office** field with the name of the dental office or clinic where the procedure will take place.
