# dental_history - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather dental and medical history from patients, providing healthcare professionals with the necessary information for examination and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Patient Information" section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your medical history in the "Medical History" section, including any past medical conditions, medications, or medical tests you have undergone.
3. List your current and past medications in the "Medication List" section.
4. In the "Dental History" section, include any past dental procedures or treatments you have received.
5. List any allergies you may have in the "Allergies" section.
6. If you have any medical conditions not listed, provide more information in the "Medical Condition Other" and "Medical Condition Other Description" sections.
7. List any past surgeries, medical tests, or procedures in the "Surgical History" section.
8. In the "Family History" section, provide any relevant information about your family medical history.
9. List any medical procedures that may affect your health in the "Medical Procedure History" section.
10. If you have a regular dentist, provide their name, phone number, and address in the "Dental History Dentist" sections.
11. If you have a previous dentist, list their name in the "Previous Dentist" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (1)**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History (2)**: Provide any past medical conditions, medications, or medical tests you have undergone.
* **Medication List (3)**: List all current and past medications taken.
* **Dental History (4)**: Include any past dental procedures or treatments received.
* **Allergies (5)**: List any allergies you may have.
* **Medical History Additional Info (6)**: Provide more information about your medical history.
* **Medical Condition (7)**: Choose any medical conditions you may have from the list (Asthma, Diabetes, High Blood Pressure).
* **Medical Condition Other (8)**: If none of the above apply, specify another medical condition.
* **Medical Condition Other Description (9)**: Provide more information about your medical condition.
* **Medications (10)**: List any past or current medications taken.
* **Medical Test Results (11)**: Provide any medical test results or information.
* **Surgical History (12)**: List any past surgeries or medical procedures undergone.
* **Family History (13)**: Provide any relevant information about your family medical history.
* **Medical Procedure History (14)**: List any medical procedures that may affect your health.
* **Dental History Dentist (15)**: List the name of your general dentist or dentist that you see regularly.
* **Dental History Dentist Phone (16)**: List the phone number of your general dentist or dentist that you see regularly.
* **Dental History Dentist Address (17)**: List the address of your general dentist or dentist that you see regularly.
* **Previous Dentist (18)**: List the name of your previous or current general dentist.
