# Dentist Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dentist Job Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to apply for a position as a dentist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields.
2. Describe your relevant work experience in the "Work Experience" field.
3. Specify your level of education (Bachelor, Master, or Doctoral) in the "Education" field.
4. Select your relevant certifications (None, True, or Partially) in the "Certification" field.
5. Choose your language proficiency (English or Spanish) in the "Language" field.
6. Attach your resume in the "Resume" field.
7. Specify your availability for the position in the "Availability" field.
8. Select the date and time you are available to start in the "Availability Date" and "Availability Time" fields.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your resume or identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Work Experience** (text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience, including any relevant positions, job titles, and work details.
* **Education** (select_one, required): Select your highest level of education (Bachelor, Master, or Doctoral).
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter any relevant job titles or positions you have held.
* **Years in Practice** (number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been in practice.
* **Certification** (select_multiple, required): Select all relevant certifications you hold.
* **Language** (select_one, optional): Select your primary language (English or Spanish).
* **Resume** (note, optional): Attach your resume for consideration.
* **License** (select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant licenses you hold.
* **Availability** (text, optional): Describe your availability for the position.
* **Availability Date** (date, optional): Select the date you are available to start.
* **Availability Time** (time, optional): Select the time you are available to start.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to submit your application.
* **Submit Button** (text, required): This is a placeholder for the submit button.
* **Submit Success Message** (text, optional): Enter a success message for the submit process.
* **Submit Cancel Message** (text, optional): Enter a cancel message for the submit process.
* **Submit Error Message** (text, optional): Enter an error message for the submit process.
* **Form Submit Success** (text, optional): Enter a success message for the form submission process.
* **Form Submit Error** (text, optional): Enter an error message for the form submission process.
