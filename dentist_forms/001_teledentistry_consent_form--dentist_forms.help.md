# Teledentistry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teledentistry Consent Form is a document used to collect patient information and consent for teledentistry services. It is designed to ensure that patients understand and agree to the terms of their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Patient Info" section with your personal details.
2. Enter your "Contact Info" in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Select the type of care you are seeking (Telehealth, Phone, or In-Person) in the "What kind of care are you seeking?" dropdown menu.
4. If applicable, enter the date and time of your appointment in the "Consent Date" and "Consent Time" fields.
5. Add any additional notes about your care in the "Additional Notes" field.
6. Enter your phone number and email address in the respective fields.
7. Indicate whether this form was signed by checking the "Was this form signed?" dropdown menu.
8. If applicable, enter the date and time when you signed the form.
9. Finally, indicate whether the form has been submitted by checking the "Form submitted?" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (text): Enter your personal details, such as name and address.
* **Contact Info** (text): Enter your contact information, such as phone number and email address.
* **What kind of care are you seeking?** (select_one): Choose from the dropdown menu to select the type of care you are seeking (Telehealth, Phone, or In-Person).
* **Consent Date** (date): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Consent Time** (time): Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Additional Notes** (note): Enter any additional notes about your care.
* **Phone Number** (number): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Was this form signed?** (select_one): Check the dropdown menu to indicate whether this form was signed.
* **Date signed** (date): Enter the date when you signed the form.
* **Time signed** (time): Enter the time when you signed the form.
* **Form submitted?** (select_one): Check the dropdown menu to indicate whether the form has been submitted.
