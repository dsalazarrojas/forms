To assess the risk of caries (cavity) in patients, this form collects information about their demographics, clinical history, and lifestyle factors. The form is designed to evaluate the presence of risk factors that may contribute to tooth decay, such as cavities, gum disease, and behavioral habits like diet and oral hygiene practices. By answering the questions, the form aims to provide a quantitative assessment of the patient's overall caries risk level.

This form is intended for dental professionals to evaluate patients with varying degrees of caries risk. Patients with a low risk level are generally considered to have a lower chance of developing cavities, while those with a high risk level are more likely to be at risk of cavies.

**Please note:** The risk assessment results are based on the information provided by the patient and should not be used as a definitive diagnosis. A dentist's evaluation and professional judgment are always necessary for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

# Caries Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Caries Risk Assessment Form is designed to help dental professionals evaluate a patient's caries risk level based on their demographic, clinical history, and lifestyle factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the patient's contact information (page 1).
2. Answer all questions on subsequent pages, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age** (`patient_age`): Enter the patient's age.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): Enter the date of the caries risk assessment.
* **Any visible cavities** (`visible_cavities`): Answer whether the patient has had any cavities in the past 12 months.
* **White spot lesions** (`white_spot_lesions`): Answer whether the patient has experienced white spot lesions (early demineralization) on their teeth.
* **Existing fillings or restorations** (`existing_restorations`): Answer how many fillings or restorations the patient has on their teeth.
* **Signs of gum disease or gingivitis** (`gum_disease`): Answer the level of gum disease or gingivitis the patient is experiencing.
* **Visible plaque or biofilm on teeth** (`plaque_buildup`): Answer the level of visible plaque or biofilm on the patient's teeth.
* **How often do you brush your teeth daily?** (`brushing_frequency`): Answer how often the patient brushes their teeth daily.
* **Do you use fluoride toothpaste or rinse?** (`uses_fluoride`): Answer whether the patient uses fluoride toothpaste or rinse regularly.
* **Do you floss between teeth?** (`flosses_regularly`): Answer how often the patient flosses between teeth.
* **Frequency of sugary drinks consumption** (`sugary_drinks`): Answer how often the patient consumes sugary drinks.
* **How often do you snack between meals?** (`snacking_frequency`): Answer how often the patient snacks between meals.
* **Frequent consumption of sticky or sugary foods** (`sticky_foods`): Answer whether the patient frequently consumes sticky or sugary foods.
* **Received professional fluoride treatment in past year?** (`professional_fluoride`): Answer whether the patient has received professional fluoride treatment in the past year.
* **Does your water supply contain fluoride?** (`water_fluoridation`): Answer whether the patient's water supply contains fluoride.
* **Overall Caries Risk Level** (`overall_caries_risk`): Answer the overall caries risk level based on the provided information.
* **Clinical Notes or Additional Observations** (`clinical_notes`): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's caries risk assessment.

## Tips

* Answer all fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure accurate risk assessment.
* If you are unsure about any question, please respond with "Uncertain" or "Don't know".
* The form is designed to be used by dental professionals; please do not attempt to complete this form if you are not a qualified dentist or hygienist.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the form author at form@gic.mx.
