# Braces Adjustment Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Braces Adjustment Appointment Form is designed to collect vital information from patients to schedule their appointments for braces adjustment services at our dental clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the patient's personal details, including their full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
* Select a preferred appointment date and time.
* Specify the estimated duration of the appointment, if applicable.
* If you have any existing or ongoing concerns, please select them from the provided options.
* Confirm your appointment request by checking the "Confirmed" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's full name, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`patient_email`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's email address for appointment confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Select a date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required): Select a time slot for the appointment (morning or afternoon).
* **Estimated Duration Needed** (`appointment_duration`, `select_one`, optional): If you have a specific appointment duration in mind, select one of the options provided.
* **Current Braces Type** (`treatment_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the type of braces the patient currently has.
* **How Long Have You Had Braces** (`treatment_duration`, `text`, optional): Describe how long the patient has had braces.
* **Date of Last Adjustment** (`last_adjustment_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the patient's last adjustment.
* **Current Concerns or Issues** (`patient_concerns`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any current concerns or issues the patient is experiencing with their braces.
* **Detailed Description of Concerns** (`additional_concerns`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the patient's concerns.
* **Daily Brushing Habits** (`brushing_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the patient's daily brushing habits.
* **Daily Flossing Habits** (`flossing_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the patient's daily flossing habits.
* **Appointment Confirmation** (`appointment_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Check the "Confirmed" option to schedule the appointment.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth appointment process.
* If you experience any issues or have further concerns, please contact our clinic staff.
* This form is not a substitute for a professional dental consultation. If you have specific questions or concerns, please contact our clinic directly.
* A confirmation of your appointment request will be sent to you via email once this form is submitted.
