# Dental Health History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information about your dental health history. This information will help our healthcare professionals assess your overall health and provide you with the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Patient Info**: This section is where you can provide your basic contact information, such as your name and contact details.
2. **Medical History**: In this section, you can list any medical conditions or allergies you have.
3. **Dental Health**: Please provide any relevant information about your dental health, including any issues you are experiencing.
4. **Allergies**: Please select any allergies you have from the dropdown list provided.
5. **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
6. **Medical Notes**: If you have any additional medical information you would like to share with our team, this is the place to do so.
7. **Last Visit**: Please provide the date and time of your last visit to our dental clinic.
8. **Next Visit Scheduled**: If you have a scheduled visit, please provide the date of your next visit.
9. **Patient Name**: This is your name, as it will appear on your dental records.
10. **Contact Info**: You can enter any additional contact information you would like us to have on file.
11. **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info**: A form to enter your name and contact information.
* **Medical History**: A text field to list any medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Dental Health**: A text field to provide information about your dental health.
* **Allergies**: Select all allergies you experience from the provided options (None, Yes).
* **Medications**: A text field to list medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Notes**: A text field for any additional medical information.
* **Date Last Visit**: A date field for your last visit to the clinic.
* **Time Last Visit**: A time field for your last visit's time.
* **Next Visit Scheduled**: A date field for your next scheduled visit.
* **Patient Name**: A field to enter your name, as it will appear on your dental records.
* **Contact Info**: A text field for any additional contact information.
* **Emergency Contact**: A text field for your emergency contact's name and contact details.
