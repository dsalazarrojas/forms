# Oral Surgery Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer patients to an oral surgery specialist. It helps gather information about the patient's medical history, potential allergies, and the specifics of the planned surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Information** (`id: 1`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's basic information, including name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. **Medical History** (`id: 2`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
3. **Allergy and Contraindication** (`id: 3`, text, required: false): List any known allergies or contraindications that may affect the patient's treatment.
4. **Medical Conditions** (`id: 4`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if the patient has any of the following medical conditions:
	* Yes
	* No
5. **Surgical Plan** (`id: 5`, text, required: false): Outline the planned surgery, including the procedure, date, and time.
6. **Surgical Notes** (`id: 6`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or details about the planned surgery.
7. **Patient Goals** (`id: 7`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's goals and expectations for the surgery.
8. **Addendum** (`id: 8`, note, required: false): Add any additional information that is not included in the other fields.
9. **Consent** (`id: 9`, select_one, required: false): Check the box if the patient has given consent for the surgery:
	* Yes
	* No
10. **Signature** (`id: 10`, text, required: false): Include the patient's signature and date for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's basic information, including name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Allergy and Contraindication**: List any known allergies or contraindications that may affect the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Conditions**: Check if the patient has any of the listed medical conditions.
* **Surgical Plan**: Outline the planned surgery, including the procedure, date, and time.
* **Surgical Notes**: Add any additional notes or details about the planned surgery.
* **Patient Goals**: Describe the patient's goals and expectations for the surgery.
* **Addendum**: Add any additional information that is not included in the other fields.
* **Consent**: Check the box if the patient has given consent for the surgery.
* **Signature**: Include the patient's signature and date for verification.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consents from the patient before completing this form.
* Complete the form accurately and truthfully to ensure the best possible care for the patient.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the oral surgery specialist.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant healthcare professionals for guidance.
