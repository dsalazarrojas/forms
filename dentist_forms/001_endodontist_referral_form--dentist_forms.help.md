# endodontist_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The endodontist_referral_form is designed to collect information for referrals to endodontists from dentists. The form captures the necessary details about the patient and the referral process to ensure a smooth transition of care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the referring dentist.
2. Provide the patient's name.
3. Enter the patient's email address.
4. Enter the patient's phone number.
5. Enter the patient's address.
6. Enter the address of the dentist's office.
7. Select the procedure(s) that the patient is being referred for.
8. Add any additional notes about the patient's case.
9. Sign and date the form as the referring dentist.
10. Sign and date the form as the endodontist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dentist Name** (text): Enter the name of the referring dentist.
* **Patient Name** (text): Enter the name of the patient being referred.
* **Patient Email** (email): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (text): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Address** (text): Enter the patient's address.
* **Dentist Address** (text): Enter the address of the dentist's office.
* **Procedure** (text): Select the procedure(s) that the patient is being referred for.
* **Notes** (text): Add any additional information about the patient's case.
* **Dentist Signature** (text): The referring dentist must sign and date this form.
* **Endodontist Signature** (text): The endodontist must sign and date this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Ensure that the form is signed and dated by both the referring dentist and the endodontist.
- Keep the form accurate and up-to-date.
- Use this form for referrals to endodontists only.
