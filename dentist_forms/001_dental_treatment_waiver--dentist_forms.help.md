# Dental Treatment Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients before undergoing dental treatment. The information collected will help the dentist or clinic staff to assess the patient's medical history, authorize treatment, and obtain consent from the patient or their emergency contact.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the patient.
*   Make sure to review each field carefully and provide detailed answers where possible.
*   If a field is not applicable, please mark it as "Not Applicable" or "Unknown".
*   Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it to the dentist or clinic staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (`general_information`, Text, Optional): This field is used to collect basic information about the patient, such as their name, date of birth, and contact details.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Text, Optional): This field is used to collect information about the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or medications they are currently taking.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Optional): This field is used to collect contact information for the patient's emergency contact, such as a family member or friend.
*   **Dentist Authorization** (`dentist_authorization`, Text, Optional): This field is used to collect the name and contact information of the patient's dentist or clinic staff member who will be authorizing the treatment.
*   **Submission** (`submission`, Text, Optional): This field is used to collect any additional comments or information about the patient's submission, such as their consent or any specific instructions.
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, Text, Optional): This field is used to collect the patient's signature, indicating their consent to the treatment.
