# Dental Lab Work Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by dental clinics or offices to gather necessary information from patients before performing lab work. It collects patient contact information, medical history, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact E-Mail" field.
3. Add your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Describe your insurance information in the "Insurance Information" field.
5. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
6. Select the types of patient information you are consenting to share in the "Consent" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
8. Enter the time of your visit in the "Time of Visit" field in HH:MM format.
9. Sign the "Patient Signature" field to indicate your consent.
10. Your dentist or practitioner will sign the "Practitioner Signature" field to confirm their agreement.
11. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
12. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
13. Enter the emergency contact person's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
14. Enter the emergency contact person's email address in the "Emergency Contact E-Mail" field.
15. Provide the emergency contact person's address in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
16. Enter the emergency contact person's city in the "Emergency Contact City" field.
17. Enter the emergency contact person's state in the "Emergency Contact State" field.
18. Enter the emergency contact person's zip code in the "Emergency Contact Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
*   **Contact E-Mail** (`Contact E-Mail`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Add your contact phone number.
*   **Insurance Information** (`Insurance Information`, `text`, required): Describe your insurance details.
*   **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant medical history.
*   **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of patient information you are consenting to share.
*   **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Time of Visit** (`Time of Visit`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit in HH:MM format.
*   **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `note`, required): Sign to indicate your consent.
*   **Practitioner Signature** (`Practitioner Signature`, `note`, required): Your dentist or practitioner will sign to confirm their agreement.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, required): Provide your emergency contact person's name.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact E-Mail** (`Emergency Contact E-Mail`, `email`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's email address.
*   **Emergency Contact Address** (`Emergency Contact Address`, `text`, required): Provide the emergency contact person's address.
*   **Emergency Contact City** (`Emergency Contact City`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's city.
*   **Emergency Contact State** (`Emergency Contact State`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's state.
*   **Emergency Contact Zip** (`Emergency Contact Zip`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's zip code.
