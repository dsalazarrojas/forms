# Dental Insurance Breakdown Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Insurance Breakdown Form is used to gather information about a patient's dental insurance breakdown for the purpose of processing claims or payments related to dental procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are authorized to access and complete this form.
2. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
4. Select the insurance provider in the "Insurance Provider" field.
5. Enter the date of the procedure in the "Procedure Date" field.
6. Select the time of the procedure in the "Procedure Time" field.
7. Enter the total charges for the procedure in the "Total Charges" field.
8. Enter the amount covered by insurance in the "Covered Amount" field.
9. Select the payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
10. Sign the form in the "Patient Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, not required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification or medical records.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, not required): Enter the patient's address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, not required): Enter the name of the patient's insurance provider.
* **Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the dental procedure.
* **Procedure Time** (`procedure_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the dental procedure.
* **Total Charges** (`total_charges`, number, not required): Enter the total charges for the procedure.
* **Covered Amount** (`covered_amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount covered by insurance.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Select the payment method used for the procedure. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, not required): Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
