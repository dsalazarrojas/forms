# Dental HIPAA Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's visit to a medical facility, ensuring HIPAA compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields accurately and with the correct information.
2. Ensure you use the correct format for date inputs (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Choose one of the provided options for selection inputs.
4. Sign the signature fields with your genuine signature.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
