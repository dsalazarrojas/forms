# Dental HIPAA Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's visit to a medical facility, ensuring HIPAA compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields accurately and with the correct information.
2. Ensure you use the correct format for date inputs (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Choose one of the provided options for selection inputs.
4. Sign the signature fields with your genuine signature.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the medical provider.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Provider Address** (`medical_provider_address`, text, required): Enter the medical provider's address.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required): Enter the date of service in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, text, required): Enter the authorization information.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign with your genuine signature.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, required): Sign with your genuine signature.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of service.
* **Patient IPI** (`patient_ipi`, text, required): Enter the patient's IPI.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required): Enter the date of authorization in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter the patient's ID.
* **Medical Provider IPI** (`medical_provider_ipi`, text, required): Enter the medical provider's IPI.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for allergies.
* **Medical Provider Notes** (`medical_provider_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes from the medical provider.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical conditions.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required): Sign with your genuine consent.
* **Medical History Notes** (`medical_history_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the patient's medical history.
* **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, select_one, required): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' for the patient's medical status.
* **Medical Provider Specialization** (`medical_provider_specialization`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the medical provider's specialization.
* **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, select_one, required): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' for the patient's medical status.
* **Medical History Notes** (`medical_history_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the patient's medical history.
