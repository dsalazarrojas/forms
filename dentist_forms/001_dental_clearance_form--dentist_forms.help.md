# dental_clearance_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for dental professionals to obtain information from patients prior to dental procedures. It collects patient information, medical history, and treatment plans to ensure a safe and effective treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal details under "Patient Information".
2. Provide any relevant medical history under "Medical History".
3. List any dental conditions or previous dental work done under "Dental Conditions".
4. Outline any pre-surgical treatment plans under "Treatment Plans".
5. If the patient has any allergies, list them under "Patient Allergies".
6. Specify any current medications the patient is taking under "Current Medications".
7. Report any recent medical tests or exams under "Medical Tests".
8. Indicate the patient's eligibility for dental treatment options under "Dental Treatment Options".
9. Determine the patient's eligibility for medical treatment options under "Medical Treatment Options".
10. Choose whether any surgical options are required under "Surgical Options".
11. Decide on the use of dental anesthesia under "Dental Anesthesia".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (1)**:
	+ This field collects the patient's basic information, such as their name, contact details, and identification.
* **Medical History (2)**:
	+ Please enter any relevant medical information about the patient that may affect their treatment.
* **Dental Conditions (3)**:
	+ Describe any pre-existing dental conditions or past dental work done on the patient.
* **Treatment Plans (4)**:
	+ Describe the treatment plan you have recommended for the patient.
* **Patient Allergies (5)**:
	+ List any allergies the patient has that we should be aware of.
* **Current Medications (6)**:
	+ List any medications the patient is currently taking that may affect their treatment.
* **Medical Tests (7)**:
	+ Report any recent medical tests or exams that may affect their treatment.
* **Dental Treatment Options (8)**:
	+ Check "Yes" if the patient is eligible for dental treatment, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Treatment Options (9)**:
	+ Check "Yes" if the patient is eligible for medical treatment, "No" otherwise.
* **Surgical Options (10)**:
	+ Check "Yes" if any surgical options are required, "No" otherwise.
* **Dental Anesthesia (11)**:
	+ Check "Yes" if dental anesthesia is required, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your dentist or healthcare professional for guidance.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used to diagnose or treat any medical condition.
