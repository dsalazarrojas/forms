# Denture Treatment Cost Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information necessary for a dentist to provide an accurate cost estimate for denture treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and other relevant information.
2. Select your Gender.
3. Provide your Contact Info for follow-up communication.
4. If necessary, upload any relevant medical images.
5. Add any additional notes for the doctor.
6. Add any additional notes for the patient.
7. Select the name of the doctor who will be performing the treatment.
8. Enter your Email and Phone Number for communication.
9. Provide your home Address.
10. Choose the desired treatment options.
11. Enter any medical conditions or issues.
12. Provide any relevant medical history.
13. Select the type of denture you need.
14-19. Confirm the type of denture you are interested in by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
