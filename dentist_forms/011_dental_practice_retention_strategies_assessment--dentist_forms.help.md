# Dental Practice Retention Strategies Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help dental practices assess their retention strategies, identifying areas of improvement and best practices to better serve their patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each field as thoroughly as possible.
2. Select only the options that apply to your practice.
3. If a field is not relevant, you may leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification (1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, or account number to help us identify the patient.
* **Current Retention Efforts (2)**: Select the level of retention efforts your practice currently uses (Excellent, Good, or Fair).
* **Retention Efforts Rating (3)**: Rate the effectiveness of your current retention efforts (1-5).
* **Comparison with Others (4)**: Select how your practice compares with others in terms of retention efforts (Above, Average, or Below).
* **Improvement Strategies (5)**: Describe any strategies you are using or plan to use to improve retention (e.g., regularly reviewing schedules, responding to online reviews, etc.).
* **Current Strategies Used (6)**: Select the strategies your practice is currently using to improve retention (e.g., reviewing schedules, responding to online reviews, etc.).
* **Effectiveness of Current Strategies (7)**: Rate the effectiveness of your current strategies (Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, or Not Very Effective).
* **Areas for Improvement (8)**: Note any areas where you believe your practice needs improvement.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (9)**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving your practice's retention strategies.
