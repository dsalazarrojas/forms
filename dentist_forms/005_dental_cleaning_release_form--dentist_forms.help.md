# dental_cleaning_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their dental cleaning experience, including their personal details, contact information, dental history, allergies, medical conditions, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient's Name" field.
2. Provide the contact information (email address or phone number) in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Describe the patient's dental history in the "Dental History" field.
4. Answer the question "Are there any allergies?" in the "Allergies" field.
5. If the patient has allergies, list them in the "Allergies" field. Otherwise, select "No".
6. Provide medical condition information in the "Medical Condition" field.
7. Answer the question "Medical Aid" in the "Medical Aid" field.
8. Select the medical aid option that applies to the patient.
9. Enter the name of the dentist performing the procedure in the "Dentist's Name" field.
10. If applicable, provide additional medical history in the "Additional Medical History" field.
11. List the patient's medications in the "Medications" field, if applicable.
12. Answer the question "Consent" in the "Consent" field.
13. Select the consent option that applies to the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient's Name** (`text`, required): Enter the full name of the patient.
* **Contact Information** (`text`, required): Enter the patient's email address or phone number.
* **Dental History** (`text`, required): Describe the patient's dental history.
* **Allergies** (`select_multiple`, required): Answer "Yes" if the patient has allergies, and list them in the field.
* **Medical Condition** (`text`, required): Provide medical condition information.
* **Medical Aid** (`select_one`, required): Select the medical aid that applies to the patient.
* **Dentist's Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the dentist performing the procedure.
* **Additional Medical History** (`text`, optional): Provide additional medical history if required.
* **Medications** (`select_multiple`, optional): List the patient's medications if applicable.
* **Consent** (`select_one`, required): Select the consent option that applies to the patient.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the patient or a healthcare professional.
