# Dentist Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from claimants about their claim experiences with a dentist. It helps gather relevant details for the claimant's claim process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the form carefully and accurately with your claimant information.
- Make sure to provide all necessary details, especially your name, address, contact information, insurance provider, and dental condition.
- Select the correct payment method if you are using one.
- If you have a referring dentist, please indicate so.
- Provide accurate information about your clinical diagnosis, treatment type, procedure code, and procedure cost.
