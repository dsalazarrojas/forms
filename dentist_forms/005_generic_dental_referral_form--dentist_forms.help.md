# Generic Dental Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients or dentists for a referral purpose. The form is used to collect patient information, dental referral details, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Patient Information** (1. **Patient Name**, text, **required**): Please fill in the patient's name.
2. **Dentist Referral** (2. **Dentist Referral**, select_multiple, **not required**): Select 'Yes' if you are referring the patient to a dentist or 'No' if not.
3. **Patient Contact** (3. **Patient Contact**, text, **not required**): Fill in the patient's contact information, if available.
4. **Medical Information** (4. **Patient Medical Info**, text, **not required**): Provide the patient's medical information, if applicable.
5. **Dentist Information** (5. **Dentist Info**, text, **not required**): Fill in the dentist's information, if necessary.
6. **Referral Reason** (6. **Referral Reason**, select_one, **not required**): Select the reason for the referral from the available options:
	* Dental Emergency
	* General Checkup
	* Dental Implants
	* Oral Surgery
	* Periodontal Disease
	* Other
7. **Referral Information** (7. **Referral Info**, text, **not required**): Provide any additional information about the referral.
8. **Referral Location** (8. **Referral Location**, text, **not required**): Fill in the referral location, if applicable.
9. **Referral Details** (9. **Referral Details**, text, **not required**): Provide any additional details about the referral.
10. **Medical History** (10. **Medical History**, text, **not required**): Fill in the patient's medical history, if necessary.

## Tips

- Please ensure all fields are filled out thoroughly and accurately.
- Mark fields as 'N/A' or 'Not Applicable' if they do not apply to the patient or referral.
- Consult with the patient and obtain their consent for referral before filling out this form.
