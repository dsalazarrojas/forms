# dental_patient_information_update_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update or collect new dental patient information. It is essential to fill it out accurately and completely to ensure that the dental practice has up-to-date and correct information about the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the patient's information.
2. Enter the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field (if applicable).
8. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Number" field.
9. Enter the emergency contact person's name in the "Emergency Contact Person" field (if applicable).
10. Enter the emergency contact person's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Number" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person the patient would like to contact with regards to their treatment. This field is optional.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the person listed in "Contact Person".
* **Emergency Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person the patient would like to be contacted in case of an emergency. This field is optional.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the person listed in "Emergency Contact Person".
* **Medical Insurance**: Enter the patient's medical insurance information, if applicable.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address, if applicable.
* **Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions or allergies the patient may have, if applicable.
* **Medical Insurance**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if the patient has medical insurance. 

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If a field is marked as optional, it is only necessary if the information is applicable or relevant.
* Check the patient's existing records before updating their information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the patient or their legal representative for clarification.
