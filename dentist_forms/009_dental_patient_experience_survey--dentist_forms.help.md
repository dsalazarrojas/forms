# Dental Patient Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Dental Patient Experience Survey form. The form is intended to gather feedback about your experience at the dental clinic, and your input will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the option that best describes your experience with our clinic from the **Patient Survey** section.
2. If you are comfortable sharing, enter your appointment details, such as the date, time, and services received, in the **Appointment Details** field.
3. In the **Dental Services** section, select all the services you received during your visit.
4. Rate your experience with the dental staff by selecting an option in the **Staff Experience** field.
5. Choose how you would describe your overall experience in the **Patient Experience** field.
6. Provide feedback on your experience with the dentist by selecting options in the **Dentist Feedback** field.
7. In the **Staff Suggestions** field, provide any suggestions or comments you have about the staff.
8. Indicate whether or not you would recommend the clinic to others by selecting an option in the **Patient Recommend** field.
9. Rate your overall experience in the **Overall Experience** field.
10. Enter the date and time you visited the clinic in the **Date Visited** and **Time Visited** fields, respectively.
11. Enter the time you arrived and finished your appointment in the **Time Arrived** and **Time Finished** fields, respectively.
12. If you underwent a surgical procedure, enter the date of the surgery in the **Date of Surgery** field.
