# Dental Excuse Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document a student's absence from school due to dental excursions or appointments with a dentist. It provides necessary information to school administrators to process the student's excusal.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Start by filling out your name and contact information in the first few fields.
- Then provide your student's name and date of birth.
- Next, fill out the details of the dental appointment: reason for absence, date of the appointment, time, and duration.
- Provide your relationship with the student and your contact information.
- Finally, sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, **required**): The student's name.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, **required**): The student's date of birth.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, **optional**): The name of the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, **required**): A brief explanation of why the student is absent.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, **optional**): The student's current grade level (yes or no).
* **Parent Relationship** (`parent_relationship`, select_one, **optional**): Whether you are the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Dentist Name** (`dentist_name`, text, **required**): The name of the student's dentist.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, **required**): The date of the dental appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, **required**): The time of the dental appointment.
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, text, **optional**): The length of the dental appointment.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, **optional**): The parent's contact phone number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, **required**): The name of the school the student attends.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, **optional**): The name of the student's teacher.
* **Doctor Note** (`doctor_note`, note, **required**): Additional comments from the dentist or doctor.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, **optional**): The student's signature confirming the information.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, **optional**): The parent's signature confirming the information.
* **School Signature** (`school_signature`, text, **optional**): The school administrator's signature confirming the information.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, **optional**): The dentist's or doctor's signature confirming the information.
* **School Name 2** (`school_name_2`, text, **optional**): The name of the school (if the student has attended multiple schools).
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, **optional**): The school's contact phone number.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, **optional**): The teacher's contact phone number.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, **optional**): The school's contact email address.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, **optional**): The dentist or doctor's contact phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, **optional**): The student's home address.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, **optional**): The school's address.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the school administrator for guidance.
- Please sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
- The completed form can be used as a document to process the student's excusal from school.
