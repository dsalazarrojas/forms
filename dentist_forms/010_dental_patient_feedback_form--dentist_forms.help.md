# dental_patient_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients about their experiences at the dentist's office. This form helps dentists understand their patients' satisfaction levels and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your feedback in the "Patient Feedback" text field.
2. Rate your overall experience (if applicable) using the number rating system provided.
3. Specify your rating value (if any) in the "Rating Value" field.
4. Enter your feedback about the dentist in the "Dentist Feedback" text field.
5. Select the date and time of your visit using the date and time picker (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Feedback (Patient Feedback)**: Provide your thoughts and experiences about your visit to the dentist's office. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Rating (rating)**: Rate your overall experience at the dentist's office. This field is optional and only applicable if you have had an experience at the dentist's office.
* **Rating Value (rating_value)**: If you have rated your experience, please specify the value of your rating (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
* **Dentist Feedback (dentist_feedback)**: Share your thoughts and experiences about the dentist. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Date (date)**: Select the date of your visit to the dentist's office. This field is optional and only applicable if you have had an experience at the dentist's office.
* **Time (time)**: Select the time of your visit to the dentist's office. This field is optional and only applicable if you have had an experience at the dentist's office.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback to help us improve our services.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please address them in the "Dentist Feedback" field.
* If you have any suggestions or compliments, please specify them in the "Patient Feedback" field.
* This form is designed to be completed by the patient, but if you are a parent/guardian completing the form on behalf of a minor, please ensure that the information provided is accurate and reflects the child's experiences.
