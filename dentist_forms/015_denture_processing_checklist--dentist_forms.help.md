# Denture Processing Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document relevant information during the denture processing workflow. It covers key aspects such as patient details, dental history, impression information, fabrication details, quality control, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Read the form title and understand its purpose.
2. Fill out the "Patient Info" section by selecting one of the provided options for "Patient Name", "Date of Birth", "ID Number", "Address", and "Phone Number" and "Email".
3. Provide dental history by selecting all relevant options under "Dental History" that apply to the current case.
4. In the "Impression Info" section, select one of the options for "Impression Material", "Impression Type", and "Impression Size".
5. In the "Fabrication Info" section, select one option for "Fabrication Method", "Fabrication Type", and "Fabrication Date".
6. In the "Quality Control" section, choose all relevant options that apply to the current case.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.
8. Select a reviewer or status for the current case in the "Review" section.
9. Provide a signature in the "Signature" section.
10. Finally, enter the date and time of completion in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text): This is a brief introduction section where you can provide any relevant context or information about the current case.
* **Patient Info** (Select One/Select Multiple): Please select one of the provided options for "Patient Name", "Date of Birth", "ID Number", "Address", and "Phone Number" and "Email".
* **Dental History** (Select Multiple): Choose all the relevant options for "Dental Insurance", "Dental Coverage", and "Dental Coverage Type" that apply to the current case.
* **Impression Info** (Select One): Select one option for "Impression Material", "Impression Type", and "Impression Size" to document the patient's impression details.
* **Fabrication Info** (Select One): Choose one option for "Fabrication Method", "Fabrication Type", and "Fabrication Date" to document the denture's fabrication details.
* **Quality Control** (Select Multiple): Select all relevant options for "Quality Check", "Quality Issue", and "Quality Status" that apply to the current case.
* **Notes** (Notes): Add any additional comments or notes relevant to the current case.
* **Review** (Select One): Select a reviewer or status for the current case, such as "Review By", "Review Status", and "Review Date".
* **Signature** (Text): Provide a signature to indicate completion of the form.
* **Date Completed** (Date): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Time Completed** (Time): Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Submitted By** (Email): Enter the email of the person submitting the form.

Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
