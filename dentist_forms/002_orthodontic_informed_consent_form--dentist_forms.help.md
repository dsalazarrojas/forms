# Orthodontic Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing an orthodontic procedure. It ensures that the patient is aware of the treatment terms and conditions, as well as providing other relevant details such as contact information and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form with the patient's information and signature.
2. Ensure that the patient understands the treatment terms and conditions before signing.
3. Sign the form yourself as the dentist or orthodontic specialist.
4. Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Patient Contact Information** (`patient_contact_info`, `note`, optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including their address, phone number, and email.
* **Treatment Terms** (`treatment_terms`, `select_one`, required): Choose the acceptance of the treatment terms and conditions of this orthodontic procedure.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the signature of the patient and the dentist or orthodontic specialist.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the procedure.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the procedure.
* **Procedure Details** (`procedure_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the procedure.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's signature.
* **Dentist Signature** (`dentist_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the dentist or orthodontic specialist's signature.
* **Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the procedure (if different from the date entered above).
* **Follow-up Appointments** (`follow_up_appointments`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the frequency of follow-up appointments.
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the next follow-up appointment.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Doctor Contact** (`doctor_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the dentist or orthodontic specialist's contact information.
* **Other Contacts** (`other_contacts`, `text`, optional): Enter any other contacts that may be relevant to the patient's care.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's insurance information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the emergency contact person (if different from the patient).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the dentist or orthodontic specialist's email address.
* **Other Doctors** (`other_doctors`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose any other doctors involved in the patient's care.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): Enter any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical conditions relevant to the patient's care.
