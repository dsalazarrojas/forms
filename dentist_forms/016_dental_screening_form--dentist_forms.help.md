# Dental Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from patients and healthcare providers for dental screening purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. For multiple-select fields, choose all applicable options.
3. Use the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD) for date fields.
4. Use the email format (e.g., example@example.com) for email fields.
5. Ensure that the contact method, contact number, email address, and provider phone number are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, required): Choose the patient's gender (Male or Female).
* **Patient Race** (`patient_race`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable patient racial categories (American Indian/Alaska Native, Asian, Black or African American, Native Hawaiian or Pacific Islander, White).
* **Patient Ethnicity** (`patient_ethnicity`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable patient ethnic categories (Not Hispanic or Latino, Hispanic or Latino).
* **Patient Medical Group** (`medical_group`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical group information.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the provider's name.
* **Provider Date of Birth** (`provider_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the provider's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Provider Gender** (`provider_gender`, select_one, required): Choose the provider's gender (Male or Female).
* **Provider Race** (`provider_race`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable provider racial categories (American Indian/Alaska Native, Asian, Black or African American, Native Hawaiian or Pacific Islander, White).
* **Provider Ethnicity** (`provider_ethnicity`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable provider ethnic categories (Not Hispanic or Latino, Hispanic or Latino).
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, required): Choose the patient's language (English or Spanish).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose the contact method for follow-up visits (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number for follow-up visits.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address for follow-up visits.
* **Next Visit** (`next_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the next visit.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, time, required): Enter the follow-up time.
* **Dental Screening Questionnaire** (`dental_screening_questionnaire`, text, required): Answer the dental screening questions.
* **Oral Health** (`oral_health`, select_one, required): Choose the patient's oral health status (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): List all patient medical conditions.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): List all patient medications.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List all patient allergies.
* **Provider Phone** (`provider_phone`, text, required): Enter the provider's phone number.
* **Provider Email** (`provider_email`, email, required): Enter the provider's email address.
