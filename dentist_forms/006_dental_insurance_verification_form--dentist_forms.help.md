# Dental Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Insurance Verification Form is used to verify patient insurance information for dental procedures. Healthcare providers use this form to confirm the validity of a patient's insurance coverage before providing treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Provide the insurance details, such as the insurance carrier, policy number, and group number.
3. Enter the medical provider's information, including their name and contact details.
4. Fill out the billing information, including the patient's address and payment information.
5. Enter the insurance card information, such as the card holder's name and card number.
6. Check the claim status, if applicable.
7. Add any additional information that may be required for the verification process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)**: This field is used to collect the patient's name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Insurance Details (page 2)**: This field is used to enter the insurance carrier, policy number, and group number to verify the patient's insurance coverage.
* **Medical Provider (page 3)**: This field is used to enter the medical provider's name and contact details.
* **Billing Information (page 4)**: This field is used to enter the patient's address and payment information.
* **Insurance Card Info (page 5)**: This field is used to enter the insurance card holder's name and card number.
* **Claim Status (page 6)**: This field is used to check the claim status, if applicable.
* **Additional Information (page 7)**: This field is used to add any additional information that may be required for the verification process.
* **Submission Date (page 8)**: This field is used to enter the date the form was submitted.
