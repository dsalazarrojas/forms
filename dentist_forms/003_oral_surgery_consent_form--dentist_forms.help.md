# oral_surgery_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The oral surgery consent form is designed to gather essential information from patients before an oral surgery procedure. This form is used to collect general information, patient details, medical history, allergies, medications, and medical conditions to ensure a safe and successful operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Patient Information" field with your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
2.  In the "Medical History" field, describe any existing medical conditions that may affect your oral surgery.
3.  List any allergies or sensitivities you may have in the "Allergies" field.
4.  In the "Medications" field, list all medications you are currently taking.
5.  If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please describe them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
6.  Finally, list any medications you are taking in the "Medications List" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information**: This is an overview of the form and its purpose.
*   **Patient Information**: Please enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
*   **Medical History**: Describe any existing medical conditions that may affect your oral surgery.
*   **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
*   **Medications**: List all medications you are currently taking.
*   **Medical Conditions**: Describe any pre-existing medical conditions.
*   **Medications List**: List any medications you are taking.

## Tips

*   Please double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submission.
*   If you're unsure about any information, consult with your healthcare provider.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the best possible outcome for your oral surgery.
