# Dental Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Employee Evaluation Form is a tool to assess the performance and overall experience of a dental employee.

## How To Complete This Form

* Read each field carefully and answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
* Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as "Required").
* You can leave blank or skip optional fields if you are unsure or have no information to provide.
* Click on the submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the dental employee being evaluated.
* **Job Title**: Optional field to indicate the employee's job title.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time**: Optional field to indicate the evaluation time.
* **Evaluation Notes**: Optional field for additional comments or notes about the evaluation.
* **Overall Experience**: Briefly describe the employee's overall experience working with them.
* **Strengths and Weaknesses**: List the employee's strengths and weaknesses, if applicable.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas where the employee needs improvement, if applicable.
* **Recommendation**: Provide a recommendation for the employee's next steps, such as promotion, training, or further evaluation.
* **Evaluation Rating**: Choose one of the five options (Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor) to rate the employee's performance.
* **Comments**: Optional field for any additional comments or feedback about the employee.
