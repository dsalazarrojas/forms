# dental_treatment_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental treatment informed consent form is a document that aims to inform patients about the procedures they will undergo, potential risks and benefits, and the role of the dentist and patient in the treatment process. This form ensures that patients understand and agree to the treatment plan, including any necessary medical tests or exams, anesthesia, and aftercare instructions. It also captures emergency contact information and insurance details for billing and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. **Fill in the patient's information** on the "Patient Information" page.
2. **Provide details about the procedure** on the "Procedure Information" page.
3. **Check and sign the acknowledgment waiver** on the "Acknowledgment Waiver" page.
4. **Enter contact information** for the patient and emergency contact (if applicable) on the "Contact Information" and "Emergency Contact" pages.
5. **Provide insurance information** for billing purposes on the "Insurance Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`1`, `patient_info`, text, required: false): This page is where you will enter your personal details, such as your name, contact number, and address.
* **Procedure Information** (`2`, `procedure_info`, text, required: false): Here, you will provide details about the dental treatment you are about to receive, including the procedure name, date, and duration.
* **Acknowledgment Waiver** (`3`, `acknowledgment_waiver`, text, required: false): Read and sign this waiver carefully. It outlines the risks and benefits associated with the treatment and the role of the patient in making decisions about their care.
* **Contact Information** (`4`, `contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, for communication purposes.
* **Emergency Contact** (`5`, `emergency_contact`, text, required: false): If applicable, provide the contact information for an emergency contact, such as a family member or friend.
* **Insurance Information** (`6`, `insurance_info`, text, required: false): Fill in your insurance details for billing and coverage purposes.
