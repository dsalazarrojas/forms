# Dental Treatment Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing any dental treatment. It ensures that patients are aware of the details of the treatment and understand what to expect.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Patient Name", "Contact Number", and "Patient Email" fields.
2. Provide your contact information so that our office can reach out to you with any updates or questions.
3. Enter the date and time of your scheduled procedure in the "Procedure Date" and "Procedure Time" fields.
4. In the "Procedure Details" field, describe the dental treatment you are scheduled to receive.
5. Sign and date the form to indicate that you understand and agree to the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Name**
* Label: Patient Name
* Description: This is the patient's name as it appears on their medical record. Please enter your name as it is listed on your identification documents.

### **Contact Information**
* Label: Contact Details (optional)
* Description: This is a text area for you to provide any additional contact information, if applicable.

### **Contact Number**
* Label: Contact Number
* Description: Enter your contact phone number so that our office can reach out to you with any updates or questions.

### **Patient Email**
* Label: Patient Email
* Description: Enter your email address so that our office can communicate with you via email.

### **Procedure Date and Time**
* Label: Procedure Date/Procedure Time
* Description: Enter the date and time of your scheduled dental procedure.

### **Procedure Details**
* Label: procedure-details
* Description: Describe the details of the treatment you are scheduled to receive.

### **Signature**
* Label: Signature
* Description: Sign and date this form to indicate that you understand and agree to the treatment. Please print or sign your name.

### **Signature Date**
* Label: Signature Date
* Description: Date and sign this form to indicate your consent.
