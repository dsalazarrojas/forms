# Pet Dental Anesthetic Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from pet owners for consent and other relevant details for a pet's dental procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section as follows:

1. **Pet Information (pet_info)**: Provide a brief description of your pet, including its name, species, and age.
2. **Owner Information (owner_info)**: Provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
3. **Consent**: Please sign and date this section to indicate that you have read and understood the information provided.
4. **Dental Services**: Select all dental services that will be performed on your pet.
5. **Anesthesia Type**: Choose the type of anesthesia to be used during the procedure.
6. **Medical Condition**: If your pet has any medical conditions that may affect the procedure, please select from the list.
7. **Emergency Contact**: Provide the contact information for an emergency contact person.
8. **Emergency Contact Information**: Provide any additional information about your emergency contact.
9. **Veterinarian Signature**: The veterinarian will sign and date this section to confirm that the form has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Information (pet_info)**:
	+ A brief description of your pet.
* **Owner Information (owner_info)**:
	+ Your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Consent**:
	+ A legal document indicating that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Dental Services**:
	+ Select all dental services that will be performed on your pet, including:
		- Anesthesia
		- Dental exam
		- Dental cleaning
		- Dental procedure
		- Radiographs
		- Surgery
		- Other (specify)
* **Anesthesia Type**:
	+ Choose the type of anesthesia to be used during the procedure, including:
		- Isoflurane
		- Ketamine
		- Propofol
		- Xylazine
		- Other (specify)
* **Medical Condition**:
	+ Select from the list if your pet has any medical conditions that may affect the procedure, including:
		- Anesthesia allergy
		- Heart condition
		- Kidney condition
		- Liver condition
		- Other (specify)
* **Emergency Contact**:
	+ Provide contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Information**:
	+ Provide any additional information about your emergency contact, such as their address or phone number.
* **Veterinarian Signature**:
	+ The veterinarian will sign and date this section to confirm that the form has been completed.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the best possible care for your pet.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask your veterinarian.
* This form is a legal document and should be kept confidential.
