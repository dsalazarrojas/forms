# Occlusal Splints Design Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your preferences and design outcomes for occlusal splints. Your input will help us understand your needs and preferences for dental splints.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button when you have filled out all the required fields.
2. Fill out the fields as prompted, using the options provided in each field.
3. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Design Preferences** (`design_preferences`): This field is not required, so you can skip it if you prefer. Please provide any design preferences you have for occlusal splints.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`): Similar to "Design Preferences", this field is optional. Share any challenges you face when designing occlusal splints.
* **Design Outcomes** (`design_outcomes`): This field is also optional, and we'd like to know what you aim to achieve when designing occlusal splints.
* **Preferred Splint Material** (`preferred_splint_material`): Select all the materials you prefer for the splint, such as Acrylic, Metal, or Metal Ceramic.
* **Preferred Splint Style** (`preferred_splint_style`): Choose the style of the splint that suits your needs, including Full Contoured, Partial Contoured, or Non Contoured.
* **Preferred Splint Design** (`preferred_splint_design`): Select the design of the splint that you prefer, such as Customized or Non Customized.
* **Occlusal Splint Size** (`occlusal_splint_size`): Choose the size of the splint from the available options (Small, Medium, Large) or provide a custom size if necessary.
* **Occlusal Splint Size Other** (`occlusal_splint_size_other`): If your splint size doesn't fit in the provided options, use this field to specify your preferred size.
* **Other Challenges** (`challenges_other`): Share any other challenges you face when designing occlusal splints.

## Tips
- Make sure to select all options that apply for each field to ensure we get a comprehensive understanding of your preferences and challenges.
- If you have any further questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
