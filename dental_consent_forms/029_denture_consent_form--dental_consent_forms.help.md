# Denture Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information required for denture treatment, including medical history, consent for treatment options, and patient contact information. Please fill out this form to ensure that your dentist has all necessary information for effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. Please make sure to:

*   Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
*   Enter the date and time of your visit.
*   Provide any medical history, allergies, or medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
*   Select the treatment options you are willing to consider.
*   Enter the name of your dentist and the location of your office.
*   Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your visit.
*   **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your visit.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant medical history that may affect your treatment.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies you have that may impact your treatment.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
*   **Treatment Options** (`treatment_options`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the treatment options you are willing to consider. Please choose one or more of the following:
    *   Dental Implant
    *   Dental Crown
    *   Dental Bridge
    *   Dental Veneer
    *   Dental Filling
    *   Dental Extraction
    *   Dental Root Canal
    *   Dental Whitening
*   **Dentist Name** (`dentist_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your dentist.
*   **Office Location** (`office_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of your office.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, optional): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
*   **Date SIGNED** (`date_signed`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you sign the form.
*   **Time SIGNED** (`time_signed`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you sign the form.
*   **Printed Name** (`printed_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your printed name for signature verification.
*   **Printed Title** (`printed_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your printed title for signature verification.
*   **Printed Office Location** (`printed_office_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the printed office location for signature verification.
*   **Printed Signature** (`printed_signature`, `text`, optional): Enter your printed signature for verification.

Tips: Please make sure to review your information carefully before signing the form, and ensure that your dentist has all necessary information for effective treatment. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your dentist.
