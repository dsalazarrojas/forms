# dental_record_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from patients to release their dental records to a third party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. **New Dentist Information** (`new_dentist`, text, required): Enter the name, address, and contact information of the new dentist or third party requesting the records.
3. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history or any health conditions that may be relevant to their treatment.
4. **Allergies** (`allergies`, select multiple, optional): Check any allergies the patient has, such as latex or medications.
5. **Meds** (`medications`, select one, optional): Check if the patient takes any medications.
6. **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions the patient has.
7. **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, optional): List any medical tests or procedures the patient has undergone.
8. **Dental Records** (`dental_records`, text, optional): Specify the specific dental records being released, such as radiographs or impressions.
9. **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm consent.
10. **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date the form is signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: This field is used to record the patient's contact information, date of birth, and any other relevant details. This information will be used to identify the patient and ensure that the correct records are released.
* **New Dentist Information**: This field is used to record the contact information of the new dentist or third party requesting the dental records.
* **Medical History**: This field is used to describe any medical conditions or health concerns the patient has, which may be relevant to their dental treatment.
* **Allergies**: Check if the patient has any allergies, such as latex or medications, and list them in this field.
* **Meds**: Check if the patient takes any medications, including the name and dosage.
* **Medical Conditions**: Describe any medical conditions the patient has, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Medical Tests**: List any medical tests or procedures the patient has undergone, such as blood work or surgeries.
* **Dental Records**: Specify which dental records are being released, such as radiographs or impressions.
* **Patient Signature**: This field requires the patient to sign and date the form to confirm their consent to release their dental records.
* **Date Signed**: This field requires the date the form is signed, which is essential for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
