# Dental Treatment Quote Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate a quote for dental treatment and obtain approval from the patient before proceeding with the treatment. It is intended to ensure that both the patient and the dental clinic are on the same page regarding the treatment plan and cost.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Treatment Quote` section with the details of the treatment plan.
2. Enter the `Patient Name` in the corresponding field.
3. Enter the `Quote Price` in the `Quote Price` field.
4. Provide your `Payment Terms` in the `Payment Terms` field.
5. Sign and date the `Signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Treatment Quote (treatment_quote)**: Enter a brief description of the proposed treatment plan.
* **Patient Name (patient_name)**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Treatment Plan (treatment_plan)**: Describe the details of the treatment plan, including any relevant medical history or special considerations.
* **Quote Price (quote_price)**: Enter the total price of the proposed treatment in numbers only.
* **Payment Terms (payment_terms)**: Indicate when and how payment is expected (e.g. deposit, payment plan, etc.).
* **Signature (signature)**: Sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and understand the treatment quote and payment terms.
