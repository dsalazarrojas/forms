# Dental Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental screening form is a crucial document used to collect important information about patients before any dental treatments or procedures. It helps the dentist understand the patient's medical history, allergies, and current medications to ensure safe and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully and completely**, providing accurate information about your health status.
2. **Select the correct medical condition**, if applicable, from the provided options.
3. **List any allergies or medical conditions**, if applicable, from the provided options.
4. **Describe your medical history**, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or hospitalizations.
5. **List any current medications**, if applicable, from the provided options.
6. **Provide a brief description of your dental history**, including any previous dental procedures, cavities, or other relevant information.
7. **Read and sign the consent form**, indicating your understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of care.
8. **Sign and date the form**, indicating that you have reviewed and completed it accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info**: Please enter your name, date of birth, and other demographic information.
* **medical_condition**: Please select the medical condition(s) that applies to you, if any. This will help us understand your general health status.
* **allergies**: If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please select from the provided options. If you have a different allergy, please specify it in the space provided.
* **medical_history**: Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or hospitalizations. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your dentist or healthcare provider.
* **medications**: If you are taking any medications, please list them in the space provided. If you are unsure, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* **dental_history**: Describe any previous dental procedures, cavities, or other relevant information.
* **consent**: Please read and sign the consent form, indicating your understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of care.
* **patient_signature**: Sign and date the form, indicating that you have reviewed and completed it accurately.
* **provider_signature**: The dentist or healthcare provider will sign and date the form, confirming that they have reviewed and completed it accurately.
* **date**: Please enter the date you completed the form.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for medical consultation or advice. If you have any questions or concerns, please consult your healthcare provider.
