# Teledentistry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant for patients who will be attending tele-dentistry consultations. It is used to collect personal and medical information necessary for the dentist to provide proper care and guidance during the virtual consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Double-check the provided information before submitting the form.
* If you need any assistance, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for further support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (name, text, required/optional): This field is used to capture your name for the consultation record. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email** (email, email, required/optional): This field is used to collect your email address for follow-up and communication purposes. Please enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **date-of-birth** (date, date, required/optional): This field is used to capture your birth date for medical record purposes. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **medical-condition** (medical-condition, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide information about your medical conditions that may affect your dental health. Please enter any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or concerns.
