# Dental Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dental Clearance Form" is used to gather patient information, medical history, and treatment plans to ensure that the dentist has the necessary details to provide safe and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in a clear and legible handwriting or using your preferred digital device.
- Ensure that you complete each field according to your best knowledge and understanding.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your doctor or assistant for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Page 1)**: This field is used to gather basic information about the patient. Please provide your name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History (Page 2)**: This field is used to gather information about your medical history, including any past medical conditions, allergies, or current medical issues. 
* **Medications (Page 3)**: Please indicate if you are currently taking any medications or supplements.
* **Medical Condition (Page 4)**: This field is used to provide any relevant medical information that may affect your treatment.
* **Treatment Plans (Page 5)**: This field is used to outline any treatment plans you have with your doctor.
* **Doctor Info (Page 6)**: This field is used to provide the phone number or email of your doctor.
* **Patient Signature (Page 7)**: Please indicate whether you are the patient or have signed this form on behalf of the patient.
* **Doctor Signature (Page 8)**: Please indicate whether the doctor has reviewed and signed this form.
* **Date Signed (Page 9)**: This field is used to record the date the doctor signed this form.
* **Date Completed (Page 10)**: This field is used to record the date the form was completed.
* **Submitted By (Page 11)**: Please indicate who submitted this form.
