# implant_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The implant_consent_form is a patient consent form used for dental implant procedures. It collects information about the patient, including contact details, medical history, and medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient's name and contact details, including address, phone number, and email.
2. Enter your patient's date of birth.
3. Provide your patient's medical history, including allergies and medications.
4. Enter your patient's medical record number.
5. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name and phone number (if applicable).
6. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, note, required): Enter your contact details, including address, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you consent to the dental implant procedure, otherwise check "No".
* **Date Of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, date, optional): Enter the date of consent.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the doctor performing the procedure.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the clinic.
* **Clinic Address** (`clinic_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the clinic.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the doctor performing the procedure.
* **Doctor License** (`doctor_license`, text, required): Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Clinic License** (`clinic_license`, text, required): Enter the clinic's license number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required): Enter your medical record number.
* **Patient Date Of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Provide a detailed account of your medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies you have.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, required): Provide a detailed account of your medical record.
* **Surgical Record** (`surgical_record`, text, required): Provide information about any previous surgical procedures you have undergone.
* **Other Medical Info** (`other_medical_info`, text, required): Provide any other medical information that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Make sure to sign and date the form.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
* Review the form with your doctor before signing it.
