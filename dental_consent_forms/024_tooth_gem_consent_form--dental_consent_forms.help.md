# Tooth Gem Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tooth Gem Consent Form is used to obtain consent from patients for the Tooth Gem procedure, a treatment that aims to preserve the tooth structure while addressing dental issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully to understand the questions and sections.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately, taking the time to provide detailed responses.
3. Sign the form to indicate your understanding and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tooth Gem Consent Form 1: Introduction**: This page is an introduction to the Tooth Gem procedure. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose of the form and the procedure.
* **What is a Tooth Gem? (2)**: This section provides an explanation of what a Tooth Gem is and how it works. Please read it carefully to understand the concept.
* **Associated Risks (3)**: This section lists potential risks associated with the Tooth Gem procedure. Please take the time to read and understand the risks before proceeding.
* **Aftercare Requirements (4)**: This section outlines the aftercare requirements for the patient after the Tooth Gem procedure. Please read it carefully to understand your responsibilities.
* **Tooth Gem Consent (5)**: This section asks you to select your consent for the Tooth Gem procedure. Please choose "Tooth Gem Consent" if you understand and agree to the procedure.
* **Date of Service (6)**: This field is not required, but please enter the date of service if you have one.
* **Patient Signature (7)**: Please sign here to indicate your understanding and consent to the Tooth Gem procedure.
* **Practitioner Signature (8)**: Please sign here as the practitioner performing the Tooth Gem procedure.
* **Date of Practitioner Signature (9)**: This field is not required, but please enter the date of your signature as the practitioner.
* **Additional Notes (10)**: Please use this section to add any additional notes about the Tooth Gem procedure, if needed.
* **Date (11)**: This field is not required, but please enter the date of the consent form completion, if needed.

## Tips

* Please read each section carefully and accurately complete the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your practitioner.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
