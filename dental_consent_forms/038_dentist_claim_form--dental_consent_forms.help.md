# Dentist Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dentist Claim Form is a document used to provide essential information about a patient's visit to your practice. The form helps to clarify the treatment provided, cost associated with the treatment, and any payment information related to the claim. This form should be completed in full to ensure accurate and efficient processing of the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Dentist Claim Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Patient Information** section (page 1).
2. Provide your patient's details, including any relevant medical history or special instructions.
3. Move on to the **Claim Details** section (page 2), where you can enter the specific treatment provided to the patient.
4. Enter the **Treatment Cost** (page 3), which will be used to calculate the claim amount.
5. Provide **Payment Information** (page 4), including any relevant payment methods or invoices.
6. Enter the **Patient Address** and **Patient Phone** (pages 5 and 6) to ensure we can contact the patient for any follow-up questions.
7. Finally, provide the **Claim Date** and **Claim Time** (pages 7 and 8), which should match the date and time the treatment was provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant medical history.
* **Claim Details (page 2)**: Describe the treatment provided to the patient, including any procedures or services rendered.
* **Treatment Cost (page 3)**: Enter the total cost of the treatment, including any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Payment Information (page 4)**: Provide payment details, such as the payment method used and any applicable invoices.
* **Patient Address (page 5)**: Enter the patient's address to facilitate communication and billing.
* **Patient Phone (page 6)**: Enter the patient's phone number in case we need to contact them.
* **Claim Date (page 7)**: Enter the date the treatment was provided.
* **Claim Time (page 8)**: Enter the time the treatment was provided, matching the claim date.
