# Orthodontic Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to serve as an informed consent form for patients undergoing orthodontic treatment. It is designed to inform patients of their rights and responsibilities and to gather necessary contact information for treatment and emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic contact information in the first page.
2. Answer the treatment details questions in the second page.
3. Sign the signature page.
4. Fill out the contact information of the dentist or orthodontist in the fifth page.
5. If necessary, fill out the contact information of the emergency contact person in the sixth page.
6. Sign the patient signature page.
7. Date and time of the appointment can be filled out in the seventh page.
8. Provide any additional contact information for the dentist or orthodontist in the eighth page.
9. Fill out any additional contact information for the dentist or orthodontist in the ninth page.
10. Sign and date the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`prophets patient info`, text, required: false): This is the first page of the form. Please fill out your basic contact information such as your name, address, and phone number.
* **Treatement Details** (`treatment details`, text, required: false): This page is where you will provide detailed information about your treatment, such as the type of treatment you will receive and any other relevant details.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): This is the patient's signature page. Please sign this page to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Contact Info Prophets** (`contact info`, text, required: false): This page is for the contact information of your dentist or orthodontist.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient signature`, note, required: false): This is the patient's signature page. Please sign this page to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): This page is for the date of your appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): This page is for the time of your appointment.
* **Contact Info Dds** (`contact info dds`, text, required: false): This page is for the contact information of your dentist or orthodontist.
* **Orthodontist** (`orthodontist info`, text, required: false): This page is for the contact information of your orthodontist.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency contact`, text, required: false): This page is for the contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Contact Info** (`contact info`, text, required: false): This page is for any additional contact information.
* **Contact Info Dds1** (`contact info dds1`, text, required: false): This page is for any additional contact information of your dentist or orthodontist.
* **Dds Info 1** (`dds info1`, text, required: false): This page is for any additional information of your dentist or orthodontist.

## Tips
This form is a comprehensive guide to inform and educate you about your rights and responsibilities in an orthodontic treatment. Please take your time to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your dentist or orthodontist.
