# Dental Cleaning Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information and obtain consent from patients before undergoing dental cleaning procedures. It helps ensure that patients are aware of the treatment they will receive and agree to it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or medical records.
2. Provide your address, which will be used for communication and record-keeping purposes.
3. Enter your date of birth, which is essential for our records.
4. Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and scheduling purposes.
5. Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and record-keeping purposes.
6. Provide a brief description of your medical history, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or medical records.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address, which will be used for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth, which is essential for our records.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and scheduling purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your medical history, if any. This will help us understand your medical condition and take necessary precautions during the procedure.

## Tips
Please note that this form is essential for our records and will be kept confidential. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask our staff.
