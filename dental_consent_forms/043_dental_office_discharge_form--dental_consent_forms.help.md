# Dental Office Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's information and treatment details during their visit to the dental office.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Info" field.
2. Describe the treatment details provided by the dentist or hygienist in the "Treatment Details" field.
3. Review and acknowledge any instructions or advice given to the patient in the "Acknowledgments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (patient_info)**: This field is used to collect the patient's basic information, such as name, contact details, and medical history.
* **Treatment Details (treatment_details)**: In this field, describe the dental procedures, medications, or other treatments provided to the patient.
* **Acknowledgments (acknowledgments)**: This field is used to record any instructions or advice given to the patient after their treatment, including any post-care instructions or follow-up appointments.
 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* Keep the "Treatment Details" field concise and clear for easy reference.
* Review the "Acknowledgments" field carefully to ensure all instructions are understood by the patient.
