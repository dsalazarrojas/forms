# High School Class Of 2014 Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering merchandise related to the high school class of 2014. You can use this form to select the type of clothing you want to order, along with your preferred color and size.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter your class year in the "Class Year" field.
2. Select your desired shirt size and color.
3. Choose your preferred hoodie size and color.
4. Decide on the quantity of clothing you'd like to order.
5. Select the type of order (e.g., Class of, School, Team).
6. Add any comments or additional information in the "Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class Year** (`class_year`, text, required/optional): Enter the year of your high school graduation (2014).
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of your shirt (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_one, optional): Select the color of your shirt (Black, White, Grey, Blue, Red).
* **Hoodie Size** (`hoodie_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of your hoodie (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Hoodie Color** (`hoodie_color`, select_one, optional): Select the color of your hoodie (Black, White, Grey, Blue, Red).
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of order you'd like to place (Class of, School, Team).
* **Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of items you'd like to order.
* **Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your order.
