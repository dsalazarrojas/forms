# Size Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Size Information Collection Form is designed to gather essential size details from customers to ensure accurate fitting and order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field explanation below to ensure you complete all necessary fields.
- Type your response accurately and completely for each field.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, contact us using the contact information provided on this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Size Information Collection Form 1** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter a brief description of the item you would like to order, if applicable.
* **Customer Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or government-issued ID.
* **Garment Type** (Select One, Required/Optional): Choose the type of garment (e.g., shirt, pants, dress, etc.) you are interested in.
* **Garment Type Option 1-5** (Text, Required/Optional): Select the specific type of garment you are interested in (e.g., long-sleeve shirt, short-sleeve shirt, etc.). 
* **Size Chart** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide any relevant information about the size chart you'd like to reference for your garment.
* **Pant Length** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your preferred pant length (e.g., regular, long, short, etc.).
* **Pant Width** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your preferred pant width (e.g., slim, regular, loose, etc.).
* **Pant Height** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your preferred pant height (e.g., petite, regular, tall, etc.).
* **Sleeve Length** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your preferred sleeve length (e.g., short, long, regular, etc.).
* **Collar Size** (Select One, Required/Optional): Choose whether you need a collar size (e.g., yes, no).
* **Collar Size Option 1-4** (Text, Required/Optional): Select the specific collar size option you need (e.g., small, medium, large, etc.).
* **Garment Width** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your preferred garment width (e.g., narrow, broad, etc.).
* **Garment Height** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your preferred garment height (e.g., tall, regular, etc.).
* **Fit Comments** (Note, Required/Optional): Share any special fit preferences or comments about your order.
* **Size Comment** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional size-related comments you'd like to include.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with updates or questions about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number for additional communication or questions.
* **Submit** (Text, Required/Optional): Double-check your information and click this button to submit your form.
