# Outfit Planner Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outfit Planner Form is used to collect information about an event or occasion from the client, including the event name, date and time, client contact details, and stylist contact details. This information will help the stylist in planning and preparing the outfit for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the event name in the `event_name` field.
2. Enter the date of the event in the `event_date` field.
3. Enter the time of the event in the `event_time` field.
4. Enter your name in the `client_name` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `client_email` field.
6. Enter your phone number in the `client_phone` field.
7. Enter the name of the stylist assigned to you in the `stlyist_name` field.
8. Enter the email address of the stylist in the `stlyist_email` field.
9. Enter the phone number of the stylist in the `stlyist_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_name** (text): Enter the name of the event or occasion where you will be attending. For example, "Birthday Party" or "Job Interview".
* **event_date** (date): Enter the date of the event. Make sure to use the correct date format, e.g., "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **event_time** (time): Enter the time of the event. Make sure to use the 24-hour format, e.g., "14:30".
* **client_name** (text): Enter your name as the client.
* **client_email** (email): Enter your email address that the stylist can use to contact you.
* **client_phone** (text): Enter your phone number that the stylist can use to contact you.
* **stlyist_name** (text): Enter the name of the stylist assigned to you.
* **stlyist_email** (email): Enter the email address of the stylist that can be used to contact them.
* **stlyist_phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the stylist that can be used to contact them.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid any potential issues.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, ask your stylist for clarification.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
