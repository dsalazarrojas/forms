# Event T Shirt Order Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event T Shirt Order Confirmation form is used to collect participant information and order confirmations for events. This form is designed to be completed by event participants to provide their details and confirm their t-shirt order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your First Name and confirm your t-shirt size.
2. Enter your Email Address to receive order confirmations.
3. Make sure to submit the form only once per participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: participant_first_name (Text)
	+ This field is for your first name. Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be printed on the t-shirt.
* **T Shirt Size**: participant_t_shirt_size (Select One)
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you would like to order a t-shirt, 'No' if you do not want to order a t-shirt.
* **Participant Email**: participant_email (Email)
	+ Enter your email address where you would like to receive order confirmations and updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate participant information.
* Review your form submission carefully before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact event organizers for assistance.
