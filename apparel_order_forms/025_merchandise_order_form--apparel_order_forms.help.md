# Merchandise Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Merchandise Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from customers to process orders for merchandise items. This form should only be used to place new orders for merchandise items and not for any other purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Merchandise Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Order Details section with the required information about the item being ordered.
2. Provide Shipping Details for the order.
3. Enter Billing Details for the order.
4. Select the item(s) Ordered from the available options.
5. Enter the quantity ordered.
6. Fill out the Customer Information section with the customer's name, email, and phone number.
7. Choose the payment type and amount for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the item being ordered.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the shipping address and any special delivery instructions.
* **Billing Details** (`billing_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the billing address and any special payment instructions.
* **Merchandise Ordered** (`merchandise_ordered`, select_one, required/optional): Select the item being ordered from the available options.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of items being ordered.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the payment method (credit card, cash, or PayPal).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the payment amount for the order.

## Tips
* Double-check the order details and customer information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the payment amount matches the order total.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact the order support team for assistance.
