# Short Sleeve T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to process their orders for short sleeve t-shirts. To complete this form, customers will need to provide their contact details, select a product, choose a payment method, and include any order notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing your contact details, including your email and phone number.
2. Select the product you wish to order from the available options.
3. Choose one or more payment methods for your order.
4. Enter any additional order notes you have for your order.
5. Finally, specify the total amount of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details (1)**: Enter your name and email address in the required field.
* **Select Product (2)**: Choose the specific product you wish to order from the available options. Please select only one product.
* **Payment Method (3)**: Choose one or more payment methods to complete your order. Options include "Payment 1" and "Payment 2".
* **Order Notes (4)**: Enter any additional notes or comments you have for your order.
* **Email (5)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Total Amount (7)**: Enter the total amount of your order in numeric format.
* **Customer Name (8)**: Enter your name.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact details and order notes for accuracy.
* Select the correct product and payment method for your order.
* Enter a valid email and phone number.
* Ensure the total amount is a numeric value.
