# festival_t_shirt_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for pre-ordering festival t-shirts. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the event details sections (event_details, event_details_2).
2. Fill out your customer information (name, organization, email, phone).
3. Enter your delivery address and instructions.
4. Choose your payment method and payment amount.
5. Select your t-shirt options (size, color, design, order type).
6. Review your order and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_details**: This field is for entering event details. Please enter any relevant information about the event.
* **event_details_2**: This field is for entering any additional event details.
* **name**: This field is for entering your name.
* **organization**: This field is for entering your organization's name (if applicable).
* **email**: This field is for entering your email address.
* **phone**: This field is for entering your phone number.
* **delivery_address**: This field is for entering your delivery address.
* **delivery_instructions**: This field is for entering any special delivery instructions.
* **payment_method**: This field is for selecting your payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or Cash).
* **payment_amount**: This field is for entering the payment amount.
* **payment_currency**: This field is for selecting the payment currency (USD, EUR, or GBP).
* **festival_t_shirt_size**: This field is for selecting the size of the t-shirt (S, M, L, or XL).
* **festival_t_shirt_color**: This field is for selecting the color of the t-shirt (red, blue, or green).
* **t_shirt_design**: This field is for selecting the design of the t-shirt (plain, design, or logo).
* **t_shirt_design_2**: This field is for selecting additional t-shirt design options.
* **order_t_shirt**: This field is for confirming whether or not you want to order a t-shirt.
* **order_t_shirt_size**: This field is for selecting the size of the t-shirt to order.
* **order_t_shirt_color**: This field is for selecting the color of the t-shirt to order.
* **order_t_shirt_type**: This field is for selecting the type of t-shirt to order (adult or youth).
* **order_t_shirt_design**: This field is for selecting the design of the t-shirt to order.

## Tips
* Please double-check your event details and customer information to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to select the correct payment method and payment amount.
* Confirm your t-shirt options carefully to ensure you are getting the right size, color, and design.
* If you have any special instructions for delivery, please enter them in the delivery_instructions field.
* Review your order carefully before submitting the form.
