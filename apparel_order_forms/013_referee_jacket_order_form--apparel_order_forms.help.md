# Referee Jacket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Jacket Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for referee jackets. This guide will walk you through each field of the form to help you complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your order status: Please choose whether you want to order a jacket or not.
2. Choose your payment method: Select the payment method you would like to use (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, or American Express).
3. Enter your customer information: Provide your name, email, and phone number.
4. Select the order type: Choose whether you want a men's, women's, or kids' jacket.
5. Enter the quantity of jackets you want to order.
6. Enter the date you would like the order to be processed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your order. If you select "Yes" for this field, it will indicate that you are making an order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose the payment method you would like to use for this order. This will help the store or provider process your payment accurately.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you want a men's, women's, or kids' jacket.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of jackets you would like to order.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like the order to be processed.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your order details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* We will process your order as soon as possible after receiving your submission.
