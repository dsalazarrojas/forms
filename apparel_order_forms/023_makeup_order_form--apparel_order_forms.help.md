# Makeup Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a makeup order. It is intended to be used by customers or clients who want to place an order for a makeup product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `User Input` field with your desired product or service.
2. Enter your `Name` in the `Name` field.
3. Provide your `Email` address in the `Email` field.
4. Select the `Date` for your desired appointment or order.
5. If you have a `Photo`, please upload it in the `Photo` field.
6. Add any additional `Notes` in the `Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the product or service you want to order.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date for your desired appointment or order.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): Upload a photo to be used for your order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or information for your order.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly to ensure accurate processing of your order. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
