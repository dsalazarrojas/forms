# Club Branded Water Bottle Preorder Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Club Branded Water Bottle Preorder Form is used to place an order for a water bottle with your club's branding. This form gathers information about you, your order preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your information: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your membership status.
3. Select your preferred water bottle color.
4. Choose the size of the water bottle you wish to order.
5. Enter the number of water bottles you want to order.
6. Review your total order cost.
7. Choose how you'd like to receive your order.
8. Enter any special instructions or requests.
9. Select your payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (member_name, text, required): Enter your full name so we can contact you about your order.
* **Email Address** (member_email, email, required): Enter your email address to receive confirmation of your order and any updates about it.
* **Phone Number** (member_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for any reason.
* **Membership Status** (membership_status, select_one, required): Choose your current membership status to help us process your order.
* **Water Bottle Color** (bottle_color, select_one, required): Select the color you prefer for your water bottle.
* **Bottle Size** (bottle_size, select_one, required): Choose the size of water bottle you'd like to order.
* **Number of Bottles** (quantity, number, required): Enter the number of bottles you'd like to order.
* **Price Per Bottle** (unit_price, number, required): This field is auto-calculated and not editable; review the total cost for accuracy.
* **Total Order Cost** (total_order_cost, number, required): This field is auto-calculated and not editable; review for accuracy.
* **Want Personalization** (personalization, select_one, optional): If you'd like to add your name to your water bottle, choose from the options.
* **Personalization Text** (personal_text, text, optional): If you chose personalization, enter your name or initials here.
* **Pickup or Delivery Method** (pickup_method, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to receive your order.
* **Preferred Pickup Location** (pickup_location, text, optional): If you chose club meeting pickup, enter the location of the meeting.
* **Delivery Address** (delivery_address, text, optional): If you chose mail or home delivery, enter your address here.
* **Special Instructions** (special_instructions, text, optional): Enter any special requests or instructions for your order.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to pay for your order.
