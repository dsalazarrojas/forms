# Hero T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hero T Shirt Order Form is a form designed to collect information for ordering Hero T-shirts. It's intended for customers to provide their order details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the order options (yes/no) on the first page.
2. Enter your shipping and billing addresses on the second page.
3. Choose your T-Shirt designs, sizes, and colors on the third page.
4. Enter your order details, customer contact information, and payment terms on the fourth page.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Order Form** (`order_form`, select multiple, required): 
  This field is a yes/no option to confirm if you have an order.

- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): 
  Enter your shipping address where the T-shirt will be delivered.

- **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required): 
  Enter your billing address where you will be charged for the order.

- **T-Shirt Designs** (`t_shirt_designs`, select one, required): 
  Select the design of the T-shirt you want to order. Options include Apple, Cherry, and Orange.

- **T-Shirt Sizes** (`t_shirt_sizes`, select multiple, required): 
  Choose the size of the T-shirt you want to order. Options include Small, Medium, and Large.

- **T-Shirt Colors** (`t_shirt_colors`, select one, required): 
  Select the color of the T-shirt you want to order. Options include Red, Blue, and Yellow.

- **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, required): 
  Enter any additional information about your order.

- **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone_number`, text, required): 
  Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): 
  Enter your email address for communication purposes.

- **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): 
  Enter the total amount of the order.

- **T-Shirt Quantity** (`t_shirt_quantity`, number, required): 
  Enter the quantity of T-shirts you want to order.

- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select one, required): 
  Select the payment method for your order. Options include Cash On Delivery (COD), Pay Online, and Pay Offline.

- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, required): 
  Select how you want to pay for the order. Options include Bank Transfer, Card Swipe, and Cash.

- **Order Status** (`order_status`, select one, required): 
  Select the status of your order. Options include New Order, In Progress, and Complete.

- **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, required): 
  Enter any comments about your order.

- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required): 
  Enter any additional comments about your order.

- **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required): 
  Sign your name to confirm your order.

- **Order Number** (`order_number`, number, required): 
  Enter a unique order number for your order.

- **Created By** (`created_by`, select one, required): 
  Select if you created this order.

- **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select one, required): 
  Select if you updated this order.

- **Created At** (`created_at`, date, required): 
  Enter the date this order was created.

- **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, required): 
  Enter the date this order was updated.

- **Created By ID** (`created_by_id`, select one, required): 
  Select if you created this order ID.

- **Updated By ID** (`updated_by_id`, select one, required): 
  Select if you updated this order ID.

- **T Shirts** (`t_shirts`, text, required): 
  Enter any T-shirt information.

Tips:

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your order details before submitting.
* Review and verify your order before finalizing.
