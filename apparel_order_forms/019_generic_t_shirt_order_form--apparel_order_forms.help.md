# Generic T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generic T Shirt Order Form is a form used to collect customer information for a T-shirt order, allowing you to provide custom-tailored shirts to customers based on their specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the relevant fields. 
2.  Choose your preferred T Shirt Size from the dropdown list.
3.  Select the desired colors for your order.
4.  Enter the quantity of shirts you'd like to order.
5.  Enter the price of your order.
6.  Add any additional notes or instructions for your order.
7.  Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name to be used for the order.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name to be used for the order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to be used for communication about your order.
* **T Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of the T-shirt you'd like to order from the available options.
*   • Small
*   • Medium
*   • Large
*   • Extra Large
*   • Extra Extra Large
* **Color** (`color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the colors for your T-shirt order from the available options.
*   • White
*   • Black
*   • Navy Blue
*   • Red
*   • Green
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of T-shirts you'd like to order.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price you'd like to pay for your order.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, optional): Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or instructions for your order.
* **First Name Error** (`first_name_error`, text, optional): This field will display an error message if there's an issue with your first name.
* **Last Name Error** (`last_name_error`, text, optional): This field will display an error message if there's an issue with your last name.
* **Email Error** (`email_error`, text, optional): This field will display an error message if there's an issue with your email.
* **T Shirt Size Error** (`t_shirt_size_error`, text, optional): This field will display an error message if there's an issue with your T-shirt size.
* **Color Error** (`color_error`, text, optional): This field will display an error message if there's an issue with your color selection.
* **Quantity Error** (`quantity_error`, text, optional): This field will display an error message if there's an issue with your quantity.
* **Price Error** (`price_error`, text, optional): This field will display an error message if there's an issue with your price.
* **Submit Error** (`submit_error`, text, optional): This field will display an error message if there's an issue with your submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for your order to avoid any errors.
* If you encounter an error message for any field, review the field carefully and correct any mistakes before submitting your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
