# Employee Merchandise Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Merchandise Order Form is used to order merchandise for work-related needs. Please follow the guide below to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Employee Name.
2. Select your department from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the name of the item you wish to order.
4. Specify the quantity of the item you need.
5. Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
6. Enter the date you would like to receive the merchandise by.
7. Enter your Employee ID.
8. Write a brief description of the item being ordered.
9. Enter the total cost of the order.
10. Choose the order status (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
11. You may add any additional notes about the order if necessary.
11. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
12. Enter your phone number for contact.
13. Enter your company name.
14. Select your department.
15. Enter the name of your manager.
16. Enter your manager's email address.
17. Enter your manager's phone number.
18. Enter the Employee Merchandise ID.
19. Enter the Employee Merchandise Order ID.
20. Enter the Employee Merchandise Order Number.
21. Enter the date of the order.
22. Enter the total cost of the order.
23. Add any additional notes about the order (if necessary).
24. Choose the status of the order (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
