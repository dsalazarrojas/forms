# School Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a school uniform order. It's essential to fill it out correctly to ensure the order is processed accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the products you wish to order by checking the "Yes" box next to each product.
2. Fill in the customer's information, including their name, email, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Choose your preferred payment method and fill in the corresponding information.
4. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" next to "Confirm Order".
5. Fill in any special instructions for the order, if necessary.
6. Finally, confirm your school's information, including the school name, address, city, state, and zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Form Input** (`order_form_input`, number): This is where you input your order number, if applicable.
- **Products** (`product_list`, select_multiple): Select the products you wish to order by checking the "Yes" box next to each product.
- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text): Fill in the customer's name, phone number, and email address.
- **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text): Enter the customer's shipping address, city, state, and zip code.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one): Choose how you wish to pay, either "Yes" for online payment or "No" for offline payment.
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text): Fill in any additional payment information, if applicable.
- **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, select_one): Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" next to "Confirm Order".
- **School Products** (`school_products`, select_multiple): Select the products that are part of the school's uniform, if applicable.
- **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one): Choose the status of your order, either "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the order.
- **School Information** (`school_info`, text): Fill in the school's name, address, city, state, and zip code.
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, number): Fill in the total cost of the order.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email): Enter the customer's email address.
- **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text): Enter the customer's address.
- **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text): Enter the customer's city.
- **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text): Enter the customer's state.
- **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text): Enter the customer's zip code.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text): Enter the school's name.
- **School Address** (`school_address`, text): Enter the school's address.
- **School City** (`school_city`, text): Enter the school's city.
- **School State** (`school_state`, text): Enter the school's state.
- **School Zip** (`school_zip`, text): Enter the school's zip code.
- **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text): Enter the school's phone number.

Tips: Ensure all fields are filled in correctly and accurately to avoid any issues with order fulfillment. If you have any difficulties or questions, contact the school's administration.
