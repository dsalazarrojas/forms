# shirt_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place an order for custom shirts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred shirt size in the "Shirt Size" field.
6. Select your desired shirt color in the "Shirt Color" field. You may select multiple colors.
7. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
8. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
9. Select your preferred shirt design in the "Shirt Design" field.
10. Enter the quantity of the custom shirt you wish to order in the "Shirt Quantity" field.
11. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
12. Enter your shipping city in the "Shipping City" field.
13. Enter your shipping state in the "Shipping State" field.
14. Enter your shipping zip code in the "Shipping Zip" field.
15. Select the status of your payment in the "Payment Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, required): Select your preferred shirt size.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_multiple, required): Select your desired shirt color. You may select multiple colors.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Shirt Design** (`shirt_design`, select_one, required): Select your preferred shirt design.
* **Shirt Quantity** (`shirt_quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the custom shirt you wish to order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Enter your shipping city.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, required): Enter your shipping state.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, required): Enter your shipping zip code.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your payment.
