# High Visibility Gear Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to process orders for high-visibility gear, which are essential for various applications such as safety, emergency response, and other critical operations. The form collects essential information from the customer, including contact details, order specifications, and logistical information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the customer's name and contact person's name in the "customer_name" and "contact_person" fields, respectively.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide the customer's phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Enter the date of the order in the "order_date" field.
5. Enter the time of the order in the "order_time" field.
6. Add any additional comments or instructions about the order in the "description" field.
7. Enter any customer notes or special requests in the "customer_note" field.
8. Select the quantity of high-visibility gear ordered in the "quantity_ordered" field.
9. Choose the color of the high-visibility gear from the options provided in the "color" field.
10. Select the size of the high-visibility gear from the options provided in the "size" field.
11. Enter any additional details about the order in the "order_details" field.
12. Provide any special instructions for the order in the "special_instruction" field.
13. Enter the shipping address in the "shipping_address" field.
14. Enter the city, state, and zip code of the shipping address.
15. Enter the country of the shipping address.
16. Select the status of the order from the options provided in the "order_status" field.
17. Select the type of order from the options provided in the "order_type" field.
18. Choose the shipping method from the options provided in the "shipping_method" field.
19. Enter any additional comments about the order in the "order_comments" field.
20. Enter the total cost of the order in the "order_total" field.
21. Sign and date the order with the "customer_signature" field.
22. Enter the date and time of the signature in the "signature_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name to whom the high-visibility gear is being ordered for.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person who is responsible for the order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Description** (`description`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or instructions about the order.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any customer notes or special requests.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, required/optional): Select the quantity of high-visibility gear ordered.
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, required): Choose the color of the high-visibility gear from the options provided.
* **Size** (`size`, select_multiple, required): Select the size of the high-visibility gear from the options provided.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the order.
* **Special Instruction** (`special_instruction`, text, required/optional): Provide any special instructions for the order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the shipping address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of the shipping address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of the shipping address.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the order from the options provided.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of order from the options provided.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Choose the shipping method from the options provided.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about the order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the order with the customer's signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of the signature.
