# Custom Hang Tag Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Hang Tag Order Form is a digital form that enables customers to submit orders for custom hang tags. To complete the form successfully, please follow the instructions below.
## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, simply click on the "Start Order" button to begin filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artwork Upload (artwork_input)**: Upload your chosen artwork or design for the hang tag. This will be printed on the tag. You can upload a file from your computer or use a URL to link to an image online.
* **Shipping Info (shipping_info)**: Enter your shipping information, including your address, so we can deliver your order to you quickly and accurately. Please make sure to include your email address as this will be used as a contact point.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)**: Please enter your payment information to complete the order. You can choose from available payment options or add a new payment method.
* **Customer Info (customer_info)**: This is a text field for customers to input any additional comments or information they wish to provide regarding their order or account.
* **Quantity (quantity_input)**: Enter the number of hang tags you require for your order. This will help us know how many tags to produce.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach a clear and high-quality image for the artwork to ensure proper printing.
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact our support team for help.
