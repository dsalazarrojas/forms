# Uniform Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information from customers for the uniform department. It helps to gather details such as customer name, contact information, and order specifics like size, color, and quantity. This form is typically used in the context of ordering uniforms for a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
*   Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
*   Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
*   Enter the city where you are located in the "City" field.
*   Enter the state where you are located in the "State" field.
*   Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
*   Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
*   Select your preferred size for the uniform in the "Size" field.
*   Select your preferred color for the uniform in the "Color" field.
*   Select the quantity of uniforms you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
*   Enter the date you would like to receive your order in the "Date" field.
*   Enter the time you would like to receive your order in the "Time" field.
*   Select additional sizes you would like to order in the "Select Multiple Size" field.
*   Select additional colors you would like to order in the "Select Multiple Color" field.
*   Enter any additional notes about your order in the "Note" field.
*   Enter the tool that you would like to be assigned for your order in the "Assigned Tool" field.
*   Enter the category of your order in the "Category" field.
*   Enter your customer ID in the "Customer ID" field.
*   Enter the form ID for your order in the "Form ID" field.
*   Enter your customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
*   Enter your customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
*   Enter your customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
*   Enter the date you would like your order to be submitted in the "Order Date" field.
*   Enter the date and time your order was submitted in the "Submitted At" field.
