# Sports Apparel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for their sports apparel order. It is essential to provide accurate details to ensure that your order is processed correctly and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the correct options for "Order Details" and "Order Items" fields.
- Enter the "Order Date" and "Order Time" if required.
- Provide any customer notes or comments for our reference.
- Select the shipping address type from the options provided.
- Enter your contact phone number and email address to ensure we can contact you for any further information or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the order, including any specific requirements or special requests. This field is essential for our team to understand your needs accurately.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, select_multiple, required): Select the sports equipment or apparel you wish to order, such as Baseball Jersey, Basketball Shorts, Football Cleats, or Soccer Ball. Make sure to choose all the items you want to include in your order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): If you know the exact date of your event or competition, please enter it here. This helps our team schedule the order delivery and processing accordingly.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the exact time of your event or competition. This information is helpful for our team to plan the order delivery and processing.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): If you have any specific requests, questions, or comments, please include them here for our reference.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, select_one, required): Select the type of shipping address from the options provided: Home or Office. This helps us determine the correct shipping method and address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you for any further information or updates.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your order status.

## Tips
- Double-check your selections and input before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- Use the available options in the drop-down menus to select the correct information.
- Make sure to enter accurate and clear information in the form fields for our team to process your order efficiently.
