# Team Branded Face Mask Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Branded Face Mask Order Form is a tool for authorized customers or employees to place orders for team-branded face masks. This form captures all the necessary information to process the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Order Information" section with your order details.
2. Provide your "Customer Info" in the designated field.
3. Select the required "Size" you prefer.
4. If you need more than one "Quantity", separate each with a comma.
5. Choose your preferred "Payment Method".
6. Enter your "Email" address.
7. Fill in your "Phone" number.
8. Enter your "Address".
9. Add any "Notes" regarding your order.
10. Fill in the "Payment Details" field with any additional payment instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Information (Page 1)
* **Order Information** (`order_info`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you can enter any additional information about your order.

### Customer Info (Page 2)
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This is where you can enter any details about yourself as a customer.

### Size (Page 3)
* **Size** (`size`, select_one, optional): Here, select the size you prefer for your order.

### Quantity (Page 4)
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, select_multiple, optional): If you need multiple quantities, separate each with a comma.

### Payment Method (Page 5)
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method for your order.

### Email (Page 6)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Phone (Page 7)
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Address (Page 8)
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.

### Notes (Page 9)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order here.

### Payment Details (Page 10)
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional payment instructions here.

### Total (Page 11)
* **Total** (`total`, number, optional): This field is not currently being used. You can disregard it.
