# Custom Logo Embroidery Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for custom logo embroidery orders, allowing you to provide your logo design and specifications for a unique embroidered product. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the order details on page 1.
2.  Upload your logo details on page 2.
3.  Provide payment details on page 3.
4.  Specify your product preferences on page 4.
5.  Review your order on page 5.
6.  Click "Submit" to finalize your order on page 6.
7.  Review your order summary on page 7.
8.  Optionally, provide review details on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (page 1)** (`order_details`, `text`, required: false): This is the starting page where you can enter the necessary details for your custom logo order. Please provide as much information as possible to help us understand your requirements.
* **Logo Details (page 2)** (`logo_details`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can upload or describe the logo you want to be embroidered. Please be specific about the design, color scheme, and any additional features you'd like to include.
* **Payment Details (page 3)** (`payment_details`, `text`, required: false): On this page, you can provide your payment information to complete the order. Please ensure that the payment method and details are accurate and up-to-date.
* **Product Preferences (page 4)** (`product_preferences`, `text`, required: false): This is the page where you can specify any specific preferences for your custom logo product, such as fabric type, size, or material. Please be as detailed as possible to help us create the perfect item for you.
* **Order Review (page 5)** (`order_review`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can review the details of your order and ensure everything is accurate. Please take a moment to double-check the information before proceeding.
* **Submit (page 6)** (`submit_button`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to finalize your order. Please review your order summary before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* **Order Summary (page 7)** (`order_summary`, `text`, required: false): On this page, you'll find a summary of your custom logo order. Please review the details to confirm that they're correct.
* **Review Details (page 8)** (`review_details`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to provide feedback or review your order further, this is the page for that. Please let us know if you're satisfied with the final product or if you'd like to suggest improvements.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information for your custom logo order.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask us for clarification.
