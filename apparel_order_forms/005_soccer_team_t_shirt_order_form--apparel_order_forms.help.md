# Soccer Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Team T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect information for ordering custom soccer team T-shirts. This form is intended for team managers, coaches, or administrators to place orders for their teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team name in the "Team name" field.
2. Select the team size from the options provided.
3. Choose the color of the shirt from the options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
4. Select the shirt size from the options: Large, X-Large, Small, or Medium.
5. Choose the color for the shirt from the options: Black, White, Gray, or Colorful.
6. Enter the quantity of shirts needed.
7. Select the shipping method from the options: Ground, Air, or Express.
8. Choose the payment method from the options: Bank transfer, PayPal, or Credit card.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.
10. Enter the order date and time.
11. Enter the total cost of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the team.
* **Team size** (number, required/optional): Select the number of players in the team.
* **Team color** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the color of the team's uniform.
* **Shirt size** (select_one, required/optional): Select the size of the shirt.
* **Color choice** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the color of the shirt.
* **Quantity** (number, required/optional): Enter the number of shirts needed.
* **Shipping** (select_one, required/optional): Select the shipping method for the order.
* **Payment method** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the method of payment.
* **Notes** (note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Order Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Order Total** (number, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the order.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for a smooth order process.
- Use the correct format for date and time entry (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD for date, HH:MM for time).
- Double-check the order total to ensure it's correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the order team for assistance.
