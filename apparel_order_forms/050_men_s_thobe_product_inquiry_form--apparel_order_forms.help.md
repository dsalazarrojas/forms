# Men's Thobe Product Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to inquire about or order a product. To use this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product you are inquiring about by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the "Select A Product" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. Select your date of birth.
6. Choose whether you identify as male or female.
7. Select the type of product you are interested in.
8. Choose the fabric type of the product.
9. Select the size of the product.
10. Choose the color of the product.
11. If you have any additional details or information about your inquiry, please enter them in the provided text box.
12. If you have any further comments, please enter them in the provided text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select A Product** (`product_selection`, select_one, required: false): Select the product you are inquiring about or choose "No" to indicate you have no preference.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Select your date of birth.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you identify as male or female.
* **Type** (`product_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of product you are interested in.
* **Fabric** (`fabric`, select_one, required: false): Choose the fabric type of the product.
* **Size** (`size`, select_one, required: false): Select the size of the product.
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, required: false): Choose the color of the product.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, required: false): If you have any additional details or information, please enter them here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): If you have any further comments, please enter them here.
