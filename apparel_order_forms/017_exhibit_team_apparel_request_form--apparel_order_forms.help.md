# Exhibit Team Apparel Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting team apparel for the exhibit team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Select the style of apparel you would like to request.
2. Choose the sizes you require (separate them with commas if multiple sizes are needed).
3. Select the color(s) of apparel you would like to request.
4. Enter the quantity of apparel you would like to request.
5. Fill out the "Requested By" field with your name and title.
6. Enter the date the apparel is needed by.
7. Add any additional information that may be relevant to the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Style**: 
  This field allows you to choose the style of apparel you would like to request. Please select one of the available options: Black, White, or Grey.

* **Select Size**: 
  This field allows you to choose the size(s) of apparel you would like to request. You can select one or multiple sizes (separate them with commas). Please review the team's style guide to ensure that the selected sizes are compliant.

* **Select Quantity**: 
  This field allows you to enter the quantity of apparel you would like to request. Please enter a numerical value.

* **Select Color**: 
  This field allows you to choose the color(s) of apparel you would like to request. Please select one or multiple colors. 

* **Requested By**: 
  This field allows you to fill out your name and title. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.

* **Requested Date**: 
  This field allows you to enter the date the apparel is needed by. Please ensure that the date is in the correct format.

* **Additional Information**: 
  This field allows you to add any additional information that may be relevant to the request. Please be as detailed as possible.

* **Email**: 
  This field is for entering your email address. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.

* **Phone**: 
  This field is for entering your phone number. This is optional, but please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.

* All the "Note" fields are not applicable to this form.
