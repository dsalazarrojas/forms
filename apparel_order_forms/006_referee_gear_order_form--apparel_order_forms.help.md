# Referee Gear Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Referee Gear Order Form is designed to collect information from referees for their apparel orders. This form will help the retailer or organization process the orders efficiently and ensure accurate shipping and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `referee_apparel_order` field with a description of the order.
2. Select the shipping details from the `shipping_details` field.
3. Add any additional order terms in the `order_terms` field.
4. Enter the shipping address in the `shipping_address` field.
5. Provide your phone number in the `phone_number` field.
6. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
7. Select the date of the order from the `date_of_order` field.
8. Choose the time of the order from the `time_of_order` field.
9. Enter your name in the `referee_name` field.
10. Enter your referee number in the `referee_number` field.
11. Select your referee association from the `referee_association` field.
12. Agree to the `order_terms_agreement` field.
13. Enter the referee league in the `referee_league` field.
14. Enter the retailer in the `retailer` field.
15. Add any order notes in the `order_notes` field.
16. Indicate whether the order has been submitted by selecting `order_status` to "Active" or "Completed".
17. Enter the order ID in the `order_id` field.
18. Select the shipping method from the `shipping_method` field.
19. Choose the payment method from the `payment_method` field.
20. Enter the date the order was submitted from the `order_submitted_date` field.
21. Enter the date the order was updated from the `order_updated_date` field.
22. Enter the person who updated the order from the `order_updated_by` field.
