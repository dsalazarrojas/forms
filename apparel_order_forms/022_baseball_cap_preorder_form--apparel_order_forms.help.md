# Baseball Cap Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baseball Cap Preorder Form is a crucial tool for customers to order custom baseball caps with precise specifications. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to fill it out correctly, and a field-by-field explanation to ensure a seamless and successful order process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each field carefully before filling it out.
2.  Ensure all fields with the `required` tag are filled out as they are essential for order processing.
3.  Double-check your input data for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please input your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address to receive order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Provide your best contact number for communication.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete street address for delivery.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city for delivery.
* **State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province for delivery.
* **ZIP Code** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code for delivery.
* **Cap Style** (`cap_style`, `select_one`, required): Select the style of cap you want (e.g., Classic Fitted, Structured Snapback, etc.).
* **Cap Color** (`cap_color`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary color of your cap from the given options.
* **Brim Color** (`brim_color`, `select_one`, required): Select the color of the brim from the options provided.
* **Logo Placement** (`logo_placement`, `select_one`, required): Decide where the logo should be placed on your cap.
* **Logo Type** (`logo_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of logo (e.g., Embroidered, Printed, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Input the number of caps you want to order.
* **Size Breakdown** (`size_breakdown`, `text`, optional): Provide a breakdown of the sizes you want to order (e.g., L, XL, etc.).
* **Customization Text** (`customization_text`, `text`, optional): Input any text you want to add to your cap.
* **Font Style** (`font_style`, `select_one`, optional): Select the preferred font style for your customization text.
* **Thread Color** (`thread_color`, `text`, optional): Choose the color for embroidery if applicable.
* **Upload Logo File** (`logo_file_upload`, `text`, optional): If you have a logo file, upload it for use in your order.
* **Expected Delivery Date** (`estimated_delivery`, `date`, required): Input the date you need the caps by.
* **Rush Order** (`rush_order`, `select_one`, required): Decide if you need expedited production.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Discount Code** (`discount_code`, `text`, optional): Input any promo code if applicable.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or information for your order.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): Help us understand how you found out about us.
