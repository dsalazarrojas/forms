# gym_branded_towel_restock_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you order gym branded towels. It collects information about your gym's towel restocking needs, delivery preferences, and any special instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the first field, "gym_details", with a brief description of your gym.
2. If your gym has any specific towel preferences, select the desired towels from the "product_selections" field.
3. Choose a delivery date for your order in the "delivery_date" field.
4. Specify a delivery time if desired in the "delivery_time" field.
5. Add any special notes or instructions in the "notes" field.
6. If you have additional information about your gym, provide it in the "gym_details_2", "gym_details_3", and "gym_details_4" fields.
7. Finally, add any special delivery instructions or comments in the "delivery_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **gym_details** (`gym_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your gym.
* **product_selections** (`product_selections`, select multiple, optional): Select the towels you need to restock. This field offers the following options:
	+ Towel 1
	+ Towel 2
	+ Towel 3
* **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you want your order delivered.
* **delivery_time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): If you want to specify a delivery time, enter it here.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any special notes or instructions about your order.
* **gym_details_2** (`gym_details_2`, text, optional): If you have any additional information about your gym, provide it here.
* **gym_details_3** (`gym_details_3`, text, optional): Add any further details about your gym.
* **gym_details_4** (`gym_details_4`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your gym.
* **delivery_note** (`delivery_note`, text, optional): Add any special delivery instructions or comments here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with your order, contact us at form@gic.mx.
