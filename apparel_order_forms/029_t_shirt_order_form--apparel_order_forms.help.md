> # T-Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The T-Shirt Order Form is used to collect information from customers when they place an order for a t-shirt. It helps us understand what color, size, and design the customer wants for their t-shirt, as well as other relevant details such as shipping method and price.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal contact information, including Customer Name, Shipping Address, and Email.
2. Choose the T-Shirt Color and Size that you prefer.
3. Indicate if you want to purchase more than one t-shirt and enter the quantity in the Quantity field.
4. Select your preferred t-shirt design from the options provided.
5. Specify the price you are willing to pay for the t-shirt.
6. Choose your preferred shipping method.
7. Click the "Submit" button to confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address to ensure that we can send your t-shirt to the correct location.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
* **T-Shirt Color** (`T-Shirt Color`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the color of the t-shirt you prefer. You can select from one of the options provided.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`T-Shirt Size`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the size of the t-shirt you prefer. You can select multiple sizes if you want to purchase more than one t-shirt.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required/optional): If you want to purchase more than one t-shirt, enter the quantity here.
* **T-Shirt Design** (`T-Shirt Design`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the design of the t-shirt you prefer. You can select from one of the options provided.
* **T-Shirt Price** (`T-Shirt Price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the t-shirt. Please note that this is a suggested price range, and actual prices may vary.
* **Shipping Method** (`Shipping Method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the shipping method you prefer. You can select from one of the options provided.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit your order and complete the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that we can process your order correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns about your order, feel free to contact us at our contact email or phone number.
