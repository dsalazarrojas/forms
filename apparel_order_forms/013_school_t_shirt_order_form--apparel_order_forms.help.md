# School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect information for ordering school t-shirts. It will be used to gather details about the customer and their order preferences, such as size, color, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `customer_info` field with your name, school, and other relevant details.
2. Select your preferred t-shirt size from the `t_shirt_size` dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred t-shirt color from the `t_shirt_color` dropdown menu.
4. Enter your email address in the `customer_email` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `customer_phone` field.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the `payment_method` dropdown menu.
7. Choose the status of your payment by selecting either `paid` or `unpaid` from the `payment_status` dropdown menu.
8. Add any additional order notes in the `order_notes` field if necessary.
9. Finally, click the submit button to send in your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_details**: Enter a brief description of your order, if necessary.
* **customer_info**: Enter your name, school, and other relevant details.
* **shipping_details**: Enter your shipping address or any other shipping-related information.
* **t_shirt_size**: Select your preferred t-shirt size from the dropdown menu:
	+ `xsmall`
	+ `small`
	+ `medium`
	+ `large`
	+ `xlarge`
* **payment_method**: Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu:
	+ `online payment`
	+ `offline payment`
* **order_notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **payment_status**: Select the status of your payment by choosing either `paid` or `unpaid`.
* **t_shirt_color**: Select your preferred t-shirt color from the dropdown menu:
	+ `red`
	+ `blue`
	+ `yellow`
	+ `green`
	+ `black`
	+ `white`
* **customer_email**: Enter your email address.
* **customer_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **payment_status**: Select the status of your payment by choosing either `paid` or `unpaid`.
