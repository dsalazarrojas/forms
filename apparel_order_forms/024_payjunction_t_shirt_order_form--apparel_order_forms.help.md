# PayJunction T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for a custom T shirt order. You will be asked for personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone) and order details (T Shirt Color, T Shirt Size, and T Shirt Quantity). You will also be asked to provide payment information (Card Type, Card Number, and CVV) and shipping details (Shipping Address, Shipping City, Shipping State, and Shipping Zip).

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone).
2. Select the color and size of the T shirt you would like to order.
3. Specify the quantity of T shirts you would like to order.
4. Choose the payment method (Card Type) and enter the required card details.
5. Enter your shipping address, city, and state.
6. Enter your shipping zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **T Shirt Color**: Select the color of the T shirt you would like to order.
* **T Shirt Size**: Select the size of the T shirt you would like to order.
* **T Shirt Quantity**: Enter the number of T shirts you would like to order.
* **Card Type**: Select the type of credit card you would like to use for payment.
*   *Card Number*: Enter the 16-digit number on the front of your credit card.
*   *CVV*: Enter the 3-digit security code on the back of your credit card.
*   *Shipping Address*: Enter the address where you would like the T shirt to be shipped.
*   *Shipping City*: Enter the city where you would like the T shirt to be shipped.
*   *Shipping State*: Enter the state where you would like the T shirt to be shipped.
*   *Shipping Zip*: Enter the zip code where you would like the T shirt to be shipped.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at your convenience.
