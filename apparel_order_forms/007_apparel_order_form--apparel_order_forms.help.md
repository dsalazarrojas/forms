# Apparel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Apparel Order Form is designed to collect necessary information for processing apparel orders online. By filling out this form, you will be able to:

* Provide accurate shipping and billing information.
* Select the product you wish to purchase.
* Choose your preferred payment method.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Information:** Please provide your basic details in this section. This includes:
	* Full Name: Enter your full name as you wish to be addressed.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address where we can contact you for updates.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
	* Shipping Address: Enter your shipping address where the order will be delivered.
	* City: Enter the city where you are located.
	* State/Province: Enter the state or province where you are located.
	* ZIP/Postal Code: Enter your zip code for accurate delivery.

2. **Product Selection:** Choose the product type you wish to purchase from the options provided.
* Product Category: Select a category for your preferred product, such as T-Shirts, Hoodies, etc.
* Color: Choose the color of the product you like.
* Size: Choose the size that fits you best.
* Quantity: Specify how many of the selected product you need.

3. **Payment Information:**
	* Need another product?: If you wish to order more than one product, select "True" to proceed.
	* Preferred Payment Method: Choose how you wish to pay for your order, such as credit card, debit card, etc.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name:** Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address:** Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for updates.
* **Phone Number:** Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Shipping Address:** Enter the address where you want your order delivered.
* **City:** Enter the city where you are located.
* **State/Province:** Enter the state or province where you are located.
* **ZIP/Postal Code:** Enter your zip code for accurate delivery.
* **Product Category:** Choose the category of product you wish to purchase.
* **Color:** Select the color of the product you prefer.
* **Size:** Choose the size that fits you best.
* **Quantity:** Specify how many of the selected product you need.
* **Need another product?:** If you wish to order more than one product, select "True" to proceed.
* **Preferred Payment Method:** Choose how you wish to pay for your order, such as credit card, debit card, etc.
* **Any special requests or instructions?:** Enter any special requests or instructions for your order if needed.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have chosen the correct product category, color, and size.
* Select a payment method that suits your needs and is convenient for you.
* If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please include them in the "Any special requests or instructions?" field.
