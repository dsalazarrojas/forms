# Spirit Wear Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spirit Wear Order Form is designed to collect essential information for apparel orders, including order details, customer information, payment details, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Order Information** section with the required details about your order.
2.  Proceed to the **Customer Information** section, where you'll provide your contact details and any additional notes or comments.
3.  In the **Payment Information** section, enter the payment details, including the total payment amount, payment method, and payment date.
4.  Confirm the order status and add any additional comments in the final **Order Status and Notes** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Information (id: 1)**: Enter the order date, as this is a required field.
* **Items Ordered (id: 3)**: Select the items you would like to order from the available options.
* **Quantity Ordered (id: 4)**: Enter the quantity of each item you wish to order.
* **Shipping Address (id: 5)**: Enter your shipping address, including house number and street address.
* **Customer Information (id: 6)**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Shipping Method (id: 7)**: Select your preferred shipping method from the available options.
* **Payment Type (id: 8)**: Select the payment type you would like to use for your order.
* **Customer Name (id: 9)**: Enter your name as it should appear on the order.
* **Contact Phone (id: 10)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any order updates.
* **Customer Email (id: 11)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Notes (id: 12)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Customer Notes (id: 13)**: Enter any additional customer notes or comments.
* **Order Instructions (id: 14)**: Enter any specific order instructions or requests.
* **Comments (id: 15)**: Add any additional comments about your order.
* **Order Status (id: 16)**: Confirm the status of your order.
* **Additional Order Notes (id: 17)**: Enter any additional order notes or comments.
* **Payment Total (id: 18)**: Enter the total amount you will pay for your order.
* **Payment Method (id: 19)**: Select the payment method you would like to use for your order.
* **Payment Date (id: 20)**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **Refund (id: 21)**: Select the refund option if applicable.
* **Payment Status (id: 22)**: Confirm the payment status.
* **Payment Comments (id: 23)**: Add any additional payment comments.
* **Customer Signature (id: 24)**: Sign your name to confirm your order.
* **Customer Signature Date (id: 25)**: Enter the date you signed the order.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the customer support team.
