# Winter Collection Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Collection Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to reserve items from our winter collection before they are available for general sale. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for the pre-ordered items, ensuring a smooth and efficient pre-ordering and shipping process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out the form, click on the "Submit" button, which will lead you to a page with the form fields.
2.  Start by selecting the "Customer Info" option that best represents you or the intended recipient. This will help us better understand who will be receiving the order.
3.  Enter the Email field with the correct email address you want us to use for communication regarding your pre-order.
4.  Fill in your Name and any additional "Order Notes" you'd like to share with us.
5.  Select your preferred Shipping Preferences from the options provided.
6.  Choose your preferred Payment Method.
7.  Provide the Delivery Address where you want the order to be delivered.
8.  Select the desired Delivery Date and Time for your order.
9.  Finally, fill in the Quantity and Price for the items you are pre-ordering, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Info (Customer 1, Customer 2)** (`customer_info`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of customer that best represents you or the intended recipient.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the correct email address to use for communication.
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your name or the name of the recipient.
*   **Shipping Preferences** (`shipping_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more shipping options from Ground, Express, and Standard.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred payment method from Credit Card, Bank Transfer, and PayPal.
*   **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required: false): Provide the address where you'd like the order delivered.
*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the desired delivery date for your order.
*   **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the desired delivery time for your order.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes or details for your order.
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false): Fill in the quantity of items you are pre-ordering.
*   **Price** (`price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the price for the items you are pre-ordering.
*   **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, required: false): Share any other information you'd like about your order.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your email address for correct spelling and formatting.
*   For accurate processing, please ensure you select the correct customer type and shipping preferences.
*   If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
*   If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
*   Remember, providing accurate information is essential for smooth and efficient processing of your pre-order.
