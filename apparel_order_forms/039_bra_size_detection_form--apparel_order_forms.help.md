# Bra Size Detection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bra Size Detection Form is designed to help you find your accurate bra size. Fill out this form and we'll provide you with personalized recommendations for your bra size and style.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
3. **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you'd like to receive your bra size recommendations.
4. **Measurement Method**: Choose how you will provide your measurements:
	* **I will measure myself**: You will take your own measurements using a tape.
	* **I have professional measurements**: You already have a bra fitting done by a professional.
	* **I need guidance**: You would like help with your measurements.
5. **Underbust Measurement**: Enter the measurement around your ribcage under the bust in inches.
6. **Bust Measurement**: Enter the measurement around the fullest part of your bust in inches.
7. **Measurement Tape Used**: Indicate if you used a soft measuring tape.
8. **Measurement Taken Standing**: Choose if your measurements were taken while standing or not.
9. **Bra Worn During Measurement**: Choose if you were wearing a bra during the measurement.
10. **Current Bra Size**: Enter your current bra size (if you know it).
11. **Current Bra Fit**: Choose how your current bra fits:
	* **Perfect**
	* **Too tight**
	* **Too loose**
	* **Cups too small**
	* **Cups too big**
	* **Band rides up**
	* **Straps dig in**
12. **Band Feel**: Choose how the band feels:
	* **Comfortable and snug**
	* **Too tight**
	* **Too loose**
	* **Rides up in back**
13. **Cup Feel**: Choose how the cups fit:
	* **Smooth and full coverage**
	* **Spillage or overflow**
	* **Gaping**
	* **Wrinkling**
14. **Strap Feel**: Choose how the straps feel:
	* **Comfortable**
	* **Dig into shoulders**
	* **Keep falling down**
	* **Leave marks**
15. **Underwire Comfort**: Choose how the underwire feels:
	* **Comfortable**
	* **Pokes or digs in**
	* **Sits on breast tissue**
	* **Prefer no underwire**
16. **Breast Shape**: Choose your breast shape:
	* **Round**
	* **Teardrop**
	* **Bell**
	* **Slender**
	* **Asymmetric**
17. **Breast Fullness**: Choose where the fullness is:
	* **Full on top**
	* **Full on bottom**
	* **Evenly full**
	* **Close set**
	* **Wide set**
18. **Preferred Style**: Choose your preferred bra style:
	* **T-shirt bra**
	* **Push-up**
	* **Sports bra**
	* **Wireless**
	* **Lace**
	* **Minimizer**
	* **Balconette**
	* **Full coverage**
	* **Plunge**
	* **Strapless**
19. **Primary Use**: Choose your primary use for the bra:
	* **Everyday wear**
	* **Special occasions**
	* **Sports and exercise**
	* **Work**
	* **Sleep**
20. **Calculated Size**: This will display your recommended size.
21. **Sister Sizes**: This will display alternative sizes to try.
22. **Brand Recommendations**: This will display suggested brands.
23. **Fit Tips**: This will display additional advice.
24. **Consent to Contact**: Choose if you consent to us contacting you with recommendations.
