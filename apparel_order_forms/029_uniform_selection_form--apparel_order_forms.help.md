# Uniform Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to select a uniform size, style, quantity, and other details for a custom uniform order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and select the relevant information for your uniform order.
2. Select the correct uniform size from the "Uniform Sizes" page.
3. Choose your preferred uniform style from the "Select Style" page.
4. Determine the quantity of uniforms needed for your order from the "Select Quantity" page.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" page.
6. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number, in the "Email" and "Phone" pages.
7. If applicable, select a date for your uniform delivery or pickup.
8. If applicable, select a time for your uniform delivery or pickup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Uniform Sizes** (page 1): Select the size of the uniform you need from the options: Small, Large, or X-Large.
* **Select Style** (page 2): Choose your preferred uniform style from the options: Style 1, Style 2, or Style 3.
* **Select Quantity** (page 3): Determine how many uniforms you need, selecting from the options: Quantity 1, Quantity 2, or Quantity 3.
* **Note** (page 4): Add any additional notes or comments about your uniform order.
* **Email** (page 5): Provide your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (page 6): Enter your phone number for communication and updates.
* **Date** (page 7): If you selected a uniform delivery or pickup date, choose the specific date for your order.
* **Time** (page 8): If you selected a uniform delivery or pickup time, choose the specific time for your order.
* **Note** (page 9): Add any final notes or comments before submitting your order.
