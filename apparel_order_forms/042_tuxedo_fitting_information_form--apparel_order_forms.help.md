# Tuxedo Fitting Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tuxedo Fitting Information Form is designed to collect important information about a client's fitting needs for a tuxedo. This form allows you to gather essential details about the event, client size, and contact information to provide a smooth and tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Event Details** field by providing the event title, date, and any other relevant information.
2. Enter the **Client Size Details** field with the client's size specifications, including any specific measurements or preferences.
3. Fill out the **Contact Details** field with the client's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (text, required: false): Enter the event title, date, and any other relevant information about the event. For example, "Black-Tie Gala" or "Wedding Reception."
* **Client Size Details** (text, required: false): Provide the client's size specifications, including measurements or preferences. Be as specific as possible, for example, "Height: 5' 8", Waist: 34"
* **Contact Details** (text, required: false): Enter the client's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address. For example, "John Doe, (123) 456-7890, [johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com)"

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the client's size specifications for accuracy.
* Use the provided fields to ensure that you have all the necessary information to provide a well-fitting tuxedo.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for support.
