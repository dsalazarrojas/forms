# Alumni Merchandise Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for alumni to order merchandise items such as T-shirts, hoodies, and caps. It is intended for personal use or as gifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your full name as the buyer.
2. Enter your email address for order confirmation.
3. Select the merchandise item you would like to order.
4. If applicable, choose your size for the selected item.
5. Specify the quantity of the item you would like to order.
6. Enter your shipping address for delivery.
7. Choose your preferred payment method.
8. Optionally, add any special instructions for fulfillment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer name** (`Buyer name`, text, required): This is the full name of the person placing the order or the gift recipient.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive order confirmation.
* **Item selection** (`Item selection`, select_one, required): Choose the type of merchandise item you would like to order (e.g., T-shirt, hoodie, cap).
* **Size selection** (`Size selection`, select_one, optional): If you selected an item that comes in different sizes, choose your size here (e.g., S, M, L, XL).
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required): Specify the number of units of the selected item you would like to order.
* **Shipping address** (`Shipping address`, text, required): Enter your full shipping address for delivery.
* **Payment method** (`Payment method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal, bank transfer).
* **Special instructions** (`Special instructions`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or notes for the fulfillment of your order, you can add them here.
