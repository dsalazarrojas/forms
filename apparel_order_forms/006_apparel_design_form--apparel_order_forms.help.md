# Apparel Design Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for apparel design orders, allowing you to create custom designs based on your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your order information, which includes:
	* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on the order.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions.
2. If you're representing a company, enter the **Company Name** if it's not already provided.
3. Select the **What product do you need designed?** option that best describes the item you want to design.
4. Choose the **Product Size** option that matches your order.
5. Enter the **Number of Units** you require.
6. Select a **Primary Color** from the options provided.
7. If you want to add a secondary color, select an option from the **Secondary Color** field.
8. Describe your design in the **Design Details** field, including any logos, text, graphics, or specific details.
9. Choose a **Printing Method** from the options provided.
10. Estimate your **Budget** for the order, selecting an option that best fits your needs.
11. Select a **Delivery Date** for the order.
12. If you have any **Special Requests or Notes**, enter them in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it should appear on the order.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company if it's not already provided.
* **What product do you need designed?** (`product_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes the item you want to design.
* **Product Size** (`product_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that matches your order size.
* **Number of Units** (`quantity_needed`, `number`, required): Enter the number of units you require.
* **Primary Color** (`primary_color`, `select_one`, required): Select a primary color from the options provided.
* **Secondary Color** (`secondary_color`, `select_one`, optional): Select an option from the **Secondary Color** field if you want to add a secondary color.
* **Design Details** (`design_details_text`, `text`, required): Describe your design in this field, including any logos, text, graphics, or specific details.
* **Printed Method** (`print_technique`, `select_one`, optional): Select a printing method from the options provided.
* **Estimated Budget** (`budget_budget`, `select_one`, optional): Select an option that best fits your budget.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Select a delivery date for the order.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests or notes you have.

This guide should help you fill out the form with confidence. If you have any further questions, don't hesitate to reach out.
