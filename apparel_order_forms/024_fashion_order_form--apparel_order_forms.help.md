# fashion_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fashion_order_form is a simple order form used to select and purchase fashion-related products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the products you wish to purchase from the list of options.
2. Enter the quantity of the product(s) you have selected.
3. Click "submit" to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select products** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the fashion products you want to purchase. You will be presented with a list of options; choose all relevant products by checking the checkboxes beside them.
* **Quantity** (`number`, optional): Enter the quantity of the product(s) selected. Please note that this field is meant to be filled in after selecting the products.
* **Submit** (`text`, optional): Once you have selected and quantified your products, click "submit" to complete the order.

## Tips
* Be sure to review your selections before submitting the form to ensure all desired products are included in your order.
* If you have trouble selecting products or need further assistance, contact our customer support team.
