# Clothing Order Form Template WorldPay UK - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details for a clothing item from customers, including their personal contact information, product selection, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: 
    - **Full Name:** Enter your full name.
    - **Email Address:** Enter your contact email.
    - **Phone Number:** Enter your phone number.
2. Select your product: 
    - **Select Product:** Choose the item you wish to order.
3. Enter product specifics: 
    - **Size:** Select the size of the item.
    - **Quantity:** Enter the number of items you wish to order.
    - **Unit Price (GBP):** Enter the price per item.
4. Enter your delivery address: 
    - **Delivery Address:** Enter your full delivery address.
    - **City or Town:** Enter your city or town.
    - **Postcode:** Enter your UK postcode.
5. Choose your shipping method:
    - **Shipping Method:** Select your preferred delivery method.
6. Review your order details:
    - Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately.
    - If your billing address is different from your delivery address, fill in the billing address details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1):** The customer's name.
* **Email Address (2):** The customer's contact email.
* **Phone Number (3):** The customer's phone number.
* **Product Information (4):** This section is for customers to add a note about the item they wish to order.
* **Select Product (5):** Choose the item from the catalogue.
* **Product Description (6):** Enter any additional details about the product style and specifications.
* **Colour (7):** Enter the preferred colour of the item.
* **Size (8):** Select the size of the item from the options provided.
* **Quantity (9):** Enter the number of items you wish to order.
* **Unit Price (GBP) (10):** Enter the price per item in pounds.
* **Line Total (GBP) (11):** This is automatically calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Delivery Address (12):** Enter your full delivery address.
* **City or Town (14):** Enter your city or town.
* **Postcode (15):** Enter your UK postcode.
* **County (16):** Enter the county or state (optional).
* **Country (17):** Enter the country of delivery.
* **Billing Address (18):** Enter your billing address if different from delivery address.
* **Billing Same as Delivery Address (19):** If your billing address is the same as your delivery address, select 'True'.
* **Shipping Method (22):** Select your preferred delivery method.
* **Subtotal (GBP) (24):** This is automatically calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Shipping Cost (GBP) (25):** This is automatically calculated based on the shipping method.
