# Fashion Design Idea Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and organize fashion design ideas for further review and action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Use the "Fashion References" field to provide any relevant references for your design idea.
3. Enter the design timeline, including start and end dates.
4. Provide a budget for your design idea, if applicable.
5. Describe your design idea in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fashion References**: This field is used to provide any relevant references for your fashion design idea.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Design Timeline**: Enter the timeline for your fashion design idea, including start and end dates.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: false
* **Budget**: If you have a budget in mind for your design idea, enter it here.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: false
* **Collection Name**: Enter a name for your fashion collection.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Collection Date**: Enter the date for your fashion collection.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: false
* **Description**: Describe your fashion design idea in this field.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false

Note: Some fields are not required, so please only fill in the fields that are relevant to your fashion design idea.
