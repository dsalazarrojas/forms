# Costume Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Costume Order Form Template is a simple, easy-to-use form designed to collect information from customers for costume orders. Fill out this form to place an order for a costume with our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Make sure to enter your information accurately and completely.
3. Review your form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the order.
### Customer Email
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
### Order Date
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like the order fulfilled on.
### Order Time
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like the order fulfilled on.
### Customer Address
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your home or office address for us to ship the order to.
### Customer City
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, optional): Enter the city for your address.
### Customer State
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, optional): Enter the state for your address.
### Customer Zip
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code for address completion.
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your order, such as special requests or instructions.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or questions, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
