# School T Shirt Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track student t-shirt purchases for school events or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select all students who purchased a t-shirt from the "School T Shirt Purchases" field.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Select the student's preferred t-shirt size from the available options in the "Student Size" field.
4. Choose the type of purchase from the "Purchase Class/Activity" field.
5. Select the payment method used for the purchase from the "Payment Method" field.
6. If applicable, mark the "School Admin" field as "Yes" if the purchase was processed by a school administrator.
7. Enter the purchase date in the "Purchase Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School T Shirt Purchases**: Select all students who purchased a t-shirt.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name who purchased a t-shirt.
* **Student Size**: Select the student's preferred t-shirt size.
* **Purchase Class/Activity**: Choose the type of purchase (e.g., class, activity).
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the purchase.
* **School Admin**: Mark "Yes" if the purchase was processed by a school administrator.
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date of the purchase.
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the t-shirt purchase.
* **Fee Amount**: Enter the fee amount for the t-shirt purchase.
* **Purchase Activity**: Choose the type of purchase (e.g., activity).
* **School Admin**: Mark "Yes" if the purchase was processed by a school administrator. 

Note: This form requires no payment, the "Total Cost" and "Fee Amount" fields are only for information purposes and should be filled only if required.
