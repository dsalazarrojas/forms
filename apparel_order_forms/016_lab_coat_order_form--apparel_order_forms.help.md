# Lab Coat Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lab Coat Order Form is a tool used to gather information for lab coat orders. It collects details such as size, color, style, quantity, and other relevant information to ensure accurate and efficient lab coat ordering.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2.  Fill in the required fields with the correct information, including size, color, and style of the lab coat.
3.  Select the correct quantity of lab coats required.
4.  Add any additional notes if needed.
5.  Enter your contact details (email and phone number) for follow-up.
6.  Choose the correct lab coat order option.
7.  Finally, add any additional order notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Lab Coat Size** (`lab_coat_size`): Select the size of the lab coat you need, using the size options provided in the dropdown.
*   **Lab Coat Color** (`lab_coat_color`): Choose the color of the lab coat you need from the options provided in the dropdown.
*   **Lab Coat Style** (`lab_coat_style`): Select one or more styles of lab coats you need from the options provided in the dropdown.
*   **Quantity** (`lab_coat_quantity`): Enter the number of lab coats you need.
*   **Notes** (`lab_coat_notes`): If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here.
*   **Email** (`lab_coat_email`): Enter your email address for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (`lab_coat_phone`): Enter your phone number for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Order Date** (`lab_coat_date`): Enter the date you need the lab coats by.
*   **Order Time** (`lab_coat_time`): Enter the time you need the lab coats by.
*   **Time Zone** (`lab_coat_time_zone`): Enter your time zone for accurate scheduling.
*   **Lab Coat Order** (`lab_coat_order`): Choose the reason for your lab coat order from the options provided in the dropdown.
*   **Additional Order Notes** (`lab_coat_order_notes`): If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here.

This guide is designed to be concise and easy to understand. Please let me know if you need further clarification or have any questions.
