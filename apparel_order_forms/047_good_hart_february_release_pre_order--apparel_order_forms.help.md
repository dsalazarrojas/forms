# good_hart_february_release_pre_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-order form for the "Good Hart" product release in February 2024. It will help the company prepare for the release by collecting customer information and product preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
5. Select your shipping city in the "Shipping City" field.
6. Select your shipping state in the "Shipping State" field.
7. Enter your shipping zip code in the "Shipping Zip" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Select the products you are interested in by choosing "Yes" for each product in the "Product" field.
10. Select multiple products you are interested in by choosing "Yes" for each product in the "Product Multi Select" field.
11. Enter the desired date for your order in the "Date" field.
12. Enter the desired time for your order in the "Time" field.
13. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
14. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address.
* **Shipping Address** (4): Enter your shipping address.

* **Shipping State** (6): Select your shipping state.
* **Shipping Zip** (7): Enter your shipping zip code.
* **Phone** (8): Enter your phone number.
* **Product** (9): Select "Yes" for each product you are interested in.
* **Product Multi Select** (10): Select "Yes" for multiple products you are interested in.
* **Date** (11): Enter the desired date for your order.
