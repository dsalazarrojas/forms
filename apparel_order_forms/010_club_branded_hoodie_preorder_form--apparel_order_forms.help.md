# Club Branded Hoodie Preorder Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Club Branded Hoodie Preorder Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to pre-order a branded hoodie from the club. This form is essential for processing pre-orders and gathering necessary details for order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the provided field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** to receive order confirmation.
3. If you are a registered **Club Member**, provide your **Club Member ID**.
4. Select your preferred **Hoodie Size** from the provided options.
5. Choose your preferred **Hoodie Color Preference** from the options.
6. Enter the **Quantity** of hoodies you want to order.
7. Make sure to select a valid **Payment Method** for your order.
8. Agree to the **Terms and Conditions** of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Provide your full name for order processing and communication.
	+ Required field
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive order confirmation.
	+ Required field
* **Phone Number**: Optional field for additional contact.
* **Club Member ID**: If you are a registered club member, provide your member ID for identification.
	+ Optional field
* **Hoodie Size**: Select your preferred size for the hoodie.
	+ Required field
	+ Options: XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL, XXXL
* **Hoodie Color Preference**: Choose your preferred color for the hoodie.
	+ Required field
	+ Options: Navy Blue, Black, Grey, White, Charcoal, Forest Green
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of hoodies you want to order.
	+ Required field
* **Unit Price per Hoodie**: This field displays the price per unit of the hoodie.
	+ Read-only field
* **Total Order Cost**: This field automatically calculates the total cost of the order based on the quantity and unit price.
	+ Read-only field
* **Customization Options**: Choose from various customization options for personalization.
	+ Optional field
* **Custom Text or Name**: If you choose a customization option, enter your custom text or name.
	+ Optional field
* **Delivery Address**: Provide your full street address for delivery purposes.
	+ Required field
* **City**: Enter your city for delivery purposes.
	+ Required field
* **State or Province**: Enter your state or province for delivery purposes.
	+ Required field
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your ZIP or postal code for delivery purposes.
	+ Required field
* **Payment Method**: Select your payment method.
	+ Required field
	+ Options: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check
* **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the order.
	+ Required field
