# Makeup Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Order Form is used to collect necessary information for the makeup order process. This includes order details, shipping information, payment method, and delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Order Details section with the required information.
2.  Enter your Shipping Address in the Shipping Address section.
3.  Provide your contact information in the Customer Information section.
4.  Select the Payment Type and Method you prefer.
5.  Choose the Shipping Method that suits your needs.
6.  Select the desired Delivery Date and Time.
7.  Enter the Delivery Location.
8.  Sign the form by providing a digital signature.
9.  Fill out any other optional fields that you feel are relevant to your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Details** (`Order Details`, text, required): Enter the details of the order, including any specific products or services you want.
*   **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address, including the address line 1, line 2, city, state, and zip code.
*   **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, number, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for any updates or concerns.
*   **Customer E-mail** (`Customer E-mail`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your order.
*   **Total** (`Total`, number, required): Enter the total amount of the order.
*   **Payment Type** (`Payment Type`, select\_multiple, required): Choose the type of payment you prefer, such as Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
*   **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select\_one, required): Choose the specific payment method you prefer.
*   **Shipping Method** (`Shipping Method`, select\_multiple, required): Choose the shipping method that suits your needs, such as Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, or Free Shipping.
*   **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, date, required): Select the desired delivery date for your order.
*   **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, time, required): Select the desired delivery time for your order.
*   **Delivery Location** (`Delivery Location`, text, required): Enter the specific location where you want to receive your order.
*   **Customer Signature** (`Customer Signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your digital signature.
*   **Customer Address** (`Customer Address`, text, required): Enter your address.
*   **Sales Rep** (`Sales Rep`, text, optional): Enter the name of the sales representative who assisted you with your order.
*   **Sales Rep Phone** (`Sales Rep Phone`, number, optional): Enter the phone number of the sales representative who assisted you with your order.
*   **Sales Rep E-mail** (`Sales Rep E-mail`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the sales representative who assisted you with your order.
*   **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or instructions you have for your order.
*   **Shipping Notes** (`Shipping Notes`, text, optional): Enter any shipping notes or instructions you have for your order.
*   **Customer Comments** (`Customer Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about your order.
*   **Order Comments** (`Order Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about your order.
*   **Special Instructions** (`Special Instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or requirements you have for your order.
*   **Sales Rep Notes** (`Sales Rep Notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the sales representative who assisted you with your order.
*   **Sales Rep Signature** (`Sales Rep Signature`, text, optional): Enter a digital signature of the sales representative who assisted you with your order.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter all required fields to ensure that your order is processed accurately and efficiently.
*   Use a digital signature to sign the form, which can be a scanned image of your handwritten signature or a typed signature.
*   If you have any special requests or instructions, make sure to enter them in the relevant fields so that the sales representative who will be handling your order can take note of them.
*   Review your order details and shipping information carefully before submitting the form to ensure that everything is accurate.
