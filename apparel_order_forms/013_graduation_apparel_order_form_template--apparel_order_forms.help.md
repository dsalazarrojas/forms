# Graduation Apparel Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Graduation Apparel Order Form Template is a simple form designed to collect information for graduation orders. It is used to collect student and order details, such as name, email, phone number, order details, graduation apparel size, color, quantity, and total cost.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student information: Enter the student's name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the graduation apparel size and color from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter the graduation details: Enter the quantity and total cost of the graduation apparel, and select the date and time for the graduation order.
4. Add any additional order details in the text field.
5. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduation Apparel Order Form** (`graduation_apparel_order_form`): This is the main section of the form where you can input any additional order details.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone Number** (`student_phone`): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Enter any additional order details, such as special requests or instructions.
* **Graduation Apparel Size** (`graduation_apparel_size`): Select the size of the graduation apparel from the dropdown menu.
* **Graduation Apparel Color** (`graduation_apparel_color`): Select the color of the graduation apparel from the dropdown menu.
* **Graduation Apparel Quantity** (`graduation_apparel_quantity`): Enter the quantity of graduation apparel ordered.
* **Graduation Apparel Total** (`graduation_apparel_total`): Enter the total cost of the graduation apparel.
* **Graduation Order Date** (`graduation_order_date`): Enter the date of the graduation order.
* **Graduation Order Time** (`graduation_order_time`): Enter the time of the graduation order.
* **Graduation Message** (`graduation_message`): Enter any additional message or comments for the graduation order.
