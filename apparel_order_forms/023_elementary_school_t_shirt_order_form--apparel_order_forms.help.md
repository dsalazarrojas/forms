# Elementary School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elementary School T Shirt Order Form is a simple form designed for users to order Elementary School T-shirts. It helps to gather information about the user's preferences and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
- Please fill in your preferred Color(s) by selecting one or more from the available options.
- Select your preferred Size from the options provided.
- Choose the type of T-Shirt you want to order from the options.
- Confirm whether or not you want to proceed with the order by selecting "Yes" or "No".
- Please provide your Email and Phone number for contact purposes.
- Fill in any additional Note if required.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
  Please provide any relevant details or comments about your order.
- **Select Color** (`select_color`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more colors you prefer for your Elementary School T-Shirt.
  You can choose multiple colors by selecting more than one option.
- **Select Size** (`select_size`, select one, optional): Select the size you prefer for your Elementary Shirt.
  Please choose one size from the given options.
- **Select Shirt Type** (`select_shirt_type`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of T-Shirt you'd like to order.
  You can select one or more options.
- **Submit** (`submit`, select one, required): Confirm your order by selecting "Yes".
  Please note that submitting this form will be considered as confirmation of your order.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
  If left blank, the default date will be used.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
  If left blank, the default time will be used.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
  Please ensure the email is correctly formatted.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  Please ensure the number is correctly formatted.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
  If required, please fill in the details.
