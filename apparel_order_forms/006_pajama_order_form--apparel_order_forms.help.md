# Pajama Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pajama Order Form is a user-facing form designed to collect information from customers who wish to place an order for pajamas. This form helps us understand what customers are looking for in their pajama purchase and ensures that their order is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your user information (name, email, etc.) on the first page.
- Provide your shipping details, including the address to which you would like your order delivered.
- Finally, enter your payment information to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Page 1)** (`user_info`, text, **Required (Note: While not explicitly marked as required in the form, it's implied that this is a necessary field for any order)**): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Shipping Details (Page 1)** (`shipping_details`, text, **Optional**): Provide your shipping address and any special delivery instructions. If you do not need to ship to a different address, leave this blank.
* **Payment Information (Page 3)** (`payment_info`, text, **Optional**): Enter your payment method, including credit card information or other payment details.
