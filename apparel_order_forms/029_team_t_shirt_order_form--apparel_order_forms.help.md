# Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect information for team orders of custom t-shirts. This form helps the team administrator gather essential details from the team members about their orders, including team name, order date, shirt color, quantity, team logo, order type, customer name, customer email, customer phone number, and team size. Please note that all fields are optional except for team size and shipping method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the required information about the team order.
* Select the desired shirt color from the drop-down menu.
* Choose the team logo from the multi-select menu.
* Select the order type from the drop-down menu.
* Enter the customer's name and email address.
* Enter the customer's phone number.
* Select the team size from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_name** (text): Enter the name of the team that is placing the order.
* **order_date** (date): Select the date for the order.
* **color** (select_one): Choose the color of the t-shirt.
* **quantity** (number): Enter the number of t-shirts ordered.
* **team_logo** (select_multiple): Select up to three team logos to be printed on the t-shirt.
* **order_type** (select_one): Select the type of order (e.g., promotional, event).
* **customer_name** (text): Enter the customer's name.
* **customer_email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer_phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **team_size** (select_one): Select the size of the team (e.g., small, medium, large).
* **shipping_method** (select_one): Select the shipping method (e.g., ship to store, ship to home, ship to work).
