# Cultural Festival Costume Order Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in ordering custom cultural festival costumes. It is used to gather essential details about the festival, size requirements, delivery preferences, and contact information. Please fill out this form to place your order and we will contact you with further instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the requested fields with accurate and complete information.
2.  Choose your preferred delivery method (if applicable).
3.  Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Festival Name
• **festival_name** (<font face="monospace">text</font>) - A description of the festival or event for which you are ordering a costume. Enter the name or a brief description of the festival.

### Size Details
• **size_details** (<font face="monospace">text</font>) - Please provide details on your size requirements for the costume, such as your height, weight, or other measurements. This will help us create a proper fit.

### Delivery Method
• **delivery_info** (<font face="monospace">select one</font>) - Choose between "Delivery" or "Pickup" for your costume. If you select "Delivery", we will provide more information on our delivery options and costs. If you select "Pickup", you will need to come to our store to pick up your costume.

### Contact Information
• **contact_first_name** (<font face="monospace">text</font>) - Enter your first name.
• **contact_last_name** (<font face="monospace">text</font>) - Enter your last name.
• **email** (<font face="monospace">email</font>) - Enter your email address.
• **phone** (<font face="monospace">text</font>) - Enter your phone number.
• **vendor_info** (<font face="monospace">text</font>) - Enter any additional information about your purchase, such as any special requests or allergies.
• **order_notes** (<font face="monospace">text</font>) - Enter any notes or comments about your order.

### Order Reference
• **order_id** (<font face="monospace">text</font>) - This is a unique reference code for your order. It will be provided to you after you submit this form.

## Tips
- Please double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us at your preferred contact information.
