# t_shirt_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The t_shirt_product_order_form is a form designed to collect information from customers to facilitate the order process for t-shirts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page if you wish to understand more about the form.
2. Fill in your customer details in the "customer_details" field.
3. Enter information about the t-shirt you wish to purchase, including details such as design, size, and style, in the "t_shirt_info" field.
4. Enter payment information in the "payment_info" field.
5. Enter shipping details, including destination and any special instructions, in the "shipping_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_details** (text, required: false): Enter your name, email, and other relevant contact information here.
* **t_shirt_info** (text, required: false): Enter information about the t-shirt you wish to purchase, including details such as design, size, and style.
* **payment_info** (text, required: false): Enter your payment method and details, including card type and expiration date.
* **shipping_info** (text, required: false): Enter your shipping details, including destination and any special instructions.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately to ensure a smooth order process.
* If you need help with the form or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
