# kayleighs_lipsense_order_form_final - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kayleighs_lipsense_order_form_final form is used to collect order information from customers for various types of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "customer_details" page.
2. Add shipping information, including address, in the "shipping_address" page.
3. Select a delivery date for your order in the "delivery_date" page.
4. Choose your payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Other) and enter any relevant details in the "payment_method" page.
5. List the items you wish to purchase in the "items_to_buy" page.
6. Enter the quantity of each item in the "quantity" page.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your order in the "order_notes" page.
8. Enter any discount code or additional information in the "discount_code" page.
9. Review and submit your order for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information, such as email and phone number.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Provide your shipping address, including street, city, and state.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, text, required/optional): Select a delivery date for your order.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose from the following options:
  * Cash
  * Bank Transfer
  * Credit Card
  * Other
- **Items to Buy** (`items_to_buy`, text, required/optional): List the items you wish to purchase.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of each item.
- **Comments** (`order_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
- **Discount Code** (`discount_code`, text, required/optional): Enter any discount code or additional information.
- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
- **Assigned Sales Person** (`assigned_salesperson`, text, required/optional): Identify the sales person assigned to your order.
- **Order Subtotal** (`order_subtotal`, number, required/optional): Review and enter the order subtotal.
- **Items Subtotal** (`items_subtotal`, number, required/optional): Review and enter the items subtotal.
- **Grand Total** (`grand_total`, number, required/optional): Review and enter the grand total.
- **Items 1-10** (`items_ordered_1`, `items_ordered_2`, ..., `items_ordered_10`, text, required/optional): List each item you wish to purchase, with quantity if applicable.
- **Additional Items** (`items_ordered_11`, `items_ordered_12`, ..., `items_ordered_20`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional items not listed above.
- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Tips
- Ensure you enter accurate information for all fields.
- Double-check your order details before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the sales team.
