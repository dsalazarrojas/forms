# College Apparel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to place orders for college apparel items. It collects information from customers, including order date, contact details, apparel preferences, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your order date.
2.  Enter your full name.
3.  If you are a student, provide your student ID.
4.  Enter your email address.
5.  Provide your phone number.
6.  Choose the type of apparel item you wish to order.
7.  Select the color of your apparel item.
8.  Choose your preferred size.
9.  Enter the quantity of items you wish to order.
10. Choose a design option for your apparel item.
11. If desired, add custom text or personalization to your order.
12. If applicable, enter the price per item for your order.
13. Enter the total order cost.
14. Provide your full shipping address.
15. Enter the city of your shipping address.
16. Enter the state of your shipping address.
17. Enter the postal code of your shipping address.
18. Enter the country of your shipping address.
19. Choose a shipping method for your order.
20. Select a payment method for your order.
21. If applicable, provide any special instructions for your order.
22. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order. This will help us process your order efficiently.
*   **Customer Name**: Enter your full name to help us address you correctly.
*   **Student ID**: If you are a student, enter your student ID for verification purposes.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Apparel Item Type**: Choose the type of apparel item you wish to order. This will help us process your order accurately.
*   **Color**: Select the color of your apparel item.
*   **Size**: Choose your preferred size.
*   **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you wish to order.
*   **Design Option**: Choose a design option for your apparel item.
*   **Custom Text or Personalization**: If desired, enter any custom text or personalization you wish to add to your order.
*   **Price per Item**: If applicable, enter the price per item for your order.
*   **Total Order Cost**: If applicable, enter the total cost of your order.
*   **Shipping Address**: Enter your full shipping address to ensure timely delivery.
*   **City**: Enter the city of your shipping address.
*   **State**: Enter the state of your shipping address.
*   **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your shipping address.
*   **Country**: Enter the country of your shipping address.
*   **Shipping Method**: Choose a shipping method that suits your needs. Options include standard shipping, expedited shipping, overnight shipping, and campus pickup.
*   **Payment Method**: Select a payment method that suits your needs. Options include credit card, debit card, campus account, PayPal, and check.
*   **Special Instructions**: If you have any special requests, enter them here.
*   **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of your order.
