# Dancewear Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dancewear Order Form Template is designed to collect customer information and order details for dancewear products. This template helps guide customers through the process of placing an order, ensuring that all essential details are collected for smooth order processing and customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's information in the **Customer Info** field.
2. Select the payment method from the options provided.
3. Enter the shipping address in the **Shipping Address** field.
4. Add any special instructions or notes in the **Special Instructions** field.
5. Enter the customer's billing information in the **Billing Info** field.
6. Select the order date and time in the **Order Date** and **Order Time** fields.
7. Enter the total order value in the **Order Total** field.
8. Enter the customer's phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, **Required**): Enter the customer's name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Order Products** (`Order Products`, text, **Optional**): List the products ordered, including the quantity and any specific requests or notes.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select one, **Optional**): Select the payment method: American Express, Discover, Mastercard, or Visa.
* **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, text, **Optional**): Enter the shipping address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Special Instructions** (`Special Instructions`, note, **Optional**): Add any special instructions or notes for the order.
* **Customer Notes** (`Customer Notes`, text, **Optional**): Enter any additional information or comments for the customer.
* **Billing Info** (`Billing Info`, text, **Required**): Enter the customer's billing information, including the name on the card, card number, and expiration date.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, date, **Optional**): Select the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`Order Time`, time, **Optional**): Select the time of the order.
* **Order Total** (`Order Total`, number, **Optional**): Enter the total value of the order.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter the customer's phone number.
