# Homemade Mask Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place custom orders for homemade masks. It collects customer information, order details, and shipping preferences for each order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your shipping address, including your city and zip code.
3. Specify the order details, including the type, color, and quantity of masks you want to order.
4. Choose your preferred shipping method and payment type.
5. Add any customer notes or messages for the order.
6. Complete the form by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Name (`customer_name`)
• **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Customer Email (`customer_email`)
• **Customer Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for order updates and confirmation.

### 3. Customer Phone (`customer_phone`)
• **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for order updates and confirmation.

### 4. Shipping Address (`shipping_address`)
• **Shipping Address**: Enter your full shipping address where the order will be delivered.

### 5. City (`shipping_city`)
• **City**: Enter the city where the shipping address is located.

### 6. Zip (`shipping_zip`)
• **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the shipping address.

### 7. Order Details (`order_details`)
• **Order Details**: Describe the type of mask you want to order, including any custom requests or notes.

### 8. Country (`shipping_country`)
• **Country**: Enter the country where the shipping address is located.

### 9. Customer Notes (`customer_notes`)
• **Customer Notes**: Add any additional notes or messages for the order.

### 10. Order Quantity (`order_quantity`)
• **Order Quantity**: Enter the quantity of masks you want to order.

### 11. Order Type (`order_type`)
• **Order Type**: Choose the type of order you want to place, including Custom, Custom Plus, or Standard.

### 12. Order Color (`order_color`)
• **Order Color**: Select at least one color option for the order.

### 13. Order Message (`order_message`)
• **Order Message**: Add any additional notes or messages for the order.

### 14. Order Payment Type (`order_payment_type`)
• **Order Payment Type**: Choose the payment method you want to use for the order, including Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.

### 15. Order Shipping Method (`order_shipping_method`)
• **Order Shipping Method**: Choose the shipping method you prefer, including Standard, Expedited, or Overnight.

### 16. Order Total (`order_total`)
• **Order Total**: This field is not applicable for customer use. It is for internal use only.

### 17. Custom Fields (`custom_fields`)
• **Custom Fields**: Choose any custom fields that apply to your order.

### 18. Form Submission ID (`form_submission_id`)
• **Form Submission ID**: This field is not applicable for customer use. It is for internal use only.
