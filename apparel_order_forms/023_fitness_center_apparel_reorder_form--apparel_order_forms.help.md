# Fitness Center Apparel Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers or stores to facilitate the reordering of fitness center apparel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the help guide above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or documentation.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or documentation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your valid phone number that can be used for communication.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your reorder.
* **Reorder Items** (`reorder_items`, select one, required): Select the type of apparel or accessory you would like to reorder from the available options.
* **Reorder Quantity** (`reorder_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity you would like to reorder.
* **Reorder Item Type** (`reorder_item_type`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of item you would like to reorder from the available options.
* **Reorder Date** (`reorder_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to receive the reordered item.
* **Reorder Time** (`reorder_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive the reordered item.
* **Reorder Time Note** (`reorder_time_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the reorder time.
* **Store ID** (`store_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the store where the item will be picked up or delivered. 
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the customer who placed the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, required): Choose your preferred payment method from the available options.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the payment date for the reordered item.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the store where the item will be picked up or delivered.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, optional): Enter the store address where the item will be picked up or delivered.
* **Reorder Status** (`reorder_status`, select one, optional): Choose the status of your reorder from the available options.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer who placed the order.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or notes from the customer.
* **Store Manager Notes** (`store_manager_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the store manager.
* **Store Approval** (`store_approval`, select one, required): Choose whether the store has approved the reorder from the available options.
* **Customer Approval** (`customer_approval`, select one, required): Choose whether the customer has approved the reorder from the available options.
* **Store Notes** (`store_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the store.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Leave your signature as a customer.
