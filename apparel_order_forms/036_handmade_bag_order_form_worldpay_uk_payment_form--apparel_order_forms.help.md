# HandMade Bag Order Form Worldpay UK Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer and order information for hand-made bags in the UK, taking payments via Worldpay. It will guide you through what to input in each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  **Form Details** (`form_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your order, such as your bag design request.
2.  **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): Provide your name and any relevant contact information.
3.  **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required): Enter your shipping address, including your city, state, zip, and any other relevant information.
4.  **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Describe your order, including any custom requests or instructions.
5.  **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter your payment method and payment information.
6.  **Order Review** (`order_review`, text, required): Summarize your order for accuracy and any additional notes.
7.  **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Write any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us about your order.
8.  **Billing Details** (`billing_details`, text, required): Enter the billing address, including your name and any other relevant information.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all fields accurately, especially the payment details, as this will affect the order fulfillment process.
*   If you have any custom requests or specific requirements, include them in the "Order Details" field.
*   Use the "Customer Notes" field to add any extra information that might be helpful for your order.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and speed up the order process. 

This guide will help users fill out the form with ease and ensure they get accurate, timely, and proper hand-made bag orders in the UK via Worldpay.
