# Custom Beanie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Beanie Order Form is designed for customers to provide their preferences for custom beanie orders. The form collects information on beanie color, size, type, quantity, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the beanie color and size you would like.
2. Choose the type of beanie you prefer.
3. Enter the number of beanies you would like to order.
4. Provide special instructions or any other information you would like to add (optional).
5. Enter your email address for our records.
6. Enter your phone number so we can contact you (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Beanie Color** (`custom_beanie_order_form`, select_one): Choose between 'Yes' or 'No' to select the beanie color. Please note that this field is required.
* **Beanie Size** (`custom_beanie_order_form_size`, select_one): Select between 'Yes' or 'No' to select the beanie size. This field is also required.
* **Beanie Type** (`beanie_type`, select_multiple): Choose multiple options for the beanie type. This field is optional.
* **Quantity** (`custom_beanie_order_form_quantity`, number): Enter the number of beanies you'd like to order. This field is required.
* **Special Instructions** (`beanie_message`, text): Enter any special instructions or information you'd like to add about your order. This field is optional.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email): Enter your email address for our records. This field is optional.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you. This field is optional.
