# Staff Apparel Size Collection - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Staff Apparel Size Collection form is designed to collect information from employees regarding their apparel size preferences. This information will be used to order staff apparel items, such as shirts and pants, tailored to each employee's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter Employee Information**: Fill in the employee's employee ID, name, department, title, and manager's name in the corresponding fields.
2. **Select Shirt Size**: Choose the desired shirt size from the options: Small, Medium, Large, X-Large.
3. **Select Pant Size**: Choose the desired pant size from the options: Small, Medium, Large, X-Large.
4. **Select Sleeve Length**: Choose the desired sleeve length from the options: Short, Long.
5. **Select Shirt Item**: Choose the type of shirt you would like to order, from the options: Polo, T-Shirt, Hoodie.
6. **Select Jacket Size**: Choose the desired jacket size from the options: Small, Medium, Large.
7. **Select Pant Item**: Choose the type of pant you would like to order, from the options: Short, Long.
8. **Select Shirt Type**: Choose the desired shirt type from the options: Short Sleeve, Long Sleeve.
9. **Select Pant Type**: Choose the desired pant type from the options: Short Sleeve, Long Sleeve.
10. **Add Note**: Enter any additional comments or requests in the note field.
11. **Enter Contact Information**: If you have any contact information, please enter it in the email, phone, and employee number fields.
12. **Review and Submit**: Review all the information you have entered and click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name.
* **Department** (`department`): Select your department from the available options.
* **Title** (`employee_title`): Enter your title or position.
* **Manager's Name** (`employee_manager`): Enter your manager's name.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`): Select the size of shirt you would like to order from the options: Small, Medium, Large, X-Large.
* **Pant Size** (`pant_size`): Select the size of pant you would like to order from the options: Small, Medium, Large, X-Large.
* **Sleeve Length** (`sleeve_length`): Select the sleeve length of shirt you would like to order from the options: Short, Long.
* **Shirt Item** (`shirt_item_request`): Select the type of shirt you would like to order from the options: Polo, T-Shirt, Hoodie.
* **Jacket Size** (`jacket_size`): Select the size of jacket you would like to order from the options: Small, Medium, Large.
* **Pant Item** (`pant_item_request`): Select the type of pant you would like to order from the options: Short, Long.
* **Shirt Type** (`shirt_type`): Select the type of sleeve of shirt you would like to order from the options: Short Sleeve, Long Sleeve.
* **Pant Type** (`pant_type`): Select the type of sleeve of pant you would like to order from the options: Short Sleeve, Long Sleeve.
* **Note** (`note`): Enter any additional comments or requests.
* **Department ID** (`department_id`): Enter your department ID number.
