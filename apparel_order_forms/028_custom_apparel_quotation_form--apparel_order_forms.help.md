# Custom Apparel Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for custom apparel orders. It is used to gather details about the product, customer, and order status for quotation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Select the relevant options for the payment method and product type from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional information that is relevant to the order in the "Order Comments" field.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Apparel Form**: This is the main form where you can enter custom apparel details.
* **Product Details**: Enter a brief description of the product you wish to order.
* **Customer Info**: Enter your name and email address for billing purposes.
* **Custom Apparel Form 1**: This is an additional custom apparel form for more details.
* **Order Details**: Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the order.
* **Billing Info**: Enter your billing address and any relevant details.
* **Shipping Info**: Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any notes about the customer or order.
* **Order Status**: Enter the current status of the order.
* **Customer Status**: Enter the current status of the customer.
* **Billing Status**: Enter the current billing status of the order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the order (e.g. Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Cash, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Status**: Select the current payment status of the order (e.g. Paid, Pending, Unpaid).
* **Product Type**: Select the type of product being ordered (e.g. T-shirt, Hoodie, Socks, Pants, Jacket, Hat).
* **Product Color**: Enter the color of the product (e.g. red, blue, green).
* **Product Size**: Enter the size of the product (e.g. small, medium, large).
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any notes about the customer.
* **Custom Fields 1**: Enter any custom fields 1 information.
* **Custom Fields 2**: Enter any custom fields 2 information.

Note: Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
