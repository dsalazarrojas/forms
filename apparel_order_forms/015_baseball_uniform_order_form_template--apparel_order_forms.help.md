# Baseball Uniform Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather the necessary information for the production of custom baseball uniforms, including team details, uniform specifications, sizing information, customization options, and delivery and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the Team Information section with your team's name, contact person's name, email, and phone number.
2. Proceed to the Uniform Specifications section, where you will select the jersey, pants, and sock colors, as well as the logo and logo placement on the jersey.
3. In the Sizing Information section, provide the total number of uniforms needed and the distribution of sizes.
4. In the Customization Options section, select any additional embroidery or design elements you would like to have on the uniforms.
5. Finally, in the Delivery and Payment section, choose your preferred delivery date, address, budget per uniform, payment method, and confirm your order information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team name** (`team_name`, required): Enter your team's name.
* **Contact person name** (`team_contact_person`, required): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted regarding this order.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the email address of the person you would like to be contacted regarding this order.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the person you would like to be contacted regarding this order.
* **Jersey color** (`jersey_color`, required): Select the color of the jersey you would like to have.
* **Pants color** (`pants_color`, required): Select the color of the pants you would like to have.
* **Sock color preference** (`sock_color`, optional): Select the color of the socks you would like to have.
* **Do you have a team logo file?** (`team_logo`, required): Select if you have a logo file to attach or if you will provide it later.
* **Logo placement on jersey** (`logo_placement`, optional): Select where on the jersey you would like to have the logo placed.
* **Total number of uniforms needed** (`total_quantity`, required): Enter the total number of uniforms needed.
* **Size distribution** (`size_distribution`, required): Provide counts for each size in the extra small, small, medium, large, extra large, and XXL sizes.
* **Include player names on jerseys?** (`player_names`, optional): Select if you would like to have player names on the jerseys.
* **Include player numbers on jerseys?** (`player_numbers`, required): Select if you would like to have player numbers on the jerseys.
* **Sleeve type** (`sleeve_type`, required): Select the type of sleeves you would like to have.
* **Uniform style preference** (`uniform_style`, optional): Select the style of the uniform you would like to have.
* **Additional embroidery or design elements** (`additional_embroidery`, optional): Enter any additional details for any additional embroidery or design elements.
* **Requested delivery date** (`delivery_date`, required): Enter the date you would like to have the uniforms delivered.
* **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, required): Enter the address where the uniforms should be delivered.
* **Approximate budget per uniform** (`budget_per_uniform`, optional): Select the approximate budget per uniform.
* **Preferred payment method** (`payment_method`, optional): Select your preferred payment method.
* **I confirm this order information is accurate** (`confirmation`, required): Confirm that the provided information is accurate.
