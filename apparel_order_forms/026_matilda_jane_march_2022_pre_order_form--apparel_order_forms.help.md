# Matilda Jane March 2022 Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for pre-orders of the "Matilda Jane March 2022" collection to ensure we have all the necessary details for processing orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select your shipping option. If you have any special requests or comments, please include them in the relevant fields. You can save your progress and come back later if you need to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_info** (1, text, optional): Please enter any general information about your order, such as product color or style.
* **customer_info** (2, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about your order.
* **shipping_info** (3, select_one, optional): Select the shipping option that best suits your needs, which will be applied to all orders placed.
  * Select "Standard Shipping" for the standard shipping method.
  * Select "Express Shipping" for expedited shipping at an additional cost (please contact us for more information).
* **shipping_address** (4, text, optional): Enter your shipping address if you want to ship to a different address than your billing address.
* **payment** (5, text, optional): If you have any specific payment instructions or concerns, please enter them here.
* **order_comments** (6, text, required): Enter your order comments to ensure we understand your order correctly. This will be a required field.
* **order_comments_2** (7, text, required): Enter any additional comments about your order, such as product customization or gift wrapping.
* **order_comments_3** (8, text, required): Enter any other comments or special requests you may have about your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
