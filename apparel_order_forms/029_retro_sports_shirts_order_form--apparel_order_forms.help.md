# retro-sports-shirts-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order retro sports shirts. It is divided into several fields that ask for necessary information to process your order. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields on each page as prompted. You will have the option to move on to the next page once you have completed all required fields on the current page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: This is the field to enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   Required: No, but recommended for order fulfillment.
*   Type: Text

### Shipping Address
* **Shipping Address**: This is the field to enter your shipping address, where your order will be delivered.
*   Required: No, but recommended for order fulfillment.
*   Type: Text

### Shirt Size
* **Shirt Size**: Choose the size of the shirt you want to order. Options are small, large, and medium.
*   Required: Yes
*   Type: Select One

### Order Date
* **Order Date**: This is the date you would like to have your order fulfilled by.
*   Required: No
*   Type: Date

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order. Options are Credit card, PayPal, and Bank transfer.
*   Required: Yes
*   Type: Select Multiple

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
*   Required: No
*   Type: Email

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
*   Required: No
*   Type: Text

### Order Note
* **Order Note**: This is a field for any additional notes or messages about your order.
*   Required: No
*   Type: Note

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field, try to fill it out as accurately as possible.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact us directly.
