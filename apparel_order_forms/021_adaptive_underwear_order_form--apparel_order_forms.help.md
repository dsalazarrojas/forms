# Adaptive Underwear Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information, preferences, and order details for adaptive underwear orders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete all required fields**:
	* Ensure that you enter the correct information for "Customer name", "Contact email", "Shipping address", "Size", "Quantity", and "Billing method".
2. **Choose your preferences**:
	* Select the desired size and color for your order.
3. **Provide optional details**:
	* If you have specific instructions or requests for tailoring or packaging, enter them in the "Special instructions" field.
4. **Review and submit**:
	* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (id: 2):
	+ Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the order and for shipping purposes.
* **Contact email** (id: 3):
	+ Enter your email address where you would like to receive order confirmation and updates.
* **Shipping address** (id: 4):
	+ Enter your full shipping address, including street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Size** (id: 5):
	+ Select the size that fits you best, from XS to XL.
* **Color choice** (id: 6):
	+ Select the color you prefer for your order. If not specified, we will default to a neutral color.
* **Quantity** (id: 7):
	+ Enter the number of units you would like to order.
* **Adaptive features required** (id: 8):
	+ If you have specific requirements for fasteners or modifications, enter them here. This field is optional.
* **Billing method** (id: 9):
	+ Select the payment method you prefer for this order, such as credit card, invoice, or PayPal.
* **Special instructions for fulfillment** (id: 10):
	+ If you have any specific requests for customizations, modifications, or packaging, enter them here. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have provided accurate and detailed information to avoid delays or issues with order fulfillment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team for assistance.
