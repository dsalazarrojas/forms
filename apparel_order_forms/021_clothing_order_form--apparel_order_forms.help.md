# Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The clothing order form is designed to collect information about your order, including contact details, product information, shipping details, and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the contact information section with your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the "Item Selection" section, choose the items you want to order. You can list multiple items if desired.
3. In the "Order Details" section, fill out the product name, category, color, size, and quantity for each item.
4. In the "Shipping Information" section, provide your shipping address, city, state, zip code, and country.
5. In the "Billing Information" section, choose whether to use the same address as shipping or a different address, and fill out the billing address if different.
6. In the "Payment Method" section, choose your payment method.
7. In the "Special Instructions" section, add any special requests or messages.
8. Finally, agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your full name for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for contact and order updates.
* **-- Item Selection --**:
	+ This section is used to list multiple items you want to order.
* **Product Name or Style**: Name or code of the clothing item.
* **Product Category**: Category of the clothing item (e.g. Shirts and tops, pants and jeans, etc.).
* **Color Selection**: Desired color for this item.
* **Size**: Size of the item (e.g. XS, S, M, L, etc.).
* **Quantity**: How many of this item you want to order.
* **Item Total**: Calculated total price for the item.
* **-- Additional Item Orders --**:
	+ This section is used to order additional items if desired.
* **Additional Products**: List any additional items with details.
* **-- Shipping Information --**:
	+ This section is used to provide shipping details.
* **Shipping Address**: Your full shipping address.
* **City, State, Zip Code**: Location details for shipping.
* **Country**: Destination country for shipping.
* **Shipping Method**: Shipping method (e.g. standard shipping, express shipping, etc.).
* **-- Billing Information --**:
	+ This section is used to provide billing details.
* **Is Billing Address the Same as Shipping**: Whether to use the same address as shipping or a different address.
* **Billing Address if Different**: Full billing address if different from shipping.
* **Payment Method**: Your chosen payment method (e.g. credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **-- Special Instructions --**:
	+ This section is used to add any special requests or messages.
* **Special Instructions or Gift Message**: Customizations or delivery notes.
* **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and is intended to be a concise and practical explanation of how to complete the form.
