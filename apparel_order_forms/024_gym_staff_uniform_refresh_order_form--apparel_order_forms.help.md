# Gym Staff Uniform Refresh Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Staff Uniform Refresh Order Form is designed to help staff members place orders for uniform refreshments.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, select "Yes" or "No" for the uniform_reorder_requests field to indicate if you need a uniform refresh.
- Enter your staff name in the staff_name field.
- Choose your preferred uniform size from the options provided in the staff_size field.
- Enter the quantity of uniforms you need in the quantity_requested field.
- Select "delivery" or "pickup" in the delivery_type field to choose how you'd like to receive your uniforms.
- Enter the date you'd like to receive your uniforms on in the delivery_date field.
- Select a date you'd like to receive your uniforms on in the pickup_date field, if applicable.
- Provide any additional notes you'd like to share in the notes field.
- Enter your email address in the staff_email field.
- Enter your phone number in the staff_phone field.
- Finally, enter the name of your gym in the gym_name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **uniform_reorder_requests** (`uniform_reorder_requests`, select_multiple): Check "Yes" to indicate that you need a uniform refresh.
- **staff_name** (`staff_name`, text): Enter your name as a staff member.
- **staff_size** (`staff_size`, select_one): Choose your preferred uniform size (small, medium, large).
- **quantity_requested** (`quantity_requested`, number): Enter the number of uniforms you need.
- **delivery_type** (`delivery_type`, select_one): Select "delivery" to have your uniforms delivered or "pickup" to collect them in-person.
- **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, date): Enter the date you'd like to receive your uniforms on.
- **pickup_date** (`pickup_date`, date): Select a date you'd like to receive your uniforms on, if you've chosen "pickup".
- **notes** (`notes`, note): Provide any additional comments or information about your order.
- **staff_email** (`staff_email`, email): Enter your email address.
- **staff_phone** (`staff_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
- **gym_name** (`gym_name`, text): Enter the name of your gym.
