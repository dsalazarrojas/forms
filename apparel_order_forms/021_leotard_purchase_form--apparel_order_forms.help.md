# Leotard Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary for the purchase of leotards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your first and last name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your address in the "address" field.
4. Choose the desired style of leotard in the "style" field.
5. Choose the desired size(s) of leotard in the "size" field.
6. Enter the credit card number for payment in the "card_number" field.
7. If you would like to receive a confirmation email, select "Yes" in the "form_submission_url" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (first_name)** (`Text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email (email)** (`Email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address (address)** (`Text`, required): Enter your current address.
* **Style (leotard_style)** (`Select One`, required): Choose the style of leotard you wish to purchase.
* **Size (leotard_size)** (`Select Multiple`, required): Choose the size(s) of leotard you wish to purchase.
* **Card Number (card_number)** (`Text`, required): Enter the credit card number for payment.
* **Form Submission URL (form_submission_url)** (`Select One`, optional): If you wish to receive a confirmation email, select "Yes".
