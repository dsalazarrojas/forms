# custom_suit_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom suit pre-order form is designed to gather information for customers who want to pre-order custom suits, jackets, pants, or skirts. This form is used to collect essential details that will be used to process the order accurately and communicate with the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the "name" field.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** in the "email" field, making sure it's a valid email address.
3. Provide your **Customer Phone** number in the "phone" field.
4. Select the type of garment you want to pre-order:
	* **Suit**
	* **Jacket**
	* **Pants**
	* **Skirt**
5. Choose the order type:
	* **Made to Measure**
	* **Custom Made**
6. Choose the fabric for your garment:
	* **Wool**
	* **Cotton**
	* **Polyester**
	* **Silk**
7. Enter the desired **Size** for your garment.
8. Select the **Color** of your garment:
	* **Black**
	* **Blue**
	* **Gray**
	* **Green**
	* **Red**
9. Provide the **Price** for your pre-order.
10. Choose your preferred **Payment Method**:
	* **Credit Card**
	* **PayPal**
	* **Bank Transfer**
	* **Invoice**
11. Select the **Order Status**:
	* **Pending**
	* **In Progress**
	* **Completed**
12. Choose the **Payment Status**:
	* **Paid**
	* **Partially Paid**
	* **Not Paid**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communication about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Garment Type** (`garment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of garment you want to pre-order (e.g., suit, jacket, pants, or skirt).
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Choose between "Made to Measure" or "Custom Made" for your garment.
* **Fabric** (`fabric`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more fabrics for your garment (e.g., wool, cotton, polyester, or silk).
* **Size** (`size`, number, required): Enter the desired size for your garment.
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, required): Select the color for your garment.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price for your pre-order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method for this pre-order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your order (e.g., pending, in progress, completed).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Choose the payment status of your pre-order (e.g., paid, partially paid, not paid).

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you've selected all required fields to avoid errors during order processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your order, contact the customer support team.
