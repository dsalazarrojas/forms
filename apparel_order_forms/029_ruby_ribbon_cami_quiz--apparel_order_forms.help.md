# Ruby Ribbon Cami Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ruby Ribbon Cami Quiz form is used to collect information from customers to create a customized clothing item, such as a camisole. This information includes body measurements and style preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the form name.
* Enter your camisole size.
* Enter your height in inches.
* Enter your width (circumference around your natural waistline).
* Enter your bust measurement.
* Enter your underbust measurement.
* Enter your outter bust measurement (this might be an option for specific camisole types).
* Enter your hip measurement.
* Enter your waist measurement.
* Enter your camisole length.
* Select your preferred style from the dropdown menu.
* Add any additional notes or comments.
* Enter your contact email address (this will be used for communication about your order).
* Enter your phone number (this will be used for communication about your order).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cami Quiz** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter a unique name for your camisole order.
* **Cami Size** (`size`, number, optional): Enter your camisole size.
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Enter your height in inches.
* **Bust** (`bust`, number, optional): Enter your bust measurement.
* **Under Bust** (`under_bust`, number, optional): Enter your underbust measurement.
* **Outter Bust** (`outter_bust`, number, optional): Enter your outter bust measurement. This might be an option for specific camisole types.
* **Hip** (`hip`, number, optional): Enter your hip measurement.
* **Waist** (`waist`, number, optional): Enter your waist measurement.
* **Length** (`length`, number, optional): Enter your camisole length.
* **Style** (`style`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred camisole style from the dropdown menu.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address for communication about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication about your order.

## Tips
* Double-check your measurements to ensure accuracy.
* Select the correct style for your preferences.
* Provide any additional comments or notes to help with the customization process.
* Make sure to enter your correct contact information for communication.
