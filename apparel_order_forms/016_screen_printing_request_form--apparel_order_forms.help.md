# Screen Printing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for screen printing requests, including order details, artwork, quote requests, production planning, and customer information. Please provide accurate and relevant details for a smooth process.

## How to Complete This Form
1. Scroll through the form and ensure all required fields are filled out.
2. Enter information in the designated fields according to your needs.
3. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (`order_details`)**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Artwork Info (`artwork_info`)**: Provide details about the artwork for your order.
* **Quote Requests (`quote_requests`)**: Specify if you require a quote for your order.
* **Quote Requests Quote (`quote_requests_quote`)**: Select 'Yes' if you want a quote, 'No' otherwise.
* **Quote Requests Note (`quote_requests_note`)**: Enter any additional information related to your quote request.
* **Artwork (`artwork`)**: Specify if you have artwork available for your order.
* **Deadline (`deadline`)**: Enter the deadline for your order in date format.
* **Deadline Time (`deadline_time`)**: Enter the specific time for your deadline.
* **Production Planning (`production_planning`)**: Enter any special production planning information.
* **Production Planning Note (`production_planning_note`)**: Enter any additional production planning notes.
* **Order Notes (`order_notes`)**: Enter any notes about your order.
* **Order Notes Note (`order_notes_note`)**: Enter any additional information related to your order notes.
* **Email (`email`)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (`phone`)**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Artwork 1 (`order_details_art`)**: Enter the first item of your order.
* **Artwork 2 (`order_details_art2`)**: Enter the second item of your order.
* **Order Details Deadline (`order_details_deadline`)**: Enter the deadline for your order in time format.
* **Order Details Deadline 2 (`order_details_deadline_2`)**: Enter the date of your order deadline.
* **Order Details Note (`order_details_note`)**: Enter any additional information related to your order.
* **Order Details Note 2 (`order_details_note_2`)**: Enter any additional information related to your order.
* **Customer Information (`customer_info`)**: Enter any relevant customer information.
* **Customer Info Note (`customer_info_note`)**: Enter any additional information related to your customer.
* **Shipping (`shipping`)**: Enter any shipping information.
* **Shipping Note (`shipping_note`)**: Enter any additional information related to your shipping.
* **Shipping Info (`shipping_info`)**: Enter any additional shipping information.
