# state_football_championship_t_shirt_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users create a custom state football championship t-shirt that represents their team spirit. Users can select from various options such as T-Shirt size, color, and text to create a unique design that shows off their team's name, number, and style.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Information section with your name, email, and any other contact information you would like to provide.
2. In the Custom Shirt Info section, enter the text you would like to appear on your t-shirt, such as your team's name or number.
3. Select the T-Shirt Size and T-Shirt Color that you prefer.
4. Enter the T-Shirt Number you would like to feature on your t-shirt.
5. Choose the quantity of t-shirts you would like to order.
6. Select the payment method you would like to use for your order.
7. Finally, enter any additional order details in the Order Details section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and any other contact information you would like to provide.
* **Custom Shirt Info** (`custom_shirt_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the text you would like to appear on your t-shirt, such as your team's name or number.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of the T-shirt you prefer.
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ X-Large
	+ XX-Large
* **T-Shirt Color** (`t_shirt_color`, `select_one`, optional): Select the color of the T-shirt you prefer.
	+ Blue
	+ Red
	+ Green
	+ Yellow
	+ White
* **T-Shirt Number** (`t_shirt_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the number you would like to feature on your t-shirt.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the payment method you would like to use for your order.
	+ Cash
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Apple Pay
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional details or instructions for your order.
