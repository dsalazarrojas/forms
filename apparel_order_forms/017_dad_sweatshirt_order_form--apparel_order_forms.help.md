# dad_sweatshirt_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dad_sweatshirt_order_form is designed to collect information for an order of sweatshirts. This form is intended to gather details such as the customer's name, phone number, email address, order date, shirt size, and color preference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Select a date for your order in the **Order Date** field.
5. Choose your preferred **Shirt Size** from the available options.
6. Select your preferred **Color Choice** by choosing one of the options: "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should be printed on the sweatshirt.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any potential follow-up communications.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for any further communications or updates.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date when you would like to place your order.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, text, optional): Choose your preferred shirt size.
* **Color Choice** (`color_choice`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you want the sweatshirt to be colored, or "No" if you want it to be plain white.
