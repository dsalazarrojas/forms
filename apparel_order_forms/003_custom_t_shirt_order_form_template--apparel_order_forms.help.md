# Custom T Shirt Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom T Shirt Order Form Template is designed to collect essential information from customers for custom T-shirt orders.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with the customer's information.
- Select the desired color and style of the T-shirt from the options provided.
- Choose the design type for the T-shirt from the options provided.
- Enter the customer's email address and phone number.
- Provide shipping details for the order, including address, city, state, zip code, and country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant information.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **T-Shirt Details** (`tshirt_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the order.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter the payment method and details.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of the order.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's shipping address.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes or instructions for the order.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Custom Fields** (`custom_fields`, text, required: false): Enter any custom fields or additional information required for the order.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **T-Shirt Color** (`custom_field_1`, select_one, required: true): Select the desired color for the T-shirt.
  - This field is required to ensure that the customer orders a T-shirt with the correct color.
- **T-Shirt Size** (`custom_field_2`, select_one, required: true): Select the desired size for the T-shirt.
  - This field is required to ensure that the customer orders a T-shirt that fits correctly.
- **T-Shirt Style** (`custom_field_3`, select_one, required: true): Select the desired style for the T-shirt.
  - This field is required to ensure that the customer orders a T-shirt of the correct style.
- **T-Shirt Design** (`tshirt_design`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the desired design for the T-shirt.
  - This field is required to ensure that the customer orders a T-shirt with the correct design.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: true): Enter the customer's email address.
  - This field is required to ensure that the customer can be contacted regarding the order.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: true): Enter the customer's phone number.
  - This field is required to ensure that the customer can be contacted regarding the order.
- **Shipper Name** (`shipping_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the shipper.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Shipper Company** (`shipping_company`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the shipper's company.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Address Line 1** (`shipping_address_1`, text, required: false): Enter the first line of the shipping address.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Address Line 2** (`shipping_address_2`, text, required: false): Enter the second line of the shipping address.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **City** (`shipping_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the shipping address.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **State/Region** (`shipping_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state or region of the shipping address.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Zip/Postal Code** (`shipping_postal_code`, text, required: false): Enter the zip or postal code of the shipping address.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required: false): Enter the country of the shipping address.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Shipper Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the shipper.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
- **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or instructions for the order.
  - This field is not required but is recommended for business use.
