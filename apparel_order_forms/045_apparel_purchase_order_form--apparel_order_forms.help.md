# Apparel Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apparel Purchase Order Form is used to create and submit purchase orders for apparel items. It captures the necessary information from the supplier, logistics, and item details to process the order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Purchase Order (PO) Number** in the first field.
2. Enter the **Supplier Name** in the second field.
3. Enter the **Supplier Email** in the third field.
4. Enter the **Billing Address** in the fourth field.
5. Select the **Shipping Method** from the options provided (Sea Freight, Air Freight, Ground Transport, or Express Courier).
6. Enter the **Order Date** in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
7. Enter the **Item Description** in the fifth field.
8. Enter the **Total Quantity** of the item being ordered.
9. Enter the **Unit Price** of the item.
10. If applicable, enter the **Applicable Tax Rate** (in %).
11. The **Total Amount Due** will be calculated automatically based on the unit price and quantity.
12. Finally, enter your **Authorized Signature Name**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase Order (PO) Number**: A unique identifier for the purchase order. Please enter a valid PO number.
* **-- Supplier Information --**: This section contains information about the supplier.
*   **Supplier Name**: The name of the supplier.
*   **Supplier Email**: The email address of the supplier.
*   **-- Logistics and Billing --**: This section contains information about the order logistics and billing.
*   **Billing Address**: The address where the supplier will be billed.
*   **Shipping Address**: The address where the item will be shipped.
*   **Shipping Method**: The method used to transport the item (Sea Freight, Air Freight, Ground Transport, or Express Courier).
*   **Order Date**: The date when the order is expected to be completed.
*   **-- Itemized Order --**: This section contains information about the items being ordered.
*   **Item Description**: A brief description of the item being ordered.
*   **Total Quantity**: The total number of items being ordered.
*   **Unit Price**: The price of each item.
*   **Applicable Tax Rate (%)**: The tax rate applicable to the order (if applicable).
*   **Total Amount Due**: The calculated total amount due, including tax.
*   **Authorized By (Signature Name)**: The name of the person authorizing the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and valid information in all fields.
* Double-check the calculation of the **Total Amount Due**.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
