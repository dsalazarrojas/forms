# Custom Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The custom clothing order form is designed to collect all necessary information for a custom clothing order, ensuring accuracy and efficiency in the ordering process.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the required and optional details.
2. Choose your preferred shirt design, color, sleeve length, collar type, and sleeve style.
3. Select your payment method and payment status.
4. Input your shipping information, including your address and email.
5. Enter any additional order notes or custom order number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (1)**: Enter your name, email, phone number, street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Order Details (2)**: Briefly describe your order, including any specific requests.
* **Shirt Design (3)**: Select the design of your custom clothing item from the available options: Red, Blue, or Green.
* **Color Selection (4)**: Choose the color of your clothing item from the available options: Red, Blue, or Green.
* **Size Selection (5)**: Select the size of your clothing item from the available options: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Sleeve Length (6)**: Choose the sleeve length of your clothing item from the available options: Short, Long, or Extra Long.
* **Collar Type (7)**: Select the collar type of your clothing item from the available options: Round, V-Neck, or Crew.
* **Sleeve Style (8)**: Choose the sleeve style of your clothing item from the available options: Short, Long, or None.
* **Hem Type (9)**: Select the hem type of your clothing item from the available options: None, Folded, or Pleated.
* **Order Notes (10)**: Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Shipping Information (11)**: Enter your shipping address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (13)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Information (14)**: This field is not used as it seems to be a duplicate of Shipping Information (11).
* **Order Date (15)**: Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Order Time (16)**: Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
* **Notes (17)**: Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Payment Method (18)**: Choose your preferred payment method from the available options: Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Payment Status (19)**: Select the payment status of your order from the available options: Pending, Paid, or Declined.
* **Order Number (20)**: Enter your order number.
* **Custom Order Number (21)**: Enter any custom order number, if applicable.

## Tips

- Double-check your order details for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Use a valid email address and phone number.
- Choose the correct shipping information to ensure timely and efficient delivery.
- Select the payment method and payment status accordingly to track your order's progress.
