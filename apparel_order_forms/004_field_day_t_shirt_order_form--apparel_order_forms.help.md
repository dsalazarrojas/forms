# Field Day T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for Field Day T Shirt orders, which is a type of apparel order. The form is intended for customers to provide their personal and order details, including customer info, order info, payment info, shipping info, and order summary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the **Customer Info** field with your name and contact details.
2. Then, provide the **Order Info** field with the type and quantity of the T Shirt(s) you wish to order.
3. Next, enter your **Payment Info** details, such as the payment method and amount.
4. Please provide your **Shipping Info**, including the address and any special instructions.
5. Review the **Order Summary** field to confirm your order details.
6. Finally, read the **Confirmation** field to ensure you have accurately filled out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Info (1)** (Text, Optional)
Customer Info field is where you can enter your name and contact details, such as your email and phone number.

### 2. **Order Info (2)** (Text, Optional)
Order Info field is where you can provide the type and quantity of the T Shirt(s) you wish to order. Be specific about the style, color, and size you prefer.

### 3. **Payment Info (3)** (Text, Optional)
Payment Info field is where you can enter your payment method and amount. Please ensure you provide accurate payment details to avoid any issues with your order.

### 4. **Shipping Info (4)** (Text, Optional)
Shipping Info field is where you can provide your address and any special instructions for delivery. Please double-check your address for accurate shipping.

### 5. **Order Summary (5)** (Text, Optional)
Order Summary field is where you can review and confirm your order details, including customer info, order info, payment info, and shipping info.

### 6. **Confirmation (6)** (Text, Optional)
Confirmation field is where you can read through your order details to ensure you have accurately filled out the form. Please review carefully before submitting.

## Tips
- Be thorough when filling out the form to avoid missing any necessary information.
- Double-check your order and payment details for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
