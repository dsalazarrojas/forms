# Apparel Size And Quantity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apparel Size And Quantity Form is used to collect order-specific details for apparel items with different sizes and quantities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Order Reference Number to associate the form with the specific order.
2. Fill in the Client or Company Name and Contact Email to ensure the order recipient can be contacted.
3. Enter the Item Name / Description to identify the type of apparel being ordered.
4. Enter the date of submission to track when the order was received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Reference Number (order_ref)**: Enter a unique reference number for the order, which will help us track and manage the order.
* **Client or Company Name (client_full_name)**: Enter the name of the client or company placing the order.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter the email address of the person or company placing the order.
* **Item Name / Description (garment_item_name)**: Enter a brief description of the item ordered, such as "Staff Uniform T-Shirt".
* **-- Size and Quantity Breakdown -- (size_breakdown_header)**: This field serves as a separator for the size and quantity section.
* **Size Small Quantity (qty_small)**: Enter the quantity of small sizes ordered.
* **Size Medium Quantity (qty_medium)**: Enter the quantity of medium sizes ordered.
* **Size Large Quantity (qty_large)**: Enter the quantity of large sizes ordered.
* **Size XL Quantity (qty_xl)**: Enter the quantity of XL sizes ordered.
* **Size XXL Quantity (qty_xxl)**: Enter the quantity of XXL sizes ordered.
* **Total Number of Items (Manual Confirmation) (total_quantity_confirm)**: Confirm the total number of items ordered by manually entering the sum of all sizes.
* **Special Size Requirements or Custom Fits (special_size_notes)**: Enter any special requirements or custom fits for the ordered items, such as "Tall sizes" or "Specific measurements".
