# Sports T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teams and players order sports team shirts. Please provide your team name, order type, date and time of order, team size, shirt type, color, quantity, price, and any additional order notes or comments. You will also need to provide contact information for your team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of order you are making (Team, Player, or Fan).
2. Enter your team name and any additional information about your team.
3. Choose the type of shirt you want (Long Sleeve, Short Sleeve, or Other).
4. Select the color of the shirt.
5. Specify the quantity of shirts you want to order.
6. Enter the price per shirt.
7. Provide any additional order notes or comments.
8. Enter your team's contact information (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (text, required: false):
	+ This field is optional, but it's the name of your team that will be ordering the shirts.
* **Order Type** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose whether you are ordering for a Team, Player, or Fan.
* **Order Date** (date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you would like to place the order.
* **Order Time** (time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time you would like to place the order.
* **Team Size** (number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of team members that will be wearing the shirts.
* **Shirt Type** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of shirt you want to order (Long Sleeve, Short Sleeve, or Other).
* **Color** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the color of the shirt.
* **Quantity** (number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of shirts you want to order.
* **Price** (number, required: false):
	+ Enter the price per shirt.
* **Order Notes** (text, required: false):
	+ Add any additional information about your order.
* **Order Comments** (text, required: false):
	+ Add any additional comments or questions you have about your order.
* **Team Email** (email, required: false):
	+ Enter the email address of your team's contact person.
* **Team Phone** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter the phone number of your team's contact person.
* **Team Address** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter the address of your team's contact person.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields and ensure that your order is accurate to avoid any errors. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
