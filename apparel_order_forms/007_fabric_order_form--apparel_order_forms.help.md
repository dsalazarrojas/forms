# fabric_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fabric_order_form is used to gather information from customers when they place an order for fabric or materials. This form helps to streamline the ordering process by collecting essential details about the order, product, and customer's shipping address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the **Order Details** field. This is the main section of the form where you can provide a brief description of your order.
2. Provide product details in the **Product Details** field. This is an optional field where you can specify the product you are ordering, if applicable.
3. Enter your shipping address in the **Shipping Address** field. This is necessary to ensure that your order is delivered to the correct location.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the **Comments** field. This is an optional field where you can provide any extra information about your order.
5. Enter your customer phone number in the **Customer Phone** field. This is an optional field, but it can be helpful for our customer service team to contact you in case of any issues.
6. If you have any specific instructions or comments about your order, you can enter them in the **Order Comments** field. This is an optional field where you can provide any extra information about your order.
