# Wedding Address Book Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Address Book Form is a tool designed to collect and store the contact information of your wedding guests. This form helps you to gather essential details such as their name, email, phone number, and address to keep in touch and communicate with them efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's **First Name** in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter the guest's **Last Name** in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter the guest's **Email** in the `Email` field (format: `username@example.com`).
4. Enter the guest's **Phone** in the `Phone` field (e.g., 123-456-7890).
5. Enter the guest's **Street Address** in the `Street` field.
6. Enter the guest's **City** in the `City` field.
7. Enter the guest's **Province or State** in the `Province` field.
8. Enter the guest's **Postal Code** in the `Postal Code` field (format: `A1A 1A1`).
9. Enter the guest's **Country** in the `Country` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (text, required)
* Label: First Name
* Explanation: Enter the guest's first name, as this will be used to identify them.

### Last Name (text, optional)
* Label: Last Name
* Explanation: Enter the guest's last name, if applicable (if they use a surname).

### Email (email, required)
* Label: Email
* Explanation: Enter the guest's email address (e.g., `jane.doe@example.com`).

### Phone (text, required)
* Label: Phone
* Explanation: Enter the guest's phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).

### Street (text, optional)
* Label: Street
* Explanation: Enter the guest's street address.

### City (text, optional)
* Label: City
* Explanation: Enter the guest's city.

### Province or State (text, optional)
* Label: Province
* Explanation: Enter the guest's province or state.

### Postal Code (text, required)
* Label: Postal Code
* Explanation: Enter the guest's postal code (format: `A1A 1A1`).

### Country (text, optional)
* Label: Country
* Explanation: Enter the guest's country.
