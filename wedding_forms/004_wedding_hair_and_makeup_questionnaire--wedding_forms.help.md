# Wedding Hair And Makeup Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about wedding hair and makeup services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of hair services you would like to offer on your wedding day.
2. Choose the type of makeup services you would like to offer.
3. Enter the date of your wedding.
4. Enter the time of your wedding.
5. Enter the venue where your wedding will take place.
6. Enter the name of the hair stylist who will be providing the services.
7. Enter the name of the makeup artist who will be providing the services.
8. Enter your name and contact information for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Hair and Makeup Questionnaire** (`wedding_hair_and_makeup_questionnaire`, text, optional): This is the title of the form that you are currently filling out.
* **hair_services** (`hair_services`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options to select the type of hair services you would like to offer:
	+ Blowout
	+ Updo
	+ Color correction
	+ Makeup
	+ Hair straightening
* **makeup_services** (`makeup_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options to select the type of makeup services you would like to offer:
	+ Bridal hair
	+ Bridal makeup
	+ Groom
* **Wedding Day** (`wedding_day`, date, required): Enter the date of your wedding.
* **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your wedding.
* **Wedding Venue** (`wedding_venue`, text, required): Enter the name of the wedding venue where your wedding will take place.
* **Hair Stylist** (`hair_stylist`, text, required): Enter the name of the hair stylist who will be providing the services.
* **Makeup Artist** (`makeup_artist`, text, required): Enter the name of the makeup artist who will be providing the services.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name so that we can contact you to confirm your booking.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you to confirm your booking.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to confirm your booking.
