# Wedding Ceremony Script Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help couples create a personalized wedding ceremony script for their special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your preferences for each question.
3. Use the provided fields to enter any additional information or special requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome Page** (`welcome_page`, none, required: false): This page is the first page of your ceremony script. You can add any welcome message or welcome rituals you'd like to start the ceremony.
* **Couple Information** (`couple_info_page`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the people who will be participating in the ceremony. This can include the bride, groom, officiant, and other special guests.
* **Wedding Officiant Info** (`wedding_officiant_info_page`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of officiant you'd like to have at your wedding. This can be a pastor, rabbi, justice of the peace, or minister.
* **Ceremony Info** (`ceremony_info_page`, none, required: false): This page is for any additional information about your ceremony, such as the time, date, or location.
* **Vows** (`vows_page`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of vows you'd like to exchange during your ceremony. This can be traditional or non-traditional vows.
* **Ring Exchanging** (`ring_exchanging_page`, select_one, required: false): Select how you'd like to exchange rings during your ceremony. This can be right hand, left hand, same hand, or other.
* **Unity Covenant** (`unity_covenant_page`, none, required: false): This page is for any special unity covenants or rituals you'd like to include in your ceremony.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests_page`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose any special requests you'd like to include in your ceremony, such as a ring toss or sparkler send-off.
* **Ceremony Script** (`ceremony_script_page`, none, required: false): This page is for any additional information or notes you'd like to include in your ceremony script.
* **Closing** (`closing_page`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of closing you'd like to have at the end of your ceremony. This can be traditional or non-traditional closing.
* **Final Page** (`final_page`, none, required: false): This page is the final page of your ceremony script. You can add any final remarks or comments here.
* **Review Page** (`review_page`, select_multiple, required: false): Review your ceremony script before finalizing it. You can choose to approve or reject it here.

Note: The required field indicates that the field is not required to be filled out, but it's always a good idea to provide as much information as possible to create a complete and personalized ceremony script.
