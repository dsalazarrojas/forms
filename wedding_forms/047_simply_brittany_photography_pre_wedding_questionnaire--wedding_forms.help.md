# simply_brittany_photography_pre_wedding_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-wedding questionnaire designed for Simply Brittany Photography to gather essential information about your wedding event. This information will help the photographer prepare for your special day and ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for you and your guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Enter your wedding details, such as the bride and groom's names, location, date and time of the wedding, and ceremony type.
3. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. If you have any special requests or notes for the photographer, use the "Note to Photographer" and "Special Requests" fields.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Wedding Details (Page 1)
* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your wedding's details, such as the location, date, and time of the wedding.
* **Bride** (`bride`, text, required/optional): Enter the bride's name.
* **Groom** (`groom`, text, required/optional): Enter the groom's name.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the wedding.
* **Date of Wedding** (`date_of_wedding`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Time of Wedding** (`time_of_wedding`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the wedding.
* **Ceremony Type** (`ceremony_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of ceremony.

### Contact Information (Page 2)
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, text, required/optional): Enter how you know the contact person.

### Additional Information (Page 3)
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address where the wedding will take place.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests attending the wedding.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, required/optional): Enter any special requests or notes for the photographer.
* **Note to Photographer** (`note_to_photographer`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes or messages you would like to pass to the photographer.

### Tips

* Make sure to double-check your contact information to ensure it is accurate.
* If you have any special requests or questions for the photographer, please include them in the "Note to Photographer" field.
* This form will be used to prepare for your special day, so please provide as much detail as possible.
