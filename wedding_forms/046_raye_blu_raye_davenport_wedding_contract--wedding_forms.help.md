# raye_blu_raye_davenport_wedding_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for a wedding contract with Raye Blu Raye Davenport. It is designed to gather essential details about the client's preferences and schedule for the wedding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the wedding date in the "Wedding Date" field. This is the date you want to schedule the wedding.
3. Choose the start time for the wedding ceremony in the "Start Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the client who is getting married.
  *This is the first step in gathering information about the client.*

- **Wedding Date** (`Wedding Date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for the wedding. This is the date when the wedding ceremony will take place.
  *This field ensures that you can plan for the correct date.*

- **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, required/optional): Choose the start time for the wedding ceremony. This will determine when the wedding proceedings will begin.
  *This is essential for planning the timing of the ceremony.*
