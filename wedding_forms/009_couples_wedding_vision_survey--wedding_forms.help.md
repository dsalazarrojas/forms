# Couples' Wedding Vision Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help couples plan their wedding ceremony and reception by gathering their ideas and preferences for various aspects of their special day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your name and your partner's name as per your preference.
2. Select a date for your wedding.
3. Indicate your preferences for the venue, whether it's a yes or no for the given options. This will help us understand your priorities.
4. Choose your preferred options for food, music, dance, decorations, photography, and other services.
5. Enter the address of your chosen venue.
6. If you have any other preferences or comments, please type them in the "Notes" field.
7. Provide your email and phone number for us to reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Couples' Wedding Vision Survey**
	+ This is the title of the form, and you can enter any notes or comments about your wedding ceremony and reception.
* **Name of Couples** 
	+ Please enter the names of yourselves and your partner(s).
* **Date of Wedding**
	+ Enter the date you've chosen for your special day.
* **Venue Preferences**
	+ Please indicate your preferences for the venue by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Budget**
	+ If you're not sure about the budget, please don't worry about it for now, and we'll discuss it later.
* **Priority of Food Service**
	+ Select your preferred level of priority for food services, such as "Yes", "No", or "Both".
* **Priority of Music**
	+ Indicate your priorities for music, whether it's "Yes", "No", or "Both".
* **Priority of Dance**
	+ Similarly, select your preferences for dance services.
* **Priority of Decor**
	+ Choose your preferences for decorations.
* **Priority of Photography**
	+ Select your priorities for photography.
* **Priority of Coffee Service**
	+ Indicate your preferences for coffee services.
* **Priority of Transportation**
	+ Choose your priorities for transportation.
* **Priority of Accommodation**
	+ Select your priorities for accommodation.
* **Notes**
	+ If you have any other comments or preferences, please enter them here.
* **Email**
	+ Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone**
	+ Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Venue Address**
	+ Enter the address of your chosen venue.
* **Venue Type**
	+ Please enter the type of venue you've chosen (e.g., hotel, park, etc.).
* **Priority of Food**
	+ Select your priorities for food services.
* **Priority of Service**
	+ Indicate your priorities for services, such as event planning, etc.
* **Priority of Activities**
	+ Choose your priorities for activities, such as games, etc.
* **Vendor Venue**
	+ If you have a specific vendor in mind, please enter their name.
* **Venue**
	+ Enter the name of the venue you've chosen.
* **Vendor**
	+ Enter the name of your wedding vendor.
* **Contact Person**
	+ Please enter the name of the person you'd like us to contact in case we need more information.
