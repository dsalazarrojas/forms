# Facebooth Quotation 1 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a quotation for a photobooth service at an event. It collects information about the event and helps event organizers create a quotation that suits their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you are organizing.
2. Provide a brief description of the event.
3. Enter your email address and phone number.
4. Choose the photobooth duration that suits your event (2, 3, or 4 hours).
5. Select the price point for the photobooth service.
6. Select the tool you would like to be assigned to the photobooth service.
7. Enter the event date and time.
8. Provide the event location.
9. Add any additional notes about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you are organizing, such as Photobooth for Wedding or Photobooth for Birthday Party.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Photo Booth Choice**: Choose the duration of the photobooth service you would like to book, such as 2 hours, 3 hours, or 4 hours.
* **Price**: Enter the price point for the photobooth service.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool you would like to be assigned to the photobooth service.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information about your event to ensure a smooth booking process.
* If you have any questions or need help with filling out this form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
