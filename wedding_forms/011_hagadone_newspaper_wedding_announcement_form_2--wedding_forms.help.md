# hagadone-newspaper-wedding-announcement-form-2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a wedding for a public announcement. Fill out the form to provide details about the wedding, including the date, location, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the required fields**: Make sure to fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the labels. These fields are:
	* `wedding_date` (Wedding Date)
	* `bride_and_groom` (Bride and Groom)
	* `weddin_address` (Weddin Address)
2. **Add optional fields**: You can add information to the optional fields if you'd like to provide more details about the wedding. These fields include:
	* `Wedding Planner Business` (Wedding Planner Business)
	* `weddington_time` (Weddington Time)
	* `weddin_guests` (Weddin Guests)
	* `wedding_vender` (Wedding Vender)
	* `weddin_website` (Weddin Website)
	* `wedin_facebook` (Wedin Facebook)
	* `wedin_telegram` (Wedin Telegram)
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled in all the fields, submit the form to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Planner Business** (`wedding_planner_business`, text, optional): Enter the name of the wedding planner or event organizer handling your wedding.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the wedding in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Weddington Time** (`weddington_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the wedding in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Weddin Guests** (`weddin_guests`, text, optional): Enter the number of guests invited to the wedding.
* **Bride and Groom** (`bride_and_groom`, text, required): Enter the names of the bride and groom.
* **Wedding Vender** (`wedding_vender`, text, optional): Enter the name of the wedding vender or vendor that provided services for the wedding.
* **Weddin Address** (`weddin_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the wedding venue.
* **Weddin Website** (`weddin_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the wedding venue or planner.
* **Wedin Facebook** (`wedin_facebook`, text, optional): Enter the Facebook page of the wedding venue or planner.
* **Wedin Telegram** (`wedin_telegram`, text, optional): Enter the Telegram channel of the wedding venue or planner.
