# Wedding Dvd Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from couples planning a wedding to create a personalized video production that captures their special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
2. Provide details about your wedding in the "Wedding Details" field.
3. Specify the video services you would like us to provide in the "Video Services" field.
4. If you require additional services, please mention them in the "Additional Services" field.
5. Enter your billing information in the "Billing" field.
6. Finally, sign the "Form Signature" field to confirm your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Please enter your name and contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, text, required): Describe your wedding ceremony, location, and other relevant details.
* **Video Services** (`video_services`, text, required): Select the video services you require (e.g. pre-wedding, post-wedding, highlight reel, etc.).
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, text, optional): If you need any additional services such as drone footage or aerial shots, mention them here.
* **Billing** (`billing`, text, required): Enter your billing information for payment purposes.
* **Form Signature** (`form_signature`, text, required): Sign and date this form to confirm your information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure we can provide the best possible service for your wedding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will get in touch with you to confirm your information and provide further instructions once we receive your form submission.
