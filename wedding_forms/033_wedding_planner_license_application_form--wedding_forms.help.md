# wedding_planner_license_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The wedding planner license application form is used to gather essential information from wedding planners and their businesses for the purpose of applying for a wedding planner license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the contact information section with your business details, including your business name, address, telephone number, email, and website (if applicable).
2. Indicate the services you offer as a wedding planner.
3. Specify the number of years your business has been in operation.
4. Answer whether your business has a license and what type it is.
5. Provide your business license number (if applicable).
6. Confirm if your business has insurance and who provides it.
7. Specify the wedding license number you are applying for.
8. Enter the fee associated with the wedding license application.
9. Indicate if a fee is required for the application.
10. Choose the payment method for the application fee (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Planner Business Name** (`wedding_planner_business_name`): Enter your business name as it is registered.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`): Enter your business address where you operate from.
* **Business Telephone** (`business_telephone`): Enter your business telephone number.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`): Enter your business email address.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`): Enter your business website (if applicable).
* **Wedding Planning Services Offered** (`wedding_planning_services_offered`): List the wedding planning services your business offers.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Business License Type** (`business_license_type`): Indicate if your business has a license, and if so, what type of license it is.
* **Business License Number** (`business_license_number`): Enter your business license number (if applicable).
* **Business Insurance** (`business_insurance`): Confirm if your business has insurance.
* **Business Insurance Provider** (`business_insurance_provider`): If your business has insurance, enter who provides it.
* **Wedding License Number** (`wedding_license_number`): Enter the license number you are applying for.
* **Wedding License Fee** (`wedding_license_fee`): Enter the fee associated with the wedding license application.
* **Application Fee** (`application_fee`): Indicate if a fee is required for the application.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose the payment method for the application fee (if applicable).

## Tips
- Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
