# Makeup, Lashes And Brow Services COVID 19 Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for both the client and the employee for makeup, lashes, and brow services during the COVID-19 pandemic. It covers liability waivers, services rendered, and essential contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the **Wedding Forms** page, select **COVID 19 Liability Waiver** from the list of available forms.
2. On the **COVID 19 Liability Waiver** page, read and agree to the terms of the liability waiver.
3. On the **Services Rendered** page, select the services you would like to receive during your appointment (Lash, Makeup, or Brow services).
4. On the **Lash Services**, **Makeup Services**, and **Brow Services** pages, select one or more services that you would like to receive.
5. On the **Signature** page, sign your name as proof of agreement.
6. On the **Date** and **Time** pages, fill in your signature date and time.
7. On the **Client Details** page, fill in your personal contact information (name, email, phone number, and address).
8. On the **Employee Details** page, fill in your personal contact information (name, ID number, phone number, and email).
9. On the **Salon Details** page, fill in the salon's contact information (name and address).
10. On the **Notes** page, add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Forms**: Select the type of wedding form required for your appointment.
* **COVID 19 Liability Waiver**: Agree to the terms of the liability waiver, which includes accepting potential risks and consequences.
* **Services Rendered**: Select one or more services that you would like to receive during your appointment (Lash, Makeup, or Brow services).
* **Lash Services**: Select one or more Lash services from the options (Lash Extensions, Lash Perm, or Lash Tent).
* **Makeup Services**: Select one or more Makeup services from the options (Microblading, Microshading, or Makeup).
* **Brow Services**: Select one or more Brow services from the options (Microblading, Microshading, Brow Tent, or Arch).
* **Signature**: Sign your name as proof of agreement.
* **Date**: Fill in the date your form was signed.
* **Time**: Fill in the time your form was signed.
* **Client Name**: Fill in your name.
* **Client Email**: Fill in your email address.
* **Client Phone**: Fill in your phone number.
* **Salon Name**: Fill in the salon's name.
* **Salon Address**: Fill in the salon's address.
* **Employee Name**: Fill in the employee's name.
* **Employee ID**: Fill in the employee's ID number.
* **Employee Phone**: Fill in the employee's phone number.
* **Employee Email**: Fill in the employee's email address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes.
