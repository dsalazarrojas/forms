# Wedding Photography Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Photography Schedule form is designed to help wedding planners and photographers coordinate and plan the photography schedule for a wedding. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your form details in the first field.
2. Provide your couples' details in the second field.
3. Choose your desired photographer packages in the third field.
4. List any special shots you would like to have during the wedding in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Details**: A field to enter any additional form details that are necessary for the planner or photographer to know. 
* **Couples Details**: A field to provide any necessary details about the couple getting married. 
* **Photographer Packages**: A field to select which photographer packages you would like to have during the wedding. Please select all desired packages. 
* **Special Shots**: A field to list any special photography shots you would like to have during the wedding.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in each field to ensure accurate and effective coordination.
* If you have any questions or concerns regarding the form, please do not hesitate to reach out.
