# Wedding Hair & Makeup Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Hair & Makeup Service Order Form is used to collect information from brides-to-be for their wedding hair and makeup services. This form helps the wedding planning team to confirm the bride's wedding details, contact information, hair and makeup services required, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your name in the "Brides Name" field.
2.  Enter your contact information (email and phone number) in the "Contact Info" field.
3.  Select the date and time of your wedding in the "Wedding Date" and "Wedding Time" fields.
4.  Indicate whether you require hair services (e.g., updo, curls, etc.) in the "Hair Services" field.
5.  Indicate whether you require makeup services (e.g., foundation, eyeliner, etc.) in the "Makeup Services" field.
6.  In the "Notes" field, provide any additional information or special requests you may have.
7.  Select how the services will be assigned (e.g., via chat, email, etc.) in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Brides Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the bride.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter your email address and phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
*   **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your wedding.
*   **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of your wedding.
*   **Hair Services** (`hair_services`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you require hair services such as updo, curls, etc.
*   **Makeup Services** (`makeup_services`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you require makeup services such as foundation, eyeliner, etc.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information or special requests you may have.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be assigned services (e.g., via chat, email, etc.).
