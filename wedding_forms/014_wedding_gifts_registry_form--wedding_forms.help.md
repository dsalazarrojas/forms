# wedding_gifts_registry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for couples to create a wedding registry for their special day. It collects information about the type of gift, gift category, recipient's contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of gift from the options provided.
2.  Choose a category for the gift from the options listed.
3.  If desired, add a message to the gift.
4.  Enter the recipient's email address.
5.  Enter the recipient's phone number.
6.  Specify the gift amount.
7.  Enter the date the gift is to be given.
8.  Choose the time of day for the gift.
9.  Add any additional notes or comments about the gift.
10.  Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Gifts Registration Form** (1, Text, Optional): This is the form title.
* **Gift Type** (2, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of gift you'd like to give. Options include Bride, Groom, Couple, or Other.
* **Gift Category** (3, Select One, Optional): Select a category for the gift, such as Honeymoon, Kitchen, Home, or Other.
* **Gift Message** (4, Text, Optional): Add a personal message to the gift if desired.
* **Recipient Email** (5, Email, Optional): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Recipient Phone** (6, Text, Optional): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Gift Amount** (7, Number, Optional): Specify the amount of the gift.
* **Gift Date** (8, Date, Optional): Enter the date the gift will be given.
* **Gift Time** (9, Time, Optional): Choose the time of day for the gift.
* **Gift Note** (10, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the gift.
* **Submit** (11, Text, Optional): Click this button to complete the form.
* **Cancel** (12, Text, Optional): Click this button to cancel the form.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by couples, and the fields are meant to collect information about their preferences and details for their wedding guests.
