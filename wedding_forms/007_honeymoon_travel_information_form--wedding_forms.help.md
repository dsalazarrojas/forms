# Honeymoon Travel Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the honeymoon travel plans of a couple, allowing the travel agency to tailor the perfect trip for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter the destination of your honeymoon trip in the "Destination" field.
3. Enter the date you plan to depart on for your trip in the "Date of Departure" field.
4. Enter the date you plan to return on for your trip in the "Date of Return" field.
5. Select the type of travel you are looking for (e.g., romantic getaway, adventure trip, etc.) from the "Travel Type" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the number of people (including yourself) that will be traveling.
7. Describe the type of accommodations you are looking for (e.g., hotel, resort, etc.).
8. Indicate if you would like to book transportation (e.g., flight, car rental, etc.) for your trip.
9. Describe any activities or excursions you are interested in (e.g., sightseeing, adventure activities, etc.).
10. Add any additional comments or information about your trip.
11. Enter your email address and phone number so that the travel agency can contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Destination** (`destination`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where you plan to go on your honeymoon.
- **Date of Departure** (`date_of_departure`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to leave for your honeymoon.
- **Date of Return** (`date_of_return`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to return from your honeymoon.
- **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of travel experience you are looking for (e.g., romantic, adventure, etc.).
- **Number of People** (`number_of_people`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people that will be traveling with you.
- **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, `text`, optional): Describe the type of accommodations you are looking for (e.g., hotel, resort, etc.).
- **Transportation** (`transportation`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you would like to book transportation for your trip.
- **Activities** (`activities`, `text`, optional): Describe any activities or excursions you are interested in (e.g., sightseeing, adventure activities, etc.).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your trip.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so that the travel agency can contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so that the travel agency can contact you.
- **Honeymoon Budget** (`honeymoon_budget`, `number`, optional): Enter your estimated honeymoon budget.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have provided all the necessary information to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the travel agency for clarification.
- Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
