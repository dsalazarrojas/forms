# Knot Style Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Knot Style Selection Form is a tool to collect information on the style of knots or rope arrangements desired for the wedding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select a single choice for the "Wedding Knot Style" field.
2. Choose one or more options for the "Rope Knot", "Rope and Knot", and "Square Knot" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Wedding Knot Style (1)
• **Wedding Knot Style** (`wedding_knot_style`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to choose a single style for the wedding knot. Please select one of the options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Rope Knot (2)
• **Rope Knot** (`knot_style_option_1`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more options for the rope knot style. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' depending on your preference.

### Rope and Knot (3)
• **Rope and Knot** (`knot_style_option_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Similar to the previous field, choose one or more options for the rope and knot style. You can select 'Yes' or 'No' depending on your preference.

### Square Knot (4)
• **Square Knot** (`knot_style_option_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more options for the square knot style. Select 'Yes' or 'No' depending on your preference.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any of the options, you can always ask for clarification.
- This form is for your convenience, and your input will help ensure that the wedding decorations or ceremony setup meets your expectations.
