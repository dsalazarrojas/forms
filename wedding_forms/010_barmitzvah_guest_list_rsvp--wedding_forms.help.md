# Barmitzvah Guest List RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the celebrant and their family prepare for the Barmitzvah celebration by gathering essential information from guests about their attendance, preferences, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name as you would like it to appear on the invitation.
2. Enter your email address to receive event updates and information.
3. Provide your phone number in case the organizers need to reach you.
4. Specify your relationship to the celebrant (e.g., family member, family friend, friend of the honoree, etc.).
5. Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes, I will attend" or "No, I cannot attend".
6. If attending, select the number of guests in your party, including yourself.
7. List the names of any additional guests in your party (optional).
8. Indicate if children will be attending (number of children).
9. Confirm if you will attend the ceremony and the reception.
10. Select your preferred meal option.
11. Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies.
12. Indicate your preference for alcohol consumption.
13. Share any song requests for the dance floor (optional).
14. Confirm if you need transportation assistance.
15. Specify if you need parking information.
16. Indicate if you need hotel recommendations.
17. Choose how you prefer to deliver your gift (if applicable).
18. Confirm if you are comfortable with being included in event photos.
19. Specify if you have any special accommodations or needs.
20. Share a personal message with the celebrant (optional).
21. Confirm that you understand the RSVP deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the invitation.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address to receive event updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): Provide your phone number in case the organizers need to reach you.
* **Relationship to the Honoree** (Required): Specify how you know the celebrant (e.g., family member, family friend, friend of the honoree, etc.).
* **Will You Be Attending** (Required): Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes, I will attend" or "No, I cannot attend".
* **Number of Guests Including Yourself** (Optional): If attending, select the number of guests in your party, including yourself.
* **Names of Additional Guests** (Optional): List the names of any additional guests in your party.
* **Will Children Be Attending** (Required): Indicate if children will be attending (number of children).
* **Will You Attend the Ceremony** (Required): Confirm if you will attend the ceremony.
* **Will You Attend the Reception** (Required): Confirm if you will attend the reception.
* **Meal Preference** (Required): Select your preferred meal option.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (Optional): Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Alcohol Preference** (Required): Indicate if you will consume alcohol.
* **Song Requests for Dancing** (Optional): Share any song requests for the dance floor.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance** (Required): Confirm if you need transportation assistance.
* **Will You Need Parking** (Required): Specify if you need parking information.
* **Do You Need Hotel Recommendations** (Required): Indicate if you need hotel recommendations.
* **Gift Delivery Preference** (Optional): Choose how you prefer to deliver your gift (if applicable).
* **Photo Consent** (Required): Confirm if you are comfortable with being included in event photos.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (Optional): Specify if you have any special accommodations or needs.
* **Message to the Honoree** (Optional): Share a personal message with the celebrant.
* **RSVP Deadline Acknowledged** (Required): Confirm that you understand the RSVP deadline.

Note: Please make sure to review each field carefully and fill it in accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable celebration for everyone involved.
