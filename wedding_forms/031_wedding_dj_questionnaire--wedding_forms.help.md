# Wedding DJ Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding DJ Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information from the couple about their preferences for a wedding reception. This information will help the event planner or DJ tailor the event to the couple's tastes and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Select the options that best fit your preferences.
3. Fill in the text fields with your information.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding DJ Questionnaire Form**: This is just a label to help identify the form.
* **Couple's Preferences**: This section allows you to select your preferences for the wedding reception.
	+ **Yes/No**: Choose "Yes" if you would like to have the following:
		- Special requests fulfilled
		- A specific wedding theme
	+ **Music Preferences**: Choose "Yes" if you prefer to have a specific type of music played during the reception.
* **Couple's Information**: This section is used to gather your personal details:
	+ **Couple First Name**: Please fill in your first name.
	+ **Couple Last Name**: Please fill in your last name.
	+ **Couple Phone**: Please fill in your phone number.
	+ **Couple Email**: Please fill in your email address.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, please select them below. (Note: This field is not required but highly recommended)
* **Wedding Theme**: Choose a theme that best describes your wedding:
* **Music Preferences**: Choose the type of music you would like to be played during the reception.
* **Music Length**: Choose how long you would like the music to be played during the reception.
* **Photo Slides**: Choose if you would like to have photo slides during the reception.
* **Photo Description**: If you would like to have a photo description, please fill in the text field.
* **Music Length**: Choose how long you would like the music to be played during the reception.
