# wedding_rehearsal_slot_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a wedding rehearsal slot. It requires the bride's name, date of the wedding, start and end times, number of guests, contact name, and acceptance of terms. Please fill out the form accurately and do not book a rehearsal slot multiple times for the same date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the bride's name in the "Bride's Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the wedding in the "Date of Wedding" field.
3. Enter the start time in the "Start Time" field.
4. Enter the end time in the "End Time" field.
5. Enter the number of guests in the "Number of Guests" field.
6. Enter your contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
7. Accept the terms and conditions in the "Accept Terms" field.
8. Agree to the terms in the "Agree with the Terms" field.
9. (Optional) Select any special requests from the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bride's Name (text, required)**: Please enter the name of the bride.
* **Date of Wedding (date, required)**: Enter the date of the wedding. This field is required.
* **Start Time (time, required)**: Enter the start time of the wedding rehearsal.
* **End Time (time, required)**: Enter the end time of the wedding rehearsal.
* **Number of Guests (number, required)**: Enter the number of guests attending the wedding rehearsal.
* **Contact Name (text, required)**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Email (email, optional)**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Message (text, optional)**: Enter any additional message or request (optional).
* **Accept Terms (select multiple, required)**: Select "Yes" to accept the terms and conditions.
* **Agree with the Terms (select one, required)**: Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm your acceptance of the terms.
* **Special Requests (select multiple, optional)**: Select any special requests such as catering, decorations, music, or photography.
