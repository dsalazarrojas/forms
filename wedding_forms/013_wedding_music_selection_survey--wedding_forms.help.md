# Wedding Music Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the couple communicate their musical preferences to their wedding planner or DJ. It will help you select the music that will be played during different parts of your special day.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Instructions

- Please answer the questions in a way that reflects your personal preferences.
- Choose the music type that best fits your style and mood.

# Tips

* Be honest about your preferences and tastes in music.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, just choose the one that you think best fits your style.
* If you have any specific song requests, please let us know in the "Favorite Song" field.
* If you want to add any other music venue or style that is not listed, please use the "Other Music Venue" and "Other Music Style" fields.
