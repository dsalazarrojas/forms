# Wedding Planner Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Planner Nomination Form is used to collect information for the Wedding Planner Award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the nominee's experience from the options provided in the "Wedding Planner Experience" field.
2. Provide the nominee's information, including their name, email, title, organization, and bio.
3. Enter the nominator's name and email address.
4. Add a personal message to the nominator to provide additional context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Planner Nomination Form**: This is a title field to identify the form.
* **Nominee Name**: This is a field to enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Email**: This is a field to enter the email address of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Title**: This is a field to enter the title of the person being nominated (optional).
* **Nominee Organization**: This is a field to enter the organization of the person being nominated (optional).
* **Nominee Bio**: This is a field to enter a brief description of the person being nominated (optional).
* **Wedding Planner Experience**: Select one or more options that best describe the nominee's experience in the wedding planning industry.
* **Nominator Name**: This is a field to enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email**: This is a field to enter a valid email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Title**: This is a field to enter the title of the person submitting the nomination (optional).
* **Nominator Organization**: This is a field to enter the organization of the person submitting the nomination (optional).
* **Nominator Phone**: This is a field to enter the phone number of the person submitting the nomination (optional).
* **Nominator Message**: This is a field to enter a personal message from the person submitting the nomination.

## Tips
Please ensure that the nominee's email address is valid and that the nominator's name and email are accurate.
