# Wedding Reception Planner Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Reception Planner Form is designed to collect information from couples to plan their wedding reception.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the bride and groom's names and contact information.
2. Provide details about the wedding ceremony, including date, time, and location.
3. Choose the venue for the reception.
4. Select the style of the reception, e.g., formal, informal, buffet, etc.
5. Enter information about the guests, including number and preferences.
6. Specify the food and beverages to be served.
7. Choose the entertainment services required, e.g., music, DJ, photo booth, etc.
8. List any other professional services needed, e.g., florist, cake designer, etc.
9. Enter any additional wedding details.
10. Specify the couple's preferences, e.g., budget, color scheme, etc.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bride/Groom Information**: Enter the names and contact information of the bride and groom.
* **Wedding Ceremony**: Enter the date, time, and location of the wedding ceremony.
* **Wedding Reception Venue**: Enter the venue chosen for the reception.
* **Wedding Reception Style**: Choose the style of the reception, e.g., formal, informal, buffet, etc.
* **Guests Information**: Enter the number and details of the guests, including any special requests or requirements.
* **Food and Beverages**: Specify the food and beverages to be served at the reception.
* **Entertainment Services**: Choose the entertainment services required, e.g., music, DJ, photo booth, etc.
* **Other Professional Services**: List any other professional services needed, e.g., florist, cake designer, etc.
* **Wedding Details**: Enter any additional details about the wedding, e.g., special requests, decorations, etc.
* **Couple Preferences**: Specify the couple's preferences, e.g., budget, color scheme, etc.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the planner can tailor the reception to your needs.
* Double-check the details before submitting the form to ensure that there are no mistakes or omissions.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, feel free to contact us.
