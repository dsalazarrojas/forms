# Top Hats Wedding Estimate - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a wedding estimate. The goal is to gather details about the wedding, including the date, venue, start and end times, number of guests, budget, and estimate total. This information will be used to provide a comprehensive estimate for the wedding planning and coordination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the wedding in the `wedding_date` field.
2. Select the type of venue where the wedding will take place (if applicable) in the `wedding_venue_type` field.
3. Provide the address of the wedding venue in the `wedding_venue_address` field.
4. Set the start and end times of the wedding in the `wedding_start_time` and `wedding_end_time` fields, respectively.
5. Enter the estimated number of guests attending the wedding in the `wedding_guests` field.
6. Indicate the estimated budget for the wedding in the `wedding_budget` field.
7. Enter the estimated total for the wedding in the `wedding_estimate_total` field.
8. Provide the name of the person or organization estimating the wedding details in the `estimated_by` field.
9. Enter the date on which the estimate was completed in the `estimated_on` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **wedding_date**: Enter the date of the wedding. This field is a calendar date field where you can select a date.
* **wedding_venue**: Enter the name of the venue where the wedding will take place.
* **wedding venue type**: Select the type of venue where the wedding will take place (if applicable). Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **wedding venue address**: Enter the address of the wedding venue.
* **wedding_start_time**: Enter the start time of the wedding.
* **wedding_end_time**: Enter the end time of the wedding.
* **wedding_guests**: Enter the estimated number of guests attending the wedding.
* **wedding_budget**: Enter the estimated budget for the wedding.
* **wedding_estimate_total**: Enter the estimated total for the wedding.
* **estimated_by**: Enter the name of the person or organization providing the estimate.
* **estimated_on**: Enter the date on which the estimate was completed.
