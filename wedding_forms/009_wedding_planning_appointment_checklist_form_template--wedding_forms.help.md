# Wedding Planning Appointment Checklist Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Wedding Planning Appointment Checklist Form Template is a comprehensive form designed to help wedding planners and coordinators manage and organize wedding appointments efficiently. This form helps to ensure that all important details are captured, communicated, and followed up on.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Schedule Checklists**: Select the checklists that are relevant to the appointment.
2.  **Vendor Details**: Enter the details of the vendor providing services for the wedding.
3.  **Vendor Quote**: Enter the quote provided by the vendor.
4.  **Contact Details**: Choose the contact method for the vendor.
5.  **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms agreed upon with the vendor.
6.  **Payment Schedule**: Choose the payment schedule for the vendor.
7.  **Payment Status**: Enter the current payment status.
8.  **Vendor Contact**: Enter the contact information for the vendor.
9.  **Vendor Email**: Enter the email address of the vendor.
10.  **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of the appointment.
11.  **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of the appointment.
12.  **Appointment Status**: Choose the status of the appointment.
13.  **Notes**: Enter any notes about the appointment.
14.  **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to the appointment.
15.  **Created by**: Choose who created the appointment.
16.  **Last Updated**: Enter the last updated date and time of the appointment.
17.  **Created at**: Enter the date and time the appointment was created.
18.  **Updated at**: Enter the date and time the appointment was last updated.
19.  **Deleted at**: Enter the date and time the appointment was deleted.
20.  **Created by ID**: Enter the ID of the person who created the appointment.
21.  **Assigned Tool ID**: Enter the ID of the tool assigned to the appointment.
22.  **Updated by ID**: Enter the ID of the person who last updated the appointment.
23.  **Updated at ID**: Enter the ID of the tool used to update the appointment.
24.  **Deleted at ID**: Enter the ID of the person who deleted the appointment.
25.  **Version ID**: Enter the ID of the version of the appointment.
