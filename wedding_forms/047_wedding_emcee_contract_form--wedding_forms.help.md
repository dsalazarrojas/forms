# wedding_emcee_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wedding_emcee_contract_form is a form used to collect information from clients for an EMCEE at their wedding event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
* For fields with multiple options, select all the services you would like to book the EMCEE for.
* Make sure to include the payment terms and payment date if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding EMCEE Contract Form** (`title`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, but it's not necessary to fill in as it's just a placeholder.
* **Services** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the services you would like the EMCEE to provide, such as DJ, MC, etc.
* **Rates** (`rates_offered`, number, required: false): Enter the rate you are willing to pay for the EMCEE's services.
* **Schedules** (`schedules`, select_one, required: false): Choose the schedule that best suits your event, which could be Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Services Agreed** (`services_agreed`, select_multiple, required: false): If you have agreed on services with the EMCEE, select the options that apply.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required: false): Choose the payment terms you have agreed upon with the EMCEE, which could be Hourly, Flat Rate, or Projected Hours.
* **Payment Terms Notes** (`payment_terms_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes regarding the payment terms.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you expect to make the payment.
* **EMCEE Services Notes** (`emcee_services_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments for the EMCEE services.
* **Client EMCEE Notes** (`client_emcee_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes from the client to the EMCEE.
* **EMCEE Rate** (`emcee_rate`, number, required: false): Enter the rate you have agreed upon with the EMCEE.
* **Client Rate** (`client_rate`, number, required: false): Enter the rate you are willing to pay for the EMCEE's services.
* **Client EMCEE Agreement** (`client_emcee_agreement`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments from the client regarding the EMCEE agreement.

Note: This form is not a formal contract and is used for informational purposes only.
