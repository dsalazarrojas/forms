# Destination Wedding Planning Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from couples to create personalized wedding quotations.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, ensure you have all the necessary details for the couple and their wedding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Wedding Details** (wedding_details): Enter a brief description of the wedding, including the date, location, and any specific requirements or requests.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Text

- **Couple Info** (couple_info): Provide the names and contact information of the couple getting married.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Text

- **Guest List** (guest_list): Enter a list of guests' names, including the number of guests attending.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Text

- **Services Offered** (services_offered): Select the wedding services you are interested in (e.g., venue management, catering, decor, etc.).
  - Required: No
  - Type: Text

- **Quotation Request** (quotation_request): Describe your quotation request, including any specific requirements or questions you have.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Text

- **Travel Details** (travel_details): Provide travel information, including flight or train arrangements, transportation, and accommodation details.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Text

- **Payment Details** (payment_details): Enter your payment terms, including payment methods and schedules.
  - Required: No
  - Type: Text

## Tips

- Ensure you provide accurate and detailed information to help wedding planners create a personalized quotation.
- Be as specific as possible in your answers to ensure a clear understanding of your needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
