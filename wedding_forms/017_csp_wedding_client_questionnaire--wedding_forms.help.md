# CSP Wedding Client Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from couples planning their wedding. Please provide your details accurately, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification on any field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name and partner's name in the respective text fields.
2. Select the correct wedding date using the date picker.
3. Enter your primary contact phone number.
4. Provide the necessary details for each venue (ceremony, reception, and getting ready location).
5. Choose the correct start time for the ceremony and indicate if you're doing a first look.
6. Select the time for family formals.
7. If applicable, list any specific must-have photos for the photography session.
8. Enter the total number in your bridal party.
9. List any other vendors involved in the wedding (planner, florist, DJ, etc.).
10. Add any additional notes or information that might be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Partner 1 Full Name** (`partner_1_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name for accurate documentation.
- **Partner 2 Full Name** (`partner_2_name`, `text`, required): Enter your partner's full name for accurate documentation.
- **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, `date`, required): Select the correct wedding date using the provided date picker.
- **Primary Phone Number** (`primary_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your primary phone number for contact purposes.
- **Ceremony Venue Name and Address** (`ceremony_location`, `text`, required): Enter the full address of the ceremony location, including any specific details that may be helpful for the vendors.
- **Reception Venue Name and Address** (`reception_location`, `text`, optional): If different from the ceremony location, enter the address of the reception venue.
- **Getting Ready Location** (`getting_ready_location`, `text`, required): Provide the address of the location where you'll be getting ready for the ceremony.
- **Ceremony Start Time** (`ceremony_start_time`, `time`, required): Select the start time for the ceremony.
- **Are you doing a First Look?** (`first_look`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you're doing a first look on your wedding day.
- **Preferred Time for Family Formals** (`family_formals_time`, `text`, required): Select your preferred time for family formals.
- **Specific Must-Have Photos** (`must_have_shots`, `text`, optional): List any unique or special photography requests.
- **Total Number in Bridal Party** (`bridal_party_size`, `number`, required): Enter the total number in your bridal party.
- **Other Vendors** (`vendor_list`, `text`, optional): List any other vendors involved in your wedding, such as a planner, florist, or DJ.
- **Anything else we should know** (`extra_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information that may be helpful for us to know.
