# Wedding Dance Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information for wedding planning, particularly for the dance planning process. It includes questions on the wedding date, time, location, theme, music, and floor plan to ensure a smooth and enjoyable wedding celebration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the wedding date and time according to the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` for the date and `HH:MM:SS` for the time.
2. Provide the exact location of the wedding, including the venue name, address, and any other relevant details.
3. Choose the wedding theme that best suits the couple's preferences.
4. Decide on whether or not to have a wedding musician and select accordingly.
5. Choose the person responsible for wedding music, whether it's the wedding host, musician, or another individual.
6. Describe the wedding floor plan, including the layout and any unique features.
7. Select the type of dance floor that fits the wedding venue and number of guests.
8. Specify the height and width of the dance floor, if applicable.
9. Choose the type of dance that will be performed, from simple to very advanced.
10. Determine the duration of the dance and enter the time in hours.
11. Add any additional notes about the floor plan if necessary.
12. Provide any specific height and width of the dance floor if applicable.
13. Enter the height of the wedding floor in feet.
14. Enter the width of the wedding floor in feet.
15. Add any notes for the wedding floor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Wedding Date** (`weddingdate`, date, required: false)
The wedding date is the day of the celebration. Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
### **Wedding Time** (`weddingtime`, time, required: false)
The wedding time is the scheduled start time of the celebration. Enter the time in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
### **Wedding Location** (`weddinglocation`, text, required: false)
