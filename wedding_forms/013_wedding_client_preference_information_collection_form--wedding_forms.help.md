# Wedding Client Preference Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients about their wedding event, including couple details, event specifics, and vendor requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary details about the couple's names, contact information, and event specifics.
2. Enter the couple's names in the "Couple Names" field.
3. Provide the couple's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the couple's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the wedding date in the "Wedding Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Select the desired event time from the "Event Time" field.
7. Choose the event location from the "Event Location" field.
8. Select the number of guests expected from the "Number of Guests" field.
9. Enter the allocated budget for the event in the "Budget" field.
10. Indicate the type of event (e.g., Wedding Reception, Ceremony, Engagement Party, etc.) in the "Event Type" field.
11. Select the event theme from the "Event Theme" field (e.g., Beach, Rustic, Formal, etc.).
12. Enter any additional planners or vendors (e.g., caterer, florist, etc.) in the "Planners" and "Vendors" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Couple Names**: Enter the names of the couple getting married (e.g., John Doe and Jane Doe).
* **Email**: Enter the couple's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the couple's phone number.
* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of the wedding in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time**: Select the desired time of the event (e.g., 3:00 PM).
* **Event Location**: Choose the location of the event (e.g., Venue, Restaurant, Park).
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the expected number of guests.
* **Budget**: Enter the allocated budget for the event.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event (e.g., Wedding Reception, Ceremony, Engagement Party, etc.).
* **Event Theme**: Choose the theme of the event (e.g., Beach, Rustic, Formal, etc.).
* **Planners**: Enter any additional planners or coordinators.
* **Vendors**: Enter any additional vendors or services required for the event.
* **Wedding Theme Options**: Choose the specific theme for the event (e.g., Beach, Rustic, Formal, etc.).
