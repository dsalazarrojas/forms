# Wedding Videography Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture essential information for the wedding videography services, including the services required, the date of the wedding, and the contact details of the videographer. It will help us understand your needs and ensure that our videography services can be tailored to make your special day unforgettable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the information required in the following fields:

1. **Wedding Videography Agreement**: Please enter a title for the agreement, if desired.
2. **Videography Services**: Select one of the offered videography services that suits your needs. This could be Wedding Videography Services, Wedding Videography Services with Drone, or Wedding Videography Services with Aerial Videography.
3. **Date of Wedding**: Enter the date of your wedding. Please use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. **Videographer Name**: Enter the name of the videographer who will be capturing your special day.
5. **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number where you can be reached for any further questions or details.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any further questions or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Videography Agreement**: A brief title for the agreement.
* **Videography Services**: Choose the type of videography services you require.
  - **Wedding Videography Services**: The basic videography services offered.
  - **Wedding Videography Services with Drone**: Videography services with the addition of drone coverage.
  - **Wedding Videography Services with Aerial Videography**: Videography services with aerial coverage.
* **Date of Wedding**: The date of your wedding. Please use mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Videographer Name**: The name of the videographer who will be capturing your special day.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number where you can be reached for any further questions or details.
* **Email**: Your email address where you can be reached for any further questions or details.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
