# Wedding Video Package Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to inquire about purchasing a wedding video package from our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Provide details about your event in the "Event Details" field.
3. Select the desired wedding video package(s) in the "Video Packages" field.
4. If you have any special requests, type them in the "Special Requests" field.
5. Decide whether you would like to receive a quote by selecting "Send Quote" in the "Send Quote" field.
6. You can upload media related to your event in the "Upload Media" field.
7. Review and confirm your form submission in the "Form Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Please enter your name, email address, and phone number to contact you regarding your inquiry.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): Describe your event (wedding) details, including date, location, and number of guests.
* **Video Packages** (`video_packages`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the wedding video package(s) you are interested in purchasing. Options include Wedding Package 1, Wedding Package 2, and Wedding Package 3.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests or instructions, type them here.
* **Send Quote** (`send_quote`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Send Quote" to receive a quote for your selected video package.
* **Upload Media** (`upload_media`, `note`, optional): Upload media related to your event, such as photos or videos.
* **Media Uploaded** (`uploaded_media`, `note`, optional): A record of the media you have uploaded.
* **Upload Video** (`upload_video`, `text`, optional): If you have a video to upload, enter the URL or file here.
* **Video Length** (`video_length`, `number`, optional): The length of the video you uploaded.
* **Video Type** (`video_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of video you are uploading (Video 1, Video 2, or Video 3).
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, `text`, optional): If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us here.
* **Review Us** (`review_us`, `text`, optional): Share your review of our service here.
* **Upload Video URL** (`upload_video_url`, `text`, optional): If you have a video URL, enter it here.
* **Video File** (`video_file`, `text`, optional): If you have a video file, enter the URL or file here.
* **Review** (`review`, `text`, optional): Share your review of our service here.
