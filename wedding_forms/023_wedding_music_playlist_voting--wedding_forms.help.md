# Wedding Music Playlist Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Music Playlist Voting form is designed to collect your opinions on the most popular songs to play during our special day. Your input will help us create a memorable and enjoyable atmosphere.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your name.
3. Choose your favorite music genre.
4. Select your favorite songs from the list or add a new song if it's not already on the list.
5. Choose which songs you would like to vote for or add to the playlist.
6. Enter your email address (optional, but recommended for future contact).
7. Add any other notes or comments you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our special day.
* **What genre of music do you enjoy?**: Choose one or more of the provided genres that best fit your taste in music.
* **Song Name**: If you'd like to add a song that's not already on our list, enter its name here.
* **Add Song**: Select one or more songs from the provided list or add a new song using the previous field.
* **Vote**: Choose which songs you would like to vote for to add to our playlist. You can select one or more songs.
* **Add Vote**: Enter your vote(s) for one or more songs, e.g., "I love this song!" or "Add this song to the playlist!"
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you with updates or confirmations.
* **Phone**: Not currently used, feel free to leave blank.
* **Note**: Share any other notes or comments you'd like to add.
* **Date**: Not currently used, feel free to leave blank.
* **Time**: Not currently used, feel free to leave blank.
* **Submit**: Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to finalize your input.
