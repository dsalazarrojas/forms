# Corsage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corsage Order Form is designed to allow customers to order corsages and flowers for various occasions. To place an order, please fill out this form with your details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your customer name and phone number.
2. Select the type of corsage you wish to order.
3. Choose the flowers you'd like to include in your corsage.
4. Specify the occasion for your corsage order.
5. (Optional) Add a message for the recipient.
6. Select a date and time for your order.
7. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter your full name as you'd like it to appear on the order.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Crosage Type (corsage_type)**: Select the type of corsage you're interested in ordering (e.g., bouquet, arrangement, etc.).
* **Flowers (flowers)**: Choose the flowers you'd like to include in your order (e.g., rose, lily, daisies).
* **Occasion (occasion)**: Specify the occasion for the corsage order (e.g., birthday, anniversary, etc.).
* **Message (message)**: Add a personal message to be included with your order (optional).
* **Order Date (order_date)**: Select the date you'd like to have your order delivered or ready.
* **Order Time (order_time)**: Choose the time for your order to be prepared or delivered.
* **Corsage Price (corsage_price)**: This field is not applicable, as corsage prices are not specified in the provided YAML. If you'd like to include a price field, please add it to the form schema before publishing this guide.
* **Tips**: If you're unsure about any field, please contact our team for assistance. We're here to help!
