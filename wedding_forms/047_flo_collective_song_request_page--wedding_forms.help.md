# Flo Collective Song Request Page - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Flo Collective Song Request Page is a simple form designed to gather users' preferences for specific songs and artists.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your song preferences in the `song_preferences` field. You can describe the type of song you're looking for, such as "pop music" or "classical music".
2. In the `song_title` field, enter the title of the specific song you're requesting. This could be a well-known song or a song you've been thinking about.
3. In the `song_artist` field, enter the name of the artist associated with the song you've mentioned in the previous field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **song_preferences** (text, optional): Describe the type of song you're looking for (e.g., "pop music" or "classical music").
* **song_title** (text, optional): Enter the title of a specific song you're interested in.
* **song_artist** (text, optional): Type in the name of the artist associated with the song you've mentioned in the previous field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in your preferences accurately so that we can try to find the song you're looking for.
- If you're unsure about any information, feel free to provide any details that you think might help.
- If you have any other questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
