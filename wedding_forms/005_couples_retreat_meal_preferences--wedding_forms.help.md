# couples_retreat_meal_preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the meal preferences and dietary needs of couples attending a retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "General Preferences" section with any relevant information about your preferences.
2. If you have any dietary needs, please specify them in the "Dietary Needs" section.
3. In the "Meal Choice" section, select the meal options you are interested in.
4. For the "Breakfast", "Lunch", and "Dinner" sections, select the meal options you are interested in.
5. If you have any special requests or preferences not listed, please mention them in the "Additional Info" section.
6. If you have any other food preferences, please specify them in the "Other Food" section.
7. Please provide any special requests you have in the "Special Requests" section.
8. If you have any additional information about your dietary needs, please provide it in the "Dietary Needs Text" section.
9. If you have any additional information about your meal choice, please provide it in the "Meal Choice Text" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Preferences**: This section is for any general information about your preferences, such as vegetarian or vegan.
* **Dietary Needs**: This section is for any specific dietary needs or restrictions you have, such as gluten-free or dairy-free.
* **Meal Choice**:
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in this meal option.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in this meal option.
* **Breakfast**:
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in this meal option.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in this meal option.
* **Lunch**:
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in this meal option.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in this meal option.
* **Dinner**:
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in this meal option.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in this meal option.
* **Snack**:
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in this meal option.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in this meal option.
* **Other**:
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in this meal option.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in this meal option.
* **Other Food**: This section is for any other food preferences you have, such as specific ingredients or allergies.
* **Special Requests**: This section is for any special requests you have, such as dietary requirements or allergies.
* **Special Requests Text**: This section is for any additional information about your special requests.
* **Dietary Needs Text**: This section is for any additional information about your dietary needs.
* **Meal Choice Text**: This section is for any additional information about your meal choice.
* **Other Preferences**: This section is for any other preferences you have, such as seating arrangements or transportation requirements.
