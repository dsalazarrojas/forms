# Wedding Flower Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Flower Consultation Form is a questionnaire designed to help us understand your preferences for your wedding flowers. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and we'll use the information to provide you with personalized flower consultation and proposals for your special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on each page (indicated by a number) to proceed with the questions.
* For each question, select the relevant option that best describes your preference.
* You can select multiple options for some questions if applicable.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a star \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Flower Type** (2, select_one, optional): Select the type of wedding flowers you are interested in for your wedding (e.g., roses, peonies, or a mix of both).
* **Flower Focal Point** (3, select_multiple, optional): Choose the flowers that you would like to see as the main attraction at your wedding (e.g., one type of flower, a mix of flowers, or none).
* **Flower Color** (4, select_multiple, optional): Select the colors you would like to see in your wedding flowers (e.g., red, pink, yellow, or a mix).
* **Wedding Dress Color** (5, select_one, optional): Select the color of the wedding dress you will be wearing (if applicable, e.g., ivory, blush, or red).
* **Wedding Theme** (6, select_multiple, optional): Choose the theme or style that best describes your wedding (e.g., classic, rustic, or bohemian).
* **Wedding Flower Budget** (7, number, optional): Provide a budget for your wedding flowers (if you're willing to share this information). If not, please leave it blank.
* **Other Wedding Flower Budget Information** (8, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your wedding flower budget, you can provide it here (e.g., any constraints or specific preferences).
