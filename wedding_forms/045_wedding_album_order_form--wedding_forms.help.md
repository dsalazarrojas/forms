# Wedding Album Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Album Order Form is a form designed for couples to provide information about their wedding album preferences. This form is used to gather details such as album size, materials, design, photo type, quantity, color scheme, and any special requests they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear idea of your wedding album preferences.
2. Select your album size preference from the options provided.
3. Choose your preferred materials for the album.
4. Select the design options that match your style.
5. Choose the type of photos you'd like included in the album.
6. Specify the quantity of albums you'd like to order.
7. Select the color scheme that suits your taste.
8. If you have any special requests, such as a specific font or message, mention them in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Album Size** (`Album Size`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the size of the album that best suits your needs. The options are typically 8x10 inches, 8x12 inches, or 12x12 inches.
* **Materials** (`Materials`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the materials you'd like for your album, such as leather, wood, or cloth.
* **Design** (`Design`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the design style that reflects your taste, such as modern, traditional, or rustic.
* **Photo Type** (`Photo Type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of photos you'd like included, such as wedding ceremony, reception, or honeymoon photos.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required/optional): Specify the number of albums you'd like to order.
* **Color Scheme** (`Color Scheme`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the color scheme that suits your wedding theme, such as monochromatic, bi-tonal, or tri-tonal.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requests, such as a specific font or message, mention them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any delays or errors in the creation of your wedding album.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
* We appreciate your input and will do our best to create a beautiful and personalized wedding album that reflects your love story.
