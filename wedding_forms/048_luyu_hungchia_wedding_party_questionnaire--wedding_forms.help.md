# luyu_hungchia_wedding_party_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from wedding party members about their food, drink, and headcount preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your answer for each question carefully, as it will help us determine the best experience for you at the wedding party.
2. You can skip questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Organize** (`select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you will be attending as a member of the wedding party, otherwise answer "No".
* **guest_name** (`text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your guest(s) that will be attending the wedding.
* **guest_phone** (`text`, required: false): Please enter the phone number of your guest(s) that will be attending the wedding.
* **guest_email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of your guest(s) that will be attending the wedding.
* **guest_date** (`date`, required: false): Select the date of arrival of your guest(s) at the wedding.
* **guest_time** (`time`, required: false): Select the estimated time of arrival of your guest(s) at the wedding.
* **guest_message** (`note`, required: false): Please enter any special message or notes for your guest(s).
* **head_count** (`number`, required: false): Please enter the estimated headcount for your guest(s).
* **head_count_hint** (`text`, required: false): This is a hint for the headcount field. Please enter any additional information about your headcount.
* **food_type** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of food you will be having at the wedding. Choose all that apply.
* **drink_type** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of drink you will be having at the wedding. Choose all that apply.
* **special_request** (`text`, required: false): Please enter any special requests or notes for your guest(s).
* **head_count_estimate** (`number`, required: false): Please enter your estimated headcount for the wedding party.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your wedding coordinator for clarification.
