# General Photography Wedding Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and manage a general wedding photography contract between a photographer and their client. It captures information about the wedding details, contract terms, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the following information as accurately as possible:

1. **Bride Name**: The name of the bride.
2. **Groom Name**: The name of the groom.
3. **Email**: The email of the bride or groom.
4. **Phone**: The phone number of the bride or groom.
5. **Contract Date**: The date the contract was signed.
6. **Wedding Date**: The date of the wedding ceremony.
7. **Ceremony Time**: The time of the wedding ceremony.
8. **Reception Time**: The time of the reception.
9. **Venue**: The location of the wedding ceremony and reception.
10. **Location**: The physical location of the wedding.
11. **Photographer Name**: The name of the photographer.
12. **Photographer Email**: The email of the photographer.
13. **Photographer Phone**: The phone number of the photographer.
14. **Contract Terms**: Any additional terms of the contract.
15. **Contract Signature**: The status of the contract (True, False, or Pending).
16. **Contract Date Signed**: The date the contract was signed.
17. **Wedding Details**: Any additional information about the wedding.
18. **Photographer Rate**: The rate of the photographer.
19. **Payment Method**: The method of payment (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
20. **Contract Expires**: The date the contract expires.
21. **Contract Status**: The status of the contract (Active, Pending, or Expired).
22. **Contract Notes**: Any additional information about the contract.
23. **Contract Comments**: Any additional comments about the contract.
24. **Contract Total Amount**: The total amount of the contract.
