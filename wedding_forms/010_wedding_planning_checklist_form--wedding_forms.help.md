# Wedding Planning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This wedding planning checklist form is designed to assist couples in planning their wedding day, gathering essential details and keeping them organized.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, start by entering the required fields, and then proceed to the following sections:

*   Fill out the first section with general information about the wedding, such as venue details, wedding party, and date and time.
*   In the next sections, provide more specific information about the wedding details, such as budget, guest list, decorations, ceremony, and reception details.
*   The last sections cover essential information about vendors and payment arrangements.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields marked as 'required'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Details** (`wedding_planning_checklist_form_1`, text): Enter a brief description of your wedding, including location, date, and any other relevant details.
  
  This section serves as a general overview of the wedding and provides a starting point for the rest of the planning process.

- **Venue Details** (`venue_details`, text): Enter details about the venue where the wedding will take place, including address, phone number, and any other relevant information.
  
  This field helps you keep track of the venue's specifics, making it easier to communicate with the venue staff and vendors.

- **Wedding Party** (`wedding_party_details`, text): Enter a list of wedding party members, including their names, roles, and contact information.
  
  Having a clear list of your wedding party can help with coordinating logistics and communication on the big day.

- **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, text): Enter the wedding's date and time.
  
  This field allows you to set a clear timeline for the wedding events and schedule.

- **Budget Breakdown** (`budget_breakdown`, text): Enter an estimate of the overall budget for the wedding, including costs for venue, food, decorations, etc.
  
  Having a clear budget in mind can help you make financial decisions and avoid overspending.

- **Guest List** (`guest_list`, text): Enter a list of expected guests, including their names and contact information.
  
  This helps with headcounts, seating arrangements, and communication with guests.

- **Decorations** (`decorations`, text): Enter a description of the decorations and overall aesthetic you envision for the wedding.
  
  This field helps you visualize and communicate your decoration ideas to vendors and helpers.

- **Ceremony Details** (`ceremony_details`, text): Enter a description of the ceremony's specifics, including order of events and rituals.
  
  Having a clear understanding of the ceremony can help with planning and communication with officiants and vendors.

- **Reception Details** (`reception_details`, text): Enter a description of the reception's specifics, including venue, food, and entertainment.
  
  This field helps with planning and coordinating the reception events.

- **Vendors** (`vendor_info`, text): Enter a list of vendors involved in the wedding, including their contact information and services provided.
  
  Keeping track of vendors and their services can help with communication and coordination on the big day.

- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text): Enter payment details for the wedding, including methods and amounts.
  
  This field helps with financial planning and tracking.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Use this form as a starting point for discussing and planning your wedding with your partner, family, and vendors.
