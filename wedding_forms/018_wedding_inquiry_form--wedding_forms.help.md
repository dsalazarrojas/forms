# Wedding Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Inquiry Form is used to collect information from wedding planners and coordinators regarding a wedding. This form helps us understand the client's preferences and requirements for a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the wedding date and time in the `wedding_date` field.
2. Enter the wedding location in the `wedding_location` field.
3. Indicate the number of guests attending the wedding in the `number_of_guests` field.
4. Select the desired wedding services in the `wedding_services` field.
5. Enter the contact number in the `contact_number` field.
6. Add your email address in the `email` field.
7. Write any additional message or instructions in the `message` field.
8. Select the assigned tool in the `assigned_tool` field.
9. Choose the user(s) assigned to this event in the `assigned_user` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Date (wedding_date)**: Enter the date of the wedding. This field is optional but recommended for our records.
* **Wedding Time (wedding_time)**: Enter the time of the wedding. This field is optional but recommended for our records.
* **Wedding Location (wedding_location)**: Enter the venue or location where the wedding will take place.
* **Number of Guests (number_of_guests)**: Indicate the estimated number of guests attending the wedding. This field helps us plan and prepare.
* **Wedding Services (wedding_services)**: Select the services you would like to have for your wedding. Options include "1", "2", and "3".
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: Enter your contact number so we can reach you for any questions or updates.
* **Email (email)**: Add your email address so we can communicate with you efficiently.
* **Message (message)**: Write any additional information or instructions about the wedding.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: Select the tool or platform assigned to this event. Options include "chatjimmy" and "Other".
* **Assigned User (assigned_user)**: Choose the user(s) assigned to this event. Options include "chatjimmy" and "Other".
