# Wedding Vendor Proposal Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Vendor Proposal Upload Form is used to collect proposal information from vendors who want to offer their services to our clients. This form helps us understand their proposal in detail and allows us to efficiently evaluate their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information such as the proposal title, description, price, and services offered.
2. Check the proposal photo options that apply to your proposal.
3. Check the proposal file options that apply to your proposal.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Proposal**: This is the main title of your proposal. Please enter a brief and descriptive title that summarizes your proposal. 
* **Proposal Title**: This is a more detailed title that will be used to identify your proposal. Please make sure it is clear and concise.
* **Proposal Description**: This is a detailed description of your proposal. Please provide as much information as possible about your services and what you can offer.
* **Proposal Price**: This is the price of your proposal. Please enter the price accurately, including any taxes or fees.
* **Services**: Check the services you offer. Please select all applicable services.
* **Proposal Photos**: Check if your proposal includes photos.
* **Proposal Files**: Check if your proposal includes files such as brochures, contracts, or other attachments.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your proposal.
* **Email**: Please enter your contact email address. This will be used by our team to contact you for further information.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your contact phone number. This will be used by our team to contact you for further information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields before submitting your proposal.
* Please be as detailed as possible in your proposal description and services offered.
* Please attach any relevant files that support your proposal.
* We recommend checking your email and phone number for any communications from our team.
