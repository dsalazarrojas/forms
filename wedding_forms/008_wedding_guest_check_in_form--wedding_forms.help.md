# Wedding Guest Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Wedding Guest Check In Form is a tool designed to gather essential details from wedding guests to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all. This form helps the hosts to plan and prepare for the wedding party accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your guest's details in the 'Guest Details' section.
2. Enter your email address in the 'Guest Email' field.
3. Choose a 'Arrival Time' that suits you best.
4. Specify the 'Arrival Date' of your visit.
5. If you have a 'Plus One', select 'Yes' and provide their details in the 'Guest Name' field.
6. Choose how many guests are accompanying you in the 'Number of Guests' field.
7. If you have any 'Special Requests', use the 'Special Requests' field to provide detailed information.
8. Indicate if you require 'Parking' and 'Special Instructions' fields will be provided accordingly.
9. If you have any 'Special Requirements' or 'Allergic Details', use the 'Special Requirements' or 'Allergic Details' fields to provide detailed information.
10. Finally, confirm if you have any 'Medications' and provide 'Medications Details' if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide your first name, last name, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required/optional): Select a time that suits you best for arriving at the wedding party.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required/optional): Specify the date of your arrival.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_guests`, number, required/optional): Indicate how many guests are accompanying you.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requests or needs, please provide detailed information in this field.
* **Assigned Host** (`assigned_host`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your host from the provided options.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Use this field to provide any additional information or comments.
* **Wedding Party** (`wedding_party`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose which wedding party you will be attending (Bride, Groom, Both, or Neither).
* **Is Plus One?** (`is_plus_one`, select_one, required/true): Indicate if you have a 'Plus One' and select 'Yes'.
* **Is Baby?** (`is_baby`, select_one, required/true): Confirm if you have a baby.
* **Is Parking Needed?** (`is_parking`, select_one, required/true): Indicate if you require 'Parking' for your visit.
* **Parking Details** (`parking_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional 'Parking Details' if necessary.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required/optional): If you have any 'Special Instructions', provide them in this field.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, required/optional): Use this field to provide any 'Special Requirements' or 'Allergic Details'.
* **Is Allergic?** (`is_allergic`, select_one, required/true): Indicate if you have any 'Allergic' conditions.
* **Allergic Details** (`allergic_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any 'Allergic Details' if necessary.
* **Is Medications?** (`is_medications`, select_one, required/true): Confirm if you have any 'Medications' or 'Medications Details'.
* **Medications Details** (`medications_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any 'Medications Details' if necessary.
* **Is Food Restrictions?** (`is_food_restrictions`, select_one, required/true): Indicate if you have any 'Food Restrictions' or 'Food Restrictions Details'.
* **Food Restrictions Details** (`food_restrictions_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any 'Food Restrictions Details' if necessary.
* **Is Special Requests?** (`is_special_requests`, select_one, required/true): Confirm if you have any 'Special Requests' or 'Special Requests Details'.
* **Special Requests Details** (`special_requests_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any 'Special Requests Details' if necessary.
