# Online Wedding Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for online payment processing for wedding services or related expenses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Client Info** field with the client's name and other relevant details.
2. Provide the **Service Details** in the following field.
3. Enter the **Service Price**, which is the amount to be paid for the service.
4. Select the **Payment Method** from the available options (Cash, Credit, PayPal, Bank).
5. Enter the **Payment Amount**, which should match the **Service Price** field.
6. Select the **Payment Status** from the available options (Paid, Not Paid).
7. If you wish to record the **Payment Date**, you may enter it in the corresponding field (optional).
8. In the **Note** field, you can include any additional comments or notes about the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Info** (1.1, text, required): Fill in the client's name and other relevant details.
- **Service Details** (1.2, text, required): Describe the service for which payment is being made.
- **Service Price** (1.3, number, required): Enter the amount to be paid for the service.
- **Payment Method** (1.4, select_one, required): Select the method of payment (Cash, Credit, PayPal, Bank).
- **Payment Amount** (1.5, number, required): Enter the payment amount, which should match the **Service Price**.
- **Payment Status** (1.6, select_one, required): Select the payment status (Paid, Not Paid).
- **Payment Date** (1.7, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of payment (e.g., when the payment was made or is due).
- **Note** (1.8, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the payment.
