# Wedding Day Playlist Suggestion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Day Playlist Suggestion Form is designed to collect music requests and preferences from wedding guests. The form will help wedding planners or DJs create a customized playlist for the wedding ceremony or reception.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. List the guests you would like to be played for in the "Guest List" field.
2. In the "Music Request" field, enter the name of the song you would like to be played.
3. Select the type of music you prefer (e.g., Acoustic, Dance, Classical, etc.) in the "Song Type" field.
4. In the "Song Name" field, enter the title of the song you requested.
5. Enter the name of the artist performing the song in the "Artist Name" field.
6. If applicable, enter the album name for the song in the "Album Name" field.
7. Enter the release year of the song in the "Release Year" field.
8. Select whether you want to submit or cancel your request.
9. Optionally, add a note for the DJ in the "Note to DJ" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter your mobile number in the "Mobile" field.
12. Select whether or not you want a QR code generated for the song in the "QR Code" field.
13. If generating a QR code is selected, enter the text you'd like to appear on it in the "QR Code Text" field.
14. Select whether or not you want to send an email with the QR code in the "Send Email" field.
15. Finally, enter the text for the QR code in the "Code" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest List** (`guest_list`, text, required): List the guests you would like to be played for. This field is required and will help the wedding planners or DJs know who to prioritize the playlist for.
* **Music Request** (`music_request`, text, required): Enter the name of the song you would like to be played. This field is required and will help the wedding planners or DJs know what songs to include in the playlist.
* **Song Type** (`song_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of music you prefer (e.g., Acoustic, Dance, Classical, etc.). This field is required and will help the wedding planners or DJs know the type of music to play for you.
* **Song Name** (`song_name`, text, required): Enter the title of the song you requested. This field is required and will help the wedding planners or DJs know what song to play.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist performing the song. This field is required and will help the wedding planners or DJs know who is performing the song.
* **Album Name** (`album_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the album name for the song. This field is optional and will only be used if the album information is available.
* **Release Year** (`release_year`, date, required): Enter the release year of the song. This field is required and will help the wedding planners or DJs know the age of the song.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select whether you want to submit or cancel your request. This field is required and will finalize your request.
* **Note to DJ** (`note`, note, optional): Optionally, add a note for the DJ in this field. This field is optional and will only be used if you want to provide additional information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in this field. This field is required and will help the wedding planners or DJs contact you if needed.
* **Mobile** (`mobile`, text, required): Enter your mobile number in this field. This field is required and will help the wedding planners or DJs contact you if needed.
* **QR Code** (`qr_code`, select_multiple, required): Select whether or not you want a QR code generated for the song in this field. This field is required and will help you share the song information with others.
* **Generate QR Code** (`generate_qr`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you want to generate a QR code for the song in this field. This field is required and will help you decide if you want a QR code.
* **QR Code Text** (`qr_code_text`, text, optional): If generating a QR code is selected, enter the text you'd like to appear on it in this field. This field is optional and will only be used if you generate a QR code.
* **Code** (`qr_code_text`, text, required): Enter the text for the QR code in this field. This field is required and will be used to generate the QR code.
* **Send Email** (`send_email`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you want to send an email with the QR code in this field. This field is required and will help you share the QR code with others. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is valid.
* Use the "Generate QR Code" field to decide if you want a QR code generated for the song.
* If generating a QR code is selected, use the "QR Code Text" field to enter the text you'd like to appear on it.
* Use the "Send Email" field to decide if you want to share the QR code with others.
* Enter your email address and mobile number correctly to ensure you can be contacted if needed.
