# Wedding Photo Sharing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from wedding guests about their attendance and photo sharing preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address on the form.
2. Select the number of photos you would like to share.
3. Choose which photos you would like to share (up to 3 photos).
4. Add any additional notes about your photos.
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Attach any files you would like to share (optional).
7. Click "Submit" to send your information.
8. Review your confirmation message to ensure your information was sent correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it should be printed on the wedding invitation.
* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of the wedding (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Photos**: Select the number of photos you would like to share with the wedding planners.
* **Select Photos**: Choose which photos you would like to share with the wedding planners. You can select up to 3 photos.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your photos.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Attach Files**: Upload any additional files you would like to share with the wedding planners (optional).
* **Submit**: Click to send your information to the wedding planners.
* **Confirmation**: Review your confirmation message to ensure your information was sent correctly.
* **Back to Home**: Click to return to the home page.
* **Share Photos**: Select how you would like to share your photos (Facebook or Twitter).
