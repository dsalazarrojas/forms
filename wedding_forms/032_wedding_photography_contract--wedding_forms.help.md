# Wedding Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for wedding photographers to collect necessary details and agreements from clients before starting the photography session. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the engagement and its conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Wedding Details**: Enter the couple's names, the wedding date, and a brief description of the event in the respective fields.
2.  **Photographer Details**: Provide your name and contact information so that the client can easily get in touch with you.
3.  **Contract Type**: Choose the type of contract that suits the engagement, whether it's for the wedding, a pre-event, or a post-event contract.
4.  **Location**: Enter the location of the event.
5.  **Event Details**: Briefly describe the event or session details.
6.  **Contract Start and End Date**: Enter the start and end dates of the contract.
7.  **Payment Details**: Enter the payment information for the session.
8.  **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information for the client.
9.  **Contract Signature**: Sign and date the contract to validate the agreement.
10. **Terms and Conditions**: Enter the terms and conditions of the contract.
11.  **Contract Terms**: Enter any additional terms of the contract.
12.  **Contract Notes**: Enter any additional contract notes.
13.  **Client Review**: Have the client review and approve the contract.
14.  **Photographer Review**: Confirm that you have reviewed the contract.
15.  **Final Check**: Confirm that the contract has been finalized.
16.  **Payment Confirmation**: Confirm that payment has been made or not.
17.  **Contract Status**: Enter the status of the contract, whether it's active, inactive, or expired.
18.  **Contract Renewal**: Indicate whether the contract needs to be renewed or not.
19.  **Contract Cancellation**: Indicate whether the contract needs to be cancelled or not.
20.  **Signature**: The client's signature validates the agreement.
