# Wedding Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering a wedding in our system. It is used to gather information about the couple getting married, including their type of wedding, date, location, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of wedding from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date of the wedding in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter the location of the wedding.
4. Enter the name of the bride.
5. Enter the date of birth of the bride (in MM/DD/YYYY format).
6. Enter the email address of the bride.
7. Enter the name of the groom.
8. Enter the date of birth of the groom (in MM/DD/YYYY format).
9. Enter the email address of the groom.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What kind of wedding 
* **Wedding Type** (Wedding Registration Form, type, required/optional): This field is used to select the type of wedding. Please choose one of the following options:
  * Yes or No.
*Note: This field is not required, but it helps us to understand the type of wedding you are registering.*

### Wedding Date 
* **Wedding Date** (Wedding Registration Form, date, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date of the wedding. Please enter the date in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 02/25/2022). 
*Note: This field is not required, but it helps us to understand the timing of the wedding.*

### Wedding Location 
* **Wedding Location** (Wedding Registration Form, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the location of the wedding. Please enter the full address, including street, city, and state.

### Bride Name 
* **Bride Name** (Wedding Registration Form, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the name of the bride.
