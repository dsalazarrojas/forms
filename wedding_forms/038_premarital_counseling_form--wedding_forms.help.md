# Premarital Counseling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Premarital Counseling Form is a tool used to record important information about premarital counseling sessions. It helps couples and counselors track details about the session, the counselor, the client, and any previous counseling sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you are completing this form because you and your partner are preparing for marriage and have not had previous counseling sessions.
2.  Fill in the required fields to the best of your ability.
3.  If a field is not required, you are not obligated to fill it in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Counseling Session Details (Page 1)** (`counseling_session_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for recording the details of the current premarital counseling session. Please provide any relevant information about what was discussed during the session.
*   **Counselor Name (Page 1)** (`counselor_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for recording the name of the counselor who conducted the session.
*   **Client Name (Page 1)** (`client_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for recording the names of the couple going through the premarital counseling session.
*   **Number of Previous Counseling Sessions (Page 1)** (`number_of_previous_counseling_sessions`, `number`, optional): This field is for recording the number of previous premarital counseling sessions the couple has had.
*   **Number of Previous Counseling Sessions Years (Page 1)** (`number_of_previous_counseling_sessions_years`, `number`, optional): This field is for recording how many years ago the previous counseling sessions occurred.
*   **Date of Previous Counseling Sessions (Page 1)** (`date_of_previous_counseling_sessions`, `date`, optional): This field is for recording the date of the previous counseling sessions.
*   **Time of Previous Counseling Sessions (Page 1)** (`time_of_previous_counseling_sessions`, `time`, optional): This field is for recording the time of the previous counseling sessions.
*   **Reason for Counseling (Page 1)** (`reason_for_counseling`, `text`, optional): This field is not required, but you can use it to record the reason why the couple is going through premarital counseling.
