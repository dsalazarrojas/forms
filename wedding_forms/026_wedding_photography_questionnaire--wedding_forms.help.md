# Wedding Photography Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Photography Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your wedding photography needs and preferences. This form is meant to be a starting point for potential clients to share their ideas and requirements with you, a wedding photographer. Please answer the questions honestly to help us provide the best possible services for your special day.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, simply fill out each section with your details.
*   Start with the General Information section, where you can provide your contact details.
*   Next, move on to the Wedding Details section, where you can share information about your wedding.
*   Then, go to the Photography Services section, where you can specify the services you need from us.
*   After that, answer the Style Preferences section, where you can choose your preferred style of photography.
*   The Photo Preferences section is where you can specify your photo preferences, such as number of shots and editing services.
*   In the Payment Details section, please provide any relevant payment information.
*   In the Review section, you can rate your experience and provide any comments or feedback.
*   Finally, submit your form by clicking the "Submit" button.
