# Beauty and Groom Show Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from vendors or exhibitors about their booth and services offered at the beauty and groom show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Exhibitor Name** in the "Exhibitor Name" field.
2. Enter your **Company Name** in the "Company Name" field.
3. Provide your **Contact Email** in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Fill in your **Business Address** in the "Business Address" field.
6. If you have a **Website**, provide it in the "Website" field.
7. List your **Social Media Handles** in the "Social Media Handles" field.
8. Select your **Vendor Type** from the list (e.g., Bridal Beauty Services, Hair Styling).
9. Choose how **long you have been in business** from the options provided.
10. Select your **booth size preference** from the options (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
11. Indicate whether you **need electricity** for your booth.
12. Check if you **need a table** for your booth.
13. If you need **chairs**, indicate how many you need.
14. Choose the date of the **show you are attending**.
15. Select your **setup time preference** from the options (e.g., Early Morning, Morning).
16. Check if you are bringing **promotional materials** to the show.
17. Indicate if you will be doing **live demonstrations**.
18. Confirm that you understand the **booth fee**.
19. Choose your **payment method** for the booth fee.
20. If you have any **additional comments**, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Name**: This is your full name that will be displayed on our website and event materials.
* **Company Name**: This is your business name that will be displayed on our website and event materials.
* **Email Address**: This is your contact email address where we will send you important updates and information about the event.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number where we can contact you for further information.
* **Business Address**: This is your business address for shipping and other logistical purposes.
* **Website**: If you have a website, please provide it to us so we can check your portfolio.
* **Social Media Handles**: List your social media handles so we can promote your brand on our social media channels.
* **Vendor Type**: Select the category of services you offer (e.g., Bridal Beauty Services, Hair Styling).
* **Years in Business**: How long have you been operating your business.
* **Booth Size Preference**: Choose the size of booth you would like to have at the event.
* **Electricty Needed**: Indicate if you will need electricity for your booth.
* **Table Needed**: Check if you need a table for your booth.
* **Number of Chairs Needed**: If you need chairs, indicate how many.
* **Show Date**: Choose the date of the show you will be attending.
* **Setup Time Preference**: Choose when you would like to start setting up your booth.
* **Promotional Materials**: Check if you are bringing promotional materials to the event.
* **Live Demonstrations**: Indicate if you will be doing live demonstrations.
* **Booth Fee Understood**: Confirm that you understand the booth fee.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you will pay the booth fee.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional information or comments, enter them here.
