# Wedding Day Questionnaire RHWC Updated - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the wedding day details. It is intended for wedding coordinators and planners to gather necessary information for wedding planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and questions carefully.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting to fill out the form.
3. Complete each field according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Wedding Day Details** (`wedding_day_details`, `text`, optional)
Enter a brief description of the wedding day details, such as the wedding ceremony and reception locations, and any notable events or activities planned.

### 2. **Wedding Party** (`wedding_party_details`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Indicate whether the wedding party will be present on the wedding day. Select 'Yes' if the party will be present, or 'No' if not.

### 3. **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, `date`, optional)
Enter the date of the wedding in YYYY-MM-DD format. This will help the wedding planner keep track of the wedding schedule.

### 4. **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, `time`, optional)
Enter the time of the wedding in HH:MM AM/PM format.

### 5. **Ceremony Details** (`ceremony_details`, `text`, optional)
Enter a brief description of the ceremony details, such as the ceremony location and order of events.

### 6. **Reception Details** (`reception_details`, `text`, optional)
Enter a brief description of the reception details, such as the reception location and layout.

### 7. **Wedding Party Attire** (`wedding_party_attire`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the attire of the wedding party. Options include White, Black, Beige, and Other.

### 8. **Wedding Theme** (`wedding_theme`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the theme of the wedding. Options include Modern, Traditional, Vintage, and Other.

### 9. **Dress Code** (`dress_code`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the dress code for the wedding. Options include Formal, Casual, Semi-Formal, and Other.

### 10. **Reception Location** (`reception_location`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the location of the reception. Options include Indoor, Outdoor, Outdoor/Indoor Hybrid, and None.

### 11. **Photo Gallery** (`photo_gallery`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Indicate whether a photo gallery will be present on the wedding day. Select 'Yes' if a photo gallery will be present, or 'No' if not.

### 12. **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional)
Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for contact and coordination of the wedding day.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required in the form schema.
