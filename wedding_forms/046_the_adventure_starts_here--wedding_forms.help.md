# the-adventure-starts-here-wedding-forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for gathering information about a client's wedding details. The form is used to create and manage wedding contracts for a photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Form Data** (`wedding_form_data`): This is the main section of the form where you can enter general information about the wedding.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`): Enter the name of the photographer.
* **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`): Enter the email of the photographer.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the name of the client.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`): Enter the duration of the contract.
* **Photography Services** (`photography_services`): Check if the photographer is offering photography services.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Check the payment method.
* **Photo Delivery Method** (`photo_delivery_method`): Check the photo delivery method.
* **Location** (`location`): Check the location.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`): Add any special instructions.
* **Contract Signature** (`contract_signature`): Check if the contract signature is required.
* **Contract Agreement Terms** (`contract_agreement_terms`): Check the contract agreement terms.
* **Contract Total Hours** (`contract_total_hours`): Enter the total hours of the contract.
* **Contract Terms and Conditions** (`contract_terms_and_conditions`): Check the terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Contract Payment Terms** (`contract_payment_terms`): Check the payment terms of the contract.
* **Contract Signature Date** (`contract_signature_date`): Enter the date of the contract signature.
* **Contract Signature Time** (`contract_signature_time`): Enter the time of the contract signature.
* **Contract Signature Status** (`contract_signature_status`): Check the status of the contract signature.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the tool assigned to the contract.
* **Payment Receipt Confirmation** (`payment_receipt_confirmation`): Check if the payment receipt is confirmed.
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_details`): Check the payment method details.
* **Contract Total Cost** (`contract_total_cost`): Enter the total cost of the contract.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`): Check the status of the contract.
