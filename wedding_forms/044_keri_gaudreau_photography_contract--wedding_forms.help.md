# keri_gaudreau_photography_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from couples planning to get married and provide details about their wedding ceremony and reception.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "bride_info" and "groom_info" fields with your names and contact information.
2. Provide details about your wedding ceremony, including any specific customs, rituals, or activities that you would like to include.
3. Describe your wedding reception, including the date, time, location, and any special details about the venue or decorations.
4. In the "contract_details" field, provide any additional details or requirements for the photographer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **bride_info** (`Bride's Name`, `text`, required): Enter the bride's name, including her maiden name if applicable.
* **groom_info** (`Groom's Name`, `text`, required): Enter the groom's name, including any aliases or nicknames.
* **ceremony_details** (`Wedding Ceremony Details`, `text`, required): Describe the ceremony, including any specific customs, rituals, or activities that you would like to include.
* **reception_details** (`Wedding Reception Details`, `text`, required): Provide details about your wedding reception, including the date, time, location, and any special details about the venue or decorations.
* **contract_details** (`Contract Details`, `text`, required): In this field, you can provide any additional details or requirements for the photographer, such as special requests or limitations.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
