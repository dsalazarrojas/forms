# Wedding RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to RSVP to a wedding event. Please fill out the form to let us know if you are attending and what time you plan to arrive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
- Fill out the form with your contact information and wedding event details.
- Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
- Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
- Enter the date of the wedding in the "Date" field.
- Enter the time of the wedding in the "Time" field.
- Select "True" to indicate that you are attending the wedding, or "False" if you are not.
- If you would like to include a message or note for us, enter it in the "Message" field.
- Finally, submit the form to complete your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Enter the first name of the guest.
- **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter the last name of the guest.
- **Email** (email, email, required): Enter the email address of the guest.
- **Date** (date, date, required): Enter the date of the wedding in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Time** (time, time, required): Enter the time of the wedding in the format 12:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
- **Attending** (attending, select_one, required): Select "True" to indicate that you are attending the wedding, or "False" to indicate that you are not.
- **Message** (message, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments for the wedding. This field is not required.
