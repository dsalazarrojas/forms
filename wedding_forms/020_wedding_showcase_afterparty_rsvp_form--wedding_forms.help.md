# Wedding Showcase Afterparty RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Showcase Afterparty RSVP Form is a tool to help the event organizers manage the guest list and arrangements for the afterparty. Guests can use this form to provide their details and express their intention to attend the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Make sure to fill in all fields marked as "required" to ensure that your RSVP is processed correctly.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be listed for the event.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to RSVP - Active or Inactive. If you choose Active, we will expect you to attend the event.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, optional): If you choose to RSVP Active, please select your expected attendance date.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, optional): If you choose to RSVP Active, please select your expected attendance time.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or need to be contacted, please provide your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have any questions or need to be contacted, please provide your email.
* **Guest Comments** (`guest_comment`, note, optional): If you have any specific requests or comments, please use this field.
* **Type of RSVP** (`rsvp_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of RSVP that best suits your situation.
* **Number of Guests** (`guest_count`, number, optional): If you are attending with others, please specify the number of guests.
