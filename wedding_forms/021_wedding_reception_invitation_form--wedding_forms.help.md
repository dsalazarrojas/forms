# wedding_reception_invitation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details for a wedding reception invitation. It should only be used for genuine wedding reception invitations and not for any other purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Wedding Date** field with the actual date of the wedding.
2. Fill in the **Wedding Time** field with the time of the wedding.
3. Fill in the **Wedding Location** field with the location of the wedding.
4. Fill in the **Reception Date** field with the actual date of the wedding reception.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Date** (`Wedding Date`, date, required: false): Fill in the date of the wedding.
* **Wedding Time** (`Wedding Time`, time, required: false): Fill in the time of the wedding.
* **Wedding Location** (`Wedding Location`, text, required: false): Fill in the location of the wedding.
* **Reception Date** (`Reception Date`, date, required: false): Fill in the date of the wedding reception.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the dates and times you fill in the form to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that the location is correct and easily readable.
* Use a clear and legible font when filling in the form.
* Do not use this form for any other purpose or event.
