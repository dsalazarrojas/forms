# Wedding Decor/Planning Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about wedding decoration and planning preferences from couples getting married. The data collected will help wedding event planners understand their needs and create a personalized and stress-free experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant category for your wedding form, such as "Wedding Forms" or "Other".
2. Provide a brief description of the purpose and context of your wedding decoration and planning process.
3. Choose the tools and services you have assigned to assist with your wedding planning (e.g., chatjimmy, chat jimmy).
4. Enter the ID of the wedding form.
5. Specify the desired output file format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the category that best describes your wedding form (e.g., "Wedding Forms" or "Other").
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a short description of the purpose and context of your wedding decoration and planning process.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the tools and services you have assigned to help with your wedding planning (e.g., chatjimmy, chat jimmy).
* **Form ID** (`form_ids`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID of the wedding form.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required: false): Specify the desired output file format for your wedding decoration and planning information.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and clear information to ensure that your wedding event planner can effectively tailor their services to your needs.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure you've provided the necessary details.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask your wedding event planner for clarification.
