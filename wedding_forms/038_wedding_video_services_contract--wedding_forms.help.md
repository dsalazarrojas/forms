# wedding_video_services_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for couples to document their wedding details for their video services contract.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following fields with your wedding information:
	* Wedding Date
	* Time
	* Couple Name
	* Ceremony Location
	* Reception Location
	* Photographer

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of your wedding. (Type: date, Required: false)
* **Time**: Enter the time of your wedding. (Type: time, Required: false)
* **Couple Name**: Enter the names of the couple getting married. (Type: text, Required: false)
* **Ceremony Location**: Enter the location where the ceremony will take place. (Type: text, Required: false)
* **Reception Location**: Enter the location where the reception will be held. (Type: text, Required: false)
* **Photographer**: Enter the name of your photographer. (Type: text, Required: false)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required information accurately to avoid any confusion with your photographer or wedding planner.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your wedding planner or photographer for clarification.
* This form is for your reference and documentation purposes only, and it's recommended that you review it carefully before signing any contracts.
