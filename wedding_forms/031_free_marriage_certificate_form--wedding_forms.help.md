# Free Marriage Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Free Marriage Certificate Form is used for capturing marriage certificate information for official records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct fields to fill out the form accurately.
2. Enter the correct marriage date under "Marriage Date".
3. Enter the Groom's name, age, and signature under the corresponding fields.
4. Enter the Bride's name, age, and signature under the corresponding fields.
5. Enter the location of the marriage under "Marriage Place".
6. Enter the time of the marriage under "Marriage Time".
7. Enter the details of the witnesses, if any.
8. Enter the signature date under "Signature Date".
9. Enter your signature under "Groom Signature" and "Bride Signature".
10. Enter the notary signature under "Notary Signature".
11. Enter the marriage certificate number and ID under the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marriage Date**: Enter the date of the marriage in a standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Groom Name**: Enter the name of the groom.
* **Groom Age**: Enter the age of the groom.
* **Groom Signature**: Enter your signature as the groom.
* **Bride Name**: Enter the name of the bride.
* **Bride Age**: Enter the age of the bride.
* **Bride Signature**: Enter your signature as the bride.
* **Marriage Place**: Enter the location where the marriage took place.
* **Marriage Time**: Enter the time of the marriage.
* **Witnesses**: Enter details of any witnesses present during the marriage.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Groom Signature**: Enter your signature as the groom.
* **Bride Signature**: Enter your signature as the bride.
* **Notary Signature**: Enter your notary signature.
* **Marriage Certificate Number**: Enter the marriage certificate number.
* **Marriage Certificate ID**: Enter the marriage certificate ID.
