# Wedding Car Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for a wedding car booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required details for the wedding ceremony, including the venue, number of guests, date, and time.
2.  Choose the preferred vehicle type for the occasion.
3.  Specify the color of the wedding car desired.
4.  If applicable, provide additional details such as the number of cars needed and an alternative wedding venue.
5.  Finally, provide the details of the couple, including their names and the desired color of the car.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Wedding Venue** (`wedding_venue`, `text`, required): This field asks for the location of the wedding ceremony.
*   **Wedding Vehicle Type** (`wedding_vehicle_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the preferred type of vehicle for the wedding from the options provided.
*   **Wedding Guests** (`wedding_guests`, `number`, required): This field is used to specify the number of people attending the wedding.
*   **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the wedding.
*   **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, `time`, required): Specify the time of the wedding.
*   **Bride's Name** (`bride_name`, `text`, required): Fill in the name of the bride.
*   **Groom's Name** (`groom_name`, `text`, required): Fill in the name of the groom.
*   **Desired Wedding Car Color** (`desired_color`, `select_one`, required): Choose the color of the wedding car you desire.
*   **Desired Color 2** (`desired_color_2`, `select_one`, required): This field should be used for any alternative or secondary color preference.
*   **Wedding Cars Needed** (`wedding_cars_needed`, `number`, required): Specify the number of cars needed for the wedding procession.
*   **Wedding Time 2** (`wedding_time_2`, `time`, required): If you have an alternative time for the wedding, enter it here.
*   **Wedding Venue 2** (`wedding_venue_2`, `text`, required): If the wedding is taking place at a different venue, enter it here.
*   **Desired Color 3** (`desired_color_3`, `select_one`, required): This field should be used for any other color preferences.
