# Wedding Planner Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Planner Appointment Form is a tool used by wedding planners or couples to schedule appointments for discussing wedding plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the wedding planner you would like to schedule an appointment with from the dropdown list.
2. Enter the date of the wedding you are planning.
3. Enter the time of the wedding you are planning.
4. Enter the phone number of the couple (if applicable).
5. Add any additional notes about the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Planner** (`Select One`): Choose the wedding planner you would like to schedule an appointment with. This dropdown list is populated with names of available wedding planners.
* **Wedding Date** (`Date`): Enter the date of the wedding you are planning.
* **Wedding Time** (`Time`): Enter the time of the wedding you are planning.
* **Couple's Phone Number** (`Text`): Enter the phone number of the couple (if applicable). This can be used by the wedding planner to contact the couple about the appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (`Date`): Choose the date for the appointment with the wedding planner. This should match the wedding date if possible.
* **Appointment Time** (`Time`): Choose the time for the appointment with the wedding planner.
* **Notes** (`Note`): Add any additional notes about the appointment, such as special requests or requirements.
