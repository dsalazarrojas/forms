# Wedding Day Photography Planner Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help wedding photographers plan and prepare for the wedding day photography session. It gathers essential details about the event, including the type of event, venue, time, and other important information.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
* Select the correct options for each field, taking into account the details of the wedding event.
* Make sure to review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of event (e.g., wedding, engagement, anniversary).
* **Wedding Venue** (`wedding_venue`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the venue(s) where the wedding will take place.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the wedding event.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the wedding event.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the wedding event.
* **Parents** (`parents`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether parents will be attending the wedding event.
* **Kids** (`kids`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether kids will be attending the wedding event.
* **Photography** (`photography`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether photography services will be required during the event.
* **Reception Venue** (`reception_venue`, select_one, required: false): Select the venue where the reception will take place.
* **Reception Time** (`reception_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the reception event.
* **Reception Date** (`reception_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the reception event.
* **Reception** (`reception`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether a reception will be held after the wedding event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or information about the wedding event.
* **Bride Email** (`bride_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the bride.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* Use the notes field to add any important details or reminders.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the photographer.
