# wedding_theme_finder_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you discover a wedding theme that suits your wedding style, preferences, and circumstances. Please answer the questions honestly to receive a personalized theme suggestion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions on each page. Each page will ask a series of questions related to different aspects of your wedding. You can select multiple options for each question, and you don't need to answer all questions on all pages. Once you've completed all the pages, click the "Submit" button to receive your wedding theme suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Wedding Theme Finder Quiz** (`title`, `text`, required: `false`): This is the introduction page of the form.
* **What style are you envisioning for your wedding?** (`wedding_style`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Please choose a style that best describes your wedding vision, such as classic, beach, rustic, etc.
* **What's your favorite color scheme?** (`color_scheme`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Select one or more color schemes that appeal to you, such as monochromatic or pastel.
* **What theme elements are you drawn to?** (`theme_element`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Choose one or more theme elements that you like, such as flowers, greenery, or drapes.
* **How many guests are attending your wedding?** (`number_guests`, `number`, required: `true`): Please enter the number of guests you expect to attend your wedding.
* **What date is your wedding?** (`wedding_date`, `date`, required: `true`): Enter the date of your wedding in MM/DD format.
* **What time is your wedding?** (`wedding_time`, `time`, required: `true`): Enter the time of your wedding.
* **Where will the ceremony take place?** (`ceremony_location`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Choose one or more locations for your ceremony, such as garden, beach, or historic site.
* **Where will the reception take place?** (`reception_location`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Select one or more locations for your reception, such as garden, beach, or hotel.
* **What dress code would you like to have for your wedding?** (`dress_code`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Choose one or more dress codes, such as formal, casual, or dressy-casual.
* **What theme would you like your wedding to be?** (`wedding_theme`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Select one or more themes that appeal to you, such as classic, boho, or vintage.
* **What color would you like your wedding theme to be?** (`wedding_color`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Choose a color that matches your theme, such as red, pink, or purple.
* **What kind of decorations would you like at your wedding?** (`wedding_decor`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Select one or more decoration types, such as flowers, greenery, or drapes.
* **What kind of music would you like at your wedding?** (`wedding_music`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Choose one or more music genres, such as pop, rock, or classical.
* **What kind of wedding favors do you like?** (`wedding_favors`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Select one or more favor types, such as small, personal, or small-personal.
* **Is there anything else we should know about our wedding theme?** (`final_question`, `text`, required: `false`): This is an optional text field for any additional information about your wedding theme.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, required: `false`): Click this button to submit your answers and receive your wedding theme suggestion.
