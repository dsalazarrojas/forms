# Wedding Cake Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their wedding preferences, including the date, time, location, and type of their wedding. This information will be used to provide personalized consultations and guidance to help you plan the perfect wedding cake that fits their needs and tastes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form's required fields, such as First Name, Email, and Phone.
2. Provide the details of your wedding, including the Date, Time, Location, and Type of your wedding.
3. Choose the shape and color of your wedding cake from the options provided.
4. Specify the number of guests attending the wedding and whether there will be children present.
5. Select the desired flavor and style of your cake.
6. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share about your wedding preferences.
7. Finally, provide a budget for your wedding cake and any specific tools or equipment you would like to be assigned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`wedding_cake_consultation_form_data`, text, not required): This field is not required but provides space for any additional comments or information you would like to share about your wedding.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your wedding in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of your wedding in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Wedding Location** (`wedding_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of your wedding.
* **Wedding Theme** (`wedding_theme`, text, not required): Enter the theme of your wedding, e.g. beach, garden, etc.
* **Wedding Color** (`wedding_color`, text, not required): Enter the colors you prefer for your wedding cake.
* **Wedding Type** (`wedding_type`, text, not required): Enter the type of wedding you are having, e.g. civil, religious, etc.
* **Wedding Cake Shape** (`wedding_cake_shape`, select multiple, not required): Choose the shape of your wedding cake from the options: round, square, heart, circle.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, not required): Enter the number of guests attending the wedding.
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, number, not required): Enter the number of children attending the wedding.
* **Cake Size** (`cake_size`, text, not required): Enter the size of the cake you are looking for.
* **Cake Taste** (`cake_taste`, text, not required): Enter the flavor of cake you prefer.
* **Cake Flavor** (`cake_flavor`, text, not required): Enter the flavor of cake you prefer.
* **Cake Color** (`cake_color`, text, not required): Enter the color of cake you prefer.
* **Cake Style** (`cake_style`, text, not required): Enter the style of cake you prefer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your wedding preferences.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, not required): Enter your budget for the wedding cake.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, not required): Enter any specific tools or equipment you would like to be assigned.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your wedding preferences.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, not required): This field is not used for your benefit, please do not enter anything.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, not required): This field is not used for your benefit, please do not enter anything.

Note: All fields are not required, but please fill out as much information as possible to help us provide the best service for your wedding cake needs.
