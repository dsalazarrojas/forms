# Wedding Photography Contract Cancellation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a one-time cancellation request form for a wedding photography contract. It is used to collect information from the client to understand the reason for the cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the `first name` field.
2. If you want to be contacted, enter your email address in the `email` field (not required but recommended).
3. Select a date for the `date of cancellation`.
4. Provide a brief explanation for the `reason for cancellation`.
5. Sign and date the `photographer signature` field to confirm your identity and authority to cancel.
6. Sign and date the `client signature` field to confirm your client's identity and acceptance of the cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name** (text, required): This is your full first name.
* **email** (email, optional): This is your email address. If you want to be contacted regarding the cancellation, please enter it here.
* **date of cancellation** (date, optional): Enter the date you want the cancellation to take effect. Please format the date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **reason for cancellation** (text, required): This is a brief explanation for the cancellation. Please provide a clear and concise reason.
* **photographer signature** (note, required): This is where the photographer confirms their identity and authority to cancel the contract. Please sign and date this field to confirm.
* **client signature** (note, required): This is where the client confirms their identity and acceptance of the cancellation. Please sign and date this field to confirm.
