# Wedding Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide wedding planners with essential information about a wedding event, allowing them to create a customized quote for the client. Please complete the form accurately to ensure that the planner can provide an accurate quote for your special day.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Page 1: What is your wedding date**
	* This page asks for the date of your wedding. Enter the date of your wedding ceremony in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
2. **Page 2: What is your ceremony venue**
	* Provide the name and location of the venue where the ceremony will take place.
3. **Page 3: What is the number of guests**
	* Enter the estimated number of guests you expect to attend the wedding.
4. **Page 4: What is your budget**
	* Enter your estimated budget for the wedding planning and coordination services.
5. **Page 5: Which vendor are you looking to get quote from**
	* Select all vendors that you are looking to obtain a quote from (e.g. Wedding Planner, Wedding Cake Baker, Wedding Photographer, etc.).
6. **Page 6: Vendor Contact Information**
	* Enter any contact information (e.g. email, phone number, or social media handle) for the selected vendors.
7. **Page 7: Vendor Quote**
	* This field is not currently used for the form, please proceed to the next page.
8. **Page 8: Vendor Quote Details**
	* Please provide any additional details about the vendor quote you are requesting (e.g. specific services, dates, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your wedding date**: A date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`
* **What is your ceremony venue**: A descriptive text for the venue where the ceremony will take place.
* **what_is_the_number_of_guests**: An estimate of the number of guests attending the wedding.
* **What is your budget**: A number representing your estimated budget for wedding planning and coordination services.
* **Which vendor are you looking to get quote from**: A list of selected vendors to receive quotes from (e.g. Wedding Planner, Wedding Cake Baker, Wedding Photographer, etc.).
* **Vendor Contact Information**: Contact information (e.g. email, phone number, or social media handle) for the selected vendors.
* **Vendor Quote**: This field is not currently used for the form, please disregard.
* **Vendor Quote Details**: Additional details about the vendor quote you are requesting (e.g. specific services, dates, etc.).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have selected all relevant vendors for which you need quotes.
* If you need to add or remove vendors, use the "Select Multiple" feature on page 5.
* If you need to provide additional details about the vendor quote, use page 8.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the "Wedding Quote Request Form" accurately and efficiently, ensuring that you receive a well-crafted quote for your special day.
