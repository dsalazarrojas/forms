# Wedding Ceremony Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from wedding guests who attended the ceremony. Your input will help us improve the overall experience for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information, which includes your first name, email, and event details.
2. Provide your feedback and suggestions on the ceremony.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (Required)
* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your invitation or wedding card.

### Email (Optional)
* **Email**: Your email address to help us contact you in case we need more information about your feedback.

### Phone (Optional)
* **Phone**: Your phone number to help us contact you with any follow-up questions.

### Ceremony Date (Optional)
* **Ceremony Date**: The date of the wedding ceremony you attended.

### Ceremony Time (Optional)
* **Ceremony Time**: The time of the wedding ceremony you attended.

### Event Details (Optional)
* **Event Details**: Please describe the ceremony in as much detail as possible. What was the best part? What could be improved?

### Feedback (Optional)
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts on the ceremony. What did you like or dislike about it?

### Suggestions (Optional)
* **Suggestions**: Do you have any suggestions for future events? We value your input!

## Tips
* Please be honest and specific with your feedback.
* We appreciate your input and will use it to improve the ceremony.
* If you have any photos or videos from the ceremony, feel free to share them with us.
