# Wedding Coordinator Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help wedding coordinators gather essential information from clients about their wedding preferences, budget, and contact details. By filling out this form, clients can communicate their needs and expectations to the wedding coordinator, making it easier to tailor services and provide a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. Be honest and clear in your responses.
3. Review your answers for completeness and consistency.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the wedding coordinator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Preferences (1)**: Describe your wedding preferences, including the type of wedding you're planning (e.g., traditional, non-traditional, cultural, etc.).
* **Budget Information (2)**: Provide a detailed breakdown of your estimated budget for the wedding, including any specific expenses or allocations you'd like to allocate for different aspects of the wedding (e.g., venue, food, decorations, etc.).
* **Contact Details (3)**: Share your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Requirements (4)**: List any specific requirements you have for the wedding, such as number of guests, ceremony location, or any unique needs or requests.
* **Preferences (5)**: Describe any specific preferences you have for the wedding, such as music, flowers, or photography.
* **Budget (6)**: Confirm your estimated budget for the wedding.
* **Dates and Times (7)**: Provide the date and time of the wedding ceremony and reception.
* **Contact Details 1 (8)**: Share any additional contact information, such as an emergency contact or a secondary contact.
 
## Tips

* Be as detailed and specific as possible in your responses.
* If you have any doubts or uncertainties, don't hesitate to reach out to the wedding coordinator.
* This form is meant to be a collaborative process, so feel free to add or modify fields as needed.
* Remember to review and revise your answers as changes to your plans or needs arise.
