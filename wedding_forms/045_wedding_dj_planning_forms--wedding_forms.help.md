# wedding_dj_planning_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for wedding couples to provide their DJ with necessary information for planning their special day. It covers the overall ceremony, cocktail and dinner plans, dance party, and other important details that will help the DJ create a memorable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields according to your wedding details.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly, as this information will help the DJ understand your preferences and plan accordingly.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure that everything is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overview** (`overview`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of your overall wedding plans and expectations.
* **Ceremony** (`ceremony`, `text`, required: false): Details about the ceremony, such as order of events, number of attendees, etc.
* **Cocktail & Dinner** (`cocktail_dinner`, `text`, required: false): Information about the cocktail and dinner plans, including number of guests, food and beverage preferences, etc.
* **Dance Party** (`dance_party`, `text`, required: false): Details about the dance party, including music preferences, lighting and decor plans, etc.
* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Option 1 details
	+ Option 2: Option 2 details
	+ Option 3: Option 3 details
* **Reception Layout** (`reception_layout`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Ballroom
	+ Patio
	+ Outdoor
	+ Cocktail Area
	+ Dance Floor
* **Photo Booth** (`photo_booth`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ True
	+ False
* **Playlist Preferences** (`playlist_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Any additional preferences you have for the playlist, such as specific artists or genres.
* **Song Preferences** (`song_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Genre 1
	+ Genre 2
	+ Genre 3
	+ Genre 4
	+ Genre 5
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): Any special requests you have for the DJ, such as specific songs or dances.
* **Music Preferences** (`music_preferences`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Genre 1
	+ Genre 2
	+ Genre 3
* **Song List** (`song_list`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Playlist Order** (`playlist_order`, `text`, required: false): Any specific order or flow you have for the playlist.
* **Song Length** (`song_length`, `text`, required: false): Any specific song length you prefer.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): The start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): The end time of the event.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): The date of the event.
* **Special Requests 2** (`special_requests2`, `text`, required: false): Any additional special requests you have.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Your contact information for the DJ.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Your phone number.
* **Wedding Dress** (`wedding_dress`, `text`, required: false): Details about the wedding dress.
* **Attire** (`attire`, `text`, required: false): Details about the attire for the event.
* **Parking Info** (`parking_info`, `text`, required: false): Information about parking for the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Any additional notes or comments you have for the DJ.
