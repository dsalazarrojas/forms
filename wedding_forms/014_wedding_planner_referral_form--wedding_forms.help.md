# Wedding Planner Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to refer potential clients to our wedding planning services. As a wedding planner, you have had a positive experience with us and want to share that with your network. This form helps you collect information about your referred clients and their needs, allowing us to follow up and provide them with personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title as the wedding planner.
2. Enter the client's name and contact information.
3. Provide the date of the referral.
4. Briefly describe how you knew us (e.g., through a previous event or online research).
5. Share how we helped you (e.g., with event planning, vendor recommendations, or other services).
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client you are referring.
* **Wedding Planner** (`wedding_planner_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or your company's name as the wedding planner.
* **Vendor** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): If you are referring a client to one of our recommended vendors, enter their name here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you referred the client to us.
* **How did you know us?** (`how_did_you_know_us`, text, optional): Describe how you knew us, for example, through a previous event or online research.
* **How did we help you?** (`how_did_we_help_you`, text, optional): Share how our services helped you with the client, such as event planning, vendor recommendations, or other services.
