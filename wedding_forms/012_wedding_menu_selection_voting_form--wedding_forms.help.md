# Wedding Menu Selection Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from guests attending a wedding event. It's essential to complete the form accurately to ensure a successful and enjoyable event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate through the pages to select the relevant sections for your responses.
2. Fill out each field according to the required and optional fields indicated in the form.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest RSVP (Select Multiple)** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" if you plan to attend the wedding, and "No" if you cannot attend.
* **Guest Name (Text)** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your invitation.
* **Guest Email (Email)** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for any necessary communication.
* **Guest Meal Selections (Select Multiple)** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred meal options for the wedding reception.
* **Guest Message (Note)** (`5`, `note`, optional): If you have any special requests or messages for the planner, type them here.
* **Planner Name (Text)** (`6`, `text`, required): Type in the name of the person who invited you.
* **Event Date (Date)** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the wedding event.
* **Event Time (Time)** (`8`, `time`, optional): If you'd like to specify the time you plan to arrive or a specific time for the event, enter it here.
* **Event Location (Text)** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the wedding event will take place.
* **Planner Message (Note)** (`10`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional messages or requests for the planner.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field according to your personal preferences and circumstances.
* Review your responses carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the event planner for assistance.
