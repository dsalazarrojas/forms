# wedding_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Consultation Form is designed to collect information from wedding clients about their upcoming wedding details. This form helps us understand their preferences and needs, ensuring that we can provide tailored services for a memorable and enjoyable event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your wedding in the `Wedding Date` field. This is where you will specify the date of your special day.
2. Choose the time of your wedding in the `Wedding Time` field. This will help us coordinate our services according to your schedule.
3. Enter the location of your wedding in the `Wedding Location` field. This can be the venue of your wedding or any other specific location that you have in mind.
4. Specify the estimated number of guests that will be attending your wedding in the `Number of Guests` field. This helps us plan for the right amount of supplies and staff.
5. Select the dress code for your event from the `Dress Code` dropdown menu. This will give us an idea of what attire your guests will be wearing.
6. If you have any special requests or requirements for your event, please type them in the `Special Requests` field.
7. Choose the theme of your wedding from the `Wedding Theme` dropdown menu. This will help us understand the style and tone of your event.
8. If you have any wedding favors, please specify them in the `Wedding Favors` field.
9. Enter your name as the client in the `Client Name` field. This is who we will be addressing throughout our communication.
10. Enter your email address in the `Client Email` field. This is how we will contact you with updates and information.
11. Enter your phone number in the `Client Phone` field. This is for any urgent or last-minute communication.
12. If you have a budget in mind for your wedding, please specify it in the `Wedding Budget` field.
13. Provide a brief description of your wedding budget in the `Wedding Budget Description` field.
14. If you have any specific budget allocation, please specify it in the `Wedding Budget Allocation` field.
15. Choose the cuisine for your wedding from the `Wedding Cuisine` dropdown menu. This will help us plan the food and beverages for your event.
16. If you have any specific catering requirements, please type them in the `Wedding Catering` field.
17. Specify the services you require for your wedding in the `Wedding Services` field.
18. If you have any feedback or comments, please type them in the `Client Feedback` field.
19. Provide a brief description of the services you require in the `Wedding Services Description` field.
20. If you have any feedback or comments for the vendor, please type them in the `Vendor Feedback` field.
21. If you have any specific services required from the vendor, please specify them in the `Vendor Services` field.
22. If you have any payment terms or conditions, please type them in the `Payment Terms` field.
23. If you have any specific payment methods, please specify them in the `Payment Method` field.
24. If you have any payment schedules, please specify them in the `Payment Schedule` field.
25. Finally, please specify the total payment amount in the `Payment Total` field.
