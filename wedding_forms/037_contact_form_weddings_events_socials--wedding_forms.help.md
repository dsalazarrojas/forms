# Contact Form Weddings, Events, Socials - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients about their event needs and preferences to assist event planners in providing suitable services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name in the "Client Full Name" field.
2. Provide your primary email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the type of event in the "Type of Event" field.
5. Enter the preferred date for the event in the "Event Date" field.
6. Describe the desired theme or style for the event in the "Event Theme" field.
7. Enter the estimated number of guests for the event in the "Estimated Guest Count" field.
8. Select the realistic budget range for the event in the "Realistic Budget Range" field.
9. Choose the preferred catering service type in the "Catering Preference" field.
10. Provide any dietary restrictions in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
11. Enter any additional information or message for the event planner in the "Your Detailed Message" field.
12. Confirm the date of submission with the current date in the "Date of Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name (Client Full Name, text, required)**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, required)**: Enter your primary email address to ensure efficient communication.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter your best number for the event planner to contact you.
* **Type of Event (Type of Event, select_one, required)**: Choose the type of event (e.g., Wedding, Social Gathering, Corporate Event, Birthday Party, Anniversary, Other).
* **Event Date (Event Date, date, required)**: Enter the preferred date for the event.
* **Event Theme (Event Theme, text, required)**: Describe the desired theme or style for the event.
* **Estimated Guest Count (Estimated Guest Count, number, required)**: Enter the estimated number of people attending the event.
* **Realistic Budget Range (Realistic Budget Range, select_one, required)**: Choose the realistic budget range for the event.
* **Catering Preference (Catering Preference, select_one, required)**: Choose the preferred catering service type (e.g., Buffet Style, Plated Service, Family Style, Cocktail Reception, Food Stations).
* **Any Dietary Restrictions (Dietary Restrictions, text, required)**: Provide any dietary restrictions (e.g., Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free).
* **Your Detailed Message (Your Detailed Message, text, required)**: Enter any additional information or message for the event planner.
* **Date of Submission (Date of Submission, date, required)**: Confirm the date of submission with the current date.
