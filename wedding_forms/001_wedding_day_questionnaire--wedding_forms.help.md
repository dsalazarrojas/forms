# Wedding Day Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Day Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential information about your wedding day. It's intended for couples, wedding planners, or event coordinators to gather necessary details for planning and execution. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete to help create a memorable and enjoyable experience for you and your guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully, and fill out the requested information for each field.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and relevant details for each question.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the explanations below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Requests** (<type:text>): Please provide basic requests for your wedding day, such as special add-ons, if any.
* **Special Addons** (<type:text>): Enter any special add-ons you'd like to have during the wedding, like music, decorations, or other amenities.
* **Ceremonies** (<type:select_one>): Choose whether you'd like to have any ceremonies performed during the wedding, such as a blessing, reading, or song.
* **Contact Details** (<type:text>): Provide your contact information, including email address and phone number.
* **Major Sponsors** (<type:select_multiple>): Choose the major sponsors of your wedding, if applicable.
* **Event Organizers** (<type:select_multiple>): Identify the event organizers, if any.
* **Date** (<type:date>): Enter the date of your wedding in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (<type:time>): Select the time of your wedding.
* **Venue** (<type:text>): Specify the venue where the wedding will take place.
* **Note** (<type:note>): Add any additional comments or details about your wedding.
* **Email** (<type:email>): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (<type:text>): Provide your phone number for any further communication.
