This form is designed to help event planners and coordinators gather essential information about a wedding event. The form is intended to collect data on the key aspects of the wedding, including the wedding date, location, details about the event packages and logistics, contact information, and other essential elements. To avoid duplication, please fill out the form only once for each unique wedding event.

### wedding-event-plan-questionnaire - Help Guide

#### Purpose

The wedding-event-plan-questionnaire is a comprehensive tool for event planners and coordinators to gather and organize information about a wedding event.

#### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form one page at a time, starting from the first page.
2. Ensure each page is completed accurately and thoroughly before moving on to the next page.
3. Review each field carefully and enter relevant information.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Date (page 1)** (`wedding_date`, `date`, required: false): 
Enter the date of the wedding event. This is the date when the wedding ceremony will take place.
* **Wedding Location (page 1)** (`wedding_location`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the location of the wedding event. This could be a venue, hotel, or any other location where the wedding will take place.
* **Wedding Package (page 1)** (`wedding_package`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Select the type of wedding package you are planning to use. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Contact Details (page 1)** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the contact details of the person in charge of the wedding event.
* **People to be Contacted (page 1)** (`people_to_be_contacted`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
Select the individuals who will be contacted in regards to the wedding event. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Wedding Coordinator (page 1)** (`wedding_coordinator`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the name of the wedding coordinator in charge of the event.
* **Family Situations (page 1)** (`family_situations`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
Select the family situations that apply to the wedding event. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Photography (page 1)** (`photography`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Select whether photography will be included in the wedding event. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Wedding Logistics (page 1)** (`wedding_logistics`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the wedding logistics details, such as catering, transportation, etc.
* **Meals (page 1)** (`meals`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
Select the type of meals that will be served during the wedding event. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
