# Tuxedo Fitting Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's tuxedo fitting experience. It's meant to be completed by clients to provide details about their event, size chart, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth and personalized fitting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your event in the "Event Details" field.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the "Client Size Chart" question to indicate if you've had a size chart made or if you're unsure.
4. Enter the date and time of your event in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields, respectively.
5. Specify the location of your event in the "Event Location" field.
6. Add any dress-related details in the "Dress Details" field.
7. Provide your contact information in the "Client Contact Info" field.
8. Leave any additional notes or comments about your event in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Client Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (text, required): Enter your name so that we can properly address you and keep your records.
* **Event Details** (text, required): Provide a brief description of your event, including any important details about the occasion.
* **Client Size Chart** (select_one, optional): Have you had a size chart made for your event? If so, answer "Yes", if not, answer "No".
* **Event Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your event, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Event Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your event, in 12-hour format (e.g., 2:30 PM).
* **Event Location** (text, optional): Specify the location of your event.
* **Dress Details** (text, optional): Add any additional dress-related details you'd like to mention.
* **Client Contact Info** (text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and any other relevant details.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Leave any additional notes or comments about your event.
* **Client Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can keep in touch with you.
