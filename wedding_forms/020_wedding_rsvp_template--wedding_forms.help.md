# Wedding RSVP Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to indicate your response to a wedding invitation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with your information:
  - Enter your name and email address in the **guest_name** and **guest_email** fields.
  - Check the box to indicate if you will **attending**.
  - If attending, select how many **guests_attending** will be accompanying you.
  - If you and your guests plan to **will_attend**, choose "Yes".
  - If you plan to attend but not your guests, choose "No" in **will_attend** and enter the number of **number_of_guests_attending**.
  - Enter any **comments** you may have.
  - If you have children attending with you, select "Yes" in **guests_children_attending**.
  - Enter your estimated **rsvp_at** and **rsvp_time**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Details**: This field is for the wedding details, such as the name of the person who invited you and the date of the wedding. Please leave this field blank.
* **guest_name**: Please enter your full name.
* **guest_email**: Enter your email address. This will help the wedding party contact you with any updates or information.
* **guests_attending**: If you will be bringing guests with you, select the number of guests that will be attending from the dropdown menu.
* **number_of_guests_attending**: If you are not sure how many guests will be with you, but you know that you will be attending, enter the number of guests you expect to bring.
* **will_attend**: Indicate if you and your guests plan to attend the wedding.
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you think would be helpful to the wedding party.
* **rsvp_at**: Enter your estimated date of response. Please format this date as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **rsvp_time**: Enter your estimated time of response. Please format this time as HH:MM AM/PM.
* **guests_children_attending**: If you are bringing children with you, select "Yes".
