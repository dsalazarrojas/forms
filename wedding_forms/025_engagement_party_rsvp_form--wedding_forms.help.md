# Engagement Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help the host gather essential information from guests about their attendance and party details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Host Name**, the name of the person or organization hosting the engagement party.
2. Select the **RSVP Date** for which you are responding.
3. Choose your **RSVP Time** for the party.
4. Provide **Party Details**, such as any special requests or notes about the party.
5. Indicate whether you will be **Attending** (True), **Not Attending** (False), or are unsure (maybe).
6. Enter your **Guest's Name** if you are responding on behalf of another person.
7. Specify your **Guest's Relationship** with the host, such as friend or family member.
8. If applicable, list the **Additional Guests** you are bringing along.
9. If you have a message for the host, enter it in the **Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Host Name**: The name of the person or organization hosting the engagement party.
* **RSVP Date**: The date on which you plan to attend or respond to the party.
* **RSVP Time**: The time at which you plan to arrive or depart from the party.
* **Party Details**: Any special requests or notes about the party you would like to share with the host.
* **Attending**: Indicate whether you will be attending the party by choosing 'True', 'False', or 'Maybe'.
* **Guest's Name**: If you are responding on behalf of another person, enter their name here.
* **Guest's Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the host, such as friend, family member, or colleague.
* **Additional Guests**: List the names of any guests you are bringing along.
* **Message**: If you have any comments or messages for the host, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please respond with your honest answer to attending.
* If you are unsure about attending, choose 'Maybe'.
* If you have any special requests or needs, include them in the Party Details field.
* If you are bringing additional guests, include their names and any relevant details.
* If you have a message for the host, keep it concise and clear.
