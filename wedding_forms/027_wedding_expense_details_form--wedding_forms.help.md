# wedding_expense_details_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wedding expense details form is designed to collect and categorize expenses related to wedding events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vendor information in the "vendor" field.
2. Select the expense date from the calendar picker.
3. Enter the amount of the expense in the "amount" field.
4. Choose one or more categories of the expense from the options in the "category" field.
5. Choose one or more payment methods from the options in the "payment_method" field.
6. Provide notes about the expense in the "notes" field, if necessary.
7. Enter the tool used to record the expense in the "assigned_tool" field, if applicable.
8. Repeat the above steps for each category and payment method as necessary.
