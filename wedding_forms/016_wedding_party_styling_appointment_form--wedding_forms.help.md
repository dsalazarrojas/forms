# Wedding Party Styling Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Party Styling Appointment Form is used to gather information about the party's styling needs and contact details for wedding party planning and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required and optional fields as specified.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Use the options provided in the "Services Needed" field to select the services required for the wedding party.
4. In the "Appointment Date" and "Appointment Time" fields, enter the date and time of the appointment.
5. Use the "Services" field to select whether all services are required.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Contact Report" field to indicate whether you want to receive a report of your contact details.
7. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "Appointment Location" field to select whether an appointment location is required.
8. Enter any additional services details in the "Services Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Party Styling Form** (`party_styling_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can describe the party's styling needs.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the person's name who will be responsible for the party.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the party.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the party.
- **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the appointment.
- **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the appointment.
- **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select the services needed for the party from the provided options.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the appointment.
- **Services** (`services`, select_one, optional): Select whether all services are required.
- **Contact Report** (`contact_report`, select_one, optional): Select whether you want to receive a report of your contact details.
- **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether an appointment location is required.
- **Services Details** (`services_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the services needed.
- **Contact Report** (`contact_report`, select_one, optional): Select whether you want to receive a report of your contact details.
- **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether an appointment location is required.
