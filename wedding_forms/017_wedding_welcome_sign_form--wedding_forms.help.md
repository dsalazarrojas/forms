# Wedding Welcome Sign Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Welcome Sign Form is a crucial tool for wedding organizers to collect essential guest information, facilitating smooth planning and execution of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your Guest Information section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your email address in the Guest Email field.
3. Provide your phone number in the Guest Phone field.
4. Select the date and time of your planned visit in the Event Date and Event Time fields, respectively.
5. Indicate the location where you will be attending in the Event Location field.
6. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your attendance in the Guest Attending field.
7. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to respond to the RSVP question in the RSVP Response field.
8. Provide a brief message in the RSVP Message field if you'd like to share something with the wedding organizers.
9. Enter the number of attendees you are bringing in the Attendee Count field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information** (Required): Please enter your full name.
* **Guest Name** (Required): Enter the name of the person you are registering (if different from your name).
* **Guest Email** (Required): Enter your active email address so we can contact you.
* **Guest Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number for easier contact.
* **Event Date** (Required): Select the date you plan to attend the wedding. 
* **Event Time** (Required): Select the time you plan to visit the wedding.
* **Event Location** (Required): Enter the location of the wedding (e.g., venue name or address).
* **Guest Attending** (Required): Indicate whether you can attend the wedding by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **RSVP Response** (Required): Confirm your RSVP by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **RSVP Message** (Optional): Leave a message for the wedding organizers with your RSVP response.
* **Attendee Count** (Required): Enter the number of people in your party attending the wedding.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- For the Attendee Count field, please enter the number of guests you are bringing with you.
- If you have any special requests or messages for the wedding organizers, please use the RSVP Message field.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, feel free to contact the wedding organizers for assistance.
