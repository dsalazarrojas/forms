# Marriage Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marriage Registration Form is a document that collects essential information from couples and their officiants to record the details of their marriage for legal and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your name and your partner's name in the "couple_info" field.
2.  Enter the details of your wedding, including the date and location, in the "wedding_details" field.
3.  Provide information about the officiant who performed your marriage, including their name and contact details, in the "officiant_info" field.
4.  Sign and date the "signatures" field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
5.  Enter your contact information, including your address and phone number, in the "contact_info" field.
6.  List the names of your witnesses in the "witness_info" field.
7.  Select the date and time of your wedding in the "date_time" field.
8.  Enter any additional information about the ceremony, such as the location or decorations, in the "ceremony_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Couple Information
*   **Couple Info** (`couple_info`, text, required): Enter your name and your partner's name.
### Wedding Details
*   **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, text, required): Enter the date and location of your wedding.
### Officiant Information
*   **Officiant Info** (`officiant_info`, text, required): Enter the name and contact details of the person performing your marriage.
### Signatures
*   **Signatures** (`signatures`, text, required): Sign and date this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
### Contact Information
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your address and phone number.
### Witness Information
*   **Witness Info** (`witness_info`, text, required): List the names of your witnesses.
### Date and Time
*   **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, required): Select the date and time of your wedding.
### Ceremony Information
*   **Ceremony Info** (`ceremony_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the ceremony, such as the location or decorations.
