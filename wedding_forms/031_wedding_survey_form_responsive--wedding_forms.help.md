# Wedding Survey Form Responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about potential attendees' ability to attend your wedding. It's meant to be a simple and user-friendly way to gather RSVPs and any additional comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Will they be able to attend your wedding?" with either "True", "False", or "unsure" from the dropdown menu.
2. If you have any additional notes or comments, type them in the "Note" field. This is an optional field.
3. If you want to provide your contact information, enter your email address in the "Email" field and/or your phone number in the "Phone" field. Both fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Will they be able to attend your wedding?** (`wedding_survey_form_responsive_answer`, select_one, required: false): Answer this question with either "True", "False", or "unsure" from the dropdown menu. This field is used to gather information about your guests' plans.
* **Note** (`wedding_survey_form_responsive_note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or notes, type them in this field. This field is optional and only for personal messages or reminders.
* **Email** (`wedding_survey_form_responsive_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you want to provide your contact information. This field is optional.
* **Phone** (`wedding_survey_form_responsive_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you want to provide your contact information. This field is optional.
