# Wedding Shot List Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Shot List Worksheet is a tool for wedding planners and photographers to plan and organize the shots required for a wedding. It helps them capture all the essential moments and shots for a seamless and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a category for the wedding shot list, such as "Wedding Ceremony," "Reception," or "Honeymoon."
2. Enter the venue where the wedding will take place.
3. Select the photographer(s) responsible for capturing the shots.
4. Choose the attire for the ceremony and photographer.
5. Determine the number of guests and the photo location(s) for the shots.
6. Decide on the number of hours and travel time required for the shoot.
7. Provide contact information for the wedding planner and photographer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a category for the wedding shot list, such as "Wedding Ceremony," "Reception," or "Honeymoon."
* **Venue** (`venue`, `text`, required: false): Enter the venue where the wedding will take place.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the photographer(s) responsible for capturing the shots.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Ceremony Attire** (`ceremony_attire`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the attire for the ceremony, such as "Formal," "Semi-formal," "Casual," or "Other (Specify)."
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required: false): Determine the number of guests attending the wedding.
* **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the location(s) for the photos, such as "Outdoor" or "Indoor."
* **Photographer Attire** (`photographer_attire`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the attire for the photographer(s), such as "Formal," "Casual," or "Other (Specify)."
* **Number of Hours** (`number_of_hours`, `number`, required: false): Determine the number of hours required for the shoot.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, `number`, required: false): Enter the travel time required for the shoot.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number for the wedding planner and photographer.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact email for the wedding planner and photographer.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the wedding planner and photographer.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information or message for the wedding planner and photographer.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and adjust the shot list regularly to ensure everything is captured.
* Use the contact information to reach out to the wedding planner and photographer with any updates or questions.
* Use the form as a starting point and adjust as needed for each wedding.
