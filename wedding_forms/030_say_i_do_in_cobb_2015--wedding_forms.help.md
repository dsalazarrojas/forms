# Say I Do In Cobb 2015 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from bridal show event organizers to provide a comprehensive overview of the events. The collected data will be used to create a directory or database of bridal show events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the relevant information about your bridal show event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bridal Show Event Info** (1, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter a brief description or title of the bridal show event.
* **Vendor Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the vendor participating in the event.
* **Vendor Type** (3, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following options to indicate the type of vendor (Vendor, Wedding Planner, Florist).
* **Contact Information** (4, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information for the vendor or event organizer.
* **Email** (5, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the vendor or event organizer.
* **Vendor Services** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or multiple services provided by the vendor (e.g. services1, services2, services3).
* **Wedding Planner** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the wedding planner associated with the event.
* **Notes** (8, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the event or vendor.
