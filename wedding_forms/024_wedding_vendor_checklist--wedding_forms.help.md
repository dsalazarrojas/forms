# Wedding Vendor Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Vendor Checklist form is designed to help wedding planners and coordinators track and manage information about their wedding vendors. This includes contact details, rates, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the vendor's name in the "What is the vendor name" field.
2. Select the type of vendor (Caterer, Florist, or Photographer) from the "Vendor Type" dropdown menu.
3. Fill in the "Contact Info" field with any additional notes or comments about the vendor.
4. Enter the wedding date in the "Wedding Date" field.
5. Fill in the "Vendor Website" field with the vendor's website URL.
6. Enter the vendor's phone number in the "Vendor Phone" field.
7. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the vendor's email address in the "Vendor Email" field.
9. Update the "Follow Up" field to indicate if you need to follow up with the vendor (Yes/No).
10. Select the vendor's status (Active, Inactive, Cancelled) from the "Vendor Status" dropdown menu.
11. Enter the vendor's address in the "Vendor Address" field.
12. Enter the wedding time in the "Wedding Time" field.
13. Enter the vendor's rate in the numerical "Vendor Rate" field.
14. Select the vendor's rating (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) from the "Vendor Rating" dropdown menu.
15. Enter the follow-up date in the "Follow Up Date" field.
16. Fill in any additional message or comment in the "Vendor Message" field.
17. Enter any additional message or comment in the "Vendor Message Phone" field.
18. Fill in any additional notes or comments about the vendor in the "Vendor Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the vendor name**: Enter the name of the vendor, such as "Smith Catering".
* **Vendor Type**: Select the type of vendor, e.g., Caterer, Florist, or Photographer.
* **Contact Info**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the vendor.
* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Vendor Website**: Enter the vendor's website URL.
* **Vendor Phone**: Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the vendor.
* **Vendor Email**: Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Follow Up**: Indicate if you need to follow up with the vendor (Yes/No).
* **Vendor Status**: Select the vendor's status (Active, Inactive, Cancelled).
* **Vendor Address**: Enter the vendor's address.
* **Wedding Time**: Enter the wedding time.
* **Vendor Rate**: Enter the vendor's rate.
* **Vendor Rating**: Select the vendor's rating (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the follow-up date.
* **Vendor Message**: Fill in any additional message or comment about the vendor.
* **Vendor Message Phone**: Enter any additional message or comment about the vendor (phone number).
* **Vendor Note**: Fill in any additional notes or comments about the vendor.
