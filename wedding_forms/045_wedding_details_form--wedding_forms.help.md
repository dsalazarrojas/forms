# Wedding Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for a wedding event, including venue, date, and number of guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each text field in sequence, from **Wedding Details Form** to **Wedding Details Form Part 9**.
2. Enter your text responses in the provided fields.
3. Review your answers before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Details Form** (required/optional): Enter a brief description of the wedding event.
* **Wedding Details Form Part 1** (optional): Enter the venue's name or address.
* **Wedding Details Form Part 2** (optional): Enter the date of the wedding event.
* **Wedding Details Form Part 3** (optional): Enter the number of guests attending.
* **Wedding Details Form Part 4** (optional): Enter any special events or activities planned.
* **Wedding Details Form Part 5** (optional): Enter the dress code for the event.
* **Wedding Details Form Part 6** (optional): Enter any additional details or instructions.
* **Wedding Details Form Part 7** (optional): Enter the contact information of the wedding coordinator.
* **Wedding Details Form Part 8** (optional): Enter any other relevant information.
* **Wedding Details Form Part 9** (optional): Enter any final notes or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field in sequence.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
