# Wedding Dress Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you find the perfect wedding dress based on your style, preferences, and budget. It will ask you to select your preferred style, neck line, sleeve length, fabric type, color scheme, wedding dress designer, price range, special features, favorite style, dress length, wedding day, preferred dress color, and price.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select your preferred style of wedding dress from the options provided.
* Choose your preferred neck line from the options provided.
* Select your preferred sleeve length from the options provided.
* Choose your preferred fabric type from the options provided.
* Select your preferred color scheme from the options provided.
* Choose your preferred wedding dress designer from the options provided.
* Select your price range from the options provided.
* Choose any special features you would like your wedding dress to have.
* Select your favorite style of wedding dress.
* Choose your preferred dress length from the options provided.
* Select a date for your wedding day (if applicable).
* Choose your preferred dress colors.
* Select a price for your wedding dress (if applicable).
* Finally, confirm your favorite wedding dress designer.
