# Engagement Story Session Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an Engagement Story Session booking. The goal is to schedule and prepare for a unique and intimate storytelling experience for couples to share their love and commitment to each other.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred dates for the session from the list of options.
2. Provide your name and any additional details regarding the session.
3. If you have any special requests or instructions, please let us know in the "Manage Bookings" field.
4. Fill in the "Photographer Details" field with the name and contact information of the photographer assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **session_details_label (Text)** (`session_details`, Optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information or notes about the session. This can include any specific requests, preferences, or questions you may have.
* **scheduled_dates_label (Select Multiple)** (`scheduled_dates`, Optional): Select one or more days for the session from the list of options. Please note that the availability of dates may vary depending on the photographer's schedule.
* **manage_bookings_label (Text)** (`manage_bookings`, Optional): If you have any special requests or instructions for the session, please provide them here. This can include anything from location changes to special equipment requirements.
* **photographer_details_label (Text)** (`photographer_details`, Optional): Fill in the name and contact information of the photographer assigned to you. This will help us to keep in touch with them and ensure a smooth coordination for the session.
