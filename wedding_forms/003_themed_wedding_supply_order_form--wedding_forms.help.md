# themed_wedding_supply_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a themed wedding event. It helps event planners gather necessary details to tailor the event according to the couple's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully** and fill in the information as needed.
2. **Choose your wedding theme** from the dropdown menu (page 1: Wedding Theme Choices).
3. **Describe your chosen theme** in a few sentences (page 2: Theme Description).
4. **Tell us how many guests** will be attending the wedding (page 3: How many guests are attending?).
5. **Provide the wedding date** (page 4: Wedding Date).
6. **Choose the wedding time** (page 5: Wedding Time).
7. **Enter the wedding budget** (page 6: Wedding Budget).
8. **Add any additional notes** about your event (page 7: notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Wedding Theme Choices

* **Wedding Theme Choices** (`select_one`): Choose the theme of your wedding from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the overall ambiance and style of your event.

### Page 2: Theme Description

* **Theme Description** (`text`): Briefly describe the chosen theme in a few sentences. This will help us get a better understanding of your vision.

### Page 3: How many guests are attending?

* **How many guests are attending?** (`select_multiple`): Choose the number of guests attending the wedding. This will help us plan accordingly for the event.

### Page 4: Wedding Date

* **Wedding Date** (`date`): Provide the date of the wedding. This will help us plan the schedule and timing of the event.

### Page 5: Wedding Time

* **Wedding Time** (`time`): Choose the time of the wedding. This will help us coordinate the start and end times of the event.

### Page 6: Wedding Budget

* **Wedding Budget** (`number`): Enter the budget for the wedding. This will help us ensure that we stay within your budget for the event.

### Page 7: notes

* **notes** (`note`): Add any additional notes or comments about the wedding. This is a free text field where you can provide any extra information that's relevant to your event.
