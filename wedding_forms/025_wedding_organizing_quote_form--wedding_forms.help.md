# Wedding Organizing Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a couple's wedding planning needs, including details about their event, the services they require, and their contact information. This information will be used by our team to provide a quote for our wedding organizing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information, including your preferred method of contact (e.g. email, phone, or mail).
2. Provide details about your wedding date and location.
3. Select the services you would like us to provide for your wedding (e.g. wedding planning, coordination, design, photography, videography, DJ, music, decor, catering).
4. Enter the estimated budget for your wedding.
5. Provide the expected number of guests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Couple Details** (`couple_details`): A brief description of your wedding and you as a couple.
* **Services Requested** (`wedding_services_requested`): Select which services you would like us to provide for your wedding (e.g. wedding planning, coordination, design, photography, videography, DJ, music, decor, catering).
* **Budget** (`budget`): Enter your estimated budget for the wedding.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`): The date you would like to have your wedding.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`): Enter the estimated number of guests you expect to attend your wedding.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`): Select your preferred method of contact (e.g. email, phone, or mail).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address.
* **Message** (`message`): Any additional information about your wedding you would like to share with us.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`): Any other details or requests you have for your wedding.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): The tool you would like us to use to manage your event (e.g. Google Calendar, Asana, etc.).
* **Assigned Team** (`assigned_team`): The team members you would like us to work with on your wedding.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): The person creating this form (not editable).
