# wedding_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wedding RSVP form is a crucial tool used by wedding organizers to gather information from guests about their intentions, contact preferences, and special requests for the wedding. This form helps the organizers understand who is coming, how to contact them, and what special requirements they may need to be accommodated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the guest's name in the **Guest Name** field.
2.  Indicate the number of guests attending in the **Guest Number** field.
3.  Select the **RSVP Status** that best describes your intention to attend the wedding.
4.  Choose your preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, Text/SMS, Other) in the **How you'd like to get in touch with us** field.
5.  If you have any comments or special requests, enter them in the **Any comments** field.
6.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
7.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
8.  If you have any special requests (dietary restrictions, special accommodations, etc.), select the relevant options in the **Special Requests** field.
9.  If you have any additional comments about your special requests, enter them in the **Special Requests Comment** field.
10. Make sure to enter your dietary restrictions in the **Dietary Restrictions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Guest Name**: This field is used to record the name of the guest.
    *   Required field.
*   **Guest Number**: This field is used to indicate the number of guests attending the wedding.
    *   Required field.
*   **RSVP Status**: This field allows you to select your intention to attend the wedding.
    *   Options: Not Attending, Attending, May be able to attend, Maybe attending, Attending as Guest of another couple.
    *   Required field.
*   **How you'd like to get in touch with us**: This field allows you to select your preferred method of contact.
    *   Options: Phone, Email, Text/SMS, Other.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Any comments**: This field is used to enter any comments or special requests.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Email**: This field is used to enter your email address.
    *   Required field.
*   **Phone**: This field is used to enter your phone number.
    *   Required field.
*   **Special Requests**: This field allows you to select any special requests (dietary restrictions, special accommodations, etc.) you may have.
    *   Options: Dietary restrictions, Special Accommodations, Other (please specify below), None.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Special Requests Comment**: This field is used to enter any additional comments about your special requests.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Dietary Restrictions**: This field is used to enter any dietary restrictions you may have.
    *   This field is not required.
