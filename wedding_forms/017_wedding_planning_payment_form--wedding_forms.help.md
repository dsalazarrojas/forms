# Wedding Planning Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information for wedding planning services. Please complete the form accurately to ensure smooth payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and honestly.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the available payment methods: Credit Card, E-Check, or PayPal.
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount to be paid for the wedding planning services.
* **Vendor** (`vendor`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name of the vendor or service provider.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.

## Tips
- Ensure accurate and secure payment processing by double-checking your submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
- Review and update your payment information as needed.
