# Standard Wedding Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential details from wedding clients, allowing the photographer to understand their requirements for a successful and enjoyable wedding photography experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the date of the ceremony in the "Ceremony Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter the time of the ceremony in the "Ceremony Time" field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
7. Enter the location of the ceremony in the "Ceremony Location" field.
8. Enter the date of the reception in the "Reception Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
9. Enter the time of the reception in the "Reception Time" field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
10. Enter the location of the reception in the "Reception Location" field.
11. Select the desired package options for your wedding photography services by checking the boxes next to "Package Option 1", "Package Option 2", and "Package Option 3".
12. Read and agree to the terms and conditions by typing "Yes" in the "Terms and Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Ceremony Date** (date): Enter the date of the ceremony in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Ceremony Time** (time): Enter the time of the ceremony in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Ceremony Location** (text): Enter the location of the ceremony.
* **Reception Date** (date): Enter the date of the reception in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Reception Time** (time): Enter the time of the reception in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Reception Location** (text): Enter the location of the reception.
* **Package Option 1** (select_multiple): Select "Yes" if you want to include this package option in your wedding photography services.
* **Package Option 2** (select_multiple): Select "Yes" if you want to include this package option in your wedding photography services.
* **Package Option 3** (select_multiple): Select "Yes" if you want to include this package option in your wedding photography services.
* **Terms and Conditions** (text): Type "Yes" to indicate that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
