# Wedding Invitation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Invitation Form is a tool for collecting details related to a wedding invitation, including venue, event date, reception time, and other relevant information. This form helps to gather all the necessary details to create a comprehensive and organized wedding invitation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the wedding details such as venue, event date, and reception time.
* Select the wedding invitation template and template designer (if applicable).
* Provide the contact number and event location.
* Enter any additional notes for the wedding invitation.
* Specify the status of the invitation (Active or Inactive).
* If applicable, select the designer email and published date and time.
* Finally, review and submit the form to ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Details**: This field is for entering the details of the wedding, such as the venue, event date, and reception time.
* **Venue**: Enter the location of the wedding ceremony and reception.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the wedding event.
* **Reception Time**: Enter the time of the wedding reception.
* **Wedding Invitation Template**: Select whether a template is to be used for the wedding invitation.
* **Template Designer**: Select whether a designer is involved in creating the invitation template.
* **Designer Email**: Enter the email of the designer (if applicable).
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for the wedding party.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Wedding Invitation Note**: Enter any additional notes or details for the wedding invitation.
* **Published By**: Select the person publishing the invitation.
* **Published Date**: Enter the date the invitation was published.
* **Published Time**: Enter the time the invitation was published.
* **Published By Designer**: Select the designer who published the invitation.
* **Published By Email**: Enter the email of the designer who published the invitation.
* **Wedding Invitation Status**: Select the status of the invitation (Active or Inactive).
