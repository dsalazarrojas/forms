# Home Office Setup Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is here to help you understand every step of the Home Office Setup Giveaway Entry process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you're using a valid email address as we'll be contacting you with updates on this giveaway.
- If you've already submitted an entry, please don't resubmit.
- Take your time, fill out the form accurately, and don't hesitate to ask if you need further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Home Office Setup Giveaway Entry Form (Home Office Setup Giveaway Entry Form, text, required)**: This is the main entry point for your information. Enter your response in the required format specified.
*   **Name (Name, text, required)**: Please provide your name as it appears on your official documents. This will help us verify your identity for the purpose of this giveaway.
*   **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you with updates, reminders, and any further information about this giveaway.
*   **Phone (Phone, text, optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your number. This is not required, and we'll only use it if necessary.
*   **Work From Home (Work From Home, select one, optional)**: Select your current work setup from the provided options: Home Office, Remote, Other, or Undisclosed.
*   **Why (Why, select one, optional)**: Please choose why you work from the selected option in "Work From Home" above.
*   **Insights (Insights, note, optional)**: Share your insights or thoughts about your current work setup. This is an optional field for any additional information you'd like to provide.
*   **Interested (Interested, select multiple, required)**: Indicate your level of interest in the giveaway by selecting one or more of the options: Interested, Somewhat, Not Interested.
*   **Preferred (Preferred, select multiple, optional)**: Select your preferred setup options for your home office. Choose from: Ergonomic Office Furniture, Standing Desk, Headset, External Monitor, White Noise Cancellation.
*   **Additional Notes (Additional Notes, note, optional)**: If there's anything you'd like to share or add that's not covered in the above fields, use this space to do so.
