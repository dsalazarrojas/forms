# Twitch Giveaway Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants who want to join a giveaway event on Twitch. Fill out this form to provide your information and be entered into the giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
3. Enter your Twitch username in the field "Twitch Username".
4. Enter your Discord username in the field "Discord Username".
5. Enter your channel name in the field "Channel Name".
6. Select your preferred entry method from the options "Giveaway" or "Drop" in the field "Entry Method".
7. You can add any additional tags related to your participation in the field "Discord Tags".
8. Optionally, add any additional tags you would like to be associated with you in the field "Tags".
9. Finally, select your Timezone in the field "Timezone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your Twitch account or any other email address you'd like to be contacted at.
* **Twitch Username**: Enter your username on the Twitch platform, which is usually your unique identifier.
* **Discord Username**: Enter your username on the Discord platform, which is usually your unique identifier.
* **Channel Name**: Enter the name of your Twitch channel, which can be the name of your stream or any other channel you're associated with.
* **Entry Method**: Select how you want to be entered into the giveaway. "Giveaway" means you'll be randomly selected, and "Drop" means you'll receive a notification and you'll be able to claim your prize.
* **Discord Tags**: If you have any specific Discord roles or groups that you're a part of, add them here.
* **Tags**: Optionally add any other tags or categories you'd like to be associated with.
* **Timezone**: Select your Timezone from the list of options, which is usually based on your geographical location.
