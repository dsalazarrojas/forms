# Easter Giveaway Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Giveaway Form is a survey designed to collect personal and demographic information from users for marketing and promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3.  Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4.  Select your Birthday in the Birthday field.
5.  Choose your Preferred Language from the dropdown options.
6.  Select your Preferred Location from the dropdown options.
7.  Enter your Favorite Food in the Favorite Food field.
8.  Select your Favorite Color from the dropdown options.
9.  Select your preferred terms and conditions from the dropdown options.
10. Fill out your First 100, Last 100, First Thing, and Last Thing fields.
11. Select a Time for your Time field.
12. Fill out your Decimal field.
13. Select Multiple Thing 1, Multiple Thing 2, Multiple Thing 3, Multiple Thing 4, and Multiple Thing 5 from the dropdown options.
14. Select One Thing from the dropdown options.
15. Select Yes or No for the Checkbox Thing.
16. Select Multiple Thing 2, Multiple Thing 3, Multiple Thing 4, and Multiple Thing 5 from the dropdown options.
