# Support Code Giveaway Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who would like to register for a support code giveaway. Fill out the form with the required information and submit it to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as indicated by the asterisk (*) next to each field.
2. Select the required options from the Select One and Select Multiple fields.
3. Ensure that the email and phone fields are correctly formatted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Giveaway Form (id: 1)**: This field is a text field where you can enter any relevant information to support your registration.
* **support_code (id: 2)**: This field is not actually used in the form, it seems to be a leftover field. You can ignore it.
* **name (id: 3)**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **email (id: 4)**: Enter your email address that you use to access your account.
* **phone (id: 5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **date (id: 6)**: Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **time (id: 7)**: Enter the time in 24-hour format.
* **Select One (id: 8)**: Select one of the available options from the dropdown menu. This field is required.
* **Select Multiple (id: 9)**: Select one or more of the available options from the dropdown menu. This field is not required.
* **note (id: 10)**: This field is for any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Email Confirmed (id: 11)**: Enter a confirmation of your email address.
* **Phone Confirmed (id: 12)**: Enter a confirmation of your phone number.
* **Date Confirmed (id: 13)**: Enter the date you confirm.
* **Time Confirmed (id: 14)**: Enter the time you confirm.

Please note that the fields with an ID that is an even number are not required, you can fill them out if you have the information but it's not mandatory.
