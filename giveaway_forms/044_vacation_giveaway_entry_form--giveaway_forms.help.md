# vacation-giveaway-entry-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vacation giveaway entry form is designed to collect information from individuals who would like to participate in a vacation giveaway contest. By filling out this form, users can enter their details, which will be used to determine the winner of the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Submit" button to begin the process.
- Fill out your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, Date of Birth, Address, Country, City, State, Zip Code fields, which are all located on the first section of the form.
- In the "Which destination" field, select the preferred destination for the vacation.
- Ensure you have filled out all the required fields correctly, as indicated by the asterisks (\*) in the field labels. Note that this form is not mandatory, so users can choose to fill it out partially.
- Click on the "Submit" button to finalize your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that can be used for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country where you reside.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Which destination** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select a destination from the options provided.

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields correctly before submitting.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* The selected destination will be used to determine the winner of the contest, so choose wisely.
