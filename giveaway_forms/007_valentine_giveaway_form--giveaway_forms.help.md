# Valentine Giveaway Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Valentine Giveaway Form is designed to collect information for a Valentine's Day event. This form helps us understand your interest and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your First Name in the text box.
2. Enter your Last Name in the text box.
3. Provide your Email address in the email format.
4. If you would like to be contacted about this event, enter your Phone number in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is known to you. This will help us personalize our communication with you.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is known to you. This is for our records and helps us identify you correctly.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for updates and information about the event.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): If you want us to contact you about this event, enter your phone number here. Please note that we will not share your phone number with anyone.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting, as this will help us get in touch with you correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
