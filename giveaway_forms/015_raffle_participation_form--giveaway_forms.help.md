# Raffle Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Raffle Participation Form is designed to collect participant information and the amount contributed by each participant in a raffle drawing. This form is used to track and verify participant details and contributions. Please ensure you only submit the form once to avoid duplication of efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Raffle Participation Form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the participant information on the first page.
* Enter the amount contributed on the second page.
* Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (Participant Information)
* **Participant Information (Participant Information)** (`participant_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details as required.

### Amount Contributed (Amount Contributed)
* **Amount Contributed (Amount Contributed)** (`amount_contributed`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount contributed by you to the raffle drawing. This amount will be used for the participant's entry into the drawing.

### Contact Information (Contact Information)
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information or comments about your participation. This field is optional and can include your phone number, address, or any other details you think will help us contact you if you win.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the designated raffle administrators.
* You can only submit this form once to avoid creating duplicate entries in the raffle drawing.
