# Health Product Raffle Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Health Product Raffle Entry" form is used to collect information from users who want to participate in a raffle for a health product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field.
2. Provide your **Goals** in the text field (optional).
3. Enter your valid **Email** address in the format "example@example.com".
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the format "123-456-7890".
5. Enter your **Age** in the format "XX", where "XX" represents the number of years you are.
6. Select your preferred **Product** from the dropdown list.
7. Select your **Country** from the dropdown list.
8. Choose your preferred **Address** from the dropdown list.
9. Enter your **Date** of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
10. Enter your preferred **Time** of birth in the format "HH:MM:SS".
11. (Optional) Enter any additional **Note** about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Goals** (text, optional): Enter any health-related goals or objectives you have.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter a valid phone number in the format "123-456-7890".
* **Age** (number, required): Enter your age in the format "XX", where "XX" represents the number of years you are.
* **Product** (select one, optional): Choose the health product you are interested in.
* **Country** (select one, optional): Choose the country you are from.
* **Address** (select multiple, required): Choose all the addresses that apply to you.
* **Date** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Time** (time, required): Enter your time of birth in the format "HH:MM:SS".
* **Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.
