# Winter Wellness Bundle Giveaway Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the Winter Wellness Bundle Giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select if you would like to enter the giveaway by choosing between "Yes" and "No".
2. Enter your name in the provided text field.
3. Enter your email address in the provided email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the provided text field.
5. Specify your date of birth in the provided date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Giveaway Entry** (`giveaway_entry`, select_multiple, not required): Select whether you would like to enter the giveaway.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY, where MM is the month, DD is the day, and YY is the year.
