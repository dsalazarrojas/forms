# smoky_mountains_giveaway_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for a giveaway related to the Smoky Mountains.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.
2. Please fill out the form with your accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (`first_name`, text, **optional**): Please enter your first name as it is displayed on your identification.
* **Last Name (`last_name`, text, **optional**): Please enter your last name as it is displayed on your identification.
* **Email (`email`, email, **optional**): Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, **optional**): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date (`date`, date, **optional**): Please enter a date of your visit or interest for the Smoky Mountains.
* **Time (`time`, time, **optional**): Please enter the time of your visit or interest for the Smoky Mountains.
* **Note (`note`, note, **optional**): Please enter any additional information or comments about your visit or interest.
* **What do you like about the Smoky Mountains? (`select_one`, select_one, **optional**): Select one of the options provided (Scenic views, Hiking trails, Water falls).
* **Select all that apply (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select all the options that apply to you, such as Scenic views, Hiking trails, Water falls.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your participation in this giveaway and look forward to hearing from you!
