# Clothing Collection Giveaway Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your clothing preferences and needs for a giveaway opportunity. Please answer the questions accurately to ensure that the giveaway items are suitable for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the information requested.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (Participant Name): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **-- Clothing Collection Preferences --**: This section outlines your general clothing preferences.
* **Your Style Preference**: Choose the type of clothing you prefer, such as Classic and timeless or Contemporary and trendy.
* **Clothing Categories**: Select the categories of clothing that interest you, like Everyday wear or Accessories.
* **Size Range**: Choose your size range, such as XS to M or L to XL.
* **Prefered Colors or Patterns**: Enter any preferred colors or styles you have.
* **-- Brand and Designer Preferences --**: This section outlines your brand and designer preferences.
* **Are you interested in specific brands or designers?**: Choose Yes, have favorites or No specific preference.
* **List any favorite brands**: Enter any favorite brands you have.
* **-- Condition and Quality --**: This section outlines your condition and quality expectations.
* **Preferred Condition of Items**: Choose Like new or gently used or Any condition.
* **Quality Expectations for giveaway items**: Choose High quality, excellent brands or Any quality acceptable.
* **-- Special Requests --**: This section outlines any special requests you have.
* **Are you seeking any specific items?**: Enter any specific items you are seeking, such as a suit or seasonal items.
* **Do you need clothing for special occasions?**: Choose Yes, job interview or Yes, wedding or formal event.
* **-- Impact and Purpose --**: This section outlines how you will use the clothing.
* **How will these clothes benefit you?**: Choose Career transition or job search or Building professional wardrobe.
* **How important is having quality clothing to your confidence?**: Choose Very important or Important.
* **-- Logistics and Preferences --**: This section outlines your logistics and preferences.
* **Preferred pickup method**: Choose In-person pickup or Local delivery or Shipping to address.
* **When are you available for pickup or delivery?**: Enter any available dates or timeframe.
* **Any additional preferences or requirements?**: Enter any additional preferences or requirements you have.
* **May we contact you about this giveaway opportunity?**: Choose Yes, please contact or By email only or No further contact.

Note: The form will only work properly if it is filled out once per participant to avoid duplication and ensure accuracy.
