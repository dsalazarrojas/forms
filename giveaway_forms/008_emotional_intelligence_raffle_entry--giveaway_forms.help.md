# emotional_intelligence_raffle_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "emotional_intelligence_raffle_entry" form is a tool to collect participant information and comments for an emotional intelligence raffle. The form is designed to gather details from both participants and a single winner, promoting emotional intelligence within a specific organization or community.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Participant Reflection**: Please provide a brief reflection about your understanding or experience with emotional intelligence.
2. **Enter your Participant Information**: Provide your name and email address so we can contact you if you're selected as the winner. You can also enter a phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
3. **Add any comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the emotional intelligence raffle.
4. **Submit your entry**: Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to enter your raffle entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Reflection** (Participant Reflection, text, **Required**): A place to describe your understanding or experience with emotional intelligence.
* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, **Required**): Please enter your full name so we can contact you if you're selected as the winner.
* **Participant EMail** (Participant EMail, email, **Required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you via email if you win.
* **Participant Phone** (Participant Phone, text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you via phone if you win. If not provided, we'll only contact you via email.
* **Participant Comments** (Participant Comments, text, **Optional**): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the emotional intelligence raffle.
* **Winner Name** (Winner Name, text, **Required**): Please enter the name of the person who will receive the prize or recognition, if not you.
* **Winner EMail** (Winner EMail, email, **Required**): Enter the email address of the person who will receive the prize or recognition, if not you.
* **Winner Phone** (Winner Phone, text, **Optional**): Enter the phone number of the person who will receive the prize or recognition, if not you.
* **Winner Comments** (Winner Comments, text, **Optional**): Enter any comments about the winner, such as their background or experience with emotional intelligence.
