# Contest Winner Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contest Winner Update Form is used to collect detailed information from contest winners to ensure proper prize distribution and to gather valuable feedback and testimonials. This form is only accessible to contest winners and should not be used for any other purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are a contest winner by verifying your contest participation details.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Winner Full Name** (`winner_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contest Name** (`contest_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contest you won.
* **Contest Date** (`contest_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the contest.
* **Prize Category** (`prize_category`, select_one, required): Select the type of prize you won (Cash Prize, Gift Card, Product Package, Experience, Scholarship, Trophy or Award, or Other).
* **Prize Tier** (`prize_tier`, select_one, required): Select your placement in the contest (First Place, Second Place, Third Place, Runner-up, Honorable Mention, Grand Prize).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your street address for prize delivery.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city for your mailing address.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state or province for your mailing address.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code for your mailing address.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select your country of residence (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, or Other).
* **Age Verification** (`age_verification`, select_one, required): Verify that you are 18 or older.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): If you are a minor, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Contact** (`guardian_contact`, text, optional): If you are a minor, enter the email and phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Tax Form Required** (`tax_form_required`, select_one, required): If your prize is over $600, select "Yes, I will submit" or indicate that it's not applicable.
* **Prize Delivery Preference** (`prize_delivery_preference`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive your prize (Mail to address, Pick up in person, Digital delivery, or Other).
* **Publicity Consent** (`publicity_consent`, select_one, required): If you consent to being featured in promotions, select "Yes, I consent" or prefer not to be featured.
* **Testimonial Willingness** (`testimonial_willingness`, select_one, optional): If you are willing to share your testimonial about the contest, select "Yes, happy to share" or indicate otherwise.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or special requests.
