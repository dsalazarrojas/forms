# Claim Your Free Item - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to claim a free item for customers. Fill out the form with your information to start the claim process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your shipping address, including street, city, and postal code.
3. Provide your email address for shipping updates.
4. Enter your phone number for optional contact (not required).
5. Select the free item you would like to claim.
6. If provided, enter the coupon code for the free item.
7. Confirm that you meet the eligibility criteria.
8. Select "I confirm" to indicate that you have read and agree to the terms for processing your data.
9. Choose the expected delivery timeframe for the free item.
10. Use the current date as the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Shipping address**: Enter your street, city, and postal code for shipping purposes.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for shipping updates.
* **Phone number**: Optional contact phone number for any shipping-related inquiries.
* **Item choice**: Select the free item you would like to claim from the available options.
* **Coupon code**: If provided, enter the coupon code for the free item.
* **Eligibility confirmation**: Confirm that you meet the eligibility criteria for claiming the free item.
* **Consent to use data for fulfillment**: Select "I confirm" to indicate that you have read and agree to the terms for processing your data.
* **Expected delivery timeframe**: Choose the expected delivery timeframe for the free item.
* **Submission date**: Use the current date to indicate when you submitted your claim.
