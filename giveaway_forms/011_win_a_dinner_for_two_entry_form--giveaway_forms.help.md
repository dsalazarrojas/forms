# win-a-dinner-for-two-entry-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "win-a-dinner-for-two-entry-form" is designed to capture information from users for a dinner giveaway contest. It has three pages with fields that ask for personal details, preferences, and confirmation of entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, navigate through the pages and fill out the fields as follows:

1. **Enter your name**
	* This is a text field where you can enter your name.
	* Please provide your real name as it will be used for notification and communication purposes.
2. **Enter your email**
	* This is an email field where you can enter your email address.
	* Please provide a valid email address that you have access to and check for updates.
3. **Confirm your email**
	* This is another email field where you can confirm your email address.
	* Please enter the same email address you provided in the previous field.
4. **Select your prize preference**
	* This is a selection field where you can choose the size of your party.
	* Select the size of your party (Dinner for 2, Dinner for 4, or Dinner for 6).
5. **Confirm prize preference**
	* This is another selection field where you can confirm your prize preference.
	* Please select the same preference you chose in the previous field.
6. **Select a date**
	* This is a date field where you can select a date for the dinner reservation.
	* Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. **Select a time**
	* This is a time field where you can select a time for the dinner reservation.
	* Please enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
8. **Select a restaurant**
	* This is a multiple selection field where you can choose one or more restaurants.
	* Please select at least one restaurant.
9. **Win a prize**
	* This is a note field with a checkbox.
	* Please check this box to confirm that you would like to win a prize.
10. **Additional comments**
	* This is an optional text field where you can provide additional comments.
	* Please feel free to provide any additional information about your preferences.
11. **Confirm your entry**
	* This is a selection field with multiple options.
	* Please select "Yes" to confirm your entry.
12. **Confirm your entry (again)**
	* This is another selection field with multiple options.
	* Please select "Yes" to confirm your entry and agree to the terms and conditions of the contest.
