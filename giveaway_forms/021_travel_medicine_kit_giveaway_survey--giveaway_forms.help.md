# Travel Medicine Kit Giveaway Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from travelers to provide a free travel medicine kit. Please fill it out thoroughly and accurately to improve your chances of receiving the giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your Traveler Information, which includes your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose the type of medicine kit you would like to receive.
4. Describe your travel details, including your destination, duration, and purpose of travel.
5. If you have any existing medical conditions, please describe them in the Medical Condition field.
6. If you have any travel-specific medical needs, please describe them in the Travel Medication field.
7. In the Emergency Contact field, provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
8. Optionally, provide additional comments about your travel plans and requirements.
9. Finally, provide your email and phone number in the Email and Phone Number fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler Information (text)**: This field is meant for your name and other identifying information.
* **Contact Information (text)**: This field is meant to collect your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* **Medicine Kit (select_multiple)**: Choose the type of medicine kit you would like to receive. Please select all relevant options.
* **Travel Details (text)**: Describe your travel plans, including your destination, duration, and purpose of travel.
* **Medical Condition (text)**: If you have any existing medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Travel Medication (text)**: If you have any travel-specific medical needs, please describe them here.
* **Emergency Contact (text)**: Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Additional Comments (text)**: Optionally, provide any additional comments or information about your travel plans.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address to receive communication about your giveaway application.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your giveaway application.
