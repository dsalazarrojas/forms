# Wearable Technology Giveaway Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to enter a giveaway for wearable technology. The purpose of this form is to gather information from users about their preferences and demographics to determine eligibility for the giveaway. The form is used to collect contact information, device preferences, and other relevant details to ensure that the giveaway is fair and accessible to all eligible users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select your preferred options for the multiple-choice fields.
3. Enter your email address correctly in the "Email" field.
4. Check the boxes for the "Agree to terms and conditions" and "Agree to privacy policy" fields.
5. Choose your preferred brand of wearable technology in the "Your preferred brand of wearable technology" field.
6. Select how you would like to be contacted in the "Consent to contact" field.
7. Select your preferred contact method in the "Preferred contact method" field.
8. Enter any promotion code you may have in the "Promotion Code (if you have one)" field.
9. Describe how you found out about this promotion in the "How did you find out about this promotion" field.
10. Select which device you own in the "Which device do you own" field.
11. Enter any other device you may own in the "Other" field.
12. Enter the number of devices you own in the "How many devices do you own" field.
13. Select which brands you like in the "Which brands do you like" field.
14. Enter your monthly income in the "How much do you earn in a month?" field.
15. Select your occupation in the "Occupation" field.
