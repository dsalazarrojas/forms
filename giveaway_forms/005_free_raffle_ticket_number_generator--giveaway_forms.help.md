# Free Raffle Ticket Number Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Free Raffle Ticket Number Generator is a form designed to create unique raffle ticket numbers for your events, promotions, or giveaways. Fill out this form to collect and verify the winner's information for prize redemption purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a ticket number in the "Ticket Number" field. This is a unique identifier for the raffle ticket.
2. Fill out the "Winner Name" field with the full name of the winner.
3. Enter the "Winner Email" field with the winner's email address.
4. Optionally, fill out the "Winner Phone" field with the winner's phone number.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ticket Number** (`ticket_number`, number, required): Enter a unique ticket number for the raffle ticket. This should match the number on the actual ticket or prize box.
* **Winner Name** (`winner_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the winner.
* **Winner Email** (`winner_email`, email, optional): Enter the winner's email address for prize redemption purposes.
* **Winner Phone** (`winner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the winner's phone number (if provided).
