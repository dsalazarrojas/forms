# Care Package Giveaway Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Care Package Giveaway Event Registration Form is used to gather essential details from participants to facilitate the distribution of care packages. This form helps volunteers understand who needs help, what kind of support they require, and when they can receive the care packages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Provide your contact phone number for pickup coordination.
3. If you have a contact email, enter it for any updates or communication from the event organizers.
4. Select your preferred time window for package pickup.
5. Specify the number of care packages you need.
6. If you have any dietary or allergy restrictions, enter them in the "Dietary or allergy notes" field.
7. Choose your preferred pickup location (if available).
8. Confirm your consent to share your contact information with event volunteers for coordination.
9. Finally, add any additional comments or information that might be helpful for the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, required, text): Enter your full name for registration in the event.
* **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, required, text): Provide your phone number for volunteers to contact you about pickup coordination.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, optional, email): Enter your email address for any updates or communication from the event organizers.
* **Preferred pickup time** (`preferred_pickup_time`, optional, time): Select a time window when you can receive your care package.
* **Number of packages needed** (`number_of_packages_needed`, required, number): Enter the number of care packages you require.
* **Dietary or allergy notes** (`dietary_or_allergy_notes`, optional, text): List any dietary or allergy restrictions you have.
* **Pickup location preference** (`pickup_location_preference`, optional, select_one):
	+ Main site
	+ Satellite site
	+ Home delivery request
* **Consent to share contact** (`consent_to_share_contact`, required, select_one):
	+ True
	+ False
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, optional, text): Add any additional information about your needs or preferences.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate contact information for smooth coordination.
- Be specific with your dietary or allergy notes for volunteers' attention to detail.
- Consider your preferred pickup location carefully, as it might affect the availability of volunteers.
