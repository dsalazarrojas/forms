# Giveaway Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a giveaway event. It helps us understand who is interested in participating and what they are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Registrant Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe the event details in the "Event Details" field.
5. Add any additional notes about your registration in the "Registrant Notes" field (optional).
6. Fill in your address, city, state, zip, and country in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant Name**:
	+ This field is required and should contain your name.
	+ Please enter the name that you would like to be associated with this registration.
* **Email**:
	+ This field is required and should contain a valid email address.
	+ This is the email address we will use to contact you.
* **Phone**:
	+ This field is required and should contain your phone number.
	+ Please format your phone number in the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Event Details**:
	+ This field is required and should describe the event you are interested in.
	+ Please provide as much information as possible about the event.
* **Event Notes**:
	+ This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or information about your registration.
	+ You can use this field to ask questions or provide context about your registration.
* **Registrant Address**:
	+ This field is required and should contain your physical address.
	+ Please enter your address in the format "Street Address, Apartment Number, City, State, Zip".
* **Registrant City**:
	+ This field is required and should contain the city where you live.
* **Registrant State**:
	+ This field is required and should contain the state where you live.
* **Registrant Zip**:
	+ This field is required and should contain your zip code.
* **Registrant Country**:
	+ This field is required and should contain the country where you live.

Note: Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields accurately and completely.
