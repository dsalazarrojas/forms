# Drawing Ticket Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drawing Ticket Registration form is used to register individuals for a drawing event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your information in the Registration Form section.
2. Provide details about the drawing you are entering in the Drawing Details section.
3. Enter your contact information in the Contact Details section.
4. Confirm your submission by typing in the Confirmation field.
