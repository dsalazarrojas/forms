# home-goods-shopping-spree-entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a home goods shopping spree promotion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Type your address in the "Address" field.
4. Provide your city in the "City" field.
5. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
6. Select your country from the list.
7. Confirm your eligibility for the promotion by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Eligibility" field.
8. Confirm your consent to participate by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Consent" field.
9. Choose your preferred contact method from the list.
10. Select your preferred contact method from the list.
11. If you have a zip code for your alternative address, enter it in the "Zip/Postal Code" field.
12. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
13. If you have an alternative email, enter it in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address to be contacted.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Type your address to help us find you.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter your city to help us find you.
* **Zip** (Zip, text, required): Enter your zip code to help us find you.
* **Country** (Country, text, required): Select your country of residence.
* **Confirm Eligibility** (Confirm Eligibility, select multiple, required): Confirm if you are eligible for the promotion.
* **Confirm Consent** (Confirm Consent, select one, required): Confirm your consent to participate.
* **Contact Preference** (Contact Preference, select multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Preferred Contact Method, select multiple, optional): Select your preferred contact method.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (Zip/Postal Code, number, optional): Enter your alternative zip code.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your alternative email.
* **None** (None, text, optional): This field is not currently used. 

Please note that this form is for internal purposes and is not meant to be filled by external users. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
