> # Tea Basket Giveaway Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Tea Basket Giveaway Entry" form is a user-facing form designed to collect information from participants in a giveaway event organized by a tea company. The form is used to gather details about the participant, their preferred delivery location and time, and the type of tea they would like to receive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the basic information section, which includes providing your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select a location from the dropdown menu where you would like the tea to be delivered.
3. Choose a preferred delivery date and time in the format of MM/dd/yyyy HH:MM.
4. Add any special delivery notes, such as parking instructions or access codes.
5. Select the number of participants you would like to receive the tea for.
6. If you are a participant, select your preferred location from the dropdown menu.
7. Finally, confirm that you have participated in the giveaway by selecting "Yes" or "No" and adding any additional notes if necessary.
