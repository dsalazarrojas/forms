# mobile_device_giveaway_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to enter information for a mobile device giveaway. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform, and is released under CC BY 4.0.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information.
2. Enter your participant email address.
3. Confirm your participant email address.
4. Enter your participant zip code.
5. Select your participant state.
6. Enter your participant date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_info** (text, not required): Enter your basic participant information.
* **participant_email** (email, not required): Enter your email address. This field is for you to enter your email address for future communications.
* **confirm_email** (email, not required): Re-enter your email address to confirm its accuracy. This is a confirmation of your email address.
* **participant_zip** (text, not required): Enter your zip code for administrative purposes.
* **participant_state** (select_multiple, not required): Select your state of residence. You can choose multiple states if applicable.
* **participant_date_of_birth** (date, not required): Enter your date of birth. This field is for age verification or other purposes that require your birthdate.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
- Select all applicable states for your state of residence.
- Use a valid date format for your date of birth (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
