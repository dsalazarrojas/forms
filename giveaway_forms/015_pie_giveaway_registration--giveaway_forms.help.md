# pie_giveaway_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pie_giveaway_registration" form is used for participants to register for a giveaway event. This event offers a free product or service, and users must provide necessary information to be eligible and receive the prize.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required information, indicated by the "required" label in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
2. Review each field to ensure you're providing accurate and complete information.
3. Submit your entry once you've completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <form_info>
* **Form Information** (`form_info`, text, required): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your entry.
### <name>
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed if you win the giveaway.
### <email>
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address where the giveaway prize will be delivered. Please make sure it's a valid email address.
### <phone>
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number where you can be reached for delivery details.
### <confirm_eligibility>
* **Confirm Eligibility** (`confirm_eligibility`, select_one, required): Choose "Eligible" if you meet the conditions of the giveaway event. If you are not sure, you can ask for clarification.
### <delivery_details>
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required): Please provide address or other delivery information to ensure successful delivery of the prize.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before submitting your entry.
- Only fill out this form once. Duplicate entries will not be accepted.
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth delivery process.
