# Product Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Product Giveaway Entry Form" is designed to collect information from users who are interested in participating in a product giveaway. The form aims to gather basic contact details and preferences of the users, as well as their social media presence and the reasons for their entry. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to complete it, and a detailed explanation of each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Product Interested In, and Agreement.
2. Select your preferred social media platform(s) from the Social Media field.
3. Choose how you heard about the giveaway from the How did you hear field.
4. Optionally, enter any comments or additional information in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name. This field is not required but is optional for demographic purposes.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name. This field is not required but is optional for demographic purposes.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This is a required field for us to contact you in case of any updates or notifications.

### Company
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter your company name. This field is not required but is optional for corporate purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This field is not required but is optional for us to contact you.

### Product Interested In
* **Product Interested In** (`product_interested_in`, select_one, required): Choose the product you are interested in. This field is required to ensure you are entering the correct giveaway.

### Social Media
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Select any social media platform(s) you are present on. This field is optional and helps us track your online presence.

### How did you hear
* **How did you hear** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about the giveaway. This field is optional but helps us understand our marketing effectiveness.

### Why entering
* **Why entering** (`why_entry`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for entering the giveaway. This field is optional and helps us understand your motivations.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share. This field is optional and helps us understand your thoughts.

### Agreement
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the giveaway. This field is required to ensure you understand and accept our rules.

### Entry Terms
* **Entry Terms** (`entry_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Select any terms or conditions that apply to you. This field is optional and helps us track any special circumstances.

Note: By completing this form, you agree to the terms and conditions of the giveaway. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
