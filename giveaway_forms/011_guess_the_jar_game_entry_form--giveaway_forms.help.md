# Guess The Jar Game Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guess The Jar Game Entry Form is a submission form designed for users to participate in the game by predicting a specific date and time for a draw. The form collects essential information about the entry, which is then used to determine the winner of the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Name**: Enter your name as you want it to be displayed on the game.
	* **User Name**: Enter your unique identifier for future reference.
	* **User Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
2. Optional fields:
	* **Entry Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you predict for the draw.
	* **Draw Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you predict for the draw.
	* **Winner Message**: Enter a message for the winner (if you are the winner).
	* **Draw Method**: Choose how the draw will be conducted (Random or Automated).
	* **Entry Status**: Select the status of your entry (Confirmed, Unconfirmed, or Incomplete).
	* **User Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
	* **User Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your entry.
