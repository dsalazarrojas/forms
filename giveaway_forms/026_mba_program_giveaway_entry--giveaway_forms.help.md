# mba_program_giveaway_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a giveaway entry form for MBA programs. It collects information to assess applicant fit and gauge interest in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, select the "Program Name" field with the name of the MBA program you are interested in.
- Provide your "School Name" and "Email" in their respective fields, as these are mandatory fields.
- In the "Motivation" field, type a short paragraph explaining why you are interested in the MBA program.
- If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field. This is optional.
- In the "Why Program" field, write a short paragraph explaining why you are interested in the program.
- For "What Next Step", write a short paragraph on your next steps regarding the program.
- In "Program Interest", select all the programs that interest you.
- Select "Yes" for "How Did You Find Us" if you found us through a specific channel (e.g. social media, search engine, referral).
- In "Other Programs", select all other programs that interest you if any.
- For "Comments", type any additional information you would like to share.
- In "Terms and Conditions", select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
- Lastly, select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions in "Confirm Terms and Conditions" and "Agree" to finalize your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (1): Enter the name of the MBA program you are interested in.
* **School Name** (2): Enter your school name.
* **Motivation** (3): Type a short paragraph explaining why you are interested in the MBA program.
* **Email** (4): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (5): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Why Program** (6): Type a short paragraph on why you are interested in the program.
* **What Next Step** (7): Type a short paragraph on your next steps regarding the program.
* **Program Interest** (8): Select all the programs that interest you.
* **How Did You Find Us** (9): Select "Yes" if you found us through a specific channel (e.g. social media, search engine, referral).
* **Other Programs** (10): Select all other programs that interest you if any.
* **Comments** (11): Type any additional information you would like to share.
* **Terms and Conditions** (12): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Confirm Terms and Conditions** (13): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agree** (14): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
