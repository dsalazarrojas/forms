# Top Shopper Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for participants to enter a giveaway event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "first_name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "last_name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "phone".
5. Enter the date you entered the giveaway in the field labeled "date_entered".
6. Enter the time you entered the giveaway in the field labeled "time_entered".
7. If you wish to provide additional information about your participation, please use the field labeled "participant_info" or "participant_info_2".
8. Once all fields are completed, please review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
* **email (Email)**: Enter your email address. A valid email address is required to be able to contact you in case of a giveaway win.
* **phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number. This can be a helpful way for us to contact you if you win the giveaway.
* **date_entered (Date)**: Enter the date you entered the giveaway. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy for this field.
* **time_entered (Time)**: Enter the time you entered the giveaway. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **participant_info (Note)**: If you have any additional information about your participation, please enter it here. This can be anything you feel is relevant to your entry.
* **participant_info_2 (Text)**: If you have any further information about your participation, please enter it here. This can be additional details or explanations for your entry.
