# Team Pep Rally Giveaway Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Pep Rally Giveaway Order Form is used to order spirit items for a school event. This form is used to gather necessary information from schools to process their order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields, which are marked as required. For optional fields, you can leave them blank if you do not have the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_info_name`, text): Please fill out your school's name as it should appear on the order.
* **School Address** (`school_info_address`, text): Please enter your school's address.
* **School Phone** (`school_info_phone`, text): Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Email** (`school_info_email`, email): Enter your school's email address.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text): Please enter your contact information if different from the school's contact information.
* **Name** (`contact_info_name`, text): Enter your name.
* **Phone** (`contact_info_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`contact_info_email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Spirit Items** (`spirit_items`, select_one): Choose the spirit items you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Other Spirit Items** (`spirit_items_other`, text): If you would like to order an item not listed, please specify it here.
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, select_one): Choose the shipping option from the options provided.
* **Other Shipping** (`shipping_other`, text): If you would like to use a different shipping method, please specify it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): Add any comments or special requests for your order here.
* **Other Comments** (`comments_other`, note): Add any additional comments or special requests here.
* **Date** (`school_info_date`, date): Enter the date you would like the items to be shipped on.
* **Time** (`school_info_time`, time): Enter the time you would like the items to be shipped on.
* **Zip** (`school_info_zip`, number): Enter your school's zip code.
* **Spirit Items Count** (`spirit_items_count`, text): Enter the number of spirit items you would like to order.
* **Shipping Count** (`shipping_count`, text): Enter the number of shipping options you would like to use.
* **Comments Count** (`comments_count`, text): Enter the number of comments you would like to add.
* **City** (`school_info_city`, text): Enter your school's city.
* **State** (`school_info_state`, select_one): Choose your school's state from the options provided.
* **Country** (`school_info_country`, select_one): Choose your school's country from the options provided.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely. If you have any questions or need assistance with this form, please contact us.
