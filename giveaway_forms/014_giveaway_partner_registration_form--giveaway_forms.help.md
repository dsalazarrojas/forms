# Giveaway Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for giveaway partners to register their details and preferences. This information will help us understand your organization's needs and ensure a smooth collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your giveaway partner details in the "Giveaway Partner Details" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Select your giveaway partner information in the "Giveaway Partner Information" field.
4. Fill in your partner information in the "Partner Info" field.
5. Enter your partner's name in the "Partner Name" field.
6. Enter your partner's URL in the "Partner URL" field.
7. Enter a valid email address for your partner in the "Partner Email" field.
8. Enter your partner's phone number in the "Partner Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Giveaway Partner Details** (`giveaway_partner_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a brief description of your giveaway partner.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your contact information. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Giveaway Partner Information** (`giveaway_partner_info`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the type of giveaway partner you are. Please choose the option that best represents your partner.
* **Partner Info** (`partner_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide additional information about your partner.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your partner's name.
* **Partner URL** (`partner_url`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your partner's URL.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter a valid email address for your partner.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your partner's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to us for clarification.
* You can save your progress on this form at any time by clicking the "Save" button.
* Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to finalize your registration.
