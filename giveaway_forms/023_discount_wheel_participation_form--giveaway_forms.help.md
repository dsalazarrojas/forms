# Discount Wheel Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for customers to participate in a promotional campaign where they can spin a wheel and potentially win discounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure you have permission from your company to participate in the campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required): Please provide your name and contact information so we can contact you if you win.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Please provide any contact details you feel will help us reach you if you win.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Please check the box to confirm that you are over 18 and willing to participate.
* **Spin to Win** (`spin_to_win`, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about your spin to win, if you have any.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, optional): Please enter a unique ID associated with your account or email address.
* **Promotion Information** (`promotion_info`, text, optional): Please provide any information about the promotion you are participating in.
* **Participant Data** (`participant_data`, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about yourself that may be relevant to the campaign.
* **Participant Information 2** (`participant_info_2`, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about yourself that may be relevant to the campaign.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to be eligible for the promotion.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [insert contact information].
* Good luck with the spin!
