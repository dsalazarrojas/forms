# Popcorn Prize Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Popcorn Prize Collection Form" is used to collect and process prize claims from winners. This form ensures that we have all the necessary information to deliver prizes correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the corresponding field. This is the first name of the winner.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field. This is the last name of the winner.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the corresponding field. This is the contact email of the winner.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the corresponding field. This is the phone number of the winner.
5. Describe the **Prize Details** in the corresponding field. This is a description of the prize you won.
6. Confirm that you are the **Winner** by selecting the correct option from the dropdown menu.
7. Choose a **Delivery Method** for your prize. You can select "Pickup at store", "Ship to winner", or enter "Other (please describe)" if your chosen method is not listed.
8. If you selected "Ship to winner", enter your **Delivery Address** in the corresponding field. This is the address where you would like your prize delivered.
9. Enter the **Delivery Date** in the corresponding field. This is the date you would like your prize to be delivered.
10. Enter the **Delivery Time** in the corresponding field. This is the time you would like your prize to be delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: The first name of the winner.
	* Type: text
	* Required: yes
- **Last Name**: The last name of the winner.
	* Type: text
	* Required: yes
- **Email**: The contact email of the winner.
	* Type: email
	* Required: yes
- **Phone Number**: The phone number of the winner.
	* Type: text
	* Required: yes
- **Prize Details**: A description of the prize won.
	* Type: text
	* Required: yes
- **Winner Confirmation**: Confirm that you are the winner.
	* Type: select one
	* Required: yes
- **Delivery Method**: Choose a delivery method for your prize.
	* Type: select one
	* Required: no
- **Delivery Address**: The address where you would like your prize delivered.
	* Type: text
	* Required: yes
- **Delivery Date**: The date you would like your prize to be delivered.
	* Type: date
	* Required: yes
- **Delivery Time**: The time you would like your prize to be delivered.
	* Type: time
	* Required: yes
