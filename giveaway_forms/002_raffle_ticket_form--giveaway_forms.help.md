# Raffle Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Raffle Ticket Form is designed to collect participant information and process their raffle entries. Ensure you have all the required fields filled before submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Fill out your info" field with your personal details.
2. Select your preferred raffle entry details in the "Raffle Details" field.
3. Choose your preferred raffle ticket(s) in the "Select Raffle Tickets" field.
4. Enter the entry fee in the "Entry Fee" field.
5. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Optionally, check your raffle status in the "Raffle Status" field. 
9. Optionally, check your raffle type in the "Raffle Type" field.
10. Optionally, enter the raffle date and time in the "Raffle Date" and "Raffle Time" fields respectively.
11. Optionally, enter the entry start date and time in the "Entry Start Date" and "Entry Time" fields respectively.
12. Optionally, enter the entry end date and time in the "Entry End Date" and "Entry End Time" fields respectively.
13. Optionally, check your entry status in the "Entry Status" field.
14. Optionally, enter the raffle owner's name in the "Raffle Owner" field.
15. Optionally, enter entry fees for additional participants in the "Entry Fee 1", "Entry Fee 2", "Entry Fee 3", "Entry Fee 4", "Entry Fee 5", "Entry Fee 6", "Entry Fee 7", and "Entry Fee 8" fields.
