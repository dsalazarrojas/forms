# Mothers Day Giveaway Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Mothers Day Giveaway Form Template" is designed to be a fun and lighthearted way for users to share their thoughts and interests about their mother or themselves. This form aims to collect personal and demographic information to potentially win a prize related to Mother's Day.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Ensure all required fields (none in this case) have been filled out correctly.
3. Select your options for the select fields (e.g., favorite presentation, favorite food, hobbies).
4. Enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted if you win.
5. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if you win. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth to provide more information about yourself.
* **Mother's Day Message** (`mother_s_day_message`, text, optional): Share a message with your mother or a fun fact about her.
* **Message to Mom** (`message_to_mom`, text, optional): Share a message with your mom.
* **Message to Self** (`message_to_self`, text, optional): Share a message with yourself or a funny fact about you.
* **Favorite Presentation** (`favorite_presentation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your favorite presentation options (card, plant, other).
* **Other** (`favorite_presentation_other`, text, optional): If "other" was chosen, please specify your favorite presentation option.
* **Mom's Interest** (`mom_s_interest`, select_one, optional): Choose your mom's interest (art, crafts, music, nature, sports).
* **Favorite Color** (`favorite_color`, select_one, optional): Choose your favorite color (blue, red, yellow, green, purple).
* **Mother's Day Date** (`mother_s_day_date`, date, optional): Enter the Mother's Day date to provide more context.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your favorite foods (chocolate, flowers, gift).
* **Least Favorite Food** (`least_favorite_food`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your least favorite foods (spicy, sugary, fattening).
* **Family Status** (`family_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your family status (single, married, partner, divorced, widowed).
* **Number of Kids** (`kids`, number, optional): Enter the number of kids you have.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Favorite Hobby** (`favorite_hobby`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your favorite hobbies (reading, traveling, hiking, gaming, watching movies).
* **Education** (`education`, select_one, optional): Choose your education level (bachelor, master, PhD, none).
* **Favorite Food Group** (`favorite_food_group`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your favorite food groups (vegie, meat, seafood, dairy).
* **Favorite Cuisine** (`favorite_cuisine`, select_one, optional): Choose your favorite cuisine (Italian, Chinese, Mexican, Japanese, Indian).
* **Favorite Drink** (`favorite_drink`, select_one, optional): Choose your favorite drink (coffee, tea, juice, water, beer).
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): Share any additional information you'd like.
