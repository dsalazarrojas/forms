# rsvp_for_event_giveaway_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect RSVP and contact information for event attendees who are interested in entering a giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Go to the first page of the form, "Event Details", and enter the relevant information for the event you are interested in.
2. On the second page, "Attendee Details", select "Yes" if you are attending the event, or "No" if you are not.
3. On the third page, "Contact Details", enter your contact information.
4. Finally, on the fourth page, "Email", enter your email address to confirm your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter the title and date of the event you are interested in entering the giveaway for.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you are attending the event, or "No" if you are not. This will help the event organizers confirm your attendance.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter any additional event details you would like to provide, such as event location or time.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your first and last name, phone number, and any other contact information you would like to provide.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to confirm your entry and receive any updates about the giveaway.
