## Purpose
The Makeover Hamper Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in receiving a makeover hamper. This form is typically used by event organizers or organizers of giveaways to collect contact details and other relevant information for participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Makeover Hamper Registration form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name**: Enter your full name in the provided text field.
2. Enter your **Email**: Provide a valid email address where you can be reached for further information or updates.
3. Check **Consent**: Select the option indicating you are consenting to receive a makeover hamper.
4. Fill in your **Address**: Enter your address to receive the makeover hamper.
5. Enter your **Phone**: Provide your phone number in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name**
 *   **Name** (`name`, `text`, `true`)
The Name field is where you enter your full name. This field is required as it is necessary to identify you for the makeover hamper registration.

### 2. **Email**
 *   **Email** (`email`, `email`, `true`)
The Email field is a valid email address where you can be reached for further information or updates about your makeover hamper registration.

### 3. **Consent**
 *   **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, `true`)
The Consent field is a select_one field with two options: 'True' or 'False'. This field is required as it ensures the participant agrees to receive a makeover hamper.

### 4. **Address**
 *   **Address** (`address`, `text`, `true`)
The Address field is a text field where you can enter your address to receive the makeover hamper.

### 5. **Phone**
 *   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `true`)
The Phone field is a text field for you to provide your phone number. This field is required for the participant to contact you for further information or updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your registration is completed successfully.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the makeover hamper, do not hesitate to contact the event organizers.
