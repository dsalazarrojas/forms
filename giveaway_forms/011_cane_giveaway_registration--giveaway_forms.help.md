# Cane Giveaway Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a free cane giveaway program. If you have any interest in this program, please complete this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
2. Provide accurate and honest answers.
3. Review the form carefully to avoid duplication and incorrect information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Enter your first name.
  - This is the first name you were given at birth or the name your parents gave you.
  
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Enter your last name.
  - This is the last name you were given at birth or the name your parents gave you.
  
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
  - This is to confirm your eligibility and age for the program.
  
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, Required): Enter your email address.
  - This is where we will send updates about the program and other related information.
  
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Required): Enter your phone number.
  - This is the best number we can use to contact you for updates.
  
- **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, Required): Enter your street address.
  - This is your home address.
  
- **City** (`city`, text, Required): Enter your city.
  - This is the city where you reside.
  
- **State/Province** (`state`, text, Required): Enter your state or province.
  - This is the state or province where you reside.
  
- **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, Required): Enter your ZIP code.
  - This is the postal code of your area.
  
- **Country** (`country`, text, Required): Enter your country of residence.
  - This is the country where you reside.
  
- **Why do you need a cane?** (`cane_reason`, select_one, Required): Select the reason you need a cane.
  - This helps us understand your needs for the program.
  
- **Cane Preference** (`cane_preference`, select_one, Required): Select the type of cane you prefer.
  - This helps us tailor the cane to your needs.
  
- **Any specific cane specifications needed?** (`cane_specifications`, text, Optional): Enter any specific specifications you need for your cane.
  - This can be height, weight capacity, color, etc.
  
- **Do you have a cane type preference?** (`cane_preference`, select_one, Required): Select the type of cane you prefer.
  - This helps us tailor the cane to your needs.
  
- **I confirm I am at least 18 years old** (`age_confirmation`, select_one, Required): Confirm your age.
  - This is to ensure you are eligible for the program.
  
- **I reside within the eligible service area** (`residency_confirmation`, select_one, Required): Confirm your residency.
  - This is to ensure you are eligible for the program.
  
- **Have you received assistance through this program before?** (`previous_recipient`, select_one, Optional): Select if you have received assistance before.
  - This helps us prevent duplicate registrations.
  
- **Preferred contact method** (`contact_method`, select_one, Required): Select your preferred contact method.
  - This helps us communicate with you effectively.
  
- **Would you like to receive updates about community assistance programs?** (`newsletter`, select_one, Optional): Select if you want to receive updates.
  - This helps us keep you informed about related programs.
