# Mid Autumn Festival Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mid Autumn Festival Giveaway Entry Form is a digital form designed to collect information from attendees of the festival for the purpose of organizing the giveaway. The form collects personal details and preferences from attendees to ensure they are entered into the giveaway draw.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
- Start by providing your personal details such as Name and Email.
- Select the best time to contact you.
- Enter your date preferences if you have any.
- Indicate your availability on the specific days you will be attending the festival.
- Finally, confirm your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you.
* **Phone**: Provide a number where you can be reached.
* **Date**: If you have any specific date preferences, please enter them here.
* **Time**: Select a time that suits you best.
* **Note**: This is an optional field for any additional information about your preferences.
* **Available Days**: Select one or more days you are available to participate in the giveaway. 
* **Interest in Activities**: Select one or more activities that you are interested in attending. 

Please note that this form is for internal use only, and your information will be used only for this festival.
