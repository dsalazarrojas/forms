# Paranormal Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paranormal Giveaway Entry Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a paranormal-themed giveaway. This form is intended for individuals who wish to enter the giveaway and have a legitimate interest in the paranormal genre.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) to ensure you are eligible to enter the giveaway.
2. Enter your name and contact information accurately and completely.
3. Select your preferred prize category from the options provided.
4. Choose the value of the prize you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication regarding the giveaway.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, but this is not required.
* **Date of birth** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Entry date** (`entry_date`, date, required): Enter the date you entered this form.
* **Favorite paranormal genre** (`favorite_paranormal_genre`, select_one, required): Choose the paranormal genre that interests you the most.
* **How did you enter?** (`how_entered`, select_one, optional): If you didn't enter through one of the provided options, describe how you entered.
* **Social media** (`social_media`, text, optional): Enter any social media platform you are associated with.
* **Online event** (`online_event`, text, optional): Enter any online event you participated in.
* **Online ad** (`online_ad`, text, optional): Enter any online ad you saw.
* **Other source** (`other_source`, text, optional): Enter any other source that led you to this form.
* **Prize preferred** (`prize_preferred`, select_multiple, required): Choose up to two prize categories that interest you the most.
* **Prize value** (`prize_value`, number, required): Enter the value of the prize you are interested in.
* **Prize category** (`prize_category`, select_one, required): Choose the type of prize you are interested in.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Ensure you meet the eligibility criteria before submitting your entry.
* The information collected will be used to determine your eligibility and contact winners.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
