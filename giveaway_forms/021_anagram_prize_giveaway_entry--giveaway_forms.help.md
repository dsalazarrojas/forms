# Anagram Prize Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be a giveaway entry form for an anagram prize giveaway. Fill out this form to participate in the giveaway and potentially receive a prize.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely.
2.  Make sure to enter a correct solution to the anagram challenge.
3.  Agree to the giveaway terms and conditions.
4.  Select the correct country of residence.
5.  Choose the correct way you heard about the giveaway.
6.  Choose whether to subscribe to the newsletter or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): Enter your valid email address that we can contact you with.
*   **Social Media Handle** (`social_media_handle`, Text, Optional): If you have a social media handle, enter it here. This can be your username on any social media platform.
*   **The Anagram Challenge** (`section_anagram_challenge`, Note, Required): This is the anagram challenge you need to solve to enter the giveaway. The anagram is: LISTEN. Try to unscramble the letters to form a word or phrase that fits the hint provided.
*   **Your Answer** (`your_answer`, Text, Required): Enter the solution to the anagram challenge.
*   **Submission Info** (`section_submission_info`, Note, Required): This section provides additional information about the submission.
*   **How did you hear about this giveaway?** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, Select One, Required): Choose the correct way you heard about this giveaway.
*   **Country of Residence** (`country_of_residence`, Select One, Required): Choose your country of residence from the given options.
*   **Do you agree to the Giveaway Terms and Conditions?** (`agree_to_terms`, Select One, Required): Agree to the terms and conditions of this giveaway.
*   **Would you like to subscribe to our newsletter?** (`subscribe_to_newsletter`, Select One, Optional): If you want to stay up-to-date with our latest news and promotions, choose this option.
