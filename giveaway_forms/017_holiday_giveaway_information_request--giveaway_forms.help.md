# Holiday Giveaway Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants about a holiday giveaway. This information will be used to process participant requests and ensure that gifts are distributed fairly and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's details, including their name, contact number, email, and participant address.
2. Select the gift(s) that the participant would like to receive.
3. Provide any special requests or messages that the participant would like to include with their gift.
4. Enter any participant messages that the participant would like to include with their gift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Enter the participant's name, contact number, and email address.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the participant's email address to ensure that we can contact them regarding their gift.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the participant's contact number so we can contact them regarding their gift.
* **Name of Participant** (`name_of_participant`): Enter the participant's name to identify them.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`): Enter the participant's address for gift delivery purposes.
* **Participant Postal Code** (`participant_postal_code`): Enter the participant's postal code to ensure accurate gift delivery.
* **Gift Picked** (`gift_picked`): Select the gift(s) that the participant would like to receive.
* **Holiday Wishes** (`holiday_wishes`): Enter any holiday wishes or messages that the participant would like to include with their gift.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`): Enter any special requests or messages that the participant would like to include with their gift.
* **Participant Message 1-16** (`participant_message1`, `participant_message2`, etc.): Enter any additional messages that the participant would like to include with their gift.
