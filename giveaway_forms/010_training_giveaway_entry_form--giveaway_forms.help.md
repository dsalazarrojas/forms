To ensure the entry is valid, users must not repeat entries and should only submit new entries. The purpose of this form is to collect data for the training program's evaluation and improvement.

# Training Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information and preferences for the training program's evaluation and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First name in the `First name` field.
2. Fill in your Last name in the `Last name` field.
3. Enter your Email in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Enter your Date of birth in the `Date of birth` field in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
6. Enter the time of completion in the `Time of completion` field.
7. Enter the name of your training provider in the `Training provider` field.
8. Enter the name of the course you have attended in the `Course name` field.
9. Select your agreement to the terms of the training program by selecting `I agree` in the `I understand the terms` field.
9. Optionally, provide any additional information in the `Additional info` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format yyyy-mm-dd.
* **Time of completion** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you completed the training in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Training provider** (`training_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your training provider.
* **Course name** (`course_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you have attended.
* **I understand the terms** (`consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select `I agree` to confirm you understand the terms of the training program.
* **Review** (`review`, `text`, required/optional): Optionally, enter any additional comments or feedback about the training program.
* **Additional info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required/optional): Optionally, enter any additional information about your training experience.
