# Win Free Days Of Enrollment Contest Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students and their school representatives to enter a contest to win free days of enrollment. It is essential to fill out the form correctly and accurately to be considered for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Name.
2. Select your Age Group.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number (if applicable).
5. Enter your School Name.
6. Select your Student Grade.
7. Select your School Type (Public, Private, or School District).
8. Select your Student Grade 2 (if applicable).
9. Enter your Teacher's Name (if applicable).
10. Enter your School Name 2 (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Age Group** (`Age Group`, `select_one`, required): Select your age group from the options provided.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **School Name** (`School Name`, `text`, required): Enter your school name as it appears on your school's official documents.
* **Student Grade** (`Student Grade`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your student grade from the options provided.
* **School Type** (`Student School`, `select_one`, required): Select whether your school is Public, Private, or School District.
* **Student Grade 2** (`Student Grade 2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your student grade 2 from the options provided.
* **Teacher** (`Teacher`, `text`, required): Enter your teacher's name (if applicable).
* **School** (`School`, `text`, required): Enter your school name 2 (if applicable).

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
* Only one entry per person or school can be submitted.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the form's purpose and the explanations above.
* Make sure to review your entry carefully before submitting it.
