# Win A Free Training Session Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in a free training session promotion. The form is only accessible to those who are eligible for the promotion.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field, which will be verified as part of the entry process.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, which is optional but helps us contact you.
5. Confirm your interest in a training session by selecting "Yes" in the "Training Session" field.
6. Provide any additional details about the training session you are interested in, if applicable.
7. If you received a promotion, enter any details in the "Promotion" field.
8. Confirm how you heard about the promotion by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "How did you hear" field.
9. Enter the date you won in the "Win Date" field.
10. Choose your preferred trainer(s) by selecting "Yes" for each one in the "Trainer" field.
11. If you want to specify a location for the training, enter it in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address to be contacted about your entry and the free training session.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted about your entry and the free training session.
*   **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_one, required): Confirm your interest in a training session.
*   **Training Session Details** (`training_session_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the training session you are interested in.
*   **Promotion** (`promotion`, text, optional): If you received a promotion, enter any details about it.
*   **How did you hear** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Confirm how you heard about the free training session promotion.
*   **Win Date** (`win_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you won the free training session.
*   **Trainer** (`trainer`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred trainer(s) for the free training session.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, optional): If you want to specify a location for the training, enter it here.
