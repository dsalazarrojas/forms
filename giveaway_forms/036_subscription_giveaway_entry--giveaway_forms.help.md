## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a plan that best suits your needs.
2. Select the channel you'd like to associate with your entry.
3. Fill out any additional details specific to the selected plan.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
5. Specify your time of day and date for the giveaway period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plan (plan)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the available plans (Plan A, Plan B, or Plan C) to express your interest in the giveaway.
* **Plan A Details (plan_a_details)** (`text`, optional): If you selected Plan A, provide any additional details about your interest in this plan.
* **Plan B Details (plan_b_details)** (`text`, optional): If you selected Plan B, provide any additional details about your interest in this plan.
* **Plan C Details (plan_c_details)** (`text`, optional): If you selected Plan C, provide any additional details about your interest in this plan.
* **Channel (channel)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the channel you'd like to associate with your entry (Channel A or Channel B).
* **Channel A Details (channel_a_details)** (`text`, optional): If you selected Channel A, provide any additional details about your interest in this channel.
* **Channel B Details (channel_b_details)** (`text`, optional): If you selected Channel B, provide any additional details about your interest in this channel.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Time of Day (time_of_day)** (`time`, optional): Specify the time of day you are most interested in participating in the giveaway.
* **Date (date)** (`date`, optional): Specify a date for the giveaway period.

**Tips**

* Please ensure you've selected the correct plan and channel for your interest.
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about your interests and availability.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* You can always reach out to us if you need help or clarification on any of the fields.
