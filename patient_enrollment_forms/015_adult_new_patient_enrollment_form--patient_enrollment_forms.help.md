# Adult New Patient Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Adult New Patient Enrollment Form is used to collect and store essential information about new adult patients. This information includes contact details, medical history, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form sections as required and ensure that all required fields are completed.
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name**: Enter the patient's full name, including first and last names.
- **Date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Address**: Enter the patient's address in the format: Street, city, state, postal code.
- **Phone number**: Enter the patient's mobile phone number, which will be used for appointment reminders.
- **Email address**: Enter the patient's email address, which will be used for appointment reminders and other communications (optional).
- **Insurance provider**: Enter the name of the patient's insurance provider and policy number (optional).
- **Primary medical complaint**: Briefly describe the reason for the visit.
- **Existing medical conditions**: List any existing medical conditions or allergies the patient has.
- **Emergency contact name and phone**: Enter the name and contact phone number of the emergency contact person, ensuring the information is accurate and up-to-date.

Note: This form is required for new adult patients and must be filled out completely for accurate and efficient care provision.
