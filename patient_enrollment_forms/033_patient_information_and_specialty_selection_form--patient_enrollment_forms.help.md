# Patient Information And Specialty Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Information And Specialty Selection Form is designed to collect patient demographic data and medical specialty information. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential details about patients, such as their name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email. Additionally, the form allows patients to select their medical specialty, which is crucial for understanding their medical history and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your Name, Date of Birth, and Address fields. These fields are important for identifying you as a patient.
2. In the Medical Specialty field, select all the specialties that apply to you from the list of options. This will help healthcare providers understand your medical history and needs.
3. If you have a second medical specialty, select it in the Medical Specialty 2 field.
4. Enter your Phone Number and Email fields to ensure we can contact you.
5. If you have any additional medical specialties, select them in the Medical Specialty 3 to Medical Specialty 6 fields.
6. Finally, add any additional notes about your medical history or conditions in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Medical Specialty**: Select all the specialties that apply to you from the list of options. This will help healthcare providers understand your medical history and needs.
* **Medical Specialty 2**: Select any additional specialties that apply to you. This will help healthcare providers understand your medical history and needs.
* **Medical Specialty 3 to 6**: Select any additional specialties that apply to you. These fields allow you to list multiple specialties.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about your medical history or conditions. This is a free-text field for you to provide any additional information that may be relevant.
