# Pediatric Practice Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Practice Onboarding Survey is a form that helps us understand your new patient information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on each page to begin.
2. Provide your new patient information in the "New Patient Info" page.
3. Answer the questions in the "Parent Info" page.
4. Enter your emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" page.
5. Provide consent details in the "Consent Details" page.
6. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Patient Info** (`new_patient_info`, text, required: false):
This page is where you'll provide your new patient information, such as your name and contact details.
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, required: false):
Please answer these questions about your parent(s) or guardian(s), providing their name(s), relationship, and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false):
Enter the name, relationship, and contact information of your emergency contact person(s).
* **Consent Details** (`consent_details`, text, required: false):
Please provide consent for our services, explaining how you'd like us to contact you and when you'd like to receive updates.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out each page thoroughly and accurately.
- If you have any questions, feel free to ask.
- This form is an optional step in our onboarding process, but it will help us provide better services to you and your family.
