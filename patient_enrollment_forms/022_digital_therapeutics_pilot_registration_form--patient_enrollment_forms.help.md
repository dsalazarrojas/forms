# Digital Therapeutics Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Therapeutics Pilot Registration Form is designed for patients to register for the Digital Therapeutics Pilot program. This form will gather information on the patient's medical condition, treatment plan, and medication history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have filled out all required fields.
2. Fill out all fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Enrolment Form (Page 1)**
	+ This is the first page of the form where you can start your registration process. Please make sure to fill out this field accurately and truthfully.
* **Contact Information**
	+ Fill out your contact information, such as name, email address, and phone number. This will help us keep in touch with you about your registration and any further updates.
* **Medical History**
	+ Describe your medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments you have undergone.
* **Medication History**
	+ List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Medical Condition**
	+ Describe your current medical condition or any conditions you have had in the past.
* **Medical Condition Notes**
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments about your medical condition.
* **Previous Tx**
	+ Indicate whether you have had any previous treatments for your medical condition.
* **Medication Treatment Plan**
	+ Describe your treatment plan, including any medications you are taking or have taken in the past.
* **Treatment Plan Dosage**
	+ Indicate the dosage of your treatment plan.
* **Treatment Plan Frequency**
	+ Indicate the frequency of your treatment plan.
* **Medical Condition Treatment Plan Notes**
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments about your treatment plan.
* **Treatment Plan Side Effects**
	+ Indicate whether you have experienced any side effects from your treatment plan.
* **Expected Outcome**
	+ Indicate whether you expect your condition to improve, remain the same, or worsen with treatment.
* **Previous Outcome**
	+ Indicate the outcome of your previous treatment, if any.
* **Expected Duration**
	+ Estimate the expected duration of your treatment.
* **Start Date**
	+ Indicate the start date of your treatment plan.
* **Medication Tx**
	+ Indicate whether you are taking any medications for your condition.
* **Treatment Plan Notes**
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments about your treatment plan.
* **Medical Condition Treatment Plan Medication Dosage**
	+ Indicate the dosage of your treatment plan medication.
* **Medical Condition Treatment Plan Medication Frequency**
	+ Indicate the frequency of your treatment plan medication.
* **Medical Condition Treatment Plan Medication Side Effects**
	+ Indicate whether you have experienced any side effects from your medication.
* **Medical Condition Treatment Plan Medication Previous Tx**
	+ Indicate whether you have had any previous treatments for your medication.
* **Medical Condition Treatment Plan Medication Dosage Amount**
	+ Provide the amount of your treatment plan medication.
* **Medical Condition Treatment Plan Medication Frequency Quantity**
	+ Indicate the quantity of your treatment plan medication.
* **Medical Condition Treatment Plan Medication Side Effects Previous Tx**
	+ Indicate whether you have had any previous side effects from your medication.
