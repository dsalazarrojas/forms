# Patient Intake And Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Intake And Appointment Request Form is designed to gather essential information from patients who are new to the service or require a consultation. This form helps healthcare providers to understand the patient's needs and schedule an appointment accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field. This is a required field.
5. Select the desired Appointment Date for your consultation.
6. Choose the preferred Appointment Time for your consultation.
7. Provide a brief explanation for the purpose of your appointment in the Appointment Reason field. This is a required field.
8. If you have a preferred healthcare provider, select the assigned tool from the drop-down menu. If not, select "Other" as your preferred provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`patient_first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your valid email address to receive communication from our service.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number where we can reach you for any updates or concerns.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the desired date for your consultation.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, **required**): Choose the preferred time slot for your appointment.
* **Appointment Reason** (`appointment_reason`, `text`, **required**): Briefly describe the reason for your appointment, such as a follow-up or initial consultation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your preferred healthcare provider from the list. If not listed, select "Other" and we will contact you to arrange an alternative.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly and accurately to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any concerns or issues with the form, please contact our support staff for assistance.
