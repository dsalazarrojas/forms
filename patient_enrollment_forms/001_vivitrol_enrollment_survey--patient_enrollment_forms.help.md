This form is designed to collect information from patients for various purposes, including treatment planning and outcomes assessment.

- **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, required: false): This field is for providing general patient information such as name, age, contact details, etc.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe any previous medical conditions, allergies, or surgeries.
- **Eligibility** (`treatment_eligibility`, text, required: false): Check if you meet the eligibility criteria for the treatment.
- **Consent** (`consent`, text, required: false): Confirm that you understand and agree to the treatment and its terms.
- **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, required: false): Provide any follow-up information or instructions.
- **Patient Enrollment** (`patient_enrollment`, text, required: false): Enter your patient ID or any other relevant enrollment information.
- **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, text, required: false): Report your current patient status, such as active, inactive, or discharged.
- **Patient Outcomes** (`patient_outcomes`, text, required: false): Document any relevant patient outcomes or progress.
- **Vivitrol Treatment** (`vivitrol_treatment`, text, required: false): Describe the specific treatment you are undergoing or have undergone.
