# Health Organization Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from healthcare organizations that want to enroll in the system. It's a crucial step for organizations to provide their details for proper system management and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your organization in the **Organization Name** field.
2. Provide your organization's address in the **Organization Address** field.
3. Enter your contact email in the **Contact Email** field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
5. Enter the name of the person to contact in the **Contact Name** field.
6. If applicable, select whether your organization provides healthcare services **Healthcare Providers** field.
7. Choose the **Enrollment Status** of your organization (Active or Inactive).
8. If applicable, provide the date when your organization was enrolled in the **Enrollment Date** field.
9. If applicable, select your time zone in the **Time Zone** field.
10. Enter any additional notes about your organization in the **Note** field.
11. Enter the number of patients your organization has enrolled in the **Patient Count** field.
12. Enter the date when this form was submitted in the **Enrollment Form Submission Date** field.
13. If applicable, enter the time zone offset for your organization in the **Time Zone Offset** field.
14. Enter the maximum number of patients your organization can handle in the **Patient Count Max** field.
15. Enter the unit of measurement for the maximum patient count in the **Patient Count Max Unit** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (text)**: Enter the name of your healthcare organization.
* **Organization Address (text)**: Enter your organization's physical address.
* **Contact Email (email)**: Enter the email address of the person to contact.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact.
* **Contact Name (text)**: Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Healthcare Providers (select multiple)**: If your organization provides healthcare services, select 'Yes'.
* **Enrollment Status (select one)**: Choose whether your organization is Active or Inactive.
* **Enrollment Date (date)**: If you're enrolling for the first time, enter the date of enrollment.
* **Time Zone (time)**: Select your time zone if different from the system's default time zone.
* **Note (note)**: Enter any additional notes about your organization.
* **Patient Count (number)**: Enter the number of patients your organization has enrolled.
* **Enrollment Form Submission Date (date)**: Enter the date when this form was submitted.
* **Time Zone Offset (number)**: Enter the time zone offset for your organization (if applicable).
* **Patient Count Max (number)**: Enter the maximum number of patients your organization can handle.
* **Patient Count Max Unit (text)**: Select the unit of measurement for the maximum patient count.
