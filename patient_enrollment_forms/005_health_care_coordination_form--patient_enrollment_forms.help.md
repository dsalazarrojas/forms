# Health Care Coordination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Care Coordination Form is designed to collect information from patients to facilitate the coordination of their medical care. This form gathers various details about the patient's medical history, contact information, allergies, medications, medical conditions, insurance, and other relevant health-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in each field with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank, but be sure to save your progress regularly.
4. Once you have filled out all fields, review the form for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter your full name, date of birth, and any other identifying details.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe your medical history, including any illnesses, surgeries, or previous injuries.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
- **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required): List the names, phone numbers, and relationships of individuals you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
- **Care Plan** (`care_plan`, text, required): Describe your current care plan, including any medication regimens or treatment plans.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
- **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): List all your medications, including dosages and frequencies.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): Describe any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.
- **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, required): List any recent or upcoming medical tests, including dates and results.
- **Insurance** (`insurance`, text, required): Provide your insurance information, including policy numbers and providers.
- **Patient Enrollment Information** (`patient_enrollment_info`, text, required): Enter your patient enrollment details, including dates and enrollment status.
- **Patient Enrollment Status** (`patient_enrollment_status`, text, required): Indicate your current patient enrollment status.
- **Referrals** (`referrals`, text, required): List any referrals to other healthcare providers or specialists.
- **Other Medical Info** (`other_medical_info`, text, required): Provide any additional medical information not covered by other fields.
- **Additional Medical History** (`medical_history_2`, text, required): Describe any additional medical history you would like to share.
- **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required): Describe your family medical history.
- **Health Status** (`health_status`, text, required): Report your current health status.
- **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text, required): Share any mental health concerns or issues.
- **Substance Abuse** (`substance_abuse`, text, required): Report any substance abuse issues or concerns.
- **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, text, required): Provide any notes or comments about your care.
- **Provider Info** (`provider_info`, text, required): Enter your healthcare provider's information, including name and contact details.
- **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, text, required): List the names and contact details of your healthcare team members.
- **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, required): Provide any other relevant medical information not covered by other fields.
- **Emergency Contacts 2** (`emergency_contacts_2`, text, required): List any additional emergency contacts.
- **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Select the appropriate option to indicate your consent and agreement to share your medical information.
