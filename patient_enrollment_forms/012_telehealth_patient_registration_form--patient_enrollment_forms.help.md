# Telehealth Patient Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Telehealth Patient Registration Form is a form designed to collect essential patient information and medical details for telehealth services. This form helps our team assess your eligibility and prepare your treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately. The form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields that we require to process your request.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your legal identification.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your legal identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`3`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Gender** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Email** (`5`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Medical History** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical history, please provide a brief description.
* **Medical Condition** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any medical conditions you have from the provided options.
* **Medication Allergies** (`9`, `text`, optional): If you have any medication allergies, please describe them.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`10`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`11`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please describe their relation to you.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`12`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their phone number.
* **Insurance Provider** (`13`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please provide your insurance provider information.
* **Medical Provider** (`14`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please provide your medical provider information.
* **Medical Record Number** (`15`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please provide your medical record number.
* **Medical Record Provider** (`16`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please provide the provider of your medical record.
* **Patient ID Number** (`17`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please provide your patient ID number.
* **Patient Signature** (`18`, `note`, required): Please sign and date this form to confirm your identity.
* **Provider Signature** (`19`, `note`, optional): Our provider will sign and date this form to confirm their role.
* **Upload File** (`20`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please upload any supporting documents.
* **Upload File Label** (`21`, `text`, optional): If you uploaded a file, please enter a label for it.
* **Upload File Type** (`22`, `select_one`, optional): Select the file type from the provided options.
* **Upload File Name** (`23`, `text`, optional): If you uploaded a file, please enter the file name.
* **Upload File Size** (`24`, `text`, optional): If you uploaded a file, please enter the file size.
* **Upload File Path** (`25`, `text`, optional): If you uploaded a file, please enter the file path.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely. If you're unsure about any field, you may contact us for assistance.
