# Diabetes Patient Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diabetes Patient Application Form is designed to collect relevant information from patients with diabetes for medical evaluation and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If applicable, enter your medical record ID in the "Medical Record ID" field.
6. Select your diabetes type from the list provided in the "Diabetes Type" field.
7. List any current diabetes medications in the "Diabetes Medication" field.
8. Provide any relevant medical conditions or history in the "Medical Condition" field.
9. Describe your treatment plan in the "Treatment Plan" field.
10. List any allergies you have in the "Allergies" field.
11. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
12. If applicable, enter your blood pressure reading in the "Blood Pressure" field.
13. Enter your height and weight in the "Height" and "Weight" fields, respectively.
14. Report your current blood sugar levels in the "Blood Sugar Levels" field.
15. List any diabetes-related complications or issues you are experiencing in the "Diabetes Complications" field.
16. Provide information on recent medical tests or exams you have undergone in the "Medical Tests" field.
17. Describe your recent treatment outcomes or progress in the "Treatment Outcomes" field.
18. Enter the date of your next follow-up appointment in the "Next Follow-up Date" field.
19. If applicable, select an assigned tool or equipment you are using in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, Text, Required): Please enter your first name as per your identity.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, Text, Required): Please enter your last name as per your identity.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Please enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Medical Record ID** (Text, Required): If you have a medical record ID, please enter it here. This is used for record-keeping purposes.
* **Diabetes Type** (Select One, Required): Select your diabetes type from the list provided.
* **Diabetes Medication** (Select Multiple, Optional): List any current diabetes medications you are taking.
* **Medical Condition** (Text, Optional): Provide any relevant medical conditions or history you would like to report.
* **Treatment Plan** (Text, Optional): Describe your treatment plan and goals.
* **Allergies** (Text, Optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Blood Pressure** (Number, Optional): If applicable, enter your current blood pressure reading.
* **Height** (Text, Optional): Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight** (Text, Optional): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Blood Sugar Levels** (Number, Optional): Report your current blood sugar levels.
* **Diabetes Complications** (Text, Optional): List any diabetes-related complications or issues you are experiencing.
* **Medical Tests** (Text, Optional): Provide information on recent medical tests or exams you have undergone.
* **Treatment Outcomes** (Text, Optional): Describe your recent treatment outcomes or progress.
* **Follow-up Appointments** (Text, Optional): Report your follow-up appointments.
* **Next Follow-up Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional): If applicable, select an assigned tool or equipment you are using.
* **Doctor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.

Tips:

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* You may leave optional fields blank if not applicable.
* If you are unsure of any field, please discuss with your healthcare provider before filling out this form.
* This form is meant to be a starting point for your medical evaluation and treatment planning. Your healthcare provider will review and discuss your submitted form with you to tailor a comprehensive care plan suited to your needs.
