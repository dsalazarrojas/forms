# Dropbox Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic, medical, and contact information from patients for intake purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
4. Enter your **Email** address.
5. Enter your **Phone** number.
6. Enter your **Medical Record Number** (if applicable).
7. Enter the **Medical Record Date** (if applicable).
8. Enter the name of your **Medical Record Provider** (if applicable).
9. Enter your **Address**.
10. Enter any relevant **Medical History**.
11. List any **Medication Allergies**.
12. List any **Medical Conditions**.
13. Enter any additional **Medical History** details.
14. Enter your **Social Security Number**.
15. Enter any **Medical Provider Info** (if applicable).
16. Enter any relevant **Medical Test Results** (if applicable).
17. Enter the name of your **Medical Provider** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: The name the patient is known by. (Required) (`first_name`, `text`, Required)
* **Last Name**: The surname of the patient. (Required) (`last_name`, `text`, Required)
* **Date of Birth**: The patient's date of birth in `MM/DD/YYYY` format. (Required) (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required)
* **Email**: The patient's email address. (Required) (`email`, `email`, Required)
* **Phone**: The patient's phone number. (Optional) (`phone`, `text`, Optional)
* **Medical Record Number**: The patient's medical record number. (Optional) (`medical_record_number`, `text`, Optional)
* **Medical Record Date**: The date associated with the patient's medical record. (Optional) (`medical_record_date`, `date`, Optional)
* **Medical Record Provider**: The name of the patient's medical record provider. (Optional) (`medical_record_provider`, `text`, Optional)
* **Address**: The patient's address. (Optional) (`address`, `text`, Optional)
* **Medical History**: Any relevant medical history of the patient. (Optional) (`medical_history`, `text`, Optional)
* **Medication Allergies**: Any medication allergies of the patient. (Optional) (`medication_allergies`, `text`, Optional)
* **Medical Conditions**: Any medical conditions the patient has. (Optional) (`medical_conditions`, `text`, Optional)
* **Medical History Other**: Any additional medical history details. (Optional) (`medical_history_other`, `text`, Optional)
* **Social Security Number**: The patient's social security number. (Optional) (`social_security_number`, `text`, Optional)
* **Medical Provider Info**: Any additional medical provider information. (Optional) (`medical_provider_info`, `text`, Optional)
* **Medical Test Results**: Any medical test results. (Optional) (`medical_test_results`, `text`, Optional)
* **Medical Provider**: The name of the patient's medical provider. (Optional) (`medical_provider`, `text`, Optional)
