# Osteopathic New Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from new clients to create their profile and ensure they can access healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form at the end of the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing basic details about yourself, such as your name, contact information, and other relevant information.
* **Insurance Information** (`Insurance Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering your insurance details, including policy number, provider, and other relevant information.
* **Medical Information** (`Medical Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is for sharing your medical history, allergies, or other relevant health information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required: false): This field is for listing the contact details of an emergency contact person, including their name, phone number, and relationship with you.
