# Blank FHO Enrolment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blank FHO Enrolment Form is a patient intake form designed to collect essential information from patients for healthcare services. This form is used to gather personal details, address, and contact information for the purpose of enrolling patients into our healthcare system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details and information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
3. Review and sign the consent section before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Patient Information --
* **Patient full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of birth**: Provide your birth date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Health card number**: Enter your provincial health identifier number.
* **Address**: Fill out your current street address and postal code.
* **Phone number**: Enter your best contact number.

### Enrolment Information
* **Emergency contact name**: If you have an emergency contact, provide their name.
* **Emergency contact phone**: If you have an emergency contact, provide their phone number.
* **Primary care reason**: Briefly explain the reason for your enrolment.
* **Consent signature name**: Sign your name to indicate consent for data collection and processing.

### Tips
* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, reach out to your healthcare provider for assistance.
* Remember to keep your contact information up-to-date to ensure smooth communication.
