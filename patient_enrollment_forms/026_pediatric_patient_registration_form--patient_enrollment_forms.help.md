# Pediatric Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from pediatric patients, their parents or guardians, and their emergency contacts. It's crucial for our healthcare team to have accurate and up-to-date information to provide the best possible care. Please ensure that you're filling out this form for the right purpose and not duplicating efforts.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **General Info** section with the patient's basic details.
2. Provide the patient's **Contact Info** in the next section, if applicable.
3. In the **Emergency Info** section, fill in the emergency contact's details, if applicable.
4. In the **Parent Info** section, provide the parent or guardian's details.
5. Finally, provide any relevant **Medical Info** about the patient, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Info (<label>General Info</label>, <type>text</type>, **required**)
This section collects the patient's basic information such as name, age, and date of birth.

*   *Name*: Please fill in the patient's full name.
*   *Age*: Enter the patient's age in years.
*   *Date of Birth*: Provide the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Contact Info (<label>Contact Info</label>, <type>text</type>, **optional**)
This section is for providing the patient's contact information, including phone number and email.

*   *Contact Name*: If the patient has a preferred contact name, please provide it here.
*   *Phone Number*: Enter the patient's phone number.
*   *Email*: Provide the patient's email address.

### Emergency Info (<label>Emergency Info</label>, <type>text</type>, **required**)
This section is for providing emergency contact information for the patient.

*   *Emergency Contact Name*: Provide the name of the emergency contact.
*   *Relationship*: Indicate the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient (e.g., parent, guardian, sibling, etc.).
*   *Phone Number*: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
*   *Address*: Provide the emergency contact's address.

### Parent Info (<label>Parent Info</label>, <type>text</type>, **required**)
This section is for providing parent or guardian information.

*   *Parent Name*: Provide the parent or guardian's name.
*   *Relationship*: Indicate the relationship between the parent or guardian and the patient (e.g., mother, father, guardian, etc.).
*   *Phone Number*: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
*   *Address*: Provide the parent or guardian's address.

### Medical Info (<label>Medical Info</label>, <type>text</type>, **optional**)
This section is for providing any relevant medical information about the patient.

*   *Allergies*: If the patient has any allergies, please list them.
*   *Medications*: If the patient is taking any medications, list them.
*   *Medical Condition*: If the patient has any medical conditions, describe them.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   If you're unsure about any information, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
*   This form is for pediatric patients only. If you're registering an adult patient, please use the adult patient registration form.
