# Remedial Massage Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remedial Massage Intake Form is designed to collect information from clients to ensure they receive the best possible experience during their remedial massage session. This form gathers details about the client, their session preferences, and any medical information that may be relevant to the massage therapist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions in the **Client Information** page, providing your contact details and any relevant health information.
2. On the **Session Info** page, provide details about the type of massage you would like to receive and any specific areas of tension you would like to target.
3. If you have any medical information that may be relevant to your massage session, please provide it in the **Medical Information** page.
4. Finally, on the **Payment Information** page, provide the payment details for your session.
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please feel free to include them in the **Additional Comments** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information, text, required)**: Please provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you to confirm your appointment and answer any questions you may have.
* **Session Info (Session Info, text, required)**: Describe the type of massage you would like to receive (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue, hot stone) and any areas of tension you would like to target (e.g., neck, back, legs).
* **Medical Information (Medical Information, text, optional)**: If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns that may impact your massage session, please provide this information so we can better understand your needs.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information, text, required)**: Please provide your payment method and details (e.g., credit card, cash, check).
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional)**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, please feel free to include them here.
