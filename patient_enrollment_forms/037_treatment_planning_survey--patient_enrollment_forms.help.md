# Treatment Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treatment Planning Survey form is designed to collect comprehensive information from patients to aid healthcare professionals in planning their treatment. This form is likely used in a clinical or hospital setting to gather essential details about the patient's medical history, preferences, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Patient Enrollment Form on Page 1, providing your personal details such as your phone number and email.
2. Answer the questions on Page 2, which cover various medical aspects, including your medical history, allergies, medical procedures, and current medical conditions.
3. Select the relevant options for each question, making sure to answer accurately and truthfully.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Enrollment Form** (`patient_enrollment_form`, text, optional): This form is for your personal details, including your phone number and email.
* **Treatment Planning Survey** (`treatment_planning_survey`, text, optional): This form is for your medical history, preferences, and needs.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Please enter the date of your visit to the healthcare provider.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit to the healthcare provider.
* **Treatment Planning Survey Notes** (`treatment_planning_survey_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or information about your treatment planning.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Doctor Phone Extension** (`doctor_phone_extension`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's phone extension for contact purposes.
* **Doctor Contact** (`doctor_contact`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's contact information.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter your insurance provider information.
* **Insurance Provider Phone** (`insurance_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter your insurance provider's phone number.
* **Insurance Provider Email** (`insurance_provider_email`, email, optional): Enter your insurance provider's email address.
* **Medical Records Number** (`medical_records_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical records number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please describe your medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any allergies. Please select "Yes" if you have an allergy and "No" if you do not.
* **Medical Procedure Requests** (`medical_procedure_requests`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any medical procedure requests. Please select "Yes" if you have a medical procedure request and "No" if you do not.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe your current medical condition.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, optional): Please select your patient status, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Patient Caregiver Status** (`patient_caregiver_status`, select_one, optional): Please select your patient caregiver status, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Patient Caregiver Relationship** (`patient_caregiver_relationship`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have a patient caregiver. Please select "Yes" if you have a caregiver and "No" if you do not.
* **Patient Medication List** (`patient_medication_list`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you are on a patient medication list. Please select "Yes" if you are on a list and "No" if you are not.
* **Patient Medications** (`patient_medications`, select_multiple, optional): List your current medications.
* **Patient Vital Signs** (`patient_vital_signs`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any vital signs concerns. Please select "Yes" if you have a concern and "No" if you do not.
* **Medical Procedure Notes** (`medical_procedure_notes`, select_multiple, optional): Provide any additional notes about your medical procedures.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure completeness and accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
* This form is for your benefit and will help your healthcare provider better understand your needs.
* Please take the time to answer all fields truthfully and accurately to ensure the best possible care for your needs.
