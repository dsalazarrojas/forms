# Chronic Care Program Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Care Program Lead Form is a patient-facing form designed to collect necessary information for enrolling patients in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's personal details, including their full name, date of birth, gender, email address, phone number, and address information.
2. List your current medications and medical history.
3. Provide your insurance provider, insurance ID number, and primary care physician's name and phone number.
4. Select your chronic conditions and indicate the time since first diagnosis.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone call, text message, email, mail, or patient portal).
6. Specify your preferred language for communication.
7. Indicate your transportation needs and whether you have a caregiver.
8. Provide your consent to participate in the program and acknowledge your understanding of your privacy rights.
9. Add any additional comments you would like to make.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
### **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
### **Gender** (3, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
### **Email Address** (4, email, required): Enter your email address for program communication.
### **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Enter your phone number for appointment reminders.
### **Street Address** (7, text, required): Enter your full address.
### **City** (8, text, required): Enter the city of your address.
### **State or Province** (9, text, required): Enter the state or province of your address.
### **Postal Code** (10, text, required): Enter the postal code of your address.
### **Insurance Provider** (12, text, required): Enter your insurance provider's name.
### **Insurance ID Number** (13, text, required): Enter your member ID number.
### **Primary Care Physician** (14, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of your primary care physician.
### **Chronic Conditions** (17, select_multiple, required): Select all the chronic conditions that apply to you.
### **How Long Diagnosed** (18, select_one, required): Select when you were first diagnosed with a chronic condition.
### **Current Medications** (19, text, required): List all your current medications.
### **Program Interest** (21, select_one, required): Select why you are interested in the program.
### **Program Goals** (22, select_multiple, required): Select what you hope to achieve through the program.
### **Preferred Contact Method** (24, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be contacted.
### **Best Time to Contact** (25, select_one, required): Select when you are available for contact.
### **Language Preference** (26, select_one, required): Select your preferred language for communication.
### **Transportation Needs** (28, select_one, required): Indicate if you need transportation help.
### **Caregiver Support** (29, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a caregiver.
### **Consent to Participate** (31, select_one, required): Select to confirm your agreement to participate in the program.
### **Privacy Acknowledgment** (32, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your privacy rights.
### **Additional Comments** (33, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
