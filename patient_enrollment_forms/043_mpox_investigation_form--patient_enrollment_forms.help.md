# Mpox Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Investigation Form is used to collect information from patients suspected to have been exposed to mpox, which is a serious skin disease. Healthcare providers or contact tracers use this form to gather details about the patient's exposure history, including their contact information, symptoms, and travel history. This information is essential for contact tracing and outbreak investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section with accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mpox Case Description**: A brief description of the case (optional). This is a free-text field where healthcare providers or contact tracers can provide a brief account of the case.
* **Case Type**: A select box with four options: Community Outbreak, Travel Related, Occupational, and Household. This field helps healthcare providers or contact tracers understand the category of the mpox exposure.
* **Patient Name**: The name of the patient (optional). Please ensure that the name is spelled correctly.
* **Contact Number**: The contact number or phone number of the patient (optional). This field is used for follow-up contact and investigation.
* **Mpox Exposure Location**: A select box with four options: Home, Work, School, and Unknown. This field helps healthcare providers or contact tracers understand where the patient was exposed to mpox.
* **Healthcare Provider**: The name of the healthcare provider who treated the patient (optional). This field is used to track the patient's medical history.
* **Patient DOB (Date of Birth)**: The date of birth of the patient (optional). This field is used to verify the patient's age and identity.
* **Symptoms**: A brief description of the patient's symptoms (optional). This is a free-text field where healthcare providers or contact tracers can provide a brief account of the patient's symptoms.
* **Travel History**: A brief description of the patient's travel history (optional). This is a free-text field where healthcare providers or contact tracers can provide a brief account of the patient's travel history.
* **Travel Date**: The date the patient traveled (optional). This field is used to track the patient's travel history.
* **Case Status**: A select box with four options: Active, Resolved, Recovered, and Removed. This field helps healthcare providers or contact tracers understand the current status of the case.
* **Exposure Start Date**: The date when the patient was exposed to mpox (optional). This field is used to track the exposure history.
* **Exposure Duration**: The duration of the exposure (optional). This field helps healthcare providers or contact tracers understand how long the patient was exposed to mpox.
* **Symptoms Start Date**: The date when the patient started experiencing symptoms (optional). This field is used to track the symptoms' onset.
* **Case Type**: A select box with four options: Community Outbreak, Travel Related, Occupational, and Household. This field is used to categorize the case.
* **Country of Travel**: A select box with four options: USA, Africa, Asia, Europe, and Other. This field helps healthcare providers or contact tracers understand the patient's travel history.
* **Additional Comments**: A free-text field for any additional information or comments. This field is used to capture any further information about the case.
