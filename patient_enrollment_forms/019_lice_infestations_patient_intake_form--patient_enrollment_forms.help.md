# Lice Infestations Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lice Infestations Patient Intake Form is designed for collecting patient information and history related to lice infestations from medical professionals or health services. This form will guide you through the process of filling out the form, explaining each field's purpose and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's information on page 1, providing any required contact details.
2. On page 2, report your current allergy status and medical history.
3. On page 3, describe your lice infestation history, including any previous treatments and symptoms.
4. On page 4, list any medications you are currently taking.
5. On page 5-6, report any lice infestation-related events, such as relocations, outbreaks, and follow-up status.
6. On page 7, provide any additional comments or recommendations about your lice infestation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in your patient's basic information, such as name, contact details, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Provide any contact details necessary for follow-up or further medical assistance.
* **Allergy Status** (`allergy_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your current allergy status from the options provided (Lice, Mites, Fleas, Other).
* **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your current medical status from the options provided (Healthy, Sick, Pregnant, Other).
* **Lice Infestation History** (`lice_infestation_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed history of your lice infestations.
* **Lice Symptoms** (`lice_symptoms`, `text`, required: false): Describe any symptoms you have experienced due to lice infestations.
* **Lice Treatment History** (`lice_treatment_history`, `text`, required: false): Report any previous lice treatment experiences.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): List any contact details, such as phone numbers or email addresses, for further assistance.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Report any relevant medical history.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Lice Relocation History** (`lice_relocation_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you have ever relocated to an area with lice infestation (True, False, Other).
* **Lice Control Measures** (`lice_control_measures`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any lice control measures you have taken (True, False, Other).
* **Lice Outbreak** (`lice_outbreak`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Report if you have experienced an lice outbreak (True, False, Other).
* **Lice Control Measures** ( duplicate field, `select_multiple`, required: false): Similar to Lice Control Measures above.
* **Lice Treatment Outcome** (`lice_treatment_outcome`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Report the outcome of any lice treatment you have undergone (Success, Failure, Other).
* **Lice Follow-Up** (`lice_follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you have followed up with medical professionals for lice treatments (True, False, Other).
* **Lice Recommendations** (`lice_recommendations`, `text`, required: false): Provide any recommendations or comments about your lice infestation.
* **Lice Comments** (`lice_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your lice infestation.
* **Other Allergies** (`other_allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Report if you have other allergies (True, False, Other).
* **Lice Allergies** (`lice_allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Report if you have lice allergies (True, False, Other).
* **Other Medical History** (`other_medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Report any other medical history.
* **Lice History** (`lice_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe your lice infestation history.
* **Lice Status** (`lice_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Report your lice status (Healthy, Sick, Other).
* **Lice Outbreak Status** (`lice_outbreak_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Report if you have experienced an lice outbreak (True, False, Other).
* **Lice Follow-Up Status** (`lice_follow_up_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Report if you have followed up with medical professionals for lice treatments (True, False, Other).

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact the healthcare provider or medical institution.
* This form is designed to help medical professionals better understand your lice infestation, so please provide as much information as possible.
