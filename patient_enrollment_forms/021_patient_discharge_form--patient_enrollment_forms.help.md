# Patient Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Discharge Form is used to document a patient's discharge from a medical facility, capturing vital information to ensure a smooth and safe discharge process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Go to the first page, "Patient Information", and enter the patient's details as required.
*   Proceed to the "Medical Facility" page and select the correct facility from the list.
*   In the "Primary Care Provider" page, select all the relevant care providers (e.g., PCP, NP, EP) who have been involved in the patient's care.
*   On the "Date of Discharge" page, select the date of the patient's discharge.
*   Choose the "Discharge Status" of the patient from the options provided.
*   On the "Medication List" page, provide any medications the patient is currently taking or will be taking upon discharge.
*   On the "Medications" page, indicate whether the patient is taking medications or not.
*   In the "Allergies" page, select any allergies the patient has or is suspected to have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (page 1, text): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
*   **Medical Facility** (page 2, select_one): Select the hospital, clinic, or urgent care where the patient is being discharged from.
*   **Primary Care Provider** (page 3, select_multiple): Select all the primary care providers (e.g., PCP, NP, EP) who have been involved in the patient's care.
*   **Date of Discharge** (page 4, date): Enter the date of the patient's discharge.
*   **Discharge Status** (page 5, select_one): Choose the patient's discharge status from the options provided (e.g., Discharged, Transferred, Deceased).
*   **Medication List** (page 6, note): Provide a list of the patient's current medications or any medications they will be taking upon discharge.
*   **Medications** (page 7, select_multiple): Indicate whether the patient is taking medications or not.
*   **Allergies** (page 8, select_multiple): Select any allergies the patient has or is suspected to have.

## Tips
*   Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   Verify the patient's details with the patient before submitting the form.
*   Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
