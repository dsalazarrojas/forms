# Patient Admission And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Admission And Consent Form is used to collect essential information about a patient's identity, contact information, medical history, and consent for treatment. This form is designed to be filled out in a single sitting, and all required fields must be completed before moving on to the next page.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the form page by page**, ensuring that all required fields are completed before moving on to the next page.
2. **Fill out the form fields as instructed**:
	* **Patient Identification**: Enter your patient's identification information, such as their name and date of birth.
	* **Contact Information**: Enter your patient's contact information, including their address and phone number.
	* **Medical History**: Describe any significant medical history or conditions that may be relevant to the patient's treatment.
	* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
	* **Consent Form**: Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate consent for treatment.
	* **Contact Preferences**: Indicate how you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name, phone number, and address of an emergency contact.
	* **Medical Insurance**: Enter the patient's medical insurance information, including their payer ID.
	* **Next of Kin**: Enter the name and contact information of the next of kin.
	* **Medication Allergies**: List any medication allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
	* **Medical History Other**: Describe any other medical history or conditions that may be relevant to the patient's treatment.
	* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
	* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
	* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
	* **Patient Consent**: Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate consent for treatment.

## Tips
