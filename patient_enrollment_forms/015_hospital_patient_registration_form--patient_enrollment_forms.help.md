# Hospital Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Patient Registration Form is a critical tool for collecting patient information for treatment and care. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly will help our medical team provide the best possible care for you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully and completely.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you're unsure about any information, consult with a medical professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter your current address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Patient Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for medical emergencies.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for secure communication with medical staff.
* **Patient Medical History** (`patient_medical_history`, text, required): Provide a brief description of any medical conditions, allergies, or surgeries you've experienced.
* **Medical Conditions** (`patient_medical_conditions`, text, required): List any medical conditions or illnesses you're currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, text, required): List any allergies, including medications or substances you're allergic to.
* **Medications** (`patient_medications`, text, required): List any medications you're currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Next of Kin** (`patient_next_of_kin`, text, required): Identify a next of kin or emergency contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship to Next of Kin** (`patient_relationship_to_kin`, text, required): Describe your relationship to the next of kin (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required): Provide contact information for your emergency contact, including name and phone number.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
