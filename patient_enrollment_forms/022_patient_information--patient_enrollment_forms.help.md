# Patient Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Information form is used to gather data about a new patient. This form is used for administrative purposes and is not intended for clinical or medical diagnosis. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to ensure proper patient registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "New Patient" to indicate whether the form is for a new patient or not.
2. Provide the patient's contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Enter any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Patient**: (`new_patient`, select_one, optional) 
Indicates whether the form is for a new patient. Select "Yes" if the patient is new, "No" if the patient already has an existing registration.
* **Contact Info**: (`contact_info`, text, optional) 
Enter the patient's contact information, including name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History**: (`medical_history`, text, optional) 
Provide any relevant medical history, including previous diagnoses, allergies, or medical conditions that may be relevant to the patient's care.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to ensure proper patient registration.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* For multiple patients, please fill out this form separately for each patient.
