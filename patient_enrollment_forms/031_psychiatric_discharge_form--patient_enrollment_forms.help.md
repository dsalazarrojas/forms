# psychiatric_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The psychiatric_discharge_form is a critical document used to capture essential information for the discharge process of a patient receiving psychiatric treatment. This form helps healthcare professionals gather details about the patient's treatment history, diagnosis, medical history, and post-discharge instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by filling out the Patient Information page (page 1), which asks for basic details such as the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant medical history or allergies.
- Next, proceed to the Medical History page (page 2), where you can document the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, illnesses, or allergies.
- On the Diagnosis page (page 3), select the relevant diagnosis from the provided options.
- On the Treatment Plan page (page 4), outline any treatment plans or instructions for the patient's post-discharge care.
- For Follow-up Appointments (page 5), indicate any scheduled follow-up appointments or check-ups.
- On the Medications page (page 6), select any medications the patient is currently taking or needs to take post-discharge.
- Finally, provide Contact Information (page 7), which includes contact details for the patient or their representative.
- End by obtaining the patient's signature (page 8) to confirm their understanding of the discharge instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`Patient Information`, `text`, required/optional): This section is used to gather basic details about the patient, including their name, address, and any relevant medical history or allergies.
* **Medical History (Page 2)** (`Medical History`, `text`, required/optional): Document the patient's medical history, including previous diagnoses, illnesses, or allergies.
* **Diagnosis (Page 3)** (`Diagnosis`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the relevant diagnosis from the provided options.
* **Treatment Plan (Page 4)** (`Treatment Plan`, `text`, required/optional): Outline any treatment plans or instructions for the patient's post-discharge care.
* **Follow-up Appointments (Page 5)** (`Follow-up Appointments`, `date`, required/optional): Indicate any scheduled follow-up appointments or check-ups.
* **Medications (Page 6)** (`Medications`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any medications the patient is currently taking or needs to take post-discharge.
* **Contact Information (Page 7)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide contact details for the patient or their representative.
* **Patient Signature (Page 8)** (`Patient Signature`, `number`, required/optional): Obtain the patient's signature to confirm their understanding of the discharge instructions.
