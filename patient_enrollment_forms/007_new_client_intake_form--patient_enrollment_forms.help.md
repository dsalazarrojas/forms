# New Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Intake Form is designed to collect information from new clients for onboarding, data collection, and client management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the requested information. Make sure to provide accurate and detailed answers to the best of your ability.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
3. Use the available options for select fields to choose the most appropriate response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the client.
* **Client Company** (`client_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client's company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Provide the client's email address for correspondence.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number for communication.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional): Enter the client's physical address.
* **Note** (`new_client_intake_form_7`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information or notes about the client.
* **Service Selection** (`new_client_intake_form_8`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose one of the available options to select the service you wish to use.
* **Service Selection (Multiple)** (`new_client_intake_form_9`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose multiple services from the available options.
* **Onboarding Date** (`new_client_intake_form_10`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to onboard the client.
* **Onboarding Time** (`new_client_intake_form_11`, time, optional): Enter the time you wish to onboard the client.

Note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and detailed information will help ensure a smooth onboarding process.
