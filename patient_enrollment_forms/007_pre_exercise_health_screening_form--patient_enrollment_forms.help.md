# Pre-Exercise Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre-Exercise Health Screening Form is designed to gather essential information about a patient's health status before beginning an exercise program. This form helps the healthcare provider to assess the patient's suitability for physical activity and identify any potential risks or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the first question about medical screenings.
2. In the medical conditions field, type any medical conditions that may affect your ability to exercise.
3. List any medications or supplements you're currently taking.
4. Describe any allergies or sensitivities you have.
5. Provide a brief history of your medical history, including any previous injuries or illnesses.
6. Identify any health risk factors that may affect your exercise participation.
7. State your personal exercise goals.
8. List the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
9. In the exercise precautions field, provide any specific instructions or precautions your healthcare provider should be aware of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Screening Form** (`screen_1`): Answer this question with either "Yes" or "No", indicating whether you've undergone medical screenings or have other medical conditions that may affect your ability to exercise.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): List any medical conditions that may impact your ability to exercise, such as diabetes, hypertension, or heart conditions.
- **Medications** (`medications`): Report any medications or supplements you're currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`): Describe any allergies or sensitivities you have, including any reactions or severity.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Provide a brief history of your medical history, including any previous injuries or illnesses.
- **Health Risk Factors** (`health_risk_factors`): Identify any health risk factors that may affect your exercise participation, such as smoking, obesity, or previous heart conditions.
- **Goals** (`goals`): Clearly state your personal exercise goals, including any specific objectives or expectations.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): List the name, phone number, and email of an emergency contact person.
- **Exercise Precautions** (`exercise_precautions`): Provide any specific instructions or precautions your healthcare provider should be aware of, such as joint restrictions, mobility limitations, or equipment requirements.
