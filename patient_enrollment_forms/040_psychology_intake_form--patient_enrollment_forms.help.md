# Psychology Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychology Intake Form is a comprehensive questionnaire designed to gather information about a patient's demographic data, mental health history, medical history, and other relevant details. This form will help our clinicians assess your mental health status and provide personalized support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out your demographic information, including your name, contact details, and emergency contact information.
2. Provide an overview of your mental health history, including any past diagnoses, medications, and treatment experiences.
3. Specify any substance use history, including frequency and duration.
4. Describe your family medical history, including any genetic predispositions or family members with mental health conditions.
5. Outline your physical health information, including any chronic conditions or medical treatments.
6. List your medical history, including previous surgeries, hospitalizations, or significant medical events.
7. Provide details about your military history, if applicable.
8. Share information about your cultural background and cultural identity.
9. Specify your educational background and work history.
10. Describe your relationship history, including any romantic or familial relationships.
11. Outline your social activities and leisure habits.
12. Discuss your family dynamics and relationship with your family members.
13. Describe your personality traits and behaviors.
14. Outline your behavior history, including any notable behaviors or habits.
15. Share your counseling history, including any past therapy experiences.
16. Provide details about your mental health diagnoses, if applicable.
17. List any allergies or medical conditions that may impact your treatment.

## Tips
* Please answer each question accurately and honestly, as this information will help us better understand your mental health status.
* If you are unsure about any question or have difficulty filling out a specific field, please feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is intended to be comprehensive, so please be thorough in your responses.
* If you have any additional information you'd like to share or would like to discuss your responses, please include it in the comments section or with your clinician.
