# Mpox Self Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Self Screening Questionnaire is a form designed to identify potential cases of Mpox in individuals. It gathers information about symptoms, recent travel, and medical attention received for these symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. Enter dates in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Select all relevant symptoms from the list.
4. Answer the vaccination and medication questions based on your actual experience.
5. If you have received vaccination or medication, enter the correct date and dose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction**: This section is a general introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### 2. Symptoms
* **Symptoms**: Select all symptoms you are experiencing from the list below. Mpox can present with a variety of symptoms, including fever, rash, muscle pain, joint pain, swollen lymph nodes, fatigue, headache, and sore throat. If you are unsure about any symptom, please do not select it.

### 3. Recent Travel
* **Recent Travel**: Enter the dates of your recent international travel. If you have not traveled recently, please enter "None".

### 4. Sexual Contacts
* **Sexual Contacts**: Select all relevant options from the list below. This information helps identify potential transmission routes of Mpox. If you are unsure about your sexual contacts, please do not select any options.

### 5. Monkeypox Vaccination
* **Monkeypox Vaccination**: Answer "True" if you have received the Mpox vaccination and "False" otherwise.

### 6. Monkeypox Test Result
* **Monkeypox Test Result**: Answer "Positive" if you have received a positive test result for Mpox and "Negative" otherwise.

### 7. When Did Your Symptoms Start
* **When Did Your Symptoms Start**: Enter the date when you first experienced symptoms. This helps track the onset of symptoms.

### 8. How Long Have Your Symptoms Lasted
* **How Long Have Your Symptoms Lasted**: Enter the duration of your symptoms.

### 9. Have You Received Medical Attention
* **Have You Received Medical Attention**: Answer "True" if you have received medical attention for these symptoms.

### 10. Monkeypox Medication
* **Monkeypox Medication**: Answer "True" if you have taken medication for Mpox and "False" otherwise.

### 11. Name of Medication
* **Name of Medication**: If you have taken medication, enter the actual name of the medication.

### 12. Dose of Medication
* **Dose of Medication**: If you have taken medication, enter the dose you took.

### 13. When Did You Start Medication
* **When Did You Start Medication**: If you have started medication, enter the date when you first took it.

### 14. When Did You Finish Medication
* **When Did You Finish Medication**: If you have finished medication, enter the date when you last took it.

### 15. Have You Experienced Any Side Effects
* **Have You Experienced Any Side Effects**: Answer "True" if you have experienced side effects from the medication and "False" otherwise.

### 16. Describe Side Effects
* **Describe Side Effects**: If you have experienced side effects, describe them in detail.

### 17. Where Did You Receive Vaccination
* **Where Did You Receive Vaccination**: If you have received vaccination, enter the location where you got it.

### 18. What Dose of Vaccination
* **What Dose of Vaccination**: If you have received vaccination, enter the actual dose you received.

**Tips:**

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any question, please do not select any option.
* This form is for self-screening purposes only. If you suspect you have Mpox, please contact your healthcare provider immediately.
