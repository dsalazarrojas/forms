# Health Nutrition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from patients about their health and nutrition goals, dietary habits, and medical history. The form is used to help health coaches and nutritionists understand the patient's needs and create personalized nutrition plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their contact details.
2. Provide details about their dietary habits and preferences.
3. Share information about their medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions.
4. Specify any medications they are taking.
5. Mention any food preferences or dislikes.
6. Enter the names and contact details of their emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (Required/Optional): Provide a brief description of the patient, including their name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (Required/Optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including their phone number and email address.
* **Dietary Information** (Required/Optional): Describe the patient's current dietary habits, including their eating habits, meal frequency, and any dietary restrictions.
* **Wellness Goals** (Required/Optional): Specify the patient's wellness goals, such as weight loss or improving overall health.
* **Nutrition Plans** (Required/Optional): Share any existing nutrition plans or recommendations the patient has received from a healthcare professional.
* **Health Coaches** (Required/Optional): List the names and contact details of any health coaches or nutritionists the patient is working with.
* **Emergency Contact** (Required/Optional): Enter the name and contact information of the patient's emergency contact person.
* **Medical History** (Required/Optional): Share information about the patient's medical history, including any chronic conditions or allergies.
* **Medications** (Required/Optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (Required/Optional): Specify any food allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Food Preferences** (Required/Optional): Describe the patient's food preferences, including any dislikes or dislikes.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when filling out the form to ensure accurate and effective nutrition planning.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please consult with a healthcare professional.
* Please note that this form is not a substitute for a medical consultation, and patients should always consult with a healthcare professional before making any significant changes to their diet or lifestyle.
