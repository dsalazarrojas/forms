# Eye Exam Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eye Exam Form Template is designed to collect patient data for an eye exam. This form helps healthcare professionals gather necessary information for accurate diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Patient Information, Date of Birth, Exam Date, Medical Specialty, Referring Doctor, Reason for Visit, and Referral Source.
2. Select the relevant medical conditions for the patient from the options provided.
3. Provide additional notes for Patient Medical History and Referral Reason.
4. Confirm the referral status and patient status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's personal details, including their name and other identifying information.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Exam Date**: Enter the date of the eye exam.
* **Medical Specialty**: Select the relevant medical specialty, such as Ophthalmology or Cardiology.
* **Referring Doctor**: Enter the name of the doctor referring the patient.
* **Reason for Visit**: Select the reason for the patient's visit (e.g., New Patient, Follow-up, etc.).
* **Patient Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history for the patient.
* **Glaucoma**: Select any glaucoma-related issues (e.g., Glaucoma, Other).
* **Cataracts**: Select any cataracts-related issues (e.g., Cataract, Other).
* **Diabetes**: Select any diabetes-related issues (e.g., Diabetes, Other).
* **Hypertension**: Select any hypertension-related issues (e.g., Hypertension, Other).
* **Macular Degeneration**: Select any macular degeneration-related issues (e.g., Macular Degeneration, Other).
* **Myopia**: Select any myopia-related issues (e.g., Myopia, Other).
* **Astigmat**: Select any astigmat-related issues (e.g., Astigmat, Other).
* **Referral**: Select any referral-related issues (e.g., Referral, Other).
* **Referral Source**: Confirm whether the referral source is Yes or No.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Referral Type**: Confirm whether the referral is Yes or No.
* **Referral Status**: Select the status of the referral (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Patient Status**: Select the status of the patient (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Patient Status Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patient's status.

Note: The "Other" option is available for conditions that are not listed in the provided options.
