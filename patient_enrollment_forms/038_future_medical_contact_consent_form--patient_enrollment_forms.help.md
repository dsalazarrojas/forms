# Future Medical Contact Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from a patient or their authorized contact for the sharing of medical information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name and contact information, including their phone number, email, address, and medical provider.
2. Select the relationship between the patient and contact person.
3. Choose who you wish to share medical information with, and whether you give permission for them to share it.
4. Sign and date the form to indicate your understanding and agreement to share medical information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (1, text, required): The name of the patient who is signing this form.
* **Contact Name** (2, text, required): The name of the person signing on behalf of the patient, if different from the patient themselves.
* **Relationship** (3, text, required): The relationship between the patient and the contact person (e.g. parent, guardian, spouse, etc.).
* **Contact Phone** (4, text, optional): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (5, email, optional): The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (6, text, optional): The contact address of the contact person.
* **Medical Provider** (7, text, optional): The name of the medical provider or facility that will be receiving the medical information.
* **Medical Record Number** (8, text, optional): The medical record number associated with the patient's medical information.
* **Medical Record Type** (9, text, optional): The type of medical record (e.g. electronic, paper, etc.).
* **Medical Information to Share** (10, text, optional): The specific medical information to be shared (e.g. lab results, medical history, etc.).
* **Sharing Permission** (11, select_multiple, required): Check all the boxes to indicate who you give permission to share medical information with (e.g. doctor, hospital, insurance company, etc.).
* **Signature Date** (12, date, required): The date the form was signed.
* **Signed By** (13, text, optional): The name of the person signing the form.
* **Contact Signature Date** (14, date, optional): The date the contact person signed the form.
* **Contact Signed By** (15, text, optional): The name of the contact person who signed the form.
* **Consent Date** (16, date, required): The date the patient or contact person gave their consent to share medical information.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you sign the form on the date indicated in the **Consent Date** field.
- Make sure to review the **Sharing Permission** field carefully to ensure that you are giving permission to the correct individuals or organizations.
- If you need to share medical information with multiple entities, you can select multiple options in the **Sharing Permission** field.
- The **Consent Date** field indicates when the patient or contact person gave their consent to share medical information, not when you signed the form.
