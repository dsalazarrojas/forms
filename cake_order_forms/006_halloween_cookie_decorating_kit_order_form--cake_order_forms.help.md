# Halloween Cookie Decorating Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your Halloween cookie decorating kit order.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your holiday theme preference.
2. Choose the type of cookies you want to order.
3. Indicate the quantity of cookies you want to order.
4. Select your color scheme preference.
5. Choose your decoration type.
6. Indicate whether you want to ship your order.
7. Provide any special instructions for your order.
8. Enter your contact name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Theme** (`holiday_theme`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you want a holiday theme included with your cookie decorating kit.
* **Cookie Type** (`cookie_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you want a specific type of cookie, such as a certain flavor or shape, included with your kit.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of kits you want to order.
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you want a specific color scheme for your kit.
* **Decoration Type** (`decoration_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you want a specific decoration type, such as a certain design or style, included with your kit.
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you want to ship your kit.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, required/optional): Enter any special instructions or requests you have for your order.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about your order.

Tips:

* Make sure to select at least one holiday theme and one color scheme to make your order more interesting.
* If you have specific decoration or cookie type requests, be sure to select those as well.
* If you have any special instructions, such as a gift message or special delivery request, be sure to include those in the special instructions field.
