# Custom Cake Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a custom cake order. It will help you create a unique cake tailored to your specific needs and preferences. Please fill out the form below to provide us with your order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each page of the form.
2. Review and verify the accuracy of your input before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Enter the type of order you are placing. This can include a description of the cake, any special requests, or modifications you'd like to make.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`): Provide details about the delivery of your order, such as the date and time you'd like it to be delivered.

### Page 2: Cake Details
* **Cake Details** (`cake_details`): Enter any specific details about the cake you are ordering, such as the size, flavor, or design.

### Page 3: Payment Details
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Enter your payment information so we can process your order.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.

### Page 4: Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): This field is repeated from previous page and serves as a confirmation of your order. 
* **Special Details** (`special_details`): Enter any special requests or instructions for your order.

Note: This form has 7 fields with the same name "order_details" in different pages. For a correct display, we will use "Order Details", "Cake Details", "Payment Details", "Order Details" and "Special Details" respectively for the 7 fields.
