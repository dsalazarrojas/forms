# valentine diy kits order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for ordering DIY craft kits for Valentine's Day. The form is designed to be completed by customers who want to purchase these kits online.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "customer info" section with your name, email, and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
2. In the "contact info" section, provide your address, phone number, and any other contact preferences.
3. In the "product info" section, choose the specific DIY craft kit you'd like to order and provide any additional comments or special requests.
4. In the "payment info" section, select your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal, etc.).
5. Finally, in the "delivery info" section, choose your preferred delivery date and any additional delivery instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**:
  - This field is used to collect your name, email, and any other relevant details about you.
  - Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
  - Use your personal email address where you can be reached.

* **Contact Info**:
  - This section is used to gather your address and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your order.
  - Enter your full mailing address, including street number and apartment number if applicable.
  - Provide your phone number so we can reach you if needed.

* **Product Info**:
  - Choose the specific DIY craft kit you'd like to order from the list provided.
  - Enter any additional comments or special requests you have about your order, such as customization options.

* **Payment Info**:
  - Select your preferred payment method, such as credit card or PayPal.
  - Enter your payment details as required for your chosen method.

* **Delivery Info**:
  - Choose your preferred delivery date for your order.
  - Enter any additional delivery instructions, such as "deliver to the front door" or "leave on the porch".
