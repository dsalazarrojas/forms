# Theme Cake Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Theme Cake Order Form is a tool designed to help customers place orders for custom cakes. This form is used to collect information about the customer's preferences, contact details, and payment options for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number to ensure that we can contact you about your order.
3. Enter the date and time you would like to place the order.
4. If you have any special custom requests for your cake, please provide them in the text box.
5. Enter any payment information, including your preferred payment method (PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay).
6. Enter any additional notes about your order.
7. Review and confirm your order details to ensure everything is accurate.
8. Select the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your full name as it should be on the order.
* **address**: Enter your address where the cake will be delivered or picked up.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **order_date**: Select the date you would like to place the order.
* **order_time**: Select the time you would like to place the order.
* **custom_cake_order**: If you have any special requests, enter them here.
* **payment_info**: Enter any additional payment information.
* **order_details**: Enter any additional details about your order.
* **total_cost**: You will see this field automatically calculated based on your selections.
* **submit**: Select this option to confirm and complete your order.
* **payment_method**: Select your preferred payment method (PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay).
* **note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **name_2**: Enter your full name (optional).
* **address_2**: Enter your address (optional).
* **email_2**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **phone_2**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **custom_cake_order_2**: If you have any special requests, enter them here (optional).
* **payment_info_2**: Enter any additional payment information (optional).
* **order_details_2**: Enter any additional details about your order (optional).
* **total_cost_2**: You will see this field automatically calculated based on your selections (optional).
* **submit_2**: Select this option to confirm and complete your order (optional).
* **payment_method_2**: Select your preferred payment method (optional).
* **note_2**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your order (optional).
* **submit_3**: This is not actually a real field in this form, it seems to be a duplicate of **submit_2**, so you can ignore it.
* **payment_method_2** and **submit_2** (fields 23 and 22, respectively) are actually intended for a second order, and **payment_method_2** and **submit_2** are not actually required fields in the first order.

Note: The last few fields are actually duplicates of the original fields and should be ignored in the help guide.
