# Cake Order Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for customers to agree on the details of their cake order, including design specifications, payment terms, and delivery information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required fields (marked as **Required** in the fields below).
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number): Enter your best contact number.
* **Address** (Address): Enter your full mailing address.
* **Order Date** (Order Date): Enter the date you are placing the order.
* **Event Date** (Event Date): Enter the date you need the cake for.
* **Event Time** (Event Time): Enter the time you need the cake. (Optional)
* **Cake Description** (Cake Description): Enter a detailed description of the cake you are ordering.
* **Cake Size** (Cake Size): Enter the dimensions or serving size of the cake.
* **Cake Flavor** (Cake Flavor): Select the flavor of cake you want (e.g. Vanilla, Chocolate, etc.).
* **Frosting Flavor** (Frosting Flavor): Select the flavor of frosting you want (e.g. Vanilla buttercream, Chocolate buttercream, etc.).
* **Filling Type** (Filling Type): Select the type of filling you want between the cake layers (e.g. Buttercream, Fruit preserve, etc.). (Optional)
* **Design Specifications** (Design Specifications): Enter any special design requirements for the cake.
* **Color Scheme** (Color Scheme): Enter any colors you want to be used for the cake design.
* **Cake Message** (Cake Message): Enter any text you want to be written on the cake.
* **Total Price** (Total Price): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Deposit Amount** (Deposit Amount): Enter the amount you are paying as deposit for the order.
* **Deposit Paid Date** (Deposit Paid Date): Enter the date you made the deposit payment.
* **Balance Due** (Balance Due): Enter the remaining balance due.
* **Balance Due Date** (Balance Due Date): Enter the date by which the balance must be paid.
* **Delivery Method** (Delivery Method): Select how you want to receive the cake (e.g. Customer pickup, Bakery delivery, etc.).
* **Delivery Fee** (Delivery Fee): Enter any additional delivery cost if applicable.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment** (Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment): Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
* **Refund Policy Acknowledgment** (Refund Policy Acknowledgment): Confirm that you understand the refund policy.
* **Allergy Acknowledgment** (Allergy Acknowledgment): Confirm that you understand the risk of cross-contamination.
* **Customer Signature** (Customer Signature): Sign your name digitally or type it.
* **Agreement Date** (Agreement Date): Enter the date of this agreement.
* **Bakery Representative** (Bakery Representative): Enter the name of the bakery staff member who assisted you.
* **Additional Terms** (Additional Terms): Enter any special terms or conditions for your order.

Please review this form carefully before submitting, as it is a legally binding agreement between you and the bakery.
