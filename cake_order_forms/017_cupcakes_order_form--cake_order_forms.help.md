# Cupcakes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cupcakes Order Form is designed to collect user information for cupcake orders. This form will help the cupcake company to gather all necessary details required to fulfill the order efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

*   Click on the "Start" or "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
*   Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk \*.
*   For any field, if you have no information to enter or need more space to enter your information, you can click on the "Add" button to add an additional line for the field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name and other contact details in this section.
*   **Delivery Date** (`contact_information_delivery_date`, `date`, required): Select the date for which the customer would like to receive their order.
*   **Address** (`contact_information_address`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's delivery address in this section.
*   **Intended Amount** (`intended_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of cupcakes the customer would like to order.
*   **Flavours** (`flavours`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the flavors of cupcakes the customer would like to order. You can choose one or multiple flavours from the available options.
*   **Further Comments** (`further_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments or requests for the order in this section.
*   **Phone** (`contact_information_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's phone number in this section.
*   **E-Mail** (`contact_information_email`, `email`, required): Enter the customer's email address in this section.
*   **Notes** (`contact_information_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes for the order in this section.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Be as specific as possible when providing information to ensure correct order fulfillment.
*   If you have any issues or need help with the form, contact the cupcake company's support team.
*   The company will use the information provided to deliver the cupcakes to the customer's location on the chosen date.
