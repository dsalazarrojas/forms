# HOMEMADE CAKE ORDER FORM - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homemade Cake Order Form is designed to collect information from clients for custom cake orders. This form helps bakers gather essential details about the client's preferences and requirements for their cake.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address in the "Client Name" and "Client Email" fields, respectively.
2.  Select the date and time you would like to order the cake.
3.  Choose the type of cake you prefer from the "Cake Type" dropdown menu.
4.  Select the size of the cake you want from the "Cake Size" dropdown menu.
5.  Choose the flavors you would like for the cake from the "Cake Flavor" dropdown menu.
6.  If you have any additional information or special requests, type it in the "Additional Info" field.
7.  Enter your comments or any special instructions for the baker in the "Client Comments" field.
8.  Select the payment method you would like to use for the order.
9.  If you have any comments or instructions for the baker, enter them in the "Baker Comments" field.
10. Review the "Order Status" and "Payment Status" fields to ensure the baker has received your order correctly.
11. Finally, select the "Cake Design" options that best match your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Your name is required for contact and billing purposes.
* **Client Email**: Your email address is used for communication and order updates.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to place your order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time you would like to place your order.
* **Cake Type**: Choose from Vanilla, Chocolate, or Red Velvet.
* **Cake Size**: Select from Small, Large, or Extra Large.
* **Cake Flavor**: Select from Strawberry, Blueberry, or Lemon.
* **Cake Message**: Leave any extra notes or comments about your order in this field.
* **Payment Method**: Choose from Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any extra information or special requests you have for your cake.
* **Client Comments**: Enter any comments or special instructions for the baker.
* **Baker Comments**: Enter any comments or instructions for the baker.
* **Order Status**: Review the status of your order here.
* **Payment Status**: Review the status of your payment here.
* **Cake Design**: Select from Simple, Decorated, or Custom.
* **Client Comments**: Enter any comments or special instructions for the baker.
* **Client Email**: Your email address is used for communication and order updates.
* **Client Name**: Your name is required for contact and billing purposes.
