# holiday_cake_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and order details during the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information section with your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
2. In the order details section, provide a brief description of the cake order you'd like to place.
3. In the delivery information section, specify the delivery date and any special instructions for the cake delivery.
4. In the payment information section, provide the payment method and any other relevant payment details.
5. Finally, in the order confirmation section, review and confirm your order details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`name`, `type`: text, required: false): Please provide your name and contact email to help us contact you regarding your order.
- **Order Details** (`name`, `type`: text, required: false): Briefly describe the type and quantity of cake you'd like to order.
- **Delivery Information** (`name`, `type`: text, required: false): Specify the date and time you'd like the cake to be delivered, and any special instructions for the delivery process.
- **Payment Information** (`name`, `type`: text, required: false): Provide the payment method and any other relevant payment details.
- **Order Confirmation** (`name`, `type`: text, required: false): Review and confirm your order details before submission.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact and payment information to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- If you'd like to place multiple orders, you may need to fill in this form multiple times.
