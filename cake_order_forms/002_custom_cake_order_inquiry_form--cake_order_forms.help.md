# Custom Cake Order Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide us with information about their custom cake order. Please fill out the form below to help us understand your preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all the necessary fields, marked as required, to ensure we can process your order accurately.
- You can leave optional fields blank if you do not have the required information.
- If you have any special requests or instructions, please provide them in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name and contact information.
- **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, optional): Provide your delivery preferences (e.g. time, date, location).
- **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional): Describe the type of cake or dessert you are interested in ordering.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Leave your contact phone number and email address so we can reach out to you.
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): If you are paying by credit card or PayPal, please provide your payment method preference.
- **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Provide your shipping address and any special delivery instructions.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose a delivery time that works for you.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific delivery date in mind, please provide it.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Provide your name and title (e.g. Mr., Mrs., Miss, etc.).
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Leave your contact phone number.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of dessert you are interested in ordering (Cake, Cupcake, Pastry, Pie, Cake with Frosting).
