# Wedding Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a wedding cake order. It will help us process your order and ensure that your special day is celebrated with the perfect cake.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide the details about the wedding cake you'd like to order.
2. Enter the delivery details for the cake.
3. Provide your contact information so we can get back to you with any questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Cake Details** (1): In this field, please describe the type of wedding cake you're interested in ordering. Be as specific as possible, including the flavor, size, and design you have in mind.
- **Delivery Details** (2): Please provide the delivery details for your wedding cake, including the date, time, and location of the delivery. If you have any special requests or instructions, such as "deliver to the reception" or "leave on the front porch", please include them here.
- **Contact Details** (3): In this field, please enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address. This will help us get in touch with you with any questions or updates about your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- If you have any special requests or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We'll be in touch with you soon to confirm your order and provide any further instructions.
