# Afterpay Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Cake Order Form is a crucial tool for customers to submit their orders for special occasions and events. It allows us to gather essential details about the customer and their cake preferences, ensuring that we can create a customized experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the customer's name and contact information.
2. Select the type of cake that you prefer (e.g., Layer cake, Sheet cake, Cupcakes, or Custom sculpted).
3. Choose your preferred flavor (e.g., Vanilla, Chocolate, Red velvet, Lemon, or Other).
4. Indicate the size or number of servings of cake that you would like.
5. Select whether you would like to pick up your cake or have it delivered.
6. Enter the date when you need the cake.
7. If desired, add a personal message to be printed on the cake.
8. Choose to pay with Afterpay or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name** (customer_name): Enter the full name of the customer, as this will be used for communication and cake preparation.
* **Contact number** (contact_number): Provide your phone number so we can coordinate the pickup or delivery of the cake.
* **Cake type** (cake_type): Choose the type of cake that suits your needs (Layer cake, Sheet cake, Cupcakes, or Custom sculpted).
* **Flavor** (flavor): Select your preferred flavor for the cake (Vanilla, Chocolate, Red velvet, Lemon, or Other).
* **Size or servings** (size): Enter the approximate number of servings you need.
* **Pickup or delivery** (pickup_or_delivery): Decide between pickup and delivery options. Delivery may incur extra fees.
* **Event date** (event_date): Enter the date when you need the cake.
* **Personalization text for cake** (personalization_text): If you'd like to add a short message to the cake, enter it here (optional).
* **Pay with Afterpay** (afterpay_option): Choose to pay with Afterpay or not.
