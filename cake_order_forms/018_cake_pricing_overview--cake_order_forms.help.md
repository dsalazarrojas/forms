# Cake Pricing Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers provide information about their cake needs for a cake ordering process. It collects essential details about the customer, event details, cake preferences, and delivery information to ensure that the cake is made according to their specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your name and email address.
2. Specify the type of event (e.g., birthday, wedding, anniversary) and date of the event.
3. Choose the number of servings needed for the cake.
4. Select your preferred cake flavor, size, and number of layers.
5. Choose your preferred frosting type.
6. Indicate the level of design complexity you prefer.
7. If needed, provide any special design requirements.
8. Select the delivery method (pickup or delivery) and, if applicable, enter the delivery address.
9. Choose a budget range for the cake.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the cake.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any further communication.
* **Type of Event**: Select the type of event for which you are ordering the cake (e.g., birthday, wedding, anniversary).
* **Planned Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Number of Servings Needed**: Specify the number of servings needed for the cake.
* **Preferred Cake Flavor**: Select your preferred cake flavor (e.g., chocolate, vanilla, strawberry).
* **Cake Size**: Choose the size of the cake you prefer (e.g., 6 inch, 8 inch, 10 inch, or sheet cake).
* **Number of Cake Layers**: Enter the number of layers you prefer.
* **Frosting Type**: Select the type of frosting you prefer (e.g., buttercream, cream cheese).
* **Design Complexity Level**: Indicate the level of design complexity you prefer (e.g., simple, moderate, complex).
* **Special Design Requirements**: If you have any special design requirements, enter them here.
* **Delivery or Pickup**: Select whether you want to pick up the cake or have it delivered.
* **Delivery Address (if applicable)**: If you selected delivery, enter the address where the cake will be delivered.
* **Budget Range**: Choose the budget range for the cake.
