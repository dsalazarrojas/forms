# Cake Order Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your cake order, including your contact details, event type, preferences, and any special requests or comments.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information: Provide your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of event: Choose the type of event you're planning (e.g., birthday, wedding, anniversary, graduation, corporate event, baby shower, or bridal shower).
3. Enter the planned event date.
4. Estimate the number of guests attending the event.
5. Select your preferred cake flavors.
6. Choose your preferred cake style (optional).
7. If you have dietary restrictions, select all that apply.
8. Set your budget range (optional).
9. Add any special requests or comments.
10. Review your form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **1. Your Name**
* (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
### **2. Email Address**
* (`email_address`, `email`, required): Provide your valid email address.
### **3. Phone Number**
* (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
### **4. Type of Event**
* (`event_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of event (e.g., birthday, wedding, anniversary, graduation, corporate event, baby shower, bridal shower, or other).
### **5. Planned Event Date**
* (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
### **6. Estimated Number of Guests**
* (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Estimate the number of guests attending the event.
### **7. Preferred Cake Flavors**
* (`preferred_flavors`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all your preferred cake flavors (e.g., chocolate, vanilla, strawberry, red velvet, lemon, carrot).
### **8. Frosting Preferences**
* (`frosting_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all your preferred frosting flavors (e.g., buttercream, cream cheese, chocolate ganache, fondant, whipped cream).
### **9. Do you have dietary restrictions?**
* (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply if you have dietary restrictions (e.g., gluten-free, dairy-free, vegan, nut-free, sugar-free).
### **10. Budget Range**
* (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Choose your budget range (e.g., under $100, $100-$250, $250-$500, $500-$1000, $1000+).
### **11. Preferred Cake Style**
* (`cake_style`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred cake style (e.g., elegant and formal, fun and whimsical, modern and minimalist, traditional, no preference).
### **12. Design Ideas or Inspiration**
* (`design_ideas`, `text`, optional): Describe your vision or design ideas for the cake.
### **13. Color Preferences**
* (`color_preferences`, `text`, optional): Enter any color preferences for the cake.
### **14. Have you ordered from us before?**
* (`previous_cakes`, `select_one`, optional): Answer whether you have ordered from us before.
### **15. Special Requests or Comments**
* (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Add any special requests or comments about your order.
### **16. Review and Submission**
* Please review your form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.

Please submit your completed form to ensure a smooth and enjoyable cake-ordering experience.
