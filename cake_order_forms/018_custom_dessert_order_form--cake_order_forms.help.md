# Custom Dessert Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to place a custom dessert order. Fill out the form with your contact details, order preferences, delivery address, and order date and time to ensure that your custom dessert is made and delivered correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required): Enter your contact name and contact number for us to get in touch with you.
* **Order Preferences** (order_preferences, text, required): Specify any dietary restrictions or special requests for your custom dessert.
* **Delivery Address** (delivery_address, text, required): Enter your delivery address so that we can deliver your custom dessert on time and accurately.
* **Order Date** (order_date, date, required): Choose the date you would like to receive your custom dessert.
* **Order Time** (order_time, time, required): Select the time you would like to receive your custom dessert.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact details and delivery address to ensure accuracy.
* Clearly specify any dietary restrictions or special requests for your custom dessert.
* Choose a date and time that works for you to receive your custom dessert.
