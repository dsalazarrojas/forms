# Cupcake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cupcake Order Form" is designed to collect customer information and order details for a bakery or food establishment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section (Customer Info) with your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your order details in the "Order Details" section, including the type of cupcake you'd like to order and any specific requests or instructions.
3. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): Fill in your name and email to help us know who to contact for order updates. This is a one-time entry, and you're not required to fill this out if you're only placing an order once.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): Describe your order here, including the type of cupcake you'd like to order and any special requests. Be as detailed as you need to help us understand your order.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required): Enter your payment method and any additional notes on your payment. This is a required field to complete the order.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, required): Click this button to submit your order. You can only submit the form once you've filled in all required fields.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly. If you have any questions or issues with your order, do not hesitate to contact us.
