# it_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience with IT services and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Answer each question to the best of your ability.
* Select the relevant options for each question, taking care to read each option carefully.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to elaborate in the "Additional Comments" section.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction Level** (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied): 
  This question asks about your general feeling towards the IT services and support provided. Please select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **IT Support Responsiveness** (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Not applicable):
  This question assesses how promptly and effectively the IT support team responds to your requests for assistance. Choose the option that reflects your experience.
* **IT System Reliability** (Always reliable, Mostly reliable, Sometimes unreliable, Often unreliable):
  This question gauges the reliability of the IT systems and tools you use daily. Select the option that accurately represents your perception.
* **Preferred Support Method** (Workshops, Online tutorials, One-on-one sessions, Documentation, Peer support):
  This question helps us understand what type of support or training you would find most beneficial for new technologies. Choose the options that you think would be helpful.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**:
  If there are any specific IT services, tools, or processes you'd like to see improved, please share your thoughts in this free-text field.
* **General Comments or Suggestions**:
  If you have any further comments or suggestions regarding IT services or support, please share them here. This is a great opportunity to provide more detailed feedback.
