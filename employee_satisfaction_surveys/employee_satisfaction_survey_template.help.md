# employee_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on employee satisfaction, communication, and benefits to help identify areas for improvement within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the best response from the provided options.
2. For the "Overall" question, select the rating that best reflects your current level of satisfaction.
3. For the "Which of the following benefits are most important to you" question, select all that apply.
4. Answer the open-ended questions ("What could improve your experience at work?" and "Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?") in as much detail as you like.
5. Make sure to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?**:
	+ This question asks for your overall level of satisfaction with your current role.
	+ Choose the rating that best reflects your current feelings.
* **How would you rate communication from management?**:
	+ This question assesses your perception of the communication from management.
	+ Choose the rating that best reflects your opinion.
* **Do you feel supported in your professional development?**:
	+ This question explores whether you feel supported in your growth and development.
	+ Choose true, false, or somewhat.
* **Which of the following benefits are most important to you?**:
	+ Select all the benefits that you value the most.
	+ Options include:
		- Health Insurance
		- Paid Time Off
		- Retirement Plan
		- Flexible Work
		- Professional Development
		- Wellness Programs
* **What could improve your experience at work?**:
	+ This question allows you to provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?**:
	+ This question provides an opportunity to share any further thoughts or ideas.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Select all relevant benefits when answering the "Which of the following benefits are most important to you" question.
* Use specific details when answering the open-ended questions.
* If you have any further suggestions or comments, please share them.
