# candidate_experience_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from candidates on their experience with our hiring process. Your input will help us improve the overall candidate journey and make it a more positive and efficient experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as they appear, selecting from the provided options or typing in your feedback. If you have any specific comments or suggestions, please use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall, how satisfied were you with your candidate experience?`, single, required):
	+ We want to know how satisfied you are with your overall experience with our hiring process. Please select the answer that best reflects your feelings.
* **Job Description and Requirements Clarity** (`How clear was the job description and requirements for the role?`, single, required):
	+ We're interested in understanding how clear we were about the job role and expectations. Please select the answer that best describes your experience.
* **Communication** (`How would you rate the communication you received throughout the process?`, single, required):
	+ We want to know how effectively you were communicated with during the hiring process. Please rate the communication as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Effective Aspects of the Interview Process** (`Which aspects of the interview process were effective? (select all that apply)`, multiple, required):
	+ We're looking for your honest feedback on what worked well during the interview process. Please select all the aspects that you felt were effective.
* **Recommendation to Apply to Our Company** (`Based on your experience, would you recommend applying to our company?`, single, required):
	+ We value your opinion and would like to know if you would recommend our company as a place to work. Please select "Definitely Yes", "Likely Yes", "Neutral", "Unlikely Yes", or "Definitely No".
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`What specific changes would improve our candidate experience?`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any ideas or suggestions on how we can improve the candidate experience, please share them with us in this text field.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`Do you have any other feedback or comments?`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions that didn't fit in the other fields, please share them with us in this text field.
