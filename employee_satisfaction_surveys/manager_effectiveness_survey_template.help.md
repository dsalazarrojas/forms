# manager_effectiveness_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The manager effectiveness survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their direct manager's effectiveness and impact. The goal is to help managers identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance employee satisfaction and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Submit" to begin the survey.
2. For questions with multiple options, choose one or more relevant answers.
3. For questions with text fields, provide clear and concise answers.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction (Very satisfied/ Satisfied/ Neutral/ Dissatisfied/ Very dissatisfied)**: This question asks about your general satisfaction with your direct manager.
* **Manager's Communication Style and Clarity (Excellent/ Good/ Fair/ Poor/ Very Poor)**: Rate your manager's communication style and how clear they are in their explanations.
* **Feedback and Support (Strongly agree/ Agree/ Neutral/ Disagree/ Strongly disagree)**: Assess whether your manager provides effective feedback and support for your professional development.
* **Manager's Strengths (select all that apply)**: Choose the areas where your manager excels, such as providing clear direction, offering constructive feedback, empowering the team, promoting collaboration, or effectively managing workload.
* **Actions for Improvement**: In this text field, suggest specific actions your manager could take to improve your work experience or team's effectiveness.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any further feedback or thoughts about your manager's effectiveness or impact.

## Tips
* Please be honest and concise in your answers.
* Ensure you answer all required fields before submitting the form.
* Remember that this survey is a tool for improvement, not a criticism or complaint platform.
* Your feedback is essential to helping your manager become more effective and supportive.
