# employee_engagement_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their overall satisfaction and engagement with their role and organization. It includes questions on their level of satisfaction, communication with management, support for professional development, and work-life balance. Additionally, it provides space for employees to suggest improvements and share any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your honest opinion.
2. Select one of the provided options for each question.
3. For the "What improvements could enhance your experience at work?" and "Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?" fields, type your thoughts and ideas freely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?**
	* (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)
	* Please select one of the provided options that best describes your current level of satisfaction with your role.

### 2. **How would you rate communication from management?**
	* (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
	* Choose one of the provided options that reflects your experience with communication from management.

### 3. **Do you feel supported in your professional development?**
	* (true, false, Somewhat)
	* Select "true" if you feel supported, "false" if you don't, or "Somewhat" if it's somewhere in between.

### 4. **How would you describe your current work-life balance?**
	* (Very good, Good, Neutral, Poor, Very poor)
	* Choose one of the provided options that best describes your current work-life balance.
