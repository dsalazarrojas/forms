# job_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their job satisfaction, with the goal of identifying areas for improvement within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, selecting the options that best describe your experiences and feelings about your job.
2. For the multiple-choice question, select all that apply.
3. Write your answers to the text questions in as much detail as you like.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure you're comfortable sharing your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction**: This question asks how satisfied you are with your role at the organization. Please select one of the options:
	+ Very satisfied: You are extremely happy with your role and feel that it aligns well with your goals and expectations.
	+ Somewhat satisfied: You are somewhat pleased with your role, but there are some areas where you would like to see improvement.
	+ Neutral: You feel neither extremely nor somewhat satisfied with your role.
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied: You are somewhat unhappy with your role, but there are some positive aspects.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You are extremely unhappy with your role and feel that it does not align with your goals and expectations.
* **Communication from Management**: This question asks how you would rate communication from management. Please select one of the options:
	+ Excellent: Communication from management is clear, effective, and respectful.
	+ Good: Communication from management is mostly clear and effective, but may be inconsistent at times.
	+ Fair: Communication from management is sometimes clear, but often unclear or frustrating.
	+ Poor: Communication from management is frequently unclear or unhelpful.
* **Support for Professional Development**: This question asks if you feel supported in your professional development. Please select one of the options:
	+ true: Yes, you feel supported in your professional development.
	+ false: No, you do not feel supported in your professional development.
	+ Somewhat: You may feel somewhat supported, but there are some areas where you would like to see improvement.
* **Job Satisfaction Factors**: This question asks which of the following factors most contribute to your job satisfaction. Please select all that apply:
	+ Challenging work
	+ Work-life balance
	+ Team collaboration
	+ Recognition
	+ Growth opportunities
	+ Compensation
* **Additional Comments**: This question is an open-ended text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience at work.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: This question is another open-ended text field where you can provide any additional thoughts or ideas about how the organization can improve your experience at work.
