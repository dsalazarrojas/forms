# Employee On-boarding Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from new employees on their experiences during the on-boarding process, with the goal of identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided, and choose the option that best describes your experience.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For the open-ended question, provide as much detail as you can to help us understand your feedback.
4. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall On-Boarding Experience** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This question asks you to describe your general feeling about your on-boarding experience.
* **Adequate Information and Resources** (`true`, `Mostly`, `Partially`, `false`): This question gauges whether you felt you received sufficient information and resources to start your role.
* **Support from Manager** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This question rates your experience with the support from your manager during on-boarding.
* **Most Helpful Aspects of On-Boarding** (`HR paperwork`, `System access`, `Role clarity`, `Team introductions`, `Initial training`, `Company culture overview`): This question asks you to select the aspects of on-boarding that were most helpful to you.
* **Integrating into the Team and Culture** (`Completely`, `Mostly`, `Somewhat`, `Not yet`): This question evaluates how easily you felt you integrated into your team and the company culture.
* **What Could be Improved** (`text`): Provide specific suggestions on how to improve the on-boarding process for future employees.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`text`): Share any additional thoughts or ideas you have about the on-boarding process.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide detailed feedback to help us make informed improvements.
* If you have any suggestions, please provide specific examples or ideas.
* Your input is invaluable in helping us refine our on-boarding process. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
