## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from employees about their experiences and perceptions regarding internal communication and their satisfaction with their role within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, providing honest feedback based on your experiences.
2. For the "Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?" question, select the option that best describes your feelings.
3. Rate "How would you rate communication from management?" based on your perception of its quality.
4. Indicate your level of agreement with "Do you feel supported in your professional development?" by selecting one of the given options.
5. Choose all the communication channels that you find most effective for you.
6. Provide specific answers to "What specific aspects of internal communication could be improved?" and "Do you feel informed about important company decisions and changes?"
7. If you have any additional comments or suggestions regarding internal communication, type them in the "Any additional comments or suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `single`, required)
  - This question helps us understand your level of satisfaction with your role. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How would you rate communication from management?** (`communication_from_management`, `single`, required)
  - This question assesses your perception of the quality of communication from management.
* **Do you feel supported in your professional development?** (`development_support`, `single`, required)
  - Indicate how often you feel supported in your professional development.
* **Which communication channels do you find most effective?** (`communication_channels`, `multiple`, required)
  - Select all the channels that you find most effective for communication.
  - Options: Email, Team meetings, Instant chat, Intranet, Company announcements
* **What specific aspects of internal communication could be improved?** (`communication_improvement`, `text`, required)
  - Share your thoughts on specific areas that you feel need improvement.
* **Do you feel informed about important company decisions and changes?** (`informed_of_decisions`, `single`, required)
  - Indicate how often you feel informed about important company decisions and changes.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions regarding communication?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
  - If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please type them here.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your answers.
* Provide specific examples when possible.
* Take as much time as you need to answer each question carefully.
* Remember that all answers are anonymous and confidential.
