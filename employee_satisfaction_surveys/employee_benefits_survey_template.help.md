# employee_benefits_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the current employee benefits package to improve satisfaction and offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question about your overall satisfaction with the current benefits package.
2. Select all the benefits that are most important to you.
3. Choose how well you understand the benefits available to you.
4. Answer whether the current paid time off policy meets your needs.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions about benefits you would find valuable.
5. Add any other comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall, how satisfied are you with the current employee benefits package?`, single): Rate your overall satisfaction with the current benefits package by selecting one of the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Important Benefits** (`Which of the following benefits are most important to you?`, multiple): Select all the benefits that are most important to you. You can choose multiple options. The benefits listed are: Health insurance, Retirement plan, Paid time off, Flexible work options, Professional development, Wellness programs, and Childcare support.
* **Understanding of Benefits** (`How well do you understand the benefits available to you?`, single): Choose how well you understand the benefits available to you. You can select one of the options: Very well, Well, Somewhat, Not well, or Not at all.
* **Paid Time Off Policy** (`Do you feel the current paid time off policy meets your needs for work-life balance?`, single): Answer whether the current paid time off policy meets your needs for work-life balance. You can select one of the options: Very well, Well, Somewhat, Not well, or Not at all.
* **Additional Benefits** (`What additional benefits would you find most valuable?`, text): Provide any additional benefits you would find valuable.
* **Comments or Suggestions** (`Do you have any other comments or suggestions regarding employee benefits?`, text): Add any other comments or suggestions you may have about employee benefits.
