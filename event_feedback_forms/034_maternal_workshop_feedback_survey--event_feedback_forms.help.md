# Maternal Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants who have attended the Maternal Workshop. The purpose of the survey is to gather information on their experience, satisfaction, and suggestions for improvement. This feedback will help us refine and enhance future workshops to better meet the needs of our participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Share your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the workshop by selecting one of the satisfaction ratings options.
4. If you would recommend the workshop, select one of the options provided under "Likely To Recommend". If not, please explain why in the "Likely To Recommend Reason" field.
5. Provide any improvement ideas or suggestions you have for future workshops in the "Improvement Ideas" field.
6. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Participant Name** (`maternal_workshop_participant_name`, text, optional)
This field is for you to provide your name as a workshop participant.

### 2. **Feedback** (`maternal_workshop_feedback`, text, optional)
Please share your honest feedback about the workshop, including any positive or negative experiences you had.

### 3. **Satisfaction Rating** (`maternal_workshop_satisfaction_rating`, number, optional)
Rate your satisfaction with the workshop on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".

### 4. **Likely To Recommend** (`maternal_workshop_likely_to_recommend`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the options to indicate whether you would likely recommend the workshop to others. Options are:
* Option 1
* Option 2
* Option 3

### 5. **Likely To Recommend Reason** (`maternal_workshop_likely_to_recommend_reason`, text, optional)
If you did not select to recommend the workshop, please explain why in this field.

### 6. **Improvement Ideas** (`maternal_workshop_improvement_ideas`, text, optional)
Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving future workshops.

### 7. **Additional Comments** (`maternal_workshop_additional_comments`, text, optional)
Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the workshop.
