### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Webinar Feedback --** (`feedback_intro`): This is a note field that introduces the purpose of the form.
*   **Your Name** (`participant_name`): This is an optional text field where you can provide your name.
*   **Date of the Webinar** (`webinar_date`): This field requires you to select the date of the webinar you attended.
*   **Overall Webinar Rating** (`overall_rating`): Rate the webinar from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Did the webinar meet your expectations?** (`expectation_met`): Select 'True' if the webinar met your expectations, 'False' otherwise.
*   **Rate the Speakers Knowledge** (`speaker_knowledge_rating`): Rate the speakers from Excellent to Poor.
*   **Rate the Webinar Platform Ease of Use** (`platform_ease_rating`): Rate the ease of use of the webinar platform from Very Easy to Very Difficult.
*   **What was your favorite topic covered?** (`favorite_topic`): Provide a text description of your favorite topic.
*   **Which topic would you like more information on?** (`additional_info_needed`): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information.
*   **How relevant was the information to your community?** (`information_relevance`): Select how relevant the information was from Very Relevant to Not Relevant.
*   **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (`additional_comments`): This is an optional field for any additional comments or suggestions.
*   **Would you attend another webinar from us?** (`attend_again`): Select 'True' if you'd like to attend another webinar.
*   **What topics would you be interested in for future webinars?** (`future_webinar_topics`): This is an optional field for suggestions on future topics.
*   **Your Email Address** (`followup_email`): This is an optional email field for follow-up communication.
*   **-- Thank You --** (`thank_you_footer`): This is a note field to acknowledge your contribution.
