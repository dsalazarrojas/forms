# Health Clinic Lecture Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Clinic Lecture Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the lecture, providing valuable insights to the organizers to improve their future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. For rating questions, use the provided scale to indicate your level of satisfaction (Overall rating and Overall rating (5 stars)).
3. For text-based questions, provide a clear and concise description of your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "This was a very useful lecture" and "What was the overall take away from this event?" fields.
5. If you would like to be contacted for further discussions, please provide your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience (1)**: Rate your overall experience at the lecture on a scale from 1-5.
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Average
	+ 4: Good
	+ 5: Excellent
* **Quality of Lecture** (2): Rate the quality of the lecture, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **This was a very useful lecture** (3): If you found the lecture valuable, please provide a brief description of why it was useful to you.
* **What was the overall take away from this event?** (4): Describe the key points or takeaways from the lecture that you found most interesting or useful.
* **Overall Rating (1)** (5): Rate the overall quality of the lecture on a 1-5 scale.
* **Overall Rating (5)** (6): Rate the overall quality of the lecture on a 5-star scale.
* **Would you recommend this lecture to a friend?** (7): Answer if you would recommend the lecture to a friend, with options ranging from 1 (No) to 2 (Yes).
* **Contact E-mail** (8): If you would like to be contacted for further discussions, please provide your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (9): If you would like to be contacted for further discussions, please provide your phone number.
* **Name** (10): Please provide your name for contact purposes.
