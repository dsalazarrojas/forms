# Sports Event Volunteer Recognition Survey - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit a Response" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter your name and email address to be recognized and contacted about any feedback or suggestions you provide.
3. Share your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification about your feedback.
4. Write a brief message describing your experiences and suggestions for the event.
5. Rate the overall experience of the event on a scale of 1 to 5.
6. Choose how you rate the recognition efforts made by the event staff.
7. Provide any additional suggestions or comments about the event.
8. Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
9. Choose the date of the event you are providing feedback on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Survey** (`text`, required: false): This is a free-form text field where you can share any thoughts, comments, or experiences from the event. This is your chance to give us an honest and open feedback about the event.
* **Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name so that we can recognize you for your participation and use your feedback for improvement.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your feedback and suggestions.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Share your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification about your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`text`, required: false): Write any additional comments or suggestions you have about the event in this text field.
* **Rate the Event** (`number`, required: false): Rate the overall experience of the event on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Recognition Efforts** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose how you rate the recognition efforts made by the event staff.
* **Suggestion** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional suggestions or comments about the event.
* **Event Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Event Date** (`date`, required: false): Choose the date of the event you are providing feedback on.
