# Creative Writing Workshop Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation on the Creative Writing Workshop. We use this information to improve the workshop, our facilitators, and our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and answer all required questions.
2. For text fields, provide a brief but detailed response.
3. For select_one fields, choose only one option that best describes your experience.
4. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Topic** (`workshop_topic`): Tell us about the topic of the workshop you attended. This will help us understand the subject matter covered.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`): Please provide the date of the workshop you attended.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`): Provide the start and end time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`): Indicate the length of the workshop in hours or minutes.
* **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`): Provide the name of the facilitator who led the workshop.
* **Facilitator Title** (`facilitator_title`): Please provide the title or profession of the facilitator.
* **Facilitator Biography** (`facilitator_biography`): If you're familiar with the facilitator's work, feel free to share their bio or background information.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Please provide your name as a participant.
* **Participant Organization** (`participant_organization`): If you're representing an organization, please provide their name.
* **Participant Contact Info** (`participant_contact_info`): Share any relevant contact information for further communication.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`): Please provide your job title or profession.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): Provide your phone number for follow-up or communication.
* **Workshop Evaluation** (`workshop_evaluation`): Let us know if you found the workshop helpful or not.
* **Do you Recommend this Workshop** (`workshop_recommendation`): Share your recommendation for this workshop and future workshops.
* **Suggestions for future Workshops** (`workshop_suggestion`): Any ideas or suggestions you have for future workshops.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): If there's anything else you'd like to share or add, please do so here.
