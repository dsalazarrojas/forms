# Skydiving Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the Skydiving Event experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have a computer or mobile device with access to the internet.
* Click on the form link to access the online form.
* Fill out the form fields with your honest and accurate feedback.
* Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: *Enter the name of the event you participated in* (`text`, required)
* **Participant Name**: *Enter your name* (`text`, required)
* **Participant Email**: *Enter your email address* (`email`, required)
* **Event Date and Time**: *Enter the date and time of the event* (`date`, required)
* **Event Location**: *Enter the location of the event* (`text`, required)
* **Feedback**: *Provide your honest feedback on the event* (`text`, required)
* **Overall Rating**: *Rate the event's overall quality on a scale of 1-5* (`number`, required)
* **Enjoyed The Event**: *Select how much you enjoyed the event* (`select_one`, required)
	+ Enjoyed
	+ Didn't Enjoy
* **Would Recommend**: *Select if you would recommend the event* (`select_multiple`, required)
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Suggestions**: *Provide any suggestions for improvement* (`text`, required)
* **Additional Comments**: *Provide any additional comments or feedback* (`text`, optional)

Please note that some fields are required to provide the necessary information for us to improve the event, while others are optional and provide additional context. 

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your participation in helping us improve our events. 

Remember to be honest and accurate in your feedback, as it will help us create a better experience for future participants.
