# Event Crowd Management Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Crowd Management Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from attendees on the crowd management aspects of an event. This feedback will help us understand how we can improve our crowd management and provide a better experience for our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your honest and detailed feedback.
2. Make sure to provide your contact information so we can get back to you if needed.
3. Be as specific as possible with your answers, but keep it clear and concise.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Event Feedback" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (id: 2, label: Event Details, type: text, required: false)
	+ Please provide the name of the event you are providing feedback on. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Crowd Management** (id: 3, label: Crowd Management, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ Rate the crowd management during the event. Choose from:
		- Very poor
		- Poor
		- Average
		- Good
		- Excellent
* **Attendee Satisfaction** (id: 4, label: Attendee Satisfaction, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the attendee experience. Choose from:
		- Very dissatisfied
		- Dissatisfied
		- Neutral
		- Satisfied
		- Very satisfied
* **Event Feedback** (id: 5, label: Event Feedback, type: text, required: false)
	+ Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the event.
* **Contact Info** (id: 6, label: Contact Info, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get back to you if needed.
* **Name** (id: 7, label: Name, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name.
* **Email** (id: 8, label: Email, type: email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (id: 9, label: Phone, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Event Name** (id: 10, label: Event Name, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **How would you rate the crowd management during this event?** (id: 3, label: Crowd Management, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ Rate the crowd management during the event. Choose from:
		- Very poor
		- Poor
		- Average
		- Good
		- Excellent

## Tips
* Please be honest and detailed with your feedback.
* If you've already submitted feedback, don't submit again.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us directly.
