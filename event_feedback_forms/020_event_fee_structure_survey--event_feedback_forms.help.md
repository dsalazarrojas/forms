# Event Fee Structure Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Fee Structure Survey is designed to gather information about how an event charges its fees. This form aims to help us understand the structure of event fees to provide better service and support. Please fill out the survey honestly to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each question or fill in the text fields as needed. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing different aspects of event fees. Make sure to answer all questions to ensure we get a complete understanding of your event's fee structure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Details
* **Event Type** (`event_fee_structure_survey_page_1`): Select the type of event you are filling out the survey for.
  * `base price`: A base price is a fixed fee charged to attendees, regardless of their choices.
  * `price per attendee`: A price per attendee is charged to attendees based on their individual choices.
  * `price per item`: A price per item is charged for specific items or services, rather than a fixed fee.
  * `other`: Please select this option if your event fee structure does not fit into the above categories.

### Pricing and Revenue
* **Pricing and Revenue** (`event_fee_structure_survey_page_2`): Select the type of revenue your event generates.
