# Event Planner Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Planner Offboarding Form is used to gather feedback from event planners on events that have been assigned to them. This feedback is essential for the organization to understand the success and areas of improvement for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Project Name** where the event was assigned to you.
2. Select the **Event Date** when you started or completed the project.
3. Enter your **Planner Name** as it appears on your profile.
4. Select the **Project Status** of the project (e.g., In Progress, Completed).
5. Enter the **Tasks Completed** number for the project.
6. Provide any **Feedback** on your experience with the event.
7. Enter your **Email** for contact purposes.
8. Enter your **Phone** number for communication.
9. Add any **Additional Info** about the project that is not covered in the other fields.
10. Provide any **Comments** you want to share about the project.
11. Enter your **Submitted By** name (e.g., John Doe).
12. Select the **Submitted On** date when you submitted this form.
13. Enter your **Project Manager** name if applicable.
14. Enter any **Event Details** that are specific to the event and not covered in the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you were assigned to, e.g., "Marketing Campaign 2023".
* **Event Date**: Enter the date when you started or completed the project.
* **Planner Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your profile, e.g., "John Doe".
* **Project Status**: Select the status of the project from the dropdown menu (e.g., In Progress, Completed).
* **Tasks Completed**: Enter the number of tasks you completed for the project.
* **Feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments about your experience with the event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Additional Info**: Add any additional details about the project that are not covered in the other fields.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments you want to share about the project.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name as it appears on your profile, e.g., "John Doe".
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date when you submitted this form.
* **Project Manager**: Enter the name of your project manager if applicable.
* **Event Details**: Enter any event-specific details that are not covered in the other fields.

## Tips

* Please complete this form only once for each event.
* Ensure that you enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your project manager or HR representative for assistance.
