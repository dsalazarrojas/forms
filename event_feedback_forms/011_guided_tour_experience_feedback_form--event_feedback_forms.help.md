# Guided Tour Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience during a guided tour. Your input is valuable in helping us improve future tours and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing an overall description of your experience during the tour.
2.  Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
3.  If you have any specific comments about the tour, share them in the space below.
4.  If there were any particular aspects that you disliked, let us know.
5.  Share any suggestions for improvement that you think would make the tour better.
6.  Lastly, provide an email address or phone number if you would like a response to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What was your overall experience during the tour?**
  This is a text field where you can describe your general impression of the tour.
  Please be as detailed or brief as you like.

### 2. **How would you rate your experience?**
  Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### 3. **What did you like most about the tour?**
  Share with us what you enjoyed the most about the tour.

### 4. **What did you like least about the tour?**
  If there was something you disliked or would change about the tour, let us know.

### 5. **Any suggestions for improvement?**
  We value your suggestions and would love to hear any ideas you might have for making the tour better.

### 6. **What is your email address? (optional)**
  If you would like to receive a response to your feedback, please provide your email address here.

### 7. **What is your phone number? (optional)**
  This field is optional and is meant for those who might prefer to receive a call for follow-up.

### 8. **Any additional comments?**
  This is the final chance to add any other details you think we should know about your experience.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
- You can provide feedback on as many or as few fields as you like.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
