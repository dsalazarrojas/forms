# Trade Show Floor Layout Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from trade show attendees and organizers on their experience with the trade show floor layout, overall satisfaction, and other aspects of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form, which are labeled with a page number.
2. Answer the questions on each page according to your experience and opinion.
3. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. If you have any suggestions or comments, use the "Feedback" and "Suggestions" fields to provide them.
5. Finally, submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trade Show Layout Feedback**: 
  * Type: Text Field
  * Purpose: Share your thoughts on the trade show floor layout, including any positive or negative comments you may have.
* **Overall Experience**:
  * Type: Select One
  * Purpose: Indicate how satisfied you were with your overall experience at the trade show, with options from "Not at all" to "Extremely".
* **Layout Clarity**:
  * Type: Text Field
  * Purpose: Share your thoughts on how easy or difficult it was to navigate the trade show floor, including any comments on signage, direction, and layout.
* **Navigation**:
  * Type: Text Field
  * Purpose: Share any thoughts on the navigation of the trade show floor, including any comments on the ease of finding exhibitors, sponsors, or other important elements.
* **Signage**:
  * Type: Text Field
  * Purpose: Share any comments on the signage and overall visual appearance of the trade show floor.
* **Feedback**:
  * Type: Text Field
  * Purpose: Share any feedback or comments on your experience with the trade show, including any suggestions for improvement.
* **Suggestions**:
  * Type: Text Field
  * Purpose: Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the trade show.
* **Event Type**:
  * Type: Select One
  * Purpose: Indicate the type of event you attended, with options from "Conference", "Trade Show", "Workshop", or "Other".
* **Attendees Count**:
  * Type: Number Field
  * Purpose: Estimate the number of attendees you saw at the trade show.
* **Event Duration**:
  * Type: Time Field
  * Purpose: Estimate the duration of the event.
* **Date**:
  * Type: Date Field
  * Purpose: Enter the date you attended the trade show.
* **Time**:
  * Type: Time Field
  * Purpose: Enter the time you attended the trade show.
* **Contact Person**:
  * Type: Text Field
  * Purpose: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for organizing the trade show.
* **Contact Email**:
  * Type: Email Field
  * Purpose: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**:
  * Type: Text Field
  * Purpose: Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, use the "Feedback" and "Suggestions" fields to provide them.
* If you're unsure of any of the fields, don't worry! We're here to help, and any feedback is valued.
