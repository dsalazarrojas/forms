# Urology Grand Rounds Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urology Grand Rounds Feedback form is designed to gather constructive feedback from attendees on the quality and relevance of a session or presentation. Your input will help organizers and instructors improve future sessions, ensuring a more engaging and valuable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. You can select multiple options where applicable.
3. For fields with "Other" as an option, please use the text field provided to elaborate on your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Rating (1)**: Rate the session on a scale (e.g., 1-5), where 1 means the session was not helpful and 5 means it was very helpful.
* **Overall Rating (2)**: Rate the overall session experience on a scale (e.g., 1-5), where 1 means the session was not enjoyable and 5 means it was very enjoyable.
* **Session Topics (3)**: Check all the topics that were covered in the session that you found helpful or relevant. You can select multiple topics.
* **Speaker Feedback (4)**: Check the areas where the speaker excelled or struggled. You can select multiple feedback points.
* **Content Quality (5)**: Rate the quality of the content presented on a scale (e.g., 1-5), where 1 means the content was not clear and 5 means it was very clear.
* **Relevance to Practice (6)**: Rate the relevance of the session content to your daily practice on a scale (e.g., 1-5), where 1 means the content was not relevant and 5 means it was very relevant.
* **Suggestions (7)**: Provide any suggestions for improvement or changes you would like to see in future sessions.
* **Additional Comments (8)**: Share any additional comments or feedback about the session that you think is important.

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive with your feedback.
* Your input will help improve the quality and relevance of future sessions.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please feel free to contact the organizers or instructors.
