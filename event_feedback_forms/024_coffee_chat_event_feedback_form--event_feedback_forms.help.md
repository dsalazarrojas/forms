# Coffee Chat Event Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of a coffee chat event. It is a brief and straightforward way to collect information about the event's overall quality, venue, staff, and networking opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields.
2. Enter the date you attended the event in the "Event Date Attended" field.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event in the "Overall Satisfaction" field, using the scale from 1 - very unsatisfied to 5 - very satisfied.
4. Select how well the event met your expectations in the "Event Met Your Expectations" field, using the scale from 1 - far below to 5 - far exceeded.
5. Indicate if you would attend another coffee chat event, and your level of certainty in the "Would You Attend Again" field.
6. Rate the venue's comfort and atmosphere in the "Venue Comfort and Atmosphere" field, using the scale from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
7. Evaluate the quality of the venue's accessibility in the "Venue Accessibility" field, using the scale from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
8. Rate the quality of the refreshments provided at the event in the "Quality of Refreshments" field, using the scale from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
9. Assess the networking opportunities provided during the event in the "Networking Opportunities" field, using the scale from 1 - none to 5 - excellent.
10. Indicate if you made any meaningful connections during the event in the "Did You Make Meaningful Connections" field.
11. Rate the quality of the conversations during the event in the "Quality of Conversations" field, using the scale from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
12. Evaluate the event's start time and schedule flow in the "Event Started on Time" and "Schedule Flow and Timing" fields, respectively.
13. Rate the helpfulness of the staff or event organizers in the "Staff or Organizer Helpfulness" field, using the scale from 1 - not helpful to 5 - extremely helpful.
14. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`text`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback report.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Event Date Attended** (`date`, required): Enter the date you attended the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event using the scale from 1 - very unsatisfied to 5 - very satisfied.
* **Event Met Your Expectations** (`select_one`, required): Select how well the event met your expectations, using the scale from 1 - far below to 5 - far exceeded.
* **Would You Attend Again** (`select_one`, required): Indicate if you would attend another coffee chat event, with your level of certainty.
* **Venue Comfort and Atmosphere** (`select_one`, required): Rate the comfort and atmosphere of the venue using the scale from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
* **Venue Accessibility** (`select_one`, required): Evaluate the ease of finding and accessing the venue, using the scale from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
* **Quality of Refreshments** (`select_one`, required): Rate the quality of refreshments provided at the event using the scale from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
* **Networking Opportunities** (`select_one`, required): Assess the networking opportunities provided during the event, using the scale from 1 - none to 5 - excellent.
* **Did You Make Meaningful Connections** (`select_one`, required): Indicate if you made any meaningful connections during the event.
* **Quality of Conversations** (`select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the conversations during the event, using the scale from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
* **Event Started on Time** (`select_one`, required): Evaluate if the event started on time.
* **Schedule Flow and Timing** (`select_one`, required): Assess the schedule flow and timing of the event, using the scale from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
* **Staff or Organizer Helpfulness** (`select_one`, required): Rate the helpfulness of the staff or event organizers, using the scale from 1 - not helpful to 5 - extremely helpful.
* **What Went Well** (`text`, required): Provide any positive feedback about the event.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`text`, required): Provide any constructive suggestions for improvement.
* **Topics for Future Events** (`text`, required): Suggest any topics you would like to see in future coffee chat events.
* **Additional Comments** (`text`, required): Provide any other feedback or suggestions.
