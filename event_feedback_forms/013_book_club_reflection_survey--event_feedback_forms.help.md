# Book Club Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from book club members to help the book club improve its performance and better meet the needs of its members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name and Email Address on pages 1 and 2, respectively.
2. Select your membership duration on page 3.
3. Choose your typical meeting attendance frequency on page 4.
4. Rate your overall experience, satisfaction with book selections, discussion quality, and meeting format on pages 5, 6, 7, and 8, respectively.
5. Choose your preferred meeting length on page 9.
6. Select your favorite aspect of the book club on page 10.
7. Identify the aspect you'd like to change on page 11.
8. Select genres you'd like to see more of on page 12.
9. Rate your sense of belonging to the community on page 13.
10. Determine if the book club is worth your time on page 14.
11. Decide if you'd recommend the book club to others on page 15.
12. Indicate your plans for future participation on page 16.
13. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions on page 17.
11. Enter the survey completion date on page 18.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`member_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first and last name to help us know you better.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email so we can reach out to you for future updates and feedback.
* **How long have you been a member** (`membership_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you've been a member to help us understand your experience.
* **How often do you attend meetings** (`meeting_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Choose your typical meeting attendance frequency.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall experience with the book club.
* **Satisfaction with Book Selections** (`book_selection_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): How satisfied are you with the book selections?
* **Discussion Quality Rating** (`discussion_quality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of discussions during meetings.
* **Satisfaction with Meeting Format** (`meeting_format_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): How satisfied are you with the meeting structure?
* **Preferred Meeting Length** (`preferred_meeting_length`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred meeting length.
* **What Do You Enjoy Most** (`favorite_aspect`, `select_one`, required): Select your favorite aspect of the book club.
* **What Would You Change** (`least_favorite_aspect`, `select_one`, required): Identify the aspect that needs improvement.
* **Genres You Want More Of** (`genre_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose genres you'd like to see more often.
* **Do You Feel Part of the Community** (`community_feeling`, `select_one`, required): Rate your sense of belonging to the community.
* **Is the Book Club Worth Your Time** (`value_for_time`, `select_one`, required): Determine if the book club is worth your time.
* **Would You Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Decide if you'd recommend the book club to others.
* **Do You Plan to Continue Participating** (`future_participation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your plans for future participation.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, required): Provide any other feedback or suggestions.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Enter the survey completion date.
