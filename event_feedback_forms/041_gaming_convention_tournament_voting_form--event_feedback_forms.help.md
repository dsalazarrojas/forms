# Gaming Convention Tournament Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from attendees of gaming conventions, specifically for tournament events. It is essential to fill out this form to help event organizers understand how well the tournament was received by attendees, what could be improved, and to collect ratings for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your User Info section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the Match 1 and Match 2 sections, rate the quality of the match using the provided scale.
3. Select whether or not the match was won by a player in each section.
4. Provide notes for each match in the Match 1 Notes and Match 2 Notes sections.
5. Enter your additional comments in the Additional Comments section.
6. Enter your email address, as it will be used to contact you for follow-up questions.
7. Select the category and event you attended.
8. Choose the date and time of the event you attended.
9. Enter the location of the event you attended.
10. Optionally, enter your ratings and comments for the event.
11. Select the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`User Info`, text, required): Fill in your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Match 1** (`Match 1`, number, required): Rate the quality of the match.
* **Match 2** (`Match 2`, number, required): Rate the quality of the match.
* **Match 1 Winner** (`Match 1 Winner`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the match was won by a player.
* **Match 2 Winner** (`Match 2 Winner`, select_one, required): Select whether or not the match was won by a player.
* **Match 1 Notes** (`Match 1 Notes`, text, required): Provide notes for the match.
* **Match 2 Notes** (`Match 2 Notes`, text, required): Provide notes for the match.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category you attended.
* **Event** (`Event`, select_multiple, required): Select the event you attended.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Choose the date of the event you attended.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Choose the time of the event you attended.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event you attended.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, number, required): Enter your rating for the event.
* **Rating Why** (`Rating Why`, text, required): Enter why you gave the rating.
* **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, text, required): Enter any suggestions for the event.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Select this button to submit your feedback.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified.
