# Festival Spin And Win Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Spin And Win Survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from festival attendees. The information collected will help the organizers improve future events. This form is completely optional, and you can fill it out at your convenience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as follows:
1. Enter your User Information in the first field.
2. Answer whether you attended the festival in the second field.
3. If you attended the festival, provide the date and time you attended in the third and fourth fields, respectively.
4. Share your thoughts on the festival in the fifth field.
5. Make any suggestions for the next festival in the sixth field.
6. Let us know if you'd like to be contacted for future events in the seventh field.
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes (if desired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information**: This is where you can input your name, email address, or any other contact information you'd like to provide.
    *   **Event Attended**: Please answer yes if you attended the festival.
    *   **Event Date**: If you did attend, select the date you attended the festival.
    *   **Event Time**: Select the time you attended the festival.
    *   **Feedback**: Share your honest thoughts and opinions about the festival.
    *   **Suggestions**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have for the next festival.
    *   **Want to be Contacted**: Let us know if you'd like to be contacted by us for future events.
    *   **Email Address**: If you'd like us to contact you, provide your email address.
    *   **Phone Number**: If you'd like us to contact you, provide your phone number.
