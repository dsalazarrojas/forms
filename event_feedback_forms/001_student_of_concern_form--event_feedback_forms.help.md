# Student Of Concern Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Of Concern Form is used to report incidents or concerns involving students, allowing staff to provide timely and effective support to students in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Student**: Enter the student's name in the first field.
2. **Date**: Select the date of the incident in the second field.
3. **Event Type**: Choose the type of event or incident that occurred from the dropdown menu.
   - **Classroom Incident**: A concern related to academic performance or classroom behavior.
   - **School Incident**: A concern that happened on campus, but not in the classroom.
   - **School Event**: A concern related to a school-sponsored event or activity.
   - **School Event-Other**: A concern related to a school-sponsored event or activity that doesn't fit into the other categories.
   - **Other**: Choose this option if the event doesn't fit into any of the above categories.
4. **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred, such as a classroom, hallway, or cafeteria.
5. **Behavior**: Provide a brief description of the student's behavior or actions leading up to or during the incident.
6. **Support**: Choose one or more support options that the student may require, such as:
   - **Counseling Office**: The student may benefit from counseling or support from the counseling office.
   - **School Counselor**: The student may benefit from support from a school counselor.
   - **Other**: Choose this option if the student requires support from someone else, such as a family member or friend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student** (`student`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student involved.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the incident.
- **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of event or incident that occurred.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
- **Behavior** (`behavior`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the student's behavior or actions leading up to or during the incident.
- **Support** (`support`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more support options that the student may require.
