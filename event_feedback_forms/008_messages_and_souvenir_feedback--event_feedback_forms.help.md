# Messages And Souvenir Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a particular event. This information will help the event organizers to understand how customers perceived the event and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a rating for your overall experience with the event (page 1).
2. Write a review of your experience with the event (page 2).
3. Share a photo of your purchase or event-related item (page 3).
4. Rate the souvenir you received (page 4).
5. Describe the souvenir you received (page 5).
6. Provide the name of the event (page 6).
7. Enter the date of the event (page 7).
8. Share your contact information (page 8).
9. Share a story about your experience with the event (page 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating** (page 1): Please rate your overall experience with the event, using a scale of 1-5.
* **What did you think of your purchase?** (page 2): Write a review of your experience with the event, sharing your thoughts and opinions.
* **Share a photo of your purchase** (page 3): If you took a photo of your purchase or event-related item, please share it with us. This will help us get a better understanding of your experience.
* **How would you rate this souvenir?** (page 4): Rate the souvenir you received, using a scale of 1-5.
* **What is the souvenir?** (page 5): Describe the souvenir you received, including any notable details or features.
* **Event name** (page 6): Please enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date** (page 7): Enter the date of the event.
* **Your Contact Info** (page 8): Share your contact information with us, so we can follow up with you regarding your feedback.
* **Share a story** (page 9): Share a story about your experience with the event, including any notable moments or highlights.
