# Leadership Skills Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on the leadership skills training program, helping us to improve the quality and effectiveness of the training.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant rating or choice for each field, based on your experience with the training program.
2. Type any additional comments or suggestions in the 'Feedback' and 'Suggestions' fields.
3. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leadership Skills**: This field is for you to rate the level of leadership skills training you received. Please select 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor' based on your experience.
* **Session Participation**: This field is for you to rate the level of participation and engagement in the training session. Select 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor'.
* **Overall Effectiveness**: This field is for you to rate the overall effectiveness of the training program. Enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Future Planning**: This field is for you to rate the future planning and goals of the training program. Enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Leadership Development**: This field is for you to rate the level of leadership development you experienced during the training. Select 'Leadership skills are well developed', 'Leadership skills are somewhat developed', 'Leadership skills are slightly developed', or 'No improvement is needed'.
* **Training Content**: This field is for you to rate the quality and relevance of the training content. Select 'The training was very informative', 'The training was somewhat informative', 'The training was not informative', or 'The training content was not relevant'.
* **Trainer's Performance**: This field is for you to rate the performance of the trainer. Select 'The trainer was very effective', 'The trainer was somewhat effective', 'The trainer was not effective', or 'The trainer was not present'.
* **Feedback**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the training program.
* **Suggestions**: This field is for you to provide any additional suggestions or ideas for improvement of the training program.
* **Contact Info**: This field is for you to provide your name, email, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Evaluation Date**: This field is for you to enter the date you evaluated the training program.
* **Trainer's Name**: This field is for you to enter the name of the trainer.
* **Trainer's Email**: This field is for you to enter the email of the trainer.
* **Trainer's Phone**: This field is for you to enter the phone number of the trainer.
* **Trainer's Department**: This field is for you to enter the department of the trainer.
* **Trainer's Position**: This field is for you to enter the position of the trainer.
* **Trainer's Location**: This field is for you to enter the location of the trainer.
* **Trainer's Designation**: This field is for you to enter the designation of the trainer.
