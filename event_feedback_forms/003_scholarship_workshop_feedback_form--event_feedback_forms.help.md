# Scholarship Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from workshop participants about their experience with our scholarship program. Your input will help us improve future workshops and provide better services to our users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully to understand the questions and fields provided.
2. Fill in all required fields (marked as "required") to complete the form.
3. Select the correct option for the "scholarship_program" field, if applicable.
4. Provide your rating on the scale of 1-5 for the "rating" field.
5. Enter your comment about your experience in the "comment" field, if you wish.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (<label>Introduction</label>): This is a brief text field where you can type any additional information you'd like to share about your experience.
* **scholarship_program** (<label>scholarship_program</label>): This field allows you to select the specific scholarship program you attended, if you were part of one. Please select the option that best represents your experience.
* **Rating** (<label>rating</label>): Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Comment** (<label>comment</label>): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your experience, please share them here.
* **Email** (<label>email</label>): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
* **Name** (<label>name</label>): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our feedback records.
