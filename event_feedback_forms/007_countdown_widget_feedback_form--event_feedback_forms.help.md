# Countdown Widget Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Countdown Widget Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users about the countdown widget used in events. This form helps us identify areas for improvement and understand how to better serve you with our tools and features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select one of the feedback options from the "Feedback Type" dropdown menu: 
    - Feedback about the countdown widget
    - Feedback about the overall event
    - Other (if your feedback doesn't fit into the above categories)
2.  Provide a detailed description of your feedback in the "Description" text box.
3.  Select the event related to your feedback from the "Event" dropdown menu.
4.  Rate your experience with the widget using the "Rating" field (optional).
5.  Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the "Comments" text box.
6.  Enter your email address to help us get in touch with you if needed.
7.  Provide your name to associate your feedback with your identity.
8.  Submit the form to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Feedback Type (required: false)**: 
    - Select one of the options to indicate the type of feedback you are providing.
*   **Description (required: false)**: 
    - Enter a detailed description of your feedback about the countdown widget.
*   **Event (required: true)**: 
    - Choose the event related to your feedback from the list.
*   **Rating (required: false)**: 
    - Enter a numerical rating to indicate your satisfaction with the widget.
*   **Comments (required: true)**: 
    - Provide additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the widget.
*   **Email (required: true)**: 
    - Enter your email address to help us get in touch with you if needed.
*   **Name (required: true)**: 
    - Enter your name to associate your feedback with your identity.
*   **Submission Date (required: false)**: 
    - Automatically generated date and time of submission.
