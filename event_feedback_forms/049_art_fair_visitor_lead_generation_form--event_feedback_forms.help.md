# Art Fair Visitor Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Fair Visitor Lead Generation Form is a collection of questions designed to gather information about art fair visitors. The form is used to capture contact information, interests, and preferences of visitors to help galleries and artists better understand their target audience and tailor their marketing efforts. This form is intended for visitors to provide their honest feedback and interests, which will help us understand how to improve future art fairs and provide valuable insights for the art market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Fill in your contact information (name, email, phone number).
3. Select your job title and type of visitor.
4. Indicate your interests in different art types (multiple selections allowed).
5. Choose your buying or commissioning interest.
6. Select your budget range for art purchases.
7. Provide feedback on your overall experience at the art fair.
8. Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, text, or social media).
9. Choose the types of information you would like to receive (multiple selections allowed).
10. Add any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`visitor_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`visitor_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, **required**): If you have a job title, please enter it here.
* **Type of Visitor** (`visitor_type`, select_one, **required**): Please select one of the following:
	+ Collector
	+ Corporate buyer
	+ Gallery owner
	+ Museum curator
	+ Artist
	+ Media
	+ Enthusiast
	+ Other
* **Types of Art Interested In** (`art_interests`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all types of art that interest you:
	+ Contemporary art
	+ Photography
	+ Sculpture
	+ Digital art
	+ Installation
	+ Painting
	+ Mixed media
	+ Other
* **Buying or Commissioning Interest** (`buying_interest`, select_one, **required**): Please select one of the following:
	+ Active buyer
	+ Considering purchase
	+ Just browsing
	+ Corporate buyer
	+ Not interested
* **Budget Range for Art Purchases** (`budget_range`, select_one, **required**): Select your budget range for art purchases:
	+ Under $1000
	+ $1000 - $5000
	+ $5000 - $15000
	+ $15000 - $50000
	+ Over $50000
	+ Not applicable
* **Overall Experience at Art Fair** (`overall_experience`, select_one, **required**): Please select your overall experience at the art fair:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Favorite Galleries or Artists** (`favorite_galleries`, text, **optional**): If you have a favorite gallery or artist, please enter their name.
* **Specific Artworks or Artists You Are Interested In** (`artworks_of_interest`, text, **optional**): If you are interested in specific artworks or artists, please enter their name or description.
* **Likely to Return to Future Art Fairs** (`likely_to_return`, select_one, **required**): Please select how likely you are to return to future art fairs:
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Maybe
	+ Unlikely
	+ Not interested
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, **required**): Please select your preferred contact method:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Text
	+ Social media
* **Information You Would Like to Receive** (`information_interests`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select all types of information you would like to receive:
	+ Event announcements
	+ New artist featured
	+ Gallery news
	+ Upcoming exhibitions
	+ Art market insights
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_info`, text, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them here.
