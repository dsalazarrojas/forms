# Retirement Webinar Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the "Retirement Webinar Feedback Survey" form to help you provide constructive feedback on your recent webinar experience. Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each page:

1. Select the type of webinar you attended from the dropdown menu.
2. Write the title of the webinar you attended.
3. Rate the content quality of the webinar (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Rate the presentation quality of the webinar (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
5. Rate the quality of the speaker(s) (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
6. Choose how engaging you found the webinar (Very engaging, Somewhat engaging, Not very engaging, Not at all engaging).
7. Indicate if you experienced any technical difficulties during the webinar (True or False).
8. Write any additional feedback or comments about the webinar in the space provided.
9. Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback.
10. Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **event_type** (select_one, required: false): This field allows you to select the type of webinar you attended.
	* Choose the option that best describes the webinar you participated in.
- **session_title** (text, required: false): Provide the title of the webinar as you remember it.
	* Enter the exact title of the webinar as you recall.
- **content_quality** (select_multiple, required: false): Rate the quality of the content presented during the webinar.
	* Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to reflect your opinion.
- **presentation_quality** (select_multiple, required: false): Rate the presentation style and quality of the webinar.
	* Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to express your assessment.
- **speaker_quality** (select_multiple, required: false): Evaluate the performance of the speaker(s) during the webinar.
	* Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to describe your experience.
- **engagement** (select_one, required: false): Indicate how engaging you found the webinar.
	* Select Very engaging, Somewhat engaging, Not very engaging, or Not at all engaging to reflect your attention level.
- **technical_difficulties** (select_one, required: false): Confirm if you experienced any technical difficulties during the webinar.
	* Answer True or False, depending on whether you encountered any issues.
- **feedback** (note, required: false): Share any other thoughts or suggestions you have about the webinar.
	* Write your comments, concerns, or suggestions for improvement.
- **email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback.
	* Type your email address for potential follow-up communication.
- **phone** (text, required: false): Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
	* Enter your phone number for potential follow-up contact.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
- Be honest and sincere in your feedback to help improve future webinars.
- If you have any technical difficulties while completing the form, please contact the support team for assistance.
