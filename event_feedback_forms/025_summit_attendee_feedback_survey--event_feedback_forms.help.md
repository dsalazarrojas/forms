# Summit Attendee Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from Summit attendees on their experience at the event. The feedback will help us improve future Summits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following sections:

* Enter your email address in the `email` field.
* Rate your satisfaction with each session you attended.
* Choose your favorite speaker(s) from the `speaker_suggestion` field.
* Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the `additional_comments` field.
* Answer the question about whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions in the `follow_up` field.
* Enter your phone number in the `phone` field (optional).
* If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions, enter your follow-up message in the `follow_up_message` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **attendee_info**: Enter your name, company, and any other relevant details about your experience at the Summit.
* **summit_satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the Summit on a scale of 1-5.
* **session_improvement**: Choose all the sessions that you think could be improved. You can select multiple sessions.
* **speaker_suggestion**: Choose your favorite speaker(s) from the list, who you think did an excellent job presenting their session.
* **session_satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with each session on a scale of 1-5.
* **overall_satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the Summit on a scale of 1-5.
* **suggestions**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the Summit.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **follow_up**: Answer whether you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **follow_up_message**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions, enter your message here.
* **session_rating**: Rate the difficulty level of each session on a scale of 1-5.
* **additional_comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the Summit.
* **speaker_suggestion**: This field will ask you to choose your favorite speaker from the list who you think did an excellent job.
