# Camp Games Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from camp participants about their experiences with the games. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your name (if you're willing to share), or you can leave it blank and still submit the form.
2.  Choose a game and fill in its title.
3.  Rate how fun you found the game (from 1 to 10).
4.  Choose a difficulty level you found the game to be (Too Easy, Just Right, Challenging, or Too Difficult).
5.  Evaluate how clear the game rules were for you (1 - Not Clear, 2 - Somewhat Clear, 3 - Very Clear).
6.  Choose how long the game lasted for you (Too Short, Perfect Length, Too Long).
7.  Rate how much the game encouraged teamwork and cooperation (Yes - High Teamwork, Somewhat, No - Individual Effort).
8.  Choose the best part of the game for you.
9.  If you'd like, suggest any improvements to the game rules.
10.  Indicate how safe you felt during the game (Yes - Very Safe, Mostly Safe, No - Unsafe Moments).
11.  If you felt the game was unsafe, please explain why.
12.  Decide if you'd like to play this game again (Definitely, Maybe, Not really).
13.  Lastly, rate how much the game contributed to your feeling of being part of the camp (1 to 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Camp Games Participant Feedback --** (Note): This is the header for the game feedback form. It's here to guide you through the rest of the form.
* **Participant Name (Optional)**: If you'd like to share your name, please do so here. This will help us address any feedback you provide.
* **Name of the Game**: This is where you choose a game that you'd like to provide feedback on.
* **Fun Factor Rating**: Please rate how much fun you had with the game, from 1 (Boring) to 10 (Amazing).
* **Difficulty Level**: Choose how challenging you found the game (Too Easy, Just Right, Challenging, Too Difficult).
* **Clarity of the Rules**: Rate how clear the game rules were for you (1 - Not Clear, 2 - Somewhat Clear, 3 - Very Clear).
* **Game Duration**: Choose how long the game lasted (Too Short, Perfect Length, Too Long).
* **Teamwork and Cooperation**: Evaluate how much the game encouraged teamwork and cooperation (Yes - High Teamwork, Somewhat, No - Individual Effort).
* **What was the best part of this game?**: Share with us what made this game enjoyable for you.
* **Suggestions for Rule Changes**: If you have any ideas on how to improve the game, please share them with us.
* **Safety Considerations**: Indicate how safe you felt during the game (Yes - Very Safe, Mostly Safe, No - Unsafe Moments).
* **Details of Safety Concerns**: If you felt the game was unsafe, please explain your concerns.
* **Would you want to play this game again?**: Decide if you'd like to play this game in the future (Definitely, Maybe, Not really).
* **Camp Spirit Contribution**: Rate how much the game made you feel part of the camp (1 to 10).
