# Team Celebration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users after an event, allowing us to evaluate and enhance future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **How Was The Event**: Describe your experience during the event.
2. **What Made It Worthwhile**: Share what made the event enjoyable or impactful for you.
3. **What Didn't Go Well**: Share any challenges or difficulties you faced during the event.
4. **What Did I Miss**: Share anything you felt was missing or overlooked during the event.
5. **What Can We Improve On**: Suggest any changes or improvements we can make for future events.
6. **Overall Experience**: Share your overall impression of the event.
7. **Will You Attend Again**: Choose your answer based on your interest in attending future events.
8. **Why Didn't Attend**: If not attending, explain the reason.
9. **Event Type** (optional): Select the type of event (Team or Work Commitment).
10. **Rating** (optional): Enter a numerical rating of your experience (1-5).
11. **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and phone number to allow us to contact you.
12. **Event Date and Time** (optional): Enter the date and time of the event.
13. **Notes** (optional): Share any additional comments or feedback.
