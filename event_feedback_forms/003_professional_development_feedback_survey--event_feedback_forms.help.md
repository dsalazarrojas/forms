# Professional Development Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback from users about professional development seminars and workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to provide us with your feedback.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as you can in the text box or select from the options provided.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "additional feedback" section at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. How did you feel about the seminar?
* **How did you feel about the seminar** (`how_did_you_feel_about_the_seminar`, note, required: false): This question is an open-ended note field where you can describe your overall feeling about the seminar. Please provide a brief summary of your thoughts and emotions about the seminar.

### 2. Seminar Rating
* **Seminar Rating** (`seminar_rating`, number, required: false): This question asks for a numerical rating of the seminar, with a scale likely being 1-5 or 1-10. Please select a number that best represents your rating of the seminar.

### 3. Presentation Materials
* **The Presentation Materials** (`the_presentation_materials`, select_one, required: false): This question asks you to select one of the options provided about the presentation materials. Please choose the option that best describes your opinion about the materials.

### 4. Speaker Performance
* **Speaker Performance** (`speaker_performance`, select_multiple, required: false): This question allows you to select multiple options about the speaker's performance. You may choose one, two, or all of the provided options that best describe your opinion about the speaker.

### 5. Seminar Content
* **Seminar Content** (`seminar_content`, number, required: false): This question asks for a numerical rating of the seminar content, with a scale likely being 1-5 or 1-10. Please select a number that best represents your rating of the content.

### 6. Overall Rating
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): This question asks for a numerical rating of the overall seminar, with a scale likely being 1-5 or 1-10. Please select a number that best represents your overall rating of the seminar.

### 7. Additional Feedback
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, note, required: false): This is an open-ended note field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the seminar. Please use this space to elaborate on your thoughts and opinions.

### 8. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This question asks for your email address. Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.

### 9. Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): This question asks for your name. Please enter your full name.

### 10. Institution
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required: false): This question asks for the name of the institution or organization you are affiliated with. Please enter the name of your institution.

### 11. Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): This question asks for the name of the department you are affiliated with. Please enter the name of your department.

### 12. Role
* **Role** (`role`, text, required: false): This question asks for your role or position in the institution. Please enter your role or position.

### 13. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This question asks for your phone number. Please enter your phone number.

Note: Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
