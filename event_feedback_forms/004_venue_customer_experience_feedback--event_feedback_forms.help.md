# Venue Customer Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback from customers about their experience at a venue. It is used to gather insights about the event's success, staff performance, and potential areas for improvement. Please complete this form to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are providing feedback about a specific event.
2. Answer each field according to your experience and knowledge of the event.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your responses, especially in the "Event Details" and "Additional Event Details" fields.
4. If you have any concerns or compliments about the event team, you can select multiple names from the "Event Team" field.
5. Choose one of the options for "Satisfaction" that best reflects your overall experience.
6. Fill in your contact information (Email and Phone) to allow us to follow up on your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (event_details): Please provide a brief description of the event you attended.
* **Guest Experience** (guest_experience): Tell us about your overall experience at the event.
* **Venue Information** (venue_info): Share any notable aspects of the venue that made your experience unique.
* **Event Team** (event_team): Select any team members you had contact with or were impressed with their performance.
* **Satisfaction** (satisfaction): Choose one of the options that best reflects your overall satisfaction with the event.
* **Venue Manager** (venue_manager): Select any staff members that you had contact with or were impressed with their management of the event.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Note** (note): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the event.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the event.
* **Additional Event Details** (event_details_2): Provide any additional information about the event that might be helpful for us to know.
* **Phone** (phone): Note: This field is a text field and is intended for entering a phone number, not for providing feedback about the event. It seems to be a placeholder field and should not be completed unless you have a phone number that relates to your feedback. 
* **Time** (time): Note: This field is a time field, but since the form is about event feedback, this field is not applicable and should be ignored. 

Please note that some fields are optional, but providing detailed and honest feedback will help us improve our services.
