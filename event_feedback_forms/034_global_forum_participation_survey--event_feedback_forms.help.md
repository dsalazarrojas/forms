# Global Forum Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about the event's organization and execution. It is essential to fill this form out only once for each event.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Read each field carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
* Enter your name and email address as a participant.
* Provide details about the event you participated in (date, time, name, description).
* Rate the event using the rating scale provided.
* Select any relevant options that apply to your experience with the event.
* Leave a note if you have any comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you participated in. This will help us to track which events are receiving feedback.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event you participated in. This will help us to track the timing of the event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you participated in. This will help us to identify which event you are providing feedback about.
* **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of the event. This will help us to understand the nature of the event.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant of the event. This will help us to identify you as a participant and associate your feedback with your name.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address as a participant. This is a required field to help us contact you if we need more information or have questions about your feedback.
* **Participant Feedback**: Share your thoughts and experiences about the event. This will help us to improve future events.
* **Participant Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it here. This will help us to contact you for further clarification or discussion about your feedback.
* **Rating Scale**: Rate your experience with the event using the scale provided. This will help us to understand your overall satisfaction with the event.
* **Select One**: Choose the option that best describes your experience with the event. This will help us to understand your specific preferences or concerns.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose any relevant options that apply to your experience with the event. This will help us to understand your overall experience.
* **Participant Note**: Leave any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the event. This will help us to improve future events.
