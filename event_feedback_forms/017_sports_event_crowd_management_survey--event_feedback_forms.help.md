# sports_event_crowd_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from event attendees about their experience at a sports event. It aims to gather insights on crowd management, safety concerns, and other relevant details that can help event organizers improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the instructions and understanding the purpose of the form.
2. Fill in the required fields and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (`intro`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a text field where you can provide a brief introduction or any initial thoughts about your experience at the event.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, select_one, optional):
	+ Please indicate if you experienced any safety concerns during the event by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Flow Rating** (`flow_rating`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the overall flow of the event on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Share any additional feedback or comments about your experience at the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the characteristics of the event that you attended, such as 'Yes' for Yes, 'No' for No.
* **Safety Rating** (`safety_rating`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate the safety of the event on a scale, with options 'Good', 'Fair', 'Poor'.
* **Event Attended** (`event_attended`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate if you attended the event, with options 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select how frequently you attend sports events, with options 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate your age group, with options 'Yes' for Yes, 'No' for No.
* **Spectator Flow** (`spectator_flow`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Rate the overall flow of spectators at the event, with options 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Crowd Density** (`crowd_density`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the crowd density during the event on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Crowd Emotions** (`crowd_emotions`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate if you felt the crowd's emotions during the event, with options 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Event Length** (`event_length`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the length of the event on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is short and 10 is long.
* **Time** (`event_time`, time, optional):
	+ Select the time of the event.
* **Date** (`event_date`, date, optional):
	+ Select the date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, optional):
	+ Select the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`event_end_time`, time, optional):
	+ Select the end time of the event.

## Tips
* Please answer all the fields truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback and information will help event organizers improve future events.
