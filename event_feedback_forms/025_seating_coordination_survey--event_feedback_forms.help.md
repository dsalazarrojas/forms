# Seating Coordination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your seating preferences and arrangements for events that require reserved seating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the page with the options you'd like to answer on each question.
2. Answer each question according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seating Coordination Survey** (`survey_page_1`, text, not required): You can enter any additional comments or thoughts about the survey. This is not required but will help us get a better understanding of your preferences.
* **How do you like your coffee?** (`seating_preference`, select_multiple, not required): Please select all that apply from the options Black, White, Green.
* **Seating Location** (`seating_location`, select_one, not required): Choose a location from The Main Hall, The Conference Room, The Backstage Room.
* **Date** (`seating_date`, date, not required): Enter your preferred seating date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`seating_time`, time, not required): Enter your preferred seating time in the format HH:MM.
* **Additional Comments** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or thoughts about the seating.
* **Email** (`seating_email`, email, not required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`seating_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Notes** (`seating_additional_notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or thoughts about the event.
* **Seating Coordination Survey** (`survey_page_2`, text, not required): You can enter any additional comments or thoughts about the survey. This is not required but will help us get a better understanding of your preferences.
