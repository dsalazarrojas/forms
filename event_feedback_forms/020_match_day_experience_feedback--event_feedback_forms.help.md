# Match Day Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants about their experience on the match day event. Your input is valuable in helping us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select "Submit" after filling out the required and optional fields as follows:

1. **Match Day Experience** (`match_day_experience`, Text, Optional): Describe your experience on the match day event. This will help us understand your thoughts and feelings about the event.
2. **Rating** (`rating`, Number, Optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1-5. The higher the number, the better your experience was.
3. **Recommended For Improvement** (`recommended_for_improvement`, Text, Optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve the event, please share them in this field.
4. **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
5. **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, Number, Optional): Select a numerical value that represents your rating. Note that this field is meant to complement your rating.
6. **Rating Comments** (`rating_comments`, Text, Optional): Provide more details about your rating, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Match Day Experience**:
	+ Enter a brief description of your match day experience. This could include your favorite part of the event, what you enjoyed most, or any notable moments that stood out.
* **Rating**:
	+ Choose a number from 1-5 that best represents your experience. A rating of 5 means you had an excellent experience, while 1 means you had a poor experience.
* **Recommended For Improvement**:
	+ If there is anything you think we can do to make future events better, please share your suggestions.
* **Comments**:
	+ Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about your experience.
* **Rating Score**:
	+ Enter a numerical value that represents your rating. This field is meant to complement your rating.
* **Rating Comments**:
	+ Provide more details about your rating, if needed.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Keep your answers concise and brief.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
