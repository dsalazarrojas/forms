# post_event_business_impact_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from event attendees, allowing event organizers to measure the success and areas for improvement of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have attended the event and have a clear understanding of the topics discussed.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and honest feedback.
3. Be as detailed as possible in the "Additional Comments" section, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Post Event Business Impact Survey** (`event_title`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
- **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the event you attended.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of event you attended:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
- **Attended Event** (`event_attended`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you attended the event:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
- **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as an attendee.
- **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the event:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
- **Likely to Attend** (`event_likely_to_attend`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you would likely attend a similar event:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
- **Recommend** (`event_recommend`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you would recommend this event:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
- **Suggest Improvements** (`event_improve`, text, required/optional): Provide any suggestions for improvements.
- **Additional Comments** (`event_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
- **Agree with Event** (`event_agree`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you agree with the event:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
- **Disagree with Event** (`event_disagree`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you disagree with the event:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
- **No Opinion** (`event_no_opinion`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you have no opinion on the event:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
- **Most Important** (`event_important`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you found this event to be the most important:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

## Tips
* Ensure you have attended the event before filling out this form.
* Be honest and accurate with your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or details in the "Additional Comments" section for event organizers to understand your perspective.
