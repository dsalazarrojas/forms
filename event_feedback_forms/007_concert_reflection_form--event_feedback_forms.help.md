# Concert Reflection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from concert attendees about their experience at the event. It aims to understand their emotional response, satisfaction with various aspects of the concert, and overall enjoyment. By providing your honest feedback, you will help us to improve future concerts and provide better experiences for our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer it according to your experience.
2. Provide your honest and detailed feedback for each question.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
4. Please answer all required questions (marked as "required" in the form).
5. You can provide additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Reflections" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Concert Name** (`concert_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the concert you attended.
* **Concert Date** (`concert_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of the concert.
* **First Time Attending** (`first_time_attendee`, select_one, required): Check "Yes First Time" if this was your first time attending this concert.
* **Emotional Response** (`emotional_response`, select_one, required): Choose how the concert made you feel:
	+ Extremely Moved
	+ Very Enjoyable
	+ Pleasant Experience
	+ Neutral
	+ Disappointed
* **Highlight Moment** (`highlight_moment`, text, required): Please describe the best part of the concert.
* **Musical Performance Quality** (`musical_performance`, select_one, required): Rate the musical execution:
	+ Exceptional
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
* **Stage Presence** (`stage_presence`, select_one, required): Rate the performers' engagement with the audience:
	+ Outstanding

* **Setlist Satisfaction** (`setlist_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the song selection:
