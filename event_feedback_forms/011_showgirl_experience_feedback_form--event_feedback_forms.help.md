# Showgirl Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences at showgirl events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

1.  Fill out each field as instructed below.
2.  Please ensure you have attended a showgirl event before filling out this form.
3.  If you have already submitted feedback for the same event, your new submission will not be recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Experience** (`event_experience`, text, required: false): Describe your experience at the showgirl event. Please provide as much detail as possible.
*   **Event Organizer** (`showgirl_experience_feedback_form_event_organizer`, text, required: false): Please provide the name of the event organizer.
*   **Date** (`showgirl_experience_feedback_form_date`, date, required: false): If possible, provide the date of the event.
*   **Time** (`showgirl_experience_feedback_form_time`, time, required: false): If possible, provide the time of the event.
*   **Location** (`showgirl_experience_feedback_form_location`, text, required: false): Please provide the name of the location where the event took place.
*   **Comments** (`showgirl_experience_feedback_form_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about your experience.
*   **Email** (`showgirl_experience_feedback_form_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you wish to receive a response or follow-up.
*   **Phone** (`showgirl_experience_feedback_form_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you wish to receive a follow-up call.
*   **Rating** (`showgirl_experience_feedback_form_rating`, select_one, required: false): Rate your experience. Please choose one of the following options: Very Good, Good, Average, Fair, Poor.
*   **Attachment** (`showgirl_experience_feedback_form_attachment`, note, required: false): If you have attached a file or image related to your experience, you can upload it here. Please note that attachments will be reviewed before being made public.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and provide detailed feedback to help event organizers improve future showgirl events.
*   If you wish to receive a response or follow-up, please provide your email address or phone number.
*   Attachments will be reviewed before being made public, so please ensure that they are relevant and respectful.

Note: The form fields and structure may change over time. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
