# promotional_event_customer_opinion_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from customers who have attended promotional events. It helps us understand their experiences and satisfaction levels, which we use to improve our events and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you have attended a promotional event, please fill out this form only once.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your event host or contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (Event Details, text, optional): Please describe the promotional event you attended, including its title, date, and location.
* **Satisfaction** (Satisfaction, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event, using the scale:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Neutral
* **Feedback** (Feedback, note, optional): Share your thoughts and suggestions about the event, including what we did well and what we could improve on.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you and keep you informed about upcoming events and services.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Please provide your name and title/position, so we can address you personally and show appreciation for your feedback.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form honestly and completely, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and input, and we will use your feedback to improve our events and services.
