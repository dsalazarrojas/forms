# harp_performance_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on performances related to harp music, allowing the event organizers to assess and improve the overall experience of their events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select your choices for the multiple-choice questions.
3. Fill in any text fields with your thoughts and comments.
4. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

* **Harp Performance** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe your overall impression of the harp performance. This field is optional, but your feedback is valuable.
* **Music Style** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the style of music performed during the event:
	+ Harp Folk
	+ Harp Baroque
	+ Harp Pop
* **Performance Level** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all levels that apply to the harp player's performance:
	+ '1'
	+ '2'
	+ '3'
* **Harp Tune** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): If you remember any particular harp tune that was played during the event, please mention it here.
* **Event Details** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional details about the event that you think are relevant to your feedback.
* **Music Performance** (`6`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the music performance:
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Event Clarity** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply to the event organization:
	+ Clarity
	+ Clarity 1
	+ Clarity 2
* **Harp Player** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any comments or feedback about the harp player, please mention it here.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`9`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event:
	+ '1'
	+ '2'
	+ '3'
* **Event Atmosphere** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply to the event atmosphere:
	+ Atmosphere

	+ Atmosphere 2
* **Communication** (`11`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the communication:
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Harp Tune Quality** (`12`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the harp tune quality:
	+ '1'
	+ '2'
	+ '3'

Note: Please provide your feedback as honestly as possible, and all fields are optional unless specified.
