# Employee Diversity Workshop Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will explain each field in the form and how to complete it effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully and take a moment to reflect on your overall experience at the workshop.
2. For ratings fields (1, 2, 3, 4, and 5), rate your satisfaction on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is "not at all likely" and 5 is "very likely".
3. For the "Suggestion" field (4), provide any suggestions or comments you have about the workshop.
4. For the "Email" and "Phone number" fields (7 and 8), please provide your contact information if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (1)**: This is a brief introduction to help you get familiar with the form and the workshop content. Please take a moment to read it carefully.
* **How likely are you to recommend this workshop (2)**: Rate your likelihood of recommending the workshop to others based on your experience.
* **How satisfied are you with the facilitator (3)**: Rate your satisfaction with the facilitator's performance.
* **Suggestion (4)**: Provide any suggestions or comments about the workshop you would like to share with us.
* **How likely are you to attend another workshop (5)**: Rate your likelihood of attending another workshop similar to this one.
* **How likely are you to recommend this workshop to others (6)**: Rate your likelihood of recommending the workshop to others.
* **Email (7)**: If you would like to be contacted for further feedback or follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Phone number (8)**: If you would like to be contacted for further feedback or follow-up, please provide your phone number.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or details about your experience when possible.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the "Suggestion" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
