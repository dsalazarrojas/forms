# Returnee Invitation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users who have attended an event. Your responses will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the appropriate options from the first page and fill in the text field on the second page. You can skip pages that are not applicable to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Feedback (Select one or more)** (`event-feedback-form-1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: true/): Select one of the following options to describe your experience:
  + 1: The event was well-organized and met my expectations.
  + 2: The event was average, I have some suggestions for improvement.
  + 3: The event was poorly organized and did not meet my expectations.
* **Additional Feedback** (`event-feedback-form-2`, `text`, required/optional: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them here. This will help us understand your thoughts and improve future events.
* **Time Arrived** (`event-feedback-form-3`, `time`, required/optional: false): Please specify the time when you arrived at the event. This will help us understand the traffic flow and timing of the event.
  Note: Please format the time using the following format: HH:MM AM/PM or 24-hour format.
