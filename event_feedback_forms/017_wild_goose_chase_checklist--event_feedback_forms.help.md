## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Challenge 1** (`challenge_1`, select_one): Select the challenge you completed.
*   **Challenge 2** (`challenge_2`, select_multiple): Select multiple challenges you participated in.
*   **Upload 1** (`upload_1`, text): Upload any additional documents or attachments related to your participation in the event.
*   **Upload 2** (`upload_2`, text): Upload any additional documents or attachments.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text): Share your thoughts and feedback about the event.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you participated in.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event you participated in.
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you attended.
*   **Team Name** (`team`, text): Enter the name of your team, if applicable.
*   **School Name** (`school`, text): Enter the name of your school or institution, if applicable.
*   **Contact Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
*   **Contact Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
