# Running Trail Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience on a running trail. Your feedback is crucial in helping event organizers and planners improve their offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and thoroughly.
2. Provide detailed information for each field.
3. Avoid submitting feedback for the same event multiple times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Introduction** (Required)
Explain your purpose for visiting the trail and any expectations you had prior to your visit.

### 2. **Trail Name** (Required)
Enter the name of the trail you visited.

### 3. **Water Points** (Required)
Select the water points on the trail from the options provided:

*   '1' for One water point
*   '2' for Two water points
*   '3' for Three water points

### 4. **Trail Conditions** (Required)
Choose the trail conditions you experienced:

*   Clear
*   Cloudy
*   Rainy

### 5. **Terrain Difficulty** (Required)
Select the terrain difficulty you encountered:

*   Easy
*   Medium
*   Hard

### 6. **Feedback** (Required)
Describe your overall feedback on the trail.

### 7. **Additional Comments** (Optional)
If you have any additional comments or feedback that don't fit in the provided fields, please use this section.

### 8. **Email** (Optional)
Provide your email address for event organizers to contact you if needed.

### 9. **Phone** (Optional)
Provide your phone number for event organizers to contact you if needed.

### 10. **Assigned** (Required)
Select the event organizer or planner who assigned you to this trail:

*   chatjimmy
*   Other

### 11. **Comments** (Optional)
If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use this section.

### 12. **Event Name** (Required)
Enter the name of the event you attended.

### 13. **Event Date** (Required)
Enter the date of the event you attended in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.

### 14. **Event Location** (Required)
Enter the location of the event.

### 15. **Course Length** (Required)
Enter the length of the course in kilometers or miles.

### 16. **Course Elevation Gain** (Required)
Enter the elevation gain of the course in meters or feet.

### 17. **Course Type** (Required)
Select the type of course:

*   Trail
*   Road
*   Mixed

### 18. **Course Surface** (Required)
Choose the surface of the course:

*   Grass
*   Dirt
*   Gravel
*   Concrete

### 19. **Course Direction** (Required)
Select the direction of the course:

*   Clockwise
*   Counter Clockwise

### 20. **Course Climate** (Required)
Choose the climate of the course:

*   Hot
*   Cold
*   Rainy

### 21. **Course Weather** (Required)
Select the weather of the course:

*   Sunny
*   Cloudy
*   Rainy

### 22. **Course Visibility** (Required)
Select the visibility of the course:

*   Good
*   Poor
*   None

### 23. **Course Notes** (Optional)
If you have any additional notes or feedback about the course, please use this section.

### 24. **Event Organizer** (Required)
Enter the name of the event organizer.

### 25. **Event Website** (Optional)
Enter the website of the event if available.

Please note that fields marked as "Required" must be completed for your feedback to be valid. Thank you for taking the time to provide your valuable feedback!
