# trade_show_attendee_experience_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and ratings from attendees of trade shows to help evaluate the quality of the event and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall experience with the trade show on the "Overall Experience Rating" page.
2. Indicate how likely you are to recommend the event to others on the "Likely to Recommend" page.
3. Evaluate the value you received for the money you spent on the "Value for Money" page.
4. Rate your interest in the categories and sessions presented at the event on the "Category Interest" and "Session Interest" pages, respectively.
5. Indicate how likely you are to attend a future event similar to this one on the "Likely to Attend Next" page.
6. Rate how likely you are to recommend the event venue on the "Likely to Recommend Venue" page.
7. Provide any additional comments or suggestions on the "Feedback" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience Rating** (Page 1, Required): This field allows you to rate your overall experience at the trade show. Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Likely to Recommend** (Page 2, Required): This field asks how likely you are to recommend the event to others. Select from "Not Likely", "Somewhat Likely", "Likely", "Very Likely", or "Extremely Likely".
* **Value for Money** (Page 3, Required): This field asks how you evaluate the value you received for the money you spent on the event. Think about whether the event was worth the cost, and rate it on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Category Interest** (Page 4, Required): This field allows you to rate your interest in the categories presented at the event. Select all categories that apply to you from "Not Interested", "Somewhat Interested", "Interested", "Very Interested", or "Extremely Interested".
* **Session Interest** (Page 5, Required): Similar to "Category Interest", this field asks about your interest in the sessions presented at the event.
* **Likely to Attend Next** (Page 6, Required): This field asks how likely you are to attend a similar event in the future. Select from "Not Likely", "Somewhat Likely", "Likely", "Very Likely", or "Extremely Likely".
* **Likely to Recommend Venue** (Page 7, Required): Similar to "Likely to Recommend", this field asks how likely you are to recommend the event venue.
* **Feedback** (Page 8, Required): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have for the event. Please provide any feedback you think would be helpful to the organizers.
