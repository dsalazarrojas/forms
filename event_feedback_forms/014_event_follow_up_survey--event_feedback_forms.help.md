# Event Follow Up Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Follow Up Survey is designed to gather feedback from event attendees about their experience at the event. This feedback will help us improve future events and provide better services to our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
2. Choose the correct event type (e.g., Event, Meeting, Conference).
3. Enter the date of the event.
4. Provide the location of the event.
5. Choose the number of attendees who attended the event.
6. Select your level of satisfaction with the event (Good, Fair, Poor).
7. Enter any comments or feedback you'd like to share about the event.
8. Fill in your contact information (first name, last name, email, phone number) so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **event_feedback_form**
(event_follow_up_survey, text, required: false)
This is the main section where you can provide feedback about the event. Please write your thoughts and opinions about your experience at the event.

### **event_type**
(event_type, select_one, required: false)
Choose the type of event that took place (Event, Meeting, Conference).

### **event_date**
(event_date, date, required: false)
Enter the date of the event.

### **event_location**
(event_location, text, required: false)
Provide the location of the event.

### **event_time**
(event_time, time, required: false)
Enter the time of the event.

### **event_attendees**
(event_attendees, number, required: false)
Choose the number of attendees you saw at the event.

### **event_satisfaction**
(event_satisfaction, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your level of satisfaction with the event (Good, Fair, Poor).

### **event_feedback**
(event_feedback, text, required: false)
Provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.

### **contact_first_name**
(contact_first_name, text, required: false)
Enter your first name.

### **contact_last_name**
(contact_last_name, text, required: false)
Enter your last name.

### **contact_email**

### **contact_phone**
(contact_phone, text, required: false)

Note: This form is designed to be as clear and concise as possible, but if you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
