# # Feedback Form B&amp;M 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Feedback Form B&amp;M 2020 is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the field labeled "first_name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "last_name".
3. Provide your email address in the field labeled "email".
4. Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback about in the field labeled "event_name".
5. Share your feedback in the field labeled "feedback".
6. Select the date when you attended the event in the field labeled "date".
7. Enter the time you attended the event in the field labeled "time".
8. Identify the tool or feature that was assigned to you to use during the event in the field labeled "assigned_tool".
9. Describe any category or area of the event that you want to provide feedback on in the field labeled "category".
10. Enter any additional description of your feedback in the field labeled "description".
11. Choose who you submitted this feedback to (e.g., "Feedback Team", "Production Team", "Event Team", etc.) in the field labeled "submitted_by".
12. Select which form you would like to submit your feedback to in the field labeled "submitted_to" (e.g., "Feedback Form B&amp;M 2020", "Event Feedback Forms", etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **event_name** (Text): Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback about.
* **feedback** (Text): Share your feedback about the event.
* **date** (Date): Select the date you attended the event.
* **time** (Time): Select the time you attended the event.
* **assigned_tool** (Text): Identify the tool or feature that was assigned to you to use during the event.
* **category** (Text): Describe the category or area of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **description** (Text): Enter any additional description of your feedback.
* **submitted_by** (Select One): Choose who you submitted this feedback to.
* **submitted_to** (Select Multiple): Select which form you would like to submit your feedback to.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when providing feedback.
* You can submit feedback to multiple forms if needed.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
