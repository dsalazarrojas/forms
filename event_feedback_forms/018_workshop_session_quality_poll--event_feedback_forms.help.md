# Workshop Session Quality Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Session Quality Poll is a form designed to collect feedback from attendees on their experience during the workshop. This feedback will help us improve the quality of future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate the session: Answer the first question on how well the session was rated.
2. Evaluate the content quality: Choose how well-structured the content was.
3. Assess the instructor performance: Choose how well the instructor performed.
4. Share your overall impression: Select all that apply from the options provided.
5. Add any additional comments: You can provide any additional feedback or comments you'd like to share.
6. Enter your contact information: If you'd like to receive updates or follow-up questions, please provide your email address or phone number.
7. Provide the date and time of the session: Enter the date and time of the workshop session.
8. Confirm the location: Select the location of the workshop.
9. Choose the session length: Select the length of the workshop session.
10. Add any additional questions: If you have any questions that weren't asked, you can provide them here.
11. Confirm submission: Finally, let us know if you submitted this form or not.
12. Provide any additional contact information: If you'd like to be contacted, you can provide any additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How was the session rated** (`session_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the session from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
- **How well-structured was the content** (`content_quality`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well-structured the content was, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **How well did the instructor perform** (`instructor_performance`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well the instructor performed, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **What was your overall impression** (`overall_impression`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Any additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required): Provide any additional feedback or comments.
- **E-mail** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in the format +1-123-456-7890.
- **Date/Time** (`date_time`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time of the workshop session in YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM format.
- **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required): Select the location of the workshop.
- **Session length** (`session_length`, `select_one`, required): Select the length of the workshop session, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Other (specify)** (`session_length_other`, `text`, required): If the workshop session was not one of the standard lengths, provide the length in text.
- **Additional questions** (`additional_questions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any additional questions you have, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you submitted this form or not.
- **Other (specify)** (`submitted_by_other`, `text`, required): If not submitted, provide the reason in text.
- **Session date/time** (`session_date_time`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time of the workshop session.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can contact the session organizers for clarification.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, please add them in the designated fields.
