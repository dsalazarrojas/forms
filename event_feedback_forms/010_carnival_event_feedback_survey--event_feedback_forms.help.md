# Carnival Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Carnival Event Feedback Survey form, providing information on how to complete it and what each field means.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in the required fields (indicated by **required: true** in the form).
2. Answer the questions based on your experience at the carnival.
3. Provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Carnival Event Feedback Survey** (1): This is the title of the form, which is simply a label to indicate the purpose of the form.
* **Your Name** (2): This is an optional field for you to provide your name, which will be used for prize notifications or other communication.
* **Email Address** (3): This is also an optional field for you to provide your email address, which will be used for prize notifications or other communication.
* **Date of Visit** (4): Please enter the date you visited the carnival. This is a required field as it helps us understand when you attended.
* **Time of Visit** (5): If you'd like to share, please provide an approximate time of visit.
* **Who Did You Attend With?** (6): Choose from the options how many people were with you at the carnival. This helps us understand group dynamics.
	+ Alone
	+ With spouse or partner
	+ With children
	+ With friends
	+ With extended family
	+ With school or church group
	+ Other
* **Number in Your Group** (7): Enter the total number of people in your group.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (8): Rate your overall experience at the carnival using the provided options.
