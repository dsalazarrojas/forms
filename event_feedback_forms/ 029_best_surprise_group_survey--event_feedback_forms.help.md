# Best Surprise Group Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Surprise Group Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience in a "Best Surprise Group" event. This form will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number (optional).
2. Select the date that is relevant to your experience in the event.
3. Evaluate the event by choosing the primary selection that best applies (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
4. If you have any quantity or rating to provide, enter it in the corresponding field.
5. Provide any additional details about your experience.
6. Confirm your agreement with any applicable options.
7. If applicable, estimate any value related to your experience.
8. If you have a preferred time for a future event, please enter it.
9. Finally, provide any closing comments or remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Full name, text, required): Enter your legal full name for contact purposes.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, optional): Include your country code if you are outside the local region.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Select the date that is relevant to your experience in the event.
* **Primary selection** (Primary selection, select_one, required): Choose the option that best applies (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Quantity or rating** (Quantity or rating, number, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
* **Additional details** (Additional details, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about your experience.
* **Agreement** (Agreement, select_multiple, required): Select any applicable options (I agree, I need follow up, or Not applicable).
* **Estimated value** (Estimated value, number, optional): Use decimal format when applicable.
* **Preferred time** (Preferred time, time, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (Final comments or notes, text, optional): Enter any closing remarks or comments.
