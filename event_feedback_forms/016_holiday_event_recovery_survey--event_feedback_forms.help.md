# Holiday Event Recovery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "Holiday Event Recovery Survey" form, helping you understand the purpose, completion process, and field explanations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions honestly, and make sure to submit your feedback only if you attended the holiday event.
2. Select or type your responses for each question according to the options provided.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by "required: true") before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Welcome to this Survey
* **Welcome to this survey**: This is a note that welcomes you to the survey.

### 2. How was your holiday event?
* **How was your holiday event?**: Select "Yes" if you enjoyed the event and "No" if you did not.
	+ Note: This question is optional, and your response will help us gauge the overall satisfaction with the event.

### 3. Event Satisfaction Rating
* **On a scale of 1 to 5, how satisfied were you with the event?**: Rate your satisfaction level from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
	+ Note: This question is required to help us understand your level of satisfaction with the event.

### 4. What was the highlight of the event for you?
* **What was the highlight of the event for you?**: Share your thoughts about the most enjoyable part of the event.
	+ Note: This question is optional, and your response will help us identify areas of improvement.

### 5. Feedback
* **Was there something that we did particularly well or poorly?**: Share any positive or negative experiences you had during the event.
	+ Note: This question is optional, and your response will help us refine our event planning.

### 6. Future Interest
* **How likely are you to attend our events in the future?**: Select "Yes" if you're interested in attending future events, and "No" otherwise.
	+ Note: This question is optional, and your response will help us determine the demand for future events.

### 7. Contact Information
* **How do you prefer to be contacted for more information about our future events?**: Provide your preferred method of contact (e.g., email, phone number).
	+ Note: This question is required to help us contact you for future events.

### 8. Comments or Suggestions
* **Any comments or suggestions?**: Share any additional thoughts or ideas you have about the event or future events.
	+ Note: This question is optional, and your response will help us improve our events.

### 9. Contact Us
* **We would love to hear from you!**: Provide your email address so we can contact you for more information about future events.
	+ Note: This question is required to allow us to contact you for future events.
