# Product Innovation Judges Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from product innovation judges on the products they have evaluated. This feedback will help us understand the judges' perceptions and experiences with the products, enabling us to improve our products and services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your judge information, including your name, title, organization, and position.
2. Evaluate each product and rate it on a scale of 1-5 for innovation score and overall score.
3. Provide feedback on the product's name, presentation, and any additional comments you may have.
4. Enter any additional feedback or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Judge Information** (`judge_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself and your background as a judge.
* **Email** (`judge_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address, if you want us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`judge_phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number, if you want us to contact you.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating** (`product_rating`, number, optional): Rate the product on a scale of 1-5 for its overall innovation.
* **Innovation Score** (`innovation_score`, number, optional): Rate the product's innovation score on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Feedback** (`product_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments you may have on the product's design or functionality.
* **Presentation** (`product_presentation`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments you may have on the product's presentation.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, optional): Rate the product's overall score on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Innovation** (`product_innovation`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback on the product's innovation potential.
* **Product Improvement** (`product_improvement`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions or ideas for improvement on the product.
* **Product Innovation Score** (`product_innovation_score`, number, optional): Rate the product's innovation potential on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Recommendation** (`product_recommendation`, text, optional): Enter any recommendation or suggestions on the product.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback on the product.
* **Additional Feedback** (`page_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback on the product.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that may be helpful for us to know.
* **Name** (`judge_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Title** (`judge_title`, text, optional): Enter your title or position for identification purposes.
* **Organization** (`judge_organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization or company for identification purposes.
* **Position** (`judge_position`, text, optional): Enter your position or role within your organization.
* **Department** (`judge_department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team within your organization.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit your feedback.
