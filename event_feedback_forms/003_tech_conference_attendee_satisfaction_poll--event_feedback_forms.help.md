# Tech Conference Attendee Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Conference Attendee Satisfaction Poll is a survey designed to collect feedback from attendees on their overall experience at the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Provide your overall rating of the conference on the "overall" page.
3. Rate the speakers on the "speaker" page.
4. Rate the material (e.g., food, venue, etc.) on the "material" page.
5. Provide any additional feedback on the "feedback" page.
6. Enter your email address on the "email" page.
7. (Optional) Enter your phone number on the "phone" page.
8. Click the "submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page is a brief welcome message.
* **Overall**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference on a scale of 1-5.
* **Speaker**: Rate the quality of the speakers on a scale of 1-5.
* **Material**: Rate the quality of the conference material (e.g., food, venue, etc.) on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have for the conference.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you and keep you informed about future conferences.
* **Phone**: This field is optional and can be used to provide an additional contact method.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit your feedback.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form only once during the conference.
* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us and we will be happy to help.
