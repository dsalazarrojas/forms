# Public Sector Leadership Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Sector Leadership Program Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with the Public Sector Leadership Program. This form helps us understand what works well and what areas need improvement, allowing us to make the program more effective and beneficial for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question based on your honest experience with the Public Sector Leadership Program.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and their options.
3. For select-one questions, choose one option that best represents your answer.
4. For select-multiple questions, choose all options that apply.
5. For text fields, provide a clear and concise answer.
6. For date and time fields, enter the date and time of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`form_1`): A note to explain the purpose and importance of the evaluation.
* **Overall Program Satisfaction** (`form_2`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program. Choose one option that best represents your experience (Satisfied or Dissatisfied).
* **Content Clarity** (`form_3`): Indicate if the program content was clear and easy to understand (Yes or No).
* **Speaker** (`form_4`): Enter the name of the speaker or facilitator.
* **Presentation Skills** (`form_5`): Provide a numerical score to rate the speaker's presentation skills (e.g., 1-10).
* **Interaction** (`form_6`): Describe your experience with the interaction with other participants (e.g., networking, discussions).
* **Content** (`form_7`): Provide feedback on the program content (e.g., relevance, effectiveness).
* **Recommendations** (`form_8`): Suggest any improvements or suggestions for future program development.
* **Comments** (`form_9`): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the program.
* **Additional Comments** (`form_10`): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the program.
* **Evaluation Date** (`form_11`): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`form_12`): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Location** (`form_13`): Indicate if the evaluation took place at the same location as the program (Yes or No).
* **Evaluations** (`form_14`): Indicate if you have evaluated this program before (Yes or No).
* **Participants** (`form_15`): Indicate if you are a participant in the program (Yes or No).
* **Organization** (`form_16`): Indicate if your organization is participating in the program (Yes or No).
* **Position** (`form_17`): Indicate if you are in a position to benefit from the program (Yes or No).
* **Email** (`form_18`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`form_19`): Enter your phone number.
* **Evaluation Type** (`form_20`): Indicate if you are evaluating this program for personal or professional reasons (Yes or No).
* **Form Submission** (`form_21`): Indicate if you are submitting this form electronically (Yes or No).
* **Form Completion** (`form_22`): Indicate if you have completed this form (Yes or No).
* **Form Completion Date** (`form_23`): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Form Completion Time** (`form_24`): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Submit** (`form_25`): Confirm that you have submitted this form (Yes or No).
