# Henna Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with an event hosted by our artists. Your input will help us to evaluate the quality of service provided and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest feedback.
- Select the corresponding answers for the multiple-choice questions.
- If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Satisfaction** (`Number`, `Optional`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
* **Artist Rating** (`Select One`, `Optional`): Rate the artist's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Artist Comments** (`Text`, `Required`): Provide specific comments about your experience with the artist.
* **Artist Recommendations** (`Text`, `Optional`): If you would recommend this artist to a friend, please provide the reasons why.
* **Artist Name** (`Text`, `Required`): Enter the name of the artist who hosted your event.
* **Event Comments** (`Text`, `Optional`): Provide any comments about the event itself.
* **Event Date** (`Date`, `Required`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`Time`, `Required`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`Text`, `Optional`): Enter the location of the event.
* **Artist Experience** (`Number`, `Optional`): Rate your experience with the artist on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Would Recommend Event** (`Select Multiple`, `Optional`): Indicate whether you would recommend this event to others.
* **Comments** (`Text`, `Optional`): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Event ID** (`Text`, `Optional`): Enter the ID of the event (if available).
* **Event Name** (`Text`, `Optional`): Enter the name of the event (if available).
* **Contact E-mail** (`E-mail`, `Required`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`Text`, `Required`): Enter your phone number.
* **Client Name** (`Text`, `Required`): Enter your name.
* **Client Phone** (`Text`, `Optional`): Enter your phone number.
* **Client E-mail** (`E-mail`, `Optional`): Enter your email address.
* **Additional Comments** (`Text`, `Optional`): Provide any additional comments.
* **Event Type** (`Select One`, `Required`): Choose the type of event (Wedding, Party, Workshop, Photo Shoot, or Other).
* **Number of Participants** (`Number`, `Optional`): Enter the number of participants.
* **Number of Artists** (`Number`, `Optional`): Enter the number of artists present.
* **Artist Fees** (`Select One`, `Required`): Choose the artist fees range ($0-$50, $51-$100, $101-$200, or Other).
* **Artist Travel** (`Select One`, `Required`): Indicate whether the artist traveled for the event.
