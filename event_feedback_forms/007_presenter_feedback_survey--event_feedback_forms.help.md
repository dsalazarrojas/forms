# Presenter Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees on the presentation skills and content of the presenters at the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions below. Each question is explained in further detail in the field-by-field explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Event Feedback Survey (Required: False)**
This is the title of the form, and you are not required to provide this information if you do not wish to.

### **2. Presenter (Required: False)**
Please enter the name of the presenter you are providing feedback on.

### **3. Presentation Skills (Select Multiple, Required: False)**
Choose all that apply for the presenter's presentation skills:
* Excellent
* Poor
* Average

### **4. Presentation Content (Required: False)**
Please provide a brief description of the presenter's presentation content.

### **5. Presenter Availability (Select One, Required: False)**
Choose the presenter's availability:
* Available
* Not Available
* Not Applicable

### **6. Presentation Format (Select One, Required: False)**
Choose the presentation format:
* True
* False

### **7. Communication Skills (Required: False)**
Please enter the name of the presenter's organization if you are familiar with it.

### **8. Comments (Required: False)**
Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the event or presenter.

### Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* If you are familiar with the presenter's organization, please provide the name of the organization.
* This form is not intended for self-evaluation, please do not fill out this form for yourself.
* If you are unable to complete the form, you can contact us at form@gic.mx for further assistance.
