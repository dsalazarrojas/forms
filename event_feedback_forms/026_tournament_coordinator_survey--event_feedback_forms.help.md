# Tournament Coordinator Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from tournament coordinators about their experience with the tournament organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Name** (<name>, text, optional): Enter the title of the tournament you were involved with.
* **Staff Name** (<name>, text, optional): Enter the name of the staff member who coordinated your event.
* **What important things should we change about the tournament?** (<label>, text, optional): Provide any significant suggestions or criticisms of the tournament.
* **What minor things should we change about the tournament?** (<label>, text, optional): Share minor suggestions or criticisms about the tournament.
* **How would you rate our communication during the tournament?** (<label>, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with our communication during the tournament.
* **Any other comments about the tournament?** (<label>, text, optional): Leave any other comments or thoughts about the tournament.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve.
* Your input is valuable and will help us improve our services for future events. 

Note: Since the form is not required for all fields, users can still submit the form even if they do not fill in all fields. However, please note that your feedback will be more comprehensive if you fill in all the fields.
