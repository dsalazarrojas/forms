# Friend Roast Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Friend Roast Feedback Form is designed to collect participant feedback after an event. This form helps us understand what worked well and what didn't during the event, allowing us to make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the event that you participated in.
2. Select the type of event that took place (e.g., roast, dinner, or comedy show).
3. If desired, provide a note or brief comment about your experience.
4. Suggest any improvements or changes for future events.
5. Rate how funny you found the event (on a scale of 1 to 4).
6. Enter your email address and phone number (optional, but recommended in case you want a response to your feedback).
7. Finally, choose the date and time of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event that you participated in.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event that took place (e.g., Roast, Dinner, Comedy).
* **Participant Feedback**: Provide a brief note or comment about your experience. This is an optional field, but if you have something specific to share, please do so here.
* **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improving future events, enter them here.
* **Humor Level**: Rate how funny you found the event, using the scale:
	+ Not funny at all
	+ Somewhat funny
	+ Very funny
	+ Extremely funny
* **Constructive Feedback**: Enter any constructive feedback you have for the event organizers.
* **Rating**: Rate the event on a scale of 1 to 4, where 1 is the lowest and 4 is the highest.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can respond to your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the event.

## Tips
* Be specific and clear when providing feedback.
* Focus on providing constructive feedback that can help us improve future events.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
