# Virtual Event Quality Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Event Quality Questionnaire is a form used to collect feedback from attendees on the quality of virtual events. Event organizers use this information to evaluate and improve the performance of virtual events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details as an attendee who has attended a virtual event.
2. Provide the name of the event you attended.
3. Enter the date of the event you attended.
4. Select the level of quality you experienced during the event (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor).
5. Share your feedback about the event in the space provided.
6. If you have any further comments, suggestions, or areas for improvement, type them in the text box.
7. If you have any contact information (email or phone number) that would be helpful for us to follow up, please enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name (1)
* **Event Name**: This is the title of the virtual event you attended.
* **Type**: Text field for entering event name.
* **Required**: Optional, but recommended for clarity.

### Event Date (2)
* **Event Date**: Enter the date on which you attended the virtual event.
* **Type**: Date field for entering event date.
* **Required**: Optional, but recommended for accuracy.

### Time (3)
* **Time**: Enter the time you attended the virtual event.
* **Type**: Time field for entering event time.
* **Required**: Optional, but recommended for clarity.

### Rating (4)
* **Rating**: Select the level of quality you experienced during the event (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor).
* **Type**: Select one option from the dropdown list.
* **Required**: Optional, but recommended for useful feedback.

### Feedback (5)
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts about the event in a text box.
* **Type**: Text field for entering event feedback.
* **Required**: Optional, but encouraged for detailed feedback.

### Email (6)
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you would like to be contacted for follow-up or additional questions.
* **Type**: Email field for entering email address.
* **Required**: Optional.

### Phone (7)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Type**: Text field for entering phone number.
* **Required**: Optional.

### Improvement Areas (8)
* **Improvement areas**: List areas where you felt the event could be improved.
* **Type**: Text field for entering improvement areas.
* **Required**: Optional, but encouraged for specific feedback.

### Suggestions (9)
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions you have for future improvements.
* **Type**: Text field for entering suggestions.
* **Required**: Optional, but encouraged for useful input.

### Event ID (10)
* **Event ID**: Enter the ID number of the virtual event.
* **Type**: Number field for entering event ID.
* **Required**: Optional, but recommended for reference.

### Organizer Name (11)
* **Organizer Name**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Type**: Text field for entering organizer name.
* **Required**: Optional.

### Organization (12)
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization hosting the event.
* **Type**: Text field for entering organization name.
* **Required**: Optional.

### Department (13)
* **Department**: Enter the department name of the organization hosting the event.
* **Type**: Text field for entering department name.
* **Required**: Optional.

### Job Title (14)
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Type**: Text field for entering job title.
* **Required**: Optional.

### Attendee Name (15)
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as an attendee.
* **Type**: Text field for entering attendee name.
* **Required**: Optional.

## Tips

* Please complete this form honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be specific about areas for improvement and suggestions for future events.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is valuable to us and will help improve virtual events.
