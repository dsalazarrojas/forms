# Soccer Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from soccer players about their experience at a recent soccer event. The purpose of this form is to help us understand the quality of our services and make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as prompted. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the feedback field. You can access the form at any time, but please submit it as soon as possible after the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Date**: This field is for selecting the date of the event you attended. Enter the date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Name**: This field is for entering the name of the event you attended.
* **Session Length**: This field is for selecting the length of the session, which can be Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Session Length Value**: This field is for entering a numerical value to further specify the session length. This is optional.
* **Event Level**: This field is for selecting the overall level of the event, which can be Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced. This field is optional.
* **Event Organizer**: This field is for entering the name of the event organizer. This field is optional.
* **Feedback**: This field is for providing any additional feedback about your experience at the event. This field is optional but highly recommended.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit the form as soon as possible after the event to ensure timely and accurate feedback.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
