# Enrichment Activity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrichment Activity Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from participants and event organizers on the effectiveness and enjoyment of enrichment activities conducted during events. This feedback will help in identifying areas of improvement and successes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Enrichment Activity Feedback Form from the available options.
2. Fill out each field in the form, providing detailed and specific feedback on each section.
3. Submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Enrichment Activity Feedback Form
* **participant_id**: A unique number that identifies the participant who submitted the feedback.
* **event_id**: A unique number that identifies the event the feedback is related to.
* **enrichment_type**: Select one or multiple enrichment types that you experienced during the event (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **rating**: Rate the quality of the enrichment activities on a scale of 1-10.
* **comment**: Provide a detailed and specific comment on the enrichment activities.
* **suggestions**: Suggest any improvements or additions to the enrichment activities.
* **assigned_tool**: Provide any tools that were assigned to you during the event.
* **category**: Select the category of the event (Category 1, Category 2, or Category 3).
* **description**: Provide a detailed description of the enrichment activities.
* **participant_satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the participant experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **event_satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.
* **overall_satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the entire event on a scale of 1-10.
* **participant_comments**: Provide any comments or feedback you have as a participant.
* **event_comments**: Provide any comments or feedback you have as an event organizer.
* **event_organizer_comments**: Provide any comments or feedback you have for the event organizers.
* **toolkit_comments**: Provide any comments or feedback you have on the tools used during the event.
* **toolkit_suggestions**: Suggest any improvements or additions to the tools used during the event.
* **participant_additional_comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have as a participant.
* **participant_rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the participant experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **participant_satisfaction_score**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the participant experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **event_satisfaction_score**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.
* **overall_satisfaction_score**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the entire event on a scale of 1-10.
* **event_organizer_feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments for the event organizers.
* **comments_and_suggestions**: Provide any comments or feedback you have for the event or the organizers.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide detailed and specific feedback in each field.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please provide them in the comments field.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the suggestions field.
