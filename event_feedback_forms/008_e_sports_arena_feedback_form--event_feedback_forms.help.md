# E Sports Arena Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Sports Arena Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences at events held at the E Sports Arena. This feedback will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name and Email address in the first and second fields.
2. Provide a brief title for your feedback in the third field, "Feedback Title".
3. Describe your feedback in the third field, "Feedback Description".
4. Rate your experience in the fourth field, "Rating".
5. Add any additional details about your feedback in the fifth field, "Feedback Details".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you about your feedback.
* **Feedback Title** (`feedback_title`, text, Optional): Provide a brief title for your feedback.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, Optional): If you attended an event, please enter the name of the event.
* **Feedback Description** (`feedback_description`, text, Optional): Describe your experience and any suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, Optional): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, text, Optional): Add any additional details about your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* You can rate your experience based on the quality of the event, the staff, the facilities, or any other aspect that you feel is relevant.
* If you have any photos, videos, or other materials that support your feedback, feel free to attach them to your submission.
