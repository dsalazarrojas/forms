# Science Fair Organization Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants or attendees of the science fair event. Your input will help us understand how to improve the organization of future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer them according to your experience and observations.
2. Fill in all required fields, which are marked as "required".
3. Provide as much detail as possible in text fields, such as "Event Details", "Reasons for Feedback", "Improvement Suggestions", "Overall Experience", and "Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Event Details**: Please describe the event that you attended, including the date, location, and any notable happenings.
* **Event Team**: Please select the options that apply to your experience with the event team, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Feedback Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Reasons for Feedback**: Select all that apply to your reasons for giving feedback, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Event Satisfaction**: Describe your overall satisfaction with the event.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Suggest any ways that we can improve the event in the future.
* **Overall Experience**: Describe your overall experience at the event.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions that you may have.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
