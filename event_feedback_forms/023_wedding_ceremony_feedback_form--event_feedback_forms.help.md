# Wedding Ceremony Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a wedding ceremony. It will help wedding planners, coordinators, and other stakeholders assess the quality of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the "First Name" field. This is an optional field, but it's great if you'd like to be recognized in the feedback report.
2. Enter your **last name** in the "Last Name" field. Again, this is optional.
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field. Please note that this email address will not be used for spam or any other purpose other than to contact you with any questions or concerns about your feedback.
4. Rate the ceremony with a score from 1 to 5 in the "Rating" field. 1 is Poor, 2 is Fair, 3 is Neutral, 4 is Good, and 5 is Excellent.
5. Share any comments or suggestions you have about the ceremony in the "Comments" field. This is optional, but it's a great opportunity to provide detailed feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear in the feedback report.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear in the feedback report.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you with any questions or concerns about your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the ceremony with a score from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor, 2 is Fair, 3 is Neutral, 4 is Good, and 5 is Excellent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the ceremony. This is a great opportunity to provide detailed feedback.
