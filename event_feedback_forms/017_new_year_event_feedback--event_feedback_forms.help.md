# new_year_event_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "new_year_event_feedback" form, which is used to collect feedback and ratings from users about a recent event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: **Overall Experience** (id: 2), **Email** (id: 5), and **Feedback Submission Time** (id: 10).
2. If you want to rate the event, select one of the three options under **Event Rating** (id: 3).
3. If you have comments or suggestions, type them in the **Comments and Suggestions** (id: 4) field.
4. Choose the social media platforms you'd like to share your feedback on under **Social Media** (id: 7).
5. If you agree to follow up on your feedback, select **Follow Up** (id: 8) to true.
6. Add any additional comments or thoughts in the **Additional Comments** (id: 9) field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details (id: 1)**: Describe the event you attended or participated in. This is not required, but it's helpful for our team to understand the context of your feedback.
* **Overall Experience (id: 2)**: Rate your overall experience at the event on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Event Rating (id: 3)**: Choose a rating for the event, with options 1, 2, or 3.
* **Comments and Suggestions (id: 4)**: Type any comments or suggestions you have about the event. This is optional, but it's a great way to provide more detailed feedback.
* **Email (id: 5)**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed. This is a required field.
* **Phone (id: 6)**: This field is optional, and you can enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Social Media (id: 7)**: Choose which social media platforms you'd like to share your feedback on. This will allow us to share your feedback with a broader audience.
* **Follow Up (id: 8)**: Select "True" to agree to follow up with you on your feedback. This is optional.
* **Additional Comments (id: 9)**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts, type them in here. This is optional.
* **Feedback Submission Time (id: 10)**: This field is not something you'll need to fill out, but it's required for us to track when you submitted your feedback.
