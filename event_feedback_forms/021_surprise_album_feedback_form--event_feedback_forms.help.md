We are creating this guide to help users understand the purpose and usage of the "surprise_album_feedback_form". This form is designed to collect feedback from users about surprise albums, and it is essential that we get accurate and complete information from them. This guide will walk you through each field and its intended use, ensuring that users understand the form's purpose and can complete it accurately.

# surprise_album_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on surprise albums, allowing us to gauge user reactions, identify areas for improvement, and better understand their experiences. Please provide your honest feedback in the spaces provided.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide useful feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the spaces provided.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
3. If a field is not relevant to your experience, you can leave it blank or skip it.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the dedicated comment fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Album Name
**Album Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the name of the album you are giving feedback on.

### Release Date
**Release Date** (`2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the album was released.

### Rating
**Rating** (`3`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience with the album, on a scale of 1-5.

### What did you think?
**What did you think?** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts about the album.

### Suggestions
**Suggestions** (`5`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them here.

### Category
**Category** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category the album belongs to (Music, Art, Film, or Other).

### Artist Name
**Artist Name** (`7`, `text`, required: false): If you know the artist or band behind the album, please provide their name.

### Email
**Email** (`8`, `email`, required: false): Please provide your email address so we can reach out to you for further feedback.

### Phone Number
**Phone Number** (`9`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted directly, please provide your phone number.

### Notes
**Notes** (`10`, `note`, required: false): Leave any additional notes or comments about your experience.

### Comments
**Comments** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any comments that apply to your experience.

### Event Name
**Event Name** (`12`, `text`, required: false): If the album was part of an event, please provide the name of the event.

Please note that all fields are optional unless specified as required. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us.
