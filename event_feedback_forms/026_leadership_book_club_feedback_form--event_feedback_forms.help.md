This form is designed to gather feedback from members of the leadership book club on their experiences with the book, with a focus on rating their favorite elements of the book. To prevent duplicate entries, ensure that you've not already submitted feedback on the book. 

# Leadership Book Club Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Book Club Feedback Form is intended for club members to express their personal opinions on books, providing ratings and feedback on various aspects of the books.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form, and you will be directed to a new page with the form.
2. Fill in the required fields:
   * `first_name`: Enter your first name.
   * `last_name`: Enter your last name.
   * `email`: Enter your email address. 
3. Fill in the optional field:
   * `phone`: Enter your phone number, if you wish to do so.
4. Fill in the main feedback section:
   * `feedback`: Write any comments or feedback you have about the book.
5. Provide your ratings:
   * `rating`: Rate the book on a scale. Note that this field is optional and will not impact your submission.
6. Answer the yes/no questions:
   * `favorite_book`: Select 'Yes' if you liked this book, 'No' otherwise.
   * `favorite_character`: Select 'Yes' if your favorite character in this book was well-developed, 'No' otherwise.
   * `favorite_author`: Select 'Yes' if you liked the author's writing style, 'No' otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (`title`, text, required)**: Enter a brief title that you think best represents the book's summary.
* **First Name (`first_name`, text, required)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (`last_name`, text, required)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (`email`, email, required)**: Enter your email address. Please ensure it is correct, as it will be used for correspondence.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number if you wish to do so.
* **Feedback (`feedback`, note, required)**: Write any comments or feedback you have about the book.
* **Rating (`rating`, number, optional)**: Rate the book on a scale (not used to impact your submission). 
* **Favorite Book (`favorite_book`, select_multiple, required)**: Select 'Yes' if you liked this book. 
* **Favorite Character (`favorite_character`, select_one, required)**: Select 'Yes' if your favorite character was well-developed. 
* **Favorite Author (`favorite_author`, select_one, required)**: Select 'Yes' if you liked the author's writing style. 

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting.
