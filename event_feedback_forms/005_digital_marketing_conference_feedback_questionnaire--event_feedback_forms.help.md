# Digital Marketing Conference Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Digital Marketing Conference Feedback Questionnaire" is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience with the conference. This feedback will help us improve future events and ensure that you, as a valued attendee, get the most out of our conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For select multiple and select one questions, choose the options that best apply to your experience.
3. For text fields, type in your thoughts and comments.
4. Enter your email address if you wish to receive event updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How did you enjoy the conference** (Conference Feedback): This is an open-ended question where you can share your overall feelings and thoughts about the conference.
- **Which speaker(s) did you most enjoy** (Speakers): Choose the speakers that impressed you the most with their presentations.
- **How would you rate the venue** (Venue): Rate the quality of the venue from 1-10, with 10 being excellent.
- **Which session(s) did you most enjoy** (Sessions): Choose the sessions that resonated with you the most.
- **How would you rate the catering** (Catering): Rate the quality of the catering, also from 1-10.
- **How would you rate the event organization** (Organization): Rate the event organization and logistics from 1-10.
- **How would you rate the time management** (Time Management): Rate the effective use of time during the conference from 1-10.
- **Additional feedback** (Additional Feedback): Share any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about the conference.
- **Email** (Email): Enter your email address if you want to receive updates about future events.
- **Phone** (Phone): This field is currently not applicable.
- **Name** (Name): Enter your name for identification purposes.
- **Date** (Date): This field is currently not applicable.
- **Time** (Time): This field is currently not applicable.
- **Which session(s) did you attend?** (Sessions Attended): Choose the sessions you participated in.
- **What type of attendee were you?** (Attendee Type): Select the category that best describes your role at the event.
- **How did you find the feedback form** (Feedback Form): Share your thoughts on the form's usability and effectiveness.
- **Which session(s) did you most enjoy?** (Most Enjoyed Sessions): Choose the sessions that you found most engaging and valuable.
- **How did you rate the feedback form?** (Feedback Form Rating): Rate the feedback form's usability and effectiveness from 1-10.
- **Is there anything else you'd like to share with us?** (Additional Feedback): This is an open-ended question for any further comments or suggestions you have.
- **Which session(s) did you most enjoy?** (Most Enjoyed Sessions): This is a duplicate question, please refer to the above section.
 
Note: Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, and feel free to share any suggestions or ideas you have for future improvements. Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback!
