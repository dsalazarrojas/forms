# Meeting Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Feedback Form is a tool designed to gather feedback from participants after a meeting. It allows users to provide their opinions and thoughts on the meeting's name and email, and to share any comments they may have about the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the meeting in the "Meeting Name" field.
2. Enter the email address associated with the meeting in the "Meeting Email" field.
3. Provide any comments or feedback you have about the meeting in the "Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Name (<page 1>)
* **Meeting Name** (`Meeting Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the meeting you are providing feedback on.

### Meeting Email (<page 1>)
* **Meeting Email** (`Meeting Email`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with the meeting. This will help us ensure that your feedback reaches the intended recipient.

### Comment (<page 1>)
* **Comment** (`Comment`, note, required): Share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about the meeting. This is an opportunity to provide more in-depth feedback that may not fit in the other two fields.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the "Meeting Name" field is accurately completed to avoid confusion.
* Double-check the "Meeting Email" field for any typos or incorrect addresses.
* Be as specific as possible in your "Comment" field to provide valuable feedback.
* If you are not sure about something, feel free to ask your meeting organizer or contact us for assistance.
