# Downtown Visitor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from visitors about their experiences with events hosted in the Downtown area. Your input will help us improve future events and create a better experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the First Name field with your full first name.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Type your email address in the E-mail field.
4. If you attended an event, enter the date you attended in the Event Date field.
5. Provide feedback about your experience in the Feedback field.
6. If the event you attended had a name, enter it in the Event Name field.
7. Rate your experience by selecting one of the provided options in the Rating field.
8. Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience in the Comments field.
9. If you have any questions or need to contact us, leave a message in the Contact field.
10. Provide any additional information that you think will help us improve future events in the Additional Info field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full last name.
* **E-mail** (`email`, Email, Optional): Type your email address. If you don't have an email address, you can leave this field blank.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date, Optional): If you attended an event, enter the date you attended. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, Optional): Describe your experience, including what worked well and what didn't.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, Text, Optional): If the event you attended had a specific name, enter it here.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Select One, Optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Contact** (`contact`, Text, Optional): If you have any questions or need to contact us, leave a message here.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, Note, Optional): Leave any additional information that will help us improve future events.
* **Category** (`category`, Text, Optional): If you have feedback for a specific category, enter it here.
 
Note: The "Required" fields are marked with an asterisk (*) and are necessary to complete the form. The "Optional" fields are not necessary but will help us better understand your feedback.
