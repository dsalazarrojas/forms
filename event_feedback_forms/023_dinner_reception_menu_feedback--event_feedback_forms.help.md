# Dinner Reception Menu Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from attendees on the menu and service provided at the dinner reception. Your input will help us understand what we are doing well and what we can improve on to provide better dining experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for "Menu Feedback" that best describes your experience with the menu provided.
2. Choose one or more of the options for "Service" that best describe your experience with the service provided.
3. Rate the overall dish you received on a scale of 1-10.
4. If you have any additional feedback, enter it in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Menu Feedback** (`Main Page Menu Feedback`, `select_one`, required): Choose this option if you felt that the menu had too much, too little, or was just right in terms of salt content.
* **Service** (`Main Page Service`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more options that best describe your experience with the service provided. "Excellent" indicates a high level of service, "Good" is a standard level of service, "Fair" is a below-average level of service, and "Poor" is a very poor level of service.
* **Overall Dish Rating** (`Main Page Dish Rating`, `number`, required): Rate the overall quality of the dish you received on a scale of 1-10.
* **Additional Feedback** (`Main Page Additional Feedback`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional feedback or comments, enter them here. This is an optional field and is not required to be filled out.
