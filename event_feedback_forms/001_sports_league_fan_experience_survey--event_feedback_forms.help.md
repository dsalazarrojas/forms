## Purpose
The Sports League Fan Experience Survey is designed to collect feedback from attendees of sports events to gauge their overall experience. The survey aims to gather information on various aspects of the event, including overall experience, team performance, venue conditions, food and beverage quality, staff friendliness, game flow, value for money, will to attend again, and fan engagement. This feedback will help event organizers improve future events and enhance the sports league fan experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the designated text or select fields.
2. For text fields, provide a brief description or comment on the question.
3. For select fields, choose one or more options that best answer the question.
4. Ensure you answer all required questions (indicated by a red asterisk (*) in the field label).
5. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Overall Experience** (Overall Experience, text, required/optional): Share your overall impression of the event. What did you think of the overall atmosphere, excitement, and quality of the event?

- **Team Performance** (Team Performance, text, required/optional): Describe your experience with the team that hosted the event. How did they perform on the day of the event?

- **Venue Conditions** (Venue Conditions, text, required/optional): Report on the condition and quality of the venue. Was it clean, well-maintained, and comfortable?

- **Food and Beverage Quality** (Food and Beverage Quality, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply to the quality of food and beverages served. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Poor

- **Staff Friendliness** (Staff Friendliness, select_one, required/optional): Choose the staff's attitude towards attendees. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Very friendly
	+ Option 2: Somewhat friendly
	+ Option 3: Not very friendly

- **Game Flow** (Game Flow, text, required/optional): Describe your experience with the flow of the game. Were there any issues or delays that affected your experience?

- **Value for Money** (Value for Money, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts on the value for money of the event. Did you feel you got a good balance between cost and quality?

- **Will Attend Again** (Will Attend Again, select_one, required/optional): Choose your likelihood of attending future events. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Very likely
	+ Option 2: Somewhat likely
	+ Option 3: Not likely

- **Fan Engagement** (Fan Engagement, text, required/optional): Provide any suggestions or comments on how to improve fan engagement at future events.

- **Improvement Suggestions** (Improvement Suggestions, text, required/optional): Share any ideas or suggestions you have on how to improve future events or enhance the sports league fan experience.
