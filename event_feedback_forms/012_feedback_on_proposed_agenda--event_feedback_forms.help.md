# Feedback On Proposed Agenda - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Feedback On Proposed Agenda" form is intended for stakeholders to provide feedback on the proposed agenda of an event. This feedback will help us improve the agenda to better meet the needs of our attendees. Please ensure that your feedback is constructive and relevant to the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the proposed agenda carefully and gather your thoughts.
2. Fill in the fields below with your feedback.
3. Submit your feedback for our review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agenda Item** (1, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can provide feedback on a specific item on the proposed agenda.
* **Rating (1-5)** (2, number, optional)
	+ This field allows you to rate your overall satisfaction with the current proposed agenda.
* **Proposed Start Time** (3, time, optional)
	+ This field is not relevant as the proposed start time is not included in the form.
* **Proposed End Time** (4, time, optional)
	+ This field is not relevant as the proposed end time is not included in the form.
* **Proposed Start Time** and **Proposed End Time** fields are not used in the form, so you can safely ignore them.

## Tips
* Please provide concrete and specific feedback.
* Keep your feedback concise and to the point.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Agenda Item" field.
