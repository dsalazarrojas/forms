# Chamber Of Commerce Event Interest Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback and interest from Chamber of Commerce members and stakeholders to inform event planning and programming decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Select your membership status and how often you attend Chamber events.
3. Choose how important networking opportunities, business development, and educational content are to you.
4. Select your preferred event topics, format, time, and duration.
5. Indicate how often you would like the Chamber to host events.
6. Let us know if you are interested in sponsoring or co-hosting events.
7. Choose your preferred speaker types.
8. Select targeted member groups you are interested in engaging with.
9. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
10. Indicate if you are okay with the Chamber following up with you about events and receiving event announcements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your Chamber member email address.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you represent a business, enter its name.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current membership level.
	+ Active Member
	+ Associate Member
	+ Investor Member
* **How Often Do You Attend Chamber Events** (`past_event_attendance`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you attend Chamber events.
	+ Regularly
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never Attended
* **Networking Opportunities Priority** (`networking_priority`, select_one, required): Choose the importance of networking for you.
	+ Very Important
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
* **Business Development Priority** (`business_development_priority`, select_one, required): Choose the importance of business growth topics.
	+ Very Important
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
* **Educational Content Priority** (`education_priority`, select_one, required): Choose the importance of learning sessions.
	+ Very Important
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
* **Preferred Event Topics** (`preferred_event_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Select topics of interest to you.
	+ Leadership Development
	+ Marketing and Branding
	+ Technology and Innovation
	+ Finance and Economics
	+ Workforce Development
	+ Sustainability
	+ International Trade
* **Preferred Event Format** (`preferred_event_format`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred event format.
	+ In-Person Only
	+ Virtual Only
	+ Hybrid (In-Person and Virtual)
	+ No Preference
* **Preferred Event Time** (`preferred_event_time`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred time for events.
	+ Breakfast Meeting 7-9am
	+ Lunch Meeting 12-1pm
	+ After-Work 5-6pm
	+ Evening 6-8pm
	+ Weekend
	+ No Preference
* **Preferred Event Duration** (`preferred_event_duration`, select_one, optional): Choose the preferred event duration.
	+ 1 Hour
	+ 1.5 Hours
	+ 2 Hours
	+ 2+ Hours
	+ No Preference
* **Desired Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you would like the Chamber to host events.
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ As Needed
* **Interest in Sponsoring or Co-hosting Events** (`partnership_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate your interest in sponsoring or co-hosting events.
	+ Very Interested
	+ Somewhat Interested
	+ Not Interested
* **Preferred Speaker Types** (`speaker_type_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Select speaker types that interest you.
	+ Industry Experts
	+ Local Business Owners
	+ National Thought Leaders
	+ Government Officials
	+ Non-profit Leaders
* **Interest in Targeted Member Groups** (`special_population_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select targeted member groups you are interested in engaging with.
	+ Young Professionals
	+ Female Entrepreneurs
	+ Minority-Owned Businesses
	+ Women in Business
	+ Tech Leaders
* **Additional Feedback or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional ideas or feedback.
* **Permission to Follow Up** (`follow_up_permission`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are okay with the Chamber following up with you about events.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Add Me to Event Mailing List** (`email_list_opt_in`, select_one, required): Choose if you wish to receive event announcements.
	+ Yes, Add Me
	+ No, Remove Me
	+ Keep Current Status
