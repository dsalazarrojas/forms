# Videomaker Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Videomaker Feedback Survey is a tool for event attendees to provide feedback on their experience with Videomaker's video production services. This feedback will help Videomaker to improve its services and better serve its customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form, which includes questions about video production services, rating, comments, and other relevant information.
2. For each page, fill out the questions accordingly, and make sure to provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Video Production Services
* **Video Production Services** (`video_production_services`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide feedback on the specific video production services you used. Please be as specific as possible and mention any services that were particularly useful or not useful to you.

### Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): This field asks for your overall rating of the service you received. Please rate your experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide more detailed feedback on your experience with Videomaker's services. If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, this is the place to mention them.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is where you can provide your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive more information on future services.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or to receive more information on future services.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional feedback or comments that are not mentioned in the other fields.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): This field asks which tool you were assigned during the event. Please select one of the options provided or type "Other" if none of the options match your experience.

### Event Name
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide the name of the event where you used Videomaker's services.

### Event Date
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): This field is where you can provide the date of the event where you used Videomaker's services.

### Event Time
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): This field is where you can provide the time of the event where you used Videomaker's services.

### Tips
* Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services!
