# Holiday Rush Management Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Holiday Rush Management Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with event-related holiday rush management. This feedback is crucial in helping us improve our processes and ensure that future events are well-managed.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required and optional fields as applicable.
2.  Be as detailed and specific as possible with your feedback.
3.  Ensure that the feedback relates to the event you are providing it for.
4.  Click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of the event that you are providing feedback about. This will help us associate your feedback with the relevant event.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Please provide the date of the event that you are providing feedback about.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Please provide the time of the event that you are providing feedback about.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Please provide the location of the event that you are providing feedback about.
*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please provide your name as the customer providing feedback.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Please provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please provide detailed feedback about your experience with the event.
