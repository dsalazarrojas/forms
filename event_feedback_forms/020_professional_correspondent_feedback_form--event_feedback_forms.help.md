# Professional Correspondent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Correspondent Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from professionals who have collaborated with us on events. This feedback is invaluable for us to understand our strengths and weaknesses and make necessary improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "What was your overall experience?" field. This will give us an idea of your general impression of our service.
2. If you'd like to provide your correspondent's name, please fill out the "Correspondent Name" field. This is optional but helps us keep track of who provided the feedback.
3. Share your thoughts about our service in the "What do you think about our service?" field. This is a text area where you can provide as much detail as you'd like.
4. Rate our service on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "Rate our service" field.
5. If you know the date and time of the event, please provide it in the "Date of the event" and "Time of the event" fields respectively.
6. If you have any additional event details, you can share them in the "Event details" field.
7. Finally, please enter a valid email address in the "Email" field. This will allow us to contact you if we need more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was your overall experience?** (Required: false, Type: text)
	+ This field is where you can provide a brief description of your overall experience with our service. Try to be as concise as possible while still giving us a good idea of your impression.
* **Correspondent Name** (Required: false, Type: text)
	+ This is an optional field where you can provide the name of the correspondent who worked with us on the event.
* **What do you think about our service?** (Required: false, Type: note)
	+ This field is a text area where you can provide your thoughts and opinions about our service.
* **Rate our service** (Required: false, Type: number)
	+ This field is a scale from 1 to 10 where you can rate our service based on your experience.
* **Date of the event** (Required: false, Type: date)
	+ If you know the date of the event, please provide it here.
* **Time of the event** (Required: false, Type: time)
	+ If you know the time of the event, please provide it here.
* **Event details** (Required: false, Type: text)
	+ This field is where you can provide any additional event details that you think are relevant to your feedback.
* **Email** (Required: false, Type: email)
	+ Please enter a valid email address associated with you or your correspondent.
