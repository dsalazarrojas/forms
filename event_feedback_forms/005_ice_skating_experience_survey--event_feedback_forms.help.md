# Ice Skating Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience at an ice skating event. It's meant to help event organizers assess the success and quality of their events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your experience at the event, including whether you enjoyed the event and why.
2. Rate your experience on a scale.
3. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
5. Fill in the event details, such as the event name, location, and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Feedback** (`event_feedback_forms`): 
  • **Yes/No**: Check if you enjoyed your experience at the event.
* **How was your ice skating experience?** (`ice_skating_experience`): 
  • **Option 1, Option 2, Option 3**: Choose the best description of your experience.
* **Rate your experience** (`rating`): 
  • **Number**: Rate your experience on a scale, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Additional Comments** (`comment`): 
  • **Text**: Enter any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`): 
  • **Email address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`): 
  • **Phone number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`): 
  • **Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`): 
  • **Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): 
  • **Text**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): 
  • **Text**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): 
  • **Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`): 
  • **Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`): 
  • **Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`): 
  • **Number**: Enter the duration of the event.
* **Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`): 
  • **Number**: Enter the number of people who attended the event.
* **Number of People You Attended With** (`number_of_people_you_attended_with`): 
  • **Number**: Enter the number of people you attended the event with.
* **Skater Level** (`skater_level`): 
  • **Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert**: Choose your level of skating experience.
* **Equipment Used** (`equipment_used`): 
  • **Skates, Helmets, Elbow Pads, Knee Pads, Gloves**: Choose the equipment you used during the event.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): 
  • **Text**: Enter any additional feedback about your experience.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): 
  • **Public, Private, Virtual**: Choose the type of event.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`): 
  • **Active, Archived, Completed**: Choose the status of the event.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`): 
  • **Text**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event URL** (`event_url`): 
  • **Text**: Enter the URL of the event.
