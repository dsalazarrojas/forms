# Panel Discussion Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on the effectiveness of a panel discussion session. It will help the organizers or moderators of the session to evaluate its overall effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions with as much detail as possible, being truthful to your experience. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, text, required): Please provide a brief summary of your overall impression of the session. What did you think about the panelists' presentation, engagement, and overall flow of the session?
- **Relevance to Interests** (`relevance_to_interests`, number, required): How relevant was the session to your interests?
- **Most Useful Information** (`most_useful_info`, text, required): What was the most useful information or take away that you got from the session? 
- **Panelists' Presentation Skills** (`panelists_presentation_skills`, number, required): How would you rate the panelists' presentation skills (e.g. clarity, organization, confidence)?
- **Q&A Session** (`qa_session`, number, required): How engaging was the Q&A session?
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for the organizers?
- **Helpful Session** (`helpful_session`, checkbox, required): How helpful was the session for you? (Option A: Yes, Option B: No)
- **Attend Future Session** (`attend_future_session`, checkbox, required): Would you attend a future session on the same topic? (Option A: Yes, Option B: No)
- **Liked Most About** (`liked_most_about`, text, required): What did you like most about the session? 
- **Disliked Most About** (`disliked_most_about`, text, required): What did you dislike most about the session? 
- **Future Session Notification** (`future_session_notification`, checkbox, required): Would you like to receive a notification for a future session on the same topic? (Option A: Yes, Option B: No)
- **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, not required): If there's any other information you would like to share with the organizers, please feel free to add it here. 

## Tips
- Please answer the questions truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
- Be specific when answering the questions.
- The organizers will use your feedback to improve future sessions.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them in the "Additional Comments" field. 
- If you would like to receive a notification for a future session on the same topic, select "Yes" in the "Future Session Notification" field.
