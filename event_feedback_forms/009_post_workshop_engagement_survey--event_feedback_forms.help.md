# Post Workshop Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after a workshop, gathering information on their satisfaction, key takeaways, and improvement ideas. This helps us evaluate the workshop's effectiveness and identify areas for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Satisfaction (Page 1)
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop on a scale from 1 to 10.
### Key Takeaways (Page 2)
* **Key Takeaways** (`key_takeaways`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which key takeaways you found most valuable or useful during the workshop. Select all that apply.
### Improvement Ideas (Page 3)
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): Share any ideas you have on how we can improve the workshop for future participants.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or concerns, please do not hesitate to share them with us.
