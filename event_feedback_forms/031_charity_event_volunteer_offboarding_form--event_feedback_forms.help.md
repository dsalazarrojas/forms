# Charity Event Volunteer Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand your experience and satisfaction with our charity events and gather feedback to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details:
	* Your name as it appears on your identification.
	* Your email address associated with your volunteer work.
2. Select the event you participated in:
	* Choose the event from the provided options.
3. Rate your experience:
	* Choose one of the options for overall experience (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Answer some questions about your experience:
	* Was the training adequate for the event?
	* Did you receive sufficient support from coordinators?
	* Was your role clear during the event?
5. Optional:
	* Share a highlight of your experience (Best moment).
	* If you faced any challenges, mention them.
	* Provide any additional feedback about your experience.
6. Finally:
	* Would you volunteer again for a charity event?
	* Would you recommend our charity to others?

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`volunteer_email`, `email`, required): Provide your contact email address.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Select the event you participated in from the provided options.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the event was held.
* **Role Performed** (`role_performed`, `text`, required): Describe your position or role during the event.
* **Total Hours Contributed** (`hours_contributed`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours you contributed during the event.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall experience with the event (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Training Adequacy** (`training_adequacy`, `select_one`, required): Rate the training provided before the event (Very adequate, Adequate, Somewhat adequate, Inadequate, Very inadequate).
* **Support from Coordinators** (`support_received`, `select_one`, required): Rate the support you received from event coordinators (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor).
* **Role Clarity** (`role_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate how clear your role was during the event (Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, Very unclear).
* **Highlight of Your Experience** (`highlight_experience`, `text`, optional): Share a highlight or best moment from your experience.
* **Challenge Faced** (`challenge_faced`, `text`, optional): If you faced any challenges, describe them.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestion_improvement`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for future events.
* **Would You Volunteer Again** (`volunteer_again`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your interest in volunteering again (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Would You Recommend Volunteering** (`recommend_to_others`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you would recommend our charity to others (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Skills Gained** (`skills_gained`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any skills you gained during the event (Communication, Teamwork, Problem solving, Leadership, Time management, Customer service, Event planning, None).
* **Materials Returned** (`materials_returned`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you returned all event materials (Yes all returned, Yes some remaining, No materials issued).
* **Stay Connected for Future Events** (`stay_connected`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your interest in staying connected with our charity for future events (Yes please, No thank you).
