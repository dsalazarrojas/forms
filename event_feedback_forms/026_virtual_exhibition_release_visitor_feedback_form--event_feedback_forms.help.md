# Virtual Exhibition Release Visitor Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors after a virtual exhibition event. Your comments will help us improve future events!

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your information. You will need to provide feedback about your recent virtual exhibition visit. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Name** (text): Tell us the name of the virtual exhibition you visited.
* **Feedback** (text): Please share your thoughts about your experience at the virtual exhibition. This can include what you liked, disliked, or suggestions for improvement.
* **Satisfaction** (number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the virtual exhibition on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rating** (number): Rate your satisfaction with the following aspects of the event on a scale of 1-10:
	+ Exhibition content
	+ Navigation
	+ Overall organization
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information or would like to follow-up on your feedback.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed. (Optional)
* **Comments** (text): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please let us know!
* **Event Date** (date): Tell us the date of the virtual exhibition you visited.
* **Event Time** (time): Tell us the time of the virtual exhibition you visited.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form only once per visit, to avoid duplication of effort and ensure your feedback is counted.
* Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve future events.
