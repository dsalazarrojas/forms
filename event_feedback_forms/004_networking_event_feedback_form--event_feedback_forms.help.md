# Networking Event Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a networking event. It will help event organizers evaluate the success of the event and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your feedback and information about the event.
2. Provide as much detail as you can in the optional fields to help event organizers better understand your experience.
3. Submit your feedback by filling out the form submit field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Please enter the name of the networking event you attended.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or ID.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Share your thoughts and comments about the event. Describe what worked well and what didn't.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your professional email address to help event organizers contact you with questions or follow-up information.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department you belong to from the available options (Accounting, Engineering, Sales, Operations, Other).
* **Frequency of Attending** (`frequency_of_attending`): Choose how often you attend networking events (Daily, Monthly, Rarely, Never).
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`): Enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Enter the type of the event.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`): Select the status of the event (In progress, Completed, Cancelled, Upcoming).
* **Attendees** (`attendees`): Indicate if you attended the event (Yes, No).
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate your overall experience with the event (a number between 1 and 5).
* **Comment** (`comment`): Leave any additional comments or suggestions about the event.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`): Enter a unique identifier for the event (e.g., event#123).
* **Event Category** (`event_category`): Enter the category of the event (e.g., Conference, Workshop, Training).
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): Briefly describe the event.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`): Enter your confirmation that you submitted the form.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`): Enter the status of your form submission.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`): Enter any comments or questions about the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`): Enter the version of the form you used.
 

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but providing as much information as possible will help event organizers better evaluate and improve their events.
