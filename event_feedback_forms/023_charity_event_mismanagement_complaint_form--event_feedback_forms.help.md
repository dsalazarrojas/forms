# Charity Event Mismanagement Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect information about mismanagement issues or concerns that occurred at charity events managed by our organization. Please fill out this form to help us understand the details of the issue and take corrective action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the complainant's name, email, and phone number (if available).
2. Identify the event that was affected by the issue.
3. Choose the category of the complaint.
4. Provide a detailed description of what happened.
5. If applicable, specify the individuals involved and whether there were witnesses.
6. Indicate if you have evidence to support your complaint.
7. Check if you have previously complained about the same issue to our organization or authorities.
8. Describe how this issue impacted you personally.
9. Specify the resolution you are seeking.
10. Indicate the urgency level of the issue.
11. Choose whether you wish to remain anonymous.
12. Finally, permit us to contact you for further information, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`complainant_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name.
* **Email Address** (`complainant_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`complainant_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please provide it.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Specify the name of the charity event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Provide the date the event occurred.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Indicate the location of the event.
* **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Financial mismanagement, Volunteer mistreatment, Safety concerns, False advertising, Discrimination or harassment, Poor organization, Misuse of funds, Other.
* **Detailed Description** (`complaint_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed account of what happened.
* **Individuals Involved** (`individuals_involved`, text, optional): List the names or roles of those involved.
* **Witnesses** (`witnesses`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: True, False.
* **Witness Contact Information** (`witness_contacts`, text, optional): If available, provide witness contact information.
* **Evidence Available** (`evidence_available`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Yes I will provide, Yes but cannot share, No evidence.
* **Previous Complaints** (`previous_complaints`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Yes to the organization, Yes to authorities, No this is first.
* **Impact Description** (`impact_description`, text, required): Describe how this issue impacted you personally.
* **Desired Resolution** (`desired_resolution`, text, required): Specify the outcome you seek.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Not urgent, Somewhat urgent, Urgent, Very urgent.
* **Anonymous Option** (`anonymous_option`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Yes keep anonymous, No identity can be shared.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: True, False.
