# Community Program Participant Satisfaction Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from community program participants about their experience and satisfaction with the program. This feedback will help us understand what we're doing well and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Information** (page 1) with your details.
2. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** (pages 2-3).
3. If you have an **Email Address**, please enter it (page 4). This is optional.
4. If you have a **Participant ID**, please enter it (page 5). This is optional.
5. On page 6, provide some background information about your participation in the program.
6. Enter the **Program Name** (page 7) and select the correct date of your **Session Date** (page 8).
7. For **Total Sessions Attended** (page 9), enter the number of sessions you've attended.
8. On page 10, **Satisfaction Ratings**, rate your experience with the provided options.
9. Rate your experience with the **Overall Satisfaction** (page 11), **Quality of Program Content** (page 12), **Facilitator Effectiveness** (page 13), **Venue Quality** (page 14), **Quality of Materials Provided** (page 15), **Program Pacing** (page 16), **Difficulty Level** (page 17), and **How Engaged Did You Feel** (page 18).
10. For **Learning Outcomes** (page 19), describe what you gained from the program.
11. On page 20, rate whether the **Learning Objectives** were met.
12. If you have any **Key Takeaways**, describe them (page 21).
13. On page 22, select the **Skills Improved** you gained from the program.
14. Describe how you plan to **Apply What You Learned** (page 23).
15. If you have any **Recommendations** for the program, please describe them (page 24).
16. If there's anything that could be **Improved**, describe it (page 25).
17. For **Topics for Future Sessions**, suggest any topics you'd like to see in future sessions (page 26).
18. On page 27, rate whether you would **Recommend This Program**.
19. On page 28, rate whether you will **Continue in This Program**.
20. If you need **Additional Support**, select the option (page 29).
21. If you need **Support**, describe what you need (page 30).
22. Finally, if you have any **Additional Comments**, please share them (page 31).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (page 1)**: This is the first page of the form where you'll enter your details. This page is for background information about your participation.
* **First Name (page 2)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (page 3)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address (page 4)**: If you have an email address, please enter it. This is optional.
* **Participant ID (page 5)**: If you have a participant ID, please enter it. This is optional.
* **Program Information (page 6)**: Provide some background information about your participation in the program.
* **Program Name (page 7)**: Enter the name of the program you're participating in.
* **Session Date (page 8)**: Select the date of the session you attended.
* **Total Sessions Attended (page 9)**: Enter the number of sessions you've attended.
* **Satisfaction Ratings (page 10)**: Rate your experience with the provided options.
* **Overall Satisfaction (page 11)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
* **Quality of Program Content (page 12)**: Rate the quality of the program content.
* **Facilitator Effectiveness (page 13)**: Rate the effectiveness of the facilitator.
* **Venue Quality (page 14)**: Rate the quality of the venue (if applicable).
* **Quality of Materials Provided (page 15)**: Rate the quality of the materials provided.
* **Program Pacing (page 16)**: Rate the pace of the program.
* **Difficulty Level (page 17)**: Rate the difficulty level of the program.
* **How Engaged Did You Feel (page 18)**: Rate how engaged you felt during the program.
* **Learning Outcomes (page 19)**: Describe what you gained from the program.
* **Learning Objectives (page 20)**: Rate whether the learning objectives were met.
* **Key Takeaways (page 21)**: Describe any key takeaways you gained from the program.
* **Skills Improved (page 22)**: Select the skills you improved during the program.
* **Apply What You Learned (page 23)**: Describe how you plan to apply what you learned.
* **Recommendations (page 24)**: If you have any recommendations, describe them.
* **Improvements (page 25)**: If there's anything that could be improved, describe it.
* **Topics for Future Sessions (page 26)**: Suggest any topics you'd like to see in future sessions.
* **Recommend This Program (page 27)**: Rate whether you would recommend this program.
* **Continue in This Program (page 28)**: Rate whether you will continue in this program.
* **Additional Support (page 29)**: Select whether you need additional support.
* **Support Needed (page 30)**: If you need support, describe what you need.
* **Additional Comments (page 31)**: Share any additional comments or feedback.

Note: This help guide is intended to be a general guide and may not cover every possible scenario. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out.
