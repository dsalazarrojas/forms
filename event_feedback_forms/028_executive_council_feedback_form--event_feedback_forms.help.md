# Executive Council Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from Executive Council members about their experiences and perceptions of the council's meetings, performance, and potential suggestions for improvement. Your input is valuable in helping us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a clear and concise manner, providing as much detail as you feel is necessary.
2. You can submit one or multiple pages of feedback, depending on your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Feedback** (<meeting_feedback>): A text field where you can describe your overall impression of the recent meeting.
* **Performance of the Council** (<council_performance>): Provide specific examples or instances where you felt the council was effective or ineffective.
* **Suggestions** (<suggestions>): Share any suggestions you have for the council's improvement.
* **Contact** (<contact>): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you regarding your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, using real-life examples to illustrate your points.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, feel free to attach them to the form or email them to us separately.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
