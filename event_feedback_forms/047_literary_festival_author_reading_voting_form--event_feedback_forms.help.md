# literary_festival_author_reading_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees about the authors who read at the literary festival. It aims to gather information on their experiences, opinions, and ratings of the authors' performances.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section carefully, making sure to provide accurate information.
2. For the "Festival Name" field, enter the name of the festival you attended.
3. Enter the author's name in the "Author Name" field.
4. Rate the authors' performance using the "Rating" field with 1-5 stars or 10-100 points.
5. Provide any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
6. If you have a favorite book from the festival, select it from the "Favorite Book" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if provided).
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if provided).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Name** (`festival_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the festival where you attended the author's reading.
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the author who read at the festival.
* **Rating (1-5)** (`rating_1_to_5`, number, required): Rate the author's performance using 1-5 stars.
* **Rating (10-100)** (`rating_10_to_100`, number, required): Rate the author's performance using 10-100 points.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Provide any comments or feedback about the author's performance.
* **Favorite Book** (`favorite_book`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite book from the festival's list of options.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address (if provided).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if provided).
