# Conference Keynote Speaker Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on keynote speakers at conferences. It will help us understand which speakers were well-received, what aspects of their presentation were most engaging, and what we can improve for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by scrolling through the form and reading each field's label and hint.
2. Fill in all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
3. Select your answers for each field based on your experience and thoughts.
4. If you're unsure about a field, refer to the hint for guidance.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Keynote Speaker Voting**: This is the section where you can vote for your favorite keynote speaker.
* **Your Name**: Please provide your name for our records. This is optional but helps us confirm your vote.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for confirmation purposes. This is optional but helps us keep track of your vote.
* **Conference Name**: Enter the name of the conference you attended.
* **Conference Date**: Provide the date of the conference you attended.
* **Attendee Type**: Select your role at the conference (e.g., General Attendee, VIP Attendee, etc.).
* **Favorite Keynote Speaker**: Select the keynote speaker you enjoyed the most.
* **Speaker Name**: If you remember the speaker's name, enter it here (this is optional).
* **Speaker Rating**: Rate the speaker's performance (Outstanding, Excellent, etc.).
* **Content Quality**: Rate the quality of the speaker's content (Outstanding, Excellent, etc.).
* **Presentation Style**: Rate the speaker's presentation style (Outstanding, Excellent, etc.).
* **Engagement Level**: Rate the speaker's engagement level (Very Engaging, Engaging, etc.).
* **Relevance Rating**: Rate how relevant the speaker's content was to your work (Very Relevant, Relevant, etc.).
* **Key Takeaways**: Share any key takeaways or insights you gained from the speaker's presentation (optional).
* **Would Recommend Speaker**: Indicate whether you would recommend the speaker for future events.
* **Speaker Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the speaker (optional).
* **What Worked Well**: Share any strengths or aspects of the speaker's performance that went well (optional).
* **Areas for Improvement**: Share any suggestions or areas for improvement for future speakers (optional).
* **Topics of Interest**: Share any topics you'd like to hear more about in future speakers (optional).
* **Future Speaker Suggestions**: Suggest any future speakers you think would be a good fit for the conference (optional).
* **Format Preference**: Indicate your preferred format for future keynote presentations (In Person, Virtual, Hybrid, etc.).
* **Preferred Session Length**: Select the ideal duration for future keynote sessions (30 minutes, 45 minutes, etc.).
* **Interaction Preference**: Indicate your preferred style for future Q and A sessions (Presentation Only, Short Q and A, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional feedback or comments you'd like to provide (optional).
* **Vote Confirmation**: Review and submit your vote carefully to ensure everything is correct.
