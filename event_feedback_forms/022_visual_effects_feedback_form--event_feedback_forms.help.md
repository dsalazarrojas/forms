# Visual Effects Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visual Effects Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback on visual effects used in events and projects. This feedback is crucial for improving the visual effects and making future events and projects more engaging and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required information, which is marked as required (indicated by *Required* in the field label).
2. Choose the correct feedback type by selecting one of the available options (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
3. Provide detailed feedback on the visual effects used in the event or project.
4. Suggest improvements or changes for the visual effects.
5. Type your comments about the event or project.
6. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event** (`event_id`, Number, *Required*): Enter the ID of the event for which you are providing feedback.
* **Project** (`project_id`, Number, *Required*): Enter the ID of the project for which you are providing feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, *Required*): Enter a valid email address to receive any follow-up emails about your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_type`, Select One, *Required*): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Positive: The visual effects were effective and met your expectations.
	+ Negative: The visual effects were not effective and did not meet your expectations.
	+ Neutral: The visual effects were average and neither met nor missed your expectations.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, Note, *Optional*): Provide any suggestions or ideas for improving the visual effects.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, *Optional*): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the event or project.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text, *Required*): Type in your name or a unique identifier to submit your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Note, *Optional*): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the visual effects.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, *Optional*): Enter a valid email address to receive any follow-up emails about your feedback.
* **Event** (`event_id`, Number, *Optional*): Enter the ID of the event for which you are providing feedback (if you don't know the ID, you can leave this field blank).
