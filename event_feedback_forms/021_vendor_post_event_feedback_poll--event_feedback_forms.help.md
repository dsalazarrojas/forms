# Vendor Post Event Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by vendors to provide feedback on their experience with our events. It is essential to fill this form to help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "rate_our_event" field and choose the rating that best reflects your overall satisfaction with the event.
2. In the "satisfaction" field, choose your level of satisfaction with the event.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, type them in the "vendor_comments" field.
4. Select "will_recommend" field to indicate whether or not you would recommend our services to other vendors.
5. Select "will_attend_next_event" field to indicate whether or not you plan to attend our next event.
6. If you want to share your email address, type it in the "email" field.
7. If you want to share your phone number, type it in the "contact_number" field.
8. If you want to share your name, type it in the "vendor_name" field.
9. If you want to share your email address, type it in the "vendor_email" field.
10. If you want to share your phone number, type it in the "vendor_phone" field.
11. If you want to share your website, type it in the "vendor_website" field.
12. If you want to share the venue where the event took place, type it in the "venue" field.
13. If you want to share the date and time of the event, type it in the "date" and "time" fields respectively.
14. If you want to share the location of the event, type it in the "location" field.
15. If you want to share the date of the event, type it in the "event_date" field.
16. If you want to share the time of the event, type it in the "event_time" field.
17. If you want to share the location of the event, type it in the "event_location" field.
18. If you want to share the type of the event, type it in the "event_type" field.
19. If you want to share the topic of the event, type it in the "event_topic" field.
20. If you want to share the length of the event, type it in the "event_length" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_title**: Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **feedback_rating**: Choose your rating from 1 to 5 to reflect your overall satisfaction with the event.
* **satisfaction**: Choose your level of satisfaction with the event.
* **vendor_comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the event.
* **will_recommend**: Indicate if you would recommend our services to other vendors.
* **will_attend_next_event**: Indicate if you plan to attend our next event.
* **email**: If you want to share your email address with us, type it here.
* **contact_number**: If you want to share your phone number with us, type it here.
* **vendor_name**: Enter your name as the vendor.
* **vendor_email**: If you want to share your email address with us, type it here.
* **vendor_phone**: If you want to share your phone number with us, type it here.
* **vendor_website**: If you want to share your website, type it here.
* **venue**: Enter the venue where the event took place.
* **date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **event_date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **event_time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **event_location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **event_type**: Enter the type of the event.
* **event_topic**: Enter the topic of the event.
* **event_length**: Enter the length of the event.

## Tips
1. Please fill out the form thoroughly and honestly, as it will help us improve our services.
2. You can submit the form anonymously, if you prefer.
3. If you have any issues submitting the form, contact us for assistance.
4. We value your feedback and will use it to improve our events and services.
