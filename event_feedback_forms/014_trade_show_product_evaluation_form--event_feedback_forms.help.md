# Trade Show Product Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Show Product Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and information from customers after a trade show, helping us to improve our products and services for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the **Event Type** that best describes the trade show where you interacted with our product.
2. Choose the **Product Type** that best describes the product you interacted with.
3. Describe the **Quality of Display** of our product at the trade show.
4. Share your **Overall Experience** with our product at the trade show.
5. Provide any **Additional Feedback** you may have about our product or trade show experience.
6. Answer **Yes** or **No** if you were able to meet the **Product Team** during the trade show.
7. If you met the **Product Team**, provide their names in the **Product Team Members** field.
8. Enter the name of the **Trade Show** where you interacted with our product.
9. Enter the **Date** of the trade show.
10. Enter the **Time** of the trade show.
11. Enter the **Location** of the trade show.
12. Enter your **Contact Information**, including your name, email, and phone number.
13. Enter any other **Additional Comments** about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type** (`event_type`, required): Select the type of event where you interacted with our product (e.g., Trade Show).
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, required): Choose the type of product you interacted with (e.g., Product 1).
* **Quality of Display** (`quality_of_display`, required): Describe the quality of display of our product at the trade show (e.g., well-presented, poorly presented).
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, required): Share your overall experience with our product at the trade show (e.g., positive, negative).
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, required): Provide any additional feedback you may have about our product or trade show experience.
* **Product Team** (`page_2_1`, optional): Answer **Yes** or **No** if you were able to meet the product team during the trade show.
* **Product Team Members** (`page_2_2`, required): If you met the product team, provide their names in this field.
* **Trade Show** (`page_2_3`, required): Enter the name of the trade show where you interacted with our product.
* **Date** (`page_2_4`, required): Enter the date of the trade show.
* **Time** (`page_2_5`, required): Enter the time of the trade show.
* **Location** (`page_2_6`, required): Enter the location of the trade show.
* **Contact** (`page_2_7`, required): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Email** (`page_2_8`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`page_2_9`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Other Comments** (`page_2_10`, required): Enter any other additional comments about your experience.
