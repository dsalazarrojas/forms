# Knowledge Lab Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants in the Knowledge Lab sessions. It helps us understand their session attendance, overall satisfaction, and improvement suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the sessions you attended from the list provided.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the experience on a scale of 1-10.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the text box.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Attendance**: This field allows you to select all the sessions you attended from the list provided. Please choose all the sessions that apply to you.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the experience on a scale of 1-10. This will help us understand how we can improve our services.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for us to improve our services, please type them in this field.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or updates.
