## B2B Launch Event Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of a B2B launch event. It's intended to assess their overall experience, satisfaction, and areas for improvement. The feedback form is meant to help event organizers and stakeholders understand attendees' needs and preferences for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill out each question thoroughly and honestly**. Your feedback is essential in helping us improve future events.
2.  **Be specific with your answers**. Try to provide concrete reasons or examples to support your ratings or comments.
3.  **Use your real name and email address** so we can follow up with you and keep you informed about our future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Full Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your full name, which will be displayed on our internal feedback report.
* **2. Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the name of the company you represent.
* **3. Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): This is your current job title or position within your organization.
* **4. Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your business email for follow-up and any updates we might send you about future events.
* **5. Event Date Attended** (`event_date`, `date`, required/optional): Indicate which session or event you attended.
* **6. Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event, using the scale provided.
* **7. Venue Rating** (`venue_rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): Evaluate the event venue using the scale provided.
* **8. Content Quality** (`content_quality`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the quality of the presentation content using the scale provided.
* **9. Speaker Performance** (`speaker_rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): Assess the performance and quality of the speakers and presentations using the scale provided.
* **10. Networking Opportunities** (`networking_opportunities`, `select_one`, required/optional): Evaluate the networking opportunities provided at the event using the scale provided.
* **11. Event Organization** (`event_organization`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the event coordination and logistics using the scale provided.
* **12. Most Valuable Session** (`most_valuable_session`, `text`, optional/optional): If there's a session that stood out to you, please describe it here.
* **13. Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, optional/optional): Share any areas where we could improve for future events.
* **14. Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_recommend`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate how likely you are to recommend this event to colleagues and peers.
* **15. Interest in Future Events** (`future_event_interest`, `select_one`, required/optional): Let us know if you'd attend future events from us and how likely that might be.
* **16. Topics of Interest for Future Events** (`topics_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, optional/optional): Select from the list the topics you'd be interested in seeing at future events.
* **17. Additional Comments** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional/optional): If there's anything else you'd like to share or suggest, please use this field.
