# Epic Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Epic Feedback Survey form is designed to collect feedback from event attendees. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible to help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you have all the necessary information before beginning.
2. Answer each question carefully, taking into account your overall experience during the event.
3. Be honest and provide constructive feedback where possible.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "What did we get right" and "What did we get wrong" fields to provide more detailed feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the event name** (`event_feedback_survey_1`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture the name of the event you attended. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **How did you feel during the event** (`event_feedback_survey_2`, text, optional): This field is a chance for you to share your overall emotions and experiences during the event. Describe your feelings and any notable moments that stood out.
* **Rate the food and beverage experience** (`event_feedback_survey_3`, number, optional): This question is on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is poor and 5 is excellent. Rate your experience with the food and beverages provided.
* **Was the venue accessible** (`event_feedback_survey_4`, text, optional): This field is to assess the accessibility of the venue for you. Describe any issues or concerns you had with accessing the venue.
* **How likely are you to attend again** (`event_feedback_survey_5`, select_one, required): This question asks about your likelihood of attending the event again. Choose from the options: Not likely at all, Somewhat likely, Very Likely, or Extremely Likely.
* **Would you recommend this event** (`event_feedback_survey_6`, select_multiple, required): This question is about your recommendation of the event to others. Choose all that apply from the options: Not recommended, Somewhat recommended, Very recommended, or Extremely recommended.
* **What did we get right** (`event_feedback_survey_7`, text, optional): Share any positive experiences or moments that you enjoyed during the event. This will help us know what we're doing well.
* **What did we get wrong** (`event_feedback_survey_8`, text, optional): This field is for sharing any negative experiences or areas of improvement. Be constructive and provide specific feedback to help us improve.
