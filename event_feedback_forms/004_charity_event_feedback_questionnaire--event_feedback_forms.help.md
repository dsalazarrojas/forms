This guide will help users navigate the Charity Event Feedback Questionnaire. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees about their experience at a charity event. This feedback is crucial for the organizers to understand what worked well and what didn't, allowing them to improve future events. By completing this form, you will be providing valuable insights that will help make a positive impact on the charity and its beneficiaries.

Before you begin, please note that some fields are required, marked as "required: true" in the form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by "required: true").
3. You can save your progress and come back later if needed.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required: true): This is your complete name, as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Please provide your email address for follow-up communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **Name of Event Attended** (`event_name`, `text`, required: true): Please specify which charity event you attended.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: true): When did you attend the event?
* **Was This Your First Time at This Event** (`first_time_attendance`, `select_one`, required: true): Have you attended this event before?
* **Overall Event Organization** (`event_organization`, `select_one`, required: true): How well was the event organized?
* **Check-in and Registration Process** (`registration_process`, `select_one`, required: true): Was the check-in and registration process smooth?
* **Venue Quality and Accessibility** (`venue_quality`, `select_one`, required: true): How would you rate the venue's quality and accessibility?
* **Food and Beverage Quality** (`food_beverages`, `select_one`, required: false): How were the refreshments?
* **Understanding of Charity Mission** (`mission_understanding`, `select_one`, required: true): How clear was the charity's mission to you?
* **Likelihood to Make a Donation** (`donation_likely`, `select_one`, required: true): How likely are you to make a donation?
* **Amount You Plan to Donate** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required: false): If you plan to donate, please estimate the amount.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Event** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): How satisfied are you with the event?
* **Suggestions for Future Events** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Any ideas on how we can improve future events?
* **Would You Recommend This Event to Others** (`recommend_to_others`, `select_one`, required: true): Will you recommend this event to your friends and family?

Please complete the form according to your experience and feedback, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification.
