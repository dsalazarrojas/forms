# Artisan Market Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from visitors to the Artisan Market on their overall experience, including the quality of artisan products, vendor hospitality, and market facilities. The feedback collected will help us understand areas of improvement and provide better services to our visitors. Please note that your feedback is anonymous and will not be used for any direct sales or marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the date of your visit to the market.
2.  Choose how you visited the market (as a vendor, shopper, both, or event staff).
3.  Indicate if you visited the market for the first time or not.
4.  Select the market you visited.
5.  Rate your overall experience at the market (1-10).
6.  Evaluate the quality of artisan products.
7.  Rate the variety of products available.
8.  Select the price level of artisan products.
9.  Rate the vendor's hospitality and customer service.
10. Rate the market's facilities and layout.
11. Rate the overall cleanliness of the market.
12. Evaluate the accessibility and parking facilities.
13. Indicate if you made any purchases.
14. Enter the approximate amount of your purchase (if applicable).
15. Suggest any improvements for the market.
16. Select your likelihood to return to the market.
17. Choose if you would recommend the market to others.
18. If you'd like to stay updated about the market, provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Market Visit**: 
    Date of Market Visit (`visit_date`, date, required): The date of your visit to the market.
* **Visitor Type**: 
    Visitor Type (`visitor_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you visited the market (as a vendor, shopper, both, or event staff).
* **First Time Visiting This Market**: 
    First Time Visiting This Market (`first_time_visitor`, select_one, required): Indicate if you visited the market for the first time or not.
* **Which Market Did You Attend?**:
    Which Market Did You Attend (`market_name`, text, required): Select the market you visited.
* **Rate Overall Market Experience - 1-10**:
    Rate Overall Market Experience - 1-10 (`overall_experience`, number, required): Rate your overall experience at the market (1-10).
* **Quality of Artisan Products**:
    Quality of Artisan Products (`vendor_quality`, select_one, required): Evaluate the quality of artisan products.
* **Variety of Products Available**:
    Variety of Products Available (`product_variety`, select_one, required): Choose how many varieties of products were available.
* **Product Pricing**:
    Product Pricing (`pricing`, select_one, required): Select the price level of artisan products.
* **Vendor Hospitality and Customer Service**:
    Vendor Hospitality and Customer Service (`vendor_hospitality`, select_one, required): Rate the vendor's hospitality and customer service.
* **Facilities and Layout**:
    Facilities and Layout (`facilities`, select_one, required): Rate the market's facilities and layout.
* **Overall Cleanliness**:
    Overall Cleanliness (`cleanliness`, select_one, required): Rate the overall cleanliness of the market.
* **Parking and Accessibility**:
    Parking and Accessibility (`parking_accessibility`, select_one, required): Evaluate the accessibility and parking facilities.
* **Did You Make Any Purchases Today?**:
    Did You Make Any Purchases Today (`purchases_made`, select_one, required): Indicate if you made any purchases.
* **Approximate Purchase Amount**:
    Approximate Purchase Amount (`purchase_amount`, text, required): Enter the approximate amount of your purchase (if applicable).
* **Suggested Improvements**:
    Suggested Improvements (`improvements`, text, required): Suggest any improvements for the market.
* **Likelihood to Return to This Market**:
    Likelihood to Return to This Market (`return_likelihood`, select_one, required): Select your likelihood to return to the market.
* **Would You Recommend to Others?**:
    Would You Recommend to Others (`recommend_others`, select_one, required): Choose if you would recommend the market to others.
* **Email Address for Market Updates**:
    Email Address for Market Updates (`email_for_newsletter`, email, required): If you'd like to stay updated about the market, provide your email address.
