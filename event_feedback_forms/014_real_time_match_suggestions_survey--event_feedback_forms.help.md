# Real Time Match Suggestions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Time Match Suggestions Survey is a form used to collect feedback on events, aiming to improve the overall experience and provide better matches. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us understand your thoughts and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Real Time Match Suggestions Survey, follow these steps:
1. Select the category that best describes the event you're providing feedback on.
2. Provide a brief description of the event.
3. Choose the title of the event.
4. Select your preference from the list of options.
5. Add any additional notes if you'd like.
6. Enter your email address (optional).
7. Enter your phone number (optional).
8. Provide your name and any other information related to the user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select one of the provided options that best describes the event you're providing feedback on.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Title**: Enter the title of the event.
* **Preferences**: Choose the option that best represents your preference.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional) if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional) if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Form ID**: This field is not currently relevant, please do not fill it out.
* **User Preferences**: This field is not currently relevant, please do not fill it out.
