# Yoga Class Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with our yoga classes. Your input helps us improve the quality and effectiveness of our classes, so we can provide the best experience for you and all our participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions accurately and honestly.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Feedback" field.
