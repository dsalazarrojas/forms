# Caregiver Support Workshop Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from caregivers who attended a workshop on their experience, satisfaction, and overall impression of the event. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better support in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the sections**: The form is divided into several sections, including Workshop Details, Participant Feedback, Facilitator Evaluation, Logistics and Suggestions, and Additional Comments. Please answer the questions in each section based on your experience.
2. **Fill out the required fields**: Make sure to complete all the fields marked as "required" in the form. These fields are essential for us to understand your feedback.
3. **Be specific and honest**: Provide detailed and honest answers to each question. This will help us improve our services and provide better support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Workshop Details
* **Workshop Title**: Enter the full name of the workshop you attended.
* **Date of Workshop**: Select the date of the workshop you attended.
* **Facilitator Name**: Enter the name of the main instructor or facilitator.
* **Section Participant Feedback**
	+ **How satisfied were you with the workshop overall?**: Rate your overall satisfaction from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
	+ **Content Relevance**: Rate how relevant the workshop content was to your role as a caregiver, from 1 (not relevant) to 5 (very relevant).
	+ **Presentation Clarity**: Rate how clear the presentation of information was, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Most Useful Topic**: Specify one main takeaway or most useful topic covered during the workshop.
* **Least Useful Topic**: (Optional) Specify one topic that you found the least useful or most challenging to understand.
* **Section Facilitator Evaluation**
	+ **Rate the facilitator's knowledge of the topic**: Rate the facilitator's knowledge of the topic, from 1 (not enough) to 5 (very good).
	+ **Engagement Level**: Rate the facilitator's ability to engage participants, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
	+ **Question Handling**: Rate how well the facilitator handled questions, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Logistics and Suggestions**
	+ **Quality of Venue or Online Platform**: Rate the quality of the venue or online platform, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
	+ **Time Allocation**: Rate whether the time allocated for the workshop was sufficient, select from Too Short, Just Right, or Too Long.
	+ **Future Topics**: Suggest topics you'd like to see in future workshops.
	+ **Improve Suggestion**: Provide one suggestion on how we could improve the workshop.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

### Tips

* Please be specific and provide detailed answers to each question.
* If you need help or clarification, you can reach out to us for assistance.
* Your feedback is crucial for us to improve our services and provide better support.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
