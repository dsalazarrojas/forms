# First Time Attendee Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from first-time attendees of our events. The information collected will help us understand your experience and preferences, allowing us to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields, select your answers from the provided options, and submit your response. Please ensure that all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \* or marked as "required" in the field label) are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. event_details
* **event_details** (text): Please provide a brief description of the event you attended.

### 3. event_date
* **event_date** (date): Enter the date of the event you attended. This will help us track the dates of previous events.

### 4. session_id
* **session_id** (number): Enter the session ID or code provided by the event organizer. This helps us track which sessions were attended.

### 5. name
* **name** (text): Enter your name so we can address our response to you.

### 6. email
* **email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

### 7. department
* **department** (select_one): Indicate whether you were attending as part of a department or team.

### 8. role
* **role** (select_multiple): Select all roles that apply to describe your position or role during the event.

### 9. comments
* **comments** (text): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the event.

### 10. additional_comments
* **additional_comments** (text): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please share them here.

### 11. attended_before
* **attended_before** (select_multiple): Indicate whether you had attended one of our events before.

### 12. recommend
* **recommend** (select_one): Let us know if you would recommend our event to others.

### 13. rating
* **rating** (number): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5.

### 14. attended_before_survey
* **attended_before_survey** (select_one): If you have attended one of our surveys before, select "Yes".

### 15. session_length
* **session_length** (number): Rate the length of the session, from 1 to 5.

### 16. first_time_attended
* **first_time_attended** (select_one): Indicate whether this was your first time attending one of our events.

### 17. session_type
* **session_type** (select_multiple): Select all session types that apply to your experience.

### 18. feedback
* **feedback** (text): Share any feedback or suggestions you have about the event.

### 19. rating2
* **rating2** (number): Rate the relevance of the material presented, from 1 to 5.

### 20. rating3
* **rating3** (number): Rate the quality of the presenters, from 1 to 5.

### 21. rating4
* **rating4** (number): Rate the overall engagement of the audience, from 1 to 5.

### 22. rating5
* **rating5** (number): Rate the overall organization of the event, from 1 to 5.

### 23. rating6
* **rating6** (number): Rate the quality of the materials or resources provided, from 1 to 5.

### 24. rating7
* **rating7** (number): Rate the accessibility of the event space, from 1 to 5.

### 25. submit
* **submit** (select_one): Confirm that you have completed and submitted the survey.

Please submit your response to help us improve our events!
