# netball-finals-venue-feedback-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The netball-finals-venue-feedback-survey form is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions about the netball finals venue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you're providing feedback for a specific event.
2. Fill in the required fields (venue-location and email) if applicable.
3. Provide your honest feedback about your experience at the netball finals venue.
4. Choose your level of overall satisfaction and select any applicable officials that were involved in the event.
5. Add any additional comments about your experience.
6. Enter your email address for us to get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Venue Feedback Page** (1): Enter a brief description of your experience at the netball finals venue.
* **Venue Location** (2): Enter the name of the venue where the event took place.
* **Event Day** (3): Enter the date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Venue Contact** (4): Enter the contact information of the venue management.
* **Netball Officials** (5): Select any officials that were involved in the event that you would like to acknowledge for their service. This may include netball officials or umpires.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (6): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event by selecting from the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments** (7): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience. You can write as much or as little as you like.
* **Email** (8): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you for further clarification or follow-up.
* **Phone** (9): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for additional information.
