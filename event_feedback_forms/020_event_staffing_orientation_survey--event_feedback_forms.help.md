# Event Staffing Orientation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Staffing Orientation Survey is designed to gather feedback from event staff about their experiences and perceptions of the event they recently participated in. This survey is intended to be used to identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for future event planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and answers carefully.
2. Choose the options that best fit your experiences and perceptions.
3. Provide honest and constructive feedback.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, as they are all required fields.
5. Double-check your form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Staffing Survey** (`event_staffing_orientation_survey_page_1`, Select Multiple, **Required**):
	+ This section asks how you rate your overall experience with the event's staffing.
	+ Choose from the options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Event Feedback** (`event_staffing_orientation_survey_page_2`, Text, **Optional**):
	+ Write a brief description of your feedback or suggestions for future events.
* **Event Feedback 2** (`event_staffing_orientation_survey_page_3`, Text, **Optional**):
	+ Write a further description or elaboration on your feedback or suggestions.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and honest as possible in your feedback.
- If you have any concerns or suggestions, feel free to share them in the text fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
