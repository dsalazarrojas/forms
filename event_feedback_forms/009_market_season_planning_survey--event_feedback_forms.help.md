# Market Season Planning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a tool to gather feedback from participants about their perceptions and expectations for the upcoming market season. It includes questions to capture your thoughts on the season, the products you think are relevant, and any potential improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the context of the form.
2. Answer the "What do you think?" question with your honest thoughts about the upcoming market season.
3. Select one of the four seasons (Spring, Summer, Fall, or Winter) that you think is the most suitable for the market season.
4. Choose the products that you think are relevant for the market season.
5. Optionally, provide any improvements you can think of for the products.
6. Add any comments or suggestions you have about the market season.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, not required): This is the starting point for the form, providing background information about the market season planning survey.
* **What do you think?** (`2`, `text`, required): Share your honest thoughts and opinions about the upcoming market season. Be as specific as possible.
* **Seasons** (`3`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the season that you think is the most suitable for the market season. Select one of the four options: Spring, Summer, Fall, or Winter.
* **Products** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the products that you think are relevant for the market season. You can choose multiple products. Options: A, B, C.
* **Improvements** (`5`, `text`, not required): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvements, feel free to share them.
* **Comments** (`6`, `text`, not required): Add any comments or additional information you'd like to share about the market season.

## Tips
- Be honest and specific in your answers.
- Choose the most suitable season and products that you think will be relevant for the market season.
- If you have any suggestions or improvements, feel free to share them.
