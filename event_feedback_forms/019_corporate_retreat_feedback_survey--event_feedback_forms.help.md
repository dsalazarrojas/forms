# Corporate Retreat Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants about our corporate retreat. This feedback will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve for future events. Your input is valuable to us and will help shape the experience for our future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply from the select multiple question.
3. Enter your contact information if you have any questions or need further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Insights** (`participant_insights`, text, optional): Please provide any insights you gained from this event.
* **Satisfaction Levels** (`satisfaction_levels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the options below:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, text, optional): Describe your overall experience at this event.
* **Event Attendance** (`event_attendance`, number, optional): Please enter the number of people that attended the event.
* **Event Participation** (`event_participation`, number, optional): Please rate your level of participation at this event.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, optional): Please enter the date and time of the event you attended.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If available, please enter the time of the event. This will help us with scheduling.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): If you have any questions or need further assistance, please enter your email address here.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please add any additional comments or suggestions you have for future events.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us using the contact information above.
* Your feedback is valuable to us and will help improve our corporate retreats.
