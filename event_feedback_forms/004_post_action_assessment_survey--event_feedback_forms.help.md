# post_action_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback after an event, gathering information on participant experience, attendance, and other relevant aspects of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form carefully, providing your honest feedback and experiences about the event.
2. For fields where you can select multiple options, choose all that apply.
3. If a field is not required, you can still fill it in if you have additional information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Participants** (`event_participants`, number, optional): This field is likely meant to gather information about the number of participants or attendees at the event. You can enter a numerical value to represent this.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply regarding whether the session (or event) was in-person, online, or other formats. 
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter a description or address where the event took place.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the session or event, in a unit of time that you prefer (e.g., minutes, hours, days).
* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, date, optional): Enter the date(s) of the event. This could be a single date or a range of dates.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the start and end time of the event, or the duration. You can use 24-hour format or your preferred time format.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the event.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide more information, enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you want to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or to provide more information, enter your phone number here.
* **Session Topics** (`session_topics`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the session was primarily about 'Yes' or 'No' topics.
* **Event Topics** (`event_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply regarding the topics covered during the session.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your feedback to help event organizers improve future events.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the event organizers.
