# Judging Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Judging Workshop Feedback Survey form, explaining each field and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all that apply from the provided options for each field.
2. For open-ended questions (text fields), provide a brief but clear answer.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
4. Submit the form once you've completed all the necessary questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Rating** (`workshop_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is for rating the overall quality of the workshop. You can select a number between 1 and 4.
* **What did you learn?** (`workshop_education`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the items that apply from the list, indicating what you found valuable or useful from the workshop.
* **How fun was the workshop?** (`workshop_fun`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply to indicate your experience with the fun aspect of the workshop.
* **Workshop Impressions** (`workshop_impressions`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your overall impression of the workshop, focusing on what stood out or left an impression on you.
* **How satisfied are you with the workshop?** (`workshop_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with the workshop, from Option 1 to Option 4.
* **Comments** (`feedback_comments`, `text`, required: false): Offer any additional comments or thoughts about your experience at the workshop.
* **Suggestions** (`feedback_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Suggest any improvements or changes you think could enhance future workshops.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter a valid email address to receive any follow-up communications or updates related to the workshop.

## Tips
- Be honest and sincere in your feedback.
- Use clear and concise language when answering open-ended questions.
- Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us for support.
- Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your participation in helping us improve our workshops!
