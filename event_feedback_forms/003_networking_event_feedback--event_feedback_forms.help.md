# Networking Event Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of networking events. The goal is to collect information about their experiences, connections made, and outcomes achieved during the event. This feedback will help event organizers understand how to improve future events and better serve their attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Provide feedback about your experience at the event in the "Provide feedback" field.
3. Select all the connections you made during the event from the "What connections did you make?" field.
4. Choose the outcomes you achieved during the event from the "What outcomes did you achieve?" field.
5. If you have any suggestions for future events, enter them in the "Do you have any suggestions?" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Provide feedback** (text, optional): Share your thoughts about the event, including what you liked and disliked.
* **What connections did you make?** (select multiple, optional): Select all the connections you made during the event, such as:
	+ Made a new connection
	+ Reconnected with someone
	+ Did not make any new connections
* **What outcomes did you achieve?** (select one, optional): Choose one or more of the outcomes you achieved during the event, such as:
	+ Networking opportunities
	+ Learning opportunities
	+ Neither
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (text, optional): Share any ideas you have for future events.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with follow-up questions or to send you more information about future events.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and describing your connections and outcomes.
* If you have any suggestions, be sure to enter them in the "Do you have any suggestions?" field.
* If you want to be contacted about future events, be sure to enter your email address in the "Email" field.
