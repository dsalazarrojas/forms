# Portrait Photography Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from clients about their experience with our portrait photography service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, please enter your response on the first page.
2. On the second page, rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
3. On the third page, provide any additional details about your session, such as notes about your photographer and their performance.
4. On the fourth page, select your favorite image from the session.
5. On the fifth page, you can provide more detailed information about your rating if you'd like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start of form** (`form_start`, text, required: false): Enter your response or any relevant comments here.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service. Please choose a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Session details** (`session_details`, note, required: false): Share any additional details about your session that you feel are relevant to your overall satisfaction.
* **Favorite image** (`favorite_image`, date, required: false): Select the date of your favorite image from the session.
* **Rating details** (`rating_details`, text, required: false): If you'd like to provide more information about your rating, please do so here.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your feedback and use it to improve our service!
