## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event details and preferences for planning and organization purposes.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

3. Provide accurate information for each field to ensure seamless event planning and execution.

* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.

* **Guest Count**: Enter the expected number of guests.
