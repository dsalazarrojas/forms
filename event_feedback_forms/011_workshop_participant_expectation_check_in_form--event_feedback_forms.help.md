# Workshop Participant Expectation Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by workshop participants to help the facilitator gauge their expectations, readiness, and engagement during the workshop. It's meant to be a quick and concise way to gather information that will help you tailor the workshop to the needs of all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Participant Expectation" question by selecting the number that best describes your expectations for the workshop.
2. Choose your "Readiness" level by selecting the number that best describes how prepared you feel for the workshop.
3. Select all topics that interest you from the "Topics And Interests" dropdown.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, type them in the "Follow Up Questions" text field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about yourself in the "Participant Notes/Comments" text field.
6. Indicate which topics we will cover during the workshop by selecting all that apply from the "Session Topics" dropdown.
7. If you would like to be rated on participant expectation and readininess, please fill out the "Participant Expectation Rate" and "Readiness Rate" fields with a brief description.
8. If you are interested in any of the topics we will be covering during the workshop, please select "True" from the "Session Topics Interested" dropdown for each topic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Expectation** (number): This field asks you to rate your expectations for the workshop, with options ranging from "Very High" to "Very Low".
* **Readiness** (number): This field asks you to rate your readiness for the workshop, with options ranging from "Very High" to "Very Low".
* **Topics And Interests** (select multiple): Select all topics that interest you.
* **Follow Up Questions** (text): If you have any questions or concerns, type them in this field.
* **Participant Notes/Comments** (text): Add any additional notes or comments about yourself.
* **Session Topics** (select multiple): Indicate which topics we will cover during the workshop. 
* **Participant Expectation Rate** (text): If you would like to be rated on participant expectation, please fill out this field with a brief description.
* **Readiness Rate** (text): If you would like to be rated on readiness, please fill out this field with a brief description.
* **Session Topics Interested** (select multiple): Indicate which topics you are interested in.
* **Follow Up Questions Asked** (text): If you have any questions or concerns after the workshop, type them in this field.
* **Participant Notes/Comments** (text): Add any additional notes or comments about yourself after the workshop.
* **Participant Engagement** (number): This field is not currently being used in this form but if you have any additional questions about this field please direct them to your facilitator.

## Tips
Please answer these questions honestly and to the best of your ability. This will help us tailor the workshop to your needs and ensure a productive and enjoyable experience for all participants. If you are unsure of any question, please ask your facilitator for guidance.
