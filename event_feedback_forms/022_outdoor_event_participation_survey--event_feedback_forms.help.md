# Outdoor Event Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees to outdoor events, helping us to improve the quality and content of our events. Your input will help us understand what you like and dislike about our events and make adjustments accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields and added any necessary comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the outdoor event you attended.
* **Event Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the outdoor event you attended. Please format the date in the standard date format (MM/dd/yyyy).
* **Event Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of the outdoor event you attended.
* **Attendee Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as an attendee of the outdoor event.
* **How often do you attend outdoor events?** (select_one, required/optional): 
    + 'Yes' if you frequently attend outdoor events
    + 'No' if you do not attend outdoor events
* **What activities would you like to see more of at our outdoor events?** (select_multiple, required/optional): 
    + Select all the activities you would like to see more of at our outdoor events
* **Event Organizer** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the event.
* **Was the event well-organized?** (select_one, required/optional): 
    + 'Yes' if the event was well-organized
    + 'No' if the event was not well-organized
