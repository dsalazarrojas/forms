# Community Club Feedback Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from members about their experience with the community club. This feedback will help the club understand what works well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out your name and email address in the first three fields.
2. Select your membership type and duration.
3. Choose the number of events you attended in the past year.
4. Select your favorite club activities.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with club activities and communications.
6. Choose your preferred communication method.
7. Rate the value for money of your membership.
8. Rate the quality of club facilities.
9. If applicable, suggest new activities you'd like to see offered.
10. Indicate if you're interested in volunteering.
11. Select the areas where you'd like to volunteer.
12. Rate how likely you are to recommend the club to others.
13. Suggest areas for improvement.
14. Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This is the field for your first name.
* **Last Name**: This is the field for your last name.
* **Email Address**: This is the field for your email address, which will be used for follow-up communications.
* **Membership Type**: This is the field to select the type of membership you have (e.g., Full Member, Associate Member, etc.).
* **Membership Duration**: This is the field to select how long you've been a member (e.g., Less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, etc.).
* **Events Attended**: This is the field to select the approximate number of events you attended in the past year.
* **Favorite Club Activities**: These are the fields to select your preferred club activities (e.g., Social Gatherings, Educational Workshops, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction with Activities**: This is the field to rate your overall satisfaction with club activities (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
* **Communication Rating**: This is the field to rate how well the club communicates with you (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, etc.).
* **Preferred Communication Method**: These are the fields to select your preferred communication method (e.g., Email, Text Message, etc.).
* **Value for Money**: This is the field to rate the value you feel you get from your membership (e.g., Excellent Value, Good Value, etc.).
* **Club Facility Rating**: This is the field to rate the quality of club facilities (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, etc.).
* **New Activities You Would Like to See**: This is the field to suggest new activities you'd like to see offered (if applicable).
* **Would You Like to Volunteer**: This is the field to indicate if you're interested in volunteering.
* **Volunteer Areas of Interest**: These are the fields to select areas where you'd like to volunteer (e.g., Event Planning, Communications, etc.).
* **Would You Recommend This Club**: This is the field to rate how likely you are to recommend the club to others (e.g., Very Likely, Likely, etc.).
* **What Could the Club Improve**: This is the field to suggest areas for improvement.
* **Additional Comments**: This is the field to add any additional comments or feedback.

Note: Please be sure to fill out all fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible to provide the club with valuable insights and suggestions for improvement.
