# Event Meal Contribution Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback from individuals who were fed at an event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several pages, each with a different set of questions.
2. Please fill out the form based on your actual experience at the event.
3. Start by rating the overall quality of the meal you received.
4. Enter the quantity of guests you fed.
5. Choose the date and time of the event.
6. If you would like to bring a meal in the future, select the corresponding option.
7. Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
8. Finally, enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Ratings (1)**: Rate the quality of the meal you received on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Comments (2)**: Enter any comments or feedback you may have about the meal.
* **Will you be willing to bring a meal in the future? (3)**: Choose this option if you are interested in bringing a meal in the future.
* **Event Date (4)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time (5)**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Quantity of guests you fed (6)**: Enter the number of guests you fed.
* **Quality of meal (7)**: Rate the quality of the meal you received on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Future Meal (8)**: Choose this option if you are willing to bring a meal in the future.
* **Comments (9)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Who did you feed? (10)**: Choose who you fed at the event (e.g. Organizer, Volunteer, Other).
* **Email (11)**: Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
* **Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Comments (13)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Any other feedback (14)**: Enter any other comments or feedback you may have.
