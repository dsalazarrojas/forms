# Post Conference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Conference Survey is designed to collect feedback from attendees about their experience at the conference. This survey aims to help organizers evaluate the event's success and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Answer the rating questions by selecting a number between 1 and 10 for the "Ratings 1 to 10" question and a specific number for the "Ratings 5 points", "Rating 4 points", and "Rating 5 points" questions.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Select" and "Select Multiple" questions.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
6. Select a date range for the "Date Range" question.
7. Enter a time range for the "Time Range" question.
8. Enter any decimal value for the "Decimal" question.
9. Rate the speaker by selecting a number between 1 and 10 for the "Rating" question.
10. Enter the speaker's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
10. Enter a brief description of the event in the "Event" field.
11. Select a specific number for the "Rating Scale Prophets Points" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Survey**: Provide a brief description of your experience at the conference. 
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your conference badge or business card.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the conference.
* **Expectations**: Provide any expectations you had about the conference.
* **Ratings 1 to 10**: Rate your overall experience at the conference on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Ratings 5 points**: Rate your experience on a scale of 5 points.
* **Date**: Enter the date you attended the conference.
* **Time**: Enter the time you attended the conference.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the conference or speakers.
* **Select**: Choose whether you select yes or no.
* **Select Multiple**: Select all applicable options (yes or no).
* **Date Range**: Select a date range you attended the conference.
* **Time Range**: Select a time range you attended the conference.
* **Decimal**: Enter a decimal value.
* **Rating**: Rate the speaker by selecting a number between 1 and 10.
* **Speaker Name**: Enter the speaker's name.
* **Speaker Email**: Enter the speaker's email address.
* **Speaker Phone**: Enter the speaker's phone number.
* **Event**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Rating 4 points**: Rate the speaker on a scale of 4 points.
* **Rating 3 points**: Rate the speaker on a scale of 3 points.
* **Rating 5 points**: Rate the speaker on a scale of 5 points.
* **Rating Scale Prophets Points**: Rate the speaker on a specific scale of points.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Provide clear and concise answers for each question.
* If you have any technical issues or difficulties filling out the form, contact the conference organizers.
