# Recreational Program Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on their experiences with the recreational program. Your input will help us to improve and refine the program for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin the process.
2.  Fill out each page of the form, working through the sections as they appear.
3.  Review each field to ensure you have entered accurate and complete information.
4.  Click the "Submit" button at the end of the form to finalize your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Feedback (Page 1)
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `note`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any comments or feedback you have about your experience with the program. This could include suggestions for improvement, positive comments, or concerns. 

### Rating (Page 2)
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to rate your overall satisfaction with the program. Please select the number that best represents your rating from 1 to 5.

### Event Details (Page 3)
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing additional information about the event you participated in. You can enter as much or as little information as you feel is necessary.

### Date Participated (Page 4)
* **Date Participated** (`date_participated`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date on which you participated in the event.

### Time Participated (Page 5)
* **Time Participated** (`time_participated`, `time`, required: false): Please enter the time you participated in the event.

### Staff Support (Page 6)
* **Staff Support** (`staff_support`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for rating the support provided by the staff. Please select the number that best represents your experience from 1 to 5.

### Recommendations (Page 7)
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions or recommendations for the program, please enter them here.

### Comments (Page 8)
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or questions that don't fit into the other fields, you can enter them here.

### Submit (Page 9)
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Clicking this button will finalize your feedback form. Make sure to carefully review your input before submitting.
