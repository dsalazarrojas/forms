# Post Christmas Party Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from participants about their experience at a Christmas party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the necessary details about the party in the "Party Details" section. This will help us understand the context of your experience.
2. Next, select the type of food you enjoyed during the party by clicking on the "Food" options.
3. Indicate the type of music that was played during the party by selecting all that apply from the "Music" options.
4. Describe the atmosphere of the party in the "Atmosphere" field. This will help us get a better sense of the party's ambiance.
5. Rate your experience with the food by selecting a number that corresponds to how you feel about it.
6. Rate how you felt during the party by selecting a number that corresponds to your level of happiness.
7. In the "Party Suggestions" field, provide any suggestions or ideas you have for future parties.
8. If you would like to be contacted, please provide your email address.
9. Finally, if you would like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party Details (Text)** (Required: No): This is the first section of the form where you can provide any details about the party that you think are relevant.
* **Food (Select One)** (Required: No): This field allows you to select the type of food that was served at the party. You can choose one option from the list.
	+ Pizza
	+ Sushi
	+ Tacos
* **Music (Select Multiple)** (Required: No): This field allows you to select the types of music that were played during the party. You can choose all that apply.
	+ Christmas Carol
	+ Pop
	+ Rock
* **Atmosphere (Text)** (Required: No): Describe the atmosphere of the party in a few words.
* **How would you rate the food? (Number)** (Required: No): Rate your experience with the food by selecting a number that corresponds to how you feel about it. (1-5)
* **How did you feel during the party? (Number)** (Required: No): Rate how you felt during the party by selecting a number that corresponds to your level of happiness. (1-5)
* **Party Suggestions (Note)** (Required: No): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for future parties.
* **Email (Email)** (Required: No): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your email address.
* **Phone (Text)** (Required: No): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
