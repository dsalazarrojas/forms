# Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from participants on their experience and rating of a training session. This form is meant to help improve the quality of future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Go to the form and click on the "Start Form" button to begin.
- Fill out the "Session Details" page with any relevant information about the session you attended.
- Proceed to the "Feedback" page and enter your comments about your experience with the session.
- On the "Rating" page, select a rating for the session from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
- Optionally, add any additional comments on the "Comments" page.
- Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the session you attended, such as the date, time, and location.
- **Feedback** (`feedback_details`, text, optional): Enter your thoughts and comments about your experience with the session.
- **Rate Session** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the session on a scale of 1 to 5, where:
  - 1: Poor
  - 2: Average
  - 3: Good
  - 4: Very Good
  - 5: Excellent
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the session. 

## Tips
- Please be honest and constructive with your feedback, as it will help us improve the quality of our training sessions.
- If you have any specific suggestions or ideas for improvements, feel free to mention them in the "Comments" field. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
