# Extraordinary Experiences Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extraordinary Experiences Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experiences with events. It helps us understand what worked well and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Event Name** (Field 1) with the name of the event you are providing feedback about.
2. Choose the **Event Date** (Field 2) of the event from the calendar or select the date of the event.
3. Enter your **Participant Name** (Field 3) and your email address in the **Email** (Field 7) field.
4. Provide your **Feedback** (Field 4) about the event in the text field.
5. Rate your **Rating** (Field 5) of the event using the number input.
6. If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them in the **Suggestion** (Field 6) field.
7. Select the **Category** (Field 9) of the event from the dropdown menu (e.g., Event Feedback, Other).
8. Select up to three **Prophets** (Field 11) from the dropdown menu (e.g., Team 1, Team 2).
9. You can optionally provide **Comments** (Field 12) about the event.
10. If you wish to provide additional contact information, you can enter your **Phone** (Field 8) number.
11. Finally, select the **Department** (Field 10) related to the event from the dropdown menu (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Events).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name (Field 1)**: The name of the event you are providing feedback about.
- **Event Date (Field 2)**: The date of the event you attended or participated in.
- **Participant Name (Field 3)**: Enter your name as the participant of the event.
- **Feedback (Field 4)**: Provide your feedback about the event in this text field.
- **Rating (Field 5)**: Rate your experience with the event using a number (e.g., 1-5).
- **Suggestion (Field 6)**: Enter any suggestions for improvement for the event.
- **Email (Field 7)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **Phone (Field 8)**: Provide your phone number if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions.
- **Category (Field 9)**: Choose the category related to the event (e.g., Event Feedback, Other).
- **Department (Field 10)**: Select the department related to the event (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Events).
- **Prophets (Field 11)**: Select up to three prophets related to the event.
- **Comments (Field 12)**: Optional comments about the event.
