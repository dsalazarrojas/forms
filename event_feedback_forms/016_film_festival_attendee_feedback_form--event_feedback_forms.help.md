# Film Festival Attendee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from film festival attendees, allowing us to understand their experiences and satisfaction with the event. The collected data will be used to improve future film festival events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event you attended in the `event` field.
2. Select the date you attended the event in the `date` field.
3. Choose the time you attended the event in the `time` field.
4. Select your level of satisfaction with the event in the `attendees` field.
5. Indicate which aspects of the event you were most satisfied or dissatisfied with in the `preferences` field (select multiple options).
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the `comments` field (optional).
7. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event using the numerical scale in the `satisfaction` field.
8. Optionally, provide a rating for the event using the numerical scale in the `rating` field.
9. Share any additional comments or feedback in the `comments` field (optional).
10. Enter your contact email address in the `contact` field if you wish to be contacted for future events.
11. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field (optional).
12. Enter any additional address information in the `address` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event** (`event`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you attended.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you attended the event.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time you attended the event.
- **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with the event (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Very Dissatisfied, Not at All Satisfied).
- **Preferences** (`preferences`, select_multiple, required): Indicate which aspects of the event you were most satisfied or dissatisfied with (action, music, food, other).
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
- **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event using a numerical scale.
- **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Optionally, provide a rating for the event using a numerical scale.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback.
- **Contact** (`contact`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address if you wish to be contacted for future events.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter any additional address information (optional).

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields to provide a complete feedback.
- If you have any suggestions or complaints not addressed in this form, please contact the festival organizers directly.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [contact email].
