# Event Outcome Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Outcome Report Form is a survey to collect feedback on events, gathering information on the outcome, likes, dislikes, and suggestions. This form helps event organizers and attendees understand what worked well and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. For each question, provide a clear and concise answer.
3. You can add or remove pages as needed to accommodate your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Outcome** (`event_outcome_report_form_1`, text, required: false)
This field is where you can describe the outcome of the event. Be as detailed as possible, and provide any relevant information about what happened during the event.

### **What did you like?** (`event_outcome_report_form_2`, text, required: false)
In this field, list the things you enjoyed or appreciated about the event. Be specific about what made the event enjoyable or useful for you.

### **What did you dislike?** (`event_outcome_report_form_3`, text, required: false)
Here, list the things that did not go well or that you did not enjoy about the event. Be specific about what you did not like or found problematic.

### **Do you have a suggestion?** (`event_outcome_report_form_4`, text, required: false)
If you have any ideas on how to improve the event, please share them in this field. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we will take it into consideration.

### **What would you like to see different?** (`event_outcome_report_form_5`, text, required: false)
This field is for suggestions or ideas you may have on how to make the event better in the future. Be as specific as possible, and thank you for your input.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can add or remove pages as needed to accommodate your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
