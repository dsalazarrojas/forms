# corporate_retreat_feedback_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "corporate_retreat_feedback_assessment_form" is designed to collect feedback and assessment from participants after a corporate retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your participant information (Participant Information).
*   Provide your company name and job title (Participant Company, Participant Job Title).
*   Answer each question in the subsequent sections with your honest feedback and assessment.
*   Make sure to fill out each field, as they are all optional and important for a comprehensive assessment.
*   Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required): Fill in your name, department, and any other relevant participant information.
*   **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
*   **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
*   **Participant Job Title 2** (`participant_job_title_2`, text, required): Enter your job title (for second instance, if needed).
*   **...** (`participant_job_title_14`, text, required): Enter your job title (for 13th instance, if needed).
