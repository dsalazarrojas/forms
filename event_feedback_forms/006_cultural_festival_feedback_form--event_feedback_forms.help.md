# cultural_festival_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural_festival_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of cultural festivals, allowing us to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name.
2. Enter the date you visited the festival.
3. Choose the festival activities you attended.
4. Rate your overall experience.
5. Leave any comments or suggestions you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. <label> Event Details</label>
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your visit, if you have any.

### 2. <label> name</label>
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.

### 3. <label> email</label>
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).

### 4. <label> Date of Visit</label>
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date you visited the festival.

### 5. <label> Time of Visit</label>
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Choose the time you visited the festival.

### 6. <label> Attended Activities</label>
* **Attended Activities** (`attended_activities`, select_multiple, optional): Select the festival activities you attended. You can choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Cultural Festival A
	+ Cultural Festival B
	+ Cultural Festival C
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ None

### 7. <label> Rating</label>
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.

### 8. <label> Comments</label>
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Leave any comments or suggestions you might have.

### 9. <label> Suggestions</label>
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for future festivals.
