# Festival Stallholder Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Stallholder Feedback Survey is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about your experience at the festival. Your feedback will help us improve future events and make them more enjoyable for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions in the text fields by typing your responses directly into the corresponding text boxes.
2. For the multiple-choice questions (e.g., "overall_satisfaction" and "future_participation_interest"), select the option that best represents your response.
3. You can add your comments in the "comments" text field if you have any additional thoughts or suggestions.
4. Make sure to save your progress by clicking the "Submit" button when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Stallholder Feedback Survey Form**: This is the main form where you'll be answering the questions about your experience at the festival.
* **Vendor Name**: Please enter your stallholder name or the name of your company/organization.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the festival. Choose the option that best represents your experience.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Future Participation Interest**: Let us know if you're interested in participating in future events. Select "Yes" if you're likely to participate, and "No" if you're not.
* **Comments**: Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you might have about the festival.
* **Will Participate**: Tell us if you're likely to participate in future events, or not. Select all that apply.
* **Event Name**: Please enter the name of the festival you're referring to in this survey.
