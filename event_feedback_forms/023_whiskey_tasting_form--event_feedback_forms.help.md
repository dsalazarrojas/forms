# Whiskey Tasting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on the tasting experience of a particular whiskey. It is intended for employees of a wine and spirits store or tasting room to provide detailed and honest feedback on the whiskey's qualities.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide a thorough and accurate review, please complete each of the following fields:

1. Fill in the "whiskey_name" field with the name of the whiskey you are reviewing.
2. Describe the whiskey's aroma in the "aroma" field. Try to be as specific as possible, noting any distinctive flavors or scents you detect.
3. Describe the whiskey's appearance in the "appearance" field. This could include the color, clarity, or any notable features.
4. Describe the whiskey's finish in the "finish" field. This is the lingering aftertaste or sensation left on your palate after taking a sip.
5. Rate your overall experience with the whiskey in the "overall experience" field using the number scale provided.
6. Score the whiskey in the "score" field using the same number scale.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments about the whiskey in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **whiskey_name** (whiskey_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the whiskey you are reviewing.
* **aroma** (aroma, text, optional): Describe the whiskey's aroma in detail.
* **appearance** (appearance, text, optional): Describe the whiskey's appearance, including its color, clarity, or any notable features.
* **finish** (finish, text, optional): Describe the whiskey's finish, or the lingering aftertaste or sensation left on your palate.
* **overall experience** (overall experience, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the whiskey using the number scale provided. This will help us understand how you perceive the whiskey's quality and enjoyment.
* **score** (score, number, optional): Score the whiskey using the same number scale as the overall experience rating.
* **notes** (notes, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the whiskey, such as any notable characteristics or issues you encountered.

Note: The scale for the overall experience and score fields can be used as follows:
0-1: Dislike
2-3: Not Impressed
4-5: Average
6-7: Like
8-9: Great
10: Exceptional

Tips:
* Try to be as detailed and specific as possible in each field to provide a thorough review.
* Use the number scale consistently for both the overall experience and score fields.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please feel free to add them in the notes field.
