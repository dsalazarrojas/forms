# Escape Room Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Escape Room Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from event attendees on their experience at an escape room event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with information about your experience at the escape room event.
2. Answer each question based on your honest opinion and feedback.
3. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
4. You can skip questions that do not apply to your experience.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form truthfully based on your experience.
* Provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you're unsure about an option, select the one that best fits your experience.

* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
