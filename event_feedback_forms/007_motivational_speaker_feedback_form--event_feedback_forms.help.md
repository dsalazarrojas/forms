# Motivational Speaker Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Motivational Speaker Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback about the motivational speaker's performance and the quality of the event. This feedback will help us to improve the speaker's performance and make future events better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in all the required fields (marked as required in the form).
* For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank if you don't have any information to provide.
* Be honest and provide accurate information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Rating (1)** (number, not required): This field allows you to rate the motivational speaker's performance on a scale of 1 to 5. Please rate the speaker's performance based on their ability to engage and motivate the audience.
* **Session Quality (2)** (number, not required): This field allows you to rate the quality of the session, including the speaker's presentation, organization, and overall experience. Please rate the session quality based on your experience.
* **Speaker Message (3)** (note, not required): This field is a text box where you can provide any additional comments or messages about the speaker or the event. Please use this field to provide any feedback that doesn't fit in the other fields.
* **Event Name (4)** (text, not required): This field is to record the name of the event where you attended the speaker. If you're not sure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Feedback Message (5)** (text, not required): This field is similar to the Speaker Message field, but for any overall feedback about the event.
* **Speaker Name (6)** (text, not required): This field is for recording the name of the speaker. If you can't remember their name, you can leave this field blank.
* **Session Date (7)** (date, not required): This field is for recording the date of the session. If you don't know the exact date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Session Time (8)** (time, not required): This field is for recording the time of the session. If you don't know the exact time, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide accurate information in each field.
* If you have any issues or difficulties with the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Your feedback is highly appreciated and will help us to improve the speaker's performance and the overall event quality.
