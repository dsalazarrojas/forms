The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the performance of an audience, typically after a play has been performed. This form is designed to capture various aspects of the audience's reaction, including their mood, notable laugh responses, and overall impression of the performance. The feedback collected will be used to improve future performances and better understand the audience's experience. Please ensure that you are familiar with the performance you are reporting on before completing this form.

## Audience Reaction Play Report Form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as "required". These fields are:
    *   Play Title: Enter the title of the play that was performed.
    *   Performance Date: Enter the date of the performance.
    *   Performance Time: Choose the time of the performance (Matinee, Evening, or Other).
    *   General Mood of the Audience: Describe the overall atmosphere of the audience during the performance.
    *   Report Prepared By: Enter your name as the person who prepared this report.
2.  The other fields are optional and can be filled out if you have additional information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Play Title** (play_title, text, required):
    *   Enter the title of the play that was performed.
*   **Performance Date** (performance_date, date, required):
    *   Enter the date of the performance.
*   **Performance Time** (performance_time, select_one, required):
    *   Choose the time of the performance (Matinee, Evening, or Other).
*   **Estimated House Size** (house_size, number, optional):
    *   Enter the approximate number of audience members.
*   **General Mood of the Audience** (overall_mood, text, required):
    *   Describe the overall atmosphere of the audience during the performance.
*   **Notable Laugh Responses** (laugh_response, text, optional):
    *   Describe which scenes got the most laughs from the audience.
*   **Quality of Applause** (applause_quality, select_one, required):
    *   Choose the quality of the applause (Sustained and Loud, Polite and Average, Weak, or Standing Ovation).
*   **Significant Quiet Moments** (quiet_moments, text, optional):
    *   Describe moments when the audience was quiet or seemed unengaged.
*   **Any technical issues that affected the audience?** (technical_issues_noted, text, optional):
    *   Describe any technical issues that affected the performance or the audience's experience.
*   **Impact of Late Arrivals** (late_arrivals_impact, text, optional):
    *   Describe how late arrivals affected the performance or the audience's experience.
*   **Other Performance Notes** (additional_observations, text, optional):
    *   Enter any other relevant information about the performance.
*   **Report Prepared By** (report_prepared_by, text, required):
    *   Enter your name as the person who prepared this report.

Tips:

*   Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form.
*   If you have any doubts or questions about a field, refer to the hints provided.
*   This form is designed to collect information about the audience's experience, so be honest and accurate in your responses.
