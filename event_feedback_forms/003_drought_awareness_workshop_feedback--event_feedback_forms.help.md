# Drought Awareness Workshop Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Drought Awareness Workshop feedback form. Please refer to it to understand what each field is asking you to do.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each field according to your experience with the workshop.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by a *).
3. Be as detailed as possible in your responses, especially in the text fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Additional feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How many times have you attended this type of workshop before?**
	+ This field is asking about your prior experience with similar workshops.
	+ Please answer with a number.
