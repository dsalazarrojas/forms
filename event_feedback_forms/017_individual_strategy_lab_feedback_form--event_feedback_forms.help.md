# Individual Strategy Lab Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from participants on their individual strategy lab experience. This feedback will help us understand their perception of the event, identify areas of improvement, and provide valuable insights to enhance future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Participant's name (event_feedback_form_participant_name)
	* Rating of your overall experience (event_feedback_form_rating)
	* Whether you attended the event or not (participant_attended)
	* Status of the event (event_status)
	* Reasons for your rating (if selected)
2. Optionally, provide additional feedback in the comments field (event_feedback_form_comments)
3. Enter your participant's email address (participant_email)
4. Optionally, enter your participant's phone number (participant_phone)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Feedback Form Participant
* **Participant Name** (event_feedback_form_participant_name): Enter your name as it appears on your participant badge or ID.
* **Rating** (event_feedback_form_rating): Provide your rating of the overall event experience on a scale of 0-10.
* **Participant Email** (participant_email): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Participant Phone** (participant_phone): Optionally, enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Event Name** (event_feedback_form_event_name): Enter the name of the event you participated in.
* **Event Start Date** (event_feedback_form_event_start_date): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date** (event_feedback_form_event_end_date): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Event Location** (event_feedback_form_event_location): Enter the location of the event.
* **Attended** (participant_attended): Select 'True' if you attended the event, 'False' otherwise.
* **Event Status** (event_feedback_form_event_status): Select the current status of the event (Completed, In Progress, Not Started).
* **Reasons for Rating** (event_feedback_form_participant_rating_reasons): Select the reasons for your rating (if applicable).
* **Comments** (event_feedback_form_participant_comments): Optionally, provide additional comments or feedback.

**Tips**

* Be honest and specific in your feedback to help us improve future events.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
