This help guide is intended to be used by end users to understand the purpose and usage of the "theme_park_feedback_form" form. The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users who have visited the theme park. This form is intended to be used by users to provide their overall satisfaction, feedback, and experiences during their visit. Before filling out this form, please ensure that you have visited the theme park recently and have a clear understanding of your experiences.

When filling out this form, please follow these steps:
1) Provide your honest feedback about your overall experience at the theme park.
2) Select the specific rides and attractions you experienced during your visit.
3) Rate your overall satisfaction with the park.
4) Add any additional feedback or comments about your visit.
5) Enter your email address for contact purposes.
6) Provide the date and time of your visit.
7) Indicate whether you would visit the park again.
8) Describe any additional details about your visit.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Theme Park Visit**: Enter a brief description of your visit to the theme park, including any notable events or experiences.
* **Rides and Attractions**: Select all the rides and attractions you experienced during your visit. (Multiple selections are allowed)
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the theme park, on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Feedback Text**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes, in case we need to follow up with you.
* **Feedback Date**: Enter the date of your visit to the theme park.
* **Feedback Time**: Enter the time of your visit to the theme park.
* **Would you visit again?**: Indicate whether you would visit the theme park again, with options to choose from "True" or "False".
* **Visit Details**: Enter any additional details about your visit, such as any challenges or difficulties you faced.
* **How long did you stay in the park?**: Enter the duration of your visit, including hours or any other relevant information.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the user who assigned this form to you.
