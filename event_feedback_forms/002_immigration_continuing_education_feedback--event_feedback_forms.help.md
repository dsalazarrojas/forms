# Immigration Continuing Education Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from immigration session attendees to gauge the quality and effectiveness of the sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide detailed feedback on the session in the "Immigration Session Feedback" field.
2. Evaluate the session's quality by selecting one of the options in the "Overall Evaluation" field.
3. Suggest future topics you would like to see covered in future sessions in the "Topic Suggestions" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can provide them in the "Additional Notes" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, but this is optional.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, but this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Immigration Session Feedback** (`event_feedback_form`, text, optional): Provide detailed feedback on the session.
* **Session Evaluation** (`session_evaluation`, number, optional): Evaluate the session's quality (1-5).
* **Topic Suggestions** (`topic_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest future topics you would like to see covered in future sessions.
* **Future Topics** (`future_topics`, select_multiple, required): Select all the topics you would like to see covered in future sessions.
* **Overall Evaluation** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, required): Rate the session's quality (Very Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional Notes** (`feedback_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
