**Virtual Dinner Party Feedback Form - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
-----------

The Virtual Dinner Party Feedback Form is a survey designed to gather feedback from attendees about their experience at a virtual dinner party. This form will help us understand what worked well and what didn't, allowing us to improve future events.

**How To Complete This Form**
-------------------------

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the form by entering your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
3. Enter the date and time of the event, including any additional information about the event location.
4. Rate your experience using the provided rating scale.
5. Share any comments or feedback about your experience.
6. Select one or more options for Event Type, Event Host, and Catering.
7. Enter any Additional Comments about your experience.
8. If you want to provide feedback about the service quality, please use the "Service Quality" field.
9. Finally, enter your Overall Experience rating and any Recommendations for improvement.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**
-------------------------

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for future follow-up.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the event location.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, optional): Share any comments about your rating.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes the type of event you attended.
* **Event Host** (`event_host`, select_one, optional): Select the host of the event.
* **Event Catering** (`event_catering`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options that best describe the catering at the event.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, text, optional): Share any feedback about the service quality.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Share any recommendations for improvement.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Select one option for follow-up.
* **Next Event** (`next_event`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next event.
* **Next Event Time** (`next_event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the next event.
* **Next Event Location** (`next_event_location`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the next event location.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments.

**Tips**
-------

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you need to provide feedback about multiple events, please submit a separate form for each event. 

This help guide is designed to assist end-users in completing the Virtual Dinner Party Feedback Form in a clear and efficient manner.
