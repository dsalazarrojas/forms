# venue_rental_experience_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "venue_rental_experience_questionnaire" form is used to collect feedback from customers about their rental experience with a venue. It helps us understand customer perception of our services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Answer the questions about the venue you rented.
2.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
3.  Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Venue** (`venue`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select from a list of venues you have rented from us. You can select multiple venues if applicable.
*   **Event** (`event`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to specify the type of event you attended at the venue (e.g., '1', '2', '3', '4', or '5').
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your event.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of your event.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of your event.
*   **Rental Experience** (`rental_experience`, `note`, required: false): Write a brief description about your rental experience.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false): Provide any feedback you may have about your experience.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
*   **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
*   **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): If you're representing a company, enter the company name.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact name associated with your company or you.
*   **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact title (e.g., 'Event Coordinator', 'Manager', etc.).
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact email.
*   **Event Type** (`event_type`, `text`, required: false): Describe the type of event you attended (e.g., wedding, conference, etc.).
*   **Attendees** (`attendees`, `text`, required: false): Enter the number of attendees at your event.
*   **Venue Rating** (`venue_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the venue from 1-5, with 5 being the highest rating.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any comments about your experience.
*   **Venue Rating Scale** (`venue_rating_scale`, `number`, required: false): Rate the venue on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Submission** (`submission`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose if you submitted the correct documents to the venue (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help us improve our services. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the designated contact person.
