# Karaoke Contest Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Karaoke Contest Judging Form is designed to collect feedback and scores from judges on the performances they have witnessed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the performance (Pop, Rock, Country, Jazz, or Other).
2. Enter the name of the singer performing.
3. Assign a score from 1-10 to the singer's performance.
4. Indicate if the singer's stage presence was good or bad.
5. Evaluate the singer's communication skills as good or bad.
6. Provide an overall score on the performance (Good or Bad).
7. If necessary, indicate if the singer is a winner or not.
8. Provide any additional comments about the performance.
9. Enter the name of the person who assigned the score.
10. Enter the tool or platform used for assignment.
11. Enter the date and time of submission.
12. Enter the date and time of creation.
13. Enter the name and email address of the person who submitted the score.
14. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the score.
15. Enter the email address of the person who assigned the score.
16. Enter the phone number of the person who assigned the score.
17. Select the category of the performance.
18. Select the time of assignment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Select One): Select the category of the performance (Pop, Rock, Country, Jazz, or Other).
* **name_of_singer** (Text): Enter the name of the singer performing.
* **singer_score** (Number, 1-10): Assign a score from 1-10 to the singer's performance.
* **best_dressed** (Select Multiple): Indicate if the singer's stage presence was good or bad.
* **performance_score** (Select Multiple): Evaluate the singer's communication skills as good or bad.
* **stage_presence** (Select Multiple): Indicate if the singer's stage presence was good or bad.
* **communication_score** (Select Multiple): Evaluate the singer's communication skills as good or bad.
* **overall_score** (Select Multiple): Provide an overall score on the performance (Good or Bad).
* **winner** (Select Multiple): If necessary, indicate if the singer is a winner or not.
* **comments** (Note): Provide any additional comments about the performance.
* **assigned_to** (Text): Enter the name of the person who assigned the score.
* **assigned_by** (Text): Enter the tool or platform used for assignment.
* **assigned_at** (Date and Time): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **created_at** (Date and Time): Enter the date and time of creation.
* **submitted_by** (Text): Enter the name of the person who submitted the score.
* **submitted_by_email** (Email Address): Enter the name and email address of the person who submitted the score.
* **submitted_by_phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the score.
* **assigned_by_email** (Email Address): Enter the email address of the person who assigned the score.
* **assigned_by_phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person who assigned the score.
* **category** (Select One): Select the category of the performance.
* **assigned_at** (Time): Select the time of assignment.

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's highly recommended to fill them for better evaluation and record keeping.
