# Music Event Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a music event from its performers. The purpose of this form is to determine the performer's availability for the event, and to collect additional details such as event name, dates, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about the music event.
* Select the correct availability option from the dropdown menu.
* Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
* Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performer** (`performer`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the performer(s) involved in the music event.
* **Performer Email** (`performer_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the performer or contact person for the event. This will help us reach out to them with updates and information.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the music event.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the music event.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the music event.
* **Availability** (`availability`, Select One, Optional): Choose one of the following availability options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the music event.
* **Attendee Count** (`attendee_count`, Number, Optional): Enter the expected number of attendees for the music event.
