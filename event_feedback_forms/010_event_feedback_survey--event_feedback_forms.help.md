# Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from event attendees on their overall satisfaction and experience with the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with your feedback.
2. Choose the relevant event information from the drop-down menus.
3. Fill in your name and email address for contact purposes.
4. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (\*): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Venue Rating** (\*): Rate the venue of the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Speaker Rating** (\*): Rate the speaker of the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Name** (\*): Enter your name.
* **Email** (\*): Enter your email address.
* **Date** (\*): Choose the date of the event from the drop-down menu.
* **Start Time** (\*): Choose the start time of the event from the drop-down menu.
* **Venue** (\*): Choose the venue of the event from the drop-down menu.
* **Speaker** (\*): Choose the speaker of the event from the drop-down menu.
* **Type** (\*): Choose the type of event from the drop-down menu.
* **Event Name**: Choose the name of the event from the drop-down menu.
* **Overall Satisfaction Score** ( ): Provide a satisfaction score on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction Average** (\*): Enter an average satisfaction score on a scale of 1-5.
* **Venue Satisfaction**: Rate the venue's satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Speaker Satisfaction**: Rate the speaker's satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
* **Event Feedback**: Provide any feedback on the event, speaker, or venue.
* **Feedback Organizer**: Provide any feedback on the event organizer.
* **Overall Satisfaction Average 2** (\*): Provide an average satisfaction score for the event.

Please make sure to complete all the required fields and any additional fields you have feedback on. If you need help with any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us.
