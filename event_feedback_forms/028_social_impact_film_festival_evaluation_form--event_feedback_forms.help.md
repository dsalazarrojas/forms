# Social Impact Film Festival Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate a film presented at the Social Impact Film Festival. It's meant to gather feedback on the film's content, impact, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- Fill out each field as prompted, and make sure to select at least one option from the select fields.
- You can skip any fields that do not apply to your experience or opinion.
- Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Festival Name** (text): Enter the name of the film festival you are evaluating.
- **Film Title** (text): Enter the title of the film you are evaluating.
- **Evaluation Rating** (number): Rate the film's social impact on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
- **Evaluation Comment** (text): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the film.
- **Category** (select_multiple): Select one or more categories that best describe the film (e.g. Drama, Comedy, Documentary).
- **Subcategory** (select_one): Select a more specific subcategory that best fits the film (e.g. Drama > Romance, Comedy > Satire).
- **Impact Level** (select_one): Rate the film's social impact on a scale of High, Medium, Low.
- **Rating Score** (number): Rate the film's overall quality on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
- **Feedback** (text): Provide any general feedback or comments about your experience with the film.
- **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes (optional).
- **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
- **Submitted By** (select_one): Indicate whether you are a festival attendee, juror, or organizer.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have trouble with any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Your input is greatly appreciated and will help us improve future film festival events.
