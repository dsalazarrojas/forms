# Presentation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees on the presentation they have watched or attended. The goal is to provide valuable insights for the speaker and event organizers to improve future presentations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all required fields to ensure that your feedback is accurate and complete.
2. Be honest and provide your genuine thoughts and feelings about the presentation.
3. Use the provided options and fields to give as much detail as possible about your experience.
4. If you have any questions or doubts, ask your event organizer for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall experience?** (presentation_feedback_survey_1, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes' if you had a positive experience, and 'No' if you did not have a positive experience.
* **How would you rate this presentation?** (rating, number, required): Rate the presentation on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Do you have any feedback about this presentation?** (feedback, note, required): Provide any comments or feedback about the presentation.
* **What is your name?** (name, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed next to your feedback.
* **What is your email?** (email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Do you have a phone number?** (phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **What is the title of the presentation?** (presentation_title, text, required): Enter the title of the presentation as you remember it.
* **When was the presentation held?** (presentation_date, time, required): Enter the date and time of the presentation.
* **What is the name of the event?** (event_name, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **When was the event held?** (event_date, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Which session was the presentation in?** (session, select_one, required): Select the session where the presentation was given.
* **Do you have a presentation file you can share?** (presentation_file, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have a presentation file, and 'No' if you do not.
* **Was this feedback survey completed on your own device or at a public computer?** (feedback_survey_completed, select_one, required): Select where you completed the feedback survey.
* **Do you have any additional comments about this presentation?** (additional_comments, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, provide them here.
* **What is the location of the event?** (event_location, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **When was your session held?** (session_time, time, required): Enter the time of the session.
* **Was the session on this date?** (session_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the session.
* **How long was the presentation?** (presentation_length, number, required): Enter the length of the presentation in minutes.
* **How long was your session?** (session_length, number, optional): Enter the length of the session in minutes.
* **Do you have any additional feedback about the event?** (additional_feedback, note, optional): If you have any additional feedback, provide it here.
* **How would you rate the speaker?** (speaker, number, optional): Rate the speaker on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How would you rate the presentation content?** (presentation_content, number, optional): Rate the presentation content on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How would you rate the event facilities?** (event_facilities, number, optional): Rate the event facilities on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Overall rating** (overall_rating, number, optional): Rate the overall rating of the presentation.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Provide any comments you have about the presentation.
