# post_event_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Post Event Feedback Survey** form is designed to collect feedback from users after an event. This feedback will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
2. Ensure you answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you have any comments or additional feedback, use the text fields (feedback_text, question13, question16, question17, question18, question20, question22, and question23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event ID (event_id)
* **event_id** (`event_id`, number, *): Please enter the ID of the event you are providing feedback for. This will help us track your feedback correctly.

### Event Date (question1)
* **event_date** (`question1`, date, *): Enter the date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format.

### Feedback Level (feedback_level)
* **feedback_level** (`feedback_level`, select_one, *): Rate your feedback level for the event:
	+ Very Good
	+ Average
	+ Not good

### Feedback Text (question3)
* **feedback_text** (`question3`, text, *): Share your feedback or comments about the event.

### Email (question4)
* **email** (`question4`, email, *): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up questions.
