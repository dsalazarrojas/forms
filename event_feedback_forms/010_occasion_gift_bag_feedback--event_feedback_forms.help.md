# Occasion Gift Bag Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the occasion gift bag provided to customers or clients. Your input will help us understand their experiences and identify areas of improvement to better serve them in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. If you have any trouble or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback on Swag** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_1`, text, required): Describe your overall experience with the occasion gift bag, including its quality, relevance, and overall impression.
* **Gift Bag** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_2`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the gift bag's contents, including any items or products you received.
* **Overall Experience** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_3`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options to describe your overall experience:
	+ Gifted
	+ Not Gifted
	+ Other
* **Will Use Again** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_4`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether or not you will use the gift bag again, with the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Will Recommend** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_5`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options to indicate if you would recommend the gift bag:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_6`, number, required): Provide a numerical value between 1 and 10 to rate your overall satisfaction with the gift bag.
* **Why Not** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_7`, text, optional): If you selected "Not Gifted" for the "Overall Experience" question, please provide a brief explanation on why you didn't feel gifted.
* **How Likely Recommend** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_8`, time, required): Choose a time interval to indicate how likely you would recommend the gift bag to others:
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Often
	+ Always

## Tips
Please be honest and specific in your responses. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
