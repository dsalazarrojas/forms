# love_celebration_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from event attendees on their experience and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a brief description of your event impression in the Event Impression field.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale in the Overall Rating field.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in the Suggestion field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Impression (Event Impression)** (`event_impression`, note, required: false): A brief description of your experience at the event.
* **Overall Rating (Overall Rating)** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale.
* **Suggestion (Suggestion)** (`suggestion`, text, required: false): Provide specific feedback or ideas for improvement.
