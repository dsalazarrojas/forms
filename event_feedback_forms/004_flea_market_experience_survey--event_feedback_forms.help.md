# Flea Market Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and experiences from flea market attendees. The goal is to gather information on the overall experience of the event, including the event date, category, organizer, and any suggestions for improvement. This data will help us understand the effectiveness of our flea market events and identify areas for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Select the date of the flea market event you attended from the calendar.
3. Choose the category of the event from the options provided.
4. Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text field.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: Select the date you attended the flea market event. This is a calendar date picker field where you can choose the correct date from the provided options.
* **Event Category**: Choose the category that best describes the type of event you attended. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer who hosted the flea market.
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions or feedback about the event in the text field. This is an open-ended field where you can provide any comments or ideas you may have.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when rating your overall experience.
- Use clear and concise language when providing suggestions.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please let us know and we will be happy to assist.
- Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our flea market events.
