# Mobile Bartending Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Bartending Service Feedback Survey is used to gather information about events that have taken place or are scheduled to take place, and the level of service quality provided by the mobile bartenders. The feedback collected will help the company to improve its services and better understand customer needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the details of the event in the first page.
2. Answer the questions about the event and the service quality provided.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any feedback or suggestions.
4. Click on the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`): Enter the email of the event organizer.
* **Mobile Bartender Name** (`mobile_bartender_name`): Enter the name of the mobile bartender who served you.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`): Rate the service quality provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Comments** (`rating_comments`): Enter any comments about the rating you gave.
* **Satisfaction Comments** (`satisfaction_comments`): Enter any comments about your overall satisfaction with the service.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional feedback or suggestions you have.
* **Testimonials** (`testimonials`): Enter any testimonials or comments about the service.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click on this button to send your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for any further feedback.
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`): Enter the name of the venue where the event took place.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Select the type of event (e.g. Wedding, Birthday, Corporate, Graduation).
* **Event Size** (`event_size`): Select the size of the event (e.g. Small, Medium, Large).
* **Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`): Select how often you have this type of event (e.g. One-time, Regular, Irregular).
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`): Rate the service quality provided on a scale of 1-4 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`): Rate the communication quality provided on a scale of 1-4 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service on a scale of 1-4 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Will Choose Mobile Bartender Again** (`will_choose_mobile_bartender_again`): Select if you will choose the mobile bartending service again.
* **Will Recommend** (`would_recommend`): Select if you will recommend the mobile bartending service to others.

Note: All fields are required to be filled in to ensure that we can process your feedback accurately.
