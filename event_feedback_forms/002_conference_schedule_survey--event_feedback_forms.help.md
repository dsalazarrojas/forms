# Conference Schedule Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Conference Schedule Survey is a feedback form designed to gather information from attendees about their experience with the conference schedule. The survey consists of several questions that help us understand what worked well, what could be improved, and what suggestions you might have for future conferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the session you attended from the list provided.
2. Rate the session on a scale of 1 to 5.
3. Provide any comments or feedback you have about the session.
4. Indicate your preference for schedule changes, such as more sessions on the same day or longer breaks between sessions.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference on a scale of 1 to 5.
6. Indicate whether you would recommend the conference to a colleague.
7. Provide any suggestions for future conferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Please provide your name as the attendee of the session.
* **Attendee email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): If you would like us to follow up with you regarding your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Session attended** (`session_attended`, select_one, required): Select the session you are reviewing from the list provided.
* **Session rating** (`session_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the session on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Session comments** (`session_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback you have about the session.
* **Scheduling preference** (`scheduling_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose which schedule change would improve your experience.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Would recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the conference to a colleague.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions you might have for future conferences.
