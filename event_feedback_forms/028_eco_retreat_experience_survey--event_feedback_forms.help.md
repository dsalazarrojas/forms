# Eco Retreat Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Retreat Experience Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience at the Eco Retreat event. Your responses will help us understand what worked well and what didn't, allowing us to refine the event for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each field honestly, thinking back to your experience at the Eco Retreat.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your responses, especially in the `comments` field.
3. Ensure you've answered all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Introduction**
*   <label>Introduction</label> (`<type>note</type>`): This field is a space for you to briefly describe your experience at the Eco Retreat. Please feel free to share any standout moments, challenges, or concerns you had during your stay.

### 2. **Overall Experience**
*   <label>Overall Experience</label> (`<type>number</type>`): Rate your overall experience at the Eco Retreat on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Fair" and 5 is "Excellent".

### 3. **Event Details**
*   <label>Event Details</label> (`<type>text</type>`): Share your thoughts about the organization, logistics, and general atmosphere of the event.

### 4. **Food**
*   <label>Food</label> (`<type>select_multiple</type>`): Indicate which of the following applied to your experience with food at the Eco Retreat:
    - Yes
    - No

### 5. **Comments**
*   <label>Comments</label> (`<type>text</type>`): Share any additional thoughts or feedback you have about your experience at the Eco Retreat. This is your chance to elaborate on your responses to previous questions or bring up any issues you didn't mention elsewhere.

### 6. **Rating**
*   <label>Rating</label> (`<type>select_one</type>`): Rate the Eco Retreat on a scale from 1 (Fair) to 5 (Excellent).

### 7. **Recommendations**
*   <label>Recommendations</label> (`<type>text</type>`): Suggest any improvements or changes that you think would enhance the Eco Retreat experience for future participants.

### 8. **Email**
*   <label>Email</label> (`<type>email</type>`): Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback or to receive updates about future Eco Retreat events.

### 9. **Contact**
*   <label>Contact</label> (`<type>text</type>`): Provide any contact information you'd like to share, such as your phone number or a link to your social media profile.

### 10. **Submission Date**
*   <label>Submission Date</label> (`<type>date</type>`): This field is automatically populated and shows the date and time you submitted your form.

### 11. **Submission Time**
*   <label>Submission Time</label> (`<type>time</type>`): Similar to `Submission Date`, this field is also automatically populated and shows the time you submitted your form.

### 12. **Participant Name**
*   <label>Participant Name</label> (`<type>text</type>`): This is automatically populated with the name you used to submit the form and serves as a unique identifier for your feedback.

Please note that the form fields are marked as "required" or "optional". If a field is marked as "required", please ensure you provide a response before submitting the form.
