# gala_dinner_fundraising_event_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from event attendees after a gala dinner fundraising event. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand how well the event met your expectations and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your honest feedback.
2. Choose your rating for the event overall and specific aspects such as food, venue, and program.
3. Select the correct event host from the options provided.
4. Choose all that apply for food quality and event venue.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event. This helps us understand how well we met your expectations.
* **Event Details**: Please describe your experience at the event. This will help us understand what we can improve on for future events.
* **Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the following aspects of the event: food, venue, event host, and program. This will help us prioritize areas for improvement.
* **Event Host**: Please select the name of the event host who greeted you and provided assistance during the event. This helps us ensure that our event hosts are trained to provide the best experience for you.
* **Food Quality**: Choose all that apply for food quality: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Event Venue**: Choose all that apply for event venue: The Ritz, The Plaza, or The Park.
* **Event Program**: Please add any additional comments or feedback about the event program. This will help us refine our program for future events.
* **Event Organizer**: If you have any feedback for the event organizer, please provide it in this field.
* **Comments**: If you have any general comments or suggestions, please add them in this field.
* **Event Attendee**: Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you for any additional information or follow up on your feedback.
* **Event Phone**: This field is for additional contact information. Please provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Event Address**: Please provide your event address to help us understand where you are located.
* **Event Date**: This field is for the date of the event you attended. Please provide the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Time**: This field is for the time of the event you attended. Please provide the time in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Event Duration**: Rate the length of the event. This will help us ensure that future events are well-paced.
* **Event Host**: Please select the name of the event host who greeted you and provided assistance during the event. This helps us ensure that our event hosts are trained to provide the best experience for you.
* **Event Organiser**: Please provide any additional feedback for the event organizer. This will help us refine our event planning for future events.
