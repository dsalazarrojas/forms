# promoter_daily_activity_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your daily activities related to events, helping us improve our services and event management system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the **Event Type** that best describes the event you were involved in (Event or Non-Event).
2. Choose the **Activity Type** that matches your daily activities (On-Site, Off-Site, or Hybrid).
3. Select all the **Activity Locations** that apply to your daily activities (On-Site, Off-Site, Hybrid, Other, or Unknown).
4. Provide detailed **Activity Details** about your activities, including any relevant notes.
5. If applicable, enter the **Activity Duration** in hours.
6. Enter the **Promoter Team** members involved in the event.
7. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** for contact purposes.
8. Enter the **Event ID** of the event you were involved in (if applicable).
9. Add any **Event Notes** about the event.
10. Add any additional **Notes** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Activity**: A text field for you to describe your daily activities.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you were involved in (Event or Non-Event).
* **Activity Type**: Select the type of activity you performed (On-Site, Off-Site, or Hybrid).
* **Activity Location**: Select all the locations where you performed your daily activities (On-Site, Off-Site, Hybrid, Other, or Unknown).
* **Activity Details**: Provide a detailed description of your daily activities.
* **Activity Duration**: If applicable, enter the duration of your activities in hours.
* **Promoter Team**: Enter the names of the team members who were involved in the event.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Event ID**: Enter the event ID number (if applicable).
* **Event Notes**: Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information you may have.

Note: This form is a critical component of our event management system, and your input is invaluable for improving our services and ensuring a smooth event execution.
