# Reminder Setup Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reminder Setup Survey is a form designed to collect feedback about events. Its purpose is to help us understand your experience with events and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Reminder Setup Survey" page with your thoughts and comments.
2. On the next page, select the relevant fields from the list to create your feedback report.
3. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reminder Setup Survey** (`reminder_setup_survey`, optional): 
    This field is for providing feedback about events. You can type in any comments, suggestions, or thoughts you have about the events you attended. Keep it brief and to the point.
* **event_feedback_form_fields** (event_feedback_form_fields, optional): 
    Choose the specific fields you want to include in your event feedback report. Select the ones that are relevant to your experience.
* **form_fields** (form_fields, optional): 
    This field is currently not in use.
