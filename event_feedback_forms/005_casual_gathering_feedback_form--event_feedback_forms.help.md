# Casual Gathering Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casual Gathering Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from event attendees to help hosts and organizers improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions that apply to your event experience.
2. Select the options that best reflect your opinion or experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Optional - your name): Please enter your name, if you'd like to be associated with your feedback.
* **Email Address** (For follow-up if needed): Enter a valid email address for follow-up or communication.
* **Event Date** (Date of the gathering you attended): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Type of Gathering** (Type of event was this): Select the type of event you attended, such as "Birthday Party", "Housewarming", "Casual Get-together", etc.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (How would you rate the event overall): Rate the overall experience, from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Venue Rating** (Rate the venue or location): Rate the venue or location, from "Excellent" to "Not Applicable" (if not applicable).
* **Food Quality** (Rate the food provided): Rate the food provided, from "Excellent" to "Not Provided".
* **Drinks Quality** (Rate the beverages provided): Rate the drinks provided, from "Excellent" to "Not Provided".
* **Atmosphere Rating** (Rate the overall atmosphere): Rate the overall atmosphere, from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Music and Entertainment** (Rate the music or entertainment): Rate the music or entertainment, from "Excellent" to "Not Applicable" (if not applicable).
* **What Did You Enjoy Most** (Highlight of the event): Describe the highlight of the event, what you enjoyed most about it.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (What could be improved): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Would You Attend Again** (Would you come to a similar event): Select whether you would attend a similar event again.
* **Feedback for the Host** (Any comments for the host): Provide any comments or feedback for the host.
* **Additional Comments** (Any other thoughts or feedback): Provide any additional thoughts or feedback.
