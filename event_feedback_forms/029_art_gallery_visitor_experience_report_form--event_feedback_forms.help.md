# Art Gallery Visitor Experience Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Visitor Experience Report Form is designed to gather feedback from visitors to understand their overall experience, satisfaction, and preferences. It's used to improve the gallery's services, exhibitions, and facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date you visited the gallery.
2. Provide your name (optional).
3. Enter your email address (optional).
4. Choose the type of visitor you are (e.g., first-time, repeat, member, tourist, etc.).
5. Select who you visited with (e.g., alone, with partner, with friends, etc.).
6. Choose the size of your group.
7. Select the exhibition you visited.
8. Rate the overall satisfaction with your visit.
9. Rate the quality of the exhibition.
10. Rate the selection of artwork.
11. Rate the layout and flow of the exhibition.
12. Rate the lighting.
13. Rate the clarity of signs and labels.
14. Rate the friendliness of the staff.
15. Rate the knowledge of the staff.
16. Rate the cleanliness of the facility.
17. Rate the comfort level.
18. Rate the value for money.
19. Select your favorite exhibit.
20. Select what you liked least.
21. Choose the amenities you used.
22. Rate the amenities provided.
23. State if you would recommend the gallery.
24. Choose how likely you are to return.
25. Suggest improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visit Date**: Date of your visit.
	+ Enter the date you visited the gallery.
* **Visitor Name**: Your name (optional).
	+ If you'd like to provide your name for future reference.
* **Visitor Email**: Email for follow-up (optional).
	+ Enter your email address for any follow-up or special promotions.
* **Visitor Type**: Type of visitor.
	+ Choose how you're categorizing yourself (first-time, repeat, member, tourist, etc.).
* **Group Type**: Who did you visit with.
	+ Select who you visited with (e.g., alone, with partner, with friends, etc.).
* **Group Size**: Number of people in your group.
	+ Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Exhibition Visited**: Which exhibition you saw.
	+ Select the specific exhibition you visited.
* **Visit Duration**: How long you spent.
	+ Choose the time you spent at the gallery (less than 30 minutes to more than 3 hours).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction.
	+ Rate how satisfied you were with your visit.
* **Exhibition Quality**: Rate the quality of the exhibition.
	+ Rate how good the exhibition was.
* **Artwork Selection**: Rate the selection of artwork.
	+ Rate how well the artwork was presented.
* **Exhibition Layout**: Rate the layout and flow of the exhibition.
	+ Rate how well the gallery was organized.
* **Lighting Quality**: Rate the lighting.
	+ Rate the quality of the lighting.
* **Signage Clarity**: Rate the clarity of signs and labels.
	+ Rate how clear and easy to read the signs were.
* **Staff Friendliness**: Rate the friendliness of the staff.
	+ Rate how friendly and helpful the staff were.
* **Staff Knowledge**: Rate the knowledge of the staff.
	+ Rate how knowledgeable the staff were.
* **Facility Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness.
	+ Rate how clean the facility was.
* **Comfort Level**: Rate your comfort level.
	+ Rate how comfortable you felt in the gallery.
* **Value for Money**: Rate the value for money.
	+ Rate how good a value you felt you got for your money.
* **Favorite Exhibit**: What was your favorite piece or section.
	+ Select your favorite piece or section.
* **Least Favorite Exhibit**: What did you like least.
	+ Select what you liked least.
* **Amenities Used**: Select amenities you used.
	+ Choose which amenities you used (e.g., gift shop, cafe, etc.).
* **Amenities Rating**: Rate the amenities.
	+ Rate the quality of the amenities provided.
* **Would Recommend**: Would you recommend this gallery?
	+ Choose if you would recommend the gallery.
* **Likelihood to Return**: How likely are you to return.
	+ Choose how likely you are to visit again.
* **Improvements Suggested**: What could be improved.
	+ Suggest any improvements or changes to the gallery.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback.
	+ Add any additional comments or suggestions.
