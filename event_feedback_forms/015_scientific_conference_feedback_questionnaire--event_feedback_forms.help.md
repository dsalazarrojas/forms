# Scientific Conference Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from conference attendees about their experience at the scientific conference. This will help us improve future conferences and provide better services to attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information, including email, phone number, name, affiliation, and job title.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference on a scale of 1-5.
3. Rate the quality of the sessions you attended, including the speakers and their performances.
4. Choose the options that best describe the quality of the venue and networking opportunities.
5. Provide additional comments about your experience.
6. Enter the name of the conference you attended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the conference you attended.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, 1-5): Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference on a scale of 1-5.
* **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, number, 1-5): Rate the quality of the sessions you attended, including the speakers and their performances.
* **Session Interest** (`session_interest`, number, 1-5): Rate your interest in the sessions you attended.
* **Speaker Rating** (`speaker_rating`, number, 1-5): Rate the quality of the speakers and their performances.
* **Speaker Interest** (`speaker_interest`, number, 1-5): Rate your interest in the speakers and their topics.
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the venue.
* **Catering** (`catering`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the options that best describe the quality of the catering:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Networking** (`networking`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best describes your experience with networking:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Conference Attendance** (`conference_attendance`, number, optional): Choose the options that best describe your conference attendance:
	+ Option 1: First-time attendee
	+ Option 2: Attended previously
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, text, optional): Describe the length of the sessions you attended.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the session you attended.
* **Attendee Affiliation** (`attendee_affiliation`, text, optional): Enter your affiliation.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Attendee Organizations** (`attendee_organizations`, text, optional): Enter the organizations you are affiliated with.
* **Attended Previous Conferences** (`attended_previous_conferences`, text, optional): Describe if you have attended previous conferences.
* **Recommend Conference** (`recommend_conference`, text, optional): Describe if you would recommend this conference.
* **Attended Session** (`attended_session`, text, optional): Describe if you attended any sessions.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, text, optional): Describe the length of the sessions you attended.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback about your experience.

Note: Some fields are optional, and you can skip them if you don't have the information.
