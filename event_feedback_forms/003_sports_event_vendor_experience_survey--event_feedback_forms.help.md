# Sports Event Vendor Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from vendors on their experience with sports events. The information collected will help event organizers understand the strengths and weaknesses of the event from the vendor's perspective, ultimately informing decisions to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to complete all fields that are marked as "Required".
3. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Describe the event you participated in, including the location, date, and any notable details.
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, optional): Share any relevant information about yourself or your company, such as your name, company name, or contact details.
* **Event Outcome** (`event_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the outcome of the event, including any successes or challenges you experienced.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the event on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Select Outcomes** (`select_outcomes`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the available options, which may include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you participated in.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you participated in.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience with the event.
