# end_of_year_party_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "end_of_year_party_feedback_form" is designed to collect feedback from employees on the end-of-year party organized by the company. This form will help the party planning committee understand what kind of party theme, music, and food options employees would enjoy the most, as well as gather information on the event location, date and time, and any additional feedback or comments employees may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the theme for the party from the provided options.
2. Choose your music preferences for the party.
3. Enter the location where the party will be held.
4. Choose the date and time for the party.
5. Select the food options you would like to have at the party.
6. If you have any additional feedback or comments, type them in the "Feedback" field.
7. Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any further details.
8. Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Theme** (Yes/No, required): Select the party theme you would like to see at the party.
* **Music Preferences** (Yes/No, required): Choose your music preferences for the party.
* **Event Location** (Text, required): Enter the location where the party will be held.
* **Date and Time** (Date, required): Choose the date and time for the party.
* **Food Options** (Yes/No, required): Select the food options you would like to have at the party.
* **Feedback** (Note, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them here.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any further details.
* **Phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
