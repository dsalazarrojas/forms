# Real Time Feedback Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect real-time feedback from users about a specific event. It is intended to gather information about their experience, satisfaction, and potential suggestions or ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Select the Event Date (optional but recommended for accuracy).
4. Provide Feedback about your experience.
5. Choose your Satisfaction level (e.g., Not Satisfied, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied).
6. Optionally, provide a Suggestion.
7. Enter your Phone number (optional).
8. Rate your experience (select one or multiple options e.g., Not Satisfied, Satisfied, Very Satisfied).
9. Select your Time Zone (Pacific Time (US & Canada), Central Time (US & Canada), Eastern Time (US & Canada), Atlantic Time (Canada), Australia Time (Eastern), Australia Time (Central), Australia Time (Western)).
10. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`<name>`, `text`, `true`/`false`): Your first name is required.
* **Email** (`<name>`, `email`, `true`/`false`): Your email address is required for communication purposes.
* **Event Date** (`<name>`, `date`, `false`/`true`): Select the date of the event you are providing feedback on (optional but recommended).
* **Feedback** (`<name>`, `note`, `true`/`false`): Provide a detailed description of your experience (required).
* **Satisfaction** (`<name>`, `select_one`, `true`/`false`): Choose your satisfaction level about the event (e.g., Not Satisfied, Satisfied, Very Satisfied).
* **Suggestion** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`/`true`): Optionally, provide a suggestion or comment about the event.
* **Phone** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`/`true`): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted (optional).
* **Rating** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, `true`/`false`): Rate your experience with one or multiple options (e.g., Not Satisfied, Satisfied, Very Satisfied).
* **Time** (`<name>`, `time`, `false`/`true`): Select the time of the event you are providing feedback on (optional).
* **Time Zone** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, `true`/`false`): Select your time zone (Pacific Time (US & Canada), Central Time (US & Canada), Eastern Time (US & Canada), Atlantic Time (Canada), Australia Time (Eastern), Australia Time (Central), Australia Time (Western)).

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please provide clear and concise descriptions.
* Remember to select the correct Time Zone for your location.
