# Hallowfest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback from attendees on their Hallowfest experience. Your input will help us improve and make next year's event even more enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely, including all the fields, as this will provide us with a more comprehensive understanding of your experience.
2.  Make sure to submit the form once you have finished filling out all the fields.
3.  If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Event Name**
`event_name`, text, required: false

*   This is the name of the event you attended. Please enter the name as it was officially named by the event organizers.

### 2. **Date Attended**
`date_attended`, date, required: false

*   This field is for the date when you attended the event. Please use the date you were present at the event, not when you were registered.

### 3. **Time Spent**
`time_spent`, number, required: false

*   Please provide the number of hours or minutes you spent at the event. Be as precise as possible for our record-keeping purposes.

### 4. **Favorite Part**
`favorite_part`, select_multiple, options: (Pumpkin Patch, Haunted House, Face Painting, Food and Drinks)

*   Choose the activity or area that you enjoyed the most during the event.

### 5. **Rating**
`rating`, select_one, required: false

*   Rate your overall experience at the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 6. **Comments**
`comments`, note, required: false

*   Provide any comments or suggestions you have about the event. This is a great opportunity to share your thoughts and ideas with us.

### 7. **Contact E-mail**
`contact_email`, email, required: false

*   If you want to get updates or news about future events, please provide your contact email address.

### 8. **Contact Phone**
`contact_phone`, text, required: false

*   If you want to share your phone number, please do so if you're willing to be contacted about future events or have any urgent questions.

Please let us know if you need help or clarification on anything!
