# Contest Scoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Contest Scoring Survey" is a form designed for judges to score and provide feedback on contestants who participated in events such as art, photography, writing, music, dance, speech, innovation, and sports competitions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and assigned judge ID in the "Judge Name" and "Judge ID" fields.
2. Enter the contest name and date in the "Contest Name" and "Contest Date" fields.
3. Select the category of the contest from the "Category" field.
4. Enter the contestant's name and assigned number in the "Contantant Name" and "Contestant Number" fields.
5. Score the contestant's technical skill, creativity, presentation, originality, and overall impression from 1 to 10 in the corresponding fields.
6. Select which scoring criteria were applied from the "Scoring Criteria Used" field.
7. Enter any areas for improvement and additional comments in the "Areas for Improvement" and "Additional Comments" fields (optional).
8. Provide a recommendation for the contestant's advancement by selecting "Highly Recommend", "Recommend", "Neutral", or "Do not Recommend" in the "Recommendation" field.
9. Enter your name as a signature in the "Judge Signature" field.
10. Enter the time of scoring submission in the "Submission Time" field.
