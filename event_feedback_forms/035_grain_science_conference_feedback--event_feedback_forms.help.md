# grain_science_conference_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

The grain science conference feedback form is designed to gather detailed information from attendees about their experience at the conference. The collected data will be used to improve future conferences, enhance attendee satisfaction, and facilitate better decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are an attendee of the conference. If you have not attended, please do not complete this form.
2. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
3. Select the correct session attended from the options provided.
4. Provide honest and detailed ratings and comments for each of the rating fields.
5. Complete all required fields to ensure your feedback is actionable and helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Information (attendee_info)**: Enter your name and any relevant contact information to help us contact you if needed.
* **Rate Conference (rating)**: Rate the conference on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about the conference.
* **Suggestions**: Suggest any improvements or changes you would like to see in future conferences.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Notes (feedback)**: Share any additional notes or details about your experience.
* **Date**: Enter the date you attended the conference.
* **Time**: Enter the time you attended the conference.
* **Session Attended (session_attended)**: Select the session you attended from the options provided.
* **Rating Agenda (rating_agenda)**: Rate the agenda of the conference on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Rating Materials (rating_materials)**: Rate the materials provided during the conference on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Rating Speaker (rating_speaker)**: Rate the speaker's performance on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Rating Facilities (rating_facilities)**: Rate the conference facilities on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Rating Food and Beverage (rating_food_and_beverage)**: Rate the food and beverage provided at the conference on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Rating Travel (rating_travel)**: Rate the travel arrangements on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Rating Vendors (rating_vendors)**: Rate the vendors on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Overall Rating (rating_overall)**: Rate your overall conference experience on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Comments (comments)**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about the conference.
* **Suggestions (suggestions)**: Suggest any improvements or changes you would like to see in future conferences.
* **Overall Comments (comments_overall)**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about the conference.
* **Overall Suggestions (suggestions_overall)**: Suggest any improvements or changes you would like to see in future conferences.

Note: The required fields are marked as such in the form, and it's essential to complete all of them to provide valuable feedback.
