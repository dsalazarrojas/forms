# Festival Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees about their experience at the festival. We value your input and use it to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select all that apply for the "What Did You Like" and "Favorite Food" questions.
3. If you have any improvement ideas, please type them in the "Improvement Ideas" field.
4. If you have any comments, please type them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rate Your Experience** (`attendee_rating`, text, optional): Rate your overall experience at the festival on a scale of 1-5.
* **What Did You Like** (`favorite_aspects`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the aspects you enjoyed about the festival, such as the food, music, or activities.
* **Do You Have Any Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, note, optional): Share any suggestions you have for how we can improve future events.
* **What Was Your Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite food from the list of options.
* **Who Was Your Favorite Band** (`favorite_band`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite band from the list of options.
* **What Was Your Overall Experience Like** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): Describe your overall experience at the festival in a few sentences.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the festival.
* **Contact Information** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted with updates, enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, enter your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the person or department this feedback is assigned to.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select how you submitted your feedback.
