# Theme Park Attraction Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theme Park Attraction Satisfaction Survey aims to gather feedback from visitors on their experiences with theme park attractions. This information will help us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each field.
2. Answer the questions accurately and truthfully.
3. Fill in all required fields. If a field is not marked as required, you can leave it blank if you're not willing to share that information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Theme Park Attraction (Page Ten, Label: Theme Park attraction name)**: This question asks for the name of the attraction you visited.
  * Select one of the options: Theme Park 1, Theme Park 2, or Theme Park 3.
* **Theme Park Attraction (Page Twenty One, Label: Theme Park attraction name)**: This question also asks for the name of the attraction you visited.
  * Select one of the options: Theme Park 1, Theme Park 2, or Theme Park 3.
* **Date of Visit (Page Twenty Three, Label: Date of visit)**: This question asks for the date of your visit.
  * Use the date format you're familiar with (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of Visit (Page Twenty Four, Label: Time of visit)**: This question asks for the time of your visit.
  * Use the time format you're familiar with (e.g., HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Comments (Page Four, Label: Comments)**: This is an open-ended question where you can share your thoughts, feelings, or suggestions about your experience.
* **Contact Info (Page Fifteen, Label: Contact Info)**: This field is for providing your contact information if you're willing to share it.
* **Email (Page Eighteen, Label: Email)**: This field is for entering your email address.
* **Phone (Page Seventeen, Label: Phone)**: This field is for entering your phone number.
* **Attraction Rating (Page Three, Label: Attraction rating)**: This question asks for your rating of the attraction on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Did you enjoy the experience? (Page Fourteen, Label: Did you enjoy the experience?)**: This question asks for your rating of your experience on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Would you visit again? (Page Fifteen, Label: Would you visit again?)**: This question asks if you would visit the attraction again.
* **Theme Park 1, 2, or 3 (Page Twenty One, Label: Theme Park attraction name)**: This question asks for the name of the attraction you visited.
  * Select one of the options: Theme Park 1, Theme Park 2, or Theme Park 3.
* **Theme Park 1, 2, or 3 (Page Twenty Two, Label: Theme Park attraction name)**: This question also asks for the name of the attraction you visited.
  * Select one of the options: Theme Park 1, Theme Park 2, or Theme Park 3.

Note: If a field is not marked as required, you can leave it blank if you're not willing to share that information.
