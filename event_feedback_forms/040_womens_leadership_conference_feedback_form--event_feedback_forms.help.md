# Womens Leadership Conference Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees of the Women's Leadership Conference. This feedback will help us evaluate the event's success, identify areas for improvement, and plan future events that meet the needs and expectations of our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Email address, which will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
3. Rate your overall experience with the conference using the rating options provided.
4. Select the categories that apply to your feedback, such as Keynote Speaker, Food and Beverage, or Overall Experience.
5. Type your comments or suggestions in the Feedback field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is displayed on your badge or identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is displayed on your badge or identification.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you about your feedback.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the conference using the options: Excellent, Good, or Fair.
* **Category**: Select all the categories that apply to your feedback, such as Keynote Speaker, Food and Beverage, or Overall Experience.
	+ Keynote Speaker: Rate the Keynote Speaker's performance and provide comments if needed.
	+ Food and Beverage: Rate the quality and variety of food and beverages provided.
	+ Overall Experience: Rate the overall experience of the conference, including content, logistics, and atmosphere.
* **Feedback**: Write any additional comments or suggestions you have about the conference. This is your chance to provide us with any details that didn't fit into the rating and category questions.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to provide us with useful feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the Feedback field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
