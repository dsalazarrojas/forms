# Service And Hospitality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of service and hospitality provided during an event. The information collected will be used to improve the services and experiences offered by the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your abilities.
2. Use the provided fields to submit any suggestions or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (event_details, note, optional): This field is a text box where you can provide any relevant details about the event.
* **Service Experience** (service_experience, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the service provided during the event, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Hospitality Experience** (hospitality_experience, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the staff, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Suggestions** (suggestions, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best describes the kind of suggestion you have for us.
* **Service Quality** (service_quality, number, optional): Rate the quality of the service provided during the event, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Event Rating** (event_rating, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the event, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Staff Helpfulness** (staff_helpfulness, number, optional): Rate the helpfulness of the staff, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Staff Welcoming** (staff_welcoming, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if the staff was welcoming, otherwise choose 'No'.
* **Event Facilities** (event_facilities, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the facilities that best describe your experience with the event facilities.
* **Staff Helpfulness Scale** (staff_helpfulness_scale, number, optional): Rate the helpfulness of the staff on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (comments, select_one, optional): Choose how useful the event was to you.
* **Event Recommend** (event_recommend, select_one, optional): Choose if you would recommend this event to others.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, optional): Enter your name, if you want to be contacted.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted.
* **Event Name** (event_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Event Start** (event_start, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event you attended.
* **Event End** (event_end, time, optional): Enter the end time of the event you attended.
* **Form Submission** (form_submission, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Form Submission 2** (form_submission_1, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Form Submission 3** (form_submission_2, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Form Submission 4** (form_submission_3, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Form Submission 5** (form_submission_4, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, use the provided fields to submit them.
* If you want to be contacted, please enter your name, email, and/or phone number.
