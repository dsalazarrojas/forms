# Reception Entertainment Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from event attendees after a reception. It will help us understand how our guests perceived the entertainment provided during the event. We recommend filling this out only if you have participated in this specific reception.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that you have attended the reception for which this form is intended.
2. Answer the questions on each page as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. If you have any further comments or feedback, please include them in the "Other comments or feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Entertainment** (`page_2`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This field asks you to select if the entertainment offered during the reception was satisfactory or not. Choose 'Yes' if you enjoyed the entertainment, or 'No' if you didn't.
* **Select Another Option** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ This field is for selecting any other entertainment options offered at the reception that you didn't select as 'Yes', but still attended. Choose 'Yes' for each option you attended.
* **How many of these options did you choose?** (`page_4`, `number`, **required**):
	+ Please indicate how many of the options you selected as 'Yes' in the previous field.
* **How likely are you to recommend this event?** (`page_6`, `number`, **required**):
	+ Rate how likely you are to recommend this reception to others, with higher numbers indicating a higher likelihood of recommendation.
* **Other comments or feedback** (`page_5`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ If you have any other comments or feedback about the reception, please type them here.
* **Arrival time** (`page_7`, `time`, **optional**):
	+ Please enter the time you arrived at the reception.
* **Departure time** (`page_8`, `time`, **optional**):
	+ Please enter the time you left the reception.

## Tips
* Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask a staff member for clarification.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
