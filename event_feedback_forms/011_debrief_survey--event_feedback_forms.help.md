# Debrief Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This debrief survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from attendees of events, meetings, or training sessions. Your input is valuable to us in improving our events and making them more valuable for future attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as thoroughly and honestly as possible.
2. Select the rating for Overall Effectiveness Rating from 1 to 5.
3. Submit your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
4. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Feedback Form** (`event_feedback_form`, text, **optional**): 
    This is where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the event.
* **What Went Well** (`what_went_well`, text, **optional**): 
    Share with us what you felt went well or was beneficial during the event.
* **What to Improve** (`what_to_improve`, text, **optional**): 
    Suggest areas for improvement for future events or sessions.
* **Overall Effectiveness Rating** (`overall_effectiveness_rating`, select_one, **required if other fields are filled**): 
    Rate the overall effectiveness of the event on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, **optional**): 
    Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the event.

## Tips

* Please be as specific and concrete as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for future events, please let us know.
* Your input is greatly appreciated and helps us improve our events.
