# Road Safety Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Road Safety Workshop Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with the road safety workshop. This form helps us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the first text field.
2. Enter your last name in the second text field.
3. Provide your email address in the third text field, in the format `username@example.com`.
4. Select the date of the workshop you attended in the calendar format.
5. Choose the time you attended the workshop (e.g., morning, afternoon).
6. Rate your overall experience with the workshop as either "Good" or "Fair" in the drop-down menu.
7. Write any specific feedback you have about the workshop in the text area below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (1)
• **First Name** (text, required): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.

### Last Name (2)
• **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.

### Email (3)
• **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com` to facilitate communication.

### Event Date (4)
• **Event Date** (date): Select the date of the workshop you attended. If you're unsure, check your calendar or contact us for clarification.

### Time of Day (5)
• **Time of Day** (time): Choose the time of day you attended the workshop (e.g., morning, afternoon).

### Rating (6)
• **Rating** (select one, optional): Rate your overall experience with the workshop as either "Good" or "Fair".

### Feedback (7)
• **Feedback** (text, required): Describe any specific feedback or suggestions you have about the workshop.

### Suggestions for Improvement (8)
• **Suggestions for Improvement** (text, optional): If you have any ideas on how we can improve future workshops, please share them here.
