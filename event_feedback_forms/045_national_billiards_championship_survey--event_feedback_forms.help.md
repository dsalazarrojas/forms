# National Billiards Championship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from various stakeholders about the National Billiards Championship event. Your input will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each field with your thoughts and experiences about the National Billiards Championship event.
2. Make sure to submit your feedback before leaving the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, Text, Optional): This field is for you to enter the name of the event you are providing feedback about.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, Select One, Optional): Please select the type of event you participated in or observed. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date, Optional): If you remember the date of the event, please enter it here.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, Time, Optional): If you remember the time of the event, please enter it here.
* **Player Feedback** (`player_feedback`, Text, Optional): Share your thoughts about the player experience. What did we do well? What could we improve?
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, Text, Optional): Share your thoughts about the staff and their performance. What did we do well? What could we improve?
* **Sponsor Feedback** (`sponsor_feedback`, Text, Optional): Share your thoughts about the sponsors and their support. What did we do well? What could we improve?
* **Fan Feedback** (`fan_feedback`, Text, Optional): Share your thoughts about the fans' experience. What did we do well? What could we improve?
* **Event Suggestions** (`event_suggestions`, Text, Optional): If you have any suggestions for future events, please enter them here.

## Tips
Please be honest and thorough with your feedback. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
