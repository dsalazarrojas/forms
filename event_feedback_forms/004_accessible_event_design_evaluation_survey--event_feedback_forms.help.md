# Accessible Event Design Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to evaluate the accessibility of an event. Your feedback will help the event organizers identify areas of improvement to make future events more accessible and enjoyable for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and fill out the relevant fields. Each page is labeled with a header, and each field has a label and an option list. Please select the option that best describes your experience or preference. You are not required to fill out every field, but providing as much information as possible will help us understand your experience better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Optional)** (`full_name`, `text`, optional): Please provide your name for contact purposes, if you wish to share it.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive more information about future events, please provide your email address.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Please provide the name of the event you attended.
* **Date of Event** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the best description of your role or affiliation with the event: General attendee, Speaker or presenter, Volunteer, Staff or organizer, Vendor or exhibitor.
* **Do You Identify as Having a Disability or Access Need?** (`disability_or_access_need`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you identify as having a disability or access need: True, False, or Prefer not to say.
* **Rate the Physical Accessibility of the Venue (entrances, pathways, seating)** (`venue_physical_access`, `select_one`, required): Please rate the physical accessibility of the venue: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Rate the Accessibility of Restrooms** (`restroom_accessibility`, `select_one`, required): Please rate the accessibility of restrooms: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor, Did not use.
* **Rate the Signage and Wayfinding** (`signage_wayfinding`, `select_one`, required): Please rate the signage and wayfinding: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Were Event Materials Provided in Accessible Formats?** (`materials_accessibility`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether event materials were provided in accessible formats: Yes, fully accessible, Partially accessible, Not accessible, Not applicable.
* **Was Live Captioning or Sign Language Interpretation Available?** (`captioning_available`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether live captioning or sign language interpretation was available: Yes, and it was effective, Yes, but quality was poor, No, but it was needed, No, and it was not needed.
* **Were Assistive Technology or Listening Devices Available?** (`assistive_technology_support`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether assistive technology or listening devices were available: True, False, Not sure, Not needed.
* **Overall Rating of Event Accessibility** (`overall_accessibility_rating`, `select_one`, required): Please rate the overall accessibility of the event: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Did You Request Any Accommodations in Advance?** (`accommodations_requested`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you requested any accommodations in advance: Yes, and they were fully met, Yes, but they were only partially met, Yes, but they were not met, No, I did not request any.
* **Describe Any Accessibility Barriers You Encountered** (`barriers_encountered`, `text`, optional): Please describe any accessibility barriers you encountered.
* **What Improvements Would Make Future Events More Accessible?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Please provide any suggestions for improvements to make future events more accessible.
* **Would You Attend a Future Event by This Organizer?** (`would_attend_again`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you would attend a future event by this organizer: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably not, Definitely not.
