# Tournament Food Vendor Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from food vendors on their experience during the tournament event. We want to understand how well we managed the event and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Organizer Info with your contact details.
2. Provide your Food Vendor Info, including your ID, name, email, and phone number.
3. Rate your Overall Experience, using the provided scale.
4. Select the Food Quality and Temperature according to your experience.
5. Add any Comments or feedback about the event.
6. Provide a Rating for the event.
7. Fill in the Event Name, Date, and Time of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Info (1)**: Provide the contact details of the event organizer(s).
* **Food Vendor Info (2)**: Enter your food vendor information, including your ID, name, email, and phone number.
* **Overall Experience (3)**: Rate your overall experience during the event, using the provided scale.
* **Food Quality (4)**: Select the quality of the food provided to you.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Food Temperature (5)**: Select the temperature of the food provided to you.
	+ Cold
	+ Warm
	+ Hot
* **Event Info (6)**: Provide a numerical value indicating your level of satisfaction with the event.
* **Comments (7)**: Add any additional feedback or comments about the event.
* **Rating (8)**: Rate your experience with the event.
* **Vendor ID (9)**: Enter your vendor ID number.
* **Vendor Name (10)**: Enter your vendor name.
* **Vendor Email (11)**: Enter your vendor email address.
* **Vendor Phone (12)**: Enter your vendor phone number.
* **Event Name (13)**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date (14)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time (15)**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Feedback (16)**: Add any additional feedback or comments about the event.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified as optional.
