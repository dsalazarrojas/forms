# Friendsgiving Event Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from attendees of an event, helping event organizers to understand what worked well and what can be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide valuable feedback.
2. Make sure you have attended the event before submitting your feedback.
3. Select the relevant options for each question, and add any additional comments or notes if needed.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event
• **Event** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_1`, text, required): Please describe the event you attended. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
### Host
• **Host** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_2`, text, required): Please tell us about the host and their performance during the event.
### Food
• **Food** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_3`, text, required): Describe the food served at the event. Was it good, bad, or somewhere in between?
### Music
• **Music** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_4`, text, required): How would you rate the music played during the event? Was it too loud, too quiet, or just right?
### Overall
• **Overall** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_5`, select_one, required): Rate the overall experience of the event.
### How
• **How** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_6`, date, required): What date did you attend the event on?
### Was
• **Was** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_7`, time, required): What time did the event start and finish?
### Experience
• **Experience** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_8`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you had a positive or negative experience during the event.
### of
• **of** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_9`, text, required): Describe your overall experience of the event in a few words.
### the
• **the** (`friendsgiving_event_survey_10`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
