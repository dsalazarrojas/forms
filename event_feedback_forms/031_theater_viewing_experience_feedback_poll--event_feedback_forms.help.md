# Theater Viewing Experience Feedback Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their viewing experience at a theater. It allows users to rate their overall experience and provide specific feedback about what they liked or disliked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the first page for any instructions or information.
2. On the "rating" page, select how you would rate your overall viewing experience using the provided options.
3. If you have any specific feedback, please type it in the "feedback" field.
4. If you'd like to provide your contact information, please fill in the "first_name", "last_name", "email", and "phone" fields as you see fit.
5. If you have any additional feedback, please type it in the "additional_feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (Page 1): This is an introductory page that provides context and instructions for the form.
* **How would you rate your overall viewing experience?** (Page 2):
  - Select one of the provided options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What did you like/dislike about your viewing experience?** (Page 3):
  - Type any specific feedback you have about your experience.
* **First Name** (Page 4):
  - Type in your first name (if you'd like to provide it).
* **Last Name** (Page 5):
  - Type in your last name (if you'd like to provide it).
* **Email** (Page 6):
  - Type in your email address (if you'd like to provide it).
* **Phone** (Page 7):
  - Type in your phone number (if you'd like to provide it).
* **Additional Feedback** (Page 8):
  - Type in any additional feedback you have about your experience.

## Tips

- Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
- If you have a suggestion or criticism, we'd be happy to hear it.
- If you have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
- This form is for your viewing experience only. If you have other feedback or concerns, please contact us separately.
