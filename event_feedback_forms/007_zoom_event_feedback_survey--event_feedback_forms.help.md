# Zoom Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a survey designed to gather feedback on events held on Zoom. It helps us understand the effectiveness and impact of the events, which is crucial for improving future events. Your feedback will help us make informed decisions and provide better experiences for attendees and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select the correct options for each field according to your experience.
3. Make sure to fill out the form only once for each event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your Zoom profile.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Select how satisfied you were with the event, from Highly Satisfied to Not Satisfied.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the event, if you can remember it.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Share any thoughts or comments about the event. This is an open space for you to express your opinions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your Zoom profile.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to provide it.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the event.
* **Zoom Meeting ID** (`zoom_meeting_id`, text, optional): Enter the Zoom Meeting ID for the event, if you can remember it.
* **Participant Count** (`participant_count`, number, required): Select the number of attendees you had at the event.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required): Select how many attendees you had at the event, from Less than 10 to 50 or more.
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, required): Select the language used during the event, from English, Spanish, French, or Other.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, required): Select your time zone, from UTC+0, UTC-8, UTC+8, or Other.
