# lip_sync_performance_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the performance of a person in a lip sync event. It helps in evaluating and improving individual performances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the performance rating for the individual being evaluated.
2. Choose how well you think they synchronized their movements with the audio.
3. Rate their stage presence.
4. Rate their creativity.
5. Provide any comments about their performance (optional).
6. Choose how you compare their performance to others (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor).
7. Provide any additional comments about their performance (optional).
8. Enter the name of the person who assigned them to judge or evaluate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Rating** (lip_sync_rating): Rate the overall performance of the individual, considering factors such as their singing, dancing, and overall showmanship.
* **Synchronization** (synchronization): Rate how well the performer's movements matched the audio.
* **Stage Presence** (stage_presence): Rate the individual's comfort and confidence on stage.
* **Creativity** (creativity): Rate the originality and uniqueness of their performance.
* **Comments** (comments): Provide any additional comments about their performance. 
* **Compare Performances** (compare_performances): Choose how you compare their performance to others (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor).
* **Comments Other** (comments_other): Provide any additional comments about their performance.
* **Assigned Judge Name** (assigned_judge_name): Enter the name of the person who assigned them to be evaluated.

## Tips

* Be as specific and honest as possible when providing feedback.
* Consider the individual's strengths and weaknesses when filling out this form.
* Provide constructive feedback that can help improve the individual's performance.
* If you have any doubts or questions about this form, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
