# Tea Day Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tea Day Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of the Tea Day event. This feedback will help the event organizers understand what went well and what can be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each field carefully and fill out as much information as you can.
2. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form, as it will help the event organizers understand your experience.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, if any.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Feedback Form Introduction** (optional): This field is an introduction to the feedback form, providing context and instructions for the rest of the form.
* **What did you do before the event?** (optional): Describe what you did before attending the Tea Day event. This will help the event organizers understand your background and experience leading up to the event.
* **What did you most enjoy about the event?** (optional): Write about what you enjoyed most about the event. This could be a particular session, speaker, or aspect of the event.
* **What did you find most challenging about the event?** (optional): Describe what you found most challenging about the event. This could be a difficulty you faced or an area for improvement.
* **Overall satisfaction** (optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rate the food** (optional): Rate the quality of the food on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rate the drinks** (optional): Rate the quality of the drinks on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rate the sessions** (optional): Rate the quality of the sessions on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rate the facilitators** (optional): Rate the quality of the facilitators on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rate the venue** (optional): Rate the quality of the venue on a scale of 1-5.
