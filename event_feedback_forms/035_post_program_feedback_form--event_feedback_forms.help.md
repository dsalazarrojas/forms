# post_program_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "post_program_feedback_form" is a survey designed to collect feedback on a program after its completion. This form will help the organizers of the program to evaluate and improve its quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary fields with your honest and detailed feedback.
2. Answer each field according to your experience and understanding.
3. If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact us at [contact email].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Details**: Please provide a brief description of the program and its content.
* **Participant Details**: Share some personal information about yourself, including your name and any relevant background that might be helpful in understanding your feedback.
* **Session Details**: Give a detailed explanation of the session(s) you attended, including what was covered, and what you thought was most helpful or least helpful.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date(s) you attended the session(s) you are providing feedback on.
* **Session Time**: Enter the start and end time of the session(s) you attended, in the format `HH:MM` or `HH:MM:SS`.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and honest as possible in your feedback.
- Make sure to answer all the required fields (though they are not strictly required, we appreciate your cooperation).
- If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the free-text fields.
