# corporate_team_building_activity_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants about corporate team building activities. It helps the organization understand what worked well and what didn't, and to improve future team building events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are providing feedback for the correct activity.
2. Fill in the "Activity Name" field with the name of the activity you are providing feedback on.
3. Rate your overall enjoyment of the activity on the "Rating 1 - 5" scale.
4. Add any additional feedback or comments about the activity in the "Feedback" field.
5. If applicable, provide your name, department, job title, and email.
6. Indicate the size of your team that participated in the activity.
7. Specify the date and time of the activity.
8. Add any additional comments or thoughts about the activity in the "Comments" field.
9. Select the type of activity (Team-Building, Social, or Other).
10. Rate your enjoyment of the activity on the "Rating 2 - 5" and "Rating 3" scales.
11. Provide a rating of the activity (1-5) in the "Rating 4" and "Rating 5" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, `text`, required): The name of the activity you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating 1 - 5** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall enjoyment of the activity on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Add any additional feedback or comments about the activity.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Your work email.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Your job title.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, required): The size of your team that participated in the activity.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): The date of the activity.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): The time of the activity.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Any additional comments or thoughts about the activity.
* **Activity Type** (`activity_type`, `select_one`, required): The type of activity (Team-Building, Social, or Other).
* **Rating 2 - 5** (`rating_2`, `select_one`, required): Rate your enjoyment of the activity on a scale from 2 to 5.
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, `number`, required): A rating of the activity (1-5).
* **Rating 4** (`rating_4`, `number`, required): A rating of the activity (1-5).
* **Rating 5** (`rating_5`, `number`, required): A rating of the activity (1-5).

Please note that some fields may not be relevant to you or your activity, as they are optional and not required.
