# Color Ensemble Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Ensemble Feedback Form is a tool for event attendees to provide their opinions and suggestions about the event. By completing this form, you will be helping the event organizers understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely. Some fields are required, and they are marked as such.
2. If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the "Additional comments" field.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them in the "Suggestions for improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Attendee name** (text, required)
Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback.

### **Event date** (date, required)
Enter the date of the event you attended in YYYY-MM-DD format.

### **Overall satisfaction** (rating, required)
Select a rating from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to express your overall satisfaction with the event.

### **Favorite aspect of event** (text, optional)
Describe what you enjoyed most about the event.

### **Suggestions for improvement** (text, optional)
Provide any practical feedback you have for the event organizers to consider.

### **Speaker quality rating** (rating, optional)
Select a rating from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to express your opinion of the speaker's quality.

### **Venue rating** (rating, optional)
Select a rating from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to express your opinion of the venue's quality.

### **Would recommend** (yes/no, required)
Check "Yes" if you would recommend the event to others and "No" otherwise.

### **Additional comments** (text, optional)
Enter any additional thoughts or comments you have about the event.
