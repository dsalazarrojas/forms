# Fashion Show Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fashion Show Feedback Form is designed to gather information from attendees of the fashion show event. Your responses will help us understand your experience and provide valuable input for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions based on your experience at the fashion show event.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. You can provide as much detail as you'd like in the text fields.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them in the "Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fashion Show Feedback** (`event_feedback_form_1`, `note`, not required):
  Please provide a brief description of your experience at the fashion show event.
* **Event Date** (`event_feedback_form_2`, `date`, not required):
  Enter the date of the fashion show event you attended.
* **Event Time** (`event_feedback_form_3`, `time`, not required):
  Enter the time of the fashion show event you attended.
* **Event Location** (`event_feedback_form_4`, `text`, not required):
  Enter the location of the fashion show event you attended.
* **Fashion Show Rating** (`event_feedback_form_5`, `number`, not required):
  Rate your experience at the fashion show event on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Overall Experience** (`event_feedback_form_6`, `text`, not required):
  Describe your overall experience at the fashion show event.
* **Suggestions** (`event_feedback_form_7`, `text`, not required):
  Share any suggestions you have for improving future fashion show events.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_feedback_form_8`, `text`, not required):
  Please enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Staff** (`event_feedback_form_9`, `text`, not required):
  Please enter the name of the event staff.
* **Event Security** (`event_feedback_form_10`, `text`, not required):
  Please enter any notable comments or concerns about event security.
