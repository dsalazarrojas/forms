# Post Event Innovation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Post Event Innovation Survey. The form is divided into several sections, each designed to collect specific information about your experience at the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your knowledge and experience.
2. You can leave any optional fields blank if they don't apply to your situation.
3. Make sure to review all the sections before submitting your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Feedback** (`event_feedback`): A field for any comments or notes about your experience at the event.
* **Ratings** (`ratings`): Rate your overall experience at the event using the scale provided.
* **Innovative Ideas** (`innovation_ideas`): Select all ideas you found innovative or interesting during the event.
* **Open Ideas** (`open_ideas`): Select one idea you found particularly innovative or interesting during the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`): A field for any additional comments about your experience at the event.
* **Name** (`name`): Your name, used for internal follow-up purposes.
* **Email** (`email`): Your email address, used for internal follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Your phone number, used for internal follow-up purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): The date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): The time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): The location of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): The type of event you attended (e.g., Conference, Workshop, Program).
* **Comments** (`comments`): A field for any additional comments about your experience at the event.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): A field for any additional feedback about your experience at the event.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`): A field for any suggestions about future events.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`): Rate the quality of the event using the provided scale.
* **Rating Scale Label** (`rating_scale_label`): Select a label for your rating (e.g., Excellent, Good, Okay, Bad, Not at all).
* **Rating Scale Important** (`rating_scale_important`): Rate how important it was for you that the event had a high rating scale.
* **Follow-up Question** (`follow_up_question`): Select one of the provided options regarding follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`): Select a time frame for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`): Select a method for follow-up (e.g., Email, Phone call, In-person, Other).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`): Select a frequency for follow-up (e.g., Once a week, Once a month, Once a quarter, Never).
