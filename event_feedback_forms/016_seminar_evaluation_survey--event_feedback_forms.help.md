# Seminar Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the seminar evaluation survey form. The form is designed to gather feedback on the overall quality and effectiveness of a seminar. Your input is valuable in helping us improve our seminar offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and honestly, based on your personal experience with the seminar.
- Please answer each question carefully, as your feedback is important to us.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the `seminar_recommendation_1` field for this purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the overall quality of the seminar?** (`seminar_evaluation_survey_1`, text, optional): Please rate the overall quality of the seminar, from poor to excellent.
* **Participant satisfaction** (`seminar_participant_satisfaction_1`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following options to rate your satisfaction with the seminar:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Content of the seminar** (`seminar_content_1`, text, optional): Describe the content of the seminar, including any notable points or takeaways.
* **Organization of the seminar** (`seminar_organization_1`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the organization and logistics of the seminar.
* **Value for money?** (`seminar_value_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to rate your perception of value for money:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Recommendation** (`seminar_recommendation_1`, text, optional): Provide any additional recommendations or comments for improving the seminar.
* **Attendance of seminar** (`seminar_attendance_1`, number, optional): If applicable, estimate the number of attendees at the seminar.
* **Date of seminar** (`seminar_date_1`, date, optional): Enter the date of the seminar (if not already filled out).
* **Time of seminar** (`seminar_time_1`, time, optional): Enter the time of the seminar (if not already filled out).
