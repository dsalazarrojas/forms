# Games Attendance Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees about their experience at games events. It will help event organizers understand what worked well and what didn't during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide any additional comments or feedback you may have. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form) to ensure your feedback is submitted successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event ID**: This field is required to identify which event you're providing feedback about. Please enter the corresponding number for the event you attended.
* **Event Title**: This is the title of the event you're providing feedback about. Please enter the name of the event as it was displayed on the event page or marketing materials.
* **Attendee Name**: This field is optional, but it's helpful for event organizers to know who's providing feedback. If you're not comfortable sharing your name, you can leave this field blank.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts about the event. What did you think of the event? What were some of the highlights or low points?
* **Event Score**: Rate your overall experience at the event. Choose a score from 1 to 5 to indicate your satisfaction.
* **Will You Recommend This Event**: This is a select one field where you can choose one of the options provided. Please select the option that best represents your intention to recommend the event to others.
* **Additional Feedback Details**: If you have any additional comments or feedback about the event, please share them here.
* **Email**: This field is required for event organizers to contact you with follow-up questions or to send you updates about future events.
* **Phone**: This field is optional, but if you'd like to be contacted by event organizers directly, please provide your phone number.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other thoughts or comments you'd like to share about the event?

Please note that all fields are subject to change, and new fields may be added or removed in the future. If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please don't hesitate to contact event organizers.
