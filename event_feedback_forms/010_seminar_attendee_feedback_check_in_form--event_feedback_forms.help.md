# Seminar Attendee Feedback Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seminar Attendee Feedback Check In Form is designed to collect feedback from seminar attendees after a session. It's essential to provide us with your honest and true feedback so we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Attendee Email" field. Please note that this field is required if you want to be notified about any updates or responses to your feedback.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Attendee Phone" field.
4.  Enter the date of the session you attended in the "Session Date" field. The correct format for this field is YYYY-MM-DD.
5.  Enter the time of the session you attended in the "Session Time" field. The correct format for this field is HH:MM (24H).
6.  Choose your attendance status ("Active" or "Inactive") in the "Session Attendance Status" field.
7.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the seminar (on a scale) in the "Attendee Rating" field.
8.  Provide your feedback or comments in the "Attendee Feedback" field, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Attendee Name
*   **Attendee Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
### Attendee Email
*   **Attendee Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (your_name@example.com). This field is required if you want to be notified about any updates or responses to your feedback.
### Attendee Phone
*   **Attendee Phone** (`text`, `phone`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for any further communication or updates.
### Session Date
*   **Session Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the seminar you attended in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
### Session Time
*   **Session Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the seminar you attended in the format HH:MM (24H).
### Session Attendance Status
*   **Session Attendance Status** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your attendance status as "Active" or "Inactive".
### Attendee Rating
*   **Attendee Rating** (`number`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the seminar on a scale of 1-5.
### Attendee Feedback
*   **Attendee Feedback** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any feedback or comments you have about the seminar.
