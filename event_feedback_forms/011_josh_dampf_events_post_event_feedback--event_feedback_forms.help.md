# josh_dampf_events_post_event_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees after an event has concluded. Your input is crucial in helping us understand what worked well and what didn't during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the event you attended.
2.  Select the date of the event you attended.
3.  List all attendees of the event.
4.  Share your thoughts and impressions about the event.
5.  Rate your overall experience (optional).
6.  Provide any additional comments or feedback.
7.  Indicate if you plan to attend the next event (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the event you attended. This will help us understand which event you are providing feedback about.
* **Event date** (2, date, required): Select the date of the event you attended. This helps us keep track of past events.
* **Attendees** (3, number, required): Please list all attendees you met or interacted with during the event. This will help us understand the dynamics and interactions within the group.
* **What did you think?** (4, text, required): Share your thoughts and impressions about the event. Be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Rating** (5, select_one, optional): Provide an overall rating of your experience (Excellent, Good, Poor, or Terrible).
* **Comments** (6, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them here.
* **Will you attend next event?** (7, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you plan to attend the next event or if you are undecided.
* **Feedback to organizers** (8, text, optional): Share any feedback or suggestions you have for the event organizers. This is an opportunity to help us improve future events.
