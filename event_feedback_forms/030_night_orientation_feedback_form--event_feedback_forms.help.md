# Night Orientation Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the Night Orientation event. Your input will help us improve our future events. Please provide your honest feedback, and let us know what you liked or disliked about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required".
2. Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name (<font size="2">1</font>)

*   **First name** (text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last name (<font size="2">2</font>)

*   **Last name** (text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email (<font size="2">3</font>)

*   **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and to receive future event updates. We'll never spam you.

### Phone (<font size="2">4</font>)

*   **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for additional communication.

### Date (<font size="2">5</font>)

*   **Date** (date, optional): Select the date of the Night Orientation event you attended.

### Time (<font size="2">6</font>)

*   **Time** (time, optional): Select the time of the Night Orientation event you attended.

### Event Date (<font size="2">7</font>)

*   **Event Date** (date, optional): Select the date of the Night Orientation event you attended. 

# Tips
*   Make sure to provide accurate dates and times for the event, so we can use this information to improve our next events. 
*   If you have any specific feedback, please include it in the comments section provided for that purpose. 

This guide is designed to provide clear and concise instructions for completing the Night Orientation Feedback Form. If you have any further questions, feel free to contact us at your convenience.
