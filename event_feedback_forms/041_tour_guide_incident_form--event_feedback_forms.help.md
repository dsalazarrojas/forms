# Tour Guide Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Guide Incident Form is used to collect information about incidents that occur during a tour. This form helps in documenting and reporting incidents in a standardized and organized manner, ensuring that critical information is captured and reviewed for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Operator**: Choose the name of the tour operator who was present during the incident. If the operator is not listed, select "None" or "Other" and provide the name in the "Incident Description" field.
2. **Enter the Date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred.
3. **Specify the Time**: Enter the time when the incident occurred.
4. **Describe the Incident**: Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.
5. **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
6. **Incident Details**: Add any additional details about the incident, if necessary.
7. **Incident Cause**: If known, enter the reason or cause of the incident.
8. **Action Taken**: Describe any actions taken after the incident.
9. **Incident Outcome**: Provide an update on the outcome or resolution of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Operator** (`tour_operator`, Select One, Optional): Choose the name of the tour operator who was present during the incident.
* **Date** (`tour_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, Time, Optional): Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Location** (`incident_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, Text, Optional): Add any additional details about the incident, if necessary.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, Text, Optional): If known, enter the reason or cause of the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, Text, Optional): Describe any actions taken after the incident.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, Text, Optional): Provide an update on the outcome or resolution of the incident.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate information is captured.
* If an incident has not occurred during the tour, you can mark the "Incident Information" field as complete but leave the other fields blank.
* Use the "Other" option under "Operator" to enter the name of a non-listed tour operator, if necessary.
* Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
