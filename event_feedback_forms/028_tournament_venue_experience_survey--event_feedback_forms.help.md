# Tournament Venue Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from attendees and participants about their experience at a tournament venue. It helps us to identify areas for improvement and understand where we excel or need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections and questions to ensure you're providing feedback about your experience at the tournament venue.
2. Answer all the required questions. If a question is not required, you can still provide feedback, but it's not necessary.
3. Use the available space to provide as much detail as you need to answer each question.
4. Submit the form once you've completed all the necessary steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parking Rating (1/5)**: Rate your experience with parking at the tournament venue. Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being very good.
* **Staff Rating (2/5)**: Rate your experience with staff at the venue. Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being very good.
* **Venue Rating (3/5)**: Rate your experience with the venue itself. Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being very good.
* **Amenities Rating (4/5)**: Rate your experience with the amenities provided at the venue. Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being very good.
* **Overall Satisfaction (5/5)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience at the tournament venue. Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being very good.
* **Additional Comments**: Use this space to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience at the tournament venue. This is an optional field, so feel free to add as much or as little information as you'd like.
* **Event Name**: If you're providing feedback for a specific event, please enter the name of the event to help us associate your feedback with the correct event. This is an optional field, but it helps us to track feedback for individual events.
