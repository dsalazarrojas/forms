# Tourist Attraction Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from tourists who have attended an event at the attraction. Your input will help us understand your experience and make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields (Event Name, Event Date, Rating, Email, and Phone Number).
2. Choose how you want to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail).
3. Enter your name (if you want to share it).
4. Provide comments about your experience.
5. Share any suggestions you have for improving the event.
6. Answer the questions about what you liked or disliked about the event and what can be improved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (Required): Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date** (Required): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Event Time** (Optional): Enter the time of the event you attended (if applicable).
* **How Would You Rate Your Experience** (Required): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments** (Optional): Share any comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Do You Have Suggestions For Improvement** (Optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the event.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Name** (Optional): Enter your name (if you want to share it).
* **Contact Method** (Required): Choose how you want to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Contact Number** (Optional): Enter your phone number (if you choose to be contacted by phone).
* **Address** (Optional): Enter your address (if you want to be contacted by mail).
* **What Did You Like About The Event** (Optional): Share what you liked about the event.
* **What Did You Dislike About The Event** (Optional): Share what you disliked about the event.
* **What Can Be Improved For The Event** (Optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the event.

# Tips

* Please be honest and clear in your feedback.
* Try to be as specific as possible when sharing your comments and suggestions.
* If you have any attachments or photos, please attach them to this form.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, please add them in the comments field.
