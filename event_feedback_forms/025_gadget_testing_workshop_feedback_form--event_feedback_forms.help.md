# Gadget Testing Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience in the Gadget Testing Workshop. Your input will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the workshop and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Follow the steps below to complete this form:
	* Scroll through each page of questions, reading each one carefully.
	* Answer each question to the best of your ability.
	* Use the space provided for each question to type in your response.
2. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional)
	* This field is for providing any additional information about yourself, the participant. This could include your name, company, or role.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional)
	* This field is for any additional details you'd like to provide about the event, such as the date, location, or any notable events that occurred.
- **How would you rate your experience?** (`rating_1`, number, optional)
	* Rate your experience in the workshop on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Not Satisfactory" and 5 being "Excellent".
- **How would you rate the content?** (`rating_2`, number, optional)
	* Rate the content of the workshop on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Not Satisfactory" and 5 being "Excellent".
- **How would you rate the facilitator?** (`rating_3`, number, optional)
	* Rate the facilitator of the workshop on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Not Satisfactory" and 5 being "Excellent".
- **How would you rate the materials?** (`rating_4`, number, optional)
	* Rate the materials provided during the workshop on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Not Satisfactory" and 5 being "Excellent".
- **How would you rate the overall experience?** (`rating_5`, number, optional)
	* Rate your overall experience at the workshop on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Not Satisfactory" and 5 being "Excellent".
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional)
	* This field is for any additional feedback you may have about the workshop.
- **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, optional)
	* This field is for rating your overall satisfaction with the workshop, on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Not Satisfactory" and 5 being "Excellent".

## Tips

* Please provide honest and constructive feedback.
* Keep your answers concise and focused on your experience.
* You may add any additional details you'd like in the "Participant Information" field.
* If you have any specific suggestions or recommendations, please include them in the "Feedback" field.
