# Sports Event Parking Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users who have attended a sports event and have experience with the parking facilities. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the questions that apply to your experience.
2. Choose the options that best fit your experience from the select_one and select_multiple fields.
3. Enter your response in the text field.
4. Submit your feedback when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the sports event parking survey**: This is an introduction to the form and provides background information about the survey.
* **Did you attend the event**: Answer "Yes" if you attended the event, "No" if you did not attend.
* **How did you park your vehicle**: Choose how you parked your vehicle from the select_multiple options.
* **Where did you park your vehicle**: Enter the location where you parked your vehicle.
* **How satisfied are you with the parking**: Rate your satisfaction with the parking on a numerical scale.
* **What time did you park your vehicle**: Enter the time when you parked your vehicle.
* **Did you leave your vehicle unattended**: Answer "Yes" if you left your vehicle unattended, "No" if you did not.
* **Which parking facility did you use**: Choose the parking facility you used from the select_one options.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately based on your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them in the text field.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask and we will be happy to help.
