# Fan Engagement Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from event participants about their experience with our events and content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing feedback in the "Feedback" field about your experience with the event.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event in the "Satisfaction" field using the numeric scale (1-10).
3. Answer the multiple-choice question in the "Content preferences" field by selecting all that apply.
4. Elaborate on your experience in the "Event experience" field.
5. Share what you liked about our event in the "Fan preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback (fan_engagement_form)**: This is the main text field where you can share your thoughts and comments about the event.
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Satisfaction (satisfaction)**: This field is a numeric scale (1-10) where you can rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
	+ **Type:** Number
	+ **Required:** No
* **Content preferences (content_preferences)**: This field is a multiple-choice question where you can express your interest in different types of content (e.g., music, art, food).
	+ **Type:** Select multiple
	+ **Required:** No
* **Event experience (event_experience)**: This field is a text field where you can elaborate on your experience with the event.
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Fan preferences (fan_preferences)**: This field is a text field where you can share what you liked about our event.
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** No
