# film-festival-screening-audience-feedback-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from audience members who have attended a film festival screening. This feedback will help us evaluate the quality of our screenings and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These include:
	* film name
	* rating
	* feedback
	* did you attend alone or with others
	* time
	* did you watch until the end
	* how much did you enjoy it
	* film-length
	* film-mood
	* music
	* plot
	* film-score
	* recommend
	* film_artist
	* film_director
	* film_genre
	* film_locations
	* film_year
	* film_language
	* film_ratings
	* film_duration
	* additional-comments
2. Choose the correct answer or option for each field. If a field is marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple", select only one or multiple options that apply.
3. Leave blank fields or those not relevant to the event.
4. If you have any additional comments, please include them in the "additional-comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **film name** (2): Enter the name of the film you attended.
* **film-category** (3): If known, enter the category of the film (e.g., drama, comedy, etc.).
* **rating** (4): Rate the film out of a scale, usually 1-5.
* **feedback** (5): Share your thoughts and opinions about the film.
* **Did you attend alone or with others** (7): Indicate if you attended the screening alone or with a group of people.
* **time** (8): Enter the start and end time of the screening.
* **did you watch until the end** (9): Indicate if you watched the entire film from start to finish.
* **how much did you enjoy it** (10): Rate how much you enjoyed the film (very much, somewhat, not much, not at all).
* **film-length** (11): Indicate if the film was too long, too short, or just right.
* **film-mood** (12): Rate the mood of the film (very serious, somewhat serious, somewhat light-hearted, very light-hearted).
* **music** (13): Indicate if the music was too loud, just right, or too soft.
* **plot** (14): Provide a brief summary of the plot.
* **film-score** (15): Rate the overall score or quality of the film.
* **recommend** (16): Indicate if you would recommend the film to others.
* **film-artist** (17): If known, enter the artist or artist group behind the film.
* **film-director** (18): If known, enter the film's director.
* **film-genre** (19): If known, enter the genre of the film.
* **film-locations** (20): If known, enter the film's locations.
* **film-year** (21): If known, enter the year of the film's release.
* **film-language** (22): If known, enter the language of the film.
* **film-ratings** (23): If known, enter the film's ratings.
* **film-duration** (24): If known, enter the film's duration.
* **additional-comments** (25): Include any additional thoughts or comments about the film.

Please note that all fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required.
