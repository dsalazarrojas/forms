# Exhibit Quality Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors about the quality of the exhibit. You are free to provide as much or as little information as you like, and your responses will help us improve future exhibits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Visit Details**: Provide a brief description of your visit to the exhibit, including any relevant details about your experience.
2. **Quality**: Choose one of the options below to rate the quality of the exhibit:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
3. **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your visit, you can provide them in the space below.
4. **Email**: If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address.
5. **Feedback**: If you have any other feedback or suggestions, you can provide it in the space below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Details** (`exhibit_visit_details`, text, optional): A brief description of your visit to the exhibit.
* **Quality** (`exhibit_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the exhibit as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): A space for any additional comments or thoughts you have about your visit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address, if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`visitor_feedback`, time, optional): A space for any other feedback or suggestions you have.

## Tips
* Please be as brief or as detailed as you like in your responses.
* You can skip any fields you don't want to complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
