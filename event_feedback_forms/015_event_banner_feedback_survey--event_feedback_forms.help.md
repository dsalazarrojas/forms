# Event Banner Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from event attendees on the event banner displayed during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button to start the survey.
2. Select the location of the banner from the options provided.
3. Type or select your comments about the banner content.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the event banner on a scale of 1-5.
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments about the event banner.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes, if you choose to provide it.
7. Click on "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Feedback** (`event_feedback_page_1`, text, optional): Briefly describe your experience with the event banner. You can type any thoughts or impressions you have about it.
- **Banner Location** (`event_banner_location`, select_one, optional): Select the location where the banner was displayed during the event. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
	+ Left side of the stage: The banner was displayed on the left side of the stage.
	+ Right side of the stage: The banner was displayed on the right side of the stage.
	+ Center of the stage: The banner was displayed in the center of the stage.
	+ Near the entrance: The banner was displayed near the entrance of the event venue.
	+ Other: The banner was displayed somewhere else.
- **Banner Content** (`event_banner_content`, text, optional): If you have any comments about the content of the banner, type them here.
- **Rating (1-5)** (`event_feedback_rating`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the banner on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
- **Additional Comments** (`event_feedback_comments`, text, optional): If you have any other comments or thoughts about the banner, type them here.
- **Email** (`event_feedback_email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted for any follow-up questions or discussion, enter your email address here. Your email will not be shared publicly.
- **Phone** (`event_feedback_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone for any follow-up questions or discussion, type your phone number here.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your experience with the banner.
* Try to be objective and honest in your feedback.
* If you have already submitted feedback for this event, do not resubmit.
