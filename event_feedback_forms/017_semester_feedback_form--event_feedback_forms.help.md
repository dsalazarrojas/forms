# semester_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after an event, specifically a semester. It helps us to gather information about their experience, satisfaction, and future attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your feedback about the event in the "Feedback" field.
2. Enter your "Participant ID" if you have one.
3. Indicate if you participated in the semester by selecting "True" or "False" in the "semester_participated" field.
4. Answer questions about your attendance in the event by selecting "True", "False", "partially", or "did not attend" in the "attended_all_events", "attended_all_lectures", "attended_all_tutorials", and "attended_all_seminars" fields.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event by selecting "very satisfied", "somewhat satisfied", "neutral", "somewhat dissatisfied", or "very dissatisfied" in the "overall_satisfaction" field.
6. Let us know if you would recommend this event to other participants by selecting "True" or "False" in the "recommend_to_participants" field.
7. Answer questions about your future attendance by selecting "True", "False", "maybe", or "not applying" in the "will_attend_next_semester" field.
8. If you have suggestions or comments, type them in the "suggestions" field.
9. If you want to be contacted, provide your email, phone number, or email in the "contact_information", "contact_phone", "contact_email" fields.
10. Finally, enter your "event_id", "semester", "participant", "event_start", "event_end", "event_duration", and "participant_id" fields if they are available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide your feedback about the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `number`, required: false): If you have a participant ID, enter it here.
* **Semester Participated** (`semester_participated`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you participated in the semester by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Attended All Events** (`attended_all_events`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer questions about your attendance in the event by selecting "True", "False", "partially", or "did not attend".
* **Attended All Lectures** (`attended_all_lectures`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer questions about your attendance in the event by selecting "True", "False", "partially", or "did not attend".
* **Attended All Tutorials** (`attended_all_tutorials`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer questions about your attendance in the event by selecting "True", "False", "partially", or "did not attend".
* **Attended All Seminars** (`attended_all_seminars`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer questions about your attendance in the event by selecting "True", "False", "partially", or "did not attend".
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event by selecting "very satisfied", "somewhat satisfied", "neutral", "somewhat dissatisfied", or "very dissatisfied".
* **Recommend to Participants** (`recommend_to_participants`, `select_one`, required: false): Let us know if you would recommend this event to other participants by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Will Attend Next Semester** (`will_attend_next_semester`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer questions about your future attendance by selecting "True", "False", "maybe", or "not applying".
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): If you have suggestions or comments, type them here.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `email`, required: false): If you want to be contacted, provide your email, phone number, or email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the event ID.
* **Semester** (`semester`, `number`, required: false): Enter the semester number.
