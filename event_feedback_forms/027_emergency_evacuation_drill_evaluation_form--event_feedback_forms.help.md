# emergency_evacuation_drill_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_evacuation_drill_evaluation_form is used to collect feedback and evaluation data for emergency evacuation drills. This form is essential for understanding the effectiveness of the drill and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of several sections, each designed to gather specific information about the emergency evacuation drill. Please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the accurate information about the drill.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields to help with evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Evacuation Drill** (`emergency_evacuation_drill`, text, optional): This field is for the drill's title.
* **Drill Participants Attended** (`drill_participants_attended`, number, optional): Enter the number of participants who attended the drill.
* **Emergency Evacuation Drill Date** (`emergency_evacuation_drill_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the drill.
* **Emergency Evacuation Drill Time** (`emergency_evacuation_drill_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the drill.
* **Emergency Evacuation Drill Facilitator** (`emergency_evacuation_drill_facilitator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facilitator.
* **Facilitator Experience** (`facilitator_experience`, text, optional): Briefly describe your experience as a facilitator.
* **Facilitator Comments** (`facilitator_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments about the facilitator's performance.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments from the participants.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest ways to improve the emergency evacuation drill.

Note: The fields with "optional" label are not required, but it's recommended to fill them in for more comprehensive evaluation.
