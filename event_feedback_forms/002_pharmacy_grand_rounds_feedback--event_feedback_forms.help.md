# Pharmacy Grand Rounds Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for you to provide feedback about your experience with the pharmacy's grand rounds. Your input is valuable to us and will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each field in the form according to the instructions below.
2. If you need to add more information about any field, please use the "comment" field provided.
3. Make sure to check your answers before submitting the form.
4. You can choose to submit or not submit your feedback, we encourage you to provide your honest thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session** (`1`, number, required/optional): Please enter the session number (if any) for which you are providing feedback.
* **Date** (`2`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the session or event in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Rating** (`3`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`4`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or details about your experience.
* **Feedback** (`5`, note, required/optional): Provide any other feedback or suggestions you might have about the session.
* **Rating Score** (`6`, number, required/optional): If you have a specific rating score in mind, feel free to enter it.
* **Submit** (`7`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the session. Options are: I am satisfied, I am not satisfied, I am neutral.
* **Email** (`8`, email, required/optional): If you'd like to receive a confirmation email about your feedback, please enter your email address.
