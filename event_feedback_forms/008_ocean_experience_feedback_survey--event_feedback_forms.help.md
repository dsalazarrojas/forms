# ocean_experience_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ocean_experience_feedback_survey is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our ocean tours. It is a simple form with several questions that help us understand what our customers enjoyed most about their tour and what they would like to see improved.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read each question carefully and answer as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
- Select the correct options for each multiple-choice question.
- Enter your feedback in the text fields, using as much detail as you need to answer the question.
- Use the correct format for date and time fields, for example, MM/DD/YY HH:MM AM/PM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Who was your tour guide?** (Select One)
	+ This question is asking about the tour guide who led your tour. Please select the name of your guide from the list.
* **When did you take the tour?** (Date)
	+ Enter the date of your tour in the format MM/DD/YY, for example, 01/15/2022.
* **What time did you take the tour?** (Time)
	+ Enter the time of your tour in the format HH:MM, for example, 02:00 PM.
* **What did you like most about the tour?** (Text)
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can enter anything you liked about your tour.
* **Additional Feedback** (Text)
	+ If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them here.
* **Email** (Email)
	+ Please enter your email address if you want to receive any follow-up information or offers.
* **Phone** (Text)
	+ Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your tour.
* **Category** (Select One)
	+ Please select the category that best fits your tour experience.
* **Subcategory** (Select Multiple)
	+ Select one or more subcategories that fit your tour experience.
* **Rating** (Number)
	+ Rate your tour experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Review** (Text)
	+ Enter a brief review of your tour experience.

## Tips

- Please answer each question as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
- If you are unsure about any question, it's okay to ask for clarification.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact info].
