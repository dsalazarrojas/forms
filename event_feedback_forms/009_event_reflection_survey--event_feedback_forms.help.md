# Event Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and reflection on a specific event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as requested. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (`event_reflection_survey_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Email** (`event_reflection_survey_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address associated with your organization or institution.
- **Date of Event** (`event_reflection_survey_date_of_event`, date, required): Enter the date on which the event took place.
- **Rating** (`event_reflection_survey_rating`, select_one, required): Select one of the following ratings:
	+ Highly Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Not At All Satisfied
- **Comments** (`event_reflection_survey_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the event.
- **Recommend** (`event_reflection_survey_recommend`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ True
- **Follow-up** (`event_reflection_survey_follow_up`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes, I'm interested
	+ No, thanks
