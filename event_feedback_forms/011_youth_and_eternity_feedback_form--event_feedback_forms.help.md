# Youth And Eternity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about a specific event. Your feedback will help us understand how to improve the event and make future events more enjoyable for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section. This is the first page of the form.
2. Answer the questions about your participation in the event in the "Event Participation" section.
3. Share your thoughts about your overall experience with the event in the "Feedback" section.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the event in the "Event Satisfaction" section.
5. Choose whether or not you would recommend this event to others in the "Recommend" section.
6. Share any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" section.
7. Provide your email address in the "E-Mail" section.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.
9. Select the event that this form is related to in the "Event" section.
10. Choose the date you attended the event in the "Date" section.
11. Select the time you attended the event in the "Time" section.
12. Add any notes or additional information about your experience in the "Note" section.
13. If you've completed the form for a specific event, you can enter the event's unique identifier in the "Form ID" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (text, required): Enter your name, email address, and any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Event Participation** (text, optional): Describe your level of participation in the event (e.g., attended, sponsored, participated in a specific activity).
* **Feedback** (text, optional): Share your thoughts about the event, what you enjoyed, and what you didn't enjoy.
* **Event Satisfaction** (number, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with the event on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Recommend** (select multiple, optional): Choose whether or not you would recommend this event to others and why (e.g., yes, no, not sure, reasons).
* **Suggestions** (text, optional): Share any suggestions for future events.
* **Event Attendance** (number, optional): Rate how many events you've attended in the past or plan to attend in the future.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the event.
* **E-Mail** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates or follow-up questions.
* **Event** (select one, optional): Select the event that this form is related to.
* **Date** (date, optional): Choose the date you attended the event.
* **Time** (time, optional): Choose the time you attended the event.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or information about your experience.
* **Form ID** (number, optional): If you've completed the form for a specific event, enter the event's unique identifier (e.g., event code).
