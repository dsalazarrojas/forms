# super_bowl_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and information from Super Bowl event attendees. Please complete the form to help us understand your experience and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \* in the label).
2. Select the relevant options from the select-one fields (e.g., Score Board, Event Name).
3. Enter your text answers in the text fields (e.g., Event Feedback, Favorite Food).
4. Enter your date of attendance in the Date Attended field.
5. Enter your contact information (phone number and email) if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Super Bowl Event Details**: Please enter a brief description of the event you attended.
* **How Many Attendees**: Please select the number of people you attended the event with.
* **Score Board**: Select your rating of the event from 1 to 10.
* **Favorite Food**: What was your favorite food item from the event?
* **Event Feedback**: Share any thoughts or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Date Attended**: Enter the date you attended the event.
* **Time Spent**: (Optional) Please enter the time you spent at the event.
* **Contact Information**: Please enter your phone number and email address if you'd like us to follow up with you about your experience.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Event Name**: Select the name of the event you attended (e.g., Super Bowl, Event 2, Event 3).

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form.
