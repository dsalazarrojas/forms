# Event Presenter Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Presenter Feedback Form is a crucial tool for gathering feedback from event presenters, allowing us to understand their experiences and perspectives. This form will help us identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, use the "Feedback" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Presenter Feedback** (<name> <type> <required>): This field is where you can share your overall feedback about the event. This can be anything from suggestions for improvement to comments on how well the event went.
* **Overall Experience** (<name> <type> <required>): Rate your overall experience with the event from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Event Content** (<name> <type> <required>): Evaluate the quality of the event's content. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Would You Attend** (<name> <type> <required>): Indicate whether you would attend this event again. Choose from the options: 'True', 'False', or unsure.
* **Date** (<name> <type> <required>): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Time** (<name> <type> <required>): Enter the time of the event you attended. (Note: This field is optional)
* **Feedback** (<name> <type> <required>): This field is where you can share any additional comments or suggestions about the event.
* **Email** (<name> <type> <required>): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you directly.
* **Phone** (<name> <type> <required>): This field is optional and should only be filled out by event presenters who want to be contacted directly.

Please ensure that you fill out this form honestly and thoroughly, and we will use your feedback to make necessary changes to future events.
