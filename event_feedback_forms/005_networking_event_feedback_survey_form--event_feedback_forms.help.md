# Networking Event Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your opinions on your recent attendance at a networking event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the pages you'd like to complete by clicking on the radio button next to each page number.
2. For each page, select all the options that apply to your experience by checking the relevant boxes or entering your answers in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attended Networking Event?**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you attended the event.
  - Options: Yes, No
  - Required: Optional
- **Event Details**: Choose 'Yes' if you had the opportunity to interact with potential collaborators, partners, or investors.
  - Options: Yes, No
  - Required: Optional
- **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience at the event. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 5.
  - Type: Number
  - Required: Optional
- **Networking Opportunities**: Select all the options that apply to your experience:
  - Options: Yes, No
  - Required: Optional
- **Networking Connections**: Rate the number of meaningful connections you made at the event. Please enter a numerical value.
  - Type: Number
  - Required: Optional
- **Follow-up Conversations**: Please provide any follow-up conversations or discussions you had with attendees, organizers, or sponsors. This can include phone numbers, email addresses, or names of individuals.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Optional
- **Additional Follow-up Conversations 2** to **Additional Follow-up Conversations 9**: Please provide any additional information about your follow-up conversations.
