# Town Hall Meeting Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the town hall meeting. Your input will help us improve future meetings and provide a better experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question truthfully, based on your personal experience of the meeting.
3. Select the most suitable options for each field, taking into account your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you learn about your town hall meeting?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_1`, text, required: false)
	+ Please share what you gained from attending the meeting. What new insights, information, or knowledge did you acquire?
* **What did you find most interesting about this meeting?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_2`, text, required: false)
	+ What aspect of the meeting caught your attention, or what topic did you find the most engaging?
* **How would you rate this meeting's overall quality?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_3`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose a rating from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to express your opinion on the meeting's overall quality.
* **What suggestions do you have for future meetings?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_4`, text, required: false)
	+ Share your ideas and suggestions on how we can improve future meetings. What would you like to see changed or added?
* **Do you have any further comments or feedback?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_5`, text, required: false)
	+ If you have any additional thoughts or concerns, please share them here.
* **Is there anything we can improve on?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_6`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Check all the options that apply to indicate areas where we can improve.
* **What time did you arrive at the meeting?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_7`, time, required: false)
	+ Please enter the time you arrived at the meeting.
* **What time did you leave the meeting?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_8`, time, required: false)
	+ Enter the time you left the meeting.
* **When do you think you will attend the next meeting?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_9`, date, required: false)
	+ Select a date for when you expect to attend the next meeting.
* **Do you want to attend the next meeting?** (`town_hall_meeting_feedback_survey_10`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose True if you plan to attend the next meeting, False otherwise.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your participation!
