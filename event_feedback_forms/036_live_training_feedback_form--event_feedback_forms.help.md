# Live Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants on live training sessions provided by the organization. It is essential to fill out this form to help the organization improve the quality of the training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the event date of the training session you attended.
2. Enter the name of the session you attended.
3. Rate the session on a scale of 1-5.
4. Provide your feedback on the session.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them in the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the live training session you attended.
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the session you attended.
* **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the session on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide your feedback on the session, including what you liked and what you disliked.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them in this field.

## Tips
* Please be specific and honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve the quality of the training sessions.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for future sessions, please include them in the "Additional Feedback" field.
* If you have any technical issues or problems during the session, please mention them in the "Feedback" field.
