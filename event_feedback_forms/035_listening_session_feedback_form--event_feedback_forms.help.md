# Listening Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Listening Session Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback about the songs played during a listening session. This form helps to gather ratings and comments for each song, allowing us to understand your opinion on the music played.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback on the listening session, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of each song played during the session in the corresponding "Song" field (e.g., "Song 1", "Song 2", etc.).
2. For each song, provide a rating by selecting a numerical value in the "Rating" field (e.g., "Rating 1", "Rating 2", etc.).
3. Optionally, provide more details about the scale used for rating in the "Scale" field (e.g., "Scale 1", "Scale 2", etc.).
4. Decide whether to submit your feedback by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field.
