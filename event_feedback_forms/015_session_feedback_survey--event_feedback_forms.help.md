# Session Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you understand what the Session Feedback Survey form is and how to complete it effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select a page number to navigate through the form.
2. For each page, read the label carefully and choose the most accurate option or provide a clear text response if required.
3. If a field is required, ensure you fill it out before proceeding to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback_survey`, 1, false): This field allows you to provide a brief description of your thoughts on the session's overall feedback.
* **Session Effectiveness** (`session_effectiveness`, 2, false): This field asks whether the session was effective in achieving its goals. Please select 'Yes' if you believe the session was effective and 'No' otherwise.
* **Session Outcomes** (`session_outcomes`, 3, false): This field is for providing more details on the outcomes of the session. Please provide any notable outcomes or takeaways from the session.
* **Educator Feedback** (`educator_feedback`, 4, false): This field allows you to give feedback on the educator or facilitator's performance. Please select 'Yes' if they were effective and 'No' otherwise.
* **Event Outcomes** (`event_outcomes`, 5, false): This field is similar to 'Session Outcomes' but focused on the event as a whole, not just the session. Please provide any notable outcomes or takeaways from the event.
* **Session Improvement** (`session_improvement`, 6, false): This field asks whether there are any areas for improvement for future sessions. Please select 'Yes' if you have suggestions and 'No' otherwise.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, 7, false): This field is a numerical rating of your overall satisfaction with the session, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, 8, false): This field is for providing any additional suggestions you have for improving the session or event.
