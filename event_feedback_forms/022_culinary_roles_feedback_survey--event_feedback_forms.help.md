# Culinary Roles Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have interacted with a culinary team or organization. The goal is to gather data on the user's culinary role, their overall rating of the experience, and any additional comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the culinary roles you have interacted with by checking the relevant boxes.
2. Provide an overall rating of your experience.
3. Indicate how often you interact with the team.
4. Offer any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback (role)** (`feedback`, `select_multiple`): Select one or multiple culinary roles you have interacted with, such as Chef, Pastry, or Manager.
* **Other Role** (`other_role`, `text`): If you have interacted with a role not listed, please specify it here.
* **Role Other Text** (`role_other_text`, `text`): Provide more details about the other role you selected.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`): Rate your overall experience from 1-10.
* **How Often** (`how_often`, `select_one`): Indicate how often you interact with the team: daily, weekly, monthly, or rarely.

## Tips

* Please select all relevant culinary roles you have interacted with.
* Be as specific as possible when providing additional feedback.
