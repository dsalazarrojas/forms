# post-game-fan-feedback-poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from fans after a game, gathering information on their overall satisfaction, experiences with venue amenities, and other specific aspects of the event. Your input will help us understand how to improve future game-day experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each question carefully and select the answer that best reflects your experience.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (*): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (e.g., 1-5 scale).
* **Venue Amenities**: List some of the amenities you saw or experienced at the venue, such as food options or restrooms.
* **Event Experience** (*): Select one of the options that best describes your event experience (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Game Day Experiences** (*): Select all that apply from the list of options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Food and Beverage** (*): Select all that apply from the list of options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **Staff and Communication** (*): Select one of the options that best describes your experience with staff and communication (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Parking and Transportation** (*): Select one of the options that best describes your experience with parking and transportation (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any issues with the form or your answers, don't hesitate to contact us for support.
