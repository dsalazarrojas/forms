# Drama Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "Drama Workshop Feedback Survey" form, explaining each field and providing instructions on how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please take a few minutes to review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2. Fill out the form completely, providing your honest feedback for each field.
3. If any field is not applicable to your experience, please mark it as "None of the above" or indicate that it is not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the workshop session you attended.
* **Workshop Participants** (`workshop_participants`, select_multiple, optional): Please select whether or not the workshop had a diverse range of participants.
	+ "Yes" : The workshop had a diverse range of participants.
	+ "No" : The workshop did not have a diverse range of participants.
* **Feedback on Materials** (`feedback_on_materials`, select_one, optional): Provide feedback on the quality of materials provided for the workshop.
	+ "Workshop materials were provided." : The materials were provided and satisfactory.
	+ "Workbooks or printed materials were not provided." : Materials were not provided.
	+ "None of the above." : Other issues or concerns with materials.
* **Feedback on Facilitators** (`feedback_on_facilitators`, select_one, optional): Provide feedback on the performance of the facilitators.
	+ "Excellent" : The facilitators were exceptional.
	+ "Good" : The facilitators were average.
	+ "Fair" : The facilitators were somewhat disappointing.
	+ "Poor" : The facilitators were very disappointing.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience at the workshop.
	+ "Excellent" : The workshop was exceptional.
	+ "Good" : The workshop was average.
	+ "Fair" : The workshop was somewhat disappointing.
	+ "Poor" : The workshop was very disappointing.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or recommendations for future workshops.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help improve the workshop.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Recommendations" field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a staff member or organizer for clarification.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback on your experience!
