# Theater Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a theater's availability and conditions to help event planners and coordinators make informed decisions about event scheduling and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select "Yes" or "No" for each day you want to indicate if the theater is available.
2.  Enter a time in 24-hour format, if the theater is available at a specific time.
3.  Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the theater has seating capacity.
4.  Enter the estimated number of people attending the event.
5.  Select "Yes" or "No" for each accessibility feature you have at the theater.
6.  Add any notes or comments about the theater's condition or concerns.
7.  Enter your email address for us to contact you.
8.  Enter your phone number.
9.  Enter your name.
10.  Choose a category for your event.
11.  Write a brief description of your event.
12.  Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Days**: Select the days of the week that the theater will be available.
* **Time**: Enter the time of day when the theater will be available. Please use 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
* **Seating**: Confirm if the theater has seating capacity for your event.
* **Group Size**: Enter the estimated number of people attending the event.
* **Accessibility**: Confirm if the theater has any accessibility features such as wheelchair ramps, elevators, or accessible seating.
* **Notes**: Add any notes or comments about the theater's condition or concerns.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Name**: Enter your name or the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Category**: Choose a category for your event (e.g., performance, concert, meeting).
* **Description**: Write a brief description of your event.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to send the form and we will review it.
