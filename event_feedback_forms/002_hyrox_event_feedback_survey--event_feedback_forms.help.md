# hyrox_event_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hyrox_event_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback from event participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** in the "Event Name" field. This is the title of the event that you participated in.
2. Enter the **Event Location** in the "Event Location" field. This is where the event took place.
3. Provide a **Satisfaction Score** in the "Satisfaction Score" field. Rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale.
4. If you have any **Improvement Suggestions**, enter them in the "Improvement Suggestions" field. This is an optional field, so you can skip it if you don't have any suggestions.
5. Enter your **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field. This is your name as a participant.
6. Enter your **Event Date** in the "Event Date" field. This is the date of the event you participated in.
7. Enter your **Participant Email** in the "Participant Email" field. Make sure to use the correct email format (e.g., "name@example.com").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (Required): Enter the title of the event.
* **Event Location** (Required): Enter the name of the venue or location where the event took place.
* **Satisfaction Score** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (Optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them here. This is a free-form text field for you to provide your thoughts.
* **Participant Name** (Required): Enter your name as a participant in the event.
* **Event Date** (Required): Enter the date of the event in the format specified.
* **Participant Email** (Required): Enter your email address in the format "name@example.com".
