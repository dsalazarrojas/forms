# Episode Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings for episodes, providing an opportunity for the creators or owners of the episodes to understand their audience's opinions and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter the episode ID to help us identify the episode you are evaluating.
2. Select a rating for the episode, from 1 (poor) to 7 (excellent).
3. Provide your feedback on the episode in the text area.
4. Enter a rating value, which is the specific value of your rating (e.g., 4 for a 4-star rating).
5. Select multiple rating scales to choose the aspects of the episode that you want to rate.
6. Enter a date for when you watched or attended the episode.
7. If applicable, add any additional comments or notes about the episode in the text area.
8. Enter your contact information for follow-up or to confirm your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Episode** (episode_id, text, required): Enter the ID or title of the episode you are evaluating.
* **Rating** (rating, number, required): Select a rating for the episode, from 1 (poor) to 7 (excellent).
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, required): Provide a detailed review or comment about the episode.
* **Rating Value** (rating_value, number, required): Enter the specific value of your rating (e.g., 4 for a 4-star rating).
* **Rating Scale** (rating_scale, select_one, required): Choose the rating scale that best suits your evaluation.
* **Rating Value 2** (rating_value2, number, required): Enter the specific value of your second rating.
* **Rating Scale 2** (rating_scale2, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple rating scales that apply to your evaluation.
* **Date** (date, date, required): Enter the date when you watched or attended the episode.
* **Time** (time, time, required): Enter the time when you watched or attended the episode.
* **Note** (note, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the episode.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your contact information for follow-up or to confirm your evaluation.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up or to confirm your evaluation.
* **Created At** (created_at, date, required): This field is automatically populated and indicates when the evaluation was created.
* **Updated At** (updated_at, date, required): This field is automatically populated and indicates when the evaluation was last updated.
* **Deleted At** (deleted_at, date, required): This field is automatically populated and indicates when the episode was deleted. 

Please note that some fields, like "Rating Scale 2", "Rating Value 2", and "Created At", "Updated At", "Deleted At" are not used in most scenarios. However, they are included in case you need to collect more specific data or ratings in the future.
