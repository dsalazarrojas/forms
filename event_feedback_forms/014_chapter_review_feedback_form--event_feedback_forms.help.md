# Chapter Review Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from chapter members or attendees after a chapter event, helping to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Chapter Review Feedback --** (`section_header`, note, not required): This is a heading that introduces the feedback form.
* **respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, not required): Fill in your name if you want to provide feedback. It is optional for anonymous feedback.
* **event date** (`event_date`, date, required): Fill in the date of the chapter meeting or event you are providing feedback for.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event. Choose one of the options from Very dissatisfied to Very satisfied.
* **top highlights** (`top_highlights`, text, not required): Describe the top things that worked well during the meeting.
* **areas for improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, not required): Suggest improvements for future meetings.
* **volunteer interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, not required): Indicate your level of interest in volunteering.
* **session relevance** (`session_relevance`, select_one, required): Rate the relevance of the topics covered during the event. Choose one of the options from Not relevant to Highly relevant.
* **additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Share any other feedback or thoughts you have.
* **follow up contact** (`follow_up_contact`, email, not required): If you'd like to receive a follow-up response, provide your email address.
