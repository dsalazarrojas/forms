# Stadium Amenity Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on your overall experience at the stadium. Your input will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for future visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the best answer that reflects your experience.
2. Rate your experience using the provided scale (e.g., number or select_one/number options).
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have in the "Comments" and "Additional Comments" fields.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience** (`OverallExperience`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the stadium on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Food and Beverage** (`FoodandBeverage`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with food and beverage services at the stadium, such as food quality, variety, and service speed.
* **Cleanliness** (`Cleanliness`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply from the following:
	+ Option 1: The stadium was very clean.
	+ Option 2: The stadium was clean but not perfect.
	+ Option 3: The stadium was not clean.
* **Amenities** (`Amenities`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply from the following:
	+ Option 1: I used the restroom facilities.
	+ Option 2: I used the food and beverage outlets.
	+ Option 3: I used the parking facilities.
	+ Option 4: I used other amenities (please specify).
* **Accessibility** (`Accessibility`, select_one, required: false): Select one option that best describes your experience:
	+ Option 1: The stadium was easily accessible for people with disabilities.
	+ Option 2: The stadium was moderately accessible for people with disabilities.
	+ Option 3: The stadium was not accessible for people with disabilities.
* **Staff Friendliness** (`Stafffriendly`, number, required: false): Rate the friendliness of the staff on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Parking** (`Parking`, number, required: false): Rate the parking facilities on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`AdditionalComments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.

Tips:

* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve our services.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the "Comments" and "Additional Comments" fields.
* If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions, please provide your email and phone number.
