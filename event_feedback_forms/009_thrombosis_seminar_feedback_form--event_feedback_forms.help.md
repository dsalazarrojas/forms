# Thrombosis Seminar Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Thrombosis Seminar Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback from users who attend the thrombosis seminar. This feedback is invaluable in helping us refine the content and overall quality of the seminar, making it better for everyone in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please fill in the required fields:
    *   **Name**: Enter your first and last name in this field.
    *   **Department**: Choose your department from the select options.
    *   **Feedback**: Share your detailed feedback about the seminar.
    *   **Contact**: Provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.
    *   **Additional Info**: If you want to share more details, you can do so here.
2.  The **Name** and **Feedback** fields are mandatory to ensure that your feedback is properly recorded. You must fill these in to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name to help us identify you correctly.
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Choose your department from the select options to help us understand your perspective accurately.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Share your honest and detailed feedback about the seminar.
*   **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, required): Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you with any further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
*   **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information or comments that you'd like to share with us, please provide them here.

## Tips

*   Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback to help us understand your concerns better.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, don't hesitate to ask us.
*   Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input in making the seminar better.
