# Open Evening Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of an event about their experience. It covers various aspects, including the purpose of the event, the relevance of the event topic, the most interesting aspect, satisfaction with event organization, willingness to attend another event, suggestions, venue impressions, likelihood to recommend, event duration, and any additional comments or suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Provide clear and concise answers in the fields provided.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label.
4. If you have any other comments or suggestions, use the "other_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the purpose of this event?** (page 1, field 1): This field asks about the main objective or goal of the event. Please provide a brief description of why the event was held.
* **How would you rate the relevance of the event topic to your interests?** (page 1, field 2): This field asks you to rate how relevant the event topic was to your personal interests. Use a number to indicate your level of interest, with 1 being the least interested and 5 being the most interested.
* **What was the most interesting aspect of the event?** (page 1, field 3): This field is for any particularly notable or engaging part of the event. Feel free to describe the moment or experience that stood out to you.
* **Were you satisfied with the event organization?** (page 2, field 4): This field asks about your overall satisfaction with how the event was organized, including planning, execution, and communication.
* **Would you attend another event like this?** (page 2, field 5): This field determines your willingness to participate in similar events in the future.
* **Do you have any suggestions for future events?** (page 3, field 6): This field is for any ideas or feedback you have for future events, such as changes, improvements, or new ideas.
* **What did you think of the venue?** (page 3, field 7): This field is for your impressions of the venue where the event took place. Describe the layout, facilities, or any notable features.
* **How likely are you to recommend this event to a friend?** (page 4, field 8): This field asks about your confidence level in recommending the event to others, with 0 being not at all likely and 10 being extremely likely.
* **How long did you stay at the event?** (page 4, field 9): This field asks for the length of time you spent at the event.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions?** (page 5, field 10): This field is for any additional thoughts, ideas, or feedback you might have that don't fit in other fields.
