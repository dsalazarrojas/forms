# Post Event Networking Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on the recent post-event networking session. Your input will help us improve future events and ensure they are engaging and valuable for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Answer the questions about your experience with the meeting, its location, content, and overall organization in the respective fields.
3. For the "Would you attend again" question, choose one of the options provided.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please provide your first name.
  - **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Please provide your last name.
  - **How were the meetings?** (`feedback_on_meetings`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts on the meeting experience.
  - **Feedback on Content** (`feedback_on_content`, text, required/optional): Describe your opinion on the meeting content.
  - **Location Feedback** (`feedback_on_location`, text, required/optional): Tell us about your experience with the meeting location.
  - **Networking Feedback** (`feedback_on_networking`, text, required/optional): Express your feelings about the networking opportunities.
  - **Scheduling Feedback** (`feedback_on_scheduling`, text, required/optional): Give us your thoughts on the meeting scheduling.
  - **Suggestion for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required/optional): Share any suggestions you might have for improvement.
  - **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 10.
  - **Would you attend again?** (`would_attend_again`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the provided options.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please provide your phone number.
  - **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions.

Note: Some fields are optional, but we appreciate your participation as it will help us improve future events. If you have any further questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
