# Soul Steel Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soul Steel Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from users after an event. This form is used by the event organizers to gauge the effectiveness of the event and make necessary improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button to start the survey.
2. Select the appropriate page (1) and fill out the "Event Feedback" field with your thoughts on the event's concept.
3. Move to the next page (2) and enter your name in the "participant_name" field.
4. On the next page (3), provide your feedback on the "What did you think of the event's concept?" field.
5. Next (4), share your thoughts on "What did you think of the event's execution?"
6. On the subsequent pages (5-9), rate the event's "Concept", "Execution", "Organisation", "Content", "Ambiance", and "Speaker" respectively, using a scale of 1-5.
7. Make sure to provide genuine and detailed feedback to help the event organizers improve the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Feedback**: A text field where you can share your general thoughts on the event's concept. 
* **participant_name**: This field requires your name to be attributed to your feedback. Please ensure it is accurate to reflect your genuine identity.
* **What did you think of the event's concept?**: Share your thoughts on the event's idea or idea behind it.
* **What did you think of the event's execution?**: Provide feedback on how well the event was organized and executed.
* **Event Concept**: Rate the event's concept on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Event Execution**: Assess the event's execution on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Event Organisation**: Evaluate the event's organization on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Event Content**: Rate the event's content on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Event Ambiance**: Provide your assessment of the event's ambiance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Event Speaker**: Rate the event's speaker on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

## Tips
* Please be specific with your feedback to help the event organizers make accurate improvements.
* Avoid using jargon or overly technical terms that might not be understood by non-experts.
* Ensure that your feedback is respectful and constructive.
* Submit your feedback within the given timeframe to guarantee it reaches the event organizers in a timely manner.
