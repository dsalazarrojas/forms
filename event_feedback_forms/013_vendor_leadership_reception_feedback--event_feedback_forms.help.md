# Vendor Leadership Reception Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from attendees on the leadership reception event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Vendor Leadership Reception Feedback" form.
2. Review the form fields and provide your feedback on the event.
3. Fill in the form by entering your feedback in the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Leadership Reception Feedback Form** (`vendor_leadership_reception_feedback_form`, text, optional): This field is the main section of the form where you can provide your overall feedback on the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and experiences on the event.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you attended the event.
* **Additional Attendees Information** (`attendees_other`, text, optional): If you attended with others, please specify their names here.
* **Vendor** (`vendor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor you interacted with.
