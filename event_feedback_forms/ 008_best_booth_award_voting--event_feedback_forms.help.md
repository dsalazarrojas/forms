# Best Booth Award Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Best Booth Award Voting" form is designed to collect feedback on the quality and effectiveness of various booth presentations during events. This form helps event organizers understand which booths are performing well and which areas need improvement. Your input is crucial in helping us create an engaging and informative event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Personal Information** section, which includes:
	* **Full name**: Enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Email address**: Provide a valid email address for contact purposes.
	* **Phone number**: Include your country code if your phone number is outside the local region (optional but recommended).
2. Select the relevant **Date** for your feedback, as this will help in identifying the specific time frame of the event.
3. In the **Primary selection** section, choose the option that best applies from the provided options:
	* **Excellent**: The presentation was outstanding in all aspects.
	* **Good**: The presentation was satisfactory but had some minor issues.
	* **Fair**: The presentation was average, with notable room for improvement.
	* **Poor**: The presentation was not satisfactory.
4. If applicable, provide a **Quantity or rating** for the presentation, using whole numbers for a more accurate score.
5. Add any **Additional details** about the presentation that you think are relevant to the feedback.
6. Confirm your **Agreement** with the presentation by selecting any applicable options:
	* **I agree**: You found the presentation informative and engaging.
	* **I need follow-up**: You would like more information, clarification, or a follow-up on the presentation.
	* **Not applicable**: The presentation was not relevant to you.
7. If applicable, provide an **Estimated value** of the presentation, using decimal format for more precision.
8. Finally, enter your preferred **Preferred time** for the presentation if scheduling is required (optional).
9. Add any **Final comments or notes** about the presentation, if you'd like to provide additional context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`3`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (`4`, `text`, optional): Include your country code if your phone number is outside the local region.
* **Date** (`5`, `date`, required): Select the relevant date for your feedback.
* **Primary selection** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best applies from **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, or **Poor**.
* **Quantity or rating** (`7`, `number`, optional): Provide a whole number for a more accurate score.
* **Additional details** (`8`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about the presentation.
* **Agreement** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement with the presentation by selecting any applicable options.
* **Estimated value** (`10`, `number`, optional): Use decimal format for more precision.
* **Preferred time** (`11`, `time`, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (`12`, `text`, optional): Add any additional context or comments about the presentation.
