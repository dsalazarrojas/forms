# Art Exhibition Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors about their experience at the art exhibition. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out each field with your thoughts and observations about your visit to the art exhibition.
* Be as detailed as you can in your responses.
* If you have any questions or need help with a particular field, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Visitor name
* **Visitor name** (`visitor_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name, so we can address your feedback to you.
### 2. Visit date
* **Visit date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you visited the exhibition.
### 3. Exhibition rating
* **Exhibition rating** (`exhibition_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the exhibition overall, based on your experience.
### 4. Favorite piece or exhibit
* **Favorite piece or exhibit** (`favorite_piece`, `text`, optional): If you have a favorite piece or exhibit from the exhibition, please share its title or description.
### 5. Signage clarity
* **Signage clarity** (`signage_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate how clear the exhibit labels were during your visit.
### 6. Staff helpfulness
* **Staff helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the helpfulness of the staff during your visit.
### 7. Facilities cleanliness
* **Facilities cleanliness** (`facilities_cleanliness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cleanliness of the facilities, such as restrooms and gallery areas.
### 8. Accessibility
* **Accessibility experience** (`accessibility`, `text`, optional): If you encountered any barriers or difficulties during your visit, please describe them.
### 9. Suggestions for improvement
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have any ideas on how we can improve the exhibition, please share them.
### 10. Contact for follow-up
* **Contact for follow-up** (`contact_for_follow_up`, `email`, optional): If you'd like a response to your feedback, please provide your email address.

Note: If you have any questions or need help with a particular field, feel free to ask. We appreciate your input!
