# Sleepover Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a sleepover event. It gathers information about the event's details, overall experience, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Provide the title of the event.
3. Enter the date the event took place.
4. Enter the start time of the event.
5. Enter the end time of the event.
6. Enter the duration of the event.
7. Enter the location of the event.
8. Enter the name of the event's organizer.
9. Summarize your overall experience with the event.
10. Provide any positive comments about the event.
11. Identify areas where the event could improve.
12. Add any additional comments about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the feedback report.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the event as it was known or titled.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the event took place.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the length of the event in hours.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required): Enter the address or location where the event took place.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, `text`, required): Enter the name or title of the event's organizer.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `text`, required): Summarize your experience with the event, e.g., "It was great!", "It was okay.", etc.
* **Positive Comments** (`positive_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any positive comments or feedback about the event.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, required): Identify areas where the event could improve, e.g., more food, different venue, etc.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the event.

Note: This form is designed for user feedback and should not be used for promotional purposes.
