# Critical Care Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from workshop participants on their experience with the Critical Care Workshop. It will help us understand how you felt about the workshop's content, organization, and overall quality. Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Critical Care Workshop Feedback**:
	+ This field is a free-text area where you can share your thoughts and opinions about the workshop.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your experience with the workshop, highlighting what you enjoyed and what you think could be improved.
* **Overall Rating**:
	+ This field is a 5-point scale where you can rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop.
	+ Please select one of the five options to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **How satisfied are you with this workshop?**:
	+ This field is also a 5-point scale where you can rate your satisfaction with the workshop.
	+ Please select one of the five options to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **What can we do better?**:
	+ This field is a free-text area where you can suggest ways to improve the workshop.
	+ Please provide specific and actionable suggestions for future workshops.
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ This field is a free-text area where you can share any additional thoughts or ideas you have about the workshop.
	+ Please keep it concise and relevant to the workshop.
* **Email**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter your email address.
	+ Please make sure to enter a valid email address if you would like to receive updates or notifications about future workshops.
* **Phone**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter your phone number.
	+ Please make sure to enter a valid phone number if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or questions.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* Be specific and concrete when providing feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
