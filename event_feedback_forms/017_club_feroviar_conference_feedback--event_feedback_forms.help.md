# Club Feroviar Conference Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for attendees to provide feedback on their experience at the Club Feroviar Conference. Your responses will help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with the conference.
3. Rate the quality of the content, speakers, and other aspects of the event.
4. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your registration or business card.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted if needed.
* **Organization or Club**: If you represent an organization or club, enter its name.
* **Conference Date Attended**: Select the date you attended the conference.
* **Sessions or Workshops Attended**: Select all the sessions or workshops you attended.
* **Content Relevance and Quality**: Rate the quality of the content provided during the conference. Choose the best option that reflects your experience.
	+ Poor: The content was not relevant or was of poor quality.
	+ Fair: The content was somewhat relevant, but not particularly memorable or impactful.
	+ Good: The content was good and somewhat relevant.
	+ Very Good: The content was very relevant and well-presented.
	+ Excellent: The content was outstanding and exceeded your expectations.
* **Speaker Quality and Knowledge**: Rate the quality of the speakers. Choose the best option that reflects your experience.
	+ Poor: The speakers were not knowledgeable or engaging.
	+ Fair: The speakers were somewhat knowledgeable, but not particularly memorable or impactful.
	+ Good: The speakers were good and somewhat engaging.
	+ Very Good: The speakers were very knowledgeable and engaging.
	+ Excellent: The speakers were outstanding and exceeded your expectations.
* **Networking Opportunities**: Rate the value of the networking opportunities. Choose the best option that reflects your experience.
	+ Poor: The networking opportunities were not useful or were limited.
	+ Fair: The networking opportunities were somewhat useful, but not particularly memorable or impactful.
	+ Good: The networking opportunities were good and somewhat useful.
	+ Very Good: The networking opportunities were very useful.
	+ Excellent: The networking opportunities were outstanding and exceeded your expectations.
* **Venue and Facilities**: Rate the quality of the venue and facilities. Choose the best option that reflects your experience.
	+ Poor: The venue and facilities were not satisfactory.
	+ Fair: The venue and facilities were somewhat satisfactory.
	+ Good: The venue and facilities were good.
	+ Very Good: The venue and facilities were very good.
	+ Excellent: The venue and facilities were outstanding and exceeded your expectations.
* **Food and Catering**: Rate the quality of the food and catering. Choose the best option that reflects your experience.
	+ Poor: The food and catering were not satisfactory.
	+ Fair: The food and catering were somewhat satisfactory.
	+ Good: The food and catering were good.
	+ Very Good: The food and catering were very good.
	+ Excellent: The food and catering were outstanding and exceeded your expectations.
* **Event Organization and Flow**: Rate the effectiveness of the event organization. Choose the best option that reflects your experience.
	+ Poor: The event was not well-organized.
	+ Fair: The event was somewhat well-organized, but had some issues.
	+ Good: The event was well-organized and somewhat smooth.
	+ Very Good: The event was very well-organized and efficient.
	+ Excellent: The event was outstandingly well-organized and exceeded your expectations.
* **Usefulness of Provided Materials**: Rate the usefulness of the materials provided during the event. Choose the best option that reflects your experience.
	+ Not Useful: The materials were not useful.
	+ Somewhat Useful: The materials were somewhat useful.
	+ Useful: The materials were useful.
	+ Very Useful: The materials were very useful.
	+ Extremely Useful: The materials were extremely useful.
* **Likelihood to Attend Next Year**: Rate your likelihood of attending the conference next year. Choose the best option that reflects your experience.
	+ Definitely Not: You have no intention of attending next year.
	+ Probably Not: You are unlikely to attend next year.
	+ Maybe: You are unsure if you will attend next year.
	+ Probably Yes: You are likely to attend next year.
	+ Definitely Yes: You are certain to attend next year.
* **Topics for Next Year**: Provide any suggestions for topics you would like to see covered at future events.
* **Overall Feedback and Suggestions**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for the event.

Note: All fields are mandatory, except for **Venue and Facilities** and **Food and Catering**, which are optional.
