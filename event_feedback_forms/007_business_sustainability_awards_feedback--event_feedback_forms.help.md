The Business Sustainability Awards Feedback form is designed to collect input from attendees on their experience of the Business Sustainability Awards event. ## Business Sustainability Awards Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Sustainability Awards Feedback form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience at the Business Sustainability Awards event. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each page of the form, starting from Page 1.
2. Answer each question carefully and provide clear and concise responses.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "How Can We Improve the Event" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Please fill out your full name as registered with the event.
* **Your Organization** (`organization_name`, text, required): Please enter your company or organization name, if any.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, email, required): Please enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Your Role at the Event** (`attendance_role`, select_one, required): Please select your primary role at the event:
	+ Attendee
	+ Speaker
	+ Sponsor
	+ Organizer
	+ Media
	+ Volunteer
* **Overall Event Organization** (`event_organization`, select_one, required): Please rate the overall organization of the event:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Venue and Facilities Experience** (`venue_experience`, select_one, required): Please rate the quality of the venue and facilities:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Quality of Content and Presentations** (`content_quality`, select_one, required): Please rate the quality of the content and presentations:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Networking Opportunities** (`networking_opportunities`, select_one, required): Please rate the quality of networking opportunities:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Relevance of Sustainability Focus** (`sustainability_focus`, select_one, required): Please rate the relevance of the sustainability focus:
	+ Highly Relevant
	+ Relevant
	+ Somewhat Relevant
	+ Not Relevant
	+ Not at All Relevant
* **Quality of Award Winners Selected** (`award_winners`, select_one, required): Please rate the quality of award winners selected:
	+ Excellent Choices
	+ Good Choices
	+ Average
	+ Poor Choices
	+ Very Poor Choices
* **What Did You Enjoy Most About the Event** (`most_enjoyed`, text, optional): Please highlight any positive experiences you had at the event.
* **How Can We Improve the Event** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Please provide any constructive feedback on how we can improve the event.
* **Would You Recommend This Event to Others** (`recommend_event`, select_one, required): Please indicate if you would recommend the event to others:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Will You Attend Next Year** (`future_attendance`, select_one, optional): Please indicate if you plan to attend next year's event:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No

Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
