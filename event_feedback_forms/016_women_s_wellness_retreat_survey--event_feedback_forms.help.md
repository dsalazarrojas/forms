# Women's Wellness Retreat Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of women's wellness retreats. It includes various fields to collect opinions, ratings, and demographic information. Your input will help event organizers create future events that meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the following fields:
	* `Survey Questions`: A free-text field where you can provide any comments or opinions about the retreat.
	* `Rating Scale`: A scale with options from 1 to 5, where you can rate your overall experience of the retreat.
	* `Feedback`: A free-text field for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
	* `Email`: Your email address, which will be used to identify you as a participant.
	* `Phone`: Your phone number (optional, but recommended in case we need to contact you about future events).
	* `Event Planner`: Select the organization or person who handled the event planning (Retreat Organizer, Wellness Studio, or Event Planner).
	* `Suggestions`: Any ideas or suggestions you have for future events.
	* `Comments`: Any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
	* `Date`: The date of the retreat (optional, but helpful for event planners).
	* `Time`: The time of the retreat (optional, but helpful for event planners).
	* `Submitted By`: Your name or title, which will be displayed on the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Questions**: Provide any comments or opinions about the retreat. Please be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Rating Scale**: Rate your overall experience of the retreat using the scale from 1 to 5. A 5-star rating indicates a highly positive experience, while a 1-star rating indicates a poor experience.
* **Feedback**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about the retreat, such as what you liked or disliked.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about future events.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about future events.
* **Event Planner**: Select the organization or person who handled the event planning.
* **Suggestions**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have for future events.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the retreat, if you remember it.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the retreat, if you remember it.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name or title, which will be displayed on the report.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields accurately and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask the event organizers for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event planners using the provided email or phone number.
