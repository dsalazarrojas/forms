# Participant Networking Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Participant Networking Feedback Questionnaire, an essential tool for event organizers to gauge participant engagement and satisfaction with the networking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event you attended in the "Participant Event Name" field.
2. Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you interacted with other participants during the event.
3. Provide feedback on your interactions with other participants by selecting all that apply from the "Feedback on Interaction" field.
4. Rate your overall networking experience on a scale from 1 to 5 in the "Overall Networking Satisfaction" field.
5. Share your suggestions for improving future events in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
6. Check if the event facilitators were available and approachable in the "Event Facilitators" field.
7. Indicate whether you found the event format to be effective or not in the "Feedback on Format" field.
8. Enter the location where the event was held in the "Event Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Event Name** (`participant_event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you attended to help us identify the specific event you are providing feedback about.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you attended an event with other participants, or "No" to indicate that you did not.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to help us associate your feedback with your participation in the event (optional but recommended).
* **Feedback on Interaction** (`feedback_on_interaction`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to indicate how you interacted with other participants, such as "Yes, I interacted with other participants", "Yes, I met new people", etc.
* **Networking Experience** (`networking_experience`, number, optional): Rate your overall networking experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving future events.
* **Event Facilitators** (`event_facilitators`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that the event facilitators were available and approachable, or "No" to indicate that they were not.
* **Overall Networking Satisfaction** (`overall_networking_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the networking experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Feedback on Format** (`feedback_on_format`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to indicate whether the event format was effective or not, such as "Too short", "Too long", etc.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the event was held.
