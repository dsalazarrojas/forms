# Soundscape Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after an event, focusing on their ratings, emotions experienced, suggestions, and comments about the event organization and facilitation. The form will help us understand what participants think about the soundscape experience and provide valuable insights to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please answer the questions honestly and accurately**. We value your feedback and want to make sure we capture your true feelings and thoughts about the soundscape experience.
2. **Fill in each field with your responses**. Each field is clearly labeled to help you navigate the form.
3. **Submit your form when you're finished**. You can save and come back to the form later if you need more time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Ratings (1)**:
	+ Type: Number
	+ Purpose: Rate your overall experience with the soundscape.
	+ Hint: Think about your emotions and satisfaction with the event.
* **Overall Emotions (2)**:
	+ Type: Number
	+ Purpose: Rate your emotions experienced during the event, with higher numbers indicating more positive emotions.
	+ Hint: Think about your emotional state during the event and how it affected you.
* **Suggestions (3)**:
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Share your ideas and suggestions for improving future soundscape events.
	+ Hint: Be as specific and detailed as possible with your suggestions.
* **Artist Notes (4)**:
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Share any comments or thoughts you have about the artist and their performance.
	+ Hint: Be kind and constructive in your feedback.
* **Event Organizer Notes (5)**:
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Share any comments or suggestions you have about the event organization and logistics.
	+ Hint: Focus on what went well and what could be improved.
* **Facilitator Notes (6)**:
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Share any comments or suggestions you have about the facilitator's performance.
	+ Hint: Be specific and focus on behaviors or actions that helped or hindered your experience.

## Tips
* Please take your time and answer each question honestly and thoughtfully.
* Be as specific as possible with your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We value your feedback and will use it to improve future soundscape experiences.
