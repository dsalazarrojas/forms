# Winter Illumination Voting Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of the Winter Illumination event. Your participation will help us improve the event in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out as many or as few questions as you feel comfortable answering. Each question has a corresponding field where you can enter your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating_1`, Number, Optional)
Enter your overall rating of the Winter Illumination event. This can be any numerical value between 1 and 5.

### 2. **Favorite Color** (`favorite_color`, Text, Optional)
Choose your favorite color from the event. You can enter any color you like, or leave this field blank if you don't have a favorite color.

### 3. **Favorite Character** (`favorite_character`, Text, Optional)
Choose your favorite character from the event. You can enter any name or description of the character you enjoyed most.

### 4. **Event Experience** (`event_experience`, Text, Optional)
Describe your overall experience at the event. Share any highlights or low points you'd like to mention.
