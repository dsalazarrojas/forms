# corporate_training_program_participation_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from participants after they have attended a corporate training program. It helps us understand their overall satisfaction, experience with the trainer, and areas for improvement in the training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the `Training Program Name`, which is the name of the training program you attended.
2. Provide the location of the training session.
3. Enter the date you attended the training session.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the training program on a scale of 1-5 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Dissatisfied).
5. Enter any comments or suggestions you have about the training program.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you.
7. Rate the trainer's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the training program (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
9. Check if you are enrolled in the training program (True or False).
10. Identify any areas for improvement in the training program (Content, Delivery, Organization, Timing, Other).
11. Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
12. Rate your overall satisfaction with the trainer's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
13. Enter the name of the training program you attended (again, for comparison).
14. Enter the duration of the training session.
15. Enter the start and end dates of the training session.
16. Enter the time spent in the session.
17. Check if the trainer was present in the session (Present, Absent, Unable to Attend).
18. Enter any improvement suggestions for the training program.
19. Enter any notes about the session.
20. Enter an evaluation of the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Program Name** (Text): Enter the name of the training program you attended.
* **Location** (Text): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Date Attended** (Date): Enter the date you attended the training session.
* **Rating** (Number): Rate your satisfaction with the training program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (Text): Enter any comments or suggestions you have about the training program.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Trainer Performance** (Select One): Rate the trainer's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select Multiple): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training program (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Enrollment** (Select Multiple): Check if you are enrolled in the training program (True or False).
* **Improvement** (Select Multiple): Identify any areas for improvement in the training program (Content, Delivery, Organization, Timing, Other).
* **Trainer Name** (Text): Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Trainer Evaluation** (Text): Enter an evaluation of the trainer.
* **Training Program Name 2** (Text): Enter the name of the training program you attended (again, for comparison).
* **Training Duration** (Text): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Start Date** (Date): Enter the start date of the training session.
* **End Date** (Date): Enter the end date of the training session.
* **Session Time** (Text): Enter the time spent in the session.
* **Session Days** (Text): Enter the number of days spent in the session.
* **Trainer Attendance** (Select One): Check if the trainer was present in the session (Present, Absent, Unable to Attend).
* **Training Improvement** (Text): Enter any improvement suggestions for the training program.
* **Session Minutes** (Text): Enter any notes about the session.
* **Session Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about the session.
* **Trainer Evaluation** (Text): Enter an evaluation of the trainer.
