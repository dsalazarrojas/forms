# summer_stage_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "summer_stage_feedback" form is designed to collect feedback from event attendees to help the event organizers and management team make informed decisions for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the event from the calendar.
2. Enter the name of the event in the provided text field.
3. Type the location of the event in the text field.
4. Provide your feedback about the event in the note field.
5. Enter the name of the person or organization that organized the event in the text field.
6. Enter your email address in the email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Type the location of the event.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Provide your honest feedback about the event.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization that organized the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields as they are crucial for providing accurate feedback.
* If you're unsure about any information, feel free to ask the event organizers or management team for assistance.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can type them in the "Feedback" field.
* The information collected from this form will help us improve our events for the next season.
