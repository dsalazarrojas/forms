# Counseling Event Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with the counseling event. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make future events more valuable for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to include any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Feedback** (`event_feedback_form_1`): Describe the event in your own words. Please provide your thoughts and experiences.
* **Rating** (`event_feedback_form_2`): Choose one of the options to rate your overall experience:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **How would you rate the session facilitator?** (`event_feedback_form_3`): Choose one or more of the options to rate the facilitator:

	+ Fair
