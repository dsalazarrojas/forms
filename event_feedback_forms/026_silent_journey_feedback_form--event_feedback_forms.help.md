# Silent Journey Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Silent Journey Feedback Form is a tool to collect feedback from participants after a silent journey experience. This form helps us understand your thoughts, feelings, and perceptions about the journey, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and create a more enriching experience for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as you like.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, choosing from the dropdown menus.
3. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if you'd like to receive any follow-up information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think of the silent journey?** (`title`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and feelings about the journey. This is a open-ended text field where you can write as much or as little as you like.
* **How did you feel about the level of silence during the journey?** (`silence`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options to describe your experience:
	+ Extremely satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Extremely dissatisfied
	+ Not sure
* **What connections did you make with your fellow participants?** (`connection`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Stronger sense of community
	+ New friendships
	+ Improved relationships
	+ No connections made
* **What did you learn about yourself during the journey?** (`learning`, text, optional): Share your personal growth and insights gained from the experience.
* **What did you learn about personal growth during the journey?** (`growth`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ A lot
	+ A little
	+ Not much
* **Do you have any recommendations for future silent journeys?** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Share your suggestions and ideas for improving the experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive any follow-up information.
