# festival_experience_feedback_survey_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback from festival attendees about their recent experience at the event. The feedback will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve on for future events. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and share your thoughts with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to get an idea of what we're looking for.
2. Rate your overall experience at the festival by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
3. Provide any comments or thoughts you have about your experience.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
5. If you'd like to share your phone number, you can do so in the "Your Phone" field.
6. If you have any suggestions for us, please type them in the "Suggestions" field.
7. Finally, click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rate Your Experience** (`rate_your_experience`, Number, required): Rate your overall experience at the festival from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Provide Comments** (`comments`, Text, required): Share any thoughts, positive or negative, about your experience. This will help us understand what we did well and where we can improve.
* **Your Email** (`email`, Email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Your Phone** (`phone`, Text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your phone number.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, Text, optional): If you have any suggestions for us, please type them in this field.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text, required): Click this button to send your feedback to us.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide constructive feedback.
- Remember, your feedback is anonymous, and we'll only use it to improve future events.
