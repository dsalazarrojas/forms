# Healthcare Conference Attendee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from conference attendees to help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have attended the conference before submitting your feedback.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. If you have suggestions or comments, use the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: This field is used to collect your contact information, which will help us respond to your feedback and stay in touch if needed.
* **Session Ratings**: This field is where you can rate your experience with the sessions you attended. Please provide your honest ratings to help us understand areas of improvement.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts on the conference, speakers, and content. This is a great opportunity to provide suggestions and comments on how we can improve.
* **Rating**: Please rate your overall experience with the conference. This will help us gauge the quality of the event.
* **Suggestion**: If you have any suggestions for improving the conference, please provide them here.
* **Comments**: If you have additional comments or suggestions that didn't fit in the previous fields, please add them here.
* **Agree with Speaker**: Please indicate if you agree with the speaker's presentation style and content.
* **Agree with Content**: Rate your agreement with the conference content.
* **Recommend Conference**: Indicate if you would recommend this conference to others.
* **Attend Other Events**: Let us know if you plan to attend other healthcare conferences in the future.
* **Improve Conference**: Share your ideas on how to improve the conference. This is a great opportunity to provide constructive feedback.
* **Contact Us**: If you have any questions or need to get in touch with us, please use this field to provide your contact information.
