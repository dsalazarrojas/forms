# National Policy Conference Raffle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees of the National Policy Conference on the sessions they attended. This feedback will be used to improve future conferences and make them more enjoyable and informative for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct answer for each question according to your experience and understanding.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the overall session you attended from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Which session did you attend** (`session_attended_1`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you attended the first session, 'No' otherwise.
* **Which session did you attend** (`session_attended_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all sessions you attended from the list, if applicable.
* **Which session did you attend** (`session_attended_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all sessions you attended from the list, if applicable.
* **Overall Session Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate the session you attended from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Session Content** (`session_content`, `text`, required): Describe the content of the session you attended.
* **Session Value** (`session_value`, `text`, required): Describe the value you gained from attending the session.
* **Raffle Entry** (`raffle_entry`, `text`, required): Enter your raffle entry for a chance to win a prize.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Suggestions for future session improvements** (`session_improvement`, `text`, optional): Suggest any improvements for future sessions.
* **Idea for future session content** (`session_improvement_ideas`, `text`, optional): Suggest any ideas for future session content.
* **Why these ideas?** (`session_improvement_ideas_why`, `text`, optional): Explain why you suggested these ideas.
* **More details about these ideas** (`session_improvement_ideas_details`, `text`, optional): Provide more details about your suggested ideas.
