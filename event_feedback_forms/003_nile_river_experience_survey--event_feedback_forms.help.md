# nile_river_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Nile River Experience Survey** form is designed to collect feedback and information about customer experiences with the Nile River cruise. This form will help us understand your satisfaction with our service and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions accurately.
2. Fill in your name and contact information (email and phone number).
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" section.
4. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing any general information about your experience with the Nile River cruise.
- **Cruise Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any details about your cruise, such as the date, duration, and activities you participated in.
- **Tour Details** (`3`, `text`, required: false): If you were on a guided tour, please describe the tour and any notable points about it.
- **Satisfaction** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the Nile River cruise experience, using a scale of 1-5.
- **Feedback** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Share any feedback you have about your experience, such as what you liked or disliked.
- **Contact Info** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and contact information so we can follow up with you.
- **Comments** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
- **Email** (`8`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address, which we will use to contact you.
- **Phone** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, which we will use to contact you.
- **Notes** (`10`, `note`, required: false): Leave any additional notes or comments that are not suitable for the "Comments" section.
- **Assigned To** (`11`, `text`, required: false): If this form was assigned to a specific person, please enter their name.
- **Assigned By** (`12`, `text`, required: false): If this form was assigned by a specific person, please enter their name.
- **Form ID** (`13`, `number`, required: false): A unique identifier for the form, do not enter anything here.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions accurately and honestly.
- If you need help with any part of the form, do not hesitate to ask.
- Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve our service.
