# Tournament Safety Measures Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help the organizers of the tournament understand how well the safety measures were implemented during the event. Your feedback will help us identify what worked well and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (Tournament Name, Rating, and Contact Information).
2. Provide any relevant comments or observations about the safety measures in the "Safety Measures" field.
3. Rate your overall experience with the safety measures on a scale of 1-5.
4. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have about the safety measures.
5. In the "Contact Information" field, provide your name and any other contact information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Name**: Please enter the name of the tournament you are providing feedback on.
* **Safety Measures**: Describe any observations or experiences you had with the safety measures in place during the tournament. For example, you might mention any issues you encountered or any measures that you thought were particularly effective.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the safety measures on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have about the safety measures. This is a great place to elaborate on your observations or ideas for improvement.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your name and any other contact information you would like to share so that we can follow up with you if necessary.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when describing your experiences with the safety measures.
* If you have any concerns or issues, try to provide as much detail as you can so that we can better understand the situation.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the safety measures, please share them in the "Comments" field.
