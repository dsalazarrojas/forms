# Sports Merchandise Vendor Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from sports merchandise vendors on their experience with events. Your input helps us understand your satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields and understand what we're asking you for.
2. Fill out each field as requested, taking care to provide accurate and complete information.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (Event Name, Text, Optional): Please enter the name of the event you're referring to. This helps us understand which event you're providing feedback about.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, Date, Optional): If applicable, enter the date of the event. This helps us pinpoint your experience within a specific time frame.
* **Venue Name** (Venue Name, Text, Optional): If the event took place in a specific venue, provide its name for reference.
* **Product Name** (Product Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the product you sold or managed during the event. This helps us understand what type of merchandise was involved.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, Note, Optional): Share any comments, thoughts, or suggestions you have about your experience. This is your chance to let us know how we can improve.
* **Rating** (Rating, Number, Optional): If you'd like to rate your overall experience, please enter a number from 1 to 5. A higher number indicates a better experience.
* **Vendor Name** (Vendor Name, Text, Optional): Please enter your name as a vendor for reference and to associate it with your feedback.
* **Vendor Email** (Vendor Email, Email, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, provide your email address. We'll keep it confidential.
* **Vendor Phone** (Vendor Phone, Text, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number.
* **Vendor Notes** (Vendor Notes, Note, Optional): If you'd like to add any additional notes or comments, this is the place to do it.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask and we'll clarify.
* Your feedback is valuable to us and we appreciate your input.
