# sports_fan_experience_at_venue_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports_fan_experience_at_venue_survey form is designed to collect feedback from sports fans on their experience at a venue after an event. This feedback will help event organizers and management to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the overall experience for fans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Date of the event you attended.
2. Select the type of event (e.g., Football, Basketball, Baseball).
3. Choose the venue where the event took place (e.g., Madison Square Garden, Yankee Stadium, Fenway Park).
4. Rate the Event Score (1-10).
5. Rate your Event Satisfaction (1-10).
6. Rate your Fan Satisfaction (1-10).
7. Choose the climate of the event (e.g., Air-conditioned, Not air-conditioned, Not applicable).
8. Select the venue's facilities (e.g., Cleanliness of the venue, Availability of food and beverages, Availability of restrooms).
9. Rate the helpfulness of the staff (1-10).
10. Select the helpfulness of the event staff (e.g., Friendly, Not friendly, Not applicable).
11. Choose the type of parking available (e.g., On-site parking, Off-site parking).
12. Rate the ticket price (1-10).
13. Select how strong your interest is in attending the event again (e.g., Strong, Somewhat, Not at all).
14. Rate how often you plan to attend the event in the future (1-10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, Select One): Choose the type of event (e.g., Football, Basketball, Baseball).
* **Venue** (`venue`, Select Multiple): Select the venue where the event took place (e.g., Madison Square Garden, Yankee Stadium, Fenway Park).
* **Event Score** (`event_score`, Number): Rate the Event Score from 1 to 10.
* **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction`, Number): Rate your Event Satisfaction from 1 to 10.
* **Fan Satisfaction** (`fan_satisfaction`, Number): Rate your Fan Satisfaction from 1 to 10.
* **Event Climate** (`event_climate`, Select Multiple): Choose the climate of the event (e.g., Air-conditioned, Not air-conditioned, Not applicable).
* **Venue Facilities** (`venue_facilities`, Select Multiple): Select the venue's facilities (e.g., Cleanliness of the venue, Availability of food and beverages, Availability of restrooms).
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, Number): Rate the helpfulness of the staff from 1 to 10.
* **Event Staff** (`event_staff`, Select Multiple): Select the helpfulness of the event staff (e.g., Friendly, Not friendly, Not applicable).
* **Parking** (`parking`, Select One): Choose the type of parking available (e.g., On-site parking, Off-site parking).
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, Number): Rate the ticket price from 1 to 10.
* **Fan Interest** (`fan_interest`, Select Multiple): Select how strong your interest is in attending the event again (e.g., Strong, Somewhat, Not at all).
* **Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`, Number): Rate how often you plan to attend the event in the future from 1 to 10.
