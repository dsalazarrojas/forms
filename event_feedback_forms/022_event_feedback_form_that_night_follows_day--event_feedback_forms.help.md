# Event Feedback Form That Night Follows Day - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on events held the night before. It will help event organizers evaluate and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your participant name and email.
3. Choose your preferred contact method and time of the event.
4. Select all the options that apply to you.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_feedback_form_title`, text, required): This is where you enter the title of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Participant Name** (`event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day_participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone** (`event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Note** (`event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Select One** (`event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day_select_one`, select_one, required): Choose all the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Select Multiple** (`event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day_select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Date** (`event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Time** (`event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event you are providing feedback on.
