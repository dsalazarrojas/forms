# Art Gallery Visitor Preference Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your visit to the art gallery. It will help us to better understand your preferences and interests, so we can tailor our events and services to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the date of your visit to the art gallery.
2.  Choose the time of your arrival.
3.  Fill in your name and contact information.
4.  Choose your age group and visit frequency.
5.  Select the purpose of your visit.
6.  Choose your art interests.
7.  Select your preferred medium of art.
8.  Choose the type of exhibitions you prefer.
9.  Select the programs you are interested in.
10. Choose how you would like to be contacted.
11. Sign up for our newsletter.
12. Receive notifications about upcoming events.
13. Express your interest in membership.
14. Indicate your interest in supporting the gallery.
15. Indicate your interest in volunteering.
16. Indicate your interest in purchasing artwork.
17. Tell us how you heard about us.
18. Choose the social media platforms you use.
19. Indicate any accessibility needs.
20. Provide any additional accessibility details.
21. Share any feedback about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In Date**: Please select the date of your visit to the art gallery.
  * Type: date
  * Required: True
* **Check In Time**: Choose the time of your arrival at the art gallery.
  * Type: time
  * Required: False
* **Visitor Name**: Fill in your name.
  * Type: text
  * Required: False
* **Visitor Email**: Provide your email address.
  * Type: email
  * Required: False
* **Visitor Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: False
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip or postal code.
  * Type: text
  * Required: False
* **Age Group**: Choose your age group.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: False
* **Visit Frequency**: Choose how often you visit art galleries.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: True
* **Visit Purpose**: Choose the purpose of your visit.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: True
* **Art Interests**: Select your art interests.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: True
* **Preferred Medium**: Choose your preferred medium of art.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: False
* **Exhibition Preference**: Choose the type of exhibitions you prefer.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: False
* **Program Interests**: Select the programs you are interested in.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: False
* **Communication Preference**: Choose how you would like to be contacted.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: True
* **Newsletter Signup**: Sign up for our newsletter.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: True
* **Event Notifications**: Receive notifications about upcoming events.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: True
* **Membership Interest**: Express your interest in membership.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: True
* **Donation Interest**: Indicate your interest in supporting the gallery.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: False
* **Volunteer Interest**: Indicate your interest in volunteering.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: False
* **Purchase Interest**: Indicate your interest in purchasing artwork.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: False
* **How Heard**: Choose how you heard about us.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: False
* **Social Media Preference**: Choose the social media platforms you use.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: False
* **Accessibility Needs**: Indicate any accessibility needs.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: True
* **Accessibility Details**: Provide any additional accessibility details.
  * Type: text
  * Required: False
* **Visit Feedback**: Share any feedback about your visit.
  * Type: text
  * Required: False

**Tips**

* Be as specific as possible when filling in the fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact us.
* Your feedback is valuable and helps us to improve our services.
