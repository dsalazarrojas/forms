# COVID 19 Intubation Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to guide healthcare professionals in completing a thorough and accurate intubation checklist for patients. It ensures that all necessary checks are performed before and after intubation to minimize risks and ensure patient safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the patient's name, medical record number, and date and time of intubation.
2. Confirm that the pre-intubation checks have been completed, including sedation level adequate, pre-intubation medications given, airway assessed for difficulty, and emergency equipment available.
3. Select the type of endotracheal tube used and ensure it is properly sized and positioned.
4. Confirm that the tube is securely taped or fastened and that the tube position is marked.
5. Verify that the ventilator settings are correct and that breath sounds are present bilaterally.
6. Confirm that capnography is available and that the tube is not disconnected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist Date** (id: 2): Date of the intubation procedure.
* **Checklist Time** (id: 3): Time of the intubation procedure.
* **Patient Name** (id: 4): The name of the patient undergoing intubation.
* **Medical Record Number** (id: 5): The patient's unique identification number in the medical record.
* **Date of Intubation** (id: 6): Date of the intubation procedure.
* **Nurse Name** (id: 7): The name of the nurse performing the intubation.
* **Pre-Intubation Checks** (id: 8): A note section for recording any pre-intubation checks performed.
* **Sedation Level Adequate** (id: 9): Confirm that the patient's sedation level is adequate for intubation.
* **Pre-Intubation Medications Given** (id: 10): Confirm that pre-intubation medications have been given.
* **Airway Assessed for Difficulty** (id: 11): Confirm that the airway has been assessed for difficulty.
* **Emergency Equipment Available** (id: 12): Confirm that emergency equipment is available in case of unexpected complications.
* **PPE Properly Donned** (id: 13): Confirm that personal protective equipment (PPE) is properly donned by the healthcare team.
* **Tube Information** (id: 14): A note section for recording tube details.
* **Endotracheal Tube Type** (id: 15): The type of endotracheal tube used (oral, nasal, or other).
* **Tube Size** (id: 16): The size of the endotracheal tube used.
* **Tube Depth at Teeth** (id: 17): The depth of the endotracheal tube at the teeth.
* **Cuff Pressure Checked** (id: 18): Confirm that the cuff pressure has been checked.
* **Tube Position Confirmed** (id: 19): Confirm that the tube position is correct and that an X-ray or clinical confirmation has been obtained.
* **Post-Intubation Checks** (id: 20): A note section for recording post-intubation checks.
* **Bilateral Breath Sounds** (id: 21): Confirm that bilateral breath sounds are present.
* **Ventilator Settings Verified** (id: 22): Confirm that ventilator settings are correct.
* **Capnography Confirmed** (id: 23): Confirm that capnography is available and not disconnected.
* **Tube Securely Taped or Fastened** (id: 24): Confirm that the tube is securely taped or fastened.
* **Tube Position Marked** (id: 25): Confirm that the tube position is marked.
