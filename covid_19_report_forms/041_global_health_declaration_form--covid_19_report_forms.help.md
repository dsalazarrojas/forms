# Global Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Health Declaration Form is designed to collect essential information related to the COVID-19 pandemic for health risk assessment and prevention purposes. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the risks and take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of the virus.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field as per your travel history and health status.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from a healthcare professional.
4. Make sure to submit the form completely and accurately before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel History** (`1`, `text`, required): Please describe your recent travel history, including destinations, dates, and duration of travel.
* **Symptoms** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether you are experiencing any symptoms related to COVID-19, such as fever, cough, or fatigue.
* **Travel Destination** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter the country or region you have traveled to or plan to travel to.
* **Temperature** (`4`, `number`, required): Report your body temperature in Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Exposure Risk** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have been exposed to anyone with COVID-19 or in a high-risk environment.
* **Vaccination Status** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you are vaccinated or not.
* **Test Results** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Report if you have been tested positive or negative for COVID-19.
* **Medical Conditions** (`8`, `text`, required): List any pre-existing medical conditions you have.
* **Contact History** (`9`, `text`, optional): Describe your contact history with others, if any.
* **Social Distance** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you practice social distancing.
* **Personal Medical Equipment** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm if you have personal medical equipment, such as a mask or respirator.
* **Travel Documents** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have any travel documents, such as a passport or ID card.
* **Test Location** (`13`, `text`, optional): Report the location where you were tested.
* **Travel Date** (`14`, `date`, required): Enter the date you traveled or plan to travel.
* **Test Method** (`15`, `select_one`, optional): Report if you were tested using a PCR or rapid test.
* **Contact Frequency** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the frequency of your contact with others.
* **Travel Frequency** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Report how often you travel.
* **Travel Duration** (`18`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of your travel.
* **Travel Distance** (`19`, `number`, required): Report the distance you traveled.
* **Personal Medical Info** (`20`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant medical information about yourself.
* **Travel Method** (`21`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you traveled by air or land.
* **Test Result Comments** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments about your test results.
* **Travel Reason** (`23`, `text`, optional): Describe the reason for your travel.
* **Travel Country** (`24`, `select_one`, required): Report the country or region you traveled to or plan to travel to.
* **Travel City** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter the city or region you traveled to or plan to travel to.

Please note that this form has been designed to be completed accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
