# covid_19_liability_acknowledgment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for COVID-19 liability acknowledgment. It's designed to capture information about an individual's understanding of potential risks and safety measures when interacting with a team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as directed.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (Participant Information, text, optional): This section captures basic participant information such as name, email, and phone number. This is for identification purposes only.
* **Risk Protocol** (Risk Protocol, text, optional): This is a free-text field where you can describe the risk protocols in place for your team. This is for your reference and information purposes.
* **Safety Measures** (Safety Measures, select multiple, optional): You can select one or more safety measures that apply to your team, such as wearing a face mask, maintaining social distancing, or staying home if sick.
* **Liability Acknowledgment** (Liability Acknowledgment, select one, optional): You must choose to either "I understand and agree" or "I do not understand and do not agree" with the liability terms.
* **Signature** (Signature, date, optional): This is where you'll sign and date your agreement.
* **Date** (Date, time, optional): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, optional): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email** (Participant Email, email, optional): Enter your email address as a participant.
* **Participant Phone** (Participant Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number as a participant.
* **Participant Address** (Participant Address, text, optional): Enter your address as a participant.
