# COVID 19 Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help facilities assess their safety and preparedness in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. It will help facilities evaluate and document their safety measures, identify areas for improvement, and ensure compliance with public health guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions carefully and accurately. If you are unsure of any information, please provide the best available answer or indicate that you are unsure. This form is divided into several sections, each covering different aspects of the facility's safety and preparedness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): This is the date on which this assessment is being conducted.
* **Facility or Business Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the facility or business being assessed.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, `text`, required): This is the street address of the facility or business.
* **City** (`facility_city`, `text`, required): This is the city where the facility or business is located.
* **State** (`facility_state`, `text`, required): This is the state or province where the facility or business is located.
* **ZIP Code** (`facility_zip`, `text`, required): This is the postal code of the facility or business.
* **Type of Facility** (`facility_type`, `select_one`, required): This is the type of facility or business, such as office building, retail store, restaurant, etc.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Title** (`assessor_title`, `text`, required): This is the job title of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Maximum Occupancy** (`total_occupancy`, `number`, required): This is the maximum number of people allowed in the facility or business at any given time.
* **Current Occupancy Limit** (`current_occupancy_limit`, `number`, required): This is the reduced capacity limit in place due to COVID-19.
* **Is health screening conducted at entrances** (`entrance_screening`, `select_one`, required): This question asks whether health screening is conducted at entrances, which is crucial in preventing the spread of the virus.
* **What is your mask policy** (`mask_policy`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the face covering requirements for employees and visitors.
* **Are masks available for those who need them** (`mask_supply_available`, `select_one`, required): This question inquires about the availability of masks for those in need.
* **Are hand sanitizer stations available** (`hand_sanitizer_stations`, `select_one`, required): This question asks whether hand sanitizer stations are available for the use of employees and visitors.
* **How often are high-touch surfaces cleaned** (`cleaning_frequency`, `select_one`, required): This question seeks information on how often high-touch surfaces are cleaned in the facility or business.
* **What type of ventilation system is in place** (`ventilation_system`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the type of ventilation system used in the facility or business.
* **Are social distancing markers in place** (`social_distancing_markers`, `select_one`, required): This question inquires about the availability of social distancing markers in high-traffic areas.
* **Are plexiglass barriers installed** (`plexiglass_barriers`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the availability of plexiglass barriers at service counters.
* **What is your employee sick leave policy** (`employee_health_policy`, `select_one`, required): This question seeks information on the employee sick leave policy in the facility or business.
* **What is your employee vaccination policy** (`employee_vaccination_policy`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the vaccination requirements for employees in the facility or business.
* **Do you have a protocol for handling positive cases** (`positive_case_protocol`, `select_one`, required): This question inquires about the protocol for handling positive cases of COVID-19 in the facility or business.
* **Will you cooperate with contact tracing efforts** (`contact_tracing_cooperation`, `select_one`, required): This question asks whether the facility or business will cooperate with contact tracing efforts in the event of a positive case.
* **Is COVID-19 safety signage posted** (`signage_posted`, `select_one`, required): This question seeks information on the availability of COVID-19 safety signage in the facility or business.
* **Are there any compliance issues or concerns** (`compliance_issues`, `text`, optional): This is an optional question where you can provide any compliance issues or concerns related to the COVID-19 safety protocols.
* **What improvements are planned** (`improvement_plan`, `text`, optional): This is an optional question where you can provide any planned improvements or action items for the facility or business.
* **Next Assessment Date** (`next_assessment_date`, `date`, optional): This is an optional question where you can provide the next assessment date for the facility or business.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when completing this form.
- Ensure that you have the most up-to-date information before submitting.
- If you are unsure of any information, please provide the best available answer or indicate that you are unsure.
- Please note that some answers may require clarification or further investigation to ensure the accuracy of the assessment.
