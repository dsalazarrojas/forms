# Workplace Readiness Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Readiness Declaration Form is a necessary tool for ensuring a safe working environment during the COVID-19 pandemic. Please review the form carefully and answer the questions honestly to help us assess your workplace readiness.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please review the form carefully before completing it.
- Answer each question to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Information
- **Employee Information**: This is the main section where you will provide your basic information to identify yourself as an employee.
  - Please type your name and other relevant details as prompted.
  - This information will help us verify your identity and ensure you are the correct employee.

### Symptoms
- **Symptoms**: Please select all the symptoms you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past 14 days.
  - 'Yes' means you are currently experiencing the symptom.
  - 'No' means you are not experiencing the symptom.
  - Select all relevant symptoms.

### Health and Safety Precautions
- **Health and Safety Precautions**: Choose the health and safety precautions you are taking or plan to take to minimize the risk of transmission.
  - Please select all the precautions you are taking or plan to take.
  - For example, Wash hands frequently, Wear a face mask, Keep distance from others, or Get tested.

### Travel History
- **Travel History**: Please describe any recent travel history, including the dates and locations of your travel.
  - Please be specific about the places you visited and when you returned.

### Health Declaration
- **Health Declaration**: Please describe your health status, including any medical conditions or illnesses you are experiencing.
  - Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.

### Return to Work
- **Return to Work**: Please confirm your planned return to work date and any relevant details.
  - Please specify the date you plan to return to work and any special arrangements you need to make.

### Signature
- **Signature**: Please sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and commitment to following workplace health and safety guidelines.
  - Please make sure to sign and date the form before submitting it.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative.
- The information collected will be used to assess your workplace readiness and ensure your safety and the safety of others.
- Please note that this form is subject to change as the pandemic situation evolves.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
