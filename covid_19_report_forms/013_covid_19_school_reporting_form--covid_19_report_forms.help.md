# COVID 19 School Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect data from schools on COVID-19 related metrics such as student and staff vaccination rates, number of positive cases, and safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the report date.
2. Enter the school name, district, address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Provide the principal's name, email, and contact information.
4. Enter the reporter's name, title, email, and contact information.
5. Fill in the total student and staff enrollment numbers.
6. Select the current learning model (in-person, hybrid, remote, or virtual academy).
7. Report the number of students and staff fully vaccinated, and their vaccination percentages.
8. Indicate if the school has hosted or plans to host a vaccine clinic.
9. Report the number of student and staff positive cases in the past week.
10. Enter the number of students and staff currently in quarantine.
11. Report the number of classroom closures in the past week.
12. Select the safety measures currently in place at the school.
13. Indicate if the school is currently in outbreak response mode.
14. Add any additional comments or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you are reporting this data.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the school.
* **School District** (`school_district`, text, required): Enter the name of the school district.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter the street address of the school.
* **City** (`school_city`, text, required): Enter the city location of the school.
* **State** (`school_state`, text, required): Enter the state where the school is located.
* **ZIP Code** (`school_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the school.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required): Enter the main office phone number of the school.
* **Principal Name** (`principal_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school principal.
* **Principal Email** (`principal_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the school principal.
* **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
* **Reporter Title** (`reporter_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the reporter.
* **Reporter Email** (`reporter_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the reporter.
* **Total Student Enrollment** (`total_student_enrollment`, number, required): Enter the current total student enrollment number.
* **Total Staff Count** (`total_staff_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of staff members.
* **Current Learning Model** (`learning_model`, select_one, required): Select the current learning model (in-person, hybrid, remote, or virtual academy).
* **Number of Students Fully Vaccinated** (`students_vaccinated_count`, number, required): Enter the number of students fully vaccinated.
* **Student Vaccination Percentage** (`students_vaccinated_percentage`, select_one, required): Select the approximate percentage of students vaccinated.
* **Number of Staff Fully Vaccinated** (`staff_vaccinated_count`, number, required): Enter the number of staff fully vaccinated.
* **Staff Vaccination Percentage** (`staff_vaccinated_percentage`, select_one, required): Select the approximate percentage of staff vaccinated.
* **Has your school hosted a vaccine clinic** (`vaccine_clinic_hosted`, select_one, required): Indicate if the school has hosted or plans to host a vaccine clinic.
* **Number of Student Positive Cases This Week** (`positive_cases_students_week`, number, required): Enter the number of new student positive cases in the past 7 days.
* **Number of Staff Positive Cases This Week** (`positive_cases_staff_week`, number, required): Enter the number of new staff positive cases in the past 7 days.
* **Number of Students Currently in Quarantine** (`quarantine_students`, number, required): Enter the number of students currently in quarantine.
* **Number of Staff Currently in Quarantine** (`quarantine_staff`, number, required): Enter the number of staff currently in quarantine.
* **Number of Classroom Closures This Week** (`classroom_closures`, number, required): Enter the number of classroom closures due to COVID-19 in the past week.
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, select_multiple, required): Select all the safety measures currently in place at the school.
* **Is your school currently in outbreak response mode** (`outbreak_response`, select_one, required): Indicate if the school is currently in outbreak response mode.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information reported is accurate and up-to-date.
* Report consistently to provide reliable data.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your local health department or school administrators.
