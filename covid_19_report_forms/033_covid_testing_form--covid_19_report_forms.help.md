# Covid Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid Testing Form is a collection of fields that gather information about patients who have undergone Covid-19 testing. This form is designed to help healthcare professionals accurately record and track Covid-19 testing data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Patient Info" page and fill in the relevant fields as described below.
2. Proceed to the "Covid Test Result" page and select one of the available options for the "Covid Test Result".
3. Choose a date for the "Test Date" field by clicking on the calendar icon.
4. Enter the "Test Location" where the test was performed.
5. Select all applicable options under "Consent" to indicate your understanding of the test and its implications.
6. Enter your "Contact Number" in the required format.
7. Enter your "Email" address.
8. Provide your "Address".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information.
* **Covid Test Result** (`test_result`, select_one, required): Select whether your Covid-19 test result is "Covid Positive", "Covid Negative", or "Pending".
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the test from the calendar icon.
* **Test Location** (`test_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the test was performed.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options to indicate your understanding of the test and its implications.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number in the required format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your address.
