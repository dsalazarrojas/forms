# covid_contact_tracing_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Contact Tracing Form is designed to collect information about a patient's contact tracing data for healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the patient (patient_name).
2. Select the symptoms the patient is experiencing (symptoms).
3. Record the date and time of the contact (contact_date).
4. Enter the patient's contact details (contact_details).
5. Enter the patient's healthcare ID number (healthcare_id).
6. Enter the patient's medical record number (medical_record_number).
7. Select whether the contact method was in-person (contact_method).
8. Enter the patient's phone number (patient_phone).
9. Enter the patient's contact address (contact_address).
10. Enter the location of the contact (location).
11. Enter the location of the patient (contact_location).
12. Select the type of exposure (exposure_type).
13. Select whether the patient has a travel history (travel_history).
14. Enter any contact notes (contact_notes).
15. Enter the patient's status (patient_status).
16. Select the test result (test_result).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_person**: Enter the name of the person who is the primary point of contact.
* **symptoms**: Select all the symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **contact_date**: Enter the date and time of the contact.
* **contact_details**: Enter any additional details about the contact, such as the location and duration.
* **patient_name**: Enter the name of the patient.
* **healthcare_id**: Enter the healthcare ID number of the patient.
* **medical_record_number**: Enter the medical record number of the patient.
* **healthcare_provider_name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **contact_method**: Select whether the contact was in-person or not.
* **patient_phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **contact_address**: Enter the address where the contact occurred.
* **location**: Enter the location where the contact occurred.
* **contact_location**: Enter the location of the patient's contact.
* **exposure_type**: Select the type of exposure the patient had.
* **travel_history**: Select whether the patient has a travel history.
* **contact_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the contact.
* **patient_status**: Select the patient's status.
* **test_result**: Select whether the patient has tested positive or negative for COVID-19.

## Tips
* Always fill out this form accurately and completely.
* This form should only be used for new or ongoing cases, not for follow-up visits or test results.
* If the patient has a healthcare ID number, please enter it to ensure accurate record keeping.
* If the patient has a medical record number, please enter it to ensure accurate record keeping.
* If the patient has a travel history, please select 'Yes' to indicate this.
* If the patient has tested positive or negative for COVID-19, please select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
