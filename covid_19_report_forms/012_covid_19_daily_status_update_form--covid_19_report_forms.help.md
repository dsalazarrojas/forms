# Covid 19 Daily Status Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Daily Status Update Form is designed to facilitate healthcare professionals in tracking and updating patient information on a daily basis. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring timely interventions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the patient's status by choosing "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Patient Status" field.
2. Provide the patient's ID number in the "Patient Id" field.
3. Enter the vaccination status, choosing from "Vaccinated", "Not Vaccinated", or "Partially Vaccinated" options.
4. Describe any symptoms the patient is currently experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
5. Enter the patient's test results, choosing from "Positive", "Negative", or "Pending" options.
6. Record any hospitalization information in the "Hospitalization Info" field.
7. Add any medical notes or observations in the "Medical Notes" field.
8. Identify the medical team member responsible for submitting the update.
9. Enter the timestamp for the update.
10. Confirm the update by selecting "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text): Enter any additional information about the patient not covered in other fields.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one): Select the patient's current vaccination status.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text): Describe any symptoms the patient is currently experiencing.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text): Enter any contact information for the patient.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, select_multiple): Select the patient's test results.
* **Hospitalization Info** (`hospitalization_info`, text): Record any hospitalization information.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note): Add any medical notes or observations.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, select_one): Identify the medical team member responsible for submitting the update.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple): Confirm the submitter's identity.
* **Patient Id** (`patient_id`, number): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, time): Enter the timestamp for the update.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select_multiple): Confirm the updater's identity.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date): Confirm the date and time of the update.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text): Add any additional comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text): Enter the patient's address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one): Select the patient's status as active or inactive.
* **Active Status** (`active_status`, select_multiple): Select the patient's status.
* **Medical Team Info** (`medical_team_info`, text): Add any medical team information.
* **Medical Team Name** (`medical_team_name`, text): Enter the medical team name.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text): Enter any additional patient information.
* **Test Result Info** (`test_result_info`, text): Enter any test result information.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, select_one): Select the patient's test result.
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date): Enter the date of the test.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date): Confirm the date and time of the update.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form to ensure accurate patient records.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you are submitting the correct information.
* Double-check the patient's ID number to avoid errors.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
