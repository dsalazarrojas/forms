# COVID 19 Test Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The COVID 19 Test Billing Form is designed to collect necessary information for billing and insurance purposes for patients who have undergone COVID-19 tests. This form helps healthcare providers accurately record and report test results and expenses to the insurance provider.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents required for the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Review and verify the information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Billing Date**: Enter the date of billing for the COVID-19 test. This is the date when the test was performed or when you expect to be paid. Use the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient First Name**: Enter the patient's legal first name as it appears on their identification. This is required to identify the patient accurately.
* **Patient Last Name**: Enter the patient's legal last name as it appears on their identification. This is required to identify the patient accurately.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format. This helps verify the patient's age.
* **Gender**: Select the patient's gender to help identify their demographic characteristics. Choose from:
  - Male
  - Female
  - Non-binary
  - Prefer not to say
  - Other
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number to contact them for further billing information or questions.
* **Email Address**: Enter the patient's email address for billing or payment-related correspondence.
* **Home Address**: Enter the patient's street address. This is where the billing information will be sent to.
* **City**: Enter the patient's city where their home address is located.
* **State**: Enter the patient's state where their home address is located.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the patient's zip code for postal purposes.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the insurance provider's name. This helps us identify which insurance company is covering the patient's expenses.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter the patient's insurance policy or member ID number. This helps us identify which policy is covering the expenses.
* **Insurance Group Number**: If you have a group policy, enter the group number to identify which policy is covering the expenses. (Optional)
* **Policy Holder Name**: If you are not the patient, enter the policy holder's name. This is the person who is covered under the policy.
* **Policy Holder Date of Birth**: If you are not the patient, enter the policy holder's date of birth. (Optional)
* **Policy Holder Relationship**: If you are not the patient, choose the relationship between you and the policy holder. Choose from:
  - Self
  - Spouse
  - Parent
  - Child
  - Other
* **Do you have secondary insurance?**: Check if you have secondary insurance coverage. If yes, you will need to provide more information below.
* **Secondary Insurance Provider**: If you have secondary insurance, enter the name of the secondary insurance provider. This helps us identify which policy is covering the expenses.
* **Secondary Policy Number**: If you have secondary insurance, enter the policy number of the secondary insurance provider. This helps us identify which policy is covering the expenses.
* **Type of COVID-19 Test**: Select the type of COVID-19 test performed. Choose from:
  - PCR molecular test
  - Rapid antigen test
  - Rapid PCR test
  - Antibody test
  - Home collection test
* **Date of Test**: Enter the date when the COVID-19 test was performed. Use the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Ordering Provider NPI Number**: Enter the National Provider Identifier (NPI) number of the healthcare provider who ordered the test.
* **CPT or Billing Code**: Enter the procedure code for the test performed. This helps us identify which test was performed.
* **ICD-10 Diagnosis Code**: Enter the ICD-10 diagnosis code related to the test performed. This helps us identify the diagnosis.
* **Reason for Test**: Select the reason why the COVID-19 test was performed. Choose from:
  - Symptomatic patient
  - Close contact exposure
  - Pre-procedure screening
  - Healthcare worker screening
  - Travel requirement
  - Work requirement
  - School requirement
  - Patient request
* **Was prior authorization obtained?**: Check if prior authorization was obtained for the test. If yes, you will need to enter the authorization number below.
* **Authorization Number**: If prior authorization was obtained, enter the authorization number. This helps us verify the authorization.
* **Patient Financial Responsibility**: Check the patient's financial responsibility for the test. Choose from:
  - No patient responsibility - fully covered
  - Copay only
  - Deductible applies
  - Coinsurance applies
  - Self-pay - no insurance
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select the preferred payment method for the patient. Choose from:
  - Credit card
  - Debit card
  - HSA or FSA card
  - Check
  - Cash
  - Payment plan
* **Is billing address same as home address?**: Check if the billing address is the same as the patient's home address. If no, enter the billing address below.
* **Billing Address**: If the billing address is different from the patient's home address, enter the billing address here.
* **Additional Billing Information**: Enter any other billing information that may be relevant to the test. This could include other expenses or services not covered by insurance.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date when the form was signed. Use the MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary information before completing the form.
* Fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review and verify the information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the patient or their insurance provider.
* This form is intended for billing purposes only and should not be used for other medical purposes.
