# Contactless Elevator Operation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contactless Elevator Operation Consent Form is a document that gathers consent from building occupants to operate elevators without human contact during emergencies or other situations where physical contact may be a health risk. This form is essential for the safe and efficient operation of contactless elevator protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and instructions.
2. Provide your full name, which will be used to identify you as the participant.
3. Enter your email address for contact and confirmation purposes.
4. If you have a phone number, provide it for urgent communications.
5. Carefully read and select your response to the consent statement.
6. Enter the date when your consent becomes active.
7. Briefly describe the purpose of data use for your personal information.
8. If necessary, provide information on the withdrawal process for your consent.
9. Enter any additional comments or remarks about your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant name** (`Participant name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you wish to be identified.
- **Organization** (`Organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.
- **Email address** (`Email address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone number** (`Phone number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, provide it for urgent communications.
- **Consent statement** (`Consent statement`, select_one, required): Select "I consent" if you agree to participate in contactless protocols or "I do not consent" if you do not.
- **Consent effective date** (`Consent effective date`, date, required): Enter the date when your consent becomes active.
- **Purpose of data use** (`Purpose of data use`, text, required): Briefly describe the reason your personal data is being collected.
- **Withdrawal process information** (`Withdrawal process information`, note, optional): If you wish to withdraw your consent, include the steps to do so here.
- **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, text, optional): Enter any other remarks or comments about your consent.

## Tips
- Ensure you understand the form's purpose and instructions before proceeding.
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
