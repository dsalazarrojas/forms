# COVID 19 Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for COVID-19 testing and vaccination purposes only. It is intended to gather information about individuals who are interested in getting tested or vaccinated against COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions accurately and honestly.
2. Ensure that all mandatory fields (marked as "Required: true") are completed.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please contact the relevant authority or healthcare professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date**: The date when you consent to get tested or vaccinated.
* **First Name**: Your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender**: Your gender. Please select from the options provided.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
* **Home Address**: Your residential address.
* **City**: Your city of residence.
* **State**: Your state of residence.
* **ZIP Code**: Your postal code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: The full name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact**: The relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Insurance Provider**: The name of your insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Your insurance policy number (if applicable).
* **Primary Care Physician Name**: The name of your primary care physician (if applicable).
* **Physician Phone**: The office phone number of your primary care physician (if applicable).
* **Are you interested in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine?**: Yes or No, indicating your interest in getting vaccinated.
* **If yes, why do you want to get vaccinated?**: Select the reason(s) for your interest in getting vaccinated.
* **Do you have a vaccine brand preference?**: Select from the options provided (if applicable).
* **Do you have any underlying medical conditions?**: Select from the options provided (if applicable).
* **Are you a healthcare worker?**: Yes or No, indicating your employment status.
* **Are you an essential worker?**: Yes or No, indicating your employment status.
* **Have you previously tested positive for COVID-19?**: Yes, No, or Not sure, indicating your infection status.
* **When did you test positive?**: If you answered Yes or No, select the approximate date of your previous infection (if applicable).
* **I consent to receive information about COVID-19 testing and vaccination**: Yes or No, indicating your information consent status.
* **I consent to be contacted about testing and vaccination opportunities**: Yes or No, indicating your contact consent status.
* **I consent to share my information with health authorities for public health purposes**: Yes or No, indicating your data sharing consent status.
* **Signature Date**: The date when you sign this form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all mandatory fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact the relevant authority or healthcare professional for guidance.
* This form is for COVID-19 testing and vaccination purposes only.
