# COVID 19 Lab Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about COVID-19 lab tests, including test details, patient information, and test results.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the **Report Date** field and choose the date the test was performed.
2. Enter the **Report Time** field to select the time the test was performed.
3. Enter the **Lab Report ID**, a unique identifier for the lab report.
4. Enter the **Laboratory Name**, where the test was performed.
5. Enter the **Pathologist or Technician Name**, the person who performed the test.
6. Select the **Patient Full Name**, the person who was tested.
7. Enter the **Medical Record Number**, the patient's unique identifier.
8. Enter the **Date of Birth**, the patient's date of birth.
9. Select the **Sample Type**, the type of sample taken (e.g., Nasopharyngeal Swab, Oropharyngeal Swab, etc.).
10. Enter the **Sample Collection Date** and **Sample Collection Time**, when the sample was collected.
11. Enter the **Sample Received Date**, when the sample was received by the laboratory.
12. Select the **Test Type**, the type of test performed (e.g., RT-PCR, Rapid Antigen, etc.).
13. Enter the **Test Method**, the method used to perform the test.
14. Select the **Test Result**, the outcome of the test (e.g., Positive, Negative, etc.).
15. If applicable, enter the **CT Value** and **Viral Load**.
16. Enter the **Result Interpretation**, an explanation of the test results.
17. Enter the **Clinical Recommendation**, any further advice or guidance for the patient's care.
18. Select the **Quality Control Passed**, whether the test met quality control standards.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date**: Enter the date the test was performed.
* **Report Time**: Enter the time the test was performed.
* **Lab Report ID**: A unique identifier for the lab report, used to avoid duplication of effort.
* **Laboratory Name**: Enter the name of the laboratory where the test was performed.
* **Pathologist or Technician Name**: Enter the name of the person who performed the test.
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the full name of the patient.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's unique identifier in the medical record system.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Sample Type**: Select the type of sample taken (e.g., Nasopharyngeal Swab, Oropharyngeal Swab, etc.).
* **Sample Collection Date**: Enter the date the sample was collected.
* **Sample Collection Time**: Enter the time the sample was collected.
* **Sample Received Date**: Enter the date the sample was received by the laboratory.
* **Test Type**: Select the type of test performed (e.g., RT-PCR, Rapid Antigen, etc.).
* **Test Method**: Enter the method used to perform the test.
* **Test Result**: Select the outcome of the test (e.g., Positive, Negative, etc.).
* **CT Value**: If applicable, enter the CT value.
* **Viral Load**: If applicable, enter the viral load value.
* **Result Interpretation**: Enter an explanation of the test results.
* **Clinical Recommendation**: Enter any further advice or guidance for the patient's care.
* **Quality Control Passed**: Select whether the test met quality control standards.
