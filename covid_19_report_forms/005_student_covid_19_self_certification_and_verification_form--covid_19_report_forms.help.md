# Student COVID 19 Self Certification And Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their COVID-19 status, including their symptoms, travel history, exposure to potential COVID-19 carriers, and testing experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Daily Screening" question with "Yes" or "No".
2. If you select "Yes", answer the following questions about your symptoms:
   - Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list.
3. Provide your name as the student.
4. Enter the date you are completing the form.
5. Answer the "Have you traveled recently?" question with "True" or "False".
6. Answer the questions about your exposure to potential COVID-19 carriers:
   - Select "True" or "False" for each question, indicating whether you have had contact with someone who has COVID-19 or works in a high-risk industry.
7. Answer the questions about your testing experiences:
   - Select "True" or "False" for each question, indicating whether you have received a positive test result or not.
