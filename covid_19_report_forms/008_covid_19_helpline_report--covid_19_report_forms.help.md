# COVID 19 Helpline Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Helpline Report is used to document and manage calls from individuals seeking assistance, guidance, or support regarding COVID-19 related concerns. This form helps helpline staff to gather essential information, provide effective support, and follow up on the caller's progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the caller's personal details, including their name, age, phone number, and email address.
2. Record the date and time of the call.
3. Specify the type of complaint or concern the caller has (e.g., health symptoms, access to testing, vaccination questions, etc.).
4. Provide a detailed description of the caller's issue.
5. If the call is related to health concerns, describe the symptoms the caller is experiencing.
6. If necessary, recommend treatment or support for the caller.
7. Indicate whether follow-up is required and schedule a follow-up date if applicable.
8. Document the current resolution status of the call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caller's Full Name** (`caller_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person calling the helpline.
* **Age** (`caller_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the caller if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (`caller_phone`, text, required): Enter the caller's phone number for follow-up or communication.
* **Email Address** (`caller_email`, email, optional): Enter the caller's email address for follow-up or communication.
* **Date of Call** (`call_date`, date, required): Record the date of the call.
* **Time of Call** (`call_time`, time, optional): Record the time of the call.
* **Type of Complaint** (`complaint_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of complaint or concern the caller has (e.g., health symptoms, access to testing, etc.).
* **Detailed Description of Complaint** (`complaint_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the caller's issue.
* **If health related, describe symptoms** (`symptom_details`, text, optional): Describe the symptoms the caller is experiencing if related to health concerns.
* **Treatment or Support Recommended** (`treatment_recommended`, select_multiple, optional): Select any recommended treatments or supports for the caller (e.g., testing, medical evaluation, vaccination guidance, etc.).
* **Details of Recommended Treatment** (`treatment_notes`, text, optional): Provide additional notes about the recommended treatment.
* **Does this caller require follow-up?** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether follow-up is required for the caller.
* **Recommended Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up date if necessary.
* **Current Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, required): Document the current resolution status of the call (e.g., resolved, in progress, pending further action, etc.).
* **Helpline Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the helpline staff member who assisted the caller.
* **Staff ID** (`staff_id`, text, optional): Enter the helpline staff member's ID for tracking purposes.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes about the call or caller.
