# ประกวม Case Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is a case report template designed to document and track incidents related to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **suspect name**: Enter the name of the individual who may be suspected of having COVID-19.
* **suspect location**: Provide the location where the contact was made with the suspect.
* **contact date**: Record the date when the contact with the suspect was made.
* **contact time**: Enter the time when the contact with the suspect was made.
* **suspect symptoms**: Describe the symptoms displayed by the suspect, if any.
* **reported by**: Identify the person or entity that reported the incident.
* **contact method**: Specify the method used to contact the suspect (e.g., phone, email, in-person).
* **contact source**: Indicate the source of the information being reported (e.g., personal observation, report from another individual, medical records).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete reporting.
* Use clear and concise language when describing symptoms and other fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your institution's COVID-19 reporting guidelines for clarification.
