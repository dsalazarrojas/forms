# Self Certification Of Vaccination Status - Help Guide
## Purpose

This self-certification form is intended for employees to provide accurate and up-to-date information about their vaccination status. The form helps in maintaining accurate records and ensures adherence to workplace safety and compliance standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each field carefully and honestly, as this information will be used for your records and for the benefit of your workplace.
2. Ensure that you have all necessary vaccination documentation and details ready before starting.
3. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your HR or relevant department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Information** (`Employer Information`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your current employer's name and contact information, if applicable.
* **Employee Details** (`Employee Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current employee ID or any other unique identifier for our records.
* **Vaccine Status** (`Vaccine Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from the options:
	+ Fully Vaccinated
	+ Partially Vaccinated
	+ Not Vaccinated
* **Vaccination Date** (`Vaccination Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your last or first vaccination.
* **Vaccine Dose Number** (`Vaccine Dose Number`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, enter the number of doses you have received.
* **Vaccine Manufacturer** (`Vaccine Manufacturer`, `text`, required: false): If known, enter the vaccine manufacturer's name.
* **Vaccination Site** (`Vaccination Site`, `text`, required: false): If known, enter the site where you received the vaccination.
* **Additional Tips:**
	+ Ensure the accuracy of your responses, as this form is used for official records and compliance purposes.
	+ Review each field carefully to avoid errors.
	+ Submit the form only after ensuring that all fields are complete and accurate.
	+ If you need help or have any concerns, contact your HR or relevant department for assistance.
