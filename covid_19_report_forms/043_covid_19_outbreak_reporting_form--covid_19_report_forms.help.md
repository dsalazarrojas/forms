# COVID 19 Outbreak Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about COVID-19 outbreaks in a facility setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Facility Information**:
	* Enter the name of the facility experiencing the outbreak
	* Select the type of facility (e.g. Hospital, School, etc.)
	* Enter the facility's address
	* Enter the contact name, phone number, and email of the person reporting the outbreak
2. **Outbreak Information**:
	* Enter the date the outbreak started
	* Enter the number of confirmed cases
	* Enter the number of suspected cases (if known)
	* Enter the number of deaths related to COVID-19
	* Enter the number of hospitalizations
	* Enter the number of ICU admissions
	* Enter the number of staff members affected
	* Enter the number of residents or clients affected
3. **Response Measures**:
	* Select all measures taken to contain the outbreak (e.g. Isolation of Positive Cases, Quarantine of Contacts, etc.)
	* Enter the type of external support requested

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (`Facility Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the facility experiencing the outbreak
* **Facility Type** (`Facility Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of facility (e.g. Hospital, School, etc.)
* **Facility Address** (`Facility Address`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's address
* **Reporting Contact Name** (`Reporting Contact Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person reporting the outbreak
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the person reporting the outbreak
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the person reporting the outbreak
* **Report Date** (`Report Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form is being submitted
* **Outbreak Start Date** (`Outbreak Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the outbreak started
* **Number of Confirmed Cases** (`Number of Confirmed Cases`, `number`, required): Enter the number of confirmed cases
* **Number of Suspected Cases** (`Number of Suspected Cases`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of suspected cases (if known)
* **Number of Deaths Related to COVID-19** (`Number of Deaths Related to COVID-19`, `number`, required): Enter the number of deaths related to COVID-19
* **Number of Hospitalizations** (`Number of Hospitalizations`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hospitalizations
* **Number of ICU Admissions** (`Number of ICU Admissions`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of ICU admissions
* **Number on Mechanical Ventilation** (`Number on Mechanical Ventilation`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of mechanical ventilation
* **Total Population in Facility** (`Total Population in Facility`, `number`, required): Enter the total population in the facility
* **Attack Rate percentage** (`Attack Rate percentage`, `number`, required): Enter the attack rate percentage
* **Number of Staff Members Affected** (`Number of Staff Members Affected`, `number`, required): Enter the number of staff members affected
* **Number of Residents or Clients Affected** (`Number of Residents or Clients Affected`, `number`, required): Enter the number of residents or clients affected
* **Measures taken to contain outbreak** (`Measures taken to contain outbreak`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all measures taken to contain the outbreak (e.g. Isolation of Positive Cases, Quarantine of Contacts, etc.)
* **External support requested** (`External support requested`, `select_one`, required): Select whether external support has been requested
* **Type of support received** (`Type of support received`, `text`, optional): Enter the type of support received
