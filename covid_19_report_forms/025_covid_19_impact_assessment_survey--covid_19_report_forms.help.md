# COVID 19 Impact Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on the economic, operational, and workforce impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on your organization. The data collected will help you understand the effects of the pandemic and make informed decisions to mitigate and recover from these effects.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on each page tab to navigate through the sections of the form.
2.  Answer each question carefully, as your responses will help us understand the impact of the pandemic on your organization.
3.  If you need to add more information to any question, use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Your Role or Position**: Choose the title of your role within the organization (e.g., CEO, Manager, Employee, etc.).
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Business, Non-Profit, Educational Institution, Healthcare, Government, or Other).
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date when you are completing this survey.
* **Revenue Impact**: Choose the level of revenue impact since the pandemic (Significant Decrease, Moderate Decrease, Slight Decrease, No Change, or Increase).
* **Percentage Change**: If you selected a decrease, estimate the percentage change in revenue (e.g., 10%, 20%, etc.).
* **Profitability**: Choose the impact on profitability (e.g., Severely Reduced, Moderately Reduced, Slightly Reduced, No Impact, Improved).
* **Unexpected Cost Increases**: Select all areas where there have been unexpected cost increases (e.g., PPE and Supplies, Remote Work Technology, Cleaning and Sanitation, etc.).
* **Operational Disruption Level**: Choose the level of operational disruption (e.g., Complete Closure, Severe Disruption, Moderate Disruption, Minimal Disruption, No Disruption).
* **Supply Chain Affected**: Select if your supply chain has been affected (Yes, Severely, Yes, Moderately, Slightly, False).
* **Staffing Impact**: Choose the level of staffing impact (Significant Staff Loss, Some Staff Loss, No Staff Changes, Staff Increase).
* **Employee Health and Safety Concerns**: Choose the level of employee health and safety concerns (Significant Concerns, Some Concerns, Minimal Concerns, No Concerns).
* **Success with Remote Work Arrangements**: Choose how successful you were with remote work arrangements (Very Successful, Moderately Successful, Somewhat Successful, Not Successful).
* **Customer Loss or Decreased Demand**: Choose the level of customer loss or decreased demand (Severe Loss, Moderate Loss, Slight Loss, No Loss, Increased Demand).
* **Market Changes**: Enter any changes in market conditions (e.g., market shift, new trends, etc.).
* **Recovery Outlook**: Choose the expected recovery timeline (Within 3 Months, 3-6 Months, 6-12 Months, Over 1 Year, Uncertain).
* **Types of Support Needed**: Select all types of support you need (Financial Assistance, Technical Support, Healthcare Resources, Employee Support, Marketing Assistance, Other).
