# Covid 19 Tracing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees who have been potentially exposed to COVID-19. This information will be used to assess and manage potential risks associated with COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name, email, and contact phone number in the required fields.
2. Describe any symptoms you are experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
3. Report your current health status by selecting one of the options in the "Health Status" field.
4. Provide any relevant medical history, including any previous illnesses or medical conditions, in the "Medical History" field.
5. Describe any recent travel history in the "Travel History" field.
6. If you have attended any medical appointments or events, please describe them in the "Medical Attendance" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your official employee name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official employee email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Describe any COVID-19 related symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as fever, cough, or sore throat.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, required): Report your current body temperature in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required): Describe any recent travel history, including destinations and dates.
* **Exposure** (`exposure`, text, required): Describe how you think you may have been exposed to COVID-19.
* **Symptoms Duration** (`symptoms_duration`, number, required): Report the duration of your symptoms, in days or hours.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Report any previous illnesses or medical conditions that may be relevant to your current health status.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Healthy: You are healthy and not experiencing any symptoms.
	+ Sick: You are experiencing symptoms and are currently ill.
	+ Recovering: You are recovering from COVID-19.
* **Medical Attendance** (`medical_attendance`, note, required): Describe any recent medical appointments or events you have attended.
* **Medical Attendance Date** (`medical_attendance_date`, date, required): Report the date of your most recent medical appointment.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for assistance.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your HR department or supervisor.
