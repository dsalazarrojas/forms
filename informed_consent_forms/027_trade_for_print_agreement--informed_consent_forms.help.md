# Trade For Print Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information and agreements from both the photographer and the model for a trade for print agreement, which is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a photo shoot or print project.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Confirm your agreement status for the project, including your photographer and model agreements.
3. Provide printing information, including details about the print process and any relevant terms.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Information** (`photographer_contact_info`, `text`, required): This field is where the photographer will provide their contact information, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Model Information** (`model_info`, `text`, required): This field is where the model will provide their contact information, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Model Agreement** (`model_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where the model will confirm their agreement status for the project.
* **Photographer Agreement** (`photographer_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where the photographer will confirm their agreement status for the project.
* **Printing Information** (`printing_info`, `text`, required): This field is where the photographer will provide printing details, including paper type, color, and size.
* **Printing Terms** (`printing_terms`, `text`, required): This field is where the photographer will confirm the printing terms, including color mode and ink usage.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): This field is where the photographer will provide the date of the project.
* **Photographer's Name** (`photographer_name`, `text`, required): This field is where the photographer will provide their name.
* **Model's Name** (`model_name`, `text`, required): This field is where the model will provide their name.
* **Model's Address** (`model_address`, `text`, required): This field is where the model will provide their address.
* **Photographer's Address** (`photographer_address`, `text`, required): This field is where the photographer will provide their address.
* **Model's Email** (`model_email`, `email`, required): This field is where the model will provide their email.
* **Photographer's Email** (`photographer_email`, `email`, required): This field is where the photographer will provide their email.
* **Model's Phone** (`model_phone`, `text`, required): This field is where the model will provide their phone number.
* **Photographer's Phone** (`photographer_phone`, `text`, required): This field is where the photographer will provide their phone number.
* **Print Details** (`print_details`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where the photographer will confirm printing details, including paper type, color, and size.
* **Signature Confirmation** (`signature_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where both parties will confirm their signature status.
* **Confirmation of Agreement** (`photographer_agreement_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where both parties will confirm their agreement status.
* **Confirmation of Printing Terms** (`printing_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where both parties will confirm their printing terms.
* **Model Confirmation** (`model_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where the model will confirm their agreement status.
* **Photographer Confirmation** (`photographer_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where the photographer will confirm their agreement status.
* **Terms of Use** (`terms_of_use`, `text`, required): This field is where both parties will confirm their understanding of the terms of use.
