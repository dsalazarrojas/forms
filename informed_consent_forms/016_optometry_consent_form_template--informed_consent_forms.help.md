# Optometry Consent Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Optometry Consent Form Template is designed to collect informed consent from patients before undergoing optometry services. This form ensures that patients understand the risks and benefits associated with the services they are seeking, and that they have provided their informed consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient.
2.  **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3.  **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
4.  **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
5.  **Medical History**: Select all medical conditions or allergies that apply to the patient.
6.  **Consent Status**: Select whether the patient has provided their consent for the optometry services.
7.  **Optometry Services**: Select all services that the patient is willing to receive.
8.  **Allergies**: Enter any allergies or sensitivities that the patient has.
9.  **Medical Conditions**: Select all medical conditions that the patient has.
10. **Medications**: Select all medications that the patient is currently taking.
11. **Medical Procedures**: Select all medical procedures that the patient is willing to undergo.
12. **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
13. **Consent Date**: Enter the date when the patient provided their consent.
14. **Practice Name**: Enter the name of the practice.
15. **Practice Address**: Enter the address of the practice.
16. **Practice Phone**: Enter the phone number of the practice.
17. **Practice Website**: Enter the website of the practice.
18. **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the insurance provider.
19. **Insurance Number**: Enter the patient's insurance number.
20. **Insurance Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the insurance provider.
21. **Insurance Number Phone**: Enter the phone number of the insurance provider associated with the patient's insurance number.
22.  **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
23.  **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person.
24.  **Emergency Contact Alternate Phone**: Enter the phone number of the alternate emergency contact person.
25.  **Emergency Contact Alternate Name**: Enter the name of the alternate emergency contact person.

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): The name of the patient.

*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select\_multiple, required): List of medical conditions or allergies that apply to the patient.
*   **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, select\_one, required): Whether the patient has provided their consent for the optometry services.
*   **Optometry Services** (`optometry_services`, select\_multiple, required): List of services that the patient is willing to receive.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List of allergies or sensitivities that the patient has.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select\_multiple, required): List of medical conditions that the patient has.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, select\_multiple, required): List of medications that the patient is currently taking.
*   **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, select\_multiple, required): List of medical procedures that the patient is willing to undergo.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): The name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): The date when the patient provided their consent.
*   **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, required): The name of the practice.
*   **Practice Address** (`practice_address`, text, required): The address of the practice.
*   **Practice Phone** (`practice_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the practice.
*   **Practice Website** (`practice_website`, text, required): The website of the practice.
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): The name of the insurance provider.
*   **Insurance Number** (`insurance_number`, text, required): The patient's insurance number.
*   **Insurance Provider Phone** (`insurance_provider_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the insurance provider.
*   **Insurance Number Phone** (`insurance_number_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the insurance provider associated with the patient's insurance number.

*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): The relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Alternate Phone** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the alternate emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Alternate Name** (`emergency_contact_alternate_name`, text, required): The name of the alternate emergency contact person.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Be accurate when filling in the patient's date of birth.
*   Select all relevant medical conditions and allergies.
*   Double-check the patient's consent status.
*   Ensure that the patient has provided their consent for the optometry services.
*   Fill in all relevant emergency contact information.
*   Enter the correct phone number and website of the practice.
*   Verify the insurance provider details.
*   Double-check the patient’s insurance number and phone number of the insurance provider.
*   Fill in all relevant details for the alternate emergency contact person.
