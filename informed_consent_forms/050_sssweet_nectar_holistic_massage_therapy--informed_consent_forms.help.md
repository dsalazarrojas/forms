# Sssweet.nectar Holistic Massage Therapy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for informed consent and essential information for clients to provide their permission to receive holistic massage therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and date of birth.
2. Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
3. Enter your email address for contact information.
4. Answer whether you have any medical conditions or allergies.
5. Choose the treatment plan you wish to receive.
6. Indicate whether you have any medications.
7. Fill in your emergency contact information (name, relationship, phone number, and address).
8. Provide your medical history.
9. Add any medical notes.
10. Answer the emergency contact relationship questions (relationship, notes).
11. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Informed Consent** (Informed Consent): This field is for you to provide your informed consent to receive holistic massage therapy services.
* **Name** (Name, text): Fill in your complete name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date): Enter your date of birth to ensure we have your birthdate on record.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text): Enter your phone number for easy contact and communication.
* **Email** (Email, email): Provide your email address for contact information and updates.
* **Massage Therapist** (Massage Therapist, select multiple): Select whether you will be receiving massage therapy from a licensed therapist (Yes/No).
* **Consent** (Consent, select one): Indicate whether you consent to receive holistic massage therapy services (Yes/No).
* **Treatment Plan** (Treatment Plan, select multiple): Select the treatment plans you wish to receive (Yes/No).
* **Allergies** (Allergies, select multiple): Check whether you have any allergies.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select multiple): Check whether you have any medical conditions.
* **Medications** (Medications, select multiple): Check whether you are currently taking any medications.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Emergency Contact Info, note): This field is for you to provide emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, note): Fill in the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, note): Answer the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Emergency Contact Address, text): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (Emergency Contact Notes, note): Add any additional notes about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (Emergency Contact Phone 2, text): Enter another phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text): Provide your medical history for our reference.
* **Medical Notes** (Medical Notes, text): Add any medical notes.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (Emergency Contact Relationship 2, note): Answer the relationship between you and your second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Notes** (Emergency Contact Relationship Notes, note): Add any notes about your emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2 Notes** (Emergency Contact Relationship 2 Notes, note): Add any notes about your second emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 3** (Emergency Contact Relationship 3, note): Answer the relationship between you and your third emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 4** (Emergency Contact Relationship 4, note): Answer the relationship between you and your fourth emergency contact.

## Tips
- Please fill in all the fields that apply to you.
- Ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
