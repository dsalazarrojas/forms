# Property Damage Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a property damage incident, including the description of the damage, the party liable for the damage, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the property damage in the **What is the description of the property damage?** field.
2. Determine if the party responsible for the damage is **Yes** or **No**.
3. If the party is responsible, enter their name in the **What is the name of the party responsible?** field.
4. Enter the release date of the damage in the **What is the release date?** field.
5. Specify the type of property damaged in the **What type of property is damaged?** field.
6. Describe the type of damage that occurred in the **What type of damage has occurred?** field.
7. Enter the release amount, if applicable, in the **What is the release amount?** field.
8. Enter the contact information for the party responsible for the damage in the **Who can we contact for more information?** field.
9. Enter the contact information for this organization in the **What is our contact information?** field.
10. Enter the email address for this organization in the **What is our email address?** field.
11. Enter the address of this organization in the **What is our address?** field.
12. Enter the address of the party responsible for the damage in the **What is the party's address?** field.
13. Enter the release date 2, if applicable, in the **Release Date 2** field.
14. The party responsible for the damage should sign below in the **Party Signature** field.
15. This organization should sign below in the **Our Signature** field.
16. Enter the email address of this organization in the **Our Email** field.
17. Enter the address of this organization in the **Our Address** field.
18. Enter the email address of the party responsible for the damage in the **Party Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the description of the property damage?**: Enter a brief description of the property damage.
* **Who is the party liable for the damage?**: Check **Yes** if the party is responsible, otherwise check **No**.
* **What is the release date?**: Enter the date the damage was released.
* **What is the name of the party responsible?**: Enter the name of the party responsible for the damage.
* **What type of property is damaged?**: Enter the type of property that was damaged.
* **What type of damage has occurred?**: Enter the type of damage that occurred.
* **What is the release amount?**: Enter the amount of the release, if applicable.
* **Who can we contact for more information?**: Enter the contact information for the party responsible for the damage.
* **What is our contact information?**: Enter the contact information for this organization.
* **What is our email address?**: Enter the email address of this organization.
* **What is our address?**: Enter the address of this organization.
* **What is the party's address?**: Enter the address of the party responsible for the damage.
* **Release Date 2**: Enter the second release date, if applicable.
* **Party Signature**: The party responsible for the damage should sign here.
* **Our Signature**: This organization should sign here.
* **Our Email**: Enter the email address of this organization.
* **Our Address**: Enter the address of this organization.
* **Party Email**: Enter the email address of the party responsible for the damage.
* **Party Address**: Enter the address of the party responsible for the damage.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" can be skipped if not applicable. However, providing accurate and complete information in those fields will help ensure a smooth and efficient process.
