# COVID 19 Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from individuals participating in activities or programs offered by the organization, specifically addressing the risks and liabilities associated with COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each field and its corresponding label carefully.
2. Answer each field truthfully and accurately.
3. Ensure you are providing your informed consent to participate in the activity or program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id: 2): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (id: 3): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 5): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Organization Name (if applicable)** (id: 6): If you are participating in an activity or program related to the organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **I understand the risks of COVID-19** (id: 8): Select 'True' to indicate you understand the risks associated with COVID-19.
* **I understand how COVID-19 is transmitted** (id: 9): Select 'True' to indicate you understand how COVID-19 is transmitted.
* **I understand the precautions being taken** (id: 10): Select 'True' to indicate you understand the precautions being taken to mitigate COVID-19 risks.
* **I do not have COVID-19 symptoms** (id: 12): Select 'True' to indicate you do not have any symptoms associated with COVID-19.
* **I have not tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days** (id: 13): Select 'True' to indicate you have not tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **I have not been in close contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19** (id: 14): Select 'True' to indicate you have not been in close contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **I accept and assume the risks of COVID-19** (id: 16): Select 'I accept' to indicate you understand and accept the risks associated with COVID-19.
* **I release this organization from any liability related to COVID-19** (id: 17): Select 'I agree' to indicate you release the organization from any liability related to COVID-19.
* **I waive any right to make claims against this organization** (id: 18): Select 'I agree' to indicate you waive any right to make claims against this organization.
* **Date of Agreement** (id: 19): Enter the date of your agreement in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **I have read and fully understand this waiver** (id: 20): Select 'True' to indicate you have read and understood this waiver.

## Tips

* Please answer each field accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the organization for clarification.
* The information collected through this form will be used to determine your level of understanding and agreement with the risks associated with COVID-19.
