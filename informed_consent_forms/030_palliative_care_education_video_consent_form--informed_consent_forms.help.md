# Palliative Care Education Video Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain participant consent and collect demographic information for the palliative care education video program.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Participant Information**: Enter your name, email, phone number, and participant relationship to provide context about your involvement in the program.
2.  **Demographic Information**: Select your age, gender, and condition from the provided options.
3.  **Consent Information**: Indicate whether you have been informed about the program and if you have given your consent to participate.
4.  **Video Information**: Provide the title and description of the video you will be watching, and the location and duration of the video.
5.  **Upload Video**: Choose whether you will upload a video file or indicate that you do not have a video file to upload.
6.  **Consent**: Select the option to confirm that you have given your consent to participate in the program.
7.  **Completion**: Enter the date and time you completed the video.
