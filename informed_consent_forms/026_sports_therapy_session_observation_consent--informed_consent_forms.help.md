# Sports Therapy Session Observation Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather informed consent from participants in a sports therapy session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the participant's email in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Enter the purpose of the observation in the "Purpose of Observation" field.
4. Select the date and time of the observation in the "Date of Observation" and "Time of Observation" fields.
5. Enter the location of the observation in the "Observation Location" field.
6. If the participant has any notes, enter them in the "Participant Notes" field.
7. Select any of the options in the "Participant Agreement" field that apply to the participant's understanding and agreement.
8. Sign the form by entering your name in the "Agreement Signed By" field.
9. Enter the date and time you signed the form in the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed" fields.
10. Finally, sign your name in the "Participant Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (Email, Required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Purpose of Observation** (Text, Required): Enter the purpose of the observation.
* **Participant Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Observation Purpose** (Text, Required): Enter the purpose of the observation.
* **Signature Participant** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Signature Therapist** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as the therapist.
* **Date of Observation** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Time of Observation** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the observation.
* **Observation Location** (Text, Required): Enter the location of the observation.
* **Participant Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes you have about the participant.
* **Observation Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes about the observation.
* **Participant Agreement** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any of the options that apply to your understanding and agreement.
* **Agreement Signed By** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Date Signed** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Participant Signature** (Text, Optional): Enter your signature as the participant.
* **Therapist Signature** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as the therapist.
* **Participant Signature Image** (Text, Optional): Enter a link to a signature image.
* **Participant Agreement Options:**
  - **I understand that I will be observed**
  - **I will not share the observation data with anyone**
  - **I will not use the observation data for any purpose other than for my own use**
