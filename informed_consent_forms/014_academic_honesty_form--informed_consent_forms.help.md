# Academic Honesty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to declare academic honesty in relation to any incident that occurred during the academic year. The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and transparent process for students to acknowledge and address any misconduct or issues that may have happened.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name and student id in the respective fields. These are your full legal name and institutional identifier.
2. Read the statement of understanding carefully and select the correct option that you agree to comply with the academic honesty policy.
3. If you have any incidents to report, describe them in the incident description field. This is an optional field, so only fill it if you have a related incident.
4. If any corrective actions have been agreed upon, provide a brief outline in the corrective action field. Again, this is an optional field.
5. Enter the reviewer or instructor's name who will be overseeing the case.
6. Finally, select a date for the declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Academic Honesty Declaration --** (`honesty_header`, note, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Your full legal name.
* **Student id** (`student_id`, text, required): Your institutional identifier.
* **Statement of understanding** (`statement_of_understanding`, select one, required): Acknowledge the academic honesty policy and state that you will comply with it.
	+ Options: I understand and will comply, Other.
* **Incident description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Describe any related incident if applicable.
* **Corrective actions agreed** (`corrective_action`, text, optional): Outline any agreed-upon corrective measures if applicable.
* **Reviewer or instructor name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Person who will be overseeing the case.
* **Date of declaration** (`date`, date, required): Date when the form is submitted.
* **Electronic signature** (`signature`, text, required): Type your full name to sign the form.

## Tips
* Please fill in the required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask your instructor or reviewer for guidance.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the form for your records.
