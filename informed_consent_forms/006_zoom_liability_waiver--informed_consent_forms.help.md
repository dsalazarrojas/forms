# ZOOM Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from attendees before they engage in any activities or events related to ZOOM.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information as prompted.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what you are agreeing to.
3. Sign the form in the presence of an authorized representative of the organization hosting the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name (`attendee_name`, text, required):** Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Attendee Relationship (`attendee_relationship`, text, optional):** If you are attending with a group or organization, please describe your relationship with that group or organization.
* **Emergency Contact Name (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional):** If you have an emergency contact that we should be aware of, please enter their name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional):** If your emergency contact is associated with your group or organization, please describe their relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional):** Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Date (`date`, date, optional):** If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the event, please specify the date your last medical check-up was.
* **Time (`time`, time, optional):** Please enter the time of your last medical check-up.
* **Attendee Medical Conditions (`attendee_medical_conditions`, text, optional):** If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the event, please describe them.
* **Emergency Contact Medical Info (`emergency_contact_medical_info`, text, optional):** If your emergency contact has any medical conditions that may affect their ability to assist you during the event, please describe them.
* **Attendee Signing Date (`attendee_signing_date`, date, optional):** Please sign this form on the date you complete it.
* **Attendee Signature (`attendee_signature`, text, optional):** Please sign your name as a physical signature. This is a required field for the form to be complete.
* **Emergency Contact Signature (`emergency_contact_signature`, text, optional):** Please sign your name as a physical signature if you are an emergency contact.

## Tips
Please review the form carefully before completing it, and make sure you understand what you are agreeing to. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the event organizers.
