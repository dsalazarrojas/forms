# Dental Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Clearance Form is designed to obtain clearance or approval from patients for procedures or treatments in a dental clinic setting. The form helps to ensure that patients understand and agree to the necessary steps or interventions before undergoing treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the patient.
2. Enter the date of the procedure or treatment.
3. Describe the procedure or treatment you are seeking clearance for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (text)**: Enter your name as the patient.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the procedure or treatment.
* **Procedure (text)**: Describe the procedure or treatment you are seeking clearance for. Be as specific as possible to ensure the doctor or dentist understands your needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to read the form carefully and answer all required fields ( Patient Name and Procedure).
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your doctor or dentist for clarification.
* This form is meant to be used in conjunction with other forms or checklists to ensure your safety and well-being during treatment.
