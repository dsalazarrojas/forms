# Freight Dispatch Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Dispatch Agreement form is designed to collect information from users regarding the dispatching service they have opted for or declined.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To begin, review the form to understand what each field represents and its relevance to your choice regarding the dispatching service.
2.  Select all the options that apply to you by checking the checkboxes next to each corresponding field.
3.  Make sure to fill all the fields that have hints or specific instructions provided.
4.  Review the form before submitting it to ensure you've filled all the required fields accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Information** (`agreement_form`, select_multiple, optional): This section asks whether you have opted for or declined the dispatching service. Select 'Yes' if you have opted for it, and 'No' otherwise.
*   **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): This field is for your identification and should represent your full name as it appears on your official records.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are representing your company, this field should reflect your company's full name as it is known in the official records.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field should contain a valid email address for contact purposes. Please make sure it is accurate.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your complete address for dispatching service records.
*   **Dispatching Service** (`dispatching_service`, text, optional): Briefly describe the dispatching service you have opted for or declined.
*   **Terms Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to confirm that you accept all the terms and conditions related to the service. This section is important for understanding the conditions under which the dispatching service is provided.
*   **User Signature** (`user_signature`, note, optional): This field is for your acknowledgement and confirmation of the information filled in this form.
*   **Carrier Signature** (`carrier_signature`, note, optional): This field is intended for the carrier or dispatching service provider to acknowledge and confirm the information provided. 

## Tips
*   This form can be filled by anyone involved in the dispatching process.
*   Please be accurate when filling out all the fields.
*   Review and confirm the accuracy of the information provided in this form before submitting.
