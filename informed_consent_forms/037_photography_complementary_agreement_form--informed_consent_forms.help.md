# Photography Complementary Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a client's informed consent for a photography service. It collects the client's name, service details, photographer's information, signature, date, time, and terms and conditions of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Describe the service you would like to be performed in the "Service Details" field.
3. Enter your information as the photographer in the "Photographer Information" field.
4. Sign and date the form by entering your signature and the current date in the respective fields.
5. Enter the time of the service in the "Time" field.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, optional): This is where you enter your name as the client.
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, optional): Describe the service you would like to be performed, such as "photographing a wedding" or "taking portraits."
* **Photographer Information** (Photographer Information, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information as the photographer.
* **Client Signature** (Client Signature, note, optional): Sign your name to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the service.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the service.
* **Photographer Signature** (Photographer Signature, text, optional): Sign your name as the photographer to confirm you have agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, note, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the service.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
- Review and agree to the terms and conditions carefully before signing the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the photographer with the provided contact information.
