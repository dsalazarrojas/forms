# yoni_therapys_vaginal_steam_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and consent for the treatment planning process. It's a crucial document that helps ensure the patient's medical history and treatment plans are accurately recorded and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure you provide complete and up-to-date medical history.
3. Review each field to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous treatments or conditions.
* **Treatment Description** (`treatment_description`, text, required): Describe the treatment you are seeking or have been recommended.
* **Treatment Outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, select_one, required): Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you've had this treatment before or are planning to have it.
* **Other** (`treatment_outcome_other`, text, optional): If you selected "No" for the previous field, enter a brief description of why.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Sign your name to indicate that you've read and understand the form.
* **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the treating healthcare provider.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Patient Date Of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your birthdate, if applicable.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter your age, if applicable.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or concerns you have.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Describe the treatment plan recommended for you.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to indicate your consent.
* **Consent Other** (`consent_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for the previous field, enter a brief description.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent.
* **Other Medical Condition** (`other_medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe any other medical conditions or concerns you have.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (`other_medical_conditions_other`, text, optional): If you have "Other Medical Conditions", enter a brief description.
* **Other Medical Conditions Notes** (`other_medical_conditions_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your other medical conditions.
* **Medical History - Medical Condition Treatment Plan** (`medical_history_medical_condition_treatment_plan`, text, optional): Describe how your medical history relates to your treatment plans.
