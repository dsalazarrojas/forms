# Parent Acknowledgement Form For Covid 19 School Procedures - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Acknowledgement Form is designed to gather parental understanding and consent for the school's COVID-19 procedures, ensuring parents are aware of and agree to follow school rules and guidelines during this time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully review each field and enter the required information:

1. **Acknowledgement**: Enter your acknowledgement of the school's COVID-19 procedures.
2. **Terms and Conditions**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your understanding of the terms and conditions.
3. **Acknowledgement of Understanding**: Enter your acknowledgement of understanding of the COVID-19 procedures.
4. **e-Signature**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your electronic signature.
5. **Terms and Conditions Text**: Enter the date you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
6. **Parent Acknowledgement**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your parent's acknowledgement.
7. **Student Acknowledgement**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your student's acknowledgement.
8. **School Acknowledgement**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm the school's acknowledgement.
9. **Acknowledgement Date**: Enter the date you have read and understood the COVID-19 procedures.
10. **Parent Name**: Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
11. **Parent Email**: Enter your email address.
12. **Parent Phone**: Enter your phone number.
13. **Student Name**: Enter your child's name.
14. **Student Email**: Enter your child's email address.
15. **Student Phone**: Enter your child's phone number.
16. **Student Grade**: Enter your child's grade level.
17. **School Grade**: Enter the school grade level.
18. **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name.
19. **Class Name**: Enter the class name.
20. **Subject**: Enter the subject taught.
21. **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
22. **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number.
23. **School Name**: Enter the school name.
24. **Acknowledgement Signature**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your acknowledgement signature.
25. **Acknowledgement Date**: Enter the date you have read and understood the COVID-19 procedures.
