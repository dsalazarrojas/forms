# COVID 19 Intake Form For Massage Therapy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Intake Form is a necessary step before starting a massage therapy session. It helps us understand your medical history, health status, and preferences for treatment. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your full name, date of birth, phone number, and email address.
2. Answer questions about your COVID-19 health status, including past diagnosis, symptoms, and vaccination status.
3. Provide information about your medical history, including chronic conditions, current medications, and allergies.
4. Indicate your preferences for massage therapy, including frequency, intensity, and areas of focus.
5. Finally, confirm your consent for massage therapy and acknowledge our COVID-19 safety protocols.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Date of Birth (2)** (`client_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age and potential health risks.
* **Phone Number (3)** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email Address (4)** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
* **First Time Client (5)** (`first_time_client`, select_one, required): Indicate if this is your first time visiting us.
* **COVID-19 Health Screening (6)** (`section_header_1`, note, required): This section is for your COVID-19 health screening. Please answer the questions carefully.
* **COVID-19 Diagnosis in Past 14 Days (7)** (`covid_diagnosis`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had a COVID-19 diagnosis in the past 14 days.
* **Time Since Recovery (8)** (`covid_recovery_time`, text, required): If you have had COVID-19, indicate when you have recovered.
* **Current COVID-19 Symptoms (9)** (`covid_symptoms`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Describe Symptoms (10)** (`symptom_description`, text, required): If you are experiencing symptoms, describe them briefly.
* **Recent COVID-19 Exposure (11)** (`covid_exposure`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had recent COVID-19 exposure.
* **Vaccination Status (12)** (`section_header_2`, note, required): This section is for your vaccination status. Please answer carefully.
* **Vaccination Status (13)** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Indicate your vaccination status.
* **Medical History (14)** (`section_header_3`, note, required): This section is for your medical history. Please answer carefully.
* **Chronic Health Conditions (15)** (`chronic_conditions`, text, required): List any chronic health conditions you have.
* **Current Medications (16)** (`current_medications`, text, required): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Allergies (17)** (`allergies`, text, required): List any known allergies you have.
* **Massage Frequency (18)** (`massage_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you would like to receive massage therapy sessions.
* **Problem Areas or Conditions (19)** (`problem_areas`, text, required): Describe areas or conditions you would like to focus on during massage therapy.
* **Preferred Massage Intensity (20)** (`massage_intensity`, select_one, required): Indicate your preferred massage intensity.
* **Consent and Safety (21)** (`section_header_5`, note, required): This section is for your consent and understanding of our COVID-19 safety protocols.
* **I Declare I Am Healthy and Safe for Massage Therapy (22)** (`health_declaration`, select_one, required): Confirm you are healthy and safe for massage therapy.
* **I Understand and Accept COVID-19 Safety Protocols (23)** (`safety_protocols_understood`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand and accept our COVID-19 safety protocols.
* **I Consent to Massage Therapy Treatment (24)** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent for massage therapy treatment.
