# data_analytics_research_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants in research studies to ensure they are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the research project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the terms and conditions of the research project.
2. Answer the questions accurately and truthfully.
3. If you are participating in research, select 'Yes' for the corresponding question.
4. If you are not participating in research, select 'No' for the corresponding question.
5. Enter any requested information, such as your name, email, and contact information.
6. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you participating in research?** (`research_participation`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer this question with either 'Yes' or 'No', indicating your participation status.
* **What is research?** (`research_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the research project you are participating in.
* **Name?** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yy.
* **time of birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required: false): Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM.
* **researcher name** (`researcher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the researcher.
* **researcher email** (`researcher_email`, email, required: false): Enter the researcher's email address.
* **researcher institution** (`researcher_institution`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the researcher's institution.
* **Have you read and signed a consent form?** (`participant_informed`, select_one, required: false): Answer this question with either 'Yes' or 'No', indicating whether you have read and signed a consent form.
* **Consent?** (`participant_consent`, select_one, required: false): Answer this question with either 'Yes' or 'No', indicating your consent status.

## Tips

* Answer the questions carefully and truthfully.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the researcher.
