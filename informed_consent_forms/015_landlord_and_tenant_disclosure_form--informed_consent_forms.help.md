# Landlord And Tenant Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document key information about a landlord-tenant relationship, including the rental agreement terms, lease start and end dates, rent and deposit details, and other relevant terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **General Information** section with the landlord's and tenant's names, addresses, and contact information.
2. In the **Landlord Info** section, provide the landlord's name and contact information.
3. In the **Tenant Info** section, provide the tenant's name and contact information.
4. In the **Rental Agreement** section, specify the rental agreement terms, including the lease start and end dates, rent, and deposit.
5. In the **Tenant Termination** section, specify the conditions under which the lease can be terminated.
6. In the **Landlord Termination** section, specify the conditions under which the landlord can terminate the lease.
7. In the **Landlord Rental Agreement** section, describe the landlord's rental agreement terms.
8. In the **Tenant Rental Agreement** section, describe the tenant's rental agreement terms.
9. In the **Additional Terms** section, specify any additional terms or conditions of the lease.
10. In the **Lease End Date** section, select the date when the lease will end.
11. In the **Lease Start Date** section, select the date when the lease will start.
12. In the **Lease Term** section, specify the duration of the lease.
13. In the **Rent** section, specify the monthly rent amount.
14. In the **Deposit** section, specify the deposit amount.
15. In the **Utilities** section, specify any utilities included in the rent.
16. In the **Rent Due Date** section, select the date when rent is due.
17. In the **Lease End Date** section, select the date when the lease will end.
18. In the **Landlord Phone** section, specify the landlord's phone number.
