# Boy Scouts Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boy Scouts Permission Form is a mandatory document that requires completion by parents or guardians for their child's participation in scouting activities. This form ensures the scout's safety and well-being during events and trips.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and understand the requirements before filling it out.
- Gather all necessary information, including contact details, medical history, and emergency contact information.
- Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, with the scout's parent or guardian's signature.
- Submit the form to the troop leader or organizer before the scheduled activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Scout First Name (scout_first_name)
- This field is required and should be filled with the scout's first name.

### Scout Last Name (scout_last_name)
- This field is required and should be filled with the scout's last name.

### Troop Number (troop_number)
- This field is required and should be filled with the troop number assigned to the scout.

### Parent or Guardian First Name (parent_guardian_first)
- This field is required and should be filled with the parent or guardian's first name.

### Parent or Guardian Last Name (parent_guardian_last)
- This field is required and should be filled with the parent or guardian's last name.

### Home Address (home_address)
- This field is required and should be filled with the full home address of the parent or guardian.

### Home Phone (home_phone)
- This field is required and should be filled with the primary contact number for the parent or guardian.

### Mobile Phone (mobile_phone)
- This field is required and should be filled with the cell phone number of the parent or guardian.

### Email Address (email)
- This field is required and should be filled with the email address of the parent or guardian for easy communication.

### Activity Name (activity_name)
- This field is required and should be filled with the name of the activity or event.

### Activity Description (activity_description)
- This field is required and should be filled with a brief description of the activity or event.

### Activity Start Date (activity_date_start)
- This field is required and should be filled with the departure date and time of the activity.

### Activity End Date (activity_date_end)
- This field is required and should be filled with the return date and time of the activity.

### Activity Location (activity_location)
- This field is required and should be filled with the location where the activity will take place.

### Transportation Method (transportation_method)
- This field is required and should be chosen from the options: "Troop vehicles", "Parent drivers", "Commercial bus", "Airplane", "Personal vehicle", or "Other".

### Activity Cost (activity_cost)
- This field is required and should be filled with the cost per scout for the activity.

### Payment Included (payment_included)
- This field is required and should be chosen from the options: "Yes, paid in full", "Partial payment made", or "No payment yet".

### Medical Insurance Provider (medical_insurance_provider)
- This field is required and should be filled with the name of the insurance company.

### Insurance Policy Number (insurance_policy_number)
- This field is required and should be filled with the policy number.

### Physician Name (physician_name)
- This field is required and should be filled with the primary care doctor's name.

### Physician Phone (physician_phone)
- This field is required and should be filled with the doctor's contact number.

### Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)
- This field is required and should be filled with any medical conditions the scout has.

### Current Medications (current_medications)
- This field is required and should be filled with all medications the scout is currently taking.

### Allergies (allergies)
- This field is required and should be filled with any allergies the scout has.

### Dietary Restrictions (dietary_restrictions)
- This field is optional and should be filled with any food restrictions the scout has.

### Swimming Ability (swimming_ability)
- This field is required and should be chosen from the options: "Non-swimmer", "Beginner", "Intermediate", or "Advanced".

### Medical Treatment Authorization (medical_treatment_authorization)
- This field is required and should be chosen from the options: "Yes, I authorize" or "False".

### Liability Waiver (liability_waiver)
- This field is required and should be chosen from the options: "Yes, I waive" or "False".

### Pickup Authorization (pickup_authorization)
- This field is optional and should be filled with the name of who can pick up the scout.

### Code of Conduct (code_of_conduct)
- This field is required and should be chosen from the options: "Yes, agreed" or "False".

### Parent or Guardian Signature (parent_guardian_signature)
- This field is required and should be filled with the parent or guardian's full legal name as a signature.

### Signature Date (signature_date)
- This field is required and should be filled with the date of signing.

### Scout Signature (scout_signature)
- This field is required and should be filled with the scout's signature.

### Scoutmaster Name (scoutmaster_name)
- This field is required and should be filled with the troop scoutmaster's name.

### Alternate Emergency Contact (emergency_contact_alternate)
- This field is optional and should be filled with the name and contact number of the alternate emergency contact.
