# Skin Cooling Gel Patch Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Cooling Gel Patch Consent Form is a document that gathers information about your skin condition, medical history, and other relevant details. This form is designed to ensure that you understand the treatment and its potential risks and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all required fields.
2. For the "Skin Condition" field, select the condition that best describes your current skin health.
3. For the "Medical Conditions" and "Medications" fields, select all relevant medical conditions and medications you are currently taking.
4. Choose the application method for the skin cooling gel patch from the provided options.
5. If applicable, sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, text, optional): This section is for providing your name, email, and phone number. Please fill in your contact information, but it is not required.
* **Skin Condition** (Skin Condition, select_one, required): Select the skin condition that best describes your current skin health. Options include Normal Skin, Acne, Psoriasis, Dermatitis, and Other (please specify).
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select_multiple, required): Select all medical conditions that you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past. Options include Allergies, High Blood Pressure, Diabetes, and Other (please specify).
* **Medications** (Medications, select_multiple, required): Select all medications you are currently taking. Options include Aspirin, Ibuprofen, and Other (please specify).
* **Application Method** (Application Method, select_multiple, required): Choose the method of application for the skin cooling gel patch. Options include Patches, Cream, and Spray.
* **Signature** (Signature, note, optional): If you wish to sign the form, please print your name and date below.
* **Date Signed** (Date Signed, date, optional): If you sign the form, please enter the date you signed it.
* **Time Signed** (Time Signed, time, optional): If you sign the form, please enter the time you signed it.
* **User Email** (User Email, email, optional): Please enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **User Phone** (User Phone, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Provider Signature** (Provider Signature, text, optional): If you are a healthcare provider, please sign your name below.
* **Provider Name** (Provider Name, text, optional): If you are a healthcare provider, please enter your name.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled before proceeding with treatment.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
