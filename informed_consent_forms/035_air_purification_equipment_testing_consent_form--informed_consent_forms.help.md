# Air Purification Equipment Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Purification Equipment Testing Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants before testing of air purification equipment. This form is intended for use in a laboratory or testing environment where participants may be exposed to potentially hazardous substances or procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are a participant and have the necessary clearance and permissions to provide informed consent.
2. Enter your full name in the "Participant name" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the testing procedures in the "Test description" field.
5. Estimate the expected duration of the testing in the "Expected duration of testing" field.
6. Summarize the benefits and risks of the testing in the "Benefits and risks" field.
7. Indicate your agreement to the testing terms by selecting "True" for "Consent for testing".
8. Provide details on how your data will be stored and used in the "Data use and privacy" field.
9. Date and sign your consent in the "Date of consent" field.
10. If you have any questions or concerns, please enter them in the "Questions or concerns" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name**: A field for entering your full name for consent records.
* **Contact email**: A field for entering your email address for test results and communications.
* **Test description**: A field for a brief description of the testing procedures.
* **Expected duration of testing**: A field for estimating the time needed for the testing.
* **Benefits and risks**: A field for summarizing the benefits and potential risks of the testing.
* **Consent for testing**: A field to indicate your agreement to the testing terms. Please select "True" if you are in agreement.
* **Data use and privacy**: A field to explain how your data will be stored and used.
* **Date of consent**: A field to record the date you provided your consent.
* **Questions or concerns**: A field to enter any questions or concerns you may have regarding the testing.
