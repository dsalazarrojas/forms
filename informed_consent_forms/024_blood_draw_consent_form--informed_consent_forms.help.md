# Blood Draw Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture informed consent from patients for blood draws. It is a critical component of the patient's medical record and is intended to provide a legal record of the patient's informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review each section carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability. Make sure to include your date and time of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): If you have a medical record number, please enter it here. If not, please leave this field blank.
- **Contact Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter your contact phone number.
- **I understand the purpose and procedure of blood draw** (`procedure_explanation`, select_one, required): Please select 'I agree' if you understand the purpose and procedure of blood draw.
- **I acknowledge potential risks of blood draw** (`risks_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Please select 'I agree' if you acknowledge the potential risks of blood draw, such as minor bruising and infection.
- **I understand the benefits of blood testing** (`benefits_understood`, select_one, required): Please select 'I agree' if you understand the benefits of blood testing.
- **Alternative procedures have been discussed** (`alternatives_discussed`, select_one, optional): If you have discussed alternative procedures with your physician, please select 'Yes'. If not, please select 'Not applicable'.
- **I confirm the phlebotomist is qualified** (`phlebotomist_qualified`, select_one, required): Please select 'I agree' if you confirm that the phlebotomist is qualified.
- **I consent to multiple needle sticks if needed** (`vein_access_consent`, select_one, required): Please select 'I agree' if you consent to multiple needle sticks if needed.
- **Infection control procedures will be followed** (`infection_control_confirmed`, select_one, required): Please select 'I agree' if you confirm that infection control procedures will be followed.
- **I consent to proper sample handling and testing** (`sample_handling_consent`, select_one, required): Please select 'I agree' if you consent to proper sample handling and testing.
- **I wish to discuss results with physician** (`results_discussion`, select_one, optional): If you wish to discuss the results with your physician, please select 'Yes'.
- **Have you had the opportunity to ask questions?** (`questions_asked`, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes' if you have had the opportunity to ask questions.
- **I voluntarily consent to this blood draw** (`final_consent`, select_one, required): Please select 'I agree' if you voluntarily consent to this blood draw.
- **Date and Time of Consent** (`consent_date_time`, date, required): Please enter the date and time you complete this form.

Note: Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly to maintain patient safety and medical record integrity.
