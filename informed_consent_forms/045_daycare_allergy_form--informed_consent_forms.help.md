# Daycare Allergy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about a child's allergies and medical needs from their parents or guardians. This form helps daycare staff ensure the child's safety and well-being while in their care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the child's name in the "Child Name" field.
2. Enter the child's age.
3. Select all applicable allergy types that the child has from the "Allergy Type" field.
4. If the child takes any medication, enter the name and dosage of the medication in the "Medication" and "Dosage" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the child's full name as it appears on their birth certificate or identification document.
* **Child Age** (`child_age`, number, required): Enter the child's age in years.
* **Allergy Type** (`allergy_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all allergy types that the child has, such as peanuts, eggs, or dairy products. Use the dropdown menu to select from the available options.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): If the child takes any medication, enter the name of the medication here.
* **Dosage** (`dosage`, number, optional): If the child takes any medication, enter the dosage or frequency of the medication here.
* **Reaction Description** (`reaction_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe any known reactions or symptoms the child experiences when exposed to an allergen.
