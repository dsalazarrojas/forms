# Return To In Person School Information And Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture and update student's in-person school information and commitment. It is meant to be completed by parents or guardians to provide additional school details and confirm their child's return to school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the student's return to in person school date, located at **return_to_school** (field 13).
2. Fill in the student's name and parent's name in their respective fields **student_name** (field 4) and **parent_name** (field 2).
3. Enter the student's date of birth in **student_date_of_birth** (field 5).
4. Select the student's grade level in **student_grade** (field 6).
5. Enter the parent's contact information in **parent_phone** (field 3).
6. Confirm your agreement to send your child to school in **parent_agreement** (field 23) and **student_agreement** (field 25).
7. Provide additional school information in **school_information** (field 8) and **school_commitment** (field 17).
8. Enter the date of school commitment in **school_commitment_date** (field 24).
9. Enter the parent's email address in **parent_email** (field 19).
10. Enter the student's signature in **student_signature** (field 15).
11. Enter the parent's signature in **parent_signature** (field 14).
12. Enter the date of return to school in **return_date** (field 16).
13. Enter the school's location in **school_location** (field 10) if it is not already known.
14. Provide additional school information in **school_information_additional** (field 11) and **student_message** (field 22).
15. Enter a message for the school in **school_message** (field 21).
16. Enter a message for the student in **student_message** (field 22).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **return_to_in_person_school_information** (Field 1): 
  - This is the title of the form, which should not be filled in.
- **return_to_in_person_school_information** (Field 2): 
  - Enter the parent's name.
- **parent_phone** (Field 3): 
  - Enter the parent's contact information (phone number or other contact details).
- **student_name** (Field 4): 
  - Enter the student's name.
- **student_date_of_birth** (Field 5): 
  - Enter the student's date of birth.
- **student_grade** (Field 6): 
  - Select the student's grade level.
- **parent_contact** (Field 7): 
  - Select 'Yes' to confirm you will be the primary contact for your child.
- **school_commitment** (Field 8): 
  - Enter any additional school information.
- **school_information** (Field 9): 
  - Enter any additional school information not already provided.
- **school_location** (Field 10): 
  - Enter the school's location, if not already known.
- **school_information_additional** (Field 11): 
  - Provide any additional school information.
- **student_attendance** (Field 12): 
  - Leave this field empty, it is not used in the form.
- **return_to_school** (Field 13): 
  - Enter the date your child will return to school.
- **parent_signature** (Field 14): 
  - Sign as the parent or guardian of the child.
- **student_signature** (Field 15): 
  - Sign as the student.
- **return_date** (Field 16): 
  - Enter the date for return to school.
- **school_information_commitment** (Field 17): 
  - Enter any commitment or agreement related to school.
- **school_information_commitment_date** (Field 18): 
  - Enter the date of the commitment.
- **parent_email** (Field 19): 
  - Enter the parent's email address.
- **parent_message** (Field 20): 
  - Leave this field empty, it is not used in the form.
- **school_message** (Field 21): 
  - Leave this field empty, it is not used in the form.
- **student_message** (Field 22): 
  - Leave this field empty, it is not used in the form.
- **parent_agreement** (Field 23): 
  - Select 'Yes' to confirm agreement to send your child to school.
- **school_commitment_date** (Field 24): 
  - Enter the date of the school commitment.
- **student_agreement** (Field 25): 
  - Select 'Yes' to confirm the student's agreement to attend school.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled in all the required fields before submitting the form.
- Review all the information for accuracy before submission.
- Ensure that you have signed all required fields with your signature.
