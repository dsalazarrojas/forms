# Vaccine Refusal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document a patient's refusal of a vaccine. It is used to record the reasons behind the refusal, the patient's medical history, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or allergies.
3. **Reason for Refusal**: Provide a clear and concise reason for the vaccine refusal.
4. **Vaccine Type**: Select the type of vaccine the patient is refusing.
5. **Medical Condition**: Describe any relevant medical conditions that may impact the patient's ability to receive the vaccine.
6. **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
7. **Patient Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that the patient has read and understands the information provided.
8. **Healthcare Provider**: Sign the form to confirm that they have discussed the vaccine refusal with the patient and have documented the patient's medical history.
9. **Date**: Record the date the form was completed.
10. **Time**: Record the time the form was completed.
11. **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that they have reviewed and agreed to the patient's vaccine refusal.
12. **Patient Contact**: Enter any contact information for the patient.
