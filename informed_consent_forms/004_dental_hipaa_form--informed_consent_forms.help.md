# Dental HIPAA Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental HIPAA Form is used to collect informed consent from patients before dental procedures. It gathers information about the patient's medical history, emergency contacts, and other relevant details to ensure their understanding and agreement to the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for your patient's information on page 1: Patient Information.
2. Provide any relevant medical history details in the text field on page 2: Medical History.
3. Select all applicable medical conditions that apply to your patient on page 3: Medical Condition.
4. Enter the emergency contact person's number in the text field on page 6: Emergency Contact.
5. Enter the emergency contact person's name on page 7: Emergency Contact Name.
6. Select the relationship of the emergency contact person on page 8: Relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Information**: Select the type of information you are providing about the patient. This could be their name, phone number, or address.
* **Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.

### Page 2: Medical History
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history details for the patient.

### Page 3: Treatment Plans
* **Treatment Plans**: Select all applicable treatment plans for the patient. This could include Anesthesia, Biopsy, Cast, Cleaning, Consultation, Diagnostic Imaging, Extraction, Fluoride, General Anesthesia, Oral Surgery, or Restorative.

### Page 4: Medical History Details
* **Medical History Details**: Provide any other relevant medical history details for the patient.
