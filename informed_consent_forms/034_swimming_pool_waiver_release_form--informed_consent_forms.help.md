# swimming_pool_waiver_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the waiver and release of liability for guests visiting a swimming pool. It is a legal document that acknowledges the guest's understanding of the pool's rules and safety guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Read and understand this document.
2.  Check "I have read and understand this document" to confirm that you have read and understood the document.
3.  Provide your name as the guest.
4.  Indicate your relationship to the guest (e.g. Self, Parent, Caregiver, etc.).
5.  Enter the date you completed the form.
6.  Choose the correct time of day you completed the form.
7.  Check if you have read and signed this document (answer Yes, No, or Decline).
8.  If you have answered "Yes" to step 6, provide a signature.
9.  Choose the correct form ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I have read and understand this document**:
	+ This is a check box that you must check to confirm that you have read and understood the document.
* **Guests Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the guest who is completing this form.
* **Relationship to Guest**:
	+ Choose the relationship you have with the guest (e.g. Self, Parent, Caregiver, etc.).
* **Date**:
	+ Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time**:
	+ Choose the time of day you completed this form.
* **Have you read and signed this document**:
	+ Check "Yes" if you have read and signed this document. If you have not, choose "No" or "Decline".
* **Guest Signature**:
	+ If you have answered "Yes" to the above question, provide a signature from the guest.
* **Form 41265**:
	+ If applicable, choose the correct form ID (e.g. 41265).
* **Form 65155**:
	+ If applicable, choose the correct form ID (e.g. 65155).
