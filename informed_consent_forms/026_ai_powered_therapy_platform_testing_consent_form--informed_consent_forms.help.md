# AI Powered Therapy Platform Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect informed consent from participants before they use the AI-powered therapy platform. It ensures that participants understand the testing process, their rights, and what data will be collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what is being asked.
2. Enter your participant name.
3. Provide your contact email address for study communications.
4. Select one of the provided options for the consent statement.
5. Choose the data collected during testing that applies to you.
6. Provide emergency contact information if you wish to do so (optional).
7. Sign the form with your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: This is your name as a participant. Please enter your first and last name.
* **Contact email**: This is the email address we will use for study communications.
* **Study description**: This is a brief summary of the platform testing process. Please do not enter any information here.
* **Consent to participate in platform testing**: Please select one of the provided options to indicate your consent.
	+ I consent to participate
	+ I do not consent to participate
* **Data collected during testing**: Please select all that apply to the types of data that will be collected during testing.
	+ Session transcripts
	+ Interaction logs
	+ Usage analytics
	+ Survey responses
* **Withdrawal rights**: Please do not enter any information here.
* **Permission to use anonymized data for research**: Please select one of the provided options to indicate your permission.
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Emergency contact information**: This is optional but provides emergency contact information.
* **Electronic signature name**: This is your name for the purpose of signing the form.
* **Date of consent**: This is the date on which you signed the form.

## Tips
Please ensure that you understand the form before submitting it. If you have any doubts or concerns, contact us for assistance.
