# COVID 19 Volunteer Consent Form For Summer Camp - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from volunteers to ensure a safe and healthy environment for campers and staff. It will help the camp organizers assess the risk of COVID-19 transmission and take necessary measures to mitigate it.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please ensure you have a clear understanding of the form before starting.
2.  Fill out the required information for each field carefully and accurately.
3.  Review each section carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (camp_volunteer_name, text, required): Provide your complete legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (camp_volunteer_dob, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Email Address** (camp_volunteer_email, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (camp_volunteer_phone, phone, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Current Address** (camp_volunteer_address, text, required): Provide your current residential address.
* **Summer Camp Name** (camp_camp_name, text, required): Enter the name of the summer camp where you will be volunteering.
* **Camp Dates** (camp_camp_dates, text, required): Enter the dates the camp will run.
* **Volunteer Role** (camp_volunteer_role, text, required): Describe your role at the camp (e.g., counselor, chef, etc.).
* **Will You Have Direct Contact with Children?** (camp_children_interaction, select_one, required): Select "Yes, direct contact," "No direct contact," or "Minimal contact."
* **I Understand the COVID-19 Transmission Risks** (camp_understand_risks, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I understand," "I have questions," or "False."
* **I Understand COVID-19 Safety Precautions** (camp_understand_precautions, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I understand," "I have questions," or "False."
* **I Have No COVID-19 Symptoms Currently** (camp_no_current_symptoms, select_one, required): Select "No symptoms," "Have symptoms," or "Unsure."
* **I Have Not Been Exposed to COVID-19 Recently** (camp_no_covid_exposure, select_one, required): Select "No recent exposure," "Yes, exposed," or "Unsure."
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (camp_vaccination_status_camp, select_one, required): Select "Fully vaccinated and boosted," "Fully vaccinated, not boosted," "Partially vaccinated," "Not vaccinated," or "Prefer not to disclose."
* **Any Health Conditions Affecting Safety** (camp_health_conditions_camp, text, optional): Describe any health conditions that may impact your ability to safely participate in the camp.
* **Commitment to Safety Measures** (camp_follow_safety_protocols, select_one, required): Select "I commit," "I cannot commit."
* **I Will Report Any Symptoms Immediately** (camp_report_symptoms, select_one, required): Select "I agree," "I do not agree."
* **I Will Quarantine if Exposed or Positive** (camp_quarantine_if_exposed, select_one, required): Select "I agree," "I do not agree."
* **I Have Read and Understand This Consent Form** (camp_consent_acknowledgment, select_one, required): Select "I have read and understand," "I do not fully understand."
* **Date of Consent** (camp_consent_date, date, required): Enter the date you are signing this form.

## Tips
* Please be honest when answering each question as it will help the camp organizers ensure a safe environment for all participants.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, do not hesitate to reach out to the camp organizers.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
