# Skin Care Pre Treatment Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Care Pre Treatment Consultation Form is a questionnaire designed to gather information from patients before undergoing skin care treatments. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's medical history, allergies, and other relevant information to provide personalized care and ensure a safe and successful treatment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. If you have any allergies, medical conditions, or current medications, provide details in the corresponding fields.
3. Be sure to include your medical history, doctor's name, and medical provider's name if applicable.
4. Double-check that all fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name, as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name, as it appears on your identification documents.

### Age
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, optional): Enter your age in years.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.

### Address
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your home address or current location.

### Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions you have or are experiencing.

### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies you have.

### Current Medications
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief summary of your medical history, including previous illnesses, surgeries, or treatments.

### Medical Insurance
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, optional): If you have medical insurance, provide the details.

### Doctor's Name
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a primary care physician, enter their name.

### Medical Provider
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, optional): If you have a medical provider, enter their name.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and truthful information is essential for a successful and safe treatment experience.
