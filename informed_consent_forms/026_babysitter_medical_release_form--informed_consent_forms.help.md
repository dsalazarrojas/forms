## Informed Consent Forms
Informed consent forms are an essential part of ensuring that parents and guardians are aware of and agree to the care and treatment of their children during their absence. They provide a clear and concise summary of the emergency care procedures that will be followed and the responsibilities of the caregiver. This form ensures that parents and guardians understand their child's medical history, allergies, and other essential information.

Before filling out this form, make sure to:

* You are the legal guardian or parent of the child.
* You have all the necessary information to complete the form accurately.
* You have reviewed the form carefully and understand its contents.

If you have filled out this form before, please ensure that you are not duplicating information or submitting outdated information.

## Babysitter Medical Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to inform and obtain consent from parents or guardians for the care of their child while they are away. It collects necessary information about the child's medical history, emergency contacts, and any special care instructions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the child's full name, the parent or guardian's full name, and the emergency contact's name and phone number.
2.  List any allergies or medical reactions the child may have, including medications and foods.
3.  List the child's current medications, including dosage and administration times.
4.  If applicable, provide the insurance provider and policy number.
5.  Read and sign the consent statement, authorizing the caregiver for emergency care.
6.  Sign your name in the "Name of person signing" field, and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Child name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the child, including any middle name or surname.
*   **Parent or guardian name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
*   **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the full name and phone number of an emergency contact.
*   **Allergies and reactions** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or medical reactions the child may have, including medications and foods that cause an allergic reaction.
*   **Current medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any current medications the child is taking, including dosage and administration times.
*   **Insurance provider and policy number** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the insurance provider and policy number for the child.
*   **Consent statement** (`consent_statement`, note, required): Read and sign this statement, authorizing the caregiver for emergency care.
*   **Name of person signing** (`signature_name`, text, required): Sign your name in the field below.
*   **Date signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date the form when signing.

## Tips

*   Be sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   Review the form carefully before signing.
*   Keep the form on hand for future reference.
*   Return the form to the caregiver or emergency contact for them to review before use.
