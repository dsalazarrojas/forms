# video consultation informed consent form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and record the details of a video consultation, including the patient's consent, consultation details, and other relevant information. It is intended to be completed by the healthcare provider and patient during the video consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's email address in the "Patient Email" field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field.
5. Indicate the patient's consent to the video consultation by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Video Consent" field.
6. Provide additional comments or notes in the "Video Consent Note" field.
7. Specify the video technology used for the consultation (e.g., Zoom, Skype, Google Meet).
8. Enter the date of the video consultation in the "Video Consultation Date" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
9. Enter the time of the video consultation in HH:MM:SS 24HR format.
10. Specify the duration of the video consultation.
11. Indicate the status of the video consultation (e.g., Scheduled, Ongoing, Complete).
12. Provide additional comments or notes in the "Video Consultation Note" field.
13. Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
14. Enter the department of the consultation.
15. Enter the location of the consultation.
16. Specify the type of consultation (e.g., Video Consultation, Follow-up, New Patient Visit).
17. Enter the name of the referring provider (if applicable).
18. Enter the reason for the referral (if applicable).
19. Provide additional comments or notes in the "Video Consultation Note 2" field.
20. Specify the language of the consultation.
21. Enter the link to the video recording (if applicable).
22. Enter the link to the audio recording (if applicable).
23. Enter the transcription of the video recording (if applicable).
24. Enter any additional comments or notes about the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the email address of the patient.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the phone number of the patient.
* **Video Consent**: Indicate the patient's consent to the video consultation by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Video Consent Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the patient's consent.
* **Video Technology Used**: Specify the video technology used for the consultation (e.g., Zoom, Skype, Google Meet).
* **Video Consultation Date**: Enter the date of the video consultation in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Video Consultation Time**: Enter the time of the video consultation in HH:MM:SS 24HR format.
* **Video Consultation Duration**: Specify the duration of the video consultation.
* **Video Consultation Status**: Indicate the status of the video consultation (e.g., Scheduled, Ongoing, Complete).
* **Video Consultation Note**: Provide additional comments or notes about the consultation.
* **Video Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Video Department**: Enter the department of the consultation.
* **Video Location**: Enter the location of the consultation.
* **Video Consultation Type**: Specify the type of consultation (e.g., Video Consultation, Follow-up, New Patient Visit).
* **Video Referring Provider**: Enter the name of the referring provider (if applicable).
* **Video Referring Reason**: Enter the reason for the referral (if applicable).
* **Video Consultation Note 2**: Provide additional comments or notes about the consultation.
* **Video Language**: Specify the language of the consultation.
* **Video Video**: Enter the link to the video recording (if applicable).
* **Video Audio**: Enter the link to the audio recording (if applicable).
* **Video Transcription**: Enter the transcription of the video recording (if applicable).
* **Video Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the consultation.
