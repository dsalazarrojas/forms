# Testosterone Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Testosterone Treatment Consent Form is used to record a patient's consent for testosterone treatment. This form is used to ensure that the patient understands and agrees to receive treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details as prompted in the **patient-information** page.
2. Provide information about your treatment as requested in the **treatment-information** page.
3. Confirm your consent in the **consent** page.
4. Enter the date and time of your consent in the **consent-date** and **consent-time** pages.
5. Indicate the duration of your consent in the **consent-duration** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient-information (Text, Required, Optional)**: This page is used to record your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact information.
* **treatment-information (Text, Required, Optional)**: In this page, please provide any relevant information about your treatment, including the name of the medication, dosage, and any other relevant details.
* **consent (Text, Required, Optional)**: This is the page where you will confirm that you understand and agree to receive treatment. Please indicate your willingness to receive treatment by writing your signature or initials.
* **consent-date (Date, Required, Optional)**: Enter the date on which you gave your consent.
* **consent-time (Time, Required, Optional)**: Enter the time at which you gave your consent.
* **consent-duration (Number, Required, Optional)**: Indicate how long you expect the consent to be valid for. Please note that this may vary depending on your treatment plan and provider instructions.
