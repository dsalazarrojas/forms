# Digital Classroom Feature Testing Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Classroom Feature Testing Consent form is a user-facing document designed to collect information from users who are willing to participate in testing and feature evaluation of the digital classroom environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the purpose and instructions to ensure you understand the intent of the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Enter your information accurately and truthfully.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a brief description of yourself, if you wish.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are able to participate in testing.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available to participate in testing.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information.
* **Name (1)** (`name_1`, text, optional): Enter any additional name.
* **Name (2)** (`name_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional name.

## Tips

- Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
- This form is not a mandatory requirement, but it is necessary for testing and feature evaluation purposes.
- The information collected will be used for testing and feature evaluation purposes only.
