# Acupuncture Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to inform patients about the risks, benefits, and alternatives associated with acupuncture treatment. It is essential to review this form carefully before signing it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Read through each page carefully and make sure you understand what is required and what you are agreeing to.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Sign and date the last page to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **-- Nature of Treatment --** (`treatment_description`, note, required): This section explains the nature of the treatment you will receive, including the insertion of sterile, disposable needles through the skin at specific points, as well as other possible techniques like cupping, moxibustion, or Tui Na massage.
* **-- Risks and Side Effects --** (`risk_disclosure`, note, required): This section lists potential risks and side effects of the treatment, including minor bruising, bleeding, temporary soreness, or lightheadedness, as well as very rare risks like infection or pneumothorax.
* **I have been informed of and understand the potential risks and side effects** (`risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Please select 'I Understand and Acknowledge' to confirm you understand the risks and side effects.
* **Do you have a bleeding disorder or take blood thinners?** (`blood_clotting_disorders`, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes (Please specify below)' if you have any bleeding disorders or take blood thinners, or 'False' if you do not.
* **Details of Bleeding Disorders / Medications** (`blood_thinners_detail`, text, required): If you selected 'Yes (Please specify below)' for the previous question, please provide further details about your bleeding disorders or medications.
* **Do you have a pacemaker or electronic medical implant?** (`pacemaker_check`, select_one, required): This is essential to know for electrical stimulation treatments. Please select 'True' if you have a pacemaker or electronic medical implant, or 'False' if you do not.
* **Is there any possibility that you are pregnant?** (`pregnancy_check`, select_one, required): Please select 'True' if you are or may be pregnant, or 'False' if you are not.
* **-- Consent Agreement --** (`consent_agreement_header`, note, required): This is the agreement section of the form where you confirm you understand your rights and responsibilities as a patient.
* **I understand that I have the right to refuse or discontinue treatment at any time** (`right_to_withdraw`, select_one, required): Please select 'I Understand' to confirm you understand your right to refuse or discontinue treatment.
* **I understand that acupuncture is not a substitute for conventional medical diagnosis** (`medical_consultation`, select_one, required): Please select 'I Understand' to confirm you understand the limitations of acupuncture treatment.
* **I hereby authorize the performance of acupuncture and related procedures** (`final_consent_signature`, select_one, required): Please select 'I Give My Consent' to confirm you agree to the treatment.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_consent`, date, required): Please sign and date the last page to confirm your agreement.
