# Online Learning Reflection Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Learning Reflection Story Consent Form is a tool designed to collect consent and a reflection story from students, parents, and other stakeholders involved in online learning experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form sections as instructed, making sure to provide your personal details and consents as required.
2. Ensure that you have read and understood the purpose and instructions before proceeding.
3. If you need assistance or clarification on any field, please contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Information (Page 1)
* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, required/optional): Please provide your personal details such as name, email, and contact information if you wish to share your story.

### Parent Consent (Page 2)
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, text, required/optional): This section is for your parent or guardian to give their consent for you to share your online learning story. Please have your parent or guardian fill out this section carefully.

### School Consent (Page 3)
* **School Consent** (`school_consent`, text, required/optional): This section is for your school or institution to give their consent for you to share your online learning story. Please have your school representative fill out this section accurately.

### Student Consent (Page 4)
* **Student Consent** (`student_consent`, text, required/optional): This section is for you to give your consent to share your online learning story. Please fill this section carefully and honestly.

### Other Consent (Page 5)
* **Other Consent** (`other_consent`, text, required/optional): This section is for anyone else to give their consent for you to share your online learning story. Please provide their name and contact information if possible.

### Story (Page 6)
* **Story** (`story`, note, required): Please share your online learning reflection story in the space provided below. This is a required field to ensure that you have a chance to share your thoughts and experiences related to online learning.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out your personal details and consents accurately and honestly.
* Take care to provide your story in a clear and detailed manner.
* If you need assistance or have concerns, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
