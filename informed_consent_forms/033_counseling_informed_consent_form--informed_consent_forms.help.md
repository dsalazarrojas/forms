# Counseling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Counseling Informed Consent Form is used to collect personal information and emergency contact details from clients. It is a crucial document that serves as a record of the client's consent and agreement to participate in the counseling process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Provide the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
7. Enter the contact information of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
8. Describe the relationship between you and the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
9. Enter the name of the person next of kin (if any) in the "Next of Kin" field.
10. Sign the "Consent Form" field by typing your name.
11. Date the "Consent Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you easily.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Date of Birth** (optional): Enter your date of birth to ensure we can verify your identity.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (optional): Provide the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (optional): Enter the contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Next of Kin** (optional): Enter the name of the person next of kin (if any).
* **Consent Form** (optional): Sign your name to indicate your consent to participate in the counseling process.
* **Consent Date** (optional): Date the consent form to record the date of agreement.

Remember to review and sign the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all the information provided is accurate.
