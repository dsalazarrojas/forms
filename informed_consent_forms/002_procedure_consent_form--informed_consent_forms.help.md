# Procedure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procedure Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing a medical procedure. This form helps to ensure that patients understand the risks, benefits, and requirements of the procedure, which is essential for their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your medical history and the procedure you are about to undergo.
2. Read and understand the risks and benefits associated with the procedure.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
4. Have a medical staff member sign and date the form as well, to confirm that you have been informed and that you are aware of the risks and benefits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (Page 1)
	+ This field is used to gather information about your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are currently taking.
	+ Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure that the medical staff can make informed decisions about your care.
* **Procedure Details** (Page 2)
	+ This field is used to describe the procedure you are about to undergo, including what it entails and what you can expect.
	+ Please answer in your own words, and be as detailed as possible.
* **Risks and Benefits** (Page 3)
	+ This field is used to explain the risks and benefits associated with the procedure.
	+ Please read and understand the information provided, and ask any questions you may have.
* **Signature** (Page 4)
	+ This field is used to sign and date the form, confirming that you have read and understood the information provided.
	+ Please sign and date the form carefully and legibly.
* **Date of Consent** (Page 5)
	+ This field is used to record the date on which you gave your informed consent for the procedure.
	+ Please ensure that the date is accurate and legible.
* **Date of Procedure** (Page 6)
	+ This field is used to record the date on which the procedure is scheduled to take place.
	+ Please ensure that the date is accurate and legible.
* **Medical Staff Signature** (Page 7)
	+ This field is used by a medical staff member to sign and date the form, confirming that you have been informed and that you are aware of the risks and benefits.
	+ Please have a medical staff member sign and date the form carefully and legibly.
* **Patient Signature** (Page 8)
	+ This field is used to sign and date the form, confirming that you have read and understood the information provided.
	+ Please sign and date the form carefully and legibly.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 9)
	+ This field is used to provide any additional comments or information that you may have about your procedure.
	+ Please be as detailed as possible, but ensure that your comments are clear and concise.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any of the information provided, please ask a medical staff member for clarification.
- This form is confidential and will be kept on file for your medical record.
