# Consent To Opt Out Of Face Mask Requirement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians to opt out of a face mask requirement in schools. It is essential to understand that the decision to opt out may have implications on the student's health risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked with *):
	* Student Name
	* Student ID Number
	* Date of Birth
	* Parent or Guardian Name
	* Contact Email Address
	* Contact Phone Number
	* Address
	* Relationship to Student
	* Opt Out Decision
	* Reason for Opting Out
	* Reason Details
	* Alternative Precautions
	* Alternative Precautions Other
	* Emergency Contact Name
	* Emergency Contact Phone
	* Parent or Guardian Signature
	* Date of Signature
2. Choose the correct option for the fields with a drop-down menu:
	* Relationship to Student
	* Opt Out Decision
	* Reason for Opting Out
	* Alternative Precautions
	* Approval Status
	* Do You Understand the Risks
	* School Policy Understood
	* Will You Comply with Other Safety Measures
3. If you select "Yes" for "Medical Condition" or "Mental Health Considerations" under "Reason for Opting Out", please provide supporting documentation and fill in the corresponding fields:
	* Healthcare Provider Name
	* Healthcare Provider Contact
4. If you select "Other" under "Reason for Opting Out" or "Alternative Precautions", please provide a description in the corresponding field:
	* Reason Details
	* Alternative Precautions Other

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Date**: Date of form submission.
* **Student Name**: Full name of student.
* **Student ID Number**: Student identification number.
* **Date of Birth**: Student date of birth.
* **Grade Level**: Current grade or year level.
* **School Name**: Name of school.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Full name of parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email Address**: Contact email address.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Contact phone number.
* **Address**: Mailing address.
* **Relationship to Student**: Relationship to the student. Options: Mother, Father, Legal Guardian, Foster Parent, Other.
* **Opt Out Decision**: Are you opting out of mask requirement. Options: Yes - Opting Out, No - Will Continue Wearing Mask.
* **Reason for Opting Out**: Select primary reason. Options: Medical Condition, Mental Health Considerations, Personal Belief, Religious Belief, Other.
* **Reason Details**: Provide additional explanation.
* **Alternative Precautions**: Select alternative safety measures. Options: Social Distancing, Regular Hand Washing, Stay Home When Sick, Regular Testing, Improved Ventilation at Home, Limit Exposure Outside School, Other.
* **Alternative Precautions Other**: Describe alternative precautions if other selected.
* **Is Medical Documentation Attached**: If medical reason, do you have documentation? Options: Yes - Attached, Yes - Will Provide, False, Not Applicable.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Name of doctor or provider if applicable.
* **Healthcare Provider Contact**: Phone or email for provider.
* **Do You Understand the Risks**: Do you understand potential increased risk. Options: Yes - Fully Understood, Yes - Generally Understood, False.
* **Risks Acknowledged**: Acknowledge health risks. Options: Yes - I Acknowledge Risks, False.
* **School Policy Understood**: Do you understand school policies. Options: True, False.
* **Will You Comply with Other Safety Measures**: Will you follow other school safety rules. Options: True, False.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number for emergency contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as signature.
* **Date of Signature**: Date when signing.
* **Student Signature**: Student signature if age appropriate.
* **School Administrator Name**: Name of administrator reviewing form.
* **Approval Status**: For school use. Options: Pending Review, Approved, Denied, More Information Required.

# Tips

* Please ensure that you have a valid reason for opting out of the face mask requirement.
* If you select "Yes" for "Medical Condition" or "Mental Health Considerations" under "Reason for Opting Out", please provide supporting documentation.
* Please describe alternative precautions if other selected under "Alternative Precautions".
* The parent or guardian must sign the form, but if the student is old enough, they may also sign it.
* This form is for schools to review and approve your request.
