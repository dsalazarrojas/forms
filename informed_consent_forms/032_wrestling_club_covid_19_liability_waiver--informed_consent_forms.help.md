# wrestling_club_covid_19_liability_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wrestling club's COVID-19 liability waiver form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians about their child's medical history, emergency contact information, and consent to participate in the club's activities. This form helps to protect the club's liability and ensure participants' safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Parent Information**: Enter the parent's name, phone number, and email address.
2. **Emergency Contact Information**: Provide the name, phone number, and email address of the emergency contact person.
3. **Consent to Treatment**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to medical treatment for their child.
4. **Medical Conditions**: Select any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies that the child may have.
5. **Medications**: Select any medications the child is currently taking.
6. **Emergency Medical Information**: Provide any additional emergency medical information about the child.
7. **Allergies**: Report any allergies or medical conditions that the child may have.
8. **Medical History**: Enter any previous medical history relevant to the child's participation.
9. **Consent to Liability**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to the club's liability waiver.
10. **Consent to COVID-19 Claims**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to COVID-19 claims.
11. **Consent to Video Recording**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to video recording of their child during club activities.
12. **Emergency Contact Signature**: Sign off as the emergency contact person.
13. **Parent Signature**: Sign off as the parent/guardian.
14. **Coach Signature**: Sign off as the coach.
15. **Consent to Participation**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to their child's participation in the club.
16. **Consent to Medical Treatment**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to medical treatment for their child.
17. **Consent to Club Policies**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to the club's policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information (id: 1)**: Enter the parent's name, phone number, and email address.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the parent/guardian can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Information (id: 2)**: Provide the name, phone number, and email address of the emergency contact person.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the emergency contact person can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (id: 3)**: Describe the relationship between the emergency contact person and the child.
	+ This field is not required but can be useful to understand the emergency contact person's connection to the child.
* **Consent to Treatment (id: 4)**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to medical treatment for their child.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the parent/guardian understands the club's medical treatment policies.
* **Medical Conditions (id: 5)**: Select any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies that the child may have.
	+ This field helps the club's medical staff to understand the child's medical history.
* **Medications (id: 6)**: Select any medications the child is currently taking.
	+ This field ensures that the club's medical staff is aware of any medications the child is taking.
* **Emergency Medical Information (id: 7)**: Provide any additional emergency medical information about the child.
	+ This field can be useful to understand any specific medical conditions or needs the child may have.
* **Allergies (id: 8)**: Report any allergies or medical conditions that the child may have.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the club's staff is aware of any allergies or conditions that may affect the child's participation.
* **Medical History (id: 9)**: Enter any previous medical history relevant to the child's participation.
	+ This field can be useful to understand any previous medical conditions or injuries the child may have.
* **Consent to Liability (id: 10)**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to the club's liability waiver.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the parent/guardian understands the club's liability policies.
* **Consent to COVID-19 Claims (id: 11)**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to COVID-19 claims.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the parent/guardian understands the club's COVID-19 policies.
* **Consent to Video Recording (id: 12)**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to video recording of their child during club activities.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the parent/guardian understands the club's video recording policies.
* **Emergency Contact Signature (id: 13)**: Sign off as the emergency contact person.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the emergency contact person has acknowledged their role.
* **Parent Signature (id: 14)**: Sign off as the parent/guardian.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the parent/guardian has acknowledged their consent.
* **Coach Signature (id: 15)**: Sign off as the coach.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the coach has acknowledged their role.
* **Consent to Participation (id: 17)**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to their child's participation in the club.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the parent/guardian understands the club's participation policies.
* **Consent to Medical Treatment (id: 18)**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to medical treatment for their child.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the parent/guardian understands the club's medical treatment policies.
* **Consent to Club Policies (id: 20)**: Confirm that the parent/guardian consents to the club's policies.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the parent/guardian understands the club's policies.
* **Emergency Contact Information 2 (id: 21)** to **Emergency Contact Information 6 (id: 25)**: Provide any additional emergency contact information.
	+ These fields are not required but can be useful to understand any additional emergency contact information.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review and confirm all fields carefully before submitting.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask a club representative for assistance.
* Please sign and date all relevant fields.
