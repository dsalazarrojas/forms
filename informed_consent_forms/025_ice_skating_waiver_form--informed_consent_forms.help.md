# Ice Skating Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ice Skating Waiver Form is a mandatory document that must be completed by all participants in our ice skating program. It ensures that you are aware of the risks involved with ice skating and provides us with essential contact information and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Provide your emergency contact information (name and phone number).
4. Select your level of ice skating experience (Option 1, Intermediate, or Advanced).
5. Choose the type of equipment you will be using (Option 1, Gloves, or Skates).
6. If applicable, list any medical conditions you have.
7. Select your relationship to your emergency contact (Option 1, Guardian, or Other).
8. Agree to our terms and conditions.
9. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Skating Experience**: Select the level of ice skating experience you have (Option 1, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Equipment Provided**: Choose the type of equipment you will be using (Option 1, Gloves, or Skates).
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select your relationship to your emergency contact (Option 1, Guardian, or Other).
* **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to our terms and conditions by signing and dating the form.
* **Waiver**: Sign and date the form to acknowledge the risks involved with ice skating.
* **Date**: Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you sign the form.
* **Skating Location**: Enter the location where you will be participating in ice skating.
* **Skating Program**: Enter the name of the ice skating program you are participating in.
* **Skating Duration**: Enter the duration of your ice skating participation.
* **Skating Frequency**: Select how often you participate in ice skating (Option 1, 3-5 times/week, or 6 or more times/week).
* **Emergency Contact Consent**: Agree to our emergency contact consent by selecting the correct option.
* **Signature 1**: Enter your signature.
* **Signature 2**: Enter the signature of your emergency contact.
* **Terms of Use**: Enter your agreement to our terms of use.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Timestamp**: Enter the timestamp when you sign the form.

Note: This guide assumes that you have already accessed the form through our website or mobile application. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
