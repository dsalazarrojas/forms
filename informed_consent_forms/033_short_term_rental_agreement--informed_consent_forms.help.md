# Short Term Rental Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for collecting information and ensuring that all necessary details are documented for a short term rental agreement between the renter and the landlord.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Ensure that you fill in the correct information for both the renter and the landlord.
3. Sign the agreement with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renter Name**: \*\* The name of the person renting the unit.
* **Unit Address**: \*\* The address of the unit being rented.
* **Start Date**: \*\* The date on which the rental period begins.
* **End Date**: \*\* The date on which the rental period ends.
* **Renter Signature**: \*\* A physical signature of the person renting the unit.
* **Landlord Signature**: \*\* A physical signature of the person/organization renting out the unit.
* **Unit Type**: \*\* A description of the type of unit being rented (e.g. apartment, house, room, etc.).
* **Unit Details**: \*\* A brief description of the unit and its features (e.g. number of bedrooms, amenities, etc.).
* **Terms & Conditions**: \*\* The terms and conditions of the rental agreement, including but not limited to payment terms, duration of stay, and expectations for the rented unit.
* **Contact Info**: \*\* Contact information for the renter, including phone and email.
* **Emergency Contact**: \*\* A contact person to be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Renter Phone**: \*\* The phone number of the person renting the unit.
* **Renter Email**: \*\* The email of the person renting the unit.
* **Landlord Phone**: \*\* The phone number of the person/organization renting out the unit.
* **Landlord Email**: \*\* The email of the person/organization renting out the unit.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the agreement.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the landlord.
* Keep a copy of the signed agreement for your records.
