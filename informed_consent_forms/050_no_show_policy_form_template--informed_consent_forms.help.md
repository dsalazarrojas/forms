# No Show Policy Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The No Show Policy Form Template is designed to document and communicate no-show incidents with customers. This form should be used when a customer is unable to attend their scheduled appointment or session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant option from the "Contact Method" field to indicate how you would like to be contacted for further communication.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field if you would like to be contacted by phone.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you would like to be contacted by email.
5. Provide a clear and concise explanation for the no-show in the "No Show Reason" field.
6. Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field if necessary.
7. Select the time zone for the appointment in the "Time Zone" field.
8. Enter the date and time of the appointment in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
9. Enter any customer notes or feedback in the "Customer Notes" field.
10. Enter any staff notes or feedback in the "Staff Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Attendance Policy** (`attendance_policy`, text, optional): Please describe the attendance policy that was communicated to you.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please enter your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to indicate how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by email.
* **No Show Reason** (`no_show_reason`, text, optional): Please provide a clear and concise explanation for the no-show.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the appointment.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_multiple, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any customer notes or feedback.
* **Staff Notes** (`staff_notes`, note, optional): Enter any staff notes or feedback.
