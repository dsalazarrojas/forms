# Patient Journey Mapping Project Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Journey Mapping Project is a research initiative that aims to improve patient experiences and outcomes. This consent form is a crucial part of the project, and it's essential to complete it carefully and accurately to ensure your participation and information are properly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient information on page 1.
2. Enter your contact details on page 2.
3. Choose your consent status on page 3.
4. Leave a signature on page 4.
5. Add your contact signature on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information (page 1)
* **Patient Information**: This field is where you can provide your personal details, such as your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information about yourself as a patient.

### Contact Details (page 2)
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.

### Patient Consent (page 3)
* **Patient Consent**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ **Yes**: You consent to participate in the Patient Journey Mapping Project.
	+ **No**: You do not consent to participate in the project.
	+ **Conditional**: You consent, but with conditions that need to be discussed with the project team.

### Signatures (page 4)
* **Signatures**: Leave your signature, which will serve as proof of your consent.

### Contact Signature (page 5)
* **Contact Signature**: Add your signature as the contact person or representative for the patient.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the project's terms and conditions before completing this form.
* Double-check your information and choices before submitting the form.
* If you need any assistance or have questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
