# Vehicle Compliance Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain vehicle compliance waivers from customers for vehicle upgrades or other relevant modifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page, and follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Upgrades** (`Vehicle Upgrades`, Select Multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all vehicle upgrades you would like to acknowledge.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, Text, required: false):
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your vehicle documents or records.
* **Email** (`Email`, Email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached for further inquiries.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, Text, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for further inquiries.
* **Signature Date** (`Signature Date`, Date, required: false):
	+ Date of your signature on this waiver.
* **Vehicle Model** (`Vehicle Model`, Text, required: false):
	+ Enter the vehicle model number or make (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Upgrade Type** (`Upgrade Type`, Text, required: false):
	+ Brief description of the upgrade or modification.
* **Additional information** (Note: none)
Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete for proper documentation and compliance purposes.

## Tips
Please review your information carefully before submitting this form. Ensure you understand what you are agreeing to.
