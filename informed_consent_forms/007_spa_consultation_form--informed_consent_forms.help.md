# Spa Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Consultation Form is designed to collect information from clients interested in booking a spa service. This form helps us understand their needs, preferences, and availability to provide the best possible experience. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that we can tailor our services to your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your client information, including your name and contact details.
2.  Select the services you are interested in, including massage, facial, and manicure options.
3.  If you have any additional services in mind, please select them from the list.
4.  Specify your preferences, such as any specific areas of concern or areas of interest.
5.  Indicate your availability for the appointment, including your preferred date and time.
6.  If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide details in the medical field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info**: This section is for your basic contact information, including your name and contact details.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide your phone number and email address so we can reach out to you for further details.
* **Services**: Select the specific services you are interested in, such as massage, facial, or manicure. You can choose only one or multiple services.
* **Services Additional**: If there are any other services you would like to consider, please select them from the list.
* **Preferences**: Describe any specific areas you would like us to focus on during your service or if you have any special requests.
* **Availability**: Indicate your preferred date and time for the appointment, and we will do our best to accommodate it.
* **Medical**: If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide details so we can take necessary precautions.
