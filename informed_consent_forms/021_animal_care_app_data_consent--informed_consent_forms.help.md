# Animal Care App Data Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to document your consent for the collection and usage of your pet's data within our Animal Care App. By submitting this form, you are giving us permission to collect and use data for the following purposes:

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1: Pet Owner Information**
	* Enter your pet's owner full name in the "Pet Owner Full Name" field.
	* Enter your primary contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
	* Enter your street address in the "Address" field.
2. **Step 2: Pet Information**
	* Enter the name of your pet in the "Pet's Name" field.
	* Select the type of pet you have from the "Species" field (e.g., Dog, Cat, Bird, Rabbit, etc.).
	* Enter your pet's age in the "Age" field.
	* If applicable, enter your pet's microchip number in the "Microchip Number" field.
3. **Step 3: Data Collection Consent**
	* Select all the types of data you are comfortable with us collecting about your pet, such as "Pet health records", "Vaccination history", etc.
	* Select the primary purpose of data collection for your pet, such as "Pet health management" or "Veterinary care coordination".
	* Choose who you would like to have access to your pet's data, such as your primary veterinarian, emergency veterinary clinics, etc.
4. **Step 4: Data Storage and Security**
	* Read and acknowledge that we will store your data securely.
	* Understand that you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data.
	* Acknowledge that we have taken industry-standard security measures to protect your data.
5. **Step 5: Consent and Rights**
	* Understand that your consent is valid for a certain period and can be revoked by you at any time.
	* Select all the rights you want to grant us, such as access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, etc.
6. **Step 6: Marketing and Analytics**
	* Decide if you want to receive marketing communications from us via email or SMS.
	* Agree to allow us to collect anonymous usage analytics for app improvement.
7. **Step 7: Signature and Acknowledgment**
	* Sign your name in the "Owner Signature" field.
	* Enter the date of signature in the "Date of Signature" field.
	* If applicable, enter the name of a witness in the "Witness Name" field.
