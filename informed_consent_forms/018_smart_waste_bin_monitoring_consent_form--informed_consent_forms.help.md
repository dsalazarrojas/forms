This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in a waste bin monitoring study. Informed consent is a critical component of any research study, ensuring that participants are aware of the purpose, risks, and benefits of the study and that they agree to participate voluntarily. The form's purpose is to collect demographic information about the participant and their relationship with the waste bin being monitored, and to obtain their consent to be part of the monitoring program.

The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields. This guide will walk you through each section and explain the purpose and requirements for each field.

# Smart Waste Bin Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from participants in the waste bin monitoring study. The study aims to monitor waste bin levels and provide insights on waste management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the Participant Details section.
2. Provide your address in the Address section.
3. Indicate the location of the waste bin in the Waste Bin Location section.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Choose your preferred option regarding consent.
6. Provide your email address (optional).
7. Enter your phone number (optional).
8. Add any relevant participant notes (optional).
9. Enter a unique label for the address (optional).
10. Enter the location coordinates of the waste bin (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
• **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering your name, surname, or any other relevant details that you feel comfortable sharing about yourself.

### Address
• **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering your physical address where the waste bin is located. Please enter the exact address, including house number and street name.

### Waste Bin Location
• **Waste Bin Location** (`waste_bin_location`, text, required/optional): This field is for indicating where the waste bin is located, such as inside your home, on your property, or in a public area.

### Date Of Birth
• **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): This field is for entering your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.

### Consent
• **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): This field is for selecting one of the two options regarding consent. Please choose the option that best represents your understanding of the study.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, email, optional/optional): This field is for entering your email address. This information will be used for communication purposes only.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional/optional): This field is for entering your phone number. This information will be used for communication purposes only.

### Participant Notes
• **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional/optional): This field is for adding any additional information about yourself or your waste bin that you feel is relevant to the study.

### Address Label
• **Address Label** (`address_label`, text, optional/optional): This field is for entering a unique label for your address, such as your name or a distinctive description.

### Location Coordinates
• **Location Coordinates** (`location_coordinates`, text, optional/optional): This field is for entering the coordinates of the waste bin, such as latitude and longitude.
