# Waiver_brow_lamination_tint - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waiver Brow Lamination Tint form is a document used by professionals in the beauty industry to obtain informed consent from clients for brow lamination and tint services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form in the `Waiver Brow Lamination Tint` field. This field should be a brief and clear description of the form purpose.
2. Select the category of the form from the `Category` dropdown menu. In this case, the options are `Informed Consent Forms` and `Salon Forms`.
3. In the `Description` field, provide a detailed description of the document, as it is used to inform clients of the risks and liabilities associated with the procedure.
4. Enter the tool assigned to the form in the `Assigned Tool` field.
5. Enter the ID of the form in the `Form IDs` field.
6. Specify the output file in the `Output File` field.
7. Finally, enter the name of the person who created the form in the `Created By` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Waiver Brow Lamination Tint
* **Waiver Brow Lamination Tint** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief and clear description of the form purpose.

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the form from the dropdown menu. Options are: Informed Consent Forms, Salon Forms.

* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the document used to inform clients of risks and liabilities associated with the procedure.

* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the tool assigned to the form.

* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, text, required: false): Enter the ID of the form.

* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): Specify the output file.

* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who created the form.

* **Description** (`description_long`, text, required: false): Enter a description of the document, which is used to inform clients of risks and liabilities associated with the procedure.
