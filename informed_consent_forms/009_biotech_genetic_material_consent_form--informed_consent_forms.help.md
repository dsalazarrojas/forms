# Biotech Genetic Material Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants before genetic material collection and analysis. It covers the purpose, risks, and benefits of the research, as well as participants' rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read the form carefully and thoroughly.
2.  Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3.  Sign the consent document once you have read and understood the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Mailing Address** (`participant_address`, text, required): Enter your address for study correspondence.
*   **Type of Genetic Material to be Collected** (`material_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
    *   Blood Sample
    *   Saliva Sample
    *   Tissue Sample
    *   Hair Sample
    *   Buccal Swab
    *   Other
*   **Collection Method** (`material_collection_method`, text, required): Describe how the sample will be collected.
*   **Purposes for Genetic Analysis** (`research_purposes`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
    *   Disease Research
    *   Genetic Sequencing
    *   Biomarker Discovery
    *   Clinical Trials
    *   Ancestry Analysis
    *   Other Academic Research
*   **I Consent to my Genetic Data Being Shared with Other Researchers** (`data_sharing_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   I Agree
    *   I Do Not Agree
*   **I Consent to Potential Commercial Use of Research Results** (`commercial_use`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   I Agree
    *   I Do Not Agree
*   **I Consent to Being Contacted for Future Research Opportunities** (`future_contact`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   I Agree
    *   I Do Not Agree
*   **I Understand the Potential Risks of Genetic Research** (`understand_risks`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   I Understand
    *   I Do Not Understand
*   **I Understand That I May Not Benefit from This Research** (`understand_benefits`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   I Understand
    *   I Do Not Understand
*   **I Acknowledge That My Data Will be De-Identified and Kept Confidential** (`privacy_measures`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   I Agree
    *   I Do Not Agree
*   **Data Retention Period** (`data_retention`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   For Duration of Study Only
    *   5 Years After Study
    *   10 Years After Study
    *   Indefinitely for Research
*   **I Want the Option to Request Deletion of My Genetic Data** (`data_deletion_option`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   I Agree
    *   I Do Not Agree
*   **I Understand I Can Withdraw from This Study at Any Time** (`right_to_withdraw`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   I Agree
    *   I Do Not Agree
*   **Compensation for Participation** (`compensation`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options:
    *   No Compensation
    *   Gift Card
    *   Research Credit
    *   Other Compensation
*   **Final Consent** (`full_consent`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
    *   I Consent
    *   I Do Not Consent
*   **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent.

## Tips
*   Please review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider or research team for clarification.
*   You can withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
*   Your participation is voluntary, and you have the right to decline or withdraw from the study at any time.
