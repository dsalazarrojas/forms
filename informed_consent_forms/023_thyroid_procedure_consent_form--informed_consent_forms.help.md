# Thyroid Procedure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thyroid Procedure Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from patients or their guardians for procedures related to thyroid health. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you have provided your consent for the procedure by selecting 'True' in the 'Consent Given' field.
3. Sign and date the form in the 'Date of Signature' field.
4. Have the doctor sign and date the form in the 'Date of Signature' field (under 'Doctor's Signature').
5. If a witness is present, have them sign and date the form in the 'Date of Witness Signature' field.
6. Ensure that all required fields have been completed accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): The name of the patient or individual for whom the procedure is intended.

### Email
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): The contact email of the patient or individual for whom the procedure is intended.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): The contact phone number of the patient or individual for whom the procedure is intended.

### Address
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): The address of the patient or individual for whom the procedure is intended.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): The contact phone number of the person completing this form.

### Date of Consent
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, date, required): The date when the consent was given.

### Procedure Description
* **Procedure Description** (`procedure_description`, text, required): A brief description of the medical procedure.

### Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): A description of the medical condition being addressed by the procedure.

### Consent Given
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required): Select 'True' to indicate that consent has been given for the procedure.

### Date of Signature
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, date, required): The date when the form was signed by the patient or individual.

### Patient Signature
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, required): The signature of the patient or individual for whom the procedure is intended.

### Doctor's Name
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): The name of the medical professional performing the procedure.

### Doctor's Signature
* **Doctor's Signature** (`doctor_signature`, note, required): The signature of the medical professional performing the procedure.

### Facility Name
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): The name of the medical facility where the procedure will be performed.

### Facility Signature
* **Facility Signature** (`facility_signature`, note, required): The signature of the medical facility where the procedure will be performed.

### Witness Name
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): The name of the witness present during the consent process.

### Witness Signature
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, note, optional): The signature of the witness present during the consent process.

### Date of Witness Signature
* **Date of Witness Signature** (`consent_signature_date`, date, optional): The date when the witness signed the form.

### Witness Address
* **Witness Address** (`witness_address`, text, optional): The address of the witness present during the consent process.

### Witness Phone
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, text, optional): The contact phone number of the witness.

### Facility Address
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, optional): The address of the medical facility where the procedure will be performed.

### Facility Phone
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`, text, optional): The contact phone number of the medical facility.

### Doctor's Title
* **Doctor's Title** (`doctor_title`, text, optional): The title of the medical professional performing the procedure.

### Patient ID Number
* **Patient ID Number** (`patient_id_number`, text, optional): A unique identification number for the patient.

### Consent Form Version
* **Consent Form Version** (`consent_form_version`, text, required): The version of the consent form being used. 

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Review and understand each field before completing it to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the medical professional or facility staff.
* Complete the form in a secure and confidential environment. 

This guide is designed to ensure that the Thyroid Procedure Consent Form is completed accurately and effectively, reducing the risk of errors and ensuring that all parties involved are aware of the procedure and its requirements.
