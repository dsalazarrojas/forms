## How To Complete This Form

* To complete this form, please select the options that accurately represent your agreement or disagreement with the terms and conditions listed.
* Please fill out each field carefully and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the researcher or project team member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Drone Footage Consent** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' if you agree to participate in the research project and allow the collection of your drone footage.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree or have any concerns.
* **Have you been part of any previous research projects or studies involving drones?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' if you have participated in previous research projects or studies involving drones.
	+ Select 'False' if you have not participated in previous research projects or studies involving drones.
* **Researcher Name** (Text)
	+ Enter the name of the researcher who will be collecting your drone footage.
* **Researcher Contact** (Text)
	+ Enter the contact information of the researcher who will be collecting your drone footage.
* **Researcher Contact Phone** (Text)
	+ Enter the phone number of the researcher who will be collecting your drone footage.
* **Researcher Email** (Email)
	+ Enter the email address of the researcher who will be collecting your drone footage.
* **Research Project Title** (Text)
	+ Enter the title of the research project.
* **Research Project Location** (Text)
	+ Enter the location where the research project will take place.
* **Research Drone Type** (Select Multiple)
	+ Select all applicable drone types that will be used in the research project (Aerial, Ground).
* **Date of Footage Collection** (Date)
	+ Enter the date when the drone footage will be collected.
* **Time of Footage Collection** (Time)
	+ Enter the time when the drone footage will be collected.
* **Duration of Footage Collection** (Number)
	+ Enter the duration of time for which the drone footage will be collected.
* **Footage Collection Height** (Number)
	+ Enter the height from which the drone footage will be collected.
* **Research Purpose** (Text)
	+ Enter a brief description of the research purpose.
* **Additional Information** (Text)
	+ Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your participation in the research project.
* **Do you grant permission for the collection of your personal data?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to grant permission for the collection of your personal data.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not grant permission.
* **Do you agree to the Terms of Service?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to agree to the terms of service.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree.
* **Do you agree to have your image/footage used for marketing and promotional purposes?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to agree to have your image/footage used for marketing and promotional purposes.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree.
* **Do you agree to share your contact information with the researcher?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to agree to share your contact information with the researcher.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree.
* **Do you agree to be contacted by researchers or project team members?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to agree to be contacted by researchers or project team members.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree.
* **Do you agree to allow data to be collected and stored in a secure database for 30 years?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to agree to allow data to be collected and stored in a secure database for 30 years.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree.
* **Do you agree to allow data to be accessed and used by authorized parties for research and development?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to agree to allow data to be accessed and used by authorized parties for research and development.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree.
* **Do you agree to allow data to be accessed and used by authorized parties for commercial purposes?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to agree to allow data to be accessed and used by authorized parties for commercial purposes.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree.
* **Do you agree to be contacted by the researcher in the event that a legal issue arises?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to agree to be contacted by the researcher in the event that a legal issue arises.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree.
* **Do you agree to be contacted by the researcher for any updates or information related to your project?** (Select One)
	+ Select 'True' to agree to be contacted by the researcher for any updates or information related to your project.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not agree.

## Tips

* Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the terms and conditions before submitting your consent.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the researcher or project team member.
* Your participation in this research project is voluntary and you can withdraw your consent at any time.
* The researcher will handle your personal data in accordance with the terms of service and applicable laws.
