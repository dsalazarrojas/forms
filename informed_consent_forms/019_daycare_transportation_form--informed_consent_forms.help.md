# Daycare Transportation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Transportation Form is a document used to collect information about transporting children to or from a daycare center. The form is used to ensure that all necessary details are captured for safe and efficient transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the drop off time field with the time you will be dropping off the child at the daycare center.
2. Enter the address where you will be dropping off the child.
3. Provide the contact information for the person dropping off the child.
4. Describe the vehicle you will be using for transportation.
5. Enter the vehicle's year and make.
6. Select the daycare center where the child will be dropped off.
7. Enter the child's name and age.
8. Choose the method of transportation you will be using (walk, drive, ride).
9. Enter any additional notes about the transportation method.
10. Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
11. Enter any additional notes about the drop off time.
12. Enter any additional notes about the pickup time.
13. Enter any additional notes about the drop off address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drop off time** (`drop_off_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you will be dropping off the child at the daycare center.
* **Drop off address** (`drop_off_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where you will be dropping off the child.
* **Drop off contact** (`drop_off_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the contact information for the person dropping off the child.
* **Vehicle description** (`vehicle_description`, `text`, required): Describe the vehicle you will be using for transportation.
* **Vehicle year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle make** (`vehicle_make`, `select_one`, required): Select the make of the vehicle you will be using.
* **Vehicle model** (`vehicle_model`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the model of the vehicle you will be using.
* **Vehicle license plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, `text`, required): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Daycare center** (`daycare_center`, `select_one`, required): Select the daycare center where the child will be dropped off.
* **Child name** (`child_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the child being transported.
* **Child age** (`child_age`, `number`, required): Enter the age of the child.
* **Transportation method** (`transportation_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the method of transportation you will be using (walk, drive, ride).
* **Transportation note** (`transportation_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the transportation method.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency name** (`emergency_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Drop off time note** (`drop_off_time_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the drop off time.
* **Pickup time note** (`pickup_time_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the pickup time.
* **Drop off address note** (`drop_off_address_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the drop off address.
* **Vehicle description note** (`vehicle_description_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the vehicle description.
* **Vehicle year note** (`vehicle_year_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the vehicle year.
* **Vehicle make note** (`vehicle_make_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the vehicle make.
* **Vehicle model note** (`vehicle_model_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the vehicle model.
* **Daycare center note** (`daycare_center_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the daycare center.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the child's safety.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your transportation coordinator for clarification.
