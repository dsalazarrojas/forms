# Employee Laptop Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Laptop Agreement Form is used to gather information from employees regarding their laptop usage and agreement terms. This includes details such as laptop make, model, and employment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are filling out this form for your organization's internal use only.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If a field is not applicable to you, please leave it blank.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Laptop Make** (`laptop_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of your company-provided laptop. If you don't know the make, please leave it blank.
- **Laptop Model** (`laptop_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of your company-provided laptop. If you don’t know the model, please leave it blank.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID number. If you don’t know your ID number, please ask your HR department for help.
- **Agreement Start Date** (`agreement_start`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your laptop usage agreement. If you are unsure of the start date, please check with your HR department.
- **Agreement End Date** (`agreement_end`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your laptop usage agreement. If you are unsure of the end date, please check with your HR department.
- **Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address. If you are not sure of your email address, please ask your IT department for help.

## Tips
- Ensure you are filling out this form for your organization's internal use only.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your IT or HR department for assistance.
- This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with anyone outside of your organization without permission.
