# Broadband Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Broadband Service Agreement form is used by internet service providers to collect necessary information from customers to establish a broadband service contract. This form is used for both new and existing customers, and may be used to update or modify existing agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section, including the full name, email address, phone number, billing address, and service installation address.
2. Select the broadband service plan that best suits your needs.
3. Choose the connection type and data cap option that you prefer.
4. Enter the agreed monthly service rate.
5. Select the contract term.
6. Select a preferred installation date.
7. Enter the installation fee amount.
8. Choose any equipment to rent from the provided options.
9. Select any additional services you would like to add.
10. Acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions, including early termination fees, acceptable use policies, and privacy policies.
11. Enroll in automatic payments or opt-out.
12. Sign the form electronically with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on any government-issued identification.
* **Customer Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for account communications.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your billing address for service charges.
* **Service Installation Address** (`service_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where service will be installed.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (`city_state_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the city, state, and ZIP code for service installation.
* **Service Plan Selected** (`service_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select the broadband service plan that best suits your needs from the provided options.
* **Connection Type** (`connection_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of connection you prefer from the provided options.
* **Agreed Monthly Rate** (`monthly_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the agreed monthly service rate.
* **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, `select_one`, required): Select the contract duration from the provided options.
* **Preferred Installation Date** (`installation_date`, `date`, required): Select your preferred installation date.
* **Installation Fee** (`installation_fee`, `number`, required): Enter the installation fee amount.
* **Equipment Rental** (`equipment_rental`, `select_multiple`, required): Select equipment to rent from the provided options.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional services you would like to add from the provided options.
* **Data Cap Option** (`data_cap`, `select_one`, required): Select your data cap preference from the provided options.
* **Early Termination Fee Acknowledgment** (`early_termination_fee`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge and agree to the early termination fee.
* **Acceptable Use Policy Agreement** (`acceptable_use_policy`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge and agree to the acceptable use policy.
* **Privacy Policy Agreement** (`privacy_policy`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge and agree to the privacy policy.
* **AutoPay Enrollment** (`autopay_enrollment`, `select_one`, required): Enroll in automatic payments for convenience.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form electronically with your full name.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your service provider directly.
- Review and understand the terms and conditions before agreeing to them.
