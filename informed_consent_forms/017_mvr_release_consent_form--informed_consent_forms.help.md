# mvr_release_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mvr_release_consent_form is used to obtain informed consent from employees for their medical information to be shared with their employer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please fill in the required fields accurately and honestly.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding.
* Once completed, please sign and date the form accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (`Employee`, text, required/optional): 
  Provide your employee name and identification number if available.
* **Employer** (`Employer`, text, required/required): 
  Enter your employer's name and ID number if available.
* **Driving History** (`Driving History`, text, required/required): 
  Provide information about any driving incidents, accidents, or citations you have experienced.
* **Violations** (`Violations`, text, required/optional): 
  List any violations or tickets you have received.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/required): 
  Enter the date on which you are granting consent for your medical information to be shared with your employer.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, note, required/required): 
  Sign your employee signature, indicating that you understand the terms and conditions of this consent.
* **Employer** (`Employer`, note, required/required): 
  Sign and date your employer's signature, indicating that they understand the terms and conditions of this consent.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/required): 
  Enter the date on which your employer has signed this consent form.
* **Title** (`Title`, text, required/optional): 
  Enter your job title or position if you wish to disclose it.
* **Title** (`Title`, text, required/optional): 
  Enter your employer's title or position if you wish to disclose it.

## Tips
* This form is for informational purposes only and is not intended to be used as a medical or legal document.
* Please make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
