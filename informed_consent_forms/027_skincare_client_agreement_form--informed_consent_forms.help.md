# Skincare Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their medical history, contact information, and agreement to participate in the skincare program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name and email address.
2. Select your contact preferences, such as the date and time you would like to be contacted.
3. Choose your emergency contact information, including the name, phone number, and relationship of your emergency contact.
4. Provide any medical conditions, medications, or allergies you have.
5. Share any relevant medical history.
6. Finally, select your agreement status to participate in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as you wish to be addressed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Date**: Choose a date and time that suits you best for contact.
* **Contact Time**: Choose a time that suits you best for contact.
* **Client Agreement**: Select whether you are 18 years or older to participate in the program.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person (e.g., family member, friend, or neighbor).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions**: Describe any medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical History**: Share any relevant medical history you have.

Please note that some fields are optional, but it is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure the best possible care and attention from the skincare program.
