# Hospital Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Admission Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's medical history, treatments, and hospital stay to ensure effective patient care and facilitate prompt attention from medical staff. It is essential that patients understand the purpose and significance of the form before completing it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the questions and fields.
2. Ensure that you have the patient's consent to collect and process their personal data.
3. Complete each field accurately and honestly, providing as much information as possible.
4. Check the form for completeness before submitting it to the hospital staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)** 
  * **Patient Info (text)**: Please provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries.
  * **Medical History (text)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous hospitalizations, surgeries, or significant medical events.
  * **Medication List (select multiple)**: List the patient's current medications, including name, dosage, and frequency.
  * **Allergies (select one)**: Choose any known allergies or sensitivities the patient may have.
  * **Emergency Contacts (select multiple)**: List the patient's emergency contacts, including name, relationship, and contact information.
  * **Medical Conditions (select one)**: Choose any known medical conditions the patient may have.
  * **Patient Registrations (select multiple)**: List any previous hospital registrations or admissions.
  * **Medical Tests (text)**: Describe any recent medical tests or exams the patient has undergone.
  * **Medical Procedures (select one)**: Choose any recent medical procedures the patient has undergone.
  * **Patient Info (text)**: Provide additional information about the patient, including any relevant medical history or concerns.
  * **Doctor Info (text)**: Note any relevant information about the patient's doctor or medical team.
  * **Patient Status (select one)**: Choose the patient's current status, such as admitted, discharged, or transferred.
  * **Medical Status (select one)**: Choose the patient's medical status, such as stable, critical, or unstable.
  * **Hospital Info (text)**: Provide any relevant information about the patient's hospital stay, including room number and floor.
  * **Doctor Info 2 (text)**: Note any additional information about the patient's doctor or medical team.
  * **Discharge Info (select one)**: Choose the patient's discharge status, such as discharge to home or discharge to another hospital.
  * **Other Info (text)**: Provide any additional information about the patient's hospital stay, including any relevant medical history or concerns.
