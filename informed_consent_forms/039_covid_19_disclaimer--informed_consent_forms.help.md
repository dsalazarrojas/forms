# COVID 19 Disclaimer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "COVID-19 Disclaimer" form is a critical part of our business's safety protocols, designed to inform and protect our clients and staff from the risks associated with COVID-19. By completing this form, you will declare your understanding of and agreement to take necessary precautions against COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you have a clear understanding of all the questions and agreements outlined in this form. Review each field carefully and make sure you answer accurately. If you're unsure about any section, please ask for clarification. Once you've completed the form, submit it for review. We'll ensure you understand and agree to the terms before providing services.
