# Flight Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for flight planning, coordination, and status updates. It's intended for internal use within an organization, likely for airport staff, air traffic controllers, or other personnel involved in flight operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (if any). The required fields are marked as such in the field-by-field explanations below.
2. Enter the necessary information for each field as prompted.
3. Review and update your answers as needed.
4. Click the "Submit" button when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First page
* **First page** (page 1): This is the introduction page, providing a general overview of the form.

### Flight details
* **Flight details** (field 2): Enter the details of the flight, including any relevant information such as flight number, departure and arrival times, and flight status (e.g., Active or Inactive).

### Flight plan
* **Flight plan** (field 3): Enter the planned flight details, including any relevant flight routes, schedules, or other necessary information.

### Passenger info
* **Passenger info** (field 4): Enter any information about the passengers, such as names, contact details, or special requirements.

### Flight status
* **Flight status** (field 5): Select the current status of the flight (Active or Inactive). This field is not required, but it helps keep track of flight activities.

### Flight notes
* **Flight notes** (field 6): Enter any additional comments or notes about the flight, such as weather conditions, air traffic control information, or other relevant details.

### Flight updates
* **Flight updates** (field 7): Enter any updates or changes to the flight plan or status.

### Flight extensions
* **Flight extensions** (field 8): Enter any extensions or modifications to the flight plan or status.

### Submit
* **Submit** (field 9): Select "Yes" to confirm that the form is complete and ready for review or approval, or "No" to indicate that it's not ready yet. This field is not required, but it helps track the form's status.

### Date
* **Date** (field 10): Enter the date of the flight.

### Time
* **Time** (field 11): Enter the scheduled time of the flight.

Note: The "Date" and "Time" fields are not required but provide additional relevant information about the flight.
