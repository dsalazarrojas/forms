# Fitness Transformation Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant for individuals sharing their fitness transformation story with our organization. It gathers essential information like name, contact details, and consent for sharing their story. Your data will be handled according to our internal policies and external regulations, such as GDPR and HIPAA.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form. These fields are marked as such.
2. Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
3. Enter your date of birth in the standard format of MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Provide your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your active and valid email address that you can access.
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter the same email address provided in the previous field for verification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the standard format (MM/DD/YYYY). This is for identification and age verification purposes only.
* **Phone**: Provide your active phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter a physical address or an active email that we can use to contact you.
* **Photo Release**: Choose "Yes" if you are willing to share your photo. This is for marketing purposes.
* **Social Media**: Share with us which social media platforms you would like us to share your story on. This is for promotional purposes.
