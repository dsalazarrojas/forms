# lash_and_brow_tint_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent for lash and brow tints. It is designed to be completed by medical professionals to ensure that patients understand the potential risks and benefits of the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the questions and what is being asked.
* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* Attach a copy of the signed form to the patient's file.
