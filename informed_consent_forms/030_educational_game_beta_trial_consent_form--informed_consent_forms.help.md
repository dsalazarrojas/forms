# educational_game_beta_trial_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to provide their informed consent and details for an educational game beta trial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the Participant Details** (Page 1):
	* Enter your name, email address, and other required information.
2. Fill out the Game Details (Page 2): 
	* If you wish, provide any additional information about the game you are participating in.
3. Read and sign the Informed Consent (Page 3):
	* Carefully read the provided text and agree to participate.
4. Fill out the Researcher Details (Page 4):
	* Enter the researcher's name, email, and other required information.
5. Add any Additional Comments (Page 5):
	* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.
6. Review and sign the Participant and Researcher Agreements (Pages 6 and 7):
	* Carefully read and agree to the terms of the study.
