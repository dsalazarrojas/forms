# performance_archive_preservation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance_archive_preservation_consent_form is a form used to obtain informed consent from users regarding the preservation and archiving of their performance-related data. This form helps ensure that users understand and agree to the handling of their data, including storage and usage purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing your user information in the **user_info** field.
2. Next, provide details about your performance in the **performance_details** field.
3. In the **recording_info** field, specify any relevant details about the performance or recording.
4. In the **storage_info** field, describe where your performance data is currently stored or will be stored.
5. Indicate where your performance data is located in the **storage_location** field.
6. Specify the device used to record your performance in the **recording_device** field.
7. Select the storage method used for your performance data in the **storage_method** field.
8. In the **notes** field, add any additional comments or context about your performance data.
9. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
10. Provide your phone number in the **phone** field.
11. If applicable, indicate the date and time you granted consent using the **date_of_consent** and **time_of_consent** fields.
12. Confirm that you have signed the consent form by entering your signature in the **user_signed_consent** field.
13. Confirm that the performance archives personnel have accepted your consent by entering their signature in the **performance_archives_signed_consent** field.
14. Confirm that the archivist has signed the consent form by entering their signature in the **archivist_signed_consent** fields.
15. Finally, specify the date and time you submitted the form using the **date_of_submission** and **time_of_submission** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information so that we can reach out to you if needed.
* **performance_details** (`performance_details`, text, optional): Describe your performance, including any relevant details such as date, location, or event.
* **recording_info** (`recording_info`, text, optional): Provide any information related to how your performance was recorded, including equipment or methods used.
* **storage_info** (`storage_info`, text, optional): Describe where your performance data is currently stored or will be stored, including digital or physical formats.
* **storage_location** (`storage_location`, text, optional): Indicate the location where your performance data is currently stored or will be stored.
* **recording_device** (`recording_device`, select_one, optional): Specify the device used to record your performance, such as Audio Recorder, Video Camera, or Other.
* **storage_method** (`storage_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method used for storing your performance data, such as Cloud Storage, External Drive, or On-site Storage.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or context about your performance data.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your performance data.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **date_of_consent** (`date_of_consent`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you granted consent for the preservation and archiving of your performance data.
* **time_of_consent** (`time_of_consent`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time you granted consent for the preservation and archiving of your performance data.
* **user_signed_consent** (`user_signed_consent`, text, optional): Confirm that you have signed the consent form by entering your signature.
* **performance_archives_signed_consent** (`performance_archives_signed_consent`, text, optional): Confirm that the performance archives personnel have accepted your consent by entering their signature.
* **archivist_signed_consent** (`archivist_signed_consent`, text, optional): Confirm that the archivist has signed the consent form by entering their signature.
* **date_of_submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, optional): Specify the date you submitted the form.
* **time_of_submission** (`time_of_submission`, time, optional): Specify the time you submitted the form.

Please note that the optional fields are not required and you can skip them if not applicable.
