# dog_behavior_training_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from dog owners about their dogs' behavior, training needs, and health conditions to ensure our team can provide the best possible training consultation for each dog.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete each field carefully, ensuring you answer all required fields. You can select multiple options for fields that allow multiple selections. When filling out the form, keep in mind that we are here to help your dog get the best possible training consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Training Status** (`dog_training_status`, select_one, required): Select the current training status of your dog from the options provided.
* **Dog Type** (`dog_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of dog you have from the options provided.
* **Dog Age** (`dog_age`, number, optional): If known, provide your dog's age.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, optional): If known, provide your dog's breed.
* **Dog Weight** (`dog_weight`, text, optional): If known, provide your dog's weight.
* **Dog Height** (`dog_height`, text, optional): If known, provide your dog's height.
* **Dog Color** (`dog_color`, text, optional): If known, provide your dog's color.
* **Dog Health Conditions** (`dog_health_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select all health conditions that apply to your dog.
* **Dog Medications** (`dog_medications`, select_multiple, optional): If your dog is currently taking any medications, select them from the options provided.
* **Dog Allergies** (`dog_allergies`, select_multiple, optional): If your dog has any allergies, select them from the options provided.
* **Training Location** (`dog_training_location`, text, optional): If known, provide the location where your dog is trained.
* **Training Frequency** (`dog_training_frequency`, text, optional): If known, provide the frequency of your dog's training.
* **Dog Owner First Name** (`dog_owner_first_name`, text, optional): Provide your first name.
* **Dog Owner Last Name** (`dog_owner_last_name`, text, optional): Provide your last name.
* **Dog Owner Email** (`dog_owner_email`, text, optional): Provide your email address.
* **Dog Owner Phone** (`dog_owner_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number.
* **Dog Owner Relationship** (`dog_owner_relationship`, text, optional): If known, describe your relationship to the dog.
* **Dog Owner Signature** (`dog_owner_signature`, text, optional): Provide your signature as the dog owner.
* **Dog Owner Date** (`dog_owner_date`, date, optional): Date of submission.
* **Dog Behavior 1** (`dog_behavior_training_1`, text, optional): Describe your dog's behavior.
* **Dog Behavior 2** (`dog_behavior_training_2`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, describe your dog's behavior in the provided options.
* **Training Goal** (`dog_training_goal`, text, optional): If known, state your dog's training goal.
* **Dog Behavior 3** (`dog_behavior_training_3`, select_one, optional): Select an option regarding your dog's behavior.
* **Owner Notes** (`dog_owner_notes`, note, optional): Leave any additional notes for our team.
* **Dog Owner Notes** (`dog_owner_notes`, note, optional): Leave any additional notes for our team.
