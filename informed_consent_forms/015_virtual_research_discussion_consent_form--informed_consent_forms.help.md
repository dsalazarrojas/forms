# Virtual Research Discussion Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Research Discussion Consent Form is a document that captures participant information and informed consent for virtual research studies. This form is designed to ensure that participants understand the study and its requirements before participating in the research.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and carefully.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
3. Signature blocks require a handwritten signature or digital equivalent.
4. Date fields require the date in mm/dd/yyyy format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Name**: A field to input the participant's name.
* **Email Address**: A field to input the participant's email address.
* **Contact Number**: A field to input a phone number for contact, if desired.
* **Participant Type**: A field to select the participant's type (Research Participant, Study Staff, Other, or Not Specified).
* **Research Study**: A field to input the research study name.
* **Participant Signature**: A field for the participant to sign their name.
* **Date of Birth**: A field to input the participant's date of birth.
* **Gender**: A field to select the participant's gender (Male, Female, Other, or Not Specified).
* **Marital Status**: A field to select the participant's marital status (Married, Single, Divorced, Separated, or Not Specified).
* **Ethnicity**: A field to select the participant's ethnicity (African American, Asian, Caucasian, Hispanic or Latino, Native American, Other, or Not Specified).
* **Language**: A field to select the participant's language (English, Spanish, Chinese, French, or Not Specified).
* **Country**: A field to select the participant's country (USA, Canada, Australia, China, India, or Not Specified).
* **Research Study Title**: A field to input the research study title.
* **Research Study Description**: A field to input a description of the research study.
* **Research Study Type**: A field to select the research study type (Online, On-site, or Not Specified).
* **Research Study Duration**: A field to input the duration of the research study.
* **Participant Informed Consent**: A field for the participant to read and sign the consent form.
* **Participant Informed Consent 2**: A field to read and sign the consent form again.
* **Participant Signature 2**: A field for the participant to sign their name.
* **Date of Signature 2**: A field to input the date of signature.
* **Research Study Signature**: A field for the research study staff to sign.
* **Date of Signature**: A field to input the date of signature.
* **Research Study Signature 2**: A field for the research study staff to sign again.
* **Research Study Signature 3**: A field for the research study staff to sign.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding.
* If unsure about any field, contact the research study staff for clarification.
* Ensure all fields are complete before submitting the form.
