# Phlebotomy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for phlebotomy procedures, which involve the removal of blood samples from a patient for medical testing. The form ensures that patients understand the purpose and risks of the procedure, and that they give their informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your contact information and any other relevant details as required.
3. Sign the form to confirm that you have understood the procedure and its risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details as required. This section helps medical staff to identify you and contact you if necessary.
* **Procedure Information**: Enter a brief description of the phlebotomy procedure, including any relevant medical information. This section helps medical staff understand the purpose of the procedure.
* **Informed Consent**: This section explains the risks and benefits of the procedure. Read it carefully and sign below to confirm that you have understood the procedure and its risks.
* **Signature**: Sign below to confirm that you have read and understood the procedure and its risks. This is a required field.
* **Medical Staff Signature**: Sign below to confirm that you have informed the patient about the procedure and its risks.
* **Capture Date**: Enter the date and time the informed consent was captured.
* **Capture Time**: Enter the time the informed consent was captured.
* **Medical Staff**: Enter the name and contact information of the medical staff member who is performing the phlebotomy procedure.
* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Medical Staff Name**: Enter the name of the medical staff member who is performing the phlebotomy procedure.
* **Patient Contact**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information, including email or fax number.
* **Medical Information**: Enter any additional medical information relevant to the procedure.
* **Blood Draw Details**: Enter any additional details about the blood draw procedure, including the location and any relevant medical information.
* **Medical Staff Signature**: Sign below to confirm that you have informed the patient about the procedure and its risks.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign below to confirm that you have read and understood the procedure and its risks.
* **Medical Information**: Enter any additional medical information relevant to the procedure.
* **Blood Draw Date**: Enter the date of the blood draw procedure.
* **Capture Time**: Enter the time the blood draw procedure was captured.
* **Medical Staff Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the medical staff member performing the procedure.
* **Patient Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the patient.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the contact person.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Medical Staff Email**: Enter the email of the medical staff member performing the procedure.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Medical Staff Fax**: Enter the fax number of the medical staff member performing the procedure.
* **Patient Fax**: Enter your fax number.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and is not intended to be a substitute for medical advice or treatment. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider.
