# HRA Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HRA Claim Form is designed to collect information from users to determine their eligibility for a certain service or resource. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth processing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and make sure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field, if applicable.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to all questions.
4. Attach any required documents or files, if necessary.
5. Submit the form once you've completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Form** (hra_claim_form_first_page): Select 'Yes' if you have a legitimate claim, or 'No' if you don't.
* **First Page Date** (hra_claim_form_first_page_date): Enter the date you would like to claim for. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **First Page Time** (hra_claim_form_first_page_time): Enter the time you would like to claim for. Use 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Name** (hra_claim_form_first_page_name): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Email** (hra_claim_form_first_page_email): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will help us contact you about your claim.
* **Phone** (hra_claim_form_first_page_phone): Enter a phone number that you can be reached at. This will help us contact you about your claim.
* **Amount** (hra_claim_form_first_page_amount): Enter the amount you would like to claim for. Please use the currency specified in the currency field.
* **Currency** (hra_claim_form_first_page_currency): Select the currency you would like to claim in. This will help us process your claim correctly.
* **Description** (hra_claim_form_first_page_description): Provide a brief description of your claim. This will help us understand your needs and process your claim efficiently.
* **Attachment** (hra_claim_form_first_page_attach_file): Attach any required documents or files that support your claim. Please make sure they are in a supported format.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Use clear and concise language when providing descriptions or attachments.
* Attachments should be in a format that is easily readable and understandable.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for support.
* Please note that this form is for claiming a resource or service, and you should only submit it if you have a legitimate claim.
