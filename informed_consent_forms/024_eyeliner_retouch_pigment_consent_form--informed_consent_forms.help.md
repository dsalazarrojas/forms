# Eyeliner Retouch Pigment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Eyeliner Retouch Pigment Consent Form is a document used to gather information from clients before performing the eyeliner retouching procedure. It serves as a legal agreement between the client and the practitioner, ensuring that both parties are on the same page regarding the procedure, its risks, and the consent for its execution.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the **Client Details** page (page 1) with the client's basic information, such as name and contact details, to enable communication and scheduling.
- On the **Medical Details** page (page 2), provide any medical information that is relevant to the procedure, as this will help the practitioner assess the client's suitability.
- On the **Consent To Procedure** page (page 3), select **Do you consent to have the procedure?** to indicate your agreement. If you choose **Do not consent**, please explain your reason so that the practitioner can discuss alternatives.
- Sign the form on the **Signature** page (page 4) to confirm your consent and understanding of the procedure.
- Provide the date you sign on the **Date** page (page 5) to ensure the form is timestamped and legally binding.
- The **Practitioner Info** page (page 6) is optional but allows you to include additional details or comments for the practitioner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details (Client Details)**: Enter your name and contact information in this field. This will help the practitioner contact and schedule you for the procedure.
* **Medical Details (Medical Details)**: Provide any relevant medical information that may affect the procedure. This could include previous surgeries, allergies, or medications.
* **Consent To Procedure (Consent To Procedure)**: Select "Do you consent to have the procedure?" to confirm your agreement. If you do not consent, provide an explanation for your reasons.
* **Signature (Signature)**: This is where you sign to confirm your agreement and understanding of the procedure's implications.
* **Date (Date)**: Include the date you sign this form, ensuring it is timestamped and legally binding.
* **Practitioner Info (Practitioner Info)**: This field is optional but allows you to include additional details or comments for the practitioner, such as questions or concerns.

### Tips

- Make sure you understand the implications of the procedure before completing this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the procedure, ask the practitioner for clarification before proceeding.
- Ensure you sign and date the form after you have read and understood the implications of the procedure.
