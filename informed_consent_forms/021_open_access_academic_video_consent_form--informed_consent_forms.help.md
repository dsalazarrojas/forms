# Open Access Academic Video Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from participants in an academic video recording or publication project. It is intended to be used in conjunction with other data collection tools, such as video recordings and participant information sheets.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text boxes.
4. Ensure that you have reviewed the participant information sheet and understand the project's purpose and scope.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the questions and provided your informed consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Open Access Academic Video Consent Form** (`1`, `select_multiple`, optional): This is the main section of the form. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are giving your consent to participate in the video recording or publication project.
* **Participant Information** (`2`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information about yourself that may be relevant to the project.
* **Contact Details** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): This section is used to record your contact information. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are willing to share your contact information with the researcher or project coordinator.
* **Research Use** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the type of research use you are consenting to:
	+ Teaching
	+ Research
	+ Both
* **Participant Audio/Video** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the type of audio/video recording you are consenting to:
	+ Recorded audio and video
	+ No audio or video
	+ Only audio
	+ Only video
* **Participant Image** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select the type of images you are consenting to:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Participant Audio/Video Consent** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are consenting to audio/video recording.
* **Participant Image Consent** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are consenting to images being recorded.
* **Participant Audio/Video Consent Details** (`10`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional details about your consent regarding audio/video recording.
* **Participant Image Consent Details** (`11`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional details about your consent regarding images.
* **Participant Audio/Video Consent Date** (`12`, `date`, optional): Please provide the date you consented to audio/video recording.
* **Participant Image Consent Date** (`13`, `date`, optional): Please provide the date you consented to images being recorded.
* **Participant Audio/Video Consent Time** (`14`, `time`, optional): Please provide the time you consented to audio/video recording.
* **Participant Image Consent Time** (`15`, `time`, optional): Please provide the time you consented to images being recorded.
* **Researcher Audio/Video Consent** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are consenting to audio/video recording by the researcher.
* **Researcher Image Consent** (`17`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are consenting to images being recorded by the researcher.
* **Researcher Consent** (`18`, `select_one`, optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are consenting to the researcher's involvement in the project.
* **Researcher Consent Date** (`19`, `date`, optional): Please provide the date you consented to the researcher's involvement in the project.
* **Researcher Consent Time** (`20`, `time`, optional): Please provide the time you consented to the researcher's involvement in the project.
* **Participant Consent** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are consenting to participate in the project.
* **Participant Consent Details** (`22`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional details about your consent.
* **Video Capture Details** (`23`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional details about the video recording.
* **Publish Details** (`24`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional details about the publication.
* **Publish** (`25`, `select_one`, optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are consenting to publication.

## Tips

* Please take your time to read each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the researcher or project coordinator.
* This form is not a substitute for the participant information sheet or any other project documentation.
* Ensure that you have reviewed the participant information sheet and understand the project's purpose and scope.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the questions and provided your informed consent.
