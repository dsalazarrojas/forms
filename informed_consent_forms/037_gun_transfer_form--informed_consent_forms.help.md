# Gun Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gun Transfer Form is used to document the transfer of a gun between two individuals. It captures essential information about the gun owner, the recipient, the transfer details, and any consent or waivers required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Gun Owner's Information**: On the first page, provide the details of the individual transferring the gun, including their name and any other relevant information.
2. **Provide Recipient Information**: On the second page, enter the details of the person receiving the gun, including their name and any other relevant information.
3. **Enter Transfer Details**: On the third page, describe the gun transfer, including the date and any relevant notes.
4. **Consent and Waivers**: On the fourth page, select one of the options for consent and waivers.
5. **Record the Transfer Date**: On the fifth page, enter the date of the gun transfer.
6. **Add a Signature (optional)**: On the last page, you can add a timestamp for your signature (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gun Owner Information**: Enter the name and any other relevant details of the individual transferring the gun.
* **Recipient Information**: Enter the name and any other relevant details of the person receiving the gun.
* **Transfer Details**: Describe the gun transfer, including the date and any other relevant notes.
* **Consent and Waivers**: Select one of the options for consent and waivers.
	+ **Label 1**: Option 1 for consent and waivers.
	+ **Label 2**: Option 2 for consent and waivers.
	+ **Label 3**: Option 3 for consent and waivers.
* **Transfer Date**: Enter the date of the gun transfer.
* **Signature**: Add a timestamp for your signature if required.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.
- If a field is marked as optional, you may omit it if not applicable.
