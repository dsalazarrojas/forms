# Employee Handbook Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge that the employee has read and understands the company handbook. It is essential to sign this form to confirm that the employee is aware of their responsibilities and expectations in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the company handbook.
2. Select the corresponding option for "Do you understand this form is a document that outlines expectations and guidelines of the workplace?".
3. Select "True" or "False" for "Do you agree to comply with the guidelines outlined in the handbook?".
4. In the "Employee Acknowledgement" field, add any comments or notes you want to include.
5. Sign and date the form by filling in the "Supervisor Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Handbooks**: This field is for employees to type in the version of the company handbook they have read.
* **Date Read**: This field is for employees to enter the date they have read and understood the handbook.
* **Do you understand this form is a document that outlines expectations and guidelines of the workplace?**: This field is a multiple-choice question where employees select "I understand and agree to the terms of the Employee Handbook" or "I do not understand and need further clarification".
* **Do you agree to comply with the guidelines outlined in the handbook?**: This field is also a multiple-choice question where employees select "True" or "False".
* **Employee Acknowledgement**: This is a text field where employees can add any comments or notes they want to include.
* **Supervisor Signature**: This field is for the supervisor to sign and date the form.
* **Date Acknowledged**: This field is for the supervisor to enter the date they have acknowledged the employee's understanding of the handbook.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the company handbook thoroughly before filling out this form.
* Select the correct options for "Do you understand this form" and "Do you agree to comply with the guidelines".
* Keep the "Employee Acknowledgement" field for any comments or notes you want to include.
* Sign and date the form in the "Supervisor Signature" field.
* Ensure that the form is completed and signed correctly to avoid any delays or issues.
