# Waiver Of Lien Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waiver Of Lien Form is used by landlords or property managers to release a lien on a rental property when a tenant has vacated the property and the landlord or property manager has no further claims or liens on the property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Renter Name" field, which is required to identify you as the tenant who has vacated the property.
2. Check the box next to "Property or Vehicle" to indicate if you have a lien on a property or a vehicle. Select "Yes" if you have a lien, "No" otherwise.
3. Enter the date you vacated the property in the "Date" field. This date will confirm when you left the property.
4. If you selected "Yes" in the "Property or Vehicle" field, enter the time you vacated the property in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Renter Name** (`renter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your lease agreement.
* **Property or Vehicle** (`property_or_vehicle`, select_one, required): Check this box to indicate if you have a lien on a property or a vehicle. Select "Yes" if you have a lien, "No" otherwise.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you vacated the property. 
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you vacated the property. Only enter this field if you have selected "Yes" in the "Property or Vehicle" field.

## Tips
* Ensure you have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of your lease agreement before filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about what to fill out in any of the fields, please contact your landlord or property manager.
* This form is only for use in cases where you have vacated the property and the landlord or property manager has no further claims or liens on the property.
