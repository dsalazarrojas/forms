# Group Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants in a group therapy setting. It is essential to ensure that participants understand the goals, objectives, and expectations of the group therapy session, as well as their own rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please read each section carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
2. For each question, provide a clear and concise answer.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the therapist.
4. Once you have completed the form, please sign and date it to indicate your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What is group therapy?** (`group_therapy_informed_consent_form`)
* This field is a short text box where you will describe in your own words the concept of group therapy.
* Please provide a brief explanation of what group therapy is and how it will benefit you.
* Example: "Group therapy is a type of therapy where a group of people with similar issues or concerns come together to share their experiences and work through their problems in a supportive environment."

### 2. **What are the goals and objectives of this group therapy?** (`group_therapy_goals`)
* This field is a short text box where you will describe the specific goals and objectives of this group therapy session.
* Please provide a brief explanation of what you hope to achieve or work on during the therapy sessions.
* Example: "The goal of this group therapy is to provide a safe and supportive environment to share my experiences and learn from others, with the objective of improving my self-esteem and confidence."

### 3. **I understand that this group therapy form is a legally binding document** (`informed_consent`)
* This field is a select-one question where you will indicate whether you have read and understood this document.
* Please select the correct answer based on your understanding.
* Example: I have read and understood this document.

### 4. **Do you agree that all sessions will be kept confidential?** (`confidentiality`)
* This field is a select-multiple question where you will indicate your agreement to keep the sessions confidential.
* Please select all that apply.
* Example: Yes, I agree that all sessions will be kept confidential.

### 5. **What date will group therapy start?** (`group_therapy_start_date`)
* This field is a date field where you will provide the start date of the group therapy session.
* Please enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### 6. **When will group therapy end?** (`group_therapy_end_date`)
* This field is a date field where you will provide the end date of the group therapy session.
* Please enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### 7. **What is the name of the therapist?** (`therapist_name`)
* This field is a short text box where you will provide the name of the therapist leading the session.
* Please provide the name of the therapist who will be leading the group therapy sessions.

### 8. **What is the therapist contact information?** (`therapist_contact_information`)
* This field is a short text box where you will provide the contact information of the therapist.
* Please provide the email address, phone number, or other contact information of the therapist.

### 9. **How many participants are expected to join the therapy group?** (`number_of_participants`)
* This field is a short text box where you will provide the expected number of participants in the group therapy session.
* Please enter the number of people expected to join the therapy group.
* Example: 5 people

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the therapist.
* Once you have completed the form, please sign and date it to indicate your understanding and agreement.
