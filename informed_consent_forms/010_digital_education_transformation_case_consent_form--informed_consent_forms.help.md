# digital_education_transformation_case_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital education transformation case consent form is designed to collect information from participants in a digital education transformation case. This form is used to gather demographic data, consent, and other relevant details from participants or their parents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with the necessary information, including your participant information, parent information (if applicable), and signature.
3. Date and sign the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info**: This section is for gathering basic information about the participant, such as any necessary demographic data.
* **Participant Signature**: This is where the participant (or parent/guardian) signs to indicate their consent to participate in the digital education transformation case.
* **Parent Info**: This section is for gathering information from parents or legal guardians, if applicable.
* **Date**: This field is for recording the date on which the consent is given.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Ensure that you have understood the purpose and requirements of the form before completing it.
* If you are a parent or guardian, make sure to sign the form to indicate your consent on behalf of the participant.
* Date and time of consent must match the local time zone of your location.
