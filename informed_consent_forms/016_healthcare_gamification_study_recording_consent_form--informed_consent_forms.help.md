# Healthcare Gamification Study Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect user consent and other relevant information for a healthcare gamification study. It is essential that users understand the purpose and implications of participating in the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **user_info** field with your goals for participating in the study. This field is not required.
2. Answer the **recording_info** field with a brief description of the recording process and data collection methods used in the study. This field is not required.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **consent** field to indicate your willingness to participate in the study and agree to the terms and conditions. This field is required.
4. For the **recording_permissions** field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you agree to the recording and tracking of your data. This field is not required.
5. In the **data_collection** field, enter the number of data points you agree to have collected during the study. This field is not required.
6. In the **tracking** field, enter any additional information about tracking methods used. This field is not required.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **submission_tracking** field to indicate whether you agree to submission tracking. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please enter your goals for participating in the study. This will help us understand your motivations and expectations.
* **recording_info** (`recording_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the recording process and data collection methods used in the study. This will help us understand how your data will be collected and recorded.
* **consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to participate in the study and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes".
* **recording_permissions** (`recording_permissions`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you agree to the recording and tracking of your data, or "No" if you do not.
* **data_collection** (`data_collection`, number, optional): Enter the number of data points you agree to have collected during the study.
* **tracking** (`tracking`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about tracking methods used.
* **submission_tracking** (`submission_tracking`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you agree to submission tracking, or "No" if you do not.
