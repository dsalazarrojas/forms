# Faculty Tech Integration Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from faculty members for technical integration purposes, such as recording and data collection. It is designed to ensure that faculty members are aware of and agree to the terms of the technical integration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Name in the designated field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your Email address in the designated field. This is a required field.
3. Select your Role by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the dropdown menu. This is a required field.
4. Choose whether you consent to Recording or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the dropdown menu. This is a required field.
5. Choose whether you consent to Data collection or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the dropdown menu. This is a required field.
6. If applicable, enter any Interview Details in the designated field.
7. Select the Consent Status by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the dropdown menu. This is a required field.
8. Select the Recording Status by selecting Active or Inactive in the dropdown menu. This is a required field.
9. Select the Interview Status by selecting Active or Inactive in the dropdown menu. This is a required field.
10. Enter any Notes if applicable in the designated field.
11. Enter any additional Notes 2 if applicable in the designated field.
12. Enter any Consent Note if applicable in the designated field.
13. Enter any Recording Note if applicable in the designated field.
14. Enter your Signature in the designated field. This is not a required field.
15. Enter your Signature 2 in the designated field. This is not a required field.
16. Enter the Interview Start Time if applicable in the designated field.
17. Enter the Interview Duration if applicable in the designated field.
18. Enter the Recording Device used during the interview in the designated field.
19. Enter the Recording Quality if applicable in the designated field.
20. Enter the Recording File if applicable in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your university ID or other identification.
*   **Email**: Enter your university email address. This will help us contact you for any further information or clarification.
*   **Role**: Select "Yes" if you are a faculty member and "No" if you are not.
*   **Interview Details**: Enter any additional information about the interview you would like to share.
*   **Recording Consent**: Select "Yes" if you consent to recording the interview and "No" if you do not.
*   **Data Consent**: Select "Yes" if you consent to data collection and "No" if you do not.
*   **Consent Status**: Select "Yes" if you give your consent and "No" if you do not.
*   **Recording Status**: Select "Active" if the recording is active and "Inactive" if it is not.
*   **Interview Status**: Select "Active" if the interview is active and "Inactive" if it is not.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the interview.
*   **Notes 2**: Enter any additional comments or information about the interview.
*   **Consent Note**: Enter any additional comments or information about your consent.
*   **Recording Note**: Enter any additional comments or information about the recording.
*   **Signature**: Sign and date this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the consent form.
*   **Signature 2**: Sign and date this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the consent form.

## Tips
*   This form should not be repeated for the same faculty member within the same academic semester.
*   Please review all fields carefully and make sure to enter accurate information.
