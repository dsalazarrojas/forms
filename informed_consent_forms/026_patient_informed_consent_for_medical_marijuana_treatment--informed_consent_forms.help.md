# patient_informed_consent_for_medical_marijuana_treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing medical marijuana treatment. This form helps patients understand the risks and benefits of treatment, their medical condition, and the potential risks and benefits of medical marijuana.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Ensure that you understand the risks and benefits of medical marijuana treatment.
3. Sign the form to indicate that you have read and understood the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Please provide your basic information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Risks of Treatment** (`risks_of_treatment`, text, optional): List any risks or concerns you have regarding the treatment.
* **Benefits of Treatment** (`benefits_of_treatment`, text, optional): List any benefits you expect from the treatment.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, select_one, optional):
	+ Option 1: I understand the risks and benefits of medical marijuana treatment and am willing to proceed.
	+ Option 2: I do not understand the risks and benefits of medical marijuana treatment and require further explanation.
	+ Option 3: I am not willing to proceed with medical marijuana treatment.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your medical condition.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): List any medical history that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Medication Use** (`medication_use`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Doctor Information** (`doctor_info`, text, optional): Provide the doctor's contact information and medical credentials.
* **Informed Consents** (`informed_consents`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Option 1: I have read and understood the risks and benefits of medical marijuana treatment.
	+ Option 2: I have discussed my medical condition with my doctor and understand the treatment options.
	+ Option 3: I have signed this form to indicate that I am providing informed consent for medical marijuana treatment.
* **Other Informed Consents** (`other_informed_consents`, text, optional): Provide any additional informed consents or agreements you have made with your doctor.
