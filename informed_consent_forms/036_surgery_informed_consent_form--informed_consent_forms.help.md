# Surgery Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing surgery. It outlines the risks and benefits associated with the procedure, as well as the patient's responsibilities and expectations after the surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully and ask questions if you need clarification.
2. Sign the informed consent section to indicate that you understand the information provided.
3. Fill out the patient name field with your full name as it appears on your ID or medical record.
4. Enter the date of the scheduled surgery.
5. Provide the name of the surgeon or healthcare provider performing the surgery.
6. Describe the procedure being performed, including any relevant details.
7. Specify the anesthesia method used, if applicable.
8. Describe your expected recovery process, including post-op recovery room details.
9. Provide any discharge instructions or next steps for your care.
10. Enter any contact information for your emergency contact, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**:
	+ A brief overview of the surgery and its purpose.
	+ Please read this section carefully and ask questions if you need clarification.
* **Informed Consent**:
	+ Check the box to indicate that you understand the information provided.
	+ This section ensures that you have been informed about the risks and benefits associated with the surgery.
* **Signature**:
	+ Please sign your name to confirm your understanding of the information provided.
	+ This is a required field, as it indicates that you have carefully reviewed and understood the information.
* **Patient Name**:
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or medical record.
	+ This is a required field, as it helps identify you as the patient.
* **Date of Surgery**:
	+ Enter the date of the scheduled surgery.
	+ This field helps ensure that we have accurate records of your surgery date.
* **Surgeon**:
	+ Enter the name of the surgeon or healthcare provider performing the surgery.
	+ This field helps us identify the healthcare provider responsible for your care.
* **Procedure**:
	+ Describe the surgery or procedure being performed.
	+ Provide any relevant details, such as the location or duration of the surgery.
* **Anesthesia**:
	+ Specify the type of anesthesia used, if applicable.
	+ This field helps us understand any potential risks or side effects associated with the anesthesia.
* **Post-op Recovery Room**:
	+ Describe your expected recovery process, including any post-op recovery room details.
	+ This field helps us plan for your care and recovery.
* **Discharge Instructions**:
	+ Provide any instructions or next steps for your care after discharge.
	+ This field helps ensure you have a clear understanding of your post-surgery care.
* **Next Steps**:
	+ Provide any additional information about your next appointments or follow-up care.
	+ This field helps us track your progress and provide ongoing care.
* **Patient Contact**:
	+ Enter any contact information for your emergency contact, if applicable.
	+ This field helps us reach out to your emergency contact in case of any issues.
