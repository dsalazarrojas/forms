# Parent Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Authorization Form is designed to collect information from a parent or guardian about their relationship with a child, including their name, contact information, and consent for participation in activities or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Parent Name field with the child's parent or guardian's name.
2. Select the relationship type from the Relationship field using the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the parent or guardian's phone number in the Phone field (if applicable).
4. Enter the parent or guardian's email address in the Email field (if applicable).
5. Enter the parent or guardian's address in the Address field (if applicable).
6. Review the Parent Consent field to select the options for consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the child's parent or guardian.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, required): Choose the type of relationship between the parent and child (e.g., Mother, Father, Grandparent).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's address.
* **Parent Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options to indicate the parent's consent for the child's participation in activities or programs.
