# Amenity Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants before they use the amenities provided by the facility. It ensures that participants are aware of the risks associated with using the facilities and that they are able to provide informed consent before participating.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
2. **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
3. **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, **required**): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
4. **Age** (`Age`, `number`, **required**): Enter your age.
5. **Gender** (`Gender`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your gender from the options provided.
6. **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `note`, **optional**): This is a note section where you can provide any additional contact information you feel is necessary.
7. **Street Address** (`Street Address`, `text`, **required**): Enter your street address.
8. **City** (`City`, `text`, **required**): Enter your city.
9. **State/Province** (`State/Province`, `text`, **required**): Enter your state or province.
10. **ZIP/Postal Code** (`ZIP/Postal Code`, `text`, **required**): Enter your zip or postal code.
11. **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number.
12. **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address.
13. **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `note`, **optional**): This is a note section where you can provide any emergency contact information you feel is necessary.
14. **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
15. **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
16. **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`Relationship to Emergency Contact`, `text`, **required**): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
17. **Amenity Selection** (`Amenity Selection`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all the amenities you plan to use from the options provided.
18. **Specify Other Amenity** (`Specify Other Amenity`, `text`, **optional**): If you selected "Other" in the amenity selection section, describe the other amenity you wish to use.
19. **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `select_one`, **required**): Describe any medical conditions that may affect your use of amenities.
20. **Physical Limitations** (`Physical Limitations`, `select_one`, **required**): Describe any physical limitations that may affect your use of amenities.
21. **Medical Conditions Detail** (`Medical Conditions Detail`, `text`, **optional**): If you answered "yes" to the medical conditions question, please describe your condition in more detail.
22. **Physical Limitations Detail** (`Physical Limitations Detail`, `text`, **optional**): If you answered "yes" to the physical limitations question, please describe your limitation in more detail.
23. **Medications** (`Medications`, `select_one`, **optional**): If you are taking medications, select yes. If not, select no.
24. **Medications Detail** (`Medications Detail`, `text`, **optional**): If you selected "yes" to the medications question, list your medications.
25. **Assumption of Risk** (`Assumption of Risk`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the risk acknowledgment you are comfortable with.
26. **Physical Capability** (`Physical Capability Statement`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your physical capability statement.
27. **Release Statement** (`Release Statement`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the release statement that you agree to.
28. **Indemnification** (`Indemnification Agreement`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the indemification agreement you agree to.
29. **Rules and Regulations** (`Rules and Regulations`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the rules and regulations you agree to follow.
30. **Specific Rules** (`Specific Rules Acknowledged`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all the specific rules you agree to follow.
31. **Medical Consent** (`Medical Consent`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the medical consent you agree to.
32. **Photo and Video Release** (`Photo and Video Release`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the photo and video release you agree to.
33. **Minor Consent** (`Minor Consent (if under 18)`, `select_one`, **optional**): If you are under 18, select yes. If not, select no.
34. **Parent/Guardian Name** (`Parent/Guardian Name`, `text`, **optional**): If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
35. **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`Parent/Guardian Signature`, `text`, **optional**): If you are under 18, enter the signature of your parent or guardian.
36. **Parent/Guardian Date** (`Parent/Guardian Signature Date`, `date`, **optional**): If you are under 18, enter the signature date of your parent or guardian.
37. **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`, `text`, **required**): Enter your signature.
38. **Date of Signature** (`Date of Signature`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of your signature.
39. **Witness Name** (`Witness Name`, `text`, **optional**): If required, enter the name of the witness.
40. **Witness Signature** (`Witness Signature`, `text`, **optional**): If required, enter the signature of the witness.
41. **Waiver Validity** (`Waiver Validity`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the validity of the waiver.
42. **Waiver Start Date** (`Waiver Start Date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the start date of the waiver.

Note: Make sure to review the form carefully and ask for assistance if you are unsure about any part of it.

This guide is for educational purposes only and may need to be adapted to fit your specific facility's needs.
