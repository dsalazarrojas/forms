# Parental Authorization Form For Minors - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for parents or guardians to provide authorization and medical information for minors, ensuring we can provide proper care and services to them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's name in the `minor_name` field. This is the child's full name as it appears on their official identification documents.
2. Enter the child's date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field. This is the child's birth date, which is usually found on their passport or birth certificate.
3. Select the relationship between the parent/guardian and the child from the `relationship` dropdown. This is the parent/guardian's relationship to the child, such as Mother, Father, or Guardian.
4. Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency in the `contact_name` field. This is the name of the person who can be contacted in case of an unexpected event or medical emergency.
5. Enter the email address of the person to contact in case of an emergency in the `contact_email` field. This will be used to send important updates or instructions to.
6. Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency in the `contact_phone` field. This is a phone number where the person can be reached in case of an unexpected event or medical emergency.
7. If the child has a medical history or any relevant health information, enter it in the `medical_history` field. This information will help us provide proper care and services to the child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Minor Name** (`minor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the child's full name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the child's birth date, which is usually found on their passport or birth certificate.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select one, required: false): Select the relationship between the parent/guardian and the child, such as Mother, Father, or Guardian.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who can be contacted in case of an unexpected event or medical emergency.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person who can be contacted in case of an unexpected event or medical emergency.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number where the person can be reached in case of an unexpected event or medical emergency.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): If the child has a medical history or any relevant health information, enter it in this field. This information will help us provide proper care and services to the child.
