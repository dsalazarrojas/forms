# Telemedicine Informed Consent Form California - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for telemedicine services in California. It is a required document for healthcare providers to ensure compliance with state regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the general information section with your name, date of birth, and contact information (phone and email).
2. Provide your patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact information.
3. Enter your provider's information, including your name and contact information.
4. Select the type of telemedicine service you are consenting to (e.g., Telemedicine Informed Consent Form California).
5. Specify the start and end dates of the telemedicine agreement.
6. Sign the form above the patient's and provider's signature lines.
7. Date and time of completion should be filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, **required: false**): Please enter your name, date of birth, and contact information (phone and email) to identify yourself as the patient.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter your patient's name, date of birth, and contact information to help the provider identify the patient.
* **Provider Information** (`provider_info`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter your name and contact information to ensure the provider knows how to reach you.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, **required: false**): Select the type of telemedicine service you are consenting to (e.g., Telemedicine Informed Consent Form California).
* **Telemedicine Agreement Start Date** (`telemedicine_agreement`, `date`, **required: false**): Specify the start date of the telemedicine agreement.
* **Telemedicine Agreement End Date** (`telemedicine_agreement_end_date`, `date`, **required: false**): Specify the end date of the telemedicine agreement.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, **required: false**): Sign above to confirm your consent.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_provider`, `text`, **required: false**): Sign above to confirm your consent.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, **required: false**): Enter the date of completion.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, **required: false**): Enter the time of completion.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, **required: false**): Add any additional comments or notes about the form completion.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required: false**): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter your phone number.
