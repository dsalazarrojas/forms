# Covid 19 Health Screening Visitor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about visitors to a healthcare facility or organization, to take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Fill in the required fields: contact_details, travel_history.
3. Fill in the optional fields as necessary and relevant to your situation.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Enter your name, age, and any other general information about yourself.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact phone number and email address.
* **Screening Information**: Enter any information about your screening process, if applicable.
* **Medical History**: Enter any medical history, if applicable.
* **Travel History**: Enter any travel history, if applicable.
* **Contact Tracing**: Enter any contact tracing information, if applicable.
* **Symptoms**: Enter any symptoms you are experiencing, if applicable.
* **Screening Questions**: Answer the screening questions as accurately as possible, using the provided options.

## Tips

* Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Be aware of the risks associated with COVID-19.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please consult a healthcare professional.
* This form is subject to change as the situation evolves.
* If you have any issues or concerns with the form, please contact: form@gic.mx.
