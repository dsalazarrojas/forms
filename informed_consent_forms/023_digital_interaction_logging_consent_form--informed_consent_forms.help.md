# digital_interaction_logging_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record employee digital interactions and ensure that employees are aware of and agree to the terms of digital interaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID (if provided).
2. Enter your Date of Birth.
3. Select your Department (if you have interacted with the digital system in this context).
4. Provide the Reason for Interaction (e.g., accessing company resources, troubleshooting, etc.).
5. Enter the Method of Interaction (e.g., email, phone, in-person, etc.).
6. Check the box for Monitor Prophets (if applicable).
7. Select the Approval Status (if you have already been approved).
8. Enter the Approved By (if you have been approved).
9. Add any Notes related to the interaction.
10. If you have already signed the form, enter the date and time you signed it.
11. If you are the form completer, enter the date and time you completed the form.
12. Enter the ID of the person who completed the form.
13. Enter the IP address of the device used to access the digital system.
14. Enter the User Agent (if you have access to this information).
15. Enter the User IP address.
16. Enter the User Browser.
17. Enter the Operating System of the device used.
18. Enter the Location (e.g., country, region, city).
19. Enter the User Language.
20. Select the Browser Type (e.g., Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge, etc.).
21. Enter the User OS (if applicable).
22. Enter any additional details in the Reason Signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department you belong to or were interacting with.
* **Reason for Interaction** (`reason_for_interaction`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation of why you interacted with the digital system (e.g., accessing company resources, troubleshooting, etc.).
* **Method of Interaction** (`method_of_interaction`, `text`, required): Specify how you interacted with the digital system (e.g., email, phone, in-person, etc.).
* **Monitor Prophets** (`monitor_prophets`, `text`, optional): Check this box if you have interacted with a monitor propher.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your interaction (Not Yet Approved, Approved).
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who approved your interaction.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to your interaction.
* **Employee Signed** (`employee_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Approver Signed** (`approver_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date the approver signed this form.
* **Reason Signed** (`reason_signed`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation for why you signed this form.
* **Signed At** (`signed_at`, `time`, required): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, `number`, required): Enter the ID of the person who completed this form.
* **Form Completed At** (`form_completed_at`, `time`, required): Enter the date and time you completed the form.
* **Form Completed By** (`form_completed_by`, `number`, required): Enter your ID number.
* **IP Address** (`ip_address`, `text`, required): Enter the IP address of the device used to access this form.
* **User Agent** (`user_agent`, `text`, required): Enter the User Agent string (e.g., Chrome/91.0.4472.164 (Windows 10; Windows NT) AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko) Safari/537).
* **User IP** (`user_ip`, `text`, required): Enter the IP address of your device.
* **User Browser** (`user_browser`, `text`, required): Enter the name and version of the browser used to access the form.
* **Operating System** (`operating_system`, `text`, required): Enter the Operating System version (e.g., Windows 10, macOS High Sierra, etc.).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location (e.g., country, region, city) where you interacted with the digital system.
* **User Language** (`user_language`, `text`, optional): Enter your native language or the language used to interact with the digital system.
* **Browser Type** (`browser_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of browser used (e.g., Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge, etc.).
* **User OS** (`user_os`, `text`, optional): Enter the type of Operating System used.
* **Additional Details** (`reason_signed`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or context about your interaction or signing of the form.
