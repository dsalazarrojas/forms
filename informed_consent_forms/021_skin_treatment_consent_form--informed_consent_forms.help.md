# Skin Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Treatment Consent Form is a form used to collect informed consent from patients prior to skin treatment. It gathers information about the patient's skin type, medical history, and allergies, as well as their signature of consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Enter your name in the "Patient Name" field.
3.  Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4.  Choose your skin type from the "Skin Type" drop-down menu.
5.  In the "Medical Conditions" field, provide any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
6.  If you have any allergies, list them in the "Allergies" field.
7.  Describe any ongoing medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" field.
8.  Share your skin treatment history in the "Skin Treatment History" field.
9.  Describe your medical history, including any previous treatments or surgeries, in the "Medical History" field.
10.  Sign and date your consent in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Skin Type** (`Skin Type`, select_one, optional): Choose your skin type from the drop-down menu. Options include:
	+ Normal
	+ Acne
	+ Eczema
	+ Psoriasis
	+ Dermatitis
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, text, optional): List any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, list them here.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, text, optional): Describe any ongoing medications you are currently taking.
* **Skin Treatment History** (`Skin Treatment History`, text, optional): Share your skin treatment history, including any previous treatments or surgeries.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Describe your medical history, including any previous treatments or surgeries.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required): Sign and date your consent to proceed with treatment.
