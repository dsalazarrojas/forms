# microdermabrasion_aftercare_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the aftercare instructions and precautions for microdermabrasion treatments. Patients or customers will be asked to provide their answers and feedback after undergoing a microdermabrasion treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as "Required" in the form.
3. Select the options that apply to your situation.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your treatment provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **microdermabrasion_aftercare_1_label** (microdermabrasion_aftercare_1, text, required/optional): This field is for providing a brief description of your microdermabrasion experience. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **microdermabrasion_aftercare_2_label** (microdermabrasion_aftercare_2, text, required): This field is to provide information about any discomfort or pain experienced after the treatment.
