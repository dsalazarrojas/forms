# Vaccine Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from patients who wish to waive the vaccine requirement. It is intended for patients who have a medical reason for refusing vaccination and wish to document their decision with a healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Each field is required unless otherwise specified.
2. **Use the "Patient Name" field to enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents**.
3. **Enter your "Date of Birth" in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY)**.
4. **In the "Medical History" field, please describe any medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant health information**.
5. **The "Doctor Signature" field is optional but recommended for patients who are under the age of 18 or have a medical condition that requires additional attention from a healthcare provider**.
6. **The "Patient Signature" field is optional but recommended for patients who are 18 or older and are able to give informed consent**.
7. **Enter the "Date Signed" field in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY)**.
8. **Enter the name of your doctor or healthcare provider in the "Doctor Name" field**.
9. **If you are refusing vaccination, please provide a clear reason in the "Reason for Refusal" field**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **Required**): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, **Optional**): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant health information.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, **Optional**): This field is recommended for patients under 18 or with a medical condition requiring additional attention.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, **Optional**): This field is recommended for patients 18 or older and able to give informed consent.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, **Optional**): Enter the date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, **Optional**): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Reason for Refusal** (`reason_for_refusal`, text, **Optional**): If you are refusing vaccination, please provide a clear reason.
