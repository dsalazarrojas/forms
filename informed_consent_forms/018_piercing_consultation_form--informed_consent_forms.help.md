# Piercing Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piercing Consultation Form is a necessary step in the piercing process. This form helps the practitioner understand your medical history, allergies, and contact information, ensuring a safe and effective piercing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your client information (page 1).
2. Answer the piercing information questions (page 2).
3. Disclose any allergies or medical history (page 3).
4. Give your consent to proceed with the piercing (page 4).
5. Provide emergency contact information (page 5).
6. Choose your contact method (page 6).
7. Select your contact method type (page 7).
8. Provide additional contact information (page 8).
9. Sign the form (page 9).
10. Review and agree to the terms and conditions (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Info (Page 1)
* **Client Info** (client_info): Provide your name, date of birth, and other contact information.
* **Contact Method Info** (contact_method_info): This field is not currently in use. However, you can provide any additional information about your contact method in this field.

### Piercing Info (Page 2)
* **Piercing Info** (piercing_info): Describe the type of piercing you are interested in.
* **Piercing Location** (not available): This field is not currently in the form. However, you can describe the location of the piercing in the **Piercing Info** field.

### Allergy Info (Page 3)
* **Allergy Info** (allergy_info): List any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your piercing experience.

### Medical History (Page 3)
* **Medical History** (medical_history): Describe any medical conditions that may affect your piercing experience.

### Consent (Page 4)
* **Consent** (consent): Please indicate your consent to proceed with the piercing procedure.

### Emergency Contact (Page 5)
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.

### Contact Info (Page 5)
* **Contact Info** (contact_info): Provide contact information for yourself, such as email or phone number.

### Contact Method (Page 6)
* **Contact Method** (contact_method): Choose how you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency or follow-up.

### Contact Method Type (Page 6)
* **Contact Method Type** (contact_method_type): Select the type of contact method you prefer (e.g., phone, email).

### Signature (Page 8)
* **Signature** (signature): This field is reserved for a digital signature, which is used for form completion validation.

### Terms and Conditions (Page 9)
* **Terms and Conditions** (terms_condition): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the piercing.

### Additional Contact Info (Page 10)
* **Contact Info 1 - 13** (contact_info_1 - 13): Provide any additional contact information as required by the practitioner.

Please review and complete this form carefully to ensure that we have all the necessary information for a safe and effective piercing experience.
