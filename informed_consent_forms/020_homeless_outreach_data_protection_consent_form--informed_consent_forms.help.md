# Homeless Outreach Data Protection Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a data protection consent form used to collect information from individuals in need of homeless outreach services. It aims to ensure that the collected data is used responsibly and with the individual's consent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the outreach team that will be working with you from the options provided.
2. Enter your preferred contact name, which can be your actual name or an alias.
3. Enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
4. Provide your address.
5. Choose your preferred method of contact (calls, texts, or emails) for follow-up interactions.
6. Indicate the type of data you consent to use, such as contact information, follow-up data, and demographics.
7. Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact, if applicable.
8. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
9. Provide any medical information you'd like to share, which will help us provide better services.
