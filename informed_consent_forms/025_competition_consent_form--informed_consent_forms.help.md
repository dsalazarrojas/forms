# Competition Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Competition Consent Form is a document that requires competitors to provide informed consent to participate in a competition. This form is a mandatory part of the competition registration process, and it helps ensure that participants are aware of the rules, regulations, and potential risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow the instructions below:

*   Complete each field carefully, making sure to enter accurate and truthful information.
*   If you are a parent or guardian, you will need to provide information about the competitor, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
*   As a competitor, you will need to provide your emergency contact number and any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
*   You will also need to agree to the media release consent, rules acknowledgment, liability waiver, and digital consent acknowledgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Official Competition Name** (`competition_name_official`): Enter the official name of the competition, e.g., National Math Olympiad 2026.
*   **Competitor Full Name** (`competitor_full_name`): Enter the full name of the competitor, including their surname and given name.
*   **Competitor Date of Birth** (`competitor_dob`): Enter the competitor's date of birth in the format `DD/MM/YY`.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name (If Under 18)** (`guardian_full_name`): If the competitor is under 18, enter the parent or guardian's name.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_number`): Enter a phone number where you can be reached during the competition.
*   **Relevant Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`allergies_medical_conditions`): If applicable, list any relevant medical conditions or allergies the competitor may have.
*   **Media Release Consent** (`media_release_consent_comp`): Select `Yes, I consent` if you agree to being photographed or filmed for promotional purposes.
*   **Rules Acknowledgment** (`rules_acknowledgment_comp`): Select `I agree` if you understand and will follow all competition rules and regulations.
*   **Liability Waiver Acknowledgment** (`liability_waiver_acknowledgment`): Select `I acknowledge and accept` if you understand and accept all risks associated with participation.
*   **Date Signed** (`consent_form_date_signed`): The date you are signing this form, automatically populated with today's date.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to read each field carefully before entering your information.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the competition organizers for guidance.
*   Complete each section accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth competition experience. 

This guide is intended to be used by end users to help them complete the Competition Consent Form accurately and efficiently.
