# Performance Lighting Design Documentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants in performance lighting design projects. It captures essential details about the project, such as contributor information, production details, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and contact email to identify yourself as the contributor.
2. Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for any questions or updates.
3. Choose the purpose of the production (e.g., commercial, educational, or personal).
4. If your production purpose is "Other", specify the reason in the text box.
5. Select the type of consent you give for the project (e.g., Option 1 or Other).
6. If you selected "Other" for consent, specify the reason in the text box.
7. Enter the date you give consent for the project.
8. Provide the name of the production company or organization involved.
9. Enter the title of the production, if applicable.
10. Enter the date and time of the event.
11. Add any additional notes about the project or your consent.
12. If applicable, select the category of the form (e.g., Informed Consent Forms).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contributor Name**: Your name, used to identify you as the contributor.
* **Email**: Your email address, used for communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Your phone number, optional for contact purposes.
* **Production Purpose**: The reason for the production, such as a commercial or educational purpose.
* **Other (please specify)**: If "Production Purpose" is set to "Other", provide a brief description of the reason.
* **Consent**: The type of consent you give for the project, such as Option 1 or Other.
* **Other (please specify)**: If "Consent" is set to "Other", provide a brief description of the reason.
* **Date**: The date you give consent for the project.
* **Production Company**: The name of the production company or organization involved.
* **Production Title**: The title of the production, if applicable.
* **Event Date**: The date of the event related to the production.
* **Event Time**: The time of the event, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Additional comments or notes about the project or consent.
* **Tool**: Optional field for specifying the tool or software used in the production.
* **Category**: The category of the form, such as Informed Consent Forms.
