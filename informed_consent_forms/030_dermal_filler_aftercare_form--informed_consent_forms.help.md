# Dermal Filler Aftercare Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for clients to report their experiences and observations after receiving dermal fillers. It will help us track and monitor any potential side effects, ensuring we can provide the best possible care and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client ID number if you have one.
2. Select the date and time of the treatment.
3. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you experienced any side effects or reactions from the dermal filler treatment.
4. Provide the number of fillers you received for the affected area.
5. If you experienced any side effects or reactions, describe them in the text box provided (side_effects and other_side_effects fields).
6. Answer the yes or no questions regarding bruising/swelling, redness/discoloration, and lumps/bumps.
7. Select whether you experienced sensitivity after the treatment.
8. Enter any other side effects you may have experienced in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client ID** (`client_id`, number, optional): This is your unique identifier if you have one. Please enter it if you do have it.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you received the dermal filler treatment.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you received the dermal filler treatment.
- **Filler Type** (`filler_type`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' to indicate whether you received dermal fillers.
- **Area Treated** (`area_treated`, select_multiple, optional): Select the area(s) where you received dermal fillers (e.g., lips, cheeks, etc.).
- **Number of Fillers** (`num_fillers`, number, optional): Enter the number of fillers you received.
- **Side Effects** (`side_effects`, note, optional): Describe any side effects or reactions you experienced after the treatment.
- **Bruising/Swelling** (`bruising_swelling`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' for any areas that experienced bruising or swelling.
- **Redness/Discoloration** (`redness_discoloration`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' for any areas that experienced redness or discoloration.
- **Lumps/Bumps** (`lumps_bumps`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' for any areas that experienced lumps or bumps.
- **Sensitivity** (`sensitivity`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' for any areas that experienced sensitivity after the treatment.
- **Other Side Effects** (`other_side_effects`, note, optional): Describe any other side effects you experienced after the treatment.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have about your treatment.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified as required. If you have any questions or concerns about your treatment, feel free to contact us.
