# rdv_peds_authorization_for_medical_treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rdv_peds_authorization_for_medical_treatment form is used to collect information for medical treatment authorization in the context of a patient's medical condition. This form is required to be completed for each patient.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please navigate through each page and fill in the required information for each field. The form has multiple pages with various fields that need to be completed. Start from the first page and move through the form step by step.
- The form is divided into several sections, each with a specific purpose. Please make sure to fill in the required information for each field to ensure accurate and complete data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical Treatment Form** (`medical_treatment_form`, text, required): This page is where you will provide a brief overview of the patient's medical treatment.
  - Enter a short description of the medical treatment that the patient requires.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Please enter the name of the medical condition that the patient is being treated for.
- **Medical Condition Details** (`medical_condition_details`, text, required): Provide more information about the patient's medical condition.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the person responsible for the patient's care.
  - Ensure that the contact person is aware of the patient's medical condition and can provide assistance if necessary.
- **Medical Treatment Authorization** (`medical_treatment_authorization`, select_one, true/false, required): Indicate whether or not the patient is authorized to receive medical treatment for their condition.
- **Authorized Person** (`authorized_person`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person who authorized the medical treatment.
  - This should be the name and title of the person who gave approval for the treatment.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required): This is a note field where you can add any additional information about the patient's medical history.
  - You can use this field to provide any relevant medical history that may impact the treatment.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required information is included.
