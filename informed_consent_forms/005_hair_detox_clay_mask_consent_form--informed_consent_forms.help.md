# Hair Detox Clay Mask Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from clients for a hair detox clay mask treatment. It is meant to ensure that clients understand the treatment and its implications before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Details section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the Health Information section, provide any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities that may be relevant to the treatment.
3. In the Treatment Details section, describe the treatment you are consenting to, including the type of hair detox clay mask, the duration, and any other relevant details.
4. In the Client Agreement section, acknowledge that you understand the treatment and its implications, and confirm that you are consenting to it.
5. Finally, sign the Signature section with your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details to help us get in touch with you.
* **Health Information**: If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities that may affect the treatment, please list them here.
* **E-Signature**: This is where you will sign this form electronically, confirming that you have read and understand the treatment and its implications.
* **Treatment Details**: Describe the hair detox clay mask treatment you are consenting to, including the type, duration, and any other relevant details.
* **Client Agreement**: Acknowledge that you understand the treatment and its implications, and confirm that you are consenting to it.
* **Treatment Options**: Select "Yes" if you are consenting to the treatment, or "No" if you are not.
* **Hair Type**: Select "Yes" if you have a specific hair type that is relevant to the treatment, or "No" if you do not.
* **Signature**: This is where you will sign this form electronically, confirming that you have read and understand the treatment and its implications.
