# Smart Appliance Activation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Appliance Activation Consent Form is a template designed to collect necessary permissions and information from customers before activating their smart appliances. This form helps ensure a smooth setup experience and informs users about the activation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the appropriate options for each field based on your preferences and needs.
2. Choose from the options provided for each field, such as "Authorized" or "Not Authorized" for Activation Details, "Installation Date" or "Installation Time" for Scheduling Preferences, and "Customer Address" or "Customer Phone" for Customer Info.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
4. Review your selections and submit the form to complete the activation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Activation Details** (`Activation Details`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose "Authorized" if you agree to activate the smart appliance, or "Not Authorized" if you do not wish to proceed with activation.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`Scheduling Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Installation Date" if you want to schedule the installation, or "Installation Time" if you want to schedule the installation time.
* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose "Customer Address" to provide your home address, or "Customer Phone" to provide your contact phone number.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your smart appliance activation.
