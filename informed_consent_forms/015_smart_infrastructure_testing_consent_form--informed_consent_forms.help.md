# Smart Infrastructure Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Infrastructure Testing Consent Form is used to collect informed consent from participants in testing projects. This form helps ensure that participants understand the nature of the testing and agree to participate in it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are participating in.
2. **Participant Information**: Select your type of participant (Participant, Contractor, or Observer).
3. **Test Location**: If you are a participant, enter the location where the testing will take place.
4. **Testing Dates**: Enter the dates on which you will be participating in the testing.
5. **Testing Times**: Enter the times you will be participating in the testing.
6. **Participant Consent**: Check all that apply regarding your participation.
7. **Observer Consent**: Check if you are an observer and consent to participate.
8. **Participant Contract**: Select the agreement that applies to you.
9. **Participant Agreement Signed**: Enter the date you signed the participant agreement.
10. **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
11. **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
12. **Test Location Map**: (Optional) Upload a map of the test location.
13. **Participant Signature**: Sign this form to confirm your participation.
14. **Participant Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are participating in. This will help us identify the project for which you are giving consent.
* **Participant Information**: Select your type of participant: Participant (you are participating in the testing), Contractor (you are conducting the testing), or Observer (you are observing the testing but not participating).
* **Test Location**: If you are a participant, enter the location where the testing will take place. This is where the testing will occur.
* **Testing Dates**: Enter the dates on which you will be participating in the testing.
* **Testing Times**: Enter the times you will be participating in the testing.
* **Participant Consent**: Check all that apply regarding your participation. This includes indicating if you are a participant in this testing project and if you are not a participant.
* **Observer Consent**: Check the box if you are an observer and consent to participate. If you are not an observer, leave this field blank.
* **Participant Contract**: Select the agreement that applies to you. This ensures you understand the terms of your participation.
* **Participant Agreement Signed**: Enter the date you signed the participant agreement. This confirms that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Test Location Map**: (Optional) Upload a map of the test location to help us identify where the testing will take place.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign this form to confirm your participation. This is a mandatory field to ensure you are committed to participating.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a participant.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address.
* **Participant Postal Code**: Enter your postal code (zip code).
* **Participant City**: Enter your city.
* **Participant Country**: Enter your country.
* **Participant ZIP Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Participant Language**: Enter the language you prefer to communicate.
* **Participant Time Zone**: Enter your time zone.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Participant Gender**: Select your gender.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure you complete it accurately to avoid any issues or misunderstandings during the testing process.
