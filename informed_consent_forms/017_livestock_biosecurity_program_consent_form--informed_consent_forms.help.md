## Purpose
The Livestock Biosecurity Program Consent Form is used to gather participant consent and collect information about livestock and farm details. This form is part of the Livestock Biosecurity Program, which aims to promote biosecurity best practices among livestock farmers and ranchers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details in the Participant Details section.
2. Provide information about the animals you are participating with in the Animal Information section.
3. Indicate your consent to participate in the Participant Consent section by selecting either "yes" or "no".
4. Add any additional farm details in the Farm Details section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This section is where you should provide your name, contact information (e.g., email address and phone number), and other relevant participant details. 
* **Animal Information**: Please list the type, number, and breed of the animals you are participating with. 
* **Participant Consent**: This question is to confirm whether you have read and understand the program's terms and conditions. Select "yes" if you have read and agree to the terms, "no" if you have not. 
* **Farm Details**: This section is for you to provide any additional information about your farm, such as the farm name, location, and contact information.
