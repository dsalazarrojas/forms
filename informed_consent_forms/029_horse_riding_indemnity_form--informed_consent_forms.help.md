# Horse Riding Indemnity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Horse Riding Indemnity Form is a critical document that outlines the terms and conditions of horse riding activities. It's essential to understand the potential risks involved and take responsibility for one's actions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Review each section carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Horse Riding Indemnity Form (Page 1)** (`title`, text, not required): This page is an introduction to the form and explains its purpose.
*   **Rider Information (Page 2)** (`rider_info`, text, required): Please provide your full name, address, and contact information.
*   **Waiver (Page 3)** (`waiver`, text, required): You must acknowledge that horse riding involves risks and that you're aware of the potential consequences. Please sign below to confirm your understanding.
*   **Horse Information (Page 4)** (`horse_info`, text, required): Describe the horse you're riding, including its breed, age, and any health issues.
*   **Emergency Contact (Page 5)** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the contact information for a responsible adult who will be notified in case of an emergency.
*   **Signature (Page 6)** (`signature`, text, required): Sign this document to confirm that you've read and understood the waiver.
*   **Date (Page 7)** (`signature_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you're signing this document.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
*   Review each section carefully before submitting.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
*   Remember, this form is a legal document that protects both you and the horse.
