# Chiropractic Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal document that ensures you understand the nature of chiropractic care, potential risks, and benefits before starting treatment. Completing this form demonstrates your informed consent and gives us the authority to perform the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Answer all questions honestly.
- Sign and date the form to indicate your understanding and consent.
- If you are under 18, obtain a parent or guardian's signature.
- If a witness is required, ask them to sign below.
- Review and understand each question carefully before answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)**: Please provide identification details to help us tailor your treatment.
	+ **Patient First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
	+ **Patient Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
	+ **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
	+ **Date of Treatment**: Enter the date you plan to start treatment.
* **Understanding Chiropractic Care (page 2)**: Please confirm you understand the basics of chiropractic care.
	+ **I understand that chiropractic treatment involves manual adjustments**: Answer "Yes, I understand" if you've read and comprehend the explanation.
	+ **The purpose of chiropractic care is to improve spinal function and health**: Answer "Yes, I understand" if you've read and comprehend the explanation.
* **Procedures and Consent (page 3-4)**: List the procedures that involve adjustments.
	+ **Procedures Consent (field 9)**: Please list any procedures that involve chiropractic techniques you've agreed to.
	+ **Consent to Adjustments (field 10)**: Confirm you've agreed to spinal adjustments.
	+ **Consent to Joint Manipulation (field 11)**: Confirm you've agreed to joint manipulation.
	+ **Consent to Soft Tissue Therapy (field 12)**: Confirm you've agreed to soft tissue therapy.
	+ **Consent to Therapeutic Modalities (field 13)**: Confirm you've agreed to therapeutic modalities.
	+ **Consent to X-rays (field 14)**: Confirm you've agreed to X-rays if clinically indicated.
* **Risks Acknowledgment (page 5)**: Please understand potential risks.
	+ **I understand that all healthcare treatments carry risks**: Answer "Yes, I understand" if you've read and comprehend the explanation.
	+ **The potential risks have been explained to me (field 17)**: Confirm you've received explanations.
	+ **I understand common risks include temporary soreness, stiffness, or mild discomfort**: Answer "Yes, I understand" if you've read and comprehend the explanation.
	+ **I understand rare but serious risks may occur (field 19)**: Confirm you've read and comprehend the explanation.
* **Benefits Acknowledgment (page 6)**: Please confirm you understand the benefits.
	+ **The potential benefits have been explained to me (field 22)**: Confirm you've received explanations.
* **Patient Rights (page 7)**: Please confirm you understand your rights.
	+ **I understand I have the right to refuse any treatment at any time (field 28)**: Answer "Yes, I understand" if you've read and comprehend the explanation.
	+ **I understand I can stop treatment at any time without penalty (field 29)**: Confirm you've read and comprehend the explanation.
* **Questions and Opportunities (page 8)**: Take the chance to ask questions.
	+ **I have had the opportunity to ask questions (field 31)**: Confirm you've had the chance to ask questions.
	+ **All my questions have been answered (field 32)**: Confirm you've received answers.
* **Voluntary Consent (page 9)**: Confirm your consent is voluntary.
	+ **My consent is given voluntarily without coercion (field 34)**: Answer "Yes, I understand" if you've read and comprehend the explanation.
* **Form Completion (page 10)**: Please sign and date the form.
	+ **Patient Signature (field 36)**: Sign your name to confirm you've read and understand the form.
	+ **Date of Signature (field 37)**: Enter the date you sign the form.
	+ **Doctor Signature (field 39)**: Sign your name as the doctor to confirm you've explained the form.
	+ **Doctor Signature Date (field 40)**: Enter the date you sign the form.

## Tips

- Read each field carefully before answering.
- Ask questions if you are unsure about any part of the form.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding.
- If you need help or clarification, ask your healthcare provider.
- Keep a copy of the form for your records.
