# Makeup Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Intake Form is designed to collect information from clients for makeup services. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are gathered for providing the best possible service to the client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields with the most accurate information possible.
2. If a field is not required, you may leave it blank.
3. For fields that allow text input, provide a clear and concise description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Makeup Date** (`makeup_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the makeup service.
* **Service Name** (`service_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the service provided.
* **Makeup Details** (`makeup_details`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional details about the service, such as the type of makeup or specific requests.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, Note, Optional): Enter any special instructions or requests from the client.
* **Makeup Intake Form ID** (`makeup_intake_form_id`, Text, Required): Enter a unique identifier for the intake form.
* **Assigned Artist** (`assigned_artist`, Select One, Required): Choose whether the artist is assigned or not.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Select One, Required): Choose the tool assigned to the artist.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check your input for accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification before submitting the form.
- Remember to keep confidential any client information you gather with this form.
