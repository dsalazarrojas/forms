# Professional Counseling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients before starting professional counseling services. It is a legal document that ensures both the client and the counselor understand the client's consent and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information (email, phone number, address).
2. Select your consent options (Client Information, Informed Consent Information, Risks And Limitations, Confidentiality).
3. Provide your date of birth and gender.
4. Disclose your insurance status and details.
5. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (Client Information, text, optional): Please fill in your contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
* **Consent** (Select multiple, required): Select all options that apply:
	+ Client Information
	+ Informed Consent Information
	+ Risks And Limitations
	+ Confidentiality
* **Client Information Date** (Date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Risks Description** (Text, optional): Describe any risks or limitations you would like to discuss with your counselor.
* **Limitations Description** (Text, optional): Describe any limitations you would like to discuss with your counselor.
* **Confidentiality** (Select one, required): Choose whether you want confidentiality with your counselor.
* **Signature** (Text, optional): Please sign below to confirm your consent.
* **Client Signature** (Text, optional): Please sign below to confirm your consent.
* **Counselor Signature** (Text, optional): The counselor will sign below to confirm they have informed you of the risks and limitations.
* **Date of Service** (Date, optional): Enter the date of your first session.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Gender** (Select one, required): Choose your gender.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Text, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance** (Select one, required): Choose whether you have insurance coverage.
* **Insurance Number** (Text, optional): Enter your insurance ID number.
* **Insurance Provider** (Text, optional): Enter your insurance provider's name.
* **Insurance Group Number** (Text, optional): Enter your insurance group number.
* **Billing Code** (Select one, required): Choose your billing code.
* **Billing Comments** (Text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your billing.
