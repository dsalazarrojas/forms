# telehealth_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **telehealth_informed_consent_form** is an essential document that ensures patients and healthcare providers have a clear understanding of the telehealth services they are receiving.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for **user_info** from the provided options.
2. Enter the name of the **telehealth_provider_name** in the text field.
3. Enter the **patient_signature_date** in the date field.
4. Enter the **provider_signature_date** in the date field.
5. Sign your **patient_signature** in the text field.
6. Sign your **provider_signature** in the text field.
7. Select the answer for **patient_consent** from the provided options.
8. Select the answer for **provider_consent** from the provided options.
9. Enter the **date_of_last_contact** in the date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info**: Please select the correct answer to indicate if you have read and understood the telehealth services provided. 
* **telehealth_provider_name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider delivering the telehealth services.
* **patient_signature_date**: Enter the date you signed this consent form.
* **provider_signature_date**: Enter the date the healthcare provider signed this consent form.
* **patient_signature**: Please sign this form to confirm you have read and understood the telehealth services provided.
* **provider_signature**: Please sign this form to confirm they have read and will provide care as described.
* **patient_consent**: Select yes if you have read, understood, and agree to the telehealth services provided. Select no if not.
* **provider_consent**: Select yes if you have read, understood, and agree to provide care as described. Select no if not.
* **date_of_last_contact**: Enter the date of the last contact with the healthcare provider.

## Tips
- Please ensure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Make sure to read and understand the telehealth services provided before completing this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the healthcare provider.
