# Case Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from participants before they begin a case study. This form gathers essential information about the study, its purpose, procedures, and potential risks and benefits associated with participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2.  Complete all required fields to ensure that your consent is informed and voluntary.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your researcher or principal investigator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Case Study Title** (`study_title`, required): This field is the title of the case study. Please enter a brief and descriptive title that summarizes the study's purpose.
*   **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, required): Enter the date you are providing your consent for the case study. Use the MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Researcher or Principal Investigator Name** (`researcher_name`, required): This field is the name of the researcher or principal investigator conducting the case study. Please enter their name as it appears on their business card or contact information.
*   **Research Institution or University** (`research_institution`, required): Enter the name of the research institution or university conducting the case study.
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, required): This field is your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, required): Enter your date of birth using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Participant ID Number** (`participant_id_number`, optional): If you have a participant ID number, please enter it for record-keeping purposes. This is not required but helps the researchers keep track of participants.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for questions or updates.
*   **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, optional): If you have a contact email address, please enter it so that the researchers can reach you with updates or questions.
*   **Purpose of the Case Study** (`study_purpose`, required): This field is a brief description of the case study's purpose. Please explain it in simple, non-technical terms.
*   **What Will Be Involved in the Study?** (`study_procedures`, required): Describe the procedures and time commitment involved in the study. Be as detailed as possible.
*   **Expected Duration of Study Participation** (`study_duration`, required): Enter the expected duration of your participation in the study.
*   **Data Collection Methods** (`data_collection_methods`, required): Select all data collection methods that will be used in the study (e.g., Interviews, Surveys, Medical Records Review, Psychological Testing, Observation, Focus Groups, Questionnaires, Other).
*   **Potential Risks of Participation** (`potential_risks`, required): Describe any potential risks associated with participation in the study.
*   **Potential Benefits of Participation** (`potential_benefits`, optional): Describe any direct or indirect benefits associated with participation in the study.
*   **How Will Confidentiality Be Protected?** (`confidentiality_measures`, required): Describe the measures taken to protect confidentiality and participant privacy.
*   **Will Information Be De-Identified?** (`de_identification`, required): Select how the information will be de-identified (Yes, completely de-identified, Yes, coded only, No, identifiable information will be used, Undecided).
*   **How Will Results Be Used or Published?** (`publication_use`, required): Describe how the results will be used or published.
*   **I Voluntarily Consent to Participate in This Case Study** (`voluntary_agreement`, required): Select "Yes, I agree" to indicate that you voluntarily consent to participate in the case study.

## Tips

*   Please review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your researcher or principal investigator.
*   Complete all required fields to ensure that your consent is informed and voluntary.
