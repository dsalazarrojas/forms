**virtual doulas consent form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather informed consent from clients for virtual doulas to provide postpartum support and care. The form is designed to ensure that clients are aware of the services offered, terms and conditions, and payment details, and that they provide their consent for the doulas to provide postpartum support. This form is typically used to onboard clients and gather necessary information for the doulas to provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the General Information page to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. Provide your contact details and doula contact details on the corresponding pages.
3. Choose your preferred payment method and enter the required payment details.
4. Review and agree to the Payment Terms and Conditions.
5. Sign and confirm your agreement with the doulas.
