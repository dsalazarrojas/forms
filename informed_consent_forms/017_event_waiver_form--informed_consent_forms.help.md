# Event Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Waiver Form is a necessary document for participants engaging in potentially hazardous events to ensure their safety and well-being. By completing this form, participants provide essential information and agree to the risks associated with the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, ask the event organizer or representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **event_waiver_form_1** (`event_waiver_form_1_label`, text, not required)
This field is a general event waiver form section. You may write any comments, notes, or information relevant to the event.

### 2. **event_waiver_form_2** (`event_waiver_form_2_label`, text, not required)
This field is another general event waiver form section. You may write any comments, notes, or information relevant to the event.

### 3. **participant_name** (`participant_name_label`, text, not required)
Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.

### 4. **participant_email** (`participant_email_label`, email, not required)
Enter your email address. This will be used for event communication and updates.

### 5. **participant_phone** (`participant_phone_label`, text, not required)
Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.

### 6. **participant_age** (`participant_age_label`, number, not required)
Enter your age.

### 7. **participant_agreement** (`participant_agreement_label`, note, not required)
Check the box to acknowledge you have read and understood the participant information and liability release form.

### 8. **participant_waiver** (`participant_waiver_label`, select_multiple, not required)
Check the box to indicate you are granting the waiver. The options are "Yes" or "No".

### 9. **event_description** (`event_description_label`, text, not required)
Enter a brief description of the event or activity you will be participating in.

### 10. **risk_list** (`risk_list_label`, select_multiple, not required)
Check the box to list any risks you acknowledge or are aware of. The options are "Yes" or "No".

### 11. **event_waiver_signature** (`event_waiver_signature_label`, select_multiple, not required)
Check the box to indicate your signature. The options are "Yes" or "No".
