# Permanent Makeup Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Release Form is a document that requires clients to provide their informed consent for permanent makeup services. It ensures that clients are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of these services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and contact email and phone number.
2. Indicate whether you consent to photos being taken of you during the service.
3. If you consent to photos, please describe the photos you are consenting to (e.g., "yes" for all photos, "only during the service," etc.).
4. If you do not consent to photos, please specify a start and end date for when you wish to be photographed.
5. Describe the permanent makeup service you are receiving.
6. Indicate whether the service is for "yes" or "no".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Photo Release**: Indicate whether you consent to photos being taken of you during the service. If you choose "yes," no further action is required. If you choose "no," please proceed to the next field.
* **Describe The Photos**: If you chose "no" to photos in the previous field, please describe the type of photos you are consenting to or specify a start and end date for when you wish to be photographed.
* **Photo Release Start Date**: If you chose "no" to photos, this field is used to specify the start date when you wish to be photographed.
* **Photo Release End Date**: If you chose "no" to photos, this field is used to specify the end date when you wish to be photographed.
* **Photo Release Location**: This field is not used in this form as it is optional.
* **Permanent Makeup Service Description**: Describe the permanent makeup service you are receiving.
* **Permanent Makeup Service Type**: Indicate whether the service is for "yes" or "no".
* **Photo Release Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments regarding photo release.
