# pilates_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from participants before they begin a Pilates program. The form is intended to gather essential information about the participant's physical condition, medical conditions, emergency contact, and consent date. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure you understand what is being asked and why.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by providing your participant information, including any necessary details.
* Next, assess your physical condition by selecting one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name and any relevant details.
* List any medical conditions you have, such as High Blood Pressure or Diabetes, or select "Other (Please specify)" if you have a condition not listed.
* Write a brief medical history in the provided space.
* If applicable, provide information about your insurance provider.
* If necessary, provide instructions for your emergency contact.
* Finally, select a date for your consent, and write your instructor's consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info**: A text field for providing participant information.
*  **Participant Physical Condition**: Assess your physical condition using one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
*  **Emergency Contact**: A text field for providing emergency contact information.
*  **Medical Conditions**: A multiple-choice field for listing any medical conditions you have, such as High Blood Pressure or Diabetes.
*  **Medical History**: A text field for writing a brief medical history.
*  **Insurance Provider**: A text field for providing insurance information, but this is optional.
*  **Emergency Instructions**: A text field for providing emergency instructions, but this is optional.
*  **Consent Date**: A date field for selecting the date of your consent.
*  **Instructor Consent**: A text field for writing your instructor's consent.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, consult with your instructor or healthcare professional.
* This form is for your protection and the protection of your fellow participants.
* Please review and sign the form before the start of the program.
