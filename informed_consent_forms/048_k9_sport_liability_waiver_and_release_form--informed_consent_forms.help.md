# K9 Sport Liability Waiver And Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The K9 Sport Liability Waiver and Release Form is a document that requires participants to acknowledge and accept the risks associated with participating in K9 sports activities. It's essential to fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you plan to participate in the event.
2. Provide the name of the event you're participating in.
3. Enter the location of the event.
4. Enter the start time of the event.
5. Provide your name, your parent's name, and your parent's phone number (if you're a minor).
6. Enter your date of birth.
7. If you have any medical conditions, procedures, or insurance, please provide this information.
8. Choose your event participation level (Novice, Intermediate, or Advanced).
9. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
10. Acknowledge that you've read and understood the waiver, emergency release, and liability terms.
11. Confirm that you'll follow the rules of the event.
