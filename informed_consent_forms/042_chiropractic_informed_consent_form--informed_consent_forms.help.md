## Purpose
The Chiropractic Informed Consent Form helps chiropractors get patients' agreements and signatures for treatment. It's a standard practice in healthcare settings to ensure patients understand what they're getting themselves into and that the doctor is aware of their concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Write your full name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Enter your date of birth for verification purposes, if required.
3.  Provide a brief explanation of the procedure in the "Procedure Explanation" field.
4.  Summarize the potential risks and benefits of the treatment in the "Risks and Benefits" field.
5.  Select one of the two options for "Consent Given". Choose "I consent" to agree to the procedure or "I do not consent" to decline it.
6.  Sign with your typed name on the "Name for Signature" field.
7.  Fill in the date of your consent in the "Signature Date" field.
8.  If you have any questions or concerns, type them in the "Additional Questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): For identification purposes only, your date of birth for verification.
*   **Explanation of Procedure** (`Procedure Explanation`, note, optional): A brief summary of what the treatment involves. This helps you understand what to expect from the procedure.
*   **Risks and Benefits** (`Risks and Benefits`, note, optional): A list of common risks and expected benefits associated with the treatment. This will help you make an informed decision.
*   **Consent Given** (`Consent Given`, select_one, required): Choose "I consent" to agree to the treatment or "I do not consent" to decline it.
*   **Name for Signature** (`Signature Name`, text, required): Type your name to sign off on the consent.
*   **Signature Date** (`Signature Date`, date, required): Fill in the date of your consent.
*   **Additional Questions** (`Additional Questions`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, type them here for the doctor to address.

## Tips
*   Always read the form carefully and ask questions if you're unsure about anything.
*   Make sure to understand the risks and benefits before making a decision.
*   If you don't consent, discuss your reasons with the doctor for further guidance.
*   Remember, your signature authorizes the treatment, so make sure you understand what you're signing.
