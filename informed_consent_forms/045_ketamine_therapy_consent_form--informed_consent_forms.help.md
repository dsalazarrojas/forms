# Ketamine Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from patients about their medical history, allergies, and other relevant health topics before undergoing ketamine therapy. This form is intended to help medical professionals assess the patient's suitability for the therapy and ensure their safety during the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Answer each question carefully and accurately**. Your responses will be reviewed by medical professionals to help you receive safe and effective treatment.
2. **Complete all required fields**. While some fields may be optional, it is crucial to fill out all fields to ensure that medical professionals have a complete understanding of your health history.
3. **Be honest and thorough** in your responses. If you are unsure about any information, please consult your healthcare provider or ask a medical professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: Please provide your basic contact information and medical identification details.
* **Medical History**: Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or hospitalizations that may impact your treatment.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any current or past medical conditions that may affect your treatment, such as allergies, asthma, or diabetes.
* **Allergies**: Specify any allergies you have, including medications or substances that may cause adverse reactions.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Therapy Info**: Provide details about your treatment goals, expectations, and any questions or concerns.
* **Risks and Benefits**: Carefully consider the potential risks and benefits associated with ketamine therapy. Discuss any questions or concerns with your healthcare provider.
* **Signature**: Sign and date this form to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms outlined in this form. Your signature confirms that you are aware of the risks and benefits associated with the treatment and are willing to participate in the therapy program.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider or a medical professional for guidance.
* Please review and sign the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information.
* If you have any medical emergencies or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider immediately.
