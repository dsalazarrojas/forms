## Dental Implant Consent Form - Help Guide
### Purpose

This form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients for dental implant procedures. It gathers essential information about the patient, dentist, procedure details, and implant specifics to ensure that the patient is fully aware of the treatment and its implications. 

Please note that this form is a general template and might not cover all the specifics of your practice. You should adapt it according to your local regulations and practice standards.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's basic details such as name, address, and contact information.
2. **Dentist Information**: Provide details about the dentist performing the procedure.
3. **Procedure Information**: Enter the specifics of the dental implant procedure, including the type, location, and material of the implant.
4. **Consent Date**: Fill in the date on which the patient provided consent for the procedure.
5. **Implant Type**: Select the type of dental implant the patient will receive, such as CERAMIC, TITAN, or other (for other types of implants not listed).
6. **Location**: Enter the location of the implant in the patient's mouth.
7. **Implant Material**: Choose the material of the implant, such as TITAN or other.
8. **Implant Size**: Select the size of the implant, such as 4x6, 6x6, or other.
9. **Implant Surface**: Choose the surface of the implant, such as SMOOTH, MICRO textured, or other.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (id: 1)**: Enter the patient's full name, address, and contact information (name, email, and phone number).
* **Dentist Information (id: 2)**: Enter your name, contact information (email and phone number), and professional credentials (license number).
* **Procedure Information (id: 3)**: Describe the specifics of the procedure, including the area of the mouth where the implant will be placed.
* **Consent Date (id: 4)**: Fill in the date on which the patient provided consent for the procedure.
* **Implant Type (id: 5)**: Select the type of dental implant the patient will receive, such as CERAMIC or TITAN.
* **Location (id: 6)**: Enter the specific location of the implant in the patient's mouth, such as the tooth or jaw.
* **Implant Material (id: 7)**: Choose the material of the implant, such as TITAN or other.
* **Implant Size (id: 8)**: Select the size of the implant, such as 4x6 or 6x6.
* **Implant Surface (id: 9)**: Choose the surface of the implant, such as SMOOTH or MICRO textured.

## Tips

Please review each field carefully and make sure to provide accurate information. This form is a crucial document that ensures the patient's informed consent and your liability as the dentist performing the procedure.
