# Release Of Information Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Release Of Information Template" form is used for collecting sensitive information about individuals, including their personal details, medical conditions, and insurance providers. This form is often used for administrative and medical purposes.
 
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by a "required" label) accurately and truthfully.
2. Enter the necessary details in each field, including:
	* First name and Last name
	* Date of birth
	* SSN
	* Address
	* City
	* State
	* Zip
	* Phone number
	* Email
	* Emergency contact name, city, state, and zip (if applicable)
	* Emergency contact phone number and email (if applicable)
	* Insurance provider name, address, city, state, and zip (if applicable)
	* Insurance provider phone number and email (if applicable)
3. Select the correct insurance plan from the provided options.
4. Sign the "release of info" field, indicating that you are releasing the information provided.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **SSN**: Enter your social security number for identification purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address where you can be reached for any further information or communication.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact city**: Enter the city of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact state**: Enter the state of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact zip**: Enter the zip code of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Insurance provider name**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance provider address**: Enter the address of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance provider city**: Enter the city of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance provider state**: Enter the state of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance provider zip**: Enter the zip code of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance provider phone**: Enter the phone number of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance provider email**: Enter the email of your insurance provider.
* **Medical condition**: Enter any medical conditions you have that you would like to disclose.
* **Insurance plan**: Select your insurance plan from the provided options: Medicare, Medicaid, Private Insurance, or None.
* **Release of info**: Sign this field to indicate that you are releasing the information provided.
* **Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that you are the individual providing the information.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the organization or person collecting this information for assistance.
* Review your organization's data protection policies, such as HIPAA rules, for guidance on sensitive data collection and storage.
