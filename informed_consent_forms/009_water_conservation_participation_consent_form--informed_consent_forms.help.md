# Water Conservation Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Water Conservation Participation Consent Form is a document that participants must complete to indicate their agreement to participate in water conservation programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. On the first page, provide your details as a participant.
2. On the second page, select the conservation programs you are interested in participating in.
3. On the third page, read and agree to the participant agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (`participant_details`, text, required):*
	+ This field is where you provide your personal details. Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Conservation Programs (`conservation_programs`, select multiple, not required):**
	+ This field is where you select the conservation programs you are interested in participating in. You can choose one or more from the available options.
* **Participant Agreement (`participant_agreement`, text, not required):**
	+ This field is where you read and agree to the participant agreement. Please ensure you understand the terms before agreeing.
