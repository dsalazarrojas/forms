# hydrafacial_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hydrafacial_consent_form is a document used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing a hydrafacial treatment. It is a crucial step in the treatment process, as it ensures that the patient understands the risks and benefits associated with the treatment and agrees to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and understand each field carefully before filling out the form.
2. Fill in your personal information in the "Patient Information" section.
3. Provide details about the treatment in the "Treatment Information" section.
4. Date and time of the consent process in the "Consent Date" and "Consent Time" fields.
5. Include your signature in the "Patient Signature" field.
6. Date and time of the treatment in the "Date of Service" and "Time of Service" fields.
7. Add any applicable contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
8. Check the "Hydrafacial Contraindications" field if any contraindications apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (Patient Information): This field is used to collect personal details about the patient, such as their name and contact information.
* **Treatment Information** (Treatment Information): This field is used to collect details about the treatment, such as the type and any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **Consent Date** (Consent Date): This field is used to record the date on which the consent process was completed.
* **Consent Time** (Consent Time): This field is used to record the time of day when the consent process was completed.
* **Patient Signature** (Patient Signature): This field is used to capture the patient's signature, indicating their agreement to participate in the treatment.
* **Date of Service** (Date of Service): This field is used to record the date on which the hydrafacial treatment was provided.
* **Time of Service** (Time of Service): This field is used to record the time of day when the hydrafacial treatment was provided.
* **Practitioner Name** (Practitioner Name): This field is used to collect the name of the practitioner providing the treatment.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information): This field is used to collect any relevant contact information for the practitioner or treatment provider.
* **Hydrafacial Contraindications** (Hydrafacial Contraindications): This field is used to record any contraindications that may apply to the treatment.
