# Sugaring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sugaring" consent form is a document that you must complete to declare your consent for services provided. Please review each section carefully and sign to indicate your acceptance.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Read each section carefully and answer all fields that are marked as required.
* Enter the correct date when you received the services (Service Date).
* Enter the correct time when you received the services (Service Time).
* Select the type of service you received from the options provided (Service Type).
* If applicable, enter your contact number and email for easy communication.
* Enter the address where the services were provided (Service Address).
* Enter the name of the service provider (Service Provider).
* Sign the form to indicate your consent for the services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sugaring Consent Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Service Date**: Enter the date when you received the services. Please use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Service Time**: Enter the time when you received the services. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you received from the options provided. This will help us to tailor our communication and services to your needs.
* **Contact Number**: If you'd like to be contacted by us, please enter your phone number here.
* **Contact Email**: If you'd like to be contacted by us via email, please enter your email address here.
* **Service Address**: Enter the address where the services were provided.
* **Service Provider**: Enter the name of the person who provided the services.
* **Client Signature**: Please sign this field to indicate your consent for the services provided. Your signature serves as proof of your acknowledgment and acceptance.
