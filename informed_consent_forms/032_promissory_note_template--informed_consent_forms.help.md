# promissory_note_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promissory note template form is used to document a promissory note for a financial transaction. It captures essential details such as deadline, due date, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in the deadline field with the agreed-upon deadline for the promissory note.
- Enter the due date for the promissory note in the due_date field.
- Enter the amount of the promissory note in the amount field.
- If applicable, provide a reason for the promissory note in the reason field.
- Select the payment method used for the promissory note from the payment_method field.
- If necessary, add comments regarding the promissory note in the comments field.
- Enter the contact information for the parties involved in the contact field.
- Enter the email address for the parties involved in the email field.
- Select whether a signature is required for the promissory note in the signature field.
- Enter the date the promissory note was signed in the date_signed field.
- Enter the date the promissory note was given to the other party in the date_given field.
- Enter the date the promissory note is due in the date_due field.
- Enter the date the promissory note will be returned in the date_returned field.
- Select the type of signature required for the promissory note in the signature_type field.
- Enter the date the promissory note was signed as a date_signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Deadline** (Deadline, required): Enter the agreed-upon deadline for the promissory note.
* **Due Date** (Due Date, required): Enter the due date for the promissory note.
* **Amount** (Amount, required): Enter the total amount of the promissory note.
* **Reason** (Reason, optional): Provide a reason for the promissory note, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, optional): Select the payment method used for the promissory note (Yes/No).
* **Comments** (Comments, optional): Add any comments regarding the promissory note.
* **Contact** (Contact, optional): Enter contact information for the parties involved.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Enter the email address for the parties involved.
* **Signature** (Signature, optional): Select whether a signature is required for the promissory note (Yes/No).
* **Date Signed** (Date Signed, required): Enter the date the promissory note was signed.
* **Date Given** (Date Given, required): Enter the date the promissory note was given to the other party.
* **Date Due** (Date Due, required): Enter the date the promissory note is due.
* **Date Returned** (Date Returned, required): Enter the date the promissory note will be returned.
* **Signature Type** (Signature Type, optional): Select the type of signature required for the promissory note (Yes/No).
* **Date Signature** (Date Signature, required): Enter the date the promissory note was signed.
