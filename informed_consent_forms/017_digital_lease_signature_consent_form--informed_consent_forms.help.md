# Digital Lease Signature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect informed consent from tenants for a digital lease agreement. It ensures that the tenant understands and agrees to the terms of the lease.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your first name and last name.
*   Provide your address.
*   Enter your email address.
*   Enter your phone number.
*   Enter your date of birth.
*   If applicable, provide a signature for your acknowledgment.
*   Enter the date of move-in for your leased property.
*   Enter the lease length in months.
*   Enter your rent amount.
*   Choose how often you will pay rent.
*   Select your landlord(s) from the provided options.
*   Enter the date of move-out for your leased property.
*   Select the reason for move-out from the provided options.
*   Confirm your understanding of lease terms.
*   Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   Select your relationship with your emergency contact.
*   Enter your emergency contact number.
*   Enter your emergency contact email address.
*   Enter your emergency contact's address.
*   Provide a note for your emergency contact (optional).
*   Sign this form to confirm your informed consent and agreement to the digital lease terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required): Enter your current address.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your current address.
*   **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state of residence.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter your zip code of your current address.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number that you can be reached at.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign this form to confirm your informed consent and agreement to the digital lease terms.
*   **Date of Move In** (`date_of_move_in`, date, required): Enter the date you are moving into the leased property.
*   **Lease Length in Months** (`lease_length_in_months`, number, required): Enter the lease length in months.
*   **Rent** (`rent`, number, required): Enter your monthly rent amount.
*   **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you pay rent (e.g., monthly, bimonthly).
*   **Landlord** (`landlord`, select_multiple, required): Select your landlord(s) from the provided options.
*   **Date of Move Out** (`date_of_move_out`, date, required): Enter the date you will be moving out of the leased property.
*   **Reason for Move Out** (`reason_for_move_out`, select_one, required): Select the reason you are moving out from the provided options.
*   **Tenant Awareness of Lease Terms** (`tenant_awareness_of_lease_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are aware of the lease terms.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse).
*   **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your emergency contact.
*   **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, required): Sign this form to confirm your informed consent and agreement to the digital lease terms.
*   **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or note for your emergency contact.
