# Fat Cavitation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients and their family members for medical professionals to assess and treat their medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and Contact Number.
* Enter your Email address, which will be used to contact you.
* Provide your Medical Condition and any relevant Medical History.
* Choose whether you consent to the use of your information for medical purposes.
* Enter any additional Medical Notes if necessary.
* Finally, enter the information of the Doctor who will be treating you, including their name, phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your home or office address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number for future communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for secure communication.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): Describe your medical condition or any health issues you are experiencing.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Check the box to indicate whether you agree or disagree to the use of your information for medical purposes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical history or allergies.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your assigned doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your assigned doctor.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter the email of your assigned doctor.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional medical notes or information.
* **Client Intake Form ID** (`client_intake_form_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique ID for this form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter a unique ID for this form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the tool assigned to you by your doctor.
* **Description** (`form_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your condition or treatment.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your doctor for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your doctor or medical professional.
* This form will be stored securely and will only be used for medical purposes.
* You can always update or modify your information later if needed.
