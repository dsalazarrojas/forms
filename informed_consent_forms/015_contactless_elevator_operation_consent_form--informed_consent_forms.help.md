# Contactless Elevator Operation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from occupants to use the contactless elevator operation system. The form is required for all users who wish to use the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your occupant details in the "Occupant Full Name", "Building and Unit Number", and "Email Address" fields.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Read and understand the system's operation by reviewing the attached user guide.
4. Confirm that you understand how the contactless elevator operation works by selecting "True" in the "I understand how the contactless elevator operation works" field.
5. Consent to the collection of your usage data for performance monitoring by selecting "True" in the "I consent to the collection of my usage data for performance monitoring" field.
6. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the privacy policy regarding your data by selecting "True" in the "I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data" field.
7. Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting "I Agree" in the "I confirm that the information provided is accurate" field.
8. Select the date of consent by entering a valid date in the "Date of Consent" field.
9. If you have any questions or comments, please use the "Any questions or comments?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Occupant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Building and Unit Number**: Enter the building and unit number where you will be using the contactless elevator operation system.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for assistance or updates.
* **I understand how the contactless elevator operation works**: Please read and understand how the contactless elevator operation system works before using it.
* **I consent to the collection of my usage data for performance monitoring**: Please select "True" if you agree to the collection of your usage data for performance monitoring.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data**: Please select "True" if you have read and agreed to the privacy policy regarding your data.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate**: Please select "I Agree" if you confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Any questions or comments?**: Please use this field to ask questions or provide comments about the contactless elevator operation system.
