# Medical Cannabinoids Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Cannabinoids Informed Consent Form is a critical document that requires patients to provide informed consent before undergoing medical cannabinoids treatment. This form helps ensure that patients understand the treatment plan, potential benefits, and risks associated with medical cannabinoids.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field (ID 1).
2. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field (ID 2).
3. Provide the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field (ID 3).
4. Enter the date of consent in the "Date of Consent" field (ID 4).
5. Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate that consent has been given in the "Consent Given" field (ID 5).
6. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID Number" field (ID 6) if required by your institution.
7. Enter the patient's age in the "Age of Patient" field (ID 7).
8. Enter the patient's weight in the "Weight of Patient" field (ID 8).
9. Sign the form in the "Doctor Signature" field (ID 9) to indicate that you have reviewed and discussed the treatment plan with the patient.
10. Enter the date of signature in the "Date of Signature" field (ID 10) to record the date of signing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the patient.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's address for future reference and communication.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required: true): Enter the name of the doctor who is providing treatment.
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, date, required: false): Record the date when the consent was given.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required: true): Select "Yes" to indicate that the patient has given consent for the treatment.
* **Patient ID Number** (`patient_id_number`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's ID number if required by your institution.
* **Age of Patient** (`patient_age`, number, required: false): Enter the patient's age.
* **Weight of Patient** (`patient_weight`, number, required: false): Enter the patient's weight.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required: true): Sign the form to indicate that you have reviewed and discussed the treatment plan with the patient.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, required: true): Enter the date of signing the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and discuss the treatment plan with the patient before signing the form.
* Ensure that the patient understands the potential benefits and risks associated with medical cannabinoids.
* Keep a record of the form completion and signing dates for future reference.
