# Digital Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Consultation Form is a tool designed to collect information from users for digital consultation purposes. It aims to gather general information, contact details, medical conditions, medications, and other relevant data to facilitate effective digital interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about yourself, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your contact information so that we can get in touch with you.
3. If applicable, list any medical conditions you are experiencing.
4. If you are taking any medications, please list them.
5. Describe your hair style and color.
6. Provide any relevant medical history.
7. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their details.
8. List any allergies you have.
9. If you have any other medical conditions, please describe them.
