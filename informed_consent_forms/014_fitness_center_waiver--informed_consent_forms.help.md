# fitness_center_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals to provide their informed consent for participation in fitness center activities. It collects contact information, emergency contact details, and a waiver for the participant to acknowledge their understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of the fitness center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Form Title** (page 1) to provide a brief description of your visit or program.
2. Enter your **full_name** (page 2) to identify yourself.
3. Provide your **email** (page 3) for contact purposes.
4. Enter your **phone** (page 4) number for emergency contact.
5. Add your **address** (page 5) where you can be reached.
6. Provide an **emergency_contact** (page 6) person's name and their **emergency_phone** (page 7) and **emergency_address** (page 8).
7. Describe your **emergency_relationship** (page 9) with the emergency contact person.
8. If applicable, provide **emergency_other** (page 10) details for the emergency contact person.
9. Read and acknowledge that you **i_understand** (page 11) the waiver terms.
10. Select the correct option for **i_sign** (page 12) to indicate your understanding.
11. If you have signed the waiver, enter the date and time of signing in **i_date** (page 13) and **i_time** (page 14).
12. Leave your signature in **signature** (page 15).
13. Optionally, sign again in **signature2** (page 16).
14. Enter the date of signing in **date2** (page 17).
15. Enter the time of signing in **time2** (page 18).
16. Enter your waiver text in **waiver** (page 19).
17. Enter the date of the waiver in **i_waiver_date** (page 20).
18. Enter the time of the waiver in **i_waiver_time** (page 21).
19. Leave your waiver signature in **i_waiver_signature** (page 22).

* **Form Title** (page 1): A brief description of your visit or program.

* **email** (page 3): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (page 4): Your phone number for emergency contact.
* **address** (page 5): Your address where you can be reached.
* **emergency_contact** (page 6): Name of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_phone** (page 7): Phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_address** (page 8): Address of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_relationship** (page 9): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_other** (page 10): Provide any additional details for the emergency contact person.
* **i_understand** (page 11): Acknowledge that you have read and understand the waiver terms.
* **i_sign** (page 12): Select the option indicating that you understand and agree to the waiver terms.
* **i_date** (page 13): Date of signing (if applicable).
