# Temporary Guardianship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temporary Guardianship Form is used to collect information about the individual's temporary guardian, which may include a family member, friend, or other individual responsible for their care and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out the Temporary Guardianship Form page.
2. Enter the date of birth of the individual you are responsible for in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Select the type of relationship you have with the guardian in the "Guardian Relationship" field. You can choose from "Guardian," "Family Member," "Friend," or "Other."
4. Enter the contact information for the guardian, including phone number and email (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
5. If you need to provide any additional information about the guardian, use the "Additional Guardian Info" field.
6. Enter the guardian's address, including city, in the "Guardian Address" and "Guardian City" fields.
7. Once you have filled out all the required fields, review your submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Temporary Guardianship Form** (`temporary_guardianship_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description or purpose for this form (e.g., "Temporary Guardianship for [Individual's Name]").
* **Date of Birth** (`temporary_guardianship_form_date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the individual's date of birth.
* **Time of Guardianship** (`temporary_guardianship_form_time_of_guardianship`, time, required/optional): Enter the duration or period of guardianship.
* **Guardian Name** (`temporary_guardianship_form_guardian_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the guardian responsible for the individual.
* **Guardian Relationship** (`temporary_guardianship_form_guardian_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of relationship you have with the guardian (e.g., "Guardian," "Family member," "Friend," or "Other").
* **Guardian Phone** (`temporary_guardianship_form_guardian_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian E-mail** (`temporary_guardianship_form_guardian_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the guardian's email address.
* **Additional Guardian Info** (`temporary_guardianship_form_additional_guardian_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the guardian.
* **Guardian Address** (`temporary_guardianship_form_guardian_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the guardian's address.
* **Guardian City** (`temporary_guardianship_form_guardian_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the guardian's city.
* **Tips**: Make sure to fill out all required fields, and if you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities or support team.
