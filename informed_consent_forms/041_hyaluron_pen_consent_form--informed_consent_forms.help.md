# Hyaluron Pen Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from clients for Hyaluron Pen treatments. It ensures that clients are aware of the procedure, its risks, and benefits, and provides a record of their consent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of procedure you are undergoing (Hydra or Non-Surgical).
3. Choose the treatment area (Face, Neck, Back, or Other).
4. Select the hyaluronic pen concentration (20mg/mL, 40mg/mL, or 60mg/mL).
5. Indicate the number of units used.
6. If applicable, enter the total cost of the treatment.
7. Sign the form to indicate your consent.
8. Provide the name and signature of a witness, if required (e.g., a family member or a colleague).
9. The doctor or treatment provider will sign and date the form to confirm the treatment.
