# Parent Permission Form For Field Trip - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for parents/guardians to provide their consent and essential contact information for their child's participation in a field trip. It's crucial that parents/guardians understand the purpose of the trip and their child's role in it, and that they're able to provide accurate contact information to ensure their child's safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your child's school in the **school_name** field.
2. Select the date of the field trip in the **date_of_trip** field (use the format DD/MM/YYYY).
3. Choose the time of departure for the field trip in the **departure_time** field (use the 24-hour clock format).
4. Provide a brief description of the field trip in the **trip_details** field.
5. Enter the name of your child's teacher or chaperone who will be accompanying them on the trip in the **teacher_chaperone** field.
6. Provide the contact information of an emergency contact person in the **emergency_contact** field.
7. Enter your contact phone number in the **phone_number** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
9. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your permission for your child to participate in the field trip in the **permission** field.
9. Sign and date the form in the **signature** field to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_name**: The name of your child's school.
* **date_of_trip**: The date of the field trip. Enter the date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **departure_time**: The time of departure for the field trip. Enter the time in the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **trip_details**: A brief description of the field trip, including the destination, activities, and any other relevant details.
* **teacher_chaperone**: The name of your child's teacher or chaperone who will be accompanying them on the trip.
* **emergency_contact**: The name and contact information of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **phone_number**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **email**: Your email address where you can be reached.
* **permission**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that you grant permission for your child to participate in the field trip, and 'No' to decline.
* **signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the trip details and rules.
