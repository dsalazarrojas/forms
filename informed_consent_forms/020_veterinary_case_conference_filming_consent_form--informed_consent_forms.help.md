# Veterinary Case Conference Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants in a veterinary case conference filming project. It ensures that participants understand the purpose, use, and risks associated with the filming and recording of their case discussions. The form helps to establish a clear agreement on the terms of participation and ensures that all parties involved are aware of their responsibilities and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide participant details**: Enter the participant's details in the `participant_details` field. This includes any relevant information about the participant, such as their role or profession.
2. **Enter participant name**: Enter the participant's name in the `participant_name` field.
3. **Provide participant contact information**: Enter the participant's contact information in the `participant_contact_info` field.
4. **Provide participant consent**: Check the box to indicate whether the participant has provided consent for the filming and recording of their case discussion. This is the `participant_consent` field.
5. **Select teaching and training purposes**: Select all the reasons why the participant's case discussion will be filmed (e.g., Teaching and presentations, Research, Professional events). This is the `teaching_and_training` field.
6. **Enter participant data**: Enter any additional information about the participant in the `participant_data` field.
7. **Agree to be recorded**: Select whether the participant agrees to be recorded during the case conference ( `consent_to_record` field).
8. **Agree to use recorded content**: Select whether the participant agrees to have their recorded content used ( `consent_to_use` field).
9. **Read and agree to participant agreement**: Read and agree to the participant agreement in the `participant_agreement` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This field is for providing any relevant information about the participant, such as their role or profession.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Contact Information**: Enter the participant's contact information, including their address, email, or phone number.
* **Participant Consent**: This field indicates whether the participant has given their consent for the filming and recording of their case discussion.
* **Teaching and Training**: Select all the reasons why the participant's case discussion will be filmed (e.g., Teaching and presentations, Research, Professional events).
* **Participant Data**: Enter any additional information about the participant.
* **Consent to Record**: This field indicates whether the participant agrees to be recorded during the case conference.
* **Consent to Use**: This field indicates whether the participant agrees to have their recorded content used.
* **Participant Agreement**: This field is for the participant to read and agree to the participant agreement.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to provide all required fields (marked with `required: true`).
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that participants understand the purpose and terms of the form before they sign it.
