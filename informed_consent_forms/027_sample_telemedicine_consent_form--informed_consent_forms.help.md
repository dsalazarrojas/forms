# Sample Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to provide their informed consent for telemedicine services. It is designed to gather essential information about the patient, their medical history, and contact details. Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely to facilitate effective communication and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand its purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in your patient information, including your name, contact details, and any relevant medical history.
3. Provide your informed consent for telemedicine services by signing the signature request section.
4. Sign your name to confirm your consent.
5. Review your contact information to ensure it is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (1)** (Name, Text, Required): Enter your name, which will be used to identify you throughout the telemedicine process.
* **Medical History (2)** (Name, Text, Required): Describe your medical history, including any allergies, medications, or previous medical conditions that may be relevant to your care.
* **Informed Consent (3)** (Name, Text, Required): This section is for you to confirm that you have understood and agreed to the terms and conditions of the telemedicine services.
* **Signature Request (4)** (Name, Text, Required): Please sign below to confirm that you have read and understood the consent statement.
* **Signature (5)** (Name, Text, Required): Sign your name to confirm your informed consent.
* **Contact Information (6)** (Name, Text, Required): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email, to ensure that we can reach you effectively.
