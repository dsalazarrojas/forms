# Compassion Fatigue Research Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that research participants are fully informed and understand the purpose, risks, and benefits of the study. It also outlines how their data will be handled and stored, and provides options for anonymity and access restrictions.

## How To Complete This Form
Please read each section carefully, and answer all questions accurately. The form is split into several sections:

1.  **Contact Information**: Please provide your name, email, and phone number to enable researchers to contact you with questions or concerns.
2.  **Study Information**: Fill in the title of the research study and the institution or organization conducting it.
3.  **Consent Sections**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate your understanding and consent for audio, video, and photo recordings, as well as how they will be used.
4.  **Data Handling**: Choose how you want your data to be handled in terms of storage, retention, and access.
5.  **Anonymity and Access**: Decide how you want to be identified in recordings and who should have access to them.
6.  **Withdrawal and Compensation**: Understand your right to withdraw from the study at any time and what compensation you may receive.
7.  **Additional Conditions**: If you have any additional conditions or restrictions, please specify them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study Title** (`study_title`, `text`, required): This is the name of the research study you are participating in.
* **Principal Investigator** (`principal_investigator`, `text`, required): This is the name of the lead researcher conducting the study.
* **Institution** (`institution`, `text`, required): This is the institution or organization conducting the research.
* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): This is your legal name as a participant.
* **Participant ID Number** (`participant_id`, `number`, optional): This is an assigned participant identification number.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): This is your contact email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): This is your contact phone number.
* **I understand the purpose of this study** (`study_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to indicate you understand the study's purpose.
* **Consent to Audio Recording** (`recording_type_audio`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to consent to audio recordings.
* **Consent to Video Recording** (`recording_type_video`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to consent to video recordings.
* **Consent to Photographs** (`recording_type_photos`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to consent to photographs.
* **Purpose of Recordings** (`recording_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Choose how the recordings will be used.
* **I understand how data will be stored** (`data_storage`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to indicate you understand data storage.
* **Data Retention Period** (`data_retention`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long the recordings will be kept.
* **Anonymity Preference** (`anonymity_option`, `select_one`, required): Decide how you want to be identified in recordings.
* **Access Restrictions** (`access_restrictions`, `select_one`, required): Choose who should have access to the recordings.
* **I understand I can withdraw consent** (`withdrawal_rights`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to indicate you understand your right to withdraw.
* **Withdrawal Deadline Understanding** (`withdrawal_deadline`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you can request withdrawal.
* **I understand potential risks** (`risks_understood`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to indicate you understand risks.
* **I understand potential benefits** (`benefits_understood`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to indicate you understand benefits.
* **Have all your questions been answered** (`questions_answered`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Yes" if all questions were answered.
* **Voluntary Participation Statement** (`voluntary_participation`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to indicate you understand participation is voluntary.
* **Compensation Information** (`compensation_info`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you will receive compensation.
* **Researcher Contact Provided** (`contact_info_provided`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" to indicate you have contact info for questions.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): This is the date you sign this form.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, optional): This is the name of the witness to your consent.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, optional): This is the witness's signature.
* **Additional Conditions** (`additional_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any special conditions or restrictions, please specify them.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
- If you are unsure about any question, ask the researcher for clarification.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before signing.
- If you are not comfortable with any aspect of the study, please do not participate.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
