# Refugee Empowerment Storytelling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from refugees for the use and sharing of their stories, photos, videos, and audio recordings. It ensures that their stories are shared according to their wishes and gives them control over their own narratives.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by selecting the correct category for the form from the options provided.
- Fill in a brief description of the story being shared.
- Select the form provider from the options provided (e.g., chatjimmy, others).
- Choose the types of media being shared (e.g., stories, photos, videos, audio).
- Identify who owns the media (e.g., the individual, the story teller, Jotform).
- Confirm the story teller's consent for the sharing of their story.
- Select the purpose of sharing the media (e.g., for education, for research, for social media, for personal use).
- Determine who the media will be shared with (e.g., public, private, story teller).
- Confirm the story teller's agreement for the sharing of their story.
- Fill in the date of consent.
- Enter the submission date of the form.
- Enter your email address.
- Enter the story teller's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the correct category for this form from the options provided.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: `false`): Fill in a brief description of the story being shared.
* **Form Provider** (`form_provider`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose the form provider from the options provided (e.g., chatjimmy, others).
* **Types of Media** (`media_types`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the types of media being shared (e.g., stories, photos, videos, audio).
* **Who Owns the Media** (`media_owners`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Identify who owns the media (e.g., the individual, the story teller, Jotform).
* **Storyteller Consent** (`storyteller_consent`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Confirm the story teller's consent for the sharing of their story.
* **Purpose of Sharing** (`media_sharing_purpose`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the purpose of sharing the media (e.g., for education, for research, for social media, for personal use).
* **Sharing With** (`sharing_with`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Determine who the media will be shared with (e.g., public, private, story teller).
* **Storyteller's Agreement** (`storyteller_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Confirm the story teller's agreement for the sharing of their story.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Fill in the date of consent.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the submission date of the form.
* **User** (`user`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
* **Storyteller** (`storyteller`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter the story teller's email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- Be honest about the types of media and their purpose.
- Select the correct sharing settings according to the story teller's wishes.
