# Digital Health Education Film Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Health Education Film Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from participants in a digital health education film. It captures information about the participant, their consent status, filming details, and filming schedule. This form is crucial in ensuring that participants understand their rights and responsibilities, and that their data is properly handled. Make sure to only fill this form out when creating new content or modifying existing content. If this form is already complete, review and update the fields as necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Digital Health Education Film Consent Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing each page and filling out the required fields. If a page is not required, you can skip it.
2. On the "Participant Information" page, enter the participant's name, ID number (if applicable), and any other relevant details. This information will help us identify and contact the participant if needed.
3. On the "Consent Status" page, select "Consent" if the participant has agreed to be filmed, and "No Consent" if they have not. This will help us determine if the participant's data can be used.
4. On the "Filming Details" page, enter any relevant details about the filming, such as a brief description.
5. On the "Filming Date" page, select the date when the participant will be filmed.
6. On the "Filming Time" page, select the time when the participant will be filmed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information (Page 1)
* **Participant Information (Participant Name, ID, etc.)** (`participant_information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's name, ID number (if applicable), and any other relevant details. This information will help us identify and contact the participant if needed.

### Consent Status (Page 2)
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Consent" if the participant has agreed to be filmed, and "No Consent" if they have not.

### Filming Details (Page 3)
* **Filming Details** (`filming_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant details about the filming, such as a brief description.

### Filming Date (Page 4)
* **Filming Date** (`filming_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date when the participant will be filmed.

### Filming Time (Page 5)
* **Filming Time** (`filming_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time when the participant will be filmed.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* If the participant's consent status changes, update their consent status field accordingly.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
