# Academic Study Video Diary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from participants in an academic video diary study. The form is used to gather essential information about each participant, including their name, contact details, institutional affiliation, study title, principal investigator, and other relevant study information. By providing their consent, participants agree to participate in the video diary study, allowing researchers to collect video data for academic research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your **Participant full name** in the first field. This is a required field and will be used to identify you throughout the study.
2. Enter your **Email address** in the second field. This is a required field and will be used for communication purposes.
3. Enter your **Phone number** in the third field. This is an optional field but is highly recommended for any questions or concerns.
4. Select your **Date of birth** from the date picker calendar. This is a required field and will be used to verify your age eligibility for the study.
5. If you are affiliated with an **Institutional affiliation**, please enter it in this field. This is a required field as it will be used to identify your institutional affiliation.
6. Enter the **Title of the research study** that you will be participating in. This is a required field as it will be used to identify the study you are participating in.
7. Enter the **Name of the principal investigator** who is conducting the study. This is a required field as it will be used to identify the study lead.
8. In the **Study Information** section, review the details about the study and its purpose. This section explains that the study uses video diary entries to collect qualitative data about your academic experiences over a defined period.
9. Select one of the options to indicate if you **voluntarily agree to participate** in the study. Make sure to choose "Yes - I consent" if you are willing to participate.
10. Similarly, select one of the options to indicate if you **consent to recording video diary entries** as part of the study. Choose "Yes - I consent" if you are willing to participate in this way.
11. Choose one of the options to indicate how you **consent to your video data being used** for academic research purposes. Select "Yes - anonymized use only" if you want your data to be anonymized, "Yes - with my identity disclosed" if you want your identity to be disclosed, or "No - I do not consent" if you are not comfortable with data use.
12. Select one of the options to indicate if you **understand that video recordings will be stored securely** and only be accessed by authorized research personnel. Choose "Yes - I understand" to confirm your understanding.
13. Select one of the options to indicate if you **understand you can withdraw from the study at any time without penalty**. Choose "Yes - I understand" to confirm your understanding.
14. Finally, select one of the options to indicate how you **consent to your data being retained**. Choose "Duration of the study only", "Up to 1 year after study completion", "Up to 5 years after study completion", or "Indefinitely for archival purposes" based on your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant full name**: Your full name will be used to identify you throughout the study.
* **Email address**: This is your official email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for any questions or concerns.
* **Date of birth**: Select your date of birth to verify your age eligibility for the study.
* **Institutional affiliation**: If you are affiliated with an institution, please enter it here.
* **Title of the research study**: Enter the title of the study you will be participating in.
* **Name of the principal investigator**: Enter the name of the lead investigator conducting the study.
* **Study Information**: Review the study details and its purpose.
* **I voluntarily agree to participate in this video diary study**: Select "Yes - I consent" if you are willing to participate.
* **I consent to recording video diary entries as part of this study**: Select "Yes - I consent" if you are willing to record video diary entries.
* **I consent to my video data being used for academic research purposes**: Choose "Yes - anonymized use only", "Yes - with my identity disclosed", or "No - I do not consent" based on your preference.
* **I understand that video recordings will be stored securely and accessed only by authorized research personnel**: Choose "Yes - I understand" to confirm your understanding.
* **I understand I may withdraw from the study at any time without penalty**: Choose "Yes - I understand" to confirm your understanding.
* **I consent to my data being retained for the following period**: Choose "Duration of the study only", "Up to 1 year after study completion", "Up to 5 years after study completion", or "Indefinitely for archival purposes" based on your preference.
* **Date of consent**: Select the date you are giving your consent.
* **Any questions or concerns about your participation**: If you have any questions or concerns, please enter them here.
