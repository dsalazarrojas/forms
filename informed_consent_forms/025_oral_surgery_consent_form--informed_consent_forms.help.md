# Oral Surgery Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oral Surgery Consent Form is a document that requires patients to provide informed consent before undergoing oral surgery. This form aims to inform patients about the risks, benefits, and procedures involved in the surgery, as well as their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you understand the information and questions asked.
2. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, date of birth, contact details, and email address.
3. Provide the necessary information about the surgery, including the date and time of the procedure.
4. Sign the form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Patient Contact Details** (`patient_contact_details`, note, required): Enter your current contact information, including your address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can reach you for any updates or follow-ups.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for any emergencies or updates.
* **Surgeon Name** (`surgeon_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the surgeon who will be performing the surgery.
* **Date of Surgery** (`date_of_surgery`, date, required): Enter the date when the surgery will take place.
* **Time of Surgery** (`time_of_surgery`, time, required): Enter the time when the surgery will take place.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ask the clinic staff if you have any questions or doubts about the form or the surgery procedure.
* Take a copy of the completed form for your records and future reference.
