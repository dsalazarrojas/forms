# Moderna Booster Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about individuals interested in the Moderna booster. Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure you receive the right guidance and recommendations for your vaccination needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
3. Provide your Phone number for easy contact.
4. Select your interest in the Moderna booster (yes/no).
5. Indicate your interest in the Pfizer booster (yes/no).
6. Let us know if you are interested in the Johnson & Johnson booster (yes/no).
7. Enter your Date of Birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
8. Enter your Time of Birth (hh:mm).
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the Note field.
10. Finally, indicate if you are interested in other vaccines (yes/no) and if you are interested in AstraZeneca boosters (yes/no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like to be known.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like to be known.
### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
### Are you interested in the Moderna booster?
* **Are you interested in the Moderna booster?** (`interested_in_moderna_booster`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you are interested in the Moderna booster.
### Are you interested in the Pfizer booster?
* **Are you interested in the Pfizer booster?** (`interested_in_pfizer_booster`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you are interested in the Pfizer booster.
### Are you interested in the Johnson & Johnson booster?
* **Are you interested in the Johnson & Johnson booster?** (`interested_in_johnson_johnson_booster`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you are interested in the Johnson & Johnson booster.
### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your Date of Birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
### Time of Birth
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your Time of Birth in hh:mm.
### Note
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your vaccination needs.
### Are you interested in other vaccines?
* **Are you interested in other vaccines?** (`interested_in_others`, select_multiple, optional): Select all vaccines you are interested in, if any.
### Are you interested in AstraZeneca boosters?
* **Are you interested in AstraZeneca boosters?** (`interested_in_astra_zeneca_boosters`, select_multiple, optional): Select all AstraZeneca boosters you are interested in, if any.

## Tips
* Please fill out all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
* You can add any additional comments or questions in the Note field.
* If you need to update your information, please fill out the form again with your new details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us using the contact information provided.
