# Virtual Conferencing Participant Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Virtual Conferencing Participant Consent Form is a mandatory form that needs to be completed by all participants in virtual conferencing events. It is used to collect basic participant information, event details, and consent to participate. This form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the **Participant Info** section with your name and any additional information that you would like to share with the organizers.
* In the **Event Info** section, provide a brief description of the event that interests you, including any specific details you think would be useful for the organizers.
* Select **Agree** or **Decline** from the **Participant Consent** section to indicate your intention to participate in the event.
* If you have a preferred contact method, provide it in the **Contact Info** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Info (participant_info)

* **Participant Info** (text, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional information about yourself that you would like to share with the organizers.

### Event Info (event_info)

* **Event Info** (text, optional): In this section, describe the event that interests you, including any specific details that would be helpful for the organizers.

### Participant Consent (participant_consent)

* **Participant Consent** (select_one, optional): Select **Agree** to indicate that you intend to participate in the event. If you select **Decline**, your submission will be rejected.

### Contact Info (contact_info)

* **Contact Info** (text, optional): If you have a preferred contact method (e.g., phone number, email, social media), provide it here so that the organizers can reach out to you.

## Tips

* Please make sure to read and understand the terms and conditions of the event before completing this form.
* Be honest in your response and provide accurate information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organizers directly.
