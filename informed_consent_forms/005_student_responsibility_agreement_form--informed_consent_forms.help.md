# Student Responsibility Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students regarding their responsibilities and agreements with their teacher and parent.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all answers carefully from the options provided.
2. Enter the student's signature in the corresponding signature field.
3. Enter the teacher's signature and other details in the corresponding fields.
4. Enter the student's demographic information (name, grade, school, class, subject).
5. Enter the parent's contact information (name, title, phone, email, address, school, class, subject).
6. Review all fields carefully and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Responsibility Form (student_responsibility_form)**: A multiple-choice question where students select their level of understanding of school rules. Please choose 'Yes' if you have read and understand the school rules or 'No' if you haven't.
* **Student Signature (student_signature)**: Please enter your student signature as a numeric value (e.g., 1, 2, etc.).
* **Teacher Signature (teacher_signature)**: Enter the teacher's signature as a numeric value (e.g., 1, 2, etc.).
* **Date Signed (date_signed)**: Please enter the date the student signed this form in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **School Rules Read (school_rules_read)**: A multiple-choice question where students select whether they have read school rules or not. Please choose 'Yes' if you have read and understand the school rules or 'No' if you haven't.
* **School Rules Understood (school_rules_understood)**: A multiple-choice question where students select whether they understand school rules or not. Please choose 'Yes' if you have understood school rules or 'No' if you haven't.
* **Teacher Name (teacher_name)**: Enter the teacher's name in the text field.
* **Teacher Title (teacher_title)**: Enter the teacher's title in the text field.
* **Teacher Department (teacher_department)**: Enter the teacher's department in the text field.
* **Teacher School (teacher_school)**: Enter the teacher's school in the text field.
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the student's name in the text field.
* **Student Grade (student_grade)**: Enter the student's grade in the text field.
* **Student School (student_school)**: Enter the student's school in the text field.
* **Student Class (student_class)**: Enter the student's class in the text field.
* **Student Subject (student_subject)**: Enter the student's subject in the text field.
* **Student Phone (student_phone)**: Enter the student's phone number in the text field. (Optional)
* **Student Email (student_email)**: Enter the student's email address in the text field. (Required)
* **Teacher Email (teacher_email)**: Enter the teacher's email address in the text field. (Required)
* **Student Address (student_address)**: Enter the student's address in the text field. (Required)
* **Teacher Note (teacher_note)**: Enter any additional notes from the teacher in the text field. (Optional)
* **Student Note (student_note)**: Enter any additional notes from the student in the text field. (Optional)
* **School Rules (school_rules)**: Enter any additional school rules in the text field. (Required)
* **Parent Name (parent_name)**: Enter the parent's name in the text field. (Optional)
* **Parent Title (parent_title)**: Enter the parent's title in the text field. (Optional)
* **Parent Phone (parent_phone)**: Enter the parent's phone number in the text field. (Optional)
* **Parent Email (parent_email)**: Enter the parent's email address in the text field. (Optional)
* **Parent Address (parent_address)**: Enter the parent's address in the text field. (Optional)
* **Parent School (parent_school)**: Enter the parent's school in the text field. (Optional)
* **Parent Class (parent_class)**: Enter the parent's class in the text field. (Optional)
* **Parent Subject (parent_subject)**: Enter the parent's subject in the text field. (Optional)
