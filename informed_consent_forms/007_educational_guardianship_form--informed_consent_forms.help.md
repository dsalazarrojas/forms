# Educational Guardianship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from individuals related to minors or students for educational guardianship purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guardian's arrangements for the minor or student.
2. Provide the parent's name and contact information (email and phone number).
3. Enter the date of birth of the minor or student.
4. Select the type of consent (Guardian, Authorized Representative, or Both).
5. Enter the guardian's signature.
6. Enter the parent's signature.
7. Specify the relationship between the parent and the minor or student (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, or Other).
8. If necessary, provide additional details about the relationship (e.g., Other Relationship).
9. Enter the address of the parent (not required).
10. Select the state where the guardian resides (not required).
11. Enter a zip code (not required).
12. Check or specify whether to share information with the minor or student (True, False, or No Response).
13. If consent is granted, enter the details to share (e.g., with whom).
14. Add any additional notes about the consent.
15. Enter the date and time of the consent (not required).
16. Select the time zone where the guardian resides (e.g., Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific).
17. Enter the time zone offset (e.g., UTC-5, UTC-6, etc.).
18. Enter the time zone display format (not required).
19. Enter any additional time zone information if needed (not required).
