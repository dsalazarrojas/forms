# sculptra_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The sculptra_consent_form is a medical consent form used to obtain informed consent from patients before treatment. It gathers essential information about the patient's medical condition, allergies, and previous medical experiences. The form also ensures that the patient understands the risks and benefits of the treatment and has provided their consent for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the sculptra_consent_form, follow these steps:

1. **Medical Condition**: If you have a medical condition that may affect your treatment, fill in this field with your condition.
2. **Age**: Enter your age.
3. **Meds and Allergies**: List any medications and allergies you are taking or have.
4. **Surgical Procedures**: If you have had previous surgical procedures, list them here.
5. **Anesthesia**: Select the type of anesthesia used during your procedure (General, Regional, IV, or Local).
6. **Informed Consent**: Select all the options that reflect your understanding of the risks and benefits of the treatment.
7. **Signature and Date**: Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
8. **Provider Information**: Enter your healthcare provider's contact information (name, address, phone, email, and fax).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sculptra Informed Consent** (`sculptra_informed_consent`): This field is where you acknowledge that you have understood the potential risks and benefits of the treatment. Select all the options that apply.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`): Enter your birthdate to confirm your identity.
* **Age** (`age`): Enter your current age.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): If you have a medical condition that may affect your treatment, list it here.
* **Other Medical Condition** (`other_medical_condition`): If you have any other medical conditions not listed above, specify them here.
* **Meds and Allergies** (`medications`): List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`): List any allergies you have.
* **Surgical Procedures** (`surgical_procedures`): If you have had previous surgical procedures, list them here.
* **Anesthesia** (`anesthesia`): Select the type of anesthesia used during your procedure (General, Regional, IV, or Local).
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`): Confirm that you have understood the potential risks and benefits of the treatment. Select all the options that apply.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Date** (`date`): Date the form when you sign it.
* **Signature Name** (`signature_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information** (`contact`): Enter your contact information (name, address, phone, email, and fax).
* **Provider Information** (`provider`): Enter your healthcare provider's contact information (name, address, phone, email, and fax).
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your healthcare provider's address.
* **Fax** (`fax`): Enter your healthcare provider's fax number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your healthcare provider's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your healthcare provider's phone number.
* **Other Contact Info** (`other_contact_info`): If you have any other contact information for your healthcare provider, enter it here.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`): If you have any additional patient consent, specify it here.
* **Other Patient Consent** (`other_patient_consent`): If you have any other patient consent, specify it here.
* **Provider Signature** (`signature_provider`): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent as the healthcare provider.
* **Provider Name** (`name_of_provider`): Enter your healthcare provider's name as it appears on your identification documents.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and understand the risks and benefits of the treatment before signing the form.
* Be honest and accurate when filling in the form.
* Ensure that your healthcare provider has your current contact information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, address them with your healthcare provider before signing the form.
