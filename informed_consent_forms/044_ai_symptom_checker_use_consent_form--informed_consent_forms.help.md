# AI Symptom Checker Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Symptom Checker Use Consent Form is a document that users must complete to provide their informed consent to use the AI symptom checker. The form is used to ensure that users understand the tool's limitations and the implications of using it, and to collect their contact information and signature for any follow-up or future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the information provided.
2.  Fill in your name, contact email (if provided), and a brief summary of your symptoms.
3.  Select the correct options for consent, understanding of the tool's limitations, and permission to share anonymized data for research.
4.  Read the emergency instructions carefully.
5.  Sign the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (`--user_information--`): This is a note section where you can add any relevant information about yourself.
*   **User name** (`user_name`): This field is optional and can be left blank if you do not wish to provide your name.
*   **Contact email** (`contact_email`): This field is optional and can be used for follow-up communication. If you do not provide your email, the tool's developers will not be able to contact you.
*   **Symptom summary** (`symptom_summary`): Please provide a brief description of your symptoms. This will help the tool's developers understand your condition and provide accurate information.
*   **Consent to use AI symptom checker** (`consent_statement`): Select "I consent to use the symptom checker" to agree to use the tool. If you select "I do not consent to use the symptom checker", you will not be able to use the tool.
*   **Understanding of tool limitations** (`understanding_limitations`): Select "I understand this is not a diagnosis" to acknowledge that the tool is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis. Select "I do not understand" if you have questions or concerns.
*   **Permission to share anonymized data for research** (`data_sharing_preference`): Select "True" if you agree to share anonymized data for research purposes. Select "False" if you do not agree.
*   **Emergency instructions** (`emergency_instructions`): This is a note section that provides information on what to do in urgent situations.
*   **Electronic signature name** (`signature`): Type your full name to sign the form.
*   **Signature date** (`signature_date`): Date and sign the form to complete the consent.
