# Beard Hot Towel Service Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients for the Beard Hot Towel Service. It includes their personal details, medical history, and other relevant information required for the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Provide your Date of Birth.
3. Enter your Contact Phone Number.
4. Choose a Service Date for your appointment.
5. Enter the name of your service provider (Barber Name).
6. Select any current skin conditions you have from the list provided.
7. Indicate your heat sensitivity level.
8. If you have any relevant medical conditions, please enter them here.
9. List any current medications, especially blood thinners.
10. If you've had any recent facial procedures, please list them here.
11. Confirm that you understand the service description.
12. Confirm that you will communicate your temperature comfort level.
13. Confirm that you understand the risks associated with the service.
14. Confirm that you give your consent for the hot towel service.
15. If you've chosen any additional services, list them here.
16. Sign your full name in the space provided.
17. Date and sign the form with the date you complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Service Date**: Choose the date of your appointment.
* **Barber Name**: Enter the name of your service provider.
* **Current Skin Conditions**: Select any current skin conditions you have from the list provided.
* **Heat Sensitivity**: Indicate your level of heat sensitivity.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any relevant medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Medications**: If you are taking any medications, especially blood thinners, list them here.
* **Recent Procedures**: If you've had any recent facial procedures, list them here.
* **Service Description Understood**: Confirm that you understand the service description.
* **Temperature Comfort**: Confirm that you will communicate your temperature comfort level.
* **Risks Understood**: Confirm that you understand the risks associated with the service.
* **I Consent to Hot Towel Service**: Give your consent for the hot towel service.
* **Additional Services**: If you've chosen any additional services, list them here.
* **Client Signature**: Sign your full name in the space provided.
* **Signature Date**: Date and sign the form with the date you complete it.
