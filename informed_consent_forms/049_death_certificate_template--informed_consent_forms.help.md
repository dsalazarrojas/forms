# Death Certificate Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Death Certificate Template is a form used to document a death, capturing essential information about the deceased and the circumstances of their passing. This form is meant to be completed by medical professionals or authorized personnel in situations where a death occurs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the Certificate Details section with the required information about the deceased.
- Select the correct Cause of Death and Place of Death from the provided options.
- Enter the Time of Death, which should be the exact date and time of the deceased's passing.
- If applicable, include the information about the Certifying Officer and Informant, including their signatures and contact information.
- Review and submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Certificate Details
* **Certificate Number** (1, text, required): Enter the unique identifier number assigned to the deceased.
* **Certificate Date** (1, text, required): Enter the date of the certificate issue.
* **Cause of Death** (5, select_one, required): Select the reason for the deceased's passing, which can be "Death from natural causes", "Death from unnatural causes", or "Undetermined".
* **Place of Death** (6, select_multiple, required): Indicate the location where the deceased passed away, which can be "Hospital", "Home", "Hospice", "Nursing home", "Skilled nursing", or "Assisted living".
* **Time of Death** (7, date, required): Enter the exact date and time of the deceased's passing.
* **Certifying Officer Info** (2, text, optional): Enter information about the Certifying Officer, including their name, title, and contact information.
* **Informant Details** (3, text, optional): Enter information about the Informant, including their name, relationship to the deceased, and contact information.
* **Deceased Details** (4, text, optional): Enter information about the deceased, including their name, date of birth, and date of death.
* **Certifying Officer Signature** (8, text, optional): Enter the signature of the Certifying Officer, which serves as verification of the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Informant Signature** (9, text, optional): Enter the signature of the Informant, which serves as verification of the accuracy of the information provided.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered to ensure it is correct and complete.
- Use the provided options for Cause of Death and Place of Death to ensure the correct information is selected.
- If unsure about any field, do not hesitate to seek guidance from a medical professional or authorized personnel.
