# Production Design Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Design Showcase Consent Form is used to gather information and consent from participants in a production design project. This form is essential for ensuring that all project stakeholders are aware of their responsibilities, rights, and obligations throughout the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and section to understand the required information.
2. Fill in your details on the "Participant Details" page.
3. Provide project information on the "Project Information" page.
4. Sign the "Consent Agreements" page to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the project terms.
5. Sign the "Participant Signature" and "Project Manager Signature" pages as required.
6. Complete the "Production Manager Signature" page if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
• **Participant Details** (ID: 1, `text`, required: false): This section is for you to provide your personal details, such as your name and title. Please ensure you fill in your correct information to help us identify you as a project participant.

### Project Information
• **Project Information** (ID: 2, `text`, required: false): Here, you can provide any relevant information about the project, such as project name, description, or timelines. This will help us better understand the project context.

### Consent Agreements
• **Consent Agreements** (ID: 3, `text`, required: false): Review and sign this section to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the project terms and conditions.

### Signatures
• **Signatures** (ID: 4, `text`, required: false): Please review and sign this section as required.

### Participant Signature
• **Participant Signature** (ID: 5, `number`, required: false): Sign your name as a participant to confirm your agreement. Note that this field is for a numeric value, which may be a unique code or a number that identifies you as a participant.

### Project Manager Signature
• **Project Manager Signature** (ID: 6, `number`, required: false): Sign your name as a project manager to confirm your agreement. Like the participant signature, this field is for a numeric value.

### Production Manager Signature
• **Production Manager Signature** (ID: 7, `number`, required: false): If required, sign your name as a production manager to confirm your agreement.
