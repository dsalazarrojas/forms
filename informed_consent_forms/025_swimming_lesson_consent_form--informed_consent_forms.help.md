# Swimming Lesson Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students and their guardians to ensure a safe and enjoyable swim lesson experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide enrollment information (page 1)
	* Fill in your name, contact information, and other details as requested on the first page.
2. Share contact details (page 2)
	* Enter your contact information and any relevant details about you or your swimmer.
3. Provide medical history (page 3)
	* Share any medical conditions, treatments, or allergies you or your swimmer may have.
4. Add emergency contact information (page 4)
	* Enter contact details for a parent or guardian who can be reached in case of an emergency.
5. Share allergy information (page 5)
	* List any allergies or sensitivities you or your swimmer may have.
6. Provide swim lesson information (page 6)
	* Share any relevant details about your swimming experience, skill level, or swim lesson goals.
7. Add emergency contact information (page 7)
	* Enter additional contact details for a parent or guardian who can be reached in case of an emergency.
8. Specify emergency contact relationship (page 8)
	* Indicate your relationship to the swimmer (e.g., parent, guardian, or caregiver).
9. Grant medical treatment authorization (page 9)
	* Confirm that you allow your swimmer to receive medical treatment in case of an emergency.
10. Provide insurance information (page 10)
	* Share your insurance information, if applicable.
11. Confirm emergency contact permission (page 11)
	* Agree to grant your swimmer's emergency contacts permission to act on your behalf.
12. Enter swimmer details (page 12)
	* Provide information about your swimmer, such as age, height, or weight.
13. Obtain parent consent (page 13)
	* Confirm that a parent or guardian has consented to your swimmer participating in lessons.
14. Get swimmer consent (page 14)
	* Confirm that your swimmer has consented to participate in lessons.
15. Obtain medical consent (page 15)
	* Confirm that both you and your swimmer have consented to the necessary medical procedures.
16. Enter swimmer's medical history (page 16)
	* Share any medical conditions or allergies your swimmer may have.
17. List swimmer's allergy information (page 17)
	* Share any allergies or sensitivities your swimmer may have.
18. Confirm swimmer's medical clearance (page 18)
	* Confirm that your swimmer has been medically cleared to participate in swim lessons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enrollment Information** (`Enrollment Information`, text, required): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required): Share your contact information and any relevant details.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): List any medical conditions, treatments, or allergies you or your swimmer may have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, required): Enter contact details for a parent or guardian who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`Emergency Contact Information`, text, required): Enter additional contact details for a parent or guardian who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, text, required): Indicate your relationship to the swimmer (e.g., parent, guardian, or caregiver).
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, required): Share any medical conditions, treatments, or allergies you or your swimmer may have.
* **Allergy Information** (`Allergy Information`, text, required): List any allergies or sensitivities you or your swimmer may have.
* **Swimming Lesson Information** (`Swimming Lesson Information`, text, optional): Share any relevant details about your swimming experience, skill level, or swim lesson goals.
* **Emergency Contact Permission** (`Emergency Contact Permission`, text, required): Agree to grant your swimmer's emergency contacts permission to act on your behalf.
* **Swimmer Details** (`Swimmer Details`, text, required): Enter your swimmer's information, such as age, height, or weight.
* **Parent Consent** (`Parent Consent`, text, required): Confirm that a parent or guardian has consented to your swimmer participating in lessons.
* **Swimmer Consent** (`Swimmer Consent`, text, required): Confirm that your swimmer has consented to participate in lessons.
* **Medical Consent** (`Medical Consent`, text, required): Confirm that both you and your swimmer have consented to the necessary medical procedures.
* **Swimmer Medical History** (`Swimmer Medical History`, text, required): Share any medical conditions or allergies your swimmer may have.
* **Swimmer Allergy Information** (`Swimmer Allergy Information`, text, required): Share any allergies or sensitivities your swimmer may have.
* **Swimmer Medical Clearance** (`Swimmer Medical Clearance`, text, required): Confirm that your swimmer has been medically cleared to participate in swim lessons.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and completely to ensure your swimmer's safety and comfort during lessons.
* If you are unsure about any of the information requested, please contact us for guidance.
* We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in keeping your swimmer safe during lessons.
