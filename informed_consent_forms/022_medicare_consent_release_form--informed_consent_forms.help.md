# medicare_consent_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather informed consent from patients for medical care. It helps ensure that patients understand their rights, responsibilities, and the scope of care they will receive.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully.
3. Ensure you have the patient's consent and understanding before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Healthcare Organization** (`healthcare_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare organization providing care to the patient.
* **Patient ID Number** (`patient_id_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's identification number or account number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider caring for the patient.
* **Date of Care Begin** (`date_of_care_begin`, date, optional): Enter the date the patient began receiving care in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Care End** (`date_of_care_end`, date, optional): Enter the date the patient ended receiving care in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Care Provider** (`care_provider`, select_one, optional): Select the name of the care provider from the options provided.
* **Other Provider** (`other_provider`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional care providers involved in the patient's care.
* **Reason for Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the reason the patient is transferring care.
* **Patient Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship between the patient and the care provider.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the patient signed the form in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the patient signed the form in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): The witness should sign this field to confirm the patient's signature.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): The patient should sign here to indicate their understanding and consent to care.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness who signed the form.
