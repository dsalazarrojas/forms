# Surgery Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients and surgeons before a surgical procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Enter the patient's information in the "patient_information" field.
3. Enter the surgeon's information in the "surgeon" field.
4. Provide details about the procedure in the "procedure" field.
5. List any relevant medical history, allergies, or medications in the "medical_history" field.
6. List any allergies or medications that may be relevant to the procedure in the "allergies" field.
7. Enter details about the anesthesia type in the "anesthesia_details" field.
8. List the surgical team members in the "surgical_team" field.
9. Select the patient's consent status in the "patient_consent" field.
10. Select the surgeon's consent status in the "surgeon_consent" field.
11. Select the anesthesia type in the "anesthesia_details" field.
12. Select the surgical team members in the "surgical_team" field.
13. Select the patient's age group in the "patient_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_information (text)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **surgeon (text)**: Enter the surgeon's name and contact information.
* **procedure (text)**: Provide a detailed description of the surgical procedure.
* **medical_history (text)**: List any relevant medical history, allergies, or medications.
* **allergies (text)**: List any allergies that may be relevant to the procedure.
* **medications (text)**: List any medications that may be relevant to the procedure.
* **anesthesia_details (text)**: Enter details about the anesthesia type and dosage.
* **surgical_team (text)**: List the surgical team members, including surgeons, anesthesiologists, and nurses.
* **patient_consent (select_one)**: Select "Yes" if the patient has given consent, "No" if they have not, or "Unsure" if consent is pending.
* **surgeon_consent (select_multiple)**: Select "Yes" if the surgeon has given consent, "No" if they have not, and "Unknown" if consent is pending.
* **surgical (text)**: Enter details about the surgical procedure, including the surgical method and any complications.
* **anesthesia_type (select_one)**: Select the type of anesthesia to be used, such as "None", "Local", "General", "Regional", or "Total IV".
* **surgical_team (select_multiple)**: Select the surgical team members, including surgeons, anesthesiologists, and nurses.
* **patient_details (select_multiple)**: Select the patient's age group, such as "1-10", "11-20", "21-30", etc.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed each field carefully and provided accurate information.
