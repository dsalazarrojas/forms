# digital_library_resource_case_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "digital_library_resource_case_consent_form" is a form used to gather informed consent for accessing digital library resources. It ensures users understand and agree to the terms of use and access to these resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Answer each field truthfully and accurately.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **What resource do you wish to access** (`resource`, `select_multiple`, required)
Choose the resources you wish to access from the available options. Please select "Yes" if you wish to access one or more resources.

### **Permissions** (`permissions`, `text`, required)
Explain what permissions you require to access the resources. Be specific about what you need access to.

### **Do you wish to access these case materials** (`case_materials`, `select_multiple`, required)
Choose "Yes" if you wish to access the case materials, and "No" otherwise.

### **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required)
Select "I understand and agree" if you have read and agree to the terms of use. Select "I do not understand and do not agree" if you have not read and do not agree.

### **Contact name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required)
Enter your name as the contact person for this request.

### **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required)
Enter your email address where you can be contacted.

### **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required)
Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.

### **Further note** (`contact_note`, `text`, optional)
Add any additional notes or comments about your request, if necessary.

### **Do you have any further notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional)
Add any additional information that may be helpful for us to know.

### **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required)
Please sign this form to confirm your consent.

Note: This form must be submitted in order to be processed.
