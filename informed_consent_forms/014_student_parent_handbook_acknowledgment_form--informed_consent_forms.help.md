# Student Parent Handbook Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify that the parent or guardian of a student has read and understood the contents of the student parent handbook.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required student and parent information (pages 1-7).
2. Select "Yes" or "No" regarding the handbook being attached to the form (page 8).
3. Fill in the school year attended (page 9).
4. Enter your student's ID (page 10).
5. Enter your phone number (page 11).
6. (Optional) Enter your school email address (page 12).
7. (Optional) Enter your school address (page 13).
8. Leave the signature field blank as it's meant for a digital signature (page 14).
9. (Optional) Fill in the date and time you signed the form (pages 15-16).
10. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have read and signed this form (page 17).
11. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have read and signed this form (page 18).
12. (Optional) Enter your school's address (page 19-20).
13. (Optional) Enter your school's city (page 21).
14. (Optional) Enter your school's state (page 22).
15. (Optional) Enter your school's zip code (page 23).
16. (Optional) Enter your school's country (page 24).
17. Fill in the current form version (page 25).
18. (Optional) Fill in the date and time you signed this form (page 26).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (page 1)**: Enter the name of your child as it appears on their records.
* **Parent Name (page 2)**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian of your child.
* **Signature Date (page 3)**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Handbook Attached (page 4)**: Indicate if the handbook is attached to this form.
* **School Year (page 5)**: Enter the current school year your child is attending.
* **Student ID (page 6)**: Enter your child's ID as per the school records.
* **Parent Phone (page 7)**: Enter your phone number.
* **School Email (page 8)**: (Optional) Enter your school email address.
* **School Address (page 9)**: (Optional) Enter your school address.
* **Digital Signature (page 10)**: Leave this field blank.
* **Date Signed (page 11)**: Leave this field blank.
* **Acknowledgement (page 12)**: Read and acknowledge the contents of the parent handbook.
* **Form Version (page 13)**: Fill in the current form version.
* **Acknowledge Date (page 14)**: Fill in the date you signed this form.
* **Acknowledge Time (page 15)**: Leave this field blank.
* **Acknowledge User (page 16)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have read and signed this form.
* **School Email (page 17)**: Leave this field blank as it's a duplicate of School Email (page 8).
* **Parent Signature (page 18)**: Leave this field blank as it's a digital signature.
* **Form ID (page 19)**: This field is not necessary for this purpose.
* **Date Signed (page 20)**: Leave this field blank as it's meant for the date of signature.
* **Time Signed (page 20)**: Leave this field blank as it's meant for the time of signature.
* **Signature By (page 21)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have read and signed this form.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in the required fields correctly to complete this form accurately.
* Some fields are optional and do not need to be filled in.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your school administration.
