# Animal Welfare Event Coverage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from individuals involved in animal welfare events for media coverage. The purpose of this form is to ensure that participants or owners of animals involved in events are aware of the intended use and scope of the media coverage and grant permission for its use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant or owner's name in the **Participant or Owner Name** field.
2. List all animals involved in the coverage in the **Animal Names** field.
3. Enter the event name or title in the **Event Name or Title** field.
4. Select the date of the event in the **Date of Event** field.
5. Choose the authorized media types in the **Authorized Media Types** field by selecting all that apply.
6. Select where the media will be used in the **Intended Use and Scope** field by choosing all that apply.
7. Choose whether to identify participants by full name in the **Permission to Identify by Full Name** field.
8. Select the duration of the consent in the **Duration of Consent** field.
9. Read and acknowledge the revocation policy in the **Revocation Policy Acknowledgment** field.
10. Sign the **Digital Signature** field by entering your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant or Owner Name**: This field requires the participant or owner's name to be associated with the consent.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
* **Animal Names**: List all animals involved in the coverage.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
* **Event Name or Title**: Enter the name or title of the event.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes
* **Date of Event**: Select the date of the event.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: Yes
* **Authorized Media Types**: Choose all media types that apply.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: Yes
* **Intended Use and Scope**: Select all where the media will be used.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: Yes
* **Permission to Identify by Full Name**: Choose whether to identify participants by full name.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: Yes
* **Duration of Consent**: Select the duration of the consent.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: Yes
* **Revocation Policy Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge the revocation policy.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: Yes
* **Digital Signature**: Sign with your full name.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Yes

## Tips
* Please ensure that the participant or owner understands the intended use and scope of the media coverage before granting consent.
* If unsure, please consult with the event organizer or media representative.
* This form is a formal record of consent; please keep it safe and confidential.
* Note that you can revoke your consent in writing at any time if you change your mind.
