# Contractor Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contractor Release Form is a formal agreement that releases the client from any remaining financial obligations to the contractor, and the contractor from any claims against the client. This form is used to settle disputes, cancel contracts, or terminate agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Information**: Fill in the client's full name, email address, and phone number.
2. **Contractor Information**: Enter the contractor's name and address.
3. **Service Details**: Select the type of service provided and describe the service in detail.
4. **Service Location**: Enter the address where the service was performed.
5. **Release Type**: Choose the type of release, such as Mutual Release, Client Release of Contractor, or Service Cancellation.
6. **Reason for Release**: Select the reason for the release, such as Project Completed Satisfactorily, Mutual Agreement, or Unforeseen Circumstances.
7. **Release Reason Details**: Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for the release.
8. **Financial Settlement**: Enter the total contract value, amount paid to date, and outstanding balance.
9. **Payment Settlement**: Choose how the outstanding amounts will be handled.
10. **Release Terms**: Check that you release all known claims.
11. **Understanding Terms**: Confirm that you understand the terms of this release.
12. **Voluntary Agreement**: Confirm that you are signing this release voluntarily.
13. **No Coercion**: Confirm that you are not under coercion or duress.
14. **Authorized Signer**: Enter the name and title of the person signing.
15. **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signing.
16. **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the witness, if applicable.
17. **Additional Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: The full name of the client.
* **Client Email**: The email address for communication.
* **Client Phone**: The contact phone number of the client.
* **Client Address**: The full mailing address of the client.
* **Contractor Name**: The name of the contractor or company.
* **Contractor Address**: The business address of the contractor.
* **Type of Service**: The type of service provided (e.g., Home Renovation, Repair Work, etc.).
* **Service Description**: A detailed description of the service provided.
* **Service Location**: The address where the service was performed.
* **Original Contract Date**: The date when the service agreement was made.
* **Service Start Date**: The date when work began.
* **Service End Date**: The date when work was completed or terminated.
* **Release Type**: The type of release (e.g., Mutual Release, Client Release of Contractor, etc.).
* **Reason for Release**: The reason for the release.
* **Release Reason Details**: A detailed explanation of the reason for the release.
* **Total Contract Value**: The original value of the contract.
* **Amount Paid to Date**: The total amount already paid.
* **Outstanding Balance**: Any remaining amount due.
* **Payment Settlement**: How the outstanding amounts will be handled.
* **Release Claims**: Check that you release all known claims.
* **Understand Terms**: Confirm that you understand the terms of this release.
* **Voluntary Agreement**: Confirm that you are signing this release voluntarily.
* **No Coercion**: Confirm that you are not under coercion or duress.
* **Authorized Signer Name**: The name of the person signing.
* **Signer Title**: The title or relationship to the parties.
* **Signature Date**: The date of signing.
* **Witness Name**: The name of the witness, if applicable.
* **Additional Terms**: Any special conditions or agreements.

Note: This guide assumes that you have already reviewed the contract or agreement in question and are familiar with its details. It's essential to review the form carefully before signing, ensuring that all information is accurate and that both parties understand their responsibilities and obligations.
