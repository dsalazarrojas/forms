# Awesome Ink Studios Tattoo Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Awesome Ink Studios Tattoo Informed Consent Form is a document that you, as the client, must fill out before getting a tattoo. This form is designed to protect both you and our studio from potential risks associated with the tattooing process. By filling out this form, you are giving us necessary information about your health and consent to proceed with the tattoo.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Fill out all required fields, as indicated by the asterisks (\*).
3. Ensure that you have provided all necessary information, especially in the medical disclosure section.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Client Name, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Please enter your date of birth to verify that you are 18+ or have parent/guardian consent.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Please enter your valid email address where you can be reached for aftercare instructions and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Phone, Required): Please enter your phone number where we can contact you for emergencies and aftercare instructions.
* **Tattoo Description** (Text, Required): Briefly describe the tattoo design you want to get.
* **Tattoo Location on Body** (Text, Required): Specify the location on your body where you want the tattoo to be placed.
* **Artist Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your artist's name as chosen from our available artists.
* **Relevant Medical History** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check any medical conditions that apply to you. This information is crucial for your safety.
* **Are you currently under the influence of drugs or alcohol?** (Select One, Required): Please answer this question truthfully to ensure your safety during the process.
* **I consent to being tattooed by Awesome Ink Studios** (Select One, Required): You must agree to be tattooed by our studio before we can proceed.
* **I acknowledge the inherent risks of tattooing** (Select One, Required): You must understand that tattooing has potential risks, including infection, scarring, or allergic reactions.
* **I acknowledge that I have received aftercare instructions** (Select One, Required): Please confirm that you have received and understand the aftercare instructions.
* **Photo Release for Social Media and Portfolio** (Select One, Required): Please choose whether or not you give permission to share your photos on our social media or in our portfolio.
* **Client Digital Signature** (Text, Required): Type your full name to confirm your signature.
* **Date of Signature** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (Text, Required): Please provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.

Please remember to sign and date the form after completing it, then return it to us before we can begin the tattooing process.
