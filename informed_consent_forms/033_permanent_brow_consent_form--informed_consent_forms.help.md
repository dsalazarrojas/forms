# Permanent Brow Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Permanent Brow Consent Form is a document that must be completed by a client before undergoing any permanent brow services such as tattooing or microblading. The form collects information about the client, the services they wish to receive, and other relevant details. This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the **Client Info** section. This includes your name, which will be used to identify you as the client.
2. In the **Services** section, select the type of service you wish to receive (e.g., tattooing or microblading). You can choose multiple services by using the checkmark.
3. Leave the **Signature** section blank, as it will be signed by our staff after reviewing your information.
4. Enter the **Date** of your appointment, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Enter the **Time** of your appointment, in the format 24-hour clock (e.g., 10:00).
6. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used to communicate with you about your appointment.
7. Enter your **Phone** number, which will be used for any necessary follow-up communication.
8. Finally, leave the **Client Signature** section blank, as it will be signed by our staff after reviewing your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client Info**: This section collects your personal contact information.
  * **Client Info**: A text field where you can enter your name and any other identifying information.
  * **Hint**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
  * **Required/Optional**: Optional.
- **Services**: Select the service you wish to receive.
  * **Services**: A multiple-choice field where you can select one or more services.
  * **Hint**: Choose one or more of the available services.
  * **Required/Optional**: Optional.
- **Signature**: This section is for our staff to sign after reviewing your information.
  * **Signature**: A note field where our staff will sign after reviewing your information.
  * **Hint**: Leave blank.
  * **Required/Optional**: Optional.
- **Date**: Enter the date of your appointment.
  * **Date**: A date field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
  * **Hint**: Enter the date of your appointment.
  * **Required/Optional**: Optional.
- **Time**: Enter the time of your appointment.
  * **Time**: A time field in the format 24-hour clock (e.g., 10:00).
  * **Hint**: Enter the time of your appointment.
  * **Required/Optional**: Optional.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
  * **Email**: An email field.
  * **Hint**: Enter your email address.
  * **Required/Optional**: Optional.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  * **Phone**: A text field for your phone number.
  * **Hint**: Enter your phone number.
