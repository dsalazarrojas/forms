# Real Estate ESG Data Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Real Estate ESG Data Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from data owners for the use of their real estate ESG data. This form ensures that data owners are aware of how their data will be used and stored.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your Name in the **Name** field, as it is a required field to identify you as the data owner.
2.  Enter your Email in the **Email** field, this will allow us to keep you updated about how your data will be used and stored.
3.  Select the purposes for which you consent to use your ESG data. You can choose from the following options:
	*   **Data for Reporting**: This option is for data that will be used for reporting purposes, such as compliance or audit reports.
	*   **Data for Analysis**: This option is for data that will be used for analysis, such as for identifying trends or patterns.
	*   **Data for Communications**: This option is for data that will be used for marketing or promotional activities, such as for advertising or public relations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (text, required): Your full name as the data owner.
*   **Email** (email, required): Your email address for future contact and communication.
*   **ESG Data Use Consent** (select multiple, optional):
    +   **Data for Reporting**: You consent to use your real estate ESG data for reporting purposes.
    +   **Data for Analysis**: You consent to use your real estate ESG data for analysis purposes.
    +   **Data for Communications**: You consent to use your real estate ESG data for communications or marketing activities.
