# social_media_policy_acceptance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media policy acceptance form is used to obtain acceptance from employees on our company's social media policy. This form requires employees to provide their email address, phone number, and other relevant information. The form is required for all employees to complete, and the information collected will be used for internal purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the social media policy provided in this form.
2. Answer all the questions in this form accurately and completely.
3. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Choose the social media platforms where you are active or intend to be active.
5. Review and agree to the terms of the social media policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **social media policy** (1): Please provide a clear and concise answer to the social media policy provided in the form. This is a required field and is marked with an asterisk (\*).
* **email** (2): Enter your email address that you use for your work account. This is a required field and is marked with an asterisk (\*).
* **phone** (3): Enter your work phone number. This is a required field and is marked with an asterisk (\*).
* **social media usernames** (4): List all social media usernames that you use for work purposes. Enter each username separately, e.g., "username1, username2, username3".
* **social media platforms** (5): Select all the social media platforms where you are active or intend to be active. Choose from the following options: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, Snapchat, and TikTok. This field is optional.
* **company name** (6): Enter your company name as it appears on your business card or other official documents. This field is optional.
* **employee id** (7): Enter your employee ID number. This is a required field and is marked with an asterisk (\*).
* **date** (8): Enter the current date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD". This is a required field and is marked with an asterisk (\*).
* **time** (9): Enter the current time in the format "HH:MM AM/PM". This is a required field and is marked with an asterisk (\*).
* **note** (10): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to include. This field is optional.
