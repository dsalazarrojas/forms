# Human Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining informed consent from patients or participants in various studies, research, or treatments. Fill out the form accurately to ensure their privacy and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully to understand what each field requires.
2. Fill out the fields with accurate and honest information.
3. Review the form before signing to ensure it's completed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the consent form.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the following: home address, email, phone number, or social media.
- **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you consented to participate in the study, research, or treatment.
- **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you consented to participate in the study, research, or treatment.
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the patient or participant.
- **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the patient or participant.
- **Patient Contact Number** (`patient_contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact number of the patient or participant.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
- **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, date, required: false): Enter the date of consent.
- **Time of Consent** (`time_of_consent`, time, required: false): Enter the time of consent.
- **Consent Form Version** (`consent_form_version`, text, required: false): Enter the version of the consent form you're using.
