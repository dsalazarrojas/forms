# property_value_study_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from property owners and researchers to participate in a property value study.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

1. Complete the **Study Participant Details** page:
	* Enter your **Property Owner** name.
	* Select your **Property Manager**.
	* Provide your **Researcher Details**.
2. Move on to the **Property Details** page:
	* Enter your **Address**.
	* Select your **City**.
	* Enter your **State**.
	* Enter your **Zip**.
	* Select your **Country**.
	* Enter your **Property Number**.
3. On the **Study Purpose** page:
	* Select the **Survey Type** (e.g., Valuation, Market Study, Feasibility Study).
4. On the **Agreement** page:
	* Select whether you **Agree** or **Disagree** to participate.
	* Select the date you **Agreed** (if you agree).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Participant Details**:
	+ **Property Owner**: Enter your name as the property owner.
	+ **Property Manager**: Select your property manager from the options.
	+ **Researcher Details**: Provide details about the researcher.
* **Property Details**:
	+ **Address**: Enter your address.
	+ **City**: Enter your city.
	+ **State**: Enter your state.
	+ **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
	+ **Country**: Select your country from the options.
	+ **Property Number**: Enter your property number.
* **Study Purpose**:
	+ **Survey Type**: Select the type of survey (e.g., Valuation, Market Study, Feasibility Study).
* **Agreement**:
	+ **Participant Agreed**: Select whether you agree or disagree to participate.
	+ **Participant Agreed Date**: Select the date you agreed to participate (if you agreed).
	+ **Researcher Agreed**: Select whether the researcher agrees or disagrees to participate.
	+ **Researcher Agreed Date**: Select the date the researcher agreed to participate (if they agreed).
* **Property Value**:
	+ **Property Value**: Enter the property value.
* **Participant Comments**:
	+ **Participant Comments**: Enter any comments you have about the study.
* **Currency**:
	+ **Property Value Currency**: Select the currency for the property value (e.g., USD, CAD).
* **Study Date**:
	+ **Survey Date**: Select the date of the survey.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out. If a field is marked as optional, it is not required to be filled out.
