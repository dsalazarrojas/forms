# Pedagogical Innovation Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pedagogical Innovation Showcase Consent Form is a crucial step in the process of sharing innovative ideas and projects between educators. This form is used to collect user information, project details, and consent to participate in the showcase.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Info**: Enter your name and any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
2. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your project.
3. **School**: Select your school from the options provided.
4. **Project**: Select up to three projects you'd like to showcase.
5. **Category**: Select up to three categories for your project.
6. **Consent**: Check the box to indicate your consent to participate in the showcase.
7. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
8. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
9. **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your project or your experience with the showcase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your project.
* **School**: Select the school where you are affiliated with.
* **Project**: Select up to three projects you'd like to showcase. Please note that this field is a number field, so you can enter up to three projects.
* **Category**: Select up to three categories for your project.
* **Consent**: Check the box to indicate your consent to participate in the showcase.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your project or your experience with the showcase.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your project is properly showcased.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to give us a clear understanding of your project.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
