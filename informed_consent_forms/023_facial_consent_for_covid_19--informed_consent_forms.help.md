## Purpose
This form is used for obtaining client consent for various treatments, taking into account the COVID-19 pandemic context. It's essential to have a clear understanding of the client's consent and treatment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and consent from the client before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all required fields have been filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): The date on which the client gave their consent for the treatment. 
* **Treatment Provider** (`treatment_provider`, select_one, optional): The provider who will be performing the treatment. Options include "Client's Choice", "Salon's Choice", and "Spa's Choice".
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, select_multiple, required): The client's consent to the treatment. Options include "Yes" and "No".
* **Client Signature Date** (`client_signature_date`, date, required): The date on which the client signs their consent.
* **Provider Signature Date** (`provider_signature_date`, date, required): The date on which the provider signs the consent.
* **Treatments Consent Notes** (`treatment_consent_notes`, note, optional): Additional notes about the client's consent, if necessary.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): The client's full name.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required): The client's address or contact information.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, select_one, required): The type of treatment the client is consenting to. Options include "Facial", "Haircut", and "Massage".
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): The method of contact information. Options include "Email" and "Phone".
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The client's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): The client's phone number.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, optional): Additional notes about the client.
* **Provider Notes** (`provider_notes`, note, optional): Additional notes about the client's treatment and consent from the provider.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely, and review it before submitting it to ensure that all required fields have been filled out correctly. If you're unsure about any information, refer to your organization's guidelines or seek clarification from a supervisor.
