# peptide_therapy_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to provide informed consent for peptide therapy, a type of medication or treatment that involves the use of peptides to manage various medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the patient information section with your personal details.
2. Select the type of therapy you are receiving.
3. Provide your medical history and any preexisting conditions.
4. Date and sign the consent form to confirm your understanding and agreement to the therapy.
5. Sign the form as the patient and your doctor to confirm your consent.
6. Provide the clinic information for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This section collects the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Therapy Type** (`therapy_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of peptide therapy you are receiving. Options are Peptide Therapy and Other.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical history, including any preexisting conditions or allergies.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Date and sign the consent form to confirm your understanding and agreement to the therapy.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required): Sign the form as the patient to confirm your consent.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Sign the form as the patient to confirm your consent.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required): Sign the form as the doctor or healthcare provider to confirm your consent.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the clinic or healthcare facility.
* **Clinic Address** (`clinic_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the clinic or healthcare facility.
* **Clinic Phone** (`clinic_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the clinic or healthcare facility.
* **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`, text, required): Read and understand the consent statement carefully before signing.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Medical History Conditions** (`medical_history_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (`other_medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medication you are currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions Treated** (`medical_conditions_treated`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you are currently treating.
* **Preexisting Medical Conditions** (`preexisting_medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any preexisting medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Conditions Surgical** (`medical_conditions_surgical`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have that require surgical treatment.

## Tips
* Please read the consent statement carefully before signing the form.
* Ensure that you understand the therapy and its risks before giving consent.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your doctor or healthcare provider.
* Sign the form as the patient and your doctor or healthcare provider to confirm your consent.
