# Remote Project Collaboration Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Project Collaboration Disclosure Consent Form is designed to collect information about your remote project collaboration. This form aims to gather essential details about you and the project, ensuring transparency and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, go through each page carefully and fill in the required fields. Ensure that all fields are completed, as the form is designed to capture essential details about your collaboration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Project Info**: Enter the project title and description in this field. This is the first page of the form.
    *   Enter the project title and description in this field.
*   **Contact Details**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and other relevant contact information in this field.
    *   Ensure that your contact details are accurate and up-to-date.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address in this field.
    *   Enter your email address correctly.
*   **Role**: Select your role in the project from the provided options.
    *   Choose the correct role from the options.
*   **Location**: Select your location from the options provided.
    *   Choose the correct location for your collaboration.
*   **Date**: Enter the date for which you want to report your collaboration details.
    *   Enter the correct date.
*   **Time**: Choose the time you want to report your collaboration details.
    *   Select the correct time.
*   **Note**: Enter a note about your collaboration details in this field.
    *   Add a note about your collaboration, if necessary.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field.
    *   Enter your phone number correctly.
*   **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are collaborating on.
    *   Enter the correct project name.
*   **Project Status**: Select the status of the project from the provided options.
    *   Choose the correct status.
*   **Collaboration Type**: Select the type of collaboration you are undertaking from the options provided.
    *   Choose the correct type of collaboration.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact the project lead for clarification.
*   This form is designed to capture essential information about your remote project collaboration. Ensure that you fill out all required fields to avoid any errors.
