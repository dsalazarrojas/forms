# Offer To Purchase Real Estate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for a buyer and a realtor to collaborate on the purchase of real estate. It captures essential information to facilitate a clear understanding of the agreement between the parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is required and why.
2. Fill in the required fields as specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the buyer.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the property you are interested in purchasing.
* **Property Type** (`real_estate_type`, `text`, required: false): Select the type of property you are interested in (e.g., house, apartment, etc.).
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, `number`, required: false): Enter the square footage of the property.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the property.
* **Earnest Money** (`earnest_monies`, `number`, required: false): Enter the earnest money you are willing to provide.
* **Buyer Contact Number** (`buyer_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number.
* **Realto Contact Number** (`realtor_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the realtor's contact number.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Realto Email** (`realtor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the realtor's email address.
* **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Realto Phone** (`realtor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the realtor's phone number.
* **Buyer Message** (`buyer_message`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or messages for the realtor.
* **Realto Message** (`realtor_message`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or messages for the buyer.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required: false): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the sale.
* **Buyer Agreement** (`buyer_agreement`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the buyer's agreement with the terms and conditions. Options: Buyer Acknowledgement and Agreement, Buyer Acknowledgement and Agreement 1, Buyer Acknowledgement and Agreement 2.
* **Realto Agreement** (`realtor_agreement`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the realtor's agreement with the terms and conditions. Options: Realto Acknowledgement and Agreement, Realto Acknowledgement and Agreement 1, Realto Acknowledgement and Agreement 2.
* **Buyer Note** (`buyer_note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments for the realtor.
* **Realto Note** (`realtor_note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments for the buyer.
* **Buyer Date** (`buyer_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you intend to sign the agreement.
* **Realto Date** (`realtor_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the realtor intends to sign the agreement.
* **Buyer Time** (`buyer_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you intend to sign the agreement.
* **Realto Time** (`realtor_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the realtor intends to sign the agreement.
* **Buyer Sign Date** (`buyer_sign_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you sign the agreement.
* **Realto Sign Date** (`realtor_sign_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the realtor signs the agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and agree to the terms and conditions carefully before signing the agreement.
* Ensure that both you and the realtor are on the same page with your agreements.
* Double-check your contact information and ensure it is accurate.
* Keep in mind that this form is for information purposes only and is not a binding agreement.
