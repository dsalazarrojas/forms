# Skin Care Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Care Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from clients before proceeding with skin care procedures. It serves as a legal and procedural record of the client's understanding and acceptance of the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Procedure Description** (Page 1, id: 1): Enter a clear and concise description of the skin care procedure that you will be performing. This should include information about what the procedure entails, its expected duration, and any potential risks or side effects.

2. **Client Name** (Page 2, id: 2): Enter the client's full name. This is the primary contact information for the client.

3. **Client Contact Info** (Page 3, id: 3): Enter the client's contact information, such as phone number and email address. This information will be used to reach the client in case of any post-procedure follow-up or emergency.

4. **Consent Given** (Page 4, id: 4): Select 'Yes' if the client has given their consent for the procedure. Select 'No' if they have declined or not provided their consent.

5. **Date of Consent** (Page 5, id: 5): Enter the date on which the client provided their informed consent.

6. **Dermatologist Name** (Page 6, id: 6): Enter your name as the treating dermatologist.

7. **Client Signature** (Page 7, id: 7): The client should sign this field to acknowledge that they have read and understood the procedure description and have given their informed consent.

8. **Dermatologist Signature** (Page 8, id: 8): As the treating dermatologist, you should sign this field to confirm that you have explained the procedure and risks to the client and have received their informed consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Procedure Description** (Page 1, id: 1): A detailed description of the skin care procedure, including information about what to expect and any potential risks or side effects.
* **Client Name** (Page 2, id: 2): The client's full name, which will be used for documentation and communication purposes.
* **Client Contact Info** (Page 3, id: 3): The client's contact information, such as phone number and email address, to facilitate post-procedure follow-up and emergency contact.
* **Consent Given** (Page 4, id: 4): A yes or no answer indicating whether the client has given their informed consent for the procedure.
* **Date of Consent** (Page 5, id: 5): The date on which the client provided their informed consent.
* **Dermatologist Name** (Page 6, id: 6): The name of the treating dermatologist who will be performing the procedure.
* **Client Signature** (Page 7, id: 7): The client's signature confirming that they have read and understood the procedure description and have given their informed consent.
* **Dermatologist Signature** (Page 8, id: 8): The treating dermatologist's signature confirming that they have explained the procedure and risks to the client and have received their informed consent.

## Tips
* Ensure that the client has a clear understanding of the procedure and risks before obtaining their consent.
* Explain the risks and benefits of the procedure to the client and ensure they have read and signed the consent form.
* Keep a record of the client's consent for future reference and in case of any legal issues.
