# Online Learning Parental Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents or guardians to give consent for their child to participate in our online learning programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your child's name in the **parent_name** field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the **email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **parent_name**: Enter your child's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding your child's online learning progress.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information for your child's name and email address.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
