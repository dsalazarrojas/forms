# global_health_research_partnership_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals or organizations interested in collaborating on global health research initiatives. It collects contact details, research partnership information, and other relevant data to establish a clear understanding of the partnership and its roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant contact information for the partnership (Global Health Research Partnership).
2. Enter the title and description of the research project.
3. Provide the objectives of the research project.
4. Choose the location where the research project will take place.
5. Enter the date and time the research project is expected to start.
6. Add your phone number and email address for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact information**: Select the relevant contact information for the partnership (Global Health Research Partnership).
  * Options: Yes, No
* **research partnership**: Enter the title of the research project.
* **research title**: Enter a brief description of the research project.
* **research objectives**: Enter the objectives of the research project.
* **location**: Choose the location where the research project will take place.
  * Options: Select One, Another Option
* **date**: Enter the date the research project is expected to start.
* **time**: Enter the time the research project is expected to start.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
