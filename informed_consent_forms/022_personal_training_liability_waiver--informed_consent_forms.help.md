# Personal Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information necessary for informed consent from individuals participating in training programs. The form is essential for the training organization to protect itself and the individual from potential legal liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* Provide your current address.
* Enter your email address (if you have one).
* Enter your date of birth.
* Enter your phone number (if you have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Your current address, which is essential for contacting you in case of an emergency or if we need to reach you.
* **Email**: Your personal email address, which is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth, which helps us verify your identity.
* **Phone**: Your phone number, which is optional but recommended for emergency contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult the relevant documentation or seek guidance from our staff.
