# social_responsibility_research_interview_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather informed consent from research participants in social responsibility research interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page and ensure you understand the questions and their purpose.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_permission** (`participant_permission`, text, required/optional): This field allows you to indicate your permission to participate in the research interview. Please type a short message indicating your willingness to participate.
* **signature** (`signature`, date, required/optional): This field is for signing the consent form, indicating that you have read and understood the research information and agree to participate.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please provide your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing any additional contact information or notes that you think would be helpful for us.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Please provide your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for your name.
* **organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): If you are representing an organization, please provide the name of your organization.
* **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): If you are representing an organization, please provide the name of the contact person.
* **participant_address** (`participant_address`, text, required/optional): This field is for your physical address.
* **participant_zip** (`participant_zip`, text, required/optional): This field is for your postal code.

Note: Fields marked as "required/optional" can be either filled out or left blank, depending on the specific needs of your participation in the research.
