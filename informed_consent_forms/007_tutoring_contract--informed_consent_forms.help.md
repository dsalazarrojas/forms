# Tutoring Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutoring Contract form is designed to document the terms and conditions of the tutoring relationship between a client and a tutor. It is used to ensure that both parties have a clear understanding of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Client Info section with your personal details.
2. Enter your Client Name and email address.
3. Provide your Tutor's Info, including their name.
4. Review and agree to the Contract Terms.
5. Sign and date the Client Signature and Tutor Signature sections.
6. Select the Review section to indicate who will be reviewing the contract (Client, Tutor, or Both).
7. Add any additional review notes in the Review Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info**: Enter your personal details, such as your name and email address.
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as the client.
* **email**: Enter your email address associated with your name.
* **Tutor Info**: Enter your tutor's name.
* **Contract Terms**: Review and agree to the terms outlined in this section.
* **description**: A brief description of the contract terms.
* **agreement**: Check the box to indicate your agreement to the contract terms.
* **Client Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement.
* **Tutor Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm the tutor's agreement.
* **Review**: Select who will be reviewing the contract (Client, Tutor, or Both).
* **review_description**: A brief description of the review process.
* **review_date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **review_time**: Enter the time of the review.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number associated with your name.
* **review**: A section for additional review notes.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the contract.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of the contract.
* **frequency**: A description of the tutoring frequency.
* **termination**: A description of the contract termination policy.
* **renewal**: A description of the contract renewal policy.
* **cancellation**: A description of the contract cancellation policy.
* **reviewed**: Check the box to indicate if you have reviewed the contract.
* **reviewed**: Check the box to indicate if the tutor has reviewed the contract.
* **notes**: Add any additional review notes.
