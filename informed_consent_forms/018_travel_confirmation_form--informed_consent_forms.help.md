# Travel Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm travel arrangements with customers and gather essential information for our travel agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in all the required fields with accurate information about your travel plans.
2.  Choose the correct travel agency and airline from the provided options.
3.  Select the method of travel confirmation (phone, email, or text).
4.  Check the confirmation box to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
5.  Enter your phone number and email address if applicable.
6.  Add any additional notes about your travel plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agency** (travel_agency): Enter the name of the travel agency you are traveling with.
* **Customer Name** (customer_name): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Travel Date** (travel_date): Enter the date of your travel in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Travel Time** (travel_time): Enter the time of your travel in the format (HH:MM).
* **Travel Airline** (travel_airline): Select the airline you will be flying with.
* **Travel Hotel** (travel_hotel): Select the hotel you will be staying at.
* **Travel Confirmation Method** (travel_confirmation_method): Choose how you would like to receive confirmation (phone, email, or text).
* **Travel Confirmation Date** (travel_confirmation_date): Enter the date you expect to receive confirmation.
* **Customer Confirmation** (customer_confirmation): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone): Enter your phone number if you want to receive confirmation by phone.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email): Enter your email address if you want to receive confirmation by email.
* **Customer Notes** (customer_notes): Add any additional notes about your travel plans.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your travel agent for assistance.
