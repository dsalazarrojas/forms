# personal_trainer_client_intake_and_liability_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personal trainer client intake and liability form is a document that must be filled out by each client before starting a personal training program. It collects information about the client's health details, risk assessment, emergency contact information, insurance information, and informed consent to participate in the program.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. You will find a brief explanation of each field below.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Intake** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can enter any medical conditions or concerns you may have. Please provide any information you think is relevant to your training.
- **Health Details** (`2`, `text`, optional): In this field, please provide any existing medical conditions or health concerns you have that may affect your training. This includes any surgeries, allergies, or health conditions you may have.
- **Risk Assessment** (`3`, `text`, optional): Here you can describe any risks or hazards that you believe may affect your training. This includes any physical limitations or concerns you may have.
- **Informed Consent** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Please select 'True' if you have read and understand the risks involved in the personal training program and are willing to participate. Select 'False' if you have not read or do not understand the risks.
- **Emergency Contact Info** (`5`, `text`, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
- **Contact Info** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and contact information, including phone number and email.
- **Medical History** (`7`, `text`, optional): Describe any past medical history that may be relevant to your training.
- **Fitness Level** (`8`, `text`, optional): Describe your current fitness level, including any exercise experience or past athletic achievements.
- **Goal Setting** (`9`, `text`, optional): Set your goals for the personal training program, including any specific goals or objectives you have.
- **Physical Activity Level** (`10`, `text`, optional): Describe your current level of physical activity, including any regular exercise or activity levels.
- **Emergency Plan** (`11`, `text`, optional): Describe your emergency plan, including any steps to be taken in case of an injury or medical emergency.
- **Insurance Info** (`12`, `text`, optional): Provide insurance information, including policy number and provider.
- **Waiver** (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'True' if you have signed and returned the waiver provided, and 'False' if you have not.

Note: This help guide only covers the fields for the personal trainer client intake and liability form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the personal trainer.
