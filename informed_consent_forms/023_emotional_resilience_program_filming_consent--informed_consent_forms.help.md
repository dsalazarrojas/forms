# emotional_resilience_program_filming_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants to film activities related to an emotional resilience program. This includes activities such as filming at home, school, or in other locations, using various instruments such as cameras, microphones, or video recorders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what you are consenting to.
2. Answer all questions and select relevant options.
3. Provide your email address for contact purposes.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Filming Consent (1)**: This is the main consent section. Please indicate your agreement to participate in filming activities.
* **Filming Instruments (2)**: Select the instruments you are comfortable with being filmed with, such as camera, microphone, or video recorder.
* **Filming Location (3)**: Choose the location where the filming will take place, such as home, school, wellness program, or outdoors.
* **Filming Schedule (4)**: This is where you can input your availability for filming, using the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Informed Consent (5)**: This is a note section for you to provide any additional information or questions you may have about the filming process.
* **Contact Info (6)**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for any further questions or updates.
* **Recording Consent (7)**: Please note your willingness to be recorded, if you do not want to be recorded, please let us know and we will make arrangements accordingly.
* **Filming Consent Signature (8)**: Please provide a handwritten or digital signature to confirm your consent.
