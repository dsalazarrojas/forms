# Fitness Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Informed Consent Form is a crucial document that helps ensure you understand your responsibilities and expectations when participating in a fitness program. This form guides you through the necessary information to provide informed consent for the program you are about to begin.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. If you are unsure about any aspect of the program or have questions, please contact the fitness professional responsible for your program for clarification.
3. Ensure that you understand the implications of providing this information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport. This is a required field.
* **Program Details**: Describe your fitness goals, the program you are interested in, and any specific requirements or limitations you have (e.g., injuries, health conditions). This is an optional field.

* **Client Information**: Choose the type of client information that best describes you:
	+ Information 1
	+ Information 2
	+ Information 3
* **Program Duration**: Enter the number of weeks or sessions you expect to participate in the program. This is an optional field.
* **Program Frequency**: Select the frequency of your workouts:
	+ Frequency 1
	+ Frequency 2
	+ Frequency 3
* **Client Sign Date**: Enter the date you are scheduled to start the program. This is an optional field.
* **Client Sign Time**: Enter the time you are scheduled to start the program. This is an optional field.
* **Fitness Professional Information**: If you have any relevant medical information or concerns regarding your fitness program, please provide details here. This is an optional field.
* **Client Consent**: Acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the program. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have read and understood all the information provided before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the fitness professional responsible for your program for clarification.
* Remember to review your answers carefully and provide accurate information to ensure that you and the fitness professional are on the same page regarding your fitness goals and program details.
