# Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from shelter residents for participation in various assistance programs and activities. The form helps ensure that residents understand and agree to the terms and conditions of their participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and understand each section.
2.  Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3.  Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form Title**: This is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled and does not require an answer.
* **Category**: This field is used to categorize the consent type, e.g., "Yes" or "No". You must select one of the provided options to indicate your consent status.
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form Description**: This field is for the shelter staff to briefly describe the purpose and scope of the form. Please provide a clear and accurate description.
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form Assistance Programs**: This field allows you to select one or multiple programs you would like to participate in. If you do not select any programs, select "None" to indicate that you do not want to participate.
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form Signed Consent**: This field is used to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the form. Select "Yes" if you have read and agreed to the terms.
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form Date Of Birth**: This field requires you to enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form Date Of Admission**: This field requires you to enter the date of your admission to the shelter in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form Resident Id**: This field requires you to enter a unique resident ID, which is used to identify you in the shelter's database.
* **Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form Resident Id**: This field is a duplicate of the previous field, and it's likely a mistake in the form design. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your ID status.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before completing it.
* If you are unsure about any section, please ask shelter staff for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact shelter administration.
