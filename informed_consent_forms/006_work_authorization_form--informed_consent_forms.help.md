# Work Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain work authorization for employees, allowing for the validation of their employment status and work location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Select the correct employment status from the options provided.
3. Enter the date of the work authorization in the provided field.
4. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter the name of the company you are employed by or associated with.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your current job title or role.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date Of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your current address where you live and work.
* **Work Location** (Work Location, text, required): Enter the location where you work.
* **Nationality** (Nationality, text, required): Enter your country of origin or nationality.
* **Employment Status** (Employment Status, select_one, required): Select your current employment status from the provided options.
* **Work Authorization Date** (Work Authorization Date, date, required): Enter the date when this form is authorized for work.
* **Work Authorization Date** (Work Authorization Date, date, required): Enter the date when this form is authorized for work.
