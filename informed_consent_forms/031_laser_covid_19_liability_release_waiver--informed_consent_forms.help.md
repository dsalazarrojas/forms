# Laser Covid 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent and medical information from individuals who are about to undergo a medical procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and review the form.
2. Answer all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. Make sure to provide your correct contact information.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your doctor or healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **i_agree**: Select 'Yes' if you agree to the terms and conditions of the procedure. If you do not agree, select 'No'.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your agreement to the terms and conditions of the procedure.
* **first_name**: Enter your first name.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your name for medical records.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your name for medical records.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	+ Type: `date`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your date of birth for medical records.
* **address**: Enter your current address.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your contact information for emergency purposes.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
	+ Type: `email`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your contact email for emergency purposes.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your contact phone number for emergency purposes.
* **medical_condition**: Select 'Yes' if you have any medical conditions that may be relevant to the procedure. If you do not have any medical conditions, select 'No'.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Purpose: To obtain information about your medical conditions.
* **allergies**: Enter any allergies or medical conditions that you may have.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain information about your allergies or medical conditions.
* **emergency_contact_name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain the contact information of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Enter the relationship of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain the relationship of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain the contact phone number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_address**: Enter the address of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain the contact address of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_email**: Enter the email address of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	+ Type: `email`
	+ Purpose: To obtain the contact email of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_permission**: Select 'Yes' if you give permission to contact the emergency contact.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Purpose: To obtain permission to contact the emergency contact.
* **medical_insurance**: Select 'Yes' if you have medical insurance. If you do not have medical insurance, select 'No'.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Purpose: To obtain information about your medical insurance.
* **consent**: Provide your signature indicating that you have read and understand the information presented.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your informed consent.
* **signature**: Provide your signature indicating that you have read and understand the information presented.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your signature.
* **signature_date**: Enter the date you signed the form.
	+ Type: `date`
	+ Purpose: To obtain the date you signed the form.
* **signature_time**: Enter the time you signed the form.
	+ Type: `time`
	+ Purpose: To obtain the time you signed the form.
* **signed_by**: Enter your name as it appears on the signature.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain your name as it appears on the signature.
* **date_signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.
	+ Type: `date`
	+ Purpose: To obtain the date you signed the form.
* **time_signed**: Enter the time you signed the form.
	+ Type: `time`
	+ Purpose: To obtain the time you signed the form.
* **medical_procedure**: Describe the medical procedure you are about to undergo.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain information about the medical procedure.
* **medical_procedure_details**: Provide any additional information about the medical procedure.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Purpose: To obtain any additional information about the medical procedure.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before signing.
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your doctor or healthcare provider for clarification.
* Please note that this form is for medical purposes only and should not be used for any other purposes.
