# Radiofrequency Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Radiofrequency Treatment Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for radiofrequency treatment. This form ensures that patients understand the risks and benefits of the treatment and are willing to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the patient's address in the `address` field.
5. Enter the patient's phone number in the `phone_number` field.
6. Enter the patient's email address in the `email_address` field.
7. Select whether you confirm or do not confirm your consent in the `consent` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (required, text): This is the patient's first name.
* **last_name** (required, text): This is the patient's last name.
* **date_of_birth** (required, date): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **address** (required, text): This is the patient's address.
* **phone_number** (required, text): This is the patient's phone number.
* **email_address** (required, email): Enter the patient's email address.
* **consent** (required, select_one): Select whether you confirm or do not confirm your consent for radiofrequency treatment.
