# Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from facility owners or managers to publish their energy data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully, providing as much detail as you need to provide accurate information.
2. Make sure that the fields marked as "required" are filled out, even if they are optional. If a field is marked as "required", please fill it out.
3. Review the form to ensure that all the necessary information is provided before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: Please enter the name of the facility, e.g., "Facility XYZ".
* **Facility Energy Data**: Please select one of the options: "Publish" or "Do Not Publish".
* **Research and Benchmarking**: Please select one or multiple options: "Research", "Benchmarking", "Both".
* **Publishing Method**: Please select one of the options: "Reporting", "Benchmarking", "Research".
* **Reporting Frequency**: Please select one of the options: "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Annually".
* **Data Ending**: Please enter the date up to which the data should be published.
* **Consent Date**: Please enter the date of signing this form.
* **Facility Contact**: Enter the contact person's name and other relevant information for the facility.
* **Facility Email**: Enter the email address of the facility.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility Representative**: Enter the representative's name for the facility.
* **Facility Representative Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility representative.
* **Facility Representative Email**: Enter the email address of the facility representative.
* **Facility Representative Address**: Enter the address of the facility representative.
* **Facility Representative Job Title**: Enter the job title of the facility representative.
* **Facility Representative Relationship**: Please select one of the options: "Authorized Person", "Facility Manager", "Data Analyst".
* **Facility Representative Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information for the facility representative.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization.
* **Organization Address**: Enter the address of the organization.
* **Organization Contact**: Enter the contact person's name and other relevant information for the organization.
* **Organization Email**: Enter the email address of the organization.
* **Organization Phone**: Enter the phone number of the organization.
* **Organization Representative**: Enter the representative's name for the organization.
* **Organization Representative Email**: Enter the email address of the organization representative.

Please note that some fields might be marked as "required" or "optional" depending on the context, so please review the form carefully before submitting it.
