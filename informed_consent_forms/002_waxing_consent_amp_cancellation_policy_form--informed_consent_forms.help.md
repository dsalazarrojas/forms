# Waxing Consent & Cancellation Policy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to inform customers about the waxing services provided by your business, including the service process, requirements, and payment methods. It also outlines the cancellation policy and any associated fees. The form aims to establish a clear understanding of your services and policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please select the service(s) you wish to wax**:
	* Fill in the text box to describe the waxing services you wish to use.
2. **Please review the cancellation policy**:
	* Fill in the text box with your company's cancellation policy.
3. **Describe our service process**:
	* Explain the steps and procedures your business follows for the waxing services.
4. **List our service requirements**:
	* Describe any specific requirements you have for customers to follow.
5. **Choose your payment methods**:
	* List the accepted payment methods for your services.
6. **Outline our cancellation fees**:
	* Describe any fees associated with cancelling services.
7. **Get in touch with us**:
	* Leave a text box for customers to contact your business with any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waxing Services** (`waxing_services`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where customers can describe the waxing services they want to use.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, text, optional):
	+ A text field for customers to review your company's cancellation policy.
* **Service Process** (`service_process`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can describe the steps followed by your business for the waxing services.
* **Requirements** (`requirements`, text, optional):
	+ A text field for customers to read and follow any specific requirements for your services.
* **Payment Methods** (`payment_methods`, text, optional):
	+ A text field for customers to see the payment options accepted by your business.
* **Cancellation Fees** (`cancellation_fees`, text, optional):
	+ A text field for customers to understand any fees associated with cancelling services.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, text, optional):
	+ A text field for customers to contact your business with any questions or concerns.
