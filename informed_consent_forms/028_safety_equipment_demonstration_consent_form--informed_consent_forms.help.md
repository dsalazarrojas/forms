# Safety Equipment Demonstration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on safety equipment demonstrations to ensure accurate and reliable information for future reference and safety evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the participant's information (name and organization)
2. Specify the location where the demonstration was held
3. Enter the instructor's name and organization
4. Answer questions about the safety equipment used and its condition
5. Provide the demonstration date and time
6. Enter the duration of the demonstration
7. Specify the number of participants
8. Answer questions about the safety equipment inspection and testing
9. Mention any other equipment used during the demonstration
10. Review and sign the informed consent section

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Organization** (`participant_organization`, text, required): Please enter the participant's organization.
* **Demonstration Location** (`demonstration_location`, text, required): Specify the location where the demonstration was held.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Please enter the instructor's name.
* **Instructor Organization** (`instructor_organization`, text, required): Please enter the instructor's organization.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, note, required): Please acknowledge that you have received informed consent from the participant.
* **Safety Equipment Used** (`safety_equipment_used`, text, required): List the safety equipment used during the demonstration.
* **Demonstration Date** (`demonstration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the demonstration.
* **Demonstration Time** (`demonstration_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the demonstration.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of the demonstration.
* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, required): Enter the number of participants.
* **Safety Equipment Condition** (`safety_equipment_condition`, text, required): Describe the condition of the safety equipment used.
* **Other Equipment Used** (`other_equipment_used`, text, required): List any other equipment used during the demonstration.
* **Was the Safety Equipment Damaged?** (`safety_equipment_damaged`, select_one, required): Answer if the safety equipment was damaged during the demonstration.
* **Was the Safety Equipment Maintained?** (`safety_equipment_maintained`, select_one, required): Answer if the safety equipment was maintained before use.
* **Was the Safety Equipment Inspected?** (`safety_equipment_inspected`, select_one, required): Answer if the safety equipment was inspected before use.
* **Was the Safety Equipment Tested?** (`safety_equipment_tested`, select_one, required): Answer if the safety equipment was tested before use.
* **Was the Safety Equipment Certified?** (`safety_equipment_certified`, select_one, required): Answer if the safety equipment was certified for use.
* **Was the Safety Equipment Deteriorated?** (`safety_equipment_deteriorated`, select_one, required): Answer if the safety equipment was in a state of deterioration.
* **Was the Safety Equipment Replaced?** (`safety_equipment_replaced`, select_one, required): Answer if the safety equipment was replaced before or during the demonstration.
* **Was the Safety Equipment Repaired?** (`safety_equipment_repaired`, select_one, required): Answer if the safety equipment was repaired before or during the demonstration.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to provide reliable data for future reference and evaluation.
