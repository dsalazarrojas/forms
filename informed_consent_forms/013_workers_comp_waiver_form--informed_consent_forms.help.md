# workers_comp_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workers_comp_waiver_form is designed to collect information from employees about their workplace injuries, including their details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the employee's name and contact information in the "employee" field.
2. Provide the employer's name and contact information in the "employer" field.
3. Enter the date of the injury in the "date_injured" field in the format of MM/DD/YY.
4. Enter the time of the injury in the "time_injured" field.
5. Specify the location of the injury in the "location_injured" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee name** (employee, text, required): Enter your full name and contact information, including your address and phone number.
- **Employer name** (employer, text, required): Enter your employer's name and contact information, including their address and phone number.
- **Date of Injury** (date_injured, date, required): Enter the date of the injury in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **Time of Injury** (time_injured, time, required): Enter the time of the injury.
- **Location of Injury** (location_injured, text, required): Enter the location where the injury occurred.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
- If you need to make any corrections or changes, you can contact the relevant authorities or submit the form with the updated information.
- This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for medical advice.
