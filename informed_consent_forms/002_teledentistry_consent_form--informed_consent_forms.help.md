# teledentistry_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect patient information for tele-dentistry services, which is an online consultation service provided by dental clinics or offices. The form aims to ensure that patients understand the consent process and provide essential details about their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Patient Info" page to begin.
2. Enter your name and any other relevant details.
3. On the "Contact Info" page, provide your phone number and email (if applicable).
4. In the "Appointment Info" page, provide any additional details about your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Patient Info)** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number and email address (if applicable) so that we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Appointment Info (Appointment Info)** (`appointment_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about your appointment, such as the date, time, or reason for the consultation.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have already scheduled an appointment before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* This form will be used to guide your tele-dentistry experience, so please fill it out carefully and accurately.
