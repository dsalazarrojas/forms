# Mindfulness Program Video Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information, contact details, and explicit consent for a mindfulness program video participation. It is designed to ensure that participants understand their responsibilities, rights, and expectations before engaging with the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your participant information on the first page. This includes any relevant details about your identity and program involvement.
2. On the second page, enter your contact details, which will be used for further communication and program coordination.
3. Finally, read and sign the consent form to confirm your understanding of the program's terms and expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information
**Participant Information (Page 1, **Participant Information**) - This is the first part of the form where you will enter your general information about yourself and your relationship with the program.

### Participant Contact Details
**Participant Contact Details (Page 2, **Participant Contact Details**) - Please enter your contact information for program communication and coordination, such as email, phone number, or address.

### Consent
**Consent (Page 3, **Consent**) - This section is critical as it outlines the program's terms and expectations for participation. Please read carefully and sign to confirm your understanding.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for contact details.
* Take the time to read and understand the consent form before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the program or form, do not hesitate to contact the program administrators.
* You are free to withdraw your consent at any time, and your data will be handled in accordance with the program's data protection policies.
