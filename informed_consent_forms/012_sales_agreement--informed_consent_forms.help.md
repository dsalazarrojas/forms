# Sales Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms and conditions between a seller and a buyer for a sales transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form where required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Agreement first page** (`sales_agreement_first_page`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you can enter any additional information related to the sales agreement. This field is optional, but it's a good place to include any relevant details or comments.
* **sales_agreement_parties** (`sales_agreement_parties`, select_one, **required**): This field asks if you agree that the parties are in the sales agreement. Select 'Yes' to confirm that both the seller and buyer are in agreement.
* **what the seller agrees to** (`sales_agreement_promise`, text, **required**): Describe what the seller is agreeing to in the sales agreement. This is an essential part of the agreement, so make sure to be clear and accurate in your description.
* **describe the seller's agreement** (`sales_agreement_promise_description`, text, **required**): Provide a detailed description of the seller's agreement. This field should complement the previous field and help clarify the terms of the agreement.
* **what the buyer agrees to** (`sales_agreement_parties`, select_one, **required**): Similar to the previous field, this field asks if you agree that the buyer is in the sales agreement. Select 'Yes' to confirm that the buyer is in agreement.
* **describe the buyer's agreement** (`sales_agreement_buyer_agreement_description`, text, **required**): Describe what the buyer is agreeing to in the sales agreement. This is an essential part of the agreement, so make sure to be clear and accurate in your description.
* **Signature Date** (`sales_agreement_signature_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date you sign the sales agreement. This is a required field, so make sure to fill it out accurately.
* **Signature of the Seller** (`sales_agreement_signature_seller`, text, **required**): Sign the sales agreement with your name as the seller.
* **Signature of the Buyer** (`sales_agreement_signature_buyer`, text, **required**): Sign the sales agreement with your name as the buyer.
