# Shooting Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shooting Waiver Form is a document that requires participants to provide their information and emergency contact details, and to agree to the form's conditions. The form is designed to be completed by participants before participating in shooting events or activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Shooting Waiver Form**: This is the main section where you enter your name and other identifying information. You can enter your name as it appears on your identification, and provide any additional information that you think is relevant.
2. **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address.
5. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
6. **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
7. **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Choose how you are related to the emergency contact person. Options include:
	* Emergency Contact
	* Family
	* Parent
	* Spouse
	* Other
8. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
9. **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
10. **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
11. **Participant Agreement**: Choose whether you agree to the form's conditions. Options include:
	* I agree
	* I don't agree
12. **Waiver Signature**: This is where you sign the form to confirm your agreement.
13. **Participant Signature**: This is your signature, which is required to confirm your participation.
14. **Instructor Signature**: This section is for the instructor to sign and confirm that they have reviewed the form.
## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask the instructor.
* The form must be signed by both you and the instructor.
* Please review the form carefully before signing.
* You are required to agree to the form's conditions before signing.
* The form is intended for internal use within the organization, and any external distribution is prohibited.
