# esthetician_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esthetician Consent Form is used to gather information and consent from clients for esthetician services offered by the clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Name** (salon_name, text, required): Enter the name of the salon.
* **Applicant Name** (applicant_name, text, required): Enter your name as the client or esthetician.
* **Services Offered** (services_offered, select_multiple, required): Select all services offered from the list. If none are selected, please type 'None'.
*   'Yes' and 'No' options for selecting services.
* **Date of Visit** (date_of_visit, date, required): Enter the date of visit for the esthetician services.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all services that apply for the esthetician visit.
* Double-check the date of visit for accuracy.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, please contact the clinic staff.
* This form is meant for esthetician services only, please do not use for other purposes.
