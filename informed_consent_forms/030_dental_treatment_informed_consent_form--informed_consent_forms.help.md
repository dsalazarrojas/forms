# Dental Treatment Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from patients undergoing dental treatment. It gathers information about the patient's personal details, treatment procedure, acknowledgment, dentist information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Fill in your personal details, such as name, address, and contact information.
2. **Dental Procedure (Page 1)**: Describe the dental treatment you are about to undergo, including the procedure's details and any risks or benefits associated with it.
3. **Acknowledgment (Page 1)**: Sign and date this section to acknowledge that you have understood the treatment's risks and benefits.
4. **Dentist Information (Page 1)**: Write down the name and contact information of your dentist or healthcare provider.
5. **Contact Details (Page 1)**: Provide your contact information in case the dentist or healthcare provider needs to reach out to you.

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand the treatment details before signing the acknowledgment section.
