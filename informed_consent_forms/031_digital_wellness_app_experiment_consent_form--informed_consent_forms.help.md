# Digital Wellness App Experiment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Wellness App Experiment Consent Form is a crucial document that protects the rights and interests of participants in a digital wellness app experiment. It outlines the study's objectives, risks, and benefits, and requires participants to consent to their participation. This form is essential for both participants and researchers to ensure a fair and transparent experiment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your first name and last name.
*   Provide your email address.
*   Enter your date of birth.
*   Choose your participation status (study participation, participant details, researcher note, etc.).
*   Select the study start date and study end date.
*   Enter your enrollment date.
*   Provide your phone number.
*   Choose your study status and participant status.
*   Add any study notes and participant notes.
*   Choose your consent date and consent time.
*   Specify the study duration.
*   Select your participant incentives.
*   Choose whether you've had a participant interview.
*   Provide your signature as a participant.
*   Sign with your researcher's signature.
*   Confirm your date of birth.
*   Confirm your participant incentives and participant interview.
*   Choose which incentives you received.
*   Confirm whether you participated in an interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`1`, text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`2`, text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`3`, email, required): Provide a valid email address associated with your account.
*   **Date of Birth** (`4`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Study Participation** (`5`, select_one, required): Choose how you wish to participate (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   **Participant Details** (`6`, note, required): Add any additional details relevant to your participation.
*   **Researcher Note** (`7`, note, required): Enter any notes or comments from the researcher.
*   **Study Start Date** (`8`, date, required): Enter the date when the study began.
*   **Study End Date** (`9`, date, required): Enter the date when the study ended.
*   **Enrollment Date** (`10`, date, required): Enter the date when you enrolled in the study.
*   **Phone** (`11`, text, required): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Study Status** (`12`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of your study participation (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   **Participant Status** (`13`, select_multiple, required): Choose your participant status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   **Study Notes** (`14`, note, required): Enter any notes about the study.
*   **Participant Notes** (`15`, note, required): Add any notes relevant to your participation.
*   **Consent Date** (`16`, date, required): Enter the date when you gave your consent.
*   **Consent Time** (`17`, time, required): Enter the time when you gave your consent.
*   **Study Duration** (`18`, number, required): Specify the duration of the study in days or months.
*   **Participant Incentives** (`19`, select_multiple, required): Choose the incentives you received as a participant (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   **Participant Interview** (`20`, select_one, required): Choose whether you participated in an interview (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   **Participant Signature** (`21`, text, required): Provide your signature as a participant.
*   **Researcher Signature** (`22`, text, required): Sign with your researcher's signature.
*   **Participant DOB Confirmed** (`23`, select_multiple, required): Confirm whether your date of birth is correct.
*   **Participant Incentives Confirmed** (`24`, select_multiple, required): Confirm which incentives you received.
*   **Participant Interview Confirmed** (`25`, select_multiple, required): Confirm whether you participated in an interview.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
*   Ensure you have read and understood the study's terms and conditions.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your researcher.
