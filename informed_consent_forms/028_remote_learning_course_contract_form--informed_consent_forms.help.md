# remote_learning_course_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential user information for Remote Learning Course contracts. It ensures all necessary details are captured correctly, reducing the likelihood of discrepancies or lost contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields.
2. Review each field's explanation below for guidance on the specific information needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Name (1)** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the course you're interested in or currently taking. This helps us tailor the course's content and resources to your needs.
- **Student Name (2)** (text, required: false): Provide the name of the student or learner participating in the course. This ensures we can address any questions or requests they might have.
- **Email (3)** (email, required: false): Enter the learner's email address. This is how we'll be communicating with them about course updates, reminders, or any issues that may arise.
- **Phone (4)** (text, required: false): Provide the learner's phone number. We might need to reach out to you for urgent course-related matters.
- **Date of Birth (5)** (date, required: false): Enter the learner's date of birth. This helps us understand their age and adjust the course content accordingly (if needed).
- **Course Start Date (6)** (date, required: false): Select the start date of the course. This helps us track progress and plan for course milestones.
- **Course End Date (7)** (date, required: false): Choose the end date of the course. This helps us understand the duration of your participation and schedule future interactions.
- **Agreement Term (8)** (select one, required: false): Choose the length of time you're committing to the course. Options are 1 month, 2 months, or 3 months. Please select the term that best suits your needs.
- **Payment Amount (9)** (number, required: false): Enter the payment amount for the course, if applicable. This is crucial for accurate billing and financial planning.
- **Course Type (10)** (select multiple, required: true): Select all types of courses you're participating in. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'. Indicate whether you're taking a general course, a specialized course, or both. This helps us provide the most relevant resources and support.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you're unsure about any field or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
