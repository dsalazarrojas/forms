# irb_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants in a study or research project. The form includes fields for participant information, study details, and informed consent signatures. It is designed to ensure that participants understand the purpose and terms of the study, and that they provide their informed consent before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information in the "Participant Information" field, including your name, title, and institution.
2. Provide the study information in the "Study Information" field, including the study title, description, and purpose.
3. Enter your research institution in the "Research Institution" field.
4. Select the name of the prophets (Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, or Dr. Williams) who will be conducting the research.
5. Enter the date and time of consent in the "Date of Consent" and "Time of Consent" fields, respectively.
6. Provide the informed consent text in the "Informed Consent Text" field.
7. Sign the "Informed Consent Signature" field to indicate that you have read and understood the consent text.
8. Sign the "Researcher Signature" field as a representative of the research institution.
9. Read and agree to the "Study Informed Consent" terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (Participant Information): Enter your name, title, and institution.
* **Study Information** (Study Information): Enter the study title, description, and purpose.
* **Research Institution** (Research Institution): Enter your institution (e.g. University of California, Berkeley).
* **Prophets** (Prophets): Select the name of the researcher(s) conducting the study (Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, or Dr. Williams).
* **Date of Consent** (Date of Consent): Enter the date of consent in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of Consent** (Time of Consent): Enter the time of consent in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Informed Consent Text** (Informed Consent Text): Enter the informed consent text provided by the researcher.
* **Informed Consent Signature** (Informed Consent Signature): Sign to indicate that you have read and understood the consent text.
* **Researcher Signature** (Researcher Signature): Sign as a representative of the research institution.
* **Study Informed Consent** (Study Informed Consent): Read and agree to the study informed consent terms.
