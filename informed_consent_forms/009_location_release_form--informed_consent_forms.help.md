# Location Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the release of a location by a party or organization. It will collect necessary information from the releasing party to facilitate the process and provide a record of the release agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the releasing party's information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Enter the release party's name, address, phone number, and email, if different from the releasing party.
3. Specify the title of the release party and the location being released.
4. Enter the date and time of the release.
5. Add any additional location description, if necessary.
6. Confirm that the filmmaker's signature and agreement to release are complete and that they understand the terms of the release.
7. Confirm that the client's signature and agreement to release are complete and that they understand the terms of the release.
8. Date and sign the document to finalize the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Party Name**: The name of the party or organization being released from the location.
* **Release Party Address**: The address of the party or organization being released from the location.
* **Release Party Phone**: The phone number of the party or organization being released from the location.
* **Release Party Email**: The email of the party or organization being released from the location.
* **Title of the Release Party**: The title or position of the party or organization being released from the location.
* **Location**: The address of the location being released from.
* **Date/Time**: The date and time of the release.
* **Location Description**: A brief description of the location being released from, if necessary.
* **Filmmaker Name**: The name of the filmmaker or production company releasing the location.
* **Filmmaker Address**: The address of the filmmaker or production company releasing the location.
* **Filmmaker Phone**: The phone number of the filmmaker or production company releasing the location.
* **Filmmaker Email**: The email of the filmmaker or production company releasing the location.
* **Title of the Filmmaker**: The title or position of the filmmaker or production company releasing the location.
* **Filmmaker Agreement**: A selection indicating the filmmaker's agreement to release the location.
* **Filmmaker Signature**: The signature of the filmmaker or production company releasing the location.
* **Client Name**: The name of the client or company being released to.
* **Client Address**: The address of the client or company being released to.
* **Client Phone**: The phone number of the client or company being released to.
* **Client Email**: The email of the client or company being released to.
* **Title of the Client**: The title or position of the client or company being released to.
* **Client Agreement**: A selection indicating the client's agreement to release the location.
* **Client Signature**: The signature of the client or company being released to.
* **Date of Location Release**: The date of the location release.
* **Time of Location Release**: The time of the location release.

**Tips**

* Ensure that the information entered is accurate and complete.
* Make sure that the filmmaker and client have signed and agreed to the release.
* Review the form carefully before finalizing the release.
* Keep a record of the release for future reference.
