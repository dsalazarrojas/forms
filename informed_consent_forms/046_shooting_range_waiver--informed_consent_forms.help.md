# Shooting Range Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from participants before they start shooting at the range. It's essential to read and understand the form carefully to ensure a safe and enjoyable shooting experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name and date of birth.
2. If you're a minor, provide your parent or guardian's name.
3. Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach out to you.
4. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
5. If you have any medical conditions or history, please disclose them.
6. Confirm your agreement to the release of medical information.
7. Sign below to confirm your agreement.
8. Sign as a witness to confirm your participant's agreement.
9. Enter the current date and time.
10. Enter your IP address (this is not for tracking purposes, but to ensure you're accessing the form from a specific location).
11. Repeat the same process for the second participant (if applicable).
12. Finally, enter your IP address (again, this is not for tracking purposes, but to ensure you're accessing the form from the correct location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participants Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth (2)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Parent or Guardian's Name (3)**: If you're a minor, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Contact Phone Number (4)**: Enter a phone number we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Emergency Contact Name (5)**: Enter the name of an emergency contact who can be reached if you're injured or have an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number (6)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History (7)**: If you have any medical conditions, enter them in the space provided.
* **Medical Condition (8)**: If you have any medical conditions, enter them in the space provided.
* **Release of Liability (9)**: This is a confirmation that you're aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the shooting range. Please sign below.
* **Signature (10)**: Sign to confirm your agreement.
* **Witness Signature (11)**: A witness must sign to confirm your agreement.
* **Release of Medical Information (12)**: Confirm that you're aware of and agree to the release of medical information.
* **Release of Medical Information Options (13)**: This is where you'll specify any medical conditions or information you're releasing.
* **Participant Agreement (14)**: Confirm that you're aware of and agree to the participant agreement terms.
* **Participant Agreement Options (15)**: This is where you'll specify any participant agreement terms you're agreeing to.
* **Shooting Range Terms (16)**: Read and understand the terms and conditions of the shooting range.
* **Participant Signature 2 (17)**: Sign to confirm your agreement.
* **Witness Signature 2 (18)**: A witness must sign to confirm your agreement.
* **Participant Date (19)**: Enter the current date.
* **Participant Time (20)**: Enter the current time.
* **Participant IP Address (21)**: Enter your IP address (this is not for tracking purposes, but to ensure you're accessing the form from the correct location).
* **Participant IP Address 2 (22)**: If you're a second participant, enter their IP address.
* **Participant IP Address 3 (23)**: If you're a second participant, enter their IP address.
* **Participant IP Address 4 (24)**: This field is not required, so you can leave it blank.
* **Participant IP Address 5 (25)**: This field is not required, so you can leave it blank.
