# Psychotherapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the informed consent between a client and a therapist in a psychotherapy setting. It covers the terms of the therapy sessions, boundaries, and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and ensure you understand the terms and expectations outlined.
2. Fill in your personal details and contact information in the "Emergency Contact Info" section.
3. Check the boxes for your emergency contact person and their relationship to you.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form**: This section is where you will provide your informed consent to participate in the therapy sessions.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in your thoughts and expectations.
* **Privacy Policy**: This section outlines the guidelines for protecting your personal information.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to read and understand the privacy policy.
* **Terms Of Service**: This section outlines the rules and expectations for the therapy sessions.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to read and understand the terms of service.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: This section is for providing contact information for an emergency contact person.
	+ Type: This is a select multiple field for choosing who will be your emergency contact person and their relationship.
	+ Options: Person, Contact Person
	+ Hint: Please choose the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History**: This section is for you to note any relevant medical history or conditions that may impact the therapy.
	+ Type: This is a note field for you to write down any medical history.
* **Date**: This section is for you to select the date of your informed consent.
	+ Type: This is a date field for you to select the date.
* **Time**: This section is for you to select the time of your informed consent.
	+ Type: This is a time field for you to select the time.
* **Location**: This section is for you to select the location of your informed consent.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in the location.
* **Reason**: This section is for you to provide a reason for your informed consent.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in the reason.
* **Signature**: This section is for you to sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
	+ Type: This is a select multiple field for choosing who will sign the form, Client or Therapist.
	+ Options: Client, Therapist
	+ Hint: Please sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Date Signed**: This section is for you to select the date you signed the form.
	+ Type: This is a date field for you to select the date.
* **Time Signed**: This section is for you to select the time you signed the form.
	+ Type: This is a time field for you to select the time.
* **Location Signed**: This section is for you to select the location you signed the form.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in the location.
* **Reason Signed**: This section is for you to provide a reason for signing the form.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in the reason.
* **Signed By**: This section is for you to choose who signed the form.
	+ Type: This is a select multiple field for choosing who signed the form, Client or Therapist.
	+ Options: Client, Therapist
	+ Hint: Please choose who signed the form.
* **Image of Signature**: This section is for you to provide an image of your signature.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to upload the image.
* **Note**: This section is for you to add any additional notes or comments.
	+ Type: This is a note field for you to write down any additional notes.
* **Emergency Contact Person**: This section is for you to select the person who will be your emergency contact.
	+ Type: This is a select multiple field for choosing who will be your emergency contact.
	+ Options: Person, Contact Person
	+ Hint: Please choose who will be your emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: This section is for you to fill in the relationship between you and your emergency contact person.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in the relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: This section is for you to provide contact information for your emergency contact person.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in the contact information.
* **Phone**: This section is for you to provide a phone number for your emergency contact person.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in the phone number.
* **Email**: This section is for you to provide an email address for your emergency contact person.
	+ Type: This is an email field for you to fill in the email address.
* **Address**: This section is for you to provide an address for your emergency contact person.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in the address.
* **Alternate Phone**: This section is for you to provide an alternate phone number for your emergency contact person.
	+ Type: This is a text field for you to fill in the alternate phone number.
* **Alternate Email**: This section is for you to provide an alternate email address for your emergency contact person.
	+ Type: This is an email field for you to fill in the alternate email address.

## Tips

* Please read through each section carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any of the terms or expectations.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information for your emergency contact person.
* Sign and date the form in the presence of the therapist to confirm your consent.
