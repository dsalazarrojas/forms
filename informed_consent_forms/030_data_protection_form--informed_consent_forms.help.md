# Data Protection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Protection Form is a collection of information that helps us ensure our organization's data protection and privacy obligations are met. This form will help us understand the type of data being collected, how it's collected, and how it's protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Contact Information section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your company name in the Company Name field.
3. Provide detailed information about your company in the Company Information field.
4. Select the type of data you're protecting (e.g., Customer, Employee, Client, or Other).
5. Identify the person in your company who will be your contact person for this request.
6. Enter your company's address in the Company Address field.
7. Specify how the data is being collected (e.g., Online, Offline, or Both).
8. Provide information about data security measures in place to protect the data.
9. Enter your company's phone number in the Company Phone field.
10. Review your answers and confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email, and phone number to enable us to contact you regarding your data protection request.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or lettershead.
* **Company Information** (`company_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your company, including its products or services.
* **Data Type** (`data_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of data you're protecting (e.g., Customer, Employee, Client, or Other).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Identify the person in your company who will be your contact person for this request.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's address as it appears on your business cards or lettershead.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify how the data is being collected (e.g., Online, Offline, or Both).
* **Data Security** (`data_security`, `text`, required: false): Describe the measures in place to protect the data (e.g., encryption, backups, etc.).
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `note`, required: false): Review and confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure our organization can meet its data protection and privacy obligations.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your contact person for clarification.
* This form is a confidential document. Please do not share it with anyone outside of your organization.
