# Acupuncturist Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to provide informed consent for telehealth consultations with acupuncturists. It ensures that patients understand the limitations and risks associated with virtual sessions compared to in-person visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each section and understand the information provided.
2. Enter your full name and location during the telehealth session.
3. Confirm that you have access to the necessary technology for the session.
4. Acknowledge the risks and limitations of telehealth consultations.
5. Consent to the use of HIPAA-compliant platforms.
6. Provide a backup phone number in case of technical failure.
7. Sign and consent to the use of telehealth services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Full Name**: Enter your full name, including any initials or titles (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Dr.).
- **Lead Acupuncturist / Provider**: Enter the name of the acupuncturist providing the consultation.
- **-- Description of Services --**: This section explains the purpose and nature of telehealth consultations.
- **I acknowledge I have access to the necessary technology for the session**: Confirm that you have a high-speed internet connection and a device with a camera and microphone.
- **-- Risks and Limitations --**: Acknowledge the limitations and risks associated with telehealth consultations, including the inability to perform physical palpation or needle insertion.
- **I understand the limitations of telehealth compared to in-person visits**: Confirm that you understand the risks and limitations of virtual sessions.
- **I understand that electronic systems may have potential security risks**: Acknowledge the security risks associated with electronic systems used for telehealth consultations.
- **-- Emergency Procedures --**: This section outlines emergency procedures in case of technical failure or medical emergency during the session.
- **Your Physical Location During Telehealth Sessions**: Enter the street address where you will be located during the session.
- **Backup Phone Number**: Enter a backup phone number in case of technical failure or emergency during the session.
- **I consent to the use of HIPAA-compliant platforms for my consultation**: Confirm that you consent to the use of HIPAA-compliant platforms for your telehealth session.
- **I understand I have the right to terminate the telehealth session at any time**: Confirm that you understand you have the right to end the session at any time.
- **Electronic Signature - I give my informed consent for telehealth services**: Confirm that you provide informed consent for the use of telehealth services.
- **Date of Consent**: Enter the date on which you provided informed consent for the telehealth session.

Note: The fields marked as "select_one" require you to choose one of the available options, while "text" fields require you to enter your full response. Make sure to review each field carefully and provide accurate and informed responses.
