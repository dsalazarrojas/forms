# Behavioral Health Feedback Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavioral Health Feedback Interview Consent Form is designed to gather informed consent from participants before conducting a Behavioral Health Feedback Interview.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Participant Information**: This section requires your personal details such as name, age, phone number, and email address.
	* **Participant Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Age**: Enter your age in years.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
2. **Interview Details**: This section provides information about the interview.
	* **Purpose of the Interview**: Choose one of the options to indicate the purpose of the interview.
	* **Interview Format**: Choose one of the options to indicate the format of the interview.
	* **Estimated Interview Duration**: Choose one of the options to indicate the estimated duration of the interview.
3. **Consent Statements**: This section requires your agreement to participate in the interview.
	* **I Understand the Purpose of this Interview**: Select "True" to indicate that you understand the purpose of the interview.
	* **I Understand my Participation is Voluntary**: Select "I Agree" to indicate that you understand your participation is voluntary.
	* **I May Withdraw from the Interview at Any Time**: Select "I Agree" to indicate that you can withdraw from the interview at any time.
	* **My Responses Will be Kept Confidential**: Select "I Agree" to indicate that you understand your responses will be kept confidential.
	* **The Interview May be Recorded for Accuracy**: Select "I Agree" or "I Do Not Agree" to indicate your preference.
	* **My Personal Information will be Anonymized**: Select "I Agree" or "I Do Not Agree" to indicate your preference.
	* **Data May be Used for Research and Improvement**: Select "I Agree" to indicate that you agree to the use of your data for research and improvement.
	* **All my Questions Have Been Addressed**: Select "True" to indicate that all your questions have been addressed.
	* **I Consent to Participate in this Interview**: Select "I Agree" to indicate that you consent to participate in the interview.

## Tips
- Please review each field carefully to ensure accurate completion.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the interviewer or the organization conducting the study.
- Some fields are marked as optional, but it's recommended to complete them for the purpose of the interview and the organization's records.
- Ensure that you understand the purpose and scope of the interview before proceeding.
