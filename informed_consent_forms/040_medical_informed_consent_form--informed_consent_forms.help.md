# medical_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Informed Consent Form is used to document a patient's understanding of their medical condition, treatment options, and potential risks. It is a legally binding document that protects both the patient and the medical provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have understood and are agreeing to the medical procedure and treatment being discussed.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information about your medical condition, treatment options, and potential risks.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the medical procedure and treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Medical Condition (2)**: Select the medical condition you are being treated for from the list of options.
* **Medical Record (3)**: Enter your medical record number or any other relevant identification number.
* **Date of Admission (4)**: Enter the date you were admitted to the hospital or medical facility.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email (7)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact (8)**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Signature (9)**: Sign and date this field to indicate your agreement to the medical procedure and treatment.
* **Medical Provider (10)**: Enter the name and contact information of your medical provider.
* **Medical Procedure (11)**: Enter a brief description of the medical procedure or treatment.
* **Medical History (12)**: Enter any relevant medical history or allergies.
* **Allergy (13)**: Select any allergies you have from the list of options.
* **Treatment Plan (14)**: Enter a detailed description of your treatment plan.
* **Medical Image (15)**: Enter any relevant medical images or attachments.
* **Medical Notes (16)**: Enter any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Medical Signature (17)**: Sign and date this field to indicate your agreement to the medical procedure and treatment.
* **Medical Terms and Conditions (18)**: Enter any relevant medical terms and conditions.
* **Medical Privacy (19)**: Select any medical privacy options from the list.
* **Medical Procedure Notes (20)**: Enter any relevant medical procedure notes.
* **Medical Test Results (21)**: Enter any relevant medical test results.
* **Medical Treatment Outcome (22)**: Enter the expected treatment outcome.
* **Medical Follow-up (23)**: Enter the date and time of your follow-up appointment.
* **Medical Next Follow-up (24)**: Enter the date and time of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Medical Review By (25)**: Enter the date and time of your medical review by the doctor.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood all the information before signing the form.
* Answer all fields accurately and honestly.
* Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the medical procedure and treatment.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
