# Makeup Pre Procedure Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients before a procedure, likely for a medical or aesthetic procedure such as a surgical operation or a makeup session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit it to the relevant authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First question (id: 1)**: This is the title of the form, which is a text field that should not be filled out.
* **Are you satisfied with the last appointment you had with our studio? (id: 2)**: Select the answer that best describes your experience with our previous appointment.
	+ Option 1: Yes, I am satisfied.
	+ Option 2: No, I am not satisfied.
	+ Option 3: I am neutral.
* **Last appointment (id: 3)**: Enter the date of your last appointment with us. This will help us track your history with our services.
* **First name (id: 4)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name (id: 5)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (id: 6)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (id: 7)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Pre procedure instructions (id: 8)**: Enter any pre-procedure instructions or notes that you want to share with us.
* **Medical condition (id: 9)**: Describe your current medical condition, if any.
* **Medical condition details (id: 10)**: Provide more information about your medical condition, if necessary.
* **Allergies (id: 11)**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medical history (id: 12)**: Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or operations.
* **Previous appointments (id: 13)**: List any previous appointments you have had with us.
* **Medication (id: 14)**: Describe any medications you are currently taking.
* **Current medications (id: 15)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Pre procedure contraindications (id: 16)**: Describe any conditions that may contraindicate the procedure.
* **Procedure type (id: 17)**: Describe the type of procedure you are having.
* **Procedure details (id: 18)**: Provide more information about the procedure you are having.
* **Pre procedure preparation (id: 19)**: Describe any pre-procedure preparations you need to make.
* **Client wishes (id: 20)**: Describe any wishes or requests you have for your procedure.
* **Client wishes details (id: 21)**: Provide more information about your wishes or requests.
* **Client wishes comments (id: 22)**: Leave any additional comments about your wishes or requests.
* **Procedure notes (id: 23)**: Leave any additional notes about your procedure.
* **Next appointment (id: 24)**: Enter the date of your next appointment.
* **Signature (id: 25)**: Select the options that apply to you.
	+ Option 1: I understand and acknowledge the terms and conditions of this form.
	+ Option 2: I do not understand the terms and conditions of this form.
* **Pre procedure questionnaire signature date (id: 26)**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Pre procedure questionnaire signature time (id: 27)**: Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Pre procedure questionnaire signature (id: 28)**: Sign this form to confirm you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
