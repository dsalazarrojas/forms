# Water Project Beneficiary Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Project Beneficiary Interview Consent Form is a crucial step in the process of engaging with water project beneficiaries to ensure informed consent and agreement to the project terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. If required, please provide a valid email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. If required, please enter your contact number in the "Participant Contact Number" field.
4. Select "Informed Consent Given" or "Informed Consent Not Given" to indicate your level of consent in the "Informed Consent" field.
5. Read and agree or disagree with the project terms and conditions in the "Agreement" field. Select all that apply to indicate your stance.
6. Provide a signature in the "Participant Signature" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Fill in your name to identify yourself as a participant.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Select the date on which you are signing this form. This is an essential field to ensure that we capture the exact date of your consent.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the project with which we are working. This will help us track which project you are associated with.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address to allow us to reach out to you if needed.
* **Participant Contact Number** (`participant_contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you for any updates or queries.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have given informed consent for the project or not. This is a crucial step in our process to ensure we are working with consenting participants.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to indicate your stance on the project terms and conditions. This will help us understand your level of agreement and ensure that we are on the same page.
* **Participant Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Please sign this field to confirm your consent and agreement. If you have trouble writing, you can use this field to explain or provide any additional comments.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have read and understood the project terms and conditions before proceeding.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask us.
- We appreciate your participation and contribution to the project.
