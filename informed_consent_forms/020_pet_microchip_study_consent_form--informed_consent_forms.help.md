# Pet Microchip Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Pet Microchip Study Consent Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and address as the pet owner.
2. Enter your pet's microchip number.
3. Provide a brief description of the study details.
4. Select the type of pet you own.
5. Enter your pet's breed.
6. Specify your pet's color.
7. Enter your pet's age.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Owner (1)
*   **Pet Owner**: Enter your name as the owner of the pet participating in this study.
*   **Type**: text input
*   **Required**: false

### Pet Microchip Number (2)
*   **Pet Microchip Number**: Enter the microchip number of your pet.
*   **Type**: text input
*   **Required**: false

### Pet Microchip Study Details (3)
*   **Pet Microchip Study Details**: Provide a brief description of the study details.
*   **Type**: text input
*   **Required**: false

### Pet Type (4)
*   **Pet Type**: Select the type of pet you own (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
*   **Type**: select_one
*   **Required**: false

### Pet Breed (5)
*   **Pet Breed**: Enter the breed of your pet.
*   **Type**: text input
*   **Required**: false

### Pet Color (6)
*   **Pet Color**: Enter your pet's color.
*   **Type**: text input
*   **Required**: false

### Pet Age (7)
*   **Pet Age**: Enter your pet's age.
*   **Type**: text input
*   **Required**: false

### Owner Signature (8)
*   **Owner Signature**: This is where you sign to confirm your consent.
*   **Type**: note
*   **Required**: false

### Pet Signature (9)
*   **Pet Signature**: This is where your pet signs to confirm their participation. (Note: This is optional and only applicable to pets that can sign, i.e., dogs and cats.)
*   **Type**: note
*   **Required**: false

### Veterinarian Signature (10)
*   **Veterinarian Signature**: This is where the veterinarian verifies that the form has been properly completed.
*   **Type**: note
*   **Required**: false

### Date Completed (11)
*   **Date Completed**: Enter the date you completed this form.
*   **Type**: date
*   **Required**: false

### Time Completed (12)
*   **Time Completed**: Enter the time you completed this form.
*   **Type**: time
*   **Required**: false

### Date Received (13)
*   **Date Received**: Enter the date this form was received by the study team.
*   **Type**: date
*   **Required**: false

### Contact Number (14)
*   **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number where you can be reached.
*   **Type**: text input
*   **Required**: false

### Study ID (15)
*   **Study ID**: Enter a study ID number.
*   **Type**: text input
*   **Required**: false
