This form is designed to obtain electronic informed consent from users. Informed consent is a critical aspect of many business operations, especially when dealing with sensitive data or services that require explicit acceptance by the user.

The purpose of this form is to collect necessary contact information and demographic details of the user, along with their consent to use specific services or access certain data. This form ensures that the user has understood the terms and conditions of the service and is willing to accept its implications.

Before proceeding, please ensure that this form is being used in an appropriate context and that the user understands the significance of providing informed consent.

## Electronic Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain electronic informed consent from users, ensuring they understand and accept the terms and conditions of our services or access to specific data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your date of birth and signature details.
3. Choose the type of signature (Physician, Client, or Witness).
4. Select your user type (user, organization, or department).
5. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user-id (User ID)** (`1`): Enter your user ID number (if applicable).
* **contact-first-name (Contact First Name)** (`2`): Enter your first name.
* **contact-last-name (Contact Last Name)** (`3`): Enter your last name.
* **contact-email (Contact Email)** (`4`): Enter your email address.
* **contact-phone (Contact Phone)** (`5`): Enter your phone number.
* **date-of-signature (Date of Signature)** (`6`): Enter the date you are signing this form.
* **date-of-birth (Date of Birth)** (`7`): Enter your date of birth.
* **signature (Signature)** (`8`): Sign your name in the provided space.
* **consent-statement (Consent Statement)** (`9`): This is a placeholder for the consent statement to be agreed upon.
* **date-of-signature-confirmation (Date of Signature Confirmation)** (`10`): Enter the date you confirmed this consent.
* **consent-signed-by (Consent Signed By)** (`11`): This field is not used; however, it may be used for internal purposes.
* **date-signed (Date Signed)** (`12`): Enter the date and time you signed this form.
* **user-signed (User Signed)** (`13`): This field is not used; however, it may be used for internal purposes.
* **signature-date (Signature Date)** (`14`): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **user-agrees (User Agrees)** (`15`): Select one of the options to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **user-agrees-other (User Agrees Other)** (`16`): If "Other" is selected in the previous field, enter your reason for not agreeing.
* **date-agreed (Date Agreed)** (`17`): Enter the date you agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **user-organisation (User Organisation)** (`18`): Enter the name of your organization.
* **user-organisation-id (User Organisation ID)** (`19`): Enter your organization ID number (if applicable).
* **user-department (User Department)** (`20`): Enter the name of your department (if applicable).
* **user-title (User Title)** (`21`): Enter your title or position.
* **signature-type (Signature Type)** (`22`): Select the type of signature (Physician, Client, or Witness).
* **user-name (User Name)** (`23`): Enter your name for record-keeping purposes.
* **user-position (User Position)** (`24`): Enter your position or job title.
* **user-id-number (User ID Number)** (`25`): Enter your user ID number (if applicable).

## Tips

* Ensure you understand the terms and conditions before signing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or administrator.
* Please sign this form carefully, as it is a legally binding document.
