# Henna Brow Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Henna Brow Consent Form is a document that requires patients to provide informed consent before undergoing a henna brow treatment. This form ensures that patients are fully aware of the treatment details, its potential risks and benefits, and are providing their consent voluntarily.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Fill in the patient's information, including any relevant medical history or allergies.
2. **Procedure**: Describe the henna brow treatment you plan to perform, including any potential risks or complications.
3. **Informed Consent**: Select all the options that apply to confirm that you have read and understand the information provided, that you are providing your consent voluntarily, and that you are aware of your obligation to not undergo this procedure if you wish.
4. **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understand the information provided.
5. **Date**: Fill in the date on which you signed the form.
6. **Time**: Fill in the time on which you signed the form.
7. **Staff Signature**: Sign and date the form for staff to confirm that they have obtained your informed consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (patient_info)**: This field is used to collect the patient's name, date of birth, or any other relevant medical history.
* **Procedure (procedure)**: This field is used to describe the henna brow treatment you plan to perform, including any potential risks or complications.
* **Informed Consent (informed_consent)**: This field requires you to select all the options that apply to confirm that you have read and understand the information provided, that you are providing your consent voluntarily, and that you are aware of your obligation to not undergo this procedure if you wish.
	+ **I have read and understand the information provided above**: This option confirms that you have read and understand the information provided about the henna brow treatment.
	+ **I am providing this information of my own free will**: This option confirms that you are providing your consent voluntarily.
	+ **I am aware that I am under no obligation to undergo this procedure**: This option confirms that you are aware of your obligation to not undergo this procedure if you wish.
* **Signature (signature)**: Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understand the information provided.
* **Date (date)**: Fill in the date on which you signed the form.
* **Time (time)**: Fill in the time on which you signed the form.
* **Staff Signature (staff_signature)**: Sign and date the form for staff to confirm that they have obtained your informed consent.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand all the information provided before signing the form.
* Sign the form before the procedure is performed.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
