# Microdermabrasion Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients before undergoing microdermabrasion treatment. It helps us understand your medical history, insurance details, and emergency contact information to ensure your safety and successful treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, making sure to provide all requested information.
- Ensure that you select the correct emergency contact and insurance provider.
- Sign and date the consent section, indicating your awareness of the risks and consent to proceed with treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, optional): Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, number, optional): Please enter your contact number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required): Select at least one emergency contact person from the provided list.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): Please provide your insurance information, if applicable.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_one, required): Select your insurance provider from the provided options.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter your policy number.
* **Policy Dollar Amount** (`policy_dollar_amount`, number, optional): Enter the policy dollar amount.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your policy.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant medical history, including allergies, medications, and medical conditions.
* **Medication Use** (`medication_use`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Please list any allergies you have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Provide a list of any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, text, optional): List any medical procedures you have had.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): Please list any family medical history.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your provider.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, select_one, required): Select your level of awareness of the risks and consent to proceed with treatment.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Date of your consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, optional): Time of your consent.
* **Consent Signature** (`consent_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date your consent.

**Tips:**

* Please fill out the form honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your provider for clarification.
* Review your responses before signing the form to ensure accuracy.
* Please note that this form is a requirement for all clients undergoing microdermabrasion treatment.
