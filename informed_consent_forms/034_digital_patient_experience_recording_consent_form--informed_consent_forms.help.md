# Digital Patient Experience Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for recording their experiences, providing a record of their medical history, and outlining the purpose and method of the recording. It is a crucial document that ensures patients are aware of what they are agreeing to, and healthcare professionals can accurately record and use their information for research, treatment, or documentation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **General Information**, including their contact details such as address and phone number.
2. List any **Medical Conditions** that the patient has, if applicable.
3. Select the **Recording Instrument** to be used, which can be a phone call, video recording, or digital recording.
4. Choose the **Recording Method**, which can be done in-person, over the phone, or via online form.
5. Provide your **Consent**, stating that you agree to the recording.
6. Sign the **Signature** section to confirm your agreement.
7. Enter the **Date Signed** and **Time Signed** to ensure accuracy.
8. Select the **Medical Professional** involved, such as a doctor, nurse, or other healthcare provider.
9. Enter the **Healthcare Provider** who will be conducting the recording.
10. Choose the **Location** where the recording will take place.
11. Describe your **Relationship with the Patient**.
12. Select the **Recording Purpose**, which can be for research, treatment, or documentation.
13. Enter the **Recording Start** and **Recording Stop** times.
14. Specify the **Recording Duration**.
15. Add any additional **Recording Notes**, if necessary.
16. Optionally, enter **Recording Location** and **Additional Notes**.
17. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Please enter your contact details, including address and phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have, such as Diabetes, Asthma, etc.
* **Medications**: If you have any ongoing medications, list them here.
* **Recording Instrument**: Select the method to be used for the recording (e.g., phone call, video recording, digital recorder).
* **Recording Method**: Choose how the recording will be conducted (e.g., in-person, phone call, video call, email, online form).
* **Consent**: Please sign and date this section to confirm your agreement to the recording.
* **Signature**: Sign here to confirm your agreement.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Medical Professional**: Select the type of medical professional involved in the recording.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider conducting the recording.
* **Location**: Choose the location where the recording will take place.
* **Relationship with Patient**: Describe your relationship with the patient.
* **Recording Purpose**: Select the purpose of the recording (e.g., research, treatment, documentation).
* **Recording Start**: Enter the start time of the recording.
* **Recording Stop**: Enter the stop time of the recording.
* **Recording Duration**: Specify the duration of the recording.
* **Recording Notes**: Add any additional notes for the recording.
* **Additional Notes**: Any other notes or information to be recorded.
* **Recording Location**: Enter the location of the recording (optional).
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
