# Ownership Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ownership Declaration Form is used to collect information from the pet owner, ensuring that they have provided informed consent and accurate details about themselves and their pet. This form is used to verify pet ownership and is not intended for pets that are not owned by the person filling out the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. If you have a pet, enter your pet's name in the Pet Name field.
4. Enter your pet's breed in the Pet Breed field.
5. Enter your pet's age in the Pet Age field. Please use numbers only (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
6. Enter your email address in the Owner Email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the Owner Phone field.
8. Enter your address in the Address field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`owner_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`owner_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required/optional): If you have a pet, enter its name.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, required/optional): If you have a pet, enter its breed.
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, required/optional): If you have a pet, enter its age. Please use numbers only (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`owner_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.

## Tips
- Ensure you've filled out the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, feel free to reach out to our support team for assistance.
- Remember, this form is used to verify pet ownership, so please only declare ownership of pets that you are actually responsible for.
