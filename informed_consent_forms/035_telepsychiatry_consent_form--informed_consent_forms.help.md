# Telepsychiatry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Telepsychiatry Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before providing telepsychiatric services. Informed consent is a legal and ethical requirement that ensures patients understand their rights, responsibilities, and the potential risks and benefits associated with treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required fields.
*   Make sure to enter your name, email, and date of birth correctly.
*   Provide your emergency contact's name, relationship, and phone number.
*   Sign the form to indicate your agreement to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (2): Enter your email address that we will use to contact you.
*   **Phone** (3): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (4): Enter the name of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Relationship** (5): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact, e.g., spouse, parent, or sibling.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (6): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Date of Birth** (7): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Address** (8): Enter your current address.
*   **Relationship Type** (9): Describe the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact, e.g., spouse, parent, or guardian.
*   **Authorized to Contact Emergency Contact** (10): Check the box if you authorize your emergency contact to receive information about your treatment.
*   **Patient Signature** (11): Sign your name to indicate your agreement to the terms of service.
*   **Healthcare Provider Signature** (12): Sign your name to indicate your agreement to the terms of service and to confirm your identity as a healthcare provider.
