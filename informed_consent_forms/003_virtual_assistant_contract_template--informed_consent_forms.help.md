# Virtual Assistant Contract Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients for the creation of a Virtual Assistant Contract Template. It helps establish a clear agreement between the virtual assistant and their client, ensuring both parties understand the terms and conditions of their collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Customer Details** section with the client's name, address, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. (Optional) Fill in the **Contract Term** section if you need to specify any specific contract terms or conditions.
3. (Optional) Fill in the **Payment Terms** section if you need to clarify payment details or schedules.
4. Sign and date the **Customer Signature** field to confirm your agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name, address, email, and any other necessary contact information to ensure clear communication.
* **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, `text`, optional): If you have specific contract terms or conditions, enter them here to be included in the contract template.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, optional): Describe your payment terms, such as payment frequency or methods, if not already included in a separate agreement.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date this field to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions outlined in this template.
