# Automated Service Scheduling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients for the automated service scheduling system.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Service Name" field and enter the name of the service that the client is requesting.
2. Select the "Scheduled Service Date" field and choose the date that the service is scheduled for.
3. Select the "Scheduled Service Time" field and choose the time that the service is scheduled for.
4. Select the "Service Provider Name" field and enter the name of the service provider.
5. Select the "Client Full Name" field and enter the client's full name.
6. Select the "Client Email" field and enter the client's email address.
7. Select the "Client Phone Number" field and enter the client's phone number.
8. Select the "Date of Birth" field and enter the client's date of birth, if applicable.
9. Select the "Type of Service" field and choose the type of service that the client is requesting.
10. Select the "Service Description" field and enter a brief description of the service that the client is requesting.
11. Select the "Expected Service Duration" field and choose the expected duration of the service.
12. Select the "Health Conditions" field and enter any health conditions that the client would like to disclose.
13. Select the "Allergies" field and enter any allergies that the client has.
14. Select the "Medications" field and enter any medications that the client is currently taking.
15. Select the "Consent to Automated Scheduling and Reminders" field and choose whether the client consents to automated scheduling and reminders.
16. Select the "Consent to Data Collection and Use" field and choose whether the client consents to data collection and use.
17. Select the "Consent to Assumption of Risks" field and choose whether the client assumes all risks associated with the service.
18. Select the "Date of Consent" field and choose the date of consent.
