# Telemedicine Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients for telemedicine services. It's a legal requirement to ensure that patients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of their treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the date of your telemedicine appointment or consultation.
3. Enter the name of your telemedicine provider.
4. In the "Informed Consent" section, select all the statements that apply to you.
5. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
6. Finally, review and agree to the terms and conditions of your treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your patient's name here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of your telemedicine appointment or consultation.
* **Telemedicine Provider** (`telemedicine_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of your telemedicine provider.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, enter their name here. This could be a family member, friend, or other individual that can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person (e.g., family member, friend, etc.).
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent8`, text, required): This is a section where you will select all the statements that apply to you. Please review the options carefully and select the ones that are true for you.
	+ I understand that I can stop taking my medications anytime.
	+ I understand that I can change my mind about taking my medications.
	+ I understand that I am responsible for my own healthcare.
	+ I understand that I must inform my healthcare provider about any changes in my medical history.
	+ I understand that I am responsible for ensuring that my medications are taken as directed.
	+ I will inform my healthcare provider of any changes in my medication regimen.
	+ I will take my medications as directed by my healthcare provider.
	+ I will report any side effects to my healthcare provider.
* **Tips**:
	+ Please review the informed consent section carefully and select all the statements that apply to you.
	+ If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
	+ Make sure to provide accurate contact information for your emergency contact person if you have one.
