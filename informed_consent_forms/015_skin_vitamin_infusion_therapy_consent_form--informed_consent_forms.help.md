## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Patient Details section with the patient's information.
2. Answer the Medical History question truthfully.
3. Select the correct responses for Treatment Consent, Clinics & Services, and Dermatology Practices sections.
4. Provide any Additional Comments if necessary.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the treatment.
6. Have the healthcare provider sign and date the form to confirm that they have explained the treatment to you.
