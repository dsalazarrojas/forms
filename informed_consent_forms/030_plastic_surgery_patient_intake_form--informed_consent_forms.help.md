# Plastic Surgery Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plastic Surgery Patient Intake Form is a crucial document used to gather essential information from patients before undergoing a plastic surgery procedure. This form helps healthcare providers assess the patient's medical history, contact information, and consent status, ensuring a safe and successful surgical experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have answered all required fields.
2. Enter your personal information in the "Patient Information" page (page 1).
3. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email (page 2).
4. Answer questions about your medical history, medical condition, and allergies (pages 3-5).
5. Confirm your understanding of the procedure and any medical team involvement (pages 6-7).
6. Sign and date the "Patient Signature" and "Doctor Signature" fields (pages 8 and 22).
7. Review and confirm your consent status (pages 9 and 24).
