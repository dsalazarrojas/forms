# Hh Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hh Consultation Form is designed to collect basic contact information and a consultation request from users. This form serves as an initial step in a consultation process, where users can provide their details and schedule a consultation with the relevant personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address accurately.
2. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select a Date for the consultation.
4. Choose a Time for the consultation.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the Note field.
6. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a Date for the consultation in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a Time for the consultation in the format 12:00:00 AM or 12:00:00 PM.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the consultation.
* **Second First Name** (`first_name_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional name or details, if applicable.
