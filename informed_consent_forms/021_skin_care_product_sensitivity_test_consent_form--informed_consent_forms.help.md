# Skin Care Product Sensitivity Test Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your skin type, history, and sensitivity to specific products. It will help us understand your skin's sensitivity and potential reactions to different products.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please answer the questions in the following pages. Each page has specific questions tailored to the information we need to gather.
2.  For each question, please select the best option from the available choices or provide a brief response in the text fields.
3.  Make sure to answer all the questions carefully and honestly to help us get accurate information about your skin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, select_one, required): This question asks if you understand the test involves using a variety of products for 7 days. Please select one of the available options to indicate your understanding.
    *   I understand that I will be asked to use a variety of products for 7 days.
    *   I understand that I will need to come back in 7-10 days.
    *   I am aware that the products tested may cause irritation or allergic reactions.
    *   I consent to be tested with these products and understand that this test does not replace other medical treatment or diagnosis.
*   **What is your skin history?** (`skin_history`, text, required): This question asks about your skin history to help us understand your skin's condition.
*   **What is your skin type?** (`skin_type`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to indicate your skin type:
    *   Dry
    *   Normal
    *   Oily
    *   Sensitive
*   **Are you sensitive to any specific products?** (`skin_sensitivity`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the products you are sensitive to from the available options.
*   **What are the ingredients of the products you have used?** (`skin_ingredients`, text, required): Describe the ingredients of the products you have used to help us understand your skin's reaction.
*   **Are you aware of any skin reactions or allergies you have?** (`skin_issues`, text, required): Describe any skin reactions or allergies you have experienced.
*   **Are you experiencing any skin issues or concerns?** (`skin_concerns`, text, required): Describe any skin issues or concerns you are experiencing.
*   **Have you used any other products recently?** (`other_products`, text, required): Describe any other products you have used recently.
*   **Have you experienced any skin sensitivity issues?** (`test_conclusion`, text, required): Describe any skin sensitivity issues you have experienced during the test.

## Tips

*   Please answer all the questions carefully and honestly to help us get accurate information about your skin.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
