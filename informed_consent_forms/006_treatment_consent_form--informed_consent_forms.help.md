# Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before they receive medical treatment. It ensures that patients understand the risks and benefits of the treatment and are willing to accept it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **patient name** in the patient name field.
2. Select your **treatment type** from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the **treatment date** you will be receiving the treatment.
4. Enter the name and contact information of your **healthcare provider**.
5. Sign your **patient signature** in the designated field.
6. Sign your **provider signature** in the designated field.
7. Enter the date and time of your **consent date** and **consent time**.
8. Enter your **patient contact information** and **provider contact information**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your medical records.
* **treatment type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of treatment you are receiving (e.g. surgery, medication, etc.).
* **treatment date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date you will be receiving the treatment.
* **healthcare provider** (text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your healthcare provider (e.g. doctor's name, clinic name).
* **patient signature** (text, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm you have received and understood the treatment information.
* **provider signature** (text, required/optional): Sign your healthcare provider's name to confirm they have explained the treatment to you.
* **consent date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date you gave your consent for the treatment.
* **consent time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time you gave your consent for the treatment.
* **patient contact info** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact information (e.g. phone number, email).
* **provider contact info** (text, required/optional): Enter your healthcare provider's contact information (e.g. phone number, email).

Note: Fields marked as required/optional may not be required for all treatments, but are required for this specific form.
