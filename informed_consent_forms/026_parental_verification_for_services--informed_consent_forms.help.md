# Parental Verification For Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather parental consent and contact information for services provided to children or students. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure the best possible outcomes for the child.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2.  Fill out your contact details, including your name and any relevant phone numbers or email addresses.
3.  Indicate the services your child will be participating in.
4.  Choose your preferred method of contact for follow-up actions.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments as needed.
