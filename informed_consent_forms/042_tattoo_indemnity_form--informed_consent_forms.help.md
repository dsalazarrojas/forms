# Tattoo Indemnity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from clients before performing a tattoo or piercing service. It is designed to ensure that clients are aware of the risks and aftercare instructions associated with the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please select one of the following services:
    + Semi-Private Area
    + Full-Sleeve Tattoo
    + Tattoo Sleeve
    + Minimal Tattoo
*   Enter your client's name.
*   Enter your client's email address.
*   Enter your client's phone number.
*   Enter your artist's name.
*   Select the type of service you will be performing (Tattoo or Piercing).
*   Briefly describe the service you will be providing.
*   Note any risks associated with the procedure.
*   Provide aftercare instructions for the client.
*   Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Tattoo Type** (id: 2): Select the type of tattoo service you will be performing from the options provided.
*   **Client Name** (id: 3): Enter your client's name accurately.
*   **Client E-Mail** (id: 4): Enter your client's email address. Please ensure that the address is correct to ensure we can contact you with any updates or questions.
*   **Client Phone** (id: 5): Enter your client's phone number. Please ensure that the number is correct and up-to-date.
*   **Artist Name** (id: 6): Enter your artist's name. This should be your name as it will appear on the client's records.
*   **Tattoo Service** (id: 7): Select the type of service you will be performing (Tattoo or Piercing).
*   **Tattoo Service Description** (id: 8): Briefly describe the service you will be providing. This can include details about the location, size, and style of the tattoo.
*   **Risks** (id: 9): Note any risks associated with the procedure. This can include information about skin sensitivity, allergies, or other potential complications.
*   **Aftercare Instructions** (id: 10): Provide detailed instructions for the client's aftercare routine.
*   **Client Signature** (id: 11): Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the content.
*   **Artist Signature** (id: 12): Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the content.
*   **Signature Date** (id: 13): Date the signature to ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
