# COVID 19 Health Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants before administering the COVID-19 vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification or medical record.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Enter your phone number and email address for contact.
4. Give your street address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Provide the name and contact number of your emergency contact.
6. Indicate whether you are interested in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine.
7. Select your choice regarding being willing to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
8. If you have any prior allergic reactions to vaccines, describe them.
9. Confirm whether you have any pre-existing medical conditions that could affect the vaccine.
10. Describe your medical conditions if they affect the vaccine.
11. Indicate whether you are pregnant or nursing.
12. Select whether you have reviewed and understand vaccine information.
13. Confirm whether you understand the risks and benefits of the vaccination.
14. Indicate if this choice is made voluntarily.
15. Confirm that you are aware of and agree to waive your rights to pursue legal action.
16. Date and sign this form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (Participant Full Name, text, required): Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification or medical record.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, required): Provide your street address.
* **City, State, Zip Code** (City, State, Zip Code, text, required): Enter your city, state, and zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **-- Vaccine Interest --** (Vaccine Interest, note, required): This field is for information only and does not require input.
* **Are you willing to receive COVID-19 vaccine** (Are you willing to receive COVID-19 vaccine, select_one, required): Select Yes, I Am Willing or No, I Am Not Willing.
* **Reasons for hesitation if applicable** (Reasons for hesitation if applicable, text, optional): If you have hesitation or concerns regarding the vaccine, please describe them here.
* **Prior allergic reactions to vaccines** (Prior allergic reactions to vaccines, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have had prior allergic reactions to vaccines.
* **Describe prior reactions** (Describe prior reactions, text, optional): If you have had prior reactions, describe them here.
* **Pre-existing medical conditions affecting vaccine** (Pre-existing medical conditions affecting vaccine, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have any pre-existing medical conditions that could affect the vaccine.
* **Describe medical conditions** (Describe medical conditions, text, optional): If you have medical conditions that could affect the vaccine, describe them here.
* **Are you pregnant or nursing** (Are you pregnant or nursing, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are pregnant or nursing.
* **-- Understanding and Acknowledgment --** (Understanding and Acknowledgment, note, required): This field is for information only and does not require input.
* **I have reviewed vaccine information** (I have reviewed vaccine information, select_one, required): Confirm that you have reviewed vaccine information.
* **I understand risks and benefits of vaccination** (I understand risks and benefits of vaccination, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the risks and benefits of the vaccination.
* **This is my voluntary choice** (This is my voluntary choice, select_one, required): Indicate whether this is your voluntary choice.
* **I waive my rights to pursue legal action** (I waive my rights to pursue legal action, select_one, required): Confirm that you are aware of and agree to waive your rights to pursue legal action.
* **Date of Waiver** (Date of Waiver, date, required): Date and sign this form to confirm your agreement.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider or relevant medical staff.
