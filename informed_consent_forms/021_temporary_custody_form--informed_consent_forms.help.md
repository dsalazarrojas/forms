# Temporary Custody Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for temporary custody arrangements. It is intended for situations where a child's parents or guardians need to agree on or document temporary custody arrangements, such as when one parent needs to temporarily take care of the child or when the child is staying with the other parent's family. The form is meant to facilitate open communication and understanding between parents, ensuring that everyone is on the same page and aware of the terms of the temporary custody agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form.
2. Fill out the information for Parent 1 and Parent 2, including any necessary details about the child's name, contact information, and the reason for the temporary custody arrangement.
3. Describe the child custody arrangement, including the dates and conditions of the arrangement.
4. Sign and date both the Parent 1 and Parent 2 sections.
5. Add any notes or comments as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent 1 Information**: This section is for the parent or guardian providing temporary care of the child. Enter your name, contact information (phone number and email), and a brief explanation for the temporary custody arrangement.
* **Parent 2 Information**: This section is for the other parent or guardian involved. Enter your name, contact information (phone number and email), and a brief explanation for the temporary custody arrangement.
* **Child Custody Arrangement**: Describe the temporary custody arrangement, including the dates (start and end dates) and any conditions or restrictions.
* **Temporary Custody Start Date**: Enter the date when the temporary custody arrangement will begin.
* **Temporary Custody End Date**: Enter the date when the temporary custody arrangement will end.
* **Parent 1 Signature**: Sign and date this section as proof of your agreement to the temporary custody arrangement.
* **Parent 2 Signature**: Sign and date this section as proof of your agreement to the temporary custody arrangement.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the temporary custody arrangement. This is an optional section for further clarification or context.

## Tips
- Be sure to keep the notes section for any additional information that might be helpful to refer back to in the future.
- Make sure both parents or guardians have reviewed and signed the form before submitting it.
- Store the completed form in a safe and secure location, such as a file or digital storage.
