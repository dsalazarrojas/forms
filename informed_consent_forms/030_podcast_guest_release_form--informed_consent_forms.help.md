# Podcast Guest Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests about their release preferences for their podcast episode. It is intended to be used by podcast hosts and producers to gather the necessary details for copyright and liability purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the guest's information, including their name, email, and other relevant details.
2.  Provide the URL of the podcast where the episode will be released.
3.  Enter the title of the podcast and the episode title.
4.  Choose the channels where you would like to release the episode (e.g., social media, specific channels, etc.).
5.  Select the date and time when you would like the release to expire.
6.  Review your answers and submit the form.
