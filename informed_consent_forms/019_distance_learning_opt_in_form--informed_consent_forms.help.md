# Distance Learning Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students, teachers, or other stakeholders regarding their experiences and preferences with distance learning. This data will help us better understand the feasibility and demand of offering distance learning options within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Ensure you are filling this form for the intended purpose and are not duplicating efforts.
* Please select one option for the school plan in the dropdown list.
* Enter numerical values for fields where applicable.
* Select the date and time the form was conducted using the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distance Learning Opt In Form** (`1`, `number`, required): This form is the title of the form, indicating the purpose of the form.
* **School Plan** (`2`, `select_one`, required): This field asks you to select one of the provided options for the school plan.
* **Students Enrolled** (`3`, `number`, required): Enter the number of students who have opted-in for distance learning.
* **Students Who Did Not Enroll** (`4`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of students who did not enroll in distance learning (if applicable).
* **Reasons for Not Enrolling** (`5`, `text`, required): Please enter the reasons why students did not enroll in distance learning.
* **Comments** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about distance learning.
* **School Name** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school or institution you are affiliated with.
* **Students Count** (`8`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of students associated with the school or institution.
* **Date Conducted** (`9`, `date`, required): Select the date the distance learning form was conducted.
* **Time Conducted** (`10`, `time`, required): Select the time the distance learning form was conducted.

## Tips

* Please be accurate and thorough in your responses to provide valuable feedback.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
