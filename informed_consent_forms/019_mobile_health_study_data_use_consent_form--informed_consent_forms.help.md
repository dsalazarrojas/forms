# Mobile Health Study Data Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in a mobile health study, enabling researchers to collect and use their data while respecting their privacy and confidentiality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow these steps:
   - Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field. This number will be used to contact you with any questions or concerns regarding the study.
   - Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field. This email will be used for further communication related to the study.
   - Read and understand the "Consent to Data Collection" field carefully. This is a critical section that indicates your agreement to participate in the study and provide data.
   - In the "Consent to Use for Research" field, confirm your understanding of how your data will be used in research activities.
   - In the "Consent to Share with Participants" field, specify whether you agree or disagree to share your data with other participants.
   - Sign your name in the "Participant Signature" field to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, number, required): Enter your primary contact number where you can be reached with any questions or concerns regarding the study.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required): Enter your email address where further communication related to the study will be sent.
* **Consent to Data Collection** (`Consent to Data Collection`, number, required): This field indicates your agreement to participate in the study and provide data. Please read the instructions carefully before signing.
* **Consent to Use for Research** (`Consent to Use for Research`, number, required): Confirm your understanding of how your data will be used in research activities.
* **Consent to Share with Participants** (`Consent to Share with Participants`, number, required): Specify whether you agree or disagree to share your data with other participants.
* **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`, note, required): Sign your name to confirm your consent to the terms and conditions outlined in the form.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you have read and understood each field before signing the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us using your contact number or email.
- Remember, your data will only be used as agreed upon in this form.
- Once you have signed the form, your data will be considered valid for use according to your consent.
