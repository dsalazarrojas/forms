# yoga_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather emergency contact information from clients and obtain their consent to be contacted in case of an emergency.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, as it is a legally binding document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): This field is not required but is the first field that you will see on the page. It is meant to be a brief description of who the emergency contact is.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and you, such as "Client", "Family Member", "Spouse", "Partner", "Friend", or "Other".
* **Report Injury** (`emergency_report_injury`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you have experienced any injuries, select the type(s) of injury you experienced.
* **Emergency Contact Agree** (`emergency_contact_agree`, `select_one`, required: false): Check "True" to agree that the emergency contact is authorized to make medical decisions on your behalf.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign the form to indicate that you have read and understood the information provided.

## Tips
* Please note that this form is intended to be filled out by the client themselves, so ensure that the client understands their role in the informed consent process.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask your administrator or healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form should not be used for other purposes, such as insurance claims or payment processing.
