# Medical Record Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Record Release Form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients regarding their medical records. This form ensures that patients are aware of and agree to the terms of their record release, which is crucial for maintaining the accuracy and integrity of medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your basic patient information.
2.  Provide your medical history, including any relevant details about your medical conditions.
3.  Specify the medical organization you are affiliated with or have a relationship with.
4.  Indicate whether you grant access to your medical record or not.
5.  Provide the reason for your record release request.
6.  Sign the form to confirm your consent.
7.  Enter the date and time of signing.
8.  Provide your medical record number (if applicable).
9.  If necessary, specify the record status.
10. Finally, confirm whether you grant or deny access to your medical records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, Text, Required): This field is used to collect general information about the patient, such as name, contact details, etc.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Text, Required): Provide any medical conditions you have, surgeries, or previous treatments you have undergone.
* **Medical Organization** (`medical_organization`, Text, Required): If you have a relationship with a specific medical organization, please provide its details.
* **Record Access** (`record_access`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you grant or deny access to your medical record.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, Text, Required): Briefly describe the reason for requesting access to your medical record.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, Note, Required): Sign this form to confirm your consent.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of signing.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Required): Enter the time of signing.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, Text, Required): If applicable, provide your medical record number.
* **Record Status** (`record_status`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose whether your medical record is Active, Inactive, or Unknown.
* **Medical Record Release** (`medical_record_release`, Select One, Required): Decide whether you grant or deny access to your medical record.
* **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, Note, Required): Sign this form if you have authorized someone else to access your medical records.

## Tips

- Please make sure to complete the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your medical provider for guidance.
- Remember to sign the form to confirm your consent.
