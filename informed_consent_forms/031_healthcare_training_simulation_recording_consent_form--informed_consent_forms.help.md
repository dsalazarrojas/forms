# Healthcare Training Simulation Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture patient information and details about a healthcare training simulation. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your data is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Patient Name** in the "patient name" field.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "date of birth" field.
3. Enter your **Contact Number** in the "contact number" field.
4. Enter your **Address** in the "address" field.
5. Select the **Date of Simulation** in the "date of simulation" field.
6. Select the **Start Time** of the simulation in the "start time" field.
7. Select the **End Time** of the simulation in the "end time" field.
8. Choose the **Simulation Type** from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter a brief description of the simulation in the "simulation description" field.
10. Enter the **Simulation Location** in the "simulation location" field.
11. Enter the **Simulation Facility** in the "simulation facility" field.
12. Enter the name of the **Simulation Instructor** in the "simulation instructor" field.
13. Enter the name of the **Simulation Supervisor** in the "simulation supervisor" field.
14. Enter the name of the **Simulation Recorder** in the "simulation recorder" field.
15. Select the **Simulation Date** in the "simulation date" field.
16. Select the **Simulation Time** in the "simulation time" field.
17. Confirm if you have given **Patient Informed Consent** in the "patient informed consent" field.
18. Sign your name for the **Patient Signature** in the "patient signature" field.
19. Confirm if the simulation was **Recorded** in the "simulation recorded" field.
20. Enter the name of the **Recorded By** in the "recorded by" field.
21. Enter the **Record Type** in the "record type" field.
22. Enter the **Patient Informed Consent Date** in the "patient informed consent date" field.
23. Enter the name of the person who gave you **Patient Informed Consent By** in the "patient informed consent by" field.
24. Select the status of your **Patient Informed Consent** in the "patient informed consent status" field.
25. Select the status of your **Patient Signature** in the "patient signature status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Contact Number** (4, text, required): Enter your contact number (phone number or email address).
* **Address** (5, note, required): Enter your current address or the address where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Simulation** (6, date, required): Select the date of the simulation.
* **Start Time** (7, time, required): Select the start time of the simulation.
* **End Time** (8, time, required): Select the end time of the simulation.
* **Simulation Type** (9, select_one, required): Choose the type of simulation you participated in.
* **Simulation Description** (10, text, required): Enter a brief description of the simulation.
* **Simulation Location** (11, text, required): Enter the location of the simulation.
* **Simulation Facility** (12, text, required): Enter the facility where the simulation took place.
* **Simulation Instructor** (13, text, required): Enter the name of the simulation instructor.
* **Simulation Supervisor** (14, text, required): Enter the name of the simulation supervisor.
* **Simulation Recorder** (15, text, required): Enter the name of the simulation recorder.
* **Simulation Date** (16, date, required): Select the date of the simulation.
* **Simulation Time** (17, time, required): Select the time of the simulation.
* **Patient Informed Consent** (18, select_one, required): Confirm that you have given your informed consent for the simulation.
* **Patient Signature** (19, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your informed consent.
* **Simulation Recorded** (20, select_one, required): Confirm if the simulation was recorded.
* **Recorded By** (21, text, required): Enter the name of who recorded the simulation.
* **Record Type** (22, text, required): Enter the type of recording (e.g., audio, video).
* **Patient Informed Consent Date** (23, date, required): Enter the date you gave your informed consent.
* **Patient Informed Consent By** (24, text, required): Enter the name of who gave you informed consent.
* **Patient Informed Consent Status** (25, select_one, required): Select the status of your informed consent (Active, Inactive, Expired).
* **Patient Signature Status** (26, select_one, required): Select the status of your signature (Active, Inactive, Expired).
