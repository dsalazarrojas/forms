# Professional Counselling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended for professional counsellors to obtain informed consent from their clients. It captures essential information for the treatment process, including the client's name, consent date and time, contact methods, and agreement terms. This form helps establish a clear understanding between the counsellor and the client, ensuring both parties are aware of their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and the client's name in the respective text fields.
2. Select the date and time of consent using the date and time fields.
3. Choose your preferred contact methods (e.g., phone, email, text/message) from the select multiple options.
4. Sign the client's signature field and the counsellor's signature field to confirm your agreement and consent.
5. Select the consent status (Agreed, Refused, or Unknown) from the select one options.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the notes field.
7. Enter your agreement terms and date in the client agreement fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (<form_title>): Enter a brief title for the counselling session or agreement.
* **Client Name** (<client_name>): Enter the client's full name.
* **Consent Date** (<consent_date>): Select the date of the consent.
* **Consent Time** (<consent_date_time>): Select the time of the consent.
* **Client Relationship** (<client_relationship>): Briefly describe your relationship with the client.
* **Therapist Name** (<therapist_name>): Enter your name as the counsellor.
* **Therapist Title** (<therapist_title>): Enter your title or profession (e.g., counsellor, therapist).
* **Client Contact Info** (<client_contact_info>): Enter the client's contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Contact Methods** (<contact_methods>): Choose how you want to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, text/message).
* **Client Signature** (<client_signature>): Sign to confirm your agreement and consent.
* **Therapist Signature** (<therapist_signature>): Sign to confirm your agreement and consent.
* **Consent Status** (<consent_status>): Indicate whether the client agreed, refused, or is unknown.
* **Notes** (<notes>): Add any additional comments or information about the client.
* **Client Agreement** (<client_agreement>): Enter your agreement terms with the client.
* **Client Agreement Date** (<client_agreement_date>): Select the date of the agreement.
* **Notes for Treatment** (<notes_for_treatment>): Add any notes or comments about the treatment process.

## Tips

* Ensure you understand the form's purpose and the client's expectations before completing this form.
* Review and discuss each field with the client to ensure clarity and agreement.
* Keep the form up-to-date and accurate for future reference and treatment planning.
