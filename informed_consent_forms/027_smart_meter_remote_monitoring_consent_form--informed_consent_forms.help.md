# smart_meter_remote_monitoring_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from individuals who wish to participate in remote monitoring of their smart meters. The form collects essential information to ensure that participants' rights are respected and that all necessary data is accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name.
2. Enter your Last name.
3. Provide your Contact phone number.
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Sign and date the form to indicate your understanding of the remote monitoring terms and conditions.
6. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
7. Date the form to confirm the date of your consent.
8. (Optional) Enter the name of the provider responsible for monitoring your smart meter.
9. (Optional) Enter the name of your property manager.
10. Add any additional notes about your participation in the remote monitoring program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/true): Enter your last name.
- **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/true): Enter your contact phone number. This is used to contact you in case of any issues or questions regarding your smart meter.
- **Email address** (`email`, email, required/true): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
- **Signature date** (`signature_date`, date, optional/false): Date and sign the form to confirm you have read and understood the remote monitoring terms and conditions.
- **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/true): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
- **Consent date** (`consent_date`, date, required/true): Date the form to confirm the date of your consent.
- **Provider name** (`provider_name`, text, optional/false): Enter the name of the provider responsible for monitoring your smart meter. If you are unsure who this is, please leave this field blank.
- **Property manager name** (`manager_name`, text, optional/false): Enter the name of your property manager. If you are unsure who this is, please leave this field blank.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional/false): Add any additional notes about your participation in the remote monitoring program.
