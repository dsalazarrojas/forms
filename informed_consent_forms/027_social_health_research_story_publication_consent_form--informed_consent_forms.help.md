# Social Health Research Story Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a Social Health Research Story Publication Consent Form, which is used to obtain informed consent from participants to publish their research stories. It is essential to ensure that all participants understand the purpose and scope of the research study and agree to its publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" from the "Research Story Form" field to indicate your willingness to participate in the research study and publish your story.
2. Enter your name, contact information, and other relevant details in the "Name", "Contact Information", "Email", "Phone", and "Location" fields, if required.
3. Enter your preferred date for the research story publication in the "Research Story Publication" field.
4. If you have any concerns about the publication of your story, please express them in the "Story Publication Anonymity" field.
5. Read and agree to the "Participant Agreement" and "Researcher Agreement" fields, which outline the terms and conditions of the research study.
6. Enter your institution and research team information, if applicable, in the "Researcher Organization", "Researcher Institution", and "Research Team" fields.
7. Enter your research study ID, if provided.
8. Finally, sign and date the form by completing the "Consent Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Story Form**: Check "Yes" if you are willing to participate in the research study and allow your story to be published. Check "No" if you decline.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Contact Information**: Provide your contact information (e.g., phone number, email address) so that we can reach you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Location**: Enter your location (e.g., city, state, country) where you can be reached.
* **Research Story**: Please provide a brief description of your research story.
* **Publication Preferences**: Check "Yes" if you agree to publish your story with your name, and "No" if you prefer to remain anonymous.
* **Research Story Publication**: Enter the date you would like your story to be published.
* **Story Publication Anonymity**: If you have concerns about the publication of your story, please express them here.
* **Participant Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the research study.
* **Researcher Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the research study.
* **Researcher Organization**: Enter your research organization and department/unit.
* **Researcher Institution**: Enter your research institution.
* **Research Team**: Enter your research team name and member(s).
* **Research Study ID**: Enter the ID number of your research study, if provided.
* **Consent Date**: Sign and date the form to indicate your consent to participate and publish your story.
