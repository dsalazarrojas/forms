# integrity_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The integrity declaration form is used to declare the company's integrity in its financial transactions, business practices, and other related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company details section with your company's name and any other relevant information.
2. Declare your agreement or disagreement with the company's integrity in the "i_agree" section.
3. Fill in the declaration date and time in the "declaration_date" and "declaration_time" fields.
4. Provide any additional declaration notes in the "declaration_notes" field.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
6. List any relevant company subsidaries, person involved, employees, directors, partners, affiliates, and review notes.
7. Review and submit the form to declare your company's integrity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **company_details**:
	+ (Required) Enter your company's name and any other relevant information.
* **company_name**:
	+ (Required) Enter your company's name.
* **company_description**:
	+ Optional) Enter a brief description of your company.
* **declaration_details**:
	+ (Required) Declare your company's integrity in its financial transactions, business practices, and other related matters.
* **i_agree**:
	+ (Required) Select 'Yes' if you agree with the company's integrity declaration, or 'No' if you disagree.
* **i_decline**:
	+ (Optional) Select 'Yes' if you wish to decline the declaration, or 'No' if you do not.
* **declaration_date**:
	+ (Required) Enter the date of the declaration.
* **declaration_time**:
	+ (Required) Enter the time of the declaration.
* **declaration_signing**:
	+ (Optional) Provide any additional declaration notes.
* **declaration_notes**:
	+ (Optional) Enter any additional notes or comments about the declaration.
* **declaration_email**:
	+ (Required) Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **declaration_phone**:
	+ (Optional) Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **declaration_address**:
	+ (Optional) Enter your company's address for contact purposes.
* **company_subsidaries**:
	+ (Optional) List any relevant company subsidaries.
* **declaration_person**:
	+ (Optional) Enter the person involved in the declaration.
* **declaration_person_share**:
	+ (Optional) Enter the person's share in the declaration.
* **declaration_employees**:
	+ (Optional) List the number of employees involved in the declaration.
* **declaration_employees_share**:
	+ (Optional) Enter the share of employees in the declaration.
* **declaration_directors**:
	+ (Optional) List the number of directors involved in the declaration.
* **declaration_directors_share**:
	+ (Optional) Enter the share of directors in the declaration.
* **declaration_partners**:
	+ (Optional) List any partners involved in the declaration.
* **declaration_partners_share**:
	+ (Optional) Enter the share of partners in the declaration.
* **declaration_affiliates**:
	+ (Optional) List any affiliates involved in the declaration.
* **declaration_affiliates_share**:
	+ (Optional) Enter the share of affiliates in the declaration.
* **declaration_review**:
	+ (Optional) Enter any review notes about the declaration.

Note: Some fields are marked as (Required) or (Optional) to indicate their mandatory status. Please fill in the required fields accurately and truthfully.
