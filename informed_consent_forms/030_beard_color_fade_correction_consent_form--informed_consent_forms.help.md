# Beard Color Fade Correction Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from clients before undergoing a beard color correction treatment. It ensures that clients are aware of the risks and procedures involved in the treatment and have given their consent to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal and demographic information in the first five fields: First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Provide your appointment details in the next field: Appointment Date.
3. Inform your barber or stylist about the service provider in the next field: Barber or Stylist Name.
4. Enter your current and desired beard color information in the next two fields: Current Beard Color and Desired Color Result.
5. If you have any previous color treatments, provide this information in the field: Previous Color Treatments.
6. Answer questions about your medical history in the fields: Hair Dye Allergies, Patch Test Required, and Scalp or Skin Conditions.
7. Confirm your understanding of the treatment risks, possible reactions, aftercare instructions, and color results variability in the next three fields: I Understand Treatment Risks, I Understand Possible Reactions, and I Understand Color Results May Vary.
8. Finally, indicate your consent to proceed with the treatment by selecting the correct option in the field: I Consent to Proceed.
9. Sign your name in the last field: Client Signature, and enter the date you signed in the field below: Signature Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Barber or Stylist Name** (`barber_stylist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your service provider.
* **Current Beard Color** (`current_beard_color`, text, required): Enter your current beard color.
* **Desired Color Result** (`desired_color`, text, required): Enter the desired color result for your beard.
* **Previous Color Treatments** (`previous_color_treatments`, text, required): Enter any previous color treatments you have undergone.
* **Hair Dye Allergies** (`hair_dye_allergies`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Yes - PPD Allergy, Yes - Other Allergy, No Known Allergies, Not Sure.
* **Patch Test Required** (`patch_test_required`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Yes - Completed, Yes - Scheduled, No - Not Done.
* **Scalp or Skin Conditions** (`scalp_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any scalp or skin conditions, enter them here.
* **I Understand Treatment Risks** (`treatment_risks_understood`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: 'True', 'False'.
* **I Understand Possible Reactions** (`possible_reactions`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: 'True', 'False'.
* **I Understand Aftercare Instructions** (`aftercare_instructions`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: 'True', 'False'.
* **I Understand Color Results May Vary** (`color_guarantee`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: 'True', 'False'.
* **I Consent to Proceed** (`consent_to_proceed`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: 'True', 'False'.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
