# Transcript Release Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Transcript Release Authorization Form is used to authorize the release of a student's transcript to a specific recipient. This form is required for students who are requesting a transcript for external purposes, such as applying to colleges or universities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Student Information section with your name, date of birth, and student ID number.
2. Select the type of transcript you are requesting (Official or Unofficial).
3. Enter the recipient's name, email address, and address.
4. Select who is authorized to release the transcript (Student, Parent, or Guardian).
5. Enter the reason for the request.
6. Date the request was made.
7. Sign and date the form with your student signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Student ID Number, Student Name, Student Date of Birth, Student Address, Student Phone, Student Signature)**: Enter your student information, including your student ID number, name, date of birth, address, phone number, and signature.
* **What type of transcript are you requesting (Transcript Type)**: Select whether you are requesting an Official or Unofficial transcript.
* **Recipient Information (Recipient Name, Recipient Email, Recipient Address)**: Enter the recipient's name, email address, and physical address.
* **Authorized By (Authorized By)**: Select who is authorized to release the transcript (Student, Parent, or Guardian).
* **Authorized By Note (Authorized By Note)**: Enter any additional information about who is authorized to release the transcript.
* **Reason for Request (Reason for Request)**: Enter the reason you are requesting the transcript.
* **Date Requested (Date Requested)**: Enter the date the transcript was requested.
* **School Information (School Name, School Code, School Phone, School Email, School Address, School Signature, School Date of Birth)**: Enter the school's information, including name, code, phone number, email address, physical address, and signature.
* **School Information Note (School Information Note)**: Enter any additional information about the school.
* **Recipient School Address (Recipient School Address)**: Enter the recipient school's address.
* **Recipient School Phone (Recipient School Phone)**: Enter the recipient school's phone number.
* **Recipient School Email (Recipient School Email)**: Enter the recipient school's email address.
* **School Signature (School Signature)**: Enter the school's signature.
* **School Date of Birth (School Date of Birth)**: Enter the school's date of birth.
* **Authorized By School (Authorized By School)**: Select who is authorized to release the transcript for the school (Student, Parent, or Guardian).
* **Authorized By School Note (Authorized By School Note)**: Enter any additional information about who is authorized to release the transcript for the school.
