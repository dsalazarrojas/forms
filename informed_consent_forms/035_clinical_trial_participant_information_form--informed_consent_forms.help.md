# Clinical Trial Participant Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Clinical Trial Participant Information Form is a critical document that collects essential information from participants in clinical trials. This form is designed to ensure that each participant's data is accurate, complete, and reliable. The information collected will be used by the study team to assess participant eligibility and prepare them for study visits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Carefully read the instructions for each field and ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2.  Fill in the required fields (\* marked) accurately and thoroughly.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the study team.
4.  Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Study ID** (\*): This is the unique identifier assigned to your study. Please ensure that you enter the correct ID number.
*   **Study Title**: This is the title of the study you are participating in. It is not required but helps the study team understand the context of the form.
*   **Study Site ID**: This is the identifier for the study site where you will be participating. Please ensure that you enter the correct ID number.
*   **First Name** (\*): This is your first name.
*   **Last Name** (\*): This is your last name.
*   **Date of Birth** (\*): This is your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Age** (\*): This is your current age.
*   **Gender** (\*): Please select your gender from the options provided.
*   **Race** (Optional): This is your racial or ethnic identity. Select one of the provided options or choose "Other" if it is not listed.
*   **Ethnicity** (Optional): This is your ethnicity. Select one of the provided options or choose "Other" if it is not listed.
*   **Street Address** (\*): This is your current street address.
*   **City** (\*): This is your city or town where you currently live.
*   **State or Province** (\*): This is your state or province of residence.
*   **Zip or Postal Code** (\*): This is your zip or postal code.
*   **Country** (\*): This is your country of residence.
*   **Phone Number** (\*): This is your current phone number.
*   **Email Address** (\*): This is your email address.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (\*): This is the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (\*): This describes your relationship with your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (\*): This is your emergency contact's phone number.
*   **Primary Care Physician Name**: This is the name of your primary care physician. If you do not have one, please enter "None".
*   **Primary Care Physician Phone**: This is your primary care physician's phone number. If you do not have one, please enter "None".
*   **Health Insurance Provider**: This is your health insurance provider. If you do not have one, please enter "None".
*   **Insurance Policy Number**: This is your insurance policy number. If you do not have one, please enter "None".
*   **Current Occupation**: This is your current occupation. If you are not working, please enter "Not working".
*   **Education Level**: This is your highest level of education completed. Select one of the provided options.
*   **Smoking Status**: This describes your smoking status. Select one of the provided options.
*   **Alcohol Use**: This describes your alcohol use. Select one of the provided options.
*   **Primary Diagnosis or Condition** (\*): This is your primary diagnosis or medical condition.
*   **Date of Diagnosis**: If applicable, this is the date of your diagnosis in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Current Medications** (\*): This is a list of your current medications with dosages.
*   **Medication Allergies**: If you have allergies to any medications, list them here.
*   **Other Allergies**: If you have allergies to anything else, list them here.
*   **Relevant Medical History** (\*): Please list any relevant medical history, including past surgeries.
*   **Family Medical History**: If any medical history in your family is relevant to the study, list it here.
*   **Have You Participated in Research Before?**: This asks if you have participated in research studies before. Select "True" or "False".
*   **Informed Consent Status** (\*): This indicates your informed consent status. Select one of the provided options.
*   **Consent Date**: If you have signed consent, enter the date of consent.
*   **Eligibility Status** (\*): This indicates your eligibility status. Select one of the provided options.
*   **Enrollment Date**: If eligible, this is the date of your enrollment.
*   **How Did You Hear About This Study?**: This describes how you heard about the study. Select one of the provided options.
*   **Do You Have Reliable Transportation?**: This indicates your transportation availability. Select one of the provided options.
*   **Availability for Study Visits**: This describes your visit availability. Select one of the provided options.
*   **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or information you would like to share about yourself can be listed here.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
*   Ensure that you answer all required fields (\*).
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the study team for clarification.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
