# Child Travel Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Travel Consent Form is a crucial document that provides parents or legal guardians with the opportunity to provide information about their child's travel plans, including their consent, emergency contact details, and medical clearance. This form helps ensure the child's safety and well-being during the trip.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the child's and parent/guardian's details such as name, address, email, phone, and date of birth.
2. Provide the child's travel destination, departure and return dates, travel method, and purpose of the trip.
3. Fill in the accommodation details and medical information such as medical conditions, allergies, and medications.
4. Specify any emergency contact and relationship information.
5. Consent to medical treatment in case of an emergency and indicate any medical limitations.
6. Indicate whether the child is authorized to return with the same travel group.
7. Add any additional permissions or restrictions.
8. Finally, sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date**: Date of travel consent. (Required)
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Legal first name of parent or guardian giving consent. (Required)
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Legal last name of parent or guardian giving consent. (Required)
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Primary email address for contact. (Required)
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Primary phone number for contact. (Required)
* **Parent or Guardian Address**: Full street address. (Required)
* **Child First Name**: Legal first name of the child. (Required)
* **Child Last Name**: Legal last name of the child. (Required)
* **Child Date of Birth**: Please provide the child's birth date. (Required)
* **Child Passport Number**: Passport number if applicable. (Optional)
* **Travel Destination**: Full destination including city and country. (Required)
* **Travel Departure Date**: Date of departure. (Required)
* **Travel Return Date**: Date of return. (Required)
* **Travel Purpose**: Purpose of the travel. (Select One: Vacation, Family visit, School trip, Sports event, Performance, Religious pilgrimage, Other) (Required)
* **Accompanying Persons**: List all adults who will be accompanying the child. (Required)
* **Relationship to Child**: Relationship of accompanying adults to the child. (Required)
* **Transportation Method**: How will the child be traveling? (Select One: Airplane, Car, Bus, Train, Boat or ship, Multiple methods) (Required)
* **Accommodation Details**: Where will the child be staying? (Required)
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Full name of emergency contact. (Required)
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number for emergency contact. (Required)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Relationship of emergency contact to child. (Select One: Parent, Guardian, Grandparent, Aunt or Uncle, Family friend, Other) (Required)
* **Medical Insurance Information**: Provider name and policy number. (Required)
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies. (Required)
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications with dosages. (Optional)
* **Medical Treatment Consent**: Do you consent to emergency medical treatment if needed? (Select One: Yes, I fully consent, Yes, but contact me first, Yes, with limitations noted, No, do not consent) (Required)
* **Medical Limitations**: Specify any limitations on medical consent. (Optional)
* **Return Travel Authorization**: Do you authorize the child to return with the same group? (Select One: Yes, same group, Yes, with different chaperones, No, different return arrangements, Child will not return with group) (Required)
* **Additional Permissions or Restrictions**: Any additional permissions or restrictions. (Optional)
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature. (Required)
* **Signature Date**: Date of electronic signature. (Required)
