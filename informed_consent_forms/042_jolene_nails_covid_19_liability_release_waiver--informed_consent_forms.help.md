# Jolene Nails COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Liability Release Waiver form is designed to collect informed consent from clients before starting COVID-19-related treatments or services at Jolene Nails.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Review the form**: Carefully read through each field to understand what information is required.
2. **Enter your information**: Fill in your patient name and healthcare provider name (if applicable) in the respective fields.
3. **Select an answer**: For the "accept the terms of service" field, select "I Agree" to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or official documents. This field is for your reference and is not a required field.
* **accept the terms of service (2)**: This field is a select-one field where you can choose to "I Agree" or "I Disagree" to indicate your acceptance of the terms of service. If you select "I Agree", you confirm that you have read and understood the terms of service. If you select "I Disagree", you will be unable to proceed with your treatment.
* **patient name (3)**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on any documents or correspondence related to your treatment.
* **healthcare provider name (4)**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider, if applicable. If you are not a healthcare provider, this field can be left blank.

## Tips
* Please take the time to carefully read and understand the terms of service before selecting "I Agree".
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, do not hesitate to contact the form maintainer at form@gic.mx.
* This form is intended to be used in conjunction with other medical forms and documents that you may have been provided by Jolene Nails.
