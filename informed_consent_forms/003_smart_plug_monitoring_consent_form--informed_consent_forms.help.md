# Smart Plug Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals or their legal representatives to provide informed consent for monitoring smart devices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Consent**: Enter your agreement or disagreement to the monitoring of your smart plug devices. This is not a required field but is highly recommended for users to express their consent.

2. **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information so we can reach you for any queries or updates.

3. **Signature**: Add your signature as proof of your consent. This can be a digital or handwritten signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent**: A simple text field where you can type your agreement or disagreement to the monitoring of your smart plug devices. 
* **Contact Details**: This field is optional and allows you to enter your name, email address or phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Signature**: A field to add your digital or handwritten signature as proof of your consent.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you understand the purpose and risks of monitoring your smart plug devices.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
- You are free to revoke your consent at any time by contacting us.
- We will not share your contact details with any third party without your explicit consent.
