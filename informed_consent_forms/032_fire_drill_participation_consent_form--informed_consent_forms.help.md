# Fire Drill Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants in a fire drill exercise, ensuring they are aware of the necessary information and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through the form carefully and understand the questions and instructions.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3.  If any information is missing or incorrect, please contact your organization's management or the person responsible for the form.
4.  Make sure to save your progress and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Info (`participant_info`):** This field is a brief text area for you to provide any relevant information about yourself as a participant.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Contact Info (`contact_info`):** Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`):** Check if you have an emergency contact person.
    *   Type: Select One
    *   Options: Yes, No
    *   Required: No
*   **Medical Info (`medical_info`):** If you have any medical conditions, please describe them here.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Allergy/Medical Condition (`allergy_medical_condition`):** If you have any allergies or medical conditions, please indicate here.
    *   Type: Select Multiple
    *   Options: Yes, No
    *   Required: No
*   **Emergency Contact Name (`emergency_contact_name`):** Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Contact Relationship (`contact_relationship`):** Choose the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
    *   Type: Select One
    *   Options: Yes, No
    *   Required: No
*   **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`):** Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Emergency Contact Email (`emergency_contact_email`):** Enter your emergency contact person's email address.
    *   Type: Email
    *   Required: No
*   **Other Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact_other`):** If you have other emergency contacts, provide their details here.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Relationship Other (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`):** Describe the relationship between you and your other emergency contact person.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Acknowledgment (`acknowledgment`):** Confirm your understanding and acceptance of the emergency procedures.
    *   Type: Select Multiple
    *   Options: Yes, No
    *   Required: No
*   **Emergency Info (`emergency_info`):** If you have any additional emergency information, please provide it here.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No

## Tips

*   Please make sure to answer all the questions accurately and truthfully.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to your organization's management or the person responsible for the form.
*   Complete the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure the participant's safety and the success of the fire drill.
