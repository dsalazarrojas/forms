# Paintball Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from participants in a paintball event. It serves two main purposes:
*   Gathering details for emergency contact in case of an accident or medical issue
*   Releasing liability for the event

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill out all required fields.
2.  If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or fill it out if you want to provide additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Paintball Waiver Form** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
This is a general field that likely prompts for your name, which will be used for identification within the form. It's not required to provide your name if it's not necessary for the event organizers.

### **2. Paintball Event Name** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
This field is used to note the specific paintball event you are participating in. You can type the name of the event as it was advertised or described to you.

### **3. Paintball Event Date** (`<name>`, date, `false`): <br>
Fill out the date of the event. This helps event organizers keep track of which events are taking place and when.

### **4. Paintball Event Time** (`<name>`, time, `false`): <br>
Enter the time the event is scheduled to take place.

### **5. Paintball Event Location** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
Provide the location of the paintball event, so the organizers and emergency services can easily find you if needed.

### **6. Paintball Event Organizer** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
This field is intended to capture the name of the person or organization in charge of the paintball event. If you have this information, please provide it. Otherwise, this field is not required.

### **7. Paintball Waiver Signed** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
This field might be used to confirm that you have agreed to the terms and conditions of the paintball event. You may not need to fill this out if it's not necessary for your participation.

### **8. Paintball Emergency Contact** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
Here you should list the name of the person you have arranged to contact in case of an emergency, if this applies to you.

### **9. Paintball Emergency Contact Relationship** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
Indicate your relationship with your emergency contact, such as parent, guardian, spouse, or partner.

### **10. Paintball Emergency Contact Phone** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
Fill out the contact phone number of your emergency contact in case the organizers or medical staff need to reach out to them.

### **11. Paintball Emergency Contact Address** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
Provide the address of your emergency contact so that medical or emergency services can reach them.

### **12. Paintball Event Organizer Phone** (`<name>`, text, `false`): <br>
If you have the contact number of the event organizer, please fill it out.
