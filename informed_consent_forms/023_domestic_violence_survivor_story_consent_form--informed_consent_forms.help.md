# Domestic Violence Survivor Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help assess your situation as a domestic violence survivor. The information you provide will be used to provide you with necessary support and services. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in a safe and private environment.
2. Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
3. If you need help or have questions, you can ask for assistance.
4. When you've finished, submit the form and a staff member will review it to provide you with support and services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survivor Info** (id: 1) - This field is for your basic information.
  * A brief description of yourself, including your name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (id: 2) - This field is for your contact information.
  * Please provide your name, phone number, and any other contact details you're comfortable sharing.
* **Story** (id: 3) - This field is for your story.
  * Describe your experience with domestic violence in as much detail as you can.
* **Location** (id: 4) - This field is for your location.
  * Provide the address or location where you're currently staying.
* **Date First Met Abuser** (id: 5) - This field is for the date you first met the abuser.
  * If you don't remember the exact date, provide an approximate date.
* **Abuser Info** (id: 6) - This field is for information about the abuser.
  * Describe the abuser's characteristics, including their name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Abuse Details** (id: 7) - This field is for more information about the abuse.
  * Describe the type of abuse you experienced, including physical, emotional, and financial abuse.
* **Medical Info** (id: 8) - This field is for any medical information.
  * If you've had any medical issues due to the abuse, please describe them.
* **Mental Health Info** (id: 9) - This field is for any mental health information.
  * If you've experienced any mental health issues due to the abuse, please describe them.
* **Relationship Status** (id: 10) - This field is for your relationship status.
  * Please answer whether you're currently in a relationship with the abuser or if you're not.
* **Relationship Partner Info** (id: 11) - This field is for information about your relationship partner.
  * Describe your relationship partner's characteristics, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **Support Systems** (id: 12) - This field is for your support systems.
  * List any support systems you have, such as family, friends, or support groups.
* **Safety Plans** (id: 13) - This field is for your safety plans.
  * Describe any safety plans you have in place to keep yourself safe.
* **Community Resources** (id: 14) - This field is for any community resources.
  * List any community resources you've accessed or are interested in accessing.
* **Mental Health Services** (id: 15) - This field is for any mental health services.
  * If you've accessed or are interested in accessing mental health services, please describe them.
* **Legal Services** (id: 16) - This field is for any legal services.
  * If you've accessed or are interested in accessing legal services, please describe them.
* **Emergency Contacts** (id: 17) - This field is for emergency contacts.
  * Please list any emergency contacts you have, such as emergency numbers or safe places to go.
* **Medical Services** (id: 18) - This field is for medical services.
  * If you've accessed or are interested in accessing medical services, please describe them.
* **Emergency Transportation** (id: 19) - This field is for emergency transportation.
  * Please answer whether you have a way to get to a safe place in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contacts Who Can Pick Up** (id: 20) - This field is for emergency contacts who can pick you up.
  * Please list any emergency contacts who can pick you up.
* **Emergency Contacts Who Can Pick Up Info** (id: 21) - This field is for emergency contacts who can pick you up information.
  * Please provide any relevant information about your emergency contacts who can pick you up.
* **Emergency Contacts Who Can Pick Up Relationship** (id: 23) - This field is for emergency contacts who can pick you up relationship.
  * Please answer whether your emergency contacts can pick you up and have a relationship with you.
* **Transportation Method** (id: 24) - This field is for your transportation method.
  * Please answer whether you have a way to get to a safe place in case of an emergency.
* **Transportation Method Info** (id: 25) - This field is for transportation method information.
  * If you've had issues with getting to a safe place, please describe the challenges you've faced.
