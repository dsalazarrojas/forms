# Experiment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Experiment Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from study subjects before conducting experiments. This form ensures that the subject understands and agrees to the procedures and risks involved in the experiment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your subject information, including your name and any other identifying details.
2. Indicate whether the experimental design is in place, or if there are changes to be made.
3. Answer the question regarding the study informant, whether you are acting as the study informant or not.
4. Provide your contact information, including your contact phone number and contact address.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Select the date on which you gave your consent.
7. Finally, ensure that all the information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Subject Information** (subject_information, text, required: false): This is the section where you will provide your identifying details such as your name and any other relevant information.
- **Experimental Design** (experimental_design, text, required: false): This field is where you can provide information about the experimental design, including any changes to be made.
- **Study Informant** (study_informant, select_multiple, required: false): This field will ask if you are the study informant or not. Please select 'Yes' if you are the study informant, or 'No' if you are not.
- **Contact Details** (contact_details, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to select how you would like to be addressed, such as Mr, Mrs, Ms, or Dr.
- **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required: false): This is the field where you will enter your contact phone number.
- **Consent Date** (consent_date, date, required: false): This is the date on which you gave your consent.
- **Contact Address** (contact_address, text, required: false): This is the field where you will enter your contact address.
- **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required: false): This is the field where you will enter your contact email address.
