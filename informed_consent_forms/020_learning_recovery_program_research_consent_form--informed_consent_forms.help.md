# Learning Recovery Program Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from parents/guardians for a child's participation in the Learning Recovery Program. This form is used to collect relevant personal and demographic information from parents/guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to complete this form.
2. Enter the child's school name.
3. Enter the child's name.
4. Enter the child's email address.
5. Enter the child's phone number.
6. Enter the child's address.
7. Enter the child's age.
8. Enter the child's date of birth.
9. Enter the parent/guardian's name.
10. Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
11. Select the relationship between the parent/guardian and the child.
12. Enter the consent date.
13. Sign and date the form.
14. Enter any additional notes or comments.
15. Select whether you agree or disagree to participate in the program.
16. Enter the name of the emergency contact.
17. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
18. Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the child.
19. Enter any medical conditions that may be relevant to the program.
20. Enter any medical notes or comments.
21. Enter any allergies or medical procedures that may be relevant to the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the child's school.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the child participating in the program.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the child's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the child's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the child's home address.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter the child's age.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent/guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`parent_relationship`, select_one, required: false): Select the relationship between the parent/guardian and the child (Mother, Father, Grandparent, or Other).
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the parent/guardian is giving consent.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date the form.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you agree or disagree to participate in the program.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, select_one, required: false): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the child (Mother, Father, Grandparent, or Other).
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): Enter any medical conditions that may be relevant to the program.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any medical notes or comments.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, text, required: false): Enter any allergies or medical procedures that may be relevant to the program.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Enter the child's medical history.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, required: false): Enter any medications the child may be taking.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, required: false): Enter any medical procedures that may be relevant to the program.
