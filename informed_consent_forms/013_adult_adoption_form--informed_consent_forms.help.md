# Adult Adoption Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Adoption Form is a document used to formalize a relationship between two adults. This form is typically used when an adult wants to establish or confirm their relationship with another adult.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the adopter's information:
	* **Adopter Full Name**: Enter the full name of the adult seeking to adopt.
	* **Adopter Occupation**: Enter the occupation of the adult seeking to adopt.
	* **Relationship to Adoptee**: Choose a relationship type (e.g., Step-parent, Family friend) from the provided options.
2. Fill in the adoptee's information:
	* **Adoptee Full Name**: Enter the full name of the adult to be adopted.
	* **Adoptee Date of Birth**: Enter the adoptee's date of birth. Please note that the adoptee must be at least 18 years old.
	* **Adoptee Current Address**: Enter the current address of the adoptee.
3. Provide purpose and statement of intent:
	* **Primary Objectives**: Select all applicable reasons for the adoption (e.g., Formalize parent-child relationship, Estate planning, Immigration and residency).
	* **Statement of Intent**: Briefly describe why you are seeking this adoption.
4. Confirm consents and sign the form:
	* **Adopter Consent**: Choose whether you consent to this adoption.
	* **Adoptee Consent**: Choose whether the adoptee consents to this adoption.
	* **Date of Signing**: Enter the date and time of signing.
	* **Notary Public Information**: If the form has been notarized, enter the notary public's name and commission number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Adopter Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of the adult seeking to adopt.
* **Adopter Occupation** (Text, Required): Enter the occupation of the adult seeking to adopt.
* **Relationship to Adoptee** (Text, Required): Choose a relationship type (e.g., Step-parent, Family friend) from the provided options.
* **Adoptee Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of the adult to be adopted.
* **Adoptee Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter the adoptee's date of birth. The adoptee must be at least 18 years old.
* **Adoptee Current Address** (Text, Required): Enter the current address of the adoptee.
* **Primary Objectives** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable reasons for the adoption.
* **Statement of Intent** (Text, Required): Briefly describe why you are seeking this adoption.
* **Adopter Consent** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you consent to this adoption.
* **Adoptee Consent** (Select One, Required): Choose whether the adoptee consents to this adoption.
* **Date of Signing** (Date, Required): Enter the date and time of signing.
* **Notary Public Information** (Text, Optional): If the form has been notarized, enter the notary public's name and commission number.
