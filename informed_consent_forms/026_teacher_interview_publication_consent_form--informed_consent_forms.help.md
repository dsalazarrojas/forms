# Teacher Interview Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from teachers regarding the publication of materials related to their interviews. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Select the correct publication type (Publicly or Privately).
3. Choose whether to give consent to the publication (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please fill in your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Please fill in your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **School**: Please enter the name of your school.
* **Department**: Please enter the name of your department.
* **Teacher ID**: Please enter your teacher ID number.
* **Teacher Name**: Please fill in your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Interview Date**: Please enter the date of the interview.
* **Publication Type**: Select whether the publication will be made publicly or privately available.
* **Publication Consent**: Choose whether to give consent to the publication (Yes or No). 
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.
* **Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Date**: Please enter the current date to confirm your signature.
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Role**: Please enter your role at the school (e.g. teacher, principal, etc.).
* **School District**: Please enter the name of the school district.
* **School Address**: Please enter the address of your school.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your email address.
* **Website**: If applicable, please enter your school or personal website.
* **Job Title**: Please enter your job title.
* **Teacher Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Teacher Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Teacher Website**: If applicable, please enter your school or personal website.
* **Teacher Name**: Please fill in your name as it appears on your official documents.
## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review the form before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled in correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
