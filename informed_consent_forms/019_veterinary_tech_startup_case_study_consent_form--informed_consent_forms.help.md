# veterinary_tech_startup_case_study_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent and gather information from veterinary professionals and clinic staff to contribute to a case study on veterinary technology startups. It is designed to collect contact and role information, consent, and clinic details for the purpose of the case study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and email address.
2. Select your role in the veterinary industry (vet, vet tech, or clinic staff).
3. Indicate whether you consent to participate in the case study.
4. Agree to the terms of service.
5. If you are a clinic, enter your clinic name, address, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)** (`user_info`, text, optional): Fill in your name and email address.
* **Name (2)** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email Address (3)** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Role (4)** (`role`, select_one, optional):
	+ Vet: I am a veterinarian.
	+ Vet tech: I am a veterinary technician or assistant.
	+ Clinic staff: I am staff at a veterinary clinic.
* **Consent Given (5)** (`consent_given`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes: I consent to participate in the case study.
	+ No: I do not consent to participate in the case study.
* **Agreement (6)** (`agreement`, note, optional): I understand that my participation in this case study is...
* **Terms of Service Agreement (7)** (`terms_of_service`, text, optional): Agree to the terms of service.
* **Signed Date (8)** (`signed_date`, date, optional): Date you signed this form.
* **Signed Time (9)** (`signed_time`, time, optional): Time you signed this form.
* **Signed By (10)** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Your name as signed.
* **Clinic Name (11)** (`clinic_name`, text, optional): Name of your clinic.
* **Clinic Address (12)** (`clinic_address`, text, optional): Address of your clinic.
* **Clinic Phone (13)** (`clinic_phone`, text, optional): Phone number of your clinic.
* **Clinic Email (14)** (`clinic_email`, text, optional): Email address of your clinic.

## Tips
* Please fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are representing a clinic, please fill in all clinic fields with the correct information.
* If you are an individual, please fill in your user information and role accordingly.
* If you consent to participate, please indicate so by selecting "Yes" in the Consent Given field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the form owner for clarification.
