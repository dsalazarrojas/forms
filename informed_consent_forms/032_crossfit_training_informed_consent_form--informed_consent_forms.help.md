# Crossfit Training Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants before they begin Crossfit training. It helps to ensure that trainers are aware of any medical conditions or allergies that may impact the participant's safety during training. This form is a mandatory requirement for all participants and should be completed thoroughly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name, address, and contact information under the "User Information" section.
2. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and relationship to you under the "Emergency Contact" and "Emergency Contact Relationship" sections.
3. Under "Medical Conditions," please list any existing medical conditions that may impact your training, such as diabetes, heart conditions, or allergies.
4. Describe your medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions Description" section.
5. In the "Aware of Medical Conditions" section, indicate how aware you are of your medical conditions and any concerns you may have.
6. Provide your consent to participate in the training under the "Consent" section.
7. Finally, indicate your age under the "Age" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)**: Please enter your full name, address, and contact information to ensure that we can contact you if needed.
* **Emergency Contact (2)**: Provide the name of the emergency contact person and their relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (3)**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions (4)**: List any existing medical conditions that may impact your training, such as diabetes, heart conditions, or allergies.
* **List of Medical Conditions (5)**: Provide a detailed list of your medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions Description (6)**: Describe your medical conditions in detail.
* **Aware of Medical Conditions (7)**: Indicate how aware you are of your medical conditions and any concerns you may have.
* **Consent (8)**: Provide your consent to participate in the training, acknowledging that you understand the risks and benefits involved.
* **Age (9)**: Please enter your age accurately to ensure that you are eligible for the training program.
* **Medical Equipment (10)**: If you have any medical equipment that may impact your training, such as a pacemaker or respirator, please list it here.

## Tips
* Please complete this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible training experience.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* It is essential to be honest about your medical conditions to ensure your safety during the training.
