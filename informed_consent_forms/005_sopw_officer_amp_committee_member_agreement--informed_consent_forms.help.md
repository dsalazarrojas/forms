# sopw_officer_committee_member_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for Sopw officer committee members to confirm their integrity and confidentiality. It's a crucial document that ensures all members adhere to the committee's code of conduct, maintain the highest level of integrity, and handle sensitive information with care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please read the form carefully before proceeding.
2.  Answer each field as truthfully as possible.
3.  Enter your name or a brief statement in "officer_and_committee_member_agreement" (ID 1).
4.  Type in your confirmation of integrity in "confirm_integrity" (ID 2).
5.  Confirm your commitment to confidentiality in "confirm_confidentiality" (ID 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Officer And Committee Member Agreement** (`officer_and_committee_member_agreement`, text, optional):
    *   Enter your name or a brief statement confirming your agreement with the committee's code of conduct.
*   **Confirm Integrity** (`confirm_integrity`, text, optional):
    *   Type in your confirmation of maintaining the highest level of integrity in your work and dealings with the committee and Sopw.
*   **Confirm Confidentiality** (`confirm_confidentiality`, text, optional):
    *   Confirm your commitment to handling sensitive information with care.
