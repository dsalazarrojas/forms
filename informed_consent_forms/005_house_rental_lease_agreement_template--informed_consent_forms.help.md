# House Rental Lease Agreement Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for a House Rental Lease Agreement, which is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions of a rental property. It is designed to be completed by both tenants and landlords to document the details of the rental property and the agreement between them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Review each section carefully** and answer all questions as accurately and completely as possible.
2. **Fill out the form in the provided sections** (Tenant Information, Landlord Information, Property Information, etc.).
3. **Sign the form** when finished to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms outlined in the document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information (Page 1)**:
  * **Tenant Information (tenant_info)**: Enter your personal information, including name, address, and contact details.
* **Landlord Information (Page 1)**:
  * **Landlord Information (landlord_info)**: Enter the landlord's personal information, including name, address, and contact details.
* **Property Information (Page 2)**:
  * **Property Information (property_info)**: Enter details about the rental property, including address, location, and amenities.
* **Lease Terms (Page 3)**:
  * **Lease Terms (lease_terms)**: Outline the terms of the lease, including start date, end date, rent amount, and payment method.
* **Payment Terms (Page 3)**:
  * **Payment Terms (payment_terms)**: Specify the payment details, including frequency and method of payment.
* **Security Deposit (Page 4)**:
  * **Security Deposit (security_deposit)**: Enter the amount of security deposit and any conditions for its return.
* **Termination Terms (Page 4)**:
  * **Termination Terms (termination_terms)**: Outline the circumstances under which the lease can be terminated, including notice period and termination fees.
* **Signature (Page 5)**:
  * **Signature (signature)**: Sign the document to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms outlined in the document.

## Tips
* **Review the entire form carefully** before signing to ensure that all terms are understood and agreed upon.
* **Keep a copy of the signed form** for your records.
* **Make sure to communicate** with your landlord or tenant regarding any changes or questions about the lease terms.
