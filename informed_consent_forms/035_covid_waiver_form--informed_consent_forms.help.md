# Covid Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect informed consent from patients who are receiving or have received COVID-19 treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's medical organization name where they are receiving treatment.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth.
3. Enter the patient's address.
4. Enter the patient's city.
5. Enter the patient's state.
6. Enter the patient's postal code.
7. Select the type of medical organization (e.g. Hospital, Clinic, Private, etc.).
8. Indicate the patient's COVID-19 vaccine status (e.g. True, False, Partially, Refused).
9. Indicate if the patient has provided their consent (True, False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Organization Name**: Enter the name of the medical organization where the patient is receiving treatment.
* **Patient Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient City**: Enter the patient's city.
* **Patient State**: Enter the patient's state.
* **Patient Postal Code**: Enter the patient's postal code.
* **Medical Organization Type**: Select the type of medical organization where the patient is receiving treatment (e.g. Hospital, Clinic, Private, etc.).
* **Patient Vaccine Status**: Indicate the patient's COVID-19 vaccine status (e.g. True, False, Partially, Refused).
* **Patient Consent**: Indicate if the patient has provided their consent (True, False).

## Tips
- Please ensure you have the patient's consent before proceeding with treatment.
- If the patient's vaccine status is Partially or Refused, please note that this may require additional documentation.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask the patient or their guardian for clarification.
