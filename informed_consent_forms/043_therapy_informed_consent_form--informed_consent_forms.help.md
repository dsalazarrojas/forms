# Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Informed Consent Form is a document used by therapists to gather essential information from patients before starting therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated as "required").
3.  Review each field below for a clear explanation of what is being asked for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): This is where you enter your name as the patient.
*  **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact details, such as phone number and email.
*  **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Provide your current address.
*  **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Share any medical history or allergies you have that may affect your therapy.
*  **Therapy Goals** (`therapy_goal`, select_one, required: false): Choose one or more goals for your therapy sessions, such as "Goal 1" or "Goal 2".
*  **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): If you have an emergency contact, provide their name and relationship to you.
*  **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*  **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
*  **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required: false): If you have a preferred therapist, provide their name.
*  **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your therapy session.
*  **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your therapy session.

## Tips
*  Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
*  If you're unsure about any field, ask your therapist for clarification.
*  If you need to make changes to your form, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
