# Child Education Case Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining informed consent from parents or guardians for their child's participation in a research study. It ensures that the individual has a clear understanding of the study's purpose, procedures, risks, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you understand each question before answering.
3. Sign and date the form after completing it.

## Tips

*   Answer each question honestly and with the best effort possible.
*   Ensure you have read and understood each question before proceeding.
*   If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   This form should only be completed once for each child participating in the research study.
