# Car Dealer Purchase Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Dealer Purchase Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a vehicle sale between a buyer and a seller. It is essential for both parties to understand and agree to the terms presented in this form to ensure a smooth and transparent transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, start by entering the Buyer's name, address, and email.
- Next, provide the Vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), make, model, year, and sale price.
- Indicate if the sale includes a trade-in vehicle and provide details about it, if applicable.
- Confirm if financing is required and select the correct option.
- Enter the sale price and deposit amount (if any).
- Date and time of agreement signing must be entered accurately.
- Sign and print your name as the buyer and seller.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check the vehicle details for accuracy.
- Review and agree to the terms and conditions before signing.
- Keep a record of this agreement for future reference.
