# Confidential Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Confidential Enrollment Form is a crucial tool for collecting student information for the purpose of providing tailored support and services. It is essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure that the student receives the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the Student Information section, which includes providing your student's full name, date of birth, and grade level for enrollment.
2.  Move on to the Parent/Guardian Information section, where you will be asked to provide your relationship to the student and your contact details.
3.  Next, you will be asked to provide the student's medical information, including any known medical conditions or allergies.
4.  In the Emergency Contact section, you will be asked to provide the name and phone number of the contact person in case of emergencies.
5.  Finally, you will need to provide your informed consent to the collection and storage of this confidential data and agree to the terms of enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name (`student_full_name`, `text`, required):** Enter the student's full name, as it appears on their official documents.
*   **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required):** Enter the student's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   **Gender (`gender`, `select_one`, required):** Select the student's gender from the options provided.
*   **Grade Level for Enrollment (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required):** Choose the student's grade level for enrollment from the options provided.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name (`guardian_name`, `text`, required):** Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
*   **Relationship to Student (`relationship_to_student`, `select_one`, required):** Select the relationship between the parent or guardian and the student from the options provided.
*   **Primary Phone Number (`primary_phone`, `text`, required):** Enter the primary phone number for communication.
*   **Email Address (`email_address`, `email`, required):** Enter the student's email address for secure communication.
*   **Residential Address (`home_address`, `text`, required):** Enter the student's residential address for delivery and communication purposes.
*   **Known Medical Conditions or Allergies (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required):** Enter any known medical conditions or allergies that the student has.
*   **Emergency Contact Name (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required):** Enter the name of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required):** Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Informed Consent (`data_privacy_consent`, `select_one`, required):** Select the option indicating your consent to the collection and storage of this confidential data.
*   **Agree to Terms of Enrollment (`enrollment_agreement`, `select_one`, required):** Select the option agreeing to the terms of enrollment.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information provided to ensure that it is up-to-date and correct.
*   If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
*   Please note that this form is confidential and sensitive in nature, and it is essential to handle it with care and respect.
