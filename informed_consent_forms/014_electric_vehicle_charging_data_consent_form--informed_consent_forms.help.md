# Electric Vehicle Charging Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect electric vehicle charging data and gather information on drivers, vehicles, locations, and other relevant data for further analysis and research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the driver information section with your name, address, and other relevant details.
2. Provide information about your vehicle, including make, model, and year.
3. Select your preferred location for charging data collection.
4. Choose your level of consent for data collection.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Enter your email address and phone number (optional).
7. Select a category for your data (optional).
8. Choose a date and time for data collection (optional).
9. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Information** (`driver_info`, text, required): This section requires you to provide your name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, required): This section asks for information about your vehicle, including make, model, and year.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required): Select all the locations where you would like your data to be collected.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Choose your level of consent for data collection:
	+ Option 1: I consent to the collection of my electric vehicle charging data.
	+ Option 2: I do not consent to the collection of my electric vehicle charging data.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your preferences or requirements.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have an email address, please enter it so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can reach you if needed.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select a category for your data:
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, date, optional): Choose a specific date and time for data collection (optional).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time of day for data collection (optional).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your preferences or requirements.
