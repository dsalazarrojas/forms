# Reiki Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from clients before they receive Reiki treatment. Please read through the form carefully and fill it out completely to ensure you're aware of what to expect from our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to provide all necessary information, such as client history, medical conditions, and emergency contact details.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your practitioner for clarification.
4. Once you've completed the form, please review it carefully before submitting it to your practitioner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client History** (`client_history`, text, optional): Please provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any relevant illnesses, injuries, or health conditions.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List all known allergies or medical conditions that may affect your treatment or interaction with our practitioners.
* **Reiki Consent** (`reiki_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes to indicate your consent to receive Reiki treatment. Please select both options, as both are required for informed consent.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person in case of an unforeseen situation during or after treatment.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please provide a detailed history of any medical conditions or treatments you've had in the past, especially if they may impact your treatment or health.
