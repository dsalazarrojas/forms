# Microdermabrasion Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microdermabrasion Medical History Form is designed to collect relevant information about a client's medical history. This includes their allergies, current medications, medical conditions, and family history. This information will help medical professionals provide better care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Information**: Enter your name, address, and contact information.
2. **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any allergies, medications, and medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
3. **Allergies**: Select any allergies you have, such as food, medication, or other allergies.
4. **Medications**: Select the medications you are currently taking, such as Aspirin, Amlodipine, or Acetaminophen.
5. **Conditions**: Select any medical conditions you are currently experiencing, such as High Blood Pressure, High Cholesterol, or Diabetes.
6. **Family History**: Select any medical conditions your family members have, such as High Blood Pressure, High Cholesterol, or Diabetes.
7. **Medical Notes**: Provide any additional medical notes or information that is relevant to your treatment.
8. **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
9. **Consent**: Indicate your consent to proceed with the treatment or procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (Client Information, text, required): Enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, note, optional): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any allergies, medications, and medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, select_multiple, optional): Select any allergies you have, such as food, medication, or other allergies.
* **Medications** (Medications, select_one, required): Select the medications you are currently taking, such as Aspirin, Amlodipine, or Acetaminophen.
* **Conditions** (Conditions, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical conditions you are currently experiencing, such as High Blood Pressure, High Cholesterol, or Diabetes.
* **Family History** (Family History, select_multiple, required): Select any medical conditions your family members have, such as High Blood Pressure, High Cholesterol, or Diabetes.
* **Medical Notes** (Medical Notes, text, optional): Provide any additional medical notes or information that is relevant to your treatment.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Emergency Contact Info, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Consent** (Consent, select_one, required): Indicate your consent to proceed with the treatment or procedure.
