# Professional Disclosure Statement And Informed Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from patients about themselves and their medical history, as well as to obtain their informed consent before undergoing medical treatments or procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "General Information" page with your personal details.
2. Enter the date you sign this form in the "Signature Date" field.
3. Provide your informed consent by typing it in the "Informed Consent" field.
4. Enter your name as the patient in the "Patient Name" field.
5. Enter your name as the doctor or healthcare provider in the "Doctor's Name" field.
6. Enter the name of the hospital or institution where you will be receiving treatment in the "Hospital Name" field.
7. Sign your name as the doctor or healthcare provider in the "Signature" field.
8. Sign your name as the patient in the "Patient Signature" field.
9. Enter the date of the treatment or procedure in the "Date" field.
10. Sign your name as the doctor or healthcare provider in the "Doctor's Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you enter your personal details, such as your contact information and any medical history.
* **Signature Date** (`2`, `date`, required/optional): This is where you enter the date you sign this form.
* **Informed Consent** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you state your understanding of the treatment or procedure and any risks involved.
* **Patient Name** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you enter your name as the patient.
* **Doctor's Name** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you enter your name as the doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Hospital Name** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you enter the name of the hospital or institution where you will be receiving treatment.
* **Signature** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you sign as the doctor or healthcare provider, indicating your agreement to perform the treatment or procedure.
* **Patient Signature** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): This is where the patient signs the form, indicating their agreement to undergo the treatment or procedure.
* **Date** (`9`, `date`, required/optional): This is where you enter the date of the treatment or procedure.
* **Doctor's Signature** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you sign as the doctor or healthcare provider, indicating your agreement to perform the treatment or procedure and your verification of the patient's information.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all the required fields before signing this form.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date.
- This form should only be used for medical treatments or procedures, and not for any other purpose.
