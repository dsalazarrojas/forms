# Personal Training Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather essential information from individuals participating in personal training programs. It's a crucial document that requires informed consent from participants to ensure their comfort and safety throughout the training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and participant ID.
2. Provide the date of birth of the person you are enrolling in the training program.
3. List any emergency contact information, including a phone number and a person's name.
4. Share any medical history that may be relevant to the training program.
5. Indicate which medical conditions or medications you may be taking.
6. Select whether or not you are willing to share your medical history.
7. Provide information about your insurance provider, if applicable.
8. List any medical supplies you may require during the training program.
9. Identify an emergency contact person.
10. Confirm the date of your medical history.
11. Consent to sharing your medical history and personal training information.
12. Confirm your consent to participate in the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Name** (training_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the training program you are enrolling in.
* **Participant ID** (participant_id, text, optional): Enter your unique participant ID, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the person you are enrolling in the training program.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Phone Number** (emergency_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or concerns you may have, including any medications you are taking.
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical conditions or allergies that may be relevant to the training program.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Insurance Provider** (insurance_provider, text, optional): Enter your insurance provider's information, if applicable.
* **Insurance Card** (insurance_card, text, optional): Attach a copy of your insurance card, if required.
* **Medical Supplies** (medical_supplies, text, optional): List any medical supplies you may require during the training program.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (emergency_contact_person, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical History Date** (medical_history_date, date, optional): Confirm the date of your medical history.
* **Consent to Medical History** (consent_to_medical_history, select_one, optional): Select whether or not you are willing to share your medical history.
* **Consent** (consent, select_one, optional): Confirm your consent to participate in the training program.
* **Emergency Services** (emergency_services, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not you require emergency services during the training program.
