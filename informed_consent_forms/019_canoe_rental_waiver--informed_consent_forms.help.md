# Canoe Rental Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Canoe Rental Waiver is a form that aims to obtain informed consent from individuals renting canoes or other watercraft for recreational activities. It ensures that renters are aware of the inherent risks associated with paddle sports and releases the rental company from liability in case of accidents or injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully to understand its requirements and purpose.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information about yourself.
3. Sign and date the form before submitting it to the rental company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Provide your first name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Last Name**: Provide your last name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **Email Address**: Enter your personal email address.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your personal phone number.
- **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
- **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
- **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
- **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your ZIP code or postal code.
- **Rental Date**: Enter the date you plan to rent the watercraft.
- **Type of Watercraft Renting**: Select the type of watercraft you plan to rent (Canoe, Kayak, Stand-up Paddleboard, Tandem Canoe, or Other).
- **Rental Duration**: Select the duration of your rental (Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, Half day, Full day, Overnight, or Multi-day).
- **Water Body for Rental**: Enter the location where you plan to paddle.
- **Paddling Experience Level**: Select your level of paddling experience (No experience - Never paddled, Beginner - Few times only, Intermediate - Regular paddler, Advanced - Experienced paddler, or Expert - Competitive or professional).
- **Swimming Ability**: Select your swimming ability (Strong swimmer, Adequate swimmer, Weak swimmer, or Cannot swim).
- **Physical Fitness Level**: Select your physical fitness level (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
- **Do you have any medical conditions affecting water activities?**: Select Yes, No, or Unsure.
- **If yes, please describe conditions**: If you have a medical condition affecting water activities, provide a brief description.
- **Are you taking any medications that affect balance or judgment?**: Select Yes, No, or Unsure.
- **Are you pregnant or possibly pregnant?**: Select Yes, No, or Prefer not to say.
- **I will wear a Coast Guard approved life jacket**: Select Yes, I agree.
- **I have received and understand the safety instructions**: Select Yes, I confirm.
- **I acknowledge the inherent risks of paddle sports**: Select Yes, I acknowledge.
- **I voluntarily assume all risks and release the rental company from liability**: Select Yes, I agree.
- **If under 18, parent/guardian name and signature**: If you are under 18 years old, please have a parent or guardian sign this form.

## Tips

- Please provide accurate and truthful information about yourself.
- Make sure to read through the form carefully before submitting it to the rental company.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the rental company for clarification.
- This form is intended to be filled out by one person per watercraft rental.
- If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please be honest when answering the questions.
- If you are under 18 years old, please have a parent or guardian sign this form in addition to your own signature.
- Please sign and date the form before submitting it to the rental company.
