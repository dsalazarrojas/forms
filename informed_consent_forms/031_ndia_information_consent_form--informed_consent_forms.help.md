# NDIA Information Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information related to a client and one or two witness(es). It appears to be used for a consensual agreement or partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form:

1. Fill in the client's information, starting with their name and contact details.
2. Choose whether the client has a relationship with you (the form filler).
3. Provide the client's date of birth and address.
4. Select a witness 1 (if any) and provide their name, contact details, and phone number.
5. Choose whether the witness 1 has a relationship with you.
6. If a witness 1 is selected, provide their signature and notes.
7. Select a witness 2 (if any) and provide their name, contact details, and phone number.
8. Choose whether the witness 2 has a relationship with you.
9. If a witness 2 is selected, provide their signature and notes.
10. Finally, fill in any client notes and click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (1)**: Enter the full name of the client.
* **Client Contact (2)**: Enter the client's contact details, if available.
* **Client Relationship (3)**: Check whether you have a relationship with the client.
* **Consent Date (4)**: Enter the date of the consent or partnership agreement.
* **NDIA Officer (5)**: Enter your full name and position (if required).
* **Signature (6)**: Provide your digital signature.
* **Witness 1 Name (7)**: Enter the name of the witness 1, if applicable.
* **Witness 1 Contact (8)**: Enter the witness 1 contact details, if available.
* **Witness 1 Relationship (9)**: Check whether witness 1 has a relationship with you.
* **Witness 1 Signature (10)**: If witness 1 is selected, provide their digital signature.
* **Witness 1 Notes (11)**: Enter any notes for witness 1.
* **Witness 2 Name (12)**: Enter the name of the witness 2, if applicable.
* **Witness 2 Contact (13)**: Enter the witness 2 contact details, if available.
* **Witness 2 Relationship (14)**: Check whether witness 2 has a relationship with you.
* **Witness 2 Signature (15)**: If witness 2 is selected, provide their digital signature.
* **Witness 2 Notes (16)**: Enter any notes for witness 2.
* **Client DOB (13)**: Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Client Address (14)**: Enter the client's address.
* **Client Phone (15)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **NDIA Reference Number (16)**: Enter a unique reference number for the client.
* **Witness 1 Phone (17)**: Enter the witness 1 phone number.
* **Witness 2 Phone (18)**: Enter the witness 2 phone number.
* **Witness 1 Email (19)**: Enter the witness 1 email address.
* **Witness 2 Email (20)**: Enter the witness 2 email address.
* **Witness 1 Address (21)**: Enter the witness 1 address.
* **Witness 2 Address (22)**: Enter the witness 2 address.
* **Client Notes (23)**: Enter any notes for the client.
* **Witness 1 Notes (24)**: Enter any notes for witness 1.
* **Witness 2 Notes (25)**: Enter any notes for witness 2.
