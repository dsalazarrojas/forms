# health_insurance_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Intake Form is designed to collect information from patients about their health insurance coverage and medical history. This form is used to gather essential information for healthcare providers to make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and what options are available.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any questions or options, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
4. Submit the form to your healthcare provider to review and discuss your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info (`patient_info`):
* **Patient Information**: This section is for you to provide basic information about yourself, such as your name and contact details. Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.

### Medical History (`medical_history`):
* **Medical History**: This field is for you to describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures you have experienced. Provide as much detail as possible to help your healthcare provider understand your medical background.

### Insurance Information (`insurance_info`):
* **Insurance Provider**: Please select the health insurance provider you are currently covered with. This will help your healthcare provider understand your insurance coverage and make informed decisions about your care. Options available are Aetna, Blue Cross, and UnitedHealth.

### Next Steps (`next_steps`):
* **Next Steps**: Select one or more of the following options to determine the next steps in your care. You can:
	+ Contact patient: Allow your healthcare provider to contact you for further information or to discuss your care.
	+ Review insurance information: Provide additional information about your insurance coverage for your healthcare provider to review.
	+ Follow up with physician: Schedule a follow-up appointment with your healthcare provider to discuss your care.

### Medical History 1-5 (`medical_history_1`, `medical_history_2`, `medical_history_3`, `medical_history_4`, `medical_history_5`):
* **Medical History 1-5**: Please describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures you have experienced. Provide as much detail as possible to help your healthcare provider understand your medical background.

Note: These fields are optional and can be used to provide additional information about your medical history.

Tips:
* Please provide accurate and honest information about your insurance coverage and medical history.
* If you are unsure about any questions or options, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Ensure that you review the form carefully before submitting it to your healthcare provider.
