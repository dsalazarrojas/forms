# lead_based_paint_disclosure_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lead-based paint disclosure form is used to inform users about the lead-based paint disclosure requirements and obtain their consent for the application of lead-based paint in a residential or commercial property. This form is a crucial part of the process to ensure compliance with relevant regulations and protect users from potential health risks associated with lead-based paint.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Fill in your name and email address in the designated fields.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone_number field.
4. Review and submit the form after completing all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **lead_based_paint_disclosure** (`lead_based_paint_disclosure`, text, required/optional): This field is used to record the user's response to the lead-based paint disclosure. Please provide a clear and concise answer to this question.
* **name** (`name`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your name. Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This field is used to collect your email address. Please provide a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your phone number. Please provide a valid phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your representative or contact us directly.
* This form is for information purposes only and should not be used for any other reason.
