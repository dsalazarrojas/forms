# Hair Root Lift Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for patients undergoing a Hair Root Lift treatment. It is designed to gather essential information about the patient's health, appointment details, and consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your client information in the first field.
2.  Next, provide your health details in the second field.
3.  In the third field, specify your appointment details.
4.  Finally, read and sign the consent section carefully to acknowledge your understanding of the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`title`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide your name, date of birth, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a client. This information will help us to understand your identity and contact you effectively.
* **Health Details** (`health_details`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide any pre-existing medical conditions, allergies, or medications that might affect your treatment. This is crucial for your health and safety.
* **Appointment** (`appointment`, `text`, required: `false`): Please specify the date, time, and other appointment details to ensure that we have the right information about your scheduled treatment.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required: `false`): Please read and sign this section carefully to acknowledge that you understand the treatment process, risks involved, and that you are aware of the treatment duration and any post-treatment care required. This is an essential step to ensure that you are making an informed decision about your treatment.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any miscommunication.
* Ensure that you have read and understood the consent section before signing it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
