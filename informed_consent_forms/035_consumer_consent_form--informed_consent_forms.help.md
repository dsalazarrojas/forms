# Consumer Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from consumers for a specific purpose, such as data collection, service participation, or marketing communications. It collects information about the consumer, including their personal details, contact information, and consent for data sharing and usage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the consumer's personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Email Address: Enter your primary email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
	* Street Address: Enter your mailing address.
	* City: Enter the city where you reside.
	* State/Province: Enter your state or province.
	* ZIP/Postal Code: Enter your postal code.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Select the type of consent you are giving (e.g., data collection, service participation, etc.).
4. Enter a detailed description of what you consent to.
5. Specify the purpose of the consent.
6. Indicate what data will be collected and how it will be used.
7. Decide if you consent to share data with third parties (if applicable).
8. Choose how data will be used (e.g., for marketing, research, etc.).
9. Select the retention period for the collected data.
10. Mention any potential risks involved with the consent.
11. Indicate what benefits you will receive from participating.
12. Acknowledge that your participation is voluntary.
13. Confirm that you have the right to withdraw consent at any time.
14. Choose an option to indicate that your information will be kept confidential.
15. Enter your signature and the date.
16. If required, enter the name and signature of a witness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Consumer First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Consumer Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Consumer Email, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (Consumer Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (Consumer Address, text, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (Consumer City, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State/Province** (Consumer State/Province, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (Consumer ZIP, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Organization Name** (Organization Name, text, required): Enter the name of the organization requesting consent.
* **Type of Consent** (Type of Consent, select_one, required): Select the type of consent you are giving (e.g., data collection, service participation, etc.).
* **Consent Description** (Consent Description, text, required): Enter a detailed description of what you consent to.
* **Purpose of Consent** (Purpose of Consent, text, required): Specify the purpose of the consent.
* **Data to Be Collected** (Data to Be Collected, text, required): Indicate what data will be collected.
* **How Data Will Be Used** (How Data Will Be Used, text, required): Describe how the collected data will be used.
* **Will Data Be Shared?** (Will Data Be Shared, select_one, required): Decide if data will be shared with third parties (if applicable).
* **Data Retention Period** (Data Retention Period, text, required): Select the retention period for the collected data.
* **Potential Risks** (Potential Risks, text, optional): Mention any potential risks involved with the consent.
* **Benefits of Participation** (Benefits of Participation, text, optional): Indicate what benefits you will receive from participating.
* **Voluntary Participation** (Voluntary Participation, select_one, required): Acknowledge that your participation is voluntary.
* **Right to Withdraw** (Right to Withdraw, select_one, required): Confirm that you have the right to withdraw consent at any time.
* **Confidentiality Assurance** (Confidentiality Assurance, select_one, required): Choose an option to indicate that your information will be kept confidential.
* **Contact for Questions** (Contact for Questions, text, required): Enter the contact information for questions or concerns.
* **Marketing Communications Consent** (Marketing Communications Consent, select_one, optional): Agree or disagree to receive marketing communications.
* **Third Party Sharing Consent** (Third Party Sharing Consent, select_one, optional): Agree or disagree to share data with third parties.
* **Consumer Signature** (Consumer Signature, text, required): Type your full name as signature.
* **Signature Date** (Signature Date, date, required): Enter the date of signing.
* **Witness Name** (Witness Name, text, optional): Enter the name of a witness if required (if not, leave blank).
* **Witness Signature** (Witness Signature, text, optional): Enter the signature of a witness if required (if not, leave blank).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification from the organization requesting consent.
* If you are required to sign as a witness, please sign in the presence of the consumer.
* This form is to be filled out and submitted by the consumer, not by a third party on their behalf.
