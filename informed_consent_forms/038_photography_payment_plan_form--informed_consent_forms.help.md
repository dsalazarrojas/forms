# photography_payment_plan_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography payment plan form is a tool used to collect payment information for photography services provided to clients. It is designed to facilitate the payment process between the photographer and their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select a payment method from the options provided.
3. Provide any additional bank transfer details, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment** (1, number, optional):
	+ Enter the amount to be paid for the photography services provided.
* **Date of shoot** (2, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the photography shoot, if known.
* **Photographer** (3, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as the photographer.
* **Client** (4, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as the client.
* **Services** (5, select multiple, optional):
	+ Select the services provided for the payment, such as Photography, Editing, and Delivery.
* **Price** (6, number, optional):
	+ Enter the price to be paid for the services selected.
* **Payment method** (7, select one, optional):
	+ Select the payment method to be used, such as Bank transfer, Cash, or Card.
* **Bank transfer details** (8, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional details required for the bank transfer, such as account number and bank name.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Select all services provided for the payment.
- Enter the correct price and payment method for the services selected.
- If using bank transfer, provide all necessary details to avoid delays in payment processing.
