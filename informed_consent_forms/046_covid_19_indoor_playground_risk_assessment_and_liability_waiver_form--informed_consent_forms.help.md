# Covid 19 Indoor Playground Risk Assessment And Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the risks associated with indoor play activities during the COVID-19 pandemic and to obtain waiver of liability agreements from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and provide accurate information about your health status, medical conditions, and emergency contact information.
2. Answer the questions honestly and truthfully.
3. Choose the correct options for each field.
4. Print or save a copy of the form for your records.

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining the purpose and importance of completing it. Please read and understand the form before proceeding.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, `number`, required: false): This question asks you to evaluate your current risk level for COVID-19. Please consider your current symptoms, health status, and any known risks. Choose a number that best represents your level of risk.
* **Waiver Agreement** (`waiveragreement`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to choose one of the options below. Please select the option that best fits your situation.
	+ Assumption of Risk: This means that you understand and accept the risks associated with indoor play activities, even if you don't disclose any medical conditions.
	+ Waiver: This means that you don't want to take any risks associated with indoor play activities and would like to be excused from participation.
* **Medical Condition** (`medicalcondition`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section asks you to select any medical conditions that you may have. Please choose all that apply.
	+ Heart condition: Do you have a heart condition or any heart-related issues?
	+ High blood pressure: Do you have high blood pressure or any related issues?
	+ Diabetes: Do you have diabetes or any related issues?
	+ Other (Please specify): If you have any other medical conditions, please specify them.
* **Health Insurance** (`healthinsurance`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to confirm if you have health insurance. Please select one of the options below.

* **Emergency Contact** (`emergencycontact`, `text`, required: false): This section asks for your emergency contact information. Please provide the name and phone number of someone who can be reached in case of an emergency.

	+ Blood test: Have you undergone a blood test or have any blood-related issues?

	+ Other (Please specify): If you have any other medical procedures, please specify them.
