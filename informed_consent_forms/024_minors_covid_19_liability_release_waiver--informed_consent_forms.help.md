# Minors COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minors COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver is a form that collects information about a minor's participation in activities or events where they may be exposed to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the minor's name in the "Minor Name" field.
2. Select the relationship between you and the minor (Guardian, Parent, or Authorized Representative).
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Indicate whether the minor has a medical condition (Yes or No).
6. Indicate whether the minor has insurance coverage (Yes or No).
7. Review and submit the form to confirm that you have provided informed consent for the minor's participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Minor Name**: The minor's name as it appears on their identification or medical records.
* **Relationship To Minor**: Select the relationship between you and the minor (Guardian, Parent, or Authorized Representative). This field ensures that the form is completed by the correct representative.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition**: Indicate whether the minor has any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact their participation. This information will be used to ensure that medical staff are aware of any potential risks.
* **Insurance Payer**: Indicate whether the minor has insurance coverage that will cover any medical expenses related to their participation. This information will be used to ensure that the minor's medical expenses are covered in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are the correct representative before filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are complete and accurate.
