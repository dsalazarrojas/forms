# Student Peer Review Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from students participating in peer review research. It is essential to understand that by completing this form, students acknowledge that they have been informed about the research and its purpose, and they agree to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Information** section with any relevant details about the research.
2. Enter your **Student Name** and **Instructor Name** in their respective fields.
3. Answer the **Research Purpose** question to demonstrate your understanding of the research goals and objectives.
4. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Student Informed Consent** to indicate your agreement to participate in the research.
5. If applicable, enter a brief statement explaining your **Student Informed Consent** in the **Student Informed Consent Text** field.
6. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Research Agreement** to indicate your agreement to participate in the research.
7. Provide your **Contact Details**, including your email address, phone number, and physical address (if applicable).
8. Enter your **Email Address**, which will be used for communication purposes.
9. Enter your **Phone Number** (if applicable).
10. Enter your **Physical Address** (if applicable).
11. Add any additional comments or concerns in the **Additional Comments** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Information**: Enter any relevant details about the research, such as the research title, description, or purpose.
*   **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official student record.
*   **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor associated with the course or research project.
*   **Research Purpose**: Briefly describe the research purpose, goals, and objectives.
*   **Student Informed Consent**: Select **Yes** to indicate that you have been informed about the research and its purpose, and you agree to participate. Select **No** to indicate that you decline to participate.
*   **Student Informed Consent Text**: Enter a brief statement explaining your informed consent.
*   **Research Agreement**: Select **Yes** to indicate that you understand the research conditions and agree to participate. Select **No** to indicate that you decline to participate.
*   **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and physical address (if applicable).
*   **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
*   **Address**: Enter your physical address (if applicable).
*   **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns about your participation in the research.
