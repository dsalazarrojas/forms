# Off Label Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Off-Label Consent Form is used to document a patient's agreement to use a medication that has not been approved for its intended use. This form ensures that both the patient and healthcare provider are aware of the potential risks and benefits associated with using an off-label medication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Enter the date the patient gave their consent in the **consent_date** field.
3. Specify the name of the medication the patient is agreeing to use in the **medication_name** field.
4. The patient should sign their name in the **patient_signature** field, indicating their agreement to use the medication.
5. The healthcare provider should sign their name in the **provider_signature** field, indicating their approval of the medication.
6. Enter the date the healthcare provider received the patient's consent in the **date_received** field.
7. Enter the date the consent was provided to the patient in the **date_provided** field.
8. Select whether the patient has given their consent (**Yes/No**) in the **off_label_consent_checkbox** field.
9. If desired, enter any comments or additional information in the **comments** field.
10. If desired, enter the patient's ID number in the **patient_id** field.
11. Enter the date the consent was signed in the **date_signed** field.
12. Check the box indicating that the consent was signed (**signed**) in the **signed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **consent_date**: Enter the date the patient gave their consent.
* **medication_name**: Specify the name of the medication the patient is agreeing to use.
* **patient_signature**: The patient signs their name, indicating their agreement to use the medication.
* **provider_signature**: The healthcare provider signs their name, indicating their approval of the medication.
* **date_received**: Enter the date the healthcare provider received the patient's consent.
* **date_provided**: Enter the date the consent was provided to the patient.
* **off_label_consent_checkbox**: Select whether the patient has given their consent (**Yes/No**).
* **comments**: Enter any comments or additional information about the consent.
* **consent_given**: Enter a statement indicating that the consent was given.
* **patient_id**: Enter the patient's ID number (if applicable).
* **date_signed**: Enter the date the consent was signed.
* **signed**: Check the box indicating that the consent was signed. 

## Tips
Please ensure that both the patient and healthcare provider sign the form, and that the consent date and time are recorded accurately. Make sure the patient's ID number is included, if applicable. If there are any issues or questions, please contact a healthcare professional for assistance.
