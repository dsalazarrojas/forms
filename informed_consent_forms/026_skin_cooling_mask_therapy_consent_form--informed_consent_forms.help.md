# Skin Cooling Mask Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Cooling Mask Therapy Consent Form is a crucial document that helps healthcare professionals ensure that clients are aware of and agree to the skin cooling mask therapy process. The form helps to collect necessary information about the client's health status and gather their consent before starting the therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the client's health details on page 1.
2.  Provide your consent to undergo the skin cooling mask therapy on page 2.
3.  Sign the form to confirm your consent on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Health Details** (`skin_cooling_mask_therapy_consent_form_data`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your current health status, including any medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments related to skin health.
*   **Client Consent** (`skin_cooling_mask_therapy_consent_form_consent`, text, optional): Please type your consent to undergo the skin cooling mask therapy. This is an agreement to receive treatment and understand the potential risks involved.
*   **Signature** (`skin_cooling_mask_therapy_consent_form_signature`, text, optional): Please sign the form to confirm your consent and indicate that you have read and understood the therapy process and potential risks.

## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the client health details section.
*   Make sure to read and understand the therapy process and potential risks before signing the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare professional before signing the form.
