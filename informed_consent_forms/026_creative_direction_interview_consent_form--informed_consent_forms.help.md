# creative_direction_interview_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect informed consent from participants for creative direction interviews. It is essential to have participants' understanding and agreement on the project's purpose, scope, and potential risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the participant's details in the "Participant Details" section.
2.  Confirm the creative direction and project use in the "Creative Direction" and "Project Use" section.
3.  Review and agree to the participant agreement in the "Participant Agreement" section.
4.  Indicate whether you have obtained the participant's signature of consent in the "Signature" section.
5.  Select the project use for which you have obtained consent in the "Project Use" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's name and other relevant details. This section is used to collect basic participant information.
* **Creative Direction** (`creative_direction`, text, required/optional): Confirm the creative direction of the project. This will help participants understand the project's objectives and scope.
* **Project Use** (`project_use`, text, required/optional): Specify how the project will be used. This will help participants understand the project's purpose and potential risks involved.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, note, required/optional): Review and agree to the participant agreement. This section outlines the terms and conditions of the project.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have obtained the participant's signature of consent. Select "Yes" if you have obtained their signature, or "No" if not.
* **Project Use (2)** (`project_use`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the specific ways the project will be used. This section helps participants understand the project's scope and potential risks involved. Select all applicable options that apply.
