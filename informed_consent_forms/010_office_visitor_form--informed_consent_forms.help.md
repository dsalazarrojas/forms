# Office Visitor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Visitor Form is a document that collects information from individuals visiting the office. It is used to ensure a smooth and secure experience for both visitors and office staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. For the "covid_precautions" field, select "Yes" if you have been vaccinated or are willing to comply with COVID-19 safety measures.
3. Enter your emergency contact information in the "emergency_contact" field.
4. Provide your contact information in the "contact_info" field.
5. Specify the purpose of your visit in the "purpose_visit" field.
6. Enter the name of the staff member you are visiting in the "staff_name" field.
7. Enter the employee ID number associated with the staff member you are visiting in the "employee_id" field.
8. Sign the "visitor_signature" field with the date you signed.
9. Sign the "office_signature" field, which is the date and time of your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **welcome (Text, Required)**: A field for the visitor to write a brief welcome message or introduction.
* **office_rules (Note, Optional)**: A field for the visitor to read and acknowledge the office rules.
* **covid_precautions (Select One, Optional)**: Select "Yes" if you have been vaccinated or are willing to comply with COVID-19 safety measures.
* **emergency_contact (Text, Required)**: Enter your emergency contact information, including name and phone number.
* **contact_info (Text, Required)**: Provide your contact information, including your name and phone number.
* **purpose_visit (Text, Optional)**: Specify the purpose of your visit.
* **staff_name (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the staff member you are visiting.
* **employee_id (Text, Required)**: Enter the employee ID number associated with the staff member you are visiting.
* **visitor_signature (Date, Required)**: Sign this field with the date you signed the form.
* **office_signature (Date, Required)**: Sign this field with the date and time of your visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure you've reviewed and understood the office rules before signing the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask an office staff member for assistance.
* If you need to revisit the office, please note that your previous form might still be on file. 

Note: The "office_rules" field is a note field and is intended to be read by the visitor, not filled out. It is not a required field. The "covid_precautions" field is an optional field and is intended to be filled out by the visitor, but it is not required.
