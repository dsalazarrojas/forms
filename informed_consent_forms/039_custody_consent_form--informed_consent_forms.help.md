# Custody Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custody Consent Form is used to capture the consent of the legal guardian for a patient's treatment or care. This form is essential for ensuring that the legal guardian is aware of and agrees to the treatment and care provided by the medical facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Parent Information**: Please enter the name and address of the legal guardian.
2. **Legal Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the legal guardian.
3. **Legal Guardian Relationship**: Select the relationship between the legal guardian and the patient (Mother, Father, Guardian, or Other).
4. **Address**: Enter the address of the medical facility.
5. **City**: Enter the city of the medical facility.
6. **State**: Enter the state of the medical facility.
7. **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the medical facility.
8. **Email**: Enter the email address of the legal guardian.
9. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the legal guardian.
10. **Medical Facility Name**: Enter the name of the medical facility.
11. **Medical Facility Address**: Enter the address of the medical facility.
12. **Medical Facility City**: Enter the city of the medical facility.
13. **Medical Facility State**: Enter the state of the medical facility.
14. **Medical Facility Zip**: Enter the zip code of the medical facility.
15. **Date of Admission**: Enter the date of the patient's admission to the medical facility.
16. **Expected Duration of Care**: Enter the expected duration of care for the patient.
17. **Medical Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the medical facility.
18. **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's care.
19. **Signature**: Enter your signature as the legal guardian.
20. **Signature Note**: Enter any notes or comments about your signature.
21. **Legal Guardian Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the legal guardian.
22. **Medical Facility Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the medical facility.
23. **Medical Staff Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the medical staff.
24. **Parent Signature**: Enter your signature as the parent or guardian.
25. **Medical Facility Signature**: Enter your signature as a medical facility representative.
