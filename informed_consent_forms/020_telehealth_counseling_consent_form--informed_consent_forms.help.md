# Telehealth Counseling Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Telehealth Counseling Consent Form is designed to gather information from both the patient and provider to ensure that telehealth services are delivered and received safely and effectively. This form should only be completed by patients undergoing telehealth counseling services. Please note that this form is not intended to be a comprehensive consent form for all telehealth services. Review the form carefully and only complete it if you are sure you are ready to begin telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the form carefully before starting to complete it.
2. Fill out the form using the provided fields.
3. Be sure to select the correct options for "services provided" based on your specific needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name and any other identifying information you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Provider Information** (`Provider Information`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your provider's name and any other identifying information you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Date of Service** (`Date of Service`, `date`, required/optional): Please select the date on which you will be receiving telehealth services.
* **Time of Service** (`Time of Service`, `time`, required/optional): Please select the time at which you will be receiving telehealth services.
* **Services Provided** (`Services Provided`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select the services you will be receiving (e.g., Telehealth Counseling Session, Medication Management, Other Services).
* **Patient Phone** (`Patient Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Provider Phone** (`Provider Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your provider's phone number so that we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Patient Email** (`Patient Email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Provider Email** (`Provider Email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your provider's email address so that we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Signature Date** (`Signature Date`, `date`, required/optional): Please select the date on which you sign this form, indicating your consent to receive telehealth services.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* Make sure to select only the services you will be receiving.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please review the form carefully before signing it, as it is a binding agreement.
