# Film Location Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a film location, including its owner, address, and other relevant details. This helps film producers and production teams gather necessary information before filming at the location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name and contact information of the film location owner.
2. Provide the address of the location.
3. Enter the name of the film production company involved.
4. Enter the date and time of the shoot (or the expected dates and times of the shoot).
5. If necessary, add any additional comments or details about the location or shoot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Film Location Owner** (`film_location_owner`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the location's owner. This information will help the production team get in touch with them if needed.
- **Film Location Address** (`film_location_address`, text, optional): Enter the exact address of the film location, including any necessary details such as apartment numbers or building names.
- **Film Production Company** (`film_production_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the film production company involved in the shoot.
- **Date of Shooting** (`date_of_shooting`, date, optional): Enter the date of the shoot, or the expected dates of the shoot.
- **Time of Shooting** (`time_of_shooting`, time, optional): Enter the time of the shoot, or the expected time of the shoot.
- **Additional Details** (`film_location_release_form_2`, `film_location_release_form_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the location or shoot that may be relevant to the production team.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the correct contact information for the location owner, so they can be reached if needed.
- Ensure the address is accurate and complete, including any additional details that may be necessary.
- Be clear and concise in the production company name and date/time of shoot.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the location owner or production team for assistance.
