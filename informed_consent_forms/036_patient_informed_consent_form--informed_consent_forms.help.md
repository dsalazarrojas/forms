# Patient Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Informed Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing medical procedures or treatments. This form is essential to ensure that patients understand their rights, risks, and benefits associated with the proposed treatment. By completing this form, healthcare providers can obtain a patient's consent and demonstrate their commitment to transparency and patient-centered care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name.
2. Enter the patient's age in years.
3. Provide the patient's contact number and address.
4. Identify the emergency contact person and their contact number.
5. Indicate the informed consent status (Not Obtained, Obtained, or Pending).
6. Date the signature.
7. Sign your name in the signature field.
8. Optionally, provide the informed consent expiration and start dates.
9. Enter any relevant medical procedure or condition information.
10. Add any comments or notes regarding the patient's informed consent.
11. Enter the name and contact information of the medical institution and the doctor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Informed Consent Form** (`patient_informed_consent_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Please enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age in years.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide the patient's contact number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, optional): Identify the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact number.
* **Informed Consent Status** (`informed_consent_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the informed consent status (Not Obtained, Obtained, or Pending).
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Date the signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Sign your name in this field.
* **Informed Consent Expires** (`informed_consent_expires`, date, optional): Enter the informed consent expiration date.
* **Informed Consent Start Date** (`informed_consent_start`, date, optional): Enter the informed consent start date.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical procedure information.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical condition information.
* **Patient Informed Consent Form Comments** (`patient_informed_consent_form_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes regarding the patient's informed consent.
* **Medical Institution** (`medical_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical institution.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor.
* **Doctor Contact** (`doctor_contact`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's contact information.
* **Patient Informed Consent** (`patient_informed_consent`, text, optional): Enter any additional information regarding the patient's informed consent.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have obtained the patient's informed consent before proceeding with any medical treatment.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure that all required information is provided.
* Keep a copy of the form for patient records and reference.
