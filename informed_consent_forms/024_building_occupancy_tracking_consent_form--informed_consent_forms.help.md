# Building Occupancy Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for occupancy tracking, which is a system that monitors and tracks individuals' presence and activities within a building or specific areas. The purpose of this form is to ensure that you understand how your data will be collected, used, and protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and answer all required questions.
2.  Provide your consent to participate in the occupancy tracking system.
3.  Select the correct options for how your data will be collected, used, and protected.
4.  Confirm your understanding and acceptance of the privacy policy.
5.  Provide your voluntary participation and withdrawal option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name (`participant_name`):** Please enter your complete legal name as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Email Address (`participant_email`):** Enter your contact email address where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
*   **Phone Number (`participant_phone`):** Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
*   **Employee or ID Number (`employee_id`):** If you are an employee, enter your employee ID number. This is optional but recommended for tracking purposes.
*   **Department or Team (`department`):** If applicable, select the department or team you belong to.
*   **Primary Building Location (`building_location`):** Select the building where you spend most of your time.
*   **Floor or Area (`floor_area`):** If applicable, select the specific floor or area you occupy.
*   **-- Occupancy Tracking Method -- (`tracking_method_notice`):** This section explains how occupancy tracking will be conducted.
*   **Tracking Technology Used (`tracking_technology`):** Select how your occupancy will be tracked (e.g., Badge or Card Swipe, Biometric, WiFi Detection, Sensors, Check-In System, or Other).
*   **Type of Data Collected (`data_collected`):** Select the types of data that will be collected (e.g., Entry and Exit Times, Area or Room Visited, Duration of Stay, Frequency of Visits, Anonymous Counts).
*   **-- Data Usage and Privacy -- (`data_usage_notice`):** This section explains how your data will be used and protected.
*   **Purpose of Data Collection (`data_usage`):** Select the purposes for which your data will be used (e.g., Space Optimization, Occupancy Planning, Facility Management, Health and Safety, Resource Allocation).
*   **Data Protection Measures (`data_protection`):** Select the measures to protect your data (e.g., Encrypted Storage, Limited Access, Regular Deletion, Anonymized Data, Multiple Safeguards).
*   **Data Retention Period (`data_retention_period`):** Select the period for which your data will be kept (e.g., 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year, 2 Years, Indefinitely).
*   **Will Data Be Shared With Third Parties (`third_party_sharing`):** Select whether your data will be shared externally (e.g., No, Not Shared, Yes, With Authorization, Limited Sharing, Anonymized Sharing Only).
*   **Consent Statement (`consent_statement`):** This section contains your agreement to participate in the occupancy tracking system.
*   **I Voluntarily Consent to Occupancy Tracking (`voluntary_participation`):** Select to agree or disagree to participate in the system.
*   **I Understand and Accept Privacy Terms (`understand_privacy`):** Select to acknowledge understanding of the privacy policy.
*   **I Understand I Can Withdraw Consent (`withdrawal_option`):** Select to be aware that you can withdraw your consent at any time.
*   **Date of Consent (`consent_date`):** The form will automatically populate the current date.
*   **Questions or Concerns (`additional_questions`):** If you have any questions or concerns, type them in this field.

**Remember to review each section carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any of the fields.**
