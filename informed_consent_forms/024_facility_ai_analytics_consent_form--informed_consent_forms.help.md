# Facility AI Analytics Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from facility employees or users for the collection and analysis of facility-related data for AI analytics purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided fields and ensure you understand what each field represents.
2. Answer each field truthfully and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility AI Analytics Consent Form** (`facility_a_i_analytics_consent_form`): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your consent.
* **Facility AI Analytics Purpose** (`facility_a_i_analytics_purpose`): Please describe the specific purpose of the data collection and analysis you are consenting to.
* **Facility AI Analytics Consent** (`facility_a_i_analytics_consent`): Select 'Yes' if you agree to the collection and analysis of your data for the described purpose.
* **Facility AI Analytics Consent Given** (`facility_a_i_analytics_consent_given`): Please provide your name and title/position for record-keeping purposes.
* **Facility AI Analytics Consent Date** (`facility_a_i_analytics_consent_date`): Enter the date on which you are providing your consent.
* **Facility AI Analytics Consent Time** (`facility_a_i_analytics_consent_time`): Enter the time on which you are providing your consent.
* **Facility AI Analytics Consent Version** (`facility_a_i_analytics_consent_version`): Please enter the version number of this form, if applicable.
* **Facility AI Analytics Consent Revisions** (`facility_a_i_analytics_consent_revisions`): If there have been any revisions to this form, select 'Yes' to indicate that you have reviewed and agree to the changes.
* **Facility AI Analytics Consent Status** (`prophets_ai_analytics_consent_status`): Please provide any comments or status updates regarding your consent.

## Tips
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or its purpose, please contact the facility operations or management team.
* Ensure that you have carefully reviewed each field and understood its purpose before completing this form.
* You can save and print this form for your records.
* Please note that this form is only intended for facility-related data collection and analysis purposes, and not for any other use.
