# Renewable Energy Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Participation Consent Form is a document used to collect and document participant consent and other relevant information for a renewable energy project or initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Details**: Provide a brief description of the participant (e.g., their name, affiliation, or role).
2. **Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
3. **Consent**: Select one or more of the provided consent options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. **Signature**: Sign and date your consent, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This field is used to collect demographic information about the participant.
* **Email**: Enter the participant's email address to facilitate communication and updates.
* **Consent**: Select one or more of the provided options to indicate the participant's consent status. The options may be used to indicate different levels of consent (e.g., explicit, implied, or conditional).
* **Signature**: This field is intended for participants to sign and date their consent, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have obtained consent from all participants before proceeding with the project or initiative.
* Review the provided options carefully and select the correct consent status for each participant.
* Keep the participant's contact information secure and confidential.
