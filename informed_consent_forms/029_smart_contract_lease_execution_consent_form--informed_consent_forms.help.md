# Smart Contract Lease Execution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect informed consent from tenants or landlords for the execution of a smart contract lease. It ensures that all parties involved in the lease agreement are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the contract.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each page carefully and enter the required information.
2.  Provide your informed consent and reason for the lease agreement on the corresponding page.
3.  Enter the property address, contract execution date, lease start and end dates, rent amount and frequency, and any other relevant information.
4.  Review and agree to the terms of the smart contract lease.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Tenant or Landlord Name** (Page 1):
    *   This field is for entering your name as a tenant or landlord.
*   **Property Address** (Page 2):
    *   Enter the address of the property involved in the lease agreement.
*   **Contract Execution Date** (Page 3):
    *   Enter the date on which the contract will be executed.
*   **Lease Start Date** (Page 4):
    *   Enter the start date of the lease agreement.
*   **Lease End Date** (Page 5):
    *   Enter the end date of the lease agreement.
*   **Rent Amount** (Page 6):
    *   Enter the amount of rent you will pay for the property.
*   **Rent Frequency** (Page 7):
    *   Select the frequency of rent payments (Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly).
*   **Consent Provided** (Page 8):
    *   Select 'Yes' if you consent to the smart contract lease and 'No' if you do not.
*   **Reason for Consent/Non-Consent** (Page 9):
    *   Enter the reason for your consent or non-consent.
*   **Consent Provided Reason** (Page 10):
    *   This field is optional and can be used to provide additional information about your consent or non-consent.

## Tips

*   Please review each page carefully before submitting the form.
*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the authorized personnel.
