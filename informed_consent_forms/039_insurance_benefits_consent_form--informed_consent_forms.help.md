# Insurance Benefits Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about insurance benefits that a patient may be receiving, including the name of the facility providing the benefits, the patient's name, and the date of service. This form also gathers consent from the patient for the treatment or service they are receiving.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by filling out the "Facility name" field with the name of the facility providing the benefits.
* Next, fill out the "Patient name" field with the patient's name.
* Choose your insurance provider from the "Insurance provider" dropdown list or type in "Other (if other, please specify)" if you are using a different insurance provider.
* If using a different insurance provider, fill out the "Other (if other, please specify)" field with the name of the new insurance provider.
* Select the "Date of Service" field and choose the date of service.
* Select the "Time of Service" field and choose the time of service.
* Finally, fill out the "Consent date" and "Consent time" fields with the date and time of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **insurance_benefits_consent_form**: This is the main section of the form where you will enter a brief description of your insurance benefits.
* **Facility name**: This is the name of the facility providing your insurance benefits.
* **Patient name**: This is the name of the patient who is receiving the benefits.
* **Insurance provider**: Choose your insurance provider from the dropdown list or enter "Other" to specify a different insurance provider.
* **Other (if other, please specify)**: If you are using a different insurance provider, type in the name of the new insurance provider in this field.
* **Date of Service**: Select the date on which you are providing the service.
* **Time of Service**: Select the time of service.
* **Consent date**: Enter the date on which you received consent from the patient.
* **Consent time**: Enter the time of consent from the patient.
* **Consent signature**: This is a note field for the patient's signature.
* **Signature**: Enter the patient's signature in this field.
 
Note: The fields marked as "required" are required to be filled out.
