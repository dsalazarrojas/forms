# Lease Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lease Amendment Form is used to modify or update the terms and conditions of a lease between a landlord and a tenant. This form ensures that any changes made to the lease are documented and acknowledged by both parties, maintaining transparency and clarity in the rental agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the required fields**: Carefully review the form and fill in all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). This includes providing accurate and up-to-date information about the changes being made to the lease.
2. **Ensure correct formatting**: Double-check the formatting of dates and numbers to ensure they are entered correctly.
3. **Clearly describe the changes**: Provide a detailed description of the changes being made to the lease, including the reasons behind them.
4. **Sign and date the form**: Both the tenant and landlord must sign and date the form to confirm their agreement to the changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): This field is used to record the tenant's name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date the changes are made to the lease.
* **Description of Changes** (`description`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the changes being made to the lease, including the reasons behind them.
* **New Rent** (`new_rent`, number, optional): This field is used to record the new rent amount.
* **Reason for Amendment** (`reason`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the reason for the changes being made to the lease.
* **New Terms and Conditions** (`new_terms`, text, optional): This field is used to record any new terms or conditions added to the lease.
* **Description of New Terms** (`new_terms_description`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the new terms or conditions.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, optional): This field is used to record the signature of the person signing the form.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, optional): This field is used to record the tenant's signature.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, text, optional): This field is used to record the landlord's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date the form is signed.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, optional): This field is used to record the reason for the change.
* **Change Amount** (`change_amount`, number, optional): This field is used to record the amount of change made.
* **New Rent Amount** (`new_rent_amount`, number, optional): This field is used to record the new rent amount.
* **Change Type** (`change_type`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the type of change made.
* **New Terms** (`new_terms_2`, text, optional): This field is used to record additional new terms or conditions.
* **Change Date** (`change_date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date of the change.
* **Additional Comments** (`new_terms_3`, text, optional): This field is used to record any additional comments or notes about the change.

Note: This help guide is for users who are filling out the form, and not for administrators or developers who are responsible for designing or modifying the form.
