# Air Filtration Device Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect informed consent from participants in a study or trial involving air filtration devices. It ensures that participants are aware of the procedures, benefits, and risks associated with the testing or installation of these devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your participant name**: In this field, enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
2. **Provide your contact email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used for confirmation and record-keeping purposes.
3. **Describe the intervention**: In this field, explain what the testing or installation of the air filtration device entails, including what it will do and how it is installed.
4. **Summarize benefits and risks**: Briefly describe the expected benefits and any potential risks associated with the testing or installation of the device.
5. **Confirm your consent**: Select "True" to indicate that you have read and understand the information provided and agree to participate in the study or trial.
6. **Provide contact phone number (optional)**: If you prefer to be contacted for urgent matters, provide your phone number.
7. **Date of consent**: Enter the date on which you provided this consent.
8. **Additional comments or questions**: If you have any questions or concerns, use this field to share them with the investigators.
