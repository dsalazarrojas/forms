# Lien Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lien Release Form is used to document the removal of a lien from a property. This form is typically used when a lien holder acknowledges the removal of the lien and provides necessary information about the lien and the reason for removal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the form as "Lien Release Form".
2. Select the lien holder from the provided options.
3. Enter the name of the borrower, if applicable.
4. Enter the date when the lien was placed.
5. Enter the amount paid to remove the lien, if applicable.
6. Enter the date when the lien will be removed.
7. Provide a reason for the lien's removal.
8. Enter your acknowledgement of the form's content.
9. Sign and date the Lien Holder signature field, acknowledging the removal of the lien.
10. Sign and date the Borrower signature field, if the Borrower is a party to the document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Lien Release Form** (`title`, required): Enter the name of this form as "Lien Release Form".
*  **Lien Holder** (`lien_holder`, optional): Select the individual or entity holding the lien.
*  **Borrower** (`borrower`, optional): Enter the name of the individual or entity to whom the lien applies.
*  **Lien Date** (`lien_date`, optional): Enter the date when the lien was placed.
*  **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, optional): Enter the amount paid to remove the lien, if applicable.
*  **Release Date** (`release_date`, optional): Enter the date when the lien will be removed.
*  **Reason For Removal** (`reason_for_removal`, optional): Provide a reason for the lien's removal.
*  **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, optional): Enter your acknowledgement of the form's content.
*  **Signature - Lien Holder** (`signature_lie_n_holder`, optional): Sign and date the Lien Holder signature field, acknowledging the removal of the lien.
*  **Signature - Borrower** (`signature_borrower`, optional): Sign and date the Borrower signature field, if the Borrower is a party to the document.
*  **Signature Date - Lien Holder** (`signature_date_lie_n_holder`, optional): Enter the date of the Lien Holder's signature.
*  **Signature Date - Borrower** (`signature_date_borrower`, optional): Enter the date of the Borrower's signature.

## Tips
- Ensure that the lien is to be removed before filling out this form.
- Verify that you have the authority to remove the lien.
- Double-check the provided information for accuracy.
- Sign and date all applicable signature fields.
