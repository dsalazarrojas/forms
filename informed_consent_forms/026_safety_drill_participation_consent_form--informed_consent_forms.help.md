# Safety Drill Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact details, medical information, and consent from participants who will be taking part in a safety drill.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
2. Provide any relevant medical information in the "Medical Information" section.
3. In the "Consent" section, select one of the three options: "I understand and agree", "No", or "Decline".
4. If you have an emergency contact, provide their details in the "Emergency Contact" section.
5. In the "Safety Drill Information" section, provide any additional information about the safety drill.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details (Required)**: This is where you should enter your name, phone number, and email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed.

### Medical Information
* **Medical Information (Optional)**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies that we should be aware of, please enter them here.

### Consent
* **Consent (Required)**: Please select one of the three options: 
	+ **I understand and agree**: You have read and understood the safety procedures and risks involved in the drill, and you are willing to take part.
	+ **No**: You are declining to participate in the safety drill.
	+ **Decline**: You are not providing any further information or participation.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact (Optional)**: If you have an emergency contact person that we should know, please enter their details here.

### Safety Drill Information
* **Safety Drill Information (Optional)**: If you have any additional comments or questions about the safety drill, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately, as it will help us to communicate with you more effectively.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, feel free to ask for clarification.
