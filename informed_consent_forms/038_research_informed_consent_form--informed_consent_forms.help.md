# Research Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Informed Consent Form is a crucial document that helps ensure research participants are fully informed about the purpose, risks, and benefits of the study or experiment. This form is a legally binding agreement between the researcher and the participant that outlines the participant's rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are essential for the participant's informed consent.
2. For fields not marked with an asterisk, provide as much detail as possible to ensure the participant's understanding.
3. Ensure that all fields are accurate and truthful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **research_informed_consent_form** (`research_informed_consent_form`, text, *): Provide a clear and concise description of the research or experiment, including its purpose, risks, and benefits.
* **participant_information** (`participant_information`, text, ): Provide any necessary information about the participant, such as their name, age, contact information, or relevant medical history.
* **researcher_information** (`researcher_information`, text, ): Provide information about the researcher, including their name, affiliation, and contact information.
* **consent_type** (`consent_type`, select_one, *): Select the type of consent that applies to the research or experiment (e.g., "Option 1" or "Option 2").
* **participant_signature** (`participant_signature`, date, ): Sign and date the form to indicate that the participant has read and understood the form.
* **date_signature** (`date_signature`, date, ): Date the form to indicate that the participant has signed it on the specified date.
* **researcher_signature** (`researcher_signature`, date, ): Sign and date the form to indicate that the researcher has witnessed the participant's signature.
* **researcher_name** (`researcher_name`, text, ): Provide the researcher's name.
* **researcher_affiliation** (`researcher_affiliation`, text, ): Provide the researcher's affiliation or institution.
* **research_title** (`research_title`, text, ): Provide a brief title of the research or experiment.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, text, ): Provide the participant's phone number, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have obtained informed consent from all participants before proceeding with the research or experiment.
* Store the signed form in a secure and confidential manner.
* Retain the form for the duration of the research project or as required by law.
