# Clinical Research Participation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in participating in clinical research studies. It collects essential information about the applicant to facilitate the study's screening and selection process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Provide your full name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
3.  Choose the clinical study you are applying for from the given options.
4.  Indicate how you found out about the study.
5.  Disclose any chronic medical conditions you have.
6.  List any prescription medications you are currently taking.
7.  Confirm if you have participated in any clinical study in the last 6 months.
8.  Acknowledge that you are 18 years of age or older.
9.  Confirm your understanding of participation's voluntary nature and your ability to withdraw at any time.
10. Provide a digital signature with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email Address** (Required): Enter your current email address.
*   **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **-- Study Interests --** (Optional): Briefly describe the clinical study that interests you.
*   **Which clinical study are you applying for?** (Required): Select the study you are interested in from the list.
*   **How did you hear about this clinical research study?** (Optional): Choose how you found out about the study.
*   **-- Preliminary Screening --** (Optional): This section is for additional information.
*   **Do you have any chronic medical conditions?** (Required): List any ongoing chronic medical conditions you have.
*   **Are you currently taking any prescription medications?** (Required): List any prescription medications you are taking.
*   **Have you participated in any clinical study in the last 6 months?** (Required): Confirm your participation status in the last 6 months.
*   **-- Informed Consent Acknowledgement --** (Optional): This section is for acknowledgment of study terms.
*   **I confirm that I am 18 years of age or older** (Required): Confirm your age eligibility.
*   **I understand that participation is voluntary and I can withdraw at any time** (Required): Confirm your understanding of study participation terms.
*   **I consent to being contacted by the research team for further screening** (Required): Confirm your consent to be contacted.
*   **Digital Signature - Type Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
*   Use the correct format for date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the research team.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
