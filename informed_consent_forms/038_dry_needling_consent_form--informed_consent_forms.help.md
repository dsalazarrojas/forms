**Dry Needling Consent Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Dry Needling Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing dry needling treatment. This form is designed to provide a clear understanding of the treatment, its risks and benefits, and to ensure that the patient is aware of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select your Date of Birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Provide the name of an emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
8. Enter the name of your doctor in the "Doctor" field.
9. Describe any medical conditions that may be relevant to your treatment in the "Medical Condition" field.
10. Summarize your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
11. Describe your treatment plan in the "Treatment Plan" field.
12. Read and sign the "Informed Consent" section, indicating that you have understood the risks and benefits of the treatment.
13. Sign the "Patient Understanding" section, confirming that you have read and understood the information provided.
14. Date and sign the form in the "Signature Date" field.
15. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
16. Date and sign the form with your doctor's name, phone number, and address in the "Doctor Signature" field.
17. Enter your clinic's name in the "Clinic Name" field.
18. Enter your doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
19. Enter your doctor's phone number in the "Doctor Phone" field (optional).
20. Enter your doctor's email address in the "Doctor Email" field (optional).
21. Enter your doctor's license number in the "Doctor License" field (optional).
22. Enter your clinic's address in the "Clinic Address" field (optional).
23. Enter your clinic's phone number in the "Clinic Phone" field (optional).
24. Enter your clinic's license number in the "Clinic License" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can receive communication.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Doctor** (`doctor`, text, required): Enter the name of your treating doctor.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Describe any medical conditions that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Summarize your medical history that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required): Describe your treatment plan and any specific instructions provided by your doctor.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, text, required): Read and sign this section to indicate that you have understood the risks and benefits of the treatment.
* **Patient Understanding** (`patient_understanding`, text, required): Sign this section to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and sign this section to confirm that you have completed the form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required): Date and sign this section with your name, phone number, and address.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your clinic.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter your doctor's name.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Doctor License** (`doctor_license`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's license number.
* **Doctor Address** (`doctor_address`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's address.
* **Clinic Address** (`clinic_address`, text, optional): Enter your clinic's address.
* **Clinic Phone** (`clinic_phone`, text, optional): Enter your clinic's phone number.
* **Clinic License** (`clinic_license`, text, optional): Enter your clinic's license number.

## Tips

* Please make sure to read and understand the entire form before completing it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your doctor.
* This form is a legally binding agreement between you and your doctor.
* Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
