# Online Discussion Archiving Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather users' online discussion archiving preferences, including their signed consent for data archiving. It is essential to understand that the form is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title to describe the online discussion you want to archive.
2. Enter your first and last name to identify yourself.
3. Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
4. Specify the discussion details, including any relevant context or information about the discussion.
5. Select whether you signed the consent for data archiving.
6. Choose the archiving option, either to archive or not to archive the discussion.
7. If you choose to archive, provide a reason for archiving the discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (form_1_form_title): Enter a brief title of the online discussion you want to archive.
* **First Name** (form_1_first_name): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (form_1_last_name): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (form_1_email): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.
* **Discussion Details** (form_1_discussion_details): Provide a brief description of the discussion, including any relevant context or information.
* **Signed Consent** (form_1_signed_consent): Select 'Yes' if you agree to sign the consent for data archiving or 'No' if you decline.
* **Archiving Option** (form_1_archiving_option): Choose whether you want to archive or not archive the discussion.
* **Archiving Reason** (form_1_archiving_reason): If you choose to archive, provide a brief reason for archiving the discussion.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form title accurately reflects the discussion you want to archive.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.
* Be as specific as possible when providing discussion details.
* Clearly select whether you agree or decline to sign the consent for data archiving.
* Choose the correct archiving option based on your preferences.
* If archiving, provide a valid reason to ensure the discussion is properly recorded.
