# Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from an employee for an employment contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your name in the field labeled "Employee Name" (required field).
* Enter your email address in the "Employee Email" field (required field).
* Enter the contract start date in the "Contract Date" field (required field).
* Enter the contract end time in the "Contract Time" field (required field).
* Sign the "Employee Signature" field with your signature to confirm your agreement.
* Sign the "Employer Signature" field with your signature to confirm the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your work.
* **Contract Date**: Enter the date when your employment contract begins.
* **Contract Time**: Enter the time when your employment contract begins.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms of the employment contract.
* **Employer Signature**: Sign your name as the employer or authorized representative to confirm the agreement and terms of the employment contract.
