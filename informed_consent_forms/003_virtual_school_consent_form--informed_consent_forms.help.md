# Virtual School Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual School Consent Form is a form designed to collect information from parents and students for the purpose of obtaining their consent and ensuring a smooth experience for virtual school participation. This form is typically used in educational settings where students may be participating in virtual programs or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the required information for each field, which includes:
	* Parent Name
	* Student Name
	* Date of Participation (in the format mm/dd/yyyy)
	* Student Signature
	* Parent Signature

Note: If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the parent or guardian who is giving consent for their child's participation in the virtual school program.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the student who will be participating in the virtual school program.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, required/optional): Please enter the age of the student. Note: This field is not required, but it is recommended for demographic purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter a phone number where you can be reached if needed.
* **Date of Participation** (`date_of_participation`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date (mm/dd/yyyy) of the student's participation in the virtual school program.
* **Time of Participation** (`time_of_participation`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time of the student's participation in the virtual school program.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the school or institution where the student will be participating in the virtual school program.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, note, required/optional): Please sign below to confirm that you are the student participating in the virtual school program. Your signature will be kept on file for records.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required/optional): Please sign below to confirm that you are the parent/guardian giving consent for your child's participation in the virtual school program. Your signature will be kept on file for records.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
