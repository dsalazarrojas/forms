# COVID 19 Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Vaccine Consent Form is a document that you are required to complete before receiving the COVID-19 vaccine. It is designed to ensure that you are aware of the risks and benefits of the vaccine, as well as your medical history and previous COVID-19 status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. Review the informed consent section, which includes information about the benefits and risks of the vaccine.
3. Answer questions about your medical history, including any severe allergies to vaccine components, chronic medical conditions, and current medications.
4. Answer questions about your previous COVID-19 status, including whether you have had COVID-19 before and when you had it.
5. Review the section on parent or guardian information if you are a minor.
6. Sign the consent agreement, date, and acknowledge that you have read and understood the informed consent information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Your complete legal name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 01-01-1990).
* **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Current Address**: Your street address where you can be contacted for any medical needs.
* **I Have Received and Read the Vaccine Information Sheet**: Please select "I have read" to indicate that you have reviewed the vaccine information sheet.
* **I Understand the Benefits of COVID-19 Vaccination**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm that you understand the benefits of the vaccine.
* **I Understand the Possible Risks of COVID-19 Vaccination**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm that you understand the possible risks of the vaccine.
* **I Authorize Vaccination to Proceed**: Select "I authorize vaccination" to confirm that you are willing to receive the vaccine.
* **I Understand I Can Decline the Vaccine**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm that you understand that declining is an option.
* **Severe Allergies to Vaccine Components**: Please list any severe allergies to vaccine ingredients.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions**: Please list any ongoing health conditions.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Are You Pregnant or Breastfeeding**: Please select your current pregnancy or breastfeeding status.
* **Have You Had COVID-19 Before**: Please select whether you have had COVID-19 before.
* **Date of Previous COVID-19 Infection**: If you have had COVID-19 before, please enter the date you were infected.
* **I Agree to the Terms of This Informed Consent**: Select "I agree" to confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of this informed consent.
* **Date of Consent**: Please enter the date you are signing this form.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: If you are a minor, please enter the name of your parent or legal guardian.
* **I Agree to the Terms of This Informed Consent**: Select "I agree" to confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of this informed consent.

Note: Please review each field carefully and answer accordingly. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask.
