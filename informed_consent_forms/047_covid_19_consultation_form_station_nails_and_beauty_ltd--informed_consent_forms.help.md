# COVID 19 Consultation Form Station Nails and Beauty Ltd - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients before undergoing a beauty service at Station Nails and Beauty Ltd. It helps the salon to identify potential risks associated with COVID-19 and ensure a safe environment for both clients and staff. Please complete this form to help us better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully.
2. Answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
3. Use the provided options for select fields.
4. Sign and date the form before submitting it to the salon staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Appointment Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (6, time, required): Enter the time of your scheduled appointment.
* **Service Requested** (7, select_one, required): Choose the service you plan to receive today.
* **Therapist Name** (8, text, optional): If known, enter the name of the assigned therapist.
* **Health Screening** (9, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Current Symptoms** (10, select_multiple, required): If you have any symptoms, select all that apply.
* **Known COVID-19 Exposure** (11, select_one, required): Confirm if you have had known exposure to COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **Recent Positive Test** (12, select_one, required): Confirm if you have had a recent positive test for COVID-19 in the past 10 days.
* **Vaccination Status** (13, select_one, optional): Choose your current vaccination status for COVID-19.
* **Risk Acknowledgment** (14, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the risks associated with COVID-19.
* **Safety Measures Acknowledgment** (15, select_one, required): Confirm that you will comply with safety measures, including mask wearing.
* **Mask Requirement** (16, select_one, required): Choose your preference for mask wearing during service.
* **Hand Sanitizing** (17, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to hand sanitizing before service.
* **Liability Waiver** (18, select_one, required): Confirm that you release the salon from claims.
* **Assumption of Risk** (19, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you assume all risks associated with the service.
* **Rescheduling Policy** (20, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the rescheduling policy.
* **Cancellation Fee** (21, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the late cancellation policy.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions** (22, text, optional): If applicable, list any relevant medical conditions.
* **Allergies** (23, text, optional): If applicable, list any product allergies.
* **Previous Reactions** (24, text, optional): If applicable, list any previous reactions to products.
* **Nail Condition** (25, text, optional): If applicable, list any current nail issues.
* **Special Requests** (26, text, optional): If applicable, list any special requests.
* **Consent for Service** (27, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to the service.
* **Photo Consent** (28, select_one, optional): Choose whether you consent to having your photo taken.
* **Client Signature** (29, text, required): Sign your full name.
* **Therapist Signature** (30, text, optional): Sign your full name, only if the therapist is signing on your behalf.
* **Date Signed** (31, date, required): Date your signature.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your therapist for guidance.
* This form is a mandatory requirement for all clients.
* Failure to complete this form may result in cancellation of your appointment.
* Review and understand all policies before signing the form.

Note: This help guide is subject to change as per the salon's policies and procedures. Please review and update accordingly.
