## Project Documentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Documentation Consent Form is designed to gather information about a project, including its documentation, participant details, and researcher information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's informal name in the "Participant Informal Name" field.
2. Enter the participant's formal name in the "Participant Formal Name" field (optional).
3. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field (optional).
4. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field (optional).
5. Enter the project's name in the "Project Name" field.
6. Provide a brief description of the project in the "Project Description" field.
7. Select the purpose of the project from the "Purpose" field.
8. Sign the "Participant Signature" field with your signature.
9. Date your signature in the "Participant Signature Date" field.
10. Enter your researcher name in the "Researcher Name" field (optional).
11. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization Name" field (optional).
12. Enter the location of the project in the "Location" field.
13. Select the date of the project in the "Project Date" field (optional).
14. Select the time of the project in the "Time" field (optional).
15. Indicate whether photos or videos will be taken or used during the project by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Photos/Videos" field.
16. Indicate whether materials will be used or shared during the project by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Materials" field.
17. Select the consent type (Explicit, Implicit, or Both) for the project.
18. Date the researcher's signature in the "Researcher Signature Date" field.
19. Sign the "Researcher Signature" field with your signature.
20. Check the participant's informal name in the "Participant Check" field.
21. Check the researcher's informal name in the "Researcher Check" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Documentation** (Text): Enter a brief description of the project documentation.

* **Participant Phone** (Text): Enter the participant's phone number (optional).
* **Project Name** (Text): Enter the project's name.
* **Project Description** (Text): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Purpose** (Select One): Select the purpose of the project (Educational, Research, Promotional).
* **Participant Signature** (Note): Sign the form with your signature.
* **Participant Signature Date** (Date): Date your signature.
* **Researcher Name** (Text): Enter your researcher name (optional).
* **Organization Name** (Text): Enter your organization's name (optional).
* **Location** (Text): Enter the location of the project.
* **Project Date** (Date): Enter the date of the project (optional).
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time of the project (optional).
* **Photos/Videos** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether photos or videos will be taken or used during the project.
* **Materials** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether materials will be used or shared during the project.
* **Consent Type** (Select One): Select the consent type (Explicit, Implicit, Both).

* **Researcher Signature Date** (Date): Date your signature.
