# Body Sculpting Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Body Sculpting Consent Form is designed to gather information about a patient's consent and understanding of a body sculpting procedure. This form ensures that patients are fully informed about the procedure, its risks, benefits, and expectations before proceeding with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure that the patient has provided accurate and complete information.
2. For fields with a date type, use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. For fields with a select_one type, select the option that best reflects the patient's response.
4. Ensure that the patient has signed and dated the form in the presence of a healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): This is the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Body Sculpting Procedure Name** (`procedure_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the body sculpting procedure the patient is undergoing.
* **Areas to be Treated** (`treatment_areas`, text, required): List the areas of the body to be treated.
* **Procedure Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the provider performing the procedure.
* **Scheduled Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the scheduled procedure in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **The procedure has been fully explained** (`procedure_explained`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, thoroughly" if the procedure has been fully explained to the patient.
* **I understand the procedure and steps** (`understand_procedure`, select_one, required): Select "True" if the patient understands the procedure and its steps.
* **Expected results discussed** (`expected_results_discussed`, select_one, required): Select "True" if the expected results have been discussed with the patient.
* **I have realistic expectations about results** (`realistic_expectations`, select_one, required): Select "True" if the patient has realistic expectations about the results.
* **Risks and side effects explained** (`risks_explained`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, thoroughly" if the risks and side effects have been fully explained to the patient.
* **I understand the risks** (`understand_risks`, select_one, required): Select "True" if the patient understands the risks.
* **Possible complications discussed** (`complications_risks_discussed`, select_one, required): Select "True" if the possible complications have been discussed with the patient.
* **Recovery time and downtime explained** (`recovery_time_explained`, select_one, required): Select "True" if the patient understands the recovery time and downtime.
* **Alternative treatments discussed** (`alternatives_discussed`, select_one, required): Select "True" if alternative treatments have been discussed with the patient.
* **I have fully disclosed medical history** (`medical_history_disclosed`, select_one, required): Select "True" if the patient has fully disclosed their medical history.
* **I have disclosed all medications** (`medications_disclosed`, select_one, required): Select "True" if the patient has disclosed all medications.
* **I have disclosed all allergies** (`allergies_disclosed`, select_one, required): Select "True" if the patient has disclosed all allergies.
* **I am not pregnant or nursing** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Select "Not Pregnant" if the patient is not pregnant or nursing.
* **All my questions have been answered** (`questions_answered`, select_one, required): Select "True" if all questions have been answered.
* **I voluntarily consent to this procedure** (`voluntary_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I Consent" if the patient voluntarily consents to the procedure.
* **I assume all risks associated with procedure** (`assumption_of_risk`, select_one, required): Select "I Accept" if the patient assumes all risks associated with the procedure.

## Tips

* Ensure that the patient has a clear understanding of the procedure, its risks, and benefits before proceeding with the form.
* Review the patient's medical history and other medical documents to ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use this form in conjunction with other medical documents and protocols to ensure that patients are properly informed and protected.
* Store this form in the patient's medical record for future reference.
* Update this form as necessary to reflect changes in the procedure or patient's status.
* Consider using a healthcare professional to witness the patient's signature.
* Ensure that the patient understands the importance of informed consent and takes the necessary steps to document and record the consent form accurately.
