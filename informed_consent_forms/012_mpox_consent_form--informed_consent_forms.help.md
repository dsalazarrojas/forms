# Mpox Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before administering the MPOX vaccine. The form collects patient contact information, medical history, and consent for vaccine administration. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and the type of information collected.
2. Ensure the patient understands the purpose of the form and the information being collected.
3. Answer each field accurately and thoroughly, taking into account any specific requirements or restrictions for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide a contact number or email address where the patient can be reached for further questions or concerns.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Report any known medical conditions or allergies the patient has.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): If the patient has any known allergies, select "Yes" next to each allergy.
* **Vaccine Side Effects** (`vaccine_side_effects`, select_multiple, optional): If the patient has experienced any side effects after receiving the MPOX vaccine, select "Yes" next to each effect.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of consent for the vaccine administration.
* **Vaccine Name** (`vaccine_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vaccine administered to the patient, if known.

## Tips
- Ensure accurate and thorough completion of each field to avoid duplication of information or errors.
- Review each field for specific requirements or restrictions before completing.
- Use the collected information for the patient's vaccine administration process and future follow-up.
