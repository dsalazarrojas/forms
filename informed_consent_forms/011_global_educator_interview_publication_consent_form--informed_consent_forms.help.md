# Global Educator Interview Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from educators before publishing their interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the "Category" question: "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you agree to the terms of publication.
2. If you answer "Yes" to the category question, enter the category in the "Category" field.
3. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
4. Enter your school's URL in the "School URL" field.
5. Select the date you signed this form in the "Date of Signature" field.
6. Sign your name in the "Sign Here" field.
7. Enter your name in the "Interviewer Name" field.
8. Enter your project name in the "Project Name" field (this is the name of the project or initiative your interview is part of).
9. Enter the URL of your project in the "Project URL" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: This question asks you to agree or disagree with the terms of publication. Please select "Yes" if you consent to the terms.
* **Category** (text field): This is where you enter the category of your school or organization.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school or organization where you were interviewed.
* **School URL**: Enter the website URL of your school or organization.
* **Date of Signature**: Select the date you signed this form.
* **Sign Here**: Sign your name here to confirm your consent.
* **Interviewer Name**: Enter your name as the interviewer.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project or initiative your interview is part of.
* **Project URL**: Enter the URL of the project or initiative your interview is part of.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer carefully and thoughtfully, as this is a formal consent form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask your project administrator for clarification.
* Only fill out this form once for each publication you're consenting to.
