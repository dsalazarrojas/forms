# Swimming Lessons Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swimming Lessons Liability Waiver form is a document that obtains legal consent from parents or guardians for children participating in swimming lessons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and agree to the terms of the liability waiver by selecting "Yes" or "No" for the appropriate question.
2. Enter the name of the child participating in swimming lessons in the "Swimmer's Name" field.
3. Provide the child's date of birth.
4. Enter the name and relationship of the emergency contact person, such as "Grandparent" or "Coach's Mother".
5. Enter the phone number, email, and address of the emergency contact person.
6. Provide any additional contact information for the emergency contact person, such as an alternate phone number or email.
7. Leave any additional comments or messages for the emergency contact person in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Swimming Lessons Liability Waiver** (`swimming_lessons_liability_waiver`): Agree to the terms of the liability waiver by selecting "Yes" or "No". This indicates that you understand and accept the risks associated with swimming lessons and that you will not hold the organization responsible for any injuries or accidents that may occur.
* **Swimmer's Name** (`swimmer_name`): Enter the name of the child participating in swimming lessons.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Provide the child's date of birth to help the organization verify their age and eligibility for the swimming lessons.
* **Parent or Guardian** (`parent_or_guardian`): Enter your name and relationship to the child, such as "Mother" or "Father".
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Enter the relationship of the emergency contact person to the child, such as "Grandparent" or "Coach".
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_alternate_contact`): Enter the name of the alternate emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone`): Enter the phone number of the alternate emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_alternate_email`): Enter the email address of the alternate emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_alternate_address`): Enter the address of the alternate emergency contact person.
* **Additional Contact Message** (`emergency_contact_message`, `emergency_contact_alternate_message`, `emergency_contact_alternate_message_2`, `emergency_contact_message_2`, `emergency_contact_message_3`, `emergency_contact_message_4`, `emergency_contact_message_5`, `emergency_contact_message_6`, `emergency_contact_message_7`, `emergency_contact_message_8`): Leave any additional comments or messages for the emergency contact person.
