# COVID 19 Booster Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather necessary information for medical professionals to assess and prepare you for the COVID-19 booster vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the required fields in the order they are presented.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, especially in the medical history section.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification if needed.
4. Make sure to sign the final section (Consent Signature) to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Your full name as it appears on your official identification document.
    *   Please enter your full name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`): Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
    *   Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Patient ID** (`patient_id`): Your patient ID number or any unique identifier assigned by your healthcare provider.
    *   Please enter your patient ID number or the unique identifier assigned by your healthcare provider.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Your phone number for follow-up and contact purposes.
    *   Please enter your phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`): Your valid email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
    *   Please enter your valid email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
*   **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`): The date and time of your scheduled appointment for vaccination.
    *   Please enter the date and time of your scheduled appointment for vaccination.
*   **Type of Initial COVID-19 Vaccine** (`initial_vaccine_type`): The type of vaccine administered in your initial vaccination series.
    *   Please select the type of initial COVID-19 vaccine you received.
*   **Date of First Dose** (`first_dose_date`): The date of your first COVID-19 vaccination dose.
    *   Please enter the date of your first COVID-19 vaccination dose.
*   **Date of Second Dose** (`second_dose_date`): The date of your second COVID-19 vaccination dose (if applicable).
    *   Please enter the date of your second COVID-19 vaccination dose, if applicable.
*   **Any Reactions to Previous Doses?** (`previous_reactions`): Have you experienced any reactions or side effects after your previous doses?
    *   Please select "Yes" if you experienced any reactions or side effects after your previous doses.
*   **Reactions Details** (`reaction_details`): Please describe any reactions or side effects you experienced after your previous doses.
    *   If you selected "Yes" in the previous question, please describe any reactions or side effects you experienced after your previous doses.
*   **Chronic Health Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`): Do you have any chronic health conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or asthma?
    *   Please enter any chronic health conditions you have.
*   **Immunocompromised Status** (`immunocompromised`): Are you immunocompromised due to a medical condition or treatment?
    *   Please select "Yes" if you are immunocompromised due to a medical condition or treatment.
*   **Current Medications** (`current_medications`): Please list any medications you are currently taking.
    *   Please list any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Known Drug Allergies** (`drug_allergies`): Do you have any known allergies to medications?
    *   Please select "Yes" if you have any known allergies to medications.
*   **Allergies to Vaccine Components?** (`vaccine_component_allergy`): Do you have any allergies to vaccine components?
    *   Please select "Yes" if you have any allergies to vaccine components.
*   **I Understand the Benefits of the Booster Vaccine** (`benefits_understood`): Do you understand the benefits of the booster vaccine?
    *   Please select "Yes" if you understand the benefits of the booster vaccine.
*   **I Understand Potential Risks and Side Effects** (`risks_understood`): Do you understand the potential risks and side effects of the booster vaccine?
    *   Please select "Yes" if you understand the potential risks and side effects of the booster vaccine.
*   **All Relevant Medical Information Provided** (`information_provided`): Has all relevant medical information been provided to you by your healthcare provider?
    *   Please select "Yes" if all relevant medical information has been provided to you.
*   **My Questions Have Been Answered to My Satisfaction** (`questions_answered`): Have your questions and concerns been addressed to your satisfaction?
    *   Please select "Yes" if your questions and concerns have been addressed to your satisfaction.
*   **Consent Signature** (`consent_signature`): Do you voluntarily consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccine?
    *   Please select "Yes" to confirm your consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccine.
