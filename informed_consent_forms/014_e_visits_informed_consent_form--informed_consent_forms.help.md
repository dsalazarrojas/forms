# E Visits Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Visits Informed Consent Form is a critical document that ensures patients understand the benefits, risks, and schedule of e-visits. It helps to establish a clear understanding between the patient and healthcare provider, reducing the risk of misunderstandings and ensuring informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the provided information carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Ensure you understand the benefits and risks of e-visits before providing your consent.
3. Select your preferred schedule and contact information accurately.
4. Clearly indicate your consent for e-visits and data sharing.
5. Add any additional comments or information as needed.
6. Review and agree to the terms of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Informed Consent Overview** (Page 1, Required): This section provides an overview of the e-visits program and its benefits and risks. Please read this section carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any of the information.
* **Benefits of E-Visits** (Page 2, Required): This section outlines the advantages of e-visits, which may include convenience, reduced wait times, and easier access to care. Choose the benefits that apply to you.
* **Risks of E-Visits** (Page 3, Required): This section explains the potential risks associated with e-visits, such as limited face-to-face interaction. Choose the risks that apply to you.
* **E Visits Schedule** (Page 4, Required): Select the date and time for your e-visits. Make sure to choose a date and time that works for you.
* **Frequency of E Visits** (Page 5, Optional): If you would like to schedule multiple e-visits, choose the frequency that best suits your needs (Once, Daily, or Weekly).
* **Contact Information** (Page 6, Required): Enter your email address or phone number where you can be reached for follow-up e-visits and communication.
* **Consent for E Visits** (Page 7, Optional): Please indicate whether you agree or disagree to participate in e-visits.
* **Consent for Data Sharing** (Page 8, Optional): If you agree to data sharing, select the options that apply to you (True or False).
* **Additional Comments** (Page 9, Required): If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them here.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 10, Required): Enter your date of birth to ensure accurate identification and eligibility for e-visits.
* **Time of Availability** (Page 11, Required): Select a time that works best for your e-visits.
* **Additional Information** (Page 12, Optional): If you have any other information you would like to share, please do so here.
* **Schedule for Follow-Up E Visits** (Page 13, Required): Choose the date for your follow-up e-visits.
* **Time for Follow-Up E Visits** (Page 14, Required): Select the time for your follow-up e-visits.
* **Review and Agreement** (Page 15, Required): Carefully review the form and agree to the terms by checking the box.

By following these steps and understanding each field, you will be able to complete the E Visits Informed Consent Form accurately and make informed decisions about your e-visits.
