# Housekeeping Frequency Adjustment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from residents who want to adjust their housekeeping frequency. It is essential to ensure that the resident's frequency adjustment request is recorded and communicated with them accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
- Enter your resident details, including your name.
- Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, SMS, or a combination of these).
- Enter the requested changes to the housekeeping frequency.
- Check the box next to the statement "I agree to the housekeeping frequency adjustment" to indicate your consent.
- You can optionally add any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
- Finally, enter your contact details (phone number or email address) so that we can reach out to you with updates on your housekeeping frequency adjustment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Details**: This field is used to collect your personal details, such as your name, to ensure that we can identify you and communicate with you effectively.
* **Housekeeping Frequency**: Here, you can enter the specific changes you would like to make to your housekeeping frequency. Please be clear and concise in your request.
* **Consent**: This field is used to obtain your consent to the housekeeping frequency adjustment. Please check the box next to "I agree to the housekeeping frequency adjustment" to indicate your consent.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method from the options provided (phone, email, SMS, or a combination of these). This will ensure that we can communicate with you effectively and efficiently.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information you would like to provide, please enter them here.
* **Contact Details**: Please enter your contact details (phone number or email address) so that we can reach out to you with updates on your housekeeping frequency adjustment.
* **Other Contact Methods**: If your preferred contact method is not listed (e.g., social media), please specify the other contact method you would like to use.
* **Other Contact Details**: If you have provided an alternative contact method, please enter the details for that method.

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us using your preferred contact method.
- We will respond to your request and provide updates on your housekeeping frequency adjustment accordingly.
