# patient_portal_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient portal consent form is a critical component of the patient portal system, designed to ensure that patients are informed and willing participants in their healthcare management. This form collects patient demographic information, selects their preferred level of access to their medical records, and grants consent for healthcare providers to access their data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your patient information on the **Patient Information** page (page 1).
2. Review and select your preferred level of access to your medical records on the **Patient Portal Access** page (page 3).
3. Select which data categories you are willing to have accessed by healthcare providers on the **Patient Data** page (page 4).
4. Confirm your access rights on the **Access Rights** page (page 5).
5. Provide your contact information and email address on the **Contact Information** page (page 6).
6. Enter your date of birth on the **Date of Birth** page (page 8).
7. Enter your time of birth on the **Time of Birth** page (page 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (1: text, required): Provide your full name, date of birth, and email address.
- **Patient Rights** (2: note, required): This field is not directly editable by the user, but it provides a space for healthcare providers to note any specific rights or considerations for the patient.
- **Patient Portal Access** (3: select_one, required): Select your preferred level of access to your medical records:
  - Select 1: Basic access to your medical records.
  - Select 2: Limited access to your medical records with additional controls.
  - Select 3: Full access to your medical records.
- **Patient Data** (4: select_multiple, required): Select which data categories you are willing to have accessed by healthcare providers:
  - Select 1
  - Select 2
  - Select 3
  - Select 4
- **Access Rights** (5: select_one, required): Select your preferred access rights:
  - Select 1
  - Select 2
  - Select 3
- **Contact Information** (6: text, required): Enter your contact address and phone number.
- **Email** (7: email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Date of Birth** (8: date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Time of Birth** (9: time, required): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
