# Hormone Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather informed consent from patients before they undergo hormone therapy. It's essential to have a clear understanding of the treatment and the medical team involved in their care to ensure a smooth and safe treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on the main page, providing the required information about yourself and your consent status.
2. On the "patient-info" page, select the correct answer regarding your consent status.
3. On the "medical-team" page, select the medical team members involved in your care. You can choose one or more options.
4. On the "procedure-info" page, provide any additional information about the procedure you're undergoing.
5. On the "medical-team-note" page, provide any notes or comments you'd like to share with the medical team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **main-page** (text, optional): This is the main page where you'll provide essential information about yourself and your consent status. Please fill in the required fields to the best of your ability.
* **patient-info** (select_one, optional): This question asks if you're a consenting or non-consenting patient. Please select the correct answer based on your understanding of the treatment.
* **medical-team** (select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select the medical team members involved in your care. You can choose one or more options from the list.
* **procedure-info** (text, optional): If you have any additional information about the procedure, you can provide it here.
* **medical-team-note** (note, optional): This field is for any notes or comments you'd like to share with the medical team. 

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all the required fields to ensure that your information is complete.
- If you're unsure about any of the options, please ask your medical provider for clarification.
- Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
