# Clinical Workflow Automation Study Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather informed consent from participants in a clinical workflow automation study. This form is a crucial part of the study's ethical clearance process, ensuring that participants understand their role, responsibilities, and expectations in the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. **Read each page carefully**: Each page of the form is a critical part of the consent process. Please take your time to understand each section before proceeding.
2. **Fill in the required fields**: Each field marked as required must be filled in completely and accurately. Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.
3. **Select the correct options**: For fields with multiple options, select the one that best applies to your situation.
