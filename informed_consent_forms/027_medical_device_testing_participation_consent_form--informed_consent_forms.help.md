# Medical Device Testing Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to document the participation of individuals in medical device testing. The form gathers information about the participant, the testing schedule, location, and contact details. It also includes space for medical and testing-related notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Details** (page 1). This is where you'll enter your basic contact information.
2. On **Device Information** (page 2), provide details about the medical device being tested. 
3. On **Testing Schedule** (page 3), select a date and time for the testing session.
4. **Start Time** and **End Time** (page 4) fields allow you to specify the duration of the testing session.
5. **Testing Location** (page 5) is the address or location where the testing will take place.
6. **Medical Condition** (page 6) is an optional field where you can note any medical conditions relevant to the testing.
7. **Testing Equipment** (page 7) is another optional field where you can specify any equipment related to the testing.
8. On **Testing Supervisor** (page 8) you can enter the name of the person overseeing the testing.
9. In **Contact Details** (page 9), provide contact information for the participant or testing supervisor.
10. In **Contact Details**, enter the **Name**, **Phone Number**, and **Email Address** for the contact person.
11. On **Form Submission** (page 10), select one of the provided options to indicate your form submission status.
12. In **Medical Notes** and **Testing Notes** (page 11-12), you can add any additional medical-related notes or testing information.
13. Finally, **Testing Comments** (page 13) is an optional field for any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This is the first page of the form, where you'll enter your basic contact information.
* **Device Information**: Enter details about the medical device being tested.
* **Testing Schedule**: Select a date for the testing session.
* **Start Time**: Specify the start time of the testing session.
* **End Time**: Specify the end time of the testing session.
* **Testing Location**: Enter the location where the testing will take place.
* **Medical Condition**: If relevant, note any medical conditions you wish to disclose.
* **Testing Equipment**: If relevant, list any testing equipment being used.
* **Testing Supervisor**: Enter the name of the person overseeing the testing.
* **Contact Details**: Provide contact information for the participant or testing supervisor.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Form Submission**: Select one of the provided options to indicate your form submission status.
* **Medical Notes**: Add any additional medical-related notes.
* **Testing Notes**: Add any additional testing-related notes.
* **Testing Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the testing session.

## Tips

- Please fill in all required fields.
- Ensure that the testing schedule and location are correct.
- If you have any medical conditions, disclose them in the **Medical Condition** field.
- If you have any testing equipment, list it in the **Testing Equipment** field.
- If you have any testing comments, use the **Testing Comments** field.
- Review your form carefully before submission.
