# Online Teaching Case Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from participants in online teaching case studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your participant details.
2. Select your role in the case study.
3. Provide any additional remarks or comments.
4. Add your email address (if you want to be contacted about the study).
5. Add your phone number (if you want to be contacted about the study).
6. Select your availability and schedule.
7. Select any other relevant information.
8. Sign this form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, `text`, false): Enter your name, department, and affiliation. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Roles** (`Roles`, `select_one`, true): Select your role in the case study. This will help us understand your involvement in the study.
* **Signatures** (`Signatures`, `text`, false): This is a text field for any additional remarks or comments you may want to provide.
* **Agreements** (`Agreements`, `text`, false): Enter any agreements or contracts that you have signed for this case study.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, false): This is a text field for any additional information you want to provide.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, false): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about the study.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, false): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about the study.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, true): Select the date on which you want to be included in the case study.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, false): Select your availability and schedule.
* **Other** (`Other`, `select_multiple`, false): Select any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Other Other** (`Other Other`, `select_multiple`, false): Select any other additional relevant information.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, false): Sign this form to confirm your consent.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, `text`, false): Enter any agreement or contract that you have signed for this case study.
