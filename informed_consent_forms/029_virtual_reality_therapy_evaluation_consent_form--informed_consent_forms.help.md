# Virtual Reality Therapy Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect the necessary information for the evaluation and treatment of clients, ensuring that they understand the purpose, risks, and benefits of Virtual Reality (VR) therapy. It is also used to record the client's consent for data collection, which is essential for research and treatment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your basic contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of information you consent to share:
	* Client Information
	* Treatment Information
	* Other (please specify)
3. Specify whether you understand and agree to the data collection.
4. Indicate whether you consent to data collection.
5. Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share with the therapist.
6. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Text Field): Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **VR Treatment** (Text Field): Describe the type of VR treatment you are undergoing or interested in.
* **Consent** (Select One Field): Select one of the options:
	+ Client Information: You agree to share your client information with the therapist.
	+ Treatment Information: You agree to share your treatment information with the therapist.
	+ Other (please specify): If you have other information you would like to share, please specify.
* **Client Consent** (Select Multiple Field): Indicate which options you agree to:
	+ I understand and agree: You consent to the terms and conditions of the therapy.
	+ I do not understand or do not wish to provide this information: You decline to share any information.
	+ Other (please specify): If you have other comments or concerns, please specify.
* **Data Collection** (Select One Field): Select one of the options:
	+ True: You consent to data collection for research or treatment purposes.
	+ False: You decline to share your data.
* **Notes** (Text Field): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share with the therapist.
* **Email** (Email Field): Enter your email address for communication with the therapist.
* **Phone** (Text Field): Enter your phone number for communication with the therapist.
* **Therapist Info** (Text Field): Enter the name and contact information of your assigned therapist.
* **Additional Info** (Text Field): Enter any other information you would like to share with the therapist.
* **Signature** (Text Field): Please sign and date this form to confirm your consent.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask your assigned therapist.
* The information collected will be kept confidential and used only for research and treatment purposes.
