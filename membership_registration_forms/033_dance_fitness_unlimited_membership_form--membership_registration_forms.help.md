# Dance Fitness Unlimited Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for signing up for a membership at Dance Fitness Unlimited. Please complete the form with accurate information to ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your membership plan from the options provided.
2. Enter your full name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number (if required).
4. Choose your preferred payment method (Stripe or Offline).
5. Provide any additional payment details (if applicable).
6. Sign your electronic signature.
7. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plan Selection** (`Plan Selection`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of membership you are interested in. Please choose between Dance, Fitness, or Membership.
* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): If your phone number is required for your membership, enter it here.
* **Membership Plan** (`Membership Plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the membership plan that suits your needs. You can choose multiple options if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred payment method, either Stripe or Offline.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, `text`, required: false): If you selected Offline payment method, provide any additional payment details.
* **E-Signature** (`E-Signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign your electronic signature as proof of your identity.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting 'True'.

## Tips
- Ensure you select the correct membership plan and payment method.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact Dance Fitness Unlimited staff for assistance.
