# Actuaries Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Actuaries Guild Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential members to facilitate the membership application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by carefully reading the instructions and this help guide to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
3. Review each field to ensure you have provided the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Preferred Name / Display Name**: Enter your preferred name to be used in the guild directory.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Primary Email Address**: Enter your professional email address that you use for work-related communications.
* **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Desired Membership Tier**: Select your desired membership tier from the options provided.
* **Current Professional Designations**: Enter any relevant professional designations or certifications you hold.
* **Primary Area of Practice**: Select your primary area of practice from the options provided.
* **Current Employer / Organization**: Enter your current employer or organization.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Years of Actuarial Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked as an actuary.
* **Highest Degree Earned**: Enter your highest degree earned.
* **Areas of Guild Interest**: Select areas where you would like to contribute to the guild.
* **I agree to abide by the Guild Professional Code of Conduct**: Select "I Agree" to indicate your commitment to the code of conduct.
* **Application Date**: This field will be automatically populated with the current date.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely to avoid delays in the membership application process.
