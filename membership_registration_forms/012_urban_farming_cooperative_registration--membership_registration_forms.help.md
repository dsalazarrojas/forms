# Urban Farming Cooperative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Farming Cooperative Registration form is a simple, multiple-page form designed to collect contact information and interests from potential members of the urban farming cooperative. The form is intended to be user-friendly and easy to complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
2.  Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3.  Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Fill in your Address in the "Address" field.
6.  Select your interests from the list provided in the "Interests" field. You can select multiple options.
7.  Confirm your consent to join the cooperative by selecting "True" in the "Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: Your first name should be entered here.
- **Last name**: Enter your last name.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Address**: Fill in your address where you can be contacted.
- **Interests**: Select the types of activities or areas of interest that you are passionate about.
  *   Urban Farming
  *   Organic Gardening
  *   Permaculture
  *   Community Gardens
  *   Urban Agriculture
  *   Greenhouse Gas
  *   Food Security
  *   Local Food Systems
  *   Climate Change
  *   Food Sovereignty
- **Consent**: Confirm your consent to join the cooperative by selecting "True" in the dropdown menu. 

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields correctly to ensure your registration is successful.
*   Enter your interests accurately, as this will help us tailor services and information to your needs.
*   Selecting "True" in the consent field indicates that you are willing to receive emails and other communications from us.
