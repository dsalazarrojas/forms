# Gym Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register for a gym membership. It collects information about the registrant, including their name, contact details, and membership preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to help us contact you with updates and information.
3. Provide your Phone Number so we can reach you if needed.
4. Enter your Birth Date for identification purposes.
5. Select your Membership Type (Yes/No) to indicate your interest in becoming a member.
6. Choose your Membership Term (Yes/No) to specify your membership duration.
7. Fill in your Payment Amount for your membership fee.
8. Select your Payment Method (Yes/No) to indicate your preferred payment method.
9. Choose your Payment Status (Active/Inactive) to reflect your payment status.
10. Enter the Payment Date and Payment Time for your payment details.
11. Fill in any additional Payment Note if needed.
12. Select your Membership Status (Active/Inactive) to reflect your current status.
13. Enter your Membership Start Date and Membership End Date for your membership timeline.
14. Choose your Membership Term (Yes/No) to specify your membership duration.
15. Fill in your Membership Type ID for your membership type.
16. Enter your Membership ID for your membership ID.
17. Provide your Gym Name, Gym Location, Gym Address, and Gym Phone Number to help us get in touch with you.
18. Enter your Gym Email for us to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address to contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where we can reach you if needed.
* **Birth Date**: Your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Membership Type**: Select 'Yes' if you are interested in becoming a member, 'No' otherwise.
* **Membership Term**: Choose 'Yes' to specify a membership duration, 'No' otherwise.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay for your membership fee.
* **Payment Method**: Select 'Yes' if you prefer to pay online, 'No' otherwise.
* **Payment Status**: Choose 'Active' if you have paid for your membership, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date you made your payment.
* **Payment Time**: Enter the time you made your payment.
* **Payment Note**: Enter any additional information about your payment.
* **Membership Status**: Select 'Active' if you are currently a member, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Membership Start Date**: Enter the date your membership started.
* **Membership End Date**: Enter the date your membership will end.
* **Membership Term**: Choose 'Yes' to specify a membership duration, 'No' otherwise.
* **Membership Type ID**: Enter your membership type ID.
* **Membership ID**: Enter your membership ID.
* **Gym Name**: Enter the name of the gym you are applying to.
* **Gym Location**: Enter the location of the gym you are applying to.
* **Gym Address**: Enter the address of the gym you are applying to.
* **Gym Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the gym you are applying to.
* **Gym Email**: Enter the email of the gym you are applying to.
