## Purpose
The City Wide Chapter Registration form is designed for local community chapters to register with the city-wide network. This form collects essential information to help the chapter become part of the city-wide chapter network and provides a central repository of chapter data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button after filling out all required fields.
2.  Fill out the chapter information section, which includes the official chapter name and city region.
3.  Enter the lead contact's name, email, and phone number.
4.  Provide the current membership count and chapter mission statement.
5.  Select the chapter's primary focus areas and meeting frequency (if applicable).
6.  If your chapter maintains a social media or website, please include the relevant link.
7.  Specify what support your chapter needs from the city-wide network.
8.  Confirm that your chapter agrees to follow the city-wide governance bylaws.
9.  Finally, set the date of registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Chapter Information
*   **Official Chapter Name** (`chapter_name`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of your local chapter.
### City Region or District
*   **City Region or District** (`city_region`, `text`, required): Select the city region or district your local chapter operates in.
### Primary Chapter Contact
*   **Lead Contact Full Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the contact person for your chapter.
*   **Lead Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person for your chapter.
*   **Lead Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for your chapter.
### Chapter Details
*   **Current Number of Members** (`current_membership_count`, `number`, required): Enter the current number of members in your chapter.
*   **Chapter Mission Statement** (`chapter_mission`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your chapter's goals and objectives.
### Primary Focus Areas
*   **Primary Focus Areas** (`primary_focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all applicable focus areas from the list (Youth Mentorship, Environmental Sustainability, Public Health, Arts and Culture, Local Business Support, Housing Advocacy).
### Meeting Frequency
*   **Regular Meeting Frequency** (`meeting_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often your chapter meets (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
### Additional Information
*   **Chapter Social Media or Website** (`social_media_link`, `text`, optional): If your chapter has a social media presence or website, enter the relevant link here.
*   **Support Needed** (`resource_needs`, `text`, optional): Specify what support your chapter needs from the city-wide network.
### Agreement
*   **We Agree to Follow the City-Wide Governance Bylaws** (`agreement_governance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your chapter agrees to follow the city-wide governance bylaws.
### Registration
*   **Date of Registration** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Set the date of registration.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
*   If your chapter has a social media presence, please include the link.
*   If your chapter meets regularly, please select the meeting frequency.
