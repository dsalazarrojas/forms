# Member Registration And Ministry Alignment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for members to register and align their interests and availability with their serving opportunities within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the text field at the top of the page.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the text field below the "First name" field.
3. Enter your valid **Email** address in the text field.
4. Enter your **Phone number** in the text field.
5. Select a date for your **Availability** from the calendar picker.
6. Select your **Ministry interests** by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
7. Select your **Serving opportunities** by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
8. If you have any additional information for **Ministry alignment**, please enter it in the text field.
9. Optionally, you may add a **Note** for the organization's records.
10. Select the member you are **Assigned to** from the dropdown menu (only "62773" is available as an option, or choose "Other" for a custom selection).
11. If applicable, select a **Assigned at** time from the time picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, Text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, Text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Availability** (`availability`, Date, required): Select a date for your availability.
* **Ministry interests** (`ministry_interests`, Select Multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you have an interest in ministry, "No" otherwise.
* **Serving opportunities** (`serving_opportunities`, Select One, required): Select "Yes" if you are interested in serving opportunities, "No" otherwise.
* **Ministry alignment** (`ministry_alignment`, Text, optional): Enter any additional information about your ministry alignment.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, optional): Optionally add any notes for the organization's records.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, Select One, required): Select the member you are assigned to (only "62773" is available as an option, or choose "Other" for a custom selection).
* **Assigned at** (`assigned_at`, Time, optional): Select a time for assignment, if applicable.
