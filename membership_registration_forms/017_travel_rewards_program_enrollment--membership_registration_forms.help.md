# Travel Rewards Program Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Rewards Program is designed to provide exclusive benefits and discounts to its members. By enrolling in this program, you will be able to access special offers, discounts, and rewards tailored to your travel preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is how we will contact you with program updates and benefits.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is optional but will help us contact you with special offers and promotions.
4. Select your preferred method of receiving travel updates in the "Receive Travel Updates" field.
5. Choose how often you travel in the "How Often Travel" field.
6. Select the segment of the program that best describes you in the "What Segment" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Enter your time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field in the format HH:MM.
9. If you have any comments or additional information about yourself, you can enter them in the "Comments" field.
10. Select your travel preferences in the "Travel Preferences" field. You can choose multiple preferences.
11. If you have any other travel preferences not listed, you can select them in the "Other Travel Preferences" field.
12. Choose your travel style in the "Travel Style" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the program.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address as we will use it to contact you with program updates and benefits.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number to receive special offers and promotions.
* **Receive Travel Updates** (`email_opt_in`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to receive travel updates: `True` for yes, `False` for no.
* **How Often Travel** (`preferred_travel_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how often you travel: `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly`, `quarterly`, or `yearly`.
* **What Segment** (`program_segment`, `select_one`, required): Select the segment of the program that best describes you: `loyalty`, `referral`, or `partner`.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required): Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any comments or additional information about yourself, enter them here.
* **Travel Preferences** (`travel_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your travel preferences: `beach`, `city`, `cultural`, `nature`, `adventure`, or `history`.
* **Other Travel Preferences** (`other_travel_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any other travel preferences not listed, select them here.
* **Travel Style** (`travel_style`, `select_one`, required): Choose your travel style: `luxury`, `budget`, `adventure`, `relaxation`, `cultural`, or `nature`.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as this is how we will contact you with program updates and benefits.
* Choose your travel style and preferences accurately, as these will help us tailor the rewards and services offered to you.
* If you have any comments or additional information, feel free to enter them in the "Comments" field.
