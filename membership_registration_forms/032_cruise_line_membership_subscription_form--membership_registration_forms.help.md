# cruise_line_membership_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help users subscribe to a cruise line membership. It collects user and contact information, payment details, and membership preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user info and contact information.
2. Choose your payment method and date.
3. Input your payment amount and email address.
4. Select your membership level, cruise line, ship, cabin, and membership status.
5. Choose the start and end dates of your membership.
6. If you have any notes, input them in the notes field.
