# Fitness Chain Multi Location Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fitness Chain Multi Location Membership Form" is a registration form designed for new members to provide their details and preferences for membership at various locations of the "Fitness Chain" organization. This form is used to collect information for membership registration, ensuring that all necessary details are captured for a seamless membership experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the preferred membership plan from the options provided.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes if necessary.
5. Provide your Membership ID (if applicable).
6. Choose your preferred branch location from the options.
7. Enter emergency contact information, including the relationship with the person and their phone number.
8. Enter the emergency contact's email address.
9. Provide any additional emergency notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  - **Select Membership Plan** (`select_plan`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred membership plan from the options provided. You can select multiple plans if applicable.
  - **Other Membership Plan** (`select_plan_other`, select_one, required: false): If your preferred membership plan is not listed, choose "Other" and describe it in the "Additional Comments" field.
  - **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your visit to the branch location.
  - **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your membership registration.
  - **Membership ID** (`membership_id`, number, required: false): Enter your Membership ID (if applicable).
  - **Branch Location** (`branch_location`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred branch location from the options provided.
  - **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
  - **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, select_one, required: false): Choose the relationship between you and your emergency contact person.
  - **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
  - **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required: false): Enter your emergency contact person's email address.
  - **Emergency Notes** (`emergency_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional emergency notes or comments.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the "Fitness Chain" organization for assistance.
- Double-check your contact information and emergency contact details for accuracy.
