# Cultural Association Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the renewal process of cultural associations. It is intended for existing members to update their information, membership details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Complete the member information section (page 1) with your details.
3. Proceed to the membership details section (page 2) and update your information as necessary.
4. Finally, provide your payment information in the payment info section (page 3).
5. Click the "submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Member Information (Page 1)
* **Member Information** (`member_info`, text, required): This section is for you to provide your member information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.

### Membership Details (Page 2)
* **Membership Details** (`membership_details`, text, required): This section is for you to update your membership details, including your membership type, status, and any other relevant information. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.

### Payment Information (Page 3)
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required): This section is for you to provide your payment information, including your payment method and any other relevant payment details. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips
- Please review each section carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact our support team at `form@gic.mx`.
