# Coffee Shop Tasting Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for potential members of a coffee shop tasting membership program. It collects demographic and personal preferences to tailor the experience to the user's needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the member information section with your details.
2. Select your membership tier, duration, and start date.
3. Indicate your coffee experience level.
4. Choose your preferred flavor profiles and roast preferences.
5. Select your preferred tasting day and schedule preferences.
6. Indicate if you'll be attending with others or alone.
7. State your membership goals.
8. Mention any allergies or health considerations.
9. Select your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This is the first name of the person filling out the form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): This is the last name of the person filling out the form.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): This is the email address of the person filling out the form, which will be used for membership communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): This is the phone number of the person filling out the form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): This is the date of birth of the person filling out the form, not required for membership registration.
* **Membership Tier** (`membership_tier`, `select_one`, required): Choose your membership level: Silver, Gold, Platinum, or Premium.
* **Membership Duration** (`duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of your membership: 3 months, 6 months, 12 months, or Ongoing.
* **Membership Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Choose the start date of your membership.
* **Coffee Experience Level** (`coffee_experience`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your experience level with coffee: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Connoisseur.
* **Roast Preferences** (`roast_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all roast preferences that interest you: Light Roast, Medium Roast, Dark Roast, or All equally.
* **Preferred Origins** (`origin_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all origins that interest you: Latin America, Africa, Asia-Pacific, Single Origin, or Blends.
* **Flavor Profiles of Interest** (`flavor_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all flavor profiles that interest you: Fruity, Chocolate, Nutty, Floral, Spicy, Earthy, Sweet, or Bold.
* **Preferred Tasting Day** (`tasting_day_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred tasting day: Weekday morning, Weekday afternoon, Weekend morning, Weekend afternoon, Evening, or Flexible.
* **Will you Attend Alone or with Others** (`group_size`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you'll be attending with others or alone.
* **Membership Goals** (`membership_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all membership goals: Learn about coffee origins, Improve tasting skills, Discover new favorites, Social engagement, or Educational development.
* **Allergies or Health Considerations** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): Mention any dietary or health considerations.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Monthly Billing.
