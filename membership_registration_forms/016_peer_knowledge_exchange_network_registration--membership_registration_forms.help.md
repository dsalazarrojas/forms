# Peer Knowledge Exchange Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information about an individual's background, expertise, and contact details for the Peer Knowledge Exchange Network.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2.  Select the relevant options for each field.
3.  Review and agree to the terms of the network.
4.  Confirm your membership type and submission details.
5.  Submit the form for review and consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Details (id: 1)**: A brief description of the individual's background and experience.
*   **Learning Goals (id: 2)**: A description of the individual's learning goals and expectations from the network.
*   **Peer Details (id: 3)**: A description of the individual's background and expertise, including their role and organization.
*   **Expertise (id: 4)**: A selection of the individual's relevant areas of expertise.
*   **Membership Type (id: 5)**: A selection of the type of membership desired.
*   **Member Type (id: 6)**: A selection of the type of member the individual wishes to be.
*   **Email (id: 7)**: The individual's email address.
*   **Phone Number (id: 8)**: The individual's phone number.
*   **Date (id: 9)**: The date of the submission.
*   **Time (id: 10)**: The time of the submission.
*   **Notes (id: 11)**: Any additional comments or information relevant to the submission.
*   **Agreement (id: 12)**: A text field for the individual to agree to the network's terms.
*   **Text of Agreement (id: 13)**: A text field for the individual to confirm their understanding of the agreement.
*   **Confirmation (id: 14)**: A selection of whether the individual confirms or declines the membership.
*   **Additional Info (id: 15)**: Any additional information relevant to the submission.
*   **Organization (id: 16)**: The individual's organization name.
*   **Role (id: 17)**: The individual's role within the organization.
*   **Contact Info (id: 18)**: A selection of the individual's contact information.
*   **Address (id: 19)**: The individual's address.
*   **City (id: 20)**: The city of the individual's address.
*   **State (id: 21)**: The state of the individual's address.
*   **Zip Code (id: 22)**: The zip code of the individual's address.
*   **Country (id: 23)**: The country of the individual's address.
*   **Confirm Email (id: 24)**: A confirmation of the email address.
*   **Confirm Phone (id: 25)**: A confirmation of the phone number.
