# Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Renewal Form is a form for existing members to provide updated information to continue their membership. This form is used to collect personal details and membership specifics to facilitate the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Membership Renewal Form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure that you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
6. Enter your Address in the Address field.
7. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
8. Enter your Date Joined (when you initially joined the membership) in the Date Joined field.
9. Enter your Renewal Date (the date you wish to renew your membership) in the Renewal Date field.
10. Select your Membership Level from the Membership Level field.
11. Select your Membership Type from the Membership Type field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Email**: Enter your current email address where you can be contacted for membership updates and news.
* **Phone**: Enter your current phone number where you can be contacted for membership updates and news.
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you can receive membership-related communications and mail.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Date Joined**: Enter the date you initially joined the membership in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
* **Renewal Date**: Enter the date you wish to renew your membership in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2025).
* **Membership Level**: Select the level of membership that best describes your current status:
	+ Level 1: Basic membership level with limited benefits.
	+ Level 2: Standard membership level with standard benefits.
	+ Level 3: Premium membership level with enhanced benefits.
* **Membership Type**: Select the type of membership that best describes your status:
	+ Type A: Individual membership.
	+ Type B: Family membership.
	+ Type C: Corporate membership.
