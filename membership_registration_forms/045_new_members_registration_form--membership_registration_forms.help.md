# New Members Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new members during the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
5. If applicable, select your member type from the "Member Type" field.
6. If you have any additional information to provide, enter it in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *required*): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, *optional*): This field is optional and allows you to enter your phone number.
* **Member Type** (`member_type`, `text`, *required*): Select your member type from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, *optional*): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, `time`, *optional*): This field is currently not used for any specific purpose; it is optional and can be filled in if desired.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `note`, *optional*): Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your registration.

## Tips

* Make sure your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* If you have trouble selecting your member type, ask a staff member for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for support.
