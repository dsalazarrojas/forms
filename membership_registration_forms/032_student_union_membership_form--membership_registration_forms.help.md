# Student Union Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Union Membership Form is used to register students as members of the Student Union. It collects data for membership registration, which is essential for student union administration and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct options for fields like "student_union_status" and "student_union_payment_status".
3. Enter any relevant comments or additional details in the "additional_comments", "status_comments", and "payment_comments" fields.
4. Review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or records.
* **Student Email**: Enter your university-provided email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Student Email Verification**: Select "Verified" if your email address has been verified by the university, or "Unverified" if not.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "DD/MM/YYYY".
* **Day**: Enter the day of your birth (01-31).
* **Month**: Enter the month of your birth (01-12).
* **Year**: Enter the year of your birth (4-digit number).
* **Student Union Fee**: Enter the membership fee amount (if applicable).
* **Student Union Status**: Select "Active" if you are currently a member, "Inactive" if not.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your membership status update (if applicable).
* **Comment**: Enter any relevant comments about your membership status.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you submitted your membership application.
* **Application Time**: Enter the time you submitted your membership application (if applicable).
* **Student Union Payment Status**: Select "Paid" if you have paid your membership fee, "Pending" if not, or "Unpaid" if still pending.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you made your payment (Bank, Card, or Cash).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you paid for membership (if applicable).
* **Additional Fees**: Enter any additional fees (if applicable).
* **Status Comments**: Enter any comments about your membership status.
* **Payment Comments**: Enter any comments about your payment status.
* **Additional Details**: Enter any additional details or comments.
* **Membership Number**: Enter your membership number (if assigned).
* **Application ID**: Enter your membership application ID (if assigned).

Please review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
