# Alumni Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Alumni Registration Form is a necessary step for alumni to become a part of the community. It provides essential information for membership registration. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Start Form" button to begin the registration process.
2.  Fill out each field as instructed, and be sure to read the hints provided for each section.
3.  Select your membership type from the options provided.
4.  Complete all required fields (marked as **required/true**).
5.  Review each section carefully before proceeding.
6.  Review your form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, required/true): Enter your registration date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, required/true): Select your preferred membership level from the options provided.
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, required/true): Enter your first name to complete the registration process.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, required/true): Enter your last name to complete the registration process.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, required/true): Enter your primary contact email.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, required/true): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required/false): Enter your Date of Birth in MM/DD/YYYY format, if available.
*   **Street Address** (`street_address`, required/true): Enter your street address to complete the registration process.
*   **City** (`city`, required/true): Enter your city of residence.
*   **State/Province** (`state`, required/true): Enter your state or province of residence.
*   **Zip/Postal Code** (`zip_code`, required/true): Enter your zip or postal code.
*   **Country** (`country`, required/true): Select your country of residence from the options provided.
*   **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, required/true): Enter your graduation year to complete the registration process.
*   **Degree Earned** (`degree`, required/true): Select the degree earned from the provided options.
*   **Major/Field of Study** (`major`, required/true): Enter your major or field of study.
*   **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, required/false): If available, enter your student ID number.
*   **Donation Interest** (`donation_interest`, required/false): Select whether you are interested in making a donation to support your alma mater.
*   **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, required/false): Enter your desired donation amount in USD if making a donation.
*   **Donation Designation** (`donation_designation`, required/false): Select where your donation will be directed.
*   **Alumni Newsletter Subscription** (`newsletter_subscription`, required/true): Choose whether you want to receive the alumni newsletter through email or print.
*   **Receive Event Notifications** (`event_notifications`, required/true): Select whether you want to receive event notifications.
*   **Include in Alumni Directory** (`directory_inclusion`, required/true): Choose whether you allow other alumni to find your contact information.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, required/true): Select "I agree to the terms and conditions" to complete the registration process.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and thorough when filling out each section.
*   Ensure you meet the minimum requirements for membership.
*   Review each section carefully before proceeding to avoid errors.
