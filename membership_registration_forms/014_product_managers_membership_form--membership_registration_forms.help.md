# Product Managers Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from product managers to register their membership in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your role in the "Role" field (e.g., "Yes" for product manager).
5. If you are associated with a product, select the product in the "Product" field.
6. Enter your department in the "Department" field (if applicable).
7. Enter your company name in the "Company" field (if applicable).
8. Enter your job title in the "Job title" field (if applicable).
9. Enter your location in the "Location" field (if applicable).
10. Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities in the "Job description" field (if applicable).
11. If you have a reporting manager, select "Yes" in the "Reporting manager" field.
12. If you are part of a team, select "Yes" in the "Team members" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required/optional): Select your role within the organization (e.g., "Yes" for product manager).
* **Product** (`product`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the product you are associated with.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter your location.
* **Job description** (`job_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Reporting manager** (`reporting_manager`, select_one, required/optional): If you have a reporting manager, select "Yes".
* **Team members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you are part of a team, select "Yes".
