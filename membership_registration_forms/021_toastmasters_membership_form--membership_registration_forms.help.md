# Toastmasters Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to join a Toastmasters club. It is used to gather details about the individual, such as their name, contact information, and preferences for club activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by a asterisk (\*).
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name* (**`First Name`**, `text`): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name* (**`Last Name`**, `text`): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email* (**`email`**, `email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (**`phone`**, `text`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Club Preferences* (**`club_preferences`**, `select_multiple`): Select the clubs you would like to be a part of (multiple selections allowed).
* **Club Location* (**`club_location`**, `select_multiple`): Select the location(s) where you would like to attend club meetings (multiple selections allowed).
* **Contact Preference Method* (**`contact_preference_method`**, `select_one`): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Method 1, Method 2, or Method 3).
* **Meeting Days* (**`meeting_days`**, `select_multiple`): Select the days of the week you would like to attend meetings (multiple selections allowed).
* **Meeting Time* (**`meeting_time`**, `time`): Enter the time you would like to meet (format: `HH:MM`).
* **Meeting Duration* (**`meeting_duration`**, `number`): Enter the duration of your meetings in minutes.
* **Meeting Frequency* (**`meeting_frequency`**, `select_multiple`): Select the frequency of your meetings (multiple selections allowed).
* **Meeting Location* (**`meeting_location`**, `select_one`): Choose the location of your meetings (Location 1, Location 2, or Location 3).
* **Meeting Method* (**`meeting_method`**, `select_one`): Choose the method of your meetings (Method 1, Method 2, or Method 3).
* **Club Type* (**`club_type`**, `select_multiple`): Select the type(s) of clubs you would like to be a part of (multiple selections allowed).
* **Additional Comments** (**`additional_comments`**, `text`): Provide any additional comments or information about your preferences.
