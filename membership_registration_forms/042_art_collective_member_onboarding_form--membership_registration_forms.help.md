# Art Collective Member Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and understand your artistic background, interests, and preferences for membership in our art collective. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information for consideration and a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the fields on each page, making sure to answer all the required fields (marked as `required` in this guide).
* You can navigate between pages by scrolling down or using the navigation links at the bottom of each page.
* When you finish filling out the form, click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
* **Full Name**: Please provide your full name as you would like to be referred to within the collective.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address we will use to communicate with you about your membership and any updates.
* **Phone Number**: Please provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: This is an optional field for demographic purposes.
* **Years of Experience**: Please provide your years of experience in your artistic discipline.
* **Brief Artistic Statement or Philosophy**: Please describe your artistic philosophy or a brief statement about your artistic approach.

### Artistic Background
* **Primary Art Discipline**: Please select your primary art discipline from the options provided.
* **Portfolio Website or Link**: If you have a portfolio website, please provide the URL.

### Social Media and Online Presence
* **Instagram Handle**: If you have an Instagram account, please provide your handle.
* **Personal Website URL**: If you have a personal website, please provide the URL.
* **Other Social Media Platforms**: If you have other social media platforms, please list them here.

### Collective Involvement
* **Desired Membership Type**: Please select the type of membership you are interested in.
* **Areas of Participation Interest**: Please select the areas you are interested in participating in.
* **Availability for Meetings and Events**: Please select your availability for meetings and events.
* **Conflicts of Interest with Collective**: Please indicate if you have any conflicts of interest with the collective, and describe them if necessary.

### Agreement and Confirmation
* **I Agree to Abide by Collective Bylaws and Code of Conduct**: Please confirm your agreement to abide by the collective's bylaws and code of conduct.

### Additional Information or Questions
* **Additional Information or Questions**: If you have any additional information or questions, please provide them here.
