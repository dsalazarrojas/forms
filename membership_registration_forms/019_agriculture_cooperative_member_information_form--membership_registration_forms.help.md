# Agriculture Cooperative Member Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from farmers or agricultural businesses interested in joining the agriculture cooperative. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to provide the cooperative with the necessary details to manage your membership effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your member details, including your name and primary contact information.
2. Choose your membership type (Individual Farmer, Partnership, Corporation, or LLC).
3. If you have a farm name different from your member name, provide it.
4. Select your primary production type (grain, dairy, beef, pork, poultry, vegetables, or fruit).
5. Enter your total acreage owned or leased.
6. If applicable, provide an approximate livestock head count.
7. Specify the number of years you have been farming.
8. Select the cooperative services you are interested in (marketing, supply purchasing, financing, agronomy consulting, storage/drying).
9. Choose a voting delegate name, who will represent you at meetings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name**: Enter your individual or business name as the member of the cooperative. 
* **Membership Type**: Choose your legal structure: Individual Farmer, Partnership, Corporation, or LLC. 
* **Farm Name**: If different from member name, enter the farm's name. 
* **Mailing Address**: Provide your primary mailing address.
* **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the person you would like us to contact regarding your membership. 
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number for communication. 
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for newsletters and updates. 
* **Primary Production**: Choose your main source of income (grain, dairy, beef, pork, poultry, vegetables, or fruit). 
* **Total Acreage**: Enter the total acreage you own or lease.
* **Livestock Head Count**: Enter an approximate number of livestock, if applicable.
* **Years Farming**: Enter the number of years you have been farming.
* **Services Interested In**: Select all services you are interested in, such as marketing, supply purchasing, financing, agronomy consulting, or storage/drying.
* **Voting Delegate Name**: Choose the person who will represent you at cooperative meetings.
