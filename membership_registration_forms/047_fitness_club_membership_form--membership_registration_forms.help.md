# Fitness Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for joining the fitness club. It consists of three pages: the main registration page, a page for payment information, and a final page for payment confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Main Registration Page**. This page is not required to be filled out but it is recommended to provide a clear picture of who you are.
2. Proceed to the **Payment Information Page** where you will provide payment details.
3. Finally, move on to the **Payment Confirmation Page** where you will confirm the payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Registration Page
* **Main Page** (`main-page`, text, required: false):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you will enter your details.
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant information you want to share with the club.

### Payment Information Page
* **Payment Info Page** (`payment-info-page`, text, required: false):
	+ On this page, provide your payment details such as your payment method and the amount you wish to pay.

### Payment Confirmation Page
* **Payment Confirm Page** (`payment-confirm-page`, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm your payment details to complete the registration process.
	+ Make sure all the details entered on the payment information page are accurate.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your payment details to avoid any issues.
* If you encounter any issues, contact us for support.
