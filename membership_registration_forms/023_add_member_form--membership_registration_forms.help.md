# Add Member Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Add Member Form is a crucial tool to collect membership information from individuals or groups, ensuring a smooth and efficient membership process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct membership level that suits your needs.
3. Enter your desired membership start date.
4. Provide a brief summary of your billing information.
5. Enter your emergency contact name and phone number (if applicable).
6. Select your areas of interest for membership activities.
7. Add any additional notes or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Your primary contact name for membership communications.
* **Email address**: The email address for receiving membership communications and updates.
* **Phone number**: Your primary phone number for membership-related calls and updates.
* **Membership level**: Select the membership tier that aligns with your needs (Basic, Premium, Student, Senior).
* **Membership start date**: The date you would like your membership to be effective from.
* **Billing information summary**: A brief description of your payment method or billing address.
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: The name and phone number of someone to contact in case of emergencies (if you don't have an emergency contact).
* **Interests and activities**: Check the areas of interest for membership activities (Events, Workshops, Volunteering, Newsletter).
* **Additional notes**: Any additional information or special requests you have for your membership (e.g., accessibility needs).
