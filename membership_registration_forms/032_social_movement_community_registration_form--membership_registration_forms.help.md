This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from individuals interested in joining a social movement community. The information collected will be used to facilitate communication and community interactions within the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields (marked as `true` in the form).
2.  Be accurate and truthful in your responses.

* **Personal Info (Personal Info, text, required)**: Please provide a brief description of yourself.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, text, required)**: Enter your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone number, or social media handle).
* **Occupation (Occupation, select_one, required)**: Select your current occupation from the provided options (e.g., Developer, Artist, Scientist, or Teacher).

* **Confirm Your Email (Confirm Your Email, select_multiple, required)**: Confirm your email address by selecting 'True'. This is to ensure we can contact you securely and efficiently.
