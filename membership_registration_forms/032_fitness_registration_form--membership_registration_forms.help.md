# Fitness Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Registration Form is designed to gather information from individuals who are interested in joining a fitness program or seeking guidance on their fitness journey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please fill in your details as required.
2. Select your relevant options for each field as accurately as possible.
3. Ensure that you submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address in this field. This will be used to communicate with you regarding your fitness journey.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number in this field.
* **Why Fitness** (`motivation`, `text`, required: false): Briefly explain why you are interested in joining a fitness program.
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, required: false): Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Sport** (`sport_background`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one or more sports you have a background in.
* **Fitness** (`fitness_background`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more areas of fitness you are interested in.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you have health insurance.
* **Nutrition** (`nutrition_background`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more dietary preferences.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to submit your registration.
