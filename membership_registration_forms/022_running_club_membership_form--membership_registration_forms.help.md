# running_club_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The running club membership form is designed to collect personal details from individuals interested in joining the running club. The form gathers basic information such as name, contact, and demographic details to enable the club to manage member data and provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name**: Please fill in your first name.
2. Enter your **Last name**: Please fill in your last name.
3. Enter your **Email**: Provide a valid email address to help us stay in touch with you.
4. Enter your **Phone number**: Please enter your phone number for communication purposes.
5. Enter your **Date of birth**: Fill in your Date of birth (DD/MM/YY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`First name`, text, required): A field to enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, text, required): A field to enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): A field for your email address to ensure we can stay in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): A field to enter your phone number for communication.
* **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, date, required): A field to enter your date of birth, in the format DD/MM/YY.
