# Dog Walking Club Pet Owner Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Walking Club Pet Owner Membership Form is designed to collect information from pet owners who want to join the club. This form helps us understand the owner's and pet's details to provide a safe and enjoyable dog walking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to enter your phone number and email address correctly.
3. If you have a dog, select the correct breed, size, and walk frequency.
4. Choose your preferred membership type and payment method.
5. Provide any necessary medical information about your pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter your pet's name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required): Enter your phone number in the format "1234567890".
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format "username@example.com".
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, optional): If you have a dog, select its breed from the list.
* **Dog Size** (`dog_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of your dog (Large, Medium, Small).
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the membership type that suits your needs (Standard, Premium, Basic).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Stripe, PayPal, Bank).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, date, optional): Choose a date and time for your dog walks, if applicable.
* **Walk Frequency** (`walk_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of dog walks (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Group Walk** (`group_walk`, select_multiple, optional): If you prefer to walk with other dogs, select this option.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Pet Diet** (`pet_diet`, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies of your pet.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies of your pet.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): Enter any medication your pet is taking.
* **Veterinary Clinic** (`veterinary_clinic`, text, optional): Enter the name of your pet's veterinary clinic.
* **Veterinarian** (`veterinarian`, text, optional): Enter the name of your pet's veterinarian.
* **Pet License** (`pet_license`, text, optional): Enter your pet's license number.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, required): Sign this form to confirm your membership.
* **Owner Date** (`owner_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Owner Time** (`owner_time`, time, required): Enter the time you signed this form.

## Tips
- Please review each field carefully and enter accurate information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our membership team for assistance.
- A valid phone number and email address are required for communication purposes.
- Membership details can be updated or changed at any time.
- The emergency contact information is for your safety and your pet's. Please ensure it is accurate.
