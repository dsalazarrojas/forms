# Canoe Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Canoe Club Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to become members of our Canoe Club. This form helps us understand your paddling experience, skills, and interests to provide you with suitable services and ensure your safety on and off the water.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Welcome to our Canoe Club!** Read the introductory note to understand our club's purpose and what to expect.
2. Select your preferred language for the form.
3. Fill out your **Personal Information**:
	* Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
	* Select your **Date of Birth** to confirm your age.
	* Choose your **Gender**.
4. Provide your **Contact Information**:
	* Enter your **Email Address**.
	* Enter your **Phone Number**.
	* Enter your **Street Address**, **City**, **State/Province**, and **ZIP or Postal Code**.
5. Share your **Paddling Experience**:
	* Select your **Paddling Experience Level** (beginner, intermediate, advanced, etc.).
	* Let us know if you've **Paddled a Canoe Specifically** (yes or no).
	* Indicate if you can **Swim** (strong, adequate, weak, or not at all).
6. Choose your **Participation Preferences**:
	* Select the types of **Paddling Activities** that interest you (recreational, racing, touring, etc.).
	* Indicate how often you plan to **Participate** (weekly, biweekly, monthly, etc.).
7. **Describe Your Paddling Goals** (optional).
8. Choose your **Membership Type** (individual, family, student, etc.).
9. Share **Emergency Contact Information**:
	* Enter the name and **Phone Number** of an emergency contact.
	* Indicate your **Relationship to the Contact**.
10. **Confirm Liability and Safety**:
	* Agree to the **Liability Waiver** and assume all risks.
	* Confirm you'll **Follow Club Safety Rules**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Required to confirm your age.
* **Email Address**: Your valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Best contact number.
* **Street Address**: Your physical address.
* **City**: City where you live.
* **State/Province**: State or province where you live.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: ZIP or postal code for your address.
* **Paddling Experience Level**: Select your level of experience.
* **Paddled a Canoe Specifically**: Have you paddled a canoe before?
* **Can Swim**: Can you swim?
* **Comfort Level on Water**: How comfortable are you on the water?
* **Types of Paddling Activities**: Select all activities that interest you (e.g., recreational, racing, touring).
* **Participation Frequency**: How often do you plan to participate?
* **Paddling Goals**: Briefly describe your goals (optional).
* **Membership Type**: Choose your membership type (individual, family, student, etc.).
* **Emergency Name**: Name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Contact**: Relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Liability Waiver**: Confirm you agree to the liability waiver and assume all risks.
* **Follow Club Safety Rules**: Confirm you'll follow all club safety rules.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your emergency contact or contact the Canoe Club administrators.
* We'll review your application and get back to you soon.
