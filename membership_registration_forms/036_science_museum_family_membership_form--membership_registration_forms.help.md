# Science Museum Family Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Museum Family Membership Form is designed to gather information from family members who wish to become Science Museum members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date details.
3. Make sure to answer all questions completely.
4. If you are unsure about any section, contact the museum staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Membership Application** (Page 1)
	+ A description of the household applying for membership.
* **family_name** (Page 1)
	+ The name of the family applying for membership.
* **email** (Page 1)
	+ A valid email address for communication with the museum.
* **phone_number** (Page 1)
	+ A working phone number for the family.
* **street_address** (Page 1)
	+ The address of the household.
* **city** (Page 1)
	+ The city where the household is located.
* **state** (Page 1)
	+ A selection to indicate if the household member has any special needs or requirements. Please select either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **zip_code** (Page 1)
	+ The zip code of the household's location.
* **member_name** (Page 1)
	+ The name of the household member who completed the form.
* **birth_date** (Page 1)
	+ The birth date of the household member.
* **member_relationship** (Page 1)
	+ The relationship of the household member to the applicant.
* **family_member_1** (Page 1)
	+ The name of the first household member (if applicable).
* **family_member_2** (Page 1)
	+ The name of the second household member (if applicable).
* **family_member_3** (Page 1)
	+ The name of the third household member (if applicable).
* **family_member_4** (Page 1)
	+ The name of the fourth household member (if applicable).
* **family_member_5** (Page 1)
	+ The name of the fifth household member (if applicable).
* **number_of_children** (Page 1)
	+ A numerical value representing the number of children in the household.
* **age_children** (Page 1)
	+ A numerical value representing the age of the children in the household.
* **family_income** (Page 1)
	+ A numerical value representing the household income.
* **occupation** (Page 1)
	+ A description of the household member's occupation.
* **education_level** (Page 1)
	+ A selection to indicate the household member's education level. Please select one or more of the following options: High, Low.
* **employment_status** (Page 1)
	+ A selection to indicate the household member's employment status. Please select one or more of the following options: Active, Inactive.
* **household_income** (Page 1)
	+ A numerical value representing the household income.
* **how_did_you_hear_about_us** (Page 1)
	+ A selection to indicate how the household member heard about the museum. Please select one or more of the following options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **notes** (Page 1)
	+ A field for any additional comments or information about the household.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any section, contact the museum staff for assistance.
- The form will be reviewed by museum staff before approval.
