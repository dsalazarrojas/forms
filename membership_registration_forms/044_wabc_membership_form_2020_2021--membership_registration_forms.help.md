# Wabc Membership Form 2020 2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wabc Membership Form 2020 2021 is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to become members of the Wabc organization. This form is used to capture essential details about the applicant, including their contact information and membership preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select your preferred membership type in the "Membership type" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
6. Input your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field, if desired.
8. Select the duration of your membership in the "Membership duration" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Member First Name, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last name** (Member Last Name, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, required): Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Membership type** (Membership type, select_multiple, required): Select the type of membership that best suits your needs. Options include:
  * Boxing Membership
  * General Membership
  * Associate Membership
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of local-part1@domain.com.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Provide your physical address for any updates or news about Wabc events.
* **Join date** (Join date, date, required): Select the date you wish to join Wabc.
* **Membership duration** (Membership duration, select_one, required): Choose the duration of your membership:
  * 1 month
  * 3 months
  * 6 months
  * 12 months
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
* Select the membership type that best fits your needs and preferences.
* Choose a membership duration that aligns with your financial and time commitments.
