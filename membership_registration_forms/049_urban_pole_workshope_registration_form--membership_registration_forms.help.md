# urban_pole_workshope_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information for registering a workshop or class in an urban setting. This form will help the organization or community to track and manage workshop details for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title**, which should be a brief and descriptive title for the workshop or class.
2. Provide a detailed **Description** of the workshop or class, including its purpose, goals, and objectives.
3. Select the **Category** that best fits the workshop or class, helping the organization to group and organize similar events.
4. Enter the **Class Time**, indicating when the workshop or class will start and end.
5. Specify the **Class Days**, which is the number of days the workshop or class will take place.
6. List the **Class Participants**, including the number of people expected to attend and any specific requirements or restrictions.
7. Identify a **Buddy**, who will likely be a designated facilitator or leader for the class.
8. Select the **Poles will be provided by** option, indicating whether the organization will provide or the participants will bring their own poles.
9. Answer **Bring a friend with you**, specifying if a friend is allowed to join the class.
10. Enter the **Limit**, which might indicate a maximum number of participants or other restrictions.
11. Specify the **Tools**, which might include equipment or materials needed for the workshop or class.
12. Enter the **Classes will start at** and **Classes will end at** times, ensuring the schedule is accurate.
13. Enter the **Form ID**, which is a unique identifier for the form, likely used for internal tracking purposes.
14. Finally, select the **Assigned Tool**, which might be a specific tool or resource assigned to the workshop or class.
