# International Business Professionals Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Business Professionals Membership Form is a tool for collecting and managing membership information for International Business Professionals. This form helps to update and maintain accurate records of member details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's name in the "Organization Name" field.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Provide your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (must be a valid email address).
6. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
7. Select your country of origin in the "Country" field.
8. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
9. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Choose your membership type (Individual or Company) in the "Membership Type" field.
11. Enter the date you joined or renewed your membership in the "Membership Date" field.
12. Select your status (Active or Inactive) in the "Status" field.
13. If applicable, enter your renewal date in the "Renewal Date" field.
14. If applicable, enter your expiration date in the "Expiration Date" field.
15. Note the date and time you created the record in the "Created At" field.
16. Note the date and time the record was updated in the "Updated At" field.
17. Note the date and time the record was deleted (if deleted) in the "Deleted At" fields.
18. Enter the name of the person who created the record in the "Created By" field.
19. Enter the name of the person who updated the record in the "Updated By" field.
20. Enter the name of the person who deleted the record (if deleted) in the "Deleted By" field.
21. Enter any additional notes in the "Note" and "Note 2" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (1, text, required): Enter your organization's full name.
* **First Name** (2, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (3, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Job Title** (4, text, required): Enter your job title or profession.
* **Email** (5, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Company** (6, text): Enter your company's name.
* **Country** (7, text): Enter your country of origin.
* **Address** (8, text): Enter your address.
* **Phone** (9, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Membership Type** (10, select_multiple, required): Choose between Individual or Company membership.
* **Membership Date** (11, date, required): Enter the date you joined or renewed your membership.
* **Status** (12, select_one, required): Choose between Active or Inactive status.
* **Renewal Date** (13, date): Enter the date of your next renewal.
* **Expiration Date** (14, date): Enter your membership expiration date.
* **Created At** (15, time): Note the date and time you created the record.
* **Updated At** (16, time): Note the date and time the record was updated.
* **Deleted At** (17, time): Note the date and time the record was deleted (if deleted).
* **Created By** (18, text): Enter the name of the person who created the record.
* **Updated By** (19, text): Enter the name of the person who updated the record.
* **Deleted By** (20, text): Enter the name of the person who deleted the record (if deleted).
* **Note** (21, note): Enter any additional notes about your membership.
* **Note 2** (22, note): Enter any additional notes about your membership.
* **Membership Status Update Date** (23, date): Note the date of your membership status update.
* **Status Update By** (24, text): Enter the name of the person who updated your status.
* **Status Update Note** (25, note): Enter any additional notes about your membership status update.
