# Fitness Program New Member Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new members to register for our fitness program. The purpose of this form is to collect the necessary information to identify the member, understand their membership type, and establish emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with the following information:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email (must be valid)
	* Date of birth (in the format MM/DD/YY)
	* Phone number
	* Membership type (Personal or Corporate)
	* Emergency contact name
	* Emergency contact phone number
	* Relationship to emergency contact
	* Medical condition (if applicable)
2. If you have a medical condition, please select "I have a medical note available" under "Medical documentation" if you are bringing documentation with you. Otherwise, select "I will provide documentation at check-in".
3. Optionally, you can provide an emergency contact message, which will be passed on to the emergency contact in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to be contacted by us. Please make sure it is valid and formatted correctly.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Membership type**: Select either Personal or Corporate membership type.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Select your relationship to the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Medical condition**: If you have a medical condition, please enter it in the field provided.
* **Medical documentation**: If you have a medical note, please select "I have a medical note available". Otherwise, select "I will provide documentation at check-in".
* **Emergency contact message**: If you want to provide a message to be passed on to the emergency contact in case of an emergency, enter it here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a staff member for assistance.
* If you have any medical conditions, please let us know so we can provide necessary accommodations.
