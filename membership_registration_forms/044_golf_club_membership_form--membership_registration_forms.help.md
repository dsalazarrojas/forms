**Membership Registration Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The Golf Club Membership Form is designed to gather necessary information from individuals seeking to join the golf club.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form carefully** and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. **Complete each field accurately**. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
3. **Submit the form once completed**. Please do not submit duplicate forms for the same person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you are interested in becoming a member, 'No' otherwise.
* **Member Type** (`member_type`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all types of membership that interest you (e.g., 'Yes' for each type).
* **Address** (`address`, note, optional): Enter your address if different from your current location.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Membership Type Options**:
	+ If you select 'Yes' for Membership Type, you will be presented with additional membership options.
* **Member Type Options**:
	+ If you select multiple 'Yes' for Member Type, you will be presented with more information about each type.

Please take your time to review and complete this form accurately. If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to reach out to us. 

**Tips**

* Use the format mm/dd/yyyy for the date of birth.
* Ensure your email and phone number are up to date and accurate.
* Please review the membership type and member type selections carefully as they may have further implications for your membership. 

Note: The address field is a note field which means it's intended for free text input. You can enter your address if you have one, otherwise, it's optional. 

Please submit the form once you have reviewed and completed it accurately.
