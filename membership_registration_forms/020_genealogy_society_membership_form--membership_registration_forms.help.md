# Genealogy Society Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Genealogy Society Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential members who wish to join the Genealogy Society. This form will be used to determine the type of member you are applying to be and gather relevant details about your genealogy interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the first page of the form, which includes fields for First Name, Surname, Email, and Contact Phone Number.
2. Select your genealogy interest from the options provided on the second page.
3. Choose the type of member you wish to apply to be, selecting all applicable options from the dropdown list.
4. Once you have filled out all required fields, submit the form to complete your membership registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name, as it appears on official documents and records.
* **Surname**: Your surname, as it appears on official documents and records.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be contacted by the Genealogy Society.
* **Contact Phone Number**: A contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **What is your genealogy interest?**: Select one of the available options to describe your genealogy interests.
	+ Genealogy research: You are interested in conducting genealogical research.
	+ Genealogical education: You are interested in learning about genealogy and its practices.
	+ Genealogy research assistance: You require help with genealogical research.
	+ Other (please specify): You have a unique genealogy interest not listed above.
* **What type of member are you applying to be?**: Select all that apply from the available options:
	+ New Member: You are a new member applying for membership.
	+ Associate Member: You are an associate member, possibly with a student or junior membership.
	+ Regular Member: You are a regular member with full access to society resources.
	+ Institutional Member: You represent an institution that is interested in genealogy.
	+ Family Member: You are a family member of a current or past member.
* **Second Page**: This is the second page of the form, which is used to indicate that you have completed the first page.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure your membership application is complete and processed efficiently.
* If you have any difficulties or questions while filling out the form, feel free to contact the Genealogy Society for assistance.
* The Genealogy Society reserves the right to request further information or documents to support your membership application.
* Upon completion and submission of the form, a representative of the Genealogy Society will contact you to discuss your membership application and provide further instructions.
