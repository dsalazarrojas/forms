# Technology Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technology Club Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential members to create a membership profile. This form helps the club administrators to verify the membership application and ensure that the member's details are correct.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and contact information.
2. Provide a brief description of your interest in the Technology Club.
3. Check the boxes of the membership types you are interested in.
4. Upload any supporting documents or information as required.
5. Submit the form to complete your membership application.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the fields accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the Technology Club administrators.
* Do not share any sensitive information without the consent of the Technology Club.
* The form can be submitted multiple times, but each submission will be reviewed separately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
