# Coterie Membership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering as a member of the Coterie. Please complete this form to provide your information so we can process your membership application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Membership Type" from the provided options to indicate your status.
2. Enter your "Name" in the designated field.
3. Provide your "Email" address.
4. Enter your "Contact Number" (if required).
5. Enter your "Address" (if required).
6. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Type** (Membership Type, select one, required): Select the type of membership you want to register for:
	+ Membership
	+ Non-Member
	+ Volunteer
	+ Guest
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Enter your full name as you want it to appear in our records.
* **Email** (Email, text, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your address if required for your membership type.
* **Membership Registration Form** (Membership Registration Form, text, optional): This is the first page of the form, which should be automatically filled in by the system, but you need to review and confirm it.
