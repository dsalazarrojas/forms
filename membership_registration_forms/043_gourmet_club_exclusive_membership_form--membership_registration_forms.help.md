# Gourmet Club Exclusive Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gourmet Club Exclusive Membership Form is a registration form used by users to sign up for the Gourmet Club membership. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure you receive exclusive benefits and updates from the club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Phone number and Address in the text fields.
3. Enter your Zip Code in the numeric field.
4. Select your preferred Membership Type from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your culinary interests from the single-select menu.
6. Agree to the terms of the membership by typing "I agree" in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address, ensuring it is a valid email format (e.g., [username@example.com](mailto:username@example.com)).
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Address** (5, text, required): Enter your address, including your street, apartment number (if applicable), and any other relevant details.
* **Zip Code** (6, number, required): Enter your zip code in the format XXXXX.
* **Membership Type** (7, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple membership types from the dropdown menu, depending on your preferences:
	+ Basic Membership
	+ Premium Membership
	+ Lifetime Membership
* **Culinary Interests** (8, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following culinary interests:
	+ Fine Dining
	+ Foodie Culture
	+ Food Science
	+ Food Styling
	+ Food Criticism
	+ Food History
	+ Food Anthropology
* **Agreement** (9, text, required): Type "I agree" to accept the terms of the membership.
