# Zoo Family Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals and families to register for membership at the local zoo. It collects necessary information for membership application processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the membership application form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your desired membership package(s) in the **Package Selection** field.
2.  Enter the **Start Date** for your membership to begin on.
3.  Provide the number of members in your household in the **Household Members** field.
4.  Enter the contact person's name in the **Contact Person** field.
5.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
6.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
7.  (Optional) Provide any additional information about your household in the **Additional Information** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Package Selection
*   **Package Selection**: Choose one or multiple membership packages that best fit your needs. This includes selecting from available options:
    +   Membership Package 1
    +   Membership Package 2
    +   Membership Package 3

### Start Date
*   **Start Date**: Enter the date you wish to begin your membership on. This is a required field and will determine the start date of your membership.

### Household Members
*   **Household Members**: Enter the number of people residing in your household. This helps us understand the size of your household and provide you with the appropriate membership benefits.

### Contact Person
*   **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who will be the primary contact for your membership.

### Start Date 2
*   **Start Date 2**: This field is not explicitly explained in the YAML inputSchema, so it is assumed to be redundant with **Start Date**. It's likely meant to be another date field but is not used for the purpose of this form.

### Additional Information
*   **Additional Information**: This field is not required, but if you have any additional information about your household or any special requests, feel free to include it here. This can be helpful for us to better understand your needs.

### Email
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact and communication purposes.

### Phone Number
*   **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number for contact and communication purposes.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by zoo members and is used for membership application processing. The form is intended to be clear and simple, making it easy for users to complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact the zoo administration.
