# Sports Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Club Membership Form is designed to collect necessary information from users to manage their membership status and preferences within the sports club. This form helps the club administrators track and update member details accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Select your membership type (Individual or Family) in the "Membership Type" field.
8. Choose your preferred membership term from the options provided in the "Membership Term" field.
9. Select any special needs you may have in the "Special Needs" field.
10. Choose your membership status (Active or Inactive) in the "Membership Status" field.
11. Select your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer or Online Payment) in the "Payment Method" field.
12. Enter bank transfer details in the "Bank Transfer Details" field (if applicable).
13. Enter online payment details in the "Online Payment Details" field (if applicable).
14. Select your payment status (Paid or Unpaid) in the "Payment Status" field.
15. Enter the date you have been a member since in the "Member Since" field.
16. Choose your preferred payment plan (Monthly or Quarterly) in the "Payment Plan" field.
17. Enter any membership status notes in the "Membership Status Note" field (if applicable).
18. Enter any membership notes in the "Membership Notes" field (if applicable).
19. Enter payment notes in the "Payment Notes" field (if applicable).
20. Enter any special requests in the "Special Requests" field (if applicable).
21. Sign the "Member Agreement" field.
22. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
23. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Contact Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address that the club administrators can use to contact you.
*   **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for the club to reach you.
*   **Date Of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
*   **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your current address where you can be contacted or sent mail.
*   **Membership Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose whether you are an Individual or Family member.
*   **Membership Term** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your preferred membership term (6 months, 12 months, 24 months).
*   **Special Needs** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any special needs you may have (Disability, Language).
*   **Membership Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose whether you are Active or Inactive.
*   **Payment Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, Online Payment).
*   **Bank Transfer Details** (Note, Optional): Enter bank transfer details if you're paying by bank transfer.
*   **Online Payment Details** (Note, Optional): Enter online payment details if you're paying online.
*   **Payment Status** (Select One, Optional): Select your payment status (Paid, Unpaid).
*   **Member Since** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you have been a member since.
*   **Membership Expiration** (Date, Optional): Enter your membership expiration date.
*   **Payment Plan** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred payment plan (Monthly, Quarterly).
*   **Membership Status Note** (Note, Optional): Enter any membership status notes if necessary.
*   **Membership Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any membership notes if necessary.
*   **Payment Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any payment notes if necessary.
*   **Special Requests** (Note, Optional): Enter any special requests if necessary.
*   **Member Agreement** (Note, Required): Sign this to agree to the club's terms and conditions.
*   **Contact Person** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
