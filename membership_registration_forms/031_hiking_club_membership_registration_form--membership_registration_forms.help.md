# hiking_club_membership_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "hiking_club_membership_registration_form" is used to register new members with the hiking club. It is a crucial step in maintaining an up-to-date membership list for the club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your **Email Address** in the "email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "phone" field.
5. Select your **Membership Type** from the options: **Individual**, **Group**, or **Family**.
6. If applicable, select the **Membership Start Date** and **Membership End Date** for your membership period.
7. Enter any additional **Notes** regarding your membership or application.
8. Choose your **Category** of membership (e.g., "Membership Registration Forms").
9. Provide a **Description** of your membership or application (optional).
10. If assigned, enter the **Assigned Tool** for your membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (text, optional)
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name (text, optional)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email (email, optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for club communication.

### Phone (text, optional)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for club communication.

### Membership Type (select_one, optional)
* **Membership Type**: Select your type of membership: **Individual**, **Group**, or **Family**.

### Membership Start Date (date, optional)
* **Membership Start Date**: Enter the date your membership started.

### Membership End Date (date, optional)
* **Membership End Date**: Enter the date your membership will end (if applicable).

### Notes (note, optional)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your membership or application.

### Category (select_multiple, optional)
* **Category**: Select your category of membership (e.g., "Membership Registration Forms").

### Description (text, optional)
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your membership or application.

### Assigned Tool (text, optional)
* **Assigned Tool**: If assigned, enter the tool or equipment assigned to you for your membership.
