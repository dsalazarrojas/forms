# HR Professionals Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for HR professionals to register or edit their membership details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with your correct information.
- Ensure you're using the correct format for the membership start and end dates.
- If you're unsure about your assigned tool, you can select "Other" and enter your preferred tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Job Title** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your current job title or role.
* **Company** (6, text, required/optional): Enter your current or past employer's name.
* **Membership Plan** (7, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from Basic, Premium, or Enterprise plans.
* **Membership Start Date** (8, date, required/optional): Enter the date you started your membership in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Membership End Date** (9, date, required/optional): Enter the date your membership will end in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Created At** (10, time, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.
* **Updated At** (11, time, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.
* **Description** (12, note, required/optional): Enter any additional details about your membership.
* **Assigned Tool** (13, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred tool or select "Other" if you don't have a preferred tool.
