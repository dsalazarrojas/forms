# hybrid_team_membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the membership application form for new team members. Use this form to submit your application to join the hybrid team.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you're eligible to join the team and have read the team's guidelines. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and clear information about yourself.
2. Be honest and truthful in your answers.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Team Member Application**: This is the title of the form. Please enter your application to join the team.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter your primary email address where you can be reached for team communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number where you can be reached for team communication.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title or role.
* **Experience**: Enter your years of experience in the relevant field. This can include work experience, internship, or volunteer work.
* **Skills**: Select one or more relevant skills you possess from the dropdown menu. This includes your expertise in areas like software development, team leadership, or management.
* **Availability**: Select one or more options that describe your availability for work.
* **Preferred Work Schedule**: Select a time format that suits your schedule.
* **Preferred Work Location**: Select the type of work location you prefer from the dropdown menu.
* **Availability Why**: Write a brief note explaining why you're available to work.
* **Work Experience**: Select one or more relevant work experience from the dropdown menu.
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Select the method you prefer to communicate with the team.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of someone who can contact your emergency contact in case of emergencies.
* **Submit**: Select the "Submit" option to complete your application.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Double-check your answers before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the team lead for clarification.
* If you're not comfortable with any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
