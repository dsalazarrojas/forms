# Brewery Exclusive Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brewery Exclusive Membership Form is designed to gather information from potential members to facilitate a personalized experience tailored to their interests and preferences. By filling out this form, you'll be able to join our exclusive membership program and enjoy benefits such as exclusive beer releases, member discounts, and early access to events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Date of Birth, Membership Tier Selection, Membership Cost, Beer Interests, Expected Visit Frequency, Benefits You Are Most Interested In, and Payment Method).
2. Select your preferred membership tier, payment method, and frequency of visits.
3. Choose your beer interests, event participation, and event types of interest.
4. Provide your address and additional information if you wish to receive our newsletter or have special requests.
5. Finally, confirm you agree to the membership terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification and rewards purposes.
* **Membership Tier Selection** (6, select_one, required): Choose your preferred membership level from Bronze to Platinum.
* **Membership Cost** (7, select_one, required): Select your annual membership fee.
* **Beer Interests** (8, select_multiple, required): Select up to three interests from craft beers, IPAs, stouts and porters, lagers, wheat beers, sours, experimental brews, or beer education.
* **Expected Visit Frequency** (9, select_one, required): Select how often you plan to visit the brewery.
* **Benefits You Are Most Interested In** (11, select_multiple, required): Select up to three benefits from exclusive beer releases, member discounts, early access to events, exclusive events, complimentary tastings, VIP seating, merchandise discounts, or birthday specials.
* **Event Participation Interest** (12, select_one, not required): Select whether you're interested in event notifications, and if so, specify whether it's for all events or selected events.
* **Event Types of Interest** (13, select_multiple, not required): Select up to three event types from tastings, brewery tours, beer education, live music, trivia nights, food pairings, seasonal celebrations, or member-only events.
* **Home Address** (14, text, not required): Provide your residential address if you wish to receive mailings.
* **City** (15, text, not required): Enter your city.
* **Zip Code** (16, text, not required): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Occupation** (17, text, not required): Enter your profession or job.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (18, select_one, not required): Select how you discovered the brewery.
* **Payment Method** (19, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **I agree to the membership terms and conditions** (20, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the membership terms and conditions.
* **Would you like to receive our newsletter** (21, select_one, not required): Select whether you wish to receive our newsletter.
* **Special Requests or Comments** (22, text, not required): Enter any additional information or special requests.
