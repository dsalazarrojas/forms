# Returning User Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for existing users to update their information or change their status and roles within the system.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure you have access to the system and are logged in as an administrator or have the necessary permissions.
2.  Click on the "Returning User Registration" form to start the registration process.
3.  Fill out the required and optional fields as needed:
    *   `existing_user`: Select "Yes" if you are an existing user.
    *   `contact_info`: Provide your contact information (name, email, phone, address).
    *   `email`: Enter your email address.
    *   `phone`: Enter your phone number.
    *   `date_of_birth`: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
    *   `timezone`: Select your time zone.
    *   `note`: Provide any additional notes or comments.
    *   `user_status`: Select your desired status (Active, Inactive, Suspended).
    *   `existing_user_status`: Select your existing status (New, Existing).
    *   `user_role`: Select your desired role (Administrator, Editor, Member).
    *   `existing_user_role`: Select your existing role (Administrator, Editor, Member).
4.  Review the form for any errors or warnings before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **existing_user**: This field is used to identify if you already exist in the system. Select "Yes" to confirm.
*   **contact_info**: Please fill in your contact information (name, email, phone, address).
*   **email**: Enter your email address.
*   **phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **timezone**: Select your time zone.
*   **note**: Add any additional notes or comments.
*   **user_status**: Select your desired status (Active, Inactive, Suspended).
*   **existing_user_status**: Select your existing status (New, Existing).
*   **user_role**: Choose your desired role (Administrator, Editor, Member).
*   **existing_user_role**: Select your existing role (Administrator, Editor, Member).
