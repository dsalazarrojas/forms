# Cricket Club Member Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cricket Club Member Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to become members of the Cricket Club. This form helps to gather essential details about each prospective member, including their name, contact information, membership type, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective text fields.
2. Provide your **Email** address to allow us to communicate with you regarding club events and updates.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**, which will help us contact you in case of important announcements or membership changes.
4. Select your **Membership Type** from the options provided:
	* **Cricket Club Member**: This is the standard membership option.
	* **Associate Member**: For individuals who want to support the club without being a full member.
	* **Sponsor**: For individuals who wish to sponsor a member or contribute to the club.
5. Enter your **Address** to allow us to send you updates and information about the club.
6. Provide your **Date of Birth** to help us verify your identity.
7. Enter the **Date of Registration** to track when you submitted your membership application.
8. Choose a **Membership Start Date** for your membership to begin.
9. Select your **Membership Status**:
	* **Active**: You are an active member of the club.
	* **Inactive**: You are not currently an active member but may be reinstated in the future.
	* **Pending**: Your membership application is being reviewed or processed.
10. Enter any **Notes** about your membership or any special circumstances that we should be aware of.
11. If applicable, select your **Payment Method** and enter the payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name (e.g., John). (<name>)
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname (e.g., Doe). (<name>)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you. (Email)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact. (Phone Number)
* **Membership Type**: Select the type of membership that suits you best. (Membership Type)
* **Address**: Enter your full address for membership updates and communications. (Address)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify your identity. (Date of Birth)
* **Date of Registration**: Enter the date you submitted this form. (Date of Registration)
* **Membership Start Date**: Choose the date when your membership is to begin. (Membership Start Date)
* **Membership Status**: Select your current membership status. (Membership Status)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your membership or circumstances. (Notes)
* **Payment Method**: If applicable, select your payment method and enter the payment details. (Payment Method)

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with your membership.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* The form will guide you through the necessary fields to complete your membership registration.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are completed.
* We will process your membership application as soon as possible after receiving your submission.
