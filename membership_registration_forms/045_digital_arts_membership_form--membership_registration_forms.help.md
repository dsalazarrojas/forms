# Digital Arts Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Arts Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential members and guide them through the process of joining the Digital Arts membership program. This form is used to assess eligibility and facilitate a smooth membership application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your Phone number for us to contact you.
4. Select your preferred Membership Type (Arts Member or Patron Member).
5. If you are an artist, write a brief Artist Statement (up to 1000 words).
6. Choose how many Art Form Submissions you have made.
7. If you have submitted art, select the date you submitted it.
8. Provide the details of your Emergency Contact (name, phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (First name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address for us to communicate with you regarding your membership application.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Membership Type** (Membership Type, select_one, optional): Select whether you would like to be an Arts Member or a Patron Member.
	+ Arts Member: Suitable for artists who actively participate in the Digital Arts community.
	+ Patron Member: Suitable for individuals who support the Digital Arts mission but are not artists themselves.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for demographic purposes.
* **Membership Type** (Membership Type, select_one, optional): Select your preferred membership type.
* **Artist Statement** (Artist Statement, text, optional): Write a brief statement about your artistic interests, skills, and goals.
* **Artist Statement Length** (Artist Statement Length, select_one, optional): Choose how long your artist statement is.
	+ 1-1000 words: Maximum length limit.
* **Art Form Submissions** (Art Form Submissions, select_multiple, optional): Select how many art forms you have submitted.
* **Join Date** (Join Date, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your art form if you have made any.
* **Art Form Submissions** (Art Form Submissions, select_multiple, optional): Select how many art forms you have submitted.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, optional): Provide the name of an emergency contact.
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
