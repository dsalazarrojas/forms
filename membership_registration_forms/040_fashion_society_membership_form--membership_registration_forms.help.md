# Fashion Society Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect membership information from users who want to join the Fashion Society. Please fill out this form to become a part of our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have filled out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address that you use for communication.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for membership-related activities.
 
**Tips**
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, please contact us for assistance.
* You can only submit this form once; duplicate submissions will not be processed.
