# Colorado Mountain Club Trekking Registration Form WorldPayUS Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Colorado Mountain Club trekking registration form is designed to collect necessary information from participants for the trekking events. This form ensures that participants provide accurate and complete information, which the club organizers use to confirm and follow up on participant details and provide event information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Member name** field with your full name.
2. Enter your **Email address** in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3. Provide your **Phone number** as an emergency contact.
4. Select the **Trek selected** from the provided options.
5. Enter the **Start date** of the trek in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
6. If you have any **Medical conditions**, please describe them in the text field.
7. Provide the **Emergency contact name and phone number** for your emergency contacts.
8. Enter the **Payment reference** from WorldPayUS.
9. Read and agree to the **Waiver agreement** by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Member name**: A field where you'll enter your full name for the registration purposes.
- **Email address**: Enter your email address for event confirmations and updates.
- **Phone number**: A field to provide an emergency contact phone number for you.
- **Trek selected**: Select the trekking route or event you're registering for.
- **Start date**: Enter the start date of the trek in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Medical conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, describe them here.
- **Emergency contact**: Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Payment reference**: Enter the WorldPayUS transaction reference for payment purposes.
- **Waiver agreement**: Select "I agree" to agree to the club's terms and waiver.
