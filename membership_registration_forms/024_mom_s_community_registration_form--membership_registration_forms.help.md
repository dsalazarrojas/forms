# Moms_Community_Registration_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moms_Community_Registration_Form is designed to collect demographic information about the mother and her children for registration in the community. This form is intended to be completed by the mother herself.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Signature.
2. Provide dates of birth for each child, including the mother's date of birth.
3. Select the appropriate gender for each child.
4. Choose the number of children and enter their names in the corresponding fields.
5. Review and agree to the Terms of Use.
6. Sign the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the mother. 
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the mother.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the mother.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the mother.
* **Birth Date**: Enter the date of birth of the mother.
* **Child Birth Date**: Enter the date of birth of each child, in the corresponding field.
* **Child 1 Name**: Enter the name of the first child. 
* **Child 1 Gender**: Select the gender of the first child.
* **Child 1 Birth Date**: Enter the date of birth of the first child.
* **Children**: Select how many children you have.
* **Child Count**: Enter the number of children you have.
* **Child 2 Name**: Enter the name of the second child. 
* **Child 2 Gender**: Select the gender of the second child.
* **Child 2 Birth Date**: Enter the date of birth of the second child.
* **Child 3 Name**: Enter the name of the third child. 
* **Child 3 Gender**: Select the gender of the third child.
* **Child 3 Birth Date**: Enter the date of birth of the third child.
* **Child 4 Name**: Enter the name of the fourth child. 
* **Child 4 Gender**: Select the gender of the fourth child.
* **Child 4 Birth Date**: Enter the date of birth of the fourth child.
* **Additional Information**: Add any additional information about the children.
* **Agreement**: Select I agree to the Terms of Use.
* **Terms of Use**: Review and agree to the Terms of Use.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature of the mother.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the form completion.

Note: Fields with no label or weak label are explained as best-effort based on the provided YAML. 

Please review and agree to the Terms of Use before signing the form. The community reserves the right to use the information collected on this form for its purposes, and the mother understands and agrees to these terms.
