# Clan Membership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from prospective clan members to help us better understand their background, experience, and expectations. It will be used to evaluate your suitability for membership in our clan and to ensure a smooth integration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field carefully and accurately. You can navigate through the form by clicking on the "Next" button at the bottom of each page. When you're finished, click "Submit" to send us your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Gamer Information
* **Gamer Tag or Username**: Please enter your primary gaming username.
* **Real Name**: Enter your real name for verification purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for clan communications.
* **Discord Username**: Enter your Discord username for clan chat and coordination.
* **Age**: You must be 18 or older to join the clan. Enter your age.
* **Location**: Enter your city and country.
* **Time Zone**: Select your local time zone.
* **Primary Gaming Platform**: Select the platform you mainly play on.
* **Years of Gaming Experience**: Indicate how many years you have been gaming.
* **Competitive Experience**: Indicate your level of competitive gaming experience.
* **Preferred Games**: Select all games you regularly play.
* **Current Rank or Level**: Enter your current rank or level (optional).
* **Preferred Role or Position**: Enter your preferred role or position in team games (optional).
* **Gaming Availability**: Select when you are usually online.
* **Hours Per Week Available for Clan Activities**: Select your estimated gaming time per week.

### Page 2: Clan Integration
* **Do You Use Voice Chat**: Indicate your preference for voice chat.
* **Why Do You Want to Join Our Clan**: Tell us about yourself and why you want to join our clan.
* **What Skills Can You Bring to the Clan**: Describe your skills that can be beneficial to the clan (optional).
* **Have You Been in a Clan Before**: Indicate if you have been in a clan before.
* **Previous Clan Name**: Enter the name of your previous clan (if applicable).
* **Why Did You Leave**: Briefly explain why you left your previous clan (if applicable).

### Final Steps
* **Are You Interested in Tournaments**: Indicate your interest in competitive clan events.
* **Do You Agree to Follow Clan Rules**: Confirm that you agree to follow the clan's code of conduct and guidelines.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Share anything else you'd like to tell us about yourself or ask us.
* **Submit**: Click "Submit" to send us your application.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact us through our Discord server or email.
* We will review your application and get back to you soon.
