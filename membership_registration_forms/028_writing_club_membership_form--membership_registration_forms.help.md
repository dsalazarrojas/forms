# writing_club_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Writing Club membership form is a crucial step in joining our community of writers. It helps us understand who you are, what kind of writer you are, and tailor our support and resources to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
### First Name
*   *First Name* (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
*   *Last Name* (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.

### Email
*   *Email* (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.

### Birth Date
*   *Birth Date* (`birth_date`, `date`, required: false): If you want to share your birth date, do so. This information will help us understand when you were born. If you don't feel comfortable sharing your birth date, that's fine too. You can skip this field.

### Phone Number
*   *Phone* (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): If you have a phone number, please fill it in.

### Writing Interests
*   *Writing Interests* (`writing_interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options from the list: Fiction, Non-Fiction, Poetry, Scriptwriting, Copywriting. This will help us understand what type of writing you enjoy.

### Membership Type
*   *Membership Type* (`membership_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of membership you are interested in: Basic, Premium.

### Bio
*   *Bio* (`bio`, `note`, required: false): If you want to share a short bio about yourself, feel free to do so. This will help other writers get to know you better.

### About Us
*   *About Us* (`about_us`, `text`, required: false): You don't need to fill this out, but if you're curious about the Writing Club, you can learn more about us here.

### Terms and Conditions
*   *Terms and Conditions* (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required: false): Take a moment to read through our Terms and Conditions before submitting the form.

### Submit
*   *Submit* (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Once you've filled out all the fields, click submit to complete the membership registration process.

## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form. This will help us provide you with the right resources and support.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at [your email address].
*   If you're unsure about the type of membership to choose, take a moment to think about it and come back when you're ready.
*   Remember to read the Terms and Conditions carefully before submitting the form.
