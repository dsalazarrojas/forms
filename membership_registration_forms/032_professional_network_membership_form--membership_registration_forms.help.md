# Professional Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for professional network membership registration. It is a collection of fields that gather personal and professional data for membership purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Indicate your Nationality in the "Nationality" field.
7. Choose your Occupation in the "Occupation" field.
8. Select your Industry in the "Industry" field.
9. Enter your Years of Experience in the "Years of Experience" field.
10. Upload or describe your Profile Picture in the "Profile Picture" field.
11. Choose your Membership Type in the "Membership Type" field.
12. Select your Membership Plan in the "Membership Plan" field.
13. Select your Subscription Period in the "Subscription Period" field.
14. Choose your Member Status in the "Member Status" field.
15. Select your Payment Method in the "Payment Method" field.
16. Enter your Payment Date in the "Payment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, select_one, required/optional): Select your nationality for identity purposes.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your occupation for professional purposes.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your industry for professional purposes.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required/optional): Enter your years of experience for professional evaluation.
* **Profile Picture** (`profile_picture`, note, required/optional): Upload or describe your profile picture for visual representation.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your membership type for access privileges.
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your membership plan for subscription purposes.
* **Subscription Period** (`subscription_period`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your subscription period for renewal purposes.
* **Member Status** (`member_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your member status for activity purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your payment method for financial purposes.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your payment date for accounting purposes.
