# Regional Officer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register regional officers for [Your Organization's] regional teams. It collects necessary contact information and agreements from individuals interested in becoming a regional officer.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Registration Details**: Provide your basic information such as First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. **Region and Role**: Select the region and role you are applying for.
3. **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, including a contact person and region name.
4. **Agreements**: Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of becoming a regional officer. You will need to agree to multiple terms for each of the 12 agreements listed.
5. **Final Submission**: Once you've filled out all the necessary fields, click submit to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address. It will be used for communication purposes.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required): Enter your contact person's name.
* **Region** (`region`, optional): Select the region you are applying for.
* **Region Role** (`region_role`, required): Select the role you are applying for (Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Member).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Region Assignment Date** (`region_assignment_date`, required): Enter the date you will be assigned to the region.
* **Region Name** (`region_name`, required): Enter the name of the region you are applying for.
* **Agreement 1-12** (`agreement1`, required, `agreement2`, required, `agreement3`, required, `agreement4`, required, `agreement5`, required, `agreement6`, required, `agreement7`, required, `agreement8`, required, `agreement9`, required, `agreement10`, required, `agreement11`, required, `agreement12`, required): Confirm your agreement to each of the 12 listed terms and conditions.
