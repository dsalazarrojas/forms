# Dance Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about potential members interested in joining the dance club.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with your details and submit it to the club administrators.
2.  Ensure that you fill in all the required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, contact the club administrators for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **name** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Fill in your name to help us know who you are.
*   **email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
*   **phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Fill in your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX-XXX-XXXXX`.
*   **start date** (`date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you plan to start dancing with us. Please use the `mm/dd` format (e.g., `01/10/2024`).
*   **end time** (`time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time you plan to end dancing with us. Please use the `HH:MM` format (e.g., `10:00 PM`).
*   **membership type** (`membership_type`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Choose how you plan to be involved with the club. Select all that apply:
    +   **Yes**
    +   **No**
*   **membership level** (`membership_level`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your preferred membership level:
    +   **High**
    +   **Low**
*   **comments** (`comments`, `note`, **required**): Share any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   If you need to update your information, please contact the club administrators for assistance.
*   We appreciate your feedback and suggestions on how to improve the form.
