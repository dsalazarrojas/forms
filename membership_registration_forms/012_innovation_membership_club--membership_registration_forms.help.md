# innovation_membership_club - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "innovation_membership_club" form is used to register individuals interested in joining an innovation-focused club or community. This form collects basic information to facilitate membership registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to the first question, "membership_club," by choosing either "Yes" or "No."
2. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your date and time of birth in the "date_of_birth" and "time_of_birth" fields, respectively.
6. If you have any additional information or notes, type them in the "note" field.
7. Once you've completed all the fields, click "submit" to submit your membership registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **membership_club** (`membership_club`, select_one, not required): This is the first question on the form. Choose "Yes" if you're interested in joining the club, or "No" if you're not.
* **name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your full name as you'd like it to be displayed in the club.
* **email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address to facilitate communication with the club.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **time_of_birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, not required): Enter your time of birth in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **note** (`note`, note, not required): If you have any additional information you'd like to share, type it here.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click to submit your membership registration for review.
