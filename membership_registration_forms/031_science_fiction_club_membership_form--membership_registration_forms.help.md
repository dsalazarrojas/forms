# Science Fiction Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the Science Fiction Club. It will help us get to know you better and ensure a smooth membership experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date Of Birth field.
4. Select your Club Type from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred Membership Type from the dropdown menu.
6. If you'd like to share any additional information, use the Message field.
7. Review your answers carefully and submit the form once you're done.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* Label: First Name
* Type: text
* Required: False
* Explanation: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* Label: Last Name
* Type: text
* Required: True
* Explanation: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* Label: Email
* Type: email
* Required: True
* Explanation: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.

### Date Of Birth
* Label: Date Of Birth
* Type: date
* Required: True
* Explanation: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Club Type
* Label: Club Type
* Type: select_one
* Required: True
* Explanation: Choose the category that best describes your affiliation with the club:
	+ Science Fiction Club
	+ School Group
	+ Library
	+ Other

### Address
* Label: Address
* Type: text
* Required: False
* Explanation: Enter your address if you'd like to receive mail from us or if you have any other contact information you'd like to share.

### Membership Type
* Label: Membership Type
* Type: select_multiple
* Required: True
* Explanation: Choose the type of membership you're interested in:
	+ Regular
	+ Premium
	+ Free

### Message
* Label: Message
* Type: text
* Required: False
* Explanation: If you have any additional comments or questions, please share them with us here.
