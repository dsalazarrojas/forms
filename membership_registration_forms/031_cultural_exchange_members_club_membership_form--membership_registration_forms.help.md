# Cultural Exchange Members Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Exchange Members Club Membership Form is a registration form for new members to sign up with the club. It collects essential information about the member, including name, contact details, and membership status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter your Address in the Address field.
5. Select your Country of residence in the Country field.
6. Enter your Organization (if applicable) in the Organization field.
7. Choose your Role in the Role field (either Member or Sponsor).
8. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
9. If you have been a member before, enter the date you first joined in the Member Since field.
10. Select your current Member Status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
11. Provide any Additional Information about yourself in the Note field.
12. Read and agree to the Agreement and Terms of membership.
13. Confirm your agreement to the terms by selecting Agree.
14. Finally, select how you would like to proceed: Submit, Save and Continue, Save and Edit, or Cancel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: `false`): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: `false`): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`id: 3`, `type: email`, required: `false`): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number in the format `(+X) X.X.XX.XX`.
* **Address** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: `false`): Enter your current address.
* **Country** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: `false`): Select your country of residence.
* **Organization** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: `false`): Enter your organization (if applicable).
* **Role** (`id: 8`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): Select your role as Member or Sponsor.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 9`, `type: date`, required: `false`): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Member Since** (`id: 10`, `type: date`, required: `false`): If you have been a member before, enter the date you first joined.
* **Member Status** (`id: 11`, `type: select_multiple`, required: `true`): Select your current member status as Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Additional Information** (`id: 12`, `type: note`, required: `false`): Add any additional information about yourself.
* **Agreement and Terms** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required: `false`): Read and agree to the terms of membership.
* **Confirm Agreement and Terms** (`id: 14`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): Confirm your agreement to the terms by selecting Agree.
* **Submit** (`id: 15`, `type: select_one`, required: `true`): Select Submit to complete your membership registration.
* **Save and Continue** (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, required: `false`): Select Save and Continue to save your progress and continue later.
* **Save and Edit** (`id: 17`, `type: select_one`, required: `false`): Select Save and Edit to save your progress but not submit the form.
* **Save and Exit** (`id: 18`, `type: select_one`, required: `false`): Select Save and Exit to save your progress and cancel your membership registration.
* **Cancel** (`id: 19`, `type: select_one`, required: `false`): Select Cancel to cancel your membership registration.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure successful submission.
* Review your answers before confirming agreement to the terms.
* If you encounter any issues, please contact the club administrator for assistance.
