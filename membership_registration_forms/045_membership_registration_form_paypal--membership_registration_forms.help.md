# Membership Registration Form PayPal - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in registering for a PayPal membership program. It is not intended for other purposes such as payment processing or transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
* Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
* Select the correct options for professional or student association.
* Review your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **membership_registration_form_paypal** (`membership_registration_form_paypal`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is currently set to "Membership Registration Form PayPal". Please do not fill in this field as it is for internal use.
* **membership_name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **membership_email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that you use for communication. This will be used as a contact point.
* **membership_phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **membership_address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your mailing address.
* **membership_occupational_affiliations** (`occupational_affiliations`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant work or professional affiliations.
* **membership_special_skills** (`special_skills`, text, required/optional): Enter any special skills you possess.
* **membership_professional_associate** (`professional_associate`, select_one, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate your professional association status (Option 1, Option 2). If this field is not relevant, please leave blank.
* **membership_student_associate** (`student_associate`, select_one, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate your student association status (Option 1, Option 2). If this field is not relevant, please leave blank.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
* Select the correct options for your professional or student association status.
* Double-check your information before submission.
