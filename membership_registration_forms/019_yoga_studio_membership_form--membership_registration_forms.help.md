# yoga_studio_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The yoga studio membership form is designed to collect information from prospective members to create a new membership account. This form is used to gather essential details such as name, contact information, payment method, and billing address to facilitate the membership process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the membership form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields with your personal details.
2. Provide your **Phone** number (if applicable).
3. Select your **Membership Type**: choose from Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual.
4. Choose your **Payment Method**: select from Credit Card or Bank Transfer.
5. Set your **Payment Schedule**: select a date for your payment to be processed.
6. Set your **Payment Frequency**: select Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
7. Enter your **Payment Amount**.
8. Fill in your **Address**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip** for your address.
9. Enter your **Country** where you live.
10. Provide your **Date of Birth**.
11. Set your **Membership Start Date**.
12. Set your **Membership End Date**.
13. Select your **Payment Status**: choose from Active or Inactive.
14. Select your **Membership Status**: choose from Active, Pending, or Cancelled.
15. If applicable, add any **Notes** about your membership.
16. If applicable, add any **Payment Notes** about your payment method.
17. Fill in your **Billing Address**, **Billing City**, **Billing State**, and **Billing Zip**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it should be displayed on your membership account.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it should be displayed on your membership account.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for any updates or notifications.
* **Membership Type** (Required): Choose from Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual to select your membership duration.
* **Payment Method** (Required): Select from Credit Card or Bank Transfer to choose your payment method.
* **Payment Schedule** (Required): Select a date for your payment to be processed.
* **Payment Frequency** (Required): Select Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually to choose when you want to be charged.
* **Payment Amount** (Required): Enter the amount you are willing to pay for your membership.
* **Address** (Required): Enter your address for your membership account.
* **City** (Optional): Enter your city for your address.
* **State** (Required): Enter your state for your address.
* **Zip** (Required): Enter your zip code for your address.
* **Country** (Required): Enter your country where you live.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Membership Start Date** (Required): Select the date your membership starts.
* **Membership End Date** (Required): Select the date your membership ends.
* **Payment Status** (Required): Choose from Active or Inactive to indicate your payment status.
* **Membership Status** (Required): Choose from Active, Pending, or Cancelled to indicate your membership status.
* **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional notes about your membership.
* **Payment Notes** (Optional): Add any additional notes about your payment method.
* **Billing Address** (Required): Enter your billing address for payment purposes.
* **Billing City** (Required): Enter your city for your billing address.
* **Billing State** (Required): Enter your state for your billing address.
* **Billing Zip** (Required): Enter your zip code for your billing address.
