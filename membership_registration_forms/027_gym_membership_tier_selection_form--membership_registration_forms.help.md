# Gym Membership Tier Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to select the most suitable gym membership tier for individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the first page labeled "Member Information" to provide your name and any other relevant details.
2. On the second page, labeled "Membership Information", select the membership tier that best suits your needs.
3. Finally, review the "Acknowledgement Information" page for any important details or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Information (Member Information)** (`member_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details to help us understand your needs.
* **Membership Information (Membership Information)** (`membership_info`, text, required): Select the membership tier that best suits your needs.
* **Acknowledgement Information (Acknowledgement Information)** (`acknowledgement_info`, text, required): Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions of your membership.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately to get the right membership tier.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- This form is for general membership registration and may not cover all possible membership types.
