# Afms Ex Jam Alumni Member Database - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afms Ex Jam Alumni Member Database form is designed to collect and store personal and professional information of alumni members from the AFMS community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in the personal and professional fields as instructed.
3. Submit the form after verifying that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete legal name as per your identification documents.
* **Preferred Name** (`preferred_name`, `text`, optional): If different from your legal name, provide the name you prefer to be called.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender identity.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address for alumni communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **AFMS Member ID** (`afms_member_id`, `text`, optional): Your AFMS member identification number, if available.
* **EX-JAM Group** (`ex_jam_group`, `text`, required): Select the EX-JAM group you were part of.
* **Years of Membership** (`membership_years`, `text`, required): Specify the years you were a member of AFMS.
* **Highest Rank Attained** (`rank_attained`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify the highest rank you achieved in AFMS.
* **Positions Held** (`positions_held`, `text`, optional): List any leadership positions you held in AFMS.
* **Achievements and Recognitions** (`achievements_recognitions`, `text`, optional): Describe any notable achievements or recognitions received in AFMS.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City, State, Country** (`city_state_country`, `text`, required): Enter the city, state, and country of your current address.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter the postal or zip code of your current address.
* **Alternate Email** (`alternate_email`, `email`, optional): Enter an alternate email address for backup contact.
* **Current Occupation** (`current_occupation`, `text`, required): Specify your current job title or profession.
* **Employer or Organization** (`employer_organization`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide the name of your current employer or organization.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry sector you work in.
* **Professional Specialization** (`professional_specialization`, `text`, optional): Describe your area of professional specialization.
* **Alma Mater** (`alma_mater`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide the name of your school of graduation.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the year of graduation from your school.
* **Interest in Alumni Events** (`interest_in_alumni_events`, `select_one`, required): Select if you are interested in attending alumni events.
* **Preferred Communication Frequency** (`preferred_communication_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you would like to receive alumni communications.
* **Interest in Volunteer Opportunities** (`volunteer_opportunities_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select if you are interested in volunteering for alumni activities.
* **Areas of Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of volunteer activities that interest you.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Electronic Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign to confirm your alumni registration.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
* If you encounter any issues while filling the form, contact the relevant authorities for support.
