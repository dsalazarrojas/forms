# Church Choir Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Choir Membership Registration Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in joining the church choir. This form helps us to manage the membership process, keep our records up-to-date, and ensure that we can communicate with our members effectively. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to enable us to provide the best experience for you and our choir community.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by providing your member name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your usual vocal part or indicate if you're unsure.
3. Enter your years of singing experience (if applicable).
4. Specify your weekly availability for rehearsals.
5. If you have any health considerations that may affect your participation, please let us know.
6. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
7. Confirm your consent to be photographed for choir promotions.
8. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member name** (1): Please enter your full name for our records.
* **Email address** (2): Enter your valid email address so we can send you rehearsal reminders and notifications.
* **Phone number** (3): Provide your contact number for urgent notices or messages.
* **Vocal part** (4): Select your usual singing part from the options provided or indicate if you're unsure.
* **Years of singing experience** (5): If you have experience, please estimate the number of years you've been singing. If you're new to singing, just leave this blank.
* **Weekly availability for rehearsals** (6): Specify the days and times you're available to participate in rehearsals.
* **Health considerations** (7): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, please let us know so we can accommodate your needs.
* **Emergency contact** (8): Provide the name and phone number of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Agree to photo release** (9): Confirm your consent to be photographed for choir promotions.
* **Additional notes** (10): Share any other relevant information about yourself or your singing experience.
