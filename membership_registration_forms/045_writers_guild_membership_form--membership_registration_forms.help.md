# Writers' Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Writers' Guild Membership Form is used to register writers for membership in the Writers' Guild. It's a necessary step for writers to become part of the Guild and access its benefits and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether the writer has information they'd like to provide (Writer Information field).
2. Enter the writer's name.
3. Enter the writer's contact email.
4. Enter the writer's phone number.
5. Select whether the writer has writing experience.
6. Enter the writer's interests.
7. Select whether the writer has a resume.
8. Write a note about the writer's workshop.
9. Select the type of membership the writer is interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Writer Information (Select)**: Choose whether you have information to provide about your writing experience, interests, or other details.
	+ Select "Yes" if you have information, "No" otherwise.
* **name**: Enter your name as you'd like it to be listed in the Writers' Guild directory.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted about Guild events and updates.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **writer_experience (Select)**: Choose whether you have writing experience or not.
	+ Select "Yes" if you have experience, "No" otherwise.
* **writer_interests**: List your writing interests or genres.
* **writer_resume (Select)**: Choose whether you have a resume or not.
	+ Select "Yes" if you have a resume, "No" otherwise.
* **workshopp (Note)**: If you have a workshop or writing group, provide details about it here.
* **membership_type (Select)**: Choose the type of membership you're interested in.
	+ Select the type that best fits your needs and goals.
