# Sustainable Learning Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new members into the Sustainable Learning Community, gathering information about their interests, contact details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Form field with your thoughts on joining the community.
2. Provide your Member Details, including your name and contact information.
3. Select your Member Interests from the given options.
4. Choose your Participation Preferences from the available options.
5. Add any additional Contact Details you'd like to share.
6. Provide a title for your registration and decide if you'd like to share it on social media.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (Registration Form, text, required): A general section to gather your thoughts on joining the community.
* **Member Details** (Member Details, text, optional): Provide your personal details such as name, email, and other relevant information.
* **Member Interests** (Member Interests, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you're interested in a particular area.
* **Participation Preferences** (Participation Preferences, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you're interested in a particular participation option.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, optional): Add any additional contact information you'd like to share.
* **Save and Share** (Save and Share, text, optional): Provide a title for your registration and decide if you'd like to share it on social media.

## Tips

* Be accurate and consistent in filling out each section to avoid duplication of effort in our internal systems.
* Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, please contact us for clarification.
