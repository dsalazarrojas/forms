# Tennis Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Club Membership Form is designed to collect contact and personal details from individuals interested in joining the tennis club membership program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information ready, including your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number.
2. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. Please note that we will be using this to contact you regarding your membership application and other club information.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your given first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your given last name.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, required: false): Enter your personal email address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have provided accurate contact information, as this will be used to contact you about your membership application.
* If you have any issues filling out the form or need further assistance, feel free to contact us for help.
* Please note that this form does not require your email and phone as mandatory fields, however, we encourage you to provide these details for easier communication and faster processing of your membership application.
