# Car Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects personal and vehicle information from potential members to help the club manage its membership and provide a personalized experience. Please complete the form carefully to ensure your membership application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and what is optional.
2. Fill out your personal details, including your full name, email address, phone number, home address, and emergency contact information.
3. Select your desired membership category (Individual, Family, Student, Professional, or Lifetime Member).
4. Enter your vehicle's make, model, year, and vehicle identification number (VIN).
5. Describe any modifications made to your vehicle, if applicable.
6. Indicate how you primarily use your vehicle (Daily Driver, Track/Racing Only, Weekend Enthusiast, or Show/Display Only).
7. Select the activities you are interested in (Group Cruises, Track Days, Charity Events, Car Shows, or Technical Workshops).
8. Confirm your acceptance of the club's rules and regulations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Provide a working email address where the club can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for emergencies or important notifications.
* **Home Address**: Provide your mailing address for club communications and event invitations.
* **Membership Category**: Select your preferred membership type:
	+ Individual: For individual members.
	+ Family: For family members with multiple members registered at the same address.
	+ Student: For students with a valid student ID.
	+ Professional: For professionals with a valid professional ID.
	+ Lifetime Member: For lifetime members who have been with the club since its inception.
* **Car Make**: Enter the brand name of your vehicle (e.g., Ford, Toyota).
* **Car Model**: Enter the model name of your vehicle (e.g., Mustang, Corolla).
* **Car Year**: Enter the year your vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)**: Enter your vehicle's 17-digit VIN number.
* **List Any Modifications**: Describe any performance or aesthetic changes made to your vehicle.
* **Primary Car Use**: Indicate how you primarily use your vehicle:
	+ Daily Driver: You drive your vehicle daily for personal use.
	+ Track/Racing Only: You primarily use your vehicle for track or racing purposes.
	+ Weekend Enthusiast: You drive your vehicle occasionally for enjoyment.
	+ Show/Display Only: You display your vehicle for show purposes, not driven daily.
* **Activities You Are Interested In**: Select the club events you are interested in participating in:
	+ Group Cruises: Join group rides and drives.
	+ Track Days: Participate in track events and racing activities.
	+ Charity Events: Help with charity events and fundraising.
	+ Car Shows: Show off your vehicle at club-organized car shows.
	+ Technical Workshops: Participate in workshops and learn about car maintenance and modification.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **I Accept the Club Rules and Regulations**: Confirm your acceptance of the club's rules and regulations. Please see the club website for more information.
* **Anything Else You Want to Share?**: Provide any additional information or requests you have.
