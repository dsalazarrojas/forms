# Tech Conference Early Bird Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees interested in securing early bird membership for a tech conference. The form will be used to gather relevant details such as event name, attendee's personal details, payment method, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the name of the event you are interested in attending in the "Event Name" field.
*   Provide your personal details including your name, email, phone number, and any other required information.
*   Choose your preferred payment method and amount.
*   Indicate the type of ticket you require.
*   Fill out the address, city, country, and zip for event location.
*   Provide your company details and social media handles.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name (1)
*   **Event Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the event you are interested in attending.

### Name (2)
*   **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on your ticket.

### Email (3)
*   **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.

### Phone (4)
*   **Phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up calls or updates.

### Payment Method (5)
*   **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Choose from Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal to make a payment.

### Payment Amount (6)
*   **Payment Amount** (number, required): Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the early bird offer.

### Ticket Type (7)
*   **Ticket Type** (select_multiple, required): Select the type of ticket you require. Options are Single Ticket, Double Ticket, or Family Ticket.

### Notes (8)
*   **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments regarding your early bird offer.

### Event Date (9)
*   **Event Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event you are interested in attending.

### Event Time (10)
*   **Event Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the event you are interested in attending.

### Address (11)
*   **Address** (text, required): Enter your physical address to help us locate you for event purposes.

### City (12)
*   **City** (text, required): Enter the city where the event location is.

### Country (13)
*   **Country** (text, required): Enter the country where the event location is.

### ZIP/Postal Code (14)
*   **ZIP/Postal Code** (text, required): Enter the zip or postal code of the event location.

### Event Name (15)
*   **Event Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the event again for verification purposes.

### Attendees (16)
*   **Attendees** (text, required): Indicate the number of attendees you plan to bring along with you.

### Company (17)
*   **Company** (text, required): Enter your company name as it should be displayed on your ticket.

### Job Title (18)
*   **Job Title** (text, required): Enter your job title to help us understand your background.

### Industry (19)
*   **Industry** (text, required): Enter your industry to help us understand your background.

### Company URL (20)
*   **Company URL** (text, required): Enter your company website URL.

### Job URL (21)
*   **Job URL** (text, required): Enter the job URL where you can be reached.

### Twitter Handle (22)
*   **Twitter Handle** (text, required): Enter your Twitter handle.

### Facebook URL (23)
*   **Facebook URL** (text, required): Enter your Facebook URL.

### LinkedIn URL (24)
*   **LinkedIn URL** (text, required): Enter your LinkedIn URL.

### LinkedIn ID (25)
*   **LinkedIn ID** (text, required): Enter your LinkedIn ID.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check your details for any errors before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
