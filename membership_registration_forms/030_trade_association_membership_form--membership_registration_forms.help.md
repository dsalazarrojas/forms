# Trade Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Association Membership Form is designed to gather information from potential members about their interest in joining the association, their contact preferences, and membership plan selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields according to your preferences. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Interest in becoming a member** (`interest_in_becoming_a_member`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you are interested in becoming a member, or "No" if you are not.
* **How did you find out about us** (`how_did_you_find_out_about_us`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the ways you found out about the association (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Word of mouth).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your interest in joining the association.
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the membership plan that suits your needs (Basic, Premium, Enterprise).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the contact methods you would like to use for communication (Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your physical address.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate contact information to receive updates and news from the association.
- Select the correct membership plan that suits your business needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to use the comments field to let us know.
