# digital_marketing_group_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register users for a digital marketing group.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the "How To Complete This Form" section.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time** (Time, time, Optional): Enter the time you are available.
* **Note** (Note, note, Optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Membership Type** (Membership Type, select_one, Required): Choose your membership type (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Confirm Membership** (Confirm Membership, select_one, Required): Confirm your membership type.
* **Confirm Membership Type** (Confirm Membership Type, select_one, Required): Confirm your membership type.
* **Confirm Membership Status** (Confirm Membership Status, select_one, Required): Confirm your membership status.
* **Confirm Membership Date** (Confirm Membership Date, date, Optional): Enter the date you confirmed your membership.
* **Membership Level** (Membership Level, select_one, Optional): Choose your membership level (Basic or Pro).
* **Membership Status** (Membership Status, select_one, Optional): Choose your membership status (Active or Inactive).
* **Membership Date** (Membership Date, date, Optional): Enter the date of your membership.
* **Created At** (Created At, date, Optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Updated At** (Updated At, date, Optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Deleted At** (Deleted At, date, Optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Confirmed At** (Confirmed At, date, Optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Membership Level Description** (Membership Level Description, text, Optional): Enter a description of your membership level.
* **Role Description** (Role Description, text, Optional): Enter a description of your role within the group.
* **Membership Status Description** (Membership Status Description, text, Optional): Enter a description of your membership status.
* **Membership Level ID** (Membership Level ID, number, Optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Membership Status ID** (Membership Status ID, number, Optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
