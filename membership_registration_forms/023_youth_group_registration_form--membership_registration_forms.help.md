# Youth Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Group Registration Form is a form used for the registration of youth groups into a program. It is to be filled out by a parent or guardian on their behalf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form in one session without saving or leaving the page.
2. Please be sure to provide accurate and complete information.
3. Please select "Agree" to the terms of service at the end of the form.
