# Membership Dues Renewal - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for current members to renew their membership dues. Please provide accurate information to ensure a smooth renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of membership you currently hold.
2. Indicate your current membership status.
3. Choose your membership terms.
4. Enter your current membership dues amount.
5. Provide your membership expiration date.
6. Enter the amount of your membership dues.
7. Select the status of your membership renewal.
8. Enter the number of years you have been a member.
9. Enter the membership fee.
10. Select your membership status for the new terms.
11. Choose the membership terms for the new membership.
12. Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of membership do you have?** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of membership you currently hold, such as Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **What is your current membership status?** (`membership_status`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options: Active, Inactive, Renewing.
* **What are your membership terms?** (`membership_terms`, select_one, required): Choose the terms of your membership, such as Option A or Option B.
* **What are your current membership dues?** (`membership_dues`, number, required): Enter the current membership dues amount.
* **What is your membership expiration date?** (`membership_expiration_date`, date, required): Provide the date when your membership expires.
* **What is your membership fee?** (`membership_fee`, number, required): Enter the fee for your membership.
* **What is your membership renewal status?** (`membership_renewal_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your membership renewal: Active, Inactive, Renewing.
* **What is the amount of your membership dues?** (`membership_dues_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of membership dues you pay.
* **How long have you been a member?** (`membership_duration`, date, required): Provide the date since you became a member.
* **Membership Fee 2** (`membership_fee_2`, number, required): Enter the second fee for your membership.
* **What is your membership status?** (`membership_status_2`, select_one, required): Select your membership status for the new terms: Active, Inactive, Renewing.
* **Membership Terms 2** (`membership_terms_2`, select_one, required): Choose the terms of your membership for the new membership.
* **Is this information accurate?** (`membership_review`, checkbox, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
