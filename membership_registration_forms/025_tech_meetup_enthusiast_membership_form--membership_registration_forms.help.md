# tech_meetup_enthusiast_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about potential members interested in joining our tech meetup community.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
3.  Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
4.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
6.  If you've been a member of our community before, select your **Member Since** date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7.  Select how often you attend meetups in the **Meetup Frequency** field.
8.  Choose your preferred meetup day(s) from the **Preferred Meetup Day** field.
9.  Select your area(s) of interest from the **Interested In** field.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about yourself in the **Note** field.
11. Confirm that we have your correct email address by selecting **Confirm Email**.
12. Confirm that we have your correct phone number by selecting **Confirm Phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name here.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the format of `username@domain.com`.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in the format of `(123) 456-7890`.
*   **Member Since** (`member_since`, `date`, required): If you've been a member before, select the date you first joined in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Meetup Frequency** (`meetup_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you attend meetups.
*   **Preferred Meetup Day** (`preferred_meetup_day`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred meetup day(s).
*   **Interested In** (`interested_in`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your areas of interest.
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, required): Add any additional information about yourself.
*   **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `select_one`, required): Verify that your email address is correct.
*   **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, `select_one`, required): Verify that your phone number is correct.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us better serve you.
*   If you have any issues while filling out this form, feel free to contact us.
