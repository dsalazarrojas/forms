# Heritage Site Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be filled out by individuals interested in joining the Heritage Site. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your membership application is processed successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully before proceeding.
2. Fill out the User Info section with your details.
3. Provide information in the Membership Info section as prompted.
4. Click the Submit button to send in your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Info (Page 1)
* **User Info**: 
	+ (text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ (type): Text
	+ required: No
* **(empty)**: This field is for additional information, you can leave it blank if not necessary.

### Membership Info (Page 2)
* **Membership Info**:
	+ (text): Please provide your membership type and any additional details.
	+ (type): Text
	+ required: No

### Submit (Page 3)
* **Submit**:
	+ (text): Once you have completed the form, click this button to submit your membership application.
	+ (type): Text
	+ required: No

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
- We will review and process your membership application as soon as possible after receiving it.
