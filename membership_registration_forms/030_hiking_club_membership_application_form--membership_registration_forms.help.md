# Hiking Club Membership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hiking Club Membership Application Form is a public-facing form designed to collect information from potential members who wish to join the Hiking Club. This form helps the club's administration process new member applications efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information for each field, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details.
2. Review each field's explanation below for further guidance on specific requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about your membership status and club news.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Address** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your current address for delivery purposes and emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (6, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Membership Type** (7, select_one, required/optional): Choose from Individual, Group, or Family membership options. Please select the category that best describes your application.
* **Emergency Contact** (8, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are applying as a group or family, ensure that all members are included in the membership type field.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to reach out to us directly.
