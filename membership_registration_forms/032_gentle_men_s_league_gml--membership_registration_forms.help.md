# Gentle Men's League Gml - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gentle Men's League Gml registration form is a tool for gathering information from potential members of the organization. This form allows you to collect personal details, contact information, and other relevant data to create or update a member's record.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Type your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your Organization affiliation (if applicable) in the "Organization" field.
5. Select your Role within the organization in the "Role" field.
6. Choose your Category (Member or Non-Member) in the "Category" field.
7. Write a brief bio in the "Bio" field (if required).
8. Provide your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field (if required).
9. Select your Membership Type (Basic, Premium, or Deluxe) in the "Membership Type" field.
10. Enter your Address in the "Address" field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your organization (if applicable).
- **Role** (`role`, text, required/optional): Select your role within the organization (e.g., employee, member, etc.).
- **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you are a Member or Non-Member of the organization.
- **Bio** (`bio`, note, required/optional): Write a brief description of yourself.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.
- **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select your membership type (Basic, Premium, or Deluxe).
- **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your mailing address.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the league's administration for assistance.
- This form is for registration purposes only; do not use it for any other purpose.
