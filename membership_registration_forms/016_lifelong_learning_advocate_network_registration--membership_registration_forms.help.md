# Lifelong Learning Advocate Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about you so that we can create a profile for you in the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network. This network is a community of individuals who are passionate about promoting lifelong learning initiatives and opportunities. By registering, you will be able to connect with others who share your interests and work together to create a culture of lifelong learning in your own organization, community, or nation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the First name field.
2. Enter your Last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field. Please ensure that your email address is the one you use to receive important updates from us.
4. Enter your Organization in the Organization field. If you don't have an organization, you can enter a brief description of your current role or position.
5. Select your Role in the Role field. Choose one that best describes your work or activities related to promoting lifelong learning.
6. Enter your Location in the Location field. This can be your city, state, or country.
7. Select your interests related to promoting lifelong learning from the Interests field.
8. Enter your motivations for joining the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network in the Motivations field.
9. Enter your preferred contact method in the Contact method field. This can be your phone number or email address.
10. Enter your contact number in the Contact number field. If you have a contact number different from your email, you can enter it here.
11. Enter your website URL in the Website URL field, if you have one.
12. Enter a description of yourself and your work related to promoting lifelong learning in the Description field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`First name`, text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in the network.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in the network.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you use to receive important updates from us.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization or current role if you have one.
* **Role** (`Role`, text, optional): Select one that best describes your work or activities related to promoting lifelong learning.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, optional): Enter your city, state, or country.
* **Interests** (`Interests`, select multiple, optional): Select all the interests that you have related to promoting lifelong learning.
* **Motivations** (`Motivations`, text, optional): Enter your reasons for joining the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network.
* **Contact method** (`Contact method`, text, optional): Enter your preferred contact method, such as phone number or email.
* **Contact number** (`Contact number`, number, optional): Enter your contact number if different from your email.
* **Website URL** (`Website URL`, text, optional): If you have a website, enter the URL here.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, optional): Enter a description of yourself and your work related to promoting lifelong learning.
