### Perishable Goods Alliance Registration Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The Perishable Goods Alliance Registration Form is a crucial document for any individual or partnership interested in registering with the Perishable Goods Alliance. This form is the primary vehicle for providing necessary information about the registering party, their partnership details, and their contact information. Completing this form accurately ensures a successful registration and initiation of the partnership creation process within the alliance.

#### How To Complete This Form
To ensure a seamless and accurate registration process, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information required for the form fields, as specified in each field explanation below.
2.  Fill out all required fields, indicated by the `Required: true` label in each field explanation.
3.  Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and clarity of information provided.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Name** (`User Name`, text, optional): This field is for you to input your name, which will be used for identification purposes within the Perishable Goods Alliance.
*   **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address here. This field will be used for communication and updates within the alliance.
*   **Partnership Name** (`Partnership Name`, text, optional): If you are registering as a partnership, input the name of your partnership here.
*   **Partnership Type** (`Partnership Type`, select_one, optional): If your partnership has a type that is different from an individual, select the appropriate type from the provided options. This could be a business or other partnership type relevant to the Perishable Goods Alliance.
*   **Partnership Category** (`Partnership Category`, select_one, optional): Select whether your partnership is a category that the Perishable Goods Alliance considers relevant. This could be a specific business category or type of partnership.
*   **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, optional): If you are registering as a partnership, input the name of the contact person responsible for communication and representation within the alliance.
*   **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here for communication purposes. This is optional but recommended for ease of contact.
*   **Partnership Address** (`Partnership Address`, text, optional): If you are registering as a partnership, input the physical address of your partnership here.
*   **Partnership City** (`Partnership City`, text, optional): Enter the city where your partnership is located.
*   **Partnership State** (`Partnership State`, select_one, optional): Select the state or region where your partnership is located.
*   **Partnership Postcode** (`Partnership Postcode`, select_one, optional): Select whether your partnership has a postcode or not. This might be relevant for international partnerships.
*   **Partnership Country** (`Partnership Country`, select_one, optional): Select the country where your partnership is located.
*   **Partnership Website** (`Partnership Website`, text, optional): If your partnership has a website, input the URL here for reference and promotion.
*   **Partnership Notes** (`Partnership Notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments relevant to your partnership.
*   **User Notes** (`User Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information relevant to your own identity and purpose within the Perishable Goods Alliance.

#### Tips
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid delaying the registration process.
*   If you are registering as a partnership, ensure that all partnership fields are filled out with accurate information.
*   Double-check your contact information for accuracy to facilitate easy communication.
