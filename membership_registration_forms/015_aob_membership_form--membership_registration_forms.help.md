# AOB Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the AOB Membership Form is to collect necessary information from potential members to assess their suitability for membership in our organization. This form helps us understand your artistic background, interests, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the general information page (page 1) with your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  On the second page, select your primary artistic medium and choose the styles you work in (if applicable).
3.  Choose your experience level based on the years you've been practicing art professionally.
4.  On the third page, select the type of membership you're applying for, choose your marketing interests, event participation, and volunteer opportunities, and select your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`member_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete legal name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email Address** (`member_email`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address for membership communications.
*   **Phone Number** (`member_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for membership inquiries.
*   **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your professional name as an artist (if different from your full name).
*   **Primary Artistic Medium** (`artistic_medium`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary medium you work with (e.g., Painting, Sculpture, Photography, Digital Art, etc.).
*   **Artistic Styles** (`artistic_styles`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the styles you work in (e.g., Abstract, Realism, Impressionism, Contemporary, etc.).
*   **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience based on the years you've been practicing art professionally.
*   **Exhibition History** (`exhibition_history`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe any notable exhibition experience you've had.
*   **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, `text`, optional): Provide a URL to your online portfolio or artwork gallery.
*   **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of membership you're applying for (e.g., Individual, Student, Senior, Corporate, Patron).
*   **Marketing Interests** (`marketing_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select how you'd like to promote your work through our organization (e.g., Online Gallery, Newsletter Features, Social Media Promotion, etc.).
*   **Event Participation Interest** (`event_participation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your interest in participating in organization events (e.g., Yes, All Events, Yes, Selected Events, etc.).
*   **Volunteer Opportunities** (`volunteer_opportunities`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your interest in volunteering for organization activities.
*   **Payment Method for Membership Fee** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Check, Bank Transfer).

**Tips:**

*   Ensure your email address is accurate and active.
*   Be honest about your experience level to ensure you're applying for the correct membership category.
*   Select all relevant artistic styles you work in to increase your visibility within the organization.
*   Clearly state your exhibition history to help us evaluate your profile.
*   If you're unsure about the payment method, you can ask for assistance or guidance.
*   Indicate your interests honestly to receive tailored marketing opportunities.
