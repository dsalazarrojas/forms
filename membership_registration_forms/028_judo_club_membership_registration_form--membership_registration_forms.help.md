# Judo Club Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Judo Club Membership Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals interested in joining the Judo Club. This form helps the club's administrative staff to efficiently process membership applications and provide a secure and enjoyable experience for all members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the terms outlined in the "club rules" and "payment policy" notes.
2. Provide your name, birth date, and parent name (if applicable) in the respective fields.
3. Enter your phone number and emergency contact information (phone number) if necessary.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **club rules** (note, optional): This section provides an overview of the club's rules and regulations. Please take a moment to read and understand them before proceeding.
* **waiver** (note, optional): This is a general waiver that outlines the club's policies and your responsibilities as a member.
* **privacy policy** (note, optional): This is a note explaining the club's handling of your data and personal information.
* **payment policy** (note, optional): This outlines the club's payment terms and policies.
* **name** (text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **birth date** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **parent name** (text, optional): If you are registering on behalf of a child, please enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **phone number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication and emergency contact.
* **emergency contact** (text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **emergency contact phone number** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **member signature** (note, optional): Please sign and date this form to confirm your application.
