# Summer Book Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Book Club Registration Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in joining the Summer Book Club. The form is intended to ensure that we have the necessary information and preferences from all members before starting the club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the email field. (Note: Please ensure the email address is correct to ensure you receive updates and notifications.)
4. Fill in your **Address** in the text field.
5. Enter your **Child's Name** (if applicable) in the text field.
6. Select your **Gender** by choosing from the provided options.
7. Enter your **Age** in the numerical field.
8. Select your **Interests** by choosing from the provided options.
9. Fill in your **Wish List** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: This field is not required for the registration process but will be used to identify the form submission in our records.
* **Name**: Please fill in your name as it should be spelled and formatted on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX XXX XXXX".
* **Email**: This field requires a valid email address that you check frequently. Please ensure it is correct to receive updates and notifications from the book club.
* **Address**: Enter your address in the provided text field.
* **Child's Name**: If you are registering with a child, please enter their name in the text field.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options to help us better understand our member demographics.
* **Age**: Enter your age to help us tailor the book club experience.
* **Interests**: Select your interests from the provided options to help us suggest books and plan the reading schedule.
* **Wish List**: Suggest any books or genres you are interested in or would like to see in the book club schedule.
