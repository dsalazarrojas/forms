# e_sports_membership_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register potential members for sports teams, gathering essential information such as names, contact details, and team affiliation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Provide your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Indicate your affiliation with the team by selecting 'Yes' if you're a team member or 'No' otherwise in the "team_name" field.
4. If you're the team leader, enter your name in the "team_leader_name" field. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field, following the standard email format (username@domain.com).
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Enter your given name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter your surname (e.g., Doe).
* **Team Affiliation** (team_name, select_one, required): Indicate your status with the team by selecting 'Yes' if you're a team member or 'No' otherwise.
* **Team Leader's Name** (team_leader_name, text, optional): If you're the team leader, enter your name here.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address following the standard format (username@domain.com).
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
