# Kite Flying Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kite Flying Club Membership Form is a registration form designed for individuals who wish to become members of the Kite Flying Club. This form helps the club administer membership applications and assign suitable roles and responsibilities within the club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Kite Flying Club Membership Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields. These fields are required to identify you as a member of the club.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number**, which will be used for internal communication and contact purposes.
3. Select your preferred **Membership Type** from the options provided, which will determine your level of involvement and benefits within the club.
4. Choose your **Preferred Location**, selecting one or multiple options where you would like to participate in club activities.
5. Select your **Kite Color**, which will identify your personal preference for kite flying events.
6. Optionally, you can add additional notes or comments in the **Note** field, providing any relevant information not captured in the other fields.
7. Select a **Submitted At** date and time for when you completed the form, which helps track the progress of your application.
8. You can select who **Submitted By**, which will help the club track who submitted the form on your behalf.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name, which is used to identify you as a member of the club. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, which is used in conjunction with your first name to identify you as a member of the club. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Your email address, which is used for internal communication and contact purposes. This is a required field.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number, which is used for internal communication and contact purposes. This is a required field.
* **Membership Type**: Choose from Basic, Premium, or Elite to determine your level of involvement and benefits within the club. This is a required field.
* **Preferred Location**: Select one or multiple locations where you would like to participate in club activities. This is a required field.
* **Kite Color**: Select a color that you prefer for your kite flying activities, which will help identify your personal preference. This is a required field.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments you feel are relevant to your membership application. This is an optional field.
* **Submitted At**: Select the date and time when you completed the form, which helps track the progress of your application. This is a required field.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form on your behalf, which will help the club track who submitted the application. This is an optional field.
