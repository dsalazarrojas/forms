# Scentsy Sample Mail And Birthday Club - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential members to join the Scentsy Sample Mail And Birthday Club. By filling out this form, you will be able to sign up for the club's services and be part of a community that shares similar interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Membership Registration form on Page 1.
2. Provide your Mailing Info on Page 2.
3. Select your Interests on Page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Registration** (`membership_registration`, text, required: false): This is the first page where you need to fill out your general information to register for the club. You can provide any details you feel are relevant to your membership.
* **Mailing Info** (`mailing_info`, text, required: false): On this page, please provide your name and address so we can get in touch with you and send you information about club events.
* **Interests** (`interests`, text, required: false): Here, you can select any interests you have that align with the club's goals. This will help us know you better and tailor our communication to you.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields, even if they are not marked as required. This will help us have a complete and accurate record of your membership information.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx. We will be happy to assist you.
