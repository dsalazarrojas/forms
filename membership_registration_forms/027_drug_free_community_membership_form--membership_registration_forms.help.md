# Drug Free Community Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drug Free Community Membership Form is designed to collect essential information from new members and their non-profit organizations. This information will be used to update the member's status and provide access to exclusive resources and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your member details, including your name and email address.
2.  Enter your phone number and commitment details.
3.  Select your payment plan and payment amount (if applicable).
4.  Confirm your membership status and renewal status.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments about your membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details (`Member Details`, `text`, required: false)**: This field is used to collect your basic information, including your name and email address.
* **Name (`Name`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your name as it appears on official records.
* **Email (`Email`, `email`, required: false)**: Enter your email address associated with your non-profit organization.
* **Phone (`Phone`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your non-profit organization's phone number.
* **Commitment (`Commitment`, `text`, required: false)**: Describe your commitment to the Drug Free Community.
* **Commitment Date (`Commitment Date`, `date`, required: false)**: Enter the date of your commitment.
* **Signature (`Signature`, `note`, required: false)**: You can add any additional notes or comments about your signature.
* **Coalition ID (`Coalition ID`, `select_one`, required: false)**: Select the coalition you are a member of.
* **Non-Profit Name (`Non-Profit Name`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your non-profit organization's name.
* **Non-Profit Email (`Non-Profit Email`, `email`, required: false)**: Enter your non-profit organization's email address.
* **Non-Profit Phone (`Non-Profit Phone`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your non-profit organization's phone number.
* **Non-Profit Website (`Non-Profit Website`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your non-profit organization's website.
* **Non-Profit Address (`Non-Profit Address`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your non-profit organization's address.
* **Payment Plan (`Payment Plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false)**: Select your payment plan (Monthly, Yearly, or Quarterly).
* **Payment Amount (`Payment Amount`, `number`, required: false)**: Enter your payment amount (if applicable).
* **Membership Status (`Membership Status`, `select_one`, required: false)**: Confirm your membership status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Renewal Status (`Renewal Status`, `select_one`, required: false)**: Confirm your renewal status (Renewing, Not Renewing).
* **Membership Date (`Membership Date`, `date`, required: false)**: Enter the date of your membership.
* **Membership Type (`Membership Type`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your membership type.
* **Coalition (`Coalition`, `select_one`, required: false)**: Select the coalition you are a member of.
* **Coalition Committee (`Coalition Committee`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter the coalition committee you are a part of.
* **Non-Profit Ownership (`Non-Profit Ownership`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your non-profit organization's ownership information.
* **Non-Profit Leads (`Non-Profit Leads`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your non-profit organization's leaders' names and titles.
* **Non-Profit Board Members (`Non-Profit Board Members`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your non-profit organization's board members' names and titles.
* **Member Notes (`Member Notes`, `note`, required: false)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your membership.

Please take your time to complete this form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
