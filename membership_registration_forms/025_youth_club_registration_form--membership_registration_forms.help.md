# Youth Club Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Youth Club Registration Form is a form designed for youth members to register themselves with the club. It gathers essential information about the member, their emergency contact, and their background. This form will help the club's administrators to keep track of member information and provide support when needed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the first two fields.
2. Provide your **Email** address and **Phone** number.
3. Share your **Educational Background** and **Experience** in the respective fields.
4. List your **Skills** that you think are relevant to the club.
5. Share your **Goals** for joining the club.
6. Provide information about your **Emergency Contact**, including their **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Relationship**, **Phone**, **Email**, and **Address**. This information will help us to reach them in case of an emergency.
7. Choose your **Gender**.
8. Select your **Country** and **State** where you live.
9. Enter your **City**, **Zip Code**, and **Age**.
10. If applicable, choose the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Occupation** from the lists.
11. Enter your **Emergency Contact Annual Income** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Educational Background** (Text): Share a bit about your educational background, such as your educational level and institution.
* **Experience** (Text): Share any relevant experience that you think is important to mention.
* **Skills** (Text): List any skills you have that you think are relevant to the club.
* **Goals** (Text): Share your goals for joining the club.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (Text): Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (Text): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., Mom, Dad, Brother).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Text): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Message** (Text): Add any message to your emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (Number): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (Select One): Choose your gender.
* **Country** (Select One): Choose your country of residence.
* **State** (Select One): Choose your state of residence.
* **City** (Text): Enter the city where you live.
* **Zip Code** (Number): Enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Select One): Choose the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Occupation** (Select One): Choose the occupation of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Annual Income** (Number): If you want to provide this information, enter the annual income of your emergency contact.
