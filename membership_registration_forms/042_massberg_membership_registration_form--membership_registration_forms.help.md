# MASSBERG Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MASSBERG Membership Registration Form is a critical step in becoming a part of the MASSBERG community. By filling out this form, you'll provide essential information about yourself and your family, which will help us understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by carefully reading each field's description.
- Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address in the respective fields.
- Enter your Phone Number and Date of Birth accurately.
- Select your Family Name and choose the correct options for Massberg Family Members.
- Choose the correct answer for Massberg Family Member sections, considering each question carefully.
- Add any relevant Comments, if applicable.
- Finally, submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, **Optional**): This is your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, **Optional**): This is your personal last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **Optional**): Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, **Optional**): Enter your date of birth accurately.
* **Family Name** (`family_name`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your family name if different from your given name.
* **Massberg Family Members** (`family_members`, Select Multiple, **Optional**): Choose from the provided options to represent your family.
* **Massberg Family** (`massberg_family`, Select One, **Required**): Select the correct answer to indicate your relationship with the MASSBERG community.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, Date, **Optional**): This field is for internal use; you can leave it blank.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, **Optional**): Add any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Massberg Family Member** (`massberg_family_member`, Select One, **Optional**): Choose your response to this question carefully.
* Massberg Family Member fields 2-13 are similar to `massberg_family_member`, each representing a different member of your family.

Note: Some fields are marked as **Optional**, but it's essential to fill them out accurately if you want to ensure a successful registration process.
