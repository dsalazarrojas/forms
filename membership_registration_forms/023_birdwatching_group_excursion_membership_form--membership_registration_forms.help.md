# Birdwatching Group Excursion Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand your preferences and needs for birdwatching group excursions. By filling it out, you'll help us tailor excursions that fit your interests and abilities. Please answer all questions honestly, and if you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal information.
2. Choose your membership type and renewal status.
3. Tell us about your birdwatching experience and interests.
4. Indicate your availability for excursions.
5. Specify your preferred communication method.
6. Provide emergency contact information.
7. Acknowledge the risks of outdoor activities.
8. Finally, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (member_first_name, text, required): Enter your first name. This will help us address you properly.
* **Last Name** (member_last_name, text, required): Enter your last name. This will help us contact you and address you properly.
* **Email Address** (member_email, email, required): Enter your primary email address. This will be our main contact point for excursions and club updates.
* **Phone Number** (member_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you in case of emergencies or excursions changes.
* **Mailing Address** (member_address, text, required): Enter your address. This will help us send you club updates and information.
* **Membership Type** (membership_type, select_one, required): Choose your membership level: Annual Individual, Annual Family, Monthly, Student, Lifetime, or Guest.
* **New Member or Renewal** (membership_renewal, select_one, required): Indicate whether this is your first membership or a renewal. This will help us tailor our communication and services.
* **Birdwatching Experience Level** (experience_level, select_one, required): Tell us about your birdwatching experience: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
* **Favorite Bird Species** (favorite_birds, text, optional): Indicate which bird species interest you most.
* **Binoculars and Equipment** (binoculars_equipment, select_one, required): Specify whether you have observation gear: Yes, Have Good Equipment, Yes, Have Basic Equipment, No, But Plan to Get, or No.
* **Interested in Group Excursions** (interested_in_excursions, select_one, required): Indicate whether you're interested, very interested, or not interested in joining our group excursions.
* **Preferred Excursion Type** (preferred_excursion_type, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of excursions you'd like to join: Local Parks, Hiking Trails, Wetlands and Water Areas, Mountain Regions, Coastal Areas, Early Morning Walks, Weekend Trips, or Multi-Day Trips.
* **Mobility Considerations** (mobility_considerations, select_one, required): Tell us about any mobility needs or limitations: No Special Considerations, Prefer Shorter Walks, Need Wheelchair Access, or Other Accommodations Needed.
* **Weekend Availability** (weekend_availability, select_one, required): Indicate your weekend availability: Available Most Weekends, Available Some Weekends, Limited Availability, or Mostly Unavailable.
* **Weekday Availability** (weekday_availability, select_one, required): Indicate your weekday availability: Available Weekdays, Available Some Weekdays, Limited Availability, or Not Available Weekdays.
* **Comfortable with Early Morning Starts** (early_morning_preference, select_one, required): Indicate whether you're comfortable with early morning starts: Yes, Prefer Early, Yes, Can Do Early, Prefer Later, or Not Possible.
* **Interested in Overnight Trips** (overnight_trip_interest, select_one, optional): Indicate your interest in multi-day excursions: Yes, Very Interested, Interested, Maybe, or Not Interested.
* **Areas of Interest** (club_interest_areas, select_multiple, optional): Select the areas that appeal to you: Species Identification, Photography, Behavioral Observation, Bird Conservation, Sound Recording, Scientific Research, Social Enjoyment.
* **Will to Lead Trips** (willing_to_lead_trips, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you're willing to lead excursions: Yes, Very Willing, Willing to Help, Maybe, or Not Willing.
* **Interest in Volunteering** (willing_to_volunteer, select_one, optional): Indicate your interest in volunteering for club activities: Yes, Very Interested, Interested, Maybe, or Not Interested.
* **Volunteer Areas** (volunteer_areas, select_multiple, optional): Select the areas you'd like to volunteer with: Event Coordination, Habitat Conservation, Education, Data Collection, Social Media, Member Communications.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, required): Provide the name of a person to contact in case of emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, text, optional): Indicate any medical conditions that may affect your participation in excursions.
* **I Acknowledge the Risks** (liability_acknowledgment, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the risks of outdoor activities: I Acknowledge, I Do Not Acknowledge.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (preferred_communication, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted: Email, Phone, SMS Text, or Mail.
* **Subscribe to Newsletters** (newsletter_consent, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you'd like to receive club newsletters and updates: Yes, Subscribe, No, Thank You.
