# Finance Professionals Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for finance professionals to register as members of our organization. It collects your personal details, contact information, and job role or organization affiliation to help us manage our internal records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the following information:
   * First Name
   * Last Name
   * Email address (in the format `your_name@example.com`)
2. If you have a phone number, feel free to provide it. This is optional.
3. Choose your category from the options provided (Member, Non-Member, Both).
4. Select your profession from the list (Financial Analyst, Financial Advisor, Financial Consultant).
5. If you are associated with an organization, please provide its name.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can use the text area below to add them.
7. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format `your_name@example.com`.
- **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
- **Type** (`form_type`, select_one, required): Choose your type (Member, Non-Member, Both).
  - Select Member to indicate you are a current member of our organization.
  - Select Non-Member to indicate you are not a member.
  - Select Both to indicate you are a member and also not a member.
- **Profession** (`form_profession`, select_multiple, required): Choose your profession from the list (Financial Analyst, Financial Advisor, Financial Consultant).
  - Select all applicable options if you hold multiple professions.
- **Organization** (`form_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization you are affiliated with.
- **Note** (`form_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about yourself.
