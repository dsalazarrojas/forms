# Environmental Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The environmental membership subscription form is designed to collect essential information from individuals or groups applying for membership in an environmental organization. The gathered data will be used for processing membership applications and maintaining accurate internal records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your organization's details, including name and any other relevant information.
2. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Select the subscription plan that suits your needs (Individual or Group).
4. If applicable, provide any additional payment details.
5. Add any notes or comments about your application, if needed.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Set the date and time you wish to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`): Enter your organization's name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your name and any contact method (email or phone number) to be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`): Select whether you wish to be an Individual or Group member. This option is required.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): If applicable, provide any payment information, such as payment amount and method.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your membership application.
* **E-mail** (`email`): Enter your email address for correspondence and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for phone-based communication.
* **Date-Time** (`date_time`): Set the date and time you wish to be contacted. This field is not required, but it helps the organization's team to schedule follow-up actions.
* **Time** (`time`): Set the time you prefer to be contacted. This field is not required, but it helps to schedule appointments or meetings.
* **Date** (`date`): Set the date you prefer to be contacted. This field is not required, but it helps to schedule appointments or meetings.
* **Tips**
- Ensure that all required fields are filled to avoid delays in processing your membership application.
- Double-check your details for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organization's administration team for assistance.
