# Participant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Application Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from potential members of an organization or community. The form is meant to be used for original applications and not for re-submissions or updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information in the `participant_form` field.
2. Enter your contact information in the `contact_info` field.
3. Enter your registration information in the `registration_info` field.
4. Add your event details in the `event_details` field.
5. Enter your participant details in the `participant_details` field.
6. Add your emergency contact information in the `emergency_contact` field.
7. Enter any relevant medical information in the `medical_info` field.
8. Finally, provide payment information in the `payment_info` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_form`): Please enter your participant name, ID, or any other relevant information required by the organization or community.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your name, email address, phone number, or any other means of communication.
* **Registration Information** (`registration_info`): Provide any registration information required by the organization or community, such as membership type or level.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Enter any event-related information, such as event dates, location, or schedule.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Enter any additional information about yourself as a participant, such as your role or position.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`): Provide the name, relationship, and contact details of an emergency contact person.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`): Enter any medical information that is relevant to your participation in the event or organization.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`): Enter any payment details, such as payment method or amount due.
