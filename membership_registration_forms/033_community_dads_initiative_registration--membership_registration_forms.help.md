# Community Dads Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Dads Initiative Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in the program's activities such as parenting workshops, mentorship programs, and community events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form. These fields are:
	* Registrant name
	* Email address
	* Areas of interest (selecting at least one activity)
	* Availability for meetings or events
	* Emergency contact name and phone
	* Consent to participate and use data
2. Provide your contact phone number and home address if you wish to be contacted by the program coordinators.
3. Select your areas of interest from the provided options.
4. Provide your availability for meetings or events in the text field.
5. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
6. Select your consent to participate and use data from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant name** (Registrant name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required): Enter your valid email address to receive communications from the program.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, required): Enter your contact phone number to enable easy communication.
* **Home address** (Home address, text, optional): Enter your home address to help with outreach efforts (optional).
* **Areas of interest** (Areas of interest, select_multiple, required): Select at least one activity you are interested in participating in.
	+ Parenting workshops
	+ Mentorship
	+ Community events
	+ Volunteer outreach
* **Availability for meetings or events** (Availability for meetings or events, text, required): Enter your availability for meetings or events in the format of days and times.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (Emergency contact name and phone, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Consent to participate and use data** (Consent to participate and use data, select_one, required): Select "I consent" to agree to the program's terms.
