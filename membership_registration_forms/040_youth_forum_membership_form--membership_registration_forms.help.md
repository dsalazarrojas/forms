# youth_forum_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The youth forum membership form is used to register members of a youth forum. To register, a parent or legal guardian must provide accurate and up-to-date information about themselves and their child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Youth Group Name field with the name of the group your child is a member of.
2. Enter the name of the parent or legal guardian who is registering the child.
3. Enter the email address of the parent or legal guardian.
4. Fill in the Phone field with the contact phone number of the parent or legal guardian.
5. Enter the Address field with the address of the parent or legal guardian.
6. Fill in the Date of Birth field with the date of birth of the child.
7. Fill in the Time of Birth field with the time of birth of the child.
8. Select the Membership Type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Youth Group Name** (`youth_group_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the youth group your child is a member of.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name and the name of the parent or legal guardian who is registering the child.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the child.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter the time of birth of the child.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, text, required): Select the type of membership you are registering for.
