# Sustainable Living Group Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users as members of the Sustainable Living Group. It collects information that will help the group's administrators know who is a member and their level of involvement with the group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose whether you are interested in being a member of the Sustainable Living Group.
2. Enter your contact information so the group's administrators can get in touch with you.
3. Tell us about the sustainable practices you follow.
4. Let us know if you belong to any other member groups within the group.
5. Select your desired membership level (High or Low).
6. Enter the date you signed up for membership.
7. Enter the time you signed up for membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Details** (Membership Details, Select Multiple, **Required: No**): Check the box if you wish to be a member of the Sustainable Living Group.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, Text, **Required: No**): Enter your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information you wish to provide.
* **Sustainable Practices** (Sustainable Practices, Text, **Required: No**): Describe the sustainable practices you follow, such as using public transport or recycling.
* **Member Groups** (Member Groups, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose the member groups you belong to within the Sustainable Living Group.
* **Membership Level** (Membership Level, Select One, **Required: No**): Select a membership level (High or Low) to indicate how involved you wish to be with the group.
* **Signup Date** (Signup Date, Date, **Required: No**): Enter the date you signed up for membership.
* **Signup Time** (Signup Time, Time, **Required: No**): Enter the time you signed up for membership.

Note: Fields marked as "Required: No" are optional, but providing them will allow the group's administrators to better understand your membership.
