# Digital Platform Exclusive Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Platform Exclusive Membership Form is for registering new members on our digital platform. Only invited users or those with a valid reason to join should fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid reason to join our platform and have been properly invited.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **New Member Application** (`new_member_application`, `text`, optional): Enter your application for joining our platform.
  This field is not required and serves as a way for you to express your interest in joining our platform.

- **Platform Details** (`platform_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about your interest in our platform.
  This field is not required and can be used to give more context about your application.

- **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your desired membership level (Basic, Plus, or Premium).
  Select the membership level that best suits your needs.

- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information.
  This field is not required but can be useful for us to contact you.

- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
  We will use this to verify your email and contact you for further instructions.

- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
  This can be used to contact you in case we need more information.

- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
  We use this for identity purposes but also to ensure you're eligible for our services.

- **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional): You must agree to our terms and conditions to proceed.
  Please ensure you have read and understood our terms before selecting both "I agree".

- **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Review your application before submission.
  Double-check your information before clicking the submit button.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submission.
* Review our terms and conditions before agreeing.
* If you're still unsure, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
