# Professional Networking Group Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow existing members of the Professional Networking Group to renew their membership. The form will collect updated information to maintain accurate records and ensure continued participation in group activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your member's first name in the **Member First Name** field.
2. Enter your member's last name in the **Member Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select your current **Membership Type** (Yes/No).
6. Choose your **Membership Level** (High/Low).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member First Name** (`member_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
* **Member Last Name** (`member_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Provide your personal email address where you can be contacted for group activities.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for group activities.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you would like to continue being a member, and "No" if you are resigning.
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, select one, required/optional): Choose between "High" and "Low" membership levels, which might have different benefits and responsibilities.
