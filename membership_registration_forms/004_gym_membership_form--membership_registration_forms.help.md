# Gym Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the Gym Membership Form, ensuring you provide all necessary information for your membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Provide your contact information:
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number.
3. Choose your membership type:
	* Select the membership type that best suits your needs (Gym, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
4. Enter emergency contact information:
	* Emergency Contact First Name: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
	* Emergency Contact Last Name: Enter the last name of your emergency contact person.
	* Relationship: Choose how you're related to your emergency contact person (Parent, Child, Spouse, Friend).
	* Address: Enter your emergency contact's address.
	* City: Enter your emergency contact's city of residence.
	* State: Enter your emergency contact's state of residence.
	* Zip: Enter your emergency contact's zip code.
	* Phone: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
	* Email: Enter your emergency contact's email address.
5. Set your membership dates:
	* Membership Start Date: Enter the date you'd like your membership to start.
	* Membership End Date: Enter the date you'd like your membership to end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Emergency Contact First Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Last Name** (text, optional): Enter the last name of your emergency contact person.
- **Relationship** (select_multiple, optional): Choose how you're related to your emergency contact person (Parent, Child, Spouse, Friend).
- **Address** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
- **City** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's city of residence.
- **State** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's state of residence.
- **Zip** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's zip code.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
- **Email** (email, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
- **Membership Type** (select_one, optional): Select the membership type that best suits your needs (Gym, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
- **Membership Start Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you'd like your membership to start.
- **Membership End Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you'd like your membership to end.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed to ensure a successful membership registration.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with gym staff for assistance.
* This form is designed to gather information necessary for your membership registration and emergency contact details. Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
