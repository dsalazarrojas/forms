# AA Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AA Meeting Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to join an AA meeting. This form helps meeting organizers understand attendees' preferences, needs, and circumstances, ensuring a comfortable and supportive environment for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Personal Information**:
	* Enter your full name as it appears on your legal documents.
	* If you prefer to be known by a different name, enter it in the "Preferred name" field.
	* Provide your date of birth (optional) and age to help meeting staff understand your needs.
2. **Emergency Contact**:
	* Enter the name and phone number of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
3. **Meeting Preferences**:
	* Choose your preferred meeting format (in-person, online, or phone meeting).
	* Select the days you are usually available to attend meetings.
	* Choose your preferred time window for meetings.
4. **Support & Resources**:
	* If you have a sponsor, enter their name.
	* Select any support services you are currently using or interested in.
	* Describe any medical or mental health conditions that may impact your meeting attendance.
5. **Consent & Confidentiality**:
	* Read and agree to abide by meeting guidelines and confidentiality policy.
6. **Additional Comments**:
	* Use this space to share any additional information about yourself that you'd like meeting staff to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your legal full name as it appears on your government-issued documents.
* **Preferred name**: Enter the name you would like others to use when referring to you.
* **Date of birth**: Provide your date of birth for meeting staff to understand your needs.
* **Age**: Enter your age to help meeting staff understand your level of sobriety.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for meeting updates and resources.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for meeting staff to contact you.
* **Mailing address**: Enter your street, city, state, and postal code for meeting materials and resources.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Preferred meeting format**: Choose the format you prefer for regular meetings (in-person, online, or phone).
* **Meeting days**: Select the days you are usually available to attend meetings.
* **Preferred time of day**: Choose the time window you prefer for meetings.
* **Length of continuous sobriety**: If comfortable, share your length of continuous sobriety to help meeting staff understand your needs.
* **Support services**: Select any support services you are currently using or interested in.
* **Medical conditions**: Share any medical or mental health conditions that may impact your meeting attendance.
* **Accessibility needs**: Describe any accessibility needs or accommodations you require.
* **Consent agreement**: Read and agree to abide by meeting guidelines and confidentiality policy.
* **Additional comments**: Use this space to share any additional information about yourself that you'd like meeting staff to know.
