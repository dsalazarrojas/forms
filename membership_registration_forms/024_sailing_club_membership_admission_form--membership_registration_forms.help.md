# Sailing Club Membership Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from prospective members of the sailing club, enabling the club to tailor its services and activities to meet the needs of its members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Indicate your sailing experience by selecting the corresponding answer in the "Sailing Experience" section.
3. Choose your sailing frequency in the "Sailing Frequency" section.
4. Select your membership type in the "Membership Type" section.
5. Provide information about your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" section.
6. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" section.
7. Enter any additional information in the "Additional Information" section.
8. Review and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (id: 1)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, phone number, and email address to help us contact you.
* **Sailing Experience (id: 2)** (`Sailing Experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more of the following options: Yes, No, Less than 2 years, More than 2 years, Not applicable. This will help us understand your level of sailing experience.
* **Sailing Frequency (id: 3)** (`Sailing Frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how often you sail: Once a week, Several times a week, Rarely, Never, Not applicable. This will help us understand your sailing habits.
* **Membership Type (id: 4)** (`Membership Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the following membership types: Individual, Family, Student, Life. This will determine the type of membership you qualify for.
* **Emergency Contact (id: 5)** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name, phone number, and email address of an emergency contact, in case we need to reach out to them.
* **Payment Method (id: 6)** (`Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following payment methods: Credit Card, Cheque, Online Payment, Bank Transfer, Other. This will determine the payment method you prefer to use.
* **Payment Details (id: 7)** (`Payment Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your payment method or preference.
* **Additional Information (id: 8)** (`Additional Information`, `text`, required: false): Provide any other relevant information about yourself or your sailing experience.
* **Terms and Conditions (id: 9)** (`Terms and Conditions`, `note`, required: false): Review and agree to our terms and conditions before submitting the form. 

## Tips
* Please fill in all the required fields to ensure your membership application is complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask our staff for guidance.
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
