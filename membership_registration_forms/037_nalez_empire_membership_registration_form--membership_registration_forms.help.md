# Nalez Empire Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nalez Empire Membership Registration Form is a form designed to gather information for registration as a member of the Nalez Empire.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. For optional fields, fill out as much or as little information as you see fit.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Registration Name** (user_input_name)
* A text field where you can enter your name as it would like to be displayed in the Nalez Empire database.

### 2. **Email** (user_input_email)
* A field to enter your email address for contact purposes.

### 3. **Phone** (user_input_phone)
* Enter your phone number for communication.

### 4. **Membership Plan** (user_input_select_one)
* Select the plan that suits your membership needs: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.

### 5. **Membership Start Date** (user_input_date)
* Enter the date you'd like your membership to start from.

### 6. **Membership End Time** (user_input_time)
* Enter the time you'd like your membership to end.

### 7. **Special Instructions** (user_input_note)
* A field for any special instructions or notes that you'd like to include in your registration.

### 8. **Special Abilities** (user_input_select_multiple)
* Select any special abilities you possess from the list.

### 9. **Username** (user_input_username)
* Enter a unique username that you'd like to use for logging in.

### 10. **Password** (user_input_password)
* Enter a password that you'd like to use for logging in.

### 11. **Confirm Password** (user_input_password_confirm)
* Confirm your password for security purposes.

### 12. **Agreement** (user_input_agreement)
* Select whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the Nalez Empire.

Tips: Please make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting the form. If you encounter any issues, feel free to contact us for help.
