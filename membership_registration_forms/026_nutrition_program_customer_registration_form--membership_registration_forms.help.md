# Nutrition Program Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register customers for the nutrition program, allowing them to access personalized services such as nutrition advice, cooking classes, and healthy food products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your basic contact information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and business name (if applicable).
2. Select the type of program you are interested in, such as nutrition advice, cooking classes, or healthy food products.
3. Provide your location and website (if applicable).
4. Add a description of your interest in the program (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Please enter a valid email address to receive communication from the nutrition program.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Please enter a valid phone number to receive calls from the nutrition program.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_multiple, not required): Please select one or more programs you are interested in:
	+ Nutrition advice
	+ Cooking classes
	+ Healthy food products
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, not required): Please enter your business name (if applicable).
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Please enter your location to help the nutrition program tailor their services to your area.
* **Website** (`website`, text, not required): Please enter your website (if applicable).
* **Description** (`description`, text, not required): Please provide a brief description of your interest in the program (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Select all relevant programs you are interested in to receive comprehensive services.
