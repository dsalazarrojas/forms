# Library Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Membership Registration Form is designed for individuals to register as library members. This form is used by library staff to collect information about new members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name, first and last.
2. Enter your email address and phone number to contact you.
3. Provide your address, city, and zip code to help us send you updates or notifications.
4. If applicable, choose your membership category.
5. Agree to our terms of service.
6. Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
7. Click "Submit" to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Member** (1): Enter your name in the "New Member" field.
* **First Name** (2): Enter your first name or given name.
* **Last Name** (3): Enter your last name or surname.
* **Email** (4): Enter your email address to contact you. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
* **Phone** (5): Enter your phone number to contact you.
* **Address** (6): Enter your address to help us send you updates or notifications.
* **City** (7): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (8): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **Zip** (9): Enter your postal zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (10): Enter your date of birth if you'd like to share it.
* **Membership Category** (11): Choose your membership category if you'd like to be included in our member database.
* **Agreed to Terms of Service** (12): Select "Yes" to agree to our library's terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments** (13): Add any additional information you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in for accurate registration.
* The membership category will help us tailor our services to your needs.
* We'll use your email and phone number to contact you for updates and notifications.
* If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
