# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the membership registration form. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "Club Name" field with the name of your club or organization.
2. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
3. Provide your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your name in the "Contact Name" field.
5. Enter your birth date in the "Birth Date" field.
6. Select your gender from the options provided in the "Gender" field.
7. Choose your membership type from the options provided in the "Membership Type" field.
8. Select your member type from the options provided in the "Member Type" field.
9. Choose your membership level from the options provided in the "Membership Level" field.
10. Enter your join date in the "Join Date" field.
11. Enter your expiration date in the "Expiration Date" field.
12. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
13. Enter the date you've been a member in the "Member Since" field.
14. Select your member status from the options provided in the "Member Status" field.
