# Forum Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forum Membership Form is used to sign up for or make changes to forum membership. Fill out the form with your details to become a registered member or update your membership status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a unique username. This will be your identifier on the forum.
2. Enter a valid email address that you have access to. This will be used for communication and password reset.
3. Enter your name as you wish it to be displayed on the forum.
4. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. Select your membership type: Yes or No.
6. Choose your membership term: Yes or No.
7. If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Username** (text, required): Enter a unique username that you will use to log in to the forum.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that you have access to. This will be used for communication and password reset.
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name as you wish it to be displayed on the forum.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Membership Type** (select_one, required): Select Yes if you wish to be a registered member, No otherwise.
* **Membership Term** (select_multiple, required): Select Yes for a fixed-term membership, No for a non-fixed term membership.
* **Note** (note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here.
