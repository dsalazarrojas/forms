# theater_membership_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to subscribe to the theater membership program. It is essential to provide the required information to complete the membership process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Theater Name** in the first field. This will help us identify which theater you are interested in becoming a member of.
2. Provide your **Email** address. This will be your contact information for future communications and updates.
3. Create a **Password** for your account. This will be used to secure your membership information.
4. Confirm your **Password** by re-entering it in the **Confirm Password** field.
5. Select your preferred **Membership Type** from the dropdown menu:
	* Basic
	* Premium
	* Premium Plus
6. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu:
	* Credit Card
	* PayPal
	* Cash
7. Enter your **Payment Amount**.
8. Select your **Payment Frequency** from the dropdown menu:
	* Monthly
	* Annually
9. Choose a **Membership Date** for your subscription.
10. Select an **Expiration Date** for your membership.
11. Provide a **Note** if you have any specific requests or comments.
12. Enter your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Theater Name**: Enter the name of the theater you are interested in becoming a member of.
* **Email**: Provide your email address for future communications and updates.
* **Password**: Create a secure password for your account.
* **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password to confirm it is correct.
* **Membership Type**: Choose your preferred membership type from the options provided.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay for your membership.
* **Payment Frequency**: Select how often you want to pay for your membership:
	+ Monthly
	+ Annually
* **Membership Date**: Choose a date for your membership to start.
* **Expiration Date**: Select a date for your membership to expire.
* **Note**: Provide any additional comments or requests you have.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
