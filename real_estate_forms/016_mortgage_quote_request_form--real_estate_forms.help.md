# mortgage_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "mortgage_quote_request_form" is a tool used to collect information required to obtain a mortgage quote. This form is designed to gather essential details about the client, property, and loan specifics to provide accurate and personalized quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the "Client Information" page, which includes fields for name, property address, and contact information.
- Proceed to the "property_details" page, where you will input information about the property, including its value, age, and type.
- Next, move on to the "Loan Information" page, where you will provide details about the loan, such as term, interest rate, and amount.
- Continue to the "Employment" page, where you will input information about your employment status, income, and employment type.
- On the "Employment Length" page, input the length of your employment and your employment verification information.
- Finally, review the "Notes" page for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required): Provide your name and contact information.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required): Enter your contact details for follow-up purposes.
### Property Details
* **Property Details** (`property_info`, text, required): Describe the property you are seeking to mortgage.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of property (Single Family, Multi Family, Condo, Townhouse, Duplex).
* **Property Age** (`property_age`, time, required): Enter the age of the property.
### Loan Information
* **Loan Information** (`loan_info`, text, required): Describe the loan you are seeking.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, number, required): Enter the term of the loan.
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, number, required): Enter the interest rate for the loan.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required): Enter the loan amount.
### Employment
* **Employment Status** (`employment_info`, text, required): Describe your employment status.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your employment type (Salary, Hourly, Freelance, Commission, Contract).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter your years of experience.
### Employment Verification
* **Employment Verification** (`employment_verification`, text, required): Provide employment verification information.
### Credit Information
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, required): Enter your credit score.
* **Credit Score Rating** (`credit_score_rating`, number, required): Enter your credit score rating.
### Income
* **Income** (`income`, text, required): Enter your income.
* **Income Range** (`income_range`, select_multiple, required): Select your income range (Low, Medium, High).
### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or information about your loan request.
