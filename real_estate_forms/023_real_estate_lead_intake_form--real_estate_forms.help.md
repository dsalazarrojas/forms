# Real Estate Lead Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage information about real estate leads. Please fill out this form carefully to provide essential details about the lead, making it easier for the real estate team to follow up on the lead and ensure a smooth conversion process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked with *) as best as possible.
2. Be as accurate as possible when entering the lead's contact information.
3. Choose the correct lead type (Residential, Commercial, or Land) that best fits the lead's purpose.
4. Select the appropriate listing agent (Jimmy, Jane, or John) who will handle the lead.
5. Update the lead status to track its progress (New, In Progress, or Closed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Name** (`lead_name`, text, optional): Enter the lead's name as it's known to them.
* **Lead Address** (`lead_address`, text, optional): Enter the lead's address for reference.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the lead's contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the lead's contact phone number.
* **Lead Message** (`lead_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the lead.
* **Lead Type** (`lead_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more lead types (Residential, Commercial, or Land) that best fits the lead.
* **Realty Name** (`realty_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the realty company or agent associated with the lead.
* **Realty Address** (`realty_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the realty company or agent.
* **Listing Agent** (`listing_agent`, select_one, optional): Select the listing agent (Jimmy, Jane, or John) who will handle the lead.
* **Listing Agent Phone** (`listing_agent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the listing agent's phone number (not required for now).
* **Listing Agent Email** (`listing_agent_email`, email, optional): Enter the listing agent's email address (not required for now).
* **Lead Notes** (`lead_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the lead.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_one, optional): Select the lead status (New, In Progress, or Closed) to update the lead's progression.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, text, optional): Enter where the lead originated (not required for now).

## Tips
* Fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to help the real estate team track and convert the lead.
* Review and update the lead status regularly to ensure a smooth conversion process.
* Use the lead type and agent selection to ensure the lead is properly managed and assigned.
