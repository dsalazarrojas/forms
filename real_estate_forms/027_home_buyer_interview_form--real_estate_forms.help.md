# Home Buyer Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a home buyer's preferences and needs to help real estate agents or property managers understand the buyer's requirements for their next property purchase.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Buyer's Preferences** section with your personal information and preferences.
2. Enter your **Budget** in the corresponding field.
3. Set your **Budget Max** limit.
4. Select the type of **Property Details** you are looking for.
5. Provide your **Contact Info**.
6. Select multiple **Property Type(s)** you are interested in.
7. Choose multiple **Desired Area(s)** you are interested in buying in.
8. Select multiple **Preferred Features** you are looking for in a property.
9. Ignore the **Property Type (again)** and **Desired Area (again)** sections as they are duplicates.
10. Determine a desired closing date or move-in date for the property in the **Date** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer's Preferences** (required): This section is used to collect the buyer's personal information and preferences regarding their home buying experience.
* **Budget** (not required): This field asks for the buyer's budget for the new property.
* **Budget Max** (required): This field is used to determine the maximum budget for the buyer.
* **Property Details** (required): This field provides an option to select the type of property the buyer is looking for.
* **Contact Info** (required): This section asks for the buyer's contact information.
* **Property Type** (required): This field allows the buyer to select multiple types of properties they are interested in.
* **Desired Area** (required): This field enables the buyer to select multiple areas they are interested in buying in.
* **Preferred Features** (required): This field lets the buyer select multiple features they are looking for in a property.
* **Date** (required): This field might be used to determine a desired closing date or move-in date for the property.
