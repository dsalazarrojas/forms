# Contract To Close - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contract To Close form is used to collect essential details about a real estate transaction, including buyer and seller information, property details, financing, and closing terms. This form helps real estate professionals manage the closing process efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for both the buyer and seller.
2. Select the correct property type from the options provided.
3. Enter the purchase price, earnest money, and closing date.
4. Choose the financing type and lender information (if applicable).
5. Check the appraisal and inspection contingencies.
6. Enter the closing location and title company information.
7. Provide details about homeowners insurance and insurance agent (if applicable).
8. Add any additional terms or contingencies.
9. Review and submit the form for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the buyer.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, email, required): Enter the buyer's contact email address.
* **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, text, required): Enter the buyer's contact phone number.
* **Seller Name** (`seller_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the seller.
* **Seller Email** (`seller_email`, email, required): Enter the seller's contact email address.
* **Seller Phone** (`seller_phone`, text, required): Enter the seller's contact phone number.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property from the options provided (e.g., Single family home, Condominium, etc.).
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, required): Enter the sale amount of the property.
* **Earnest Money** (`earnest_money`, number, required): Enter the deposit amount.
* **Closing Date** (`closing_date`, date, required): Enter the date of closing.
* **Closing Location** (`closing_location`, text, required): Enter the location of closing.
* **Financing Type** (`financing_type`, select_one, required): Select the financing type from the options provided (e.g., Conventional loan, FHA loan, etc.).
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, optional): Enter the mortgage amount (if applicable).
* **Lender Name** (`lender_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the lender (if applicable).
* **Loan Officer** (`loan_officer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the loan officer (if applicable).
* **Loan Officer Phone** (`loan_officer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the loan officer (if applicable).
* **Appraisal Contingency** (`appraisal_contingency`, select_one, required): Check if an appraisal is required.
* **Inspection Contingency** (`inspection_contingency`, select_one, required): Check if inspection is required.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of inspection (if applicable).
* **Title Company** (`title_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the title company.
* **Title Agent** (`title_agent`, text, optional): Enter the name of the title agent.
* **Homeowners Insurance** (`homeowners_insurance`, text, optional): Enter the name of the insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Insurance Agent** (`insurance_agent`, text, optional): Enter the name of the insurance agent (if applicable).
* **Proration Date** (`proration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of proration (if applicable).
* **Personal Property Included** (`personal_property_included`, text, optional): Enter any personal property included with the sale (if applicable).
* **Repairs Required** (`repairs_required`, text, optional): Enter any repairs required (if applicable).
* **Repair Credit** (`repair_credit`, number, optional): Enter the credit amount for any repairs (if applicable).
* **Closing Costs Buyer** (`closing_costs_buyer`, number, optional): Enter the buyer's closing costs.
* **Closing Costs Seller** (`closing_costs_seller`, number, optional): Enter the seller's closing costs.
* **Special Assessments** (`special_assessments`, text, optional): Enter any special assessments (if applicable).
* **Occupancy Date** (`occupancy_date`, date, required): Enter the date the buyer can occupy the property.
* **Possession Terms** (`possession_terms`, text, optional): Enter any special terms regarding possession (if applicable).
* **Buyer Agent Name** (`buyer_agent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the buyer's real estate agent.
* **Buyer Agent Phone** (`buyer_agent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the buyer's real estate agent.
* **Seller Agent Name** (`seller_agent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the seller's real estate agent.
* **Seller Agent Phone** (`seller_agent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the seller's real estate agent.
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the closing attorney.
* **Additional Terms** (`additional_terms`, text, optional): Enter any special conditions or terms (if applicable).
* **Contingencies List** (`contingencies_list`, text, optional): Enter any contingencies (if applicable).
