# real_estate_documentation_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_documentation_order_form is a template for documenting the details of a real estate transaction, providing a comprehensive overview of the process and required fields.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in your buyer information on **Page 1** of the form.
2.  Proceed to **Page 2** and enter details about the property, such as its current condition, location, and other relevant information.
3.  On **Page 3**, review and complete the contract details.
4.  On **Page 4**, provide information related to the loan and any other documents required for the transaction.
5.  On **Page 5**, list any additional documents that may be needed for the transaction.
6.  On **Page 6**, select the buyers who will be involved in the transaction.
7.  Enter information about the realtor on **Page 7**, including their contact details and any other relevant information.
8.  On **Page 8**, enter the realtor's address, ensuring accuracy and completeness.
9.  Complete the realtor's phone number on **Page 9**.
10. Enter the realtor's email address on **Page 10**.
11. On **Page 11**, select the state for which the realtor is licensed.
12. Enter any notes about the realtor on **Page 12**.
13. Finally, include the realtor's signature on **Page 13**, ensuring it is accurate and legible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Info** (`buyer_info`, text, required: false):
    + Enter your name and contact details as the buyer.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, required: false):
    + Describe the property, including its location, condition, and any other relevant details.
* **Contract Review** (`contract_review`, text, required: false):
    + Review and document the terms of the contract.
* **Loan Info** (`loan_info`, text, required: false):
    + Enter information related to the loan and any other relevant documents.
* **Other Documents** (`other_documents`, text, required: false):
    + List any other documents required for the transaction.
* **Buyer Contact** (`buyer_contact`, select_multiple, required: false):
    + Select all the buyers involved in the transaction.
* **Realtor Info** (`realtor_info`, text, required: false):
    + Enter your contact details as the realtor.
* **Realtor Address** (`realtor_address`, text, required: false):
    + Enter your address as the realtor.
* **Realtor Phone** (`realtor_phone`, number, required: false):
    + Enter your phone number as the realtor.
* **Realtor Email** (`realtor_email`, email, required: false):
    + Enter your email address as the realtor.
* **Realtor State** (`realtor_state`, select_one, required: false):
    + Select the state for which you are licensed.
* **Realtor Notes** (`realtor_notes`, text, required: false):
    + Enter any additional notes about yourself as the realtor.
* **Realtor Signature** (`realtor_signature`, text, required: false):
    + Include a legible signature as the realtor.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it for final review and approval.
