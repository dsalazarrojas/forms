# real_estate_visitor_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_visitor_check_out_form is used to collect information from visitors to a real estate property. This form is used to track visitors, their purpose of visit, and the property being visited.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields. Start by entering your:
* First Name
* Last Name
* Contact Number
* Date Visit
* Time Arrival
* Time Depart
* Property ID
* Property Address
* Property Status
* Property Type
* Property Area
* Property Price Range
* Property Features
* Contact Request (whether or not to contact you)
* Follow-up frequency
* Assigned Agent

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1) (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2) (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (3) (`Company`, text, optional): If you are visiting the property on behalf of a company, enter the company name.
* **Job Title** (4) (`Job Title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Contact Number** (5) (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (6) (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date Visit** (7) (`Date Visit`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time Arrival** (8) (`Time Arrival`, time, required): Enter the time of your arrival.
* **Time Depart** (9) (`Time Depart`, time, required): Enter the time of your departure.
* **Purpose Visit** (10) (`Purpose Visit`, select_one, required): Select the reason for your visit from the options provided.
* **Feedback** (11) (`Feedback`, note, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the property.
* **Contact Method** (12) (`Contact Method`, select_multiple, required): Select the method by which you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, In-person).
* **Follow-up** (13) (`Follow-up`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of follow-up contact.
* **Notes** (14) (`Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the visit.
* **Assigned Agent** (15) (`Assigned Agent`, text, required): Enter the name of the assigned agent.
* **Comments** (16) (`Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the visit.
* **Additional Comments** (17) (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the visit.
* **Property ID** (18) (`Property ID`, text, required): Enter the ID of the property being visited.
* **Property Address** (19) (`Property Address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property being visited.
* **Property Status** (20) (`Property Status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the property (Active, Inactive, Pending, Sold).
* **Property Type** (21) (`Property Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property (Commercial, Residential, Industrial).
* **Property Area** (22) (`Property Area`, select_one, required): Select the area of the property (North, East, South, West).
* **Property Price Range** (23) (`Property Price Range`, select_multiple, required): Select the price range of the property ($0 - $100k, $100k - $200k, $200k - $300k, $300k - $500k, $500k - $1M).
* **Property Features** (24) (`Property Features`, select_multiple, required): Select the features of the property (Pool, Gym, Parking, Fenced, View).
* **Contact Request** (25) (`Contact Request`, select_one, required): Select whether or not to contact you.
