# real_estate_open_house_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_open_house_checklist_form is a tool used to organize and track the progress of open house events. This form helps capture details related to the event, the visitors, and the tasks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by selecting the "Open House Details" page.
2. Fill in the required and optional details as they pertain to the open house event.
3. Move on to the "Open House Visitors" page to record the visitors' data.
4. On the "Open House Tasks" page, document any tasks or activities related to the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Open House Details**
	+ **Open House Details** (`Open House Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the open house event, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Open House Visitors**
	+ **Open House Visitors** (`Open House Visitors`, `text`, required/optional): Record the number of visitors or a list of attendees for the event.
* **Open House Tasks**
	+ **Open House Tasks** (`Open House Tasks`, `text`, required/optional): Note down any tasks or activities that occurred during the event, such as maintenance tasks, vendor interactions, or other notable occurrences.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in the Open House Details section accurately and thoroughly.
- For the Open House Visitors section, you can estimate the number of visitors or list individual attendees.
- In the Open House Tasks section, be as detailed as possible when recording activities.
- This form is a general-purpose guide; if you need to add or modify fields for your specific use case, please feel free to do so.
