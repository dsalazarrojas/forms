# Rental Application Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Application Interest Form is a tool used to gather information from users about their interest in renting a property. This information will be used to determine the user's suitability for the property and to contact them about their interest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page with your general contact information.
2. On the second page, provide specific details about the rental property you are interested in, including the address, type of property, and lease duration.
3. On the third page, select one of the options provided.
4. On the fourth page, select all the amenities or features that you are interested in having in the rental property.
5. On the fifth page, enter the date and time you are interested in visiting the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name, Phone Number and Email (contact-info)**: This field is used to gather your contact information.
* **Rental Address, Property Type and Lease Duration (rental-property-details)**: This field is used to gather specific details about the rental property you are interested in.
* **Select One (rental-application-interest-form)**: This is likely where you can select your level of interest or preference regarding the property.
* **Select Multiple (rental-application-interest-form)**: This field allows you to select multiple options from a list, possibly regarding amenities or features.
* **Date and Time (date-of-interest)**: Enter the date and time you are interested in visiting the property.
* **Additional Comments (additional-comments)**: This field is available for you to enter any additional information or comments about your interest in the property.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, click the submit button to send your interest application.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your interest is properly recorded.
* Be as specific as possible when providing details about the rental property you are interested in.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more information.
