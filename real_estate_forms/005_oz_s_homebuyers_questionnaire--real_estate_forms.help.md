# OZs_Homebuyers_Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help real estate agents understand your preferences and needs for a new property. Please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure that you receive personalized recommendations for your dream home.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read each question and choose the best answer for you.
2. Fill in the answers to all the questions, even if you're not sure about some of the options.
3. Be honest about your needs and preferences.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Common Interest**: A number that indicates the level of interest you have for a particular property feature.
* **Property-Type**: The type of property you are looking for (Single Family Home, Townhouse, Condo, Land, or Other).
* **Price-Range**: The price range of the property you are looking for.
* **Desired-Area-Size**: The size of property you are looking for in square feet.
* **Number-of-Bedrooms**: The number of bedrooms you need in your new home.
* **Number-of-Bathrooms**: The number of bathrooms you need in your new home.
* **Lot-Size-in-Acres**: The size of the property lot in acres.
* **Year-Built**: The year the property was built.
* **Year-Renovated**: The year the property was renovated.
* **Square-Footage**: The total square footage of the property.
* **Number-of-Stories**: The number of stories of the property.
* **Lot-Size-in-Feet**: The size of the property lot in feet.
* **Zoning**: The zoning designation of the property.
* **Flood-Zone**: The flood zone designation of the property.
* **Property-Status**: The status of the property (Active, Pending, Under Contract, or Sold).
* **HOA-Fees**: The monthly HOA fees for the property.
* **Annual-Property-Taxes**: The annual property taxes for the property.
* **Property-Type**: The type of property you are looking for (Single Family Home, Townhouse, Condo, Land, or Other).
* **Common-Area-Shared**: Whether the property has common areas shared with other units.
* **HOA-Fees**: The monthly HOA fees for the property.
* **HOA-Pets**: Whether pets are allowed in the property.
* **HOA-Parking**: Whether parking is allowed in the property.
* **HOA-Outdoor-Amenities**: Whether outdoor amenities are allowed in the property.
* **HOA-In-Unit-Amenities**: Whether in-unit amenities are allowed in the property.
* **HOA-Outdoor-Security-Features**: Whether outdoor security features are allowed in the property.
