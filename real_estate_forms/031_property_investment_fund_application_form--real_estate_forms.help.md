# Property Investment Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for investors to provide information about their investment preferences, experience, and contact details for the property investment fund.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the investor information section with your details. 
- Select your investment experience from the options provided. 
- Describe your investment objectives and frequency. 
- Indicate your risk tolerance level. 
- Provide your return expectation. 
- Enter your contact information, including phone and address. 
- Confirm your signature and date. 
- Finally, review and acknowledge the terms of service before submitting your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Investor Info**: Provide your name, email, and contact details. (Text)
- **Investor Investment Experience**: Select your experience level as an investor. (Select One: Investment Firm, Financial Advisor, or Investor)
- **Investment Property Details**: Describe the type of property you're interested in investing in. (Text)
- **Investor Investment Experience**: Describe your investment experience. (Text)
- **Investment Objectives**: Describe your investment objectives. (Text)
- **Investment Amount**: Enter the amount you're looking to invest. (Number)
- **Investment Frequency**: Select how often you plan to invest. (Select Multiple: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually)
- **Investment Term**: Enter the duration of your investment. (Time)
- **Exit Strategy**: Describe your exit strategy. (Text)
- **Risk Tolerance**: Select your risk tolerance level. (Select One: Low, Medium, High, or Very High)
- **Return Expectation**: Enter your expected return on investment. (Number)
- **Contact Info**: Enter your phone number and address. (Text, Text)
- **Email**: Enter your email address. (Email)
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number. (Text)
- **City**: Enter the city where you reside. (Text)
- **State**: Enter the state where you reside. (Text)
- **Country**: Enter the country where you reside. (Text)
- **Zip**: Enter your zip code. (Text)
- **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about your investment preferences. (Text)
- **Signature**: Sign your name. (Text)
- **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign. (Date)
- **Confirm Signature**: Confirm that you've signed the form. (Select One: True, False)
- **Terms of Service**: Review and acknowledge the terms of service. (Text)
- **Submit**: Confirm your application submission. (Select One: Submit, Cancel)
