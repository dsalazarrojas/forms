# Property Investment Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for investors to provide information about their investment preferences, experience, and contact details for the property investment fund.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the investor information section with your details. 
- Select your investment experience from the options provided. 
- Describe your investment objectives and frequency. 
- Indicate your risk tolerance level. 
- Provide your return expectation. 
- Enter your contact information, including phone and address. 
- Confirm your signature and date. 
- Finally, review and acknowledge the terms of service before submitting your application.
