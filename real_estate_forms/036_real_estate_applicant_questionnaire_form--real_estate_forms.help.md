# Real Estate Applicant Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from applicants interested in real estate, whether it's for renting or buying. The data collected will help real estate agents or other relevant parties understand the applicant's background, financial status, and preferences, aiding them in providing the best possible services and matching them with suitable properties.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you answer every question.
2. Select the applicable options for each field, paying attention to the labels and hints provided.
3. Enter numerical values accurately, considering the options listed or the expected format.
4. Fill out each section fully before proceeding to the next, ensuring consistent and comprehensive data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Home Status (Page 1)
* **Home Status**: This field requires a selection that reflects your current situation regarding your residence status. Options include Primary residence, Second home, Investment, Rental, and Other.
* Choose the one that best describes your current situation.

### Employment Status (Page 1)
* **Employment Status**: Select the option that best describes your employment status. Options include Employed, Self employed, Unemployed, and Retired.
* Choose the one that accurately reflects your employment status.

### Annual Income (Page 2)
* **Annual Income**: This field is for entering your annual income.
* Please enter the correct numerical value as it applies to your annual income.

### Monthly Expenses (Page 2)
* **Monthly Expenses**: This field is for entering your monthly expenses.
* Enter the numerical value accurately to reflect your monthly expenses.

### Credit Score (Page 3)
* **Credit Score**: This field requires selecting your credit score from the provided options.
* Choose the range that best describes your credit score.

### Years in Housing (Page 4)
* **Years in Housing**: This field is for entering the number of years you've been in your current housing.
* Enter the correct numerical value.

### Previous Housing History (Page 4)
* **Previous Housing History**: Select the option that best describes your previous housing situation.
* Choose the one that accurately reflects your housing history.

### Current Residence (Page 5)
* **Current Residence**: This field is for entering your current place of residence.
* Enter the text as it applies to your current residence.

### Desired Residence Type (Page 5)
* **Desired Residence Type**: Select the type of residence you are interested in.
* Choose the one that best matches your needs.

### Years of Experience (Page 6)
* **Years of Experience**: This field is for entering the number of years you've gained experience in a particular field.
* Enter the correct numerical value.

### Years of Education (Page 6)
* **Years of Education**: This field is for entering the number of years you've had education.
* Enter the correct numerical value.

### Previous Real Estate Experience (Page 7)
* **Previous Real Estate Experience**: Select whether you have previous real estate experience.
* Choose the one that best describes your experience.

### Real Estate Agent (Page 7)
* **Real Estate Agent**: This field is for entering the name of your real estate agent (if you have one).
* Enter the text as it applies to your real estate agent.

### Contact Method (Page 8)
* **Contact Method**: Select the method you prefer to contact you.
* Choose the one that best suits your preferences.

### Preferred Contact Time (Page 8)
* **Preferred Contact Time**: Select the time of day you prefer to be contacted.
* Choose the one that best suits your schedule.

By following these steps and explanations for each field, you should be able to fill out the Real Estate Applicant Questionnaire Form accurately and effectively.
