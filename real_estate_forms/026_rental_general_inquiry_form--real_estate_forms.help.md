# Rental General Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental General Inquiry Form is designed to collect relevant information from potential renters, property owners, or other interested parties about a rental property. This form serves as a means to gather essential details for inquiries related to renting or buying a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
3. Provide your phone number so we can reach you via phone.
4. Write a brief message about your inquiry.
5. Select the type of property you are interested in (e.g., Studio, 1-3, 4+ Bedrooms).
6. Choose the property status (e.g., For Rent, Leased, Sold, Coming Soon).
7. Enter your desired move-in date.
8. If applicable, choose your preferred move-in time.
9. Describe any additional details about the property or your requirements.
10. Enter the property's address, city, state, zip, and country.
11. Select your preferred method of contact (e.g., Email, Phone, Phone & Email).
12. Choose your preferred language for communication.
13. Select your preferred contact time (e.g., Anytime, Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
14. Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., Call, Email, Text, Video Call).
15. Finally, agree to our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (form_first_name): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (form_last_name): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (form_email): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (form_phone): Provide your phone number for phone contact.
* **Message** (form_message): Briefly describe your inquiry or interest in the property.
* **Property Type** (form_property_type): Select the type of property you are interested in (e.g., Studio, 1-3, 4+ Bedrooms).
* **Property Status** (form_property_status): Choose the property status (e.g., For Rent, Leased, Sold, Coming Soon).
* **Move In Date** (form_move_in_date): Enter your desired move-in date.
* **Move In Time** (form_move_in_time): If applicable, choose your preferred move-in time.
* **Additional Details** (form_additional_details): Describe any additional details about the property or your requirements.
* **Property Address** (form_property_address): Enter the property's address.
* **Property City** (form_property_city): Enter the property's city.
* **Property State** (form_property_state): Enter the property's state.
* **Property Zip** (form_property_zip): Enter the property's zip code.
* **Property Country** (form_property_country): Enter the property's country.
* **Property Province** (form_property_province): This field is optional and may not apply in all regions.
* **Property Neighborhood** (form_property_neighborhood): This field is optional and may not apply in all regions.
* **Property Postal Code** (form_property_postal_code): This field is optional and may not apply in all regions.
* **Property Region** (form_property_region): This field is optional and may not apply in all regions.
* **Property Area** (form_property_area): This field is optional and may not apply in all regions.
* **Preferred Method of Contact** (form_preferred_method_of_contact): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, Phone & Email).
* **Language** (form_language): Select your preferred language for communication (e.g., English, Spanish, Other).
* **Preferred Contact Time** (form_preferred_contact_time): Choose your preferred time for contact (e.g., Anytime, Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (form_preferred_contact_method): Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g., Call, Email, Text, Video Call).
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (form_agree_terms_and_conditions): Accept our terms and conditions to proceed.

Note: Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
