# Subcontractor Prequalification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information from potential subcontractors to evaluate their qualification to work on specific projects. This form helps ensure that the subcontractor meets the required standards and qualifications to be considered for project participation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field to the best of your abilities.
2. Be sure to review the form thoroughly before submitting.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subcontractor Info (`subcontractor_info`):**
	+ Enter the name or identifier of the subcontractor (e.g., John Doe or Company X).
* **Project Info (`project_info`):**
	+ Briefly describe the project you are applying for.
* **Contact Info (`contact_info`):**
	+ Enter the email address or phone number of the subcontractor's contact person.
* **Phone Number (`phone_number`):**
	+ Enter the subcontractor's phone number.
* **Experience (`experience`):**
	+ Provide a brief explanation of your relevant experience and qualifications.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are not necessary for the form submission, but it is still recommended to fill them out for a more comprehensive review.
