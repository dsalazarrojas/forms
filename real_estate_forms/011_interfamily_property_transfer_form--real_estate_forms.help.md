# Interfamily Property Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interfamily Property Transfer Form is a document used to record the transfer of property between family members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter the name of the donor and donee, their addresses, and the date of the transfer.
3. Provide the consideration (amount) for the transfer, if applicable.
4. Include property details, such as its current status and any relevant documentation.
5. Document the signatures of all parties involved, including the donor, donee, and any witnesses.
6. Enter the signature date and time, and the location where the signatures were obtained.
7. Enter any contact information for the donor and donee, such as email and phone numbers.
8. Complete any additional fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Members** (`family_transfer`): This section is for general information about the family members involved in the transfer.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`): Enter the name of the person transferring the property.
* **Donor Date of Birth** (`donor_date_of_birth`): Enter the date of birth of the person transferring the property.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`): Enter the address of the person transferring the property.
* **Donee Name** (`donee_name`): Enter the name of the person receiving the property.
* **Donee Address** (`donee_address`): Enter the address of the person receiving the property.
* **Consideration** (`consideration`): Enter the amount of consideration (value) of the property transfer, if applicable.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`): Provide any additional information about the property being transferred.
* **Date of Transfer** (`date_transfer`): Enter the date of the property transfer.
* **Transfer Document** (`transfer_document`): Attach or describe the document related to the property transfer.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`): Check 'Yes' if there are signatures from all parties involved; otherwise, check 'No'.
* **Donor's Signature** (`signature_1`): Enter the donor's signature for the transfer.
* **Donee's Signature** (`signature_2`): Enter the donee's signature for the transfer.
* **Witness 1 Signature** (`signature_3`): Enter the signature of the first witness to the transfer.
* **Witness 2 Signature** (`signature_4`): Enter the signature of the second witness to the transfer.
* **Witness Signatures** (`signatures`): Check 'Yes' if there are signatures from all witnesses; otherwise, check 'No'.
* **Signature Date** (`sign_date`): Enter the date the signatures were obtained.
* **Signature Date and Time** (`sign_date_time`): Enter the date and time the signatures were obtained.
* **Signature Time** (`sign_time`): Enter the time the signatures were obtained.
* **Signature Location** (`sign_location`): Enter the location where the signatures were obtained.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email of the donor or donee.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the donor or donee.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the donor or donee.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city of the donor or donee.
* **Country** (`country`): Enter the country of the donor or donee.
