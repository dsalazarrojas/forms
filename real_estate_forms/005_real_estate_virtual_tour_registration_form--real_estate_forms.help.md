The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in scheduling a virtual tour of a property. This form is likely used by real estate agents or agencies to gather contact and appointment information from potential clients. To prevent duplicate registrations, this form should be filled out only once per individual.

The form collects the following information:

* First name (required)
* Last name (required)
* Email address (required)
* Phone number (required)
* Appointment date and time (required)
* Property address (required)
* Type of property (required)
* Availability (required)
* Real estate agency (required, options: JotForm, Other, Real Estate Agency)
* Real estate license(s) (required, multiple options)
* Message (required)

The form is designed to be completed by one person per submission. If multiple people want to schedule a virtual tour together, they should fill out separate forms. 

This guide will walk you through how to complete this form.

## Purpose
The Real Estate Virtual Tour Registration Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in scheduling a virtual tour of a property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select a date and time for the appointment in the Appointment field.
6. Enter the property address in the Property Address field.
7. Select the type of property in the Property Type field.
8. Choose your availability in the Availability field.
9. Select your real estate agency from the options in the Real Estate Agency field.
10. Choose your real estate license(s) from the options in the Real Estate License field.
11. Enter any additional message or comments in the Message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Appointment**: Select a date and time for the appointment.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property you are interested in.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (e.g. residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Availability**: Choose your availability (e.g. morning, afternoon, etc.).
* **Real Estate Agency**: Select your real estate agency from the options.
* **Real Estate License**: Choose your real estate license(s) from the options.
* **Message**: Enter any additional comments or messages.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out the form correctly before submitting it.
* If you are having trouble with any of the fields, contact your real estate agent or agency for assistance.
* If you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment, contact the real estate agent or agency directly.
