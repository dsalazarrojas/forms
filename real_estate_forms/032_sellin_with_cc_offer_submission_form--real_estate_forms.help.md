# Sellin With CC Offer Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for selling listings using CC. Sellers need to fill out this form with their offer details to trigger internal actions and notifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary approvals before filling this form.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. If a field is marked as required, please fill it in completely.
4. Review your submission before clicking the submit button to confirm everything is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **seller name** (seller name, text, required): Enter the name of the seller who is offering the listing.
* **listing id** (listing id, number, required): Type in the specific id of the listing you are offering.
* **offer amount** (offer amount, number, required): Enter the amount of the offer you are providing.
* **offer date/time** (offer date/time, date, required): Choose the date and time of your offer.
* **offer terms** (offer terms, note, optional): If you have any conditions or terms for your offer, enter them here.
* **contact number** (contact number, text, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **buyer message** (buyer message, text, required): Enter any message for the buyer, e.g. the reasons for your offer.
* **cc agent name** (cc agent name, text, required): Enter the name of the CC agent who should be informed about your offer.
* **cc agent email** (cc agent email, email, required): Enter the email of the CC agent who should be informed about your offer.
* **cc agent phone** (cc agent phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the CC agent who should be informed about your offer.
* **submit** (submit, select one, required): Confirm that you have filled out this form accurately and truthfully.

## Tips

* Please double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact your CC representative.
* Review your submission carefully before sending it in.
* Ensure you have necessary approvals to submit this form.
