## Purpose
The Resident Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback from residents about their experience with a particular property or service. This form helps us understand their concerns, suggestions, and overall satisfaction with their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the first field. This is a required field to help us identify who is submitting the feedback.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field. This is also a required field as we need to contact you if we need more information.
3. In the third field, provide your **Feedback** about your stay. This can be as short or as detailed as you like, and it will help us understand your concerns and suggestions.
4. On the second **Page**, you will see another **Feedback** field where you can add more details if needed.
5. In the fourth field, rate your **Satisfaction** with your stay on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 means "Very Satisfied".
6. Finally, rate your **Overall Satisfaction** with your stay on a scale of 1-5, similar to the previous field.

### Name
**Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.

### Email
**Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.

### Feedback (Page 1)
**Feedback** (Text, Required): Provide your feedback about your stay. Be as detailed as you like, and we'll take it into consideration.

### Feedback (Page 2)
**Feedback** (Text, Required): Add any additional feedback you may have about your stay.

### Satisfaction
**Satisfaction** (Number, Required): Rate your satisfaction with your stay on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 means "Very Satisfied".

### Overall Satisfaction
**Overall Satisfaction** (Number, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your stay on a scale of 1-5, similar to the previous field.
