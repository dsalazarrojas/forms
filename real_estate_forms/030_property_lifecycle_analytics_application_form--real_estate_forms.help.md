# Property Lifecycle Analytics Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data about property lifecycle analytics applications. It will help you understand how properties are being used or managed throughout their lifecycle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Select the correct options for each field.
* Provide the required information in the corresponding fields.
* Click the "Submit" button to save your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Lifecycle Analytics Application Form** (`property_lifecycle_analytics_application_form`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to apply lifecycle analytics to a property, "No" otherwise.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you want to contact someone regarding the property, "No" otherwise.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company related to the property.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the property is of type, "No" otherwise.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property  Location** (`property_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the property.
* **Property  Status** (`property_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Active" if the property is active, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Property  Goals** (`property_goal`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the property has goals, "No" otherwise.
* **Application  Reason** (`application_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for applying lifecycle analytics to the property.
* **Property  Details** (`property_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional details about the property.
* **Contact  Notes** (`contact_notes`, note, optional): Enter any contact notes about the property. 

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about the property.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your administrator for guidance.
* This form is for data collection only, and your answers will help improve property management processes.
