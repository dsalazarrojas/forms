# Real Estate Flood Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the property's flood risk and disclosure status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property's address in the "Property Address" field.
2. Check the "Flood Zone" field to indicate if the property is in a flood zone.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Flood Insurance" field to indicate if the property has flood insurance.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Flood Certificate" field to indicate if the property has a flood elevation certificate.
5. If applicable, enter the date you moved in and/or moved out of the property in the "Date Moved In" and "Date Moved Out" fields.
6. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Flood Disclosure**: Enter a brief statement describing the property's flood risk and disclosure status. (text, required: false)
- **Property Address**: Enter the property's address in the format "123 Main St, Anytown, USA". (text, required: false)
- **Flood Zone**: Check "Yes" if the property is in a flood zone, "No" otherwise. (select_multiple, required: false)
- **Date Moved In**: Enter the date you moved into the property in the format MM/DD/YYYY. (date, required: false)
- **Date Moved Out**: Enter the date you moved out of the property in the format MM/DD/YYYY. (date, required: false)
- **Flood Elevation Certificate**: Note any information about the property's flood elevation certificate. (note, required: false)
- **Flood Insurance**: Check "Yes" if the property has flood insurance, "No" otherwise. (select_multiple, required: false)
- **Flood Certificate**: Check "Yes" if the property has a flood elevation certificate, "No" otherwise. (select_multiple, required: false)
