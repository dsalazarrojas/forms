# Routine Inspection Property Management AUS - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Routine Inspection Property Management AUS form is used to document the routine inspection of a property in Australia. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected and recorded for efficient property management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field, if applicable.
2. Enter all required information in the designated fields.
3. Complete the form in collaboration with the tenant and property owner, as required.
4. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Routine Inspection Property Management AUS** (`routine_inspection_property_management_aus`, text, not required): Enter a title or description of the property management activity.
* **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, text, not required): Provide details about the tenant, including their name, contact information, or other relevant details.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, not required): Enter information about the property, such as its address, location, or other relevant details.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, not required): Record the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, not required): If the tenant has signed the form, capture their signature here.
* **Property Owner Signature** (`property_owner_signature`, text, not required): If the property owner has signed the form, capture their signature here.
* **Assigned Inspector** (`assigned_inspector`, text, not required): Enter the name of the assigned inspector, if applicable.
* **Property Inspector Status** (`property_inspector_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the inspection (Not Started, Ongoing, Completed).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments or feedback about the inspection.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form, if applicable.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, not required): Record the date the form was submitted.
* **Property Inspector Signature** (`property_inspector_signature`, text, not required): If the property inspector has signed the form, capture their signature here.
