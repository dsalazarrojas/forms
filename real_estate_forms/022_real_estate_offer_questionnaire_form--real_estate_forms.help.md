# Real Estate Offer Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about real estate offers, including contact details, offer specifics, and property information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, contact method (yes/no), and contact name.
2. Enter your contact email (if applicable).
3. Provide your contact phone number (if applicable).
4. Enter the contact message (if applicable).
5. Enter the property address.
6. Specify the offer amount.
7. Indicate the payment terms (yes/no).
8. Enter any additional terms or conditions.
9. Select the contact method(s) of interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your name and other relevant contact details.
* **Offer Details** (`offer_details`, text, required): Describe the offer you are making, including any specific terms or conditions.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, required): Provide details about the property, such as its address.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Indicate your preferred contact method (yes/no).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization making the offer.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or messages.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property being offered.
* **Offer Amount** (`offer_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the offer.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the payment terms (yes/no).
* **Additional Terms** (`additional_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions.
* **Contact Date** (`contact_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the contact.
* **Contact Time** (`contact_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of contact.
* **Contact Method 1** (`contact_method_1`, select_one, optional): Indicate another preferred contact method (yes/no).
* **Contact Method 2** (`contact_method_2`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate any other additional contact methods (yes/no).

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review each field carefully to avoid duplication or misinterpretation.
* Enter all relevant information to facilitate the real estate transaction process.
