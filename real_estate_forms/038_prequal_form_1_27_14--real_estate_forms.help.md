# Prequal Form 1 27 14 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to prequalify individuals for employment purposes. The form gathers information about the individual's personal and employment details to determine their eligibility for employment opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for Social Security Number field to indicate whether you have a social security number.
5. Enter your Address in the Address field.
6. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for Zip field to indicate whether you are a US resident.
7. Enter your Prequal Amount in the Prequal Amount field.
8. Enter your Income in the Income field.
9. Select your Employment Status from the options (Active or Inactive).
10. Enter your Employment Date in the Employment Date field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
11. Enter your Employment Income in the Employment Income field.
12. Enter your Employment Address in the Employment Address field.
13. Enter your Employer Name in the Employer Name field.
14. Enter your Employer Address in the Employer Address field.
15. Enter your Employment Start Date in the Employment Start Date field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
16. Enter your Employment End Date in the Employment End Date field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
17. Enter the number of years you have been employed in the Employment Years field.
18. Enter the number of years you have been employed in the Employment Years Employed field.
19. Select your reason for employment from the options (Yes or No) in the Reason for Employment field.
20. Enter a brief description of your employment in the Employment Reason field.
21. Select your Employment Type from the options (Yes or No) in the Employment Type field.
22. Select your Employment Status from the options (Active or Inactive) in the Employment Status field.
23. Select your Employment Employment Status from the options (Active or Inactive) in the Employment Employment Status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have a social security number, 'No' otherwise.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you are a US resident, 'No' otherwise.
* **Prequal Amount** (`prequal_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are prequalifying for.
* **Income** (`income`, number, required): Enter your income.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Employment Date** (`employment_date`, date, required): Enter your employment date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment Income** (`employment_income`, number, required): Enter your employment income.
* **Employment Address** (`employment_address`, text, required): Enter your employment address.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter your employer's name.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required): Enter your employer's address.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required): Enter your employment start date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, required): Enter your employment end date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment Years** (`employment_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been employed.
* **Employment Years Employed** (`employment_years_employed`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been employed.
* **Reason for Employment** (`reason_for_employment`, select_multiple, required): Select your reason for employment (Yes or No).
* **Employment Reason** (`employment_reason`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your employment.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your employment type (Yes or No).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Employment Employment Status** (`employment_employment_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your employment status (Active or Inactive).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure of any of the fields, please refer to the explanations above or contact the form administrator for assistance.
