# Move Out And In Instant Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for a move out and in quote request. It is intended to be used in a B2B or B2C context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select the "Move Date" from the calendar.
3. Choose the move type from the options provided (In, Out, Both).
4. If applicable, select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
5. Enter the number of movers required.
6. Enter the weight of the items being moved.
7. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
8. You can optionally add any additional services required (Packing and unpacking, Furniture disassembly/Reassembly, Equipment disassembly/Reassembly, Storage and Delivery).
9. Enter the estimated price for the move.
10. Provide any additional service details.
11. Optionally, provide any service terms or conditions.
12. Add any comments from the customer or proprietors and property managers.
13. Finally, add any move out and move in details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (1)** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Move Date (2)** (`move_date`, date, required): Select the date of the move.
* **Move Type (3)** (`move_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of move (In, Out, Both).
* **Property Type (4)** (`property_type_prophets`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Number of Movers (5)** (`number_of_movers`, number, required): Enter the number of movers required.
* **Move Weight (6)** (`move_weight`, number, required): Enter the weight of the items being moved.
* **Proprietors and Property Managers (7)** (`prophets_and_proprieters`, text, optional): Enter the names of the proprietors and property managers.
* **Contact Number (8)** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email (9)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Additional Services (10)** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services required (Packing and unpacking, Furniture disassembly/Reassembly, Equipment disassembly/Reassembly, Storage and Delivery).
* **Estimated Price (11)** (`estimated_price`, number, required): Enter the estimated price for the move.
* **Service Details (12)** (`service_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional service details.
* **Service Terms (13)** (`service_terms`, text, optional): Enter any service terms or conditions.
* **Customer Comments (14)** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the customer.
* **Move Out Details (15)** (`move_out_details`, text, optional): Enter details about the move out process.
* **Move In Details (16)** (`move_in_details`, text, optional): Enter details about the move in process.
* **Move Out and In Details (17)** (`move_out_and_in_details`, text, optional): Enter any details about the move out and move in process.
* **Proprietors and Property Managers Comments (18)** (`prophets_and_proprietors_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the proprietors and property managers.
