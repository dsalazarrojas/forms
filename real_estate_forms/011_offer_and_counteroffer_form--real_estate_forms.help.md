# Offer And Counteroffer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Offer And Counteroffer Form is used to collect information from both the buyer and seller in a real estate transaction, ensuring transparency and accuracy in the communication process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill out the fields that are relevant to your situation.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the "Buyer Name", "Buyer Contact Info", "Buyer Title", "Buyer Company", "Buyer Phone", and "Buyer Email" fields.
3. Enter the address of the property in the "Property Address" field.
4. Enter the price of the offer in the "Offer Price" field.
5. If applicable, enter any terms or conditions of the offer in the "Offer Terms" field.
6. If the seller has made a counteroffer, enter "Yes" in the "Counteroffer" field.
7. If applicable, enter any terms or conditions of the counteroffer in the "Counteroffer Terms" field.
8. Enter your signature and the date in the "Buyer Signature" and "Date Signed" fields.
9. Enter your signature and the date in the "Seller Signature" and "Seller Signature Date" fields.
10. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields have been filled out correctly and accurately.
