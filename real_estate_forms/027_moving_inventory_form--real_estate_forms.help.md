# Moving Inventory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about items being moved, including their status, date, time, origin, destination, and a description of the movement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and identify the relevant information you need to provide.
2. Enter the item ID (if applicable).
3. Indicate the status of the items (e.g., "Yes" or "No" for availability).
4. Enter the date and time of the movement.
5. Provide a description of the movement, including the location from where the item is being moved and the location to where it is being moved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **item_id** (item_id, number, Optional): Enter the unique ID of the item being moved.
* **items** (items, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate the status of the items by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **date** (date, date, Optional): Enter the date of the movement.
* **time** (time, time, Optional): Enter the time of the movement.
* **from_location** (from_location, text, Optional): Enter the location from where the item is being moved.
* **to_location** (to_location, text, Optional): Enter the location to where the item is being moved.
* **description** (description, text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the movement.

## Tips
* Be accurate when selecting the status of the items (items field).
* Ensure the date and time entered are correct and relevant to the movement.
* Provide sufficient details in the description field to help with tracking and tracing the movement.
