# request_a_showing_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The request_a_showing_form is used to request a showing for a property listing. This form is designed for real estate agents or brokers to collect information from potential showings to facilitate the viewing of a property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the instructions and ensure you have not already submitted a showing request for this property.
2. Navigate through the multiple pages to gather all the necessary information.
3. Fill in the required fields, marked as `Required: true`.
4. Submit the form to send the information to the agent or broker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Listing Info** (`listing_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide a brief description of the property listing.
* **Showing Preferences** (`showing_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple preferences for the showing, such as Agent, Owner, or Other.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information, such as email or phone number.
* **Showing Request** (`showing_request`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose a time slot for the showing, such as 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM.
* **Showings** (`showings`, number, required: false): Enter the number of showings you are requesting.
* **Showing Date/Time** (`showing_date_time`, date, required: false): Select a date and time for the showing.
* **Showing Time** (`showing_time`, time, required: false): Choose a specific time for the showing.
* **Showing Type** (`showing_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of showing, such as In-Person, Virtual, or Phone Only.
* **Agent Info** (`agent_info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the agent or broker.
* **Showing Preferences 2** (`showing_preferences_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose an additional preference for the showing, such as In-Person, Virtual, or Phone Only.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the contact name associated with the showing.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email associated with the showing.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number associated with the showing.
* **Showing Message** (`showing_message`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information or message for the showing.
* **Follow-up Info** (`follow_up_info`, text, required: false): Enter any follow-up information for the showing.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes for the showing.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the explanations above.
- If you have already submitted a showing request for this property, please do not submit another.
