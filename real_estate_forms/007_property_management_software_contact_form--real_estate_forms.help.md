# Property Management Software Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who need assistance or have questions about the Property Management Software. It's a contact form for support and general inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as accurately as possible. The form is divided into the following pages:

* Contact Form
* Property Details
* Feature Interests
* Contact Method
* Additional Information (Email, Phone, Date, Time, and Note)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Form
* **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, text, optional): Enter your message or question here, and our support team will get back to you.
### Property Details
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Describe the property you are inquiring about.
### Feature Interests
* **Feature Interests** (`feature_interests`, select multiple, optional): Check the features you are interested in (e.g., mortgage, lease, rental).
### Contact Method
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, other).
### Additional Information
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to be contacted on (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to be contacted on (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or details about your inquiry.
### Submitted By
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name or title so we can address you properly.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in the property details section to help us better understand your inquiry.
* Select all relevant feature interests to ensure we can assist you effectively.
* Choose a contact method that suits your preference.
* Enter a valid email and phone number to facilitate communication.
* Use the date and time fields if you have a specific time-sensitive inquiry.
* Use the note field to add any additional context to your inquiry.
