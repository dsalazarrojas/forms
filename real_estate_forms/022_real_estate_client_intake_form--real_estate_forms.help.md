# real_estate_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information for real estate purposes, such as client intakes. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your client data is up-to-date and secure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the first name, last name, and contact phone fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter the client's email address.
3. Select the lead source and type of property.
4. Choose the lead status.
5. Enter any notes regarding the client interaction.
6. Fill out the contact date and time.
7. Set a follow-up date and time.
8. Enter the property address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Enter the client's last name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Required): Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_multiple, Optional): Select where the lead came from (e.g., online, referral, etc.).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of property (residential, commercial, land).
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_one, Optional): Select the status of the lead (Active, Inactive, Lead).
* **Assigned Agent** (`assigned_agent`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the assigned agent.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, Optional): Enter any notes regarding the client interaction.
* **Contact Date** (`contact_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of the client contact.
* **Contact Time** (`contact_time`, time, Optional): Enter the time of the client contact.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, Optional): Set a follow-up date for the client.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, Optional): Set a follow-up time for the client.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, Optional): Enter the property address (if applicable).
