# AR Tenant Application Form 1 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from applicants for an apartment rental application. It is used to collect personal and financial data, as well as employment and emergency contact information, to assess the applicant's qualifications for rental.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide a valid email address that will be used for application communications in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for contact inquiries.
4. Enter your current residential address in the "Current Address" field.
5. Provide the name of your current landlord in the "Current Landlord Name" field.
6. Enter the phone number of your current landlord in the "Current Landlord Phone" field.
7. Select the length of time you have lived at your current address in the "Rental Period at Current Address" field.
8. Enter the monthly rent you pay at your current address in the "Monthly Rent at Current Address" field.
9. Select the reason you are leaving your current residence in the "Reason for Moving" field.
10. Choose your current employment status in the "Employment Status" field.
11. If applicable, enter the name of your current employer in the "Employer Name" field.
12. If applicable, enter the phone number of your current employer in the "Employer Phone" field.
13. Enter your monthly income in the "Monthly Income" field.
14. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
15. Enter contact information for personal references in the "Personal References" field.
16. Indicate whether you have any pets in the "Pets" field.
17. Select whether you are a smoker in the "Smoking" field.
18. Indicate whether you have any criminal convictions in the "Criminal History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Name (1)
* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete legal name.

### Email Address (2)
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address that will be used for application communications.

### Phone Number (3)
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for application inquiries.

### Current Address (4)
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current residential address.

### Current Landlord Name (5)
* **Current Landlord Name** (`current_landlord`, `text`, required): Provide the name of your current landlord.

### Current Landlord Phone (6)
* **Current Landlord Phone** (`current_landlord_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your current landlord.

### Rental Period at Current Address (7)
* **Rental Period at Current Address** (`rental_period`, `text`, required): Select the length of time you have lived at your current address.

### Monthly Rent at Current Address (8)
* **Monthly Rent at Current Address** (`monthly_rent`, `number`, required): Enter the monthly rent you pay at your current address.

### Reason for Moving (9)
* **Reason for Moving** (`reason_for_moving`, `text`, required): Select the reason you are leaving your current residence.

### Employment Status (10)
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current employment status.

### Employer Name (11)
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your current employer (if applicable).

### Employer Phone (12)
* **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your current employer (if applicable).

### Monthly Income (13)
* **Monthly Income** (`monthly_income`, `number`, required): Enter your monthly income.

### Emergency Contact (14)
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.

### Personal References (15)
* **Personal References** (`references`, `text`, required): Enter contact information for personal references.

### Pets (16)
* **Pets** (`pets`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have any pets.

### Smoking (17)
* **Smoking** (`smoking`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are a smoker.

### Criminal History (18)
* **Criminal History** (`criminal_history`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have any criminal convictions.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth application process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
