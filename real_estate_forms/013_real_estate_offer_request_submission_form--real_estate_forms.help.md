# Real Estate Offer Request Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide real estate agents with information about potential buyers' offers on a property.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required and optional information.
- Ensure that you have permission from the buyer to submit their contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Buyers' Email** (`buyers_email`, Email, optional): Enter the buyer's email address.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, Text, optional): Enter the address of the property for sale.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, Select One, optional): Select the type of property (e.g., House, Apartment, Condo).
- **Property Price** (`property_price`, Number, optional): Enter the price of the property.
- **Buyers' Phone** (`buyers_phone`, Text, optional): Enter the buyer's phone number.
- **Agent's Note** (`agent_note`, Note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the agent.
- **Buyer's Name** (`buyers_name`, Text, optional): Enter the buyer's first and last name.
- **Agent's Email** (`agents_email`, Email, optional): Enter the agent's email address.
- **Agent's Phone** (`agents_phone`, Text, optional): Enter the agent's phone number.
- **Property Description** (`property_description`, Note, optional): Provide a brief description of the property.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the real estate agent directly.
