# Real Estate Buyer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from buyers about their preferences and needs for a real estate transaction. The data collected will help real estate agents or brokers understand the buyer's requirements and provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information, if any.
2. For any field that is optional, only input the information relevant to your needs.
3. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Street** (`street_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the real estate you're interested in.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the location you prefer.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the location you prefer.
* **Zip** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the location you prefer.
* **Desired Mortgage** (`desired_mortgage`, number, optional): Enter the maximum mortgage you're willing to take.
* **Desired Property Type** (`desired_property_type`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the type of property you're interested in from the options:
		- Single Family
		- Condo
		- Townhouse
		- Duplex
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the type of location you prefer from the options:
		- City
		- Suburbs
		- Rural
		- Coastal
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter your budget for the real estate transaction.

## Tips
Please ensure you input accurate and up-to-date information, especially for email and phone numbers. You can only choose one location type from the options for `Desired Property Type` and `Preferred Location`.
