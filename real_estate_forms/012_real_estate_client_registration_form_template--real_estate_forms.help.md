# real_estate_client_registration_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "real_estate_client_registration_form_template" is used to collect client registration information for real estate services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the form title.
2. For each field, enter the requested information in the specified format.
3. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
* **First Name**: A text field for your first name.
* Type: text
* Required: Yes

### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name**: A text field for your last name.
* Type: text
* Required: Yes

### Email (3)
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address.
* Type: email
* Required: Yes

### Phone (4)
* **Phone**: A text field for your phone number.
* Type: text
* Required: Yes

### Date of Birth (5)
* **Date of Birth**: A date field for your date of birth.
* Type: date
* Required: Yes

### Address (6)
* **Address**: A text field for your address.
* Type: text
* Required: Yes

### Agent (7)
* **Agent**: A text field for your agent name or identifier.
* Type: text
* Required: Yes

### Agency (8)
* **Agency**: A text field for your agency name or identifier.
* Type: text
* Required: Yes

### Contact (9)
* **Contact**: A select menu with two options: First Option 1, Second Option 1, and Second Option 2.
* Type: select_one
* Required: Yes

### Agreement Signed (10)
* **Agreement Signed**: A note field to confirm agreement on the terms of the real estate service.
* Type: note
* Required: Yes

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you need to update your information, use this form to make changes.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about the form, contact your real estate agent or agency representative.
