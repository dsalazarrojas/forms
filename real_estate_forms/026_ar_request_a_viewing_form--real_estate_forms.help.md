# AR Request A Viewing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a viewing of a property. It captures essential information from the applicant to coordinate and facilitate a smooth viewing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and provide any additional information that may be relevant to your viewing request. Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and comprehensively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1): Enter your complete legal name to help the agent identify you correctly.
* **Email Address** (2): Provide a valid email address that the agent can contact you on for arrangement coordination.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your contact number for the agent to reach out to you for viewing coordination.
* **Property Address** (4): Enter the address of the property you wish to view.
* **Property ID** (5): If you have a reference number or ID for the property, please provide it. This helps the agent locate the property easily.
* **Preferred Viewing Date** (6): Select a preferred date for the viewing to help plan the event efficiently.
* **Preferred Viewing Time** (7): Choose a preferred time for the viewing to suit your schedule.
* **Alternative Dates** (8): If there are other dates that work for you, provide them here.
* **Number of People Viewing** (9): Specify how many people will be attending the viewing.
* **Relationship Status** (10): If you're a married couple or have a family member accompanying you, please indicate.
* **Financing Status** (11): Let us know if you're pre-approved for financing or not.
* **Reason for Viewing** (12): Briefly explain why you're interested in this property.
* **Special Requirements** (13): If you have any special requests or requirements, please share them here.
* **Agent Preference** (14): If you have a preferred agent to show you the property, let us know.
* **Additional Comments** (15): Any other information that might be relevant to your viewing request.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately for a smooth and efficient viewing experience.
