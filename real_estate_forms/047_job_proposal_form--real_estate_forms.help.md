# Job Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture essential information about a job proposal, including client contact details, job requirements, and payment terms. It helps in streamlining the sales process and ensuring that all necessary information is provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the job in the "Job Description" field.
5. List the services offered in the "Services Offered" field.
6. Enter a brief summary of the pricing details in the "Pricing Summary" field.
7. Specify the proposal's validity date in the "Proposal Validity Date" field.
8. Enter the total amount due in the "Total Amount Due" field.
9. Select the payment terms (One-time, Installment, or Lump Sum) in the "Payment Terms" field.
10. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
11. Choose how frequently payments will be made (Every Month, Every Week, or Every Two Weeks) in the "Payment Frequency" field.
12. Indicate whether the proposal is accepted or not in the "Proposal Accepted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: The client's name.
* **Phone**: The client's phone number.
* **Email**: The client's email address.
* **Job Description**: A detailed description of the job requirements and scope.
* **Services Offered**: A list of services that will be performed for the client.
* **Pricing Summary**: A brief summary of the pricing details.
* **Proposal Validity Date**: The date when the proposal is valid until.
* **Total Amount Due**: The total amount the client owes.
* **Payment Terms**: The payment terms (One-time, Installment, or Lump Sum).
* **Payment Amount**: The amount to be paid.
* **Payment Frequency**: The frequency of payments (Every Month, Every Week, or Every Two Weeks).
* **Proposal Accepted**: Whether the proposal is accepted or not.
