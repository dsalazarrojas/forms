# Tenant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from tenants about the condition and maintenance of rental properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Tenant Feedback Form" page.
2. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose the feedback type from the options provided:
	* General: Any general comments or suggestions about the property.
	* Maintenance: Issues related to the maintenance or repairs of the property.
	* Repair: Urgent repairs or issues that need immediate attention.
4. Type in your feedback in the "Additional Information" field if you have any specific concerns or comments.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to the property management team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Feedback Form (1)**: This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the purpose of the form.
* **Name (2)**: Please enter your name to ensure that your feedback is attributed to you.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you if necessary.
* **Feedback Type (4)**: Choose the category that best describes your feedback.
* **Additional Information (5)**: Add any additional details or comments about your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Ensure that you are using the correct email address.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact the property management office directly.

Note: The following fields are optional, but your feedback is more valuable if you provide as much information as possible.
