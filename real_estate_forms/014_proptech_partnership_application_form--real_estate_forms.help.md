# PropTech Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for companies that wish to apply for the PropTech Partnership program. Please fill it out to provide us with your company details and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Go through each section of the form carefully and make sure to fill out all the fields required.
2. Enter your company's details accurately.
3. Fill out the contact information of the representative who will be the primary contact.
4. Describe your company's goals and objectives for the partnership.
5. Make sure to select relevant options that best represent your company's characteristics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details**: This section is for providing information about your company. Please enter your company name, address, phone number, and website.
* **Company Contact**: This is the contact information of the representative who will be the primary contact for our team.
* **Collaboration Goals**: Here, you can describe your goals and objectives for the partnership.
* **Contact First Name**: This is the first name of the company representative who will be the contact.
* **Contact Last Name**: This is the last name of the company representative who will be the contact.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the company representative who will be the contact.
* **Contact Phone**: This is the phone number of the company representative who will be the contact.
* **Collaboration Goals**: Describe your goals and expectations for the partnership.
* **Collaboration Objectives**: Outline your company's objectives for the partnership.
* **Collaboration Expectation**: Describe what you expect from the partnership.
* **Why Partnership**: Explain why you want to partner with us.
* **Company Industry**: Please select the industry that best describes your company.
* **Company Sector**: Select the sector that best represents your company.
* **Company Type**: Please select the type that best describes your company (private or public).
* **Company Size**: Select the size of your company (small, medium, large, extra-large).
* **Company Location**: Select the location where your company is based (city, state, region).
* **Collaboration Focus**: Select the area where your company focuses (innovation, technology, finance).
* **Partnership Goals**: Select the goals you have for the partnership (growth, profit, market).
* **Partnership Expectation**: Select the expectations you have from the partnership (growth, profit, market).
* **Submission Confirmation**: This is a confirmation note that you have read and understood the form.
  This form is used to ensure that you understand the purpose and fields of this form. Please go through each field carefully and make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your application is complete. If you have any questions, feel free to contact us for clarification.
