# Property Refinance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Refinance Application Form is a tool designed for individuals and couples to provide information about their property and financial situation to assess their eligibility for a refinance loan. This form helps lenders understand the property's value, type, and other relevant details, as well as the borrower's credit history, income, and employment status. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (User Name, Property Address) and your contact details (Contact Number, Email, Preferred Contact Method).
2. Provide details about your property (Property Type, Property Value, Property Address).
3. Answer questions about your loan preferences (Loan Amount, Loan Term, Desired Interest Rate).
4. Select your credit score and credit history status.
5. Choose the type of property you desire (Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse).
6. Answer questions about your co-borrower (if applicable), including their name, employment status, and employment length.
7. Select your income proof and employment status.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, optional): The amount of money you want to refinance or borrow.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, number, optional): The length of time you want the loan to last.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): The type of property you currently own or desire (Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse).
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, number, optional): The value of your property.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): The address of your property.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address for contact and communication.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): How you prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, select_one, optional): The status of your current loan (Active, Inactive, Denied).
* **Reason for Loan Denial** (`reason_for_loan_denial`, text, optional): The reason for your current loan status (if denied).
* **Co Borrower Name** (`co_borrower_name`, text, optional): The name of your co-borrower (if applicable).
* **Co Borrower Relationship** (`co_borrower_relationship`, select_one, optional): The relationship between you and your co-borrower (Spouse, Child, Parent, Other).
* **Co Borrower Percentage** (`co_borrower_percentage`, number, optional): The percentage of ownership or involvement of your co-borrower in the property.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, optional): Your current credit score.
* **Credit History** (`credit_history`, select_one, optional): The status of your credit history (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Income Proof** (`income_proof`, select_multiple, optional): The type of income proof you have (W2, Tax Return, Paystub).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, optional): The status of your employment (Employed, Self-Employed, Retired).
* **Employment Length** (`employment_length`, select_one, optional): The length of your employment.
* **Co Borrower Employment Status** (`co_borrower_employment_status`, select_multiple, optional): The employment status of your co-borrower.
* **Co Borrower Employment Length** (`co_borrower_employment_length`, select_one, optional): The length of employment of your co-borrower.
* **Property Type 2** (`property_type_2`, select_one, optional): The type of property you currently own or desire (Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse).
* **Desired Property Type** (`desired_property_type`, select_one, optional): The type of property you desire (Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse).
* **Credit History 2** (`credit_history`, select_one, optional): The status of your credit history (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Desired Interest Rate** (`desired_interest_rate`, number, optional): The interest rate you are interested in for your loan.
* **Current Interest Rate** (`current_interest_rate`, number, optional): The current interest rate of your existing loan.
* **Co Borrower Credit History** (`co_borrower_credit_history`, select_one, optional): The status of your co-borrower's credit history (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
