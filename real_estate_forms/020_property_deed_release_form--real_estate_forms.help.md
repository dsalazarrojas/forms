# Property Deed Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for recording property deeds and capturing the relevant details about a property's transfer.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form by following these steps:

1. **Enter the Home Address**: Fill out the Home Address field with the correct address of the property being transferred. This will ensure that the form correctly identifies the property and its location.
2. **Select Property Type**: Choose the correct type of property from the dropdown options. This will help identify the property's classification.
3. **Enter Number of Units**: If the property has multiple units, enter the number of units in the Number of Units field. This is optional.
4. **Enter Transfer Date**: Fill out the Transfer Date field with the date of the transfer. This will help identify the date of the transfer.
5. **Enter Transfer Time**: If applicable, fill out the Transfer Time field with the time of the transfer.
6. **Select Transfer Type**: Choose the correct type of transfer from the dropdown options. This will help identify the nature of the transfer.
7. **Enter Transfer Price**: If the transfer is priced, fill out the Transfer Price field with the amount of the transfer.
8. **Enter Transferor's Name**: Fill out the Transferor's Name field with the name of the individual or entity transferring the property.
9. **Enter Transferor's Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the transferor.
10. **Enter Transferor's Email**: Enter the email address of the transferor.
11. **Enter Transferee's Name**: Fill out the Transferee's Name field with the name of the individual or entity receiving the property.
12. **Enter Transferee's Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the transferee.
13. **Enter Transferee's Email**: Enter the email address of the transferee.
14. **Reason for Relese (Optional)**: If the transfer has a reason, fill out the Reason for Relese field with a brief description.
15. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or details about the transfer.
16. **Property Status**: Select the correct status of the property from the dropdown options.
17. **Property Type Status**: Select the correct type of property from the dropdown options.
18. **Additional Documents**: If additional documents are required, fill out the Additional Documents field with their details.
19-25. **Additional Documents (2-8)**: If additional documents are required, fill out the corresponding fields with their details.
