# HOA Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This HOA Application Form is designed to gather necessary information from homeowners or property managers for the smooth processing of their HOA applications. Please complete the form with accuracy to ensure a speedy and efficient assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your details as requested in the form fields.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Use a real email address for Contact EMail and Property Manager EMail.
4. Enter a valid phone number for Contact Phone and Property Manager Phone.
5. Attach necessary documents as per the Additional Documents field.
6. Provide property details as per the Property Details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homeowner Name** (`homeowner_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the homeowner or property manager.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact EMail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter your current contact address.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you're submitting your application.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to move in, if known.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, required): Specify the type of property you're applying for (e.g., house, apartment, etc.).
* **HOA Dues** (`hoa_dues`, number, required): Enter the amount you're willing to pay for HOA fees.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select how you'll pay for HOA fees (Check, Online Banking, Cash, Other).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional information, enter it here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact EMail** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your emergency contact person's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact person's address.
* **Property Manager Name** (`property_manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your property manager.
* **Property Manager Phone** (`property_manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your property manager's phone number.
* **Property Manager EMail** (`property_manager_email`, email, required): Enter your property manager's email address.
* **Property Owner Signature** (`property_owner_signature`, text, required): Sign with your signature as the property owner.
* **Property Manager Signature** (`property_manager_signature`, text, required): Sign with your signature as the property manager.
* **Additional Documents** (`additional_documents`, text, required): Attach any necessary documents for the application.
* **Property Documents** (`property_documents`, text, required): Attach property-specific documents.
* **Property Images** (`property_images`, text, required): Attach images of the property.
* **Payment Receipt** (`payment_receipt`, text, optional): Attach proof of payment.
* **Payment Confirmation** (`payment_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm payment details.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, required): Enter your property's details.
