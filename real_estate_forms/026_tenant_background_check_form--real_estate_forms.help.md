# Tenant Background Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Background Check Form is used to collect essential information about a tenant, including their personal and employment details, to verify their background and suitability as a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name as per your government-issued ID.
2. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your email address for communication.
4. Select your current address from the provided options.
5. Input your Date of Birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
6. If applicable, list your previous employers, selecting all relevant ones.
7. Choose your Previous Job from the provided options.
8. Enter your previous salary.
9. Select the Reason for Move, which is crucial for the landlord to understand.
10. List your references, selecting all relevant ones.
11. Choose your current Employment Status.
12. Select your current income range.
13. Choose your Credit Score range.
14. Select your Employment History length.
15. Enter the Lease Start Date in mm/dd/yyyy format.
16. If you have an existing lease, enter the Lease End Date (mm/dd/yyyy).
17. Select the Lease Term for the new lease.
18. Select the type(s) of property you are interested in (residential, commercial, or industrial).
19. Finally, select the Property Size (small, medium, or large).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (Text, Required)**: Please enter your full name as per your government-issued ID.
* **User Phone (Text, Not Required)**: Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **User Email (Email, Not Required)**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **User Address (Select One, Not Required)**: Select your current address from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth (Date, Required)**: Enter your Date of Birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Previous Employers (Select Multiple, Not Required)**: If applicable, list your previous employers, selecting all relevant ones.
* **Previous Jobs (Select One, Not Required)**: Choose your Previous Job from the provided options.
* **Previous Salary (Number, Required)**: Enter your previous salary.
* **Reason for Move (Text, Required)**: Select the Reason for Move, which is crucial for the landlord to understand.
* **References (Select Multiple, Not Required)**: List your references, selecting all relevant ones.
* **Employment Status (Select Multiple, Not Required)**: Choose your current Employment Status.
* **Income (Select One, Required)**: Select your current income range.
* **Credit Score (Select One, Required)**: Choose your Credit Score range.
* **Employment History (Select One, Not Required)**: Select your Employment History length.
* **Lease Start Date (Date, Required)**: Enter the Lease Start Date in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Lease End Date (Date, Not Required)**: If you have an existing lease, enter the Lease End Date (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Lease Term (Select Multiple, Not Required)**: Select the Lease Term for the new lease.
* **Property Type (Select Multiple, Not Required)**: Select the type(s) of property you are interested in (residential, commercial, or industrial).
* **Property Size (Select One, Not Required)**: Select the Property Size (small, medium, or large).

Note: Required fields are denoted by "Required", while Not Required fields are optional and can be skipped.
