# Seller Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from the seller about themselves and their property to facilitate a successful transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form by following the instructions below:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the fields provided.
2. Select the best time to contact you during the day.
3. Choose how soon you'd like to sell your property.
4. Provide the address of your property.
5. Select the type of property you are selling (e.g., House, Apartment, Condo).
6. Indicate the reason(s) you'd like to sell your property (e.g., Financial difficulties, Moving to a new city, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **seller_name** (`text`, required): Enter your full name as the seller.
* **seller_phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your property.
* **seller_email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address so we can communicate with you.
* **property_address** (`text`, required): Enter the address of your property for sale or rent.
* **property_type** (`select_one`, options: House, Apartment, Condo): Indicate the type of property you are selling.
* **reason_to_sell** (`select_multiple`, options: Financial difficulties, Moving to a new city, Other): Select all applicable reasons for selling your property.
* **best_time_to_contact** (`select_one`, options: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Anytime): Indicate your preferred time to contact you.
* **how_soon_to_sell** (`select_one`, options: Immediately, 1-3 months, 4-6 months, More than 6 months): Select how soon you'd like to sell your property.
* **seller_address** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional address information about your property.
* **move_out_section** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional information about the move-out process or property specifics.
