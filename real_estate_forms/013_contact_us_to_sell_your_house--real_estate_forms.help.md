# Contact Us to Sell Your House - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Contact Us to Sell Your House" form is designed to gather information from homeowners who want to sell their houses. The goal is to help potential buyers find and connect with sellers quickly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your property.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure a successful sale.
3. Review your answers before submitting to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Homeowner Information
* **Full Name** (`full_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your best number where you can be contacted.
* **Property Street Address** (`property_address`): Enter the address of the property you're selling.
* **City** (`property_city`): Enter the city of the property's location.
* **State** (`property_state`): Enter the state of the property's location.
* **Zip Code** (`property_zip`): Enter the zip code of the property's location.

### Property Information
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`bedrooms_count`): If applicable, enter the number of bedrooms in your property.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`bathrooms_count`): If applicable, enter the number of bathrooms in your property.
* **Approximate Square Footage** (`approximate_sqft`): If applicable, enter the approximate square footage of your property.

### Selling Details
* **How Soon Are You Looking to Sell?** (`selling_timeframe`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 30 days
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ Just curious about the value
* **What Is Your Primary Reason for Selling?** (`reason_for_selling`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Relocating
	+ Upsizing
	+ Downsizing
	+ Financial Reasons
	+ Inherited Property
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Is the Property Currently Listed with an Agent?** (`listing_status`): Select 'True' if the property is listed, 'False' otherwise.

### Additional Comments
* **Anything Else We Should Know About Your Property?** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional details about your property, such as recent upgrades or special features.
