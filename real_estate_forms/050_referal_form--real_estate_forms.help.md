# Referal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referal Form is used to report a referral or lead generated from sales efforts by real estate agents or property managers. It provides essential information for the sales team to follow up with the referral and convert it into a sales lead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Referal Form** fields with the required information.
2. Select the **referal_status** field to indicate the status of the referral (new or old).
3. Enter the **referal_date** field with the date of the referral.
4. Select the **referal_time** field with the time of the referral.
5. Fill out the **contact_details** field with any additional information about the person being referred.
6. Enter the **contact_number** field with the contact number of the person being referred.
7. Enter the **email_address** field with the email address of the person being referred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **referal_form_1 (referal_form)**: Enter a brief description of the referral.
* **real_estate_form_2 (real_estate)**: Enter a brief description of the real estate property (e.g., "new listing", "open house", etc.).
* **real_estate_form_3 (partner_name)**: Enter the name of the real estate partner or agent who made the referral.
* **real_estate_form_4 (referal_status)**: Select the status of the referral (new or old).
* **real_estate_form_5 (referal_date)**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **real_estate_form_6 (referal_time)**: Select the time of the referral.
* **real_estate_form_7 (contact_details)**: Enter any additional information about the person being referred.
* **real_estate_form_8 (contact_number)**: Enter the contact number of the person being referred.
* **real_estate_form_9 (email_address)**: Enter the email address of the person being referred.
