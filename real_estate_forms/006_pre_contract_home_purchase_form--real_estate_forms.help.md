# Pre-Contract Home Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential details about a pre-contract home purchase, simplifying the process of documenting and tracking client agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Home Address" field on page 1.
2. Proceed to page 2 and fill out the "Sellers Name", "Buyers Name", "Contact Number", "Contract Date", "Contract Date Time", "Buyers Signature" fields.
3. Move on to page 3 and fill out the "Contact Email", "Contact Address", "Agent Name", "Agent Company", "Agent Email", "Agent Phone", "Agent Address" fields.
4. Go to page 4 and fill out the "Property Type", "Property Price", "Property Status", "Agent Address" fields.
5. On page 5, fill out the "Agents Signature" field.
6. Fill out the "Payment Terms Accepted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address (Page 1)**: Enter the address of the property being purchased.
* **Sellers Name (Page 2)**: Enter the name of the seller.
* **Buyers Name (Page 2)**: Enter the name of the buyer.
* **Contact Number (Page 2)**: Enter the contact number of the seller or buyer.
* **Contract Date (Page 2)**: Enter the contract date.
* **Contract Date Time (Page 2)**: Enter the contract date and time.
* **Buyers Signature (Page 2)**: Enter the signature of the buyer.
* **Contact Email (Page 3)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Address (Page 3)**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Agent Name (Page 3)**: Enter the name of the real estate agent.
* **Agent Company (Page 3)**: Enter the company name of the real estate agent.
* **Agent Email (Page 3)**: Enter the email address of the real estate agent.
* **Agent Phone (Page 3)**: Enter the phone number of the real estate agent.
* **Agent Address (Page 3)**: Enter the address of the real estate agent.
* **Property Type (Page 4)**: Enter the type of property being purchased.
* **Property Price (Page 4)**: Enter the price of the property.
* **Property Status (Page 4)**: Select the status of the property (Pending, Approved, Declined).
* **Agents Signature (Page 5)**: Enter the signature of the real estate agent.
* **Buyers Acknowledgement (Page 5)**: Enter the acknowledgement of the buyer.
* **Agents Acknowledgement (Page 5)**: Enter the acknowledgement of the real estate agent.
* **Property Description (Page 5)**: Enter a description of the property.
* **Contract Date Time (Page 5)**: Enter the contract date and time.
* **Payment Terms Accepted (Page 5)**: Enter the payment terms accepted.
