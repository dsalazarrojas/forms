# Lease Agreement Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lease agreement delivery form is used to collect essential information for a comprehensive lease agreement, which includes tenant contact details, lease start and end dates, rent amount, lease terms, and signed documents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling in the first page.
2. Select the tenant contact options that apply to the lease agreement.
3. Enter the lease start and end dates accurately.
4. Choose the correct lease terms and signed document options for the agreement.
5. Fill in any other optional sections that are relevant to the lease.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Contact** (contact_information, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options for the tenant's contact information, including phone numbers, email addresses, or any other relevant contacts.
* **Lease Start Date** (lease_start_date, date, required): Enter the exact start date of the lease agreement.
* **Lease End Date** (lease_end_date, date, required): Enter the exact end date of the lease agreement.
* **Lease Terms** (lease_terms, select_one, required): Choose the option that best represents the lease terms (e.g., month-to-month, six-months, etc.).
* **Rent Amount** (rent_amount, number, required): Enter the exact rent amount for the lease period.
* **Signed By** (signed_by, select_one, required): Choose the option representing the person signing the lease agreement.
* **Date Signed** (date_signed, date, required): Enter the date on which the lease agreement was signed.

## Tips
* Double-check the entered dates for accuracy.
* Ensure that all required fields have been completed before submitting the form.
* Review all selected options for the lease terms and signed documents for correctness.
