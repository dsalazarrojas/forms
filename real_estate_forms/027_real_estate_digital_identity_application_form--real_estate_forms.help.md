# Real Estate Digital Identity Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting digital identity information from clients who are applying for real estate services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (1)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Provide your phone number and email address for communication purposes.
* **Identification (3)**: Enter your identification number or any other government-issued ID number.
* **Property Details (4)**: Describe the property you are applying for, including location and type.
* **Contact Preferences (5)**: Specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or mail).
* **Employment Status (6)**: Indicate your current employment status (e.g., employed, unemployed, etc.).
* **Income Verification (7)**: Enter your annual income or any other relevant financial information.
* **Tax Return (8)**: Provide your tax return information or any other relevant tax details.
* **Employment History (9)**: Describe your previous employment experiences and details.
* **Education Background (10)**: Enter your educational background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Professional Certifications (11)**: List any relevant professional certifications or licenses.
* **Employment Verification (12)**: Verify your current employment status with your employer or HR department.
* **Income Verification Details (13)**: Provide detailed information about your income verification process.
* **Employment Verification Details (14)**: Provide detailed information about your employment verification process.
* **Tax Return Details (15)**: Provide detailed information about your tax return process.
* **Employment History Details (16)**: Provide detailed information about your employment history.
* **Education Background Details (17)**: Provide detailed information about your education background.
* **Professional Certifications Details (18)**: Provide detailed information about your professional certifications.
* **Employment Verification Details 2 (19)**: Provide additional information about your employment verification process.

Note: Each field is optional unless marked as required. Please fill out the form according to the guidelines above, and if you have any doubts, feel free to reach out to the support team.
