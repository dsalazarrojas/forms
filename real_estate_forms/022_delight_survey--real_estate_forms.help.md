# Delight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delight Survey is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with real estate services or goods. Your input will help us understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to provide better services and goods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the type of service or good you are providing feedback on.
2. Provide a brief description of the service or good you are providing feedback on.
3. Identify which tools were assigned to the service or good you are providing feedback on (select all that apply).
4. Enter any additional comments or ratings (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category**: Select the type of service or good you are providing feedback on.
  - This field is for categorizing the service or good you are providing feedback on.
  - You can choose one of the three options: Real Estate Forms, Other, or Finance.
* **description**: Describe the service or good you are providing feedback on.
  - Enter a brief description of the service or good you are providing feedback on.
  - This can be a simple sentence or a short paragraph.
* **assigned_tool**: Identify which tools were assigned to the service or good you are providing feedback on.
  - You can select all that apply from the three options: chatjimmy, chatbilly, chatliz.
  - This field is for reporting which tools were used to provide the service or good.
* **input 5**: Enter any additional comments or ratings (optional).
  - This is a text field where you can enter any additional comments or ratings you have about the service or good.
  - You can enter as much or as little as you like.

## Tips
- Please answer all fields that apply to your feedback.
- If you have any additional comments or ratings, please enter them in the "input 5" field.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
