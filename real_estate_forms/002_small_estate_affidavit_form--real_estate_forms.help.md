# Small Estate Affidavit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for a small estate affidavit, which is used to verify the details of an estate or will in a legal or administrative context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the estate in the `Estate Name` field. This field should include the name of the estate being referred to in the affidavit.
2.  Provide the address of the estate in the `Address` field. This should include the street address, city, state, and zip code of the estate's location.
3.  Enter the city of the estate's location in the `City` field.
4.  Enter the state of the estate's location in the `State` field.
5.  Enter the zip code of the estate's location in the `Zip Code` field.
6.  Select the county where the estate is located from the options in the `County` field.
7.  Enter the phone number of the estate or the contact person in the `Phone` field.
8.  Select the relationship of the executor or affiant to the estate from the options in the `Relationship` field.
9.  If the relationship is other than the options provided, enter the details in the `Other Relationship` field.
10. Enter the date of birth of the deceased in the `Deceased's Date of Birth` field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
11. Enter the date of birth of the primary executor in the `Primary Executor's Date of Birth` field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
12. Enter the date of death of the primary executor in the `Primary Executor's Date of Death` field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
13. Enter the date of the affidavit in the `Affidavit Date` field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
14. Enter the notarized docket number of the estate in the `Notarized Docket Number` field.
15. Enter the name of the court where the estate is being managed in the `Court Name` field.
16. Enter the address of the court where the estate is being managed in the `Court Address` field.
17. Enter the city of the court's location in the `Court City` field.
18. Enter the state of the court's location in the `Court State` field.
19. Enter the zip code of the court's location in the `Court Zip` field.
20. Enter the docket number of the estate in the `Court Docket Number` field.
21. Enter the name of the person filing the form in the `Filed By` field.
22. Select whether the person signing the form has a signature from the options in the `Signature` field.
23. Select whether the affiant has a signature from the options in the `Affiant Signature` field.
24. Enter the name of the notary public who is verifying the form in the `Notary Name` field.
25. Select whether the notary public has a seal from the options in the `Notary Seal` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estate Name** (`estate_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the estate being referred to in the affidavit.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide the address of the estate, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the estate is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where the estate is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Enter the zip code of the estate's location.
* **County** (`county`, text, optional): Select the county where the estate is located.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the estate or the contact person.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship of the executor or affiant to the estate.
* **Other Relationship** (`relationship_other`, text, optional): If the relationship is other than the options provided, enter the details.
* **Deceased's Date of Birth** (`deceased_dob`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the deceased in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Primary Executor's Date of Birth** (`primary_executors_dob`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the primary executor in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Primary Executor's Date of Death** (`primary_executors_dod`, date, optional): Enter the date of death of the primary executor in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Affidavit Date** (`affidavit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the affidavit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Notarized Docket Number** (`notarized_docket_number`, text, optional): Enter the notarized docket number of the estate.
* **Court Name** (`court_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the court where the estate is being managed.
* **Court Address** (`court_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the court where the estate is being managed.
* **Court City** (`court_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the court's location.
* **Court State** (`court_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the court's location.
* **Court Zip** (`court_zip`, number, optional): Enter the zip code of the court's location.
* **Court Docket Number** (`court_docket_number`, text, optional): Enter the docket number of the estate.
* **Filed By** (`filed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person filing the form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, optional): Select whether the person signing the form has a signature.
* **Affiant Signature** (`affiant_signature`, select_one, optional): Select whether the affiant has a signature.
* **Notary Name** (`notary_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the notary public who is verifying the form.
* **Notary Seal** (`notary_seal`, select_one, optional): Select whether the notary public has a seal.
