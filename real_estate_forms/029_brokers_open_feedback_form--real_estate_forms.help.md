# Brokers Open Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from agents on properties they have shown to potential clients. The feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and optimize the showing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required field "Property Reference" with the internal property ID or address.
2. Provide your name in the "Agent Name" field.
3. Choose your agency from the "Agency" field.
4. Fill out the "Overall Feedback" field with your general impressions of the property.
5. If you have any comments on the asking price, please type them in the "Pricing Comments" field.
6. If you have any suggestions to improve showings, type them in the "Showing Suggestions" field.
7. Indicate the reported buyer interest level from "Low", "Moderate", or "High".
7. If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, provide your contact information in the "Follow up Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Reference (<type> text, required)**: Enter the internal property ID or address of the property you are providing feedback on.
* **Agent Name (<type> text, optional)**: Enter your name as an agent.
* **Agency (<type> text, optional)**: Choose your agency from the available options.
* **Overall Feedback (<type> text, optional)**: Type a brief description of your general impressions of the property.
* **Pricing Comments (<type> text, optional)**: Enter any comments you have about the asking price.
* **Showing Suggestions (<type> text, optional)**: Suggest any improvements for showings.
* **Interest Level (<type> select_one, optional)**: Choose the reported buyer interest level from "Low", "Moderate", or "High".
* **Follow up Contact (<type> text, optional)**: If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, provide your contact information.
