# Rent Schedule Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rent Schedule Form is used to collect essential information about rent payments from tenants, including rent start and end dates, payment schedules, rent frequency, and due dates. This form is crucial for maintaining accurate records and ensuring timely payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Tenant Name** in the text field. This is a required field.
2. Provide the **Tenant Email** in the email field. This is a required field.
3. Enter the **Tenant Contact** information in the text field. This is a required field.
4. Select the **Payment Start Date** from the date picker calendar.
5. Choose the **Payment End Date** from the date picker calendar.
6. Select the preferred **Payment Schedule** from the options provided.
7. Enter the **Rent Start Date** from the date picker calendar.
8. Select the **Rent End Date** from the date picker calendar.
9. Choose the **Rent Frequency**, which is how often the rent will be paid (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
10. Enter the **Rent Amount** as a numerical value.
11. Select the **Due Date** from the date picker calendar.
12. Choose the **Payment Term** from the options provided (Rent Due Now, Pay In Arrears, Pay In Advance).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Email** (`tenant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the tenant.
* **Tenant Contact** (`tenant_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the tenant.
* **Payment Start Date** (`payment_start_date`, date, required): Choose the date when payments will begin.
* **Payment End Date** (`payment_end_date`, date, required): Choose the date when payments will end.
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, select_multiple, required): Select the preferred payment schedule (Monthly, Bi-Weekly, Weekly).
* **Rent Start Date** (`rent_start_date`, date, required): Choose the start date of the rent period.
* **Rent End Date** (`rent_end_date`, date, required): Choose the end date of the rent period.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of rent payments (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of rent to be paid.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, required): Choose the date when rent is due.
* **Payment Term** (`payment_term`, select_one, required): Select the payment term (Rent Due Now, Pay In Arrears, Pay In Advance).
