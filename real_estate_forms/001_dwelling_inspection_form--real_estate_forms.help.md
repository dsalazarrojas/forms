# Dwelling Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dwelling Inspection Form" is a tool used to inspect and evaluate various aspects of a residential property, including its exterior condition, structural integrity, and safety features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to understand what information you need to provide.
2. Select the correct response for each field based on your observation or inspection results.
3. Ensure you fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Dwelling Inspection Overview (Page 1)
* **1. Dwelling Inspection Overview**: This section is a brief description of the property being inspected. Please provide a concise summary of the property's condition, including its age, size, and any notable features.

### 2. Inspection Area (Page 2)
* **2. Inspection Area**: Enter the specific area of the property you are inspecting, such as the attic, basement, or exterior.

### 3. Exterior (Page 2)
* **3. Exterior**: Describe the overall condition of the property's exterior, including its appearance, maintenance, and any notable features.

### 4. Exterior Condition (Page 2)
* **4. Exterior Condition**: Select the condition of the property's exterior from the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 5. Roof (Page 3)
* **5. Roof**: Choose the type of roof the inspected property has from the following options:
	+ Flat
	+ Gabled
	+ Hip
	+ Butt

### 6. Gutters and Downspouts (Page 3)
* **6. Gutters and Downspouts**: Describe the condition and functionality of the property's gutters and downspouts.

### 7. Windows (Page 3)
* **7. Windows**: Select the type of windows used in the property from the following options:
	+ Wooden
	+ Vinyl
	+ Fiberglass
	+ Aluminum

### 8. Doors (Page 4)
* **8. Doors**: Choose the type of doors used in the property from the following options:
	+ Wooden
	+ Steel
	+ Fiberglass
	+ Aluminum

### 9. Floors (Page 4)
* **9. Floors**: Describe the condition and type of flooring used in the property, including any notable features.

### 10. Foundation (Page 4)
* **10. Foundation**: Select the condition of the property's foundation from the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 11. Walls (Page 4)
* **11. Walls**: Describe the condition and type of walls used in the property, including any notable features.

### 12. Electrical (Page 5)
* **12. Electrical**: Describe the condition and type of electrical systems used in the property, including any notable features.

### 13. Plumbing (Page 5)
* **13. Plumbing**: Describe the condition and type of plumbing systems used in the property, including any notable features.

### 14. Heating (Page 5)
* **14. Heating**: Describe the condition and type of heating systems used in the property, including any notable features.

### 15. Cooling (Page 5)
* **15. Cooling**: Describe the condition and type of cooling systems used in the property, including any notable features.

### 16. Fire Safety (Page 6)
* **16. Fire Safety**: Describe the condition and type of fire safety features used in the property, including any notable features.

### 17. Water Safety (Page 6)
* **17. Water Safety**: Describe the condition and type of water safety features used in the property, including any notable features.

### 18. Safety Equipment (Page 6)
* **18. Safety Equipment**: Choose the types of safety equipment installed in the property from the following options:
	+ Smoke detector
	+ Carbon monoxide detector
	+ Fire extinguisher
	+ First aid kit
