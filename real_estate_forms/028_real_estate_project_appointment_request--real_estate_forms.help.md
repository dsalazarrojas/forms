# Real Estate Project Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an appointment with a real estate professional to discuss a project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your street address in the **Street Address** field.
2. Provide your name in the **Client Name** field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
5. Select a preferred appointment date in the **Preferred Appointment Date** field.
6. Choose a preferred appointment time in the **Preferred Appointment Time** field.
7. Enter the name of the project in the **Project Name** field.
8. Select the type of property related to the project from the **What type of property?** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your street address where the appointment will be conducted.
* **Client Name** (`name`, text, optional): Provide your full name for the client.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Select a date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the appointment.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project.
* **What type of property?** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property related to the project (Residential, Commercial, or Mixed-Use).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with the form, feel free to contact us.
* This form will be reviewed and responded to within a reasonable timeframe after submission.
