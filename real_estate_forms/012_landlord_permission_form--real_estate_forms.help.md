# Landlord Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Permission Form is designed to collect information from tenants and landlords to facilitate a smooth and transparent process for pet-related requests. It captures essential details about the pet, such as breed, size, age, weight, and health status, as well as contact information and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide tenant information, including contact email and phone number.
2. Enter pet-related information, such as breed, size, age, weight, and health status.
3. Enter landlord contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Review and complete any additional fields that apply to your situation.
5. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information** (text): Enter the tenant's name, email, and phone number.
* **Pet Request** (text): Describe the pet-related request you're making.
* **Pet Info** (text): Enter the pet's breed, size, age, weight, and health status.
* **Landlord Permission** (text): Confirm your permission for the pet.
* **Rental Property Info** (text): Enter property details, if applicable.
* **Tenant Signature** (note): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Landlord Signature** (note): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of this permission.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of this permission.
* **Pet Breed** (select_one): Select the pet's breed (e.g., Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish, Other).
* **Pet Size** (select_multiple): Select the pet's size (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, Xlarge).
* **Pet Age** (number): Enter the pet's age.
* **Pet Weight** (number): Enter the pet's weight.
* **Pet Type** (select_multiple): Select the pet's type (e.g., Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish, Other).
* **Pet Name** (text): Enter the pet's name.
* **Pet License** (text): Enter the pet's license number.
* **Pet Health** (select_multiple): Select the pet's health status (e.g., Healthy, Diseased, Unknown).
* **Pet Description** (text): Enter any additional pet-related information.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Landlord Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Landlord Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Landlord Address** (text): Enter your address.
* **Landlord Notes** (note): Add any additional landlord notes.
* **Tenant Notes** (note): Add any additional tenant notes.
* **Form Signature** (note): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Landlord Signature 2** (note): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Date 2** (date): Enter the date of this permission.
* **Time 2** (time): Enter the time of this permission.
