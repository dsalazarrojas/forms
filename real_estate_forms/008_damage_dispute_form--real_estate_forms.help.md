# Damage Dispute Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Damage Dispute Form is designed to collect information about damage disputes involving real property. This form helps users provide all necessary details to resolve conflicts efficiently and fairly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of dispute from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of the damage.
3. Choose the type of damage that occurred.
4. Enter the date of the damage (MM/DD/YYYY format).
5. Add any additional details about the damage.
6. Select the property owner(s) involved in the dispute.
7. Enter the insurance company name, if applicable.
8. Provide the insurance policy number, if applicable.
9. Enter the property manager's name, if applicable.
10. Enter the property manager's email, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dispute Type**: Select the type of damage dispute from the dropdown menu. (Select One)
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the damage. (Text)
* **Damage Type**: Choose the type of damage that occurred. (Select One)
* **Damage Date**: Enter the date of the damage (MM/DD/YYYY format). (Date)
* **Damage Details**: Add any additional details about the damage. (Text)
* **Property Owner**: Select the property owner(s) involved in the dispute. (Select Multiple)
* **Insurance Company**: Enter the insurance company name, if applicable. (Text)
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter the insurance policy number, if applicable. (Text)
* **Property Manager**: Enter the property manager's name, if applicable. (Text)
* **Manager Email**: Enter the property manager's email, if applicable. (Email)
