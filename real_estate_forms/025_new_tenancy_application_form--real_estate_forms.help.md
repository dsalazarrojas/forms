# New Tenancy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive application form for a new tenancy or rental agreement. It collects essential information about the tenant, landlord, and rental details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing your tenant details, including your name, address, contact number, and email.
- Next, fill in the rental history section with any previous rental experiences.
- Move on to the financial information section to provide your annual income and employment status.
- Proceed to the employment verification section to confirm your employment status.
- Then, provide your rental status, move-in and move-out dates, and the type of property you're interested in renting.
- Finally, add any additional comments or notes in the 'Notes' section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Name** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your current employment status from the options provided.
- **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your employment type from the options provided.
- **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, `number`, required): Enter your annual income.
- **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the rent amount you're willing to pay.
- **Move in Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, required): Choose the move-in date for the rental.
- **Move in Time** (`move_in_time`, `time`, required): Choose the move-in time.
- **Move out Date** (`move_out_date`, `date`, required): Choose the move-out date.
- **Move out Time** (`move_out_time`, `time`, required): Choose the move-out time.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of property you're interested in renting.
- **Rental Term** (`rental_term`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the rental term from the options provided.
- **Rental History** (`rental_history`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant rental history.
- **Financial Info** (`financial_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant financial information.
- **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm your application.
- **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm receipt of the application.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the rental application.
