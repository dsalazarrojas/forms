## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Isolated Routine**: This field is required to enter the name of the property being inspected.
* **inspector**: Enter your name as the inspector conducting the assessment.
* **inspector_email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **property_address**: Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **date_of_inspection**: Use the built-in date picker to select the date of the inspection.
* **time_of_inspection**: Use the built-in time selector to choose the time of the inspection.
* **notes**: Record any relevant notes or observations during the inspection.
* **property_status**: Select the property status: Inspected, Not Inspected, or Deferred. This determines the current state of the property.
* **deferred_reason**: If the property status is "Deferred", enter a brief reason for deferring the inspection.
