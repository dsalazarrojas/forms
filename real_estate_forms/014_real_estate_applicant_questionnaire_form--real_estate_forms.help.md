# real_estate_applicant_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a real estate applicant, including personal and financial details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) after the field label.
2. For fields with multiple options, select all applicable choices by clicking on them.
3. For fields with date or numerical options, enter the relevant information as prompted.
