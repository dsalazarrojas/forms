# Prescreen Rental Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescreen Rental Questionnaire is designed to collect demographic data from potential renters to help property managers quickly screen out candidates that do not meet the basic requirements for renting a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and provide any additional information as requested. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and efficient screening process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Property Address** (`rental_property_address`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture the address of the rental property you are interested in.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on any documents or communication related to your rental application.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication related to your rental application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for communication related to your rental application.
* **Rental Date Start** (`rental_date_start`, `date`, optional): Enter the desired start date of your rental agreement.
* **Rental Date End** (`rental_date_end`, `date`, optional): Enter the desired end date of your rental agreement.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to move into the rental property.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, `number`, optional): Enter your current credit score. Please note that a higher credit score is usually preferred by landlords.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, `number`, required): Enter your annual income. This will help us assess your income and employment stability.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Employed
	+ Retired
	+ Unemployed
	+ Disabled
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your employment type from the options provided.
	+ Part-time
	+ Full-time
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Seasonal
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant information about your rental history, such as previous rental properties or evictions.
* **Criminal Record** (`criminal_record`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether you have a criminal record or not.
	+ True
	+ False
