# Mortgage Pre Approval Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather necessary information for a mortgage pre-approval assessment. It will help us evaluate your credit history, employment, income, and assets to determine your eligibility for a mortgage.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your co-borrower (if applicable).
2.  Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
3.  Attach any supporting documents required for the fields that have specific instructions on the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Buyer Info** (`Buyer Info`, text, required: false):
    Please enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you regarding your mortgage pre-approval.
*   **Credit History** (`Credit History`, text, required: false):
    This field is for you to describe your credit history, including any past mortgage or loan payments.
*   **Employment** (`Employment`, text, required: false):
    Provide detailed information about your current or past employment, including job title, company, and length of employment.
*   **Income** (`Income`, number, required: false):
    Enter your annual income to be considered for the mortgage pre-approval.
*   **Assets** (`Assets`, text, required: false):
    List any assets you own, such as properties, vehicles, or investments.
*   **Income Verification** (`Income Verification`, date, required: false):
    If required by your employer or tax authority, attach a document verifying your income for the past two years.
*   **Employment Verification** (`Employment Verification`, time, required: false):
    If required by your employer or tax authority, attach a document verifying your employment details for the past two years.
*   **Credit Score** (`Credit Score`, text, required: false):
    Please provide your current credit score for our review.
*   **Credit Score 2** (`Credit Score 2`, text, required: false):
    This field is for a secondary credit score, if you have multiple scores to report.
*   **Co Borrower Info** (`Co Borrower Info`, text, required: false):
    If you have a co-borrower, please provide their name and contact information so we can get in touch with them.
*   **Co Borrower Employment** (`Co Borrower Employment`, text, required: false):
    This field is for your co-borrower's current or past employment information.
*   **Co Borrower Income** (`Co Borrower Income`, number, required: false):
    Enter your co-borrower's annual income to be considered for the mortgage pre-approval.
*   **Co Borrower Assets** (`Co Borrower Assets`, text, required: false):
    List any assets your co-borrower owns, such as properties, vehicles, or investments.
*   **Co Borrower Credit Score** (`Co Borrower Credit Score`, text, required: false):
    Please provide your co-borrower's current credit score for our review.
