# Land Purchase Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Land Purchase Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from potential buyers interested in purchasing land from our company. This form helps us understand their requirements and preferences, enabling us to provide personalized assistance and support throughout the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your buyer information in the "Buyer Information" section.
2. Specify the details about the property you are interested in, such as type (e.g., Lot, Land, Property).
3. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
4. If applicable, provide the purchase price of the land.
5. If you are working with an agent, enter their details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Information (1)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your personal details, such as name and address, to help us get in touch with you.
* **Property Information (2)** (`text`, required: false): Describe the property you are interested in, including its location, size, and any specific features.
* **Contact Information (3)** (`text`, required: true): Enter your contact phone number and email address so we can reach you for follow-up inquiries.
* **Buyer Phone (4)** (`number`, required: true): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you quickly and efficiently.
* **Buyer Email (5)** (`email`, required: true): Enter your email address to receive updates and communications from us.
* **Property Type (6)** (`select_one`, options: Lot, Land, Property, required: false): Choose the type of property you are interested in (Lot, Land, or Property).
* **Purchase Price (7)** (`number`, required: false): If you know the purchase price, please enter it. We'll use this information to provide you with more detailed quotes and offers.
* **Agent Information (8)** (`text`, required: false): If you are working with an agent, enter their details to facilitate communication and coordination.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the "Property Information" section to help us better understand your requirements.
* If you have any specific questions or concerns, feel free to ask, and we'll be happy to assist you.
* We'll respond to your inquiries and provide personalized support throughout the land purchase process.
