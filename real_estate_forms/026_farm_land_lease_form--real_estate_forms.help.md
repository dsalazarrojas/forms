# Farm Land Lease Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Farm Land Lease Form is a document that helps farm owners and tenants to formalize their agreement for renting or leasing a piece of land. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the lease, including the start and end dates, the size and area of the land, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the farm lease size, which is the area of land being leased.
2. Provide the name and contact information of the land owner.
3. Enter the name and contact information of the tenant.
4. Specify the start and end dates of the lease, including the exact date and time.
5. Enter the farm address, which can include the location and any other relevant details.
6. Indicate whether the farm is allowed to have tenants or not.
7. Write in any lease terms or conditions that need to be respected.
8. Enter the rent price for the farm land.
9. Specify the duration of the lease, including the start and end dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Lease Size** (`farm_lease_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the farm land being leased. This can be expressed in acres or other units of measurement.
* **Land Owner Name** (`land_owner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity that owns the farm land.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity that will be leasing the farm land.
* **Tenant Contact** (`tenant_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the tenant, including their address and phone number.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the lease, including the exact date and time.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the lease, including the exact date and time.
* **Farm Land Area** (`farm_land_area`, number, optional): Enter the area of the farm land being leased.
* **Allowed Tenants** (`allowed_tenants`, select_multiple, optional): Check yes or no if the farm allows tenants or not.
* **Farm Address** (`farm_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the farm land, including the location and any other relevant details.
* **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`, text, optional): Write in any lease terms or conditions that need to be respected.
* **Rent Price** (`rent_price`, number, optional): Enter the rent price for the farm land.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the lease, including the start and end dates.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* Check the lease terms and conditions to ensure they are clear and understandable.
* Save the completed form for future reference.
