# real_estate_offer_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about real estate offers, including buyer and property details, offer terms, and status updates. It's intended for use within a real estate business or platform to manage offers, track progress, and facilitate communication between parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide buyer information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter property details, such as location, type, and price.
3. Select the relevant offer details and terms accepted.
4. Update the offer status as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Buyer Information** (`buyer_info`, text, not required): Enter the buyer's name.
- **Property Information** (`property_info`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the property being offered.
- **Offer Details** (`offer_details`, text, not required): Provide additional details about the offer, such as any special conditions or notes.
- **Buyer's Offer** (`buyers_offer`, select_multiple, not required): Select the options that apply to the buyer's offer.
- **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the buyer's phone number.
- **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, email, not required): Enter the buyer's email address.
- **Property Location** (`property_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the property.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of property (House, Apartment, Condo, Townhouse).
- **Property Price** (`property_price`, number, not required): Enter the price of the property.
- **Seller Information** (`seller_info`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the seller.
- **Seller Name** (`seller_name`, text, not required): Enter the seller's name.
- **Seller Phone** (`seller_phone`, text, not required): Enter the seller's phone number.
- **Seller Email** (`seller_email`, email, not required): Enter the seller's email address.
- **Offer Accepted** (`offer_accepted`, select_one, not required): Select whether the offer is accepted or not.
- **Offer Comments** (`offer_comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments about the offer.
- **Offer Terms Accepted** (`offer_terms_accepted`, select_one, not required): Select whether the offer terms are accepted or not.
- **Offer Terms Status** (`offer_terms_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the offer terms.
- **Seller Terms Accepted** (`seller_terms_accepted`, select_one, not required): Select whether the seller terms are accepted or not.
- **Seller Terms Status** (`seller_terms_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the seller terms.
- **Offer Date** (`offer_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the offer.
- **Offer Time** (`offer_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the offer.
- **Offer Status** (`offer_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the offer.
- **Seller Status** (`seller_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the seller.
- **Offer Terms Status** (`offer_terms_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the offer terms.
- **Seller Terms Status** (`seller_terms_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the seller terms.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes about the offer.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about the offer.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that you're filling out the correct information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask a colleague or seek further guidance.
