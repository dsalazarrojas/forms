# real_estate_closing_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "real_estate_closing_checklist" is used to gather necessary information for the real estate closing process. This form helps to ensure that all required documents and tasks are completed before finalizing the sale or purchase of a property. The form includes fields for property address, seller and buyer information, loan details, closing date and time, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the seller agent is involved in the closing process.
3. Select the type of title report required from the "Title Report" dropdown list.
4. Enter the appraisal date (if applicable) in the "Appraisal" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether loan documents are required.
6. Enter the escrow instructions in the "Escrow Instructions" field.
7. Enter the closing date and time in the "Closing Date Time" field.
8. Select the type of property from the "Property Type" dropdown list.
9. Enter the loan amount in the "Loan Amount" field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the buyer agent is involved in the closing process.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the buyer acknowledged the closing information.
12. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the lender acknowledged the closing information.
13. Enter any additional escrow instructions in the "Escrow Instructions 2" field.
14. Enter the escrow fees (if applicable) in the "Escrow Fees" field.
