This form is designed for sales lead tracking and management. It collects information about the lead source, contact details, and lead status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the lead source from the options provided.
2. Enter the full name of the person.
3. Enter the email address of the person.
4. Enter the phone number of the person.
5. Enter the company name of the person.
6. Select the job title of the person.
7. Select the sales agent responsible for the lead.
8. Select the lead status from the provided options.
9. Choose the source type of the lead.
10. Enter the lead value.
11. Select the currency of the lead value.
12. Enter the amount of the lead value.
13. Enter the date of the lead value.

* **Lead Source (Page 1)**: Enter the source where the lead was generated (e.g., email, phone, referral, etc.).
* **Full Name (Page 2)**: Enter the full name of the person associated with the lead.
* **Email (Page 2)**: Enter the email address of the person.
* **Phone Number (Page 2)**: Enter the phone number of the person.
* **Company (Page 2)**: Enter the name of the company associated with the lead.
* **Job Title (Page 2)**: Enter the job title of the person.
* **Sales Agent (Page 3)**: Select the sales agent responsible for the lead.
* **Lead Status (Page 3)**: Select the status of the lead from the provided options (e.g., new, hot, cold, warm).
* **Source Type (Page 3)**: Choose the type of source that generated the lead (e.g., email, phone, referral, etc.).
* **Lead Value (Page 4)**: Enter the value of the lead.
* **Lead Value Currency (Page 4)**: Select the currency of the lead value.
* **Lead Value Amount (Page 4)**: Enter the amount of the lead value.
