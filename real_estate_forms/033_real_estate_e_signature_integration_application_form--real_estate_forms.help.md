# Real Estate E-Signature Integration Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Real Estate E-Signature Integration Application Form is designed to gather essential information from real estate teams to facilitate a smooth integration process with E-signature platforms. It's recommended that only real estate team members fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the Integration Platform from the list of options.
2. Choose the Status of your Integration Request.
3. Optionally, add any additional Comments or Notes related to your request.
4. Enter your Email and Phone for contact purposes.
5. Provide any additional Integration Request Notes that are relevant to your team's request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Real Estate E-Signature Integration Application** (`real_estate_e_signature_integration_application_form`): This is the form title and the start of the application process.
* **Real Estate Team** (`real_estate_team_name`): Here, you should enter your real estate team name as it appears on your business cards or within the company directory. This helps with team identity and organization.
* **Office Name** (`real_estate_office_name`): Enter your office name, as this is a crucial identifier for team affiliation and location.
* **Integration Request** (`real_estate_integration_request`): Provide a brief, one-sentence summary of the reason for your E-signature integration request.
* **Integration Platform** (`real_estate_integration_platform`): This selection is a critical field. Please choose the appropriate platform your team is requesting for integration:
	+ **Broker**: This is typically associated with real estate brokerages.
	+ **Accounting**: This could be related to accounting software or services specific to real estate teams.
	+ **Document Management**: This might be linked to document management solutions for real estate teams.
* **Integration Request Status** (`real_estate_integration_request_status`): Select the current status of your request:
	+ **Pending**: This indicates that your request is still being processed.
	+ **Approved**: Your request has been accepted, and you can proceed with integration.
	+ **Rejected**: Your request was not accepted. Please resubmit with updated information.
* **Integration Date** (`real_estate_integration_date`): Enter the desired date for integration, in date format (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **Integration Time** (`real_estate_integration_time`): Choose the preferred time for integration, using 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Comments** (`real_estate_comments`): Use this space to add any relevant comments or notes about your E-signature integration request.
* **Email** (`real_estate_email`): Enter your work email address for contact purposes within the company.
* **Phone** (`real_estate_phone`): Enter your work phone number for easy contact within the company.
* **Integration Request Notes** (`real_estate_integration_request_notes`): Here, you can add any additional details or context regarding your E-signature integration request.

## Tips
- Be accurate when selecting your Integration Platform to avoid delays in the process.
- Ensure that your real estate team name is correctly registered as it appears company-wide.
- Enter your email and phone number so that your team can be contacted efficiently regarding the progress of your E-signature integration request.
