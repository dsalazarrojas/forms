# Buyer Broker Compensation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms and conditions of the relationship between a real estate buyer and a broker. It outlines the compensation, responsibilities, and duration of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details and those of your broker.
2. Carefully review the terms and conditions before signing the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): The full name of the buyer.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, email, required): The email of the buyer.
* **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, text, required): The contact number of the buyer.
* **Broker Name** (`broker_name`, text, required): The full name of the broker.
* **Brokerage Company** (`broker_company`, text, required): The name of the brokerage company.
* **Broker Email** (`broker_email`, email, required): The contact email of the broker.
* **Broker Phone** (`broker_phone`, text, required): The contact number of the broker.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): The date the agreement is effective.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, not required): The address of the property being purchased.
* **Expected Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, required): The estimated or agreed price of the property.
* **Compensation Type** (`compensation_type`, select_one, required): How the broker will be compensated (Percentage of purchase price, Flat fee, Hourly rate, Combination).
* **Compensation Amount** (`compensation_amount`, text, required): The dollar amount or percentage of the compensation.
* **Source of Compensation** (`compensation_source`, select_one, required): Who pays the broker (Seller, Buyer, Both parties, Divided).
* **Commission Percentage** (`commission_percentage`, number, not required): If applicable, the commission percentage.
* **Type of Representation** (`representation_type`, select_one, required): The type of representation (Exclusive Representation, Limited Representation, Transactional Representation).
* **Duration of Agreement** (`agreement_duration`, text, required): The duration of the agreement.
* **Special Conditions or Modifications** (`special_conditions`, text, not required): Any additions to the agreement.
* **I Accept the Terms of This Agreement** (`agreement_accepted`, select_one, required): Must agree to proceed.

Note: Review the form carefully before signing to ensure all parties have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions.
