## right_of_first_refusal_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "right_of_first_refusal_request_form" is designed to collect information for a right of first refusal request, which is a formal declaration that one or more specific rights, titles, interests, easements, and other rights, or interest in a parcel of land are being reserved by an individual or entity until a specific action is taken or a time period has passed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide property details, including any relevant information about the property, such as its location and condition.
2. Enter contact information, including the person or entity requesting the right of first refusal.
3. Describe the request details, including the specific rights or interests being reserved.
4. Enter the date of the request.
5. Choose the current status of the request.
6. Indicate whether the request was made by the property owner or another entity.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the property being requested, including its location, size, and condition.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the name, address, and contact information of the person or entity requesting the right of first refusal.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required: false): Describe the specific rights or interests being reserved, such as a right of way or an easement.
* **Date Of Request** (`date_of_request`, date, required: false): Enter the date the request was made.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the current status of the request, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether the request was made by the property owner or another entity, such as a third party.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the request that may be helpful for further reference.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the property and request details.
* Double-check the request status and ensure it accurately reflects the current situation.
* Be clear and concise in your comments and additional comments.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your supervisor or the property administrator.
