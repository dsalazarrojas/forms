# Landlord And Tenant Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord And Tenant Disclosure Form is designed to collect information about a rental property, including landlord and tenant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the **landlord_information** field.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the **tenant_information** field.
3. Indicate your occupancy status in the **landlord_occupancy** field.
4. Indicate your occupancy status in the **tenant_occupancy** field.
5. Select the **landlord_tenants** field to choose from a list of pre-defined tenants.
6. Select the **tenant_tenants** field to choose from a list of pre-defined tenants.
7. Enter any additional tenant information in the **landlord_tenants_other** field.
8. Describe the terms of your rental agreement in the **rental_agreement** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **landlord_and_tenant_disclosure_form**: This is the form title.
* **landlord_information**: Enter your name and contact information as the landlord.
* **tenant_information**: Enter your name and contact information as the tenant.
* **landlord_occupancy**: Check whether you are currently occupying the property or not.
* **tenant_occupancy**: Check whether you are currently occupying the property or not.
* **landlord_tenants**: Choose from a list of pre-defined tenants.
* **tenant_tenants**: Choose from a list of pre-defined tenants.
* **landlord_tenants_other**: Enter any additional tenant information not listed in the previous field.
* **rental_agreement**: Describe the terms of your rental agreement.
