# Property Financing Assistance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Financing Assistance Application form is designed to collect contact information and property details from individuals seeking financing assistance. This information will be used to provide relevant assistance to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the property you are seeking financing for, including its address and any relevant information.
3. Describe the loan details you are seeking, including the amount and type of loan.
4. If applicable, provide information about the loan investigator or representative handling your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`text`, optional): This section collects your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Property Details (property_details)** (`text`, optional): This section is for providing details about the property you are seeking financing for, including its address.
* **Loan Details (loan_details)** (`text`, optional): This section is for describing the loan details you are seeking, including the amount and type of loan.
* **Loan Investigator (loan_investigator)** (`text`, optional): If applicable, provide information about the loan investigator or representative handling your case.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to ensure a smooth and efficient review process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the designated contact person for assistance.
* This form is intended for gathering information and not for collecting sensitive financial data.
