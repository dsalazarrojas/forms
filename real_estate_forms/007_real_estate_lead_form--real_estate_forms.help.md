# Real Estate Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Real Estate Lead Form is designed to collect essential information from potential customers interested in buying or renting a property. This form helps our team understand their requirements and preferences, allowing us to provide personalized services and support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Lead Generation" option from the first question.
2. Choose your preferred method of interaction with our team ("Yes" or "No").
3. Provide your customer details (full name, email, phone number, or address).
4. Select the type of property you are interested in (property type or location).
5. Indicate your purpose for buying, renting, or other (e.g., to sell your current property).
6. If applicable, select your preferred commute time.
7. Choose your desired move-in date and preferred move-in month and year.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Generation** (lead_generation_form, select_multiple, required: false): 
  This field asks you to choose how you would like to interact with our team.
* **Customer Details** (customer_details, select_one, required: false): 
  Enter your full name, email, phone number, or address to help us contact you.
* **Property Details** (property_details, select_one, required: false): 
  Select the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, condominium) or location you are interested in.
* **Customer Purpose** (customer_purpose, select_one, required: false): 
  Choose your purpose for buying or renting a property (e.g., to sell your current property, etc.).
* **Preferred Commute** (preferred_commute, number, required: false): 
  If applicable, select a time frame (e.g., 1, 2, etc.) for your preferred commute to the property.
* **Desired Move In Date** (desired_move_in_date, date, required: false): 
  Enter a date for when you would like to move into the property.
* **Preferred Move In Month** (preferred_move_in_month, select_one, required: false): 
  Select a month for when you would like to move into the property (e.g., Month 1, Month 2, etc.).
* **Preferred Move In Year** (preferred_move_in_year, select_one, required: false): 
  Choose a year for when you would like to move into the property (e.g., Year 1, Year 2, etc.).
