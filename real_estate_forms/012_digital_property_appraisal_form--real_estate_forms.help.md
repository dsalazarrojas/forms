# Digital Property Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is meant to assist users in understanding the purpose and usage of the Digital Property Appraisal Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Home Address" field with the property's address.
2. Enter the appraiser's name and other relevant contact information in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide the date of sale (or other relevant date information).
4. Enter the purchase price of the property.
5. Select the current use of the property (residential, commercial, or industrial).
6. Select the type of property (single-family home, townhouse, condo, or apartment).
7. Enter the property's condition, size, and other relevant characteristics.
8. Enter the square footage and lot size of the property.
9. Provide the year the property was built.
10. Select the property's status (active, inactive, or vacant).
11. Enter any property use restrictions.
12. Enter the appraisal date.
13. Enter the property's value.
14. Leave the appraiser's signature, name, title, and license fields blank, as they are not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the property being appraised.
* **Appraiser** (`appraiser`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the appraiser's name and other relevant contact information.
* **Date of Sale** (`date_of_sale`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the property was sold (or other relevant date information).
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price at which the property was purchased.
* **Current Use** (`current_use`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current use of the property (residential, commercial, or industrial).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of property (single-family home, townhouse, condo, or apartment).
* **Property Condition** (`property_condition`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the condition of the property.
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the square footage of the property.
* **Lot Size** (`lot_size`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the size of the property's lot.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the property (active, inactive, or vacant).
* **Property Use Restrictions** (`property_use_restrictions`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any property use restrictions.
* **Appraisal Date** (`appraisal_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the appraisal.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the value of the property.
* **Appraiser Signature** (`appraiser_signature`, `text`, required/optional): This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Appraiser Name** (`appraiser_name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Appraiser Title** (`appraiser_title`, `text`, required/optional): This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Appraiser License** (`appraiser_license`, `text`, required/optional): This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Office Address** (`office_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the office address of the appraiser.
* **Office City** (`office_city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city of the office.
* **Office State** (`office_state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state of the office.
* **Office Zip** (`office_zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the office.
* **Office Phone** (`office_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the office.
