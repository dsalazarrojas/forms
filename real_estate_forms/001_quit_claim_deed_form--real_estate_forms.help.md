# quit_claim_deed_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to aid property owners in filing a quit claim deed, transferring property ownership to another party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields with the required information about the property and the transaction.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Double-check the information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Owner** (`property_owner`, text, optional): The name of the current property owner.
* **Graantee Name** (`grantee_name`, text, optional): The name of the person or entity receiving the property transfer.
* **Consideration** (`consideration`, number, optional): The monetary value of the property transfer.
* **Deed Date** (`deed_date`, date, optional): The date the deed was created.
* **Deed Recording Date** (`deed_recording_date`, date, optional): The date the deed was recorded.
* **County Taxes** (`county_taxes`, number, optional): The amount of taxes owed to the county.
* **State Taxes** (`state_taxes`, number, optional): The amount of taxes owed to the state.
* **Other Taxes** (`other_taxes`, text, optional): Any other taxes related to the property transfer.
* **Mortgagee Name** (`mortgagee_name`, text, optional): The name of the person or entity with a mortgage on the property.
* **Mortgage Date** (`mortgage_date`, date, optional): The date the mortgage was taken.
* **Mortgage Recording Date** (`mortgage_recording_date`, date, optional): The date the mortgage was recorded.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): The address of the property being transferred.
* **County** (`county`, select_one, optional): The county where the property is located.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): The state where the property is located.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): The city where the property is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): The zip code of the property location.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, optional): A description of the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Acreage** (`acreage`, number, optional): The size of the property in acres.
* **Lot Number** (`lot_number`, text, optional): The lot number of the property.
* **Deed Book** (`deed_book`, text, optional): The deed book number.
* **Deed Page** (`deed_page`, text, optional): The deed page number.
* **Deed Receipt** (`deed_receipt`, text, optional): The receipt for the deed.
* **Recording District** (`recording_district`, text, optional): The district where the deed was recorded.
* **County Tax Dollars** (`county_tax_dollars`, number, optional): The amount of county taxes.
* **Total Dollars** (`total_dollars`, number, optional): The total amount of money involved in the property transfer.
