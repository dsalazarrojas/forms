# Estate Planning Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help gather information about your estate and preferences for estate planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields first. The form is not strictly required, so you can choose to answer only the questions that are relevant to you.
2. Answer each question carefully, and make sure to provide accurate information about your estate and preferences.
3. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can use the "Note" field to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: This field is intended to capture your basic information, such as your name, contact details, etc.
  * (`client_info`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
  * Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Asset Values**: This field is meant to gather information about the value of your assets.
  * (`asset_values`, `number`, required/optional: not required)
  * Enter the approximate value of your assets, such as cash, investments, or real estate.
* **Assets Owed**: This field is intended to gather information about any assets you may have borrowed or have a financial obligation for.
  * (`assets_owed`, `number`, required/optional: not required)
  * Enter the approximate amount you have borrowed or owe.
* **Estate Goals**: Please select all the goals that apply to your estate planning.
  * (`estate_goals`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: not required)
  * Goal A, Goal B, and Goal C represent different objectives for your estate planning.
* **Primary Residence**: Select the option that best describes your primary residence.
  * (`primary_residence`, `select_one`, required/optional: not required)
  * This is an important field as it may impact inheritance and property distribution.
* **Date of Death** and **Time of Death**: Please note that these fields seem out of place in this form. You can consider removing or replacing them with more relevant fields.
  * (`date_of_death` and `time_of_death`, `date` and `time`, required/optional: not required)
  * These fields might be better suited for other types of forms or documents.
* **Note**: If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them here.
  * (`note`, `note`, required/optional: not required)
  * This field is meant to be a space for any additional information that doesn't fit in other fields.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
  * (`email`, `email`, required/optional: not required)
  * This field is meant to be for professional or emergency contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  * (`phone`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
  * This field is meant for emergency contact or other important phone numbers.
* **Financial Advisor**: Select all financial advisors that apply.
  * (`financial_advisor`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: not required)
  * This field is meant to help us understand who you work with for financial planning.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields honestly and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, it's okay to ask for clarification.
* You can skip any fields that are not relevant to you or your estate.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
