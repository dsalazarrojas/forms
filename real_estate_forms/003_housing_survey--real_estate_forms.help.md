# Housing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housing Survey is a form designed to collect information from users about their housing preferences and experiences. This data will help us better understand your needs and inform future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first_name and last_name fields with your name.
2. Enter your address in the address field.
3. Type your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone_number field.
5. Select one of the housing experience options in the experience field.
6. (Optional) If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the comments field.
7. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address, including the street address, apartment number (if applicable), city, and state.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address. This will help us contact you with any relevant information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Housing Experience** (`experience`, select one, optional): Select one of the options that best describe your housing experience.
* **Prophet's Name** (`prophets_name`, text, optional): Unfortunately, this field seems to be a typographical error. Please ignore it or fill it in with any name that comes to mind.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to include them here.
