# Open House Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Open House Check In Form" is a registration or contact form for individuals visiting or interacting with a property. It is used by the property management or administration team to track visitors, facilitate communication, and gather notes or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address that you regularly check.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the "Date and Time" of your visit in the calendar field.
6. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
7. Enter the property number in the "Property Number" field.
8. Choose your preferred contact method from the "Contact Method" dropdown menu. You can select one of the following:
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Text
9. Choose your preferred contact preference from the "Contact Preference" dropdown menu. You can select one or more of the following:
	* Call
	* Text
	* Email
10. Enter any additional notes or comments about your visit or your preferences in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your family or surname.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date and Time** (date): Select the date and time of your visit.
* **Property Address** (text): Enter the address of the property you are visiting.
* **Property Number** (text): Enter the number of the property you are visiting.
* **Contact Method** (select_one): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text
* **Contact Preference** (select_multiple): Choose one or more preferred contact methods:
	+ Call
	+ Text
	+ Email
* **Notes** (note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your visit or preferences.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the email address and phone number you enter are valid and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the "Notes" field.
