# tenant_request_for_accommodations_covid_19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for tenants to request accommodations due to the COVID-19 pandemic. The purpose of this form is to help the housing administration understand the tenant's circumstances and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the required information.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the housing administration.
3. Ensure that you are not submitting duplicate requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (1)
  This is the title of the request. Please enter a brief title that describes your accommodation request.
- **Accommodation Type** (2)
  Select the accommodation type that best fits your needs. Choose from Studio Apartment, 1 Bedroom, 2 Bedrooms, 3 Bedrooms, or Other.
- **Number of Guests** (3)
  Enter the total number of guests that will be occupying the accommodation.
- **Start Date** (4)
  Enter the date you would like to start your accommodation.
- **End Date** (5)
  Enter the date you would like your accommodation to end.
- **Reason for Request** (6)
  Please provide a brief explanation for your accommodation request, including any relevant details or circumstances.
- **Additional Comments** (7)
  This is an optional field for you to provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for the housing administration to consider.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the housing administration.
- Review your request carefully before submitting to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
