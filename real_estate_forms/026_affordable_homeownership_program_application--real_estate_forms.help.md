# Affordable Homeownership Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants who are interested in applying for the Affordable Homeownership Program. The program aims to provide assistance with home ownership for low-to-moderate income households. The form is designed to collect essential information about the applicant's background, financial situation, and housing preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and true information.
2. Ensure that you have answered all the questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Applicant Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Co-Applicant Name**: If you are applying with a co-applicant, enter their complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for the primary applicant.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your SSN for the primary applicant (for credit check purposes).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email for program communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **Current Address**: Enter your current residential address.
* **Current Housing Status**: Select one of the following:
	+ Renting
	+ Owning
	+ Living with relatives/friends
	+ Temporary housing
	+ Other
* **Household Income Information**: This is a note section for additional information about your household income.
* **Primary Income Source**: Select one of the following:
	+ Employment
	+ Self-employment
	+ Government benefits
	+ Disability
	+ Retirement
	+ Other
* **Employer Name**: If you are employed, enter the name of your current employer.
* **Employment Length**: If you are employed, enter the length of time you have been with your current employer.
* **Annual Household Income**: Enter the total annual income for all household members.
* **Additional Income Sources**: List any additional sources of household income.
* **Household Composition**: Enter the number of people in your household (including yourself).
* **Preferred Location**: Enter the area or neighborhood you are interested in.
* **Home Type Preference**: Select one of the following:
	+ Single-family home
	+ Townhouse
	+ Condominium
	+ Cooperative
	+ Other
* **Number of Bedrooms Needed**: Select one of the following:
	+ Studio
	+ 1 bedroom
	+ 2 bedrooms
	+ 3 bedrooms
	+ 4+ bedrooms
* **Accessibility Needs**: Select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Accessibility Details**: If you select True, specify your accessibility requirements.
* **Financial Information**: This is a note section for additional information about your financial situation.
* **Down Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you have available for down payment.
* **Total Savings Amount**: Enter the total amount of savings available.
* **Credit Score Range**: Select one of the following:
	+ Under 500
	+ 500-599
	+ 600-699
	+ 700-799
	+ 800+
	+ Don't know
* **Monthly Debt Obligations**: Enter the total monthly debt payments (credit cards, loans, etc.).
* **Homeownership Goals**: This is a note section for information about your home ownership goals.
* **Homeownership Motivation**: Enter why you want to become a homeowner.
* **Desired Purchase Timeline**: Select one of the following:
	+ Within 6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2+ years
* **Housing Counseling Completion**: Select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ In progress
* **Special Programs Eligibility**: This is a note section for additional information about your eligibility for special programs.
* **First-Time Homebuyer Status**: Select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Veteran Status**: Select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Disability Status**: Select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Income Limit Compliance**: Select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Application Certifications**: This is a note section for additional information about your application.
* **Information Accuracy Certification**: Select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Electronic Signature**: Enter your electronic signature to submit your application.
