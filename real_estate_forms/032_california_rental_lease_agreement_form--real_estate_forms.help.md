# California Rental Lease Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a template for a rental lease agreement in California, used to outline the terms and conditions of renting a property. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and comprehensively to avoid any potential disputes or misunderstandings between landlords and tenants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the landlord's information section, including name, address, phone number, and email address.
2. Fill out the tenant's information section, including name, address, phone number, and email address.
3. Specify the property's details, such as address, unit number, type, number of bedrooms, and number of bathrooms.
4. Choose the lease start and end dates.
5. Enter the monthly rent amount, rent due day of the month, and security deposit amount (in dollars).
6. Select whether pets are allowed and specify the maximum number of occupants.
7. Choose which utilities are included in the rent.
8. Select which services the tenant is responsible for.
9. Enter the agreement date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the landlord or property owner.
* **Landlord Address** (3, text, required): Enter the address of the landlord or property.
* **Landlord Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter the phone number of the landlord.
* **Landlord Email Address** (5, email, required): Enter the email address of the landlord.
* **Tenant Name** (7, text, required): Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Address** (8, text, required): Enter the address of the tenant.
* **Tenant Phone Number** (9, text, required): Enter the phone number of the tenant.
* **Tenant Email Address** (10, email, required): Enter the email address of the tenant.
* **Property Address** (12, text, required): Enter the address of the property being rented.
* **Unit or Suite Number** (13, text, optional): If the property has multiple units, enter the unit or suite number.
* **Property Type** (14, select_one, required): Choose the type of property (House, Apartment, Condo, Townhouse, Commercial, or Other).
* **Number of Bedrooms** (15, number, required): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (16, number, required): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Lease Start Date** (18, date, required): Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date** (19, date, required): Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Lease Term (months)** (20, number, optional): If desired, enter the lease term in months (not required).
* **Monthly Rent Amount** (21, number, required): Enter the monthly rent amount (in dollars).
* **Rent Due Day of Month** (22, number, required): Enter the day of the month when rent is due.
* **Security Deposit Amount** (23, number, required): Enter the security deposit amount (in dollars).
* **Are Pets Allowed?** (25, select_one, required): Choose whether pets are allowed (Yes, No, With approval).
* **Maximum Number of Occupants** (26, number, required): Enter the maximum number of occupants allowed.
* **Which Utilities Are Included?** (28, select_multiple, required): Choose which utilities are included (Select all that apply: Water, Gas, Electricity, Trash, Internet, None).
* **Tenant Shall Be Responsible For** (29, select_multiple, optional): Choose which services the tenant is responsible for (Select all that apply: Maintenance, Repairs (minor), Yard work, Snow removal, Other).
* **Agreement Date** (30, date, required): Enter the date the agreement was signed.

## Tips

* Fill out the form accurately and comprehensively to avoid potential disputes or misunderstandings between landlords and tenants.
* Review the form carefully before signing the agreement.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out according to California state and local laws and regulations.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult a lawyer or real estate expert for clarification.
