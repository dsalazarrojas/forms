# real_estate_marketing_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The real_estate_marketing_intake_form is a tool for real estate agents to provide detailed information about a property listing for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as `true` in the form.
2. Be as accurate and detailed as possible in your responses.
3. If a field does not apply to your property, you can skip it or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Client Name** (text, required)
Please provide the name of the client or property owner.

### 2. **Contact Information** (text, required)
Enter the contact information for the client or property owner, including their name and other relevant contact details.

### 3. **Phone Number** (text, required)
Enter the phone number of the client or property owner.

### 4. **Email** (email, required)
Enter a valid email address for the client or property owner.

### 5. **Property Type** (select_one, optional)
Select the type of property from the options provided:
	* Single Family Home
	* Townhouse
	* Apartment
	* Commercial
	* Other

### 6. **Property Location** (text, required)
Enter the location of the property.

### 7. **Listing Price** (number, required)
Enter the listing price of the property in the format of a number with decimal places, for example: 123,456.78

### 8. **Marketing Goals** (text, required)
Describe the marketing goals for the property listing. This could include information about the target audience, marketing channels, and any specific promotions or activities.
