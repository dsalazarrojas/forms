# Real Estate Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Communication Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about a property's details, including its type, address, and owner's contact information. This form is used to communicate with clients and agents in the real estate industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the property type (House, Apartment, or Condo) in the "Property Type" field.
2. Enter the address of the property in the "Address" field.
3. If you are not required to provide a city, you can leave the "City" field blank.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address, in the "Contact Info" field.
5. Select a date and time for the appointment in the "Schedule Appointment" field.
6. Choose the type of appointment (Sales, Rental, or Other) in the "Appointment Type" field.
7. Select the time for the appointment in the "Appointment Time" field.
8. Enter any additional message in the "Message" field.
9. If applicable, provide feedback in the "Feedback" field.
10. If applicable, upload documents (ID, Passport, or Other) in the "Document" field.
11. Select the type of file to upload (Resume, ID Scan, or Other) in the "Upload" field.
12. Confirm the upload by selecting "True" in the "Confirm Upload" field.
13. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property (House, Apartment, or Condo) to provide more detailed information about the property.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property, including the house number and street name.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the property is located, if applicable.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your email address.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Schedule Appointment** (`schedule_appointment`, date, optional): Select a date and time for the appointment.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of appointment (Sales, Rental, or Other).
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Select the time for the appointment.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Enter any additional message.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_multiple, required): Select feedback options (True or False).
* **Document** (`document`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, upload documents (ID, Passport, or Other).
* **Upload** (`upload`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of file to upload (Resume, ID Scan, or Other).
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, optional): Enter the file name or URL to upload.
* **Confirm Upload** (`confirm_upload`, select_one, required): Confirm the upload by selecting "True".
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
* **Error** (`error`, text, optional): If there is an error in the form, enter the error message.
* **Success** (`success`, text, optional): Enter a success message.
* **Conditional** (`conditional`, text, optional): Enter any conditional text.
* **Conditional Logic** (`conditional_logic`, text, required): Enter the conditional logic.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter the form ID.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the form version.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check your contact information and email address.
* Select the correct property type and appointment type.
* Confirm the upload by selecting "True" in the "Confirm Upload" field.
* Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
