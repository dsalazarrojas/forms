# real_estate_sales_manager_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for the position of real estate sales manager. The form is used to evaluate the applicant's qualifications and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

1. Fill in the real_estate_agent_info field with your real estate agent information, this could be a license number or any other relevant real estate identification number.
2. In the sales_experience field, enter the number of years you have had sales experience.
3. In the manager_references field, select how you would rate the quality of your previous manager's references.
4. In the contact_info field, enter your contact information, this could be your email address and phone number.
