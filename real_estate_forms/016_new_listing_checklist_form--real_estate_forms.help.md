# new_listing_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a new listing for real estate purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property being listed.
2. **Sale Price**: Enter the sale price of the property.
3. **Square Footage**: Enter the square footage of the property.
4. **Lot Size**: Enter the lot size of the property.
5. **Number of Bedrooms**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
6. **Number of Bathrooms**: Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
7. **List Date**: Enter the date the property was listed.
8. **Sale Date**: Enter the date the property was sold.
9. **Listing Agent**: Enter the name of the listing agent.
10. **Buyer Information**: Enter any relevant information about the buyer.
11. **Listing Status**: Select the current status of the listing.
12. **Property Type**: Select the type of property (e.g., single family, condo, townhouse, duplex).
13. **Amenities**: Select any amenities available with the property (e.g., swimming pool, gym, security system).
14. **Photos**: Select types of photos available (e.g., outdoor, indoor, panorama).
15. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the property.
16. **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information for the listing.
17. **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
18. **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number.
19. **Email**: Enter the contact email.
20. **Address**: Enter the address of the listing.
21. **City**: Enter the city of the listing.
22. **State**: Enter the state of the listing.
23. **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the listing.
24. **County**: Enter the county of the listing.
25. **Status**: Select the status of the listing (e.g., under contract, sold, active).
