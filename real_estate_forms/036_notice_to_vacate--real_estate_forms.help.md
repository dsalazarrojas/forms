# notice_to_vacate - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "notice_to_vacate" form is a document that a tenant or property manager uses to notify the landlord or property owner that they plan to vacate a rented property. It's a crucial step in the process to ensure a smooth and lawful termination of the tenancy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Property Id of the rented property.
2. Provide the Property Name.
3. Enter the address of the property.
4. Enter the Landlord Name.
5. Enter the Date of Vacate.
6. Provide the Reason for Vacate.
7. Enter the Rent Amount.
8. Enter the Move Out Date.
9. Select the Notice Period.
10. Confirm whether or not you will provide a formal notice (Notice to Vacate).
11. Finally, sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Id**: Enter the unique identifier of the rented property.
* **Property Name**: The name of the property that you are vacating.
* **Address**: The address of the property.
* **Landlord Name**: The name of the landlord or property owner.
* **Date of Vacate**: Enter the date when you plan to vacate the property.
* **Reason for Vacate**: Explain the reason for vacating the property.
* **Rent Amount**: Enter the amount you are paying for rent.
* **Move Out Date**: Enter the date when you actually move out of the property.
* **Notice Period**: Select the notice period you are providing (Thirty (30) days, Fifteen (15) days, or Seven (7) days).
* **Notice to Vacate**: Confirm whether you will provide a formal notice ("True") or not ("False").
* **Signature**: Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the information provided.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill in all required fields correctly and accurately.
- Verify the information you provide is in line with your lease agreement and local laws.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
- Submit this form only after you have reviewed and understood the information.
