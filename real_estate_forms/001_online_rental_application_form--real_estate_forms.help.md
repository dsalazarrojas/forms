To create an online rental application form, the purpose is to gather information from users about a rental property they are interested in, including details about the property type and category, description, and contact information. The form also aims to gather the user's contact and address details, such as date and time of the visit, and any additional comments they may have. The fields are designed to be filled out by users who want to inquire about or rent a property listed on the platform.

This guide will walk you through each field of the form and provide explanations on how to complete it correctly.

# online_rental_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a platform for users to submit their rental inquiries or applications for a listed property. It is intended for users to provide accurate and complete information about themselves and their rental needs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:
1. Select the category of the property you are interested in.
2. Enter a brief description of the property.
3. Enter a valid form ID.
4. Select the category of the property (if different from the first category).
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about the property.
6. Enter your contact number.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Select the date and time of your intended visit.
9. Enter your address.
10. Enter the city where you will be visiting.
11. Enter the state where you will be visiting.
12. Enter the ZIP code of your location.
13. Select an assigned tool for your visit.
14. Select the status of the form (active or inactive).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Online Rental Application Form Title** (1, text, required): This field should contain the title of the rental property you are inquiring about or applying for.
* **Category** (2, select_one, required): Select the category of the property you are interested in from the options provided (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Description** (3, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the property or your rental needs.
* **Form ID** (4, number, required): Enter a unique and valid form ID.
* **Category (select_multiple)** (5, select_multiple, required): Select the category of the property (if different from the first category).
* **Description (optional)** (6, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the property.
* **Contact Number** (7, text, required): Enter a valid contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (8, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Date** (9, date, required): Select the date of your intended visit.
* **Time** (10, time, required): Select the time of your intended visit.
* **Address** (11, text, required): Enter your address where you will be visiting.
* **City** (12, text, required): Enter the city where you will be visiting.
* **State** (13, text, required): Enter the state where you will be visiting.
* **ZIP** (14, number, required): Enter the ZIP code of your location.
* **Comments** (15, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the property or your visit.
* **Assigned Tool** (16, select_one, required): Select an assigned tool for your visit from the options provided (tool1, tool2).
* **Form Status** (17, select_one, required): Select the status of the form (active or inactive).

Note: Ensure that you complete all the required fields correctly to ensure that your form is successfully submitted.
