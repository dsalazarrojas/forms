# Mortgage Calculator Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to provide an estimate of mortgage-related metrics for a given loan amount, interest rate, and property price. Users can input their desired loan amount, interest rate, and property price to receive an estimate of total interest, monthly payment, and other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your loan amount in the "Loan Amount" field.
2.  Enter the interest rate as a decimal value.
3.  Enter the number of years for the loan term.
4.  Select the payment frequency from the options provided (Quarterly, Monthly, or Bi-Annual).
5.  If other payment frequency is selected, please enter the specific details in the "Payment Frequency Other" field.
6.  Enter the property price.
7.  Enter the down payment (if applicable).
8.  Enter the mortgage rate.
9.  The form will automatically calculate and display the total interest, monthly payment, and other related metrics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form** (`mortgage_form`, text, optional): This field allows users to input any additional information that may be relevant to their mortgage calculations.
*   **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, optional): Enter the desired loan amount.
*   **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, number, optional): Enter the interest rate as a decimal value.
*   **Years** (`years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years for the loan term.
*   **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the payment frequency from the options provided (Quarterly, Monthly, or Bi-Annual).
*   **Payment Frequency Other** (`payment_frequency_other`, text, optional): If other payment frequency is selected, enter the specific details in this field.
*   **Property Price** (`property_price`, number, optional): Enter the property price.
*   **Down Payment** (`down_payment`, number, optional): Enter the down payment (if applicable).
*   **Mortgage Rate** (`mortgage_rate`, number, optional): Enter the mortgage rate.
*   **Total Interest** (`total_interest`, number, optional): This field displays the total interest calculated based on the user's input.
*   **Monthly Payment** (`monthly_payment`, number, optional): This field displays the estimated monthly payment amount.
*   **Total Interest Monthly** (`total_interest_monthly`, number, optional): This field displays the total interest calculated for the selected payment frequency.
*   **Monthly Payment Monthly** (`monthly_payment_monthly`, number, optional): This field displays the estimated monthly payment amount for the selected payment frequency.
*   **Total Interest Yearly** (`total_interest_yearly`, number, optional): This field displays the total interest calculated for the selected loan term.
*   **Monthly Payment Yearly** (`monthly_payment_yearly`, number, optional): This field displays the estimated monthly payment amount for the selected loan term.

## Tips

*   Make sure to input accurate and relevant information to receive an accurate mortgage calculation.
*   The form will automatically calculate and display the total interest, monthly payment, and other related metrics based on your input.
*   If you need to input a specific payment frequency not listed in the options, please use the "Payment Frequency Other" field.
