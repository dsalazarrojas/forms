# new_listing_info_sheet_the_ackerman_team_north_ampco - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "new_listing_info_sheet_the_ackerman_team_north_ampco" form is designed to collect and manage new listing information for the Ackerman Team in the North & South Amptco region. This form helps to gather essential details about new listings, ensuring that the team is well-informed and organized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Real Estate Forms" category to begin.
2. In the "Listing Information" field, enter a brief description of the new listing, including any relevant details.
3. In the "Submitted to TC Team" field, specify to whom the listing was submitted.
4. Finally, in the "Listing Information to Submit" field, enter any additional information about the new listing you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Real Estate Forms** (`real_estate_forms`, text, optional): This field is the category for the new listing information.
* **Listing Information** (`listing_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the new listing, including any relevant details.
* **Submitted to TC Team** (`submitted_to_tc_team`, text, optional): Specify to whom the listing was submitted.
* **Listing Information to Submit** (`listing_info_to_submit`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the new listing you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter clear and concise information about the new listing in the "Listing Information" field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the Ackerman Team for assistance.
