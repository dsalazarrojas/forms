# payment-notice-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for landlords or property managers to create and manage rent notices for tenants. It's used to collect payment information, contact details, and send reminders or notifications to tenants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Rent Amount field with the current rent amount due from the tenant.
2. Select the appropriate Payment Method from the options provided (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
3. Choose how you want to send the notice to the tenant (via Email, Fax, Mail, or Do Not Send).
4. If required, select the Payment Notice Status (Active or Inactive).
5. Fill in any additional Notes or comments for the tenant.
6. Enter the tenant's Contact Information (email address or phone number).
7. Finally, choose who sent the notice (Owner, Manager, Property Manager, or Tenant).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rent Amount**: Enter the total amount due from the tenant for the current rent cycle.
* **Payment Methods**: Select one or multiple payment methods for the notice (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
* **Payment Methods**: This field is **required** to ensure accurate and secure payment processing.
* **Pay Period**: Select a date to specify when the rent notice is applicable (e.g., 15th of the month for a monthly payment).
* **Pay By**: If applicable, select a time of day or evening when the rent is due.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or reminders for the tenant.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the tenant's email address or phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the tenant's phone number for alternative contact methods.
* **Send Notice**: Choose how to send the notice to the tenant (via Email, Fax, Mail, or Do Not Send).
* **Send Notice**: This field is **required** to determine the notice's delivery method.
* **Payment Notice Status**: Select the status of the notice (Active or Inactive).
* **Payment Notice Status**: This field is optional but helps track notice status and payment history.
* **Notice Date**: Enter the date the notice was sent or is applicable.
* **Notice Time**: Select the time of day the notice was sent or is applicable.
* **Notice Sent By**: Choose who sent the notice (Owner, Manager, Property Manager, or Tenant).
* **Notice Sent By**: This field is optional but helps track who initiated the notice process.
