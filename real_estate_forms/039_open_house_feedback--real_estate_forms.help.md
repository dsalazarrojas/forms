# Open House Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers or visitors after an open house event. Please take a few minutes to share your thoughts about your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, simply fill in the form with the required and optional fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (1, text, required): This is the primary feedback field where you can share your thoughts and opinions about the open house event. Please be specific about what you enjoyed or would like to see improved.
* **Contact Name** (2, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted to discuss your feedback, please provide your name or the name of your company/organization.
* **Contact Email** (3, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by email, please provide your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (4, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **What was the most memorable part of the open house?** (5, text, optional): Share with us what made the open house event stand out to you.
* **Subject** (6, text, optional): Briefly describe the context or purpose of your feedback.
* **Message** (7, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them with us.
* **Submit** (8, text, optional): Click this button to submit your feedback and we'll be in touch to discuss it.
