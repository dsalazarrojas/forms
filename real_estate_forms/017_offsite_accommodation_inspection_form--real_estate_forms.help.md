# offsite-accommodation-inspection-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The offsite-accommodation-inspection-form is used to collect and document information about the condition and status of offsite accommodations during the inspection process. This form helps property inspectors record their findings, recommendations, and observations to ensure accurate and consistent data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by entering the Property Details, which includes essential information about the property.
- Then, upload or link relevant Photos to provide visual evidence of the property's condition.
- If applicable, add Video Links for further demonstration of the property's features or condition.
- Document the Property Condition and describe any notable features, defects, or concerns.
- Specify the Property Type and any unique characteristics it possesses.
- Record the Property Features, such as amenities or equipment available.
- Date and time of the inspection (Inspection Date).
- Provide detailed notes about the inspection, including any findings or concerns (Inspector Notes).
- Indicate the Property Status: Active or Inactive.
- Record the duration of the inspection (Inspection Duration).
- Confirm if the accommodation features are available or not (Accommodation Features).
- Identify the Property Owner and Property Manager if applicable.
- Evaluate if an Inspection Score is warranted (Inspection Score).
- Document any Inspection Findings or concerns.
- Suggest Recommended Actions for improvement or follow-up.
- Schedule the next inspection date (Next Inspection Date).
- Add any Property Image Links or video links for further reference.
- Attach Property Document Links for supporting documents.
- Record any Other Notes or observations.
