# BTR Submit An Offer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an offer on a property. Fill out the form with your details and requirements to communicate your offer to the seller.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Buyer Name", "Buyer Email", and "Buyer Phone" fields.
2. Provide the address of the property you are making an offer on in the "Property Address" field.
3. Select the city, state, and ZIP code in the "City, State, ZIP" field.
4. Enter your offer price in the "Offering Price" field.
5. Choose the type of financing you are using in the "Type of Financing" field.
6. Enter any earnest money amount you are offering in the "Earnest Money Amount" field.
7. Select your desired closing date in the "Desired Closing Date" field.
8. Choose whether you are waiving inspection, appraisal, or loan contingencies in the "Inspection Contingency", "Appraisal Contingency", and "Loan Contingency" fields.
9. If applicable, enter any seller concessions you are requesting in the "-Requested Seller Concessions" field.
10. Provide any special conditions or terms you want to include in the "Special Conditions or Terms" field.
11. Leave a personal note to the seller in the "Personal Note to Seller" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. *Buyer Name* (Text, Required)
	* Enter your full name as the buyer or your agent's name.
### 2. *Buyer Email* (Email, Required)
	* Enter your email address for the seller to contact you.
### 3. *Buyer Phone* (Text, Required)
	* Enter your phone number for the seller to contact you.
### 4. *Property Address* (Text, Required)
	* Enter the address of the property you are making an offer on.
### 5. *City, State, ZIP* (Text, Required)
	* Enter the city, state, and ZIP code of the property.
### 6. *MLS Number* (Text, Optional)
	* Enter the MLS number of the property, if known.
### 7. *Offering Price* (Number, Required)
	* Enter the price you are offering in dollars.
### 8. *Earnest Money Amount* (Number, Required)
	* Enter the deposit amount you are offering in dollars.
### 9. *Desired Closing Date* (Date, Required)
	* Select the date you would like to close on the transaction.
### 10. *Type of Financing* (Select One, Required)
	* Choose the type of financing you are using, such as Conventional Loan, FHA Loan, VA Loan, USDA Loan, or Cash.
### 11. *Loan Amount Needed* (Number, Optional)
	* Enter the amount of loan you are seeking, if applicable.
### 12. *Down Payment Amount* (Number, Optional)
	* Enter the down payment amount, if applicable.
### 13. *Inspection Contingency* (Select One, Required)
	* Choose whether you are waiving inspection contingency or not.
### 14. *Appraisal Contingency* (Select One, Required)
	* Choose whether you are waiving appraisal contingency or not.
### 15. *Loan Contingency* (Select One, Required)
	* Choose whether you are waiving loan contingency or not.
### 16. *-Requested Seller Concessions* (Text, Optional)
	* Enter any seller concessions you are requesting, such as repairs or closing costs.
### 17. *Special Conditions or Terms* (Text, Optional)
	* Enter any unique conditions or terms you want to include in the offer.
### 18. *Personal Note to Seller* (Text, Optional)
	* Leave a personal note to the seller, if desired.
