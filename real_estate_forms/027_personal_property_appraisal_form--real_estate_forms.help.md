# Personal Property Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Property Appraisal Form is designed to collect information for personal property appraisals, such as contact information, property type, address, value, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the property type (residential, commercial, or industrial) that applies to the property being appraised.
2. Enter the contact information for the property owner or representative.
3. Provide the address of the property.
4. Enter the estimated value of the property.
5. Select the year the property was built from the list.
6. Enter the name of the appraiser or individual conducting the appraisal.
7. Choose the date and time of the appraisal.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about the property or appraisal process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact info** (contact_info, text, optional):
	+ Enter the contact information for the property owner or representative.
* **property_type** (property_type, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the type of property being appraised (residential, commercial, or industrial).
* **property_address** (property_address, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address of the property.
* **property_value** (property_value, number, optional):
	+ Enter the estimated value of the property.
* **property_year_built** (property_year_built, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the year the property was built from the list.
* **appraiser_name** (appraiser_name, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the appraiser or individual conducting the appraisal.
* **date_of_appraisal** (date_of_appraisal, date, optional):
	+ Choose the date of the appraisal.
* **time_of_appraisal** (time_of_appraisal, time, optional):
	+ Select the time of the appraisal.
* **additional_comments** (additional_comments, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about the property or appraisal process.
* **contact_method** (contact_method, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the method of contact for the appraiser (phone, email, mail, or in-person).
