# real_estate_sale_discharge_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for a real estate sale discharge. The aim is to gather accurate and complete data about the property, buyer, seller, and loan to facilitate a smooth transaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the property details on **Property Info (Page 1)**. This includes details about the property's location, type, and other relevant information.
2.  Move on to **Buyer Info (Page 2)** and provide details about the buyer, such as their name and contact information.
3.  Proceed to **Seller Info (Page 3)** and input details about the seller, including their name and contact information.
4.  Complete **Lender Info (Page 4)** by providing details about the loan, such as the loan amount and interest rate.
5.  On **Property Details (Page 5)**, add any additional information about the property, including its address and square footage.
6.  Upload any relevant **Document Attachments (Page 6)** related to the property sale.
7.  Finally, sign and date the **Signature (Page 7)** to confirm that the form has been filled accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Property Info (Page 1)**: A text field where you'll input details about the property being sold.
*   **Buyer Info (Page 2)**: A text field where you'll provide information about the buyer, such as their name and contact details.
*   **Seller Info (Page 3)**: A text field for the seller's details, including their name and contact information.
*   **Lender Info (Page 4)**: A text field where you'll input details about the loan, such as the loan amount and interest rate.
*   **Property Details (Page 5)**: A text field where you can add any additional information about the property.
*   **Document Attachments (Page 6)**: A text field where you can upload any relevant documents related to the property sale.
*   **Signature (Page 7)**: A required field for you to sign and date to confirm that the form has been filled accurately and truthfully.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to prevent errors.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
*   Attach any relevant documents to support your answers.
