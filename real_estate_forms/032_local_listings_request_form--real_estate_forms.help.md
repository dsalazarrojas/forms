# Local Listings Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Listings Request Form is designed to collect information about local listings for properties or businesses. This form will help us better understand your listing and provide a clear and concise view of your local business or property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of listing you are requesting (Residential, Commercial, Land, or Other).
2. Enter the property address.
3. Enter the city where the property is located.
4. Enter the state where the property is located.
5. Enter the zip code for the property's location.
6. Provide a brief description of the property or business.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Enter your email address.
9. If applicable, provide your website URL.
10. If applicable, enter a URL for your listing's photo.
11. Enter the latitude and longitude of the property's location.
12. Enter the number of photos, videos, and documents associated with the listing.
13. Enter the date of listing.
14. Enter the expiration date of the listing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Listing Type** (`listing_type`, select_one): Choose the type of listing you are requesting (Residential, Commercial, Land, or Other).
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text): Enter the address of the property or business.
* **City** (`city`, text): Enter the city where the property or business is located.
* **State** (`state`, text): Enter the state where the property or business is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text): Enter the zip code for the property's location.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Website** (`website`, text): If applicable, enter your website URL.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text): If applicable, enter a URL for your listing's photo.
* **Latitude** (`latitude`, number): Enter the latitude of the property's location.
* **Longitude** (`longitude`, number): Enter the longitude of the property's location.
* **Photo Count** (`photo_count`, number): Enter the number of photos associated with the listing.
* **Video Count** (`video_count`, number): Enter the number of videos associated with the listing.
* **Document Count** (`document_count`, number): Enter the number of documents associated with the listing.
* **Listing Date** (`listing_date`, date): Enter the date of listing.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date): Enter the expiration date of the listing.
