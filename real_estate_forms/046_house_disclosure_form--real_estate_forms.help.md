# House Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Disclosure Form is designed to collect detailed information about a house or property from the seller. This information is used to provide accurate and complete details about the property for potential buyers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the Seller Information section.
2. Enter the property address.
3. Select the type of property you are selling.
4. Enter the year the property was built.
5. Enter the lot size of the property.
6. Select the zoning of the property.
7. Enter the square footage of the property.
8. Select the number of bedrooms and number of bathrooms.
9. Enter the lot dimensions of the property.
10. Enter any Homeowners Association (HOA) fees, including the frequency and amount.
11. (Optional) Enter any additional HOA frequencies if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Information** (id: 1, Seller Information, required): Enter your name and contact information, so the buyer can reach out to you for questions or to schedule a viewing.
* **Property Address** (id: 2, Property Address, required): Enter the exact address of the property, including street number and name, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Property Type** (id: 3, Property Type, select one): Select the type of property you are selling, such as single-family home, townhouse, condo, or duplex.
* **Year Built** (id: 4, Year Built, number, required): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Lot Size** (id: 5, Lot Size, number, required): Enter the size of the property's lot in square feet or acres.
* **Zoning** (id: 6, Zoning, text): Enter the zoning information of the property, such as "R-1" or "Agricultural".
* **Square Footage** (id: 7, Square Footage, number, required): Enter the total square footage of the property.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (id: 8, Number of Bedrooms, select multiple, required): Select the number of bedrooms the property has, such as 2-3 or 8-9.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (id: 9, Number of Bathrooms, select multiple, required): Select the number of bathrooms the property has, such as 1 or 5 or more.
* **Lot Dimensions** (id: 10, Lot Dimensions, text): Enter the dimensions of the property's lot, such as 100x100.
* **HOA Fee** (id: 11, HOA Fee, number, required): Enter the amount of the Homeowners Association (HOA) fee.
