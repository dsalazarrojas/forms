# real_estate_client_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect real estate client information, which will be used for various purposes such as listing properties, managing client relationships, and generating reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Select your date of birth from the date picker in the "Date Of Birth" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your address in the "Address" field.
* Select your city in the "City" field.
* Enter your state in the "State" field.
* Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
* Choose the type of property you are interested in from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
* Select your desired move-in date from the date picker in the "Desired Move-In Date" field.
* Choose how you would like to be contacted from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
* Enter the name of your real estate agent in the "Agent" field.
* Enter your agent's email address in the "Agent Email" field.
* Enter your agent's phone number in the "Agent Phone" field.
* Select the status of the property you are interested in from the "Property Status" dropdown menu.
* Choose the type of loan you are interested in from the "Loan Type" dropdown menu.
* Enter the amount of the loan you are interested in in the "Loan Amount" field.
* Enter the down payment amount you are willing to pay in the "Down Payment" field.
* Enter your credit score in the "Credit Score" field.
* Select a date for your desired move-in date from the date picker in the "Desired Move-In Date 2" field.
* Enter the phone number of your real estate agent in the "Agent Phone 2" field.
* Enter the email address of your real estate agent in the "Agent Email 2" field.
* If the property type is not listed, enter "Other" and provide a description of the property type in the "Other Property Type" field.
* If you have any other property specific details, enter them in the "Other Property Description" field.

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.

* **Address** (6, text, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (7, text, required): Select your city.
* **State** (8, text, required): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (9, number, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Property Type** (10, select_one, required): Choose the type of property you are interested in.
* **Desired Move-In Date** (11, date, required): Select your desired move-in date from the date picker.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (12, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Agent** (13, text, required): Enter the name of your real estate agent.
* **Agent Email** (14, email, required): Enter your agent's email address.
* **Agent Phone** (15, text, required): Enter your agent's phone number.
* **Property Status** (16, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the property you are interested in.
* **Loan Type** (17, select_one, required): Choose the type of loan you are interested in.
* **Loan Amount** (18, number, required): Enter the amount of the loan you are interested in.
* **Down Payment** (19, number, required): Enter the down payment amount you are willing to pay.
* **Credit Score** (20, number, required): Enter your credit score.
* **Desired Move-In Date 2** (21, date, required): Select a date for your desired move-in date.
