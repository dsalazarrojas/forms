# Earnest Money Release Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earnest Money Release Request Form is used to request the release of earnest money from a buyer or seller. This form is typically used in real estate transactions to confirm the release of funds held by the buyer or seller.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Choose the appropriate options for the "Delivery Preference" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the earnest money release, if applicable.
4. Add any comments or additional information in the "Comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Earnest Money Release Request Form** (`earnest_money_release_request_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which should not be changed.
* **Buyer Signature** (`buyer_signature`, text, required/optional): This field is for the buyer to sign and date their signature, confirming their request for earnest money release.
* **Buyer Signature Date** (`buyer_signature_date`, date, required/optional): This field is for the buyer to enter the date of their signature.
* **Seller Confirmation** (`seller_confirmation`, text, required/optional): This field is for the seller to confirm their understanding of the earnest money release request.
* **Delivery Preference** (`delivery_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to receive the earnest money release, either by email, fax, or in person.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address where the earnest money release should be delivered.
* **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the buyer's phone number.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the buyer's email address.
* **Seller Email** (`seller_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the seller's email address.
* **Earnest Money Released** (`earnest_money_released`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the box to confirm that the earnest money has been released.
* **Earnest Money Released Date** (`earnest_money_released_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the earnest money release, if applicable.
* **Earnest Money Released Time** (`earnest_money_released_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the earnest money release, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about the earnest money release request.
* **Earnest Money Release Request Form Date** (`earnest_money_release_request_form_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the earnest money release request form.
* **Earnest Money Release Request Form Time** (`earnest_money_release_request_form_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the earnest money release request form.
