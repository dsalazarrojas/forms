# Tenant Financial Hardship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Financial Hardship Application is a form to be completed by tenants who are experiencing financial difficulties in paying rent or other housing-related costs. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information.
2. Provide financial information, including your current financial status and any relevant supporting documents.
3. Describe the hardship you are experiencing, including the reason and duration.
4. Attach any additional supporting documents required by your landlord or property owner.
5. Select the options that best describe your financial status and hardship details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
- **Financial Information** (`tenant_financial_details`, text, required/optional): Describe your current financial situation, including any income, expenses, and assets.
- **Rental Unit Details** (`rental_unit_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the rental unit you are experiencing hardship with.
- **Hardship Details** (`hardship_details`, text, required/optional): Explain the hardship you are experiencing and how it affects your ability to pay rent.
- **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_documentation`, text, required/optional): Attach any supporting documentation, such as pay stub, bank statements, or letters from creditors.
- **Landlord Verification** (`landlord_verification`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like your landlord or property owner to verify your hardship.
- **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`, text, required/optional): Describe the terms of your lease and any relevant conditions.
- **Rent History** (`rent_history`, text, required/optional): Provide information about your rent payment history, including late payments and any outstanding balances.
- **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose how your financial situation affects your ability to pay rent.
- **Hardship Amount** (`hardship_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of hardship you are experiencing.
- **Hardship Duration** (`hardship_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of time you are experiencing hardship.
- **Hardship Start Date** (`hardship_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your hardship began.
- **Hardship End Date** (`hardship_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your hardship is expected to end.
- **Hardship Reason** (`hardship_reason`, text, required/optional): Explain the reason for your hardship.
- **Hardship Cause** (`hardship_cause`, text, required/optional): Describe the cause of your hardship.
- **Landlord Verification Document** (`landlord_verification_document`, note, required/optional): Attach any documents that verify your landlord or property owner's identity.
- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose any additional supporting documents required by your landlord or property owner.
- **Landlord Verification Document 2** (`landlord_verification_document_2`, text, required/optional): Attach any additional documents that verify your landlord or property owner's identity.
- **Supporting Documents 2** (`supporting_documents_2`, text, required/optional): Choose additional supporting documents required by your landlord or property owner.
- **Landlord Verification Document 3** (`landlord_verification_document_3`, text, required/optional): Attach any additional documents that verify your landlord or property owner's identity.
- **Supporting Documents 3** (`supporting_documents_3`, text, required/optional): Choose additional supporting documents required by your landlord or property owner.
- **Financial Status 2** (`financial_status_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose any additional financial status affecting your ability to pay rent.
- **Hardship Cause 2** (`hardship_cause_2`, text, required/optional): Describe the cause of your additional hardship.
- **Hardship Reason 2** (`hardship_reason_2`, text, required/optional): Explain the reason for your additional hardship.
- **Hardship Duration 2** (`hardship_duration_2`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of time you are experiencing additional hardship.
- **Landlord Verification Document 4** (`landlord_verification_document_4`, text, required/optional): Attach any additional documents that verify your landlord or property owner's identity.
- **Supporting Documents 4** (`supporting_documents_4`, text, required/optional): Choose additional supporting documents required by your landlord or property owner.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields that are marked as "required" are filled in as they are necessary for your application to be processed. 
- Attach any relevant supporting documents to demonstrate your financial hardship. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your landlord or property owner for assistance.
