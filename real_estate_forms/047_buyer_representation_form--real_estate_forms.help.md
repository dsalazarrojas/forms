The Buyer Representation Form is used to collect information about a buyer who wants to be represented by a real estate agent or brokerage. This form helps the agent understand the buyer's preferences, needs, and circumstances, which will aid in finding the right property for them. The form is used to create a buyer representation agreement, which is a legal agreement between a real estate agent and a buyer. By filling out this form, the buyer is providing important information that will help the agent represent them effectively.

Before filling out this form, please ensure you are not filling it out for a duplicate representation. A buyer can only have one active representation agreement with a real estate agent or brokerage at a time.

This form is used to collect information that will be used to create a buyer representation agreement. Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information to avoid any potential issues or misunderstandings with your agent or brokerage.

## Purpose
This Buyer Representation Form is used to collect information about a buyer who wants to be represented by a real estate agent or brokerage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to review all the fields carefully and select the correct options for each field.
3. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please reach out to your real estate agent or brokerage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: Yes
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Agent Name**: The name of your real estate agent or the name of the real estate brokerage.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Brokerage Firm Name**: The name of the real estate brokerage.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Agreement Effective Date**: The date when your representation agreement begins.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Agreement Duration**: The length of time your representation agreement is valid for.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Exclusive or Non-Exclusive Representation**: Are you exclusive or non-exclusive to your real estate agent or brokerage?
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Co-Buyer Name (if applicable)**: If you are buying with someone else, please provide their name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Buyer Status**: What is your status as a buyer?
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Purchase Timeline**: When do you want to buy a property?
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Financing Status**: What is your financing status?
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Maximum Purchase Price**: What is your maximum purchase price in USD or local currency.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Property Type Preference**: What type of property do you prefer?
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: Yes
* **Preferred Geographic Area**: What area do you prefer to buy in?
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Key Features or Requirements**: Please list any additional features or requirements you have for your property.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Agent Responsibilities Understood**: Have you reviewed and understood the agent's services and duties?
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Commission and Compensation Discussed**: Have you discussed the commission and compensation with your agent?
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **I Consent to Be Represented by This Agent**: Do you consent to being represented by this agent?
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **I Acknowledge and Accept All Terms**: Do you acknowledge and accept all terms of the representation agreement?
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes

Note: This form is not a substitute for the actual representation agreement, and the agreement should be reviewed and signed by both the buyer and the agent or brokerage before any representation can begin.

Tips:
* Please review and sign the representation agreement carefully before signing.
* Make sure to ask your agent or brokerage any questions or clarify any doubts you have before signing.
* This form is used for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for the actual representation agreement.
