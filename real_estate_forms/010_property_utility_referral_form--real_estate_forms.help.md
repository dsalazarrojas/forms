# Property Utility Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a property's move-in details and contact information for utilities and services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form with the required information about your property's move-in details and contact information.
- Select the utilities and services that apply to your property.
- Enter the contact person's first name and last name.
- Enter the contact person's email address.
- Enter the contact person's phone number.
- Enter the contact person's address.
- Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Move In Date** (date): Enter the date you moved into the property.
* **Move In Details** (text): Provide a brief description of your move-in.
* **Move In Details E** (text): This field is a duplicate of "Move In Details" and appears to be an error. Please ignore this field.
* **Utilities** (select multiple): Select the utilities that apply to your property (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water, Internet, Cable/TV).
* **Utilities E** (select multiple): This field is a duplicate of "Utilities" and appears to be an error. Please ignore this field.
* **Contact First Name** (text): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (text): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Cell** (text): This field is not required and not necessary for contact information.
* **Contact Street** (text): Enter the street address of the contact person.
* **Contact City** (text): Enter the city of the contact person.
* **Contact State** (text): Enter the state of the contact person.
