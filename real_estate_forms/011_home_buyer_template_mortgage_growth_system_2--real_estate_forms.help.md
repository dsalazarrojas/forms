# Home Buyer Template Mortgage Growth System 2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide a home buyer through a process to determine their mortgage growth system's suitability for their financial situation. It is intended for informational purposes only. Please note that this form is not a substitute for professional financial or legal advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in each field, and you will be able to see a summary of your potential mortgage growth system. Please make sure to fill in all the required fields (marked as **required: true**), and you can leave out any optional fields if you do not want to complete them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): This field is where you can enter your name and any other contact information you would like to share.
  - **Address** (`address`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your address, but it is not required.
  - **City** (`city`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your city, but it is not required.
  - **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your zip code, but it is not required.
  - **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, optional): This field is where you can enter the amount of money you are looking to borrow for your home purchase.
  - **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, number, optional): This field is where you can enter the interest rate for your loan.
  - **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, optional): This field is where you can enter your credit score, but it is not recommended to enter incorrect information as it may affect the results.
  - **Credit Rating** (`credit_rating`, number, optional): This field is where you can enter your credit rating, but it is not recommended to enter incorrect information as it may affect the results.
  - **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): This field is where you can select your employment status, with options being Employed, Unemployed, or Retired.
  - **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your occupation.
  - **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select_one, optional): This field is where you can select your insurance type, with options being Term Life, Whole Life, or Mortgage Life.
  - **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your policy holder's information.
  - **Closing Date** (`closing_date`, date, optional): This field is where you can enter the date you are planning to close your mortgage.
  - **Closing Time** (`closing_time`, time, optional): This field is where you can enter the time you are planning to close your mortgage.
  - **Home Insurance Type** (`home_insurance_type`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you can select your home insurance type, with options being Homeowners or Renters.
  - **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your policy holder's information (this field is a duplicate of field 16, so feel free to leave this one blank).
  - **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is where you can enter your email address.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your phone number.
Note: Please be aware that some of the fields are optional, and leaving them out will not affect the results of your mortgage growth system analysis. Please fill in all the required fields and answer them honestly and accurately.
