# Rental History Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Rental History Verification Form" is used by landlords and property managers to gather information about a tenant's past rental experiences. This form helps to assess a potential tenant's credibility and rental history, and determine whether they are a reliable tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
3. Make sure to provide all necessary details about the tenant's rental history.

* **Tenant Info** (`tenant_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the tenant, such as their name, address, or contact information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the tenant's emergency contact or a reference who can vouch for the tenant.
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, note, optional): This is a free-form text area where you can enter any relevant information about the tenant's rental history.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, number, optional): Enter the date of birth of the tenant.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, number, optional): Enter the social security number of the tenant.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the tenant.
* **Previous Occupants** (`previous_occupants`, text, optional): Enter any previous occupants or roommates of the tenant.
* **Rental Agreements** (`rental_agreements`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the tenant has ever had rental agreements in the past, and if so, which ones (e.g. 'Yes', 'No', etc.)
* **Rental History 1-6** (`rental_history_1`, `rental_history_2`, `rental_history_3`, `rental_history_4`, `rental_history_5`, `rental_history_6`, number, optional): Enter the dates of the tenant's past rentals in chronological order.
* **Rental Property Manager** (`rental_property_manager`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the tenant has ever had a rental property manager in the past (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the tenant pays rent on time (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, optional): Enter the rent amount of the tenant's past rentals.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate the type of property the tenant rented (e.g. 'Yes', 'No', etc.).
* **Rental Start Date** (`rental_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the tenant's past rentals.
* **Rental End Date** (`rental_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the tenant's past rentals.
* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the tenant's past rentals were for a fixed term or month-to-month (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').
* **Lease Type** (`lease_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate the type of lease the tenant had in the past (e.g. 'Yes', 'No', etc.).
* **Lease Status** (`lease_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of the lease (e.g. 'Active', 'Inactive').
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tenant's landlord or property manager.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it's highly recommended to fill in as much information as possible for accurate results.
