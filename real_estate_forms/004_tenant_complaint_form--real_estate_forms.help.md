# Tenant Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Complaint Form is a tool used to collect and document tenant complaints or issues related to their rental properties. This form helps management track and resolve these concerns in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Tenant Complaint field with a brief description of the issue or concern you are experiencing.
2. Provide a detailed description of the problem in the Issue Description field.
3. Enter the date you first noticed the issue in the Complaint Date field.
4. Input your contact number in the Contact Number field so we can reach out to you for further discussion or resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Complaint** (`tenant_complaint`, text, required/optional):
  A brief description of the issue or concern you are experiencing.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required/optional):
  A detailed description of the problem, including as much information as possible.
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, date, required/optional):
  The date you first noticed the issue.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional):
  Your contact number for further discussion or resolution.
