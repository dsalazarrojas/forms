# Property Under Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about properties that are currently under contract. It is used by real estate professionals to standardize and document sales progress and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the property address on page 1.
2. Select the relevant buyer and seller information on pages 2-3.
3. Enter the sale progress, sale date, and sale amount on pages 4-6.
4. Choose the property type and sale status on page 7.
5. Enter the buyer and seller agent contact information on pages 8-9.
6. Add any additional notes on page 10.
7. Finally, enter the buyer and seller contact information on pages 11-12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the property address where the sale is taking place.
* **Buyer Info** (2, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant buyer information.
* **Seller Info** (3, select_one, optional): Select the relevant seller information.
* **Sale Progress** (4, date, optional): Enter the current sale progress date.
* **Sale Date** (5, date, optional): Enter the sale date.
* **Sale Amount** (6, number, optional): Enter the sale amount.
* **Buyer Fees** (7, number, optional): Enter the buyer fees.
* **Seller Fees** (8, number, optional): Enter the seller fees.
* **Closing Date** (9, date, optional): Enter the closing date.
* **Property Type** (10, select_one, optional): Select the property type.
* **Buyer Agent** (11, text, optional): Enter the buyer agent contact information.
* **Seller Agent** (12, text, optional): Enter the seller agent contact information.
* **Sale Status** (13, select_one, optional): Select the sale status.
* **Notes** (14, note, optional): Add any additional notes.
* **Buyer Email** (15, email, optional): Enter the buyer email.
* **Buyer Phone** (16, text, optional): Enter the buyer phone number.
* **Seller Phone** (17, text, optional): Enter the seller phone number.
