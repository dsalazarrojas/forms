# security_deposit_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The security_deposit_release_form is used to record the return of a security deposit to a tenant from a landlord. This form provides a transparent and formal way to document the transaction, ensuring accurate financial records and maintaining a clear audit trail.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked as required in the form.
2. Ensure you have the necessary information and signatures before submitting the form.
3. Double-check for any errors or discrepancies before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Signature**: The tenant must sign this field to confirm they are the rightful owner of the account and acknowledge they are releasing the security deposit.
* **Date Returned**: Enter the date the security deposit was returned to the tenant. This date should be accurate and reflect the actual date of return.
* **Landlord Signature**: The landlord must sign this field to confirm they have received the security deposit back from the tenant and to acknowledge the accuracy of the date returned.
* **Deposit Returned**: Enter the actual amount of the security deposit returned to the tenant. This amount should match the original deposit amount and any fees or penalties applied.

## Tips
* Double-check the date returned to ensure accuracy and consistency with financial records.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If there are any issues or discrepancies with the security deposit return, contact your property management team for assistance.
