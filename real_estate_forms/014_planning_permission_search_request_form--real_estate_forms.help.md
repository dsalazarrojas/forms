# Planning Permission Search Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Planning Permission Search Request Form is designed to gather information for a planning permission search. It is a general search form that allows users to input various criteria to find relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Search Address**: Enter the address you want to search for.
2. **Select Prophets**: Choose one or multiple options from the given list of prophets.
3. **Select Multiple Prophets**: Choose multiple options from the given list of prophets.
4. **Search Date**: Enter the date you want to search for (if applicable).
5. **Search Time**: Enter the time you want to search for (if applicable).
6. **Search Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to your search.
7. **Search Email**: Enter an email address to receive the search results.
8. **Search Phone**: Enter a phone number to receive search results or additional information.
9. **Search Phone 2**: Enter an alternative phone number to receive search results or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Search Address** (Search Address, text, optional): Enter the address you want to search for.
* **Select Prophets** (Select Prophets, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list of prophets to narrow down your search.
* **Select Multiple Prophets** (Select Multiple Prophets, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the list of prophets to further refine your search.
* **Search Date** (Search Date, date, optional): Enter the date you want to search for (if applicable).
* **Search Time** (Search Time, time, optional): Enter the time you want to search for (if applicable).
* **Search Note** (Search Note, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to your search.
* **Search Email** (Search Email, email, optional): Enter an email address to receive the search results.
* **Search Phone** (Search Phone, text, optional): Enter a phone number to receive search results or additional information.
* **Search Phone 2** (Search Phone 2, text, optional): Enter an alternative phone number to receive search results or additional information.

## Tips
* Make sure to input accurate and relevant information in the search fields to get the desired results.
* If you are searching for specific dates or times, ensure you enter them in the correct format.
* You can choose multiple prophets for a more refined search.
* You can enter multiple phone numbers to receive search results on different phones.
* You can enter any additional notes or comments to provide more context to your search.
