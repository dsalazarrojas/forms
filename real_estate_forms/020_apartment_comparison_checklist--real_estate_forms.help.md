# Apartment Comparison Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apartment Comparison Checklist is a tool designed to help you find the perfect apartment that matches your needs and preferences. By providing information about your budget, desired features, and requirements, you can narrow down search results and discover apartments that suit your lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
2. Provide accurate and honest information about your needs and preferences.
3. Use the dropdown menus to select options that apply to you.
4. Fill in the text fields with specific details about your requirements.
5. Save your progress and come back to complete the form at a later time if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Budget range for rent
**Budget range for rent** (`search_criteria_budget`, `text`, required)
Enter the monthly rent budget range you are willing to pay. This will help us narrow down search results to apartments within your budget.

### Number of bedrooms needed
**Number of bedrooms needed** (`number_bedrooms`, `number`, required)
Specify the number of bedrooms you are looking for in an apartment. This will help us show you apartments with the correct number of bedrooms.

### Number of bathrooms needed
**Number of bathrooms needed** (`number_bathrooms`, `number`, required)
Specify the number of bathrooms you need in an apartment. This will help us show you apartments with the correct number of bathrooms.

### Desired square footage
**Desired square footage** (`square_footage`, `number`, optional)
Enter the approximate square footage you are looking for in an apartment. This will help us show you apartments of the right size.

### Preferred neighborhood or area
**Preferred neighborhood or area** (`location_preferences`, `text`, optional)
Specify the neighborhood or area you prefer to live in. This can be a specific address, city, or region.

### Maximum commute distance to work or school
**Maximum commute distance to work or school** (`commute_distance`, `text`, optional)
Enter the maximum distance you are willing to commute to work or school.

### Must have access to public transportation
**Must have access to public transportation** (`public_transit`, `select_one`, required)
Select whether access to public transportation is essential, preferred but not essential, or not necessary for you.

### Parking requirement
**Parking requirement** (`parking_need`, `select_one`, required)
Specify whether you must have parking, prefer parking, can live without parking, or don't need parking at all.

### Outdoor space desired
**Outdoor space desired** (`outdoor_space`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the types of outdoor space you desire, such as balcony, yard, or rooftop access.

### Pet-friendly requirements
**Pet-friendly requirements** (`pet_requirements`, `select_one`, required)
Specify whether you must have pets, prefer pet-friendly, or don't have pets.

### Desired building amenities
**Desired building amenities** (`amenities_desired`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the amenities you desire, such as a gym or pool.

### Preferred lease term
**Preferred lease term** (`lease_term_preference`, `select_one`, required)
Specify the lease term you prefer, whether it's month-to-month, 3-6 months, 1 year, or 2+ years.

### Furnished apartment preference
**Furnished apartment preference** (`furnished_preference`, `select_one`, required)
Specify whether you prefer a furnished, semi-furnished, or unfurnished apartment.

### Utilities included preference
**Utilities included preference** (`utilities_preference`, `select_one`, required)
Specify whether you prefer all utilities included, some utilities included, or no utilities included.

### Required appliances
**Required appliances** (`appliances_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the appliances you require, such as a refrigerator or stove and oven.

### Noise tolerance level
**Noise tolerance level** (`noise_tolerance`, `select_one`, required)
Specify your noise tolerance level, whether it's quiet neighborhood essential, prefer quiet but flexible, or noise not a concern.

### Neighborhood priorities
**Neighborhood priorities** (`neighborhood_priorities`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the neighborhood priorities that matter to you, such as shopping nearby or restaurants and dining.

### Timeline for moving in
**Timeline for moving in** (`move_in_timeline`, `select_one`, required)
Specify your timeline for moving in, whether it's immediately, within 2 weeks, within 1 month, or 1-3 months.

### Absolute must-haves
**Absolute must-haves** (`must_haves`, `text`, optional)
Enter any absolute must-haves for the apartment, such as a specific feature or location.

### Nice-to-have features
**Nice-to-have features** (`nice_to_haves`, `text`, optional)
Enter any nice-to-have features or amenities you would like to have in the apartment but are not essential.

### Deal-breaker issues
**Deal-breaker issues** (`deal_breakers`, `text`, optional)
Enter any deal-breaker issues that would make you pass on an apartment, such as a specific feature or issue.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to get accurate search results.
* Use the dropdown menus to select options that apply to you.
* Save your progress and come back to complete the form at a later time if needed.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
* Ask us if you have any questions or need help with the form.
