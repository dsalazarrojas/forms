# Lien Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lien Release Form is used to document a lien release in a real estate transaction, ensuring that the property can be sold or transferred without outstanding liens or debts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the Borrower Name in the field provided.
2. Enter the Lienholder Name in the field provided.
3. Enter the Lien Date (the date the lien was placed) in the field provided.
4. Enter the Amount Paid to satisfy the lien.
5. Enter the Lien Amount (the amount of the lien to be released) in the field provided.
6. Enter the Paid By field with the name of the individual or entity paying off the lien.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm the lien release.
8. Obtain a signature from the Lienholder and Lender, if applicable.
9. Verify the Date Paid field with the actual date the lien was paid in the field provided.
10. Enter the Date the Amount Paid Date in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Borrower Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the borrower associated with the lien being released.
* **Lienholder Name (id: 3)**: Enter the name of the lienholder who holds the lien being released.
* **Lien Date (id: 4)**: Enter the date the lien was placed on the property.
* **Amount Paid (id: 5)**: Enter the total amount paid to release the lien.
* **Lien Amount (id: 6)**: Enter the amount of the lien being released.
* **Paid By (id: 7)**: Enter the name of the individual or entity paying off the lien.
* **Lienholder Signature (id: 8)**: If applicable, obtain a signature from the lienholder to confirm the lien release.
* **Borrower Signature (id: 9)**: If applicable, obtain a signature from the borrower to confirm the lien release.
* **Lender Signature (id: 10)**: If applicable, obtain a signature from the lender to confirm the lien release.
* **Date Paid (id: 11)**: Enter the actual date the lien was paid.
* **Amount Paid Date (id: 12)**: Enter the date the amount was paid.
