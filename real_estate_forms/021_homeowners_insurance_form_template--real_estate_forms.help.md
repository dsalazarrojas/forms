# Homeowners Insurance Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a template for homeowners insurance applications. Fill out the form to provide accurate information about your property and policy details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your home address in the **Home Address** field.
2. Select the type of your home from the **Home Type** dropdown menu.
3. Enter the square footage of your home in the **Home Square Footage** field.
4. Choose the location of your home from the **Home Location** dropdown menu.
5. Select the type of policy holder for your policy.
6. Enter the policy holder's first name in the **Policy Holder First Name** field.
7. Enter the policy holder's last name in the **Policy Holder Last Name** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
9. Select your phone number from the **Phone Number** dropdown menu.
10. Enter the policy holder's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field (MM/DD/YY format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your home, including street number and postal code.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of your home (e.g., single-family, apartment, condominium).
* **Home Square Footage** (`home_square_footage`, number, optional): Enter the total square footage of your home.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, number, optional): Enter the year your home was built.
* **Home Location** (`home_location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the location of your home (e.g., urban, rural, etc.).
* **Policy Holder Type** (`policy_holder_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of policy holder for your policy (e.g., individual, business).
* **Policy Holder First Name** (`policy_holder_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's first name.
* **Policy Holder Last Name** (`policy_holder_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, select_multiple, optional): Select your phone number (e.g., landline, mobile).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the policy holder's date of birth in the MM/DD/YY format.
