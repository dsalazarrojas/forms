# real_estate_purchase_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize a real estate purchase, providing necessary information for the transaction to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields, marked as "required".
* Enter your buyer ID, which is your unique identification number.
* Enter the authorization date for the transaction.
* Provide the property address for the purchase.
* If necessary, enter the contact number for the buyer.
* If applicable, enter the buyer's agent information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **real_estate_purchase_authorization**: This field is the main title of the form, and it is not required.
* **buyer_id**: Your buyer ID is a unique number that identifies you as the buyer of the property. Please enter it accurately.
* **authorization_date**: Enter the date when the authorization for the purchase is given.
* **property_address**: Provide the address of the property being purchased.
* **contact_number**: If you need to contact the buyer for any reason, enter their phone number here.
* **buyer_agent**: If you are purchasing through an agent, enter their information here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to ensure the form is submitted correctly.
* If you need assistance with the form, contact your real estate representative.
