# landlord_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The landlord_information_form is a comprehensive form used to collect information about a landlord's property and lease details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the contact information: Enter the landlord's name, contact number, and email address.
2. Provide property details: Enter the property address, property type, property size, and number of bedrooms and bathrooms.
3. Enter lease details: Select the lease duration, rent amount, and rent period.
4. Enter move-in and move-out information: Enter the move-in date, move-in time, move-out date, and move-out time.
5. Confirm property type and rent period for the second property (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Name** (`<name>`, text, required): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Contact Number** (`<contact_number>`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the landlord.
* **Email Address** (`<email_address>`, email, required): Enter the email address of the landlord.
* **Property Address** (`<property_address>`, text, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property Type** (`<property_type>`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property (Apartment, Condo, Townhouse).
* **Property Size** (`<property_size>`, number, optional): Enter the size of the property (optional).
* **Property Bedrooms** (`<property_bedrooms>`, number, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property (optional).
* **Property Bathrooms** (`<property_bathrooms>`, number, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property (optional).
* **Lease Duration** (`<lease_duration>`, select_one, optional): Select the lease duration (3, 6, 12).
* **Rent Amount** (`<rent_amount>`, number, optional): Enter the rent amount (optional).
* **Rent Period** (`<rent_period>`, select_one, optional): Select the rent period (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly).
* **Move In Date** (`<move_in_date>`, date, optional): Enter the move-in date.
* **Move In Time** (`<move_in_time>`, time, optional): Enter the move-in time.
* **Move Out Date** (`<move_out_date>`, date, optional): Enter the move-out date.
* **Move Out Time** (`<move_out_time>`, time, optional): Enter the move-out time.
* **Move In Condition** (`<move_in_condition>`, text, optional): Enter the move-in condition (optional).
* **Property Status** (`<property_status>`, select_one, optional): Select the property status (Active, Inactive).
* **Move In Date 2** (`<move_in_date_2>`, date, optional): Enter the move-in date for the second property (if applicable).
* **Move In Time 2** (`<move_in_time_2>`, time, optional): Enter the move-in time for the second property (if applicable).
* **Move In Condition 2** (`<move_in_condition_2>`, text, optional): Enter the move-in condition for the second property (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields for accurate form submission.
- Check the form fields carefully to avoid entering duplicate or conflicting information.
- If you have multiple properties, please fill out the fields for each property separately.
