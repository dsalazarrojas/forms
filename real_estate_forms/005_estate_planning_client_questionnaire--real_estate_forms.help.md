# Estate Planning Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Client Questionnaire is a form designed to collect essential information from clients to aid in tailoring our estate planning services to their unique needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by ensuring you have not previously completed this form or have not had an existing estate plan in our system.
- Carefully review each field to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.
- Fill in each field with the requested type and format (e.g., date for "Date of Birth").
- If a field is not required, you may leave it blank, but please note that this may impact the effectiveness of our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name as it is listed on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name as it is listed on your identification documents.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where you can be contacted.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).

## Tips
- Double-check all entered information for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- This form should only be submitted once; resubmission is not permitted.
