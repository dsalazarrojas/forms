# Property Market Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Market Analysis Report Form is designed to collect and document information about a property's market conditions, its analysis and assessment, and contact details for further reference and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the Property Manager from the options provided.
2. Enter the Property Marketability Index Score, which reflects the overall attractiveness of the property in the market.
3. Provide the Property Marketability Index Score Range, which is a subset of the overall score.
4. Set the Form Submission Status to reflect the current state of the form (In Progress, Completed, Partially Completed, or Not Started).
5. Enter the Date of Form Submission.
6. Set the Form Version.
7. Enter the Property Details, Market Analysis, and Additional Comments.
8. Enter the Contact Information.
9. Select the Date and Time of inspection.
10. Enter the number of Inspectors.
11. Provide the number of Rooms.
12. Enter the Property Age in Years.
13. Provide the Property Condition (Excellent, Average, Below Average, Poor, or Not Specified).
14. Enter the Overall Score for the property.
15. Enter the Date and Time of form completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Manager** (Select multiple): Select the property manager(s) involved in this report.
* **Property Details**: Enter a description of the property.
* **Market Analysis**: Enter any relevant market analysis for the property.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the property.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information for the property manager or representative.
* **Property Marketability Index** (Number): Enter a score between 1 and 100 that reflects the overall attractiveness of the property in the market.
* **Property Marketability Index Score Range** (Number): Enter a subset of the Property Marketability Index Score.
* **Form Submission Status** (Select one): Choose the status of the form submission (In Progress, Completed, Partially Completed, or Not Started).
* **Submission Status Date** (Date): Enter the date when the form is submitted.
* **Form Submission Time** (Time): Enter the time when the form is submitted.
* **User Name** (Text): Enter your name for reference.
* **User Company** (Text): Enter your company name for reference.
* **User Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact.
* **User Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Form Version** (Text): Enter the version of the form that is being completed.

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Be accurate and thorough when filling in the fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please do not hesitate to reach out.
