# Residential Tenancy Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from tenants to verify their rental history and assist landlords and property managers in making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the requested information. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own specific fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenants Information** (`tenant_info`, `text`, not required): Please fill out this section with your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
* **Landlords Information** (`landlord_info`, `text`, not required): If you are a tenant, please provide information about the landlord or property manager who you are currently renting from or have rented from in the past.
* **Previous Rental Information** (`previous_rental_info`, `text`, not required): If you have rented in the past, please provide details about your previous rental, including the location, dates of tenancy, and reason for vacating.
* **Tenant Referrals** (`tenant_referrals`, `select_one`, not required): Please select the type of referral you would like to be listed as (Primary or Secondary).
*   **Tenant Referrals Name** (`tenant_referrals_name`, `text`, not required): If selected as a Primary or Secondary referral, please provide the name of the person who is referring you.
*   **Tenant Referrals Address** (`tenant_referrals_address`, `text`, not required): Please provide the address of the person who is referring you.
*   **Tenant Referrals City** (`tenant_referrals_city`, `text`, not required): Please provide the city of the person who is referring you.
*   **Tenant Referrals State** (`tenant_referrals_state`, `text`, not required): Please provide the state of the person who is referring you.
*   **Tenant Referrals Post Code** (`tenant_referrals_postal_code`, `number`, not required): Please provide the post code of the person who is referring you.
*   **Tenant Referrals Phone** (`tenant_referrals_phone`, `text`, not required): Please provide the phone number of the person who is referring you.
*   **Tenant Referrals Email** (`tenant_referrals_email`, `email`, not required): Please provide the email of the person who is referring you.
*   **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, not required): Please select the type of property you are currently renting or have rented (Residential or Commercial).
*   **Property Status** (`property_status`, `select_one`, not required): Please select the status of the property you are currently renting or have rented (Rented or Vacant).
*   **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, not required): Please provide the address of the property you are currently renting or have rented.
*   **Property City** (`property_city`, `text`, not required): Please provide the city of the property you are currently renting or have rented.
*   **Property State** (`property_state`, `text`, not required): Please provide the state of the property you are currently renting or have rented.
*   **Property Post Code** (`property_postal_code`, `number`, not required): Please provide the post code of the property you are currently renting or have rented.
*   **Date Available** (`property_date_available`, `date`, not required): If the property is available, please provide the date it became available.
*   **Property Rent Per Month** (`property_rent_per_month`, `number`, not required): If the property is rented, please provide the rent per month.
*   **Notes** (`property_notes`, `note`, not required): Please provide any additional notes about the property.
*   **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, `text`, not required): If applicable, please provide the phone number of the landlord or property manager.
*   **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, `text`, not required): If applicable, please provide the name of the landlord or property manager.
*   **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, `email`, not required): If applicable, please provide the email of the landlord or property manager.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, not required): Once you have completed the form, please submit it for review.
