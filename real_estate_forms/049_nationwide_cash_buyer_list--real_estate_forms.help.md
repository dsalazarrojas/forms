# Nationwide Cash Buyer List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nationwide Cash Buyer List form is used to collect information from real estate agents or representatives about their cash buyer lists. This information helps in maintaining a comprehensive and accurate list of potential cash buyers nationwide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name, which is a required field.
2. Enter your Email address, also a required field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. If you have an address, please enter it in the Address field (optional).
5. Enter the number of Fix and Flip Properties you are interested in buying.
6. Fill in the return on investment (ROI) for these properties.
7. Select the tool assigned to you (e.g., Jimmy) from the dropdown list or choose Other if it's not listed.
8. If you have any additional Notes, please type them in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Name (Required)
* **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or introduction.

### Email (Required)
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address where you can be reached.

### Phone (Required)
* **Phone**: Enter your business phone number where you can be contacted.

### Address (Optional)
* **Address**: If you have a business address, please enter it to help us better understand your location.

### Fix and Flip Properties (Required)
* **Fix and Flip Properties**: Enter the number of fix and flip properties you are interested in buying.

### ROI (Required)
* **ROI**: Enter the return on investment (ROI) for these properties.

### Assigned Tool (Required)
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you from the dropdown list (e.g., Jimmy). If not listed, choose Other.

### Notes (Optional)
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your cash buyer list.
