# Transaction Coordinator Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transaction Coordinator Form is a document used to gather information related to real estate transactions, including contact details, property and transaction information, and status updates. This form will help you track and manage your real estate transactions efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the contact information for the transaction coordinator in the "Contact Name" and "Contact Email/Phone" fields.
2. Enter the details about the property, including "Property Address", "Property Type", and any other relevant information.
3. Select the type of transaction, such as "Active" or "Inactive" for the transaction status.
4. Fill in any additional details about the transaction in the "Transaction Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Transaction Coordinator Form** (`transaction_coordinator_form`, text, required): Enter a unique name for this transaction.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person or organization involved in the transaction.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or organization.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property involved in the transaction.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of property, e.g. single family home, condominium, etc.
- **Transaction Type** (`transaction_type`, text, optional): Select or enter the type of transaction, e.g., purchase, sale, rental.
- **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the contract or agreement.
- **Contract Time** (`contract_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the contract or agreement.
- **Closing Date** (`closing_date`, date, optional): Enter the closing date of the transaction.
- **Closing Time** (`closing_time`, time, optional): Enter the closing time of the transaction.
- **Contract Value** (`contract_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of the contract or agreement.
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client involved in the transaction.
- **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the agent or representative involved in the transaction.
- **Transaction Details** (`transaction_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the transaction.
- **Transaction Status** (`transaction_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the transaction, either "Active" or "Inactive".
