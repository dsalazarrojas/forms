# Land Ownership Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Land Ownership Records Release Form is used to collect and update information about land ownership records, ensuring accurate and up-to-date data for efficient management and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about the property.
2. Provide the correct property type (residential, commercial, industrial, etc.).
3. Enter the property address.
4. Select the relevant real estate agency (if applicable).
5. Enter the date and time of the event (if applicable).
6. Provide your contact phone number and email address.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the property (if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property** (`land_ownership_records_release_form_property`, text, required): A brief description of the property.
* **Property Type** (`land_ownership_records_release_form_property_type`, select_one, optional): The type of property (residential, commercial, industrial, etc.).
* **Property Address** (`land_ownership_records_release_form_property_address`, text, optional): The physical address of the property.
* **Agency** (`land_ownership_records_release_form_agency`, select_multiple, optional): The real estate agency involved in the ownership.
* **Date** (`land_ownership_records_release_form_date`, date, optional): The date of the event (e.g., sale, lease, transfer).
* **Time** (`land_ownership_records_release_form_time`, time, optional): The time of the event.
* **Phone** (`land_ownership_records_release_form_phone`, text, optional): The contact phone number.
* **Email** (`land_ownership_records_release_form_email`, email, optional): The contact email address.
* **Note** (`land_ownership_records_release_form_note`, note, optional): Additional comments or notes about the property.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or real estate experts.
* Keep your notes section for any additional comments or information about the property.
