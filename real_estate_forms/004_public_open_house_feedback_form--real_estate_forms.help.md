# Public Open House Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Open House Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback on the open house event. This information will be used to improve future events and provide a better experience for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Agent's Name from the list provided.
2. Enter the Contact Email of the person providing feedback.
3. Enter the Contact Phone number of the person providing feedback.
4. Enter the location of the open house event.
5. Share any additional Feedback Message regarding the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agent Name (Select One)** (`real_estate_agent`, select_one, required: false): Choose the name of the agent who hosted the open house event from the list provided.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person providing feedback.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person providing feedback.
* **Open House Location** (`open_house_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the open house event.
* **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about the event.
