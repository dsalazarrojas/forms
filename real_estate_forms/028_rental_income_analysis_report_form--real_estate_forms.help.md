# Rental Income Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the rental income of a property or a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "General Information" page and fill in any additional information that may be relevant to the rental income analysis.
2. Click on the "Financial Data" page and fill in the required financial data:
	* **Financial Data (required)**: This field collects financial data related to the rental income.
3. Click on the "Tax Info" page and fill in any tax-related information (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Data (financial_data)**: This field collects financial data related to the rental income.
* **General Information (general_info)**: This field is for any additional information that may be relevant to the rental income analysis.
* **Tax Info (tax_info)**: This field is for tax-related information (optional).
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
