# rrg_team_sfl_nysar_covid_19_health_travel_questionnaire_04_01_2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RRG Team's SFL for NY SARS COVID-19 Health Travel Questionnaire as of April 1, 2021.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title.
2. Provide a brief description of the form.
3. Select the category for this form (e.g., Real Estate Forms, Other Forms).
4. Enter the form ID (a unique number).
5. Assign a tool to this form (if applicable).
6. Fill in the submitted-by field (e.g., your name or initials).
7. Enter the submitted date (mm/dd/yyyy).
8. Enter the submitted time (HH:MM AM/PM).
9. Fill in the submitted location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for this form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the form.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category for this form (e.g., Real Estate Forms, Other Forms).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required: false): Enter a unique ID for this form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): If applicable, assign a tool to this form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name or initials of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the form was submitted (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the form was submitted (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Submitted Location** (`submitted_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the data before submission.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask the relevant department or team member for guidance.
* This form is designed to collect data on a specific date, so ensure that the submitted data is up-to-date.
