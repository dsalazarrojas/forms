# Open House Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather feedback from individuals who attended or did not attend an open house event. The form is used to assess the event's success and identify areas for improvement for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question of whether you attended the open house event.
2. Provide a rating for the open house event, using a scale of 1-5.
3. If you chose not to attend the open house event, please explain why.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or text).
5. Enter your email address and phone number.
6. Add any additional comments you would like to share about the event.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attended Open House**: Select the option that best describes your attendance status at the open house event.
* **How would you rate the Open House**: Rate the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Why did you rate it that way**: If you chose a rating, please explain why you rated the event that way.
* **What was most important for you**: Select all that apply regarding what was most important for you at the event.
