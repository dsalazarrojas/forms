# Security Deposit Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Deposit Waiver Form is used to waive the security deposit required for a rental property. This form is typically used when a tenant and landlord agree to waive the deposit, usually due to specific circumstances such as a short-term lease or a unique rental situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Fill out your name and contact information in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the correct signature option from the "Signature" field.
4. Date the signature in the "Signature Date" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Tenant Phone" and "Landlord Phone" fields.
6. Complete any other required fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Tenant Name (`tenant_name`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
### 2. Landlord Name (`landlord_name`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
### 3. Deposit Amount (`deposit_amount`, Number, Required: False)
* Enter the amount of security deposit you would like to waive.
### 4. Deposit Date (`deposit_date`, Date, Required: False)
* Enter the date the security deposit would have normally been due.
### 5. Move-in Date (`move_in_date`, Date, Required: False)
* Enter the date you moved into the rental property.
### 6. Move-in Date (Duplicate, Date, Required: False)
* This field is a duplicate of the "Move-in Date" field above and does not need to be filled out.
### 7. Security Deposit Return Date (`security_deposit_return_date`, Date, Required: False)
* Enter the date the security deposit would have normally been due to be returned.
### 8. Signature (`signature`, Select Multiple, Required: False)
* Select "Tenant", "Landlord", or "Other" to indicate who is signing the waiver.
### 9. Signature Date (`signature_date`, Date, Required: False)
* Date the signature was made.
### 10. Tenant Phone (`tenant_phone`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your phone number.
### 11. Landlord Phone (`landlord_phone`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your landlord's phone number.

## Tips
- Be sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Attach any additional documentation or explanations as necessary.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your landlord or property manager.
