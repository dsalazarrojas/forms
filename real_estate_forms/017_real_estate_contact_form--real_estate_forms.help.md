# real_estate_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_contact_form is designed to collect contact information and property details from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** in the first name field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Email address** in the third field.
4. Enter your **Phone number** in the fourth field.
5. Select your **Property type** by choosing from the options provided in the fifth field.
6. Select your **Location** by choosing from the options provided in the sixth field.
7. Write a brief **Description** of your property in the seventh field.
8. Enter any additional comments or questions in the **Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: Enter your first name to help us identify you.
- **Last name**: Enter your last name to help us identify you.
- **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
- **Phone number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
- **Property type**: Select 'Yes' if you have a real estate property, or 'No' if you do not.
- **Location**: Select 'Yes' if your property is located in a specific area, or 'No' if it is not.
- **Description**: Provide a brief description of your property.
- **Message**: Use this field to ask any questions or provide additional comments.
