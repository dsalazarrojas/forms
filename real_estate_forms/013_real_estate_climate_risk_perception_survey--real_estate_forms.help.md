# Real Estate Climate Risk Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on real estate climate risk perception. It is a survey aimed at collecting general perceptions and opinions on climate change-related risks among real estate professionals and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question carefully, considering your general knowledge and experience in the real estate industry.
- For questions with multiple options, select all applicable options that apply to your situation.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
