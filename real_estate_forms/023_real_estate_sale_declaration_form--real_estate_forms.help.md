# Real Estate Sale Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a real estate sale, including property details, buyer and seller information, and transaction details. It is intended to be used for official purposes, such as documentation and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form sections: The form is divided into four sections: Property Details, Buyer Information, Seller Information, and Transaction Details.
2. Fill out each section: Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing all required information.
3. Submit the form: Once you've filled out all sections, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details** (`property_details_form`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the property being sold, including its address and any other relevant details.
* **Buyer Information** (`buyer_info_form`, text, required): Enter the buyer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Seller Information** (`seller_info_form`, text, required): Enter the seller's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Transaction Details** (`transaction_details_form`, text, required): Describe the transaction, including any relevant dates, prices, and terms.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each section carefully and accurately.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
- If you're unsure about any information or have questions, please ask for assistance.
