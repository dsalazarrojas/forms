# Manufactured Home Appraisal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Manufactured Home Appraisal Form is designed to collect information about a manufactured home, enabling real estate professionals and other stakeholders to conduct appraisals and gather data on the property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information. Note that some fields may be optional, so only fill out the fields that are relevant to your needs.
2. Ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information before submitting the form.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Manufactured Home Address
* **Manufactured Home Address** (`manufactured_home_address`, text, optional): This field is where you should enter the address of the manufactured home.

### Manufactured Home Type
* **Manufactured Home Type** (`manufactured_home_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the home is manufactured, or 'No' if it is not.

### Manufactured Home Serial Number
* **Manufactured Home Serial Number** (`manufactured_home_serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the manufactured home, if applicable.

### Appraised Value
* **Appraised Value** (`appraised_value`, number, optional): This field is for recording the appraised value of the home. Enter a numerical value.

### Purchase Price
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, optional): Enter the purchase price of the home, if applicable.

### Year Built
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, number, optional): Enter the year the home was built.

### Square Footage
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, optional): Enter the square footage of the home.

## Tips

* Double-check the information you enter for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and share the information contained within the form.
