# Sell Us Your Mobile Home - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sell Us Your Mobile Home" form is designed to collect information about your mobile home, its features, and any other relevant details that would make it attractive to potential buyers. This form will help us assess the condition and features of your mobile home, making it easier for us to provide you with a fair and accurate offer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the field label.
2. For each field, provide as much information as possible to give us a complete picture of your mobile home.
3. If a field is not applicable, please mark it as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mobile Home Address**: The address of your mobile home, including the street number and name, city, state, and zip code.
* **Mobile Home Type**: The type of mobile home you have, such as single-wide, double-wide, or triple-wide.
* **Year Installed**: The year your mobile home was installed.
* **Mobile Home Size**: The size of your mobile home in square feet.
* **Year Made**: The year your mobile home was made.
* **Length**: The length of your mobile home in feet.
* **Width**: The width of your mobile home in feet.
* **Height**: The height of your mobile home in feet.
* **Lot Size**: The size of the lot your mobile home is on in square feet.
* **Location**: The location of your mobile home, including the street number and name, city, state, and zip code.
* **Description**: A brief description of your mobile home, including its features and any upgrades.
* **Condition**: The condition of your mobile home, such as excellent, fair, or poor.
* **Features**: The features of your mobile home, such as a porch, deck, or appliances.
* **Parking**: The parking options available with your mobile home, such as a parking pad or driveway.
* **Yard Size**: The size of the yard around your mobile home in square feet.
* **Nighborhood**: The neighborhood where your mobile home is located.
* **Price**: The price you are asking for your mobile home.
* **Financing**: The financing options available for your mobile home.
* **Insurance**: The insurance options available for your mobile home.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional notes or comments about your mobile home.

## Tips

* Please provide as much information as possible about your mobile home to give us a complete picture.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will be in touch with you to discuss your mobile home and provide you with a fair and accurate offer.
