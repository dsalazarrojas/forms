# Pre Purchase Home Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you gather and document information about a property's condition before purchasing it. It covers various aspects of the property, including structural elements, electrical and plumbing systems, HVAC systems, safety features, environmental factors, and other items of interest. This form is meant to be a comprehensive checklist for homebuyers to identify potential issues and make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the relevant pages that apply to your inspection. This form is divided into several sections that correspond to different parts of the property.
2.  Fill out each page by selecting or typing your observations for the corresponding section.
3.  Use the options provided as guidance for your answers. These options are not exhaustive and should be used as starting points for your own notes and observations.
4.  Don't hesitate to add additional comments or remarks if you feel they are necessary to provide a full picture of the property's condition.
5.  Once you've completed all sections, review your answers for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Property Details** (Page 1)
*   **Property Address** (text, required): Enter the address of the property being inspected.
*   **Location** (text, required): Describe the location of the property, including any notable features or surroundings.
*   **Age of Property** (text, required): Provide the age of the property, or when it was built.
*   **Type of Property** (text, required): Indicate the type of property (e.g., single-family home, condo, townhouse, etc.).

### 2. **Structural Elements** (Page 2)
*   **Roof** (select multiple, required): Check the condition of the roof, including its age, material, and any signs of damage or wear.
*   **Floors** (select multiple, required): Evaluate the condition of the floors, including their age, material, and any signs of damage or wear.
*   **Walls** (select multiple, required): Inspect the walls for cracks, water damage, or other signs of structural issues.
*   **Foundation** (select multiple, required): Check the foundation for any signs of damage or instability.

### 3. **Electrical Systems** (Page 3)
*   **Fuses** (select one, required): Check if the property has fuses and if they are functional.
*   **Circuit Breakers** (select one, required): Check if the property has circuit breakers and if they are functioning properly.
*   **Panel Box** (select one, required): Inspect the condition and functionality of the electrical panel box.

### 4. **Plumbing Systems** (Page 4)
*   **Water Heater** (select multiple, required): Evaluate the condition and functionality of the water heater.
*   **Water Supply** (select multiple, required): Check the water supply lines and fixtures for leaks or damage.
*   **Drain System** (select multiple, required): Inspect the condition and functionality of the drain system.

### 5. **HVAC Systems** (Page 5)
*   **Heating System** (select multiple, required): Check the functionality and condition of the heating system.
*   **Cooling System** (select multiple, required): Evaluate the condition and functionality of the cooling system.
*   **Ventilation System** (select multiple, required): Inspect the condition and functionality of the ventilation system.

### 6. **Safety Features** (Page 6)
*   **Smoke Detector** (select multiple, required): Check if smoke detectors are present and functioning properly.
*   **Fire Extinguisher** (select multiple, required): Inspect the condition and functionality of fire extinguishers.
*   **Emergency Exit** (select multiple, required): Evaluate the condition and functionality of emergency exits.

### 7. **Environmental Factors** (Page 7)
*   **Pest** (select multiple, required): Check for signs of pest infestation.
*   **Mold** (select multiple, required): Inspect for mold growth or evidence of past mold infestation.
*   **Radon** (select multiple, required): Evaluate the presence of radon.

### 8. **Other Items** (Page 8)
*   **Other Items** (text, required): Describe any other items of interest or concern.

### 9. **Comments** (Page 9)
*   **Comments** (text, required): Add any additional comments or remarks about the property's condition.

### 10. **Recommended Repairs** (Page 10)
*   **Recommended Repairs** (text, required): Suggest any recommended repairs or maintenance for the property.

### 11. **Additional Info** (Page 11)
*   **Additional Info** (text, required): Provide any additional information about the property that may be relevant to the sale.

### 12. **Property Condition** (Page 12)
*   **Property Condition** (select one, required): Evaluate the overall condition of the property (Well Maintained, Poorly Maintained, or Needs Maintenance).

### Tips
*   Take your time to carefully inspect each section and be thorough in your observations.
*   If you're unsure about any part of the form, feel free to ask for guidance or clarification.
*   Remember to also take photos or notes to supplement your answers and provide a visual record of the property's condition.
