# Residence Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residence Verification Form is a comprehensive form used to collect information about a tenant's stay in a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the tenant's stay before filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. Verify the information with the tenant and landlord before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (1): Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Property Address** (2): Enter the address of the property where the tenant resides.
* **Date Moved In** (3): Enter the date the tenant moved in.
* **Current Status** (4): Select the current status of the tenant's stay, either "Active" or "Moved Out".
* **Contact Method** (5): Select how the tenant would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Text).
* **Contact Person** (6): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (7): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (8): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Landlord Name** (9): Enter the name of the landlord or property owner.
* **Landlord Company** (10): Enter the name of the landlord's company (if applicable).
* **Property Type** (11): Enter the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, etc.).
* **Property Size** (12): Enter the size of the property (e.g., number of bedrooms, square feet, etc.).
* **Move In Date** (13): Enter the date the tenant moved in.
* **Move Out Date** (14): Enter the date the tenant moved out (if applicable).
* **Lever Start Date** (15): Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lever End Date** (16): Enter the end date of the lease (if applicable).
* **Lever Duration** (17): Enter the length of the lease (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, etc.).
* **Rent Start Date** (18): Enter the start date of the rent period (e.g., first of the month, etc.).
* **Rent Amount** (19): Enter the amount of rent paid.
* **Rent Frequency** (20): Select the frequency of rent (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Move Out Reason** (21): Enter the reason for the tenant's move-out (if applicable).
* **Tenant Signature** (22): Enter the signature of the tenant.
* **Landlord Signature** (23): Enter the signature of the landlord or property owner.
* **Verification Date** (24): Enter the date of the form submission.

Note: The form's fields are labeled and explained to guide you in filling it out accurately and effectively.
