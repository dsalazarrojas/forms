# Gift Letter For Mortgage - Help Guide
## Purpose
A Gift Letter For Mortgage is used by donors to provide mortgage lenders with information about a property being gifted. This helps the lender understand the property's ownership and transfer history, which is essential for mortgage underwriting and compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the following information about the donor:
	* The donor's name and contact details.
	* Details about the property being gifted (page 2).
2. Fill out the home information page (page 3) with the relevant details about the property.
3. Enter the gift information (page 4) and confirm that the gift is intended for the beneficiary listed on the first page.
4. Verify that the information provided is accurate (page 5).
5. Sign and date the form as the donor (page 6).
6. If you have any additional information related to the gift, you can include it on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information (Page 1)**: This section captures the donor's details, including their name and contact information. Please fill this in accurately to ensure the lender can contact you if needed.
* **Home Information (Page 2)**: Provide details about the property being gifted, such as its address, year built, and number of bedrooms.
* **Gift Information (Page 3)**: Confirm that the gift is intended for the beneficiary listed on the first page and describe the gift details.
* **Verification (Page 4)**: Double-check the information you've provided to ensure it is accurate and complete.
* **Donor's Signature (Page 5)**: This is where you should sign and date the form to confirm that you have provided accurate and true information.
* **Date (Page 6)**: Date the form to provide a clear record of when the form was completed.
* **Additional Information (Page 7)**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or information you'd like to share with the lender.
