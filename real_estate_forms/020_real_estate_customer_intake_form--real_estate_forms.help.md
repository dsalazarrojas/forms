## real_estate_customer_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The real_estate_customer_intake_form is designed to collect information from potential buyers to guide the real estate buying process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the introduction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name for personal identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name for personal identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Provide your email address for real estate communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number for quick contact and updates.
* **Desired Property Type** (`desired_property_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of property you are interested in (e.g., Single Family, Townhouse, Condo, Apartment, or Duplex).
* **Desired Property Location** (`desired_property_location`, text, **required**): Enter the location where you would like to purchase a property.
* **Desired Property Price Range** (`desired_property_price_range`, number, **optional**): Select the price range you are comfortable with for the property.
* **Desired Property Size In Sqft** (`desired_property_size_in_sqft`, number, **optional**): Enter the square footage of the property you desire.
* **Buyer Agent Name** (`buyer_agent_name`, text, **optional**): If you have a real estate agent, enter their name.
* **Buyer Agent Company** (`buyer_agent_company`, text, **optional**): If you have a real estate agent, enter their company name.
