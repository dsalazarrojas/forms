# New Buyer Compliance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Buyer Compliance Form is used to collect information about a real estate property, including its address, type, status, and compliance details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On page 1, fill in the required fields:
  * **Address 1**: Enter the primary address of the property.
  * **Address 2**: Enter the secondary address of the property, if applicable.
  * **Property Type**: Select the type of property (Single-family, Multi-unit, or Condo).
  * **Property Status**: Select the current status of the property (Active, Pending, or Sold-Out).
  * **Compliance File**: Upload the compliance file associated with the property.
  * **Compliance Date**: Enter the date of the compliance.
  * **Compliance Time**: Enter the time of the compliance.
  * **Uploaded By**: Enter the name of the person who uploaded the compliance file.
  * **File Description**: Enter a brief description of the compliance file.
  * **Uploaded At**: Enter the date the file was uploaded.
  * **Uploaded By User**: Enter the name of the user who uploaded the file.

2. On page 2, fill in the additional details:
  * **Note**: Enter any additional details about the property's compliance status.
  * **Email**: Enter the email address associated with the property.
  * **Phone**: Enter the phone number associated with the property.
  * **File**: Upload any additional files associated with the property.
  * **Uploaded By**: Enter the name of the person who uploaded the file.
  * **Uploaded At**: Enter the date the file was uploaded.
