# Apartment Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apartment Waitlist Form is used to gather information from potential tenants who are interested in being placed on a waitlist for apartments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
* Make sure to provide all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waitlist registration date**: * (registration_date) - The date you registered for the waitlist.
	+ Please enter the date you submitted your application.
* **First name**: * (first_name) - Your first name.
	+ Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: * (last_name) - Your last name.
	+ Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address**: * (email_address) - Your email address.
	+ Please enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone number**: * (phone_number) - Your phone number.
	+ Please enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Apartment complex of interest**: * (apartment_complex) - The apartment complex you are interested in.
	+ Please enter the name of the apartment complex you are interested in.
* **Preferred unit size**: * (unit_size_preference) - The number of bedrooms you prefer in a unit.
	+ Please choose the number of bedrooms you would like to have.
* **Floor preference**: * (floor_preference) - The floor preference of the unit you are interested in.
	+ Choose between Ground floor, Upper floors, High floor, or No preference.
* **Budget range for monthly rent**: * (budget_range) - The budget range you are willing to pay for monthly rent.
	+ Please enter the minimum and maximum monthly rent you are willing to pay.
* **Desired move-in date**: * (desired_move_in) - The date you are looking to move in.
	+ Please enter the date you are available to move in.
* **Preferred lease term**: * (lease_term_preference) - The length of time you would like your lease to be.
	+ Choose between Month-to-month, 3-6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 3+ years, or Flexible.
* **Type of occupancy**: * (occupancy_type) - The type of occupancy you are interested in.
	+ Choose between Individual, Couple, Family with children, Roommates, or Other.
* **Number of occupants**: * (number_of_occupants) - The number of people who will be occupying the unit.
	+ Please enter the number of people who will be living in the unit.
* **Pet ownership**: * (pet_ownership) - If you have pets.
	+ Choose between No pets, Cats, Dogs, Both cats and dogs, or Other pets.
* **Pet details**: (pet_details) - If you have pets, please provide details about them (breed, size, number).
	+ Please provide any additional information about your pets.
* **Current residential status**: * (current_residency) - Your current residential status.
	+ Choose between Currently renting elsewhere, Currently living with family, Currently living with friends, Relocating from another area, or Other.
* **Employment status**: * (employment_status) - Your employment status.
	+ Choose between Employed full-time, Employed part-time, Self-employed, Student, Retired, or Other.
* **Income verification readiness**: * (income_verification_ready) - Your income verification readiness.
	+ Choose between Yes, readily available, Yes, can obtain, Not sure, or False.
* **Rental history**: * (rental_history) - Your rental history with previous landlords.
	+ Choose between Good references available, Fair references, Poor references, First-time renter, or No references available.
* **Background check consent**: * (background_check_consent) - If you consent to background checks.
	+ Choose between Yes, I consent, or No, I do not consent.
* **Credit check consent**: * (credit_check_consent) - If you consent to credit checks.
	+ Choose between Yes, I consent, or No, I do not consent.
* **Understanding of waitlist position**: * (waitlist_position) - Acknowledgment that your waitlist position may change.
	+ Choose between Yes, I understand or False.
* **Notification preference**: * (notification_preference) - Your preferred method of notification when a unit becomes available.
	+ Choose between Email, Phone call, Text message, or No preference.
* **Additional information or requests**: (additional_information) - Any additional information or requests you have.
	+ Please provide any additional information you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your application carefully before submitting it.
* Incomplete or inaccurate forms may lead to delays or disqualification from the waitlist.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
