# Real Estate Data Privacy Opinion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather the opinions of real estate professionals on data privacy practices in the industry. This form aims to understand how real estate professionals perceive and respond to data privacy concerns, and to collect suggestions on how to improve data privacy in real estate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the survey in the "Real Estate Data Privacy Opinion Survey" field.
2. Select a category from the dropdown menu to indicate which category your response relates to.
3. Check the boxes to indicate your consent to participating in the survey.
4. Select one risk from the dropdown menu that describes the biggest risk you perceive related to data privacy.
5. In the "Data Privacy" field, provide any additional comments or explanations about your thoughts on data privacy.
6. Leave any comments or suggestions for improving data privacy in real estate in the "Client Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Real Estate Data Privacy Opinion Survey** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the survey that describes its purpose and content.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select a category to indicate which part of real estate data privacy this response relates to.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes to indicate your consent to participating in the survey and to sharing your data with the survey creators.
* **Risk** (`risk`, select_one, required): Select one risk from the dropdown menu that describes the biggest risk you perceive related to data privacy.
* **Data Privacy** (`data_privacy`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations about your thoughts on data privacy.
* **Client Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Leave any comments or suggestions for improving data privacy in real estate.
