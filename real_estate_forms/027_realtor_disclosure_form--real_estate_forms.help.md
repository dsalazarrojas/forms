# Realtor Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Realtor Disclosure Form is a critical component in the real estate industry, designed to gather information from realtors about their agency details. This form is essential for ensuring that realtors operate within the confines of the law and maintain the integrity of real estate transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Introduction** field with a brief statement about your agency or brokerage.
2. Select the **realtor_agency** field to indicate whether the realtor is associated with an agency.
3. In the **realtor_agent** field, select whether the realtor is an agent or not.
4. Enter your agency's **realtor_agency_address** in the respective field.
5. Enter your own **realtor_agent_address** in the designated field.
6. If the realtor has a **realtor_agency_broker**, indicate whether they are associated with a broker or not in the **realtor_agent_broker** field.
7. In the **realtor_agency_licenses** field, input the license number of the agency.
8. Input the **realtor_agency_license_expiration** date in the respective field.
9. Indicate the type of license for the agency in the **realtor_agency_license_type** field.
10. Enter the fee structure and explanation for the agency in the **realtor_agency_fees** fields.
11. Select the type of agency in the **realtor_agency_agency_type** field.
12. Input your **realtor_agent_commission** in the respective field.
13. Select whether you are associated with an agency or not in the **realtor_agent_agency** field.
14. Select the **realtor_agent_member** field to indicate agency membership.
15. Enter the license number and type for the agency in the **realtor_agency_license_number** and **realtor_agency_license_type** fields.
16. Input the **realtor_agency_license_expiration** date for the agency.
17. Input your own **realtor_agent_phone** and **realtor_agent_email** for communication purposes.
18. Enter the agency's website address in the **realtor_agency_website** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (id: 1)**: A brief statement about your agency or brokerage.
* **real_estate_company_info (id: 2)**: Enter your agency's name and information.
* **real_estate_agency (id: 3)**: Select whether you are associated with an agency.
* **real_estate_agent (id: 4)**: Indicate whether you are an agent or not.
* **real_estate_agency_address (id: 5)**: Enter your agency's address.
* **real_estate_agent_address (id: 6)**: Enter your own address.
* **real_estate_agent_broker (id: 7)**: Indicate whether you are associated with a broker.
* **real_estate_agency_phone (id: 8)**: Input your agency's phone number.
* **real_estate_agency_email (id: 9)**: Input your agency's email address.
* **real_estate_agency_fax (id: 10)**: Input your agency's fax number.
* **real_estate_agency_website (id: 11)**: Input your agency's website address.
* **real_estate_agency_license (id: 12)**: Enter your agency's license number.
* **real_estate_agency_license_expiration (id: 13)**: Enter your agency's license expiration date.
* **real_estate_agency_license_type (id: 14)**: Indicate the type of agency license.
* **real_estate_agency_fees (id: 15)**: Input your agency's fee structure.
* **real_estate_agency_fees_explanation (id: 16)**: Input an explanation for your agency's fees.
* **realtor_agency_agency_type (id: 17)**: Select the type of agency you are associated with.
* **realtor_agent_commission (id: 18)**: Input your commission details.
* **realtor_agent_agency (id: 19)**: Indicate whether you are associated with an agency.
* **realtor_agency_member (id: 20)**: Select whether you are a member of an agency.
* **realtor_agency_license_number (id: 21)**: Input your agency's license number.
* **realtor_agency_license_expiration (id: 22)**: Input your agency's license expiration date.
* **realtor_agency_license_type (id: 23)**: Indicate the type of license for your agency.
* **realtor_agency_fees (id: 24)**: Input your agency's fees.
* **realtor_agency_agency (id: 25)**: Select whether you are associated with an agency.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
* Double-check the information you provide for accuracy and consistency.
* Ensure that you are providing up-to-date information to maintain the integrity of real estate transactions.
* Review and verify the information before submitting the form.
