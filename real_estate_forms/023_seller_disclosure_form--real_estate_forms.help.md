# Seller Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seller Disclosure Form is designed to collect general information, property details, hazards, seller and buyer information, and any additional comments or disclosures. This form is intended for use in real estate transactions to facilitate communication and exchange of information between parties involved in the sale or lease of a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Fill out the **General Info** section with the general information about the property and the sale or lease.
3. In the **Property Details** section, provide detailed information about the property, including its condition, location, and any other relevant details.
4. In the **Hazards** section, mention any known hazards or issues with the property.
5. In the **Seller Info**, **Seller Comments**, **Buyer Comments 1-10**, and **Buyer Disclosures 1-5** sections, enter any additional comments or disclosures related to the property and its sale or lease.
6. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure all necessary information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info**:
	+ **General Info** (text): Enter a brief description of the property and the reason for the sale or lease.
* **Property Details**:
	+ **Property Details** (text): Provide detailed information about the property, including its location, size, and condition.
* **Hazards**:
	+ **Hazards** (text): Mention any known hazards or issues with the property.
* **Seller Info**:
	+ **Seller Info** (text): Enter any additional information about the seller.
* **Seller Comments**:
	+ **Seller Comments** (text): Enter any comments or concerns from the seller.
* **Buyer Comments 1-10**:
	+ **Buyer Comments 1-10** (text): Enter any comments or concerns from the buyer.
* **Buyer Disclosures 1-5**:
	+ **Buyer Disclosures 1-5** (text): Enter any disclosures or concerns from the buyer.

Note: This help guide assumes that all these fields are meant to be collected for different scenarios or purposes. If that's not the case, the guide might need to be adjusted accordingly.
