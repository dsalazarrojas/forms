# Rental Living Spaces Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Living Spaces Form is designed to gather essential information about your rental living spaces. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences for your current or desired rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your registration status from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Provide your address.
6. Choose the type of property you are interested in (Apartment, House, or Condo).
7. Enter the square footage of the property.
8. Specify the number of bedrooms in the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you are registering as a new tenant or if you already have a current lease.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your current or desired address.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of property you are looking for (Apartment, House, or Condo).
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, required: false): Enter the square footage of the property.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`number_of_bedrooms`, number, required: false): Specify the number of bedrooms in the property.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the fields marked as required are filled in order to submit a complete form.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, do not hesitate to ask!
