# Retail Space Lease Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Space Lease Extension Form is a document used by tenants to request an extension of their current retail space lease. This form should only be used after discussing the lease extension with your representative from the landlord's company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the information requested. Make sure to check the "How it works" section for any specific instructions or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord** (`landlord_contact`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the person you are in contact with regarding this lease extension.
* **Tenant** (`tenant_contact`, text, optional): Please enter your contact information.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the lease started.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the current end date of the lease.
* **Lease Term Length** (`lease_term_length`, text, optional): Please enter the desired lease term length.
* **Space Location** (`space_location`, text, optional): Please enter the location of the space.
* **Lease Details** (`lease_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details or requests regarding the lease.
* **Rental Rate** (`rental_rate`, number, optional): Enter the current rental rate.

## Tips
* Make sure to have all necessary information ready before filling out this form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your representative from the landlord's company.
* This form is only for retail space leases. If you have already filled out a lease extension form for this space, do not fill out this form again.
