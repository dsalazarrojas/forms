# rental_property_appraisal_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rental_property_appraisal_request_form is used to collect information about a rental property, including its details, appraiser, address, value, and transaction history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are completing the form for a rental property.
2. Select the property details, including the property type and status.
3. Choose the appraiser who will be conducting the appraisal.
4. Enter the property address, appraised value, and purchase price.
5. Enter the purchase date and sale price (if applicable).
6. Select the closing date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Property Details (1)
• **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the property.
### Appraiser (2)
• **Appraiser** (`appraiser`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more appraisers from the list of available appraisers.
### Address (3)
• **Address** (`property_address`, note, optional): Enter the address of the rental property.
### Appraised Value (4)
• **Appraised Value** (`appraised_value`, number, optional): Enter the appraised value of the rental property.
### Purchase Price (5)
• **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, optional): Enter the purchase price of the rental property.
### Purchase Date (6)
• **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the rental property was purchased.
### Sale Price (7)
• **Sale Price** (`sale_price`, number, optional): Enter the sale price of the rental property (if applicable).
### Closing Date (8)
• **Closing Date** (`closing_date`, date, optional): Enter the closing date of the rental property (if applicable).
### Property Type (9)
• **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property, such as Single Family, Condo, or Townhouse.
### Property Status (10)
• **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the property, such as Active, Sold, or Off Market.
