# Open House Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open House Inspection Form is used to gather feedback from buyers after an open house inspection. This feedback is essential for understanding the buyer's thoughts on the property's condition and the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name as the person providing feedback (Buyer Name).
2.  Enter your contact information (Contact Info).
3.  Enter a valid phone number (Phone).
4.  Choose the date and time of the inspection (Date and Time).
5.  Select the inspection items that you would like to comment on (Inspection Items).
6.  Enter any additional notes or comments you have about the inspection (Notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer Feedback (Buyer Feedback)**: Enter your overall feedback about the inspection and the property's condition.
* **Buyer Name (Buyer Name)**: Enter your name as the person providing feedback.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)**: Enter your contact information, such as your email address.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date and Time (Date and Time)**: Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Items (Inspection Items)**: Select all the inspection items that you would like to comment on. The options include:
	+ Condition of the kitchen
	+ Condition of the bathroom
	+ Condition of the roof
	+ Condition of the yard
	+ Condition of the basement
	+ Condition of the foundation
	+ Condition of the garage
	+ Condition of the attic
* **Additional Notes (Notes)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Take the time to carefully review the inspection items and choose the ones that are relevant to your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, be as detailed as possible to help the seller understand your feedback.
