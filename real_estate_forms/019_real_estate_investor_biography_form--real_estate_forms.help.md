# Real Estate Investor Biography Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a real estate investor's identity and professional credentials. This information can be used for networking, marketing, or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each field.
2. Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank if you don't have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (3, text, required: false): Enter your job title or professional designation (e.g., Realtor, Agent, Broker, etc.).
* **Email** (4, email, required: false): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (6, text, required: false): Enter your business or company address (if applicable).
* **Company** (7, text, required: false): Enter your company or organization name (if applicable).
* **Website** (8, text, required: false): Enter your company or personal website (if applicable).
* **Bio** (9, note, required: false): Provide a brief personal bio about yourself and your professional background.
* **Image URL** (10, text, required: false): Enter the URL of your professional profile picture or logo (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure the best results.
* You can leave blank any field that you don't have the information for.
* If you need help or have any questions, contact our support team for assistance.
