# new_construction_property_reservation_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients or customers for a new construction property reservation contract. It's a crucial document that outlines the terms and conditions of the reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the relevant details in the provided fields.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Unit Details (1)
* **Unit Details**: Enter the details of the unit you are reserving, including its address, size, and any other relevant information.

### Buyer Details (2)
* **Buyer Details**: Enter the name and contact information of the buyer or client.

### Reservation Details (3)
* **Reservation Details**: Enter the details of the reservation, including the reservation date, time, and any special instructions.

### Payment Details (4)
* **Payment Details**: Enter the payment information, including the amount and method of payment.

### E-Signature (5)
* **E-Signature**: Enter your electronic signature to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the reservation contract.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
