# Todays Mortgage Rates - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your mortgage rates and preferences, so we can provide you with more accurate and personalized loan quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of loan you are interested in from the "What type of loan" field.
2. Choose the type of property you are interested in from the "What type of property" field.
3. Enter your annual income in the "Annual Income" field.
4. Select your employment status from the "What is your employment status" field.
5. Choose how often you receive your income from the "How often is your income paid" field.
6. Select your income source from the "What is your income source" field.
7. Enter any additional information as required in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Refinance Property**: Enter the address of the property you want to refinance.
* **What type of loan**: Select the type of loan you are interested in (e.g. 2-3% ARM, 5/1 ARM, etc.).
* **What is your income type**: Select your income type (e.g. Single, Married, etc.).
* **Annual Income**: Enter your annual income.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the amount of the loan you are interested in.
* **Loan Term (years)**: Enter the term of the loan you are interested in (e.g. 15, 30 years, etc.).
* **Home Price**: Enter the price of the property you are interested in.
* **Interest Rate**: Enter the interest rate you are interested in (if known).
* **What type of mortgage rate**: Select the type of mortgage rate you are interested in (e.g. Fixed, Adjustable, etc.).
* **Credit Score**: Enter your credit score (if known).
* **What type of property**: Select the type of property you are interested in (e.g. Primary Residence, Investment, etc.).
* **What is your employment status**: Select your employment status (e.g. Employed, Self-Employed, etc.).
* **How often is your income paid**: Select how often you receive your income.
* **What is your income source**: Select your income source (e.g. Business, Salary, etc.).
* **Annual Income Frequency**: Enter how often you pay your income.
