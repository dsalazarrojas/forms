# Notice Of Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Notice of Entry Form efficiently and correctly. The form is used to document the entry of individuals or goods into a property, ensuring accurate records and standardized reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the Landlord Information section with the relevant details about the property owner or manager.
- Provide the Tenant Information section with the details about the person or entity entering the property.
- Add the Property Details section to note any relevant property specifics, such as the location or unit number.
- Specify the Purpose Of Visit to indicate the reason for the entry.
- Enter the Date And Time of the entry event.
- Record the Date for the Notice Of Entry Form.
- Sign and date the Notice Of Entry Form to validate its completion.
- If applicable, include the Landlord Signature and Property Manager Signature fields for validation from the respective parties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Information** (`landlord_information`, text, required: false): Enter the landlord's or property manager's details such as name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Tenant Information** (`tenant_information`, text, required: false): Provide the tenant's name, contact details, or any other information that identifies them.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, required: false): Record the property's address, unit number, or any other specifics.
* **Purpose Of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, text, required: false): Clearly state the reason for the entry, e.g., delivery, inspection, or visitor.
* **Date And Time** (`date_and_time`, time, required: false): Enter the exact time and date of the entry event.
* **Notice Of Entry Form Date** (`notice_of_entry_form_date`, date, required: false): Record the date of the entry event.
* **Notice Of Entry Form Signature** (`notice_of_entry_form_signature`, note, required: false): Include your signature as evidence of form completion.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, text, required: false): Obtain the landlord's or property manager's signature to validate their knowledge of the entry.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager_signature`, text, required: false): If applicable, obtain the property manager's signature for validation of their knowledge of the entry.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and correctly.
- Use the space provided for each field to keep the form organized and easy to read.
- If you need to make changes or add more details, refer back to this help guide for clarity.
- Keep a record of all completed forms for future reference and record-keeping purposes.
