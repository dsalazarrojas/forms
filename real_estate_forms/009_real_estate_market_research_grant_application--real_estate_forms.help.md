# real_estate_market_research_grant_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations seeking to apply for a real estate market research grant. The grant is intended to support research initiatives that contribute to the advancement of the real estate industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form information on **Form Info**.
2. Provide your contact details on **Contact Info**.
3. Share your background and relevant experience on **Applicant Info**.
4. Describe your project proposal on **Project Info**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Info**:
	+ *Form Title* (`title`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the title of your real estate market research grant application.
* **Contact Info**:
	+ *Name* (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name or the name of your organization.
	+ *Phone* (`phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number or the phone number of your organization.
	+ *Email* (`email`, text, required/optional): Enter your email address or the email address of your organization.
* **Applicant Info**:
	+ *Background* (`background`, text, required/optional): Describe your background and relevant experience in the real estate industry.
	+ *CV* (`cv`, text, required/optional): Attach your curriculum vitae or resume, highlighting your relevant experience and qualifications.
* **Project Info**:
	+ *Project Title* (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title of your project proposal.
	+ *Project Description* (`description`, text, required/optional): Describe your project proposal and its objectives.
	+ *Budget* (`budget`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed breakdown of your project's budget.
	+ *Timeline* (`timeline`, text, required/optional): Outline your project's timeline and milestones.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in each field.
* Attach relevant documents or attachments where possible.
* Proofread your application carefully before submission.
