# real_estate_referral_network_signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real estate referral network signup form is designed for real estate agents to join the platform and receive referrals from partners. The form collects necessary information to connect agents with potential clients in their area.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your real estate agency's name in the "What is your real estate agency's name?" field.
2. Provide your name in the "What is your partner's name?" field.
3. Select the method you will use to refer your partners in the "How will you refer your partners?" field.
4. Check if your partner's location is in your service area in the "Is your partner's location in your service area?" field.
5. Enter your partner's email address in the "What is your partner's email address?" field.
6. Confirm that you agree with the referral terms by selecting "True" in the "Do you agree with our referral terms?" field.
7. Optionally, provide feedback on the referral terms in the "Can you provide feedback on our referral terms?" field.
8. Confirm your partner's contact details by checking "Yes" in the "Can you confirm your partner's contact details?" field.
9. Enter a confirmation message for your partner in the "Partner Confirm" field.
10. Provide feedback on the referral terms by checking "Option A" or "Option B" in the "Are you sure about our referral terms?" field.
11. Optionally, provide additional feedback in the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your real estate agency's name?**: Enter your real estate agency's name to associate with your partner's referral information.
* **What is your partner's name?**: Enter your partner's name to connect with their referral information.
* **How will you refer your partners?**: Choose the method you will use to refer your partners, such as phone, email, or social media.
* **Is your partner's location in your service area?**: Check "Option A" if your partner's location is within your service area, or "Option B" if it's not.
* **What is your partner's email address?**: Enter your partner's email address to receive notifications and updates from the platform.
* **Do you agree with our referral terms?**: Select "True" to agree with the referral terms and conditions.
* **Can you provide feedback on our referral terms?**: Optionally, provide feedback on the referral terms if you have any concerns or suggestions.
* **Can you confirm your partner's contact details?**: Check "Yes" to confirm your partner's contact details.
* **Partner Confirm**: Enter a confirmation message for your partner to ensure accurate referral information.
* **Are you sure about our referral terms?**: Check "Option A" or "Option B" to provide feedback on the referral terms.
* **Additional Feedback**: Optionally, provide additional feedback or comments on the referral terms.
