# Property Investment Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Investment Interest Form is used to collect information from potential investors and gauge their interest in investing in properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
2. Share your investment interests, such as the type of property you are interested in (residential, commercial, or agricultural).
3. Select your preferred method of contact (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, you can include them in the "Additional Comments" section.
5. Finally, select the investment options that interest you (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investment Interest Form** (`investment_interest_form`): This is the main form section where you can provide your investment interests.
* **Property Investor Information** (`property_investor_information`): Please provide any relevant information about yourself, such as your investment experience or goals.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`): If you have a specific property in mind, you can provide its details here.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): This is your opportunity to share your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Investment Options** (`investment_options`): Select the options that interest you, such as:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): If you have any questions or additional comments, please include them here.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Email** (`email`): This is your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): This is your phone number.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Select the type of property you are interested in, such as residential, commercial, or agricultural.
* **Date** (`date`): This date field is not currently used, but please feel free to fill it out if you have a specific date in mind.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your investment interests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your submission and follow up with you shortly.
