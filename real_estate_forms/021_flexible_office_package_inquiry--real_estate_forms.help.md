# Flexible Office Package Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit an inquiry about office packages. Please fill out the form to send a message to our team, and we'll get back to you with more information about the available options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
2. Type the name of the company in the "Company" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Type your message in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email (user_email)**: Enter your email address where we can get in touch with you.
* **Company (company)**: Type the name of the company you represent or are inquiring for.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your business or personal phone number.
* **Message (message)**: Type a brief message with your inquiry or question about office packages.
