# Drill Site Visitor Induction Safety Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about visitors to a drill site, ensuring their safety and induction. It is an essential part of site visitation procedures to ensure a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the drill site details section with relevant information about the site.
2. Provide your visitor's information, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Identify the emergency contact details.
4. Select the correct drill site access options.
5. Choose the necessary drill site equipment and safety procedures.
6. Specify the drill site emergency exit and procedure.
7. Select the drill site safety equipment and procedures.
8. Identify the first aider for the site.
9. Specify the drill site induction date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drill Site Details**: Enter the details of the drill site you will be visiting. This includes the name, address, or location of the site.
* **Visitor Information**: Please enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Visitor Name**: Enter your name as the visitor.
* **Visitor Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used as a contact point.
* **Visitor Phone**: Enter your phone number in case of emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information for your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 2**: Enter the contact information for your secondary emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 3**: Enter the contact information for your tertiary emergency contact person.
* **Visitor Induction Date**: Enter the date of your induction.
* **Visitor Induction Time**: Enter the time of your induction.
* **Drill Site Access**: Choose the correct drill site access option that applies to you.
* **Drill Site Equipment**: Select the drill site equipment you will be using.
* **Drill Site Risk Assessment**: Choose the correct drill site risk assessment.
* **Drill Site Emergency Exit**: Choose the correct drill site emergency exit.
* **Drill Site Emergency Procedure**: Describe any specific emergency procedures that apply to you.
* **Drill Site Safety Equipment**: Select the correct drill site safety equipment.
* **Drill Site Safety Procedure**: Choose the correct drill site safety procedure.
* **Drill Site Safety Inspection**: Choose the correct drill site safety inspection.
* **Drill Site Safety Equipment Inspection**: Select the correct drill site safety equipment inspection.
* **Drill Site Safety Training**: Choose the correct drill site safety training.
* **Drill Site Safety Equipment Location**: Select the correct drill site safety equipment location.
* **Drill Site Safety Procedure Training**: Choose the correct drill site safety procedure training.
* **Drill Site Induction Certificate**: Select the correct drill site induction certificate.
* **Drill Site Induction Date/Time**: Enter the date and time of your induction.
* **Drill Site Emergency Exit Path**: Describe the exit path in case of an emergency.
* **Drill Site First Aider**: Enter the name of your first aider.
* **Drill Site First Aider (2)**: Enter the name of your secondary first aider.
* **Drill Site First Aider (3)**: Enter the name of your tertiary first aider.
* **Drill Site Induction Location**: Select the correct drill site induction location.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete this form accurately and truthfully.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ask for help if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
