# Tenant Buyer Road Map - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help property managers and owners understand your needs and preferences as a potential tenant. It will help them to assess your eligibility and provide you with more relevant property options that match your criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the "Personal Information" section.
	* Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
	* Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
2. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
3. Enter the address you are currently at in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the address of the property you are interested in in the "Move-in location" field.
5. Select the date you plan to move in from the "Date of move in" calendar.
6. Select the date you plan to move out from the "Date of move out" calendar.
7. Enter the reason you are moving in the "Reason for moving" field.
8. In the "Renter type" field, select your status as a renter (individual, family, couple, or other).
9. Select the utilities you are responsible for paying for in the "Utilities" field.
10. Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the "Number of bedrooms" and "Number of bed and bath" fields.
11. Enter the square footage of the property you are interested in in the "Sqft" field.
12. Enter the price of the property you are interested in in the "Price" field.
13. Enter the rent you are willing to pay in the "Rent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter the address you are currently at.
* **Move-in location**: Enter the address of the property you are interested in.
* **Date of move in**: Select the date you plan to move in from the calendar.
* **Date of move out**: Select the date you plan to move out from the calendar.
* **Reason for moving**: Enter the reason you are moving.
* **Renter type**: Select your status as a renter (individual, family, couple, or other).
* **Utilities**: Select the utilities you are responsible for paying for (electricity, gas, water, or trash).
* **Number of bedrooms**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the property you are interested in.
* **Number of bed and bath**: Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the property you are interested in.
* **Sqft**: Enter the square footage of the property you are interested in.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the property you are interested in.
* **Rent**: Enter the rent you are willing to pay.
* **Utilities**: Select the utilities you are responsible for paying for (electricity, gas, water, or trash).
* **Move in location**: Enter the address of the property you are interested in.
* **Move out location**: Enter the address you will be moving out to.
* **Number of bed and bath**: Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the property you are interested in.
* **Country**: Enter the country of the property you are interested in (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any attachments or documents to provide, please attach them separately and mention them in the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the property manager or owner directly.
