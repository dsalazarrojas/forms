# tipp_hill_rental_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential tenants who are interested in renting a property at Tipp Hill. By completing this form, you will be able to provide detailed information about your rental application, which will be reviewed by the landlord. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to avoid delays in the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in your name, email, and phone number to identify yourself as a potential tenant.
3. Provide the apartment address you are interested in renting.
4. Select the start and end dates of the rental period.
5. Choose the rent frequency (monthly or yearly).
6. Enter the rent amount and lease duration.
7. Indicate your status as a renter (single person, couple, or family).
8. Select your renter frequency (daily, monthly, or yearly) and duration (short-term or long-term).
9. Add any additional information if necessary.
10. If applicable, provide your landlord's name, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Apartment Address** (`apartment_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the apartment you are interested in renting.
* **Rent Start Date** (`rent_start_date`, `date`, required): Select the start date of the rental period.
* **Rent End Date** (`rent_end_date`, `date`, required): Select the end date of the rental period.
* **Rent Period** (`rent_period`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the frequency of your rent payments (monthly or yearly).
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total rent amount you are willing to pay per rental period.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, `number`, required): Indicate how many months or years you are looking to rent for.
* **Security Deposit** (`security_deposit`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of security deposit you are willing to pay.
* **Renter Status** (`renter_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your status as a renter (single person, couple, or family).
* **Renter Frequency** (`renter_status_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how frequently you rent (daily, monthly, or yearly).
* **Renter Status Duration** (`renter_status_duration`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the duration of your renter status (short-term or long-term).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): If needed, add any additional details about your rental application.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, `text`, optional): If you are working with a landlord, enter their name.
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the landlord's phone number.
* **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the landlord's email address.
