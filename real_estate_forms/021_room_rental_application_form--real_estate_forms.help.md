# Room Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for tenants to apply for a room rental service. It collects general information about the tenant and rental agreement details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Tenant Info** on Page 1.
2. Provide **Rental Info**, including details about the rental property.
3. Enter your **Contact Info** for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Payment Info** for processing rent payments.
5. Check the **Agreement** terms and conditions of the rental.
6. Sign the **Signature** section to confirm your agreement.
7. Upload required **Upload Documents** as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tenant Info** (`tenant_info`, required: false): Fill in your name, address, and other contact information.
  This field is for providing your basic contact details.
- **Rental Info** (`rental_info`, required: false): Enter details about the rental property, including type, location, and duration.
  This field helps us understand the specifics of the rental property you're interested in.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, required: false): Enter your phone number, email, and other contact details for communication purposes.
  This field is for ensuring we can contact you efficiently.
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, required: false): Enter your payment method and details for rent payments.
  This field is for processing rent payments and other transactions.
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, required: false): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the rental service.
  This field is for confirming your understanding and agreement to the rental terms.
- **Signature** (`signature`, required: false): Sign your name to confirm your agreement and consent to the rental terms.
  This field is for your formal signature to complete the rental process.
- **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, required: false): Upload relevant documents as prompted.
  This field is for uploading required documents for the rental application.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- Review the agreement terms carefully before signing.
- Upload all required documents promptly to avoid delays in processing your application.
