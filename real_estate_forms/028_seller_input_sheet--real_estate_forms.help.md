# Seller Input Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seller Input Sheet is designed to collect information from sellers about their products, including contact details, product descriptions, and other relevant data. This form is used in a real estate context to ensure accurate and consistent data for processing and managing product listings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you've filled out all required fields.
2. Fill out the form accurately and consistently, using the same format for all entries.
3. Double-check your entries for errors or inconsistencies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (1)**: Enter the seller's contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Product Info (2)**: Enter a brief description of the product, including its condition and any relevant details.
* **Purchase Date (3)**: Enter the date the product was purchased.
* **Seller Info (4)**: Enter any additional information about the seller, such as notes or comments.
* **Feedback Comments (5)**: Share any feedback or comments about the product or service.
* **Contact Email (6)**: Enter the seller's email address.
* **Contact Phone Number (7)**: Enter the seller's phone number.
* **Contact Fax Number (8)**: Enter the seller's fax number (if applicable).
* **Purchase Price (9)**: Enter the price of the product at the time of purchase.
* **Product Condition (10)**: Select the condition of the product (new, used, refurbished, or unknown).
* **Contact Method (11)**: Check all applicable methods the seller would like to be contacted (phone, email, text-message).
* **Seller Notes (12)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the seller.
* **Product Name (13)**: Enter the name of the product.
* **Purchase Date/Time (14)**: Enter the date and time the product was purchased.
* **Contact Address (15)**: Enter the seller's address.
* **Seller Name (16)**: Enter the seller's name.
* **Contact City (17)**: Enter the city of the seller's contact information.
* **Contact State (18)**: Select the state of the seller's contact information.
* **Contact Zip (19)**: Enter the seller's zip code.
* **Contact Country (20)**: Enter the seller's country of residence.
* **Product Description (21)**: Enter a detailed description of the product.
* **Contact Alternate Phone (22)**: Enter an alternate phone number for the seller (if applicable).
* **Contact Alternate Fax (23)**: Enter an alternate fax number for the seller (if applicable).
* **Purchase Location (24)**: Enter the location where the product was purchased.
* **Seller Signature (25)**: Enter the seller's signature (if required).
