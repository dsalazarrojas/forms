# Handover Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Handover Agreement Form is a document that helps establish a clear understanding between landlords and tenants when handing over property ownership or possession. It outlines the terms and conditions of the handover, including property details, agreements, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand its purpose and the information it requires.
2.  Fill in the relevant fields with accurate and detailed information about the property, landlord, and tenant.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are completed, but do not worry if some fields are not required for your specific handover agreement.
4.  Use the provided hints as guidance to help you fill in the fields correctly.
5.  Once you have filled out the form, review it carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the exact address of the property being handed over.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, office, etc.).
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the property in square meters or feet.
* **Property Features** (`property_features`, text, optional): List any notable features of the property, such as number of bedrooms or bathrooms.
* **Landlord Details** (`landlord_details`, text, optional): Provide information about the landlord, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
* **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, text, optional): Provide information about the tenant, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the handover agreement was made.
* **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the handover agreement was made.
* **Agreement Note** (`agreement_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the handover agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before signing it.
* Store the completed form for future reference.
* Update the form as needed to reflect changes in the handover agreement.
