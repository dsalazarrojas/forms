# real_estate_ethics_certification_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for real estate professionals to obtain certification or confirmation of their commitment to a code of ethics in the real estate industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and complete responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: A field for the applicant to enter their personal details.
	+ **Label:** real_estate_ethics_certification_application_form
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Applicant Info**: A field for the applicant to enter their real estate agent information.
	+ **Label:** real_estate_agent_info
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **License Info**: A field for the applicant to enter their real estate license details.
	+ **Label:** real_estate_license_info
	+ **Type:** number
	+ **Required:** false
* **Certification Info**: A field for the applicant to enter their certification details.
	+ **Label:** real_estate_certification_info
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Agreement**: A field to select the agreement to abide by a specific code of ethics.
	+ **Label:** real_estate_agreement_to_code_of_ethics
	+ **Type:** select_multiple
	+ **Options:** Brokerage, Broker, Real Estate Association
	+ **Required:** false
* **Training Provider**: A field to select whether the applicant's training provider is 'True' or 'False'.
	+ **Label:** real_estate_training_provider
	+ **Type:** select_one
	+ **Options:** True, False
	+ **Required:** false
* **Agreement Date**: A field for the applicant to enter the date of their agreement.
	+ **Label:** real_estate_agreement_date
	+ **Type:** date
	+ **Required:** false

## Tips
* Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and complete responses.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your administrator or supervisor for guidance.
* Ensure you are selecting the correct options for the Agreement and Training Provider fields.
