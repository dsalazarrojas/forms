# Page 1 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from renters or potential renters about their move-in details, which will help the landlord or property manager process the lease agreement and manage the rental property effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your move-in date and time.
3. Provide your lease start and end dates.
4. Choose the lease term (yes or no).
5. Enter your rent amount.
6. Enter your unit number and type (yes or no).
7. Enter the building name.
8. Specify whether you have move-in documents (yes or no).
9. List any parking information.
10. Choose unit amenities (yes or no).
11. Select the rent frequency (yes or no).
12. Choose whether you have utilities (yes or no).
13. Enter any other relevant information about the property.
14. Provide emergency contact details, including name, relationship, phone number, email, and address.
15. Add any notes about the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Move In Date** (date): Enter the date you plan to move in.
* **Move In Time** (time): Enter the time you plan to move in (optional).
* **Lease Start Date** (date): Enter the start date of your lease.
* **Lease End Date** (date): Enter the end date of your lease (if applicable).
* **Lease Term** (select_one): Choose whether this is a yes or no lease.
* **Rent** (number): Enter the amount of rent you will pay.
* **Unit Number** (text): Enter the number of the unit you will be occupying.
* **Unit Type** (select_one): Choose whether this unit has the necessary amenities (yes or no).
* **Building Name** (text): Enter the name of the building where you will be living.
* **Move In Documents** (select_multiple): Choose whether you have any move-in documents (yes or no).
* **Parking Info** (text): Enter any parking details, such as parking permit number or parking location.
* **Unit Amenities** (select_multiple): Choose whether the unit has the necessary amenities (yes or no).
* **Rent Frequency** (select_one): Choose whether you will pay rent on time (yes or no).
* **Utilities** (select_one): Choose whether you will use utilities provided by the landlord (yes or no).
* **Other Info** (text): Enter any other information about the property that is relevant to your lease.
* **Emergency Contact** (text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (text): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (text): Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed for the form to be valid.
