# Shared Housing Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a tenant's living situation for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each question and answer honestly.
2. Fill out the form with the most accurate information available.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required: false): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required: false): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, Required: false): Please enter your current address.
* **Move-in Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, Required: false): Please enter the date you moved into the property.
* **Move-out Date** (`move_out_date`, `date`, Required: false): Please enter the date you moved out of the property.
* **Rental Agreement Start Date** (`rental_agreement_start_date`, `date`, Required: false): Please enter the start date of your rental agreement.
* **Rental Agreement End Date** (`rental_agreement_end_date`, `date`, Required: false): Please enter the end date of your rental agreement.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, `text`, Required: false): Please enter your landlord's name.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, `text`, Required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on the lease.
* **Room Mates Names** (`room_mates_names`, `text`, Required: false): Please list the names of any room mates.
* **Room Mates Relationship** (`room_mates_relationship`, `select_one`, Required: true): Please select the relationship between you and your room mates (Friend, Family, Coworker, Other).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_multiple`, Required: true): Please select the type of property you are renting (Single Family Home, Multi Family Home, Condo, Apartment).
* **Move-in Furniture Status** (`move_in_furniture_status`, `select_one`, Required: true): Please select the status of the furniture when you moved in (None, Some, Partially, Fully).
* **Security Deposit Status** (`security_deposit_status`, `select_one`, Required: true): Please select the status of the security deposit (True, False).
* **Rent Status** (`rent_status`, `select_one`, Required: true): Please select the status of your rent (Paid, Not Paid).
* **Utilities Included** (`utilities_included`, `select_multiple`, Required: true): Please select the utilities that are included in your rent (Water, Electricity, Gas, Cable, Internet, Heat, Trash and Recycling, Parking, None).
* **Utilities Status** (`utilities_status`, `select_one`, Required: true): Please select the status of the utilities (Owner pays, Tenant pays).
* **Rent Price** (`rent_price`, `number`, Required: false): Please enter the rent price.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, `select_multiple`, Required: true): Please select the frequency of your rent (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Lease Length** (`lease_length`, `select_multiple`, Required: true): Please select the length of your lease (Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, Year, None).
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `select_one`, Required: true): Please select the notice period (None, 30 days, 60 days, 90 days).
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, Required: false): Please enter your signature.

Note: The form will not be submitted until all required fields are filled out.
