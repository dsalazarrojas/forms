# Commercial Appraisal Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Commercial Appraisal Review Form is used to review commercial properties to ensure that they are appraised accurately and in compliance with relevant standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required information in the following fields:
    - Reviewer Name
    - Reviewer Credentials
    - Review Date
    - Property Address
    - Property Type
    - Property Size
    - Year Built
    - Appraiser Name
    - Appraisal Date
    - Appraised Value
    - Appraisal Purpose
    - Data Accuracy
    - Comparable Selection
    - Methodology
    - Adjustments
    - Final Value Opinion
    - USPAP Compliance
    - Regulatory Compliance
    - Value Agreement
    - Revised Value Estimate (if necessary)
    - Deficiencies
    - Additional Comments (if necessary)
    - Recommendation
2.  For each field, ensure that you provide accurate and thorough information. If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant documentation or seek guidance from a supervisor or colleague.
3.  Ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Reviewer Name**: Enter your full name as the reviewer.
    - Hint: This field is required to track who is conducting the review.
- **Reviewer Credentials**: Enter your professional certifications or qualifications relevant to the review.
    - Hint: This field helps us track your expertise level in conducting the review.
- **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
    - Hint: This field is required to track the review timeline.
- **Property Address**: Enter the full property address.
    - Hint: This field is required to track the property details.
- **Property Type**: Select the type of commercial property (e.g., Office Building, Retail Space, Warehouse, etc.).
    - Hint: This field helps us track the property type for accurate valuation.
- **Property Size**: Enter the square footage of the property.
    - Hint: This field is required to track the property size for accurate valuation.
- **Year Built**: Enter the construction year of the property.
    - Hint: This field helps us track the property's age and development.
- **Appraiser Name**: Enter the name of the original appraiser.
    - Hint: This field is required to track the appraiser's identity.
- **Appraisal Date**: Enter the date of the original appraisal.
    - Hint: This field is required to track the appraisal timeline.
- **Appraised Value**: Enter the appraised property value.
    - Hint: This field is required for accurate valuation.
- **Appraisal Purpose**: Select the reason for the original appraisal (e.g., Purchase, Refinance, etc.).
    - Hint: This field helps us track the purpose of the original appraisal.
- **Data Accuracy**: Evaluate the accuracy of the property data.
    - Hint: This field helps us track the data accuracy for the review.
- **Comparable Selection**: Evaluate the quality of comparable properties used for the appraisal.
    - Hint: This field helps us track the quality of comparables used.
- **Methodology**: Evaluate the appropriateness of the appraisal methods used.
    - Hint: This field helps us track the appraisal methods used.
- **Adjustments**: Evaluate the quality of adjustments made during the appraisal.
    - Hint: This field helps us track the adjustments made.
- **Final Value Opinion**: Select your opinion on the value conclusion.
    - Hint: This field helps us track your value opinion.
- **USPAP Compliance**: Evaluate whether the appraisal meets USPAP standards.
    - Hint: This field helps us track compliance with USPAP standards.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Evaluate whether the appraisal meets regulatory requirements.
    - Hint: This field helps us track compliance with regulatory requirements.
- **Value Agreement**: Select whether you agree with the appraised value.
    - Hint: This field helps us track your opinion on the value.
- **Revised Value Estimate**: Enter your revised value estimate (if different from the original).
    - Hint: This field is optional and used for your alternative value opinion.
- **Deficiencies**: List any deficiencies found during the review.
    - Hint: This field is optional and used for tracking deficiencies.
- **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations.
    - Hint: This field is optional and used for tracking additional comments.
- **Recommendation**: Select the recommended action for the review.
    - Hint: This field is required for the review outcome.

## Tips

- Ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that your information is accurate and thorough.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult relevant documentation or seek guidance from a supervisor or colleague.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and access to complete the review.
