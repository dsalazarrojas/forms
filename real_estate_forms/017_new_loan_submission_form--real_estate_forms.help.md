# new_loan_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_loan_submission_form is a form designed to facilitate the submission of new loan requests. This form captures essential information from borrowers, including loan details, user information, and bank particulars. It is an essential tool for loan application processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  On the **New Loan** page, enter the relevant details about your loan request.
2.  Click on **Submit** to proceed to the **Loan Information** page.
3.  On the **Loan Information** page, provide further details about your loan request, if any.
4.  Click on **Submit** to move on to the **Loan Details** page.
5.  On the **Loan Details** page, supply more information about your loan request.
6.  Click on **Submit** to continue.
7.  On the **Bank Information** page, enter details about your bank.
8.  On the **User Information** page, fill in your information.
9.  On the **Contact Information** page, enter your contact details.
10. On the **Loan Status** page, review your loan details.
11. Click on **Submit** to review your submission on the **Confirmation Page**.
12. Finally, click on **Submit** to submit your loan request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Loan** (`new_loan_form_page`, text, not required): Enter your loan request details, such as loan amount, interest rate, and loan term.
- **Loan Information** (`loan_info_page`, text, not required): Provide additional loan information that is not captured on the **New Loan** page.
- **Loan Details** (`loan_details_page`, text, not required): Enter more details about your loan request, such as collateral or loan purpose.
- **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, not required): Proceed to the next page or complete your submission.
- **Bank Information** (`bank_info_page`, text, not required): Enter your bank details, including branch and account number.
- **User Information** (`user_info_page`, text, not required): Enter your personal information, such as name and address.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info_page`, text, not required): Enter your contact details, including email and phone number.
- **Loan Status** (`loan_status_page`, text, not required): Review and confirm your loan details before submission.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Finalize your submission.
- **Error Page** (`error_page`, text, not required): In case of an error, this page will appear.
- **Confirmation Page** (`confirmation_page`, text, not required): Review your submission before it is processed.
- **Success Page** (`success_page`, text, not required): This is the final page where you will receive confirmation of your submission.
