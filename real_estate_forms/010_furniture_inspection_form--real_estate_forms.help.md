# Furniture Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Furniture Inspection Form is used by property managers to collect information about the condition and condition of furniture in a building or property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the General Information section.
2. Rate the condition of the furniture in the condition field.
3. Select the type of material that the furniture is made of in the material_type field.
4. Indicate whether or not damage is present on the furniture in the damage_present field.
5. Provide details about the damage in the damage_details field.
6. Indicate whether or not photos were taken of the furniture in the photos field.
7. Add any additional comments or information about the furniture in the additional_comments field.
8. Sign your name in the signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`General Information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, contact number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **condition** (`condition`, number, required: false): Rate the condition of the furniture from Good to Bad.
* **material_type** (`material_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of material that the furniture is made of, such as wood, plastic, etc.
* **damage_present** (`damage_present`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether or not damage is present on the furniture, with options True or False.
* **damage_details** (`damage_details`, text, required: false): Provide details about the damage, such as location and severity.
* **photos** (`photos`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether or not photos were taken of the furniture, with options True or False.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about the furniture.
* **photos** (`photos`, text, required: false): Note: This field is not actually a field in the classical sense, but rather a note for the property manager or inspector to sign their name.

## Tips

* Make sure to rate the condition of the furniture accurately and truthfully.
* Provide clear and detailed information about any damage.
* Take photos of the furniture if possible.
* Use the additional comments field to add any other relevant information about the furniture.
* Sign your name in the signature field to confirm that you have completed the form.
