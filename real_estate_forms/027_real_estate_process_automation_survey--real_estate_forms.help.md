# Real Estate Process Automation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from real estate professionals on their experiences with various tools and challenges they face in the industry. Your input will help us gauge the level of satisfaction with different tools and services, as well as identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question accurately and completely to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select all applicable options when given the opportunity, or provide a brief explanation when prompted.
3. For free-form text fields, provide as much detail as you can, but don't feel overwhelmed; brief answers are also valuable.
4. Don't hesitate to mention any challenges or pain points you're experiencing with any of the tools or services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Questions**: This section is an opportunity to provide any general questions or thoughts you have about your experiences with tools or services in the real estate industry. Be as specific as possible and try to keep your answer concise.
* **Tools**: This section asks about the tools you're currently using. Please select all tools that apply to you, and feel free to add any additional tools not listed that you're familiar with.
* **Challenges**: Similar to the tools section, please select all challenges you're currently facing, or feel free to add any additional challenges not listed that you're experiencing.
* **Satisfaction**: This question asks about your overall satisfaction level with the tools and services you're using. Please select one of the options that best describes your feelings.
* **Feedback**: This is an opportunity for you to provide any additional feedback you may have about your experiences with tools or services. Keep it brief but as specific as possible.
* **Tools Used**: Again, similar to the "Tools" section, please select all tools you're familiar with, or feel free to add any additional ones not listed.
* **Challenges Faced**: This question asks about any challenges you're currently facing with tools or services. Similar to the "Challenges" section, please select all that apply.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Please select one of the options that best describes your level of satisfaction with the tools or services.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Similar to the previous question, please select one of the options that best describes your overall satisfaction with the tools or services.
* **Recommendations**: This is an opportunity for you to provide any recommendations or suggestions you have about areas where the tools or services can be improved.
* **Comments**: This section is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experiences with tools or services. Please keep it as specific as possible.
