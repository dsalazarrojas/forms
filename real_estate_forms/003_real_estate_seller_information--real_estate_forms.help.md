# real_estate_seller_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from real estate sellers, including their general contact information, property details, and financial information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start with the General Information page.
2. Fill in the text field with the required information.
3. Proceed to the Contact Information page.
4. Fill in the text field with the required information.
5. Continue to the Property Details page.
6. Fill in the text field with the required information.
7. Move to the Financial Information page.
8. Fill in the text field with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required/optional): This page is intended to collect the general information about the seller.
  - **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): This page is meant to collect the contact information of the seller.
  - **Property Details** (`Property Details`, `text`, required/optional): This page is designed to collect the details about the property being sold.
  - **Financial Information** (`Financial Information`, `text`, required/optional): This page is intended to collect the financial information about the property or transaction.

## Tips
- Please fill in the information accurately and completely.
- If a page does not require a specific field, you can skip it or leave it blank.
- If you're an agent/representative, please ensure you have the seller's permission before completing this form.
