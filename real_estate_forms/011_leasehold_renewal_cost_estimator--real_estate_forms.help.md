# Leasehold Renewal Cost Estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help property owners estimate the cost of renewing their leasehold. It collects information about the property and its owner's details to provide a rough estimate of the costs involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information about your property and its owner's details.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately to provide the best possible estimate.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Location**: Enter the address of the property you are renewing the leasehold for.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial) to provide a more accurate estimate.
* **Property Status**: Select the current status of the property (Owned, Leased, or Other) to provide a more accurate estimate.
* **Lease Period**: Enter the length of the lease.
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date**: Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Property Value**: Enter the current value of the property.
* **Renovation Status**: Select the renovation status of the property (Under Construction, Renovated, or Not Renovated).
* **Property Owner's Name**: Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Property Owner's Contact Number**: Enter the property owner's contact number.
* **Property Owner's Email**: Enter the property owner's email address.
* **Property Owner's Address**: Enter the address of the property owner.
* **Property Owner's Other Name**: Enter any other name of the property owner (if applicable).
* **Property Owner's Other Contact Number**: Enter the other contact number of the property owner (if applicable).
* **Property Owner's Other Email**: Enter the other email address of the property owner (if applicable).
* **Property Owner's Other Address**: Enter the other address of the property owner (if applicable).
* **Property Details**: Enter any additional information about the property.
* **Renewal Request**: Select whether you are requesting to renew the leasehold.
* **Follow Up Details**: Enter any follow-up instructions or details for the renewal process.
* **Follow Up Instructions**: Enter any additional instructions for follow-up.
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the follow-up date.
* **Follow Up Time**: Enter the follow-up time.

Note: The follow-up fields are not mandatory and can be left blank if not applicable.
