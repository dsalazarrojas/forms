# move_in_cost_payment_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "move_in_cost_payment_agreement_form" is used to collect payment agreement information from a renter or tenant when they move into a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand the required information.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully.
3. If a field is marked as optional, you can choose to provide additional information if you wish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Move In Cost Payment Agreement
* **Move In Cost Payment Agreement**: This is the title of the form, and it is a summary of the form's purpose.
### Page 2: Select One
* **Select One**: This field allows you to indicate whether you agree to pay for the costs associated with your move-in. Please select 'Yes' if you agree, and 'No' if you do not.
### Page 3: Select Multiple
* **Select Multiple**: This field allows you to indicate whether you agree to pay for multiple costs associated with your move-in. Please select all applicable options.
### Page 4: Date
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to move in or the date you have actually moved in. This field is used to track the move-in date.
### Page 5: Time
* **Time**: If you would like to provide a specific time of move-in, you can enter it here. This field is optional and only necessary if you have a specific move-in time.
### Page 6: Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you regarding any important information or updates related to your rental property.
### Page 7: Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you regarding any urgent matters or updates related to your rental property.
### Page 8: Note
* **Note**: If you have any additional information or comments, you can enter them here. This field is optional and only for your reference.
