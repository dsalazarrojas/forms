# rental_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rental application form is used to collect information from individuals interested in renting a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Choose the type of real estate you are interested in (House, Apartment, or Townhouse) in the "Type of Real Estate" fields.
5. Leave any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): This is the field for your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This is the field for your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): This is the field for your date of birth.
* **Type of Real Estate** (`select_type_of_real_estate`, `select_multiple`, required): This is the field where you can select the type of real estate you are interested in (House, Apartment, or Townhouse).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required): This is the field for any additional comments or notes.
