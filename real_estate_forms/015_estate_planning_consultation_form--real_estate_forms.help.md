# Estate Planning Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Consultation Form is designed to help you determine your real estate planning needs and preferences. Please fill out this form to provide us with information about your estate forms, lawyer involvement, estate planning details, consultation preferences, and real estate planning services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your answers for each field, and fill out any additional details where necessary. You can choose multiple options for the fields where allowed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Estate Forms** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false): Check all the estate forms you have, such as wills, trusts, powers of attorney, and others.
* **Lawyer** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false): Do you have a lawyer or would you like to have one involved in your estate planning process?
* **Estate Planning Details** (type: note, required: false): Provide any additional estate planning details that you think we should know about (e.g., specific assets, beneficiaries, or wishes).
* **Consultation Details** (type: date, required: false): Choose a date and time for a consultation.
* **Real Estate Planning Services** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false): Check all the real estate planning services you're interested in (e.g., buying or selling property, property management, etc.).
