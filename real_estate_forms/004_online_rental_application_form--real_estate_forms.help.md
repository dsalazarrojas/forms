# Online Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants seeking rental properties. It covers contact, rental history, personal, employment, rental preferences, emergency contact, and rental agreement details.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, start by providing your contact information, which includes your name, email, and phone number.
- After filling out the contact information, answer the questions under the "Rental History", "Personal Details", "Employment Details", "Rental Preferences", "Emergency Contact", and "Rental Agreement" sections.
- For each of these sections, provide as much detail as required or as you feel is necessary.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) or indicated as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact`, `text`, required): Provide your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, `text`, required): Describe your rental history, including previous rental properties and the reasons for moving or leaving.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, required): Share personal details, such as address, employment status, and any other relevant information.
* **Employment Details** (`employment`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide employment details, including your job title, employer, and any other relevant work information.
* **Rental Preferences** (`rental_preferences`, `text`, optional): Specify your rental preferences, such as desired location, lease term, and other specific requirements.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Identify an emergency contact person, including their name and contact information.
* **Rental Agreement** (`rental_agreement`, `text`, optional): Outline your agreement terms, such as payment method, lease start date, and end date.
* **Rental Agreement 2** (`rental_agreement_2`, `text`, optional): (same as "Rental Agreement") This field is identical in purpose to "Rental Agreement".
* ... (The same goes for the subsequent "Rental Agreement 3", "Rental Agreement 4", "Rental Agreement 5", "Rental Agreement 6")

# Tips
- Be truthful and honest in your submissions, as the information collected will be used to evaluate your rental application.
- If unsure about any information, please ask for clarification.
- If you have any issues or questions, do not hesitate to contact the form administrator.
