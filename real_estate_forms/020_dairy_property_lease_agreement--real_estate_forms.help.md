# Dairy Property Lease Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the creation of a dairy property lease agreement between a landowner and a farmer. The form captures essential information to outline the terms and conditions of the lease, ensuring a clear understanding between the parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **Landowner Info** page, providing information about the landowner.
2. Move on to the **Farmer Info** page, entering details about the farmer.
3. The **Lease Terms** page is mandatory and requires a clear statement of the lease terms.
4. On the **Terms and Conditions** page, enter any additional terms and conditions of the lease.
5. The **Rent** page requires a numeric value for the agreed-upon rent.
6. Finally, provide details about the property on the **Property Details** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landowner Info** (`landowner_info`, text, optional): Enter the landowner's name and contact information.
* **Farmer Info** (`farmer_info`, text, optional): Enter the farmer's name and contact information.
* **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`, text, mandatory): Describe the specific terms and conditions of the lease.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Add any additional terms and conditions of the lease.
* **Rent** (`rent`, number, mandatory): Enter the agreed-upon rent amount.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the property being leased, such as location and size.

## Tips
- Ensure that all mandatory fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all terms and conditions are clearly outlined.
- Save the completed form for future reference and record-keeping purposes.
