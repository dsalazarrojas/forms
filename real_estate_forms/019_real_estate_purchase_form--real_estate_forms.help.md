# Real Estate Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Real Estate Purchase Form, a comprehensive form designed to collect information from the submitter, buyer, and real estate agent during a real estate transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your contact information on the "Contact Information" section (Field 2).
2. If applicable, upload a copy of the contract of sale (Field 3).
3. Enter details about the property being purchased (Field 6).
4. Read and agree to the cost disclosure terms (Field 7).
5. Sign the cost disclosure statement (Field 15).
6. Sign the real estate agreement (Field 11).
7. Sign the real estate agent agreement (Field 19).
8. Sign the submitter information (Field 20).
9. Sign the cost disclosure statement (Field 21).
10. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the real estate agreement (Field 23).
11. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the cost disclosure statement (Field 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Field 2)** (`name`, `email`, `phone`, `street`, `city`, `state`, `zip`, `phone`, `email`, `agent_name`, `agent_phone`, `agent_email`): Enter your contact details to ensure we can get in touch with you for any updates or queries. This information will be kept confidential.
* **Contract of Sale (Field 3)** (`text`): If you have a contract of sale, please upload it here to confirm the terms of the purchase.
* **Property Details (Field 6)** (`address`, `type`, `price`, `status`, `sqft`, `year_built`, `lot_size`, `bedrooms`, `bathrooms`, `floors`, `garages`, `stories`): Enter details about the property being purchased, such as its address, type, price, and other relevant details.
* **Cost Disclosure (Field 7)** (`text`): Read and agree to the cost disclosure terms carefully before proceeding.
* **Sign Cost Disclosure Statement (Field 15)** (`signature`): Sign the cost disclosure statement to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Real Estate Agent (Field 18)** (`name`, `phone`, `email`): Enter contact information for the real estate agent involved in the purchase.
* **Real Estate Agent Agreement (Field 19)** (`text`): Read and agree to the real estate agent agreement carefully before proceeding.
* **Sign Real Estate Agent Agreement (Field 19)** (`signature`): Sign the real estate agent agreement to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Sign Submitter Information (Field 20)** (`signature`): Sign the submitter information to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Sign Cost Disclosure Statement (Field 21)** (`signature`): Sign the cost disclosure statement to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Real Estate Agreement Agreed (Field 23)** (`text`): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the real estate agreement.
* **Cost Disclosure Statement Agreed (Field 23)** (`text`): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the cost disclosure statement.
* **Sign Real Estate Agent Agreement Signed (Field 24)** (`signature`): Sign the real estate agent agreement to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Sign Cost Disclosure Agreement Signed (Field 25)** (`signature`): Sign the cost disclosure agreement to confirm your understanding and agreement.

Please note that this is not a substitute for legal advice. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the real estate agent or the submitter for guidance.
