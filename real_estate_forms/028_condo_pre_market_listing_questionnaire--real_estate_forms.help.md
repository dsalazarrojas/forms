# Condo Pre Market Listing Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Condo Pre Market Listing Questionnaire is designed to gather essential information about a condo unit before it is put on the market for sale. This form helps provide a clear and concise overview of the condo's features, price, and marketing strategy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the property owner or listing agent's name in the "Owner name" field.
2. Fill in the full street address of the property in the "Property address" field.
3. For properties with multiple bedrooms and bathrooms, enter the total number of each in the "Bedrooms and bathrooms" field (e.g., 2 bed 2 bath).
4. If applicable, enter the approximate square footage of the unit in the "Unit size square feet" field.
5. Enter the year the property was constructed in the "Year built" field.
6. If there have been any recent upgrades or renovations, provide a brief description in the "Recent upgrades or renovations" field.
7. Enter the monthly common charges (HOA fees) in the "HOA fees monthly" field.
8. Set your target asking price for the property in the "Listing price expectation" field.
9. Highlight any unique selling points or features of the property in the "Unique property features" field.
10. Indicate your preferred marketing channels or open house options in the "Marketing preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner name** (Owner name, text, required): Enter the property owner or listing agent's name to associate with this property listing.
* **Property address** (Property address, text, required): Fill in the full street address of the property.
* **Unit size square feet** (Unit size square feet, number, optional): Enter the approximate square footage of the unit.
* **Bedrooms and bathrooms** (Bedrooms and bathrooms, text, required): Enter the total number of bedrooms and bathrooms (e.g., 2 bed 2 bath).
* **Year built** (Year built, number, optional): Enter the year the property was constructed.
* **Recent upgrades or renovations** (Recent upgrades or renovations, text, optional): Provide a brief description of any recent improvements or renovations made to the property.
* **HOA fees monthly** (HOA fees monthly, number, optional): Enter the monthly common charges (HOA fees).
* **Listing price expectation** (Listing price expectation, number, required): Set your target asking price for the property.
* **Unique property features** (Unique property features, text, optional): Highlight any unique selling points or features that set this property apart.
* **Marketing preferences** (Marketing preferences, text, optional): Indicate your preferred marketing channels or open house options.
