# real-estate-agent-payment-portal - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information from real estate agents for transactions related to property sales or purchases.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, **required**): Enter your goals for this transaction.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select a payment method for this transaction. Options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Property** (`property_info`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the property related to this transaction. Options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the payment amount for this transaction.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date of this transaction.
* **Transaction Summary** (`transaction_summary`, `text`, **optional**): Provide a brief summary of the transaction.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional payment information.

Note: The fields marked as **required** must be filled in for the form to be submitted successfully.
