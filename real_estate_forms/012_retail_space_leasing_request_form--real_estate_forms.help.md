# Retail Space Leasing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential tenants or property owners about retail space leasing requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your name of the space you are interested in leasing.
2. Enter the location of the space.
3. Specify the size of the space.
4. Indicate the type of space you are looking for (e.g. Retail, Warehouse, Office, etc.).
5. Enter your estimated budget for the space.
6. Provide your name and contact information.
7. If there are any specific requirements for the space, describe them in the "Space Requirements" field.
8. Select the budget range for the space from the options provided.
9. Choose the availability of the space from the options provided.
10. Enter the date and time you are submitting the leasing request.
11. Select the status of the submission from the options provided.
12. Add any comments about the submission if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Space Name** (Text): Name of the space
* **Location** (Text): Location of the space
* **Space Size** (Text): Size of the space
* **Space Type** (Text): Type of space (Retail, Warehouse, Office, etc.)
* **Space Budget** (Number): Estimated budget for the space
* **Tenant Name** (Text): Name of the tenant
* **Tenant Contact** (Email): Contact information of the tenant
* **Space Requirements** (Text): Additional requirements for the space
* **Budget Range** (Select One): Estimated budget range for the space
	+ 0-100
	+ 100-500
	+ 500-1000
	+ 1000-5000
* **Space Availability** (Select Multiple): Availability of the space 
	+ Available
	+ Available after 1 week
	+ Available after 2 weeks
	+ Available after 3 weeks
* **Submission Date** (Date): Date of submission of the leasing request
* **Submission Time** (Time): Time of submission of the leasing request
* **Submission Status** (Select One): Status of the submission 
	+ New Submission
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
	+ Archived
* **Submission Comments** (Note): Comments about the submission if necessary

## Tips
- Please complete each field accurately to ensure a smooth leasing process.
- Some fields are optional, and you can skip them if not required.
- If you have any questions or need help, please reach out to your real estate agent or property manager.
