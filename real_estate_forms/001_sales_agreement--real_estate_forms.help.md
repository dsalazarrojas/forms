# Sales Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms and details of a sales agreement, ensuring both the buyer and seller are on the same page. It helps to clarify payment terms and acknowledgments, promoting transparency and a successful sales process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the sales agreement in the **Sales Agreement Details** field.
2. Enter the buyer's information in the **Buyer Information** field.
3. Enter the seller's information in the **Seller Information** field.
4. Specify the payment terms in the **Payment Terms** field.
5. Confirm if the buyer has acknowledged the terms in the **Buyer Acknowledgement** field.
6. Confirm if the seller has acknowledged the terms in the **Seller Acknowledgement** field.
7. Add the date and time of the sales agreement in the **Date** and **Time** fields, respectively.
8. Enter any additional contact information (e.g., email and phone number) in the **Email** and **Phone** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Agreement Details**: A text field where you can add any additional details about the sales agreement.
* **Buyer Information**: A text field where you can enter the buyer's contact information.
* **Seller Information**: A text field where you can enter the seller's contact information.
* **Sales Agreement Terms**: A text field where you can add the terms of the sales agreement.
* **Payment Terms**: A dropdown menu with options for selecting the payment terms. Choose from:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Buyer Acknowledgement**: A dropdown menu where you can select whether the buyer has acknowledged the terms:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Seller Acknowledgement**: A dropdown menu where you can select whether the seller has acknowledged the terms:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Date**: A date field where you can select the date of the sales agreement.
* **Time**: A time field where you can select the time of the sales agreement.
* **Email**: An email field where you can enter the buyer's or seller's email address.
* **Phone**: A text field where you can enter the buyer's or seller's phone number.
