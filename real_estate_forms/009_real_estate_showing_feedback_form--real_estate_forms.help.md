# Real Estate Showing Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Showing Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback and information from attendees after a showing, allowing property agents to collect valuable insights and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Property Address** field with the address of the property that was shown.
2. Select the **Showing Date** when the showing took place.
3. Enter the **Agent Name** who conducted the showing.
4. Provide the **Attendee Name** who was present during the showing.
5. Enter your **Feedback** about the showing, including any comments or concerns you may have.
6. If you have any **Suggestions** for improvement, please share them in this field.
7. Add your **Contact Info** so we can get back to you with any further questions or updates.
8. Choose the attendees who were present during the showing in the **Attendees** field.
9. Finally, select the **Showing Time** when the showing took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Address** (`text`, `required`): Enter the address of the property that was shown.
- **Showing Date** (`date`, `required`): Select the date when the showing took place.
- **Agent Name** (`text`, `required`): Enter the name of the agent who conducted the showing.
- **Attendee Name** (`text`, `required`): Provide the name of the attendee who was present during the showing.
- **Feedback** (`text`, `required`): Enter your feedback about the showing, including any comments or concerns you may have.
- **Suggestions** (`text`, `optional`): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them in this field.
- **Contact Info** (`text`, `optional`): Add your contact info so we can get back to you with any further questions or updates.
- **Attendees** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Choose the attendees who were present during the showing.
- **Showing Time** (`time`, `required`): Select the time when the showing took place.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure we can process your feedback effectively.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to add them in the **Feedback** field.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them in the **Suggestions** field.
* Make sure to select the correct **Attendees** who were present during the showing.
* Double-check the **Showing Date** and **Showing Time** fields for accuracy.
