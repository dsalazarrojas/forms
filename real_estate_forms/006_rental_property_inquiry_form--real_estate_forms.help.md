# Rental Property Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Property Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from renters interested in renting a rental property. This form will help us understand their preferences and needs, and provide them with relevant information and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2.  Enter a valid email address in the Email field. This will allow us to contact you with more information and updates.
3.  Enter your phone number in the Phone field. This will help us contact you for further discussion.
4.  Indicate your location preference in the Location field (e.g. City, Suburbs, Rural, Downtown, Coastal).
5.  Select the type of property you are interested in (e.g. Studio, One-bedroom, Two-bedroom, Three-bedroom, Four-bedroom).
6.  Select any additional amenities you would like in the Amenities field (e.g. Pool, Gym, Parking, Wi-Fi, Balcony).
7.  If desired, specify a preferred move-in date in the Move In Date field.
8.  Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
9.  If desired, provide additional information in the Agent Information field.
10.  If desired, provide your agent's phone number in the Agent Phone field.
11.  If desired, provide your agent's email in the Agent Email field.
12.  If desired, provide your agent's address in the Agent Address field.
13.  If desired, provide your agent's license number in the Agent License Number field.
14.  If desired, provide your agent's name in the Agent Name field.
15.  Finally, click the submit button to send the form.
