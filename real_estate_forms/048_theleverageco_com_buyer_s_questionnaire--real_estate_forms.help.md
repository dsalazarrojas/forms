# TheLeverageCo.com | Buyer's Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The TheLeverageCo.com | Buyer's Questionnaire form is designed to collect demographic and preference information from potential homebuyers. This information will be used internally by TheLeverageCo.com to better understand their target audience and improve their real estate services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select one or more options from the dropdown menu to answer "What do you want to buy?" and "What is your budget?".
6. Choose your preferred location from the dropdown menu.
7. Select your preferred area from the dropdown menu.
8. Optionally, enter your preferred city in the "preferred_city" field.
9. If your location is not listed in the dropdown menu, you can enter it in the "other_location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name (text)**: Enter your first name here. Please keep it short and concise.
* **last name (text)**: Enter your last name here.
* **email (email)**: Enter your valid email address here.
* **phone (text)**: Enter your phone number here.
* **what do you want to buy (select one)**: Choose one or more from the options:
	+ House
	+ Apartment
	+ Townhouse
	+ Other
* **what is your budget (select multiple)**: Choose one or more from the options:
	+ 100k
	+ 200k
	+ 300k
	+ 400k
	+ 500k
	+ Other
* **location (text)**: Enter your preferred location if it's not listed in the dropdown menu.
* **preferred area (select one)**: Choose one from the options:
	+ Beach
	+ Mountain
	+ City
	+ Rural
* **preferred city (text)**: Enter your preferred city here.
* **other location (text)**: If your location is not listed in the dropdown menu, enter it here.

## Tips

* Please keep your answers short and concise.
* If you have any other comments or information, feel free to add it in the "other_location" field.
* This form is for internal use only, and the information collected will not be shared with third parties.
