# Real Estate Broker Price Opinion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for real estate agents or brokers to provide their professional opinion on the price of a property. It's likely used to gather their assessments and feedback on the current market value of a specific property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information:
	* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name as a real estate agent or broker.
	* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address associated with your real estate license or office.
2. Provide your price opinion:
	* **Real Estate Broker Price Opinion Form** (`real_estate_broker_price_opinion_form`): Enter your assessment of the property's market value. This field is not required, but it's the main reason for filling out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Real Estate Broker Price Opinion Form** (`real_estate_broker_price_opinion_form`): This field is used to enter your professional opinion on the property's price. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name as a real estate agent or broker. This field is optional but helps with internal tracking and reporting.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address associated with your real estate license or office. This field is optional but ensures accurate communication and record-keeping.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your contact information correctly to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* If you're unsure about the property's market value, you can research and use market data to inform your opinion.
* Please be objective and professional in your price assessment, considering factors like market trends and recent sales data.
