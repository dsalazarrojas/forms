# Home Buyer Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from home buyers about their experience, preferences, and expectations when searching for a home. Your responses will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best match your experiences and preferences.
3. Enter your responses in the text fields provided.
4. Scroll down to the end of the form and submit your completed survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are you looking for** (`what_are_you_looking_for`): This question helps us understand what you were looking for when searching for a home. Choose the option that best matches your needs and preferences.
	+ Option 1: A specific type of property (e.g. single-family home, condo, townhouse)
	+ Option 2: A specific location (e.g. neighborhood, city, region)
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify in the text field)
* **What were your previous goals** (`what_were_your_previous_goals`): This question helps us understand what you were looking for in a home previously. Please enter your previous goals in the text field.
* **What was your budget** (`what_was_your_budget`): This question helps us understand your budget constraints. Enter a numerical value in the text field.
* **When did you start searching** (`when_did_you_start_searching`): This question helps us understand when you started searching for a home. Choose the time period that best matches your experience.
	+ Option 1: Less than 1 month ago
	+ Option 2: 1-3 months ago
	+ Option 3: 3-6 months ago
	+ Option 4: More than 6 months ago
* **What brought you to the area** (`what_brought_you_to_the_area`): This question helps us understand what motivated you to search for a home in this area. Please enter your answer in the text field.
* **Why did you choose this home** (`why_did_you_choose_this_home`): This question helps us understand why you chose this specific home. Please enter your reasons in the text field.
* **What is your purpose** (`what_is_your_purpose`): This question helps us understand your purpose for buying a home. Choose the options that best match your intentions.
	+ Option 1: To live in the home
	+ Option 2: To invest in the property
	+ Option 3: To rent out the property
* **What is your current location** (`what_is_your_current_location`): This question helps us understand where you currently live. Choose the options that best match your current location.
	+ Option 1: Urban area
	+ Option 2: Suburban area
	+ Option 3: Rural area
* **What is your previous location** (`what_is_your_previous_location`): This question helps us understand where you previously lived. Please enter your previous location in the text field.
* **What is your favorite part of the home** (`what_is_your_favorite_part_of_the_home`): This question helps us understand what you like most about the home. Please enter your favorite part in the text field.
* **What is your favorite room** (`what_is_your_favorite_room`): This question helps us understand what room you like most about the home. Please enter your favorite room in the text field.
* **How do you feel about the home** (`how_do_you_feel_about_the_home`): This question helps us understand your emotions towards the home. Choose the options that best match your feelings.
	+ Option 1: I love it
	+ Option 2: I like it
	+ Option 3: It's okay
* **What did you like most** (`what_did_you_like_most`): This question helps us understand what you liked most about the home. Choose the options that best match your experiences.
	+ Option 1: Location
	+ Option 2: Amenities
	+ Option 3: Layout
* **What do you like least** (`what_do_you_like_least`): This question helps us understand what you liked least about the home. Choose the options that best match your experiences.
	+ Option 1: Location
	+ Option 2: Amenities
	+ Option 3: Layout
* **Why did you buy** (`why_did_you_buy`): This question helps us understand why you purchased this home. Please enter your reasons in the text field.
* **Why did you choose us** (`why_did_you_choose_us`): This question helps us understand why you chose our company. Choose the options that best match your experiences.
	+ Option 1: Better communication
	+ Option 2: Faster response time
	+ Option 3: Better pricing
* **What is the most difficult part** (`what_is_the_most_difficult_part`): This question helps us understand what was the most challenging part of the home buying process. Please enter your reasons in the text field.
* **What could we improve** (`what_could_we_improve`): This question helps us understand how we can improve our services. Please enter your suggestions in the text field.
* **What is your suggestion** (`what_is_your_suggestion`): This question helps us understand your suggestions for improvement. Please enter your suggestions in the text field.
* **What do you want to say** (`what_do_you_want_to_say`): This question helps us understand any additional comments or feedback you may have. Please enter your thoughts in the text field.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): This question helps us understand any additional comments or feedback you may have. Please enter your thoughts in the text field.
* **Thank you for your input** (`thank_you_for_your_input`): Please let us know if you have any additional comments or feedback.
* **Contact us** (`contact_us`): This question helps us understand how to contact you with any follow-up questions or concerns. Please enter your contact information in the text field.
