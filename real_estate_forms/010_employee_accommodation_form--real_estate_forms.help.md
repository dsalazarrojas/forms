# Employee Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Accommodation Form is used to collect information from employees regarding their accommodation needs and requirements. This form helps HR or personnel departments to assess and manage employee requests for company-provided accommodation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your work email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your work phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your work address in the "Address" field.
5. Describe your accommodation needs and requirements in the "Accommodation Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (id 2, text, required): Enter your full name as you want it to be displayed on any communication related to your accommodation request.
* **Email** (id 3, email, required): Enter your work email address, which will be used for communication about your accommodation request.
* **Phone** (id 4, text, required): Enter your work phone number, which will be used for communication about your accommodation request.
* **Address** (id 5, text, required): Enter your work address, including the building number and floor if applicable.
* **Accommodation Details** (id 6, text, required): Describe your specific accommodation needs and requirements, such as preferred location, start date, and any other relevant details. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email and phone numbers are accurate and up-to-date.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your accommodation needs and requirements.
* If you have any supporting documents, such as a copy of your ID or a lease agreement, please attach them to your submission.
