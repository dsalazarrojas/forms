# Uniform Borrower Assistance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Borrower Assistance Form is a tool designed to collect information about your current financial situation to provide guidance and support from our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question thoroughly and accurately. If you need help or clarification, do not hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current mortgage status?** (`mortgage_status`, `text`, required): Please indicate your current mortgage status, such as "current" or "defaulted".
* **What is your monthly payment amount?** (`monthly_payment`, `number`, required): Enter your monthly payment amount.
* **Are you experiencing financial difficulty?** (`financial_difficulty`, `select_one`, required): Answer 'True' if you are currently experiencing financial difficulty.
* **If yes, what is the reason for the financial difficulty?** (`financial_difficulty_reason`, `text`, optional): Provide an explanation for your financial difficulty.
* **What is your monthly income?** (`income_1`, `text`, optional): Enter your monthly income.
* **What is your spouse's monthly income? (if applicable)** (`income_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your spouse's monthly income, if applicable.
* **What is your employment status?** (`employment_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from the options: "Employed", "Self-employed", "Unemployed", "Retired", "Disabled".
* **What is your source of income?** (`income_source_1`, `select_one`, optional): Select from the options: "Salary", "Self-employment", "Alimony", "Pension", "Other".
* **What is your spouse's source of income?** (`income_source_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select from the options: "Salary", "Self-employment", "Alimony", "Pension", "Other".
* **Do you have any assets?** (`assets`, `select_one`, optional): Answer 'True' if you have assets.
* **What is your asset description 1?** (`asset_1`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of your first asset.
* **What is the value of your asset 1?** (`asset_1_value`, `number`, optional): Enter the value of your first asset.
* **What is your asset description 2?** (`asset_2`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of your second asset.
* **What is the value of your asset 2?** (`asset_2_value`, `number`, optional): Enter the value of your second asset.
* **Do you have any liabilities?** (`liabilities`, `select_one`, required): Answer 'True' if you have liabilities.
* **What is your liability description 1?** (`liability_1`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of your first liability.
* **Have you spoken with any loan providers?** (`loan_provider`, `select_one`, optional): Answer 'True' if you have spoken with a loan provider.
* **If yes, what is the name of the loan provider?** (`loan_provider_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the loan provider.
* **If yes, what is the contact person's name?** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **If yes, what is the contact number?** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact number.
* **What is your preferred method of communication?** (`communication_method`, `select_one`, required): Select from the options: "Phone", "Email", "Mail".
* **What is your preferred contact time?** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required): Enter your preferred contact time.
* **Is there any additional information you would like to share?** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share with our organization.
* **If you would like to be contacted by our organization, what is your email address?** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted.
* **Please print your signature below** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date your form to indicate your completion and submission of the information provided.
