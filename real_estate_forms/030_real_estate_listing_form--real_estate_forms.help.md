# real_estate_listing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a real estate listing, which can be used for various business purposes such as marketing, sales, or property management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of listing you are creating:
	*   New listing
	*   Renovation
	*   Realty
2. Choose the type of property:
	*   Residential
	*   Commercial
	*   Agricultural
3. Enter the property value.
4. Enter the street address of the property.
5. Enter the city of the property.
6. Choose the state where the property is located:
	*   California
	*   New York
	*   Florida
7. Enter the zip code of the property.
8. Enter the name of the seller.
9. Enter the email address of the seller.
10. Enter the phone number of the seller.
11. (Optional) Enter the name of the company the seller represents.
12. Describe the property design and build details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Listing Type (Listing type)**: This field determines the type of listing you are creating.
*   **Property Type (Property type)**: This field determines the type of property you are dealing with.
*   **Property Value (Property value)**: Enter the value of the property.
*   **Street Address (Street address)**: Enter the street address of the property.
*   **City (City)**: Enter the city where the property is located.
*   **State (State)**: Choose the state where the property is located.
*   **Zip Code (Zip code)**: Enter the zip code of the property.
*   **Seller Name (Name)**: Enter the name of the seller.
*   **Seller Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the seller.
*   **Seller Phone (Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the seller.
*   **Seller Company (Company)**: (Optional) Enter the name of the company the seller represents.
*   **Property Design and Build (Property design and build)**: Describe the design and build details of the property.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
