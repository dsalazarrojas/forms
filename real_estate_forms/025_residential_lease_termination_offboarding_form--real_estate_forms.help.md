# Residential Lease Termination Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the termination of a residential lease, including details such as move-out date, reason for termination, and outstanding balances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the tenant information section, which includes the tenant's name and contact information.
2. Provide the move-in date and move-out date of the tenant.
3. Specify the reason for the move-out.
4. Enter the notice period, which is the time frame between the move-out date and the actual termination date.
5. Enter the notice period end date, which is the date by which the tenant must vacate the premises.
6. Enter the rent due date, which is the date by which the rent should be paid.
7. Enter the outstanding rent, which is the remaining balance of rent owed by the tenant.
8. Specify the status of the outstanding rent, which can be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.
9. Enter the outstanding utilities balance, which includes any utility bills that are still owed by the tenant.
10. Specify the status of the outstanding utilities, which can be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.
11. Enter the outstanding repossession fees, which are any additional fees charged for the repossession of the property.
12. Enter the move-out inspection details, which can include Landlord, Tenant, or Third Party as applicable.
13. Enter any comments regarding the move-out inspection.
14. Specify the status of the outstanding rent amount, which can be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.
15. Enter any comments regarding the outstanding rent amount.
16. Specify the status of the outstanding utilities amount, which can be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information (Tenant Information)** (`tenant_info`, text, optional): Enter the tenant's name and contact information to whom the lease is addressed.
* **Lease Terms (Lease Terms)** (`lease_terms`, text, optional): A brief summary of the lease terms, including any relevant details about the termination of the lease.
* **Move In Date (Move In Date)** (`move_in_date`, date, optional): The date on which the tenant moved in.
* **Move Out Date (Move Out Date)** (`move_out_date`, date, optional): The date on which the tenant is moving out.
* **Reason for Move Out (Reason for Move Out)** (`reason_for_move_out`, text, optional): A brief explanation of why the tenant is terminating the lease.
* **Notice Period (Notice Period)** (`notice_period`, number, optional): The number of days between the move-out date and the actual termination date.
* **Notice Period End Date (Notice Period End Date)** (`notice_period_end_date`, date, optional): The date by which the tenant must vacate the premises.
* **Rent Due Date (Rent Due Date)** (`rent_due_date`, date, optional): The date by which the rent should be paid.
* **Outstanding Rent (Outstanding Rent)** (`outstanding_rent`, number, optional): The remaining balance of rent owed by the tenant.
* **Outstanding Rent Amount (Outstanding Rent Amount)** (`outstanding_rent_amount`, number, optional): The outstanding rent amount.
* **Outstanding Rent Status (Outstanding Rent Status)** (`outstanding_rent_status`, select_one, optional): The status of the outstanding rent, which can be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.
* **Move Out Inspection (Move Out Inspection)** (`move_out_inspection`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply regarding the move-out inspection details.
* **Move Out Inspection Comments (Move Out Inspection Comments)** (`move_out_inspection_comments`, text, optional): Any comments regarding the move-out inspection.
* **Outstanding Utilities Balance (Outstanding Utilities Balance)** (`outstanding_utilities_balance`, number, optional): The outstanding balance of utility bills owed by the tenant.
* **Outstanding Utilities Amount (Outstanding Utilities Amount)** (`outstanding_utilities_amount`, number, optional): The outstanding amount of utilities owed by the tenant.
* **Outstanding Utilities Status (Outstanding Utilities Status)** (`outstanding_utilities_status`, select_one, optional): The status of the outstanding utilities, which can be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.
* **Outstanding Repossession Fees (Outstanding Repossession Fees)** (`outstanding_repossession_fees`, number, optional): Any additional fees charged for the repossession of the property.
* **Outstanding Repossession Fees Comments (Outstanding Repossession Fees Comments)** (`outstanding_repossession_fees_comments`, text, optional): Any comments regarding the outstanding repossession fees.
* **Outstanding Rent Status (Outstanding Rent Status)** (`outstanding_rent_status`, select_one, required): The status of the outstanding rent, which can be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.
* **Outstanding Rent Amount Status (Outstanding Rent Amount Status)** (`outstanding_rent_amount_status`, select_one, required): The status of the outstanding rent amount, which can be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.
* **Outstanding Repossession Fees (Outstanding Repossession Fees)** (`outstanding_repossession_fees`, number, optional): Any additional fees charged for the repossession of the property.
* **Outstanding Utilities Balance (Outstanding Utilities Balance)** (`outstanding_utilities_balance`, number, optional): The outstanding balance of utility bills owed by the tenant.
* **Outstanding Utilities Amount (Outstanding Utilities Amount)** (`outstanding_utilities_amount`, number, optional): The outstanding amount of utilities owed by the tenant.
* **Outstanding Utilities Status (Outstanding Utilities Status)** (`outstanding_utilities_status`, select_one, optional): The status of the outstanding utilities, which can be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Enter accurate dates and balances to ensure correct calculations.
* If there are any outstanding balances or fees, make sure to document them clearly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
