# Property Technology Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Technology Partner Contact Form is designed to collect contact information from property technology partners for internal collaboration and partnership purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the property technology provider in the "Property Technology Provider" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your message in the "Message" field.
6. Select the correct "Property Technology Partners" and "Real Estate Team" from their respective dropdown menus.
7. Select the correct "Property Management Team" from the dropdown menu.
8. If you want to schedule a meeting, enter the desired date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Technology Provider**: Enter the name of the property technology provider you would like to collaborate with.
* **Name**: Enter your name as the representative of the property technology provider.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for collaboration.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for collaboration.
* **Message**: Enter a brief message describing your interest in collaboration.
* **Property Technology Partners**: Select all relevant property technology partners that you would like to collaborate with. This includes:
	+ Google
	+ Amazon
	+ IBM
	+ Microsoft
	+ SAP
	+ Oracle
* **Real Estate Team**: Select all relevant real estate teams that you would like to collaborate with. This includes:
	+ Zillow
	+ Redfin
	+ Realtor.com
	+ Opendiary
	+ Realogy
	+ REACH
* **Property Management Team**: Select all relevant property management teams that you would like to collaborate with. This includes:
	+ Zillow
	+ Redfin
	+ Realtor.com
	+ Opendiary
	+ Realogy
	+ REACH
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule a meeting (optional).
* **Time**: Enter the time you would like to schedule a meeting (optional).
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to send the form.
