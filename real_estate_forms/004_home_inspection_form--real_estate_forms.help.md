# Home Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Inspection Form is designed to collect data about a property's condition, location, and client information. This form is used for real estate purposes, such as inspecting properties for potential buyers or sellers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
2. Select the inspection date from the calendar.
3. Enter the inspector's name and organization in the respective fields.
4. Choose the type of property from the "Property Type" field.
5. Select the property's usage from the "Property Use" field.
6. Rate the property's condition from the "Property Condition" field.
7. Enter the property's location and neighborhood details.
8. Add any additional property notes.
9. Enter the client's name, address, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address**: Enter the complete address of the property, including the street number, street name, city, state, and zip code.
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection from the calendar. This is the date when the inspection was performed.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **Inspector Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or company that hired the inspector.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property from the options:
	+ Residential
	+ Commercial
	+ Industrial
* **Property Use**: Select the usage of the property from the options:
	+ Single Family Home
	+ Multi-Family Home
	+ Duplex
	+ Triplex
	+ Industrial
	+ Commercial
* **Property Condition**: Rate the property's condition from the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Property Location**: Enter any additional location details about the property, such as its proximity to public transportation or nearby landmarks.
* **Property Neighborhood**: Enter any notable features of the surrounding neighborhood, such as nearby parks or schools.
* **Property Notes**: Add any additional notes or observations about the property's condition.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or property owner.
* **Client Address**: Enter the address of the client or property owner.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the phone number of the client or property owner.
* **Client Email**: Enter the email address of the client or property owner.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the calendar to select the inspection date.
* Double-check the property's type and usage to ensure accuracy.
* Be concise with property notes, but provide enough detail to be useful.
* Ensure the client's contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
