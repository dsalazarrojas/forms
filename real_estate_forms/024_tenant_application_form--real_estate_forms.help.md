# Tenant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential tenants to facilitate the rental application process. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth leasing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully before starting.
2. Fill out each field with your correct information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
4. Review your information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, Optional): Select the date you plan to move in.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, Optional): Enter the rent amount you are willing to pay.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, number, Optional): Enter the length of the lease you are interested in.
* **Lease Type** (`lease_type`, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of lease you are looking for (Residential, Commercial, Mixed Use).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the type of property you are interested in (Apartment, Condo, Townhouse, Single Family, Duplex).
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred location (North, South, East, West).
* **Available Rooms** (`available_rooms`, number, Optional): Enter the number of rooms you require.
* **Security Deposit** (`security_deposit`, number, Optional): Enter the security deposit amount you are willing to pay.
* **Move In Furniture** (`move_in_furniture`, select_one, Optional): Choose if you want to move in with or without furniture (True, False, Negotiable).
* **Move In Equipment** (`move_in_equipment`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the equipment you require for the property (Refrigerator, Stove, Dishwasher, Microwave, Washing Machine, Dryer, Internet, Television).
* **Pets Allowed** (`pets_allowed`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose if you have pets and the size of your pets (None, Small, Medium, Large).
* **Smoking Policy** (`smoking_policy`, select_one, Optional): Choose the smoking policy you prefer (No Smoking, Smoking Allowed, Negotiable).
* **Move In Date/Time** (`move_in_date_time`, time, Optional): Select the move-in date and time.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose how frequently you want to pay rent (Monthly, Bi-Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Monthly, Annually).
* **Rent Amount Flexible** (`rent_amount_flexible`, select_one, Optional): Choose if you are willing to negotiate on rent amount (True, False).
* **Utilities Included** (`utilities_included`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose which utilities you want to be included (Electricity, Water, Gas, Heat, Internet, Cable).
* **Parking Included** (`parking_included`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose if parking is included (Included, Not Included).
* **Amenities** (`amenities`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the amenities you require for the property (Pool, Gym, Parking, View, View of Lake, View of Park).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, Optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Landlord Information** (`landlord_info`, text, Optional): Enter any information about your landlord preferences.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, Optional): Sign the form to confirm your application.
