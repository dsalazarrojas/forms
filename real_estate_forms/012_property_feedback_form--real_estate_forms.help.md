# Property Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback and comments from the public about specific properties. This form helps the property owners or managers to understand the experiences and opinions of the visitors, which can be used to improve the property's services or amenities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property name in the "Property Name" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Contact Name" field. While not required, this field is optional and helps the property owner or manager to address you directly.
3. Enter the address of the property in the "Property Address" field. This is a required field to ensure that you are providing feedback about a specific location.
4. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field. This is a required field to ensure that we can contact you back if needed.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field. This is an optional field, but it helps the property owner or manager to contact you if they need more information.
6. Provide your feedback or comments about the property in the "Feedback" field. This is a required field and should include any suggestions, praises, or criticisms you have about your experience.
7. If you have any additional comments or questions, you can include them in the "Comments" field. This is an optional field.
8. In the "Message" field, provide any other information you want to share with the property owner or manager. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Name**: `property-name` (text, required): This is the name of the property you are providing feedback about. Please enter the name as it appears on the property's sign, brochure, or other marketing materials.
* **Contact Name**: `contact-name` (text, optional): This is your name, which is optional. If you provide your name, the property owner or manager can address you directly.
* **Property Address**: `property-address` (text, required): This is the address of the property you are providing feedback about. Please enter the full address, including the street number and city.
* **Contact Email**: `contact-email` (email, required): This is your email address, which is required. The property owner or manager can use this to contact you if they need more information.
* **Contact Phone**: `contact-phone` (text, optional): This is your phone number, which is optional. If you provide your phone number, the property owner or manager can contact you if they need more information.
* **Feedback**: `feedback` (text, required): This is the main field where you can provide your feedback, comments, or suggestions about the property. Please keep your feedback clear and concise.
* **Comments**: `comments` (text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, you can include them in this field. 
* **Message**: `message` (text, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information you want to share with the property owner or manager.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise feedback about the property.
* Make sure to include all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
