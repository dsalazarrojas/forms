# Simple One Page Lease Agreement Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to record the essential details of a lease agreement between a landlord and a tenant. It is meant to be a basic outline for a single-page document.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Landlord Details in the first section. Please provide the name and contact information of the landlord.
2. Enter the Tenant Details in the second section. Provide the name and contact information of the tenant.
3. In the Lease Terms section, describe the agreed-upon terms of the lease, such as rent, duration, and any conditions or restrictions.
4. Enter the Move In Date, which is the date the tenant is allowed to move in.
5. Enter the Move Out Date, which is the date the lease will expire or the tenant is expected to move out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Details** (`landlord_details`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the landlord.
* **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the tenant.
* **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`, text, optional): Describe the agreed-upon terms of the lease, such as rent, duration, and any conditions or restrictions.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the tenant is allowed to move in.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the lease will expire or the tenant is expected to move out.

## Tips

* This form is designed to be a basic outline and might not include all the details necessary for a comprehensive lease agreement.
* If you are in need of a more detailed lease agreement, consider seeking professional legal assistance.
* Review and proofread the form carefully before signing.
* Ensure to tailor the form according to your specific needs and local laws and regulations.
