# RHH Pending Summary Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RHH Pending Summary Sheet is a form used to collect and document information about a property sale or purchase agreement. It helps real estate professionals track and manage pending sales or purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the agreement, which should reflect the property being sold or purchased.
2. Fill in the Purchase Price, which is the amount the buyer is offering to pay for the property.
3. Enter the Sale Price, which is the amount the seller is offering to sell the property for.
4. Provide the name and contact information for the Seller and Buyer.
5. Enter the Closing Date, which is the date when the sale is expected to be finalized.
6. Enter the Closing Time, which is the time when the sale is expected to be finalized.
7. Enter the Property Address, which is the address of the property being sold or purchased.
8. Enter the Loan Amount, which is the amount of money involved in the loan associated with the sale or purchase.
9. Enter the Seller's Phone number and Buyer's Phone number.
10. Enter the Seller's Email and Buyer's Email (if provided).
11. Select the type of property (House, Commercial, Condominium).
12. Select the status of the property (For Sale, For Rent, For Lease, Leased).
13. Add any additional Notes about the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **RHH Purchase Agreement Form**: Enter a brief description or title of the agreement.
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the amount the buyer is offering to pay for the property.
* **Sale Price**: Enter the amount the seller is offering to sell the property for.
* **Seller**: Enter the name and contact information of the seller.
* **Buyer**: Enter the name and contact information of the buyer.
* **Closing Date**: Enter the date when the sale is expected to be finalized.
* **Closing Time**: Enter the time when the sale is expected to be finalized.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property being sold or purchased.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the amount of money involved in the loan associated with the sale or purchase.
* **Seller Phone**: Enter the seller's phone number.
* **Buyer Phone**: Enter the buyer's phone number.
* **Seller Email**: (Optional) Enter the seller's email address.
* **Buyer Email**: (Optional) Enter the buyer's email address.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (House, Commercial, Condominium).
* **Property Status**: Select the status of the property (For Sale, For Rent, For Lease, Leased).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Use this form for properties with a pending sale or purchase agreement.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your real estate professional for guidance.
