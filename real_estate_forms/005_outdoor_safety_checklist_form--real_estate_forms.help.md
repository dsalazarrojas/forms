# Outdoor Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Safety Checklist Form is designed to help you inspect and evaluate outdoor areas, ensuring they are safe for individuals or participants. This form guides you through a series of sections, each focusing on key aspects of outdoor safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select all areas that you have inspected on the first page, "Inspect Areas".
2.  Choose the most relevant fire safety policy from the options on the second page, "Fire safety policies".
3.  Describe any hazards you find in the "Hazard Identification" section on the third page.
4.  For each hazard, describe the safety measures you will take or have taken to address it in the "Safety measures" section.
5.  On the fifth page, "Emergency Response", select all relevant emergency response protocols.
6.  In the "Participant Information" section, choose the relevant participant status.
7.  On the seventh page, "Review Status", select the review status.
8.  In the final section, "Review Notes", provide any notes or comments about the inspection.
9.  On the last page, "Completion Status", select the completion status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Inspect Areas (id: 1)
*   **Inspect Areas** (`Inspect Areas`, `select_multiple`, **required**: **false**): Select all areas you have inspected to ensure they are safe for participants.

### Fire safety policies (id: 2)
*   **Fire safety policies** (`Fire safety policies`, `select_one`, **required**: **false**): Choose the most relevant fire safety policy that applies to your outdoor area.

### Hazard Identification (id: 3)
*   **Hazard Identification** (`Hazard Identification`, `note`, **required**: **false**): Describe any hazards you have found during the inspection.

### Safety Measures (id: 4)
*   **Safety Measures** (`Safety measures`, `note`, **required**: **false**): Describe the safety measures you have taken or will take to address the hazards identified in the previous section.

### Emergency Response (id: 5)
*   **Emergency Response** (`Emergency Response`, `select_multiple`, **required**: **false**): Select all emergency response protocols relevant to your outdoor area.

### Participant Information (id: 6)
*   **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, `select_one`, **required**: **false**): Choose the participant status, which reflects the current state of the participant.

### Participant Status (id: 7)
*   **Participant Status** (`Participant Status`, `select_multiple`, **required**: **false**): Select all participant statuses that apply to your outdoor area.

### Review Status (id: 8)
*   **Review Status** (`Review Status`, `select_one`, **required**: **false**): Select the review status, reflecting the outcome of your inspection.

### Review Notes (id: 9)
*   **Review Notes** (`Review Notes`, `note`, **required**: **false**): Enter any review notes or comments you have about the inspection.

### Completion Status (id: 10)
*   **Completion Status** (`Completion Status`, `select_one`, **required**: **false**): Select the completion status, indicating the outcome of your safety inspection.
