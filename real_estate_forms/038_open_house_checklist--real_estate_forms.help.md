# Open House Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from open house participants, allowing them to express their interest in a property, provide feedback, and potentially book a viewing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For each section, provide a brief but concise answer in the text field.
3. Make sure to fill in all the required fields, indicated by the "required" label.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (General Info, text, optional): This section is for general information about yourself. Please provide your name, email, or any other contact details if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Location Info** (Location Info, text, optional): In this section, describe the location of the property you are interested in or have viewed.
* **Property Condition** (Property Condition, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the property's condition.
* **Property Details** (Property Details, text, optional): This section is for any additional information about the property.
