# Home Loan Buyers Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Loan Buyers Guide is a form designed to provide a structured approach to understanding mortgage options and requirements. It is divided into sections that gather information on loan options, personal details, and loan terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your mortgage options from the options provided.
2. Provide your personal details, including your full name, phone number, email, and date of birth.
3. Enter your income, credit score, and loan amount.
4. Specify your home type and loan term.
5. Select your loan status and mortgage option.
6. Enter your closing costs, property tax, insurance costs, and HOA fees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mortgage Options** (`mortgage_options`, `select_multiple`): Select one or more mortgage options that best fit your needs (Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA).
* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, `number`): Enter your credit score to help us better understand your financial situation.
* **Income** (`income`, `number`): Enter your annual income to help us assess your loan eligibility.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, `select_one`): Choose the type of home you are interested in (Home, Condo, Townhouse, Duplex).
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`): Enter the loan amount you are interested in.
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, `text`): Enter the interest rate you are considering.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, `select_multiple`): Choose the loan term that suits your needs (15, 20, 25, 30).
* **Closing Cost** (`closing_cost`, `number`): Enter the closing cost associated with your loan.
* **Property Tax** (`property_tax`, `number`): Enter the property tax associated with your loan.
* **Insurance Cost** (`insurance_cost`, `number`): Enter the insurance cost associated with your loan.
* **HOA Cost** (`hoa_cost`, `number`): Enter the HOA cost associated with your loan.
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, `select_one`): Select the loan status that best describes your situation (Approved, Rejected, Pending).
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time Of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`): Enter your time of birth.
* **Loan Application Status** (`loan_application_status`, `select_one`): Select the loan application status that best describes your situation (Approved, Rejected, Pending).
* **Mortgage Option** (`mortgage_option`, `select_multiple`): Select one or more mortgage options that best fit your needs (Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA).

## Tips

* Ensure you have filled out all required fields to submit the form accurately.
* Double-check your information to avoid errors.
* Consult with a financial advisor if you are unsure about any of the options provided.
