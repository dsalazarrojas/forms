# Your Dream Home Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you provide your dream home preferences. It will guide you through a series of questions to determine what you're looking for in your ideal home.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question as accurately as possible, taking into account your true preferences.
- You can select multiple options for fields that allow it (e.g. number of bedrooms).
- If you have specific requirements or concerns, feel free to include them in the "Additional Information" field (not shown in this guide).
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Home Type** (`Home Type`, select_one, optional):
	* Select the type of home you are interested in: Condo, Single Family Home, Townhouse, or Other.
- **Number of Bedrooms** (`Number of Bedrooms`, select_multiple, optional):
	* Choose how many bedrooms you would like in your home: 1, 2, 3, or 4 or more.
- **Price Range** (`Price Range`, select_one, optional):
	* Select the price range you are interested in: Under 200,000, 200,000-400,000, or 400,000 or more.
- **Location** (`Location`, select_multiple, optional):
	* Select where you would like to live: City, Suburb, or Rural.
- **Desired Move In Date** (`Desired Move In Date`, date, optional):
	* Enter the date you would like to move into your new home.
- **Budget Range** (`Budget Range`, number, optional):
	* Enter your budget range for your new home.
- **Preferred Language** (`Preferred Language`, select_one, optional):
	* Select your preferred language: English, Spanish, or French.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`Preferred Contact Method`, select_one, optional):
	* Select how you would like to be contacted: Phone, Email, or In Person.
