# real_estate_agent_outreach_script - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from real estate agents about their outreach scripts. It will help the company understand their preferences and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your agent name in the "Agent Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the contact method(s) you prefer in the "Contact Method" field. You can choose one or multiple methods, such as "Home Buyers", "Home Sellers", etc.
4. Choose a preferred time for contact in the "Preferred Contact Time" field.
5. Select a preferred date for contact in the "Preferred Contact Date" field.
6. Choose the source of your leads in the "Lead Source" field. You can select from "Online Advertising", "Online Directories", etc.
7. Select the type of property you prefer in the "Preferred Property Type" field. You can choose "Single Family", "Condo", etc.
8. Select the location you prefer in the "Preferred Location" field. You can choose "Downtown", "Outskirts", etc.
9. Enter your budget range in the "Budget" field.
10. Select the type of property you are interested in in the "Property Type" field.
11. Choose the number of bedrooms you are interested in in the "Number of Bedrooms" field.
12. Select the number of bathrooms you are interested in in the "Number of Bathrooms" field.
13. Select the square footage you are interested in in the "Square Footage" field.
14. Select the size of property you are interested in in the "Property Size" field.
15. Choose the view time you prefer in the "Preferred View Time" field.
16. Select the view date you prefer in the "Preferred View Date" field.
17. Choose the follow-up date you prefer in the "Preferred Follow-up Date" field.
18. Select the features of property you are interested in in the "Property Features" field. You can choose "Pool", "Yard", etc.
19. Select the follow-up method you prefer in the "Preferred Follow-up Method" field. You can choose "Call", "Email", etc.
20. Select the status of your lead in the "Lead Status" field. You can choose "New", "Hot", etc.
21. Choose the budget range you are interested in in the "Budget Range" field.
22. Select the type of property you are interested in in the "Property Type Range" field.
23. Select the contact method you prefer in the "Contact Method Range" field.
24. Select the language you prefer in the "Preferred Language" field.
25. Select the culture you prefer in the "Preferred Culture" field. You can choose "Western", "Eastern", etc.
26. Select the source of your lead in the "Lead Source Range" field.
27. Select the contact method you prefer in the "Preferred Contact Method Range" field.
