# Landlord Property Management Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Property Management Intake Form is used to collect information about a landlord's property management. This form helps property managers or administrators gather data on various aspects of a property, including address, type, and landlord/tenant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate information.
2.  Provide the required information for each field.
3.  Take your time to complete the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the property.
*   **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, required: false): Enter the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, etc.).
*   **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the landlord or property owner.
*   **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the tenant or resident.
*   **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the property.
*   **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of rent payments (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly, etc.).
*   **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the monthly rent amount.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the method of payment (e.g., Cash, Check, Online Transfer, etc.).
*   **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the lease.
*   **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the lease.
*   **Tenant Contact Info** (`tenant_contact_info`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the contact method for the tenant (e.g., Phone, Email, Address, etc.).
*   **Property Insurance Provider** (`property_insurance_provider`, select_one, required: false): Select the provider of property insurance.
*   **Property Insurance Status** (`property_insurance_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of property insurance (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
*   **Property Insurance Coverage** (`property_insurance_coverage`, text, required: false): Enter the coverage details of the property insurance.
*   **Property Insurance Policy Number** (`property_insurance_policy_number`, text, required: false): Enter the policy number of the property insurance.
*   **Property Insurance Expiration Date** (`property_insurance_expiration_date`, date, required: false): Enter the expiration date of the property insurance.
*   **Rent Frequency (other)** (`rent_frequency_other`, text, required: false): Enter any other frequency of rent payments.
*   **Lease Term Length** (`lease_term_length`, select_one, required: false): Select the length of the lease term (e.g., Month, Year, etc.).
*   **Lease Status** (`lease_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the lease (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
*   **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the property (e.g., Rented, Vacant, etc.).
*   **Tenant Signature Date** (`tenant_signature_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the tenant's signature.
*   **Tenant ID Number** (`tenant_id_number`, number, required: false): Enter the tenant's ID number.
*   **Property SQFT** (`property_sqft`, text, required: false): Enter the square footage of the property.
*   **Rent Amount (other)** (`rent_amount_other`, text, required: false): Enter any other rent amount details.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure you have all required information to complete the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, review the field label for further clarification.
*   Use the form to help you organize and track information about your property management.
