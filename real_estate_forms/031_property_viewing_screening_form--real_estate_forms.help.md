# property_viewing_screening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from prospective tenants during the viewing and screening process for a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the prospective tenant's information in the `prospective_tenant_info` field.
2. Select the type of property the prospective tenant is interested in from the `property_type` field.
3. Choose the viewing preferences from the `viewing_preferences` field.
4. Enter the prospective tenant's contact information, including their email and phone number, in the `contact_info` field.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the `note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **prospective_tenant_info** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the prospective tenant's information, such as their name, address, and contact details.
* **property_type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of property the prospective tenant is interested in, such as a house, apartment, or commercial property.
* **viewing_preferences** (Select One, Optional): Select the viewing preferences of the prospective tenant, such as daytime viewing or evening viewing.
* **contact_info** (Text, Optional): Enter the prospective tenant's contact information, including their email and phone number.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter the prospective tenant's email address.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the prospective tenant's phone number.
* **note** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the prospective tenant.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient viewing and screening process.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or omissions.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact the landlord or property owner for clarification.
