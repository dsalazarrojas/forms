# new_tenant_leaserealtor - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage new tenant lease agreements for rental properties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Start Date" field to select the start date of the lease period.
2. Click on the "End Date" field to select the end date of the lease period.
3. Select the "Lease Start Date" to set the date when the lease is scheduled to begin.
4. Enter the "Rent" amount for the lease period.
5. Choose the "Lease Term" from the options provided (30, 60, or 90 days).
6. Enter the "Move In Date" when the tenant is scheduled to move in.
7. Enter the "Move Out Date" when the tenant is scheduled to move out.
8. Select the "Unit Type" to indicate the type of unit being leased (1, 2, or 3 bedrooms).
9. Select the "Unit Size" to indicate the size of the unit being leased (1-2, 3-4, or 5-6 rooms).
10. Choose the "Rent Frequency" to indicate how frequently the rent is paid (weekly, monthly, or bi-weekly).
11. Select the "Move In Condition" to indicate the state of the unit when the tenant moves in (vacant, furnished, or partially furnished).
12. Enter the "Lease Amount" for the entire lease period.
13. Enter the "Deposit" amount for the lease.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Rent Frequency (frequency)" to indicate if rent frequency is relevant for this lease.
15. Enter the interval at which the rent is paid for "Rent Frequency (interval)" if "Rent Frequency (frequency)" is "Yes".
16. Enter the "Tenant ID" of the tenant leasing the unit.
17. Enter the "Property ID" of the property being leased.
18. Enter the "Lease ID" for the lease agreement.
19. Enter the "Landlord ID" of the landlord or property manager.
20. Select the "Rent Frequency Type" to indicate how the rent is paid (weekly, monthly, or bi-weekly).
21. Select the "Unit Type" to indicate the type of unit being leased (1, 2, or 3 bedrooms).
22. Select the "Unit Size" to indicate the size of the unit being leased (1-2, 3-4, or 5-6 rooms).
23. Enter the "Tenant Address" of the tenant leasing the unit.
24. Enter the "Property Address" of the property being leased.
25. Enter any additional "Notes" about the lease.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the lease period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the lease period.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the lease is scheduled to begin.
* **Rent** (`rent`, number, required): Enter the rent amount for the lease period.
* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the lease from 30, 60, or 90 days.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the tenant is scheduled to move in.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the tenant is scheduled to move out.
* **Unit Type** (`unit_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of unit being leased (1, 2, or 3 bedrooms).
* **Unit Size** (`unit_size`, select_multiple, required): Select the size of the unit being leased (1-2, 3-4, or 5-6 rooms).
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how frequently the rent is paid (weekly, monthly, or bi-weekly).
* **Move In Condition** (`move_in_condition`, select_multiple, required): Select the state of the unit when the tenant moves in (vacant, furnished, or partially furnished).
* **Lease Amount** (`lease_amount`, number, required): Enter the lease amount for the entire lease period.
* **Deposit** (`deposit`, number, required): Enter the deposit amount for the lease.
* **Rent Frequency (frequency)** (`rent_frequency_frequency`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if rent frequency is relevant for this lease.
* **Rent Frequency (interval)** (`rent_frequency_interval`, number, required): Enter the interval at which the rent is paid for "Rent Frequency (frequency)" is "Yes".
* **Tenant ID** (`tenant_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the tenant leasing the unit.
* **Property ID** (`property_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the property being leased.
* **Lease ID** (`lease_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the lease agreement.
* **Landlord ID** (`landlord_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the landlord or property manager.
* **Rent Frequency Type** (`rent_frequency_type`, select_one, required): Select how the rent is paid (weekly, monthly, or bi-weekly).
* **Unit Type** (`unit_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of unit being leased (1, 2, or 3 bedrooms).
* **Unit Size** (`unit_size`, select_multiple, required): Select the size of the unit being leased (1-2, 3-4, or 5-6 rooms).
* **Tenant Address** (`tenant_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the tenant leasing the unit.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property being leased.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the lease.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a real estate professional.
* Double-check the lease details to ensure accuracy and compliance with the lease terms and conditions.
