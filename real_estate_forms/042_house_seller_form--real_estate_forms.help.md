# House Seller Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Seller Form is designed to collect detailed information about houses being sold by individual sellers or real estate agents. This form will be used to create a comprehensive database of houses for sale, making it easier for potential buyers to find and contact the seller.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the title of the house being sold.
2. Enter the price of the house in the "price" field.
3. Enter the contact information, including name, contact number, and email address.
4. Select the type of property (House, Condo, or Townhouse) from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the approximate size of the property from the "Property Size" dropdown menu.
6. Select the status of the property (Active, Inactive, or Sold) from the "Property Status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **House Details (house_details)** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the house.
* **Price (price)** (`number`, required): Enter the price of the house.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)** (`text`, optional): Enter the contact number of the seller.
* **Email Address (email_address)** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address of the seller.
* **Property Type (property_type)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of property (House, Condo, or Townhouse).
* **Property Size (property_size)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the approximate size of the property (1-500 sqft, 501-1000 sqft, or 1001-2000 sqft).
* **Property Status (property_status)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the property (Active, Inactive, or Sold).
