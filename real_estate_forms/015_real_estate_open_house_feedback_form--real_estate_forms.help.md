# real_estate_open_house_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and comments from individuals who have attended an open house event in the real estate industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Share your Feedback and Comments in the fields labeled "Feedback" and "Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted for follow-up or to receive more information.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, note, required/optional): Share your thoughts, opinions, or comments about the open house event. This is your chance to tell us what you liked, disliked, or what could be improved.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the event. This is your chance to share more details or ask questions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (First Name and Email) for accurate tracking and follow-up.
* Keep your comments and feedback clear and concise for better understanding.
* You can submit your feedback anonymously if you prefer.
