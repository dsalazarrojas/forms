# Real Estate Settlement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for real estate settlement claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the real-estate-settlement-claim field with a brief description of your claim.
2. Select the relevant brokerages involved in the transaction.
3. Choose the settlement companies associated with the claim.
4. Select the property teams involved in the claim.
5. If you have supporting documents, you can upload them in the supporting-documents-upload field.
6. If you have a link to a supporting document, you can enter it in the supporting-documents-link field.
7. Add any additional information about your claim in the additional-info field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Real Estate Settlement Claim** (`real-estate-settlement-claim`, text, required): A brief description of your real estate settlement claim.
* **Brokerages** (`brokerages`, select_one, optional): Select the brokerage involved in the transaction.
* **Settlement Companies** (`settlement-companies`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the settlement companies associated with the claim.
* **Property Teams** (`property-teams`, select_multiple, optional): Select the property teams involved in the claim.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting-documents-upload`, text, optional): Upload supporting documents related to your claim.
* **Supporting Documents Link** (`supporting-documents-link`, text, optional): Enter a link to a supporting document.
* **Additional Info** (`additional-info`, text, optional): Add any additional information about your claim.
