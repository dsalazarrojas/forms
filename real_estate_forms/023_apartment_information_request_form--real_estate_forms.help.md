# Apartment Information Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Apartment Information Request Form is designed to help real estate agents understand your apartment requirements and preferences, ensuring that we can provide you with the most suitable matches.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review each field carefully** to ensure you're providing accurate and relevant information.
2. **Fill out the form completely** and truthfully, as this will help us better understand your needs.
3. **Use a specific and detailed response** when filling out fields like "Must-have features" or "Additional preferences."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client full name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your active email address where we can contact you for updates and follow-ups.
* **Phone number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you for further discussions.
* **Request date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you're looking to move in by.
* **Agent name** (`agent_name`, text, optional): If you have an agent handling your request, please enter their name.
* **Budget minimum - monthly rent** (`budget_minimum`, number, required): Enter the lowest amount you're willing to pay for rent each month.
* **Budget maximum - monthly rent** (`budget_maximum`, number, required): Enter the highest amount you're willing to pay for rent each month.
* **Preferred locations or neighborhoods** (`preferred_locations`, text, required): List the neighborhoods or areas you'd like to live in.
* **Minimum number of bedrooms** (`bedrooms_minimum`, number, required): Enter the minimum number of bedrooms you require.
* **Maximum number of bedrooms** (`bedrooms_maximum`, number, optional): Enter the maximum number of bedrooms you're willing to consider.
* **Minimum number of bathrooms** (`bathrooms_minimum`, number, required): Enter the minimum number of bathrooms you require.
* **Maximum number of bathrooms** (`bathrooms_maximum`, number, optional): Enter the maximum number of bathrooms you're willing to consider.
* **Minimum square footage** (`square_footage_minimum`, number, optional): Enter the minimum square footage you're willing to consider.
* **Maximum square footage** (`square_footage_maximum`, number, optional): Enter the maximum square footage you're willing to consider.
* **Desired move-in date** (`move_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date you'd like to move into your new apartment.
* **Preferred lease term length** (`lease_term_preference`, number, optional): Enter the length of the lease you prefer, in months.
* **Furnished or unfurnished preference** (`furnished_preference`, select_one, required): Indicate if you prefer a furnished, semi-furnished, or unfurnished apartment.
* **Pet requirements** (`pet_requirements`, select_one, required): Select if you allow, only accept cats, only accept dogs, or no pets at all.
* **Parking requirement** (`parking_requirement`, select_one, required): Indicate if you must have, prefer to have, or do not need parking.
* **Utilities preference** (`utilities_preference`, select_one, required): Select if you want utilities included in the rent or prefer to pay separately.
* **Required amenities** (`amenities_required`, select_multiple, optional): Select all amenities you consider non-negotiable.
* **Neighborhood priorities** (`neighborhood_priorities`, select_multiple, optional): Select all neighborhoods or features that are essential to you.
* **Must-have features** (`must_haves`, text, optional): List any non-negotiable features you require.
* **Additional preferences or notes** (`additional_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or preferences you have.
* **Preferred communication method** (`communication_preference`, select_one, required): Indicate your preferred method of communication.
* **Desired follow-up frequency** (`followup_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you'd like to be contacted for updates.
