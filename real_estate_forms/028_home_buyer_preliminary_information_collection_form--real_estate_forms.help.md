# Home Buyer Preliminary Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects preliminary information from home buyers to assist in the process of evaluating their loan applications. The form includes various sections for personal, contact, employment, financial, and property search information. Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely to ensure efficient processing of your loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section completely and accurately.
2. Enter your personal information in the Personal Info section.
3. Provide your contact information in the Contact section.
4. Describe your employment history in the Employment section.
5. Enter your financial information in the Financial section.
6. Select your preferred property characteristics in the Property Search section.
7. Enter your loan information in the Loan section.
8. Select your credit report type in the Credit Report section.
9. Enter your credit score information in the Credit Score Info section.
10. Select your employment status and type in the Employment Status and Employment Type sections.
11. Enter your income source in the Income Source section.
12. Enter your loan amount and term in the Loan Amount and Loan Term sections.
13. Select your interest rate and loan type in the Interest Rate and Loan Type sections.
14. Select your closing cost in the Closing Cost section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (Personal Info, text, required): Enter your name, address, and date of birth.
* **Contact** (Contact, text, required): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Employment** (Employment, text, required): Describe your employment history, including job title, employer, and duration of employment.
* **Financial** (Financial, text, required): Enter your financial information, including income and expenses.
* **Property Search** (Property Search, text, required): Select the type of property you are looking for, such as single-family home or condominium.
* **Loan** (Loan, text, required): Enter your loan information, including loan amount and term.
* **Credit Report** (Credit Report, select_one, required): Select one of the following credit reports: Credit Report A, Credit Report B, or Credit Report C.
* **Credit Score Info** (Credit Score Info, text, required): Enter your credit score information, including credit score and history.
* **Employment Status** (Employment Status, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: Employed, Self-Employed, or Retired.
* **Employment Type** (Employment Type, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: Full-time, Part-time, or Self-employed.
* **Income Source** (Income Source, text, required): Enter your income source, such as salary or self-employment.
* **Income** (Income, text, required): Enter your income information, including income amount and frequency.
* **Employment Info** (Employment Info, text, required): Enter any additional employment information.
* **Loan Amount** (Loan Amount, number, required): Enter a dollar amount for your loan.
* **Loan Term** (Loan Term, select_one, required): Select one of the following loan terms: 5 years, 10 years, or 15 years.
* **Interest Rate** (Interest Rate, select_one, required): Select one of the following interest rates: 3.5, 4.5, or 5.5.
* **Loan Type** (Loan Type, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: Fixed, Adjustable, or Variable.
* **Closing** (Closing, text, required): Enter any additional closing information.
* **Closing Cost** (Closing Cost, select_one, required): Select one of the following closing costs: 100, 200, or 300.

Note: This form requires a total of 20 fields to be completed. Please ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and completely to ensure efficient processing of your loan application.
