# Real Estate Buyer Qualification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect relevant information about potential buyers to help real estate agents or companies identify qualified leads and tailor their approach to meet the needs and interests of these individuals. The goal is to ensure that the form is easy to complete and provides the necessary details to move forward in the home buying process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct options for each field based on your answers.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Make sure to save your progress as you move through the form, as you cannot go back to previous pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, `text`, not required): The source from which you heard about the real estate company, e.g., social media, friend, online ad.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Real Estate Type** (`real_estate_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of real estate you are interested in, e.g., residential, commercial, or other.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, not required): Enter the desired location for your real estate purchase.
* **Estimated Price** (`estimated_price`, `number`, not required): Estimate the price range you are interested in spending on your real estate purchase.
* **Time Frame** (`time_frame`, `time`, not required): Specify the time frame in which you plan to make the purchase.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, not required): Add any additional information that you think would be helpful to the real estate agent or company.
* **Lead Type** (`lead_type`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check the types of leads you are interested in, e.g., new home, relocation, or investment.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of your lead, e.g., qualified, not qualified, or pending.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or contact the real estate company for clarification.
* Completing this form does not commit you to any real estate purchase or obligation.
* The real estate company will use the information collected in this form to tailor their approach to your needs and interests.
