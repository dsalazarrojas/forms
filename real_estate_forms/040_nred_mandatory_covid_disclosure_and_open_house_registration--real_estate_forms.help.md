# NRED Mandatory COVID Disclosure And Open House Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for NRED (Non-Resident Employees and Dependents) Mandatory COVID Disclosure and Open House Registration. This form is used to ensure that non-resident employees and dependents disclose their COVID-19 status and register for open house events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in fields 1 and 2, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in field 3. This is a required field.
3. If you need to, enter your phone number in field 4.
4. If applicable, enter your company and job title in fields 5 and 6, respectively.
5. In field 7, please provide your NRED COVID Disclosure as a brief note.
6. In field 8, enter any NRED guidance you may have.
7. Choose from field 9 how you would like to be updated (e.g., NRED Guidance or NRED Disclosure).
8. Select either "I have signed the NRED COVID Disclosure" or "I have not signed the NRED COVID Disclosure" in field 10.
9. If you wish to receive updates via email, fill in fields 21-24 as required.
10. If you have any additional notes or comments, please provide them in field 19.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for updates and communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you need to be contacted.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): If you're an employee, enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): If you're an employee, enter your job title.
* **NRED COVID Disclosure** (`disclosure`, note, required/optional): Provide a brief note about your COVID-19 status.
* **NRED Guidance** (`nred_guidance`, text, required/optional): Enter any NRED guidance you may have.
* **Send email to me** (`send_email`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to be updated (e.g., NRED Guidance or NRED Disclosure).
* **I have signed the NRED disclosure** (`nred_disclosure_signed`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether you have signed the NRED COVID Disclosure.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the current date if you need to provide a specific date for registration.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time if you need to provide a specific time for registration.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address if you need to provide this information.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter your city if you need to provide this information.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter your state if you need to provide this information.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter your country if you need to provide this information.
* **Postal Code** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your postal code (zip code).
* **Send updates** (`send_update`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you want to receive updates (True or False).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **NRED updates** (`nred_update_guidance`, text, required/optional): Enter any NRED updates you may have.
* **Email subject** (`email_subject`, text, required/optional): Enter the subject of the email.
* **Email body** (`email_body`, text, required/optional): Enter the content of the email.
* **Email from** (`email_from`, text, required/optional): Enter the sender's name or email.
* **CC** (`email_cc`, text, required/optional): Enter any CC email addresses.
* **NRED updates** (`nred_update`, text, required/optional): Enter any NRED updates you may have.
