# Renter Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Renter Application Form is a tool for landlords or property managers to collect information from individuals or entities seeking to rent a property. This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form completely, including all required fields.
2. Enter your personal contact information, such as email and phone number, to help us contact you efficiently.
3. Provide accurate details about your previous and desired move-in and move-out dates.
4. Answer yes or no questions about your rent frequency and type preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is displayed on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is displayed on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Street** (4, text, optional): Enter the name of the street where you wish to rent a property.
* **City** (5, text, optional): Enter the city or town where the property is located.
* **State** (6, text, optional): Enter the state or province where the property is located.
* **Zip** (7, text, optional): Enter the zip or postal code of the property's location.
* **Phone** (8, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date Of Birth** (9, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Move In Date** (10, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to move in.
* **Move Out Date** (11, date, optional): Enter the date you expect to move out.
* **Rent Frequency** (12, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you prefer to rent a property frequently, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Renter Type** (13, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you rent properties, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Income** (14, text, optional): Enter your annual income from all sources.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and correctly.
