# Real Estate Agent Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by Real Estate Agents to verify the details of a Client's property transaction and obtain the necessary signatures for the transaction to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Client's Name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the Property Address in the "Property Address" field.
3. Enter the Client's Email in the "Client Email" field.
4. Enter the Client's Phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
5. Enter your Name in the "Agent Name" field.
6. Enter the Date and Time of the Closing in the "Date of Closing" and "Time of Closing" fields, respectively.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Client Signature" and "Agent Signature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the Client's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Property Address**: Enter the full address of the property in question, including the street number and postal code.
* **Client Email**: Enter the Client's email address associated with their identification.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the Client's phone number as it appears on their identification.
* **Agent Name**: Enter your full name as a Real Estate Agent.
* **Date of Closing**: Enter the date of the property closing in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Time of Closing**: Enter the time of the property closing in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00:00.
* **Client Signature**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the Client.
* **Agent Signature**: Enter any additional notes or comments from yourself as a Real Estate Agent.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to the Client for signature.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Keep the form organized and easily accessible for future reference.
