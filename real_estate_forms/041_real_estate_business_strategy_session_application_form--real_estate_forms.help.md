# real_estate_business_strategy_session_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients and customers provide information about their business strategy session preferences and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your session details, including the date and time you are available to meet.
2. Enter your contact information, including phone number, email address, and physical address.
3. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or in-person).
4. Specify your preferred session length and preferred contact preferences (phone, email, or in-person).
5. Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
6. Enter your follow-up information, including follow-up questions, comments, and actions.
7. Specify your follow-up date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details**: Enter the date and time you are available to meet.
* **Client Information**: Enter your name and contact details, including phone number, email address, and physical address.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or in-person).
* **Contact Preferences**: Specify your preferred contact method (phone, email, or in-person).
* **Strategy Session Topics**: Enter any specific topics you would like to cover during the session.
* **Next Steps**: Enter any next steps you would like to take after the session.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Contact City**: Enter your city.
* **Contact State**: Enter your state.
* **Contact Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Contact Country**: Choose the country where you are located (United States, Canada, or Mexico).
* **Session Length**: Specify the length of the session you prefer.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the session.
* **Strategy Session Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the session.
* **Follow Up Questions**: Enter any follow-up questions you have.
* **Follow Up Comments**: Enter any follow-up comments you have.
* **Follow Up Actions**: Enter any follow-up actions you would like to take.
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up.
* **Strategy Session Outcomes**: Enter the outcomes of the strategy session.
* **Strategy Session Attendance**: Enter the number of people attending the session.

Please note that the form fields are required only when indicated by the 'required' property in the YAML.
