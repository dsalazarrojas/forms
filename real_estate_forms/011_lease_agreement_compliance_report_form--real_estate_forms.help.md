# Lease Agreement Compliance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to report potential or actual lease agreement violations, helping property managers and landlords track and address compliance issues in a structured and standardized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required to fill out the form.
2. Enter the tenant's name in the "Tenant name" field.
3. Enter the property address in the "Property address" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the lease agreement in the "Lease start date" and "Lease end date" fields, respectively.
5. Select the type of lease agreement violation from the "Violation type" field.
6. Enter the date of the violation in the "Violation date" field.
7. Enter the status of the violation in the "Violation status" field.
8. If applicable, list the lease terms that are being violated in the "Lease terms violations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Tenant name
* **Tenant name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the tenant who is currently occupying the property.
### 2. Property address
* **Property address** (text, required/optional): Enter the address of the property that is being reported for lease agreement violation.
### 3. Lease start date
* **Lease start date** (date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the lease agreement.
### 4. Lease end date
* **Lease end date** (date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the lease agreement.
### 5. Violation type
* **Violation type** (text, required/optional): Select the type of lease agreement violation that is being reported.
### 6. Violation date
* **Violation date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the lease agreement violation.
### 7. Violation status
* **Violation status** (text, required/optional): Enter the current status of the lease agreement violation.
### 8. Lease terms violations
* **Lease terms violations** (text, required/optional): If the lease agreement has been violated, list the specific terms that are being breached.
