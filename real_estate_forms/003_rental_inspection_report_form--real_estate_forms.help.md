# Rental Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Inspection Report Form is designed to document the findings of a rental property inspection, providing a record of the property's condition and any issues or defects that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the tenant's information in the "Tenant Information" field.
2. Provide the address of the inspected property in the "Address" field.
3. Record the date of the inspection in the "Inspections Date" field.
4. Describe the current property condition in the "Property Condition" field.
5. List any issues found during the inspection in the "Issues Found" field. Select all applicable options that match the problems encountered.
6. Identify any repairs needed in the "Repairs Needed" field. Select a single option that best describes the required action.
7. Date and sign the "Signature Date" field with the current date.
8. Sign the "Agent Signature" field with your official signature, confirming that you conducted the inspection and recorded the findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information** (`tenant_info`, text, optional): Enter the tenant's name and contact information for future reference.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide the exact address of the inspected property, including the unit or suite number (if applicable).
* **Inspections Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Property Condition** (`property_conditions`, note, optional): Describe the current state of the property, including any notable features or characteristics.
* **Issues Found** (`issues_found`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable issues encountered during the inspection, such as leaking roofs or broken windows.
* **Repairs Needed** (`repairs_needed`, select_one, optional): Identify the most pressing repair or action needed for the property, such as fixing a leaky roof or replacing broken windows.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Date and sign this field to confirm that you conducted the inspection.
* **Agent Signature** (`agent_signature`, time, optional): Sign this field with your official signature, confirming that you conducted the inspection and recorded the findings.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all required fields, and be as detailed as possible in the "Property Condition" and "Issues Found" fields. This will help ensure a comprehensive record of the property's condition and any required repairs.
