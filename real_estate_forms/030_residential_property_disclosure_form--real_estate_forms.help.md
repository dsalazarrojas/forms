# Residential Property Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information about a residential property.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a category for the property from the drop-down menu. This will help categorize the property in the real estate system.
2. Provide a brief description of the property in the text field.
3. List the sources of information about the property, such as public records or personal knowledge.
4. Describe the surroundings of the property, including any nearby features or amenities.
5. Note the condition of the property, including any major repairs or issues.
6. Select the age of the property from the drop-down menu.
7. Choose the size of the property from the drop-down menu.
8. Select the intended use of the property from the drop-down menu (e.g., primary residence, investment, rental).
9. Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the numeric fields.
10. Enter the lot size, square footage, number of stories, and year built in the numeric fields.
11. Enter the zip code, city, state, and country of the property.
12. Enter the address of the property in the text field.
13. Enter any additional notes or information about the property in the text field.
14. Enter the name of the property owner in the text field.
15. Enter the name of the real estate agent involved in the property transaction in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select a category for the property from the drop-down menu. This will help categorize the property in the real estate system.
* **description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the property.
* **property-sources** (`property_sources`, text, optional): List the sources of information about the property, such as public records or personal knowledge.
* **property-surroundings** (`property_surroundings`, text, optional): Describe the surroundings of the property, including any nearby features or amenities.
* **property-condition** (`property_condition`, note, optional): Note the condition of the property, including any major repairs or issues.
* **property-age** (`property_age`, select_one, optional): Select the age of the property from the drop-down menu.
* **property-size** (`property_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the property from the drop-down menu.
* **property-use** (`property_use`, select_one, optional): Select the intended use of the property from the drop-down menu (e.g., primary residence, investment, rental).
* **property-bedrooms** (`property_bedrooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **property-bedbaths** (`property_bedbaths`, number, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **property-yrs-old** (`property_yrs_old`, number, optional): Enter the age of the property in years.
* **property-lot-size** (`property_lot_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the property's lot.
* **property-sqft** (`property_sqft`, number, optional): Enter the square footage of the property.
* **property-stories** (`property_stories`, number, optional): Enter the number of stories in the property.
* **year-built** (`property_year_built`, number, optional): Enter the year the property was built.
* **property-zip** (`property_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the property.
* **property-city** (`property_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the property.
* **property-state** (`property_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the property.
* **property-country** (`property_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the property.
* **property-address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property.
* **property-neighborhood** (`property_neighborhood`, text, optional): Enter the neighborhood of the property.
* **property-municipalities** (`property_municipalities`, text, optional): Enter the municipalities of the property.
* **property-notes** (`property_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about the property.
* **property-owner** (`property_owner`, text, optional): Enter the name of the property owner.
* **property-agent** (`property_agent`, text, optional): Enter the name of the real estate agent involved in the property transaction.
