# Online Commercial Lease Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential tenants for a commercial real estate lease. It helps landlords assess a tenant's eligibility and suitability for a lease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name and contact details, under "Tenant Info".
2. Enter the property address you wish to lease.
3. Provide details about the rent you are willing to pay.
4. Specify the lease terms, including any conditions or requirements.
5. If applicable, enter the security deposit amount you are willing to pay.
6. Indicate the duration of the lease you are interested in.
7. Confirm whether you are willing to sign the lease agreement.
8. Finally, sign the lease agreement by selecting "Yes" in the "Landlord Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Tenant Info
* **Tenant Info (text, required/optional)**: This is your personal information, including your name and contact details.

### Property Address
* **Property Address (text, required/optional)**: Enter the address of the property you wish to lease.

### Rent Details
* **Rent Details (text, required/optional)**: Provide a description of the rent you are willing to pay. For example, "monthly rent" or "$X per square foot".

### Lease Terms
* **Lease Terms (note, required/optional)**: Describe any specific conditions or requirements you have for the lease. For example, "Must have a parking spot" or "Can have pets".

### Security Deposit
* **Security Deposit (number, required/optional)**: If you are willing to pay a security deposit, enter the amount here. For example, "1000".

### Rental Amount
* **Rental Amount (number, required/optional)**: If you are willing to pay more than the base rent, enter the total amount you are willing to pay. For example, "1200" for a base rent of 1000 and additional fees.

### Lease Duration
* **Lease Duration (number, required/optional)**: Specify the length of time you wish to lease the property. For example, "12 months" or "24 months".

### Tenant Signature
* **Tenant Signature (select_one, required/optional)**: Confirm your agreement to sign the lease by selecting "Yes". If you are not willing to sign, select "No".

### Landlord Signature
* **Landlord Signature (select_one, required/optional)**: The landlord will confirm their agreement to lease to you by selecting "Yes". You should not select "No" as this indicates the landlord is not interested in leasing to you.
