# real_estate_digital_mapping_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a digital mapping application form for real estate projects. It is designed to collect information about a project, including its details, owner, name, location, type, size, documents, description, contacts, email, website, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Project Details" page and enter your project's title, owner, and location. This page is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have this information.
2. On the "Project Type" page, select the applicable options from the dropdown menu. This field is used to indicate whether the project is a 'Yes' or 'No' project type.
3. On the "Project Size" page, enter the size of your project in numerical format. This field is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have this information.
4. On the "Upload Documents" page, you can upload any relevant documents related to your project. This field is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have any documents to upload.
5. On the "Project Description" page, enter a brief description of your project. This field is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have a description.
6. On the "Project Contacts" page, enter any contacts related to your project. This field is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have any contacts.
7. On the "Project Email" page, enter the email address of the project owner or contact. This field is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have an email address.
8. On the "Project Website" page, enter the website address of your project. This field is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have a website.
9. On the "Project Notes" page, enter any additional notes or comments about your project. This field is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have any notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter your project's title, owner, and location.
* **Project Owner** (`project_owner`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project owner.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the applicable options from the dropdown menu:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Project Size** (`project_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of your project in numerical format.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, note, optional): Upload any relevant documents related to your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Contacts** (`project_contacts`, text, optional): Enter any contacts related to your project.
* **Project Email** (`project_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the project owner or contact.
* **Project Website** (`project_website`, text, optional): Enter the website address of your project.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your project.
