# Apartment Leasing License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for a real estate license. It includes personal details, experience, and background information required for the licensure process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2.  Be sure to provide all necessary details for the licensure process.
3.  Carefully review the form for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Current residential address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your current address.
* **State and Zip Code** (`state_zip`, text, required): Enter the state and zip code of your current address.
* **Social Security Number** (`ssn`, text, required, Last 4 digits only): Enter the last four digits of your Social Security Number.
* **Driver's License Number** (`drivers_license_number`, text, required): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Driver's License State** (`drivers_license_state`, text, required): Enter the state of your driver's license.
* **Years of experience in real estate** (`experience_real_estate`, number, required): Enter your years of experience in real estate.
* **Have you held real estate licenses before?** (`previous_licenses`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have held real estate licenses before, 'False' otherwise.
* **Details of previous licenses** (`previous_license_details`, text, required): If you have answered 'True', provide the details of your previous licenses.
* **Type of license applying for** (`license_type_applying`, select_one, required): Select the type of license you are applying for (Individual lease agent, Property manager, Broker, or Other).
* **Do you have any criminal convictions?** (`criminal_record`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have any criminal convictions, 'False' otherwise.
* **If yes, please provide details** (`criminal_details`, text, required): If you have answered 'True', provide the details of your criminal convictions.
* **Highest level of education** (`education_background`, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education (High school diploma, Associate degree, Bachelor degree, Master degree, Professional certification, or Other).
* **Real estate training or certifications** (`real_estate_training`, text, optional): Enter any real estate training or certifications you have.
* **Sponsoring broker or company name** (`sponsoring_broker`, text, required): Enter the name of the sponsoring broker or company.
* **Sponsoring company address** (`sponsoring_company_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the sponsoring broker or company.
* **Proposed employment start date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required): Enter the proposed start date of your employment.
* **Professional references provided** (`references`, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes, provided' if you have professional references provided, 'Will provide' if you will provide, or 'Not provided' if you do not have them.
* **I authorize background check** (`background_check_authorization`, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes, I authorize' if you authorize a background check, 'False' otherwise.
* **I agree to license terms and conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Answer 'Yes, I agree' if you agree to the license terms and conditions, 'False' otherwise.
* **Application signature date** (`application_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your application signature.
