# Residential Rent Deferment Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residential Rent Deferment Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms of a rent deferment agreement between a landlord and a tenant. It captures essential information to ensure both parties understand their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the landlord's information in the "Landlord Info" field, including their name and contact details.
2. Enter the tenant's information in the "Tenant Info" field, including their name and contact details.
3. Select the date of the agreement in the "Deferment Agreement Date" field.
4. Enter the end date of the deferment agreement in the "Deferment End Date" field.
5. Choose the duration of the deferment agreement in the "Deferment Agreement Duration" field.
6. Enter the total rent amount in the "Rent Amount" field.
7. Provide a clear and concise reason for the deferment agreement in the "Deferment Reason" field.
8. Sign the form with your tenant's signature in the "Tenant Signature" field.
9. Sign the form with your landlord's signature in the "Landlord Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Info**: Enter the landlord's name and contact details, including their address, phone number, and email.
* **Tenant Info**: Enter the tenant's name and contact details, including their address, phone number, and email.
* **Deferment Agreement Date**: Select the date when the deferment agreement becomes effective.
* **Deferment End Date**: Enter the date when the deferment agreement will end.
* **Deferment Agreement Duration**: Choose the duration of the deferment agreement, e.g., 1 month, 2 months, etc.
* **Rent Amount**: Enter the total rent amount to be deferred.
* **Deferment Reason**: Enter a clear and concise reason for the deferment agreement, e.g., financial difficulties, medical reasons, etc.
* **Tenant Signature**: Enter your tenant's signature, ensuring you have their consent to the agreement.
* **Landlord Signature**: Enter your signature, indicating your acceptance of the agreement.
