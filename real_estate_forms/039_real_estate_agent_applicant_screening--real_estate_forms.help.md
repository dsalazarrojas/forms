# Real Estate Agent Applicant Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential real estate agents to screen their qualifications and background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your applicant information in the "Applicant Information" page.
2. Select your relevant experience from the "Experience" page, which includes Agent Brokerage, Real Estate Agent, Real Estate Salesperson, and Real Estate Appraiser.
3. Indicate your level of education from the "Education" page, which includes High School Diploma, Associate's Degree, Bachelor's Degree, and Master's Degree.
4. Check your licenses status from the "Licenses Status" page, which includes Active, Expired, and Pending.
5. Upload a copy of your resume or C.V. in the "Resume" page.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" page, which includes your name, email, and address.
7. Verify your email and phone number on the "Contact" page.
8. Enter your address on the "Address" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (1)**: This section is where you enter your personal details. Please enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Experience (2)**: This section is where you select your relevant experience in the real estate industry. You can choose from Agent Brokerage, Real Estate Agent, Real Estate Salesperson, and Real Estate Appraiser.
* **Education (3)**: This section is where you indicate your level of education. You can choose from High School Diploma, Associate's Degree, Bachelor's Degree, and Master's Degree.
* **Licenses Status (4)**: This section is where you check your licenses status. Please select one of the following options: Active, Expired, or Pending.
* **Resume (5)**: This section is where you upload a copy of your resume or C.V. Please upload a clear scan or a photo of your resume.
* **Contact Information (6)**: This section is where you enter your contact information. Please enter your name, email, and address.
* **Name (7)**: This section is where you enter your full name.
* **Email (8)**: This section is where you enter your email address.
* **Phone (9)**: This section is where you enter your phone number.
* **Address (10)**: This section is where you enter your address.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have a clear and legible scan or photo of your resume or C.V. before uploading it.
* Please enter accurate and up-to-date contact information.
* This form is for screening purposes only and is not a guarantee of employment.
