**Final Walkthrough Checklist - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the overall condition of a property, identifying any issues that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each field accurately and provide any additional comments or notes that you feel are relevant to the property's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Conditions**: Please provide any general comments or notes about the property's overall condition. This is a text field where you can enter any information that is not covered by other fields on this form.
* **Electrical Systems**: This field is for selecting multiple electrical systems present in the property, such as Furnace, Generators, and Water Heater.
* **Plumbing Systems**: Similar to the Electrical Systems field, this is for selecting multiple plumbing systems present in the property, such as Water Pump, Water Heaters, and Sewer Line.
* **Heating and Cooling**: This field is for selecting multiple heating and cooling systems present in the property, such as Central AC, Space Heaters, and Heat Pumps.
* **Electrical Outlets**: Please enter the total number of electrical outlets present in the property.
* **Doors and Hardware**: This field is for entering any notes or comments about the doors and hardware present in the property.
* **Flooring**: Please enter any comments or notes about the flooring in the property.
* **Windows**: Please select the type of windows present in the property, such as Double Pane, Triple Pane, or Regular.
* **Roof**: Please enter any comments or notes about the roof's condition.
* **Exterior**: This field is for entering any comments or notes about the exterior of the property.
* **Interior Doors**: Please enter any comments or notes about the interior doors in the property.
* **Interior Walls**: This field is for entering any comments or notes about the interior walls in the property.
* **Exterior Doors**: Please enter any comments or notes about the exterior doors in the property.
* **Foundation**: Please select the condition of the foundation, such as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Basement**: Please enter any comments or notes about the basement in the property.
* **Crawlspace**: This field is for entering any comments or notes about the crawlspace in the property.
* **Attic**: Please enter any comments or notes about the attic in the property.
* **Number of Electrical Outlets**: This field is for re-entering the number of electrical outlets, as the answer to this field may not have changed since the initial assessment.
* **Electrical Outlets Working**: Please enter any comments or notes about the electrical outlets in the property.
* **Electrical Outlet Notes**: This field is for entering any additional comments or notes about the electrical outlets in the property.
* **Plumbing Fittings**: Please enter any comments or notes about the plumbing fittings in the property.
* **Water Meter**: This field is for entering any comments or notes about the water meter in the property.
* **Septic System**: Please enter any comments or notes about the septic system in the property.
* **Electrical Panel**: Please enter any comments or notes about the electrical panel in the property.
* **Other Notes**: This field is for entering any additional comments or notes that are not covered by other fields on this form.
