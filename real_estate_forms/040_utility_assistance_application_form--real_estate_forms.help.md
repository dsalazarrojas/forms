# Utility Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users to assess their eligibility for utility assistance. This form is used to gather personal, address, and contact information, as well as details about the utility assistance requested, and any additional comments or information the user wishes to provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Info section with your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select the type of utility assistance you are requesting (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water, Sewer, or Cable).
3. Provide your contact information, including a phone number and email address.
4. Describe the utility assistance you are requesting in the Utility Assistance Details section.
5. Add any additional comments or information in the Additional Comments section.
6. Select the category of assistance you are applying for (e.g., Real Estate Forms or Other).
7. Choose who you would like to be assigned to process your application (e.g., chatjimmy or other user).
8. Enter the description of your application.
9. Finally, review your application carefully and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Info**: This section is where you input your name and any other relevant contact information.
*   **Address**: Enter your address to help the organization assess your eligibility.
*   **Utility Type**: Select the type of utility assistance you are requesting (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water, Sewer, or Cable).
*   **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including a phone number and email address.
*   **Utility Assistance Details**: Describe the utility assistance you are requesting in this section.
*   **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your application.
*   **Category**: Select the category of assistance you are applying for (e.g., Real Estate Forms or Other).
*   **Assigned To**: Choose who you would like to be assigned to process your application (e.g., chatjimmy or other user).
*   **Output File**: This section is not applicable as it seems to be a redundant field. 
*   **Description**: Enter a brief description of your application.
*   **Assigned To (Again)**: This field is identical to "Assigned To" and seems to be a redundant field. 
*   **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your application.
*   **Form Version**: This field is not applicable as it seems to be a redundant field.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
*   If you need to resubmit an application, contact the relevant authorities or the organization to inquire about the status of your previous application.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant authorities or contact the organization for assistance.
