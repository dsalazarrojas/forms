# real_estate_esg_data_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining consent and collecting information about the use and sharing of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) data. It is intended for authorized personnel who are responsible for managing this data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the purpose of the form and understand what information is being requested.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (2, text, required/optional): This is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Purpose** (3, text, required/optional): Describe the purpose of collecting and sharing ESG data.
* **Data Use** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate how the data will be used (e.g., Yes for "Yes" or No for "No").
* **Data Source** (5, select_one, required/optional): Choose the source of the data (e.g., data source 1, data source 2, or data source 3).
* **Data Frequency** (6, select_one, required/optional): Select the frequency at which the data will be collected (e.g., data frequency 1, data frequency 2, or data frequency 3).
* **Reporting** (7, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate how often the data will be reported (e.g., Yes for "Yes" or No for "No").
* **Sharing** (8, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate how the data will be shared (e.g., Yes for "Yes" or No for "No").
* **Communication** (9, text, required/optional): Provide additional information about communication related to the ESG data.
* **Signature** (10, text, required/optional): Sign and date the form to confirm consent.
* **Signature Date** (11, date, required/optional): Date and sign the form to confirm consent.
* **Signature Name** (12, text, required/optional): Provide your name as the person signing the form.
* **Company** (13, text, required/optional): Provide the company name related to the ESG data.
* **Role** (14, text, required/optional): Indicate your role related to the ESG data.
* **Phone Number** (15, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (16, email, required/optional): Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Address** (17, text, required/optional): Provide your address for contact purposes.
* **ESG Commitment** (18, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your commitment to ESG practices (e.g., Yes for "Yes" or No for "No").
* **ESG Policy** (19, text, required/optional): Provide information about the ESG policy related to the data.
* **Review Date** (20, date, required/optional): Date the form after review.
* **Review By** (21, text, required/optional): Provide the name of the person reviewing the form.
* **Review Name** (22, text, required/optional): Provide the name of the person reviewing the form.
* **Reviewer Name** (23, text, required/optional): Provide the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (24, email, required/optional): Provide the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (25, text, required/optional): Provide the phone number of the reviewer.

## Tips
- Please complete the form accurately and honestly.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask for clarification.
- Review the form for completeness before signing and dating it.
