# Grant Deed Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Grant Deed Form is used to document the transfer of ownership of real property between individuals or entities. This form is a legal document that confirms the grantor (the party transferring the property) has given the grantee (the party receiving the property) ownership of the specified real property.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields.
2. Enter the name of the grantor (the party transferring the property) in the Grantor Name field.
3. Enter the name of the grantee (the party receiving the property) in the Grantee Name field.
4. Enter the address of the grantor in the Grantor Address field.
5. Enter the address of the grantee in the Grantee Address field.
6. Select the county where the property is located from the County field.
7. Enter the state where the property is located in the State field.
8. Check if any county fees were paid in the County Fees field.
9. If applicable, enter the consideration amount given to the grantee in the Consideration Amount field.
10. Check how the consideration was given in the Consideration Method field.
11. If other consideration method is selected, enter the additional method in the corresponding field.
12. Enter the date the grantor signed the form in the Grantor Signed Date field.
13. Enter the time the grantor signed the form in the Grantor Signed Time field.
14. Enter the date the grantee signed the form in the Grantee Signed Date field.
15. Enter the time the grantee signed the form in the Grantee Signed Time field.
16. Enter the grantor's signature in the Grantor Signature field.
17. Enter the grantee's signature in the Grantee Signature field.
18. Enter the witness signature in the Witness Signature field.
19. Enter the witness's name in the Witness Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Deed Form** (`text`, required): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **Grantor Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the party transferring the property.
* **Grantee Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the party receiving the property.
* **Grantor Address** (`text`, required): Enter the address of the party transferring the property.
* **Grantee Address** (`text`, required): Enter the address of the party receiving the property.
* **County** (`select_one`, required): Select the county where the property is located.
* **State** (`select_one`, required): Select the state where the property is located.
* **County Fees** (`number`, optional): Check if any county fees were paid.
* **Consideration Amount** (`number`, optional): Enter the amount given as consideration.
* **Consideration Method** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select how the consideration was given.
	+ **Cash** (`text`, optional): Enter the cash consideration method.
	+ **Property** (`text`, optional): Enter the property consideration method.
	+ **Other** (`text`, optional): Enter the other consideration method.
* **Grantee Address 1** (`text`, optional): Enter the address of the grantee.
* **Grantee Name 1** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the grantee.
* **Grantor Address 1** (`text`, optional): Enter the address of the grantor.
* **Grantor Name 1** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the grantor.
* **Grantor Signed Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date the grantor signed the form.
* **Grantor Signed Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time the grantor signed the form.
* **Grantee Signed Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date the grantee signed the form.
* **Grantee Signed Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time the grantee signed the form.
* **Grantor Signature** (`text`, optional): Enter the grantor's signature.
* **Grantee Signature** (`text`, optional): Enter the grantee's signature.
* **Witness Signature** (`text`, optional): Enter the witness's signature.
* **Witness Name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the witness.
