# home_value_estimate_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help real estate professionals or clients estimate the value of a property based on its characteristics and historical data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the property type from the options provided (residential, commercial, industrial).
2. Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
3. Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
4. Enter the square footage of the property.
5. Enter the lot size of the property.
6. Enter the property's address.
7. Enter the city where the property is located.
8. Enter the state where the property is located.
9. Enter the zip code of the property.
10. Enter the date the property was sold (if applicable).
11. Enter the price the property was sold for (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Type** (`residential`, `commercial`, `industrial`): Select the type of property (residential, commercial, or industrial) to provide more accurate estimates.
* **Number of Bedrooms**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms**: Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Square Footage**: Enter the total square footage of the property.
* **Lot Size**: Enter the size of the property's lot or land.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the property, including street, suite or apartment number.
* **City**: Enter the city where the property is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where the property is located.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the property.
* **Date Sold**: Enter the date the property was sold (if applicable).
* **Sold Price**: Enter the price the property was sold for (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for the best estimates.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the property owner or the local real estate office.
* The form is designed to provide an estimate based on the entered data, but it is not a definitive valuation method.
