# property_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property inquiry form is used to gather information from users about a property inquiry. It is designed to collect contact information about the property owner, details about the property, and the type of inquiry being made.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information in the first field, "contact_info".
2. Enter the property details in the second field, "property_details".
3. Select the type of inquiry from the options provided in the third field, "inquiry_type".
4. Enter a brief description of the inquiry in the fourth field, "inquiry_description".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_info**: A text field where you can enter your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **property_details**: A text field where you can enter details about the property, such as its location, size, and amenities.
* **inquiry_type**: A dropdown menu where you can select the type of inquiry you are making, such as "viewing", "showing", or "leasing".
* **inquiry_description**: A text field where you can enter a brief description of the inquiry, such as the reason for the inquiry or any specific questions you have. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your inquiry is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for property inquiries only, and any other type of inquiry will not be processed.
