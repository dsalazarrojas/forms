# Estate Review Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Review Acknowledgement Form is used to verify the status of an estate's review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant parties involved in the review process.
2. Indicate the estate administrator's involvement.
3. Record the date and time of the review.
4. Choose the outcome of the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Estate Admin**: Select whether the estate administrator is involved in the review process.
  - This field is used to determine if the estate administrator is part of the review process.
  - Please select 'Yes' if the estate administrator is participating in the review.
  - Please select 'No' if the estate administrator is not participating.
- **Real Estate Team**: Select whether the real estate team is involved in the review process.
  - This field is used to determine if the real estate team is part of the review process.
  - Please select 'Yes' if the real estate team is participating in the review.
  - Please select 'No' if the real estate team is not participating.
- **Office**: Select whether the office is involved in the review process.
  - This field is used to determine if the office is part of the review process.
  - Please select 'Yes' if the office is participating in the review.
  - Please select 'No' if the office is not participating.
- **Stakeholders**: Select whether stakeholders are involved in the review process.
  - This field is used to determine if stakeholders are part of the review process.
  - Please select 'Yes' if stakeholders are participating in the review.
  - Please select 'No' if stakeholders are not participating.
- **Estate Review Acknowledgement**: Record the acknowledgement of the review process.
  - This field is used to record the date of the review.
  - Please enter the date the review was completed.
- **Time of Review**: Record the time of the review.
  - This field is used to record the time the review was completed.
  - Please enter the time using 24-hour format (HH:MM).
  - Please ensure the time is accurate and consistent.
- **Estate Administrator**: Select the estate administrator involved in the review.
  - This field is used to determine who was involved in the review.
  - Please select the estate administrator who participated in the review.

## Tips
- Please ensure all relevant parties are involved in the review process.
- If the estate review acknowledgement form is not required, you can skip it.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant parties for clarification.
