# property_inventory_office_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property_inventory_office_contact_form is a form designed to collect contact information and property details from individuals or organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact details section with your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide information about the property you are inquiring about, including its type (residential, commercial, or agricultural).
3. If you wish to upload documents related to the property, you can do so using the "upload_documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: This field is where you can enter your name, phone number, and email address. This information will be used to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates on your inquiry.
* **Property Info**: Enter the details about the property you are inquiring about, such as its address or any specific questions you have.
* **Inquiry**: Use this field to describe the purpose of your inquiry or what you are looking for.
* **Upload Documents**: If you have relevant documents related to the property, you can upload them here. This will aid the office in understanding your inquiry better.
* **Contact Office**: Enter the name and contact information of the office you are inquiring with.
* **Tenant Name**: If you are inquiring about a property with a tenant, enter their name here.
* **Landlord Name**: If the property has a landlord, enter their name here.
* **Property Manager**: If there is a property manager assigned to the property, enter their name here.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are inquiring about (residential, commercial, or agricultural).
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the office can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so the office can contact you.
* **Date**: If you are inquiring about a specific date-related issue, enter the date here.
* **Time**: If you are inquiring about a specific time-related issue, enter the time here.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to aid the office in addressing your inquiry efficiently.
- Be concise and to the point in your answers.
- If you are unsure about any information or have additional questions, do not hesitate to ask.
