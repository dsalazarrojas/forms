# Real Estate Viewing Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential homebuyers or renters for real estate agents or property managers to schedule viewings and appointments.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
2. Provide details about the property you are interested in in the "Property Info" section.
3. Select a contact method (phone or email) for the agent to reach out to you in the "Contact Method" section.
4. Enter your property ID (if applicable) in the "Property ID" section.
5. Fill in the appointment details, including the time and date in the "Appointment Time" and "Appointment Date" sections.
6. Provide any additional information about the agent or property in the "Agent Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please fill in your name and contact details so that the agent can get in touch with you.
- **Property Info** (`property_info`, text, required: false): Describe the property you are interested in, including its location and any other relevant details.
- **Date and Time** (`date_time`, text, required: false): Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the viewing.
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted by the agent (Phone or Email).
- **Property ID** (`property_id`, number, required: false): If applicable, enter the property ID number.
- **Agent Info** (`agent_info`, text, required: false): If you have any specific requests or questions for the agent, please provide them here.
- **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of the appointment.
- **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the appointment.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling in your contact and property information.
- Make sure to double-check the appointment details before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact the agent directly.
