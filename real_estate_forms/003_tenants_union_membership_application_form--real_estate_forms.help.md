# Tenants Union Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and contact information from potential union members. The data collected will be used to verify identities and facilitate communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form data on the first page.
2. Provide personal information on the second page.
3. Enter your contact information on the third page.
4. Sign the consent form on the fourth page.
5. Choose your position(s) applying for on the fifth page.
6. Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions on the sixth page.
7. Enter your address on the seventh page.
8. Provide your email address on the eighth page.
9. Enter your phone number on the ninth page.
10. Add any additional notes on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, required: false): This is the main form data entry field. You can enter any relevant information you'd like the union to know.
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required: false): Enter your personal details here, such as name and date of birth.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required: false): Please read and sign this consent form to acknowledge that you understand the terms and conditions of the union.
* **Position Applying** (`position`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the position you're applying for among the given options.
* **Terms & Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required: false): Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the union.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address to be contacted for union-related matters.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to be contacted via email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to be contacted via phone.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments that you'd like to share with the union.
