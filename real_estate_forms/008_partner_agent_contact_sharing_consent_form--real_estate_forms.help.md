# partner_agent_contact_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The partner_agent_contact_sharing_consent_form is a form used to capture the contact information sharing consent and preferences from clients in the real estate industry. This form aims to ensure that clients understand how their contact information is being shared with other parties and to obtain their consent for such sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Review the form to understand what information is required and what options are available.
2.  Select the sharing preferences that align with your client's needs and preferences.
3.  Provide the necessary contact information for sharing.
4.  Review and confirm your sharing preferences and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information of the client, such as name and address.
*   **Sharing Preferences** (`sharing_preferences`, select_one, required: true): Select the preferred method of sharing contact information with other parties (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the client for sharing.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the client for sharing.
*   **Sharing Consent** (`sharing_consent`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the types of information that can be shared with other parties (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
*   **Sharing Frequency** (`sharing_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Select the frequency of sharing contact information with other parties (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
*   **Sharing Method** (`sharing_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the method of sharing contact information with other parties (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
*   **Contact Sharing** (`contact_sharing`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of contact information that can be shared with other parties (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

Note: The options for **Sharing Preferences**, **Sharing Consent**, **Sharing Method**, and **Contact Sharing** fields are provided in the form as `Option 1`, `Option 2`, and `Option 3`. However, the actual options may vary based on your specific use case and industry requirements.
