# real_estate_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from real estate agents for the purpose of processing their applications for employment with a real estate company. The form is divided into sections for personal information, contact details, work experience, and other relevant fields. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your personal information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. Next, fill in your contact details, such as your phone number and email address.
3. Then, provide information about your work experience, including job positions and relevant work details.
4. After that, you can provide information about your education and any relevant skills.
5. Finally, review all the provided fields and ensure that you have filled in all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info (1)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Details (2)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Address (3)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your home or work address, including street name, city, and zip code.
* **Job Application (4)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Describe your job application, including your job title, job description, and any other relevant details.
* **Resume (5)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Upload your resume, or type in a brief description of your resume.
* **References (6)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your references, including their names and contact details.
* **Employment History (7)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): List your previous employment history, including job title, employer, and duration of employment.
* **Education (8)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your education background, including degree earned and institution attended.
* **Work Experience (9)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Describe your work experience, including job title, employer, and duration of employment.
* **Job Preferences (10)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Specify your job preferences, including job type, industry, and salary range.
* **Skills (11)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): List your relevant skills, including programming languages, tools, and certifications.
* **Language Skills (12)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your language skills, including languages spoken and proficiency level.
* **Certifications (13)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): List any relevant certifications, including issuing authority and date obtained.
* **Availability (14)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Specify your availability, including work schedule and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contacts (15)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your emergency contacts, including name and contact details.
* **Salary (16)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your expected annual income range.
* **Expected Annual Income (17)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your expected annual income range.
* **Job Search Status (18)** (`text`, required: false): Specify your job search status, including whether you are actively searching or not.
* **Job Search Start Date (19)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you started your job search.
* **Job Title (20)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your job title, including any relevant job titles.
* **Job Title ID (21)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your job title ID, if applicable.
* **Work Status (22)** (`text`, required: false): Specify your current work status, including employed or unemployed.
* **Job Search Type (23)** (`text`, required: false): Specify the type of job you are searching for, including part-time or full-time.
* **Job Searching Since (24)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the date you started job searching.
* **Job Type (25)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the type of job you are searching for, including industry or sector.
