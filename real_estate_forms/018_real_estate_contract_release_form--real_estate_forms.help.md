# Real Estate Contract Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release or modify existing real estate contracts. It is intended for use by authorized real estate professionals to document the release or modification of contracts between parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select the Date Signed from the calendar, or enter the date manually.
4. Select the Date Completed from the calendar, or enter the date manually.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required/optional): Select the date you signed the contract, or enter the date manually. The date should be in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required/optional): Select the date the contract is being released, or enter the date manually. The date should be in the format MM/DD/YY.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth release or modification of the contract.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your real estate professional before completing this form.
* Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
