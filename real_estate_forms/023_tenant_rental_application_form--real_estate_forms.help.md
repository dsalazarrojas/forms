# Tenant Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from tenants applying to rent a property. Please fill it out accurately and completely to ensure a smooth rental application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields.
3. Review the form multiple times to ensure all information is correct before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Application Form** (`rental_application_form`, text, not required)
    This field is intended for additional comments or information about the rental application.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required)
    Please fill out your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required)
    Fill out the address where you currently reside or would like to reside.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required)
    Please provide your date of birth.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required)
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required)
    Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Rental Agreement Date** (`rental_agreement_date`, date, not required)
    This field is intended for the date you would like to start your rental agreement.
* **Renter Status** (`renter_status`, select_one, not required)
    Please select your current renter status from the options provided.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, number, not required)
    Fill out the desired length of your lease.
* **Monthly Rent** (`monthly_rent`, number, not required)
    Enter the monthly rent you are willing to pay.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, not required)
    Fill out the date you would like to move in.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, not required)
    This field is intended for the date you would like to move out.
* **Security Deposit** (`security_deposit`, number, not required)
    Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the security deposit.
* **Rental Agreement Duration** (`rental_agreement_duration`, number, not required)
    Fill out the desired length of your rental agreement.
* **Rental Fee** (`rental_fee`, number, not required)
    Enter the rental fee you are willing to pay.
* **Move In Fee** (`move_in_fee`, number, not required)
    Enter the move in fee you are willing to pay.
* **Move Out Fee** (`move_out_fee`, number, not required)
    Enter the move out fee you are willing to pay.
* **Additional Security Deposit** (`additional_security_deposit`, number, not required)
    Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the additional security deposit.
* **Late Fee** (`late_fee`, number, not required)
    Enter the late fee you are willing to pay.
* **Other Charges** (`other_charges`, text, not required)
    Fill out any additional charges you are willing to pay.
* **Renter Identification** (`renter_identification`, text, not required)
    Please provide your renter identification number or government ID number.
* **Renter Name** (`renter_name`, text, not required)
    Fill out the name of the renter.
* **Renter Email** (`renter_email`, email, not required)
    Enter the email address of the renter.
* **Renter Phone Number** (`renter_phone_number`, text, not required)
    Enter the phone number of the renter.
* **Tenant Referral** (`tenant_referral`, text, not required)
    Fill out any tenant referral information.
