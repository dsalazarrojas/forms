# Homestead Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about you and your homestead needs. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to help us assess your eligibility for a homestead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (1): Please provide your name and contact information.
* **Contact** (2): This is a text field where you can provide any additional contact information you feel is relevant.
* **Property** (3): Please describe the property you are interested in or currently own.
* **Employment** (4): Provide information about your employment status and history.
* **Income** (5): Enter your current income.
* **Financial** (6): This field is likely for financial information related to your employment or income.
* **Purpose of Move** (7): Indicate whether you are moving for a specific purpose, such as for a new job or education.
* **Expected Move In Date** (8): Enter the date you plan to move in.
* **Move In Location** (9): Provide the address or location of your new homestead.
* **Move In Type** (10): Indicate the type of property you are interested in or currently occupying (e.g., single-family home, condominium, etc.).
* **Move In Status** (11): Choose the status of your current move (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Employment Status** (12): Select your current employment status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Occupation** (13): Indicate whether you are currently employed or have a specific occupation.
* **Employment Length** (14): Enter the length of your current employment.
* **Previous Move In Dates** (15): This is a field for taking notes about your previous move-in dates.
* **Previous Move Out Dates** (16): This is a field for taking notes about your previous move-out dates.
* **Move Out Reason** (17): Indicate the reason for your previous move out.
* **Move In Date** (18): Enter the date you moved in.
* **Move Out Date** (19): Enter the date you moved out.
* **Move Length** (20): Enter the length of your current or previous move.
* **Move Length Unit** (21): Indicate the unit of time for your move length (e.g., months, years, etc.).
* **Property Type** (22): Indicate the type of property you are interested in or currently occupying.
* **Property Type 2** (23): Select multiple property types you are interested in or currently occupying.
