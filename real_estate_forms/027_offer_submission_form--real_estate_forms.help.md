# Offer Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Offer Submission Form is used by real estate agents or companies to submit offer details for a real estate offer. This form helps track and manage offers, ensuring accurate and timely updates to the status of offers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information about the offer.
2. Select the relevant options from the provided dropdown menus for real estate forms, recruiter forms, and offer submission status.
3. Enter any additional offer submission details or rejection reason if necessary.
4. Confirm the real estate company's involvement and address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Offer Form Data** (`offer_form_data`, text, required/optional): Enter the data related to the offer.
* **Offer Submission Form Data** (`offer_submission_form_data`, text, required/optional): Provide additional information about the offer submission.
* **Offer Submission Date** (`offer_submission_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the offer submission.
* **Offer Submission Time** (`offer_submission_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the offer submission.
* **Real Estate Forms** (`real_estate_forms`, select_one, required/optional): Select the relevant real estate form for the offer (e.g., '86447' or '97083').
* **Recruiter Forms** (`recruiter_forms`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the offer submission is a recruiter form (select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Offer Submission Details** (`offer_submission_details`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the offer submission.
* **Offer Submission Status** (`offer_submission_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of the offer submission (Active or Inactive).
* **Offer Submission Form Status** (`offer_submission_form_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the form (Active or Inactive).
* **Offer Acceptance Date** (`offer_acceptance_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of acceptance of the offer.
* **Offer Acceptance Time** (`offer_acceptance_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of acceptance of the offer.
* **Offer Acceptance Status** (`offer_acceptance_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the offer acceptance (Yes or No').
* **Offer Rejection Reason** (`offer_rejection_reason`, note, required/optional): Enter the reason for rejecting the offer.
* **Real Estate Company** (`real_estate_company`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the real estate company is involved in the offer submission (Yes or No').
* **Real Estate Company Address** (`real_estate_company_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the real estate company (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and consistently to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the offer submission guidelines or consult with the relevant authority.
* Double-check the real estate form and status to ensure the correct selection.
