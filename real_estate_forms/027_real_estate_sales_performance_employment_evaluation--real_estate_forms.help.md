# Real Estate Sales Performance Employment Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Sales Performance Employment Evaluation form is designed to assess the performance of sales agents in the real estate sector. It provides a structured framework to evaluate their strengths and weaknesses, aiding in identifying areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and select the relevant information.
2. Ensure you answer all questions accurately and honestly.
3. Fill out the form according to your experiences and understanding of your sales performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page** (Select one): This field asks whether you have completed a particular period of experience. Select 'Yes' if you have, 'No' otherwise.
* **agent** (Text): Enter your name or agent ID.
* **years_of_experience** (Number): Enter the number of years you have been working in real estate sales.
* **average_sale_price** (Number): Enter the average sale price of properties you have sold.
* **number_of_sales** (Number): Enter the total number of sales you have made.
* **sale_price_range** (Select multiple): Indicate whether you have sold properties within a certain price range (e.g., yes, no).
* **performance_evaluation_1_to_5** (Select multiple): Indicate whether your performance has met or exceeded expectations for sales within 1-5 units sold.
* **performance_evaluation_6_to_10** (Select multiple): Indicate whether your performance has met or exceeded expectations for sales within 6-10 units sold.
* **performance_evaluation_11_to_15** (Select multiple): Indicate whether your performance has met or exceeded expectations for sales within 11-15 units sold.
* **performance_evaluation_16_to_20** (Select multiple): Indicate whether your performance has met or exceeded expectations for sales within 16-20 units sold.
* **performance_evaluation_21_to_25** (Select multiple): Indicate whether your performance has met or exceeded expectations for sales within 21-25 units sold.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
