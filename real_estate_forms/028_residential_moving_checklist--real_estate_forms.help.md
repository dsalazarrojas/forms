# Residential Moving Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive checklist for residential moving, covering planning, packing, and post-moving tasks. It helps users gather and organize essential information for a smooth and efficient move.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Moving Planning Form** (Page 1) with your moving date, estimated moving time, and any other important dates related to your move.
2. On the **Packing Form** (Page 2), list the items you need to pack, including furniture, clothes, and other essentials.
3. Provide details about your **Moving Details** (Page 3), including the moving address, parking, and elevator information.
4. Enter your **Contact Info** (Page 4) for the landlord/property manager, movers, or other relevant contacts.
5. In **Household Information** (Page 5), describe your household composition, number of rooms, and any special requests.
6. On the **Move In Details** (Page 6), list the dates for moving in, utilities setup, and other post-moving tasks.
7. For **Utilities** (Page 7), note any existing utility accounts, setup instructions, and provider information.
8. Enter your vehicle information, including make, model, and license plate number, on the **Vehicle** (Page 8) page.
9. Provide details about your vehicle insurance and registration on the **Vehicle Insurance** (Page 9) and **Vehicle Registration** (Page 10) pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Moving Planning Form** (`Moving Planning Form`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your planned moving date, estimated moving time, and any other relevant moving details.
- **Packing Form** (`Packing Form`, `text`, required/optional): List the items you need to pack for the move, including furniture, clothes, and other essentials.
- **Moving Details** (`Moving Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about your moving address, parking, and elevator information.
- **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact information for the landlord/property manager, movers, or other relevant contacts.
- **Household Information** (`Household Information`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your household composition, number of rooms, and any special requests.
- **Move In Details** (`Move In Details`, `text`, required/optional): List the dates for moving in, utilities setup, and other post-moving tasks.
- **Utilities** (`Utilities`, `text`, required/optional): Note any existing utility accounts, setup instructions, and provider information.
- **Vehicle** (`Vehicle`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your vehicle information, including make, model, and license plate number.
- **Vehicle Insurance** (`Vehicle Insurance`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about your vehicle insurance, including policy number and expiration date.
- **Vehicle Registration** (`Vehicle Registration`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your vehicle registration information, including plate number and expiration date.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Be thorough when listing items to pack and utilities to setup.
- Review and update your contact information regularly to ensure it's up-to-date.
