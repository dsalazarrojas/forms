# Lease Renewal Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect updated information for a lease renewal. It is intended for tenants to provide their latest details, including lease start and end dates, rent information, and other relevant details. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and update this form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and update this form.
* Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
* Complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information (1)**: Enter the name and contact information of the tenant.
* **Lease Start Date (2)**: Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date (3)**: Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Lease Duration (4)**: Enter the duration of the lease in numbers (e.g., 12 months).
* **Rent Start Date (5)**: Enter the start date of the rent period.
* **Rent End Date (6)**: Enter the end date of the rent period.
* **Rent Interval (7)**: Enter the frequency of rent (e.g., monthly, quarterly, bi-monthly).
* **Rent Frequency (8)**: Select the rent frequency (e.g., Rent Monthly, Rent Quarterly, Rent Bi-Monthly).
* **Lease Status (9)**: Select the current status of the lease (e.g., Active, Pending, Inactive).
* **Lease Type (10)**: Select the type of lease (e.g., Commercial, Residential, Industrial).
* **Tenant Contact Information (11)**: Enter the contact information of the tenant.
* **Landlord Contact Information (12)**: Enter the contact information of the landlord.
* **Lease Terms (13)**: Enter any additional terms or conditions of the lease.
* **Additional Information (14)**: Enter any other relevant information about the lease.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your landlord or property manager.
