# House Rules Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The House Rules Agreement Form is designed to outline the rules and regulations of a rental property. It helps tenants understand their responsibilities and obligations as a tenant, while also serving as a reference point for property managers or landlords to communicate with tenants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before completing it.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. If you're unsure about any part of the form, don't hesitate to ask your property manager or landlord for clarification.
4. Sign and date the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **House Rules Agreement** (Text): This section is a brief overview of the rules and regulations that apply to the rental property.
* **Property Details** (Text): Provide information about the property, including its address and any unique characteristics.
* **Home Address** (Text): Enter the exact address of the rental property.
* **Occupancy Rules** (Text): Describe any restrictions on occupancy, such as maximum number of occupants or visitors allowed.
* **Move In Date** (Date): Record the date you plan to move in.
* **Move Out Date** (Date): Record the date you plan to move out.
* **Rent** (Number): Enter your expected rent amount.
* **Lease Duration** (Number): Choose the length of your lease in months.
* **Security Deposit** (Number): Enter the amount of the security deposit.
* **Utilities** (Select One/Many): Indicate which utilities you'll be paying for (e.g., Water, Electricity, etc.).
* **Payment Terms** (Select One/Many): Choose how you'll pay your rent (e.g., Cash, Check, Credit Card, etc.).
* **Late Fees** (Number): Set the late fee amount.
* **Cleaning Fees** (Number): Set the cleaning fee amount.
* **Pet Policy** (Select One): Choose whether you're allowed to have pets in the property.
* **Move In Inspection** (Select One/Many): Indicate which areas of the property you'll inspect upon move-in.
* **Move Out Inspection** (Select One/Many): Indicate which areas of the property you'll inspect upon move-out.
* **Subletting** (Select One): Choose whether you'll be subletting the property.
* **Renters Insurance** (Select One): Choose whether you'll be required to have renters insurance.
* **Lease Options** (Select One/Many): Choose from available lease options (e.g., Month-to-Month, Yearly, etc.).
* **Lease Term** (Select One): Choose the length of your lease in months.
* **Lease Start Date** (Date): Record the start date of your lease.
* **Lease End Date** (Date): Record the end date of your lease.
* **Lease Renewal Policy** (Select One): Choose whether your lease can be renewed.
* **Lease Renewal Fees** (Number): Set the lease renewal fee amount.
* **Lease Remarks** (Text): Add any additional comments or remarks about the lease.
