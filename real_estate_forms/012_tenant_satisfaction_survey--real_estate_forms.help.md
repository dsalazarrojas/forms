# Tenant Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your feedback on your satisfaction with the living experience in this residential property. Your input is valuable in helping us understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to make improvements to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reading the introduction to understand the purpose and scope of this survey.
2.  Answer each question according to your genuine experience and feelings.
3.  Fill in the required fields as specified.
4.  For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
5.  For questions with open-ended text fields, provide detailed and specific answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Overall Satisfaction Score**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your living space on a scale.
*   **Living Space**: How satisfied are you with your living space?
*   **Amenities**: Select all amenities you have used or would like to use during your tenancy.
*   **Amenities List**: Select all amenities from the list that are available in your living space.
*   **Lease Renewal Likelihood**: Rate how likely you are to renew your lease.
*   **Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of your lease.
*   **Tenant Suggestions**: Share any suggestions or improvements you would like to see in your living space.
*   **How do you prefer to be contacted?**: Select all channels you would prefer for communication with the landlord or property management.
*   **Emergency Contacts**: Select all emergency contact personnel from the list.
*   **Emergency Contacts List**: Select one emergency contact from the list.
*   **Preferred Contact Mode**: Select your preferred mode of contact.
*   **Contact Frequency**: Select all contact frequencies you are open to.
*   **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact details.
*   **Emergency Phone Number**: Enter an emergency phone number.
