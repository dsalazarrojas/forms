# Smart Real Estate Compliance Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Real Estate Compliance Application Form is designed to collect information from real estate professionals about their compliance status and properties. This form is intended for real estate companies, agents, or individuals to report their compliance status, property information, and other relevant details. The form is used to help real estate authorities and regulatory bodies track and monitor compliance in the real estate sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Enter your name, title, and other relevant details in the "Application Form" section (page 1).
3. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section (page 2).
4. Select the appropriate contact method(s) in the "Contact Method" section (page 2).
5. Enter any additional details or notes in the "Additional Notes" section (page 6).
6. Check your compliance status in the "Compliance Status" section (page 3).
7. Specify if you hold a real estate license in the "Real Estate License" section (page 4).
8. Attach any relevant documents or files in the "Attachments" section (page 5).
9. Finally, review your answers and ensure everything is correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (page 1):
	+ Enter your name, title, and other relevant details to identify yourself.
* **Applicant Information** (page 2):
	+ Provide your basic contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Information** (page 2):
	+ Enter your contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Contact Method** (page 2):
	+ Select the methods by which you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, SMS, or Postcard).
* **Contact Details** (page 3):
	+ Enter any additional contact details if you prefer.
* **Property Information** (page 3):
	+ Enter any relevant details about the property you are applying for compliance.
* **Compliance Status** (page 3):
	+ Select your current compliance status (Compliant, Non-Compliant, or Unknown).
* **Action Items** (page 4):
	+ Enter any action items or notes about your compliance status.
* **Attachments** (page 4):
	+ Attach any relevant documents or files to support your application.
* **Additional Notes** (page 5):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Real Estate License** (page 5):
	+ Indicate if you hold a real estate license.
* **Attachments** (page 6):
	+ Attach any relevant documents or files to support your application.
* **Additional Notes** (page 6):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
