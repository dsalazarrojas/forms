# Listing Concierge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your listing to display it on our platform. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure your listing is correctly displayed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button once you have filled out all the required fields. Note that some fields are required to be filled out in order for your listing to be correctly displayed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Listing Address**: This is the address of the property you are listing. Please enter the full address in the format "House Number, Street Name, City, State, ZIP".
* **List Price**: Enter the price of your listing. This will help us display the correct price on your listing.
* **List Type**: Select the type of listing you are creating. Please choose from the options "Yes" or "No".
* **List Status**: Select the status of your listing. Choose from the options "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Date Available**: Enter the date your listing is available. This is a date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Time Available**: Enter the time your listing is available. Please format this as "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **List Note**: Enter any additional notes about your listing. This is an optional field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This is used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will be displayed on your listing.
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the agent involved in your listing.
* **Agent Email**: Enter the email address of the agent involved in your listing.
* **Agent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the agent involved in your listing.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields correctly to ensure your listing is displayed accurately.
- Double-check your listing details before submitting the form.
- Use the correct format for dates and times to ensure accurate display.
- If you have any questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
