# BPO Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BPO form is a tool used to collect information about a property's value and condition. It helps real estate professionals gather data about a property's address, type, and estimated value.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
2. Select the property type from the list of options.
3. Enter the year the property was built.
4. Enter the total square footage of the property.
5. Select the number of bedrooms and bathrooms.
6. Enter the garage spaces.
7. Select the overall property condition.
8. Select the roof condition.
9. Select the foundation condition.
10. Select the HVAC system status.
11. Enter the estimated property value.
12. Enter the basis for the valuation.
13. Enter the number of comparable sales analyzed.
14. Enter the current market conditions assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`report_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are completing this form. This is an important date for documentation and tracking purposes.
* **Broker Name** (`broker_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the person preparing this BPO.
* **Broker Contact Information** (`broker_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information, including phone and email.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the full street address of the property.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the property is located.
* **State and ZIP Code** (`state_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the state and ZIP code of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property, such as Single Family Home, Condo, etc.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, `number`, optional): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Total Square Footage** (`total_square_footage`, `number`, required): Enter the total square footage of the property.
* **Lot Size** (`lot_size`, `text`, optional): Enter the size of the property lot in acres or square feet.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`bedrooms`, `number`, required): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`bathrooms`, `number`, required): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Garage Spaces** (`garage_spaces`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of garage spaces.
* **Overall Property Condition** (`property_condition`, `select_one`, required): Select the overall property condition, such as Excellent, Good, etc.
* **Roof Condition** (`roof_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Select the roof condition, such as Excellent, Good, etc.
* **Foundation Condition** (`foundation_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Select the foundation condition, such as Excellent, Good, etc.
* **HVAC System Status** (`hvac_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the HVAC system status, such as New, Good, etc.
* **Estimated Property Value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated value of the property.
* **Basis for Valuation** (`value_basis`, `text`, required): Enter the basis for the valuation, such as comparable sales, cost approach, etc.
* **Comparable Sales Analyzed** (`comparable_sales`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of comparable sales analyzed.
* **Market Conditions Assessment** (`market_conditions`, `text`, optional): Enter the current market conditions assessment.
