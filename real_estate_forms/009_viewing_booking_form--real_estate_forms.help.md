# Viewing Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Viewing Booking Form" is designed for individuals who want to book a viewing for a property. The form collects essential information to facilitate a smooth and convenient viewing experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Provide your contact details and additional information about your viewing preferences.
3. Submit your booking request.
