# Land Owner Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Land Owner Form Template is a comprehensive form used to collect essential information about a property and its owners. This includes contact details, property information, and documents related to the property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your contact information on the "Contact Information" page.
2. Add property details on the "Property Information" page.
3. Add any additional information you feel is relevant on the "Additional Information" page.
4. Enter your contact details on the "Contact Details" page.
5. Upload or enter information about documents related to the property on the "Documents" page.
6. Specify the location of the property on the "Location" page.
7. Enter your contact information again on the "Contact Information 2" page (optional).
8. Provide details about documents on the "Document Information" page (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you about the property.
* **Property Information** (`property_info`, text, required: false): Provide details about the property, such as its address and description.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Add any extra information you feel is relevant to the property or your situation.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's name, email address, and phone number for the person responsible for the property.
* **Documents** (`documents`, text, required: false): Upload or enter information about documents related to the property, such as receipts, contracts, or certificates.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Specify the location of the property, including city, state, or zip code.
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_info_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional contact information for the property owner (optional).
* **Document Information** (`document_info`, text, required: false): Provide details about documents, such as their type and description.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* You can upload files or enter information about documents in the "Documents" page.
* Use a clear and concise description for "Property Information" to help us understand the property's details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
