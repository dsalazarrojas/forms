# title_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals requesting a title, ensuring that we have all the necessary details to process their request efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your home address in the "Home Address" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This field is required.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field. This field is required.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter your title requester's name in the "Title Requester Name" field. This field is required.
6. Enter your title requester's address in the "Title Requester Address" field. This field is optional.
7. Enter a brief reason for your request in the "Reason for Request" field. This field is required.
8. Select the type of title you are requesting from the "Title Type" dropdown menu. You can choose from "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
9. If you have any additional information or comments, you can enter them in the "Additional Info" field. This field is optional.
10. Finally, please sign and date the "Signature" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address**: Enter your current home address. This field helps us understand your location.
* **Phone Number**: A valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: A valid email address for communication and verification purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of birth as per your identification documents. Ensure it's in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Title Requester Name**: Enter the name of the person requesting the title.
* **Title Requester Address**: If applicable, enter the address of the person requesting the title. This field is optional.
* **Reason for Request**: A brief description of why you are requesting a title. This field is required.
* **Title Type**: Select the type of title you are requesting from the options provided.
* **Additional Info**: Any additional details or comments you'd like to share.
* **Signature**: Please sign and date this field to confirm your identity and request.
