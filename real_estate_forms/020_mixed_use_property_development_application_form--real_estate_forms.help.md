**Mixed Use Property Development Application Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about proposed mixed-use property development projects. It is intended for property developers to provide details about their project, including its purpose, location, size, and budget. The form is used to facilitate the review and evaluation process of mixed-use property development applications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Property Development Questions**: Select the purpose of the property development (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
2. **Property Name**: Enter the name of the property.
3. **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property.
4. **Contact Information**: Provide contact details for the project lead or property manager.
	* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
5. **Property Manager Information**: Provide details about the property manager.
	* **Property Manager Name**: Enter the name of the property manager.
	* **Property Manager Email**: Enter the email of the property manager.
	* **Property Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the property manager.
6. **Project Details**: Provide additional information about the project.
	* **Project Description**: Enter a brief description of the project.
	* **Project Location**: Enter the location of the project.
	* **Project Size**: Enter the size of the project.
	* **Project Budget**: Enter the budget for the project.
	* **Project Timeframe**: Enter the timeframe for the project.
7. **Project Status**: Select the status of the project (Active, Inactive, or Other).
8. **Project Status Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the project status.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use a specific and descriptive title for the property and project.
* Be prepared to provide supporting documentation or information as needed.
* Review and proofread your submission before submitting.
