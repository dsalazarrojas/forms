# Tenant Counteroffer Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for tenants to submit a formal counteroffer for a rental proposal.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the rental proposal and understand the terms.
2. Carefully read and fill in the necessary fields.
3. Attach any supporting files for your counteroffer.
4. Submit your counteroffer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Counteroffer Submission** (`Counteroffer Submission`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your counteroffer.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as per your tenant agreement.
* **Counteroffer Date** (`counteroffer_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your counteroffer.
* **Counteroffer Time** (`counteroffer_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your counteroffer.
* **Counteroffer Amount** (`counteroffer_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of your counteroffer.
* **Counteroffer Details** (`counteroffer_details`, note, optional): Enter a detailed description of your counteroffer.
* **Counteroffer Reason** (`counteroffer_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your counteroffer.
* **Tenant Contact** (`tenant_contact`, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Property Manager Email** (`property_manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Note** (`property_manager_note`, note, optional): Enter any notes for the property manager.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Landlord Contact** (`landlord_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the landlord.
* **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the landlord.
* **Landlord Note** (`landlord_note`, note, optional): Enter any notes for the landlord.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the property address.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the property manager.
* **Tenant Address** (`tenant_address`, text, optional): Enter the tenant address.
* **Tenant Agreement** (`tenant_agreement`, select_one, required): Select the tenant agreement you are submitting.
* **Landlord Agreement** (`landlord_agreement`, select_one, required): Select the landlord agreement you are submitting.
* **Counteroffer Status** (`counteroffer_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your counteroffer.
* **Counteroffer Comments** (`counteroffer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments for your counteroffer.
* **Counteroffer Files** (`counteroffer_files`, text, optional): Attach any supporting files for your counteroffer.
* **Landlord Files** (`landlord_files`, text, optional): Attach any supporting files for the landlord.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the property.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select who submitted this counteroffer.
