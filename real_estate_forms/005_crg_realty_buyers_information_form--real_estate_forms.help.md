# CRG_Realty Buyers Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CRG_Realty Buyers Information Form is designed to collect essential details from potential homebuyers about their property preferences and needs. This form will help us tailor our services to provide you with the most suitable property listings that match your criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
3. Provide your **Current Address** to help us understand your location.
4. In the **Property Search Preferences** section, select the area you are interested in purchasing a property in and choose the type of property that suits your needs.
5. Indicate your desired **Price Range** and the minimum number of **Bedrooms** and **Bathrooms** you require.
6. Select when you plan to **Purchase** and how you will **Finance** the property.
7. If you have a **Flexible timeline**, please specify any additional financing details in the **Additional Financing Details** field.
8. Choose your preferred **Time to Contact You**, and any **Additional Comments or Special Requirements**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Address**: Provide your current address to help us understand your location.
* **What area are you looking to purchase in?**: Select the area you are interested in purchasing a property in.
* **Type of Property**: Choose the type of property that suits your needs (Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse, Multi-Family, or Land).
* **Price Range**: Select the price range you are comfortable with.
* **Minimum Number of Bedrooms**: Enter the minimum number of bedrooms you require.
* **Minimum Number of Bathrooms**: Enter the minimum number of bathrooms you require.
* **When are you looking to purchase?**: Select when you plan to purchase (Immediately, Within 1 month, Within 3 months, Within 6 months, or Flexible timeline).
* **How will you finance the purchase?**: Choose how you plan to finance the property (Pre-approved for mortgage, Exploring mortgage options, Cash purchase, or Other).
* **Additional Financing Details**: If you have a flexible timeline, provide any additional financing details.
* **Best Time to Contact You**: Select your preferred time to contact you (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime).
* **Additional Comments or Special Requirements**: If you have any additional comments or special requirements, please enter them here.
