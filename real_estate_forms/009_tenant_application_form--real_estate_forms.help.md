# Tenant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Application Form is a tool designed to collect essential information from individuals applying for tenancy or rental properties. This form helps landlords or property managers gather all necessary details in an organized and structured way, making the application process smoother and more efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
3. Specify your rental preferences in the "Rental Preferences" section.
4. Answer any additional questions in the "Additional Questions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
• **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, not required): This section is where you can enter your name and any other relevant contact information you'd like to share with the landlord or property manager.

### Contact Details
• **Contact Details** (`contact_details_1`, text, not required): Please provide your contact details, such as your address, phone number, or email, so that the landlord or property manager can get in touch with you.

### Rental Preferences
• **Rental Preferences** (`rental_preferences_1`, text, not required): In this section, specify your preferences for the rental property, such as the type of accommodation you're looking for (e.g., studio, one-bedroom, two-bedroom), the location, or any specific amenities you require.

### Additional Questions
• **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions_1`, text, not required): If you have any other questions or concerns you'd like to mention, please do so in this section. This is an opportunity to provide any further information that might be relevant to your application.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid delays in the application process.
* Double-check your responses for any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you're unsure about any information, feel free to ask your landlord or property manager for clarification.
* Be concise in your responses, but don't hesitate to elaborate if necessary.
