# Lease Buyout Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Buyout Program Application Form is used to collect information from tenants who are interested in purchasing their rented property. This form ensures that all required information is collected correctly and efficiently for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the first section, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the name of the property you are interested in purchasing.
3. Enter the dates of your lease, including the move-in and move-out dates.
4. Enter the address of the property you are interested in purchasing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Property Name**: Enter the name of the property you are interested in purchasing.
* **Move-in Date**: Enter the date you moved into the property.
* **Move-out Date**: Enter the date you plan to move out of the property.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property you are interested in purchasing.
* **Reason for Buyout**: Enter a brief explanation of why you are interested in purchasing the property.
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of your lease.
* **Lease End Date**: Enter the end date of your lease.
* **Rent Per Month**: Enter the monthly rent you are paying for the property.
* **Deposit Required**: Enter any deposit requirements for the property, if applicable.
* **Deposit Required**: This field is intended to capture any deposit required for the property purchase. Please enter a brief description of the deposit required, if any.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth review of your application.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your landlord or property manager for assistance.
