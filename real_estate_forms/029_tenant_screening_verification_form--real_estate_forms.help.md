# Tenant Screening Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Screening Verification Form is a document used to collect information about a tenant's rental history, references, and contact details. This form helps real estate agents, property managers, and other stakeholders to verify a tenant's identity and assess their suitability for a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's questions carefully and answer each field accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that you provide all required information.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to capture the tenant's name, address, and other relevant contact information. Please provide your full name and any other contact details that you are comfortable sharing.
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe your previous rental history, including the length of time you've been renting, the types of properties you've rented, and any relevant information about your rental experiences.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, required: false): List two professional references who are willing to vouch for your character and rental history. Please provide their names, job titles, and contact information.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm that the information you've provided is accurate.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding this application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you regarding this application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and truthful.
* If you have any concerns or questions about the form, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* You can submit your form online or in-person, depending on your preference.
