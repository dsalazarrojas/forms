# Inspection Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inspection Registration Form is used to register an inspection for a real estate listing. This form is used to provide additional details about the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and contact information, including your email and phone number.
2. Indicate the price range you are looking for in a property.
3. Add any additional notes about your inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of your company/organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your inspection.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number or the number of your representative.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, select_one, optional): Choose from Low, Medium, or High to indicate the price range you are looking for in a property.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your inspection or the property you are interested in.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can contact you and provide you with the best results.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your inspection registration and contact you within 24 hours to discuss further details.
