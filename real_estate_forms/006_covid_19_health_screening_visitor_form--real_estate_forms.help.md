# Covid 19 Health Screening Visitor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to assist users in understanding each section and field of the Covid-19 Health Screening Visitor Form. The form has been created to gather essential information from visitors, aiming to ensure a safe and secure environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2.  Fill in the form with your true and accurate information as much as possible.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Info

*   **General Info** (`General Info`, `text`, not required): A brief introduction section that allows you to write about any relevant background information that may be useful for the screener or staff member to know about you.

### Medical History

*   **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, not required): A section for you to describe any health issues or medical conditions you may currently be experiencing or have experienced.

### Travel History

*   **Travel History** (`Travel History`, `text`, not required): A space where you can share any recent or upcoming travel plans that may impact your health or interactions with others.

### Contact Info

*   **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, not required): Fill in your contact information so that we can reach out to you with any additional questions or updates.

### Exposure Risk

*   **Exposure Risk** (`Exposure Risk`, `text`, not required): Describe any potential exposure to the COVID-19 virus, such as recent contact with a confirmed case or being in a high-risk setting.

### Symptoms

*   **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, `text`, not required): Report any symptoms you're currently experiencing or have experienced since the last screening.

### Medical Practices

*   **Medical Practices** (`Medical Practices`, `text`, not required): Provide information about any health and safety measures you have taken or are taking to prevent COVID-19 transmission.

### Visitor Info

*   **Visitor Info** (`Visitor Info`, `text`, not required): Write down any information about the visit, like who you are visiting and the duration.

### Contact Info 2

*   **Contact Info 2** (`Contact Info 2`, `text`, not required): This field is intended for secondary contact information.

### Exposure Risk 2

*   **Exposure Risk 2** (`Exposure Risk 2`, `text`, not required): This field is for reporting any additional potential exposure to COVID-19.

### Symptoms 2

*   **Symptoms 2** (`Symptoms 2`, `text`, not required): Describe any new or ongoing symptoms you're experiencing.

### Medical Practices 2

*   **Medical Practices 2** (`Medical Practices 2`, `text`, not required): This is where you can share further health and safety measures you're implementing to prevent the virus's spread.

# Tips

*   Please answer each field as truthfully as possible.
*   Use clear and concise writing.
*   Be thorough, but avoid excessive writing.
*   You can ask for help or clarification if you're unsure about any field.
*   Remember that your cooperation is crucial for maintaining a safe environment.
*   If you're unsure or have questions, reach out to the relevant staff member or screener.
