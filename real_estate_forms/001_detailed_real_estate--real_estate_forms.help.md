# Detailed Real Estate - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Detailed Real Estate" form is designed to collect comprehensive real estate-related information from users in the industry. This form is intended to be a template for capturing essential details from clients or agents, helping to streamline the data collection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Leave optional fields blank or provide information if applicable.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the person submitting the form.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide the address related to the real estate transaction.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization associated with the real estate.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization associated with the real estate.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property being dealt with, e.g., Office, Residential, or Commercial.
- **Realty Company** (`realty_company`, text, optional): Identify the real estate company involved in the transaction.
- **Realtor Name** (`realtor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the realtor or agent handling the transaction.
- **Realty Phone** (`realty_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the realty company or agent.
- **Client Note** (`client_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the real estate transaction.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting.
- Use the "Property Type" field to categorize the transaction correctly.
- For fields with labels like "Contact Info" and "Realty Phone," ensure to include the correct format (e.g., phone number format for the phone field).
