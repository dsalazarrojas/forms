# Crash Pad Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crash Pad Agreement Form is used to collect information about a crash pad, its details, and the tenant's and landlord's agreement status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the Tenant Details section with your personal and contact information.
2.  Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' for the Crash Pads section.
3.  Enter the Start Date, End Date, Total Rent, and any other applicable dates.
4.  Enter any utilities or utilities 2 information that applies to the crash pad.
5.  Select the Rent Frequency.
6.  Enter the Move In Date and Move Out Date.
7.  Enter the Move In Fee, Move Out Fee, and any other fees that apply.
8.  Select 'I agree' or 'I do not agree' for the Crash Pad Agreement, Tenant Agreement Status, and Landlord Agreement Status.
9.  Enter the Crash Pad Agreement Signed Date and the name of the person who signed the agreement.
10. Enter any additional notes about the crash pad.
