# resident_travel_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The resident_travel_request_form is a form used to request travel information and make it easier for residents to submit their travel requests in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "traveler_name" field.
2. Select your preferred destination(s) from the "preferred_destination" field.
3. Enter your travel date in the "travel_date" field.
4. Choose the type of travel you are requesting (e.g., Hotel, Flight, Train) in the "travel_type" field.
5. Provide any additional travel details in the "travel_details" field.
6. Enter your flight number (if applicable) in the "flight_number" field.
7. Enter the name of your hotel (if applicable) in the "hotel_name" field.
8. Enter your flight arrival and departure dates in the "flight_arrival_date" and "flight_departure_date" fields, respectively.
9. Enter your travel agent's name in the "travel_agent" field.
10. Add any travel notes in the "travel_notes" field.
11. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
12. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
13. Enter your preferred travel date in the "preferred_travel_date" field.
14. Enter your flight arrival time in the "flight_arrival_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **traveler_name (text)**: Enter your name.
* **preferred_destination (select_multiple)**: Select the destination(s) you prefer to travel to. Options: Yes, No.
* **travel_date (date)**: Enter the date you plan to travel.
* **travel_type (select_one)**: Choose the type of travel you are requesting (e.g., Hotel, Flight, Train).
* **travel_details (text)**: Provide any additional travel details.
* **flight_number (text)**: Enter your flight number (if applicable).
* **hotel_name (text)**: Enter the name of your hotel (if applicable).
* **flight_arrival_date (date)**: Enter your flight arrival date.
* **flight_departure_date (date)**: Enter your flight departure date.
* **travel_agent (text)**: Enter your travel agent's name.
* **travel_notes (note)**: Add any travel notes.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **contact_number (text)**: Enter your contact number.
* **preferred_travel_date (date)**: Enter your preferred travel date.
* **flight_arrival_time (time)**: Enter your flight arrival time.
