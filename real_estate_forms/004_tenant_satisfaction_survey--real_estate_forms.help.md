**Tenant Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide**
==============================

### Purpose
The Tenant Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to gauge the satisfaction levels of tenants in various areas of their experience with a property or landlord. It collects feedback on aspects such as satisfaction with the home's condition, communication from the landlord/property manager, rent levels, and more. This survey aims to provide insights that can help property owners and managers improve the overall tenant experience and build stronger relationships with their tenants.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  **Answer each question honestly**: Take your time to thoughtfully answer each question, and be as accurate as possible.
2.  **Select one option for each question**: For questions with multiple options, choose the one that best reflects your feelings or experiences.
3.  **Provide specific answers for open-ended questions**: For questions that require text answers, please type in your thoughts and reasons for your feelings or experiences.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your satisfaction level with your home?** (`satisfaction_level`): This question assesses your overall satisfaction with the home's condition and living experience.
*   **How likely are you to renew your lease?** (`lease_renewal_likelihood`): This question gauges your intent to continue renting the property and your likelihood of renewing your lease.
*   **How do you like communication from your landlord/property manager?** (`communication_communication`): This question evaluates your satisfaction with the communication you receive from your landlord or property manager.
*   **How would you rate the maintenance and repairs in your home?** (`maintenance_maintenance`): This question assesses the quality of maintenance and repairs in your home and whether it meets your expectations.
*   **How would you rate the level of rent in your home?** (`rent_level`): This question investigates your perception of the rent level in your home, whether it's reasonable or too high.
*   **How would you rate the space and amenities in your home?** (`space_and_amenities`): This question evaluates the quality and sufficiency of the space and amenities provided in your home.
*   **How long do you want to lease your home for?** (`lease_duration`): This question gauges your preference for lease duration and how long you want to stay in your current home.
*   **What date are you looking to renew your lease?** (`lease_renewal_date`): This question asks for your desired lease renewal date, which helps property managers plan for future renewals.
*   **How do you like the payment of rent?** (`rent_payment`): This question assesses your satisfaction with the rent payment process and methods used.
*   **How secure do you feel about your tenancy?** (`lease_security`): This question evaluates your level of security and confidence in the property or landlord.
*   **Are you happy with the lease maintenance fund?** (`lease_maintenance_fund`): This question investigates your satisfaction with the lease maintenance fund and whether it's managed effectively.
*   **What was the condition of your home when you moved in?** (`lease_move_in_condition`): This question asks for your impression of the home's condition when you initially moved in.
*   **What do you expect your home to be like when you move out?** (`lease_move_out_condition`): This question gauges your expectations for the home's condition when you leave.
*   **How would you like to be notified about rent increase?** (`rent_increase_notice`): This question assesses your preferred method of rent increase notification.
*   **How satisfied are you with the process of making maintenance requests?** (`maintenance_request_facilitation`): This question evaluates your satisfaction with the process of submitting and receiving maintenance requests.
*   **How quickly do you think maintenance requests are typically responded to?** (`maintenance_response_time`): This question investigates your perception of the time it takes for maintenance requests to be addressed.
*   **How quickly do you think communication from your landlord/property manager typically takes place?** (`communication_response_time`): This question gauges your perception of communication speed and responsiveness.
*   **How would you rate the quality of communication from your landlord/property manager?** (`communication_response_quality`): This question assesses the quality of communication you receive from your landlord or property manager.
*   **How often would you like to be reminded about lease renewal?** (`lease_renewal_period`): This question investigates your desired frequency of lease renewal reminders.
*   **What reason would you provide for not renewing your lease?** (`lease_renewal_reason`): This question seeks the reason for your decision not to renew your lease.
*   **How would you like to be notified about lease renewal?** (`lease_renewal_notice`): This question evaluates your preferred method of lease renewal notification.
*   **What factors influence your decision to renew or not renew your lease?** (`lease_renewal_decision`): This question investigates the factors that affect your decision to renew or not renew your lease.
*   **How would you like to be notified about lease termination?** (`lease_termination_notice`): This question assesses your desired method of lease termination notification.
*   **How would you like to receive communication from your landlord/property manager?** (`lease_communication_channel`): This question evaluates your preferred communication method with your landlord or property manager.
*   **What is the ideal time for lease renewal notice?** (`lease_renewal_notice`): This question investigates your preferred lease renewal notice duration.
