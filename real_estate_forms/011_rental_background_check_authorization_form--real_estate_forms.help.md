# rental_background_check_authorization_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The rental background check authorization form is a document used to gather information and authorization from tenants seeking to rent a property. This form is typically used by property owners or managers to verify the identity and authorization of potential tenants. It helps to ensure that the tenant is allowed to rent the property and can provide necessary documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the tenant.
2. Provide the name of the person authorizing you to rent the property.
3. Enter the date you sign this form.
4. Indicate your title or position if you are an authorized person.
5. Enter the name of the company you represent if you are an authorized person.
6. Review and sign the form to acknowledge that you are authorized to rent the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name**: Please enter your full name as the tenant.
* **Authorized Person**: Enter the name of the person who has given you permission to rent the property.
* **Date Of Signature**: Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Authorized Person Title**: If you are an authorized person, enter your title or position (e.g., Property Manager, Landlord, etc.).
* **Company Name**: If you are an authorized person, enter the name of your company.
* **Form Footer**: This is not a required field, but it can be used to add additional information or notes about the form.

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult the property owner or manager for clarification.
