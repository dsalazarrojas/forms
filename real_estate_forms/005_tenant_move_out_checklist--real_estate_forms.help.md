Tenant Move Out Checklist - Help Guide
=====================================

### Purpose
The Tenant Move Out Checklist is a form used to document the details of a tenant's move out from a rental property. This form helps landlords and property managers to efficiently collect and track information about the tenant's move out, including the date of move out, reason for move out, and property status. It also helps to identify any issues or defects in the property that need to be addressed before re-renting the property.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including:
	* Property Status: Select the current status of the property (Available, Rented, Leased)
	* Property Type: Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, Industrial)
	* Move out Reason: Select the reason for the tenant's move out (Lease Ended, Lease Renewal, Eviction, Other)
2. Enter the move in and move out dates and times for the property.
3. Provide any additional notes about the property and the tenant.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Move in Date**: The date the tenant moved in.
* **Tenant Name**: The name of the tenant who moved out.
* **Contact Number**: The contact information of the tenant.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the tenant.
* **Move out Date**: The date the tenant moved out.
* **Move out Time**: The time the tenant moved out.
* **Property Status**: The current status of the property (Available, Rented, Leased).
* **Property Notes**: Any additional notes about the property.
* **Tenant Notes**: Any additional notes about the tenant.
* **Move out Reason**: The reason for the tenant's move out (Lease Ended, Lease Renewal, Eviction, Other).
* **Other Move out Reason**: If the reason for move out is not listed, enter the reason here.
* **Tenant Move out Checklist**: Select all the items that were completed or inspected during the move out (Appliances, Fixtures, Carpets, Paint, Windows, Doors, Fencing, Siding, Roofs, Electrical, Plumbing, Insulation, Flooring, Walls, Ceilings, Gutting, Landscaping).
* **Property Type**: The type of property (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Property Address**: The address of the property.
* **Property City**: The city where the property is located.
* **Property State**: The state where the property is located.
* **Property Zip**: The zip code of the property.
* **Property Country**: The country where the property is located.
* **Property Region**: The region where the property is located.
* **Contact Name**: The name of the contact person.
* **Contact Job Title**: The job title of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Company**: The company of the contact person.
