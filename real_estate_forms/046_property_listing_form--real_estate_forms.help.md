# Property Listing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture information about a real estate property, including its details and the agent or representative handling the listing. The form will help ensure accurate and complete data for efficient property management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the owner's name and address in the first two fields, ensuring they are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Specify the property's preferred price and select the amenities that apply, such as a basement or parking.
*   Upload property pictures to showcase its features (optional).
*   Provide the agent's name, contact information, and relationship with the property owner.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the "Required" labels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Owner's Name** (`property_owner_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the property owner.
*   **Owner's Address** (`property_owner_address`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the property owner's full address.
*   **Preferred Price** (`property_preferred_price`, `number`, **Required**): Specify the preferred price of the property.
*   **Property Details** (`property_details`, `text`, **Optional**): Add any additional information about the property.
*   **Property Amenities** (`property_ammenities`, `select_multiple`, **Required**):
    *   Select the available amenities, such as **Basement**, **Parking**, **Pool**, or **Yard**.
*   **Property Pictures** (`property_pictures`, `text`, **Optional**): Upload property pictures to provide a visual representation.
*   **Agent's Name** (`property_agents_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the agent handling the listing.
*   **Agent's Contact** (`property_agents_contact`, `text`, **Required**): Provide the agent's contact information.
*   **Agent's Email** (`property_agents_email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter the agent's email address.
*   **Agent's Relationship** (`property_agents_relationship`, `select_one`, **Required**):
    *   Select the relationship with the property owner: **Friend**, **Relative**, or **Client**.
