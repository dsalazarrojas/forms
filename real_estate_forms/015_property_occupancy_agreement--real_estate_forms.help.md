# Property Occupancy Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information from landlords and tenants regarding property occupancy agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the following fields:
1. **Landlord Name**: Enter the landlord's name as it appears on official documents.
2. **Landlord Email**: Enter the landlord's email address (optional).
3. **Landlord Phone**: Enter the landlord's phone number (optional).
4. **Property Name**: Enter the name of the property (e.g., "123 Main Street").
5. **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property (e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown USA").
6. **Property Type**: Enter the type of property (e.g., "Residential," "Commercial," etc.).
7. **Property Status**: Enter the current status of the property (e.g., "Rented," "Vacant," etc.).
8. **Tenant Name**: Enter the tenant's name as it appears on official documents.
9. **Tenant Contact Info**: Enter any relevant contact information for the tenant (e.g., phone number, email).
10. **Move-in Date**: Enter the date the tenant moved into the property.
11. **Move-out Date**: Enter the date the tenant plans to move out of the property (if known).
12. **Tenant Rent**: Enter the monthly rent amount.
13. **Rent Frequency**: Enter the frequency of rent payments (e.g., "Monthly," "Bi-Monthly," etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Name** (`Landlord Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the landlord or property owner.
* **Landlord Email** (`Landlord Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the landlord (if applicable).
* **Landlord Phone** (`Landlord Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the landlord (if applicable).
* **Property Name** (`Property Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the property (e.g., the street address).
* **Property Address** (`Property Address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property (e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown USA").
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, text, optional): Enter the type of property (e.g., "Residential," "Commercial," etc.).
* **Property Status** (`Property Status`, text, optional): Enter the current status of the property (e.g., "Rented," "Vacant," etc.).
* **Tenant Name** (`Tenant Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tenant (e.g., John Doe).
* **Tenant Contact Info** (`Tenant Contact Info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information for the tenant (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Move-in Date** (`Move-in Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the tenant moved into the property (if known).
* **Move-out Date** (`Move-out Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the tenant plans to move out of the property (if known).
* **Tenant Rent** (`Tenant Rent`, number, optional): Enter the monthly rent amount.
* **Rent Frequency** (`Rent Frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of rent payments (e.g., "Monthly," "Bi-Monthly," etc.).
