# estate-management-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The estate-management-form is a tool for estate management professionals to gather information and track progress on a specific property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Property Address** in the corresponding field.
2. Indicate whether the property has a **Property Owner** by selecting "Yes" or "No".
3. Select the **Property Type** by choosing multiple options from the provided list.
4. Enter the **Property Status** by selecting either "Active" or "Inactive".
5. Provide a detailed **Request Description** of the property's condition or issue.
6. Choose the **Request Category** by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. Enter the **Request Date** using the date picker.
8. Enter the **Property Manager** responsible for the property.
9. Enter the **Contact Number** of the person responsible for the property.
10. Enter the **Contact Email** of the person responsible for the property.
11. Add any **Additional Information** about the property.
12. If applicable, upload **Property Images** as a note.
13. Indicate whether **Maintenance Requests** are required by selecting "Yes" or "No".
14. Select the **Maintenance Status** as "Active" or "Inactive".
15. Optionally, indicate whether the **Prophet** is "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address** (text, required): Enter the address of the property, including house number and street name.
* **Property Owner** (select_one, required): Indicate whether the property has a known owner.
* **Property Type** (select_multiple, required): Select all applicable types of property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Property Status** (select_one, required): Enter the current status of the property (Active or Inactive).
* **Request Description** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of the property's condition or issue.
* **Request Category** (select_one, required): Indicate the category of the request (e.g., maintenance, renovation, etc.).
* **Request Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the request.
* **Property Manager** (text, required): Enter the name of the property manager.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter the contact number of the person responsible for the property.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter the email of the person responsible for the property.
* **Additional Information** (text, required): Add any additional details about the property.
* **Property Images** (note, optional): Upload property images as notes.
* **Maintenance Requests** (select_multiple, required): Indicate whether maintenance requests are required.
* **Maintenance Status** (select_one, required): Select the maintenance status (Active or Inactive).
* **Prophet** (select_one, optional): Indicate whether the property has a prophet (Yes or No).
