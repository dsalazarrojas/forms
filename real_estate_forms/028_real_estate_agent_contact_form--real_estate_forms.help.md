# real_estate_agent_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect real estate agent contact information and inquiry details for efficient communication and response.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are populated.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the relevant "Property Type" and "Inquiry Type" as applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name for the real estate agent to address you by.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for the real estate agent to contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for the real estate agent to contact you.
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, select multiple, required): Select all relevant property types that apply (Yes or No).
* **Inquiry Type** (`Inquiry Type`, select multiple, required): Select all relevant inquiry types that apply (Yes or No).
