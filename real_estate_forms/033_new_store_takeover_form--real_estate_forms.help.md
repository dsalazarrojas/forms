# New Store Takeover Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a new store takeover, including the store name, location, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the store name in the "Store Name" field.
2. Select the date of the takeover in the "Takeover Date" field using the YYYY-MM-dd format.
3. Enter the owner's name in the "Owner Name" field.
4. Enter the store's location in the "Store Location" field.
5. Provide a brief reason for the takeover in the "Takeover Reason" field.
6. Enter any additional store details in the "Store Details" field.
7. Enter the owner's contact information in the "Owner Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Name** (Store Name, text, required): Enter the name of the store being taken over.
* **Takeover Date** (Takeover Date, date, required): Select the date of the takeover using the YYYY-MM-dd format.
* **Owner Name** (Owner Name, text, required): Enter the name of the owner of the store.
* **Store Location** (Store Location, text, required): Enter the location of the store.
* **Takeover Reason** (Takeover Reason, text, required): Provide a brief reason for the takeover.
* **Store Address** (Store Address, text, optional): Enter the store's address (if applicable).
* **Owner Contact** (Owner Contact, text, optional): Enter the owner's contact information.
* **Store Details** (Store Details, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the store.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label.
* Use the YYYY-MM-dd format for the "Takeover Date" field.
* Be concise and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact your internal teams for assistance.
