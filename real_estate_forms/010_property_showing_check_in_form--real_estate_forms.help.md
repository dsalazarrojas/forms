# property_showing_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property showing check-in form is used to collect information and schedule a property showing. It's essential to fill out this form before the property showing to ensure we have all necessary data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details as a visitor, including your name and contact information.
2. Provide information about the property you plan to visit, such as its address.
3. Enter the date and time of the scheduled showing.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the showing.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Information**: Enter your name and contact details so we can get in touch with you before and after the showing.
* **Property Information**: Provide the address or location of the property you plan to visit.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Showing Date And Time**: Enter the date and time of the scheduled showing. Make sure to double-check the date and time to avoid any confusion.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the showing, such as any specific requests or concerns.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you after the showing.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any additional communication before or after the showing.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient showing experience.
* If you have any last-minute changes or updates, please contact us in advance so we can adjust the showing schedule accordingly.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the showing, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
