# Property Appraisal Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Appraisal Checklist Form is designed to collect comprehensive data about properties, covering various aspects of a property, including property details, physical condition, and financial considerations. This data will help users make informed decisions or assessments about the property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by providing property details on the first page. This section is not required but will help in getting a better understanding of the property.
* On the second page, describe the physical condition of the property, including any notable features or concerns.
* On the third page, consider the financial aspects of the property, including any relevant costs or considerations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Information** (`Property Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the property, including its address, location, or any notable details.
* **Physical Condition** (`Physical Condition`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the physical state of the property, including any damages, defects, or notable features.
* **Financial Considerations** (`Financial Considerations`, `text`, required/optional): Consider the financial implications of the property, including purchase price, rental income, or any other relevant costs or benefits.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and detailed information about the property to ensure a comprehensive appraisal.
* Be as thorough as possible when describing the physical condition and financial considerations.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
