# Rental Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Reference Form is designed to collect rental reference information from tenants or property managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to required fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
3. Enter the name of the person providing the reference in the "Reference Person Name" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the reference period in the "Reference Start Date" and "Reference End Date" fields.
5. Enter the rent amounts for the reference period in the "Reference Start Rent" and "Reference End Rent" fields.
6. Enter the address associated with the rental property in the "Reference Address" field.
7. Enter the contact phone number of the reference person in the "Reference Contact Phone" field.
8. Enter the name of the landlord or property manager in the "Landlord Name" field.
9. Enter the title of the landlord or property manager in the "Landlord Title" field.
10. Enter a description of the relationship between you and the landlord or property manager in the "Landlord Relationship" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the landlord or property manager in the "Landlord Phone" field.
12. Enter the email address of the landlord or property manager in the "Landlord Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to associate with this reference.
* **Reference Person Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person providing the reference.
* **Reference Start Date** (date, required): Enter the start date of the reference period.
* **Reference End Date** (date, required): Enter the end date of the reference period.
* **Reference Start Rent** (number, required): Enter the rent amount for the start of the reference period.
* **Reference End Rent** (number, required): Enter the rent amount for the end of the reference period.
* **Reference Address** (text, required): Enter the address associated with the rental property.
* **Reference Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the reference person.
* **Landlord Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the landlord or property manager.
* **Landlord Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the landlord or property manager.
* **Landlord Relationship** (text, required): Enter a description of the relationship between you and the landlord or property manager.
* **Landlord Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the landlord or property manager.
* **Landlord Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the landlord or property manager.
