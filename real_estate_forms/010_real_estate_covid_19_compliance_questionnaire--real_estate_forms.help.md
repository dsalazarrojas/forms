# real_estate_covid_19_compliance_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "real_estate_covid_19_compliance_questionnaire" is a form designed to gather information about a visitor's or occupant's access to a property. It helps ensure that the property is compliant with COVID-19 safety measures and guidelines. This form is used to collect data on the property access history, COVID-19 status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Fill out the form completely, as all fields are optional and required fields are marked.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
4. Submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Access** (`property_access`): Please provide a brief description of your property access history.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Visitor Information** (`visitor_info`): Enter any visitor information, such as name and contact details.
* **COVID-19 Status** (`covid_status`): Select the current status of COVID-19:
	+ No COVID-19 symptoms
	+ Have COVID-19
	+ Have had COVID-19 recently
	+ Unsure
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`): Describe your recent travel history, including dates and destinations.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): List any COVID-19 symptoms you are currently experiencing.
* **Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing`): Indicate whether you have been in contact with anyone with COVID-19:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not Sure
* **Health Declaration** (`health_declaration`): Provide a brief health declaration, including any relevant medical information.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`): Select your current vaccination status:
	+ False
	+ Partially Vaccinated
	+ Fully Vaccinated
* **Test Result** (`test_result`): Select your current test result:
	+ No Test Done
	+ Negative
	+ Positive
* **Visitor Pledge** (`visitor_pledge`): Select your willingness to follow property instructions and take precautions:
	+ I will follow the instructions of the property manager
	+ I will take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of COVID-19
	+ Not Sure
* **Property Condition** (`property_condition`): Describe the current property condition.
* **Property Access Type** (`property_access_type`): Describe the type of property access (e.g., in-person, virtual).
* **Contactless Transaction** (`contactless_transaction`): Indicate whether you have engaged in contactless transactions:
	+ False
	+ True
* **Property Access Time** (`property_access_time`): Enter the time spent accessing the property.
* **Property Access Frequency** (`property_access_frequency`): Select how often you access the property:
	+ Less than once a week
	+ 1-2 times a week
	+ 3-4 times a week
	+ 5-6 times a week
	+ Every day
* **Property Access Duration** (`property_access_duration`): Describe the duration of property access.
* **Contactless Transaction Type** (`contactless_transaction_type`): Describe the type of contactless transaction.
* **Property Access Time In Hours** (`property_access_time_in_hours`): Enter the time spent accessing the property in hours.
* **Property Access Frequency In Days** (`property_access_frequency_in_days`): Enter the frequency of property access in days.
* **Visitor Pledge Frequency** (`visitor_pledge_frequency`): Describe your visitor pledge frequency.
* **Visitor Pledge Frequency In Days** (`visitor_pledge_frequency_in_days`): Enter the visitor pledge frequency in days.
* **Property Access Time In Seconds** (`property_access_time_in_seconds`): Enter the time spent accessing the property in seconds.
* **Property Access Time In Seconds 2** (`property_access_time_in_seconds_2`): Enter the time spent accessing the property in seconds (second field for consistency).
* **Contactless Transaction Frequency In Days** (`contactless_transaction_frequency_in_days`): Enter the frequency of contactless transactions in days.
