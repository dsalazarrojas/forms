# Real Estate Buyer Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from real estate buyers to help them find their perfect home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
2. Provide accurate and relevant information about your preferences and search criteria.
3. Ensure that you answer all the required questions.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Names of all prospective buyers** (id: 2, Label: Names of all prospective buyers, Type: text, Required: true)
	+ This field is for the names of all individuals who will be using this form to search for a property.
* **What is your current housing situation?** (id: 3, Label: What is your current housing situation?, Type: select_one, Required: true)
	+ This field is to determine your current living situation, which will help us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Desired property type** (id: 4, Label: Desired property type, Type: select_one, Required: true)
	+ This field is to determine the type of property you are interested in buying.
* **Preferred neighborhoods or cities** (id: 5, Label: Preferred neighborhoods or cities, Type: text, Required: true)
	+ This field is for you to list up to 3 preferred neighborhoods or cities where you would like to live.
* **Minimum number of bedrooms** (id: 6, Label: Minimum number of bedrooms, Type: number, Required: true)
	+ This field is to determine the minimum number of bedrooms you require in your new home.
* **Minimum number of bathrooms** (id: 7, Label: Minimum number of bathrooms, Type: number, Required: true)
	+ This field is to determine the minimum number of bathrooms you require in your new home.
* **Must-have features** (id: 8, Label: Must-have features, Type: select_multiple, Required: false)
	+ This field is for you to select all the features that you must have in your new home.
* **Maximum purchase price** (id: 10, Label: Maximum purchase price, Type: number, Required: true)
	+ This field is to determine your maximum budget for the property.
* **Have you been pre-approved for a mortgage?** (id: 11, Label: Have you been pre-approved for a mortgage?, Type: select_one, Required: true)
	+ This field is to determine your pre-approval status for a mortgage.
* **Intended down payment percentage** (id: 12, Label: Intended down payment percentage, Type: number, Required: false)
	+ This field is to determine your intended down payment percentage.
* **Ideal closing date** (id: 14, Label: Ideal closing date, Type: date, Required: true)
	+ This field is to determine your ideal closing date for the property.
* **What is the most important factor in your search?** (id: 15, Label: What is the most important factor in your search?, Type: select_one, Required: true)
	+ This field is to determine the most important factor in your property search.
* **Any other specific requirements or concerns?** (id: 16, Label: Any other specific requirements or concerns?, Type: text, Required: false)
	+ This field is for you to mention any other specific requirements or concerns you may have.
