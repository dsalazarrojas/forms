# tenant_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tenant referral form collects information from a tenant about their referral to a new tenant in the property management system. This includes details like contact information, move-in date, and rent preferences to ensure accurate and up-to-date data in the system.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has multiple pages. Please complete each page sequentially.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details** (`form_details`, `text`, required/optional): This is the introductory page of the form. Please enter a brief description of the referral.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or lease agreement.
* **Contact Number** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact number to ensure we can get in touch with you for any important updates or information.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Move-in Date** (`date_of_move_in`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to move into the property.
* **Reason for Relocation** (`reason_for_relocation`, `text`, required/optional): Explain why you're referring this new tenant to our property management system.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the rent amount you're paying for the property.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your rent payment schedule, e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly, etc.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional): Once you've filled out all the fields, click the submit button to send your referral information to the property management team.
