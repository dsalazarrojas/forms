# short_term_rental_agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose

The short_term_rental_agreement form is a document designed to record the essential details and agreements between a landlord and a tenant for a short-term rental arrangement. It captures the necessary information for a smooth rental experience, including the property manager's details, manager information, tenant and owner signatures, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the **Rental Agreement** page. This page is a general overview of the rental terms and conditions.
2.  Move on to the **Property Manager** page, where you will enter the property manager's details.
3.  The **Manager Information** page is where you will add any additional information about the property manager.
4.  On the **Tenant Signature** page, the tenant will sign and date the agreement.
5.  Finally, the **Owner Signature** page is where the owner will sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Rental Agreement** (`rental_agreement`, `note`, required: false): This is the main page where you will enter the rental terms and conditions. This can include the rental period, rent amount, and other relevant details.
*   **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, `text`, required: false): Enter the property manager's name, email, and other relevant contact details.
*   **Manager Information** (`manager_info`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional information about the property manager, such as their phone number or address.
*   **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, `text`, required: false): The tenant will sign and date this page to confirm their acceptance of the rental terms.
*   **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, `text`, required: false): The owner will sign and date this page to confirm their acceptance of the rental terms.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
*   Use a pen or a digital signature for the tenant and owner signatures.
*   Keep a copy of the signed agreement for future reference.
