# Detailed Real Estate - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your real estate needs or preferences. Please answer the questions to help us better understand your requirements and provide you with relevant information and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the options for "Buy or Sell" to indicate your intention to buy, sell, or lease a property.
2. Choose the type of property you are looking for from the list of available options.
3. Select one or more time periods from the list to indicate when you want to buy, sell, or lease the property.
4. If you have any additional information or comments, you can use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more details.
5. Enter your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
6. Enter the desired price for the property.
7. Provide any additional comments or information about your real estate needs in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buy or Sell** (`buy_sell_lease`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose from Buy, Sell, or Lease to indicate your intention.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select one of the available property types, such as House, Apartment, Condo, or Land.
* **Time Period** (`time_period`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Choose one or more time frames from the list, indicating when you want to buy, sell, or lease the property.
* **Additional information** (`note`, `note`, `optional`): Enter any additional details or information about your real estate needs.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`text`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Desired Price** (`number`, `number`, `required`): Enter the price you are looking for in the property.
* **Additional Comments** (`text`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional comments or information about your real estate needs.
