# Lot And Block Property Survey Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a survey about a specific lot and block property. It gathers details from the survey requester, such as their information and contact details, and the specifics of the requested survey, including type and details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Fill out each section**: Complete each field with the required information for the purpose of the survey.
2.  **Fill in Survey Requester Info, Survey Requester Details**: Enter the survey requester's name and any other details they wish to provide for the survey.
3.  **Choose a Survey Type**: Select the type of survey required from the available options.
4.  **Select Property Type (if applicable)**: If the survey is relevant, choose the applicable property type from the options provided.
5.  **Fill out Additional Survey Request Details**: Provide as much detail as possible about the specific survey request.
6.  **Enter Phone and Email**: Enter the survey requester's contact details for easy communication and follow up on the survey.
7.  **Choose the Date and Time for the Survey**: Enter the desired date and time for the survey.
8.  **Add a Note (if needed)**: Leave any additional comments or information about the survey request.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Survey Request Form**: This is the main section where you enter the general details of your survey request.
*   **Survey Requester Info**: Enter your name as the survey requester, so we can identify who is requesting the survey.
*   **Survey Requester Details**: Add any further details you would like to provide for the survey request.
*   **Survey Type**: Select the type of survey you are requesting. This could be for general information or specific details like property boundaries or zoning.
*   **Property Type**: If you are requesting a specific type of information about the property, choose this option.
*   **Survey Request Details**: Provide as much detail as possible about what you are looking for in the survey.
*   **Phone**: Enter your contact information for easy communication and follow-up on the survey request.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for easy communication and follow-up on the survey request.
*   **Date**: Choose the date on which you would like the survey to be conducted or completed.
*   **Time**: Choose the time at which you would like the survey to be conducted.
*   **Note**: Add any additional comments or information about the survey request that would be helpful for us.
