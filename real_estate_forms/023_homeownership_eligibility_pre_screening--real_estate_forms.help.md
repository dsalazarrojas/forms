# Homeownership Eligibility Pre Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to pre-screen homebuyers for eligibility for a home loan. It collects various personal and financial information to assess the applicant's ability to obtain a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal and financial information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your situation.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your basic contact information such as name, email, and phone number.
* **What is your monthly income before taxes?** (`income`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your monthly income before taxes.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your employment status (Working full-time, Working part-time, Unemployed, Retired).
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select your marital status (Married, Single, Other (e.g., Domestic partner)).
* **Address History** (`address_history`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select your address history (1-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years).
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your employment history.
* **What is your credit score?** (`credit_score`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your credit score.
* **Do you have any outstanding debts or judgments?** (`debt_to_income`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe any outstanding debts or judgments.
* **What are your assets?** (`assets`, `text`, required: `false`): List your assets.
* **Financial History** (`financial_history`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your financial history.
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your rental history.
* **What type of property are you looking to purchase?** (`property_type`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the type of property you're looking to purchase (Single-family home, Condo, Townhouse, Other).
* **What is the value of the property you are looking to purchase?** (`property_value`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the value of the property you're looking to purchase.
* **How much of a loan are you looking to secure?** (`loan_amount`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the loan amount you're looking to secure.
* **What is your planned down payment?** (`down_payment`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your planned down payment.
* **What is the closing cost for the home you are looking to purchase?** (`closing_cost`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the closing cost for the home you're looking to purchase.
* **Property Type 2** (`property_type_2`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the type of property you're looking to purchase (Single-family home, Condo, Townhouse, Other).
* **Property Value 2** (`property_value_2`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the value of the property you're looking to purchase.
* **Loan Amount 2** (`loan_amount_2`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the loan amount you're looking to secure.
* **Down Payment 2** (`down_payment_2`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your planned down payment.
* **Closing Cost 2** (`closing_cost_2`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the closing cost for the home you're looking to purchase.
* **Are you applying with a non-traditional credit history?** (`other_credit`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select if you're applying with a non-traditional credit history.
* **Other Credit 2** (`other_credit_2`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select if you're applying with a non-traditional credit history.
* **Other Credit 3** (`other_credit_3`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select if you're applying with a non-traditional credit history.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Submit" to complete and submit the form, or "Cancel" to exit.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can contact our customer support for assistance.
