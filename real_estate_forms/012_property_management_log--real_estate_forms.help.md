# Property Management Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Log is a form used to record and document visits to properties managed by the organization. It captures details of the properties, owners, and visit activities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the Property Type from the options provided.
2. Choose all applicable Property Status from the options provided.
3. Enter relevant Maintenance Activities, Tenant Issues, Comments, Date and Time of Visit, Property Owner details, Contact Number, Email Address, and Address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Type** (property_type, select_one, required):
	+ Select the type of property you are visiting (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Property Status** (property_status, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose all applicable statuses for the property (e.g., occupied, vacant, etc.).
* **Property Manager** (property_manager, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name and title of the property manager (if applicable).
* **Maintenance Activities** (maintenance_activities, text, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant maintenance activities performed during the visit.
* **Tenant Issues** (tenant_issues, text, optional):
	+ Record any issues reported by the tenant during the visit.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments about the visit.
* **Date of Visit** (date_of_visit, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the visit in the required format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Time of Visit** (time_of_visit, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the visit in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Property Owner** (property_owner, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the property owner.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, optional):
	+ Enter the contact number of the property owner or manager.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the property owner or manager.
* **Address** (address, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address of the property.
## Tips
* Ensure the form is filled out accurately and consistently.
* Review the form regularly to ensure up-to-date information.
* Consult with the property manager or owner if you are unsure about any information.
