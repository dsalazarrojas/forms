# We Buy Houses - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "We Buy Houses" form is a real estate form used to collect information about houses being sold. This form helps us understand the characteristics of the house, including its type, size, and mortgage status, so we can assess its value and offer a fair price.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the house in the **We Buy Houses** field.
2. Choose the type of house from the **What Type of House** dropdown menu.
3. Enter the garage size in the **Garage Size** field.
4. Select any applicable options for **Basement**, **How Many Bathrooms**, and **How Many Bedrooms**.
5. Choose the status of the mortgage on the house in the **Is There a Mortgage on the House** dropdown menu.
6. Enter the current lender's name in the **Current Lender** field.
7. Enter the balance of the mortgage in the **Mortgage Balance** field.
8. Choose whether the house is occupied or not in the **Is the House Occupied** dropdown menu.
9. Enter the name of the contact person submitting the form in the **Contact Person** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **We Buy Houses**: This is the title of the house being sold. Enter the name of the house in this field.
* **What Type of House**: Choose the type of house from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the house's characteristics.
* **Garage Size**: Enter the size of the garage in this field.
* **Basement**: Check any applicable options for the basement, such as whether it's finished or unfinished.
* **How Many Bathrooms**: Enter the number of bathrooms in the house.
* **How Many Bedrooms**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the house.
* **Is There a Mortgage on the House**: Choose whether there is a mortgage on the house from the dropdown menu.
* **Current Lender**: Enter the name of the current lender on the mortgage.
* **Mortgage Balance**: Enter the balance of the mortgage on the house.
* **Is the House Occupied**: Choose whether the house is occupied or not from the dropdown menu.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person submitting the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to help us understand the value of the house.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
