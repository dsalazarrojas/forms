# Buyers Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyers Application form is designed to gather information from potential real estate investors, allowing our team to provide personalized guidance and investment opportunities tailored to your specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate and honest information about yourself.
2. Be sure to select the relevant options for each field based on your true preferences and circumstances.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Address** (`applicant_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current street address.
* **Years of Real Estate Investment Experience** (`years_real_estate_experience`, `number`, optional): If you have experience investing in real estate, please enter the number of years you have been investing.
* **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the investment goals that apply to you:
	+ Passive income
	+ Capital appreciation
	+ Portfolio diversification
	+ Retirement planning
	+ Wealth building
	+ Long-term hold
	+ Short-term profit
* **Preferred Investment Strategy** (`investment_strategy`, `select_one`, required): Select the investment strategy that best suits your goals and preferences:
	+ Buy and hold for long term
	+ Rental income focus
	+ Flip for profit
	+ Mixed strategy
	+ Hands-off passive investment
* **Property Type Interest** (`property_type_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the property types that interest you:
	+ Single-family homes
	+ Multi-family properties
	+ Commercial real estate
	+ Land
	+ Condos
	+ Townhouses
	+ Mixed-use properties
* **Geographic Markets of Interest** (`investment_markets`, `text`, required): Enter the cities or regions where you are interested in investing in real estate.
* **Investment Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Select the budget range that applies to your investment goals:
	+ Under $50,000
	+ $50,000 - $100,000
	+ $100,000 - $250,000
	+ $250,000 - $500,000
	+ $500,000 - $1 million
	+ Over $1 million
	+ Flexible
* **Available Cash Reserves** (`cash_reserves`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the amount of cash reserves you have available for investment.
* **Financing Available or Needed** (`financing_available`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have financing available or need to explore options:
	+ All cash
	+ 20-50% down payment available
	+ Less than 20% down payment available
	+ Need 100% financing
	+ Unsure
* **Estimated Credit Score Range** (`credit_score_estimate`, `select_one`, optional): If you're comfortable sharing, please select your estimated credit score range:
	+ Below 600
	+ 600 - 650
	+ 650 - 700
	+ 700 - 750
	+ Above 750
	+ Not sure
* **Property Management Preference** (`property_management`, `select_one`, required): Select your preference for property management:
	+ Self-manage properties
	+ Hire professional management
	+ Want completely hands-off
	+ Undecided
* **Rental Property Management Experience** (`rental_experience`, `select_one`, optional): If you have experience managing rental properties, please select your level of experience:
	+ Extensive experience
	+ Some experience
	+ No experience
	+ Do not want to manage
* **Expected Return on Investment (ROI)** (`expected_roi`, `number`, optional): If you have an idea of the expected ROI, please enter the percentage per year.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `select_one`, required): Select your risk tolerance:
	+ Conservative
	+ Moderate
	+ Aggressive
	+ Very aggressive
* **Time You Can Commit** (`time_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Select your time commitment for real estate investing:
	+ Full-time real estate investor
	+ Part-time investor
	+ Minimal time - passive only
	+ Other arrangement
* **Additional Information About Your Investment Interests** (`additional_interests`, `text`, optional): Provide any other details that might be relevant to your investment goals and preferences.
* **Are You Ready to Invest in Qualified Deals?** (`ready_to_invest`, `select_one`, required): Select when you are ready to invest:
	+ Yes, ready immediately
	+ Yes, within 3-6 months
	+ Yes, within 12 months
	+ Exploring options
	+ Not yet ready
