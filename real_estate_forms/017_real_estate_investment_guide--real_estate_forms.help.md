# real_estate_investment_guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_investment_guide form is designed to collect information from clients about their investment preferences and goals. This information will help you provide personalized guidance and recommendations for their real estate investments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Client Preferences" page and provide a brief description of the client's preferences.
2.  On the "Financial Status" page, enter the client's current financial status, which will help us understand their financial situation.
3.  On the "Investment Goals" page, enter the client's investment goals, such as long-term or short-term investments.
4.  On the "Investment Purposes" page, enter the purpose of the investment, such as buying a house or paying off debt.
5.  On the "Financial Resources" page, enter the client's financial resources, such as income and assets.
6.  On the "Financial Status" page (second occurrence), enter any additional information about the client's financial status.
7.  On the "Risk Tolerance" page, select the client's risk tolerance level, from 0 to 10.
8.  On the "Investment Horizon" page, select the expected date for the investment.
9.  On the "Expected Return" page, enter the expected return on investment.
10.  On the "Investment Frequency" page, enter the frequency of the investment.
11.  On the "Financial Institution" page, select the financial institution the client is working with.
12.  On the "Contact Details" page, enter the client's contact information.
13.  On the "Email" page, enter the client's email address.
14.  On the "Notes" page, enter any additional notes about the client.
15.  On the "Real Estate License" page, enter the client's real estate license (if applicable).
16.  On the "Investment Type" page, select the type of investment the client is interested in.
17.  On the "Client Name" page, enter the client's name.
18.  On the "Client Address" page, enter the client's address.
19.  On the "Client City" page, enter the client's city.
20.  On the "Client State" page, enter the client's state.
21.  On the "Client Zip" page, enter the client's zip code.
22.  On the "Client Phone" page, enter the client's phone number.
23.  On the "Client Email" page, enter the client's email address (if not already entered).
24.  On the "Client Language" page, select the client's language.
