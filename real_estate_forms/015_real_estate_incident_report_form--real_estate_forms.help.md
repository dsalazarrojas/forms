# real_estate_incident_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_incident_report_form is a structured form designed for real estate agents or staff to report incidents that have occurred on properties under their management. This form ensures that all relevant information is collected in a single location, reducing duplication and improving incident management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the incident date and time when the incident occurred.
2. Enter the location where the incident happened, which is the specific address or location of the property.
3. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the incident description field.
4. Select the type of incident that occurred (Fire, Theft, Other).
5. List all witnesses who saw or were involved in the incident.
6. Select the current status of the incident (Open, Closed, In Progress).
7. If necessary, attach any images or videos related to the incident in the incident image and incident video fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (id: 2, required: false): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (id: 3, required: false): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Location** (id: 4, required: false): Enter the address or location of the property where the incident happened.
* **Incident Description** (id: 5, required: false): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Property Address** (id: 6, required: false): Enter the address of the property where the incident occurred.
* **Property Manager** (id: 7, required: false): Enter the name of the property manager.
* **Landlord** (id: 8, required: false): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Incident Type** (id: 9, required: true): Select the type of incident that occurred (Fire, Theft, Other).
* **Witnesses** (id: 10, required: true): List all witnesses who saw or were involved in the incident.
* **Incident Details** (id: 11, required: false): Provide additional information about the incident.
* **Action Taken** (id: 12, required: false): Enter any actions or steps taken after the incident.
* **Status** (id: 13, required: true): Select the current status of the incident (Open, Closed, In Progress).
* **Incident Image** (id: 14, required: false): Attach any images related to the incident.
* **Incident Video** (id: 15, required: false): Attach any videos related to the incident.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure effective incident management.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
