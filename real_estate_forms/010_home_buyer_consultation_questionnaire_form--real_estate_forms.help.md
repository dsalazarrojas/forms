# Home Buyer Consultation Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help home buyers provide their preferences and needs when searching for a new home. This information will be used by real estate agents and brokers to provide personalized recommendations and save time in the home search process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of home you are interested in, such as Single Family Home, Townhouse, or Condo.
3. Provide your budget for the new home.
4. Indicate the number of bedrooms and bathrooms you are looking for.
5. Choose the size of the home you prefer, including the number of floors and square footage.
6. Select your preferred location, including city, suburbs, or rural areas.
7. Provide your preferred move-in date or date range.
8. Choose the number of parking spaces and garage spaces you need.
9. Select the type of lot you prefer, including corner lot, linear lot, or other.
10. Indicate your desired view, such as ocean view, mountain view, or country view.
11. Choose the features you want in your new home, such as pool, gourmet kitchen, or high ceilings.
12. Describe the neighborhood characteristics you prefer, such as neighborhood fellowship, recreational activities, or affordability.
13. Select the housing type you prefer in the neighborhood, such as single-family homes, townhouses, or condos.
14. Choose the amenities you want in your new home, such as pool, gourmet kitchen, or large kitchen island.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (1)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you.
* **Home Type (2)**: Select the type of home you are interested in, such as Single Family Home, Townhouse, or Condo.
* **Budget (3)**: Enter your budget for the new home.
* **Number of Beds (4)**: Choose the number of bedrooms you are looking for.
* **Number of Bath (5)**: Enter the number of bathrooms you need.
* **Number of Floors (6)**: Choose the number of floors you prefer.
* **Square Footage (7)**: Enter the square footage of the home you prefer.
* **Neighborhood (8)**: Enter the neighborhood you are interested in, such as city, suburbs, or rural areas.
* **Preferred Move In Date (9)**: Enter your preferred move-in date or date range.
* **Preferred Move Out Date (10)**: Enter your preferred move-out date or date range.
* **Preferred Move Date Range (11)**: Enter your preferred move date range.
* **Home Location (12)**: Choose the location of your new home, such as city, suburbs, or rural areas.
* **Number of Parking Spaces (13)**: Enter the number of parking spaces you need.
* **Number of Garage Spaces (14)**: Enter the number of garage spaces you need.
* **Lot Size (15)**: Enter the size of the lot you prefer.
* **Lot Type (16)**: Choose the type of lot you prefer, such as corner lot or linear lot.
* **View (17)**: Choose the view you prefer, such as ocean view or mountain view.
* **Home Features (18)**: Choose the features you want in your new home, such as pool, gourmet kitchen, or high ceilings.
* **Nighborhood Characteristics (19)**: Describe the neighborhood characteristics you prefer, such as neighborhood fellowship or recreational activities.
* **Neighborhood Safety (20)**: Choose the safety level of the neighborhood you prefer.
* **Neighborhood Fellowship (21)**: Choose the level of neighborhood fellowship you prefer.
* **Neighborhood Recreation (22)**: Choose the level of recreational activities you prefer.
* **Neighborhood Affordability (23)**: Choose the affordability level of the neighborhood you prefer.
* **Neighborhood Housing Type (24)**: Choose the housing type you prefer in the neighborhood, such as single-family homes or townhouses.
* **Home Amenities (25)**: Choose the amenities you want in your new home, such as pool, gourmet kitchen, or large kitchen island.
