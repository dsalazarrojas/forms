# property_routine_inspection_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Routine Inspection Report form is a tool used by property inspectors to report on the condition of a house. This form gathers information on the property's current condition, including the inspector's findings and photos.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **formid**, which is the unique identifier for this inspection report.
2. Enter the **house_id**, the unique identifier for the house being inspected.
3. Choose a valid **date_of_inspection** for the date you conducted the inspection.
4. Select the **inspector_id**, your unique identifier.
5. Enter your **inspector_name**.
6. If applicable, enter your **client_id**, the unique identifier for the client.
7. Enter the address of the **house_address**.
8. Enter the **room number** where the inspection was conducted (e.g. 1, 2, 3, etc.).
9. Enter a **condition score** (out of 100) based on your assessment of the property's condition.
10. Provide your **comments** on the property's condition.
11. Check the **photos** box if you took photos during the inspection.
12. Choose a valid **date of sale** for the date the property was sold (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **formid**: Enter a unique identifier for this inspection report.
* **house_id**: Enter the unique identifier for the house being inspected.
* **date_of_inspection**: Choose a valid date for the inspection.
* **inspector_id**: Select your unique identifier.
* **inspector_name**: Enter your name as the inspector.
* **client_id**: If applicable, enter the client's unique identifier.
* **house address**: Enter the address of the house.
* **room number**: Enter the number of the room where the inspection was conducted.
* **condition score**: Enter a score (out of 100) of the property's condition.
* **comments**: Provide comments on the property's condition.
* **photos**: Check the box if you took photos during the inspection.
* **date of sale**: Choose a valid date for the date the property was sold (if applicable).
