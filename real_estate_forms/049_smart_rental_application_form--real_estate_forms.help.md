# smart_rental_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The smart rental application form is designed to collect essential information from prospective renters, making it easier for property managers or landlords to assess their eligibility and suitability for a rental unit.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:
- Click on the "Start Form" button to begin filling out the form.
- Each page is a separate section of the form, so navigate through them using the navigation buttons.
- Enter your information clearly and accurately in the provided fields.
- Use the "Submit" button to save your progress and proceed to the next page.
- Continue completing the form until you reach the last page, "Signature Page".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Rental Application
* **Rental Application** (text, required/optional): This field is for a brief introduction or description of your rental application.
### Page 2: Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information** (text, required/optional): Here, you can enter your contact details, including your name, email, and other relevant contact information.
### Page 3: Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (text, required/optional): Fill in your contact information, such as phone number and email address, so the landlord can reach you easily.
### Page 4: Property Details
* **Property Details** (text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe the specific rental property you are applying for, including its address and unit type.
### Page 5: Lease Terms
* **Lease Terms** (text, required/optional): Here, you can agree or negotiate the lease terms, such as rent amount, duration of lease, and other conditions.
### Page 6: Rental History
* **Rental History** (text, required/optional): Share your rental history, including past rental experiences and any relevant information about your rental background.
### Page 7: References
* **References** (text, required/optional): Provide information about your professional or personal references, such as your employer or landlord from previous rentals.
### Page 8: Emergency Contacts
* **Emergency Contacts** (text, required/optional): Enter the contact information of your emergency contacts, such as family members or emergency contacts.
### Page 9: Rental Agreement
* **Rental Agreement** (text, required/optional): This field is for you to agree or accept the terms of the rental agreement.
### Page 10: Rent Terms
* **Rent Terms** (text, required/optional): Here, you can discuss and agree on the rent terms and conditions.
### Page 11: Signature Page
* **Signature Page** (text, required/optional): This final page is for you to sign and date the rental agreement, confirming your acceptance of the rental terms.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
- Double-check your contact and lease terms to ensure clarity and consistency.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the property manager or landlord directly.
- Review and sign the final "Signature Page" to confirm your agreement.
