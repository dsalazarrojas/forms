# real_estate_legally_binding_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Legally Binding Agreement Form is a document used to create a formal agreement between a client and a real estate agent. This form outlines the terms and conditions of their representation, including client and agent information, agreement terms, termination, and fees.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reviewing the form carefully to understand its purpose and scope.
2.  Fill in the client and agent contact information (fields 2, 3, 4, 5).
3.  Specify the start and end dates of the agreement (fields 6, 7).
4.  Select the agreement term (field 8) and duration (field 9) that suits the transaction.
5.  Define the notice period and unit of measurement for termination (fields 10, 11).
6.  Specify the agent's fee and rate (fields 13, 14).
7.  Indicate the client's fee (field 15).
8.  Select the reason for termination (field 17).
9.  Specify the termination date and notice period (fields 18, 20).
10.  Fill in any additional termination reason details (field 21).
11.  Sign the form with the client's and agent's signature (fields 22, 23).
12.  Add any additional comments or notes about the agreement (field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_name** (field 2): Enter the name of the client. This field is optional but recommended for formal identification purposes.
* **client_address** (field 3): Enter the address of the client for communication purposes.
* **real_estate_agent_name** (field 4): Enter the name of the real estate agent or company. This field is optional but recommended for identification purposes.
* **real_estate_agent_address** (field 5): Enter the address of the real estate agent or company. This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
* **start_date** (field 6): Specify the start date of the agreement.
* **end_date** (field 7): Specify the end date of the agreement.
* **real_estate_agreement_term** (field 8): Select the term of the agreement from the options provided. This will define the scope of the representation.
* **real_estate_agreement_term_duration** (field 9): Select the duration of the agreement from the options provided. This will define the length of the agreement.
* **notice_period** (field 10): Enter the notice period required for termination.
* **notice_period_unit** (field 11): Select the unit of measurement for the notice period (e.g., days, months, years).
* **notice_period_duration** (field 12): Enter the notice period duration.
* **real_estate_agent_fee** (field 13): Enter the agent's fee for services.
* **real_estate_agent_fee_rate** (field 14): Enter the fee rate or percentage for the agent's fee.
* **client_fee** (field 15): Enter the client's fee for services.
* **termination_reason** (field 17): Select the reason for termination from the options provided.
* **termination_date** (field 18): Specify the termination date of the agreement.
* **termination_notice_period** (field 20): Enter the termination notice period required for termination.
* **termination_notice_period_unit** (field 21): Select the unit of measurement for the termination notice period.
* **termination_reason_details** (field 21): Enter any additional details about the termination reason.
* **client_signature** (field 22): Sign the form with the client's signature.
* **agent_signature** (field 23): Sign the form with the agent's signature.
* **termination_date_note** (field 24): Enter any additional comments or notes about the termination date.
* **real_estate_agreement_form_notes** (field 25): Enter any additional comments or notes about the agreement.

Note: The actual labels and field names may vary based on the field id in the YAML provided.
