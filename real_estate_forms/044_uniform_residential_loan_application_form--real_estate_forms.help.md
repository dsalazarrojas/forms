# Uniform Residential Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Residential Loan Application Form is a tool used to collect information from loan applicants to assess their eligibility for a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill in your loan application details on the "Loan Application" page.
3. Provide your contact information on the "Contact Information" page.
4. Enter details about your property on the "Property Details" page.
5. Select your employment status on the "Employment Status" page.
6. Choose your income sources on the "Income Sources" page.
7. Select the frequency of your income on the "Income Frequency" page.
8. Choose the methods you have used for income verification on the "Income Verification" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Loan Application** (`loan_application`, text, not required): Enter your loan application details, such as the loan amount, interest rate, and loan term.
* **Borrower Information** (`borrower_information`, text, not required): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, not required): Enter your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, not required): Enter details about the property you are applying for, such as its location and value.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or more of the following employment statuses: 
  * Employed
  * Self-employed
  * Retired
  * Disabled
  * Unemployed
  * Pursuing Higher Education
* **Income Sources** (`income_sources`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or more of the following income sources: 
  * Wages
  * Investments
  * Interest
  * Dividends
  * Rental
  * Other (please specify)
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, select_one, not required): Select the frequency of your income from the following options: 
  * Monthly
  * Bi-annually
  * Annually
  * Daily
  * Weekly
* **Income Verification** (`income_verification`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or more methods you have used for income verification from the following options: 
  * Payroll
  * Tax Return
  * Bank Statement
  * Social Security
  * Other (please specify)

## Tips
