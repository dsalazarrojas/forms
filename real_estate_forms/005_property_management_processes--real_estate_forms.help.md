# property-management-processes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "property-management-processes" form is used to document or update property records, collecting essential information about a property's type, status, address, and contact details of the manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property's type in the "property_type" field.
2. Select the current status of the property in the "property_status" field.
3. Enter the property's address in the "property_address" field.
4. Enter the contact phone numbers separated by commas in the "contact_number" field.
5. Enter the email addresses separated by commas in the "email_address" field.
6. Enter a brief description of the property in the "description" field.
7. Enter the manager's name in the "manager_name" field.
8. Enter the manager's email address in the "manager_email" field.
9. Enter the manager's phone number in the "manager_phone" field.
10. Enter the manager's address in the "manager_address" field.
11. Enter the date and time of submission in the "submission_date" field in the format "YYYY-MM-dd" and "HH:MM:SS" respectively.
11. Enter the time of submission in 24-hour format in the "submission_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_management**: Enter the name of the property being managed.
* **property_type**: Select the category or type of the property.
* **property_status**: Select the current status of the property (active, inactive, or pending).
* **property_address**: Enter the property's address in the required format.
* **contact_number**: Enter the property's contact phone numbers separated by commas.
* **email_address**: Enter the property's email addresses separated by commas.
* **description**: Provide a brief description of the property.
* **manager_name**: Enter the name of the property's manager.
* **manager_email**: Enter the email address of the property's manager.
* **manager_phone**: Enter the phone number of the property's manager.
* **manager_address**: Enter the address of the property's manager.
* **submission_date**: Enter the date of submission in the format "YYYY-MM-dd".
* **submission_time**: Enter the time of submission in 24-hour format.
