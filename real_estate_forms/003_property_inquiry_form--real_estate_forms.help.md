# property_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property inquiry form is used to collect and process inquiries about properties. It helps to determine the status and features of a specific property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of property from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
2. Indicate the source of the inquiry from the "Inquiry Source" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the address of the property in the "Property Address" field.
4. Select the date and time of the inquiry from the "Inquiry Date" and "Inquiry Time" fields, respectively.
5. Choose the current status of the property from the "Property Status" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the name of the customer who made the inquiry in the "Customer Name" field.
7. Enter the email address of the customer in the "Customer Email" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the customer in the "Customer Phone" field.
9. Enter any notable features of the property in the "Property Features" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property from the dropdown menu to help us understand the nature of the inquiry.
* **Inquiry Source**: Indicate where the inquiry came from, such as a phone call, email, or visit.
* **Property Address**: Enter the exact address of the property, including street number and postal code.
* **Inquiry Date**: Select the date the inquiry was made.
* **Inquiry Time**: Select the time the inquiry was made.
* **Property Status**: Choose the current status of the property, whether it's Active or Inactive.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer who made the inquiry.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer who made the inquiry.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer who made the inquiry.
* **Property Features**: Describe any notable features of the property, such as pool, view, etc.
