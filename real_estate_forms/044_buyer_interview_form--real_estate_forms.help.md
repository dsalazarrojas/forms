# Buyer Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from potential homebuyers to provide a customized home buying experience tailored to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your personal contact information**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can communicate with you efficiently.
2. **Choose your interview date**: Select the date and time you would like to have the interview.
3. **Identify your real estate agent**: Enter the name of the real estate agent assigned to assist you.
4. **Specify your buyer status**: Indicate whether you are a first-time or repeat homebuyer, investor, or relocating/downsizing.
5. **Determine your co-buyer status**: If there is another buyer involved, select "Co-Buyer Present" or "Co-Buyer to Follow".
6. **Choose your closing timeline**: Select the desired time frame to close the purchase.
7. **Indicate your financing status**: Select your current financing status: pre-approved, pre-qualified, needs pre-approval, or cash buyer.
8. **Enter your lender's name (if pre-approved)**: If you are pre-approved, provide the name of your lender.
9. **Set a maximum price**: Enter the maximum amount you are willing to spend on a property.
10. **Specify your down payment percentage**: Optional, but useful for your lender and real estate agent.
11. **Select property types**: Choose all the types of properties you are interested in (e.g., single-family detached, townhouse, condo, etc.).
12. **Choose the number of bedrooms**: Select the number of bedrooms you prefer or are willing to have.
13. **Enter location preferences**: Provide any specific cities, neighborhoods, or zip codes you have in mind.
14. **Specify your maximum commute distance**: If applicable, enter the maximum distance you are willing to commute (in miles or minutes).
15. **List priority features or amenities**: Specify any must-have features or amenities you are looking for in a property.
16. **Enter your property condition preference**: Select the level of property condition you are willing to accept (e.g., move-in ready, minor updates, etc.).
17. **Specify other preferences or constraints**: Enter any other essential details that might impact your homebuying experience.
18. **Agree on next steps**: Confirm the actions you are willing to take next.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Your full name as the buyer.
* **Email Address** (`buyer_email`, email, required): Your personal contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`buyer_phone`, text, required): Your phone number for communication.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required): The date and time you would like to have the interview.
* **Real Estate Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required): The name of your assigned real estate agent.
* **Buyer Status** (`buyer_status`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are a first-time or repeat homebuyer, investor, or relocating/downsizing.
* **Co-Buyer or Co-Signer** (`cobuyer_present`, select_one, required): Specify if you are a single buyer, co-buyer present, or co-buyer to follow.
* **Timeline to Purchase** (`timeline_to_purchase`, select_one, required): Select the desired time frame to close the purchase.
* **Financing Status** (`financing_status`, select_one, required): Select your current financing status (pre-approved, pre-qualified, needs pre-approval, etc.).
* **Lender Name** (`lender_name`, text, optional): If you are pre-approved, provide the name of your lender.
* **Maximum Purchase Price** (`maximum_price`, number, required): Enter the maximum amount you are willing to spend on a property.
* **Down Payment Percentage** (`down_payment_percent`, number, optional): Specify the percentage of down payment you are willing to make.
* **Property Types Interested In** (`property_type_wants`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the types of properties you are interested in (e.g., single-family detached, townhouse, etc.).
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`number_bedrooms`, select_one, required): Select the number of bedrooms you prefer or are willing to have.
* **Location Preferences** (`location_preferences`, text, required): Provide any specific cities, neighborhoods, or zip codes you have in mind.
* **Maximum Commute Distance** (`commute_distance`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the maximum distance you are willing to commute (in miles or minutes).
* **Priority Features or Amenities** (`priority_features`, text, optional): Specify any must-have features or amenities you are looking for in a property.
* **Property Condition Preference** (`property_condition_preference`, select_one, required): Select the level of property condition you are willing to accept (e.g., move-in ready, minor updates, etc.).
* **Other Preferences or Constraints** (`other_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any other essential details that might impact your homebuying experience.
* **Agreed Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Confirm the actions you are willing to take next.
