# Real Estate Counter Offer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for real estate agents to respond to a counter offer from a buyer. It helps to collect necessary information to address the buyer's counter offer and potentially negotiate further in the real estate deal.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant party (buyer or seller) from the "buyer_info" dropdown list. This will determine which party you are addressing.
2. Enter the property address, if applicable, in the "property_address" field.
3. Type the buyer's counter offer terms in the "counter_offer" field.
4. If applicable, specify the counter price in the "counter_price" field.
5. If applicable, enter the counter offer date in the "counter_date" field.
6. Select any terms that apply to the counter offer from the "counter_terms" dropdown list.
7. If you have any additional comments or notes regarding the buyer's counter offer, enter them in the "buyer_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **buyer_info (Select)**: Select the party to whom you are responding with this counter offer (Buyer or Seller).
* **property_address (Text)**: Enter the address of the property related to the counter offer.
* **counter_offer (Text)**: Enter the buyer's counter offer terms. This can be a brief description of their proposal.
* **counter_price (Number)**: If the buyer is proposing a price change, enter the new price.
* **counter_date (Date)**: If the counter offer has a specific date, enter it here.
* **counter_terms (Select)**: Select any terms that apply to the counter offer (Term 1, Term 2, Term 3).
* **buyer_note (Note)**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the buyer's counter offer.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form to ensure that you capture all necessary information.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the original buyer's counter offer for context.
* Save a copy of this form for your records for future reference.
