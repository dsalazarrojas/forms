# Resident Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Application Form is designed to collect information about a resident applying for housing or rental purposes. This form will help us assess your application and process it in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Please ensure you provide the most up-to-date information about yourself and your living situation.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (<label> First Name, `first_name`, Text): Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (<label> Last Name, `last_name`, Text): Enter your full last name.
* **Email** (<label> Email, `email`, Email, optional): Enter your email address to be contacted in case of any issues or queries.
* **Phone** (<label> Phone, `phone`, Text): Provide your contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Address** (<label> Address, `address`, Text): Enter the address where you're currently living or wish to live in the future. Include your street address, city, state, zip, and apartment number (if applicable).
* **City** (<label> City, `city`, Text): Enter the city where you're living in or planning to live in.
* **State** (<label> State, `state`, Select One): Select your state of residence.
* **Zip** (<label> Zip, `zip`, Text): Enter your zip code for accurate postal delivery.
* **Rent Start Date** (<label> Rent Start Date, `rent_date`, Date): Enter the date you plan to start renting the property.
* **Move-In Date** (<label> Move-In Date, `move_in_date`, Date): Enter the date you plan to move into the property.
* **Move-Out Date** (<label> Move-Out Date, `move_out_date`, Date): Enter the date you plan to move out of the property (if applicable).
* **Rent Amount** (<label> Rent Amount, `rent_amount`, Number): Enter the agreed-upon rent amount for the property.
* **Rent Frequency** (<label> Rent Frequency, `rent_frequency`, Select One): Select how often you pay rent, i.e., weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Rent Start Date** (<label> Rent Start Date, `rent_start_date`, Date): Enter the date you started renting the property (if applicable).
* **Lease Duration** (<label> Lease Duration, `lease_duration`, Number): Specify the length of your lease in months or years (if applicable).
* **Move-In Location** (<label> Move-In Location, `move_in_location`, Text): Describe the location where you moved into or plan to move into the property.
* **Move-In Type** (<label> Move-In Type, `move_in_type`, Select One): Select the type of housing you're moving into, i.e., single, double, triple, or four-plex.
* **Move-In Floor** (<label> Move-In Floor, `move_in_floor`, Number): Enter the floor number where you're or will be moving into (if applicable).
* **Move-In Elevator** (<label> Move-In Elevator, `move_in_elevator`, Select One): Indicate whether the property has an elevator or not.
* **Move-Out Floor** (<label> Move-Out Floor, `move_out_floor`, Number): Enter the floor number where you plan to move out of (if applicable).
* **Move-Out Elevator** (<label> Move-Out Elevator, `move_out_elevator`, Select One): Indicate whether the property has an elevator or not at the time of moving out.
* **Rent Status** (<label> Rent Status, `rent_status`, Select One): Indicate the status of your rent, i.e., paid, pending, or not paid.
* **Rent Type** (<label> Rent Type, `rent_type`, Select One): Select the type of rent you're paying, i.e., leased, month-to-month, or other.
* **Renter Type** (<label> Renter Type, `renter_type`, Select One): Select your type, i.e., individual, couple, family.
* **Signature** (<label> Signature, `signature`, Note): Please sign the form to acknowledge that you've filled it out accurately and completely.

**Tips:**

* Ensure you review all fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of your entries.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our team for clarification.
