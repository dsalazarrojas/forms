# <internal>LISTING SUBMISSION SHEET</internal> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect listing submission data for real estate properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as registered agent.
2.  Provide your professional email address for listing communications.
3.  Enter your direct contact number for urgent inquiries.
4.  Enter the property's full street address, including unit/suite if applicable.
5.  Select the primary property classification (House, Apartment, etc.).
6.  Provide the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the property.
7.  Enter the approximate livable area in square feet.
8.  Enter the original construction year.
9.  Enter the expected sale price.
10.  Select the current status of the property.
11.  Choose the desired listing start date.
12.  Check all applicable features of the property.
13.  Enter the proposed marketing budget.
14.  Enter the commission rate for sales activities.
15.  Add any additional notes about the listing.
16.  Confirm if management approval is required for the listing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Agent full name**: Enter your legal name as registered agent. 
*   **Agent email address**: Enter your professional email for listing communications. 
*   **Agent phone number**: Enter your direct contact number for urgent inquiries. 
*   **Property address**: Enter the full street address, including unit/suite if applicable. 
*   **Property type**: Select the primary property classification (House, Apartment, etc.). 
*   **Number of bedrooms**: Enter the count of all bedrooms that meet local building codes. 
*   **Number of bathrooms**: Enter the count of all bathrooms, including half-baths as 0.5. 
*   **Square footage**: Enter the approximate livable area in square feet. 
*   **Year built**: Enter the original construction year. 
*   **Listing price**: Enter the expected sale price in US dollars. 
*   **Property status**: Select the current condition of the property (Vacant, Occupied, etc.). 
*   **Listing date**: Enter the desired listing start date. 
*   **Property features**: Select all the features applicable to this property (Garage, pool, etc.). 
*   **Marketing budget**: Enter the estimated amount for marketing and promotional activities. 
*   **Commission rate**: Enter the percentage of sale price paid as commission. 
*   **Listing agent notes**: Add any special instructions, disclosures, or selling points about the listing. 
*   **Approval required**: Confirm if management approval is required for the listing.
*   **Does this listing require management approval?**: Check if special pricing or terms apply to this listing. 

Please review the form carefully and provide accurate information to ensure efficient processing of your listing.
