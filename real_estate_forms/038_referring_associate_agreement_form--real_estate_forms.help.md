# Referring Associate Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from associates who are referring partners or collaborators. It is intended to be used for partnerships, collaborations, or agreements between two or more parties. The form is a text-based template that allows associates to provide their details and confirm their agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide the title of the referring associate's form.
2. Enter the client's form, which may be another form used in conjunction with this one.
3. Confirm the binding agreement, which is a critical part of the partnership or collaboration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Title** (`title`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the title of the referring associate's form.
### **Referring Associate's Form** (`referring_associate_form`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the form used by the referring associate.
### **Client's Form** (`clients_form`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the client's form used in conjunction with this partnership or collaboration.
### **Binding Agreement** (`binding_agreement`, `text`, required: false)
Confirm the binding agreement for this partnership or collaboration.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully read and understand the terms before completing this form.
* Provide accurate and honest information about the referring associate and their form.
* Review and agree to the binding agreement carefully before signing off on this partnership or collaboration.
