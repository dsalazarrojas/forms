# Estate Plan Client Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information about the client and their family as part of the estate planning process. It's crucial to fill out this form accurately to provide personalized services and ensure that the estate plan is tailored to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the personal contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number).
2. If applicable, fill out the spouse's information (Spouse's First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number).
3. If applicable, list the first child's information (First Child's Name and Age) and any additional children.
4. If applicable, list any other dependents' information (Dependent's Name, Age, Email, and Phone Number).
5. Check the box for "Additional Dependents" if you have any other dependents not listed above.
6. Finally, review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (Text, Required)
Enter your first name.

### Last Name (Text, Required)
Enter your last name.

### Email (Email, Required)
Enter your email address.

### Phone Number (Text, Required)
Enter your phone number.

### Spouse's First Name (Text, Optional)
If you're married, enter your spouse's first name.

### Spouse's Last Name (Text, Optional)
If you're married, enter your spouse's last name.

### Spouse's Email (Email, Optional)
If you're married, enter your spouse's email address.

### Spouse's Phone Number (Text, Optional)
If you're married, enter your spouse's phone number.

### First Child's Name (Text, Optional)
If you have children, enter the first child's name.

### First Child's Age (Number, Optional)
If you have children, enter the first child's age.

### Last Child (Text, Optional)
If you have children, enter any additional children's names.

### Number of Children (Number, Optional)
If you have children, enter the total number of children you have.

### Dependent's First Name (Text, Optional)
If you have dependents, enter the first dependent's name.

### Dependent's Last Name (Text, Optional)
If you have dependents, enter the dependent's last name.

### Dependent's Age (Number, Optional)
If you have dependents, enter the dependent's age.

### Dependent's Email (Email, Optional)
If you have dependents, enter the dependent's email address.

### Dependent's Phone Number (Text, Optional)
If you have dependents, enter the dependent's phone number.

### Additional Dependents (Checkbox, Optional)
If you have other dependents not listed above, check this box.

### Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please reach out to your financial advisor or estate planning professional.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
