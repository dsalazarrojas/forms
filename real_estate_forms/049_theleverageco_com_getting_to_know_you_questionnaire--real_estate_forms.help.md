# TheLeverageCo.com | Getting To Know You Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your preferences, experiences, and property needs to provide you with the most suitable service from TheLeverageCo.com.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review each page and select the relevant options that match your preferences and experiences.
2. Enter your responses accurately and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the home you are inquiring about or would like to find.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in any communications with us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted at your convenience.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding any updates or inquiries.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the languages you prefer to communicate in (English, Spanish, French).
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about TheLeverageCo.com (Social Media, Online Search, Word of Mouth, Referral, Print Ad, Other).
* **When can we contact you?** (`when_can_we_contact_you`, time, optional): Choose a time of day when you are most available for contact.
* **What are you sending for?** (`what_are_you_sending_for`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of property you are inquiring about (Home, Apartment, Condo, Townhouse, Other).
* **Other Property Type** (`other_property_type`, text, optional): If the above options do not apply to you, please specify the type of property you are sending for.
* **How did you find us?** (`how_did_you_find_us`, select_one, optional): Select how you found TheLeverageCo.com (Website, Social Media, Word of Mouth, Referral, Online Search, Other).
* **Have you worked with us before?** (`have_you_worked_with_us_before`, select_one, optional): Select if you have worked with us before (True, False).
* **What brought you to us?** (`what_brought_you_to_us`, text, optional): Enter the reason you are reaching out to us.
* **What did you like most?** (`what_did_you_like_most`, text, optional): Enter the aspect you liked most about our service or experience.
* **What did you dislike?** (`what_did_you_dislike`, text, optional): Enter the aspect you disliked about our service or experience.
* **How can we improve?** (`how_can_we_improve`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions or improvements you have for our service.
* **Is it your first time buying?** (`is_it_your_first_time_buying`, select_one, optional): Select if this is your first time buying a property (True, False).
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, number, optional): Enter the number of children you have.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests you have.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`number_of_bedrooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property you are inquiring about.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`number_of_bathrooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property you are inquiring about.
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, optional): Enter the square footage of the property you are inquiring about.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, number, optional): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Lot Size** (`lot_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the lot the property sits on.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the property you are inquiring about.
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`, number, optional): Enter the price the property was sold for.
