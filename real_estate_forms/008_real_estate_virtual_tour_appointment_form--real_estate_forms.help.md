# Real Estate Virtual Tour Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Real Estate Virtual Tour Appointment Form is used to schedule a virtual tour for a potential client interested in a real estate property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the potential client.
2. Enter the name of the real estate business you are working with or interested in working with.
3. Select a tour date for the virtual tour.
4. Select a tour time for the virtual tour.
5. Enter your email address so that we can contact you with more information.
6. Enter your phone number for us to contact you with more information.
7. Add any additional notes about the appointment in the "Appointment Note" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. <label> Potential Client </label> (<name> potential_client, <type> text, required: false)
 Enter your name as the potential client.

### 2. <label> Real Estate Business </label> (<name> real_estate_business, <type> text, required: false)

 Select a tour date for the virtual tour.

### 4. <label> Tour Time </label> (<name> tour_time, <type> time, required: false)
 Select a tour time for the virtual tour.

### 5. <label> Potential Client Email </label> (<name> potential_client_email, <type> email, required: false)

### 6. <label> Potential Client Phone </label> (<name> potential_client_phone, <type> text, required: false)
 Enter your phone number for us to contact you with more information.

### 7. <label> Appointment Note </label> (<name> appointment_note, <type> note, required: false)
 Add any additional notes about the appointment in this field.

### 8. <label> Submit </label> (<name> submit, <type> text, required: false)
 Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth virtual tour experience.
* If you have any questions or need to reschedule the appointment, contact us directly.
* We will contact you with more information about the virtual tour once we receive your form submission.
