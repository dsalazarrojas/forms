# real_estate_development_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "real_estate_development_voting_form" is a user-facing form designed to collect feedback and opinions on real estate developments from end-users.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the type of property you are evaluating by choosing an option from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
3.  Briefly describe the project in the "Project Description" field.
4.  Enter a score between 1-100 to express your sentiment about the project.
5.  Provide a score between 1-100 to indicate the project's priority.
6.  Enter any updates or requests related to the project in the "Updates Requests" field.
7.  If you have any comments or thoughts about the project's sentiment, enter them in the "Sentiment Comment" field.
8.  Choose the current status of the project from the "Project Status" dropdown menu.
9.  Enter the project's email address for contact purposes.
10.  Enter the project's phone number for further contact.
11.  Add any additional comments or notes about the project in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Property Type**: Select the type of property being evaluated (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
*   **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
*   **Project Description**: Briefly describe the project in this field.
*   **Sentiment Score**: Enter a score between 1-100 to express your sentiment about the project.
*   **Priority Score**: Enter a score between 1-100 to indicate the project's priority.
*   **Updates Requests**: Enter any updates or requests related to the project.
*   **Sentiment Comment**: Provide a comment about the project's sentiment.
*   **Project Status**: Choose the current status of the project (Under Review, Under Construction, Completed, Cancelled).
*   **Email**: Enter the project's email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter the project's phone number for further contact.
*   **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the project.

## Tips
*   Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Project Description" field.
*   Be honest when entering your sentiment and priority scores.
*   Enter accurate contact information for the project to ensure proper communication.
*   Keep the "Note" field for any additional comments that don't fit in other fields.
