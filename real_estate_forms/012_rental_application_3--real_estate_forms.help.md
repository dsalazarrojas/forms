# rental_application_3 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The rental application form is used to gather information from users who are interested in 
renting a property. This form is essential for processing their application and matching 
them with available properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of rental you are looking for from the "Rent type" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the type(s) of property you are interested in from the "Property type" field.
7. Select the desired move-in date and time from the calendars and clock.
8. Once you have filled all the fields, review your information for accuracy and correctness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Rent type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of rental you are interested in, such as 
  single-family home, apartment, or shared accommodation.
* **Property type** (select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of property you are 
  interested in, such as house, apartment, or condo.
* **Move in date** (date, required): Select the desired move-in date using the calendar.
* **Move in time** (time, required): Select the desired move-in time using the clock.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid delays in processing your 
  application.
- If you need help or have questions, please contact our support team.
- Once you have submitted your application, you will receive a confirmation email with 
  further instructions.
