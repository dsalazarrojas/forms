# Lot Survey Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on a lot survey, gathering information about the lot's status, price, size, location, features, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the current status of the lot from the options provided (Sold, Under-construction, Available, Leased).
2. Enter the price of the lot in numeric format.
3. Enter the size of the lot in square meters.
4. Describe the location of the lot.
5. List any notable features of the lot.
6. Select the condition of the lot from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
7. Choose whether or not you recommend the lot for sale or rent.
8. Enter any additional notes about the lot survey.
9. Provide your name.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Enter the name of the lot owner.
13. Enter the email address of the lot owner.
14. Enter the phone number of the lot owner.
15. Enter the name of the surveyor who conducted the survey.
16. Choose the date the survey took place.
17. Enter the time of day the survey took place.
18. Enter any additional notes about the survey.
19. Enter your title on the survey.
20. Enter the name of the company you work for on the survey.
21. Enter your position on the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the current status of the lot?** (`lot_survey`, text, required): Enter the current status of the lot, such as Sold, Under-construction, Available, or Leased.
* **What is the status of the lot?** (`lot_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the lot from the options provided.
* **What is the price of the lot?** (`lot_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the lot in numeric format.
* **What is the size of the lot in square meters?** (`lot_size`, number, required): Enter the size of the lot in square meters.
* **What is the location of the lot?** (`lot_location`, text, optional): Describe the location of the lot.
* **What are the notable features of the lot?** (`lot_features`, text, optional): List any notable features of the lot.
* **What is the condition of the lot?** (`lot_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select the condition of the lot from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Do you recommend this lot for sale or rent?** (`lot_recommendation`, select_one, optional): Choose whether or not you recommend the lot for sale or rent.
* **Any notes on the lot?** (`lot_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the lot.
* **What is your name?** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **What is your email address?** (`agent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`agent_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **What is the name of the lot owner?** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the lot owner.
* **What is the email address of the lot owner?** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the lot owner.
* **What is the phone number of the lot owner?** (`user_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the lot owner.
* **Who conducted the lot survey?** (`lot_surveyor`, text, required): Enter the name of the surveyor who conducted the survey.
* **When did the lot survey take place?** (`lot_survey_date`, date, required): Choose the date the survey took place.
* **What time of day did the lot survey take place?** (`lot_survey_time`, time, required): Enter the time of day the survey took place.
* **Any notes on the lot survey?** (`lot_survey_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the survey.
* **What title do you hold on the lot survey?** (`lot_surveyor_title`, text, optional): Enter your title on the survey.
* **Which company do you work for on the lot survey?** (`lot_surveyor_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company you work for on the survey.
* **What is your position on the lot survey?** (`lot_surveyor_position`, text, optional): Enter your position on the survey.
