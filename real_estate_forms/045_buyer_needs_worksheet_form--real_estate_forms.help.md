## Buyer Needs Worksheet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from buyers about their needs and preferences for a property purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the page-by-page instructions below. Make sure to fill in all required fields and provide as much detail as possible for your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name** (id: 1)
	+ A text field to input your full name.
* **Email Address** (id: 2)
	+ A field to input your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 3)
	+ A field to input your contact phone number.
* **Co-Buyer Name (if applicable)** (id: 4)
	+ A field to input the name of a co-buyer (if applicable).
* **Worksheet Date** (id: 5)
	+ A date field to input when this worksheet was completed.
* **Real Estate Agent Name** (id: 6)
	+ A text field to input the name of your real estate agent.
* **Current Housing Situation** (id: 7)
	+ A select-one field to choose your current housing situation.
* **Total Available Budget** (id: 8)
	+ A number field to input your total available budget.
* **Down Payment Available** (id: 9)
	+ A number field to input the amount of down payment available.
* **Pre-Approval Amount** (id: 10)
	+ A number field to input the maximum loan amount (if pre-approved).
* **Lender Name** (id: 11)
	+ A text field to input the name of the lender (if pre-approved).
* **Preferred Loan Type** (id: 12)
	+ A select-one field to choose your preferred loan type.
* **Desired Property Type** (id: 13)
	+ A select-multiple field to choose the types of properties you are interested in.
* **Minimum Number of Bedrooms** (id: 14)
	+ A number field to input the minimum number of bedrooms required.
* **Minimum Number of Bathrooms** (id: 15)
	+ A number field to input the minimum number of bathrooms required.
* **Desired Square Footage Range** (id: 16)
	+ A text field to input the range of square footage required.
* **Location Must-Haves** (id: 17)
	+ A text field to input the must-have features in a location.
* **Must-Have Property Features** (id: 18)
	+ A text field to input the must-have features in a property.
* **Areas of Flexibility** (id: 19)
	+ A text field to input areas where you can compromise.
* **Desired Timeline for Closing** (id: 20)
	+ A select-one field to choose your desired timeline for closing.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible for each field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your real estate agent.
- This form will help tailor the agent's services to your needs, providing you with a personalized experience.
