# Apartment Inspection (Matris) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apartment Inspection (Matris) form is used to gather information about the condition of a rental property. This includes various aspects such as exterior and interior features, plumbing and electrical systems, and overall habitability. The form is designed to guide inspectors through the inspection process and provide a comprehensive report of their findings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Inspection ID**, a unique identifier for each inspection.
2. Select the **Inspection Date**, the date on which the inspection took place.
3. Enter the **Inspector Name**, the name of the person performing the inspection.
4. Enter the **Inspector Company**, the name of the inspection company.
5. Enter the **Property Address**, the full address of the inspected property.
6. Enter the **Apartment Unit Number**, the identifier for the inspected unit.
7. Select the **Property Type** (e.g., Apartment, Condominium, etc.).
8. Enter the **Unit Size** (optional, if applicable).
9. Enter the **Number of Bedrooms** and **Number of Bathrooms**, as applicable.
10. Select the **Type of Inspection** (e.g., Pre-occupancy, Move-in, etc.).
11. Indicate if **Occupants were present** during the inspection.
12. Select the **Weather Conditions** during the inspection.
13. Complete the **Exterior Inspection** section, evaluating the condition of the building's exterior, entrance, and lobby.
14. Complete the **Interior Inspection** section, evaluating the condition of the interior, walls, ceiling, flooring, windows, doors, kitchen, bathroom, and electrical systems.
15. Complete the **Electrical Inspection** section, evaluating the condition of electrical outlets, light fixtures, heating and cooling systems, and safety equipment.
16. Complete the **Safety Inspection** section, evaluating the condition of smoke detectors, carbon monoxide detectors, fire extinguishers, and locks.
17. Enter any **Critical Issues**, **Major Issues**, **Minor Issues**, and **Repairs Needed** that were found during the inspection.
18. Enter an estimated **Repair Cost**, if applicable.
19. Determine if the unit is **Habitable**, and if not, provide an assessment.
20. Enter any **Recommendations** for repairs or maintenance.
21. Select if a **Follow-up is required**, and if so, the timeframe.
22. Enter your **Inspector Signature**, as a digital signature.
23. If present, enter the **Resident Signature**, as a digital signature.
24. Enter the **Inspection Completion Date**, the date the inspection was finalized.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection ID** (1): A unique identifier for each inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (2): The date on which the inspection took place.
* **Inspector Name** (3): The name of the person performing the inspection.
* **Inspector Company** (4): The name of the inspection company.
* **Property Address** (5): The full address of the inspected property.
* **Apartment Unit Number** (6): The identifier for the inspected unit.
* **Property Type** (8): The type of property (e.g., Apartment, Condominium, etc.).
* **Unit Size** (9): The size of the unit (optional, if applicable).
* **Number of Bedrooms** (10): The number of bedrooms in the unit.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (11): The number of bathrooms in the unit.
* **Type of Inspection** (12): The purpose of the inspection (e.g., Pre-occupancy, Move-in, etc.).
* **Were Occupants Present?** (13): Were occupants present during the inspection.
* **Weather Conditions** (14): The conditions during the inspection (e.g., Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy, etc.).
* **Building Exterior Condition** (16): Evaluate the condition of the building's exterior.
* **Entrance and Lobby** (17): Evaluate the condition of the entrance and lobby.
* **Hallways and Stairwells** (18): Evaluate the condition of the hallways and stairwells.
* **Lighting in Common Areas** (19): Evaluate the functionality of lighting in common areas.
* **Walls Condition** (21): Evaluate the condition of the walls.
* **Ceiling Condition** (22): Evaluate the condition of the ceiling.
* **Flooring Condition** (23): Evaluate the condition of the flooring.
* **Windows Condition** (24): Evaluate the condition of the windows.
* **Doors Condition** (25): Evaluate the condition of the doors.
* **Kitchen Cabinets** (27): Evaluate the condition of kitchen cabinets.
* **Countertops** (28): Evaluate the condition of countertops.
* **Sink and Plumbing** (29): Evaluate the condition of the sink and plumbing.
* **Appliances** (30): Select all appliances that are present and working.
* **Toilet Functionality** (32): Evaluate the condition of the toilet.
* **Shower or Tub** (33): Evaluate the condition of the shower or tub.
* **Bathroom Ventilation** (34): Evaluate the condition of the bathroom ventilation system.
* **Water Pressure** (35): Evaluate the overall pressure of the water system.
* **Electrical Outlets** (37): Evaluate the functionality of electrical outlets.
* **Light Fixtures** (38): Evaluate the functionality of light fixtures.
* **Heating System** (40): Evaluate the condition of the heating system.
* **Cooling System** (41): Evaluate the condition of the cooling system.
* **Smoke Detectors** (43): Evaluate the condition of smoke detectors.
* **Carbon Monoxide Detector** (44): Evaluate the condition of carbon monoxide detectors.
* **Fire Extinguisher** (45): Evaluate the condition of fire extinguishers.
* **Locks and Security** (46): Evaluate the condition of door and window locks.
* **Critical Issues** (47): Describe any critical issues found during the inspection.
* **Major Issues** (48): Describe any major issues found during the inspection.
* **Minor Issues** (49): Describe any minor issues found during the inspection.
* **Repairs Needed** (50): List all repairs needed.
* **Estimated Repair Cost** (51): Enter an estimated cost for repairs, if applicable.
* **Habitable** (53): Determine if the unit is habitable or not.
* **Recommendations** (54): Enter any suggested actions or repairs.
* **Follow-up Required** (55): Determine if a follow-up is required.
* **Inspector Signature** (56): Enter a digital signature as the inspector.
* **Resident Signature** (57): Enter a digital signature as the resident, if present.
* **Inspection Completion Date** (58): The date the inspection was finalized.
