# Landlord Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Questionnaire is designed to collect information from landlords about their properties and lease agreements. This form is used to gather data for property management, tenant relationships, and lease agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the property and landlord.
2. Enter the contact information of the landlord.
3. Fill out the property address.
4. Specify the tenant's preferences.
5. Enter the lease term details.
6. Provide the security deposit amount.
7. Enter the move-in and move-out dates.
8. Specify the rental income and frequency.
9. Choose the lease end date(s) and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the property and landlord.
* **Contact Info** (email, required: false): Enter the landlord's email address.
* **Property Address** (text, required: false): Enter the property address.
* **Tenant Preferences** (text, required: false): Specify any special requirements or needs of the tenant.
* **Lease Term** (text, required: false): Enter the lease term details.
* **Security Deposit** (number, required: false): Enter the security deposit amount.
* **Move In Date** (date, required: false): Choose the move-in date from the list of options.
* **Move Out Date** (date, required: false): Choose the move-out date from the list of options.
* **Rental Income** (number, required: false): Enter the rental income amount.
* **Rent Frequency** (select_one, required: false): Choose the rent frequency (monthly or weekly).
* **Lease End Date** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose the lease end date(s).
* **Lease Status** (select_one, required: false): Choose the lease status (active or inactive).
* **Goals** (select_multiple, required: false): Specify if the landlord has any goals for the property (yes or no).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately to ensure correct data collection.
* Review and verify the information before submitting the form.
* Contact the property management team if you have any questions or need assistance.
