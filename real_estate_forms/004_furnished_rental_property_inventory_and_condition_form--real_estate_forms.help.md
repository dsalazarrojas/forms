# Furnished Rental Property Inventory And Condition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather detailed information about a furnished rental property, including its address, current occupancy status, type, size, rent amount, move-in and move-out dates, utilities, condition, and contact details. This information will be used to create a comprehensive inventory and condition report for the property.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Property Details Page (Page 1)
Complete this page with the property's address, which is usually its physical location.

### Step 2: Occupancy Status Page (Page 2)
Choose the current occupancy status from the options provided:

*   Landlord: The property is currently owned or managed by the landlord.
*   Tenant: The property is currently rented to a tenant.
*   Vacant: The property is not currently occupied by anyone.

### Step 3: Property Information Page (Page 3)
Enter the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, etc.).

### Step 4: Rental Information Page (Page 4)
Enter the property size and rent amount.

### Step 5: Maintenance and Services Page (Page 5)
Select all the utilities that are included with the rent (electricity, gas, water, internet, cable, etc.).

### Step 6: Contact Information Page (Page 6)
Enter the contact details (name, phone number, email address, address) of the person responsible for the property.

### Step 7: Notes and Status Page (Page 7)
Add any additional notes or status updates about the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **property_address** (Required)
This field is for the physical address of the property. Enter the property's street number, street name, city, state, and zip code.

### 2. **current_occupancy_status** (Required)
Choose the current occupancy status of the property from the options provided.

### 3. **property_type** (Required)
Enter the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, etc.).

### 4. **property_size** (Required)
Enter the size of the property in square feet.

### 5. **property_status** (Required)
Choose the property status from the options provided:

*   Active: The property is available for rent.
*   Sold: The property has been sold.
*   Off Market: The property is not available for rent.

### 6. **rent_amount** (Required)
Enter the monthly rent amount.

### 7. **move_in_date** (Required)
Enter the date the tenant moved in.

### 8. **move_out_date** (Required)
Enter the date the tenant moved out.

### 9. **goals** (Required)
Enter any goals for the property's maintenance or updates.

### 10. **utilities** (Required)
Select all the utilities that are included with the rent (electricity, gas, water, internet, cable, etc.).

### 11. **condition** (Required)
Enter a detailed description of the property's condition.

### 12. **notes** (Optional)
Add any additional notes about the property.

### 13. **contact_number** (Required)
Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### 14. **email_address** (Required)
Enter the email address of the contact person.

### 15. **contact_person** (Required)
Enter the name of the contact person.

### 16. **contact_address** (Required)
Enter the contact address.

### 17. **property_manager** (Required)
Choose the property manager from the options provided.

### 18. **property_status_notes** (Required)
Add any additional notes about the property's status.

### 19. **contact_email** (Required)
Enter the email address of the contact person.

### 20. **condition_notes** (Required)
Enter any additional notes about the property's condition.

### 21. **contact_notes** (Optional)
Add any additional notes about the contact person.

### 22. **notes_for_property_owner** (Required)
Add any additional notes for the property owner.

### 23. **utilities_notes** (Required)
Add any additional notes about the utilities.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the relevant sections above for guidance.
*   Ensure you have the necessary permissions and information to complete this form accurately.
