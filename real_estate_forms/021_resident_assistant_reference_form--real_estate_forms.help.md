# resident_assistant_reference_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The resident_assistant_reference_form is a reference form used to gather essential information from residents in a building or facility. The form is meant to be completed by residents to provide accurate and up-to-date information about their living situation, contact details, and preferences. This information will be used by building staff to effectively assist residents and communicate with them.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully before starting to complete it.
2.  Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (1) (`general_info`, text, optional):
    Enter a brief description of your living situation, including any relevant details about your current address, living status (e.g., tenant, owner, guest), and other important information that may help the building staff assist you more effectively.
* **Resident Info** (2) (`resident_info`, text, optional):
    Provide your name, preferred contact method (email, phone number, or both), and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (3) (`contact_info`, text, optional):
    Enter your contact information, including your preferred method of contact (email, phone number, or both), and any other relevant details about your contact preferences.
* **Assigned Staff** (4) (`assigned_staff`, select_multiple, optional):
    Select the staff member(s) assigned to assist you, which may include a resident assistant, building staff, or other personnel.
* **Staff Comments** (5) (`staff_comments`, text, optional):
    Add any additional comments or requests to your assigned staff member(s) to ensure they understand your needs and preferences.
* **Resident Comments** (6) (`resident_comments`, text, optional):
    Enter any comments or feedback about your living situation or the building's services that may be helpful for the staff to know.
* **Resident Signature** (7) (`resident_signature`, text, optional):
    Please sign this form to confirm that you have reviewed and provided accurate information.
* **Staff Signature** (8) (`staff_signature`, text, optional):
    The staff member will sign this form to confirm that they have received and reviewed your information.

## Tips

*   Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you need help or have questions about any field, don't hesitate to ask your assigned staff member or contact building management.
*   Make sure to keep your contact information up to date to ensure effective communication with the building staff.
