# RRG_SFL_NYSAR_COVID_19_Health_Travel_Questionnaire_04_01_2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect health-related information from individuals who have traveled, with the aim of gathering data about their travel history, health status, and potential exposure to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel History**: This section is used to gather information about your recent travel history.
* **Have you had a fever**: This question is asking if you have experienced a fever in the past week.
* **Symptoms**: This question is asking if you have experienced any symptoms related to COVID-19, such as cough, sore throat, or difficulty breathing.
* **Traveled outside of US**: This question is asking if you have traveled outside of the United States.
* **Have you experienced any symptoms**: This question is asking if you have experienced any symptoms related to COVID-19.
* **Close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case**: This question is asking if you have had close contact with someone with a confirmed COVID-19 case.
* **Test result**: This question is asking if you have tested positive or negative for COVID-19.
* **Have you been quarantined**: This question is asking if you have been quarantined due to COVID-19 exposure.
* **Health care provider**: This question is asking about your healthcare provider for COVID-19 treatment.
* **Contact information**: This is an optional field where you can provide contact information if you are willing.
* **Who issued your test result**: This question is asking who issued your test result (e.g., healthcare provider, laboratory, etc.).
* **Test date**: This is the date when your test was conducted.
* **Travel date**: This is the date when you traveled.
* **Where did you travel**: This question is asking about the destination of your recent travel.
* **How long did you travel**: This is an optional field where you can specify the duration of your travel.
* **Type of travel**: This question is asking the type of travel (e.g., business, leisure, etc.).
* **Purpose of travel**: This question is asking the purpose of your travel.
* **Contact name**: This is an optional field where you can provide the name of your contact person.
* **Contact number**: This is an optional field where you can provide the contact number of your contact person.
* **Email**: This is an optional field where you can provide your email address.
* **Have you a health condition**: This question is asking if you have a pre-existing health condition.
* **Health condition**: This is an optional field where you can provide more information about your health condition.
* **Have you been vaccinated**: This question is asking if you have received a COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Type of vaccine**: This question is asking the type of vaccine you received.

## Tips

* Ensure that you answer all required questions carefully and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for advice.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider.
