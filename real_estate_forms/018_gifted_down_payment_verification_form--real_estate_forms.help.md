# Gifted Down Payment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gifted Down Payment Verification Form is a form used to verify the gifted down payment on a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of down payment details you are verifying (Option 1 or Option 2)
2. Select the source of the funds (Option 3 or Option 4)
3. Enter the address of the property
4. Enter the amount of the down payment
5. Select the date of transfer (if applicable)
6. Enter the name of the transferor
7. Enter the address of the transferor (if applicable)
8. Upload proof of funds
9. Upload the agreement document

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Down Payment Details** (gifted_down_payment_details, select_one, required): Select the type of down payment details you are verifying.
* **Funds Source** (gifted_funds_source, select_multiple, required): Select the source of the funds for the down payment.
* **Property Address** (gifted_property_address, text, required): Enter the address of the property that the down payment is for.
* **Down Payment Amount** (gifted_down_payment_amount, number, required): Enter the amount of the down payment.
* **Date of Transfer** (gifted_date_of_transfer, date, optional): Enter the date of transfer (if applicable).
* **Transferor Name** (gifted_transferor_name, text, required): Enter the name of the individual or entity that gifted the down payment.
* **Transferor Address** (gifted_transferor_address, text, optional): Enter the address of the transferor (if applicable).
* **Proof of Funds** (gifted_proof_of_funds, text, required): Upload proof of funds for the down payment.
* **Agreement Document** (gifted_agreement_document, text, required): Upload the agreement document for the down payment.
