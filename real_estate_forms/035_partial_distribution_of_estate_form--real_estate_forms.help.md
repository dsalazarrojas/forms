# Partial Distribution Of Estate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the partial distribution of a real estate estate, ensuring that the executor and beneficiaries have a clear understanding of the distribution plan and details regarding the assets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Beneficiary Info** section with the names and contact information of the beneficiaries who will receive the partial distribution.
2. Select the **Asset Info** options to indicate whether the assets will be distributed or not.
3. Provide the **Distribution Plan** details in the text field.
4. If applicable, fill out the **Date of Death** field with the date of the decedent's passing.
5. In the **Executor Info** section, select one or more executors who will handle the distribution.
6. Enter the **Share Distribution** details, specifying the percentage of the assets being distributed.
7. Enter the **Asset Value**, which is the value of the assets being distributed.
8. Provide the **Estate Value**, which is the total value of the estate.
9. If relevant, fill out the **Tax Year**, which indicates the tax year the distribution applies to.
10. Finally, fill out the **Partial Distribution Date**, which is the date the partial distribution will occur.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Beneficiary Info** (Required): Enter the name and contact information of the beneficiary or beneficiaries who will receive the partial distribution.
- **Asset Info** (Required): Select "Yes" if the assets will be distributed or "No" if not.
- **Distribution Plan** (Required): Describe the distribution plan, including any specific instructions or conditions for the distribution.
- **Date of Death** (Optional): If the decedent has passed away, enter the date of death.
- **Executor Info** (Required): Select one or more executors who will handle the distribution.
- **Share Distribution** (Required): Enter the percentage of the assets being distributed.
- **Asset Value** (Required): Enter the value of the assets being distributed.
- **Estate Value** (Required): Enter the total value of the estate.
- **Tax Year** (Optional): If applicable, enter the tax year the distribution applies to.
- **Partial Distribution Date** (Required): Enter the date the partial distribution will occur.
