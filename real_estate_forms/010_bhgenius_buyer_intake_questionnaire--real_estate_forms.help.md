# BHGenius Buyer Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "BHGenius Buyer Intake Questionnaire" is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information from homebuyers to understand their preferences and priorities when searching for a new property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and honestly to help us understand your needs and priorities.
2. Enter your contact information at the end of the form to stay informed about listings and updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name, as we would like to address you personally.
- **Email Address** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone Number** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your best phone number for us to contact you.
- **Current Address** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your current address for reference.
- **Buying Timeline** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select the timeline for your home purchase:
	+ Within 1 month
	+ 1 to 3 months
	+ 3 to 6 months
	+ 6 to 12 months
	+ Just looking
- **First Time Home Buyer** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Check "True" if this is your first time buying a home, or "False" otherwise.
- **Mortgage Pre-Approval Status** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Check the status of your pre-approval:
	+ Yes, pre-approved
	+ In progress
	+ Not yet started
	+ Paying cash
- **Minimum Price Range** (`8`, `number`, required): Enter the lowest price you would consider for your new home.
- **Maximum Price Range** (`9`, `number`, required): Enter the highest price you would consider for your new home.
- **Property Type Preference** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property you prefer:
	+ Single Family Home
	+ Townhouse
	+ Condominium
	+ Multi-Family
	+ Land
	+ Any
- **Number of Bedrooms** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of bedrooms you need:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 or more
- **Number of Bathrooms** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of bathrooms you need:
	+ 1
	+ 1.5
	+ 2
	+ 2.5
	+ 3 or more
- **Desired Square Footage** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select the minimum square footage you would consider:
	+ Under 1000
	+ 1000 to 1500
	+ 1500 to 2000
	+ 2000 to 3000
	+ Over 3000
- **Preferred Locations or Neighborhoods** (`14`, `text`, required): List the areas or neighborhoods you are interested in.
- **Must-Have Features** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the features you absolutely need:
	+ Garage
	+ Basement
	+ Fireplace
	+ Hardwood floors
	+ Updated kitchen
	+ Master suite
	+ Fenced yard
	+ Pool
	+ Central air
- **Nice to Have Features** (`16`, `text`, optional): If you have a list of features you would like but do not absolutely need.
- **Deal Breakers** (`17`, `text`, optional): If you have any deal breakers (things that would make you walk away from a property).
- **Current Housing Situation** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Select your current living situation:
	+ Renting
	+ Owning
	+ Living with family
	+ Other
- **Reason for Moving** (`19`, `text`, optional): If you have a reason for your home search, please describe it.
- **Are You Working with Other Agents** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Check "True" if you have signed a buyer agreement with another agent, or "False" otherwise.
- **How Did You Hear About Us** (`21`, `select_one`, required): How did you learn about our services:
	+ Online search
	+ Social media
	+ Friend or family
	+ Previous client
	+ Advertisement
	+ Other
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Phone call
	+ Text message
	+ Email
	+ Any method
- **Best Time to Contact** (`23`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred time to contact, enter it here.
- **Additional Comments or Questions** (`24`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
