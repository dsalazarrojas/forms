# Tenant Income Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Tenant Income Declaration Form" is used to gather financial information from tenants to help landlords, property managers, or rental administrators make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

* Read each field carefully and answer honestly to ensure accurate and thorough financial information.
* Ensure you answer all required fields to avoid form submission issues.
* If you have trouble with any question, contact your rental administrator for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Income** (`id: 2`): A numerical value representing the total household income per month, including any additional sources of income, such as alimony, dividends, or other financial support.
* **Monthly Net Rental Income** (`id: 3`): A numerical value representing the total income from renting out the current property, including any rental income received from other properties or businesses.
* **Employment Status** (`id: 4`): A brief description of your employment status, including employed, self-employed, or unemployed.
* **Employment Type** (`id: 5`): Select your employment type from the options provided: employed, self-employed, or unemployed.
* **Business Occupation** (`id: 6`): If applicable, provide a brief description of your business occupation or business type.
* **Annual Income** (`id: 7`): A numerical value representing the annual income, including any bonuses or commissions received.
* **Other Income** (`id: 8`): Enter any other sources of income not mentioned above, including but not limited to, dividends, interest, or other financial support.
* **Dependant Support** (`id: 9`): A numerical value representing any dependant support you receive per month, including spousal or child support.
* **Monthly Expenses** (`id: 10`): A numerical value representing any regular, monthly expenses you incur.
* **Rent** (`id: 11`): Select 'yes' or 'no' if you pay rent for any other properties or have any other rent-related expenses.
* **Utilities** (`id: 12`): Select 'yes' or 'no' if you have any utility-related expenses.
* **Other Expenses** (`id: 13`): Select 'yes' or 'no' if you have any other regular expenses not mentioned above.
* **Vehicle Expenses** (`id: 14`): Select 'yes' or 'no' if you have any vehicle-related expenses.
* **Other Vehicle Expenses** (`id: 15`): Enter a numerical value for any other vehicle expenses not mentioned above.
* **Total Rent** (`id: 16`): A numerical value representing the total rent paid per month.
* **Total Expenses** (`id: 17`): A numerical value representing the total, regular monthly expenses, including but not limited to, rent, utilities, vehicle expenses, and other expenses.

Please ensure you answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your rental administrator for assistance.
