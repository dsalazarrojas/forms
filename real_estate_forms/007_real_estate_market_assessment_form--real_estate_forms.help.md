# Real Estate Market Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Market Assessment Form is a tool used by real estate professionals to collect and analyze data on properties. It is intended to be used for market assessment and valuation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the property type from the options provided under "Property Type".
2.  Enter the age of the property in years under "Property Age". 
3.  Enter the size of the property in square feet under "Property Size".
4.  Select the current status of the property from the options provided under "Property Status".
5.  Enter any relevant details about the neighborhood, such as notable features or amenities, under "Neighborhood Details".
6.  Select the type of neighborhood from the options provided under "Neighborhood Type".
7.  Enter any relevant information about market trends, such as current demand or market conditions, under "Market Trends".
8.  Enter the current value of the property under "Property Value".
9.  Enter any relevant information about the current market conditions, such as current listings or sales, under "Market Conditions".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Type** (`Property Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property from the options provided. This will help the real estate professionals to better understand the type of property being assessed. 
- **Property Age** (`Property Age`, number, required): Enter the age of the property in years. This will help to determine the property's age and potential impact on its value.
- **Property Size** (`Property Size`, number, required): Enter the size of the property in square feet. This will help to understand the property's size and its impact on its value.
- **Property Status** (`Property Status`, select_multiple, required): Select the current status of the property from the options provided. This will help to understand the property's market status and potential impact on its value.
- **Neighborhood Details** (`Neighborhood Details`, text, required): Enter any relevant details about the neighborhood, such as notable features or amenities. This will help to understand the property's surroundings and its impact on its value.
- **Neighborhood** (`Neighborhood`, text, required): Enter the name of the neighborhood. This will help to understand the property's location and its impact on its value.
- **Neighborhood Type** (`Neighborhood Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of neighborhood from the options provided. This will help to understand the property's neighborhood type and its impact on its value.
- **Market Trends** (`Market Trends`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about market trends, such as current demand or market conditions. This will help to understand the property's market conditions and its potential value.
- **Property Value** (`Property Value`, number, required): Enter the current value of the property. This will help to determine the property's value and its impact on its market performance.
- **Market Conditions** (`Market Conditions`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the current market conditions, such as current listings or sales. This will help to understand the property's market conditions and its potential value.
