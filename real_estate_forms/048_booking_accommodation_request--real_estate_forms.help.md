# Booking Accommodation Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Booking Accommodation Request" form is designed for guests to submit their accommodation requests, including their personal details, accommodation preferences, and any special requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your booking.
2.  Provide your address, city, and state to help us find the most suitable accommodation for you.
3.  Choose your preferred accommodation type, such as Studio, One Bedroom, Two Bedroom, etc.
4.  Indicate your bed preference, including King Bed, Queen Bed, Two Double Beds, etc.
5.  If you have any special requests, such as requiring a washing machine or pet-friendly accommodation, select them from the options provided.
6.  Specify your estimated arrival time and the number of guests in your party.
7.  Select your preferred payment method, such as credit card, debit card, bank transfer, or cash on arrival.
8.  If you have any additional comments or requests, provide them in the text field.
9.  Confirm that you agree to the booking terms by selecting "Yes I agree" from the options.
10.  Finally, select the date you are requesting to stay and the number of nights you would like to book.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmation about your booking. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for booking coordination. (Required)
* **Street Address**: Enter your home address to help us find the most suitable accommodation. (Optional)
* **City**: Enter the city you will be staying in. (Optional)
* **State**: Enter the state you will be staying in. (Optional)
* **Postal Code**: Enter your ZIP code to find the most suitable accommodation. (Optional)
* **Country**: Select your country of origin. (Optional)
* **Check-In Date**: Choose the date you will arrive for your booking. (Required)
* **Check-Out Date**: Choose the date you will depart from your booking. (Required)
* **Number of Nights**: Specify the total number of nights you would like to stay. (Required)
* **Number of Guests**: Indicate the total number of guests in your party. (Required)
* **Number of Adults**: Specify the number of adults. (Required)
* **Number of Children**: Specify the number of children. (Optional)
* **Ages of Children**: List the ages of the children in your party. (Optional)
* **Accommodation Type**: Select your preferred accommodation type, such as Studio or Three Bedroom. (Required)
* **Bed Preference**: Choose your preferred bed type, such as King Bed or Two Double Beds. (Optional)
* **Special Requests**: List any special requirements you may have for your stay, such as pet-friendly or washing machine. (Optional)
* **Amenities Needed**: Select any amenities you require, such as WiFi or Parking. (Optional)
* **Purpose of Stay**: Specify why you are staying, such as leisure or business. (Optional)
* **Will You Bring a Pet**: Select if you will be bringing a pet. (Required)
* **Pet Details**: Describe your pet, if you are bringing one. (Optional)
* **Estimated Arrival Time**: Provide your estimated arrival time. (Optional)
* **Do You Need Airport Transportation**: Select if you need airport transportation. (Optional)
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method, such as credit card or cash. (Required)
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information or requests. (Optional)
* **I Agree to Booking Terms**: Confirm that you agree to the booking terms. (Required)
* **Request Date**: Choose the date you are requesting to stay. (Required)
