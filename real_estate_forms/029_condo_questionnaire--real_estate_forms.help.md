# Condo Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist users in completing the Condo Questionnaire form. The form is intended to gather information about your preferences and requirements for a condo, and it will help real estate agents and brokers better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your personal information, including your name.
2. Select your unit number (if applicable).
3. Choose your purpose for purchasing or renting a condo.
4. Indicate your desired number of bedrooms.
5. Choose your budget range for the condo.
6. Select any must-have features for the condo.
7. Specify your relocation timeline.
8. If you're working with a real estate agent, provide their contact information.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This is your name and contact information.
* **Unit number** (`unit_number`, text, optional): If you have a specific unit number in mind, please enter it here.
* **Purpose of interest** (`interest_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose why you're interested in a condo:
	+ Buying
	+ Renting
	+ Investing
	+ Other
* **Desired number of bedrooms** (`desired_bedrooms`, number, optional): Enter the minimum number of bedrooms you're looking for.
* **Budget range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select a budget range for your condo:
	+ Under 200k
	+ 200k to 400k
	+ 400k to 600k
	+ Above 600k
* **Must have features** (`must_have_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select any features that are non-negotiable for you:
	+ Parking
	+ Balcony
	+ In unit laundry
	+ Pet friendly
* **Relocation timeline** (`relocation_timeline`, select_one, optional): When do you plan to move:
	+ Immediately
	+ 1 to 3 months
	+ 3 to 6 months
	+ More than 6 months
* **Real estate agent contact** (`agent_contact`, text, optional): If you're working with a real estate agent, enter their contact information.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any other preferences or comments you have about your condo search.
