# Real Estate Listing Boost Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for a real estate listing boost campaign. Please fill out this form to provide required information and express your interest in the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk *).
3. Use the provided options for select fields to choose the most accurate answer.
4. Double-check your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Commitment** (`participant_commitment`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of your commitment to the campaign.
* **Current Listings** (`current_listings`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select the listings you currently have available for promotion.
* **Target Listings** (`target_listings`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select the listings you would like to target with the campaign.
* **Campaign Rules** (`campaign_rules`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose a campaign rule that aligns with your target listings.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `note`, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about your agreement.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address to receive updates and information about the campaign.
* **Agreement Confirmation** (`agreement_confirmation`, `text`, optional):
	+ Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the campaign.
* **Other Field** (`other_field`, `text`, optional):
	+ Note: The "Agreement" field serves as a catch-all for any additional information or terms and conditions you'd like to share or acknowledge. This field is not intended to replace any existing agreements or contracts.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have read and understand all campaign rules before selecting an option.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy to receive important updates from the campaign organizers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the campaign organizers.
