# TheLeverageCo.com | Sellers Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TheLeverageCo.com | Sellers Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information from sellers about their property, contact details, and other relevant information. This form is intended for use by real estate agents or property managers to streamline the buying or selling process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill in the required fields on each page, starting with the "Main" page.
3. Move on to the next page, "Seller Info", and fill in the required fields.
4. Continue to the next page, "Property", and fill in any relevant information.
5. Move on to the "Contact" page and fill in the required contact information.
6. On the "Confirm" page, answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Each field has a specific purpose, explained below:

* **Main** (`id: 1`): This page is where you'll start filling out the form. It's an optional page, but filling it out will help us understand your overall situation.
* **Seller Info** (`id: 2`): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name and address.
* **Property** (`id: 3`): Describe the property you're interested in selling or buying. This page is optional, so only fill it out if relevant.
* **Needs** (`id: 4`): If you're selling, describe what you're looking for in a buyer. If you're buying, describe what you're looking for in a property.
* **Contact** (`id: 5`): Fill in your contact information, including email and phone numbers.
* **Email** (`id: 6`): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`id: 7`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Other Contact Info** (`id: 8`): If you have any other contact information, such as a website or social media handle, please enter it here. This page is optional.
* **Notes** (`id: 10`): Add any additional notes or comments about your situation.
* **Confirm** (`id: 12`): Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm your information.
* **Confirm Question** (`id: 13`): Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm your questions.
* **Confirm Answer** (`id: 14`): Enter your answer to the confirm question.
* **Confirm Answer Notes** (`id: 15`): Add any additional notes or comments about your answer.
* **Confirm Date** (`id: 16`): Enter the date you're looking to complete the transaction.
* **Confirm Time** (`id: 17`): Enter the time you're available to complete the transaction.
* **Confirm Location** (`id: 18`): Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm your location.
* **Confirm Location Question** (`id: 19`): Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm your location question.
* **Confirm Location Answer** (`id: 20`): Enter your location.
* **Confirm Location Notes** (`id: 21`): Add any additional notes or comments about your location.
* **Other Location Info** (`id: 22`): If you have any other location information, enter it here. This page is optional.
* **Confirm Other** (`id: 23`): Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm other information.
* **Confirm Other Notes** (`id: 24`): Add any additional notes or comments about other information.
* **Confirm Other Question** (`id: 25`): Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm other question.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each page completely before moving on to the next one.
* If you have any doubts about a field, please ask our support team for assistance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
