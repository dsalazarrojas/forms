# real_estate_license_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate License Extension Form is designed to assist real estate professionals in submitting a request for an extension of their real estate license. This form collects essential information required for processing the extension request and helps ensure that you meet the necessary requirements for a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Select the status of your current real estate license.
2. Indicate the type of license you hold.
3. Enter your license number.
4. Provide your license renewal date and expiration date.
5. Decide on the extension period.
6. Enter the renewal fee details, including the amount and currency.
7. Select the certification status for the extension period and fee.
8. Confirm your license status after the extension.
9. Indicate the type of license renewal.
10. Review and confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Real Estate License Status** (1, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of your real estate license. This will help us understand your current position and process your extension request accordingly.
* **Real Estate License Type** (2, select_one, required: false): Indicate the type of license you hold. This information is essential for us to verify your credentials.
* **Real Estate License Number** (3, text, required: false): Enter your real estate license number. This is a unique identifier for your license and helps us to locate your record.
* **License Renewal Date** (4, date, required: false): Provide the date of your last license renewal. This helps us to understand your renewal history.
* **License Expiration Date** (5, date, required: false): Enter the expiration date of your current license. This is crucial for determining the extension period.
* **License Expiration Date Certification** (6, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the expiration date has been certified or not.
* **Renewal Fee** (7, number, required: false): Enter the amount of the renewal fee you are paying.
* **Renewal Fee Currency** (8, select_one, required: false): Select the currency of the renewal fee you are paying.
* **Other** (9, text, required: false): If your renewal fee is not in any of the listed currencies, provide a detailed explanation here.
* **Real Estate License Extension Form Renewal Fee Currency Other** (10, text, required: false): If you selected "Other" for the renewal fee currency, provide the actual currency here.
* **Licensed Since** (11, number, required: false): Enter the number of years you have been licensed.
* **License Expiration Date Extension** (12, date, required: false): Enter the new expiration date after the extension period.
* **Extension Period** (13, select_one, required: false): Decide on the extension period you require.
* **License Renewal Status** (14, select_one, required: false): Indicate your current license renewal status.
* **License Status After Extension** (15, select_one, required: false): Confirm your license status after the extension.
* **Renewal Type** (16, select_one, required: false): Select the type of license renewal you are requesting.
* **Renewal Fee Amount** (17, number, required: false): Enter the amount of the renewal fee.
* **License Expiration Date Extension Certification** (18, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the extension period has been certified or not.
* **Renewal Fee Currency Certification** (19, select_one, required: false): Select whether the renewal fee currency has been certified or not.
* **Real Estate License Extension Form Renewal Fee Other Certification** (20, text, required: false): If your renewal fee currency certification is different from the listed options, provide a detailed explanation here.
* **Submit** (21, text, required: false): Once you have completed all the fields, click this button to submit your form.
