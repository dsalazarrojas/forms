# mortgage_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive mortgage application form designed to gather information about the applicant's identity, property, and financial situation to facilitate a mortgage application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: Enter your first name, last name, email, phone, and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide property information: Select the type of property you are applying for, indicate the size of the property, and enter any additional notes if necessary.
3. Enter financial information: Enter your income, list your assets, and enter the value of your assets (if applicable).
4. Specify loan details: Enter the loan amount, select the loan term, choose the loan type, and select the interest rate.
5. Provide additional contact information: Enter your email and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Property type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property you are applying for (e.g., minimalist, luxury, vacation).
* **Property size** (`property_size`, select_multiple, required): Select the size of the property (e.g., 1000-5000, 5001-10000, 10001-20000).
* **Income** (`income`, number, required): Enter your annual income.
* **Assets** (`assets`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of assets you have (e.g., Cash, Savings, Stocks, Bonds, Real estate).
* **Assets value** (`assets_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of your assets (if applicable).
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Date of birth year** (`date_of_birth_year`, number, optional): Enter the year you were born (if applicable).
* **Time of birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth (if applicable).
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender (e.g., Male, Female, Other).
* **Marital status** (`marital_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select your marital status (e.g., Single, Married, Divorced, Widow, Widow(er)).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes to the loan officer.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes (if applicable).
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter your additional email address (if applicable).
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter your additional phone number (if applicable).
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Enter your additional address (if applicable).
* **Loan amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required): Enter the loan amount you are applying for.
* **Loan term** (`loan_term`, select_one, required): Select the loan term (e.g., 30 years, 60 years).
* **Loan type** (`loan_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of loan you are applying for (e.g., Fixed, Adjustable, Variable).
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, select_one, required): Select the interest rate (e.g., 3-5, 6-8, 9-11).
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the assigned tool (e.g., ChatJimmy, Other).
* **Note 3 and 4** (`note`, note, optional, but not actually present in the original form): I'm assuming this one is a leftover from the previous version of the form and has been removed. If it's actually present, please let me know, and I'll add it accordingly.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any additional information that is not included in this form, please enter it in the notes field.
* If you need any help or clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us.
