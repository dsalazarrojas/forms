# Home Insurance Quote - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a home insurance quote, typically used by real estate agents or brokers to provide clients with relevant insurance options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your name, address, phone number, and email address to identify yourself.
- Select the type of insurance you are interested in (Home, Renters, or Auto).
- Choose your desired coverage level (High or Low).
- Enter your date of birth, if applicable.
- Specify if you have visited the property at a specific time (though this field is not required).
- Enter any additional notes or comments about your insurance needs.
- You can also provide additional details about the assigned tool, output file, and category for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Insurance Quote**: A unique identifier for the form.
* **Name**: Your name for the form submission.
* **Address**: The address associated with the form.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Insurance Type**: Choose between Home, Renters, or Auto insurance.
* **Coverage Type**: Select between Yes or No for coverage type, though this field may not be clear in its purpose.
* **Coverage Level**: Select between High or Low for coverage level.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth, if applicable.
* **Time of Visit**: The time of your visit, though this field is not required.
* **Note**: Any additional comments or notes about your insurance needs.
* **Assigned Tool**: The tool assigned to handle the form submission.
* **Output File**: The output file associated with the form.
* **Form ID**: A unique identifier for the form, not to be filled in by the user.
* **Category**: The category for the form.
* **Description**: A description for the form.
* **Output File Name**: The name of the output file.
* **Form Title**: The title for the form.
* **Assigned Tool 2**: The second tool assigned to handle the form submission.
* **Category 2**: The second category for the form.
* **Description 2**: A description for the second form.
* **Form Id 2**: A unique identifier for the second form, not to be filled in by the user.
* **Output File 2**: The second output file associated with the form.
* **Assigned Tool 3**: The third tool assigned to handle the form submission.
* **Description 3**: A description for the third form.

**Tips** 
- Ensure you have filled in all required fields accurately.
- Double-check your selections for insurance type, coverage level, and coverage type.
- Enter your date of birth if applicable.
- The "Time of Visit" field is not required and should not affect your quote if left blank.
- Be as specific as possible when entering notes or comments.
- The form is designed to guide the insurance quoting process, and filling it out accurately will help agents or brokers provide you with the best quotes.
