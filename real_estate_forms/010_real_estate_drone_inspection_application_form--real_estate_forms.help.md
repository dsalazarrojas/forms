# Real Estate Drone Inspection Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for real estate drone inspections. It provides a structured format for collecting information about properties, clients, and drone inspection specifics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the address of the property being inspected.
* Enter the name of the client or property owner.
* Enter the property ID number (if applicable).
* Provide a brief description of the address where the inspection took place (if necessary).
* Enter the date of the drone inspection.
* Choose whether or not to include a contact person in the inspection report.
* Enter the contact phone number and email address of the contact person (if applicable).
* Enter the email address of the inspector or organization.
* Provide any additional comments or details about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the client or property owner.
* **Property ID** (`property_id`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the ID number of the property (if applicable).
* **Address 2** (`address2`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide a brief description of the address where the inspection took place (if necessary).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of the drone inspection.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose whether or not to include a contact person in the inspection report.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter the email address of the inspector or organization.
* **Address 3** (`address3`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any additional details about the address (if necessary).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter the contact email address of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Drone Inspection Type** (`drone_inspection_type`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose the type of drone inspection being conducted (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
* **Drone Inspection Date** (`drone_inspection_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of the drone inspection.
* **Drone Inspection Time** (`drone_inspection_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time of the drone inspection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any additional comments or details about the inspection.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information to ensure a thorough inspection report.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by real estate professionals for real estate drone inspections.
