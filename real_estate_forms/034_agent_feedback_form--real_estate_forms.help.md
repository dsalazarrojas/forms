# Agent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their interactions with real estate agents. This form helps to evaluate the agent's performance in several key areas, including communication, timeliness, local market knowledge, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name.
2.  Enter the agent's name.
3.  Select the type of transaction (e.g., Sale, Purchase, Rental).
4.  Rate the agent's communication, timeliness, local market knowledge, and overall satisfaction.
5.  Rate the likelihood of referring the agent to others.
6.  Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client providing feedback.
*   **Agent name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the agent being evaluated.
*   **Transaction type** (`transaction_type`, select\_one, required): Choose the type of transaction (e.g., Sale, Purchase, Rental).
*   **Communication rating** (`communication_rating`, select\_one, required): Rate the agent's communication clarity (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Timeliness rating** (`timeliness_rating`, select\_one, required): Rate the agent's responsiveness and timeliness (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Local market knowledge** (`local_market_knowledge`, select\_one, required): Rate the agent's expertise in the local market (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select\_one, required): Rate your final satisfaction with the agent (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied).
*   **Referral likelihood** (`referral_likelihood`, select\_one, required): Indicate your likelihood of referring the agent to others (Definitely, Probably, Maybe, Unlikely).
*   **Additional comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any other feedback or comments about the agent's performance.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete all required fields to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the agent's performance.
*   Use the rating scale consistently to ensure accurate and comparable feedback.
*   Add any additional comments to provide context and insights about the agent's performance.
*   This form is intended to provide helpful feedback for the agent. Please be constructive and respectful in your comments.
