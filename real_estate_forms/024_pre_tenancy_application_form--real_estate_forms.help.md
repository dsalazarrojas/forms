# Pre Tenancy Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Tenancy Application Form is used to gather information from prospective tenants to process their rental application. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to increase the chances of your application being successful.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:

1. Tenant Information: Please provide your basic identification and contact details.
2. Contact Information: You can choose how you would like to be contacted.
3. Renter History: Share any relevant information about your previous renting experiences.
4. Rental Preferences: Select the type of property you are looking to rent.
5. Property Type: Choose the type of property you are looking to rent.
6. Renter Information: This is a text box for any additional information you would like to share.
7. Contact Method: Choose how you would like to be contacted.
8. Move In Date: Select the date you would like to move in.
9. Move In Time: Select the time you would like to move in.
10. Lease Length: Specify the length of your desired lease.
11. Rent Frequency: This is a text box for any additional information about your rent payment schedule.
12. Rent Amount: Specify the amount you are willing to pay for rent.
13. Security Deposit: Specify the amount you are willing to pay as a security deposit.
14. Move Out Status: Choose the move out status of your application.
15. Move Out Date: Select the date you would like to move out.
16. Move Out Time: Select the time you would like to move out.
17. Notes: This is a text box for any additional information you would like to share.
18. Additional Information: This is a text box for any additional information you would like to share.
19. Emergency Contact: Provide your emergency contact person's name.
20. Emergency Contact Name: Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
21. Emergency Contact Phone: Provide the phone number of your emergency contact person.
22. Emergency Contact Email: Provide the email of your emergency contact person.
23. Relationship: Specify your relationship with your emergency contact person.
24. Relationship Type: Specify the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact person.
25. Property Address: Provide the address of the property you are renting.
26. Move Out Status: Choose the move out status of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information (1)**: This is where you will need to provide your basic identification and contact details. This includes your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Here, you can choose how you would like to be contacted. You can select phone, email, or in person.
* **Renter History (3)**: Please share any relevant information about your previous renting experiences, such as any evictions or issues with previous landlords.
* **Rental Preferences (4)**: Select the type of property you are looking to rent, such as studio, 1-4 bedrooms, etc.
* **Property Type (5)**: Choose the type of property you are looking to rent, such as single family home, apartment, condo, etc.
* **Renter Information (6)**: This is a text box for any additional information you would like to share about yourself.

* **Move Out Date (15)**: Select the date you would like to move out.
* **Move Out Time (16)**: Select the time you would like to move out.
* **Notes (17)**: This is a text box for any additional information you would like to share.
* **Additional Information (18)**: This is a text box for any additional information you would like to share.
* **Emergency Contact (19)**: Provide your emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Name (20)**: Provide the name of your emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out this form completely and accurately to increase the chances of your application being successful.
* Please note that the move in and move out dates and times are not set in stone and can be adjusted as needed.
* You can add any additional information you would like in the "Notes" and "Additional Information" text boxes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
