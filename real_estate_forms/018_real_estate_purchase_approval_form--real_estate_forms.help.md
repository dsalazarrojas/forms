# real_estate_purchase_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_purchase_approval_form is a document used to obtain approval for the purchase of real estate properties. It is a required document that ensures the property purchase process is efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information required for the form. Fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any mistakes or missing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **real_estate_purchase_form** (1, text, not required): This field is where you enter the real estate purchase form.
- **property_address** (2, text, not required): Enter the property address.
- **property_type** (3, select_one, required): Choose the type of property from the options: residential, commercial, or agricultural.
- **purchase_price** (4, number, required): Enter the purchase price.
- **property_status** (5, select_one, required): Choose the property status from the options: active, inactive, or pending.
- **property_condition** (6, select_one, required): Choose the property condition from the options: excellent, good, average, or poor.
- **property_features** (7, note, not required): Enter any notable features of the property.
- **purchase_date** (8, date, required): Enter the date of purchase.
- **property_value** (9, number, required): Enter the value of the property.
- **property_area** (10, number, required): Enter the area of the property.
- **property_size** (11, number, required): Enter the size of the property.
- **property_owner_name** (12, text, not required): Enter the name of the property owner.
- **property_owner_address** (13, text, not required): Enter the address of the property owner.
- **property_owner_phone** (14, text, required): Enter the phone number of the property owner.
- **property_owner_email** (15, email, required): Enter the email of the property owner.
- **property_owner_relationship** (16, select_one, required): Choose the relationship between you and the property owner from the options: friend, family, or colleague.
- **property_inspection_fee** (17, number, required): Enter the inspection fee.
- **property_inspection_fee_frequency** (18, select_multiple, required): Choose the frequency of property inspection from the options: once, twice, or quarterly.
- **property_inspector_comments** (19, note, not required): Enter any comments from the property inspector.
- **property_inspector_signature** (20, text, required): Enter the signature of the property inspector.
- **property_approver_signature** (21, text, required): Enter the signature of the property approver.
- **property_purchase_approval** (22, note, not required): Enter any approval notes.
- **property_rejection_reason** (23, note, not required): Enter any rejection reason.
- **property_additional_comments** (24, note, not required): Enter any additional comments.
- **property_reviewed_by** (25, text, not required): Enter the person who reviewed the property.
