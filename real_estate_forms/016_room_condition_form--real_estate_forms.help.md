# room condition form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Room Condition Form is designed to gather information about the condition of a room or property. This form helps assess the state of the room, including any damages or issues that need to be addressed. By completing this form, users will help maintain accurate and up-to-date records of property conditions, facilitating efficient maintenance and repair processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling in the Room Details section**, providing a brief description of the room or property being assessed.
2. In the **Photos** section, attach any relevant images or photos that demonstrate the condition of the room or property.
3. For the **Marks** section, select all the marks or imperfections that apply to the room or property from the provided options.
4. In the **Damages** section, choose the damages or defects that exist in the room or property from the given options.
5. For the **Issues** section, select any issues or problems that need to be addressed in the room or property.
6. In the **Notes** section, provide any additional comments or observations about the room or property condition.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as specific as possible when describing the condition of the room or property.
* Attach clear and relevant photos to support your observations.
* Select all applicable marks, damages, and issues to ensure accurate assessment.
* Keep the notes section concise and focused on the most critical information.
* Complete the form for each room or property separately, as needed.
