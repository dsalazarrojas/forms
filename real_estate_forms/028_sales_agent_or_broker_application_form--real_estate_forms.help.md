# Sales Agent Or Broker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential sales agents or brokers who are interested in working with the real estate company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Provide truthful and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your full name, address, and phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Employment Information** (`Employment Information`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your current or most recent employment history.
* **Years of Experience** (`Years of Experience`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of years you have been working as a sales agent or broker.
* **Employment Status** (`Employment Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your current employment status, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Previous Companies** (`Previous Companies`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, list your previous companies you have worked for.
* **Previous Company 1** (`Previous Company 1`, `text`, required: false): If you listed 'Previous Companies', enter the name of your first previous company.
* **Previous Company 2** (`Previous Company 2`, `text`, required: false): If you listed 'Previous Companies', enter the name of your second previous company.
* **Previous Company 3** (`Previous Company 3`, `text`, required: false): If you listed 'Previous Companies', enter the name of your third previous company.
* **Education Information** (`Education Information`, `text`, required: false): Provide your education background, including degree level and field of study.
* **Degree Level** (`Degree Level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your degree level, either 'High' or 'Low'.
* **Institution** (`Institution`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your educational institution.
* **Field of Study** (`Field of Study`, `text`, required: false): Enter your field of study.
* **Personal Information** (`Personal Information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal information, including your full name, address, and birth date.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Birth Date** (`Birth Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your birth date.
* **License Information** (`License Information`, `text`, required: false): Provide your license information, including license type and number.
* **License Type** (`License Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your license type, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **License Number** (`License Number`, `number`, required: false): Enter your license number.
* **License Status** (`License Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your license status, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
