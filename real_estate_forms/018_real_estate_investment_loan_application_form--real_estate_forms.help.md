# real_estate_investment_loan_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from real estate investors and loan applicants to pre-qualify loan applicants for real estate investments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Provide details about the loan you are applying for in the "Loan Details" section.
4. Specify the type of property you are applying for in the "Property Type" section.
5. Enter the property's location in the "Property Location" section.
6. Enter the loan amount, term, and rate in the "Loan Amount", "Loan Term", and "Loan Rate" sections respectively.
7. Enter the loan status, purpose, and security type in the "Loan Status", "Loan Purpose", and "Loan Security" sections.
8. Enter the property's value, address, city, state, zip, and country in the "Property Value", "Property Address", "Property City", "Property State", "Property Zip", and "Property Country" sections.
9. Enter the loan date and time in the "Loan Date" and "Loan Time" sections.
10. Enter any additional loan notes or comments in the "Loan Note" section.
11. Finally, enter your email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (1)**: This section is used to capture your contact information including name, email, and phone number.
* **Loan Details (2)**: This section is used to capture information about the loan you are applying for including the loan amount, term, and rate.
* **Property Location (3)**: This section is used to capture the location of the property you are applying for.
* **Property Type (4)**: This section is used to capture the type of property you are applying for, such as Apartment, House, or Other.
* **Loan Amount (5)**: This section is used to capture the loan amount you are applying for.
* **Loan Term (6)**: This section is used to capture the loan term in years.
* **Loan Status (7)**: This section is used to capture the current status of your loan application.
* **Loan Purpose (8)**: This section is used to capture the purpose of your loan application.
* **Property Value (9)**: This section is used to capture the value of the property you are applying for.
* **Credit Score (10)**: This section is used to capture your credit score.
* **Income (11)**: This section is used to capture your income.
* **Loan Rate (12)**: This section is used to capture the loan rate.
* **Loan Fees (13)**: This section is used to capture any fees associated with the loan.
* **Loan Security (14)**: This section is used to capture the type of loan security you are applying for, such as Collateral, Non-Collateral, or Unsecured.
* **Property Address (15)**: This section is used to capture the address of the property you are applying for.
* **Property City (16)**: This section is used to capture the city of the property you are applying for.
* **Property State (17)**: This section is used to capture the state of the property you are applying for.
* **Property Zip (18)**: This section is used to capture the zip code of the property you are applying for.
* **Property Country (19)**: This section is used to capture the country of the property you are applying for.
* **Loan Date (20)**: This section is used to capture the date of the loan.
* **Loan Time (21)**: This section is used to capture the time of the loan.
* **Loan Note (22)**: This section is used to capture any additional comments or notes about the loan.
* **Email (23)**: This section is used to capture your email address.
* **Phone (24)**: This section is used to capture your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool (25)**: This section is used to capture the tool assigned to you.

# Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact the loan officer.
* Please be aware that the form is subject to change, and any updates will be communicated to you accordingly.
