# Commission Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Commission Approval Form is used to obtain approval for a commission request from a salesperson. This form collects information about the commission request, including the agent's name, property address, transaction amount, commission percentage, and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the agent's name in the "Agent name" field.
2. Enter the property address in the "Property address" field.
3. Enter the transaction amount as a whole number.
4. Select the commission percentage from the options provided.
5. Specify the name of the person required to approve the commission request.
6. Select the current status of the commission request from the options provided.
7. Optionally, enter any additional comments or notes about the approval.
8. Enter the date when the commission request was made.
9. (Optional) Enter the name of the person approving the commission request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agent name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the salesperson requesting approval.
* **Property address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address related to the commission request.
* **Transaction amount** (`transaction_amount`, number, required): Enter the whole number amount of the commission request.
* **Commission percentage** (`commission_percentage`, number, required): Enter the numeric percent value for the commission request.
* **Approval required by** (`approval_required_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or department required to approve the commission request.
* **Approval status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the commission request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Approval notes** (`approval_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the approval (optional).
* **Date of request** (`date_of_request`, date, required): Enter the date when the commission request was made.
* **Approver name** (`approver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person approving the commission request (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please review the form carefully or seek assistance from a supervisor.
* This form is intended for new commission requests; please do not use it for updates or revisions.
