# septic_system_camera_inspection_summary - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the findings of a septic system camera inspection. It is intended for maintenance, repair, or regulatory purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Buyer Information field with the relevant details about the property owner or entity responsible for the septic system.
2. Provide the location of the septic system in the Location field.
3. Indicate the condition of the system in the Condition of the System field. Choose from the options Inspected, Not Inspected, or Not Applicable.
4. Enter the date when the photos were taken in the Photos taken field.
5. Specify the name and contact information of the camera operator in the Camera Operator field.
6. Add any relevant notes about the inspection in the Notes field.
7. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the Inspection date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer Information** (`Buyer Information`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the entity responsible for the septic system.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, optional): Provide the address or location of the septic system.

* **Photos taken** (`Photos taken`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the camera inspection was performed.
* **Camera Operator** (`Camera Operator`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the individual who performed the camera inspection.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant notes about the inspection.
