# Pre Purchase Home Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Purchase Home Inspection Form is designed for real estate professionals to gather information about a property's condition and the buyer's decision after a pre-purchase home inspection. This form helps facilitate communication and decision-making between the buyer and seller.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the "Home Address" field**: Enter the address of the property being inspected.
2. **Provide Buyer Information**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself or the person who will be taking ownership of the property.
3. **Enter Inspection Date and Time**: Record the date and time of the inspection.
4. **Describe the Property Condition**: Provide a detailed summary of the property's condition, including any notable defects, damages, or issues found during the inspection.
5. **Explain Exterior and Interior Details**: Describe the property's exterior and interior features, including any notable features or issues.
6. **Add Inspection Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the inspection.
7. **Enter Seller Information**: If applicable, enter any information about the seller, such as their contact information.
8. **Make a Decision**: Select one of the four options:
	* **Accept**: The property meets the buyer's requirements and they are prepared to move forward with the purchase.
	* **Reject**: The property does not meet the buyer's requirements and they are not interested in purchasing.
	* **Conditional**: The property has some issues that need to be addressed before the buyer is willing to move forward with the purchase.
	* **Other (please specify)**: If none of the above options apply, use this field to provide a detailed explanation of your decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address**: The address of the property being inspected.
* **Buyer Information**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself or the person who will be taking ownership of the property.
* **Inspection Date**: The date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time**: The time of the inspection.
* **Condition Summary**: A detailed summary of the property's condition, including any notable defects, damages, or issues found during the inspection.
* **Exterior Details**: A description of the property's exterior features, including any notable features or issues.
* **Interior Details**: A description of the property's interior features, including any notable features or issues.
* **Inspection Notes**: Any additional comments or notes from the inspection.
* **Seller Information**: If applicable, enter any information about the seller, such as their contact information.
* **Buyer Decision**: Select one of the four options:
	+ Accept: The property meets the buyer's requirements and they are prepared to move forward with the purchase.
	+ Reject: The property does not meet the buyer’s requirements and they are not interested in purchasing.
	+ Conditional: The property has some issues that need to be addressed before the buyer is willing to move forward with the purchase.
	+ Other (please specify): If none of the above options apply, use this field to provide a detailed explanation of your decision.
* **Other Decision (if applicable)**: If the buyer's decision is not one of the above options, use this field to provide a detailed explanation of their decision.
