# Homeowners Insurance Quote Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for homeowners insurance quotes, helping us understand your coverage needs for your home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Answer all questions on each page carefully to ensure accurate information collection.
* Review each field label for any hints or instructions provided.
* Fill in the required fields with the most accurate information possible.
* Do not leave any field blank if it is marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Contact Information, text, required/optional):** This field is for personal contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address. Please provide accurate contact details so we can reach you for further questions or quotes.
* **Home Type (Home Type, select one, optional):** Select the type of home you own, which can be Address, Condo, House, or Other. This helps us understand your property type.
* **Home Age (Home Age, number, optional):** Enter the age of your home, which will help us evaluate its value and risk factors.
* **Square Footage (Square Footage, number, optional):** Provide the square footage of your home, which affects the coverage amount and policy premium.
* **Coverage Type (Coverage Type, select one, optional):** Choose the type of coverage you require, such as Homeowners, Tenant, Business, or Other. This informs our insurance quote.
* **Coverage Amount (Coverage Amount, number, optional):** Enter the coverage amount you are looking for, which influences the policy premium.
* **Home Location (Home Location, text, optional):** Describe your home's location, which can affect the insurance quote.
* **Additional Info (Additional Info, note, optional):** If there's anything else you'd like to share about your home or insurance preferences, use this field to provide additional information.
* **Address (Address, text, optional):** Enter your home's street address for our records.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, optional):** Provide your phone number for further contact and follow-up questions.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, optional):** Enter your email address for communication and quote updates.
* **Policy Start Date (Policy Start Date, date, optional):** If you're looking to start a policy on a specific date, provide it for us to take into account.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields with "required" status are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any doubts or questions, refer to the field label or contact us for assistance.
* This form is for insurance quotes only, so we will not share your information with anyone else. 

Note: This guide is designed to be clear, concise, and user-friendly, following the original form's structure and content for a seamless user experience.
