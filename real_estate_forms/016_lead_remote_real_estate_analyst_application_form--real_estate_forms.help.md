# Lead Remote Real Estate Analyst Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of the application process for Lead Remote Real Estate Analyst positions. It helps us understand your background, experience, and qualifications to assess your suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the **Applicant Information** section.
2. Provide your current contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
3. Describe your relevant work experience in the **Professional Experience** section.
4. Share your educational background in the **Education** section.
5. List your professional references in the **References** section.
6. Upload your resume in the **Upload Resume** section.
7. Upload your portfolio in the **Upload Portfolio** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (`apprentice_info`, text, not required): Enter your name, as it will appear on the form.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Provide your current contact information, including email address and phone number.
* **Professional Experience** (`experience`, text, not required): Describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, not required): This field is similar to the previous one, but it seems redundant. We recommend skipping this field.
* **Education** (`education`, text, not required): Share your educational background, including degree names, institutions, and dates of attendance.
* **References** (`references`, text, not required): List your professional references, including their name, title, and contact information.
* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, not required): Upload a scanned or electronic copy of your resume.
* **Upload Portfolio** (`upload_portfolio`, text, not required): Upload a sample of your work portfolio.

## Tips
* Please make sure to upload only relevant files for the **Upload Resume** and **Upload Portfolio** sections.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the **Professional Experience** and **Education** sections.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
