# real_estate_waste_reduction_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_waste_reduction_registration_form is used to register real estate properties for waste reduction programs. This form captures information about the property, such as contact details, property type, and waste reduction goals and commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the contact person's name in the **contact_person** field.
2. Enter the contact person's email address in the **contact_email** field.
3. Enter the contact person's phone number in the **contact_phone** field.
4. Select the type of property in the **property_type** field.
5. Enter the property address in the **address** field.
6. Enter the city in the **city** field.
7. Enter the state in the **state** field.
8. Enter the zip code in the **zip_code** field.
9. Enter the country in the **country** field.
10. Enter the size of the property in the **property_size** field.
11. Select the waste reduction programs the property is enrolled in in the **waste_reduction_program** field.
12. Enter the start date of the program in the **program_start_date** field.
13. Enter the end date of the program in the **program_end_date** field.
14. Enter the property's waste reduction goal in the **real_estate_waste_reduction_goal** field.
15. Enter the property owner's commitment to waste reduction in the **waste_reduction_commitment** field.
16. Enter the date of the property owner's waste reduction commitment in the **waste_reduction_commitment_date** field.
