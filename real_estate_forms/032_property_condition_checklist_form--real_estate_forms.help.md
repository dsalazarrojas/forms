# property_condition_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the condition of a property, including general, exterior, interior, structural, electrical, plumbing, heating, appliances, fixtures, and other notable features.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each field carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2.  For fields with multiple options, select the most relevant or appropriate option.
3.  For fields with text input, provide a clear and concise description.
4.  If a field has multiple options, choose the option that best fits the property's condition.
5.  For fields with select options, select only the options that apply to the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Condition**: Describe the overall state of the property's general condition.
*   **Exterior Condition**: Assess the exterior condition of the property, including any notable features or concerns.
*   **Interior Condition**: Evaluate the interior condition of the property, including any notable features or concerns.
*   **Structural Condition**: Assess the structural condition of the property, including any potential safety hazards or needed repairs.
*   **Electrical Condition**: Review the electrical condition of the property, including any potential safety concerns or needed repairs.
*   **Plumbing Condition**: Evaluate the plumbing condition of the property, including any potential safety concerns or needed repairs.
*   **Heating Condition**: Assess the heating condition of the property, including any potential safety concerns or needed repairs.
*   **Appliances**: Select the appliances that are currently installed and in working order.
*   **Fixtures**: Select the fixtures that are currently installed and in working order. (Note: Fixtures 2 is a separate field for additional fixtures.)
*   **Fixtures 2**: Select additional fixtures that are currently installed and in working order.
*   **Other Condition**: Describe any other notable conditions or features of the property.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about the property.
*   **Notes 2**: Add any additional comments or observations about the property (Note: This field is optional and meant for supplementary information).
*   **Date**: Enter the date of the property assessment.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of the property assessment.
*   **Property Status**: Select the status of the property (e.g., available for rent, sold, or under renovation).
*   **Rent**: Enter the rent amount for the property.
*   **Lease Length**: Enter the length of the lease in months or years.
*   **Security Deposit**: Enter the amount of the security deposit.
*   **Utilities**: Describe the utilities included with the property.
*   **Rent Control Agreement**: Select any rent control agreements in place.
*   **Utilities**: Describe any additional utilities included with the property.

Note: This form is for internal use only and should not be filled out unless authorized by the property owner or management company.
