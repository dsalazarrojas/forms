The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential real estate finance partners about their partnership application. This form helps partners provide details about their financial capabilities, investment goals, and any inquiries they may have. It is essential to ensure this form is completed accurately and thoroughly to facilitate the processing of their application.

## real_estate_finance_partnership_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The real_estate_finance_partnership_application form is a critical document that helps us understand your partnership requirements and capacity. This information will be used to review and consider your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:
1.  Fill in the form details (Section 1).
2.  Provide any partner inquiries (Section 2).
3.  Specify your funding capacity (Section 3).
4.  Indicate your investment goals (Section 4).
5.  Enter your contact information (Section 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details (form_details)** (`form_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your partnership application.
* **Partner Inquiries (partner_inquiries)** (`partner_inquiries`, text, required: false): If you have any questions or concerns about the application process, provide them here.
* **Funding Capacity (funding_capacity)** (`funding_capacity`, number, required: false): Indicate the amount of funding your partnership is seeking.
* **Investment Goals (investment_goals)** (`investment_goals`, select_one, required: false):
	+ **Short term investment**: Select this for short-term financial investments.
	+ **Long term investment**: Select this for long-term financial investments.
	+ **Medium term investment**: Select this for medium-term financial investments.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
