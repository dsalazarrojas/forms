# Lease Renewal Or Termination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the lease agreement, either by renewing the lease for an additional period or terminating the tenancy. It is a critical document that needs to be filled out accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
* Fill out the form with the correct dates and information.
* Make sure to sign and date the form in the designated areas.
* Return the completed form to the landlord or their representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Start Date** (1): The start date of the current lease period. Please enter the date in MM/dd/yyyy format.
- **End Date** (2): The end date of the current lease period. Please enter the date in MM/dd/yyyy format.
- **Notice Period** (3): The number of days' notice that needs to be given for termination. This field is optional.
- **Reason for Termination** (4): The reason for terminating the lease. Choose one of the following options: 
    - Tenant Requested
    - Landlord Requested
    - Other (please specify)
- **Tenant Signature Date** (5): The date the tenant signed the form. This field is not editable.
- **Landowner Signature Date** (6): The date the landlord signed the form. This field is not editable.
- **Landlord Comments** (7): Any comments or notes from the landlord regarding the termination or renewal.
- **Tenant Comments** (8): Any comments or notes from the tenant regarding the termination or renewal.
- **Termination Status** (9): The status of the termination request. Choose one of the following options: 
    - Accepted
    - Declined
    - In Progress
- **Lease Document Upload** (10): An optional field to attach a scanned copy of the lease document.
- **Tenant Signature** (11): A note field for the tenant to sign the form.
- **Landlord Signature** (12): A note field for the landlord to sign the form.
- **Start Date Note** (13): A note field for any additional information about the start date.
- **End Date Note** (14): A note field for any additional information about the end date.
- **Notice Period Note** (15): A note field for any additional information about the notice period.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach all required documents and signatures as needed.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
