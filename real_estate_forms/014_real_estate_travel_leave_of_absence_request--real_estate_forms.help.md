# Real Estate Travel Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Travel Leave Of Absence Request form is designed to collect information about employee travel leave of absence requests, including personal details, travel dates, and approvals status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID in the `employee_id` field.
2. Select the start date and end date of your travel in the `start_date` and `end_date` fields, respectively.
3. Enter the expected return date in the `return_date` field.
4. Provide a valid reason for your travel in the `reason` field.
5. Select the approval status of your travel request in the `travel_status` field.
6. Enter your supervisor's name, email, phone number, and any additional notes in the `supervisor_name`, `supervisor_email`, `supervisor_phone`, and `supervisor_notes` fields.
7. Enter your manager's name, email, phone number, and any additional notes in the `manager_name`, `manager_email`, `manager_phone`, and `manager_notes` fields.
8. Select the approval status of your travel request in the `approval_status` field.
9. Click the "Submit" button to send your request for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **travel_request**: This field is not applicable as it's not present in the form.
* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, Number, **Required**): Enter your employee ID number.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, Date, **Required**): Select the start date of your travel.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, Date, **Required**): Select the end date of your travel.
* **return_date** (`return_date`, Date, **Required**): Enter the expected return date.
* **reason** (`reason`, Text, **Required**): Provide a valid reason for your travel.
* **supervisor_approval** (`supervisor_approval`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Select the approval status of your travel request.
* **manager_approval** (`manager_approval`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Select the approval status of your travel request.
* **travel_destination** (`travel_destination`, Text, **Required**): Enter the travel destination.
* **travel_length** (`travel_length`, Text, **Required**): Enter the travel length.
* **travel_purpose** (`travel_purpose`, Text, **Required**): Enter the travel purpose.
* **travel_arrival_date** (`travel_arrival_date`, Date, **Required**): Select the travel arrival date.
* **travel_return_date** (`travel_return_date`, Date, **Required**): Select the travel return date.
* **travel_status** (`travel_status`, Select One, **Required**): Select the travel status.
* **notes** (`notes`, Note, **Required**): Enter any additional notes.
* **supervisor_name** (`supervisor_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **supervisor_email** (`supervisor_email`, Email, **Required**): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **supervisor_phone** (`supervisor_phone`, Text, **Required**): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **supervisor_notes** (`supervisor_notes`, Note, **Required**): Enter any additional notes from your supervisor.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your manager's name.
* **manager_email** (`manager_email`, Email, **Required**): Enter your manager's email address.
* **manager_phone** (`manager_phone`, Text, **Required**): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **manager_notes** (`manager_notes`, Note, **Required**): Enter any additional notes from your manager.
* **approval_date** (`approval_date`, Date, **Required**): Select the approval date.
* **approval_status** (`approval_status`, Select One, **Required**): Select the approval status of your travel request.
