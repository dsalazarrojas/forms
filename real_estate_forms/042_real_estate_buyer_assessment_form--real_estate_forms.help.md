# real_estate_buyer_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your needs and preferences as a real estate buyer. Please answer the following questions to help us provide you with tailored recommendations for your real estate needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form completely, and make sure to provide accurate information.
2. Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Online Chat).
3. Fill in your phone number (if selected as the preferred contact method).
4. If you prefer to contact us by email, enter your email address.
5. Enter any additional contact note.
6. Indicate your budget for the purchase.
7. Select your primary purpose for buying real estate (Primary Residence, Investment, or Vacation Home).
8. Rate your credit situation (Good, Fair, or Poor).
9. Select the types of properties you are interested in (Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse, or Apartment).
10. Choose your preferred location(s) (City, Suburbs, or Country).
11. Select your preferred location type (Coastal, Mountainous, or Rural).
12. Select a desired move-in date.
13. Choose your preferred real estate agent (Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer Information**: This is the first step in filling out the form.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you prefer to be contacted. You can choose from Phone, Email, or Online Chat.
* **Phone**: If you selected Phone as your contact method, enter your phone number here.
* **Email**: If you prefer to be contacted by email, enter your email address here.
* **Note**: Add any additional contact note.
* **Buyer Budget**: Enter your budget for the purchase.
* **Buyer Purpose**: Select why you are buying real estate (Primary Residence, Investment, or Vacation Home).
* **Buyer Credit**: Rate your credit situation (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Property Type**: Choose the types of properties you are interested in (Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse, or Apartment).
* **Preferred Location**: Select where you would like to live (City, Suburbs, or Country).
* **Preferred Location 2**: Choose your preferred location type (Coastal, Mountainous, or Rural).
* **Desired Move-in Date**: Enter your desired move-in date.
* **Preferred Agent**: Choose your preferred real estate agent (Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary).
