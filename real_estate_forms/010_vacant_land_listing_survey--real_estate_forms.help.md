# Vacant Land Listing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacant Land Listing Survey form is designed to collect information about vacant land listings, including properties that are available for purchase or lease. This form helps gather relevant details about the land's characteristics to facilitate its listing and sale or leasing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing the land's address in the "land address" field.
2. Select the property type from the available options (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
3. Enter the property size in the "property size" field.
4. Enter the lot size in the "lot size" field.
5. Specify the land's status (Vacant, Leased, Sold) in the "land status" field.
6. Choose the zoning from the available options (R-1, R-2, R-3).
7. Select the land use from the available options (Agricultural, Conservation, Recreation).
8. Choose the property use from the available options (Single Family Residence, Multi Family Residence, Commercial Building).
9. Enter the acreage and acres details.
10. Enter the frontage and depth details.
11. Enter the width and boundary details.
12. Provide the survey date.
13. Enter the surveyor's name.
14. Add any additional notes about the land.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **land address** (Text, required): Enter the address of the vacant land.
* **property type** (Select One, required): Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **property size** (Number, optional): Enter the size of the property (e.g., 10 acres).
* **lot size** (Text, optional): Enter the size of the lot (e.g., 5 acres).
* **land status** (Select Multiple, optional): Specify the land's status (Vacant, Leased, Sold).
* **zoning** (Select One, optional): Choose the zoning (R-1, R-2, R-3).
* **land use** (Select One, optional): Select the land use (Agricultural, Conservation, Recreation).
* **property use** (Select One, optional): Choose the property use (Single Family Residence, Multi Family Residence, Commercial Building).
* **acreage** (Number, optional): Enter the acreage of the land.
* **acres** (Text, optional): Enter the acres of the land.
* **frontage** (Text, optional): Enter the frontage details.
* **depth** (Text, optional): Enter the depth details.
* **width** (Text, optional): Enter the width details.
* **boundary** (Text, optional): Enter the boundary details.
* **survey_date** (Date, optional): Provide the survey date.
* **surveyor** (Text, optional): Enter the surveyor's name.
* **notes** (Text, optional): Add any additional notes about the land.
