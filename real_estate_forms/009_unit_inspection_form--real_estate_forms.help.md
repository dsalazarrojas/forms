# Unit Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unit Inspection Form is a tool for collecting and documenting the condition of a rental unit at move-in and move-out. It helps to ensure that both the tenant and landlord have a clear understanding of the unit's condition and any damages or repairs needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Fill out the form as truthfully as possible. If there are any discrepancies between the tenant's and landlord's answers, discuss and resolve them before proceeding.
3. Sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Unit Inspection Form (1)**: This is the title of the form.
* **Rental Unit Inspection Form (2)**: This is the label for page 1 of the form, which is a description of the form.
* **Move-in Date (3)**: Enter the date the tenant moved in.
* **Move-out Date (4)**: Enter the date the tenant moved out.
* **Tenant Name (5)**: Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Contact (6)**: Enter contact information for the tenant, such as phone number and email.
* **Unit Number (7)**: Enter the unit number of the rental unit.
* **Building Name (8)**: Enter the name of the building.
* **Unit Condition (9)**: Briefly describe the current condition of the unit.
* **Damage Found (10)**: List any damages or defects found in the unit.
* **Repairs Needed (11)**: List any repairs needed for the unit.
* **Tenant Signature (12)**: Sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Landlord Signature (13)**: Sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Landlord Date (14)**: Enter the date the form was completed by the landlord.
* **Property Manager (15)**: Enter the name of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Contact (16)**: Enter contact information for the property manager.
* **Comments (17)**: Enter any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Move-in Condition (18)**: Select the condition of the unit at move-in.
* **Move-out Condition (19)**: Select the condition of the unit at move-out.
* **Damage (20)**: Select the level of damage found.
* **Damage Type (21)**: Select the type of damage found.
* **Cause of Damage (22)**: Select the cause of the damage.
* **Damage Level (23)**: Select the level of damage found.
* **Repairs Cost (24)**: Enter the cost of repairs.
* **Repairs Status (25)**: Select the status of repairs.
* **Completed By (26)**: Select who completed the repairs.
* **Completed By (27)**: This is a duplicate label, please ignore.
* **Comments (28)**: Enter any additional comments.
