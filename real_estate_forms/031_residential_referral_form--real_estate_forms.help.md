# residential_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "residential_referral_form" is a form used to gather information about a referred property or residence. This form helps our team evaluate and process the submission for further action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your contact E-mail in the **Contact E-mail** field. This field is required to ensure our team can reach you for follow-up questions or clarification.
4. Enter your contact Phone number in the **Contact Phone** field. This information will help our team contact you directly.
5. Select the type of property from the **Property Type** dropdown menu. Choose from options like Apartment, Condo, Townhouse, House, or Other.
6. Enter the **Street Address** of the property.
7. Enter the **City** of the property.
8. Choose the **State** of the property from the dropdown menu. Please select your state correctly to ensure accurate processing.
9. Enter the **Zip Code** of the property.
10. Provide a brief **Description** of the property, if you have any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Contact E-mail** (Contact E-mail, email, required): Enter your contact email so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up.
* **Property Type** (Property Type, select_one, required): Select the type of property from the dropdown menu (Apartment, Condo, Townhouse, House, or Other).
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, required): Enter the street address of the property.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter the city where the property is located.
* **State** (State, select_one, required): Select the state where the property is located from the dropdown menu.
* **Zip Code** (Zip Code, text, required): Enter the zip code of the property.
* **Description** (Description, text, required): Enter a brief description of the property, if you have any additional details.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
- If you're unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
- Ensure accurate information is provided for property type, street address, city, state, and zip code to ensure correct processing.
