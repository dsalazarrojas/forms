# Global Strategy Innovation Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Strategy Innovation Fellowship Application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a Global Strategy Innovation Fellowship program. This form is meant to help applicants provide details about themselves, their motivations for applying, and their relevant experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Global Strategy Innovation Fellowship Application form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Applicant Details section with your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Upload your CV and other relevant documents in the CV Uploads section.
3. Write a clear and concise motivation statement explaining why you are applying for the fellowship.
4. Provide details about your relevant experience in the Experience section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Applicant Details**
* **Applicant Details (text)**: In this section, please provide your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information. Make sure to double-check your details for accuracy.

### **CV Uploads**
* **CV Uploads (text)**: Please upload your CV and any other relevant documents to support your application. Make sure to attach only PDF or DOCX files.

### **Motivation Statement**
* **Motivation Statement (text)**: In this section, please write a clear and concise statement explaining why you are applying for the Global Strategy Innovation Fellowship program. Make sure to focus on your goals, motivations, and relevant experience.

### **Experience**
* **Experience (text)**: In this section, please provide details about your relevant experience, including any internships, projects, or relevant work history. Make sure to highlight any accomplishments or achievements that demonstrate your skills and qualifications for the fellowship.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your application is complete and eligible for review.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Provide clear and concise responses in the motivation statement and experience sections.
* Only upload PDF or DOCX files in the CV Uploads section.
* Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in all sections of the form.
