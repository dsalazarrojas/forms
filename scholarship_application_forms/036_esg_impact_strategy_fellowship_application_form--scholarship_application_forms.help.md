# ESG Impact Strategy Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ESG Impact Strategy Fellowship Application Form is designed to gather information from potential fellows about their contact details, research experience, research goals, motivation, and other relevant aspects of their application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Page 1).
2. Provide your references (Page 1).
3. Describe your research experience (Page 1).
4. Outline your research goals (Page 1).
5. State your motivation for applying (Page 1).
6. Indicate your availability for the fellowship (Page 2).
7. Select your availability for the fellowship (Page 2).
8. Provide a time commitment for the fellowship (Page 2).
9. Confirm your time commitment (Page 2).
10. Add any final application information (Page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`contact_information`, text, required):
	+ This is your name and contact information (e.g., email and phone number).
* **References (Page 1)** (`references`, text, required):
	+ Provide names and contact information for two to three relevant references.
* **Research Experience (Page 1)** (`research_experience`, text, required):
	+ Describe your relevant research experience, including any academic, professional, or research-related projects.
* **Research Goals (Page 1)** (`research_goals`, text, required):
	+ Clearly outline your research goals and objectives.
* **Motivation (Page 1)** (`motivation`, text, required):
	+ Explain why you're interested in the ESG Impact Strategy Fellowship and how it aligns with your research goals.
* **Availability for Fellowship (Page 2)** (`availability_for_fellowship`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate if you're available to participate in the fellowship.
* **Fellowship Availability (Page 2)** (`fellowship_availability`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all the times you're available for the fellowship.
* **Time Commitment (Page 2)** (`time_commitment`, time, required):
	+ Enter the time you're willing to commit for the fellowship.
* **Time Commitment 2 (Page 2)** (`time_commitment_2`, time, required):
	+ Enter the time you're willing to commit for the fellowship (if different from the first time commitment).
* **Final Application Information (Page 3)** (`final_application_information`, text, required):
	+ Add any additional information to support your application.

Note: The "Other" option in the "Availability for Fellowship" and "Fellowship Availability" fields is intended to capture any other availability that doesn't fit into the "Yes" or "No" categories.
