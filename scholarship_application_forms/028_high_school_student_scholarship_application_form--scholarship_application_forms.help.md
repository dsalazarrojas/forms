# High School Student Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a general guide for high school students to apply for scholarships. It covers various aspects of a student's life, including personal details, academic history, and interests. The form is designed to help students showcase their strengths, achievements, and qualifications for scholarship opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
2.  Ensure you have all required information and documents (if specified).
3.  Review the form thoroughly before submission.
4.  Attach any additional supporting documents or materials (if requested).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (page 1)**:
	+ **Name** (required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Email** (required): Enter your valid email address.
	+ **Phone** (optional): If you have a phone number, enter it. This can be used for further communication.
* **Contact Information (page 2)**:
	+ **Parent/Guardian Name** (optional): If you're under 18, enter your parent or guardian's name and contact information.
	+ **Parent/Guardian Contact Number** (optional): Enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
	+ **Parent/Guardian Email** (optional): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Academic History (page 3)**:
	+ **School Name** (optional): Enter the name of your high school.
	+ **Grade Level** (optional): Select your current grade level.
	+ **Year of Graduation** (optional): Enter the year you plan to graduate from high school.
* **Education Details (page 4)**:
	+ **GPA** (optional): Enter your current GPA if applicable.
	+ **Academic Subjects** (optional): List the subjects you're taking in school.
* **Family Background (page 5)**:
	+ **Parent/Guardian Name** (optional): If you're under 18, enter your parent or guardian's name and contact information.
	+ **Number of Siblings** (optional): If you're not the only child, enter the number of siblings.
* **Academic References (page 6)**:
	+ **Reference Name** (required): List the name of a teacher or academic counselor who can vouch for your academic abilities.
	+ **Reference Relationship** (optional): Specify your relationship with the reference (e.g., teacher, counselor, or coach).
* **Student Documents (page 7)**:
	+ **Transcript** (optional): Attach your official transcript from your school.
	+ **Test Scores** (optional): Attach any test scores or results that demonstrate your academic abilities.
* **Student Accomplishments (page 8)**:
	+ **Describe your accomplishments** (optional): In this section, describe any notable achievements, awards, or recognition you've received in academics, arts, or sports.
* **Student Activities (page 9)**:
	+ **List your activities** (optional): In this section, list any clubs, organizations, or volunteer work you've participated in.
* **Student Goals (page 10)**:
	+ **Describe your goals** (optional): In this section, describe your short-term and long-term academic and personal goals.
* **Student Objectives (page 11)**:
	+ **Describe your objectives** (optional): In this section, describe your academic and personal objectives.
* **Student Aptitudes (page 12)**:
	+ **Describe your aptitudes** (optional): In this section, describe your skills, talents, or abilities that make you a strong candidate.
* **Student Interests (page 13)**:
	+ **List your interests** (optional): In this section, list any hobbies, interests, or activities you enjoy.
* **Student Skills (page 14)**:
	+ **Describe your skills** (optional): In this section, describe any technical, social, or artistic skills you've developed.
* **Student Languages (page 15)**:
	+ **List your languages** (optional): In this section, list any languages you're proficient in.
* **Student Hobbies (page 16)**:
	+ **List your hobbies** (optional): In this section, list any activities you enjoy outside of academics.
* **Student Organizations (page 17)**:
	+ **List your organizations** (optional): In this section, list any clubs, teams, or organizations you've participated in.
* **Student References (page 18)**:
	+ **Reference Name** (required): List the name of a teacher, counselor, or other academic contact who can vouch for your abilities.
	+ **Reference Relationship** (optional): Specify your relationship with the reference.
* **Student Certifications (page 19)**:
	+ **List your certifications** (optional): In this section, list any certifications or qualifications you've acquired.
* **Student Awards (page 20)**:
	+ **List your awards** (optional): In this section, list any awards or recognition you've received.
* **Student Publications (page 21)**:
	+ **List your publications** (optional): In this section, list any publications or creative works you've published.
* **Student Projects (page 22)**:
	+ **Describe your projects** (optional): In this section, describe any projects you've worked on.
* **Student Participations (page 23)**:
	+ **List your participations** (optional): In this section, list any events, competitions, or workshops you've participated in.
* **Submit Application (page 24)**:
	+ **Submit your application** (optional): Double-check your information and submit the form once you're satisfied with your answers.

## Tips

* Ensure you've answered all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Attach any supporting documents or materials as requested.
* Proofread your form before submission for any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the scholarship administrators for guidance.
* This form is not a guarantee of selection, but it will help showcase your strengths and qualifications.
