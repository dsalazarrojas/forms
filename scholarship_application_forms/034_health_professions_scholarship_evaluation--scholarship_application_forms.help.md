# Health Professions Scholarship Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the quality of applicants for the Health Professions Scholarship. The form is used to assess the applicant's potential for success in the health professions program based on their rating scale scores and reviewer feedback.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Candidate Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Rating Scale Score in the second field.
3. Provide your Reviewer Feedback in the third field.
4. For each Rating Scale question, select the relevant option from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the corresponding Weight for each Rating Scale question.
6. Add any additional comments in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name**: <em>Candidate Name</em> is the first field in the form. Please enter your name exactly as it appears on your official identification documents.

# Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and completely.
* Make sure to enter the correct weights for each rating scale question.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the scholarship committee.
