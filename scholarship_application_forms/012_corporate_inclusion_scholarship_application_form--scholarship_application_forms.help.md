# Corporate Inclusion Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to apply for a scholarship offered by the corporation. It collects contact information, educational background, and motivation to support the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, address, and other relevant details.
2. In the Education section, provide your field of study and the institution you're currently attending or have attended.
3. In the Motivation section, explain why you're applying for the scholarship and what you hope to achieve with it.
4. Fill out the contact email and phone number fields with your current contact information.
5. For the Field of Study field, select your current field of study from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (contact, text, optional): This section requires your contact information, including your name and address.
* **Education** (education, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your educational background, including your field of study and the institution you're currently attending or have attended.
* **Motivation** (motivation, text, optional): Write a short paragraph explaining why you're applying for the scholarship and what you hope to achieve with it.
* **Email** (contact_email, email, optional): Enter your current email address.
* **Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your current phone number.
* **Field of Study** (education_field_of_study, select_multiple, optional): Select your current field of study from the options provided.
* **Institution** (education_institution, text, optional): Provide the name of the institution you're currently attending or have attended.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email and phone number for errors before submitting.
* If you have any trouble filling out a field, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
