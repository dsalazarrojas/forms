# Smart Packaging Innovation Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for applicants to apply for the Smart Packaging Innovation Grant, which supports innovative packaging solutions that aim to improve the packaging industry. The form requires information about the applicant, project details, and innovative packaging solution. The application process helps the selection committee to evaluate the applicant's project and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your contact information (Contact Person, Contact Address, Contact Number, and Email) in the Main Page section.
2. Select your organization from the dropdown menu in the Organization section.
3. Enter your project's budget and duration in the Project Budget and Project Duration sections.
4. Describe your project's status, innovation, objectives, outcome, and expected outcome in the Innovation Description, Innovation Objectives, Innovation Outcome, and Innovation Expected Outcome sections.
5. List your team members and their positions, emails, and phone numbers in the Team Members section.
6. Select the category and subcategory of your innovation in the Innovation Category and Innovation Sub Category sections.
7. Add any relevant keywords for your innovation in the Innovation Keywords section.
8. Upload your innovation file and image in the Innovation File and Innovation Image sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person**: Enter your name as the contact person for your project.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your physical address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Organization**: Select your organization from the dropdown menu.
* **Project Budget**: Enter your project's budget.
* **Project Duration**: Enter your project's duration.
* **Project Status**: Select your project's status.
* **Innovation Description**: Describe your innovative packaging solution.
* **Innovation Objectives**: Describe your project's objectives.
* **Innovation Outcome**: Describe the expected outcome of your project.
* **Innovation Expected Outcome**: Describe the expected outcome of your project.
* **Project Leader Name**: Enter your name as the project leader.
* **Project Leader Email**: Enter your email address as the project leader.
* **Team Members**: List your team members with their positions, emails, and phone numbers.
* **Team Member Position**: Select the position of each team member.
* **Team Member Email**: Enter each team member's email address.
* **Team Member Phone**: Enter each team member's phone number.
* **Project Team**: Describe your project team.
* **Innovation Category**: Select the category of your innovation.
* **Innovation Sub Category**: Select the subcategory of your innovation.
* **Innovation Keywords**: Add any relevant keywords for your innovation.
* **Innovation File**: Upload your innovation file.
* **Innovation Image**: Upload your innovation image.
