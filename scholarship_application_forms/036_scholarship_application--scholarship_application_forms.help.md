# Scholarship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for a scholarship. It helps us understand your qualifications, financial needs, and other relevant details to assess your eligibility for the scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details in the **User Inputs** section.
2. Provide your **Contacts** information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Share your education background, including your educational institution and degree earned.
4. Describe your employment history, including your job position and duration of employment.
5. Share details about your previous awards, including the award name, year awarded, and a brief description.
6. If applicable, describe your financial need and scholarship amount required.
7. Specify the award period, which is the duration for which you are applying for the scholarship.
8. Provide details about the scholarship term you are applying for, including any specific requirements or restrictions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Inputs**: <br>
  This section asks for general user details, such as name, address, and any other relevant information.
  - **User Inputs 2**: <br>
    This section is asking for contact information, such as phone number and address.
  - **Education**: <br>
    This section is for providing details about your educational background, including the institution and degree earned.
  - **Employment**: <br>
    This section is for describing your employment history, including your job position and duration of employment.
  - **Award**: <br>
    This section is for sharing details about any previous awards you have received, including the award name and year awarded.
  - **Scholarship**: <br>
    If applicable, this section is for describing your financial need and scholarship amount required.
  - **References**: <br>
    This section is for providing references, including name, title, and contact information.
  - **Financial Need**: <br>
    This section is for describing your financial needs and how the scholarship will be used.
  - **Scholarship Amount**: <br>
    This section is for specifying the amount of scholarship you are applying for.
  - **Award Period**: <br>
    This section is for specifying the duration of the award, which is the period for which you are applying for the scholarship.
  - **Scholarship Term**: <br>
    This section is for providing details about any specific term or conditions of the scholarship.

## Tips
Please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly. If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
