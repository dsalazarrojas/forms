# Marine Research Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from applicants to the Marine Research Fellowship program. It is recommended to read this guide carefully before filling out the form.

## How to Complete This Form
Before you start filling out the form, ensure that you have all the required information ready. Follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Provide your applicant information in the "Applicant Information" section.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Select the research area that aligns with your interests and application in the "Research Area" section.
4. Specify your research focus in the "Research Focus" section.
5. Describe your research objectives in the "Research Objectives" section.
6. Describe your research plan in the "Research Plan" section.
7. Attach a copy of your CV to the "CV" section.
8. List relevant references in the "References" section.
9. List your relevant publications in the "Publications" section.
10. Specify your research experience in the "Research Experience" section.
11. Provide information about your education history in the "Education History" section.
12. List your work history in the "Work History" section.
13. Enter your current position in the "Current Position" section.
14. Specify your affiliation in the "Affiliation" section.
15. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
16. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information**: Provide your name, institution, and any other relevant identifying details.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your address, phone number, and email address.
* **Research Area**: Select the area of research that best aligns with your application. Choose from the following options: Marine Conservation, Coastal Ecosystems, Ocean Acidification, Ocean Acidification and Climate Change, Ocean and Human Health, Marine Biodiversity, or Other.
* **Research Focus**: Select all the research focus areas that apply to your application. Choose from the following options: Conservation and Policy, Marine Policy, Marine Science, Ocean Science, or Other.
* **Research Objectives**: Describe your research objectives and goals. This section is open-ended, so please provide a clear and concise description of your research plan.
* **Research Plan**: Describe your research plan, including the methodology and timeline. This section is also open-ended, so please provide a clear and concise description of your research plan.
* **CV**: Attach a copy of your CV, which should include your education, work experience, and any other relevant information.
* **References**: List the names and contact details of people who can vouch for your research abilities and expertise.
* **Publications**: List all your relevant publications, including journal articles, conference papers, book chapters, and books.
* **Research Experience**: Specify your research experience, including any relevant positions or roles you have held.
* **Education History**: Provide a brief description of your education background, including institutions and degrees earned.
* **Work History**: Provide a brief description of your work background, including positions and institutions you have worked for.
* **Current Position**: Enter your current position and institution.
* **Affiliation**: Specify your current affiliation, if any.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Information**: This section is only for internal use. Please do not fill it out unless instructed to do so by the fellowship organizers.
