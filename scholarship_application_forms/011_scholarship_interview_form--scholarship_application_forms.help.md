# Scholarship Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect evaluation and recommendation information from interviewers for scholarship candidates. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Do not duplicate efforts by filling out this form if you have already evaluated and recommended a candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_information`): This section is used to gather basic information about the candidate, such as name and contact details. 
  * Please enter the candidate's name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields. 
  * If the candidate has any other contact information, such as a LinkedIn profile or website, you may include it in the text field provided. 

* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`): This section is used to record the details of the interview, such as the date, time, and location. 
  * Please enter the date and time of the interview in the respective fields. 
  * If the interview was held at a specific location, enter the location in the text field provided. 

* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`): This section is used to evaluate the candidate's qualifications, experience, and fit with the scholarship. 
  * Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the candidate meets the evaluation criteria. 

* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`): This section is used to determine how the evaluation was conducted. 
  * Please select all methods that apply to your evaluation process. 

* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`): This section is used to rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-10. 
  * Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to indicate your evaluation score. 

* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`): This section is used to provide additional comments about the candidate's performance. 
  * Please enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the candidate's performance. 

* **Evaluation Result** (`evaluation_result`): This section is used to indicate whether the candidate was recommended or not. 
  * Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the candidate was recommended. 

* **Interview Duration** (`interview_duration`): This section is used to record the duration of the interview. 
  * Please enter the duration of the interview in minutes. 

* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`): This section is used to record the date of the interview. 
  * Please enter the date of the interview in the format YYYY-MM-DD. 

* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`): This section is used to record the location of the interview. 
  * Please enter the location of the interview in the text field provided. 

* **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`): This section is used to record any additional notes about the evaluation process. 
  * Please enter any additional notes or feedback you may have about the evaluation process. 

* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`): This section is used to rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-10. 
  * Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to indicate your evaluation score. 

* **Evaluation Rationale** (`evaluation_rationale`): This section is used to provide a reason for the evaluation decision. 
  * Please enter a brief explanation of why you selected the evaluation score and result. 

* **Evaluation Decision** (`evaluation_decision`): This section is used to confirm the evaluation decision. 
  * Please select "Yes" or "No" to confirm the evaluation decision. 

* **Evaluation Recommendation** (`evaluation_recommendation`): This section is used to provide recommendations for the candidate. 
  * Please select all recommendations that apply. 

* **Evaluation Comments Recommendation** (`evaluation_comments_recommendation`): This section is used to provide additional comments or feedback about the recommendation. 
  * Please enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the recommendation. 

* **Evaluation Score Recommendation** (`evaluation_score_recommendation`): This section is used to rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-10. 
  * Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to indicate your evaluation score. 

* **Evaluation Rationale Recommendation** (`evaluation_rationale_recommendation`): This section is used to provide a reason for the recommendation decision. 
  * Please enter a brief explanation of why you selected the recommendation. 

* **Evaluation Decision Recommendation** (`evaluation_decision_recommendation`): This section is used to confirm the recommendation decision. 
  * Please select "Yes" or "No" to confirm the recommendation decision. 

* **Evaluation Scale Recommendation** (`evaluation_scale_recommendation`): This section is used to rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-10. 
  * Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to indicate your evaluation score. 

* **Evaluation Result Recommendation** (`evaluation_result_recommendation`): This section is used to indicate whether the candidate was recommended or not. 
  * Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the candidate was recommended. 

* **Evaluation Notes Recommendation** (`evaluation_notes_recommendation`): This section is used to record any additional notes or feedback about the recommendation. 
  * Please enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the recommendation. 

* **Evaluation Score Recommendation 2** (`evaluation_score_recommendation2`): This section is used to rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-10. 
  * Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to indicate your evaluation score. 

* **Evaluation Rationale Recommendation 2** (`evaluation_rationale_recommendation2`): This section is used to provide a reason for the recommendation decision. 
  * Please enter a brief explanation of why you selected the recommendation. 

* **Evaluation Decision Recommendation 2** (`evaluation_decision_recommendation2`): This section is used to confirm the recommendation decision. 
  * Please select "Yes" or "No" to confirm the recommendation decision. 

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed. 
* Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness. 
* Do not duplicate efforts by filling out this form if you have already evaluated and recommended a candidate. 
* Enter any additional comments or feedback in the respective text fields where applicable. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the scholarship committee.
