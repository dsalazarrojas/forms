# Financial Modeling Consulting Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Modeling Consulting Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect information from potential applicants for a Financial Modeling Consulting Fellowship. The form is intended to gather relevant data to evaluate applicants for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Applicant Information** page with your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant information you'd like to share.
2. On the **Personal Info** page, provide your personal bio, including your education background, work experience, and any other relevant information about yourself.
3. In the **Contact Details** page, provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email address.
4. The **Education Info** page is where you can list your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
5. The **Employment History** page is where you can list your previous work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
6. The **Financial Info** page is where you can provide your financial information, including your income, expenses, and any other relevant financial details.
7. The **Financial Support** page is where you can list any financial support you have received or expect to receive.
8. The **References** page is where you can list any professional references you'd like to provide.
9. The **Additional Info** page is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to the fellowship application.
10. Finally, the **Financial Modeling Consulting** page is where you can provide any information about your financial modeling consulting experience or skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Information (Page 1)**: This page is where you can enter your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant information you'd like to share.
  - **Title (text)**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your application.
  - **Email (text)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
  - **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
- **Personal Info (Page 2)**: This page is where you can provide information about yourself, including your education background, work experience, and any other relevant details.
  - **Personal Bio (text)**: Describe yourself in a few sentences, including your education, work experience, and any other relevant information.
  - **Other Relevant Info (text)**: Enter any other information about yourself that you think is relevant to the fellowship application.
- **Contact Details (Page 3)**: This page is where you can provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email address.
  - **Address (text)**: Enter your home address or other contact address.
  - **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
  - **Email (text)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
- **Education Info (Page 4)**: This page is where you can list your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
  - **Degree 1 (text)**: Enter your highest degree earned.
  - **Institution 1 (text)**: Enter the name of the institution where you earned your degree.
  - **Year 1 (text)**: Enter the year you earned your degree.
- **Employment History (Page 5)**: This page is where you can list your previous work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
  - **Job Title 1 (text)**: Enter your job title.
  - **Company 1 (text)**: Enter the name of the company you worked for.
  - **Employment Dates 1 (text)**: Enter the dates of your employment.
- **Financial Info (Page 6)**: This page is where you can provide your financial information, including your income, expenses, and any other relevant financial details.
  - **Income (text)**: Enter your annual income.
  - **Expenses (text)**: Enter your monthly expenses.
- **Financial Support (Page 7)**: This page is where you can list any financial support you have received or expect to receive.
  - **Financial Support 1 (text)**: Enter any financial support you have received.
- **References (Page 8)**: This page is where you can list any professional references you'd like to provide.
  - **Reference 1 (text)**: Enter the name of your first reference.
  - **Reference 1 (text)**: Enter the title and contact information of your first reference.
- **Additional Info (Page 9)**: This page is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to the fellowship application.
  - **Additional Info (text)**: Enter any other information about yourself that you think is relevant.
- **Financial Modeling Consulting (Page 10)**: This page is where you can provide any information about your financial modeling consulting experience or skills.
  - **Financial Modeling Experience (text)**: Describe any financial modeling consulting experience you have.
  - **Financial Modeling Skills (text)**: List any financial modeling skills you possess.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
- Double-check your entries for spelling and grammar errors.
- Ensure all fields are filled out completely before submitting your application.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
