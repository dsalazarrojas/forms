# Academic Transformation Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to support applicants seeking funding for academic transformation projects. The purpose of the form is to gather essential information about the applicant and their proposed project.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by filling in the applicant information section, providing your name, department, position, and institutional email address.
- Move on to the project details section, where you will enter the project title, category, executive summary, and key objectives.
- Ensure you provide accurate and clear information about the budget and timeline.
- Finally, confirm that you have all necessary supporting documents ready for upload and that your department head has approved the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Applicant or Researcher Name (1)** (`applicant_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the primary investigator for the project.
* **Academic Department (2)** (`academic_department`, **text**, required): Enter the department where the project is based.
* **Position or Title (3)** (`position_title`, **text**, required): Enter your position or title, e.g., Associate Professor, Research Fellow.
* **Institutional Email (4)** (`professional_email`, **email**, required): Enter your official university email address.
* **Proposed Project Title (7)** (`project_title`, **text**, required): Enter a short, descriptive title for your project.
* **Category of Transformation (8)** (`transformation_category`, **select_one**, required): Select the primary focus area of your project (e.g., Pedagogical Innovation, Technological Integration, etc.).
* **Executive Summary or Abstract (9)** (`project_abstract`, **text**, required): Enter a summary of your project, up to 250 words.
* **Key Project Objectives (10)** (`project_objectives`, **text**, required): List the main goals of your project.
* **Total Funds Requested in USD (12)** (`total_funds_requested`, **number**, required): Enter the total funds you are requesting.
* **Brief Budget Justification (13)** (`budget_justification`, **text**, required): Provide a summary of major expenses.
* **Estimated Project Start Date (14)** (`project_start_date`, **date**, required): Enter the date when project activities will begin.
* **Estimated Project Completion Date (15)** (`project_end_date`, **date**, required): Enter the date when the final report is due.
* **Are your supporting documents ready for attachment? (17)** (`files_ready_for_upload`, **select_one**, required): Check if you have all supporting documents ready for upload.
* **Has your department head approved this application? (19)** (`institutional_approval`, **select_one**, required): Confirm if your department head has approved this application.

Please ensure that you have provided all required information and that your department head has given their approval before submitting this form.
