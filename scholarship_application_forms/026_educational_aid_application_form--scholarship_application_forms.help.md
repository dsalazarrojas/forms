# Educational Aid Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Aid Application Form is designed to collect information about students applying for educational aid. This form helps the organization to assess the student's eligibility for aid and determine the amount of support they can receive.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the student information section with your name, email, and other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information, including phone number and address.
3. Enter your scholarship information, such as scholarship name and student ID.
4. Describe your educational details, including school name, grade level, and course subjects.
5. Specify the course start and end dates, and course duration.
6. Enter financial information, including aid amount and type.
7. Upload supporting documents, such as proof of income or transcripts.
8. Confirm your information and terms of the application.
