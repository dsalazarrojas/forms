# Educational-Systems-Innovation-Challenge-Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational-Systems-Innovation-Challenge-Application form is designed to collect project ideas, impact descriptions, supporting documents, and team member information from individuals or teams interested in participating in the Educational Systems Innovation Challenge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and what fields are optional.
2. Enter your project ideas in the "Project Ideas" field.
3. Describe the impact of your project in the "Impact Description" field.
4. Upload any supporting documents related to your project in the "Supporting Documents" field.
5. List your team members in the "Team Members" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Ideas** (`project_ideas`, `text`, required: false): A field to enter your project ideas and concepts for the Educational Systems Innovation Challenge.
* **Impact Description** (`impact_description`, `text`, required: false): A field to describe the potential impact of your project on educational systems.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, required: false): A field to upload any relevant documents that support your project, such as research papers or project proposals.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `text`, required: false): A field to list the members of your team, including their names and roles.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers for each field to ensure that your application is reviewed accurately.
* Use the "Supporting Documents" field to provide any relevant evidence or proof that supports your project.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the challenge, contact us at form@gic.mx.
