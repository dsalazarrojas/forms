# Bioinformatics In Medicine Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bioinformatics In Medicine Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Bioinformatics In Medicine fellowship program. This form is a part of the application process for the fellowship, which is aimed at supporting researchers and professionals in the field of bioinformatics and its applications in medicine. This form should be filled out carefully and accurately to help the selection committee evaluate your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2.  Fill out each field accurately and clearly.
3.  Attach relevant documents (e.g., curriculum vitae, publications or preprints) as required.
4.  Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
5.  Double-check your work before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address for correspondence about your application.
- **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
- **Current affiliation** (`current_affiliation`, text, optional): Specify your current institution or employer.
- **Highest degree obtained** (`highest_degree`, text, required): Enter the degree you have obtained and the institution that granted it.
- **Research experience summary** (`research_experience_summary`, text, required): Describe your relevant research experience.
- **Publications or preprints** (`publications_or_preprints`, text, optional): List your major publications or provide links.
- **Statement of intent** (`statement_of_intent`, text, required): Explain why you are interested in the fellowship.
- **References** (`references`, text, required): Provide names and contact details for two references.
- **Availability start date** (`availability_date`, date, optional): Specify when you could start the fellowship.
- **Curriculum vitae** (`curriculum_vitae_note`, note, required): Please upload your CV through the portal or provide a link.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Attach relevant attachments (e.g., CV, publications) as required.
- Proofread your work before submitting.
