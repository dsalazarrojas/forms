# Sound Design Research Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound Design Research Grant Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants who are seeking funding for their sound design research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- For fields with options, select the most relevant and correct choice.
- For fields with attachments, attach relevant and clear documentation.
- Review your form before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name is required for identification purposes. Please provide your full first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name is required for identification purposes. Please provide your full last name.
* **Organization**: If you are affiliated with an organization, please provide the name of your organization.
* **Contact Name**: The name of the person who will be the primary contact for this application.
* **Email**: A valid email address is required for communication purposes. Please ensure it is a correct and active email.
* **Phone Number**: A valid phone number is required for communication purposes.
* **Project Name**: A clear and concise description of your project.
* **Project Duration**: The expected duration of your project, in months.
* **Funding Source**: If you are receiving funding from another source, please provide the name of the funding source.
* **Description**: A detailed description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Budget**: A breakdown of the budget for your project, including expected expenses and revenue.
* **Attachments**: Please upload any relevant documentation or files related to your project.
* **Attachment Description**: A brief description of the attachment, including its relevance to your project.
* **Attachment**: The actual attachment that you are uploading.

## Tips
- Ensure that all attachments are clear and legible.
- Use proper file formats and sizes for attachments.
- Review and proofread your form before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
