# Educational Grant Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Grant Declaration Form is a document used by students to declare their intention to enroll in a program at an institution. This form helps the institution track student progress and ensures that the student is aware of their obligations and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, taking into account that you are the representative of the student or the student themselves.
- Ensure that you are not duplicating any existing declaration by checking with your institution's administration.
- Complete each page of the form in order to ensure that you have all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Grant Declaration** (`educational_grant_declaration_form_1`): This is the declaration statement where you are to declare your intention to enroll in a program at the institution. Enter a short description of your plans, goals and expectations.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter your full name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format `month/day/year`.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter your valid email address associated with your student account.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`): Enter your valid phone number that the institution can use to contact you.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`): Enter the date you are declaring your intention to enroll.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`): Enter the name of the institution where you are enrolling.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`): Enter the address of the institution where you are enrolling.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the person you are in contact with at the institution.
* **Contact Person Phone** (`contact_person_phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Person Email** (`contact_person_email`): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`): Enter the program you are enrolling in.
* **Academic Program Start Date** (`academic_program_start_date`): Enter the date you are starting the program.
* **Expected Completion Date** (`expected_completion_date`): Enter the expected completion date of the program.
* **Declaration Status** (`declaration_status`): Describe the current status of your declaration.
* **Declaration Status Note** (`declaration_status_note`): Add any additional notes about your declaration status.
* **Declaration Status Update Date** (`declaration_status_update_date`): Enter the date you updated your declaration status.
* **Institution Approval Status** (`institution_approval_status`): Enter the status of your declaration with the institution.
* **Institution Approval Status Note** (`institution_approval_status_note`): Add any additional notes about your institution approval status.
* **Institution Approval Status Update Date** (`institution_approval_status_update_date`): Enter the date you updated your institution approval status.
* **Declaration Form Submission** (`declaration_form_submission`): Describe your previous declaration form submissions.
* **Declaration Form Submission Date** (`declaration_form_submission_date`): Enter the date you submitted the declaration form.
* **Declaration Form Submission Note** (`declaration_form_submission_note`): Add any additional notes about your declaration form submissions.
* **Declaration Form Approval Status** (`declaration_form_approval_status`): Enter the status of your declaration form.
* **Declaration Form Approval Status Note** (`declaration_form_approval_status_note`): Add any additional notes about your declaration form approval status.
* **Declaration Form Approval Status Update Date** (`declaration_form_approval_status_update_date`): Enter the date you updated your declaration form approval status.
