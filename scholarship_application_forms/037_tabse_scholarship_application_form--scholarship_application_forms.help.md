# TABSE Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your educational background, work experience, and personal references to evaluate your eligibility for the TABSE scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your user input details on page 1.
2. On page 2, provide your contact details and education history, including your education level, field of study, start and end dates, and the institution you attended.
3. If you have relevant work experience, please provide details about your occupation, company, start and end dates, and any other relevant information.
4. Finally, provide information about your personal references, including their names, job titles, relationship with you, and their contact information.
5. Make sure to fill all required fields and ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`User Input`, text, optional): Please enter your name, email, and any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your address and phone number.
* **Education History** (`Education History`, text, optional): Describe your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Education Level** (`Education Level`, select_one, optional): Select your highest educational level achieved (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate).
* **Field of Study** (`Field of Study`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the fields you have studied (e.g., Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics).
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your education.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your education.
* **Attended Institution** (`Attended Institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution you attended.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, text, optional): Describe your work experience, including job titles and dates.
* **Occupation** (`Occupation`, text, optional): Enter your occupation or job title.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or past company.
* **Work Experience Start Date** (`Work Experience Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your work experience.
* **Work Experience End Date** (`Work Experience End Date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your work experience.
* **Personal References** (`Personal References`, text, optional): Provide information about your personal references, including their names, job titles, and contact information.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your personal reference.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of your personal reference.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, text, optional): Enter your relationship with your personal reference.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of your personal reference.
* **Reference Type** (`Reference Type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of reference (e.g., Professional Reference, Academic Reference, Character Reference).
* **Number of References** (`Number of References`, number, optional): Enter the number of personal references provided.
* **Reference Name** (`Reference Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of each personal reference.
* **Reference Job Title** (`Reference Job Title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of each personal reference.
* **Reference Contact Info** (`Reference Contact Info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of each personal reference.
* **Reference Note** (`Reference Note`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about each personal reference.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely to increase the chances of a successful application.
* Double-check your entries for spelling and grammar mistakes before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
