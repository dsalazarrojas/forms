# Youth Athlete Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from youth athletes who are applying for a grant to support their athletic pursuits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "Athlete Details" section.
2. Describe your achievements and experiences in the "Achievements" section.
3. If you are experiencing financial difficulties or have a strong need for support, please explain in the "Financial Need" section.
4. Upload any relevant documents that support your application in the "Supporting Documents" section.
5. Please have your coach, athletic director, or non-profit organization representative provide a recommendation for your application in the "Coach Recommendation", "Athletic Director Recommendation", "School Administration Recommendation", or "Non-Profit Organization Recommendation" sections.
6. Once all required sections are filled out, review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Details** (required): This is where you provide your name, address, and contact information.
* **Achievements** (optional): Describe your past athletic achievements, awards, or experiences. This can include sports-related achievements, academic achievements, or other relevant accomplishments.
* **Financial Need** (optional): If you are experiencing financial difficulties or have a strong need for support, please explain your situation. This will help us understand your circumstances and make a more informed decision.
* **Supporting Documents** (optional): Upload any documents that support your application, such as proof of financial need, letters of recommendation, or other relevant paperwork.
* **Coach Recommendation** (optional): If you are currently training with a coach, please ask them to provide a recommendation for you. This can be a written statement or an email.
* **Athletic Director Recommendation** (optional): If you are a member of a school or club team, please ask your athletic director for a recommendation.
* **School Administration Recommendation** (optional): If you are a student-athlete, ask your school administrator for a recommendation.
* **Non-Profit Organization Recommendation** (optional): If you are affiliated with a non-profit organization, ask them to provide a recommendation for you.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields to avoid incomplete applications.
* Upload supporting documents in a clear and legible format.
* Ask your recommender to provide specific examples or experiences that demonstrate your qualifications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
