# Summer Camp Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Camp Scholarship Application Form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians to help the scholarship committee evaluate their child's application for a summer camp scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each section as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Ensure that you are applying for your own child, and not for someone else.
3. Be aware that the scholarship committee may contact you for additional information or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information (Page 1)** (<label> Parent Information, type: note, required: false): This section collects general information about you, the parent or guardian completing the form.
	+ Please provide your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Camper Information (Page 2)** (<label> Camper Information, type: note, required: false): This section collects information about the child you are applying for.
	+ Please provide the child's name, date of birth, and any relevant medical information.
* **Income Details (Page 3)** (<label> Income Details, type: note, required: false): This section helps the scholarship committee understand your financial situation.
	+ Please report your annual household income, and any other relevant income sources.
* **Reasons for Application (Page 4)** (<label> Reasons for Application, type: note, required: false): This section helps the scholarship committee understand why you are applying for a scholarship.
	+ Please explain why you are seeking a summer camp scholarship, and what benefits it would bring to your child.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are honest and accurate when completing each section.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the form creator or the scholarship committee.

This guide is meant to be a starting point, and additional instructions or requirements may be requested by the scholarship committee.
