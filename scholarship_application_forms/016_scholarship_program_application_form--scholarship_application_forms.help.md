# scholarship_program_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential details from applicants for the scholarship program, ensuring a fair and efficient evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the third field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourth field.
5. Input your **Date of Birth** in the fifth field. Use the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
6. Enter your **Address** in the sixth field.
7. Provide your **Educational Details** in the seventh field.
8. Enter your **Program Details** in the eighth field.
9. Select your **Contact Preference** in the ninth field.
10. Enter your **Contact Information** in the tenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your active email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Address (6)**: Enter your current address where you can be contacted.
* **Educational Details (7)**: Provide your educational background and qualifications.
* **Program Details (8)**: Describe the program you are applying for and why you are a good fit.
* **Contact Preference (9)**: Indicate how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, mail).
* **Contact Information (10)**: Provide any additional contact information that may be necessary for the scholarship committee.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in completely and accurately.
- Double-check your input for spelling and formatting errors before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the scholarship committee.
