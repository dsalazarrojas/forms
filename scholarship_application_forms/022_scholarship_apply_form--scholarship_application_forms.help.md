# Scholarship Apply Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and contact information from applicants for a scholarship program. Please provide accurate and truthful information to help the scholarship committee assess your eligibility and application quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field as instructed.
2. Ensure that all required fields (marked as "required") are completed.
3. Double-check your contact email address for accuracy.
4. Review and verify your submitted information for errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Scholarship Apply Form** (`scholarship_apply_form`): This is the title of the form, and does not require completion.
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`): Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant demographic information.
* **Application Details** (`application_details`): Describe your academic and professional background, and explain why you are a suitable candidate for the scholarship.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address where we can contact you for further information. Please ensure it is accurate and active.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
