# Volunteer Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for volunteer scholarships.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address to identify yourself as an applicant.
2. Provide your school name and school year to give us context about your educational background.
3. Enter the number of volunteer hours you have completed.
4. Select the scholarship type you are applying for.
5. Indicate the level of your school (e.g. undergraduate, graduate, etc.).
6. Choose how you submitted the application (e.g. online, via email).
7. Check the review status of your application (e.g. pending, reviewed).
8. If your application has been reviewed, indicate the review date and the reviewer's name.
9. If your application has been reviewed, select the decision status and provide a reason for the decision.
10. Enter the award amount and payment term.
11. Indicate whether the application fee has been paid or not.
12. Select the scholarship committee that handled your application.
13. Choose who reviewed your application.
14. Enter a review score out of 10.
15. Add any additional information in the "Additional Fields" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, optional): Please fill in your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide a phone number where we can contact you.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school name where you studied or currently studying.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total number of volunteer hours you have completed.
* **Volunteer Date** (`volunteer_date`, date, optional): The date you completed your volunteer hours.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, text, optional): Enter the academic year you are in (e.g. 2022-2023).
* **Scholarship Type** (`scholarship_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of scholarship you are applying for.
* **School Level** (`school_level`, select_one, optional): Indicate your school level (e.g. undergraduate, graduate, etc.).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select how you submitted your application (e.g. online, email).
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): Check the review status of your application (e.g. pending, reviewed).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): The date you submitted your application.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): If reviewed, enter the date your application was reviewed.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): If reviewed, enter the reviewer's name.
* **Review Comment** (`review_comment`, text, optional): If reviewed, enter a comment from the reviewer.
* **Decision Status** (`decision_status`, select_one, optional): Select the decision status of your application (e.g. awarded, not awarded).
* **Reason for Decision** (`reason_for_decision`, text, optional): If not awarded, enter a reason for the decision.
* **Award Amount** (`award_amount`, number, optional): Enter the award amount you received.
* **Payment Term** (`payment_term`, select_one, optional): Select the payment term (e.g. one-time, recurring).
* **Paid Out** (`paid_out`, select_one, optional): Check if the application fee has been paid.
* **Application Fee** (`application_fee`, number, optional): Enter the application fee (if any).
* **Scholarship Committee** (`scholarship_committee`, select_one, optional): Select the scholarship committee that reviewed your application.
* **Review By** (`review_by`, select_one, optional): Select who reviewed your application.
* **Review Score** (`review_score`, number, optional): Enter the review score out of 10.
* **Additional Fields** (`additional_fields`, note, optional): Add any additional information that's relevant to your application.
