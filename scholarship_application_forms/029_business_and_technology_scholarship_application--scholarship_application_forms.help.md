# Business And Technology Scholarship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Business And Technology Scholarship Application form, which aims to collect comprehensive information from applicants for a scholarship program in the business and technology fields.

## How To Complete This Form

To submit a successful application, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide accurate and honest information about yourself, your education, and your financial situation.
2.  Answer each question thoroughly, and make sure to provide clear and concise responses.
3.  Review your application carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your birth date.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your mailing address.
- **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
- **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province.
- **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
- **Academic Information** (`academic_info`, `note`, not required): This is a space to provide more details about your education, but it's not compulsory.
- **Institution Name** (`institution`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your school or university.
- **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of institution where you are currently studying (e.g., High School, Community College, University, etc.).
- **Major or Field of Study** (`major`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary field of study (e.g., Business Administration, Computer Science, etc.).
- **Current Year of Study** (`current_year`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your current year of study (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, etc.).
- **Current GPA** (`gpa`, `number`, required): Enter your current GPA.
- **Expected Graduation Date** (`expected_graduation`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you plan to graduate.
- **Financial Information** (`financial_info`, `note`, not required): This section is optional, but it's a chance to provide more details about your financial situation.
- **Level of Financial Need** (`financial_need`, `select_one`, required): Describe how you would rate your financial need (e.g., High Need, Moderate Need, etc.).
- **Current Financial Aid** (`current_aid`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select any financial aid you currently receive (e.g., Federal Grants, State Grants, etc.).
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your current employment status (e.g., Not Employed, Part-Time, etc.).
- **Scholarship Essay** (`scholarship_essay`, `note`, not required): This is an optional space to provide more details, but it's not required for submission.
- **Essay Question 1** (`essay_question_1`, `text`, required): Answer the first essay question: "Why do you deserve this scholarship?"
- **Essay Question 2** (`essay_question_2`, `text`, required): Answer the second essay question: "How will this scholarship help your career goals?"
- **Essay Question 3** (`essay_question_3`, `text`, not required): This essay question is optional, but provide more details about a challenge you've overcome.
- **Achievements** (`achievements`, `note`, not required): This section is optional, and you can provide information about your honours, awards, or recognition.
- **Academic Achievements** (`academic_achievements`, `text`, not required): Enter any academic honors, awards, or recognition you've received.
- **Extracurricular Activities** (`extracurricular`, `text`, not required): List any clubs, organizations, or activities you are involved in.
- **Community Service** (`community_service`, `text`, not required): Describe any volunteer work or community involvement you've had.
- **Leadership Roles** (`leadership_roles`, `text`, not required): If you've held leadership positions, list them here.
- **References** (`references`, `text`, required): Provide information about your references.
- **Transcript** (`transcript_upload`, `text`, required): Describe how you will submit your transcript (e.g., uploaded, mailed, etc.).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional information you'd like to share.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate in your responses.
*   Review your application carefully before submission.
*   Double-check your contact information.
