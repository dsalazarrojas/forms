# Scholarship Program Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Program Approval Form is a document that collects and verifies information from applicants to the scholarship program. It is essential for the application process and should be completed with attention to detail.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Review the entire form carefully**: Before starting, take a moment to review the form to understand what information is being asked.
2.  **Answer each question honestly**: Provide accurate and truthful information in each field.
3.  **Submit the form once complete**: Once you have filled out the form, review it carefully and submit it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info Form** (`applicant_info_form`, Text, Optional):
    This section collects the applicant's personal details. Please enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information as prompted.
* **Program Details** (`program_details_form`, Text, Optional):
    In this section, please provide a brief description of the program you are applying for, including the scholarship name and any relevant details.
* **Approval Form** (`approval_form`, Text, Optional):
    This section is for the approval of your application. Please fill out your supervisor's or program coordinator's name and email address for verification purposes.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
*   Remember to keep your contact information up-to-date to ensure that we can contact you efficiently.
