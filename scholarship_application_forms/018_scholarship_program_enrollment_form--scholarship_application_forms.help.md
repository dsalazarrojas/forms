# Scholarship Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from students who wish to enroll in a scholarship program. The purpose of this form is to collect contact details, academic and family information, financial information, and other relevant documents required for scholarship application. Please ensure you have all necessary documents ready before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on the "Contact Information" page.
2. Enter your academic details on the "Academic Info" page.
3. Provide your guardian's information on the "Guardian Information" page.
4. Enter your family's information on the "Family Information" page.
5. Enter your address on the "Address" page.
6. Fill in your emergency contact details on the "Emergency Contact" page.
7. Indicate your student status on the "Student Status" page.
8. Enter your scholarship information on the "Scholarship Information" page.
9. Fill in your financial information on the "Financial Information" page.
10. Request a transcript on the "Transcript Request" page.
11. Upload your resume on the "Upload Resume" page.
12. Upload your transcript on the "Upload Transcript" page.
13. Upload other required documents on the "Upload Other Document" page.
14. Upload proof of your ID on the "Upload ID Proof" page.
15. Confirm your submission on the "Confirm Submission" page.
16. Upload a screenshot of your device or system on the "Upload Screenshot" page.
17. Upload your signature on the "Upload Signature" page.
18. Upload proof of your residency on the "Upload Proof of Residency" page.
19. Upload your passport on the "Upload Passport" page.
20. Upload your birth certificate on the "Upload Birth Certificate" page.
21. Upload your degree certificate on the "Upload Degree Certificate" page.
22. Upload your ID card on the "Upload ID Card" page.
23. Upload other documents on the "Upload Other Documents" page.
