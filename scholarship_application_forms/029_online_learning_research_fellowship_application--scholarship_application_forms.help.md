# Online Learning Research Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help applicants apply for an Online Learning Research Fellowship. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about the applicant's background, research interests, and goals. By filling out this form, applicants can provide a comprehensive overview of their qualifications and research objectives, which will be evaluated by the selection committee for the fellowship award.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal and professional information in the "Candidate Information" section.
2. Describe your motivation for applying for the Online Learning Research Fellowship and the specific objectives of your research in the "What motivates your interest in this online research fellowship?" and "What are the objectives of your research?" sections.
3. Identify the area of study that aligns with your research interests in the "What area of study are you interested in?" section.
4. Outline the problem you aim to solve with your research in the "What problem do you want to solve with this research?" section.
5. Specify the methods you plan to use for your research in the "What methods do you plan to use for your research?" section.
6. Describe the expected outcomes of your research in the "What are the expected outcomes of your research?" section.
7. Upload your CV and other relevant supporting documents (if required).
8. Provide links to your publications and references (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`Candidate Information`, text, required: false): Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **What motivates your interest in this online research fellowship?** (`research_question`, text, required: false): Describe the motivations and reasons behind your application. 
* **What are the objectives of your research?** (`research_objectives`, text, required: false): Outline the specific goals and objectives of your research project.
* **What area of study are you interested in?** (`research_area`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the following areas: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, or Computer Vision.
* **What problem do you want to solve with this research?** (`research_question2`, text, required: false): Describe the problem you aim to tackle with your research.
* **What methods do you plan to use for your research?** (`research_methods`, text, required: false): Specify the methods and techniques you will employ in your research.
* **What are the expected outcomes of your research?** (`research_outcomes`, text, required: false): Describe the expected results and outcomes of your research.
* **CV link** (`cv_link`, text, required: false): Upload a link to your CV or resume.
* **Research experience** (`research_experience`, note, required: false): Provide a brief description of your research experience, including any relevant projects or achievements.
* **Publication link** (`publication_link`, text, required: false): Upload a link to a relevant publication or paper.
* **Reference links** (`reference_links`, text, required: false): Provide links to any relevant references or citations.
* **Research proposal** (`research_proposal`, text, required: false): Write a brief summary of your research proposal.
* **CV upload** (`cv_upload`, text, required: false): Upload your CV or resume.
* **Transcripts** (`transcript`, text, required: false): Upload transcripts of any relevant academic or professional certifications.
