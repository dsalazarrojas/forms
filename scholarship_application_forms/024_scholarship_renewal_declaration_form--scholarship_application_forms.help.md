# Scholarship Renewal Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Scholarship Renewal Declaration Form is used by students to provide information necessary to renew a scholarship. This form is used internally by the institution to review and process the renewal request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Enter your current student name in the "Student Name" field.
- Enter your student ID in the "Student ID" field.
- Select the name of your current program from the "Program Name" field.
- Enter the start date of your current scholarship in the "Start Date" field.
- Enter the end date of your current scholarship in the "End Date" field.
- Enter the reason for your scholarship renewal request in the "Reason for Renewal" field.
- Add any additional comments about your scholarship renewal request in the "Comments" field.
- Select the option that best represents who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.
- Enter the date the form was submitted in the "Submitted On" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Scholarship Renewal Declaration**: This field is the main content area where you can enter your renewal request.
* **Student Name**: Enter your current student name as it appears on your records.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID as it appears on your records.
* **Program Name**: Select the name of your current program.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your current scholarship.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your current scholarship.
* **Reason for Renewal**: Explain why you are requesting a scholarship renewal.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your scholarship renewal request.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
