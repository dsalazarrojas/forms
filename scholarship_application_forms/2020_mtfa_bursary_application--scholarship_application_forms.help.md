This form is a 2020 Mtfa Bursary Application form, designed to collect information from applicants seeking financial assistance. Before proceeding, please ensure that you are submitting a new application or renewal. If you have previously submitted an application, ensure that you are not duplicating your efforts.

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

**How To Complete This Form**

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with your accurate and complete information.
2. Provide the necessary supporting documentation as required.
3. Answer questions truthfully and comprehensively.
4. Ensure all sections are filled out completely before submitting.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): We will contact you using this email.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): Include your country code if applicable.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the given options.
* **Home address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your home address, including street, city, postal code, and country.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, text, required): Enter your nationality.
* **Current educational institution** (`current_institution`, text, required): Enter the name of your current educational institution.
* **Program of study** (`program_of_study`, text, required): Enter the degree or certification program you are enrolled in.
* **Year of study** (`year_of_study`, select_one, required): Select your year of study from the given options.
* **Current GPA** (`gpa`, decimal, required): Enter your current GPA on a scale of 0.0 to 4.0.
* **Expected graduation date** (`expected_graduation_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you expect to graduate.
* **Application date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are applying.
* **Type of application** (`application_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of application you are submitting (new, renewal, transfer, or amendment).
* **Amount of bursary requested** (`bursary_amount_requested`, decimal, required): Enter the amount of bursary you are requesting in local currency.
* **Purpose or reason for applying** (`purpose`, text, required): Explain why you need financial assistance.
* **Academic achievements** (`academic_achievements`, text, optional): List any scholarships, awards, or honors you have received.
* **Extracurricular activities** (`extracurricular_activities`, text, optional): List any clubs, sports, volunteer work, or leadership roles you are involved in.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your current employment status.
* **Annual household income** (`annual_income`, decimal, required): Enter your estimated total household income in local currency.
* **Special financial circumstances** (`financial_circumstances`, text, optional): Describe any exceptional situations affecting your finances.
* **References** (`references`, text, required): Provide two references with contact information.
* **Additional supporting information** (`supporting_details`, text, optional): Add any other information that supports your application.
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, select_one, required): Confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other information you'd like to share.

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are filled out completely before submitting.
* Provide truthful and comprehensive information.
* Double-check for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
