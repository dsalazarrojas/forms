# Human Centered Education Project Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Human Centered Education Project Application form is designed to collect information from applicants for a specific project focused on human-centered education initiatives. This form helps the project team assess and evaluate applications from interested parties, ensuring all relevant details are captured to make informed decisions about project approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form data section with any relevant details about yourself or your organization.
2.  Provide your contact information, including any relevant contact details such as email address and phone number.
3.  Describe your project information, including any background or context related to the project.
4.  Select the project objectives that best align with your project.
5.  Enter the project budget, if applicable.
6.  Provide a justification for your project budget, if requested.
7.  Upload any supporting documents required for your project.
8.  Outline your project goals and outcomes.
9.  List any partners involved in your project.
10. Review and submit the form once all required information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. form_data
*   **Form Data** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): A text field where you can provide any relevant details about yourself or your organization.

### 2. contact_info
*   **Contact Info** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): Fill in your contact details such as email address and phone number to ensure the project team can reach you if needed.

### 3. project_info
*   **Project Info** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): This field is for describing your project background or context, if any.

### 4. project_objectives
*   **Project Objectives** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): Choose the objectives that best align with your project goals, from the list provided (Objectives 1-10).

### 5. project_budget
*   **Project Budget** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): Enter the project budget if applicable.

### 6. project_budget_justification
*   **Project Budget Justification** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): A text field for providing a justification for your project budget, if requested.

### 7. supporting_documents
*   **Supporting Documents** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): Upload any necessary documents for your project, such as project plans, timelines, or resumes.

### 8. project_goals
*   **Project Goals** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): Describe your project goals, which are the outcomes or objectives you aim to achieve with this project.

### 9. project_outcomes
*   **Project Outcomes** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): Outline your project outcomes, which are the results or accomplishments that you hope to achieve.

### 10. project_partners
*   **Project Partners** (`name`, `type`, **Required:** no): List any partners involved in your project, from the options provided (Partner 1-10).
