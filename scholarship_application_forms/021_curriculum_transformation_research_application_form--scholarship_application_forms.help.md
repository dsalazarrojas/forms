# Curriculum Transformation Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Transformation Research Application Form is used to collect information from researchers who wish to apply for a scholarship to support their curriculum transformation research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are eligible to apply for the scholarship.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, providing all required and optional information.
3. Submit the form to the scholarship committee for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, note, required: false): This is the introduction to the form. It is not a required field and is simply a heading.
* **Section 1** (`section_1_header`, note, required: false): This is the header for the first section of the form. It is not a required field and is simply a heading.
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): This is where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it should be displayed on the scholarship documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Section 2** (`section_2_header`, note, required: false): This is the header for the second section of the form. It is not a required field and is simply a heading.
* **Research Proposal** (`research_proposal`, text, required: false): This is where you can provide a detailed description of your research proposal.
* **Research Proposal Details** (`research_proposal_details`, text, required: false): Please provide any additional details about your research proposal.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required: false): If applicable, please provide your budget details. If not, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are eligible to apply for the scholarship before completing this form.
* Please ensure that you are not duplicating an application that has already been submitted.
* If you are unsure about your eligibility or have already submitted an application, please contact the scholarship committee for clarification.
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely, providing all required and optional information.
* Submit the form to the scholarship committee for review.
