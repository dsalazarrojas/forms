# Scholarship Application Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for students to gather the necessary information for scholarship applications. The form will collect data on the student's personal details, academic performance, and supporting documents. Completing this form thoroughly will ensure that the scholarship application is complete and submitted correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student Name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your valid Email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Student Phone" field (optional).
4. Select the date you requested your transcript, if applicable, in the "Transcript Request Date" field.
5. Select the date you requested your GPA information, if applicable, in the "GPA Request Date" field.
6. Specify the status of your transcript request in the "Transcript Request Status" field (Complete, Incomplete, or Not Started).
7. Do the same for your GPA request status in the "GPA Request Status" field.
8. Enter your Student Address, City, and Zip code (if applicable) in the respective fields.
9. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Student DOB" field.
10. Enter your Student GPA and select your Student GPA scale from the options provided in the "Student GPA" and "Student GPA Scale" fields respectively.
11. Select your Student Citizenship Status from the options provided.
12. Enter your Social Security Number (SSN) in the "Student SSN" field.
13. If required, enter your essay or additional comments in the "Student Essay" and "Additional Comments" fields respectively.
14. Select the provider of your transcript (High School, University, Community College) in the "Transcript Provider" field.
15. Enter any additional information for transcript request date in the "Transcript Request Date Additional Info" field.
16. Do the same for your GPA request date in the "GPA Request Date Additional Info" field.
17. Sign the form with your Student Signature, Parent Signature, and School Signature (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Transcript Request Date**: Select the date you requested your transcript.
* **GPA Request Date**: Select the date you requested your GPA information.
* **Transcript Request Status**: Specify the status of your transcript request (Complete, Incomplete, or Not Started).
* **GPA Request Status**: Specify the status of your GPA request (Complete, Incomplete, or Not Started).
* **Student Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Student City**: Enter your city.
* **Student State**: Enter your state.
* **Student Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Student DOB**: Enter your Date of Birth.
* **Student GPA**: Enter your current GPA.
* **Student GPA Scale**: Select the GPA scale that you used.
* **Student Citizenship Status**: Select your current citizenship status.
* **Student SSN**: Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Student Essay**: Enter your essay or comments (if required).
* **Transcript Provider**: Select the provider of your transcript (High School, University, Community College).
* **Transcript Request Date Additional Info**: Enter any additional information for transcript request date.
* **GPA Request Date Additional Info**: Enter any additional information for GPA request date.
* **Student Signature**: Sign the form with your student signature.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign the form with your parent signature.
* **School Signature**: Sign the form with your school signature (if required).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or comments about your application.
