# Student Enrolment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Enrolment Application Form is designed to collect information about a student's enrollment status, personal details, and documentation requirements. This form is intended for parents or guardians to provide necessary information to ensure accurate and efficient student enrollment processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Student Enrolment Application Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure all fields are correctly filled before submitting the form.
3. Review and confirm the submitted information before finalizing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: A text field where you can enter the student's name.
* **Student Age**: A number field to enter the student's age.
* **Student Date of Birth**: A date field to enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Address**: A text field where you can enter the student's address.
* **Parent Details**: A text field where you can enter any additional details about the student's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Name**: A text field to enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Name 2**: A text field to enter any additional parent's name.
* **Guardian Details**: A text field where you can enter any additional details about the student's guardian.
* **Guardian Name**: A text field to enter the guardian's name.
* **Guardian Name 2**: A text field to enter any additional guardian's name.
* **Student Enrolment**: A select_one field to confirm the student's enrollment status.
* **Student Enrolment 2**: A select_one field to confirm the student's enrollment status (if required).
* **School Year**: A select_one field to select the school year the student is enrolled in.
* **Student Enrolment Status**: A select_one field to confirm the student's enrollment status.
* **Student Enrolment Date**: A date field to enter the date of the student's enrollment.
* **Student Enrolment Note**: A note field for any additional comments or notes about the student's enrollment.
* **Student Email**: A text field to enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: A text field to enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Documentation**: A select_multiple field to select the types of documentation required.
* **Student Date of Birth Document**: A text field to enter the date of birth document details.
* **Student Address Document**: A text field to enter the address document details.
* **Guardian Documentation**: A select_multiple field to select the types of documentation required.
* **Parent Documentation**: A select_multiple field to select the types of documentation required.
* **Submit**: A select_one field to finalize the form submission.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
