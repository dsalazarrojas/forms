# pearls_scholarship_app - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PEARLS scholarship application form is designed to collect information from students who want to apply to the PEARLS program. The form helps us understand your background, academic status, and other relevant details to assess your eligibility for the scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus for select type fields.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the program administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: This section is for providing your general information, such as your name, email, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you regarding your application.
* **PEARLS Program**: Select the PEARLS program you are applying for (PEARLS, PearlsPlus, or PearlsMax).
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **School Name**: Enter your school's name.
* **School Year**: Enter your current grade level (e.g., 9-12).
* **Expected Graduation Date**: Enter your expected graduation date.
* **Current Grade Level**: Select your current grade level (e.g., 1-12).
* **Student Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Parental Contact Name**: Enter your parent's name.
* **Parental Contact Phone**: Enter your parent's phone number.
* **Parental Contact Email**: Enter your parent's email address.
* **Student-Parental Relationship**: Select how you are related to your parent (Biological, Adopted, Fostered).
* **Student-Parental Relationship Other**: If you selected "Other", describe your relationship with your parent.
* **School District**: Enter your school district's name.
* **Student-English Level**: Select your English language level (e.g., native, non-native).
* **Student-Math Level**: Select your math level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Student-Reading Level**: Select your reading level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Student-Social Studies Level**: Select your social studies level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Student-Science Level**: Select your science level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Hispanic Origin**: Select your Hispanic origin (Hispanic or Non-Hispanic).
* **Student Race**: Enter your racial identity (if applicable).
* **Student Disability**: Enter any disability you have (if applicable).
* **Student-Citizenship**: Select your citizenship status (Citizen or Non-Citizen).
* **Student-Country of Origin**: Enter your country of origin (if not the United States).
* **Student-Language**: Select your native language (e.g., English, Spanish).
* **Other (If other, specify)**: If you selected "Other", specify your language.
* **Lived Abroad**: Select if you have lived abroad.
* **If yes, country**: If you selected "Yes", enter the country where you lived.
* **School District Other**: If you selected "Other", describe your school district.
* **Student Languages Spoken**: Select your languages spoken (e.g., English, Spanish, Both).
* **Additional Languages Spoken**: Enter any additional languages you speak.
* **Other (Specify)**: If you selected "Other", specify the language you speak.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the program administrator.
