# Global Teaching Strategies Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Teaching Strategies Project application form is designed to collect information from applicants about their background, experience, and qualifications for the project. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the 'Required' label on each field.
2. Enter your project title and a brief description of your proposed project.
3. Provide your name and email address.
4. Enter your date of birth and the date you are applying for the project.
5. List the school you attended and your degree program.
6. Check your teaching area of expertise.
7. Select your teaching level (High or Low).
8. Enter the number of years you have experience in your teaching area.
9. Attach your CV if you have one (not required but recommended).
10. Write a brief project proposal explaining why you are a good fit for the project.
11. Provide any additional comments about your application.
12. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (1, text, Required): Enter a brief and descriptive title for your project.
* **Applicant Name** (2, text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (3, email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (4, text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Application** (6, date, Required): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
* **School Attended** (7, text, Required): Enter the school you attended.
* **Degree Program** (8, text, Required): Enter your degree program (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Ph.D.).
* **Teaching Area** (9, select_multiple, Required): Check all teaching areas you have expertise in.
* **Teaching Level** (10, select_one, Required): Select your teaching level (High or Low).
* **Experience (Years)** (11, number, Required): Enter the number of years you have experience in your teaching area.
* **CV Attachment** (12, note, Optional): Attach your CV if you have one.
* **Project Proposal** (13, text, Required): Write a brief proposal explaining why you are a good fit for the project.
* **Additional Comments** (14, text, Optional): Provide any additional comments about your application.
* **Signature** (15, text, Required): Sign and date the form to confirm your application.

Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to increase your chances of being accepted into the Global Teaching Strategies Project.
