# real_estate_market_research_grant_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for applicants to apply for a grant for real estate market research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your contact information, including name, email, and job title.
2. Provide a brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
3. Outline the budget for your project, including estimated costs and expenses.
4. Select the funding source for your project (Government Grants, Private Grants, or Other).
5. Choose the project type (University, Nonprofit, or Real Estate Organization).
6. Provide any relevant notes or comments for the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Info** (1): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Info** (2): Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Project Info** (3): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Goals** (4): Enter a brief description of your project goals and objectives.
* **Project Budget** (5): Enter the estimated budget for your project.
* **Project Location** (6): Enter the location of your project.
* **Project Description** (7): Enter a detailed description of your project.
* **Project Start Date** (8): Enter the expected start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (9): Enter the expected end date of your project.
* **Project Budget Amount** (10): Enter the total budget for your project.
* **Funding Source** (11): Select the source of funding for your project (Government Grants, Private Grants, or Other).
* **Project Status** (12): Select the current status of your project (Active, Pending, or Completed).
* **Project Type** (13): Select the type of project (University, Nonprofit, or Real Estate Organization).
* **Submitter Info** (14): Enter any additional information about yourself as the submitter.
* **Submitter Name** (15): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (16): Enter your email.
* **Submitter Job Title** (17): Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Institution** (18): Enter the institution you represent (if applicable).
* **Submitter Contact Info** (19): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Submitter Phone** (20): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Note** (21): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Review Notes** (22): Enter any additional comments or notes for the reviewer.
