# Wildlife Conservation Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Conservation Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Wildlife Conservation Fellowship program. The form is used to gather details about the applicant's general information, contact details, work experience, education background, motivation for conservation, research interest, project proposal, references, and attachments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your general information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your contact details, including your country and postal code.
3. Share your work experience related to wildlife conservation.
4. Describe your education background, including your academic degrees and institutions.
5. Explain your motivation for conservation and why you are applying for the fellowship.
6. Share your research interest and how it relates to conservation.
7. Propose a project that you would like to undertake as part of the fellowship.
8. List your references, including their names, titles, and contact information.
9. Upload any relevant attachments, such as your resume or a cover letter.
10. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: This field is used to collect your basic contact information. Please fill in your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Contact Details**: This field is used to collect your country and postal code. Please note that this information will be used to contact you and may be shared with the fellowship organizers.
* **Work Experience**: This field is used to collect your work experience related to wildlife conservation. Please describe any relevant work experience, including your job title, job description, and the duration of your employment.
* **Education Background**: This field is used to collect your education background. Please describe your academic degrees, institutions, and any relevant coursework or research experience.
* **Conservation Motivation**: This field is used to collect your motivation for conservation and why you are applying for the fellowship. Please answer this question in a few paragraphs.
* **Research Interest**: This field is used to collect your research interest and how it relates to conservation. Please describe your research focus and how it aligns with the fellowship's goals.
* **Project Proposal**: This field is used to collect a project proposal that you would like to undertake as part of the fellowship. Please describe your project, including its objectives, methodology, and expected outcomes.
* **References**: This field is used to collect the names, titles, and contact information of your references. Please ensure that your references are aware of your application and are willing to be contacted by the fellowship organizers.
* **Attachments**: This field is used to upload any relevant attachments, such as your resume or a cover letter. Please ensure that your attachments are in a format that can be opened by the fellowship organizers.
* **Submit**: This field is used to submit your application. Please review your application carefully before submitting it.
