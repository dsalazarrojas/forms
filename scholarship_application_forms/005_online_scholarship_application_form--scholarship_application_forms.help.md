# Online Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information for online scholarship applications. It covers details about the applicant's name, contact information, academic background, and qualifications relevant to the scholarship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the **Email** field.
3. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Select the scholarship program you are applying for from the **Scholarship Program** dropdown menu.
5. Choose your current and intended academic level from the **Academic Program** dropdown menu.
6. Select your current academic level from the **Academic Level** dropdown menu.
7. Choose your current and intended academic levels (if different) from the **Current Level** dropdown menu.
8. Upload your resume in the **Resume** field if required by the scholarship.
9. Upload your transcript in the **Transcript** field if required by the scholarship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Required/Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: This is the field where you will enter your full name.
* **Email** (Required/Optional): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
	+ Type: email
	+ Purpose: This is the field where you will enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Required/Optional): Enter your phone number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: This is the field where you will enter your phone number.
* **Scholarship Program** (Required/Optional): Choose the scholarship program you are applying for from the list of options.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Purpose: This is the field where you will select the scholarship program you are interested in.
* **Academic Program** (Required/Optional): Choose your intended academic program from the list of options.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: This is the field where you will select your intended academic program.
* **Academic Level** (Required/Optional): Choose your current academic level from the list of options.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: This is the field where you will select your current academic level.
* **Current Level** (Required/Optional): Choose your current and intended academic level (if different) from the list of options.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Purpose: This is the field where you will select your current and intended academic levels.
* **Resume** (Required/Optional): Upload your resume if required by the scholarship.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: This is the field where you will upload your resume.
* **Transcript** (Required/Optional): Upload your transcript if required by the scholarship.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: This is the field where you will upload your transcript.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have attached all the required documents (resume and transcript) separately.
* Double-check your selections and information before submitting the application.
* If you have any doubts or queries, please contact the scholarship committee for clarification.
