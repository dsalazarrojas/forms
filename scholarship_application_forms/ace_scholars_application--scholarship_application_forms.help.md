# ACE Scholars Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide provides assistance to help applicants complete the ACE Scholars Application form accurately and efficiently. This form is designed to gather information about your personal, academic, and financial situation to evaluate your eligibility for the ACE Scholars award.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure you have all necessary documents and information ready. This form is divided into sections, each with specific fields that require completion. 

1. **Personal Information**: Fill in your Full name, Email address, Date of birth, Home address, and Phone number (if provided).
2. **Academic Information**: Enter your Current school or institution, Expected graduation date, Intended major or field of study, Current GPA, Academic year level, and other relevant details.
3. **Church Affiliation**: Provide your local A.M.E. Zion Church's name and the name of your Pastor.
4. **Financial Information**: Enter the Expected cost of attendance for the next academic year, Other scholarships or financial aid received, and a brief explanation of your financial need.
5. **Essay Questions**: Answer the Academic and career goals essay, Service commitment essay, and Additional comments or notes (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <label> Full name</label> (Required)
Please enter your legal name as it appears on official documents. This is a required field to validate your identity.

### 2. <label> Email address</label> (Required)
Enter the email address you want us to use for correspondence. This is a required field for communication purposes.

### 3. <label> Date of birth</label> (Required)
Enter your date of birth to verify your age and eligibility.

### 4. <label> Home address</label> (Required)
Provide your street address, city, state, and ZIP code to help us contact you.

### 5. <label> Current school or institution</label> (Required)
Enter the name of the school you are currently attending.

### 6. <label> Expected graduation date</label> (Required)
Enter your expected graduation date to assess your academic progress.

### 7. <label> Intended major or field of study</label> (Required)
List your intended major or field of study to help us understand your academic focus.

### 8. <label> Current GPA</label> (Required)
Enter your cumulative GPA on a 4.0 scale to demonstrate academic performance.

### 9. <label> Academic year level</label> (Required)
Select your current academic year level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, Graduate student).

### 10. <label> Name of your local A.M.E. Zion Church</label> (Required)
Provide the name of your local A.M.E. Zion Church.

### 11. <label> Pastor's name</label> (Required)
Enter the name of your pastor to verify your church affiliation.

### 12. <label> Years as active member in A.M.E. Zion Church</label> (Required)
Enter the number of years you have been an active member of A.M.E. Zion Church.

### 13. <label> Leadership roles in church (select all that apply)</label> (Optional)
List all leadership roles you have within the church.

### 15. <label> Other scholarships or financial aid received</label> (Optional)
List any other scholarships or financial aid you have received.

Provide a brief explanation of your financial situation.

Add any additional comments or notes that you think would be helpful.
