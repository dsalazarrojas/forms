# Educational Research Startup Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential applicants for the Educational Research Startup Grant. The grant is meant to support innovative research projects in education.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as a researcher, including your name.
2. Provide the name and contact information of your university or institution.
3. Specify whether you have any documents required for the application.
4. Enter the details of your proposed project, including the grant period and start date.
5. Enter the amount of the grant you are seeking.
6. Confirm your contact email and phone number.
7. Optionally, provide the name and address of the institution where your project will be conducted.
8. Fill out the project title and description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **researcher** (Text, Required/Optional): This is the field where you will enter your name as the researcher. Please make sure to enter your name correctly.
* **university** (Text, Required): This is the field where you will enter the name of your university or institution.
* **budget** (Number, Required): This is the field where you will enter the amount of grant you are seeking.
* **documents** (Select Multiple, Required): This is a field where you will select whether you have any required documents.
* **proposal** (Note, Optional): This is a field where you can provide additional information about your project.
* **contact_email** (Email, Required): This is the field where you will enter your contact email.
* **contact_phone** (Text, Optional): This is the field where you will enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_address** (Text, Optional): This is the field where you will enter your contact address.
* **category** (Select One, Required): This is the field where you will select the category of your project.
* **subcategory** (Select One, Optional): This is the field where you will select the subcategory of your project.
* **grant_amount** (Number, Required): This is the field where you will enter the amount of grant you are seeking.
* **grant_period** (Time, Required): This is the field where you will enter the period of the grant you are seeking.
* **grant_start** (Date, Required): This is the field where you will enter the start date of the grant.
* **grant_end** (Date, Required): This is the field where you will enter the end date of the grant.
* **grant_status** (Select One, Optional): This is the field where you will select the status of the grant.
* **institution_name** (Text, Required): This is the field where you will enter the name of your institution.
* **institution_address** (Text, Optional): This is the field where you will enter the address of your institution.
* **institution_country** (Text, Optional): This is the field where you will enter the country of your institution.
* **institution_state** (Text, Optional): This is the field where you will enter the state of your institution.
* **institution_city** (Text, Optional): This is the field where you will enter the city of your institution.
* **institution_zip** (Text, Optional): This is the field where you will enter the zip code of your institution.
* **institution_phone** (Text, Optional): This is the field where you will enter the phone number of your institution.
* **institution_email** (Email, Optional): This is the field where you will enter the email of your institution.
* **project_title** (Text, Required): This is the field where you will enter the title of your project.
* **project_description** (Text, Required): This is the field where you will enter the description of your project.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately.
- Make sure to attach all necessary documents before submitting the application.
- Double-check your contact information for errors.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, contact the relevant authorities.
- Do not submit duplicate applications.
