# scholarship_personal_information_update - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship_personal_information_update form is used to collect and update the personal information of a student applying for a scholarship. This form gathers crucial details such as contact information, student background, and family details to assess the student's eligibility for the scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter your student's ID number in the "student_id" field.
3. Provide your contact number in the "contact_number" field (optional).
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Select your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
6. Choose your gender from the "gender" dropdown menu.
7. Select your scholarship program of interest from the "scholarship_program" dropdown menu.
8. Choose whether you are a current student from the "current_student" dropdown menu.
9. Select your previous school from the "previous_school" dropdown menu.
10. Choose your highest qualification achieved from the "highest_qualification" dropdown menu.
11. Select your current grade from the "current_grade" dropdown menu.
12. Enter your student address in the "student_address" field.
13. Enter your country of residence in the "student_country" field.
14. Enter your guardian's name in the "guardian_name" field.
15. Enter your guardian's contact number in the "guardian_contact_number" field (optional).
16. Enter your guardian's email in the "guardian_email" field (optional).
17. Enter your guardian's address in the "guardian_address" field (optional).
18. Enter your parent's income in the "parent_income" field.
19. Upload a photo of your student in the "student_image" field.
20. Upload supporting documents in the "student_document" field.
21. Provide any additional student certificates or credentials in the "student_certificate" field (optional).
22. Enter information about your parent's education in the "parent_education" field.
23. Select your career aspiration from the "student_career" dropdown menu.
24. Choose your current student status from the "student_status" dropdown menu.
25. Add any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name**: A text field to enter your student's name.
* **student_id**: A numerical field to enter your student's ID number.
* **contact_number**: A text field for your contact number, but not required.
* **email**: An email field to enter your email address.
* **date_of_birth**: A date field to select your date of birth.
* **gender**: A dropdown menu to choose your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **scholarship_program**: A dropdown menu to select your scholarship program of interest.
* **current_student**: A dropdown menu to choose whether you are a current student (true or false).
* **previous_school**: A dropdown menu to select your previous school.
* **highest_qualification**: A dropdown menu to choose your highest qualification achieved.
* **current_grade**: A dropdown menu to select your current grade.
* **student_address**: A text field to enter your student address.
* **student_country**: A text field to enter your country of residence.
* **guardian_name**: A text field to enter your guardian's name.
* **guardian_contact_number**: A text field for your guardian's contact number, but not required.
* **guardian_email**: An email field to enter your guardian's email, but not required.
* **guardian_address**: A text field to enter your guardian's address, but not required.
* **parent_income**: A numerical field to enter your parent's income.
* **student_image**: A text field to upload a photo of your student.
* **student_document**: A text field to upload supporting documents.
* **student_certificate**: A text field to provide additional student certificates or credentials, but not required.
* **parent_education**: A text field to enter information about your parent's education.
* **student_career**: A dropdown menu to select your career aspiration.
* **student_status**: A dropdown menu to choose your current student status.
* **notes**: A field to add any additional notes or comments, but not required.
