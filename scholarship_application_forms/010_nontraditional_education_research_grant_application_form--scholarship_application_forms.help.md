# Nontraditional Education Research Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants for non-traditional education research grants. It is used to gather details about the applicant's background, research topic, grant amount, and other relevant information to facilitate the review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Applicant Info section with your name, institution, and contact information (page 1).
2. Provide a brief description of your research topic in the Research Topic section (page 2).
3. Enter the grant amount you are requesting in the Grant Amount section (page 3).
4. Select the funding agency from the dropdown list in the Funding Agency section (page 4).
5. Choose the review committee members from the dropdown list in the Review Committee section (page 5).
6. Select the review process from the dropdown list in the Review Process section (page 6).
7. Enter the grant period in the Grant Period section (page 7).
8. Enter the submission deadline in the Submission Deadline section (page 8).
9. Provide any additional information or comments in the Review Timeline section (page 9) (optional).
10. Review and follow the submission guidelines in the Submission Guidelines section (page 10) (optional).
11. Attach any supporting documents or files in the Submission Form section (page 11) (optional).
12. Upload any additional attachment or supporting files in the Submission Attachment section (page 12) (optional).
13. Provide comments or feedback from the reviewer in the Reviewer Comments section (page 13) (optional).
14. Offer any suggestions for the review process in the Reviewer Suggestions section (page 14) (optional).
15. Enter any feedback or comments from the reviewer in the Reviewer Feedback section (page 15) (optional).
16. Select the review results from the dropdown list in the Review Results section (page 16).
17. Sign the Applicant Signature section with your signature (page 17).
18. Sign the Grant Manager Signature section with the grant manager's signature (page 18).
19. Sign the Reviewer Signature section with the reviewer's signature (page 19).
20. Answer the research questions in the Research Question sections (pages 20-26).
