# Scholarship Fund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Fund Request Form is used to collect information from applicants seeking support or funding through the scholarship program. The form helps the review committee evaluate each applicant's eligibility and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate and efficient processing of your application, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your personal details in the first field, "Applicant Details".
2.  Provide your contact information in the next field, "Contact Information".
3.  In "Scholarship Request", write a detailed description of your scholarship request, including the purpose, amount, and duration of the request.
4.  For "Funding Needs", provide a brief description of your financial needs.
5.  If you're affiliated with an organization, please provide your "Organization Information" in the "Organization Information" field.
6.  Describe your "Financial History" in the "Financial History" field.
7.  In "Review and Approval", select "Yes" if you've reviewed and agreed with all conditions. If this doesn't apply, choose "Other" and specify the reason.
8.  Similarly, in "Review and Approval 2", choose "Yes" if you've reviewed all conditions and requirements. If not, select "No" and provide a justification.
9.  For "Review and Approval 3", choose "Yes" if you've reviewed all conditions and requirements. If not, select "No" and provide a justification.
10.  In "Review and Approval 4", choose "Yes" if you've reviewed all conditions and requirements. If not, select "No" and provide a justification.
11.  In "Submission Agreement", indicate your understanding of the submission terms and conditions by entering the corresponding number.
12.  Finally, provide any additional comments or information in "Submission Agreement 2".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details**: Enter your full name, date of birth, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your name, email address, phone number, and any other contact information.
* **Scholarship Request**: Describe your scholarship request, including the purpose, amount, and duration of the request.
* **Funding Needs**: Describe your financial needs and how the scholarship will be used.
* **Organization Information**: If you're affiliated with an organization, provide its name, address, and contact information.
* **Financial History**: Describe your financial history, including any past financial aid or grants received.
* **Review and Approval**: Confirm that you've reviewed and agreed with all conditions of the scholarship program.
* **Review and Approval 2**: Confirm that you've reviewed all conditions and requirements of the scholarship program.
* **Review and Approval 3**: Confirm that you've reviewed all conditions and requirements of the scholarship program.
* **Review and Approval 4**: Confirm that you've reviewed all conditions and requirements of the scholarship program.
* **Submission Agreement**: Understand and agree to the submission terms and conditions of the scholarship program.
* **Submission Agreement 2**: Provide any additional comments or information related to your submission.
