# Sample Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for scholarship applicants to submit their applications for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully, making sure to provide enough detail for evaluation.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, not required)
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant details you want to share about yourself.
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, not required)
	+ Provide additional information that helps us understand your background and experiences.
* **Scholarship Details** (`scholarship_details`, text, not required)
	+ Share any relevant details about the scholarship you are applying for.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required)
	+ Enter your email, phone number, or any other way to contact you.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, not required)
	+ Share your educational background, including any degrees, diplomas, or certifications.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, text, not required)
	+ Provide information about your work experience, including job roles and dates.
* **Personal References** (`personal_references`, text, not required)
	+ List any relevant personal references, including their name, job title, and contact information.
