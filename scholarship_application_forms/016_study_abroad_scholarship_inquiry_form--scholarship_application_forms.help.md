# study_abroad_scholarship_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in applying for a study abroad scholarship. The information collected will be used to assess their eligibility and guide them through the scholarship application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information in the "Student Information" section.
2. Enter details about the scholarship you are interested in, including the type of scholarship and any specific requirements.
3. Share your contact information with us so we can get in touch with you about your application.
4. If applicable, upload any necessary documents as per the requirements of the scholarship.
5. In the "Submission Information" section, provide any additional details about your submission.
6. Finally, review your submission preferences in the "Scholarship Committee Preferences" section.
7. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (1)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ This field is where you can provide your name, email address, and other personal details.
* **Scholarship Information (2)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Here, you can enter details about the scholarship you are interested in, including its title, type, and any specific requirements.
* **Contact Information (3)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ This field is where you can provide your contact number and other means we can get in touch with you.
* **Scholarship Committee Information (4)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ If you have any specific requests or requirements for the scholarship committee, you can mention them here.
* **Upload Documents (5)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Upload any necessary documents as per the requirements of the scholarship. Make sure to check the requirements before uploading any files.
* **Upload Additional Documents (6)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ This field is for uploading any other documents that may be required for your scholarship application.
* **Student Accommodations (7)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ If you have any special accommodations or requirements, you can mention them here.
* **Submission Information (8)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Provide any additional details about your submission, such as your research proposal, language proficiency, etc.
* **Scholarship Committee Preferences (9)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Here, you can specify your preferences regarding the scholarship committee, such as a specific committee member or a specific time for a review.
* **Submit (10)**: 
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Click on "Submit" to complete the form and submit your inquiry.
