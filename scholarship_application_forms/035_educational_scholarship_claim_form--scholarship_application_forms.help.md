# Educational Scholarship Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to claim a scholarship for their educational expenses. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure your claim is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your student information in the fields marked as required.
2. Select the correct scholarship type and amount if applicable.
3. Upload required documents to support your claim.
4. Fill out your institution information if applicable.
5. Upload additional documents if required.
6. Sign the form with your student signature.
7. Have your institution representative sign the form with their signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or institution you are currently enrolled in or have been enrolled in.
* **Course Enrolled In** (`course_enrolled_in`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you are currently enrolled in or have been enrolled in.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Scholarship Type** (`scholarship_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of scholarship you are claiming for (e.g., part-time or full-time).
* **Scholarship Amount** (`scholarship_amount`, number, required): Enter the scholarship amount you are claiming.
* **Required Documents** (`required_documents`, select_multiple, required): Select the required documents to upload (e.g., transcripts, resumes, letters).
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, note, required): Upload the selected documents to support your claim.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Upload Additional Documents** (`upload_additional_documents`, note, required): Upload any additional documents required by the institution.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter your student address.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the institution where you are enrolled.
* **Upload Institution Documents** (`upload_institution_documents`, note, required): Upload any documents required by the institution.
* **Student Statement** (`student_statement`, text, optional): Enter a statement from the student explaining their scholarship claim.
* **Institution Statement** (`institution_statement`, text, optional): Enter a statement from the institution explaining their support for the scholarship claim.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, note, required): Sign the form with your student signature.
* **Institution Signature** (`institution_signature`, note, required): Sign the form with the institution representative's signature.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Student Country** (`student_country`, text, required): Enter the country where you are from.
* **Student State** (`student_state`, text, required): Enter the state where you are from.
* **Student City** (`student_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you are from.
* **Institution City** (`institution_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the institution where you are enrolled.
* **Institution State** (`institution_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the institution where you are enrolled.
