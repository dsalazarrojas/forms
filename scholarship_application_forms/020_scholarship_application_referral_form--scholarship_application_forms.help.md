# scholarship-application-referral-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for referral purposes, to collect information about a student or researcher who is being referred for a scholarship application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each page of the form, one at a time.
2. Answer each field accurately and completely, based on your knowledge of the referree.
3. Make sure to provide enough information to help evaluate the referree's eligibility and suitability for a scholarship.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the referree as you know it.
* **Referral Email** (`referral_email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter the email address of the referree if available.
* **Referral Phone** (`referral_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the phone number of the referree if available.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the student or researcher being referred.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter the email address of the student or researcher being referred if available.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the phone number of the student or researcher being referred if available.
* **Student Graduation Date** (`student_graduation_date`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date of graduation of the student or researcher being referred.
* **Student Degree** (`student_degree`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the student's degree level:
	+ High School Diploma
	+ Associates
	+ Bachelors
	+ Masters
	+ Phd
* **Student Field of Study** (`student_field_of_study`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the field of study of the student or researcher being referred.
* **Student Institution** (`student_institution`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the institution where the student or researcher received their degree.
* **Referral Institution** (`referral_institution`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the institution where you are referring from.
* **Referral Title** (`referral_title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your title or position at the referral institution.
* **Student Scholarship** (`student_scholarship`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please indicate if the student or researcher has received or is in the process of receiving a scholarship:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Research Experience** (`student_research_experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please indicate the student or researcher's research experience:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-5 years
	+ More than 5 years
* **Research Focus** (`student_research_focus`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a brief description of the student or researcher's research focus.
* **Referral Research Focus** (`referral_research_focus`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a brief description of the research focus of the referree at your institution.
* **Research Publications** (`student_research_publications`, `text`, required: false): Please list any publications of the student or researcher.
* **Referral Research Publications** (`referral_research_publications`, `text`, required: false): Please list any publications of the referree at your institution.
* **Research Presentations** (`student_research_presentations`, `text`, required: false): Please list any presentations of the student or researcher.
* **Referral Research Presentations** (`referral_research_presentations`, `text`, required: false): Please list any presentations of the referree at your institution.
* **Student Scholarship Awards** (`student_scholarship_awards`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please indicate if the student or researcher has received or is in the process of receiving an award:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Referral Scholarship Awards** (`referral_scholarship_awards`, `text`, required: false): Please list any awards received by the referree at your institution.
* **Student Scholarship Letters** (`student_scholarship_letters`, `text`, required: false): Please attach any scholarship letters received by the student or researcher.
* **Referral Scholarship Letters** (`referral_scholarship_letters`, `text`, required: false): Please attach any scholarship letters received by the referree at your institution.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, required: false): Please submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please do not submit duplicate referrals for the same person.
* This form is for referral purposes only, not for scholarship applications.
