# Educational Resilience Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect application information from individuals who wish to participate in the Educational Resilience Project. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that you do not miss any required fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact details section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide a brief summary of your proposed project in the proposal info section.
3. In the applicant information section, enter your relevant educational background.
4. Describe your educational resilience project in the project section.
5. If applicable, upload files related to your project in the upload files section.
6. Confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the submission terms in the confirm submission section.
7. Finally, click the submit button to complete and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to enable us to contact you if we need more information.
* **Proposal Info** (`proposal_info`, text, optional): Briefly describe your proposed project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): Enter your relevant educational background, including your academic credentials, qualifications, and experience.
* **Educational Background** (`educational_background`, text, optional): Describe your educational background, including your relevant coursework, research experience, and any relevant skills or qualifications.
* **Educational Resilience Project** (`educational_resilience_project`, text, optional): Describe your educational resilience project, including its objectives, goals, and any relevant details.
* **Upload Files** (`upload_files`, text, optional): If you have any relevant files, such as resumes or project proposals, you can upload them here.
* **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the submission terms.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete and submit your application.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have reviewed the form carefully and that it is relevant to your needs before proceeding. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
