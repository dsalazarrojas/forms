# Scholarship Period Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Period Extension Form is a form designed to help students request an extension to their scholarship period. This form is used to gather information about the student's situation and ensure that their request is legitimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for your extension request.
3. Specify your current academic program.
4. If necessary, upload any supporting documentation that backs up your request.
5. If you have any additional information that you would like to provide, you can do so in the "Additional Supporting Documentation" field.
6. Enter the date you would like your scholarship period to be extended to, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents. This field is required.
- **Email Address**: Enter your official email address where you can be reached by our office. This field is required.
- **Reason for Extension**: Provide a clear and concise reason for your request. This field is required.
- **Academic Program**: Select the academic program you are currently enrolled in or plan to enroll in (e.g., Bachelor of Arts and Sciences, Master of Science, etc.). This field is optional.
- **Supporting Documentation**: Upload any documentation that supports your request, such as a letter from your academic advisor or professor. This field is optional.
- **Additional Supporting Documentation**: If you have any additional information you would like to provide, you can upload it here. This field is optional.
- **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number. This field is optional.
- **Current Academic Year**: Enter the current academic year you are in. This field is optional.
- **Requested Extension Date**: Enter the date you would like your scholarship period to be extended to, using the format MM/DD/YYYY. This field is optional.
- **Student Email**: Enter your official student email address where we can contact you. This field is optional.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields carefully.
* Provide clear and concise answers to your reason for extension.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact our office.
