# Culinary Scholarship Application Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for the Culinary Scholarship. It is a required part of the scholarship application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your first name and last name in their respective fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in their respective fields.
3. If you have a resume, you may upload it as a PDF or Word document to provide more information about your background and experience.
4. If you have an unofficial transcript, you may upload it as a PDF document.
5. Write a brief essay (less than 500 words) describing your goals and motivations for applying for the scholarship.
6. Provide a letter of recommendation from a mentor or academic advisor.
7. Enter the name of the school you are currently enrolled or planning to enroll in.
8. Choose the program you are applying for (Culinary Arts, Hospitality Management, or Other).
9. Enter the date you submitted your application.
10. Select the type of scholarship you are applying for (Merit-based, Needs-based, or Other).
11. Select the status of your application (Pending, Approved, Denied, or Withdrawn).
12. Enter the date you would like to receive the scholarship award (if applicable).
13. Enter the amount of the scholarship award (if applicable).
14. Enter the frequency of the scholarship award (if applicable).
15. Select the type of award you are applying for (One-time, Recurring, or Semesterly).
16. Enter the number of the application (if applicable).
17. You may upload additional files or information as needed.
18. Enter any other relevant information about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD format (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Resume Upload**: Upload a PDF or Word document of your resume.
* **Transcript Upload**: Upload an unofficial transcript in PDF format.
* **Essay**: Write a brief essay (less than 500 words) describing your goals and motivations for applying for the scholarship.
* **Letter of Recommendation**: Provide a letter from a mentor or academic advisor.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school you are currently enrolled or planning to enroll in.
* **Program Enrollment**: Choose the program you are applying for (Culinary Arts, Hospitality Management, or Other).
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Scholarship Type**: Select the type of scholarship you are applying for (Merit-based, Needs-based, or Other).
* **Application Status**: Select the status of your application (Pending, Approved, Denied, or Withdrawn).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Review Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the review team.
* **Scholarship Award**: Enter the amount of the scholarship award (if applicable).
* **Award Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the scholarship award (if applicable).
* **Award Frequency**: Enter the frequency of the scholarship award (if applicable).
* **Award Type**: Select the type of award you are applying for (One-time, Recurring, or Semesterly).
* **Application Number**: Enter a unique number for your application (if applicable).
* **File Upload**: Upload any additional files or information related to your application.
* **Other**: Provide any other relevant information about your application.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person or organization submitting the application.
