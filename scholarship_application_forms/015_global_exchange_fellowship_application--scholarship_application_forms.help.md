# Global Exchange Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about yourself and your referee as part of the Global Exchange Fellowship application process. Please complete this form to provide us with necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form sections with your personal details and upload your CV if applicable.
2. For each section, ensure you have the required information before proceeding.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Candidate Details
* **Candidate Details** (`Candidate Details`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is to provide basic information about yourself.
* **Motivation Statement** (`Motivation Statement`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is to write a brief statement outlining your motivation for applying to the Global Exchange Fellowship.
* **CV Upload** (`CV Upload`, `text`, optional)
	+ This is where you can upload your CV if it is not provided elsewhere.
* **CV Text** (`CV Text`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing a text-based CV if not uploading.
* **University Details** (`University Details`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing details about your university.
* **NGO Details** (`NGO Details`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing information about the NGO you are associated with.
* **Foundation Details** (`Foundation Details`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing information about the foundation you are associated with.
* **Program Details** (`Program Details`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing details about the program you are applying for.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing your contact information.
* **Candidate Additional Info** (`Candidate Additional Info`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing any additional information about yourself.
* **Referee Name** (`Referee Name`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing the name of your referee.
* **Referee Affiliation** (`Referee Affiliation`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing the affiliation of your referee.
* **Referee Relationship** (`Referee Relationship`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing a brief description of your relationship with your referee.
* **Referee Phone** (`Referee Phone`, `text`, optional)
	+ This is for providing the contact phone number of your referee.
* **Referee Email** (`Referee Email`, `email`, optional)
	+ This is for providing the email address of your referee.
* **Referee Additional Info** (`Referee Additional Info`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for providing any additional information about your referee.
* **Referee Signature** (`Referee Signature`, `note`, optional)
	+ This section is for your referee to sign and provide confirmation of their relationship with you.
* **Referee CV Upload** (`Referee CV Upload`, `text`, optional)
	+ This is where your referee can upload their CV if it is not provided elsewhere.
* **Referee CV Text** (`Referee CV Text`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for your referee to provide a text-based CV if not uploading.
* **Referee Other Relation** (`Referee Other Relation`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ This section is for selecting the relationship you have with your referee. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Referee Relation Others** (`Referee Relation Others`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ This section is similar to "Referee Other Relation" with options of 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Referee Relation Other Details** (`Referee Relation Other Details`, `text`, optional)
	+ This section is for describing other relationships you have with your referee.
* **Referee Signature Confirmation** (`Referee Signature Confirmation`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ This section is for confirming your referee's signature. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- Upload your CV if applicable.
- Review each section carefully before submitting your application.
- Ensure your referee fills out their respective sections accurately and honestly.
- Confirm your referee's signature in the "Referee Signature Confirmation" section.
