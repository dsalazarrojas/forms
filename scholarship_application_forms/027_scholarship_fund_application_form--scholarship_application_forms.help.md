# Scholarship Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Fund Application Form is designed to collect personal and financial information from users who wish to apply for a scholarship fund. The form is a basic, single-page application that aims to gather necessary details to evaluate applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read the form instructions and ensure you understand the scholarship fund's purpose and target audience.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, providing all required and optional information.
3. Review your application before submitting to ensure everything is complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (1)**:
	+ Label: User Input
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Explanation: This field is a general information field where you can provide any additional details about your application. You can share your goals, motivations, or anything else that you think is relevant to your application.
* **Scholarship Details (2)**:
	+ Label: Scholarship Details
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Explanation: In this field, provide a brief description of the scholarship you are applying for, including its purpose, target audience, and eligibility criteria.
* **Contact Info (3)**:
	+ Label: Contact Info
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Explanation: Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number, so that we can get in touch with you regarding your application.

## Tips
* Please provide all required and optional information accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check your application before submitting to ensure everything is complete and correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
