# Open Learning Design Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Open Learning Design Grant. The purpose of the form is to assess the applicant's eligibility and fit for the grant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have reviewed the eligibility criteria and that your application meets all the requirements.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, using the provided guidance as a reference.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission Tracking** (`form_submission_tracking`, Note, Not required): This field is used to record the submission tracking information. Please do not fill out this field, as it is not required.
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, Text, Not required): This field is used to collect the applicant's information. Please provide your name, email address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, Select one, Not required): Please select one of the provided options to indicate your level of experience in the field of learning design.
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, Text, Not required): This field is used to describe your learning objectives. Please provide a clear and concise description of your objectives.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, Text, Not required): This field is used to describe your project. Please provide a clear and concise description of your project.
* **Budget Breakdown** (`budget_breakdown`, Number, Not required): This field is used to provide a breakdown of your budget. Please provide a clear and concise description of your expenses.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, Date, Not required): This field is used to provide a timeline for your project. Please provide a clear and concise description of your project timeline.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, Text, Not required): This field is used to describe the evaluation criteria for your project. Please provide a clear and concise description of the criteria that you would like to be evaluated.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, Select multiple, Not required): Please select all the options that apply to confirm your submission.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Not required): This field is used to provide your email address. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Not required): This field is used to provide your phone number. Please ensure that it is accurate.
