# Scholarship Application 2018 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for scholarship applications in 2018. It is used to gather data from applicants and streamline the review and evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the category of the scholarship application.
2. Provide a brief description of the application.
3. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
4. Enter the date and time of the application.
5. Select the criteria that the scholarship should be awarded based on.
6. If necessary, provide additional information about the application.
7. Enter the name or ID of the person who submitted the application.
8. Select or enter the email address of the person who submitted the application.
9. If the application was submitted to a specific person or department, select or enter their name or ID.
10. Enter the phone number of the person or department the application was submitted to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the scholarship application.
* **Description** (`description`, note, required: true): Provide a brief description of the scholarship application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: true): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: true): Enter the date of the scholarship application.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: true): Enter the time of the scholarship application.
* **Scholarship Criteria** (`scholarship_criteria`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the criteria that the scholarship should be awarded based on.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information about the scholarship application.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, number, required: true): Enter the ID or name of the person who submitted the application.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, required: true): Select the person or department to whom the application was submitted.
* **Submitted To Email** (`submitted_to_email`, email, required: true): Enter the email address of the person or department to whom the application was submitted.
* **Submitted To Phone** (`submitted_to_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person or department to whom the application was submitted.
