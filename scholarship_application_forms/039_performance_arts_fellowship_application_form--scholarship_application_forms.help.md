# Performance Arts Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Arts Fellowship Application Form is designed to gather information about applicants' backgrounds, project proposals, and contact details to evaluate their suitability for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Attach required documents (e.g., resume, project budget) as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Background**: A brief description of the applicant's background and experience in the performance arts field.
* **Project Proposal**: A detailed description of the applicant's project, including goals, objectives, and expected outcomes.
* **Resume**: A copy of the applicant's resume, highlighting relevant experience and qualifications.
* **Project Budget**: A detailed breakdown of the project's budget, including expenses and revenue projections.
* **Artist Statement**: A statement outlining the applicant's artistic vision, goals, and approach.
* **Resume Upload**: Upload a PDF copy of the applicant's resume.
* **Project Duration**: The duration of the project, including start and end dates.
* **Project Location**: The location where the project will take place.
* **Artist Name**: The artist's name and contact information.
* **Artist Email**: The artist's email address.
* **Artist Phone**: The artist's phone number.
* **Organization**: The organization or institution affiliated with the artist.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check your submissions for errors and inconsistencies.
- Attach relevant supporting documents as needed.
- Proofread your responses for grammar and spelling errors.
