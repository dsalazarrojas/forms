# Student Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Grant Application Form is designed to collect all necessary information for student grant applications. This includes student details, grant type, amount, start and end dates, frequency, period, status, comment, application methods, decision, financial aid, funding, payment term, payment method, payment status, repayment status, and repayment term. This ensures that the application process is thorough and helps to avoid missing crucial details.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields that you must fill out to complete the application. Follow these steps to complete the form:

1.  Student Information: Provide your student name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2.  Personal Details: Provide any other information that is relevant to your application.
3.  Grant Type: Select the type of grant you are applying for from the provided options.
4.  Grant Amount: Enter the grant amount you are requesting.
5.  Grant Start and End Dates: Enter the start and end dates of the grant period.
6.  Grant Frequency: Choose how often you would like to receive the grant.
7.  Grant Period: Select the duration of the grant period.
8.  Grant Status: Select the current status of your grant application.
9.  Grant Comment: Provide any additional comments about your grant application.
10. Grant Application: Select the methods you used to apply for the grant.
11. Grant Decision: Choose the outcome of your application.
12. Financial Aid: Choose the financial aid you are applying for.
13. Grant Funding: Choose the source of funding for your grant.
14. Grant Payment Term: Choose the term for the grant payment.
15. Grant Payment Method: Select the method you would like to receive the grant payment.
16. Grant Payment Status: Select the status of your grant payment.
17. Grant Repayment Status: Choose the status of your grant repayment.
18. Grant Repayment Term: Choose the term for your grant repayment.
19. Student Signature: Sign and date the form with your student signature.
20. Grantor Signature: Sign and date the form with the grantor's signature.
21. Submit: Click the submit button to complete the application.
