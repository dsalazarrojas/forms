# Open Education Resource Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for the Open Education Resource Grant application process. It will help us understand your project's details and evaluate its eligibility for funding.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field carefully and make sure to fill it in accurately.
- You can save your progress and come back later if needed.
- Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (1)**: Enter a brief title of your project, ideally a few words that describe it.
* **Institution Name (2)**: Enter the name of the institution or organization associated with your project.
* **Project Title (3)**: Provide a more detailed title of your project, ideally 1-2 sentences.
* **Applicant Name (4)**: Enter your name, or the name of the person submitting the application.
* **Email (5)**: Enter a valid email address associated with your institution or project.
* **Phone (6)**: Provide a contact number where we can reach you.
* **Project Description (7)**: Describe your project, its goals, and its expected outcomes in 1-2 paragraphs.
* **Project Start Date (8)**: Enter the expected start date of your project.
* **Project End Date (9)**: Enter the expected end date of your project.
* **Project Budget (10)**: Enter the total budget for your project.
* **Select Type (11)**: Choose the type of grant you are applying for from the provided options.
* **Select Option 1 (12)**: Choose one or more options from the dropdown list for further project details.
* **Note (13)**: Add any additional notes about your project, if necessary.
* **Time (14)**: Enter the time of your availability for a project meeting or call.
* **Select Multiple Time (15)**: Choose one or more times from the dropdown list for your availability.
* **Select Multiple Time 1 (16)**: Choose one or more times from the dropdown list for further project details.
* **Select Multiple Time 2 (17)**: Choose one or more times from the dropdown list for further project details.
* **Form Description (18)**: Provide a brief description of the form, if desired.
* **Assigned to (19)**: Choose who is assigned to manage your project, ideally a person or team name.
* **Form Submitter (20)**: Choose the person submitting the form, ideally their name or team name.
* **Email Address (21)**: Enter the email address of the form submitter.
* **Form Submission Time (22)**: Enter the time of submission, ideally in 24-hour format.
* **Submitter (23)**: Choose the person submitting the form, ideally their name.
* **Submitter Email (24)**: Enter the email address of the form submitter.
* **Submitted By (25)**: Choose who submitted the form, ideally a person or team name.
