# Public Education Advocates Fund Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Education Advocates Fund Application form is designed to collect information about projects that aim to promote public education. This form will help us understand your project's goals, objectives, and funding needs. Please note that this form does not guarantee funding, and the review process may be subject to change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. For fields that are not required, you can provide additional information if you feel it is relevant to your project.
3. Make sure to attach supporting documents as needed, as specified in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required): This section is meant to gather basic information about your project. Please enter your project title, your name, and your institution/organization.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Describe your project in detail, including its goals and objectives. This section should give us a clear understanding of what your project is about.
* **Funding Amounts** (`funding_amounts`, text, required): Specify the amount of funding you are requesting. Please be accurate and realistic.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required): Attach relevant documents that support your project, such as resumes, letters of recommendation, or project proposals.
* **Project Goals and Objectives** (`project_goals_objectives`, text, required): Describe the specific goals and objectives of your project. This section should be detailed and concise.
* **Budget Plan** (`budget_plan`, text, required): Outline your project's budget, including estimated costs and timelines.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, required): Provide a specific date for when your project will start and end.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, text, required): Define the criteria that will be used to evaluate your project's success.
* **Evaluation Process** (`evaluation_process`, text, required): Explain how you will be evaluated, including the specific criteria and processes used.
* **Review Committee** (`review_committee`, text, required): Identify the review committee members who will review your project.
* **Decision Process** (`decision_process`, text, required): Describe the decision-making process for awarding funding.
* **Supporting Document 1-14** (`supporting_document_1-14`, text, optional): Please attach supporting documents as needed, up to 14 documents.

Tips:
* Make sure to attach relevant and up-to-date documents.
* Use clear and concise language in your answers.
* Proofread your form before submitting it.
