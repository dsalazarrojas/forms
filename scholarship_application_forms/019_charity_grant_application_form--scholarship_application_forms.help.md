# Charity Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from applicants for a charity grant. The form is designed to help us understand your background, financial needs, and educational goals. Please fill out this form completely and honestly to increase your chances of receiving a grant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section carefully and thoroughly. Make sure to provide accurate information and answer all required fields. If you need help or have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
• **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Last Name
• **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. Email Address
• **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your primary contact email.

### 4. Phone Number
• **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number.

### 5. Date of Birth
• **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### 6. Mailing Address
• **Mailing Address** (text, required): Enter your complete mailing address.

### 7. Eligibility Information (optional)
• **Eligibility Information** (note): This section is for your personal information, and you may skip it if not applicable.

### 8. Citizenship Status
• **Citizenship Status** (select_one, required): Select your current citizenship status: Citizen, Permanent Resident, Student Visa, or Other.

### 9. Approximate Annual Family Income
• **Approximate Annual Family Income** (select_one, required): Select your approximate annual family income: Under 25k, 25k-50k, 50k-75k, 75k-100k, or Over 100k.

### 10. Academic Background (optional)
• **Academic Background** (note): This section is for your educational information, and you may skip it if not applicable.

### 11. Current Education Level
• **Current Education Level** (select_one, required): Select your current education level: High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Graduate Program, or Other.

### 12. Name of School or University
• **Name of School or University** (text, required): Enter the name of your current institution.

### 13. Field of Study or Major
• **Field of Study or Major** (text, required): Enter your field of study or major.

### 14. Current GPA or Academic Performance (optional)
• **Current GPA or Academic Performance** (number, required): Enter your current GPA or academic performance, if applicable.

### 15. Financial Need (optional)
• **Financial Need** (note): This section is for your financial information, and you may skip it if not applicable.

### 16. Amount of Grant Needed
• **Amount of Grant Needed** (number, required): Enter the amount of grant you need.

### 17. Explain Your Financial Need
• **Explain Your Financial Need** (text, required): Describe your financial circumstances.

### 18. Educational Goals
• **Educational Goals** (text, required): Enter your educational goals.

### 19. Career Aspirations
• **Career Aspirations** (text, required): Enter your career aspirations.

### 20. How Will This Grant Help You
• **How Will This Grant Help You** (text, required): Explain how this grant will have an impact on you.

### 21. Community or Volunteer Involvement (optional)
• **Community or Volunteer Involvement** (text): Enter information about your community or volunteer involvement, if applicable.

### 22. Application Submission Date
• **Application Submission Date** (date, required): Enter the current date.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and honestly.
- Double-check your information for accuracy.
- Attach any supporting documents if required.
- Follow up with us if you have any questions or concerns.
