# Scholarship Reference Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship reference submission form is used by referees to provide information about their relationship with the applicant and their qualifications for the scholarship. The form is used to gather detailed information about the referee's relationship with the applicant, their character, and abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (email and phone number) in the relevant fields.
2. Select your relationship with the applicant from the dropdown menu (e.g. Parent, Family Friend, Mentor, etc.).
3. Choose how you have known the applicant from the dropdown menu (e.g. Social Media, Work, School/University, etc.).
4. Select the length of time you have known the applicant from the dropdown menu (e.g. Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, etc.).
5. Rate the applicant based on the provided rating scale (e.g. 1-5).
6. Provide comments about the applicant in the text area.
7. Select the submission status (e.g. Pending, Approved, Rejected).
8. Enter the submission ID and referee ID.
9. Enter the referee URL and referee email (if applicable).
10. Select the submission date and time.
11. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the applicant.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the applicant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email of the applicant.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the applicant.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you last saw the applicant.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you last saw the applicant.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate the applicant based on the provided scale (1-5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any comments or observations about the applicant.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_multiple, required): Select the rating scale that best describes your assessment of the applicant.
* **Other Rating** (`rating_scale_other`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional rating scales that are not listed above.
* **Referee Type** (`referee_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of referee you are (e.g. School, University, Foundation).
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, required): Select how you are related to the applicant (e.g. Parent, Family Friend, Mentor, etc.).
* **Years Known** (`years_known`, select_multiple, required): Select the length of time you have known the applicant.
* **How Known** (`how_known`, select_multiple, required): Select how you have known the applicant (e.g. Social Media, Work, School/University, etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the applicant.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the submission (e.g. Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, number, required): Enter the submission ID.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, optional): Enter the user ID.
* **Referee ID** (`referee_id`, number, required): Enter the referee ID.
* **Referee URL** (`referee_url`, text, optional): Enter the referee URL.
* **Referee Email** (`referee_email`, email, optional): Enter the referee email.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the submission.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the submission was created.
