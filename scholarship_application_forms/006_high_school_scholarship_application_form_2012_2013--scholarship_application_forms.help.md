# High School Scholarship Application Form 2012 2013 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from high school students who are applying for the 2012-2013 scholarship. The form will be used to evaluate each applicant's eligibility for the scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field's explanation carefully to understand what information is required.
- Fill out the required fields (those marked as "required") to avoid any delays or issues with your application.
- Use the provided options for select fields (e.g., "Contact Preferences", "Student Academic Preferences", "Major Preferences") to select the most accurate and relevant answer.
- If a field is optional, provide as much information as possible to help the scholarship committee review your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Information (id: 1)**: This field is used to collect your school's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Student Information (id: 2)**: This field is used to collect your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Contact Preferences (id: 3)**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Home, School, Both, or Other).
* **Academic Information (id: 4)**: Provide any relevant academic achievements, courses taken, or other academic information that may be useful for the scholarship committee.
* **Parent Contact Information (id: 5)**: This field is used to collect your parent's name, address, and contact information. If you are an emancipated minor, please mark the checkbox for "I am an emancipated minor".
* **Student Academic Preferences (id: 6)**: Select one or more of the areas you are interested in (English, Math, Science, Foreign).
* **School Information (id: 7)**: This field is used to collect your school's name, address, and other relevant details. Please note that this field is a duplicate of the first "School Information" field (id: 1), and you should use the same information as the first field.
* **Major Preferences (id: 8)**: Select one or more of the areas you are interested in (Business, Science, Engineering, Art).
* **Student Details (id: 9)**: Provide any additional information that may be relevant for the scholarship committee to review.

## Tips
- Double-check your application form to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the scholarship committee for clarification.
- Attach any supporting documents (e.g., transcripts, letters of recommendation) as required by the scholarship committee.
