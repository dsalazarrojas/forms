# Scholarship Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The scholarship claim form is a document that helps applicants claim their scholarships. It is used to provide necessary information for processing and verification of scholarship claims.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the requested information in the following pages:
	* Page 1: Enter the student's name and the course they are enrolled in.
	* Page 2: Enter the student's ID number and program name (if applicable).
	* Page 3: Select the semester they are enrolled in and the award year.
	* Page 4: Enter the award amount and the award currency.
	* Page 5: Enter the award date, award recipient name, and institution name (if applicable).
	* Page 6: Select the award type and enter a short description of the award.
	* Page 7: Select the claim status and claim date (if applicable).
	* Page 8-9: Enter any reason for ineligibility, additional notes, and upload any necessary attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Student Name**: Enter the name of the student who is claiming the scholarship.
* **Course Enrolled In**: Enter the name of the course the student is currently enrolled in.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter the unique student ID number.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program the student is enrolled in (if applicable).
* **Semester Enrolled**: Select the semester the student is currently enrolled in (Fall or Spring).
* **Award Year**: Enter the year the award was received (optional).
* **Award Amount**: Enter the amount of the award received.
* **Award Currency**: Enter the currency of the award (optional).
* **Award Date**: Enter the date the award was received (optional).
* **Award Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the award recipient (if applicable).
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution the award was received from (if applicable).
* **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department the award was received from (if applicable).
* **Award Type**: Select the type of award received (Type A, Type B, or Type C).
* **Award Description**: Enter a short description of the award.
* **Claim Status**: Select the claim status (Active or Inactive).
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date the claim was submitted (optional).
* **Reason for Ineligibility**: Enter any reason why the student is ineligible for the award (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information about the award or claim.
* **Uploaded Documents**: Upload any necessary documents related to the award.
* **Uploaded Files**: Upload any files related to the award (if applicable).
* **Uploaded Images**: Upload any images related to the award (if applicable).
* **Uploaded Videos**: Upload any videos related to the award (if applicable).
* **Uploaded Screenshots**: Upload any screenshots related to the award (if applicable).
* **Uploaded Audio**: Upload any audio files related to the award (if applicable).
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the claim.

Note: All required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) in the field label.
