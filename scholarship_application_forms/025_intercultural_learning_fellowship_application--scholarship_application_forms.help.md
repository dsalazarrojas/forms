# Intercultural Learning Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intercultural Learning Fellowship Application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in applying for the Intercultural Learning Fellowship. This form is used to gather essential details about the applicants, including their background, qualifications, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out each section with accurate and detailed information.
- Provide attachments for supporting documents, if required.
- Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information
* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, required/optional): This section is meant to collect basic information about the applicant, including name, email, and other contact details. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information in this section.
* **User Information Attachment** (`user_information_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the user information, such as a passport or ID.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): This section is designed to collect the applicant's contact information, including address, phone number, and other relevant details. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Contact Information Attachment** (`contact_information_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the contact information.

### Motivations
* **Motivations** (`motivations`, text, required/optional): This section is meant to collect the applicant's reasons for applying for the Intercultural Learning Fellowship. Please provide a clear and concise statement explaining why you are interested in the fellowship.
* **Motivations Attachment** (`motivations_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the motivations, such as a letter of intent.

### Education Background
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required/optional): This section is designed to collect information about the applicant's education background, including degrees, institutions, and relevant courses. Please provide accurate and detailed information.
* **Education Background Attachment** (`education_background_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the education background, such as a transcript or certificate.

### Research Interests
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, text, required/optional): This section is meant to collect information about the applicant's research interests and areas of study. Please provide a clear and concise statement explaining your research interests.
* **Research Interests Attachment** (`research_interest_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the research interests.

### Professional Experience
* **Professional Experience** (`professional_experience`, text, required/optional): This section is designed to collect information about the applicant's professional experience, including job titles, companies, and relevant work experience. Please provide accurate and detailed information.
* **Professional Experience Attachment** (`professional_experience_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the professional experience.

### References
* **References** (`references`, text, required/optional): This section is meant to collect information about the applicant's professional references, including names, titles, and contact information. Please provide accurate and detailed information.
* **References Attachment** (`references_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the references, such as a letter of recommendation.

### Language Skills
* **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, text, required/optional): This section is designed to collect information about the applicant's language skills, including languages spoken, read, and written. Please provide accurate and detailed information.
* **Language Attachment** (`language_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the language skills, such as a language proficiency certificate.

### Personal Statement
* **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, text, required/optional): This section is meant to collect a statement from the applicant, explaining why they are interested in the fellowship and what they hope to achieve. Please provide a clear and concise statement.
* **Personal Statement Attachment** (`statement_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the personal statement.

### Attachments
* **CV Attachments** (`cv_attachments`, text, required/optional): This section is used to submit a supporting document for the CV.
* **Motivation Attachments** (`motivation_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the motivations.
* **Education Background Attachments** (`education_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the education background.
* **Research Interests Attachments** (`research_interest_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the research interests.
* **Professional Experience Attachments** (`professional_experience_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the professional experience.
* **References Attachments** (`references_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the references.
* **Language Attachments** (`language_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the language skills.
* **Personal Statement Attachments** (`statement_attachment`, text, required/optional): This attachment is used to submit a supporting document for the personal statement.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information in each section.
- Review and submit the form once completed.
- Check the attachments for supporting documents before submitting. 

This help guide is designed to assist you in completing the Intercultural Learning Fellowship Application form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out. 

Note: This guide is a general overview of the form and its fields. Please ensure that you read and follow the specific requirements and instructions provided by the fellowship organizers and administrators.
