# Dropbox Scholarship Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dropbox Scholarship Document Upload Form is a tool used to upload and track documents related to scholarships. This form is intended for users to confirm whether they have any documents to upload and to provide clear information about their uploaded files.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Determine whether you have any documents to upload.
2.  Fill out the form with the required information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, not required): 
   Please provide a brief description of your scholarship application. You can describe the scholarship program you are applying for, your academic goals, and any other relevant information.
* **Documents to Upload** (`documents_to_upload`, select_multiple, required): 
   Check if you have any documents to upload. If you select "Yes", you can proceed with the upload process.
* **Uploaded Documents** (`uploaded_documents`, text, not required): 
   List the documents you have uploaded, including their names and types.
* **Document Name** (`document_name`, text, not required): 
   Enter the name of the document you are uploading.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_one, not required): 
   Choose the type of document you are uploading (e.g. transcript, letter of recommendation, etc.).
* **Upload File** (`uploaded_file`, text, not required): 
   Provide the file path or URL of the document you are uploading.
* **Uploaded Document Link** (`uploaded_document_link`, text, not required): 
   Provide a link to the uploaded document.
* **Document Size** (`document_size`, number, not required): 
   Enter the size of the uploaded document in megabytes.
* **Uploaded On** (`uploaded_on`, date, not required): 
   Enter the date and time of the upload.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all necessary fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth scholarship application process.
* Use a clear and concise language when describing your application and uploaded documents.
* Upload documents in the required format to ensure they can be read and reviewed correctly. 

This form is designed to help streamline the scholarship application process by guiding users through a series of questions that ensure they have all necessary information for their application. By using this form, users can easily upload and track their documents, making the process more efficient.
