# Scholarship Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Renewal Form is used by students to update their information and circumstances after their initial scholarship application. This form is necessary for students who are continuing their studies and have experienced changes in their academic or financial status that may affect their eligibility for the scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your student information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the date your program started and ended (if applicable).
3. Explain why you are renewing your scholarship.
4. Choose your current academic status and financial status.
5. Report your expected degree earnings.
6. Describe your current academic progress.
7. Update your student status.
8. Provide the date you expect to complete your program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your current email address that you check frequently.
* **Phone**: Enter your current phone number where you can be reached.
* **Program Start Date**: Enter the date you started your program (if still in progress).
* **Program End Date**: Enter the date you completed your program (if applicable).
* **Reason for Renewal**: Briefly explain why you are renewing your scholarship.
* **Current Academic Status**: Choose one of the available options:
	+ Active: You are currently studying and making progress towards your degree.
	+ Inactive: You have temporarily stopped studying and plan to resume.
	+ On Leave of Absence: You have taken a leave of absence from your studies.
	+ Terminated: Your studies have been terminated due to non-attendance or other reasons.
	+ Withdrawn: You have withdrawn from your program.
	+ Suspended: Your studies are suspended due to academic or financial reasons.
* **Financial Status**: Choose all that apply:
	+ Full: You are receiving full financial support for your studies.
	+ Partial: You are receiving partial financial support for your studies.
	+ Unfunded: You are not receiving any financial support for your studies.
	+ None: You have not received financial support for your studies.
* **Expected Degree Earnings**: Enter your expected degree earnings as a numerical value.
* **Academic Progress**: Describe your current academic progress in a few sentences.
* **Student Status Update**: Briefly describe any changes in your student status since your initial application.
* **Program Completion Date**: Enter the date you expect to complete your program.
* **Student Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
