# General Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The General Scholarship Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants to be considered for a scholarship. This form helps us evaluate your qualifications, achievements, and goals, and determine your eligibility for a scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your contact information and general scholarship application details.
2.  Share your academic record, including any relevant coursework or academic achievements.
3.  Fill out the applicant information section, providing us with any additional details about yourself.
4.  Answer the questions about the scholarship program you are applying for.
5.  Write a scholarship essay that explains why you deserve the scholarship.
6.  Attach any required documents, such as a resume, transcript, and letters of recommendation.
7.  Upload any additional documents that support your application.
8.  Confirm whether you want to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Scholarship Application** (`1`): Describe in 1-2 paragraphs why you are a good fit for this scholarship program.
* **Applicant Contact Information** (`2`): Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Academic Record** (`3`): Include relevant coursework, academic achievements, and any other relevant educational information.
* **Applicant Information** (`4`): Provide any additional details about yourself that you think are relevant to your application.
* **Scholarship Program Information** (`5`): Answer the questions about the scholarship program you are applying for.
* **Scholarship Essay** (`6`): Write a 500-word essay explaining why you deserve the scholarship.
* **Resume** (`7`): Attach a copy of your resume, highlighting your skills, experience, and achievements.
* **Transcript** (`8`): Attach a copy of your transcript, showing your academic history.
* **Letters of Recommendation** (`9`): Attach letters of recommendation from academic or professional mentors.
* **Other Documents** (`10`): Attach any additional documents that support your application.
* **Additional Questions** (`11`): Answer any additional questions asked.
* **Submit** (`12`): Choose `Yes` to submit your form, or `No` if you want to cancel.
* **Applicant Agreement** (`13`): Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the scholarship program.
* **Scholarship Program Agreement** (`14`): Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the scholarship program.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Attach all required documents.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* Review the terms and conditions of the scholarship program carefully before submitting your form.
