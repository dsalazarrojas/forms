# Scholarship Award Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for confirming the receipt of a scholarship award. It helps ensure that the award recipient acknowledges and confirms their scholarship details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct confirmation option for the scholarship award.
2. Enter the recipient's information as it appears on the official scholarship documents.
3. Provide a brief description of the scholarship, including the award amount and any other relevant details.
4. Confirm whether the recipient has accepted or declined the scholarship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Award Confirmation** (`award_confirmation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the correct option from the list to confirm receipt of the scholarship.
* **Recipient Information** (`recipient_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the recipient's name and other relevant details as it appears on the official scholarship documents.
* **Scholarship Details** (`scholarship_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the scholarship, including the award amount and any other relevant details.
* **Confirm Scholarship** (`confirm_scholarship`, select_one, required/optional): Select "True" to confirm that the recipient has accepted the scholarship, or "False" to decline it.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the recipient's information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If the recipient declines the scholarship, please provide a reason for declining.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the scholarship administrator for clarification.
