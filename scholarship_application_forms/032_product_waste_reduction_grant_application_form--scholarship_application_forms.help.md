# Product Waste Reduction Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Waste Reduction Grant Application Form is a tool to support the grant program for product waste reduction. This application form collects project information, budget details, contact data, and other relevant details for evaluation and consideration by the grant committee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a detailed project proposal, including your project's goals and objectives.
2. Submit a project budget, including estimated costs and funding requirements.
3. Attach project documents, if necessary, to support your proposal.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Select one of the options for the 'Select One' field.
6. Select multiple options for the 'Select Multiple' field, if applicable.
7. Enter the date and time for the project.
8. Choose a category from the 'Category Select' field.
9. Select multiple categories from the 'Category Select Multiple' field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Proposal (Project Proposal)**: A brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Project Budget (Project Budget)**: Estimated costs and funding requirements for your project.
* **Project Documents (Project Documents)**: Any supporting documents or attachments necessary for your proposal, such as photos or reports.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes. (Optional)
* **Select One (Select One)**: Choose one of the available options to support your project.
* **Select Multiple (Select Multiple)**: Choose multiple options to support your project. (Optional)
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date your project will start or be completed.
* **Time (Time)**: Enter the time your project will start or be completed.
* **Category Select (Category Select)**: Choose one category from the available options to support your project.
* **Category Select Multiple (Category Select Multiple)**: Choose multiple categories to support your project. (Optional)
* **Note (Note)**: Any additional comments or notes about your project.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out thoroughly and accurately.
* Attach any necessary supporting documents or files.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors or duplication of effort.
