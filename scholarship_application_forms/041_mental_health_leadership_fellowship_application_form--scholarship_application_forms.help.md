# Mental Health Leadership Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Leadership Fellowship Application Form is used to gather information about each applicant's background, education, and experience in mental health leadership. This information will be used to evaluate your eligibility for the Mental Health Leadership Fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your personal information, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details in the **Personal Info** section.
2. Next, select your education and relevant experience in mental health in the **Education**, **Mental Health Experience**, **Mental Health Education**, and **Leadership Roles** sections.
3. Choose your interests and qualifications in mental health leadership in the **Program Interests** and **References** sections.
4. Add any additional information or notes you'd like to share with the program administrators in the **Experience** and **Mental Health Background** sections.
5. Finally, review your answers for accuracy and click the **Submit** button to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Info
* **Personal Info**: This section is where you can add any additional personal information that you think would be useful for the program administrators to know about you. It's not a required field, so you can choose to add information or leave it blank.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name and any other contact information that you'd like to share with the program administrators. This could include email, phone numbers, or addresses. It's not a required field, so you can choose to add information or leave it blank.

### Education
* **Education**: Select all of the options that best describe your education and relevant training in mental health. You can choose multiple options.

### Program Interests
* **Program Interests**: Choose the options that best reflect your interests and qualifications in mental health leadership. You can choose one option.

### Experience
* **Experience**: This section is for you to add any additional information or notes about your relevant experience in mental health. It's not a required field, so you can choose to add information or leave it blank.

### Leadership Roles
* **Leadership Roles**: Select all of the options that best describe your leadership roles or experience in mental health. You can choose multiple options.

### Mental Health Background
* **Mental Health Background**: Add any additional information or notes about your mental health background. It's not a required field, so you can choose to add information or leave it blank.

### Mental Health Experience
* **Mental Health Experience**: Select all of the options that best describe your experience in mental health. You can choose multiple options.

### Mental Health Education
* **Mental Health Education**: Select all of the options that best describe your mental health education. You can choose multiple options.

### References
* **References**: Choose the references that best reflect your experience and qualifications in mental health leadership. You can choose multiple options.

### Submit
* **Submit**: This is the final step. Click the **Submit** button to complete your application.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your answers to ensure that the program administrators have the best understanding of your qualifications and experience.
* Make sure to proofread your application for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact the program administrators.
