# Aleyris Martinez Memorial Scholarship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aleyris Martinez Memorial Scholarship Form is a comprehensive application form designed to gather essential information about you, the applicant, to determine your eligibility for the scholarship. It is crucial that you fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a fair evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal details, including your first name, last name, date of birth, contact information, and other demographic details.
2. Provide information about your academic background, including high school name and address, graduation year, current GPA, and class rank.
3. Specify your intended college or university and major.
4. Describe your reasons for applying for this scholarship and your personal statement.
5. Share information about your extracurricular activities, community service, leadership experience, and awards or honors received.
6. Describe your financial need and any other scholarships you have applied for.
7. Provide information about your references.
8. Attach any required documents, such as transcripts and recommendation letters.
9. Complete the essay topic by describing how Aleyris has inspired you.
10. Outline your future career goals.
11. Provide any additional information you want to share.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your legal first name.
- **Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your legal last name.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select your gender from the options provided.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, `required`): Enter your primary contact email.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full mailing address.
- **High School Name** (`high_school_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your high school.
- **High School Address** (`high_school_address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your high school address.
- **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, `required`): Enter the year you graduated from high school.
- **Current GPA** (`gpa`, `number`, `required`): Enter your current GPA.
- **Class Rank** (`class_rank`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your rank in class.
- **Intended College/University** (`intended_college`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the college or university you intend to attend.
- **Intended Major** (`intended_major`, `text`, `required`): Enter the field of study you plan to pursue.
- **Why Are You Applying?** (`scholarship_reason`, `text`, `required`): Describe your interest in applying for this scholarship.
- **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, `text`, `required`): Share a brief description of yourself.
- **Connection to Aleyris** (`connection_to_aleyris`, `text`, `optional`): Describe your connection to Aleyris Martinez.
- **Extracurricular Activities** (`extracurricular_activities`, `text`, `required`): List any extracurricular activities or roles you have held.
- **Community Service** (`community_service`, `text`, `required`): Describe any volunteer work you have done.
- **Leadership Experience** (`leadership_experience`, `text`, `required`): Share any leadership roles you have held.
- **Awards and Honors** (`awards_honors`, `text`, `optional`): List any academic or other awards or honors you have received.
- **Financial Need** (`financial_need`, `text`, `required`): Describe your financial need and how this scholarship will help.
- **Other Scholarships Applied** (`other_scholarships`, `text`, `optional`): List any other scholarships you have applied for.
- **Reference 1 Name** (`reference_1_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your first reference.
- **Reference 1 Email** (`reference_1_email`, `text`, `required`): Enter the email of your first reference.
- **Reference 1 Relationship** (`reference_1_relationship`, `text`, `required`): Describe your relationship with your first reference.
- **Reference 2 Name** (`reference_2_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your second reference.
- **Reference 2 Email** (`reference_2_email`, `text`, `required`): Enter the email of your second reference.
- **Reference 2 Relationship** (`reference_2_relationship`, `text`, `required`): Describe your relationship with your second reference.
- **Transcript Attached** (`transcript_attached`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you have attached a transcript.
- **Recommendation Letters Attached** (`recommendation_letters_attached`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you have attached recommendation letters.
- **Essay Response** (`essay_topic`, `text`, `required`): Describe how Aleyris has inspired you.
- **Future Career Goals** (`future_goals`, `text`, `required`): Share your future career goals.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any additional information you want to share.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, `required`): Confirm that you certify all information is accurate.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Attach required documents, including transcripts and recommendation letters.
* Ensure that you follow the submission guidelines for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the explanations above.
