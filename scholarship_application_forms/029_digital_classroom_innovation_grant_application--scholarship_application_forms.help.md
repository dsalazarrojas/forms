# Digital Classroom Innovation Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive application for the Digital Classroom Innovation Grant. It is intended for applicants to submit their project proposals, providing detailed information about their project ideas, budget, and funding requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Application Form** section with your project's basic information.
	* Provide a brief title and description of your project.
	* Enter the required fields and any additional information that might be relevant.
2. In the **Applicant Information** section, fill in your contact details.
3. In the **Technology Information** section, describe the technology or tools you plan to use for your project.
4. In the **Project Details** section, provide a detailed description of your project, including its objectives, expected outcomes, and any relevant documentation.
5. In the **Budget** section, specify your budget breakdown and any supporting documents.
6. In the **Supporting Documents** section, upload any relevant supporting documents for your project.
7. In the **Submission Information** section, confirm your submission and provide any additional comments.
8. In the **Review Status** section, review your application and confirm receipt of the submission.
9. In the **Submission History** section, view a record of your previous submissions.
10. In the **Review Comments** section, view any comments from the review committee.
11. In the **Grant Amount** section, specify the grant amount.
12. In the **Grant Currency** section, choose the grant currency.
13. In the **Grant Start Date** section, specify the start date of the grant period.
14. In the **Grant End Date** section, specify the end date of the grant period.
15. In the **Grant Total** section, specify the total grant amount.
16. In the **Grant Recipient** section, choose the grant recipient.
17. In the **Grant Category** section, choose one or more grant categories.
18. In the **Grant Type** section, choose the grant type.
19. In the **Grant Funding** section, choose one or more funding sources.
20. In the **Grant Status** section, choose the current grant status.
21. In the **Grant Comments** section, provide any additional comments.
22. In the **Grant Decision Date** section, specify the date of the grant decision.
23. In the **Grant Funding Amount** section, specify the grant funding amount.
24. In the **Grant Currency Symbol** section, specify the grant currency symbol.
25. In the **Grant Currency Name** section, specify the grant currency name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (Application Form, text, required): A brief title and description of your project.
* **Applicant Information** (applicant_info, text, required): Enter your contact details for communication purposes.
* **Technology Information** (technology_info, text, required): Describe the technology or tools you plan to use for your project.
* **Project Details** (project_details, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your project, including its objectives, expected outcomes, and any relevant documentation.
* **Budget** (budget_info, text, required): Specify your budget breakdown and any supporting documents.
* **Supporting Documents** (supporting_documents, text, required): Upload any relevant supporting documents for your project.
* **Submission Information** (submission_info, text, required): Confirm your submission and provide any additional comments.
* **Review Status** (review_status, text, required): Review your application and confirm receipt of the submission.
* **Submission History** (submission_history, text, required): View a record of your previous submissions.
* **Review Comments** (review_comments, text, required): View any comments from the review committee.
* **Grant Amount** (grant_amount, number, required): Specify the grant amount.
* **Grant Currency** (grant_currency, select_one, required): Choose the grant currency.
* **Grant Start Date** (grant_start_date, date, required): Specify the start date of the grant period.
* **Grant End Date** (grant_end_date, date, required): Specify the end date of the grant period.
* **Grant Total** (grant_total, number, required): Specify the total grant amount.
* **Grant Recipient** (grant_recipient, select_one, required): Choose the grant recipient.
* **Grant Category** (grant_category, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more grant categories.
* **Grant Type** (grant_type, select_one, required): Choose the grant type.
* **Grant Funding** (grant_funding, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more funding sources.
* **Grant Status** (grant_status, select_one, required): Choose the current grant status.
* **Grant Comments** (grant_comments, text, required): Provide any additional comments.
* **Grant Decision Date** (grant_decision_date, date, required): Specify the date of the grant decision.
* **Grant Funding Amount** (grant_funding_amount, number, required): Specify the grant funding amount.
* **Grant Currency Symbol** (grant_currency_symbol, text, required): Specify the grant currency symbol.
* **Grant Currency Name** (grant_currency_name, text, required): Specify the grant currency name.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* Double-check your budget and ensure that it aligns with your project's scope and requirements.
* Provide clear and concise descriptions of your project and its objectives.
* Upload any relevant supporting documents for your project.
* Review your application carefully before submitting it for review.
* Be prepared for any questions or comments from the review committee.
