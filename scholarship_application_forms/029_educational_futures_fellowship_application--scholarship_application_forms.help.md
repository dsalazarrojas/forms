# educational-futures-fellowship-application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Futures Fellowship is a program designed to support individuals in their academic and professional development. This form is used to gather information from applicants to determine eligibility and suitability for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email**: Enter your active email address for communication purposes.
3. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. **Motivation Statement**: Write a brief explanation of why this fellowship is important to you.
	* For example: "I am interested in this fellowship because it will provide me with the opportunity to [briefly state the benefits you hope to gain from the fellowship]."
5. **University Affiliations**: Select all universities you are affiliated with.
6. **Upload File**: If required, upload any additional files as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This field is used to collect your full name.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your active email address.
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Motivation Statement**: This field is used to collect a brief explanation of why this fellowship is important to you.
* **University Affiliations**: This field is used to collect all universities you are affiliated with.
* **Upload File**: This field is used to upload any additional files as requested.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when writing your motivation statements.
* Only select universities you are actually affiliated with.
* Check that you have all necessary documents and information before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
