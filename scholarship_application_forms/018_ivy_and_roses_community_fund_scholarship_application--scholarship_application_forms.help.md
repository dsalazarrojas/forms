The purpose of this form is to collect information from students or individuals interested in applying for the Ivy and Roses Community Fund Scholarship. The form is used to gather details about the applicant's personal information, contact details, education history, academic programs, scholarship information, references, employment history, financial information, and membership in scholarship committees. This form is designed to help the scholarship committee evaluate each applicant's qualifications and make informed decisions about awarding the scholarship.

## Purpose

The Ivy and Roses Community Fund Scholarship is a financial award given to students or individuals who demonstrate exceptional academic, personal, and professional qualities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each section and provide the required information for each field. Make sure to fill in all the necessary fields and provide accurate and truthful information.

* **Personal Info** (`Personal Info`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect your personal details such as your name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you regarding your application.
* **Education History** (`Education History`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your education history, including any relevant degrees or certifications you hold.
* **Academic Programs** (`Academic Programs`, `text`, required: false): List the academic programs or courses you are currently enrolled in or have completed.
* **Scholarship Info** (`Scholarship Info`, `text`, required: false): If you have any existing scholarships, grants, or other financial aids, please list them here.
* **References** (`References`, `text`, required: false): Provide names and contact information for at least two academic or professional references.
* **Employment History** (`Employment History`, `text`, required: false): Describe any work experience you have, including job titles and duration of employment.
* **Financial Info** (`Financial Info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any information about your financial situation that may be relevant to your application.
* **Scholarship Committees** (`Scholarship Committees`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you are or have been a member of any scholarship committees.
* **Scholarship Committees Select** (`Scholarship Committees Select`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if you have been selected or have experience working with any scholarship committees.
* **Academic Programs Select** (`Academic Programs Select`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if you are currently enrolled or have completed any academic programs.
