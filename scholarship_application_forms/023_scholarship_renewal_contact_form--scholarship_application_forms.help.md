# Scholarship Renewal Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship renewal contact form is a crucial document for students to communicate their updated academic and financial status to the scholarship administrator. It ensures that the student's eligibility for the scholarship is maintained and that their application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information required for the form.
2. Enter your Student ID number in the corresponding field.
3. Fill in your Name as it appears on your ID.
4. Provide your current Email address.
5. Enter your current Phone number.
6. Update your Address (if it has changed).
7. Select your current Academic Status (High School, College, or University).
8. Choose your current Financial Status (Stable, Unstable, or Unknown).
9. Enter your Date of Birth.
10. Update your Student Status (Active or Inactive).
11. Add any additional Notes about your current situation.
12. If applicable, enter the Scholarship ID provided to you by the scholarship administrator.
13. Confirm the Updated Status of your application (Pending, Completed, or In Progress).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number as provided by your educational institution.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email**: Enter your current email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your current phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your current address (if it has changed).
* **Academic Status**: Select your current academic level (High School, College, or University).
* **Financial Status**: Choose your current financial situation (Stable, Unstable, or Unknown).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Student Status**: Select your current student status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments about your current situation.
* **Scholarship ID**: If provided, enter the Scholarship ID given to you by the scholarship administrator.
* **Updated Status**: Confirm the status of your application (Pending, Completed, or In Progress).
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the renewal application.
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date you submitted the renewal application.
* **Submit Time**: Enter the time you submitted the renewal application.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your submission.
