# digital_pedagogy_fellowship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Pedagogy Fellowship application form is designed for potential applicants to share their background, research interests, and teaching experiences to be considered for the fellowship. Please ensure that the information you provide accurately represents your experiences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Provide clear and concise responses in the text fields.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information**: This section collects basic information about you, including your name and contact details.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address and any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Academic Background**: Describe your academic background, including any relevant education, degrees, or certifications.
* **Education**: Describe your educational background, including institutions and degrees earned.
* **Teaching Experience**: Share your teaching experience, including any relevant courses or programs.
* **Research Interests**: Describe your research interests and areas of focus.
* **Research Plans**: Share your plans for future research projects or studies.
* **Teaching Plans**: Describe your plans for future teaching projects or courses.
* **Statement of Purpose**: Explain why you are applying for the Digital Pedagogy Fellowship.
* **Statement of Interest**: Share any additional information about why you are a strong candidate for the fellowship.
* **Uploaded Files**: Upload any relevant documents, such as certifications or transcripts.
* **Uploaded Certificate**: Upload any relevant certificates or licenses.
* **Uploaded Transcript**: Upload your academic transcript.
* **Uploaded Resume**: Upload a copy of your resume.
* **Uploaded References**: Upload any relevant references or letters of recommendation.
* **Uploaded Other Documents**: Upload any additional documents that support your application.
* **Uploaded Additional Documents**: Upload any additional documents that support your application.
* **Uploaded Other Documents 2**: Upload any additional documents that support your application.

## Tips
* Ensure that all uploaded documents are in PDF format.
* Use clear and concise language in all text fields.
* Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
