# Outstanding Citizen Scholarship Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are applying for the Outstanding Citizen Scholarship. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the student's academic, leadership, and community service experiences. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to increase your chances of being considered for the scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the fields with your personal and academic information.
2.  Provide details about your leadership and community service experiences.
3.  Write a compelling essay about why you deserve the scholarship.
4.  Ask for recommendations from teachers, mentors, or supervisors who can vouch for your character and abilities.
5.  Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the scholarship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter your permanent address, including street number and apartment or suite number if applicable.
* **City** (`student_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`student_state`, text, required): Enter the state where you live.
* **ZIP** (`student_zip`, number, required): Enter your postal code (zip code) for your address.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school you are currently attending or have attended.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the school you are currently attending or have attended.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the school you are currently attending or have attended.
* **School State** (`school_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the school you are currently attending or have attended.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, number, required): Enter the postal code (zip code) of the school you are currently attending or have attended.
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that is active and monitored regularly.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Academic Details** (`academic_details`, text, required): Describe your academic achievements and goals.
* **Leadership Details** (`leadership_details`, text, required): Describe your leadership experiences and achievements.
* **Community Service Details** (`community_service_details`, text, required): Describe your community service experiences and achievements.
* **Scholarship Essay** (`scholarship_essay`, text, required): Write a compelling essay about why you deserve the scholarship (max 500 words).
* **Scholarship Recommendations** (`scholarship_recommendations`, text, required): Get recommendations from teachers, mentors, or supervisors who can vouch for your character and abilities.
* **Student Agreement** (`student_agreement`, note, required): Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions of the scholarship.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the guidelines and requirements before filling out the form.
* Proofread your answers for grammar and spelling errors.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Attach any supporting documents required for the scholarship.
* Follow the submission guidelines to ensure timely delivery of your application.
