# the_ron_settles_memorial_foundation_scholarship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ron Settles Memorial Foundation Scholarship Application form is designed to collect information from applicants for the scholarship fund established in memory of Ron Settles. This form is used to gather essential details about the applicant, their education history, academic background, contact information, and other relevant data required for the scholarship assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you provide all necessary documents as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Info** (1, text, optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information as per your preference.
- **Personal Info** (2, text, optional): Provide your personal contact details, including your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Education History** (3, text, optional): Share your educational background, including institutions you have attended and degrees earned.
- **Academic Info** (4, text, optional): Enter your current academic status and other relevant academic details.
- **Contact Info** (5, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including your name, email address, phone number, and any other contact information.
- **Scholarship Info** (6, text, optional): Provide information about the scholarship you are applying for, including the specific program you are interested in.
- **Upload Documents** (7, text, optional): Upload any required documents, such as identification, academic records, or letters of recommendation.
- **Review and Agreement** (8, text, optional): Carefully read the agreement and review the terms before submitting your application.
- **Submit** (9, text, optional): Review your application before clicking the submit button, ensuring that all required fields are complete and accurate.
