# Higher Education Leadership Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Higher Education Leadership Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Higher Education Leadership Fellowship, a program that supports leadership development and growth in the higher education sector. This form helps us understand the applicant's background, experience, and qualifications, and is used to review and evaluate applications for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information about yourself.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Use the provided options for select fields to choose the correct answers.
4. Attach any required attachments or certifications.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (1, `applicant_info`): Enter your name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Personal Details** (2, `personal_details`): Provide a brief description of yourself, including any relevant personal experiences or qualifications.
* **Work Experience** (3, `work_experience`): List your previous work experience, including job titles, institutions, and dates of employment.
* **Leadership Experience** (4, `leadership_experience`): Describe your leadership experience, including leadership roles, positions held, and relevant accomplishments.
* **Education Background** (5, `education_background`): List your education background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Education Certifications** (6, `education_certifications`): List any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
* **Research Interests** (7, `research_interests`): Describe your research interests and areas of study.
* **Professional Skills** (8, `professional_skills`): List any relevant professional skills you possess.
* **Awards and Recognition** (9, `awards_and_recognition`): List any notable awards or recognition you have received.
* **References** (10, `references`): Provide contact information for two professional references.
* **Application Form Attachments** (11, `application_form_attachments`): Attach any required certifications or documents.
* **Signature** (12, `signature`): Sign and date the form, confirming that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
* **Agreement to Terms** (13, `agreement_to_terms`): Agree to the terms and conditions of the fellowship.
* **Contact Info** (14, `contact_info`): Provide your contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
* **Other Contact Info** (15, `other_contact_info`): Provide any additional contact information.
* **Additional Info** (16, `additional_info`): Provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application.
* **Terms and Conditions** (17, `terms_conditions`): Read and understand the terms and conditions of the fellowship.
* **Privacy Policy** (18, `privacy_policy`): Read and understand the privacy policy of the fellowship.
* **Disclaimers** (19, `disclaimers`): Read and understand the disclaimers of the fellowship.
* **Submit Form** (20, `submit_form`): Click "Submit" to complete the application.
* **Date of Birth** (21, `date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Application** (22, `time_of_application`): Enter the time you are applying.
* **Education Institution** (23, `education_institution`): List your educational institution.
* **Education Field** (24, `education_field`): Enter your area of study.
* **Submitter's Info** (25, `submitter_info`): Enter your information as the person submitting the form.
* **Agreement to Terms** (14, `agreement_to_terms`): Agree to the terms and conditions of the form, confirming that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
* **Submit Form** (20, `submit_form`): Click "Submit" to complete the application.

## Tips

* Make sure to attach any required attachments or certifications.
* Use clear and legible handwriting for written fields.
* Review and proofread your application carefully before submission.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [contact email].
