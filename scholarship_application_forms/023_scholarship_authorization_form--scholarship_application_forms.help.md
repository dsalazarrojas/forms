# Scholarship Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Authorization Form is a document that allows applicants, students, or parents/guardians to provide necessary information for scholarship authorization. This form is designed to collect relevant details, including personal, contact, academic, and scholarship-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any other required fields, as indicated by the `*` symbol.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, required: false): This field collects personal information about the applicant, such as their name and contact number.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): This field gathers contact information about the applicant, including their phone number and email address.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, required: false): This field is intended to collect information about the student's academic background, including their school name and student ID.
* **Academic Details** (`academic_details`, text, required: false): This field collects information about the applicant's academic background, including their GPA and course of study.
* **Educational Institution** (`educational_institution`, text, required: false): This field gathers information about the educational institution the applicant is attending or has attended.
* **Guardian Details** (`guardian_details`, text, required: false): This field is intended to collect information about the applicant's guardian or parent.
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, required: false): This field is similar to Guardian Details but for parents.
* **Scholarship Details** (`scholarship_details`, text, required: false): This field collects information about the scholarship, including the name and amount.
* **Review Details** (`review_details`, text, required: false): This field allows the applicant to provide any additional comments or information.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form's creators at form@gic.mx.
