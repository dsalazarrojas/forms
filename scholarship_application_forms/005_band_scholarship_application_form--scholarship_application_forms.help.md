# Band Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Band Scholarship Application Form is used to collect information from students applying for a band scholarship. This form is intended to gather relevant data for eligibility and consideration for the scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student full name as it appears on school records.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Enter your graduating high school name.
4. Enter your primary instrument played.
5. Enter the total number of years you have participated in band programs.
6. If applicable, enter your current GPA on a 0-4 scale (with decimal points allowed).
7. Enter your preferred contact email address.
8. If you have an alternative contact number, enter it here.
9. In the "Scholarship essay summary" field, briefly describe your musical background and goals.
10. If you have a band teacher or mentor, provide their name and email address.
11. (Optional) If you have already submitted the application, enter the date you submitted it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on school records.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (month/day/year).
* **Graduating school name**: Enter the name of your high school.
* **Primary instrument**: Enter the instrument you have primarily played in band programs.
* **Years participating in band**: Enter the total number of years you have participated in band programs.
* **Current GPA**: Enter your current GPA on a 0-4 scale, with decimal points allowed (e.g., 3.5).
* **Applicant email address**: Enter your preferred contact email address.
* **Applicant phone number**: Enter an optional contact number.
* **Scholarship essay summary**: Briefly describe your musical background and goals.
* **Teacher recommendation contact**: If you have a band teacher or mentor who can vouch for your musical abilities, enter their name and email address.
* **Submission date**: If you have already submitted the application, enter the date you submitted it.
