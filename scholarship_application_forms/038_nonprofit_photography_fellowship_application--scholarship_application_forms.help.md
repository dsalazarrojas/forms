# Nonprofit Photography Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Photography Fellowship Application form is used to gather information from applicants to evaluate their eligibility for the fellowship program. This form is for individuals seeking funding or support for a specific project or proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding to the next page.
3. Upload relevant files such as your portfolio and resume as necessary.
4. Review and submit your application carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Application
* **Application**: This is the first page of the form where you will enter your name.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it should be listed on the fellowship.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are representing.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for any further communication.
* **Proposal**: Enter a brief summary of your proposal or project.

### Page 2
* **Portfolio Upload**: Upload your portfolio of relevant work or samples.
* **Resume**: Upload your resume for evaluation purposes.

### Page 3
* **Review Request**: Select the type of review you require:
	+ Application and Portfolio Review
	+ Application and Resume Review
	+ Application, Portfolio and Resume Review
* **Review Date**: Select the date for the review to occur.
* **Review Time**: Select the time for the review to occur.

### Page 4
* **Submission Type**: Select the type of submission:
	+ Full submission
	+ Partial Submission
	+ Other
* **Goals**: Enter your specific goals for the fellowship.
* **Submission Details**: Enter any additional details about your submission.

### Page 5
* **Review Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback for the reviewer.
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of your submission:
	+ Submitted
	+ Under Review
	+ Approved
	+ Rejected
* **Other Status**: Enter any additional status for your submission.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name for submission purposes.
* **Submitter E-mail**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Submitter Number**: Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
