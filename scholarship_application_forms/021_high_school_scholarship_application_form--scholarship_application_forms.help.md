# High School Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Scholarship Application Form is a form used to collect information from high school students applying for a scholarship. The form gathers details about the student's high school information, student profile, contact information, academic background, extracurricular activities, and other relevant details.
 
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Attach any required documents and upload them in the designated sections.
4. Review the form for completeness and correctness before submitting.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
 
* **High School Information** (`high_school_scholarship_application_form`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide your high school name and other relevant details.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your student profile details such as name, email, phone number, address, and student number.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your parents', guardians', or contact person's information for any questions or updates.
* **Academic Information** (`academic_info`, text, required/optional): Describe your academic background, including your GPA, academic level, and any relevant academic achievements.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (`extracurricular_info`, text, required/optional): List any relevant extracurricular activities you have participated in, such as sports, clubs, or volunteer work.
* **Community Service** (`community_service`, text, required/optional): Describe any community service or volunteer work you have done.
* **Financial Information** (`financial_info`, text, required/optional): If you wish to provide financial information, such as financial aid or scholarships received, use this field.
* **Awards** (`awards_info`, text, required/optional): List any awards or academic achievements you have received.
* **References** (`references_info`, text, required/optional): List two references, with their name, title, and contact information.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required/optional): Upload any required documents, such as transcripts or letters of recommendation.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required/optional): Once you have completed the form, click this button to submit your application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications about your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to receive updates and notifications about your application.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required/optional): Enter your time of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **School Name** (`student_school`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Year** (`student_year`, number, required/optional): Enter your year in school.
* **Academic Level** (`academic_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current academic level.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required/optional): Select your gender.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_one, required/optional): Select your ethnicity.
