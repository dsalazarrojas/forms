# Scholarship Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Nomination Form is designed to facilitate the nomination of outstanding individuals for academic scholarships. This form helps identify and recognize students, faculty, or staff who excel in their field and deserve to be awarded scholarships to support their continued education and professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are submitting a nomination for a deserving individual.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Select the relevant scholarship(s) for the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominee**: Please enter the name of the person you are nominating. 
* **Nominating Institution**: Enter the name of the institution (e.g., university, college, or school) the nominee is affiliated with.
* **Nominee title**: Enter the title of the nominee (e.g., student, teacher, or professor).
* **Scholarship**: Select the scholarship(s) for which you are nominating the individual. (Example: Scholarship A, Scholarship B, or Scholarship C)
* **Nomination Period**: This field is not currently used (please ignore it).
* **Nomination Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the nomination.
* **Reason for Nomination**: Provide a brief explanation for why you are nominating this individual.
* **Nominee Contact Information**: Enter the contact information (e.g., name, email, phone) for the nominee.
* **Nominator Contact Information**: Enter your own contact information (e.g., name, email, phone) so that we can reach you if needed.
* **Nominator Institution**: Enter the name of your institution (e.g., university, college, or school).
* **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Nominator Comments**: Add any additional comments or supporting materials that may be useful in the nomination process.
* **Nominator Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm your submission.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and complete information to ensure a strong nomination.
* Double-check your entries for spelling, grammar, and formatting errors before submitting.
* Attach any required supporting documents or materials with your submission.
* The nomination process is ongoing, and submissions will be reviewed on a regular basis.
