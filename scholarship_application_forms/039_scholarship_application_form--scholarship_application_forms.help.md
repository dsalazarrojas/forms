# scholarship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship application form is designed to collect contact and demographic information from applicants for a scholarship program. This form will help us to review and evaluate applicant applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
6. Enter the date you submitted your application in the "Date of application" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
7. Fill in your address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter your city in the "City" field.
9. Select your state of residence from the dropdown list.
10. Enter your zip code in the "Zip code" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Date of application** (`date_of_application`, date, required: false): Enter the date you submitted your application in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your full address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required: false): Select your state of residence from the dropdown list.
* **Zip code** (`zip_code`, number, required: false): Enter your 5-digit zip code.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used for scholarship application evaluation.
