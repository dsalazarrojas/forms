# social_innovation_in_education_fellowship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the Social Innovation in Education Fellowship application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your basic information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Share your education background, including any relevant degrees or institutions you've attended.
3. Describe your experience in the field of social innovation and education.
4. Outline your goals and motivations for applying for the fellowship.
5. Summarize your project proposal, including its objectives and goals.
6. Provide any relevant references or supporting information.
7. Confirm your name, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **basic_info** (1, text, required: false): This field is for your basic contact information.
* **name** (2, text, required: false): Please provide your name as it should be listed in our records.
* **email** (3, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (4, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **date_of_birth** (5, date, required: false): Please enter your date of birth.
* **education_background** (6, text, required: false): Share your educational background, including any relevant degrees or institutions.
* **experience** (7, text, required: false): Describe your experience in the field of social innovation and education.
* **goals_and_motivation** (8, text, required: false): Outline your goals and motivations for applying for the fellowship.
* **project_proposal** (9, text, required: false): Summarize your project proposal, including its objectives and goals.
* **references** (10, text, required: false): Provide any relevant references or supporting information.
* **submitter_name** (11, text, required: false): Confirm your name as it should be listed in our records.
* **submitter_phone** (12, text, required: false): Confirm your phone number for contact purposes.
* **submitter_email** (13, email, required: false): Confirm your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
Please ensure that all information is accurate and complete. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us.
