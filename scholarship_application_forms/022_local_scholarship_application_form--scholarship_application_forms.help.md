# local_scholarship_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the application process for a local scholarship. It is intended to collect necessary information from applicants to assess their eligibility and qualifications for the scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Applicant Information" page, which requests basic details about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
2. Next, move on to the "Contact Details" page, where you can provide your address and phone number for communication purposes.
3. Proceed to the "Academic Background" page, which asks about your educational background, including your level of study and any relevant academic achievements.
4. On the "Scholarship Details" page, you can provide specific information about the scholarship you are applying for, including the name of the scholarship and the amount you are requesting.
5. If required, upload relevant documents to support your application on the "Upload Documents" page.
6. Finally, review and submit your application on the "Review and Submit" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): This is the first page where you can enter your basic details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): On this page, you can provide your address and phone number for us to contact you.
* **Academic Background** (`academic_info`, text, required: false): Here, you can describe your educational background, including your level of study and any relevant academic achievements.
* **Scholarship Details** (`scholarship_details`, text, required: false): On this page, you can specify the scholarship you are applying for and the amount you are requesting.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required: false): If required, you can upload relevant documents to support your application on this page.
* **Review and Submit** (`review_and_submit`, text, required: false): Review your application and click "Submit" to complete your application.
