# scholarship_application_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship application permission form is used to gather essential information from applicants to ensure that their data is shared correctly with relevant parties. This form helps applicants understand what their information will be shared with and gives them control over their data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "Required" in the form.
2. For optional fields, provide information if you feel it's necessary for the scholarship application process.
3. Review your completed form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Details** (`application_details`, `text`, Required): This field is used to enter a brief description of your scholarship application.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, `email`, Required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you about your scholarship application.
* **Applicant Phone** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, Required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you about your application.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Application Time** (`application_time`, `time`, Required): Enter the time you submitted your application.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, Optional): If you have any additional information or comments about your application, you can enter them here.
* **Permissions** (`permissions`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select which parties you are willing to share your information with:
	+ Yes: You are willing to share your information with the selected parties.
	+ No: You are not willing to share your information with the selected parties.
* **Parent Permission** (`permission_1`, `select_one`, Optional): If you are under 18, select the name of your parent or guardian who has given you permission to apply for the scholarship.
* **Guardian Permission** (`permission_2`, `select_one`, Optional): If you are under 18 and do not have a parent listed as your permission holder, select the name of your guardian who has given you permission to apply for the scholarship.
* **Other Permission** (`other_permission`, `select_one`, Optional): If you have selected "Other" as your permission holder, please enter their name here.
