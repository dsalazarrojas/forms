# CAHPS Health Plan Survey Version Adult Medicaid Survey 5.0 - Help Guide
## Purpose

The CAHPS Health Plan Survey Version Adult Medicaid Survey 5.0 is designed to measure the quality of care and services provided by a health plan. This survey aims to gather feedback from adult Medicaid members on their experiences with their health plan to provide valuable insights to plan administrators for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the date of your last visit or interaction with the health plan.
2.  For questions about your age, select the corresponding range that best describes you.
3.  Rate your overall health status by selecting one of the provided options.
4.  Indicate how long you have been a member of this health plan.
5.  For questions about your experience with the health plan, select one of the provided options for each question.
6.  Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, leaving no fields blank.
7.  For the last question, provide any suggestions or comments about how the plan can improve.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Please select the date of your last visit or interaction with the health plan.
*   **Your Age** (`member_age`, `select_one`, required): Select the age range that best describes you. This will help us understand your demographic.
*   **Overall Health Status** (`health_status`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall health status as it is currently. Choose one of the options that best suits your situation.
*   **How Long with This Health Plan** (`plan_enrollment_length`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how long you have been a member of this health plan.
*   **Rating of Health Plan** (`plan_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the health plan.
*   **Easy to See Needed Doctor** (`doctor_access`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have found it easy to see a needed doctor.
*   **Easy to See Specialist** (`specialist_access`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you found it easy to see a specialist.
*   **Good Care Coordination** (`care_coordination`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you found the care coordination process good.
*   **Satisfaction with Medication Coverage** (`medication_coverage`, `select_one`, required): Select how satisfied you are with the medication coverage provided by the plan.
*   **Claims Handled Promptly** (`claims_handling`, `select_one`, required): Select whether claims were handled promptly and efficiently.
*   **Customer Service Quality** (`customer_service`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of customer service you received from the plan.
*   **Preventive Care Covered** (`preventive_care`, `select_one`, required): Select whether preventive care services are covered by the plan.
*   **Can Get Emergency Care** (`emergency_access`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you were able to get emergency care when needed.
*   **Affordability of Plan** (`plan_affordability`, `select_one`, required): Select how affordable you find the health plan to be.
*   **Value for the Cost** (`plan_value`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall value for the cost of the plan.
*   **Recommend This Health Plan** (`recommend_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you would recommend this health plan to others.
*   **Overall Satisfaction with Plan** (`plan_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the health plan.
*   **How Can We Improve** (`improvements_needed`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions or comments about how the plan can improve.
