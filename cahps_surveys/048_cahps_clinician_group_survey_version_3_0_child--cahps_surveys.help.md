# CAHPS Clinician and Group Survey Version 3.0 (Child) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the quality of care provided to children by clinicians in a healthcare setting, ensuring that the clinician is providing the best possible experience for both the child and their family.
## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form for each visit you make to the clinic.
- Answer the questions based on your personal experience and interaction with the clinician.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (<code>survey_date</code>, Required): Date of the visit.
* **Child Age** (<code>child_age</code>, Required): Choose the age range of the child from the options provided.
* **Reason for Visit** (<code>visit_reason</code>, Required): Briefly describe the reason you visited the clinic.
* **Visit Date** (<code>visit_date</code>, Required): Date of the visit.
* **Easy to Get an Appointment** (<code>appointment_easy</code>, Required): Choose how easy it was to get an appointment with the clinician.
* **Wait Time Acceptable** (<code>wait_time</code>, Required): Choose how acceptable the wait time was.
* **Clinician Respectful to Child** (<code>clinician_respectful</code>, Required): Rate how respectful the clinician was to the child.
* **Explained Things Child Could Understand** (<code>clinician_explained</code>, Required): Rate how well the clinician explained things to the child.
* **Listened to Your Concerns** (<code>parent_listen</code>, Required): Rate how much the clinician listened to your concerns.
* **Discussed Treatment Plan** (<code>treatment_plan</code>, Required): Rate how well the clinician discussed the treatment plan with you.
* **Involved You in Decisions** (<code>parent_input</code>, Required): Rate how much the clinician involved you in decisions about your child's care.
* **Office Staff Helpful and Courteous** (<code>office_staff_helpful</code>, Required): Rate the helpfulness and courtesy of the office staff.
* **Office Comfortable for Children** (<code>office_comfort</code>, Required): Rate how comfortable the office environment was for the child.
* **Follow-up Care Arranged** (<code>follow_up</code>, Required): Rate whether follow-up care was arranged.
* **Preventive Care Discussed** (<code>preventive_care</code>, Required): Rate whether preventive care was discussed.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (<code>overall_care</code>, Required): Rate your overall experience with the clinician.
* **Recommend to Others** (<code>recommend_clinician</code>, Required): Rate how likely you are to recommend the clinician to others.
* **Additional Comments** (<code>additional_feedback</code>, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the clinician.
