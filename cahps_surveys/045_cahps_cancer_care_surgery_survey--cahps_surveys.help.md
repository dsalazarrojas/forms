# CAHPS Cancer Care Surgery Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The CAHPS Cancer Care Surgery Survey is designed to gauge patient satisfaction and experiences after surgery. It aims to collect feedback on various aspects of care received, including communication, pain management, recovery process, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Patient ID or Medical Record Number` field with your unique identifier.
2. Select the `Survey Date` as today's date.
3. Choose the date when your surgery was performed from the calendar picker.
4. Answer the questions about your experience with the healthcare team, including:
	* Whether the doctor explained the surgery clearly
	* If the risks and benefits were discussed
	* How well the anesthesia was explained
	* Pain management after surgery
	* Wound care education
	* Activity restrictions
	* Medication instructions
	* Warning signs to seek additional care
	* Emotional support available
	* Follow-up appointment scheduled
5. Rate your experience with the surgical staff on professionalism, cleanliness, and communication.
6. Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the recovery process and the success of the surgery.
7. Indicate if you would recommend the surgeon.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the `Additional Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter your unique patient ID or medical record number.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Select today's date.
* **Surgery Date** (`surgery_date`, date, required): Choose the date when your surgery was performed.
* **Pre-surgery Communication** (`pre_surgery_communication`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the doctor explained the surgery clearly (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Risks and Benefits Discussed** (`risks_benefits_discussed`, select_one, required): Confirm if the risks and benefits were discussed during informed consent (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Anesthesia Explained** (`anesthesia_explained`, select_one, required): Rate how well the anesthesia was explained (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Pain Management After Surgery** (`pain_management`, select_one, required): Evaluate pain control strategies after surgery (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Wound Care Education** (`wound_care_education`, select_one, required): Assess post-operative wound care guidance (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Activity Restrictions Explained** (`activity_restrictions`, select_one, required): Indicate if recovery guidelines were provided (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Medications Explained Clearly** (`medication_instructions`, select_one, required): Confirm if post-operative medications were explained clearly (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Warning Signs Explained** (`complication_signs`, select_one, required): Rate if warning signs to seek additional care were explained (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Emotional Support Available** (`emotional_support`, select_one, required): Evaluate mental health support after surgery (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No, Did not need).
* **Follow-up Appointment Scheduled** (`follow_up_scheduled`, select_one, required): Confirm if a follow-up appointment was scheduled (Yes, scheduled, Will be scheduled, No follow-up).
* **Surgical Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate surgical staff's professionalism (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Facility Cleanliness and Safety** (`surgical_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Evaluate the operating room standards (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Nurse Communication** (`nurse_communication`, select_one, required): Assess communication with the nursing staff (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Satisfaction with Recovery Process** (`recovery_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the recovery process (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Too Soon to Tell).
* **Overall Surgical Outcome** (`surgical_outcome`, select_one, required): Rate the success of the surgery (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Would You Recommend This Surgeon** (`recommend_surgeon`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would recommend the surgeon (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you're unsure about any question, use the provided options as guidance.
* Your feedback is valuable in helping healthcare providers improve care quality.
* This survey is designed to be completed by patients who have undergone cancer care surgery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the healthcare team or survey administrator.
