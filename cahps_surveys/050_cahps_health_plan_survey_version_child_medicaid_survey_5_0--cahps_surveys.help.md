# CAHPS Health Plan Survey Version Child Medicaid Survey 5.0 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from child Medicaid beneficiaries about their experience with their health plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Survey Date**: Select the date you are completing this form. This will help us track the progress of our surveys over time.
2. **Child Age**: Choose the age range that best fits your child's age. This will help us understand how the plan's services are received by children of different ages.
3. **Child Overall Health Status**: Rate your child's overall health status. This will help us understand the plan's ability to meet the health needs of children.
4. **Plan Duration**: Choose how long your child has been under the plan's coverage. This will help us understand how long the plan has been in place for your child.
5. **Plan Rating**: Rate your child's overall experience with the plan. This will help us understand the plan's quality of care.
6. **Primary Care Access**: Choose whether you find it easy to get your child to a pediatrician or primary care doctor.
7. **Specialist Access**: Choose whether you find it easy to get your child to a specialist when needed.
8. **Waiting Times**: Choose whether the waiting times for appointments and services are acceptable.
9. **Doctor Quality**: Rate the quality of care provided by your child's doctor.
10. **Medication Access**: Choose whether you find it easy to get your child the medications they need.
11. **Preventive Services**: Choose whether you find preventive services, such as well-child checks and vaccines, easily available.
12. **Emergency Access**: Choose whether you find it easy to get your child emergency services when needed.
13. **Mental Health Services**: Choose whether you find mental health services covered by the plan.
14. **Plan Affordability**: Choose whether you find the plan's services affordable.
15. **Customer Service**: Rate the quality of the plan's customer service.
16. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the plan.
17. **Recommend Plan**: Choose whether you would recommend the plan to other families.
18. **Suggestions**: Leave any suggestions you may have for improving the plan.

Note: Please answer each question based on your child's experience with the plan and not on your personal opinions or feelings. Your input will help us continuously improve the services provided to our beneficiaries.
