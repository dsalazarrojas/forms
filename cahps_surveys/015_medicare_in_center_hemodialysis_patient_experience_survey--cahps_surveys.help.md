# medicare_in_center_hemodialysis_patient_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Medicare In Center Hemodialysis Patient Experience Survey** is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with Medicare in Center Hemodialysis providers. This information will help improve the quality of care and services offered by these providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each section and fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly. If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the surveyor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address where you received the services from the provider (if applicable).
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you received the services from the provider.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you received the services from the provider.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `number`, required): Enter the zip code where you received the services from the provider.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date you received the services from the provider.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the provider who offered the services.
* **Type of Provider** (`provider_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of provider you received services from (Dialysis Facility, Nephrologist, or Other).
* **How likely are you to recommend this provider to a friend or family member?** (`survey_question_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend this provider to a friend or family member.
* **What was the overall quality of care you received from this provider?** (`survey_question_2`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall quality of care you received from this provider.
* **How would you rate the cleanliness of the dialysis center?** (`survey_question_3`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cleanliness of the dialysis center.
* **How would you rate the comfort level of the staff?** (`survey_question_4`, `select_one`, required): Rate the comfort level of the staff.
* **How would you rate the communication with your dialysis staff?** (`survey_question_5`, `select_one`, required): Rate the communication with your dialysis staff.
* **How likely are you to return to this provider for future services?** (`survey_question_6`, `select_one`, required): Rate how likely you are to return to this provider for future services.
* **Comments/Additional Comments** (`survey_question_7`, `note`, optional): Share any additional comments or concerns you have.
* **Date Surveyed** (`date_surveyed`, `date`, required): Enter the date this survey was completed.
* **Time Surveyed** (`time_surveyed`, `time`, required): Enter the time this survey was completed.
* **Surveyor Name** (`surveyor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the surveyor who completed this survey.
* **Surveyor Organization** (`surveyor_organization`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the organization that this survey was conducted on behalf of.
* **Tool Used** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Enter the tool or equipment used during this survey.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Surveyor Email** (`surveyor_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of the surveyor who completed this survey.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the ID of this form for reference purposes.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled in order to complete the survey. If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the surveyor.
