# CAHPS Child Hospital Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the CAHPS Child Hospital Survey, a questionnaire that gathers feedback on the care provided to your child during their hospital stay. The survey aims to assess the quality of care and help hospitals identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, based on your experience with your child's hospital care.
2. Select the answer that best reflects your thoughts and feelings.
3. For multiple-choice questions, choose the option that best describes your experience.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Child Age
* **Child Age** (Required): Your child's age at the time of the hospital visit. Please enter the age in years.

### 2. Hospital Visit Date
* **Hospital Visit Date** (Required): The date of your child's hospital visit. Please enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### 3. Parent or Guardian Name
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Required): Your full name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### 4. Email Address
* **Email Address** (Required): Your email address. This will help us contact you for follow-up if needed.

### 5. Reason for Hospital Admission
* **Reason for Hospital Admission** (Required): Choose the reason why your child was admitted to the hospital.
	+ Emergency/Urgent
	+ Planned Surgery
	+ Scheduled Treatment
	+ Injury or Accident
	+ Other

### 6. Length of Hospital Stay
* **Length of Hospital Stay** (Required): The number of days your child stayed in the hospital. Please enter the number of days.
