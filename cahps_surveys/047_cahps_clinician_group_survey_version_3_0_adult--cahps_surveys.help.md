# CAHPS Clinician and Group Survey Version 3.0 (Adult) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from adult patients about their experience with clinicians and healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Survey Date" field to enter the date of your recent visit to a healthcare provider.
2. Choose the "Your Age" field and select the age range that applies to you.
3. In the "Reason for Visit" field, describe the reason for your recent visit.
4. Enter the "Visit Date" field with the date of your visit.
5. Rate your experience with the clinician in the "Ease of Scheduling" field.
6. Rate your experience with the clinician's communication skills in the "Appointment Wait Time Was Reasonable" field.
7. Rate your experience with the clinician's listening skills in the "Clinician Listened Carefully" field.
8. Rate your experience with the clinician's communication skills in the "Clinician Explained in Clear Language" field.
9. Rate your experience with the treatment plans discussed with the clinician in the "Discussed Treatment Plans" field.
10. Rate your experience with being involved in medical decisions with the clinician in the "Involved in Medical Decisions" field.
11. Rate your experience with the clinician's family involvement in care in the "Family Involvement in Care" field.
12. Rate your experience with the office staff's professionalism in the "Office Staff Professional and Helpful" field.
13. Rate your experience with the office environment in the "Office Was Clean and Comfortable" field.
14. Rate your experience with follow-up care in the "Follow-up Care Arranged" field.
15. Rate your overall experience with the care provider in the "Overall Rating of Care" field.
16. Rate your likelihood of recommending the clinician to others in the "Recommend Clinician to Others" field.
17. If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date (1)**: Enter the date of your recent visit to a healthcare provider.
* **Your Age (2)**: Select the age range that applies to you.
* **Reason for Visit (3)**: Describe the reason for your recent visit.
* **Visit Date (4)**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Ease of Scheduling (5)**: Rate your experience with the ease of scheduling appointments.
* **Appointment Wait Time Was Reasonable (6)**: Rate your experience with the wait time at the clinic.
* **Clinician Listened Carefully (7)**: Rate your experience with the clinician's listening skills.
* **Clinician Explained in Clear Language (8)**: Rate your experience with the clinician's communication skills.
* **Discussed Treatment Plans (9)**: Rate your experience with treatment plans discussed with the clinician.
* **Involved in Medical Decisions (10)**: Rate your experience with being involved in medical decisions.
* **Family Involvement in Care (11)**: Rate your experience with the clinician's family involvement in care.
* **Office Staff Professional and Helpful (12)**: Rate your experience with the office staff's professionalism.
* **Office Was Clean and Comfortable (13)**: Rate your experience with the office environment.
* **Follow-up Care Arranged (14)**: Rate your experience with follow-up care.
* **Overall Rating of Care (15)**: Rate your overall experience with the care provider.
* **Recommend Clinician to Others (16)**: Rate your likelihood of recommending the clinician to others.
* **Additional Comments (17)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.

Note: Please answer each field truthfully and to the best of your ability. Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve the care experience for all patients.
