# CAHPS Cancer Care Drug Therapy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
<Thinking>

This form is designed to gather feedback about patient experiences with cancer care drug therapy. It will help healthcare providers assess the quality of care provided to patients, including communication, side effect management, emotional support, and overall satisfaction with care. This form is intended for patients who have received treatment as part of a cancer care program.

The form is part of the [CAHPS (Clinic and Ambulatory Patient Satisfaction] program, which aims to improve patient satisfaction and care outcomes by providing actionable feedback to healthcare providers.

<Thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form carefully, providing your honest feedback about your experience with cancer care drug therapy.
2. For each question, choose the option that best describes your answer.
3. You can use the hints provided below each question to help you complete the form.
4. Take as much time as you need to complete the form. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient ID (1)
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, optional): This is your medical record number. If you don't know your medical record number, you can leave this field blank.

### Survey Date (2)
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): The date on which you are completing this form.

### Drug Therapy Start Date (3)
* **Drug Therapy Start Date** (`treatment_start_date`, date, required): The date on which your treatment began.

### Doctor Explained Treatment Plans Clearly (4)
* **Doctor Explained Treatment Plans Clearly** (`doctor_communication`, select_one, required): Please rate how clearly your doctor explained your treatment plans. Choose one of the following options:
