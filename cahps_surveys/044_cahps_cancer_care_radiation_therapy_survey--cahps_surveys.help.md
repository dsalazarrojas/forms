# CAHPS Cancer Care Radiation Therapy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The CAHPS Cancer Care Radiation Therapy Survey is a tool for cancer patients to provide feedback about their experience with radiation therapy at a cancer care facility. This survey aims to evaluate the quality of care provided, communication between healthcare providers, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your medical record number (Patient ID).
2. Choose a date to complete the survey.
3. Provide information about your radiation treatment period.
4. Answer questions about your experience with your doctor and the care team.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the treatment results and overall experience.
6. Provide any additional feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID (1)**: Your medical record number. This helps us to identify you and your treatment.
* **Survey Date (2)**: The date on which you are completing this survey.
* **Radiation Treatment Period (3)**: An approximate timeline for your radiation treatment.
* **Doctor Explained Treatment Plan Clearly (4)**: How well did your doctor explain your treatment plan? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No)
* **Risks of Radiation Explained (5)**: Were the risks of radiation therapy discussed with you? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No)
* **Side Effects Were Addressed (6)**: Did the care team provide support with side effects? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No, No side effects)
* **Skin Care Guidance Provided (7)**: Were you given instructions on skin care during treatment? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No)
* **Support With Fatigue (8)**: Did the care team provide strategies for managing fatigue? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No, No fatigue)
* **Dietary Guidance Provided (9)**: Were nutrition recommendations made for you? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No)
* **Emotional Support Services Available (10)**: Were mental health support services offered? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No, Did not need)
* **Easy to Schedule Appointments (11)**: How easy was it to schedule appointments? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No)
* **Wait Times Acceptable (12)**: Were wait times reasonable? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No)
* **Staff Treated You With Respect (13)**: How would you rate the professionalism of the staff? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No)
* **Good Coordination Between Providers (14)**: How well did the care team work together? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No, One provider only)
* **Follow-up Plan Explained (15)**: Was the follow-up plan explained to you? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No)
* **Overall Treatment Experience (16)**: Rate your overall experience with radiation therapy. (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Satisfaction With Treatment Results (17)**: How satisfied are you with the treatment results? (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Too Soon to Tell)
* **Would You Recommend This Facility (18)**: How likely are you to recommend this facility? (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Probably No, Definitely No)
* **Additional Comments (19)**: Is there anything else you would like to share about your experience?
