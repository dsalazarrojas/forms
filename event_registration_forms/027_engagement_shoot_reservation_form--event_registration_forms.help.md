# Engagement Shoot Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engagement Shoot Reservation Form is a tool used to collect information from clients for an engagement shoot reservation. This form is designed to be completed by the client and helps the photographer gather essential details about the client and session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the client details, including the client's name and phone number.
2. Select the photographer you would like to work with.
3. Enter the session details, including the dates, times, and locations.
4. Add any session notes or additional information.
5. Confirm that you have submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details (client_details)**: Enter your name and any other contact information as requested.
* **Session Details (session_details)**: Enter the dates and times for your session.
* **Photographer Details (photographer_details)**: Select the photographer you would like to work with.
* **Session Notes (session_notes)**: Add any additional notes or information about your session.
* **Client Phone (client_phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Photographer (photographer)**: Select the photographer you would like to work with.
* **Session Date (session_date)**: Enter the date for your session.
* **Session Time (session_time)**: Enter the time for your session.
* **Session Location (session_location)**: Enter the location for your session.
* **Session Date 2 (session_date_2)**: Enter the second date for your session, if needed.
* **Session Time 2 (session_time_2)**: Enter the time for your second session, if needed.
* **Session Location 2 (session_location_2)**: Enter the location for your second session, if needed.
* **Session Notes 2 (session_notes_2)**: Add any additional notes or information about your second session.
* **Photographer Details 2 (photographer_details_2)**: Select the photographer you would like to work with for your second session.
* **Form Submitted (form_submitted)**: Confirm that you have submitted the form.
* **Form Status (form_submitted_2)**: This is a confirmation of your form submission status.
* **Client Name (client_name)**: Enter your name as the client.
* **Client Phone (client_phone_2)**: Enter your phone number as the client.
* **Photographer Details 2 (photographer_details_2)**: Select the photographer you would like to work with.
* **Session Location 2 (session_location_2)**: Enter the location for your second session.
* **Session Notes 2 (session_notes_2)**: Add any additional notes or information about your second session.
* **Form Submitted 2 (form_submitted_2)**: Confirm that you have submitted the form for your second session.
* **Form Status 2 (form_submitted_2)**: This is a confirmation of your form submission status for your second session.
