# Beauty Event Accessibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Event Accessibility Survey is a form designed to help event organizers and planners improve the accessibility of their beauty events. By providing feedback, attendees can help ensure that future events are more inclusive and accessible for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly to help us improve the accessibility of beauty events.
2. If a question doesn't apply to your experience, just skip it.
3. You can submit as many responses as you'd like, but each response should be for a specific event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name, which will help us track your responses and provide personalized feedback.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address is also optional, but it helps us contact you for more information or to follow up on your feedback.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date Attended** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Venue Accessibility** (`venue_accessibility`, select_one, required): Rate the accessibility of the venue:
	+ Very Poor: The venue was not accessible for me due to a lack of features or services.
	+ Poor: The venue had some accessibility issues, but I was able to still attend.
	+ Average: The venue was neutral in terms of accessibility.
	+ Good: The venue was accessible, with some features or services available.
	+ Excellent: The venue was very accessible, with excellent features and services.
* **Seating Availability and Comfort** (`seating_availability`, select_one, required): Rate the availability and comfort of seating:
	+ Very Poor: There was no seating available or it was uncomfortable.
	+ Poor: Seating was scarce or uncomfortable.
	+ Average: Seating was available but not ideal.
	+ Good: Seating was available and comfortable.
	+ Excellent: The seating was excellent and comfortable.
* **Restroom Accessibility** (`restroom_accessibility`, select_one, required): Rate the accessibility of the restrooms:
	+ Very Poor: The restrooms were not accessible for me.
	+ Poor: The restrooms had some accessibility issues.
	+ Average: The restrooms were neutral in terms of accessibility.
	+ Good: The restrooms were accessible, with some features or services available.
	+ Excellent: The restrooms were very accessible, with excellent features and services.
* **Parking Accessibility** (`parking_accessibility`, select_one, optional): Rate the accessibility of parking:
	+ Very Poor: There was no parking available or it was difficult to access.
	+ Poor: Parking was scarce or difficult to access.
	+ Average: Parking was available but not ideal.
	+ Good: Parking was available and easy to access.
	+ Excellent: The parking was excellent and easy to access.
	+ Not Applicable: This question doesn't apply to you, as you didn't attend the event or didn't use parking.
* **Any Accessibility Challenges Faced** (`accessibility_challenges`, text, optional): If you faced any accessibility challenges, please describe them in this field.
* **Sound Quality and Clarity** (`sound_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the sound quality and clarity:
	+ Very Poor: The sound quality and clarity were poor.
	+ Poor: The sound quality and clarity were not ideal.
	+ Average: The sound quality and clarity were neutral.
	+ Good: The sound quality and clarity were good.
	+ Excellent: The sound quality and clarity were excellent.
* **Closed Captioning Availability** (`captioning_availability`, select_one, required): Rate the availability of closed captioning:
	+ Not Available: Closed captioning was not available.
	+ Available but Poor Quality: Closed captioning was available but poor quality.
	+ Adequate: Closed captioning was available and decent quality.
	+ Good: Closed captioning was available and good quality.
	+ Excellent: Closed captioning was available and excellent quality.
* **Sign Language Interpreters Available** (`sign_language_interpreters`, select_one, required): Rate the availability of sign language interpreters:
	+ Yes: Sign language interpreters were available and helpful.
	+ No: Sign language interpreters were not available.
	+ Not Needed: You didn't need sign language interpreters.
* **Lighting Conditions** (`lighting_conditions`, select_one, required): Rate the lighting conditions:
	+ Poor: The lighting conditions were poor.
	+ Fair: The lighting conditions were not ideal.
	+ Good: The lighting conditions were good.
	+ Excellent: The lighting conditions were excellent.
* **Sensory Sensitivities or Issues** (`sensory_sensitivities`, text, optional): If you have any sensory sensitivities or issues, please describe them in this field.
* **Format of Event Materials** (`material_format`, select_one, required): Rate the format of event materials:
	+ Only Standard Print: Only standard print materials were available.
	+ Some Alternatives: Some alternative formats were available, but not all.
	+ Multiple Formats Available: Multiple formats were available, including digital and large print.
	+ Excellent Variety: The event materials were available in an excellent variety of formats.
* **Advance Information About Event** (`advance_information`, select_one, required): Rate the availability of advance information:
	+ Not Provided: No advance information was provided.
	+ Minimal: Only minimal information was provided.
	+ Adequate: Adequate information was provided, but not all details.
	+ Comprehensive: Comprehensive information was provided about the event.
* **Staff Helpfulness and Knowledge** (`staff_helpfulness`, select_one, required): Rate the helpfulness and knowledge of event staff:
	+ Very Poor: The staff was not helpful or knowledgeable.
	+ Poor: The staff was somewhat unhelpful or uninformed.
	+ Average: The staff was neutral in terms of helpfulness and knowledge.
	+ Good: The staff was helpful and knowledgeable.
	+ Excellent: The staff was very helpful and knowledgeable.
* **Availability of Accessibility Assistance** (`accessibility_assistance`, select_one, required): Rate the availability of accessibility assistance:
	+ None Available: No accessibility assistance was available.
	+ Limited: Limited accessibility assistance was available.
	+ Adequate: Adequate accessibility assistance was available.
	+ Excellent: Excellent accessibility assistance was available.
* **Suggestions for Future Event Accessibility** (`accessibility_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the accessibility of future events, please describe them in this field.
* **Would You Attend Future Events** (`would_attend_again`, select_one, required): Rate your likelihood of attending future events:
	+ Definitely Not: You wouldn't attend future events.
	+ Probably Not: You might not attend future events.
	+ Unsure: You're unsure about attending future events.
	+ Probably Yes: You might attend future events.
	+ Definitely Yes: You will definitely attend future events.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback to help us improve the accessibility of future events.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them in the "Suggestions for Future Event Accessibility" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
