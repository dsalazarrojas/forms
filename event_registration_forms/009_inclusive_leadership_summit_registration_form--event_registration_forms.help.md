# inclusive_leadership_summit_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inclusive Leadership Summit Registration Form is used to gather information from participants to ensure a successful and inclusive event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name and contact email.
2. Choose your role in the summit (e.g., presenter, attendee, sponsor).
3. Specify your role details (e.g., your job title, department, or affiliation).
4. Select your summit purpose (e.g., to learn, to network, to contribute).
5. Choose your accommodation options (e.g., in-house, external, none).
6. Select your language preferences (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
7. Provide emergency contact information.
8. If you have any special requests or needs, please specify them in the dedicated text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the summit materials.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with summit updates and information.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Choose your role in the summit (e.g., presenter, attendee, sponsor).
* **Role Details** (`role_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about your role and expectations.
* **Summit Purpose** (`summit_purpose`, select_one, optional): Select your purpose for attending the summit (e.g., to learn, to network, to contribute).
* **Summit Purpose Details** (`summit_purpose_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about your summit purpose.
* **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, select_one, optional): Choose your accommodation options (e.g., in-house, external, none).
* **Accommodation Options** (`accommodation_options`, select_one, optional): Select your accommodation options (e.g., Option 6, Option 7).
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about your accommodation preferences or requirements.
* **Accommodation Requirements** (`accommodation_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any specific accommodation requirements.
* **Language Preferences** (`language_preferences`, select_one, optional): Select your language preferences (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
* **Language Preferences Options** (`language_preferences_options`, select_one, optional): Select your language preferences options (e.g., Option 8, Option 9).
* **Language Preferences Details** (`language_preferences_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about your language preferences.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of someone who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about your emergency contact.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or needs, please specify them in this field.
* **Special Requests Details** (`special_requests_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about your special requests.
* **Special Requests Other** (`special_requests_other`, text, optional): If you have any other special requests, please specify them here.
