# Adventure Activity Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adventure Activity Permission Form is used to capture necessary information from participants and guardians before they can participate in an adventure activity, ensuring their safety and the activity's success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's full name in the "Participant full name" field.
2. If the participant is under 18 years old, enter the guardian's full name in the "Guardian full name" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the activity in the "Activity details" field.
4. If applicable, list any medical conditions or allergies in the "Medical conditions or allergies" field.
5. As a guardian or participant, sign your full name in the "Guardian or participant signature name" field to indicate consent.
6. Enter the date you signed the form in the "Date of consent" field.
7. If applicable, enter the emergency contact's name and phone number in the "Emergency contact name and phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant full name** (required): Enter the full name of the participant, including their first and last name.
* **Guardian full name** (optional): Enter the full name of the guardian, if the participant is under 18 years old.
* **Activity details** (required): Briefly describe the activity or adventure that the participant will be participating in.
* **Medical conditions or allergies** (optional): List any medical conditions or allergies that the participant has.
* **Guardian or participant signature name** (required): As a guardian or participant, sign your full name to indicate your consent.
* **Date of consent** (required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (required for activity organizers): Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Photographic release** (optional): Check "True" to allow the use of photos taken during the activity for promotional purposes, or "False" if you wish to opt out.
