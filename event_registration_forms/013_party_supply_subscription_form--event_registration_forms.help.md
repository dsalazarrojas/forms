# Party Supply Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Supply Subscription Form is a collection of fields that gather customer and party information. This information helps us provide you with the best experience possible. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.
- Provide your customer information (name, email, phone number).
- Provide your party information (date, time, location, type).
- Specify the payment method and amount.
- Confirm your shipping and billing addresses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: 
  - **Customer Name** (id: 12, required): Enter your name.
  - **Customer Email** (id: 13, required): Enter your email address.
  - **Customer Phone** (id: 14, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Information**: 
  - **Customer Name** (id: 15, required): Enter the name of the customer (if different).
* **Party Information**: 
  - **Party Date** (id: 16, required): Enter the date of the party.
  - **Party Time** (id: 17, required): Enter the time of the party.
  - **Party Location** (id: 18, required): Enter the location of the party.
  - **Party Type** (id: 19, required): Select the type of party.
  - **Party Guests** (id: 20, required): Enter the number of party guests.
* **Subscription Information**: 
  - **Order Frequency** (id: 21, required): Select the frequency of the order.
* **Shipping Information**: 
  - **Shipping Name** (id: 22, optional): Enter your name.
  - **Shipping Address** (id: 23, required): Enter your shipping address.
  - **Shipping City** (id: 24, required): Enter your shipping city.
  - **Shipping State** (id: 25, required): Select the state of your shipping location.
  - **Shipping Zip** (id: 26, required): Enter your shipping zip.
* **Payment Information**: 
  - **Payment Method** (id: 27, required): Select the payment method.
  - **Payment Amount** (id: 28, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Billing Information**: 
  - **Billing Name** (id: 29, optional): Enter your name.
  - **Billing Address** (id: 30, optional): Enter your billing address.
  - **Billing City** (id: 31, optional): Enter your billing city.
  - **Billing State** (id: 32, required): Select the state of your billing location.
  - **Billing Zip** (id: 33, required): Enter your billing zip.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions, please reach out to us.
- Please note that some fields are required and some are optional.
