# Surf School Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver used by surf schools to obtain participant consent and release from liability in case of accidents or injuries during surf activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read the form carefully and understand its purpose and contents.
2. Answer each question accurately and completely.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Participant Consent**
Participant Consent: This field asks you to acknowledge that you understand the risks involved in participating in surf activities and that you are aware of the surf school's liability waiver. Please answer "yes" if you are willing to participate and accept the risks.
### 2. **Acknowledge Risks**
Acknowledge Risks: This field asks you to confirm that you are aware of the risks involved in participating in surf activities. Please acknowledge that you understand the potential dangers and hazards associated with surfing.
### 3. **Emergency Contact**
Emergency Contact: In case of an emergency, please provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an accident. This could be a family member, friend, or anyone else who can take care of you in case of an emergency.
### 4. **Participant Information**
Participant Information: This field collects your information as a participant, including your name, age, height, weight, and other relevant details. Please fill out this section accurately and completely.
### 5. **Medical Conditions**
Medical Conditions: If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in surf activities, please list them here. This could include conditions such as epilepsy, diabetes, or any other medical condition that may affect your health.
### 6. **Allergies**
Allergies: If you have any allergies or sensitivities that may impact your ability to participate in surf activities, please list them here. This could include allergies to certain foods, medications, or other substances.
### 7. **Medication**
Medication: If you are taking any medication that may impact your ability to participate in surf activities, please list them here. This could include prescription medication, over-the-counter medication, or any other type of medication.
### 8. **Emergency Contact (Other)**
Emergency Contact (Other): If you have an emergency contact that is not a family member or friend, please provide their information here. This could include a neighbor, teacher, or anyone else who can take care of you in case of an emergency.

# Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your surf instructor or school staff for clarification.
* This form is a legal document, and your signature confirms that you have read and understood its contents.
