# yoga_class_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a yoga class and collect relevant information about the participant.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Your Name
Please enter your first and last name to confirm your booking.

### Step 2: Enter Your Email Address
Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your booking.

### Step 3: Select Your Participant Type
Please select whether you are a Member or a Guest to help us tailor your booking experience.

### Step 4: Choose Your Class Date and Time
Select the date and time of your preferred yoga class from the options provided.

### Step 5: Choose Your Class Type
Select which type of yoga class you would like to book.

### Step 6: Enter Any Comments or Requests
If you have any specific requests or requirements, please include them in this field.

### Step 7: Enter Your Contact Phone Number
Please enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your booking.

### Step 8: Select Your Assigned Tool
Please select which tool you would like to be assigned for your yoga class.

### Step 9: Confirm Your Form ID
Please confirm your form ID to complete your booking.

### Step 10: Output Your Booking Details
Finally, confirm whether you would like us to output your booking details to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name, which is required for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name, which is also required for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication about your booking.
* **Participant Type**: Select whether you are a Member or a Guest, which helps us tailor your booking experience.
* **Date**: Select the date of your preferred yoga class.
* **Time**: Select the time of your preferred yoga class.
* **Class Type**: Select which type of yoga class you would like to book (Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin, or Restorative).
* **Comments**: If you have any specific requests or requirements, include them in this field.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your booking.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select which tool you would like to be assigned for your yoga class.
* **Form ID**: Enter your form ID to confirm your booking.
* **Output File**: Confirm whether you would like us to output your booking details to you.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your booking is completed successfully.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask us for clarification.
* We will use the information you provide to tailor your yoga class experience to your needs.
* Please review your booking details carefully before confirming submission.
