# Parental Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining parental authorization for a minor child. It may be used in educational, healthcare, or other settings where parental consent is required for a child's participation or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the form page by page.
2. Read each page carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`form_page_1`, `label: Page 1`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Please enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Child's Name** (`form_page_2`, `label: Page 2`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the child's name who needs authorization.
* **Child's Date of Birth** (`form_page_3`, `label: Page 3`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Contact Information** (`form_page_4`, `label: Page 4`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Please enter your contact information, including your address and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`form_page_5`, `label: Page 5`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter emergency contact information, including the name and contact details of another trusted contact.
* **Medical Information** (`form_page_6`, `label: Page 6`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Describe any medical conditions or allergies that may affect the child's treatment or participation.
* **Authorization** (`form_page_7`, `label: Page 7`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Confirm your authorization for the child to participate or receive treatment.
* **Additional Information** (`form_page_8`, `label: Page 8`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the child's care or participation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Make sure to sign and date the form once completed.
