# Reentry Simulation Participant Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reentry Simulation Participant Registration form is used to collect contact information and simulation preferences from individuals who have experienced incarceration. This form helps us tailor the simulation experience to their needs and ensures they have a positive and productive engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information in each field as accurately as possible.
2. Select your desired session preferences from the list provided.
3. Agree to our terms of service and consent to participate.
4. Add any additional comments or notes if you have any specific needs or concerns.
5. Click "Submit" to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (`participant_info`)**: This field is a brief description of the participant's background and experience. Please provide a short and concise summary of your situation.
* **Name (`name`)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your participant materials.
* **Contact Number (`contact_number`)**: Enter your contact number for communication purposes, such as phone or email.
* **Email (`email`)**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone (`phone`)**: Enter your phone number for additional communication and updates.
* **Session Preferences (`session_preferences`)**: Select all the sessions you would like to participate in during the Reentry Simulation. Please note that we will do our best to accommodate your preferences.
* **Consent (`consent`)**: Agree to our terms of service and consent to participate in the Reentry Simulation.
* **Comments (`comments`)**: Add any additional comments or notes you have about your experience or concerns you have about the simulation. This is optional, but it will help us tailor the experience to your specific needs.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when filling out this form.
* Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Select all relevant session preferences to ensure we can tailor the experience to your needs.
* Agree to our terms of service and consent to participate.
