# esports-participation-inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help players report their participation in esports events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Player Details, including any relevant information about yourself as a player.
2. Provide details about the event you participated in, including the event name and any other relevant information.
3. Share any follow-up details about your participation, such as how you felt about the event or what you learned from it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Details** (`Player Details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your name, username, and any other relevant information about yourself as a player.
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the name of the event you participated in and any other relevant details, such as the date and location.
* **Follow-up Details** (`Follow-up Details`, `text`, required: false): Please share any additional thoughts or insights about your participation in the event, such as what you enjoyed or what you could improve on.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required information accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* You can use this form to report your participation in any esports event, including those organized by the same event organizers or different ones.
