# Government Policy Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants to manage their enrollment in the Government Policy Workshop. The form helps us understand your needs and preferences to tailor the workshop experience to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please go through each section of the form carefully.
2. For each section, ensure you have entered accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page (Number)**: Please confirm that you are proceeding with the enrollment.
* **Participant Information (Text)**: Enter your first and last name, company name (if applicable), title, phone extension (if applicable), and email address.
* **Participant Data (Select Multiple)**: Indicate whether you require any special arrangements or accommodations for the workshop.
* **Payment Method (Select One)**: Choose the method by which you will be paying for the workshop (Yes or No).
* **Payment Details (Number)**: Please enter the payment amount or any other relevant payment details.
* **Workshop Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the workshop you plan to attend.
* **Workshop Time (Time)**: Enter the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Participant Agreement (Note)**: Read through the participant agreement and indicate your acceptance.
* **Additional Info (Text)**: Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your enrollment.
* **Participant Comments (Text)**: Share any comments or feedback about the workshop.
* **Email Address (Email)**: Enter your participant email address.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your participant phone number.
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Participant Company (Text)**: Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Participant Title (Text)**: Enter your participant title.
* **Participant Phone Extension (Text)**: Enter your participant phone extension (if applicable).
* **Participant Email (Email)**: Enter your participant email address.
* **Participant Fax (Text)**: Enter your participant fax number (if applicable).
* **Participant Address (Text)**: Enter your participant address.
* **Workshop Location (Text)**: Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Description (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the workshop.
* **Workshop Start Time (Time)**: Enter the start time of the workshop.
* **Workshop End Time (Time)**: Enter the end time of the workshop.
* **Submission Date (Date)**: Enter the date of your submission.

Note: Some fields are optional, but ensure you fill out all relevant information for a smooth and accurate processing of your enrollment.
