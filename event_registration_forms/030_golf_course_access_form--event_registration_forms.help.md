# Golf Course Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Course Access Form is designed to collect information from golfers who wish to access the golf course. This form helps course administrators gather essential data for course management and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Golf Course Access Form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the Player Information form with your accurate name, contact details, and other relevant information.
- Specify your preferred tee time, including the date and time you would like to play.
- Provide your payment details, including any necessary payment information or options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information (Text, Optional)**: This field is where you can provide your name and contact details, such as your email address and phone number. Please make sure to include any relevant information that will help the course administrators contact you.
* **Tee Time Preferences (Text, Optional)**: This field is where you can specify your preferred tee time, including the date and time you would like to play. You can also provide any additional information or requests you may have regarding your tee time.
* **Payment Details (Text, Optional)**: This field is where you can provide your payment information, such as payment method and any necessary payment details. Please ensure that your payment information is accurate and complete.
 
## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure efficient processing of your course access request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
