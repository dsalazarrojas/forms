# Speaker Registration And Material Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for speakers to register themselves and upload materials related to their presentations or sessions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill in the required information about yourself as a speaker.
2.  Provide details about your session, including language, experience, and contact information.
3.  Upload the required materials such as images, videos, and audio for your session.
4.  Fill in any additional comments you have about your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Speaker Info** (`speaker_info`, Text, Required): Please fill in your personal details such as name, email, and other contact information.
*   **Session Info** (`session_info`, Text, Required): Provide details about your session, including title, description, and other relevant information.
*   **Speaker File** (`speaker_file`, Text, Required): Upload a file containing your speaker information, such as your bio or CV.
*   **Session File** (`session_file`, Text, Required): Upload a file containing session information, such as the session schedule or agenda.
*   **Speaker Notes** (`speaker_notes`, Text, Required): Provide any notes or additional information about your session.
*   **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, Text, Required): Provide any notes or additional information about your session.
*   **Speaker Contact** (`speaker_contact`, Text, Required): Provide your contact information as a speaker.
*   **Session Contact** (`session_contact`, Text, Required): Provide your session contact information.
*   **Speaker Availability** (`speaker_availability`, Date, Required): Select a date and time when you are available to present your session.
*   **Session Availability** (`session_availability`, Date, Required): Select a date and time when your session is available.
*   **Speaker Language** (`speaker_language`, Text, Required): Select the language you will be speaking during your session.
*   **Session Language** (`session_language`, Text, Required): Select the language of your session.
*   **Speaker Experience** (`speaker_experience`, Number, Required): Provide your experience level, such as beginner, intermediate, or expert.
*   **Session Experience** (`session_experience`, Number, Required): Provide your session experience level, such as beginner, intermediate, or expert.
*   **Speaker Image** (`speaker_image`, Text, Required): Upload an image of yourself as a speaker.
*   **Session Image** (`session_image`, Text, Required): Upload an image of your session.
*   **Speaker Video** (`speaker_video`, Text, Required): Upload a video related to your session.
*   **Session Video** (`session_video`, Text, Required): Upload a video related to your session.
*   **Speaker Audio** (`speaker_audio`, Text, Required): Upload an audio file related to your session.
*   **Session Audio** (`session_audio`, Text, Required): Upload an audio file related to your session.
*   **Speaker Other Files** (`speaker_other_files`, Text, Required): Upload any other files related to your session.
*   **Session Other Files** (`session_other_files`, Text, Required): Upload any other files related to your session.
*   **Speaker Notes 2** (`speaker_notes2`, Text, Required): Provide any additional notes or information about your session.
*   **Session Notes 2** (`session_notes2`, Text, Required): Provide any additional notes or information about your session.
*   **Speaker Comments** (`speaker_comments`, Text, Required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your session.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields accurately and completely.
*   Make sure to upload the required materials for your session.
*   Review your submission before submitting to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
