# Corporate Awards Ceremony RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Awards Ceremony RSVP Form is designed to gather essential information from attendees to help the event organizers plan and execute the ceremony smoothly. This form is used to confirm attendance, collect contact details, and gather feedback from attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To start, select the event you would like to RSVP to. Enter the event name, date, and time in the respective fields.
*   Enter your name, email, phone number, and company name in the fields provided.
*   Confirm if you will be attending the event by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
*   If you have any comments or additional information to share, enter them in the "Comments" field.
*   Finally, submit the form to complete your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Event Name
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are RSVPing to.
*   *   Please note that this field is used to identify which event you are attending.
*   *   Make sure to enter the correct name as it appears on the event listing.

### 2. Date
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you are attending.
*   *   Ensure you enter the date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy) to help event organizers track the event schedule accurately.

### 3. Time
*   **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
*   *   Format the time as 12-hour clock (e.g., 2 PM) or 24-hour clock (e.g., 14:00).

### 4. Name
*   **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
*   *   Ensure you enter your full name for proper identification and recognition.

### 5. Email
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address or your personal email address.
*   *   This field is used to contact you for event updates and confirmations.

### 6. Phone
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your company phone number or your personal phone number.
*   *   This field is used for event organizers to contact you with any last-minute updates or changes.

### 7. Company
*   **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name as it appears on your business card.
*   *   Ensure you enter the correct company name for proper identification and recognition.

### 8. Attending
*   **Attending** (`attending`, select_one, required): Confirm if you will be attending the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
*   *   Select "Yes" if you are planning to attend the event, and "No" otherwise.

### 9. Comments
*   **Comments** (`comment`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the event.
*   *   Use this field to provide any feedback, suggestions, or special requests to event organizers.

### 10. RSVP ID
*   **RSVP ID** (`rsvp_id`, text, optional): You may enter a unique ID provided to you by the event organizer or a reference number.
*   *   This field is only required if you have received a unique ID from the event organizer.

### 11. Note
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any other note or information about the event.
*   *   Use this field to provide additional context or information for event organizers.

## Tips
*   Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that you have a smooth and enjoyable experience at the corporate awards ceremony.
*   Ensure you have all necessary details ready before filling out the form, such as your name, email, phone number, and company name.
*   If you are unsure about any field or have questions, feel free to contact event organizers for clarification.
*   Remember, this form is for attendees only; do not submit it if you are staff or an organizer of the event.
