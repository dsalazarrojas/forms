# Intermediate Honors String Ensemble Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intermediate Honors String Ensemble Registration form is used to collect information from students and their parents/guardians for the Intermediate Honors String Ensemble. This form is necessary for the ensemble's organization and performance preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student's name under "Student Name".
2. Select the student's parent's instrument from the options provided under "Parent Instrument".
3. Enter the student's email address under "Student Email".
4. Enter the student's phone number under "Student Phone".
5. Select the student's grade level from the options provided under "Grade".
6. Choose the date of the rehearsal under "Rehearsal Date".
7. Choose the date of the performance under "Performance Date".
8. Select the time of the performance under "Performance Time".
9. Choose the student's instrument(s) from the options provided under "Student Instrument".
10. Enter the parent's name under "Parent Name".
11. Enter the student's date of birth under "Student DOB".
12. Enter the parent's email address under "Parent Email".
13. Enter the parent's phone number under "Parent Phone".
14. Select the student's conducting experience from the options provided under "Student Conducting Experience".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Parent Instrument** (`parent_instrument`, select_one, optional): Select the parent's instrument.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, optional): Select the student's grade level.
* **Rehearsal Date** (`rehearsal_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the rehearsal.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the performance.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the performance.
* **Student Instrument** (`student_instrument`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the student's instrument(s).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Student Conducting Experience** (`student_conducting_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's conducting experience level.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
