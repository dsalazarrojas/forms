# Nutrition Innovation Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Nutrition Innovation Seminar. Please provide the necessary information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details, including any dietary restrictions you may have.
2. Choose your registration type (Attendee or Speaker).
3. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Provide your participant address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**:
	+ <label>Participant Details</label> (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your participant details. This field is optional.
* **Contact Info**:
	+ <label>Contact Info</label> (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information to help us reach you with seminar updates.
	+ <label>Participant Name</label> (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the seminar.
	+ <label>Participant Email</label> (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive seminar updates and communication.
	+ <label>Participant Phone</label> (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to facilitate communication during the seminar.
* **Event Details**:
	+ <label>Event Details</label> (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant event details, such as dietary restrictions or special requests.
* **Registration Type**:
	+ <label>Registration Type</label> (`registration_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you are registering as an Attendee or Speaker.
* **Dietary Restrictions**:
	+ <label>Dietary Restrictions</label> (`dietary_restrictions`, text, required/optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please list them here.
* **Participant Address**:
	+ <label>Participant Address</label> (`participant_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your participant address for logistics and seminar updates.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or dietary restrictions, please specify them in the Event Details or Dietary Restrictions fields.
* If you are a Speaker, please ensure that you have the necessary materials and equipment to present your session.
