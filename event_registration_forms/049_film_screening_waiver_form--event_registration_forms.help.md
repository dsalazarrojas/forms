# Film Screening Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Screening Waiver Form is designed to collect participant information and waiver agreements for film screening events. It's intended to be a clear and concise way to communicate the terms and conditions of the event and ensure that participants are aware of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you're a participant, please provide your name, email, and date of birth.
* If you're a guardian or parent, please provide your name and relationship to the participant.
* Select the correct options for image release and waiver participation terms.
* Sign the waiver and participant signature fields (if applicable).
* Provide any additional information or comments in the waiver notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Waiver Participant Consent**: Select "I have read and understand the waiver" to indicate that you've read and agreed to the waiver terms.
* **Guardian Name**: If you're a minor participant, enter the name of the guardian or parent who is accompanying you.
* **Guardian Relationship**: Describe your relationship to the guardian or parent (e.g., mother, father, sibling, etc.).
* **Parent Permission**: If you're a minor, obtain parent/guardian permission by selecting "Parent/guardian signature" and signing below.
* **Minor Consent**: Select "I am an adult participant" to indicate that you're an adult, or "I am a minor participant requiring a parent/guardian signature" to indicate that you need permission.
* **Image Release**: Select the types of images you're willing to release (Still images, Video, or Audio).
* **Waiver Participation Terms**: Provide any additional comments or information about your participation in the event.
* **Image Usage**: Choose whether you want your images to be used publicly or privately.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign this field to indicate that you've read and agreed to the waiver terms.
* **Guardian Signature**: If you're a minor, sign this field to provide parent/guardian consent.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign this field if you're a parent/guardian.
* **Waiver Organizer Name**: Enter the name of the waiver organizer or entity responsible for the event.
* **Waiver Organizer Title**: Enter the title of the waiver organizer or entity.
* **Waiver Organizer Organization**: Enter the name of the waiver organizer's organization.
* **Waiver Organizer Contact**: Enter the contact information for the waiver organizer.
* **Participant Date**: Enter the date of the event or participation.
* **Waiver Version**: Enter the version of the waiver form you're using.
* **Participant Time**: Enter the time of the event or participation.
* **Waiver Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information about your participation.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address for communication purposes.
* **Waiver Organizer Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information from the waiver organizer.
* **Participant City**: Enter the city where the event or participation took place.
* **Waiver Prophet**: Leave this field blank; it's not applicable in this context.
