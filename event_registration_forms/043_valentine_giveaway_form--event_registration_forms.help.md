# valentine giveaway form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Valentine's Day giveaway event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the shirt sizes and colors for each participant.
3. Indicate the participant's win choice for each participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (1)** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email (2)** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Confirm Participant Email (3)** (`participant_email_confirm`, text, required/optional): Confirm the participant's email address by re-entering it.
* **Participant Phone (4)** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Date of Birth (5)** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Time of Birth (6)** (`participant_time_of_birth`, time, required/optional): Enter the participant's time of birth.
* **Shirt Size (7)** (`participant_tshirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select the participant's preferred shirt size from the options provided.
* **Shirt Color (8)** (`participant_shirt_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the participant's preferred shirt color from the options provided.
