# Flight Booking Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to book flights with your company. It collects information on flight details, customer contact information, and preferences for a smooth and efficient booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Start by entering your flight details in the "Flight Details" page.
2.  Move on to the "Customer Info" page and fill in your contact information.
3.  In the "Notes" section, feel free to add any additional comments or information about your trip.
4.  Select your preferred flight number from the options provided.
5.  Enter your phone number and email address for easy contact.
6.  Choose a date and time that suits you best for your flight.
7.  Confirm your booking by selecting "True" or "False" accordingly.
8.  Finally, provide your address for any further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flight Details** (`flight_details`, text, required): Enter your flight preferences, including flight type and other relevant details.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter your name, email address, and other contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your trip.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred flight number from the options provided.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for easy contact.
* **Flight Date** (`flight_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your flight.
* **Flight Time** (`flight_time`, time, optional): Select a time for your flight.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your booking by selecting "True" or "False" accordingly.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, note, optional): Enter your address for any further communication.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, number, optional): Enter your unique customer ID (if applicable).
* **Flight ID** (`flight_id`, number, optional): Enter your flight ID (if applicable).

## Tips

- Be accurate when entering your flight details to avoid any confusion.
- Ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at your preferred contact information.
