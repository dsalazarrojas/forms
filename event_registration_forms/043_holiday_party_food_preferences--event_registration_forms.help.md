This form, called "Holiday Party Food Preferences," is designed to gather information from users about their preferences and restrictions for a holiday party. The purpose of this form is to help the party organizers better understand individual preferences, dietary needs, and food allergies or restrictions to ensure that all guests have a safe and enjoyable experience during the holiday party.

The form is not strictly required for any particular reason, so users can feel free to participate without obligation.

To complete this form, users simply need to follow the instructions below.

## Holiday Party Food Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information about your preferences and restrictions for a holiday party.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select "Party Details" (page 1) and enter a brief description of how you plan on attending the party (e.g. "I can come on Friday" or "Will be with spouse and kids").
2.  Choose your RSVP info in the "RSVP Info" field (page 2).
3.  Indicate any dietary restrictions or preferences for the party (e.g., "Beef" or "None") in the "Dietary Restrictions" field (page 3).
4.  If applicable, specify any allergies you have (e.g., "Gluten" or "Peanuts") in the "Allergies" field (page 4).
5.  For each food item, choose one option that you like or prefer to eat. You can only select up to 5 options (e.g.  "Asian," "Italian," etc.) from "Food Preferences" field (page 5).
6.  Select your preference regarding the food choice options for the party. You can choose only one option from "Food Choice 1," "Food Choice 2," "Food Choice 3," "Food Choice 4," "Food Choice 5," or "Food Choice 6" fields (pages 6-11). 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Party Details** (page 1): Please describe in brief how you will be attending the party.
*   **RSVP Info** (page 2): Choose your RSVP info, which is the status of your presence at the party.
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (page 3): List any foods you are restricting yourself from eating at the party. You can select multiple choices from the given options. 
*   **Allergies** (page 4): If you have any allergies, please select it from the options.
*   **Food Preferences** (page 5): Choose up to 5 food options you like or prefer to eat. These options are the food types you enjoy eating.
*   **Party Details 2** (page 6): Enter any additional party details you want to share.
*   **Food Choice 1** (page 7): Select an option that you prefer for food choice 1. This is a choice for food for the party and you can only choose one option.
*   **Food Choice 2** (page 8): This is another choice for food 2 and you can only select one option.
*   **Food Choice 3** (page 9): This is another choice for food 3 and you can only select one option.
*   **Food Choice 4** (page 10): This is another choice for food 4 and you can only select one option.
*   **Food Choice 5** (page 11): This is another choice for food 5 and you can only select one option.
*   **Food Choice 6** (page 12): This is the last choice for food 6 and you can only select one option.

Note that each field is optional, except where indicated by a "required: true" in the form configuration.
