# Track And Field Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather relevant details about a Track and Field incident. It is intended for use in sports-related settings such as schools or athletic organizations to document and manage incidents that may have occurred during events or practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing detailed information in each field according to the specific section.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, but be aware that most fields in this form are optional.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the necessary sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): This field is for capturing event-specific information. Provide a brief description of the event that the incident occurred during.
* **Athlete Details** (`athlete_details`, text, optional): This field is for collecting athlete-related information. Enter any relevant details about the athlete involved in the incident.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): This field is for describing the incident itself. Be as detailed as possible about what happened.
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, optional): This field is for collecting information from any witnesses. If there were witnesses to the incident, provide their details here.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, text, optional): This field is for documenting any treatment or medical attention received by the athlete.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for collecting any relevant medical history of the athlete.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, text, optional): This field is for documenting the outcome of the incident.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, optional): This field is for collecting a witness's statement about what they saw or heard.
* **Athlete Statement** (`athlete_statement`, text, optional): This field is for the athlete's statement about their experience with the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, optional): This field is for identifying who reported the incident.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, text, optional): This field is for designating a reviewer or approver of the incident report.
* **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, text, optional): This field is for attaching any medical certification or clearance.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): This field is for capturing the witness's signature, if applicable.
* **Athlete Signature** (`athlete_signature`, text, optional): This field is for capturing the athlete's signature, if applicable.
* **Incident Reporter Signature** (`incident_reporter_signature`, text, optional): This field is for capturing the incident reporter's signature, if applicable.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, optional): This field is for capturing the reviewer's signature, if applicable.
* **Incident Summary** (`incident_summary`, text, optional): This field is for writing a brief summary of the incident.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments from the reviewer.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, text, optional): This field is for tracking the status of the incident.

Note that some fields may have specific requirements or submission limits depending on the context in which this form is used. Please refer to your organization's guidelines for specific instructions.
