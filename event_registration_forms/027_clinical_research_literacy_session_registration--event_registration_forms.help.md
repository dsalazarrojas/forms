# Clinical Research Literacy Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinical Research Literacy Session Registration form is used to register for a clinical research literacy session. It gathers information from users to tailor the session to their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields.
2. For fields with select one or multiple options, choose the relevant answer.
3. For fields with text input, provide a clear and concise answer.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`4`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your primary work phone number or a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Organization or Institution** (`6`, `text`, optional): If you have an affiliation with an institution or organization, enter the name here.
* **Job Title or Role** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or role as it appears on your official documents.
* **Professional Category** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Choose your professional category:
	+ Physician
	+ Nurse
	+ Research Coordinator
	+ Clinical Research Associate
	+ Data Manager
	+ Pharmacist
	+ Lab Technician
	+ Student
	+ Administrator
	+ Patient Advocate
	+ Other
* **Years of Experience in Clinical Research** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Choose your experience level:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ 10+ years
	+ New to Field
* **Department** (`10`, `text`, optional): If you have a department or affiliation, enter the name here.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`11`, `date`, required): Choose a date for the session that works best for you.
* **Session Format Preference** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred session format:
	+ In-Person
	+ Virtual Live
	+ Recorded On-Demand
	+ No Preference
* **Topics of Interest** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all topics you would like to learn about:
	+ Research Ethics
	+ Informed Consent Process
	+ Protocol Development
	+ Data Management
	+ Regulatory Compliance
	+ Patient Recruitment
	+ Study Monitoring
	+ Adverse Event Reporting
	+ GCP Guidelines
	+ Statistical Analysis
	+ Publishing Results
	+ Other
* **Current Knowledge Level in Clinical Research** (`14`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current knowledge level:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Expert
* **What Are Your Learning Objectives** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of what you hope to gain from the session.
* **How Are You Involved in Clinical Research** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant roles you are involved in:
	+ Principal Investigator
	+ Co-Investigator
	+ Study Coordinator
	+ Research Assistant
	+ Data Entry
	+ Monitoring
	+ Ethics Review
	+ Patient Care
	+ Administrative Support
	+ Not Currently Involved
	+ Other
* **Institution Type** (`17`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, choose your institution type:
	+ Academic Medical Center
	+ Community Hospital
	+ Private Practice
	+ Research Organization
	+ Pharmaceutical Company
	+ Government Agency
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Other
* **Are You Pursuing Clinical Research Certification** (`18`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your certification status:
	+ Yes CCRP
	+ Yes CCRCA
	+ Yes ACRP
	+ Yes Other
	+ False
	+ Not Sure
* **Dietary Requirements** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): If attending in-person, choose your dietary needs:
	+ None
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-Free
	+ Kosher
	+ Halal
	+ Other
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`20`, `text`, optional): If you require any accommodations, enter your needs here.
* **How Did You Hear About This Session** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you heard about the session:
	+ Email
	+ Colleague
	+ Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Conference
	+ Professional Organization
	+ Other
* **CEU or CME Credits Needed** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you need credits:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Professional License Number** (`23`, `text`, optional): If you need CEU or CME credits, enter your professional license number.
* **License State or Country** (`24`, `text`, optional): If you need CEU or CME credits, enter your license state or country.
* **Questions for Presenter** (`25`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific questions for the presenter, enter them here.
* **Additional Comments** (`26`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes here.
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions** (`27`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you accept the terms and conditions:
	+ Yes I Accept
	+ No I Do Not Accept
* **Registration Date** (`28`, `date`, required): The date of your registration will be automatically generated.

# Tips
* Be sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the session organizer.
* If you need any accommodations, please let us know in the "Accessibility Requirements" field.
* If you are seeking CEU or CME credits, please make sure to provide your professional license number and license state or country.
