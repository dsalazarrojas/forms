# Animal Sanctuary Open Day Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering your visit to the Animal Sanctuary's Open Day event. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your basic contact information:
	* First and last name 
	* Email address (for event confirmation)
	* Phone number (for contact)
2. Choose your visit details:
	* Age group
	* Group size
	* Preferred visit date and time
3. Specify your group composition:
	* Number of children (under 12)
	* Number of teenagers (12-17)
	* Number of adults
	* Number of seniors (65+)
4. Select animals and activities you are interested in:
	* Animals you want to see
	* Activities that interest you
5. Consider accessibility needs:
	* Wheelchair accessibility required
	* Mobility assistance required
	* Dietary requirements
6. Provide emergency contacts:
	* Name of emergency contact
	* Phone number of emergency contact

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address for event confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Age Group** (select_one, required): Choose your age category:
	+ Under 12
	+ 12-17
	+ 18-30
	+ 31-50
	+ 51-65
	+ 65+
* **Group Size** (number, required): Enter how many people are in your group.
* **Group Composition** (note, required): Describe who is in your group (not required but recommended).
* **Number of Children (under 12)** (number, optional): Enter the number of children under 12 in your group.
* **Number of Teenagers (12-17)** (number, optional): Enter the number of teenagers (12-17) in your group.
* **Number of Adults** (number, optional): Enter the number of adults in your group.
* **Number of Seniors (65+)** (number, optional): Enter the number of seniors in your group.
* **Preferred Visit Date** (date, required): Choose a date for your visit.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (select_one, required): Choose a time for your visit:
	+ 9 AM - 12 PM
	+ 12 PM - 3 PM
	+ 3 PM - 6 PM
* **Animals You Want to See** (select_multiple, required): Select the animals you are interested in:
	+ Big Cats
	+ Primates
	+ Elephants
	+ Giraffes
	+ Wolves
	+ Birds of Prey
	+ Reptiles
	+ All Animals
* **Activities of Interest** (select_multiple, optional): Select activities that interest you:
	+ Guided Tours
	+ Educational Talks
	+ Photography
	+ Picnic Areas
	+ Gift Shop Browsing
	+ Children's Activities
* **Guided Tour** (select_one, required): Choose if you would like a guided tour:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Wheelchair Accessibility Needed** (select_one, optional): If you need wheelchair accessibility:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Mobility Assistance Required** (select_one, optional): If you need mobility assistance:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Dietary Requirements** (text, optional): Enter any specific dietary needs.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Agree to Sanctuary Code of Conduct** (select_one, required): Choose to accept the sanctuary's code of conduct:
	+ Yes
	+ No
