# Behavioral Change Science Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential attendees for the Behavioral Change Science Seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Optional fields are provided for additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Personal Information --
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Organization or Company** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you're representing an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **Job Title or Role** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): If you're representing an organization, enter your job title or role.

### -- Seminar Details --
* **Which Seminar Date Are You Interested In** (`seminar_date`, `select_one`, required): Select the date you're interested in attending from the provided options.
* **Attendance Type** (`attendance_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred attendance type (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
* **Your Experience Level with Behavioral Change** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of experience with behavioral change (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).

### -- Areas of Interest --
* **Topics You Are Most Interested In** (`topics_interested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all topics you are interested in from the provided options.
* **What Are Your Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, `text`, optional): Describe what you hope to gain from attending the seminar.

### -- Accessibility and Preferences --
* **Do You Have Any Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please describe them.

### -- Accessibility and Preferences --
* **Are You Interested in Networking Sessions** (`networking_interest`, `select_one`, optional): If you're interested in networking sessions, select 'True'.
* **Have You Attended Our Seminars Before** (`prior_seminars`, `select_one`, optional): If you've attended any of our prior seminars, select 'True'.
* **How Did You Hear About This Seminar** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about the seminar.

Note: This guide assumes that you are filling out the form as a new user. If you have already registered for a seminar, please do not repeat this process.
