# Interest Registration For International Delegation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register an international delegation for an event. The information collected will help the event organizers understand who is attending and what they are expecting from the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your event name (1) if you are an organizer and need to register a delegation on your behalf.
2. Enter your participant's name (2).
3. Enter your participant's email (3).
4. Enter your participant's contact number (4).
5. Select your participant's country (5).
6. Enter your participant's organization (6).
7. Select whether your participant is part of a delegation (7).
8. Check the languages your participant speaks (8).
9. Enter any reason your participant is attending the event (9).
10. If your participant has any additional reasons for attending, provide them in the field (10).
11. Enter any comments or additional information about your participant's registration (11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (1)**: This field is used to register the event name if you are an organizer and need to register a delegation on your behalf.
* **Participant Name (2)**: This field is used to enter your participant's name.
* **Participant Email (3)**: This field is used to enter your participant's email.
* **Participant Contact Number (4)**: This field is used to enter your participant's contact number.
* **Participant Country (5)**: This field is used to select your participant's country.
* **Participant Organization (6)**: This field is used to enter your participant's organization.
* **Participant Delegation (7)**: This field is used to select whether your participant is part of a delegation.
* **Participant Language (8)**: This field is used to select the languages your participant speaks.
* **Reason for Participation (9)**: This field is used to enter any reason your participant is attending the event.
* **Additional Reason (10)**: This field is used to enter any additional reasons your participant is attending the event.
* **Comments (11)**: This field is used to enter any comments or additional information about your participant's registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by *).
* If your participant speaks more than one language, select all relevant languages in the Participant Language field.
* If you have any additional comments or information about your participant's registration, use the Comments field.
