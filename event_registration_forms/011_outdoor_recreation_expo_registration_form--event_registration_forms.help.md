# outdoor_recreation_expo_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outdoor recreation expo registration form is designed to collect information from participants about their preferred days, interests, and requirements for the event. This form helps us to better understand your needs and tailor the event to provide the best experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **General Information** section, providing a brief description of yourself.
2. In the **Preferred Days** section, select the days of the week when you are available to attend the event.
3. In the **Interests** section, choose the activities you are interested in participating in (e.g., camping, hiking, cycling, etc.).
4. In the **Requirements** section, select the essential items you need for the event (e.g., water, food, shelter, etc.).
5. Optionally, add any additional notes in the **Note** section.
6. Enter your email address in the **Email** section so we can contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of yourself.
* **Preferred Days** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days of the week when you are available to attend the event. Please choose as many or as few days as you need.
* **Interests** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Choose the activities you are interested in participating in. You can select only one option from the list.
* **Requirements** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the essential items you need for the event (e.g., water, food, shelter, etc.). You can select multiple options from the list.
* **Note** (`5`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the event.

## Tips
Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately, as this information helps us to better understand your needs and preferences. If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact us at [support email].
