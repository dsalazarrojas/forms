# Dance Class Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Class Inquiry Form is a tool used by clients or potential clients to express their interest in joining a dance class. It captures necessary information to help us understand their preferences and tailor our services to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
*   Provide your Last Name in the Last Name field.
*   Enter your Email in the Email field. Make sure to include your full email address.
*   Input your Phone number in the Phone field.
*   Indicate the name of the Dance Class you're interested in in the Class Name field.
*   Select the time you would like to attend the Class Time field.
*   Check if you can or can't attend on a Monday, Tuesday, ..., Sunday in the Class Day field.
*   If you're inquiring about a class, please input the date you're inquiring about in the Inquiry Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your valid email address to be in contact with us.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to be in contact with us.
*   **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, optional): Type in the name of the dance class you are interested in.
*   **Class Time** (`class_time`, time, optional): Choose the time slot you would like to attend the class. Please select a valid time format (HH:MM, e.g., 10:00 or 14:00).
*   **Class Day** (`class_day`, select_multiple, optional): Check the days you can attend. Please select all the days you can attend, i.e., 'Yes' for each day.
*   **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, date, optional): Only required when submitting an inquiry, enter the date you wish to join.
