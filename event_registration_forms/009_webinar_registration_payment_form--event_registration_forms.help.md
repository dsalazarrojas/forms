# webinar-registration-payment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The webinar-registration-payment-form is a two-page form designed for users to register for a webinar and provide payment details. This form helps us gather necessary information for the event and ensures a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your webinar registration information** on the first page.
2. **Select your payment method** on the second page.
3. **Provide payment details** if required by your selected payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Webinar Registration** (`Webinar Registration`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your webinar registration information, such as your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the available payment options to proceed with your payment.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, `text`, required/optional): If your selected payment method requires payment details, such as credit card information, please provide the necessary information in this field.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid any issues with your registration.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
