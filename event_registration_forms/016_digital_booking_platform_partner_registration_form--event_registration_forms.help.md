# digital_booking_platform_partner_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from digital booking platform partners for registration on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with your relevant information. Make sure to fill out all required fields to submit a complete registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Information** (`partner_information`, `text`, required/optional: no): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your platform.
* **Service Offerings** (`service_offerings`, `text`, required/optional: no): This field is where you can list the services your platform offers.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required/optional: no): This field is where you can provide your platform's contact information.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional: no): This is the submit button. Clicking on it will send your registration information to us.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to submit a complete registration.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
