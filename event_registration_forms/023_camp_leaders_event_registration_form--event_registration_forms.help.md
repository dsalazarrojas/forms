# Camp Leaders Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Camp Leaders Event Registration Form is designed for leaders to register and provide essential information for event management. This form helps event organizers gather required data to facilitate a smooth and successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully:

1. Fill in your leader's name.
2. Enter your camp or organization name (optional).
3. Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter a phone number for contact.
5. Select your role at the event (e.g., Presenter, Attendee, Volunteer, or Organizer).
6. (Optional) Specify any dietary requirements or restrictions.
7. Choose the sessions or topics you're interested in attending.
8. Describe any special accommodation needs for accessibility.
9. Provide an emergency contact person's name and phone number.
10. Add any additional notes or information for event organizers.
