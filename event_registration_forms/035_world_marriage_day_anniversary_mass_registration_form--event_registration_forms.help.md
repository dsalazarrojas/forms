# World Marriage Day Anniversary Mass Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The World Marriage Day Anniversary Mass Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals attending mass registration events organized by churches or other organizations to commemorate marriages. The form helps event organizers to gather attendee data, contact details, and event-related information in a structured and efficient way.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event details in the "Event Details" section.
2. Provide attendee details in the "Attendee Details" section.
3. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Details" section.
4. Add any additional event information in the "Event Info" section.
5. Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and description of the mass registration event you are attending.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information about yourself as an attendee.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the event, such as the date and time it takes place.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely to ensure that your information is captured correctly.
* If you have any doubts about what information to provide or how to fill out a particular field, please do not hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* You can also reach out to us if you need help with anything related to the form or the event.
