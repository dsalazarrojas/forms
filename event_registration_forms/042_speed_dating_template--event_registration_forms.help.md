# Speed Dating Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users registering for speed dating events. It is intended to be filled out by individuals interested in participating in speed dating, providing their personal details and preferences to facilitate efficient matching.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field and should be filled out with your actual name.
2. Provide your valid **Email** in the "Email" field. This field should be a valid email address that you have access to.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This can be a mobile or landline number that you have access to.
4. Select a suitable **Time** for the event from the given options in the "Time" field. This field is required and will help in scheduling.
5. Enter the **Date** of the event in the "Date" field. This field is also required and should be the actual date of the event.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes for the event organizers, you can enter them in the "Note" field. This field is optional.
7. Choose one of the options for **Select One** in the "Select One" field. This field is required and will help in selecting a specific option for you.
8. Select multiple options for **Select Multiple** in the "Select Multiple" field. This field is also required and can be one or more of the available options.
9. For **Select One Date** in the "Select One Date" field, choose one of the given options. This field is required.
10. Lastly, choose multiple options for **Select Multiple Date** in the "Select Multiple Date" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your actual name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you have access to. This will be used for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number that you have access to. This can be a landline or mobile number.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select a suitable time for the event from the given options. This is required for scheduling.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the actual date of the event. This field is also required.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in this field.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose one of the available options in this field.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the available options in this field.
* **Select One Date** (`select_one_date`, select_one, required): Choose one of the given options in this field.
* **Select Multiple Date** (`select_multiple_date`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the available options in this field.
