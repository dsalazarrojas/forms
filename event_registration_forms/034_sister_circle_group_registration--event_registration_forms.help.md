# Sister Circle Group Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register your group for an event. Please fill out this form with your personal and event-related details accurately.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first name and last name.
2. Enter your email address correctly, as it will be used for communication.
3. Provide a valid phone number for emergency contact purposes.
4. Select your member type: Leader or Non-Leader.
5. Enter the size of your group.
6. Choose an event date (if applicable).
7. Select the event time (if applicable).
8. Enter the event location (if applicable).
9. Add any additional details about your group (optional).
10. Select your preferred communication modes (Email, Phone, WhatsApp, or Other).
11. Provide emergency contact information, including name, relation, and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address. This will be used for communication and other official purposes.
* **Phone number**: Enter a phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Member type**: Select Leader if you're leading the group; Non-Leader if you're not.
* **Group size**: Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Event date** (if applicable): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event time** (if applicable): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event location** (if applicable): Enter the location of the event.
* **Group details**: Provide any additional details about your group, such as your group's activities or interests.
* **Preferred communication mode**: Select all modes you're comfortable with (Email, Phone, WhatsApp, or Other).
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relation**: Enter your relation with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact email**: Enter the email of your emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us.
* We'll keep your data secure and confidential.
