# Visitor Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from visitors who will be on-site at a business or organization. The form is used to gather general contact information, visitor type, visit details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather the Required Information
To complete this form, you will need to provide general contact information, visitor type, visit details, and emergency contact information. Make sure to fill in all the required fields to complete the form.

### Step 2: Review and Confirm Your Information
Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form. If any fields are incomplete or incorrect, you will be notified and asked to correct them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (ID: 1)
* **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is not required but is a good place to add any additional comments or information about your visit.

### Contact Information (ID: 2)
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: true): Enter your name, title, and other relevant contact information.

### Visit Details (ID: 3)
* **Visit Details** (`Visit Details`, `text`, required: false): This field is not required but can be used to add additional information about your visit.

### Visitor Type (ID: 4)
* **Visitor Type** (`Visitor Type`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the type of visitor you are (e.g., Business Visitor, Guest, Staff, Volunteer).

### Emergency Contact (ID: 5)
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required: false): If you need to list an emergency contact, please enter their name and contact information.

### Waiver Signature (ID: 6)
* **Waiver Signature** (`Waiver Signature`, `text`, required: true): This is where you will sign the waiver, acknowledging that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of your visit.

### Date (ID: 7)
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date of your visit.

### Time (ID: 8)
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required: true): Enter the time of your visit.

### Business Name (ID: 9)
* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the business you are visiting.

### Business Address (ID: 10)
* **Business Address** (`Business Address`, `text`, required: true): Enter the address of the business you are visiting.

### Email (ID: 11)
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address.

### Phone (ID: 12)
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number.

### Emergency (ID: 13)
* **Emergency** (`Emergency`, `text`, required: false): If you need to list an emergency contact, please enter their name and contact information.

### Note (ID: 14)
* **Note** (`Note`, `text`, required: true): This field is for any additional comments or notes about your visit.

### Waiver Text (ID: 15)
* **Waiver Text** (`Waiver Text`, `text`, required: false): This field is not required but can be used to add any additional information or terms and conditions.

### Submit (ID: 16)
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required: true): Click the submit button to complete and submit your form.

Tips:
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Review the waiver text before signing it.
* Keep a copy of the signed waiver for your records.
