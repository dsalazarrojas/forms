# Team Retreat Attendance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Retreat Attendance Verification Form is used to gather information about team members attending the team retreat. Fill out this form to let us know if you're attending the team retreat so we can keep you updated and ensure we can reach you in case of any last-minute changes or emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant page (page 1 is default).
2. Enter your participant name in the Participant Name field.
3. Enter your participant email in the Participant Email field (this is where we'll contact you about the retreat).
4. Enter your participant phone number in the Participant Phone field (in case we need to reach you urgently).
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Retreat Attendance Verification** (text, required): This is the main title of the form. It's not editable, so just ignore it.
* **Participant Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Participant Email** (email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached about the retreat updates and reminders.
* **Participant Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you about any last-minute changes or emergencies.
* **Tips**: Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy to ensure we can reach you effectively.
