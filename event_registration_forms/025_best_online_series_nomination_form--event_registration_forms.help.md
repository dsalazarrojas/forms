# Best Online Series Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from nominees for the Best Online Series award. The data collected will be reviewed and considered for the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a nomination, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Submit" button to begin the process.
*   Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Your Full Name** (`nominator_full_name`, text, `required`): Fill in your full name as the person submitting the nomination.
*   **Your Email Address** (`nominator_email_addr`, email, `required`): Enter a valid email address for communication and verification purposes.
*   **Series Title** (`series_title_text`, text, `required`): Enter the official name of the online series you are nominating.
*   **Primary Streaming Platform** (`primary_streaming_platform`, select_one, `required`): Select the platform where the series is mainly broadcast.
*   **Link to Series or Playlist** (`series_link_url`, text, `required`): Enter the URL where the series or playlist can be accessed.
*   **Series Genre** (`series_genre_select`, select_one, `required`): Choose the primary category for this series from the available options.
*   **Writing and Storytelling** (`writing_rating_score`, select_one, `required`): Rate the quality of the narrative and dialogue from 1 to 5.
*   **Production Value** (`production_value_rating`, select_one, `required`): Rate the quality of visuals, lighting, and editing from 1 to 5.
*   **Acting or Hosting Performance** (`performance_rating_score`, select_one, `required`): Rate the quality of the onscreen talent from 1 to 5.
*   **Sound Design and Music** (`sound_music_rating`, select_one, `required`): Rate the audio clarity and musical accompaniment from 1 to 5.
*   **Audience Engagement and Community** (`audience_engagement_rating`, select_one, `required`): Rate the strength of the series' following from 1 to 5.
*   **Describe the Impact of this Series on its Audience** (`audience_impact_desc`, text, `required`): Explain why people watch and love this series.
*   **What makes this Series Stand out from Other Online Content** (`series_differentiation_desc`, text, `required`): Describe unique selling points of this series.
*   **Why should this Series Win the Award for Best Online Series** (`win_justification_detailed`, text, `required`): Summarize your nomination justification.
*   **Are you Affiliated with the Production of this Series** (`affiliation_disclosure`, select_one, `required`): Disclose your affiliation with the production team.
*   **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_final_date_input`, date, `required`): Enter the date of the nomination.

Note:
Please ensure that you are submitting original nominations and not duplicates. This form is designed to collect unique nominations and ensure a fair evaluation process.
