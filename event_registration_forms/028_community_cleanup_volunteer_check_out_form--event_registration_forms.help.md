# Volunteer Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from volunteers during checkout, helping event organizers efficiently manage volunteer shifts and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling in your full name as the volunteer.
2. Enter the time you started your shift.
3. Enter the time you finished your shift.
4. If applicable, describe any tasks you completed during your shift.
5. Check off all equipment you returned, confirming that everything has been returned.
6. If any incidents or injuries occurred during your shift, describe them in the provided field.
7. Finally, share your feedback on the event for future improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the volunteer.
* **Check in time** (`check_in_time`, time, required): The time you started your volunteer shift.
* **Check out time** (`check_out_time`, time, required): The time you finished your volunteer shift.
* **Tasks completed** (`tasks_completed`, text, optional): Notes on activities or tasks you performed during your shift.
* **Equipment returned** (`equipment_returned`, select_one, required): Confirm that you've returned all assigned equipment.
* **Incidents reported** (`incidents_reported`, text, optional): Report any incidents, injuries, or safety concerns that occurred during your shift.
* **Volunteer feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts, suggestions, or comments about the event for improvement and future planning.
