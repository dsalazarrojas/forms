# Expo Decorator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expo Decorator Registration Form is used to register events that will be decorated by vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol. This form contains several non-mandatory fields that you can fill in if necessary.
2. Select the correct date and time of the event from the calendar picker.
3. Enter the event name, location, and other relevant information.
4. Provide your contact information (email and phone number) so that the decorator vendor can get back to you.
5. Add any additional notes if you want to share extra information with the vendor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event that will be decorated by the vendor.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event. This will automatically display a calendar picker to help you choose the correct date.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the event. This will automatically display a time picker to help you choose the correct time.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you want to provide to the vendor.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that the vendor can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that the vendor can contact you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or information you want to share with the vendor.
