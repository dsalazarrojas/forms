# Gala Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gala Attendance Form is a registration form designed to collect information from attendees of a gala event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date Of Birth field.
5. Select the date of the event from the calendar in the Event Date field.
6. Choose the time of the event from the time picker in the Event Time field.
7. Choose whether you will be Attending or Not Attending in the Attendance field.
8. If you have any comments or notes about the event, type them in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time**: Choose the time of the event from the time picker.
* **Attendance**: Select either Attending or Not Attending to indicate your intention to attend the event.
* **Comments**: Enter any notes or comments about the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that we can contact you with event details.
* If you are registering multiple people from the same household, please fill out this form for each individual.
* If you have any issues while completing this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
