# Interest Registration For One On One - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their interest in a one-on-one session, which will be used by the organizers to schedule and prepare for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the `User Input` field.
2. Select the date of your preferred session from the calendar in the `Session Date` field.
3. Choose the time of your preferred session from the dropdown menu in the `Session Time` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `User Email` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `User Phone` field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your session in the `User Note` field.
7. Select `Yes` or `No` for whether you want a mentor for your session.
8. Select `Yes` or `No` for whether you want to have any specific topic discussed in your session.
9. Finally, review all the information you have entered and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: Enter your name to associate it with your session.
* **Session Date**: Select the date on which you would like to have your one-on-one session.
* **Session Time**: Choose the time of your preferred session.
* **User Email**: Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you.
* **User Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can easily contact you.
* **User Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your session.
* **Session Type**: Select `Yes` if you want to have a session with a mentor, `No` otherwise.
* **Session Topic**: Select `Yes` for any specific topics you want to discuss during your session, `No` otherwise.
* **Mentor**: Select `Yes` if you want a mentor, `No` otherwise.

## Tips

* Make sure to select a date and time that works for you.
* Be specific about your interests and topics you want to discuss during your session.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the organizers for assistance.
