# Charity Fundraiser Vendor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from vendors for a charity fundraiser event. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the vendor information section with your business or organization's official name, type, and owner/manager's name.
2. Enter your primary contact email and phone number.
3. Indicate your business website (if applicable).
4. Choose a preferred booth size from 6x6 ft, 8x8 ft, or 10x10 ft, or select "Custom Size" for a custom size request.
5. Specify your booth setup requirements, such as electricity, water, or tables.
6. If you are selling products or services, describe them in the products and services section.
7. Indicate if you will be serving alcohol at your booth.
8. Confirm if you have business insurance and if you accept the vendor agreement terms.
9. Provide the total booth fee amount (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business or Organization Name**: Enter your official business name.
* **Type of Business**: Describe what your business does or offers.
* **Owner or Manager Name**: Enter your name as the owner or manager of the business.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Booth Size Needed**: Select the preferred booth size from 6x6 ft, 8x8 ft, 10x10 ft, or Custom Size.
* **Setup Requirements**: Describe any specific setup requirements for your booth (e.g., electricity, water, tables).
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the charity event you are registering for.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the venue name and address of the event.
* **Products or Services**: Describe the products or services you will be offering at your booth.
* **Will You Serve Alcohol**: Indicate if you will be serving alcohol at your booth.
* **Do You Have Business Insurance**: Confirm if you have business insurance.
* **I Accept Vendor Agreement Terms**: Confirm if you accept the vendor agreement terms.
* **Booth Fee Amount**: Enter the total booth fee amount (optional).

Remember to review each field carefully and provide accurate information to ensure a successful registration process.
