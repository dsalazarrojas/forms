# AI Transformation Consulting Cohort Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from individuals or teams interested in joining the AI Transformation Consulting Cohort. It will help us understand your motivations, background, and expectations from participating in the cohort.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Applicant name
	* Email address
	* Current role and responsibilities
	* Motivation for application
	* Expected outcomes from the cohort
	* Time commitment available per week
	* Approval from employer
2. You can skip the fields for Organization and Additional information if they are not relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (Applicant name, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Current role and responsibilities** (Current role and responsibilities, text, required): Briefly describe your current role and responsibilities.
* **Motivation for application** (Motivation for application, text, required): Explain why you want to join the cohort.
* **Expected outcomes from the cohort** (Expected outcomes from the cohort, select multiple, required): Select up to three outcomes you expect to achieve from the cohort:
	+ Strategic planning
	+ Team capability building
	+ Proof of concept development
	+ Implementation roadmap
* **Time commitment available per week** (Time commitment available per week, number, required): Enter the number of hours you can commit per week.
* **Approval from employer** (Approval from employer, select one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Approved
	+ Pending
	+ Not required
* **Additional information** (Additional information, text, optional): Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your application.
