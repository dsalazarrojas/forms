# Safety Seminar Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential customers or attendees interested in a safety seminar. The goal is to generate leads for the seminar, which can help in understanding the audience's demographics, interests, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Provide your company name and position.
3. Select your company size (Small, Medium, Large, or Enterprise).
4. Choose your seminar time (Note: This field may not be applicable or relevant, depending on the type of seminar you are promoting).
5. Select one or multiple options for your job title or position (Safety Trainer, EHS Team, Event Marketer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Lead Generation** (`seminar_lead_generation`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about the seminar.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email address to ensure proper communication.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Position** (`position`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple options for your job title or position.
	+ **Safety Trainer**: You are a safety professional with expertise in safety training and development.
	+ **EHS Team**: You are part of a team responsible for managing and promoting safety and health services.
	+ **Event Marketer**: You are a professional with experience in promoting events.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Choose your company size.
	+ **Small**: A small company is typically less than 50 employees.
	+ **Medium**: A medium-sized company has 50-200 employees.
	+ **Large**: A large company has 200-1000 employees.
	+ **Enterprise**: An enterprise company has more than 1000 employees.
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, time, optional): Enter the date of the seminar. Please note that this field may not be applicable or relevant, depending on the type of seminar you are promoting. 
* **Seminar Time** (`seminar_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the seminar. Please note that this field may not be applicable or relevant, depending on the type of seminar you are promoting. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure proper lead generation.
* If any field is marked as required, it will be clearly indicated in the form.
* This form is for lead generation purposes only, and your information will be used to follow up on the seminar promotional activities.
