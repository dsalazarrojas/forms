# Youth Crime Prevention Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Crime Prevention Forum Registration form is designed for individuals to register for events related to the youth crime prevention project. This form is for attendees who are interested in participating in the event or learning more about the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1.  Enter your First Name in the first field.
2.  Enter your Email in the second field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
3.  Enter your Phone number in the third field.
4.  Choose your Attendance Interest by selecting one of the available options: "Yes", "No", or "Maybe".
5.  Enter the Event Date in the fifth field. This should be in a format that can be easily read by your calendar or a date picker tool, such as MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD.
6.  Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
**First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, not required)

*   Enter your first name here. This will help us get a better understanding of who is attending.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, `email`, not required)

*   Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required)

*   Enter a phone number we can use to contact you.

### Attendance Interest
**Attendance Interest** (`attendance_interest`, `select_one`, not required)

*   Let us know if you are interested in attending, not interested in attending, or unsure.

### Event Date
**Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, not required)

*   Enter the date of the event you are interested in or would like to attend. This should be in a format like MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD.
