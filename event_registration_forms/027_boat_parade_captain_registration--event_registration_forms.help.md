# Boat Parade Captain Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Parade Captain Registration form collects information about the boat and its crew to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options for each field as prompted.
3. Review and acknowledge the rules and waiver before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Captain Name (1)**: Enter your full name as the captain of the boat.
* **Captain Email (2)**: Provide your contact email address.
* **Captain Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name (4)**: Provide the full name of the person you've designated as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (5)**: Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Boat Name (6)**: Enter the name of your vessel.
* **Boat Make (7)**: If applicable, enter the manufacturer of your boat.
* **Boat Model (8)**: If applicable, enter the model of your boat.
* **Boat Year (9)**: If applicable, enter the year of manufacture.
* **Boat Length (10)**: Enter the length of your boat in feet.
* **Boat Type (11)**: Select the category of your boat.
* **Boat Color (12)**: Enter the primary color of your boat.
* **Boat Registration Number (13)**: Enter the official registration number of your boat.
* **Parade Category (14)**: Select the category for your boat's entry.
* **Decoration Theme (15)**: Describe your boat's theme.
* **Expected Number of Passengers (16)**: Estimate how many people will be on board.
* **Will Children Be On Board? (17)**: Check if there will be minors on board.
* **Life Jackets Available (18)**: Confirm if you have enough life jackets for all on board.
* **Parade Date (19)**: Select the date of the event.
* **Preferred Check-In Time (20)**: Enter the time you prefer to arrive.
* **Participation Fee (21)**: Select the registration fee type.
* **Payment Method (22)**: Choose how you will pay the participation fee.
* **T-Shirt Sizes (23)**: If applicable, select the sizes of crew shirts you may want.
* **Special Requirements (24)**: If you have any accessibility or special needs, please list them.
* **Liability Waiver (25)**: Confirm that you agree to the waiver terms.
* **Rules Acknowledgment (26)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the event rules.

Please fill in the form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
