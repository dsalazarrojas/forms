# Companion Program Information Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Companion Program Information Session Registration form is designed to collect information from potential participants, volunteers, or attendees about their interest in the Companion Program. This form helps us understand their needs, preferences, and motivations for attending the session. The collected data will be used to tailor the session content, format, and communication to meet the needs of our audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as *required* in the form.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses to provide a clear picture of your preferences and needs.
3. If you have any specific questions or requests, feel free to add them in the "Any additional comments or requests?" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3): Provide your best contact number for communication.
* **Mailing Address** (4): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code (optional).
* **What best describes you?** (5): Choose your relationship to the program (e.g., Potential Volunteer, Potential Participant, etc.).
* **Which companion program interests you?** (7): Select one of the available options (e.g., Senior Companion Program, Pet Companion Program, etc.).
* **Preferred Information Session Date** (8): Choose your first preferred date for the session.
* **Alternative Session Date** (9): If available, choose your second preferred date for the session.
* **Preferred Session Format** (10): Select your preferred format for attending the session (e.g., In-person, Virtual, etc.).
* **Preferred Time of Day** (11): Choose your preferred time of day for the session (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, etc.).
* **How did you hear about this program?** (12): Select the source where you heard about the program (e.g., Website, Social Media, etc.).
* **How familiar are you with companion programs?** (13): Rate your level of familiarity with companion programs (e.g., Very familiar, Somewhat familiar, etc.).
* **What motivates you to attend this session?** (14): Choose your reason for interest in the session (e.g., Considering volunteering, Looking for services for myself, etc.).
* **Please elaborate on your motivation** (15): If you'd like to provide more details about your motivation.
* **If volunteering, what is your availability?** (16): Select your availability for volunteering (e.g., Weekdays only, Weekends only, etc.).
* **How many hours per week could you volunteer?** (17): Choose your available hours for volunteering per week.
* **Do you have any special skills or experience?** (18): If applicable, provide any relevant background or skills you'd like to share.
* **Do you have any accessibility needs for the session?** (19): If yes, enter any accommodations required for the session.
* **Preferred Language for Session** (20): Select the language you'd like to use for the session (e.g., English, Spanish, etc.).
* **Do you need an interpreter?** (21): Select if you require language assistance.
* **Will you need childcare during the session?** (22): If applicable, select the number of children requiring childcare.
* **Will you need transportation assistance?** (23): Select if you require help with getting to the session.
* **Any additional comments or requests?** (24): Provide any specific questions or requests you'd like to share with us.
* **How would you like to receive session materials?** (25): Select your preferred format for session materials (e.g., Email, Mail, etc.).
* **May we contact you after the session?** (26): Select how you'd like to be contacted after the session (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Any additional comments or requests?** (27): If applicable, provide any further notes or requests.
* **Registration Date** (28): This field is pre-populated with today's date for reference.
