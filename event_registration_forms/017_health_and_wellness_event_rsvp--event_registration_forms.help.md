# Health And Wellness Event RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVP information from guests attending a health and wellness event. It helps event organizers gather essential details, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event date from the calendar provided.
2. Enter the guest's name as it appears on their identification.
3. Provide the guest's email address.
4. Choose any dietary restrictions the guest has (Gluten-Free, Vegan, or Vegetarian).
5. Select the guest's preferred language (English, Spanish, or French).
6. Indicate whether the guest is attending, declining, or maybe attending the event.
7. Optionally, add any special notes or messages for the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Please select the date of the event from the calendar provided.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required: false): Enter the guest's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required: false): Provide the guest's email address.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose any dietary restrictions the guest has (Gluten-Free, Vegan, or Vegetarian).
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, required: false): Select the guest's preferred language (English, Spanish, or French).
* **RSVP Type** (`rsvp_type`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the guest is attending, declining, or maybe attending the event.
* **Special Notes** (`special_notes`, note, required: false): Optionally, add any special notes or messages for the event organizers.
