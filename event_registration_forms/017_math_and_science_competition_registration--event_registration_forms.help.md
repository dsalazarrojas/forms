# Math And Science Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math and Science Competition Registration form is used to collect information about participants in the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your participant's age in the "Participant Age" field.
3. Enter your participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
4. Enter your participant's school in the "Participant School" field.
5. Select your science field of interest from the options provided in the "Science Field Of Interest" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event or competition.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant you are registering.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Participant School** (`participant_school`, text, optional): Enter the school name of the participant.
* **Science Field Of Interest** (`science_field_of_interest`, text, optional): Select the science field of interest for the participant.
