# Public Speaking Engagement Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about public speaking engagements. It is used to gather data about speakers, which can be used for event management and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Speaker Name** in the first field. This is the name of the person speaking at the engagement.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field. This is the contact email address of the speaker.
3. Select your **Role** from the options provided (e.g., Speaker, Moderator, Host, Panelist, Keynote, or Workshop). This indicates your position in the engagement.
4. Choose the **Engagement Type** from the options provided. This helps categorize the type of engagement (e.g., Speaker, Moderator, Host, Panelist, Keynote, or Workshop).
5. Enter the **Engagement Date** in the date field. This is the date of the engagement.
6. If desired, enter **Speaker Experience** in the text field. This is an optional field for additional information about the speaker's experience.
7. If needed, provide **Additional Info** in the text field. This is an optional field for any extra information about the engagement.

* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person speaking at the engagement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the contact email address of the speaker.

* **Engagement Date** (`engagement_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the engagement.
* **Speaker Experience** (`speaker_experience`, text, optional): Provide additional information about the speaker's experience.
