# open_data_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants and speakers to register for an Open Data Workshop. It collects essential information to facilitate smooth event coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Name** in the first field.
2.  Enter your **Email** in the second field.
3.  If you have a **Phone**, please add it in the third field (optional).
4.  In the fourth field, enter the **Event Name** as it is displayed on the workshop materials or promotional materials.
5.  Select the **Date** of the workshop when you are asked to do so.
6.  Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** of your participation in the workshop. This is when you are expected to arrive and leave.
7.  Provide a brief description of the **Topic** you wish to discuss or the workshop you are participating in.
8.  If you have any additional details, such as special requirements or requests, please include them in **Additional Info** (optional).
9.  Lastly, if you wish to add any **Participant Notes** or special instructions for the workshop staff, feel free to do so in the final field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name**: A required field where you enter your name as you would like to be referred to or listed.
*   **Email**: A required field where you enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication regarding the workshop.
*   **Phone**: An optional field for your phone number. This can be helpful for contact purposes but is not necessary.
*   **Event Name**: A required field where you enter the name of the event as it appears on workshop materials or promotional content.
*   **Date**: The date you are registered to participate in or speak at the workshop. Ensure it aligns with your event schedule and the workshop's schedule.
*   **Start Time** and **End Time**: Required fields indicating your expected start and end times at the workshop. Align these with your schedule and the workshop's schedule.
*   **Topic**: A required field describing the topic or workshop you are registering for or leading. This helps ensure you are at the correct event.
*   **Description**: A non-required field for any additional details about your workshop, topic, or your expectations.
*   **Speaker**: This field is non-required but can be used if you are leading or presenting a workshop.
*   **Additional Info**: A field for any other information you feel is necessary for organizers or staff members to be aware of for your attendance or participation.
*   **Participant Notes**: Non-required notes or instructions for the workshop staff to ensure your comfort or specific needs are met.

This form is designed to gather essential information to organize the Open Data Workshop smoothly and ensure that participants have a well-coordinated and enjoyable experience.
