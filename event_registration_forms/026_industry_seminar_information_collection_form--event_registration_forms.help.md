# Industry Seminar Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important details about the industry seminar and attendees, which will help us better understand their needs and preferences. By providing accurate and complete information, you'll enable us to deliver a more engaging and relevant seminar experience. 

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that you complete the form successfully, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the **Event Details** section.
2. Move on to **Attendee Details**, providing any required information.
3. In **Session Preferences**, select one or multiple sessions that interest you.
4. In **Event Preferences**, indicate your interest in specific event aspects.
5. If applicable, provide **Feedback and Suggestions** about your experience.
6. Answer **Event Preferences 2** to let us know which session is your priority.
7. Mark your **Attended Before** status.
8. If needed, mention **Special Arrangements** you may require.
9. Finally, provide your **Contact After Event** preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: 
  **Title:** `title`
  **Type:** `text`
  **Required:** true
  This field is where you should enter the title of the seminar you're interested in or attending.
* **Attendee Details**: 
  **Title:** `attendee_details`
  **Type:** `text`
  **Required:** true
  This field is for providing any relevant details about yourself as an attendee.
* **Session Preferences**: 
  **Title:** `session_preferences`
  **Type:** `select_multiple`
  **Required:** false
  Choose one or multiple sessions that interest you.
* **Event Preferences**: 
  **Title:** `event_preferences`
  **Type:** `select_multiple`
  **Required:** false
  Indicate your preference for event aspects.
* **Feedback and Suggestions**: 
  **Title:** `feedback_and_suggestions`
  **Type:** `text`
  **Required:** false
  Share any feedback or suggestions you might have.
* **Event Preferences 2**: 
  **Title:** `event_preferences_2`
  **Type:** `select_one`
  **Required:** false
  Select the session that's a priority for you.
* **Attended Before**: 
  **Title:** `attended_before`
  **Type:** `select_one`
  **Required:** false
  Indicate if you've attended this seminar before.
* **Special Arrangements**: 
  **Title:** `special_arrangements`
  **Type:** `text`
  **Required:** false
  Mention any special arrangements you might need.
* **Contact After Event**: 
  **Title:** `contact_after_event`
  **Type:** `checkbox`
  **Required:** false
  Indicate how you'd like to be contacted after the event.
* **Sponsorship Interest**: 
  **Title:** `sponsorship_interest`
  **Type:** `checkbox`
  **Required:** false
  Indicate your interest in sponsorship opportunities.
