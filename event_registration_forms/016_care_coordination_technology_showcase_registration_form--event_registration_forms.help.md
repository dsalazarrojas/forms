# Care Coordination Technology Showcase Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Care Coordination Technology Showcase event. It's meant to help us get to know you and understand your interests and needs for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Registration Details** page with your basic information.
2. Proceed to the **Attendee Information** page and fill out your:
	* **Attendee name**: Your full name for badge purposes.
	* **Organization**: Your affiliated workplace.
	* **Email**: Your email for registration confirmation.
	* **Job title**: Your role within your organization.
3. Move on to the **Event Details** page and select your:
	* **Session preferences**: Choose topics of interest for the event (Care coordination platforms, Telehealth solutions, Data analytics, Interoperability).
	* **Special accommodations**: If you have any accessibility or dietary needs.
	* **Emergency contact**: Name and phone number for event day use only.
4. Review the **Terms and Conditions** page and choose:
	* **Agree to event terms and conditions**: You must agree to the terms and conditions to successfully register.
5. Finally, provide any additional **Additional notes**: Share any other relevant information you'd like us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name** (Attendee name, text, required): This field is for your full name to be displayed on your badge for event identification purposes.
* **Organization** (Organization, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your affiliated workplace or organization name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Use a valid email address for registration confirmation and future updates.
* **Job title** (Job title, text, optional): Enter your role within your organization for networking purposes.
* **Session preferences** (Session preferences, select_multiple, optional): Choose topics of interest from the list provided to help us tailor the event content to your needs.
* **Special accommodations** (Special accommodations, text, optional): If you have any accessibility or dietary needs, please let us know so we can accommodate them.
* **Emergency contact** (Emergency contact name and phone, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of a contact person for event day use only.
* **Agree to event terms and conditions** (Agree to event terms and conditions, select_one, required): You must agree to the event terms and conditions to successfully register.
* **Additional notes** (Additional notes, text, optional): This field is for you to share any other relevant information you'd like us to know.
