# Fire Safety Drill Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Drill Waiver Form is a document used to ensure participant safety and responsibility during events. It is used to collect information, agreements, and signatures that confirm participants are aware of and will follow safety guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the step-by-step instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information (1)**: Enter your name and any other participant information as required.
- **Contact Details (2)**: Provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.
- **Waiver Information (3)**: Review and agree to the terms outlined in this waiver.
- **Event Information (4)**: Enter any relevant information about the event.
- **Medical Information (5)**: Share any medical conditions or allergies you have that may impact your participation.
- **Emergency Information (6)**: Provide details about emergency contact persons or procedures.
- **Participant Agreement (7)**: Confirm your understanding and agreement to the event's rules and guidelines.
- **Participant Signature (8)**: Sign to confirm your agreement and responsibility.
- **Witness Signature (9)**: The witness signature is optional, but recommended for added security.
- **Witness Information (10)**: Enter the witness's contact information for reference.
- **Event Signature (11)**: Confirm the event organizer's agreement and responsibility.
- **Event Information (12)**: Enter any event-specific information.
- **Event Agreement (13)**: Confirm the event organizer's understanding and agreement to the event's rules.
- **Medical Certification (14)**: If required, provide medical certification or clearance.
- **Participant Certification (15)**: Confirm your certification or clearance status.
- **Event Waiver (16)**: Review and agree to the event waiver.
- **Medical Waiver (17)**: Review and agree to the medical waiver.
- **Participant Acknowledgement (18)**: Confirm your understanding and agreement to the participant's responsibilities.
- **Event Acknowledgement (19)**: Confirm the event organizer's understanding and agreement to the event's responsibilities.
- **Participant Declaration (20)**: Provide a personal statement or declaration.
- **Participant Statement (21)**: Enter a statement or declaration related to your participation.
- **Participant Signature 2 (22)**: Confirm your second signature or acknowledgment.
- **Event Statement (23)**: Enter a statement or declaration related to the event.
- **Event Declaration (24)**: Enter a personal statement or declaration.
- **Event Signatures (25)**: Enter signatures of all event organizers or personnel.
