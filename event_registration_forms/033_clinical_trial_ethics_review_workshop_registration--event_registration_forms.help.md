# Clinical Trial Ethics Review Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering for the Clinical Trial Ethics Review Workshop, a comprehensive training program on ethics in clinical trial research. To register, please fill out the form below, providing the required information, and submit it. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill out the form**: Begin by filling out each field with the requested information.
2.  **Submit the form**: Once you have completed all the required fields, click the submit button to register for the workshop.
