# leadership_development_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in participating in leadership development programs as volunteers. It helps us understand your personal and contact information, volunteer details, skills, and interests to ensure relevance and non-duplication of efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps below to complete this form:

1.  Provide your personal information in the "Personal Information" section.
2.  Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3.  Describe your volunteer interests and skills in the "Volunteer Details" and "Volunteer Skills" sections.
4.  List your volunteer interests and areas of background in the "Volunteer Interests" section.
5.  Enter and confirm your email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
6.  Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your registration form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Personal Information**: Enter your personal details in this section.
*   **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information in this section, including your email and phone number.
*   **Volunteer Details**: Describe your volunteer interests and skills in this section.
*   **Volunteer Skills**: List all relevant skills, certifications, and special talents you have that you can offer as a volunteer.
*   **Volunteer Interests**: List all areas of interest or topics you have a background in.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address.
*   **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number.
*   **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your registration form.
