# Environmental Workshop Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participant registration for an environmental workshop. It is intended to gather necessary information about the participant and their emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Ensure that the emergency contact information is correct and up-to-date.
* Select the participant agreement and consent options that apply to you.
* Sign the participant signature field to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Please enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the first name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the last name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, optional): Please enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, optional): Please enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the relationship with the emergency contact from the following options: Spouse, Parent, Child, Other.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agree_to_participate_in_the_workshop`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select the participant agreement and consent options that apply to you. 
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_con_ent`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select the participant consent options that apply to you.
* **Workshop Attendance** (`workshop_attendance`, `note`, optional): Please enter any additional information about your workshop attendance.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, optional): Please sign this field to confirm your participation.
