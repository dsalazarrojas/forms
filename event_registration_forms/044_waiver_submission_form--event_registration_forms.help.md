# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and medical information from participants for event registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Provide your date of birth.
5. Check the waiver status (if applicable).
6. Select the event type (if applicable).
7. Acknowledge any risks associated with the event (if applicable).
8. Enter your emergency contact name (if applicable).
9. Specify the emergency contact relationship (if applicable).
10. Enter the emergency contact phone number (if applicable).
11. Enter the emergency contact email (if applicable).
12. Provide any medical information (if applicable).
13. Enter any medical notes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, text, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`, select_one, required): Select the status of your waiver (if applicable).
* **Date Signed** (`waiver_date_signed`, text, optional): Enter the date you signed the waiver (if applicable).
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event you are participating in (if applicable).
* **Risk Acknowledged** (`risk_acknowledged`, select_one, optional): Acknowledge any risks associated with the event (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical information (if applicable).
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes (if applicable).
* **Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your waiver (if applicable).
* **Waiver Date Signed** (`waiver_date_signed`, text, optional): Enter the date you signed the waiver (if applicable).

Note: The form fields marked as optional are only required if the corresponding waiver status is selected.
