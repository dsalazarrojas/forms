# Youth Soccer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Youth Soccer Camp Registration Form is designed to collect relevant information from parents or guardians of youth soccer players registering their children for a soccer camp.

## How To Complete This Form

### Completing the form is a straightforward process:

1. Enter the athlete's information on **page 1**.
2. Provide the parent or guardian's information on **page 2**.
3. Review and sign the **Consent and Acknowledgment** section on **page 3**.

### Tips

- Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any information, contact the event organizers for assistance.
- Review and understand the terms of the consent before signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Athlete Information (Page 1)
* **Athlete Information** (`athlete_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the athlete's name, age, date of birth, and other relevant details. This section is used to identify and verify the athlete's information.

### Parent or Guardian Information (Page 2)
* **Parent or Guardian Information** (`parent_or_guardian_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the parent or guardian's name, contact information, and other necessary details. This section is used to gather contact information and consent for the athlete's participation in the camp.

### Consent and Acknowledgment (Page 3)
* **Consent and Acknowledgment** (`consent`, text, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the camp, acknowledging that you understand the rules, regulations, and risks associated with the event. This section is essential for the parents or guardians to give their consent for their child to participate.
