# Skincare Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in attending a skincare webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "What is your email address?" field. Make sure to enter it correctly to ensure you can receive updates and information about the webinar.
3. If you have a phone number, you can add it in the "What is your phone number?" field. This is optional.
4. Select one or both segments you are interested in from the dropdown list "Which segment are you interested in?"
5. Add any additional comments or questions you have for the speaker in the "Do you have any comments or questions for the speaker?" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name? (Text, Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **What is your email address? (Text, Required)**: Enter your email address to receive updates and information about the webinar.
* **What is your phone number? (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Which segment are you interested in? (Select Multiple, Required)**: Select one or both segments you are interested in from the dropdown list.
* **Do you have any comments or questions for the speaker? (Text, Optional)**: Add any additional comments or questions you have for the speaker.
