# maritime_exhibition_invitation_rsvp - Help Guide

## Purpose
The maritime_exhibition_invitation_rsvp form is used to register your attendance and provide necessary details for a maritime exhibition event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the Exhibition Name to help the organizers identify the event.
2. Enter your Attendee Name to ensure that your identity is recorded correctly.
3. Enter your Email and Phone number to enable easy communication with you.
4. Select your RSVP Status to inform the organizers about your attendance.
5. Choose the Category and Event Type to categorize the event and provide more details.
6. Enter the Event Date, Event Time, and Location to provide necessary context.
7. Enter the Organizer's name or contact information to ensure that they can contact you back.
8. Provide the Attendees Count and Details to give the organizers an idea about the number and type of attendees.
9. Enter the Event URL if available to provide a link to the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the exhibition you are registering for.
* **Attendee Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ticket or invitation.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address to enable easy communication with the organizers.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to receive important updates about the event.
* **RSVP Status** (`select_one`, required: true):
	+ Option 1: I will attend the event.
	+ Option 2: I am unable to attend.
	+ Option 3: I am still deciding.
* **Attending Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to attend the event.
* **Arriving Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the event.
* **Note** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Category** (`select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or more categories to help the organizers categorize the event.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Event Type** (`text`, required: false): Enter the type of event to provide more details.
* **Event Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **Location** (`text`, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Organizer** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name or contact information of the event organizer.
* **Attendees Count** (`number`, required: false): Enter the number of attendees you are planning to bring.
* **Attendees Details** (`text`, required: false): Provide additional details about the attendees.
* **Event URL** (`text`, required: false): Enter the URL of the event if available.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your RSVP status to avoid confusion.
* Provide any additional details that you think might be helpful for the organizers.
