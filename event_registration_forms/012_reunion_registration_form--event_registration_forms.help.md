We are creating this help guide for the "Reunion Registration Form" to ensure that users understand the purpose and functionality of the form. This form is designed to collect information from individuals attending a reunion, which will help organizers and other attendees connect with each other. 

The form is divided into several pages, each containing a set of fields. The following guide will walk you through each page and field, explaining what to expect and how to complete it.

# Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reunion Registration Form is a form designed to collect information from individuals attending a reunion. This information will help organizers and other attendees connect with each other and have a more enjoyable experience during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and field carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your information accurately and completely for each field.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address for event organizers to contact you if needed.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the school you attended.
* **Attending Years** (`attending_years`, `number`, required: false): Enter the years you attended the school.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about your attendance.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the event organizers for clarification.
* Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
