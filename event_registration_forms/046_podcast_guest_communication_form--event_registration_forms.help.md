# Podcast Guest Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for internal communication with podcast guests, allowing them to provide their information and communicate with the team efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information. If you need to add additional information, use the "Message" field to provide more details.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address as it appears on your official communication channels.

### Message
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional information or questions you have for the team. If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them here.

### Message 2

## Tips
