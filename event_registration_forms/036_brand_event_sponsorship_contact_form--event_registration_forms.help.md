# Brand Event Sponsorship Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Event Sponsorship Contact Form is designed to collect information from potential event sponsors to facilitate effective communication and partnership opportunities with the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your company name, contact person name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your interested sponsorship level from the options provided.
3. Indicate your primary goals for this sponsorship, selecting all that apply.
4. If you have an estimated budget for the sponsorship, enter it.
5. Specify which event you are interested in sponsoring.
6. If you have sponsored the event before, describe your past experience.
7. Provide a brief description of how you would like to activate your brand at the event.
8. Choose a method for our team to follow up with you.
9. Select a date for your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Company Name** (`sponsor_company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Contact Person Name** (`sponsor_contact`, text, required): Enter your name as the contact person for this sponsorship inquiry.
* **Contact Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to follow up with you.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`sponsor_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Interested Sponsorship Level** (`sponsor_lvl`, select_one, required): Select the sponsorship level you are interested in, such as Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, or Custom Partnership.
* **Primary Goals for this Sponsorship** (`sponsor_goals`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the primary goals for this sponsorship by selecting all that apply from the options provided.
* **Estimated Sponsorship Budget** (`sponsor_budget`, number, optional): If you have an estimated budget for the sponsorship, enter it.
* **Which specific event are you interested in sponsoring?** (`target_event`, text, required): Specify which event you are interested in sponsoring.
* **Have you sponsored our events before?** (`past_sponsor_status`, text, optional): Describe your past experience with our events, if any.
* **Briefly describe how you would like to activate your brand at the event** (`activation_ideas`, text, required): Provide a brief description of how you would like to activate your brand at the event.
* **How should our team follow up with you?** (`follow_up_meth`, select_one, required): Choose a method for our team to follow up with you, such as Email, Phone Call, or In-person Meeting.
* **Date of Inquiry** (`inq_dt`, date, required): Select the date you submitted this sponsorship inquiry.

## Tips

* Please answer all mandatory fields (marked as required).
* Use clear and concise language when providing brief descriptions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
