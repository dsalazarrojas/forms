# Volleyball Match Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a volleyball match, allowing you to track match results, player performance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the match details in the first field.
2. Record the scores of the match in the second field.
3. Enter any additional player statistics in the third field.
4. Add any notes or comments about the match in the fourth field.
5. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form in the fifth field.
6. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form in the sixth field.
7. Select a date and time for the match from the calendar or enter it manually in the seventh field.
8. Choose a time for the match from the dropdown list in the eighth field.
9. Select one of the available options in the ninth field.
10. Select multiple options that apply to the match in the tenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Match Details** (`match_details`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the match.
* **Scores** (`scores`, `text`, optional): Record the scores of the match.
* **Player Stats** (`player_stats`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional player statistics.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any comments or notes about the match.
* **Email** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Date Time** (`date_time`, `date`, optional): Select a date and time for the match from the calendar or enter it manually.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Choose a time for the match from the dropdown list.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the available options.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options that apply to the match.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting.
* Use the form for its intended purpose to avoid confusion with other forms.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
