# Running Shoe Demo Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event registration information from attendees for the Running Shoe Demo Day event. Please use this guide to help you fill out the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
* Fill out at least one field to ensure form submission.
* Use the provided hints to guide you through the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Info
* **Event Info** (`attendee_info`, text, optional): This field is intended for the user to provide their event info, but we're not entirely sure what that means. Please enter any relevant information about your attendance.

### Schedule a Session
* **Schedule a Session** (`schedule_session`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more sessions from the provided options (Session 1, Session 2, Session 3) that you would like to attend.

### Waiver
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, select_one, optional): Check one of the two options: I have read and agreed to the waiver or I have not read and agreed to the waiver.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information for any purpose.

### Address
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your registration.
