# Fashion Show Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request participation in a fashion show event. To be eligible, you must provide your name, contact information, and a brief description of your role in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have read the purpose and eligibility criteria for this form.
2. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, email, phone number, and description of your role in the event.
3. If you are a model, please ensure you have a valid email address, as we will use it to communicate with you regarding your participation.
4. Double-check that you are not a duplicate participant before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name**: This is the name of the fashion show event you are interested in participating in. 
- **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
- **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
- **Email**: This is your email address. Please ensure it is valid as we will use it to communicate with you regarding your participation.
- **Phone**: This is your phone number. It is not required but will help us to contact you.
- **Participant Type**: This field allows you to select your role in the fashion show event. Choose from the options:
	* **Designer**: If you are a designer, select this option.
	* **Model**: If you are a model, select this option.
	* **Staff**: If you are a staff member, select this option.
	* **Other**: If you have another role, select this option.
- **Note**: This is an optional field for any additional information you want to provide about your participation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a valid email address to receive important communication from us.
* Double-check that you are not a duplicate participant before submitting the form.
* If you are a model, ensure that you have a valid email address so we can communicate with you regarding your participation.
