# Startup Pitch Event Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Pitch Event Leave Form is a tool used by event organizers to collect information from attendees who are leaving or have left the event. This form helps event organizers track and manage the event's progress, attendee engagement, and potential issues that may arise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested information, such as your name, date, time, and reason for leaving.
2. Provide additional event details if necessary.
3. Select the appropriate approval status for your leave.
4. Add any comments or remarks you'd like to share.
5. Review and submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Input** (`User Input`, `text`, optional)
Enter your name or any relevant information about yourself.

### 2. **Date** (`Date`, `date`, optional)
Enter the date of the event you are leaving or have left.

### 3. **Time** (`Time`, `time`, optional)
Enter the time you are leaving or have left the event.

### 4. **Event Info** (`Event Info`, `text`, optional)
Add any additional event information you'd like to provide.

### 5. **Reason** (`Reason`, `text`, optional)
Explain your reason for leaving the event.

### 6. **Approval** (`Approval`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the status of your leave from the options: Approved, Not Approved, or Pending.

### 7. **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional)
Add any additional comments or remarks you'd like to share about your leave.

### 8. **Approval Comments** (`Approval Comments`, `text`, optional)
If your leave is pending, add any comments or remarks you'd like to provide for further review.

### 9. **Status** (`Status`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the status of your leave from the options: Approved, Not Approved, or Pending.

### 10. **Event Details** (`Event Details`, `text`, optional)
Provide any additional event details you'd like to share.

### 11. **Event Id** (`Event Id`, `number`, optional)
Enter the event ID (if applicable).

Tips:
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the event organizer for clarification.
* This form is for official purposes only, so please do not use it for personal or promotional purposes.
