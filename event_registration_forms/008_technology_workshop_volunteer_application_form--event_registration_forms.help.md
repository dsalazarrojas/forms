# Technology Workshop Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in volunteering for a technology workshop event. It is intended to gather general contact information, volunteer details, event information, and other relevant details to ensure that the event organizers have a complete understanding of the volunteer's qualifications and availability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For fields labeled as "Select One", choose one of the options provided.
3. For fields labeled as "Text", enter your answer in the text box.
4. For fields labeled as "Email", enter a valid email address.
5. For fields labeled as "Number", enter a numerical value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter a brief description of yourself and your interests in volunteering for the technology workshop.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter your name and any other contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Volunteer Details** (`3`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter any relevant details about your volunteer experience and qualifications.
* **Event Information** (`4`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter the type of event you are interested in volunteering for and any other relevant information.
* **First Name** (`6`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`7`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`8`, `Email`, optional)
	+ Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`9`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Event Type** (`10`, `Select One`, optional)
	+ Choose the type of event you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Event Location** (`11`, `Select One`, optional)
	+ Choose the location of the event you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Volunteer Role** (`12`, `Select One`, optional)
	+ Choose the role you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`13`, `Number`, optional)
	+ Enter the number of hours you are available to volunteer.
* **Workshop Dates** (`14`, `Date`, optional)
	+ Enter the dates you are available to volunteer for the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`15`, `Time`, optional)
	+ Enter the time you are available to volunteer for the workshop.
* **Volunteer Skills** (`16`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter any relevant skills you have that make you a good fit for the volunteer role.
* **Volunteer Equipment** (`17`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter any equipment you own or have access to that would be useful for the volunteer role.
* **Emergency Contact** (`18`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Enter an emergency contact person's name and phone number.
* **Notes** (`19`, `Note`, optional)
	+ Enter any additional information about your volunteer application.
* **Confirm** (`20`, `Select One`, optional)
	+ Confirm that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Confirm Email** (`21`, `Email`, optional)
	+ Confirm that the email address entered is accurate.
* **Confirm Phone** (`22`, `Text`, optional)
	+ Confirm that the phone number entered is accurate.
* **Confirm Event Type** (`23`, `Select One`, optional)
	+ Confirm the type of event you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Confirm Event Location** (`24`, `Select One`, optional)
	+ Confirm the location of the event you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Confirm Volunteer Role** (`25`, `Select One`, optional)
	+ Confirm the role you are interested in volunteering for.

Note: All fields with an asterisk (*) are required fields.
