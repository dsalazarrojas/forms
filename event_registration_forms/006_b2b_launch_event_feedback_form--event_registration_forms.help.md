# B2B Launch Event Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "B2B Launch Event Feedback Form" is designed to gather feedback from attendees of the B2B Launch Event. Your input is valuable to help us understand your experience and make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your attendee name (if you're willing to share)
2. Enter the name of your company
3. Provide your email address (optional, for follow-up purposes)
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (1-5)
5. Select the favorite session or speaker of the event
6. Evaluate the quality of networking opportunities (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent)
7. Give feedback on the venue and logistics (e.g. accessibility, seating, food)
8. Indicate whether or not you would recommend this event to a colleague
9. Suggest improvements for future events
10. Consent to be contacted for follow-up purposes (you will be contacted to discuss your feedback)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name**: Please enter your name if you're comfortable sharing it. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Company name**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Email address**: Your email address will be used for follow-up purposes. If you're not comfortable sharing it, you can leave it blank.
* **Overall satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "low" and 5 is "high".
* **Favorite session or speaker**: Choose the session or speaker that impressed you the most.
* **Quality of networking opportunities**: Select the rating that best represents your experience with the networking opportunities.
* **Venue and logistics feedback**: Share your feedback on the venue and event logistics (e.g. accessibility, seating, food).
* **Recommend to colleague**: Indicate whether or not you would recommend this event to a colleague.
* **Suggestions for improvement**: Provide your suggestions on how we can improve future events.
* **Consent to be contacted**: Check the box if you consent to be contacted for follow-up purposes.

Note: All fields with a star (*) next to the label are required fields.
