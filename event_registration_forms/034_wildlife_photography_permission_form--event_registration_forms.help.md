# Wildlife Photography Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by wildlife photographers to provide information about their activities, ensure model releases or consents, and facilitate understanding with our team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your organization name if you have one.
2. Enter your personal contact information: name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Select the purpose of your photography (Wildlife Conservation, Wildlife Education, Commercial Use, or Personal Use).
4. Indicate whether you plan to obtain model releases (True, False, or Undecided) and if your organization consents to the photography (True, False).
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your identity and intentions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization you work with, if applicable.
* **Photographer Name**: Enter your personal name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where we can reach you for any questions or follow-ups.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your physical address where we can reach you for any questions or follow-ups.
* **Date Taken**: If you're reporting an existing photo, enter the date the photo was taken. Otherwise, leave this field empty.
* **Time Taken**: If you're reporting an existing photo, enter the time the photo was taken. Otherwise, leave this field empty.
* **Purpose of Photography**: Select one of the following options to indicate the intent of your photography:
	+ **Wildlife Conservation**: For photography aimed at promoting conservation efforts.
	+ **Wildlife Education**: For educational purposes, such as educational programs or documentaries.
	+ **Commercial Use**: For commercial purposes, such as advertising or promotional materials.
	+ **Personal Use**: For personal enjoyment or hobby.
* **Model Release**: Indicate if you plan to obtain model releases for the photographed models. Select 'True' if you're sure, 'False' if you're not, or 'Undecided' if you're unsure.
* **Organization Consent**: Indicate if your organization consents to the photography. Select 'True' if they do, 'False' if they don't, or 'Undecided' if you're unsure.
* **Photographer's Signature**: Please sign your name as the photographer to confirm your identity.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Organization Signature**: If your organization has a signature, please enter it here.
* **Organization Name Check**: This field is for internal verification. Please ignore it.
