# st_patricks_day_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The st_patricks_day_event_registration form is used to register participants for a St. Patrick's Day event. The form collects event details, RSVP information, attendee details, payment info, and additional comments. This information is used to manage event registrations and communicate with participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event details (id: 1).
2. Select your RSVP information (id: 2).
3. Enter attendee details (id: 3).
4. Enter your payment information (id: 4).
5. Add any additional comments (id: 5).
6. Enter your email address (id: 6).
7. Enter your phone number (id: 7).
8. Confirm your email address (id: 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details (id: 1)** (`title`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the event you wish to register for.
* **RSVP Info (id: 2)** (`rsvp_info`, select_one, not required): Select 'True' if you plan to attend the event, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Attendee Details (id: 3)** (`attendee_details`, number, not required): Enter the number of attendees (e.g., how many people are registering for the event).
* **Payment Info (id: 4)** (`payment_info`, number, not required): Enter your payment information, if applicable.
* **Additional Comments (id: 5)** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or messages you wish to include.
* **Email (id: 6)** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (id: 7)** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email (id: 8)** (`confirm_email`, email, not required): Re-enter your email address to confirm.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper event management.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact the event organiser for assistance.
