# Chromatography Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chromatography Event Registration form is a tool used to collect information from attendees who wish to register for a chromatography-related event. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to complete your event registration.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections. Please follow these steps to complete your registration:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Choose your preferred sessions from the options provided in the "Session Preferences" section.
3. If you have dietary restrictions or other accessibility needs, please indicate them in the "Dietary Restrictions" and "Accessibility Requirements" fields.
4. Share any additional comments or questions you may have in the "Additional Comments or Questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendee Information (Page 1)
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Affiliation** (text, required): Enter the name of your affiliation (University, Company, Organization).
* **Job Title or Academic Position** (text, optional): Enter your job title or academic position.

### Event Details (Page 2)
* **Event Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event you wish to attend.

### Session Preferences (Page 3)
* **Attendee Type** (select_one, required): Choose your type (Scientist/Researcher, Lab Technician, Graduate Student, Undergraduate Student, Industry Professional, or Other).
* **Research Area or Field** (select_one, required): Choose your research area or field (Analytical Chemistry, Biochemistry, Environmental Science, Pharmaceutical, Food Science, Other Chemistry, Non-Chemistry).
* **Preferred Sessions** (select_multiple, required): Choose all the sessions that interest you from the options provided.
* **Interested in Hands-on Workshop?** (select_one, required): Choose whether you are interested in the hands-on workshop.
* **Chromatography Experience Level** (select_one, required): Choose your experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert).

### Special Accommodations (Page 4)
* **Dietary Restrictions** (text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (text, optional): If you have any accessibility requirements, enter them here.

### Additional Information (Page 5)
* **Specific Research or Learning Interests** (text, optional): Share any specific research or learning interests you have.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (text, optional): Share any additional comments or questions you have.
