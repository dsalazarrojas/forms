# Car Show Booth Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Show Booth Reservation Form is designed to collect information from exhibitors to reserve a booth at the car show. This form helps us understand your needs and requirements for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (name, email, phone number, and company name if applicable).
2. Choose your preferred booth size and location.
3. Specify the number of vehicles you plan to display.
4. Enter your vehicle details for display.
5. Provide payment information (booth fee, additional fees, total estimated cost, payment method, and insurance certificate provision).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor Name** (`exhibitor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the exhibitor (Individual or business name).
* **Email Address** (`exhibitor_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the exhibitor.
* **Phone Number** (`exhibitor_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the exhibitor.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the company name of the exhibitor.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of business (Dealer, Collector, Mechanic/Shop, Car Club, Vendor, Manufacturer, Media, or Other).
* **Booth Size Preference** (`booth_size_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose the booth size preference (10x10 feet, 10x20 feet, 20x20 feet, Corner Booth, Premium Location, Custom Size).
* **Booth Location Preference** (`booth_location_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the location preference (Front Row, Middle Area, Back Area, Corner, Near Entrance, Near Concessions, No Preference).
* **Number of Vehicles to Display** (`vehicles_to_display`, `number`, required): Enter the number of vehicles to display.
* **Vehicle Details for Display** (`vehicle_details`, `text`, optional): Enter the year, make, and model of each vehicle for display.
* **Booth Fee** (`booth_fee`, `number`, required): Enter the booth fee.
* **Additional Fees** (`additional_fees`, `number`, optional): Enter any additional fees (power, setup, etc.).
* **Total Estimated Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the total estimated cost.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the payment method (Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, Online Payment, Cash on Site).
* **Insurance Certificate Provided?** (`insurance_certificate`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether an insurance certificate is provided (True, False, Not Required).
* **Additional Requests or Comments** (`additional_requests`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional requests or comments.

## Tips

* Double-check your contact information to ensure it's accurate and complete.
* Be clear and specific when providing vehicle details for display.
* Enter payment information carefully, as it affects the total cost.
* If you have any additional requests or comments, please enter them in the "Additional Requests or Comments" field.
