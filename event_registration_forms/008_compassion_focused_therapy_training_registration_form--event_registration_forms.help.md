# Compassion Focused Therapy Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in registering for Compassion Focused Therapy (CFT) training programs. It gathers essential details to ensure proper registration, accommodation, and planning for the training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the training program you wish to register for from the options provided.
2. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Professional Title, Organization, and Email Address in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose your Country of residence from the list.
4. Select your Primary Profession from the options.
5. Specify your Years of Clinical Experience and Previous CFT Experience.
6. Select your preferred Training Date and Format (in-person, live online, or recorded sessions).
7. If applicable, mention any Dietary Requirements and Accessibility Needs.
8. Describe your learning goals for the training.
9. Indicate your interest in CFT supervision and certification.
10. Choose your preferred Payment Method and specify if you need a payment plan.
11. Confirm if you are registering before the early bird deadline for a discount.
12. If multiple people are registering together, select the correct group size.
13. Let us know how you heard about the training.
14. Finally, confirm that you have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions, including the Cancellation Policy, and Data Privacy Policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Program Name**: Select the name of the training program you wish to register for from the provided options. This ensures you receive the correct materials and instructions for the selected program.
* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name for registration purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name for registration purposes.
* **Professional Title**: Describe your current job title for reference purposes during the training.
* **Organization or Practice**: State your workplace or practice to provide context for the training sessions.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your best contact number for scheduling and other communication.
* **Country**: Select the country you reside in from the provided options.
* **Primary Profession**: Select the primary profession that aligns with your work or study from the options.
* **Years of Clinical Experience**: Indicate approximately how many years you have been in clinical practice.
* **Previous CFT Experience**: Select your level of CFT training experience from the provided options.
* **Preferred Training Date**: Choose a training date that fits your schedule from the available sessions.
* **Training Format Preference**: Select the format in which you prefer to attend the training (in-person, live online, or recorded sessions).
* **Dietary Requirements**: If you have dietary restrictions, specify them here.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you require any specific accommodations, mention them here.
* **Learning Goals**: Describe what you hope to achieve or gain from this training.
* **Supervision Interest**: Indicate your interest in ongoing CFT supervision, if applicable.
* **Certification Interest**: Confirm if you plan to pursue CFT certification.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you plan to pay for the training (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Plan**: If needed, select a payment option for installment.
* **Early Bird**: Confirm if you are registering before the early bird deadline for a possible discount.
* **Group Registration**: If registering with others, specify the correct group size.
* **How Heard About**: Provide the source of your information about this training.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm you have read and agree to the training's Terms and Conditions, including the Cancellation Policy.
* **Data Privacy Policy**: Confirm you have read and agree to the Data Privacy Policy for your information protection.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: If anything else you would like to share with us, add it here.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and comprehensive, covering all aspects of the training registration process.
