# Estate Sale Early Access Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow attendees to register for an estate sale's early entry appointments. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that you receive the correct early entry information and access to the sale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please select the date and time of your preferred early entry appointment from the available options.
2.  If you wish to register for multiple appointments, please separate each date and time with a comma.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Early Entry Appointments** (`early_entry_appointments`, `time`, required: false): Select the date and time of your preferred early entry appointment.
* **Attendee Capacity** (`attendee_capacity`, `number`, required: false): If you are registering for more than one person, please enter the number of attendees you plan to bring.
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, `note`, required: false): Please provide any additional information about your registration or any special requests.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate information to ensure that we can process your registration efficiently.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you separately with the early entry appointment details once we have processed your registration.
