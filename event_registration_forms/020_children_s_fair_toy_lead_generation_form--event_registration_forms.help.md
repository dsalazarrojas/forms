# Children's Fair Toy Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents and guardians about their interests and shopping behavior to help the Children's Fair better serve their needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide information about your family, including the number and ages of your children.
3. Indicate which toy categories and brands your child(ren) is interested in.
4. Answer a few questions about your shopping behavior and preferred contact methods.
5. Let us know if you are interested in receiving promotions and offers.
6. Add any additional comments about the fair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the parent or guardian filling out this form.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address. We will use this to send updates and offers.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code for location-based offers.
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, `number`, required): Enter the number of children in your family.
* **Age of Youngest Child** (`youngest_age`, `number`, required): Enter the age of the youngest child.
* **Age of Oldest Child** (`oldest_age`, `number`, required): Enter the age of the oldest child.
* **Favorite Toy Categories** (`favorite_categories`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the toy categories your child(ren) is interested in (e.g. Action figures, Building sets, etc.).
* **Favorite Toy Brands** (`brand_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the toy brands your child(ren) is interested in (e.g. LEGO, Barbie, etc.).
* **Shopping Behavior** (`shopping_behavior`, `note`, optional): This section is for any additional information about your shopping behavior.
* **Average Spending on Toys per Month** (`average_spending`, `select_one`, optional): Select the average amount you spend on toys per month.
* **How Often Do You Buy Toys** (`purchase_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you buy toys.
* **Preferred Shopping Channels** (`shopping_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the shopping channels you prefer (e.g. in-person stores, online shopping, etc.).
* **First Time Visitor** (`first_time_visitor`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if this is your first time visiting the fair.
* **Most Interested in** (`what_interested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the things you are most interested in at the fair (e.g. Product demonstrations, Special discounts, etc.).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred contact method (e.g. Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Newsletter Subscription** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you would like to subscribe to our newsletter.
* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the social media platforms you use.
* **Interested in Promotions** (`promotional_offers`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you are interested in special offers and promotions.
* **Additional Comments** (`fair_feedback`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about the fair.
