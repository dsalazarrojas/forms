# Arts And Craft Fair Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arts and Craft Fair Application Form is used for vendors to register and provide necessary information for the fair. This form collects vendor details, such as name, contact information, and product category. It also includes sections for booth size requirements, power needs, and special requests. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your business or stage name.
3. Provide your contact email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your business address.
6. Enter the city where your business is located.
7. Enter the state or province of your business.
8. Enter your zip or postal code.
9. Select the product category that best describes your products.
10. Describe your products in the product description section.
11. If applicable, enter the number of years you have been in business.
12. Choose the booth size needed for your products and equipment.
13. Select whether electricity or power outlets are needed for your booth.
14. If required, enter any special requirements or setup needs.
15. Select the fair date you are applying for.
16. Enter the booth fee amount, if applicable.
17. Check if you have exhibited before.
18. Check if you have liability insurance attached.
19. Finally, agree to the vendor terms and conditions.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your personal last name.
* **Business or Stage Name**: Enter the name of your business or performance stage.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
* **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province of your business.
* **Zip or Postal Code**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Product Category**: Select the category that best describes your products.
* **Product Description**: Describe your products and what they represent.
* **Years in Business**: If applicable, enter the number of years you have been in business.
* **Booth Size Needed**: Choose the booth size that fits your needs and products.
* **Electricity or Power Outlet Needed**: Select whether you need electricity or power outlets.
* **Special Requirements or Setup Needs**: If needed, enter any special requirements or setup needs.
* **Fair Date You Are Applying For**: Select the fair date you are applying for.
* **Booth Fee Amount**: Enter the booth fee amount, if applicable.
* **Have You Exhibited Before**: Check if you have exhibited before.
* **Liability Insurance Certificate Attached**: Check if you have liability insurance attached.
* **I Agree to Vendor Terms and Conditions**: Check to agree to the vendor terms and conditions.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about your business.
## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure a smooth registration process.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions, contact us for assistance.
