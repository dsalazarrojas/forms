# Health Justice Advocacy Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a Health Justice Advocacy Roundtable. The collected data will help us understand the audience's demographics and interests, which will inform the event planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select your contact preferences from the "Contact Preferences" field (if applicable).
4. Identify any accessibility needs you may have in the "Accessibility Needs" field (this is a required field).
5. Choose your interests from the "Interests" field (if applicable).
6. Optionally, you can submit any additional information in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, mail).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): If you have any accessibility needs or requirements, please select them (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, sign language interpreter).
* **Interests** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any topics or areas of interest related to health justice advocacy.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): You can use this field to submit any additional information or notes about your registration.
