# Industrial Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for industrial safety training sessions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information. 
2. Ensure that you provide a unique email address for the participant. 
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Training Info (Page 1)
* **Training Info** (`training_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering general information about the training session, such as the topic or purpose of the training.

### Participant Info (Page 2)
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering information about the participant, including their name, department, or ID number.

### Safety Info (Page 3)
* **Safety Info** (`safety_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering any specific safety information about the participant, such as their previous experience or any relevant certifications.

### Training Date (Page 4)
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required/optional): This field is for selecting the date of the training session. The format is MM/DD/YYYY.

### Training Time (Page 5)
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required/optional): This field is for selecting the time of the training session. The format is HH:MM AM/PM.

### Trainer Info (Page 6)
* **Trainer Info** (`trainer_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering information about the trainer, such as their name or ID number.

### Training Status (Page 7)
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to select whether the participant has completed the training or not. Options are Active or Inactive.

### Participant Status (Page 8)
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to select whether the participant is Active or Inactive.

### Training Hours (Page 9)
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, required/optional): This field is for entering the total hours of the training session.

### Participant Hours (Page 10)
* **Participant Hours** (`participant_hours`, number, required/optional): This field is for entering the total hours the participant has completed.

### Participant Location (Page 11)
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the participant's location, such as their department or work site.

### Participant Notes (Page 12)
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required/optional): This field is for entering any additional notes or comments about the participant.

### Participant Email (Page 13)
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): This field is for entering the participant's email address. Ensure that it is unique and accurate.
