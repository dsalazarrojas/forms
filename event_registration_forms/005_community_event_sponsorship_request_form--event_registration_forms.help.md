# Community Event Sponsorship Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for community event organizers to request sponsorship from the organization. It helps us understand your event details and tailor our support according to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your event information.
2. Choose your preferred sponsorship level and any specific financial contribution.
3. Describe any in-kind contributions, if applicable.
4. Specify your desired marketing benefits.
5. Provide your billing address for invoicing purposes.
6. Confirm agreement to the sponsorship terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name of the Event**: Provide the specific event title you are requesting sponsorship for. This will help us understand the event's details and ensure we support the correct event.
* **Company or Individual Name**: Enter the name as it should appear in our marketing materials. This will help us represent your brand correctly.
* **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your event. This will ensure we communicate with the right person.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication and invoicing purposes. This should be a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication and follow-up. This should be a valid phone number.
* **Desired Sponsorship Level**: Select one of our preset packages: Platinum ($5,000+), Gold ($2,500), Silver ($1,000), Bronze ($500), or Community Partner (In-kind).
* **Specific Financial Contribution**: If known, enter the exact amount you are requesting. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Description of In-Kind Contribution**: Describe any goods or services you are offering as in-kind contributions. This helps us understand the value of your contribution.
* **Preferred Marketing Benefits**: Select the marketing benefits that are most important to your brand. You can choose multiple benefits.
* **Company Logo Status**: Indicate the status of your company logo: Sent via email, Uploaded to portal, or Needs to be designed.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address for invoicing purposes. This should be a valid address.
* **I agree to the Sponsorship Terms and Conditions**: Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions for the sponsorship.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
- Be specific about any in-kind contributions and desired marketing benefits.
- Confirm your logo status to avoid delays in processing your sponsorship request.
