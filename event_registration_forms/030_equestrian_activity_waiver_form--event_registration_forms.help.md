# Equestrian Activity Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equestrian Activity Waiver Form is designed to gather information from participants and their guardians for equestrian activities. This form ensures that participants, especially children, are aware of and understand the potential risks involved in equestrian activities such as riding horses, and that their guardians grant permission for them to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Participant Information** page with your details.
2. Proceed to the **Guardian Information** page and enter the guardian's details.
3. On the **Waiver Text** page, provide a description of the activities your child will participate in.
4. Finally, sign and date the **Parent Signature** page to confirm that you grant permission for your child to participate.

* **Participant Information (participant_info)**: Enter your name and other relevant details as required.

### Guardian Information (guardian_info)
* **Guardian Information (guardian_info)**: Enter your name and other relevant details as required.

### Waiver Text (waiver_text)
* **Waiver Text (waiver_text)**: Please describe in detail the activities your child will be participating in.

### Parent Signature (parent_signature)
* **Parent Signature (parent_signature)**: Sign and date this page to confirm that you grant permission for your child to participate in the equestrian activity.
