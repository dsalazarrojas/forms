# Wedding Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the accommodation requests of the guests attending the wedding event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields (arrival_date, departure_date, number_of_guests, accommodation_request, event_details).
2.  If you have any special requests (such as extra amenities or services), please include them in the "accommodation_request" field.
3.  For each accommodation type (hotel, vacation rental, etc.), select the types that apply to your accommodation needs.
4.  If you have selected "other" as your accommodation type, please provide details in the "accommodation_type_other_text" field.
5.  Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **arrival_date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): This field is where you enter the date you plan to arrive at the wedding event.
* **departure_date** (`departure_date`, date, required): This field is where you enter the date you plan to leave after the wedding event.
* **number_of_guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): This field is where you enter the number of guests in your party.
* **accommodation_request** (`accommodation_request`, text, required): This field is where you describe your accommodation preferences or any special requests.
* **event_details** (`event_details`, text, required): This field is where you provide details about the event you are attending.
* **accommodation_type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required): Please select one of the accommodation types (hotel, vacation rental, etc.) that apply to your needs. If "other" is selected, please describe your accommodation type in the "accommodation_type_other_text" field.
* **accommodation_type_other_text** (`accommodation_type_other_text`, text, optional): If you have selected "other" as your accommodation type, please provide details about your accommodation type here.
* **special_requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): This field is where you can make any additional requests or comments about your accommodation needs.
* **accommodation_notes** (`accommodation_notes`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional notes or comments about your accommodation preferences.
* **accommodation_type_1** (`accommodation_type_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select the "hotel" accommodation type if it applies to your needs.
* **accommodation_type_2** (`accommodation_type_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the "vacation rental" accommodation type if it applies to your needs.
* **accommodation_type_3** (`accommodation_type_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select the "bed and breakfast" accommodation type if it applies to your needs.
* **accommodation_type_4** (`accommodation_type_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select the "house sitting" accommodation type if it applies to your needs.
* **accommodation_type_other** (`accommodation_type_other`, select_multiple, optional): Select "other" if your accommodation type does not fit any of the options listed.
* **accommodation_type_other_text** (`accommodation_type_other_text`, text, optional): If you have selected "other" as your accommodation type, please describe your accommodation type here.
