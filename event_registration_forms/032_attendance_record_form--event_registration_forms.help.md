# Attendance Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Attendance Record Form. It is a structured form with several sections, each designed to collect specific information about an event or class.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Enter the **Event Name**, **Event Date**, and **Event Time** in their respective fields.
3. Select the **Instructor/Organizer** who conducted the event.
4. Fill in your **Name**, **Employee/Student ID**, and **Department/Class** you're registered for.
5. Enter your **Email Address** (if applicable).
6. Mark your **Attendance Status** as **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, **Left Early**, or **Excused Absence**.
7. If you were **Absent**, enter the **Reason for Absence**.
8. If your attendance status is **Absent**, select whether your **Absence Approval** is **Approved**, **Pending Approval**, or **Unapproved**.
9. Confirm your **Attendee Signature** by selecting **Confirmed** or **Not Confirmed**.
10. Enter the date of your **Signature Confirmation** and the **Record Keeper Name** (if not completing the form yourself).
11. Add any **Additional Comments** (if necessary).
12. Finally, select whether **Follow-Up Action Required** is **Yes** or **No**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the event or class you attended.
* **Event Date (id: 3)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time (id: 4)**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Instructor/Organizer (id: 5)**: Enter the person conducting the event.
* **Name (id: 7)**: Enter your full name.
* **Employee/Student ID (id: 8)**: Enter your unique identification number.
* **Department/Class (id: 9)**: Enter the department or class you registered for.
* **Email Address (id: 10)**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Attendance Status (id: 12)**: Select your attendance status.
* **Present/Absent (id: 12)**: Mark your attendance status as **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, **Left Early**, or **Excused Absence**.
* **Check-In Time (id: 13)**: Enter the time of your arrival.
* **Check-Out Time (id: 14)**: Enter the time of your departure.
* **Hours Attended (id: 15)**: Enter the total hours you attended.
* **Reason for Absence (id: 16)**: Enter the reason for your absence (if applicable).
* **Absence Approval (id: 17)**: Select whether your absence was approved, pending approval, or unapproved.
* **Attendee Signature (id: 19)**: Confirm your attendance by selecting **Confirmed** or **Not Confirmed**.
* **Signature Date (id: 20)**: Enter the date of your signature confirmation.
* **Record Keeper Name (id: 21)**: Enter the name of the person recording the attendance (if not completing the form yourself).
* **Special Circumstances (id: 23)**: Enter any special notes or circumstances.
* **Follow-Up Action Required (id: 24)**: Select whether follow-up action is required.
* **Additional Comments (id: 25)**: Enter any additional comments or information.
