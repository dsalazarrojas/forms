# Performance Arts Club Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help Performance Arts Club members renew their membership. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth membership renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are a valid Performance Arts Club member or have a legitimate reason to renew your membership.
2. Fill in your Member Name in the "Member Name" field. This is the name of the member who is renewing their membership.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field. This will be used as a contact point for membership updates and notifications.
4. If you are paying for membership renewal, select your Payment Method (Stripe Payment, Cash, or Credit Card) and enter the Payment Amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
5. If you have already made a payment, select the corresponding Payment Status (Paid, Partial, or Unpaid) for your payment.
6. Fill in the Event Name, Event Date, and Event Start and End Time fields with the details of the event where you will perform.
7. If you wish, enter the Renewal Reason for your membership renewal.
8. Review and submit your form carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation (e.g., driver's license).
*   **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for membership updates and notifications.
*   **Payment Amount**: If paying, enter the amount you are paying for your membership renewal. If not paying, leave this field blank.
*   **Payment Method**: Choose how you are paying for your membership renewal (Stripe Payment, Cash, or Credit Card).
*   **Payment Status**: If you have already made a payment, select the status (Paid, Partial, or Unpaid) of your payment.
*   **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event where you will perform.
*   **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event where you will perform.
*   **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event where you will perform.
*   **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event where you will perform.
*   **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment for the event (Paid, Partial, or Unpaid).
*   **Renewal Reason**: If you wish, enter the reason for your membership renewal.
