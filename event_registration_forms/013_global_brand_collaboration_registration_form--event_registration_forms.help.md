## Global Brand Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
### Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your company's and employee's collaboration preferences, role, and event participation in our global brand collaboration initiative. The form is meant to be completed by brand representatives who want to participate in our brand collaboration events.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Select all relevant collaboration opportunities that interest you.
2. Choose your partnership preferences.
3. Select the type of event you would like to participate in.
4. Enter your contact information, company details, and social media links (if applicable).
5. Provide a brief description of your company, job title, and job description (if applicable).
6. Fill in your company's address and job URL (if applicable).
7. Select the type of job you are representing.
8. Fill in your job title, job description, and job URL (if applicable).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand** (`brand`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your company name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your company contact information.
* **Collaboration Opportunities** (`collaboration_opportunities`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select all relevant collaboration opportunities that interest you.
* **Partnership Preferences** (`partnership_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Choose your partnership preferences.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select the type of event you would like to participate in.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): 
  Enter the date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): 
  Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): 
  Enter the end time of the event.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the location of the event.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): 
  Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your role in the company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your job title.
* **Bio** (`bio`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your job title.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your company URL.
* **Linkedin URL** (`linkedin_url`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your company's linkedin URL.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your company address.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select the type of job you are representing.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Job URL** (`job_url`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your job URL.

Note: All fields are optional except for your email address. Please fill in all the relevant fields as accurately as possible to ensure that we can process your collaboration request smoothly.
