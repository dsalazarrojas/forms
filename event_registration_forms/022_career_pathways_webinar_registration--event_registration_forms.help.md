# Career Pathways Webinar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a Career Pathways webinar. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the registrant, such as their name, email address, and contact preferences. This information will be used to provide a smooth and personalized experience during the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the webinar title you are registering for.
2. Enter your date and time preference for the webinar.
3. Fill in your full name and email address.
4. Optionally, provide your phone number, company name, job title, industry, registrant type, years of experience, career focus, learning goals, current challenges, and other career topics of interest.
5. Select your preferred method of notification (email, phone call, text message, or calendar invitation).
6. If you have accessibility needs, select the accommodations you require.
7. Finally, indicate how you heard about this webinar and any additional interests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Webinar Title** (`webinar_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the webinar you are registering for.
- **Webinar Date and Time** (`webinar_date`, date, required): Select the date and time you wish to attend the webinar.
- **Your Full Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for registration purposes.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address where you would like to receive webinar details and updates.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, include it here for future contact.
- **Organization or Company Name** (`organization_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name, if applicable.
- **Job Title or Role** (`job_title_role`, text, optional): Enter your job title or role for context.
- **Industry or Field** (`industry_field`, text, optional): Select the industry or field you are interested in.
- **Type of Registrant** (`registrant_type`, select_one, required): Select your registrant category (Student, Job Seeker, Early Career Professional, etc.).
- **Years of Professional Experience** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select the number of years of professional experience you have.
- **Career Focus or Industry Interest** (`career_focus`, text, optional): Enter areas of career interest or industry focus.
- **What Topics Interest You Most** (`webinar_topics_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select up to 2 topics that interest you most.
- **What Are Your Learning Goals?** (`learning_goals`, text, required): Enter your learning goals for the webinar.
- **Current Career Challenges** (`current_challenges`, text, optional): Describe any career challenges you are currently facing.
- **Can You Attend Live?** (`attendance_expectation`, select_one, required): Select whether you will attend the webinar live or not.
- **Do You Have Reliable Internet and Video Capability?** (`technology_access`, select_one, optional): Select your level of internet and video capability.
- **Preferred Notification Method** (`notification_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to 2 preferred methods of notification.
- **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, optional): If you have accessibility needs, select the accommodations you require.
- **Specific Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_details`, text, optional): Describe any specific accessibility requirements you have.
- **How Did You Hear About This Webinar?** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about this webinar.
- **Other Career Topics You Are Interested In** (`additional_interests`, text, optional): Enter any other career topics you are interested in for future webinars.
- **I Consent to Receive Career-Related Information** (`contact_consent`, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to receive career-related information.
