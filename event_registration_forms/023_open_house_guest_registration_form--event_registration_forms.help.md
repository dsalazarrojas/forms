# Open House Guest Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from guests attending an open house event. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Double-check that you have entered the correct event information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the agent hosting the event.
* **Agent Number** (`agent_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number for the agent hosting the event.
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the event, if different from the provided options.

# Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of the event information to avoid any issues with event organization.
- Use a valid email address and phone number for communication purposes.
