# Global Language Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Language Conference Registration form is a tool for attendees to provide their information and register for a conference. This form helps the event organizers collect necessary data for conference management and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Conference Name in the first field.
2. Provide your Attendee Name in the second field.
3. Enter your Email address in the third field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the fourth field.
5. Select your preferred Time for the event from the fifth field.
6. Choose a Date for the event from the sixth field.
7. Add any additional Notes in the seventh field.
8. Choose the type of event: In-person or Virtual from the eighth field.
9. Select one or more languages you are proficient in from the ninth field.
10. If you speak a language not listed, type it in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Name**: Enter the name of the conference you are registering for.
* **Attendee Name**: Type in your full name as an attendee.
* **Email**: Enter your Email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your Phone number where we can reach you.
* **Time**: Select the Time of the event in the format: 12:00 (or 12:30, 01:00, etc.).
* **Date**: Choose a Date for the event using the format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2024-03-16).
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or information about your attendance.
* **Event Type**: Select whether the event is In-person or Virtual.
* **Language**: Choose one or more languages you are proficient in.
* **Other**: If none of the listed languages fit your profile, type it here.
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter your Email address for confirmation.
* **Confirm Phone**: Re-enter your Phone number for confirmation.
