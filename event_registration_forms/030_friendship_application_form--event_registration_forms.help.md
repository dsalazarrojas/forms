# Friendship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Friendship Application Form is designed to gather information about potential friendships between individuals. This form helps us understand your preferences, interests, and relationship status to match you with like-minded individuals. Please complete this form to express your interest in forming a connection with others.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** on the first page.
2. Enter your **Birthday** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. In the **Interests** field, list up to 10 hobbies or activities you enjoy.
4. If you have any **Hobbies**, list them in the **Hobbies** field.
5. Select your favorite food from the dropdown menu in the **Favorite Food** field.
6. Choose all that apply in the **Favorite Color** field to select multiple colors.
7. Select one **Favorite Hobby** from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose the **Friend Group** you'd like to join.
9. Select your **Friend Status**, including Single, In a Relationship, or In a Relationship and Looking.
10. Choose all that apply in the **Friend Type** field to select multiple genders.
11. Select all that apply in the **Friend Level** field to select your level of expertise.
12. Choose whether you **Friendship Interests** are yes or no.
13. Select your **Friendship Status** as Active, Passive, or Casual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Birthday**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Interests**: List up to 10 hobbies or activities you enjoy, separated by commas.
* **Hobbies**: If you have any unique or specific hobbies not listed in the **Interests** field, list them here.
* **Favorite Food**: Select the type of food you enjoy the most.
* **Favorite Color**: Choose all colors you enjoy, separated by commas.
* **Favorite Hobby**: Select the hobby that is your favorite.
* **Friend Group**: Select the group you'd like to join.
* **Friend Status**: Select your current relationship status.
* **Friend Type**: Choose all that apply in terms of gender.
* **Friend Level**: Select the level of expertise you have in the selected **Favorite Hobby**.
* **Friendship Interests**: Indicate if you have an interest in meeting new friends.
* **Friendship Status**: Indicate the status of your current friendships.
* **Favorite Animal**: Choose all animals you enjoy, separated by commas.
* **Favorite Mood**: Select the mood you are in most of the time.

Please note that some fields are required and must be completed to submit the form. If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
