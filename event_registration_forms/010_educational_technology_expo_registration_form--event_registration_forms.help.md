# Educational Technology Expo Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Technology Expo Registration Form is designed to collect relevant information from attendees and participants of the event. This information will be used to prepare for the event and provide the best possible experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: 
   * **Attendee Information**: Provide a brief description of yourself and your role at the registering organization.
   * **Contact Person**: Enter your name and title, as well as the best phone number and email to reach you.
   * **Email**: Enter your work or personal email address.
   * **Phone Number**: Enter your work or personal phone number.
   * **Company**: Enter the name of your company or institution.
   * **Department**: Enter the name of your department or team.
2. You may leave the optional fields blank. 
   * **Email**: This field is used for internal contact and communication. If you don't want to be contacted, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Information**: This field is required and should describe your role at the registering organization and any relevant information about your position.
* **Contact Person**: This field is used to identify you as the main contact person for this registration. Enter your name and title.
* **email**: This field is required and should be a valid email address.
* **phone_number**: This field is required and should be a valid phone number.
* **Company**: This field is required and should be the name of your company or institution.
* **Department**: This field is required and should be the name of your department or team.
*   **Optional:**

  *   **Phone Number**: If you do not want to be contacted, you can leave this field blank.
  *   **Email**: This field is optional and can be left blank if you don't want to be contacted.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* The information collected from this form will be used to communicate with you before, during, and after the event.
