# Designer Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for designers to enter their information, which will be used to create a unique designer entry. The form is used to capture the designer's last name, first name, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your last name in the "Designer Last Name" field.
2. Enter your first name in the "Designer First Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Designer Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Designer Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Designer Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Designer First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Designer Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Designer Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) to ensure that your designer entry is complete and accurate.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, contact your administrator for assistance.
