# Architect Of The Year Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for nominating an architect for the "Architect Of The Year" award. It allows you to provide information about the nominee, their achievements, and the reasons for the nomination. Please fill out this form to submit your nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (sections 2-4).
2. Enter the nominee's information (sections 5-10).
3. Describe the nominee's achievements and qualifications (sections 14-16).
4. Optional: Add any supporting materials or evidence of the nominee's work (section 20).
5. Finally, confirm the nomination date (section 21).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (2, required): Enter your full name as the person nominating the architect.
* **Your Email Address** (3, required): Enter your email address, where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Your Phone Number** (4, required): Enter your phone number, so we can contact you for additional questions.
* **Your Organization** (5, optional): If you are nominating the architect from your organization, enter its name here.
* **Your Relationship to Nominee** (6, required): Choose how you know the nominee (Colleague, Client, Student, Professional contact, or Other).
* **Architect Name (Nominee)** (8, required): Enter the full name of the architect being nominated.
* **Nominee Email Address** (9, required): Enter the architect's email address, where they can be reached for further information.
* **Firm or Organization** (10, required): Enter the name of the architect's firm or organization.
* **Position or Title** (11, required): Enter the architect's position or title.
* **Years in Architectural Practice** (12, optional): Enter the number of years the architect has been in practice, if applicable.
* **Why Are You Nominating This Architect?** (14, required): Describe the architect's achievements and impact on the field.
* **Signature Projects or Achievements** (15, required): List major works or accomplishments that demonstrate the architect's excellence.
* **Community or Industry Impact** (16, optional): Describe how the architect has contributed to the field, community, or industry.
* **Leadership and Mentorship** (17, optional): Provide examples of leadership or mentoring roles the architect has taken on.
* **Innovation or Design Excellence** (18, optional): Describe any innovative approaches or designs the architect has implemented.
* **Supporting Materials Available** (20, optional): Check if supporting materials such as a portfolio, references, or awards are available for the nominee.
* **Date of Nomination** (21, required): Enter the date you are nominating the architect for the award.
