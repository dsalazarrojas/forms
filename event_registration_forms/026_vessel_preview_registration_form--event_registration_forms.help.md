# vessel_preview_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **vessel_preview_registration_form** is designed to collect information from participants for events. This form helps us understand the event schedule, location, and participant details.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** in the text field provided. This should be a brief title for the event.
2. Select the **Event Date** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Choose the **Event Time** in the format of HH:MM (AM/PM).
4. Select the **Time Zone** from the available options.
5. Enter the **Event Location** where the event will take place.
6. Enter the **Event Address** of the location.
7. Add any **Event Notes** you'd like to provide.
8. Provide your **Participant Name**.
9. Enter your **Participant E-mail** address.
10. Enter your **Participant Phone** number.
11. Enter your **Participant Phone Extension** if applicable.
12. Add any **Participant Notes** you'd like to provide.
13. Select the **Assigned Tool** from the available options.
14. Select all agreements you're willing to make regarding participation and sharing.
15. Select all agreements you're willing to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (2, date, required): Enter the event date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (3, time, required): Enter the event time in the format HH:MM (AM/PM).
* **Time Zone** (4, select_one, required): Select the time zone for the event.
* **Event Location** (5, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Address** (6, text, required): Enter the address of the location.
* **Event Notes** (7, note, required): Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Participant Name** (8, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Participant E-mail** (9, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (10, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Phone Extension** (11, text, required): Enter your phone extension.
* **Participant Notes** (12, note, required): Add any additional notes about yourself.
* **Assigned Tool** (13, select_one, required): Select the tool you've been assigned.
* **Participant Agrees** (14, select_multiple, required): Select all agreements you're willing to make.
* **Participant Agrees to share** (15, select_multiple, required): Select all agreements you're willing to share.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Select only the agreements you're willing to make.
* Enter relevant details for the event and yourself to ensure accurate information.
