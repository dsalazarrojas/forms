# Virtual Event Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Event Service Order Form is used to collect information for virtual event services, such as webinars, workshops, or conferences. This form is intended for internal use and is not for general public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Event Name** field with the name of the virtual event you are requesting.
2. Enter the **Event Date** when the virtual event is scheduled to take place.
3. Fill in the **Event Time** field with the scheduled start time of the virtual event.
4. Select the services you are requesting in the **Services** field. Please ensure you select all the services you need for your event.
5. Choose the desired **Service Order** in the dropdown menu.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes about your order, you can enter them in the **Comments** field.
7. Enter your **Email** address in the designated field, which will be used for communication purposes.
8. Enter your **Contact Name** in the **Contact Name** field.
9. Enter your **Contact Email** in the **Contact Email** field.
10. Enter your **Contact Phone** number in the **Contact Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name (1)**: Enter the name of the virtual event you are requesting.
* **Event Date (2)**: Enter the date of the virtual event.
* **Event Time (3)**: Enter the start time of the virtual event.
* **Order Name (4)**: Enter a unique name for your order.
* **Order Date (5)**: Enter the date you are requesting the service.
* **Order Time (6)**: Enter the start time you are requesting the service.
* **Services (7)**: Select all the services you require for your virtual event. Please ensure you select all the necessary services.
* **Comments (8)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Service Order (9)**: Choose the desired service order from the dropdown menu.
* **Email (10)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (11)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Contact Name (12)**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Email (13)**: Enter your contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone (14)**: Enter your contact person's phone number.
* **Event Type (15)**: Choose the type of virtual event you are requesting (e.g. webinar, conference, workshop).
* **Event Description (16)**: Enter a brief description of the virtual event.
* **Notes (17)**: Add any additional notes about your event.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing this form.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
