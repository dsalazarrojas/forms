# Leadership Summit Individual Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individual participants to register for the Leadership Summit. Each participant is expected to complete the form accurately and truthfully, providing their own contact and personal information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out this form completely and accurately**. Each field is optional, but providing complete information will help us serve you better.
2. **Enter your email address**. This will be used to send you notifications and updates about the summit.
3. **Enter your name**. Please use your full name or the name you prefer to be addressed by.
4. **Select your role**. Choose the role that best describes your position or function within your organization.
5. **Select your participant type**. Choose the type that best fits your participation or involvement in the summit.
6. **Enter your company**. If you are affiliated with an organization, enter its name.
7. **Enter your job title**. This will help us understand your position within your organization.
8. **Write a brief bio**. This will help us introduce you and your organization to other participants.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address that we can use to send you updates and notifications about the summit.
* **name** (name, optional): Enter your full name or the name you prefer to be addressed by.
* **role** (role, optional): Choose the role that best describes your position or function within your organization. (e.g., CEO, Manager, Team Member)
* **participant_type** (Participant Type, optional): Choose the type that best fits your participation or involvement in the summit. (e.g., Speaker, Participant, Sponsor)
* **company** (company, optional): Enter the name of your affiliated organization.
* **job_title** (Job Title, optional): Enter your position within your organization.
* **bio** (Bio, optional): Write a brief description of yourself and your organization.
