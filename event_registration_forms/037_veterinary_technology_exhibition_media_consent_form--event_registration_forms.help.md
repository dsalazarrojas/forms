# Veterinary Technology Exhibition Media Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain your consent for the recording of your likeness and image during the Veterinary Technology Exhibition. Please review the form carefully and provide your consent before attending the exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your Attendee Information in the field below.
2. Select the type(s) of media you are willing to be recorded (e.g. photo, video, interview).
3. Choose the purpose for which you want the media to be used (e.g. social media, marketing materials, research purposes).
4. Enter your email address and phone number if you want to be contacted for further information.
5. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to include.
6. Read and agree to our Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Information**: Please provide your name and any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **What type of media will be recorded?**: Select all the types of media you are willing to be recorded, such as photo, video, or interview. 
* **What would you like to use the recorded media for?**: Choose all the purposes for which you want the media to be used, such as social media, marketing materials, or research purposes. 
* **Email**: Please enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone Number**: If you want to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to include.
* **Do you consent to the recording of your likeness and image?**: Select one of the options to indicate your consent.
* **Have you read and agreed with our Terms and Conditions?**: Please select one of the options to indicate that you have read and agreed to our Terms and Conditions. 

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask our staff.
* If you have any additional media types or purposes you'd like to include, please let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.
