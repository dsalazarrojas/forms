# Best Mentor Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Mentor Voting Form is a survey designed to collect feedback and ratings from participants about the mentors they encountered during the event. Your input will help us identify the most effective mentors and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal information: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Select the date of the event: Choose the date you participated in the event.
3.  Choose the best mentor: Select the category that best describes the primary mentor you interacted with (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
4.  Provide any necessary details: If applicable, enter the number of mentors you interacted with or rate the quality of the mentorship experience.
5.  Confirm your agreement: Select the options that apply to your experience.
6.  Provide final comments or notes: If you have any additional feedback or remarks, you can include them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Include your country code if outside your local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you participated in the event.
* **Primary Selection** (`primary_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category that best describes the primary mentor you interacted with (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
* **Quantity or Rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, `number`, optional): Provide a whole number for the quantity or rating of the mentorship experience, if applicable.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about your experience.
* **Confirm Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the options that apply to your experience (I agree, I need follow up, or Not applicable).
* **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, optional): Use decimal format when providing an estimated value, if applicable.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final Comments or Notes** (`final_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any final comments or notes about your experience.
