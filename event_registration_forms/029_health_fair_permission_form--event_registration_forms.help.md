# Health Fair Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather participant consent and demographic information for a health fair event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section with your personal information.
2. Select the correct event details and medical screening information.
3. Choose your permission options.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`health_fair_permission_form_participant_details`, text, required):
    This section is for you to provide your personal details such as your name, contact number, email, and date of birth.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional):
    This section is for you to provide information about the event you are participating in.
* **Event Screening Details** (`event_screening_details`, text, optional):
    This section is for you to provide any relevant medical screening details.
* **Health Fair Permission** (`health_fair_permission`, select multiple, required):
    This section allows you to choose what medical screenings you are willing to participate in.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required):
    This is the section where you sign to confirm your consent.
* **Participant Photo ID** (`participant_photo_id`, number, required):
    This is the section where you provide your photo ID number.
* **Event Staff Signature** (`event_staff_signature`, text, required):
    This is the section where the event staff signs to confirm your consent.
* **Event Staff Photo ID** (`event_staff_photo_id`, number, required):
    This is the section where the event staff provides their photo ID number.
* **Consent Form Date** (`consent_form_date`, date, required):
    This is the date you sign the consent form.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required):
    This is your date of birth.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required):
    This is your age.
* **Participant Contact Number** (`participant_contact_number`, text, required):
    This is your contact number.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required):
    This is your email address.
* **Participant Relationship Status** (`participant_relationship_status`, select one, required):
    This is your relationship status.
* **Participant Employment Status** (`participant_employment_status`, select one, required):
    This is your employment status.
* **Participant Education Level** (`participant_education_level`, select one, required):
    This is your education level.
* **Participant Employment Type** (`participant_employment_type`, select one, required):
    This is your employment type.
* **Participant Employment Duration** (`participant_employment_duration`, select one, required):
    This is the duration of your employment.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, required):
    This is your job title.
* **Participant Health Insurance Provider** (`participant_health_insurance_provider`, select one, required):
    This is your health insurance provider.
* **Participant Primary Care Physician** (`participant_primary_care_physician`, text, required):
    This is your primary care physician.
* **Participant Medical History** (`participant_medical_history`, text, required):
    This is your medical history.
* **Participant Allergies** (`participant_allergies`, select multiple, required):
    These are your allergies.
* **Participant Medications** (`participant_medications`, select multiple, required):
    These are your medications.
* **Participant Medical Device ID** (`participant_medical_device_id`, text, required):
    This is your medical device ID.
