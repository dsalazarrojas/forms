# Annual General Meeting Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for members to provide feedback, proposals, and updates to the organization during the Annual General Meeting. It helps the organization understand member thoughts, ideas, and concerns, which will inform decision-making and improve activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your member information, including your name and membership number (if applicable).
2. Provide your contact details, including your email address and phone number, so the organization can reach out to you.
3. Select the topics you would like to be discussed during the meeting (if any).
4. If you have a specific discussion topic, describe it in the text box.
5. If you have questions or concerns, share them in the text box.
6. If you have a proposal or idea, select whether you want to present it or discuss it, and provide a description.
7. Specify how you would like to be involved in the organization, such as by joining a committee or volunteering.
8. Let the organization know if you're interested in a leadership position and specify your level of interest.
9. Share any skills or expertise you can offer to the organization.
10. Select your preferred method of communication (e.g., email, phone, text message, or mail).
11. Choose your desired communication frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, or as needed).
12. Decide whether you want to subscribe to the organization's newsletter.
13. Indicate if you need a follow-up conversation.
14. Provide any additional comments or thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name**: Your full name as a member of the organization.
* **Member ID**: Your membership number (if applicable).
* **Email Address**: Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Topics You Want Discussed at AGM**: Select areas of interest (if any).
* **Specific Discussion Topic**: Describe what you want to discuss (if any).
* **Questions for Organization Leadership**: Share any questions or concerns.
* **Concerns or Suggestions**: Share feedback or ideas.
* **Do You Have a Proposal for the AGM**: Select whether you have a proposal or idea.
* **Proposal Description**: Describe your proposal or idea.
* **Expected Impact or Benefits**: Explain how your proposal will benefit the organization.
* **Committee or Volunteer Interest**: Select areas where you'd like to get involved.
* **Leadership Position Interest**: Specify your interest level in a leadership role.
* **Skills or Expertise to Contribute**: Share your skills or expertise.
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose your preferred method of communication.
* **Communication Frequency**: Specify how often you'd like to hear from the organization.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter**: Decide whether you want to subscribe to the organization's newsletter.
* **Request Follow-Up Conversation**: Indicate if you need a follow-up conversation.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any other feedback or thoughts.

## Tips

* Be honest and open when sharing your thoughts and ideas.
* Clearly describe your proposals, ideas, and concerns.
* Select your preferred method of communication to ensure the organization can reach you.
* Specify your involvement interests to help the organization understand your capabilities and willingness to contribute.
