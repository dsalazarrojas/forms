# Rally Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rally Class Registration Form is used to collect information from participants or instructors attending a rally class. This form is used to gather demographic and contact details, as well as preferences for the class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field. This field is optional.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field. This field is optional.
3. Select your preferred Class Type from the dropdown menu. You can choose between "Rally Car", "Rally Cross", or "Rally Sprint". This field is optional.
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This field should be in the format "example@domain.com". This field is optional.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field. This field is optional.
6. Choose a Class Date for your registration. This field is optional.
7. Select your Participant Type. You can choose between "Instructor", "Participant", or "Guest". This field is optional.
8. Enter your age if you are a participant. This field is optional.
9. Enter the name and details of your Emergency Contact. This field is optional.
10. Select the number of hours you plan to participate in the class. This field is optional.
11. Choose the Class Duration in hours. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Class Type** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select your preferred class type from the available options.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address in the format "username@domain.com".
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Class Date** (`date`, optional): Choose the date for your class registration.
* **Participant Type** (`select_one`, optional): Select your participant type from the available options.
* **Participant Age** (`number`, optional): Enter your age if you are a participant.
* **Emergency Contact** (`text`, optional): Enter the name and details of your emergency contact.
* **Class Hours** (`time`, optional): Select the number of hours you plan to participate in the class.
* **Class Duration** (`number`, optional): Choose the duration of your class in hours.
