# HistoricWalkingTourSignupForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Historic Walking Tour Signup Form is designed to collect information from participants for a historic walking tour. The form helps us to gather essential details such as participant names, dates, contact information, tour scheduling preferences, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth and enjoyable tour experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date you would like to go on the tour.
2. Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the tour guide.
3. Provide your email address so we can contact you with tour updates.
4. Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any last-minute tour changes.
5. Enter your address if you would like to be contacted at home or work.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your participation in the tour.
7. Choose your preferred tour schedule (morning, afternoon, or all-day tour).
8. Indicate the number of participants joining with you.
9. Select the type of participant you are (adult, child, or senior).
10. Enter the age of each participant.
11. Select the age group of each participant (5-12, 13-59, or 60+).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tour Date**: Choose the date you would like to go on the tour. Please note that we can accommodate only one submission per participant per date.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the tour guide.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with tour updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any last-minute tour changes.
* **Address**: Enter your address if you would like to be contacted at home or work.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your participation in the tour.
* **Tour Scheduling**: Choose your preferred tour schedule (morning, afternoon, or all-day tour).
* **Number of Participants**: Indicate the number of participants joining with you.
* **Participant Type**: Select the type of participant you are (adult, child, or senior).
* **Age of Participant**: Enter the age of each participant.
* **Age Group**: Select the age group of each participant (5-12, 13-59, or 60+).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you submit only one form per participant per date.
* If you have any special requirements or needs, please mention them in the "Participant Notes" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
