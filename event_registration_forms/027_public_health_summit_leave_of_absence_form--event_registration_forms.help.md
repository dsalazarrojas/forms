# Public Health Summit Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Public Health Summit Leave Of Absence Form" is used to register for a leave of absence from the public health summit. This form collects essential information from attendees and staff members to facilitate the management of registrations and attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please ensure that you are a registered attendee or staff member of the public health summit. 

1. **Event Registration Form**: Enter your event registration details, if not already registered. 
2. **Leave Request Form**: Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., full-time, part-time, or no-show).
3. **Event Title**: If applicable, select the event title for which you are requesting leave.
4. **Attendee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your registration.
5. **Organization**: If you are affiliated with an organization, enter it here.
6. **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
7. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
8. **Leave Start Date**: Enter the date you wish to leave (leave of absence start date).
9. **Leave End Date**: Enter the date you wish to return (leave of absence end date).
10. **Reason For Leave**: Briefly describe your reason for leave.
11. **Manager Name**: If applicable, enter your manager's name.
12. **Manager Email**: If applicable, enter your manager's email address.
13. **Manager Phone**: If applicable, enter your manager's phone number.
