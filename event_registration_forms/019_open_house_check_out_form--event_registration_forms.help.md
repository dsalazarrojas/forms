# Open House Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of an open house event hosted by a real estate agent. The feedback will be used to improve future events and provide better service to potential clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question as thoroughly and truthfully as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select only one option that best represents your opinion.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by the required label).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think of the open house?** (`open_house_check_out_form_1`, text, required): Share your honest opinion about the open house. What did you like or dislike about it?
* **How did you hear about this open house?** (`open_house_check_out_form_2`, select_one, required): How did you find out about the open house? Select one of the provided options.
	+ Friends/Family
	+ Online Advertisement
	+ Newspaper
	+ Other
	+ Social Media
* **What time did you arrive at the open house?** (`open_house_check_out_form_3`, time, required): Provide the exact time you arrived at the open house. Please use 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **What time did you leave the open house?** (`open_house_check_out_form_4`, time, required): Provide the exact time you left the open house. Please use 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **What did you like most about this open house?** (`open_house_check_out_form_5`, text, required): Share what you enjoyed or appreciated about the open house.
* **Did you like the layout of the house?** (`open_house_check_out_form_6`, select_one, required): Please select one of the provided options about the house's layout.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Were you able to see a house that you are considering purchasing?** (`open_house_check_out_form_7`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options regarding your interest in purchasing a house.
	+ True
	+ False
* **How likely are you to work with this real estate agent again?** (`open_house_check_out_form_8`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that represent your likelihood of working with the agent again. 
	+ Very Likely
	+ Somewhat Likely
	+ Not Likely
	+ Not Likely At All
* **Would you like to have your contact information recorded for future follow-up?** (`open_house_check_out_form_9`, select_one, required): Please select one of the provided options regarding your preference to record your contact information.
	+ True
	+ False
* **How long did you spend at the open house?** (`open_house_check_out_form_10`, number, required): Provide the number of minutes you spent at the open house.
* **Would you like to receive a follow-up call or email after the open house?** (`open_house_check_out_form_11`, select_one, required): Please select one of the provided options about receiving follow-up communication.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Did you find the open house helpful in your home buying or selling process?** (`open_house_check_out_form_12`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options regarding the helpfulness of the open house.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Did you visit the open house with any other people?** (`open_house_check_out_form_14`, text, required): If you did not visit alone, list the names of the people you visited with.
* **Email** (`open_house_check_out_form_15`, email, optional): If you would like to provide your email address for future follow-up, enter it here.
* **Phone** (`open_house_check_out_form_16`, text, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number for future follow-up, enter it here.
* **Open House Check Out Form 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10** (`open_house_check_out_form_17`, `open_house_check_out_form_18`, `open_house_check_out_form_19`, `open_house_check_out_form_20`, `open_house_check_out_form_21`, `open_house_check_out_form_22`, `open_house_check_out_form_23`, `open_house_check_out_form_24`, `open_house_check_out_form_25`, text, optional): These fields are meant for additional comments or notes. Feel free to use them to provide any additional information or feedback.
 
## Tips
* Please be as honest and thorough in your answers.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact the real estate agent.
* Your feedback is valuable and helps us improve our services.
