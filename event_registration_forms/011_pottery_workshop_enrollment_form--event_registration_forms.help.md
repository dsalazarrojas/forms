# Pottery Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pottery Workshop Enrollment Form is used to register participants in a pottery workshop. It collects necessary information from participants to ensure a smooth and successful workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2.  Select the desired session date from the calendar provided.
3.  Enter your participant name in the text field.
4.  Choose whether you have a studio or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the dropdown menu.
5.  Enter the name of the instructor if applicable.
6.  Choose your preferred session time by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the dropdown menu.
7.  Add any additional participant notes in the text area.
8.  Enter your participant email address.
9.  Enter your participant phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Select the date you wish to attend the workshop from the calendar provided.
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Studio** (`studio_name`, select_multiple, optional): Choose "Yes" if you have access to a studio or "No" if you do not.
*   **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the instructor leading the workshop.
*   **Preferred Session** (`preferred_session`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you prefer to attend this session or "No" if you have no preference.
*   **Session Time** (`session_time`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you prefer the session time or "No" if you have no preference.
*   **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your participation.
*   **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where the organizer may contact you.
*   **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where the organizer may contact you.
