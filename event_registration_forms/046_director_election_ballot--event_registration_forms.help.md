# Director Election Ballot - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Director Election Ballot form is used to register your vote and select your preferred method of voting in the director election.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select a Board Member**: Choose the board member whose election you are voting in.
2. Fill in your **Voting Method**: Select whether you want to vote Online Ballot or In-Person Ballot.
3. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number**: Enter your personal details to allow us to contact you with any updates or results.
4. Select a **Date** and **Time**: If relevant, choose the date and time of the event or meeting where you will be casting your vote.
5. Enter any **Notes or Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information, you can provide them here.
6. If you have a preferred **Voting Method other**, please specify.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a Board Member**: 
  * (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the board member whose election you are voting in.
* **Voting Method**:
  * (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you want to vote Online Ballot or In-Person Ballot.
* **First Name**:
  * (`text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**:
  * (`text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email**:
  * (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone**:
  * (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date**:
  * (`date`, required/optional): If relevant, choose the date of the event or meeting where you will be casting your vote.
* **Time**:
  * (`time`, required/optional): If relevant, choose the time of the event or meeting where you will be casting your vote.
* **Note**:
  * (`note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
* **Other Voting Method**:
  * (`text`, required/optional): If you have a preferred voting method, please specify.
* **Comments**:
  * (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
