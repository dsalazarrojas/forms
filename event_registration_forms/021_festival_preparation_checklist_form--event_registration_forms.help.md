## festival_preparation_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The festival preparation checklist form is designed to gather information about a festival's infrastructure and requirements. This form will be used by festival organizers to document and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each field, ensuring that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **festival_location** (festival location, text, required: false): Please enter the location of the festival.
* **festival_stage_layout** (festival stage layout, number, required: false): Select the layout of the festival stage. You can choose between 2nd floor, stage, and main stage.
* **festival_sound_system** (festival sound system, select_one, required: false): Select the type of sound system required for the festival. This will help us determine the necessary sound system equipment.
* **festival_sound_system_capacity** (festival sound system capacity, number, required: false): Enter the sound system capacity, indicating the number of speakers or amplifiers needed.
* **festival_sound_system_technical_requirements** (festival sound system technical requirements, text, required: false): Describe any technical requirements for the sound system, such as specific software or interface requirements.
* **festival_power_supply_requirements** (festival power supply requirements, text, required: false): Provide any special power supply requirements for the festival.
* **festival_water_supply_requirements** (festival water supply requirements, text, required: false): List any water supply requirements for the festival.
* **festival_food_supply_requirements** (festival food supply requirements, text, required: false): Describe any food supply requirements for the festival, including catering and food vendor arrangements.
* **festival_toilet_and_shower_requirements** (festival toilet and shower requirements, text, required: false): Specify any toilet and shower requirements for festival-goers, including the number and type of facilities.
* **festival_power_supplies** (festival power supplies, text, required: false): List any power supplies that will be needed, such as generators or electrical outlets.
* **festival_water_supplies** (festival water supplies, text, required: false): Mention any water supplies that will be required, such as water tanks or pipes.
* **festival_food_supplies** (festival food supplies, select_one, required: true): Select the level of food supplies required for the festival, with options for 2.5kva, 5.0kva, and 10.0kva.
* **festival_waste_disposal** (festival waste disposal, select_one, required: true): Select the waste disposal method, with options for 2.0m, 5.0m, and 10.0m.
* **festival_waste_disposal_equipment** (festival waste disposal equipment, text, required: false): Describe any equipment needed for waste disposal, such as bins or compactors.
* **festival_camping** (festival camping, select_one, required: true): Indicate if there will be camping on site, with options for True, False, and maybe.
* **festival_equipment_provisions** (festival equipment provisions, text, required: false): Provide any equipment provisions, such as tables, chairs, or stage equipment.
* **festival_shelter_equipment** (festival shelter equipment, select_multiple, required: false): Select any shelter equipment that will be needed, including tents, canopies, and marquees.
* **festival_parking_lot** (festival parking lot, text, required: false): Mention any parking lot arrangements or requirements.
* **festival_parking** (festival parking, text, required: false): Indicate any parking requirements or restrictions.
 
Remember, all required fields are marked as such.
