# Leadership Conference Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Conference Attendance Form is designed to help you register for the Leadership Conference. It collects essential information about you, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, and event date. This form ensures that we can accurately manage attendee registrations and communicate with you before and after the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email in the "Email" field. This is the address we will use to contact you regarding the conference.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the date of the conference you plan to attend in the "Event Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your first name. This is the first part of your name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, optional): Enter your last name. This is the last part of your name.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address. We will use this to contact you regarding the conference.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you in case of last-minute changes or questions.
* **Event Date** (5, date, optional): Select the date of the conference you plan to attend. This will help us schedule and prepare for the conference.
