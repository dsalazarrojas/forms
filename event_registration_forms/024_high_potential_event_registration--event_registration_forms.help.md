# High Potential Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High Potential Event Registration form is designed to gather information from participants who are interested in attending a High Potential event. This form helps event organizers to track participant details, such as contact information, event preferences, and other relevant details.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the event title in the "Event Title" field.
4. Select the event date from the calendar in the "Event Date" field.
5. Choose the event time from the dropdown menu in the "Event Time" field.
6. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
7. Add any participant notes in the "Participant Notes" field.
8. Fill in the participant details in the "Participant Details" field.
9. Indicate your participant attendance status in the "Participant Attendance" field.
10. Provide any additional participant information in the "Participant Additional Info" field.
11. Select your participant interest in the "Participant Interest" field.
12. Confirm that you agree to the participant agreement by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu in the "Participant Agreement" field.
13. If applicable, select the date of your participant agreement in the "Participant Agreement Date" field.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (text, required/false): Enter your name to register for the event.
### Email
* **Email** (email, required/false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the event.
### Event Title
* **Event Title** (text, required/false): Enter the title of the event you are registering for.
### Event Date
* **Event Date** (date, required/false): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
### Event Time
* **Event Time** (time, required/false): Choose the time of the event from the dropdown menu.
### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (text, required/false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
### Participant Notes
* **Participant Notes** (note, required/false): Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (text, required/false): Fill in any additional details about yourself that are relevant to the event.
### Participant Attendance
* **Participant Attendance** (text, required/false): Indicate whether you will be attending the event or not.
### Participant Additional Info
* **Participant Additional Info** (text, required/false): Provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the event.
### Participant Interest
* **Participant Interest** (text, required/false): Select your interest in the event from the provided options.
### Participant Agreement
* **Participant Agreement** (select_one, required/true): Confirm that you agree to the participant agreement.
### Participant Agreement Date
* **Participant Agreement Date** (date, required/false): Select the date you agreed to the participant agreement, if applicable.
