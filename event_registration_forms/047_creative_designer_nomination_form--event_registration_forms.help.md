# Creative Designer Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data for a creative nomination process. It's essential that the internal team is aware of this so we can ensure the correct fields are filled in the right way.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Nomination Form**: This is the main section where you'll provide the nomination details.
2.  **Nomination Name**: Enter a clear and concise name for the nomination.
3.  **Nomination Title**: Briefly describe the nomination.
4.  **Nomination Description**: Provide a detailed description of the nomination.
5.  **Nomination Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted regarding this nomination.
6.  **Nomination Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted regarding this nomination.
7.  **Nomination Category**: Choose whether this nomination is 'Yes' or 'No' for the category.
8.  **Nomination Tags**: Select the relevant tags for this nomination (multiple selections are allowed).
9.  **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination.
10.  **Nomination Time**: Enter the time of the nomination.
11.  **Submit Button**: Click on this button to submit your nomination.
12.  **Nomination Form Submitter**: Enter your name and title if you'd like to be associated with this nomination.
13.  **Submission Acknowledgement**: This is a note field for any submission acknowledgement.
14.  **Submission Status**: Choose whether the submission is 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
15.  **Nomination Form Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted regarding this nomination.
16.  **Nomination Form Submitter Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted regarding this nomination.
17.  **Submission Date**: Enter the date of the submission.
18.  **Submission Time**: Enter the time of the submission.
