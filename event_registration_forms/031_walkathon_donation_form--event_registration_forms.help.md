# Walkathon Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Walkathon Donation Form is designed to collect donations for our walkathon event. This form helps us understand who is donating, how much they are giving, and how to contact them.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out this form with the following information:
   - **Walkathon Name**: Enter the name of the walkathon event you are supporting.
   - **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant who will be participating in the walkathon.
   - **Email**: Enter the email address of the participant. This will be used to contact them.
   - **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
   - **Amount**: Enter the donation amount you are contributing.
   - **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to make the payment (Cash, Card, or PayPal).
   - **Donation Amount**: Confirm the donation amount you are giving.
   - **Participant Address**: Select where the participant will be receiving their items (Billing or Shipping).
   - **Event Date**: Enter the date of the walkathon event.
   - **Participant Zip**: Enter the participant's zip code.
   - **Donation Message**: If you have any special request or message for the participant, enter it here.
   - **Donor Name**: Enter your name as the donor.
   - **Donor Address**: Enter your address.
   - **Donor Email**: Enter your email address.
   - **Donor Phone**: Enter your phone number.
   - **Donor Zip**: Enter your zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Walkathon Name**: This field is used to identify the walkathon event you are supporting.
*  **Participant Name**: This field is used to record the name of the participant who will be participating in the walkathon.
*  **Email**: This field is used to store the participant's email address for follow-up communications.
*  **Phone**: This field is used to store the participant's phone number for follow-up communications.
*  **Amount**: This field is used to record the donation amount you are contributing.
*  **Payment Method**: This field allows you to choose between three payment methods: Cash, Card, or PayPal.
*  **Donation Amount**: This field is used to confirm the donation amount you are giving.
*  **Participant Address**: This field allows you to select whether the participant will be receiving items at their Billing or Shipping address.
*  **Event Date**: This field is used to store the date of the walkathon event.
*  **Participant Zip**: This field is used to record the participant's zip code.
*  **Donation Message**: This field is used to record any special message or request for the participant.
*  **Donor Name**: This field is used to record your name as the donor.
*  **Donor Address**: This field is used to store your address.
*  **Donor Email**: This field is used to record your email address.
*  **Donor Phone**: This field is used to store your phone number.
*  **Donor Zip**: This field is used to record your zip code.
