# mental_health_summit_sponsorship_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mental health summit sponsorship form is designed for organizations and individuals interested in sponsoring the event. You will be asked to provide information about your event and the sponsorship package you are offering, including event details and promotional commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** in the field provided.
2. Choose the **Event Date** for the event you wish to sponsor.
3. Provide the **Event Location** where the event will take place.
4. Enter your **Sponsor Name** and contact information.
5. Upload your **Sponsor Logo** if you wish to display it on our promotional materials.
6. Select the **Tier Selection** that suits your sponsorship package (Yes or No).
7. Describe your **Promotional Commitment**, outlining the specific activities you plan to undertake for the summit.
8. Provide a brief overview of the **Sponsorship Terms**, including any specific conditions or requirements for sponsorship.
9. Select **Promotional Coordinators** who will be responsible for promoting your sponsorship (Yes or No).
10. Finally, provide any **Additional Information** you feel is relevant to your sponsorship package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the event you wish to sponsor.
* **Event Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (Text, Optional): Provide the location where the event will take place.
* **Sponsor Name** (Text, Required): Enter your organization or company name as the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter a contact person's name, email, and phone number.
* **Sponsor Logo** (Text, Optional): Upload an image of your logo to be used in promotional materials.
* **Tier Selection** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from Yes or No to indicate the tier of sponsorship you are offering.
* **Promotional Commitment** (Text, Optional): Describe the specific activities you plan to undertake for the summit.
* **Sponsorship Terms** (Text, Optional): Outline any specific conditions or requirements for sponsorship.
* **Promotional Coordinators** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from Yes or No to indicate if you have designated coordinators for promotion.
* **Additional Information** (Text, Optional): Provide any other relevant details about your sponsorship package.
