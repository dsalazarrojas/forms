# Nonprofit Leadership Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Nonprofit Leadership Seminar. Fill out the form to reserve a spot at the seminar. The provided information will help us understand who is coming and plan the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as required by the first and last name fields. Please enter your first and last names, if applicable. 
2. Enter your Organization, this can be your Nonprofit organization or company name.
3. Provide a valid email address in the email field, this will be our way to contact you. 
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field. This will allow us to reach out if needed before or after the seminar. 
5. If you are certain about your registration date, feel free to provide it. Otherwise, it's optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional: required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional: required): Enter your last name here. If you don't have a last name, leave this field blank.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required/optional: required): Enter the name of the organization you are representing. This could be a company or your Nonprofit organization.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional: required): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional: required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you if needed.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required/optional: required): Enter the date you plan to register for the seminar. If you are uncertain about the date, you can leave this field empty.

## Tips
* Please ensure your email and phone fields are accurate so we can contact you effectively. 
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us for help.
