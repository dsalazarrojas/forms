# Lighting Equipment Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lighting Equipment Payment Form is a simple and straightforward form designed to collect information from users for the purpose of payment for lighting equipment purchases or rentals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the third field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourth field.
5. Select a **Payment Method** from the available options (Card, Eft, or Cash).
6. Enter the **Amount** to be paid in the sixth field.
7. Choose a **Date** for the payment in the seventh field.
8. Finally, select a **Time** for the payment in the eighth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Last Name** (`user_input_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email** (`user_input_3`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (`user_input_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`user_input_5`, `select_one`, required: false): Select a payment method from the available options (Card, Eft, or Cash).
* **Amount** (`user_input_6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the exact amount to be paid for the lighting equipment.
* **Date** (`user_input_7`, `date`, required: false): Select the date for the payment.
* **Time** (`user_input_8`, `time`, required: false): Select the time for the payment.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully. If you are unsure of any information, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification. This form is intended to be completed by individuals seeking to make payments for lighting equipment purchases or rentals.
