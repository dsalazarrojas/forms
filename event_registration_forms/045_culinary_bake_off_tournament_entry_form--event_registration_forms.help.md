# Culinary Bake Off Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering entries for the Culinary Bake Off Tournament. It gathers essential information about the entrant, entry, and baker.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your registration information on the first page.
2. Fill out the contact information page.
3. Enter your entry details, including the entry name, category, subcategory, and description.
4. Add an image of your entry.
5. Specify the entry date and time (if applicable).
6. Confirm the rules and terms of the tournament.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information**:
  * **Title** (`title`): Title of the form, "Culinary Bake Off Tournament Entry Form".
  * **Registration Information** (`registration_info`): Enter a brief description of your registration.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information, including street address, zip, city, state, and country.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address as it appears on your contact information.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number as it appears on your contact information.
* **Entry Information** (`entry_info`): Enter a brief description of your entry.
* **Entry Name** (`entry_name`): Enter the name of your entry.
* **Category** (`entry_category`): Select the category of your entry (e.g., Cake, Cookie).
* **Subcategory** (`entry_subcategory`): Select the subcategory of your entry (e.g., Chocolate, Fruits, Cakes).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter the name of the person submitting the entry.
* **Submitter's Phone** (`submitter_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the entry.
* **Submitter's Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter the email address of the person submitting the entry.
* **Bakery** (`bakery`): Enter the name of the bakery or organization associated with your entry.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your street address.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city of your address.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state of your address.
* **Country** (`country`): Enter the country of your address.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of your entry.
* **Image** (`image`): Enter a description of the image of your entry.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`): Enter the URL of the image of your entry (if applicable).
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`): Enter the date you want to enter your entry.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`): Enter the time you want to enter your entry (if applicable).
* **Rules** (`rules`): Select whether you agree to follow the rules of the tournament.
* **Terms** (`terms`): Select whether you agree to the terms of the tournament.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check your entry information for any errors.
* Verify that the image URL is correct (if applicable).
