# Health Data Privacy Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Data Privacy Seminar Registration Form is a registration form designed for attendees to register for a health data privacy seminar. This form is intended to collect basic attendee information and seminar details for organization and administration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Health Data Privacy Seminar Registration Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Attendee Name**: This is your name as it appears on the seminar registration list.
2.  Provide your **Email**: This will be used to communicate with you about the seminar and any updates.
3.  Enter your **Phone Number**: This is your contact number in case we need to reach out to you.
4.  Select the **Seminar Type**: Choose the seminar type that you are interested in attending.
5.  Enter the **Registration Date**: This is the date you plan to attend the seminar.
6.  Provide any additional **Comments**: If you have any specific requests or questions, please include them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on the seminar registration list.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): This will be used to communicate with you about the seminar and any updates.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your contact number in case we need to reach out to you.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to attend the seminar.
*   **Seminar Type** (`seminar_type`, select_one, required): Choose the seminar type that you are interested in attending.
*   **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to attend the seminar.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any specific requests or questions, please include them here.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field is not required and may be used for specific feature or requirement.
