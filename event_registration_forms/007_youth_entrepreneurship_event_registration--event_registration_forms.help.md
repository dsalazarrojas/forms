# Youth Entrepreneurship Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for a youth entrepreneurship event. It captures essential information about the participant, including their name, phone number, email, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter the title of the event in the top section, although in this case, the title is not provided in this yaml.
2. Fill out each of the required and optional fields according to your information, including:
	* Participant Info (Text)
	* Participant Name (Text)
	* Participant Phone (Text)
	* Participant Email (Email)
	* And other subsequent participant info fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant Info** 
*   **Participant Info (Text)**: This field is a free text area where you can provide any additional information about the participant.
*   **Participant Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the participant.
*   **Participant Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
*   **Participant Email (Email)**: Enter the email of the participant.

Note that this form is quite extensive, but you can fill it out according to your needs and the information you have about the participants. 

Please make sure to double-check your inputs for accuracy to avoid any issues during the registration process.
