# Treasury Forecasting Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Forecasting Seminar Registration form is used to gather information about attendees, including their session preferences and contact details, to be used for internal administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the session preferences section and select all that apply.
2. Fill out the attendee details section with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Enter the email address of the attendee in the email field.
4. If the attendee has a phone number, enter it in the phone field.
5. Review the consent section and acknowledge that the attendee has read and understood the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session preferences**: Select all that apply to indicate the attendee's interests and preferences for the seminar session. This field allows multiple selections, so you can choose one or more options.
* **Attendee details**: Enter the attendee's name, job title, department, and other relevant details to help us understand their background and context.
* **Email**: Enter the attendee's email address to ensure we can contact them with seminar updates and information.
* **Phone**: If the attendee has a phone number, enter it to enable us to contact them directly.
* **Consent**: Read and acknowledge that you have understood the terms and conditions of the seminar, and that you have given permission to collect and use your data for internal purposes only.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field to ensure we can provide the best possible experience for the attendee.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the session preferences options or the attendee details guidelines.
