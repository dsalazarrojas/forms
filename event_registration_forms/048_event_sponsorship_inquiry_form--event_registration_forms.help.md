# event_sponsorship_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential event sponsors, helping us better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Sponsor Information field with your company or organization's details.
2. Provide your contact details in the Contact Details field.
3. Describe the event you are interested in sponsoring in the Event Details field.
4. Specify the sponsorship details you are interested in in the Sponsorship Details field.
5. Choose your preferred method of communication in the Communication Preferences field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Information** (`sponsor_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your company or organization's name and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Provide your name, email address, and any other contact information you'd like to make us aware of.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the event you are interested in sponsoring, including its name, date, and location.
* **Sponsorship Details** (`sponsorship_details`, text, required/optional): Specify what kind of sponsorship you are interested in, such as monetary, product, or service.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, text, required/optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted for further information, such as email, phone, or mail.

## Tips
* Be clear and concise in your responses to ensure we understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review and respond to your inquiry as soon as possible.
