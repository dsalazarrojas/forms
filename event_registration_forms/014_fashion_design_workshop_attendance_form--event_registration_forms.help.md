# Fashion Design Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Fashion Design Workshop. To ensure that we can keep track of attendees and their preferences, please complete this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** (Field 1) with your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your **Participant Email** (Field 2) to ensure we can contact you about workshop updates and confirm your attendance.
3. If you have a **Participant Phone Number** (Field 3), enter it to allow us to contact you about any last-minute workshop details.
4. For the **Workshop Date** (Field 4), select the date you plan to attend the workshop from the list of available dates.
5. Choose your **Attendance Status** (Field 5) to let us know if you plan to attend, are unable to attend, or have other plans.
6. Add any **Additional Notes** (Field 6) you'd like to share with us, such as any special requests or accommodations you may need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name (Field 1)
* **Participant Name**: A text field for you to enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### Participant Email (Field 2)
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the workshop.

### Participant Phone Number (Field 3)
* **Participant Phone Number**: If you'd like to be contacted about any last-minute workshop details, enter your phone number.

### Workshop Date (Field 4)
* **Workshop Date**: Choose the date you plan to attend the workshop from the list of available dates.

### Attendance Status (Field 5)
* **Attendance Status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Label 1: I will attend
	+ Label 2: Unable to attend
	+ Label 3: Other plans

### Additional Notes (Field 6)
* **Additional Notes**: Share any special requests or accommodations you may need with us.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
