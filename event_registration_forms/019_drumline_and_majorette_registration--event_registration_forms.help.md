# Drumline And Majorette Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register students for drumline and majorette events. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in the corresponding fields. The form is divided into multiple pages, and you will need to navigate through each page to input your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive communication about the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event you wish to register for.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of the event you wish to register for.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are registering for Drumline, Majorette, or Both programs.
* **Event Program** (`event_program`, select_multiple, optional): Select the programs you are interested in (Drumline, Majorette, or Other).
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have access to a drum set or not.
* **School or Team** (`school_or_team`, text, optional): Enter your school or team name if applicable.
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, required): Enter your grade level (note: this field is required).
