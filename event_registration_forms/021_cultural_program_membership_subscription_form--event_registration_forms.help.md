# Cultural Program Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural program membership subscription form is designed to facilitate the registration and subscription process for cultural programs. This form collects necessary information about your organization, program details, and payment preferences to help us process your subscription efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. If you need to edit or modify your submitted form, use the "cancel" and "back" buttons to navigate through the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: This field requires the name of your organization. Enter the name exactly as it is known to the public.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you are interested in subscribing to.
* **Contact Name**: Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the subscription.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Program Start Date**: If you have a specific start date for the program, enter it here.
* **Program End Date**: If you have a specific end date for the program, enter it here.
* **Subscription Plan**: Choose your preferred subscription plan from the options provided (Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-annually, Annually).
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check).
* **Payment Plan Start Date**: If your subscription starts on a specific date, enter it here.
* **Payment Plan End Date**: If you have a specific end date for your subscription, enter it here.
* **Payment Plan Frequency**: Choose the frequency of your payment plan (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Billing Address Line 1**: Enter the billing address line 1 for your organization.
* **Billing City**: Enter the city of your billing address.
* **Billing State**: Enter the state of your billing address.
* **Billing Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your billing address.
* **Billing Country**: Enter the country of your billing address.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your subscription.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your form.
* **Save and View**: Review your submitted form before saving it.
* **Cancel**: Cancel the form if you need to make changes or corrections.

## Tips

* Double-check your form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* Use the navigation buttons (Back, Next, Cancel) to navigate through the form easily.
* If you need help with any field or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
