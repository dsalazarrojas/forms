# Workplace Community Event Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Community Event Application Form is a crucial tool for workplace community event registration. It helps ensure that events are properly registered and approved by the necessary parties, reducing duplication and promoting well-planned and executed events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Make sure that the event is not already registered in the system.
3. Provide detailed and clear information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event, for example, "Team Lunch" or "Company Meeting".
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the event, including its purpose and objectives.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the start and end times of the event, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the address or venue of the event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the event, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the event, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or team responsible for organizing the event.
* **Event Coordinators** (`event_coordinators`, text, required: false): Enter the names of the people or teams responsible for coordinating the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start and end times of the event, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, number, required: false): Enter the number of people expected to attend the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): Add any additional details about the event, such as specific speakers or topics.
* **Event Owners** (`event_owners`, text, required: false): Enter the names of the people or teams responsible for the event.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information, so the event organizers can get in touch with you.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event organizers or IT department for clarification.
* Please avoid registering duplicate events to prevent confusion and overlapping events.
