# Community Fundraiser Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields with your legal name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, which will be used for event communications.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, which will be used for primary contact.
4. If you are representing an organization, enter the name in the "Organization Name" field.
5. Select the event you are participating in from the "Fundraiser Event" dropdown menu.
6. Choose how you will participate in the event from the "Type of Participation" dropdown menu.
7. If donating, select your intended donation amount from the "Intended Donation Amount" dropdown menu.
8. If sponsoring, select your sponsorship level from the "Sponsorship Level" dropdown menu.
9. Choose your preferred communication methods in the "Communication Preferences" field.
10. Select your preferred update frequency in the "Update Frequency" field.
11. If available to volunteer, select your availability in the "Volunteer Availability" field.
12. If applicable, enter any dietary restrictions in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
13. Select your T-Shirt size for event merchandise in the "T-Shirt Size" field.
14. Finally, provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for event communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Fundraiser Event** (`fundraiser_event`, select_one, required): Select the event you are participating in.
* **Type of Participation** (`participation_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you will participate in the event.
* **Intended Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, select_one, optional): If donating, select your intended donation amount.
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, select_one, optional): If sponsoring, select your sponsorship level.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred communication methods.
* **Update Frequency** (`update_frequency`, select_one, required): Select your preferred update frequency.
* **Volunteer Availability** (`volunteer_availability`, select_multiple, optional): If available to volunteer, select your availability.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions if food is provided.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select your T-Shirt size for event merchandise.
* **How Did You Hear About Event** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Where did you learn about the event?
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
