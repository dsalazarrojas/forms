# Animal Shelter Volunteer Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track the attendance and activities of volunteers at the animal shelter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Volunteer Full Name" field.
2. If you have an assigned ID number, enter it in the "Volunteer ID Number" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the date you attended the event or worked at the shelter from the calendar.
6. Enter the time you checked in and the time you checked out in the respective fields.
7. Enter the total number of hours you worked in the "Total Hours Served" field.
8. Select the department or area you worked in from the "Department or Area" field.
9. Select the primary activity you performed from the "Primary Activity" field.
10. Enter a brief description of the activity you performed in the "Activity Details" field.
11. If you worked with any animals, enter an approximate count of the number of animals you served in the "Number of Animals Served" field.
12. If you worked with specific animals, enter their names or IDs in the "Which Animals Did You Work With" field.
13. If a staff member supervised you, enter their name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
14. Select the location where you worked from the "Location of Service" field.
15. If you encountered any issues or concerns, select "True" and describe them in the "Please Describe Issues" field.
16. If you completed any training, select "True" and describe the training in the "Training Details" field.
17. If you used any special supplies, select "True" and list the supplies in the "Supplies Details" field.
18. If follow-up action is needed, select "Yes - Urgent" or "Yes - Routine" and provide details in the "Follow-up Details" field.
19. If you have any feedback or suggestions, enter them in the "Volunteer Feedback" field.
20. Finally, enter the date you are next available to volunteer in the "When Are You Next Available" field and provide your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Full Name** (required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Volunteer ID Number** (optional): If you have an assigned ID number, enter it here.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Service** (required): Select the date you attended the event or worked at the shelter.
* **Check-In Time** (required): Enter the time you checked in.
* **Check-Out Time** (required): Enter the time you checked out.
* **Total Hours Served** (required): Enter the total number of hours you worked.
* **Department or Area** (required): Select the department or area you worked in.
* **Primary Activity** (required): Select the primary activity you performed.
* **Activity Details** (required): Enter a brief description of the activity you performed.
* **Number of Animals Served** (optional): If you worked with animals, enter an approximate count of the number of animals you served.
* **Which Animals Did You Work With** (optional): If you worked with specific animals, enter their names or IDs.
* **Supervisor Name** (optional): If a staff member supervised you, enter their name.
* **Location of Service** (required): Select the location where you worked.
* **Were There Any Issues or Concerns** (required): Select "True" if you encountered any issues or concerns.
* **Please Describe Issues** (optional): If you selected "True", provide a brief description of the issues.
* **Notable Accomplishments** (optional): Enter any special accomplishments you achieved.
* **Did You Complete Any Training Today** (optional): Select "True" if you completed any training.
* **Training Details** (optional): If you selected "True", provide a brief description of the training.
* **Did You Use Any Special Supplies** (optional): Select "True" if you used any special supplies.
* **Supplies Details** (optional): If you selected "True", list the supplies you used.
* **Is Follow-up Action Needed** (required): Select "Yes - Urgent", "Yes - Routine", or "False" depending on the situation.
* **Follow-up Details** (optional): If you selected "Yes - Urgent" or "Yes - Routine", provide a brief description of the follow-up details.
* **Any Feedback or Suggestions** (optional): Enter any feedback or suggestions you have.
* **When Are You Next Available** (optional): Enter the date you are next available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Electronic Signature** (required): Enter your electronic signature by typing your full name.
* **Supervisor Electronic Signature** (optional): Enter the supervisor's electronic signature by typing their full name.
