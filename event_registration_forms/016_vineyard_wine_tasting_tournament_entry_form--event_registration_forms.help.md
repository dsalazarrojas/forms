# Vineyard Wine Tasting Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vineyard Wine Tasting Tournament Entry Form is a registration form for participants to join in wine tasting events. It collects personal and wine-related details to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the participant and the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the entry form with the required and optional information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate contact email and participant name.
3. Select the correct participant type, wine type, color, and rating for the wine you have tried.
4. Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
5. Provide any additional details about the wine you rated in the `participant_wine_varietal`, `participant_wine_year` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, text, optional): A brief description of your entry, such as your wine background or preferences. 
* **Participant** (`participant`, select one, optional): Please select your participant type, choose one from Vineyard, Winery, or Event Organizer.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you want to be identified for the event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for event updates or communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for event coordinators or organizers to contact you.
* **Participant Wine Type** (`participant_wine_type`, select one, optional): Select the type of wine you are a participant in, choose one from Still wine, Sparkling wine, or Dessert wine.
* **Participant Wine Color** (`participant_wine_color`, select one, optional): Select the color of the wine you are a participant in, choose one from Red, White, or Rosé.
* **Participant Wine Varietal** (`participant_wine_varietal`, text, optional): Enter the type of wine grape variety you are a participant in, e.g. Chardonnay, Merlot, etc. 
* **Participant Wine Year** (`participant_wine_year`, text, optional): Enter the year of the wine you are a participant in, e.g. 2018, 2019, etc.
* **Participant Wine Rating** (`participant_wine_rating`, number, optional): Rate the wine you have tried on a scale of 1-100.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the wine tasting event you are joining.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the wine tasting event. 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all the entered information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your contact details and participant details are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are a participant in multiple wines, please create separate entries for each wine. 
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact the event organizers.
