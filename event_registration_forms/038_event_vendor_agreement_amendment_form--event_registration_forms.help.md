# Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by vendors to modify or update their existing agreement terms with the organization. It is essential to ensure that the vendor's information is up-to-date and accurate before any changes can be made to their agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have a valid registration with the organization and an existing agreement in place.
2. Answer the questions below accurately and completely.
3. Enter your updated information in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. **Vendor Name (event_vendor_agreement_amendment_form_1)** (`text`, optional):
  Enter your current vendor name.
* **2. **Date of Agreement (agreement_date)** (`date`, optional):
  Enter the date of your current agreement with the organization.
* **3. **Agreement Version (agreement_version)** (`select_one`, optional):
  Select 'Yes' if you are amending your agreement terms, 'No' if you are not.
* **4. **Vendor Name Change (vendor_name_change)** (`text`, optional):
  Enter any changes to your vendor name (if applicable).
* **5. **Vendor Address (vendor_address)** (`text`, optional):
  Enter any changes to your vendor address (if applicable).
* **6. **Vendor Contact (vendor_contact)** (`text`, optional):
  Enter any changes to your vendor contact information (if applicable).
* **7. **Agreement Term Change (agreement_term_change)** (`text`, optional):
  Enter any changes to your agreement terms (if applicable).
* **8. **Reason for Change (reason_for_change)** (`text`, optional):
  Enter a brief explanation for the changes you are making to your agreement terms.
