# Military Appreciation Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests registering for military appreciation events. It is a text-based form with five sections, each collecting specific details from the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the title and description of the event in the **Event Details** section.
2. Enter your name and other guest information in the **Guest Details** section.
3. Specify any planning preferences in the **Planning Preferences** section (if applicable).
4. Provide your name and contact information in the **Event Host** section.
5. Indicate whether you are registering to participate or observe the event in the **Registration Type** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`title`, `text`, **Required**):
  This section is where you provide the title and description of the event. Please enter the title of the event and a brief description in the text field.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, `text`, **Required**):
  In this section, please enter your name and other relevant guest information, such as contact numbers and email.
* **Planning Preferences** (`planning_preferences`, `text`, **Optional**):
  This section is optional and allows you to specify any special requests or preferences for event planning. If you have no preferences, please leave this section blank.
* **Event Host** (`event_host`, `text`, **Required**):
  Please enter your name and contact information as the event host. This will ensure that we can contact you with event updates and confirmations.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, **Required**):
  In this section, you must choose whether you are registering to participate or observe the event. Selecting "Yes" means you are participating, while "No" means you are observing.
