# Landscaping Trade Show Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping Trade Show Registration form is used to collect information from exhibitors and attendees for event planning and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Exhibitor Details section with your company's information.
2. Enter the attendee details, including name, email, phone, and attendance status.
3. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Details (Exhibitor Details)**: 
  * This section collects information about your company participating in the trade show.
  * Please enter your company name, contact person, email, phone, and booth details.
* **Company (Company)**: 
  * Enter your company name.
  * This is a required field.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person)**: 
  * Enter the name of the contact person for your company.
  * This field is optional.
* **Email (Email)**: 
  * Enter your company email address.
  * This field is optional.
* **Phone (Phone)**: 
  * Enter your company's phone number.
  * This field is optional.
* **Booth Details (Booth Details)**: 
  * Describe your booth details, such as the size and location.
  * This field is optional.
* **Size (Size)**: 
  * Select the size of your booth.
  * Options: Small, Medium, Large.
  * This field is optional.
* **Number (Number)**: 
  * Enter the number of your booth.
  * This field is optional.
* **Company Name (Company Name)**: 
  * Enter your company's name.
  * This is a required field.
* **Address (Address)**: 
  * Enter your company's address.
  * This field is optional.
* **City (City)**: 
  * Enter the city of your company's location.
  * This field is optional.
* **State (State)**: 
  * Select the state where your company is located.
  * Options: CA, NY, Other.
  * This field is optional.
* **Zip (Zip)**: 
  * Enter your company's zip code.
  * This field is optional.
* **Country (Country)**: 
  * Enter the country of your company's location.
  * This field is optional.
* **Attendee Details (Attendee Details)**: 
  * This section collects information about the attendees from your company.
  * Please enter the attendee's name, email, phone, and attendance status.
* **Count (Count)**: 
  * Enter the number of attendees from your company.
  * This field is optional.
* **Name (Name)**: 
  * Enter the attendee's name.
  * This field is optional.
* **Email (Email)**: 
  * Enter the attendee's email address.
  * This field is optional.
* **Phone (Phone)**: 
  * Enter the attendee's phone number.
  * This field is optional.
* **Type (Type)**: 
  * Select the type of attendee.
  * Options: General, Exhibitor, Guest.
  * This field is optional.
* **Attendance (Attendance)**: 
  * Select the attendee's attendance status.
  * This field is optional.
* **Time (Time)**: 
  * Enter the time of the attendee's attendance.
  * This field is optional.
* **Notes (Notes)**: 
  * Add any additional notes or comments about the attendee.
  * This field is optional.
* **Submit (Submit)**: 
  * Review and submit your form once you've completed all the fields.
  * This field is not required.
* **Form ID (Form ID)**: 
  * This field is not used and can be ignored.
