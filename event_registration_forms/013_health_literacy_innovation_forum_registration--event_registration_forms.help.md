# Health Literacy Innovation Forum Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Literacy Innovation Forum Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees to provide a seamless experience for the event. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in the event details, such as event name, date, and time.
3. Provide your attendee details, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Indicate your preferences for the event, such as whether you will be attending, and if so, what specific sessions you are interested in.
5. Give consent for the event organizers to contact you.
6. Enter your email address and phone number to ensure we can reach out to you.
7. Provide your date of birth and time of arrival and departure.
8. Enter any additional details you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): This page will ask for basic event details such as event name, date, and time.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required/optional): This page will ask for your name, email, and phone number.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Here, you can indicate whether you will be attending, and if so, what specific sessions you are interested in.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required/optional): Please select whether you give consent for us to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This is your email address where we can reach out to you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): This is your date of birth.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter your time of arrival and departure.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes you would like to share with us.
* **Attendee Count** (`attendee_count`, number, required/optional): If applicable, indicate the number of attendees accompanying you.
* **Event Preferences** (`event_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your preferences for the event, if applicable.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, required/optional): Add any extra details that you think are relevant to your participation.
