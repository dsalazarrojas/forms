# Stadium Visitor Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stadium Visitor Check Out Form is designed to collect feedback and comments from visitors about their experience at an event at the stadium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in each section. Start by providing your contact information, including your name and email address. If you would like to be contacted, you can also add your phone number. You can then rate your overall experience as either Good or Fair. Add any additional comments about your visit, and select the event and venue that you attended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (1, text, required): This is where you enter your name and email address. If you'd like to add a phone number, you can do so here.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address here. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
* **Name** (3, text, required): Enter your name to help us identify you.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your phone number, this is the field to add it.
* **Rating** (5, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you rate your overall experience as Good or Fair.
* **Additional Comments** (7, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about your visit here.
* **Event** (8, select_one, optional): Select the event that you attended from the list.
* **Event Venue** (9, select_one, optional): Choose the venue where the event took place from the list.
* **Date** (10, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Time** (11, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Additional Comments** (12, note, optional): Provide any further comments or details about your visit.
