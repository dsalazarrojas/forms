# Hair Show Vendor Booth Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors who want to register for a hair show event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address and contact phone number for us to reach out to you.
3. Choose your booth size and type (Vendor or Non-Vendor).
4. Enter the booth number you want to reserve.
5. Provide the time you plan to set up and tear down your booth.
6. Enter the date and time of the show you want to participate in.
7. Pay the booth fee for your participation.
8. Upload your vendor license if you have one.
9. Fill in the show location and description for our records.
10. If you have any special requests, write them down in the special request field.
11. Add any additional information you think is relevant to your participation.
12. Finally, sign your vendor agreement by typing your name in the vendor signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Company name** (text, required): Enter your company's name as it will be listed on the show materials.
* **Vendor type** (select_one, required): Choose whether you are a vendor or non-vendor.
* **Booth number** (number, optional): Enter the number you want to reserve for your booth.
* **Booth size** (select_multiple, required): Choose the size of your booth (10x10, 10x20, 20x20, or 30x30).
* **Show name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the show you want to participate in (if different from the main show).
* **Booth setup time** (time, optional): Enter the time you plan to set up your booth before the show.
* **Booth tear down time** (time, optional): Enter the time you plan to tear down your booth after the show.
* **Contact person** (text, required): Enter the name of the person we can contact about your participation.
* **Contact email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Show date** (date, required): Enter the date of the show you want to participate in.
* **Show time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the show you want to participate in.
* **Booth fee** (number, required): Pay the booth fee for your participation.
* **Vendor License** (text, optional): Upload your vendor license if you have one.
* **Show location** (text, required): Enter the location of the show you want to participate in.
* **Show description** (note, optional): Enter a brief description of the show for our records.
* **Special request** (text, optional): Enter any special requests you have.
* **Vendor notes** (note, optional): Enter any notes about your participation or requirements.
* **Additional info** (text, optional): Add any additional information about your participation.
* **Vendor signature** (text, optional): Sign your participation agreement by typing your name.
* **Event manager notes** (text, optional): Enter any comments or notes for the event manager.
* **Event manager** (text, optional): Enter the name of the event manager who will be handling your participation.
