# digital_merchandising_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for a digital merchandising workshop. Please fill it out to provide your information and choose the sessions you'd like to attend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **workshop_info** field with a brief message (optional).
2. Provide your **attendee_name** by typing in your name.
3. Enter your valid **email** address.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone_number** field.
5. Provide your **attendee_address**.
6. Choose the **workshop_date** and **workshop_time** for the workshop you'd like to attend.
7. Select one of the available **select_workshop_sessions** options.
8. Choose the sessions you'd like to attend from the **session_1**, **session_2**, and **session_3** fields.
9. Select how many attendees will be joining you from the **attendees** field.
10. If you have any comments or notes, you can add them in the **comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **workshop_info**: A brief description of your attendance or any additional information about your participation. (text, optional)
* **attendee_name**: Your name as it appears on your ID or business cards. (text, required)
* **email**: Your company email address or contact information. (email, required)
* **phone_number**: Your phone number or contact information. (text, optional)
* **attendee_address**: Your company address or contact information. (text, optional)
* **workshop_date**: The date of the workshop you'd like to attend. (date, optional)
* **workshop_time**: The time of the workshop you'd like to attend. (time, optional)
* **select_workshop_sessions**: Choose one of the available sessions. (select_one, optional)
* **session_1**: Select any of the sessions listed. (select_multiple, required)
* **session_2**: Select any of the sessions listed. (select_multiple, required)
* **session_3**: Select any of the sessions listed. (select_multiple, required)
* **attendees**: How many attendees you'll be bringing with you. (number, optional)
* **comments**: Any additional comments or notes. (text, optional)
