# sustainability_reporting_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "sustainability_reporting_workshop_registration_form" is designed to collect participant information for a workshop focused on sustainability reporting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information for each field.
2. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_data`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred option from the drop-down menu.
	+ Choose the option that best represents your personal details.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
	+ This will be used for communication and workshop updates.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
	+ This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
	+ Use this field for any information you think might be helpful for the workshop organizers.
* **Event Preferences** (`event_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the drop-down menu.
	+ Choose your preferred options for the workshop.
* **Workshop Participants** (`workshop_participants`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from the drop-down menu.
	+ Choose the participants you wish to bring along.
* **Participant Diet** (`participant_diet`, `select_one`, optional): Select your dietary preference.
	+ Choose your dietary option (e.g., vegetarian, non-vegetarian, etc.).
* **Dietary Preferences** (`participant_diet_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your dietary preference.
	+ Choose your dietary option (e.g., yes, no).
* **T-Shirt Size** (`participant_t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select your T-shirt size.
	+ Choose your T-shirt size.
* **Dress Code** (`participant_dress_code`, `select_one`, optional): Select your dress code preference.
	+ Choose your dress code option (e.g., yes, no).
* **Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Select your attendance status.
	+ Choose your attendance option (e.g., will attend, will not attend, may attend).
* **Additional Comments** (`participant_additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
	+ Use this field for any information you think might be helpful for the workshop organizers.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select your agreement status.
	+ Choose your agreement option (e.g., agree, do not agree).
* **Agreement Comments** (`participant_agreement_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any comments or notes regarding your agreement.
	+ Use this field for any information you think might be helpful for the workshop organizers.
* **Additional Info** (`participant_additional_info`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information.
	+ Use this field for any information you think might be helpful for the workshop organizers.
