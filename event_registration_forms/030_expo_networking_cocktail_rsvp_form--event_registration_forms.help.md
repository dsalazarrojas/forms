# Expo Networking Cocktail RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for an event, providing essential details such as event name, date, time, RSVP status, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event name in the "Event Name" field.
2. Select the event date from the calendar in the "Event Date" field.
3. Choose the event time in the "Event Time" field.
4. Select your attendance status in the "RSVP Status" field: "Attending" or "Not Attending".
5. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
6. Add your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name (text)
Enter the name of the event you're registering for.

### Event Date (date)
Choose the date of the event from the calendar.

### Event Time (time)
Select the time of the event.

### RSVP Status (select_one)
Choose "Attending" if you're attending the event, or "Not Attending" if you can't make it.

### Contact Info (text)
Enter any contact information you'd like to provide, such as your name or any other details.

### Phone (text)
Enter your phone number.

### Email (email)
Enter your email address.

### RSVP Note (note)
Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
