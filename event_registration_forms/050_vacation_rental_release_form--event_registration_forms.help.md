# Vacation Rental Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Rental Release Form is used to collect information from guests renting a vacation property. This form helps us understand the guest's details and ensure that they are aware of the rental terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's **Guest Information** in the first page.
2. Read and agree to the **Rental Terms** in the second page.
3. Provide your **Email Address** in the third page.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information (text)**: Enter your name and other identification information as a guest.
* **Rental Terms (text)**: Please read and agree to the rental terms and conditions. If you do not agree, please do not submit the form.
* **Agreement (text)**: This field is not clearly defined in the provided YAML, so we'll assume it's meant to collect any additional agreement information.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for us to reach you.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll keep your information confidential and secure.
