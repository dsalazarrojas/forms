# Esthetician Trade Show Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees for the Esthetician Trade Show. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about attendees' participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Attendee Details" section.
2. Select your preferred booth in the "Booth Preference" section.
3. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section.
4. Choose your attendee type in the "Attendee Type" section.
5. Make any special requests or add any additional comments in the "Special Requests" section (optional).
6. Provide your company name and contact person's details (if applicable) in the "Company Name" and "Contact Person" sections (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (Required):
	+ This section is where you provide your personal information, including your name, title, and any other relevant details.
* **Booth Preference**:
	+ Select the booth where you would like to be located. You can choose one or multiple booths, depending on your preference.
* **Payment Info** (Required):
	+ Enter your payment information to secure your registration. This may include your payment method and payment amount.
* **Attendee Type** (Required):
	+ Choose your attendee type from the available options: Student, Professional, or Other.
* **Special Requests** (Optional):
	+ Add any special requests or comments you have regarding your participation in the event.
* **Company Name** (Optional):
	+ Provide your company name if you are representing a company.
* **Contact Person** (Optional):
	+ Enter the name of the contact person from your company.
* **Job Title** (Optional):
	+ Add your job title within your company.
* **Tips**

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers or event support team.
* The form is designed to be completed in a reasonable amount of time, so please be patient and complete it accordingly.
* You can save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.
