# dance_association_annual_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for dance association's annual tournament entry. It's meant to gather essential details for participants and their teams.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your title: Enter your name as it will be displayed in the tournament.
2. Enter your name: Enter your name to register for the tournament.
3. Enter team name: If you're registering as a team, enter your team name here.
4. Choose a category: Select the dance category you wish to compete in (e.g., Dance, Hip, Ball, etc.).
5. Choose your level: Select your skill level (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, etc.).
6. Choose your registration type: Select whether you're registering individually or as a team.
7. If registering as a team, select team registration type: Choose whether your team is a competitive or non-competitive team.
8. Enter number of participants: Enter the number of participants in your team.
9. Enter additional notes: Provide any additional information about your team or performance.
10. Enter emergency contact name: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
11. Enter emergency contact phone: Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
12. Enter emergency contact email: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
13. Enter medical condition: If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
14. Enter medical information: Provide any additional medical information for emergency purposes.
15. Sign the medical release: Enter your signature to acknowledge the medical release.
