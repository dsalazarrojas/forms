# Tour Guides Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Guides Club Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential and existing members of the club. This form helps us understand your preferences, experience, and interests, which enables us to provide personalized support and resources to help you get the most out of your membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you regarding club updates and events.
3. Provide your Phone number for emergency contact purposes.
4. Select your Membership Type (Tour Guide or Tour Participant) to indicate your level of involvement with the club.
5. Choose your Experience Level (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced) to help us understand your skill level and expertise.
6. Select the languages you speak (English, French, Spanish) to help us tailor our support and resources to your needs.
7. Provide your Tour Preferences (optional) to share your interests and favorite destinations.
8. Share your Travel Preferences (optional) to help us understand your travel habits and preferences.
9. Confirm your agreement with the Membership Terms by selecting "Yes" or "No".
10. If you need to provide emergency contact information, please fill in the Emergency Contact field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (3): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding club updates and events.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Membership Type** (5): Select "Tour Guide" if you are an experienced guide or leader, or "Tour Participant" if you are an enthusiast who wants to join the club.
* **Experience Level** (6): Choose your level of experience and expertise in travel and exploration (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Language Spoken** (7): Select the languages you speak and are comfortable communicating in (English, French, Spanish).
* **Tour Preferences** (8): If you have any specific tour-related preferences or interests, please list them here (optional).
* **Travel Preferences** (9): If you have any travel-related preferences or habits, please list them here (optional).
* **Membership Terms** (10): Confirm that you have read and agree to the Membership Terms and Conditions by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Emergency Contact** (11): If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and phone number (optional).
