# cultural_festival_performance_slot_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for requesting a performance slot in a cultural festival. To ensure relevance and non-duplication, please only submit requests for the festival you're associated with. If you've already submitted a request, kindly check your previous submission before resubmitting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information, marked as "required" in the field description.
2. Review each field for any specific instructions or requirements.
3. Double-check your submission before clicking "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Festival Name (`Festival Name`, text, required: false)

*   Enter the name of the festival you're submitting a request for.

### Contact Name (`contact name`, text, required: false)

*   Enter your name as the contact person for this submission.

### Email (`Email`, email, required: false)

*   Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Performance Type (`Performance Type`, select_one, required: false)

*   Choose the type of performance you'd like to submit for:
    *   `solo`
    *   `group`
    *   `duo`
    *   `band`

### Preferred Time Slot (`Preferred Time Slot`, time, required: false)

*   Select a time slot for your performance. Please note that specific time slots may vary depending on the festival schedule.

### Technical Needs (`Technical Needs`, text, required: false)

*   Describe any technical needs or requirements for your performance.

### Application Details (`Application Details`, text, required: false)

*   Provide any additional details about your application.

### Contact Number (`Contact Number`, text, required: false)

*   Enter your contact number for any follow-up communication.

### Assigned Tool (`Assigned Tool`, select_one, required: false)

*   Choose the tool assigned to your performance:
    *   `chatjimmy`
    *   `chat bob`
    *   `chat jim`

### Festival Date (`Festival Date`, date, required: false)

*   Enter the date of the festival you're submitting for.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review all fields carefully before submission.
*   Double-check your submission for any errors.
*   If you're unsure about any field, contact the festival organizers for clarification.
