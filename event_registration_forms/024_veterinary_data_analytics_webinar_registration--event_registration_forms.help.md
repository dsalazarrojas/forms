# veterinary_data_analytics_webinar_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration details from attendees for the veterinary data analytics webinar. Complete this form to secure your spot and provide essential information to help us tailor the webinar to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.
2. For fields with multiple options, select all relevant choices.
3. Double-check your details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main** (1, text, optional): This is the main page of the form where you can enter any additional information or comments.
* **Attendee Details** (2, text, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself, including your name and any relevant background information.
* **Role** (3, select_one, optional): Select your role in the veterinary industry (e.g., Veterinarian, Data Analyst, Student).
	+ Options: Veterinarian, Data Analyst, Student
* **Goals** (4, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your goals for attending the webinar (e.g., Learn about data analytics, Improve data visualization skills).
	+ Options: Learn about data analytics, Improve data visualization skills, Network with professionals
* **Consent** (5, note, optional): This field is not applicable in this form.
* **Email** (6, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on webinar details.
* **Contact Number** (7, text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach out with any updates.
* **Address** (8, text, optional): Enter your address for logistics purposes, if required.
* **Company** (9, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter its name for registration and marketing purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure you are honest and accurate with your submission to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requirements, please include them in the "Attendee Details" field.
* Our team will review and process your registration, and you will receive a confirmation email with webinar details once processed.
