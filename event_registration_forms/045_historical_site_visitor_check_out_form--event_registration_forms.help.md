# Historical Site Visitor Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from historical site visitors when they check out. It helps us to track visitor activity, note any issues or concerns, and gather feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
3. Select the Date and Time of your visit.
4. Provide Feedback about your experience at the historical site.
5. Indicate whether you had Site Access during your visit (Yes/No).
6. Add any additional Notes about your visit, if necessary.
7. Enter your Phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
8. Select the Category of your visit (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (id: 1) 
* **First Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name (id: 2) 
* **Last Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email (id: 3) 
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Date (id: 4) 
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Select the date of your visit.

### Time (id: 5) 
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Select the time of your visit.

### Feedback (id: 6) 
* **Feedback** (`text`, optional): Provide any comments or suggestions about your experience at the site.

### Site Access (id: 7) 
* **Site Access** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you had access to the site during your visit (Yes/No).

### Notes (id: 8) 
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your visit.

### Phone (id: 9) 
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.

### Category (id: 10) 
* **Category** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate the category of your visit (Yes/No).
