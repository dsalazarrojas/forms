# Fisheries Worker Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information from fisheries workers for check-in purposes. It collects general information, worker details, emergency contacts, job specifics, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the General Info section with your personal details.
2.  Provide your worker data, including any relevant numbers.
3.  Enter your emergency contacts' names and contact information.
4.  Select your job details, including type and status.
5.  Enter your shift start and end times, location, and vehicle information.
6.  Add any additional notes or comments if needed.
7.  Fill in your check-in and check-out dates and times.
8.  Finally, choose your job status and type from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Info (General Info)**: This section collects your personal details, such as your name, email, and phone number.
*   **Worker Data (Worker Data)**: Enter any relevant numbers related to your work or identity.
*   **Emergency Contacts (Emergency Contacts)**: Add the names and contact information of your emergency contacts.
*   **Job Details (Job Details)**: Select your job type and status from the options provided.
*   **Shift Info (Shift Info)**: Enter your shift start and end times, and the length of your shift.
*   **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your shift.
*   **Email (Email)**: Enter your contact email.
*   **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Emergency Phone (Emergency Phone)**: Enter an emergency contact phone number.
*   **Emergency Email (Emergency Email)**: Enter an emergency contact email.
*   **Manager Info (Manager Info)**: Enter your manager's contact information.
*   **Shift Start (Shift Start)**: Enter your shift start time.
*   **Shift End (Shift End)**: Enter your shift end time.
*   **Location (Location)**: Enter the location where you will be working.
*   **Notes 2 (Notes 2)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your shift.
*   **Vehicle (Vehicle)**: Enter any vehicle-related information.
*   **Shift Length (Shift Length)**: Enter the length of your shift.
*   **Check In (Check In)**: Enter your check-in date.
*   **Check Out (Check Out)**: Enter your check-out date.
*   **Job Status (Job Status)**: Select your current job status from the options provided.
*   **Job Type (Job Type)**: Select the type of job you are working on.
*   **Weather (Weather)**: Select the current weather conditions.

Remember to fill in all required fields and ensure accurate information for a smooth check-in process.
