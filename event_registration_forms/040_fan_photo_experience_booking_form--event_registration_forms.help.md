# Fan Photo Experience Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fan Photo Experience Booking Form is a collection of information from fans interested in attending a photo experience event. This form helps event organizers manage event attendance and communication with attendees.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the event date and time you wish to attend.
2.  Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can keep in touch with you about event updates and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Date
*   **Event Date**: Select the event date you wish to attend. Please note that selecting a specific date is required for event planning purposes.
### Event Time
*   **Event Time**: Select the event time you wish to attend. Please note that selecting a specific time is required for event planning purposes.
### Fan Name
*   **Fan Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or your preferred name to be called during the event.
### Email
*   **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about event updates, reminders, and details.
### Phone
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about event updates, reminders, and details.

## Tips
*   Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication and event management.
*   If you are having trouble selecting a date or time due to conflicting events, please contact us to discuss alternatives.
*   All information collected will be used for event purposes only and will be treated with the utmost respect and care.
