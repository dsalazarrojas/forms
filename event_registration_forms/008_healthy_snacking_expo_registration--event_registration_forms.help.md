# Healthy Snacking Expo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for registration to the Healthy Snacking Expo. The goal is to collect information from attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Fill in the General Information page with your name and any other details you'd like to share.
2. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
3. Share your dietary information, including any restrictions you have.
4. Select the sessions that interest you the most.
5. Finally, review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, not required): This is the first page of the form. You can fill in any general information you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please provide your name as you would like to be referred to during the event.
* **Surname** (`surname`, text, not required): If you have a preferred surname, you can provide it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for communication purposes. This will help us keep you updated on event information and details.
* **Dietary Information** (`dietary_info`, text, not required): Describe any dietary restrictions or requirements you have. This will help us ensure there are suitable food options for you during the event.
* **Dietary restrictions** (`dietary_info_dietary_restrictions`, select multiple, not required): Select any dietary restrictions you have from the provided options (Label 1, Label 2, Label 3). You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Session interests** (`session_interests`, select multiple, not required): Choose the sessions that interest you the most from the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3). You can select one or multiple options.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Review your form before submitting it. Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy.

# Tips

* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please choose the relevant options to ensure we can accommodate your needs.
* Select only the sessions that genuinely interest you the most to help us plan the event accordingly.
* Review your form before submitting it to catch any errors or inaccuracies.
