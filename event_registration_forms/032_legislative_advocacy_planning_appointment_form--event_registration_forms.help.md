# legislative_advocacy_planning_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for event planning and advocacy activities related to legislative meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Provide the name of the organization hosting the event or meeting.
2. Enter the date of the event or meeting.
3. Enter the time of the meeting.
4. Enter the location of the meeting.
5. Specify the duration of the meeting.
6. List the agenda items to be discussed in the meeting.
7. Identify the legislative officer (Director or Assistant) involved in the meeting.
8. Enter your contact email address.
9. Enter your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization hosting the event or meeting.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event or meeting in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the meeting in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes or hours.
* **Agenda Item** (`agenda_item`, text, required/optional): List the agenda items to be discussed in the meeting.
* **Legislative Officer** (`legislative_officer`, select_one, required/optional): Select the title of the legislative officer (Director or Assistant) involved in the meeting.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

**Tips**

* Make sure to enter the date in the correct format (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
* Enter the time in the format (`HH:MM`).
* Select the correct legislative officer involved in the meeting.
* Double-check the meeting location and agenda items to ensure accuracy.
* Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
