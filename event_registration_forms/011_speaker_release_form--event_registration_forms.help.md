# Speaker Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event speakers, such as their name, contact details, and agreement to event terms and conditions. It is used for internal purposes to ensure that speakers understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details about the event you are participating in, including the title and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your name as the speaker.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you with further details or instructions.
4. Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
5. Describe the content of your presentation or session.
6. Choose whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the event. Please select "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" if you agree, or "I do not agree to the terms and conditions" if you do not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: A description of the event you are participating in. For example, "Conference on AI and Machine Learning".
* **Speaker Name**: Your name as the speaker, as it will appear on event materials and promotional materials.
* **Speaker Email**: Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Speaker Phone**: Your phone number for quick contact.
* **Content Details**: A brief description of the content you will be presenting, such as "Talk: 'Introduction to Deep Learning'".
* **Terms And Conditions**: This field is not required but it's recommended to agree to the terms and conditions to ensure you understand the event's rules and regulations. Please select "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" if you agree, or "I do not agree to the terms and conditions" if you do not.
* **Event Terms And Conditions**: Not required but it's a field to describe any specific rules or requirements related to the event.
