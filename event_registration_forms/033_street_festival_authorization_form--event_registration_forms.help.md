# Street Festival Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Street Festival Authorization Form is used for event registration for local street festivals. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure smooth processing of your event application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the event date and time on the first page
* Enter the name and details of the event
* Add the contact information of the authorized official and event notes
* Select the authorized official from the list of options
* Fill out any other required fields

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Date** (1, date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar or provide the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Event Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the event, e.g., "Summer Street Fest".
- **Event Address** (3, text, required): Enter the address where the event will take place.
- **Event Time** (4, time, required): Select the time of the event from the clock or provide the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Contact Person** (5, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Title** (6, text, required): Enter the title of the contact person, e.g., "Event Coordinator".
- **Contact Organization** (7, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or company hosting the event.
- **Contact Phone** (8, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (9, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Authorized Official** (10, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate who will be the authorized official for the event.
- **Event Notes** (11, note, required): Enter any additional information about the event.
- **Form Issuer** (12, text, required): Enter the name or title of the person or entity submitting the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the event management agency for clarification.
- Submit the form with all required fields filled out to ensure smooth processing.
