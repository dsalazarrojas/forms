# self_love_experience_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self_love_experience_rsvp form is a simple online form designed to collect information from event attendees to streamline event organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the self_love_experience_rsvp form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
6. Choose the start time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
7. Choose the end time of the event in the "Event Time End" field.
8. Select your RSVP status ("True", "False", or "Maybe") for the event in the "RSVP Status" field.
9. Provide any special requests or comments in the "Special Requests" field.
10. Confirm your RSVP status ("True", "False", or "Maybe") again in the "RSVP Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Event Date** (Date): Select the date of the event you are RSVP'ing for.
* **Event Time** (Time): Choose the start time of the event.
* **Event Time End** (Time): Choose the end time of the event.
* **RSVP Status** (Select One): Choose your response to the event, "True", "False", or "Maybe".
* **Special Requests** (Text): Provide any special requests or comments about the event.
* **RSVP Status** (Select One): Confirm your RSVP status, "True", "False", or "Maybe", for accuracy and clarity.
* **First Name 2** (Text): This field is currently not explained in the original YAML.
