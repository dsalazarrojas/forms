## Habitat Build Volunteer Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register volunteers to take time off from their scheduled work hours at Habitat Build. It helps the organization to keep track of volunteer availability and schedule conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Organization Name (required)
	* Volunteer Name (required)
	* Time Off Date (required)
	* Time Off Time (required)
	* Email (required)
	* Confirmation (required)
2. You can optionally select one or more organizations you are associated with (Select Organizations)
3. You can optionally select one or more volunteer roles you have at Habitat Build (Select Volunteer Roles)
4. If you have any other roles or affiliations with Habitat Build, you can add them in the Notes section
5. If you have a reason for taking time off, you can optionally enter it in the Reason for Time Off field

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Organization Name (1)
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization, as this will be used to identify your affiliation with Habitat Build.

### Volunteer Name (2)
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your volunteer profile.

### Time Off Date (3)
* **Time Off Date**: Select the date you wish to take off from your volunteer work hours.

### Time Off Time (4)
* **Time Off Time**: Select the time you will be taking off from your volunteer work hours.

### Reason for Time Off (5)
* **Reason for Time Off**: (Optional) Enter a brief explanation for why you are taking time off. This can be a personal note or a note for other volunteers.

### Email (6)
* **Email**: Enter your email address as it is associated with your volunteer profile.

### Phone (7)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This can be used by Habitat Build staff to communicate with you regarding your time off request.

### Select Organizations (8)
* **Select Organizations**: You are affiliated with (Optional) Select the organizations you are associated with. This will help Habitat Build staff understand your context and schedule conflicts.

### Select Volunteer Roles (9)
* **Select Volunteer Roles**: You have at Habitat Build (Required) Select the roles you have at Habitat Build, such as Builder, Organizer, or Other.

### Notes (10)
* **Notes**: Additional information or context for your time off request (Optional)

### Confirmation (11)
* **Confirmation**: Enter a confirmation that your time off request is accurate and complete.
