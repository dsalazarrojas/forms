# Wedding Floral Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for successful wedding floral delivery. It helps to ensure that the flowers and other decorations are delivered correctly and on time, without any issues or last-minute changes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your contact and name details under "User Info".
2. Choose your delivery preferences from the options provided under "Delivery Preferences".
3. Provide detailed information about your delivery under "Delivery Details".
4. Let us know if you require any payment information under "Payment Info".
5. Fill in your contact details under "Contact Info".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required): This field is used to collect your name and contact information. Please fill in your name and email address.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to choose whether you prefer morning or afternoon delivery. Select all that apply.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): This field is used to provide any extra details about your delivery, such as parking instructions or a specific delivery time.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, select_one, optional): This field indicates whether you will be paying for the delivery or not. Please select "Yes" if you are paying and "No" if someone else is paying.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide your phone number or any other contact information.
