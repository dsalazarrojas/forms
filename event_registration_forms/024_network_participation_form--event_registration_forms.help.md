# Network Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Network Participation Form is designed to capture relevant details about a network participation event, which helps event organizers and participants understand the scope and objectives of the network participation event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the network participation event.
2. **What kind of network**: Select the type of network (Networks, Partners, or Other) to indicate the nature of the network participation event.
3. **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization related to the network participation event.
4. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person to contact for further inquiries.
5. **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person or organization.
6. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
7. **Address**: Enter the address of the organization or contact person.
8. **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the organization or contact person's address.
9. **Network Participation Terms**: Select 'True' if you agree to the network participation terms or 'False' if you do not. Select 'Maybe' if you are unsure.
10. **Network Participation Terms Agreement**: Select 'I Agree' if you agree to the network participation terms.
11. **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about the network participation event.
