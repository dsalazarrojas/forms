# Citrus Industry Meeting Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for attendees to register for the Citrus Industry Meeting. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure all required fields (marked as required) are filled out.
- Review your registration carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 2, <type:text>, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (id: 3, <type:text>, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email Address** (id: 4, <type:email, required): Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 5, <type:text, required): Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Company or Organization Name** (id: 7, <type:text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title** (id: 8, <type:text, required): Enter your job title within your company or organization.
* **Company Type** (id: 9, <type:select_one, required): Select the type of your company or organization from the options provided.
* **Years in Citrus Industry** (id: 10, <type:number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of years you have spent in the citrus industry.
* **Meeting Date** (id: 12, <type:date, required): Select the date of the meeting you will be attending.
* **Meeting Location** (id: 13, <type:text, required): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Attendance Type** (id: 14, <type:select_one, required): Select the type of attendance you will have (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Topics of Interest** (id: 16, <type:select_multiple, required): Select all topics that interest you from the list provided.
* **Preferred Session Times** (id: 18, <type:select_multiple, optional): Select all session times that interest you from the list provided.
* **Interested in Networking Events** (id: 19, <type:select_one, optional): Select your level of interest in networking events.
* **Registration Type** (id: 21, <type:select_one, required): Select the type of registration you are interested in (full conference, single day, etc.).
* **Number of Attendees in Your Group** (id: 22, <type:number, optional): If attending with a group, enter the number of attendees.
* **Meals Included** (id: 24, <type:select_multiple, optional): Select which meals you will be attending from the list provided.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (id: 25, <type:text, optional): If applicable, enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (id: 27, <type:text, optional): If applicable, enter any special accommodations you may need.
* **Willingness to Present or Share Information** (id: 28, <type:select_one, optional): Select your willingness to present or share information.
* **Suggested Presentation Topic** (id: 29, <type:text, optional): If willing to present, suggest a topic you are interested in.

**Tips**

- Ensure you review your registration carefully before submitting.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, please enter them in the "Additional Information" section.
- If you have any difficulties with the form, contact the meeting organizers for assistance.
