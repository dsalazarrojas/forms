# Wedding Show Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees of a wedding show, for the purpose of entering them into a giveaway drawing for prizes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information.
2. Provide your contact information.
3. Indicate your interest in attending the wedding show.
4. Choose a gift if you're interested.
5. Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
6. Select your time of visit.
7. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
8. Provide your phone number.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.
10. Enter the vendors you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the wedding show materials.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name and contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* **Attendee Interest** (`interest_selection`, select multiple, required: false)
	+ Select all the interests that apply to you.
		+ Yes
		+ No
* **Gift Choice** (`gift_choice`, select one, required: false)
	+ If you're interested in receiving a gift, select "Yes" or "No".
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required: false)
	+ Select the time you plan to visit the wedding show.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false)
	+ Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Vendors** (`vendor_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the vendors you are interested in.
