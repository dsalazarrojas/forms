# Board Member Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a board member training session. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the `Participant name` field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the `Email address` field, as this will be used for sending confirmation and materials.
3. Select the `Training session` that you would like to attend from the dropdown menu.
4. If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them in the `Dietary restrictions` field.
5. If you have any accessibility requirements, enter them in the `Accessibility requirements` field.
6. Indicate if payment is required for the training session.
7. Enter any emergency contact information in the `Emergency contact` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant name** (`Participant name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. 
- **Email address** (`Email address`, `email`, required): A valid email address that will be used for sending confirmation and materials.
- **Training session** (`Training session`, `select_one`, required): Select the training session that you are interested in. Please note that only one session can be selected. 
- **Dietary restrictions** (`Dietary restrictions`, `text`, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions that you may have, for example, vegetarian or gluten-free. 
- **Accessibility requirements** (`Accessibility requirements`, `text`, optional): Enter any accessibility requirements that you may have, for example, hearing or visual impairments. 
- **Payment required** (`Payment required`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if payment is required for the training session. 
- **Emergency contact** (`Emergency contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are registering for a session that is relevant to your role and position. 
- Only one session can be selected. 
- Please enter your emergency contact's name and phone number if you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
