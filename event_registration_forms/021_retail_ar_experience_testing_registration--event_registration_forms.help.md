# Retail AR Experience Testing Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in a retail AR experience testing session. It helps us collect valuable data for evaluating the quality and effectiveness of our augmented reality technology in real-world scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your participant email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Enter your participant phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4. Confirm your consent to participate in the testing session by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Consent" dropdown menu.
5. Provide any additional participant details in the "Participant Details" field.
6. Select a date and time for the test session in the "Test Session Date" and "Test Session Time" fields respectively.
7. Enter the number of sessions you would like to schedule for yourself in the "Schedule a Session" field.
8. If you have any specific preferences for your test session, you can enter them in the "Participant Preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to during the testing session.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your testing session.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you about any last-minute changes or updates.
- **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" to confirm your participation in the testing session, or "No" if you are no longer interested.
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): If you have any specific details or requests, such as accessibility needs or medical conditions, please let us know here.
- **Test Session Date** (`test_session_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you are available for the testing session.
- **Test Session Time** (`test_session_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you are available for the testing session.
- **Schedule a Session** (`schedule_a_session`, number, required: false): Enter the number of sessions you would like to participate in, if applicable.
- **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, text, required: false): If you have any specific preferences or requirements for your testing session, such as a preferred language or accessibility needs, please let us know here.
