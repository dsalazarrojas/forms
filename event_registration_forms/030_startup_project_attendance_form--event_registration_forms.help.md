### Startup Project Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Startup Project Attendance Form is used to gather information from participants in a startup project. This form helps project coordinators to collect important details about each participant, including their name, contact information, project affiliation, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please fill out the form completely and accurately.**
2. **Review the consent form and indicate your consent to participate.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false)
    This is the name of the participant. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false)
    This is the email address associated with your participant account. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false)
    This is the name of the project you are participating in. If you are unsure, please ask your project coordinator for the project name.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required: false)
    Please enter your goals and objectives for participating in this project. This will help project coordinators to understand your motivations and expectations.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Please indicate your consent to participate in the project by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false)
    This is your contact phone number. Please enter your phone number in the format: `123-456-7890`.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false)
    This is the date you plan to start working on the project. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false)
    This is the time you plan to start working on the project. Please enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.

## Tips
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your project coordinator or the project team.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need help with formatting or inputting information, please do not hesitate to ask.
