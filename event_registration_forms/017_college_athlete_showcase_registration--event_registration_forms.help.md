# College Athlete Showcase Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Athlete Showcase Registration form is designed to collect information from athletes who want to participate in the event, allowing coaches, scouts, and other stakeholders to discover and evaluate their skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact details.
2. Enter your academic background, including your school and major.
3. Select your primary sport and position.
4. Enter your athletic statistics and achievements.
5. Select your showcase preferences.
6. Enter your emergency contact information.
7. Give media release consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`athlete_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`athlete_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`athlete_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone Number** (`athlete_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification.
* **Current School or College** (`current_school`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current school or college.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year you plan to graduate.
* **Primary Sport** (`sport`, `select_one`, required): Select the sport you are primarily involved in.
* **Position or Role** (`position`, `text`, required): Enter your primary position or role in your sport.
* **Jersey Number** (`jersey_number`, `number`, optional): Enter your jersey number, if applicable.
* **Height - feet and inches** (`height`, `text`, required): Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Weight - lbs** (`weight`, `number`, required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Current GPA** (`gpa`, `number`, required): Enter your current GPA on a 4.0 scale.
* **Career Statistics Highlights** (`career_statistics`, `text`, required): Enter key statistics, records, or achievements.
* **Awards and Honors Received** (`awards_honors`, `text`, optional): Enter any awards or honors you have received.
* **Notable Athletic Achievements** (`athletic_achievements`, `text`, optional): Enter any notable athletic achievements.
* **Leadership Roles or Team Positions** (`leadership_roles`, `text`, optional): Enter any leadership roles or positions held.
* **Academic Major or Field of Study** (`academic_major`, `text`, required): Enter your academic major or field of study.
* **Community Service or Volunteer Hours** (`community_service`, `number`, optional): Enter any community service or volunteer hours you have completed.
* **Showcase Preferences** (`showcase_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the showcase preferences for your athlete profile.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Contact Phone** (`parent_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Media Release Consent** (`media_release`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to allow photos and video of your athlete.
