# Event Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants interested in sponsoring events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Be accurate and honest in providing your personal and business information.
3. Use the correct format for the "credit card expiration month" and "credit card expiration year" fields (e.g., 1 for January, 2 for February, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsorship Application**:
	+ This is the main section for your application.
	+ Fill out this section as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
* **Applicant name**:
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	+ This field is optional but recommended for identification purposes.
* **Applicant phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Applicant email**:
	+ Enter your valid email address.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Applicant address**:
	+ Enter your address as it appears on your identification.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Credit card number**:
	+ Enter your credit card number for payment purposes.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Credit card CVV**:
	+ Enter your credit card CVV (security code) for payment purposes.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Credit card expiration month**:
	+ Select the month your credit card expires from the options provided (e.g., 1 for January, 2 for February, etc.).
	+ This field is optional.
* **Credit card expiration year**:
	+ Select the year your credit card expires from the options provided.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Applicant business name**:
	+ If you are applying on behalf of a business, enter the business name.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Business email**:
	+ Enter your business email address.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Business phone**:
	+ Enter your business phone number.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Job title**:
	+ Enter your job title at the business.
	+ This field is optional.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form. Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with your application.
