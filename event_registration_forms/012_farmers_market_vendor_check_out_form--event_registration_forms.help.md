# farmers_market_vendor_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The farmers market vendor checkout form is designed for farmers market vendors to accurately record and report their produce sales and other transactions. This form helps ensure that all sales are accounted for and reported to the market administrators, promoting transparency and efficient inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Fill out all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Details (text)**: Enter your name and other vendor details as required.
* **Payment (text)**: Enter the payment details for the transaction.
* **Date/Time (text)**: Enter the date and time of the transaction.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the transaction.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address to enable communication and updates.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for communication and contact purposes.
* **Vendor Email (email)**: Enter the email address where you can be reached by the market administration for important updates and notifications.
* **Note**: This field is for any additional notes or comments about the transaction. 

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any delays or issues with your transaction processing.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the market administration for assistance.
