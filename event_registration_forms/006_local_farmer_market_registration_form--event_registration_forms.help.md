# Local Farmer Market Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from farmers participating in local farmer markets. It gathers essential details such as farmer, market, and event information to facilitate efficient and accurate registration processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are not already registered for this market by verifying with the market manager. If you are unsure, it's best to double-check before proceeding.
2.  Fill in your personal details in the designated fields, including:
    *   Farmer Name
    *   Phone (if applicable)
    *   Email (if applicable)
3.  Provide your market-related information:
    *   Market Name
    *   Market Id (if applicable)
    *   Vendor Name
    *   Vendor Id (if applicable)
4.  Specify event details:
    *   Event Date
    *   Event Time
5.  Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Farmer Name** (`farmer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a farmer.
*   **Market Id** (`market_id`, number, optional): If you have a market-specific ID, enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Market Name** (`market_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the market where you will be participating.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and communication.
*   **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Enter your vendor name or business name.
*   **Vendor Id** (`vendor_id`, number, optional): If you have a vendor ID, enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Specify the date of the event you will be participating in.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information regarding your participation in the market event.
