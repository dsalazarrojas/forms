# Nature Creative Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nature Creative Workshop Registration form is used to gather information about a workshop on nature-focused topics. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately to ensure that all necessary details are captured for the successful organization and execution of the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the workshop name and other basic details.
2. Provide participant information, including name, email, and any other relevant details.
3. Specify the workshop date and time.
4. Select the workshop type (Nature-focused or Other).
5. Choose accommodation preferences (On-site, Off-site, or No preference).
6. Select the workshop location.
7. Specify participant requirements and level.
8. Select participant interests.
9. Enter the workshop capacity.
10. Enter any other participant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in the name of the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Specify the date of the workshop.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required/optional): Specify the start time of the workshop.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Specify the end date of the workshop.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required/optional): Specify the end time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the workshop duration in hours.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether the workshop is Nature-focused or Other.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (`accommodation_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the accommodation preferences for the workshop (On-site, Off-site, or No preference).
* **Other Accommodation** (`accommodation_other`, `text`, required/optional): If necessary, specify other accommodation preferences.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the workshop location.
* **Participant Requirements** (`participant_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the participant requirements (English, Spanish, or Other).
* **Participant Level** (`participant_level`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the participant level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Workshop Capacity** (`workshop_capacity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the workshop capacity.
* **Participant Interests** (`participant_interests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the participant interests (Nature, Conservation, Sustainability, Wildlife, or Other).
* **Workshop Interests** (`workshop_interests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the workshop interests (Nature, Conservation, Sustainability, Wildlife, or Other).
* **Participant Attended** (`participant_attended`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of events the participant has attended.
* **Other Events Attended** (`participant_attended_other`, `text`, required/optional): If necessary, specify other events attended.
* **Participant Recommended** (`participant_recommended`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of workshops the participant has recommended.
* **Other Workshops Recommended** (`participant_recommended_other`, `text`, required/optional): If necessary, specify other workshops recommended.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any comments about the participant.
* **Workshop Facilitator** (`workshop_facilitator`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the workshop facilitator.
* **Output** (`output`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether to send an email to participants or not.
* **Other** (`other`, `text`, required/optional): If necessary, specify other workshop-related details.
