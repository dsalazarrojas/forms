# Educational Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students participating in the Educational Exchange program. This information will be used to prepare for and execute the event.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out each field accurately and completely.
*   Make sure to check for any existing registrations before submitting this form.
*   If you need help with any field or have any questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Info (1)**: This is the first page of the form where you will enter your student ID (if applicable) and other basic student information.
*   **Student Name (2)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Student Email (3)**: Enter your official email address associated with your student account.
*   **Alternate Email (4)**: If you have an alternate email address, please provide it here. This should be used for communication if the primary email is not accessible.
*   **Student Phone (5)**: Enter your student phone number for easy contact.
*   **Student Birthdate (6)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Student Program (7)**: Select your program of study from the options provided.
*   **Alternate Program (8)**: Select your alternate program of study from the options provided.
*   **Institution Information (9)**: Enter any relevant information about your institution, such as your student ID number or student association.
*   **Student ID (10)**: Enter your student ID number if applicable.
*   **Student Address (11)**: Enter your current address as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Student City (12)**: Enter the city you are currently in.
*   **Student State (13)**: Enter the state you are currently in.
*   **Student Zip (14)**: Enter the zip code of your current location.
*   **Institution ID (15)**: Enter your institution ID number if applicable.
*   **Institution Name (16)**: Enter your institution's name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Institution Address (17)**: Enter your institution's address.
*   **Institution City (18)**: Enter the city your institution is located in.
*   **Institution State (19)**: Enter the state your institution is located in.
*   **Institution Zip (20)**: Enter the zip code of your institution's location.
*   **Program Information (21)**: Enter any additional information about your program of study.
*   **Start Date (22)**: If applicable, enter the start date of your program or event.
*   **End Date (23)**: If applicable, enter the end date of your program or event.
*   **Event Information (24)**: Enter any additional details about the event you are attending or participating in.
