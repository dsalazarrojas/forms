# Mental Health Hotline Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the mental health hotline training session. Please fill out the form below to provide your details and choose your desired training session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please ensure you have the necessary authorization to register for the training session.
* Fill out all required fields, marked with \*.
* Choose your desired training session from the options provided.
* Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: \* (text) 
Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: \* (text) 
Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date Of Birth**: \* (date) 
Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email**: \* (email) 
Enter your official email address associated with your organization.
* **Phone**: (text) 
Enter your organizational phone number, if necessary.
* **Organization**: \* (text) 
Enter the name of your organization.
* **Position**: \* (text) 
Enter your position within the organization.
* **Role**: \* (select_one) 
Choose your role in the organization: 
  - **Staff**
  - **Faculty**
  - **Student**
* **Training Session**: \* (select_multiple) 
Choose one or more training sessions you wish to attend: 
  - **Session 1**
  - **Session 2**
  - **Session 3**
