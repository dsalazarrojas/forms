# Talent Retention Strategy Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants about their user experience, session details, contact information, and job details for a Talent Retention Strategy Session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Info** (`User ID`, `text`, optional): This is the first page of the form where you provide general information about yourself.
*   **Session Info** (`Session Topic`, `text`, optional): This page is about details related to the session you are registering for.
*   **Contact Info** (`Email`, `email`, optional): This page is about how you can be contacted.
*   **Job Info** (`Job Title`, `Job Responsibilities`, `text`, optional): This is about your workplace or job details if they are relevant to your session experience.
*   **Session Details** (`text`, optional): Add any other relevant session details that you think are helpful.
*   **Submit** (`text`, optional): Once you have filled out the form, click "Submit" to submit your registration.
*   **Email** (`email`, optional): Provide your email address for any session communications or updates.
*   **Phone** (`text`, optional): Provide your phone number if you have one.
