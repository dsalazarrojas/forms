# Sponsor Gift Area Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Gift Area Booking Form is used to book a gift area for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the title of the event in the "Sponsor Gift Area" field.
2. Enter the name of the sponsor in the "Sponsor Name" field.
3. Choose the date and time of the event in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
4. Enter the name of the gift area in the "Gift Area Name" field.
5. Enter the address of the gift area in the "Gift Area Address" field.
6. Specify the number of guests attending in the "Number of Guests" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Email Address" field.
9. Choose the start date of the reservation in the "Reservation Start Date" field.
10. Choose the end date of the reservation in the "Reservation End Date" field.
11. Add any additional notes about the reservation in the "Notes" field.
12. Select the status of the reservation in the "Gift Area Reservation Status" field.
13. Choose the user who will be assigned to manage the reservation in the "Assigned Jotform User" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Area Booking Form** (1): Enter a short description of the gift area.
* **Sponsor Gift Area** (2): Enter the name of the event's sponsor.
* **Sponsor Name** (3): Enter the name of the person or company sponsoring the event.
* **Event Date** (4): Enter the date of the event. The format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (5): Enter the time of the event. The format is HH:MM.
* **Gift Area Name** (6): Enter the name of the gift area.
* **Gift Area Address** (7): Enter the address of the gift area.
* **Number of Guests** (8): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Phone Number** (9): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (10): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Reservation Start Date** (11): Enter the start date of the reservation.
* **Reservation End Date** (12): Enter the end date of the reservation.
* **Notes** (13): Add any additional notes about the reservation.
* **Gift Area Reservation Status** (14): Choose the status of the reservation:
	+ Available
	+ Confirmed
	+ Cancelled
* **Assigned Jotform User** (15): Choose the user who will be assigned to manage the reservation: 
	+ Jim
	+ Jimmy
