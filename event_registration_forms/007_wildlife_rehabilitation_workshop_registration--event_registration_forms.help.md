# wildlife_rehabilitation_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for a wildlife rehabilitation workshop. It collects information about the attendees, their preferred dates, and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the "Attendee Details" section.
2. Select your preferred date(s) in the "Preferred Dates" section.
3. In the "Interests" section, choose your interest in wildlife conservation by selecting one or more options.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" sections.
5. Finally, review your submission and click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required): Enter your name and email address to be contacted by the workshop organizers.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select the dates you prefer to attend the workshop.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Choose the start time for your preferred date.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Choose the end time for your preferred date.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_one, required): Choose one or more areas of interest in wildlife conservation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): Check this field if you consent to the terms and conditions.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your registration.
* **Preferred Dates** (`preferred_dates`, select_multiple, required): Select all dates you prefer to attend the workshop.
* **Category** (`event_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category you prefer the workshop to fall under.
* **Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Add a description of the workshop.
* **Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person organizing the workshop.
* **Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Duration** (`event_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the workshop.
* **Capacity** (`event_capacity`, text, optional): Enter the capacity of the workshop.
* **Status** (`event_status`, select_one, optional): Check the status of the workshop.
* **Status** (`attendee_status`, select_multiple, optional): Check your status for attending the workshop.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the payment method for the workshop.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Check your payment status.
* **Notes** (`attendee_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your registration.
* **Payment Notes** (`payment_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about your payment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit your registration.

Note: This form is subject to change based on the organization's requirements and needs. Please ensure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
