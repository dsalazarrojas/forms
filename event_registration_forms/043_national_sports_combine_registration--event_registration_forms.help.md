# National Sports Combine Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for athletes to register for the National Sports Combine event. It's a one-page registration form that captures necessary information for the event's planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Athlete Name** field with your name as it appears on your ID.
2. Enter your **Athlete Email** in the provided field.
3. Enter the **Emergency Contact Name** in the field provided.
4. Enter the phone number of your **Emergency Contact** in the field.
5. Select the **Sports** you wish to participate in from the dropdown menu. You can select multiple sports.
6. If none of the provided sports are applicable, you can enter **Other Sport** details in the **Other Sport** field.
7. Enter the **Event Date** you wish to participate in.
8. Enter the **Event Time** you wish to participate in.
9. Fill in the **Location** where you plan to participate in the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Athlete Email**: Enter your athlete's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Sports**: Select the sport(s) you want to participate in. You can choose multiple sports.
* **Other Sport**: If you don't see your sport in the list, enter the name of the sport you want to participate in.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date you want to participate in the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time you want to participate in the event.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you want to participate in the event.
* **Participant Acknowledgement**: Read and acknowledge that you understand the participant rules and regulations.
* **Participant Acknowledgement Agreement**: Agree that you understand the participant rules and regulations.
* **Athlete Agreement**: Agree that you understand the rules and regulations for the athlete.
* **Emergency Acknowledgement**: Acknowledge that you understand the emergency procedures.
* **Coach Agreement**: Agree that you understand the coach's role and responsibilities.
* **Parent Agreement**: If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must acknowledge that they understand the rules and regulations.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the event organizers.
* Make sure to review all the agreements carefully before signing.
