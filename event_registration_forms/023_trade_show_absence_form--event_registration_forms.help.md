# Trade Show Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Show Absence Form is used to report an employee's absence or tardiness from a trade show event. This form is designed to be completed by the employee who was scheduled to attend or by their supervisor, indicating the reason for their absence or late arrival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of the trade show event (Show Date) that you missed or attended late.
2. Enter the start and end times of your scheduled trade show appearance, if applicable.
3. Provide your name as the employee attending the trade show.
4. If applicable, provide the name of your supervisor.
5. Review your information carefully and submit the form once you have completed all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Show Date
* **Show Date** (`Show Date`, date, required/optional): The date of the trade show event you attended or missed. Please fill in the date in the format MM/DD/YY.

### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, required/optional): The start time of your scheduled trade show appearance, if applicable. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### End Time
* **End Time** (`End Time`, time, required/optional): The end time of your scheduled trade show appearance, if applicable. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, required/optional): Your name as the employee attending the trade show.

### Supervisor Name
* **Supervisor Name** (`Supervisor Name`, text, required/optional): The name of your supervisor, if you are reporting your absence or tardiness. This field is not required if you are submitting your own form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields (Show Date and Employee Name) before submitting the form.
* If you are submitting on behalf of another employee, ensure that you have their permission to do so and provide their correct supervisor's name in the Supervisor Name field.
* If you have any issues with the form or need further clarification, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx)
