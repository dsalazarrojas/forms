# Heritage Festival Vendor RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors who wish to participate in the Heritage Festival. It is a required form that helps us understand your business needs and ensure a smooth experience for both you and our attendees. Please fill it out thoroughly and accurately to ensure your presence at the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `vendor_details` page with your company's information.
2. In the `booth_requirements` page, describe your booth's specific needs and requirements.
3. If you have any contact details you would like to share with us, please provide them on the `contact_details` page (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Vendor Details** (Required)
* **Vendor Name** (text, Required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
* **Booth ID** (text, Required): Enter the unique ID assigned to your booth or a brief description to help us identify it.
* **Company Address** (text, Required): Enter your company's physical address to enable us to communicate with you efficiently.
* **Contact Person** (text, Required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your business at the festival.

### **Booth Requirements** (Required)
* **Special Requirements** (text, Required): Describe any specific needs or requirements your booth may have, such as electricity or internet access.

### **Contact Details** (Optional)
* **Phone Number** (text, Optional): Provide your company's phone number for easy communication.
* **Email Address** (text, Optional): Enter your company's email address for efficient correspondence.
* **Website** (text, Optional): Share your company's website for more information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to help us serve you better.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your participation and look forward to seeing you at the festival!
