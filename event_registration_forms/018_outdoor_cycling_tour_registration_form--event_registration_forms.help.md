# Outdoor Cycling Tour Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Cycling Tour Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in joining the outdoor cycling tour. This form helps to capture essential details that ensure a safe and enjoyable tour experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the General Information section with your name, age, and any other relevant details.
2. Select whether you are interested in the tour by choosing "Yes" or "No".
3. Provide your participant information in the text field, including age, height, and any medical conditions.
4. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name, phone number, and email.
5. Check the medical clearance box if you have any medical conditions that need to be considered.
6. Check the terms and conditions box to acknowledge your understanding of the tour rules.
7. Fill in the payment information (if you've chosen to pay).
8. Click "Submit" to finalize your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Type your name and any other details you'd like to share.
* **Select Tour (2)**: Choose "Yes" if you are interested in the tour.
* **Participant Info (3)**: Provide your participant information in the text field.
* **Emergency Contact (4)**: Type in their name, phone number, and email.
* **Medical Clearance (5)**: Check the "Yes" box if you have any medical conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions (6)**: Check the box to acknowledge you've read and understood the tour rules.
* **Payment Info (7)**: Fill in the payment details if applicable.
* **Submit (8)**: Click to finalize your registration.
* **Confirm (9)**: Confirm that the information is correct.
* **Cancel (10)**: Cancel your registration.
* **Save and Preview (11)**: Save your progress and preview the form.
* **Save and Submit (12)**: Save and submit your registration.
* **Reset (13)**: Reset the form to its initial state.
* **Back (14)**: Go back to the previous page.
* **Next (15)**: Move to the next page.
* **Submit and Back (16)**: Save and submit your registration.
* **Save and Back (17)**: Save your progress and go back to the previous page.
* **Submit and Preview (18)**: Save and submit your registration and preview the form.
* **Save And Prev (19)**: Save and preview the form.
* **Save And Sub (20)**: Save and submit your registration.
* **Save And Sub Prev (21)**: Save and submit your registration and preview the form.
* **Save and Submit and Back (22)**: Save and submit your registration and go back to the previous page.
* **Save and Prev Back (23)**: Save and preview the form and go back to the previous page.
