# Outdoor Cycling Tour Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Cycling Tour Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in joining the outdoor cycling tour. This form helps to capture essential details that ensure a safe and enjoyable tour experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the General Information section with your name, age, and any other relevant details.
2. Select whether you are interested in the tour by choosing "Yes" or "No".
3. Provide your participant information in the text field, including age, height, and any medical conditions.
4. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name, phone number, and email.
5. Check the medical clearance box if you have any medical conditions that need to be considered.
6. Check the terms and conditions box to acknowledge your understanding of the tour rules.
7. Fill in the payment information (if you've chosen to pay).
8. Click "Submit" to finalize your registration.
