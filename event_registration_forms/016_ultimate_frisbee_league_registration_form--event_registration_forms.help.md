# Ultimate Frisbee League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering players for the Ultimate Frisbee League. Please complete this form with accurate and complete information about yourself or your team.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the league, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Player Name: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	* Email: Enter your valid email address.
2. Select your event type (if applicable):
	* Choose the type of event you are registering for: Baseball, Soccer, Football, or Hockey.
3. Select your payment method:
	* Choose one or more methods of payment you are using: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
4. Enter your player information:
	* Player Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD format.
	* Player Gender: Select your gender (Male or Female).
	* League ID: Enter your league ID (if you know it).
5. Select your team information (if applicable):
	* Division: Choose your division: Premier, Intermediate, Recreational, or Novice.
	* Team Size: Choose the size of your team: 1-4, 5-8, 9-12, 13-16, 17+.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name (Player Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Event Type (Event Type)**: Choose the type of event you are registering for.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Choose one or more payment methods you are using.
* **Player Date of Birth (Player Date of Birth)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD format.
* **Player Gender (Player Gender)**: Select your gender.
* **League ID (League ID)**: Enter your league ID (if you know it).
* **Division (Division)**: Choose your division.
* **Division ID (Division ID)**: Enter your division ID (if you know it).
* **Team Size (Team Size)**: Choose the size of your team.
* **Team Size ID (Team Size ID)**: Enter your team size ID (if you know it).
* **Team Number (Team Number)**: Enter the number of your team.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest while filling out this form.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact us for help.
* Please review your information before submitting to ensure accuracy.
