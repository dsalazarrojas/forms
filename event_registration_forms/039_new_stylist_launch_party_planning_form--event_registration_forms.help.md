# new_stylist_launch_party_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "new_stylist_launch_party_planning_form" is used to plan and register a new launch party event for a stylist. This form will help event planners and coordinators collect the necessary information for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with the required information for the launch party event.
2.  Ensure you select the correct options for the event type and new stylist information.
3.  Format the launch party time as 24HR time (HH:MM).
4.  Enter the correct launch party location and time zone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Launch Party Details** (`launch_party_details`): A text field where you can provide any additional details about the launch party.
*   **Business Plans** (`business_plans`): A text field where you can provide a brief description of the business plans for the launch party.
*   **New Stylist Info** (`new_stylist_info`): Select one of the options to indicate whether the stylist is new, returning, or referring.
*   **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the stylist or contact person.
*   **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the stylist or contact person.
*   **Launch Party Date** (`launch_party_date`): Enter the date of the launch party.
*   **Launch Party Time** (`launch_party_time`): Enter the time of the launch party in 24HR format (HH:MM).
*   **Launch Party Location** (`launch_party_location`): Enter the location of the launch party.
*   **Launch Party Time Zone** (`launch_party_time_zone`): Enter the time zone of the launch party.
*   **Event Type** (`event_type`): Select all the event types that apply to the launch party (e.g., Launch Party, Open House, Meet the Team).
*   **Event Details** (`event_details`): A note field where you can provide any additional details about the event.

## Tips

*   Make sure to format the launch party time as 24HR time (HH:MM).
*   Enter the correct launch party location and time zone.
*   Be as detailed as possible in the launch party details and event details fields.
