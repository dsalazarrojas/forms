# ประก Event Information Unsubscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to allow users to unregister or unsubscribe from an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you want to unsubscribe from the event by choosing one or more options in the "unsubscribe_from_event" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field to confirm your identity.
3. Select the reason for your unsubscription from the list of options in the "reason_prophets" field.
4. Enter your name in the "first_name" and "last_name" fields, if applicable.
5. Confirm that you want to unsubscribe from the event by selecting one or more options in the "confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscribe/Unsubscribe from Event** (`unsubscribe_from_event`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you wish to unsubscribe from the event or not.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to confirm your identity.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, select_one, required): Select one or more reasons for your unsubscription. This field is intended to gather information about the event you are unsubscribing from.
* **Reason for Unsubscription** (`reason_prophets`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more reasons why you wish to unsubscribe from the event.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name, if applicable.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name, if applicable.
* **Confirm Unsubscription** (`confirm`, select_one, required): Confirm that you want to unsubscribe from the event.
* **Reason for Unsubscription Confirmation** (`confirm_reason`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more reasons why you want to confirm your unsubscription.
