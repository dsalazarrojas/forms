# Investment Compliance Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Compliance Workshop Registration Form is used to register participants for the Investment Compliance Workshop. It is necessary for every participant to complete this form before attending the workshop.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Go to the first page of the form, labeled "Participant Information".
2. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Participant Phone" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`event_registration_form_1`, text, optional): This page provides a brief introduction to the participant.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up communications.
