# Renewable Energy Manufacturing Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Manufacturing Conference Registration form is for attendees to provide personal details and preferences to enhance their experience. This form helps us understand your needs and tailor the conference accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all necessary contact information (name, email, phone number).
- Answer the preferred language question (yes/no) and provide any additional comments you might have.
- If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify.
- Read and accept the Consent Form, then provide any comments about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Fill in your last name for better identification and contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact if necessary.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you prefer English or any other language you're proficient in; select 'No' if you prefer not to answer.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, mention them here.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, note, optional): Read and accept the consent form, then provide any comments about your registration.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have anything you'd like to mention or ask about the conference, use this field.
