# Industry Nomination Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about industry nominations for recognition and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Make sure to submit only one nomination per event.
3. Be clear and concise in your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name** (`form_name`): This is the title of the event or nomination being submitted. Please enter a brief and descriptive name that clearly indicates the event or nominee.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event or nomination you are submitting. This could be a conference, trade show, or any other industry-specific event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Please select the date of the event or nomination. Be sure to use the correct date format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the event or nomination.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): Briefly describe the event or nomination you are submitting. This will help our team understand the context and significance of the nomination.
* **Award Recognition** (`award_recognition`): Indicate whether the event or nomination is award-worthy. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' based on your assessment.
* **Award Recognition Type** (`award_recognition_type`): If you selected 'Yes' for award recognition, choose one or more of the award recognition types (if any).
* **Industry Nomination** (`industry_nomination`): Indicate whether the event or nomination is industry-related. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' based on your assessment.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): If you selected 'Yes' for award recognition, you may need to attach supporting documents to your nomination. Choose one or more of the options listed.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Please select the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter your name and title.
* **Organization** (`organization`): Enter the name of the organization or company related to the event or nomination.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`): Enter the name of the person or department this form is being submitted to.
* **Submitted To Email** (`submitted_to_email`): Enter the email address of the person or department this form is being submitted to.
* **Submitted To Phone** (`submitted_to_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person or department this form is being submitted to.
* **Attachments** (`form_attachments`): Indicate whether you are attaching any supporting documents to your nomination. Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Make sure to submit only one nomination per event.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our team for clarification.
