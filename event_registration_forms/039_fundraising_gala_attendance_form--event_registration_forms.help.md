# Fundraising Gala Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from attendees of the fundraising gala event. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can track your attendance and provide you with the best experience possible.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below and click the "Submit" button. Each field has a brief explanation of what information is required or recommended to enter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your invitation or your preferred name to be used for event purposes. (<small>Required field, enter your name as it appears on your invitation</small>)
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on event details and any changes. (<small>Optional field, enter your email address if you'd like to stay informed</small>)
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number, if you have one. This is optional, but it will help us reach you with last-minute updates or changes. (<small>Optional field, enter your phone number if you'd like to receive updates</small>)
* **Guest Type**: Choose whether you are attending as an Individual, Group, or Organization. (<small>Optional field, select your guest type from the options</small>)
* **Number of Guests**: Tell us how many guests will be attending with you, including yourself. (<small>Optional field, enter the number of guests in your party</small>)
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event. If you're unsure, check your invitation or our website. (<small>Required field, enter the date of the event</small>)
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event. If you're unsure, check your invitation or our website. (<small>Required field, enter the time of the event</small>)
* **Note**: Enter any additional information or comments about your attendance. This is an optional field, but it will help us understand your needs or preferences. (<small>Optional field, enter any additional comments or information</small>)
* **Additional Info**: Enter any other details or information you'd like to share with us. This is an optional field, but it will help us tailor the event to your needs. (<small>Optional field, enter any additional information you'd like to share</small>)
* **Submit**: Please select "Yes" if you will be attending the event, or "No" if you will not be able to make it. (<small>Optional field, select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your attendance</small>)

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us plan the event smoothly.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your invitation or our website for clarification.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please enter them in the "Note" or "Additional Info" fields.
