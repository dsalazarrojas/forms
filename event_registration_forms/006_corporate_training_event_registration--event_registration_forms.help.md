# Corporate Training Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register corporate training event participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, paying attention to the required fields marked with a * (asterisk).
2. Select your role (Attendee, Instructor, or Sponsor) from the provided options.
3. Choose your preferred date and session time from the available options.
4. If applicable, specify any special requirements or dietary restrictions.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Type the name of your company or organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official company email address.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role in the corporate training event (Attendee, Instructor, or Sponsor).
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, select the relevant option(s) (Gluten-free, Vegetarian, Vegan).
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, select_one, required): Choose the date you prefer to attend the event.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, select_one, required): Select the session time slot you prefer (Morning or Afternoon).
* **Session Preferences Other** (`session_preferences_other`, text, optional): If you have any other session preferences, type them here.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special requirements or requests, type them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure your email address is correctly formatted.
* Be specific when selecting your dietary requirements.
* Choose your preferred date and session time carefully to ensure your availability.
* If you have any special requirements, please specify them clearly.
