# Pre-Orientation Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre-Orientation Information Form is designed to collect essential information from students and employees for registration purposes. Please fill out this form to provide accurate and relevant details about yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is required and what fields are optional.
2. Fill out the fields with accurate and relevant information about yourself.
3. Ensure that all required fields (marked as "required: true") are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your status as:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Program of Study** (`program_of_study`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the programs you are enrolled or interested in:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the departments you are associated with:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the academic programs you are enrolled or interested in:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required: false): Enter your expected or actual graduation date.
* **Graduation Status** (`graduation_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your graduation status as:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Student Type** (`student_type`, select_one, required: false): Select your student type as:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Other Student Type** (`student_type_other`, text, required: false): Enter any other student type not listed above.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, select_one, required: false): Select your employee type as:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Other Employee Type** (`employee_type_other`, text, required: false): Enter any other employee type not listed above.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, required: false): Enter your employee title.

**Tips**

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authority or contact support for assistance.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
