# treasury_innovation_forum_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users for events held by the treasury innovation forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Organization** (if applicable).
3. Specify your **Position** (if applicable).
4. Provide your **Email** for communication purposes.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** for contact.
6. Select your registration type from the options: **Attendee**, **Speaker**, or **Exhibitor**.
7. Choose your payment type from the options: **Online Payment**, **Offline Payment**, or **No Payment**.
8. Select your payment method from the options: **Visa**, **Mastercard**, or **Amex**.
9. Enter the **Event Date** and **Event Time** of the event you are registering for.
10. Provide any additional notes in the **Note** field.
11. Enter the **Event Category** and **Event Type** if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your personal last name.
* **Organization** (`organization`): Enter your organization's name (if applicable).
* **Position** (`position`): Enter your job position (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`): Select your registration type from the options: **Attendee**, **Speaker**, or **Exhibitor**.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`): Choose your payment type from the options: **Online Payment**, **Offline Payment**, or **No Payment**.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select your payment method from the options: **Visa**, **Mastercard**, or **Amex**.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Note** (`note`): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Event Category** (`event_category`): Enter the event category if applicable.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Enter the event type if applicable.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): This field is not currently in use and has no purpose.
* **Payment Method Note** (`payment_method_note`): This field is not currently in use and has no purpose.

## Tips
* Review the fields carefully to ensure you have selected the correct options.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
