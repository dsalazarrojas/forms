# Virtual Event Platform Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and event details for virtual events. Please fill it out accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields, making sure to enter accurate and valid information.
2. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): A text field where you can add any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address where you'll receive important updates and event information.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the event starts.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the event ends.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter your address for delivery or event venue information.
* **Participant's Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you're from the United States, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, optional): Enter your city for event location information.
* **Participant Country Code** (`participant_country_code`, text, optional): Enter your country code (if not from the United States).
