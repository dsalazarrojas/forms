# international_participant_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants for an international event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Event Name" field with the title of the event you are attending.
2. Enter your "Participant Name" to identify yourself as a participant.
3. Provide your "Email" address to enable communication from the event organizers.
4. Enter your "Phone" number for any follow-up communication.
5. Enter your "Address" to help the event organizers locate you.
6. Enter the "City" where you will be attending the event.
7. Enter the "Country" where you will be attending the event.
8. Enter the "Zip Code" of your location.
9. Select your "Event Type" from the list: Event, Conference, Workshop, Seminar, Exhibition, or Trade Show.
10. Choose your "Participant Type": Speaker, Exhibitor, Sponsor, Attendee, or Vendor.
11. Enter your "Arrival Date" and "Departure Date" to indicate your event duration.
12. Enter a "Note" if you have any special requests or requirements.
13. Enter the "Assigned Tool" as per your requirements.
14. Enter the "Form ID" for identification purposes.
15. Enter the "Category" to help categorize your participation.
16. Enter a brief "Description" to introduce yourself or your team.
17. Enter a "Title" for yourself or your team.
18. Enter the "Event Dates" if different from the general event date.
19. Enter the "Country of Origin" to specify your nationality.
20. Enter the contact "Contact Person" for any follow-up communication.
21. Enter the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" for further contact.
22. Leave the "Category ID" field blank as it doesn't seem to be relevant.
23. Leave the "Event ID" field blank as it also appears to be non-relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (Text, Required): The title of the event you are attending.
* **Participant Name** (Text, Optional): Your name as a participant.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Your contact phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Your address for logistical purposes.
* **City** (Text, Optional): The city where you will be attending the event.
* **Country** (Text, Optional): The country where you will be attending the event.
* **Zip Code** (Text, Optional): The zip code of your location.
* **Event Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of event: Event, Conference, Workshop, Seminar, Exhibition, or Trade Show.
* **Participant Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose your role as a participant: Speaker, Exhibitor, Sponsor, Attendee, or Vendor.
* **Arrival Date** (Date, Optional): Enter your expected arrival date.
* **Departure Date** (Date, Optional): Enter your expected departure date.
* **Note** (Note, Optional): Any special requests or requirements you have.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Required): Enter the tool assigned to you.
* **Form ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the ID for the form.
* **Category** (Text, Optional): Enter the category for your participation.
* **Description** (Text, Optional): A brief introduction of yourself or your team.
* **Title** (Text, Required): Enter a title for yourself or your team.
* **Event Dates** (Date, Optional): Enter the event dates if different from the general event date.
* **Country of Origin** (Text, Optional): Specify your nationality.
* **Contact Person** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the contact email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Category ID** (Text, Optional): This field appears to be non-relevant, please leave it blank.
* **Event ID** (Text, Optional): This field also appears to be non-relevant, please leave it blank.
