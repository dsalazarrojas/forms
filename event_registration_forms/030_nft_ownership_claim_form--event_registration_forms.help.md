# NFT Ownership Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NFT Ownership Claim Form is used to claim ownership of a Non-Fungible Token (NFT) in a secure and transparent manner. This form gathers essential information from the claimant to verify their ownership and provide proof of the NFT's existence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are the actual owner of the NFT or have permission from the owner to claim on their behalf.
2. Enter your information in the User Input field, including your name, User ID, and any other details you'd like to share.
3. Enter the NFT's name and contract address in the corresponding fields.
4. Provide a transaction hash, which is a unique identifier for the blockchain transaction that proves ownership.
5. Enter the claimant's name and address, if you're representing someone else.
6. Enter the date and time you claim the NFT ownership.
7. Provide a proof of claim, which can be any evidence that supports your claim (e.g., email from the creator, contract receipt).
8. If applicable, provide proof of the transaction that transferred the NFT to you.
9. Upload any additional proof of NFT, such as images or certificates, to support your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (Text)**: Enter any additional information about yourself that is relevant to your claim.
* **User ID (Number)**: Enter a unique identifier assigned to you by a platform or organization that issued the ID.
* **NFT Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the Non-Fungible Token you're claiming ownership of.
* **NFT Contract Address (Text)**: Enter the contract address of the NFT on the blockchain.
* **Transaction Hash (Text)**: Enter the unique hash of the blockchain transaction that proves ownership.
* **Claimant Name (Text)**: If you're representing someone else, enter their name here.
* **Claimant Address (Text)**: Enter the address of the claimant, if different from your own.
* **Claim Date (Date)**: Enter the date you're claiming ownership of the NFT.
* **Claim Time (Time)**: Enter the time you're claiming ownership of the NFT.
* **Proof of Claim (Text)**: Upload or enter any evidence that supports your claim, such as an email or contract receipt.
* **Proof of Transaction (Text)**: Upload or enter any proof of the transaction that transferred the NFT to you.
* **NFT Proof (Text)**: Upload or enter any additional proof of the NFT, such as images or certificates.
* **NFTs Proof (Text)**: Upload or enter any additional proof of multiple NFTs, if applicable.
