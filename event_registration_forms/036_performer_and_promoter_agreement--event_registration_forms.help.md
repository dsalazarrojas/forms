# Performer and Promoter Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performer and Promoter Agreement is a form used to create an agreement between performers and promoters for events. This document outlines the terms of their collaboration, ensuring that both parties have a clear understanding of their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Performer Details**
	* Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself as a performer.
2. **Promoter Details**
	* Enter your information as the promoter of the event.
3. **Event Details**
	* Enter the event name, date, and location.
4. **Agreement Terms**
	* Describe the terms of your agreement, including any specific conditions or clauses that are not already covered in the form.
5. **Compensation**
	* If applicable, specify your expected compensation for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Details**
	* This field is used to collect your information as a performer. Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date details about yourself.
* **Promoter Details**
	* This field is used to collect your information as a promoter. Please enter your contact details and any relevant information about the event.
* **Event Details**
	* Enter the name, date, and location of the event. This information will help us to identify the specific event that this agreement applies to.
* **Agreement Terms**
	* This field is used to describe any specific terms or conditions of your agreement. Please be as clear and detailed as possible to avoid any confusion.
* **Compensation**
	* If you expect compensation for your services, please specify it here. This can include any fees, expenses, or other benefits you are expecting.
