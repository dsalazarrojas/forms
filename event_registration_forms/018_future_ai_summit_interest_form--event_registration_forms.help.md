# Future AI Summit Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in participating in the Future AI Summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your First Name.
2. Fill out your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Provide your Organization name (if applicable).
5. Specify your Role (if applicable).
6. Choose your contact preferences by selecting one or more of the options provided.
7. Enter your Industry (if applicable).
8. Specify your Location (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (First Name)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your first name. Please fill out your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name (Last Name)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your last name. Please fill out your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email (Email)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for your Email address. Please enter a valid Email address that you check regularly.

### Organization (Organization)
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): If you are affiliated with an organization, please fill out the name of the organization you are representing.

### Role (Role)
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required: false): If you have a specific role within your organization, please specify it.

### Contact Preferences (Contact Preferences)
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more of the options provided to indicate how you would like to be contacted. Please select only the options that apply to you.

### Industry (Industry)
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to specify your industry, please enter it.

### Location (Location)
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to specify your location, please enter it.
