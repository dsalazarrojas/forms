# Best Practitioner Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Best Practitioner Voting Form" is a tool for gathering information from practitioners participating in an event. This form helps the event organizers to collect essential details about the attendees, their preferences, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details, including your full name and email address.
2.  Select the relevant date for your participation in the event.
3.  Choose your primary role in the event (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
4.  Provide any additional details or comments about your experience.
5.  Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions (you can select multiple options).
6.  If required, enter the estimated value of your participation or any other relevant rating.
7.  Enter your preferred time for scheduling purposes.
8.  Finally, add any closing remarks or comments about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information (Section 1)
*   **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name for event records.
*   **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Include your country code if you're from outside the local region.

### Event Details (Section 2)
*   **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, select_one, required): Choose your role in the event (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
*   **Date** (`reference_date`, date, required): Select the relevant date for your participation.

### Additional Information (Section 3)
*   **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, number, optional): Provide a whole number for rating or quantity.
*   **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Add any additional relevant information about your experience.
*   **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): Enter the estimated value of your participation in decimal format.
*   **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Enter a time for scheduling purposes if required.
*   **Final comments** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Add any closing remarks or comments about the event.

**Tips**:

*   Make sure to fill in all the required fields to help the event organizers plan and manage the event effectively.
*   Be accurate and honest when providing your details to ensure the organizers can provide the best possible experience for you and other practitioners.
