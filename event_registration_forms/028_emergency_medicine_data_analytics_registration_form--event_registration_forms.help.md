# Emergency Medicine Data Analytics Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who are registering for an Emergency Medicine-related workshop or conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, and other contact details accurately.
2.  Select the type of workshop or conference you are registering for (Workshop or Conference).
3.  Select one or more medical specialties relevant to your practice.
4.  Enter the date and time of the workshop or conference you are registering for.
5.  Enter the location of the workshop you are registering for.
6.  Select the maximum capacity of the workshop or conference you are registering for.
7.  Enter the fees you are paying for the workshop or conference.
8.  Select whether you have medical insurance.
9.  Select the payment method you are using.
10. Enter the date you are registering for the workshop or conference.
11. Enter the person submitting the registration (usually yourself).
