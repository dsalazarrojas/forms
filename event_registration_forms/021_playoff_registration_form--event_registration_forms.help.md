# Playoff Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Playoff Registration Form is designed to collect information required for a player and their team to register for a game. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your ID.
2. Enter your team's name as it will appear on the game schedule and in the tournament brackets.
3. Enter your team's phone number and email to ensure we can contact you with schedule updates and other important information.
4. Enter your game details including the date and time you plan to play.
5. Review and submit the form after filling in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your team's name as it will appear on the game schedule and in the tournament brackets.
* **Player Number** (`player_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your player number, if applicable.
* **Division** (`division`, `text`, required: false): Enter the division you are registered for, if applicable.
* **Game** (`game`, `text`, required: false): Enter the game details, such as the game type or specific rules.
* **Player Phone** (`player_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with schedule updates and other important information.
* **Player Email** (`player_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you with schedule updates and other important information.
* **Team Phone** (`team_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your team's phone number so we can contact you with schedule updates and other important information.
* **Team Email** (`team_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your team's email address so we can contact you with schedule updates and other important information.
* **Player Address** (`player_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your player address if you need to send any mail or packages.
* **Team Address** (`team_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your team's address if you need to send any mail or packages.
* **Game Date** (`game_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the game you are registered for.
* **Game Time** (`game_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the game you are registered for.
