# Equipment Condition Monitoring Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for the Equipment Condition Monitoring Workshop. It allows you to provide your details, preferred workshop dates, and any special requirements you may have. The information collected will be used to accommodate your needs during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your attendee details in the registration form.
2. Select your preferred workshop dates.
3. If you have any special requirements, enter them in the text box.
4. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. If you have any additional comments or notes, type them in the text box.
6. Click the submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**: This field is where you can enter your basic details, including your name and any other relevant information.
	+ **Attendee Details**: This field is a text box where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your participation in the workshop.
* **Preferred Workshop Dates**: Select one or more months for your preferred workshop dates. The options are:
	+ January
	+ February
	+ March
	+ April
	+ May
	+ June
	+ July
	+ August
	+ September
	+ October
	+ November
	+ December
* **Special Requirements**: If you have any special requirements, such as accessibility needs or dietary restrictions, type them in this text box.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about the workshop.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note**: This field is a text box where you can enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Required Fields**: This field is not required and is for your information only.
