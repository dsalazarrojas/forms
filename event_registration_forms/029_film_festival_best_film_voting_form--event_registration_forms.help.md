# Film Festival Best Film Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Festival Best Film Voting Form is designed for users to vote on their favorite film in a film festival, as well as provide feedback and other event information. This form helps organizers track and understand user preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Best Film" from the options provided. Choose the film that you think is the best among the options.
2. In the "Audience Feedback" field, provide your thoughts and comments about the selected film. This is an optional field but helps the organizers understand your opinion.
3. Optionally, provide your contact information, such as email, phone number, and event name, event date, and event time.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Best Film** (`Best Film`, select_one, required): Choose the film you think is the best. Select from the options provided.
* **Audience Feedback** (`Audience Feedback`, text, optional): Provide your thoughts about the selected film.
* **Event Name** (`Event Name`, text, optional): If you are attending a specific event, provide the event name.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, optional): If you are attending a specific event, provide the date.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time, optional): If you are attending a specific event, provide the time.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Provide your email address if you want to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Event Description** (`Event Description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the event.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your vote.

**Tips**: Please fill out as much information as possible to help the organizers better understand your preferences. However, if a field is optional, feel free to skip it if you don't want to provide it.
