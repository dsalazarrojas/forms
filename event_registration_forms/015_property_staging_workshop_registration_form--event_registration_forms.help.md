# Property Staging Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from attendees for the Property Staging Workshop. It is used to register for the event, provide event details, and make payments. Please ensure all required fields are filled in accurately for a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your event details**: On the "Event Details" page, you can add any additional information about the event you would like to share with the organizers.
2. **Add attendee details**: On the "Attendee Details" page, please provide any relevant information about the attendees.
3. **Select your event preferences**: On the "Preferences" page, you can choose which options apply to you.
4. **Choose your payment method and amount**: On the "Payment" page, select your preferred payment method and amount.
5. **Specify your payment details**: On the "Payment Method" page, please enter your payment details such as currency.
6. **Agree to terms and conditions**: On the "Terms" page, please select the terms and conditions you agree to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (Registration)**: The title of your registration.
* **Event Details (Event Details)**: Add any additional information about the event.
* **Attendee Details (Attendee Details)**: Provide any relevant information about the attendees.
* **Preferences (Preferences)**: 
  + Options 1, 2, 3: Choose which options apply to you.
* **Payment (Payment)**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Enter your payment details.
* **Payment Amount (Payment Amount)**: Enter the payment amount in the required currency.
* **Payment Currency (Payment Currency)**: Select the currency for your payment.
* **Note (Note)**: Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Terms (Terms)**: Select the terms and conditions you agree to.
