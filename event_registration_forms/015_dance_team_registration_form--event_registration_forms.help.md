# Dance Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your dance team for an event. Please fill out this form to provide your team and participant information, contact details, and scheduling information to the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the registration process.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Team Name
* **Team Name**: Enter your team's name as it will be displayed on the event's registration list.

### Participant Name
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant(s) who will be attending the event. You can enter multiple names separated by commas if necessary.

### Email
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your team. This will be used for communication and updates from the event organizers.

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your team's phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### Date
* **Date**: Enter the date of the event. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Start Time
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event. Please use the format 12:00 AM/PM.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your team and participant information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need help with the registration process, please don't hesitate to contact us.
