# Portrait Photo Shoot Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register individuals for a portrait photo shoot event. It is used to collect essential information and schedule the shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (name, address, etc.) in the "Personal Information" section.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section, as this is a required field.
3. Upload a portrait image in the "Portrait Image Upload" section. This will help us understand your style and preferences.
4. Select a date and time for the shoot from the calendar in the "Date and Time" section. This is a required field.
5. Choose a time for the shoot in the "Time" field. This is a required field.
6. If you have any special requests or availability constraints, please enter them in the "Availability" section.
7. If you have any special requests or additional details, please enter them in the "Special Requests" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
* **Personal Information** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, **required: false**): This section is for personal details like your name, address, and other personal information that you would like to share with us.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, **required: true**): This section is for your phone number, email, and other contact details that we will use to communicate with you.
### Portrait Image Upload
* **Portrait Image Upload** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, **required: true**): Upload a high-quality portrait image that represents you. This will help us understand your style and preferences.
### Date and Time
* **Date and Time** (`id: 4`, `type: date`, **required: true**): Select a date and time for the photo shoot from the calendar. Please note that we may not be able to accommodate all requested dates and times.
### Time
* **Time** (`id: 5`, `type: time`, **required: true**): Choose a specific time for the photo shoot. This will help us schedule the shoot efficiently.
### Availability
* **Availability** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, **required: false**): If you have any constraints on your availability, please let us know in this section.
### Special Requests
* **Special Requests** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, **required: false**): If you have any specific requests or details, please enter them in this section.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the image you upload is in high-quality and meets our requirements.
* Be punctual and available at the scheduled time for the photo shoot.
* If you have any changes in your availability or special requests, please notify us in advance to avoid any last-minute adjustments.
