# Campground Guest Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campground Guest Check Out Form is a crucial tool used to gather essential information from guests upon their departure. This form aims to provide an in-depth understanding of their stay, allowing us to make informed decisions to improve our services and enhance their experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section carefully and completely.
2. Provide accurate and honest information to ensure data quality.
3. Review each field for required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the guest checking out.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, `email`, required): Enter the guest's email address for correspondence purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`guest_phone`, `text`, required): Provide the guest's phone number for potential communication.
* **Reservation Number** (`reservation_number`, `text`, required): Enter the number of their reservation for identification purposes.
* **Campsite Number or Name** (`campground_site`, `text`, required): Specify the campsite number or name where the guest stayed.
* **Check-Out Date** (`checkout_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of their check-out.
* **Actual Check-Out Time** (`checkout_time`, `time`, required): Indicate the time they actually checked-out.
* **Length of Stay** (`length_of_stay`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of nights they stayed (if not already indicated by reservation number).
* **Campsite Cleanliness** (`site_cleanliness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cleanliness of the campsite upon departure (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Requires Cleaning).
* **Any Damage Found** (`damage_present`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if any damage was found on the campsite or equipment (No damage, Minor damage, Major damage).
* **Describe Any Damage** (`damage_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a description of any damage found (if applicable).
* **Trash Properly Disposed of** (`trash_properly_disposed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that trash was properly disposed of during their stay (True, False).
* **Campfire Properly Extinguished** (`campfire_extinguished`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if the campfire was properly extinguished upon departure (True, False, Not Applicable).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate their overall satisfaction with their stay (Extremely Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Extremely Dissatisfied).
* **Most Enjoyed Aspects** (`most_enjoyed`, `text`, optional): Describe what they enjoyed most about their stay.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvements_suggested`, `text`, optional): Suggest any areas for improvement to enhance their stay.
* **Would You Return** (`would_return`, `select_one`, required): Indicate their willingness to return to our campground (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Final Charges Agreement** (`final_charges_agreed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that final charges have been explained and agreed upon (Yes, I agree, False).
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about their stay.

Note: Please fill out this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure we can gather the best possible insights to improve our services. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
