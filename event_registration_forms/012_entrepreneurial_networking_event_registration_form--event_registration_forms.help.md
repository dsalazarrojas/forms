# Entrepreneurial Networking Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees to facilitate the sharing of their stories and experiences during the Entrepreneurial Networking event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information. Each field is marked as "required" to ensure that you have provided the necessary details for the event organizers to process your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **About Your Yourself** (`About Your Yourself`, text, required): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, company, and role.
* **Are you a member of the Entrepreneurial Network?** (`Are you a member of the Entrepreneurial Network?`, select_one, required): Please select "I am a member" if you are a member of the Entrepreneurial Network, and "I am not a member" otherwise.
* **Have you attended an Entrepreneurial Network event before?** (`Have you attended an Entrepreneurial Network event before?`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you have attended an Entrepreneurial Network event before, and "False" otherwise.
* **What do you want to gain from the event?** (`What do you want to gain from the event?`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of what you hope to achieve or gain from the event.
* **Are you willing to share your story?** (`Are you willing to share your story?`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you are willing to share your story, and "False" otherwise.
* **What is your role?** (`What is your role?`, select_multiple, required): Please select one or more of the provided roles that apply to you (CEO/Founder, Investor/Advisor, Other).
* **How did you hear about the event?** (`How did you hear about the event?`, select_multiple, required): Please select one or more of the provided sources where you heard about the event (Social Media, Friend/Colleague, Online Search).
* **What is your company name?** (`What is your company name?`, text, required): Please provide the name of your company.
* **What is your title at your company?** (`What is your title at your company?`, text, required): Please provide your title at your company.
* **Are you willing to provide your contact information?** (`Are you willing to provide your contact information?`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you are willing to provide your contact information, and "False" otherwise.
* **Consent to use your story in promotional materials?** (`Consent to use your story in promotional materials?`, select_one, required): Please select "I agree" if you agree to have your story used in promotional materials, and "I do not agree" otherwise.
* **Consent to publish your story on social media?** (`Consent to publish your story on social media?`, select_one, required): Please select "I agree" if you agree to have your story published on social media, and "I do not agree" otherwise.
* **Do you have a photo?** (`Do you have a photo?`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you have a photo, and "False" otherwise.
* **Do you have a logo?** (`Do you have a logo?`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you have a logo, and "False" otherwise.
* **Do you have social media accounts?** (`Do you have social media accounts?`, select_multiple, required): Please select one or more of the provided social media platforms where you have an account (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram).
* **Can we follow your account?** (`Can we follow your account?`, select_multiple, required): Please select one or more of the provided social media platforms where we can follow you (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram).
* **Do you want us to share your story on your account?** (`Do you want us to share your story on your account?`, select_multiple, required): Please select one or more of the provided social media platforms where you want us to share your story (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram).
* **Is this the primary contact?** (`Is this the primary contact?`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if this is the primary contact, and "False" otherwise.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, false): This field is optional and allows you to add any additional comments or information that you would like to share with the event organizers.
