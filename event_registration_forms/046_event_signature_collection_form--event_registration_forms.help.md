# Event Signature Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information about events, including attendee details, event details, and event status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name, email, phone number, and other required personal details in the relevant fields.
3. Provide a valid signature in the "Signature" field.
4. Enter the event details, such as date, time, location, and organizer information.
5. Indicate the status of the event (Active, Completed, or Cancelled).
6. Add any additional event attendees and their details if necessary.
7. Upload any relevant event files.
8. Enter your consent and signature as an attendee.
9. Complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required): Enter your name, title, and other identifying information.
* **Event Consent** (`event_consent`, text, required): Provide your consent to attend the event.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Upload your signature (if required).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the event date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the event time.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter the event title, description, and other details.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the event location.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the event date.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the event time.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, optional): Enter the event organizer's name and contact information.
* **Event Venue** (`event_venue`, text, optional): Enter the event venue.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, optional): Select the type of event (e.g., conference, workshop, meetup).
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of the event (Active, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Event Attendees** (`event_attendees`, text, optional): Enter the names and details of any additional attendees.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the event.
* **Event Files** (`event_files`, text, optional): Upload any files related to the event.
* **Attendee Consent** (`attendee_consent`, text, optional): Provide your consent as an attendee.
* **Attendee Signature** (`attendee_signature`, text, optional): Upload your signature as an attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as an attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as an attendee.
* **Attendee Notes** (`attendee_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the event as an attendee.
* **Attendee Status** (`attendee_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of the attendee (Active, Completed, Cancelled).
