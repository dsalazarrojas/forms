# Student Scheduling Conflict Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Scheduling Conflict Report Form is designed to help students report scheduling conflicts that arise during the school year.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for Conflicting Events, as this will help administrators understand the scope of the conflict.
3. Clearly describe the scheduling conflict and its resolution status in the provided text fields.
4. Ensure that you have provided enough information for the conflict to be resolved or updated properly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Scheduling Conflict Report Form** (page 1): Enter the title of the form and confirm that you are reporting a scheduling conflict.
- **Start Date** (page 1): Enter the start date of the conflict.
- **Start Time** (page 1): Enter the start time of the conflict.
- **End Date** (page 1): Enter the end date of the conflict.
- **End Time** (page 1): Enter the end time of the conflict.
- **Scheduling Conflict** (page 1): Describe the scheduling conflict and its details.
- **Conflict Description** (page 1): Provide a detailed description of the conflict.
- **Conflict Location** (page 1): Enter the location where the conflict occurred (if applicable).
- **Conflict Date** (page 1): Enter the date of the conflict.
- **Conflict Time** (page 1): Enter the time of the conflict.
- **Conflict Duration** (page 1): Enter the duration of the conflict.
- **Conflicting Events** (page 2): Select all events that are conflicting with the schedule.
- **Conflict Resolution** (page 2): Describe the proposed resolution to the conflict.
- **Resolution Status** (page 2): Select the current resolution status of the conflict.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact your administrator or supervisor for guidance.
- This form is designed to be used in conjunction with other forms and reports to track and resolve scheduling conflicts.
- If you need to report multiple conflicts, please fill out separate forms for each conflict.
