# entrepreneurship_program_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering volunteers for the entrepreneurship program. It helps us understand your background, skills, and preferences to match you with the right opportunities within the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization name.
2. Select your role as a volunteer or staff member.
3. List your skills and areas of expertise.
4. Specify your interests and areas you'd like to focus on.
5. Provide your availability for the program.
6. Enter your date of birth.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. If you have any additional information, enter it in the note field.
10. Confirm that you are willing to participate in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's name, where you work or are associated with.
- **role** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Select 'volunteer' if you're registering as a volunteer, or 'staff' if you're registering as a staff member.
- **skills** (`3`, `text`, optional): List your areas of expertise, such as programming, design, or marketing.
- **interests** (`4`, `text`, optional): Specify your areas of interest, such as entrepreneurship, innovation, or sustainability.
- **availability** (`5`, `time`, optional): Provide your availability for the program, including days and hours you're free to work.
- **date_of_birth** (`6`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
- **email** (`7`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to contact you.
- **contact_phone** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
- **note** (`9`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself.
- **confirmation** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Confirm that you are willing to participate in the program by selecting 'True'.
