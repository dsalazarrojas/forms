# Whitewater Rafting Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This Whitewater Rafting Waiver Form is designed to ensure that participants understand and acknowledge the risks involved in whitewater rafting activities. By completing this form, participants (or their parents/guardians) will provide necessary information about their personal and medical details. This includes medical conditions, emergency contact information, and other relevant details that will help our staff provide the best possible experience for the participant.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
2. **Provide correct information**: Make sure the information provided is up-to-date and accurate.
3. **Sign the form**: Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the risks and information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (participant_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter your email address where you can be reached for emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (parent_name): Enter the name of your parent or legal guardian (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact_name): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (emergency_contact_phone): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions): List any medical conditions you may have, such as allergies or disabilities.
* **Prophets Medical Treatment** (medical_treatment): List any medical treatments or medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Equipment** (medical_equipment): List any medical equipment you use, such as an EpiPen.
* **Signature Date** (waiver_signature_date): Date and sign this form to confirm you have read and understood the risks and information provided.
* **Participant Signature** (waiver_signature): Sign this form to confirm you have read and understood the risks and information provided.
* **Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship): If the emergency contact is not a parent or guardian, please specify your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Medical Attention** (medical_attention): If you have a medical condition that requires special attention, please describe.
* **Medical Insurance** (medical_insurance): If you have medical insurance, please provide the name of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Name** (insurance_name): Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
* **Participant Address** (participant_address): Enter your home address.
* **Parent Responsible** (parent_responsible): If you are under 18, please enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Participant Occupation** (participant_occupation): Enter your occupation.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship): If the emergency contact is not a parent or guardian, please specify your relationship.
* **Emergency Phone** (emergency_contact_phone): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Notes** (medical_notes): If you have any additional medical notes or information, please enter.
* **Insurance Phone** (insurance_phone): Enter the phone number of your medical insurance provider.
* **Participant Height** (participant_height): Enter your height in inches.
* **Participant Weight** (participant_weight): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Waiver Form** (waiver_form): This is a placeholder field; please do not fill out.

Note: Please be aware that some fields may not be applicable to your specific situation. If you are unsure about any field, please ask our staff for clarification.
