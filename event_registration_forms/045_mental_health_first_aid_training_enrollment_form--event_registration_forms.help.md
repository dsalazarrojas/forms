# mental_health_first_aid_training_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants enrolling in mental health first aid training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all required information readily available.
2.  Fill in your participant name, email address, and any other requested details in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, or birth certificate).
* **Email (email)**: Provide your correct email address for future communication and updates.
* **Training Session (training_session)**: Enter the name of the training session you are enrolling in.
* **Training Provider (training_provider)**: Enter the name of the training provider you are enrolling with.
* **Participant Address (participant_address)**: Enter your complete address for delivery or communication purposes.
* **Participant City (participant_city)**: Enter the city you currently reside in or the city where you will be taking the training.
* **Participant State (participant_state)**: Enter the state where you currently reside in or the state where you will be taking the training.
* **Participant Zip (participant_zip)**: Enter your zip code for delivery or communication purposes.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact and updates.
* **Participant Country (participant_country)**: Enter the country where you currently reside in or the country where you will be taking the training.

## Tips
Please ensure accuracy and completeness in your responses to avoid delays or issues with processing your enrollment. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the training provider.
