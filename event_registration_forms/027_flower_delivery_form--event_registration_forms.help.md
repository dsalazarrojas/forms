# Flower Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flower Delivery Form is used to register a flower delivery. Please fill out this form to schedule a flower delivery for a special person. This form is required for all flower delivery requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: flower_type, number_of_flowers (if applicable) and delivery_address (if applicable).
2. Fill out any additional optional fields: first_name, last_name, delivery_date, delivery_time, message, delivery_address (if not required), number_of_flowers, message_to_delivery_person, email, and phone.
3. Make sure to double-check all information entered before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **flower_type** (Roses, Tulips, Daisies): Select the type of flower you would like to deliver.
* **first_name**: Enter the first name of the recipient (if applicable).
* **last_name**: Enter the last name of the recipient (if applicable).
* **delivery_date**: Enter the date you would like the flowers to be delivered.
* **delivery_time**: Enter the time you would like the flowers to be delivered (if applicable).
* **message**: Enter a message or note for the recipient (if applicable).
* **delivery_address**: Enter the address where the flowers will be delivered (if not required).
* **number_of_flowers**: Enter the number of flowers you would like to deliver (if applicable).
* **message_to_delivery_person**: Enter a message for the person delivering the flowers (if applicable).
* **email**: Enter the email address of the recipient (if applicable).
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the recipient (if applicable).
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the delivery person (if applicable).
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the recipient or delivery person (if applicable).
