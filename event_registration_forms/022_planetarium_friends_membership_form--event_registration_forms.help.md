# Planetarium Friends Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Planetarium Friends Membership Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in joining the Planetarium Friends membership program. By filling out this form, users can express their interest in becoming part of the community and provide demographic and interest-based information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Start by selecting "First Page" on the form's navigation.
* Review the questions on the first page and answer the questions that apply to you.
* Click "Next" to proceed to the next page.
* Repeat the same process for the second and third pages.
* Once you have completed all the pages, click "Submit" to finish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: The title of the form is used to identify the form's purpose.
* **Pages**: The form is divided into three pages, each with its own set of questions.
* **First Page (planetarium_friends_membership_form_first_page)**: This page asks for no information.
* **Second Page (planetarium_friends_membership_form_second_page)**: This page also asks for no information.
* **Third Page (planetarium_friends_membership_form_third_page)**: This page, like the others, does not ask for any information.
* **Hint**: This field is not used in the form.
* **Type**: The type of field is not relevant to the form's function.
* **Options**: There are no options to select from in the form.
* **Required/Optional**: No fields in the form are marked as required.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form honestly and accurately.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact form@gic.mx.
