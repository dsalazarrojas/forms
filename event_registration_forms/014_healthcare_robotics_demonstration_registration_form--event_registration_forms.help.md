# Healthcare Robotics Demonstration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for attendees to register for the Healthcare Robotics Demonstration event. It helps us understand your role, preferences, and provide a smooth experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required".
2. Ensure you provide accurate and clear information for all fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title
(form_title, text, required)
This is the title of the form, which is a required field. Please enter a clear and concise title for your registration.

### Attendee Name
(attendee_name, text, optional)
This field is for your name, if you'd like to provide it. It's not required but can help us tailor the event to your needs.

### Contact Email
(contact_email, email, optional)
If you'd like to receive updates or have questions about the event, please enter your contact email.

### Contact Phone
(contact_phone, text, optional)
This field is for your contact phone number. You can enter it if you'd like to receive a call about the event.

### University Name
(university_name, text, required)
Please enter the name of your university or institution. This is required for us to process your registration.

### Role
(role, select_multiple, required)
Choose one or more roles that apply to you:

* Chief
* Researcher
* Educator
* Patient

### Session Preferences
(session_preferences, select_one, optional)
Choose a session time that suits you best:

* Morning
* Afternoon
* All

### Session Time
(session_time, time, required)
Please select a specific time for the session.

### Consent
(consent, note, required)
Read and agree to our terms and conditions.

### Terms and Conditions
(terms_and_conditions, text, optional)
Please read our terms and conditions before proceeding.
