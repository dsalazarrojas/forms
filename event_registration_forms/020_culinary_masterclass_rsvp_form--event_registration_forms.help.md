# Culinary Masterclass RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants for a culinary masterclass event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the event you are attending in the **Event Name** field.
2.  Enter your name in the **Participant Name** field.
3.  Enter your email address in the **Participant Email** field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the **Participant Phone** field.
5.  Enter your address in the **Participant Address** field.
6.  Select your preferred cuisine from the options provided in the **Preferred Cuisine** field.
7.  If you have any additional information or message for the organizers, enter it in the **Participant Message** field.
8.  If you have any further notes or comments, enter them in the **Participant Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (text)**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Participant Name (text)**: Enter your name as it will be listed for the event.
* **Event Date (date)**: Select the date of the event you are attending. Please use the standard date format: YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time (time)**: Select the time of the event you are attending. Please use the standard time format: HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Participant Email (email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number. This will be used for any follow-up communications.
* **Participant Address (text)**: Enter your address for any necessary logistical arrangements.
* **Preferred Cuisine (select_multiple)**: Select all the cuisines you are interested in or open to experiencing during the event.
* **Participant Message (note)**: Enter any additional information or message you want to share with the organizers.
* **Participant Notes (note)**: Enter any further notes or comments you have regarding the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information in all fields.
- Select relevant and applicable cuisines for your preferences.
- Keep the **Participant Message** and **Participant Notes** fields concise, but feel free to share any important details.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
