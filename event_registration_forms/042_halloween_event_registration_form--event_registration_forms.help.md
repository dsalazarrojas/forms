# Halloween Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Event Registration Form is designed for users to register for the upcoming Halloween event. The form will collect relevant personal and event-specific information for event organization and planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information:
	* First Name: Please enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Please enter your last name.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Age: Please enter your age.
2. Provide event information:
	* Event Date: Enter the date of the event.
	* Event Time: Enter the time of the event.
	* Event Location: Enter the location of the event.
3. Confirm your event details:
	* Event Description: Briefly describe the event.
	* Registration Date: Enter the date you registered for the event.
	* Event Time Zone: Select the time zone of the event (EDT, EST, CDT, MDT, PDT, AST, HST, CST, MST).
4. Review and submit your registration.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false):
	+ Please enter your age.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false):
	+ Enter the time of the event.

	+ Enter a contact number.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `note`, required: false):
	+ Briefly describe the event.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you registered for the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required: false):

	+ Select the time zone of the event from the list.
