# vip_ticket_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the Vip Ticket Payment Form, which is used to process ticket payment information for VIP events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct event.
2. Fill out the payment method field: 
   - Choose between 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you want to pay with a credit card.
3. Enter the amount you wish to pay for the event. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank.
4. Enter your credit card number in the format "1234-1234-1234-1234".
5. Enter your expiration date in the format MM/DD/YY.
6. Enter the security code (CVV) for your credit card.
7. Enter the name on your credit card.
8. Enter your billing address.
9. Enter your ZIP code.
10. Enter your email address. 
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Indicate whether you want payment to be confirmed, "Yes" or "No".
12. A free text field is provided to enter any additional payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Payment Method** (`Credit Card`, select_one, not required): 
   This field is a dropdown menu where you can select whether you want to pay with a credit card or not.
- **Amount** (`Amount`, number, not required): 
   Enter the amount you want to pay for the event. You can leave this field blank if you do not want to pay at this time.
- **Card Number** (`Card number`, text, not required): 
   Enter your credit card number in the format "1234-1234-1234-1234" for it to be accepted.
- **Expiration Date** (`Expiration date`, date, not required): 
   Enter your credit card expiration date in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **Security Code** (`Security code`, text, not required): 
   Enter the security code (CVV) for your credit card.
- **Name on Card** (`Name on card`, text, not required): 
   Enter the name on your credit card.
- **Billing Address** (`Billing address`, text, not required): 
   Enter your billing address.
- **ZIP Code** (`ZIP code`, text, not required): 
   Enter your ZIP code.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, not required): 
   Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, not required): 
   Enter your phone number.
- **Payment Confirmed** (`Payment prophets`, select_one, not required): 
   Indicate whether you want your payment to be confirmed.
- **Payment Details** (`Payment details`, note, not required): 
   You can enter any additional payment details here.
