# Material Reduction Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Material Reduction Initiative. It helps us gather details from participating organizations and individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your organization.
2. Enter your name as the contact person.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number (including the extension, if applicable).
5. Choose your phone extension (if not already registered).
6. Enter the name of the event you are registering.
7. Select the date of the event.
8. Select the time of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Phone Extension** (`contact_phone_extension`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your phone extension (if applicable).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the event you are registering.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time of the event.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy to ensure we can reach you effectively.
* Be sure to enter the correct date and time for the event to help us schedule it properly.
