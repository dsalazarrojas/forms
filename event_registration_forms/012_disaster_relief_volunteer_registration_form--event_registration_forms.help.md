# Disaster Relief Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disaster Relief Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for disaster relief efforts. This form helps us to understand your needs, availability, and preferences, so we can match them with suitable opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your Email address for contact purposes.
3. Indicate your Contact Preference (Yes/No).
4. Select your available time and date for volunteering.
5. Choose your Volunteer Role (Yes/No).
6. If you have an Emergency Contact, provide their name, phone number, and email.
7. Read and agree to the Volunteer Agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (3)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you. Please use a real email address that you will check regularly.
* **Contact Preference (4)**: Select 'Yes' if you want to be contacted through email, phone, or in-person. Select 'No' if you do not want to be contacted through these methods.
* **Available Time (5)**: Choose a time slot when you are available to volunteer. Please select the time that works best for you.
* **Available Date (6)**: Choose a date when you are available to volunteer. Please select the date that works best for you.
* **Volunteer Role (7)**: Select 'Yes' if you are available for general volunteering, 'No' if you have specific volunteering preferences or limitations.
* **Emergency Contact (8)**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (9)**: If you have provided an Emergency Contact, enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email (10)**: If you have provided an Emergency Contact, enter their email address.
* **Volunteer Agreement (11)**: Please read the agreement carefully and sign it to indicate your understanding and acceptance of our volunteer terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [contact email].
* We appreciate your willingness to volunteer and help those in need!
