# Festival Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience at the festival. Your feedback will help us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Use your real name and email address for identification purposes.
3. Select your response from the options provided or type a detailed answer if the option doesn't fit your experience.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall experience at the festival. Choose from the options: Very good, Somewhat good, Neutral, Very bad.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time you attended the festival.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, required): Enter the time you attended the festival.
