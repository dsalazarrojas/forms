# Research Proposal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Proposal Registration Form is used to gather information about research projects to be registered by research teams. This form is a crucial tool for tracking and managing research activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of your research proposal in the "Research Proposal Registration" text field.
2. Specify if your research institution is affiliated with the project ("Research Institution" field).
3. Enter the title of your research project in the "Research Project Title" field.
4. Select the research areas relevant to your project from the list provided in the "Research Area" field.
5. Enter the name of the lead researcher for the project in the "Research Lead" field.
6. Enter the start and end dates of the project in the "Research Start Date" and "Research End Date" fields, respectively.
7. If applicable, enter the budget for the project in the "Research Budget" field.
8. Select the funding source for the project from the options provided in the "Research Funding" field.
9. Select the status of the project from the options provided in the "Research Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Research Proposal Registration**:
  A brief description of your research proposal.
- **Research Institution**:
  Specify if your research institution is affiliated with the project.
  (Yes/No)
- **Research Project Title**:
  Enter the title of your research project.
- **Research Area**:
  Select the research areas relevant to your project.
  (Area 1, Area 2, Area 3)
- **Research Lead**:
  Enter the name of the lead researcher for the project.
- **Research Start Date**:
  Enter the start date of the project.
- **Research End Date**:
  Enter the end date of the project.
- **Research Budget**:
  If applicable, enter the budget for the project.
- **Research Funding**:
  Select the funding source for the project.
  (Government, Private, Self-Support)
- **Research Status**:
  Select the status of the project.
  (Active, Inactive, Approved)

## Tips
- Ensure that you have all necessary information about your research project before filling out this form.
- Verify that you have not already submitted a similar proposal using this form to avoid duplication.
- Review your form submission carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
