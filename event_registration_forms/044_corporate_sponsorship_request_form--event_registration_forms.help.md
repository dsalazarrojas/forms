# corporate_sponsorship_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for corporate sponsors to request sponsorship for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the fields for your organization's information, including the name and address.
2. Provide the name and date of the event you are requesting sponsorship for.
3. Fill out the fields for your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
4. Provide the sponsor's name, title, address, phone number, and email.
5. Check the box to agree to the terms of the sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **organization**: This is the name of your organization. Please fill out the name of your organization. (Required)
* **event-name**: This is the name of the event you are requesting sponsorship for. (Not Required)
* **event-date**: This is the date of the event. Please select the date of the event. (Not Required)
* **event-time**: This is the time of the event. Please select the time of the event. (Not Required)
* **project-name**: This is the name of the project related to the event. (Not Required)
* **address**: This is the address of your organization. Please fill out the address of your organization. (Not Required)
* **contact-number**: This is your organization's contact phone number. Please fill out your contact number. (Not Required)
* **email**: This is your organization's email. Please fill out your email. (Not Required)
* **sponsor-name**: This is the name of the corporate sponsor. Please fill out the name of the corporate sponsor. (Not Required)
* **sponsor-title**: This is the title of the corporate sponsor. Please fill out the title of the corporate sponsor. (Not Required)
* **sponsor-address**: This is the address of the corporate sponsor. Please fill out the address of the corporate sponsor. (Not Required)
* **sponsor-contact-number**: This is the contact phone number of the corporate sponsor. Please fill out the contact number of the corporate sponsor. (Not Required)
* **sponsor-email**: This is the email of the corporate sponsor. Please fill out the email of the corporate sponsor. (Not Required)
* **reason-for-sponsorship**: This is the reason why you are requesting sponsorship for the event. Please fill out the reason for sponsorship. (Not Required)
* **terms-agreement**: Check this box to agree to the terms of the sponsorship. (Required)
* **agreement-date**: This is the date of the agreement. Please select the date of the agreement. (Not Required)
* **agreement-time**: This is the time of the agreement. Please select the time of the agreement. (Not Required)
* **agreement-number**: This is the agreement number. Please fill out the agreement number. (Not Required)
* **agreement-details**: This is any additional information about the agreement. Please fill out any additional agreement details. (Not Required)
* **sponsor-note**: This is a note for the corporate sponsor. Please fill out any notes for the sponsor. (Not Required)
* **contact-number**: This is the contact phone number of the representative of your organization. Please fill out the contact number. (Not Required)
* **contact-number 2**: This is the contact phone number of your representative. Please fill out the contact number 2. (Not Required)
* **contact-number 3**: This is the contact phone number of the alternate representative. Please fill out the contact number 3. (Not Required)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Please review all fields carefully to ensure accurate information.
