# Art Event Management Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Event Management Assessment Form is a comprehensive tool for evaluating and improving art events. It helps event organizers and managers gather feedback on various aspects of the event, including its success, planning, and organization. This form is designed to be a guide for event planners and organizers to assess their events and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your event details: Enter your event name, date, and type (e.g., Exhibition, Auction, Workshop, Festival, Conference).
2. Enter your contact information: Provide your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Assess your event: Evaluate your event's success, planning quality, vendor coordination, and audience engagement.
4. Identify challenges: List the main challenges you faced during the event (e.g., venue or logistics, weather or external factors, vendor or artist issues).
5. Suggest improvements: Provide ideas for improvements for future events.
6. Additional comments: Share any additional feedback or comments about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Section: Organizer Information** (`section_organizer_info`): This section is for entering your event organizer's name, email address, and phone number.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event, e.g., "Art Exhibition 2023."
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Type of Event** (`event_type`): Choose from the dropdown menu: Exhibition, Auction, Workshop, Festival, Conference, or Other.
* **Actual Attendance** (`attendance_actual`): Enter the actual number of attendees.
* **Expected Attendance** (`attendance_expected`): Enter the expected number of attendees.
* **Overall Event Success** (`overall_success`): Evaluate the event's success by choosing one of the options: Exceeded expectations, Met expectations, Partially met expectations, Below expectations.
* **Quality of Planning and Organization** (`planning_quality`): Evaluate the planning quality by choosing one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor.
* **Vendor and Participant Coordination** (`vendor_coordination`): Evaluate the vendor and participant coordination by choosing one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor.
* **Audience Engagement and Experience** (`audience_engagement`): Evaluate the audience engagement and experience by choosing one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, Poor.
* **Main Challenges Encountered** (`main_challenges`): Choose multiple challenges from the dropdown menu, if applicable.
* **Describe Major Challenges** (`challenge_details`): Provide a brief description of the major challenges faced during the event.
* **Key Improvements for Future Events** (`improvement_ideas`): Provide ideas for improvements for future events.
* **Budget Planning Feedback** (`budget_feedback`): Share your feedback on the event's budget planning.
* **Timeline and Scheduling Feedback** (`timeline_feedback`): Share your feedback on the event's timeline and scheduling.
* **Will you Organize this Event Again** (`repeat_event`): Choose one of the options: Yes - definitely, Yes - probably, Maybe, Probably not, False.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Share any additional feedback or comments about the event.
