# Transport Working Group Meeting Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect registration information from attendees for the Transport Working Group Meeting. It will help the organizers and attendees to have a better understanding of the session's schedule, preferences, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **User Input Page (Page 1)**
	* This page is used to collect general information from the attendees.
	* Please fill in the required fields.
2. **Session Preferences (Page 2)**
	* Select your preferred session options from the dropdown menu.
	* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requirements, please indicate them on this page.
3. **Contact Information (Page 3)**
	* Enter your contact information if you have not already done so.
4. **Transport Preferences (Page 4)**
	* Select your transport preferences for the meeting.
	* If you have any additional transport requirements, please include them in the text box.
5. **Session Details (Page 5)**
	* Enter any additional session details or comments.
6. **Session Policies (Page 6)**
	* Review and agree to the session policies.
7. **User Session Preferences (Page 7)**
	* Select your user session preferences for the meeting.
8. **Submit and Review (Page 8)**
	* Review your form submission and make any necessary corrections before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input Page (Page 1)**
	* **title**: The title of the form.
	* **text**: A text field to collect general information from the attendees.
* **Session Preferences (Page 2)**
	* **Yes/No (Select Multiple)**: Choose whether you want to attend the session, participate in discussions, or both.
* **Contact Information (Page 3)**
	* **text**: A text field to enter your contact information.
* **Transport Preferences (Page 4)**
	* **text**: A text field to select your transport preferences.
* **Session Details (Page 5)**
	* **text**: A text field to enter any additional session details or comments.
* **Session Policies (Page 6)**
	* A text field to review and agree to the session policies.
* **User Session Preferences (Page 7)**
	* **Yes/No (Select One)**: Choose whether you want to attend the session, participate in discussions, or both.
* **Submit and Review (Page 8)**
	* A text field to submit and review your form submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review your form submission carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the meeting organizers.
