# Eco Tourism Guide Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for guests to provide essential information for an Eco Tourism Guide, specifically for accommodation-related activities. It is crucial to ensure that the information entered is accurate and complete for the guest's stay to be processed smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Guest Information** in the first field. This is an optional field but helps us to better understand your preferences and needs.
2. Provide your **Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields. These fields are required to ensure we get your full name correctly.
3. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** to enable us to contact you regarding any updates or concerns about your stay.
4. Choose the **Checkin Date** and **Checkout Date** for your stay. This is essential to plan your accommodation accordingly.
5. Select your preferred **Room Type** from the options provided. This will help us to assign you the suitable room for your stay.
6. Enter the **Room Number** of the room you have been assigned.
7. Provide your **Accommodation ID** if you have one. This will help us to verify your accommodation details.
8. Select the type of **Accommodation Type** from the provided options.
9. Enter the **Accommodation Name** if required.
10. Enter your **Address** for easy reference and contact.
11. Add any **Notes** about your stay, if necessary.
12. If you have any **Special Request**, please enter the details in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information** (1): This is an optional field to provide additional guest information.
* **Name** (2): Please enter your full name as it should be spelled.
* **Last Name** (3): Enter the last part of your name.
* **Email** (4): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Checkin Date** (6): Select the date you plan to arrive at the accommodation.
* **Checkout Date** (7): Select the date you plan to depart from the accommodation.
* **Room Type** (8): Choose between **Private Room** and **Shared Room** for your stay.
* **Room Number** (9): Enter the room number you are assigned.
* **Accommodation ID** (10): If you have an accommodation ID, please enter it here.
* **Accommodation Type** (11): Select the type of accommodation from **Hostel**, **Hotel**, or **Resort**.
* **Accommodation Name** (12): Enter the name of the accommodation if required.
* **Address** (13): Enter your address for easy reference and contact.
* **Notes** (14): Add any notes about your stay.
* **Special Request** (15): If you have any special requests, please enter the details here.
* **Special Request Details** (16): Provide any specific details about your special request.
