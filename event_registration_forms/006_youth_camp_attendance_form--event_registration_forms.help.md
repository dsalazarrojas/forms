# Youth Camp Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and staff members attending a youth camp. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the instructions and guidelines provided in the internal reasoning block above.
- Select the correct tabs or pages (if any) for the type of information you are entering (participant or staff).
- Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on any camp-related documents.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you about the camp.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you about the camp.
- **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD or DD/MM.
- **Participant Date of Service** (`participant_dos`, select_one, optional): Select the month you will be attending the camp (e.g., Jan, Feb, Mar, etc.).
- **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the genders that apply to you.
- **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on any camp-related documents.
- **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you about the camp.
- **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you about the camp.
- **Staff Date of Birth** (`staff_dob`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD or DD/MM.
- **Staff Date of Service** (`staff_dos`, select_one, optional): Select the month you will be working at the camp (e.g., Jan, Feb, Mar, etc.).
- **Staff Gender** (`staff_gender`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the genders that apply to you.
- **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, optional): Select your role at the camp (e.g., Attendee, Staff, Volunteer).

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
- If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will use this information to ensure that you have a great experience at the camp.
