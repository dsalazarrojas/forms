# Media Training Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for media training appointments. It helps the organizers to book appointments and gather relevant details about the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **participant_name**, **media_type**, **appointment_date**, and **appointment_time**.
2. If you want to provide your **participant_phone** number, you can do so in this optional field.
3. Choose the **media_type** that suits your needs from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Training Appointment Form**: This is the title of the form and is not a field to fill in.
* **Participant Name**: Please enter your name to identify yourself for the appointment.
* **Media Type**: Select the type of media you want to engage with (e.g., video production, photography, live streaming).
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose the time slot for your appointment.
* **Participant Phone**: If you would like to provide your phone number for further communication, please do so in this optional field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure a successful appointment booking.
* Review your selection carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements, please let us know in your submission and we will do our best to accommodate your needs.
