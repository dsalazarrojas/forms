# Organizational Capability Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to register for an event by providing their information and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide participant information on the first page, including any relevant details about the participant.
2. On the second page, enter the payment details, including the payment method you'll be using.
3. Finally, enter your billing address and phone number on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you'll enter participant information.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the payment details you want to associate with your registration.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the following payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your billing address if you've chosen a payment method that requires it.
* **Phone Number** (`billing_phone`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your phone number, as this will be used for billing purposes if necessary.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful submission.
* Double-check your payment method and billing details before submitting.
