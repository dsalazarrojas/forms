# Stage Setup Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Setup Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from authorized personnel to ensure that the stage setup is properly configured and equipped for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **General Information** page with your basic details.
2. Proceed to the **Event Details** page and provide the necessary information.
3. Move on to the **Stage Requirements** page and specify your stage needs.
4. Next, visit the **Technical Requirements** page and list your technical needs.
5. Finally, provide any additional information on the **Additional Information** page.
6. Review your submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false): This page collects your basic information, such as your name and contact details.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): Here, you will provide details about the event you are planning to hold on the stage. Please ensure that you fill out this section accurately.
* **Stage Requirements** (`stage_requirements`, text, required: false): List the equipment and resources you require for the stage setup. Be specific and detailed in your response.
* **Technical Requirements** (`technical_requirements`, text, required: false): Specify any technical requirements for the stage, such as power needs and connectivity requirements.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the stage setup process.
