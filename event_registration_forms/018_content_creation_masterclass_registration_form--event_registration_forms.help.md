# Content Creation Masterclass Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect attendee registration information for the Content Creation Masterclass. Please fill out the form with your details and preferences to secure your spot.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have the following information ready: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Job Title, Industry, Content Creation Experience Level, Preferred Masterclass Date, Attendance Type, Topics of Interest, and Payment Method.
- Select your preferred masterclass date, attendance type, and payment method.
- Choose how you would like to attend the masterclass.
- Select the topics you are interested in learning about.
- Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.
- Confirm your registration and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Company Name** (text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter its name.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Industry** (select_one, required): Select the industry you work in.
* **Content Creation Experience Level** (select_one, required): Select your experience level in content creation.
* **Masterclass Date** (select_one, required): Choose a preferred masterclass date.
* **Attendance Type** (select_one, required): Select how you plan to attend the masterclass.
* **Topics of Interest** (select_multiple, required): Select the topics you are interested in learning about.
* **Learning Goals** (text, required): Describe your learning objectives for the masterclass.
* **Current Challenges** (text, optional): Share any content creation challenges you are facing.
* **Dietary Requirements** (text, optional): If you are attending in-person, list any dietary requirements.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (select_one, required): Choose how you learned about this masterclass.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select the payment method for the masterclass.
* **Registration Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of registration for your group or yourself.
* **Terms and Conditions** (select_one, required): Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
* **Marketing Consent** (select_one, required): Choose whether you would like to receive marketing communications.
* **Registration Date** (date, required): Enter the date you are registering for the masterclass.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Share any additional information about your registration.
