# Newsletter Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to volunteer for our newsletter project.

## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out this form, start by providing your name and contact information.
- Choose your volunteer type and select one or more shifts that you are available to work.
- If you have any additional comments or messages, feel free to include them in the "Message" field.
- Finally, agree to the terms and conditions of the project by selecting the corresponding option in the "Agree" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are available to volunteer.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, optional): Select the time you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Type** (`form_volunteer_type`, select_one, required): Choose the role you are interested in volunteering for: Volunteer Coordinator, Editor, Designer, Writer, Proofreader, Translator, Social Media Manager, or Other.
* **Shift** (`form_volunteer_shift`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more shifts that you are available to work: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend.
* **Message** (`form_message`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to include them here.
* **Agree** (`form_agree`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the project. If you select "False", you will not be able to volunteer.
* **Agree Message** (`form_agree_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have regarding the agreement.
* **Agree Terms** (`form_agree_terms`, select_multiple, required): Select the agreement option that best represents your understanding of the project's terms and conditions.
