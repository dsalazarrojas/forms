# Graduation Cap And Gown Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for ordering their graduation cap and gown. It helps to ensure accurate and timely ordering of caps and gowns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the three pages of the form, one by one.
2. Provide your contact information on the first page.
3. Choose your graduation attire on the second page, making sure to select the correct options for size, color, and other details.
4. Fill out any additional details on the third page, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmation of your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number to receive any urgent messages about your order.
* **Grad Attire Size** (`grad_attire_size`, `select`, required): Select the size of your graduation attire, such as 5'8" or 6'0".
* **Grad Attire Color** (`grad_attire_color`, `select`, required): Choose the color of your graduation attire, such as Gold or Silver.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details or special requests, such as "I'd like to purchase a separate mortarboard" or "I'd like my name to be embroidered on the cap".

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your selection for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can contact us for any updates or changes to your order.
