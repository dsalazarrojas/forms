# Resource Booking Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and manage resources such as meeting rooms, equipment, and other assets within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the resource you want to book (e.g., Meeting Room 1, Meeting Room 2, or Meeting Room 3).
2. Choose the equipment you need for your booking (e.g., Chair, Table, or Projector).
3. Enter the start and end dates of your booking.
4. Provide your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your booking.
6. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
