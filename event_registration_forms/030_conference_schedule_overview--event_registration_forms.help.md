# Conference Schedule Overview - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help attendees create a personalized schedule for the conference, selecting sessions and activities they are interested in, and providing any additional information that may be helpful for the organizers. By filling out this form, attendees can ensure that they have the best possible experience at the conference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields: Attendee Name, Email Address, and Primary Conference Track.
2. Select the sessions you are interested in for each day of the conference. You can choose as many or as few sessions as you like.
3. If you have any dietary requirements, please let us know by filling out the Dietary Requirements field.
4. Specify how you would like to receive session materials and access to recordings.
5. Choose whether you would like to have access to speaker meetups and Q&A sessions.
6. Select the networking events you would like to attend.
7. If you have any personal goals or objectives for the conference, please describe them in the Personal Conference Goals field.
8. Indicate any sessions that you feel are a must-attend.
9. If you have any scheduling conflicts, please describe them in the Scheduling Conflicts field.
10. Choose your preferred meal functions and meal options.
11. Specify whether you have any special requests or preferences for sessions materials.
12. Choose whether you would like to have access to recordings or not.
13. Indicate whether you will be using the conference mobile app or printing out your schedule.
14. Choose whether you require calendar export or not.
15. Indicate whether you want session reminders or not.
16. Finally, add any additional notes or information that you would like to share with the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your conference badge and any other materials.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address so that we can keep you informed about conference updates and information.
* **Registration ID** (`registration_id`, text, optional): If you have a confirmation number, please enter it here.
* **Primary Conference Track** (`conference_track`, select_one, required): Choose the area of focus you are most interested in (Technology and Innovation, Business Strategy, Research and Development, Leadership, General Interest, or Undecided).
* **Day One Sessions of Interest** (`day_one_sessions`, select_multiple, optional): Select as many sessions as you like for the first day of the conference.
* **Day Two Sessions of Interest** (`day_two_sessions`, select_multiple, optional): Select as many sessions as you like for the second day of the conference.
* **Day Three Sessions of Interest** (`day_three_sessions`, select_multiple, optional): Select as many sessions as you like for the third day of the conference.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, text, optional): Rank your workshop preferences if you have chosen to attend a workshop.
* **Networking Events Attending** (`networking_events`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the networking events you would like to attend.
* **Interested in Speaker Meetups** (`speaker_meetups`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are very interested in attending speaker meetups, maybe interested, or not at all.
* **Planning to Visit Exhibition** (`exhibition_visit`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are definitely planning to visit the exhibition or not.
* **Personal Conference Goals** (`personal_goals`, text, optional): Describe what you want to achieve or accomplish during the conference.
* **Must-Attend Sessions** (`must_attend_sessions`, text, optional): Indicate any priority sessions that you feel are absolutely essential to attend.
* **Any Scheduling Conflicts** (`scheduling_conflicts`, text, optional): If there are any times when you are unavailable, please specify them.
* **Meal Functions Attending** (`meal_functions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the meal functions you would like to attend.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any dietary requirements or restrictions you may have.
* **Session Materials Preference** (`session_materials`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to receive session materials (Digital Download, Printed Handouts, Both, or None).
* **Want Access to Recordings** (`recording_access`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would like to have access to recordings of the sessions.
* **Will Use Conference Mobile App** (`mobile_app`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you will be using the conference mobile app or printing out your schedule.
* **Need Calendar Export** (`calendar_export`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you require calendar export (iCal or Google Calendar).
* **Want Session Reminders** (`reminders`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would like to receive session reminders or not.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information or notes that you would like to share with the organizers.
