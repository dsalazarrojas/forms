## Athletic Meet Permission Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians of athletes participating in athletic events.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Select the event name and date.
2. Enter the athlete's name and date of birth.
3. Provide your own contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Answer questions about the athlete's health and medical clearance.
5. Confirm your consent and understanding of the event's liability waiver and emergency procedures.
6. Choose your transportation method to the event.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event/Meet Name (Event/Meet Name)**: Enter the name of the athletic event or meet.
	+ This field is required to identify the specific event the athlete is participating in.
* **Event Date (Event Date)**: Enter the date of the athletic meet or competition.
	+ This field is required to ensure the event occurs on the correct date.
* **Event Location (Event Location)**: Enter the city and venue of the athletic event.
	+ This field is required to ensure the event occurs at the correct location.
* **Athlete Name (Athlete Name)**: Enter the full name of the athlete.
	+ This field is required to identify the athlete participating in the event.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**: Enter the date of birth of the athlete.
	+ This field is required to confirm the athlete's age and eligibility.
* **Grade Level (Grade Level)**: Select the current grade level of the athlete.
	+ This field is not required but can help with age verification.
* **Parent/Guardian Name (Parent/Guardian Name)**: Enter your name, the parent or guardian of the athlete.
	+ This field is required to identify the person providing consent.
* **Parent/Guardian Email (Parent/Guardian Email)**: Enter your primary contact email.
	+ This field is required for communication with you about the event.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone (Parent/Guardian Phone)**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
	+ This field is required for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Emergency Contact Name)**: Enter the name of an alternative emergency contact if you are unavailable.
	+ This field is not required but can be helpful in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Emergency Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the alternative emergency contact.
	+ This field is not required but can be helpful in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)**: List any medical conditions or allergies the athlete has.
	+ This field is not required but can help with athlete safety.
* **Current Medications (Current Medications)**: List any medications the athlete is taking.
	+ This field is not required but can help with athlete safety.
* **Has athlete been ill in the past two weeks? (Has athlete been ill in the past two weeks?)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to answer this question.
	+ This field is required to ensure athlete safety.
* **Physician Medical Clearance (Physician Medical Clearance)**: Select 'Yes' or 'Not Required' to answer this question.
	+ This field is required to confirm medical clearance.
* **I give permission for my child to participate in this athletic event (Permission Acknowledgment)**: Select 'I Agree' to confirm your consent.
	+ This field is required to confirm your consent.
* **I understand and accept the liability waiver (Liability Waiver)**: Select 'I Accept' to confirm your understanding of the waiver.
	+ This field is required to confirm your understanding of the waiver.
* **I understand emergency procedures will be followed (Emergency Procedures Acknowledgment)**: Select 'I Understand' to confirm your understanding of the event's emergency procedures.
	+ This field is required to confirm your understanding of emergency procedures.
* **How will athlete travel to the event? (Transportation Method)**: Select the method of transportation the athlete will use.
	+ This field is required to plan for athlete transportation.
* **Will parent/guardian attend the event? (Spectator Attendance)**: Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Uncertain'.
	+ This field is not required but can help with event planning.
* **I certify that the information provided is accurate and complete (Parent Signature Consent)**: Select 'I Certify' to confirm your consent.
	+ This field is required to confirm your consent.

### Tips

* Please fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the event organizers for clarification.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the event organizers or a responsible person.

This form is designed to ensure the safety and well-being of the athlete. Please take the time to fill it out accurately and completely.
