# Regional Academic Decathlon Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regional Academic Decathlon Tournament Entry Form is used to register teams for the tournament. It collects information about the team, including school and team details, event dates and locations, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by a *).
2. Enter your school name and team name in the respective fields.
3. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about your team leader, including their name, phone number, and email.
4. Enter your school address, city, state, and district correctly.
5. Choose the correct division and regional event ID.
6. Enter the event date and time, ensuring they match the format specified in the date field.
7. Review your responses for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter your school's name.
* **Team Name** (`school_name_2`, text, required): Enter your team's name.
* **Team Leader's Name** (`team_leaders_name`, text, required): Enter your team leader's name.

* **Team Leader's Email** (`team_leaders_email`, email, required): Enter your team leader's email address in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter your school's address in the correct format (e.g., Street, City, State ZIP).
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required): Enter the number of team members.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the event date in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the event time in the correct format (e.g., HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the event location.
* **Division** (`division`, select_one, required): Choose the correct division for your team.
* **Regional Event ID** (`regional_event_id`, select_multiple, required): Choose the correct regional event ID for your team.
* **School Code** (`school_code`, text, required): Enter your school code.
* **Team Code** (`team_code`, text, required): Enter your team code.
* **Team Name (2)** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter your team's name.

* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to avoid any issues during the registration process.
