# Business Networking Event Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees to a business networking event. It helps us to get to know you better and ensure that you have a great experience at our event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your details in the fields provided
- Make sure to answer all the required fields
- Review and agree to the terms and conditions at the end

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Fill in your complete name as per your ID card or passport.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your position or role.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your industry sector from the list:
	+ Technology
	+ Finance
	+ Healthcare
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ Services
	+ Education
	+ Non-profit
	+ Government
	+ Other
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the number of employees in your company:
	+ Startup (1-10)
	+ Small (11-50)
	+ Medium (51-250)
	+ Large (251-1000)
	+ Enterprise (1000+)
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Select the event name you are attending from the list provided or enter the name if not available.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`, number, required): Enter the number of people who will be attending with you.
* **Networking Goals** (`networking_goals`, text, required): Describe the networking goals you are hoping to achieve.
* **Types of Connections You Seek** (`target_connections`, text, optional): Describe the types of professionals or companies you are looking to connect with.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`special_dietary_needs`, select_one, optional): Select any special dietary needs:
	+ None
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-free
	+ Kosher
	+ Halal
	+ Other
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Describe any accessibility accommodations you may need.
* **Have you attended our events before?** (`prior_attendance`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have attended our events before:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ First Time
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you will pay for your registration:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ On-site Payment
	+ Free Registration
* **Registration Fee Amount** (`registration_fee`, number, optional): Enter the event registration fee amount in USD.
* **I accept the event terms and conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Select to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
