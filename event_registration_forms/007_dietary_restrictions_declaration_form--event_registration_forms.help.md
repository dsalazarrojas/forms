# Dietary Restrictions Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect dietary restrictions information from participants in an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Registration Information** page with your basic event registration details.
2. Move on to the **Participant Details** page and fill out the required fields with your participant information.
3. Optionally, you can add additional participant details on the **Participant Details 3** and **Participant Details 4** pages.
