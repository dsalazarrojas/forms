# Win A Survival Kit Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Win A Survival Kit Contest Entry Form is designed to collect personal information from participants in the "Win A Survival Kit Contest". This information will be used to verify the identity of contestants and facilitate communication with them throughout the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the required fields.
2. Enter your Email address and check that it is correct.
3. Provide your Phone number for easy contactability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email** (Required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you about contest updates and notifications.

### Phone
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you easily in case of any updates or questions.

## Tips
* Please fill in accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth experience for us to contact and verify your entry.
* If you already have an entry in the contest, you will not be able to submit a new one to avoid duplication.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
