# digital_exhibit_creation_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for digital exhibit creation projects. It is a simple form that gathers user information and donation details for further processing and review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your User Name, which is your personal identifier for this transaction.
2. Enter your email address for contact and donation receipt purposes.
3. Provide your Phone Number for further communication.
4. Enter the amount you wish to donate for the digital exhibit creation project.
5. Select the type of donation you are making (multiple options are available).
6. Provide a brief Description of the donation, if necessary.
7. Choose your preferred Payment Method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
8. Finally, click on the Submit button to submit your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can input your personal identifier.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact and donation receipt purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount you wish to donate for the digital exhibit creation project.
* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the type of donation you are making. Options include: 
  * Option 1
  * Option 2
  * Option 3
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the donation, if necessary.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose your preferred payment method. Options include:
  * Credit Card
  * PayPal
  * Bank Transfer
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click on this button to submit your donation.
