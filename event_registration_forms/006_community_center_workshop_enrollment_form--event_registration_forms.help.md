# Community Center Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to enroll in workshops offered by the community center. The form gathers contact information, workshop selection, and other relevant details necessary for workshop administration and participant communication.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is your legal surname as it appears on your government-issued ID.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your **Email Address** in the format `your_email@example.com`. This is used for workshop communications.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
6. Enter your **Street Address** in the format "Street Number, Street Name, Apartment Number, City, State ZIP".
7. Enter your **City, State, ZIP** in the format "City, State ZIP".
8. Select your preferred **Workshop Selection** from the list provided. Choose one of the available workshops.
9. Choose your preferred **Workshop Date** from the calendar provided.
10. Indicate your **Experience Level** with the selected workshop by choosing Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
11. Choose how you **Learned about this Workshop** by selecting an option from the list provided.
12. If applicable, describe any **Accessibility Needs** you may have.
13. If refreshments will be provided, indicate any **Dietary Restrictions** you may have.
14. Choose your payment method by selecting Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Check, Scholarship, or Waiver.
15. Check if you need financial **Assistance** by selecting True or False.
16. Enter the **Emergency Contact Name** of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
17. Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone** number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
18. Agree to the **Liability Waiver Agreement** by selecting "Yes, I Agree" or "Other" (if you have concerns).
19. If you have any **Additional Comments or Questions**, enter them in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Participant First Name, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (Participant Last Name, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, required): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (Email, required): Enter your email address in the format `your_email@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (Text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Street Address** (Text, required): Enter your home address.
* **City, State, ZIP** (Text, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Workshop Selection** (Select One, required): Choose one of the available workshops.
* **Workshop Date** (Date, required): Select your preferred workshop date.
* **Experience Level** (Select One, required): Choose your experience level with the selected workshop.
* **How Did You Hear About This Workshop** (Select One, optional): Choose how you learned about this workshop.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Text, optional): Describe any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Text, optional): If refreshments will be provided, describe any dietary restrictions.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, required): Choose your payment method.
* **Scholarship Needed** (Select One, required): Check if you need financial assistance.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, required): Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (Select One, required): Agree to the liability waiver terms.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (Text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
