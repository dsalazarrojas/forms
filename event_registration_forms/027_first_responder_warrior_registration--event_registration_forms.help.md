# First Responder Warrior Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The First Responder Warrior Registration form is designed to collect information from first responders who have suffered an injury while performing their duties. This form will help the organization track and manage the registration process for these responders, ensuring they receive the necessary support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on "Submit" to begin the registration process.
2. Enter your First Responder Information, including:
	* **First Responder Information**: Provide a brief description of your role and position as a first responder.
3. Enter your:
	* **name**: This is your full name, as it appears on official documents.
	* **email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
	* **phone**: Enter your contact phone number, including area code and country code if applicable.
4. Select your:
	* **Date of Injury**: Choose the date of the incident that resulted in your injury.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Responder Information** (`first_responder_info`, text, required): A description of your role and position as a first responder.
* **name** (`name`, text, required): Your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): A valid email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number, including area code and country code if applicable.
* **Date of Injury** (`date_of_injury`, date, required): The date of the incident that resulted in your injury.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the information required, please contact your supervisor or a designated representative for guidance.
* This form is only for registered first responders. If you are not a registered first responder, you will not be able to access this form.
