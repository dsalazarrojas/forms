# Team Building Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from team members who wish to participate in a team building workshop. It is intended for team leads and participants to provide their details, preferences, and experiences related to the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your role in the team: Select your role in the team from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the number of participants you are bringing: Type the number of people you are bringing to the workshop.
3. Select your participant details: Choose your participant details such as Veggie or Non-Veggie diet, breakfast, lunch, and dinner preferences.
4. Enter your participant information: Fill in your name, email, phone number, and address.
5. Enter your team lead information (if applicable): Fill in the team lead's name, email, phone number, and address.
6. Provide your workshop experience and experience in organizing: Answer the questions about your experience with workshops and if you have organized any.
7. Confirm if you organize the workshop or lead your team: Check the boxes to confirm your role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role in Team** (`What Is Your Role In The Team`): Select your role in the team. This can be Team Lead or Team Member.
* **How Many Participants Are You Bringing** (`How Many Participants Are You Bringing`): Type the number of team members you are bringing to the workshop.
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`): Choose your participant details such as Veggie or Non-Veggie diet, and your meal preferences.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`Dietary Preferences`): Select your diet preference (Veggie or Non-Veggie).
* **Meal Preferences** (`Meal Preferences`): Choose your meal preferences for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`): If you have any special requirements, type them in this field.
* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`): Fill in your name.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`): Type your phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`Participant Address`): Fill in your address.
* **Participant Postal Code** (`Participant Postal Code`): Enter your postal code.
* **Team Name** (`Team Name`): If you are a team lead, enter your team name.
* **Team Lead's Name** (`Team Lead's Name`): If you are a team lead, fill in your team lead's name.
* **Team Lead's Email** (`Team Lead's Email`): Enter your team lead's email.
* **Team Lead's Phone** (`Team Lead's Phone`): Type your team lead's phone number.
* **Team Lead's Address** (`Team Lead's Address`): If you are a team lead, fill in your team lead's address.
* **Team Lead's Postal Code** (`Team Lead's Postal Code`): Enter your team lead's postal code.
* **Team Lead's Position** (`Team Lead's Position`): If you are a team lead, enter your team lead's position.
* **Team Lead's Workshop Experience** (`Team Lead's Workshop Experience`): Type your team lead's workshop experience.
* **Team Lead Organizes** (`Team Lead Organizes`): Check if your team lead organizes.
* **Participant's Workshop Experience** (`Participant's Workshop Experience`): Type your participant's workshop experience.
* **Participant Organizes** (`Participant Organizes`): Check if you organize the workshop.
* **Participant Leads Workshop Team** (`Participant Leads Workshop Team`): Check if you lead a team for the workshop.
* **Participant Leads Team Workshop** (`Participant Leads Team Workshop`): Check if you lead a team for the workshop.
