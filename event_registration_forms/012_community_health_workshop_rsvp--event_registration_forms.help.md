# Community Health Workshop RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Community Health Workshop RSVP" form is designed to help us get an accurate headcount for our upcoming workshop. Your responses will help us plan for food, accommodations, and other essential aspects of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your attendee information accurately and thoroughly.
2. Select your preferred session time from the options provided.
3. If you have any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, please let us know in the provided fields.
4. If you plan to bring a guest, select "True" and specify the number of guests you'll be bringing.
5. Finally, add any special notes or comments that we should know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID, passport, or driver's license.
* **Email address**: Type in your active email address where we can send updates and reminders about the workshop.
* **Phone number**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number. This is optional.
* **Preferred session time**: Choose the time slot that you prefer to attend the workshop. Our sessions are held during the morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Dietary restrictions**: If you have any food preferences or restrictions (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free), please specify them here. This will help us plan for catering.
* **Accessibility needs**: If you require any special accommodations, such as wheelchair accessibility or sign language interpretation, let us know here.
* **Will you bring a guest?**: If you're bringing a guest, select "True" to let us know.
* **Number of guests**: If you're bringing a guest, please specify the number of guests you'll be bringing.
* **Special notes**: Add any additional comments or information that you think we should know. This is a good place to mention any special circumstances or requests you may have.
