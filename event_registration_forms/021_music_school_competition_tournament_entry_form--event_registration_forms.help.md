# Music School Competition Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register students for a music school competition tournament. It is a crucial step in the registration process, and it helps the competition organizers to gather essential details about each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Read through the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information.
*   Start by filling out the student's information on page 1 (Student Information).
*   Move on to page 2 (Parent Information) and fill out the necessary details if applicable.
*   Provide the student's performance information on page 3.
*   Complete the competition information on page 4.
*   Finally, fill out the submitter's information on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Information (Student Information)**:
    `Student Information` is the first page of the form. Please enter the student's name, age, and date of birth. The student's name should be in the format "First Name Last Name" (e.g., John Smith). If the student is under 18, please provide the parent's/guardian's contact information.
*   **Parent Information (Parent Information)**:
    `Parent Information` is optional and is only required if the student is under 18. Please enter the parent's/guardian's name, contact number, and email address.
*   **Performance Information (Student Performance)**:
    `Performance Information` is a required field. Please enter the student's relevant music performance experience, skill levels, and any notable achievements.
*   **Competition Information (Competition Information)**:
    `Competition Information` is a required field. Please enter the details about the competition, including its date, location, and any specific requirements.
*   **Submitter Information (Submitter Information)**:
    `Submitter Information` is a required field. Please enter your name and contact information, as this is the person submitting the entry on behalf of the student.
