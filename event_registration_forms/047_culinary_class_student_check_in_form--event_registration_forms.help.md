# Culinary Class Student Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Class Student Check In Form is designed to help us collect necessary information from students attending our culinary classes. This form ensures that we have up-to-date student data, which helps us to better serve our students and provide a safe and enjoyable learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student ID number in the "Student" field. This is a required field and is used to identify you as a student in the class.
2. Fill in your name in the "Student Name" field. This is also a required field and helps us to personalize our communication with you.
3. If you have any contact information, please enter it in the "Contact Info" field. This is an optional field and is used in case we need to reach out to you about any important class updates or concerns.
4. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please select the relevant options in the "Dietary Restrictions" and "Allergies" fields. These fields are optional, but it's crucial to help us to ensure your safety and comfort in the class.
5. If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation in the class, please enter them in the "Medical Conditions" field. This is an optional field, but it's essential to inform our staff about your health conditions.
6. You can also provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience in the class in the "Comments" field. This is an optional field, but it helps us to improve our services and make our classes more enjoyable for everyone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student** (`Student`, number, required):
    This field is used to identify you as a student in the class. Please enter your student ID number to ensure that we can track your progress and attendance in the class.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required):
    Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, email, optional):
    If you have an email address or phone number where we can reach you, please enter it here. This helps us to send you important class updates or reminders.
* **Cooking Level** (`Cooking Level`, select_multiple, optional):
    Select all the cooking levels that apply to you:
    • Beginner
    • Intermediate
    • Advanced
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, select_multiple, optional):
    Select all the dietary restrictions that apply to you:
    • None
    • Vegetarian
    • Vegan
    • Gluten Free
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, select_multiple, optional):
    Select all the allergies that apply to you:
    • None
    • Other
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, optional):
    If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their name and contact information here.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, text, optional):
    If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation in the class, please enter them here.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional):
    If you have any feedback, suggestions, or comments about your experience in the class, please enter them here.
