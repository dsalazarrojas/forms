# Birdwatching Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for birdwatching enthusiasts to register for various tournaments hosted by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the tournament you wish to participate in from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your registration date.
3. Choose whether you are registering as an individual or as a team.
4. Enter your primary contact information (first name, last name, email, phone number, and birdwatching experience level).
5. If registering as a team, enter team member information (team name, size, and member names and emails).
6. Provide details about your participation category, counting location, and primary habitat type.
7. List the equipment you will be using for the tournament.
8. Confirm your experience with previous tournaments.
9. Select whether you have read and understand the tournament rules.
10. Confirm your commitment to ethical birdwatching practices.
11. Choose your payment method.
12. Enter any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Name** (id: 2, required): Select the tournament you wish to participate in.
* **Tournament Date** (id: 3, required): Enter the date of the tournament.
* **Registration Date** (id: 4, required): Enter today's date to confirm your registration.
* **Entry Type** (id: 6, required): Choose whether you are registering as an individual or team.
* **First Name** (id: 8, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 9, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (id: 10, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (id: 11, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Birdwatching Experience Level** (id: 12, required): Select your birdwatching experience level.
* **Team Name** (id: 14, optional): If registering as a team, enter the team name.
* **Number of Team Members** (id: 15, optional): If registering as a team, enter the total number of team members.
* **Team Member 1 Name** (id: 16, optional): If registering as a team, enter the name of team member 1.
* **Team Member 1 Email** (id: 17, optional): If registering as a team, enter team member 1's email.
* **Team Member 2 Name** (id: 18, optional): If registering as a team, enter the name of team member 2.
* **Team Member 2 Email** (id: 19, optional): If registering as a team, enter team member 2's email.
* **Team Member 3 Name** (id: 20, optional): If registering as a team, enter the name of team member 3.
* **Team Member 3 Email** (id: 21, optional): If registering as a team, enter team member 3's email.
* **Participation Category** (id: 22, required): Select your participation category (e.g., local area count, regional count, etc.).
* **Counting Location or Area** (id: 23, required): Enter the location where you will be observing birds.
* **Primary Habitat Type** (id: 24, required): Select the primary habitat type where you will be observing birds.
* **Equipment Available** (id: 25, required): List the equipment you have available for the tournament (e.g., binoculars, spotting scope, etc.).
* **Previous Tournament Participation** (id: 27, required): Select whether you have participated in a previous tournament.
* **Best Previous Result** (id: 28, optional): If applicable, enter your best previous result.
* **Goals for This Tournament** (id: 29, optional): Enter your goals for this tournament.
* **I have read and understand the tournament rules** (id: 31, required): Confirm that you understand the tournament rules.
* **I commit to ethical birdwatching practices** (id: 32, required): Confirm your commitment to ethical birdwatching practices.
* **I will participate fairly and honestly** (id: 33, required): Confirm that you will participate fairly and honestly.
* **Registration Fee Amount** (id: 35, optional): Enter the registration fee amount.
* **Payment Method** (id: 36, required): Choose your payment method (e.g., online payment, check, etc.).
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (id: 37, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
