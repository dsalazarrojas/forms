# Portfolio Review Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Portfolio Review Appointment Form is used to schedule a portfolio review session between you and a reviewer. This form ensures that both parties are aligned on the necessary details for the review.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you wish to schedule: 
    * Event Type: Choose this option if you are requesting a general portfolio review.
    * Meeting: This option is for meetings that do not require a review of a portfolio.
    * Call: This option is for calls that do not require a review of a portfolio.
2. Choose the date for your portfolio review appointment.
3. Select the time for your portfolio review appointment.
4. Provide the location for your portfolio review appointment.
5. Enter any client-specific notes that the reviewer should be aware of.
6. Provide details about the review you are requesting.
7. Choose the tool you wish to use during the review.
8. Choose the timeframe for the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_type** (`Event Type`, select_one, **required**):
  This field allows you to select the type of event you are requesting. Please choose the option that best describes your event. This will help the reviewer know what to expect during the review.
* **date** (`date`, date, **required**):
  Enter the date for your portfolio review appointment. This is a mandatory field and should be a specific date (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **time** (`time`, time, **required**):
  Select the time for your portfolio review appointment. This is a mandatory field and should be a time in 24-hour format (HH:MM:SS).
* **location** (`location`, text, **optional**):
  Provide the location for your portfolio review appointment, if applicable.
* **client_notes** (`client_notes`, text, **optional**):
  Enter any specific notes or requests the reviewer should be aware of. This is optional, but can be useful for providing context.
* **review_details** (`review_details`, text, **optional**):
  Provide an explanation of what you are requesting during the review. This is an optional field, but can help the reviewer understand your request.
* **event_timeframe** (`event_timeframe`, time, **optional**):
  Select the timeframe for your review appointment. This field is optional, but can be useful for providing a range of time when you are available.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, **required**):
  Choose the tool you wish to use during the review. This will help the reviewer be aware of the tools you will be using during the review.

## Tips

* Make sure to choose the correct event type to ensure the reviewer is aware of what to expect.
* Enter specific dates and times for the review to avoid scheduling conflicts.
* Enter accurate information to ensure the reviewer is aware of your needs.
* Choose the correct tool to ensure a smooth review process.
