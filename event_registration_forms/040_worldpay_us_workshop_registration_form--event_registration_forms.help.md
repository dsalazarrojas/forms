# Worldpay US Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worldpay US Workshop Registration Form is used for users to register for the Worldpay US Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, first name, and last name.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter your address.
5. Select your job title.
6. Choose your role (Manager, Staff, or Other).
7. Select your department (Accounting, Sales, Customer Service, or Other).
8. Choose the workshop date.
9. Enter the workshop time.
10. Enter the location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Registration Details
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Role**: Select your role (Manager, Staff, or Other).
* **Department**: Select your department (Accounting, Sales, Customer Service, or Other).

### Page 2: Additional Details
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date of the workshop.
* **Time**: Select the time of the workshop.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the workshop.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that the information is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the workshop organizers.
* This form is not mandatory, but it's recommended that you fill it out accurately to help the workshop organizers prepare for the event.
