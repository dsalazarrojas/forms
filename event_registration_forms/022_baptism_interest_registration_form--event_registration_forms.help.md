# Baptism Interest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in being baptized to understand their needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form in the following order:

1. Registration Date: Enter today's date when you submit the form.
2. First Name, Last Name: Enter your first and last name.
3. Email Address: Enter your primary email address.
4. Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
5. Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
6. Interest Level in Baptism: Choose how interested you are in baptism (Very Interested, Interested, Somewhat Interested, Just Exploring, Undecided).
7. Religious Background: Select your current faith background (Christian, Previously Christian, Other, No Religious Background, Prefer Not to Say).
8. Have You Been Baptized Before?: Choose if you have been baptized before (Yes, No, Not Sure).
9. What Prompted Your Interest in Baptism?: Share what sparked your interest in baptism.
10. Where Are You in Your Decision?: Select where you are in your decision-making process (Just Starting to Explore, Asking Questions, Seriously Considering, Ready to Commit, Already Decided).
11. Do You Attend Church?: Choose how often you attend church (Yes, Regularly, Yes, Occasionally, No, But Interested, False).
12. Church Name (if Applicable): If applicable, enter the name of the church you attend.
13. Does Your Family Support This Decision?: Choose if your family supports your decision (Yes, No, Not Sure, Not Applicable).
14. Do You Have Concerns or Questions?: Share any concerns or questions you have.
15. Preferred Learning Method: Choose how you would like to learn more about baptism (In-Person Meeting, Phone Conversation, Email Exchange, Online Video Discussion, Group Class or Workshop, One-on-One Counseling).
16. Available Meeting Times: Choose when you are usually available to meet (Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Weekend Mornings, Weekend Afternoons).
17. Additional Information About You: Share any additional information that might be helpful for us to know.
18. May We Follow Up With You?: Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up discussions (Yes, No, Only via Email, Only via Phone).
19. May We Share Your Information with the Church?: Choose if you are willing to share your information with the church.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out every required field.
* Choose your interest level honestly.
* Share any additional information that will help us understand your situation.
* If you are unsure about any question, it's okay to ask for help or clarification.
* We respect your privacy and will only use the information you provide to support your interest in baptism.
* We will follow up with you via your preferred method to discuss further.
* Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form!
