# Reusable Product Booth Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide event organizers with necessary information about vendors who want to exhibit at a trade show or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Vendor Information** section with your company's details.
2. Provide the required **Booth Details** for your product.
3. Add a brief **Product Description** to help event organizers understand your product.
4. Check the **Vendor Agreement** box to confirm you've read and agree to the terms.
5. Enter the **Date of Availability** for your product.
6. Select the **Time of Availability** for your product.
7. Add any additional **Notes** about your product or your company.
8. Enter your **Email** address.
9. Enter your **Phone** number.
10. Enter your **Fax** number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, required/optional): This is the first section of the form where you need to fill out your company's information.
* **Booth Details** (`booth_details`, text, required/optional): This section is where you need to provide information about your product, such as its name, category, etc.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): This section is where you enter your company's contact information, including your email, phone number, and fax number.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required/optional): This is where you need to describe your product briefly.
* **Vendor Agreement** (`vendor_agreement`, select_multiple, required/optional): This box should be checked to confirm you've read and agree to the terms of the trade show or event.
* **Product Availability** (`product_availability`, date, required/optional): This is where you need to enter the date when your product will be available.
* **Time of Availability** (`time_of_availability`, time, required/optional): This is where you need to select the time when your product will be available.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): This is an optional section where you can add any additional information about your product or company.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): This is the final step where you submit your application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This is where you need to enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This is where you need to enter your phone number.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, required/optional): This is where you need to enter your fax number.

Note: The fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be filled out for the form to be submitted.
