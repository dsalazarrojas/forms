# Nonprofit Event Coordinator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming a non-profit event coordinator. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Non Profit Event Coordinator**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on your application.
2. **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are applying to coordinate (Event, Meeting, or Conference). 
3. **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you are applying to coordinate.
4. **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event you are applying to coordinate.
5. **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event you are applying to coordinate.
6. **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of the event you are applying to coordinate.
7. **Applicant Name**: Enter your name.
8. **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
9. **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
10. **Resume**: Attach your resume to demonstrate your experience and skills as a non-profit event coordinator.
