# urban_loft_suite_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for booking and managing guest reservations in the Urban Loft Suite. It collects essential details required for guest registration, allowing staff to efficiently manage and provide the best possible service during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the check-in and check-out dates from the date picker fields.
2. Provide the guest's name and contact information (phone number and email).
3. Enter any special requests the guest may have.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date the guest will arrive.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, required): Enter the date the guest will depart.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the guest, such as their preferred room type or any special requirements.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any specific requests the guest has, like extra amenities or services.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Enter the guest's name for registration purposes.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Enter the guest's phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): Enter the guest's email address for secure and efficient communication.

## Tips
* Please ensure the dates entered are accurate, as this will affect room availability and staff planning.
* Use the guest's name, phone number, and email to ensure accurate registration and communication.
* Be specific when entering special requests to ensure they are met during the guest's stay.
* This form is designed for guest registration, so be concise and clear in your entries.
