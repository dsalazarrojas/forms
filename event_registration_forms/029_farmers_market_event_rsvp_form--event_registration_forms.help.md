# farmers_market_event_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register vendors and visitors for farmers market events. It collects essential information such as vendor and visitor details, RSVP type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the required fields marked as **Required**.
2. Choose the correct RSVP type (Vendor, Visitor, or Both) for your group.
3. Enter your Vendor ID or Visitor Name and Email for verification purposes.
4. Fill in your Comments if necessary.
5. Click on the Submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name**: A text field to enter your company or vendor name.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address. This is a required field for both vendors and visitors.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number, which is not required but can be useful for communication purposes.
* **Vendor Arrival Time**: A field to enter the time of your expected arrival at the market, not required but can be useful for event organizers.
* **Market Name**: A text field to enter the name of the market you are visiting. This is a required field.
* **Market Date**: A date field to enter the date of the event. This is a required field.
* **Market Time**: A time field to enter the time of the market. This is a required field.
* **Comments**: A text field to enter any comments or additional information about your visit.
* **RSVP Type**: A select menu to choose your RSVP type (Vendor, Visitor, or Both).
* **Vendors ID**: A text field to enter your Vendors ID, which is required to process your RSVP.
* **Notes**: A text field to enter any notes or additional information about your visit.
* **Visitor Name**: A text field to enter the name of the visitor. This is a required field for visitors.
* **Visitor Email**: A field to enter the email address of the visitor. This is a required field for visitors.
* **Visitor Phone**: A text field to enter the phone number of the visitor, which is not required but can be useful for communication purposes.
* **Visitor Comments**: A text field to enter any comments or additional information about the visitor.

Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely to ensure successful registration.
