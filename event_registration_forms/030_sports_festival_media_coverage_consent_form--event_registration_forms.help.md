# sports_festival_media_coverage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports festival media coverage consent form is designed to collect information from festival participants for media purposes. This form requires consent from participants to use their information for media coverage, such as interviews, photos, and videos. The form ensures that participants' information is collected and used properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name in the `participant_name` field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the `contact_email` field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
4. Select either 'yes' or 'no' in the `consent` field to indicate if you are willing to be interviewed or photographed/videoed.
5. If you have any additional information, you can provide it in the `additional_info` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name** (Text): Fill in your participant name.
* **contact_email** (Email): Provide your contact email address.
* **contact_phone** (Text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **consent** (Select One): Select 'yes' or 'no' to indicate if you are willing to be interviewed or photographed/videoed.
* **additional_info** (Note): If you have any additional information, you can provide it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are under 18 years old, please ask for parental consent before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the festival organizers.
* This form should only be filled out once per participant.
