# Retail Sampling Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Sampling Vendor Registration Form is a tool for retail sampling vendors to provide their contact information and event preferences. It helps event organizers collect and manage vendor data efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Vendor Information section with your company name, address, and other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Specify your event preferences, such as which events you are interested in participating in and which types of events you prefer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (`Retail Sampling Vendor Registration Form`, `text`, required: false): This section is for you to provide some basic information about your company, such as your company name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email, and phone number so that event organizers can easily get in touch with you.
* **Event Preferences** (`Event Preferences`, `text`, required: false): Describe the type of events you are interested in participating in, such as trade shows, product launches, or promotional events. Be as specific as possible to increase your chances of being selected for events that match your interests.
