# online_poll_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant's online poll participation consent, data protection information, and feedback. It is intended to ensure that participants understand their rights and the data protection measures in place during the online poll process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and select the options that apply to you.
2. For each field, provide the necessary information as prompted.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **consent to Collect data** (`consent_to_collect_data`, select_one, required: false)
* This field asks you to give your consent to collect your data for the purpose of participating in the online poll. Select one of the options to indicate your agreement.

### 2. **data Protection** (`data_protection`, type: note, required: false)
* This field provides information about the data protection measures in place during the online poll process. Please note that this is not a field that requires a response, but rather a statement of assurance.

### 3. **feedback** (`feedback`, type: text, required: false)
* This field is a text area where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the online poll process.

### 4. **rights Understanding** (`rights_understanding`, select_multiple, required: false)
* This field asks you to select all the options that you understand about your rights during the online poll process. Please select all that apply.

### 5. **transparency** (`transparency`, type: text, required: false)
* This field is a text area where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the online poll process.

### 6. **feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, type: text, required: false)
* This field is another text area where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the online poll process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understand all the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the online poll administrators.
* You can submit the form only once you have completed all the fields, even if some fields are not required.
