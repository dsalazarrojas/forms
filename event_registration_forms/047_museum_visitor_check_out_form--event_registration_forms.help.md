# Museum Visitor Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of a museum visitor's check out, ensuring that all necessary information is collected for future reference and potential follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, Date): Enter the date of the visitor's departure from the museum.
2. **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`, Time): Enter the time of the visitor's departure from the museum.
3. **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text): Provide any feedback or comments about the museum visit, including any issues or concerns.
4. **Museum Name** (`museum_name`, Text): Enter the name of the museum.
5. **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text): Enter the name of the contact person (e.g., a staff member).
6. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person (if applicable).
7. **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the contact person (if applicable).
8. **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or remarks about the visit.
9. **Museum Staff** (`museum_staff`, Select One): Select the staff member who will follow up with the visitor.
10. **Comments 2** (`comments2`, Text, Optional): Provide any further comments or remarks about the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Out Date**: This is the date of the visitor's departure from the museum. It is a required field, so make sure to enter the correct date.
* **Check Out Time**: This is the time of the visitor's departure from the museum. It is a required field, so make sure to enter the correct time.
* **Feedback**: This field is for any feedback or comments about the museum visit. You can provide any information you'd like to help us improve our services.
* **Museum Name**: This field is for entering the name of the museum.
* **Contact Name**: This field is for entering the name of the contact person (e.g., a staff member).
* **Phone Number** (Optional): If you have a phone number for the contact person, enter it here.
* **Email** (Optional): If you have an email address for the contact person, enter it here.
* **Comments** (Optional): This field is for any additional comments or remarks about the visit.
* **Museum Staff** (Select One): Select the staff member who will follow up with the visitor.
* **Comments 2** (Optional): This field is for any further comments or remarks about the visit.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field to ensure that we have a complete record of the visitor's check out.
* If you have any issues or concerns about the visit, include them in the feedback field.
* If you have additional comments, include them in the comments field.
* If you need to follow up with a staff member, select them from the staff list.
* Please make sure to review all the fields before submitting the form.

Please note that the fields marked as optional are only required if the corresponding information is available.
