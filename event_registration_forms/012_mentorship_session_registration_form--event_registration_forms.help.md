# Mentorship Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for mentorship sessions. Please provide accurate and complete details to help us prepare for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each field to focus on it, and enter the required information.
- Ensure you fill out all required fields (marked as required: true).
- Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant, e.g., John Doe.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Provide the participant's email address.
* **Mentor Name** (`mentor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the mentor.
* **Mentor Email** (`mentor_email`, email, required): Enter the mentor's email address.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Select the date of the session in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the session in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, required): Enter the topic of the session.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form. 

## Tips
- Double-check all required fields before submitting.
- Ensure accurate email addresses are entered for participants and mentors.
- If you encounter any issues or have questions, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
