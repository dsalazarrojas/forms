# AI Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees to the AI Event, a summit focused on artificial intelligence and its applications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the relevant fields.
2. Select your job title and attendee category.
3. Rate your experience with AI technologies and choose your interested breakout tracks.
4. Indicate your dietary requirements and preferences for networking.
5. Provide your emergency contact information.
6. Confirm that you agree to the event terms and conditions and privacy policy.
7. Proceed to the payment page for secure payment processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address where you can receive ticket updates and event information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Company / Organization** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company or organization name if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Attendee Category** (`attendee_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your category (Standard Attendee, Student / Academic, Speaker / VIP, Press / Media, or Exhibitor).
* **Experience with AI Technologies** (`ai_experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your expertise level (Expert, Intermediate, or Novice).
* **Breakout Session Interests** (`breakout_session_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the breakout tracks you are interested in.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your dietary restrictions (No restrictions, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Halal, Kosher, or Nut Allergy).
* **Opt-in to Attendee Networking App** (`networking_app_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent to share your name and company with other attendees.
* **T-Shirt Size (Complimentary)** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select your T-shirt size for the event.
* **How did you hear about this event?** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you learned about this event (Email Newsletter, LinkedIn, Colleague Referral, or Professional Association).
* **Emergency Contact (Name and Phone)** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I agree to the Event Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.
* Make sure to provide a valid email address for ticket updates and event information.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.
* The event terms and conditions and privacy policy are available on the event website.
