This form is designed to collect information from participants who want to register for the Resilience Building training program. The form is meant to gather essential details about each participant, including their workshop preferences, name, contact information, and dates of participation. This information will be used to facilitate a smooth and personalized experience for each participant.

## Resilience Building Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Select your preferred workshops from the "Participant Workshops" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to format it as per your email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. Please use the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
6. Enter your preferred date of participation in the "Date of Participation" field. Please use the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
7. Enter your preferred time of participation in the "Time of Participation" field. Please use the format "HH:MM AM/PM".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, Optional): Enter any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant for the training program.
* **Participant Workshops** (`participant_workshops`, select_one, Optional): Select the workshops that you are interested in attending. You can choose one or multiple workshops.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, Required): Enter your name as per your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your email address. Please format it as per your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Date of Participation** (`date_of_participation`, date, Optional): Enter your preferred date of participation in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Time of Participation** (`time_of_participation`, time, Optional): Enter your preferred time of participation in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
