# Women’s Tea Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect attendee information for a Women's Tea Event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your attendee information:
	* Fill in your **Attendee Name** in the `Attendee Name` field.
	* Enter your **Email** in the `Email` field.
	* Enter your **Phone** in the `Phone` field.
2. Select event details:
	* Choose the **Event Date** for the event you'll be attending.
	* Select the **Event Time** of the event.
	* Enter the **Event Location** where the event will take place.
	* Provide a brief description of the **Event Details**.
3. Provide event organizer information:
	* Enter the name of the **Event Organizer**.
4. Select your attendee type:
	* Choose your **Attendee Type** from the available options: **Female**, **Male**, or **Other**.
5. Add guest information (if applicable):
	* If you're bringing a guest, enter their **Guest Name**.
	* Enter your guest's **Email**.
	* Enter your guest's **Phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as it should be printed on the event materials.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event you'll be attending.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event you'll be attending.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event, such as its purpose or focus.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the event.
* **Attendee Type**: Choose your category from the available options: **Female**, **Male**, or **Other**.
* **Guest Name**: If you're bringing a guest, enter their name.
* **Guest Email**: If you're bringing a guest, enter their email address.
* **Guest Phone**: If you're bringing a guest, enter their phone number.
