# Parenting Skills Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register children for a parenting skills workshop, ensuring that each child receives the necessary support and accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Prophet's Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the parent or caregiver of the child participating in the workshop.
2. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address. This is necessary for communication and workshop updates.
3. **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a preferred date for the workshop, please enter it in the format `mm/dd/yy`.
4. **Preferred Workshop Time** (`preferred_workshop_time`, `select_one`, required): Select the time slot you would like to attend the workshop.
5. **Any Additional Information** (`any_additional_info`, `note`, required): Provide any relevant information about your child or you as a participant.
6. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
7. **Topics of Interest** (`topics_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the topics that interest you the most for your child.
8. **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any physical or learning requirements, please let us know.
9. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any other relevant information about your child.
10. **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your willingness to participate in the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prophet's Name**: The name of the parent or caregiver registering the child. This field is required so that we can address any questions or concerns you might have.
* **Contact Email**: A valid email address to ensure we can contact you for workshop details and updates.
* **Workshop Date**: Optional, but if you provide a specific date, we will do our best to accommodate it.
* **Preferred Workshop Time**: Select a time slot that fits your schedule. This is required so we can better manage the workshop flow.
* **Any Additional Information**: Please share any details that will help us tailor the workshop to your child's needs.
* **Contact Phone**: A valid phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Topics of Interest**: Choose topics relevant to your child's needs.
* **Participant Preferences**: If you have any special requirements, let us know so we can provide the necessary support.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other information that you think is relevant to your child's participation.
* **Participant Consent**: Confirm your willingness to participate in the workshop.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, don't hesitate to ask your workshop organizers.
* We will review all forms carefully to ensure that we can tailor the workshop to each child's needs.
