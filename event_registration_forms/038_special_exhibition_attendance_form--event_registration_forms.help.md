# Special Exhibition Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect visitor information and details about their visit to a special exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Visitor Info** in the first field.
2. Fill in your **Name** in the second field.
3. Enter the **Date** of your visit in the third field.
4. Select the **Time** of your visit from the available options in the fourth field.
5. Enter your **Email** address in the fifth field.
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the sixth field.
7. Add any additional **Notes** about your visit in the seventh field.
8. Finally, enter the **Visit Date** of your visit in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Info**: Enter any relevant visitor information or details you would like to provide.
* **Name**: Please fill in your full name as you would like to be recorded.
* **Date**: This field is for entering the date of your visit, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time**: Select the time you plan to visit the exhibition from the available options.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you with updates and information about the exhibition.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about your visit that might be helpful for our staff.
* **Visit Date**: This field is to confirm the date of your visit, please ensure it matches the date entered in the **Date** field above.

### Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all fields accurately and completely to ensure the information is correctly recorded.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
* If you are registering for multiple events or visits, you will need to fill in this form multiple times, one for each event.
