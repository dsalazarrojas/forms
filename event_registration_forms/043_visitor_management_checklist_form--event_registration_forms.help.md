# Visitor Management Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a checklist for visitor management, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered to effectively manage visitors' interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully before proceeding.
2. Provide the visitor's contact information, including their email and phone number if available.
3. Determine if the visitor's photo is to be taken.
4. Choose the tool to be assigned to the visitor.
5. Answer the security question to ensure the visitor's identity.
6. Enter the visit date and time.
7. Add any additional comments or concerns.
8. Review and confirm the visit information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): Please take a moment to read this form carefully before proceeding.
- **Visitor Contact Information** (`Visitor Contact Information`, `text`, required: true): Provide the visitor's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
  - If the visitor's contact information is not known, please enter 'N/A' or leave it blank.
  - If the visitor's contact information is known, please enter it accurately.
- **Photo** (`Photo`, `select_one`, required: true): Decide if a photo of the visitor is to be taken.
  - Choose 'Yes' if you want to take a photo of the visitor.
  - Choose 'No' if you do not want to take a photo of the visitor.
  - Choose 'Other' if you have a specific reason for not taking a photo.
- **Tool Assignment** (`Tool Assignment`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the tool to be assigned to the visitor.
  - Select the tool that best suits the visitor's needs.
- **Security Question** (`Security Question`, `select_one`, required: true): Answer the security question to ensure the visitor's identity.
  - Choose 'Yes' if the visitor's identity is verified.
  - Choose 'No' if the visitor's identity is not verified.
  - Choose 'Other' if you have a specific reason for not verifying their identity.
- **Visit Date and Time** (`Visit Date and Time`, `date`, required: true): Enter the visit date and time.
  - Enter the date and time of the visitor's visit.
  - Format: DD/MM/YY or MM/DD/YY.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or concerns.
  - Enter any relevant comments or concerns about the visitor's visit.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the visitor's email address.
  - If the visitor's email is not known, please enter 'N/A' or leave it blank.
  - If the visitor's email is known, please enter it accurately.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the visitor's phone number.
  - If the visitor's phone number is not known, please enter 'N/A' or leave it blank.
  - If the visitor's phone number is known, please enter it accurately.
- **Review and Confirmation** (`Review and Confirmation`, `checkbox`, required: true): Review and confirm the visit information.
  - Confirm the accuracy of the visit information before proceeding.
- **Additional Security Question** (`Additional Security Question`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer the additional security question.
  - Choose 'Yes' if the visitor's identity is verified for this question.
  - Choose 'No' if the visitor's identity is not verified for this question.
  - Choose 'Other' if you have a specific reason for not verifying their identity.
- **Photo Capture** (`Photo Capture`, `select_one`, required: false): Decide if the visitor's photo is to be captured.
  - Choose 'Yes' if the visitor's photo is to be captured.
  - Choose 'No' if the visitor's photo is not to be captured.
  - Choose 'Other' if you have a specific reason for not capturing their photo.
