The form "Farm Show Ethics Agreement" is designed to gather information from exhibitors, staff, vendors, and volunteers at a farm show event. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all participants understand and agree to abide by the show's rules and regulations, which are essential for a safe and successful event. This form helps to collect exhibitor information, agreement status, exhibit details, and staff, vendor, and volunteer categories. 

## Purpose
The Farm Show Ethics Agreement form is a crucial document that ensures all participants at the farm show understand and agree to follow the event's rules and regulations. It helps maintain a safe and organized environment for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Exhibitor Information**: Fill in your company or organization's information, including any relevant contact details. This will help us get in touch with you before, during, or after the event.
2. **Agreement**: Select whether you agree to abide by all show rules and regulations. This decision will determine your participation in the event.
3. **Exhibit Details**: Provide any additional information about your exhibit, such as its name, category, and size. This helps us understand your exhibit's specifics.
4. **Staff Vendors Volunteers**: Indicate whether your staff, vendors, or volunteers will be attending the event. Choose one or more of the options (Exhibitor, Vendor, or Volunteer) as relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Exhibitor Information (Exhibitor Information, Text, required: false)
* **Exhibitor Information**: Enter your company or organization's name and any other relevant contact details.

### Agreement (I Agree, Select One, required: false)
* **Agreement**: Select one of the two options:
	+ **I agree to abide by all show rules and regulations.**
	+ **I do not agree to abide by all show rules and regulations.**

### Exhibit Details (Exhibit Details, Text, required: false)
* **Exhibit Details**: Provide any additional information about your exhibit, such as its name, category, and size. This will help us understand your exhibit's specifics.

### Staff Vendors Volunteers (Staff Vendors Volunteers, Select Multiple, required: false)
* **Staff Vendors Volunteers**: Choose one or more of the options:
	+ **Exhibitor**
	+ **Vendor**
	+ **Volunteer**
