# Product Customization Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for a Product Customization Workshop. It helps the organizers collect relevant information about the participants, including their workshop preferences and dietary requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the workshop you want to attend by choosing from the available options in the "Select a Workshop" field.
2. Fill in your participant details in the "Participant Details" fields, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Specify any dietary requirements you have in the "Workshop Preferences" field.
4. If you have any other dietary preferences, you can provide more information in the "Other Dietary Preferences" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" fields, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select a Workshop** (`workshop_selection`, select_one, not required): Choose the workshop you want to attend.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, not required): Enter your name, which will be used to address you for the workshop.
* **Name** (`participant_details_name`, text, not required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`participant_details_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`participant_details_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`participant_details_note`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, text, not required): Specify any workshop preferences you have.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`workshop_preferences_diet`, select_multiple, not required): Check if you have any dietary requirements, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Other Dietary Preferences** (`workshop_preferences_other_dietary`, text, not required): If you have any other dietary preferences that are not listed, enter them here.
* **Dietary Preferences Note** (`workshop_preferences_dietary_note`, note, not required): Add any additional comments about your dietary preferences.
