# Membership Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect user registration information, including name, contact details, and membership preferences. It will help us gather data for event planning and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field if necessary. Make sure to enter a valid email address for password recovery and communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for communication and password recovery.
* **Password** (`password`, text, optional): Enter your password, which must be at least 8 characters long. Please note that this field is not for creating a new account, but for registration purposes only.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm`, text, optional): Re-enter your password to confirm it matches the one above.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of membership you are interested in (Basic, Premium, Free).
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple membership levels you are interested in (Silver, Gold, Bronze).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details you would like to share with us.

## Tips

* Please enter a valid email address to ensure easy communication and password recovery.
* For membership levels, choose all that apply.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at [your email address].
