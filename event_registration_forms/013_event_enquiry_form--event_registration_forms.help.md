# Event Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details about an event you wish to attend, organize or be a part of. Please fill in the form with accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event from the list of options provided in the "EventType" field.
2. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Fill in the "Event Time" field with the start time of the event.
4. Enter the event location in the "Event Location" field.
5. Provide the number of guests attending the event in the "Number of Guests" field.
6. Write any additional information about the event in the "Event Details" field.
7. Fill in your name in the "Your Name" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
10. Write your message for any additional information in the "Your Message" field.
11. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
12. Fill in your title in the "Title" field.
13. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
14. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **EventType** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of event you wish to participate in or organize. This field is required.
* **Event Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the event. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Event Time** (`time`, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`text`, required): Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Number of Guests** (`number`, required): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Event Details** (`text`, required): Enter any additional information about the event.
* **Your Name** (`text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Your Message** (`note`, required): Enter any additional message for the event organizer.
* **Event Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Title** (`text`, required): Enter your title.
* **Company** (`text`, required): Enter your company name.
* **Contact Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.

Note: All fields are required unless specified as "optional" in the original YAML.
