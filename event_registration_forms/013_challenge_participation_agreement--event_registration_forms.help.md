# Challenge Participation Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form, Challenge Participation Agreement, is designed for individuals who wish to participate in the challenge of your choice. It helps to ensure that participants understand and acknowledge the risks and rules associated with the challenge and provides necessary information for organizers. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, carefully read each field carefully and answer the questions accurately. Fill out the form in its entirety, as incomplete or inaccurate information may lead to disqualification from participating in the challenge. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (required): Enter your first, middle, and last names as you would like them to be listed in any official communications or recognition related to the challenge.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter a valid contact email address for communication and updates about the challenge.
* **Phone Number** (required): Provide a contact phone number for emergency situations or further communication.
* **Date of Birth** (required): Enter your participant's date of birth for age verification.
* **Are You 18 Years or Older** (required): Indicate if you are 18 years or older. This is a necessary condition for participation in most challenges.
* **Challenge Name Registering For** (required): Enter the name of the specific challenge for which you are registering.
* **Challenge Start Date** (required): Select the date when the challenge begins.
* **Challenge Duration** (required): Choose the length of time the challenge will last (e.g., 1 Day, 1 Week, 1 Month, etc.).
* **Do You Have Any Health Conditions** (required): Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation.
* **Please Describe Health Conditions** (optional): If you answered "Yes" to the above question, describe any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Physical Fitness Level** (optional): Select a level of physical fitness as it applies to your current state.
* **Have You Consulted With Your Physician** (optional): If you answered "Yes" to health concerns, indicate if you've consulted your doctor about this challenge.
* **I Assume All Risks Associated With Participation** (required): Acknowledge understanding of risks associated with participation in this challenge.
* **Consent to Medical Treatment in Emergency** (required): Grant permission for medical treatment in emergency situations.
* **Release From Liability** (required): Agree to release organizers from liability for any injuries or damages.
* **Agreement to Follow Rules and Conduct** (required): Confirm commitment to abide by challenge rules and conduct expectations.
* **Permission for Photos and Video** (optional): Allow organizers to use your likeness for promotional or other official purposes.
* **Consent to Collect and Use Personal Data** (required): Agree to the collection and use of your personal data for challenge purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (required): Enter the name of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **I Have Read and Understand This Agreement** (required): Confirm that you have read and understand all terms and conditions outlined in this agreement.
