# Health And Safety Officer Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to check in with the Health and Safety Officer when visiting the premises. It helps ensure that visitors can be properly registered and accounted for, and allows Health and Safety Officers to track and manage check-ins and check-outs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the correct option from the "Check In" dropdown menu and fill out the remaining required fields. You may only need to fill out one of the fields, depending on your status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In** (`checkin_form`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your status as a visitor. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Employee** (`employee`, text, optional): Enter your employee information, if applicable.
* **Visitor** (`visitor`, text, optional): Enter visitor information, if applicable.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number (e.g., phone number).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for your visit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of your visit.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your visit.

## Tips
This form helps ensure accurate tracking and record-keeping of visitors. Please fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
