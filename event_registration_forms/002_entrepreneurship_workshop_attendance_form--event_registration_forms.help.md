# Entrepreneurship Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entrepreneurship Workshop Attendance Form is designed to collect participant information and feedback for an entrepreneurship workshop. This form is used to gauge interest and assess the effectiveness of the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Start" button to begin.
- Fill out the required and optional fields as requested.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workshop Participant Info** (`workshop_participant_info`, text, not required): This field collects general information about the participant. Enter a brief statement describing yourself.
- **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, not required): Provide the name of the workshop you are interested in attending.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, not required): Enter your valid email address. 
- **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, not required): If you have a specific workshop date in mind, enter it here. Otherwise, leave blank.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Workshop Attendance** (`workshop_attendance`, select multiple, not required): Choose your expected attendance status: Yes, No.
- **Participant Note** (`participant_note`, note, not required): Share any additional comments or feedback about the workshop.
- **Workshop Notes** (`workshop_notes`, note, not required): Share any additional comments or feedback about the workshop.
- **Workshop Rating** (`workshop_rating`, number, not required): Rate the workshop on a scale of 1-5. 

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and honest information in all fields.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the workshop organizers.
- Submit the form to complete the process.
