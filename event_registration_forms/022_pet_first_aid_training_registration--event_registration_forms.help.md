# Pet First Aid Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Pet First Aid Training Registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Form section with your details.
2. Provide your pet's name and any other relevant information in the Pet Name field.
3. Enter your contact person and contact information in the Contact Person field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
6. Select the date and time of the training you wish to attend from the Training Date and Time fields.
7. Select the location of the training from the Training Location field.
8. Choose the session type (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) from the Training Session field.
9. If you have multiple pets, enter the number of pets you have in the Number of Pets field.
10. Select any applicable services you need during the training (Basic, Advanced, or Refresher) from the First Aid Services field.
11. If you have any notes or comments, enter them in the Notes field.
12. Select your payment method (Bank Transfer (EFT) or Cash) from the Payment Method field.
13. Confirm that you have read the course policies by selecting the correct option in the Confirmation field.
14. Finally, select your payment status (Paid or Pending) in the Payment Status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form (id: 1)**: This section is for general information such as your name and contact details.
* **pet_name (id: 2)**: Enter your pet's name and any relevant information about them.
* **contact_person (id: 3)**: Enter the name of the person you will be attending the training with.
* **email (id: 4)**: Enter your email address.
* **phone (id: 5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **training_date (id: 6)**: Select the date of the training you wish to attend.
* **training_time (id: 7)**: Select the time of the training you wish to attend.
* **training_location (id: 8)**: Select the location of the training.
* **training_session (id: 9)**: Choose the session type (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) of the training.
* **number_of_pets (id: 10)**: If you have multiple pets, enter the number of pets you will be attending with.
* **pet_type (id: 11)**: Select the type of pet you have (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **first_aid_services (id: 12)**: Select any additional first aid services you need during the training.
* **payment_terms (id: 13)**: This field is not currently used in the form, so you can ignore it.
* **notes (id: 14)**: Enter any additional comments or notes you have.
* **payment_method (id: 15)**: Select your payment method (Bank Transfer (EFT) or Cash).
* **confirmation (id: 16)**: Confirm that you have read the course policies by selecting the correct option.
* **payment_status (id: 17)**: Select your payment status (Paid or Pending).

Note: The payment_terms field is currently not being used in the form, so you can ignore it.
