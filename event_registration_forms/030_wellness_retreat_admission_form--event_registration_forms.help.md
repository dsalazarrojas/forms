# Wellness Retreat Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who want to attend a wellness retreat. The form is designed to be simple and straightforward, ensuring that you provide your details accurately so we can better serve you during the retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in all the required fields (marked as required in the form).
- You can fill in the optional fields with any additional information you want to provide.
- Make sure to enter your email and phone numbers correctly.
- For date and time, select dates and times that work for you.
- Select only one option for "Preferred Time Slot".
- In "Interests", select all the interests that resonate with you.
- Write any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as you would like to be known by us.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about the retreat.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number so we can contact you in case of any last-minute updates.
* **Start Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you plan to arrive at the retreat.
* **End Date** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time you plan to arrive at the retreat.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Select the time of the day you prefer for the retreat (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Interests** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the interests that you are interested in (Meditation, Yoga, Fitness, Wellness).
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information you want to share about your attendance or any specific needs you may have.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- We will be in touch with you soon after you submit this form to confirm your attendance.
