# Railway Lodge Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the reservation and management of railway lodge accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow these steps:
    1. Enter your check-in and check-out dates in the "Check In Dates" field.
    2. Select your room preferences from the "Room Preferences" dropdown menu.
    3. Enter your preferred check-out date in the "Check Out Date" field.
    4. Choose your preferred room type from the "Room Type" dropdown menu.
    5. Enter any guest details in the "Guest Details" text field.
    6. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
    7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
    8. If you have any specific requests or notes, please enter them in the "Note" field.
    9. Finally, enter any additional information you want to provide in the "Form Submission" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In Dates (date, required/optional: false)**: This field is used to enter the dates when you plan to check-in at the lodge. Please enter your check-in date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Room Preferences (select_multiple, required/optional: false)**: This field allows you to select the type of room you would like to reserve. You can choose between Single, Family, and Shared rooms.
* **Check Out Date (date, required/optional: false)**: Enter your planned check-out date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Room Type (select_one, required/optional: false)**: Select the type of room you would like to reserve. You can choose from Room, Suite, or Other.
* **Guest Details (text, required/optional: false)**: Enter any additional details about the guests in your group, such as their names, ages, etc.
* **Phone Number (text, required/optional: false)**: Enter your phone number so that the lodge staff can contact you with any important information or updates.
* **Email (email, required/optional: false)**: Enter your email address so that the lodge staff can contact you with any important information or updates.
* **Note (note, required/optional: false)**: If you have any specific requests or notes, please enter them here so that the lodge staff can take them into consideration.
* **Form Submission (text, required/optional: false)**: This field is for any additional information you want to provide about your stay.
