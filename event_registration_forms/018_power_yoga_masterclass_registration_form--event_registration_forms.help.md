# Power Yoga Masterclass Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to participate in the Power Yoga Masterclass. To ensure accurate and efficient processing, please fill out this form with your personal and event details.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out your personal details, including your name and contact information (email and phone number).
2. Choose your participant type (Student, Teacher, or Guest).
3. If applicable, enter the additional details such as event date, time, and participant name (if you are registering on behalf of another person).
4. Select your payment method and status.
