# Training Program Calendar - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for participants and trainers on a training program calendar. The form helps to schedule training sessions and gather essential details about participants. It is not intended for duplicate registrations or non-training program purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by entering the required information for each field.
2. Select "Schedule Training Sessions" to add a date for the training session.
3. Choose "Participant" or "Trainer" from the "Collect Participant Info" field.
4. Enter the participant's name in the "Name" field.
5. Enter the participant's email in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Add any notes about the participant in the "Notes" field.
8. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Schedule Training Sessions** (Date): Enter the date for the training session. The format is not specified, but it is expected to be in a standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Collect Participant Info** (Select Multiple): Choose whether the participant is a "Participant" or "Trainer".
- **Participant Information** (Text): Enter any information that is specific to the participant.
- **Name** (Text): Enter the name of the participant.
- **Email** (Email): Enter the participant's email address.
- **Phone** (Text): Enter the participant's phone number.
- **Notes** (Text): Add any additional notes about the participant.
- **Submit** (Note): Click this button to complete the form and submit your entry.
