# Fashion Show Booth Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for participants to register for the Fashion Show, a platform where businesses can showcase their products and connect with potential customers. By filling out this form, you'll be able to reserve a booth and provide us with essential information about your business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review your submission carefully before submitting.
3. Submit your form to reserve your booth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (<name>): Enter the name of the participant registering for the Fashion Show.
* **Contact email** (<contact_email>): Enter the email address where you can be contacted for any updates or questions.
* **Contact phone** (<contact_phone>): Enter the phone number where you can be reached for any urgent matters.
* **Business name** (<business_name>): Enter the name of the business you'll be representing at the Fashion Show (optional).
* **Description** (<description>): Briefly describe the type of products or services you'll be showcasing at the Fashion Show.
* **Business website** (<business_website>): Enter the website address of your business (optional).
* **Social media** (<social_media>): Enter your social media handle or website (optional).
* **Booth type** (<booth_type>): Select the type of booth you'd like to reserve (Fashion Show, Trade Show, or Sample Sale).
* **Booth size** (<booth_size>): Enter the size of the booth you'd like to reserve (required).
* **Booth layout** (<booth_layout>): Describe the layout of your booth (optional).
* **Business address** (<business_address>): Enter the physical address of your business (required).
* **City** (<city>): Enter the city where your business is located (required).
* **State** (<state>): Enter the state where your business is located (required).
* **Zip code** (<zip>): Enter the zip code of your business location (required).
* **Business representative name** (<business_name_representative>): Enter the name of the person representing your business at the Fashion Show (required).
* **Business email** (<business_email>): Enter the email address of the business representative (required).
* **Business phone** (<business_phone>): Enter the phone number of the business representative (required).
* **Social Security number** (<social_security_number>): Enter the Social Security number of the business representative (optional).
* **Business license number** (<business_license>): Enter your business license number (optional).
* **Business license state** (<business_license_state>): Enter the state where your business license was issued (optional).
* **Product description** (<product_description>): Briefly describe the products or services you'll be showcasing (optional).
* **Product price** (<product_price>): Enter the price of the product or service (optional).
* **Product price range** (<product_price_range>): Enter the price range of the products or services (optional).
* **Low price** (<product_price_range_low>): Enter the low end of the price range (optional).
* **High price** (<product_price_range_high>): Enter the high end of the price range (optional).

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at <contact_email>.
