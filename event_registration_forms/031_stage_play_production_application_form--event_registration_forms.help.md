# Stage Play Production Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about upcoming stage productions, including production details, contact information, and crew details. It should be filled out by production teams or crew members to provide necessary details about their involvement in the production.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information about your production.
2. Make sure to provide contact details for the production's team or crew members.
3. Double-check that all fields are completed and accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Details** (`Production Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the production, including the name of the play, the type of production (e.g. musical, drama, etc.), and any other relevant details.
* **Production Name** (`Production Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the production, as it should appear on promotional materials.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the production, in the standard date format (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY).
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the production, in the standard date format (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the venue or location where the production will take place.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the production.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact information for the production's team or crew members.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the company or organization producing the production.
* **Position** (`Position`, `text`, required: false): Enter the position or title of the crew member filling out the form.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the crew member or production contact.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the production or crew member.
* **Production Details 2** (`Production Details 2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional production details or notes.
* **Actor Details** (`Actor Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter information about the actor's involvement in the production, including their name and role.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the actor.
* **Head Shot** (`Head Shot`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the actor's head shot or photo.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, `text`, required: false): Attach or link to the actor's resume or bio.
* **Production Details 3** (`Production Details 3`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional production details or notes.
* **Actor Details 2** (`Actor Details 2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the actor's involvement in the production.
* **Crew Details** (`Crew Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter information about the crew member's role or position in the production.
* **Crew Details Name** (`Crew Details Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the crew member.
* **Crew Details Position** (`Crew Details Position`, `text`, required: false): Enter the position or title of the crew member.
* **Crew Details Email** (`Crew Details Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the crew member.
* **Crew Details Phone** (`Crew Details Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the crew member.
* **Production Details 4** (`Production Details 4`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional production details or notes.
