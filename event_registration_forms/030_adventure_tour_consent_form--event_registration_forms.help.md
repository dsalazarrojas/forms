# Adventure Tour Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Adventure Tour Consent Form is a document used to collect essential information from participants before they embark on an adventure tour. It ensures that participants are aware of the risks associated with the tour and have provided their consent to participate. This form is a crucial part of the tour preparation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask.
4. Once completed, submit the form to proceed with the tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, required): This is your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Your birthdate in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Tour Name** (`tour_name`, required): Enter the name of the adventure tour you're participating in.
* **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, required): Enter the date of the adventure tour.
* **Tour Location** (`tour_location`, required): Enter the location of the adventure tour.
* **Risks Acknowledged** (`tour_risks_acknowledged`, required): Confirm that you have read and understood the risks associated with this tour.
* **Physical Demands Acknowledged** (`physical_demands_acknowledged`, required): Confirm that you understand the physical demands of this tour.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, required): Check any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Consent to Participate** (`consent_to_participate`, required): Confirm that you consent to participate in this adventure tour.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, required): Enter the date of signing this consent form.
