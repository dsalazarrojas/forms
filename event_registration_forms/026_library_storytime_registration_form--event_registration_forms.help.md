# Library Storytime Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Storytime Registration Form is a basic registration form used to collect information from attendees of library events. It gathers personal details and event-specific information, allowing event organizers to manage participant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Story Time Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the storytime session.
- **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the storytime session.
- **Age** (number, optional): Enter your age.
- **Story** (text, optional): Enter your story or a brief description.
- **Parent Consent** (select_one, optional): If you are attending with a minor, select 'Yes' to grant consent.
- **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Event ID** (text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the event.
- **Library Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the library where the event is held.
- **Program Type** (select_one, optional): Indicate if this is a yes or no answer for attending a specific type of program.
- **Program Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the program.
- **Program Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the program.
- **Registration Type** (select_multiple, optional): Select any applicable registration types (yes or no).
