# reverse_career_fair_student_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students to be presented at the reverse career fair, allowing students to showcase their skills, interests, and background to potential employers and attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the student information section, providing your basic details such as name and contact information.
2.  Proceed to the contact info section and fill in any additional contact details you may have.
3.  List your skills and interests in the corresponding sections.
4.  Fill in your education background, career interests, and career objectives.
5.  Attach a resume to your profile, if you have one ready.
6.  Check the box to confirm your agreement to the fair terms and conditions.
7.  Finally, add any additional notes or interests that you'd like to share with the career fair team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Student Info** (`student_info`, text): Fill in your basic information such as name and any other details you'd like to share with us.
*  **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text): Add any additional contact details you'd like to share with the fair team.
*  **Skills** (`student_skills`, text): List your skills with a maximum of 3 bullet points, e.g., proficiency in programming languages, software skills, etc.
*  **Education Background** (`education_background`, text): Share your education background, including any relevant certifications or diplomas.
*  **Career Interests** (`career_interests`, text): List your career interests, e.g., areas of study, industries, or job roles you are interested in.
*  **Career Objectives** (`career_objectives`, text): Briefly explain your career objectives and goals.
*  **Resume** (`resume_uploader`, text): Attach a copy of your resume, if you have one ready.
*  **Availability** (`student_availability`, text): Share your availability for career opportunities or events.
*  **Student Agreement** (`student_agreement`, text): Check the box to confirm your agreement to the fair terms and conditions.
*  **Notes** (`student_notes`, text): Add any additional notes or information that you'd like to share with the career fair team.
*  **Additional Interests** (`student_interests`, text): List any additional interests or skills that you'd like to highlight.

## Tips

*   Make sure to be as concise as possible when filling in the form, as the goal is to present a brief overview of your profile.
*   Use bullet points to list multiple items in the "Skills" and "Career Interests" sections.
*   Proofread your form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
