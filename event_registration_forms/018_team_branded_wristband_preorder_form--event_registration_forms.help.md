# Team Branded Wristband Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Branded Wristband Preorder Form is designed to collect information from customers who wish to preorder wristbands for team events. The form is used to ensure accurate and timely delivery of wristbands and correct event attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Phone number in the corresponding fields. These are required fields and must be completed accurately.
2. Enter your Email address. This will be used to contact you regarding your preorder.
3. Select the color of the wristband you wish to preorder from the options provided.
4. Choose the event you are preordering for from the list of options.
5. Enter the quantity of wristbands you wish to preorder.
6. Choose your payment method from the options provided.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about your preorder.
8. Select the event type, event date, and event time for the event you are preordering for.
9. Enter the event location.
10. Enter the name of the team member who will be attending the event.
11. Enter the email address of the team member who will be attending the event.
12. Select the team leader from the options provided.
13. Enter a brief description of the team.
14. Enter any additional comments or notes about your preorder.
15. Finally, enter the name of the team logo, team website, event organizer, and event date-time for the event you are preordering for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here. This is a required field.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number here. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here. This will be used to contact you regarding your preorder.
* **Wristband Color**: Choose the color of the wristband you wish to preorder from the options provided.
* **Preorder For Event**: Choose the event you are preordering for from the options provided.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of wristbands you wish to preorder.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method from the options provided.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your preorder.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are preordering for.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you are preordering for.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event you are preordering for.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event you are preordering for.
* **Team Member Name**: Enter the name of the team member who will be attending the event.
* **Team Member Email**: Enter the email address of the team member who will be attending the event.
* **Team Leader**: Select the team leader from the options provided.
* **Team Description**: Enter a brief description of the team.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your preorder.
* **Team Logo**: Enter the name of the team logo.
* **Team Website**: Enter the website of the team.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Date-Time**: Enter the date and time of the event you are preordering for.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any issues with your preorder, please contact the event organizer or team leader for assistance.
