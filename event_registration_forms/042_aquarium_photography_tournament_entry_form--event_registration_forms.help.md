# Aquarium Photography Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for photographers to enter their photos into an aquarium photography tournament, allowing their work to be showcased and evaluated by a panel of experts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Photographer Name and Email Address, which are required fields.
2. Select your Photography Experience Level (Amateur, Semi-Professional, or Professional).
3. Choose how you will submit your photo (Digital Upload, Print, Slide, or Other).
4. Confirm that you grant the tournament organizers the use rights for your photo and confirm your entry fee status (Paid, Will Pay, or Requesting Waiver).
5. Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Mail).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, Text, Required): This field is where you enter your name as a photographer.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): This field is where you enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Optional): This field is where you can optionally enter your phone number for contact.
* **Photography Experience Level** (`experience_level`, Select One, Required): This field is where you select your level of photography experience (Amateur, Semi-Professional, or Professional).
* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, Note, Optional): This field is where you can optionally provide any additional information about your photo or yourself.
* **Photo Title** (`photo_title`, Text, Required): This field is where you enter the title of your photo.
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, Text, Optional): This field is where you can optionally provide a description of your photo.
* **Photo Category** (`category`, Select One, Required): This field is where you select the category of your photo (Fish and Marine Life, Water Clarity and Beauty, Aquarium Exhibits, Visitor Interaction, Conservation Focus, or Open Category).
* **How Will You Submit Your Photo** (`submission_method`, Select One, Required): This field is where you choose how you will submit your photo (Digital Upload, Print, Slide, or Other).
* **File Format or Details** (`file_format`, Text, Optional): This field is where you can optionally provide any additional file format details about your submission.
* **Grant Tournament Use Rights** (`usage_rights`, Select One, Required): This field is where you select whether you grant the tournament organizers the use rights for your photo (Yes or No).
* **Entry Fee Confirmation** (`entry_fee`, Select One, Required): This field is where you confirm your entry fee status (Paid, Will Pay, or Requesting Waiver).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preferences`, Select One, Optional): This field is where you select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Mail).

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the tournament organizers using your preferred contact method.
