# Bison Convention Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bison Convention Registration Form is used to collect registration information from attendees for the Bison Convention. The form is designed to gather personal and contact details, registration type, session preferences, dietary requirements, accommodation needs, accessibility requirements, emergency contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (page 1): 
	+ Complete this field with your registration information, including a brief description of your registration type.
* **First Name** (page 2): 
	+ Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (page 3): 
	+ Enter your legal last name.
* **Organization or Affiliation** (page 4): 
	+ Provide the name of your organization or bison association.
* **Job Title** (page 5): 
	+ Enter your current position or role.
* **Email Address** (page 6): 
	+ Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (page 7): 
	+ Enter your best contact number.
* **Registration Type** (page 8): 
	+ Select your registration category, which includes Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, Virtual Attendance, Exhibitor Pass, and Student Pass.
* **Days Attending** (page 9): 
	+ Choose which days you will attend, allowing you to select multiple days.
* **Session Preferences** (page 10): 
	+ Select any topics you are interested in, which includes Bison Conservation, Ranching Practices, Wildlife Management, Research Updates, Marketing and Sales, and Policy and Regulation.
* **Dietary Requirements** (page 11): 
	+ If applicable, indicate any special dietary needs, such as None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Kosher, Halal, or Other.
* **Accommodation Assistance** (page 12): 
	+ Indicate if you require accommodation assistance.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (page 13): 
	+ Describe any accessibility requirements you may need.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (page 14): 
	+ Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (page 15): 
	+ Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **T-Shirt Size** (page 16): 
	+ Select your preferred t-shirt size.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event** (page 17): 
	+ Select where you heard about the event, which includes options for Email Newsletter, Social Media, Colleague Referral, Website, Previous Attendee, and Other.
* **Additional Comments** (page 18): 
	+ If you have any additional comments or information to share, provide them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your registration type and days attending to ensure accuracy.
* Be specific when indicating any dietary requirements or accessibility needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
