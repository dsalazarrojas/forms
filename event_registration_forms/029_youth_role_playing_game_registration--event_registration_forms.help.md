This form is designed to collect information from users who want to register for a Youth Role Playing Game (RPG). The form gathers details about the user, their parent or guardian, and emergency contact information. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all necessary information is collected correctly and efficiently.

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

## Purpose
The form is a crucial part of the registration process for the Youth Role Playing Game. It helps us get in touch with you and ensure that you have all the necessary information to have a safe and enjoyable experience at the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the required information for each field. If you have any difficulties or need help, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Name** (id: 1, text, optional): Please fill in your name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or passport.
* **Parent Information** (id: 2, text, optional): Please provide your parent or guardian's name if you are under 18 years old.
* **Player Information** (id: 3, text, optional): Fill in your player's name, age, and role type (e.g., Dungeon Master, Player, Game Master).
* **Session Information** (id: 4, text, optional): Enter your session name and date you want to attend.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (id: 5, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Session** (id: 6, text, optional): Fill in your session name as per your session information.
* **Role Information** (id: 7, text, optional): Fill in your role name and type (e.g., Dungeon Master, Player, Game Master).
* **Emergency Contact** (id: 8, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's name, phone number, and email.
* **Role Name** (id: 9, text, optional): Enter your role name as it appears on your character sheet.
* **Session Name** (id: 10, text, optional): Enter the name of your session as per your session information.
* **Session Date** (id: 11, date, optional): Enter the date of your session.
* **Session Start** (id: 12, time, optional): Enter the start time of your session.
* **Player Name** (id: 13, text, optional): Enter your player's name as it appears on your character sheet.
* **Player Age** (id: 14, number, optional): Enter your player's age.
* **Player Gender** (id: 15, select one, optional): Choose your player's gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Parent Name** (id: 16, text, optional): Enter your parent's name (if you are under 18 years old).
* **Parent Email** (id: 17, email, optional): Enter your parent's email (if you are under 18 years old).
* **Parent Phone** (id: 18, text, optional): Enter your parent's phone number (if you are under 18 years old).
* **Role Name** (id: 19, text, optional): Enter your role name as it appears on your character sheet.
* **Role Type** (id: 20, select one, optional): Choose your role type (Dungeon Master, Player, Game Master).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 21, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (id: 22, select one, optional): Choose your relationship with the emergency contact (Mother, Father, Other, Mother and Father, Both).
* **Emergency Phone** (id: 23, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (id: 24, email, optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Player Notes** (id: 25, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your player.
* **Parent Notes** (id: 26, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Notes** (id: 27, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but they are still essential for your successful registration. Please take the time to fill in all the required fields.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields correctly.
* If you are under 18 years old, please fill in your parent's information carefully.
* In case of any difficulties, do not hesitate to contact us for help.
