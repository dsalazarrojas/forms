# Gallery Visit Communication Form - Help Guide
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Feedback** (`visitor_feedback`, `note`, optional): This field is where you can share any comments or feedback about your visit. Feel free to let us know if you liked or disliked anything about the gallery or your experience.
* **Appointment Request** (`appointment_request`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you would like to schedule an appointment with us, please select one or more options that match your needs. This will help us to contact you and arrange a suitable time for your visit.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `number`, optional): This field is where you can provide us with your contact information, such as your phone number, so we can stay in touch and follow up on your visit.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your visit and any follow-up information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you about your visit.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, `date`, optional): Let us know when you plan to visit the gallery, so we can plan accordingly.
* **Art Piece** (`art_piece`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific art piece in mind that you would like to see, please let us know here.
* **Art Piece Description** (`art_piece_description`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific details about the art piece you would like to see, please provide a brief description.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Is there anything else you would like to share with us about your visit or any specific requests?
* **Visitor Type** (`visitor_type`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the category that best describes your visit:
	+ Gallery Staff
	+ Gallery Visitor
	+ Gallery Volunteer
* **Event Attend** (`event_attend`, `select_one`, optional): If you plan to attend an event at the gallery, please select the event that interests you:
	+ Event 1
	+ Event 2
	+ Event 3
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): How often do you plan to visit the gallery?
	+ Once
	+ Multiple
	+ Regular

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your visit experience with us!
