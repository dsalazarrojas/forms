# Custom Event Logistics Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Event Logistics Request Form is a comprehensive form used to collect essential information for events. This form aims to gather details about the event planner, event specifics, vendor information, participants, and other crucial event-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information for the event you are planning.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out, indicated by a red asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Planner Contact Information

*   **Event Planner Contact Information** (`event_planner_contact_information`): This section gathers information about the person or team responsible for the event. Please provide the name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.

### Event Details

*   **Event Details** (`event_details`): This field is for providing specific details about the event, such as the event name, date, time, location, and any other relevant information.

### Vendor Details

*   **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`): Here, you should list the vendors or service providers involved in the event, including their names, contact details, and any specific services they will be providing.

### Event Participants

*   **Event Participants** (`event_participants`): This section is intended for listing the names and contact details of all individuals participating in the event.

### Event Questions

*   **Event Questions** (`event_questions`): This field is for asking any questions or clarifying any specific details about the event that need to be addressed.

### Vendor Participation

*   **Vendor Participation** (`vendor_participation`): In this section, provide any information related to the participation of vendors in the event, such as their role or services provided.

### Participant Questions

*   **Participant Questions** (`participant_questions`): Similar to the event questions, this field is for asking any questions or seeking clarification about the participants of the event.

### Event Equipment

*   **Event Equipment** (`event_equipment`): Here, list any specific equipment needed for the event, including their quantities and any other related details.

### Event Transportation

*   **Event Transportation** (`event_transportation`): In this section, provide details about the transportation arrangements for the event, such as vehicle information or travel requirements.

### Event Food and Beverage

*   **Event Food and Beverage** (`event_food_and_beverage`): This field is for specifying the food and beverage arrangements for the event, including any dietary restrictions or special requests.

### Event Safety and Security

*   **Event Safety and Security** (`event_safety_and_security`): In this section, address any safety and security concerns or arrangements for the event, including emergency contact details or procedures.

### Payment Info

*   **Payment Info** (`payment_info`): Here, provide payment-related information, such as payment methods, amounts, or any other relevant financial details.

## Tips

*   Ensure all fields are accurately completed to avoid delays in processing the event request.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on a specific field, reach out to the contact listed on this form or email form@gic.mx.
