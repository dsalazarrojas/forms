# Ticket Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and verify ticket information for participants in events or events themselves. It helps event organizers or staff maintain accurate records and ensure participant authenticity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the ticket verification form with the required information.
2. Enter your participant details in the `participant_information` field.
3. Provide ticket specifics in the `ticket_details` field.
4. Verify participant details in the `verify_participant` field.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the `notes` field.
6. Enter the participant's email address in the `participant_email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ticket Verification Form (ticket_verification_form)**: This is the main text input field for collecting ticket information.
* **Participant Information (participant_information)**: Enter your name, contact details, or any other relevant participant information.
* **Ticket Details (ticket_details)**: Provide a brief description of the ticket, including any relevant numbers or codes.
* **Verify Participant (verify_participant)**: Verify that the participant's information is correct and authentic.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the participant or ticket.
* **Participant Email (participant_email)**: Enter the participant's email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields correctly to ensure accurate participant verification.
* Be as specific as possible when filling in the `ticket_details` field to ensure accurate records.
* Keep the `notes` field for additional comments or information that may not be included in the other fields.
* Double-check the `participant_email` field for accuracy and completeness.
