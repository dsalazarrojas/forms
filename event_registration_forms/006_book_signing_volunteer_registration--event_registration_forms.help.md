# Book Signing Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for potential volunteers to sign up for book signing events. It collects contact information, availability, and other relevant details to ensure a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
3. Answer **Do you have previous volunteer experience** with "True", "False", or "Some experience".
4. Select your **Availability for the Event** from the options provided (Morning Session, Afternoon Session, Evening Session, or Full Day).
5. Choose your **Preferred Volunteer Role** from the available options (Registration Desk, Book Sales, Author Support, Setup and Cleanup, Crowd Management, Photography, or Flexible - Any Role).
6. If you speak **Languages You Speak**, select all that apply (English, Spanish, French, German, Mandarin, or Other).
7. If you have any **Physical Limitations**, provide a brief description.
8. Indicate if you need **Transportation** to the event (True, False, or Maybe).
9. Provide the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone Number** in case of an emergency.
10. Finally, accept the **Volunteer Waiver** by typing "I agree to the volunteer agreement" and read the terms carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field for your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field for your last name.
* **Email Address**: A text field for your email address; we will send event details to this email.
* **Phone Number**: A text field for your phone number; contact number for the event.
* **Do you have previous volunteer experience**: Select one of the options (True, False, or Some experience) to indicate your volunteer experience.
* **Availability for the Event**: Select multiple options (Morning Session, Afternoon Session, Evening Session, or Full Day) to indicate your availability.
* **Preferred Volunteer Role**: Select one of the options (Registration Desk, Book Sales, Author Support, Setup and Cleanup, Crowd Management, Photography, or Flexible - Any Role) to choose your volunteer role.
* **Languages You Speak**: Select multiple options (English, Spanish, French, German, Mandarin, or Other) to indicate your language proficiency.
* **Any Physical Limitations**: A text field for any physical limitations you have that we should be aware of.
* **Transportation**: Select one of the options (True, False, or Maybe) to indicate if you need transportation to the event.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: A text field for the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: A text field for the emergency contact's phone number.
* **I agree to the volunteer waiver**: A text field where you must type "I agree to the volunteer agreement" to accept the terms.

## Tips
* Be honest when answering "Do you have previous volunteer experience" to ensure we can assign you to the right role.
* Select all languages you speak in "Languages You Speak" to help us understand your communication abilities.
* Provide accurate information in "Physical Limitations" to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.
* Read and carefully review the **Volunteer Waiver** before accepting it.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.

Note: This help guide is designed to be used in conjunction with the actual form, which will ask for specific answers and provide real-time guidance to users.
