# Strategic Innovation Accelerator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Innovation Accelerator Registration Form is a form designed to collect information from applicants for the Strategic Innovation Accelerator program. It is intended to gather relevant details from applicants to support the evaluation and review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your applicant details on page 1, providing as much information as possible.
2. Move on to page 2 and provide details about the program you're applying for.
3. If required, attach relevant supporting documents on page 3.
4. Finally, submit your form on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Applicant Details
* **Applicant Details** (`Applicant Details`, `text`, required): Enter your personal or team name, contact information, and any other relevant details to help us get in touch with you.
* **Program Details** (`Program Details`, `text`, required): Describe the program(s) you're applying for. Please include any specific dates, locations, or other relevant details.
* **Supporting Documents** (`Supporting Documents`, `text`, required): If you have any supporting documents such as resumes, references, or project proposals, attach them here.

### Page 2: Program Details
* **Program Details** (`Program Details`, `text`, required): Describe your project or program idea, focusing on its innovative aspects and why it's a good fit for the Strategic Innovation Accelerator.

### Page 3: Supporting Documents (if required)
* **Supporting Documents** (`Supporting Documents`, `text`, optional): Attach relevant documents that support your application, such as project proposals, resumes, or references.

### Page 4: Submit
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required): Once you've completed the form, click this button to submit your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Attach relevant supporting documents if asked to, as this can help your application stand out.
* Contact us if you have any questions or need help with your application.
