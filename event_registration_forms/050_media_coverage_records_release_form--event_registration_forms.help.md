# Media Coverage Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Coverage Records Release Form is a tool used to collect and track the usage of media resources from individuals or organizations that have utilized them. By filling out this form, you will be providing important information about how you have used or plan to use our media materials. This will help us to verify and ensure that your use of our media content is accurate and in compliance with our guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**. These fields are required.
2. If you are representing an organization, provide the **Organization** details.
3. Choose the type of **Media Usage** you have done or plan to do (e.g., Social Media, Print Media, Video, or Audio).
4. Describe the **Media Description** of what you have used or plan to use.
5. If necessary, provide more details about your **Usage Details**.
6. Specify the **Consent Type** under which you used or plan to use our media content (e.g., Release or Use).
7. Enter the date and time when you used or plan to use our media content (**Date** and **Time**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your personal name. Please fill in your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your personal last name. Please fill in your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, please provide its name and contact details.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your official email address. This will be used for communication and follow-ups.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your contact phone number. This will be used for further information or clarification.
* **Media Usage** (`media_usage`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of media you used or plan to use (e.g., Social Media, Print Media, Video, or Audio).
* **Media Description** (`media_description`, text, optional): A brief description of what you used or plan to use (e.g., article title, project title, etc.).
* **Usage Details** (`usage_details`, text, optional): Additional information about your usage.
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of consent under which you used or plan to use our media content (e.g., Release or Use).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date when you used or plan to use our media content.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time when you used or plan to use our media content.
