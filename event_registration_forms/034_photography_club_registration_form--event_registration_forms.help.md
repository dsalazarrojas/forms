# Photography Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Club Registration Form is used to register members and non-members for events or club meetings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the registration form with your details.
2.  Ensure you provide accurate email and password entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Form**: This is the main registration area where you can provide general information about your registration.
*   **Member Name**: Please enter your full name or the name of the attendee if you're registering someone else.
*   **Email**: This is your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone**: Provide your phone number for easier communication.
*   **Password**: Enter a secure password for your account.
*   **Confirm Password**: Repeat the password you entered for verification.
*   **Member Type**: Choose whether you are a club member or not.
*   **Event Date**: Select the date of the event or meeting you are registering for.
*   **Event Time**: Choose the time of the event or meeting you are registering for.
*   **Event Details**: Provide any additional event-specific information in this text field.

## Tips

- Make sure to review and submit the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors.
- Ensure that you are registering for the correct event or meeting date and time.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the club administrators for assistance.
