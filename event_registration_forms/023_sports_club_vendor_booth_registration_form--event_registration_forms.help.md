# Sports Club Vendor Booth Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from sports clubs that want to participate in a vendor booth at an event. It helps the event organizers to understand the club's requirements and ensure that they are properly registered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required details about your sports club.
2. Select the requirements for your booth, such as electrical power and internet access.
3. Provide your payment information, including the payment method and date.
4. Ensure that you accurately fill out all the fields to ensure proper registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Details** (id: 1, type: text, required: false)
	+ This field is for providing the sports club's name and other details.
* **Booth Needs** (id: 2, type: select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the requirements for your booth, such as electrical power and internet access.
* **Payment Information** (id: 3, type: text, required: false)
	+ This field is for providing payment details, such as the payment method and amount.
* **Name** (id: 4, type: text, required: false)
	+ Please enter the name of the person who will be representing the sports club.
* **Email** (id: 5, type: email, required: false)
	+ Enter the email address of the person who will be representing the sports club.
* **Phone** (id: 6, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter the phone number of the person who will be representing the sports club.
* **Booth Setup** (id: 7, type: text, required: false)
	+ Describe the setup of your booth, including any specific requirements.
* **Electrical Power** (id: 8, type: text, required: false)
	+ If your booth requires electrical power, please describe the specific requirements.
* **Internet Access** (id: 9, type: text, required: false)
	+ If your booth requires internet access, please describe the specific requirements.
* **Water** (id: 10, type: text, required: false)
	+ If your booth requires water, please describe the specific requirements.
* **Payment Method** (id: 11, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the payment method for your registration, such as bank transfer or cash.
* **Payment Date** (id: 12, type: date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date of the payment for your registration.
* **Payment Amount** (id: 13, type: number, required: false)
	+ Enter the amount of the payment for your registration.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you accurately fill out all the fields to ensure proper registration.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
- The form is only for sports clubs, and other types of vendors are not eligible.
