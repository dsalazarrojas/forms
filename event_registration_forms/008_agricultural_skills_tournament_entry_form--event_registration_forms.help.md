# Agricultural Skills Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information for the annual Agricultural Skills Tournament. Fill out this form to register as an individual or as a team and to provide necessary details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. For team members, list all names of team members in the "Team Members" field.
3. Select the competition category from the provided options.
4. Confirm your participation type (individual or team).
5. Ensure you or your team agree to the liability waiver and media release.
6. Enter your registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Age (`age`, number, required): Enter your current age.
* **Gender (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender.
* **Email Address (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number (`phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address (`address`, text, required): Enter your full mailing address.
* **City (`city`, text, required): Enter your city of residence.
* **State (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal or ZIP code.
* **School or Organization (`school_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your school, FFA chapter, or organization.
* **Advisor or Coach Name (`advisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your supervising adult.
* **Advisor Phone (`advisor_phone`, text, required): Enter your advisor's contact phone number.
* **Advisor Email (`advisor_email`, email, required): Enter your advisor's contact email.
* **Competition Category (`competition_category`, select_one, required): Select the competition category.
* **Participation Type (`team_or_individual`, select_one, required): Select whether you are competing as an individual or a team.
* **Team Name (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter your team name if you are competing as a team.
* **Team Members (`team_members`, text, optional): List all team member names if you are competing as a team.
* **Previous Competition Experience (`previous_experience`, select_one, required): Select your previous competition experience.
* **Special Accommodations Needed (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility or dietary requirements.
* **T-Shirt Size (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, required): Select your t-shirt size.
* **Emergency Contact Name (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the liability waiver.
* **Media Release (`media_release`, select_one, required): Confirm your permission to use photos for publicity.
* **Registration Date (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your registration.
* **Payment Status (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select your current payment status.
