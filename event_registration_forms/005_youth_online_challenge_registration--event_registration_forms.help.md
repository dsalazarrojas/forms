# Youth Online Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant and parent/guardian information, consent, and other relevant details for the online youth challenge. The form is meant for parents/guardians to provide their consent for their child's participation and for participants to indicate their submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide parent/guardian details, if applicable.
3. Select the correct consent option, either with or without parent/guardian supervision.
4. Review and agree to the submission terms and conditions.
5. Provide additional comments, if necessary.
6. Submit the form to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (Text): Enter your name and any other relevant details about the participant.
*   This field is required for participant registration.

### Parent/Guardian Details
* **Parent/Guardian Details** (Text): Enter your name and any other relevant details about the parent/guardian.
*   This field is optional and only required if you are a parent/guardian.

### Consent
* **Consent** (Select Multiple):
	+ I agree that the participant will participate in the online youth challenge with their parent/guardian's supervision.
	+ I agree that the participant will participate in the online youth challenge without their parent/guardian's supervision.
	+ I decline to sign this consent form.
*   Select the correct consent option that applies to your situation.

### Submission Agreement
* **Submission Agreement** (Note): This field is not applicable for participant or parent/guardian input.
*   This field is for internal use only and not intended for participant input.

### Submission
* **Submission** (Select Multiple):
	+ I have reviewed the submission terms and conditions.
	+ I have not reviewed the submission terms and conditions.
	+ I decline to submit this form.
*   Select the correct submission option that applies to your situation.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (Select Multiple):
	+ Yes
	+ No
*   Provide additional comments about your registration or submission status if necessary.

### Submission Status
* **Submission Status** (Select One):
	+ Draft
	+ Submitted
*   Select the correct submission status that applies to your situation.

# Tips
*   Make sure to review the submission terms and conditions before submitting the form.
*   Provide accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your registration.
*   If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the challenge organizers for clarification.
