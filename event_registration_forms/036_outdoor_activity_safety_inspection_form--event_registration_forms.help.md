# Outdoor Activity Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event planners and organizers assess and mitigate risks associated with outdoor activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the outdoor activity name.
2.  Select the risk level of the activity (Low, Moderate, or High).
3.  Identify the types of hazards present during the activity (Wildlife, Environmental, or Human-Induced).
4.  Describe the safety measures in place to mitigate risks.
5.  Outline the emergency contact information in case of an accident or emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Outdoor Activity Name** (`activity_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in the name of the outdoor activity.
*   **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the risk level of the activity:
    *   Low: The activity is considered low-risk.
    *   Moderate: The activity poses some level of risk.
    *   High: The activity is considered high-risk.
*   **Hazard Type** (`hazard_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify the types of hazards present during the activity. Select all applicable options:
    *   Wildlife Hazard
    *   Environmental Hazard
    *   Human-Induced Hazard
*   **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, `text`, required): Describe the safety measures in place to mitigate risks.
*   **Safety Plan** (`safety_plan`, `text`, required): Outline the emergency contact information in case of an accident or emergency:
    *   Name
    *   Phone number
    *   Email address

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that you have a thorough understanding of the activity's risk profile before completing this form.
*   Take necessary steps to mitigate potential hazards before conducting the activity.
*   This form is for information gathering purposes only, not for certification or approval.
*   Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that it serves its intended purpose.
*   Review and revise your answers carefully before submitting the form.
