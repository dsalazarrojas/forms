# Annual Awards Afterparty RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from attendees for the annual awards afterparty event. It's essential to fill it out accurately and completely to ensure that we can plan and accommodate for your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and completely.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Select the correct options for each field.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it should be spelled correctly.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: This is your best contact number for any emergency or last-minute updates.
* **Company or Organization**: If you're representing a company or organization, enter the name here.
* **Award Category**: Choose the award category you're nominated for or interested in (or select "General Attendee" if you're not).
* **Will You Attend the Afterparty?**: Confirm your attendance by selecting the correct option.
* **Bringing a Guest**: Decide if you'll bring a guest and fill out their details if needed.
* **Guest Name**: Enter your guest's full name, if applicable.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select all the dietary restrictions that apply to you.
* **Dietary Details**: If you've selected any dietary restrictions, provide more details about them here.
* **Beverage Preference**: Indicate what you'd like to drink at the afterparty.
* **Transportation Method**: Choose how you'll get to the afterparty venue.
* **Parking Needed**: If you need parking, let us know.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: If you have any special accessibility needs, describe them here.
* **Expected Arrival Time**: Let us know when you plan to arrive at the afterparty.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Choose your T-shirt size for any afterparty merchandise.
* **Networking Interest**: Indicate your interest in participating in networking activities.
* **Photo Consent**: Consent to being photographed for marketing purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter an emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requirements or needs, list them here.
* **Will to Provide Feedback**: Consent to filling out a post-event survey.
