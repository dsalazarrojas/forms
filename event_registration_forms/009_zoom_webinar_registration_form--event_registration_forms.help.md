# Zoom Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a Zoom webinar. Please fill it out to let us know you're attending and to provide us with some basic information about yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. If you work for a company, enter the name of your company in the "company" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "job title" field.
6. Select your role in the webinar from the options provided in the "role" field.
7. Indicate how many attendees will be joining from your company in the "attendees" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (text, required: false): Please enter your first name, or your nickname if that's what you prefer to go by.
* **last name** (text, required: false): Please enter your last name.
* **email** (email, required: false): Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **company** (text, required: false): If you work for a company, please enter its name.
* **job title** (text, required: false): Please enter your job title or position within your company.
* **role** (select_multiple, required: false): Please select the role you will be playing in the webinar (e.g. Zoom Host, Zoom Guest, Other).
* **attendees** (select_multiple, required: false): Indicate how many attendees from your company will be joining the webinar. Choose from Zoom Hosts, Zoom Guests, or Other.
