# senior_all_night_party_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the Senior All-Night Party event. Please provide the required information to help us gauge interest and organize the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
* Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
* If a field is not required, you can skip it or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type**: This field asks if you are interested in attending the Senior All-Night Party. Please select 'Yes' if you are interested, and 'No' if you are not.
* **first_name**: This field is for your first name.
* **last_name**: This field is for your last name.
* **email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **guest_count**: This field asks how many guests you plan to bring. Please enter the number of guests you are bringing.
* **rsvp**: This field is used to confirm your attendance. Please select 'Yes' if you are attending, and 'No' if you are not.
* **notes**: This field is for any additional comments or notes you would like to share with us. You can use this field to provide more information about your attendance or any special requests.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure we have the correct information about your attendance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact info].
