# Affiliate Marketing Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect attendee information for affiliate marketing events. It helps us understand your preferences, needs, and demographics to provide you with the most relevant content and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the form sections in the following order:
	* Attendee Information
	* Event Preferences
	* Payment

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Attendee Information

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Company / Organization** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Business Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.

### Event Preferences

* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of ticket you prefer. Options include:
	+ General Admission
	+ VIP Pass (All Access)
	+ Virtual Attendee
	+ Speaker / Presenter
* **Days Attending** (`days_attending`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the days you plan to attend.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_requirements`, `select_one`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have, such as vegetarian or vegan.
* **Topics of Interest** (`topics_interest`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the topics you are interested in learning about. Options include:
	+ SEO Strategies
	+ Influencer Marketing
	+ Conversion Rate Optimization
	+ Email Marketing Automation
	+ Compliance & Regulation

### Payment

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your payment method. Options include:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Invoice (Corporate Only)
* **How did you hear about this event?** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about this event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields.
* Use the correct formatting for your email address.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
