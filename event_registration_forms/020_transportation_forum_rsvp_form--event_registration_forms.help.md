# Transportation Forum RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Forum RSVP Form is used to collect information about your transportation plans for events hosted or sponsored by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Select your preferred mode of transportation from the options provided.
3. Enter any additional comments or details about your transportation plans.
4. Click the "Form Submit" button to submit your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter a valid email address to receive communication from the event organizers.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +1 123-456-7890).
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, `text`, **required**): Enter the title of the event you are planning to attend.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date of the event using the date picker or enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the mode of transportation you will be using to get to the event location (Bus, Train, Car, Plane, or Other).
* **Transportation Details** (`transportation_details`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional information about your transportation plans, such as pickup and drop-off locations.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, **required**): Select the time of the event using the time picker or enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, `number`, **required**): Enter the number of people attending the event with you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or special requests you may have.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, `text`, **required**): Click this button to submit your RSVP and ensure your response is recorded.
