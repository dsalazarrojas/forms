# Submit Your Question For Q+A - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to submit questions for Q&A sessions. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide detailed information about your question, including your name, contact details, and the type of question you would like to ask. The form is used by event organizers to gather questions from attendees before the event, and it helps to ensure that everyone has a chance to ask their questions during the Q&A sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to enter your questioner's name, email, and phone number (if available).
3. Choose the type of question you would like to ask (General, Technical, or Personal).
4. Provide a brief description of your question.
5. If you want to add a priority to your question, you can select from the options (Low, Medium, or High).
6. If you have any attachments related to your question, you can add them to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your personal message or comment about your question. This is an optional field, but it can be helpful for event organizers to understand the context of your question.
* **What is your question** (`question_text`, `text`, required: true): Enter your question in this field. This is a required field and should be a clear and concise question.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event you are attending or related to your question. This field is optional, but it can help event organizers understand the context of your question.
* **Your Name** (`questioner_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your name so that event organizers can contact you to answer your question.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address so that event organizers can contact you to answer your question.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number. This field is optional.
* **What type of question would you like to ask?** (`question_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select from the options (General, Technical, or Personal) to indicate the type of question you would like to ask.
* **Question Description** (`question_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your question to help event organizers understand it.
* **Question Priority** (`question_priority`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you want to add a priority to your question, you can select from the options (Low, Medium, or High). This field is optional.
* **Question Attachment** (`question_attachment`, `note`, required: false): If you have any attachments related to your question, you can add them to this field.
