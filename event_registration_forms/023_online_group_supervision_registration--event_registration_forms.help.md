# Online Group Supervision Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering your participation in an online group supervision session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Participant Info** field with your name and any additional information you want to provide to the session's facilitator or coordinator.
2. Fill out the **Supervisor Info** field with your supervisor's name and any other relevant information.
3. Fill out the **Session Details** field with any additional session details you want to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a participant.
* **Supervisor Info**: Enter your supervisor's name and any other relevant information about your supervisor.
* **Session Details**: Enter any additional information about the session you want to attend, such as the date, time, or location.
 
## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
