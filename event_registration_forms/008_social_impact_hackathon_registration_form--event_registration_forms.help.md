# Social Impact Hackathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from attendees for the Social Impact Hackathon. It helps us understand who is attending, their preferences, and event details. Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your attendee type from the options provided (Developer, Student, Organizer).
5. Enter the name of the event you are attending in the "Name of Event" field.
6. Choose the date of the event from the calendar or enter the date manually in the "Event Date" field.
7. Select the event time in the "Event Time" field.
8. If you are attending with a team, enter the team size in the "Team Size" field.
9. If you are not attending the event, mark "Is Silent?" as "True".
10. Add any additional notes about your attendance or requirements in the "Note" field.
11. Enter your email address again in the "Email" field for confirmation purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Impact Hackathon Registration Form**: This is the title of the form, which you are currently filling out.
* **Name**: Enter your full name in this field. Please make sure to enter your actual name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for event updates and communications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Attendee Type**: Select one of the options to indicate your role in the event (Developer, Student, Organizer).
* **Name of Event**: Enter the name of the event you are attending, as it appears in our records.
* **Event Date**: Choose the date of the event from the calendar or enter it manually in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Team Size**: If you are attending with a team, enter the number of team members.
* **Is Silent?**: Mark this field as "True" if you are not attending the event.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance or requirements.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address again for confirmation purposes.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form. Please ensure you fill in all required fields to avoid any issues with your registration.
