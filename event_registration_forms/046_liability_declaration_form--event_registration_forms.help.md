# Liability Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The liability declaration form is a document that requires participants to declare their liability in relation to an accident or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

### Page 1: Participant Information

* **Participant Information**: This page is used to collect general information about the participant.
*   **Participant Name**: Please provide your name as it appears on your identification card. (Text)
*   **Participant Address**: Please provide your address. (Text)
*   **Participant Phone**: Please provide your phone number. (Text)

### Page 2: Liability Declaration

* **Liability Type**: Please select one of the options below to declare your liability.
	+ Liability on behalf of participant
	+ Liability on behalf of participant's minor child/ward
	+ Liability on behalf of participant's spouse
	+ Liability on behalf of participant's parent(s)/guardian(s)
	+ (Other options may be added in the future)
* **Liability Name**: Please provide the name of the person or entity you are taking liability for. (Text)
*   **Date of Accident**: Please provide the date of the accident or event. (Date)
*   **Time of Accident**: Please provide the time of the accident or event. (Time)
*   **Location of Accident**: Please provide the location of the accident or event. (Text)
*   **Description of Accident**: Please provide a brief description of the accident or event. (Text)

### Page 3: Signature

*   **Participant Signature**: Please sign below to indicate that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of this form. (Text)

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the form administrators.
*   The participant's signature is not required, but it is highly recommended to ensure that the participant is aware of their responsibilities and obligations in relation to the event or accident.
