# Holiday Portrait Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The holiday portrait appointment form is used to collect information from clients regarding their holiday portrait appointment, including client details, date, time, location, and session details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's information in the "Client Info" field.
2. Select the date for the appointment in the "Date" field.
3. Choose the time for the appointment in the "Time" field.
4. Enter the location for the appointment in the "Location" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the session details in the "Session Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the client's name, contact details, or any other relevant information that you need to know.
* **Date** (`date`, required/optional): Select the date for the holiday portrait appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, required/optional): Choose the time for the holiday portrait appointment.
* **Location** (`location`, required/optional): Enter the location for the holiday portrait appointment.
* **Session Details** (`details`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the session details, such as any specific requirements or needs for the appointment.
