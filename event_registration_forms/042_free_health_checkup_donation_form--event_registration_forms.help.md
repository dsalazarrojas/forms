# free_health_checkup_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from donors and event organizers for donations and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
3. Specify the Donation Amount you want to donate.
4. Choose the Frequency of Donation (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually) and the Frequency of Donation Frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
5. Select the Donation Method (Cash, Online, In-person).
6. Enter the Notes if you have any additional information.
7. Choose the Contact Method (Phone, Email, In-person).
8. Enter the Contact Info for the person you want to contact.
9. Specify the Event Date and Event Time for the event.
10. Enter the Donor Name.
11. Enter the Event Location.
12. Enter the Event Description.
13. Enter the Contact Person's name.
14. Enter the Contact Phone number.
15. Enter the Contact Email.
16. Select the Payment Method (Cash, Credit Card, Mobile Payment).
17. Choose the Payment Frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
18. Enter the Additional Details if you have any.
19. Select the Donor Frequency (Single, One-time, Ongoing).
20. Enter the Notes for any additional information.
21. Enter the Event Date 2 and Event Time 2 (if the event has a second date and time).
22. Fill in all the fields accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Donation Amount** (`Donation Amount`, `number`, required): Specify the amount you want to donate.
* **Frequency of Donation** (`Frequency of Donation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how often you would like to donate (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Frequency of Donation Frequency** (`Frequency of Donation Frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the frequency of your donation (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Donation Method** (`Donation Method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the method of your donation (Cash, Online, In-person).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information you want to provide.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the method of contact (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact information for the person you want to contact.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Donor Name** (`Donor Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as a donor.
* **Event Location** (`Event Location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`Event Description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the payment method (Cash, Credit Card, Mobile Payment).
* **Payment Frequency** (`Payment Frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the payment frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Additional Details** (`Additional Details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details you want to provide.
* **Donor Frequency** (`Donor Frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the donor frequency (Single, One-time, Ongoing).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information you want to provide.
* **Event Date 2** (`Event Date 2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the second event.
* **Event Time 2** (`Event Time 2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the second event.

Tips: Please ensure to fill in all required fields carefully and accurately to avoid any errors. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the event organizers or contact persons.
