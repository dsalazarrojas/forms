# first_data_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and organizers for the "First Data Workshop". It aims to gather essential details for a smooth and productive event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct participant type: participant or organizer.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
3. Provide payment details (if applicable).
4. Choose your workshop preferences (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
5. Choose the date and start and end times of the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Type** (participant_type): 
	+ Choose between "participant" or "organizer".
* **Contact Info** (contact_info): 
	+ Enter your name and email address to help us contact you.
* **Payment Details** (payment_details): 
	+ Enter any payment information related to your registration (e.g., payment method, amount).
* **Workshop Preferences** (workshop_preferences): 
	+ Select your level of expertise: "beginner", "intermediate", or "advanced".
* **Workshop Date** (workshop_date): 
	+ Enter the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Start Time** (start_time): 
	+ Choose the start time for the workshop.
* **End Time** (end_time): 
	+ Choose the end time for the workshop.
