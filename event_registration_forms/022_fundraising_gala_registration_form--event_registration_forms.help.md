# Fundraising Gala Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees and participants for a fundraising gala event. It helps the organizers understand who will be attending, how they will be paying, and other important details about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Event Date, Name, Email, Phone number, and any other required information.
2. Select your preferred Payment Method and Payment Type.
3. Choose the type of event you will be attending (Gala, Fundraiser, Charity Event).
4. Specify the number of attendees you will be bringing.
5. Provide your donation amount.
6. Select a payment method for your donation (if applicable).
6. Enter your Event Location and Organization (if relevant).
7. Finally, submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Date** (event_date): Enter the date of the event you plan to attend.
- **Name** (name): Enter your name as you would like to be listed on the event guest list.
- **Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you with event details.
- **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you.
- **Event Time** (event_time): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the event.
- **Donation Amount** (donation_amount): Enter the amount you plan to donate to the cause.
- **Payment Method** (payment_method): Select how you would like to pay for your ticket (American Express, Discover, Mastercard, or Visa).
- **Event Type** (event_type): Choose the type of event you will be attending (Gala, Fundraiser, Charity Event).
- **Event Location** (event_location): Enter the location of the event.
- **Organization** (organization): If relevant, enter the name of the organization hosting the event.
- **Registration Date** (registration_date) and **Registration Time** (registration_time): Enter the date and time you registered for the event (for internal purposes).
- **Payment Type** (payment_type): Select how you will be paying for your ticket (Cash, Check, Credit Card).
- **Attendee Count** (attendee_count): Specify the number of people attending with you.

## Tips
- Please review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the section above for clarification.
- Make sure to submit your form to secure your spot at the event.
