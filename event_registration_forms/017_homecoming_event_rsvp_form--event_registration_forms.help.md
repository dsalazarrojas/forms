# Homecoming Event RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homecoming Event RSVP Form is a tool for guests to register their attendance and provide necessary information for the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Fill in your name in the Guest Name field.
3. Provide your contact information in the Contact Info field.
4. Answer the questions in the Guest Info field.
5. Choose your RSVP choice (attending, attending with a plus one, or not attending) in the RSVP Choice field.
6. Choose your dinner preference (dinner or no dinner) in the second RSVP Choice field.
7. Select any dietary restrictions you may have in the Guest Diet fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Guest Name** (`guest_name_1`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you enter your name.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info_1`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your contact information, so we can reach out to you about the event.
- **Guest Info** (`guest_info_1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for any additional information you'd like to share with us.
- **RSVP Choice** (`rsvp_choice_1`, `select_one`, true/true): Choose how many guests you will be attending with. You have the option to choose between Attending, Attending (with one), and Not Attending.
- **RSVP Choice** (`rsvp_choice_2`, `select_one`, true/true): Choose whether or not you will be having dinner during the event. Your options are Dinner and No Dinner.
- **Guest Diet** (`guest_diet_1`, `select_multiple`, true/true): Select any dietary restrictions you may have, including Vegetarian, Vegan, and Gluten-free.
- **Guest Diet** (`guest_diet_2`, `select_multiple`, true/true): This is a second field for you to select any additional dietary restrictions you may have.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy.
- If you have any special requests or comments, please include them in the Guest Info field.
