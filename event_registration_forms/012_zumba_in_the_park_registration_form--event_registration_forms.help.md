# Zumba In The Park Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event organizers for registering and scheduling Zumba classes in a park setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields respectively.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Add your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your organization or group affiliation in the "Organization" field.
5. Choose your role in the event from the options in the "Role" field.
6. Select the event name from the options in the "Event Name" field.
7. Set the start and end times for your event using the time picker in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
8. Choose the date of your event from the date picker in the "Date" field.
9. Enter the location of your event in the "Location" field.
10. Select the type of class you will be offering (class, party, or event) in the "Class Type" field.
11. Enter the number of participants expected and maximum number of participants allowed in the "Number of Participants" and "Maximum Number of Participants" fields.
12. Add any additional information about your event in the "Description" field.
13. Provide any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text, required)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (email, required)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone (text, required)**: Add your phone number.
* **Organization (text, optional)**: Select your organization or group affiliation.
* **Role (select_multiple, optional)**: Choose your role in the event from the options.
* **Event Name (select_one, optional)**: Select the event name from the options.
* **Start Time (time, optional)**: Set the start time using the time picker.
* **End Time (time, optional)**: Set the end time using the time picker.
* **Date (date, optional)**: Choose the date of your event from the date picker.
* **Location (text, optional)**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Class Type (select_one, optional)**: Select the type of class you will be offering (class, party, or event).
* **Number of Participants (number, optional)**: Enter the number of participants expected.
* **Maximum Number of Participants (number, optional)**: Enter the maximum number of participants allowed.
* **Description (text, optional)**: Add any additional information about your event.
* **Additional Info (text, optional)**: Provide any additional information.
* **Confirm (note, optional)**: This is a confirmation checkbox.
