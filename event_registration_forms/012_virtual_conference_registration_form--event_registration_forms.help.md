# Virtual Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Conference Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees for a virtual conference. The form will be used to gather details about the attendee, including their name, email, phone number, address, and payment method. This information will help us understand who is attending the conference and ensure that we can provide them with the necessary resources and support.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Conference Details**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional details about the conference you are attending.
2. **Attendee Info**: Enter your name and any other information you would like to share about yourself.
3. **Payment Method**: Select one or more payment methods from the options provided:
	* Stripe
	* PayPal
	* Bank Transfer
4. **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
5. **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and communication about the conference.
6. **Phone**: Enter your phone number to receive reminders and updates about the conference.
7. **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your registration.
8. **Address**: Enter your address to help us provide you with conference location and logistical information.
9. **City**: Enter the city where you will be attending the conference.
10. **State**: Enter the state where you will be attending the conference.
11. **Zip**: Enter your zip code to help us provide you with accurate shipping and logistical information.
12. **Country**: Enter the country where you will be attending the conference.
