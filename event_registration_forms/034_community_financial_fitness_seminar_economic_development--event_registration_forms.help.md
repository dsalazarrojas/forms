## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in the Community Financial Fitness Seminar Economic Development event. The purpose of the event is to provide attendees with guidance on personal financial wellness, education, and empowerment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Be sure to select only one option for employment status and annual income.
3. Select all relevant seminar topics of interest.
4. Choose your preferred seminar date and attendance type.
5. Indicate your primary financial goal.
6. List any current financial challenges you face.
7. Check the childcare needed and accessibility needs checkboxes accordingly.
8. Select where you heard about the seminar.
9. Indicate if you are interested in follow-up services.
10. Add any additional comments or information about your registration.
