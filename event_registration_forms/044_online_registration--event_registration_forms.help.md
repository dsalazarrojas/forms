# Online Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users who are registering for an event. The form helps us track and organize event registrations in a centralized manner. It is intended for users to provide their personal details and event-related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** to identify yourself.
2. Provide your **Phone** number for us to contact you if needed.
3. Select the **Event Name** of the event you are registering for from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose the **Event Type** of the event from the dropdown menu.
5. Select the **Assigned Tool** that you will be using for the event.
6. Choose the **Category** of the event from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter a brief **Description** of the event.
8. If necessary, provide additional details in the **Note** field.
9. You can also provide a **Phone** number for any additional information.
10. Finally, fill in the **Form ID** and **Form Title** with the details of the form you are registering for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you if needed.
* **Date**: Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Time**: Select the time of the event.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information that you think we should know about your event.
* **Event Name**: Select the name of the event from the dropdown menu.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of the event from the dropdown menu.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you for the event.
* **Category**: Select the category of the event from the dropdown menu.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Output File**: You will receive an output file with your event details.
* **Form ID**: Enter the ID of the form you are registering for.
* **Form Title**: Enter the title of the form you are registering for.
* **Form Category**: Enter the category of the form you are registering for.
* **Form Description**: Enter a brief description of the form you are registering for.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check the details you enter as they will be used for event planning and coordination.
- If you are unsure about any information, you can always reach out to us for clarification.
- Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate tracking of event registrations.
