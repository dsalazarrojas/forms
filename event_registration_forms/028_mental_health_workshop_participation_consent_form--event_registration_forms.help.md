# mental_health_workshop_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of the registration process for mental health workshops. By completing this form, participants acknowledge their understanding of the workshop's recording policy, rules, guidelines, terms of service, and informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your title, which will be associated with your participation in the workshop.
2. Choose a category for your participation, such as "Event Registration Forms" or "Workshop Registration Forms."
3. Provide a brief description of your participation, which will help workshop organizers understand your intentions and goals.
4. Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and workshop updates.
5. Enter your phone number, which will be used for contact and emergency situations.
6. Add any additional note or comment related to your participation.
7. Select a date and time for your participation, although this is not required.
8. Indicate your consent to be recorded during the workshop, choosing between "I understand the workshop will be recorded and I am okay with being recorded" or "I do not want to be recorded."
9. Agree or disagree to the workshop rules and guidelines.
10. Agree or disagree to the terms of service.
11. Select whether you have read and understood the workshop description and give your consent.
12. Indicate whether you are willing to share your participant information with the workshop organizers.
13. Select whether you are willing to share your participant information with the workshop organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter your title for the workshop participation.
* **category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose a category for your participation, such as "Event Registration Forms" or "Workshop Registration Forms."
* **description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your participation, which will help workshop organizers understand your intentions and goals.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and workshop updates.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for contact and emergency situations.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional note or comment related to your participation.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for your participation, although this is not required.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for your participation, although this is not required.
* **consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your consent to be recorded during the workshop, choosing between "I understand the workshop will be recorded and I am okay with being recorded" or "I do not want to be recorded."
* **agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Agree or disagree to the workshop rules and guidelines.
* **terms_of_service** (`terms_of_service`, select_multiple, required): Agree or disagree to the terms of service.
* **participant_informed_consent** (`participant_informed_consent`, select_one, required): Select whether you have read and understood the workshop description and give your consent.
* **participant_information** (`participant_information`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are willing to share your participant information with the workshop organizers.

## Tips
Please make sure to review each field carefully and understand the implications of your choices.
