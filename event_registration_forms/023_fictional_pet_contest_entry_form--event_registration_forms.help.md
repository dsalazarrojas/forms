# Fictional Pet Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fictional Pet Contest Entry Form is designed to collect information from users about their fictional pets. This form is intended for users to submit their pet's name, a brief story, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your pet's name in the **Pet Name** field.
2. Enter a brief story about your pet in the **Pet Story** field.
3. Select the category of your pet from the **Pet Category** options (Pet A, Pet B, or Pet C).
4. Enter any additional information you'd like about the event organizer, school name, community name, submission date, and submission time in their respective fields.
5. If applicable, enter the image link or upload a photo of your pet in the **Pet Image** field.
6. Finally, enter your pet's breed in the **Pet Breed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of your pet. This field is required.
* **Pet Image**: If you have a photo of your pet, you can either upload it or enter a link to the image online. This field is optional.
* **Pet Story**: Write a brief story about your pet. This field is optional.
* **Pet Category**: Select the category of your pet from the options provided (Pet A, Pet B, or Pet C). This field is optional.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer or sponsor, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **School Name**: If you're submitting on behalf of a school or educational institution, enter the name of the school here. This field is optional.
* **Community Name**: Enter the name of your community, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you're submitting this form. This field is optional.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you're submitting this form. This field is optional.
* **Pet Breed**: Enter the breed of your pet. This field is optional.

## Tips

- Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to submit information on behalf of a school or community.
- If you're having trouble accessing the form or submitting your information, contact form support at form@gic.mx.
- Double-check that you've entered all required and optional fields correctly before submitting the form.
