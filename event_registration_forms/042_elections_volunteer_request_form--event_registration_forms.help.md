# Elections Volunteer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elections Volunteer Request Form is a tool for event organizers to collect information from interested volunteers. By filling out this form, you'll be helping us to better understand your skills, availability, and preferences, making it easier for us to schedule you for events that match your capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide a brief description of your request to volunteer**:
	* In the "Request to volunteer" field, please type a short message explaining why you want to volunteer for elections events.
2. **Tell us how you can help**:
	* In the "How can you help" field, describe the specific tasks or skills you're willing to perform. For example, if you're comfortable with technology, you might mention that you can help with event setup or social media management.
3. **Specify your availability**:
	* In the "Which days" field, select the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
4. **Share your volunteer experience and details**:
	* In the "Volunteer details" field, provide more information about your volunteer experience, including any relevant skills or qualifications.
5. **Leave your contact information**:
	* In the "Phone" field, enter your phone number so we can contact you directly.
6. **Confirm your email address**:
	* In the "Email" field, enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
7. **Double-check your email address**:
	* In the "Confirm Email" field, type the same email address you entered in the previous step to ensure we have the right contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request to volunteer (text)**: 
	* Describe why you want to volunteer for elections events.
* **How can you help (text)**:
	* Describe the specific tasks or skills you're willing to perform.
* **Which days (select_one)**: 
	* Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Information (text)**: 
	* Provide more information about your volunteer experience, including any relevant skills or qualifications.
* **Volunteer details (text)**: 
	* Share your volunteer experience and any relevant details about your skills or qualifications.
* **Phone (text)**: 
	* Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly.
* **Email (email)**:
	* Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Confirm Email (email)**: 
	* Type the same email address you entered in the previous step to ensure accuracy.
