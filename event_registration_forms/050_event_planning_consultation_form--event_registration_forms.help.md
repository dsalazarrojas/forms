# Event Planning Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide essential information for event planning consultation. Fill it out to help us better understand your event needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all fields that apply to your event.
2. Ensure you provide the required information for each field.
3. Review your entries before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event you are planning (Event Type, Meeting, Conference, Seminar).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the address of the event location.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your event.
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, optional): Provide the name of the venue.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Add any additional event-specific details.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Provide a name for your event.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client associated with your event.

## Tips
* Please provide as much information as possible to help us better understand your event needs.
* Ensure you review your entries carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
