# performance_competition_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering and processing payments for participants in a performance competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's information, including their name, address, city, state, zip, and country.
2. Select the payment method (Bank transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
3. Enter the transaction date and time.
4. Enter the fee amount.
5. Select the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
6. Enter any additional notes about the transaction.
7. Enter the participant's email and phone number.
8. Enter the participant's name and enter the event information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`): This is the main section where you enter participant details. Enter the participant's name, address, city, state, zip, and country.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`): This section collects the payment method and fee amount.
	+ **Fee** (`fee`): Enter the fee amount due for the participant.
	+ **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method from the options (Bank transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
	+ **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select the payment status from the options (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Transaction Details** (`transaction_date`, `transaction_time`): These are the dates and times related to the transaction. Enter the transaction date and time.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes about the transaction.
* **Contact Information** (`email`, `phone`): This section collects the participant's contact information.
	+ **Email** (`email`): Enter the participant's email address.
	+ **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_name`, `participant_address`, `participant_city`, `participant_state`, `participant_zip`, `participant_country`): These are additional participant details that are entered in this section.
	+ **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter the participant's name.
	+ **Participant Address** (`participant_address`): Enter the participant's address.
	+ **Participant City** (`participant_city`): Enter the participant's city.
	+ **Participant State** (`participant_state`): Enter the participant's state.
	+ **Participant Zip** (`participant_zip`): Enter the participant's zip code.
	+ **Participant Country** (`participant_country`): Enter the participant's country.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`): This is an optional field for entering event-related information. Enter any details about the event that are relevant to the participant.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date participant information.
* Choose the correct payment method and payment status for each participant.
* Use the notes field to record any additional information about the transaction.
* Enter correct contact information for the participant.
* Review and edit the form as needed to ensure accuracy and completeness.
