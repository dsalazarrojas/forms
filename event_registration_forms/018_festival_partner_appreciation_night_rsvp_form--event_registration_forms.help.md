**Festival Partner Appreciation Night RSVP Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is for festival partners to RSVP for the appreciation night event. It is not mandatory, but it is encouraged to ensure that we can plan and prepare for the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please provide the following information:

1. **Festival Partner Name**: Enter your festival partner name as it is known to us.
2. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
3. **Role**: Select the role you play at the festival (Artist, Performer, or Vendor).
4. **Attending With**: Select whether you are attending with yourself, others, or a group.
5. **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for any urgent communication.
6. **RSVP**: Select yes or no to indicate if you will be attending.
7. **Number of Attendees**: Enter the number of people attending from your team.
8. **Comment**: If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to add them here.
9. **Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend.
10. **Time**: Enter the time you plan to arrive.
11. **Form ID**: This is a unique identifier for the form, please leave this field blank.
12. **Output File**: This is not required.
