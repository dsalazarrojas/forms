# Convent Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from guests to ensure a smooth and comfortable stay at the convent. It helps the staff understand their needs, preferences, and requirements, allowing us to provide personalized services and accommodate their stay accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully, and answer all the questions accurately.
2. Use a pen or keyboard to fill out the form clearly and legibly.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Full Name** (`guest_name`, Text, required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, required): Provide your best contact number in case we need to reach you.
* **Home Address** (`address`, Text, required): Enter your full mailing address for our records.
* **Stay Details** (`section_stay_details`, Note, not required): This is a free-form text field for any additional information about your stay. You can describe your preferences, needs, or requirements.
* **Check-in Date** (`check_in_date`, Date, required): Select the date you plan to arrive at the convent.
* **Check-out Date** (`check_out_date`, Date, required): Select the date you plan to depart from the convent.
* **Number of Nights** (`number_of_nights`, Number, required): Enter the total number of nights you plan to stay.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, Number, required): Enter the total number of people in your party.
* **Room Type Preference** (`room_type_preference`, Select One, required): Choose the type of accommodation you prefer (Single, Double, Twin, Suite, or Shared Room).
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, Select One, required): Select the reason for your visit (Religious Retreat, Pilgrimage, Tourism, Business, Family Visit, or Other).
* **Group Affiliation** (`group_affiliation`, Text, not required): If you're part of a group, provide the name and details of the group.
* **Special Occasion** (`special_occasion`, Text, not required): If this visit is for a special event, describe the occasion.
* **Meal Plan Preference** (`meal_preference`, Select One, required): Choose how you'd like to be accommodated for meals (No Meals, Breakfast Only, Half Board, or Full Board).
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, Select One, not required): If you have any dietary needs (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, or Other), select the option that best describes your requirements.
* **Smoking Preference** (`smoking_preference`, Select One, required): Choose whether you'd like to be accommodated in a non-smoking area or have access to a smoking area.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, Text, not required): If you have any special requirements for accessibility, describe them here.
* **Additional Services** (`section_additional_services`, Note, not required): This is a free-form text field for any additional requests or comments.
* **Will you attend chapel services?** (`chapel_attendance`, Select One, not required): Indicate whether you plan to attend chapel services (True, False, or Maybe).
* **Interested in guided tours?** (`guided_tour_interest`, Select One, not required): If you're interested in guided tours, select True; otherwise, False.
* **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, Select One, not required): If you need transportation, select True; otherwise, False.
* **Parking Required** (`parking_required`, Select One, not required): If you need parking, select True; otherwise, False.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, required): Choose how you'd like to pay (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash on Arrival, or Check).
* **Has deposit been paid?** (`deposit_paid`, Select One, not required): Indicate whether you've already paid the deposit (True, False, or Pending).
* **I understand and agree to follow convent rules** (`understand_rules`, Select One, required): Confirm that you've read and agree to follow the convent rules (True, False).
* **I will respect quiet hours** (`quiet_hours_respect`, Select One, required): Confirm that you'll respect the quiet hours (True, False).
* **Additional Requests or Comments** (`additional_requests`, Text, not required): If you have any other requests or comments, please describe them here.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, Select One, not required): Select how you found out about the convent (Website, Recommendation, Travel Agency, Religious Organization, Social Media, or Other).
