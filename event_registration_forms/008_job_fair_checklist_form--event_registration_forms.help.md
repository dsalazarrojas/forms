# Job Fair Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Fair Checklist Form is designed to capture attendee registration and feedback data for job fairs. This form helps organizers and coordinators gather essential information about attendees, event details, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the attendee details section with your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your job title and company (if applicable).
3. Enter your phone number and any other relevant contact information.
4. Fill out the event details section with the event name, date, time, and duration.
5. Select the event status (Completed, Cancelled, or Scheduled).
6. Enter the number of attendees expected and actual.
7. Add any comments or feedback about the event.
8. Complete the post-event report and event evaluation sections (if required).
9. Review and submit the form.

* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly.
* Enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the event organizers or administrators for assistance.
