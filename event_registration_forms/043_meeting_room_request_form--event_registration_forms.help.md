# Meeting Room Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Room Request Form is used to submit a request for a meeting room. It helps ensure that meeting rooms are allocated efficiently and that all necessary information is collected about the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of meeting you are requesting. Choose from Audio/Video or Meeting/Conference.
2. Enter your contact details. This will help us get in touch with you about your meeting room request.
3. Choose whether the meeting room is requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type_options`, select_multiple): Choose the type of meeting you are requesting (Audio/Video or Meeting/Conference).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details_form`, text): Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you about your meeting room request.
* **Meeting Room Requested** (`meeting_room_request_form`, select_one): Choose whether the meeting room is requested. Select 'Yes' if you need a meeting room, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips

* Please select 'Yes' to request a meeting room, and 'No' otherwise.
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields to complete the form.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, please contact us.
