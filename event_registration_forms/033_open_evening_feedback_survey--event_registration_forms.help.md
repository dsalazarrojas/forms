# Open Evening Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Open Evening Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback on the "Open Evening" event you attended. This information will help us make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have attended the event and are providing genuine feedback.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely, as incomplete or inaccurate information may affect the quality of the feedback.
3. If a field is not required, you may leave it blank, but be aware that missing information may limit the usefulness of your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional):
    This field is to enter the name of the event you attended.
    Please enter the exact name of the event as it was advertised or promoted.
- **Date Attended** (`date_attended`, date, optional):
    This field is to enter the date you attended the event.
    Please use the standard date format (DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional):
    This field is to rate your overall experience at the event.
    Please select one of the following options:
    - Excellent
    - Good
    - Fair
    - Poor
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional):
    This field is for any additional comments or feedback you have about the event.
    Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
    This field is for your email address.
    Please enter a valid email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
    This field is for your phone number.
    Please enter a valid phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your feedback is accurate and truthful.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Feedback" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
