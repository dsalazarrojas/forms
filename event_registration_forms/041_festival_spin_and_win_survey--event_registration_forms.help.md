# Festival Spin And Win Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event organizers and participants gather information about a festival or spin-and-win type event. It allows users to register their event and provide basic details such as event type, name, and description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information about your event.
2. Fill in the required fields (event type, event name, and event details) with the accurate information about your event.
3. You can leave the other fields blank or fill them in if you have any additional information to provide.
4. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Form** (`event_registration_form`, text, required): This field is for entering any additional information about your event. Please enter any relevant details about your event in this field.
* **What type of event** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event you are registering. This can be Concert, Exhibition, Competition, Food Festival, Seminar, or Other.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your event.
* **Organizer info** (`organizer_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the event organizer or the person responsible for the event.
* **Event details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your event.
* **Tips**:
* Please enter only accurate and relevant information about your event.
* Double-check your entries for spelling and grammar before submitting the form.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, contact form@gic.mx.

Note: This help guide is a simplified version of the form's functionality and might not cover all edge cases. If you need further clarification or have any questions, please contact form@gic.mx.
