# event-design-agency-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "event-design-agency-registration" form is designed to collect information about agencies interested in participating in events. This form helps the event organizers understand the agency's services, experience, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with a brief description of your agency's event.
2. Choose the **Agency Services** that you provide (you can select multiple options).
3. Select your **Portfolio** by choosing one option from the list.
4. Enter your **Agency Experience** in the required numerical field.
5. Fill in the **Production Team** field with a brief description of your team members.
6. Enter your **Agency Contact** information in the text field.
7. Enter your **Contact Email** address.
8. Fill in the **Contact Person** text field with the name of the contact person.
9. Enter your **Contact Phone** number.
10. Provide your **Agency Address** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your event.
* **Agency Services** (`agency_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the services you provide (multiple options).
* **Portfolio** (`agency_portfolio`, select_one, required/optional): Select your portfolio option from the list.
* **Agency Experience** (`agency_experience`, number, required/optional): Enter your agency's experience in years.
* **Production Team** (`production_team`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your team members.
* **Agency Contact** (`agency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter your agency's contact information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your agency's address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions, please contact the event organizers.
