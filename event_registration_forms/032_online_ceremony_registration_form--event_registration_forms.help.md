# Online Ceremony Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information for an online ceremony. It is designed to gather details about the event date, time, location, and participant's personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event date from the calendar or enter the date manually.
2. Choose the event time from the available options.
3. Enter the event location in the provided text field.
4. Fill in your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Write any additional message or information you'd like to share.
8. Choose your attendance status from the options provided.
9. Fill in any additional comments or notes you'd like.
10. Confirm your attendance status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event. This field is not required, but it's essential to have the correct date for record-keeping purposes.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of the event from the available options.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the event where it will take place.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Message** (`participant_message`, `text`, required: false): Write any additional message or information you'd like to share.
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your attendance status from the options provided.
* **Comments** (`participant_comments`, `note`, required: false): Fill in any additional comments or notes you'd like.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or information about your participation.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, `text`, required: false): Fill in any information about your participant status.
* **Attendance Status** (`participant_attendance_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm your attendance status.
