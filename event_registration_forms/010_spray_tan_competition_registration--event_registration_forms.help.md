# Spray Tan Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spray Tan Competition Registration form is a tool for participants to register for the competition, providing their personal and professional details, category preferences, and agreements to the competition rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Salon Information, Categories, Preference, Contestant Name, Email, Phone, Category Preference, and all Rule Agreement fields (except Rule Agreement 6, 8, 11, 16).
2. Select the preferred categories from the dropdown menu in the Categories field.
3. Choose your preference from the Category Preference field.
4. Agree or disagree with each rule in the Rule Agreement fields.
5. Enter your Contestant Name, Email, and Phone numbers.
