# Short Film Production Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for short film production planning and coordination. It is intended for use by short film producers, production managers, and other relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your reservation details, including your name, contact information, and production requirements.
2. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter your production details, including the type of production and any specific requirements.
4. Select the locations, equipment, and crew members required for your production.
5. Submit the form to initiate the reservation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Reservation Details
* **Reservation Details** (`reservation_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your production, including the title, synopsis, and any specific requirements.

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number to be contacted by our team for further discussion and coordination.

* **Production Details** (`production_details`, `text`, required): Enter the type of production (e.g., documentary, fiction, etc.), genre, and any specific production requirements.

* **Location Booking** (`location_booking`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the locations required for your production from the available options:

    * Option 1: Location 1
    * Option 2: Location 2
    * Option 3: Location 3

* **Equipment Booking** (`equipment_booking`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the equipment required for your production from the available options:

    * Option 1
    * Option 2

* **Crew Booking** (`crew_booking`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the crew members required for your production from the available options:

    * Option 1
    * Option 2
    * Option 3
    * Option 4

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure efficient processing of your reservation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
- You will be contacted by our team to confirm and discuss your reservation further.
