# find_friends_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The find_friends_form is designed to collect personal details to facilitate finding an individual in a database or system.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow the steps below:
    1.  Enter your first name in the **First name** field.
    2.  Enter your last name in the **Last name** field.
    3.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Ensure that the email address is valid.
    4.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

*   Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that the email address is valid and correctly formatted.
*   If you have any questions or issues while completing this form, contact your administrator or support team.
