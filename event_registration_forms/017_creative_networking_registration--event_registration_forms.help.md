# Creative Networking Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for creative professionals and event attendees to gather necessary information to facilitate creative networking opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section thoroughly, ensuring accuracy in your responses.
2. Select "Event Details" to enter information about the event you are attending (e.g., event name, location, date).
3. In "Attendee Details", provide information about yourself, such as your name, job title, and company.
4. In "Preferences", specify how you'd like to be contacted for creative networking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (text)**: Enter a brief description of the event you are attending, including the name, location, and date.
* **Attendee Details (text)**: Provide your name, job title, and company information to help facilitate networking opportunities.
* **Preferences (text)**: Specify your preferred method of contact (e.g., email, phone, LinkedIn) to receive networking invitations or opportunities.
