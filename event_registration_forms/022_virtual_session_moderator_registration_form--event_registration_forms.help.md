# Virtual Session Moderator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering Virtual Session Moderators. It helps us to gather information about your qualifications, experience, and timezone settings to ensure that you're well-prepared to moderate our virtual sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill out your first and last name. Please use your real name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter your email address to enable us to communicate with you about the session.
3. Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you about the session.
4. Choose your experience level as a moderator: Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
5. If you have previous experience as a moderator, describe your experience in the text field.
6. Enter the Event ID for the session you wish to moderate.
7. Choose whether you're in the same timezone as the event (Yes/No).
8. Select the timezone offset from the options provided.
9. Choose whether you're aware of the timezone offset (Yes/No).
10. Select the direction of the timezone offset (Yes/No).
11. Choose whether you have a timezone offset description (Yes/No).
12. Select the timezone offset (Yes/No), this is a multi-select option.
13. If you're in the same timezone as the event, select the correct time in the time field.
14. If you're not in the same timezone as the event, provide a description of your timezone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about the session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about the session.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your experience level as a moderator: Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Moderator Experience** (`moderator_experience`, text, optional): If you have previous experience as a moderator, describe it in this text field.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, text, required): Enter the ID for the session you wish to moderate.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, time, required): Select the time in your timezone if you're not moderating in the event's timezone.
* **Timezone Description** (`timezone_description`, text, optional): If you're not in the same timezone as the event, describe your timezone.
* **Yes/No to Timezone** (`timezone_offset`, select_one, required): Choose whether you're aware of the timezone offset.
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, select_multiple, required): Select the timezone offset.
* **Timezone Offset Unit** (`timezone_offset_unit`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have a timezone offset unit.
* **Timezone Offset Direction** (`timezone_offset_direction`, select_one, required): Choose the direction of the timezone offset.
* **Timezone Offset Description** (`timezone_offset_description`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have a timezone offset description.
* **Timezone** (`timezone_offset`, select_multiple, required): Select the timezone offset.
* **Timezone Description** (`timezone`, text, optional): If you're not in the same timezone as the event, provide a description of your timezone.
