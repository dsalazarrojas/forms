# RSVP Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RSVP Communication Form is used to gather information from guests about their plans to attend an event. This form helps event organizers communicate with guests and track responses in a centralized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Details section with information about the event you are planning to attend.
2. Provide Guest Details, including any relevant information about yourself as a guest.
3. Let us know your response to attending the event. Select "Yes" if you plan to attend, "No" if you do not plan to attend.
4. Add any additional comments about your response.
5. Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on event details.
6. Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you with any last-minute updates.
7. Finally, specify the event date if you are unsure or have not already mentioned it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event, event type, or a brief description of the event.
### Guest Details
- **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself as a guest, such as your dietary restrictions or special requests.
### Response
- **Response** (`response`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your plan to attend the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
### Comment
- **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or reasons for your response.
### Email
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on event details.
### Phone
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you with any last-minute updates.
### Event Date
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Specify the event date if you are unsure or have not already mentioned it.
