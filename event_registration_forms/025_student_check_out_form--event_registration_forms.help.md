# Student Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the check-out of a student from an event, capturing essential details for accurate records and tracking attendance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's ID in the `student_id` field.
2.  Select the event ID from the `event_id` field.
3.  Enter the staff member's ID in the `staff_id` field.
4.  Fill in the student's name in the `student_name` field.
5.  Enter the student's date of birth in the `student_dob` field.
6.  Enter the name of the event in the `event_name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **student_id** (`student_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the student's unique ID number. This is used to identify the student in our system.
*   **event_id** (`event_id`, number, required/optional): Select the event ID where the student was checked out. This ensures accurate tracking of student attendance for that event.
*   **staff_id** (`staff_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of the staff member who checked the student out. This helps in tracking staff interactions with students.
*   **student_name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Fill in the student's full name for identification purposes.
*   **student_dob** (`student_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter the student's date of birth for age validation and other relevant purposes.
*   **event_name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event where the student was checked out.

## Tips

*   Always ensure you're completing the form for the correct student and event to avoid errors in our records.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact our support team for assistance.
*   Providing accurate information in all fields will help us process your request efficiently and effectively.
