# Live Event Broadcast Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about live events for release to the public. It's essential to provide accurate details about the event to ensure a smooth and error-free release process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Participant Name
	* Event Title
	* Event ID
	* Release Date
	* Production Location
2. Choose the relevant options for the following fields:
	* Do you grant permission to prophets to broadcast your voice? (Select "True" or "False")
	* Release Type: Live, Archived, or VOD (Video on Demand)
	* Production Type: Live, Pre-Recorded, or Post-Recorded
	* Broadcast Platform Prophets: Enter the names of prophets to be involved in the broadcast (optional)
3. Provide optional information:
	* Broadcast Date and Time (if applicable)
	* Participant Notes (any additional information you'd like to provide about your involvement in the event)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the participant in the event.
* **Do you grant permission to prophets to broadcast your voice?** (`broadcast_permission`, select_multiple, optional): If you grant permission, select "True", otherwise select "False".
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the event.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event.
* **Release Type** (`release_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the event is Live, Archived, or VOD.
* **Production Company** (`production_company`, text, required): Enter the production company associated with the event.
* **Production Team** (`production_team`, text, required): Enter the production team responsible for the event.
* **Broadcast Date** (`broadcast_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the broadcast will occur.
* **Broadcast Time** (`broadcast_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the broadcast (if applicable).
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the release will occur.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of release (if applicable).
* **Production Type** (`production_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the production is Live, Pre-Recorded, or Post-Recorded.
* **Production Location** (`production_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the production took place.
* **Broadcast Platform Prophets** (`broadcast_platform_prophets`, text, optional): Enter the names of prophets involved in the broadcast (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a contact email (if applicable).
* **Prophets Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of a contact person (if applicable).
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your involvement in the event.
