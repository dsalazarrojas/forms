# Corporate Open House Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for the corporate open house event. Please fill out the form to register for the event and provide details about yourself and your company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
	* Email: Enter your company email address.
	* Phone: Enter your company phone number.
2. Fill in your company details:
	* Company: Enter your company name.
	* Role: Enter your job title or position in the company.
	* Job Function: Enter your job function or department.
	* Department: Enter your department name.
3. Fill in event details:
	* Building: Enter the building where you would like to attend the event.
	* Floor: Enter the floor number of the building where you would like to attend the event.
	* Room: Enter the room number where you would like to attend the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job function or department.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your department name.
* **Building** (`building`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the building where you would like to attend the event.
* **Floor** (`floor`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the floor number of the building where you would like to attend the event.
* **Room** (`room`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the room number where you would like to attend the event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the correct building and room numbers before submitting the form.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please contact the event organizers separately.
* You can only register once for this event. If you have already registered, do not submit this form again.
