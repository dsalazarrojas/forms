# Art Exhibition Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Exhibition Permission Form is designed to collect necessary information and permissions from artists participating in an art exhibition. It helps galleries, curators, or art administrators manage artist permissions and agreements efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Provide your artist full name
* Enter your artist email address
* Enter your artist phone number
* Select the date of permission for the artwork to be exhibited
* Provide the exhibition name and its duration
* Enter the exhibition venue
* Enter the title of the artwork
* Specify the medium or material of the artwork
* Enter any artwork dimensions (if applicable)
* Enter the estimated value for the artwork's insurance
* Select permissions for the exhibition, including display, photography, and promotional use
* Specify the artist's attribution requirement
* Choose the insurance responsibility between the gallery, artist, or shared
* Enter any commission or sale agreement percentage (if applicable)
* Agree or disagree with exhibition removal procedures
* Decide if an artist statement or bio is to be included
* Finalize by acknowledging and granting the above permissions

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as an artist.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Permission Date** (date, required): Select the date of permission for the artwork to be exhibited.
* **Exhibition Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the exhibition.
* **Exhibition Date and Duration** (text, required): Enter the date and duration of the exhibition.
* **Exhibition Venue** (text, required): Enter the name and location of the exhibition venue.
* **Artwork Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the artwork.
* **Medium or Material** (text, required): Specify the medium or material of the artwork.
* **Artwork Dimensions** (text, required): Enter any dimensions of the artwork (if applicable).
* **Estimated Value for Insurance** (number, required): Enter the estimated value of the artwork for insurance purposes.
* **Permission to Display Artwork** (select one, required): Select "Yes, I permit" to grant permission for the artwork to be displayed.
* **Permission to Photograph Artwork** (select one, required): Select "Yes, I permit" to grant permission for the artwork to be photographed.
* **Permission to Use in Promotional Materials** (select one, required): Select "Yes, I permit" to grant permission for the artwork to be used in promotional materials, or "With credit only" for promotional use with artist credit.
* **Require Artist Attribution or Credit** (select one, required): Select "Yes, required" for artist attribution.
* **Insurance Responsibility** (select one, required): Select "Gallery responsible," "Artist responsible," or "Shared responsibility" for insurance responsibility.
* **Commission or Sale Agreement** (text, optional): Enter any commission or sale agreement percentage (if applicable).
* **Agree to Exhibition Removal Procedures** (select one, required): Select "Yes, I agree" to exhibition removal procedures.
* **Artist Statement or Bio Included** (select one, optional): Select "True" to include an artist statement or bio.
* **I Acknowledge and Grant the Above Permissions** (select one, required): Select "Yes, I acknowledge" to confirm the granted permissions.

## Tips

* Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and agreements before proceeding with the exhibition.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the gallery or curator for assistance.
* Remember to review and agree to the exhibition terms and conditions carefully.
