# Networking Event Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Networking Event Check In Form" is designed to collect information from attendees about their event details, contact info, role, and event type. This form will help you to efficiently register attendees for the networking event and keep a record of their participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Event Details" section with the relevant information about the event.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Attendee Info" to indicate your attendance status.
3. Select the correct "Check In Time" for your registration.
4. Fill in your "Contact Info" in the "Contact Info" field.
5. Add any "Notes" you'd like to include.
6. Provide your "Email" and "Phone" contact information.
7. Fill in your "Name", "Organization", "Role", and "Job Title".
8. Add a "Bio" about yourself.
9. Fill in the "Company", "Location", and "Event" fields.
10. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Event Type" to indicate the type of event you're attending.
11. Select the correct "Event Type Options" from the dropdown list.
12. Enter the "Event Date" and "Event Time" for the event.
13. Add any additional "Notes" about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details (1)**: Enter the details about the event, including the name, date, and time.
* **Attendee Info (2)**: Choose "Yes" to indicate you are attending the event, or "No" to indicate you are not.
* **Check In Time (3)**: Select the time you plan to check in for the event.
* **Contact Info (4)**: Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Notes (5)**: Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Name (8)**: Enter your name.
* **Organization (9)**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Role (10)**: Choose the role you are attending the event as (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Role Options (11)**: Select the specific role you are attending the event as (e.g., "Organizer" or "Other").
* **Title (12)**: Enter your job title.
* **Department (13)**: Enter your department.
* **Job Title (14)**: Enter your job title.
* **Bio (15)**: Add a brief bio about yourself.
* **Company (16)**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Location (17)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event (18)**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Type (19)**: Choose "Yes" to indicate the type of event you're attending, or "No" to indicate you are not.
* **Event Type Options (20)**: Select the specific type of event you are attending.
* **Event Date (21)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time (22)**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Notes (23)**: Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Email (24)**: Re-enter your email address (this field is identical to field 6).
* **Phone (25)**: Re-enter your phone number (this field is identical to field 7).

Please note that the "Event Type" and "Event Type Options" fields are identical and are intended to be mutually exclusive.
