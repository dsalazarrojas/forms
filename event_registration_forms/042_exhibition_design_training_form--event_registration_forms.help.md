# Exhibition Design Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibition Design Training Form is designed to gather information from participants about their interest in and experience with exhibition design training. It is intended to be a simple and intuitive form that captures the necessary details to improve the training process and provide valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name and email address in the participant information field.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are interested in the training session.
3. Provide your feedback about the training in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name and Email** (participant_info, text, required/optional): Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as a participant. This information will be used to follow up with you after the training.
* **Training Session Interest** (training_session, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are interested in the training session by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us gauge the demand for the training.
* **Feedback Question** (feedback_question, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the training session in this text field. Your feedback is valuable to us in improving the training process.

## Tips
Remember to fill out the form accurately and completely, as this information will be used to improve the training process and provide valuable insights. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
