# Digital Transformation Expo Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from companies interested in sponsoring the Digital Transformation Expo. It helps event organizers assess the level of interest and support from potential sponsors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's name in the **Company Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Provide your company's phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4. Enter your company's address in the **Company Address** field.
5. Name the person who will be the main contact for your company's sponsorship in the **Contact Person** field.
6. Enter your job title in the **Job Title** field.
7. Choose the level of sponsorship your company is interested in from the **Sponsorship Type** dropdown menu.
8. Enter the amount of financial support your company is willing to provide in the **Amount** field.
9. Optionally, provide a brief description of why your company is interested in sponsoring the Digital Transformation Expo in the **Description** field.
10. If you have any additional documents or notes to attach, you can upload them in the **File** field.
11. Enter the start and end times of the event in the **Event Start Time** and **Event End Time** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Required): Enter your company's name as it appears in your official records.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Address** (Required): Enter your company's address.
* **Contact Person** (Required): Name the person who will be the main contact for your company's sponsorship.
* **Job Title** (Required): Enter your job title.
* **Sponsorship Type** (Required): Choose the level of sponsorship your company is interested in:
	+ Gold Sponsor
	+ Silver Sponsor
	+ Bronze Sponsor
* **Amount** (Required): Enter the amount of financial support your company is willing to provide.
* **Description** (Optional): Provide a brief description of why your company is interested in sponsoring the Digital Transformation Expo.
* **File** (Optional): Attach any additional documents or notes related to your sponsorship.
* **Event Start Time** (Required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (Required): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Note** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or notes related to your sponsorship.
* **Assigned Tool** (Optional): This field is not currently used.

Tips:

* Please make sure to double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact the event organizers.
* The event organizers will review your submission and follow up with you regarding the next steps.
