# job_fair_attendance_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The job fair attendance form is designed to collect information from job seekers who have attended the job fair. This includes their personal details, event participation details, and follow-up information. It's crucial to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the **First Name** field, which requires your full name.
2. Enter a valid **Email** address to ensure we can contact you for follow-up.
3. Enter your **Contact Number** for further follow-up purposes.
4. Select the correct **Job Title** you are interested in or provide an **Other Job Title** if it's not listed.
5. Choose the correct **Event Type** for the event you attended (Job Fair, Networking Event, or Trade Show).
6. Select the size of your **Company** (Small, Medium, or Large).
7. Enter the **Event Date** and **Event Start Time** and **Event End Time** to specify the event details.
8. Optionally, enter any additional **Job Fair Details** or **Follow-up Details** if required.
9. Check the **Follow-up Status** to indicate if follow-up has been conducted (No, Followed up, or Waiting).
10. Select the **Follow-up Frequency** (One time, Multiple) to specify the follow-up schedule.
11. Choose the **Follow-up Method** (Phone, Email, In-person) for conducting follow-up activities.
12. If necessary, provide additional **Follow-up Notes** for further reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: A valid email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Contact Number**: A contact number for further follow-up and communication.
* **Job Title**: Select the job title you're interested in or provide an alternative title if not listed.
* **Company**: Choose the size of your company (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you attended (Job Fair, Networking Event, Trade Show).
* **Event Date**, **Event Start Time**, **Event End Time**: Provide the event details for accurate record-keeping.
* **Job Fair Details**: Enter any additional job fair details if required.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Provide any additional notes for follow-up activities.
* **Follow-up Status**: Indicate if follow-up has been conducted (No, Followed up, Waiting).
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Specify the follow-up schedule (One time, Multiple).
* **Follow-up Method**: Choose the follow-up method (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Follow-up Note 2**, **Follow-up Note 3**: Provide additional follow-up notes if required.
