# Educational Leadership Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Leadership Training Registration Form is a user registration form designed to collect information from users about their personal details, contact information, and preferences for the training program. This form helps the staff or faculty members of an educational institution to keep track of registered users and plan the training program effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal and contact information fields with your details as requested.
2. Select the desired training session(s) you wish to attend.
3. Choose the training location(s) you prefer.
4. Provide your date of birth if applicable.
5. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Information** (`personal_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your name and any other personal details you wish to share with the staff or faculty members of the educational institution.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact details here, such as your email address and phone number, so that the staff or faculty members can reach out to you about the training program.
- **Registration Information** (`registration_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information you think is necessary for the staff or faculty members to consider your registration for the training program.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): If you wish to provide your date of birth for any reason, please fill this field out with your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Training Session** (`training_session`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one or more of the training sessions offered by the program. Please select the session(s) you are interested in attending.
- **Training Location** (`training_location`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more of the training locations offered by the program. Please select the location(s) you prefer for the training session(s) you have chosen.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or needs, you can include them in the "Registration Information" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask the staff or faculty members of the educational institution for clarification.
