# Glam Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Glam Event Registration Form" is designed to collect information from attendees for events. This form helps event organizers gather necessary details, such as attendee contact information, attendance specifics, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information**: This is the first section of the form where you can provide any additional registration details.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or government documents.
* **Email Address**: Input your valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Attendance**: Choose the time you plan to attend the event.
* **Attendee Type**: Select whether you are a VIP, Regular, or Guest.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method, such as Cash, Card, Online, or In-App.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any further information to provide, you can do so here.
