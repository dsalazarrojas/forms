# Volunteering Program Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who are interested in participating in our volunteering program. By filling out this form, you will be matched with suitable volunteer opportunities that align with your skills and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. List any relevant skills or experiences you have in the "Skills" field.
6. Select a date and time that you are available to volunteer in the "Availability" field (this will help us match you with opportunities on days and times that fit your schedule).
7. Finally, read and acknowledge that we will be in touch with you to match you with suitable volunteer opportunities in the "Opportunity Matching" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your given name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your surname as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email (email, required/optional)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and communication.
* **Phone (text, required/optional)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for any further information or updates.
* **Skills (text, required/optional)**: List any skills, experiences, or certifications that you possess, which may be relevant to volunteer work, such as language proficiency, medical knowledge, or manual labor expertise.
* **Availability (date, required/optional)**: Select a date and time when you are available to volunteer. This will help us match you with opportunities that fit your schedule.
* **Opportunity Matching (note, required/optional)**: This field is a confirmation that we will be in touch with you to match you with suitable volunteer opportunities. Please read and acknowledge this message by checking this field.
