# Stakeholder Meeting Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from stakeholders about their attendance to a meeting. It is intended for internal use to help track attendance and gather feedback from stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Participant Last Name** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Participant Email** in the third field. This will help us contact you if we need to follow up.
4. Enter your **Participant Phone** in the fourth field.
5. Select your **Attendance Status** in the fifth field. Choose from:
	* True (attended)
	* False (did not attend)
	* Not Sure (if you're not sure or need to provide more information)
6. Enter a brief **Attendance Note** in the sixth field if you'd like to provide additional context about your attendance.
7. Select the **Meeting Date** (or leave blank if not applicable) in the seventh field.
8. Enter the **Meeting Time** (or leave blank if not applicable) in the eighth field.
9. Enter any **Comments** about the meeting in the ninth field.
10. Select your **Stakeholder Organization** in the 10th field (if applicable).
11. Enter the **Meeting Location** in the 11th field.
12. Enter the **Meeting Location Address** in the 12th field.
13. Enter the **Meeting Location Phone** in the 13th field (if applicable).
14. Enter the **Meeting Location Email** in the 14th field (if applicable).
15. Enter the **Meeting Attendance Capacity** (number of attendees) in the 15th field.
16. Select the **Meeting Start Time** (or leave blank if not applicable) in the 16th field.
17. Select the **Meeting End Time** (or leave blank if not applicable) in the 17th field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant First Name**: Enter the first name of the participant.
* **Participant Last Name**: Enter the last name of the participant.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Attendance Status**: Select the attendance status of the participant.
* **Attendance Note**: Enter any additional notes about the participant's attendance.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments about the meeting.
* **Stakeholder Organization**: Select the organization associated with the participant (if applicable).
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location Address**: Enter the address of the meeting location.
* **Meeting Location Phone**: Enter the phone number of the meeting location (if applicable).
* **Meeting Location Email**: Enter the email address of the meeting location (if applicable).
* **Meeting Attendance Capacity**: Enter the capacity of the meeting (number of attendees).
* **Meeting Start Time**: Select the start time of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Meeting End Time**: Select the end time of the meeting (if applicable).

Note: This form is intended to be completed by stakeholders who have been invited to a meeting. If you are not sure about any of the fields, please contact the meeting organiser for clarification.
