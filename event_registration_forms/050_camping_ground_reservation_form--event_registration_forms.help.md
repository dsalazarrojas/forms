# Camping Ground Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from campers to facilitate the reservation process and ensure a smooth stay at the camping ground.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the personal details section, which includes your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your camping type and preferred site.
3. Enter your payment information, including the total amount due and your preferred payment method.
4. Confirm your agreement with the cancellation and weather policies, as well as the terms and conditions.
5. Provide a signature and date to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest First Name**: First name of the camper. 
* **Guest Last Name**: Last name of the camper. 
* **Email Address**: Contact email of the camper. 
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone number of the camper. 
* **Street Address**: Your address. 
* **City**: City or town where you are staying. 
* **State**: State or province where you are staying. 
* **ZIP Code**: Postal code. 
* **Check-In Date**: Arrival date. 
* **Check-Out Date**: Departure date. 
* **Number of Nights**: Total number of nights you plan to stay. 
* **Number of Guests**: Total number of people in your group. 
* **Number of Adults**: Number of adults 18 and over. 
* **Number of Children**: Number of children under 18. 
* **Camping Type**: Type of camping (e.g., Tent camping, RV camping, etc.). 
* **Site Selection**: Choose your preferred site. 
* **RV Specifications**: If applicable, provide information about your RV. 
* **Hookup Needs**: Select all that apply (e.g., Electric 30 amp, Electric 50 amp, etc.). 
* **Equipment Rental**: Select all that apply (e.g., Tent rental, Sleeping bags, etc.). 
* **Are You Bringing Pets**: Yes or no. 
* **Pet Fee Acknowledged**: If applicable, acknowledge that you are bringing pets. 
* **Special Requests**: Any special needs or requests. 
* **Accessibility Needs**: Describe any accessibility needs. 
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select all that apply (e.g., Website, Social media, etc.). 
* **Is This Your First Time Camping**: Yes or no. 
* **Would You Like Camping Tips**: Yes or no. 
* **Base Fee**: Per night fee. 
* **Additional Fees**: Extra charges. 
* **Total Amount**: Total cost. 
* **Deposit Required**: Amount of deposit required. 
* **Payment Method**: How you will pay (e.g., Credit card, Debit card, etc.). 
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledged**: Yes or no. 
* **Weather Policy Acknowledged**: Yes or no. 
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted**: Yes or no. 
* **Guest Signature**: Type full name. 
* **Date Signed**: Date. 

Note: This is a help guide for campers to fill out the Camping Ground Reservation Form. The form is designed to collect personal details, camping preferences, and payment information to ensure a smooth stay at the camping ground.
