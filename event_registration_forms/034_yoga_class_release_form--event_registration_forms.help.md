# yoga_class_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students who are registering for a yoga class. It is used to gather contact details, medical information, and emergency contact details, as well as obtain their consent to participate in the class and sign a waiver.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your street address in the "street_address" field.
3. Enter your city in the "city" field.
4. Enter your state in the "state" field.
5. Enter your zip code in the "zip_code" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
8. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your practice, list them in the "do_you_have_any_medical_conditions" field.
9. Provide emergency contact information in the "emergency_contact_info" field.
10. Specify your relationship to the emergency contact in the "relationship_to_contact" field.
11. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
12. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the "contact_email" field.
13. Choose your consent level with the instructor in the "instructor_consent" field.
14. Select the waivers that apply to you in the "waiver" field.
15. Select the medical waivers that apply to you in the "medical_waiver" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name**: (1, text, required: false): Your first name.
- **street_address**: (2, text, required: false): Your street address.
- **city**: (3, text, required: false): The city where you live.
- **state**: (4, text, required: false): The state where you live.
- **zip_code**: (5, text, required: false): Your zip code.
- **email**: (6, email, required: false): Your email address.
- **phone**: (7, text, required: false): Your phone number.
- **have_you_read_and_understand_the_yoga_class_routine**: (8, text, required: false): This is a text field for you to indicate that you have read and understand the yoga class routine.
- **do_you_have_any_medical_conditions**: (9, text, required: false): List any medical conditions that may affect your practice.
- **emergency_contact_info**: (10, text, required: false): Provide emergency contact information, such as name and relationship.
- **relationship_to_contact**: (11, text, required: false): Specify your relationship to the emergency contact.
- **contact_phone**: (12, text, required: false): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
- **contact_email**: (13, email, required: false): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
- **instructor_consent**: (14, select_one, required: true): Choose your level of consent with the instructor.
- **waiver**: (15, select_multiple, required: true): Select the waivers that apply to you.
- **medical_waiver**: (16, select_multiple, required: true): Select the medical waivers that apply to you.
