# dropshipping_workshop_enrollment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential attendees of a dropshipping workshop. It is used to gather relevant details such as name, contact information, and experience level with dropshipping, as well as their availability for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form fields** with the required and optional details as prompted.
2. **Review** each field to ensure you are providing accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **1. First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): 
Enter your first name as it should be on any official documents.

### **2. Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): 
Enter your last name as it should be on any official documents.

### **3. Email** (`email`, email, optional): 
Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.

### **4. Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): 
Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you for further information or updates.

### **5. Dropshipping Experience** (`dropshipping_experience`, select_one, optional):
Select your level of experience with dropshipping:
   - **Beginner**: You have little to no experience with dropshipping.
   - **Intermediate**: You have some knowledge or experience with dropshipping.
   - **Advanced**: You have significant experience with dropshipping.

### **6. Products Sourced** (`products_sourced`, select_multiple, optional):
Select from the options below to indicate if you have sourced products:
   - **True**: You have sourced products in the past.
   - **False**: You have not sourced products in the past.
   - **Maybe**: You are unsure about sourcing products in the past.

### **7. Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): 
Select the date you can attend the workshop. Please format the date as `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### **8. Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, optional): 
Select the time you can attend the workshop. Please format the time as `HH:MM AM/PM`.

### **9. Note** (`note`, note, optional): 
Add any additional comments or information you would like to provide about your application.

### **10. Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): 
Click this button to submit your application.
