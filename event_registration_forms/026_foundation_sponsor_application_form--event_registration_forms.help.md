# Foundation Sponsor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The foundation sponsor application form is used to gather information from foundations and sponsors who wish to partner with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization or foundation applying to be a sponsor.
	* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the organization or foundation.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
2. Optional fields:
	* **Organization Type**: Indicate if the organization or foundation is a sponsor or not. This will help us categorize the information.
	* **Website**: Enter the website of the organization or foundation.
	* **Address**: Enter the address of the organization or foundation.
	* **Zip/Postal Code**: Enter the zip or postal code of the organization or foundation.
	* **City**: Enter the city of the organization or foundation.
	* **State**: Enter the state of the organization or foundation.
	* **Country**: Enter the country of the organization or foundation.
	* **Sponsor Info**: Enter any additional information about the sponsor or foundation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Info**: Enter a brief description of the organization or foundation.
* **Contact Details**: Enter a brief description of the contact person for the organization or foundation.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the organization or foundation.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization or foundation.
* **Organization Type**: Select whether the organization or foundation is a sponsor or not.
* **Website**: Enter the website of the organization or foundation.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the organization or foundation.
* **Zip/Postal Code**: Enter the zip or postal code of the organization or foundation.
* **City**: Enter the city of the organization or foundation.
* **State**: Enter the state of the organization or foundation.
* **Country**: Enter the country of the organization or foundation.
* **Sponsor Info**: Enter any additional information about the sponsor or foundation.
