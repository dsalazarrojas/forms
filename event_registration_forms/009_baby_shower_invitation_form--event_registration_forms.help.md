# Baby Shower Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from guests attending a baby shower. It's used to gather details about their attendance, preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
3. Choose your attending status by selecting "Yes, I'll be there" or "Regretfully, I cannot attend".
4. If you are attending, enter the number of people in your party.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, select all that apply from the list.
6. If you'd like to receive the gift registry links, select "Yes, please send them to me".
7. Enter your email address for event updates and reminders.
8. Enter your mobile number for additional contact.
9. If you'd like, leave a personal message for the parents-to-be.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hosted By** (`Hosted By`, text, required): Enter the names of the people organizing the baby shower.
* **Guest Name** (`Guest Full Name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your invitation.
* **Will you be joining us?** (`Attending Status`, select_one, required): Choose whether you'll be attending the event or not.
* **Number of people in your party** (`Total Guests Attending`, number, required): Enter the number of people attending the event, including yourself.
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions?** (`Dietary Needs`, select_multiple, false): If you have dietary restrictions, select all that apply (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, etc.).
* **Would you like the gift registry links?** (`Registry Information`, select_one, false): Choose whether you want to receive the gift registry links.
* **For event updates and reminders** (`Contact Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for event updates and reminders.
* **Mobile Number** (`Contact Phone`, text, false): Enter your mobile number for additional contact.
* **Leave a sweet note for the parents-to-be** (`Message for Parents`, text, false): Write a personal message for the parents-to-be.
