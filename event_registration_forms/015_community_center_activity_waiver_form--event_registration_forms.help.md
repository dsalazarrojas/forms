# Community Center Activity Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for an event or activity and to obtain their waiver of liability for any injuries or damages that may occur during the event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct information.
3. If you need to, you can use the provided hints to guide you through each field.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant name (`participant_name`)
Participant name is the full name of the person who will be participating in the event or activity.

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, required): Full name of participant or guardian. Please provide the full name of the person who will be participating in the event or activity.

### Participant date of birth (`participant_dob`)
Participant date of birth is the date of birth for minors.

* **Participant date of birth** (`participant_dob`, optional): Date of birth for minors. If the participant is 18 years or older, this field can be left blank.

### Emergency contact name (`emergency_contact_name`)
Emergency contact name is the name of the person to contact in an emergency.

* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required): Name of person to contact in emergency. Please provide the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.

### Emergency contact phone (`emergency_contact_phone`)
Emergency contact phone is the phone number for the emergency contact.

* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, required): Phone number for emergency contact. Please provide the phone number for the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.

### Activity description (`activity_description`)
Activity description is the name of the program or activity.

* **Activity description** (`activity_description`, required): Name of program or activity. Please provide a brief description of the activity that the participant will be participating in.

### Known medical conditions (`known_medical_conditions`)
Known medical conditions is the list of allergies, medications, or restrictions.

* **Known medical conditions** (`known_medical_conditions`, optional): List of allergies, medications, or restrictions. If the participant has any known medical conditions, please list them here.

### Liability acknowledgement (`liability_acknowledgement`)
Liability acknowledgement is the participant's agreement to assume risks.

* **Liability acknowledgement** (`liability_acknowledgement`, required): Participant agrees to assume risks. Please select one of the options to indicate whether the participant agrees to assume risks.

### Photo release consent (`photo_release`)
Photo release consent is the participant's consent to use their photo for promotion.

* **Photo release consent** (`photo_release`, optional): Consent to use photos for promotion. Please select one of the options to indicate whether the participant consents to the use of their photo for promotion.

### Signature name (`signature_name`)
Signature name is the name typed as a digital signature.

* **Signature name** (`signature_name`, required): Type full name as digital signature. Please type your full name as a digital signature to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
