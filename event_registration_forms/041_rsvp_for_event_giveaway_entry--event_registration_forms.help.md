# RSVP For Event & Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for an event and a possible giveaway entry. It gathers essential details such as event name, date, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you have checked the event calendar to confirm that the event is scheduled and you want to attend or participate in the giveaway.
2. Fill out the form with your relevant information, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
3. Review the form to ensure that you have provided accurate details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date** (2, date, optional): Enter the date of the event (if available).
* **RSVP Status** (3, select_one, required): Select whether you are planning to attend the event, do not attend, or are unsure.
* **Giveaway Entry Status** (4, select_one, required): Select whether you are interested in participating in the giveaway.
* **Event Attendee** (5, text, required): Enter your name as an attendee of the event.
* **Prophet** (6, text, required): Enter the name of the prophet or person you are representing (if applicable).
* **Contact Number** (7, text, optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes (if available).
* **Email** (8, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes (if available).
* **Note** (9, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about your event registration or giveaway entry.
* **Event Details** (10, text, optional): Provide additional information about the event (if needed).
* **Giveaway Details** (11, text, optional): Provide details about your giveaway entry (if applicable).
* **Attendee Details** (12, text, optional): Add any relevant details about your attendee experience or preferences.
* **Follow-up** (13, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of follow-up you would like to receive (Email, Phone, or None).
* **Follow-up 2** (14, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of follow-up you would like to receive (Email, Phone, or None).
* **Follow-up 3** (15, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of follow-up you would like to receive (Email, Phone, or None).
