# Yurt Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register guests to a yurt for an event. The event organizers use this form to gather information about the guests, such as their name, arrival date and time, yurt preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Guest Name** in the text field provided.
2. Select the date of your arrival from the calendar picker.
3. Choose your arrival time from the time picker.
4. Select the yurt(s) you are interested in staying in (you can select multiple yurts).
5. Choose the type of room you prefer (text field).
6. Enter your phone number (text field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Arrival Date**: Select the date of your planned arrival for the event.
* **Arrival Time**: Choose your planned arrival time for the event.
* **Yurt Selection**: Select the yurt(s) you are interested in staying in (you can select multiple yurts).
* **Room Type**: Choose the type of room you prefer (text field).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth event check-in.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please contact the event organizers directly.
* If you need to cancel or modify your reservation, please contact the event organizers as soon as possible.
