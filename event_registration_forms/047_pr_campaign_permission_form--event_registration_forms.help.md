# pr_campaign_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and obtain consent for a public relations campaign. It is meant to be filled out by individuals participating in or associated with the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your participant email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. If applicable, enter your Public Relations Campagne ID in the "Public Relations Campagne ID" field.
4. Select the date and time you gave consent for the campaign in the "Consent Date" and "Consent Time" fields.
5. If necessary, add any additional notes about your consent in the "Consent Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed in public communications related to the campaign.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can reach you for updates and information about the campaign.
* **Public Relations Campagne ID** (`public_relations_campagne_id`, text, not required): If you have a Public Relations Campagne ID, enter it here. This is not typically required.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, not required): Select the date you gave consent to participate in the campaign.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, not required): Select the time you gave consent to participate in the campaign.
* **Consent Note** (`consent_note`, note, not required): Add any additional notes about your consent here, if necessary.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant party for assistance.
* If you're filling out this form for someone else, please ensure you have their permission to do so.
