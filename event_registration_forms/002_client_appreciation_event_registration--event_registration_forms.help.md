# Client Appreciation Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register attendees for the Client Appreciation Event, a special occasion to show appreciation to our valued clients. This form collects information necessary to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your personal information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* Select your preferred event date and time.
* Indicate if you will be bringing a guest and provide their name if applicable.
* Specify any dietary restrictions or special requests you may have.
* Let us know if you need transportation assistance.
* Choose your preferred sessions and activities for the event.
* Select a T-Shirt size for your event swag bag.
* Tell us how you heard about the event.
* Make any special requests or comments.
* Finally, review and agree to the event guidelines and policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`attendee_first_name`, text, required): This is your given name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (2)** (`attendee_last_name`, text, required): This is the surname as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (3)** (`attendee_email`, email, required): This is where we will send you event details and communications.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`attendee_phone`, text, required): This is your contact number for event day communications.
* **Company Name (5)** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are bringing a guest, please specify their workplace.
* **Job Title (6)** (`job_title`, text, optional): Your current position.
* **Event Date Preference (7)** (`event_date_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred date for the event.
* **Will You Bring a Guest? (8)** (`plus_one_attending`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are bringing a guest and provide their name if applicable.
* **Guest Full Name (9)** (`guest_name`, text, optional): If you are bringing a guest, please provide their full name.
* **Dietary Restrictions (10)** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Specify any food allergies or preferences.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance? (11)** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required): Let us know if you need shuttle service or other transportation assistance.
* **Accessibility Requirements (12)** (`accessibility_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special needs or requirements, please specify.
* **Sessions You Are Most Interested In (13)** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the sessions and activities that interest you.
* **T-Shirt Size (14)** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Choose a T-Shirt size for your swag bag.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event? (15)** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Please tell us how you heard about the event.
* **Special Requests or Comments (16)** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any other special requests or comments, please let us know.
* **I Agree to Follow All Event Guidelines and Policies (17)** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Please review and agree to the event guidelines and policies.
* **Registration Date (18)** (`registration_date`, date, required): The date you are registering for the event.
