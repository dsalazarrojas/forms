# Fishing Derby Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a fishing derby tournament. It collects demographic information, fishing details, and other relevant data from anglers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the demographic information, including your first and last name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Provide your date of birth to confirm your age.
3. Select the species of fish you caught, as this information will be used for classification purposes.
4. Enter the length and weight of the fish you caught.
5. Check whether you have a valid angler's license and if you are qualified to enter the tournament.
6. If applicable, provide any special requests or comments you may have.
7. Select the date, time, and location of the event.
8. Indicate the type of fishing rod used.
9. Choose the type of bait used.
10. Select the tackle used during the fishing session.
11. Specify how the fish was caught (with your hand, net, or other).
12. If you caught multiple fish, enter the number of fish caught.
13. Report the number of fishing hours spent.
14. Indicate the number of people in your party.
15. Check if you are reserving a spot for a minor.
16. If reserving for a minor, select the minor's age category.
17. Confirm your entry by selecting 'True'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your first name, which will be used to identify you in the tournament.
* **Last Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your last name, which will be used to identify you in the tournament.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and verification purposes.
* **Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for contact purposes if needed.
* **Date of Birth (Date, Required)**: Enter your date of birth to confirm your age.
* **Species of Fish (Select Multiple, Required)**: Choose the species of fish you caught.
* **Fish Length in Inches (Number, Optional)**: Enter the length of the fish you caught.
* **Weight of Fish in Pounds (Number, Required)**: Enter the weight of the fish you caught.
* **Angler License Number (Select One, Required)**: Check whether you have a valid angler's license.
* **Is Qualified to Enter (Select One, Required)**: Confirm whether you are qualified to enter the tournament.
* **Special Requests (Text, Optional)**: If you have any special requests or comments, enter them here.
* **Comments (Text, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments or information you may have.
* **Event Date (Date, Required)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time (Time, Required)**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location (Text, Required)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Fishing Rod (Select Multiple, Required)**: Choose the type of fishing rod used.
* **Bait Used (Select One, Required)**: Choose the type of bait used.
* **Tackle Used (Select Multiple, Required)**: Choose the type of tackle used.
* **Fish Caught with (Select One, Required)**: Specify how the fish was caught.
* **Number of Fish Caught (Number, Optional)**: Enter the number of fish caught.
* **Number of Fishing Hours Spent (Number, Optional)**: Enter the number of fishing hours spent.
* **Number of People in Party (Number, Optional)**: Enter the number of people in your party.
* **Is Reserving for Minor (Select One, Required)**: Check whether you are reserving a spot for a minor.
* **Is Reserving for Minor Age (Select One, Required)**: If reserving for a minor, select the minor's age category.
* **Confirm (Select One, Optional)**: Confirm your entry by selecting 'True'.

Please note that all fields marked 'Required' are mandatory and must be completed to ensure a valid entry. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the tournament organizers for clarification.
