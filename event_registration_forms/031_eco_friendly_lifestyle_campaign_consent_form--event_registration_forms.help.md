# Eco Friendly Lifestyle Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in participating in an eco-friendly lifestyle campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the event registration option that best describes why you are interested in participating in the campaign.
2. Enter your name and any other required information.
3. Provide your email address to help us contact you.
4. Enter your phone number for further contact.
5. Enter your address for campaign-related purposes.
6. Enter your date of birth (DOB) to verify your age and eligibility.
7. Select your gender to help us tailor the campaign to your needs.
8. Choose your area of interest within the campaign by selecting one or more options.
9. Indicate your consent to participate in the campaign.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration** (Event Registration, select_one): Choose one of the options to describe why you are interested in participating in the campaign.
* **participant_name** (participant_name, text, optional): Enter your name. This will help us contact you if we need further information.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about the campaign.
* **Participant Phone** (Participant Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your address for campaign-related purposes.
* **Participant DOB** (Participant DOB, date, optional): Enter your date of birth to verify your age and eligibility.
* **Participant Gender** (Participant Gender, text, optional): Select your gender to help us tailor the campaign to your needs.
* **Participant Interest** (Participant Interest, select_one, optional): Choose one or more of the options that best describe your interest in the campaign.
* **Consent** (Consent, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your consent to participate in the campaign by selecting one or more options. Note that consent is not required for all fields.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the campaign.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and truthful information to ensure you are eligible for the campaign.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will use the information collected from this form to tailor the campaign to your needs and interests.
