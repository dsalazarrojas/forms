### Brand Ambassador Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The Brand Ambassador Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers who wish to represent the brand. This form helps us understand your contact details, availability, and relevant skills to ensure a proper fit for the volunteer role.

#### How To Complete This Form

1.  Please complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Use the provided hints as a guide to fill in the correct information.
3.  Ensure that you check the boxes accordingly for each field.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
*   **State and ZIP Code**: Enter your state and postal code.
*   **What Interests You About This Volunteer Role**: Describe why you want to volunteer and how you can contribute to the brand.
*   **Previous Volunteer Experience**: Select the level of your volunteer experience from Extensive, Some, Limited, or First time volunteering.
*   **Hours Available to Volunteer Per Week**: Enter the number of hours you can commit to volunteering per week.
*   **Preferred Start Date**: Enter the date when you can begin volunteering.
*   **Length of Commitment**: Select the length of your commitment from 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, Open-ended, or Flexible.
*   **Relevant Skills and Strengths**: Describe your relevant skills and strengths that can help you excel in the volunteer role.
*   **Language Skills**: List the languages you are proficient in.
*   **I Commit to Representing the Brand Professionally**: Check to confirm your commitment to representing the brand.
*   **I Submit This Volunteer Application**: Check to confirm your submission of the application.

#### Tips

*   Please ensure that you answer all the questions accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the application.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us.
