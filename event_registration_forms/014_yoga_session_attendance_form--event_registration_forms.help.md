# yoga_session_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "yoga_session_attendance_form" is a form designed to collect user information for attendance at yoga sessions. This form will help us understand who attended, when they attended, and how they would like to be contacted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **user_input** (name) in the text field at the top of the form.
2. Select the **date** you attended the session using the date picker.
3. Choose the **time** of the session from the available time slots.
4. Enter your **email** and **phone** information to ensure we can contact you about future sessions.
5. If you have any specific **note** about your attendance, you can provide it in the text field.
6. Let us know if you attended one or more **classes** by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input** (text field): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **select_date** (date field): This field is for choosing the date you attended the yoga session.
* **select_time** (time field): Choose the time you attended the session from the available options.
* **select_email** (email field): Enter your valid email address.
* **select_phone** (phone field): Enter your valid phone number.
* **select_note** (note field): Provide any additional information about your attendance or preferences.
* **select_one_class** (select_one field): Select "Yes" to indicate you attended this class, or "No" otherwise.
* **select_multiple_classes** (select_multiple field): Check "Yes" if you attended multiple classes.

## Tips

* Be accurate with your contact information so we can reach out to you efficiently.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask our team for clarification.
