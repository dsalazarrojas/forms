# outdoor_movie_night_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for an outdoor movie night event. It will help the organizers manage the event efficiently and ensure that everyone has a great experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter the event date you plan to attend.
3. Enter the start and end times for the event.
4. Provide your first name, last name, and contact email.
5. Enter your contact phone number.
6. Indicate the number of guests you plan to bring.
7. Select your event preferences.
8. If you have any special requests, please provide them in the space provided.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
10. Confirm your email address to ensure we can contact you about the event.
11. Confirm your phone number in case we need to reach you about the event.
12. Confirm your name to ensure we have accurate information.
13. Confirm the number of guests you plan to bring.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date of the event you plan to attend.
* **start_time** (`start_time`, `text`, required: false): Enter the start time for the event.
* **end_time** (`end_time`, `text`, required: false): Enter the end time for the event.
* **first_name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the event.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you about the event.
* **number_of_guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the number of guests you plan to bring.
* **preferences** (`preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select your event preferences.
* **special_requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): If you have any special requests, please enter them here.
* **notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
* **confirm_email** (`confirm_email`, `email`, required: false): Confirm your email address to ensure we can contact you about the event.
* **confirm_phone** (`confirm_phone`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your phone number in case we need to reach you about the event.
* **confirm_name** (`confirm_name`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your name to ensure we have accurate information.
* **confirm_number_of_guests** (`confirm_number_of_guests`, `number`, required: false): Confirm the number of guests you plan to bring.
