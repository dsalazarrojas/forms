# Honeymoon Travel Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Honeymoon Travel Information Form is designed to gather essential details about your upcoming honeymoon trip. This information will help travel agents or planners tailor the perfect experience for you and your partner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the destination(s) you are planning to visit.
2. Enter the dates of your trip (in date format).
3. Indicate the duration of your stay.
4. Provide an estimate of your travel budget.
5. Choose how you want to allocate your budget (e.g., Wedding, Anniversary, Romantic).
6. Select the type of travel experience you are seeking (e.g., Adventure, Relaxation, Food and Drink).
7. Enter the number of people in your travel group.
8. Select the location type that best fits your preferences (Beach, Mountains, City).
9. Choose the style of travel that suits you best (Nature, Culture, Adventure).
10. If you have any special requests or requirements, please describe them in this field.
11. Finally, provide your contact phone number and email address so we can reach out to you with further information or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Destination**: Enter the place or places you are planning to visit during your trip. This can be a single location or multiple destinations you wish to visit.
* **Travel Date**: Please enter the start and end dates of your trip, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Travel Duration**: Indicate the number of days you plan to stay at the destination(s).
* **Travel Budget**: Provide an estimate of your budget for the trip. This will help us understand your expectations for expenses.
* **Travel Budget Breakdown**: Choose how you want to allocate your budget, e.g., for a Wedding anniversary or for a Romantic getaway.
* **Travel Type**: Select the type of experience you are looking for, such as Adventure, Relaxation, or Food and Drink.
* **Travel Group Size**: Enter the number of people in your travel group.
* **Travel Location**: Choose the type of location that best fits your preferences, e.g., Beach, Mountains, or City.
* **Travel Style**: Select the style of travel that suits you best, e.g., Nature, Culture, or Adventure.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any specific needs or requirements for your trip, please describe them in this field.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you with further information or questions.
* **Contact Email**: Provide your email address for communication purposes.

By following these steps and filling out the form accurately, you will provide essential information for your travel agent or planner to craft a tailored experience for your honeymoon.
