# Book Weekend Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information from attendees about their preferences, interests, and needs for the Book Weekend event. This helps us tailor the event to better meet the needs and expectations of all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Choose the dates you plan to attend during the Book Weekend.
3. Indicate if this will be your first time attending the event.
4. Select the number of people accompanying you, if any.
5. Choose the reasons for your attendance, such as author meet-and-greets or book discussions.
6. Select your favorite book genres.
7. If applicable, list specific authors you'd like to meet.
8. Let us know if you're interested in attending author workshops.
9. Indicate any accessibility needs you may have.
10. Tell us how you heard about the Book Weekend event.
11. Provide any suggestions or feedback for future events.
12. Decide whether you'd like to be added to our mailing list for updates on future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Required)
	+ This is the name you'd like to be known by during the event.
* **Email Address** (Required)
	+ This is the email address where we'll send event updates and announcements.
* **Phone Number** (Optional)
	+ If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your contact number. This is not required.
* **Which Book Weekend dates will you attend**
	+ Choose the dates you plan to attend by selecting all that apply.
* **Is this your first Book Weekend?** (Required)
	+ Indicate yes if you're attending for the first time.
* **How many people will be attending with you**
	+ Select the number of companions, including yourself.
* **Who will be attending with you**
	+ Choose all that apply from the options provided.
* **What interests you most about Book Weekend**
	+ Select the reason that best describes why you're attending.
* **Your favorite book genres**
	+ Select all that apply to indicate your favorite book types.
* **Are there specific authors you want to meet?**
	+ If applicable, list the names of authors you'd like to meet.
* **Interested in attending workshops?**
	+ Indicate yes if you're interested in attending author workshops.
* **Do you have any accessibility needs?**
	+ If you have any needs we can accommodate, please let us know.
* **How did you hear about this event?**
	+ Select the source where you learned about Book Weekend.
* **What could we improve for next year?**
	+ Share your suggestions and feedback for future events.
* **Add me to the mailing list?**
	+ Decide whether you'd like to receive updates about future events.
