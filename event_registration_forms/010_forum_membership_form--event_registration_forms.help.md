# Forum Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Forum Membership Form is designed to collect information from users who want to join the forum or register for an event. The form will help you to create a profile and provide details about yourself and your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Details, Name, and Email in the first section.
2. Add your Phone number and any additional Notes or Date and Time (if applicable) in subsequent sections.
3. Choose your options for Select Options (up to three selections are allowed).
4. Confirm your selections by checking the boxes for Select Options that require a "Yes" or "No" answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (id: 1):
	+ A text field for entering your basic user information.
* **Name** (id: 2):
	+ Enter your name as it should be displayed on the forum.
* **Email** (id: 3):
	+ Enter a valid email address associated with your forum account.
* **Phone** (id: 4):
	+ A text field for entering your phone number.
* **Note** (id: 5):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Date** (id: 6):
	+ Enter the date of your event or registration.
* **Time** (id: 7):
	+ Enter the time of your event or registration.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 8):
	+ Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Select Option 1** (id: 9):
	+ Choose one option from the available list.
* **Select Option 2** (id: 10):
	+ Choose multiple options from the available list.
* **Select Option 3** (id: 11):
	+ Choose one option from the available list, required for event registration.
* **Event Date** (id: 12):
	+ Enter the date of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Time** (id: 13):
	+ Enter the time of the event.
* **Required Field 1** (id: 14):
	+ A text field for a required piece of information.
* **Required Field 2** (id: 15):
	+ Another text field for a required piece of information.
* **Select Option 1 Required** (id: 16):
	+ Choose "Yes" or "No" for this required option.
* **Select Option 2 Required** (id: 17):
	+ Choose multiple "Yes" or "No" answers.
* **Select Option 3 Required** (id: 18):
	+ Choose "Yes" or "No" for this required option.
* **Date and Time** (id: 19):
	+ Enter a date and time for a specific event or activity.
* **Date and Time Required** (id: 20):
	+ Enter a date and time for a required event or activity.
* **Select Option 1 Required Date** (id: 21):
	+ Choose "Yes" or "No" for this required date selection.
* **Select Option 2 Required Date** (id: 22):
	+ Choose multiple "Yes" or "No" answers for this required date selection.
* **Select Option 3 Required Date** (id: 23):
	+ Choose "Yes" or "No" for this required date selection.
* **Note Required** (id: 24):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Note 2** (id: 25):
	+ Another text field for additional notes or comments.
