# Holiday Cruise Reservation Form - Help Guide

This form is designed to collect essential information from travelers about their holiday cruise plans. The goal is to ensure accurate and complete data entry for the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the required fields**: The form contains fields for traveler, event, contact, and payment information. Please fill in all required fields to ensure successful submission.
2. **Double-check your answers**: Review your entries carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send your information to the event organizers.

* **Traveler Info** (`traveler_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter any additional information about yourself as a traveler, such as your name, nickname, or any other relevant details.

### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Here, please provide any specific event details you'd like to share with the organizers, such as your event preferences or interests.
