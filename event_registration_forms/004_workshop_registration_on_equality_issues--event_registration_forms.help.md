# Workshop Registration On Equality Issues - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Workshop Registration On Equality Issues" form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending a workshop on equality issues. This form is meant to be completed by workshop participants to provide details about their session choice, accessibility needs, and personal details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, follow the instructions below for each section.
2. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Choice (page 1)**: Select one of the available choices for the workshop session you would like to attend. This will help us understand your preferences and plan the workshop accordingly.
	+ If you are unsure about your session choice, please contact us and we will be happy to assist.
* **Accessibility Needs (page 2)**: If you have any accessibility needs or requirements, please provide details in this field. This will help us to make necessary arrangements to ensure your participation in the workshop.
	+ Please provide clear and concise information about your accessibility needs, so we can make the necessary arrangements.
* **Participant Details (page 3)**: Provide your participant details, including your name, email, and any other relevant information. This will help us to keep you updated about the workshop and its schedule.
	+ Please ensure that your participant details are accurate and up-to-date, as this will help us to communicate with you effectively.

## Tips

* Please take a few minutes to read through each field carefully and fill in the required information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact us and we will be happy to assist.
* Please submit the form only when you have completed all the required fields.
