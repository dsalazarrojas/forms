# Scholarship Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Check In Form is designed to collect check-in information from scholarship program participants. This form is meant to gather essential details for the scholarship program administrators to efficiently manage and track the check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, or as required by your program administrators.
2. Answer all fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Ensure to provide any additional comments or information you think relevant to your check-in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In** (`page_1` (Note), required: false): Please enter any notes or comments about your check-in (optional).
* **Check In Information** (`check_in_info` (Text), required: false): Provide any specific details about your check-in, such as arrival time or location (optional).
* **Scholarship Program** (`scholarship_program` (Select One), required: false): Choose between 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm that you are participating in a scholarship program (optional).
* **Check In Details** (`check_in_details` (Text), required: false): Enter additional details about your check-in, if necessary (optional).
* **Organization** (`organization` (Text), required: false): Enter the name of the organization you are representing, if applicable (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (`notes` (Text), required: false): Provide any additional comments or information about your check-in (optional).

## Tips
- Please double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you have any doubts or questions about the form or its purpose, contact your program administrators.
- This form will help the program administrators to process your check-in efficiently and ensure that you receive the necessary information and resources.
