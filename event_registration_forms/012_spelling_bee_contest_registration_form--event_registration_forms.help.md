# Spelling Bee Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spelling Bee Contest Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from participants and their emergency contacts for the Spelling Bee Contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Participant Info** section with your name and participant number.
2. Enter your **School Info** details, including school name, address, phone number, and email.
3. Provide your **Contact Info** and **Emergency Contact** details.
4. Enter your **Emergency Info** details, including the name and contact information of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
5. Add any **Additional Info** you would like to share with us.
6. If you would like to register multiple participants, repeat the process for each participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info**: Enter your name and participant number. If you are registering multiple participants, enter each name and participant number on a new line.
* **School Info**: Enter your school's name, address, phone number, and email. This will help us know where to send event communications and contact you if necessary.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact name and information. This will be used to reach you in case of emergency or to communicate with you about the event.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Info**: Enter the emergency contact's name and contact information.
* **Participant Info** (again): This field is intended for additional participant information.
* **Emergency Participant**: Enter the participant's name and participant number, if applicable.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used to send you event communications and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you about the event.
* **Address**: Enter your address. This will help us send you event communications and packages.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code. This will help us ensure that you receive event communications and packages.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **School Name**: Enter your school's name.
* **School Address**: Enter your school's address.
* **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Email**: Enter your school's email.
* **School Website**: Enter your school's website.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or information about your participation in the event.
* **Note**: This field is intended to capture any further additional comments or notes about your registration.
