# Charity Event Planner Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your experience and services as an event planner, especially for charity events. This will help us assess your qualifications and fit for future event planning projects.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each section carefully and thoroughly.
- Answer all required fields (marked as 'required' in the form).
- Use the options provided in the drop-down menus to select relevant answers.
- Attach your portfolio link and references (if available) for further review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Planner Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Provide your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (4, text, required): Enter your business or company name.
* **Years of Experience** (5, select_one, required): Choose the number of years you have experience in event planning, from Less than 1 year to More than 10 years.
* **Number of Charity Events Planned** (6, number, required): Estimate the approximate number of charity events you have planned.
* **Types of Events You Plan** (7, select_multiple, required): Select all types of events you plan, from Galas, Auctions, Charity runs, etc.
* **Budget Range Managed** (8, select_one, required): Choose the typical budget size you have managed for events, from Under $10000 to Over $500000.
* **Services Offered** (9, select_multiple, required): Select all services you provide as an event planner, from Full event planning to Marketing support.
* **Vendor Network** (10, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have an established vendor network, from Yes extensive to Building network.
* **Availability** (11, select_multiple, required): Select when you are available to work, from Weekdays, Weekends, Evenings, or Flexible.
* **Pricing Structure** (12, select_one, required): Choose how you charge for your services, from Flat fee to Custom quote.
* **Portfolio Website** (13, text, optional): Provide your portfolio link, if you have one.
* **References Available** (14, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have references from past clients, from True to False.
* **Describe Your Planning Approach** (15, text, required): Describe your event planning approach and methodology in a few paragraphs.
* **Describe a Challenge You Overcame** (16, text, required): Share a challenge you overcame as an event planner and how you solved it.
* **Why Should We Choose You** (17, text, required): Explain why you are a unique and qualified event planner.
* **Additional Comments** (18, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information you would like to share.

Note: This guide is for internal use only, please do not share it with external parties.
