# Conflict Resolution Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registration for the Conflict Resolution Workshop, an event aimed at providing training and education on conflict resolution skills. It is meant to gather necessary information from participants to facilitate their registration and participation in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your first name, last name, and email address to identify yourself as a participant.
2.  Provide your job title, organization, and industry to describe your professional context.
3.  Choose your preferred workshop date and format (in-person, virtual live, or hybrid).
4.  Select your current experience level with conflict resolution.
5.  Describe your role in conflict situations and the types of conflicts you typically face.
6.  Specify your learning goals and any specific conflict situations you would like to address.
7.  If applicable, provide information on your team size, dietary requirements, accessibility needs, and payment type (company or self-funded).
8.  Confirm your terms and conditions acceptance, cancellation policy understanding, and registration status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Job Title**: Describe your current position or title within your organization.
* **Organization**: Enter your current or relevant organization name.
* **Industry**: Select your current industry sector.
* **Workshop Date**: Choose your preferred workshop date from the provided options.
* **Workshop Format**: Select how you plan to attend the workshop (in-person, virtual live, or hybrid).
* **Conflict Resolution Experience**: Select your current skill level with conflict resolution.
* **Role in Conflicts**: Describe your typical role in conflict situations.
* **Types of Conflicts**: Select all types of conflicts you typically face.
* **Learning Goals**: Specify what you hope to achieve from the workshop.
* **Specific Challenges**: Describe any specific conflict situations you'd like to address (optional).
* **Team Size**: If registering a group, select the number of attendees in your group.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary requirements you have for in-person catering.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Describe any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Payment Type**: Select how you plan to pay for the workshop (self-funded, company-funded, or scholarship).
* **Company Name for Billing**: Enter your company name if it's paying for you.
* **Billing Contact Name**: Enter the name to appear on invoices.
* **Billing Email**: Enter your billing email address.
* **Are You Registering a Group**: If registering a group, select 'True'.
* **Group Size**: If registering a group, enter the number of attendees.
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted**: Confirm your agreement to workshop terms and conditions.
* **Cancellation Policy Understood**: Confirm your understanding of cancellation terms.
* **How Did You Hear About This Workshop**: Select where you heard about the workshop.
* **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments or questions.

Note: Please review and ensure the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
