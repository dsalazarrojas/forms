# Intercultural Dialogue Series Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Intercultural Dialogue Series Registration Form is designed to gather information from participants who wish to attend or participate in events hosted by the Intercultural Dialogue Series. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form carefully**: Ensure that you have filled out the correct event details before submitting the form.
2. **Fill out all required fields**: Some fields are marked as required. Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful registration.
3. **Select relevant options**: Choose the options that best suit your preferences for the event.
4. **Double-check your submission**: Review your form before submitting it to ensure that everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (Main Page): This is the first page of the form. Please fill out this page carefully and completely.
* **Registration Information** (Select One: Option 1, Option 2): Choose whether you would like to participate as a speaker, presenter, or participant.
* **Additional Information** (Text): If you have any additional information to share with us, please enter it here.
* **Event Details** (Select Multiple: Option 1, Option 2): Choose the event details that apply to you.
* **Contact Information** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you with event updates and information.
* **Event Details (date)** (Date): Select the date you plan to attend the event.
* **Event Details (time)** (Time): Select the time you plan to attend the event.
* **Event Location** (Text): Enter the location of the event.
* **Contact Information** (Text): Enter a contact name and/or phone number so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Double-check your form submission before sending it to us.
* If you need help or assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
