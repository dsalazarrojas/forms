# Stage Performance Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps event organizers track attendance and performance details for stage events. It collects information about the event, performance, and attendee's status (attendance and submitted date/time).

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the event you are attending by choosing from the available options in the "event" field.
2. Choose the performances you are interested in by selecting from the options in the "performance" field (you can select multiple performances).
3. Indicate whether you will be attending the event by selecting "True" or "False" in the "attendance" field.
4. Provide any additional comments about your attendance in the "comments" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Enter the date and time of submission in the "date" and "time" fields.
8. Enter the event ID, performance ID, and other relevant IDs as required.
9. Finally, select the "status" of your form submission as "Active" or "Inactive".

* **User** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Event** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the event you are attending.
* **Performance** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the performances you are interested in attending.
* **Attendance** (`4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether you will be attending the event.
* **Comments** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments about your attendance.
* **Notes** (`6`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any relevant notes about your attendance.
* **Email** (`7`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`9`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time** (`10`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Event ID** (`11`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the event ID.
* **Performance ID** (`12`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the performance ID.
* **Attendance ID** (`13`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the attendance ID.
* **Comments ID** (`14`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the comments ID.
* **Notes ID** (`15`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the notes ID.
* **Email ID** (`16`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the email ID.
* **Phone ID** (`17`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the phone ID.
* **Date ID** (`18`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the date ID.
* **Time ID** (`19`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the time ID.
* **Form Submission ID** (`20`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the form submission ID.
* **Submitted By** (`21`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the submitted by user ID.
* **Updated At** (`22`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the updated at timestamp.
* **Created At** (`23`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the created at timestamp.
* **Submitted At** (`24`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the submitted at timestamp.
* **Status** (`25`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of your form submission.
