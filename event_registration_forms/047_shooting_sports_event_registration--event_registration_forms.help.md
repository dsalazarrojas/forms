# Shooting Sports Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in a shooting sports event. It collects basic contact information and participant preferences to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. For the `Discipline Choices` field, select all the disciplines you are interested in participating in (e.g., rifle, pistol, shotgun, archery).
3. Read and acknowledge the `Safety Acknowledgments` section, understanding your responsibilities for a safe and enjoyable event experience.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
5. Click the `Submit` button to finalize your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it is written on your identification.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for event updates and notifications.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during the event.
* **Discipline Choices**: Select all the disciplines you are interested in participating in (e.g., rifle, pistol, shotgun, archery).
* **Safety Acknowledgments**: Read and acknowledge that you understand and will follow all safety guidelines and instructions provided by event organizers.
* **Submit**: Double-check your information and click this button to finalize your registration.
