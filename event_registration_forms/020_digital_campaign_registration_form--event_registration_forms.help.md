# Digital Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Campaign Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants or attendees for event management. This form helps track and manage event details, including participant information, event dates and times, and budget allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" format (e.g., [user@example.com](mailto:user@example.com)).
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the format used for your phone (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6. If you have participated in this event before, please enter your **Date of Campaign** in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
7. Provide the **Event Name** of the event you are registering for.
8. Enter your **Address** where you will be attending the event.
9. Enter the **City** where the event is taking place.
10. Enter the **Post Code** for the event location.
11. Choose your **Country** of residence.
12. Choose the **Event Type** from the dropdown options.
13. Select your **Participant Status** from the dropdown options.
14. Select your **Participant Category** from the dropdown options.
15. If necessary, provide any **Notes** about your participation.
16. If you are participating in multiple **Participant Status 2**, select all that apply.
17. Provide any **Event Details** about the event.
18. Enter the **Start Date** of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
19. Enter the **End Date** of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
20. Enter the **Event Duration** in the format HH:MM (e.g., 01:30 for one hour and 30 minutes).
21. Enter the **Start Time** of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
22. Enter the **End Time** of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 15:00).
23. Choose the **Event Status** from the dropdown options.
24. Enter the **Campaign Budget** in numeric format.
25. Finally, choose the **Assigned User** who will be handling your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license).
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and event updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format used for your phone (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Date of Campaign**: If you have participated in this event before, enter the date you registered for the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Address**: Enter the address where you will be attending the event.
* **City**: Enter the city where the event is taking place.
* **Post Code**: Enter the post code for the event location.
* **Country**: Choose your country of residence from the dropdown options.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you are registering for from the dropdown options.
* **Participant Status**: Select the status that applies to your participation (e.g., attending, cancelled, etc.).
* **Participant Category**: Select the category that applies to your participation (e.g., team, individual, etc.).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your participation, if necessary.
* **Participant Status 2**: Select all participant status that apply to your participation.
* **Event Details**: Provide any additional information about the event.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event in the format HH:MM (e.g., 01:30 for one hour and 30 minutes).
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 15:00).
* **Event Status**: Choose the status that applies to the event.
* **Campaign Budget**: Enter the allocated budget for the campaign.
* **Assigned User**: Choose the user who will be handling your participation.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your information before submission.
- If you are participating in an event for the first time, leave "Date of Campaign" field empty.
- If you are having trouble with the form, contact the assigned user for assistance.
