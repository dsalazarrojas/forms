# Practical Guidance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Practical Guidance Registration Form is designed to collect information about events and participants. It is intended to gather essential details to ensure smooth event execution and participant management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Event Information" page, providing a brief description of the event.
2. Move to the "Participant Information" page and enter the participant's details.
3. Select the event date and time on the "Event Date" and "Event Time" pages, respectively.
4. Enter the participant's email and phone number on the "Participant Email" and "Participant Phone" pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Information (<event_details>**, text, required): Please describe the event you are registering for.
- **Participant Information (<participant_details>**, text, required): Please provide details about the participant, such as name and any other relevant information.
- **Event Date (<event_date>**, date, required): Select the date of the event.
- **Event Time (<event_time>**, time, required): Select the time of the event.
- **Participant Email (<participant_email>**, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
- **Participant Phone (<participant_phone>**, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
