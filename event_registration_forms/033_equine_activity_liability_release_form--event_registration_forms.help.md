# Equine Activity Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equine Activity Liability Release Form is a document that requires participants to release liability and assume the risks associated with equine activities. It's a necessary step before engaging in any equine activity, such as horse riding, horse riding lessons, or horse-related events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address in the "Participant Name" and "Participant Email" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your guardian's name and relationship to you in the "Guardian Name" and "Relationship to Participant" fields, if applicable.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Participant Date of Birth" field.
4. Answer the question "Has Participant ever been injured in an equine activity?" in the "Has Participant ever been injured in an equine activity?" field.
5. If you've been injured in an equine activity, provide medical treatment information in the "Medical Treatment for Participant's Injuries" field.
6. Sign the "Witness Signature" field after the form has been completed and reviewed.
7. Sign the "Participant Signature" field to confirm you've read and understood the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: This is the field where you'll enter your name.
* **Participant Email**: This is the field where you'll enter your email address.
* **Guardian Name**: This is the field where you'll enter your guardian's name if they are responsible for your care.
* **Relationship to Participant**: This is the field where you'll describe your relationship to the participant (e.g., parent, guardian, or custodian).
* **Participant Date of Birth**: This is the field where you'll enter your date of birth.
* **Witness Name**: This is the field where you'll enter the name of a witness who can vouch for your participation in the equine activity.
* **Witness Email**: This is the field where you'll enter the witness's email address.
* **Witness Phone**: This is the field where you'll enter the witness's phone number.
* **Witness Address**: This is the field where you'll enter the witness's address.
* **Participant's Equine Experience**: This is the field where you'll describe your experience with equines (e.g., riding experience, horse care experience).
* **Participant's Equine Awareness**: This is the field where you'll describe your awareness of equine behavior and safety precautions.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the field where you'll enter the name of someone who can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This is the field where you'll enter the contact phone number.
* **Facility Name**: This is the field where you'll enter the name of the equine facility you're visiting or participating with.
* **Facility Address**: This is the field where you'll enter the address of the equine facility.
* **Facility Phone**: This is the field where you'll enter the phone number of the equine facility.
* **Facility Email**: This is the field where you'll enter the email address of the equine facility.
* **Participant's Medical Conditions**: This is the field where you'll describe any medical conditions that may impact your participation in equine activities.
* **Participant's Medical Procedure**: This is the field where you'll describe any medical procedures or treatments you've received.
* **Participant's Medical History**: This is the field where you'll describe your medical history.
* **Has Participant ever been injured in an equine activity?**: This is the field where you'll answer whether you've ever been injured in an equine activity.
* **Medical Treatment for Participant's Injuries**: This is the field where you'll describe any medical treatment you've received for your injuries.
* **Participant Signature**: This is the field where you'll sign to confirm you've read and understood the form.
* **Witness Signature**: This is the field where a witness signs to confirm they've reviewed and understood the form.
* **Equine Facility Agreement**: This is the field where you'll agree to the terms and conditions of the equine facility.
