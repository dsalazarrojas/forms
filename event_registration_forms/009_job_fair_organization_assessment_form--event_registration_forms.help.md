# Job Fair Organization Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Fair Organization Assessment Form is a tool used to collect information and insights from individuals within an organization about its events. This form will help event organizers assess the organization's readiness and preparedness for a job fair and help identify areas that require improvement. By providing detailed and accurate information, you will help the event team better understand the organization's dynamics and create a more effective and engaging experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Organization, Department, Job Title, and Contact Number.
2.  Enter your Contact Email as it will be used for further communication.
3.  Provide information on the Job Description, Job Type, Job Category, Job Level, Start Date, and End Date of the position you are assessing.
4.  Indicate your availability in hours per week.
5.  Choose the Job Status as Active, Inactive, or On Leave.
6.  Enter the Location and Description of your work environment.
7.  Enter the Requirements of the position you are assessing.
8.  Optionally, you can add Job Requirements, if any.
9.  Finally, add any additional Notes you feel are relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents. This will help us identify you correctly.
*   **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents. This will help us identify you correctly.
*   **Organization** (Required): Enter your organization's name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Department** (Required): Enter the department within your organization you belong to.
*   **Job Title** (Required): Enter your official job title within your organization.
*   **Contact Number** (Required): Enter your contact number that we can use for further communication.
*   **Contact Email** (Required): Enter your organizational email that we can use for further communication.
*   **Job Description** (Optional): Provide a detailed description of your job and responsibilities.
*   **Job Type** (Required): Select all job types that apply to your position (Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Product Manager).
*   **Job Category** (Required): Choose the category your job falls under (IT, HR, Marketing).
*   **Job Level** (Required): Indicate your job level within your organization.
*   **Start Date** (Required): Enter the date your employment started.
*   **End Date** (Required): Enter the date you expect to end your employment.
*   **Hours Per Week** (Required): Indicate how many hours you work per week.
*   **Job Status** (Required): Select one status that applies to your position (Active, Inactive, On Leave).
*   **Location** (Required): Enter the location of your workplace.
*   **Description** (Required): Provide a detailed description of your work environment.
*   **Requirements** (Required): Enter any requirements or qualifications necessary for the job.
*   **Job Requirements** (Optional): Enter any specific requirements or qualifications for the position.
*   **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional information that may be useful.

By completing this form, you will be contributing to the improvement of our event and ensuring a better experience for all attendees. Thank you for your participation!
