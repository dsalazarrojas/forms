# Lighting Crew Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lighting Crew Registration form is used to collect and track lighting crew members' information, including their first and last name, address, contact details, experience level, equipment owned, roles, availability, and other relevant details. This form will help us assign crew members to specific tasks and events based on their capabilities and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field with your correct and up-to-date information.
- Review each field to ensure you're providing accurate and complete details.
- Make sure to select multiple options if you have experience with multiple roles or own multiple types of equipment.
- Check the availability section to indicate your available dates for work.
- Use the notes field for any additional information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Your first name.
### Last name
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Your last name.
### Address
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Your physical address or contact address.
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Your email address for contact purposes.
### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Your phone number for contact purposes.
### Experience Level
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `text`, optional): Your level of experience with lighting equipment and crew management.
### Equipment Owned
* **Equipment Owned** (`equipment_owned`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of equipment you own or have experience with, such as LED, Stage, and Backstage.
### Roles
* **Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, required): List your experience with specific roles, such as Lighting Designer, Lighting Engineer, and Electrician.
### Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, optional): Indicate your available dates for work and events.
### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Any additional information or comments about your experience or availability.
### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Any tools or equipment assigned to you for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned User
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, optional): The user you're assigned to for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Project
* **Assigned Project** (`assigned_project`, `text`, optional): The project you're assigned to for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Client
* **Assigned Client** (`assigned_client`, `text`, optional): The client you're assigned to for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Event
* **Assigned Event** (`assigned_event`, `text`, optional): The event you're assigned to for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Job Title
* **Assigned Job Title** (`assigned_job_title`, `text`, optional): Your job title for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Department
* **Assigned Department** (`assigned_department`, `text`, optional): The department you're assigned to for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Date
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, `date`, optional): The date assigned to you for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Time
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, `time`, optional): The time assigned to you for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Location
* **Assigned Location** (`assigned_location`, `text`, optional): The location assigned to you for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Status
* **Assigned Status** (`assigned_status`, `text`, optional): The status of your assignment, such as confirmed or pending.
### Assigned Task
* **Assigned Task** (`assigned_task`, `text`, optional): The specific task assigned to you for specific tasks or events.
### Assigned Priority
* **Assigned Priority** (`assigned_priority`, `text`, optional): The priority level of your assignment.
### Assigned Notes
* **Assigned Notes** (`assigned_notes`, `note`, optional): Any additional notes about your assignment.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your form before submission to ensure you've filled out all required fields.
- Double-check your availability dates to ensure accuracy.
- Use the notes field for any additional comments or information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our support team.
