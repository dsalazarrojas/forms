# pre-session-information-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-session-information-form is designed to collect information from attendees about their session preferences and requirements before attending a session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Review the fields carefully to ensure you provide the required information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the session organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Name (Page 1)
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the session you are attending.

### Attendee First Name (Page 1)
* **Attendee First Name** (`attendee_first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.

### Attendee Last Name (Page 1)
* **Attendee Last Name** (`attendee_last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.

### Contact Email (Page 2)
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.

### Attendee Organisation (Page 2)
* **Attendee Organisation** (`attendee_organisation`, `text`, required: false): Enter your organization name if applicable.

### Attendee Position (Page 2)
* **Attendee Position** (`attendee_position`, `text`, required: false): Enter your position or role within your organization.

### Session Date (Page 3)
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the session you are attending.

### Session Start Time (Page 3)
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the session.

### Session End Time (Page 3)
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of the session.

### Session Duration (Page 3)
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the session in minutes.

### Number of Attendees (Page 3)
* **Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of attendees you will bring.

### Session Rooms (Page 4)
* **Session Rooms** (`session_rooms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the room(s) you would like to attend.

### Attendee Roles (Page 4)
* **Attendee Roles** (`attendee_roles`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the roles you are attending as.

### Attendee Diet (Page 4)
* **Attendee Diet** (`attendee_diet`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your dietary restrictions.

### Attendee Food Restrictions (Page 4)
* **Attendee Food Restrictions** (`attendee_food_restrictions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any food restrictions, please specify.

### Attendee Special Requirements (Page 4)
* **Attendee Special Requirements** (`attendee_special_requirements`, `text`, required: false): If you have any special requirements, please specify.

### Attendee Comments (Page 4)
* **Attendee Comments** (`attendee_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or questions, please provide them here.

### Assigned Tool (Page 5)
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you have been assigned a tool.

### Attendee Availability (Page 5)
* **Attendee Availability** (`attendee_availability`, `time`, required: false): Enter your available time slots.

### Attendee Interests (Page 5)
* **Attendee Interests** (`attendee_interests`, `text`, required: false): Enter your interests or areas of focus.

### Attendee Preferred Tool (Page 5)
* **Attendee Preferred Tool** (`attendee_preferred_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter your preferred tool.

### Attendee Tool Preference 1 (Page 5)
* **Attendee Tool Preference 1** (`attendee_tool_preference_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first tool preference.

### Attendee Tool Preference 2 (Page 5)
* **Attendee Tool Preference 2** (`attendee_tool_preference_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your second tool preference.

### Attendee Tool Preference 3 (Page 5)
* **Attendee Tool Preference 3** (`attendee_tool_preference_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your third tool preference.

### Submitted Date (Page 6)
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required: false): The date the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the session organizers.
* This form is designed to assist in making necessary arrangements and adjustments for your session needs.
