# Gift Wrapping Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Wrapping Event Registration form is used to collect information from participants to create a registration record for a gift wrapping event. This form is used to gather details about the participant, including their contact information and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Name and Contact Information" section.
2. Select the date of the event you will be attending from the calendar in the "Event Date" field.
3. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
4. Provide details about the event in the "Event Details" field.
5. Indicate whether or not you will be participating in the gift wrapping portion of the event in the "Gift List" field.
6. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone Number" field.
9. Enter your address in the "Participant Address" field.
10. Enter your city in the "Participant City" field.
11. Enter your state in the "Participant State" field.
12. Enter your zip code in the "Participant Zip" field.
13. Enter any additional comments in the "Participant Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name and Contact Information (gift_wrapping_event_registration_form)**: This is the first section of the form where you will enter your name and contact information.
* **Email Address (email)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can reach you with event updates and details.
* **Phone Number (phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with event information.
* **Event Date (event_date)**: Select the date of the event from the calendar to ensure you attend the correct event.
* **Event Time (event_time)**: Enter the time of the event to plan your schedule accordingly.
* **Event Details (event_details)**: Provide any details about the event that will help us plan and prepare for your participation.
* **Gift List (gift_list)**: Indicate whether or not you will be participating in the gift wrapping portion of the event.
* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter the name of the participant you are registering.
* **Participant Email (participant_email)**: Enter the email address of the participant you are registering.
* **Participant Phone Number (participant_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the participant you are registering.
* **Participant Address (participant_address)**: Enter the address of the participant you are registering.
* **Participant City (participant_city)**: Enter the city of the participant you are registering.
* **Participant State (participant_state)**: Enter the state of the participant you are registering.
* **Participant Zip (participant_zip)**: Enter the zip code of the participant you are registering.
* **Participant Comments (participant_comments)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the participant you are registering.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can contact you and prepare for the event correctly.
- If you are registering multiple participants, please complete this form for each participant separately.
