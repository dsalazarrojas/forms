# Walt Disney World Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Walt Disney World Information Form is designed to gather information from visitors about their experiences and preferences during their visit. This form is intended to help Walt Disney World's management team better understand guest needs and improve the overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. On the first page, enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
3. On the second page, provide more details about your visit, such as dates, arrival and departure times, and any special requests.
4. On the third page, add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (text, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **First Page** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your visit on this first page.
* **Second Page** (text, optional): Provide more details about your visit, such as dates, arrival and departure times, and any special requests.
* **Third Page** (text, optional): Add any final comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips

* Please fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Be as specific as possible when providing information about your visit.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.

Note: The form fields are all text fields, and each page is optional, which means you can skip filling out a page if you don't have any information to provide.
