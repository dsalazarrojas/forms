# skills_maintenance_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The skills maintenance registration form is used to collect information from employees who require skills maintenance. This form helps management to track the skills level of employees and keep up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the skills maintenance registration form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to select the correct skills relevant to your training or maintenance.
3. Enter the date of birth to ensure accurate tracking of skills level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address that you use for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your valid phone number that you use for communication.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_one, required: false): Select the skills that you require maintenance or training for. You can select one or multiple skills from the list.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event that you are attending or participating in.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you will be receiving the training or maintenance.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the provider of the training or maintenance.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure the correct skills level is tracked. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or support team for clarification.
