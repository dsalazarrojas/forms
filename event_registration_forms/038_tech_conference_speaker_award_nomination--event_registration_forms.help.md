# tech-conference-speaker-award-nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a speaker award nomination, which can be completed by anyone who wants to nominate a deserving speaker at a tech conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the nomination in the "Nomination Title" field.
2. Provide the name of the person you are nominating in the "Nominee Name" field.
3. Enter the email address of the person you are nominating in the "Nominee Email" field. Please note that this must be a valid email address.
4. In the "Nomination Description" field, briefly explain why you are nominating this person and why they deserve an award.
5. Enter the name of the speaker you are nominating in the "Speaker Name" field.
6. Add a brief bio of the speaker in the "Speaker Bio" field.
7. You can also provide up to three scores for the speaker, with each score having a value between 0 and 10. These scores are optional and should be used to provide additional context for the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Title** (`Nomination Title`, `text`, required: false): A brief title for the nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (`Nominee Name`, `text`, required: false): The name of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Email** (`Nominee Email`, `email`, required: false): A valid email address of the person you are nominating.
* **Nomination Description** (`Nomination Description`, `text`, required: false): Briefly explain why you are nominating this person and why they deserve an award.
* **Speaker Name** (`Speaker Name`, `text`, required: false): The name of the speaker you are nominating.
* **Speaker Bio** (`Speaker Bio`, `text`, required: false): A brief bio of the speaker.
* **Score 1** (`Score 1`, `number`, required: false): An optional score for the speaker, with a value between 0 and 10.
* **Score 2** (`Score 2`, `number`, required: false): An optional score for the speaker, with a value between 0 and 10.
* **Score 3** (`Score 3`, `number`, required: false): An optional score for the speaker, with a value between 0 and 10.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide a valid reason for the nomination, as this will help in the evaluation process.
* You can only nominate people who you believe have made significant contributions to the tech industry or have delivered exceptional speaking performances.
* If you are unsure about what to enter in any of the fields, please feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
