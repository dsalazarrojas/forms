# volunteer_orientation_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers about their attendance at session events. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in a single sitting, making sure to complete all required fields.
- Start by selecting the session date you attended.
- Enter your name as it appears on your records or identification.
- Select your role as a volunteer for the event.
- Choose your preferred contact method: email or phone.
- If you have a preferred email address, enter it for easy contact.
- If you have a phone number, enter it for easy contact.
- Add any additional comments about your experience or session.
- Select the locations you attended sessions at.
- For each session you attended, select whether you were present ("Yes") or not present ("No").
- If you were not present, provide a brief reason for your non-attendance.

* **session_date**: 
	+ Label: session_date
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: false
	+ Explanation: Please select the date you attended the session.
* **volunteer_name**: 
	+ Label: volunteer_name
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Explanation: Enter your name as it appears on your records or identification.
* **role**: 
	+ Label: role
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: false

* **contact_method**: 
	+ Label: contact_method

	+ Required: false

* **contact_email**: 
	+ Label: contact_email
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: false
	+ Explanation: Enter your email address for easy contact.
* **contact_phone**: 
	+ Label: contact_phone
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Explanation: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **comments**: 
	+ Label: comments
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Explanation: Add any additional comments about your experience or session.
* **session_location**: 
	+ Label: session_location
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: false

	+ Label: session_attendance
