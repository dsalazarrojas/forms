# Mobile Accessories Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from attendees for the Mobile Accessories Event. This information will help us understand your interests and preferences, ensuring that we can provide you with the best experience possible.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your company name (if applicable) in the "Company" field.
3.  Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Select your attendee type from the options provided in the "Attendee Type" field.
6.  Choose your segments of interest in the "Segments Of Interest" field.
7.  Select the events you'd like to sign up for in the "Sign up for" field.
8.  Enter the date and time of the event you'd like to attend in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
9.  If you have any additional comments or questions, you can enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
*  **Company** (`company`, text, optional): This is your company name if you wish to provide one.
*  **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
*  **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
*  **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, select_one, required): Choose your role at the event from the provided options.
*  **Segments Of Interest** (`segments_of_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select your areas of interest for the event.
*  **Sign up for** (`sign_up_for`, select_multiple, required): Choose the events you'd like to attend.
*  **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event (MM/DD/YYYY).
*  **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the event time (HH-MM AM/PM).
*  **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
*  **Event Date 2** (`event_date_2`, date, optional): This field is for secondary event dates if needed.
*  **Event Time 2** (`event_time_2`, time, optional): This field is for secondary event times if needed.
*  **Segments Of Interest 2** (`segments_of_interest_2`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for secondary areas of interest if needed.

**Tips**

*  Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and consistently.
*  If you have any issues or questions, please contact us at the provided email address.
