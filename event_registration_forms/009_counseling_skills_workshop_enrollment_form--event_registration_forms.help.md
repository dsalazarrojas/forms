# Counseling Skills Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counseling Skills Workshop Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from participants registering for the Counseling Skills Workshop. This form will be used for workshop planning, participant management, and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name**, which is your name as the person registering for the workshop.
2. Enter your **Email**, which is used for communication and record-keeping purposes.
3. Fill in your **Contact Number**, which is your phone number where you can be reached.
4. Select the **Workshop Date** and enter the desired **Time of Day** for the workshop you want to attend.
5. Choose the **Workshop Time**, which is the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
6. Select the **Workshop Duration**, which is the length of the workshop you are interested in.
7. Choose your **Participant Type**, which categorizes you as a participant in the workshop.
8. Select your **Participant Attendance** status, which indicates whether you plan to attend the workshop.
9. Fill in your **Emergency Contact Name**, **Emergency Contact Relationship**, and **Emergency Contact Number** and **Emergency Contact Email** for emergency contact purposes.
10. Provide any **Participant Additional Info**, **Participant Workshop Preferences**, **Workshop Host**, **Workshop Capacity**, and **Participant Workshop Experience** as needed.
11. Fill in any **Additional Info 2**, **Additional Info 3**, **Additional Info 4**, and **Additional Info 5** as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as the participant registering for the workshop.
* **Email**: Enter your email address used for communication and record-keeping purposes.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date you prefer for the workshop.
* **Time of Day**: Select the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
* **Workshop Time**: Select the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration**: Select the length of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Participant Type**: Choose the category that best describes you as a participant.
* **Participant Attendance**: Select whether you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location where the workshop will be held.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Participant Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Participant Workshop Preferences**: Enter any preferences you have for the workshop.
* **Workshop Host**: Enter the name of the workshop host.
* **Workshop Capacity**: Enter the maximum capacity of the workshop.
* **Participant Workshop Experience**: Enter your workshop experience.
* **Participant Workshop Interests**: Enter your workshop interests.
* **Additional Info 2**, **Additional Info 3**, **Additional Info 4**, and **Additional Info 5**: Enter any additional information as needed.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email and contact number for accuracy.
* Select the correct participant type and workshop duration.
* Be honest about your emergency contact information.
* Enter any necessary additional information to ensure workshop planning and participant management.
