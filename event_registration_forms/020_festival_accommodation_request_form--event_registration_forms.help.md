# Festival Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request accommodation for a festival or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your first name, surname, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred accommodation type (e.g., Hotel, Hostel, Camping).
3. Enter the number of guests attending the event with you.
4. Enter your arrival date and arrival time.
5. If necessary, provide any special requirements or requests.
6. Provide any additional accommodation requests you may have.
7. Enter the name of the event you are attending.
8. Enter the date and time of the event.
9. Enter the location of the event.
10. Review and submit your completed form.
