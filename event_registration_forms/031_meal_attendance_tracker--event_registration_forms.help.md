# Meal Attendance Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Meal Attendance Tracker form is used to collect information about the meals that guests have attended, including their name, meal type, quantity, and any special needs they may have. This data will be used to track and manage meal attendance efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Guest Name in the text field provided.
2. Select the type of meal you would like to attend from the options provided.
3. Enter the quantity of meal you would like to attend.
4. If you have any special needs or requests, type them in the text field provided.
5. Enter your email address (if required).
6. Enter your phone number (if required).
7. Enter the date and start time of the event in the format dd/mm/dd/mm hh:MM.
8. Click the submit button to send the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* Guest Name (`guest_name`): Enter your name here. This field is required.
* Meal Type (`meal_type`): Select the type of meal you would like to attend from the options provided. Options are Apple, Salad, Soup, Sandwich, and Fruits.
* Quantity (`quantity`): Enter the number of meal you would like to attend.
* Special Needs (`special_needs`): If you have any special needs or requests, type them here.
* Email (`email`): Enter your email address. This field is optional.
* Phone (`phone`): Enter your phone number. This field is optional.
* Date (`date`): Enter the date of the event in the format dd/mm/dd/mm.
* Time (`time`): Enter the start time of the event in the format hh:MM.
