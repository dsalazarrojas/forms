# Artist Arrival Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for artists and performers to provide essential information for event management, including arrival details, performance times, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure accuracy.
2. Enter your artist or performer name as it appears on the event schedule and promotional materials.
3. Provide your artist ID or confirmation number for reference.
4. Select the type of performance you will be providing.
5. Enter your scheduled performance time as confirmed with the event organizers.
6. Enter the current date and time.
7. Indicate your arrival status.
8. If your equipment is arriving separately, specify when you expect it to arrive.
9. If a parking location has been assigned, enter it here.
10. Confirm if a dressing room has been assigned.
11. Describe any special accommodation needs or requirements.
12. Confirm if you have already provided your technical and hospitality specifications.
13. Confirm your health and safety status.
14. If you have any health or safety concerns, describe them here.
15. Provide the name and contact information for your emergency contact person.
16. Provide the name of your event staff member assigned to you.
17. Add any additional notes or requirements for the event team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Performer Name**
	+ Enter the name under which you will be listed on the event schedule and promotional materials.
	+ Example: Stage name or legal name.
* **Artist ID or Confirmation Number**
	+ Enter the reference number for this event.
* **Performance Type**
	+ Select the type of performance you will be providing (e.g., Musician, Dancer, Comedian).
* **Scheduled Performance Time**
	+ Enter the time you are scheduled to perform, as confirmed with the event organizers.
* **Check-In Date**
	+ Enter the current date.
* **Actual Check-In Time**
	+ Enter the time you actually checked in with the event staff.
* **Arrival Status**
	+ Indicate if you are on time, early, or late for your performance.
* **Will Equipment Be Arriving Separately?**
	+ Indicate if your equipment is arriving separately.
* **Equipment Arrival Status**
	+ If your equipment is arriving separately, specify when you expect it to arrive.
* **Parking Location Assigned**
	+ Enter the parking location assigned to you, if any.
* **Dressing Room Assignment**
	+ Confirm if a dressing room has been assigned to you.
* **Special Accommodation Needs**
	+ Describe any specific needs or requirements you have.
* **Have You Provided Your Rider/Requirements?**
	+ Confirm if you have already submitted your technical and hospitality specifications.
* **Health and Safety Declaration**
	+ Confirm your health and safety status for the event.
* **If Any Concerns, Please Describe**
	+ Describe any health or safety concerns you have, if any.
* **Emergency Contact Name**
	+ Enter the name of the person to contact in an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**
	+ Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Event Staff Member Assigned**
	+ Enter the name of your point person for the event.
* **Additional Notes or Requirements**
	+ Add any other information you want to provide to the event team.
