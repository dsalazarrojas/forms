# Procurement And Purchasing Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for the participant to enroll in a procurement and purchasing training program. It captures essential information about the participant, such as their name, email, phone number, training program, and training date. This form also includes fields for additional details like participant company, title, and address.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the desired "Training Program" from the dropdown list.
5. Enter the date you would like to attend the training in the "Training Date" field.
6. Fill in your participant's company name in the "Company" field.
7. Enter your title in the "Title" field.
8. Fill in your address in the "Address" field.
9. If applicable, add any additional notes about yourself in the "Notes" field.
10. Provide your organization name in the "Organization" field.
11. Enter your role in the "Role" field.
12. Enter the name of the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
13. If you have a manager, fill in their name in the "Manager Name" field.
14. If you have a manager, fill in their email address in the "Manager Email" field.
15. If you have a manager, provide their phone number in the "Manager Phone" field.
16. Select the status of the training you are enrolling for in the "Training Status" field.
17. Enter the location of the training in the "Training Location" field.
18. Enter the duration of the training in the "Training Duration" field.
19. Fill in your participant status in the "Participant Status" field.
20. Enter the date of your participant status in the "Participant Status Date" field.
21. If necessary, add any comments about your participant status in the "Participant Status Comments" field.
22. Fill in the date the participant status was last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business cards or ID.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Training Program (select_one)**: Select the specific training program you wish to enroll in.
* **Training Date (date)**: Enter the date you would like to attend the training.
* **Company (text)**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business cards or ID.
* **Title (text)**: Enter your job title as it appears on your business cards or ID.
* **Address (text)**: Enter your work or home address.
* **Notes (note)**: If applicable, add any additional information about yourself.
* **Organization (text)**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Role (text)**: Enter your role in the company.
* **Department (text)**: Enter the department you belong to.
* **Manager Name (text)**: If you have a manager, enter their name.
* **Manager Email (email)**: If you have a manager, enter their email address.
* **Manager Phone (text)**: If you have a manager, enter their phone number.
* **Training Status (select_one)**: Select the status of the training you are enrolling for.
* **Training Location (text)**: Enter the location of the training.
* **Manager Title (text)**: If you have a manager, enter their title.
* **Training Duration (number)**: Enter the duration of the training in hours.
* **Participant Status (text)**: Enter your participant status.
* **Participant Status Date (date)**: Enter the date of your participant status.
* **Participant Status Comments (text)**: If necessary, add any comments about your participant status.
* **Participant Status Last Updated (date)**: Enter the date the participant status was last updated.
* **Participant Status Last Updated Time (time)**: Enter the time the participant status was last updated.
