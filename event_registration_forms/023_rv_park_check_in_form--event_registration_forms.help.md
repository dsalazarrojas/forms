# RV Park Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RV Park Check In Form is designed to gather essential information from guests arriving at the park. This includes personal details, vehicle specifics, and any additional information you'd like to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and any other guest names (if applicable) in the "Guest Information" section.
2.  Select the correct arrival date from the calendar provided in the "Arrival Date" field.
3.  Enter your estimated arrival time in the "Arrival Time" field. Please format this as "HH:MM" (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
4.  For "Arrival Method," select the most accurate arrival method for your group (e.g., RV, motorcycle, or "Other" if it doesn't fit into the other categories).
5.  In the "Vehicle Type" field, pick the type of vehicle you'll be arriving with. If it's not an RV or motorcycle, choose "Other" with a description of your vehicle.
6.  The "Vehicle Length" field asks for the length of your vehicle (in feet). Please refer to the provided options or enter "Other" for a custom length.
7.  For "Vehicle Weight," select the most accurate weight range for your vehicle.
8.  Finally, provide any additional information you'd like to share about your group or vehicle in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Information
*   **Guest Information** (`rv_park_check_in_form_guest_info`, `text`, **Required**)
    Enter your name and any other guest names (if applicable) you'd like to include.

### Arrival Date
*   **Arrival Date** (`rv_park_check_in_form_arrival_date`, `date`, **Required**)
    Select the date you plan to arrive at the park using the provided calendar.

### Arrival Time
*   **Arrival Time** (`rv_park_check_in_form_arrival_time`, `time`, **Required**)
    Enter your estimated arrival time in the format "HH:MM" (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).

### Arrival Method
*   **Arrival Method** (`rv_park_check_in_form_arrival_method`, `select_multiple`, **Required**)
    Choose how you plan to arrive at the park (e.g., RV, motorcycle, or "Other").

### Vehicle Type
*   **Vehicle Type** (`rv_park_check_in_form_vehicle_type`, `select_one`, **Required**)
    Select the type of vehicle you'll be arriving with (e.g., RV, motorcycle, or "Other" with a description).

### Vehicle Length
*   **Vehicle Length** (`rv_park_check_in_form_vehicle_length`, `select_one`, **Required**)
    Pick the length of your vehicle in feet using the provided options or enter "Other" for a custom measurement.

### Vehicle Weight
*   **Vehicle Weight** (`rv_park_check_in_form_vehicle_weight`, `select_one`, **Required**)
    Select the most accurate weight range for your vehicle.

### Additional Info
*   **Additional Info** (`rv_park_check_in_form_additional_info`, `note`, **Optional**)
    Provide any additional information about your group or vehicle you'd like to share with the park staff.

## Tips
*   Please double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submission.
*   If you're unsure about any field or have questions, don't hesitate to contact the RV Park staff for assistance.
