# Banquet Event Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for the Banquet Event, ensuring that attendees can accurately register and provide necessary details for a smooth event experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the event you will be attending.
2. Enter your full name in the "Guest Name" field.
3. (Optional) If the event is under a specific reservation name, enter that in the "Reservation Name" field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
5. Indicate the number of guests you will be bringing.
6. Select any dietary restrictions you have from the provided options.
7. If you have any special dietary notes, provide them in the "Special Dietary Notes" field.
8. If you have any accessibility needs, choose the option that best describes your requirements.
9. If you have specific accessibility details, provide them in the "Accessibility Details" field.
10. Indicate your seating preference (if any) and provide the table number if assigned.
11. If your guest is attending with you, indicate that they are attending and enter their name.
12. Indicate if you need parking information.
13. If you need parking information, provide your vehicle's license plate or color.
14. Enter your estimated arrival time.
15. Confirm your RSVP status.
16. Enter your check-in time (if applicable).
17. Add any special notes or requests in the "Special Notes or Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, Required): Enter the date of the event you will be attending.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, Required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the event materials.
* **Reservation Name** (`reservation_name`, text, Optional): If the event is under a specific reservation name, enter that name here.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, email, Optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`guest_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Number of Guests in Party** (`party_size`, number, Required): Indicate the number of people you will be bringing.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all the dietary options that apply to you.
* **Special Dietary Notes** (`special_dietary_notes`, text, Optional): If you have any special dietary notes, provide them here.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, Optional): Indicate if you have any accessibility needs.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, text, Optional): If you have specific accessibility details, provide them here.
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, select_one, Optional): Indicate your seating preference.
* **Table Assignment** (`table_assignment`, text, Optional): If you have a table number, enter it here.
* **Is Your Plus One Attending?** (`plus_one_attending`, select_one, Optional): Indicate if your guest is attending.
* **Plus One Name** (`plus_one_name`, text, Optional): If your guest is attending, enter their name.
* **Do You Need Parking Information?** (`parking_required`, select_one, Optional): Indicate if you need parking information.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, Optional): If you need parking information, provide your vehicle's license plate or color.
* **Estimated Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, Optional): Enter your estimated arrival time.
* **RSVP Confirmed?** (`rsvp_confirmation`, select_one, Optional): Confirm your RSVP status.
* **Check-In Time** (`check_in_time`, time, Optional): Enter your check-in time.
* **Special Notes or Requests** (`staff_notes`, text, Optional): Provide any special notes or requests.
