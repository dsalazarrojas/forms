# Hospitality Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from attendees for a hospitality conference. It will help the event organizers ensure that attendees have provided accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
* Select the correct options for dietary restrictions, if applicable.
* Confirm that the provided event details are accurate.
* Confirm the attendee's contact information.
* Confirm the additional information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Registration**: Enter your name and any other details that you would like to share with the event organizers.
* **Event Details**: Enter the name of the event, its description, start date, venue, and time.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number so that we can get in touch with you during the event.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please select the corresponding options (e.g., Vegan, Vegetarian, etc.).
* **Attendee Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself that you would like to share with us.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
