# Elderly Assistance Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for volunteers who want to offer their assistance to elderly individuals. By completing this form, you will be helping to make a positive impact on the lives of seniors in need.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Volunteer Name** in the first field. This is your name, and we will use it to contact you.
2. In the **Organization** field, please enter the name of the organization you are volunteering with (if any).
3. Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you via email.
4. In the **Phone** field, enter your phone number so we can reach out to you for further information.
5. Select your **Volunteer Role** by choosing from the options provided. This will help us understand your areas of interest and expertise.
6. Choose your **Available Time** by selecting a specific time of day that suits you best. This will help us schedule volunteer opportunities around your availability.
7. Select your **Available Days** by choosing the days of the week when you are available to volunteer. This will help us match you with suitable opportunities.
8. Enter your **Preferred Time** of day when you are available to volunteer.
9. Enter your **Preferred Time End** of your volunteering session.
10. In the **Available Location**, enter the area or location where you can provide services to elderly individuals.
11. Finally, in the **Volunteer Note** field, you can add any additional comments or notes about your volunteering preferences or experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name**: Your name, which we will use to contact you.
* **Organization**: The name of the organization you are volunteering with (if any).
* **Email**: Your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for further communication.
* **Volunteer Role**:
	+ **Yes**: You are interested and available to take on a volunteer role.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in taking on a volunteer role.
* **Available Time**: Your preferred time of day to volunteer.
* **Available Days**: Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
* **Preferred Time**: The time of day when you are most available to volunteer.
* **Preferred Time End**: The end time of your volunteering session.
* **Available Location**: Enter the area or location where you can provide services to elderly individuals.
* **Volunteer Note**: Additional comments or notes about your volunteering preferences or experience.

Note: Please fill in all required fields carefully and accurately.
