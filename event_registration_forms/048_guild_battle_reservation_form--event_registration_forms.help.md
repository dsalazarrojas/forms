# Guild Battle Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and manage match-ups for the Guild Battle event. It is a comprehensive form that collects information from both the event organizers and participants to ensure a smooth and safe experience for all involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Schedule your match-ups by providing the date and time for each matchup in the "Reservations" field.
2. Confirm your participation by selecting "Yes" from the "Confirm Participation" dropdown menu.
3. Provide any necessary medical conditions or disabilities that may impact your performance in the "Participation Details" field.
4. Enter your name, email, and phone number in their respective fields to provide contact information.
5. Select one of the agreement options for the participant, event organizer, or event terms of service to acknowledge your understanding and acceptance of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Matchups (Reservations)**: Enter the date and time for each matchup.
* **Confirm Participation**: Confirm your participation in the event by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
* **Participation Details**: Provide any medical conditions or disabilities that may impact your performance.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event in 24h format.
* **Event Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Address**: Enter the address of the event location.
* **Event Organizer Name**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email**: Enter the email of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email as a participant.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number as a participant.
* **Participant Note**: Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your participation.
* **Participant Agreement**: Select one of the agreement options to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the participant terms.
* **Participant Terms Agreement**: Select one of the agreement options to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the participant terms of service.
* **Event Terms Agreement**: Select one of the agreement options to confirm the event organizer's understanding and acceptance of the event terms.
* **Event Organizer Terms Agreement**: Select one of the agreement options to confirm the event organizer's understanding and acceptance of their own terms.
* **Participant Terms of Service**: Select one of the agreement options to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the participant terms of service.
* **Event Organizer Terms of Service**: Select one of the agreement options to confirm the event organizer's understanding and acceptance of their own terms of service.
* **Event Terms of Service**: Select one of the agreement options to confirm the event organizer's understanding and acceptance of the event terms of service.
* **Participant Agreement Date**: Enter the date you agree to the participant terms.
