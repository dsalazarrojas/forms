# spin_the_prize_wheel - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spin the Prize Wheel form is used to collect contact information and preferences for an event registration. It helps us understand who is interested in attending the event and what they would like to spin the prize wheel.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your **User Details** (Field 1) with your name.
- Select your preferred language from the **Language** (Field 2) dropdown menu.
- Choose your **Contact Method** (Field 3) to ensure we can reach you.
- In **Entry Details** (Field 4), provide any additional information about your entry.
- Decide whether you would like to **Spin The Wheel** (Field 5) by selecting "Yes" or "No".
- Select which prizes you are interested in from the **Prize Preferences** (Field 6) dropdown menu.
- If you'd like to be contacted, enter your email address in **Contact Email** (Field 7).
- Enter your **Contact Phone Number** (Field 8) so we can reach you if needed.
- Provide any **Additional Details** (Field 9) about your entry, such as a message or comment.
- Decide how to proceed with your submission by selecting **Submit** (Field 10), **Continue** (Field 10), or "Submit" and "Continue" is not available.
- Fill in your **First Name** (Field 11), **Last Name** (Field 12) and other personal information.
- Provide your **Street Address** (Field 13), **City** (Field 14), **State/Province** (Field 15), **Zip/Postal Code** (Field 16), and **Country** (Field 17).
- Choose your **Region** (Field 18) from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Language**: Select your preferred language for communication.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **Entry Details**: Provide any extra information about your entry.
* **Spin The Wheel**: Decide whether you'd like to spin the prize wheel.
* **Prize Preferences**: Select the prizes you're interested in.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Additional Details**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Submit/Continue**: Decide how to proceed with your submission.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Street**: Enter the street address where you can be reached.
* **City**: Enter the city where you can be reached.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you can be reached.
* **Zip/Postal Code**: Enter the zip or post code where you can be reached.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you can be reached.
* **Region**: Select the region you're interested in.
