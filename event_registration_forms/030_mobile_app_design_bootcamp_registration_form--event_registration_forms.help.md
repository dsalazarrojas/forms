# Mobile App Design Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees who want to participate in the Mobile App Design Bootcamp. It will help us get to know you better and tailor the bootcamp experience according to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Registration Details** (page 1). This is the starting point for your registration.
2. Provide your **Name** in the required field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the required field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the optional field.
5. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** of your desired bootcamp duration.
6. Fill in your **Bootcamp Details** with any additional information you'd like to provide.
7. Choose whether you have attended or want to attend a **Bootcamp** by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the optional field.
8. Enter the **Bootcamp Name** (optional).
9. Finally, specify the **Location** and **Venue** of the bootcamp (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (page 1): This is where you'll start your registration.
* **Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted about the bootcamp, enter your phone number.
* **Start Date**: Select the start date for your desired bootcamp duration.
* **End Date**: Select the end date for your desired bootcamp duration.
* **Bootcamp Details**: Add any extra information you'd like to provide about your bootcamp experience.
* **Bootcamp Name**: If you're registering for a specific bootcamp, enter its name here (optional).
* **Type**: Indicate if you've attended or want to attend a bootcamp by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' (optional).
* **Location**: If you're registering for a specific bootcamp, enter its location here (optional).
* **Venue**: If you're registering for a specific bootcamp, enter its venue here (optional).

Note: Please fill in all required fields (indicated by *) to ensure successful submission.
