# Clean Up Volunteer Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in volunteering for clean-up efforts. If you are interested, please fill out this form accurately and honestly to help us get a better understanding of your availability, skills, and any physical limitations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Volunteer name** in the space provided. This is a required field.
2. Provide your **Email address** for us to contact you with event updates and logistics. This is a required field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the space provided. This is optional.
4. List the dates you are available to volunteer. Please use the format "MM/DD/YYYY - MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 01/01/2023 - 12/31/2023). This is a required field.
5. Select the **Preferred roles** you are willing to perform from the list provided:
	* Trash pickup
	* Logistics and setup
	* Volunteer coordination
	* First aid support
6. If you have any tools or equipment you can bring, please list them in the space provided. This is optional.
7. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact. This is a required field for volunteer records.
8. If you have any physical limitations or concerns, please describe them in the space provided. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer name**:
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Description: Your full name.
* **Email address**:
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Description: Used for event updates and logistics.
* **Phone number**:
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Optional contact information for day of event.
* **Availability dates**:
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Description: List the dates you are available to volunteer in the format "MM/DD/YYYY - MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Preferred roles**:
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Description: Select the roles you are willing to perform.
	+ Options: Trash pickup, Logistics and setup, Volunteer coordination, First aid support.
* **Tools or equipment you can bring**:
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: List any tools or equipment you can bring to assist with clean-up efforts.
* **Emergency contact**:
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Description: Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Physical limitations or concerns**:
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Describe any physical limitations or concerns you have that may impact your ability to participate in clean-up efforts.
