# Faith Based Mission Trip Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants who wish to apply for a faith-based mission trip. It collects personal and contact information, trip details, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including any relevant details about your trip and medical history.
2. Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you.
3. Choose your payment method for the trip.
4. If you have any medical conditions, let us know so we can take necessary precautions.
5. Add any additional notes about yourself as a participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information (1)
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first section of the form where you provide basic information about yourself. Include your name, address, and any other relevant details about your trip.

### Trip Details (2)
* **Trip Details** (`trip_info`, `text`, required/optional): In this section, please provide details about the trip you're applying for. This could include the destination, dates, and any other relevant information.

### Contact Information (3)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Here, you need to provide your contact information so we can reach out to you about your application. Include your email address and phone number.

### Payment Details (4)
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the details of your payment method, such as the amount you're willing to pay and any other payment-related information.

### Participant Concerns (5)
* **Participant Concerns** (`participant_concerns`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any concerns or questions about the trip, please share them here.

### Emergency Contact Information (6)
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the contact information of an emergency contact person in case of unexpected situations.

### Medical Information (7)
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions that might affect your participation in the trip, please let us know so we can take necessary precautions.

### Payment Method (8)
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the payment method you prefer from the options: Visa or Mastercard.

### Medical Condition (9)
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions, please specify them here.

### Participant Notes (10)
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `text`, required/optional): This is an optional section where you can add any additional notes about yourself as a participant.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you're unsure about any section, contact the organizers of the mission trip for guidance.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
