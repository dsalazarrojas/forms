# Manufacturing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users interested in attending the manufacturing workshop. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your details for the workshop and choose your preferred topics of interest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your participant information.
2. Choose your preferred workshop topics from the select multiple dropdown menu.
3. Provide your workshop schedule and location information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: 
*   This section is to be filled out with your personal details.
*   **First Name**: This field is to be filled out with your first name.
*   **Last Name**: This field is to be filled out with your last name.
*   **Email**: This field should be filled out with your email address.
*   **Phone**: This field can be filled out with your phone number.
*   **Workshop Preferences**: Select all the topics that you are interested in attending, such as Automation and Robotics or Supply Chain Management.
*   **Registration Date**: This field is to be filled out with your date of registration.
*   **Workshop Participants**: This field can be filled out with the number of participants you plan to bring with you.
*   **Workshop Topics**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are interested in the workshop topics.
*   **Workshop Schedule**: This field is to be filled out with your schedule preferences for the workshop.
*   **Workshop Duration**: This field can be filled out with the number of hours you plan to participate in the workshop.
*   **Workshop Location**: This field can be filled out with the location of the workshop.
*   **Additional Info**: This field is to be filled out with any extra information you would like to share with the workshop organizers.
*   **Tool**: This field can be filled out with any tools you think would be useful to bring to the workshop.
*   **Notes**: This field is to be filled out with any additional comments you have.
*   **Workshop Status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate if the workshop is active or inactive.
*   **Start Date**: This field is to be filled out with the date the workshop is scheduled to start.
*   **End Date**: This field is to be filled out with the date the workshop is scheduled to end.
*   **Topic**: This field can be filled out with a brief description of the topic of the workshop.
*   **Time**: This field can be filled out with the time of the workshop.
*   **Participants Count**: This field can be filled out with the total number of participants.
*   **Topic List**: Select all the topics that are relevant to the workshop.
*   **Other Topics**: This field can be filled out with any other topics not listed.
*   **Status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate if the workshop is active or inactive.
*   **Comments**: This field is to be filled out with any comments or suggestions you have about the workshop.

This form helps the workshop organizers plan and coordinate the event effectively by gathering necessary information from participants.
