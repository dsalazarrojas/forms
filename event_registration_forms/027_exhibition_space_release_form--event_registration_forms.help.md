# exhibition_space_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The exhibition space release form is used to gather information about an exhibition space release request. It is intended for exhibition space coordinators to submit their exhibition space release requests, providing details about the exhibition space they are interested in renting or leasing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **name** in the "name" field. This is a required field and must be filled out accurately.
2. Provide your **email** in the "email" field. This is also a required field and should be a valid email address.
3. Enter your **phone number** in the "phone" field. This is a required field and should be a valid phone number.
4. Specify the number of **booths** you are requesting in the "booth_request" field. This is a required field and should be a whole number.
5. Select the type of **trade show, fair, or festival** you are interested in. This is a required field and should match your exhibition space request.
6. (Optional) Provide any additional **notes** about your request in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your exhibition space request.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you about your request.
* **booth_request**: Specify the number of booths you are interested in renting or leasing.
* **trade_shows_and_fairs**: Select the type of trade show, fair, or festival you are interested in (trade show, fair, or festival). This will help us match your request with available exhibition spaces.
* **notes**: (Optional) Provide any additional information about your exhibition space request.
