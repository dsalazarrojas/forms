# High School Grad Night Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for students and their parents to sign up for high school graduation events.

## How To Complete This Form

To sign up for the high school graduation event, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name and your name and contact information.
2. Choose your student's class (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
3. Provide the event date and time.
4. Enter the event location.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your student's name as it appears in their records.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian signing up for this event.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for communication.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 19:00).
* **Student Class**: Select your student's class:
	+ Freshman
	+ Sophomore
	+ Junior
	+ Senior
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the event.
* **Graduation Date**: Enter the date of the student's graduation.
* **Grad Time**: Enter the time of the student's graduation in 24-hour format (e.g., 19:00).
* **Grad Location**: Enter the location of the student's graduation.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy, especially your graduation date.
- If you're unsure about any information, contact the event coordinators for clarification.
- You can add notes and comments about the event in the "Notes" field if you have any specific requests or questions.
