# Dance Event Participation Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your interest and relevant details for a dance event. It will help us understand your background, dance experience, and preferences to better plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth and successful participation in the dance event, please follow these steps:
- Fill in your name, last name, email address, and phone number (optional) on pages 1 and 2.
- Choose your availability for the event on page 3.
- Select your preferred dance style and dance experience on pages 4 and 5.
- Optionally, provide any additional comments on page 8.
- Indicate your interest in the event on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FirstName** (Page 1, Text, Required): Please enter your first name.
* **LastName** (Page 1, Text, Required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (Page 2, Email, Required): Enter your valid email address to allow us to contact you about the event.
* **Phone** (Page 2, Text, Optional): If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it to ensure we can reach you if needed.
* **Availability** (Page 3, Select Multiple, Required): Select the dates and times you are available for the event.
* **DanceStyle** (Page 4, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred dance style (e.g., Ballet, Contemporary, Hip-Hop, etc.).
* **DanceExperience** (Page 5, Select One, Required): Indicate your level of dance experience (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Instructor).
* **Comments** (Page 8, Note, Optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please feel free to share them here.
* **Interest** (Page 9, Text, Required): Please type a short message expressing your interest in participating in the dance event.
* **OtherDanceStyle** (Page 10, Text, Optional): If you have a specific dance style that is not listed, please enter it here and provide any additional comments in the following field.
* **OtherDanceStyleComments** (Page 11, Note, Optional): If you have any comments about the dance style you entered in the previous field, please provide them here.
