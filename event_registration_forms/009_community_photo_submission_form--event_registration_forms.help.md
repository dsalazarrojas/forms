# Community Photo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Community Photo Submission Form" is designed to collect photos and gather necessary information from community members who want to share their photos with the organization. This form is used to ensure that the photos are submitted with the proper permissions and credits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Share Your Community Photos" section to start the submission process.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Photographer Full Name" and "Photographer Email Address" fields, respectively.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Photographer Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Enter the title of the event or activity in the "Event or Activity Title" field.
5. Enter the date the photo was taken in the "Date the Photo Was Taken" field.
6. Provide the location where the photo was taken in the "Location of the Photo" field.
7. Give a brief description or caption of the photo in the "Photo Caption or Description" field.
8. If there are people in the photo, list their names from left to right in the "Names of People in the Photo" field (optional).
9. Select "Grant Permission for Newsletter Use" and "Grant Permission for Social Media Use" options accordingly.
10. Choose whether you want photo credit in the "Would you like photo credit?" field.
11. Choose how the credit should appear in the "How should the credit appear?" field (optional).
12. Confirm that you are the owner of the photo and grant the necessary permissions by selecting "I certify that I am the owner of this photo" option.
13. Review the "Terms and Conditions" to understand the usage of the photo.
14. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Full Name**: Enter your full name as the photographer.
* **Photographer Email Address**: Enter your email address as the photographer.
* **Photographer Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Event or Activity Title**: Enter the title of the event or activity in the photo.
* **Date the Photo Was Taken**: Enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Location of the Photo**: Provide the location where the photo was taken.
* **Photo Caption or Description**: Give a brief description or caption of the photo.
* **Names of People in the Photo**: If there are people in the photo, list their names from left to right (optional).
* **Grant Permission for Newsletter Use**: Select "True" to grant permission for the photo to be used in newsletters.
* **Grant Permission for Social Media Use**: Select "True" to grant permission for the photo to be used on social media.
* **Would you like photo credit?**: Select "True" to receive photo credit.
* **How should the credit appear?**: Select how the credit should appear (optional).
* **I certify that I am the owner of this photo**: Confirm that you are the owner of the photo and grant the necessary permissions.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Review the terms and conditions of the photo usage.
* **Submission Date**: The date the photo was submitted.
