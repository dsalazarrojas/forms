# Community Family Fun Day Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Family Fun Day Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants and their families to ensure a successful and enjoyable event. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it easily.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to provide all required information.
3. Double-check your selections before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Basic Information
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for event communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Street Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your home address.
* **City, State, ZIP** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.

### Page 2: Event and Family Details
* **Family Size** (`family_size`, number, required): Enter the total number of family members attending.
* **Children Names and Ages** (`children_names_ages`, text, required): List all children attending with their ages.

### Page 3: Event and Volunteer Details
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Choose the date you plan to attend.
* **Activities of Interest** (`activities_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select the activities your family enjoys (e.g., Face Painting, Bounce Houses, etc.).
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate if you're interested in helping at the event.
* **Preferred Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to help, or select 'Not Applicable' if not interested.
* **T-Shirt Sizes Needed** (`t_shirt_sizes`, text, optional): If you're interested in volunteering, list the T-Shirt sizes and quantities needed.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you or a family member has a food allergy or restriction, list it here.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you require any accommodations, list them here.

### Page 4: Additional Information
* **Transportation Assistance Needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need transportation assistance.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Photo and Video Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate if you consent to photos and videos being taken for promotional use.
* **Liability Waiver** (`waiver_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to the liability waiver.
