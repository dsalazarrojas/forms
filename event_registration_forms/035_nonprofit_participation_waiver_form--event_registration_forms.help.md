# Nonprofit Participation Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain non-profit participation waivers from event participants. It is used to ensure that participants have a clear understanding of their waiver before participating in the event or program organized by the non-profit organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's information in the fields provided.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that the participant's information is accurate and complete.
3. Sign and date the form once you have reviewed and obtained the participant's agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`nonprofit_participation_waiver_form`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the participant, such as their contact preferences or dietary requirements.

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the participant's name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter the participant's address.
* **Waiver to Participant** (`waiver_to_participant`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture the waiver text that will be given to the participant. Please enter the waiver text as it appears on the waiver document.
* **Waiver to Organization** (`waiver_to_organization`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture the waiver text that will be given to the non-profit organization. Please enter the waiver text as it appears on the waiver document.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, note, optional): This is a text field where the participant can agree to the waiver terms. Please enter the text as it appears on the waiver document.

## Tips
* Ensure that the participant has a clear understanding of their waiver before completing this form.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that the participant's information is accurate and complete.
* Sign and date the form once you have reviewed and obtained the participant's agreement.
