This form is intended for students to vote for the prom king and queen candidates. It is likely to be used in an educational institution such as a high school or university, perhaps during prom season. The form is designed to gather information from students, including their student ID, voting preferences for the king and queen candidates, name, email address, phone number, and a timestamped vote. The form is required to be completed with all fields.

# Prom King And Queen Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow students to vote for the prom king and queen candidates. It is a tool for gathering student feedback and opinions on the prom king and queen candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student ID.
2. Select one of the king candidates.
3. Select one of the queen candidates.
4. Enter your name in the Student Name field.
5. Enter your email address in the Student Email field.
6. Enter your phone number in the Student Phone field.
7. Cast your vote by selecting a time in the Student Voting Form field.
8. Click the Submit button to submit your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID (Page 1)**: Enter your student ID number. This is a required field.
* **King Candidate (Page 1)**: Select one of the king candidates to vote for. This is a required field.
* **Queen Candidate (Page 1)**: Select one of the queen candidates to vote for. This is a required field.
* **Student Name (Page 1)**: Enter your full name as it is registered with the school. This is a required field.
* **Student Email (Page 1)**: Enter your email address as it is registered with the school. This is a required field.
* **Student Phone (Page 1)**: Enter your contact phone number. This is a required field.
* **Student Voting Form (Page 1)**: Select a time to cast your vote. This is a required field.
* **Submit (Page 1)**: Click this button to submit your completed form. This is a required field.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your information accurately and honestly.
* Only select one king and one queen candidate.
* The timestamp on the Student Voting Form field represents the time when you submit your vote.
