# Cultural Awareness Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register for cultural awareness training sessions. Please fill out the required and optional fields to provide us with your preferences and information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the language of the training session you are interested in from the "Training Session" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the location where you would like to attend the training from the "Training Location" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your preferred start and end dates for the training session in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Info**: This is where you can provide a brief description of yourself or your organization.
- **Training Session**: Choose the language of the training session you are interested in (English, Spanish, French).
- **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the training session you prefer.
- **End Date**: Enter the end date of the training session you prefer.
- **Training Location**: Select the location where you would like to attend the training (Chicago, IL; New York, NY; Los Angeles, CA).
- **Participant Name**: Enter your name or the name of the participant who will be attending.
- **Participant E-mail**: Enter your email or the email of the participant who will be attending.
- **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number or the phone number of the participant who will be attending.
- **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the training session.

**Tips**
- Make sure to select the correct language and location for your training needs.
- If you have any special requests or accommodations, please let us know in the "Notes" field.
- We will get back to you as soon as possible to confirm your registration.
