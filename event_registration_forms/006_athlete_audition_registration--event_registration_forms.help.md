# Athlete Audition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
To register for an athlete audition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred audition date from the available options.
2. Choose your preferred audition venue from the available options.
3. Complete your athlete's profile information, including:
   - Full name
   - Date of birth
   - Gender
   - Primary sport
   - Primary position or event
   - Years of competitive experience
   - Current club or school (if applicable)
4. Enter your contact information, including:
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Parent or guardian's name (if under 18)
   - Parent or guardian's contact information (if under 18)
5. Provide any relevant medical or safety information, including:
   - Allergies or medical conditions
   - Emergency contact information
6. Agree to the terms of the Audition Waiver and Release.
7. Select a registration fee payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preferred Audition Date**: Select one of the available audition dates. This is the date you prefer to attend the audition.
* **Audition Venue**: Choose the venue where you'll be auditioning. This will help us ensure that you're at the correct location at the correct time.
* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your athlete's full name as it should appear on their uniform and other materials.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your athlete's date of birth in the correct format (e.g., month, day, year).
* **Gender**: Select the correct gender for your athlete.
* **Primary Sport**: Enter your athlete's primary sport, e.g., soccer, basketball, track and field.
* **Primary Position or Event**: Enter your athlete's primary position or event, e.g., goalkeeper, point guard, 100m dash.
* **Years of Competitive Experience**: Enter the number of years your athlete has been competing.
* **Current Club or School**: Enter your athlete's current club or school (if applicable).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: If your athlete is under 18, enter the name of a parent or guardian who will be responsible for their care.
* **Parent or Guardian Contact Information**: If your athlete is under 18, enter the contact information for their parent or guardian.
* **Allergies or Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions or allergies that the coaching staff should be aware of.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **I Agree to the Terms of the Audition Waiver and Release**: Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms.
* **Registration Fee Payment Method**: Select the method by which you will pay the registration fee.

## Tips
* Please double-check your athlete's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* Review the terms of the Audition Waiver and Release on our website for further information.
