# writing_club_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential members about their writing habits, interests, and preferences to assess their suitability for membership in a writing club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the membership form (page 1) with your basic information, including your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your interests and writing frequency on page 2.
3. On page 3, choose your preferred writing topics, writer interests, and writer interests description.
4. On page 4, choose your writer genre of interest, writer frequency of interest, writer topics of interest, and writer club interests.
5. On page 5, select your membership status, membership level, writer clubs, and writer club frequency.
6. On page 6, choose your writer club topics and writer club description.
7. Finally, add any additional comments or goals you have for your writing club membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Form** (`membership_form`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a free-text input where you can provide any additional information about your membership application.
* **Member Interests** (`member_interests`, text, optional):
	+ Please list your interests as a writer, and we'll consider them when assessing your suitability for membership.
* **Writer Info** (`writer_info`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide a brief description of your writing experience and qualifications.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you about your membership.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your membership.
* **Join Date** (`join_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you'd like to join the writing club.
* **Writer Interests** (`writer_interests`, text, optional):
	+ Please list your writing interests and we'll consider them when assessing your suitability for membership.
* **Writing Genres** (`writing_genres`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the writing genres you're interested in or proficient in (e.g., fiction, non-fiction, poetry, etc.).
* **Writing Frequency** (`writing_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose how often you write (e.g., every day, 3-4 times a week, etc.).
* **Writing Topics** (`writing_topics`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the topics you're interested in writing about.
* **Writer Interests Description** (`writer_interests_description`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of your writer interests.
* **Writer Genre of Interest** (`writer_genre_of_interest`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the genre of writing you're interested in or proficient in.
* **Writer Frequency of Interest** (`writer_frequency_of_interest`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your frequency of writing interest.
* **Writer Topics of Interest** (`writer_topics_of_interest`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the topics you're interested in writing about.
* **Writer Club Interests** (`writer_club_interests`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your interests in participating in a writer's club.
* **Member Status** (`member_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose your current membership status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose your desired membership level (e.g., high, low, etc.).
* **Writer Clubs** (`writer_clubs`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the writer clubs you're interested in joining.
* **Writer Club Frequency** (`writer_club_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose how often you'd like to participate in a writer's club.
* **Writer Club Topics** (`writer_club_topics`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the topics you're interested in discussing or writing about in a writer's club.
* **Writer Club Description** (`writer_club_description`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of your ideal writer's club experience.
* **Writer Club Goals** (`writer_club_goals`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any specific goals you have for your writer's club membership.
* **Writer Club Comments** (`writer_club_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or questions you have about your membership application.
