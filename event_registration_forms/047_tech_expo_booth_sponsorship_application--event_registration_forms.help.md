# tech_expo_booth_sponsorship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from potential sponsors and exhibitors for the tech expo event. Please fill out this form to provide your company's contact details, booth package preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booth Sponsorship Application** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the main section of the form where you will provide a brief description of your company and its interest in sponsoring the tech expo.
* **Contact Details** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your company's contact details, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Package Details** (`3`, `text`, required): Describe the package you are interested in, including any specific features or requirements you need.
* **Payment Details** (`4`, `text`, required): Provide payment information, including the type of payment, method of payment, and any other relevant details.
