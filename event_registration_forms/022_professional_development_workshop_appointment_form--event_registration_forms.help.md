# professional_development_workshop_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to register participants for professional development workshops. It collects essential information for each participant, including their details, workshop preferences, and participant status. 

To confirm the purpose of the form, please verify that you are a participant of the workshops. If you are not, you are not supposed to register.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as per the guidelines below.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email** (`2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your participant email for correspondence and updates.
* **Date** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Start Time** (`4`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **End Time** (`5`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Type** (`6`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of workshop you wish to attend (e.g. Workshop 1 or Workshop 2).
* **Workshop Days** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the days of the week you prefer to attend (e.g. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
* **Workshop Time** (`8`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time slot you prefer for the workshop (e.g. 09:00 - 10:00).
* **Participant Name** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Participant Email** (`10`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the participant for correspondence and updates.
* **Participant Phone** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Workshop Location** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the venue or location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Location Notes** (`13`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the workshop location.
* **Workshop Date** (`14`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Time 2** (`15`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time slot you prefer for the workshop (HH:MM 24h format).
* **Workshop Duration** (`16`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the duration of the workshop (e.g. 01:30 - 02:30).
* **Participant Signature** (`17`, `note`, required/optional): Add your participant's signature to confirm their registration.
* **Workshop Notes** (`18`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the workshop.
* **Workshop Status** (`19`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the workshop (e.g. Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **Participant Status** (`20`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the participant (e.g. Registered, Confirmed, Cancelled).
* **Participant Notes** (`21`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the participant.
* **Participant Email 2** (`22`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the participant 2.
* **Participant Phone 2** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the participant 2.
* **Participant Name 2** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant 2.
* **Participant Comments** (`25`, `note`, required/optional): Add any comments about the participant.

## Tips

* Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* Review your submission before submitting it for validation.
* If you have any issues or need further assistance, contact us for support.
