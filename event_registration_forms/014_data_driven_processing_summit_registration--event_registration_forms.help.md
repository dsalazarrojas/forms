# Data Driven Processing Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Driven Processing Summit registration form is designed to collect attendee information, event details, and location specifics for the Data Driven Processing Summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you are attending.
2. Enter your name, email address, and job title.
3. Indicate your role at your company.
4. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
5. Provide additional information about your attendance.
6. Choose a date and time for the event.
7. Specify your time zone and offset.
8. Enter your location details, including city and state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): 
  Select the type of event you are attending from the provided options.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of `username@example.com`.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title at your company.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Indicate your role at your company.
* **Terms and Agreement** (`agreement`, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, note, optional): Optional field for additional terms and conditions.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required): Provide any additional information about your attendance.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Select the time of the event.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Optional field for start date.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Optional field for start time.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Select the end time of the event.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, text, required): Enter your time zone.
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, number, required): Enter your time zone offset.
* **Timezone Label** (`timezone_label`, text, required): Enter your time zone label.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your location.
* **Location Label** (`location_label`, text, optional): Optional field for location label.
* **Location 1** (`location_address1`, text, required): Enter your location's address 1.
* **Location 2** (`location_address2`, text, optional): Optional field for location's address 2.
* **City** (`location_city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`location_state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **Location Zip** (`location_zip`, text, required): Enter your location's zip code.
