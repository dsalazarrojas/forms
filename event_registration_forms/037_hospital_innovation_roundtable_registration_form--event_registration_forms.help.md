# Hospital Innovation Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for the Hospital Innovation Roundtable event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Fill out your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Type your Organization Name in the Organization Name field.
5. Specify your Department in the Department field.
6. Describe your Role in the Role field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your department within your organization.
* **Role** (`role`, text, Optional): Describe your role or position within your organization.

## Tips
* If you are registering for multiple participants, please fill out this form for each participant separately.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
