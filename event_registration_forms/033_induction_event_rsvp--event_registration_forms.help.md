# Induction Event RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals or guests about an upcoming event. It is likely used for internal purposes by HR or administrative staff to track attendance and gather basic details about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (Guest Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Attendee Name** (Attendee Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you will be attending the event with.
* **Attendee Role** (Attendee Role, select_one, required/optional): Select your role at the event:
	+ Manager
	+ Staff
	+ Guest
* **Guest Details** (Guest Details, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant details about your guest.
* **Special Requirements** (Special Requirements, text, required/optional): Enter any special requirements or needs you have for the event.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Event Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Expected Arrival Time** (Expected Arrival Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time you expect to arrive at the event.

Note: Please fill in all required fields to ensure your RSVP is processed correctly.
