# emotional_intelligence_workshop_admission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emotional Intelligence Workshop admission form is designed to collect information from participants interested in attending the workshop. This form helps us understand your interest and preferences, ensuring you have a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields.
* Review your information carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main form where you will enter your interest in the Emotional Intelligence Workshop. Please enter your details in this field.
* Please be as detailed as possible.
* You can use this field to add any additional comments or questions.
### 2. **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name. If you prefer not to provide your name, you can leave this field blank.
* Please enter your name exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* We will use this to address you personally.
### 3. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): This field is for your contact email address. Please enter your email address.
* Make sure to enter your actual email address.
### 4. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): This field is for your contact phone number.
* Please enter your actual phone number.
* We will use this to contact you.
### 5. **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, select_one, optional): This field is for choosing your preferred time slot for the workshop.
* Select 'Yes' if you can attend the workshop at any time.
* Select 'No' if you cannot attend the workshop at any time.
### 6. **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or questions you have.
* Please use this space to share any thoughts or queries.
* We will review them and get back to you.
### 7. **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click on this button to submit your form.
* Review your form carefully before submission.
* Click only once to avoid duplicate submissions.
