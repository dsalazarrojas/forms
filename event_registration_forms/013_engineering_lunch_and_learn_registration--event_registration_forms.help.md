# Engineering Lunch And Learn Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Engineering Lunch And Learn Registration" form is used to collect information from attendees for the Engineering Lunch And Learn event. This form is intended to gather basic contact details and meal preferences of the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your correct information.
2. Select your preferred meal option(s) from the "Meal" dropdown menu.
3. Enter any additional notes about your registration in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name as you are employed by.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department name as you are part of.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to which the event organizers can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Meal** (`meal`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more meal options that you would like to have during the event. You can choose from "Breakfast", "Lunch", and "Dinner".
* **Meal Option 1**, **Meal Option 2**, **Meal Option 3**, **Meal Option 4**, **Meal Option 5**, **Meal Option 6**, **Meal Option 7** (`meal_option1`, `meal_option2`, `meal_option3`, `meal_option4`, `meal_option5`, `meal_option6`, `meal_option7`, `text`, required): Enter any additional details about your meal preferences.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
