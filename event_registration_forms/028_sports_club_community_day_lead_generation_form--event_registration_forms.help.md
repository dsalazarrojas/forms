# Sports Club Community Day Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sports Club Community Day Lead Generation Form" is a tool used to collect information from interested individuals about their interest in attending a Sports Club Community Day event, their personal details, and their interest in specific sports. The form will be used to track and follow-up on leads generated from this event, ensuring that interested individuals receive relevant information and support from the club's leadership team.

## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out the form, start by entering your name in the "Name" field.
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field, following the standard email format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
- If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
- Select a date and time that you are interested in attending the event in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
- If you are interested in a specific sport, select "Yes" in the "Sport" field.
- If you have interest in any of the available registration forms, select "Yes" in the corresponding "Registration Form" field.
- If you would like to receive follow-ups from the club, select "Yes" in the "Follow-ups" field.
- If you would like to track your lead, select "Yes" in the "Lead Tracking" field.
- You can add any additional notes about your interest or requirements in the "Notes" field.
- Finally, enter the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field (if applicable).
- Enter the path to the file that should be output in the "Output File" field (if applicable).
- Enter the category for your lead in the "Category" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Community Day Interest**: Enter your interest in attending the Sports Club Community Day event.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Event Date**: Select a date you are interested in attending the event.
* **Event Time**: Select the time you would like to attend the event.
* **Sport**: If you are interested in a specific sport, select "Yes".
* **Registration Form**: If you are interested in any of the available registration forms, select "Yes".
* **Follow-ups**: If you would like to receive follow-ups from the club, select "Yes".
* **Lead Tracking**: If you would like to track your lead, select "Yes".
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your interest or requirements.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to you by the club leadership (if applicable).
* **Output File**: Enter the path to the file that should be output (if applicable).
* **Category**: Enter the category for your lead (if applicable).
