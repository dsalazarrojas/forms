# Guest Behavior Analytics Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration data for the Guest Behavior Analytics Summit. The information collected will be used to tailor our event experience and follow up with you after the summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form data section, including your name and any other required information.
2. Select your role and team information, if applicable.
3. Provide any additional information you think will help us better understand your team and their needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form Data** (`Registration Form Data`, `text`, required): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
  This is the section where you provide general contact information and details about your presence at the summit.
- **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
  This is your first name.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
  This is your last name.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
  This is the email address we will use to contact you after the summit.
- **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
  This is the phone number we can use to contact you after the summit.
- **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name.
  This is the company you represent.
- **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
  This is your job title at your company.
- **Role** (`Role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role at the summit.
  - If you are a speaker, select John.
  - If you are a team lead, select Jane.
  - If you are not a speaker or team lead, select Other.
  This is your role at the summit.
- **Role Other** (`Role Other`, `text`, optional): If you selected Other in the Role field, enter your other role.
  This is a field to provide more details about your role.
- **Department** (`Department`, `text`, required): Enter your department name.
  This is your department name.
- **Job Function** (`Job Function`, `text`, required): Enter your job function.
  This is your job function.
- **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's name.
  This is your manager's name.
- **Manager Title** (`Manager Title`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's title.
  This is your manager's title.
- **Manager Phone Number** (`Manager Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
  This is your manager's phone number.
- **Manager Email** (`Manager Email`, `email`, required): Enter your manager's email address.
  This is your manager's email address.
- **Team Name** (`Team Name`, `text`, required): Enter your team name.
  This is your team name.
- **Team Lead** (`Team Lead`, `select_one`, required): Select your team lead role.
  - If you are a team lead, select Jane.
  - If you are not a team lead, select John.
  - If your team lead is not Jane or John, select Other.
  This is your team lead role.
- **Team Lead Other** (`Team Lead Other`, `text`, optional): If you selected Other in the Team Lead field, enter your other team lead role.
  This is a field to provide more details about your team lead role.
- **Team Department** (`Team Department`, `text`, required): Enter your team department name.
  This is your team department name.
- **Team Job Function** (`Team Job Function`, `text`, required): Enter your team job function.
  This is your team job function.
- **Team Manager Name** (`Team Manager Name`, `text`, required): Enter your team manager's name.
  This is your team manager's name.
- **Team Manager Title** (`Team Manager Title`, `text`, required): Enter your team manager's title.
  This is your team manager's title.
- **Team Manager Phone Number** (`Team Manager Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your team manager's phone number.
  This is your team manager's phone number.
- **Team Manager Email** (`Team Manager Email`, `email`, required): Enter your team manager's email address.
  This is your team manager's email address.
- **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information you think will help us better understand your team and their needs.
  This is a field for any additional notes or details. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, as they are marked with an *.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
- If you need to provide more information, use the additional comments section.
- If you are completing this form for multiple attendees, please submit each attendee separately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
