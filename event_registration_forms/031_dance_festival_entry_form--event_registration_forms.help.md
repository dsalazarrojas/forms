# Dance Festival Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dance Festival Entry Form is used to register attendees and their parents/guardians for the Dance Festival event. The form collects personal details such as names, email addresses, and phone numbers to ensure accurate registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the attendee information section (fields 1-4) with your personal details.
2. If applicable, fill out the parent information section (fields 5-7) with your parent's details.
3. Fill out the dance school name in field 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee First Name** (`attendee_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Attendee Last Name** (`attendee_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your parent's first name (if applicable).
* **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your parent's last name (if applicable).
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your parent's phone number (if applicable).
* **Dance School Name** (`dance_school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the dance school or organization you are representing (if any).
