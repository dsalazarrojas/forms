# Pet Safety Awareness Event Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Safety Awareness Event Consent Form is designed to gather information from participants about their pets and their concerns regarding pet safety. This form will help us better understand your needs and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information about your pet before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields ( Pet Information and Activity Risks).
3. You may also provide additional information about your pet and the events you are concerned about in the Event Information field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, required: false)
This field is optional, but if you choose to provide your consent, it will help us understand your willingness to participate in the event.

### 2. **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, required: false)
You can provide any additional information about the event you are concerned about, such as its date, location, or specific hazards you would like to address.

### 3. **Pet Information** (`pet_info`, select_multiple, required: true)
Please select all applicable options that describe your pet:
* **Pet ID Number**: Enter your pet's ID number if available.
* **Species**: Choose your pet's species (e.g., dog, cat, bird, etc.).
* **Age**: Select your pet's age.

### 4. **Activity Risks** (`activity_risks`, text, required: false)
If you have any concerns about specific activities that may pose risks to your pet, please describe them in this field.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your pet.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your participation and hope you find the event informative and helpful.
