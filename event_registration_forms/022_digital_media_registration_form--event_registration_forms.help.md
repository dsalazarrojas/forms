# digital_media_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for digital media events. It collects event details, contact information, and participant agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and what information is required.
2. Enter the necessary event details in the **Event Details** field.
3. Provide contact information for the participant in the **Participant Contact** field.
4. Select the correct **Type of Event** from the provided options.
5. Enter the **Event Location** where the event will take place.
6. Provide the participant's **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Date of Birth**.
7. Select whether the participant has media in the **Media** field and provide a specific **Media Type** if applicable.
8. Confirm that the participant has agreed to participate by selecting **Participant Agreement**.
9. If applicable, select the **Agreement Date** and confirm the **Agreement Status**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: Enter the details of the event, such as the title or description.
* **Participant Contact**: Enter the contact information for the participant, including their name, email, phone number, and any additional notes.
* **Type of Event**: Select one of the provided options to indicate the type of event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Name**: Enter the participant's name.
* **Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Media**: Select whether the participant has media, and provide a specific media type if applicable.
* **Media Type**: Select one of the provided options to indicate the type of media.
* **Participant Agreement**: Confirm that the participant has agreed to participate.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date on which the participant agreed to participate.
* **Agreement Status**: Confirm the status of the participant's agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is marked as optional, ensure that you complete it if it is relevant to the participant's registration.
* Review each field carefully to avoid errors or misunderstandings.
