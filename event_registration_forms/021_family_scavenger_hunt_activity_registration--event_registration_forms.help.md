# Family Scavenger Hunt Activity Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about their activity registration for the Family Scavenger Hunt event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the participant's age in the "Participant Age" field.
3. Enter the participant's contact information in the "Participant Contact" field.
4. Select the activity date in the "Activity Date" field.
5. Choose the activity time in the "Activity Time" field.
6. Enter the number of participants in the "Number of Participants" field.
7. Provide any additional notes about the participant in the "Participant Notes" field (optional).
8. Select the options for the activity in the "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3" fields.
9. Click on "Submit and Register" to submit the registration.
10. Enter the email address to confirm the registration in the "Email Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (1): Enter the name of the participant who is registering for the activity.
- **Participant Age** (2): Enter the age of the participant who is registering for the activity.
- **Participant Contact** (3): Enter the participant's contact information (phone number, address, etc.) in this field.
- **Activity Date** (4): Select the date of the activity from the calendar.
- **Activity Time** (5): Choose the time of the activity from the dropdown menu.
- **Number of Participants** (6): Enter the total number of participants who will be joining the activity.
- **Participant Notes** (7): Provide any additional information about the participant in this field (optional).
- **Option 1** (8): Select "Yes" if the participant is available for the activity.
- **Option 2** (9): Select multiple options that apply to the participant (e.g. "Yes, I will bring snacks", "Yes, I will provide music", etc.).
- **Option 3** (10): Select "Yes" if the participant is available for the activity.
- **Submit and Register** (11): Click this button to submit the participant's registration.
- **Email Confirmation** (12): Enter the participant's email address to confirm the registration.
