# Arts And Makers Market Fair Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for artisans and makers to apply for a booth at the Arts And Makers Market Fair. It helps us understand your business or vendor name, product category, and other relevant details for booth allocation and event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your business or vendor name in the "Business or Vendor Name" field.
3. Provide your email address and phone number in the "Email Address" and "Phone Number" fields.
4. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
5. Enter your city and state or province in the "City" and "State or Province" fields.
6. Enter your zip or postal code in the "Zip or Postal Code" field.
7. Select your product category from the available options in the "Product Category" field.
8. Provide a detailed description of your handmade or artisan products in the "Detailed Product Description" field.
9. If applicable, enter the number of years you have been making or selling your products in the "Years Making or Selling Your Products" field.
10. Attach high-quality photos of your products in the "Product Images Attached" field.
11. Select your preferred booth space size in the "Preferred Booth Space Size" field.
12. If you have any special requirements or requests, enter them in the "Additional Notes or Special Requests" field.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have participated in previous fairs.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have a business insurance certificate.
15. Select your preferred payment method for the booth fee.
16. Finally, read and agree to the fair terms and conditions by selecting "Yes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business or Vendor Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your business or vendor name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Street Address** (Text, Required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (Text, Required): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (Text, Required): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Product Category** (Select Multiple, Required): Select one or more categories that best describe your handmade or artisan products. Options include Handmade Jewelry, Pottery and Ceramics, Textiles and Fiber Arts, Woodcraft, Painting and Drawing, Photography, Metalwork, Mixed Media, and Home Decor.
* **Detailed Product Description** (Text, Required): Describe your handmade or artisan products in detail. Be as specific as possible so we can better understand your products.
* **Years Making or Selling Your Products** (Number, Optional): If you have any experience in making or selling your products, please let us know.
* **Product Images Attached** (Text, Optional): Attach high-quality photos of your handmade or artisan products.
* **Preferred Booth Space Size** (Select One, Required): Select a booth size that suits your needs. Options include Small - 4x4 feet, Medium - 6x6 feet, Large - 8x8 feet, and Double Booth - 10x10 feet.
* **Booth Fee** (Number, Optional): If you are unsure of the booth fee, you can leave this field blank.
* **Electricity or Other Utilities Needed** (Select One, Optional): If you need electricity or other utilities, select "Yes" to let us know.
* **Fair Date You Are Applying For** (Date, Required): Select the fair date you are applying for.
* **Have You Participated in Previous Fairs** (Select One, Optional): If you have participated in previous fairs, select "Yes" or "Once".
* **Business Insurance Certificate Available** (Select One, Optional): If you have a business insurance certificate, select "Yes".
* **Preferred Payment Method for Booth Fee** (Select One, Optional): Select your preferred payment method for the booth fee. Options include Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, and PayPal.
* **I Agree to Fair Terms and Conditions** (Select One, Required): Please read and agree to the fair terms and conditions by selecting "Yes".
* **Additional Notes or Special Requests** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional notes or special requests, enter them here.
