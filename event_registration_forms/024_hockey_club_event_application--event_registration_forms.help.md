# Hockey Club Event Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for events organized by the hockey club. Please fill out this form to provide your information and details about your event registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Player Details" page to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter your player details in the "Player Details" page.
3. Proceed to the "Applicant" page and fill out your contact information.
4. Enter your contact details in the "Applicant" page.
5. Proceed to the "Player Age" page and fill out your age and team size.
6. Enter your age and team size on the "Player Age" page.
7. Proceed to the "Emergency Contact" page and fill out your emergency contact information.
8. Enter your emergency contact information on the emergency contact page.
9. Finally, review and confirm your application before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, not required): Enter your player details, including any relevant information about your playing experience and skills.
* **Applicant** (`player_applicant`, text, not required): Enter your name as it will be displayed for the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, not required): Enter the number of players in your team.
* **Player Age** (`player_age`, number, not required): Enter the age of each player in your team.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, not required): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, not required): Enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, not required): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, not required): Enter any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the event.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it will be displayed for the event.
* **Age** (`age`, number, not required): This field appears to be a repeat of "Player Age". Please disregard this field as it is likely a duplicate.

Note: The fields marked as "not required" can be skipped, but please ensure that all required fields are completed for a successful application submission. 

## Tips

- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting your application.
- Ensure that you have permission from all team members to register for the event.
- Be prepared to provide additional information or clarification if required during the registration process.
