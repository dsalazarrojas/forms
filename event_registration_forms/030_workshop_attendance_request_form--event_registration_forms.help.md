# Workshop Attendance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Attendance Request Form is used to register attendance for a workshop or event. It collects information about the event and the attendee to help with planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the workshop or event you wish to attend from the list of options.
2. Fill in your details, including your name, email address, phone number, and address.
3. Choose a tool you will be using during the event from the list of options.
4. Confirm your attendance by selecting either "I confirm I will attend", "I will not attend", or "Maybe".
5. If you have any additional comments or information, you can add them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Form ID** (1): Enter the unique ID of the workshop or event you are registering for.
* **Event Name** (2): Enter the name of the workshop or event you are registering for.
* **Event Start Date** (3): Enter the start date of the workshop or event.
* **Event Start Time** (4): Enter the start time of the workshop or event.
* **Event End Date** (5): Enter the end date of the workshop or event.
* **Event End Time** (6): Enter the end time of the workshop or event.
* **Event Location** (7): Enter the location of the workshop or event.
* **Attendee Name** (8): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or identification document.
* **Attendee Email** (9): Enter your email address.
* **Attendee Phone** (10): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Attendee Address** (11): Enter your physical address (optional).
* **Assigned Tool** (12): Select the tool you will be using during the event.
* **Confirm Attendance** (13): Select how you will be attending the event:
	+ I confirm I will attend
	+ I will not attend
	+ Maybe
* **Additional Comments** (14): Add any additional comments or information you want to provide.
* **Form ID 2** (15): Enter a unique ID for the form (only required for internal use).
* **Event Name 2** (16): Enter the name of the second event (if applicable).
* **Event Details** (17): Add any additional information about the event (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be accurate when entering your contact information.
* Choose the correct tool you will be using during the event.
* Confirm your attendance to help with event planning and organization.
