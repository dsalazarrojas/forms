# Fair Supply Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Fair Supply Exchange Registration Form. The form is designed to collect information from participants about their supply offers and needs, as well as their contact and participant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields: "Participant Registration", "Supply Offers", "Supply Needs", "Organization Details", "Contact Details", and "Participant Info".
2. Provide your supply offers and needs in the text fields.
3. Fill in the "Participant Registration" field with any relevant details.
4. Make sure to include accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Registration
* **Participant Registration** (`Participant Registration`, text, required): A field to enter any relevant details about your registration as a participant.
### Supply Offers
* **Supply Offers** (`Supply Offers`, text, required): A field to list any supply offers you are making or have available.
### Supply Needs
* **Supply Needs** (`Supply Needs`, text, required): A field to list any supply needs you have.
### Organization Details
* **Organization Details** (`Organization Details`, text, required): A field to provide information about the organization you represent.
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required): A field to enter your contact information.
### Participant Info
* **Participant Info** (`Participant Info`, text, optional): A field to enter any additional participant information.
### Participant Skills
* **Participant Skills** (`Participant Skills`, text, optional): A field to enter any relevant participant skills or experience.
### Participant Needs
* **Participant Needs** (`Participant Needs`, text, optional): A field to list any participant needs.
### Tips
* Make sure to review and update your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us.

This guide is intended to be a quick and easy reference for participants to complete the form accurately. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
