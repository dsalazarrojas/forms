# Youth Candy Making Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians to register their child for a youth candy making workshop or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email to contact you.
2. Provide your child's name and age.
3. Enter your child's phone number.
4. Provide the emergency contact person's name and phone number.
5. Enter the emergency contact person's email.
6. Share any allergy information relevant to your child.
7. Choose your preferred workshop schedule (morning, afternoon, or full day).
8. Confirm that you have read and understood the safety information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (text): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Workshop Date** (date): Select the date of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Workshop Time** (time): Select the time of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Student Name** (text): Enter your child's name.
* **Student Age** (number): Enter your child's age.
* **Student Phone** (text): Enter your child's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Allergy Information** (text): Share any allergy information relevant to your child.
* **Safety Agreement** (select_one): Choose "Safety Information Provided" to confirm that you have read and understood the safety information.
* **Schedule Request** (select_multiple): Select the workshop schedule that suits your needs, such as morning or afternoon.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your email address for accuracy.
- Be accurate when providing allergy information to ensure we can accommodate your child's needs.
- Choose the workshop schedule that works best for you and your child.
- Review the safety information carefully before confirming that you have understood it.
