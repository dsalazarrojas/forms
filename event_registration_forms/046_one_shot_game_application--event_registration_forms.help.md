# one_shot_game_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The one-shot game application form is used to collect player information and preferences for a one-shot game event. It is intended for players to apply to participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the player information section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your game preferences from the options provided.
3. Enter any additional notes or character information in the character notes field.
4. Upload your character sheet if required.
5. Select your game experience level.
6. Choose your availability for the event.
7. Select your role in the game (player, game master, or game master assistant).
8. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Application**: This section is for entering your player information, including your name, email, and phone number.
  * Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the game event.
  * Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates about the game event.
  * Enter your phone number to provide additional contact information.
* **Game Preferences**: Select the game preferences that align with your interests and experience.
  * Choose from the options provided to indicate your game preferences.
* **Character**: This field is for entering any additional character information.
  * Enter any additional notes or details about your character.
* **Character Notes**: This field is for entering any additional notes or information about your character.
  * Enter any additional notes or details about your character.
* **Character Sheet**: If required, upload your character sheet.
  * Upload your character sheet as a file attachment.
* **Game Experience**: Select your level of game experience.
  * Choose from the options provided to indicate your level of game experience.
* **Availability**: Enter your availability for the event.
  * Choose from the options provided to indicate your availability.
* **Role**: Select your role in the game (player, game master, or game master assistant).
  * Choose from the options provided to indicate your role.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates about the game event.
  * Enter your email address to provide additional contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to provide additional contact information.
  * Enter your phone number to receive calls or texts about the game event.
* **Notes**: This field is for entering any additional notes or information.
  * Enter any additional notes or details about your application.
* **Submitted On**: This field is auto-filled with the date and time the form was submitted.
  * This field is not editable.
* **Submitter**: This field is auto-filled with your name.
  * This field is not editable.
* **Submitter Email**: This field is auto-filled with your email address.
  * This field is not editable.
* **Submitter Phone**: This field is auto-filled with your phone number.
  * This field is not editable.
* **Game Master**: This field is for entering the game master's name.
  * Enter the name of the game master or staff member who will be running the game event.
* **Game Master Email**: This field is for entering the game master's email address.
  * Enter the email address of the game master or staff member who will be running the game event.
* **Game Master Phone**: This field is for entering the game master's phone number.
  * Enter the phone number of the game master or staff member who will be running the game event.
* **Event Id**: This field is for entering the event ID.
  * Enter the ID of the event you are applying to.
* **Form Id**: This field is for entering the form ID.
  * Enter the ID of the form you are filling out.
* **User Id**: This field is for entering the user ID.
  * Enter your unique user ID.
