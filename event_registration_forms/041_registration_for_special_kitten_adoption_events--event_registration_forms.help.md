# Registration For Special Kitten Adoption Events - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users for a registration process related to special kitten adoption events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Event Name` field with the name of the event you are registering for.
2. Enter your `Name` in the field provided.
3. Provide your `Email` address to allow us to contact you regarding the event.
4. Enter your `Contact Info` to ensure we can reach out to you about the event.
5. Select the `Event Date` in the format MM/dd.
6. Choose the `Event Time` in 12-hour format (e.g., "12:00 PM" or "02:30 PM").
7. Enter the `Event Location` where the event will take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name
• **Event Name**: `event_name` (text, optional) - Enter the name of the event you are registering for.

### Name
• **Name**: `name` (text, optional) - Enter your full name to be associated with your registration.

### Email
• **Email**: `email` (email, optional) - Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Contact Info
• **Contact Info**: `contact` (text, optional) - Enter any additional contact information for the event.

### Event Date
• **Event Date**: `event_date` (date, optional) - Choose the date of the event in MM/dd format (e.g., 12/24 for December 24th).

### Event Time
• **Event Time**: `event_time` (time, optional) - Select the time of the event in 12-hour format (e.g., "12:00 PM" or "02:30 PM").

### Event Location
• **Event Location**: `event_location` (text, optional) - Enter the location where the event will take place.

## Tips
- Ensure that you are registering for a legitimate event.
- Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
- If you need to make any modifications or corrections, please contact us using the provided contact information.
