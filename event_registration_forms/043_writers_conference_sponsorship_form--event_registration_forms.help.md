# Writers' Conference Sponsorship Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for those who wish to sponsor the writers' conference. 

## How To Complete This Form

- **Read through the form carefully**: Please make sure to complete all the necessary fields and follow the instructions in this help guide.

- **Choose your sponsorship package**: Select one of the available packages from the dropdown menu. 

- **Enter your contact information**: This includes entering your email and phone number.

- **Provide your message**: If you have any message for the conference organizers, please write it in the "Sponsor Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsorship Application**: Enter a brief description of your company or your self-introduction. This field is not required.
* **Sponsor Details**: Enter your company's name and any other details that you feel are necessary to know about you as a sponsor.
* **Select a Package**: Choose one of the available packages by selecting the corresponding option from the dropdown menu. Please ensure that you have reviewed the packages and their requirements before selecting one.
* **Sponsorship Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to sponsor for the conference. This field is not required.
* **Recognition Preferences**: Let us know about your recognition preferences such as if you want your name to be displayed on the sponsor list, on our website, or otherwise.
* **Sponsorship Level**: Select the level of sponsorship you are providing (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
* **Sponsor Message**: If you have any other message for the conference organizers, please write it in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
Note: This form is a text input, please enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX or XXX XXX XXXX.
