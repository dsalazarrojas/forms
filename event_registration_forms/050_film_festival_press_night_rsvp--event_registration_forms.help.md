# Film Festival Press Night RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Festival Press Night RSVP form is a mandatory registration form for media guests attending the Press Night event. It helps us track your attendance, gather information about your preferences, and provide you with essential event details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select your media confirmation status:
	* If you are able to confirm your attendance, select "True". If you need to cancel or cannot attend, select "False".
2. Enter your guest name as you would like it to be displayed in event materials and communication.
3. Provide your guest phone number for us to reach out with important event information.
4. Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the event.
5. If you are attending with a photo shoot or other media coverage, please indicate your consent to be photographed.
6. Choose your estimated time of arrival at the event.
7. Enter the event ID provided to you by festival organizers.
8. Confirm your RSVP status:
	* If you are attending, select "True". If you are not attending, select "False".
9. Leave any additional comments about your event preferences or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Confirmation** (`Media Confirmation`, select_one): Confirm your media status. Choose "True" if you are attending for media coverage and "False" otherwise.
* **Guest Name** (`Guest Name`, text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Guest Phone** (`Guest Phone`, text): Enter your phone number for important event information.
* **Email** (`Email`, email): Enter your email address to receive event updates and notifications.
* **Photo Consent** (`Photo Consent`, select_one): Indicate your consent for a photo shoot. Choose "True" if you consent and "False" otherwise.
* **Guest Attendance** (`Guest Attendance`, time): Choose your estimated time of arrival at the event.
* **Event ID** (`Event ID`, text): Enter the event ID provided to you by festival organizers.
* **RSVP** (`RSVP`, select_one): Confirm your attendance. Choose "True" if you are attending and "False" otherwise.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text): Enter any additional comments or requests regarding your event preferences.
