# intern_gathering_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "intern_gathering_attendance_form" is a registration form for collecting information about an internal gathering or event within a company. This form is used to gather details about the event, attendance, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the necessary information for each field.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event or gathering.
* **Attendance Info** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide information about the number of attendees, their names, and any other relevant details.
* **Event Attendance** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the number of attendees and any other relevant information.
* **Event Outcome** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): Describe the outcome or results of the event.
* **Intern Details** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name and email of the intern attending the event.
* **Manager Details** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name and email of the manager or person in charge of the event.
* **Contact Info** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Event Location** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Date** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **Manager Name** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the manager or person in charge of the event.
* **Manager Email** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the email of the manager or person in charge of the event.
* **Intern Name** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the intern attending the event.
* **Intern Email** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the email of the intern attending the event.
* **Contact Number** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the company.
* **Company Email** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the email of the company.
* **Company Address** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the address of the company.
* **Job Title** (`id: 19`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your department.
* **Company Phone** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the company.
* **Manager Phone** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the manager or person in charge of the event.
* **Attendance Date** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the date of attendance.
* **Form Submit** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, required: false): This is a submit button, click it to submit your form.

## Tips
