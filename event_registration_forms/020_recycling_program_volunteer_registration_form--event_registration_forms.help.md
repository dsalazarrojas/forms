# Recycling Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recycling Program Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from volunteers who wish to participate in recycling events or programs. This form helps us to manage volunteer registrations and ensures that we have the necessary contact and event details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Volunteer Information**:
	* Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
	* Enter your email address for easy contact and communication.
	* Enter your phone number for quick communication.
2. Choose your **Event Details**:
	* Indicate if you are available to participate in events (Yes/No).
	* Select any groups you'd like to be part of (e.g., cleanup crew, educational workshops).
3. Add any **Additional Notes**:
	* Use this section to share any relevant information about your availability or needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information**:
	+ **Volunteers Name**: A text field to enter your name.
	+ **Volunteers Email**: A field to enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
	+ **Volunteers Phone**: A field to enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Event Details**: 
	+ **Volunteers Location**: Choose "Yes" if you are available to participate in events at different locations, or "No" if you have specific location preferences.
	+ **Volunteers Group**: Select which groups you'd like to be part of, such as cleanup crew or educational workshops.
* **Additional Notes**: A text field to share any relevant information about your availability or needs.
* **Event Details (Optional)**: If you have any specific events you're interested in participating in, you can mention them here.
