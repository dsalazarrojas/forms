# Executive Directors Meeting Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register executive attendees for an upcoming meeting. It captures information about the attendee's organization, position, and meeting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization Name" field.
2. Provide your executive attendee's name in the "Executive Attendee Name" field.
3. Enter their position in the "Executive Attendee Position" field.
4. Specify the meeting venue in the "Meeting Venue" field.
5. Enter the meeting location in the "Meeting Location" field.
6. Indicate if you will attend the meeting ("Yes" or "No") in the "Attendance Type" field.
7. Select the date of attendance using the calendar picker in the "Attendance Date" field.
8. Choose the start and end times of the meeting in the "Meeting Start Time" and "Meeting End Time" fields, respectively.
9. Provide any special requirements for the meeting in the "Special Requirements" field (optional).
10. Add any additional comments about your attendance in the "Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (text, required: false)**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Executive Attendee Name (text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the executive attendee.
* **Executive Attendee Position (text, required: false)**: Enter the position of the executive attendee.
* **Meeting Venue (text, required: false)**: Enter the location of the meeting (e.g., conference room).
* **Meeting Location (text, required: false)**: Enter the physical location of the meeting (e.g., building name).
* **Attendance Type (select_one, required: false)**: Choose "Yes" if you will attend the meeting, and "No" otherwise.
* **Attendance Date (date, required: false)**: Select the date of the meeting using the calendar picker.
* **Meeting Start Time (time, required: false)**: Choose the start time of the meeting.
* **Meeting End Time (time, required: false)**: Choose the end time of the meeting.
* **Special Requirements (text, required: false)**: Enter any special requirements for the meeting (e.g., accessibility needs).
* **Comments (text, required: false)**: Add any additional comments about your attendance.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have registered to attend the meeting before completing this form.
* If you have any special requirements, please specify them in the "Special Requirements" field.
* You can add any additional comments about your attendance in the "Comments" field.
