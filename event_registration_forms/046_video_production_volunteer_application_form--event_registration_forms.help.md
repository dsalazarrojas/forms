# Video Production Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for video production events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in your **Volunteer Name** in the "Volunteer Name" field. This is a required field.
* Enter your **Contact Email** in the "Contact Email" field. This will be used to reach out to you with further instructions or questions.
* Select your **Availability Start Date** in the "Availability Start" field. This is the date you are available to start volunteering.
* Select your **Availability End Date** in the "Availability End" field. This is the date you are available to end volunteering.
* Provide more details about your **Availability Details** in the "Availability Details" field. This can include any relevant information about your schedule or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on any event materials.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Availability Start**: Select the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **Availability End**: Select the date you are available to end volunteering.
* **Availability Details**: Explain any relevant details about your schedule, work experience, or skills that would be useful for the video production team to know.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when entering your availability dates.
* If you have any scheduling conflicts or concerns, please mention them in the "Availability Details" field.
* If you have any relevant skills or experience, such as video editing or camera operation, please mention them in the "Availability Details" field as well.
* This form is for volunteer information only and should not be used for any other purpose.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form's creators at form@gic.mx.
