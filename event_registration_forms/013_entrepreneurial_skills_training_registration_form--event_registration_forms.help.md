# Entrepreneurial Skills Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entrepreneurial Skills Training Registration Form is designed to gather registration information from participants in an Entrepreneurial Skills Training program. This form helps us understand who is participating, what they aim to achieve, and what they need to know to make the most of the training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the session name you wish to register for in the "Session Name" field.
2. If you are representing an organization, please enter the name of your organization in the "Organization" field.
3. Provide your name as you wish to be referred to during and after the training session in the "Participant Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Name (session_name)
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, required: no): Enter the name of the training session you wish to register for.

### Organization (organization)
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: no): If you are representing an organization, enter the name of your organization.

### Participant Name (participant_name)
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: no): Enter your name as you wish to be referred to during and after the training session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your participant name is spelled correctly to avoid any confusion.
* If you are representing an organization, ensure that the organization name is clear and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
