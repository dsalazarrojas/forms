# family_photography_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for family photography sessions. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information to help us tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details in the "Client Details" field.
2. Enter the date and time of your preferred photography session in the "Date And Time" field.
3. List your family members who will be participating in the session in the "Family Members" field.
4. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
5. Mention any special requests you have for the session in the "Special Requests" field.
6. Indicate your photography preferences in the "Photography Preferences" field.
7. Describe the event details in the "Event Details" field.
8. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contacts" field.
9. If you have any medical conditions that may impact the session, please mention them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
10. List any additional requests in the "Additional Requests" field.
11. Provide details about the photo session in the "Photo Session" field.
12. Give us your feedback about the session in the "Session Feedback" fields.
13. Provide any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" fields.
14. Sign your name in the "Client Signature" and "Client Signature 2" fields to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
15. Sign the "Photographer Signature" and "Photographer Signature 2" fields to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
16. Repeat the same steps for the second photo session in the "Photo Session 2" and "Session Details 2" fields.
17. Give your feedback about the second session in the "Session Feedback 2" fields.
18. Provide any additional comments in the "Additional Comments 2" fields.
19. Sign your name in the "Client Signature 2" field to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
20. Sign the "Photographer Signature 2" field to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
