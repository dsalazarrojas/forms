# match_day_experience_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about their experience on match day. This feedback will help improve future events and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Match Day Experiences" section.
2. Rate your overall experience on match day.
3. Select which team you are associated with.
4. Select the date and time of the match.
5. Select the venue where the match took place.
6. Select which event you attended.
7. Enter your comments and contact information for follow-up.
8. Rate your experience on a scale and provide additional comments.
9. Select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Match Day Experiences* (1)**: This field requires you to rate your overall experience on match day on a scale (1-5).
* **Which Team* (2)**: Select the team you are associated with to provide feedback specific to your team.
* **Match Date (3)**: Enter the date of the match you attended. This field is optional but recommended for accurate feedback.
* **Start Time (4)**: Enter the start time of the match. This field is optional but recommended for accurate feedback.
* **End Time (5)**: Enter the end time of the match. This field is optional but recommended for accurate feedback.
* **Venue (6)**: Select the venue where the match took place.
* **Event (7)**: Select the event you attended.
* **Comments (8)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your experience.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address for follow-up.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number for follow-up.
* **Match Day Experience Rating (11)**: Rate your experience on a scale (1-5) and provide additional comments.
* **Rating Comments (12)**: Enter any additional comments about your rating.
* **Submit (13)**: Select "Yes" to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields (marked with an asterisk *) to ensure successful submission of the form.
- Use the optional fields to provide additional information about your experience.
- Be as specific as possible when providing comments and feedback to help improve future events and experiences.
