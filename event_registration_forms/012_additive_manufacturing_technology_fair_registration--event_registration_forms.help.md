# Additive Manufacturing Technology Fair Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is intended to assist you in completing the Additive Manufacturing Technology Fair Registration form. The form collects information necessary for the event organizers to manage attendance, networking, and communication with attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your personal details: 
   - Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
   - Provide your valid email address for event communications.
   - If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you if needed.
2. Next, select your industry sector and registration type.
   - Choose from Aerospace, Automotive, Medical/Healthcare, Education, Manufacturing, Research, Consumer Products, or Other.
   - Select Professional, Student, Academic, Vendor, Media, or Other for your registration type.
3. Indicate which days you plan to attend.
   - Select one or multiple days from the options.
4. Indicate which additive manufacturing technologies interest you.
   - Select one or multiple technologies from FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling), SLA/SLS (Stereolithography/Selective Laser Sintering), Metal 3D Printing, Bioprinting, Large Format Printing, Materials Development, or Software Solutions.
5. Indicate which applications interest you.
   - Select one or multiple applications from Prototyping, Production Parts, Medical Devices, Aerospace Components, Art and Design, Education, or Research.
6. Indicate which networking opportunities interest you.
   - Select one or multiple from Industry Professionals, Researchers, Startups, Investors, Service Providers, Equipment Manufacturers, or Material Suppliers.
7. Indicate which workshops you would like to attend.
   - Select one or multiple from Introduction to AM Technologies, Advanced Materials, Design for Additive Manufacturing, Quality Control, Post-Processing Techniques, Business Applications, or None.
8. If you have any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, indicate them in the provided text fields.
9. If you have any special requests, indicate them in the provided text field.
10. Indicate whether you grant permission to be contacted by us for future events and updates.
11. Indicate whether you are interested in exhibiting at future fairs.
12. Indicate your experience with attending similar events.
13. Indicate what you hope to gain from attending this event.
14. Finally, enter your expected date of attendance.
15. End by providing an electronic signature to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (attendee_full_name, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (attendee_email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use for event communications.
* **Phone Number** (attendee_phone, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Company/Organization** (company_organization, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, optional): Enter your professional title.
* **Industry Sector** (industry_sector, select_one, required): Choose your industry sector from the provided options.
* **Registration Type** (registration_type, select_one, required): Select your registration type from the provided options.
* **Days Planning to Attend** (attendance_days, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple days you plan to attend.
* **Technology Interests** (technology_interests, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple additive manufacturing technologies that interest you.
* **Application Interests** (application_interests, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple applications that interest you.
* **Networking Interests** (networking_interests, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple networking opportunities that interest you.
* **Workshop Interests** (workshop_interests, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple workshops you would like to attend.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (dietary_restrictions, text, optional): If you have dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Accessibility Needs** (accessibility_needs, text, optional): If you have accessibility requirements, enter them here.
* **Special Requests** (special_requests, text, optional): Enter any special requests or accommodations you need.
* **Marketing Consent** (marketing_consent, select_one, optional): Choose whether we can contact you for future events and updates.
* **Interest in Exhibiting** (exhibit_interest, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are interested in exhibiting at future fairs.
* **Previous Attendance** (previous_attendance, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have attended similar events before.
* **Expected Outcomes** (expected_outcomes, text, optional): Enter what you hope to gain from attending this event.
* **Registration Date** (registration_date, date, required): Enter the date of your registration.
* **Attendee Signature** (attendee_signature, text, required): Provide an electronic signature to confirm your registration.

Note: The provided help guide assumes the form's fields and options are as per the given YAML. If any fields or options change in the YAML, this guide might need to be updated.
