# crawfish_preorder_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in preordering crawfish.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields. Start by filling out the fields on the first page, then proceed to the next page, and so on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information (`customer_info`)
* **Customer Information**: Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
### Order Details (`order_details`)
* **Order Details**: Enter the details of the crawfish you are interested in preordering, including any specifications or special requests.
### Shipping Information (`shipping_info`)
* **Shipping Information**: Enter your shipping address, including your name, street address, city, state, and zip code.
### Payment Information (`payment_info`)
* **Payment Information**: Enter your payment information, including your payment method and any other relevant details.
### Customer Information 2 (`customer_info_2`)
* **Customer Information 2**: This field is intended for additional customer information, such as any notes or comments.
### Order Details 2 (`order_details_2`)
* **Order Details 2**: This field is intended for additional order details, such as any special requests or specifications.
### Shipping Information 2 (`shipping_info_2`)
* **Shipping Information 2**: This field is intended for additional shipping information, such as any special shipping requests or instructions.
### Payment Information 2 (`payment_info_2`)
* **Payment Information 2**: This field is intended for additional payment information, such as any payment terms or conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your preorder.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.
