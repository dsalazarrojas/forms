# Community Outreach Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for community outreach training sessions and provide information about the registrant's background, interests, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Register" button to begin the registration process.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the relevant options for each field.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (\*)** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (\*)** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address \(*)** (`registrant_email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number \*)** (`registrant_phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Have you participated in outreach programs before? \(*)** (`previous_outreach`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you have participated in outreach programs before, or "False" otherwise.
* **Details of Previous Programs \**)**: If you answered "True" to the previous question, please provide details of the programs you participated in.
* **Choose your Training Session Date \(*)** (`session_selection`, `select_one`, required): Select the date of the training session you would like to attend.
* **Your Current Experience Level \(*)** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current level of experience.
* **What areas of outreach are you interested in? \*)** (`areas_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all areas of outreach that interest you.
* **Why are you interested in this training?** (`training_motivation`, `text`, required): Please describe your motivation for attending the training.
* **Any Dietary Requirements?** (`dietary_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary requirements, please specify them.
* **Do you require accessibility accommodations?** (`accessibility_needs_check`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you require accessibility accommodations, or "False" otherwise.
* **Please Specify Accommodations Needed** (`accessibility_details`, `text`, optional): If you selected "True" to the previous question, please specify the accommodations you need.
* **Registration Confirmation**: You will receive a confirmation email shortly after submitting this form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that we can prepare for the training session correctly.
* If you have any issues with submitting the form, please contact us for assistance.
* We look forward to welcoming you to the training session!
