# Trade Show Booth Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trade Show Booth Waiver Form is a required document that exhibitors need to complete to register for a trade show. It gathers information about the exhibitor and their booth details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your booth number in the "Booth Number" field.
3. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
4. Enter your first name and last name in the "Contact First Name" and "Contact Last Name" fields, respectively.
5. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booth Number**: A unique number assigned to your trade show booth.
* **Company Name**: The name of the company exhibiting at the trade show.
* **Contact First Name**: The first name of the contact person responsible for your trade show booth.
* **Contact Last Name**: The last name of the contact person responsible for your trade show booth.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person responsible for your trade show booth, in the format of `name@domain.com`. This field is used to contact you after the form is submitted.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number of the contact person responsible for your trade show booth.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your booth number correctly to avoid any issues with your booth assignment.
- Use a company name that is recognized by the trade show organizers.
- Use a valid email address that you check regularly for updates and communications.
- Use a phone number that you can be reached at after the form is submitted.
