# Scrabble Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scrabble Event Registration form is designed to collect participant information and event details for events, tournaments, or gatherings. This form is likely the first step in the registration process and provides a comprehensive overview of the participant's profile and event specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** for your participant information.
2. Provide the **Event Name** and **Event Date** for the event you are registering for.
3. Choose your **Participant Status** as **Registered** or **Unregistered**.
4. Select the **Payment Method** and **Payment Status** according to your payment details.
5. Indicate the **Event Type** as **Tournament**, **Club Event**, or **Community Night**.
6. Select the **Event Organizer** as **Jotform**, **Local Club**, or **Tournament Organizer**.
7. Optionally, add any additional **Participant Details** in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Participant Info)**: Provide your basic contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to appear on any event materials.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Tournament Information**: Type in any additional details about the tournament you are participating in.
* **Event Name**: Provide the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Participant Status**: Select whether you are registered or not for the event.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the method of payment you are using for this event (e.g., Payment 1, Payment 2).
* **Payment Status**: Indicate if you have made a payment for this event.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount paid for this event.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are participating in (e.g., tournament, club, community night).
* **Event Organizer**: Choose who is organizing this event (e.g., Jotform, Local Club, Tournament Organizer).
* **Participant Details**: Add any additional participant information or notes here.

## Tips

* Double-check your participant information for accuracy.
* Ensure you select the correct payment method and status.
* Verify the event details (name, date, time) to ensure you are registering for the correct event.
* Be clear and concise in your participant details for better processing and organization.
