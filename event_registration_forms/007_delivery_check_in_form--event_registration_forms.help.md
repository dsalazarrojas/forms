### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select the relevant check-in information from the list on the first page.
2.  Enter the date of the delivery in the "Date" field.
3.  Enter the time of the delivery in the "Time" field.
4.  Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **option 1**: Select the relevant check-in information from the list on the page.
*   **Date**: The date of the delivery, in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
*   **Time**: The time of the delivery, in the format 12-hour clock.
*   **Name**: Your name as the individual responsible for the delivery.
*   **Phone**: Your contact phone number for delivery-related inquiries.
*   **Email**: Your email address for delivery-related correspondence.
