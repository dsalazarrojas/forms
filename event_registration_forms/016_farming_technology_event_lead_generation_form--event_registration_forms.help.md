# Farming Technology Event Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about Farming Technology events for lead generation. It is used to gather necessary details about the event, such as location, date, and event description, to help generate leads for potential attendees and participants.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with accurate information about your event.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (none are currently required, but this may change in the future).
- Use the event description field to provide a brief summary of your event.
- If you have any questions, contact us at [insert contact information].

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Lead Details**: This field is where you can describe your event in a few words.
  - This is the main description of your event.
  - Try to be as concise as possible while still conveying the essential details of your event.
- **Farm Name**: This is the name of the farm related to the event.
  - For example, if you are hosting an event on Farm X, this is where you would enter Farm X.
  - This field is not required, but it helps with filtering and categorization of events.
- **Event Name**: This is the name of your event, e.g., "Farming Technology Conference 2023".
  - This field is not required, but it helps with event branding and identification.
- **Event Description**: This field is similar to Event Lead Details, but more in-depth and detailed description of your event.
  - Use this field to provide more information about your event.
- **Event Date**: This is the date of your event in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
  - Example: 2024-02-15.
  - Make sure to enter the correct date to avoid errors in lead generation.
- **Event Time**: This is the time of your event.
  - Enter the time in 24-hour format, i.e., 14:00 for 2:00 PM.
  - This field is not required but recommended for accurate scheduling.
- **Event Location**: This is the location of your event.
  - Enter the name of the venue or a brief description of the location.
  - This field helps with location-based filtering and categorization.
- **Company**: This is the name of the company related to the event.
  - If your event is not related to a company, leave this field blank.
- **Job Title**: This is the job title of the event organizer or a contact person.
  - Enter the job title to help with contact and follow-up communications.
- **Phone**: This is the phone number of the event organizer or a contact person.
  - Enter a valid phone number for easy contact and follow-up.
- **Email**: This is the email address of the event organizer or a contact person.
  - Enter a valid email address for easy contact and follow-up.
- **LinkedIn URL**: This is the LinkedIn profile URL of the event organizer or a contact person.
  - Enter the URL if you have a LinkedIn profile, it will help with networking and lead generation.
- **LinkedIn URL**: This is the LinkedIn URL of the event organizer or a contact person.
  - Enter the URL if you have a LinkedIn profile, it will help with networking and lead generation.
