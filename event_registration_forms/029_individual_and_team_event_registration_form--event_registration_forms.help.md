# Individual And Team Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals and teams participating in events. This form is designed to capture essential details, such as participant information, event details, and team specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information (fields 3, 4, and 5).
2. If applicable, provide team member information (fields 7, 8, and 9).
3. Enter emergency contact information (fields 11, 12, and 13).
4. Select the event date and time (fields 14 and 15).
5. Choose the event location (field 16).
6. Provide a brief event description (field 17).
7. Enter team information (fields 18, 19, and 20).
8. Select team leagues and practices (fields 21, 22, and 23).
9. Enter event organizer and their email (fields 24 and 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (3)**: Your name or the name of the person registering.
* **Email (4)**: Your email address for communication and contact purposes.
* **Participant Phone (5)**: Your or team member's phone number for emergency contact.
* **Participant Information (2)**: Briefly describe or provide details about the participant(s).
* **Team Member Info (6)**: Enter team member details, if applicable.
* **Team Member Name (7)**: Name of team member 1.
* **Team Member Email (8)**: Email address of team member 1.
* **Team Member Phone (9)**: Phone number of team member 1.
* **Emergency Contact Information (10)**: Provide details of the emergency contact person or contact method.
* **Emergency Contact Name (11)**: Name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (12)**: Phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Event Info (13)**: Briefly describe the purpose or nature of the event.
* **Event Date (14)**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time (15)**: Select the time of the event.
* **Event Location (16)**: Choose the location of the event.
* **Event Description (17)**: Briefly describe the event.
* **Team Information (18)**: Enter team details, such as team size and experience.
* **Team Name (19)**: Name of the team.
* **Team Size (20)**: Size of the team.
* **Team Experience (21)**: Briefly describe the team's experience.
* **Team League (22)**: Select the leagues the team is part of.
* **Team Practice (23)**: Select the practices the team has.
* **Team League Practice (24)**: Select the leagues and practices the team is part of.
* **Event Organizer (25)**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email (26)**: Email address of the event organizer.
