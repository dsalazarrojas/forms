# Project Governance Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering project certification information for project participants. It helps project teams ensure that all team members with certification have the required documents and training.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start.
2. Fill in the required fields with your details.
3. Choose your participant type and certification type accordingly.
4. Provide any additional information about your participant type if needed.
5. Set your certification date and status.
6. If applicable, fill in your certification expiration date.
7. Review all fields before submitting for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project-name** (Text): Enter the name of the project you are registering for.
* **participant-name** (Text): Enter your name as a participant in the project.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **participant-info** (Text): Provide any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **participant-type** (Select Multiple): Select all applicable participant types that apply to you.
	+ Select 'Yes' for each type that applies.
* **participant-type-other** (Text): If you selected 'Yes' for 'Participant Type', provide more details about your participant type.
* **certification-date** (Date): Enter the date you achieved your certification.
* **certification-type** (Select One): Select the type of certification you hold.
	+ Select 'Yes' to confirm you hold the certification.
* **certification-status** (Select One): Select the status of your certification.
	+ Select 'Active' if you are currently certified.
	+ Select 'Inactive' if your certification is not currently active.
* **certification-expiration-date** (Date): If you selected 'Active' for 'Certification Status', enter the date your certification will expire.
* **Tips**: Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate certification registration. If unsure about any field, please contact the project team for assistance.

Note: The form requires you to submit a complete and accurate registration for your certification. Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting.
