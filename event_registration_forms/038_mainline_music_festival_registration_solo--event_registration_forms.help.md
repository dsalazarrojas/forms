# Mainline Music Festival Registration Solo - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mainline Music Festival Registration Solo form is designed for solo musicians to register themselves for the festival. The festival organizers will review the submitted information to select solo musicians for performance slots.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information on each page.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Registration (page 1)**: 
  Enter a brief description of yourself and your musical style.
* **Mainline Music Festival Email (page 2)**: 
  Enter your Mainline Music Festival email address.
* **First Name (page 3)**: 
  Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (page 4)**: 
  Enter your last name.
* **Address (page 5)**: 
  Enter your address.
* **City (page 6)**: 
  Enter the city you reside in.
* **State (page 7)**: 
  Enter the state you reside in.
* **Zip (page 8)**: 
  Enter your zip code.
* **Phone (page 9)**: 
  Enter your phone number.
* **Date (page 10)**: 
  Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Instrument (page 11)**: 
  Select one or more instruments you play (guitar, bass, drums, piano, or vocals).
* **Music Style (page 12)**: 
  Select your primary music style (jazz, rock, pop, classical, or folk).
* **Bio (page 13)**: 
  Enter a brief bio about yourself and your music experience.
* **Music Experience (page 14)**: 
  Enter the number of years you have been playing music.
