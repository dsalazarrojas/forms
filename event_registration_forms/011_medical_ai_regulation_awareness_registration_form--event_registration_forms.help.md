# Medical AI Regulation Awareness Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about individuals or organizations registering their awareness of medical AI regulations. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure that your registration is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Registration Information" page with your name, address, and any other relevant details.
2.  Answer the "Medical AI Regulation Awareness" question to demonstrate your understanding of medical AI regulations.
3.  Provide your contact information on the "Contact Information" page, including your email address and phone number.
4.  If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the "Additional Comments" field to express them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Information
*   **Registration Information** (`registration_form`, text, required/optional): This page collects your basic registration information, including your name and address. Please fill in your details accurately to ensure that your registration is processed correctly.

### Medical AI Regulation Awareness
*   **Medical AI Regulation Awareness** (`medical_ai_regulation_awareness`, text, required/optional): This field is designed to gauge your understanding of medical AI regulations. Please provide a brief description of your awareness and understanding of these regulations.

### Contact Information
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): This page is where you'll provide your contact details, including your email address and phone number. This information will be used to contact you if there's any further clarification needed.

### Additional Comments
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, use this field to express them. This will help us better understand your needs and concerns.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and honestly to avoid any delays in processing your registration.
*   If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification.
*   The information collected will be used to register your awareness of medical AI regulations and will not be shared with any third party.
