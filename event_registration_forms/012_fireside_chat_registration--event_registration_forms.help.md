# Fireside Chat Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for a fireside chat event. It will help the event organizers coordinate the event and tailor it to the registered attendees' interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields.
2. Select your preferences for the chat topics and engagement from the "Topic Interests" and "Engagement Preferences" fields.
3. Choose your preferred registration type (in-person, online, or hybrid) in the "Registration Type" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the event in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
5. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
6. Add any additional notes about your registration in the "Note" field.
