# Dance Performance Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals or their parents/guardians for dance performances or events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Provide your emergency contact information.
3. Confirm that you are the parent/guardian signing on behalf of the participant.
4. Answer the risk awareness questions.
5. Provide your signature and date and time of the event.
6. Enter the instructor's name, title, studio, email, and address.
7. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dance Performance Waiver Form**: This is the title of the form and is not a field to fill in. It's just a heading.
* **name** (`name`, **text**, required): Enter your name here. This is the name of the person filling out the form.
* **email** (`email`, **email**, required): Enter your email address here. This is the email address that will be used to contact you.
* **emergency contact info** (`emergency_contact_info`, **text**, optional): If there is someone we should contact in case of an emergency, enter their name and phone number here.
* **parent consent** (`parent_consent`, **select_one**, required): Confirm that you are the parent/guardian signing on behalf of the participant. Select "True" to confirm.
* **risk awareness questions** (`risk_awareness_questions`, **text**, optional): Answer any risk awareness questions that are relevant to the event.
* **risk awareness questions hints** (`risk_awareness_questions_hints`, **text**, optional): If you need more information on the risk awareness questions, you can find it here.
* **waiver text** (`waiver_text`, **text**, optional): This is where you can include any additional information or text related to the waiver.
* **signature** (`signature`, **note**, required): Sign this document to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver.
* **date** (`date`, **date**, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **time** (`time`, **time**, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **instructor name** (`instructor_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the instructor leading the event.
* **instructor title** (`instructor_title`, **text**, required): Enter the title or role of the instructor.
* **instructor studio** (`instructor_studio`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the studio or organization hosting the event.
* **instructor email** (`instructor_email`, **email**, required): Enter the email address of the instructor.
* **instructor address** (`instructor_address`, **text**, optional): Enter the address of the instructor's studio or organization.
* **instructor terms** (`instructor_terms`, **text**, optional): If the instructor has any terms or conditions they would like you to agree to, you can find them here.

Remember to review and agree to the terms and conditions of the instructor before signing the waiver.
