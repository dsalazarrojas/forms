# Corporate Workshop Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Workshop Quotation Form is a tool for customers to register their interest in attending corporate workshops. It is used to gather information about the workshop, including the topic, location, dates, and other relevant details. This form will help us provide accurate quotations for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Ensure that you fill out all the required fields (no fields are currently marked as required, but please check carefully).
* For each field, provide accurate and complete information.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Workshop Quotation Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, and it is not editable. It is here to let you know that this is the form for corporate workshops.
* **Organization Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the name of your organization.
* **Workshop Topic** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the workshop topic you are interested in.
* **Workshop Location** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the workshop will take place.
* **Start Date** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the workshop.
* **End Date** (`6`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the workshop.
* **Price Range** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the price range for the workshop.
* **Workshop Type** (`8`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the following options: In-person, Online, Hybrid.
* **Number of Participants** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the number of participants expected in the workshop.
* **Contact Person** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the workshop.
* **Email** (`11`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Description** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the workshop.
* **Assigned Tool** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the tool assigned to the workshop.

## Tips
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before submitting the form.
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to this help guide for further clarification.
