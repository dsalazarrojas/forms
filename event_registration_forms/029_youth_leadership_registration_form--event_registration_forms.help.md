# Youth Leadership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from youth participants about their leadership events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly.
2. Enter all required information for each field.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info**: This field is to collect basic participant information. Please fill out your name and other relevant details.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address for communication and delivery purposes.
* **Program Info**: Enter any information about the program you are registering for (e.g., program title, description).
* **Program Title**: Enter the title of the program you are registering for.
* **Program Description**: Enter a brief description of the program.
* **Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of the event.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the event.
* **Session Info**: Enter any additional session information.
* **Session Type**: Enter the type of session (e.g., workshop, conference).
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the session.
* **Session Location**: Enter the location of the session.
* **Participant Group**: Select the group you are registering for (e.g., Group A, Group B, Group C).
* **Program Report**: Enter the date of the program report.
* **Prophets**: Enter any additional information about the prophets.

Note: The participant group field is a select-one field, meaning you can only select one option from the list.
