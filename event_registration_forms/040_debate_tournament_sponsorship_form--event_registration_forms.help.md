# Debate Tournament Sponsorship Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential sponsors about their sponsorship details, including their contact information, sponsorship amount, and event details. This form helps us to process your sponsorship application and ensure that your support is effectively utilized for the debate tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including your name and title of your organization.
2. Enter your sponsorship details, such as the amount you plan to sponsor and the type of sponsor you are.
3. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter event details, including date, time, location, and description of the event.
5. Optionally, select the sponsorship level and provide additional comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (1)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter your name and title to introduce yourself.
* **Sponsor Info (2)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter your sponsorship information, including your company name, name, or organization name.
* **Contact Info (3)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter your contact name, email address, and phone number for us to reach you.
* **Sponsorship Agreement (4)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter your sponsorship agreement terms, such as payment details and any specific conditions.
* **Event Details (5)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter event details, including date, time, location, and description.
* **What type of sponsor are you? (6)**: 
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Description: Select the type of sponsor you are, such as Company, Individual, School/College, or Organization.
* **How much do you plan to sponsor? (7)**: 
  * Type: Number
  * Description: Enter the amount you plan to sponsor the event.
* **Contact Name (8)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Email (9)**: 
  * Type: Email
  * Description: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone (10)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Event (11)**: 
  * Type: Date
  * Description: Enter the date of the event.
* **Time of Event (12)**: 
  * Type: Time
  * Description: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location (13)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter the location of the event.
* **Sponsor Details (14)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter any additional sponsor details.
* **Title of the Event (15)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter the title of the event.
* **Type of Event (16)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter the type of event.
* **Description of Event (17)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter the description of the event.
* **Sponsorship Level (18)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter your sponsorship level, such as Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum.
* **Start Date of Event (19)**: 
  * Type: Date
  * Description: Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date of Event (20)**: 
  * Type: Date
  * Description: Enter the end date of the event.
* **Start Time of Event (21)**: 
  * Type: Time
  * Description: Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time of Event (22)**: 
  * Type: Time
  * Description: Enter the end time of the event.

* **Website of Event (24)**:

* **Additional Comments (25)**: 
  * Type: Text
  * Description: Enter any additional comments or information.
