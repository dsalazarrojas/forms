# Job Fair Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential volunteers for the Job Fair event. It helps us determine their interests, availability, and skills so we can assign them to suitable roles and ensure a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **name** (Text)
* Label: name
* Type: text
* Required: no
* Explanation: Please enter your full name.
### 2. **email** (Email)
* Label: email
* Type: email
* Required: no
* Explanation: Enter your valid email address.
### 3. **phone** (Text)
* Label: phone
* Type: text
* Required: no
* Explanation: If you want to be contacted about your application, enter your phone number.
### 4. **position** (Text)
* Label: position
* Type: text
* Required: no
* Explanation: Enter your current position or job title if applicable.
### 5. **start_date** (Date)
* Label: start-date
* Type: date
* Required: no
* Explanation: If you are available to start volunteering on a specific date, enter it here.
### 6. **end_date** (Date)
* Label: end-date
* Type: date
* Required: no
* Explanation: If you are available to end your volunteering on a specific date, enter it here.
### 7. **time** (Time)
* Label: time
* Type: time
* Required: no
* Explanation: If you are available to volunteer at a specific time, enter it here.
### 8. **volunteer_hours** (Number)
* Label: volunteer-hours
* Type: number
* Required: no
* Explanation: Indicate the number of hours you are available to volunteer.
### 9. **shift** (Select Multiple)
* Label: shift
* Type: select_multiple
* Required: no
* Explanation: Select one or multiple time slots you are available to volunteer.
### 10. **role** (Select One)
* Label: role
* Type: select_one
* Required: no
* Explanation: Choose the role that best suits your skills and interests.
### 11. **event_coordinator_notes** (Note)
* Label: event-coordinator-notes
* Type: note
* Required: no
* Explanation: If you have any specific requests or comments, add them here.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
