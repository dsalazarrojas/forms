# Virtual Job Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Job Fair Registration Form is designed for job seekers to register for virtual job fairs, allowing them to connect with employers and recruiters. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your contact information and job-specific details to interested parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll through the form, which is divided into multiple pages.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with a *asterisk* (this indicates a required field).
3. Enter your details in the corresponding fields, following the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, *optional*): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in the virtual job fair.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, *optional*): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in the virtual job fair.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *optional*): Enter your email address where you can be reached by employers and recruiters.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, *optional*): Enter the job title or position you are applying for or interested in.
* **Company** (`company`, text, *optional*): Enter the company or organization you work for or are interested in working for.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select multiple, *optional*): Select one or more job titles you are interested in or applying for. Choose from the options:
	+ Job Title 1
	+ Job Title 2
	+ Job Title 3
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, *optional*): Enter the start date of your job, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, *optional*): Enter the end date of your job, if applicable.
* **Job Fair** (`job_fair`, select one, *optional*): Select which virtual job fair you are registering for. Choose from:
	+ Virtual Job Fair 1
	+ Virtual Job Fair 2
	+ Virtual Job Fair 3
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, *optional*): Upload your resume, if you have one.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (*asterisk* indicated) to ensure your information is accurately collected.
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact the form administrators at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
