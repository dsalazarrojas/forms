# Vintage Car Exhibit Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vintage Car Exhibit Reservation Form is designed to collect information from potential attendees or car owners interested in reserving a spot in a vintage car exhibit event. This form helps event organizers or staff to efficiently manage event reservations and attendees' details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled before submission.
3. Double-check your input data for correctness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Car Year** (`car_year`, text, optional): Enter the year of the vintage car you wish to reserve a spot with.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the vintage car you wish to reserve a spot with.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the vintage car you wish to reserve a spot with.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the vintage car exhibit event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the vintage car exhibit event.

## Tips

* For accurate reservation and event management, please ensure the submitted data is correct and up-to-date.
* In case of any issues or concerns, please contact our event support team at form@gic.mx.
