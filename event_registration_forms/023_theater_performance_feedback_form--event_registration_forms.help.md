# Theater Performance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Theater Performance Feedback Form" is a tool designed to help event organizers and theater staff gather feedback from attendees about their experiences with a particular performance. This form is used to gauge audience satisfaction and identify areas for improvement for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter the Event ID of the performance you attended in the "Event ID" field.
3. Rate your overall experience with the performance by selecting a number value in the "Rating" field.
4. Provide any additional feedback about your experience in the "Feedback" field.
5. Choose the time of the performance you attended from the available options in the "Attended Performance" field.
6. Select the date of the performance you attended in the "Attended Date" field.
7. Choose the type of event you attended from the available options in the "Event Type" field.
8. Answer any additional questions or provide feedback in the "Feedback Question 1", "Feedback Question 2", and "Feedback Question 3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: This field is for you to enter your name so we can identify you as a respondent. This is not a required field.
* **Event ID**: Please enter the ID of the event you attended. If you're not sure, you can contact us and we'll help you find it.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the performance on a scale from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Feedback**: If you have any feedback or comments about your experience, please enter them here.
* **Attended Performance**: Choose the time of the performance you attended from the available options.
* **Attended Date**: Enter the date of the performance you attended.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you attended from the available options.
* **Feedback Question 1**, **Feedback Question 2**, and **Feedback Question 3**: These fields are for any additional questions or feedback about your experience. Please provide as much detail as possible.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can better understand your experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the performance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
