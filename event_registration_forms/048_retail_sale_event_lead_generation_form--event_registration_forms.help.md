# Retail Sale Event Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in purchasing products at a retail sale event. It will help generate leads for the event by capturing event lead information, such as customer contact details and event-specific information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Event Lead Generation" page with your event lead information.
2. On the "Customer Info" page, enter your name, email, and phone number.
3. On the "Contact Info" page, provide your contact information such as email and phone number.
4. On the "Product Info" page, let us know which products you are interested in.
5. On the "Event Details" page, enter any additional event-related information you would like to share.
6. On the "Event Details 2" page, enter any additional event details if needed.
7. On the "Date" page, enter the date of the event.
8. On the "Time" page, enter the time of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Lead Generation** (`event_lead`, text, required): Enter the event lead information you would like to generate.
  - This is the main category of the form, where you can enter the event lead information.
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
  - This page is used to collect your customer information.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information such as email and phone number.
  - This page is used to collect your contact information.
- **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): Let us know which products you are interested in.
  - This page is used to collect the products you are interested in.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional event-related information you would like to share.
  - This page is used to collect any additional event details.
- **Event Details 2** (`event_details_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional event details if needed.
  - This page is used to collect any additional event details.
- **Date** (`date`, text, optional): Enter the date of the event.
  - This page is used to collect the date of the event.
- **Time** (`time`, text, optional): Enter the time of the event.
  - This page is used to collect the time of the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields, as marked with *Required*.
- If you have any additional information that is not captured on this form, please enter it in the "Event Details" or "Event Details 2" page.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
