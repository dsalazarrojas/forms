# Jersey Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The jersey order form is designed to gather information from users to process their jersey orders correctly. Please complete the form with all required information to avoid delays in the order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your team from the available options.
2. Choose your preferred jersey size from the size selection options.
3. Select a color for your jersey.
4. Select a style for your jersey.
5. Choose the type of jersey you want to order (Adult, Youth, or Child).
6. Enter your name.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Add any order notes if necessary.
10. Choose your payment method (Online or Offline).
11. Enter the payment amount.
12. Select the payment status of your order (Paid, Unpaid, or Pending).
13. Select the order status (Confirmed, Rejected, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose a team**: Select the team you want to order a jersey for.
* **Select jersey size**: Choose your preferred jersey size from the available options.
* **Choose a color**: Select a color for your jersey.
* **Shirt style**: Select a style for your jersey (Short Sleeve, Long Sleeve, or Sleeveless).
* **Order type**: Choose the type of jersey you want to order (Adult, Youth, or Child).
* **Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order notes**: Add any additional notes about your order if necessary.
* **Payment method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Online or Offline).
* **Payment amount**: Enter the payment amount for your order.
* **Payment status**: Select the payment status of your order (Paid, Unpaid, or Pending).
* **Order status**: Select the order status (Confirmed, Rejected, or Pending).
