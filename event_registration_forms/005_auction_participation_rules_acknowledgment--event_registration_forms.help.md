# Auction Participation Rules Acknowledgment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Auction Participation Rules Acknowledgment form is a required step before participating in an auction event. This form helps us ensure that all participants understand and agree to the rules and terms of the auction, including payment and pickup terms. It's a critical step to confirm your understanding and agreement before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully to understand the rules and terms of the auction.
2. Fill in your Participant name, email, and Event name accurately.
3. Choose the correct answer for each of the agree-to and understand questions (e.g., "I agree to the auction participation rules", "Payment terms understood", etc.).
4. Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Provide your full name as it appears on your records.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address to which we will send a copy of this acknowledgement.
* **Event name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the auction event you are participating in.
* **Rules Acknowledgment** (`rules_acknowledgment`, note, required): This is a brief explanation of the rules and terms of the auction, which is provided at the beginning of the form. Please review it before proceeding.
* **I agree to the auction participation rules** (`agree_to_rules`, select_one, required): Choose "I agree" to confirm your understanding and agreement to the auction participation rules.
* **Payment terms understood** (`payment_terms_understood`, select_one, required): Choose "True" to confirm your understanding of the payment terms and timing.
* **Pickup and delivery terms understood** (`pickup_terms_understood`, select_one, optional): Choose "True" if you understand the pickup and delivery terms, or simply leave blank.
* **Special accommodations requested** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): If you have any special accommodations or accessibility needs, describe them here.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you have about the auction or your participation.
