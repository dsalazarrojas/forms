# drama_class_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule an appointment for a drama class.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Information** in the "User Information" field.
2. Provide your **Appointment Information**, including the schedule you are available for.
3. Select the **Class Information** you are interested in.
4. Choose whether you want to schedule an appointment for the selected class (**Schedule Appointment**).
5. Select the time you are available for the appointment (**Select Time**).
6. Choose the date you would like to schedule the appointment for (**Select Date**).
7. Select the teacher you would like to work with (**Select Teacher**).
8. Choose the workshop you are interested in attending (**Select Workshop**).
9. You can add any additional notes or comments in the **Additional Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Appointment Information** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: true): Provide information about the appointment you are interested in.
* **Class Information** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter class details, such as the class name and description.
* **Schedule Appointment** (`id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`, required: true): Select the schedule you are interested in (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Select Time** (`id: 5`, `type: select_one`, required: true): Choose the time you are available for the appointment (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Select Date** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, required: true): Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Select Class** (`id: 7`, `type: select_multiple`, required: true): Select the class you are interested in (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Select Teacher** (`id: 8`, `type: select_multiple`, required: true): Choose the teacher you would like to work with (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Select Workshop** (`id: 9`, `type: select_multiple`, required: true): Select the workshop you are interested in (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Email** (`id: 13`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`id: 15`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date for the appointment.
* **Time** (`id: 16`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the time for the appointment.
* **Teacher Name** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Phone** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Email** (`id: 19`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Information** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the teacher's information.
* **Class Name** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the class name.
