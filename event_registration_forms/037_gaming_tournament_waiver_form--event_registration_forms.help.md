# Gaming Tournament Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register and sign a waiver before participating in a gaming tournament. It collects essential information about the participant, their contact details, and the waiver signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields with a "required" tag are filled in correctly.
3. Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Organizer's Name (event_organizer_name)
* **Event Organizer's Name**: This is the name of the event organizer or the person responsible for the tournament.
* Please enter the name of the person or entity hosting the event.

### Participant's Name (participant_name)
* **Participant's Name**: This is the name of the person participating in the tournament.
* Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.

### Guardian's Name (guardian_name)
* **Guardian's Name (if applicable)**: This field is optional and only required if you are under 18 years old or are not an adult.
* If you are under 18, please enter the name of your parent or guardian.

### Contact Phone (contact_phone)
* **Contact Phone**: This is the phone number where we can reach you or your guardian in case of an emergency.
* Please enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.

### Contact Email (contact_email)
* **Contact Email**: This is the email address where we can reach you or your guardian in case of an emergency.
* Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### Date (waiver_signature_date)
* **Date**: This field is the date you sign this waiver.
* Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY to enter the date.

### Signature (waiver_signature)
* **Signature**: This is a required field where you need to sign the waiver.
* Please sign this section to confirm you have read and understood the waiver text.

### Waiver Text (waiver_text)
* **Waiver Text**: This is the text that you need to read and understand before signing the waiver.
* Please read the waiver text carefully and sign this form only if you have understood it.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly before submission.
* If you are under 18, please ensure that you have your parent or guardian's permission to participate in the tournament.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizer.
* Please keep in mind that this waiver is a legal document, and your signature is a confirmation that you have read and understood it.
