# International Missions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Missions Form is designed to capture information about international missions for administrative and operational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the "Yes" or "No" option from the drop-down menu to indicate if the mission is international. If you select "Yes", please provide the required information in the subsequent fields. Please fill in the "Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields with the relevant details. The "Date" and "Time" fields are optional, but if you select a date, you must also provide a time. The "Note" field is also optional, but it's a good place to add any additional information about the mission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. International Missions Form
* **International Missions Form** (`international_missions_form`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to indicate if the mission is international.
### 2. Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the individual or entity involved in the mission.
### 3. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the individual or entity involved in the mission.
### 4. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the individual or entity involved in the mission.
### 5. Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the mission, if applicable.
### 6. Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the mission, if applicable.
### 7. Note
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the mission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you select "Yes" correctly, as this will trigger the collection of additional information in the subsequent fields.
* Be careful when selecting a date and time, as incorrect entries may lead to confusion.
* The "Note" field is for additional information, so feel free to add any comments or details about the mission.
* If you need to make an urgent request, please contact your supervisor or emergency contact.
