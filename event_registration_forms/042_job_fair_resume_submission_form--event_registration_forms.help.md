# Job Fair Resume Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students and job seekers to submit their resumes and other relevant information for potential employment opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Ensure your resume is up-to-date and matches the requirements of the job fair.
3. Review the job details and interview process to ensure it aligns with your skills and experience.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Fair Resume Submission Form** (`job_fair_resume_submission_form_page_1`, text, required): This is the page title, which you can ignore as it's automatically populated by the form.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter a valid phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Please attach or upload your resume in PDF or Docx format. Ensure it's up-to-date and relevant to the job fair.
* **Job Fair ID** (`job_fair_id`, text, required): This is the ID number assigned to the job fair. Please ensure you're applying for the correct job fair.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter your university name.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter your job title.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter your job type.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter a brief description of your job.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required): Please enter a valid location where you can be reached for interviews or meetings.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the category of your job.
* **Job Tags** (`job_tags`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter relevant job tags.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter your job level.
* **Job Type of Employment** (`job_type_of_employment`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter your job type of employment.
* **Job Required Skills** (`job_required_skills`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter required skills for the job.
* **Job Opening Status** (`job_opening_status`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the job opening status.
* **Job Salary Range** (`job_salary_range`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the salary range for the job.
* **Job Benefits** (`job_benefits`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter job benefits.
* **Job Interview Process** (`job_interview_process`, select_multiple, required): Please select one or more interview processes that apply to you (e.g., In-person interview, Phone interview, Online assessment, Onsite interview).
* **Job Fair Details** (`job_fair_details`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter job fair details.
* **Job Fair Dates** (`job_fair_dates`, date, required): Please select a valid date for the job fair.
* **Job Fair Time** (`job_fair_time`, time, required): Please select a valid time for the job fair.
* **Job Fair Location** (`job_fair_location`, text, required): Please enter the valid location of the job fair.
