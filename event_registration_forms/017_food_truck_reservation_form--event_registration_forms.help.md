## food_truck_reservation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The food truck reservation form is used to collect information about an event that involves food trucks. This includes the event details, menu preferences, and payment information for the event organizers and their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:

1. **Event Information**
	* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text): Enter a brief description of the event, including the type of event, date, time, and location.
2. **Menu Preferences**
	* **Menu Preferences** (`menu_preferences`, text): Specify the type of food or drinks you would like to serve at the event.
3. **Truck Info**
	* **Truck Info** (`truck_info`, text): Provide details about the food truck(s) attending the event, including the truck name, number of attendees expected, and any specific requirements for the trucks.
4. **Organizer Info**
	* **Organizer Info** (`organizer_info`, text): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about your organization.
5. **Contact Info**
	* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text): Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
6. **Event Schedule**
	* **Event Schedule** (`event_schedule`, text): Enter the event schedule, including start and end times.
7. **Event Details**
	* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text): Enter any additional event details, such as special requirements or requests.
8. **Payment Info**
	* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text): Provide payment details, including payment method and amount.
9. **Review**
	* **Review** (`review`, text): Enter any review comments or feedback about the event.
10. **Confirm Info**
	* **Confirm Info** (`confirm_info`, text): Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the event details.
11. **Event Address**
	* **Event Address** (`event_address`, text): Enter the physical address of the event location.
12. **Event Notes**
	* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, text): Enter any additional event notes or comments.
13. **Menu Details**
	* **Menu Details** (`menu_details`, text): Enter the menu details, including the food and drinks to be served.
14. **Menu Price**
	* **Menu Price** (`menu_price`, text): Enter the price of the menu items.
15. **Payment Gateway**
	* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, text): Enter the payment gateway or method used for payment processing.
16. **Payment Method**
	* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text): Enter the payment method used, such as credit card or bank transfer.
17. **Payment Method Other**
	* **Payment Method Other** (`payment_method_other`, text): Enter any other payment method used.
18. **Review Details**
	* **Review Details** (`review_details`, text): Enter any review comments or feedback about the event.
19. **Event ID**
	* **Event ID** (`event_id`, text): Enter the event ID number.
20. **Event Status**
	* **Event Status** (`event_status`, text): Enter the event status, such as active or cancelled.
21. **Payment Status**
	* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, text): Enter the payment status, such as paid or pending.
22. **Payment Date**
	* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, text): Enter the payment date.
23. **Payment Amount**
	* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, text): Enter the payment amount.

## Tips

* Please make sure to complete all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing information to ensure accurate planning and execution of the event.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact the event organizers or their representatives.
