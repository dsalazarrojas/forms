# STEM Fair Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The STEM Fair Attendance Form is used to register attendees for the STEM Fair event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Select your participant role in the "participant role" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, as you would like it to be listed in our attendee list.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, as you would like it to be listed in our attendee list.
* **Participant role** (`role`, text, optional): Select your role in the STEM Fair, such as attendee, presenter, or exhibitor.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which we will use to contact you with event updates.
* **Contact phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, which we will use to contact you with event updates.
