# Real Estate Sustainability Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Sustainability Webinar Registration Form is designed to gather information from attendees of the webinar on real estate sustainability, including event details, attendee's name, company and job title, and other relevant contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the event details:
   - **Event Details**: This field is for the title of the webinar, which will be displayed on the registration confirmation email and other communication channels.
   - **Event Date**: Enter the date of the webinar, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
   - **Event Time**: Enter the time of the webinar, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
   - **Event Location**: Enter the location of the webinar.

2. Enter the attendee's information:
   - **Attendees Name**: Enter the name of the attendee.
   - **Attendees Email**: Enter the email address of the attendee.
   - **Attendees Phone**: Enter the phone number of the attendee.
   - **Attendees Company**: Enter the company of the attendee.
   - **Attendees Job Title**: Enter the job title of the attendee.
   - **Attendees Job Function**: Enter the job function of the attendee.
   - **Attendees Job Department**: Enter the department of the attendee.
   - **Other Attendees Job Title**: If the attendee has a job title that is not among the options provided, enter it here.
   - **Other Attendees Job Function**: If the attendee has a job function that is not among the options provided, enter it here.
   - **Other Attendees Job Department**: If the attendee has a department that is not among the options provided, enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`Real Estate Sustainability Webinar Registration Form`, text, required): Enter a brief title to describe the webinar.
* **Event Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the webinar, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Event Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the webinar, in 24-hour format.
* **Event Location** (text, required): Enter the location of the webinar.
* **Attendees Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Attendees Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Attendees Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Attendees Company** (text, required): Enter the company of the attendee.
* **Attendees Job Title** (text, required): Enter the job title of the attendee.
* **Attendees Job Function** (text, required): Enter the job function of the attendee.
* **Attendees Job Department** (text, required): Enter the department of the attendee.
* **Other Attendees Job Title** (text, required): Enter any other job titles not listed above.
* **Other Attendees Job Function** (text, required): Enter any other job functions not listed above.
* **Other Attendees Job Department** (text, required): Enter any other departments not listed above.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the organizer for clarification.
* Review your submission before submitting it for final review.
