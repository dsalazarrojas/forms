# Participation Request For Cyber Event - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for a cyber event, allowing event organizers to manage registrations and keep track of attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Enter the name of the participant you are registering (if different from your own).
3. Specify your role in the event.
4. Provide the name of the event you are participating in.
5. Select the date and time of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Name**: If registering for someone else, enter their name here.
* **Role**: Choose your role in the event (e.g., speaker, attendee, organizer).
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time**: Choose the time of the event from the available options.
