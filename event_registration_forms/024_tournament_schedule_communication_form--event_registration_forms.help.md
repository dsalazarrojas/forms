# tournament_schedule_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tournament schedule communication form is designed to gather and share important information about a tournament, including its schedule, status, and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Schedule Date** of the tournament, using the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. Enter the **Tournament Time** of the event, using 24-hour format (e.g., `13:00` for 1:00 PM).
3. Provide the **Match Location** of the tournament, where it will take place.
4. Enter the **Tournament Duration**, which is the length of the event.
5. Add any additional **Schedule Details**, such as specific instructions or notes.
6. Choose the **Schedule Status**, which can be Active, Inactive, or Cancelled.
7. Enter any **Schedule Notes**, which can be used for additional information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the tournament in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Tournament Time** (`tournament_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the tournament in 24-hour format (e.g., `13:00` for 1:00 PM).
* **Match Location** (`match_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the tournament, where it will take place.
* **Tournament Duration** (`tournament_duration`, text, required/optional): Enter the length of the tournament.
* **Schedule Details** (`schedule_details`, note, required/optional): Add any additional details or notes about the schedule.
* **Schedule Status** (`schedule_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of the schedule, which can be Active, Inactive, or Cancelled.
* **Schedule Notes** (`schedule_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the schedule.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the date and time correctly to avoid any conflicts or confusion.
* Use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` for the schedule date and 24-hour format for the tournament time.
* Be specific with the match location to ensure that participants know where to go.
* Clearly indicate the tournament status to prevent any misunderstandings.
* Use the schedule details field to add any important information or instructions.
* Use the schedule notes field for any additional comments or notes.
