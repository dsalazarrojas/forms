# Resume Screening Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect session preferences, contact details, and expectations from individuals interested in attending the Resume Screening Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, select one or both session preferences from the "Session Preferences" page.
2. On the "Contact Details" page, enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can reach you with updates and instructions.
3. In the "Expectations" page, briefly describe your goals, expectations, and any specific needs you may have from the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or both options for the session time: Morning, Afternoon, or Both. This will help us tailor the workshop experience to your schedule.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can reach you with updates, instructions, and event-related information.
* **Expectations** (`expectations`, text, required): Briefly describe your goals, expectations, and any specific needs you may have from the workshop. This will help us prepare and cater to your needs during the event.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your registration successfully.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, feel free to reach out to form@gic.mx for assistance.
* If you're unsure about the form or the event, double-check the form instructions or contact us for clarification.
