# Outstanding Art Festival Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Outstanding Art Festival Nomination Form is designed to collect nominations for outstanding art in various categories. The form is used to gather information about the nominated artist, their artwork, and the person submitting the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure that you have read and understood the purpose and instructions above.
* Enter the artist's name and information in the "Artist" field.
* Describe the nominated artwork in the "Outstanding Artwork" field.
* Select the category that best fits the nominated artwork.
* Provide a brief reason for the nomination in the "Nomination Reason" field.
* Enter your name and information in the "Submitter" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist** (`artist`, text, required: true): Enter the name of the artist being nominated. This is a required field and should be the full name of the artist.
* **Artwork** (`outstanding_artwork`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the artwork being nominated. This can include a title, medium, and a brief description of the artwork.
* **Nominate Category** (`nominate_category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category that best fits the nominated artwork. The options are:
	+ Art
	+ Music
	+ Performance
	+ Photography
	+ Visual
	+ Written
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required: false): Provide a brief reason why you are nominating this artist and their work. This can include specific examples or anecdotes.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name and title (if applicable) as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates and information about the nomination.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with updates and information about the nomination.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your organization or institution if you are submitting on behalf of a company or university.
