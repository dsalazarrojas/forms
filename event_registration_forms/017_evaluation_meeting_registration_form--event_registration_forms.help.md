# Evaluation Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evaluation Meeting Registration Form is a tool to gather information from participants about their expectations and needs for an evaluation meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). The form has 10 fields in total.
2. Fill in the meeting details, including the meeting time, if applicable.
3. Describe your participant expectations for the meeting, which will help the meeting organizer understand your needs and expectations.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text, required): This field is used to collect information about the meeting, such as the meeting purpose or agenda.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required): This field is used to specify the meeting time, if applicable.
* **Participant Expectations 1** (`participant_expectations_1`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as what you hope to achieve or what you need from the meeting.
* **Participant Expectations 2** (`participant_expectations_2`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as what you need to get out of the meeting.
* **Participant Expectations 3** (`participant_expectations_3`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as any specific goals or objectives.
* **Participant Expectations 4** (`participant_expectations_4`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as any specific topics or areas of discussion.
* **Participant Expectations 5** (`participant_expectations_5`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Participant Expectations 6** (`participant_expectations_6`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as any specific outcomes or results.
* **Participant Expectations 7** (`participant_expectations_7`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as any specific actions or next steps.
* **Participant Expectations 8** (`participant_expectations_8`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as any specific follow-up actions or next steps.
* **Participant Expectations 9** (`participant_expectations_9`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as any specific feedback or input.
* **Participant Expectations 10** (`participant_expectations_10`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your expectations for the meeting, such as any specific needs or support.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when providing your expectations and needs.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the meeting organizer.
* If you have any further expectations or needs after the meeting, follow up with the meeting organizer.
