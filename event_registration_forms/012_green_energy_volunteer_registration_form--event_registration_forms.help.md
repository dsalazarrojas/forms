# green_energy_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is designed to assist users in completing the green_energy_volunteer_registration_form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by carefully reading the instructions and requirements for each page.
2. Answer all required fields (marked as 'required: true') truthfully and accurately.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or enter any information you feel is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Page 1** (`page_1`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Page 2** (`page_2`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are available to volunteer for the Green Energy program.
* **Page 3** (`page_3`, `text`, required): Describe why you are interested in volunteering for the Green Energy program.
* **Page 4** (`page_4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the skills you have that are relevant to the program (e.g., environmental awareness, data analysis, etc.).
* **Page 5** (`page_5`, `text`, required): Enter your availability for the next 3 months (e.g., days and time slots).
* **Page 6** (`page_6`, `text`, required): Describe your experience in related fields (e.g., environmental projects, community service, etc.).
* **Page 7** (`page_7`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Page 8** (`page_8`, `text`, required): Confirm your availability for an interview.
* **Page 9** (`page_9`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or questions.
* **Page 10** (`page_10`, `text`, required): Enter your expected start date.
* **Page 11** (`page_11`, `text`, required): Enter your expected end date.
* **Page 12** (`page_12`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are able to commit for more than 3 months.
* **Page 13** (`page_13`, `text`, required): Enter your relevant experience working with similar projects.
* **Page 14** (`page_14`, `text`, required): Describe your motivation for participating in the Green Energy program.
* **Page 15** (`page_15`, `text`, required): Enter any certifications or licenses relevant to the program.
* **Page 16** (`page_16`, `text`, required): Enter your preferred work schedule.
* **Page 17** (`page_17`, `text`, required): Enter your preferred communication channel.
* **Page 18** (`page_18`, `text`, required): Enter any other relevant details.
* **Page 19** (`page_19`, `text`, required): Describe your experience working with diverse groups.
* **Page 20** (`page_20`, `text`, required): Enter your ability to work independently.
* **Page 21** (`page_21`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the languages you are fluent in.
* **Page 22** (`page_22`, `text`, required): Enter your ability to work under pressure.
* **Page 23** (`page_23`, `text`, required): Enter any previous experience with similar projects.
* **Page 24** (`page_24`, `text`, required): Enter your availability for training sessions.
* **Page 25** (`page_25`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant information not covered above.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Use clear and concise language when answering questions.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Green Energy team for assistance.
