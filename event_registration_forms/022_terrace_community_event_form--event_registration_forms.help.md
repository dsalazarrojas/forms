# Terrace Community Event Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the "How To Complete This Form" section.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your event.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your event.
*   **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the event.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event, such as an address or a venue name.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Optional field for providing additional comments or notes about the event.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
