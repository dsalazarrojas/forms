# Event Decor Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Decor Payment Form is designed for customers to register and pay for event decoration services. Fill out this form to ensure timely and accurate payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Customer Name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Choose a payment method from the `Payment Method` dropdown menu.
   - Select Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer as your preferred payment method.
5. Enter the total amount to be paid for the event decoration services in the `Payment Total` field.
6. Select the event date in the `Event Date` field.
7. Choose the event start time in the `Event Time` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (text)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for event decoration services.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your valid email address for communication and payment confirmation.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for any additional contact.
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Choose from the available payment options: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Total (number)**: Enter the total amount to be paid for event decoration services.
* **Event Date (date)**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time (time)**: Choose the start time of the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth payment processing.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for support.
