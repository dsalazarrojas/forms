# Press Collaboration Meetup RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from press representatives who are interested in attending a meetup or discussion. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that we can prepare adequately for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Provide your press collaboration meetup details, including your name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Indicate your accessibility needs and preferred discussion topics.
3. Let us know if you will be attending and if so, when you can join us.
4. Add any comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Press Collaboration Meetup Details**: 
  (Optional): Enter a brief description of your press collaboration meetup details.

* **Name**: 
  (Optional, required to be filled out if you're providing a comment): Provide your name.

* **Email**:
  (Optional): Enter your email address. This will help us keep you updated on the event.

* **Phone**:
  (Optional): Enter your phone number. This will help us reach you if needed.

* **Accessibility Needs**:
  (Optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please let us know so we can accommodate you.

* **Discussion Topics**:
  (Optional): Select the topics you're interested in discussing.

* **Attending Status**:
  (Optional): Let us know if you'll definitely be attending, maybe attending, or not attending.

* **RSVP Date**:
  (Optional): Enter the date you plan on attending.

* **RSVP Time**:
  (Optional): Enter the time you plan on attending.

* **Comment**:
  (Optional): Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as you can to help us prepare for the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
