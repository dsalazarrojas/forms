# Workforce Culture Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and work-related information from individuals registering for a Workforce Culture Lab.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form in sections, using the labels and fields as guidance.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. Review your answers carefully to ensure they are complete and correct.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Details

* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Work Details** (`work_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your work-related details, such as your job title and department.
* **Workshift** (`workshift`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are currently working on a shift, 'No' otherwise.
* **Start** (`workshift_start`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the start date of your workshift.
* **End** (`workshift_end`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the end date of your workshift.
* **Duration** (`workshift_duration`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the duration of your workshift.
* **Hours** (`workshift_hours`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of hours you worked.
* **Days** (`workshift_days`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of days you worked.
* **Rate** (`workshift_rate`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your rate or pay rate.
* **Rate Type** (`workshift_rate_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select 'flat rate', 'hourly rate', or 'hour rate'.
* **Note** (`workshift_note`, note, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your workshift.
* **Email** (`workshift_email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`workshift_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`workshift_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of your workshift.
* **Time** (`workshift_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time of your workshift.
* **Time Zone** (`workshift_time_zone`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select your time zone.
* **Address** (`workshift_address`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your work address.
* **City** (`workshift_city`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the city where you work.
* **State** (`workshift_state`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the state where you work.
* **Zip** (`workshift_zip`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your zip code.
* **Note** (`workshift_note`, note, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your workshift.
* **File** (`workshift_file`, text, required: false):
	+ Upload any relevant documents or files.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the person submitting this form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed and understood the instructions and guidelines provided.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authority for clarification.
* Submit the form when you have completed all the required fields.
