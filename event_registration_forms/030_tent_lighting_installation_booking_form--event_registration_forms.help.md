# Tent Lighting Installation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book tent lighting installations for events. It collects information necessary for the successful organization and execution of such events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event details:
	* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
	* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
	* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.
2. Provide event specifics:
	* **Event Duration**: Enter the expected length of the event.
	* **Lighting Type**: Choose the type of lighting required (LED, Halogen, or Fluorescent).
	* **Lighting Capacity**: Select the number of people that the lighting installation can accommodate (Small, Medium, or Large).
3. Enter contact information:
	* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the event.
	* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
	* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
4. Add any additional services:
	* **Additional Services**: Select any additional services required for the event (Power, Water, Audio/Video).
5. Provide event capacity and budget information:
	* **Event Capacity**: Enter the maximum number of people that can attend the event.
	* **Event Budget**: Enter the allocated budget for the event.
6. Confirm event details:
	* **Event Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the event.
7. Confirm client approval:
	* **Client Approval**: Indicate if client approval has been obtained.
	* **Approval Date**: Enter the date of client approval.
	* **Approval Time**: Enter the time of client approval.
	* **Approval Person**: Enter the name of the person who approved the event.
	* **Approval Number**: Enter the approval number or reference.
	* **Approval E-mail**: Enter the email address of the person who approved the event.
