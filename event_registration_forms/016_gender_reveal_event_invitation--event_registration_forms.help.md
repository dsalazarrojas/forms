# gender_reveal_event_invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests attending a gender reveal event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined in the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prophets** (Text, required): Enter the guest's name.
* **date** (Date, optional): Select the date of the event from the calendar provided.
* **time** (Time, optional): Choose the time of the day and month for the event.
* **location** (Text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **event_details** (Note, optional): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **RSVP** (Select Multiple, required): Choose how you will attend the event by selecting one or more of the provided options.
* **message** (Text, optional): Enter a message for the host or hostess if desired.
* **gender** (Select One, required): Indicate the gender of the baby.
* **color_preference** (Select Multiple, optional): Choose preferred colors for the event, such as "Blue", "Pink", or "Green".

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to attend the event.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please let us know in the "Event Details" field.
* You can contact us at form@gic.mx if you have any questions or need further assistance.
