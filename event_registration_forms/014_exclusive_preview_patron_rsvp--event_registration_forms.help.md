# exclusive_preview_patron_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patrons to RSVP for an exclusive preview event. Please fill out the form accurately to provide the necessary information for event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are registering for the correct event.
2. Fill out the required fields marked as "required".
3. Select relevant options for special needs and VIP requests.
4. Enter the requested date and time for the event.
5. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event ID** (event_id, number, required): Enter the ID number of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Name** (event_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Guest Count** (guest_count, number, optional): Enter the number of guests attending with you.
* **Special Needs** (special_needs, select_multiple, optional): Select any special needs you have, such as wheelchair, hearing impairment, or visual impairment.
* **RSVP Date** (rsvp_date, date, optional): Enter the date you will attend the event.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests.
* **Confirmed** (confirmed, select_one, optional): Confirm whether you will attend the event (True or False).
* **Special Needs Comments** (special_needs_comments, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your special needs.
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (event_time, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (event_location, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Address** (event_address, text, optional): Enter the address of the event.
* **RSVP By** (rsvp_by, date, optional): Enter the date you will RSVP by.
* **Notes** (notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **VIP Status** (vip_status, select_one, optional): Select your VIP status (VIP, VIP Plus, or None).
* **VIP Status Comments** (vip_status_comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your VIP status.
* **VIP Type** (vip_plus_type, text, optional): Enter the type of VIP treatment you require.
* **VIP Details** (vip_plus_details, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your VIP request.
* **VIP Guest Count** (vip_plus_guest_count, number, optional): Enter the number of guests with you that require VIP treatment.
* **VIP Diet** (vip_plus_diet, select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions for your VIP guests (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten Free, Meat, Other).
* **VIP Other** (vip_plus_other, text, optional): Enter any other dietary requests or preferences.
* **VIP Special Needs** (vip_plus_special_needs, text, optional): Enter any additional special needs for your VIP guests.

Please note that not all fields are required, but accurate information is necessary for event organizers to plan effectively.
