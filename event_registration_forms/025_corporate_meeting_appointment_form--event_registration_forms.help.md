# Corporate Meeting Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Meeting Appointment Form is used to register and schedule corporate meetings related to your job role. Please fill out this form to create a new meeting request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the necessary meeting details such as the topic and location of the meeting.
2. Select the date and time that you would like to have the meeting.
3. Specify the duration of the meeting and the preferred location for the meeting.
4. Add the attendees who will be joining the meeting.
5. Choose a meeting topic and preferred meeting time that suits your needs.
6. Select the available dates and times for the meeting.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details**: Enter a brief description of the meeting. This is a required field.
* **Meeting Topic**: Enter a description of the meeting topic. This is a required field.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting in the format YYYY-MM-DD. This field is required.
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting in the format HH:MM. This field is required.
* **Meeting Duration**: Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes. This field is required.
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the meeting. This is a required field.
* **Attendees**: Select the attendees who will be joining the meeting. You can select multiple attendees.
* **Meeting Topic Preferences**: Select the topic of the meeting. You can select only one topic.
* **Preferred Meeting Time**: Select a time slot for the meeting. You can select one time slot.
* **Available Dates**: Select the dates that you are available for the meeting. You can select multiple dates.
* **Preferred Meeting Time**: Select a time slot that you prefer for the meeting. You can select multiple time slots.
* **Preferred Meeting Date**: Select a date that you prefer for the meeting. You can select multiple dates.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct meeting location to ensure that all attendees can attend.
* Double-check that you have selected the correct attendees and meeting topic.
* Review your selections carefully before submitting the form to ensure that they are correct.
