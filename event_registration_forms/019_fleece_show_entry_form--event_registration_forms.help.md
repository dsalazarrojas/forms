# Fleece Show Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fleece Show Entry Form is designed to gather information from exhibitors for a Fleece Show event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in all the required and optional fields as applicable to your information.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor** (text, optional):
	+ Enter your name or the name of your company/organization.
* **Animal Info** (text, optional):
	+ Provide any relevant information about the animals you will be exhibiting.
* **Fleece Show Info** (text, optional):
	+ Share any additional details about the Fleece Show you are participating in.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information in each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
