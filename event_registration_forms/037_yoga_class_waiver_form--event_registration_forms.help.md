# Yoga Class Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Yoga Class Waiver Form" is a necessary document to be completed by participants before attending a yoga class. It ensures that each participant is aware of and has given their consent to the waivers and guidelines of the class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your event registration form information in the **Event Registration Form** section.
2. Provide your **Participant Information** in the **Participant Information** section.
3. Fill out the **Contact Details** section with your contact information.
4. In the **Waiver Consent** section, select either "Waiver" or "No Waiver" according to your preference.
5. If you selected "Waiver", enter your **Participant Name**, **Participant Email**, and **Participant Phone**.
6. In the **Medical History** section, describe any medical conditions you have or have had.
7. In the **Medical Condition** section, specify any medical conditions that may affect your practice.
8. In the **Allergy** section, list any allergies you have.
9. In the **Emergency Contact** section, provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
10. In the **Emergency Phone** section, enter your emergency contact's phone number.
11. Enter the **Event Date** and **Event Time** of the class.
12. Select the **Class Type** (Beginners, Intermediate, or Advanced).
13. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (Online or Offline).
14. If your payment is **Pending**, select the **Waiver Status** as "Pending".
15. Fill out any additional **Notes** if necessary.
16. Finally, review your submitted form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Form** (Event Registration Form): 
  * This is the starting point for the form.
  * Enter your event registration information.

* **Participant Information** (Participant Information):
  * Provide a brief description of yourself.

* **Contact Details** (Contact Details):
  * Enter your contact details, such as email and phone number.

* **Waiver Consent** (Waiver Consent):
  * Select either "Waiver" or "No Waiver" depending on your preference.
  * If you selected "Waiver", fill out the following fields:
    + **Participant Name**: Enter your name.
    + **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
    + **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.

* **Medical History** (Medical History):
  * Describe any past medical conditions you have.

* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition):
  * Specify any medical conditions affecting your practice.

* **Allergy** (Allergy):
  * List any allergies you have.

* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact):
  * Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.

* **Emergency Phone** (Emergency Phone):
  * Enter your emergency contact's phone number.

* **Event Date** (Event Date) and **Event Time** (Event Time):
  * Enter the date and time of the class.

* **Class Type** (Class Type):
  * Select the level of class you wish to attend (Beginners, Intermediate, or Advanced).

* **Payment Method** (Payment Method):
  * Choose between Online Payment and Offline Payment.

* **Payment Status** (Payment Status):
  * Select "Unpaid" if your payment is pending.
  * If paid, select "Paid".

* **Waiver Status** (Waiver Status):
  * Select "Pending" if your waiver status is pending.

* **Notes** (Notes):
  * Fill out any additional notes if necessary.

* **Submitted At** (Submitted At), **Updated At** (Updated At), **Created At** (Created At), and **Updated By** (Updated By):
  * These are system-generated fields, not editable.
  * Display timestamps for when the form was submitted, updated, created, and last updated by.
