# Startup Incubation Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about your startup to help our incubation program team assess your application. Please answer the questions truthfully to ensure a successful evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling in the form.
2. Fill in each section with accurate and complete information.
3. Review and submit the form once you've completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Startup Information** (`startup_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your startup.
* **Founder Information** (`founder_info`, text, required: false): Provide details about the founder(s) of your startup.
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, required: false): Describe your business model, products or services offered, and key features.
* **Business Financials** (`business_financials`, text, required: false): Share your company's financial situation, including revenue, expenses, and funding received.
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, required: false): Elaborate on your product or service, including its features and benefits.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required: false): Write a detailed description of your product or service.
* **Market Information** (`market_info`, text, required: false): Explain your target market, competition, and growth prospects.
* **Team Information** (`team_info`, text, required: false): Introduce your team members, their skills, and experience.
* **Startup Financials** (`startup_financials`, text, required: false): Share your startup's financial information, including income, expenses, and funding.
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options for your funding source:
	+ Private Investor
	+ Venture Capitalist
	+ Angel Investor
	+ Crowdfunding
	+ Government Grant
	+ Bootstrapping
	+ None
* **Business Model** (`business_model`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options for your business model:
	+ B2B
	+ B2C
	+ B2B2C
	+ B2B2B
	+ B2C2B
	+ None
	+ Other
* **Business Stage** (`business_stage`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options for your business stage:
	+ Idea
	+ Prototype
	+ Launch
	+ Scaling
	+ Established
	+ None
* **Revenue Model** (`revenue_model`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options for your revenue model:
	+ Subscription
	+ Advertising
	+ Sponsored Post
	+ Affiliate
	+ Sponsored Content
	+ None
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options for your business location:
	+ Online
	+ Offline
	+ Hybrid
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options for your business type:
	+ Sole Proprietorship
	+ Partnership
	+ Limited Liability Partnership
	+ Limited Corporation
	+ Not Applicable
* **Business Status** (`business_status`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options for your business status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Dissolved
* **Business Age** (`business_age`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options for your business age:
	+ 0-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ More than 10 years
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required: false): Enter the number of people on your team.
* **Team Ages** (`team_ages`, number, required: false): Enter the average age of your team members.
* **Team Languages** (`team_languages`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple languages spoken by your team:

	+ Spanish

* **Startup Equipment** (`startup_equipment`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple equipment used by your startup:

	+ Scanner
	+ Other
* **Office Location** (`office_location`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options for your office location:
	+ Home/Remote
	+ Co-working Space
	+ Downtown
* **Funding Amount** (`funding_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of funding received by your startup.
* **Startup Stage** (`startup_stage`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options for your startup stage:
	+ Idea
	+ Prototype
	+ Launch
	+ Scaling
	+ Established
	+ None
