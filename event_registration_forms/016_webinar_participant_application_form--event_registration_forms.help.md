# Webinar Participant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to submit their applications to attend a webinar. It is designed to collect necessary information for event organization and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name.
* Enter your email address.
* Enter your participant phone number.
* If you have a company affiliation, please fill in your company name.
* If applicable, select your job title from the options.
* Select your job type(s) from the options.
* If you have attended previous events, please select the date you last attended.
* Choose the date and time you would like to attend the next event.
* If you want to leave a message to the event organizer, please fill in the message box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email (Required)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Participant Phone (Required)**: Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Company (Optional)**: If you are affiliated with a company, enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title (Optional)**: If you have a specific job title, select it from the options. This helps the event organizer understand your role in your company.
* **Job Type (Required)**: Select one or more of the job types that best describe your role in your company.
* **Last Event Attended (Optional)**: If you have attended previous events, select the date you last attended.
* **Next Event (Required)**: Choose the date and time you would like to attend the next event. Be sure to choose a time slot that works for you.
* **Message (Optional)**: Leave a message to the event organizer if you want to communicate something specific.
* **Message 2 (Optional)**: If you have any other information or comments, please add them here.

## Tips
- Ensure that you only submit the form once you have been invited to an event.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact the event organizer.
- Be sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
