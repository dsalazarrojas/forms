# Outdoor Fitness Meetup Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Fitness Meetup Registration Form is designed for registering attendees for an outdoor fitness meetup. It gathers information about the attendee's preferences and details for the event to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the form with your details and preferences.
- Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
- Click submit to send your registration information to the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1**: This page is just an introductory page and serves as a filler page for the form's design. Please ignore this page and proceed to the next one.
* **Attendee Details (Page 2)**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the questions regarding your willingness to participate in a variety of outdoor activities, such as hiking, team sports, or water activities. This information will help the event organizers tailor the activities according to the attendees' preferences.
* **Waivers (Page 3)**: This field is a text area where you can input any waivers or concerns you may have regarding your participation in the outdoor activities. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Preferences (Page 4)**: Select one or more options from the list to indicate your interests and preferences for activities, such as team sports or hiking. This will help the event organizers understand your preferences and plan accordingly.
* **Submission (Page 5)**: This field is a date field where you can input the date you plan to submit your waiver and preference forms. Please make sure to input the correct date to avoid any confusion or issues.
* **Page 6**: This is a filler page and can be ignored.
* **Page 7**: This is a time field where you can input the time you plan to arrive at the event or submit your waiver and preference forms. This is not currently in use and can be ignored.
* **Additional Comments (Page 8)**: This text area is for any additional comments or concerns you have regarding your participation in the event.
* **Email (Page 9)**: This is an email field where you can input your email address for any further communication from the event organizers.
* **Page 10**: This is a filler page and can be ignored.
* **Page 11**: This page is for any additional details or comments. Please input any information that will help the event organizers understand your needs and preferences.
* **Page 12**: This is a filler page and can be ignored.
