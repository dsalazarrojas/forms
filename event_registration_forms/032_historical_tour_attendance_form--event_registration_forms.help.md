# Historical Tour Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register for a historical tour event. It will help event organizers track attendees and manage tour logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional information as instructed below.
2. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Historical Tour Attendance** (`historical_tour_attendance_form`, text, not required):
	+ This is the main title and description of the tour.
* **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, date, not required):
	+ Enter the date of the historical tour.
* **Tour Time** (`tour_time`, time, not required):
	+ Enter the time of the historical tour.
* **Tour Start Time** (`tour_start_time`, time, not required):
	+ Enter the start time of the historical tour.
* **Tour Start End Time** (`tour_start_time_end_time`, time, not required):
	+ Enter the end time of the historical tour.
* **Tour Duration** (`tour_duration`, text, not required):
	+ Enter the duration of the historical tour.
* **Tour Guides** (`tour_guides`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Choose from the options:
		- Local Guides
		- External Guides
		- Other (please specify)
* **Tour Capacity** (`tour_capacity`, select_one, not required):
	+ Choose from the options:
		- 10
		- 20
		- 30
		- 40
		- 50
* **Headcount** (`headcount`, text, not required):
	+ Enter the headcount for the historical tour.
* **Accessibility Requests** (`accessibility_requests`, text, not required):
	+ Enter any accessibility requests or special requirements for the tour.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in all required fields correctly to avoid errors.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
