# Dual Meet Participation Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through how to complete the Dual Meet Participation Interest Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply navigate through the pages and select the relevant information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Participation Interest
* **What is your interest in participating in the dual meet?** (`participation_interest`, select_multiple): This field is where you indicate your level of interest in participating in the dual meet. Select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Page 2: Availability
* **What is your availability on meet day?** (`availability`, select_one): Choose your availability on the meet day.

### Page 3: Team Information
* **Team name** (`team_name`, text): Enter the name of your team.
* **Coach name** (`coach_name`, text): Enter the name of your coach.
* **School name** (`school_name`, text): Enter the name of your school.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.

### Page 4: Availability Consent and Notes
* **Consent to have your availability shared with the meet directors?** (`availability_consent`, select_one): Choose yes or no to share your availability with the meet directors.
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Event details** (`event_details`, text): Enter any additional information about the event that you would like to share with us.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes about your participation in the dual meet.
* **Consent to share availability** (`availability_consent`, select_one): Choose yes or no to share your availability with the meet directors.
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'

### Page 5: Additional Information
* **School name** (`school_name`, text): Enter the name of your school.
* **Coach name** (`coach_name`, text): Enter the name of your coach.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
