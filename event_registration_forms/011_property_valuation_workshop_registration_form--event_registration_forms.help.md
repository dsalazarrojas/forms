# Property Valuation Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from attendees interested in attending the Property Valuation Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Input your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear on any communication materials.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on any communication materials.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you with any last-minute updates or reminders.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth to help us verify your identity (if required).
