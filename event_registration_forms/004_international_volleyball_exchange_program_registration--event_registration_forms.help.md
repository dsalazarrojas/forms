# International Volleyball Exchange Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Volleyball Exchange Program Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in the International Volleyball Exchange Program. The form is intended to gather data about the participant's personal details, nationality, sporting preferences, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your athlete's first name in the "Athlete First Name" field.
3. Enter your athlete's last name in the "Athlete Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Select your nationality from the "Nationality" dropdown menu. Options are:
	* United States
	* Canada
	* Mexico
	* Other (please specify)
8. Select your preferred sport from the "Sport" dropdown menu. Options are:
	* Volleyball
	* Basketball
	* Football
	* Baseball
9. Select your skill level from the "Level" dropdown menu. Options are:
	* Beginner
	* Intermediate
	* Advanced
10. Enter your coach's name in the "Coach Name" field.
11. Enter the name of your organization in the "Organization" field.
12. Enter the name of the event you are participating in in the "Event Name" field.
13. Enter the name of your team in the "Team Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete First Name** (1, text, required): Enter the first name of the athlete.
* **Athlete Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter the last name of the athlete.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Nationality** (6, select_one, required): Select your nationality from the dropdown menu.
* **Sport** (7, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred sport from the dropdown menu.
* **Level** (8, select_one, required): Select your skill level from the dropdown menu.
* **Coach Name** (9, text, required): Enter the name of your coach.
* **Organization** (10, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Event Name** (11, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Team Name** (12, text, required): Enter the name of your team.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team.
* Please note that the required fields are marked as "required" in the form.
