# Donor Recognition Event RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Recognition Event RSVP Form is a crucial tool for event organizers to collect essential information from donors attending or interested in attending the event. It helps event organizers gauge interest, gather contact details, and tailor the event experience according to attendee preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required."
2. Select your preferred contact method from the options provided.
3. Choose your RSVP response: Attending, Not Attending, or Maybe.
4. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with the event organizers.
5. Review your submission before submitting the form to ensure you've provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: This is the first field on the form. Please enter a brief description of the event you're interested in, such as its name, location, or a reference to its date.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as you'd like to be recognized.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so event organizers can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted about the event. Options are:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Mail
	+ Other (please specify in the "Note" field)
* **RSVP Response**: Indicate your attendance status:
	+ Attending
	+ Not Attending
	+ Maybe
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or messages you'd like to share with event organizers.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you choose "Phone" as your contact method.

Tips: If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please include them in the "Note" field. This will help event organizers tailor the event experience to your needs.
