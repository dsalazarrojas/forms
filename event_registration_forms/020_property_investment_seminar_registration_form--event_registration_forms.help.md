# property_investment_seminar_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property investment seminar registration form is designed to collect information from participants, allowing us to keep track of their details and preferences for the seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as a participant in the **Participant Name** field.
2.  Enter your email address in the **Participant Email** field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the **Participant Phone** field.
4.  Enter your company name in the **Participant Company** field.
5.  Select your designation from the options provided in the **Participant Designation** field.
6.  Choose the type of property you are interested in investing in from the options provided in the **Property Type** field.
7.  Enter the date of the seminar you would like to attend in the **Seminar Date** field.
8.  Choose the time of the seminar you would like to attend from the options provided in the **Seminar Time** field.
9.  Enter the location of the seminar you would like to attend in the **Seminar Location** field.
10. Add any additional notes about your participation in the **Participant Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant.
*   **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Participant Company**: Enter your company name.
*   **Participant Designation**: Choose your designation from the options provided.
*   **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you are interested in investing in from the options provided.
*   **Seminar Date**: Enter the date of the seminar you would like to attend.
*   **Seminar Time**: Choose the time of the seminar you would like to attend from the options provided.
*   **Seminar Location**: Enter the location of the seminar you would like to attend.
*   **Participant Notes**: Add any additional notes about your participation.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
*   Choose the correct options for each field to ensure that your information is correctly recorded.
*   If you have any issues with the form, contact the seminar organizers for assistance.
