# Volunteer Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Check Out Form is used to collect information from volunteers after an event has taken place. This form helps us keep track of event details, volunteer roles, contact information, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Name** field with the name of the event you participated in.
2. Enter the **Event Date** field with the date of the event.
3. Select your role in the **Role** field from the provided options.
4. Enter your **Name** in the field provided.
5. Enter your **Email** address if you would like to be contacted about future events.
6. If you have worked a certain number of hours, enter that number in the **Hours Worked** field.
7. Finally, provide any feedback or comments about your experience in the **Feedback** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, not required)
Enter the name of the event you participated in.

### **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, not required)
Enter the date of the event.

### **Role** (`role`, select_one, required)
Select the role you played during the event from the provided options.

### **Name** (`name`, text, not required)
Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded.

### **Email** (`email`, email, not required)
Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about future events.

### **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, not required)
If you have worked a certain number of hours, enter that number here.

### **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, not required)
Provide any feedback or comments about your experience at the event.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling in the **Event Name** and **Event Date** fields.
- If you are unsure about your role, select the option that best represents your responsibilities during the event.
- Enter your email address to receive updates and invitations to future events.
- Take a moment to provide thoughtful feedback to help us improve our events.
