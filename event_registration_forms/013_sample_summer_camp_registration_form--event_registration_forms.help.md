# Sample Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents/guardians to register their child(ren) for the summer camp. It includes fields for personal and contact details, buddy requests, and emergency contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To register your child for summer camp, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Personal Details** section with your child's details, including their date of birth.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
3. If applicable, select which buddies your child will be attending the camp with and fill in their first and last names.
4. Choose whether you consent to send your child to summer camp.
5. Fill in your emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal details**: 
  This field is used to gather your child's personal details, including their date of birth.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **Camper name**: 
  This field is used to input your child's name.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **Date of birth**:
  This field is used to input your child's date of birth.
  Type: `date`
  Required: `false`

* **Guardian details**:
  This field is used to input your guardian's contact information.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **First name**:
  This field is used to input your first name.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **Last name**:
  This field is used to input your last name.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **Email**:
  This field is used to input your email.
  Type: `email`
  Required: `false`

* **Phone**:
  This field is used to input your phone number.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **Cabin buddies**:
  This option is used to select whether your child will be with a buddy at the camp.
  Type: `select_multiple`
  Required: `false`

* **Buddy 1 name**:
  This field is used to input the name of the buddy 1.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **Buddy 1 last name**:
  This field is used to input the last name of the buddy 1.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **Buddy 2 name**:
  This field is used to input the name of the buddy 2.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **Buddy 2 last name**:
  This field is used to input the last name of the buddy 2.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

* **Consent**:
  This field is used to confirm whether you consent to send your child to the summer camp.
  Type: `select_one`
  Required: `true`

* **Emergency contact**:
  This field is used to input your emergency contact information.
  Type: `text`
  Required: `false`

Note: Please be aware that some fields have optional requirements, but they all have a purpose in organizing the summer camp experience for your child.
