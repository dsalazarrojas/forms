# Template Registration Form Church - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for registering participants for various events or activities at the church, including children's activities like "bible lessons."

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information.
2. Select your age and gender.
3. Choose the type of event you're participating in.
4. Enter the date of birth for age verification purposes.
5. Add a signature, if applicable.
6. If applicable, enter your guardian's information.
7. Sign and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name.
* **Participant Info**: Enter any relevant information about yourself that may be helpful for the event staff.
* **Parent First Name** / **Parent Last Name**: Enter your parent's or guardian's first name and last name, if applicable.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Liability Acceptance**: Select "Accept" to indicate you understand and accept the liability waiver.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Gender**: Select your gender (male, female, other, or unknown).
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you're participating in (e.g., "bible lessons").
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your registration.
* **Guardian First Name** / **Guardian Last Name**: Enter your parent's or guardian's first name and last name, if applicable.
* **Guardian Phone**: Enter your parent's or guardian's phone number, if applicable.
* **Guardian Signature**: Sign your parent's or guardian's name to confirm their consent, if applicable.

Note that some fields are only required if you're a participant's guardian, indicated by "if applicable." Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely, and review the fields carefully before submission.
