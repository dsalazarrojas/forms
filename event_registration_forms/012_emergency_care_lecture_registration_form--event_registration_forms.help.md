# emergency_care_lecture_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The emergency care lecture registration form is used to gather important information from individuals seeking to register for an emergency care lecture. This form captures essential details such as name, surname, email, phone number, and other relevant information to process the registration successfully.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the emergency care lecture registration form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) mark.
3. Double-check your contact information and other details to avoid any discrepancies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Surname** (`surname`, `text`, required): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): If someone you'd like to be contacted in case of an emergency, enter their name and contact information here.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person you listed in the previous field.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, `number`, required): This is the unique ID for the emergency care lecture you are registering for.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the emergency care lecture you are registering for.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the emergency care lecture, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the emergency care lecture, if applicable.
* **Venue** (`venue`, `text`, optional): Enter the venue where the emergency care lecture will take place.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the venue where the emergency care lecture will take place.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where the emergency care lecture will take place.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, `text`, optional): Enter the state or province where the emergency care lecture will take place.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (`zip_postal_code`, `text`, optional): Enter the zip or postal code of the venue.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, optional): Enter the country where the emergency care lecture will take place.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method from the provided options.
* **Payment Transaction ID** (`payment_transaction_id`, `text`, optional): This is the unique ID for the payment transaction.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the payment status from the provided options.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the submission status from the provided options.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click the submit button to complete your registration.
