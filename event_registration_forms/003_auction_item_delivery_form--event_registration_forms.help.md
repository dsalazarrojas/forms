# Auction Item Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect necessary information for the delivery of auction items. It helps us ensure that the delivery process goes smoothly and that the purchaser receives their item on time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the fields as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct delivery method and preferred delivery date if applicable.
3. Enter any additional delivery notes if necessary.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send us the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchaser name** (`purchaser_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person who purchased the item.
* **Purchaser contact email** (`purchaser_contact`, email, required): Enter the email address where we will send delivery updates.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the complete address where the item will be delivered, including street, city, state, and postal code.
* **Item name** (`item_name`, text, required): Enter the name or title of the item being delivered.
* **Item quantity** (`item_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of units of the item being delivered.
* **Preferred delivery date** (`preferred_delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like the item to be delivered on. If no date is selected, we will deliver the item as soon as possible.
* **Delivery method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how the item should be delivered:
  + Standard shipping: Our standard shipping service.
  + Express shipping: Expedited shipping for an additional fee.
  + Local pickup: The item will be delivered at a local pickup location.
  + Courier delivery: A courier will deliver the item to the recipient.
* **Receiver signature required** (`receiver_signature_required`, select_one, required): Check this option if a signature is required upon delivery.
* **Additional delivery notes** (`delivery_notes`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or notes for the delivery team.
