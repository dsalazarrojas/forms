# Architecture Conference Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Architecture Conference Lead Generation Form is a tool to help us understand your interests, needs, and preferences as an architecture professional. By filling out this form, you will be helping us identify potential partnerships and collaborations that can benefit you and your business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out your contact information**: Please enter your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Job Title.
2. **Select your company details**: Choose your Company Size and Company Type from the drop-down menus.
3. **Indicate your conference interests**: Check all the topics that interest you most, such as Sustainable design, Technology trends, etc.
4. **Let us know why you're attending**: If you're attending the conference for business or personal reasons, please let us know.
5. **Choose your engagement level**: Select your level of engagement with our organization.
6. **First-time conference attendee**: Let us know if this is your first time attending the conference.
7. **Past conference attendance**: If you've attended other conferences in the past year, please let us know how many.
8. **Partnership interest**: Check if you're interested in partnerships or collaborations.
9. **Willing to be contacted**: Select if you're willing to be contacted about potential opportunities.
10. **Preferred contact method**: Choose your preferred way to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your work phone number.
* **Company or Organization**: Please enter your company or organization name.

* **Company Size**: Select your company size from the drop-down menu.
* **Company Type**: Select your company type from the drop-down menu.
* **Specialization or Services**: If you have a specific specialization or services you'd like to focus on, please describe them.
* **Topics of Interest**: Check all the topics that interest you most.
* **Why are you attending this conference?**: If you're attending the conference for business or personal reasons, please let us know.
* **Engagement Level**: Select your level of engagement with our organization.
* **Is this your first conference?**: Check if this is your first time attending the conference.
* **Past conference attendance**: If you've attended other conferences in the past year, please let us know how many.
* **Interested in partnerships or collaborations?**: Check if you're interested in partnerships or collaborations.
* **Willing to be contacted?**: Select if you're willing to be contacted about potential opportunities.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose your preferred way to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, Text message, LinkedIn).
