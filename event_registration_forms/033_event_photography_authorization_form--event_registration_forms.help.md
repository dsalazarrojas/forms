# Event Photography Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from participants for photography at events. It ensures that participants are aware of the photography activities, the terms of their participation, and their consent for the use of their likeness in photos.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information:
   * Fill in the participant's name and email (Fields 2 and 3).
   * Choose the photographer's name (Field 4).
   * Fill in the photographer's email (Field 5).
2. Select the authorization and publication options:
   * Select "Yes" or "No" (Field 6) to indicate if you grant permission for photography.
   * Choose which options (Field 7) you grant permission for.
3. Provide additional consent information:
   * Fill in any additional consent or comments (Field 8).
4. Enter event details:
   * Enter the event name (Field 9).
   * Enter the event date and time (Field 10).
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's name as it appears on their photo ID or official documentation.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the photographer taking the event photos.
* **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`, email, required): Enter the photographer's email address.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to grant permission for photography, or "No" to deny it.
* **Authorized for Publication** (`authorized_for_publication`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the options for which you grant permission to publish your likeness in photos.
* **Consent Provides** (`consent_provides`, text, optional): Enter any additional consent or comments.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have filled in all required fields correctly.
* Verify that you have entered the correct event details.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you are granting the correct permissions.
