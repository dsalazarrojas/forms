# sponsorship-alignment-reception-rsvp-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event attendees for a sponsorship alignment reception. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the event, sponsorship, and attendee preferences, which will be used to plan and organize the event logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the event name**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
2. **Choose the event date**: Select the date of the event.
3. **Enter your sponsorship details**: Type in your sponsorship title.
4. **Specify your sponsor**: Enter your name as the sponsor.
5. **Indicate the number of guests**: Enter the total number of guests you plan to bring.
6. **Choose your dietary needs**: Select from the options (gluten-free, vegetarian, or other) that best describes your dietary needs.
7. **Provide additional dietary needs (if applicable)**: If you have specific dietary needs that are not listed, provide more information in the "Dietary Needs - Other" field.
8. **Indicate your attendance**: Confirm whether you will be attending the event.
9. **Add any comments**: If you have any additional comments or requests, type them in the space provided.
10. **Provide your contact information**: Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event-name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the event using the date picker calendar.
* **Sponsorship Title** (`sponsorship_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a unique title for your sponsorship.
* **Sponsor** (`sponsor`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the sponsor.
* **Guests** (`guests`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of guests you plan to bring.
* **Dietary Needs** (`dietary_needs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from the options: gluten-free, vegetarian, or other.
* **Dietary Needs - Other** (`dietary_needs_other`, text, required/optional): If you have specific dietary needs that are not listed, provide more information in this field.
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm whether you will be attending the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, type them in this space.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to reach you.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the event date to ensure you are entering the correct date.
* If you have specific dietary needs that are not listed, provide more information in the "Dietary Needs - Other" field.
* If you have any comments or requests, please include them in the "Comments" field.
* If you need to contact us, we'll use the email address you provide.
