# Hiking Club Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiking Club Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from potential members interested in joining the hiking club. This form aims to streamline the enrollment process and ensure a smooth onboarding experience for all members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information on **User Information** (Page 1).
2. Enter your contact information on **Contact Info** (Page 1).
3. Select your hiking experience level on **Hiking Experience** (Page 1).
4. Provide emergency contact information, including any relevant medical details on **Emergency Info** and **Emergency Contact** (Page 2).
5. Select the "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" option on **Terms and Conditions** (Page 2).
6. Finally, submit your form on **Submit** (Page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with the club.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including your phone number and address, so the club can reach out to you.
* **Hiking Experience**: Select the level of hiking experience you have, from Hiking to Cycling.
* **Emergency Info**: Provide any relevant medical information that may be important for the club to know.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and relationship of the person you'd like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Select the option to indicate that you've read and agree to the club's terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if applicable).
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Answer honestly and thoroughly when selecting your hiking experience level.
* Provide any relevant medical information if applicable.
* Review the terms and conditions before selecting the "I have read and agree" option.
