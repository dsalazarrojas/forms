# Change Management Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering individuals for events or trainings related to change management. It captures relevant information about the registrant, their organization, and their expectations from the event or training.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, denoted by a asterisk (*), as follows:
	* Registration Date: The date of registration.
	* First Name: Your first name as it appears on your identification.
	* Last Name: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
	* Email Address: Your professional email address.
	* Phone Number: A valid phone number for contact purposes.
	* Event or Training Name: The name of the event or training you are registering for.
	* Event or Training Date: The date of the event or training.
	* Attendance Format: The format of the event or training (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
2. Provide any additional information about your preferences and expectations as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): The date and time of your registration for the event or training.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your professional email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): A valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): The name of your company or institution.
* **Job Title or Position** (`job_title`, text, required): Your job title or position within your organization.
* **Department or Division** (`department`, text, optional): The department or division within your organization.
* **Years in Current Role** (`work_experience`, number, optional): The number of years you have been in your current role.
* **Event or Training Name** (`event_name`, text, required): The name of the event or training you are registering for.
* **Event or Training Date** (`event_date`, date, required): The date of the event or training.
* **Attendance Format** (`attendance_format`, select_one, required): The format of the event or training (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
* **Location Preference** (`location_preference`, text, optional): If applicable, specify any location preferences.
* **Change Management Experience Level** (`change_management_experience`, select_one, optional): Your experience level in change management (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, text, optional): Any learning objectives you have for the event or training.
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, text, optional): Any current challenges you are facing in change management.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, optional): Specify any accessibility accommodations you need (None, Wheelchair Access, Interpreting Services, or Other).
* **Describe Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the accessibility accommodations you need.
* **Team Registration** (`team_registration`, select_one, optional): Are others from your team registering (Yes or No).
* **Team Member Names** (`team_members`, text, optional): Names of other team members registering.
