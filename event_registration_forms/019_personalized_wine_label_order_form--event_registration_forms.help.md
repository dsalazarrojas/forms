# Personalized Wine Label Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to create a personalized wine label that matches their specific order details. To place an order, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your winery of choice from the provided options.
2. Choose the label design that suits your taste.
3. Enter your order information, including the wine bottle count.
4. Enter your email address to receive the order confirmation and label design preview.
5. Review your order and click "Submit" to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Winery Information (Page 1, id: 5)
* **Winery** (Winery A, Winery B, Winery C, select_one): Select the winery that produced the wine you are ordering.

### Label Designs (Page 1, id: 2)
* **Label Designs** (Design 1, Design 2, Design 3, select_multiple): Choose up to 3 label designs that appeal to you.

### Order Information (Page 1, id: 3)
* **Order Information** (text): Enter a brief description of your order, including the type of wine and any special requests.

### Payment Information (Page 1, id: 4)
* **Payment Information** (text): Enter your payment method and any additional payment details.

### Winery (Page 1, id: 5)
* **Winery** (Winery A, Winery B, Winery C, select_one): Select the winery that produced the wine you are ordering. This field is the same as on the "Winery Information" page, but is repeated here for clarity and convenience.

### Label Type (Page 1, id: 6)
* **Label Type** (Label 1, Label 2, Label 3, select_one): Choose the type of label you would like to order. This field will influence the final label design.

### Quantity (Page 1, id: 7)
* **Quantity** (number, required: false): Enter the number of bottles you are ordering. This will affect the production cost.

### Email (Page 2, id: 8)
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address where you will receive the order confirmation and label design preview. This field is not required but recommended for order tracking.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct winery and label design to avoid any errors in the order.
* If you have any special requests, please include them in the "Order Information" field.
* Review your order carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
