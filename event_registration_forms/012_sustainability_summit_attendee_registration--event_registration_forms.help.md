# sustainability_summit_attendee_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sustainability_summit_attendee_registration" form is designed to collect information from attendees of the sustainability summit. This form will be used to track attendee details, interests, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the general information section with your details.
2. Provide your contact information in the contact section.
3. Select your interests and activities in the activity interests section.
4. Choose your dietary needs in the dietary needs section.
5. Indicate your policy consent and email preferences in the policy consent and email preferences sections.
6. Provide any additional policy declarations if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing general information about yourself. Enter your name, title, or any other relevant details as you see fit.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing your contact details. Enter your email address, phone number, or any other contact information you feel comfortable sharing.

### Activity Interests
* **Activity Interests** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the activities you are interested in attending during the summit. You can choose more than one activity.

### Dietary Needs
* **Dietary Needs** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you have any dietary restrictions or needs, please select them here. You can choose more than one option.

### Policy Consent
* **Policy Consent** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes for the policies you consent to. This includes agreeing to the summit's code of conduct, terms and conditions, and other relevant policies.

### Email Preferences
* **Email Preferences** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how you would like to receive emails from the summit organizers. You can select multiple options.

### Policy Declaration
* **Policy Declaration** (`7`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional section for providing any additional policy declarations. Please enter any relevant information here.

### Tips
* Make sure to complete all the required fields.
* Double-check your selections in the activity interests, dietary needs, and policy consent sections.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the summit organizers.
* Review the summit's policies and terms before completing this form.
