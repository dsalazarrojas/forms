# Cruise Itinerary Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise Itinerary Extension Form is designed to collect information from clients about their extended travel plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to double-check your input for accuracy.
2. Ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct purpose and client.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address that they can be reached at.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter the client's street address, city, and state.
* **Cruise Id** (`cruise_id`, number, optional): If you know the cruise ID, please enter it here. 
* **Cabin Id** (`cabin_id`, number, optional): If you know the cabin ID, please enter it here. 
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required): Enter the date of travel.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, required): Enter the time of travel.
* **Cruise Details** (`cruise_details`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your cruise details.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional information to provide, enter it here.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any notes or comments, enter them here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the name of the tool assigned to this cruise.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): If you want to specify who submitted this form, enter their name here.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the form (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or feedback, enter them here.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, optional): If you want to specify who created this form, select their name here.
* **Created On** (`created_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Created Time** (`created_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was created.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select_one, optional): If you want to specify who updated this form, select their name here.
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Updated Time** (`updated_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was last updated.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Submitted By Full Name** (`submitted_by_full_name`, text, optional): If you want to specify who submitted this form, enter their full name here.

Note: Please double-check your input for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
