# Internal Training Registration Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Internal Training Registration Intake Form is a tool designed to collect necessary information from employees to register and manage internal training programs. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are captured and stored for reference and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the user details section with your relevant information.
2. Enter the training details in the second section.
3. Select your department from the department dropdown.
4. If applicable, choose your manager's name and email from the select lists.
5. Add any additional notes or file uploads to provide further context.
6. Enter the date and time of the training (if applicable).
7. Confirm your email address for contact purposes.
8. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
9. Confirm your manager's email address (if applicable).
10. Enter your manager's phone number (if applicable).
11. Add any manager notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required): This section captures the basic details of the user registering for the training, which helps in identifying the trainee.
* **Training Details** (`training_details`, `text`, required): Enter the specifics of the training, including the topic, objectives, and any relevant information.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department to which the training belongs. This helps in tracking and reporting purposes.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your manager's name if the training is being conducted under their supervision.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your manager's email address for contact purposes.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's phone number (if applicable).
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant notes from your manager for the training.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes you want to provide to the training team.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, `text`, optional): Upload any relevant files or documents for the training.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Confirm your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time of the training (if applicable).

Note: Some fields are marked as optional but are still important to fill in when necessary for accurate tracking and reporting.
