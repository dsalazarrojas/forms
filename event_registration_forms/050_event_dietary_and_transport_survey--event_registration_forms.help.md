# Event Dietary And Transport Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your dietary and transport requirements for an upcoming event. Please answer these questions carefully to help us ensure that we can meet your needs during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to save your responses.
2. Take your time to fill out each page of the form carefully.
3. You can move between pages using the navigation buttons.
4. If you need to go back to a previous page, use the back button.
5. When you are finished, click "Submit" to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page**: This is the initial page of the form where you can select the type of event you are attending.
* **Meal Page**: This page allows you to indicate your dietary preferences for the event. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-free
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Transport Page**: This page lets you inform us about your transportation options for the event. You can choose from the following options:
	+ I will drive myself
	+ I will be dropped off
	+ I will be picked up
	+ Other (please specify)

## Tips
- Please answer honestly about your dietary preferences and transport needs.
- If you have any special requests or concerns, please specify them in the "Other" field.
- If you are unsure about any of the options, please ask the event organizers for clarification.
- You can contact us at form@gic.mx if you have any questions or need further assistance.
