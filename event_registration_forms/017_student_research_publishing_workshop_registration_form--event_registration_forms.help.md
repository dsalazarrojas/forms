# Student Research Publishing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from students for participation in the Student Research Publishing Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Registration Form**: This section is the main form where you can provide your basic contact information.
2.  **Contact Information**: The following fields are located in this section:
	*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
	*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
	*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
	*   **Address** (`address`, `note`, required/optional): Enter your address.
3.  **Research Paper Details**: The following fields are located in this section:
	*   **Research Paper Title** (`research_paper_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title of your research paper.
	*   **Research Paper Abstract** (`research_paper_abstract`, `note`, required/optional): Enter a summary of your research paper.
	*   **Research Paper URL** (`research_paper_file_url`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the URL of your research paper.
4.  **Department and Topic**: The following fields are located in this section:
	*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your university department from the options provided.
	*   **Research Paper Topic** (`research_paper_topic`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your research paper topic.
5.  **Workshop Participation and Submission**: The following fields are located in this section:
	*   **Workshop Participation** (`workshop_participation`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate if you plan to participate in the workshop.
	*   **Submission Agreement** (`submission_agreement`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm your submission agreement status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Form**: Enter your contact information in this section.
*   **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Address**: Enter your address.
*   **Research Paper Title**: Enter a brief title of your research paper.
*   **Research Paper Abstract**: Enter a summary of your research paper.
*   **Research Paper URL**: Enter the URL of your research paper.
*   **Department**: Choose your university department from the options provided.
*   **Research Paper Topic**: Enter a brief description of your research paper topic.
*   **Workshop Participation**: Indicate if you plan to participate in the workshop.
*   **Submission Agreement**: Confirm your submission agreement status.
*   **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about your research paper.
*   **Submission Agreement**: Confirm your submission agreement status. 

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
*   Double-check your contact information for errors.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

Note: The fields marked as optional may be required depending on your specific situation. Always refer to the workshop requirements and guidelines for more information.
