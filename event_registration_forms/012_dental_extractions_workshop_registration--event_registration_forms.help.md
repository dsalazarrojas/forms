# Dental Extractions Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information for the "Dental Extractions Workshop" event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred session from the "Session Selection" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Check the "Terms Signed" field to confirm you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Select the session duration from the "Session Duration" field.
7. Select the number of delegates accompanying you from the "Number of Delegates" field.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Fill in the "Submitted By" field with your name.
10. Select the date for the session start and end from the "Session Start Date" and "Session End Date" fields.
11. Enter the date the workshop will start and end from the "Workshop Start Date" and "Workshop End Date" fields.
12. Review and submit the form.
