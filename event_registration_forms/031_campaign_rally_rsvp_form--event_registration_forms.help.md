# Campaign Rally RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Campaign Rally RSVP Form. Please read through it carefully to ensure you provide accurate and helpful information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply select the relevant fields and input the requested information. Each field is described in detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`attendee_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`attendee_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city or town of your location.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your location.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your location.
* **Rally Name** (`rally_name`, text, required): Select the name of the rally you are attending.
* **Rally Date** (`rally_date`, date, required): Select the date of the rally event.
* **Rally Location** (`rally_location`, text, required): Select the location of the rally event.
* **Number of People Attending** (`number_attending`, select_one, required): Select the number of people attending the rally. This includes yourself.
* **Will You Bring Children** (`children_attending`, select_one, required): Select whether you will bring children.
* **Do You Have Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required): Select whether you have accessibility needs.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, text, optional): If you have accessibility needs, provide a description.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, optional): Select whether you need transportation assistance.
* **Are You Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, required): Select whether you are interested in volunteering.
* **Volunteer Tasks** (`volunteer_tasks`, select_multiple, optional): If you are interested in volunteering, select the tasks you would like to help with.
* **Will You Bring Signs or Materials** (`signs_materials`, select_one, optional): Select whether you will bring signs or materials.
* **How Did You Hear About This Rally** (`how_heard_about_rally`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you heard about the rally.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): If food is provided, select your dietary restrictions.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): If rally shirts are provided, select your shirt size.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions.
* **How Would You Like to Receive Confirmation** (`confirmation_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred method of confirmation.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the Campaign Rally RSVP Form accurately and help us better prepare for the event.
