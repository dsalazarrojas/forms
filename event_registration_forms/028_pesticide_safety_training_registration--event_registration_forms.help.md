# Pesticide Safety Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register users for Pesticide Safety Training sessions. Please follow the steps below to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the session you want to attend from the session selection field.
2. Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the personal details fields.
3. Choose whether you have read and understood the terms and conditions in the acknowledgement field.
4. Confirm that your information is correct in the confirm email field.
5. Submit the form to complete the registration process.
