# DIY Workshops Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DIY Workshops Reservation Form is designed to allow participants to register for a DIY workshop event. To complete this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Participant Information**: Fill in your participant information, including your name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
2. **Choose Workshop**: Select one or more workshops that you would like to attend. This will help us confirm your seat and provide you with more details about the workshop.
3. **Date**: You can also select a date for the workshop, but this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Choose Workshop**: Select one or more workshops that you would like to attend. This field is required to confirm your seat and allow us to provide you with more details about the workshop.
  - You can select one or more workshops from the options provided. If you have any issues selecting a workshop, please contact us for assistance.
  - You can select multiple workshops by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) and clicking on multiple options.
- **Participant Name**: Enter your name to identify you as a participant.
  - Please ensure that your name is spelled correctly and includes any necessary titles (e.g., Mr., Ms., Dr.).
  - If you have a preferred name or nickname, you can enter that as well.
- **Participant Email**: Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you about event details and updates.
  - Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.
  - If you have any issues with your email, please contact us for assistance.
- **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
  - Please use a valid phone number that you check regularly.
  - If you have any issues with your phone, please contact us for assistance.
- **Notes**: This is an optional field where you can add any additional notes about your registration.
  - You can use this field to provide any additional information that you think will help us better understand your needs.
  - You can enter any information that you think will help us to provide you with a better experience.
- **Date**: Select a date for the workshop if you would like to attend on a specific date.
  - Please ensure that you select a date that is within the event timeframe.
  - If you have any issues selecting a date, please contact us for assistance.

## Tips

* Please make sure to review your form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
* We will contact you with more details about the workshop once we receive your form.
* We will also contact you to confirm your seat once we have received your form and selected the workshop.
