# HIV Stigma Awareness Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details about events related to HIV stigma awareness. It will help organizers, speakers, or attendees provide relevant information about these events to improve the effectiveness and reach of HIV stigma awareness initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Event Details** section with relevant information about the event, such as its title, description, and date.
2.  In the **Attendee Details** section, provide information about the attendees, such as their names, affiliations, and roles within the event.
3.  For **Contact Info**, enter the relevant contact details for the event organizer or the person submitting the form.
4.  If you have any additional support information, use the **Support Info** field to provide it.
5.  List any event participants in the **Event Participants** field.
6.  Enter details about the event sessions or activities in the **Event Sessions** field.
7.  If there's any other information you'd like to share about the event or initiative, use the **Additional Info** field.
8.  Finally, click the **Submit** button to send your information for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (Event Details): A field where you can describe the event, its title, and any other relevant details.
* **Attendee Details** (Attendee Details): Enter the names, affiliations, and roles of the attendees or speakers participating in the event.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info): Provide the contact details of the event organizer, the person submitting the form, or any other contact relevant to the event.
* **Support Info** (Support Info): Enter any additional support details for the event, such as volunteers or sponsors.
* **Event Participants** (Event Participants): List the names of any individuals participating in the event.
* **Event Sessions** (Event Sessions): Describe the sessions or activities held during the event.
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info): Enter any other information you think would be helpful for understanding the event or initiative.
* **Submit** (Submit): Click this button to finalize your submission and send it for processing.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Double-check all the information provided for accuracy and completeness.
- For any questions or concerns, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
