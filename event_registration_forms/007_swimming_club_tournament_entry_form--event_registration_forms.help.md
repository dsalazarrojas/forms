# Swimming Club Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register swimmers for the Swimming Club Tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the swimmer's details in the "Swimmer 1" page.
2. Enter the swimmer's details in the "Swimmer 2" page.
3. Enter the swimmer's details in the "Swimmer 3" page.
4. Select the event from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the swimmer's first name, last name, and email address.
6. Select the swim club from the dropdown menu.
7. Select the parent/coach from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter the phone number.
9. Enter the date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
10. Enter the time of day in the format HH:MM.
11. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **swimmers_name** (Text): Enter the swimmer's name.
* **parent_or_coach** (Text): Enter the swimmer's parent or coach's name.
* **swimmer_2** (Text): Enter the swimmer's name.
* **event_1** (Select One): Select the event the swimmer is participating in.
* **swimmer_1_first_name** (Text): Enter the swimmer's first name.
* **swimmer_1_last_name** (Text): Enter the swimmer's last name.
* **swimmer_1_email** (Email): Enter the swimmer's email address.
* **swimmer_2_first_name** (Text): Enter the swimmer's first name.
* **swimmer_2_last_name** (Text): Enter the swimmer's last name.
* **swimmer_2_email** (Email): Enter the swimmer's email address.
* **swim club** (Select One): Select the swim club the swimmer is affiliated with.
* **parent** (Select One): Select the swimmer's parent or coach.
* **phone_number** (Text): Enter the swimmer's phone number.
* **phone** (Text): Enter the swimmer's phone number.
* **date_of_birth** (Date): Enter the swimmer's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **time_of_day** (Time): Enter the swimmer's time of day in the format HH:MM.
* **additional_comments** (Note): Enter any additional comments about the swimmer.

## Tips
* Please ensure that each swimmer has a unique entry.
* Make sure to enter all the required fields.
