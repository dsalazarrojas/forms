# Venue Rental Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Venue Rental Intake Form is used to gather necessary information for event registration and rental processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete this form accurately.
2. Fill in your event name in the **event_name** field.
3. Provide the date of your event in the **event_date** field in the correct format (MM/DD/YY).
4. Select the correct event type in the **event_type** field, which will help us better understand your event needs.
5. Enter the number of guests attending your event in the **event_guests** field.
6. Provide the event location in the **event_location** field.
7. Add any additional event notes in the **event_notes** field.
8. Enter your name in the **contact_name** field.
9. Provide your email address in the **contact_email** field.
10. Enter your phone number in the **contact_phone** field.
11. Enter your contact address in the **contact_address** field.
