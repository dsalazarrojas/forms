# exam_registration_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for exam registration confirmation and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select the appropriate options for each field as they apply to your situation.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields.
3. Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Confirm Examination** (`confirm_examination`, select_multiple, not required): Choose whether or not the exam has been confirmed.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the exam.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, note, not required): Enter any additional information about the student, if necessary.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the student.
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the exam.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of the student.
* **Exam** (`examination`, text, not required): Enter the name of the exam.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the exam.
* **Exam Duration** (`exam_duration`, number, not required): Enter the duration of the exam.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the exam (note: this field should not be confused with the same field above).
* **Exam Status** (`exam_status`, select_one, not required): Choose whether the exam is active or inactive.
* **Exam Result** (`exam_result`, select_one, not required): Choose whether the exam was passed or failed.
* **Exam Note** (`exam_note`, select_multiple, not required): Choose whether certain notes apply to the exam.
