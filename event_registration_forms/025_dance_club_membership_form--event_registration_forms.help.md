# Dance Club Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register as a member of the dance club. It is essential to provide accurate and clear information to complete the membership registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field. Please use the format "user@example.com".
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your birth date in the "birth date" field using the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
6. Enter your birth time in the "birth time" field using the 12-hour clock format (HH:MM AM/PM).
7. Select your preferred dance style from the list in the "dance style" field.
8. Enter the length of your desired membership in the "membership length" field.
9. Provide a hint for the membership length in the "membership length hint" field (e.g., "months").
10. Choose your preferred payment method(s) from the list in the "payment method" field.
11. Enter the name of the emergency contact person in the "emergency contact name" field.
12. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person in the "emergency contact phone" field.
13. If you have any medical conditions that require attention, enter them in the "medical condition" field.
14. If necessary, provide any additional information related to your medical condition in the "medical condition note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name** (text, required): Enter your given name.
* **last name** (text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Provide your email address in the standard format "user@example.com".
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **birth date** (date, required): Enter your birth date in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **birth time** (time, optional): Enter your birth time in the 12-hour clock format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **dance style** (select_one, required): Choose your preferred dance style from the list.
* **membership length** (number, optional): Enter the length of your desired membership.
* **membership length hint** (text, optional): Provide a hint for the membership length (e.g., "months").
* **payment method** (select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred payment method(s) from the list.
* **emergency contact name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency contact phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **medical condition** (text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **medical condition note** (note, optional): Provide any additional information related to your medical condition.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure smooth processing of your membership application.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above or contact the dance club staff for assistance.
