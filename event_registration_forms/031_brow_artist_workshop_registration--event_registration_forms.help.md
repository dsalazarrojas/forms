# Brow Artist Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for the Brow Artist Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Contact email** in the second field. This will be used for confirmations and updates.
3. If you have a **Salon affiliation**, enter the name of your salon or employer in the third field. (Optional)
4. Select your **Experience level** from the options: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
5. Choose the **Course date** of the workshop you are registering for.
6. If you need any **Materials**, list them in this field.
7. Confirm your **Payment status** by selecting from the options: Paid, Pending, or Waived.
8. Finally, list any **Dietary restrictions** you may have, for our catered events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (Participant name)**:
 Participant name: *Your full name as it should be listed in registration* (`Participant_name`, `text`, **Required**)
* **Contact Email (Contact email)**:
 Contact email: *Your email for confirmations and updates* (`Contact_email`, `email`, **Required**)
* **Salon Affiliation (Salon affiliation)**:
 Salon affiliation: *Name of salon or employer* (`Salon_affiliation`, `text`, **Optional**)
* **Experience Level (Experience level)**:
 Experience level: *Select your experience level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced* (`Experience_level`, `select_one`, **Required**)
* **Course Date (Course date)**:
 Course date: *Date of the workshop* (`Course_date`, `date`, **Required**)
* **Materials Needed (Materials needed)**:
 Materials needed: *Tools or supplies participants should bring* (`Materials_needed`, `text`, **Optional**)
* **Payment Status (Payment status)**:
 Payment status: *Confirm if payment has been made: Paid, Pending, or Waived* (`Payment_status`, `select_one`, **Required**)
* **Dietary Restrictions (Dietary restrictions)**:
 Dietary restrictions: *For catered events, list any dietary restrictions* (`Dietary_restrictions`, `text`, **Optional**)
