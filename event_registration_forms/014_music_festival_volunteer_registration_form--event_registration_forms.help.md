# Music Festival Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for the music festival. It is used to match volunteers with suitable shifts and maximize their contribution to the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Scroll through the form and review the different sections.
2. Provide your volunteer information in the "Volunteer Information" section.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
4. Select your available shifts in the "Volunteer Shifts" section.
5. Enter your available volunteer hours in the "Volunteer Hours" section.
6. Share your volunteer experience in the "Volunteer Experience" section (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Information**: This section is where you can provide your name, email, and any other relevant information about your volunteering experience.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, including your email and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Volunteer Shifts**: Choose the shifts you are available to work, which include:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are willing to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Experience**: If you have previous volunteer experience, provide a brief description of your experience in this section (optional).
