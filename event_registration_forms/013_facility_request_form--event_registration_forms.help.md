# Facility Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request facilities for events. It helps gather necessary information to efficiently process and manage event requests. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access this form. To avoid duplicate entries, please do not submit multiple requests for the same event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and select your options as prompted. Make sure to review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Form**: This is the main form where you can enter all the event details.
* **Select Facility**: Choose one or more facilities that you would like to use for your event. Selecting multiple facilities will allow you to have access to multiple locations within the facility.
* **Event Start Date**: Enter the date when your event will start.
* **Event End Date**: Enter the date when your event will end.
* **Event Duration**: Enter the length of time your event will last.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the time of day when your event will start.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the time of day when your event will end.
* **Facility Location**: Enter the specific location within the selected facility where your event will take place.
* **Event Name**: Enter a brief title for your event.
* **Event Notes**: Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the event organizers.
* **Event Priority**: Choose the priority level of your event (High, Medium, Low).
* **Event Status**: Choose the status of your event (Approved, Not Approved, Pending).
* **Facility 1, Facility 2, Facility 3**: Select the facilities you would like to use for your event.
