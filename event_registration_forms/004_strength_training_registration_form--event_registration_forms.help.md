# Strength Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strength Training Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants who wish to register for a strength training program. This includes details about the participant, their contact information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing participant information on Page 1 of the form. Fill out the label with your name, email, and any other relevant details as requested.
2.  On Page 2, provide your contact information, which will be used for communication purposes. Ensure you enter accurate details as this is how we will contact you.
3.  Finally, provide payment details on Page 3. Enter your payment method and any other required information to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Information (Page 1):** Participant Information (`participant_information`, text, **optional**)
    Enter your name and any other relevant details about your participation in the strength training program.

- **Contact Information (Page 2):** Contact Information (`contact_information`, text, **optional**)
    Enter your address, phone number, and any other contact details for us to reach out to you effectively.

- **Payment Details (Page 3):** Payment Details (`payment_details`, text, **optional**)
    Provide the payment method and any other required information for the payment process.
