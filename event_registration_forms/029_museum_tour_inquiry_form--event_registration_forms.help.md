# museum_tour_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "museum_tour_inquiry_form" is a user-friendly form designed to gather information from museum visitors to plan and execute tours, track visitor engagement, and improve the overall visitor experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select the date of your visit to the museum in the "date_of_visit" field.
5. Choose the time of your visit in the "time_of_visit" field.
6. Select one option from the "select one" dropdown menu.
7. Select multiple options from the "select multiple" dropdown menu (if applicable).
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "note" field.
9. If applicable, select a tool assigned to you for the tour in the "assigned_tool" field.
10. Enter a unique identifier (ID) in the "form_id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1): Enter your first name, as displayed on your ID or passport.
* **email** (2): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **phone** (3): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **date_of_visit** (4): Select the date you plan to visit the museum.
* **time_of_visit** (5): Select the time you plan to visit the museum.
* **select one** (6): Choose one option that best suits your needs or preferences.
* **select multiple** (7): If applicable, select multiple options that best suit your needs or preferences. 
* **note** (8): Provide any additional comments or notes about your visit or tour.
* **assigned_tool** (9): If applicable, select a tool assigned to you by museum staff for the tour.
* **form_id** (10): Enter a unique identifier (ID) as provided by the museum.
* **category** (11): Enter the category of your interest or visit (e.g., "Educational," "Fun Day Out").
* **description** (12): Enter a brief description of your category or visit (e.g., "Learn about history," "Enjoy art").
* **output_file** (13): Enter the file where you can receive your output data.
* **form_id2**, **form_id3**, **form_id4**, **form_id5** (14, 17, 20, 23): These fields are for museum administrative use and should not be filled out by regular visitors.
* **category2, category3, category4, category5** (15, 18, 21, 24): These fields are for museum administrative use and should not be filled out by regular visitors.
* **assigned_tool2, assigned_tool3, assigned_tool4, assigned_tool5** (16, 19, 22, 25): These fields are for museum administrative use and should not be filled out by regular visitors.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the museum staff can plan and execute a smooth tour for you.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to museum staff for assistance.
* The form is designed to be user-friendly, and you should feel comfortable filling it out. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the museum staff.
