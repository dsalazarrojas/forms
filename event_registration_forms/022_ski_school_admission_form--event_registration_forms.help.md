# Ski School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who wish to attend a ski school. It collects contact and demographic information to help us organize and manage your registration and provide a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your information.
    *   You can type in the required and optional fields as instructed.
    *   Make sure to review and double-check your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Info** (`student_info`): This is where you will enter your name and other identifying details about yourself.
*   **Address** (`address`): Please enter your home address, including street number and postal code.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Provide your contact number, email, or both for us to reach you.
*   **Event Info** (`event_info`): Enter any relevant details about the event or lesson you are registering for.
*   **Parent Info** (`parent_info`): If you are a minor, please include a parent or guardian contact information.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and contact information for an emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Info** (`medical_info`): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please provide information for emergency purposes.
*   **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): This is where you can provide any extra details about your participation in the ski school that may be helpful for us to know.
