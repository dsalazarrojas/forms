# Community Service Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering individuals who want to participate in community service events such as volunteers, team members, and participants. The form will ask for personal details, event details, skills, experience, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth and safe event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" with a red asterisk (*).
2. Select the relevant options for your event and availability.
3. Make sure to select all that apply for skills you can contribute and project preferences.
4. If you are registering with a team, fill out the team details.
5. Finally, review your answers carefully and submit the form.
