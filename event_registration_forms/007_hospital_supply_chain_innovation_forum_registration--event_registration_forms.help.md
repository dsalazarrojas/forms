# hospital_supply_chain_innovation_forum_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Hospital Supply Chain Innovation Forum. It's designed to collect basic contact information, role and organization details, and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your basic contact information: first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your role and organization from the provided options.
3. Choose your event interests from the provided options.
4. Confirm your email address and phone number.
5. Choose whether or not you have an agenda for the event.
6. Select whether or not you have speakers to bring to the event.
7. Choose your registration status for the event.
8. Select the category for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Role** (4, select_one, required: false): Select your role: CEO, Manager, Healthcare Professional, or Other.
* **Organization** (5, text, required: false): Enter your organization name.
* **Department** (6, text, required: false): Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (7, text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Phone** (8, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (9, email, required: false): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (10, text, required: false): Confirm your phone number.
* **Event Interests** (11, select_multiple, required: false): Select your event interests: Innovation, Supply Chain, Healthcare, or Other.
* **Comments** (12, note, required: false): Add any additional comments about the event.
* **Event Date** (13, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (14, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **Registration Confirmation** (15, select_one, required: false): Choose whether or not you want to confirm your registration.
* **Terms and Conditions** (16, select_one, required: false): Choose whether or not you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (17, text, required: false): Submit your registration form.
* **Event Date 2** (18, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event (optional).
* **Event Location** (19, text, required: false): Enter the location of the event (optional).
* **Event Description** (20, text, required: false): Enter a description of the event (optional).
* **Event Type** (21, select_one, required: false): Select the type of event: Conference, Meeting, Workshop, or Seminar.
* **Has Agenda** (22, select_one, required: false): Choose whether or not the event has an agenda.
* **Agenda Items** (23, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether or not you have agenda items.
* **Speakers** (24, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether or not you have speakers to bring to the event.
* **Registration Status** (25, select_one, required: false): Choose your registration status: Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled.
* **Category** (26, text, required: false): Enter the category of the event (optional).
