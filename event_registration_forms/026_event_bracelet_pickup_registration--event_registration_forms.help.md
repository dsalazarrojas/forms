# Event Bracelet Pickup Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register an event that occurred within a specific time frame. It collects information such as the date, time, location, and contact details of the event, as well as any additional comments related to the event.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the steps below to complete the form:

1.  Fill in the `event_bracelet_pickup_date` field with the date on which the event took place. Use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD) as prompted.
2.  Provide the time the event happened in the `event_bracelet_pickup_time` field.
3.  Enter the location where the event occurred in the `event_bracelet_pickup_location` field.
4.  Enter the name of the person in charge or contact person's name in the `event_bracelet_pickup_contact_name` field.
5.  Enter the contact person's email address in the `event_bracelet_pickup_email` field.
6.  Enter the contact person's phone number in the `event_bracelet_pickup_phone` field.
7.  Add any additional comments or details about the event in the `event_bracelet_pickup_comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **event_bracelet_pickup_date** (`date`, required): Enter the date on which the event occurred.
*  **event_bracelet_pickup_time** (`time`, required): Enter the time the event took place.
*  **event_bracelet_pickup_location** (`text`, required): Enter the location of the event.
*  **event_bracelet_pickup_contact_name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the person in charge or contact person.
*  **event_bracelet_pickup_email** (`email`, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
*  **event_bracelet_pickup_phone** (`text`, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
*  **event_bracelet_pickup_comments** (`note`, required): Enter any additional comments about the event.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate information, especially the date and time of the event, as it will help in the proper processing of the event.
