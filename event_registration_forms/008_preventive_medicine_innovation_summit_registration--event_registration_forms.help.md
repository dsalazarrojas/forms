# Preventive Medicine Innovation Summit Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Preventive Medicine Innovation Summit Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees, organizers, and speakers of the Preventive Medicine Innovation Summit. This form will help us understand who will be attending the summit, their role, and any relevant event details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
*   Enter your name and email address to identify yourself.
*   Provide your phone number for further contact if needed.
*   Select your attendance type (e.g., Delegate, Organizer, Speaker).
*   Add any additional details about your participation in the summit.
*   Provide organized event details if you are an organizer or speaker.

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address where we can contact you.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further contact if needed.
*   **Attendance Type** (`attendance_type`, `select_one`, required): Select from the options:
    *   **Delegate**: If you are attending as a delegate.
    *   **Organizer**: If you are organizing the event.
    *   **Speaker**: If you are presenting at the summit.
*   **Additional Details** (`details`, `note`, required): Write down any additional information about your participation in the summit.
*   **Organized Event Details** (`organized_event_details`, `note`, required): If you are an organizer or speaker, please provide more details about the event you are organizing or speaking at.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all the required fields for a successful submission.
*   Ensure your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Be as detailed as possible in the "Additional Details" and "Organized Event Details" sections to help us better understand your role and participation.
