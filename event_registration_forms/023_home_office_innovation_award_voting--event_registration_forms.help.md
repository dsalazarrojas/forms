# home_office_innovation_award_voting - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select the innovative project you wish to vote for.
3. Ensure that you have filled in all required fields correctly and accurately.
4. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Home Office Innovation Award Voting Form** (`home_office_innovation_award_voting_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, and it is not a field that needs to be filled in. You can ignore it.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of your department or team that you are submitting for.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address used for company communication. This is how your vote will be verified and recorded.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your office phone number. This is not required for verification, but it can be useful for contact purposes if needed.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the project submission, if you know it. This is not required but can be helpful for record-keeping.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the project submission, if you know it. This is not required but can be helpful for record-keeping.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and correctly.
- You can only submit one vote per project.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact your HR department.
- The deadline for submitting this form will be communicated separately.
