# pr_session_attendee_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to register for a workshop session, providing organizers with necessary information to create a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the workshop from the options provided.
2. Choose the topics you are interested in.
3. Enter your email address, name, and phone number.
4. Select the session date and time.
5. Optionally, add any additional session details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the workshop?**: Choose from the options:
	+ Participant: I am attending the workshop as a participant.
	+ Organizer: I am organizing the workshop.
	+ Speaker: I am speaking at the workshop.
	+ Other: I have another role, please specify below.
* **What topics are you interested in?**: Select all the topics you are interested in from the list provided.
* **What is your email address?**: Enter your email address as it appears in your email account.
* **What is your name?**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents or correspondence.
* **What is your phone number?**: Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX XXX XXXX".
* **Session date?**: Select the date of the workshop session.
* **Session time?**: Select the time of the workshop session.
* **Additional session details?**: If you have any additional information about the session, please provide it here (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure that you have selected the correct role and topics to ensure that you receive relevant information and updates about the workshop.
* Double-check your email address, name, and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the workshop organizers.
* Please note that the session date and time are subject to change; if you are unable to attend, please let us know as soon as possible.
