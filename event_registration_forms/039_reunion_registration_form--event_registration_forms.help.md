# Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reunion Registration Form is designed to gather essential details about an upcoming reunion event. This form helps in creating a centralised database of events, making it easier for participants to find and join the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** (Field 1) with a short and descriptive title of your reunion event.
2. Enter your **Name** (Field 2) as it appears on your official documents, such as ID or passport.
3. Provide your **Contact Number** (Field 3) so that other participants can easily contact you for more information or questions.
4. Enter a valid **Email Address** (Field 4) so that other participants can reach out to you through this contact method.
5. Select the **Date of Event** (Field 5) accurately for the day of the scheduled reunion.
6. Choose the **Time of Event** (Field 6) correctly for the scheduled reunion time.
7. Provide the **Venue** (Field 7) where the reunion will take place.
8. If required, add a brief **Description** (Field 8) of the event to make it more visible in the database.
9. If needed, specify the **Number Attending** (Field 9) for your event.
10. If necessary, include any additional **Note** (Field 10) about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (Field 1)**: Enter a short title for the event that will be displayed in the database.
* **Name (Field 2)**: This is the name that appears on your official documents such as ID or passport.
* **Contact Number (Field 3)**: A number where other participants can contact you directly.
* **Email (Field 4)**: This is the email address that other participants can use to reach out to you for more information or questions.
* **Date of Event (Field 5)**: The date of the reunion event as accurately as possible.
* **Time of Event (Field 6)**: The scheduled time for the event.
* **Venue (Field 7)**: The location of the reunion event.
* **Description (Field 8)**: A brief description of the event to make it more visible in the database.
* **Number Attending (Field 9)**: The number of participants expected to attend the event.
* **Note (Field 10)**: Any additional details about the event that can be helpful for other participants to know.
