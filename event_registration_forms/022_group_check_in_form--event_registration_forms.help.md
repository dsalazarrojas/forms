# Group Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Group Check In Form is designed to collect information from group members upon arrival, including general contact information, arrival details, group composition, and any notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the "General Information" section with your name and any other relevant contact details.
3. Provide your "Arrival Details" in the text field.
4. Select the members of your group from the "Group Details" dropdown menu.
5. Add any notes or comments in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (text)**: This is the section where you will enter your name and any other relevant contact details. 
* **Arrival Details (text)**: Please provide your arrival details, such as date and time of arrival. 
* **Group Details (select multiple)**: Choose the members of your group from the list. Select all the members that are part of your group.
* **Notes (text)**: Add any additional notes or comments in this field. This can be helpful in case there are any specific requirements or instructions that the group needs to be aware of.
