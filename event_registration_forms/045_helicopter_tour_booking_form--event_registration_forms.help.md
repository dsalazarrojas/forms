# Helicopter Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a helicopter tour. Fill out the form to provide your details and schedule your tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Double-check that all fields are correct before submitting.
3. Submit the form to book your helicopter tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Provide your full name as it should be printed on your tour materials.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose the date you'd like to take the helicopter tour. If you don't select a date, you can select a different one when you receive the booking confirmation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time you'd like to take the helicopter tour. If you don't select a time, you can select a different time when you receive the booking confirmation.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your location or the location of your tour destination.
