# Catering Service Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Service Approval Form is used to request approval for catering services for events. This form is divided into three sections: Request Details, Event Information, and Approval Decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Request ID**, **Request Date**, **Requester Name**, **Requester Email**, **Event Name**, and **Event Date**.
2. Select the **Event Type** from the available options.
3. Enter the **Event Location**, **Guest Count**, and **Catering Vendor**.
4. Enter the **Vendor Contact Information** and **Service Type**.
5. Enter the **Total Catering Cost** and **Cost Per Person**.
6. Select the **Is budget available for this event?** and enter the **Budget Code** if applicable.
7. Select the **Approval Status**, enter any **Approval Comments**, and specify the **Approver Name**.
8. Finally, enter the **Approval Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Request ID**: A unique identifier for this request.
*  **Request Date**: The date of the event.
*  **Requester Name**: The person submitting the request.
*  **Requester Email**: The email address of the person submitting the request.
*  **Event Name**: The name of the event.
*  **Event Date**: The date of the event.
*  **Event Location**: The location of the event.
*  **Guest Count**: The estimated number of guests attending.
*  **Event Type**: The type of event (e.g. Corporate, Wedding, Fundraiser, etc.).
*  **Catering Vendor**: The name of the proposed catering company.
*  **Vendor Contact Information**: The contact information (phone and email) of the catering company.
*  **Service Type**: The type of catering service required (e.g. Buffet, Plated Service, etc.).
*  **Total Catering Cost**: The total cost for the catering service.
*  **Cost Per Person**: The cost per person.
*  **Is budget available for this event?**: Whether budget is available for this event.
*  **Budget Code**: The cost center or account code for the budget.
*  **Approval Status**: The approval status (e.g. Approved, Approved with Conditions, Denied, etc.).
*  **Approval Comments**: Reasons for the approval status.
*  **Approver Name**: The name of the person approving or reviewing this request.
*  **Approval Date**: The date of approval.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure proper approval.
* Review your email for any previous requests related to this event.
* If budget is not available, select "Needs Review" in the Is budget available for this event? field.
* If the approval status is not "Approved", enter the reasons for the decision in the Approval Comments field.
