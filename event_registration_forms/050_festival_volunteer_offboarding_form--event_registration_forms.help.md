# Festival Volunteer Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Festival Volunteer Offboarding Form is designed to collect information from volunteers who are offboarding from a festival event. This form helps to gather details about their offboarding status, confirm their last worked hours, and capture any feedback they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all the required questions.
2. Select the correct options for each field from the provided choices.
3. Ensure you have answered all the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Volunteer Offboarding** (`volunteer_offboarding_form_page_1`, type: note, required: false)
This field is for you to confirm that you are offboarding from the festival event. Simply type in a few words about your offboarding status.

### 2. **Have you worked hours on site?** (`confirm_hours_worked`, type: select_one, required: false)
Choose how many hours you have worked on site during the festival event.

### 3. **All materials have been returned** (`materials_returned`, type: select_multiple, required: false)
Indicate if all materials have been returned or if they were partially returned or not returned at all.

### 4. **Any feedback** (`feedback`, type: text, required: false)
Provide any feedback or comments about your offboarding experience.

### 5. **Hours worked** (`hours_worked`, type: number, required: false)
Input the total hours you have worked during the festival event.

### 6. **Last worked date** (`date_last_worked`, type: date, required: false)
Enter the date you last worked during the festival event.

### 7. **Last worked time** (`time_last_worked`, type: time, required: false)
Input the time you last worked during the festival event.

### 8. **Volunteer Offboarding** (`volunteer_offboarding_form_page_2`, type: note, required: false)
This field is a repeat of the previous question, for your reference.

### 9. **Confirm volunteer name** (`confirm_volunteer_name`, type: select_one, required: false)
Please confirm your name as the volunteer offboarding.

### 10. **Confirm volunteer contact** (`confirm_volunteer_contact`, type: select_one, required: false)
Confirm your contact information for future reference.

**Tips**

* Please answer all required fields to ensure accurate offboarding information.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Remember to return all materials and report your hours worked accurately to avoid any discrepancies.
