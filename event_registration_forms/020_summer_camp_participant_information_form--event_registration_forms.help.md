# summer_camp_participant_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants of the summer camp. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the participant and the camp staff. Completing this form helps us understand your personal details, emergency contacts, and any medical needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form in the provided format, answering each field as accurately and completely as possible.
* Double-check your input for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official ID or document.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official ID or document.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for future communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your full address for camp logistics.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Participant** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please describe them briefly.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required): If you have any medical notes or concerns, please describe them briefly.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, required): Share any additional information about yourself that we should know.

## Tips

* Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask a staff member for clarification.
* If you need to update any information, please contact us at your earliest convenience.
