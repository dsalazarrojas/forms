# Event Attendance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Event Attendance Registration" form is designed to collect event-related information from attendees, allowing organizers to efficiently gather contact details and event context. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your **Phone** in the "Phone" field.
4. If required, enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
6. For event-related information, fill in the **Event Name**, **Event Date**, and **Event Time** fields.
7. Finally, ensure all required fields (if any) are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name to help the event staff identify you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth for event purposes. 
* **Address**: Enter your address for event logistics or future communications.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you are attending.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event you are attending.
