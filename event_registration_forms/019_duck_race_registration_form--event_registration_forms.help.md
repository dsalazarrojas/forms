# Duck Race Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants in a duck race event for registration and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Name.
2. Enter your Contact Email and Contact Phone number.
3. Provide an Emergency Contact name and phone number (if necessary).
4. Choose your Participant Age and Relationship (Participant, Caregiver, or Emergency Contact).
5. Enter your Registration Date.
6. Select your Participant Gender (Male, Female, or Non-Binary).
7. Choose your Participant Ethnicity (Asian, Black, Hispanic, White, or Other).
8. Enter your Participant Fee.
9. Enter the Registration Fee.
10. If you would like to receive a gift, choose your Participant Gift options and quantity.
11. Choose your Participant T-Shirt Size (Small, Large, X-Large, or Other).
12. Select your Participant T-Shirt Style (Solid, Hoodie, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your t-shirt.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and communication.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Participant Relationship** (`participant_relationship`, select_one, required): Choose your relationship to the participant (Participant, Caregiver, or Emergency Contact).
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to participate in the event.
* **Participant Relationship Options** (`participant_relationship_options`, select_multiple, optional): Choose additional relationships (Participant, Caregiver, or Other) (optional).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event (if different from the registration date).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event (if different from the registration date and time).
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, required): Choose your gender (Male, Female, or Non-Binary).
* **Participant Ethnicity** (`participant_ethnicity`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your ethnicity (Asian, Black, Hispanic, White, or Other).
* **Participant Fee** (`participant_fee`, number, required): Enter your participant fee.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, number, required): Enter the registration fee.
* **Participant Gift** (`participant_gift`, select_multiple, optional): Choose if you would like to receive a gift (Yes or No).
* **Participant Gift Options** (`participant_gift_options`, text, optional): Enter any additional gift options.
* **Participant Gift Quantity** (`participant_gift_quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of gifts you would like to receive.
* **Participant Gift Price** (`participant_gift_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the gift you would like to receive.
* **Participant T-Shirt Size** (`participant_t_shirt_size`, select_one, required): Choose your t-shirt size (Small, Large, X-Large, or Other).
* **Participant T-Shirt Style** (`participant_t_shirt_style`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your t-shirt style (Solid, Hoodie, or Other).
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter your address for shipping and delivery purposes.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, optional): Enter your city for shipping and delivery purposes.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, select_one, required): Choose your state (California, New York, Florida, or Other).
* **Participant Zip** (`participant_zip`, number, required): Enter your zip code for shipping and delivery purposes.
* **Participant Gift** (`participant_gift`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to choose if you would like to receive a gift.
* **Participant Gift Options** (`participant_gift_options`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional gift options.
* **Participant Gift Quantity** (`participant_gift_quantity`, number, required): This field is used to enter the quantity of gifts you would like to receive.
* **Participant Gift Price** (`participant_gift_price`, number, required): This field is used to enter the price of the gift you would like to receive.
* **Participant T-Shirt Size** (`participant_t_shirt_size`, select_one, required): This field is used to choose your t-shirt size.
* **Participant T-Shirt Style** (`participant_t_shirt_style`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to choose your t-shirt style.
