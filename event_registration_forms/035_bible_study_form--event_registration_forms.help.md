# Bible Study Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Bible Study Form is designed to help us get to know you better and prepare for our Bible study group. Your input will help us tailor the study experience to suit your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your abilities.
2. Please fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) as they are essential for us to understand your situation.
3. For any questions that don't apply to you, feel free to leave them blank.
4. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they will help us create a positive and engaging study experience for everyone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached for study materials and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number for coordination purposes.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, optional): Choose your age group: Teen, Young adult, Adult, Senior, or Prefer not to say.
* **Bible Study Group Name** (`study_group_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the bible study group you're joining.
* **Book or Topic Being Studied** (`study_book`, text, required): Please specify what you are currently studying.
* **Preferred Study Session Schedule** (`session_schedule`, select_one, required): Choose when you would like to meet: Sunday morning, Sunday evening, Wednesday evening, Thursday evening, Weekend, Weekday morning, or Flexible.
* **Preferred Meeting Format** (`meeting_format`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to engage: In-person, Online/virtual, Hybrid, or No preference.
* **Desired Study Frequency** (`session_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to meet: Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, As scheduled, or Flexible.
* **Bible Study Experience Level** (`study_experience`, select_one, required): Select your level of experience: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Not sure.
* **Personal Bible Study Goals** (`study_goals`, select_multiple, required): Choose what you hope to achieve: Deepen understanding of Scripture, Spiritual growth, Community fellowship, Answer specific questions, Improve discipleship, Learn Bible content, Develop faith.
* **Favorite Books of the Bible** (`favorite_books`, text, optional): Please specify any favorite books.
* **Topics You Find Challenging** (`challenging_topics`, text, optional): Mention any areas that need clarification.
* **Attendance Commitment Level** (`attendance_commitment`, select_one, required): Choose how committed you are: Very committed - attend every session, Committed - attend most sessions, Flexible - attend when possible, New - exploring participation.
* **Preferred Participation Style** (`participation_style`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to engage: Active discussion, Listening and learning, Mix of both, Quiet observation, No preference.
* **Preferred Study Method** (`study_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you learn best: Verse by verse, Thematic study, Devotional approach, Interactive discussion, Lecture format.
* **Do You Need Transportation** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, optional): Check if you need help with transportation: Yes, please help arrange, No, I can arrange my own, Not applicable.
* **Do You Have Required Study Materials** (`study_materials`, select_one, required): Check if you have necessary materials: Yes, I have everything, Mostly prepared, Need some items, Need significant help.
* **How Did You Hear About This Study** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Choose how you heard about this study: Church announcement, Friend recommendation, Social media, Website, Email, Other.
* **Special Accommodations or Needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Mention any special needs or accommodations.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Special Accommodations or Needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Please specify any special needs or accommodations you may have.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions.
