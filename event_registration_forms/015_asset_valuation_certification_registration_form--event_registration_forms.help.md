# Asset Valuation Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Valuation Certification Registration Form is used to register your interest in obtaining an Asset Valuation Certification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
3. Submit the form to the authority for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, `text`, required): Your first name.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, `text`, required): Your last name.
### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Your professional email address.
### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
### Company or Organization
* **Company or Organization** (`company_affiliation`, `text`, required): Your current employer or affiliation.
### Professional Title
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`, `text`, required): Your current job title.
### Years of Valuation Experience
* **Years of Valuation Experience** (`experience_years`, `number`, required): The number of years you have in asset valuation experience.
### Current Certifications
* **Current Certifications** (`current_certifications`, `text`, optional): Other professional certifications you hold.
### Area of Specialization
* **Area of Specialization** (`specialization_area`, `select_one`, required): The type of assets you focus on. (e.g., Real Estate, Equipment and Machinery).
### Certification Level Sought
* **Certification Level Sought** (`certification_level`, `select_one`, required): The level of certification you are applying for. (e.g., Basics, Intermediate, Advanced).
### Education Background
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, `select_one`, required): The highest degree earned. (e.g., High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree).
### Field of Study
* **Field of Study** (`education_field`, `text`, required): Your academic discipline.
### Valuation Tools and Software
* **Valuation Tools and Software** (`valuation_tools_knowledge`, `text`, required): Software you are familiar with.
### Motivation for Certification
* **Motivation for Certification** (`motivation_for_certification`, `text`, required): Why you want this certification.
### Preferred Training Format
* **Preferred Training Format** (`preferred_training_format`, `select_one`, required): How you prefer to learn. (e.g., In-person Workshop, Online Course).
### Training Schedule Preference
* **Training Schedule Preference** (`training_schedule_preference`, `select_one`, required): When you would attend training. (e.g., Weekdays, Weekends).
### Preferred Training Location
* **Preferred Training Location** (`location_preference`, `text`, optional): Where you prefer training. (Note: Not required but will be used to accommodate your needs).
### Budget for Certification Training
* **Budget for Certification Training** (`budget_for_training`, `number`, optional): The amount you expect to spend on training.
### Employer Sponsoring Certification
* **Employer Sponsoring Certification** (`employer_sponsorship`, `select_one`, required): Whether your employer will sponsor your training.
### Professional References
* **Professional References** (`professional_references`, `text`, required): Names and contact information of 2 professional references.
### I Agree to Follow the Code of Ethics
* **I Agree to Follow the Code of Ethics** (`code_of_ethics_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirmation of ethics agreement.
### Commitment to Continuing Education
* **Commitment to Continuing Education** (`continuing_education_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Your commitment to ongoing learning.
### Application Date
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required): The date of submission.
### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Any other relevant information.
