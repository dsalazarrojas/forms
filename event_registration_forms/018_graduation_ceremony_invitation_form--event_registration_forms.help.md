# Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a graduation ceremony invitation. The purpose is to gather details about the ceremony, the graduate, and the invitation design to create a personalized and official invitation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Graduation Ceremony Details section with the date, time, and other relevant information about the ceremony.
2. Provide details about the graduate, including their name and any other relevant information.
3. Choose a preferred invitation design from the options provided.
4. Fill in any additional event details, such as specific instructions or requests.
5. Enter contact details, including phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Graduation Ceremony Details (Graduation Ceremony Details)
 Graduation Ceremony Details (required: false)

* The ceremony date and time should be filled in according to the schedule.
* If the ceremony is on a weekend or holiday, please indicate so.

### New Graduate Details (graduate_details)
New Graduate Details (required: false)

* Please fill in the new graduate's name and any other relevant information.

### Invitation Design (invitation_design)
Invitation Design (required: false)

* Choose the preferred invitation design from the options provided.
* The options are:
	+ Graduation Cap and Gown: A traditional graduation cap and gown design.
	+ Invitation Card: A simple card with the graduate's name and details.
	+ Certificate: A formal certificate with the graduate's name and details.

### Date and Time (date_and_time)
Date and Time (required: false)

* Fill in the ceremony date and time according to the schedule.

### Event Details (event_details)
Event Details (required: false)

* Fill in any additional event details, such as specific instructions or requests.

### Contact Details (contact_details)
Contact Details (required: false)

* Enter the contact details, including phone number and email.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in.
* Use clear and legible handwriting to ensure the invitation is easy to read.
