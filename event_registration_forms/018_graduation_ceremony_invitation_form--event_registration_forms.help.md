# Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a graduation ceremony invitation. The purpose is to gather details about the ceremony, the graduate, and the invitation design to create a personalized and official invitation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Graduation Ceremony Details section with the date, time, and other relevant information about the ceremony.
2. Provide details about the graduate, including their name and any other relevant information.
3. Choose a preferred invitation design from the options provided.
4. Fill in any additional event details, such as specific instructions or requests.
5. Enter contact details, including phone number and email.
