# library_workshop_series_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register your interest in the workshop series. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information so we can tailor the workshops to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the workshop sponsor in the `workshop_sponsor` field.
2.  Select the event date and time in the `event_date` and `event_time` fields.
3.  Enter your email address in the `email_address` field.
4.  If you have a phone number, please enter it in the `phone_number` field.
5.  Choose the topics you're interested in from the `workshop_topics` field.
6.  Specify the number of workshops you'd like to attend in the `number_of_workshops` field.
7.  Add any additional comments or information in the `additional_comments` field.
8.  Enter your name in the `name` field.
9.  Specify your organization in the `organization` field.
10. Enter your job title in the `job_title` field.
11. Enter your department in the `department` field.
12. Enter your job function in the `job_function` field.
13. Enter your work email in the `work_email` field, if applicable.
14. Enter your work phone number in the `work_phone` field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **workshop_sponsor**: Enter the person or organization that is sponsoring the workshop.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **event_date**: Select the date you'd like to attend the workshop event.
  * Type: date
  * Required: no
- **event_time**: Select the time you'd like to attend the workshop event.
  * Type: time
  * Required: no
- **email_address**: Enter your work or personal email address.
  * Type: email
  * Required: no
- **phone_number**: Enter your work or personal phone number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **workshop_topics**: Choose the topics you're interested in attending. Please select all that apply.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: yes
  * Options: topic1, topic2, topic3
- **number_of_workshops**: Specify the number of workshops you'd like to attend.
  * Type: number
  * Required: yes
- **additional_comments**: Add any additional comments or information.
  * Type: note
  * Required: no
- **name**: Enter your name.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **organization**: Enter your organization's name.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **job_title**: Enter your job title.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **department**: Enter your department.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **job_function**: Enter your job function.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
- **work_email**: Enter your work email address.
  * Type: email
  * Required: no
- **work_phone**: Enter your work phone number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
