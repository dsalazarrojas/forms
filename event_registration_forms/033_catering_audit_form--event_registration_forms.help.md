# Catering Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Audit Form is designed to assess the quality and performance of catering services. It helps auditors evaluate the food and beverage quality, service, and safety standards of a particular event or occasion. This form ensures that caterers provide satisfactory services to their clients and maintains high standards of cleanliness and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information such as the Auditor/Inspector Name, Date of Audit, and Event Name or Reference Number.
2. Choose the correct rating for each category according to your observation.
3. Describe your findings and any corrective actions required in the "Auditor Summary and Observations" and "Required Corrective Actions" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditor/Inspector Name**: This field requires the name of the person conducting the audit.
    * Type: text
    * Required: yes
* **Date of Audit**: This field is used to record the date the audit was conducted.
    * Type: date
    * Required: yes
* **Event Name or Reference Number**: Enter the name of the event or the reference number allocated to the event.
    * Type: text
    * Required: yes
* **Venue Name**: Enter the name of the venue where the audit was conducted.
    * Type: text
    * Required: yes
* **Food Temperature Compliance**: Choose the correct rating based on the condition of hot and cold foods.
    * Type: select_one
    * Options: 
        + Excellent
        + Acceptable
        + Needs Improvement
        + Unacceptable/Critical
* **Visual Presentation and Plating**: Choose the correct rating based on the presentation and plating of the food.
    * Type: select_one
    * Options:
        + Outstanding
        + Good
        + Fair
        + Poor
* **Taste and Flavor Profile**: Choose the correct rating based on the taste and flavor of the food.
    * Type: select_one
    * Options:
        + Excellent
        + Satisfactory
        + Unsatisfactory
* **Staff Uniform and Grooming**: Choose the correct rating based on the uniforms and grooming of the staff.
    * Type: select_one
    * Options:
        + Compliant
        + Minor Deviations
        + Non-Compliant
* **Punctuality of Setup and Service**: Choose the correct rating based on the punctuality of the setup and service.
    * Type: select_one
    * Options:
        + On Time
        + Slightly Delayed
        + Major Delay
* **Cleanliness of Catering Station/Kitchen Area**: Choose the correct rating based on the cleanliness of the area.
    * Type: select_one
    * Options:
        + Immaculate
        + Generally Clean
        + Untidy
        + Hazardous
* **Allergen Awareness and Labeling**: Choose the correct rating based on the allergen awareness and labeling of the food.
    * Type: select_one
    * Options:
        + Fully Labeled
        + Partially Labeled
        + No Labels
* **Auditor Summary and Observations**: Describe any observations or findings from the audit.
    * Type: text
    * Required: yes
* **Required Corrective Actions**: Describe any necessary corrective actions or improvements required.
    * Type: text
    * Required: no
* **Manager Notified**: Choose the correct answer based on whether the manager was notified during the audit.
    * Type: select_one
    * Options:
        + Yes
        + No
        + Follow-up scheduled
* **Allergen Handling Audit**: Not applicable in this version.

## Tips

* Use this form to ensure high standards of quality and service in catering operations.
* Be specific and objective when rating each category.
* Take note of any corrective actions or improvements needed.

This guide is intended for end-users to help them understand the purpose and how to complete the Catering Audit Form. The goal is to provide a clear and concise explanation of each field and its requirements.
