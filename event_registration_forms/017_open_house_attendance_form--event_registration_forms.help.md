# Open House Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from visitors attending an open house event. By filling out this form, visitors can provide essential details about themselves and their visit, which will help us better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Open House Attendance Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Select your visitor type from the options provided (e.g., Home Visitor, Potential Buyer, Referral).
3. Add any additional notes about your visit.
4. If applicable, select the assigned user or tool for your visit.
5. Choose the event date and time that you attended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Open House Attendance Form** (Page 1, 1): This field is for the form title. You can leave this field blank.
* **Visitor Information** (Page 1, 2): Provide your name and email address in this field.
* **Name** (Page 1, 3): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (Page 1, 4): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Phone** (Page 1, 5): If you have a phone number, you can enter it in this field.
* **Visitor Type** (Page 1, 6): Select your type of visit from the options provided (e.g., Home Visitor, Potential Buyer, Referral).
* **Notes** (Page 1, 7): Add any additional comments or notes about your visit.
* **Assigned User** (Page 2, 8, 9): If applicable, select the user or tool assigned to your visit.
* **Event Date** (Page 2, 10): Enter the date of the event that you attended.
* **Event Time** (Page 2, 11): Enter the time of the event that you attended.
* **Form Submitter** (Page 2, 12): This field is for the person submitting the form (likely you).
* **Form Submitted Date** (Page 2, 13): This field is for the date the form was submitted.
* **Assigned Tool** (Page 2, 14): If applicable, select the tool assigned to your visit.
* **Open House Event ID** (Page 2, 15): This field is likely for internal purposes and can be left blank.
* **Form ID** (Page 2, 16): This field is also likely for internal purposes and can be left blank.
* **Category** (Page 2, 17): This field might be used for categorizing the event or visit, but it's not required.
* **Category 1, 2, 3, 4** (Page 2, 18, 19, 20): These fields might be used for additional categorization, but they are not required.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
* We appreciate your feedback and comments about your visit to improve our services.
