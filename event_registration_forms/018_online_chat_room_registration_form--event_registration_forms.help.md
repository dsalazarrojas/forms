# Online Chat Room Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to register for online chat rooms. It collects information about the participant and their registration details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name and email address.
2. Select one of the available chat rooms.
3. Enter the date and time of the event.
4. Provide a brief description of your registration, if desired.
5. You can optionally provide your phone number and participant notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
* **Select One** (`Chat Rooms`, select one, optional): Choose one of the available chat rooms you'd like to join.
* **Date Of Event** (`Date Of Event`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you'd like to attend. 
* **Time Of Event** (`Time Of Event`, time, required): Enter the time of the event you'd like to attend.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your reason for attending the event.
* **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, enter your phone number here.
* **Participant Notes** (`Participant Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your registration.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields (Participant Name, Participant Email, Date Of Event, and Time Of Event) for a successful submission.
- Make sure to choose only one chat room for your registration. 
- You can save your submission and complete it later by clicking the "Save and continue later" button.
