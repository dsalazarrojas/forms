# virtual_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from users who want to participate in a virtual workshop.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Choose a date and time for the workshop (optional).
6. Enter any additional notes or comments.
7. Select one option from the "Select One" field.
8. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field (optional).
9. Enter a decimal or integer value in the "Decimal" or "Integer" fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the workshop or event you are registering for.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`5`, `date`, optional): Choose the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Time** (`6`, `time`, optional): Choose the time you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Note** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Select One** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Decimal** (`10`, `number`, optional): Enter a decimal value.
* **Integer** (`11`, `number`, optional): Enter an integer value.
