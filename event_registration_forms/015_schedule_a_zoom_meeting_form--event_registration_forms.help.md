# Schedule A Zoom Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you schedule a meeting on Zoom by collecting meeting details, attendee information, and scheduling preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the meeting information on **page 1**.
2. On **page 2**, select the attendees who will be participating in the meeting.
3. On **page 3**, choose the date for the meeting.
4. On **page 4**, select the time zone for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Meeting Information (page 1)
* **Meeting Information** (`title`,  no): This is the main title of the meeting. Enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **Meeting Information** (`type`, no): This field is not actually used for meeting details.

### Attendees (page 2)
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, required): Select the attendees who will be participating in the meeting. You can choose from the following options: *Yes* and *No*.

### Scheduling (page 3)
* **Scheduling** (`date`, required): Choose the date for the meeting. Please format the date in the standard MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 02/22/2024).

### Time Zone (page 4)
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, required): Select the time zone for the meeting. Choose from the following options: 
  * Pacific Time (US & Canada)
  * Mountain Time (US & Canada)
  * Central Time (US & Canada)
  * Eastern Time (US & Canada)
  * Alaska Time
  * Hawaii-Aleutians West
  * West Pacific Time

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct time zone to avoid scheduling conflicts.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
