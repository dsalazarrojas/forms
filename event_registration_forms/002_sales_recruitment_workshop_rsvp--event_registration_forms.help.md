# sales_recruitment_workshop_rsvp - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect RSVPs for a sales recruitment workshop. It gathers information from individuals who are interested in attending the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Info section with any relevant details about the event.
2. Provide your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter company name and title.
4. Fill out the company details and event details sections as required.
5. Add any additional info or notes you'd like to share.
6. Select a date and time for the workshop (not required).
7. Choose to select one or select multiple options as required.
8. Enter decimal, integer, or text information as needed.
9. Fill out the Time 2 section as needed.
10. Add any notes as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Info** (text): Enter any relevant details about the event, such as its purpose, date, and location.
* **contact_details** (text): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **submitter_info** (text): Enter your company name and title.
* **company_info** (text): Enter your company name (if different from submitter's).
* **title** (text): Enter your title or job function.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **company_name** (text): Enter your company name.
* **title_name** (text): Enter your title or job function.
* **submitter_email** (email): Enter your submitter's email address.
* **submitter_phone** (text): Enter your submitter's phone number.
* **submitter_info_2** (text): Enter any additional information about your submitter.
* **company_details** (text): Enter any additional company information.
* **event_details** (text): Enter any additional event details.
* **additional_info** (text): Enter any additional information.
* **notes** (note): Enter any notes or comments.
* **date** (date): Enter the date for the workshop (not required).
* **time** (time): Enter the time for the workshop (not required).
* **select_one** (select_one): Select one of the options (Yes/No/Other).
* **select_multiple** (select_multiple): Select multiple options (Yes/No/Other).
* **decimal** (number): Enter a decimal value.
* **integer** (number): Enter an integer value.
* **text** (text): Enter any text information.
* **time_2** (time): Enter the time for the workshop (not required).
* **note** (note): Enter any notes or comments.
