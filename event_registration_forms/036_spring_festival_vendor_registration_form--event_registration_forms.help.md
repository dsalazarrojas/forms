# spring_festival_vendor_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spring festival vendor registration form is a tool used to collect information from vendors who want to participate in the spring festival. This form helps festival organizers to gather essential details about each vendor, such as contact information, payment terms, and terms of service acceptance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Vendor Name** in the text field provided.
2. Choose whether or not you are a **Vendor Type** by selecting the corresponding option.
3. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** numbers, as these are your contact details.
4. Sign with your electronic **E Signature** to confirm your identity.
5. Add any **Notes** you have about your business or products.
6. Enter the details of your **Vendor Application**, describing your products or services and why you're suitable for the festival.
7. Accept or decline the **Vendor Terms** by selecting the corresponding option.
8. Enter the details of your **Vendor Address** and **Vendor Contact** information.
9. Add any **Vendor Additional Info** that you think is relevant to your business.
10. Provide the date of your **Vendor Signature** in the **Vendor Signature Date** field.
11. Add your **Signature** in the field provided, as a final confirmation of your application.
12. Select the method of **Payment Method**, whether you've accepted or declined it.
13. Specify the **Payment Term** and the **Vendor Payment Amount** for your participation.
14. Enter any **Additional Info** about your business or products.
15. Accept or decline the **Terms And Condition** by selecting the corresponding option.
16. Add any further **Additional Info** about your business or products.
17. Finally, confirm that you have **Accepted** the terms and conditions by selecting the corresponding option.

- **Vendor Address** (`Vendor Address`, `text`, required): Enter your business address.
- **Vendor Contact** (`Vendor Contact`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information.
- **Vendor Additional Info** (`Vendor Additional Info`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about your business or products.

- **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have accepted or declined payment terms.
- **Payment Term** (`Payment Term`, `text`, required): Specify the payment terms and amount for your participation.
