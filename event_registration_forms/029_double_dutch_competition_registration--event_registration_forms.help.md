# Double Dutch Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering a Double Dutch competition. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the name of the competition in the "Event Name" field.
2. Indicate if the team has members in the "Team Members" field.
3. If the team has emergency contacts, provide the necessary information in the "Emergency Contacts", "Emergency Contact Name", "Emergency Contact Relationship", "Emergency Contact Phone", and "Emergency Contact Email" fields.
4. Describe the competition details in the "Event Details" field.
5. Select the competition date and start time in the "Event Date" and "Event Start Time" fields, respectively.
6. Provide consent to the terms of the competition by selecting "Yes" in the "Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name
* **Event Name** (text): Enter the name of the competition.

### Team Members
* **Team Members** (select multiple): Check this box if the team has members.
  If the team has no members, leave this field blank.
  If the team has members, leave this field checked and ensure to provide the necessary information for emergency contacts.

### Emergency Contacts
* **Emergency Contacts** (text): List the names of emergency contacts in this field.
  If a team has no emergency contacts, leave this field blank.
  If a team has emergency contacts, list them in this field and ensure to provide the necessary information for each contact, including their name, relationship, phone number, and email.

### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
  If there is no emergency contact, leave this field blank.
  If there is an emergency contact, enter their name in this field.

### Emergency Contact Relationship
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the team member(s).
  If there is no emergency contact, leave this field blank.
  If there is an emergency contact, describe their relationship in this field.

### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
  If there is no emergency contact, leave this field blank.
  If there is an emergency contact, enter their phone number in this field.

### Emergency Contact Email
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email): Enter the email of the emergency contact.
  If there is no emergency contact, leave this field blank.
  If there is an emergency contact, enter their email in this field.

### Event Details
* **Event Details** (text): Describe the competition details, such as rules, procedures, and any other relevant information.
  Leave this field blank if the event has no details.

### Event Date
* **Event Date** (date): Select the date of the competition.
  Leave this field blank if the event date is not known.

### Event Start Time
* **Event Start Time** (time): Select the start time of the competition.
  Leave this field blank if the event start time is not known.

### Consent
* **Consent** (select one): Select "Yes" to agree to the terms of the competition.
  Leave this field blank to indicate that you do not agree to the terms.
  Select "No" to indicate that you are unsure or do not agree to the terms.

### Submission Details
* **Submission Details** (text): Leave this field blank, as it is unnecessary.

Note: Please ensure that all fields with labels like "Emergency Contacts", "Emergency Contact Name", "Emergency Contact Relationship", "Emergency Contact Phone", "Emergency Contact Email", "Event Details", and "Submission Details" are filled out correctly, as they are essential for the competition registration process.
