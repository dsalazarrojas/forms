# Group Booking Deposit Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Group Booking Deposit Payment Form is used to collect payment information for group bookings, including deposit amounts, payment methods, and booking status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Enter the Company name and Name of the group booking.
3. Input the Deposit Amount for the booking.
4. Select the Payment Method used for the transaction (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Discover).
5. Choose the Payment Status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
6. Choose the Booking Status (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the booking.
8. Input the Payment Date and Payment Time for the transaction.
9. Enter the Payment Amount for the transaction.
10. Select any additional Payment Instruments used (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Discover).
11. Enter the Total Payments made for the transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company (\*)**: Enter the name of the company associated with the group booking.
* **Name (\*)**: Enter the name of the group booking.
* **Deposit Amount**: Enter the deposit amount for the group booking.
* **Date of Booking**: Enter the date of the group booking.
* **Total Deposit Paid**: Enter the total deposit paid for the group booking.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method used to make the payment (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Discover).
* **Payment Status**: Choose the status of the payment (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Booking Status**: Choose the status of the booking (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the booking.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment for the transaction.
* **Payment Time**: Enter the time of the payment for the transaction.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for the transaction.
* **Additional Payment Instrument**: Select any additional payment instruments used (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Discover).
* **Total Payments**: Enter the total payments made for the transaction.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields (\*).
* Double-check the input values for accuracy.
* Use the correct payment methods and instruments as required.
* Update the booking and payment status accordingly.
