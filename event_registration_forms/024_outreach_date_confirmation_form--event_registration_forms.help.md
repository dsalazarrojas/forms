# Outreach Date Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm event dates and attendance details for outreach activities. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization's name in the field provided.
2. Provide your email address to receive updates and confirmation emails.
3. Enter your name and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select a preferred date for the event from the calendar picker.
5. Confirm the event date for the outreach activity.
6. Indicate your attendance status for the event by selecting "True", "False", or "Unable to attend".
7. Add any additional notes about your event participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's name to confirm your affiliation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for updates and confirmation emails.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required: false): Select a date from the calendar picker for your preferred event date.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Confirm the event date for the outreach activity.
* **Attendance Confirmation** (`attendance_confirmation`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "True", "False", or "Unable to attend" to confirm your event attendance.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your event participation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have entered the correct organization and event date to avoid duplicate submissions.
* If you have already submitted this form for the same organization and event date, you will be prompted to review and confirm your previous submission.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out this form, please contact your local support team for assistance.
