# 2016 Ymca Risk Management Conference - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration to the 2016 YMCA Risk Management Conference. Please fill out this form to provide your personal information, conference details, and logistics information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields.
2. Review each section carefully, ensuring you provide accurate information.
3. If you have any special needs, please specify them in the "Special needs" field.
4. Make sure to select all the days you plan to attend in the "Attendance days" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as you would like to be known.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): The email address you would like to use for correspondence.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional): Your phone number, including country code.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Your date of birth (if you prefer not to provide this, you can leave this field blank and provide your age instead).
* **Age** (`age`, `integer`, optional): If you prefer not to provide your date of birth, you can enter your age instead.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): The organization you represent or work for.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Your job title or position.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): The name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `phone`, optional): The phone number of the emergency contact, including country code.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): The relationship of the emergency contact.
* **Conference details header** (`section_header_conference_details`, `note`, required): This section is informational, indicating the start of the conference details.
* **Experience level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Your level of experience in risk management (Select from: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional).
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, required): How you heard about the conference (Select from: Social media, Friend/Family, Website, Flyer/Poster, Other).
* **Attendance days** (`attendance_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the days you plan to attend the conference.
* **T-shirt size** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, required): Your T-shirt size (Select from: XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL).
* **Logistics header** (`section_header_logistics`, `note`, required): This section is informational, indicating the start of the logistics information.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): How you would like to be contacted (Select from: Email, Phone, Text, None).
* **Consent to recording** (`consent_to_recording`, `select_one`, required): Do you consent to being recorded during the conference (Select from: True, False).
* **Consent to email updates** (`consent_to_email_updates`, `select_one`, required): Do you consent to receiving email updates about future events (Select from: True, False).
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): The payment method you will use to register (Select from: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal, Cash).
* **Registration fee paid** (`registration_fee_paid`, `select_one`, required): Have you paid the registration fee (Select from: True, False).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
* **Special needs** (`special_needs`, `text`, optional): Any special needs or accommodations you require.
* **Additional information header** (`section_header_additional_information`, `note`, required): This section is informational, indicating the start of the additional information.

Please note that some fields are marked as required, indicating they must be completed for successful registration. Ensure to provide accurate and complete information in each field. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the conference organizers.
