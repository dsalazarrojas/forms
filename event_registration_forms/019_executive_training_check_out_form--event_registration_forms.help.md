# executive_training_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The executive training check out form is used for participants to register and complete their payment information for executive training programs. This form helps to collect necessary details such as participant's name, company name, role, contact information, payment method, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Name in the text field.
2. Enter your Company Name in the text field.
3. Choose your Role from the dropdown options (Executive, Manager, Participant).
4. Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., username@example.com).
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Select your preferred Payment Method from the dropdown options (Check, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
7. Enter your Payment Amount.
8. Select a Date and Time for the training program.
9. Provide any additional Comments about your participation.
10. Enter a Form ID (if provided).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Company Name (text)**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Role (select_one)**: Choose your current role within the company, such as Executive, Manager, or Participant.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address in the format username@example.com.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number without any special characters or symbols.
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Select the payment method you would like to use, such as Check, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Amount (number)**: Enter the payment amount for the training program.
* **Date (date)**: Select the date you plan to attend the training program.
* **Time (time)**: Select the time you plan to attend the training program.
* **Comments (note)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your participation.
* **Form ID (text)**: If provided, enter the Form ID to identify your registration.
