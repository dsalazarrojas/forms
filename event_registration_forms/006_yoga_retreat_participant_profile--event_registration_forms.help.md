# Yoga Retreat Participant Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yoga Retreat Participant Profile form is designed to collect essential information about yoga retreat participants. This information will be used to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants and to ensure that any medical emergencies are handled efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Provide any relevant Medical Info in the "Medical Info" field.
5. If you have any Dietary Restrictions, select the corresponding option from the dropdown menu in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
6. Choose your Preferred Time for yoga sessions in the "Preferred Time" field.
7. If you have a specific Yoga Style preference, enter it in the "Yoga Style" field.
8. Indicate your Level of Experience in the "Level of Experience" field by selecting "Beginner", "Intermediate", or "Advanced".
9. If you have Health Insurance, enter the name of your insurance provider in the "Health Insurance" field.
10. Provide the name of your Emergency Contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
11. Enter any Allergies you may have in the "Allergies" field.
12. If you have a Medical Condition, enter it in the "Medical Condition" field.
13. Enter your Doctor's Name in the "Doctor's Name" field.
14. Enter your Doctor's Phone number in the "Doctor's Phone" field.
15. Enter your Doctor's Address in the "Doctor's Address" field.
16. Enter any Health Info you'd like to share in the "Health Info" field.
17. If you take Medication, enter it in the "Medication" field.
18. If you have an Emergency Plan, enter it in the "Emergency Plan" field.
19. Enter the name of the Medical Facility you'd like to use in case of an emergency in the "Medical Facility" field.
20. Enter the phone number of the Medical Facility in the "Medical Facility Phone" field.
21. Provide the name of your Emergency Contact's Name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
22. Provide the phone number of your Emergency Contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
23. Indicate whether you have a Medical Emergency by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Medical Emergency" field.
24. If you have any Medical Emergency Info, enter it in the "Medical Emergency Info" field.
25. Finally, provide the name and contact information of your Medical Emergency Contact in the "Medical Emergency Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Info**: If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide a brief description.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you may have (e.g. Gluten free, Lactose intolerant, etc.).
* **Preferred Time**: Choose your preferred time for yoga sessions (e.g. morning, afternoon, etc.).
* **Yoga Style**: Enter your preferred yoga style (e.g. Hatha, Vinyasa, etc.).
* **Level of Experience**: Select your level of experience: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Health Insurance**: If you have health insurance, enter the name of your provider.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Allergies**: Enter any allergies you may have.
* **Medical Condition**: If you have a medical condition, enter it here.
* **Doctor's Name**: Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor's Phone**: Enter the phone number of your doctor.
* **Doctor's Address**: Enter the address of your doctor.
* **Health Info**: Enter any health information you'd like to share.
* **Medication**: If you take any medication, enter it in this field.
* **Emergency Plan**: If you have an emergency plan, enter it here.
* **Medical Facility**: Enter the name of the medical facility you'd like to use in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the medical facility.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Emergency**: Indicate whether you have a medical emergency by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Medical Emergency Info**: If you have any medical emergency information, enter it here.
* **Medical Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of your medical emergency contact.
