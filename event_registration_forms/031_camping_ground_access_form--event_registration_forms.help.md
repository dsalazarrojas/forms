# Camping Ground Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camping Ground Access Form is used to register and record information about visitors to the camping ground. Completing this form ensures that the visitor's details are accurately recorded and helps us manage access to the camping ground effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the fields provided.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you in case of any issues or updates.
3. Provide your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
4. Select a valid photo ID from the options provided (Driver License, State ID, Passport, Military ID, or Other).
5. Enter your vehicle's make, model, year, and license plate number.
6. Choose the type of vehicle you are driving (Car, Truck, Van, RV, Motorcycle, or Other).
7. Specify the date and estimated time of arrival.
8. Choose the length of your stay (Less than 4 hours, 4-8 hours, 1-2 days, 3-7 days, 1-4 weeks, or Monthly pass).
9. Indicate the number of occupants in your vehicle.
10. If applicable, enter the campsite number or site name and your reservation confirmation number.
11. Agree to abide by the camping ground's rules and regulations.
12. Confirm that you understand and accept all safety requirements.
13. Finally, acknowledge that you have read and understand the liability waiver and terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is displayed on your photo ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is displayed on your photo ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your active email address where you can be reached in case of any issues or updates.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Valid Photo ID**: Choose a valid photo ID (Driver License, State ID, Passport, Military ID, or Other) that you have with you.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of your vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of your vehicle (e.g., Camry, F-150, etc.).
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **License Plate Number**: Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type**: Choose the type of vehicle you are driving (Car, Truck, Van, RV, Motorcycle, or Other).
* **Date of Visit**: Choose the date of your visit to the camping ground.
* **Estimated Time of Arrival**: Choose the estimated time of your arrival at the camping ground.
* **Length of Stay**: Choose the length of your stay at the camping ground (Less than 4 hours, 4-8 hours, 1-2 days, 3-7 days, 1-4 weeks, or Monthly pass).
* **Number of Occupants in Vehicle**: Enter the number of people in your vehicle.
* **Campsite Number or Site Name**: If you have a campsite assignment, enter the campsite number or site name.
* **Reservation Confirmation Number**: If you have a reservation, enter your reservation confirmation number.
* **I Agree to Abide by Campground Rules and Regulations**: Confirm that you agree to abide by the camping ground's rules and regulations.
* **I Understand and Accept all Safety Requirements**: Confirm that you understand and accept all safety requirements.
* **I Acknowledge the Liability Waiver and Terms**: Confirm that you have read and understand the liability waiver and terms.
