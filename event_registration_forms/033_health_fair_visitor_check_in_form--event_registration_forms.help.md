# health_fair_visitor_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for visitors to the health fair to provide their contact information and indicate their interest in various services offered during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Visitor Details**: Enter your name and other contact information in this field.
2. Select the services you are interested in from the list provided.
3. Enter your email address, which will be used as a contact method.
4. Enter your phone number, which will be useful for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Details (1)**: This field is where you should enter your name and other relevant contact information.
* **Services of Interest (2)**: Select all the services you are interested in from the list provided. This will help us tailor your experience at the health fair.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address, which will be used as a contact method for communication purposes.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number, which will be useful for any follow-up communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any specific questions or requests, please do not hesitate to contact us after submitting this form.
