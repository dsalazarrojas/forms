# Catering Services Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Services Billing Form is used to gather information from clients for billing purposes. This form helps in calculating the total amount due and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and fill it out in the same order as the sections appear.
2. Start by filling out the Client Information section.
3. Move on to the Event Details section and fill out the required fields.
4. Proceed to the Service Details section.
5. Fill out the Charges and Fees section.
6. Finally, enter the Total & Payment section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Name** (2, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.

### Event Details
* **Event Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Name** (8, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Location** (9, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Number of Guests** (10, number, required): Enter the number of guests attending the event.

### Service Details
* **Service Type** (12, select one, required): Select the type of service provided, e.g., Buffet Service, Plated Service.
* **Cost Per Person** (13, number, required): Enter the cost per person.
* **Subtotal (Guests x Rate)** (14, number, required): Calculate the subtotal based on the number of guests and cost per person.
* **Additional Services Charges** (15, number, optional): Enter any additional services charges (setup, cleanup, etc.).
* **Service Fee Percentage** (17, number, optional): Enter the service fee percentage (if applicable).
* **Service Fee Amount** (18, number, optional): Calculate the service fee amount based on the subtotal and service fee percentage.
* **Tax Amount** (19, number, optional): Enter the tax amount (if applicable).

### Charges and Fees
* **Service Fee Amount** (18, number, optional): This field is automatically populated based on the subtotal and service fee percentage.
* **Tax Amount** (19, number, optional): This field is automatically populated based on the subtotal and tax rate (if applicable).

### Total & Payment
* **Total Amount Due** (21, number, required): This is the total amount due, calculated automatically based on the subtotal, service fee amount, and tax amount.
* **Payment Method** (22, select one, required): Select the payment method, e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Terms** (23, select one, required): Select the payment terms, e.g., Full Payment Due, 50% Deposit, 50% Final.
* **Invoice Notes or Special Instructions** (24, text, optional): Enter any invoice notes or special instructions.
* **Billing Confirmation** (25, select one, required): Confirm that you authorize billing for the amount specified.
* **I authorize billing for the amount specified** (25, select one, required): Confirm that you authorize billing for the amount specified.
