# mental_health_workshop_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for mental health workshops. It captures essential details about the participant and their preferences for the event, such as title, contact information, and location. The form helps the event organizers prepare and tailor the workshop to the participants' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your title of the event.
2. Enter your name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter the workshop title.
5. Choose a start date for the workshop.
6. Choose an end date for the workshop.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Select a preferred location from the options provided.
9. Leave any additional comments if needed.
10. Add any personal notes for the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the mental health workshop you are registering for.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication with the workshop organizers.
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the mental health workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Start Date** (`workshop_start_date`, date, optional): Choose the start date of the workshop.
* **Workshop End Date** (`workshop_end_date`, date, optional): Choose the end date of the workshop.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, select_one, optional): Select one of the preferred locations for the workshop.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments about the workshop.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Enter any personal notes for the participant.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the event title, workshop title, and participant name are entered correctly for identification and organization purposes.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special needs, please include them in the additional comments section.
* If you have questions or concerns about the workshop, do not hesitate to contact the event organizers through the provided email address.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required and optional fields are filled in.
