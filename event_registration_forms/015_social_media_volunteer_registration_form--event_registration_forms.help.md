# Social Media Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering on our social media platforms. Your input will help us understand your interests, skills, and availability to effectively manage our events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your event registration form details, including any relevant event-specific information.
2. Share your volunteer details, such as your name, role, and any relevant qualifications.
3. Specify your volunteer availability, including the dates and times you are available to volunteer.
4. Share your volunteer experience, including any relevant skills or previous volunteer work.
5. Provide additional information about yourself, such as any relevant interests or hobbies.
6. Agree to our volunteer terms and conditions.
7. Add any additional information you think is relevant for us to know about you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Registration Form Details**
* (`event_registration_form_details`, text, optional)
This field is where you can provide any relevant details about the event you are registering for. Please include the event name, date, and any other relevant information.
### **Volunteer Details**
* (`volunteer_details`, text, optional)
Here, you can share your name, role, and any relevant qualifications you have as a volunteer.
### **Volunteer Availability**
* (`volunteer_availability`, text, optional)
Specify the dates and times you are available to volunteer. Be as specific as possible to help us schedule you effectively.
### **Volunteer Experience**
* (`volunteer_experience`, text, optional)
Share your volunteer experience, including any relevant skills or previous volunteer work. This helps us understand your qualifications and fit for our events.
### **Volunteer Information**
* (`volunteer_information`, text, optional)
Provide any additional information about yourself, such as your interests or hobbies that may be relevant to our events.
### **Volunteer Agreement**
* (`volunteer_agreement`, text, optional)
Please review and agree to our volunteer terms and conditions before submitting your registration.
### **Additional Info**
* (`volunteer_additional_info`, text, optional)
This is the final field to add any additional information you think is necessary for us to know about you.
