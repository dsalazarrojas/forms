# Copywriting_Volunteer_Registration_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers interested in participating in a copywriting project. It asks for personal details such as name and email, email confirmation, availability, writing samples, and volunteer details. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information to match volunteers with suitable projects and tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details.
2. Ensure you provide your real email address, as we will use it to communicate with you.
3. Select your availability for the project (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
4. Provide some examples of writing samples if you have any (optional but recommended).
5. Add any additional details about your volunteer experience and skills (optional but recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmed`, text, optional): Confirm that the email address you provided is correct.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the time of day you are available for the project (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Writing Samples** (`writing_samples`, text, optional): Share some examples of your writing skills if you have any.
* **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_details`, text, optional): Provide additional details about your volunteer experience and skills.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate information as you will be contacted based on your details.
* Review the form before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
