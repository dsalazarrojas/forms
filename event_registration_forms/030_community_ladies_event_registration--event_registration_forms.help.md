# Community Ladies Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees to a community event. It's designed to collect relevant information about each attendee, including their name, contact details, and event preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Attendee name** in the "Attendee name" field. This will be used for badge purposes.
2. Enter your **Email address** in the "Email address" field. This will be used for event confirmation and communication.
3. If you have a **Phone number**, enter it in the "Phone number" field. This is optional and will be used for urgent updates only.
4. Select your **Ticket type** from the options provided: General admission, VIP, or Student.
5. If you have any **Dietary preferences**, enter them in the "Dietary preferences" field.
6. If you have any **Accessibility needs**, describe them in the "Accessibility needs" field.
7. If you'd like to provide an **Emergency contact name and phone**, enter it in the "Emergency contact" field.
8. If you're bringing a **Guest**, select "True" to indicate so.
9. If you have any **Special requests**, enter them in the "Special requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee name (Required)**: The name of the attendee as it will appear on the badge.
* **Email address (Required)**: The email address used for event confirmation and communication.
* **Phone number (Optional)**: A contact number for urgent updates.
* **Ticket type**: Choose the type of ticket you need. General admission, VIP, or Student.
* **Dietary preferences**: Any dietary restrictions or preferences, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
* **Accessibility needs**: Any accommodations or assistance required for the attendee.
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: A contact person and number to reach out in case of emergencies.
* **Bringing a guest**: Select "True" if you're bringing a guest with you.
* **Special requests**: Any additional needs or preferences not covered by the other fields.
