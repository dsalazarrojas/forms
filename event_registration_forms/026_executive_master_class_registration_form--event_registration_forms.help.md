# executive_master_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The executive master class registration form is used to register participants for a professional development program. This form captures essential contact information and other relevant details to facilitate event organization and participant experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Add your Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the event materials.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information about the event.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Add your phone number to enable easy communication, but feel free to leave this field blank if you don't want to share your phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is accurate to receive important updates about the event.
* If you have any accessibility requirements, please let us know in advance so we can make necessary arrangements.
