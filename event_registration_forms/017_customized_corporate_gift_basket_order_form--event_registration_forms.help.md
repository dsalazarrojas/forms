# Customized Corporate Gift Basket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for corporate event planners and clients to customize the contents of a gift basket for corporate events or occasions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) or a red dot.
2. Select the desired gift options for the corporate gift basket.
3. Enter the event details, including the event date and name.
4. Specify the company and contact information for the gift recipient.
5. Choose a payment method and confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide about the order.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Specify the date of the event or occasion.
* **Gift Option** (`gift_option`, select_multiple, optional): Select the gift options you would like to include in the gift basket. Options include 1, 2, and 3.
* **Company Information** (`company_info`, text, optional): Enter the company name and contact information for the gift recipient.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the gift recipient.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the gift recipient.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the gift recipient.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose a payment method for the order. Options include Credit Card, PayPal, and Cash.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional payment details, if necessary.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Specify the date of the order.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event or occasion.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company hosting the event.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the company hosting the event.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the company hosting the event.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Choose the state of the company hosting the event. Options include California, New York, and Florida.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the company hosting the event.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_one, true): Confirm that you have reviewed and confirmed the order details. Options include True and False.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional event information.
* **Product List** (`product_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select the products or gift items you would like to include in the gift basket. Options include 1, 2, and 3.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_multiple, true): Confirm the product type for the gift basket. Options include Gift Option 1, Gift Option 2, and Gift Option 3.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the gift items.
* **Confirm Info** (`confirm_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional confirm information.
* **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, select_one, true): Confirm that you have reviewed and confirmed the payment details. Options include True and False.

Note: Please review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid any delays or issues with the order.
