# Dog Training Class Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register your dog for a training class. Please complete the form below to provide us with the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Fill in your dog's name**: Enter your dog's name in the "Dog Name" field.
2. **Enter your name**: Enter your name as the owner of the dog in the "Owner Name" field.
3. **Add your phone number**: Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. **Select the training class**: Choose whether your dog will be attending "Yes" or "No" training class.
5. **Enter class details**: If your dog will be attending a training class, select the date and time of the class in the "Class Date" and "Class Time" fields.
6. **Add notes (optional)**: If you have any additional information to provide, type it in the "Notes" field.
7. **Enter your email address**: Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. **Additional class info (optional)**: If you have any specific requirements or requests for your dog, type them in the "Additional Class Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Name** (`dog_name`, text, required: false): Enter your dog's name.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the owner of the dog.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Training Class** (`training_class`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether your dog will be attending "Yes" or "No" training class.
* **Class Date** (`class_date`, date, required: false): If your dog will be attending a training class, enter the date of the class.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, time, required: false): If your dog will be attending a training class, enter the time of the class.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information you have about your dog.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Additional Class Info** (`training_class_additional_info`, text, required: false): If you have any specific requirements or requests for your dog, type them in this field.
