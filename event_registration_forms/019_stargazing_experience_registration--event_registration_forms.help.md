# Stargazing Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect participant details and session information for a stargazing experience. The goal is to gather all necessary information from participants to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience during the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Read the form carefully** before starting.
2.  **Enter all required information** in the relevant fields.
3.  **Submit the form** after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**
	+ **Participant Details (participant_details)**: This section is used to collect personal details from participants. Please fill in your name, email, and any other information as required.
* **Sessions**
	+ **Sessions (session_details)**: In this section, you can choose the sessions you would like to attend. Please select the sessions that interest you the most.
* **Equipment and Policies**
	+ **Equipment and Policies (equipment_policies)**: In this section, you can read and agree to the equipment and policies for the event. This may include information on personal belongings, safety guidelines, and emergency procedures.
