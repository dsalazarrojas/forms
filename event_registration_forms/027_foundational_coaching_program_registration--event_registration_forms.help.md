# Foundational Coaching Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foundational Coaching Program Registration form is designed to collect information about participants who wish to register for the Foundational Coaching Program. This form is used to ensure that all participants have the necessary information and agreements in place to participate in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information, including your name and email.
2. Select your preferred payment method.
3. Provide details about the program you are registering for, including the start and end dates, program duration, and coaching focus.
4. Choose the coaching type and select your preferred coach.
5. Enter details about the program, including the name, description, location, start and end times, and date.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant name**: Enter your name as it should be listed in the program materials.
* **email**: Enter your email address to contact you for program updates and notifications.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number to contact you for program-related purposes.
* **start date**: Enter the date you are starting the program.
* **end date**: Enter the date you will finish the program.
* **program start date**: Enter the date the program will start.
* **program end date**: Enter the date the program will end.
* **program duration**: Enter the total number of days the program will run.
* **coaching agreements**: Enter any additional agreements or conditions that must be met for you to participate in the program.
* **payment method**: Select how you will pay for the program (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **total cost**: Enter the total cost of the program.
* **billing address**: Enter your billing address.
* **mailing address**: Enter your mailing address.
* **additional comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **coaching focus**: Select the coaching focus that aligns with your needs.
* **coaching type**: Choose the coaching type that aligns with your needs.
* **coach**: Enter the name of your coach.
* **program name**: Enter the name of the program.
* **program description**: Enter a brief description of the program.
* **program location**: Enter the location where the program will take place.
* **program start time**: Enter the start time of the program.
* **program end time**: Enter the end time of the program.
* **program date**: Enter the date the program will take place.
* **coach name**: Enter the name of your coach.
* **total participants**: Enter the total number of participants in the program.
* **payment method**: Select how you will pay for the program (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).

Please note that some fields are optional, and you can skip them if not applicable.
