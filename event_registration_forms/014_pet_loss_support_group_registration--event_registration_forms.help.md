# Pet Loss Support Group Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Loss Support Group Registration form is designed to help individuals share their experiences and connect with others who have gone through similar situations. It is meant to provide a supportive environment for those dealing with the emotional challenges of pet loss.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details, such as your first and last name.
2. Provide information about your pet, including its name and type.
3. Select the reason(s) you are seeking therapy or support.
4. Choose your preferred session time and day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of your pet.
* **Pet Type**: Select the type of pet you owned (Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Pet Breed**: If you want to specify the breed of your pet, enter it here.
* **Reason for Therapy**: Select the reason(s) you are seeking support (Seeking social support, Coping with loss, Grief management).
* **Preferred Session Time**: Select the time of day you prefer for sessions.
* **Preferred Session Day**: Select the day you prefer for sessions.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are not already registered in the support group or have not been previously registered by mistake.
- Be as specific as possible when providing information about your pet and reason for seeking support.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the support group administrators.
