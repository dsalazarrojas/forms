## Golf Score Prediction Contest - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Golf Score Prediction Contest is a form designed for participants to register their information and predict their golf scores. This form is used to collect personal details and score predictions from golf enthusiasts, allowing for a fun and engaging experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Name in the "Participant Name" field. This is the first step in registering for the contest.
2. Enter your Predicted Score, which is a numerical value representing your estimated golf score.
3. Select your T-Shirt Size from the options provided. This is required information for prize distribution purposes.
4. Enter your Participant Email address in the "Participant Email" field. This is used for communication and prize distribution purposes.
5. Enter your Participant Phone number in the "Participant Phone" field. This is optional information for emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it will be displayed during the contest.
  - This will be displayed on the leaderboard and any communication related to the contest.
- **Predicted Score** (number, required): Predict the score you think you can achieve.
  - Please make sure to enter a numerical value, as this is used for scoring purposes.
- **T-Shirt Size** (select multiple, required): Select one or more T-Shirt sizes you are interested in, in case you win a prize.
  - Please choose one or more sizes that fit your preference.
- **Participant Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address for prize distribution and communication purposes.
  - Make sure to enter a real email address that you can access.
- **Participant Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
  - This is not required but recommended for emergency situations.
- **Participant Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
  - This is not required but recommended for emergency situations.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number for efficient communication.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
