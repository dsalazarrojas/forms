# Student Workshop Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Workshop Volunteer Application Form is designed for students to register as volunteers for a workshop, providing essential details for the event organizers. This form is used to collect and manage volunteer applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your first name, last name, email, and contact phone number.
2.  Choose the workshop type (e.g., Workshops, Seminars, Webinars, or Training).
3.  Select the workshop date and time you would like to volunteer for.
4.  Confirm your volunteer hours, start date, end date, and assigned tool.
5.  Confirm your email and phone number for communication purposes.
6.  Review your application before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date of the workshop you'd like to volunteer for.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, **required**): Select the time you'd like to volunteer for.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the type of workshop you'd like to volunteer for (e.g., Workshops, Seminars, Webinars, Training).
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, `number`, **required**): Enter the number of hours you'd like to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the start date of your volunteer period.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the end date of your volunteer period.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the tool assigned to you by the organizers.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `email`, **required**): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, `text`, **required**): Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Volunteer Hours** (`confirm_volunteer_hours`, `number`, **required**): Confirm the number of volunteer hours.
* **Confirm Volunteer Start Date** (`confirm_volunteer_start_date`, `date`, **required**): Confirm the start date of your volunteer period.
* **Confirm Volunteer End Date** (`confirm_volunteer_end_date`, `date`, **required**): Confirm the end date of your volunteer period.
* **Confirm Assigned Tool** (`confirm_assigned_tool`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm the tool assigned to you by the organizers.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, **required**): Click to submit your application.
* **Review** (`review`, `note`, **optional**): Review your application before submitting.
* **Back to previous page** (`back_to_previous_page`, `text`, **optional**): Go back to the previous page.
* **Save and print** (`save_and_print`, `text`, **optional**): Save and print your application.
* **Save and share** (`save_and_share`, `text`, **optional**): Save and share your application.
* **Reset** (`reset`, `text`, **optional**): Reset the form.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, **optional**): Cancel your application.

This help guide is designed to be clear and concise, allowing users to easily understand the purpose and process of completing the Student Workshop Volunteer Application Form.
