# Vendor Application For Night Market - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow vendors to apply to participate in the Night Market event by providing necessary information about themselves, their products or services, and their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
2. Make sure to provide all required information for a successful application.
3. Review your application before submitting to ensure that everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vendor Application** (`vendor_application`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your application. Please explain why you want to participate in the Night Market event and what makes you a good fit.
- **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself and your product or service that you think is relevant to your application.
- **Booth Preferences** (`booth_preferences`, text, optional): If you have specific requirements or preferences for your booth setup, please let us know here.
- **Agreement to Guidelines** (`agreement_to_guidelines`, text, optional): Please confirm that you have read and will comply with the Night Market guidelines.
- **Vendor Signature** (`vendor_signature`, text, optional): This is where you will sign your application to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
- **Vendor Contact Info** (`vendor_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you for further questions or updates.

## Tips
- Please double-check your application for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- Make sure to provide all required information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.

By following these instructions, you will be able to complete the form successfully and increase your chances of being approved for participation in the Night Market event.
