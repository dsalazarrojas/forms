# Remote Team Cohesion Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register your team for the Remote Team Cohesion Program. Please complete this form to provide your team information and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each page to ensure you have all the required information.
- Click on each field to enter your team's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Description** (`team_description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your team in a few words.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Job Title** (`team_lead_job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the job title of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Location** (`team_lead_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Time Zone** (`team_lead_time_zone`, `text`, required): Enter the time zone of your team lead.
* **Team Members** (`team_member_names`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all team members who will participate in the program.
* **Team Members Emails** (`team_members_emails`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether team members have email access or not.
* **Team Members Phones** (`team_members_phone`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether team members have phone access or not.
* **Team Lead Availability** (`team_lead_availability`, `date`, required): Enter the date when your team lead will be available for the program.
* **Team Lead Schedule** (`team_lead_schedule`, `time`, required): Enter the schedule of your team lead.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the program.
* **Program Notes** (`program_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the program.
* **Team Lead Experience** (`team_lead_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant experience level of your team lead.
* **Team Name 2** (`team_name_2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your team (again).

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
- Review your information for accuracy before submission.
- Reach out to your team lead for any questions or concerns.
