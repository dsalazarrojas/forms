# software_training_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for a software training appointment. Please use it to register your interest in attending a training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information, including your email address and phone number.
2. Choose your availability for the training session by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
3. Confirm your appointment preferences by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
5. Select the category and event details that interest you.
6. Finally, confirm that you are interested in the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you can enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Available Dates** (`date_and_time`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is where you select the dates and times you are available to attend the training session. Choose "Yes" for each date and time you are available.
* **Training Information** (`training_info`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is where you select the training information you are interested in. Choose "Yes" for each type of training you are interested in.
* **Appointment Confirmation** (`appointment_confirmation`, select_one, required: false): This field is where you confirm your appointment preferences. Choose "Yes" to confirm your appointment or "No" to decline.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is where you enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): This field is where you can provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): This field is where you select the category of the training session.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is where you select the event details that interest you. Choose "Yes" for each event detail you are interested in.
* **Training Sessions** (`training_sessions`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is where you select the training sessions you are interested in. Choose "Yes" for each session you are interested in.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the administrator for clarification.
* Please note that this form will be used to determine your interest in attending a training session, so please be honest in your responses.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please include them in the Note field.
