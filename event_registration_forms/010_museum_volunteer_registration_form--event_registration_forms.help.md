# Museum Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Volunteer Registration Form is used to collect information from potential volunteers who want to help out at the museum. It will help staff coordinate volunteer shifts and ensure that enough volunteers are scheduled to manage various tasks and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an *
2. Enter your name and email address
3. Provide your phone number
4. Select your shift start and end times
5. Indicate your volunteer role(s)
6. Choose your available dates
7. Select your availability
8. Add any additional notes about your registration

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your volunteer badge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your registration and volunteer opportunities.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your shifts and any last-minute changes.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, false): Select the start time for your volunteer shift. Please format this using the 24-hour clock.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, false): Select the end time for your volunteer shift. Please format this using the 24-hour clock.
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_multiple, false): Select the role(s) you are interested in volunteering for, such as event volunteer, exhibit assistant, or administrative assistant.
* **Available Dates** (`available_dates`, select_multiple, false): Choose the dates you are available to volunteer. This will help us schedule you for shifts that fit your schedule.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, false): Indicate whether you are available to volunteer during regular hours, weekends, or special events.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, false): Add any additional comments or information about your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to select your available dates accurately to help us schedule you for shifts that fit your schedule.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special needs, please let us know in the notes section.
* We will contact you to confirm your registration and provide more information about your volunteer shifts.
