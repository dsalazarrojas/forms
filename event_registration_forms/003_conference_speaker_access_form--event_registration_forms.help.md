# Conference Speaker Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from conference speakers to ensure they receive the necessary resources and accommodations during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your speaker name and contact information (speaker name, email, and phone number).
2. Enter your session title and details (session title, date, and start time).
3. Select any necessary access needs (stage access, green room, parking permit, onsite badge, or AV technician support).
4. List any special equipment required.
5. Write a brief summary of your bio (1 to 2 sentences).
6. Provide an emergency contact name and phone number (if applicable).
7. Enter your expected arrival and departure times.
8. Mention any special accommodations or accessibility needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker name** (required): Enter your full name as it should appear on your badge.
* **Speaker email** (required): Enter your primary contact email for conference communications.
* **Speaker phone** (optional): Enter your mobile phone number for day-of-event contact.
* **Organization** (optional): Enter your affiliation or employer.
* **Session title** (required): Enter the exact title of your session as it will be used in the event program.
* **Session date** (required): Enter the date of your presentation.
* **Session time** (required): Enter the scheduled start time of your presentation.
* **Access needs** (optional): Select any of the following options that apply:
	+ Stage access
	+ Green room
	+ Parking permit
	+ Onsite badge
	+ AV technician support
* **Equipment needed** (optional): List any special equipment required for your presentation.
* **Short speaker bio** (required): Write a brief summary of yourself (1 to 2 sentences).
* **Emergency contact** (optional): Enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Expected arrival time** (optional): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the venue.
* **Expected departure time** (optional): Enter the time you plan to leave the venue.
* **Special accommodations** (optional): Describe any accessibility or other special needs you may have.
