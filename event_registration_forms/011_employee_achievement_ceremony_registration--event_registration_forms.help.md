# Employee Achievement Ceremony Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Achievement Ceremony Registration form is used to RSVP and provide information about your attendance at the Employee Achievement Ceremony. Please fill out this form to help us plan the event accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the RSVP field, indicating whether you will be attending the ceremony.
3. If you are attending with a guest, enter their name and relationship to you (e.g., spouse, child, friend).
4. If applicable, provide a brief message about your attendance or guest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **rsvp** (`Yes/No`): Select "Yes" if you will be attending the ceremony, and "No" if you will not be attending.
* **name**: Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **guest_name**: Enter the name of any guests you are bringing to the ceremony.
* **guest_relationship**: Enter your relationship to the guest (e.g., spouse, child, friend).
* **message**: Provide any additional comments or information about your attendance or guest.
* **status** (`Active/Inactive`): This field is likely used to track the employee's attendance status, but it seems unnecessary as "rsvp_status" is already present. Please ignore this field and do not fill it out.
* **other_status**: This field is likely used for additional status options not included in the "status" select field. Please ignore this field and do not fill it out.
* **rsvp_by** (`Yes/No`): Select "Yes" if you are RSVPing in person, and "No" if you are not RSVPing in person.
* **other_rsvp_by**: This field is likely used for additional RSVP options not included in the "rsvp_by" select field. Please ignore this field and do not fill it out.
