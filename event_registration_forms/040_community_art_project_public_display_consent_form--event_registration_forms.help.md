# Community Art Project Public Display Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Art Project Public Display Consent Form is a document that collects information from artists and creators for the public display of their artwork. This form is essential for ensuring that all parties involved are aware of their responsibilities, conditions, and rights related to the display.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form carefully and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Ensure that you have read and understood all the fields and their corresponding questions.
3. If you are unsure about any question or field, please ask your contact person for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Information** (`Consent Information`, `note`, optional): This field provides a detailed explanation of the public display consent process.
* **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which you are providing this consent.
* **Consent ID** (`Consent ID`, `text`, required): Enter a unique consent reference number.
* **Artist Information** (`Artist Information`, `note`, optional): Provide details about the artist or creator.
* **Artist Name** (`Artist Name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the artist or creator.
* **Artist Email** (`Artist Email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email address of the artist or creator.
* **Artist Phone** (`Artist Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the artist or creator.
* **Artist Address** (`Artist Address`, `text`, optional): Enter the street address of the artist or creator.
* **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the representing organization if applicable.
* **Artwork Information** (`Artwork Information`, `note`, optional): Provide details about the artwork.
* **Artwork Title** (`Artwork Title`, `text`, required): Enter the title or name of the artwork.
* **Artwork Description** (`Artwork Description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the artwork.
* **Artwork Type** (`Artwork Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of artwork (e.g., Painting, Mural, Sculpture, etc.).
* **Artwork Dimensions** (`Artwork Dimensions`, `text`, optional): Enter the size of the artwork.
* **Creation Date** (`Creation Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the artwork was created.
* **Display Information** (`Display Information`, `note`, optional): Provide details about the public display.
* **Display Location** (`Display Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the artwork will be displayed.
* **Display Address** (`Display Address`, `text`, optional): Enter the full address of the display location.
* **Display Start Date** (`Display Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the display will begin.
* **Display End Date** (`Display End Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the display will end.
* **Display Type** (`Display Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of display (e.g., Temporary Exhibition, Permanent Installation, etc.).
* **Usage Rights** (`Usage Rights`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable rights to be granted (e.g., Display Rights, Photography Rights, etc.).
* **Compensation** (`Compensation`, `select_one`, required): Select whether there is compensation for this display (Yes - Fee Payment, Yes - Revenue Share, Yes - Trade or Exchange, etc.).
* **Compensation Amount** (`Compensation Amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of compensation in USD if applicable.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`Insurance Coverage`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the artwork will be insured (Yes - Organizer Insured, Yes - Artist Insured, No Insurance, etc.).
* **Maintenance Responsibility** (`Maintenance Responsibility`, `select_one`, required): Select who is responsible for maintenance (Organizer, Artist, Shared Responsibility, etc.).
* **Consent Declaration** (`Consent Declaration`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you consent to the public display (Yes - I Fully Consent, Yes - With Conditions, No - I Do Not Consent, etc.).
* **Conditions** (`Conditions`, `text`, optional): Enter any conditions for your consent.
* **Additional Terms** (`Additional Terms`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional terms or agreements.
* **Signature Confirmation** (`Signature Confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you confirm that you have read and agree to these terms (Yes - I Confirm, No).

## Tips
* Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to avoid any misunderstandings or delays.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your contact person for clarification.
* Remember to review this form thoroughly before signing the signature confirmation.
