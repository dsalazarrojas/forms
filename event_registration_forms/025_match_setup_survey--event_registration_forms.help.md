# match_setup_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The match_setup_survey form is designed to collect participant information, match preferences, and additional notes for a specific event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Go to the first page: participant information.
2. Fill in the required (if applicable) and optional fields with your information.
3. Proceed to the next page: match preferences.
4. Fill in the required (if applicable) and optional fields with your preferences.
5. Proceed to the next page: additional notes (if applicable).
6. Fill in any additional information you have about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information (participant_information)
* **Participant Information**: (`participant_information`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter any identifying information about yourself as a participant, if required.
### Match Preferences (match_preferences)
* **Match Preferences**: (`match_preferences`, `text`, required: false)
    Describe your preferences for the match, such as your playing style or any specific requirements.
### Additional Notes (additional_notes)
* **Additional Notes**: (`additional_notes`, `text`, required: false)
    Provide any additional information you think would be helpful for the organizers or other participants to know.

## Tips
* Please fill in the fields accurately and thoroughly, as this information will help the organizers understand your needs and preferences better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
