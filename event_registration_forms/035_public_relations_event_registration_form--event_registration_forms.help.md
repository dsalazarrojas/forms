# public-relations-event-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for event organizers and attendees to provide details about a public relations event. It is used to gather information about the event and the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form fields as requested, making sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
2. Enter your name and email address in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**: 
  • (event_organizer_details) 
  Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for the event. This field is optional.
* **Attendee**: 
  • (attendee_info) 
  Enter the name(s) of the person(s) attending the event. This field is optional.
* **Email**: 
  • (email) 
  Enter the contact email of the person or organization associated with the event. This field is optional.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure seamless event planning and execution.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any errors.
- If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
