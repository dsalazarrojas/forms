# Movie Screening Rights Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Movie Screening Rights Permission Form is used to collect information about a movie screening event, ensuring that the necessary permissions and licenses are in place for hosting the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event or screening.
### Screening Date
* **Screening Date** (`screening_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the screening event.
### Screening Start Time
* **Screening Start Time** (`screening_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the screening event.
### Screening End Time
* **Screening End Time** (`screening_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the screening event.
### Screening Location
* **Screening Location** (`screening_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the screening will take place.
### Movie Title
* **Movie Title** (`movie_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the movie being screened.
### Screening Length
* **Screening Length** (`screening_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the screening event in minutes.
### Screening Capacity
* **Screening Capacity** (`screening_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the maximum number of people that can attend the screening.
### Required Equipment
* **Required Equipment** (`required_equipment`, text, optional): List any equipment required for the screening, such as projectors or sound systems.
### Required Staff
* **Required Staff** (`required_staff`, text, optional): List any staff members required for the screening, such as projectionists or sound technicians.
### Screening Licenses
* **Screening Licenses** (`screening_licenses`, text, optional): Enter any licenses or permits required for the screening.
### Screening Venue
* **Screening Venue** (`screening_venue`, text, optional): Enter the venue where the screening will take place.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely to ensure that you have all necessary permissions and licenses.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, contact the relevant authorities or production team.
* This form is for informational purposes only, and the information collected will be used to ensure that the screening event runs smoothly and according to regulations.
