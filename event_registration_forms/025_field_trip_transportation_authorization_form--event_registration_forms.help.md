# Field Trip Transportation Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain authorization from parents/guardians for students to participate in field trips, ensuring their safety and well-being during transportation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and understand the implications of this form.
3. Attach any additional documents or information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification document.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, Text, Required): Enter the parent/guardian's name as it appears on their identification document.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, Text, Required): Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 9-12, 11-12, etc.).
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the field trip.
* **Trip Time** (`trip_time`, Time, Optional): If applicable, enter the time of the field trip.
* **Destination** (`destination`, Text, Required): Enter the location of the field trip.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, Select Multiple, Required):
	+ Choose one or more methods of transportation:
		- Car
		- School Bus
		- Walking
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the vehicle details (e.g., vehicle make, model, license plate number).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Choose one or more emergency contact:
		- Parent
		- School
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter any special needs or requirements for the student.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter any medical conditions that may affect the student during the field trip.
* **Medication** (`medication`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter any medication that the student may need to take during the field trip.
* **Transportation Consent** (`transportation_consent`, Text, Required): Sign below to indicate that you have read and understand the transportation details and conditions.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the field trip.
* **Parent Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required): Sign your name as a parent/guardian, indicating that you have read and understand the form.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, Date, Required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, Time, Required): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the contact email.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the school phone number.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the school email.
* **School Fax** (`school_fax`, Text, Optional): Enter the school fax number.

Please ensure you have read and understood the form before proceeding. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school administration.
