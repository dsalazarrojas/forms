# Craft Fair Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register vendors for the craft fair. It collects necessary information about the vendor, their contact details, and product offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in all required fields to ensure a successful registration.
2. Use the provided labels as a guide to fill in the correct information.
3. Review your form before submission to ensure all fields are filled correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_information`): Enter a brief introduction about your craft, including any relevant details that describe your product offerings.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`): Enter your business or home address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the vendor's contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the vendor's contact.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the vendor's contact.
* **Booth Information** (`booth_information`): Enter details about your booth setup, including any specific requirements you may have.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`): Enter your business name or brand name.
* **Vendor Category** (`vendor_category`): Enter the category of your craft or product offering.
* **Vendor Description** (`vendor_description`): Enter a detailed description of your craft or product offering.
* **Social Links** (`vendor_social_links`): Enter any social media links related to your craft or product offering.
* **Vendor Products** (`vendor_products`): Enter a list of your product offerings.
* **Vendor Image URL** (`vendor_image_url`): Enter the URL of an image representing your craft or product offering.
* **Vendor Video URL** (`vendor_video_url`): Enter the URL of a video representing your craft or product offering.
* **Vendor Website** (`vendor_weblink`): Enter the URL of your business website.
* **Booth Location** (`booth_location`): Enter the location of your booth in the craft fair.
* **Vendor Availability** (`vendor_availability`): Enter your availability schedule for the craft fair, including start and end dates.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Use the provided fields to ensure that all required information is submitted.
* Review your form before submission to ensure that all fields are filled correctly.
