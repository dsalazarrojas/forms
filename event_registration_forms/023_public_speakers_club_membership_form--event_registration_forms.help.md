# Public Speakers Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from interested individuals who wish to join the Public Speakers Club.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your Organization Name.
2. Provide your name as the Contact Person for your organization.
3. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you about upcoming events.
4. If available, provide your phone number for easier communication.
5. Briefly describe your Job Title and Department.
6. Write a short note about your Job Description.
7. If you are a member, mention your Member Since date.
8. If you have attended any events in the past, provide the date and time of the Last Event Attended.
9. Select the Interested Events you would like to participate in.
10. Finally, select whether you Agree to the Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Provide your name as the contact person for your organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for easier communication and event updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If available, provide your phone number for easier communication.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your job title within your organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Mention your department name or area of work.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, note, required: false): Write a short note about your job responsibilities and role within the organization.
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, date, required: false): If you are a member, provide the date when you joined the public speakers club.
* **Last Event Attended** (`last_event_attended`, time, required: false): If you have attended any events, provide the date and time of the last event you attended.
* **Interested Events** (`interested_events`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the events you are interested in participating in.
* **Agree Terms** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required: false): Finally, select whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the public speakers club.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Remember to agree to the terms and conditions to ensure you can participate in events and activities of the public speakers club.
