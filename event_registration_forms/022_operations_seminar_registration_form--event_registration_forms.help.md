# Operations Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operations Seminar Registration Form is used to collect information from participants for the Operations Seminar event. This form is used to ensure that all necessary information is collected and processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the Participant Name field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the Email field.
3. Enter your **Phone** in the Phone field.
4. Select your **Role** (Participant, Speaker, or Organizer) from the dropdown menu.
5. If applicable, enter your **Registration Date** and **Time**.
6. If you have any additional comments or information, you can enter them in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your name as it would appear on the seminar materials and communications.

### **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Enter your email address where you can be contacted for seminar-related communications.

### **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for seminar-related communications.

### **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false)
Select your role at the seminar:
* Participant: This is for people attending the seminar as a participant.
* Speaker: This is for speakers presenting at the seminar.
* Organizer: This is for the people organizing the seminar.

### **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the date you registered for the seminar, if you know it.

### **Time** (`time`, time, required: false)
If applicable, enter the time you registered for the seminar.

### **Note** (`note`, note, required: false)
If you have any additional comments or information about your registration, enter them here.
