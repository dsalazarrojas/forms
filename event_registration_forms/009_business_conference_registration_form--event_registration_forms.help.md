# Business Conference Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register for a business conference. The form collects personal and conference-specific information to help us prepare for your attendance and ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and job title.
2. Select your registration level (Standard, Premium, VIP, Day, or Student) and the days you plan to attend.
3. Choose up to 3 workshops of interest (optional).
4. Select your dietary restrictions (optional).
5. If you have any special accommodations, please let us know.
6. Indicate your availability for networking events and VIP dinner attendance.
7. Opt-in or out of post-event updates and confirm your payment terms.
8. Select your payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Purchase Order, or Company Account).
9. Add any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address where we can send registration confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, required): Enter your position within your organization.
* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, required): Select your registration level (Standard, Premium, VIP, Day, or Student).
* **Which Days Are You Attending** (`conference_days`, required): Select the days you plan to attend.
* **Workshops of Interest** (`workshops_interested`, optional): Select up to 3 workshops you are interested in (Digital Transformation, Finance Strategy, Marketing Innovation, Sales Mastery, Operations Excellence, or Leadership Development).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Halal, Kosher, or None).
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations`, optional): If you need any special accommodations, describe them here.
* **Availability** (`availability`, optional): Note any availability or special requests.
* **Interested in Networking Events** (`networking_events`, required): Indicate whether you are interested in attending networking events.
* **VIP Dinner Attendance** (`vip_dinner`, optional): Select whether you plan to attend the VIP dinner.
* **Opt-In to Post-Event Updates** (`communication_opt_in`, required): Choose whether you want to receive conference materials and news.
* **Payment Terms Accepted** (`payment_terms`, required): Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required): Select your payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Purchase Order, or Company Account).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any questions, special requests, or comments.

Note: The optional fields are not required but are included to provide additional information if needed.
