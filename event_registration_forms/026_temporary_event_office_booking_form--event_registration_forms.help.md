# Temporary Event Office Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event registration information from users to book office spaces.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the required fields.
3. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, so that the office staff can follow up with you.
4. Provide detailed event details, including event type, date, start and end times, capacity, and location.
5. If necessary, attach required documents, such as contracts or licenses.
6. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: The date of the event. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Start Time**: The start time of the event. Please enter the time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **End Time**: The end time of the event. Please enter the time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Event Duration**: The duration of the event in minutes. This field is not used for calculation, but it helps office staff plan for event setup and cleanup.
* **Event Capacity**: The maximum number of attendees the event will have.
* **Event Type**: Select all the types of events that this form is for (e.g., meeting, workshop, training, etc.).
* **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Event Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person who will be responsible for contacting.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who will be responsible for contacting.
* **Contact Notes**: Add any additional comments or questions the submitter would like to have answered.
* **Event Location**: The location of the event within the office building. Please be specific about the room number or floor.
* **Office Location**: The location of the office where the event will take place (e.g., floor 3, room 10).
* **Office Floor**: The floor number of the office where the event will take place.
* **Conference Rooms**: The conference rooms or rooms with presentation capabilities that will be used for the event. Please specify the room numbers or names.
* **Office Equipment**: The office equipment that will be required for the event, such as projectors or microphones. Please be specific about what is needed.
* **Office Meeting Table**: The meeting table or seating arrangement that will be required for the event. Please specify the type and number of tables.
* **Office Seating Capacity**: The seating capacity of the office space where the event will take place. This is not a required field.
* **Required Documents**: If there are any documents that need to be attached or submitted with the form, please provide them here. This could include contracts, licenses, or other relevant paperwork.
* **Submitter Name**: The name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: The email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: The phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Notes**: Any additional comments or questions from the submitter.
* **Office Location**: The office where the event will take place (e.g., main office, satellite office).
* **Office Floor**: The floor number of the office where the event will take place.

Note: Some fields may seem redundant or unnecessary. However, they are all intended to provide a comprehensive understanding of the event and help office staff prepare for it. Please fill out all fields carefully and accurately.
