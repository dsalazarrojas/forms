# Exhibit Promotion Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibit Promotion Release Form is a tool used to collect information from exhibitors about their promotion of events and exhibits. It helps in organizing and tracking the promotional activities of exhibitors, ensuring that all necessary details are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for "Form data" to indicate if the exhibit promotion is active or not.
2. Enter the "Event name" of the exhibit in the designated field.
3. Enter the "Exhibit name" of the promotional item.
4. Provide the "Exhibitor name" and "Exhibitor email" to contact the promoter.
5. Enter the "Exhibitor phone" number for further communication.
6. Choose the "Promotional channels" through which the promotion will be carried out (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or Other (please specify)).
7. If "Other" is chosen, enter the specific "Other promotional channels" used.
8. Add any additional comments about the form in the "Form comments" section.
9. List the "Exhibition team" members involved, including their names and roles.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form data**: Select "Yes" if the exhibit promotion is active, or "No" otherwise. This field is not required.
* **Event name**: Enter the name of the event for which the exhibit is being promoted.
* **Exhibit name**: Enter the name of the exhibit or the promotional item.
* **Exhibitor name**: Provide the name of the person or organization promoting the exhibit.
* **Exhibitor email**: Enter a valid email address to contact the exhibitor.
* **Exhibitor phone**: Enter the phone number of the exhibitor for further communication.
* **Promotional channels**: Choose the social media platforms or channels through which the promotion will take place. Select all applicable options.
* **Other promotional channels**: If "Other" is chosen, enter the specific channels used.
* **Form comments**: Add any additional comments about the form.
* **Exhibition team**: List the names and roles of the team members involved in the exhibition.
 
**Tips:**
- Be accurate when entering event and exhibit names.
- Enter a valid email address for the exhibitor.
- Ensure you select all applicable promotional channels.
- Use "Other" wisely, as it requires additional input.
