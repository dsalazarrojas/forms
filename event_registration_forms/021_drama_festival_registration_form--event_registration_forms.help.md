# drama festival registration form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The drama festival registration form is used for registering participants and their productions in the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your personal details such as first name, last name, and contact email/phone number.
2. Provide production details such as production name, production type, and scheduling request.
3. If you are a group, provide group details such as group name, group size, and group contact information.
4. Fill out any other relevant fields as necessary.

* **First name** (`individual_details_1`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`individual_details_2`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Individual or group** (`individual_details_2`, select_one, optional): Choose between "Individual" or "Group".
* **Event details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter your event name and any other relevant event details.
* **Event name** (`event_details_1`, text, optional): Enter your event name.
* **Scheduling request** (`event_details_2`, text, optional): Enter your scheduling request.
* **Production details** (`production_details`, text, optional): Enter your production details.
* **Director** (`production_details_2`, text, optional): Enter your director's name.
* **Scriptwriter** (`production_details_3`, text, optional): Enter your scriptwriter's name.
* **Production company** (`production_details_4`, text, optional): Enter your production company.
* **Location** (`production_details_5`, text, optional): Enter your production location.
* **Group name** (`group_details_1`, text, optional): Enter your group name.
* **Group size** (`group_details_2`, text, optional): Enter your group size.
* **Group contact** (`group_details_3`, text, optional): Enter your group contact information.
* **Group type** (`group_details_4`, text, optional): Enter your group type.
* **Scheduling request group** (`group_details_5`, text, optional): Enter your scheduling request group.

## Tips
* If you are a group, make sure to fill out the group details fields correctly.
