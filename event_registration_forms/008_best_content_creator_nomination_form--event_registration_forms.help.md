This form is designed to collect nominations for the "Best Content Creator" award, which recognizes individuals who produce outstanding content that has made a significant impact in their respective categories. The form is intended to collect information about the nominee's work, such as their name, contact information, and details about their content, including category, platform, follower count, content quality, creativity, engagement, consistency, impact, and notable works. This form will help the nominator to highlight the strengths of the nominated creator and provide a clear and concise submission.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. Fill in the nominee's name, email address, and social media handles.
3. Select the category and platform where the nominee's content is primarily created.
4. Choose the approximate number of followers or subscribers the nominee has.
5. Evaluate the quality, creativity, and engagement of the nominee's content.
6. Describe how the nominee's content has made an impact in their field.
7. Provide a reason for your nomination.
8. You may also provide links to notable works, but it's optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`nominator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to be associated with this nomination.
* **Your Email Address** (`nominator_email`, `email`, required): We will send the confirmation email to this address.
* **Your Organization** (`nominator_organization`, `text`, optional): If you represent a company or organization, enter it here.
* **Nominee Full Name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the nominee's full name.
* **Nominee Email Address** (`nominee_email`, `email`, required): Contact email for the nominated creator.
* **Nominee Social Media Handles** (`nominee_social_handles`, `text`, required): List their main social media usernames or channels.
* **Content Category** (`content_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of content the nominee produces (e.g., Educational, Entertainment, Lifestyle, etc.).
* **Primary Platform** (`primary_platform`, `select_one`, required): Select the platform where the nominee primarily creates and shares their content (e.g., YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, etc.).
* **Approximate Follower Count** (`follower_count`, `select_one`, required): Choose the approximate number of followers or subscribers the nominee has.
* **Content Quality Rating** (`content_quality`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the quality of the nominee's content (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Average).
* **Creativity and Originality Rating** (`creativity_rating`, `select_one`, required): Assess how creative and original the nominee's content is (Extremely Creative, Very Creative, Creative, Somewhat Creative, Not Very Creative).
* **Audience Engagement Rating** (`engagement_rating`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate how well the nominee engages with their audience (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Content Consistency** (`consistency`, `select_one`, required): Rate how consistent the nominee is in posting content (Very Consistent, Mostly Consistent, Somewhat Consistent, Inconsistent, Very Inconsistent).
* **Describe Their Impact** (`impact_description`, `text`, required): Describe how the nominee's content has made a difference in their field.
* **Why Are You Nominating Them** (`nomination_reason`, `text`, required): Explain why you are nominating this creator for the Best Content Creator award.
* **Notable Work or Achievements** (`notable_work`, `text`, optional): List any notable projects, collaborations, or achievements of the nominee.
* **Links to Content Samples** (`content_samples`, `text`, optional): Provide links to the nominee's best content pieces.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any other information to support this nomination.

Note that some fields are optional but recommended for providing additional context and details about the nomination.
