# Annual Budgeting Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Annual Budgeting Event, which is a gathering of professionals focused on budgeting best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Registrant full name** in the "Registrant full name" field. This is the name of the person completing this form.
2. Enter your **organization or department** in the "Organization or department" field, if applicable.
3. Select your **role or job title** in the "Role or job title" field, as it relates to your budgeting responsibilities.
4. Enter your **contact email** in the "Contact email" field, which will be used for event communications.
5. Select all **sessions you plan to attend** from the list in the "Sessions to attend" field. You can select multiple sessions.
6. Choose your **attendance type** in the "Attendance type" field. You can choose between "In person", "Virtual", or "Hybrid".
7. If you have any **dietary restrictions**, list them in the "Dietary restrictions" field.
8. If you require an **official invoice for payment**, select "True" in the "Require invoice for payment" field.
9. Finally, provide an **emergency contact phone number** in the "Emergency contact phone number" field, in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant full name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person completing this form.
* **Organization or department** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the department or company you are affiliated with.
* **Role or job title** (`role`, text, optional): Describe your role related to budgeting responsibilities.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address that will be used for event communications.
* **Sessions to attend** (`sessions_selected`, select_multiple, required): Select all sessions you plan to attend.
* **Attendance type** (`attendance_type`, select_one, required): Choose between "In person", "Virtual", or "Hybrid".
* **Dietary restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): List any food allergies or preferences you have.
* **Require invoice for payment** (`require_invoice`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you require an official invoice for payment.
* **Emergency contact phone number** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide a contact phone number in case of an emergency.
