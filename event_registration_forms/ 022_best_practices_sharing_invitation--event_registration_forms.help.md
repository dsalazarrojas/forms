# Best Practices Sharing Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to invite others to share their best practices, which are valuable experiences, techniques, or knowledge gained through their work or personal projects. The form gathers information necessary for the invitation process, ensuring that the best practices are properly addressed and shared with the right people.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your personal information**: Enter your full name and a valid email address where you can be reached.
2. **Select your primary role**: Choose whether you are an attendee, exhibitor, or speaker.
3. **Provide a relevant date**: Choose a date that best suits your best practices sharing needs.
4. **Specify quantity or rating**: If applicable, provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
5. **Confirm your agreement**: Select any that apply from the agreement options.
6. **Share estimated value (optional)**: If necessary, provide an estimated value in decimal format.
7. **Suggest a preferred time**: If scheduling is required, enter a time for the best practices sharing session.
8. **Add final comments or notes**: Provide any closing remarks or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name**: This field requires your legal full name for proper addressing of the invitation.
- **Email address**: Enter a valid email for contact purposes.
- **Phone number**: If you are outside your local region, include your country code in your phone number.
- **Date**: Select a date that best matches your best practices sharing needs.
- **Primary selection**: Choose the option that best describes your role: Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker.
- **Quantity or rating**: If necessary, provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
- **Additional details**: Provide any extra information relevant to your best practices sharing.
- **Agreement**: Select any that apply from the provided options to confirm your agreement.
- **Estimated value**: If necessary, provide an estimated value in decimal format.
- **Preferred time**: If scheduling is required, enter a time for the best practices sharing session.
- **Final comments or notes**: Provide any final remarks or additional information.
