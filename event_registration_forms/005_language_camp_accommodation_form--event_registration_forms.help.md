# Language Camp Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants attending a language camp, which includes details about their accommodation preferences, arrival and departure dates, type of participant, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the participant's details, including their arrival and departure dates, accommodation preferences, and any additional information they want to share.
2. Select the type of participant and accommodation type that best suits their needs.
3. Enter the participant's contact person's details, including their name and email.
4. Provide the participant's name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the participant.
* **Accommodation type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of accommodation that suits the participant's needs. Options include:
	+ Label 1
	+ Label 2
	+ Label 3
* **Accommodation details** (`accommodation_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about the accommodation preferences or requirements.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, optional): Enter the participant's expected arrival date.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, optional): Enter the participant's expected departure date.
* **Participant type** (`participant_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of participant. Options include:
	+ Type A
	+ Type B
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Add any further information about the participant.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of the form.
* Double-check the participant's details and accommodation preferences before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
