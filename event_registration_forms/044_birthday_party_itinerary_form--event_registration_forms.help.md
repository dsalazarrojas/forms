# Birthday Party Itinerary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Birthday Party Itinerary Form is used to collect information about the birthday party, including the celebrant's details, party schedule, and guests' expectations. This form helps the birthday person plan and organize their special day effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the required fields**: The following fields are mandatory and must be filled out to complete the form:
	* Birthday Celebrant
	* Party Date
	* Party Start Time
	* Party End Time
	* Guest Arrival Time
	* Cleanup/Departure Time
	* Guest Name
	* Email Address
	* Will You Attend
	* Can You Arrive on Time
	* Number of Guests in Your Party
	* I confirm this information is correct
2. **Provide additional information**: You can also fill in the following fields to provide more details about the party:
	* Party Schedule/Itinerary
	* Greeting Activity or Icebreaker
	* First Activity Description
	* Meal or Snack Time
	* Second Activity Description
	* Cake Cutting Time
	* Gift Opening Time
	* Dietary Restrictions or Allergies
	* Dietary Notes
	* Activities Your Group Cannot Participate In
	* Mobility or Accessibility Needs
	* Will You Bring a Gift
	* Gift Ideas or Preferences
	* Birthday Message for Celebrant
	* Special Requests or Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Birthday Celebrant**: Enter the name of the person whose birthday it is.
* **Party Date**: Enter the date of the party in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Party Start Time**: Enter the start time of the party in 24-hour format (`HH:MM`).
* **Party End Time**: Enter the end time of the party in 24-hour format (`HH:MM`).
* **Party Location**: Enter the address or location of the party.
* **Party Schedule/Itinerary**: Describe the schedule of events for the party.
* **Greeting Activity or Icebreaker**: Suggest an activity to do when guests arrive.
* **First Activity Description**: Describe the first activity planned for the party.
* **Meal or Snack Time**: Enter the time when food will be served.
* **Second Activity Description**: Describe the second activity planned for the party.
* **Cake Cutting Time**: Enter the time when cake will be served.
* **Gift Opening Time**: Enter the time when gifts will be opened.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Select any dietary needs or allergies that guests may have (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free).
* **Dietary Notes**: Provide any specific dietary notes or requirements.
* **Activities Your Group Cannot Participate In**: List any activities that your group cannot participate in.
* **Mobility or Accessibility Needs**: Describe any mobility or accessibility needs.
* **Will You Bring a Gift**: Select whether you will bring a gift.
* **Gift Ideas or Preferences**: Suggest any gift ideas or preferences.
* **Birthday Message for Celebrant**: Write a special message for the birthday person.
* **Special Requests or Comments**: Add any special requests or comments.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the guest.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the guest.
* **Guest Arrival Time**: Enter the arrival time of the guest.
* **Greeting Activity or Icebreaker**: Suggest an activity to do when guests arrive.
* **First Activity Description**: Describe the first activity planned for the party.
* **Meal or Snack Time**: Enter the time when food will be served.
* **Second Activity Description**: Describe the second activity planned for the party.
* **Cake Cutting Time**: Enter the time when cake will be served.
* **Gift Opening Time**: Enter the time when gifts will be opened.
* **Number of Guests in Your Party**: Enter the total number of guests in your party.
* **I confirm this information is correct**: Select whether you confirm that the information provided is correct.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing additional information.
* Proofread your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to ask the birthday person if they have any special requests or requirements.
* If you have any doubts or questions, ask the birthday person or the party organizer for clarification.
