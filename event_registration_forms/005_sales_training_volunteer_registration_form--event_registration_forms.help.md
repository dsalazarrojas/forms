# Sales Training Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Training Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect basic contact information and details about your training preferences as a sales training volunteer. This form will help us understand your interests and tailor the training experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration information on page 1.
2. Enter your contact information on page 2.
3. Provide any additional details about your training preferences on page 3.
4. Add any notes or comments about your participation on page 4.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Info (Page 1)** 
	+ Enter your name and any other identifying information to let us know you're interested in participating in the sales training program.
* **Contact Info (Page 2)** 
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can reach out to you about the training program.
* **Training Info (Page 2)** 
	+ Describe your training preferences, including any areas of interest or topics you'd like to focus on during the program.
* **Notes (Page 3)** 
	+ Add any additional comments or information about your participation or training preferences.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your accurate contact information so we can contact you about the training program.
* Keep your training preferences as detailed as possible so we can tailor the experience to your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the training program, feel free to contact us using the contact information you've provided.
