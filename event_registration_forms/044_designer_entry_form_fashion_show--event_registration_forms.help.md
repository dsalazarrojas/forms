# designer_entry_form_fashion_show - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for fashion designers to register for a fashion show. It is intended for designers who wish to showcase their latest collections, designs, and creations to a targeted audience. Please ensure that you have permission to use this form and that the information provided is accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the "Designers Info" field.
2. Provide your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
4. Specify your availability in the "Availability" field.
5. Enter the name of your collection in the "Collection Name" field.
6. Indicate the number of looks you are bringing to the fashion show in the "Number Of Looks" field.
7. Choose the market segment you are targeting in the "Market Segment" field.
8. Upload sketches and images related to your collection in the "Sketches And Images" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Designers Info** (text, required): Please enter a brief description of yourself, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Organization** (text, optional): Enter the name of your organization or fashion house.
* **Contact Info** (text, optional): Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Availability** (text, optional): Indicate your availability for the fashion show, including specific dates and times you are available.
* **Collection Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your collection, including any relevant details about the style, theme, or inspiration.
* **Number Of Looks** (number, optional): Specify the number of pieces or "looks" you are bringing to the fashion show.
* **Market Segment** (text, optional): Choose the market segment you are targeting, such as women's, men's, or kids' clothing.
* **Sketches And Images** (text, optional): Upload sketches or images related to your collection, including any relevant details or context.
