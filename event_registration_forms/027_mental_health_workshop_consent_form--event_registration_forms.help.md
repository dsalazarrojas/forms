# mental_health_workshop_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent for participants in mental health workshops. It helps us understand the participant's background, workshop details, and emergency contact information, ensuring a safe and supportive environment for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Info section with your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. Provide details about the workshop you are participating in, such as the workshop title and any specific needs or concerns you have.
3. Share information about your mental health history and any relevant medical conditions.
4. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email.
5. If you have an emergency contact person, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you (e.g., parent, guardian, or other).
6. Specify the relationship you have with your emergency contact person.
7. Leave any additional comments or details about your emergency contact person in the Emergency Contact Details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (Participant Info, text, required: false): Fill in your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Workshop Info** (Workshop Info, text, required: false): Provide details about the workshop you are participating in, such as the workshop title and any specific needs or concerns you have.
* **Mental Health Info** (Mental Health Info, text, required: false): Share information about your mental health history and any relevant medical conditions.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, required: false): If you have an emergency contact person, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you (e.g., parent, guardian, or other).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, select_one, required: false): Specify the relationship you have with your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required: false): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Participant Agreement** (Participant Agreement, text, required: false): Leave any additional comments or details about your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (Emergency Contact Details, text, required: false): Leave any additional comments or details about your emergency contact person.
* **Workshop Details** (Workshop Details, text, required: false): Leave any additional comments or details about the workshop you are participating in.
