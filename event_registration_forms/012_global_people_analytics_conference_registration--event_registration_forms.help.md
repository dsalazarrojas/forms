# Global People Analytics Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help you complete the Global People Analytics Conference registration form. Please follow the steps and explanations below to ensure you complete the form correctly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Information fields on page 1-3.
2. Provide your registration information on page 4-5.
3. Select your preferences on page 6-8.
4. Confirm your terms of service and privacy policy on page 9-10.
5. Provide additional information as needed on page 11-17.
6. Complete the payment and shipping information on page 18-24.
7. Confirm your registration on page 25.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details** (Page 1): 
  * This is the first page of the form where you can provide your personal details.
  * Please fill out your name, email, and other required fields.
* **Registration Info** (Page 2): 
  * This page provides space for additional information about your registration.
  * You can fill out any information you want to share with the organizers.
* **Segment Participants** (Page 3): 
  * This field allows you to select which segments you are participating in.
  * Please select all applicable segments.
* **Registration Confirmation** (Page 4): 
  * This page confirms that you have registered for the event.
  * Please review and agree to the terms of service.
* **Payment Method** (Page 5): 
  * This field allows you to select your preferred payment method.
  * You can choose between Credit Card, Pay Pal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Info** (Page 6): 
  * This page provides space for payment information.
  * Please fill out any necessary payment information.
* **Additional Questions** (Page 7): 
  * This field allows you to answer any additional questions the organizers may have.
  * Please answer any questions you feel are relevant.
* **Comments** (Page 8): 
  * This field allows you to add any comments or feedback.
  * Please keep your comments brief and relevant.
* **Terms of Service** (Page 9): 
  * This page outlines the terms of service for the event.
  * Please review and agree to the terms of service.
* **Terms of Service Agreement** (Page 10): 
  * This field allows you to confirm your understanding of the terms of service.
  * Please select 'I agree' to confirm.
* **Privacy Policy** (Page 11): 
  * This page outlines the privacy policy for the event.
  * Please review and agree to the privacy policy.
* **Contact Preferences** (Page 12): 
  * This field allows you to select your contact preferences.
  * You can choose to receive emails from the event organizers or not.
* **Social Media** (Page 13): 
  * This field allows you to select your preferred social media platform.
  * You can choose Facebook, Twitter, or LinkedIn.
* **Follow-up Messages** (Page 14): 
  * This field allows you to select whether you want to receive follow-up messages.
  * Please select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Survey** (Page 15): 
  * This field allows you to select whether you want to participate in the survey.
  * Please select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Photos** (Page 16): 
  * This field allows you to upload photos.
  * Please select 'Upload photo' or 'Do not upload photo'.
* **Additional Fields** (Page 17): 
  * This field allows you to select any additional fields that may be required.
  * Please select all applicable fields.
* **Additional Notes** (Page 18): 
  * This field provides space for any additional notes.
  * Please fill out any additional information.
* **Confirm Email** (Page 19): 
  * This field confirms your email address.
  * Please fill out your email address.
* **Confirm Password** (Page 20): 
  * This field confirms your password.
  * Please fill out your password.
* **Confirm CVV** (Page 21): 
  * This field confirms your CVV (Credit Card Verification Value).
  * Please fill out your CVV.
* **Confirm Phone** (Page 22): 
  * This field confirms your phone number.
  * Please fill out your phone number.
* **Confirm Billing Address** (Page 23): 
  * This field confirms your billing address.
  * Please fill out your billing address.
* **Confirm Shipping Address** (Page 24): 
  * This field confirms your shipping address.
  * Please fill out your shipping address.
* **Confirm Terms of Service** (Page 25): 
  * This field confirms your understanding of the terms of service.
  * Please select 'I agree'.

## Tips

* Please review and follow all instructions carefully to ensure correct completion of the form.
* If you have any questions, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submission.
