# seminar_convention_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for a seminar convention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Information section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. If you are attending with an accompanying person (e.g., spouse, child), select their relationship from the Accompanying Person dropdown menu.
4. In the Payment section, select your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information**:
	+ (1) **title**: Enter your name as you would like it to be listed in the convention materials.
* **Contact Information**: 
	+ (2) **contact_information**: Fill in your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Accompanying Person**:
	+ (3) **accompanying_person**: Choose the relationship of the accompanying person from the dropdown menu: First, Spouse, Child, or Other. 
* **Payment**:
	+ (4) **payment**: Select your preferred payment method.

## Tips
* If you are attending with an accompanying person, please list their name and contact information as well.
* Ensure your payment method is secure and accurate to avoid any issues.
