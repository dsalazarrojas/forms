# Online Meeting Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the fields and instructions for completing the Online Meeting Authorization Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information to fill out the required fields.
2. Enter the meeting details in the "Meeting Details" field.
3. Provide your meeting date in the "Meeting Date" field.
4. Enter the meeting time in the "Meeting Time" field.
5. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
6. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Select your participant role in the "Participant Role" field.
9. Choose your consent to participate by selecting "I agree to participate" or "I do not agree to participate" in the "Participant Consent" field.
10. Read and agree to the participant terms in the "Participant Terms" field.
11. Click "Submit" to submit your information and "Cancel" to discard your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details**: Enter a brief description of the meeting, including its purpose and agenda.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the start and end time of the meeting.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as a participant in the meeting.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the meeting.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the meeting.
* **Participant Role**: Select your role in the meeting, e.g., speaker, attendee, or organizer.
* **Participant Consent**: Choose whether you agree or disagree to participate in the meeting.
* **Participant Agreement**: A text field for any further participant agreement or terms.
* **Participant Terms**: A text field for any additional participant terms or conditions.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your information and register for the meeting.
* **Cancel**: Discard your changes and start over.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate information to avoid confusion or miscommunication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* Review your submission before clicking "Submit" to ensure you have filled out all necessary fields.
* If you need to make changes, click the "Cancel" button to start over.
