**Tropical Event Registration - Help Guide**
======================================

## Purpose
The Tropical Event Registration form is used to collect information from attendees who wish to participate in the Tropical Event. This form serves as a quick and easy way to gather event-related data, ensuring that all necessary details are captured before the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with the required and any additional information about the event you wish to participate in.
2. In the **Attendee Preferences** section, select your preference for attending the event.
3. In the **Consent** section, provide your consent to participate in the event.
4. In the **Contact Info** and **Name** sections, fill in your contact information and name respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: This section is where you provide the title of the event, and any other relevant information about the event. (Type: text, Required: No)
* **Attendee Preferences**: Choose your preference to attend the event by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No'. (Type: Select multiple, Required: No)
* **Consent**: This is where you give your consent to participate in the event. Please select 'Yes' to confirm your consent. (Type: text, Required: No)
* **Contact Info**: Fill in any contact information you may want to provide, such as email or phone number, to ensure we can get in touch with you in case of any updates or questions. (Type: text, Required: No)
* **Name**: Fill in your name so we can refer to you by a name in the event communication. (Type: text, Required: No)

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required and optional fields accurately and completely to help us better communicate with you about the event.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us and we'll be happy to help.
* Once you submit the form, you will receive a confirmation email with event details.
