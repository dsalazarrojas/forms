# Music Band Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music Band Information Form is used by music event organizers to collect information about the bands or musicians performing at an event. This form helps event organizers to gather essential details about the bands, such as the type of event, name, contact email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event from the "Event Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the name of the band or musician.
3. Provide a valid email address for the band or musician.
4. Enter the phone number of the band or musician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Type (select_one)
* Select the type of event from the dropdown menu:
	+ **Concert**: A performance by a musician or band in a live setting.
	+ **Festival**: A celebration of music featuring multiple bands or musicians.
	+ **Tour**: A series of performances by a band or musician across multiple locations.

### Name (text)
* Enter the name of the band or musician.

### Email (email)
* Enter a valid email address for the band or musician.

### Phone Number (text)
* Enter a valid phone number for the band or musician.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct event type to ensure that the band or musician is properly categorized.
* Double-check the email address and phone number for accuracy to ensure that the band or musician can be contacted successfully.
* If you're unsure about any of the information, please contact the event organizer for clarification.
