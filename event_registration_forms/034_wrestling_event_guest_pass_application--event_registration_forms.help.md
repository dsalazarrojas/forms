# wrestling_event_guest_pass_application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The `wrestling_event_guest_pass_application` form is a tool used to process guest pass applications for wrestling events. It helps organizers gather necessary information from applicants and ensures they understand the event rules and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the application details, entering any relevant information about your application.
2. Acknowledge the rules and guidelines of the event by providing a brief response.
3. Enter your guest details, which will help us get to know you better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details** (Application Details, text, optional): Enter your application details, including any relevant information about your event-related experiences, goals, or expectations.
* **Rules Acknowledgement** (Rules Acknowledgement, text, optional): Briefly respond to the event rules and guidelines, indicating your understanding and willingness to comply with them.
* **Guest Details** (Guest Details, text, optional): Provide information about yourself, such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and concise responses in each field.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask the event organizers for clarification.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

By following these steps, you'll be able to successfully complete the `wrestling_event_guest_pass_application` form and provide the necessary information for your guest pass application.
