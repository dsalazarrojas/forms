# Mixed Doubles Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mixed Doubles Registration Form is used to collect registration information for participants in a Mixed Doubles sports event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details in the "Participant Details" section.
2. Enter your team name and player details in the "Team Name", "Player 1 Name", and "Player 2 Name" sections.
3. Enter the email addresses of your players in the "Player 1 Email" and "Player 2 Email" sections.
4. Select your team's experience and level in the "Team Experience" and "Team Level" sections.
5. Choose your team's formation in the "Team Formation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details**: <em>(Participant Details)</em> (<name>, <type>, required/optional): This section is for the participant's personal information.
- **Team Name** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter your team's name.
- **Player 1 Name** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter the name of the first player on your team.
- **Player 2 Name** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter the name of the second player on your team.
- **Player 1 Email** (<name, <type>, required/optional): Enter the email address of the first player on your team.
- **Player 2 Email** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter the email address of the second player on your team.
- **Team Experience** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Select your team's experience in the sport.
- **Team Level** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Select your team's level in the sport.
- **Team Formation** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Choose your team's formation in the sport.
