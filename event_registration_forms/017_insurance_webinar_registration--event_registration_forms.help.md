# Insurance Webinar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for attendees to register for the insurance webinar. Please fill in the required fields and submit your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your webinar registration form with your desired information.
2. Select your insurance type and other relevant details.
3. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Indicate if you have attended a previous webinar.
5. Add any additional comments or notes.
6. Provide any additional company details, such as industry and job function.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Webinar Registration Form**: Please fill in your desired information for the webinar registration form.
* **Contact Information**: Please fill in your email and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Insurance Type**: Please select the type of insurance that applies to you.
* **Insurance Company**: Please fill in your insurance company name.
* **First Name**: Please fill in your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please fill in your last name.
* **Email**: Please fill in your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Please fill in your phone number.
* **Have you attended one of our webinars?**: Please select "yes" if you have attended a previous webinar, and "no" if not.
* **Notes**: Please add any additional comments or notes you would like to share.
* **Date Attended**: Please fill in the date you attended the previous webinar.
* **Time Attended**: Please fill in the time you attended the previous webinar.
* **Company**: Please fill in your company name.
* **Position**: Please fill in your position.
* **Company Type**: Please fill in your company type.
* **Industry**: Please fill in your industry.
* **Insurance License Number**: Please fill in your insurance license number (if applicable).
* **Employee Number**: Please fill in your employee number (if applicable).
* **Job Title**: Please fill in your job title.
* **Job Function**: Please fill in your job function.
* **Department**: Please fill in your department.
* **Manager**: Please fill in your manager's name.
* **Manager Title**: Please fill in your manager's title.
* **Manager Email**: Please fill in your manager's email address.
