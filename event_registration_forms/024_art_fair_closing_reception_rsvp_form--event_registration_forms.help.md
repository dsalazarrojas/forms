# Art Fair Closing Reception RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us gather important information about your attendance and preferences for the Art Fair Closing Reception. Please take a few minutes to fill it out and help us make your experience as wonderful as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide as much information as you can in the optional fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (`guest_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it is written on your invitation or as you would like to be addressed.
* **Will you be attending the closing reception?** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Check Yes - I will be there or No - I am unable to attend.
* **Email Address for Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can confirm your RSVP and send you updates about the event.
* **Date of RSVP** (`rsvp_submission_date`, date, required): Please select the date by which you have submitted your RSVP.
* **Your Role in the Art Community** (`professional_role`, select_one, required): Check the box that best represents your role in the Art Community.
* **Do you have any dietary requirements?** (`dietary_requirements`, select_multiple, required): Select all the dietary restrictions you have, such as Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, etc.
* **Any accessibility requirements for the venue?** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please let us know so we can provide the necessary accommodations.
* **Preferred Beverage Category** (`beverage_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of beverage you would like to have available to you during the event.
* **Any accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Please let us know if you have any accessibility needs.
* **What was your highlight of the Art Fair this year?** (`favorite_moment`, text, optional): Share with us your favorite moment from the Art Fair.
* **Total number of people in your party** (`guest_count`, number, optional): If you are attending with a group, please let us know how many people will be with you.
* **Your RSVP will be confirmed by email to** (`email_confirmation`, email, required): This is the email we will use to confirm your RSVP and send you updates about the event.
* **Your Role in the Art Community** (`professional_role`, select_one, required): Please check the box that best represents your role in the Art Community.
* **Do you have any other feedback about the Art Fair?** (`feedback_header`, note, optional): If you have any thoughts, suggestions, or comments, feel free to share with us.
