# Leadership Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Summit Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to attend the Leadership Summit event. This form is used to gather all necessary details required for event planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the "Event Information" page to provide a brief description of the event.
2. Move on to the "Participant Information" page to fill out your details as a participant.
3. Enter your contact information on the "Contact Information" page.
4. Fill in the "Registration Information" page to provide any additional details required for registration.
5. On the "Payment Method" page, select your preferred method of payment.
6. Provide payment information on the "Payment Information" page.
7. If required, provide additional payment method details on the "Payment Method Details" page.
8. Enter any relevant participant details on the "Participant Details" page.
9. Let us know about your preferences on the "Participant Preferences" page.
10. Confirm the status of your payment on the "Payment Status" page.
11. Lastly, confirm the event status on the last page "Event Status".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Information** (`Event Information`, text, required: false): This is the page where you can provide a brief description of the event.
* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, text, required: false): Please enter your details as a participant to help us get you the most out of this summit.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required: false): Please provide your contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Registration Information** (`Registration Information`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details required for registration.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, text, required: false): Select your preferred method of payment.
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, text, required: false): Provide any required payment information for your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Method Details** (`Payment Method Details`, text, required: false): If required, enter additional details about your selected payment method.
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, text, required: false): Enter any necessary details about yourself as a participant.
* **Participant Preferences** (`Participant Preferences`, text, required: false): Let us know about your preferences so we can tailor the experience for you.
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, text, required: false): Confirm the status of your payment.
* **Event Status** (`Event Status`, text, required: false): Confirm the status of the event.
