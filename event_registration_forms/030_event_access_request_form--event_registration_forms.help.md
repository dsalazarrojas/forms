# Event Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Access Request Form is used to request access to an event for a person or entity. This form is meant to collect information from individuals or entities seeking access to the event and help event staff and management make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your name and email address to ensure we can contact you with any further questions or concerns.
3. Enter the event start and end times (if applicable).
4. If you are a speaker, vendor, or guest, select your corresponding access level.
5. Add any additional notes if you have any specific requests or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Access Request**: This field is the main purpose of the form. Describe in a few words what type of access you are requesting.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Attendee Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event you are seeking to access (if applicable).
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event you are seeking to access (if applicable).
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest you are accompanying to the event.
* **Guest Email**: Enter the valid email address of the guest you are accompanying to the event.
* **Guest Phone**: Enter the phone number of the guest you are accompanying to the event.
* **Speaker Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
* **Speaker Phone**: Enter your valid phone number.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter your company name or organization name.
* **Vendor Phone**: Enter your valid company phone number.
* **Access Level**: Select the level of access you are requesting:
	+ Guest: Access for a guest accompanying an attendee.
	+ Speaker: Access for a speaker or presenter.
	+ Vendor: Access for a vendor or sponsor.
	+ All: Access for all the above categories (typically for event staff or management).
* **Access Notes**: Add any additional information or notes that will help event staff and management make their decisions.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any other relevant information about your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask event staff or management for clarification.
* We will review your request and respond to you as soon as possible.
