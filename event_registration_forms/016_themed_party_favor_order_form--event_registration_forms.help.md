# themed_party_favor_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture event registration information for a themed party. It is a basic template for collecting party favor order details and attendee personal information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill out the "party favor order" field on page 1.
2. On page 2, enter your "Name" and provide a valid "email" address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **party favor order** (`party_favor_order`): 
    A text field where you can specify your desired party favor order. Please type your order clearly and concisely.
* **Name** (`name`): 
    Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (`email`): 
    Provide a valid email address where you can be reached for further communications or updates.
