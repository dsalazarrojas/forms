# Fine Dining Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help fine dining customers provide their event information, such as dates, times, and party sizes, to ensure accurate and efficient reservation preparation and confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the desired date for your fine dining experience.
2. Select your preferred time for the reservation.
3. Indicate the number of people in your party.
4. Provide your phone number or email address for the restaurant to contact you.
5. Select your preferred contact method for the restaurant to contact you (phone, email, text, or online form).
6. If you have any special requests, please describe them in the space provided.
7. Leave any additional notes for the restaurant staff to know about your reservation.
8. Choose the date and time you would like to be reserved.
9. Select the time slot for your reservation (this is likely for multiple time slots available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (1): The date you would like to make your fine dining reservation.
* **Time** (2): The time you would like your fine dining experience to take place.
* **Party Size** (3): The number of people attending your fine dining experience.
* **Phone** (4): Your phone number for the restaurant to contact you.
* **Email** (5): Your email address for the restaurant to contact you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (6): Select how you would like the restaurant to contact you: phone, email, text, or online form.
* **Special Requests** (7): Any additional requests you may have for your fine dining experience.
* **Notes** (8): Any additional information you would like to provide for the restaurant staff.
* **Reservation Date** (9): The date you would like to be reserved (this might be different from the date you plan to dine).
* **Reservation Time** (10): The time you would like to be reserved (this might be different from the time you plan to dine).
* **Reservation Time Slot** (11): The time slot you would like to be reserved within.
