## Business Mixer Organizer Registration Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information about your business mixer event. Please provide accurate and detailed responses to ensure that your event is properly promoted and managed.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill in the required fields**: Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are marked as required in the form.
2.  **Double-check your information**: Ensure that all provided details are accurate and up-to-date.
3.  **Proofread your form**: Review your form responses before submitting to avoid errors and inconsistencies.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organizer Full Name (\*):**
    \*\*Name of event organizer.
*   **Title or Position:**
    \*\* Job title or role of event organizer.
*   **Email Address (\*):**
    \*\*Primary contact email.
*   **Phone Number (\*):**
    \*\*Contact phone number.
*   **Organizing Company/Organization:**
    \*\*Name of organization hosting the mixer.
*   **Mixer Event Name (\*):**
    \*\*Name of the networking event.
*   **Event Date (\*):**
    \*\*When is the mixer scheduled?
*   **Event Start Time (\*):**
    \*\*What time does the mixer begin?
*   **Event End Time (\*):**
    \*\*When does the mixer end?
*   **Event Location (\*):**
    \*\*Physical address or venue name.
*   **Expected Number of Attendees (\*):**
    \*\*How many people do you expect?
*   **Event Format (\*):**
    \*\*What type of mixer is this?
        \*\*Select one: In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid.
*   **Target Audience (\*):**
    \*\*Who is this event for?
*   **Registration Fee (\*):**
    \*\*Will there be a registration fee? Select one: Free, Paid, Sliding Scale, or Sponsor Dependent.
*   **Fee Amount (if applicable):**
    \*\*Cost per attendee in USD.
*   **Main Agenda Topics:**
    \*\*What will be discussed?
*   **Speakers or Panelists:**
    \*\*Who will be speaking at the event?
*   **Special Requirements or Accommodations:**
    \*\*Any accessibility or special needs?
*   **Backup Contact Person:**
    \*\*Secondary contact information.
*   **Additional Information:**
    \*\*Any other relevant details?

Before submitting the form, double-check your information and ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your event administrator for assistance.
