# Project Management Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information about project management training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Project Management Training Form Title**: Enter the title of the project management training session.
2.  **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant attending the training session.
3.  **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project being managed.
4.  **Training Session**: Select 'Yes' if the participant is attending the training session or 'No' if not.
5.  **Session Date**: Enter the date of the training session. The date should be in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
6.  **Session Time**: Enter the time of the training session.
7.  **Participant Email**: Enter the email address of the participant.
8.  **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
9.  **Participant Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the participant.
10.  **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the project manager.
11.  **Project Manager**: Enter the name or title of the project manager.
12.  **Project Details**: Enter any additional details about the project.
13.  **Participant ID**: Enter the participant's ID number.
14.  **Project ID**: Enter the ID number of the project.
15.  **Participant Status**: Select the status of the participant as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
16.  **Session Timezone**: Enter the time zone of the training session.
17.  **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the training session.
18.  **Session Location**: Enter the location of the training session.
19.  **Session Time**: Enter the time of the training session.
20.  **Project Type**: Enter the type of project being managed.
21.  **Participant Location**: Enter the location of the participant.
22.  **Session Start Date**: Enter the start date of the training session.
23.  **Session End Date**: Enter the end date of the training session.
24.  **Participant Status Options**: Select the status of the participant as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
25.  **Manager Status**: Select the status of the project manager as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
