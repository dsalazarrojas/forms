# Educational Leadership Dialogue Series Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Leadership Dialogue Series registration form is a collection of fields designed to gather relevant information from attendees interested in participating in the Educational Leadership Dialogue Series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are registered for the event by submitting the form accurately.
2. Fill out all required fields: First Name, Email, Institution, Session Preferred, Date, and Time.
3. Select your preferred session options from the Session Preferred field.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the Note field if necessary.
5. Optional: Enter your Phone Number if you wish to be contacted for any last-minute updates or reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your personal name as it should be displayed on your name tag and any event materials.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your official email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
- **Institution** (`institution`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of your institution or organization.
- **Session Preferred** (`session_preferred`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Choose the sessions you are interested in attending from the provided options.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date you plan to attend the event.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, **Required**): Enter the time you plan to attend the event.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, **Optional**): Provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with event organizers.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for any last-minute updates or reminders.
