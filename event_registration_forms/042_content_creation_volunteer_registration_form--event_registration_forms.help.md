## Content Creation Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering volunteers to participate in content creation projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your volunteer details, including your full name, identification number, and email address.
2. Select all the skills and expertise you have, such as photography, videography, graphic design, copywriting, and social media management.
3. Choose your availability days and hours per week.
4. State your motivation for volunteering with this program.
5. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
6. Agree to the privacy policy regarding your data.
7. Set the date of registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Full Name** (`volunteer_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Volunteer Identification Number** (`volunteer_id`): Enter your identification number as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (`volunteer_email`): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`volunteer_phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Your Skills and Expertise** (`volunteer_skills`): Select all the skills and expertise you have, such as photography, videography, graphic design, copywriting, and social media management.
* **Availability Days** (`availability_days`): Select all the days you are available to volunteer.
* **Availability Hours per Week** (`availability_hours`): Enter the number of hours you are available to volunteer per week.
* **Why do you want to volunteer for this program?** (`motivation_for_volunteering`): Provide a brief explanation of why you want to volunteer.
* **Registration Completed?** (`registration_completed`): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data** (`privacy_agreement`): Agree to the privacy policy.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_date`): Enter the date of registration.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
