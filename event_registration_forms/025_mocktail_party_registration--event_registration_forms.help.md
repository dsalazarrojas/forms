# Mocktail Party Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mocktail Party Registration form is designed to collect information from event attendees for a mocktail party event. This form helps us to understand who is attending, their preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and other relevant details in the "Guest Details" field.
2. Enter the number of guests attending in the "Headcount" field.
3. Select your preferences in the "Guest Preferences" field.
4. Provide any additional information in the "Event Details" and "Mocktail Details" fields.
5. Review your submission and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mocktail Party Registration Form** (`mocktail_party_registration_form`, Text, Required): Enter your name and other relevant details.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional details about yourself, such as your contact information.
* **Headcount** (`headcount`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about your preferences.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, Text, Optional): Enter any confirmation message or comments.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional event details.
* **Mocktail Details** (`mocktail_details`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional mocktail details.
* **Guest Preferences** (`guest_preferences`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your preferences from the available options: No preferences, Some preferences, or All preferences.
