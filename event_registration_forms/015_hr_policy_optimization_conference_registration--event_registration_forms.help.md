# HR Policy Optimization Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Optimization Conference Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees to help us optimize our HR policies and improve our company's work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the steps below to complete this form:

1. Fill out all required fields, denoted by the "Required" label.
2. Enter your conference name and other details in the "Conference Name" field.
3. Enter your attendee details in the "Attendee Details" field.
4. Select your role in the "Role" field.
5. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
6. Choose your preferred date and time for the conference in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
7. Select your preferred tools and services in the "Consent" and "Preference" fields.
8. Enter any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
9. Finally, select your assigned tool from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Conference Name
* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the conference you are attending.

### Attendee Details
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, note, not required): Enter any relevant details about yourself as an attendee.

### Role
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, not required): Select your role at the conference (e.g. attendee, speaker, sponsor).

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Choose your preferred date for the conference.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Choose your preferred time for the conference.

### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, not required): Select any items you consent to (e.g. marketing emails, sponsor access).

### Preference
* **Preference** (`preference`, select_multiple, not required): Select any items you prefer (e.g. coffee, lunch, networking).

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments or feedback about the conference.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to you for the conference (e.g. chatjimmy).

### Output File
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, not required): Enter any output file name or description.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
