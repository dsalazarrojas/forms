# processing_technology_expo_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for the technology expo. Please fill out the form with your details to ensure accurate attendee management and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number for easy contact.
3. Enter your company name, if applicable.
4. Select your job title, role, and department.
5. Choose your job status, job type, and job category.
6. Select your industry of expertise.
7. Fill out the multiple job title fields for additional job titles.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text input field for your personal name. (Required)
* **Email**: A text input field for your email address. (Required)
* **Phone**: A text input field for your phone number. (Optional)
* **Company**: A text input field for your company name. (Optional)
* **Job Title**: A text input field for your primary job title. (Optional)
* **Role**: A text input field for your role or position. (Optional)
* **Department**: A text input field for your department or team. (Optional)
* **Job Function**: A text input field for your job function or responsibilities. (Optional)
* **Job Status**: A select_one field for your current job status. Choose from "Active" or "Inactive". (Optional)
* **Job Type**: A select_one field for your job type. Choose from "Yes" or "No". (Optional)
* **Job Category**: A select_multiple field for your job categories. Choose from "Yes" or "No". (Optional)
* **Industry**: A select_multiple field for your industry of expertise. Choose from "Yes" or "No". (Optional)
* **Job Title 2-13**: Multiple text input fields for additional job titles. (Optional)
