# Mother's Day Tea Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the Mother's Day Tea event. It includes fields for registering for the event, providing contact details, and specifying the relationship between the host/guest of honor and the guest.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Mother's Day Tea Details** (page 1) with the event information.
2. Provide your **Guest Name** (page 2), which is your name to be listed as a guest.
3. Choose your **RSVP Choice** (page 2) to indicate if you will be attending or not.
4. Enter your **Number of Guests** (page 2), if you are attending with anyone else.
5. Provide your **Email Address** (page 3), which will be used for communication about the event.
6. Enter your **Phone Number** (page 3), which will be used for emergency contact purposes.
7. Enter the **Event Date** (page 3), which is the date of the Mother's Day Tea.
8. Choose the **Event Time** (page 3), which is the time of the Mother's Day Tea.
9. Enter the **Event Location** (page 3), which is the venue of the Mother's Day Tea.
10. Write a **Message** (page 4) to the host or guest of honor, if you wish.
11. If you are the **Host** (page 4), enter your name and details.
12. If you are a **Guest** (page 4), enter the guest's name and relationship to the host or guest of honor.
13. Enter the **Guest Number** (page 4), which is the number of guests attending with you.
14. Specify the **Guest Relationship** (page 4), which is the relationship between the guest and the host or guest of honor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mother's Day Tea Details** (`mother_s_day_tea_details`, Text, Optional): Enter any specific details about the Mother's Day Tea event.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as a guest to be listed as attending the event.
* **Mother's Day Gift** (`mother_s_day_gift`, Text, Optional): Enter a gift idea for the host or guest of honor (optional).
* **RSVP Choice** (`rsvp_choice`, Select One, Optional): Choose between "True" (attending) and "False" (not attending).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_guests`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of guests attending with you (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Optional): Enter your contact email for event updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the Mother's Day Tea.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, Time, Optional): Choose the event time.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the venue of the Mother's Day Tea.
* **Message** (`message`, Note, Optional): Write a personal message to the host or guest of honor.
* **Host** (`mother_s_day_tea_invitation_form_host`, Text, Optional): If you are the host or guest of honor, enter your details.
* **Guest** (`mother_s_day_tea_invitation_form_guest`, Text, Optional): If you are a guest, enter the guest's name.
* **Guest Number** (`mother_s_day_tea_invitation_form_guest_number`, Text, Optional): Enter the number of guests attending with you.
* **Guest Relationship** (`mother_s_day_tea_invitation_form_guest_relationship`, Text, Optional): Specify the relationship between the guest and the host or guest of honor.
