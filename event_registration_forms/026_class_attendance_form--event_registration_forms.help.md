# Class Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Attendance Form is designed to track student attendance in educational settings such as classrooms, workshops, or lectures. It captures vital data to monitor student engagement and attendance patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the class name or section number to identify the class you're reporting on.
2.  Provide the name of the teacher or facilitator leading the class.
3.  Enter the date of the class.
4.  Record the start time of the class.
5.  Report the total number of students in the class.
6.  Indicate the number of students present.
7.  Report the number of students absent.
8.  If students were late, report their numbers.
9.  For absent students, provide their names, reason for absence, and whether documentation was provided.
10.  Record the class participation quality and any special notes or observations.
11.  Identify students who require follow-up for attendance, academic, or behavioral concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Class Name or Section**: Enter the name or section number of the class you're reporting on.
*  **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher or facilitator leading the class.
*  **Attendance Date**: Record the date of the class.
*  **Class Time**: Enter the start time of the class.
*  **Total Number of Students in Class**: Report the number of students expected in the class.
*  **Number of Students Present**: Enter the number of students who were present.
*  **Number of Students Absent**: Record the number of students who were absent.
*  **Number of Students Late**: If applicable, enter the number of students who arrived late.
*  **Names of Absent Students**: For absent students, list their names in a comma-separated format.
*  **Reason for Absence**: Select the reason for absence from the provided options.
*  **Documentation Provided for Absence**: Select whether documentation was provided for the absence.
*  **Attendance Percentage**: This field is automatically calculated based on your input.
*  **Class Participation Quality**: Select the overall participation quality of the class (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Not applicable).
*  **Lesson or Topics Covered**: Briefly describe the lesson or topics covered during the class.
*  **Special Notes or Observations**: Record any special notes or observations about the class.
*  **Follow-up Needed**: Identify students who require follow-up for attendance, academic, or behavioral concerns.
*  **Follow-up Details**: If follow-up is needed, specify the student(s) and the reason for follow-up.

## Tips
- Always ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to maintain reliable attendance records.
- If a student's reason for absence requires further documentation, ensure you've selected the correct option to denote that documentation was provided.
- This form is an essential tool for educators to monitor attendance and engagement.
