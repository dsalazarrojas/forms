## Responsive RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help you confirm your attendance to events. Please fill it out accurately to help us know if you can attend an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Select the event you want to RSVP to.
2. Fill in your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number (if you'd like us to reach you).
5. Choose whether you will be attending the event or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main** (`main_page`, optional): This is the main page of the form where you can select the event you want to RSVP to.
* **Name** (`name`, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be known at the event.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address, this will help us contact you if anything changes about the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): If you would like us to contact you on the phone, enter your phone number here.
* **RSVP** (`attending`, optional select_multiple): Choose whether you will be attending the event by selecting either "yes" or "no".

## Tips
* Be accurate with your RSVP, so we can better plan for the event.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out.
* This form is for confirmation of your event attendance, please make sure to submit the form to confirm your spot.
