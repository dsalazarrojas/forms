# Skating Competition Late Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skating Competition Late Entry Form is designed to allow participants or their representatives to enter or make changes to existing entries for a skating competition. It gathers necessary information for the event organizers, including participant details, event information, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Details section with the necessary information about the participant.
2. Enter the Event Details section with the information about the event.
3. Provide the Organizer's Contact Information, including their name, phone number, and email.
4. Add any additional Notes about the participant or event, if necessary.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Name** (participant_details): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Date** (participant_details_date): Enter the participant's date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Participant Time** (participant_details_time): Enter the participant's time of birth (HH:MM).
* **Participant Phone** (participant_details_phone): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Email** (participant_details_email): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Notes** (participant_details_notes): Add any additional notes about the participant.
* **Club** (participant_details_club): Enter the participant's club or team.
* **Grade** (participant_details_grade): Enter the participant's grade or skill level.
* **Level** (participant_details_level): Enter the participant's skill level.
* **Division** (participant_details_division): Enter the participant's division or category.
* **Sex** (participant_details_sex): Enter the participant's sex.

### Event Details
* **Event Name** (event_details_event_name): Enter the name of the event.
* **Organizer** (event_details_organizer): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Date** (event_details_date): Enter the date of the event (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Time** (event_details_time): Enter the time of the event (HH:MM).
* **Location** (event_details_location): Enter the location of the event.
* **Category** (event_details_category): Enter the category of the event.
* **Venue** (event_details_venue): Enter the venue of the event.

### Organizer's Contact Information
* **Name** (organizer_name): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Phone** (org_contact_phone): Enter the organizer's phone number.
* **Email** (org_contact_email): Enter the event organizer's email address.

### Additional Notes
* **Notes** (participant_details_notes): Add any additional notes about the participant or event.
