# Virtual Convention Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Convention Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees for a virtual convention. This form is used to gather essential details such as name, contact information, and event details to facilitate communication and organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you're registering for the correct convention.
3. Double-check the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter any additional information or comments about your attendance.
* **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted about the convention.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted about the convention.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the virtual convention you're registering for.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the virtual convention you're registering for.
