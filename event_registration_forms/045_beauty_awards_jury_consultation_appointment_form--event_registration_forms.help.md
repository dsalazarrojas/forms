# Beauty Awards Jury Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Beauty Awards Jury Consultation Appointment Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Jury Member Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select your professional title in the "Professional Title" field.
5. Choose your area of expertise from the "Area of Expertise" dropdown list.
6. Select your years of experience in the "Years of Industry Experience" field.
7. Indicate if you have previously participated in a jury, and if so, how many times.
8. Choose your preferred date for the consultation in the "Preferred Consultation Date" field.
9. Select your preferred time for the consultation in the "Preferred Time" field.
10. If you have any alternative dates, choose them in the "Alternate Date" field (optional).
11. Select your preferred consultation format (in-person, video call, phone call, or no preference).
12. Select your general availability for the consultation (weekday mornings, afternoons, evenings, weekend mornings, afternoons, or any other time).
13. Check the categories you are interested in judging from the list (select multiple).
14. If you have any dietary requirements, please mention them in the "Dietary Requirements" field.
15. If you have any accessibility needs, please mention them in the "Accessibility Needs" field.
16. Indicate if you require travel information (true or false).
17. Finally, add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Jury Member Name**: Enter your full name to be recognized as a jury member.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Professional Title**: Choose your current professional title.
* **Area of Expertise**: Select one area where you have expertise from the given options.
* **Years of Industry Experience**: Choose your years of experience in the beauty industry.
* **Previous Jury Experience**: Indicate if you have previously participated in a jury.
* **Preferred Consultation Date**: Select your preferred date for the consultation.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose your preferred time for the consultation.
* **Alternate Date**: Choose an alternative date, if available (optional).
* **Consultation Format**: Select your preferred consultation format.
* **General Availability**: Select all the days and times you are available for the consultation.
* **Categories Interested In Judging**: Select all the categories you are interested in judging from the list.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Mention any dietary requirements if applicable.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Mention any accessibility needs if applicable.
* **Travel Required**: Indicate if you require travel information.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information.
