## speed-networking-with-experts-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for a speed networking event with experts. The event organizers can use this form to collect details and preferences from attendees, including their names, email addresses, and preferred meeting times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Event Details field.
2.  Enter your Attendee Name.
3.  Write down your Goal Questions.
4.  Select your Preferred Times.
5.  Enter your Attendee E-mail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the event.
* **Attendee Name** (`Attendee Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the event materials.
* **Goal Questions** (`Goal Questions`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current projects, interests, or goals you'd like to discuss with the experts.
* **Preferred Times** (`Preferred Times`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the times of day that you are available to have a meeting. You can choose one or more times from the list.
* **Attendee E-mail** (`Attendee E-mail`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so the event organizers can contact you with further instructions and event information.
