# Art Exhibit Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Exhibit Waiver Form is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions for the submission and exhibition of art pieces. It ensures that artists understand their rights and responsibilities as creators, and that galleries understand their obligations as exhibitors. This form is designed to protect both the artist and the gallery from potential disputes or liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Enter your address and date of birth.
3.  Provide details about the exhibition, including the name, dates, and location.
4.  Describe your artwork, including its title, medium, dimensions, and estimated value.
5.  Confirm your ownership and copyright of the artwork.
6.  Grant permission for the artwork to be displayed and photographed.
7.  Indicate whether you are offering the artwork for sale.
8.  If the artwork is for sale, enter the sale price.
9.  Confirm that you understand the gallery's liability waiver.
10.  Provide information about your insurance coverage for the artwork.
11.  Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
12.  Enter the expected pickup date after the exhibition.
13.  Confirm that you accept responsibility for timely pickup.
14.  Sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Full Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the artist.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Artist Address** (`artist_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Date of Birth** (`artist_date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you wish to provide your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Exhibition Name** (`exhibit_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the exhibition.
* **Gallery or Venue Name** (`gallery_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the gallery or venue.
* **Exhibition Dates** (`exhibit_dates`, text, required): Enter the start and end dates of the exhibition.
* **Artwork Title** (`artwork_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your artwork.
* **Artwork Medium** (`artwork_medium`, text, required): Enter the medium or material used to create your artwork.
* **Artwork Dimensions** (`artwork_dimensions`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the dimensions of your artwork.
* **Artwork Estimated Value** (`artwork_value`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the estimated value of your artwork.
* **Artwork Description** (`artwork_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your artwork.
* **Artist Acknowledgments and Waivers** (`ownership_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are the original artist and owner of the artwork.
* **Copyright Ownership** (`copyright_ownership`, select_one, required): Indicate that you retain copyright ownership of your artwork.
* **Permission for Display** (`permission_display`, select_one, required): Grant permission for your artwork to be displayed.
* **Permission for Photography** (`permission_photography`, select_one, required): Grant permission for your artwork to be photographed.
* **Permission for Sale** (`permission_sale`, select_one, required): Indicate whether your artwork is available for sale.
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`, number, optional): If the artwork is for sale, enter the sale price.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the gallery's liability waiver.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have insurance coverage for the artwork.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`section_terms`, note, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to all terms and conditions.
* **Expected Pickup Date After Exhibition** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Enter the expected pickup date after the exhibition.
* **Responsibility for Timely Pickup** (`storage_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm that you accept responsibility for timely pickup.
* **Agreement to Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to all terms and conditions.
* **Date of Agreement** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this agreement.
* **Additional Notes or Special Conditions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or special conditions.
