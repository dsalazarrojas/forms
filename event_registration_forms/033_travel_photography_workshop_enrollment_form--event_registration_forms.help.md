# Travel Photography Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for participants to enroll in a travel photography workshop, providing essential details to help us prepare for your presence and needs during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please provide the required information in the corresponding fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For each field, follow the specific guidelines and instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the workshop.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding workshop updates and information.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, not required): Provide your phone number for any additional communication.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_note`, note, not required): If you have any personal notes or preferences, feel free to add them here.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, not required): Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, not required): Choose the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, not required): Enter the address of the workshop location.
* **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, select_multiple, not required): Check the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes: You have specific preferences for the workshop.
	+ No: You do not have specific preferences.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, select_multiple, not required): Check the options that apply to the workshop:
	+ Yes: You have specific preferences for the workshop.
	+ No: You do not have specific preferences.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_multiple, not required): Check the options that apply to the workshop:
	+ Yes: You have specific preferences for the workshop type.
	+ No: You do not have specific preferences.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, select_one, not required): Choose the duration of the workshop:
	+ Yes: The workshop will last for the specified duration.
	+ No: The workshop will not last for the specified duration.
* **Workshop Level** (`workshop_level`, select_one, not required): Choose the level of the workshop:
	+ High: The workshop is for experienced participants.
	+ Low: The workshop is for beginners.
* **Workshop Price** (`workshop_price`, number, not required): Enter the price of the workshop.
* **Workshop Price Currency** (`workshop_price_currency`, select_one, not required): Choose the currency of the workshop price:
	+ Yes: The price is in the currency of the workshop.
	+ No: The price is not in the currency of the workshop.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_one, not required): Check your agreement to the workshop terms:
	+ Yes: You agree with the workshop terms.
	+ No: You do not agree with the workshop terms.
* **Workshop Agreement** (`workshop_agreement`, select_one, not required): Check your agreement to the workshop terms:
	+ Yes: You agree with the workshop terms.
	+ No: You do not agree with the workshop terms.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Choose the payment method:
	+ Yes: You will use the specified payment method.
	+ No: You will not use the specified payment method.
* **Participant Attending** (`participant_attending`, select_multiple, not required): Check who will be attending:
	+ Yes: The participant and others will be attending.
	+ No: Only the participant will be attending.
* **Workshop Attending** (`workshop_attending`, select_multiple, not required): Check who will be attending:
	+ Yes: Others besides the participant will be attending.
	+ No: Only the participant will be attending.
* **Workshop Notes** (`workshop_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional workshop notes.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, not required): Add any personal notes for yourself.
* **Workshop Agenda** (`workshop_agenda`, text, not required): Enter the workshop agenda.
* **Participant Agenda** (`participant_agenda`, text, not required): Enter your personal agenda.
* **Workshop Agenda Notes** (`workshop_agenda_notes`, text, not required): Add any notes for the workshop agenda.
* **Participant Photo URL** (`participant_photo_url`, text, not required): Enter a URL for your profile photo.

Note: Required fields are those marked with an asterisk (\*) but none are present here, so you may complete the form as needed and according to your preferences.
