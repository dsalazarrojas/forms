# Douro River Exploration Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Douro River Exploration event. It collects information about the trip date, tour operator, participant details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Trip Date** in the date format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
2.  Enter the name of the **Tour Operator** who will be leading the tour (if any).
3.  In the **Participant Details** field, enter a brief description or any relevant information about the participant.
4.  Enter the **Participant Name**, which should include their first and last name.
5.  Enter the participant's **Participant Email** in the correct email format (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
6.  Enter the participant's **Participant Phone** number.
7.  Enter the participant's **Participant Address**, which should include their street address.
8.  Enter the **Tour Start Date** in the date format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
9.  Enter the **Tour Duration** in days.
10. Enter the **Number of Participants** who will be joining the tour.
11. If you are not using a tour operator, select **None** for **Tour Operator Information**. If you are using a tour operator, select **Other** and enter their name and contact information in the **Emergency Contact Info** field.
12. In the **Emergency Contact** field, enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
13. In the **Emergency Contact Info** field, enter any additional information about the emergency contact, such as their relationship to the participant or their contact details.
14. Finally, select either **Yes** or **No** for **Participant Acceptance of Terms**. This indicates that you have read and understood our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Date** (1) (Required): Enter the date for the tour.
* **Tour Operator** (2) (Optional): Enter the name of the tour operator leading the tour.
* **Participant Details** (3) (Required): Enter any relevant information about the participant.
* **Participant Name** (4) (Required): Enter the participant's name, including first and last name.
* **Participant Email** (5) (Required): Enter the participant's email address in the correct format (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
* **Participant Phone** (6) (Required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (7) (Required): Enter the participant's street address.
* **Tour Start Date** (8) (Required): Enter the date the tour will start.
* **Tour Duration** (9) (Required): Enter the duration of the tour in days.
* **Number of Participants** (10) (Required): Enter the number of participants joining the tour.
* **Tour Operator Information** (11) (Required): Select the tour operator or indicate you are not using one.
* **Emergency Contact** (12) (Required): Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (13) (Required): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Participant Acceptance of Terms** (14) (Optional): Select whether you have read and understood our terms and conditions.
