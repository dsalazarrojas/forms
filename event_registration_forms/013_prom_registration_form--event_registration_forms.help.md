# Prom Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prom Registration Form is used to collect registration details from attendees for a prom registration event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information in the form, which includes:
	* **Prom Registration Form (Text Field 1)**: Enter your name as you want it to be displayed on the prom program.
	* **Prom Details (Text Field 2)**: Provide a brief description of your plans or expectations for the prom night.
2. Select your preferences for meals and guests for the event:
	* **Meal Preferences (Select Multiple 3)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each meal option to indicate your preferences.
	* **Guests (Select Multiple 4)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each guest option to indicate how many guests you'll be bringing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prom Registration Form (Text Field 1)**:
	+ **Name**: This is where you enter your name as you want it to be displayed on the prom program.
	+ **Type**: Text field.
	+ **Required**: Yes.
* **Prom Details (Text Field 2)**:
	+ **Name**: This is where you provide a brief description of your plans or expectations for the prom night.
	+ **Type**: Text field.
	+ **Required**: No.
* **Meal Preferences (Select Multiple 3)**:
	+ **Name**: Choose your preferences for each meal option.
	+ **Type**: Select multiple options.
	+ **Required**: No.
* **Guests (Select Multiple 4)**:
	+ **Name**: Choose whether you'll be bringing any guests.
	+ **Type**: Select multiple options.
	+ **Required**: No.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your name correctly as it will be displayed on the prom program.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask event organizers.
* Complete the form carefully to ensure accurate information is collected.
