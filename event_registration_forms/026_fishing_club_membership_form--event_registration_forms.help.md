# Fishing Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishing Club Membership Form is designed to collect personal details and preferences of individuals interested in joining the club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at <form@gic.mx>.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Details (Page 1)
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, required/optional): This section is where you provide your personal information, such as your name and contact details.

### Membership Preferences (Page 2)
* **Membership Preferences** (`membership_preferences`, `text`, required/optional): In this section, you can indicate your membership preferences, such as your preferred membership type (e.g., individual, family, or corporate).

### Payment Information (Page 3)
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, required/optional): Here, you will need to provide your payment information, including any relevant payment details.

### Note (Page 4)
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): This is an open field for any additional comments or notes you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
