# Win A Beauty Treatment Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Win A Beauty Treatment Contest Entry Form is designed for customers to submit their entries for a beauty treatment contest. The form collects essential information to validate and process their entries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These include:
	* salon_name
	* entry_title
	* entry_date
	* customer_name
	* customer_email
	* customer_address
	* salon_address
	* salon_phone
	* entry_type
	* entry_terms_agreed
2. For optional fields, you can provide additional information if you wish. These include:
	* entry_time
	* customer_phone
	* salon_name_hint
	* entry_details
	* salon_license
	* entry_media
	* entry_terms_agreed_label
	* entry_terms_agreed_label_hint
3. Make sure to agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" for entry_terms_agreed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **salon_name** (\*): Enter the name of the salon where you received the treatment.
* **entry_title** (\*): Provide a brief title for your entry.
* **entry_date** (\*): Enter the date of the treatment.
* **entry_time**: If you have more details about the time of the treatment, please enter them here.
* **customer_name** (\*): Enter your name as the customer who received the treatment.
* **customer_email** (\*): Enter your email address.
* **customer_phone**: If you have a contact number, enter it here.
* **salon_phone** (\*): Enter the salon's contact number.
* **salon_name_hint**: If you have any additional information about the salon, enter it here.
* **entry_details**: If you have more details about the entry, please enter them here.
* **customer_address** (\*): Enter your address.
* **salon_address** (\*): Enter the salon's address.
* **salon_license**: If you have a salon license number, enter it here.
* **entry_type** (\*): Select "Yes" if you are entering for the contest.
* **entry_media**: If you have any media attached to your entry, upload it here.
* **entry_terms_agreed** (\*): Select "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **entry_terms_agreed_label**: If you have any comments or questions about the terms and conditions, enter them here.
* **entry_terms_agreed_label_hint**: This is a hint for the above field, not to be filled.

Tips:
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your entry is complete and valid.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [support email].
