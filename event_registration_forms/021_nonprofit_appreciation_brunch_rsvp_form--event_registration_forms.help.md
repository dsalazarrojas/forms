**Nonprofit Appreciation Brunch RSVP Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to collect RSVPs from guests for the Nonprofit Appreciation Brunch. The form will ask for the guest's name, email, phone number (optional), and RSVP status, which determines whether they are attending or not. You will also have the option to add any comments about your attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as a guest in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Select whether you will be attending or not in the "RSVP Status" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Guest Email" field, if applicable.
4. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "Guest Phone" field.
5. Provide any additional comments about your attendance in the "Comments" field, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: This is the name of the person attending the brunch. Please enter the name of the guest as it should be displayed on the invitation.
* **Guest Email**: This is the email address of the guest. Please enter the email address of the person attending, so we can keep them informed about any changes or updates.
* **Guest Phone**: This is an optional field for the guest's phone number. If you have any questions or need to be reached, please enter your phone number.
* **RSVP Status**: Select whether you will be attending or not by choosing from the options "Attending" or "Not Attending". This will help us determine the headcount for the event.
* **RSVP Status Note**: This is an optional field for you to provide a brief note about your attendance. This can include information such as dietary restrictions, seat preferences, or any other details you would like to share.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or questions you might have about the event. Feel free to share anything you would like, and we will do our best to address it.
