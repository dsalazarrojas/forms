# Craft Fair Vendor Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential vendors who want to participate in the craft fair. This information will help us understand their vendor identity, contact details, and product/service offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter your business or vendor name correctly.
3. Enter your contact person's name, email, and phone number for us to reach out to you.
4. Provide a brief description of your products or services.
5. Answer any other required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name <vendor_name>** (`Text`, optional): Enter your business or vendor name.
* **Business Name <business_name>** (`Text`, optional): Enter your company or business name.
* **Contact Name <contact_name>** (`Text`, optional): Enter the name of the person we should contact for more information.
* **Email <email>** (`Email`, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone <phone>** (`Text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Vendor Category <vendor_category>** (`Text`, optional): If you have a specific category for your products or services, enter it here.
* **Booth Details <booth_details>** (`Text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your booth or display.
* **Product Description <product_description>** (`Text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your products or services.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or needs, feel free to include them in the "Booth Details" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
