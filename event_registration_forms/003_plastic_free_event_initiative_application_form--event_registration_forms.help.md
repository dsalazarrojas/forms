# Plastic Free Event Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for events that align with the "Plastic Free Initiative" goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please make sure you have all the required information ready. The form has been divided into sections for easier navigation:
- **Page 1: Event Information**
    - **Event Name**: Enter the name of your event.
    - **Event Date**: Select the date of your event.
    - **Event Time**: Select the time of your event.
    - **Event Location**: Select whether your event is in a location where plastic usage is encouraged or not.
    - **Event Type**: Select whether your event is aligned with the initiative's goals and objectives.
- **Page 2: Contact Information**
    - **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your event.
    - **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the person responsible for your event.
    - **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your event.
- **Page 3: Event Details**
    - **Event Size**: Select the expected number of attendees for your event.
    - **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of your event.
    - **Event Image**: Upload an image related to your event (not required).
    - **Event Video**: Upload a video related to your event (not required).
    - **Event Documents**: Upload any relevant documents related to your event (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name (Page 1)**: Enter a clear and concise title for your event.
  * Required field.
  * Example: "Annual Plastic-Free Beach Clean-Up"
- **Event Date (Page 1)**: Select the date of your event using the date picker or enter the date manually in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
  * Required field.
  * Example: 2024-07-22
- **Event Time (Page 1)**: Select the time of your event using the time picker or enter the time manually in the format HH:MM.
  * Required field.
  * Example: 14:00
- **Event Location (Page 1)**: Select 'Yes' if your event is held in a plastic-free location, 'No' otherwise.
  * Required field.
  * Example: 'Yes'
- **Event Type (Page 1)**: Select 'Yes' if your event aligns with the initiative's goals and objectives, 'No' otherwise.
  * Required field.
  * Example: 'Yes'
- **Contact Person (Page 2)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your event.
  * Required field.
  * Example: John Doe
- **Contact Email (Page 2)**: Enter the email of the person responsible for your event.
  * Required field.
  * Example: johndoe@example.com
- **Contact Phone (Page 2)**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your event.
  * Required field.
  * Example: 123-456-7890
- **Event Size (Page 3)**: Select the expected number of attendees for your event.
  * Select 'Yes' for events that align with the initiative's goals and objectives.
  * Example: 'Yes'
- **Event Description (Page 3)**: Enter a brief description of your event, including any relevant details.
  * Not required but recommended.
  * Example: "Join us for a beach clean-up event to help keep our coastlines free of plastic waste."
- **Event Image (Page 3)**: Upload an image related to your event, if applicable.
  * Not required but recommended.
  * Example: A photo of a beach clean-up event in progress.
- **Event Video (Page 3)**: Upload a video related to your event, if applicable.
  * Not required but recommended.
  * Example: A video showcasing the importance of reducing plastic usage.
- **Event Documents (Page 3)**: Upload any relevant documents related to your event, if applicable.
  * Not required but recommended.
  * Example: A list of event rules and guidelines for attendees.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure that your event aligns with the initiative's goals and objectives.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact the initiative's staff for assistance.
- The initiative reserves the right to review and approve events before they are listed on their website or other platforms.
