# Ice Festival Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ice Festival Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from attendees of the Ice Festival. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in all required fields. If a field is marked as "optional", you can skip it if you wish.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Ice Festival Feedback Survey**
	* This is the title of the form, which is already filled in for your convenience.
### 2. **name**
	* Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
### 3. **email**
	* Enter your email address so we can get back to you with any updates or follow-ups.
### 4. **feedback**
	* Share your thoughts, opinions, or comments about the Ice Festival event. Be specific about what you liked and disliked.
### 5. **rating**
	* Give us your rating of the event, from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
### 6. **suggestions**
	* If you have any suggestions for future Ice Festivals, this is the place to write them down.
### 7. **agree**
	* Choose how you feel about the following: 
		+ Ice Festival: 
			+ Strongly agree
			+ Somewhat agree
			+ No opinion
			+ Disagree
			+ Strongly disagree
	* Please select the answer that best reflects your feelings.
### 8. **agree_2**
	* Choose all that apply for the following:
		+ Ice Festival
		+ False
		+ No opinion

Please note that field 7 and 8 have similar labels, but different types. Field 7 is a select_one type, where you can choose only one answer, while field 8 is a select_multiple type, where you can select multiple answers.
