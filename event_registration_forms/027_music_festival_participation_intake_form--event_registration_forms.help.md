# Music Festival Participation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register an individual's interest in participating in a music festival event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information and submit. If you are not sure which role you would like to register under, you can choose either attendee, vendor, or performer. You will also be asked to provide your name, email, and phone number. These fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you would like to participate in.
* **Role**: Select the role you would like to register under. This could be attendee, vendor, or performer.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the event. This field is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the event. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the event name to ensure you are registering for the correct event.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us and we will be happy to assist.
