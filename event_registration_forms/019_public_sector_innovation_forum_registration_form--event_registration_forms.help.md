# public_sector_innovation_forum_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The public sector innovation forum registration form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending the forum. This includes their name, contact information, organization, role, department, event attendance, date of interest, and any additional information they may want to provide.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your name in the **Name** field.
2.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3.  If you have a phone number, please enter it in the **Phone** field.
4.  If you are registered with an organization, please enter your organization's name in the **Organization** field.
5.  Select your role in the **Role** field from the available options.
6.  If you are affiliated with any of the departments listed, please select all applicable options in the **Department** field.
7.  Indicate your event attendance in the **Event Attendance** field.
8.  Enter the date you are most interested in attending in the **Date of Interest** field.
9.  Finally, provide any additional information about your interest in the forum or any special requirements you may have in the **Additional Information** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Name
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): Your name as you would like to be addressed.

### Email
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Your email address for communication purposes.

### Phone
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Your phone number for follow-up communications.

### Organization
*   **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, not required): The name of your organization if you are registered with one.

### Role
*   **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, not required): Choose your role from the options provided:
    *   Government employee
    *   Organization representative
    *   Journalist/Press
    *   Other

### Department
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose all applicable departments you are affiliated with:
    *   Information and Communications Technology (ICT)
    *   Other

### Event Attendance
*   **Event Attendance** (`event_attendance`, `time`, not required): Indicate whether you will be able to attend the event.
*   Note: This field likely refers to the start and end time of the event and your expected time of attendance.

### Date of Interest
*   **Date of Interest** (`date_of_interest`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you are most interested in attending the event.

### Additional Information
*   **Additional Information** (`note`, `note`, not required): Provide any additional information about your interest in the event or any special requirements you may have.
