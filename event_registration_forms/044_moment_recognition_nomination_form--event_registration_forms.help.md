# Moment Recognition Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moment Recognition Nomination Form is used to submit nominations for the Moment Recognition Program. This program aims to recognize and celebrate individuals or teams that have made significant contributions to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information as the submitter.
2. Provide the nominee's name and role.
3. Choose the award name and category for the nominee.
4. Enter the date and time of submission.
5. Optionally, provide the nominee's email and phone number.
6. Explain the reason for the nomination.
7. Add any additional comments about the nominee.
8. Choose who submitted the nomination (you can remain anonymous).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter (1)**: Enter your name and contact information as the submitter.
* **Nominee Name (2)**: Fill in the name of the person or team being nominated.
* **Role (3)**: Choose the role of the person or team being nominated (Nomination for Team or Nomination for Individual).
* **Award Name (4)**: Select the name of the award for which the nominee is being recognized.
* **Award Category (5)**: Choose the category for which the award is given (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
* **Nomination Date (6)**: Enter the date on which the nomination is being submitted.
* **Nominee Email (7)**: If the nominee has a valid email address, enter it here. This is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Nominee Phone (8)**: If the nominee has a phone number, enter it here. This is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Nomination Reason (9)**: Provide a brief explanation of why this nominee deserves recognition.
* **Additional Comments (10)**: Any additional information that you would like to share about the nominee.
* **Submit Date (11)**: Enter the date on which the nomination is being submitted.
* **Submit Time (12)**: Enter the time on which the nomination is being submitted.
* **Submitted By (13)**: Choose who submitted the nomination (you can remain anonymous).
