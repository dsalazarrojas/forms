# Conference Agenda Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting updates to the conference agenda. If you would like to make changes to the current agenda, please fill out this form with the necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to include all required fields.
3. If you have supporting documents, you can upload them or list them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, **text**, required): Please enter your name as the person requesting the revision.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, **email**, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you to confirm and update you on the status of your request.
* **Original item title** (`original_item_title`, **text**, required): Enter the title of the agenda item you want to revise.
* **Proposed revision details** (`proposed_revision`, **text**, required): Describe the changes you want to make to the agenda item.
* **Reason for revision** (`reason_for_revision`, **text**, required): Explain why these changes are necessary.
* **Priority level** (`priority_level`, **select_one, optional): Indicate how urgently you need the revision to be made. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Affected sessions** (`affected_sessions`, **text, optional): If other sessions will be impacted by the revision, list them here.
* **Supporting documents reference** (`supporting_documents`, **text, optional): If you have documents that support your request, you can upload or list them here.
* **Editor notes** (`editor_notes`, **text, optional): Add any internal notes or comments that the editors can use while reviewing your request.
* **Request status** (`request_status`, **select_one, optional): Select the current status of your request from Submitted, Under review, Approved, or Rejected.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at contact email.
- We will review and update you on the status of your request.
- If your request is approved, we will update the agenda accordingly.
