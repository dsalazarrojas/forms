# Wedding Coordinator Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Coordinator Onboarding Form is used to gather essential details from wedding clients to ensure smooth wedding planning and execution. Please fill out this form carefully to provide the necessary information for your wedding coordination needs.

## How To Complete This Form

- Ensure you have all the required details before filling out the form.
- Select the correct options for each field based on the provided information.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-specific explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client Name** (`client_name`, select_multiple, required): Select the name(s) of the client(s) for whom you are planning the wedding.
  - This field is required as we need to understand who is hiring us.
  - Please select only the name(s) of the client(s).

- **Wedding Theme** (`wedding_theme`, select_one, required): Choose the theme that best describes the client's wedding style.
  - Select one option from Modern, Classic, or Rustic to give us an idea of the client's taste.
  - This will help us to provide more personalized services.

- **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the wedding.
  - Please enter a numerical value.
  - This will help us to plan for sufficient resources and services.

- **Budget** (`budget`, select_one, required): Choose the budget range that the client is willing to spend on the event.
  - Select one option from Under 1000, 1000-3000, 3000-5000, 5000-10000, or Above 10000.
  - This will help us to tailor our services accordingly.

- **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the wedding, including the month and year.
  - Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - This will help us to check the availability of venue and other services.

- **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the wedding.
  - Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
  - This will help us to plan the timeline of the event.

- **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event that the client is planning.
  - Choose from Wedding, Corporate Event, or Birthday Party to help us understand the nature of the event.

- **Venue Preferences** (`venue_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any specific preferences for the venue.
  - If you have any specific venue requirements or preferences, please mention them here.
  - This will help us to find the most suitable venue for the client.

- **Catering Preferences** (`catering_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any specific preferences for catering services.
  - If you have any specific catering requirements or preferences, please mention them here.
  - This will help us to provide the best catering services for the client.

- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements for the event.
  - If you have any specific requirements or requests, please mention them here.
  - This will help us to accommodate your needs.

- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the client.
  - This will help us to contact the client and provide regular updates.
  - Please ensure this email is valid and active.

- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the client.
  - This will help us to contact the client and discuss any last-minute details.
  - Please ensure this is a valid phone number.

- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional details or comments about the client or event.
  - This will help us to understand your needs and provide better services.
  - Please ensure this is kept concise and relevant.
