# Event Ticket Sales Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and monitor information about event ticket sales.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure you have permission to access the event's ticket sales data.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Name** (event_name, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Ticket Type** (ticket_type, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the event offers ticket sales.
* **Ticket Price** (ticket_price, number, required): Enter the price of a single ticket.
* **Ticket Sales** (ticket_sales, number, required): Enter the total number of ticket sales.
* **Attendee Information** (attendee_info, note, required): Enter any additional information about the attendees.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Attendee Email** (attendee_email, email, required): Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Event Status** (event_status, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the event, whether it's 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Ticket Availability** (ticket_availability, select_multiple, required): Choose whether ticket sales are 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
* Always double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure you have permission to access the event's ticket sales data.
* Use the form for its intended purpose only, i.e., to monitor and manage event ticket sales.
