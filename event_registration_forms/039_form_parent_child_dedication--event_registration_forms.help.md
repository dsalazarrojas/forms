# Form Parent Child Dedication - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Form Parent Child Dedication" is designed to collect information for child dedication purposes. It helps us understand the details of the child and their parents, ensuring accurate and relevant data for our processes.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Review all fields to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as prompted.
* Double-check your entries before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration Form Data** (`event_registration_form_data`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about the event registration.
* **Dedication Date** (`dedication_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the child's dedication ceremony.
* **Dedication Time** (`dedication_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the child’s dedication ceremony.
* **Child First Name** (`child_first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the child's first name.
* **Child Last Name** (`child_last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the child’s last name.
* **Child Date Of Birth** (`child_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter the child’s date of birth.
* **Child Dedicated To** (`child_dedicated_to`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional information about who the child is dedicated to.
* **Parents First Name** (`parents_first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the parents' first name(s).
* **Parents Last Name** (`parents_last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the parents’ last name(s).
* **Parents Phone Number** (`parents_phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the parents' contact phone number.
* **Dedicated By** (`dedicated_by`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional information about who is dedicating the child.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification.
