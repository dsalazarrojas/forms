# nutrition_workshop_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect event registration information for the nutrition workshop. Fill out this form to provide essential details about yourself and your participation in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields with your full name.
2. Enter your "Phone" number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX or XXX.XXX.XXXX.
3. Enter a valid "Email" address.
4. Select the workshop "Date" you plan to attend.
5. Choose your "Registration Time" for the workshop.
6. Enter the date you plan to RSVP.
7. Select your "RSVP Time" for the workshop.
8. If you have any dietary restrictions, type them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
9. If you have any special requests, type them in the "Special Requests" field.
10. Optionally, provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX or XXX.XXX.XXXX.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, optional): Choose your registration time for the workshop.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to RSVP to the workshop.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, optional): Select your RSVP time for the workshop.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, type them in this field.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests, type them in this field.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Optionally, provide any additional comments about your attendance in this field.
