# Trade Fair Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Fair Application Form is designed for businesses to register for the trade fair. It collects business and contact information, product details, and other relevant data to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Business Information" section with your company's details.
2. Provide product information, including product name, description, price, and image.
3. Fill out the "Contact Information" section with your contact person's details.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields to ensure successful submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Information (1)** (text, required: false): Enter your company's name, address, and other business details.
* **Product Details (2)** (text, required: false): Describe your product, including name, description, price, and image.
* **Contact Information (3)** (text, required: false): Enter your contact person's name, email, phone, and other contact details.
* **Company Details (4)** (text, required: false): Enter your company's details, such as name, website, and phone number.
* **Company Name (5)** (text, required: false): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Website (6)** (text, required: false): Enter your company's website URL.
* **Business Email (7)** (email, required: false): Enter your business email address.
* **Business Phone (8)** (number, required: false): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Address (9)** (text, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **Business City (10)** (text, required: false): Enter your business city.
* **Business Post Code (11)** (text, required: false): Enter your business post code (zip code).
* **Business State (12)** (text, required: false): Enter your business state or region.
* **Business Country (13)** (text, required: false): Enter your business country.
* **Business Product (14)** (text, required: false): Describe your business product or service.
* **Product Name (15)** (text, required: false): Enter your product's name.
* **Product Description (16)** (text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of your product.
* **Product Price (17)** (number, required: false): Enter your product's price.
* **Product Image (18)** (text, required: false): Upload or enter your product's image URL.
* **Contact First Name (19)** (text, required: false): Enter your contact person's first name.
* **Contact Last Name (20)** (text, required: false): Enter your contact person's last name.
* **Contact Email (21)** (email, required: false): Enter your contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone (22)** (number, required: false): Enter your contact person's phone number.
* **Social Security Number (23)** (text, required: false): Enter your contact person's social security number (if applicable).
* **Business License (24)** (text, required: false): Enter your business license number (if applicable).
* **Application Date (25)** (date, required: false): Enter the date you applied for the trade fair.
