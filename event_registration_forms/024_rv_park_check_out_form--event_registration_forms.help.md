# rv_park_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the rv_park_check_out_form is to collect information from guests checking out of the RV park. This form is used to gather the date of check out, any comments from the park manager, guest feedback, guest signature, and the final balance due.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the check out date in the "Check Out Date" field. This is the date when the guest is checking out of the RV park. Please use the mm/dd/yyyy format.
2. In the "Comments" field, enter any comments from the park manager regarding the guest's stay.
3. In the "Guest Feedback" field, enter any feedback or comments from the guest.
4. In the "Guest Signature" field, enter your signature as the guest. This is to confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the final balance due.
5. In the "Final Balance" field, enter the final balance due after your stay at the RV park.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check Out Date** (check_out_date, date, required: false): Enter the date you are checking out of the RV park.
* **Comments** (park_manager_comments, text, required: false): Enter any comments from the park manager regarding your stay.
* **Guest Feedback** (guest_feedback, text, required: false): Enter any feedback or comments from you as the guest.
* **Guest Signature** (guest_signature, text, required: false): Enter your signature to confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the final balance due.
* **Final Balance** (final_balance, number, required: false): Enter the final balance due after your stay at the RV park.
