# event-planning-checklist-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information for planning and executing events. It helps ensure that all necessary tasks and considerations are covered, making it easier to manage event logistics, budget, and communication.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and fill out the required fields. Some pages, like "venue-and-entertainment", have options to select from. Choose the most suitable option for your event.
2. Enter relevant details for each field, such as dates, budgets, and notes.
3. Make sure to review and complete all pages before submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title** (`event-planning-checklist-form`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which should be kept consistent with your event name.
* **event-planning-checklist** (`planning_checklist_page`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your event planning process.
* **venue-and-entertainment** (`venue_and_entertainment_page`, required): Choose the type of venue and entertainment that suits your event from the provided options.
* **event-schedule** (`event_schedule_page`, required/optional): Enter the start and end dates of your event.
* **event-budget** (`event_budget_page`, required/optional): Enter the budget for your event.
* **event-logistics** (`event_logistics_page`, required/optional): Enter notes about event logistics, such as catering, transportation, or parking.
* **event-communication** (`event_communication_page`, required/optional): Enter the email address for event communication.
* **event-evaluation** (`event_evaluation_page`, required/optional): Select the options that best represent your event evaluation.
* **notes** (`notes_page`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your event.
* **attachments** (`attachments_page`, required/optional): Upload any relevant attachments, such as contracts or invoices.
* **event-status** (`event_status_page`, required/optional): Enter the status of your event.
* **form-submitter** (`form_submitter_page`, required/optional): Enter your name and email address as the person submitting the form.

This help guide will assist event organizers and planners in filling out the form correctly.
