# Speaker Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect speaker information for events, meetings, or conferences. It will be used to create a speaker list, which can be displayed on event schedules, promotional materials, and other event-related documents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the speaker's first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in the speaker's last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter the speaker's email address in the "speaker_email" field.
4. Enter the speaker's phone number in the "speaker_phone" field.
5. Enter the speaker's job title in the "job_title" field.
6. Enter a brief biography of the speaker in the "speaker_biography" field.
7. Enter a photo of the speaker in the "speaker_photo" field.
8. Enter a link to the speaker's video presentation in the "speaker_video_link" field.
9. Enter a link to the speaker's Facebook profile in the "speaker_facebook_link" field.
10. Enter a link to the speaker's Twitter profile in the "speaker_twitter_link" field.
11. Enter a link to the speaker's Instagram profile in the "speaker_instagram_link" field.
12. Enter a link to the speaker's LinkedIn profile in the "speaker_linkedin_link" field.
13. Enter the speaker's website in the "speaker_website" field.
14. Enter the speaker's address in the "speaker_address" field.
15. Enter the speaker's city in the "speaker_city" field.
16. Enter the speaker's province in the "speaker_province" field.
17. Enter the speaker's country in the "speaker_country" field.
18. Enter the speaker's postal code in the "speaker_postal" field.
19. Enter the speaker's phone number in the "phone" field (note: this field should not be filled in if "speaker_phone" field is already filled in).
20. Enter the speaker's native language in the "speaker_native_language" field.
21. Enter any other language the speaker can speak in the "other_language" field.
22. Enter the name of the other language in the "other_language_other" field.
23. Enter the level of English speaking ability of the other language in the "other_english" field.
24. Enter any additional comments about the speaker in the "additional_comments" field.
25. Upload any additional files related to the speaker in the "additional_files" field.
26. Enter the type of the additional files in the "additional_files_type" field.
27-32. Enter any additional files 2-5 in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text, Required): Enter the speaker's first name.
* **last_name** (Text, Required): Enter the speaker's last name.
* **speaker_email** (Text, Required): Enter the speaker's email address.
* **speaker_phone** (Text, Required): Enter the speaker's phone number.
* **job_title** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's job title.
* **speaker_biography** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief biography of the speaker.
* **speaker_photo** (Text, Optional): Enter a photo of the speaker.
* **speaker_video_link** (Text, Optional): Enter a link to the speaker's video presentation.
* **speaker_facebook_link** (Text, Optional): Enter a link to the speaker's Facebook profile.
* **speaker_twitter_link** (Text, Optional): Enter a link to the speaker's Twitter profile.
* **speaker_instagram_link** (Text, Optional): Enter a link to the speaker's Instagram profile.
* **speaker_linkedin_link** (Text, Optional): Enter a link to the speaker's LinkedIn profile.
* **speaker_website** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's website.
* **speaker_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's address.
* **speaker_city** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's city.
* **speaker_province** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's province.
* **speaker_country** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's country.
* **speaker_postal** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's postal code.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's phone number (note: this field should not be filled in if "speaker_phone" field is already filled in).
* **speaker_language** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's language.
* **speaker_native_language** (Text, Optional): Enter the speaker's native language.
* **other_language** (Text, Optional): Enter any other language the speaker can speak.
* **other_language_other** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the other language.

* **speaker_additional_files_file2** (Text, Optional): Upload any additional file 2.
* **speaker_additional_files_file3** (Text, Optional): Upload any additional file 3.
* **speaker_additional_files_file4** (Text, Optional): Upload any additional file 4.
* **speaker_additional_files_file5** (Text, Optional): Upload any additional file 5.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be accurate and thorough when filling in the speaker's information.
* Use the correct format for the speaker's name, email, and other fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask the speaker or their team for clarification.
* The form is designed to be used for speakers, not attendees or staff members.
