# Dossier Information Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dossier Information Registration form is designed to collect essential details about a client and their participant(s) for a dossier registration process. The form helps gather information such as form control name, description, client contact information, and participant details like name, address, phone number, and email. This form is intended to be used as a starting point for a dossier registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Form Control Name field with the name of the form you are registering.
2. Provide a brief description of the form in the Form Control Description field.
3. Enter the Client Name in the Client Name field.
4. Fill in the Client Address in the Client Address field.
5. Enter the Client Email in the Client Email field.
6. Provide the Client Phone number in the Client Phone field.
7. Enter the Participant Name in the Participant Name field.
8. Fill in the Participant Address in the Participant Address field.
9. Enter the Participant Phone number in the Participant Phone field.
10. Enter the Participant Email in the Participant Email field.
11. If required, provide the Participant Date of Birth and Participant Time of Birth fields with the relevant date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Control Name** (`form_control_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of the form you are registering.
* **Form Control Description** (`form_control_description`, `text`, Required): Provide a brief description of the form.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, Required): Enter the client's address.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `text`, Required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, Required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, Required): Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, Required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `text`, Required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, `date`, Required): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Time of Birth** (`participant_time_of_birth`, `text`, Required): Enter the participant's time of birth.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled correctly to ensure accurate and complete registration.
* Double-check the entered information for spelling and formatting errors.
* The form fields are self-explanatory, make sure to read each field label for a clear understanding of what it expects.
