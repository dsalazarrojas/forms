# Restaurant Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from restaurant managers who are applying for a position at your establishment. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Phone Number** (if applicable).
2. Describe your **Work Experience** in the **Work Experience** field.
3. Specify your **Education Background** in the **Education Background** field.
4. Indicate your **Availability** in the format **HH:MM (e.g., 08:00 - 17:00)**.
5. Select all **Skills** that you possess, such as **Web Development**, **Database Management**, and **Restaurant Management**.
6. Attach your **Resume**, preferably in PDF format.
7. Finally, click **Submit** to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. Make sure to use the correct format (e.g., [your email]).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format **123-456-7890**.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your previous work experience related to restaurant management.
* **Education Background**: Mention any relevant education or training you have received.
* **Availability**: Indicate your availability in the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 8am-5pm).
* **Skills**: Select all relevant skills you possess, such as **Web Development**, **Database Management**, and **Restaurant Management**.
* **Resume**: Attach your resume in PDF format.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your application.
