# Event Content Creator Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Content Creator Agreement form is a document that ensures both event creators and organizers align on event details, terms, and expectations. This form captures essential information, such as event name, date, time, location, and description, as well as creator and organizer contact details, and agreement terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as they appear on the form.
2. Enter event information, including event name, date, time, location, and description.
3. Provide creator contact information and sign the agreement.
4. Review and verify organizer contact information and sign the agreement.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form** (`event_content_creator_agreement_form`, text, required): This field is the primary entry point for the form. Enter a brief description of the form, which will be displayed to users.
* **Content Details** (`content_details`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the event content, including any relevant images or videos.
* **Event Creator Details** (`event_creator_details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information as the event creator.
* **Terms** (`terms`, text, required): Enter any terms and conditions that govern the event, including any rules or regulations.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date you agree to the terms.
* **Creator Signature Date** (`creator_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign the agreement.
* **Creator Contact Information** (`creator_contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Organizer Contact Information** (`organizer_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the event organizer.
* **Agreement Terms and Conditions** (`agreement_terms`, text, required): Enter any additional terms and conditions that apply to the event.
* **Creator Agreement** (`creator_agreement`, text, required): Enter your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement Signature** (`agreement_signature`, text, required): Enter your digital signature.
* **Creator Name** (`creator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the event creator.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the event in hours.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, required): Enter the type of event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the event.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the event organizer.
* **Agreement Notes** (`agreement_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional agreement notes or comments.
* **Creator - Organizer Agreement** (`creator_organizer_agreement`, text, required): Enter your agreement with the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Agreement** (`event_organizer_agreement`, text, required): Enter the event organizer's agreement with you.
* **Event Organizer Notes** (`event_organizer_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments from the event organizer.
* **Agreement Signature** (`agreement_signature`, text, required): This field is a digital signature of the creator and organizer, indicating agreement to the terms.
