# safari_tour_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The safari tour consent form is a document that collects essential information from participants before the tour begins. This form helps the tour leader and other participants know your details, emergency contacts, and ensures you understand the tour's rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information in the **Participant Information** field.
2. Enter your safari details in the **Safari Details** field.
3. Share the **Tour Leader Info** field with the tour leader's contact information.
4. List your emergency contacts in the **Emergency Contacts** field.
5. Read and agree to the **Participant Consent** statement.
6. Sign and date the **Participant Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to help the tour leader contact you and keep you informed about the tour.
* **Safari Details** (`safari_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about the safari tour, including the date, location, and any other relevant information.
* **Tour Leader Info** (`tour_leader_info`, `text`, required/optional): Share the contact information of the tour leader, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required/optional): List the names and contact information of emergency contacts, such as family members or friends, who should be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `text`, required/optional): Read the participant consent statement and agree to the terms and conditions of the tour.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign and date this field to confirm you have read and agreed to the participant consent statement.
