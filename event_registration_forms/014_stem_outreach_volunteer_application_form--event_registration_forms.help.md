# STEM Outreach Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The STEM Outreach Volunteer Application Form is designed to gather information from individuals who want to volunteer for the STEM Outreach program. This program aims to promote scientific, technological, and mathematical literacy in local communities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and details before starting to fill out the form.
2. Go through each section of the form carefully, providing accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Make sure to review and submit your form once you've filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): Please provide your name and any other personal details you feel are necessary to introduce yourself.
* **Personal Information** (`personal_information`, text, optional): Add any additional personal information that you think would be useful for us to know about you.
* **Volunteering Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Share any relevant experience you have that may be useful for the program.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select multiple, optional): Check all the days and time slots you are available to volunteer for.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select one, optional): Indicate your level of skill in any area relevant to the program.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, select multiple, optional): Select all the volunteer types that apply to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Add your phone number if you'd like us to call you for further information.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): Indicate your availability by time of day.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select one, optional): Select your time zone so we can schedule volunteer opportunities correctly.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with us.

## Tips

* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Make sure to review and submit your form once you've filled out all the required fields.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
