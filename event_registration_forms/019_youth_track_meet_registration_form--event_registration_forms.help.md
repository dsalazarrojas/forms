# Youth Track Meet Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Youth Track Meet event, a recurring event organized by the organization, to gather details from athletes and their parents/guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and thoroughly, please follow the steps below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: The name of the event you are registering for.
* **School Club Name**: Your school or club name.
* **Athlete Details**: A brief description of the athlete, including any relevant details.
* **Event Selections**: Select the events you would like to participate in.
*   *Event 1*: Select the event you would like to participate in for Event 1.
*   *Event 2*: Select the event you would like to participate in for Event 2.
*   *Parent Consent*: Confirm your parent's/guardian's consent for your participation.
*   *True*: Confirm your parent's/guardian's consent if it's true.
*   *False*: Confirm your parent's/guardian's consent if it's false.
*   *Date*: Enter the date of the event.
*   *Time*: Enter the time of the event.
*   *Event Date*: Enter the date of the event.

**Tips**
Please fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any issues during the event.
