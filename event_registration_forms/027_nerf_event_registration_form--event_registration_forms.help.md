# Nerf Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for events related to Nerf Guns and Blasters. It is a crucial step in the registration process for attendees to provide their details accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's details:
	* Name: Enter the participant's name as it appears on their official documents.
	* Email: Enter the participant's email address that they check regularly.
	* Phone: Enter the participant's phone number for contact purposes.
2. Choose your waivers and equipment needs:
	* Waivers: Select from the options whether you need or don't need waivers. This will help us know your requirements for the event.
	* Equipment Needs: Choose the types of equipment you will need during the event.
3. Confirm your safety understanding:
	* Prophets of Safety: Select the option that best describes your understanding of the safety protocols. Don't worry if you need to clarify or ask questions; we are here to help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Name** (Participant Details, text, Required): Enter the participant's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, Required): Enter the participant's email address that they check regularly.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, Required): Enter the participant's phone number for contact purposes.

### Waivers
* **Waivers** (Select Multiple, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select from the options whether you need or don't need waivers for the event. This will help us know your requirements.
* Options:
	+ I have read and understand the prophets of safety
	+ No waivers required
	+ Other waivers
	+ No waivers

### Equipment Needs
* **Equipment Needs** (Select Multiple, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose the types of equipment you will need during the event. This will help us ensure that we provide the necessary gear for you.
* Options:
	+ Nerf Guns
	+ Blasters
	+ Nerf Blasters
	+ No Equipment Needed

### Prophets of Safety
* **Prophets of Safety** (Select One, Select One, Not Required): Select the option that best describes your understanding of the safety protocols. This is an essential step to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.
* Options:
	+ I have read and understand the prophets of safety
	+ I have read but not understood the prophets of safety
	+ I have not read the prophets of safety

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We are here to support and assist you throughout the process.
