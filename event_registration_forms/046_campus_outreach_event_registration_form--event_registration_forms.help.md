# Campus Outreach Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register attendees for upcoming events on campus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Start by entering your personal information in the **Attendee Information** section.
2. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**.
4. If applicable, select your **School or University**.
5. In the **Event Details** section, enter the **Event Name**, **Event Date**, **Event Time**, and **Event Location**.
6. In the **Interests and Preferences** section, choose the **Academic Interests or Major Areas** that you are interested in.
7. Select the **Sessions You Plan to Attend**.
8. If you have dietary restrictions, enter them in the **Dietary Restrictions** field.
9. In the **Accessibility and Accommodations** section, indicate if you need any **Accessibility or Special Accommodations**.
10. If you require wheelchair accessibility, select the corresponding option.
11. If you need a sign language interpreter, select the corresponding option.
12. In the **Additional Information** section, indicate how you heard about the event and if this is your first campus visit.
13. Finally, provide any **Questions or Comments** and check the box to consent to receive future event notifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee First Name** (`attendee_first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Attendee Last Name** (`attendee_last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **School or University** (`attendee_school`, optional): If you are a student, enter your school or university.
* **Current Status** (`attendee_status`, required): Select your current status as a High School Student, College/University Student, Prospective Student, Parent/Guardian, Community Member, or Other.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Academic Interests or Major Areas** (`academic_interests`, optional): Select any academic interests or major areas that you are interested in.
* **Sessions You Plan to Attend** (`program_sessions`, required): Select the sessions you plan to attend.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Accessibility or Special Accommodations Needed** (`accessibility_needs`, optional): If you need any accessibility or special accommodations, enter them here.
* **Wheelchair Accessibility Required** (`wheelchair_accessible`, optional): If you require wheelchair accessibility, select True.
* **Sign Language Interpreter Needed** (`sign_language_interpreter`, optional): If you need a sign language interpreter, select True.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event** (`how_heard`, optional): Select how you heard about this event.
* **Is This Your First Campus Visit** (`campus_visit_first`, optional): Check if this is your first campus visit.
* **Questions or Comments** (`questions_comments`, optional): Enter any questions or comments you have.
* **Consent to Receive Future Event Notifications** (`consent_communication`, required): Check the box to consent to receive future event notifications.
