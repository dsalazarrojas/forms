# Virtual Screening Slot Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Screening Slot Booking Form is designed for users to book virtual screening slots by providing their personal details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4.  Select your preferred "Time" for the virtual screening slot.
5.  Select your preferred "Date" for the virtual screening slot.
6.  Optionally, provide "Notes" for any additional information you'd like to share.
7.  Confirm your booking by selecting "Confirm" from the dropdown menu.
8.  If you choose to decline the booking, enter a "Reason for decline".
9.  Enter any "Comments" you'd like to share about the virtual screening slot.
10. Confirm your time and date of the virtual screening slot by selecting a preferred time and date from the "Confirm Date and time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`Name`, Text, optional): Enter your name to be associated with your booking.
*   **Email** (`Email`, Email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your booking.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
*   **Time** (`Time`, Time, optional): Select a preferred time for the virtual screening slot.
*   **Date** (`Date`, Date, optional): Select a preferred date for the virtual screening slot.
*   **Date and time** (`Date and time`, Select Multiple, optional): Select a preferred date and time for the virtual screening slot.
*   **Notes** (`Notes`, Note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your booking.
*   **Confirm** (`Confirm`, Select One, optional): Confirm your booking to proceed with the virtual screening slot.
*   **Reason for decline** (`Reason for decline`, Text, optional): Enter a reason for declining the virtual screening slot booking.
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, Text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the virtual screening slot.
*   **Confirm Time** (`Confirm Time`, Time, optional): Select a preferred confirm time for your booking.
*   **Confirm Date** (`Confirm Date`, Date, optional): Select a preferred confirm date for your booking.
*   **Confirm Date and time** (`Confirm Date and time`, Select Multiple, optional): Select a preferred confirm date and time for your booking.
*   **Confirm Notes** (`Confirm Notes`, Note, optional): Enter any additional confirm notes or comments about your booking.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication and booking processing.
*   Review your booking details carefully before confirming to avoid any errors.
*   If you have any issues or concerns about your booking, reach out to the event organizers or administrators for assistance.
