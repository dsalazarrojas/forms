# Corporate Rebranding Event RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Corporate Rebranding Event RSVP form is used to collect information from participants about the event, including event details, guest information, and confirmations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide the following details about the event you are interested in or have already registered for:
	* In the "Event Details" field, enter the event title and any other relevant details.
	* In the "Guests" field, enter the number of guests attending the event.
	* In the "Special Requests" field, enter any special requests or requirements.
2. Confirm the event and guests' details in the respective fields:
	* In the "Confirm Event Details" field, confirm the event details.
	* In the "Confirm Guests" field, confirm the number of guests.
3. Confirm your special requests in the "Confirm Special Requests" field.
4. If necessary, provide a date to confirm your RSVP in the "RSVP Confirmation" field.
5. Enter your confirmation of event attendance in the "Event Attendance Confirmation" field.
6. Enter your confirmation of special requests in the "Special Requests Confirmation" field.
7. Finally, provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (Event Details, text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can enter any additional details about the event you are interested in or have already registered for.
* **Guests** (Guests, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any special requests or requirements.
* **Confirm Event Details** (Confirm Event Details, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm the event details you entered earlier.
* **Confirm Guests** (Confirm Guests, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm the number of guests you entered.
* **Confirm Special Requests** (Confirm Special Requests, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm your special requests.
* **RSVP Confirmation** (RSVP Confirmation, date, required: false):
	+ If necessary, provide a date to confirm your RSVP.
* **Event Attendance Confirmation** (Event Attendance Confirmation, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm that you will attend the event.
* **Special Requests Confirmation** (Special Requests Confirmation, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm your special requests.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact information, including name, phone number, and email address.
