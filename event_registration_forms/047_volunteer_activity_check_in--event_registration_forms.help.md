# Volunteer Activity Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about a volunteer's activity check in, including event details and volunteer information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the event name, date, time, and location.
2. Enter your volunteer information, including name, phone number, email, and address.
3. Choose the activity type from the provided options, or select "Other" if the activity type is not listed.
4. Enter the start and end dates and times for the activity.
5. Enter the hours worked for the activity.
6. Provide any additional comments about the activity.
7. Finally, select the submitted by option and enter the submit date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (text): Enter the name of the event you participated in.
* **Event Date** (date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (time): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (text): Enter the location of the event.
* **Volunteer Name** (text): Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Volunteer Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Volunteer Address** (text): Enter your address.
* **Activity Type** (select_one): Choose the type of activity you participated in from the provided options, or select "Other" if it's not listed.
* **Other Activity Type** (text): If you selected "Other", enter the type of activity in this field.
* **Activity Start Date** (date): Enter the date the activity started.
* **Activity Start Time** (time): Enter the start time of the activity.
* **Activity End Date** (date): Enter the date the activity ended.
* **Activity End Time** (time): Enter the end time of the activity.
* **Activity Hours** (number): Enter the total hours worked for the activity.
* **Other Activity Hours** (text): If you need to enter hours not listed, enter them in this field.
* **Volunteer Comments** (text): Provide any additional comments about your experience with the activity.
* **Activity Comments** (text): Provide any additional comments about the activity.
* **Submitted By** (select_one): Select who submitted the form.
* **Submit Date** (date): Enter the date you submitted the form.
