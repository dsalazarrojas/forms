# Texas Family Reunion Final Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects essential information about attendees at a Texas Family Reunion. Complete this form to register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Family Members Attending" field.
3. Enter the age of the attendee in the "Age" field.
4. Enter a valid email address for the attendee in the "Email" field.
5. Provide a phone number for the attendee in the "Phone" field.
6. If the attendee has any special needs, describe them in the "Special Needs" field.
7. Enter the name and relationship of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Relationship" fields.
8. Enter a phone number for the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
9. If the attendee has any special needs, provide more details in the "Special Needs Details" field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Registration Method" field, indicating how the attendee plans to register.
11. Finally, select the registration date from the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the attendee who is registering.
* **Family Members Attending** (`family_members_attending`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you will be bringing family members, "No" if not.
* **Age** (`attendee_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the attendee.
* **Email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the attendee.
* **Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): Provide a phone number for the attendee.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): If the attendee has any special needs, describe them here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe the relationship between the attendee and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Special Needs Details** (`special_needs_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about the attendee's special needs.
* **Registration Method** (`registration_method`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you will be registering online, "No" if not.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to register.
