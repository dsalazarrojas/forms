# dance_class_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow students to request extensions for previously scheduled dance classes. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the Student's First Name in the "Student Name" field.
6. Enter the Student's Email address in the "Student Email" field.
7. Enter the Student's Phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
8. Enter the start time of the extension request in the "Start Time" field.
9. Enter the end time of the extension request in the "End Time" field.
10. Enter the number of extensions requested in the "Extension" field.
11. Finally, enter the name of the dance class for which the extension is being requested in the "Class Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Student Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the student's name as they will be attending the dance class.
* **Student Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Start Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the start time of the extension request.
* **End Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the end time of the extension request.
* **Extension** (number, required/optional): Enter the number of extensions requested.
* **Class Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the dance class for which the extension is being requested.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your extension request is processed correctly.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
- Extension requests are subject to approval and may not always be granted.
- If your extension request is approved, you will receive confirmation of the new class time via email or phone.
