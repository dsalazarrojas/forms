# Family Reunion Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a family reunion event, including family members' attendance, activity details, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Family Info" section with the names of all family members attending the reunion.
2. Provide details about the reunion, such as its purpose and location.
3. Choose how you plan to attend the event ("RSVPing", "Not RSVPing", or "Maybe") in the "Reunion Details" section.
4. Add any comments or additional information about the event in the "Activity Info" and "Comments" sections.
5. Enter attendee details, such as their name and contact information, in the "Attendee Info" section.
6. Enter your contact information, including your email address, in the "Contact Info" section.
7. Share any relevant family history or background in the "Family History" section.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Info**: Provide the names of all family members attending the event. This field is not required, but it will help us know who's coming.
* **Reunion Details**: Briefly describe the purpose and details of the reunion. This section is not required but will help us understand the context of the event.
* **RSVP**: Choose how you plan to attend the event ("RSVPing", "Not RSVPing", or "Maybe"). This will help us finalize the event's guest list.
* **Activity Info**: Share any additional information or comments about the event that might be helpful.
* **Attendee Info**: Enter attendee details, such as their name and contact information.
* **Event Details**: Provide any details about the event that might be helpful.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your email address.
* **Family History**: Share any relevant family history or background that might be helpful for others to know.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about the event.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address so we can reach you with updates about the event.
