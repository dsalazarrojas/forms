# Public Event Coordinator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about public events. It includes fields for event details, coordinator and sponsor information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are logged in to the system and have access to this form.
2. Select the event status (Available, In Progress, or Completed) and provide a reason for the event status if necessary.
3. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration Form** (`event_registration_form`): This field is for entering the details of the event, but it is not required.
* **Event Coordinator Information** (`event_coordinator_info`): This field is for entering the details of the event coordinator, including their name and contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): This field is for entering the contact information of the person submitting the form.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): This field is for entering the contact number of the person submitting the form.
* **Email** (`email`): This field is for entering the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): This field is for entering the details of the event, including the date, time, and location.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): This field is for entering the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): This field is for entering the time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`): This field is for entering the duration of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): This field is for entering the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): This field is for entering a description of the event.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`): This field is for selecting the status of the event (Available, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Event Status Reason** (`event_status_reason`): This field is for providing a reason for the event status (only required if the event status is not Available).
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`): This field is for entering any additional notes about the event.
* **Speaker Information** (`event_speaker`): This field is for entering information about the speaker, including their name, contact information, and email address.
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`): This field is for entering the name of the speaker.
* **Speaker Contact Information** (`speaker_contact_info`): This field is for entering the contact information of the speaker.
* **Speaker Contact Number** (`speaker_phone`): This field is for entering the contact number of the speaker.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`): This field is for entering the email address of the speaker.
* **Sponsor Information** (`sponsor_info`): This field is for entering information about the sponsor, including their name, contact information, and email address.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`): This field is for entering the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Contact Information** (`sponsor_contact_info`): This field is for entering the contact information of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Contact Number** (`sponsor_phone`): This field is for entering the contact number of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`): This field is for entering the email address of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Address** (`sponsor_address`): This field is for entering the address of the sponsor.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the system administrator for assistance.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
