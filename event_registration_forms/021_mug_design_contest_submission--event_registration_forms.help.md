# Mug Design Contest Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to submit their entry for the mug design contest. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to provide your design preferences and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Fill out your **Name** and **Email** fields with your true and correct information.
2. If you want to be contacted for further information, fill out your **Phone** field with your phone number.
3. Select your preferred **Color** for the mug design.
4. Choose the type of design you would like to submit (**Graphic**, **Illustration**, **Text**, **Photography**, or **Other**).
5. Write a brief description of your design idea in the **Design Description** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your real name as it will be displayed on the mug design.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you want to be contacted for your design submission, enter your phone number.
* **Color** (`color`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the available colors for the mug design. Options: **Red**, **Blue**, **Green**, **Yellow**, **Black**, or **White**.
* **Design Type** (`design_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more types of design you would like to submit. Options: **Graphic**, **Illustration**, **Text**, **Photography**, or **Other**.
* **Design Description** (`design_description`, `text`, required): Write a brief description of your design idea.
