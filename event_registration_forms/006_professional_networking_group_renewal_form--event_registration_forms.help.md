# professional_networking_group_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The professional networking group renewal form is used to collect information from members for the purpose of renewal. This includes updating member details and payment information to ensure the member's status within the group is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your member information in the "Member Info" section.
2. Provide your personal and professional details in the "Member Details" section.
3. Enter your renewal information, including any necessary updates, in the "Renewal Info" section.
4. Fill out your payment information, including any payment method and amount, in the "Payment Info" section.
5. Review and accept the terms and conditions in the "Review and Accept" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Info** (`member_info`, text, not required): This section allows you to enter your basic member information.
* **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, not required): In this section, you can provide more in-depth information about yourself, including any relevant professional or personal details.
* **Renewal Info** (`renewal_info`, text, required): Here, you will enter the details of your renewal, including any updates or changes to your information.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required): In this section, you will enter your payment information, including payment method and amount.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): This section allows you to enter additional details about your payment, if necessary.
* **Review and Accept** (`review_and_accept`, text, required): In this final section, you will review and accept the terms and conditions of the professional networking group.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
