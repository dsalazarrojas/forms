# Halloween Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween party registration form is a tool used to collect information about participants attending the event. It is likely used to manage the event's capacity and ensure attendees' information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and contact information.
2. Provide your contact name, which will be used for any further communication.
3. Enter the number of participants you will be attending with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (1): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the event materials.
* **Email** (2): Enter your email address for any further communication.
* **Contact Name** (3): Provide a name to be used for any further communication or contact.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Number of participants** (5): Enter the number of people attending with you.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any confusion or delays.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please note them in the contact name field.
