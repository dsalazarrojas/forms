# Outdoor Event Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences and preferences regarding outdoor events. Your input will help us understand what you enjoy and what we can improve for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For select options, choose one or multiple options that apply to you.
3. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please include them in the "feedback" field.
4. If you have any comments or additional information, please include them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is an optional field to provide any additional context or background information about your experiences with the event.
* **How many days did you participate in the event? (event_type)**: Select the number of days you participated in the event. For example, if you attended a one-day event, select 1.
* **How many hours did you participate in the event? (event_length)**: Select the number of hours you participated in the event.
* **How often do you participate in similar events? (event_frequency)**: Choose how often you participate in similar events, such as:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Rarely
* **What would you like to see improved at this type of event? (feedback)**: Provide any feedback or suggestions on how we can improve the event.
* **Do you have any further suggestions? (suggestions)**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions on how we can improve the event.
* **Email Address (email)**: This is your email address, which will help us contact you for any further questions or clarification.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: This is your phone number, which will help us contact you for any further questions or clarification.
* **How satisfied are you with the overall event? (event_satisfaction)**: Select your level of satisfaction with the event, from 1 (very satisfied) to 5 (very dissatisfied).
* **Event Satisfaction Breakdown (event_satisfaction_breakdown)**: Choose how you feel about different aspects of the event, such as:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **How many people attended the event? (attendance)**: Select the number of people who attended the event.
* **Would you recommend this event? (recommendation)**: Choose if you would recommend this event to others, such as:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Undecided
* **What type of event would you like to participate in most often? (event_type_frequency)**: Choose the type of event you would like to participate in most often, such as:
	+ Music Festivals
	+ Food Festivals
	+ Sports Tournaments
	+ Cultural Fairs
	+ Other
* **Which location would you prefer for this type of event? (event_location)**: Choose the location you would prefer for this type of event, such as:
	+ Urban
	+ Rural
	+ Beachside
	+ Park
	+ Other
* **How old are you? (age)**: Select your age.
* **What is your gender? (gender)**: Choose your gender, such as:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-Binary
* **What is your annual income? (income)**: Choose your annual income, such as:
	+ Less than $20,000
	+ $20,000-$30,000
	+ $30,000-$40,000
	+ $40,000-$50,000
	+ $50,000-$60,000
	+ More than $60,000
* **What is your level of education? (education)**: Choose your level of education, such as:
	+ High School Diploma or Equivalent
	+ Some College or Associates Degree
	+ Bachelors Degree or Equivalent
	+ Masters Degree or Equivalent
	+ Doctoral Degree or Equivalent
* **What is your occupation? (occupation)**: Choose your occupation, such as:
	+ Student
	+ Full-time Employee
	+ Part-time Employee
	+ Self-employed
	+ Unemployed
* **How did you hear about this event? (how_heard)**: Choose how you heard about the event, such as:
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Social Media
	+ Print Media
	+ Online Search
	+ Other
* **Have you attended a similar event organized by this event organizer before? (attended_previous_events)**: Choose if you have attended a similar event by this event organizer before.
* **Would you participate in a similar event organized by this event organizer again? (would_participate)**: Choose if you would participate in a similar event by this event organizer again.
* **How often would you participate in a similar event organized by this event organizer? (how_often_would_participate)**: Choose how often you would participate in a similar event by this event organizer, such as:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Rarely
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the event or this survey.

## Tips
1. Be honest and accurate with your answers.
2. If you have any further questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
3. Your input is greatly appreciated and will help us improve future events.
