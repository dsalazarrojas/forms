# food-vendor-license-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The food-vendor-license-application-form is a form used by food vendors to apply for a license to operate. It collects necessary information from the vendor to process the application for a local health department or regulatory agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your VENDOR NAME.
2. Provide your Business Name, Address, Phone, and Email if applicable.
3. Describe your business in 1-2 sentences in the Business Description field.
4. Select the type of food business you operate from the Food Type options.
5. Choose the structure of your business (e.g., LLC, Partnership, etc.) from the License Type options.
6. Select the type of permit your business requires from the Permit Type options.
7. Select the status of your permit and license from the Status options.
8. Choose whether your license needs renewal from the License Renewal options.
9. Fill in the dates for Permit Date, Permit Expiration, and License Expiration if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **VENDOR NAME**: The name of your food business. This is a required field.
* **Business Name**: Your business trade name or DBA (Doing Business As). This is an optional field.
* **Business Address**: The address where your business is located. This is an optional field.
* **Business Phone**: The primary contact method for your business. This is an optional field.
* **Business Email**: The secondary contact method for your business. This is an optional field.
* **Business Description**: A brief description of your business in 1-2 sentences. This is an optional field.
* **Food Type**: The type of food business you operate, such as Food Truck, Restaurant, Cart, etc. This is a required field.
* **License Type**: The structure of your business, such as LLC, Partnership, Corporation, etc. This is a required field.
* **Permit Type**: The type of permit your business requires, such as Type A, Type B, Type C, etc. This is a required field.
* **Permit Date**: The date the permit was issued. This is an optional field.
* **Permit Expiration**: The date the permit expires. This is an optional field.
* **Status**: The status of your permit, such as Active, Pending, Cancelled. This is a required field.
* **License Status**: The status of your license, such as Active, Pending, Cancelled. This is a required field.
* **License Expiration**: The date your license expires. This is an optional field.
* **License Renewal**: Whether your license needs renewal. This is a required field.
