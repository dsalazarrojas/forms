# Animal Shelter Marketing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Shelter Marketing Workshop Registration Form is designed to gather information from workshop participants for the purpose of providing a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields:

* First name and last name (fields 1-2)
* Contact information (email, phone number) (fields 3-4)
* Organization and role (fields 5-7)
* Workshop preferences (fields 9-11)
* Payment and sponsorship information (fields 19-22)
* Learning expectations and goals (fields 23-25)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Field 1): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (Field 2): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (Field 3): Your email address for workshop communications.
* **Phone Number** (Field 4): Your contact number.
* **Organization Name** (Field 5): Name of your animal shelter or rescue.
* **Organization Type** (Field 6): Type of animal organization (e.g., Animal Shelter, Animal Rescue, etc.).
* **Job Title** (Field 7): Your position at the organization.
* **Workshop Selection** (Field 9): Choose the workshops you want to attend.
* **Workshop Date** (Field 10): Date you plan to attend.
* **Workshops You Want to Attend** (Field 11): Select all applicable workshops.
* **Current Marketing Skill Level** (Field 12): Your experience level with marketing.
* **Primary Interest Area** (Field 13): Main focus for the workshop.
* **Attendance Format** (Field 15): How you will participate (in-person, virtual, hybrid).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Field 16): Any dietary needs for meals.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Field 17): Any accommodations needed.
* **Roommate Request** (Field 18): Preference for housing.
* **Registration Fee Amount** (Field 20): Total fee for selected workshops.
* **Payment Method** (Field 21): Method of payment (credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **Interested in Sponsorship Opportunities** (Field 22): Want to sponsor the workshop.
* **Key Learning Goals** (Field 24): What you hope to learn.
* **Current Marketing Challenges** (Field 25): What problems you are facing.
* **Additional Comments** (Field 26): Any other information.

## Tips

* Please fill out all required fields.
* Choose all applicable workshops for your interests and goals.
* If you have any dietary or accessibility needs, please let us know so we can accommodate you.
* If you are interested in sponsorship opportunities, please let us know and we will follow up with more information.
