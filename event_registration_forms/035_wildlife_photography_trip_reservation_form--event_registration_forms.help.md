# Wildlife Photography Trip Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from participants for the Wildlife Photography Trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Enter your Participant Name.
3. Provide a valid Email.
4. Enter your Phone Number (optional).
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. Select the trip date you wish to join.
7. Choose your preferred Payment Method.
8. Enter the payment amount (must be a valid number).
9. Optionally, provide any additional Participant Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email that can be used for communication about the trip.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by trip organizers (optional).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Trip Date**: Select the date you wish to join the trip.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you wish to pay for the trip (Stripe, PayPal, or Cash).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the exact amount you are willing to pay (must be a valid number).
* **Participant Notes**: If you have any special requests or comments, enter them here (optional).

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your registration. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the trip organizers.
