# workforce_efficiency_analysis_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Workforce Efficiency Analysis Workshop registration. It gathers details about the participants and the workshop logistics to ensure a smooth and well-organized event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Add your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
5. Specify the workshop location in the "Workshop location" field.
6. Choose the time of the workshop in the "Time of workshop" field.
7. Indicate the duration of the workshop in the "Workshop duration" field.
8. Enter the expected number of participants in the "Expected participant count" field.
9. Add any additional details in the "Workshop time" field.
10. Add any additional information about the workshop in the "Workshop additional info" field.
