# Best Event Planner Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's event planning experience and performance evaluation for the Best Event Planner Award.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about the nominee's qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`nominator_full_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person nominating the event planner.
* **Your Organization** (`nominator_org_name`, text, optional): If you are nominating an event planner from your organization, enter the name of your organization.
* **Your Email Address** (`nominator_email_addr`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Event Planner Name** (`nominee_full_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event planner being nominated.
* **Company Name** (`nominee_company_name`, text, optional): If the event planner works for a specific organization, enter the name of the company.
* **Planner Email or Website** (`nominee_web_presence`, text, required): Enter the email address or website of the event planner for verification.
* **Estimated Years of Experience** (`nominee_experience_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the estimated years of experience of the event planner (1 to 3 years, 4 to 7 years, 8 to 12 years, 13 or more years).
* **Professionalism and Conduct** (`professionalism_rating_score`, select_one, required): Rate the event planner's professionalism and conduct from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Creativity and Design Vision** (`creativity_rating_score`, select_one, required): Rate the event planner's creativity and design vision from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Attention to Detail** (`detail_rating_score`, select_one, required): Rate the event planner's attention to detail from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Problem Solving Ability** (`problem_solving_rating_score`, select_one, required): Rate the event planner's problem solving ability from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Budget Management and Efficiency** (`budget_management_rating_score`, select_one, required): Rate the event planner's budget management and efficiency from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Describe a specific event** (`specific_event_description`, text, required): Describe a specific event planned by this nominee.
* **What made this event successful** (`event_success_factors`, text, required): Highlight the planner's contribution to this specific event.
* **Nominee's greatest strength** (`greatest_strength_desc`, text, required): Describe the nominee's greatest strength in one sentence.
* **Any other comments** (`additional_committee_comments`, text, optional): If you have any other comments for the awards committee, enter them here.
* **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_timestamp`, date, required): Enter the date of nomination.
