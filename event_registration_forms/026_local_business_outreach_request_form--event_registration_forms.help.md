# Local Business Outreach Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help local businesses connect with the organization for potential partnerships, sponsorships, or collaborations. Please complete this form to share your business interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Provide a brief description of the event you are interested in or would like to propose.
3. Choose the type of event you would like to organize (e.g., Trade Show, Seminar).
4. Select the services your business offers (e.g., Networking, Marketing).
5. Specify your collaboration goals (e.g., Collaboration, Sponsorship).
6. Enter your business name and contact information.
7. Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g., Email, Phone).
8. Provide the date and time you are interested in hosting the event.
9. Choose the location where the event will take place (e.g., Online, Local).
10. Select the duration of the event (e.g., Half-day, Full-day, All-day).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit**: 
  This is the submit button to finalize your form entry.
* **Name**: 
  Please enter your name as it should appear on the form and in any communication with the organization.
* **Email**: 
  Enter your business email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: 
  Enter your business phone number where you can be reached.
* **Business Name**: 
  Provide the name of your business for reference and communication purposes.
* **Address**: 
  Enter your business address for reference and communication purposes.
* **Zip Code**: 
  Enter your business zip code for reference and communication purposes.
* **Event Description**: 
  Briefly describe the event you are interested in or would like to propose. 
* **Event Type**: 
  Choose the type of event you would like to organize (e.g., Event Type, Trade Show, Seminar). 
* **Services**: 
  Select the services your business offers (e.g., Networking, Marketing).
* **Collaboration Goals**: 
  Specify your collaboration goals (e.g., Collaboration, Sponsorship). 
* **Organization**: 
  Enter your business name for reference and communication purposes.
* **Contact Person**: 
  Enter the name of the person who will be in charge of event organization. 
* **Contact Email**: 
  Enter the email address of the contact person where they can be reached.
* **Contact Phone**: 
  Enter the phone number of the contact person where they can be reached.
* **Preferred Method**: 
  Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g., Email, Phone). 
* **Event Date**: 
  Enter the date you are interested in hosting the event.
* **Time**: 
  Enter the time you are interested in hosting the event.
* **Location**: 
  Choose the location where the event will take place (e.g., Online, Local).
* **Event Duration**: 
  Select the duration of the event (e.g., Half-day, Full-day, All-day).
