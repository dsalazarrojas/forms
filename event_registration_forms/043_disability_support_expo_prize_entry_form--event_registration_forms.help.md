# Disability Support Expo Prize Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disability Support Expo Prize Entry Form is a simple online form designed to collect information from individuals who wish to enter the Disability Support Expo prize competition. This form will help us verify your eligibility and ensure a unique entry.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, please fill out the required information in each field.
2.  Start by providing your contact information on the "Contact Information" page.
3.  On the "Personal Information" page, please provide any relevant details about yourself.
4.  On the "Prize Information" page, describe the prize you are interested in.
5.  If you have any additional information you'd like to share, please use the "Additional Information" page.
6.  Finally, provide a "Additional Note" on the "Additional Note" page if you have any further comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (page 1)** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you provide your name and contact details.
	+ Please enter your full name and a valid email address or phone number.
* **Personal Information (page 2)** (`personal_info`, text, optional):
	+ In this field, please describe your relationship with the Disability Support Expo.
	+ Share any relevant details about yourself, such as your disability status or how you are connected to the expo.
* **Prize Information (page 3)** (`prize_info`, text, optional):
	+ Here, you can specify which prize you are interested in entering.
	+ Please be specific about the prize and any details that might affect the entry.
* **Additional Information (page 4)** (`additional_info`, text, optional):
	+ If there's anything else you'd like to tell us about yourself or your experience, please share it here.
	+ You can describe your skills, qualifications, or achievements related to the Disability Support Expo.
* **Additional Note (page 5)** (`note2`, note, optional):
	+ This is a text box for any additional comments or questions you have.
	+ Please use this space to provide any further information that might be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your entry carefully and get back to you soon.
