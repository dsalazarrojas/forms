# Business Partner Meet And Greet RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Partner Meet And Greet RSVP Form is a crucial tool for event planning and management. It helps us understand who is attending our events, their preferences, and any special requirements they may have. This form will help us tailor our event experience to meet your needs and ensure a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (Your Name, Email Address, Phone Number).
2. Enter your company name and job title.
3. Confirm the name of the event you are attending.
4. Provide the date and start time of the event.
5. If you are bringing guests, enter their names and number.
6. If you have dietary restrictions or special requirements, please let us know.
7. Select your RSVP status (Will you attend, No, I cannot attend, or Maybe, unsure).
8. If you have any additional comments or requests, feel free to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Your Name
* **Your Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your primary email address for contact.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.

### Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your company or organization name.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **required**): Enter your position or role.

### Event Name
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, **required**): Confirm the event name you are attending.

### Event Date
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the event.

### Event Time
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the start time of the event.

### Event Location
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter the venue or address.

### RSVP Status
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Select "Yes, I Will Attend", "No, I Cannot Attend", or "Maybe, Unsure".

### Number of Guests
* **Number of Guests Including You** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, **required**): Enter the total number of attendees, including yourself.

### Guest Names
* **Guest Names** (`guest_names`, `text`, **optional**): List the names of your additional guests.

### Dietary Restrictions
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select any special dietary needs (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.).

### Dietary Details
* **Dietary Details** (`dietary_details`, `text`, **optional**): If you selected "Other", specify your dietary needs.

### Networking Interests
* **Networking Interests** (`networking_interests`, `text`, **optional**): Share your professional interests for networking opportunities.

### Accessibility Needs
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any accessibility requirements, please specify.

### Industries You Want to Network With
* **Industries You Want to Network With** (`prefer_to_network_with`, `text`, **optional**): List the types of companies or professionals you'd like to connect with.

### Have You Attended Our Events Before?
* **Have You Attended Our Events Before?** (`prior_event_attendance`, `select_one`, **optional**): Confirm your previous attendance.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Share any other information about your attendance.

This guide is designed to help users complete the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that all necessary information is provided to ensure a successful event experience for all attendees.
