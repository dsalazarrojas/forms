# Fashion Show Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to renew the Fashion Show Agreement between participants and event organizers. This agreement outlines the terms and conditions of participating in the fashion show and is essential for ensuring a smooth and successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their purpose.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and completely, especially the fields marked as "required".
3.  Use the available fields to provide as much information as possible about the participant.
4.  Submit the form once you are confident that all the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide basic information about the participant. It's an optional field, but please provide any relevant details about the participant.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is used to collect contact information for the participant. If the participant has a representative or agent, please provide their contact details.
*   **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, optional): This field is used to outline the terms and conditions of the participant's agreement. Please ensure that you are aware of these terms before proceeding.
*   **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, number, optional): This field is used to capture the participant's signature. Please sign with your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
*   **Event Info** (`event_info`, select_one, optional): Select the event that this participant is registering for from the options provided. Please ensure that you are registering for the correct event.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): If you are unable to select the event date in the "Event Info" field, please provide the date of the event you are registering for. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect the participant's email address. Please ensure that your email address is up-to-date and correct.
*   **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the participant's phone number. Please ensure that your phone number is correct and up-to-date.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): If you are unable to select the event time in the "Event Info" field, please provide the time of the event you are registering for. Please use the `HH:MM AM/PM` format.
*   **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the participant's address. Please ensure that your address is correct and up-to-date.
*   **Event Date Time** (`event_date_time`, date, optional): If you are unable to select the event date and time in the "Event Info" field, please provide the date and time of the event you are registering for. Please use the `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM AM/PM` format.
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the participant's name. Please ensure that your name is correct and up-to-date.
*   **Agreement Renewal Date** (`agreement_renewal_date`, date, optional): This field is used to capture the date of the agreement renewal. Please use the `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
*   **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the event organizer. Please ensure that you are aware of the correct event organizer.
*   **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any additional notes or comments about the participant.
*   **Agreement Renewal Status** (`agreement_renewal_status`, text, optional): This field is used to determine the participant's agreement renewal status. Please select one of the options provided.

Please note that all the fields marked as "optional" are not required to be filled, but it's highly recommended to fill them out for accurate and efficient processing.
