# Festival Press Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Festival Press Mixer RSVP Form is a simple form designed to collect attendee information and preferences for the Festival Press Mixer event. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Provide media details (page 1)
* **Media Details**: Enter the media type you would like to bring to the event. 

2. Enter your Name (page 2)
* **Name**: Please enter your full name as it should be displayed on the name tags and event materials. 

3. Enter your Email (page 3)
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates and information about the event. 

4. Enter your Phone (page 4)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of any last minute updates or changes. 

5. Choose your RSVP Option (page 5)
* **RSVP**: Please select one of the available options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3. This will help the organizers understand your availability and preferences for the event.

6. Choose Event Date (page 6)
* **Event Date**: This field is not currently being used for this form. You may select any value for this field as it will not affect your submission. 

7. Choose Event Time (page 7)
* **Event Time**: This field is not currently being used for this form. You may select any value for this field as it will not affect your submission. 

8. Add any additional Notes (page 8)
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the organizers. This is an optional field and your response will not affect your submission. 

9. Describe the Event (page 9)
* **Event Description**: This field is not currently being used for this form. You may enter any value for this field as it will not affect your submission.
