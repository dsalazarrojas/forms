# Free Throw Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Free Throw Contest Registration form is designed to collect information from participants in the Free Throw Contest event. This form is used to gather details about each participant, including their name, email, school, and event information. The collected data will be used to organize and track participant registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Details** on page 1.
2. Provide your **Participant Name**, **Participant Email**, and **School Name**.
3. Select your **School League**.
4. Sign the **Waiver Signature** section, acknowledging that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
5. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of your participation in the format MM/dd/yyyy and HH - MM AM/PM respectively.
6. Select the **School Event** that you are participating in.
7. Provide any **Additional Information** about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (Page 1, Required): This field is used to display the participant's name and other basic details.
	+ **Participant Name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification card.
	+ **Participant Email** (Required, Email Type): Enter your valid email address.
	+ **School Name** (Required): Enter your school's name as it appears on your ID or records.
* **School League** (Required): Select the school league you are participating from.
* **Waiver Signature** (Required): Sign this section to acknowledge that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Date** (Required, Date Type): Enter the date of your participation in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (Required, Time Type): Enter the time of your participation in the format HH - MM AM/PM.
* **School Event** (Required): Select the school event you are participating in.
* **Additional Information** (Optional): Provide any additional information about your participation.
