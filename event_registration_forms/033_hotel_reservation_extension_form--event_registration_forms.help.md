# Hotel Reservation Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Reservation Extension Form is designed to allow you to extend an existing hotel reservation for a specific period of time. This form helps you to provide additional details and preferences for your stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Event Details" page.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed:
	* "Checkin Date" (optional): Enter the date you would like to check-in to your hotel room.
	* "Checkout Date" (optional): Enter the date you would like to check-out of your hotel room.
3. Choose your preferred "Room Type" (optional):
	* Standard Room
	* Suite
	* Deluxe Room
4. Enter the number of "Extension Days" (optional): Enter how many additional days you would like to extend your reservation.
5. Provide your "Guest Name" (optional): Enter the name of the guest whose room you are booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`form_data`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about your stay.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to check-in to your hotel room.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to check-out of your hotel room.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of room you would like to stay in. Options are:
	+ Standard Room
	+ Suite
	+ Deluxe Room
* **Extension Days** (`extension_days`, number, not required): Enter how many additional days you would like to extend your reservation.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the guest whose room you are booking.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your dates and room preferences to ensure your stay is accurate and comfortable.
* If you have any special requests or preferences, please include them in the "Event Details" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please note that availability and room types may be subject to change based on hotel occupancy.
