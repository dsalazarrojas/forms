# Regatta Crew RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from regatta crew members to manage their participation in a regatta event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Crew Details** (text):
	+ Enter your name and any other personal details you would like to share with the event organizers.
* **crew_name** (text):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents or as you would like to be referred to during the regatta.
* **Crew Member Details** (text):
	+ This section allows you to enter more details about yourself as a crew member.
* **First Name** (text):
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text):
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Age** (number):
	+ Enter your age.
* **Contact Email** (email):
	+ Enter your email address that you would like to be contacted on.
* **Contact Phone** (text):
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Position** (text):
	+ Enter your position on the crew, e.g. skipper, navigator, etc.
* **Boat** (text):
	+ Enter the name of your boat.
* **Crew Number** (number):
	+ Enter the number of crew members on your boat.
* **Yacht Name** (text):
	+ Enter the name of your yacht.
* **Yacht Length** (number):
	+ Enter the length of your yacht in meters.
* **Yacht Type** (text):
	+ Enter the type of your yacht, e.g. monohull, multihull, etc.
* **Yacht Design** (text):
	+ Enter the design of your yacht, e.g. sailing, rowing, etc.
* **Yacht Number** (text):
	+ Enter the number of your yacht.
* **Boat Number** (text):
	+ Enter the number of your boat.
* **Boat Type** (text):
	+ Enter the type of your boat, e.g. sailing, power, etc.
* **Boat Designer** (text):
	+ Enter the designer of your boat.
* **Boat Length** (number):
	+ Enter the length of your boat in meters.
* **Boat Designer** (text):
	+ Enter the designer of your boat.
* **Regatta** (text):
	+ Enter the regatta you are participating in.
* **Skipper** (text):
	+ Enter the skipper of your boat.
* **Yacht Name** (text):
	+ Enter the name of your yacht.
* **Regatta Info** (text):
	+ Enter any other information about the regatta you would like to share.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check your entries for spelling and formatting errors before submitting the form.
- Contact the event organizers if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
