# industrial_reliability_symposium_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The form is designed for registering attendees to the Industrial Reliability Symposium. It collects information from attendees to provide them with the necessary details about the event, including event details, payment methods, and attendee information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  First, please ensure you have not already registered for this event. If you have any doubt, please contact us.
2.  Fill out the form with your personal and contact information.
3.  Confirm your event attendance and registration type.
4.  Provide your payment details if you are paying for your registration.
5.  Enter any additional attendee information if required.
6.  Review your form before submitting it for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Job Title** (4, text, optional): Enter your job title (if any).
*   **Department** (5, text, optional): Enter your department (if any).
*   **Company** (6, text, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
*   **Contact Phone** (7, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Contact E-mail** (8, email, required): Enter a valid contact email.
*   **Event attendees** (9, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you will bring attendees.
*   **Registration Type** (10, select_one, required): Select your registration type.
*   **Date of Birth** (11, date, optional): If you want to be remembered as a special date (e.g., birthday), enter your birth date here.
*   **Time of event** (12, time, optional): If you want to be remembered as a specific event time, enter it here.
*   **Note** (13, note, optional): Add any additional note about your registration.
*   **Event details** (14, note, optional): Enter any additional event details here.
*   **Payment Type** (15, select_one, optional): Select your payment type.
*   **Payment method** (16, select_one, optional): Select your payment method.
*   **Payment date** (17, date, optional): Enter the payment date.
*   **Payment time** (18, time, optional): Enter the payment time if required.
*   **Payment note** (19, note, optional): Enter any additional payment note.
*   **Attendee details** (20, text, optional): Enter any additional attendee details.
*   **Company address** (21, text, optional): Enter your company address (if applicable).
*   **Attendee name** (22, text, optional): Enter the attendee name if you are registering for another person.
*   **Attendee e-mail** (23, email, optional): Enter the attendee email.
*   **Attendee phone** (24, text, optional): Enter the attendee phone number.
*   **Attendee relationship** (25, select_one, optional): Indicate the attendee relationship.
