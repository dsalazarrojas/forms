# Virtual Trade Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for a virtual trade fair. It captures personal details, event information, and attendee details to allow event organizers to understand who will be attending the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email, company, job title, job function, and contact number.
2. Provide information about the event you wish to attend, including the event name, date, time, and location.
3. If applicable, select the ticket you wish to purchase or confirm your attendance.
4. Optionally, provide comments or additional information about your attendance.
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Your Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Your personal name as it appears on your business card or introduction.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Your professional email address.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): The name of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Your job title or position within your company.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, required): Your job function or role within your company.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `number`, required): Your phone number or other contact information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Your company's email address.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): The name of the event you wish to attend.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): The date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): The time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required): The location of the event.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required): Your role within the company or organization.
* **Ticket** (`ticket`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the ticket type you wish to purchase. Options include Ticket 1, Ticket 2, and Ticket 3.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your attendance.
* **Certificate** (`certificate`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of certificate you wish to receive. Options include Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Confirm Event** (`confirm_event`, `select_multiple`, optional): Confirm your attendance for each selected option. Options include Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, `date`, required): The start date of the event.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, `date`, required): The end date of the event.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required): The name of the attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, `email`, required): The email address of the attendee.
* **Attendee Job Title** (`attendee_job_title`, `text`, required): The job title of the attendee.
* **Attendee Company** (`attendee_company`, `text`, required): The name of the attendee's company.
* **Attendee Job Function** (`attendee_job_function`, `text`, required): The job function of the attendee.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to complete your registration.
