# Retail Store Showcase Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Store Showcase Reservation Form is designed for customers to reserve a showcase at a retail store, providing essential information for event planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill out the form with your name and contact information.
2. Select the event date and time for your showcase.
3. Choose the type of showcase you would like to reserve.
4. Enter the number of guests attending the event.
5. If you have any special requests, such as setup or equipment requirements, please provide them in the "Special Requests" field.
6. Enter the store name and manager's name associated with the event.
7. Select the event status (Active, Inactive, Pending, or Rejected).
8. Finally, submit the form to reserve your showcase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event you would like to reserve.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the store associated with the event.
* **Showcase Type** (`showcase_type`, text, required: false): Choose the type of showcase you would like to reserve.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required: false): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requirements, such as setup or equipment needs, enter them here.
* **Store Manager** (`store_manager`, text, required: false): Enter the store manager's name associated with the event.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the event (Active, Inactive, Pending, or Rejected).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter any assigned tool or equipment associated with the event.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): Enter any output file associated with the event.
* **Form ID** (`form_ids`, number, required: false): Enter a unique form ID.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter the category of the event.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Form Notes** (`form_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about the form.
* **User Comments** (`user_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback from users.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, required: false): Enter the version of the form.
* **Form Tags** (`form_tags`, text, required: false): Enter any tags or keywords associated with the form.
* **Form Created At** (`form_created_at`, date, required: false): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Form Updated At** (`form_updated_at`, date, required: false): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Form Created By** (`form_created_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Form Updated By** (`form_updated_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **Form Published At** (`form_published_at`, date, required: false): Enter the date the form was published.
