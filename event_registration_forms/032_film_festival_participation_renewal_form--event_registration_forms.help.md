# film-festival-participation-renewal-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to renew their film submission information for a film festival. The form is designed to collect essential details about the film being submitted, including title, genre, language, format, and more. Accurate and complete input is crucial for a smooth and successful submission process.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure a seamless submission experience, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review and correct any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Identifier (form-identifier)**:
	+ A unique identifier assigned to each submission.
	+ This field is not required, but it helps track and identify submissions.
* **Film Title (film-title)**:
	+ The title of the film being submitted.
	+ Enter the exact title as it appears on the film's poster or promotional materials.
* **Festival Name (festival-name)**:
	+ The name of the film festival where the film is being submitted.
	+ If the festival is not specified, enter 'N/A' or leave it blank.
* **Film Year (film-year)**:
	+ The release year of the film.
	+ Enter the exact year the film was released.
* **Genre (genre)**:
	+ The genre of the film (e.g., action, animation, comedy, drama).
	+ Select all applicable genres from the dropdown list.
* **Language (language)**:
	+ The language of the film.
	+ Select all applicable languages from the dropdown list.
* **Film Format (film-format)**:
	+ The format of the film (e.g., 2D, 3D, 4K, 8K).
	+ Select all applicable formats from the dropdown list.
* **Film Duration (film-duration)**:
	+ The length of the film in minutes.
	+ Enter the exact duration.
* **Festival Date (festival-date)**:
	+ The date of the film festival.
	+ Enter the exact date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Submission Status (submission-status)**:
	+ The current status of the film submission.
	+ Select 'Submitted', 'Pending', or 'Withdrawn' from the dropdown list.
* **Film File Link (film-file-link)**:
	+ A link to the film file.
	+ Enter the exact URL or leave it blank if not applicable.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check for errors before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the film festival's submission guidelines or contact their support team.
