# Community Movie Night Theme Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from community members about their preferences for a movie night event. This will help the event organizers create a successful and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name (if you'd like to be contacted regarding prizes) and email address (if you'd like to receive notifications).
2. Choose your preferred movie genres.
3. Suggest any specific movie titles you'd like to see featured.
4. Indicate your preferred date for the screening (or choose to prefer a weekend).
5. Select your seating preference (lawn seating, chair seating, or blankets).
6. Indicate if you'll be attending with family.
7. Let us know if you're interested in volunteering for the event (e.g., helping with setup or concessions).
8. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter name** (`voter_name`, optional, text): This field is for those who'd like to be contacted for prizes or notifications.
* **Email address** (`email`, optional, email): This field is for those who'd like to receive notifications and updates about the event.
* **Preferred movie genres** (`preferred_genres`, required, select_multiple): Choose all the movie genres you enjoy watching.
* **Specific movie titles suggested** (`specific_titles`, optional, text): Suggest any specific movie titles you'd like to see featured.
* **Preferred date for screening** (`preferred_date`, optional, text): Indicate your preferred date for the screening, or choose to prefer a weekend.
* **Seating preference** (`seating_preference`, optional, select_one): Select your preferred seating arrangement.
* **Attending with family** (`attend_with_family`, optional, select_one): Indicate if you'll be attending with family.
* **Interested in volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, optional, select_one): Let us know if you're interested in helping out with the event.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, optional, text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
