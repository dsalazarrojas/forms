# Summit Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summit Student Application Form is designed for students to register for a summit event that promotes academic or career development. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
5. Select your expected graduation date in the "Graduation Date" field.
6. Provide information about your academic program in the "Academic Program" field.
7. Upload any relevant files in the "Upload Files" field.
8. Review and finalize your submission by checking the form for any errors before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **School Name** (`School Name`, text, required: false): Enter your school's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Graduation Date** (`Graduation Date`, date, required: false): Select your expected graduation date from the available options.
* **Academic Program** (`Academic Program`, text, required: false): Provide information about your academic program or field of study.
* **Upload Files** (`Upload Files`, text, required: false): Upload any relevant files related to your academic program or background.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required: false): Review and finalize your submission by clicking this button.

## Tips
* Ensure you've filled out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the "Graduation Date" field for correct formatting (MM/DD/YYYY).
* If uploading files, ensure they're in a supported format (e.g., PDF, DOCX, etc.).
* Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button to avoid errors.
