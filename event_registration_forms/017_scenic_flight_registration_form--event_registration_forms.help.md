# Scenic Flight Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Scenic Flight Registration Form is designed to collect personal information and tour options from customers for scenic flights. Completing this form will help us prepare for their tour and provide the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information in the "Personal Info" section.
2. Select your preferred tour option in the "Tour Options" section.
3. Fill in the required details for your booking in the "Booking Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info (text)**: Enter your name and any other personal details as required to identify you. This information will help us tailor your experience.
* **Tour Options (select_one)**: Choose one of the available tour options that suits your preferences. This will allow us to prepare for your tour and provide the best possible experience.
* **Booking Details (text)**: Fill in the required details for your booking, such as date and time of your tour. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in the "Booking Details" section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us after completing the form.
