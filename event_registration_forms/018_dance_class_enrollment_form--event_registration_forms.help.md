# Dance Class Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dance Class Enrollment Form is a simple form designed for students to sign-up for dance classes. This guide will walk you through the different fields you'll encounter in the form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your details: 
   - Student Name: Your name, as a student.
   - Email: Your contact email for communication and updates.
   - Phone: Your contact number for any further questions or concerns.
   - Date: The date you are enrolling for the class.
   - Time: The time of the class you're enrolling in.
   - Address: Your address for any mailings or notifications.
   - Class Type: Choose a dance class type (Ballet, Tap, Jazz, Hip Hop, Contemporary, or Ballroom).
   - Class Level: Choose your level of expertise (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
   - Class Frequency: How often you want to attend the class (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
   - Payment Method: Choose how you plan to make the payment (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
   - Teacher: Your preferred teacher (if any).

2. Complete payment information:
   - Payment Amount: This is the amount to be paid for the class.
   - Payment Type: If you're paying full, part, or trial for the class.
   - Class Duration: Duration of the class.
   - Class Description: Description of the class (appears optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Fill in your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for updates and communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number for any further questions or concerns.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date for the class you want to attend.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time for the class you want to attend.
* **Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter your address for any mailings or notifications.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred dance class type.
* **Class Level** (`class_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of expertise.
* **Class Frequency** (`class_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you want to attend the class.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount to be paid for the class.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, required): Enter whether you're paying full, part, or trial for the class.
* **Class Duration** (`class_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of the class.
* **Class Description** (`class_description`, text, required): Enter a short description of the class.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about your enrollment.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments to be addressed to the teacher.
* **Student Preferences** (`student_preferences`, text, optional): Add any personal preferences.
* **Teacher Preferences** (`teacher_preferences`, text, optional): Add any personal preferences for your teacher.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): This will be provided upon enrollment.
* **Class Start Date** (`class_start_date`, date, optional): The date of the class start.
* **Class End Date** (`class_end_date`, date, optional): The date of the class end.
* **Class Location** (`class_location`, text, optional): The location of the class.
* **Class Notes** (`class_notes`, text, optional): Add any class notes.
* **Class Start Time** (`class_time`, time, optional): The start time of the class.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): This will display the payment status.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select to submit your form.
