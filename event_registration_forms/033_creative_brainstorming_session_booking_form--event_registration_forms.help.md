# Creative Brainstorming Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a Creative Brainstorming Session. Please fill out the form with your details to let us know you are interested in attending the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each of the fields as required. Please make sure to review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Enter the necessary details to identify yourself as a participant.
	+ This field is a text field where you can enter any relevant details about yourself as a participant.
* **Select Date & Time** (`date_time`): Choose a date and time that suits you best.
	+ Please select one of the available time slots from the dropdown menu.
* **Brainstorming Session Topic** (`brainstorming_session_topic`): Select one or more topics that interest you the most.
	+ Please choose one or more of the available topics that match your interests.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as it should be displayed for the session.
	+ Please enter your name as it should be displayed for the session.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter any relevant contact information that may be useful for the session organizers.
	+ Please enter any relevant contact information that may be useful for the session organizers.
* **Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ Please enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`): If applicable, enter the name of your company.
	+ Please enter the name of your company if applicable.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`): Choose the size of your team.
	+ Please choose the size of your team.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`): Enter any additional notes that may be useful for the session organizers.
	+ Please enter any additional notes that may be useful for the session organizers.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to select a date and time that works best for you.
* Choose one or more topics that match your interests.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please enter them in the Participant Notes field.
* Review your submission carefully before sending it in.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at [your contact email].
