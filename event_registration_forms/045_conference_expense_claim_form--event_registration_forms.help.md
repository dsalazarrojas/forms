# Conference Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Expense Claim Form is a form used to collect information about expenses submitted by conference attendees for reimbursement. It is an essential document for the finance team to process claims related to work-related trips.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Claimant name** (page 1) with your full name as it appears on your ID.
2. Enter your **Email address** (page 1) where you can be reached for communication related to your claim.
3. Select the **Expense date** (page 1) when the expense was incurred.
4. Choose the **Expense type** (page 1) from the options provided (e.g., Travel, Meals, Lodging, Materials, Other).
5. Enter the **Amount claimed** (page 1) in USD.
6. If applicable, upload or enter the **Receipt reference** (page 1) name or reference number.
7. Optionally, enter **Project code or account** (page 1) if applicable.
8. Add any **Travel details** (page 1) if relevant to your claim.
9. Leave **Approval status** (page 1) blank, as it is used by the finance team only.
10. Add any **Additional comments** (page 1) about your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant name**: This is the name of the person submitting the claim. Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email address**: This is the address where the finance team will communicate with you about your claim. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Expense date**: This is the date when the expense was incurred. Select the correct date from the calendar.
* **Expense type**: Choose the type of expense that best describes your claim (e.g., Travel, Meals, Lodging, Materials, Other).
* **Amount claimed**: Enter the total amount in USD you are claiming as reimbursement.
* **Receipt reference**: If you have a physical receipt or reference number for your expense, please upload or enter it.
* **Project code or account**: If your expense is related to a specific project, please enter the project code or account name.
* **Travel details**: If your claim includes travel expenses, please provide any relevant details about your trip.
* **Approval status**: This field is used by the finance team and should be left blank.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional notes or comments related to your claim.
