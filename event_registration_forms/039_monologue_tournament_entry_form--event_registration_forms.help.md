# monologue_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monologue_tournament_entry_form is a registration form for the monologue tournament. It is intended to collect essential information from the user for the competition.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a unique Form ID.
2. Provide your Name and email address.
3. (Optional) Enter your phone number.
4. Write a brief Monologue.
5. Select your Category.
6. Enter a description of your monologue.
7. Specify your submitted status and time.
8. Confirm the submitted status and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (form_id, Number, Required): This is a unique identifier for the form.
* **Name** (name, Text, Required): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (email, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Monologue** (monologue, Text, Required): Write a brief monologue for the competition.
* **Category** (Category, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the category for your monologue.
* **Description** (description, Note, Required): Briefly describe your monologue.
* **Submitted at** (submitted_at, Date, Required): Specify when you submitted the form.
* **Submitted by** (submitted_by, Select One, Required): Confirm whether you submitted this form.
* **Submitted time** (submitted_time, Time, Optional): Enter the time of submission (if you'd like to specify).
* **Submitted to** (submitted_to, Select One, Optional): Confirm who submitted this form to (if you'd like to specify).
* **Form submitted at** (form_submitted_at, Select One, Optional): Confirm when the form was submitted (if you'd like to specify).

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields.
- Review your selections before submitting the form.
- Use the provided options for 'Submitted by', 'Submitted to', and 'Form submitted at' to ensure correct submission status.
