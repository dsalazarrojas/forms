# Employee Social Gathering Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for social gatherings among employees, providing a platform for staff to connect, learn, and bond with each other.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form and start filling out the details as prompted.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review and edit your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: This field is for selecting the date of the event. Please choose the correct date from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Department**: Check if you belong to any department within the company. If unsure, please ask your HR representative.
* **Shift**: Indicate if you are willing to take any shifts during the event. Multiple shifts can be selected.
* **Role**: Briefly describe your role or position within the company.
* **Email**: Enter your official work email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number for easy contact during the event.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or remarks you'd like to share with the organizers.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you are attending (e.g., training, social gathering, etc.).
* **Event Status**: Indicate if the event is active or inactive in the calendar.
* **Event Description**: Briefly describe the purpose and details of the event.
* **Employee Attendance**: Check-in to let the organizer know if you are attending the event or not.
* **Event Start Date**: Select the start date of the event from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
* **Event End Date**: Select the end date of the event from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
* **Event Start Time**: Choose the start time of the event from the time picker.
* **Event End Time**: Choose the end time of the event from the time picker.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your official employee ID number for identification purposes.
* **Employee Number**: Enter your employee number as per the company's HR records.
* **Employee Status**: Indicate your current employee status within the company (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
