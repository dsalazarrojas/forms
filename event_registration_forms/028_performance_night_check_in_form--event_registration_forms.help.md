# performance_night_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "performance_night_check_in_form" is a form designed to collect participant information for a performance event, with a focus on shirt sales and event management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields, respectively.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field (optional).
4. Select your T-shirt Size and Shirt Color from the respective drop-down menus.
5. Choose your Shirt Style from the available options.
6. Enter the Event Name and Date of the event.
7. Select your preferred Volunteer Shift and Role (if applicable).
8. Choose your preferred T-shirt Designer and Printer (if applicable).
9. Select your preferred Event Manager and T-shirt Fulfillment option (if applicable).
10. Provide any Special Requests, Medical Information, and Emergency Contact details (if applicable).
11. If applicable, select your preferred Shirt Cost, Donation Amount, and Volunteer Shift for the event.
12. Finally, preview your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your valid email address to receive event updates and communications.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes (optional).
* **T-shirt Size (Select One)**: Choose your preferred T-shirt size from the available options (XS, S, M, L, XL).
* **T-shirt Color (Select Multiple)**: Select the T-shirt colors you prefer (White, Black, Red, Blue).
* **Shirt Style (Text)**: Enter your preferred T-shirt style.
* **Event Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the event (if applicable).
* **Time (Time)**: Enter the time of the event (if applicable).
* **Shirt Order Notes (Note)**: Enter any special notes for your shirt order (if applicable).
* **Special Requests (Select Multiple)**: Select any special requests you have (Food, Drink, Other).
* **Emergency Contact (Text)**: Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Number (Text)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Info (Note)**: Enter any medical information relevant to your event participation (if applicable).
* **Shirt Cost (Number)**: Enter the cost of the shirt you wish to purchase (if applicable).
* **Shirt Cost Paid (Number)**: Enter the amount paid for the shirt (if applicable).
* **Donation (Number)**: Enter a donation amount you wish to make (if applicable).
* **Donation Amount (Select One)**: Select the amount you wish to donate (5, 10, 20).
* **Volunteer Shift (Select Multiple)**: Select the volunteer shift you are available for (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Volunteer Role (Select One)**: Select your role as a volunteer (Event Lead, Registration, Volunteer).
* **T-shirt Designer (Select One)**: Select the T-shirt designer you prefer (Design A, Design B).
* **T-shirt Printer (Select One)**: Select the T-shirt printer you prefer (Printer A, Printer B).
* **Event Manager (Select One)**: Select the event manager you prefer (Manager A, Manager B).
* **T-shirt Fulfillment (Select One)**: Select the T-shirt fulfillment option you prefer (Fulfillment A, Fulfillment B).
