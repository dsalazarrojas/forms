# Session Participation Guidelines - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants regarding their understanding and agreement to the session participation guidelines. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the session participation guidelines carefully before filling out this form.
2. For each field, provide clear and accurate information about your understanding and agreement to the guidelines.
3. Please answer all fields that apply to you, or choose "No" if none apply.
4. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
5. If you have any questions or concerns about the guidelines, contact the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Participation Guidelines (1)** (`text`, required: false):
    This field asks you to provide a brief statement about your understanding of the session participation guidelines. Please type in a short paragraph explaining your understanding of the guidelines.
* **Participant Rules (2)** (`text`, required: false):
    This field is for you to explain any rules that apply specifically to you as a participant. Please be specific about any rules that you must follow during the session.
* **Event Consequences (3)** (`text`, required: false):
    In this field, please describe any consequences that may occur if you do not follow the participant rules or session participation guidelines.
* **Participant Consequences (4)** (`text`, required: false):
    This field is for you to outline any consequences that may occur if you do not follow the participant rules or session participation guidelines.
* **Event Prohibited Items (5)** (`text`, required: false):
    In this field, list any items that are prohibited during the event, such as electronic devices or food and drink items.
* **Participant Prohibited Items (6)** (`text`, required: false):
    List any items that are prohibited from participants, such as specific materials or equipment.
* **Session Prohibited Items (7)** (`text`, required: false):
    Describe any items that are prohibited within the session area, such as animals or hazardous materials.
* **Participant Agreement (8)** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
    Please choose "I agree" if you agree to the participant rules and guidelines, and choose "No" if you do not.
* **Event Agreement (9)** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
    Please choose "I agree" if you agree to the event rules and guidelines, and choose "No" if you do not.

## Tips
- Make sure to read all fields carefully before submitting the form.
- Answer all fields that apply to you, or choose "No" if none apply.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
