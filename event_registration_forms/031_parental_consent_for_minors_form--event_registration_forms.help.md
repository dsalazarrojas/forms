# Parental Consent For Minors Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for the consent of a parent or guardian for a minor to participate in various activities. It is essential to ensure that parents or guardians are aware of and agree to the terms of these activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the activities listed below and select the ones you would like to sign up for.
2. Complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the minor and yourself as the parent or guardian.
3. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Minor name** (`minor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the minor.
* **Parent name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Contact number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number for emergencies.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the minor's date of birth.
* **Parent address** (`parent_address`, text, required): Enter your address as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
