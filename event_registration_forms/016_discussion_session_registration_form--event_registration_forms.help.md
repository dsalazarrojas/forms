# discussion_session_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The discussion session registration form is designed to collect relevant details from participants to facilitate a productive and engaging discussion session. This form should not be used for internal purposes within the organization, so please ensure you only fill it out if you have an interest in attending an external discussion session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Name in the text field provided.
2. Enter your Participant Email address to ensure we can contact you with session details.
3. Enter your Participant Phone number for any further communication needs.
4. Provide a brief description of the Discussion Session Details in the text field.
5. Add any Participant Notes that you'd like to share with the session facilitators.
6. Select the Assigned Tool from the dropdown list that you'll be using during the session.
7. Enter the Event Date when you expect to participate in the session.
8. Enter your Participant Start Time when you expect to join the session.
9. Enter your Participant End Time when you expect to leave the session.
10. Enter the Participant Reg Date when you registered for the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Participant Name**: A text field where you can enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Email**: A field to enter your email address that we'll use to contact you about the session.
* **Participant Phone**: A field to enter your phone number for any further communication needs.
* **Discussion Session Details**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of the session you're interested in.
* **Participant Notes**: A field to add any personal notes that you'd like to share with the facilitators.
* **Assigned Tool**: A dropdown list to select the tool you'll be using during the session.
* **Event Date**: A field to enter the date when you expect to participate in the session.
* **Participant Start Time**: A field to enter the time when you expect to start participating in the session.
* **Participant End Time**: A field to enter the time when you expect to leave the session.
* **Participant Reg Date**: A field to enter the date when you registered for the session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to avoid any issues during session facilitation.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
