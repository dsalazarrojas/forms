# Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for contests or events. It collects personal, contact, and waiver agreement information, ensuring a smooth and efficient registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Registration Type**: Select the type of registration (Individual Participant, Team Entry (Leader Registration), or School/Organization Delegation).
2. **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your valid contact email address.
4. **Team Name (If Applicable)**: If you are registering as a team, enter the team name.
5. **Select Contest Event**: Choose the specific contest you are registering for.
6. **Age Group**: Select your age group from the options provided.
7. **Have you participated in our contests before?**: Answer whether you have participated in this event before.
8. **Emergency Contact Full Name**: Enter the name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
9. **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
10. **I agree to the liability waiver and contest terms**: Confirm your agreement to the waiver and contest terms.
11. **Date of Registration**: Select the date on which you are registering.
12. **Registrant/Guardian Digital Signature**: Type your full name as the registrant or guardian signature.
