# Art Gallery Painting Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about potential entries for the art gallery painting tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please fill out the following fields in the order presented. Each field is described in more detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Artist Full Name, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the gallery.
* **Email Address** (Artist Email, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Artist Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Short Artist Bio** (Artist Bio, text, required): Please provide a short bio (max 200 words) that explains your background and experience as an artist.
* **Select Competition Category** (Select Competition Category, select_one, required): Choose the category you fit into:
	+ Professional - Established
	+ Emerging Artist
	+ Student (High School - University)
	+ Amateur - Hobbyist
* **Primary Painting Medium** (Primary Painting Medium, select_one, required): Choose the primary medium you use for your art:
	+ Oil
	+ Acrylic
	+ Watercolor
	+ Mixed Media
	+ Digital Painting
* **Title of the Entry Piece** (Artwork Title, text, required): Enter the title of the specific artwork you are entering.
* **Dimensions** (Artwork Dimensions, text, required): Enter the dimensions of the artwork (H x W).
* **Entry Statement** (Artist Statement, text, required): Describe the concept or inspiration behind the specific piece.
* **Is this artwork available** (Artwork Availability, select_one, required): Confirm if the artwork is available for exhibition:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Image Rights Consent** (Image Rights Consent, select_one, required): Grant permission for the gallery to use images of your artwork for promotion:
	+ I Consent
	+ Other
* **Entry Fee Payment Status** (Entry Fee Status, select_one, required): Choose your payment status:
	+ Paid Online
	+ Will pay by phone
	+ Seeking Fee Waiver (Provide explanation below)
* **Fee Waiver Explanation** (Fee Waiver Explanation, text, optional): If you selected "Seeking Fee Waiver", explain your case for fee waiver.
* **Date of Submission** (Submission Date, date, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Digital Signature** (Artist Signature, text, required): Type your name for digital signature.
* **Link to Online Portfolio** (Portfolio Link, text, optional): If you have an online portfolio, provide the link for reference.
Note: The "Fee Waiver Explanation" field is optional and only appears if you choose "Seeking Fee Waiver" in the Entry Fee Payment Status field.
