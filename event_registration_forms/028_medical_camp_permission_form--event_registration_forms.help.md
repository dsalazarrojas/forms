# Medical Camp Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from participants regarding their medical history, emergency contact, and agreement to participate in a medical camp. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure your safety and comfort.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **General Information** section.
2. Provide your **Participant Information** in the corresponding field.
3. Answer the **Medical History** question truthfully.
4. Choose your **Medication List** carefully, as it will be used for medical purposes.
5. Indicate any **Allergies** you may have.
6. Choose your **Emergency Contact** and provide their **Contact Information**.
7. Confirm your **Emergency Contact Relationship** with the selected contact person.
8. Agree or disagree with the **Participant Agreement**.
9. If necessary, add any additional **Notes** for the camp staff to consider.
10. Finally, provide any further **Notes** if needed.
