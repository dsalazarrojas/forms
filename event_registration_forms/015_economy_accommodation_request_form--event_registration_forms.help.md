# economy_accommodation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request accommodation options for an event from travel agencies. It will help us to better understand your needs and preferences for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the check-in and check-out dates for your event.
2. Specify the room preferences (single or double room).
3. Select the accommodation type that suits your needs (Hotel, Hostel, Airbnb, or Guest House).
4. If you have any special requests, please specify them in the "Special Requests" field.
5. Provide the name of your travel agency.
6. Enter the name of the contact person for your agency.
7. Enter the contact email address for your agency.
8. Enter the contact phone number for your agency.
9. Provide the accommodation address.
10. Specify the city where the event will take place.
11. Specify the state where the event will take place.
12. Enter the zip code for the event location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you plan to check-in for your event. This will help us to ensure that we have a room available for you on that date.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you plan to check-out for your event.
* **Room Preferences** (`room_preferences`, text, required/optional): We have single or double rooms available. Please specify if you have any additional room preferences.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required): Select the accommodation type that suits your needs (Hotel, Hostel, Airbnb, or Guest House).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requests, please specify them here.
* **Travel Agency Name** (`travel_agency_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your travel agency.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your agency.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Accommodation Address** (`accommodation_address`, text, required/optional): Provide the address of the accommodation.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Specify the city where the event will take place.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Specify the state where the event will take place.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code for the event location.
