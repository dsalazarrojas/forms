# Smart Factory Solutions Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Factory Solutions Training Form is designed to collect information from participants who are registering for a training session. This form is intended for internal use within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:
1. **Select the training session information page**. This is the starting point of the form.
2. **Enter training session details**. Fill out the fields as required or relevant.
3. **Move to the next page** by clicking the "Next" button or using the navigation links.
4. **Complete the remaining pages** with the required information.
5. **Review and submit** the form once all fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Training Session Information (Page 1)
* **Training Session Information** (`form_1`, text, required: false): This field is for entering any relevant information about the training session.
* **Training Date and Time** (`form_2`, time, required: false): Enter the date and time of the training session.
* **Training Location** (`form_3`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the training session.

### Participant Information (Page 2)
* **Participant Information** (`form_4`, text, required: false): This field is for entering general participant information.
* **Participant Name** (`form_5`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`form_6`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`form_7`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Company** (`form_8`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's company.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Use the provided navigation links to move between pages.
* Review your completed form before submitting.
* If you need to make changes, use the "Back" button to move to the previous page.
