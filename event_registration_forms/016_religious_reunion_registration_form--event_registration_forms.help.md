# Religious Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Religious Reunion Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals planning to attend a Religious Reunion event. This form will help the event organizers track RSVPs, gather contact information, and get a sense of who will be attending.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your RSVP response by choosing one of the options in the "rsvp" field.
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "email" field. Please use the format "example@example.com" (no "Bcc" or "CC" is required).
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Choose a date for the event in the "event_date" field. You can select any date you like, as long as it is within the event timeframe.
7. Select the time for the event in the "event_time" field.
8. Enter the location where the event will take place in the "location" field.
9. If you would like to specify the number of attendees, please enter that number in the "attendees" field.
10. If you have any additional comments or information to share, type them in the "comments" field.
11. Enter the name of the organization or group you are representing (if applicable) in the "organization" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### rsvp
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_multiple, required): Select your RSVP response to let us know if you will be attending the event.

### first_name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.

### last_name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.

### email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".

### phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

### event_date
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the event.

### event_time
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time for the event.

### location
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the event will take place.

### attendees
* **Number of Attendees** (`attendees`, number, required): If you know how many people will be attending with you, please enter that number.

### comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.

### organization
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): If you are representing an organization, please enter the name of that organization.

Tips:
* Make sure to double-check your RSVP response to ensure it accurately reflects your availability.
* Be accurate when entering your contact information, as it will be used to communicate with you about the event.
* If you need to enter multiple attendees, please separate them with a comma, like "John, Jane, Bob".
