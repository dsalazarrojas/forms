# fitness-challenge-medal-design-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information for participants to order their medals and other swag from a fitness challenge event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Follow the instructions on this guide to fill up the form.
2.  Provide accurate and complete information for all required fields.
3.  Double-check your order before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it will be printed on your medal.
* **Medal Name** (`medal_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it will be printed on your medal.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify the number of medals you want to order.
* **Medal Color** (`medal_color`, select_one, required): Choose the color of your medal.
* **Medal Text** (`medal_text`, text, required): Enter text to be printed on your medal.
* **Medal Text Position** (`medal_text_position`, select_one, required): Choose where the text should be printed on the medal.
* **Ribbon Color** (`ribbon_color`, select_one, required): Choose the color of your ribbon.
* **Ribbon Text** (`ribbon_text`, text, required): Enter text to be printed on your ribbon.
* **Ribbon Text Position** (`ribbon_text_position`, select_one, required): Choose where the text should be printed on your ribbon.
* **Back of Medal Color** (`back_of_medal_color`, select_one, required): Choose the color of the back of your medal.
* **Back of Medal Text** (`back_of_medal_text`, text, required): Enter text to be printed on the back of your medal.
* **Back of Medal Text Position** (`back_of_medal_text_position`, select_one, required): Choose where the text should be printed on the back of your medal.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, required): Enter any additional information.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state name.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter your country name.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments about your order.
* **Back of Medal Shape** (`back_of_medal_shape`, select_one, required): Choose the shape of the back of your medal.
* **Participant T-Shirt Size** (`participant_t_shirt_size`, select_multiple, required): Choose your T-shirt size.
* **Participant Diet** (`participant_diet`, select_multiple, required): Choose your dietary preference.

Please note that some fields may have a limited set of options to choose from. Ensure you select the correct option for your needs. 

This guide is designed to be as clear and concise as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the event organizers.
