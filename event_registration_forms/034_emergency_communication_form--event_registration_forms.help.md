# Emergency Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information in case of an emergency, allowing users to provide critical details such as contact person names, relationships, and incident dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or support team for clarification.
3. Fill out the form completely and truthfully, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
4. Review the form for errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Information** (`emergency_communication_form`, Note, Optional):
  This field is a text box where you can provide any relevant information about the emergency situation.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, Text, Optional):
  Enter the name of the contact person who will be handling the emergency situation.
* **Contact Person Email** (`contact_person_email`, Email, Optional):
  Enter the email address of the contact person who will be handling the emergency situation.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Optional):
  Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Select Multiple, Optional):
  Select the relationship between the individual and the emergency contact person (e.g., Spouse, Partner, Family Member).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, Date, Optional):
  Enter the date of the incident or emergency situation.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, Time, Optional):
  Enter the time of the incident or emergency situation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out the form for a legitimate emergency situation.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or support team for clarification.
* This form is not intended for routine or general communication; it is specifically designed for emergency situations.
