# Paragliding Experience Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paragliding Experience Reservation Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients to reserve a paragliding experience. This form will help us understand their preferences and needs to provide the best possible experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of paragliding experience you are interested in (Adventure, Fun, or Relaxation)
2.  Choose the date and time you would like to reserve the experience
3.  Indicate the number of people who will be attending the experience
4.  Provide your contact email and phone number
5.  Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer)
6.  If you have a promotional code or coupon, enter it here
7.  Add any additional comments or notes about your reservation
8.  Confirm that your payment is complete or indicate that it is not paid yet

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Experience** (paragliding_experience): 
  Select the type of paragliding experience you are interested in. Options include Adventure, Fun, and Relaxation.
* **Date and Time** (date_and_time): 
  Enter the date and time you would like to reserve the experience. This is a date field, so you can enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Number of People** (number_of_people): 
  Indicate the number of people who will be attending the experience. Please note that this is not required information, but it's helpful for us to know how many people are coming.
* **Email** (email): 
  Enter your contact email address so we can keep in touch with you regarding your reservation.
* **Phone** (phone): 
  Provide your contact phone number in case we need to reach you directly.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method): 
  Select the payment method you prefer. Options include Credit Card, PayPal, and Bank Transfer.
* **Total Cost** (total_cost): 
  Please note that this field is not required, but if you know the total cost, you can enter it here.
* **Coupon** (coupon): 
  If you have a promotional code or coupon, enter it here to apply it to your reservation.
* **Comments** (comments): 
  Add any additional comments or notes about your reservation. This is optional but helpful for us to understand your needs.
* **Payment Status** (payment_status): 
  Confirm that your payment is complete or indicate that it is not paid yet.
* **Cancellation Terms** (cancellation_terms): 
  Note our cancellation policy, which is that [insert policy details here]. Please review this before making a reservation.
* **Confirmation** (confirmation): 
  Confirm that you have read and agree to our cancellation terms.
