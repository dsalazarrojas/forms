## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, ensuring it is in the correct format (e.g., `username@domain.com`).
4. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" field.
6. Agree to the payment terms by entering your acceptance in the "Payment Terms" field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete the registration process.
