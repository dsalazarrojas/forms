# Performance Analytics Certification Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration data from individuals or organizations for a Performance Analytics Certification program. It requires applicants to provide personal and contact information, as well as payment details to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, ensuring it is in the correct format (e.g., `username@domain.com`).
4. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" field.
6. Agree to the payment terms by entering your acceptance in the "Payment Terms" field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
 * **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.

### Last Name
 * **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.

### Email
 * **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in this format: `username@domain.com`.

### Payment Method
 * **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method: credit, pay, or invoice.

### Payment Info
 * **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required): Enter your payment details.

### Payment Terms
 * **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required): Accept the payment terms by entering your agreement in this field.

### Submit
 * **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click this button to complete the registration process.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
- Double-check your payment method and payment info before submitting the form.
- If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact `form@gic.mx` for assistance.
