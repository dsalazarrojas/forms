# guest-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The guest-registration-form is designed to collect information from guests attending an event. This form helps us understand their preferences and needs, ensuring we provide them with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the guest-registration-form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
5. Select your Arrival Date in the Arrival Date field.
6. Enter your Room Number in the Room Number field.
7. Choose your preferred Room Type (Standard, Deluxe, or Suite) in the Room Type field.
8. Select your preferred Room Rate (if applicable) in the Room Rate field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name. For example, John.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name. For example, Doe.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX XXXX (e.g., 123) 555 1234).
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Select the date of your arrival in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, required): Enter the room number you have reserved or would like to reserve.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred room type from the options: Standard, Deluxe, or Suite.
* **Room Rate** (`room_rate`, number, required): If applicable, select your preferred room rate (e.g., $XXX).
