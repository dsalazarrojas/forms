# Photography Photo Package Order Form 2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a Photography Photo Package Order Form 2, used to gather details about an event's photography package order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct package for your event.
2. Enter the event date and time.
3. Enter the event location.
4. Enter the photographer's name and contact information.
5. Enter the package price.
6. Enter any special requests or comments.
7. Choose the photo printing options (full color, full black and white, or none).
8. Select the delivery method (pickup, delivery, or shipping).
9. Enter any additional comments or instructions.
10. Sign and confirm the order.
11. Enter the package type (full, half, or 1/4).
12. Enter the package duration (1 hour, 2 hours, or 4 hours).
13. Enter any notes or instructions for the photographer.
14. Enter the client's name and contact information.
15. Confirm whether the client will sign the agreement.
16. Enter the payment method (cash, credit card, or bank transfer).
17. Enter any payment details.
18. Enter the payment amount.
19. Enter the event date (if applicable).
20. Enter the event time (if applicable).
21. Enter the event location (if applicable).
22. Enter the photographer's name (if applicable).
23. Confirm the signature of the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Package** (Select One): Select the package that best suits your event.
*   **Event Date** (Date): Enter the date of the event.
*   **Event Time** (Time): Enter the time of the event.
*   **Event Location** (Text): Enter the location of the event.
*   **Photographer Name** (Text): Enter the name of the photographer.
*   **Photographer Email** (Email): Enter the email of the photographer.
*   **Photographer Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the photographer.
*   **Package Price** (Number): Enter the price of the package.
*   **Package Details** (Text): Enter any special details about the package.
*   **Special Request** (Text): Enter any special requests for the photographer.
*   **Total Pages** (Select Multiple): Select whether the total number of pages is yes or no.
*   **Total Photos** (Select Multiple): Select whether the total number of photos is yes or no.
*   **Photo Printing** (Select One): Choose whether to print full color, full black and white, or none.
*   **Delivery Method** (Select One): Choose whether to pick up the photos, have them delivered, or shipped.
*   **Comments** (Text): Enter any additional comments or instructions.
*   **Signature** (Select One): Confirm whether to sign the agreement.
*   **Photo Size** (Select Multiple): Select the size of the photos.
*   **Package Type** (Select One): Select the type of package (full, half, or 1/4).
*   **Package Duration** (Select Multiple): Select the duration of the package (1 hour, 2 hours, or 4 hours).
*   **Photographer Notes** (Text): Enter any notes or instructions for the photographer.
*   **Client Name** (Text): Enter the client's name.
*   **Client Email** (Email): Enter the client's email.
*   **Client Phone** (Text): Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Client Signature** (Select One): Confirm whether the client will sign the agreement.
*   **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose the payment method (cash, credit card, or bank transfer).
*   **Payment Details** (Text): Enter any payment details.
*   **Payment Amount** (Number): Enter the payment amount.
*   **Event Date 2** (Date): Enter the date of the second event (if applicable).
*   **Event Time 2** (Time): Enter the time of the second event (if applicable).
*   **Event Location 2** (Text): Enter the location of the second event (if applicable).
*   **Photographer Name 2** (Text): Enter the name of the second photographer (if applicable).
