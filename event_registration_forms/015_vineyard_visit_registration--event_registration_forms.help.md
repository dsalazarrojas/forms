# Vineyard Visit Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vineyard Visit Registration form is used to collect information from visitors about their planned visit to the vineyard.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
3. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on any vineyard materials or communications.
* **Email** (Email, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted regarding your visit.
* **Phone** (Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (Date, Required/Optional: Optional): Select the date of your visit to the vineyard.
* **Time** (Time, Required/Optional: Optional): Select the time of your visit to the vineyard.
* **Length** (Number, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter the length of your visit in hours.
* **Tour Option 1** (Select One, Required/Optional: Optional): Select the type of tour you would like to take during your visit to the vineyard.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable visit.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please contact the vineyard staff in advance to arrange for accommodations.
* Review the vineyard's website or contact staff for any specific rules or guidelines regarding the vineyard visit.
