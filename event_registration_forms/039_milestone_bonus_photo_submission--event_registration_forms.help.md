# Milestone Bonus Photo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Milestone Bonus Photo Submission form is designed to collect data from individuals participating in a milestone achievement event. This form is used to gather relevant information about the event, including photos and supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

To submit your photo and related information, follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of your photo.
2. Enter a brief description of your milestone achievement.
3. Select the date the photo was taken.
4. If necessary, upload any supporting files related to the event.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Form Data** (`event_registration_form_data`, text, not required): This field is for providing any additional information about the event, such as event details or context.
* **Photo Title** (`photo_title`, text, not required): Enter a descriptive title for your photo.
* **Milestone Achievement Description** (`milestone_achievement_description`, text, not required): Describe the significance or context of your milestone achievement.
* **Photo Date** (`photo_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Event Registration Forms File Uploads** (`event_registration_forms_file_upload`, text, not required): Upload any supporting files related to the event.
* **Photo Uploads** (`photo_uploads`, text, not required): Upload the photo you would like to submit.
* **Event Registration Forms Multiple File Uploads** (`event_registration_forms_file_upload_multiple`, text, not required): This field is similar to the previous one, but allows for multiple uploads.
