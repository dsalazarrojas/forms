# Cross Functional Management Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users for a Cross Functional Management Workshop. It helps us to gather information from users about their preferred workshop option, date, time, and any additional notes they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details in the User field.
2. Select your preferred workshop option from the Workshop dropdown list.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Add any additional notes in the Note field, if needed.
6. Select your preferred date and time in the Date and Time fields.
7. Choose any assigned tool from the Assigned Tool dropdown list.
8. Select one or more categories from the Category dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User** (`1`, `text`, not required): Enter your name or ID to identify yourself in the system.
* **Workshop** (`2`, `select_one`, not required): Select your preferred workshop option from the list. This is where you choose the workshop you want to attend.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note** (`5`, `note`, not required): Add any additional notes about your registration, such as special requests or preferences.
* **Date** (`6`, `date`, not required): Select your preferred date to attend the workshop.
* **Time** (`7`, `time`, not required): Select your preferred time to attend the workshop.
* **Assigned Tool** (`8`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the tool assigned to you for this workshop.
* **Category** (`9`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more categories that you belong to, so we can provide more targeted information and resources to you.
