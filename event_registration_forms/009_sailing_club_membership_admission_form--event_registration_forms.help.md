# Sailing Club Membership Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for new members to register for sailing club membership. It collects information about the applicant's sailing experience, contact details, and membership preferences. The form helps the club administrators to understand the applicant's background and provide a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your new member information, including your name and contact details.
2.  Select your sailing experience from the options provided.
3.  Choose how often you plan to sail with us.
4.  Choose the type of membership you would like to have.
5.  Provide emergency contact information.
6.  Select your relationship with the emergency contact.
7.  Enter additional information about yourself, if necessary.
8.  Add any notes you'd like to share with us.
9.  Select your current sailing club memberships.
10. Choose your sailing equipment.
11. Select the size and type of your sailing boat.
12. Choose the color of your sailing boat.
13. Confirm if you hold a sailing license.
14. Enter your sailing license number (if applicable).
15. Choose the type of sailing license you hold.
16. Enter the address of your current sailing club.
17. Enter the phone number of your current sailing club.
18. Enter the email address of your current sailing club.
19. Enter the website of your current sailing club.
20. Add any additional information about your sailing club (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Member Information (1)** (`New Member Information`, `text`, required/optional):
    Enter your name and any other relevant details to help us get started.
*   **Contact Info (2)** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional):
    Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
*   **Sailing Experience (3)** (`Sailing Experience`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Select how you're connected to sailing, such as being a member of another sailing club.
*   **Sailing Frequency (4)** (`Sailing Frequency`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Choose how often you plan to sail with us.
*   **Membership Type (5)** (`Membership Type`, `select_one`, required/optional):
    Choose the type of membership you'd like to have, such as individual or family.
*   **Emergency Contact (6)** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required/optional):
    Provide the name and contact information of someone we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
*   **Relationship (7)** (`Relationship`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Choose your relationship with the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Info (8)** (`Emergency Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional):
    Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.
*   **Additional Info (9)** (`Additional Info`, `text`, required/optional):
    Enter any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant.
*   **Notes (10)** (`Notes`, `text`, required/optional):
    Add any notes you'd like to share with us.
*   **Sailing Club Memberships (11)** (`Sailing Club Memberships`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Select any other sailing clubs you are a member of.
*   **Sailing Equipment (12)** (`Sailing Equipment`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Choose the type of sailing equipment you have.
*   **Sailing Boat (13)** (`Sailing Boat`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Select the size of your sailing boat.
*   **Sailing Boat Type (14)** (`Sailing Boat Type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Choose the type of sailing boat you have.
*   **Sailing Boat Color (15)** (`Sailing Boat Color`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Select the color of your sailing boat.
*   **Sailing License (16)** (`Sailing License`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Confirm if you hold a sailing license.
*   **Sailing License Number (17)** (`Sailing License Number`, `text`, required/optional):
    Enter your sailing license number (if applicable).
*   **Sailing License Type (18)** (`Sailing License Type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
    Choose the type of sailing license you hold.
*   **Sailing Club Address (19)** (`Sailing Club Address`, `text`, required/optional):
    Enter the address of your current sailing club.
*   **Sailing Club Phone (20)** (`Sailing Club Phone`, `text`, required/optional):
    Enter the phone number of your current sailing club.
*   **Sailing Club Email (21)** (`Sailing Club Email`, `email`, required/optional):
    Enter the email address of your current sailing club.
*   **Sailing Club Website (22)** (`Sailing Club Website`, `text`, required/optional):
    Enter the website of your current sailing club.
*   **Additional Info Sailing Club (23)** (`Additional Info Sailing Club`, `text`, required/optional):
    Enter any additional information about your sailing club.
*   **Submit (24)** (`Submit`, `text`, required/optional):
    Press this button to submit your application.

**Tips**:

*   Please be honest and accurate when filling in your sailing experience and equipment.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
*   We appreciate any additional information you can provide to help us get you settled in as a member.
