# Invitation Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for an event and provide your details for invitation and potential attendance tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the necessary information. You can leave fields blank if they are not required or applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Provide the date of the event you are registering for. 
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time you plan to attend the event.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name for the event registration.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your registration.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Invitation Preferences** (`invitation_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to receive an invitation. Select one or more options from the list: "Yes, send me an invite", "Only, if I am confirmed", or "No invitation". 

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays or errors in invitation and event management.
* If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or contact the event organizers for clarification. 
* This form is for registration purposes only and is not intended for public viewing.
