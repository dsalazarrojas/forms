# Sponsor Briefing Breakfast RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Briefing Breakfast RSVP Form is used to register sponsors who are attending the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Event Name**, which is the name of the event you are attending.
2. Enter the **Event Date** for reference purposes.
3. Share any **Breakfast Details** about the event, if necessary.
4. Select your **Dietary Preferences** from the available options (Vegetarian, Vegan, or Other).
5. Enter your **Contact Details**, including any relevant information.
6. Fill in your **Name** and **Email** for registration purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dietary Preferences (Required)**: This field asks you to select your dietary preference for the event.
* **Event Date (Not Required)**: This field is for reference purposes, but you can ignore it if not necessary.
* **Breakfast Details (Not Required)**: This field is for sharing any important details about the breakfast event.
* **Contact Details (Not Required)**: This field is for providing any relevant contact information.
* **Name (Not Required)**: This field is for entering your name for registration purposes.
* **Email (Not Required)**: This field is for entering your email address.
* **Event Name (Not Required)**: This field is for entering the name of the event you are attending.
* **Sponsor Briefing Breakfast RSVP Form (Not Required)**: This is just the title of the form, you can ignore it.
