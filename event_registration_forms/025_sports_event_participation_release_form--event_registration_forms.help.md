# sports_event_participation_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports event participation release form is a document that requires participants to acknowledge the inherent risks involved in sports activities and to provide essential information about themselves, such as medical conditions and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your Participant Information, including your name and contact details.
2. Acknowledge that you understand the risks involved in participating in sports activities.
3. Select the emergency contact person from the list provided.
4. Provide your contact information, including phone and email.
5. Declare any medical conditions you may have.
6. Attach any medical certificate as required.
7. Provide additional medical information if necessary.
8. Sign the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (1)** (`participant_information`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide your basic information, such as your name and contact details.
* **Risk Acknowledgment (2)** (`risk_acknowledgment`, `note`, optional): This is a statement to acknowledge that you understand the risks involved in participating in sports activities.
* **I Consent (3)** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the statements below that apply to you:
	+ I understand that participating in sports activities involves inherent risks.
	+ I acknowledge that I am aware of these risks and am willing to participate.
	+ I understand that the risks cannot be eliminated and that I am responsible for my own safety.
* **Emergency Contact (4)** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Select the emergency contact person from the list provided.
* **Contact Information (5)** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide your contact information, such as phone and email.
* **Participant Declaration (6)** (`participant_declaration`, `text`, optional): This is a statement to declare your participation in the sports event.
* **Signature (7)** (`signature`, `text`, optional): Please sign the form to confirm your participation.
* **Date Signed (8)** (`date_signed`, `date`, optional): Please provide the date you signed the form.
* **Date of Birth (9)** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): This is the date of your birth.
* **Address (10)** (`address`, `text`, optional): This is your home address.
* **Phone (11)** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is your phone number.
* **E-mail (12)** (`email`, `email`, optional): This is your email address.
* **Medical Information (13)** (`medical_information`, `text`, optional): This is a field for you to declare any medical conditions you may have.
* **Participant Agreement (14)** (`participant_agreement`, `text`, optional): This is a statement for you to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Parent or Guardian (15)** (`parent_or_guardian`, `text`, optional): This is a field for you to declare if you are a parent or guardian of a participant.
* **Emergency Contact Person (16)** (`emergency_contact_person`, `text`, optional): This is a field for you to declare the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (17)** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): This is the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Participant (18)** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): This is the relationship between you and the participant.
* **Participant Occupation (19)** (`participant_occupation`, `text`, optional): This is your occupation.
* **Emergency Contact Employment (20)** (`emergency_contact_employment`, `text`, optional): This is the employment status of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition (21)** (`participant_medical_condition`, `text`, optional): This is a field for you to declare any medical conditions you may have.
* **Medical Certificate (22)** (`medical_certificate`, `text`, optional): This is a field for you to attach any medical certificate.
* **Medical Certificate Type (23)** (`medical_certificate_type`, `text`, optional): This is the type of medical certificate.
* **Medical Certificate Date (24)** (`medical_certificate_date`, `date`, optional): This is the date of the medical certificate.
* **Medical Information Additional (25)** (`medical_information_additional`, `text`, optional): This is a field for you to provide any additional medical information.
