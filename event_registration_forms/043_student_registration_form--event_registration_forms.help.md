# Student Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Registration Form is designed to collect demographic information from students for academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required personal information fields:
	* First and Last Name: Enter your full name, including First and Last name.
	* Email: Enter your official email address.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number.
	* Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
	* Student ID: Enter your student ID number.
2. Select your Student Program:
	* Choose one of the available programs (High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, or Doctorate).
3. If applicable, select other programs:
	* If you are enrolled in a program not listed above, select "Other Program" and choose the relevant program type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First and Last Name**: Enter your full name including First and Last name.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Program**: Choose one of the available programs (High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, or Doctorate).
* **Other Program**: If you select "Other Program", choose a relevant program type (High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, or Doctorate) that is not already listed above. This is an optional field and should only be filled out if applicable.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you started your program.
