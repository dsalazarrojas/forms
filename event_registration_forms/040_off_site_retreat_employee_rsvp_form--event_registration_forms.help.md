# off_site_retreat_employee_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to register for an off-site retreat. It is used to gather information about the employee's details, attendance, and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee details in the "Employee Details" section.
2. Indicate your attendance for the event by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Attendance" section.
3. If you will be checking in at the event, select your checkin time in the "Checkin Time" section.
4. If you will be checking out at the event, select your checkout time in the "Checkout Time" section.
5. Choose your accommodation preference in the "Accommodation" section.
6. Select your meal preferences in the "Meal Preferences" section. You can select one or more options that apply to you.
7. If you have any special requests for the event, type them in the "Special Requests" section.
8. Add any comments about the event or your registration in the "Comments" section.
9. Confirm your RSVP in the "RSVP" section by selecting "Confirmed", "Tentative", or "No Show".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details**: Enter your name, job title, and email address to help us identify you and communicate with you about the event.
* **Attendance**: Select "True" if you will attend the event, and "False" if you will not.
* **Checkin Time**: If you will be checking in, select the time you plan to arrive.
* **Checkout Time**: If you will be checking out, select the time you plan to leave.
* **Accommodation**: Choose where you would like to stay for the event. You can choose to stay on-site or off-site, or both.
* **Meal Preferences**: Select which meals you would like to have during the event. You can choose to have breakfast, lunch, and/or dinner.
* **Special Requests**: Type any special requests or requirements you have for the event. For example, dietary restrictions or special equipment needed.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or thoughts about the event or your registration.
* **RSVP**: Confirm your RSVP by selecting "Confirmed", "Tentative", or "No Show". 

# Tips
* Please ensure you are accurate when answering the "Attendance" question to avoid confusion.
* Make sure to select all relevant meal preferences to ensure you receive the correct meals.
* If you have any special requests, please type them clearly and completely in the "Special Requests" section.
* Use the "Comments" section to add any additional information that may be helpful for the event organizers.
* Confirm your RSVP by selecting the correct option in the "RSVP" section.
