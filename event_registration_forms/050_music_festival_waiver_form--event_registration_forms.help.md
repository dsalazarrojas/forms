# Music Festival Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Festival Waiver Form is a document that must be filled out by participants or parents/guardians to sign a waiver for the music festival. It's an essential step to ensure that all participants are covered by the necessary agreements before attending the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal information:
	* Participant Name: Enter the name of the participant as it appears on their government-issued ID or school records.
	* Email: Enter the participant's email address.
	* Phone: Enter the participant's contact phone number.
2. Enter the parent/guardian's information (if applicable):
	* Parent Name: If you are a parent or guardian, enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or school records.
	* Parent Address: Enter your home address, including street address and city.
	* Parent Phone: Enter your contact phone number.
3. Sign the waiver:
	* Waiver Signature: Sign the waiver to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the festival's rules and regulations.
4. Select the emergency contact:
	* Emergency Contact: Choose who the participant's emergency contact is (Parent 1, Parent 2, or Emergency Contact Person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant as it appears on their government-issued ID or school records.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the participant's contact phone number.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): If you are a parent or guardian, enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or school records.
* **Parent Address** (`parent_address`, text, optional): If you are a parent or guardian, enter your home address, including street address and city.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): If you are a parent or guardian, enter your contact phone number.
* **Waiver Signature** (`waiver_signature`, note, optional): Sign the waiver to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the festival's rules and regulations.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select multiple, optional): Choose who the participant's emergency contact is (Parent 1, Parent 2, or Emergency Contact Person).
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are a parent or guardian, please ensure that the participant has a way to contact you in case of an emergency.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the festival organizers or event administrators for help.
