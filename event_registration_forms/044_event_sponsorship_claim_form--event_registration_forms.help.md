# Event Sponsorship Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event organizers about their sponsorship claims for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the event in the "Event Title" field.
2. Select the category of the event from the "Category" dropdown menu (Sponsors, Vendors, or Other).
3. Provide a brief description of the event in the "Description" field.
4. If you have any supporting documents or files, upload them in the "File" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the event in the respective fields.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Sponsorship Claim Form** (`event_sponsorship_claim_form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event.
* **Category** (`event_sponsorship_claim_form_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the event (Sponsors, Vendors, or Other).
* **Description** (`event_sponsorship_claim_form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **File** (`event_sponsorship_claim_form_file`, text, optional): If you have any supporting documents or files, upload them here.
* **Date of Event** (`event_sponsorship_claim_form_date_1`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time of Event** (`event_sponsorship_claim_form_time_1`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event in the format `HH:MM` or `HH:MM AM/PM`.
