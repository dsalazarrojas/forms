# Wedding Categorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Categorization Form is used to collect information about a wedding event, allowing users to provide details about the wedding catering quotation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the correct page and field for the information you want to input.
3. Fill out each field with the required and optional information.
4. Review your input to ensure accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guests** (`wedding_catering_quotation_form_guests`, number): Enter the number of guests attending the wedding.
* **Guest Count** (`wedding_catering_quotation_form_guest_count`, number): Enter the number of guests, which will be used to estimate the catering needs.
* **Guest Type** (`wedding_catering_quotation_form_guest_type`, select multiple): Choose the type of guests attending the wedding, such as Guest, Family, Staff, or Other.
* **Meal Plan** (`wedding_catering_quotation_form_meal_plan`, select one): Select the meal plan that suits your needs, such as Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`wedding_catering_quotation_form_dietary_restrictions`, select multiple): Choose any dietary restrictions that apply to your guests, such as No Restrictions, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, or Other.
* **Special Requests** (`wedding_catering_quotation_form_special_requests`, note): Enter any special requests or instructions for the catering staff.
* **Budget** (`wedding_catering_quotation_form_budget`, number): Enter your budget for the catering services.
* **Payment Terms** (`wedding_catering_quotation_form_payment_terms`, note): Enter any payment terms or conditions that apply to your account.

## Tips
* Double-check your inputs for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the wedding planners for assistance.
