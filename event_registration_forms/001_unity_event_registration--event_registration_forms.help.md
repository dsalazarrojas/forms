# Unity Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for events hosted by Unity, providing necessary information to ensure a comfortable and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the registration process.
2. Fill out the registration form, which consists of three sections:
	* Registration Info: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information.
	* Session Interests: Select the interests that you are interested in for the event.
	* Accessibility Info: If you have any accessibility needs, please provide information to help us accommodate you.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Info** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the interests that you're interested in for the event. This will help us tailor the sessions to your preferences.
* **Accessibility Info** (`accessibility_info`, `text`, required: false): If you have any accessibility needs, please provide information so we can accommodate you.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing accessibility information to ensure we can meet your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at [contact email/phone number].
