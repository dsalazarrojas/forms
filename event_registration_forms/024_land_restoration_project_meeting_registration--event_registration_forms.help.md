# Land Restoration Project Meeting Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals to register for the Land Restoration Project meeting. The meeting is a platform for discussion, knowledge sharing, and planning among conservation team members, NGO partners, and community members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out as much or as little information as you need**. The form is divided into different sections, and each field is optional, but please note that some fields are marked as required.
2. **Provide accurate and truthful information**. Make sure to enter your correct email address and select your role accurately.
3. **Choose your preferred meeting time** from the available options.
4. **Select a meeting date, if applicable**.
5. **Submit your registration** to secure your spot in the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Data** (Text Field, Optional):
	+ A brief description of yourself, your organization, or your affiliation (if any).
* **Select Role** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose your role in the project:
		- **Conservation Team Member**: If you're part of the project team responsible for conservation efforts.
		- **NGO Partner**: If you're a partner organization working with the project.
		- **Community Member**: If you're a community representative or involved in community outreach.
		- **Other**: If you have a different role or affiliation.
* **Preferred Meeting Time** (Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Select your preferred meeting time:
		- **600**: Meeting time is 6:00 AM (not recommended for most users)
		- **660**: Meeting time is 6:60 AM
		- **780**: Meeting time is 7:40 AM
		- **840**: Meeting time is 8:40 AM
* **Meeting Date** (Date Field, Optional):
	+ Select the date you're interested in attending the meeting (if applicable).
* **Email** (Email Field, Optional):
	+ Enter your email address for communication and updates.
