# Theater Audience Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
Theater Audience Check In Form is designed to collect information from theater audience members for events. This form helps theater staff to track audience attendance and ensure accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your **email address** in the "Email" field. This is used for communication with the theater staff.
4. Enter the **Date** of your visit in the "Date" field.
5. Enter the **Time** of your visit in the "Time" field.
6. Enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone" field. This is optional.
7. If you require any **Accessibility assistance**, describe your needs in the "Accessibility" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that the theater staff can use to contact you.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your visit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for any reason.
* **Accessibility**: If you have any accessibility needs, describe them in detail so that the theater staff can provide assistance.
