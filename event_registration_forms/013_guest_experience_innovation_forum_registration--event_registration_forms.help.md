# Guest Experience Innovation Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering guests interested in participating in the Guest Experience Innovation Forum. It helps us understand their interests, special requirements, and other details to provide a better experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Registration Form** on the first page.
2. Provide your **Attendee Details** on the second page.
3. Select your **Session Interests** on the third page.
4. If you have any **Special Requirements**, mention them on the fourth page.
5. Finally, review and submit your form on the fifth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`first_page`, text, required: false):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you can provide your basic registration information.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This is where you can provide additional details about yourself as an attendee.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose the sessions you are interested in attending by selecting the corresponding options.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, note, required: false):
	+ If you have any special requirements or needs, please mention them here so we can provide necessary arrangements.
* **Submit and Preview** (`submit_and_preview`, text, required: false):
	+ Review and submit your form before proceeding.
