# Exhibition Booth Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture exhibition booth payment information from exhibitors. It ensures that all relevant details are provided to process payments accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Exhibitor Details**: Fill out the exhibitor's name and other relevant contact information.
2. **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Check.
3. **Payment Amount**: Enter the exact amount to be paid for the booth.
4. **Payment Terms**: Choose one of the payment terms: 30 Day, 60 Day, or 90 Day payment terms.
5. **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Exhibitor Details**: 
  - **Exhibitor Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization that will be occupying the booth.
  - **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the exhibit.
  - **Booth Location**: Enter the number of the booth assigned to the exhibitor.

- **Payment Method**: 
  - **Please select one of the payment methods provided (Cash, Credit Card, or Check)**. This will determine how you will be invoiced and paid.

- **Payment Amount**: 
  - **Enter the exact amount to be paid for the booth, including any applicable taxes or fees**. This amount will be used to calculate the final invoice.

- **Payment Terms**: 
  - **Choose one of the payment terms: 30 Day, 60 Day, or 90 Day payment terms**. This will determine the payment deadline and any applicable late fees.

- **Additional Comments**: 
  - **Provide any additional comments or questions you have about the payment or exhibit**. This will help us to clarify any issues and ensure a smooth process.
