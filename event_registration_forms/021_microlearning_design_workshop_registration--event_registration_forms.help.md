# Microlearning Design Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microlearning Design Workshop Registration form is designed for attendees to register for a "Microlearning Design Workshop" event. The form collects information about the attendees, their session preferences, and any requirements they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Attendee Details" page with your name, email address, and any other details as required.
2. Move on to the "Session Preferences" page and select your preferred session options.
3. If you have any special requirements, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, please list them on the "Requirements" page.
4. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details (Page 1)** (`Attendee Details`, text, required/optional): This page is where you'll enter your name and email address. Please provide your name as it appears on your identification or business card. Your email address will be used to send you updates and notifications about the workshop.
* **Session Preferences (Page 2)** (`Session Preferences`, text, required/optional): This page allows you to select your preferred session options. Please choose one or more of the sessions that interest you the most.
* **Requirements (Page 3)** (`Requirements`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requirements, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, please list them here. This will help us provide a comfortable and enjoyable experience for you at the workshop.

## Tips

* Please be sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* We look forward to seeing you at the workshop!
