# photography_walk_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a photography walk event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide your participant information in the "Participant Info" section.
3. Select your attendance options for the photography walk in the "Attending Photography Walk" section.
4. Enter the date and time of the photography walk in the "Photography Walk Date" and "Photography Walk Time" fields.
5. Enter the location of the photography walk in the "Photography Walk Location" field.
6. Enter any special requests or comments in the "Special Requests" and "Comments" fields.
7. Select your RSVP option in the "RSVP" field.
8. Enter any additional event details in the "Event Details" field.
9. Provide your consent for photo release in the "Photo Release" field.
10. Finally, select any photo shooters who will be attending the event in the "Photo Shooters" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info (participant_info)**: This section is where you enter your basic participant information.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be used for the event.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with event details.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter a phone number where we can contact you if needed.
* **Attending Photography Walk (attending_photography_walk)**: Select the dates and times you are attending the event.
* **Photography Walk Date (photography_walk_date)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Photography Walk Time (photography_walk_time)**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Photography Walk Location (photography_walk_location)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Special Requests (special_requests)**: Enter any special requests or requests for accommodations.
* **Comments (comments)**: Enter any comments or feedback.
* **RSVP (rsvp)**: Select your RSVP option.
* **Event Details (event_details)**: Enter any additional event details.
* **Notes (notes)**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Photo Release (photo_release)**: Check the box to consent to photo release.
* **Photo Shooters (photo_shooters)**: Select any photo shooters attending the event.
