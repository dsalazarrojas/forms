# Principal Leadership Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Principal Leadership Award Nomination Form is used to nominate a principal for an award. This form gathers information about the nominee, including their school, title, department, and position, as well as the nominator's contact information and the reason for the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, select the nomination by choosing the school from the provided options.
2. Fill in the Nomination Form with a brief description of why the principal deserves the award.
3. Enter the principal's name, email address, phone number, years of serving, and other details.
4. Provide the nominator's contact information, including name, email address, phone number, job title, department, and position (if applicable).
5. Finally, submit the form to complete the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination**: (Required, Select Multiple)
    * Choose the school from the list of options that the principal is associated with.
* **Nomination Form**: (Required, Text)
    * Provide a brief description of why the principal deserves the award.
* **Name of Principal**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the full name of the principal being nominated.
* **Email Address**: (Optional, Email)
    * Enter the email address of the principal, if available.
* **Phone Number**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the phone number of the principal, if available.
* **Years of Serving**: (Required, Number)
    * Enter the number of years the principal has been serving in their position.
* **Nomination Reason**: (Required, Text)
    * Provide a brief description of why the principal deserves the award.
* **Email of Nominee**: (Required, Email)
    * Enter the email address of the principal being nominated.
* **School**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the name of the school the principal is associated with.
* **Title**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the title of the principal, such as "Director" or "Dean".
* **Department**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the department the principal is associated with.
* **Position**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the principal's position or role.
* **Supervisor**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the supervisor or mentor of the principal.
* **Supervisor Title**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the title of the supervisor or mentor.
* **Supervisor Email**: (Required, Email)
    * Enter the email address of the supervisor or mentor.
* **Supervisor Phone**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the phone number of the supervisor or mentor.
* **Supervisor Department**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the department of the supervisor or mentor.
* **Supervisor Position**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter the position or role of the supervisor or mentor.
* **Nominator's Name**: (Required, Text)
    * Enter your name and the name of the nominator.
* **Nominator's Email**: (Required, Email)
    * Enter your email address.
* **Nominator's Phone**: (Optional, Text)
    * Enter your phone number, if available.
* **Nominator's Job Title**: (Optional, Text)
    * Enter your job title.
* **Nominator's Department**: (Optional, Text)
    * Enter your department.
* **Nominator's Position**: (Optional, Text)
    * Enter your position or role.
