# Startup Exhibition Booth Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for startups to register for an exhibition. It gathers essential information about the company and its exhibition preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your booth preferences in the "Booth Preferences" section.
2.  Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
3.  Provide the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field.
4.  Enter your company email in the "Email" field. **This field is required**.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6.  Choose your booth size from the "Booth Size" dropdown menu.
7.  Select the booth location from the "Booth Location" dropdown menu.
8.  Enter the type of booth you require in the "Booth Type" field.
9.  Enter your company type in the "Company Type" field.
10.  Provide your company website in the "Website" field.
11.  Describe your company in the "Description" field.
12.  Upload a logo for your company in the "Logo" field.
13.  Upload any additional files required for the exhibition in the corresponding "File" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booth Preferences**: Describe your booth preferences.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name of the contact person.
* **Email**: Enter your company email. 
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Booth Size**: Choose your preferred booth size.
* **Booth Location**: Select your preferred booth location.
* **Booth Type**: Enter the type of booth you require.
* **Company Type**: Enter your company type.
* **Website**: Enter your company website.
* **Description**: Describe your company.
* **Logo**: Upload your company logo.
* **File 1 - 13**: Upload any additional files required for the exhibition.
