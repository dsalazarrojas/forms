# HR Inclusion Leadership Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects registration details, session interests, accessibility needs, and additional notes from participants attending the HR Inclusion Leadership Forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Registration Details** on the first page.
	* Enter your name and email address accurately.
	* Ensure your contact information is up-to-date and complete.
2. Select your **Session Interests** on the second page.
	* Choose one or more topics that align with your interests.
	* Be specific about what sessions you're interested in, so we can tailor your experience.
3. Provide any **Accessibility Needs** you may have.
	* If you need any accommodations or support, let us know.
	* We'll do our best to ensure your comfort and accessibility during the forum.
4. Add any **Additional Notes**.
	* Share any relevant information about your experience or background.
	* This helps us understand your needs and preferences better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (`name`, `string`, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the forum and your email address for communication purposes.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, `string`, required): Choose one or more topics you're interested in to help us tailor the sessions and activities to your needs.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `string`, optional): If you need any accommodations or support, let us know so we can ensure your comfort and accessibility during the forum.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant information about your experience or background to help us understand your needs and preferences better.
