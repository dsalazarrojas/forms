# Zipline Activity Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zipline Activity Liability Waiver Form is a document used to collect information from participants in activities that may involve some level of risk. The form aims to identify potential risks and gather emergency contact information in case of an accident or emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct participant information (Participant 1, 2, or 3).
2.  Choose the level of risk involved in the activity (High, Low, or Not Applicable).
3.  Select any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in the activity.
4.  Provide accurate emergency contact information, including name, phone number, address, and relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Select the participant's name that this information applies to.
* **Email**: Please enter a valid email address to receive important activity updates and notifications.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select who the emergency contact will be (Parent/Guardian or Other).
* **Address**: Enter the participant's address.
* **City**: Enter the participant's city.
* **State**: Enter the participant's state.
* **Zip**: Enter the participant's zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the participant and emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Re-enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Physical Activity**: Select the level of risk involved in the activity (High, Low, or Not Applicable).
* **Medical Information**: Select any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in this activity.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in this activity?**: Select "True" if you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate.
* **Do you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in this activity?**: Select "True" if you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate.
* **Physical Activity Limitation**: Select "True" if there are any physical activities that you have a physical limitation on.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Re-enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the participant and emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Re-enter the emergency contact's address.

## Tips

- Please review the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
- If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please consult with a medical professional before participating in the activity.
- Ensure that the emergency contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the activity organizers.
