# Conference Launch Reception RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from attendees for the Conference Launch Reception, including their contact details, preferences, and event specifics. It helps the hosts manage the event logistics and provide a seamless experience for the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through the form carefully and fill in the required information accurately and completely.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and organization (if applicable).
3. Select the correct response for the reception date, time, and venue.
4. If you are attending with a guest, provide their information.
5. Indicate your preference for refreshments, networking, and transportation.
6. Select your beverage preference and dietary requirements (if any).
7. Confirm that you understand the RSVP deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Launch Reception RSVP**: A description of the event, not editable.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear on the event materials.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Organization**: Enter your company or institution name (if applicable).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Event Details**: Briefly describe the event.
* **Conference Name**: Enter the name of the conference.
* **Reception Date**: Select the date of the reception.
* **Reception Time**: Select the start time of the reception.
* **Reception Venue**: Select the location of the reception.
* **RSVP Response**: Choose whether you will attend (Yes I Will Attend) or not (No I Cannot Attend) the reception.
* **Guest Information**: If applicable, enter your guest's name and their relationship to you.
* **Bringing a Guest**: Indicate if you are bringing a guest.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of your guest (if applicable).
* **Guest Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and your guest.
* **Refreshment Preferences**: Select your preferences for refreshments.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Beverage Preference**: Select your preferred beverage choice.
* **Networking Interest**: Indicate your level of interest in networking.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Enter any special accessibility needs.
* **Transportation Needs**: Select your transportation needs.
* **How Did You Hear About Event**: Select the source of information about the event.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any other special requests.
* **RSVP Deadline Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you are aware of the RSVP deadline.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you submit the form by the given deadline to avoid any last-minute issues.
* If you have any special needs or requests, be sure to include them in the form.
* Review and submit the form carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
