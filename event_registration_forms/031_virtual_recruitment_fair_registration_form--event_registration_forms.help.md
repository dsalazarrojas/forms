# Virtual Recruitment Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from potential attendees for the Virtual Recruitment Fair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Provide your job title, job type, job experience, education, industry, and job location preferences.
4. Choose your job status (full-time, part-time, contract, or freelance).
5. Select your job experience duration (e.g., 0-3 months, 3-6 months, etc.).
6. Choose your job education level (e.g., high school diploma, bachelor's degree, etc.).
7. Select your job industry and location preferences (e.g., tech, finance, marketing, etc.).
8. Choose your job shift preferences (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, etc.).
9. Optionally, provide a brief job description.
10. If you have a job salary in mind, enter it.
11. If you're available for an event, select the start and end dates.
12. Choose your timezone.
13. Optionally, select the start and end times for the event.
14. Leave a note if you have any additional comments or preferences.
15. Enter a verification email address to ensure we can contact you.
16. Enter a verification phone number to ensure we can contact you.
17. Finally, select the date of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (id: 1)**:
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Name (id: 2)**:
	+ Enter your full name.
* **Email (id: 3)**:
	+ Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (id: 4)**:
	+ Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Job Title (id: 5)**:
	+ Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Job Type (id: 6)**:
	+ Select the type of job you're looking for (e.g., software engineer, data scientist, etc.).
* **Job Status (id: 7)**:
	+ Choose your job status (full-time, part-time, contract, or freelance).
* **Job Experience (id: 8)**:
	+ Select your job experience duration (e.g., 0-3 months, 3-6 months, etc.).
* **Job Education (id: 9)**:
	+ Select your job education level (e.g., high school diploma, bachelor's degree, etc.).
* **Job Industry (id: 10)**:
	+ Select your job industry (e.g., tech, finance, marketing, etc.).
* **Job Location (id: 11)**:
	+ Select your job location preferences (e.g., online, remote, in-office, etc.).
* **Job Shift (id: 12)**:
	+ Select your job shift preferences (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, etc.).
* **Job Description (id: 13)**:
	+ Optionally, provide a brief description of the job you're looking for.
* **Job Salary (id: 14)**:
	+ If you have a job salary in mind, enter it.
* **Job Availability (id: 15)**:
	+ Select the start and end dates when you'll be available for the event.
* **Event Start Date (id: 16)**:
	+ Optionally, select the date of the event start.
* **Event End Date (id: 17)**:
	+ Optionally, select the date of the event end.
* **Timezone (id: 18)**:
	+ Select your timezone.
* **Event Start Time (id: 19)**:
	+ Optionally, select the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time (id: 20)**:
	+ Optionally, select the end time of the event.
* **Note (id: 21)**:
	+ Optionally, leave a note with additional comments or preferences.
* **Email Verification (id: 22)**:
	+ Enter a verification email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Verification (id: 23)**:
	+ Enter a verification phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Submission Date (id: 24)**:
	+ Optionally, select the date of your submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure we can contact you and match you with relevant job opportunities.
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the job title, job type, experience, education, industry, and location preferences.
* If you have any additional comments or preferences, leave a note for us to review.
* Double-check your contact information and verification details for accuracy.
