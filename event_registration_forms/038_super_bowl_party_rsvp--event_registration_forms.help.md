# super_bowl_party_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Super Bowl party RSVP form is used to register your attendance and provide necessary details for the party planning team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
*   Make sure to include all required fields and select all necessary options.
*   Double-check your form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event, which is likely to be the Super Bowl party.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so the party planning team knows who is RSVPing.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for further contact.
*   **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): Enter your email address for contact and updates.
*   **Attending** (`attending`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you are attending (True), not attending (False), or unsure (Maybe).
*   **Number of Guests** (`number_guests`, number, required: false): If attending, enter the number of guests you are bringing.
*   **Message** (`message`, note, required: false): Enter any additional details or messages you want to share with the party planning team.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Although not required, this field is used to specify the date of the event, likely the day of the Super Bowl.
