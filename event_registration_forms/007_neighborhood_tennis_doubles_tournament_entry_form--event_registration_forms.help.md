# Neighborhood Tennis Doubles Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neighborhood Tennis Doubles Tournament Entry Form is designed for teams to register for a neighborhood tennis doubles tournament. This form helps the tournament organizers collect necessary information about the team, including team name, participants, and their preferences. It also collects additional information such as team and participant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Team Name** field with your team's name.
2.  Decide on the participation of your team by selecting **Participants** as either "Yes" or "No".
3.  For each participant, fill in their **Name** and **Email** fields if required. If you select "No" for participants, you can skip this step.
4.  Enter each participant's **Phone** number if provided.
5.  Decide on your team's preferences such as **Team Preferences** and **Match Preferences** by selecting the options that apply.
6.  Fill in any **Additional Comments** you may have about your team.
7.  If relevant, fill in **Team League** and **Team Tennis Club** with the respective information.
8.  Finally, fill in your team's **Rating** number and select whether you are willing to play as part of a **Team Preferences** and **Match Preferences**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team Name** (Text, Required): Enter your team's name for the tournament.
*   **Participants** (Select Multiple, Optional): Decide if you want to participate in the tournament as a team.
*   **Participant 1 Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the first participant of your team.
*   **Participant 1 Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email of the first participant of your team.
*   **Participant 1 Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the first participant of your team.
*   **Participant 2 Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the second participant of your team.
*   **Participant 2 Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email of the second participant of your team.
*   **Participant 2 Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the second participant of your team.
*   **Team Preferences** (Select One, Optional): Decide on the team's preferences such as whether you want to be a single or double team.
*   **Participant Preferences** (Select Multiple, Optional): Decide on the participants' preferences such as whether they want to play a singles or doubles match.
*   **Match Preferences** (Select Multiple, Optional): Decide on your team's match preferences such as whether you want a day or night match.
*   **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional information about your team.
*   **Team League** (Text, Optional): Enter the league you belong to.
*   **Team Tennis Club** (Text, Optional): Enter the tennis club you belong to.
*   **Team Rating** (Number, Optional): Enter your team's rating number.
*   **Team Tennis Club** (Text, Optional): This field is actually **Team Tennis Club**, not **Team League**, please note this correction.

**Tips:**

*   Please fill in all required fields to ensure successful submission of your team's registration.
*   Review your form carefully before submission to avoid any mistakes.
