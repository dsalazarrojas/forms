# Event Signage Customization Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect event registration and customization information for signage production. Please fill in the details below to ensure accurate and consistent signage for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the desired font size for your event signage.
3. Choose the font color for your event signage.
4. Select the background color for your event signage.
5. Select the border color for your event signage.
6. Choose the font family for your event signage.
7. Select the font style options for your event signage.
8. Choose the font alignment options for your event signage.
9. Select the font kerning options for your event signage.
10. Choose the font tracking options for your event signage.
11. Select the font weight options for your event signage.
12. Select the font stretch options for your event signage.
13. Select the font variant options for your event signage.
14. Select the font slant options for your event signage.
15. Select the font casual options for your event signage.
16. Choose the type of event you are customizing for.
17. Select the size of your event.
18. Enter the location of your event.
19. Enter the date of your event.
20. Enter the time of your event.
21. Enter any additional notes about your event.
22. Enter the contact email for your event.
23. Enter the contact phone number for your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (1): The first page of your event signage.
* **Event Name** (2): The name of your event.
* **Logo URL** (3): The URL of your event logo.
* **Font Size** (4): The size of the font used for your event signage.
* **Font Color** (5): The color of the font used for your event signage.
* **Background Color** (6): The background color of your event signage.
* **Border Color** (7): The color of the border used for your event signage.
* **Font Family** (8): The font family used for your event signage.
* **Font Style** (9): The style of the font used for your event signage. You can choose from bold, italic, regular, strike-through, and underline.
* **Font Alignment** (10): The alignment of the font used for your event signage. You can choose from left, center, right, and justify.
* **Font Kerning** (11): The kerning of the font used for your event signage. You can choose from on or off.
* **Font Tracking** (12): The tracking of the font used for your event signage. You can choose from none, normal, wide, and proportional.
* **Font Weight** (13): The weight of the font used for your event signage. You can choose from none, bold, bolder, and black.
* **Font Stretch** (14): The stretch of the font used for your event signage. You can choose from none, ultra-condensed, condensed, normal, expanded, and ultra-expanded.
* **Font Variant** (15): The variant of the font used for your event signage. You can choose from none, italic, and oblique.
* **Font Slant** (16): The slant of the font used for your event signage. You can choose from none, italic, and oblique.
* **Font Casual** (17): The casual of the font used for your event signage. You can choose from none, normal, and wide.
* **Event Type** (18): The type of event you are customizing for. You can choose from conference, meeting, seminar, and workshop.
* **Event Size** (19): The size of your event. You can choose from small, medium, large, and extra large.
* **Event Location** (20): The location of your event.
* **Event Date** (21): The date of your event.
* **Event Time** (22): The time of your event.
* **Event Notes** (23): Any additional notes about your event.
* **Event Contact** (24): The contact email for your event.
* **Event Phone** (25): The contact phone number for your event.
