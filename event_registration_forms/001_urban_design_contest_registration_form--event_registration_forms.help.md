# Urban Design Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Urban Design Contest. Please fill it out to participate.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic information in the **User Info** field.
2. Choose your **Contest Type** from the dropdown menu.
3. Add your **Project Info**, **Project Description**, **Submission File**, and **Project Image**.
4. Set your **Submission Date** and **Submission Time**.
5. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number**.
6. Fill in your **Address**.
7. Finally, review your form to make sure everything is correct before submitting it.
