# Masquerade Ball Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Masquerade Ball Registration Form is used to collect information from attendees for the Masquerade Ball event. This form gathers essential details such as name, email, and other relevant information to facilitate communication and event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your Phone number (if available) in the "Phone" field.
4. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
5. Select the Date of Attendance from the calendar provided in the "Date of Attendance" field.
6. Enter the Time of Attendance in the "Time of Attendance" field.
7. Specify the End of Attendance in the "End of Attendance" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or identification documents.
- **Email** (2): Enter your active and valid email address that will be used for event-related communications.
- **Phone** (3): If you have a phone number, please provide it for event organizers to contact you.
- **Note** (4): Add any additional information you wish to share with the event organizers.
- **Date of Attendance** (5): Select the date you plan to attend the event.
- **Time of Attendance** (6): Specify the time you plan to arrive at the event.
- **End of Attendance** (7): Indicate the time you plan to leave the event.

## Tips
- Please double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Ensure your email address is active and valid for event-related communications.
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact the event organizers.
