# Volunteer Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Intake Form is a tool used to gather essential information from volunteers about an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following sections:

1. **Volunteer Intake Form** (Page 1, Field 1): This section is optional but is used to briefly describe the form's purpose. You can leave this field blank if you don't see the need to fill it out.
2. **Event Details** (Page 1, Field 2): Please provide a short description of the event you are volunteering for, including its name and date.
3. **Volunteer Details** (Page 1, Field 3): Enter your name, email address, and a brief description of your volunteer role in the event.
4. **Contact Details** (Page 2, Field 1): Provide your contact information (name, email address, and phone number) to ensure we can get in touch with you.
