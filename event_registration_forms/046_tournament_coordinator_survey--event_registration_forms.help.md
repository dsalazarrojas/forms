# Tournament Coordinator Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Coordinator Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience with the tournament event organization. This survey aims to collect information about participant feedback, event organization, venue conditions, and other related aspects of the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing detailed responses where possible.
2. Select the relevant option(s) for each question that best describe your experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required: false): Please share your overall feedback about your experience with the tournament event.
* **Event organization** (`event_organization`, select_one, required: false): How clear were the communication channels with staff? (Select one of the following options: 'Yes', 'No')
* **Venue conditions** (`venue_conditions`, note, required: false): Describe the conditions of the venue where the event took place.
* **Participant experience** (`participant_experience`, text, required: false): Share your overall experience as a participant in the tournament.
* **Communication with staff** (`communication`, select_multiple, required: false): How clear were the communication channels with staff? (Select all that apply: 'Clear', 'Somewhat clear', 'Not clear')
* **Staff performance** (`staff_performance`, select_one, required: false): How would you rate the staff performance? (Select one of the following options: 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Fair', 'Poor')
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the tournament? (Select one of the following options: 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Fair', 'Poor')
* **Staff welcoming** (`staff_welcoming`, select_one, required: false): How welcoming were the staff members during your participation? (Select one of the following options: 'Very welcoming', 'Somewhat welcoming', 'Not welcoming')
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Is there anything we can improve? Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Event staff** (`event_staff`, select_one, required: false): How many days prior to the event did the event staff arrive? (Select one of the following options: '1 day', '2 days', '3 days', '4 days', '5 days')
* **Participant arrival** (`participant_arrival`, select_one, required: false): How many days prior to the event did you arrive? (Select one of the following options: '1 day', '2 days', '3 days', '4 days', '5 days')
* **Event registration** (`event_registration`, select_one, required: false): How many days prior to the event did you register? (Select one of the following options: '1 day', '2 days', '3 days', '4 days', '5 days')
* **Staff orientation** (`staff_orientation`, text, required: false): Share your experience with staff orientation.
* **Participant orientation** (`participant_orientation`, text, required: false): Share your experience with participant orientation.
* **Staff training** (`staff_training`, text, required: false): Share your experience with staff training.
* **Participant training** (`participant_training`, text, required: false): Share your experience with participant training.
* **Participant support** (`participant_support`, text, required: false): Share your experience with participant support.
* **Staff supervision** (`staff_supervision`, text, required: false): Share your experience with staff supervision.
* **Communication plan** (`communication_plan`, text, required: false): Share your experience with the communication plan.
* **Event setup** (`event_setup`, select_one, required: false): How good was the event setup? (Select one of the following options: 'Very good', 'Somewhat good', 'Not good')
* **Participant familiarization** (`participant_familiarization`, text, required: false): Share your experience with participant familiarization.
* **Staff familiarization** (`staff_familiarization`, text, required: false): Share your experience with staff familiarization.
* **Participant safety** (`participant_safety`, text, required: false): Share your experience with participant safety.
* **Staff safety** (`staff_safety`, text, required: false): Share your experience with staff safety.
* **Participant suggestion** (`participant_suggestion`, text, required: false): Share any suggestions or feedback you have for improvement.

## Tips
* Please answer each question as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Comments" field.
* If you need assistance or have questions, feel free to contact us.
