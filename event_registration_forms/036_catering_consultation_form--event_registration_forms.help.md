# Catering Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather essential information from clients to provide catering services for their events.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields, marked with \*.
- If you're unsure about a particular field, provide the best information you can.
- You can add any additional comments or questions in the "Any additional details we should know" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Contact Information --
- **Full Name**: Please enter your full name.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address for us to send confirmations and follow-ups.
 
### -- Event Details --
- **Proposed Event Date**: Enter the date of your event.
- **Type of Event**: Select the type of event you're planning (e.g. wedding, corporate meeting, etc.).
- **Estimated Guest Count**: Enter the estimated number of guests attending your event.
- **Venue Name and Address**: Enter the name and address of the venue where the event will be held. If you haven't booked a venue yet, write "TBD".

### -- Catering Preferences --
- **Preferred Menu Style**: Select your preferred menu style for the event (e.g. buffet, sit-down, etc.).
- **Approximate Budget per Person**: Select your approximate budget per person for the event.

### -- Additional Services --
- **Which additional services are you interested in**: Select all the additional services you're interested in (e.g. rentals, wait staff, etc.).

### Consultation Details
- **Best Time to Schedule a Consultation Call**: Enter the best time to schedule a call with us.
- **How did you hear about our catering services**: Select how you heard about our services (e.g. word of mouth, social media, etc.).
- **Any additional details we should know**: Provide any additional details or questions you have for us.
 
Please review all the fields carefully and fill out this form accurately and completely.
