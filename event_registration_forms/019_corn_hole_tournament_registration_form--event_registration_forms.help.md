# Corn Hole Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for a Corn Hole tournament. It gathers details such as name, contact information, team and player information, tournament specifics, and rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in your name and participant name (if applicable).
2. Provide your contact phone number and email address for communication purposes.
3. Indicate the number of players participating in the tournament.
4. Enter the names and phone numbers of all players (up to 2 players per team).
5. Enter the name of the tournament.
6. Select the date and time of the tournament.
7. Specify the location of the tournament.
8. Add any additional rules or details related to the tournament.
