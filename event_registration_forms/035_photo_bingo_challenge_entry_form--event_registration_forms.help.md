# Photo Bingo Challenge Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Photo Bingo Challenge Entry Form is used to submit entries for the Photo Bingo Challenge. Please fill out this form to participate in the challenge and have your work reviewed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field as instructed. The form is divided into sections, and each section has a specific purpose.
2. Read each field's label carefully to understand what information it requires.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Bingo Challenge Entry** (`photo_bingo_challenge_entry_form`): This is the main section where you submit your entry for the Photo Bingo Challenge. Please provide a concise and clear description of your entry.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Provide accurate information about yourself, including your name, organization, and contact email. This helps us understand who is submitting the entry.
* **Photo Entries** (`photo_entries`): This option allows you to select whether you want to enter individual photos or submit a single entry that includes all your photos. If you choose to select individual photos, please ensure that each photo is clearly marked as a separate entry. If you choose to submit a single entry, ensure that it is a single, cohesive work.
* **Permissions** (`permissions`): This field is not required, but it helps us understand if there are any specific permissions or licenses associated with the photo entries. If you are unsure, please answer 'Yes' and provide a brief explanation.

## Tips

- Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out each field.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
