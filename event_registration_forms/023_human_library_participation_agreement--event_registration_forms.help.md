# Human Library Participation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event participants register for an event and confirm their understanding of the event terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your event title and description in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide your event details, such as event ID, event name, and event date, in the "Event Details", "Event ID", "Event Name", and "Event Date" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your volunteer details, such as session details and volunteer name, in the "Session Details" and "Volunteer Name" fields, respectively.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the "Volunteer Email" and "Volunteer Phone" fields, respectively.
5. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the event terms and conditions by selecting the correct option in the "Confirm Agreement" field.
6. Confirm your session details by selecting the correct option in the "Confirm Session" field.
7. Confirm your volunteer status by selecting the correct option in the "Confirm Volunteer" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: Enter the title of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Event ID**: Enter the ID number for the event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Session Details**: Provide a brief description of the session you are participating in.
* **Volunteer Details**: Provide a brief description of your volunteer role.
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Volunteer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Volunteer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Agreement**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the event terms and conditions by selecting the correct option.
* **Confirm Session**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the session details by selecting the correct option.
* **Confirm Volunteer**: Confirm that you are a volunteer for this event by selecting the correct option.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form or the event, do not hesitate to contact the event organizers.
* The form is designed to guide you through the registration process and ensure that all necessary information is collected.
