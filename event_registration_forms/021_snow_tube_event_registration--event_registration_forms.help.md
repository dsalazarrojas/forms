# snow_tube_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The snow_tube_event_registration form is a tool to collect participant information for events related to snow tubes. It is meant to be a simple and efficient way to gather essential details from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your participant details in the **Participant Details** section.
2. Enter your emergency contact information in the **Emergency Contact** section.
3. If applicable, provide your waiver consent in the **Waiver Consent** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (text, required: false):
This section is where you provide your basic participant information. Please enter your name, age, and any other relevant details as requested.
* **Emergency Contact** (text, required: false):
In case of an emergency, please provide the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to.
* **Waiver Consent** (text, required: false):
If you have any medical conditions or need to waive any liability, please provide a short statement or description.
* **Participant Details 2** (text, required: false):
This section is for any additional participant information that may be relevant to the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the event organizers.
* The form will be reviewed and processed as soon as possible after submission.
