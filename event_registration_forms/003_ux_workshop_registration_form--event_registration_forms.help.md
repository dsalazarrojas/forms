# UX Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide helps you understand the purpose and use of the UX Workshop Registration Form. The form is designed to gather information from participants who are interested in attending a UX workshop. By filling out this form, you will be able to select a workshop, provide your personal details, and confirm your attendance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the workshop you are interested in attending by choosing from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be displayed.
3. Enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you.
4. Confirm your email address by typing it in again.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.
6. If you have chosen "Yes" for "Other Payment Method", please specify the method in the text field below.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the workshop you are interested in attending.
8. Enter the start and end times of the workshop you are interested in attending.
9. Let us know if you will be attending the workshop by choosing "Yes" or "No".
10. Provide any additional details about your attendance.
11. If you have made a payment, please select the status as "Active" to indicate that you have made the payment.
12. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a Workshop** (`workshop_selection`): Choose the workshop you are interested in attending. Select from the options provided.
* **Participant Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Participant Email** (`email`): Enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`): Type in your email address again to confirm it.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`): Provide any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.
* **Participant Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number that we will use to contact you.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`): Let us know if you are interested in a workshop.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): If you have made a payment, select the method used.
* **Other Payment Method** (`payment_method_other`): If you selected "Other" above, please specify the method in this field.
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_other_details`): Provide any additional details about your payment method.
* **Workshop Start Date** (`workshop_start`): Enter the start date of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop End Date** (`workshop_end`): Enter the end date of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop Start Time** (`workshop_start_time`): Enter the start time of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop End Time** (`workshop_end_time`): Enter the end time of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`): Let us know if you will be attending the workshop.
* **Participant Attendance Details** (`participant_attendance_details`): Provide any additional details about your attendance.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): If you have made a payment, select "Active" to indicate that you have made the payment.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to complete your registration.
