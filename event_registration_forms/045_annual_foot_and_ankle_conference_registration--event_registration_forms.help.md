### Annual Foot And Ankle Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Annual Foot And Ankle Conference Registration" form is designed for attendees to register for the conference. This includes filling out personal and professional information, selecting registration packages, choosing conference and workshop preferences, and providing payment details.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in your attendee information: **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**. Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
2.  Next, select your **Professional Title or Role** (e.g., Podiatrist, Orthopedic Surgeon) and enter your **Facility or Hospital Name**, if applicable.
3.  Select your **Years of Professional Experience** and your area of **Specialty or Sub-specialty**.
4.  Choose your registration type: **Full Conference (3 days)**, **2-Day Registration**, **1-Day Registration**, **Single Session Pass**, or **Virtual Attendance**.
5.  If attending in-person, choose the **Conference Dates** you will attend and select the **Workshop Topics of Interest**.
6.  Decide on your **Meal Plan Option**: **Include all meals**, **Breakfast and lunch only**, **Lunch only**, or **No meals included**.
7.  If you need hotel arrangements, select whether you need a **Single Room**, **Double Room**, or **No Preference**.
8.  Finally, choose your **Payment Method**: **Credit Card**, **Check**, **Bank Transfer**, or **Purchase Order**.
9.  You may also include any **Special Requests or Additional Information** in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`text`, `required`): Enter your first name as it should be spelled.
* **Last Name** (`text`, `required`): Enter your last name as it should be spelled.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `required`): Enter a valid email address for conference communications.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, `required`): Enter a contact number for communication.
* **Professional Title or Role** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose your professional title or role (e.g., Podiatrist, Orthopedic Surgeon).
* **Facility or Hospital Name** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your workplace name if applicable.
* **Specialty or Sub-specialty** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your area of focus.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (`select_one`, `optional`): Select your years of professional experience (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, etc.).
* **Registration Type** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose your registration package (Full Conference, 2-Day, 1-Day, etc.).
* **Conference Dates** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Choose the conference dates you will attend.
* **Workshop Topics of Interest** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the workshop topics you are interested in.
* **Meal Plan Option** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose your meal plan option (Include all meals, Breakfast and lunch only, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`text`, `optional`): Enter any food allergies or preferences.
* **Hotel Accommodation Needed** (`select_one`, `required`): Decide if you need hotel arrangements.
* **Number of Nights Needed** (`number`, `optional`): Enter the number of nights you need hotel arrangements for.
* **Room Type Preference** (`select_one`, `optional`): Choose your room type preference (Single Room, Double Room, etc.).
* **Payment Method** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose how you will pay (Credit Card, Check, etc.).
* **Special Requests or Additional Information** (`text`, `optional`): Enter any special requests or additional information.
