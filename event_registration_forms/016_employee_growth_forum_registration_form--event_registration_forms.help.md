# Employee Growth Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register employees for events, workshops, or meetings. Fill out this form to provide information about the employee's personal details, role, department, and event attendance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email Address.
3. Select your Role (Senior Manager, Junior Manager, or Team Member).
4. Add your Department (if applicable).
5. Enter the name of your Company.
6. Select the event Type (Conference, Workshop, or Meeting).
7. Add the Attendees (select multiple attendees).
8. Add any Comments (if necessary).
9. Select your Job Title (multiple fields provided).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Role** (Role, select_one, required): Select your role (Senior Manager, Junior Manager, or Team Member).
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter the name of your department.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title 1** (Job Title 1, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Job Title 2** (Job Title 2, text, required): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Event Type** (Event Type, select_multiple, required): Select the type of event (Conference, Workshop, or Meeting).
* **Attendees** (Attendees, select_multiple, required): Select the attendees (John Doe, Jane Doe, or Bob Smith).
* **Comments** (Comments, note, required): Add any comments about your attendance.

Note: The form fields with "required" set to true are required to be filled out.
