# Crafting Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crafting Club Registration Form is designed for members to register for the crafting club. This form will help us get to know your name, email, phone number, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your First Name.
2. Then, fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Provide your Phone Number.
5. Select your member type (Regular Member, VIP Member, or Guest).
6. Choose your craft preferences (Crafting, Art, Music, Dance, Theater).
7. Select how often you participate in crafting activities (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely).
8. If you have any special Registration Date and Registration Time, please enter them.
9. Add any additional Note if you have any information to share.
10. Answer if this is your first time joining the club.
11. Finally, select the tool you are assigned to use (Chatjimmy or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Fill in your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Member Type** (`member_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select if you're a Regular Member, VIP Member, or Guest.
* **Craft Preferences** (`craft_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or multiple options that interest you (Crafting, Art, Music, Dance, Theater).
* **Craft Frequency** (`craft_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select how often you participate in crafting activities (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required/optional): If you have a preferred registration date, please enter it.
* **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, required/optional): If you have a preferred registration time, please enter it.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **First Time Member** (`first_time_member`, select_one, required/optional): Answer with "Yes" or "No" to indicate this is your first time joining the club.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select the tool you are assigned to use (Chatjimmy or Other).

Tips:
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Select only one option for member type and craft preferences.
* Enter a valid email address.
* If you're a guest, you won't be assigned a tool.
* If you're assigned a tool, please select it correctly.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
