# Overnight Hike Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for an overnight hike event. It is expected to be filled out by the participant or their emergency contact.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing all required information.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Info** (`event_info`):
	+ This field is used to capture details about the event such as its date and location.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`):
	+ This field is used to capture information about the participant, such as their name and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`):
	+ This field is used to capture information about the participant's emergency contact, such as their name and relationship to the participant.
* **Email** (`email`): 
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): 
	+ Enter the date of the event (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): 
	+ Enter the time of the event (HH:MM format).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): 
	+ Enter the location of the event.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): 
	+ Enter your participant name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): 
	+ Enter your participant email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): 
	+ Enter your participant phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): 
	+ Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): 
	+ Enter the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact (e.g., "Parent", "Guardian", etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`): 
	+ Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`): 
	+ Enter the Event ID.
* **Event Code** (`event_code`): 
	+ Enter the Event Code.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`): 
	+ Enter the Participant ID.
* **Participant ID Type** (`participant_id_type`): 
	+ Enter the type of Participant ID.
* **Emergency Contact ID** (`emergency_contact_id`): 
	+ Enter the Emergency Contact ID.
* **Emergency Contact ID Type** (`emergency_contact_id_type`): 
	+ Enter the type of Emergency Contact ID.
* **Note** (`note`): 
	+ Enter any additional notes about the participant or event.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`): 
	+ Select the status of the event (Open, Closed, In Progress, Done, Pending, Canceled).
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`): 
	+ Select the status of the participant (Pending, Confirmed, Declined, No Show, Withdrawn).
* **Form Submission ID** (`form_submission_id`): 
	+ This field is automatically generated and is used to track the form submission.
* **Form Status** (`event_status`) and **Participant Status** (`participant_status`) should be filled out according to the event and participant status respectively. 
	+ These fields are used to track the event and participant status.
