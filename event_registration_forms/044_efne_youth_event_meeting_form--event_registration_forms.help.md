# EFNE Youth Event Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EFNE Youth Event Meeting Form is used to collect information about a specific meeting related to the EFNE youth organization. It's designed for team members to report their attendance, meeting details, and other relevant information. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for the Team Member section, such as the Team Member's name.
2. If you're reporting a meeting, fill in the Meeting Date, Start Time, and End Time. 
3. Provide the Meeting Subject and Meeting Location, which should be as detailed as possible.
4. Add any Meeting Notes that are relevant to the event.
5. If you're organizing the meeting, provide the Meeting Organizer's name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member**: A required field for identifying the team member's name. Please provide your full name as it appears on your team list.
* **Meeting Date**: A required date field for specifying the date of the meeting. Format is MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY. 
* **Start Time**: A required time field for specifying the start time of the meeting. Format is HH:MM AM/PM or HH:MM 24-hour format.
* **End Time**: A required time field for specifying the end time of the meeting. Format is HH:MM AM/PM or HH:MM 24-hour format.
* **Meeting Subject**: An optional field for specifying the topic or subject of the meeting. This can be a brief description of the meeting's purpose.
* **Meeting Location**: An optional field for specifying the meeting location. This can be a physical address or a virtual meeting link.
* **Meeting Notes**: An optional field for adding any notes or comments about the meeting. This can be used to provide additional information or follow-up tasks.
* **Event Start Time**: An optional time field for specifying the start time of the event. Format is HH:MM AM/PM or HH:MM 24-hour format.
* **Event End Time**: An optional time field for specifying the end time of the event. Format is HH:MM AM/PM or HH:MM 24-hour format.
* **Meeting Organizer**: An optional field for specifying the meeting organizer's name. Please provide their name as it appears on your team list.
* **Meeting Location Details**: An optional field for providing any additional details about the meeting location. This can include room numbers, floor numbers, etc.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information provided, especially dates and times.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your team leader or the form moderator.
* The meeting notes field is a good place to add any additional context or reminders about the meeting.
* The meeting subject field can be used to create a brief agenda for the meeting.
