# Menu Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about their preferred menu items. Please answer each question honestly to help us understand your menu preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select all the menu items you prefer by checking the relevant boxes under the "Preferred Menu Items" field.
2. For each menu item, rate your satisfaction by selecting a number between 1 and 5 under the corresponding "Rating for Menu Item X" field.
3. Enter any additional feedback about your experience in the "Additional Feedback" field.
