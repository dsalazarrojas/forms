## Purpose
This form is used to manage event registration and RSVPs for various types of events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name and phone number to register for the event.
* Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with event details and updates.
* Choose your RSVP status (Yes or No) to let us know if you can attend.
* Enter the date of the event (mm/dd/yyyy) and start and end times (hh:mm am/pm).
* You can enter additional notes or comments about the event if you want.
* For some events, you might be asked to choose between "Yes" or "No" for additional questions or preferences.
