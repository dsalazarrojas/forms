# Speech Writing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the Speech Writing Workshop. It helps the workshop organizers to collect necessary information from participants to manage the event smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the text fields provided.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the format of `username@example.com`.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the format of `123-456-7890`.
4. Select your **Role** from the drop-down menu. You can choose between **Participant** and **Speaker**.
5. If you are registering for a specific **Workshop Date**, please enter it in the date format (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DD`).
6. If you are registering for a specific **Workshop Time**, please enter it in the time format (e.g., `HH:MM AM/PM`).
7. If the workshop **Workshop Duration** is longer than a standard hour, please enter the total duration in hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the field where you enter your first name. Please enter your first name as you would like it to appear on the workshop materials and communication.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the field where you enter your last name. Please enter your last name as you would like it to appear on the workshop materials and communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is the field where you enter your email address. Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This is the field where you enter your phone number. Please use the standard format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX.XXX.XXXX`.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select your role from the drop-down menu. This will help us to manage the workshop accordingly.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required: false): If you are registering for a specific workshop date, please enter it in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required: false): If you are registering for a specific workshop time, please enter it in the format of `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, `number`, required: false): If the workshop duration is longer than a standard hour, please enter the total duration in hours.
