# Book Club Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to ensure accurate and efficient recording of member attendance, completion status, and feedback on the current book. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and thank you for your participation!

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form carefully and honestly.
2. Select your answer options from the provided choices.
3. Use clear and concise language when answering the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`member_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first and last name. This is how you will be referred to in club communications.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): Enter your contact email. This will help us keep you updated on meeting schedules and announcements.
* **Membership ID** (`membership_id`, Text, Optional): If you are a member, please enter your unique ID for our records. This helps us track your membership status.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of this meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, Select One, Required): Where is the meeting held? Choose one of the available options.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, Select One, Required): How are you attending today? Choose one of the provided options.
* **Current Book Title** (`book_title_read`, Text, Required): Enter the title of the book being discussed.
* **Book Author** (`book_author`, Text, Required): Enter the author of the current book.
* **Completion Level** (`reading_completion`, Select One, Required): Select how much of the book you've read. This helps us understand your engagement with the book.
* **Book Rating** (`book_rating`, Select One, Required): Select your overall rating for the book. This helps us understand your opinion.
* **Favorite Character** (`favorite_character`, Text, Optional): If you enjoyed a character, enter their name here. This gives us insight into your favorite character.
* **Discussion Topics** (`discussion_topics`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the topics you'd like to discuss at the next meeting. Select all that apply.
* **Brought Snacks** (`brought_snacks`, Select One, Optional): If you brought snacks, select True. If not, select False. This helps us plan future meetings.
* **Next Meeting Availability** (`next_meeting_availability`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose when you are available to attend the next meeting.
* **Book Suggestions** (`book_suggestions`, Text, Optional): If you have book suggestions, enter them here. This helps us plan future reading materials.
* **Additional Comments/Feedback** (`feedback_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the meeting or book. This helps us improve our book club.
* **Volunteer Help** (`volunteer_help`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose where you'd like to help out with future meetings. This helps us distribute tasks fairly among members.
