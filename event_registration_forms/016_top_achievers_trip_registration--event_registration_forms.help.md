# Top Achievers Trip Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Top Achievers Trip Registration form is designed to collect essential information from participants to ensure a successful and enjoyable trip experience. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to help us provide the best possible experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section carefully, following the instructions provided.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as "true" in the form).
3. For text fields, type in your information accurately and completely.
4. For number fields, enter a whole number as requested.
5. Review your form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter your name and any other identifying information as requested.
* **Participant Contact Information** (`participant_details_2`, text, optional): If you have any emergency contact information, please provide it for our records.
* **Participant Details 2** (`participant_details_3`, text, optional): This field is reserved for additional participant details. Please use it only if necessary.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, required): Describe your trip in as much detail as possible, including any specific needs or requirements.
* **Trip Preferences** (`trip_preferences`, text, optional): Let us know your preferences for the trip, such as food, accommodations, or activities.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide any emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and relationship.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical information to help us provide the best possible experience for you.
* **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, number, optional): This field is reserved for participant preferences. Please enter a number as requested.
* **How many Participants** (`participant_preferences_2`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of participants attending the trip.
* **Participant's Nationality** (`participant_nationality`, text, required): Please select your nationality from the list or describe it if not available.
* **Participant's Language** (`participant_language`, text, required): Select your native or most proficient language from the list.
* **Special Requests or Requirements** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Share any special requests or requirements you may have.
* **Participant's Age** (`participant_age`, number, required): Enter your age to help us assess your suitability for the trip.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_age_2`, date, required): Enter your date of birth accurately.
* **Language Proficiency** (`participant_language_2`, text, optional): Describe your language proficiency level, if applicable.
* **Participant Language 2** (`participant_language_3`, text, optional): This field is reserved for additional language information. Please use it only if necessary.
* **Participant Age 2** (`participant_age_3`, number, optional): Enter your age if applicable.
* **Participant Language 4** (`participant_language_4`, text, optional): This field is reserved for additional language information. Please use it only if necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* Please be accurate and complete in your responses to ensure we can provide the best possible experience for you.
