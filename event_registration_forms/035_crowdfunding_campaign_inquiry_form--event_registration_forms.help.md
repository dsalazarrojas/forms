# Crowdfunding Campaign Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crowdfunding Campaign Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect information from supporters and potential supporters of a crowdfunding campaign. This form helps campaign organizers and administrators better understand campaign engagement and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form with the required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are entering accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Title** (`campaign_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the crowdfunding campaign you are interested in or supporting.
* **Supporter Name** (`supporter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Supporter Email** (`supporter_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person you are supporting.
* **Supporter Phone** (`supporter_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person you are supporting.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number, optional): Enter the amount of money donated.
* **Currency Donated** (`currency_donated`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the currency used for the donation (USD, EUR, JPY, CNY).
* **Frequency Donated** (`frequency_donated`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the frequency of the donation (Once, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Supporter Fund Raising Method** (`supporter_fund_raising_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the method used by the supporter to raise funds (Crowdfunding, Direct Donation, Other).
* **Supporter Investing Method** (`supporter_investing_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the method used by the supporter to invest (Online, Offline, Social Media).
* **Supporter Loyalty Program** (`supporter_loyalty_program`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select whether the supporter is part of a loyalty program (True, False, Maybe).

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help campaign organizers and administrators better understand campaign engagement and preferences.
