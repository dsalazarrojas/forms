# Seminar Registration Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seminar Registration Information Form is used to collect and organize essential details about seminar participants, including their contact information, interests, and seminar-specific data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your Participant Name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your seminar date and time.
3. Select your interests among the options (Option A, Option B, or Option C).
4. Add any additional notes about yourself as a participant (Participant Notes).
5. Confirm your agreement to participate (Participant Agreement).
6. Provide any additional seminar-specific notes (Seminar Notes).
7. Enter the URL of the form submission (Form Submission URL).
8. Fill in the event name and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a participant in the seminar.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, date, required): Select the date of the seminar you will attend.
* **Seminar Time** (`seminar_time`, time, required): Choose the start time of the seminar.
* **Seminar Time End** (`seminar_time_end`, time, required): Select the end time of the seminar.
* **Participant Interests** (`participant_interests`, select_multiple, required): Choose the interests that apply to you (Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about yourself as a participant.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, text, required): Confirm your agreement to participate.
* **Seminar Notes** (`seminar_notes`, text, required): Add any additional seminar-specific notes.
* **Form Submission URL** (`form_submission_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the form submission.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use the Participant Notes field to provide any additional context or information about yourself as a participant.
* The Participant Agreement field is a confirmation that you understand the terms and conditions of the seminar.
* The Seminar Notes field is for additional seminar-specific information that might be useful for the organizers.
* The Form Submission URL field is for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
* The Event Name and Location fields are essential for seminar organization and event planning.
* The Event Date field is for scheduling and planning purposes.
