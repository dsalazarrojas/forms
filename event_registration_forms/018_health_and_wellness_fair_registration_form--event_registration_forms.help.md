# health_and_wellness_fair_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "health_and_wellness_fair_registration_form" is designed to register participants for a health and wellness fair. This form is used to collect essential information about participants, including their contact details, dietary needs, and attendance information. Please fill in the form accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your name and email address to identify yourself as a participant.
2.  If you have a phone number, please enter it for easy contact. Note: This is optional.
3.  Enter your date of birth for age verification.
4.  Indicate your dietary requirements for the fair, if applicable:
    *   Gluten-free
    *   Vegan
    *   Vegetarian
    *   Other (please specify)
5.  Choose who you are attending with:
    *   Self
    *   Partner/Spouse
    *   Friend
    *   Family Member
    *   Other (please specify)
6.  Choose your participant type:
    *   Vendor
    *   Vendor Staff
    *   Volunteer
    *   Participant
    *   Guest
7.  If you are a vendor, please provide any additional information required for your presence at the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Name (1)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Participant Email (2)**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
*   **Participant Phone (3)**: Optional; use the international format with 10 digits, including the country code (e.g., +1 123 456 7890).
*   **Date of Birth (4)**: Format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1990-06-01).
*   **Participant Address (5)**: Enter your physical address for logistics.
*   **Dietary Requirements (6)**: Select all that apply, or type "Other" and specify your needs in the text field.
*   **Attending with (7)**: Choose the correct option for your companion(s).
*   **Registration Date (8)**: Enter the date you registered for the event (Format: YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Participant Type (9)**: Select your role at the event.
*   **Vendor Information (10)**: If you are a vendor, provide any additional information required for your presence at the event.

## Tips
*   Double-check your contact information and dietary requirements for accuracy.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact the event organizers.
*   Ensure you register with the correct participant type to receive the right benefits and information.
