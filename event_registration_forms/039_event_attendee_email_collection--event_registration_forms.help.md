# Event Attendee Email Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect contact information from attendees of an event, such as conferences, meetups, or seminars, to facilitate post-event follow-up and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

* Enter your email address in the "email" field.
* Enter your name in the "name" field.
* Enter your organization in the "organization" field.
* Enter your job title in the "job title" field.
* Enter your department in the "department" field.
* Enter your job function in the "job function" field.
* Enter your address in the "address" field.
* Enter your zip code in the "zip" field.
* Enter your city in the "city" field.
* Enter your country in the "country" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
* Enter your preferred language in the "language" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter your email address.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Enter your job function.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Language** (`language`, text, optional): Enter your preferred language.

## Tips
* Filling out all fields will provide a more complete and accurate record of your contact information.
* All fields are optional, but filling out any required fields (marked as "required") will help us follow up with you after the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
