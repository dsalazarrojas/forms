# island_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The island_reservation_form is a form used to collect information for booking a stay on an island, including travel dates, accommodation type, number of guests, arrival time, preferred facilities, emergency contact information, and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill out the fields as prompted, ensuring you provide the necessary information to book your stay on the island.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Dates** (travel_dates, date, required: false): Choose the travel dates for your stay on the island. Select the start and end dates of your trip.
* **Accommodation Type** (travel_type, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of accommodation you prefer for your stay, e.g., "Yes" for a specific accommodation type.
* **Number of Guests** (number_guests, number, required: false): Enter the number of guests that will be staying with you on the island.
* **Expected Arrival Time** (arrival_time, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate your expected arrival time, with options being "Yes" or "No" for morning or afternoon arrival.
* **Preferred Facilities** (preferred_facilities, select_multiple, required: false): Select the facilities you would like to have during your stay, such as "Yes" for a specific facility.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, required: false): Provide the name of the emergency contact person, who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Email** (emergency_contact_email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Special Requests** (special_requests, note, required: false): Enter any special requests or notes about your stay, e.g., specific equipment or amenities you require.

## Tips
Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
