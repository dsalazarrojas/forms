# Resilience And Well Being Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Resilience And Well Being Registration Form is designed to collect essential information for events related to resilience, well-being, and participant engagement. This form helps us gather data to better serve participants and improve event organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a program from the available options in the **Select A Program** field (page 1).
2. Fill in your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields with your personal details (pages 2-4).
3. Specify the **Date** and **Time** of the event (pages 5-6).
4. Provide any **Comments** or **Notes** about your experience or participation (pages 7-8).
5. Add any **Additional Comments** or **Additional Notes** about the event or your experience (pages 9-10).
6. Enter your **Participant Details** for the selected program, including the event **Type** and any **Participant Info** or **Program Details** (pages 11-13).
7. Complete your **Participant Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields for yourself as the participant (pages 14-16).
8. Enter the event **Date** and **Time** (pages 17-18).
9. Provide any **Program Notes** or **Additional Program Details** (pages 19-20).
10. Select the event **Type** (page 21).
11. Enter any **Participant Info** (page 22).
12. Add any **Notes** about the event or your experience (page 23).
13. Complete any additional **Participant Info** fields as needed (page 24).
14. Enter the **Submission Date** (page 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select A Program** (page 1): Choose a program you are interested in from the available options.
* **Name** (page 2): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Email** (page 3): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (page 4): Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended).
* **Date** (page 5): Specify the event date.
* **Time** (page 6): Specify the event time.
* **Comments** (page 7): Add any comments about your experience or participation.
* **Notes** (page 8): Add any additional notes about the event or your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (page 9): Add any additional comments about the event or your experience.
* **Additional Notes** (page 10): Add any additional notes about the event or your experience.
* **Participant Details** (page 11): Enter your participant details, including the event type.
* **Event Type** (page 20): Select the event type.
* **Participant Info** (page 22): Enter any participant information.
* **Program Notes** (page 19): Enter any program notes.
* **Participant 2** (page 24): Enter any additional participant information.
* **Submission Date** (page 25): Enter the submission date.
* **Participant Name** (page 13): Enter the participant name.
* **Participant Email** (page 15): Enter the participant email.
* **Participant Phone** (page 16): Enter the participant phone number.
* **Participant Info** (page 21): Enter any additional participant information.
* **Program Details** (page 20): Enter any additional program details.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We appreciate your participation and contribution to our event!
