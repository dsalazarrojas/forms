# osha_certification_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track employee training information for OSHA certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
2. If applicable, enter the trainer's name in the "Trainer" field.
3. Select the training type from the "Training Type" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the start date of the training in the "Start Date" field (if applicable).
5. Enter the end date of the training in the "End Date" field (if applicable).
6. Provide the training location in the "Training Location" field.
7. Select the training status as "Active" or "Inactive" from the "Training Status" dropdown menu.
8. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
9. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
10. Enter the total training hours in the "Training Hours" field.
11. Select whether you are certified or not in the "Training Certification" dropdown menu.
12. Enter the date of the training in the "Training Date" field (if applicable).
13. Enter the training time in the "Training Time" field (if applicable).
14. Add any additional notes about the training location in the "Training Location Notes" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (text, required): The name of the organization providing the training.
* **Trainer** (text, optional): The name of the trainer providing the training (if applicable).
* **Training Type** (text, required): The type of training you received (e.g. first aid, fire safety, etc.).
* **Start Date** (date, optional): The start date of the training (if applicable).
* **End Date** (date, optional): The end date of the training (if applicable).
* **Training Location** (text, optional): The location where the training took place.
* **Training Status** (select_one, required): Select "Active" if you are currently certified, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Employee Name** (text, required): Your name.
* **Employee ID** (text, required): Your employee ID number.
* **Training Hours** (text, required): The total hours of training you completed.
* **Training Certification** (select_one, required): Select "Certified" if you passed the training, "Not Certified" otherwise.
* **Training Date** (date, optional): The date of the training (if applicable).
* **Training Time** (time, optional): The time of the training (if applicable).
* **Training Location Notes** (text, optional): Any additional notes about the training location.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are not sure about a field, refer to your training documentation or contact your supervisor for clarification.
* Please note that this form is for official use only.
