# Field Trip Preparation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Preparation Checklist Form is used to collect essential information to ensure a smooth and safe field trip experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Choose the correct options for each field based on your knowledge and circumstances.
4. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Approval** (`school_approval`, select_one, false): Choose 'True' if the school has given approval for the field trip, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Safety Protocols** (`safety_protocols`, note, false): This field allows you to add any relevant safety protocols that should be considered for the trip.
* **Transportation** (`transportation`, select_one, false): Choose 'True' if the trip will involve transportation, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, select_multiple, false): Select 'True' for each type of accommodation that will be used for the trip (e.g., hotel, hostel, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, false): Select 'True' for each emergency contact person that will be available during the trip.
* **Field Trip Permission** (`field_trip_permission`, select_one, false): Choose 'True' if permission has been obtained for the field trip, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Food and Supplies** (`food_and_supplies`, select_multiple, false): Select 'True' for each type of food and supply that will be needed for the trip (e.g., snacks, meals, etc.).
* **Medical Care** (`medical_care`, select_multiple, false): Select 'True' for each type of medical care that will be needed for the trip (e.g., medical supplies, emergency medical kits, etc.).
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, select_one, false): Choose 'True' if insurance is necessary for the trip, and 'False' otherwise.
* **First Aid Kit** (`first_aid_kit`, select_multiple, false): Select 'True' for each first aid kit that will be brought on the trip.
* **Field Trips** (`field_trips`, select_one, false): Choose 'True' if the trip is for field trips, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Weather** (`weather`, select_multiple, false): Select 'True' for each type of weather condition that may affect the trip (e.g., rain, shine, etc.).
* **Field Trip Review** (`field_trip_review`, time, false): This field is for scheduling a review of the field trip.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, false): Add any additional information that is relevant to the field trip.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with the trip organizers or the school administration.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
