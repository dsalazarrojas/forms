# Local Artist Engagement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Artist Engagement Form is a document that helps us stay in touch with local artists and keep track of their upcoming events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Review your entries for accuracy.
3. Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Local Artist Engagement Form (Text, Optional):** This is the title of the form, which should be left blank for now.
* **Artist Name (Text, Optional):** Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Artist Email (Email, Optional):** Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Artist Phone (Text, Optional):** Provide your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Event Name (Text, Optional):** List the name of the event you're interested in participating in or promoting.
* **Event Date (Date, Optional):** Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time (Time, Optional):** Enter the time of the event.
* **Set Length (Number, Optional):** If you're an artist with a set, please enter the number of sets you'll be performing.
* **Performance Description (Text, Optional):** Briefly describe your performance or event details.

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill out as much information as you can to help us better understand your engagement with our organization.
