# Tax Strategy Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees for the Tax Strategy Conference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Attendee Details** (Page 1).
2. Select your preferred **Session Times** for each session.
3. Add any additional **Notes** about your registration.
4. Specify your **Accommodation Requirements**.
5. Provide your **Contact Details**.
6. Enter your **Job Title**.
7. Complete the form by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details**: Enter your name, email, and any other identifying information about yourself.
* **Session Preferences**: Select the time slots for each session you plan to attend.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, such as phone number and office address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Accommodation Requirements**: Specify your needs for accommodation, such as dietary restrictions.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Session 1 Time**, **Session 2 Time**, **Session 3 Time**, **Session 4 Time**, **Session 5 Time**: Select the time slots for each session you plan to attend.
