# Athletic Trainer Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help seminar organizers collect information from attendees for the Athletic Trainer Seminar. The information collected will be used to plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the seminar title, date, and location.
2.  Provide attendee information, including first name, last name, email, phone number, professional credentials, and organization/school affiliation.
3.  Select your work setting and experience level.
4.  Choose your primary session interest and any secondary session interests.
5.  Select your workshop preferences.
6.  Choose your registration type, which includes individual registration, group rate, student rate, or member rate.
7.  Pay the registration fee in USD.
8.  Indicate any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
9.  Check if you need help with accommodations and if you need continuing education credits.
10. Add any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Seminar Title**: Enter the title of the seminar you are interested in attending.
*   **Seminar Date**: Enter the date of the seminar you want to attend.
*   **Seminar Location**: Enter the city and venue name where the seminar will take place.
*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Professional Credentials**: Enter any relevant professional credentials, such as ATC, LAT, or athletic training degree.
*   **Organization/School Affiliation**: Enter your organization or school affiliation.
*   **Work Setting**: Select your primary work environment, which is High School, College/University, Professional, Clinic, Hospital, or Other.
*   **Experience Level**: Select your level of experience, which is Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, or 10+ years.
*   **Primary Session Interest**: Choose the session that interests you the most.
*   **Secondary Session Interest**: Enter any other sessions that might interest you.
*   **Workshop Preferences**: Select the workshops you are interested in.
*   **Registration Type**: Choose your registration level, which includes Individual Registration, Group Rate, Student Rate, Member Rate, or Presenter.
*   **Registration Fee**: Enter the amount in USD.
*   **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have, such as Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, or None.
*   **Accessibility Needs**: Enter any accessibility needs you have.
*   **Lodging Needed**: Check if you need help with accommodations.
*   **Continuing Education Credits**: Check if you need continuing education credits.
*   **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or requests.
