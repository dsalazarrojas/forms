# Short Term Rental Guest Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Short Term Rental Guest Check Out Form is used to document the check-out process of short-term rental guests, ensuring accurate and efficient management of guest information, payment status, and additional charges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), which are:
	* Guest Email
	* Guest Phone
	* Payment Status (Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid)
2. For optional fields, provide any relevant information, such as:
	* Guest Feedback
	* Check Out Time
	* Check Out Date
	* Additional Charges
	* Host Name
	* Host Email
	* Comments
	* Payment Method (Yes or No)
	* Payment Date
	* Payment Time
	* Total Amount Paid
	* Additional Comments
3. Ensure that you have entered accurate and complete information in all fields to ensure accurate guest records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Feedback (`guest_feedback`)
* Guest Feedback: This field is for guests to provide feedback on their stay. Please enter any comments or suggestions they may have.
 
### Check Out Time (`check_out_time`)
* Check Out Time: Enter the time the guest checked out.
 
### Check Out Date (`check_out_date`)
* Check Out Date: Enter the date the guest checked out.
 
### Additional Charges (`additional_charges`)
* Additional Charges: Enter any additional charges incurred during the stay, such as cleaning fees or late payment charges.

### Guest Name (`guest_name`)
* Guest Name: Enter the guest's name.
 
### Guest Email (`guest_email`)
* Guest Email: Enter the guest's email address. (Required)

### Guest Phone (`contact_phone`)
* Guest Phone: Enter the guest's phone number. (Required)

### Host Name (`host_name`)
* Host Name: Enter the host's name.
 
### Host Email (`host_email`)
* Host Email: Enter the host's email address.
 
### Comments (`comments`)
* Comments: Enter any additional comments or notes about the stay.
 
### Payment Status (`payment_status`)
* Payment Status: Select the payment status of the guest, which can be Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid. (Required)

### Payment Method (`payment_method`)
* Payment Method: Check if the guest made a payment. (Yes or No) (Optional)

### Payment Date (`payment_date`)
* Payment Date: Enter the date the payment was made.
 
### Payment Time (`payment_time`)
* Payment Time: Enter the time the payment was made.
 
### Additional Comments (`additional_comments`)
* Additional Comments: Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment.

### Total Amount Paid (`payment_total`)
* Total Amount Paid: Enter the total amount paid by the guest.
 
### Payment Status (`payment_status`)
* Payment Status: Select the payment status of the guest, which can be Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid. (Required)

### Payment Method (`payment_method`)
