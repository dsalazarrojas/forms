# Camping Site Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for booking a camping site. It includes fields for customer contact information, booking period, camping type, site category, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose the arrival and departure dates of your stay.
3. Select the total number of nights you will be staying.
4. Choose your preferred camping style (e.g., Tent Camping, RV Camping, etc.).
5. Select your preferred site category (e.g., Standard Site, Premium Site, etc.).
6. Specify your hookup requirements for your RV (e.g., Electric, Water, etc.).
7. If you are bringing guests, specify the number of adults and children (including toddlers) accompanying you.
8. Check if you have any special requests or preferences.
9. Specify how you would like to receive your booking confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Arrival Date**: Choose the date you will arrive at the campsite.
* **Departure Date**: Choose the date you will leave the campsite.
* **Total Number of Nights**: Enter the total number of nights you will be staying.
* **Camping Style**: Choose the style of camping that suits your needs (e.g., Tent Camping, RV Camping, etc.).
* **Site Category**: Select the type of site you prefer (e.g., Standard Site, Premium Site, etc.).
* **Hookup Requirements**: Choose the services your RV requires (e.g., Electric, Water, etc.).
* **Number of Adults**: Enter the number of adults accompanying you.
* **Number of Children (5-17)**: Enter the number of children (5-17 years old) accompanying you.
* **Number of Toddlers (under 5)**: Enter the number of toddlers (under 5 years old) accompanying you.
* **Preferred Location or View**: If you have a preferred location or view, please specify.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: If you have any mobility or accessibility needs, please specify.
* **Bringing Pets**: If you are bringing pets, choose the number of pets you will be bringing.
* **Amenities Needed**: Select the amenities you require during your stay (e.g., Fire Pit, Picnic Table, etc.).
* **Additional Special Requests**: If you have any additional requests or preferences, please specify.
* **How to Receive Booking Confirmation**: Choose how you would like to receive your booking confirmation (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
