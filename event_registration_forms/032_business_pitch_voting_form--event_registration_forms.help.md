# Business Pitch Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Pitch Voting Form is designed for judges to evaluate and rate business pitches during a pitch competition, providing a systematic way to provide constructive feedback to pitching companies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Judge Name**, your professional contact **Email Address**, and **Judge Affiliation** (optional).
2. Enter the **Event Name** and **Event Date** of the pitch competition.
3. Rate the pitch on the following criteria:
	* **Business Idea Clarity** (1-10)
	* **Market Opportunity** (1-10)
	* **Business Model Viability** (1-10)
	* **Team Capability and Experience** (1-10)
	* **Competitive Advantage** (1-10)
	* **Financial Projections** (1-10)
	* **Presentation Quality** (1-10)
4. Indicate whether you would **Invest or Support** the pitch.
5. Optionally, mention the pitch's **Strengths** and **Areas for Improvement** in the dedicated text fields.
6. Finally, enter your overall score for the pitch (1-10) and provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Judge Name (`judge_name`, text, required)
* Enter your full name as the judge.

### 2. Email Address (`judge_email`, email, required)
* Enter your professional contact email address.

### 3. Judge Affiliation (`judge_affiliation`, text, optional)
* If you are affiliated with a particular organization or industry, please mention it here.

### 4. Event Name (`event_name`, text, required)
* Enter the name of the pitch competition.

### 5. Event Date (`event_date`, date, required)
* Enter the date of the pitch competition.

### 6. Pitch Number (`pitch_number`, number, required)
* Enter the number of the pitch you are evaluating.

### 7. Company Name (`company_name`, text, required)
* Enter the name of the pitching company.

### 8. Pitch Category (`pitch_category`, select_one, optional)
* Select a category for the pitch (e.g., Technology, Retail, etc.).

### 9. Business Idea Clarity (`business_idea_clarity`, number, required)
* Rate the pitch's clarity, from 1 to 10.

### 10. Market Opportunity (`market_opportunity`, number, required)
* Rate the pitch's market opportunity, from 1 to 10.

### 11. Business Model Viability (`business_model_viability`, number, required)
* Rate the pitch's business model viability, from 1 to 10.

### 12. Team Capability and Experience (`team_capability`, number, required)
* Rate the pitch's team capability and experience, from 1 to 10.

### 13. Competitive Advantage (`competitive_advantage`, number, required)
* Rate the pitch's competitive advantage, from 1 to 10.

### 14. Financial Projections (`financial_projections`, number, required)
* Rate the pitch's financial projections, from 1 to 10.

### 15. Presentation Quality (`presentation_quality`, number, required)
* Rate the pitch's presentation quality, from 1 to 10.

### 16. Funding Request Reasonableness (`funding_request_reasonableness`, number, required)
* Rate the pitch's funding request reasonableness, from 1 to 10.

### 17. Overall Score (`overall_score`, number, required)
* Enter your final score for the pitch, from 1 to 10.

### 18. Strengths (`strengths`, text, optional)
* Optionally, mention the strengths of the pitch.

### 19. Areas for Improvement (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional)
* Optionally, provide constructive feedback for improvement.

### 20. Would You Invest or Support? (`would_invest`, select_one, optional)
* Indicate your likelihood to support the pitch.
