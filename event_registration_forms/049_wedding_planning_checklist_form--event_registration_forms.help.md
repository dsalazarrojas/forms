# Wedding Planning Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you collect information about your wedding planning tasks and vendors in an efficient and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Wedding Planning Checklist**: Fill in a brief description of your wedding planning progress and any notable events or milestones.
2. **Tasks To Complete**: List any tasks or actions that need to be taken in order to complete your wedding planning.
3. **Vendors**: Select the vendors you are using for your wedding (such as Caterer, Photographer, DJ, etc.).
4. **Tasks Status**: Update the status of each task (e.g., Completed, In Progress, Pending).
5. **Notes**: Leave any additional notes or comments about your wedding planning.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
7. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
8. **Address**: Enter your address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Planning Checklist (wedding_planning_checklist)**: This text field is used to describe the current status and details of your wedding planning process. Please use this space to jot down any important updates or milestones in your planning journey.
* **Tasks To Complete (tasks_to_complete)**: This text field is designed for you to list any outstanding tasks that need to be taken care of before the wedding. Be sure to break down larger tasks into smaller, actionable steps.
* **Vendors (vendors)**: Here, you can select the vendors you have hired to assist with your wedding. Choose from the following options: Caterer, Photographer, DJ, Venue, Wedding Cake Designer, Wedding Planner, Wedding Officiant, Wedding Dress Designer, Wedding Florist, Wedding Videographer.
* **Tasks Status (tasks_status)**: Update the status of each task by selecting one of the following options: Completed, In Progress, Pending.
* **Notes (notes)**: Use this field to jot down any additional comments or notes about your wedding planning process.
* **Email (email)**: If you would like to be contacted by us or other vendors, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone (phone)**: If you would like to be contacted by us or other vendors, please enter your phone number here.
* **Address (address)**: If you would like to provide us with your address for delivery or pickup purposes, please enter your address here.

## Tips
- Be sure to regularly update this form with new information to keep your wedding planning organized and on track.
- Use the tasks status field to keep track of what needs to be done and what is already complete.
- Don't hesitate to reach out to us if you have any questions or need assistance with your wedding planning.
