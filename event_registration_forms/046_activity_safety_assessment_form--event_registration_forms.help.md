# Activity Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Activity Safety Assessment Form is a crucial tool to evaluate the safety of an activity before it takes place. This form helps ensure that organizers and assessors identify and mitigate potential risks and hazards, making informed decisions about whether an activity can proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct date and location of the activity.
2. Enter the official name of the activity and the number of expected participants.
3. Provide the details of the assessor, including their name and position.
4. Identify any potential hazards.
5. Determine the overall risk level of the activity.
6. Specify any safety measures that are already in place.
7. Describe the safety equipment available during the activity.
8. Confirm the emergency procedures in place.
9. Verify if staff members have received safety training.
10. Decide on the assessment approval status based on the risk level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Activity** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the official name of the activity being assessed.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, date, required): Enter the scheduled date of the activity.
* **Activity Location** (`activity_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the activity will take place.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the safety assessment.
* **Assessor Position** (`assessor_position`, text, required): Enter the job title of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Estimated Number of Participants** (`participant_count`, number, required): Enter the estimated number of participants in the activity.
* **Identified Hazards** (`hazard_identification`, text, required): Describe the potential hazards identified for the activity.
* **Overall Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, required): Choose from Low, Medium, High, or Very High based on the identified hazards.
* **Safety Measures in Place** (`safety_measures_in_place`, text, required): List any safety measures that are already implemented.
* **Safety Equipment Available** (`safety_equipment_available`, text, optional): Describe the safety equipment available during the activity. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, text, required): Describe the emergency procedures in place.
* **Staff Safety Training Verification** (`staff_training_verification`, select_one, required): Choose from Yes, all staff trained, Most staff trained, Some staff trained, or No staff trained.
* **Assessment Approval** (`assessment_approval`, select_one, required): Choose from Approved, Conditionally Approved, Requires Modifications, or Not Approved based on the overall risk level.

## Tips
* Ensure the information provided is accurate and truthful.
* Review all fields before submitting the form to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
* Consult with colleagues or superiors if unsure about any field.
* Keep in mind that some fields may have specific guidelines or regulations to follow. Review the relevant guidelines before proceeding.
