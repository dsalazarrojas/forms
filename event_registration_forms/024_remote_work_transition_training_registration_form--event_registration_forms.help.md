# Remote Work Transition Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees who are undergoing remote work transition training. The form will help the training team to track the progress of employees and better understand their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Employee Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
5. Enter your position in the "Position" field.
6. Enter the date of your training session in the "Date" field.
7. Select the type of training session you are attending from the options provided in the "Training Session" field.
8. Select your current training status from the options provided in the "Training Status" field.
9. Enter the location of your training session in the "Training Location" field.
10. Enter the date of your training session in the "Training Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records.
- **Manager Name** (text, optional): Enter your manager's name, if applicable.
- **Employee Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your company account.
- **Company** (text, optional): Enter your company name.
- **Position** (text, optional): Enter your job title or current position.
- **Date** (date, optional): Select the date of your training session.
- **Training Session** (text, optional): Select the type of training session you are attending (e.g., online, in-person, etc.).
- **Training Status** (select_one, required): Select your current training status from the options provided (e.g., Active, In Progress, Complete).
- **Training Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of your training session.
- **Training Date** (date, optional): Select the date of your training session. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your manager or training coordinator for assistance.
- This form is for your own personal use and will not be shared with any third-party organizations.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the form owner at form@gic.mx.
