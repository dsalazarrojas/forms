# Videography Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Videography Workshop Enrollment Form is used to collect participant information and workshop details for videography workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the participant and the workshop.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the *required* label.
- Use a unique identifier or username for each participant to avoid duplicates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information**: 
  - **Participant Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
  - **Email** (email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
  - **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
  - **Organization** (text, required): Enter the participant's organization.
  - **Title** (text, required): Enter the participant's title.
  - **Additional Info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about the participant.
- **Workshop Details**: 
  - **Workshop Type** (select multiple, required): Select the type of workshop the participant is interested in (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced).
  - **Workshop Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the workshop.
  - **Time Slots** (select one, required): Select a time slot for the workshop (08:00-12:00, 13:00-17:00, 18:00-22:00).
  - **Workshop Language** (select one, required): Select the language of the workshop (English, Spanish, French).
  - **Workshop Duration** (number, required): Enter the duration of the workshop in hours.
  - **Workshop Start Date** (date, required): Enter the start date of the workshop.
  - **Workshop End Date** (date, required): Enter the end date of the workshop.
  - **Workshop Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the workshop.
  - **Workshop Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the workshop.
  - **Workshop Attendance** (select one, required): Select whether or not the participant will attend the workshop (True/False).
  - **Workshop Attendance Count** (number, required): Enter the number of attendees for the workshop.
  - **Workshop Total Cost** (number, required): Enter the total cost of the workshop.
  - **Payment Method** (select one, required): Select the payment method for the participant (PayPal, Bank Transfer, Cash on Delivery).
  - **Payment Status** (select one, required): Select the payment status for the participant (Paid, Pending, Not Paid).
  - **Participant Status** (select one, required): Select the participant status (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled).
  - **Workshop Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the workshop.
  - **Participant Total Cost** (number, required): Enter the total cost for the participant.
