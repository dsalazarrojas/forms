# music_festival_vendor_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music festival vendor extension form is designed to collect information from vendors interested in extending their services or products to the music festival. The form is used to gather details about the vendor's business, including their contact information, event-related information, and contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form submission page with any additional comments or information about your request.
2. Provide your vendor details, including your company name, contact person, and contact email.
3. Enter event details, such as event name, date, and location.
4. Describe the contract details, including any relevant terms or conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `note`, required: false): This is the first page of the form where you can enter any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name, the contact person, and contact email to help us understand who you are and how to contact you.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide the event name, date, and location to help us understand the context of your request.
* **Contract Details** (`contract_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the contract terms, including any relevant conditions or agreements you'd like to make.
