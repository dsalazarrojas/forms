# Laser Tag Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Laser Tag Waiver Form is a document that requires participants to provide their personal and emergency contact information, medical details, and sign a waiver to acknowledge their involvement in the Laser Tag event. This form is essential for our team to know who is participating and to ensure that everyone is aware of the event's rules and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the **Participant Information** section.
2. Provide your parental information (if applicable) in the **Parent Information** section.
3. Share any medical information you feel is necessary in the **Medical Information** section.
4. Read and sign the **Waiver** section to acknowledge your understanding of the event's rules and guidelines.
5. Provide emergency contact information, including phone and email, in the **Emergency Contact Information** section.
6. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Please fill in your name, age, and contact information (address, phone, and email).
* **Parent Information** (if applicable): This section is for your parents or guardians to provide their contact information, including their name, address, phone, and email).
* **Medical Information**: Share any medical conditions, allergies, or medications you're currently taking. This helps us to be prepared in case of an emergency.
* **Waiver**: By signing this section, you acknowledge that you understand and agree to follow the event rules and guidelines.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Provide the name, phone number, and email of an emergency contact person in case we need to reach them.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.

## Tips
- Please review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, ask us for help.
