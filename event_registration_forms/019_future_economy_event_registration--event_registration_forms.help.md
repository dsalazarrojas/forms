# Future Economy Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Future Economy Event Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees to the Future Economy conference or workshop. This form is used to gather essential details about the event and the person registering.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Event Details" field on the first page, which is used to provide information about the event.
2. On the second page, fill out the "Contact Details" field, which is used to provide your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details (Page 1)
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Details 2** (`Event Details 2`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any additional details about the event, you can enter them here.

### Contact Details (Page 2)
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your registration is completed successfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
