# Digital Event Badge Prototype Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Event Badge Prototype Application form is used to collect information from attendees to verify their identity and allow them to be considered for a digital event badge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your event details, if necessary.
2. Provide your attendee name.
3. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it for additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): 
  Enter your event details, if necessary. This field is optional, so you can leave it blank if you don't have any event details to provide.

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required: false): 
  Enter your name as it appears on your badge.

* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
  Enter your email address. This will be used as the primary contact method for you, so make sure it's accurate.

* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): 
  If you have a phone number, enter it for additional contact information.
