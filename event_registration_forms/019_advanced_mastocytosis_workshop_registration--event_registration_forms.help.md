# Advanced Mastocytosis Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the registration process for the Advanced Mastocytosis Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your full name, email address, phone number, and professional role.
2. Choose how you will attend the workshop (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
3. Select your preferred accommodation requirements, if any.
4. Indicate any dietary restrictions or allergies.
5. Share your experience with mastocytosis and your primary area of interest in the field.
6. Specify the workshop you are registering for and the date you will attend.
7. Choose your payment method.
8. Confirm your registration by agreeing to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Professional Role** (`professional_role`, select_one, required): Choose your role in the healthcare field, such as Physician, Nurse, Researcher, Medical Student, Patient Advocate, or Other.
* **Institution Organization** (`institution_organization`, text, optional): If you are registering from an institution, enter its name.
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, text, required): Select the workshop you are registering for.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the workshop you will attend.
* **Attendance Mode** (`attendance_mode`, select_one, required): Choose how you will attend the workshop (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Accommodation Requirements** (`accommodation_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Select any accommodation requirements you have, such as wheelchair accessibility, large print materials, sign language interpreter, or dietary restrictions.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, enter them here.
* **Mastocytosis Experience** (`mastocytosis_experience`, select_one, required): Choose how much experience you have with mastocytosis (No experience, Limited experience, Moderate experience, Extensive experience, Expert).
* **Primary Interest Area** (`primary_interest_area`, select_one, required): Select the area of mastocytosis that interests you most (Diagnosis, Treatment options, Patient management, Research developments, Clinical trials).
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, text, optional): Specify what you hope to learn from this workshop.
* **Networking Opportunities** (`networking_opportunities`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are interested in networking with other professionals (Yes, No, Maybe).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay for the workshop (Credit Card, Check, Institution Purchase Order, Other).
* **Registration Confirmation** (`registration_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop (Yes, No).
