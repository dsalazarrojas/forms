# Actor Coordination Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actor Coordination Communication Form is designed to facilitate communication between actors and production teams in a film or theatrical production. It collects essential information to ensure a smooth production process, including production title, contact details, transportation needs, and other relevant updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Production Title** field with the title of the production you are working on.
2. Enter your **Actor Name** in the designated field.
3. Indicate your **Role Assigned** to ensure the production team knows your character's requirements.
4. Provide your **Primary Production Contact** name and any other necessary contact information.
5. Confirm your **Call Time** for the next shoot day.
6. Select your **Transportation Requirements** from the options provided.
7. List any **Dietary Restrictions** you have for catering purposes.
8. Update your **Costume Fitting Status** to reflect your progress.
9. Choose the **Current Script Version in Possession** you are working with.
10. Report any **Upcoming Conflicts or Availability Changes** to the production team.
11. Provide your **Emergency Contact Number** in case of an urgent situation.
12. Add any **Notes to the Production Coordinator**, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Title**: Enter the title of the production you are working on.
* **Actor Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on the cast list.
* **Role Assigned**: Indicate your character's name and any special requirements.
* **Primary Production Contact**: Name and contact information of your primary contact person.
* **Call Time Confirmation**: Confirm your call time for the next shoot day.
* **Transportation Requirements**:
	+ Select "No" if you'll arrange your own transportation.
	+ Select "Yes - Pick up required" if the production needs to pick you up.
	+ Select "Yes - Travel reimbursement requested" if you need reimbursement for transportation costs.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: List any food allergies or dietary needs for catering purposes.
* **Costume Fitting Status**:
	+ Select "Completed" if you've had a fitting.
	+ Select "Scheduled" if a fitting is arranged.
	+ Select "Needs to be scheduled" if a fitting is still required.
	+ Select "Not applicable" if you don't need a costume fitting.
* **Current Script Version in Possession**: Enter the current version of the script you're working with.
* **Upcoming Conflicts or Availability Changes**: Report any changes to your schedule that may impact the production.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Provide the contact number of an emergency contact person.
* **Notes to Production Coordinator**: Add any other urgent updates or comments to the production team.
