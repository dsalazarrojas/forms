# Kiss And Tell Valentines Dance Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kiss And Tell Valentines Dance Party RSVP Form is used to collect information about guests attending the dance party. This form will ask for basic details such as name, contact information, event specifics, and party planning details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (name, email, phone number).
2. Select the date and time of the event you plan to attend.
3. Choose the location of the event.
4. Indicate the number of guests you will be bringing.
5. Confirm if you will be bringing any guests or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Kiss And Tell Valentines Dance Party Guest** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
- **guest_email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **guest_phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **date_of_event** (`date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date of the event you plan to attend.
- **time_of_event** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time of the event you plan to attend.
- **dance_party_location** (`text`, text, required/optional): Choose the location of the event.
- **number_of_guests** (`number`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing.
- **names_of_guests** (select_multiple, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether you will be bringing any guests or not.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the party organizers.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help!
