# Panel Discussion Speaker Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for speakers to register their details for a panel discussion event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Speaker's Name**, a required field that should be a brief introduction of yourself.
2. Enter your **Speaker's Email**, a required field that will be used for communication and verification purposes.
3. Select the **Topic** you will be discussing, a required field that should be specific and relevant to the event.
4. Enter the name of the **Panel** you will be part of, a required field that helps in organizing the discussion.
5. Provide a brief **Speaker Bio**, a field where you can introduce yourself and share your expertise.
6. Upload any **Files** you want to share with the event organizers, such as your resume or presentation.
7. Enter your **Speaker's Phone**, an optional field that allows speakers to provide their contact information.
8. Add a **Bio URL** if you have a personal website or social media profile, an optional field for additional information.
9. Upload a **Speaker Image** if you want to be featured on the event's promotional materials, an optional field.
10. Upload a **Speaker Audio** file if you want to share your audio content, an optional field.
11. Upload any additional **Other Files** you want to share with the event organizers, an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker's Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter a brief introduction of yourself as a speaker.
* **Speaker's Email** (`email`, required): A field to enter your email address for communication and verification purposes.
* **Topic** (`text`, required): Select the specific topic you will be discussing during the event.
* **Panel** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the panel you will be part of for organization and coordination.
* **Speaker Bio** (`text`, optional): A field where you can introduce yourself and share your expertise.
* **Files** (`text`, optional): Upload any files you want to share with the event organizers, such as your resume or presentation.
* **Speaker's Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Bio URL** (`text`, optional): Enter your personal website or social media profile URL for additional information.
* **Speaker Image** (`text`, optional): Upload a high-quality image of yourself for promotional materials.
* **Speaker Audio** (`text`, optional): Upload an audio file to share with the event organizers.
* **Other Files** (`text`, optional): Upload any additional files you want to share with the event organizers.
* **Speaker's Bio** (`note`, optional): A field to enter a short and concise bio about yourself.
