# Event Coordination Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for workshop participants to provide their information for event coordination and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Choose your preferred workshop status.
3. Select any dietary restrictions that apply to you.
4. Write any additional comments or messages in the `participant_message` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Info
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Your full name.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Your email address.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number.
* **other_participant_name** (`other_participant_name`, `text`, optional): If you are registering on behalf of someone else, please enter their name here.
* **workshop** (`workshop`, `select_one`, required): Are you attending the workshop? (Yes/No)
* **participant_diet** (`participant_diet`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your dietary restrictions (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free).
* **participant_message** (`participant_message`, `text`, optional): Any additional comments or messages.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you are registering on behalf of someone else, please provide their contact information as well.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please select all that apply to ensure our catering team can accommodate your needs.
