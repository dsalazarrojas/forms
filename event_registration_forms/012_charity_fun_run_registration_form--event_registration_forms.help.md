# Charity Fun Run Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a charity fun run event. It is used to collect information about you, the participant, in order to properly manage your registration and ensure your safety and participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you have completed all required fields.
2. Enter your information in the provided fields, following the guidelines and instructions provided.
3. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (2) (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (3) (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (4) (Required): Enter a valid email address that we will use to send event information and updates.
* **Phone Number** (5) (Required): Enter a valid phone number for communication on event day.
* **Date of Birth** (6) (Required): Enter your date of birth to determine your age category.
* **Age Category** (7) (Required): Select your age category from the options provided.
* **Race Distance** (9) (Required): Select the distance you will be participating in.
* **Participation Type** (10) (Required): Select how you will be participating (running, walking, or other).
* **T-Shirt Size** (11) (Required): Select the size you would like your event t-shirt to be.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (13) (Required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (14) (Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Any Medical Conditions** (15) (Optional): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Medication Allergies** (16) (Optional): If you have any medication allergies, list them here.
* **Fundraising Participation** (18) (Optional): If you will be participating in fundraising, select yes and enter your fundraising goal.
* **Fundraising Goal Amount** (19) (Optional): Enter your fundraising goal amount.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (21) (Required): Select yes to agree to the liability waiver terms.
* **Photo Consent** (22) (Optional): If you consent to photography, select yes.

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed in order to complete the registration process.
