# Expo Media Briefing RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to RSVP to the Expo Media Briefing event, allowing them to confirm their media type preference (e.g., Press Briefing, Video Conference, or Phone Call) and provide any special requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your preferred media type from the options provided.
2.  Enter your company name if you have one.
3.  Enter your name.
4.  Enter your email address.
5.  Enter your phone number.
6.  If you have any special requirements, enter them in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Confirmation** (`media_confirmation`, select_one): 
Choose your preferred media type to receive the Expo Media Briefing briefing. Options include Press Briefing, Video Conference, and Phone Call.
* **Company** (`company`, text): 
If you have a company, enter your company name here.
* **Name** (`name`, text): 
Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email): 
Enter your email address where you would like to receive the briefing.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): 
Enter your phone number where you would like to be contacted.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text): 
If you have any special requirements or requests, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to select your preferred media type to ensure you receive the briefing in the format you prefer.
* If you have any special requirements, be as specific as possible so we can best accommodate your needs.
* If you need to make any changes to your RSVP after submission, please contact us directly.
