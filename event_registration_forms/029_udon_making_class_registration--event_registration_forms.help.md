# udon_making_class_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a udon-making class or workshop. Fill out this form to create a new session with your chosen instructor and class details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a date for your class session using the **Session Date** field.
2. Choose the time for your class session using the **Session Time** field.
3. Enter the name of your instructor in the **Instructor Name** field.
4. Provide the class name in the **Class Name** field.
5. Select the desired start and end time for your class session using the **Session Start Time** and **Session End Time** fields respectively.
6. Specify the maximum capacity for your class using the **Class Capacity** field.
7. Enter the email address of your instructor in the **Instructor Email** field.
8. Provide the phone number of your instructor in the **Instructor Phone** field.
9. Choose the type of class enrollment using the **Class Enrollment** field.
10. Enter the registration fee for your class in the **Registration Fee** field.
11. Provide a label or description for your registration fee in the **Session Fee Label** field.
12. Enter the instructor's fee for your class in the **Instructor Fee** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`date`, required/optional): Select a date for your class session.
* **Session Time** (`time`, required/optional): Choose the time for your class session.
* **Instructor Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Class Name** (`text`, required/optional): Provide the class name.
* **Session Start Time** (`time`, required/optional): Select your desired session start time.
* **Session End Time** (`time`, required/optional): Select your desired session end time.
* **Class Capacity** (`number`, required/optional): Specify the maximum capacity for your class.
* **Instructor Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of your instructor.
* **Instructor Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Provide the phone number of your instructor.
* **Class Enrollment** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of class enrollment.
* **Registration Fee** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the registration fee for your class.
* **Session Fee Label** (`text`, required/optional): Provide a label or description for your registration fee.
* **Instructor Fee** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the instructor's fee for your class.
