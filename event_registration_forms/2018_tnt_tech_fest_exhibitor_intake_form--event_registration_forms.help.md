# 2018 TNT Tech Fest Exhibitor Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from exhibitors participating in the 2018 TNT Tech Fest event. It gathers details about the company, booth requirements, and participation preferences to assign suitable booth spaces and facilitate logistical arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Please fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
*   Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and provide enough details to allow us to assign the best possible booth space and plan for your needs.
*   If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional Comments or Questions" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company/Organization Name** (2): Enter the legal name of your organization.
*   **Booth Type Requested** (3): Select the type of booth space you need.
*   **Primary Contact Person** (4): Enter the full name of the person responsible for the booth.
*   **Contact Email Address** (5): Provide the email address used for all communications.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (6): Include the area code in the phone number.
*   **Company Website** (7): If available, provide the URL of your company website.
*   **Brief Description of Your Booth** (8): Describe what visitors will see/experience at your booth.
*   **Expected Daily Visitors** (9): Estimate the number of people visiting your booth per day.
*   **Power Requirements** (10): Specify electrical needs (e.g., outlets, amperage).
*   **Internet Access Required** (11): Indicate if you need WiFi or a hardwired connection.
*   **Special Equipment or Setup Needs** (12): List any special requirements for your booth setup.
*   **Marketing Materials Distribution** (13): Choose what materials you will distribute at your booth (e.g., Brochures, Business cards, etc.).
*   **Accessibility Accommodations** (14): Specify any special accessibility requirements for your booth.
*   **Days Attending** (15): Select which days your organization will participate in the event.
*   **Preferred Setup Time** (16): Choose when you would like to begin setting up your booth.
*   **Preferred Teardown Time** (17): Choose when you would like to end your participation.
*   **Sponsorship Interest** (18): Indicate if you are interested in becoming a sponsor.
*   **Dietary Restrictions for Catered Events** (19): Specify any dietary needs if attending catered events.
*   **Additional Comments or Questions** (20): Any other information we should know about your participation.

## Tips

*   Ensure you answer all required fields accurately and completely.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
