# Sailing Club Regatta Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sailing Club Regatta Attendance Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants, sailing club staff, and organizers to ensure a successful and enjoyable regatta event. This form will help streamline communication, enhance event execution, and provide valuable insights for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Registration Form** (Page 1)
	* Please provide a brief description of yourself and your role in the regatta.
	* You can fill in this form as a participant, sailing club staff member, or organizer.
2. **Participant Details** (Page 2)
	* Enter your name and contact information.
	* Provide any other relevant details about yourself as a participant.
3. **Sailing Club Info** (Page 2)
	* Enter your sailing club's name and contact information.
	* Provide any other relevant information about your sailing club.
4. **Sailing Club Regatta Info** (Page 3)
	* Enter the regatta's name, date, and location.
	* Provide any other relevant information about the regatta.
5. **Regatta Details** (Page 3)
	* Enter any additional details about the regatta, such as course information or rules.
6. **Sailing Club Regatta Sponsors** (Page 4)
	* If applicable, list the sponsors of the regatta event.
7. **Sailing Club Regatta Officers** (Page 4)
	* If applicable, list the key officials or officers involved in the regatta event.
8. **Sailing Club Regatta Boat** (Page 5)
	* Enter the boat information for the regatta event.
9. **Sailing Club Regatta Safety** (Page 5)
	* Enter any safety information or protocols for the regatta event.
10. **Sailing Club Regatta Weather** (Page 6)
	* Enter any weather-related information or forecasts for the regatta event.
11. **Sailing Club Regatta Notes** (Page 7)
	* Enter any additional notes or comments about the regatta event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form**
	+ This is the first page of the form where you can provide a brief description of yourself and your role in the regatta.
* **Participant Details**
	+ Please enter your name and contact information to help us reach you about the regatta event.
* **Sailing Club Info**
	+ Enter your sailing club's name and contact information to help us connect with your club.
* **Sailing Club Regatta Info**
	+ Enter the regatta's name, date, and location to help us prepare for the event.
* **Regatta Details**
	+ Enter any additional details about the regatta, such as course information or rules.
* **Sailing Club Regatta Sponsors**
	+ If your regatta has sponsors, list their names and contact information.
* **Sailing Club Regatta Officers**
	+ If your regatta has key officials or officers, list their names and contact information.
* **Sailing Club Regatta Boat**
	+ Enter the boat information for the regatta event.
* **Sailing Club Regatta Safety**
	+ Enter any safety information or protocols for the regatta event.
* **Sailing Club Regatta Weather**
	+ Enter any weather-related information or forecasts for the regatta event.
* **Sailing Club Regatta Notes**
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about the regatta event.
