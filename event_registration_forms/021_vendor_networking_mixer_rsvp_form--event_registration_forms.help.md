# Vendor Networking Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from event organizers and attendees for a vendor networking mixer event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Event Organizer Information" page.
   - Enter the title of the event.
   - Enter the event details, including event name, date, time, and location.
   - Set an RSVP deadline for the event.
2. Select the "Attendee Information" page.
   - Enter your company name.
   - Enter your email address.
   - Optionally, enter your phone number.
   - Optionally, select your dietary needs.
   - Optionally, select your networking goals.
   - Optionally, select if you will attend the mixer.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Information (event_organizer_info)**: Enter the title of the event.
* **Attendee Information (attendee_info)**: Enter your company name, email address, and optionally, enter your phone number.
* **Company Name (company_name)**: Enter your company name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Dietary Needs (dietary_needs)**: Select your dietary needs, if any.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Networking Goals (networking_goals)**: Select your networking goals, if any.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Mixer Attendance (mixers_attendance)**: Select whether you will attend the mixer.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Notes (notes)**: Enter any additional notes for the event.
* **Event Name (event_name)**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date (event_date)**: Enter the date of the event, if applicable.
* **Event Time (event_time)**: Enter the time of the event, if applicable.
* **Event Location (event_location)**: Enter the location of the event, if applicable.
* **RSVP Deadline (rsvp_deadline)**: Set a deadline for RSVPs, if applicable.
* **Mixer Count (mixers_count)**: Enter the number of mixers attending, if applicable.
