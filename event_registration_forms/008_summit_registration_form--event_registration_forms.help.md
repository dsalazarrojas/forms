# summit_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The summit_registration_form is used to register users for a summit event. It collects basic contact information and preferences for the user, allowing them to select which track(s) they are interested in attending.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. Please note that this field is a standard email format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the track(s) you are interested in by choosing from the options in the "Which track" field. Please note that you can choose only one option for this field.
6. In the "Which tracks" field, select multiple tracks you are interested in, if applicable.
7. For the "Date" field, select a date from the calendar picker.
8. Enter the Time of the event in the "Time" field.
9. In the "Note" fields, enter any additional information you feel is necessary for the organizers or your fellow attendees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it should be displayed on any event-related materials or correspondence. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it should be displayed on any event-related materials or correspondence.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address in the format `username@domain.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by organizers or fellow attendees.
* **Which track** (`select_one`, Select One, Required): Choose one track you are interested in attending from the available options. This selection will help organizers better plan the event schedule.
* **Which tracks** (`select_multiple`, Select Multiple, Optional): If applicable, choose multiple tracks you are interested in attending.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Select the date of the event using the calendar picker.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the event you plan to attend.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional information you feel is necessary for the organizers or fellow attendees.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional information you feel is necessary for the organizers or fellow attendees.
* **Note 3** (`note_3`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional information you feel is necessary for the organizers or fellow attendees.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields accurately to avoid any delays or issues with your registration.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or options, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Note that multiple registrations from the same person may be subject to review or rejection.
