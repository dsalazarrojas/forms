# Young Writers Weekend Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Young Writers Weekend Registration" form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending a writing workshop or event. This form helps the event organizers gather essential details about the event and the participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the workshop you wish to attend by choosing "Yes" or "No".
2.  Enter your name and email address for registration purposes.
3.  Provide your phone number for contact.
4.  Choose the payment method you prefer.
5.  Enter the payment amount.
6.  Provide a brief description of the event.
7.  Enter the event start and end times.
8.  Specify the workshop type.
9.  Enter the event date.
10.  Enter a brief description of the workshop.
11.  Choose the status of your payment and workshop participation.
12.  Select the status of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select a Workshop** (`workshop_selection`, select_one, required: false): Choose the workshop you wish to attend by selecting "Yes" or "No".
*   **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name for registration purposes.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the payment method you prefer.
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the payment amount.
*   **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop.
*   **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the workshop.
*   **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the event.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the event. (Note: Consider renaming to "Event Start Time" for clarity.)
*   **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, text, required: false): Specify the type of workshop.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
*   **Event Time 2** (`event_time_2`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the event. (Note: Consider renaming to "Event End Time" for clarity.)
*   **Workshop Description** (`workshop_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the workshop.
*   **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of your payment.
*   **Not Paid** (`payment_status_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you have not paid.
*   **Paid** (`payment_status_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you have paid.
*   **Workshop Status** (`workshop_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the workshop.
*   **Not Started** (`workshop_status_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the workshop has not started.
*   **Started** (`workshop_status_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the workshop has started.
*   **Finished** (`workshop_status_3`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the workshop has finished.
*   **Event Status** (`event_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the event.
*   **Event Status 1** (`event_status_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the event.
*   **Event Status 2** (`event_status_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the event.
*   **Event Status 3** (`event_status_3`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the event.

## Tips

- Make sure to select the correct workshop you wish to attend.
- Enter accurate and valid contact information.
- Choose your preferred payment method and amount.
- Provide a brief and clear description of the event.
- Ensure you have the correct payment and workshop status.
- Confirm the event status.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submission.
