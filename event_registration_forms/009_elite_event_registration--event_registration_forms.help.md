# Elite Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Elite Event Registration" form is designed to collect personal and event-related information from attendees for the "Elite Event" managed by our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Elite Event Registration" form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the event details in the "event_details" field.
6. Check the box to indicate your event preferences in the "event_preferences" field.
7. Select one of the options in the "consent" field to indicate your agreement or disagreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, required: false): Your first name is required to identify you as an attendee.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required: false): Your last name is required to identify you as an attendee.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address to be contacted regarding event updates and information.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you in case of event changes or updates.
* **Event Details** (event_details, text, required: false): Briefly describe the event you are interested in, including any relevant details or preferences.
* **Event Preferences** (event_preferences, text, required: false): Indicate your preferences or interests for the event by checking the box next to each option.
* **Consent** (consent, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options to indicate your understanding and agreement or disagreement with the terms and conditions of the event.
