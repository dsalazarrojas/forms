# Charity Event Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect comprehensive data about charity events, including event details, financial data, and feedback from attendees. It helps event organizers report on the success of their events and allows the organization to understand their fundraising efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact email address in the respective fields.
2. Provide the event name, event date, and event location.
3. Select the type of event that took place (e.g., Gala, Auction, Charity run or walk, etc.).
4. Enter the total number of attendees and the total amount of funds raised and expenses.
5. Specify the net profit made from the event.
6. Describe the fundraising activities that were conducted (e.g., Ticket sales, Silent auction, Live auction, etc.).
7. List any sponsors who supported the event.
8. Enter the total number of volunteers who participated.
9. Share your key success metrics and lessons learned from the event.
10. Provide recommendations for future events.
11. (Optional) Share any attendee feedback and media coverage.
12. Confirm that photos or videos from the event are available (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`reporter_email`, Email, Required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your charity or organization.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date the event took place.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, Text, Required): Enter the venue of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of event that took place (e.g., Gala, Auction, etc.).
* **Total Attendees** (`total_attendees`, Number, Required): Enter the actual count of attendees.
* **Total Funds Raised** (`total_funds_raised`, Number, Required): Enter the total amount of funds raised.
* **Total Expenses** (`total_expenses`, Number, Required): Enter the total costs associated with the event.
* **Net Profit** (`net_profit`, Number, Required): Calculate the funds after expenses.
* **Fundraising Activities** (`fundraising_activities`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the fundraising activities that took place (e.g., Ticket sales, Silent auction, etc.).
* **Sponsors** (`sponsors`, Text, Optional): If applicable, list any sponsors who supported the event.
* **Number of Volunteers** (`volunteer_count`, Number, Required): Enter the total number of volunteers who participated.
* **Key Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`, Text, Required): Share your key success metrics from the event.
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, Text, Required): Describe any challenges faced during the event.
* **Lessons Learned** (`lessons_learned`, Text, Required): Summarize your takeaways from the event.
* **Recommendations for Future Events** (`recommendations`, Text, Required): Provide suggestions for future events.
* **Attendee Feedback Summary** (`attendee_feedback_summary`, Text, Optional): Share any feedback from attendees (if applicable).
* **Media Coverage** (`media_coverage`, Text, Optional): Share any media mentions (if applicable).
* **Photos or Videos Available** (`photos_videos`, Select One, Optional): Confirm if photos or videos from the event are available.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date the report is submitted.
