# Refreshment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to submit refreshment requests for events, conferences, or meetings. Please ensure that the requested refreshments align with the planned schedule and resources. Duplicate requests will not be accepted. If you have already submitted a refreshment request, please do not resubmit it as it will not be considered.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the event(s) you wish to register for by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Event Registration" section.
2. If you selected "Yes," proceed to the next sections to provide more details.
3. In the "Refreshment Requests" section, describe the refreshments you would like to request.
4. Choose the type of refreshments you require (Snacks, Meals, or Other).
5. Provide your name and contact information in the "Refreshment Requester," "Contact Name," and "Contact Email" sections.
6. Enter the date and time of your request in the "Request Date" and "Request Time" sections.
7. Specify the number of guests you are requesting refreshments for in the "Number of Guests" section.
8. If you have any dietary restrictions, please list them in the "Dietary Restrictions" section.
9. If you have any special requests, please provide them in the "Special Requests" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration** (Yes, No): Select "Yes" for events you wish to register for or "No" if you are not attending.
* **Refreshment Requests** (Text): Describe the refreshments you would like to request in this field.
* **Refreshment Type** (Select One): Choose the type of refreshments you require (Snacks, Meals, or Other).
* **Refreshment Requester** (Text): Provide your name.
* **Organization Name** (Text): Enter the name of the organization requesting refreshments.
* **Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Request Date** (Date): Enter the date you are requesting refreshments for.
* **Request Time** (Time): Enter the time you are requesting refreshments for.
* **Number of Guests** (Number): Enter the number of guests you are requesting refreshments for.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Text): List any dietary restrictions you or your guests may have.
* **Special Requests** (Text): Provide any special requests you may have for refreshments.
