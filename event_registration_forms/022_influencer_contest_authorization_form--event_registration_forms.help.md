# Influencer Contest Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information from individuals who want to participate in our influencer contest.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information in the provided fields.
2. Ensure all fields are correctly filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your last name.
* **Address (address)**: Enter your residential or business address.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Consent (consent)**: Check the box to consent to participate in the contest.
* **Consent Date (consent_date)**: Select the date you gave your consent.

Note: Please note that all fields are optional by default. However, you must fill in the required fields for the form to be processed.
