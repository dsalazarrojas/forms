# Retail Digital Payment Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather registration information from attendees for the Retail Digital Payment Summit. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete all required fields (marked as `required`).
- For optional fields, you can choose not to fill them out, but it will help us get a more complete picture of your participation.
- If you need help or have any questions, contact our event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name (`1. event_name`, Text, Required)
* A brief title of the event you are registering for, e.g., "Retail Digital Payment Summit 2023".

### Attendee Name (`2. attendee_name`, Text, Optional)
* Your name as the person registering for the event.

### Email Address (`3. email_address`, Email, Optional)
* Your email address where we can reach you with event updates and information.

### Participation Type (`4. participation_type`, Select One, Optional)
* Choose how you plan to participate in the event:
	+ In-Person: You will attend the event in person.
	+ Remote: You will attend the event remotely.

### Special Requirements (`5. special_requirements`, Text, Optional)
* If you have any special requirements or requests, such as dietary restrictions, accessibility needs, or other relevant information, please let us know here.

### Company Name (`6. company_name`, Text, Optional)
* If you represent a company, please provide the name of your company.

### Job Title (`7. job_title`, Text, Optional)
* Your job title or position within your company.

Note: The fields are numbered as per the `id` property in the YAML inputSchema.
