# Conference Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Communication Form is a crucial part of the registration process, allowing attendees to provide their contact information and preferences for communication during the event. By filling out this form, attendees can ensure they receive relevant and timely updates, reminders, and notifications about the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Conference Communication Form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and understand each field's purpose.
2. Enter your attendee name, which will be used to identify you during the event.
3. Provide your conference registration ID, found on your confirmation email.
4. Enter your primary email address for receiving notifications and updates.
5. Enter your mobile number for SMS alerts or leave it blank if you don't have one.
6. Choose your preferred method of communication (email, SMS, or conference app push notifications).
7. Decide whether to subscribe to the post-conference newsletter and future event alerts.
8. Choose whether you want to receive automated session reminders before your sessions start.
9. Select whether you grant permission to include your profile in the attendee networking portal.
10. Confirm your willingness to participate in brief real-time session feedback surveys.
11. Select your preferred language for communication (English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, or Japanese).
12. (Optional) Provide an on-site emergency contact name and phone number.
13. Enter any communication notes or special requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name (Attendee Name)**: Enter your name as you would like to be known during the event.
* **Conference Registration ID (Registration ID)**: Found on your confirmation email, this is your unique identifier for the conference.
* **Primary Email for Notifications (Primary Email)**: Enter your primary email address to receive updates, reminders, and notifications about the conference.
* **Mobile Number for SMS Alerts (Primary Phone)**: Enter your mobile number for urgent schedule changes or emergency updates, or leave it blank if you don't have one.
* **Preferred Method for Official Updates (Preferred Contact Method)**: Choose how you prefer to receive official updates (email, SMS, or conference app push notifications).
* **Subscribe to Post-Conference Newsletter and Future Event Alerts (Subscribe to Newsletter)**: Decide whether to receive updates about future events and the post-conference newsletter.
* **Would you like Automated Reminders before your bookmarked sessions? (Session Reminders)**: Choose whether to receive automated reminders 15 minutes before your sessions start.
* **Grant permission to include your profile in the attendee networking portal (Networking Access)**: Select whether to grant permission to others to contact you within the app.
* **Are you willing to participate in brief real-time session feedback surveys? (Feedback Participation)**: Confirm your willingness to participate in brief surveys during the event.
* **Preferred Communication Language (Language Preference)**: Select your preferred language for communication during the event.
* **On-site Emergency Contact Name (Emergency Contact Name)**: (Optional) Provide an on-site emergency contact name in case of an emergency.
* **On-site Emergency Contact Phone Number (Emergency Contact Phone)**: (Optional) Enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **Communication Notes or Special Requests (Comments or Special Requests)**: Enter any communication-related notes or special requests you may have.
