# store_employee_training_session_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow store employees to register for training sessions. It collects relevant information for training session planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct **Training Session** field and provide the details of the session you wish to attend.
2. Enter your **Employee** information, including your name.
3. Enter your **Email** address.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** for communication purposes.
5. Select the **Training Date** for the session.
6. Choose the **Start Time** of the session.
7. Enter the **Reporting Manager** who will be responsible for the session.
8. Enter the **Reporting Manager's Email** address.
9. Select the **Session Location** for the training session.
10. Optionally, specify the **Session Duration** in minutes.
11. Click submit to complete the form and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session** (`training_session`, text, required): Enter the title of the training session you wish to attend.
* **Employee** (`employee`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Select the date of the training session.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Choose the start time of the session.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (`reporting_manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your reporting manager's email address.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, optional): Specify the duration of the training session in minutes.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the training session.

## Tips

* Ensure you complete all required fields.
* Verify the accuracy of the information entered.
* If you encounter any issues while using this form, contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
