# Pitch Competition Scoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect numerical and written feedback from team members for a pitch competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Team Name field with the name of the team you are scoring.
2. Enter a numerical score for the team in the Team Score field.
3. If applicable, enter the maximum score out of in the Team Score Out of field.
4. Provide a brief comment for the team score in the Team Score Comment field.
5. If you have any further comments about the Team Score Out of, you can provide them in the Team Score Out of Comment field.
6. Enter the numerical score that represents the team's average score in the Team Score Averaged field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competition Information**: This is where you should indicate which competition this scoring is for. Please enter the name of the competition.
* **Team Name**: This is the name of the team you are scoring. Please enter the correct team name to ensure that your score is assigned to the correct team.
* **Team Score**: This is the numerical score you are giving to the team. Please enter a score between 0 and the Team Score Out of value.
* **Team Score Out of**: This is the maximum score the team could have received. If your Team Score is not out of this range, your score will be scaled accordingly.
* **Team Score Comment**: This is a brief comment on why you scored the team the score you did. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **Team Score Out of Comment**: This is an optional field for any additional comments you have about the Team Score Out of.
* **Team Score Averaged**: This is the numerical score that represents the team's average score. Please enter the average of the scores you and other team members have given to this team.
