# Event Registration Form With Sofort Payment Method - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for event registration, including participant details and payment method preferences. It is intended for participants to register for an event and choose their preferred payment method, such as Sofort, PayPal, or Stripe. This form helps event organizers to efficiently collect the required information for event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Event Registration Form With Sofort Payment Method" link or button to access the form.
* Fill out the registration information (Page 1) with your event title, which is the main title of the event.
* Proceed to the "Event Details" page (Page 2) and provide the relevant details about the event.
* On the "Participant Info" page (Page 3), enter your participant name and any other necessary participant information.
* On the "Payment Method" page (Page 4), select your preferred payment method: Sofort, PayPal, or Stripe.
* Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Info** (`title`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
	+ This is the main title of the event. Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Details** (`event_info`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
	+ Provide a brief description of the event, including any relevant details such as date, time, and location.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
	+ Enter any additional participant information that is necessary for the event organizers to know.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional: not required)
	+ Select your preferred payment method: Sofort, PayPal, or Stripe.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
	+ Enter your name as the participant registering for the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure they are correct.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact the event organizer for assistance.
