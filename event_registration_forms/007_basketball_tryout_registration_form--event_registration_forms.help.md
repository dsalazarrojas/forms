# Basketball Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basketball Tryout Registration Form is a mandatory form for players wishing to participate in the tryout. It is used to gather information about the player, including their contact information, medical conditions, and basketball experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

1. First, review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is being asked for.
2. Fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
3. Make sure to select your preferred tryout date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 1, text, required): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, text, required): Enter your full last name.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 3, date, required): Enter your birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Grade Level** (id: 4, select one, required): Select your current grade in school from the options provided.
* **Height** (id: 5, text, required): Enter your height in feet and inches (e.g., 5'8").
* **Weight** (id: 6, number, required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Primary Position** (id: 7, select one, required): Select your main position played (e.g., Point Guard, Shooting Guard, etc.).
* **Secondary Position** (id: 8, select one, required): Select an alternative position you can play (e.g., Point Guard, Shooting Guard, etc.). If you don't have a secondary position, select "None".
* **Years of Basketball Experience** (id: 9, select one, required): Select how long you have been playing basketball.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (id: 10, text, required): Enter the full name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (id: 11, email, required): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (id: 12, text, required): Enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 13, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 14, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (id: 15, text, optional): List any relevant medical information or allergies.
* **Previous Team or School** (id: 16, text, optional): Enter where you last played basketball.
* **Preferred Tryout Date** (id: 17, date, required): Select your preferred tryout date.
* **T-Shirt Size** (id: 18, select one, required): Select your T-shirt size for the tryout shirt (e.g., Youth Small, Youth Medium, Adult Small, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (id: 19, text, optional): Enter any additional information we should know about you.
