# Group Meeting Speaker Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register speakers for group meetings. It gathers information about the speaker, session, and category they will be speaking in. The form is used by meeting organizers to collect relevant details about the speaker before the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the speaker's name, session title, and session date in the respective fields.
2. Select the category (e.g., Conference, Training, Community Event) that best describes the session.
3. Enter the speaker's organization, location, and bio.
4. If applicable, upload an image or audio file for the speaker.
5. Finally, submit the form to register the speaker for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Name**: This field is used to collect the name of the speaker. Please enter the full name of the speaker.
* **Session Title**: This field is used to collect the title of the session the speaker will be speaking in. Be as descriptive as possible.
* **Session Date**: This field is used to collect the date of the session. Use the format: Month/Day/Year (e.g., March 12, 2023).
* **Speaker Organization**: This field is used to collect the organization or company that the speaker is affiliated with.
* **Speaker Location**: This field is used to collect the location where the session will take place. Be as specific as possible.
* **Session Details**: This field is used to collect any additional details about the session, such as room number or time slot.
* **Speaker Email**: This field is used to collect the speaker's email address. Please ensure it is accurate for communication purposes.
* **Speaker Phone**: This field is used to collect the speaker's phone number.
* **Category**: This field is used to select the category (e.g., Conference, Training, Community Event) that best describes the session. Select from the options provided.
* **Speaker Bio**: This field is used to collect a brief bio of the speaker. Keep it concise and professional.
* **Speaker Image**: This field is used to upload an image of the speaker. If not provided, do not upload an image.
* **Speaker Audio File**: This field is used to upload an audio file for the speaker. If not provided, do not upload an audio file.
* **Speaker Time**: This field is not currently used for this form and has been left blank.
* **Speaker Location**: This field is not currently used for this form and has been left blank.
