# Outstanding Visual Effects Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate an event for recognition of its outstanding visual effects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the event details and ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
2. Fill out the registration form (page 1) with the required information.
3. Provide additional event details (page 2) such as event description, images, videos, and audio files.
4. Enter information about the nominee (pages 3-10) such as their name, email, phone number, role, organization, and title.
5. Double-check your inputs before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Form** (`event_registration_form`, text, optional): This is the registration form for the event. Please fill out this form with the necessary information about the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the event.
* **Event Image** (`event_image`, text, optional): Upload or enter the URL of the image related to the event.
* **Event Video** (`event_video`, text, optional): Upload or enter the URL of the video related to the event.
* **Event Audio** (`event_audio`, text, optional): Upload or enter the URL of the audio file related to the event.
* **Event Files** (`event_files`, text, optional): Upload or enter the URL of other related files to the event.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the event.
* **Nominee Name** (`event_nominator_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Email** (`event_nominator_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the nominee.
* **Nominee Phone** (`event_nominator_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the nominee.
* **Nominee Role** (`event_nominator_role`, text, optional): Enter the role of the nominee.
* **Nominee Organization** (`event_nominator_organization`, text, optional): Enter the organization of the nominee.
* **Nominee Title** (`event_nominator_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the nominee.
