## Women's Winter Study Registration - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Women's Winter Study Registration form is a collection of questions that gather participant information, preferences, and other relevant details for the Women's Winter Study.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections carefully and understand what each field represents.
2. Fill in all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) with accurate and relevant information.
3. Select relevant options for session and participant type preferences.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration (*):** Enter your participant ID or a unique identifier for the registration process.
* **Participant Preferences:** Select your preferred participant type (e.g., Session 1, Session 2, Session 3, Session 4). This will help us tailor the study sessions according to your needs.
* **Session Preferences:** Select the session type you are interested in (e.g., Session 1, Session 2, Session 3, Session 4). This helps us organize the sessions effectively.
* **Participant Details:** Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, ID card).
* **Email (*):** Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone:** Enter your phone number for any additional communication or updates.
* **Session 1 Preference:** Select the session type you prefer for the first session.

Note: Fields marked with \* are required to be filled in for successful submission.
