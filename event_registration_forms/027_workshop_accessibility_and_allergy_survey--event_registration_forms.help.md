# workshop-accessibility-and-allergy-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about workshops, including their titles, organizers, dates, locations, accessibility needs, and allergies. This form helps event organizers provide attendees with essential information about the workshop's accessibility features and potential allergens.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the workshop title in the "Workshop Title" field.
2. Enter the name of the event organizer in the "Event Organizer Name" field.
3. Provide the email address of the event organizer in the "Event Organizer Email" field.
4. Enter the start date of the workshop in the "Workshop Start Date" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Enter the end date of the workshop in the "Workshop End Date" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
6. Enter the workshop location in the "Workshop Location" field.
7. Enter any relevant notes about the workshop in the "Event Organizer Notes" field.
8. Select the accessibility needs that apply to the workshop from the "Accessiblity Needs" field.
9. Select the allergens that will be used in the workshop from the "Allergies" field.
10. Enter your name in the "Event Organizer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the workshop, e.g., "Introduction to Accessible Design".
* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person organizing the workshop.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **Workshop Start Date** (`workshop_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the workshop in YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., "2022-01-01".
* **Workshop End Date** (`workshop_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the workshop in YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., "2022-01-02".
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the workshop, e.g., "New York City Hall".
* **Event Organizer Notes** (`workshop_organizer_notes`, text, optional): Enter any relevant notes about the workshop.
* **Accessiblity Needs** (`workshop_accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select the accessibility needs that apply to the workshop, e.g., Wheelchair accessible, Service animal accessible, Sign language accessible.
* **Allergies** (`workshop_allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Select the allergens that will be used in the workshop, e.g., Peanut, Milk, Egg.
* **Event Organizer Signature** (`workshop_organizer_signature`, text, optional): Enter your name as the event organizer.
