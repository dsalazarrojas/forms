# volunteer_service_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect volunteer contact information, emergency contact details, and medical conditions or allergies for the purposes of maintaining accurate records and ensuring effective communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete the **Volunteer Info** field with a brief description of your volunteer role or position.
2.  Fill out the **Volunteer Name** field with your full name as a volunteer.
3.  Enter your contact information in the **Contact Number** field.
4.  Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
5.  Fill out the **Emergency Contact Info** field with the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
6.  Provide the name of your emergency contact in the **Emergency Name** field.
7.  Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the **Emergency Phone** field.
8.  Enter your emergency contact's email address in the **Emergency Email** field.
9.  List any **Medical Conditions** you may have in this field.
10. List any **Medical Conditions 1**, **Medical Conditions 2**, or additional medical conditions you may have in the corresponding fields.
11. List any **Allergies** you may have in this field.
12. List any **Allergy 1**, **Allergy 2**, or additional allergies you may have in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Volunteer Info**: Please provide a brief description of your volunteer role or position.
*   **Volunteer Name**: Please enter your full name as a volunteer.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number that can be used to contact you.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
*   **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have that may impact your ability to perform volunteer work.
*   **Medical Condition 1**, **Medical Condition 2**, etc.: If you have any additional medical conditions not listed, please describe them here.
*   **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, such as food or environmental allergies.
*   **Allergy 1**, **Allergy 2**, etc.: If you have any additional allergies not listed, please describe them here.

## Tips
Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that we can reach you or your emergency contact in case of any incidents or issues.
