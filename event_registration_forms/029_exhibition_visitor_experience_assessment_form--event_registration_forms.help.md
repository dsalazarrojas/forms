# exhibition_visitor_experience_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to gather your feedback and opinions about your experience at the exhibition. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that future exhibitions meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the correct option for each field.
3. Enter your answers in the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall experience** (Overall Experience, select_one): 
  This field asks for your overall rating of your experience at the exhibition. Please select one of the options:

    - Option 1
    - Option 2
    - Option 3

* **Attendance** (Attendance, number): 
  This field asks for the number of people you attended with. Please enter the correct number.

* **Feedback** (Feedback, text): 
  In this field, you can share any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.

* **Date and time of visit** (Date and time of visit, date): 
  This field asks for the date and time you visited the exhibition. Please use the format: 2000-01-01 12:00:00

* **Event staff** (Event staff, select_multiple): 
  This field allows you to rate your experience with the event staff. Please select all the options that apply:

    - event staff Option 1
    - event staff Option 2
    - event staff Option 3

* **Catering** (Catering, number): 
  This field asks for your rating of the catering provided at the exhibition. Please enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

* **Exhibition layout** (Exhibition layout, text): 
  In this field, you can provide any feedback about the layout of the exhibition. Please be as specific as possible.

* **Event materials** (Event materials, select_one): 
  This field asks for your overall impression of the exhibition materials. Please select one of the options:

    - Option 1
    - Option 2
    - Option 3

* **Exhibition location** (Exhibition location, text): 
  This field asks for the location of the exhibition. Please enter the correct location.

## Tips

* Be specific when providing feedback, as it will help us identify areas for improvement.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Feedback" field.
