# Makeup Influencer Meet Up Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information and preferences for the Makeup Influencer Meet Up event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details in the **Participant Details** section (page 1).
2. Select your event preferences in the **Event Details** section (page 2).
3. Choose your participant preferences in the **Participant Preferences** section (page 3).
4. Provide additional notes in the **Additional Notes** section (page 4).
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field (page 5).
6. Confirm your participant agreement in the **Participant Agreement** section (page 6).
