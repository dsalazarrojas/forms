# leadership_coaching_session_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership coaching session absence form is used to record information about participants' absences from coaching sessions. This form helps to track the reason for absence, session details, and other relevant participant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the coaching session date for which the participant is absent.
2. Choose the reason for absence from the provided options or provide a detailed explanation.
3. Enter the participant's name and email address.
4. Provide the participant's session ID, if applicable.
5. Select the participant's status as Active or Inactive.
6. Choose whether the assigned tool is Yes or No.
7. Select the form category, title, and description for this specific form.
8. Choose whether the form is assigned to a tool, Yes or No.
9. Enter any additional session details, if necessary.
10. Provide the participant's phone number and address.
11. Select the participant's registration status as Yes or No.
12. Provide any participant notes or comments.
13. Select the participant's location and session status.
14. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coaching Session Date** (`coaching_session_date`, date, required/optional): Date of the coaching session for which the participant is absent.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, required/optional): Briefly explain the reason for the participant's absence.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, number, required/optional): Provide the participant's session ID, if applicable.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the participant's ID.
* **Reason for Absence (Other)** (`reason_for_absence_other`, text, required/optional): If the reason for absence is not listed, provide a detailed explanation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Whether the assigned tool is Yes or No.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the form ID.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the participant's status as Active or Inactive.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required/optional): Provide additional session details, if necessary.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Event Registration Form** (`event_registration_form`, select_one, required/optional): Whether the participant has registered for the event, Yes or No.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any participant notes or comments.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's location.
* **Session Status** (`session_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the session status as Active or Inactive.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the form category.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the form title.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): Enter the form description.
* **Form Assigned Tool** (`form_assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Whether the form is assigned to a tool, Yes or No.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate tracking and reporting.
- Double-check the participant's information for accuracy.
- Provide detailed explanations for reasons for absence, if applicable.
- Review the form before submission for any errors or missing information.
