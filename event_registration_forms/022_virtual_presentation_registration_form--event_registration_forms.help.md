# Virtual Presentation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from virtual presentation attendees. It asks for their personal details and preferences regarding the presentation type to provide a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last names.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Choose the date and time of the event.
4. Enter the name of the person attending the virtual presentation.
5. If they have an email, please provide it as well.
6. Select the type of presentation you would like to attend (Presentation or Panel).
7. Optionally, provide the title of the presentation you are interested in.
8. If you are a speaker, provide your name.
9. Click "Submit" to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This is your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the virtual presentation.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the virtual presentation.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): If someone is registering for the presentation instead of you, please provide their name.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): If the attendee has an email, please provide it.
* **Presentation Type** (`presentation_type`, select one, required): Select the type of presentation you are interested in (Presentation or Panel).
* **Presentation Title** (`presentation_title`, text, optional): Provide the title of the presentation you are interested in.
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, optional): If you are a speaker, enter your name.

## Tips
* Please ensure your email is correctly formatted for it to be used as a contact point.
* Make sure to select the correct presentation type and date/time for the virtual presentation.
* If you are a speaker, please double-check that your name is correctly entered to avoid confusion.
