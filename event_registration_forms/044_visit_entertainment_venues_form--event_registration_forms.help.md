# Visit Entertainment Venues Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Visit Entertainment Venues Form" is designed to collect information about events held at entertainment venues.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide event details such as the event name, date, and time.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
3. Optionally, provide additional details such as your phone number and any feedback or message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: A required field to enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date**: A required field to enter the date of the event. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time**: A required field to enter the time of the event. Please use the time format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event Location**: An optional field to enter the location of the event. This can be a specific address, venue name, or any other relevant details.
* **Event Description**: An optional field to provide a brief description of the event.
* **Visitor Name**: A required field to enter your name.
* **Visitor Email**: A required field to enter your email address.
* **Visitor Phone**: An optional field to enter your phone number.
* **Visitor Message**: An optional field to provide any additional feedback or message.
* **Feedback**: An optional field to provide any feedback or comments about the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the event and yourself.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers or venue staff.
