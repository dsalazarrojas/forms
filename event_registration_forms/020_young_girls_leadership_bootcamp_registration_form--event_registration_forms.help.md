# Young Girls' Leadership Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users complete the Young Girls' Leadership Bootcamp Registration Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the event type from the drop-down list.
2. Enter the first name and last name of the participant.
3. Enter the participant's email address.
4. Enter the participant's phone number.
5. Select the participants who will be attending the event from the list.
6. Enter the notes related to the participant.
7. Enter the date of the event.
8. Choose the time slot for the event.
9. If necessary, add additional participants to the event.
10. Enter any notes related to the event.
11. Enter the form ID.
12. Enter the submitted by field ( likely for administrative purposes).
13. Enter the submitted date.
14. Enter the updated by field (likely for administrative purposes).
15. Enter the updated date.
16. Enter the created by field (likely for administrative purposes).
17. Enter the created date.
18. Enter the deleted by field (likely for administrative purposes).
19. Enter the deleted date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, Required: `false`)
Choose the type of event that the participant will be attending.

### **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required: `false`)
Enter the participant's first name.

### **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required: `false`)
Enter the participant's last name.

### **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required: `false`)
Enter the participant's email address.

### **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required: `false`)
Enter the participant's phone number.

### **Participants** (`participants`, `number`, Required: `false`)
Select the participants who will be attending the event.

### **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, Required: `false`)
Enter the date of the event.

### **Time Slot** (`time_slot`, `time`, Required: `false`)
Choose the time slot for the event.

### **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, Required: `false`)
Enter any notes related to the participant.

### **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, Required: `false`)
Enter the form ID.

### **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, Required: `false`)
Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

### **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, Required: `false`)
Enter the date the form was submitted.

### **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, Required: `false`)
Enter the name of the person who updated the form.

### **Updated On** (`updated_on`, `date`, Required: `false`)
Enter the date the form was updated.

### **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, Required: `false`)
Enter the name of the person who created the form.

### **Created On** (`created_on`, `date`, Required: `false`)
Enter the date the form was created.

### **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, `text`, Required: `false`)
Enter the name of the person who deleted the form.

### **Deleted On** (`deleted_on`, `date`, Required: `false`)
Enter the date the form was deleted.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the event type and date to ensure accuracy.
* If you're not sure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.
