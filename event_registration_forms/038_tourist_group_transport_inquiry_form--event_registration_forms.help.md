# Tourist Group Transport Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from the group leader or manager for a Tourist Group Transport Inquiry. It gathers details such as group size, transport type, travel and return dates and times, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the group details, including size and any additional information.
2. Select the transport type and travel agency responsible for the group.
3. Provide travel dates and times.
4. Confirm return dates and times.
5. Enter the group name, contact name, email, and phone number.
6. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Group Details** (`group_details`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the group, including any relevant information about their needs and preferences.
- **Group Size** (`group_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people in the group.
- **Transport Type** (`transport_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of transport required for the group (Private or Public).
- **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of travel.
- **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of travel.
- **Return Date** (`return_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of return.
- **Return Time** (`return_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of return.
- **Group Name** (`group_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the group.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Address** (`address`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the group's address.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the group's requirements.
- **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `select_one`, required): Select the travel agency responsible for the group.
- **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `select_one`, optional): Select the staff member assigned to handle the group.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information or details about the group.
- **Group Size 2** (`group_size_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people in the second transport group.
- **Transport Type 2** (`transport_type_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of transport required for the second group (Private or Public).
- **Travel Date 2** (`travel_date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of travel for the second group.
- **Travel Time 2** (`travel_time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of travel for the second group.
- **Return Date 2** (`return_date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of return for the second group.
- **Return Time 2** (`return_time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of return for the second group.
- **Group Type 2** (`group_type_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of group for the second group (Private or Public).
- **Transport 2** (`transport_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the second transport group.
