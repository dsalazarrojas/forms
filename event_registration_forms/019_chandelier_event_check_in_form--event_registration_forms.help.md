# Chandelier Event Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to check in guests at Chandelier events, gathering essential information about them, their registration status, and any special needs or requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately for each guest.
2. Ensure that you enter the correct check-in date and time for each guest.
3. Provide accurate contact information for each guest, including their name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check-In Date** (<font size=3>1</font>): Date of event
* **Check-In Time** (<font size=3>2</font>): Time guest checks in
* **Guest Name** (<font size=3>3</font>): Enter the full name of the attendee
* **Guest Email** (<font size=3>4</font>): Enter the email address associated with the guest account
* **Guest Phone Number** (<font size=3>5</font>): Enter the contact phone number for the guest (if applicable)
* **Event Name** (<font size=3>6</font>): Name of the event the guest is attending
* **Event Location** (<font size=3>7</font>): Location where the event is being held
* **Ticket Number** (<font size=3>8</font>): If applicable, enter the ticket number for the guest
* **Registration Confirmation Code** (<font size=3>9</font>): Enter the registration confirmation code received by the guest
* **Registration Status** (<font size=3>10</font>): Choose the registration status of the guest (Confirmed Registration, Waitlist, Invited Guest, Walk-in)
* **Guest Category** (<font size=3>11</font>): Select the type of guest (Regular Attendee, VIP, Speaker, Sponsor, Media, Staff)
* **Company or Organization** (<font size=3>12</font>): If the guest represents a company, enter the name of the organization
* **Dietary Restrictions** (<font size=3>13</font>): If the guest has any special dietary needs, enter the details here
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (<font size=3>14</font>): Select any special accommodations needed by the guest (None, Wheelchair Access, Interpreting Services, Other)
* **Describe Accessibility Needs** (<font size=3>15</font>): If the guest requires special accommodations, provide more information here
* **Guest Badge Printed and Provided** (<font size=3>16</font>): Confirm that a guest badge has been printed and provided
* **Wristband Color or Badge Type** (<font size=3>17</font>): Enter the visual identifier assigned to the guest (if applicable)
* **Event Materials Received** (<font size=3>18</font>): Confirm that the guest has received event materials
* **Special Instructions or Notes** (<font size=3>19</font>): Enter any special instructions or notes for event staff
* **Staff Check-In Confirmation** (<font size=3>20</font>): Sign in as the staff member conducting the check-in, including your name
