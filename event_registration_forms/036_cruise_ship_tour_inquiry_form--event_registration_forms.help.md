# Cruise Ship Tour Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise Ship Tour Inquiry Form is used to gather information from users interested in booking a cruise ship tour. This form helps us understand their preferences and requirements for a successful tour experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Provide your Phone Number (optional).
5. Select the type of tour you are interested in (Oceanic or Continental).
6. Enter the travel date (if known).
7. Enter the travel duration (if known).
8. Provide any Special Requests you may have.
9. Enter any Comments or feedback about the tour.
10. Select the name of the person who will be assisting you.
11. Enter the date you were assisted (if applicable).
12. Optionally, submit a form by filling out all required fields and clicking submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your first name is required.
- **Last Name**: Your last name is not required but recommended.
- **Email**: A valid email address is required. This will be used to communicate with you.
- **Phone Number**: An optional field for users to provide their phone number. 
- **Tour Type**: A required field for you to select either Oceanic or Continental tour.
- **Travel Date**: An optional field to provide the travel date. 
- **Travel Duration**: An optional field to provide the travel duration.
- **Special Requests**: A text field for you to add any special requests or instructions.
- **Comments**: A text field to add any comments or feedback about the tour.
- **Assisted By**: A required field to select the name of the person who will be assisting you.
- **Assisted Date**: An optional field to provide the date the tour was assigned.
- **Submission Date**: This field is automatically populated and not editable.
- **Submitter Name**: This field is automatically populated and not editable.
