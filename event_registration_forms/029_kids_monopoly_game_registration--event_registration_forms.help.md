# kids-monopoly-game-registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect registration information from parents/guardians and kids for a Monopoly game event. It includes contact details, consent, medical conditions, and preferred game sessions. The form helps event organizers gather essential information to accommodate the participant's needs and ensure a smooth event experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have permission from the kid to participate.
3. Select the preferred game sessions for your kid.
4. Provide any necessary medical conditions or allergies.
5. Confirm your consent for your kid to participate.
6. Enter your contact information and kid's contact information.
7. Add any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Info**: Enter your name, email, and phone number as the parent/guardian.
* **Kid Info**: Enter your kid's name, date, and time as required.
* **Event Sessions**: Select the preferred game sessions for your kid.
* **Guardian Questions**: Answer the three questions regarding kid participation.
* **Kid Consent**: Confirm your consent for your kid to participate.
* **Preferred Sessions**: Select the preferred game sessions for your kid.
* **Guardian Contact**: Enter your contact information.
* **Kid Contact**: Enter your kid's contact information.
* **Guardian Note**: Add any comments or notes from the guardian.
* **Kid Note**: Add any comments or notes from the kid.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Event Date**: Enter the event date.
* **Event Time**: Enter the event time.
* **Event Location**: Enter the event location.
* **Event Questions**: Answer the two questions regarding the event.
* **Event Comments**: Add any comments or questions regarding the event.
* **Guardian Comments**: Add any comments or questions from the guardian.
* **Kid Comments**: Add any comments or questions from the kid.
* **Guardian Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the guardian and the kid.
* **Kid Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the kid and the guardian.
* **Submitted**: The form submission date.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* Ensure you have permission from the kid to participate in the game.
* Be honest and complete in your answers.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
