# lean-innovation-forum-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lean-innovation-forum-registration-form is a tool designed to gather information from users attending events such as innovation forums, networking sessions, conferences, seminars, webinars, workshops, and pitch competitions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Fill in the required fields with your details such as first name, last name, email address, phone number, zip code, date, time, and other specific information for the event you are attending.
3. Choose the correct event type from the options provided.
4. Select multiple options as required for certain aspects of the event.
5. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete notes for any additional comments or information.
