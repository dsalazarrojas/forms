# Parent Volunteering Session RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Parent Volunteering Session RSVP Form" is designed to register parent volunteers for a specific session. It's a crucial step to ensure that we have accurate information about their availability and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Event Date**: Select the date of the event or session you wish to volunteer for. This is a required field.
2. **Event Time**: Choose the time slot for the event or session. This is also a required field.
3. **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event or session will take place. This is an optional field, but it helps us better understand the event's context.
4. **Parent Volunteer Role**: Select one of the provided options or provide a custom role in the text field below. This helps us understand your role within the event.
5. **Parent Volunteer Availability**: Choose one or more of the provided options or provide a custom availability in the text field below. This ensures we have accurate information about your availability during the event.
6. **Additional Comments**: If you'd like to share any additional information about your availability or preferences, use this text field.
7. **Parent Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official ID or record. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for our internal records.
8. **Email**: Enter your email address. This is an optional field, but it helps us reach out to you with updates or further information.
9. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This is an optional field, but it's useful for contacting you with important updates or last-minute changes.
10. **Relationship**: If you have any relationship with a child attending the event, please provide details in the text field.
11. **School/Institution**: If you're affiliated with a school or institution, please provide the name.
12. **Position**: If you have a position within the school or institution, please provide the details in the text field.
13. **Volunteer Session Details**: If you'd like to provide any additional information about the session, such as your role or expectations, use this text field.
14. **RSVP**: Please confirm your availability to volunteer for the session by writing "Yes, I'm available to volunteer" in this text field. This is a required field.

## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate information in each field to help us manage your involvement effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
* Please note that we appreciate your commitment to volunteering and look forward to having you on board!
