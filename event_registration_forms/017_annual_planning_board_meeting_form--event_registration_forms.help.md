# Annual Planning Board Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Planning Board Meeting Form is a document designed to gather information from board members and attendees about their needs and preferences for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your Email Address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Specify your Position or Title in the "Position or Title" field (optional).
5. Enter your Organization or Department in the "Organization or Department" field.
6. Choose your attendance status for the meeting in the "Will you attend the meeting" field.
7. Select your preferred attendance method (in-person, virtual, or hybrid) in the "Preferred attendance method" field (optional).
8. Enter the Meeting Date in the "Meeting Date" field.
9. Choose the Meeting Time in the "Meeting Time" field.
10. Provide the Meeting Location or Venue in the "Meeting Location or Venue" field (optional).
11. List any Dietary restrictions or allergies in the "Dietary restrictions or allergies" field (optional).
12. Specify any Special accommodations needed in the "Special accommodations needed" field (optional).
13. List any Agenda items for discussion in the "Agenda items for discussion" field (optional).
14. Mention Priority discussion topics in the "Priority discussion topics" field (optional).
15. Indicate if you need Pre-meeting preparation materials in the "Pre-meeting preparation materials needed" field.
16. Select your preferred format for materials (email, printed copies, online portal access, PDF attachments, video presentations) in the "Preferred format for materials" field (optional).
17. Provide any Budget allocation proposals in the "Budget allocation proposals" field (optional).
18. List any Strategic initiatives for the next year in the "Strategic initiatives for next year" field (optional).
19. Specify any Resource needs or requirements in the "Resource needs or requirements" field (optional).
20. Choose if you would like a copy of meeting minutes (yes, email, yes, include in portal, no) in the "Would you like a copy of meeting minutes" field (optional).
21. Mention your Availability for any follow-up meeting in the "Availability for follow-up meeting" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name.
* **Email Address (2)** (`attendee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for meeting communications.
* **Phone Number (3)** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for coordination.
* **Position or Title (4)** (`position_title`, text, optional): Specify your position or title.
* **Organization or Department (5)** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your organization or department.
* **Will you attend the meeting (6)** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Choose your attendance status (yes, I will attend, no, I will not attend, tentative - will confirm later, virtual attendance only).
* **Preferred attendance method (7)** (`attendance_method`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred attendance method (in-person, virtual - via Zoom or Teams, hybrid - flexible, not applicable).
* **Meeting Date (8)** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time (9)** (`meeting_time`, time, required): Choose the start time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location or Venue (10)** (`meeting_location`, text, optional): Provide the meeting location or venue.
* **Dietary restrictions or allergies (11)** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): List any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Special accommodations needed (12)** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): Specify any special accommodations needed.
* **Agenda items for discussion (13)** (`agenda_items`, text, optional): List any agenda items for discussion.
* **Priority discussion topics (14)** (`priority_topics`, text, optional): Mention priority discussion topics.
* **Pre-meeting preparation materials needed (15)** (`pre_meeting_prep`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need pre-meeting preparation materials (yes, please send, no, not needed, unsure).
* **Preferred format for materials (16)** (`materials_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred format for materials (email, printed copies, online portal access, PDF attachments, video presentations).
* **Budget allocation proposals (17)** (`budget_allocation`, text, optional): Provide any budget allocation proposals.
* **Strategic initiatives for next year (18)** (`strategic_initiatives`, text, optional): List any strategic initiatives for next year.
* **Resource needs or requirements (19)** (`resource_needs`, text, optional): Specify any resource needs or requirements.
* **Would you like a copy of meeting minutes (20)** (`meeting_minutes_copy`, select_one, optional): Choose if you would like a copy of meeting minutes (yes, email, yes, include in portal, no).
* **Availability for follow-up meeting (21)** (`availability_follow_up`, text, optional): Mention your availability for any follow-up meeting.
