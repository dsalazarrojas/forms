# Event Organizer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Organizer Application Form is designed to collect information from event organizers who want to submit an application to organize an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your event information in the "Event Info" field.
2. Provide main event details in the "Main Event Details" field.
3. Select the schedule for your event from the "Event Schedule" field (Monday to Sunday).
4. Enter your name and contact information in the "Event Organizer Name", "Event Organizer Email", and "Event Organizer Phone" fields.
5. Enter your address in the "Event Organizer Address" field.
6. Add any additional notes about your event in the "Event Organizer Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Info** (Event Info): Enter a brief description of your event.
- **Main Event Details** (Main Event Details): Provide a detailed description of your event, including its purpose, expected date, time, and location.
- **Event Schedule** (Event Schedule): Select the dates you would like to hold your event. You can choose from Monday to Sunday.
- **Event Organizer Name** (Event Organizer Name): Enter your name as the event organizer.
- **Event Organizer Email** (Event Organizer Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Event Organizer Phone** (Event Organizer Phone): Enter your phone number as the event organizer.
- **Event Organizer Address** (Event Organizer Address): Enter your address where the event will take place or where you can be reached.
- **Event Organizer Notes** (Event Organizer Notes): Add any additional information about your event if necessary (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure it is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
