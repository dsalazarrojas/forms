# Skiing Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for you to acknowledge and accept liability and terms of participation in skiing events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the Liability Acknowledgment section.
2. Sign and date the form.
3. Have your parent or guardian sign and date the form if you are under 18.
4. Ensure the Instructor signs and dates the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`): Please provide your basic information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Liability Acknowledgment** (`liability_acknowledgment`): Check the box to acknowledge that you understand the risks involved in skiing and accept liability for any injuries or damages that may occur.
* **Terms of Participation** (`terms_of_participation`): Read and agree to the terms of participation, which include your responsibilities and the responsibilities of the event organizers.
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`): If you are under 18, please have your parent or guardian sign and date the consent section.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement to the terms of participation.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`): The instructor or event organizer should sign and date the form to confirm their agreement to the terms of participation and liability acknowledgment.
* **Date** (`date`): Please enter the date you complete this form.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`): Enter the name of the instructor or event organizer.
* **Instructor Title** (`instructor_title`): Enter the title or position of the instructor or event organizer.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`): The instructor or event organizer should sign and date the form to confirm their agreement to the terms of participation and liability acknowledgment.
