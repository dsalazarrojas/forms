# Multiplier Event Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Multiplier Event Satisfaction Survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question based on your experience at the online event.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers to help the event team understand your feedback.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, feel free to share them in the "Note" field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number if you are willing to be contacted for further discussion or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you find the online multiplier event?** (`multiplier_event_satisfaction`, text, optional)
	+ This question is designed to get a general feel for how you perceived the event in terms of its organization and structure.
* **How would you rate the organization of the event?** (`event_organization`, number, optional)
	+ This question is a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5) where you can rate the organization of the event, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **How would you rate the content of the event?** (`event_content`, select_one, optional)
	+ This question offers three options:
		- Too much
		- Just right
		- Too little
	+ Choose the option that best describes your experience with the event content.
* **How would you rate your overall experience at the event?** (`overall_experience`, time, optional)
	+ This question also uses a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5), with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **What do you think we could have done better?** (`event_feedback`, text, optional)
	+ Use this field to share any suggestions or constructive criticism you may have about the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ If you are willing to be contacted for further discussion or feedback, enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ If you are willing to be contacted for further discussion or feedback, enter your phone number here.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional)
	+ Use this field to add any additional comments or notes about the event.
