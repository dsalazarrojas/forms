# Market Feasibility Research Session Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for market feasibility research sessions. Please fill out this form to provide your information and preferences for an upcoming session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. If you need help with any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Info (Page 1)
Participant Info (`participant_info`, Text, Optional)

* This field is for you to input your participant's name and any other relevant details. Please be sure to include any necessary contact information so we can get in touch with you about the session.

### Session Info (Page 1)
Session Info (`session_info`, Text, Optional)

* This field is for you to provide information about your interests or goals related to the market feasibility research session.

### Contact Info (Page 3)
Contact Info (`contact_info`, Text, Required)

* This is a required field, as we need to be able to contact you regarding your session. Please input your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.

### Session Preferences (Page 4)
Session Preferences (`session_preferences`, Text, Optional)

* Choose a session mode that aligns with your interests and goals. This will help us tailor the session to your needs.

### Submit (Page 5)
Submit (`submit`, Text, Required)

* Once you've filled out the form, click "Submit" to send your information to us. This is the final step in the registration process.

## Tips

* Please double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
* We're committed to keeping your information secure and confidential.
