# DJ Service Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "DJ Service Inquiry Form" is designed to collect information about your event and services required for your DJ to deliver an exceptional experience for your guests. Fill out this form to help us better understand your event needs and prepare accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Event Details** section with your event name, date, location, and any other relevant details.
2.  Select your preferred **DJ Info** from the provided options (A, B, or C) to choose the right DJ for your event.
3.  Provide your **Inquiry Details** in the text field to help us understand your specific requirements.
4.  Choose your preferred **Date and Time** for the event.
5.  Add your **Contact Info** so we can get back to you with any questions or updates.
6.  Enter your **Email** address for us to contact you about your event.
7.  Add any additional **Message** or notes about your event.
8.  Select the **Services Requested** you need for your event (e.g., Service 1, 2, or 3).
9.  Enter your **Budget** for the event.
10. Provide any additional **Event Info** about your event in the text field.
11. Choose the **Event Type** (A, B, or C) that best describes your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter the name and any other relevant details about your event.
* **DJ Info** (`dj_info`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the right DJ (A, B, or C) for your event.
* **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, text, optional): Provide any specific details about your event.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, time, optional): Enter your preferred date and time for the event.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Add your name and contact number to ensure we get back to you with any questions or updates.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you about your event.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your event.
* **Services Requested** (`services_requested`, select_one, optional): Choose the services you need for your event (Service 1, 2, or 3).
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter your budget for the event.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional event information.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of event (A, B, or C) that best describes your event.
