# Best Supporting Role Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Supporting Role Nomination Form is used to nominate an individual for recognition of their outstanding support to the team or organization. This form is used to gather information that can be used to evaluate and recognize the nominee's contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have a clear understanding of your relationship to the nominee, as it will be required for your submission to be valid.
2. Fill in the form completely and accurately, paying attention to all required fields.
3. Select a valid option for each evaluation question where applicable.
4. Provide specific examples of the nominee's supporting role in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (3, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel document.
* **Your Department** (4, text, required): Enter your department name as it appears in the organizational chart.
* **Your Email Address** (5, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Nominee Full Name** (7, text, required): Enter the full name of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Department or Role** (8, text, required): Enter the nominee's department or role in the organization.
* **Your Relationship to the Nominee** (9, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to describe your relationship to the nominee:
	+ Colleague or Peer
	+ Immediate Supervisor
	+ Direct Report
	+ Project Partner
	+ Other
* **Reliability and Dependability** (10, select_one, required): Rate the nominee's reliability and dependability from 1 to 5.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration** (11, select_one, required): Rate the nominee's teamwork and collaboration from 1 to 5.
* **Initiative and Proactiveness** (12, select_one, required): Rate the nominee's initiative and proactiveness from 1 to 5.
* **Positive Attitude and Morale Impact** (13, select_one, required): Rate the nominee's positive attitude and morale impact from 1 to 5.
* **Impact on Project Success** (14, select_one, required): Rate the nominee's impact on project success from 1 to 5.
* **Describe a specific project where this person provided critical support** (16, text, required): Provide an example of a project where the nominee provided critical support.
* **What makes this person an ideal candidate for Best Supporting Role** (17, text, required): Describe why you think the nominee is an ideal candidate for this recognition.
* **How do they support the rest of the team on a regular basis** (18, text, required): Describe the nominee's consistent daily actions that support the team.
* **One word that describes their supporting style** (19, text, required): Choose a word that best describes the nominee's supporting style.
* **Any other comments or anecdotes** (21, text, optional): Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for the evaluation committee.
* **Date of Nomination** (22, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this nomination.
