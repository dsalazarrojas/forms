# cycling_retreat_form_grey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cycling Retreat Form is designed to gather information from participants for a smooth and enjoyable experience. Please fill out this form to provide necessary details about your registration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your personal information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that your email is entered correctly to facilitate communication from the event organizers.
2. If you have any existing health or safety concerns, please describe them in the space provided under "waiver". This will help us to better understand your needs and provide necessary support.
3. Provide your contact information, including a valid date and time for your participation in the retreat.
4. Enter your address to ensure we can reach you in case of any last-minute updates or emergencies.
5. Add a signature to confirm your registration.
6. Finally, provide any additional event-specific information in the "event_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **registration_information** (1, text, not required): This is the section where you provide personal details such as your name and email address.
- **waiver** (2, text, not required): Describe any health or safety concerns you have, so we can provide the necessary support.
- **name** (3, text, not required): Enter your name, as it will be displayed in our participant list.
- **email** (4, email, not required): Enter a valid email address where we can send updates and information about the event.
- **phone** (5, text, not required): Provide your phone number, so we can contact you in case of any urgent matters.
- **date** (6, date, not required): Enter a date for your participation in the retreat.
- **time** (7, time, not required): Specify a time for your arrival and departure.
- **address** (8, text, not required): Enter your physical address, so we can reach you in case of any last-minute updates or emergencies.
- **signature** (9, text, not required): Add a signature to confirm your registration.
- **event_details** (10, text, not required): Provide any additional information about your participation in the retreat, such as your preferred activities or dietary requirements.
