# Men's Metro Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Men's Metro Registration Form is designed to collect basic contact information and event preferences from individuals who want to register for events related to metropolitan areas or commute-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and any other required information as needed.
2.  Select your event type (Metro-based or Event).
3.  Choose your contact preference (Email, Phone, or In-person).
4.  Confirm your email address for email-based contact.
5.  Enter any additional notes or comments if necessary.
6.  Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Name**
*   This field is for your name. Please enter the name you want to be known by.

### 2. **Email**
*   This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

### 3. **Phone**
*   This field is for your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.

### 4. **Date of Birth**
*   This field is for your date of birth. Please enter the date you were born.

### 5. **Gender**
*   This field is for your gender. Please select one of the following options: Male, Female, or Other.

### 6. **Contact Preference**
*   This field is for how you want to be contacted. Please select one of the following options: Email, Phone, or In-person.

### 7. **Type of Event**
*   This field is for the type of event you want to register for. Please select one of the following options: Metro-based, Commuter, or Event.

### 8. **Notes**
*   This field is for any additional comments or information about your event registration. Please enter any relevant details if needed.

### 9. **Submit**
*   Once you've completed all the required fields, click on the "Submit" button to submit your form.

### 10. **Email Verification**
*   This field is for verification of your email address. Please enter the same email address you provided earlier in the form.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number.
*   Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
