# public_park_venue_permission_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_park_venue_permission_application_form is used to submit information about an event or activity to be held at a public park. This form is intended to request permission from the park authorities to use the park space.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the event details, including the event date and start/end times.
2. Provide contact information for the event organiser and any contact person responsible for the event.
3. Enter the expected event capacity.
4. Specify the space requirements for the event.
5. Enter the park address and post office contact information.
6. Fill out the remaining fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (page 1): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (page 1): Enter the date of the event (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Event Time** (page 1): Enter the start time of the event (HH:MM format).
* **Event End Date** (page 1): Enter the end date of the event (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Event End Time** (page 1): Enter the end time of the event (HH:MM format).
* **Event Organiser** (page 1): Enter the name of the event organiser.
* **Contact Person** (page 1): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (page 1): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (page 1): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Event Capacity** (page 2): Enter the maximum number of attendees expected.
* **Event Space Required** (page 2): Enter the space required for the event (e.g., area, length, etc.).
* **Venue Requirements** (page 2): Enter any additional requirements for the venue (e.g., power supply, parking, etc.).
* **Park Address** (page 2): Enter the address of the park where the event will be held.
* **Post Office Box** (page 2): Enter the post office box number of the park.
* **Postal Code** (page 2): Enter the postal code of the park.
* **City** (page 2): Enter the city of the park.
* **Country** (page 2): Enter the country of the park.
* **State** (page 2): Enter the state of the park.
* **Region** (page 2): Enter the region of the park.
* **Post Office Address** (page 3): Enter the address of the post office where the event will be hosted.
* **Post Office City** (page 3): Enter the city of the post office.
* **Post Office Postal Code** (page 3): Enter the postal code of the post office.
* **Post Office Country** (page 3): Enter the country of the post office.
* **Post Office State** (page 3): Enter the state of the post office.
* **Post Office Region** (page 3): Enter the region of the post office.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
