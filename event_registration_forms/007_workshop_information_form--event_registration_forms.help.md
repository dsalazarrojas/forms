# Workshop Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to provide essential details about an upcoming workshop. Fill out this form to ensure that all necessary information is captured and communicated effectively to participants and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the **Workshop Name** in the first field. This is the name of the workshop, which should be descriptive and accurately represent the event.
2. Select the **Workshop Date** in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or mm/dd/yyyyy (if the workshop lasts multiple days). This field is used to determine the date of the workshop.
3. Enter the **Workshop Start Time** in the format of HH:MM AM/PM. This field is used to determine the start time of the workshop.
4. Choose the **Workshop Duration** in minutes. This field is used to determine the length of the workshop.
5. Choose the **Workshop Venue** from the available options:
	* **Online**: The workshop will be held online.
	* **Offsite**: The workshop will be held offsite.
	* **Onsite**: The workshop will be held onsite.
	* **Hybrid**: The workshop will be a mix of online and offline components.
6. Enter the **Workshop Organizer**'s name and contact information in the provided text field.
7. Enter the **Workshop Location** in the provided text field. This field is used to determine the physical location of the workshop.
8. Enter a brief **Workshop Description** that summarizes the workshop's purpose and content. This field is used to provide a brief overview of the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the workshop.
* **Workshop Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Start Time (Time)**: Enter the start time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration (Number)**: Choose the length of the workshop in minutes.
* **Workshop Venue (Select One)**: Choose the type of venue where the workshop will be held.
* **Workshop Organizer (Text)**: Enter the name and contact information of the workshop organizer.
* **Workshop Location (Text)**: Enter the physical location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Description (Text)**: Enter a brief summary of the workshop's purpose and content.
