# Corporate Responsibility Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for the Corporate Responsibility Seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the participant's information in the "Participant Information" page.
2. Provide contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
3. Enter seminar details and any additional notes in the "Seminar Information" page.
4. Select the participant's email, phone, and manager's information in the respective pages.
5. Choose the participant's role and attendee type in the "Participant Roles" and "Attendee Type" pages.
6. Finally, review and confirm the information provided and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information** (Text)
	+ This page is where you will enter the basic participant's details.
- **Contact Information** (Text)
	+ This page is where you can provide your contact information for any follow-up questions or updates.
- **Seminar Information** (Text)
	+ This is where you can add any additional information about the seminar.
- **Participant Notes** (Note)
	+ You can add any notes about the participant here.
- **Participant Emails** (Select Multiple)
	+ Select the participant's email addresses.
- **Participant Phones** (Select Multiple)
	+ Select the participant's phone numbers.
- **Seminar Date** (Date)
	+ Enter the date of the seminar.
- **Seminar Time** (Time)
	+ Enter the time of the seminar.
- **Participant Email** (Email)
	+ Enter the participant's email address.
- **Participant Phone** (Text)
	+ Enter the participant's phone number.
- **Participant Name** (Text)
	+ Enter the participant's name.
- **Participant Title** (Text)
	+ Enter the participant's title.
- **Participant Role** (Text)
	+ Choose the participant's role.
- **Participant Department** (Text)
	+ Enter the participant's department.
- **Participant Job Title** (Text)
	+ Enter the participant's job title.
- **Seminar Location** (Text)
	+ Enter the location of the seminar.
- **Seminar Address** (Text)
	+ Enter the address of the seminar location.
- **Participant Company** (Text)
	+ Enter the participant's company name.
- **Participant Organization** (Text)
	+ Enter the participant's organization name.
- **Participant Manager** (Select One)
	+ Choose the participant's manager.
- **Participant Manager Phone** (Select One)
	+ Choose the participant's manager's phone number.
- **Participant Manager Email** (Select One)
	+ Choose the participant's manager's email address.
- **Participant Attendee Type** (Select One)
	+ Choose the participant's attendee type.
- **Participant Attendee Number** (Number)
	+ Enter the number of attendees.
- **Confirm** (Select One)
	+ Confirm your registration.
- **Participant Agree** (Select One)
	+ Agree to the terms and conditions.
- **Participant Agree** (Select One)
	+ You must agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
