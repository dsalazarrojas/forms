# Risk Assessment Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess and document potential safety risks for a specific event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the risk assessment date.
2. Enter the risk assessment time.
3. Select the risk category (High, Medium, or Low) that best describes the risk level.
4. Enter the risk impact description (optional).
5. Enter the risk probability description (optional).
6. Enter a brief risk summary.
7. Enter the participant's name.
8. Enter the risk description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Risk Assessment
* **Risk Assessment**: Enter the date of the risk assessment (e.g., 2022-01-01).
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the person or team performing the risk assessment (e.g., John Doe).
### Risk Description
* **Risk Description**: Describe the potential hazards or perils that could occur during the event or activity.
### Risk Category
* **Risk Category**: Select the level of risk associated with the hazard or peril (High, Medium, or Low).
### Risk Assessment Date
* **Risk Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the risk assessment (e.g., 2022-01-01).
### Risk Assessment Time
* **Risk Assessment Time**: Enter the time of the risk assessment (e.g., 14:00).
### Risk Level
* **Risk Level**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to represent the risk level.
### Risk Impact
* **Risk Impact**: Enter a brief description of the potential impact of the risk (e.g., injury, financial loss).
### Risk Probability
* **Risk Probability**: Enter a brief description of the likelihood of the risk occurrence (e.g., unlikely, possible).
### Risk Summary
* **Risk Summary**: Enter a brief summary of the risk assessment.

Tips:
* Ensure that you have conducted a thorough risk assessment before completing this form.
* Enter accurate and truthful information to ensure a complete and accurate risk assessment.
* Review and revise the risk assessment release form as necessary to reflect any changes or updates in the risk assessment.
