# Skydiving Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skydiving Liability Waiver Form is a mandatory document that needs to be completed by all participants and their guardians before participating in a skydiving event. It is designed to ensure that all parties are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the event, including medical procedures and emergency response plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read each section carefully** before starting to complete the form.
2. **Provide accurate and truthful information** for all questions and fields.
3. **Sign and date the form** after completing all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`): This section collects basic information about the participant, including name, age, contact details, and medical conditions. Please provide your real name, address, and contact phone number.
* **Guardian Information** (`guardian_information`): This section is for guardians or parents of minor participants. Please provide your name, relationship to the participant, and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): This section collects information about the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency. Please provide their name, phone number, and relationship to the participant.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your skydiving experience, please list them here.
* **Skydiving Experience** (`skydiving_experience`): Please indicate your level of skydiving experience, from 1 to 5, where 1 is a complete beginner and 5 is an experienced skydiver.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`): If you have any experience with safety equipment, please select the level from 1 to 5.
* **Safety Procedures** (`safety_procedures`): If you have any experience with safety procedures, please select the level from 1 to 5.
* **Safety Equipment Rental** (`safety_equipment_rental`): If you plan to rent safety equipment, select the level from 1 to 5.
* **Safety Equipment Return** (`safety_equipment_return`): If you plan to return safety equipment, select the level from 1 to 5.
* **Emergency Medical Response** (`emergency_medical_response`): Please select the time it takes for emergency medical response to arrive in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Medical Facilities** (`emergency_medical_facilities`): Please select the number of emergency medical facilities available.
* **Emergency Medical Transportation** (`emergency_medical_transportation`): Please select the number of emergency medical transportation options available.
* **Medical Release** (`medical_release`): Please select the level of medical release, from 1 to 5.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Please provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Please provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Please select the relationship between the emergency contact person and the participant.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): Please sign this section to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`): This section is for guardians or parents of minor participants. Please sign this section to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`): This section is for instructors or staff. Please sign this section to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Date** (`date`): Please select the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`): Please select the time of the event.
* **Location** (`location`): Please provide the location of the event.
* **Instructor** (`instructor`): Please provide the name of the instructor.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`): Please provide the name of the course or event.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`): Please sign this section to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
